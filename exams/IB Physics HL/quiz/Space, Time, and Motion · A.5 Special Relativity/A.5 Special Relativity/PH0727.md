# PH0727

A spaceship is moving at a constant velocity relative to a station. The spacetime diagram shows the worldline of the spaceship in the frame of reference of the station.

![Screenshot 2025-06-02 at 9.56.19 AM](<images/Screenshot 2025-06-02 at 9.56.19 AM.JPEG>)

Which of the following gives the speed of the spaceship in terms of $c$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ the shaded area

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{1}{\textrm{ The shaded area}}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\tan\theta$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{1}{\tan\theta}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The ratio of $\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta y}=\dfrac{x}{ct}=\dfrac{v}{c}$ = speed of spaceship as a fraction of the speed of light

and from the graph,

- $\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta y}= \tan \theta$
