# PH1934

Two events, $A$ and $B$, are shown on the space-time diagram for an inertial reference frame. The horizontal axis represents position $x$, and the vertical axis represents $ct$.

![Screenshot 2026-03-09 at 2.40.51 PM](<images/Screenshot 2026-03-09 at 2.40.51 PM.png>)

What is the square of the space-time interval
($\Delta s$)$^2$ between $A$ and $B$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 62 m$^2$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 3900 m$^2$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 4400 m$^2$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 7800 m$^2$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Using the graph:

![Screenshot 2026-02-18 at 10.24.26 AM](<images/Screenshot 2026-02-18 at 10.24.26 AM.png>)

- $c\Delta t=120-40=80$ m

- $\Delta x=75-25=50$ m

Using the equation of the space-time interval:

- $(\Delta s)^2=(c\Delta t)^2-\Delta x^2=80^2-50^2=3900$ m$^2$
