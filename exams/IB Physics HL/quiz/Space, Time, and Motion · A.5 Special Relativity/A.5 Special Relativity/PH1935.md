# PH1935

Two rockets, $A$ and $B$, move in the same direction along a straight line. Rocket $A$ is catching up to rocket $B$. An observer on Earth measures the speed of rocket $B$ to be 0.6$c$. In the rest frame of rocket $B$, the speed of rocket $A$ is measured to be 0.5$c$.

![Screenshot 2026-02-18 at 11.14.27 AM](<images/Screenshot 2026-02-18 at 11.14.27 AM.png>)

What is the speed of rocket
$A$ as measured by the observer on Earth?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.65$c$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.75$c$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.85$c$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 1.0$c$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Using the relativistic velocity addition formula:

- $u'=\dfrac{u-v}{1-\dfrac{uv}{c^2}}$

Where $u'=0.50c$ (speed of $A$ in $B$'s reference frame) and $v=0.60c$ (speed of the frame of $B$ relative to Earth)

- $0.50c=\dfrac{u-0.60c}{1-\dfrac{u(0.60c)}{c^2}}$

- $u=\dfrac{0.50c+0.60c}{1+\dfrac{(0.50c)(0.60c)}{c^2}}=0.85c$
