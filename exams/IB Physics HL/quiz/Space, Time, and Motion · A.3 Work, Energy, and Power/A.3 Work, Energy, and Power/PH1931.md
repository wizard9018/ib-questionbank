## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Motion is along a frictionless horizontal surface:

- $W=\Delta E_k=\Delta\left(\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2\right)$

- $W\propto\Delta v^2$

In the first and final regions, where a constant positive force acts, the work done increases linearly with displacement.

- $Fs=W$

Therefore, $v^2$ increases linearly with $s$.

For the middle region, there is no work done. Hence, there is no change in $v^2$ during this period of time.
