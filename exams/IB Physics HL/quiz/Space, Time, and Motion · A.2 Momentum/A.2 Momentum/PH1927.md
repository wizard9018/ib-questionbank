# PH1927

A tennis ball of mass 0.060 kg moves towards a vertical wall with a speed of 3.0 m s$^{-1}$. It collides with the wall and rebounds along the same line with a speed of 2.0 m s$^{-1}$. The duration of the collision is 0.020 s.

What is the magnitude of the average force exerted by the ball on the wall during the collision?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 3.0 N

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 15 N

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 30 N

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 150 N

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

By Newton's Third Law, the force exerted on the wall has the same magnitude as the force exerted on the ball. The change in velocity, $\Delta v$, is due to the reversal of direction. Using a sign convention where the initial direction of motion is positive:

- $\Delta v=v_2 -v_1=(-2.0)-(3.0)=-5.0$ m s$^{-1}$

Using the impulse-momentum equation:

- $F=\dfrac{\Delta p}{t}=\dfrac{m\Delta v}{t}=\dfrac{(0.060)(-5.0)}{0.020}=-15$ N

We can ignore any negative signs as we are asked to find the magnitude of the force.

- $F=15$ N
