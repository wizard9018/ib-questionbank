# PH1892

Object $A$ has a mass 2.0 kg and is initially moving to the right with a speed of 2.0 m s$^{-1}$. Object $B$ has a mass of 3.0 kg and is initially moving to the left with a speed of 1.0 m s$^{-1}$.

![Screenshot 2026-03-10 at 7.10.50 AM](<images/Screenshot 2026-03-10 at 7.10.50 AM.png>)

The objects collide and move together following the collision. What is the speed and direction of the final object?

| | Speed / m s$^{-1}$ | Direction |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| 0.2 | To the left |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 0.2 | To the right |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 1.4 | To the left |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 1.4 | To the right |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Momentum $p=mv$ is conserved so we have:

- $p_\textrm{before}=p_\textrm{after}$

The momentum before the collision is the total momentum of $A$ and $B$. Taking right in the positive direction we have:

- $p_\textrm{before}=(2)(2)+(3)(-1)=1$ kg m s$^{-1}$

(Note that we have assigned a negative sign on object $B$'s velocity as it is pointing to the left). The final mass of the coalesced object is $2+3=5$ kg so we have:

- $1=p_\textrm{after}=m_\textrm{total}v_\textrm{final}$

- $1=5v$

- $v=0.2$ m s$^{-1}$

The sign of $v$ is positive, so the object is moving to the right.
