# PH1930

A small ball of mass $m$ is attached to a light, inextensible rope and moves in a vertical circular path of radius $r$, as shown.

![Screenshot 2026-02-18 at 9.57.44 AM](<images/Screenshot 2026-02-18 at 9.57.44 AM.png>)

The maximum tension the rope can withstand is
$T$. The ball reaches a maximum speed $v$ without the rope breaking at the lowest point.

Which expression is equal to the magnitude of the acceleration of the ball at the lowest point of the circular motion?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{2v^2}{r}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{v^2}{2r}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{T+mg}{m}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{T-mg}{m}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The forces acting on the ball at the lowest point are tension $T$ upward and weight $W$ downward. Using Newton's second law:

$\begin{aligned}F_{net}&=ma\\
\ \\
\ T-W&=ma\\
\ \\
\ T-mg&=ma\\
\ \\
 a&=\dfrac{T-mg}{m}\\
\end{aligned}$
