# PH1926

A cart is given an initial push so that it moves at a speed of 0.50 m s$^{-1}$ up a frictionless ramp. During its motion, the cart experiences an acceleration of 0.10 m s$^{-2}$ down the ramp.

What is the distance travelled by the cart after 7.0 s?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 1.05 m

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 1.25 m

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 1.45 m

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 1.65 m

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The cart initially moves up the slope with a velocity of 0.50 m s$^{-1}$ while a constant acceleration of 0.10 m s$^{-2}$ acts in the opposite direction. Taking up the slope as the positive direction, the acceleration is $-$0.10 m s$^{-2}$.

Calculating how long it takes for the cart to come to rest

- $a=\dfrac{v-u}{t}$

- $t=\dfrac{0-0.50}{-0.10}=5$ s

The cart comes to rest after 5.0 s.

Because the total time of 7.0 s is greater than this, the cart first slows down, stops, and then reverses direction. During the first 5.0 s, the distance travelled $s_1$ is given by using one of the suvat equations ($s=ut+\dfrac{1}{2}at^2$):

- $s_1=(0.50)(5.0)+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)(-0.10)(5.0)^2=1.25$ m

After stopping, the cart moves back down the slope for the remaining 2.0 s starting from rest, and the distance travelled $s_2$ in this time:

- $s_2=ut+\dfrac{1}{2}at^2=(0)(2)+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)(0.10)(2)^2=0.20$ m

The total distance $s$ travelled:

- $s=1.25$ m $+0.20$ m $=1.45$ m
