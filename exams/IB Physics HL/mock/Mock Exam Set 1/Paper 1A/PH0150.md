# PH0150

The average radius of Neptune's orbit around the Sun is approximately 30 times the orbital radius of Earth. Estimate the solar constant of Neptune.

The solar constant for Earth is $1360\,$W m$^{-2}$.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $1.5\,$W m$^{-2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $23\,$W m$^{-2}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $45\,$W m$^{-2}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $1360\,$W m$^{-2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Solar constant (S) is the intensity of sunlight at the orbit of the planet. For Earth, at a distance of 1 AU from the Sun, it is $1.36 \times 10^3\,$W m$^{-2}$. 

Intensity is inversely proportional to the square of the distance from the source: $I\propto \dfrac{1}{d^2}$.

- $\therefore$ Intensity at a distance 30 times as far will be $30^2\,(= 900)$ times weaker. 

- $S_{Neptune}=\dfrac{1360}{900}=1.5\,$W m$^{-2}$.
