# PH0718

![PH0718](images/PH0718.png)

A main sequence star with a mass of three times the Sun will evolve through a series of stages. Which of the following sequences of stages could correctly represent the path of the evolution of this star?

- A. $\hspace{1em}     A\rightarrow B\rightarrow D\rightarrow E\rightarrow G$

- B. $\hspace{1em}      C\rightarrow B\rightarrow D\rightarrow E\rightarrow G$

- C. $\hspace{1em}      A\rightarrow B\rightarrow D\rightarrow E\rightarrow F$

- D. $\hspace{1em}      C\rightarrow B\rightarrow D\rightarrow E\rightarrow F$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Since the mass of the star is greater than the Sun, its luminosity should be higher. Therefore, its position on the main sequence should be A instead of C, where C represents the Sun. Moreover, the last stage of the evolution should be white dwarf which is labelled as G.
