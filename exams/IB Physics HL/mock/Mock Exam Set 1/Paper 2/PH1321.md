# PH1321

**Maximum mark: 20**

Radiation has a number of therapeutic and medical uses.

An infrared radiation lamp is used to relieve muscle pain. The lamp has an infrared emitter made of a tungsten filament operated with a 240 V source.

The graph shows the variation of current with voltage of a tungsten filament.

1.  1. Discuss whether the filament exhibits ohmic behaviour [2 marks]

    2. Show that the resistance of the filament is approximately $320\,\Omega$ when operating at 240 V. [1 mark]

    3. Calculate the electric power of the filament when operating at 240 V. [2 marks]

The filament can be modeled as a blackbody radiator with emissivity 0.40 and surface area 61 mm$^2$.

2. 1. Assuming that all power output from the filament is in the form of radiation, show that the wavelength of the radiation with maximum intensity is approximately 860 nm. [3 marks]

    2. Determine whether this tungsten filament can be used as an infrared emitter. [1 mark]

3.  The emitter is covered with 24 g of glass. This glass absorbs 13\% of the energy emitted. When first switched on, the temperature of the glass is raised by $5\degree$C in 4 seconds. Determine the specific heat capacity of the glass. [2 marks]

Alpha radiation is also used in medicine when a patient visits a hospital for a treatment session. The alpha emitter used is actinium-225 isotope $\ce{_89^225{Ac}}$.

The decay equation of actinium into francium is shown

$\ce{_89^225{Ac}} \rightarrow \ce{_87^221{Fr}}+\ce{_2^4{\alpha}}$

The following data are given

Mass of actinium nuclide = $225.02323\,u$\
Binding energy of francium = 1657.9 MeV\
Binding energy per nucleon of alpha particle = 7.075 MeV

4. 1. Show that the binding energy of actinium is approximately 1680 MeV. [2 marks]

    2. Calculate the energy released in this alpha decay. [3 marks]

Positrons are used in medical scanning. The patient is injected with a radioactive isotope and the radiation emitted from the patient is detected and analyzed.

The following is an incomplete decay equation for the positron emission.

$\ce{_9^18{F}} \rightarrow \ce{_\square^18{O}}+\ce{_\square ^0{e}}^\square + \ce{_\square^\square {\square}}$

5. 1. Complete the decay equation. [3 marks]

    2. Explain why positrons are less harmful than alpha particles. [1 mark]

3. ::indent
    - $Q\ll=(0.13)(182.3)(4)\gg=94.8\,\ll \ce{J}\gg$ $\checkmark$

    - $c\ll=\dfrac{94.8}{(24\times10^{-3})(5)}\gg=790\,\ll \dfrac{\ce{J}}{\ce{kg}\,\ce{K}}\gg$ $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
    The energy absorbed by the glass is responsible for the rise in temperature

    - $Q=mc\Delta T$

    Since glass absorbs 13\% of the energy emitted.

    - $Q_a=0.13Q$

    Since

    - $E=P\,t=(182.3)(4)=729.2$ J

    Then

    - $Q_a=(0.13)(729.2)=94.8$ J

    The specific heat capacity of a unit mass of glass

    - $c=\dfrac{Q_a}{m\Delta T}$

    - $c=\dfrac{94.8}{(24\times10^{-3})(5)}=790\,\dfrac{\ce{J}}{\ce{kg}\,\ce{K}}$

4. 1. ::indent
        - Mass defect of Ac = $(89)(1.007276)+(225-89)(1.008665)-225.02323$ $\checkmark$

        - $\ll$ Mass defect of Ac = $1.802774\,u\gg$

        - Mass defect of Ac = (1.802774)(931.5) $\checkmark$

        - $\ll$ Mass defect of Ac = $1679.3\,\ce{MeV}\,c^{-2}\gg$

        - $\ll E_{Ac}=(1679.3)c^2=1679.3\,\ce{MeV}\gg$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        The mass defect is given by

        - mass defect $(\Delta m)$ = $Zm_p+(A-Z)m_n-m_{atom}$

        Mass defect of actinium

        - $\Delta m_{Ac}=(89)(1.007276)+(225-89)(1.008665)-225.02323=1.802774\,u$

        - $\Delta m_{Ac}=(1.802774)(931.5)=1679.3\,\ce{MeV}\,c^{-2}$

        The binding energy is given by

        - $E=(\Delta m)c^2$

        Binding energy of actinium

        - $E_{Ac}=(1679.3)(c^2)=1679.3$ MeV

    2. ::indent
        - $E\ll=(7.075)(4)\gg=28.3\,\ll \ce{MeV}\gg$ $\checkmark$

        - Energy released = $1679.3-(1657.9+28.3)$  $\checkmark$

        - $E=6.9\,\ll \ce{MeV}\gg$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        Binding energy of alpha

        - $E=(7.075)(4)=28.3$ MeV

        The energy released in this alpha decay equals negative the difference in binding energies.

        - $E=-(E_{Ac}-(E_{Fr}+E_{\alpha}))=-(1679.3-(1657.9+28.3))=6.9$ MeV
