# CH2256

Consider the structure of 3$-$cyano$-$4$-$methylbenzoic acid:

![CH2256](images/CH2256.png)

Which of the following correctly identifies the hybridization of the carbon atoms labeled 1, 2, and 3?

$\ $

|$\hspace{0.95em}$||$\hspace{0.1em}$|1|2|3
:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:
||A.||$\quad sp^2 \quad$|$\quad sp^3 \quad$|$\quad sp^2 \quad$
||B.||$sp^3$|$sp$|$sp^2$
||C.||$sp^2$|$sp$|$sp^3$
||D.||$sp^3$|$sp^2$|$sp^3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Hybridization is determined by the number of electron domains (regions of electron density) around each atom.

Carbon 1 is part of a methyl group $(\ce{-CH3})$ with four $\sigma$ bonds (three to H, one to the aromatic ring). This gives four electron domains around the carbon, resulting in tetrahedral geometry with bond angles of approximately $109\degree,$  giving $sp^3$ hybridization.

Carbon 2 is the central atom in a cyano group $(\ce{-C#N}),$ with one triple bond and one single bond (two regions of electron density). This creates two electron domains around the carbon, leading to linear geometry with a bond angle of $180\degree,$  giving $sp$ hybridization.

Carbon 3  is in the carboxylic acid group $(\ce{-COOH}),$ with three regions of electron density (a double bond to O, a single bond to the OH group, and one to the ring). With three electron domains, this carbon adopts trigonal planar geometry with bond angles of approximately $120\degree,$  giving $sp^2$ hybridization.
