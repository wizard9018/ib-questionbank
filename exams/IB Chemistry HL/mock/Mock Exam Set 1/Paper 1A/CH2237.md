# CH2237

What type of bond fission is shown in the reaction below?

![CH2237](images/CH2237.png)

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$homolytic

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$nucleophilic

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$electrophilic

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$heterolytic

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The $\ce{C-Br}$ bond undergoes heterolytic fission. In this process, both electrons from the covalent bond transfer to bromine, forming Br⁻ and leaving behind a positively charged carbon center (carbocation). This uneven electron distribution produces two ions.

Homolytic fission would split the electrons equally between both atoms, forming free radicals rather than ions.

Nucleophilic and electrophilic describe chemical species or attack mechanisms, not bond$-$breaking processes.
