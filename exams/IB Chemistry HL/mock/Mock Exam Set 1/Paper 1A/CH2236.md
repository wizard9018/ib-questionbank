## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Halogenoalkanes undergo nucleophilic substitution via two main mechanisms: $\ce{S_N1}$ mechanism are favored by tertiary halogenoalkanes, which form stable carbocations. This is a two$-$step process proceeding through a carbocation intermediate. $\ce{S_N2}$ mechanism are favored by primary halogenoalkanes due to reduced steric hindrance. This is a one$-$step process proceeding through a transition state.

To identify the correct combination, consider the structure of each halogenoalkane. Also consider the inorganic molecules which must have lone pairs to act as nucleophiles, $\ce{NH4+}$ is not a nucleophile.

The correct reaction has the tertiary 2$-$bromo$-$2$-$methylbutane undergoing an $\ce{SN1}$ reaction with ammonia to form 2,2$-$dimethylbutanamine.
