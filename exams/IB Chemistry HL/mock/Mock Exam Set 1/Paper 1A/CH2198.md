# CH2198

A student completely combusts $10.0\text{ cm}^3$ of a gaseous hydrocarbon in excess oxygen. The total volume of gaseous products is collected and measured at constant temperature and pressure. The volume is $40.0\text{ cm}^3.$ This mixture is then passed over solid potassium carbonate, which removes the water vapour. The volume of the remaining gas is $20.0\text{ cm}^3.$
What is the molecular formula of the hydrocarbon?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH2}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{C2H2}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{C2H4}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

At constant temperature and pressure, gas volumes are directly proportional to mole ratios. The combustion of a hydrocarbon produces $\ce{CO2}$ and $\ce{H2O},$ both as gases. The total volume of gas produced is $40.0\text{ cm}^3,$ but after removal of water vapour with potassium carbonate, only $20.0\text{ cm}^3$ of gas remains. Therefore, the water vapour must have had a volume of:

$40.0\text{ cm}^3\text{ products} - 20.0\text{ cm}^3\ce{\ CO2} = 20.0\text{ cm}^3\ce{\ H2O}$

From volumes:

$10.0\text{ cm}^3$ of hydrocarbon produced $20.0\text{ cm}^3\ce{\ CO2}\longrightarrow 2\text{ mol}\ce{\ CO2} : 1\text{ mol }\ce{C_xH_y}$

$10.0\text{ cm}^3$ of hydrocarbon produced $20.0\text{ cm}^3\ce{\ H2O}\longrightarrow 2\text{ mol }\ce{H2O} : 1\text{ mol }\ce{C_xH_y}$

Complete combustion of a hydrocarbon $\ce{C_xH_y}$ is described by the balanced chemical equation:

$\ce{C_xH_y + z O2 -> x CO2 + \frac{y}{2} H2O}$

Using the molar ratios that were found experimentally,

$\ce{1 C_xH_y + z O2 -> 2 CO2 + 2 H2O}$

the unknown values can be determined: $x = 2$ and $y = 4.$  The molecular formula is $\ce{C2H4}.$
