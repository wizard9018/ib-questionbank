# CH2231

A $10.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3$ sample of $0.0100 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ sodium hydroxide  is diluted to a total volume of $1000.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3$ with distilled water. What is the pH of the resulting solution?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}4$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}10$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}12$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}14$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
To calculate the pH, we must first determine the new concentration of hydroxide ions after dilution. Since sodium hydroxide is a strong base, it dissociates fully in solution:

$\ce{NaOH}\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow \ce{Na+}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{OH-}\textrm{(aq)}$

We use the dilution formula:

$C_1V_1=C_2V_2$

where:

-   *   $C_1=0.0100 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$
-   *   $V_1=10.0  \textrm{\ cm}^3$
-   *   $V_2=1000.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3$
-   *   $C_2 =$ final concentration of $\ce{OH-}$

Substituting:

$0.0100 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \times  10.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3 = C_2 \times 1000.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3$

$C_2=1.00 \times 10^{-4} \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$

So, the concentration of hydroxide ions is:

$[\ce{OH-}]=1.00 \times 10^{-4} \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$

Now we calculate pOH:

$\textrm{pOH}=-\log_{10}(1.00\times10^{-4})=4.00$

Finally, use the relationship:

$\textrm{pH}=14-\textrm{pOH}=14-4.00=10.00$
