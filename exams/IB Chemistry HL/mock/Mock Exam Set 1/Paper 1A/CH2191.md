# CH2191

A sample of 1$\text{--}$propanol is heated under reflux with acidified potassium dichromate $(\ce{H+}/\ce{K2Cr2O7}).$ The reaction mixture is monitored over time using infrared (IR) spectroscopy.

Which of the following correctly shows the characteristic IR absorptions observed at the initial, intermediate, and final stages of the reaction?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | Initial | Intermediate | Final |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | A.$\hspace{1em}$ |$1050\text{ cm}^{-1} \\ 3350\text{ cm}^{-1}$ broad | $1710\text{ cm}^{-1}$ | $1150\text{ cm}^{-1} \\ 1710\text{ cm}^{-1} \\ 2950\text{ cm}^{-1}$ v. broad |
    | B. | $1710\text{ cm}^{-1}$ | $1150\text{ cm}^{-1} \\ 1710\text{ cm}^{-1} \\ 2950\text{ cm}^{-1}$ v. broad | $1050\text{ cm}^{-1} \\ 3350\text{ cm}^{-1}$ broad |
    | C. | $1050\text{ cm}^{-1} \\ 3350\text{ cm}^{-1}$ broad | $1150\text{ cm}^{-1} \\ 1710\text{ cm}^{-1} \\ 2950\text{ cm}^{-1}$ v. broad | $1710\text{ cm}^{-1}$ |
    | D. | $1150\text{ cm}^{-1} \\ 1710\text{ cm}^{-1} \\ 2950\text{ cm}^{-1}$ v. broad | $1050\text{ cm}^{-1} \\ 3350\text{ cm}^{-1}$ broad | $1710\text{ cm}^{-1}$ |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

Under reflux a primary alcohol is oxidized first to an aldehyde and then to a carboxylic acid.  Using Section 20 of the IB Chemistry data booklet, the IR absorbances of each step can be predicted.

![CH2191](images/CH2191.png)

At the beginning of the reaction, the IR spectrum shows a broad peak around $3350\text{ cm}^{-1}$ for the alcohol $\ce{O-H}$ stretch and a peak at $1050\text{ cm}^{-1}$ for the $\ce{C-O}$ bond. As the alcohol is oxidized to an aldehyde and then to a carboxylic acid, a strong $\ce{C=O}$ stretch appears at $1710\text{ cm}^{-1},$ and the $\ce{O-H}$ signal broadens and shifts to around $2950\text{ cm}^{-1},$ characteristic of acids. The change in absorption bands reflects the disappearance of the alcohol and the formation of oxidized products.
