Each diagram shows a Maxwell$\text{--}$Boltzmann distribution of molecular energies. The dotted line is for the original temperature; the solid line is after the temperature increases. Which diagram best represents the effect of temperature on the distribution of molecular energies?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

When the temperature increases, the average kinetic energy of the particles increases. The Maxwell$\text{--}$Boltzmann distribution responds to this in two characteristic ways: the peak of the curve shifts to higher energy values, and the curve becomes broader and lower.

The dotted line in each diagram shows the original temperature, and the solid line should reflect the distribution after heating. In the correct diagram, the solid line must begin at the origin, like the original, but have its peak shifted to the right and its maximum lowered, with a longer tail extending toward higher energies. This shape change ensures the area under the curve remains constant (because the total number of molecules is unchanged), while the distribution spreads out.
