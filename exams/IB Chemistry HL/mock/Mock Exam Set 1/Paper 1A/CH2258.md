# CH2258

The standard enthalpy of formation of xenon difluoride, $\ce{XeF2}\textrm{(g)},$ is:

$\ce{Xe}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{F2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow \ce{XeF2}\textrm{(g)} \hspace{3em} \Delta H^{\minuso}_f = -105 \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}$

Using Section 12 of the IB Chemistry data booklet, what is the average bond enthalpy of the $\ce{Xe-F}$ bond in $\ce{XeF2}?$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}132 \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}158 \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}184 \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}210 \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The overall enthalpy of a gas phase reaction can be estimated using average bond dissociation enthalpies according to the formula

$\Delta H^{\minuso}_{rxn} = \sum \textrm{bonds broken} - \sum \textrm{bonds formed}$

The overall reaction breaks one $\ce{F-F}$ bond and forms two $\ce{Xe-F}$ bonds, so:

$\Delta H^{\minuso}_{rxn} = D(\ce{F-F}) - 2D(\ce{Xe-F})$

Rearranging to isolate the average $\ce{Xe-F}$ bond enthalpy:

$D(\ce{Xe-F}) = \frac{1}{2}[D(\ce{F-F}) - \Delta H_{rxn}] = \frac{1}{2} [159 - (-105)] = \ $ :answer[$132\textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}$]
