# CH2196

The carbon footprint of a fuel is the mass of $\ce{CO2}$ (in kg) released per megajoule (MJ) of energy produced during its complete combustion.  The enthalpy of combustion of octane, $\ce{C8H18}$ $(M_r = 114),$ is $-5470\text{ kJ mol}^{-1}.$  Which expression is correct for the carbon footprint of this fuel?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{44\times 1000}{5470\times 114\times 1000}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{8\times 44\times 1000}{5470\times 1000}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{5470\times 44}{1000}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{5470\times 44}{8\times114 \times 1000}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

To calculate the carbon footprint, determine the mass of $\ce{CO2}$ released per MJ of energy. First, consider the combustion of 1 mole of octane:

$\ce{C8H18(l) + \dfrac{25}{2} O2(g) -> 8 CO2(g) + 9 H2O(l)}$

One mole of octane produces 8 moles of $\ce{CO2}$ and releases $5470\text{ kJ}$ of energy. To find $\text{kg }\ce{CO2}/\text{ MJ},$ convert the the moles of $\ce{CO2}$ to kg:

$m_\ce{CO2} = (8\text{ mol }\ce{CO2})\left(\dfrac{44\text{ g }\ce{CO2}}{1\text{ mol }\ce{CO2}}\right)\left(\dfrac{1\text{ kg }\ce{CO2}}{1000\text{ g }\ce{CO2}}\right)$

and convert the energy to MJ:

$E = (5470\text{ kJ})\left(\dfrac{1\text{ MJ}}{1000\text{ kJ}}\right)$

This ratio gives the carbon footprint:

carbon footprint = :answer[$\dfrac{8 \times 44 \times 1000}{5470 \times 1000}$]
