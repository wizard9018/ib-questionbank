# CH2240

The diagram shows three gaseous samples at the particle level:

![CH2240](images/CH2240.png)

Which of the following statements are correct?

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$Sample 1 can be separated by physical means.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$Samples 2 and 3 contain only elements.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$Sample 2 is a homogeneous mixture.

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$I and II only

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$I and III only

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$II and III only

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
This question assesses particle$-$level understanding of elements, compounds, and mixtures.

Sample 1 contains white monatomic atoms (one element) and black$-$white diatomic molecules (a compound), making it a mixture that can be separated by physical means such as fractional distillation or diffusion. Statement I is correct.

Sample 2 shows black monatomic atoms and white monatomic atoms, representing a mixture of two different elements. Sample 3 displays only black atoms in two allotropic forms (monatomic atoms and triatomic molecules) representing different molecular arrangements of a single element. Statement II is correct.

Sample 2 is a homogeneous mixture because gas mixtures are always homogeneous due to complete molecular mixing and uniform distribution throughout the container. Statement III is correct.
