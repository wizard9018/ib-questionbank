# CH2279

A liquid has a freezing point of $91\ \mathrm{K}$.
What is this temperature in $^\circ\mathrm{C}$?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}-182\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}-91\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}182\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}364\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:  \
Subtract $273$ to the temperature in Kelvin to convert it to Celsius.

$91\, \mathrm{K} - 273 = -182\ ^{\circ}\textrm{C}$
