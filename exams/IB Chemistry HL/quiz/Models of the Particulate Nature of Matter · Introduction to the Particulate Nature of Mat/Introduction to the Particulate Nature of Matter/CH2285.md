# CH2285

[Maximum marks: 11]

The melting point of toluene is $-95.0\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and its boiling point is $110.6\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$.

1. Draw the heating curve for toluene from $-120\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ to $160\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}.$ Label each phase change. [3 marks]

2. State the physical state of toluene at $25.0\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}.$ [1 mark]

3. Compare the arrangement and spacing of molecules in toluene at $-120\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}$ and at $25\,{}^\circ\mathrm{C}.$ [2 marks]

4. When $5.00\ \mathrm{g}$ of $\ce{I2}$ is added to $100\ \mathrm{cm^3}$ of toluene, the mixture becomes purple.

 1. Identify the type of mixture formed. [1 mark]

 2. Suggest one laboratory technique to separate iodine from toluene and recover both substances, and sketch a labeled diagram. [4 marks]

2. liquid $\checkmark$

 $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
 $25.0\,{}^\circ\text{C}$ is above the melting point ($-95.0\,{}^\circ\text{C}$) and below the boiling point ($110.6,{}^\circ\text{C}$); therefore toluene is in the liquid state.

3. $-120\ {}^\circ\text{C}$ : molecules arranged in a fixed lattice, vibrate about fixed positions $\checkmark$\
 $25.0\ {}^\circ\text{C}$: molecules close$-$packed but not in a regular pattern, can slide past each other / flow $\checkmark$

 $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
 As temperature increases past the melting point, the ordered structure of the solid is disrupted. Intermolecular forces remain significant, so the liquid molecules stay close together but move freely.

4. 1. homogeneous $\checkmark$

 $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
 Iodine dissolves in toluene, forming a single liquid phase with a uniform purple colour. This indicates molecular iodine is soluble in the nonpolar solvent.

 $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
 Toluene is more volatile (boiling point $110.6\,{}^\circ\text{C})$ than iodine (sublimes at $184\,{}^\circ\text{C}).$ Upon heating, toluene vaporizes first and is condensed and collected, leaving solid iodine behind. This allows recovery of both pure substances.
