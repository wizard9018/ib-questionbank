# AA1359

**Maximum mark: 5**

The function $f$ is known to be an even function with domain $-8 \leq x \leq 8$.

Part of the graph of $f$ is shown below.

![AA1359q1](images/AA1359q1.JPEG)

1.    On the following axes, sketch $y=|f(x)|$ for $-8 \leq x \leq 8$. [2 marks]

![AA1359q2](images/AA1359q2.JPEG)

It is given that 

$$

\begin{aligned}

    \int_{0}^{8} |f(x)| \, \mathrm{d}x - \int_{-8}^{0} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x&= 4\\[16pt]

    \int_{0}^{8} |f(x)| \, \mathrm{d}x + \int_{-8}^{0} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x&= 28

\end{aligned}

$$

2.    Hence find

    1.    $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{8} |f(x)| \, \mathrm{d}x$;

    2.    $\displaystyle \int_{-8}^{8} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x$ [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.    We are told that $f$ is an even function which means that it has reflective symmetry about the $y$-axis.

    Additionally, we are sketching the absolute value of $f$ which means any parts where $f(x)$ is negative are reflected in the $x$-axis.

    Hence we get

    ![AA1359-a](images/AA1359-a.JPEG)

    $\text{Correct reflection in the }y\text{-axis.}$
    $\\[-2.2em]$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{\textcolor{black}{A1}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $\text{Correct reflection in the }x\text{-axis.}$
    $\\[-2.2em]$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{\textcolor{black}{A1}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.    Notice the similarity between the two given integral expressions.

        If we let $A = \displaystyle\int_{0}^{8} |f(x)|\,\mathrm{d}x$ and $B = \displaystyle\int_{-8}^{0} f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x$, then we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
                    A - B &= 4 \hspace{3.5em} \text{[1]}\\[6pt]
                    A + B &= 28 \hspace{3em}\text{[2]}
                \end{aligned}
        $$
        By adding equations [1] and [2] we can solve for A.
        $\\[-2.2em]$
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{\textcolor{black}{(M1)}}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
                    2A &= 32\\
                    A &= 16
                \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{8} |f(x)|\,\mathrm{d}x = 16
        $.
        $\\[-3em]$
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{\textcolor{black}{A1}}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
    2.    By subtracting equation [1] from equation [2] we can solve for B.
    $$
        \begin{aligned}
            -2B &= -24\\
            B &= 12
        \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{8} f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x = 12$.

    Recall that $f$ is even, hence we know $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{8} f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x = \int_{-8}^{0} f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x$.

    Therefore
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
                \int_{-8}^{8} f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x &= \int_{-8}^{0} f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x + \int_{0}^{8} f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x\\[15pt]
                &= 12 + 12\\[6pt]
                \int_{-8}^{8} f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x&=24\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
            \end{aligned}
    $$
