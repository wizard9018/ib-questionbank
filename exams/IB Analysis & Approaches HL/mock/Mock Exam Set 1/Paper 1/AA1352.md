# AA1352

**Maximum mark: 7**

The function $f$ is defined by $f(x)=4x^2-8x+1$, where $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.    Write $f$ in the form $a(x-h)^2+k$ where $a,h,k \in \mathbb{Z}$. [3 marks]

Function $g$ is defined by $g(x)=\sqrt{\dfrac{x}{2}}-1$, where $x \in \mathbb{R^+}$.

2.  1.    Find an expression for $g^{-1}(x)$.

    2.    Describe a sequence of transformations that transforms the graph of $y=g^{-1}(x)$ to the graph $f$. [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. We need to complete the square for $f(x)=4x^2-8x+1$.

    First, we should factor out the coefficient of $x^2$ from the first two terms:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    f(x) &= 4(x^2 - 2x) + 1 \tag*{\textbf{M1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Then, to complete the square inside the bracket, take half of the coefficient of $x$ and square it. $\left(\frac{-2}{2}\right)^2 = 1$.

    

    Then we add and subtract this value inside the bracket.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    f(x) &= 4(x^2 - 2x \textcolor{RVGreen}{\,\,+\,\, 1\, -\, 1}) + 1\\[6pt]

    &= 4((x-1)^2 - 1) + 1 \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}\\[6pt]

    &= 4(x-1)^2 - 4 + 1\\[6pt]

    &= 4(x-1)^2 - 3

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore $f(x) = 4(x-1)^2 - 3$.

    $\\[-2.8em]$

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{\textcolor{black}{A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2.  1. To find $y=g^{-1}(x)$, let $x = g(y)$ and rearrange to make $y$ the subject.

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        x &= \sqrt{\frac{y}{2}} - 1 \tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[12pt]

        x + 1 &= \sqrt{\frac{y}{2}}\\[12pt]

        (x+1)^2 &= \frac{y}{2}\\[9pt]

        y &= 2(x+1)^2

        \end{aligned}

        $$

        Hence $g^{-1}(x) = 2(x+1)^2\hspace{0.525em}$

        $\\[-2.8em]$

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \tag*{\textbf{\textcolor{black}{A1}}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

    2. We need to transform $g^{-1}(x) = 2(x+1)^2$ into $f(x) = 4(x-1)^2 - 3$.

        Starting from $y = 2(x+1)^2$

        Vertical stretch by scale factor 2 (parallel to $y$-axis).

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        y = 2 \times 2(x+1)^2 = 4(x+1)^2\tag*{\textbf{A1}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

        $$\text{Translation by $2$ units right and $3$ units down, }\begin{pmatrix}2\\-3\end{pmatrix}.$$

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        y &= 4(x+1-2)^2 - 3\tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\[6pt]

        y&=4(x-1)^2-3

        \end{aligned}

        $$
