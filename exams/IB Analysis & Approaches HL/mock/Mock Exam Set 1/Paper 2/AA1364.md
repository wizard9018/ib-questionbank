# AA1364

**Maximum mark: 6**

A cycling club survey is conducted on riders taking part in a long-distance event.

For each rider, the distance they rode last month, $d$ kilometres, and the number of training sessions they completed last month, $t$, are recorded.

The numbers of training sessions completed are summarised in the following box-and-whisker diagram where $L$ and $U$ are the lower and upper quartiles respectively.

![AA1364-q](images/AA1364-q.JPEG)

The interquartile range is $4$ sessions and there are no outliers in the results.

1.    Find the maximum possible value of $L$. [3 marks]

The survey data is used to obtain the following regression lines.

The line of $d$ on $t$ is found to be $d = 16t + 25$ and line of $t$ on $d$ is found to be $t = \dfrac{1}{14}d - 1.5.$

2.    One of the members cycled $135$ kilometres in last month. Estimate, to the nearest integer, how many training sessions they completed last month. [2 marks]

3.    A new member joined the club and conducted $22$ training sessions last month. Explain why it would be inappropriate to use one of the regression lines above to obtain an estimate of the new members distance travelled. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. We are told that there are no outliers, so let's consider the formulae for outliers.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Lower outliers}&<Q_1-1.5\times IQR\\[6pt]
    \text{Upper outliers}&>Q_3+1.5\times IQR
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    As we want $L$ we will use the formula for the lower outliers.
    We know the minimum is $2$ and there are no outliers so we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2&\geq L-1.5(4)\tag*{\textbf{(M1)A1}}\\[6pt]
    2&\geq L-6\\[6pt]
    8&\geq L\\[6pt]
    L&\leq 8
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore the maximum value of $L$ is $8$.
    $\\[-2.5em]$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. As we are estimating training sessions from a known distance, we need to use the regression line of $t$ on $d$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    t &= \frac{1}{14}d - 1.5\\[12pt]
    &= \frac{1}{14}(135) - 1.5 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[12pt]
    &= 8.14\ldots\\[6pt]
    &=8\text{ sessions} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The range of the observed data for the training sessions is from $2$ to $15$.

    The value $t = 22$ is $\text{outside of this observed range (extrapolation)}$.
    $\\[-2.5em]$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore it would be inappropriate to use either regression line.
