# AA1366

**Maximum mark: 6**

Events $A$ and $B$ are independent and $\mathrm{P}(A \cap B)=0.15$.

Given that $\mathrm{P}(A)=\mathrm{P}(B)-0.2$, find $\mathrm{P}(A).$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We can use the formula for $\textbf{independent events (4.6)}$ and substitute in $\mathrm{P}(A \cap B)=0.15$,  giving

$$

\begin{aligned}

\hspace{2em} \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) &= \mathrm{P}(A) \, \mathrm{P}(B) \\[6pt]

0.15 &= \mathrm{P}(A) \,\mathrm{P}(B) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}

\end{aligned}

$$

Next, substitute the relationship $\mathrm{P}(A) = \mathrm{P}(B) - 0.2$ into the formula above. 

This will form a quadratic equation in $\mathrm{P}(B)$.

$$

\begin{aligned}

0.15 &= (\mathrm{P}(B) - 0.2)\,\mathrm{P}(B) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

\mathrm{P}(B)^2 - 0.2\mathrm{P}(B) - 0.15 &= 0 

\end{aligned}

$$

Using the G.D.C. we can solve the quadratic equation for $\mathrm{P}(B)$.

$$

\begin{aligned}

\mathrm{P}(B) = 0.5 \quad &\text{or} \quad \mathrm{P}(B) = -0.3 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

\end{aligned}

$$

We know that $\mathrm{P}(B)>0$, hence we reject the negative value. Therefore

$$

\begin{aligned}

\mathrm{P}(B) &= 0.5 \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

\end{aligned}

$$

We are asked to find $\mathrm{P}(A)$, hence we get

$$

\begin{aligned}

\mathrm{P}(A) &= \mathrm{P}(B) - 0.2\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

 &= 0.5 - 0.2 \\[6pt]

 \mathrm{P}(A)&=0.3\tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\[-12pt]

\end{aligned}

$$
