# AA1373

**Maximum mark: 7**

Consider the complex numbers $w_1$ and $w_2$ where $k \in \mathbb{R}^+$.
$$
\begin{aligned}
w_1 &= 2.3e^{\mathrm{i}(3k + 2)}, \\[6pt]
w_2 &= 4.6e^{\mathrm{i}(3k - 1)}
\end{aligned}
$$
A third complex number is defined as $z=w_1-w_2$.

1.    Show that $z = 2.3e^{3k\mathrm{i}}\,(e^{2\mathrm{i}} - 2e^{-\mathrm{i}}).$ [1 mark]

2.    Write  $e^{2\mathrm{i}} - 2e^{-\mathrm{i}}$ in the form $re^{\mathrm{i}\theta}$, where $r > 0$ and $-\pi < \theta \le \pi$. [2 marks]

3.    Hence or otherwise, find the smallest value of $k$ such that $\mathrm{Re}(z)=0$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Let the complex number $z$ be defined by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    z &= 2.3e^{\mathrm{i}(3k+2)} - 4.6e^{\mathrm{i}(3k-1)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using exponent laws and factorising we get,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    z &= 2.3e^{3k\mathrm{i}}e^{2\mathrm{i}} - 4.6e^{3k\mathrm{i}}e^{-\mathrm{i}}\tag*{\textbf{A1}} \\[6pt]
    &= 2.3\left(e^{3k\mathrm{i}}(e^{2\mathrm{i}} - 2e^{-\mathrm{i}})\right)\hspace{0.5em}\text{... as required}
    \tag*{\textbf{AG}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. We can use a calculator to convert the number into the desired form.

    Using a G.D.C. and simplifying we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &= 2.993\ldots e^{2.094\ldots \mathrm{i}} \\[6pt]
    &= 2.99e^{2.09\mathrm{i}}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3. From part (b),
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    e^{2\mathrm{i}} - 2e^{-\mathrm{i}}
    &= 2.993\ldots e^{2.094\ldots \mathrm{i}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Substituting into the expression for $z$ we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    z &= 2.3e^{3k\mathrm{i}}\left(2.993\ldots e^{2.094\ldots \mathrm{i}}\right)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Simplifying
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &= 6.884\ldots e^{(3k+2.094\ldots)\mathrm{i}}
    \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Taking the real part
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{Re}(z)
    &= 6.884\ldots \cos(3k+2.094\ldots)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We can use $\mathrm{Re}(z)=0$ and form the equation
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos(3k+2.094\ldots) &= 0
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Solving for $k$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    3k+2.094\ldots &= \frac{\pi}{2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and using a calculator, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    k &= -0.1745\ldots\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Since $k \in \mathbb{R}^+$, we take the next larger solution for $\cos{(3k+2.094\ldots)}=0$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    3k+2.094\ldots &= \frac{3\pi}{2}
    \tag*{\textbf{R1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    k &= 0.8726\ldots
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    k &= 0.873\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
