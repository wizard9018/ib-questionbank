# CH2577

A tank of volume $70.0\textrm{ dm}^3$ contains $3.00\textrm{ mol}$ of argon at a fixed temperature and pressure. What amount, in mol, of argon would occupy a tank of volume $50.0\textrm{ dm}^3$ at the same temperature and pressure?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}2.14$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}4.20$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}0.467$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}1.40$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
At constant temperature and pressure, volume is directly proportional to the amount of gas (Avogadro's law):

$\dfrac{V_1}{n_1} = \dfrac{V_2}{n_2}$

Rearranging for $n_2$:

$n_2 = n_1 \times \dfrac{V_2}{V_1} = 3.00\textrm{ mol} \times \dfrac{50.0\textrm{ dm}^3}{70.0\textrm{ dm}^3} = 2.14\textrm{ mol}$

The amount of gas scales in direct proportion to the volume when temperature and pressure are held constant.
