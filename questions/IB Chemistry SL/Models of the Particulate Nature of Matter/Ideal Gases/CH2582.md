# CH2582

A balloon contains helium at $268\textrm{ K}$ and $998\textrm{ kPa}.$ The volume of the balloon is $2535\textrm{ cm}^3.$ Which expression gives the amount of helium, in mol?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}\dfrac{998 \times 2535}{1000 \times 8.31 \times 268}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}\dfrac{998 \times 2535}{8.31 \times 268}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}\dfrac{8.31 \times 268}{998 \times 2535}$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}\dfrac{1000 \times 8.31 \times 268}{998 \times 2535}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The ideal gas law is $pV = nRT.$ Rearranging for amount:

$n = \dfrac{pV}{RT}$

The gas constant $R = 8.31\textrm{ J K}^{-1}\textrm{ mol}^{-1}$ requires pressure in kPa and volume in $\textrm{dm}^3.$ Converting the given values:

$V = 2535\textrm{ cm}^3 \times \dfrac{1 \textrm{\ dm}^3}{1000 \textrm{ cm}^3}$

Substituting:

$n = \dfrac{998 \textrm{\ kPa} \times 2535 \textrm{\ cm}^3}{1000  \textrm{\ cm}^3 \textrm{\ dm}^{-3} \times 8.31 \textrm{\ kPa dm}^3 \textrm{mol}^{-1} \textrm{K}^{-1} \times 268 \textrm{\ K}} = \dfrac{998 \times 2535}{1000 \times 8.31 \times 268}\textrm{ mol}$
