# CH2584

A balloon has a volume of $3.70\textrm{ dm}^3$ at $25.0\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}.$ The volume is reduced by $20\%$ at constant pressure. What is the magnitude of the temperature change, in K?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}238.4$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}74.5$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}59.6$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}372.5$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
At constant pressure with a fixed amount of gas, volume is directly proportional to absolute temperature (Charles's law):

$\dfrac{V_i}{T_i} = \dfrac{V_f}{T_f}$

The initial temperature is $25.0\,{}^\circ\textrm{C} = 298\textrm{ K}.$ If the volume is reduced by $20\%,$ the final volume is $80\%$ of the original:

$V_f = 0.80\,V_i$

Rearranging for $T_f$:

$T_f = \dfrac{V_f \times T_i}{V_i} = \dfrac{0.8\,V_i \times T_i}{V_i} = 0.8\,T_i$

The temperature change must be determined in kelvin:

$\Delta T = T_f - T_i = 0.8\,T_i - T_i = -0.2\,T_i$

$|\Delta T| = 0.2 \times 298\textrm{ K} = 59.6\textrm{ K}$

The temperature decreases because a smaller volume at constant pressure requires a lower temperature.
