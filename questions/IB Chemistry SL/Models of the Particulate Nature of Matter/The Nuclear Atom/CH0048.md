# CH0048

The nuclear symbol for an isotope of iodine is $\ce{^{125}_{53}I}$. Which of the following is correct about the isotope?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

| $\hspace{1.1em}$  | $\hspace{0.em}$ | | Atomic Number | Mass Number | Number of protons | Number of neutrons | Number of electrons |
| :-- |:-:| :-- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
|| A. || 53 | 125 | 53 | 72 | 53 |
|| B. || 53 | 125 | 72 | 53 | 53 |
|| C. || 125 | 53 | 53 | 72 | 72 |
|| D. || 125 | 53 | 53 | 53 | 72 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
In the nuclear symbol ${}^{A}_{Z}\ce{X},$ the superscript $A$ is the mass number (total number of protons and neutrons) and the subscript $Z$ is the atomic number (number of protons). For iodine$-$125, the atomic number is $53$ and the mass number is $125.$

The number of neutrons is the difference between the mass number and the atomic number: $125 - 53 = 72.$ In a neutral atom, the number of electrons equals the number of protons, so this isotope has $53$ electrons.
