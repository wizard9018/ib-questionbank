# CH2077

Which of the following requires a chemical reaction to be separated into simpler substances?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$Elements

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$Homogeneous mixtures

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$Heterogeneous mixtures

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$Compounds

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
A compound consists of atoms joined by chemical bonds in a fixed ratio. Separating it into simpler substances requires breaking those bonds, which is a chemical reaction. Water is decomposed into hydrogen and oxygen by electrolysis.

The components of a mixture are not chemically bonded. They retain their own properties and can be present in any proportion, so they can be separated using a physical difference between them: boiling point in distillation, particle size in filtration, attraction to a stationary phase in chromatography. Both homogeneous and heterogeneous mixtures are separated by physical methods, without a chemical reaction.

An element consists of one kind of atom and cannot be broken down into simpler substances by any chemical reaction.
