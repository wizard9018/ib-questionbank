# CH0258

What is the term for the region where there is the highest probability of finding an electron?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Orbital

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Nucleus

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Orbital diagram

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Orbit

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Choice B is incorrect because electrons are not located in the nucleus. Choice C is incorrect because an orbital diagram is just a representation of electrons in an orbital. Choice D is incorrect because electrons do not have a fixed orbital around the nucleus in the quantum mechanical model. Choice A is the correct answer: orbitals are areas/ regions in space where the probability of finding an electron is very high.
