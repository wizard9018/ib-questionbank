# CH2590

**Maximum mark: 6**

A compound contains carbon, hydrogen and nitrogen only. Its molar mass is $162.26\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}.$

1. The compound contains $74.02\%$ carbon and $8.714\%$ hydrogen by mass.

    (i) Determine the empirical formula, showing your working. [2 marks]

    (ii) Determine the molecular formula. [1 mark]

2. A $0.321\textrm{ g}$ sample of the compound is dissolved in water and the solution is made up to $100.0\textrm{ cm}^3$ in a volumetric flask.

    (i) Calculate the number of molecules in the sample. [2 marks]

    (ii) Determine the concentration, in $\textrm{mol dm}^{-3},$ of the solution. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1.  $n(\ce{C}) = 6.163 \textrm{\ mol}, n(\ce{H}) = 8.645 \textrm{\ mol}, n(\ce{N}) = 1.233 \textrm{\ mol}\: \checkmark$\
    $\ce{C5H7N}$ $\checkmark$

        $\textrm{\underline{Further Explanation:}}$\
    The percentage of nitrogen is found by difference:

        $100.00 - 74.02 - 8.714 = 17.27\% \textrm{\ N}$

        In a $100\textrm{ g}$ sample, the amount of each element is:

        $n(\ce{C}) = \dfrac{74.02\textrm{ g}}{12.01\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}} = 6.163\textrm{ mol}$

        $n(\ce{H}) = \dfrac{8.714\textrm{ g}}{1.01\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}} = 8.63\textrm{ mol}$

        $n(\ce{N}) = \dfrac{17.27\textrm{ g}}{14.01\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}} = 1.233\textrm{ mol}$

        Dividing each by the smallest value:

        $\ce{C} : \ce{H} : \ce{N} = \dfrac{6.163}{1.233} : \dfrac{8.63}{1.233} : \dfrac{1.233}{1.233} = 4.998 : 7.00 : 1.000$

        The empirical formula is $\ce{C5H7N}.$

    2.  $\ce{C10H14N2}$ $\checkmark$

        $\textrm{\underline{Further Explanation:}}$\
        The empirical formula mass is:

        $M_\textrm{empirical} = 5(12.01) + 7(1.008) + 14.01 = 81.12\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}$

        $\dfrac{162.26\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}}{81.12\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}} = 2.000$

        The molecular formula is $2 \times \ce{C5H7N} = \ce{C10H14N2}.$

2.  1.  $n(\ce{C10H14N2}) = 1.98 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{ mol}$ $\checkmark$\
    $N(\ce{C10H14N2}) = 1.19 \times 10^{21}\textrm{ molecules}$ $\checkmark$

        $\textrm{\underline{Further Explanation:}}$\
        The amount of the compound is found from its mass and molar mass. Multiplying by Avogadro's constant converts amount in mol to the number of individual molecules.

        $n = \dfrac{m}{M} = \dfrac{0.321\textrm{ g}}{162.26\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}} = 1.98 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{ mol}$

        $N = n \times N_\textrm{A} = 1.98 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{ mol} \times 6.02 \times 10^{23}\textrm{ mol}^{-1} = 1.19 \times 10^{21}$

    2.  $c = 0.0198\textrm{ mol dm}^{-3}$ $\checkmark$

        $\textrm{\underline{Further Explanation:}}$\
        The volume is $100.0\textrm{ cm}^3 = 0.1000\textrm{ dm}^3.$

        $c = \dfrac{n}{V} = \dfrac{1.98 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{ mol}}{0.1000\textrm{ dm}^3} = 0.0198\textrm{ mol dm}^{-3}$
