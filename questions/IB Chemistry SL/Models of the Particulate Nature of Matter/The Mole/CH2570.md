# CH2570

What is the amount, in mol, of $\ce{NaOH}$ in $25.0\textrm{ cm}^3$ of a solution prepared by dissolving $20.0\textrm{ g}$ of $\ce{NaOH}\textrm{(s)}$ to a final volume of $500\textrm{ cm}^3$?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}0.0250$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}0.250$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}0.500$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}1.00$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The total amount of $\ce{NaOH}$ dissolved is:

$n(\ce{NaOH}) = \dfrac{20.0\textrm{ g}}{40.00\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}} = 0.500\textrm{ mol}$

The concentration of the solution is:

$c = \dfrac{0.500\textrm{ mol}}{0.500\textrm{ dm}^3} = 1.00\textrm{ mol dm}^{-3}$

The amount in $25.0\textrm{ cm}^3$ is:

$n = 1.00\textrm{ mol dm}^{-3} \times 0.0250\textrm{ dm}^3 = 0.0250\textrm{ mol}$

Converting $\textrm{cm}^3$ to $\textrm{dm}^3$ requires dividing by 1000.
