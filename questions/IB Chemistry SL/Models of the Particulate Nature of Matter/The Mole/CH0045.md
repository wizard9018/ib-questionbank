# CH0045

A mass spectrometer was used to determine that the relative ratio by mass of N : O in a molecule containing nitrogen and oxygen is $7 : 16.$ What is the most likely molecule?

- A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{NO}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{NO2}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{NO3}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{N2O5}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Convert the mass ratio to a mole ratio by dividing each mass by the respective molar mass:

$n(\ce{N}) = \dfrac{7 \textrm{\ g}}{14.01 \textrm{\ g mol}^{-1}} \approx 0.5 \textrm{\ mol N}$

$n(\ce{O}) = \dfrac{16 \textrm{\ g}}{16.00 \textrm{\ g mol}^{-1}} \approx 1 \textrm{\ mol O}$

Divide by the smallest value to obtain the simplest whole number ratio for $\textrm{N}:\textrm{O},$

$ \dfrac{0.5}{0.5} : \dfrac{1}{0.5} = 1 : 2$

The empirical formula is $\ce{NO2}.$
