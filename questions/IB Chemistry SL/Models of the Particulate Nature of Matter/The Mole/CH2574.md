# CH2574

Which information is given by the empirical formula?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$The actual number of atoms in a compound.

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$The number of atoms in one mole of a compound.

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$The simplest ratio of atoms of each element in a compound.

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$The actual number of ions in a compound.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The empirical formula shows the simplest whole$-$number ratio of atoms of each element present in a compound. For example, glucose has the molecular formula $\ce{C6H12O6}$ but its empirical formula is $\ce{CH2O},$ a $1 : 2 : 1$ ratio. The molecular formula gives the actual number of atoms in a molecule and is always a whole$-$number multiple of the empirical formula.
