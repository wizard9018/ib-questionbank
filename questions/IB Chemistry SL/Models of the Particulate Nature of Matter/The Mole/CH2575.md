# CH2575

What is the concentration of chloride ions, in $\textrm{mol dm}^{-3},$ in a solution formed by mixing $400\textrm{ cm}^3$ of $1.00\textrm{ mol dm}^{-3}$ $\ce{FeCl3}$ with $200\textrm{ cm}^3$ of $2.00\textrm{ mol dm}^{-3}$ $\ce{FeCl3}$?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}0.667$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}0.800$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}2.40$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}4.00$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The total amount of $\ce{FeCl3}$ from both solutions is:

$n(\ce{FeCl3}) = (0.400\textrm{ dm}^3 \times 1.00\textrm{ mol dm}^{-3}) + (0.200\textrm{ dm}^3 \times 2.00\textrm{ mol dm}^{-3}) = 0.800\textrm{ mol}$

Each formula unit of $\ce{FeCl3}$ produces three chloride ions in solution, so:

$n(\ce{Cl-}) = 3 \times 0.800\textrm{ mol} = 2.40\textrm{ mol}$

The total volume after mixing is $400 + 200 = 600\textrm{ cm}^3 = 0.600\textrm{ dm}^3.$ The concentration of chloride ions is:

$C(\ce{Cl-}) = \dfrac{2.40\textrm{ mol}}{0.600\textrm{ dm}^3} = 4.00\textrm{ mol dm}^{-3}$

Each $\ce{FeCl3}$ formula unit dissociates into one $\ce{Fe^{3+}}$ ion and three $\ce{Cl-}$ ions, so the chloride ion concentration is always three times the $\ce{FeCl3}$ concentration.
