# CH0020

Which of the following substances would have the lowest mass?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$2.50 moles  $\ce{H2O}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$2.50 moles  $\ce{CH4}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$2.00 moles $\ce{SO2}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$2.00 moles $\ce{CO2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The mass is the amount in moles times the molar mass, $m = n \times M:$

$m\ce{(H2O)} = 2.50\,\text{mol} \times 18.02\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1} = 45.05\,\text{g}$

$m\ce{(CH_4)} = 2.50\,\text{mol} \times 16.05\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1} = 40.1\,\text{g}$

$m\ce{(SO2)} = 2.00\,\text{moles} \times 64.07\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1} = 128.14\,\text{g}$

$m\ce{(CO2)} = 2.00\,\text{moles} \times 44.01\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1} = 88.02\,\text{g}$
