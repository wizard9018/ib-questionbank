# BM0877

**Distinguish** between internal and external stakeholders. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

Internal stakeholders are individuals or groups who are part of the organisation itself, such as employees, managers, and shareholders, whereas external stakeholders are those outside the organisation who are affected by or have an interest in its activities, such as customers, suppliers, and the local community.

**Mark allocation:**

Award **[1]** for explaining internal stakeholders.

Award **[1]** for explaining external stakeholders, with a clear contrast implied or stated.

N.B. A simple statement such as 'internal stakeholders work within the business and external stakeholders do not' is sufficient for both marks if the contrast is clear.
