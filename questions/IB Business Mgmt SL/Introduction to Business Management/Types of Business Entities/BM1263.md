# BM1263

Prime Convenience Store (PCS) is a high-tech retail outlet in Paris. It was founded five years ago by Alan Kween as a sole trader. Under this structure, Alan was the sole decision-maker, but he was personally liable for all the business's debts during a difficult first year.

As PCS grew, its “Social-Tech" model - which uses AI for inventory management while donating 10% of profits to Parisian homeless shelters - became highly successful. To protect his personal assets and fund an ambitious expansion into delivery services, Alan recently incorporated the business, transforming it into a privately held company (Ltd).

Alan currently owns 90% of the shares, with his sister owning 10%. However, to raise the €500,000 needed for a new fleet of electric delivery bikes, Alan is debating whether to sell more shares to private investors or apply for a long-term bank loan. While Alan enjoys his independence, new investors are demanding a seat on the Board of Directors, which could conflict with PCS's social enterprise objectives.

1. **Describe** one feature a sole trader. [2 marks]

2. **Explain** one advantage and one disadvantage to Alan of PCS operating as a privately held company. [4 marks]

3. With reference to PCS, **explain** one advantage and one disadvantage of using share capital to fund its expansion. [4 marks]

[10 marks]

1. **Sample response:**

- One feature of a sole trader is unlimited liability. This means the owner is legally responsible for all business debts, and their personal assets can be seized to pay back creditors if the business fails.

2. **Sample response:**

- The main advantage is limited liability. Now that PCS is incorporated, Alan's personal wealth is legally separate from the company. If the delivery expansion fails, Alan is only liable for the amount he invested in his shares, providing him with financial security.
- There are increased legal and administrative costs. As a privately held company, PCS must produce audited financial accounts for its shareholders and file them with the French government. This requires hiring external accountants and auditors, which adds recurring expenses that were not necessary when Alan operated as a sole trader with simpler record-keeping requirements.

3. **Sample response:**

- Share capital is permanent capital that does not need to be repaid. Unlike a bank loan, Alan does not have to pay monthly interest. This improves PCS's cash flow, allowing more funds to be directed toward "local community projects" rather than debt servicing.

- It leads to a dilution of control. If Alan sells shares to raise the €500,000, he may lose his “independent decision-making" power. New shareholders might prioritize profit over PCS's “social enterprise values", potentially forcing Alan to reduce the 10% profit donation to shelters.
