# BM0857

SpeedyWash is a car wash franchise operating across the UK. Omar is considering buying a SpeedyWash franchise for £25,000. He would use SpeedyWash's branding, equipment suppliers, and staff training programme. In return, he must pay 8% of monthly revenue to SpeedyWash.

**Explain** one advantage and one disadvantage for Omar of operating as a franchisee. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. **Sample response:**

Operating as a franchisee gives Omar access to SpeedyWash's established brand and customer base.  This reduces the risk of failure compared to starting from scratch, as customers are already familiar with and trust the SpeedyWash name, making it easier for Omar to attract customers from day one.

A franchise requires the franchisee to pay franchise fees, which reduces profits and available funds. Omar must pay 8% of his monthly revenue to SpeedyWash as a franchise fee in addition to the £25,000 initial fee, reducing his profit and limiting the cash he has available to reinvest or cover costs.

**Mark allocation:**

**Mark as 2 + 2**

Award **[1]** for identifying the advantage/disadvantage and **[1]** for application to Omar.

Accept other valid advantages e.g. training and support provided, easier access to finance. Accept other valid disadvantages e.g. lack of creative freedom, reputation dependent on franchisor.
