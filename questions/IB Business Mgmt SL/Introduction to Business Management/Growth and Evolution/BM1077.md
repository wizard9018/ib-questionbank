# BM1077

Zenith Horizon (ZH) is a private limited company that began as a small boutique engineering firm specializing in high-efficiency wind turbine blades. For the first ten years, ZH focused on internal (organic) growth, reinvesting its profits to open three additional manufacturing plants in its home country.

As the global demand for renewable energy surged, ZH’s board of directors decided on a strategy of external growth. Last year, they engaged in a horizontal integration by acquiring "Aero-Tech," a rival firm with a significant patent portfolio. To fund this $500 million acquisition and further global expansion, ZH is now preparing for an Initial Public Offering (IPO).

However, the transition has been difficult. The merger has led to cultural clashes between management teams, and the increased size of the workforce has resulted in slower decision-making and communication breakdowns.

1. **Define** the term horizontal integration. [2 marks]

2. **Explain** one reason why ZH might have preferred internal growth during its first ten years. [2 marks]

3. **Explain** two potential diseconomies of scale that ZH may be experiencing following its acquisition of Aero-Tech. [4 marks]

[8 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. **Sample response:**

-  Horizontal integration is an external growth strategy that occurs when a business acquires or merges with another firm operating in the same industry and at the same stage of production.

- **Mark allocation:**

- Award **[1]** for a partial definition that captures the essence of the term but lacks technical precision or misses one of the two key elements.

- Award **[2]** for a clear, accurate definition that includes both key elements: "same industry" and "same stage of production."

2. **Sample response:**

-  One reason ZH preferred internal growth is that it allows for better control over quality and specialized standards. By expanding organically through opening its own manufacturing plants rather than buying other firms, ZH could ensure that its specific "high-efficiency" engineering standards for wind turbine blades were maintained consistently across all locations during its first ten years.

- **Mark allocation:**

- Award **[1]** for a relevant reason explained and an additional **[1]** for application to the case study.

3. **Sample response:**

-  As the organization grows larger, the levels of hierarchy increase. This makes it more difficult for management to communicate objectives effectively to the expanded workforce, leading to the "slower decision-making" and misunderstandings mentioned in the case.

- The cultural clashes at ZH suggest that managers are finding it difficult to coordinate different departments. This lack of coordination means employees struggle to work together, causing internal conflict, duplicated work, and lower productivity - which increases ZH's average cost per unit.

- **Mark as 2 + 2**
- Award **[1]** for a relevant diseconomy explained and an additional **[1]** for application to the case study.
