# BM0894

BurgerBox is an established fast food brand with 45 company-owned restaurants across Canada. It is considering expanding by offering franchise agreements to independent operators, allowing them to open and run BurgerBox restaurants in new cities in exchange for an initial fee of CAD $80,000 and 6% of monthly revenue.

**Explain** one advantage and one disadvantage for BurgerBox of expanding through franchising. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

By franchising, BurgerBox can expand into new cities without having to finance the construction and setup of new restaurants itself [1]. The franchisee bears the upfront investment costs, meaning BurgerBox can grow its brand presence across Canada quickly while collecting the CAD \$80,000 initial fee and 6% monthly revenue share with relatively little financial risk [1].

As BurgerBox grants independent operators the right to run its restaurants, it becomes harder to ensure consistent food quality, customer service standards, and brand presentation across all locations [1]. If a franchisee in a new city delivers poor customer experiences, this could damage BurgerBox's overall brand reputation, potentially harming sales at all 45 company-owned restaurants as well [1].

**Mark allocation:**

**Mark as 2 + 2**

Award **[1]** for identifying the advantage/disadvantage and **[1]** for application to BurgerBox.

Accept other valid advantages e.g. ongoing royalty income stream, motivated franchisee owners. Accept other valid disadvantages e.g. risk of franchise agreement disputes, franchisees may become competitors if agreement ends.
