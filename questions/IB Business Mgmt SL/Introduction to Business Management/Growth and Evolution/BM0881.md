# BM0881

**Distinguish** between forward and backward vertical integration. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

Forward vertical integration occurs when a business merges with or acquires a company closer to the end consumer in the supply chain, such as a manufacturer buying a retailer [1], whereas backward vertical integration occurs when a business merges with or acquires a company closer to the source of supply, such as a manufacturer buying a raw material supplier [1].

**Mark allocation:**

Award **[1]** for explaining forward vertical integration.
Award **[1]** for explaining backward vertical integration, with a clear contrast implied or stated.

N.B. Accept any clear contrast - the key distinction is moving toward the customer vs moving toward the supplier.
