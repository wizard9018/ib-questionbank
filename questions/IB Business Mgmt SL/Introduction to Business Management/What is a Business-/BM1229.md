# BM1229

**Define** the term added value. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

Added value is the process of enhancing a product or service to increase its worth to customers, often by improving quality, convenience, or brand image. It is calculated as the difference between the selling price and the cost of inputs.
