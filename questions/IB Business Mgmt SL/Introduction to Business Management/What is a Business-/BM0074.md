# BM0074

Jessica Blunt is an entrepreneur who started her own T-shirt printing business, Inkspire Designs. A passionate artist, Jessica launched the business to create an income stream from her creative work, turning her artistic talent into a commercial opportunity. To help fulfil customer orders, she employs one full-time and one part-time staff member.

However, competition from larger retailers makes it difficult for her to compete on price, as they can offer bulk-produced products at lower costs. Jessica is now looking for ways to grow Inkspire Designs further and differentiate her brand in a competitive market.

1. **Define** the term customer. [2 marks]

2. **State** two characteristics of an entrepreneur. [2 marks]

3. **Outline** two challenges faced by small businesses like Inkspire Designs. [4 marks]

[8 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. **Sample response:**

- A customer is a person or organisation that purchases goods or services from a business. They may not be the final user (consumer), but are key to business revenue and satisfaction strategies.

- **Mark allocation:**

- Candidates are not expected to word their definition exactly as above.

2. **Accept any two of the following:**

- - Self-starter / Starts a business
- - Raises finance / Attracts investment
- - Organises the business (planning, operations, resources)
- - Risk-taker
- - Profit-motivated
- - Creative / Innovative
- - Resourceful
- - Resilient / Determined
- - Self-motivated

- Accept any other relevant characteristic.

- **Mark allocation:**

- Award **[1]** for each correct feature stated.

3. **Sample response:**

- One challenge faced by small businesses is intense competition from larger firms that produce in larger quantities, allowing them to offer lower prices.

- Inkspire Designs is struggling to compete with “larger retailers” that can offer “bulk-produced products at lower costs,” making it difficult for Jessica to compete on price.

- Another challenge faced by small businesses is having limited resources, such as fewer employees and less financial capacity, which can restrict growth.

- Jessica employs only “one full-time and one part-time staff member,” which limits her ability to increase output, expand marketing, and grow the business in a “competitive market.”

- **Mark allocation:**

- Award **[1]** for each challenge identified and an additional **[1]** for application to the case study.
