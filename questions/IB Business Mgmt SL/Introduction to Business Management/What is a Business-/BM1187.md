# BM1187

Fresh Fields Ltd. owns farmland where it grows organic fruits and vegetables. These crops are then sent to its factory, where they are processed into juices and packaged salads. Fresh Fields also runs a chain of health food stores, selling these products directly to health-conscious consumers.

Recently, Fresh Fields has hired nutritionists and food scientists to research future food trends and develop new health products. This research helps the company create innovative products that meet changing customer preferences.

1. **Define** the term product.[2 marks]

2. Using evidence from the case, **identify** each sector of industry in which Fresh Fields Ltd. operates. [4 marks]

3. **Explain** one benefit and one drawback for Fresh Fields Ltd. of operating in more than one sector of industry. [4 marks]

[10 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. **Sample response:**

- A product is any good or service that is offered to satisfy the needs or wants of customers. It can be a physical item, like clothing or electronics, or a service, like consulting or education, designed to provide value to consumers in exchange for money or other considerations.

- **Mark allocation:**
- Award **[1]** for a vague definition.
- Award  **[2]** for a complete definition.
- Definitions do not require application to the stimulus material.

2. **Sample response**:

- Primary Sector: Growing organic fruits and vegetables on farmland.
- Secondary Sector: Processing fruits and vegetables into juices and packaged salads in the factory.
- Tertiary Sector: Selling products directly to customers through health food stores.
- Quaternary Sector: Using nutritionists and food scientists to research future food trends and develop new products.

- **Mark allocation:**

- Award **[1]** for each sector in the context of the case study.

3. **Sample response**:

-  One benefit of Fresh Fields Ltd. operating in more than one sector of industry is greater control over the supply chain.   By growing its own organic fruits and vegetables in the primary sector, processing them into juices and packaged salads in the secondary sector, and selling them directly through its own health food stores in the tertiary sector, Fresh Fields Ltd. reduces reliance on external suppliers and retailers. This helps ensure consistent quality and allows the business to capture more value at each stage of production, increasing profits.

- One drawback of Fresh Fields Ltd. operating in more than one sector of industry is increased management complexity.
Fresh Fields Ltd. must manage organic farming operations, food processing factories, a chain of health food stores, and research and development activities simultaneously, which means managers must coordinate very different processes and specialist staff. As a result, this increases the risk of management inefficiencies, raises overhead costs, and may reduce overall profitability if resources and attention are spread too thinly.

- **Mark allocation:**
- **Mark as 2 + 2**
- Award **[1]** for a valid advantage/disadvantage and an additional **[1]** for application to the case study.
- Award a maximum of **[4]**
