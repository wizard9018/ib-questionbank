# BM854

**Define** the term opportunity cost. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

Opportunity cost is the value of the next best alternative that is given up when a decision is made to use resources in a particular way.

**Mark allocation:**

Award **[1]** for identifying that opportunity cost involves a sacrifice or trade-off.
Award **[1]** for clarifying that it refers to the next best alternative foregone when a decision is made.

N.B. Do not accept vague answers such as 'the cost of a choice' without reference to the foregone alternative.
