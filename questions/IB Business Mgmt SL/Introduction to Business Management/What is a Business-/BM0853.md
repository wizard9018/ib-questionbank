# BM0853

**Define** the term needs and wants in the context of business activity. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. **Sample response:**

Needs are the basic necessities required for survival, such as food, shelter, and clothing, whereas wants are desires for goods or services that go beyond basic necessity, such as luxury items or entertainment.

**Mark allocation:**

Award **[1]** for defining needs.
Award **[1]** for defining wants, with a clear contrast implied or stated.
N.B. Accept any clear distinction — the key contrast is survival/necessity vs desire/preference.
