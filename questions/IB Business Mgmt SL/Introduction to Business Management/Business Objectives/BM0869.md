# BM0869

SolarBright is a manufacturer of solar panels with two stated objectives: to maximise profit by reducing production costs, and to achieve carbon neutrality by 2027 as part of its CSR commitments.

1. **Describe** one way SolarBright’s objective of profit maximisation may conflict with its objective of carbon neutrality. [2 marks]

2. **Outline** one trade-off SolarBrights's management might have to make as a result of having conflicting objectives. [2 marks]

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. **Sample response:**

- Profit maximisation focuses on reducing costs by using cheaper suppliers or materials, whereas achieving carbon neutrality requires spending on sustainable inputs and processes. At SolarBright, reducing production costs may involve cheaper, less sustainable suppliers and methods, which conflicts with investing in renewable energy and sustainable processes to achieve carbon neutrality.

- **Mark allocation:**

- Award **[1]** for identifying the nature of the conflict and **[1]** for application to SolarBright.

2.  **Sample response:**

- If SolarBright focuses on achieving carbon neutrality by 2027, they will need to trade off reducing production costs and maximizing profit. SolarBright investing in sustainable materials and production methods to achieve carbon neutrality will likely increase costs and reduce its profit in the short-term.

- **Mark allocation:**

- Award **[1]** for identifying a trade-off and **[1]** for application to SolarBright.
