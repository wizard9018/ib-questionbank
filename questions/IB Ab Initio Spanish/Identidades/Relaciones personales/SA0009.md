## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{equipo}$    [1 mark]

* In paragraph 1, the text says that teachers and students $\text{\textquotedblleft}$trabajan en equipo.” The word *equipo* refers to a group of people working together, which matches the meaning of grupo.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{retos}$    [1 mark]

* In paragraph 1, it says that students help their classmates when they $\text{\textquotedblleft}$buscan nuevos retos y quieren mejorar.” The word *retos* refers to challenges or new goals that students want to achieve or overcome.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{preparar}$    [1 mark]

* In paragraph 1, it says that teachers collaborate to $\text{\textquotedblleft}$preparar clases interesantes.” The verb preparar means to organize or plan something in advance, which matches the meaning of *planear*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{trabajar juntos}$    [1 mark]

* In paragraph 1, the text says that students learn to $\text{\textquotedblleft}$trabajar juntos.” This expression refers to working together with other people, which corresponds to the meaning of *colaborar*.
