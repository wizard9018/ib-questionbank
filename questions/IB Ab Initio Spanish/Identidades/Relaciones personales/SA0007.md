# SA0007

[Puntaje máximo: 6\]

Querido Roberto:

Me gusta ser tu amigo por correspondencia. Hoy quiero presentarte a mi familia. En mi familia somos cuatro personas: mi padre, mi madre, mi hermana y yo. Mi padre trabaja en una oficina y mi madre es profesora $\underline{\textrm{estupenda}}$. Mi hermana es un poco $\underline{\textrm{vaga}}$, pero le gusta mucho la música. Nosotros somos una familia muy $\underline{\textrm{unida}}$ y nos gusta pasar tiempo juntos los fines de semana.

Un saludo,\

Ramón

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Me gusta ser tu amigo por correspondencia" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I like being your pen pal"). A pen pal is someone you exchange letters with - these exchanges are *conversaciones* (*conversations*). So Ramón is saying their exchanges make him very happy.

- *Opción incorrecta:* D

- **D**: *mis amigos* (my friends) is not correct for question 1 because the sentence $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Me alegran mucho...” refers to something related to the communication between Ramón and Roberto. The text is about their conversations, not about Ramón's friends.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- In the letter, Ramón says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hoy quiero presentarte a mi familia” (today I want to introduce my family to you) and then describes the people who live with him: his father, his mother, his sister, and himself. This corresponds to $\text{\textquotedblleft}$las personas con las que vivo.”

- *Opción incorrecta:* C

- **C**: *mi hermana* (my sister) is not correct  because Ramón says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hoy quiero presentarte a mi familia” and then describes all the people who live with him. The idea is about the group of people in his home, not only his sister.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- In the text, Ramón explains that his father works in an office and his mother is a teacher. This shows that his parents work, which corresponds to the idea that they are *trabajadores*.

- *Opción incorrecta:* F

- **F**: *músicos* (musicians) is not correct for question 3 because the text says that Ramón's father works in an office and his mother is a teacher. This shows that his parents work, but there is no information suggesting that they are musicians.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- *Fantástica* (fantastic) refers to something very good or excellent. In the text, Ramón says that his mother is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$una profesora estupenda,” which indicates that she is a very good teacher.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

- *Perezosa* (lazy) refers to a person who does not like to work much or make effort. In the text, Ramón describes his sister as $\text{\textquotedblleft}$un poco vaga.”

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- *Cercana* (close) means that people have a very close relationship and spend time together. When Ramón says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$somos una familia muy unida,” it indicates that his family members are very close and spend time together.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B, C

- **A**: *Terrible* is not correct because it implies something very bad, while *estupenda* has a positive meaning, indicating something very good.

- **B**: *Trabajadora* is not correct because it describes someone who works a lot, while *vaga* means the opposite: a lazy person.

- **C**: *Separada* is not correct because *unida* indicates closeness and connection between people, while *separada* means not together or distant.
