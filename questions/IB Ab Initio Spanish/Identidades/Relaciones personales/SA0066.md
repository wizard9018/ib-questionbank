## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$ [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mi padre trabaja en una oficina" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$My father works in an office").

- *Opciones incorrectas:* B, C

- **B**: The audio does not mention a hospital at any point.

- **C**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$mi madre es profesora en mi colegio" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$my mother is a teacher at my school"). It is the mother who works at a school, not the father.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$ [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$mi madre es profesora en mi colegio" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$my mother is a teacher at my school"). Note that *en mi colegio* (at my school) is equivalent to *en el colegio de Sofía* (at Sofía's school).

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B

- **A**: The audio says *colegio* (school), not *universidad* (university). These are two different types of educational institution.

- **B**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mi padre trabaja en una oficina" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$My father works in an office"). It is the father who works in an office, not the mother.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$ [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mi hermano tiene ocho años" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$My brother is eight years old").

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

- **A**: The audio says *ocho* (eight), not *dieciocho* (eighteen). The numbers sound similar and could be confused.

- **C**: The audio says *ocho* (eight), not *ochenta* (eighty). The numbers sound similar and could be confused.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{travieso y cariñoso}$ [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$es muy travieso pero también muy cariñoso" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$he is very naughty but also very affectionate"). Both qualities must be mentioned. Accept synonyms such as *difícil* for *travieso* and *afectuoso* for *cariñoso*. The adverbs *muy* and *también* are not required.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{al parque (juntos)}$ [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Los fines de semana vamos al parque juntos" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$On weekends we go to the park together").
