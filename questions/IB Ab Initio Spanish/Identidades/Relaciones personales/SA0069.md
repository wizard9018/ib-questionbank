## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Las respuestas pueden estar en cualquier orden.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, C, F}$        [3 marks]

* **B**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$me parec$\text{\'{\i}}$a un poco fr$\text{\'{\i}}$a y distante" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$she seemed a little cold and distant to me"). Note that *poco accesible* (*not very approachable*) and *fr$\textit{\'{\i}}$a y distante* (*cold and distant*) have the same meaning.

* **C**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$esta semana tuvimos que trabajar juntas en un proyecto" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$this week we had to work together on a project"). This confirms that Valeria and Luc$\text{\'{\i}}$a worked together this week.

* **F**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$se qued$\text{\'{o}}$ m$\text{\'{a}}$s tarde para echarme una mano" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$she stayed later to give me a hand"). Note that *echarme una mano* (*to give me a hand*) is an idiomatic expression meaning to help someone. This confirms that Luc$\text{\'{\i}}$a stayed longer to help Valeria.

Note that *colaboraron* (*they collaborated*) and *trabajar juntas* (*to work together*) have the same meaning. This tells us they worked together on a project.
