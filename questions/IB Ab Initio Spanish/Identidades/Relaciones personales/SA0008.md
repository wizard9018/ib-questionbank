# SA0008

[Puntaje máximo: 6\]

**Tener un amigo es una [-1-]**

En mi **[-2-]**, los amigos son muy importantes en la vida porque nos ayudan cuando tenemos alguna **[-3-]**  y también comparten con nosotros los momentos felices. Un buen amigo escucha, entiende y siempre intenta ayudar. Con los amigos podemos hablar, reír y hacer muchas actividades juntos, como estudiar, ir al cine a ver una película divertida o dar una **[-4-]**.

También es importante cuidar la amistad. No es necesario tener muchos amigos; a veces uno o dos buenos amigos son suficientes. Para mí, la amistad es algo muy especial que hace la vida más feliz.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* The word *fortuna* is correct because the sentence $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Tener un amigo es una fortuna" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Having a friend is a fortune") expresses that having a friend is something very valuable in life.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A

* **A**: *Pobreza* is not correct because it refers to a lack of money or resources, while the sentence talks about the value of having a friend.

* The word *opini$\textit{\'{o}}$n* is correct because the expression $\text{\textquotedblleft}$en mi opini$\text{\'{o}}$n" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$in my opinion") introduces the writer's personal view about the importance of friends.:br

* *Opciones incorrectas:* C

* **C**: *Realidad* is not correct because it refers to something true or real, but the sentence requires an expression that introduces a personal opinion.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* The word *dificultad* is correct because the text explains that friends help us ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$nos ayudan" means  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$they help us") when we are in complicated or difficult situations.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* E

* **E**: *Respuesta* is not correct because it refers to an answer to a question, while the paragraph talks about helping friends in difficult situations.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{H}$    [1 mark]

* The word *vuelta* is correct because the expression $\text{\textquotedblleft}$dar una vuelta" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to go for a walk") refers to going for a short walk outside with friends.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* G

* **G**: *Charla* is not correct because it refers to a talk or presentation. The expression $\text{\textquotedblleft}$dar una charla” is usually used in contexts such as conferences, lectures, or formal talks. However, the paragraph refers to the expression $\text{\textquotedblleft}$dar una vuelta,” which means going for a walk or taking a short walk.
