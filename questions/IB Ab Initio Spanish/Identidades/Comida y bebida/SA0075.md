**Vas a escuchar un podcast de Mar$\textbf{\'{\i}}$a hablando de las tradiciones culinarias de Per$\textbf{\'{u}}$.**

**Elige la respuesta correcta.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$La cocina peruana es especial porque...    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$mezcla ingredientes de diferentes culturas". Note that *combina elementos de distintas culturas* and *mezcla ingredientes de diferentes culturas* have the same meaning. This tells us Peruvian cuisine is special because it brings together elements from different cultures.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

- **A**: The audio says the cuisine mixes ingredients from different cultures, which means it is not purely indigenous. 

- **C**: The audio says the Spanish brought new foods that combined with local products, but the cuisine was not created by the Spanish alone.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$trajeron nuevos alimentos que se combinaron con los productos locales". Note that *se incorporaron ingredientes nuevos* and *trajeron nuevos alimentos que se combinaron* have the same meaning. This tells us the Spanish introduced new ingredients into the local cuisine.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B

- **A**: The audio says local products *se combinaron* with new ones, which means they did not disappear. 

- **B**: The audio does not mention new cooking techniques, only new foods.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la comida es mucho más que alimentarse, es parte de nuestra identidad y nuestra historia". Note that *un símbolo cultural profundo* paraphrases *parte de nuestra identidad y nuestra historia*. This tells us food has a deep cultural meaning for Peruvians.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

- **A**: The audio says food is much more than just eating, which means it is not simply a daily activity.

- **C**: The audio does not describe food as an ancient tradition specifically.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ceviche}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$El plato más famoso es el ceviche" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the most famous dish is ceviche"). This tells us that *ceviche* is the most famous dish in Peru.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{arroz}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$combina carne con verduras y arroz" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it combines meat with vegetables and rice"). This tells us that *lomo saltado* includes rice.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{región/región del país}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$cada región del país tiene sus propias recetas y tradiciones" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$each region of the country has its own recipes and traditions"). This tells us that Peruvian cuisine is varied because each region has its own recipes.
