## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{un restaurante mexicano}$    [1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$La semana pasada fui a desayunar a un restaurante mexicano con mis amigos… hab$\text{\'{\i}}$a muchas personas desayunando ah$\text{\'{\i}}$.” In Spanish,*ah$\textit{\'{\i}}$* (*there*) refers to a place mentioned before. In this case, it refers to *un restaurante mexicano*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{una bebida}$    [1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Es una bebida bastante espesa y la sirven caliente.” In Spanish, the pronoun *la* replaces a feminine noun mentioned before. Here it refers to *una bebida* (*the atole*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{mis padres}$    [1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Me gustar$\text{\'{\i}}$a volver con mis padres y recomendarles algunos de los platos.” The pronoun les refers to the people mentioned before, who are*mis padres*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* This subtitle is correct because paragraph 1 describes going to a restaurant to have breakfast: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$La semana pasada fui a desayunar a un restaurante mexicano con mis amigos.” (*Last week I went to have breakfast at a Mexican restaurant with my friends.*) This introduces a new and different breakfast experience.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* D

* **D**: *Un lugar con encanto* (*a charming place*) is not correct because the restaurant is only briefly described as $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{peque\~{n}o,}$ pero muy acogedor” (*small but very cosy*). The main idea of the paragraph is the breakfast experience.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

* This subtitle is correct because paragraph 2 talks about Atole: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Me ped$\text{\'{\i}}$ un vaso de atole… Es una bebida bastante espesa y la sirven caliente.” (*I ordered a glass of atole... It is a quite thick drink and they serve it hot.*) The paragraph focuses on something to drink.

* *Opciones incorrectas:*

* **B**: *Platos salados* (*salty dishes*) is not correct because the paragraph focuses on *atole*, which is a sweet drink, not a dish. Although *tamales de pollo* (*corn dough filled with chicken*) are mentioned, they are only a small detail, not the main topic of the paragraph.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* This subtitle is correct because paragraph 3 shows that the narrator wants to return: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Me gustar$\text{\'{\i}}$a volver con mis padres y recomendarles algunos de los platos.” (*I would like to return with my parents and recommend some of the dishes to them.*) This indicates that the narrator would repeat the experience.

* *Opciones incorrectas:*

* **E**: *Un lugar familiar* (*a family place*) is not correct because the text does not say the restaurant is for families; it only mentions that the narrator would like to return with $\text{\textquotedblleft}$mis padres” (my parents).
