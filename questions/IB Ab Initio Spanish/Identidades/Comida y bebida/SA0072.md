# SA0072

\[Puntaje m$\text{\'{a}}$ximo: 6]

**Vas a escuchar un anuncio de radio del restaurante El Rinc$\textbf{\'{o}}$n.**

:audio{#29dd53b9-1bab-4216-8691-7fa582a684e0 aid="29dd53b9-1bab-4216-8691-7fa582a684e0"}

**Escoge las tres frases verdaderas.**[3 marks]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$El Rinc$\text{\'{o}}$n solo ofrece un tipo de platillo.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$El restaurante ofrece comida tradicional mexicana.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Los ingredientes vienen de otros pa$\text{\'{\i}}$ses.

* D.$\hspace{1em}$El restaurante abre todos los d$\text{\'{\i}}$as de la semana.

* E.$\hspace{1em}$El domingo hay una oferta especial para familias.

* F.$\hspace{1em}$El restaurante est$\text{\'{a}}$ en la Calle Insurgentes.

**Completa los espacios en blanco. Usa como m$\textbf{\'{a}}$ximo tres palabras por espacio.**[3 marks]

**Las notas de un oyente**

El restaurante est$\text{\'{a}}$ abierto de lunes a s$\text{\'{a}}$bado d&#x65; **\[-4-]** a **\[-5-]** &#x64;e la noche. El n$\text{\'{u}}$mero de la calle es e&#x6C; **\[-6-]**.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Las respuestas pueden estar en cualquier orden.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, E, F}$        [3 marks]

* **B**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$los mejores platillos tradicionales mexicanos" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the best traditional Mexican dishes"). This tells us the restaurant offers traditional Mexican food.

* **E**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Los domingos ofrecemos un buffet especial para familias" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$on Sundays we offer a special buffet for families"). Note that *oferta especial* (*special offer*) and *buffet especial* (*special buffet*) have the same meaning. This tells us there is a special offer for families on Sundays.

* **F**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Estamos en la Calle Insurgentes n$\text{\'{u}}$mero cuarenta y cinco" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we are on Calle Insurgentes number forty-five"). This tells us the restaurant is on Calle Insurgentes.:br

* *Opciones Incorrectas:* A, C, D

* **A**: The audio says: *carta variada* (*varied menu*), which means the restaurant offers a wide variety of dishes, not just one type (*un solo tipo de platillo*).

* **C**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nuestros ingredientes son frescos y locales" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$our ingredients are fresh and local"), which means the ingredients come from Mexico, not *other countries* (*otros pa$\textit{\'{\i}}$ses*).

* **D**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Abrimos de lunes a s$\text{\'{a}}$bado" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we open from Monday to Saturday"), which means the restaurant does not open *every day of the week* (*todos los d$\textit{\'{\i}}$as de la semana*).

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{una}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Abrimos de lunes a s$\text{\'{a}}$bado de una a diez de la noche" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we open from Monday to Saturday from one to ten at night"). This tells us the restaurant opens at one in the afternoon.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{diez}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$de una a diez de la noche" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$from one to ten at night"). This tells us the restaurant closes at ten at night.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{cuarenta y cinco/45}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Estamos en la Calle Insurgentes n$\text{\'{u}}$mero cuarenta y cinco" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we are on Calle Insurgentes number forty-five"). This tells us the street number of the restaurant.
