**Receta del gazpacho**

El gazpacho es un plato muy popular en Espa$\text{\~{n}}$a. Para prepararlo, primero necesitas tomates, pepino, pimiento, ajo, aceite y sal. Cortas los ingredientes y los mezclas bien en una batidora. Finalmente, pones el gazpacho en la nevera y lo sirves fr$\text{\'{\i}}$o. Es una receta f$\text{\'{a}}$cil y refrescante.

* A.$\hspace{1em}$El gazpacho se toma caliente

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, D, E}$        [3 marks]

* **B**: The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cortas los ingredientes y los mezclas bien en una batidora” (*you cut the ingredients and mix them well in a blender*). This means you put the ingredients together to make gazpacho.

* **D**: The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Es una receta f$\text{\'{a}}$cil y refrescante” (*it is an easy and refreshing recipe*). This shows that the recipe is simple.

* **E**: The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$El gazpacho es un plato muy popular en Espa$\text{\~{n}}$a” (*gazpacho is a very popular dish in Spain*). This means it is common in Spain.

* *Opciones Incorrectas:*  A, C, F

* **A**: This is not correct because the option says *caliente* (*hot*). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$lo sirves fr$\text{\'{\i}}$o” (*you serve it cold*).

* **C**: This is not correct because the option says *inmediatamente* (*immediately*). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$pones el gazpacho en la nevera” (*you put the gazpacho in the fridge*) before serving it.

* **F**: This is not correct because the option says *pesado* (*heavy*). The text says: *refrescante* (*refreshing*).

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* The register of the text is neutro (neutral). The language is clear, simple and standard. For example: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Para prepararlo, primero necesitas tomates, pepino, pimiento, ajo, aceite y sal.” and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cortas los ingredientes y los mezclas bien en una batidora.” These sentences use straightforward vocabulary and instructions typical of informational texts or recipes.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* B, C, D

* **B**: This is not correct because the option says *coloquial* (*colloquial*), but the text does not include informal expressions used in everyday conversation, such as *oye*, *pues* or *mira*.

* **C**: This is not correct because the option says *formal* (*formal*), but the text does not use complex structures or very formal vocabulary typical of academic or official texts.

* **D**: This is not correct because the option says *t$\textit{\'{e}}$cnico* (*technical*), which refers to language that includes specialized vocabulary from a specific field, such as science or medicine. The text, however, uses simple everyday vocabulary that anyone can understand.
