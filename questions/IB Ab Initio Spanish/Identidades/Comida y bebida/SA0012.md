**Tradici$\textbf{\'{o}}$n oaxaque$\textbf{\~{n}}$a**

En el estado mexicano de Oaxaca, las tlayudas son el platillo tradicional. Son tortillas grandes y crujientes hechas con ma$\text{\'{\i}}$z. Normalmente se preparan con frijoles, queso, carne y salsa. Muchas familias comen tlayudas en fiestas o reuniones con amigos. Si tienes hambre, puedes comprar una tlayuda $\underline{\textrm{dorada}}$ reci$\text{\'{e}}$n hecha en alg$\text{\'{u}}$n puesto callejero. A muchas personas les gusta esta comida porque es muy $\underline{\textrm{sabrosa}}$ y forma parte de la cultura $\underline{\textrm{local}}$. Las tlayudas son el orgullo de la gastronom$\text{\'{\i}}$a oaxaque$\text{\~{n}}$a.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{tradicional}$    [1 mark]

* The text says: “las tlayudas son el platillo tradicional.” (*the traditional dish*). This shows that tlayudas are a traditional food from Oaxaca.

:br$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{alg\'{u}n puesto callejero}$[1 mark]

* The text says: “puedes comprar una tlayuda caliente reci$\text{\'{e}}$n hecha en alg$\text{\'{u}}$n puesto callejero.” (*you can buy a freshly made hot tlayuda at a street stall*). This shows where people can buy and eat tlayudas.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

* *Dorada* (*golden/crispy*) describes the texture and appearance of the tlayuda when it is freshly made and well cooked. *Tostada* (*toasted/crispy*) has the same meaning, both describe food that has been cooked until golden and crispy.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* C

* **C**: *Refrescante* is not correct because it means something cool or refreshing, while the text describes the tlayuda as caliente, which indicates warm food.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *Deliciosa* refers to food that tastes very good. In the text, it says that the tlayuda is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$muy sabrosa,” which indicates that the food is very tasty.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* E

* **E**: *Sosa* is not correct because it means food without flavour, while the text says the tlayuda is sabrosa, which indicates that it tastes very good.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *Regional* refers to something that belongs to a specific place or area. In the text, it says that the food is part of the “cultura local,” which indicates that it comes from that region.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* B

* **B**: *Extranjera* is not correct because it means something from another country, while the text says the food is part of the local culture, which indicates it comes from that place.
