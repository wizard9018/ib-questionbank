## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$

* **a**.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{productos frescos}$ [1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Muchas personas van all$\text{\'{\i}}$ porque hay productos frescos y buenos precios.” The expression *productos frescos* refers to fresh food that people can buy in the market.

* **b**.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{buenos precios}$  [1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Muchas personas van all$\text{\'{\i}}$ porque hay productos frescos y buenos precios.” The expression *buenos precios* indicates that the food in the market is not expensive.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{platos preparados}$    [1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Aparte de estos productos, tambi$\text{\'{e}}$n se pueden comprar platos preparados.” The expression $\text{\textquotedblleft}$aparte de estos productos” means apart from fresh products, and it shows that people can also buy prepared dishes in the market.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *Luego* (*afterwards*) shows the next action in the sequence, first they shopped, then they went to the restaurant.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:*

* **C**: *Mientras* (*while*) is not correct because *mientras* (*while*) refers to actions that happen at the same time, which is not the case in the paragraph.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* *Adem$\textit{\'{a}}$s* (*in addition*) adds another action, first they ate, then they also drank Inca Kola.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:*

* **G**: *Por ejemplo* (*for example*) is not correct because *por ejemplo* (*for example*) is used to introduce an example, but the sentence describes an action that happened during the visit.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{H}$    [1 mark]

* *Sin embargo* (*however*) introduces an unexpected contrast: they were enjoying their meal, but the restaurant was very busy and they had to wait.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:*

* **E**: *Aunque* (*although*) is not correct because *aunque* (*although*) introduces a contrast inside the same sentence structure, not as a separate sentence like in the text.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

* *Por eso* (*therefore*) introduces a consequence, because she loved the place, she hopes to return.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:*

* **A**: *Entonces* (*then / therefore*) is not correct because it normally introduces a conclusion or consequence, not simply the next action in the story.
