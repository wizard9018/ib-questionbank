## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{una organizaci\'{o}n local}$[1 mark]

* The word *la* in $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ayudarla" is used instead of repeating the name of the organization. It refers to una organizaci$\text{\'{o}}$n local, the group mentioned at the beginning of the text.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{buena actitud}$    [1 mark]

* The word *la* in $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Si la tienes” is used instead of repeating *buena actitud*. It refers to *buena actitud*, the quality mentioned in the previous sentence.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{en el campo}$    [1 mark]

* The word *donde* refers to a place mentioned just before. In this sentence, *donde* refers to *el campo*, the place where volunteers help plant trees and care for nature.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *Perfil de los participantes* (*participants' profile*) is correct. The answer is found in paragraph 2: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Buscamos personas con buena actitud... ser amable, responsable... trabajar con otras personas.” The paragraph describes the type of person they want, that is, the personal qualities of the volunteers.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *Requisitos* (*requirements*) is correct. The answer is found in paragraph 3: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Es imprescindible tener entre 16 y 18 a$\text{\~{n}}$os y ser bastante fuerte.” The paragraph explains the conditions that volunteers must meet, such as age and physical ability.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* B, C

* **B**: *Valoraciones* (*assessments*) is not correct because the text is not assessing people or giving opinions about their performance.

* **C**: *Lugar de trabajo* (*workplace*) is not correct because although the text mentions *el campo*, the paragraph mainly explains the requirements volunteers must have, not the place where they work.
