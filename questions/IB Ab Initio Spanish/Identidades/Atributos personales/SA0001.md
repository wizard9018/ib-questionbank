# SA0001

\[Puntaje m$\text{\'{a}}$ximo: 5]:br

**Mi mejor amigo: Rub$\textbf{\'{e}}$n**

Mi mejor amigo se llama Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n y vive en la calle Granada, en el barrio de Tet$\text{\'{u}}$an, en Madrid. Nos conocemos desde hace a$\text{\~{n}}$os porque estudiamos juntos en el colegio Nuestra Se$\text{\~{n}}$ora de la Asunci$\text{\'{o}}$n. Desde el primer d$\text{\'{\i}}$a nos llevamos genial.

Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n es una persona estupenda. Cuando alguien tiene un problema, siempre intenta ayudar, por eso mucha gente lo aprecia. Es tranquilo y le gusta mucho hacer deporte. Lo admiro porque es un amigo leal.

**Elige las respuestas correctas.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$¿En qu$\text{\'{e}}$ ciudad vive Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n?[1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Granada

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Madrid

* C.$\hspace{1em}\text{Asunci\'{o}n}$

* D.$\hspace{1em}\text{Tet\'{u}an}$

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿C\'{o}mo}$ es su relaci$\text{\'{o}}$n desde el principio?[1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Normal

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Mala

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Regular

* D.$\hspace{1em}$Buena

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿Qu\'{e}}$ piensan muchas personas sobre Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n?[1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Es antip$\text{\'{a}}$tico

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Solo ayuda a sus amigos

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Es una buena persona

* D.$\hspace{1em}$Es aburrido

**Contesta las siguientes preguntas bas$\textbf{\'{a}}$ndote en el p$\textbf{\'{a}}$rrafo 2.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿Qu\'{e}}$ le encanta hacer Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n?[1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$¿Por qu$\text{\'{e}}$ admira el narrador a Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n?[1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* The answer is found in paragraph 1: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ vive en la calle Granada, en el barrio de Tet$\text{\'{u}}$an, en Madrid." ("he lives on Granada street, in the neighbourhood of Tet$\text{\'{u}}$an, in Madrid.") The text clearly states that Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n lives in Madrid.:br

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C, D

* **A**: Granada refers to the street name (calle Granada), not the city where Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n lives.

* **C**: Nuestra Se$\text{\~{n}}$ora de la Asunci$\text{\'{o}}$n is the name of the school where they studied, not a city.

* **D**: Tet$\text{\'{u}}$an is the neighborhood where Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n lives, but the question asks for the city.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* The answer is found in paragraph 1: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Desde el primer d$\text{\'{\i}}$a nos llevamos genial." ("From the first day we got along really well.") This tells us their relationship has been very good from the start.:br

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B, C

* **A**: The text does not describe the relationship as normal, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$genial" means excellent, not average.

* **B**: The relationship is clearly positive, not bad.

* **C**: Regular suggests the relationship is so-so, but the text shows it is very good.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* Paragraph 2 describes Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n as $\text{\textquotedblleft}$cuando alguien tiene un problema, siempre intenta ayudar, por eso mucha gente lo aprecia." ("when someone has a problem, he always tries to help, that is why many people appreciate him.") This shows that many people see Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n as a good person.:br

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B, D

* **A**: The text presents Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n positively, so there is no evidence that he is unfriendly.

* **B**: This is incorrect because the text states $\text{\textquotedblleft}$cuando alguien tiene un problema, siempre intenta ayudar". Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n helps anyone, not only his friends.

* **D**: Rub$\text{\'{e}}$n is described as someone helpful, calm and loyal, not as a boring person.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{deporte}$    [1 mark]

* Paragraph 2 says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$le gusta mucho hacer deporte." ("he really likes doing sport.")

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{es un amigo leal}$    [1 mark]

* Paragraph 2 states $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Lo admiro porque es un amigo leal." ("I admire him because he is a loyal friend.").
