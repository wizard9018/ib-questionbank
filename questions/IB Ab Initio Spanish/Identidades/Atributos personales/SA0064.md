# SA0064

\[Puntaje m$\text{\'{a}}$ximo: 5]

**Vas a escuchar a una persona que busca un profesor de esperanto.**

:audio{#2a45d7a6-7a87-454b-89b0-c9ae0adb7c6b aid="2a45d7a6-7a87-454b-89b0-c9ae0adb7c6b"}

**Escoge las tres frases verdaderas.**  [3 marks]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$El esperanto es una lengua complicada.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Ofelia quiere conocer nuevas personas.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Aprender otro idioma es entretenido.

* D.$\hspace{1em}$Ofelia quiere mejorar su curr$\text{\'{\i}}$culum.

* E.$\hspace{1em}$Ofelia va a estudiar sola.

* F.$\hspace{1em}$Ofelia busca personas de distintos sitios.

**Elige la respuesta correcta.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿Qu\'{e}}$ tipo de profesor busca Ofelia?[1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Activo

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Paciente

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Responsable

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿C\'{o}mo}$ es Ofelia?[1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Amable y sociable

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Callada y reservada

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Interesada y habladora

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Las respuestas pueden estar en cualquier orden.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, C, F}$        [3 marks]

* **B**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$mi objetivo no es laboral, solo quiero hacer amigos" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$my goal is not work-related, I just want to make friends"). This tells us Ofelia's reason for learning Esperanto is social.

* **C**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$me encanta hablar con personas de culturas diferentes" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I love talking to people from different cultures"). This tells us Ofelia enjoys meeting people from other countries.

* **F**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$me gusta participar y hablar mucho en clase" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I like to participate and talk a lot in class"). This tells us Ofelia talks a lot in class.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, D, E

* **A**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$quiero aprender un idioma f$\text{\'{a}}$cil" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I want to learn an easy language"),*f$\textit{\'{a}}$cil* (easy) and *dif$\textit{\'{\i}}$cil* (difficult) are opposite in meaning.

* **D**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$prefiero un profesor abierto y sociable" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I prefer an open and sociable teacher"),  Ofelia wants the opposite of serious.

* **E**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$soy una persona curiosa y comunicativa" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am a curious and communicative person"), *introvertida* is the opposite of *comunicativa*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$        [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$me gustar$\text{\'{\i}}$a un profesor din$\text{\'{a}}$mico" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I would like a dynamic teacher"). Note that*din$\textit{\'{a}}$mico* and *activo* have the same meaning.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* B, C

* **B**: The audio never mentions *paciente* (patient) as a quality Ofelia is looking for.

* **C**: The audio never mentions *responsable* (responsible) as a quality Ofelia is looking for.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$        [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$soy una persona curiosa y comunicativa" and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$me gusta hablar mucho en clase" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am a curious and communicative person" and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I like to talk a lot in class"). Note that *curiosa* and *interesada* have the same meaning, and *comunicativa* and *habladora* have the same meaning.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B

* **A**: The audio says *prefiero un profesor abierto y sociable* , *sociable* describes the teacher Ofelia is looking for, not Ofelia herself.

* **B**: The audio says *soy una persona curiosa y comunicativa*, *callada* and *reservada* are opposite in meaning.
