# SA0061

\[Puntaje m$\text{\'{a}}$ximo: 5]

**Vas a escuchar un audio de un profesor present$\textbf{\'{a}}$ndose a la clase.**

:audio{#12a72653-5521-492e-a594-2009c60279ba aid="12a72653-5521-492e-a594-2009c60279ba"}

**Contesta las siguientes preguntas.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿C\'{o}mo}$ se llama el profesor?[1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿C\'{o}mo}$ es el profesor en la escuela?[1 mark]

**Elige la respuesta correcta.**

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿C\'{o}mo}$ es el profesor con sus amigos?[1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$amable

* B.$\hspace{1em}$organizado

* C.$\hspace{1em}$introvertido

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿Qu\'{e}}$ le gusta aprender al profesor?[1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Actividades f$\text{\'{\i}}$sicas

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Lenguas diferentes

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Programas de humor

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿C\'{o}mo}$ suele sentirse el profesor?[1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Triste

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Regular

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Feliz

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Daniel}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Me llamo Daniel." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$My name is Daniel.") This clearly tells us the teacher's name.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{responsable}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$En el colegio soy muy responsable, porque siempre hago mi trabajo a tiempo." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$At school I am very responsible, because I always do my work on time.")

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Con mis amigos soy simp$\text{\'{a}}$tico y abierto." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$With my friends I am friendly and open.) Note that *simp$\textit{\'{a}}$tico* (*friendly*) and *amable* (*kind*) have the same meaning.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* B, C

* **B**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Soy un profesor tranquilo y organizado." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am a calm and organised teacher.") This describes his character in general, not specifically with his friends.

* **C**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Con mis amigos soy... abierto." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$With my friends I am... open.") Note that *abierto* (*open*) and *introvertido* (*introverted*) are opposite in meaning.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Me gusta aprender nuevos idiomas." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I like learning new languages.") Note that *nuevos idiomas* (*new languages*) and *lenguas diferentes* (*different languages*) have the same meaning.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

* **A**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Odio los deportes." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I hate sport.") This is the opposite of what option A suggests.

* **C**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Casi siempre estoy de buen humor." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am almost always in a good mood.") Note that *de buen humor* (*in a good mood*) and *programas de humor* (*humour programmes*) are unrelated expressions.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Soy bastante positivo y casi siempre estoy de buen humor." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am quite positive and almost always in a good mood.") Note that *de buen humor* (*in a good mood*) and *feliz* (*happy*) have the same meaning.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B

* **A**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Soy bastante positivo y casi siempre estoy de buen humor." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am quite positive and almost always in a good mood.") Note that *de buen humor* (*in a good mood*) and *triste* (*sad*) are opposite in meaning.

* **B**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Soy bastante positivo y casi siempre estoy de buen humor." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am quite positive and almost always in a good mood.") Note that *bastante positivo* (*quite positive*) and *regular* (*so-so*) are opposite in meaning.
