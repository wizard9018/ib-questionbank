## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

* Paragraph 1 says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$...ayuda a j$\text{\'{o}}$venes y adultos con su salud mental."*J$\textit{\'{o}}$venes* (*young people*) and *adultos* (*adults*) represent two different age groups, so the correct ending is *personas de diferentes edades* (*people of different ages*).

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* A

* **A**: *gente con ansiedad* (*people with anxiety*) is not correct for question 1 because the text says she helps *j$\textit{\'{o}}$venes y adultos* (*young people and adults*), not only people with anxiety issues.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* Ana Mart$\text{\'{\i}}$nez says in paragraph 2: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Trabajo en una cl$\text{\'{\i}}$nica (a clinic).” A*cl$\textit{\'{\i}}$nica* (*clinic*) is a place where doctors and psychologists work. This corresponds to *un centro m$\textit{\'{e}}$dico* (*a medical center*).

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* E

* **E**: *su trabajo* (*her job*) is not correct for question 2 because the question asks *d$\textit{\'{o}}$nde trabaja* (*where she works*), not *qu$\textit{\'{e}}$ trabajo tiene* (*what job she has*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* Paragraph 3 says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Muchas personas se sienten m$\text{\'{a}}$s tranquilas despu$\text{\'{e}}$s de hablar con un profesional” In this context,*un profesional* (*a professional*) means *un experto en salud mental* (*a mental health expert*).

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* D

* **D**: *sus sentimientos* (*their feelings*) is not correct for question 3 because the sentence talks about *consultar a un profesional* (*consulting a professional*). The idea is about a person who gives help, not about feelings.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

* $\text{\textquotedblleft}$¿Puedes hablarnos de tu trabajo?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Can you tell us about your work?") This question is correct because the next sentence explains what the doctor does: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Claro. Soy psic$\text{\'{o}}$loga y trabajo en una cl$\text{\'{\i}}$nica. Cada d$\text{\'{\i}}$a hablo con personas que tienen problemas emocionales o momentos dif$\text{\'{\i}}$ciles en su vida.” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Of course. I am a psychologist and I work in a clinic. Every day I talk with people who have emotional problems or difficult moments in their lives.") The paragraph describes her job and daily work.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* D

* **D**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$¿Por qu$\text{\'{e}}$ decidiste ejercer esta profesi$\text{\'{o}}$n?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Why did you decide to practice this profession?") is not correct because paragraph 2 never explains why the doctor became a psychologist.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* $\text{\textquotedblleft}$¿Por qu$\text{\'{e}}$ es $\text{\'{u}}$til la terapia?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Why is therapy useful?") This question is correct because the paragraph explains the benefit of therapy: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ayuda a entender mejor nuestras emociones. Muchas personas se sienten m$\text{\'{a}}$s tranquilas despu$\text{\'{e}}$s de hablar con un profesional.” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$It helps people understand their emotions better. Many people feel calmer after talking to a professional.") The paragraph focuses on why therapy is useful.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* F

* **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{¿C\'{o}mo}$ podemos relajarnos?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$How can we relax?") is not correct because paragraph 3 talks about resting, talking with others and seeking help, not specifically about relaxation techniques.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{H}$    [1 mark]

* $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{¿Qu\'{e}}$ problemas son los m$\text{\'{a}}$s comunes?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$What problems are the most common?") This question is correct because the paragraph talks about common problems: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Muchos j$\text{\'{o}}$venes tienen estr$\text{\'{e}}$s por los estudios, el trabajo o las redes sociales.” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Many young people have stress because of studies, work or social media.") The paragraph describes frequent mental health problems.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* G

* **G**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{¿C\'{o}mo}$ nos afecta el estr$\text{\'{e}}$s?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$How does stress affect us?") is not correct because paragraph 4 only mentions that many young people have stress, but it does not explain its effects.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{¿Qu\'{e}}$ consejo nos dar$\text{\'{\i}}$as?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$What advice would you give us?") This question is correct because the paragraph gives advice: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Es importante descansar bien, hablar con amigos o familia y buscar ayuda profesional cuando es necesario.” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$It is important to rest well, talk with friends or family and seek professional help when necessary.") The paragraph presents different pieces of advice for mental health.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* A

* **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{¿Cu\'{a}ndo}$ deber$\text{\'{\i}}$a una persona empezar terapia?" Instead, it gives general advice for maintaining good mental health, such as resting well, talking with friends or family, and seeking professional help when necessary.
