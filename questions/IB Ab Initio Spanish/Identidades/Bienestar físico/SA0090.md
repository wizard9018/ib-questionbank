## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$me sentía agotado y sin motivación" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I felt exhausted and unmotivated). Note that *sin energía ni ganas* (*without energy or motivation*) and *agotado y sin motivación* (*exhausted and unmotivated*) have the same meaning. This tells us Rodrigo visited the doctor because he felt exhausted and unmotivated.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

- **A**: The audio does not mention *sleeping problems* (*problemas para dormir*).

- **C**: The audio does not mention *physical pain* (*dolores físicos*).

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$pensaba que pedir ayuda era una señal de debilidad" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$he thought that asking for help was a sign of weakness"). Note that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$mostraba una falta de fortaleza" (*showed a lack of strength*) and *era una señal de debilidad* (*was a sign of weakness*) have the same meaning. This tells us Rodrigo initially thought asking for help showed a lack of strength.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B

- **A**: The audio says *señal de debilidad* (*sign of weakness*), not that it was *unnecessary* (*innecesario*). 

- **B**: The audio does not mention *difficulty finding help* (*dificultad para encontrar ayuda*).

$3 - 5.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Las respuestas pueden estar en cualquier orden.*  

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, D}$        [3 marks]

- **A**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Empecé a hacer ejercicio regularmente" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I started exercising regularly"). Note that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$incorporó el deporte a su rutina" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$incorporated sport into his routine") and *hacer ejercicio regularmente* (*to exercise regularly*) have the same meaning. This tells us Rodrigo made exercise part of his routine.

- **C**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$aprendí a decir no en el trabajo cuando tenía demasiadas responsabilidades" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I learned to say no at work when I had too many responsibilities"). This tells us Rodrigo learned to manage his workload better.

- **D**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la salud mental necesita la misma atención que la salud física" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$mental health needs the same attention as physical health"). Note that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$cuidar la mente es igual de importante que cuidar el cuerpo" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$taking care of the mind is just as important as taking care of the body") and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la salud mental necesita la misma atención que la salud física" have the same meaning. This tells us Rodrigo considers mental and physical health equally important.

- *Opciones Incorrectas:*  B, E, F

- **B**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$fui recuperando el contacto con mis amigos" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I gradually reconnected with my friends"), which means Rodrigo reconnected with his friends, not the opposite (*perdió el contacto*).

- **E**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hoy me siento una persona diferente y más equilibrada" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$today I feel like a different and more balanced person"), which means Rodrigo feels better now, not that he continued feeling unwell.

- **F**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$recomendaría a cualquiera que busque ayuda si la necesita" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I would recommend anyone to seek help if they need it") and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hoy me siento una persona diferente y más equilibrada" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$today I feel like a different and more balanced person"). This shows that Rodrigo believes stress can be overcome with the right help.
