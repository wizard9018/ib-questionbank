# SA0088

[Puntaje máximo: 6\]

**Vas a escuchar una presentaci$\textbf{\'{o}}$n de un m$\textbf{\'{e}}$dico sobre la gripe.**

:audio{#90c6b873-155b-4697-bcc0-d6ba1da63f66 aid="90c6b873-155b-4697-bcc0-d6ba1da63f66"}

**Escoge las tres frases verdaderas.**[3 marks]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$La gripe es frecuente en Espa$\text{\~{n}}$a durante el invierno.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$La gripe solo causa fiebre.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$La gripe y el resfriado son igualmente graves.

* D.$\hspace{1em}$Hay que ir al m$\text{\'{e}}$dico si los s$\text{\'{\i}}$ntomas duran m$\text{\'{a}}$s de siete d$\text{\'{\i}}$as.

* E.$\hspace{1em}$El m$\text{\'{e}}$dico recomienda hacer ejercicio para mejorar.

* F.$\hspace{1em}$La vacuna anual es la mejor forma de evitar la gripe.

**Elige la respuesta correcta.**

:br$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ ¿Cu$\text{\'{a}}$ndo debe consultar a un m$\text{\'{e}}$dico?

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Si los s$\text{\'{\i}}$ntomas duran m$\text{\'{a}}$s de una semana.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Descansar y beber mucho l$\text{\'{\i}}$quido.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Hacer ejercicio y beber agua.

:br$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ ¿C$\text{\'{o}}$mo se puede prevenir la gripe?

* A.$\hspace{1em}\text{Lav\'{a}ndose}$ las manos cada d$\text{\'{\i}}$a.

* C.$\hspace{1em}\text{Poni\'{e}ndose}$ la vacuna cada a$\text{\~{n}}$o.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Las respuestas pueden estar en cualquier orden.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, D, F}$        [3 marks]

* **A**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la gripe es una enfermedad muy com$\text{\'{u}}$n en Espa$\text{\~{n}}$a, especialmente en invierno" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$flu is a very common illness in Spain, especially in winter"). This tells us flu is particularly frequent in Spain during winter.

* **D**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$si los s$\text{\'{\i}}$ntomas duran m$\text{\'{a}}$s de una semana, debe consultar a un m$\text{\'{e}}$dico" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$if symptoms last more than a week, you should see a doctor"). Note that *m$\textit{\'{a}}$s de siete d$\textit{\'{\i}}$as* and *m$\textit{\'{a}}$s de una semana* have the same meaning. This tells us you should see a doctor if symptoms last over seven days.

* **F**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la mejor manera de prevenir la gripe es vacunarse cada a$\text{\~{n}}$o" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the best way to prevent flu is to get vaccinated every year"). This tells us annual vaccination is the best way to avoid flu.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* B, C, E

* **B**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$fiebre, dolor de cabeza, cansancio y tos" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$fever, headache, tiredness and cough") — flu causes multiple symptoms, not only fever.

* **C**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la gripe es m$\text{\'{a}}$s seria" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$flu is more serious") — they are not equally serious.

* **E**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$lo m$\text{\'{a}}$s importante es descansar" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the most important thing is to rest") — the doctor recommends rest, not exercise.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$        [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$si los s$\text{\'{\i}}$ntomas duran m$\text{\'{a}}$s de una semana, debe consultar a un m$\text{\'{e}}$dico" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$if symptoms last more than a week, you should see a doctor"). This tells us you should see a doctor if symptoms last more than a week.:br

* **B**: The audio says *m$\textit{\'{a}}$s de una semana* (more than a week), not two days (*dos d$\textit{\'{\i}}$as*).

* **C**: The audio mentions cough and headache as symptoms, not as reasons to visit a doctor.

This tells us resting and drinking fluids are the key recommendations for recovery.:br

Note that *poni$\textit{\'{e}}$ndose la vacuna cada a$\textit{\~{n}}$o* and *vacunarse cada a$\textit{\~{n}}$o* have the same meaning. This tells us annual vaccination is the best prevention.
