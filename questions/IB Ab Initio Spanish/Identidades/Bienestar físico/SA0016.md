## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{salud}$    [1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Dormir bastante es muy importante para la salud.” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Sleeping enough is very important for health"). This shows that sleeping enough helps people have good health.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{antes de acostarme}$    [1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Antes de acostarme, apago el m$\text{\'{o}}$vil y leo un libro.” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Before going to bed, I turn off my phone and read a book"). This shows the person stops using the phone and reads a book before going to bed.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{energ\'{\i}a}$[1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cuando duermo bien, tengo m$\text{\'{a}}$s energ$\text{\'{\i}}$a y me siento mejor.” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$When I sleep well, I have more energy and feel better"). The word*energ$\textit{\'{\i}}$a* means strength, which matches the meaning of *fuerza*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{me siento}$    [1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cuando duermo bien, tengo m$\text{\'{a}}$s energ$\text{\'{\i}}$a y me siento mejor.” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$When I sleep well, I have more energy and feel better"). The expression*me siento* means how someone feels, which matches the meaning of *me encuentro* (*I find myself*).

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{h\'{a}bito}$[1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Es un buen h$\text{\'{a}}$bito.” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$It is a good habit"). The word*h$\textit{\'{a}}$bito* means something people do regularly, which matches the meaning of *costumbre* (*custom*).
