## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *m$\textit{\'{e}}$dicos* (*doctors*) refers to professionals who treat people's health problems. In the text, it says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Los especialistas usan remedios naturales para ayudar a los pacientes.” The word *especialistas* refers to medical professionals, which matches the meaning of *m$\textit{\'{e}}$dicos*.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* B

* **B**: *profesores* (*teachers*) is not correct because it refers to people who teach at school, while the text talks about medical professionals who treat patients.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *tratamientos* (*treatments*) refers to ways to cure or improve health problems. In the text, it says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Los especialistas usan remedios naturales...” The word *remedios* refers to things used to treat illnesses, which matches the meaning of *tratamientos*.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* E

* **E**: *ingredientes* (*ingredients*) is not correct because it refers to things used to prepare food, while the text refers to *remedios naturales* used to treat health problems.
