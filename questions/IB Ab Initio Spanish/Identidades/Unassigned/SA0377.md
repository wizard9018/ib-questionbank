# SA0377

**Complete each question. Use the correct question word.**

$\hspace{1em}$¿**\[-1-]** agua bebes al $\text{d\'{\i}a}$? -Bebo un litro.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$¿**\[-2-]** guardas la fruta? -En el refrigerador.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$¿**\[-3-]** desayunas? -Por la $\text{ma\~{n}ana}$, antes de ir al colegio.    [1 mark]

**Put the words in the correct order. Write the complete sentence.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$comes / $\text{qu\'{e}}$ / ¿ / ?    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{qu\'{e}}$ / hambre / tienes / por / ¿ / ?    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Cuánta}$    [1 mark]

- *Cuánta* is correct. It means how much, and the answer gives an amount: *un litro*.

- *Cuánta* agrees with *agua*, which is feminine singular. *Agua* uses *el* (*el agua*) because it starts with a stressed *a*, but it is a feminine noun: *el agua fría*. *Cuánto* is incorrect.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Dónde}$    [1 mark]

- *Dónde* is correct. It means where, and the answer gives a place: *en el refrigerador*.

- Remember the written accent in *dónde*. Question words always have one. A missing accent does not cost the mark, but always write it.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Cuándo}$    [1 mark]

- *Cuándo* is correct. It means when, and the answer gives a time: *por la mañana, antes de ir al colegio*.

- *Cuánto* is incorrect. It means how much, but the answer gives a time, not an amount.

**Model answer**: ¿Cuánta agua bebes al día? -Bebo un litro. ¿Dónde guardas la fruta? -En el refrigerador. ¿Cuándo desayunas? -Por la mañana, antes de ir al colegio.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{¿Qué comes?}$    [1 mark]

- The question word goes at the start of the question, straight after *¿*: *¿Qué comes?*

- *Qué* means what, and it always has a written accent in a question.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{¿Por qué tienes hambre?}$    [1 mark]

- *Por qué* means why, and it goes at the start of the question: *¿Por qué tienes hambre?*

- In questions, *por qué* is written as two words, with an accent on *qué*. In the answer, *porque* (one word, no accent) means because: *Tengo hambre porque no he desayunado*.

**Model answer**: ¿Qué comes? ¿Por qué tienes hambre?
