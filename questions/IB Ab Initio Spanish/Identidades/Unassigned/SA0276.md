# SA0276

**Choose the correct form of the adjective.**

$\hspace{1em}$Mi hermana es muy **[-1-]**.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$simpático

- B.$\hspace{1em}$simpática

- C.$\hspace{1em}$simpáticos

- D.$\hspace{1em}$simpáticas

$\hspace{1em}$Mis padres son muy **[-2-]**.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$inteligente

- B.$\hspace{1em}$inteligentos

- C.$\hspace{1em}$inteligentes

- D.$\hspace{1em}$inteligento

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- *Simpática* is correct because *mi hermana* is feminine singular, so the adjective must also be feminine singular.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, C, D

- **A**: *Simpático* is masculine singular, but *mi hermana* is feminine.

- **C**: *Simpáticos* is masculine plural, but *mi hermana* is feminine singular.

- **D**: *Simpáticas* is feminine plural, but *mi hermana* refers to one person.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- *Inteligentes* is correct because *mis padres* is plural. Adjectives ending in *-e* have the same form for masculine and feminine. We only add *-s* for the plural.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, D

- **A**: *Inteligente* is singular, but *mis padres* is plural.

- **B**: *Inteligentos* does not exist. Adjectives ending in *-e* do not change to *-o* or *-os*.

- **D**: *Inteligento* does not exist. Adjectives ending in *-e* do not change to *-o*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- *Nueva* is correct because *la camiseta* is feminine singular, so the adjective must also be feminine singular.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** B, C, D, E, F

- **B**: *Nuevos* is masculine plural, but *la camiseta* is feminine singular.

- **C**: *Cómodos* is a form of *cómodo*, but the adjective in brackets is *nuevo*.

- **D**: *Cómodas* is a form of *cómodo*, but the adjective in brackets is *nuevo*.

- **E**: *Bonitas* is a form of *bonito*, but the adjective in brackets is *nuevo*.

- **F**: *Bonitos* is a form of *bonito*, but the adjective in brackets is *nuevo*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- *Cómodos* is correct because *los pantalones* is masculine plural, so the adjective must also be masculine plural.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, D, E, F

- **A**: *Nueva* is a form of *nuevo*, but the adjective in brackets is *cómodo*.

- **B**: *Nuevos* is a form of *nuevo*, but the adjective in brackets is *cómodo*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- *Bonitas* is correct because *las chaquetas* is feminine plural, so the adjective must also be feminine plural.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C, D, F

**Grammar reminder**:

ESTAR means to be.

Use *estar* for:

* Location: **Estoy* en Madrid*.

* Temporary states and emotions: **$\textit{Est\'{a}s}$* muy cansado hoy*.

* Ongoing actions: *Ella $\textit{est\'{a}}$ estudiando*.

*Below is the conjugation of estar:*

|Pronouns |*ESTAR*|

|:--|:--:|

|*Yo* |*estoy* |

|*Tú* |*estás* |

|*Él, ella, usted* |*está* |

|*Nosotros/as* |*estamos* |

|*Vosotros/as* |*estáis* |

|*Ellos, ellas, ustedes* |*están* |

Common mistake: Don't use *estar* for permanent characteristics, for example, **Estoy* española*. Use *ser* for nationality and identity.
