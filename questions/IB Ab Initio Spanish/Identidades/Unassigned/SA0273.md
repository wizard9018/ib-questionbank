# SA0273

**Each sentence contains $\underline{\textbf{one}}$ error with the present tense form of *ser* or *estar*. Find the error and write the correct form.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Yo $\text{est\'{a}}$ con mi familia.    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Mis padres $\text{est\'{a}}$ muy cansados.    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Nosotros es de Chile.    [1 mark]

**Put the words in the correct order. Begin with the subject and write the complete sentence.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$mis abuelos / en el $\text{jard\'{\i}n}$ / $\text{est\'{a}n}$    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$mis amigos / de Ecuador / son    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{estoy}$    [1 mark]

- *Está* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but the subject is (*yo*), so the correct form is *estoy*. We use *estar* to express location.\

\

**Model answer**: Yo estoy con mi familia.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{est\'{a}n}$    [1 mark]

- *Está* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but *mis padres* refers to (*ellos*), so the correct form is *están*. We use *estar* to express how someone feels.\

\

**Model answer**: Mis padres están muy cansados.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{somos}$    [1 mark]

- *Es* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but the subject is (*nosotros*), so the correct form is *somos*. The verb *ser* is correct here because we use *ser* with *de* to express origin. Only the form needs to change.\

\

**Model answer**: Nosotros somos de Chile.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Mis abuelos est\'{a}n en el jard\'{\i}n.}$    [1 mark]

- *Mis abuelos* is the subject and *están* is the verb. The location *en el jardín* comes after the verb.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Mis amigos son de Ecuador.}$    [1 mark]

- *Mis amigos* is the subject and *son* is the verb. The origin *de Ecuador* comes after the verb.
