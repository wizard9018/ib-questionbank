# SA0280

**Choose the correct present tense form of the verb.**

$\hspace{1em}$Mi hermana y yo **[-1-]** español en casa.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$habla

- B.$\hspace{1em}$hablamos

- C.$\hspace{1em}$hablan

- D.$\hspace{1em}$hablas

$\hspace{1em}$Mis padres **[-2-]** música juntos.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$escucha

- B.$\hspace{1em}$escucháis

- C.$\hspace{1em}$escuchan

- D.$\hspace{1em}$escuchamos

**Match each sentence to the correct present tense form of the verb. There are more options than sentences.** [3 marks]

|Sentence|Options|

|:--|:--|

|Yo **[-3-]** a mi abuela por teléfono.|A.$\hspace{1em}$ayudas|

|Tú **[-4-]** a tu hermano con la tarea.|B.$\hspace{1em}$llaman|

|Ellos **[-5-]** muchos lugares durante las vacaciones.$\hspace{3em}$|C.$\hspace{1em}$visito|

| |D.$\hspace{1em}$llamo|

| |E.$\hspace{1em}$visitan|

| |F.$\hspace{1em}$ayuda|

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- *Hablamos* is correct because *mi hermana y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*). The (*nosotros/as*) form of *hablar* is *hablamos*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, C, D

- **A**: *Habla* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but *mi hermana y yo* refers to (*nosotros*).

- **C**: *Hablan* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but *mi hermana y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*).

- **D**: *Hablas* is the (*tú*) form, but *mi hermana y yo* refers to (*nosotros*).

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- *Escuchan* is correct because *mis padres* refers to (*ellos*). The (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *escuchar* is *escuchan*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, D

- **A**: *Escucha* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but *mis padres* refers to more than one person.

- **B**: *Escucháis* is the (*vosotros/as*) form, but *mis padres* refers to (*ellos*).

- **D**: *Escuchamos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but *mis padres* does not include the speaker.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- *Llamo* is correct because the pronoun is (*yo*) and *por teléfono* shows that the action is calling. The (*yo*) form of *llamar* is *llamo*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C, E, F

- **A**: *Ayudas* is the (*tú*) form of *ayudar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*yo*).

- **B**: *Llaman* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *llamar*, but the pronoun is (*yo*).

- **C**: *Visito* is the (*yo*) form of *visitar*, but *por teléfono* shows that the action is calling, not visiting.

- **E**: *Visitan* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *visitar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*yo*).

- **F**: *Ayuda* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form of *ayudar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*yo*).

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- *Ayudas* is correct because the pronoun is (*tú*) and *con la tarea* shows that the action is helping. The (*tú*) form of *ayudar* is *ayudas*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** B, C, D, E, F

- **B**: *Llaman* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *llamar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

- **C**: *Visito* is the (*yo*) form of *visitar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

- **D**: *Llamo* is the (*yo*) form of *llamar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

- **E**: *Visitan* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *visitar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

- **F**: *Ayuda* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form of *ayudar*, but the pronoun is (*tú*).

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- *Visitan* is correct because the pronoun is (*ellos*) and *muchos lugares durante las vacaciones* shows that the action is visiting. The (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *visitar* is *visitan*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C, D, F

- **A**: *Ayudas* is the (*tú*) form of *ayudar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*ellos*).

- **B**: *Llaman* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *llamar*, but *muchos lugares durante las vacaciones* shows that the action is visiting, not calling.

- **C**: *Visito* is the (*yo*) form of *visitar*, but the pronoun is (*ellos*).

- **D**: *Llamo* is the (*yo*) form of *llamar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*ellos*).

- **F**: *Ayuda* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form of *ayudar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*ellos*).
