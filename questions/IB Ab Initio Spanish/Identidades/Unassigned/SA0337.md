# SA0337

**Complete each sentence with the correct possessive adjective. The pronoun in brackets shows who the owner is.** 

$\hspace{1em}$Mi hermano y yo comemos con **[-1-]** abuelos los domingos. (nosotros)    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Tú visitas a **[-2-]** abuelos los fines de semana. (tú)    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Julia y Mateo viven con **[-3-]** madre. (ellos)    [1 mark]

**Each sentence below contains one possessive adjective error. The error is underlined. Write the correct word.**

$\hspace{1em}$Nosotros tenemos una prima en Chile. $\underline{\text{Nuestro}}$ prima se llama Valeria. **[-4-]**    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Ustedes tienen dos amigos en el barrio. $\underline{\text{Su}}$ amigos son muy simpáticos. **[-5-]**    [1 mark]

**Put the words in the correct order. Write the complete sentence.**    [1 mark]

6.$\hspace{1em}$hermanos / son / Nuestros / altos / muy

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{nuestros}$    [1 mark]

- *Nuestros* is correct because the owner is *nosotros* (*mi hermano y yo*) and *abuelos* is masculine and plural. With *nosotros/as*, the possessive changes for gender and number, so the form is *nuestros*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{tus}$    [1 mark]

- *Tus* is correct because the owner is *tú* and *abuelos* is plural. With *tú* and more than one thing, the possessive is *tus*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{su}$    [1 mark]

- *Su* is correct because the owner is *Julia y Mateo* (*ellos*) and *madre* is one person. With *ellos* and one thing, the possessive is *su*.

- There are two owners, but the possessive agrees with *madre*, not with the owners, so *sus* is not correct.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Nuestra}$    [1 mark]

- *Nuestro* must change to *Nuestra* because *prima* is feminine and singular. The form agrees with *prima*, not with *nosotros*.

- **Model answer:** Nosotros tenemos una prima en Chile. *Nuestra* prima se llama Valeria.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Sus}$    [1 mark]

- *Su* must change to *Sus* because *amigos* is plural. With *ustedes*, the possessive is *su* for one thing and *sus* for more than one thing, the same as with *ellos* and *ellas*.

- **Model answer:** Ustedes tienen dos amigos en el barrio. *Sus* amigos son muy simpáticos.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Nuestros hermanos son muy altos.}$    [1 mark]

- You receive one mark for putting all the words in the correct order to write a complete sentence.

- The possessive *nuestros* always goes directly before the noun *hermanos*. *Muy* goes before the adjective *altos*.
