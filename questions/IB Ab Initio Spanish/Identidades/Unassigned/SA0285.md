# SA0285

**Put the words in the correct order and write the verb in the correct present tense form. Begin with the subject and write the complete sentence.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$las etiquetas de los alimentos / mi hermana / (*leer*)    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$dulces / mis padres / no / antes de la cena / (*permitir*)    [1 mark]

**Each sentence contains $\underline{\textbf{one}}$ verb error. Find the error and write the correct present tense form.**

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Yo vendes fruta en el mercado los sábados.    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Vosotros abren los regalos de vuestros amigos en la fiesta.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Mi hermana lee las etiquetas de los alimentos.}$    [1 mark]

- *Mi hermana* refers to (*ella*). With (*ella*), regular *-er* verbs end in *-e*, so the correct form of *leer* is *lee*.

- The sentence begins with the subject *mi hermana*, followed by the verb *lee*. The object *las etiquetas de los alimentos* comes after the verb.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Mis padres no permiten dulces antes de la cena.}$    [1 mark]

- *Mis padres* refers to (*ellos*). With (*ellos*), regular *-ir* verbs end in *-en*, so the correct form of *permitir* is *permiten*.

- The sentence begins with the subject *mis padres*. *No* goes directly before the verb *permiten*. The object *dulces* and the time expression *antes de la cena* come after the verb.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{vendo}$    [1 mark]

- *Vendes* is the (*tú*) form, but the subject is (*yo*). With (*yo*), regular *-er* verbs end in *-o*, so the correct form is *vendo*.\

\

**Model answer**: Yo vendo fruta en el mercado los sábados.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{abr\'{\i}s}$    [1 mark]

- *Abren* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but the subject is (*vosotros*). With (*vosotros*), regular *-ir* verbs end in *-ís*, so the correct form is *abrís*. The word *vuestros* also confirms the (*vosotros*) form.

- The correct spelling is *abrís*, with an accent on the *í*. The mark is not lost for an accent error, but it is important to learn the correct spelling.\

\

**Model answer**: Vosotros abrís los regalos de vuestros amigos en la fiesta.
