# SA0284

**Choose the correct present tense form of the verb.**

$\hspace{1em}$Marta **[-1-]** con su familia.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$vivo

- B.$\hspace{1em}$vives

- C.$\hspace{1em}$vive

- D.$\hspace{1em}$vivimos

$\hspace{1em}$Mis amigos **[-2-]** a cocinar platos sanos.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$aprendemos

- B.$\hspace{1em}$aprenden

- C.$\hspace{1em}$aprende

- D.$\hspace{1em}$aprendes

**Match each sentence to the correct present tense form of the verb. There are more options than sentences.** [3 marks]

|Sentence|Options|

|:--|:--|

|Yo **[-3-]** mensajes a mi prima.|A.$\hspace{1em}$comes|

|Tú **[-4-]** mucha fruta.|B.$\hspace{1em}$escriben|

|Mis hermanos **[-5-]** zumo en el desayuno.$\hspace{3em}$|C.$\hspace{1em}$bebemos|

| |D.$\hspace{1em}$escribo|

| |E.$\hspace{1em}$beben|

| |F.$\hspace{1em}$come|

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- *Vive* is correct because *Marta* refers to (*ella*). With (*ella*), regular *-ir* verbs end in *-e*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, D

- **A**: *Vivo* is the (*yo*) form, but *Marta* refers to (*ella*).

- **B**: *Vives* is the (*tú*) form, but *Marta* refers to (*ella*).

- **D**: *Vivimos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but *Marta* refers to (*ella*).

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- *Aprenden* is correct because *mis amigos* refers to (*ellos*). With (*ellos*), regular *-er* verbs end in *-en*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, C, D

- **A**: *Aprendemos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but *mis amigos* does not include the speaker.

- **C**: *Aprende* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but *mis amigos* refers to more than one person.

- **D**: *Aprendes* is the (*tú*) form, but *mis amigos* refers to (*ellos*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- *Escribo* is correct because the subject is (*yo*) and *mensajes* shows that the action is writing. With (*yo*), regular *-ir* verbs end in *-o*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C, E, F

- **A**: *Comes* is the (*tú*) form of *comer*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*yo*).

- **B**: *Escriben* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *escribir*, but the subject is (*yo*).

- **C**: *Bebemos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form of *beber*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*yo*).

- **E**: *Beben* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *beber*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*yo*).

- **F**: *Come* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form of *comer*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*yo*).

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- *Comes* is correct because the subject is (*tú*) and *mucha fruta* shows that the action is eating. With (*tú*), regular *-er* verbs end in *-es*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** B, C, D, E, F

- **B**: *Escriben* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *escribir*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

- **C**: *Bebemos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form of *beber*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

- **D**: *Escribo* is the (*yo*) form of *escribir*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

- **E**: *Beben* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *beber*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

- **F**: *Come* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form of *comer*, but the subject is (*tú*).

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- *Beben* is correct because *mis hermanos* refers to (*ellos*) and *zumo* shows that the action is drinking. With (*ellos*), regular *-er* verbs end in *-en*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C, D, F

- **A**: *Comes* is the (*tú*) form of *comer*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*ellos*).

- **B**: *Escriben* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *escribir*, but *zumo* shows that the action is drinking, not writing.

- **C**: *Bebemos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form of *beber*, but *mis hermanos* does not include the speaker.

- **D**: *Escribo* is the (*yo*) form of *escribir*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*ellos*).

- **F**: *Come* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form of *comer*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*ellos*).

**Grammar reminder**:

In Spanish, regular *-er* and *-ir* verbs change their ending depending on the subject. To conjugate them, remove *-er* or *-ir* from the infinitive and add the correct ending.

|Subject pronoun|*-er* ending|Example: *leer*|*-ir* ending|Example: *abrir*|

|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|

|*yo*|*-o*|*leo*|*-o*|*abro*|

|*tú*|*-es*|*lees*|*-es*|*abres*|

|*él / ella / usted*|*-e*|*lee*|*-e*|*abre*|

|*nosotros/as*|*-emos*|*leemos*|*-imos*|*abrimos*|

|*vosotros/as*|*-éis*|*leéis*|*-ís*|*abrís*|

|*ellos / ellas / ustedes*|*-en*|*leen*|*-en*|*abren*|
