# SA0288

**Choose the correct present tense form of *tener*.**

$\hspace{1em}$Las gemelas **[-1-]** doce años.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$tengo

- B.$\hspace{1em}$tienes

- C.$\hspace{1em}$tiene

- D.$\hspace{1em}$tienen

$\hspace{1em}$Yo siempre **[-2-]** hambre por la mañana.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$tenemos

- B.$\hspace{1em}$tengo

- C.$\hspace{1em}$tiene

- D.$\hspace{1em}$tienes

|Sentence|Options|

|:--|:--|

|Mi familia y yo **[-3-]** el pelo oscuro.|A.$\hspace{1em}$tienes|

|Tú **[-4-]** mucha imaginación.|B.$\hspace{1em}$tenemos|

|Mi abuelo **[-5-]** la barba blanca.$\hspace{3em}$|C.$\hspace{1em}$tienen|

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- *Tienen* is correct because *las gemelas* refers to more than one person (*ellas*). We use *tener* to talk about age.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C

- **A**: *Tengo* is the (*yo*) form, but *las gemelas* refers to (*ellas*).

- **B**: *Tienes* is the (*tú*) form, but *las gemelas* refers to (*ellas*).

- **C**: *Tiene* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but *las gemelas* refers to more than one person.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- *Tengo* is correct because the subject is (*yo*). *Hambre* is a state, and in Spanish we use *tener* to say that we are hungry.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, C, D

- **A**: *Tenemos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but the subject is (*yo*).

- **C**: *Tiene* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but the subject is (*yo*).

- **D**: *Tienes* is the (*tú*) form, but the subject is (*yo*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- *Tenemos* is correct because *mi familia y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*).

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, C, D, E, F

- **A**: *Tienes* is the (*tú*) form, but *mi familia y yo* refers to (*nosotros*).

- **C**: *Tienen* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but *mi familia y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*).

- **D**: *Tiene* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but *mi familia y yo* refers to more than one person.

- **E**: *Tengo* is the (*yo*) form, but *mi familia y yo* refers to (*nosotros*).

- **F**: *Tenéis* is the (*vosotros/as*) form, but *mi familia y yo* refers to (*nosotros*).

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- *Tienes* is correct because the subject is (*tú*).

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** B, C, D, E, F

- **B**: *Tenemos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but the subject is (*tú*).

- **C**: *Tienen* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but the subject is (*tú*).

- **D**: *Tiene* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but the subject is (*tú*).

- **E**: *Tengo* is the (*yo*) form, but the subject is (*tú*).

- **F**: *Tenéis* is the (*vosotros/as*) form, but the subject is (*tú*).

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- *Tiene* is correct because *mi abuelo* refers to (*él*). We use *tener* to describe physical traits.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C, E, F

- **A**: *Tienes* is the (*tú*) form, but *mi abuelo* refers to (*él*).

- **B**: *Tenemos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but *mi abuelo* refers to one person.

- **C**: *Tienen* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but *mi abuelo* refers to one person.

- **E**: *Tengo* is the (*yo*) form, but *mi abuelo* refers to (*él*).

- **F**: *Tenéis* is the (*vosotros/as*) form, but *mi abuelo* refers to (*él*).

**Grammar reminder**:

*Tener* means "to have". It is an irregular verb, so learn its forms by heart.

|Pronoun|*tener*|

|:--|:--|

|(*yo*)|*tengo*|

|(*tú*)|*tienes*|

|(*él/ella/usted*)|*tiene*|

|(*nosotros/as*)|*tenemos*|

|(*vosotros/as*)|*tenéis*|

|(*ellos/ellas/ustedes*)|*tienen*|

**When do we use *tener*?**

|Use|Example|

|:--|:--|

|Age|*Mi tía tiene cuarenta años.* (My aunt is forty.)|

|Physical traits|*Tienes el pelo rizado.* / *Mis primas tienen los ojos azules.*|

|Personality traits|*Mi profesora tiene mucha paciencia.*|

|States|*Tenemos sueño.* / *¿Tienes frío?*|

**Remember:**

- In Spanish, age goes with *tener*, not *ser*. Say *Tengo quince años*, not *Soy quince años*.

- For hair and eyes, use *el*, *la*, *los* or *las*: *tiene el pelo rizado*, *tienen los ojos azules*.

- Check who the subject is. One person (*mi tía*, *mi profesora*) takes *tiene*. Two or more people (*mis primas*, *mis tíos*) take *tienen*. If the subject includes you (*mis amigos y yo*), use *tenemos*.
