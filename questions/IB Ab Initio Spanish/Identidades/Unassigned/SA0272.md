# SA0272

**Match each sentence to the correct present tense form of *ser* or *estar*. There are more options than sentences.** [3 marks]

|Sentence|Options|

|:--|:--|

|Yo **[-1-]** muy alto.|A.$\hspace{1em}$estoy|

|Tú **[-2-]** en la biblioteca.$\hspace{3em}$|B.$\hspace{1em}$eres|

|Ellos **[-3-]** de México.|C.$\hspace{1em}$son|

| |D.$\hspace{1em}$están|

| |E.$\hspace{1em}$estás|

| |F.$\hspace{1em}$soy|

**Choose the correct present tense form of *ser* or *estar*.**

$\hspace{1em}$Mi hermana y yo **[-4-]** muy cansadas.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$soy

- B.$\hspace{1em}$estamos

- C.$\hspace{1em}$estoy

- D.$\hspace{1em}$somos

$\hspace{1em}$Mis abuelos **[-5-]** en México.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$son

- B.$\hspace{1em}$sois

- C.$\hspace{1em}$están

- D.$\hspace{1em}$está

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

- *Soy* is correct because the pronoun is (*yo*) and *muy alto* describes a physical characteristic. We use *ser* to describe characteristics.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C, D, E

- **A**: *Estoy* is the (*yo*) form of *estar*, but *muy alto* describes a characteristic, so we use *ser*.

- **B**: *Eres* is the (*tú*) form of *ser*, but the pronoun is (*yo*).

- **C**: *Son* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *ser*, but the pronoun is (*yo*).

- **D**: *Están* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *estar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*yo*).

- **E**: *Estás* is the (*tú*) form of *estar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*yo*).

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- *Estás* is correct because the pronoun is (*tú*) and *en la biblioteca* expresses location. We use *estar* to express location.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C, D, F

- **A**: *Estoy* is the (*yo*) form of *estar*, but the pronoun is (*tú*).

- **B**: *Eres* is the (*tú*) form of *ser*, but *en la biblioteca* expresses location, so we use *estar*.

- **C**: *Son* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *ser*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

- **D**: *Están* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *estar*, but the pronoun is (*tú*).

- **F**: *Soy* is the (*yo*) form of *ser*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- *Son* is correct because the pronoun is (*ellos*) and *de México* expresses origin. We use *ser* with *de* to express origin.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, D, E, F

- **A**: *Estoy* is the (*yo*) form of *estar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*ellos*).

- **B**: *Eres* is the (*tú*) form of *ser*, but the pronoun is (*ellos*).

- **D**: *Están* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *estar*, but *de México* expresses origin, so we use *ser*.

- **E**: *Estás* is the (*tú*) form of *estar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*ellos*).

- **F**: *Soy* is the (*yo*) form of *ser*, but the pronoun is (*ellos*).

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- *Estamos* is correct because *mi hermana y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotras*), and *muy cansadas* describes how they feel. We use *estar* to express how someone feels.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, C, D

- **A**: *Soy* is the (*yo*) form of *ser*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*nosotras*).

- **C**: *Estoy* is the (*yo*) form of *estar*, but *mi hermana y yo* refers to more than one person.

- **D**: *Somos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form of *ser*, but *muy cansadas* describes how they feel, so we use *estar*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- *Están* is correct because *mis abuelos* refers to (*ellos*) and *en México* expresses location. We use *estar* to express location.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, D

- **A**: *Son* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *ser*, but *en México* expresses location, so we use *estar*.

- **B**: *Sois* is the (*vosotros/as*) form of *ser*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*ellos*).

- **D**: *Está* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form of *estar*, but *mis abuelos* refers to more than one person.
