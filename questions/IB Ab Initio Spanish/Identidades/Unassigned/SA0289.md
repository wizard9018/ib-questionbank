# SA0289

**Complete the paragraph with the correct present tense form of *tener*.** [4 marks]

Mi hermana **[-1-]** $\text{diecis\'{e}is}$ años y yo **[-2-]** quince años. Mis padres **[-3-]** el pelo rizado, pero mi hermano y yo **[-4-]** el pelo liso.

**Put the words in the correct order and write the verb in the correct present tense form. Begin with the subject and write the complete sentence.**

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$buen / sentido / del / humor / mi amigo / (*tener*)    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{tiene}$    [1 mark]

- *Mi hermana* refers to (*ella*), so the correct form is *tiene*. We use *tener* to talk about age.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{tengo}$    [1 mark]

- The subject is (*yo*), so the correct form is *tengo*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{tienen}$    [1 mark]

- *Mis padres* refers to (*ellos*), so the correct form is *tienen*. We use *tener* to describe physical traits.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{tenemos}$    [1 mark]

- *Mi hermano y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*), so the correct form is *tenemos*.\

\

**Model answer**: Mi hermana tiene dieciséis años y yo tengo quince años. Mis padres tienen el pelo rizado, pero mi hermano y yo tenemos el pelo liso.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Mi amigo tiene buen sentido del humor.}$    [1 mark]

- *Mi amigo* refers to (*él*), so the correct form of *tener* is *tiene*.

- The sentence begins with the subject *mi amigo*, followed by the verb *tiene*. *Buen sentido del humor* is a fixed expression and comes after the verb.
