# SA0269

**Match each sentence with the correct present tense form of *estar*. There are more options than sentences.** [3 marks]

|Sentence|Options|

|:--|:--|

|Lucía y Carla **[-1-]** muy contentas hoy.|A.$\hspace{1em}$están|

|Mis amigos y yo **[-2-]** en la playa.|B.$\hspace{1em}$estás|

|Alejandro **[-3-]** preocupado por el vuelo.$\hspace{3em}$|C.$\hspace{1em}$estáis|

| |D.$\hspace{1em}$estoy|

| |E.$\hspace{1em}$estamos|

| |F.$\hspace{1em}$está|

**Put the words in the correct order. Write the full sentence.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$están / Panamá / de / Mis / vacaciones / en / padres    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- *Están* is correct because *Lucía y Carla* refers to (*ellas*). The corresponding form of *estar* is *están*. We use *estar* to express how someone feels.

- *Respuestas incorrectas:* B, C, D, E, F

- **B**: *Estás* is the (*tú*) form, but *Lucía y Carla* refers to (*ellas*).

- **C**: *Estáis* is the (*vosotros/as*) form, but *Lucía y Carla* refers to (*ellas*).

- **D**: *Estoy* is the (*yo*) form, but *Lucía y Carla* refers to (*ellas*).

- **E**: *Estamos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but *Lucía y Carla* does not include the speaker.

- **F**: *Está* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but *Lucía y Carla* refers to more than one person.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- *Estamos* is correct because *mis amigos y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*). The corresponding form of *estar* is *estamos*. We use *estar* to express location.

- *Respuestas  incorrectas:* A, B, C, D, F

- **A**: *Están* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but *mis amigos y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*).

- **B**: *Estás* is the (*tú*) form, but *mis amigos y yo* refers to (*nosotros*).

- **C**: *Estáis* is the (*vosotros/as*) form, but *mis amigos y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*).

- **D**: *Estoy* is the (*yo*) form, but *mis amigos y yo* refers to more than one person.

- **F**: *Está* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but *mis amigos y yo* refers to (*nosotros*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

- *Está* is correct because *Alejandro* refers to (*él*). The corresponding form of *estar* is *está*. We use *estar* to express how someone feels.

- *Respuestas incorrectas:* A, B, C, D, E

- **A**: *Están* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but *Alejandro* refers to one person.

- **B**: *Estás* is the (*tú*) form, but *Alejandro* refers to (*él*).

- **C**: *Estáis* is the (*vosotros/as*) form, but *Alejandro* refers to (*él*).

- **D**: *Estoy* is the (*yo*) form, but *Alejandro* refers to (*él*).

- **E**: *Estamos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but *Alejandro* refers to (*él*).

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Mis padres est\'{a}n de vacaciones en Panam\'{a}.}$    [1 mark]

- *Mis padres* is the subject and *están* is the verb. The expressions *de vacaciones* and *en Panamá* come after the verb.

- *Mis padres están en Panamá de vacaciones* is also correct.
