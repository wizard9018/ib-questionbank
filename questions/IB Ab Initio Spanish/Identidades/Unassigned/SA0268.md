## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- *Estamos* is correct because the pronoun is (*nosotros*). The corresponding form of *estar* is *estamos*. We use *estar* to express location.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, D

- **A**: *Estoy* is the (*yo*) form, but the pronoun is (*nosotros*).

- **B**: *Estás* is the (*tú*) form, but the pronoun is (*nosotros*).

- **D**: *Están* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but the pronoun is (*nosotros*).

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- *Estoy* is correct because the pronoun is (*yo*). The corresponding form of *estar* is *estoy*. We use *estar* to express location.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, C, D

- **A**: *Estamos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but the pronoun is (*yo*).

- **C**: *Está* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but the pronoun is (*yo*).

- **D**: *Están* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but the pronoun is (*yo*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- *Está* is correct because the pronoun is (*ella*). The corresponding form of *estar* is *está*. We use *estar* to express location.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, D, E, F

- **A**: *Estar* is the infinitive, not a conjugated form.

- **B**: *Estás* is the (*tú*) form, but the pronoun is (*ella*).

- **D**: *Estoy* is the (*yo*) form, but the pronoun is (*ella*).

- **E**: *Estamos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but the pronoun is (*ella*).

- **F**: *Están* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but (*ella*) refers to one person.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- *Estás* is correct because the pronoun is (*tú*). The corresponding form of *estar* is *estás*. We use *estar* to express location.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, C, D, E, F

- **A**: *Estar* is the infinitive, not a conjugated form.

- **C**: *Está* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but the pronoun is (*tú*).

- **D**: *Estoy* is the (*yo*) form, but the pronoun is (*tú*).

- **E**: *Estamos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but the pronoun is (*tú*).

- **F**: *Están* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but the pronoun is (*tú*).

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

- *Están* is correct because the pronoun is (*ellos*). The corresponding form of *estar* is *están*. We use *estar* to express location.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C, D, E

- **A**: *Estar* is the infinitive, not a conjugated form.

- **B**: *Estás* is the (*tú*) form, but the pronoun is (*ellos*).

- **C**: *Está* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but (*ellos*) refers to more than one person.

- **D**: *Estoy* is the (*yo*) form, but the pronoun is (*ellos*).

- **E**: *Estamos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but the pronoun is (*ellos*).

**Grammar reminder**:

ESTAR means to be.

Use *estar* for:

- Location: **Estoy* en Madrid*.

- Temporary states and emotions: **Estás* muy cansado hoy*.

- Ongoing actions: *Ella está estudiando*.

*Below is the conjugation of estar:*

|$\hspace{1em}$ | $\textrm{ESTAR}$ $\hspace{1em}$ |

|:--|:--|

|$\hspace{1em}$*Yo* | $\hspace{1em}$*estoy*|

|$\hspace{1em}$*Tú* | $\hspace{1em}$*estás* |

|$\hspace{1em}$Él, ella, usted | $\hspace{1em}$está |

|$\hspace{1em}$Nosotros/as |estamos $\hspace{1em}$ |

|$\hspace{1em}$Vosotros/as |estáis $\hspace{1em}$ |

|$\hspace{1em}$Ellos, ellas, ustedes$\hspace{1em}$ |*están* $\hspace{1em}$ |

Common mistake: Don't use *estar* for permanent characteristics, for example, **Estoy* española*. Use *ser* for nationality and identity.
