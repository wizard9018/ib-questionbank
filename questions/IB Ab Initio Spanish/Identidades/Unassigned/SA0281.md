# SA0281

**Put the words in the correct order and write the verb in the correct present tense form. Begin with the subject and write the complete sentence.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$en el parque / mis amigos y yo / (*caminar*)    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$mucha agua / Carlos / (*tomar*)    [1 mark]

**Each sentence contains $\underline{\textbf{one}}$ verb error. Find the error and write the correct present tense form.**

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Yo desayunas fruta todos los días.    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Tú cocina pasta los viernes.    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Mis padres compramos fruta en el mercado.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Mis amigos y yo caminamos en el parque.}$    [1 mark]

- *Mis amigos y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*). The (*nosotros/as*) form of *caminar* is *caminamos*.

- The sentence begins with the subject *mis amigos y yo*, followed by the verb *caminamos*. The place *en el parque* comes after the verb.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Carlos toma mucha agua.}$    [1 mark]

- *Carlos* refers to (*él*). The (*él/ella/usted*) form of *tomar* is *toma*.

- The sentence begins with the subject *Carlos*, followed by the verb *toma*. The object *mucha agua* comes after the verb.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{desayuno}$    [1 mark]

- *Desayunas* is the (*tú*) form, but the subject is (*yo*), so the correct form is *desayuno*.\

\

**Model answer**: Yo desayuno fruta todos los días.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{cocinas}$    [1 mark]

- *Cocina* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but the subject is (*tú*), so the correct form is *cocinas*.\

\

**Model answer**: Tú cocinas pasta los viernes.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{compran}$    [1 mark]

- *Compramos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but *mis padres* refers to (*ellos*), so the correct form is *compran*. *Mis padres* does not include the speaker.\

\

**Model answer**: Mis padres compran fruta en el mercado.
