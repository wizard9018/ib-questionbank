# SA0360

**Choose the correct conditional verb form.**

$\hspace{1em}$Yo **\[-1-]** $\text{m\'{a}s}$ fruta.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}\text{comer\'{\i}as}$

* B.$\hspace{1em}\text{comer\'{\i}a}$

* C.$\hspace{1em}\text{comir\'{\i}a}$

* D.$\hspace{1em}\text{comer\'{\i}an}$

$\hspace{1em}$Mi hermana **\[-2-]** yoga.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}\text{practicar\'{\i}an}$

* B.$\hspace{1em}\text{practicar\'{\i}as}$

* C.$\hspace{1em}\text{practic\'{\i}a}$

* D.$\hspace{1em}\text{practicar\'{\i}a}$

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* With *yo*, add *$\textit{-\'{\i}a}$* to the infinitive *comer*: *$\textit{comer\'{\i}a}$*.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C, D

* **A**: *$\textit{Comer\'{\i}as}$* is the *$\textit{t\'{u}}$* form, but the subject is *yo*.

* **C**: *$\textit{Comir\'{\i}a}$* changes the stem of *comer*. The conditional keeps the full infinitive: *comer* + *$\textit{-\'{\i}a}$*.

* **D**: *$\textit{Comer\'{\i}an}$* is the *ellos/ellas/ustedes* form, but the subject is *yo*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *Mi hermana* refers to *ella*. Add *$\textit{-\'{\i}a}$* to *practicar*: *$\textit{practicar\'{\i}a}$*.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B, C

* **A**: *$\textit{Practicar\'{\i}an}$* is the *ellos/ellas/ustedes* form, but *mi hermana* refers to *ella*.

* **B**: *$\textit{Practicar\'{\i}as}$* is the *$\textit{t\'{u}}$* form, but *mi hermana* refers to *ella*.

* **C**: *$\textit{Practic\'{\i}a}$* drops *-ar* from *practicar*. The conditional keeps the full infinitive.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is *yo*. The conditional form of *beber* is *$\textit{beber\'{\i}a}$*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is *$\textit{t\'{u}}$*. Add *$\textit{-\'{\i}as}$* to *dormir*: *$\textit{dormir\'{\i}as}$*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is *nosotros/as*. Add *$\textit{-\'{\i}amos}$* to *descansar*: *$\textit{descansar\'{\i}amos}$*.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C, D

* **A**: *$\textit{dormir\'{\i}a}$* does not agree with *$\textit{t\'{u}}$* in sentence 4.

* **C**: *$\textit{beber\'{\i}as}$* does not agree with *yo* in sentence 3.

* **D**: *$\textit{descansar\'{\i}an}$* does not agree with *nosotros/as* in sentence 5.

**Grammar reminder**

The conditional expresses what someone **would do**. It also expresses wishes *me $\textit{gustar\'{\i}a}$* and advice *$\textit{deber\'{\i}as}$*.

| $\hspace{1em}$Rule | $\hspace{1em}$Forms / Examples |

| :- | :- |

| $\hspace{1em}$For regular **-ar**, **-er** and **-ir** verbs, keep the infinitive and add the ending. | $\hspace{1em}$(yo) **$\textbf{-\'{\i}a}$**; ($\text{t\'{u}}$) **$\textbf{-\'{\i}as}$**; ($\text{\'{e}l/ella/usted}$) **$\textbf{-\'{\i}a}$**; (nosotros/as) **$\textbf{-\'{\i}amos}$**; (ellos/ellas/ustedes) **$\textbf{-\'{\i}an}$** |

| $\hspace{1em}$Some verbs change their stem but use the same endings. | $\hspace{1em}$*tener: $\textit{tendr\'{\i}a}$; poder: $\textit{podr\'{\i}a}$; hacer: $\textit{har\'{\i}a}$; querer: $\textit{querr\'{\i}a}$; salir: $\textit{saldr\'{\i}a}$; poner: $\textit{pondr\'{\i}a}$; venir: $\textit{vendr\'{\i}a}$; decir: $\textit{dir\'{\i}a}$* |

| $\hspace{1em}$Common mistake: removing **-ar**, **-er** or **-ir**. | $\hspace{1em}$Use *$\textit{comer\'{\i}a}$*, not *$\textit{com\'{\i}a}$*. |
