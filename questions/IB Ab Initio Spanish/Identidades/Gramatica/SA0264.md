## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- *Soy* is correct because the pronoun is (*yo*). The corresponding form of *ser* is *soy*. We use *ser* to say what someone is.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, C, D

- **A**: *Es* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but the pronoun is (*yo*).

- **C**: *Eres* is the (*tú*) form, but the pronoun is (*yo*).

- **D**: *Son* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but the pronoun is (*yo*).

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- *Somos* is correct because *mi hermano y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*). We use *ser* with *de* to express origin.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, D

- **A**: *Son* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but *mi hermano y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*).

- **B**: *Sois* is the (*vosotros/as*) form, but *mi hermano y yo* refers to (*nosotros*).

- **D**: *Soy* is the (*yo*) form, but *mi hermano y yo* refers to more than one person.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- *Eres* is correct because the pronoun is (*tú*). The corresponding form of *ser* is *eres*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, C, D, E, F

- **A**: *Somos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but the pronoun is (*tú*).

- **C**: *Ser* is the infinitive, not a conjugated form.

- **D**: *Es* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but the pronoun is (*tú*).

- **E**: *Son* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but the pronoun is (*tú*).

- **F**: *Soy* is the (*yo*) form, but the pronoun is (*tú*).

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- *Es* is correct because the pronouns are (*él/ella*). The corresponding form of *ser* is *es*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C, E, F

- **A**: *Somos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but the pronouns are (*él/ella*).

- **B**: *Eres* is the (*tú*) form, but the pronouns are (*él/ella*).

- **C**: *Ser* is the infinitive, not a conjugated form.

- **E**: *Son* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but (*él/ella*) refers to one person.

- **F**: *Soy* is the (*yo*) form, but the pronouns are (*él/ella*).

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- *Son* is correct because the pronouns are (*ellos/ellas*). The corresponding form of *ser* is *son*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C, D, F

- **A**: *Somos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but the pronouns are (*ellos/ellas*).

- **B**: *Eres* is the (*tú*) form, but the pronouns are (*ellos/ellas*).

- **C**: *Ser* is the infinitive, not a conjugated form.

- **D**: *Es* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but (*ellos/ellas*) refers to more than one person.

- **F**: *Soy* is the (*yo*) form, but the pronouns are (*ellos/ellas*).

**Grammar reminder**

**Ser** means to be.

Use *ser* for:

- Identity: *Soy Ana*.

- Date: *Hoy es lunes*.

- Origin: *Soy de España*.

- Nationality: *Somos españoles*.

- Profession: *Ella es profesora*.

- Relation: *Ellas son mis hermanas*.
