## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *bienestar* (*well-being*) fits because the sentence says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Es importante cuidar nuestro **\[-1-]**. Para estar bien, es bueno comer sano y beber agua.” The text is talking about taking care of our well-being through healthy habits like eating well and drinking water.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* B

* **B**: *estr$\textit{\'{e}}$s* (*stress*) is not correct because the sentence talks about taking care of something positive (well-being), not stress.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *ejercicio* (*exercise*) is correct because the sentence says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Muchas personas tambi$\text{\'{e}}$n hace&#x6E;**\[-2-]** para estar fuertes.” Exercise helps people stay strong.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* F

* **F**: *movimiento* (*movement*) is not correct because in Spanish we say *hacer ejercicio*, not *hacer movimiento* in this context.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *aire* (*air*) is correct because the expression $\text{\textquotedblleft}$al aire libre” means outdoors. The sentence says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$caminar al **\[-3-]** libre ayuda a sentirse mejor,” which refers to walking outside.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* E

* **E**: *tiempo* (*time*) is not correct because the correct expression in Spanish is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$al aire libre,” not $\text{\textquotedblleft}$al tiempo libre”.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{la salud mental}$    [1 mark]

* Paragraph 2 says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$La salud mental tambi$\text{\'{e}}$n es importante. Por eso, hablar de ella con amigos o familia puede ayudarte cuando est$\text{\'{a}}$s estresado.” In Spanish, the pronoun *ella* refers to a feminine noun mentioned before. In this case, it refers to *la salud mental* (*mental health*).

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{los buenos h\'{a}bitos}$[1 mark]

* Paragraph 3 says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Los buenos h$\text{\'{a}}$bitos te ayudan a sentirte mejor. ¿Y t$\text{\'{u}}$? ¿Qu$\text{\'{e}}$ haces para mantenerlos?” In Spanish, the pronoun *los* replaces a masculine plural noun mentioned before. Here it refers to *los buenos h$\textit{\'{a}}$bitos* (*good habits*).
