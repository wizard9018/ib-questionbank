# SA0167

\[Puntaje m$\text{\'{a}}$ximo: 5]

**Vas a escuchar un podcast sobre el D$\textbf{\'{\i}}$a de Muertos en M$\textbf{\'{e}}$xico.**

:audio{#e995f801-df19-4015-8895-55c445387d96 aid="e995f801-df19-4015-8895-55c445387d96"}

**Elige la respuesta correcta.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿Cu\'{a}ntos}$ d$\text{\'{\i}}$as dura la celebraci$\text{\'{o}}$n?[1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$dos

* B.$\hspace{1em}$doce

* C.$\hspace{1em}$diez

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿Qu\'{e}}$ ponen en las ofrendas?[1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Ropa

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Fotos

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Libros

**Contesta a las siguientes preguntas.**

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿Qu\'{e}}$ preparan las familias en casa?[1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿Qu\'{e}}$ hacen en los cementerios?[1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿C\'{o}mo}$ es la celebraci$\text{\'{o}}$n?[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$se celebra el uno y dos de noviembre" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it is celebrated on the first and second of November"). This tells us the celebration lasts two days.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* B, C

- **B**: *Doce* (*twelve*) is not mentioned in the audio. It is a distractor because it sounds similar to *dos* (*two*). 

- **C**: *Diez* (*ten*) is not mentioned in the audio. It is a distractor because it also sounds similar to *dos* (*two*).

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$fotos, flores y comida" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$photos, flowers and food"), telling us one correct element placed in the *ofrendas*.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

- **A**: *Ropa* (*clothes*) is not mentioned.

- **C**: *Libros* (*books*) is not mentioned.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ofrendas}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$preparan ofrendas en casa" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$they prepare offerings at home"), telling us what families prepare at home.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{recordar (a sus familiares)}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$visitan los cementerios para recordar a sus familiares" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$they visit the cemeteries to remember their relatives"), telling us what they do there.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{alegre}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$en realidad es una celebración alegre" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it is actually a joyful celebration"). Note that the audio also mentions $\text{\textquotedblleft}$algunas personas piensan que es triste" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$some people think it is sad"), but the correct idea is the final one: *alegre* (*joyful*).
