# SA0168

\[Puntaje m$\text{\'{a}}$ximo: 5]

**Vas a escuchar un mensaje de voz de Ana a su familia sobre su boda.**

:audio{#a6c26e6f-421e-4a10-ab07-59871d5328d1 aid="a6c26e6f-421e-4a10-ab07-59871d5328d1"}

**Contesta a las siguientes preguntas.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿D\'{o}nde}$ es la ceremonia?[1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$¿A qu$\text{\'{e}}$ hora empieza la cena?[1 mark]

**Escoge las tres frases verdaderas.** [3 marks]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$La ceremonia es por la tarde.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$La fiesta es en un lugar sencillo.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Los invitados pueden llegar antes de las nueve.

* D.$\hspace{1em}$La ceremonia va a tener mucha gente.

* E.$\hspace{1em}$En la fiesta solo van a comer.

* F.$\hspace{1em}$Ana tiene muchas ganas de ver a su familia.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ en la iglesia (del centro)}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$en la iglesia del centro" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$at the church in the city centre"), telling us that the ceremony will take place at the church in the city centre.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{a las nueve}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la cena empieza a las nueve" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$dinner starts at nine"), telling us that dinner starts at nine o'clock.

$3 - 5.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Las respuestas pueden estar en cualquier orden.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, F}$        [3 marks]

* **A**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a las cinco de la tarde" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$at five in the afternoon"). This tells us the ceremony is in the afternoon, not the morning.

* **C**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$pueden llegar antes para tomar algo" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$you can arrive before to have something to drink"). This tells us guests can arrive at the party before nine o'clock.

* **F**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$con muchas ganas de verlos" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$really looking forward to seeing you"). This tells us Ana is eager to see her family.

* *Opciones Incorrectas:*  B, D, E

* **B**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{sal\'{o}n}$ elegante" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$elegant venue"), the party is in a sophisticated place, not a simple one.

* **D**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la ceremonia es peque$\text{\~{n}}$a" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the ceremony is small"),  there will not be many people at the ceremony.

* **E**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$vamos a bailar y celebrar juntos" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we are going to dance and celebrate together"),  there will be dancing too, not just food.
