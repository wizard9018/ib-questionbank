# SA0154

\[Puntaje m$\text{\'{a}}$ximo: 15]:br

**Elige entre las opciones dadas un tipo de texto apropiado para la tarea. Escribe 50-60 palabras.**

En tu ciudad hay un festival muy importante. Escribe un texto para promocionar el festival.

| $\textrm{Anuncio}$ | $\textrm{Blog}$ | $\textrm{Mensaje de texto}$ |

| :----------------: | :-------------: | :-------------------------: |

|   $\hspace{9em}$   |  $\hspace{9em}$ |        $\hspace{9em}$       |

**Convenciones del tipo de texto**:

**Anuncio**: T$\text{\'{\i}}$tulo (title), frases o p$\text{\'{a}}$rrafos cortos (short sentences/paragraphs), fecha (date), autor (author), uso de vi$\text{\~{n}}$etas/puntos/numeraci$\text{\'{o}}$n (bullet points/numbering).\

**Blog**: T$\text{\'{\i}}$tulo (title), introducci$\text{\'{o}}$n breve (short introduction), invitaci$\text{\'{o}}$n a comentarios (invitation to comments).\

**Mensaje**: Saludo (greeting), mensaje breve y directo (short, direct message), tono informal (informal tone), despedida (farewell).

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer:}}$

¡Gran Festival del Caf$\text{\'{e}}$!

Hay caf$\text{\'{e}}$ de diferentes regiones y m$\text{\'{u}}$sica en vivo.

El evento es el 10 de junio a partir de las seis de la tarde.

Nos vemos en la plaza central.

¡Va a ser muy divertido!

Si quieres venir, puedes contactarnos en info@festivadellcafe.com

$\underline{\textrm{Marking Criteria Feedback}}$

**Criterion A (Language)**:

* Use present tense to describe the event: *hay caf$\textit{\'{e}}$ de diferentes regiones, el evento es el 10 de junio*.

* Use near future to express opinion: *va a ser muy divertido*.

* Use basic vocabulary related to events: *caf$\textit{\'{e}}$, evento, plaza*.

* Common errors: verbs not conjugated (*el evento ser el 10 de junio*), confusion between *hay / tiene* (*hay caf$\textit{\'{e}}$* vs. *tiene caf$\textit{\'{e}}$*), missing a in near future (*va ser muy divertido*), incorrect structure (*va a es*), word order problems.

**Criterion B (Message)**:

* A complete answer includes key information: what (*hay caf$\textit{\'{e}}$ de diferentes regiones*), when (*el 10 de junio a partir de las seis de la tarde*), and where (*la plaza central*).

* There is one main idea to develop: promoting the festival.

* The model answer does this clearly: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hay caf$\text{\'{e}}$ de diferentes regiones... El evento es el 10 de junio... Nos vemos en la plaza central... ¡Va a ser muy divertido!”

* A weak answer is missing key information (what, when or where).

**Criterion C (Conceptual understanding**):

* **Anuncio** is the most appropriate text type: it includes a clear title (*¡Gran Festival del Caf$\textit{\'{e}}$!*), key information presented in a direct way (what, when, where), and a promotional tone (*¡Va a ser muy divertido!*). The language is simple and designed to attract attention.

* **Blog** is generally appropriate, as it could include a title and describe the festival with a slightly more personal or descriptive tone. However, it would normally be a bit more developed or reflective, not just brief promotional information.

* **Mensaje de texto** is generally inappropriate, as it requires a greeting and a farewell and is more personal, while the purpose here is to promote an event to a general audience.

Missing key conventions will cost marks in this criterion even if the Spanish is correct.
