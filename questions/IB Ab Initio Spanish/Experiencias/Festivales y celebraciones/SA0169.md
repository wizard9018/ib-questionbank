## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$el evento principal es esta noche en la plaza central" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the main event is tonight in the central square"). This tells us the main event takes place in the central square, not in a market or town hall.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

- **A**: The audio does not mention *a market* (*un mercado*) in relation to the event location. 

- **C**: The audio mentions the *mayor* *(alcalde*) giving the Grito, but the event takes place at the central square, not at a town hall.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$habrá música en vivo y comida típica para los visitantes" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$there will be live music and typical food for visitors"). This tells us there is music and food before the Grito, not fireworks or a parade.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B

- **A**: The audio says *fireworks* (*fuegos artificiales*) happen at the end of the night, not before the Grito. 

- **B**: The audio does not mention a *parade* (*un desfile*) at all.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{diez}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a las diez, el alcalde va a dar el famoso Grito de la Independencia" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$at ten, the mayor is going to give the famous Grito de la Independencia"). This tells us the Grito starts at ten o'clock.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ocho}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$pueden llegar desde las ocho para disfrutar del ambiente" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$you can arrive from eight to enjoy the atmosphere"). This tells us visitors can arrive from eight o'clock.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{fuegos artificiales}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$habrá fuegos artificiales sobre la plaza" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$there will be fireworks over the square"). This tells us there will be fireworks at the end of the night.
