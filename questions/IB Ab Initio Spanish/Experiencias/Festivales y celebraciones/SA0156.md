# SA0156

\[Puntaje m$\text{\'{a}}$ximo: 15]:br

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer:}}$

Un festival que no te puedes perder\

Por Ver$\text{\'{o}}$nica Medina

La semana pasada vi un documental sobre la Tomatina, un festival bastante conocido que se celebra en Bu$\text{\~{n}}$ol, Espa$\text{\~{n}}$a. En el documental se explica de d$\text{\'{o}}$nde viene esta tradici$\text{\'{o}}$n y se muestra c$\text{\'{o}}$mo muchas personas participan cada a$\text{\~{n}}$o en una gran batalla de tomates. Tambi$\text{\'{e}}$n hay entrevistas con gente que cuenta su experiencia.

Lo que m$\text{\'{a}}$s me llam$\text{\'{o}}$ la atenci$\text{\'{o}}$n fue la calidad de la imagen, parece que est$\text{\'{a}}$s ah$\text{\'{\i}}$. Adem$\text{\'{a}}$s, es divertido y f$\text{\'{a}}$cil de entender, y pude aprender cosas nuevas sobre la cultura espa$\text{\~{n}}$ola.

Creo que deber$\text{\'{\i}}$ais verlo porque realmente merece la pena. Combina diversi$\text{\'{o}}$n y cultura en un mismo programa y es perfecto para conocer algo nuevo sin aburrirse. ¡Seguro que os gusta!

$\underline{\textrm{Marking Criteria Feedback}}$

**Criterion A (Language)**:

* Use past tense to explain what happened: *vi un documental, me gust$\textit{\'{o}}$, aprend$\textit{\'{\i}}$ mucho, pude, etc*.

* Use present tense + impersonal structures to describe the content: *se celebra, se explica, se muestra*.

* Use idiomatic expressions to sound more natural: *me llam$\textit{\'{o}}$ la atenci$\textit{\'{o}}$n, merece la pena*.

* Use conditional to recommend: *deber$\textit{\'{\i}}$ais verlo*.

* Common errors: verbs not conjugated (*yo ver* instead of *yo vi*), lack of impersonal structures (*el festival celebra* instead of *se celebra*), using present tense to recommend (*deb$\textit{\'{e}}$is* instead of  *deber$\textit{\'{\i}}$ais*), word order problems.

**Criterion B (Message)**:

* A complete answer explains clearly what the documentary is about (*se explica el origen del festival, se muestra c$\textit{\'{o}}$mo participan las personas*), personal reaction (*me gust$\textit{\'{o}}$, me llam$\textit{\'{o}}$ la atenci$\textit{\'{o}}$n*), and why it is recommended (*merece la pena, es interesante y divertido*).

* There are three main ideas to develop: \

  $1.\hspace{1em}$Describe the documentary\

  $2.\hspace{1em}$Give personal opinion\

  $3.\hspace{1em}$Recommend it to a specific audience.

* The model answer does this clearly:\

  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Vi un documental... se explica..., me llam$\text{\'{o}}$ la atenci$\text{\'{o}}$n..., merece la pena verlo...”

* A weak answer only describes the documentary or only gives an opinion without explaining the content or recommendation.

**Criterion C (Conceptual understanding**):

* **Rese$\textbf{\~{n}}$a** is the most appropriate text type: it includes a title (*Un festival que no te puedes perder*), an author (*Por Ver$\textit{\'{o}}$nica Medina*), a clear description, opinion, and recommendation.

* **Art$\textbf{\'{\i}}$culo** is generally appropriate, as it could include a similar structure with a title and clear paragraphs directed at a specific audience.

* **Discurso** is generally inappropriate, as the purpose is to inform and evaluate rather than directly persuade in an oral tone.

Missing key conventions will cost marks in this criterion even if the Spanish is correct.
