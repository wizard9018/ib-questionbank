# SA0165

\[Puntaje m$\text{\'{a}}$ximo: 5]

**Vas a escuchar un mensaje de voz sobre un carnaval.**

:audio{#fc0fa005-6200-4cc4-9239-d52520d23d01 aid="fc0fa005-6200-4cc4-9239-d52520d23d01"}

**Elige la respuesta correcta.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿D\'{o}nde}$ es el carnaval?[1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$En el parque

* B.$\hspace{1em}$En el centro

* C.$\hspace{1em}$En la escuela

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$¿A que hora empieza el carnaval?    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$A las tres

* B.$\hspace{1em}$A las cuatro

* C.$\hspace{1em}$A las cinco

**Escoge las tres frases verdaderas.** [3 marks]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$El carnaval es este fin de semana.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$El carnaval es el domingo.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$El carnaval es por la ma$\text{\~{n}}$ana.

* D.$\hspace{1em}$Hay m$\text{\'{u}}$sica y bailes.

* E.$\hspace{1em}$Muchas personas llevan disfraces.

* F.$\hspace{1em}$Pablo va solo al carnaval.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hay un carnaval en el centro de mi ciudad" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$there is a carnival in the city centre"). This tells us the carnival is in the city centre (*el centro* = *city centre*), not in a park or school.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

* **A**: The audio does not mention a park (*parque* = *park*) at all.

* **C**: The audio does not mention a school (*escuela* = *school*) at all.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$empieza a las cinco" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it starts at five o'clock). This tells us the carnival starts at five o'clock (*a las cinco* = *at five o'clock*).

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B

* **A**:  *A las tres* (*three o'clock*) is not mentioned in the audio.

* **B**: *A las cuatro* (*four o'clock*) is not mentioned in the audio.

$3 - 5.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Las respuestas pueden estar en cualquier orden.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, D, E}$        [3 marks]

* **A**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$este fin de semana hay un carnaval" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$this weekend there is a carnival"). This tells us the carnival is happening this weekend.

* **D**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hay m$\text{\'{u}}$sica, bailes" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$there is music and dancing"). This tells us there is music and dancing at the carnival.

* **E**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$muchas personas con disfraces" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$many people with costumes"). This tells us many people wear costumes.

* *Opciones Incorrectas:*  B, C, F

* **B**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$el s$\text{\'{a}}$bado" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Saturday"),  the carnival is on Saturday, not Sunday.

* **C**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$por la tarde" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$in the afternoon"),  the carnival is in the afternoon, not the morning.

* **F**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$voy con mis amigos" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am going with my friends"), Pablo is not going alone.
