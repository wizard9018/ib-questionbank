$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{en el recinto ferial}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$se celebra en el recinto ferial" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it takes place at the fairground"). This tells us where the event is held.

:br$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{este s\'{a}bado}$[1 mark]

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$abre este s$\text{\'{a}}$bado por la ma$\text{\~{n}}$ana" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it opens this Saturday morning"). This tells us when it opens.

:br$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{comida t\'{\i}pica}$[1 mark]

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hay m$\text{\'{u}}$sica, sevillanas y comida t$\text{\'{\i}}$pica" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$there is music, sevillanas and typical food"). This tells us what food you can eat at the fair.

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$las familias pueden visitar las casetas" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$families can visit the fair tents"). This tells us who can enjoy the stalls.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{entrada}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$La entrada es gratis" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$entry is free"). This tells us that you do not need to pay to get in.
