## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- This question is correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Quiero invitarles a mi fiesta...” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I want to invite you to my party"). This shows that she is inviting people, so the correct question is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$¿Le gustaría venir?” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Would you like to come?").

- *Opción incorrecta:* H

- **H**: *¿Cuánto dura?* (*How long does it last?*) is not correct because the text says: *el próximo sábado* (*next Saturday*), which refers to the day, not the duration.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- This question is correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$va a ser un día muy importante para mí” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it will be a very important day for me"). This explains why people should come, so the correct question is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$¿Por qué deben venir?” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Why you should come?").

- *Opción incorrecta:* G

- **G**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$¿Quién está invitado?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Who is invited?") is not correct because the paragraph gives a reason, not a list of people who are allowed to join the party.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- This question is correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Habrá música, baile y una cena...” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$There will be music, dancing and dinner"). This shows what there will be at the party, so the correct answer is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$¿Qué va a haber?” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$What will there be?").

- *Opción incorrecta:* F

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$¿Qué debes ponerte?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$What should you wear?") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$voy a llevar un vestido especial” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am going to wear a special dress"), but this refers to what she's going to wear, not what she should wear. 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- This question is correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Al final, todos estaremos juntos bailando...” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$At the end, we will all be dancing together..."). This shows what happens at the end of the celebration, so the correct answer is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$¿Cómo termina?” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$How does it end?").

- *Opción incorrecta:* D

- **D**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$¿Cómo lo vamos a pasar?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$How are we going to have fun?") is not correct because the paragraph focuses on the end of the party, not on general feelings about having fun. 

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$La voy a celebrar el próximo sábado en un salón cerca de mi casa.” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am going to celebrate it next Saturday in a hall near my house"). This means the party is on Saturday, so it is during the weekend.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* B, C, D

- **B**: The option says: *en mi comedor* (*in my dining room*). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$en un salón cerca de mi casa” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$in a hall near my house"). This shows it is not in the dining room.

- **C**: The option says: *en mi casa* (*in my house*). The text says: *cerca de mi casa* (*near my house*). This means it is not inside the house.

- **D**: The option says: *el día quince* (*on the fifteenth day*). The text says: *fiesta de quince años* (*fifteenth birthday*). This refers to her age, not the date.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- The option says: *empiezo una nueva fase* (*I start a new phase*). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Es una nueva etapa en mi vida” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$It is a new stage in my life"). This shows why the celebration is important.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B, C

- **A**: The option says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$quiero celebrarlo en mi familia” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I want to celebrate it in my family"). The text says: *con mi familia* (*with my family*). This is not the reason why the party is important.

- **B**: The option says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$es un momento sin relevancia” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it is a moment without importance"). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$va a ser un día muy importante para mí” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it will be a very important day for me"). This shows the opposite.

- **C**: The option says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$me gusta la música y el baile” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I like music and dancing"). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Habrá música, baile...” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$There will be music, dancing..."). This describes activities, not the reason why it is special.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The option says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$pasar este día con todos ustedes” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to spend this day with all of you"). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Me haría mucha ilusión vivir este momento con ustedes” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I would love to share this moment with you"). This shows she wants to share the day.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C, D

- **A**: The option says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$celebrar mis 15 años de nuevo” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to celebrate my 15th birthday again"). The text does not say this. It only talks about this celebration.

- **C**: The option says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hacer un baile con mi padre” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to do a dance with my father"). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$voy a bailar un vals con mi padre” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am going to dance a waltz with my father"). This is just one activity, not the main idea.

- **D**: The option says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$estar muy feliz en mi quinceañera” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to be very happy at my quinceañera"). The text shows she is happy, but it focuses on sharing the moment with others.
