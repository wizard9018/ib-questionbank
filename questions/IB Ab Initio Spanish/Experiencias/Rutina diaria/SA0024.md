**El teletrabajo en Chile**

**\[-5-]**\

En Chile, muchas personas trabajan desde casa. Cada ma$\text{\~{n}}$ana se levantan temprano, desayunan algo sencillo y encienden el computador. Despu$\text{\'{e}}$s revisan su $\underline{\textrm{agenda}}$ y organizan el $\underline{\textrm{d\'{\i}a}}$. Todo est$\text{\'{a}}$ listo para conectarse con su $\underline{\textrm{empresa}}$ y empezar a trabajar.

**\[-6-]**\

Durante la ma$\text{\~{n}}$ana trabajan en su tarea principal. Al mediod$\text{\'{\i}}$a tienen una teleconferencia con el equipo para revisar los proyectos. Despu$\text{\'{e}}$s hacen una $\underline{\textrm{pausa}}$, y descansan unos minutos antes de continuar con sus responsabilidades.

**\[-7-]**\

Por la tarde terminan su trabajo. Despu$\text{\'{e}}$s de un d$\text{\'{\i}}$a largo frente al computador, apagan todo y empiezan otra actividad al instante. Dejan las responsabilidades laborales y se dedican a descansar, escuchar m$\text{\'{u}}$sica o ver una pel$\text{\'{\i}}$cula con su familia.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

* *planificaci$\textit{\'{o}}$n* (*planning*) refers to the organisation of activities or tasks before doing them. In the text, it says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$revisan su agenda y organizan las tareas del d$\text{\'{\i}}$a.” The word *agenda* refers to the plan or organisation of the day, which matches the meaning of *planificaci$\textit{\'{o}}$n*.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* G

* **G**: *lista* (*list*) is not correct because it refers to a written set of items, while the text uses *agenda* in the sense of planning or organising the day.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *jornada* (*workday*) refers to a full day of work. In the text, it says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{Despu\'{e}s}$ revisan su agenda y organizan el d$\text{\'{\i}}$a." The word *d$\textit{\'{\i}}$a* refers to the working day, which matches the meaning of *jornada*.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* D

* **D**: *sesi$\textit{\'{o}}$n* (*session*) is not correct because it usually refers to one meeting or one limited period of activity, while the text refers to one limited period of activity or a single meeting, while the text refers to a full working day (*d$\textit{\'{\i}}$a*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{H}$    [1 mark]

* *compa$\textit{\~{n}\'{\i}}$a* (*company*) refers to a business or organisation where people work. In the text, it says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$conectarse con su empresa”. The word *empresa* refers to the place or organisation where they work, which matches the meaning of *compa$\textit{\~{n}\'{\i}}$a*.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* B

* **B**: *f$\textit{\'{a}}$brica* (*factory*) is not correct because it refers to a specific place where products are made, while the text refers to *una empresa*, which is a more general organisation and not necessarily a factory.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *parada* (*pause / stop*) refers to a short break in an activity. In the text, it says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hacen una pausa, y descansan unos minutos antes de continuar con sus responsabilidades." The word *pausa* refers to a short stop during work, which matches the meaning of *parada*.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* F

* **F**: *siesta* (*nap*) is not correct because it refers to a sleep or nap, while the text refers to a short *pausa* during lunch time, that does not imply to sleep.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

* *Todo listo* (*Everything ready*) This title is correct because the paragraph describes how people prepare before starting work: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$revisan su agenda y organizan el d$\text{\'{\i}}$a” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$they check their schedule and organise the day"). The paragraph focuses on getting ready to begin the working day.:br

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* B

* **B**: *Un trabajo diferente* (*A different job*) is not correct because the paragraph does not describe different types of jobs, but a daily routine.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *Actividad laboral* (*Work activity*) This title is correct because the paragraph describes the tasks people do during their workday: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$trabajan en su tarea principal” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$they work on their main task"), $\text{\textquotedblleft}$tienen una teleconferencia” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$they have a teleconference") y $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hacen una pausa” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$they take a break"). The paragraph focuses on the work activities they complete during the day.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* E

* **E**: *Un d$\textit{\'{\i}}$a intenso* (*An intense day*) is not correct because the text does not emphasise difficulty or intensity, but rather routine activities.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *Cambio de ritmo* (*Change of pace*) This title is correct because the paragraph shows a clear transition after work: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$apagan todo y empiezan otra actividad al instante” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$they turn everything off and start another activity immediately") y $\text{\textquotedblleft}$se dedican a descansar” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$they dedicate themselves to resting"). The paragraph focuses on the change from work to rest.

- *Opción incorrecta:* C

- **C**: *Equilibrio laboral y familiar* (*Work-life balance*) is not correct because, although it mentions personal activities, the main idea is the change of activity, not the concept of balance between work and personal life.
