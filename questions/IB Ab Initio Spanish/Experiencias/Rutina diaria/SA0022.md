## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{cortes\'{\i}a}$[1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{empezar}$    [1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Antes de empezar a comer...” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Before starting to eat..."). The verb *empezar* means to begin, which matches the meaning of *iniciar* (*to start*).

  The words *conversan* (*talk*) and *comparten su d$\textit{\'{\i}}$a* (*share their day*) show that families talk about their day at the table.
