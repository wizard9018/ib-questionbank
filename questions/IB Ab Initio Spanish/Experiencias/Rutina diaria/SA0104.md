## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{tres y cinco a\~{n}os}$[1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$dos nenas de tres y cinco a$\text{\~{n}}$os" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$two girls aged three and five"). Note that*ni$\textit{\~{n}}$as* (*girls*) and *nenas* (*girls* in Argentine Spanish) have the same meaning. This tells us the girls are three and five years old.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Palermo}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$cuidar a dos nenas de tres y cinco a$\text{\~{n}}$os en el barrio de Palermo" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to look after two girls aged three and five in the Palermo neighbourhood"). Note that *zona* (*area*) and *barrio* (*neighbourhood*) have the same meaning. This tells us the job is in the Palermo neighbourhood of Buenos Aires.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Se acepta cualquiera de las siguientes respuestas en cualquier orden:*

* $\textrm{responsable}$

* $\textrm{paciente}$

* $\textrm{con experiencia previa con ni\~{n}os}$[1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Buscamos una persona responsable, paciente y con experiencia previa con ni$\text{\~{n}}$os" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we are looking for a responsible, patient person with previous experience with children"). This tells us the candidate must be responsible, patient and have previous experience with children.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{tres }$    [1 mark]

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$de tres a siete de la tarde" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$from three to seven in the afternoon"). This tells us what time the working day begins.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{siete}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$de tres a siete de la tarde" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$from three to seven in the afternoon"). This tells us what time the working day ends.

This tells us what professional quality is required. Note that *en el cuidado de ni$\textit{\~{n}}$os* and *con ni$\textit{\~{n}}$os* have the same meaning.
