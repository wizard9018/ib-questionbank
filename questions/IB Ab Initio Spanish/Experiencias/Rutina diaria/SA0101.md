# SA0101

[Puntaje máximo: 5\]

**Vas a escuchar a Valentina **$\textbf{present\'andose}$** a su clase.**

:audio{#da82bc1e-d817-4a20-a2f3-3af878d1fe12 aid="da82bc1e-d817-4a20-a2f3-3af878d1fe12"}

**Elige la respuesta correcta.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Valentina se levanta…    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$a las siete y media.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$a las siete.

* C.$\hspace{1em}\text{despu\'{e}s}$ de las siete.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Antes de ir al colegio, Valentina…    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$come algo dulce y bebe leche.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$come algo salado y bebe zumo.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$come algo dulce y bebe agua.

**Contesta las siguientes preguntas.**

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿C\'{o}mo}$ va Valentina al colegio?[1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$¿A qu$\text{\'{e}}$ hora empiezan las clases?[1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{¿Qu\'{e}}$ hace Valentina despu$\text{\'{e}}$s de los deberes?[1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Me levanto a las siete de la ma$\text{\~{n}}$ana" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I get up at seven in the morning). This tells us Valentina gets up at seven.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

* **A**: The audio says *las siete* (*seven*), not *seven thirty* (*siete y media*).

* **C**: The audio says *las siete* (*seven*), not *after seven* (*despu$\textit{\'{e}}$s de las siete*).

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$como tostadas con mermelada y bebo un vaso de leche" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I eat toast with jam and drink a glass of milk). This tells us Valentina eats something sweet (*tostadas con mermelada* or *toast with jam* is sweet) and drinks milk.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* B, C

* **B**: The audio says *tostadas con mermelada* (*toast with jam*), not *something savoury* (*algo salado*). The audio also says *leche* (*milk*), not *juice* (*zumo*).

* **C**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$bebo un vaso de leche" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I drink a glass of milk"), not *water* (*agua*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{en autob\'{u}s}$[1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$voy al colegio en autob$\text{\'{u}}$s" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I go to school by bus"). This tells us Valentina goes to school by bus.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{a las ocho y media/a las 8:30}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Las clases empiezan a las ocho y media" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$classes start at half past eight"). This tells us classes start at half past eight.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ve la televisi\'{o}n}$[1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hago los deberes y veo la televisi$\text{\'{o}}$n" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I do my homework and watch television"). This tells us Valentina watches television after doing her homework.
