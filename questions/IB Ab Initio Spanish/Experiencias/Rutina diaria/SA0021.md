## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

* *me despierto* *(I wake up*) fits because the sentence says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$En un d$\text{\'{\i}}$a de escuel&#x61;**\[-1-]** a las siete.” The verb me despierto means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I wake up,” which is the action that usually happens first in the morning before starting the day.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:*

* **E**: *me duermo* (*I fall asleep*) is not correct because *me duermo* refers to falling asleep, which normally happens at night, not at the start of a school day.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *me ducho* (*I shower*) is correct because the sentence says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Primero **\[-2-]** y desayuno en casa.” The routine action *me ducho* fits well before breakfast as part of a normal morning routine.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:*

* **C**: *me quito* (*I take off*) is not correct because *me quito* is used to remove clothes or objects, which usually happens at the end of the day or when you return home.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *me encanta* (*I love*) is correct because the sentence says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{Despu\'{e}s}$ voy a la escuela en autob$\text{\'{u}}$s.**\[-3-]** mi colegio.” The expression me encanta means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I love,” showing that the student likes their school.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:*

* **B**: *me siento* (*I sit down*) is not correct because the sentence is about feelings toward school, not the action of sitting.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *tomo el autob$\textit{\'{u}}$s* (*I take the bus*) fits because the sentence says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Para ir al colegio...” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$To go to school...”). The text earlier explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{Despu\'{e}s}$ voy a la escuela en autob$\text{\'{u}}$s.” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$After that I go to school by bus.”) This shows that the student goes to school by bus, so the correct ending is*tomo el autob$\textit{\'{u}}$s* (*I take the bus*).

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* B

* **B**: *charlo con mis padres* (*I chat with my parents*) is not correct because the text says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ceno con mi familia” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I have dinner with my family"). It does not say that the student chats with their parents.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *me voy a la cama* (*I go to bed*) is correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Por la noche ceno con mi familia y luego me acuesto a las diez.” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$At night I have dinner with my family and then I go to bed at ten.”) The expression me acuesto means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I go to bed.” This has the same meaning as *me voy a la cama* (*I go to bed*), so it is the correct ending to complete the sentence.

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* C

* **C**: *hago los deberes* (*I do homework*) is not correct because the text already says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Por la tarde estudio un poco.” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$In the afternoon I study a little.”) Homework or studying happens in the afternoon, not after dinner in this routine.
