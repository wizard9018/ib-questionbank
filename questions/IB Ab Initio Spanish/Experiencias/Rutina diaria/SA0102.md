# SA0102

[Puntaje máximo: 6\]

**Vas a escuchar un mensaje de voz de Ana a su amiga hablando de su nueva casa en Monterrey.**

:audio{#20959cdd-26b9-4da5-b151-0b3689080646 aid="20959cdd-26b9-4da5-b151-0b3689080646"}

**Escoge las tres frases verdaderas.**

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Ana se ha mudado recientemente.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$La casa de Ana es peque$\text{\~{n}}$a.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$La casa tiene m$\text{\'{a}}$s de trece habitaciones.

* D.$\hspace{1em}$El sal$\text{\'{o}}$n tiene mucha luz natural.

* E.$\hspace{1em}$Ana quiere tener plantas en el jard$\text{\'{\i}}$n.

* F.$\hspace{1em}$La casa est$\text{\'{a}}$ lejos del centro de la ciudad.

**Completa los espacios en blanco. Usa como m$\textbf{\'{a}}$ximo tres palabras por espacio.**

**El mensaje de Ana**

La casa tiene **\[-4-]** &#x79; dos ba$\text{\~{n}}$os.\

La habitaci$\text{\'{o}}$n preferida de Ana e&#x73; **\[-5-]** porque tiene ventanas grandes. \

El jard$\text{\'{\i}}$n e&#x73; **\[-6-]**.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$

*Las respuestas pueden estar en cualquier orden.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, D, E}$        [3 marks]

* **A**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$me acabo de mudar a mi nueva casa" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I have just moved into my new house"). Note that *recientemente* (*recently*) and *acabo de mudar* (*have just moved*) have the same meaning. This tells us Ana has just moved into her new house.

* **D**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$tiene ventanas grandes y entra mucha luz" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it has large windows and a lot of light comes in"). This tells us the living room has a lot of natural light.

* **E**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$voy a poner unas plantas" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am going to put some plants"). This tells us Ana wants to put plants in the garden.

* *Opciones Incorrectas:*  B, C, F

* **B**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$es una casa bastante grande" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it is quite a large house"), which means the house is not *small* (*peque$\textit{\~{n}}$a*).

* **C**: The audio says *tres habitaciones* (*three bedrooms*), not *thirteen* (*trece*). *Tres* and *trece* sound similar in Spanish.

* **F**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{est\'{a}}$ cerca del centro" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it is close to the city centre"), which means the house is not *far from the city centre* (*lejos del centro*).

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{tres habitaciones}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$tiene tres habitaciones y dos ba$\text{\~{n}}$os" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it has three bedrooms and two bathrooms"). This tells us the house has three bedrooms.

:br$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{el sal\'{o}n}$[1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mi habitaci$\text{\'{o}}$n favorita es el sal$\text{\'{o}}$n" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$my favourite room is the living room"). This tells us Ana's favourite room is the living room.

:br$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{peque\~{n}o}$[1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hay un jard$\text{\'{\i}}$n peque$\text{\~{n}}$o" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$there is a small garden"). This tells us the garden is small.
