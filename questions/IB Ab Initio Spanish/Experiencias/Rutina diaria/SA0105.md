## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$        [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$menos del 50% de los j$\text{\'{o}}$venes espa$\text{\~{n}}$oles colabora regularmente en casa" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$less than 50% of young Spaniards help at home regularly"). Note that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$menos de la mitad de los j$\text{\'{o}}$venes" (less than half of young people) and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$menos del 50%" (less than 50%) have the same meaning. This tells us less than half of young Spaniards help at home regularly.

* *Respuestas incorrectas:* A, B

* **A**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$menos del 50%" (less than 50%), which means less than half collaborate, not the majority ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$la mayor$\text{\'{\i}}$a").

* **B**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$menos del 50%" (less than 50%), which means less than half collaborate, not more than half ($\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{m\'{a}s}$ de la mitad").

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hacer tareas en casa es esencial para aprender a vivir de forma independiente" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$doing household chores is essential to learn to live independently"). Note that *vivir de forma aut$\textit{\'{o}}$noma* (*to live autonomously*) and *vivir de forma independiente* (*to live independently*) have the same meaning. This tells us the narrator thinks household chores teach you to live independently.

* *Respuestas incorrectas:* A, C

* **A**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$vivir en familia significa compartir las responsabilidades" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$living as a family means sharing responsibilities"), but this is given as a reason to share tasks, not as a reason why chores are important (*importantes*).

* **C**: The audio does not mention *Spanish culture* (*la cultura espa$\textit{\~{n}}$ola*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$No siempre me apetece hacerlas, pero lo hago igualmente" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I do not always feel like doing them, but I do them anyway"). Note that *tener ganas* (*to feel like doing something*) and *apetecer* (*to feel like doing something*) have the same meaning. This tells us the narrator does the chores even when he does not feel like it.

* *Respuestas incorrectas:* A, B

* **A**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$no siempre me apetece" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I do not always feel like it"), which means he does not always do them *enthusiastically* (*con mucho entusiasmo*).

* **B**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$no siempre me apetece" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I do not always feel like it"), which means he does not always enjoy doing the chores, not that he never complains (*sin quejarse nunca*).

$4\text{-}6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, D, E}$        [3 marks]

* *Pueden estar en cualquier orden.*

* **B**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$me encargo de cocinar los fines de semana y de limpiar el ba$\text{\~{n}}$o" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am responsible for cooking at the weekend and cleaning the bathroom"). This tells us the narrator has specific tasks assigned to him at home.:br

* **D**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$vivir en familia significa compartir las responsabilidades" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$living as a family means sharing responsibilities"). Note that *convivir* and *vivir en familia* have the same meaning, and *repartir las tareas* and *compartir las responsabilidades* have the same meaning. This tells us the narrator believes living together means sharing household tasks.

* **E**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$estas habilidades te preparan para cuando vives solo" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$these skills prepare you for when you live alone"). Note that *$\textit{\'{u}}$tiles para el futuro* and *te preparan para cuando vives solo* have the same meaning. This tells us the narrator considers these skills useful for the future.

* *Respuestas incorrectas:* A, C, F

* **A**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$No siempre me apetece hacerlas" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I do not always feel like doing them"), which means the narrator does not always find the chores pleasant (*agradable*).

* **C**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$A veces escucho m$\text{\'{u}}$sica para que sea m$\text{\'{a}}$s llevadero" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$sometimes I listen to music to make it more bearable"), which means the narrator does not do the chores in silence (*en silencio*).

* **F**: The audio says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{Ojal\'{a}}$ m$\text{\'{a}}$s j$\text{\'{o}}$venes pensaran lo mismo" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I wish more young people thought the same"), which means the narrator wishes more young people shared his views, implying most do not.
