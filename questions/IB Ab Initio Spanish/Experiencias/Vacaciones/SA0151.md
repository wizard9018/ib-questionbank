## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer:}}$

Felisa Gonz$\text{\'{a}}$lez\

Calle Mayor 5\

28007 Madrid - Espa$\text{\~{n}}$a

Hola, abuela:

Estoy de vacaciones en Valencia con mis amigos. Me gusta mucho el viaje. Vamos a la playa y comemos comida rica. El hotel es muy bonito y me siento muy feliz.

Un beso,\

Dani

$\underline{\textrm{Marking Criteria Feedback}}$

**Criterion A (Language)**:

* Use present tense to describe the experience: *estoy de vacaciones, vamos a la playa, comemos, es*.

* Use *me gusta* correctly: *me gusta mucho el viaje*.

* Use basic verbs in different persons: *estoy, vamos, comemos*.

* Use reflexive verbs correctly: *me siento*.

* Use adjectives to describe places and emotions: *rico, bonito, feliz*.

* Common errors: incorrect use of gustar (*yo gusto* instead of *me gusta*); missing reflexive pronoun (*siento* instead of *me siento*); missing verbs (*yo muy feliz*); no conjugation (*nosotros comer* instead of *comemos*).

**Criterion B (Message)**:

* A complete answer describes the experience: where you are (*estoy en Valencia*), what you do (*vamos a la playa, comemos comida rica*), a description (*el hotel es muy bonito*) and a feeling (*me siento muy feliz*).

* There is one main idea to develop: describing the holiday experience.

* A weak answer only mentions the place without activities or feelings.

**Criterion C (Conceptual understanding**):

* **Postal** is the most appropriate text type: short, informal, directed at a specific family member, describes an experience. Key conventions: *direcci$\textit{\'{o}}$n del destinatario* (Recipient's address), *saludo* (Greeting e.g. *Hola, abuela*), *mensaje breve* (Short personal message), *uso de primera persona* (Use of *yo*), *despedida* (Farewell e.g. *Un beso*), *firma*(Signature) — as in the model answer: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Felisa Gonz$\text{\'{a}}$lez, Calle Mayor 5..." and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Un beso, Dani"

* **Discurso** is generally inappropriate: formal and directed at a general audience, not a specific family member.

* **Blog** is generally inappropriate: public and directed at a general audience, not a specific family member.
