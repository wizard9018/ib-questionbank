## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Las respuestas pueden estar en cualquier orden.*  

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, D}$        [3 marks]

- **A**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cancún es el destino perfecto para tus vacaciones" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cancún is the perfect destination for your holidays"). This tells us Cancún is a great holiday destination.

- **C**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$El agua está caliente y es perfecta para nadar" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the water is warm and perfect for swimming"). This tells us the sea in Cancún is great for swimming.

- **D**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hay hoteles para todas las familias" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$there are hotels for all families"). This tells us there are accommodation options for all types of tourists.

- *Opciones Incorrectas:*  B, E, F

- **B**: the audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Las playas son muy bonitas" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the beaches are very beautiful"), the opposite of what statement **B** says.

- **E**: the audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$puedes comer mariscos muy ricos" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$you can eat delicious seafood"), seafood does exist in Cancún.

- **F**: the audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$el número de teléfono es 800-CANCÚN" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the phone number is 800-CANCÚN"), you reserve by phone, not by email.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{azul}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$el mar es azul" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the sea is blue"). This tells us the colour of the sea in Cancún.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{todo el año}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$El tiempo es muy bueno todo el año" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the weather is very good all year round"). This tells us the weather in Cancún is pleasant throughout the year.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{mercados}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$visitar mercados típicos" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to visit typical markets"). This tells us you can visit typical markets in Cancún.
