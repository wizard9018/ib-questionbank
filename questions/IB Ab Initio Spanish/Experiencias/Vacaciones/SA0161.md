## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$En Bogotá es común alojarse en hoteles pequeños en el centro" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$In Bogotá it is common to stay in small hotels in the city centre"). This tells us people often stay in small central accommodation in Bogotá.

- *Incorrect options:* A, B

- **A**: the audio mentions *albergues* for Medellín, not Bogotá.

- **B**: the audio mentions *fincas rurales* as a separate option, not specifically for Bogotá.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Son cómodos y están cerca de museos" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$they are comfortable and close to museums"). This tells us the hotels are popular because of their proximity to museums.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* B, C

- **B**: the audio describes hotels as *cómodos* (*comfortable*) but does not mention they are quiet.

- **C**: the audio says albergues are cheaper, not the central hotels.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$muchos viajeros prefieren albergues porque son más baratos y es fácil conocer a otras personas" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$many travellers prefer hostels because they are cheaper and it is easy to meet other people"). This tells us *albergues* are popular because they are affordable and sociable.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

- **A**: the audio says some travellers prefer hotels for comfort, not *albergues*.

- **C**: the audio does not mention *albergues* being in the countryside.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{descansar y disfrutar}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$puedes descansar y disfrutar de la naturaleza" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$you can rest and enjoy nature"). This tells us what you can do at a rural *finca*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{lo que buscas}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$el mejor alojamiento depende de lo que buscas" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the best accommodation depends on what you are looking for"). This tells us the best option varies depending on the traveller's needs.
