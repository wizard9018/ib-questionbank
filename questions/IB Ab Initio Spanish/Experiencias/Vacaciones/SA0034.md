$\underline{\textrm{}}$

Aunque las cataratas son impresionantes, la organizaci$\text{\'{o}}$n del viaje fue bastante mala y por $\underline{\textrm{su}}$ culpa, no tuve tiempo suficiente para disfrutar de cada lugar con tranquilidad.

Adem$\text{\'{a}}$s, las tarifas eran muy altas en comparaci$\text{\'{o}}$n con el servicio $\underline{\textrm{ofrecido}}$. Tambi$\text{\'{e}}$n tuve problemas en un hotel $\underline{\textrm{donde}}$ tuve que esperar dos horas para registrarme, lo que me dej$\text{\'{o}}$ bastante $\underline{\textrm{decepcionado}}$.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

- *planificado* (*planned*) is correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$un viaje organizado” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$an organized trip"), and this means the trip was prepared in advance.

- *Opción incorrecta:* E

- **E**: *improvisado* (*improvised*) is not correct because it means not planned, which is the opposite of *organizado*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- *dado* (*given*) is correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$el servicio ofrecido” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the service offered"), and this means the service that was provided.

- *Opción incorrecta:* B

- **B**: *rechazado* (*rejected*) is not correct because it means refused, not something that was provided.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{H}$    [1 mark]

- *defraudado* (*disappointed*) is correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$me dejó bastante decepcionado” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it left me quite disappointed"), which has the same meaning.

- *Opción incorrecta:* D

- **D**: *animado* (*cheerful*) is not correct because it is the opposite feeling of being disappointed.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- *agotado* (*exhausted*) is correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$el viaje me dejó bastante estresado" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the trip left me quite stressed"), and in this context both words describe the physical and emotional state after a difficult trip.

- *Opción incorrecta:* G

- **G**: *aliviado* (*relieved*) is not correct because it means the opposite, feeling better after something difficult, not tired or stressed.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{la organización del viaje}$    [1 mark]

- The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la organización del viaje fue bastante mala y por su culpa...”. In Spanish, su (its) refers to a singular noun mentioned before. In this case, it refers to la organización del viaje.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{un hotel}$    [1 mark]

- The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$tuve problemas en un hotel donde tuve que esperar...”. In Spanish, *donde* (*where*) refers to a place mentioned before. In this case, it refers to *un hotel*.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{el viaje}$    [1 mark]

- The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$creo que es mejor organizarlo por tu cuenta...”. In Spanish, *lo* (*it*) refers to a masculine singular noun mentioned before. In this case, it refers to *el viaje*.
