$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Las respuestas pueden estar en cualquier orden.*

This tells us the excursions are taking place this weekend.

* **C**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$caminar y hacer fotos en la naturaleza" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$walk and take photos in nature"). This tells us you can do outdoor activities in the mountains.

* **F**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$puedes visitar nuestra p$\text{\'{a}}$gina web" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$you can visit our website"). This tells us information is available online.:br

* **B**: the audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$visitar el mercado local y ver tradiciones t$\text{\'{\i}}$picas" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$visit the local market and see typical traditions"), the village offers more than just the market.

* **D**: the audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$las excursiones empiezan por la ma$\text{\~{n}}$ana" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$excursions start in the morning"), not in the afternoon.

* **E**: the audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$recuerda llevar agua, ropa c$\text{\'{o}}$moda y protecci$\text{\'{o}}$n solar" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$remember to bring water, comfortable clothes and sun protection") — you do need to bring specific items.
