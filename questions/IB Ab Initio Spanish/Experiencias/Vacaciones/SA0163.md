## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a las nueve y media”. ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$at nine-thirty") This gives the exact time of the excursion.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

- **A**: Nine is close, but not correct.

- **C**: Eleven is for a different activity.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$probar productos típicos” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to try typical food"). Note that *comida local and productos típicos* have the same meaning. This tells us what you can do in the village.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B

- **A**: Walking is in the park.

- **B**: Animals are in the park.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$el transporte está incluido, pero la comida no” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$transport is included but food is not"), This tells us that transport is included in the beach excursion.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

- **A**: Food is not included.

- **C**: The guided visit is for the village, not the beach.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{once}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a las once, se ofrece una visita guiada a un pueblo histórico" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$at eleven, a guided visit to a historic village is offered"). This tells us when the village excursion begins.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{mercado}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ver el mercado local" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$see the local market"). This tells us what you can visit in the village.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{comida}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la comida no" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$food is not included"). This tells us food is not covered in the beach excursion.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{antelación}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$reservar con antelación en la recepción del hotel" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$reserve in advance at the hotel reception"). This tells us to book ahead.
