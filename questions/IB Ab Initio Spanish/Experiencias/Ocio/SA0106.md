## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$tengo quince años". This tells us Lucía is fifteen years old.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B

- **A**: The audio says *quince*, not thirteen.

- **B**: The audio says *quince*, not fourteen. 

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Me gusta mucho escuchar música y bailar". This tells us Lucía dances alongside listening to music.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B

- **A**: The audio does not mention drawing.

- **B**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$No me gustan los videojuegos", which is the opposite of liking them

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Practico natación los martes y los jueves". This tells us Lucía swims on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

- *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

- **A**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$los martes y los jueves", not the weekends.

- **C**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$los martes y los jueves", not Thursdays and Fridays.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{los deportes}$    [1 mark]

- The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Me gustan más los deportes". Note that *prefiere* (in the question) and *me gustan más* (in the audio) have the same meaning. This tells us Lucía prefers sports to video games.
