## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Las respuestas pueden estar en cualquier orden.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, C, E}$        [3 marks]

* **B**: The option says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hago actividades al aire libre" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I do outdoor activities"). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$me gusta caminar en el parque” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I like walking in the park"). This shows that the person does activities outdoors.

* **C**: The option says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Los s$\text{\'{a}}$bados y los domingos veo a mis amistades” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$On Saturdays and Sundays I see my friends"). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$los fines de semana salgo con amigos” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$on weekends I go out with friends"). This means they see their friends on weekends.

* **E**: The option says: *Es importante descansar* (*It is important to rest*). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$es necesario tener tiempo para relajarse” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it is necessary to have time to relax"). This means resting is important.

* *Opciones Incorrectas:*  A, D, F

* **A**: The option says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Siempre estoy en casa” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am always at home"). This is not correct because the text says the person goes out: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$caminar en el parque” and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$voy al cine.”

* **D**: The option says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Es irrelevante estar tranquilo” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$It is not important to be calm"). This is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$es necesario tener tiempo para relajarse.”

* **F**: The option says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Los d$\text{\'{\i}}$as que no tengo colegio, salgo con mis padres” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$On days I don't have school, I go out with my parents"). This is not correct because the text only mentions*amigos*, not parents.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* The audience of the text is *a friend* (*un amigo*). The text is personal and directed to one person. For example: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$¡Hola Luis!” shows that the writer is speaking directly to someone they know. The content talks about personal activities and daily life, which is typical when writing to a friend.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C, D

* **A**: This is not correct because the option says *un profesor* (*a teacher*), but the greeting in the text is not formal.

* **C**: This is not correct because the option says *una empresa* (*a company*), but the text is not professional and does not include any formal purpose.

* **D**: This is not correct because the option says *un grupo de estudiantes* (*a group of students*), but the text is addressed to one person (Luis), not a group.

The text lists activities, for example: *escucho m$\textit{\'{u}}$sica*, *veo pel$\textit{\'{\i}}$culas*, and *salgo con amigos*.
