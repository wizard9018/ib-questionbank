## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{jugar a las canicas}$    [1 mark]

* The question says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$para aprender a...” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to learn to..."). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$me ense$\text{\~{n}\'{o}}$ a jugar a las canicas” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$he taught me to play marbles"). This shows what he learned to do.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{el suelo}$    [1 mark]

* The question says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$en el que se usan bolas de cristal sobre...” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$in which glass balls are used on..."). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$se juega en el suelo” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it is played on the ground"). This shows where the game takes place.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{un pastel}$    [1 mark]

- The question says: *preparamos juntos...* (*we prepared together...*). The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hice un pastel con mi madre” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I made a cake with my mother"). This shows what they baked.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{G}$    [1 mark]

* *juego* (*game*) refers to something fun that you do in your free time. In the text, it says that it was $\text{\textquotedblleft}$una distracci$\text{\'{o}}$n nueva,” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$a new distraction") which indicates it is something enjoyable, similar to a game.:br

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* B

* **B**: *ejercicio* (*exercise*) is not correct because it refers to physical or school activity, while the text says *distracci$\textit{\'{o}}$n* (*distraction*), which indicates something for fun.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *hogar* (*home*) refers to the place where you live. In the text, it says $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{volv\'{\i}}$ a casa,” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I returned home") which indicates the place where the person lives.:br

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* D

* **D**: *sitio* (*place*) is not correct because it means a general place, not specifically where you live.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

* *torneo* (*tournament*) refers to a situation where people compete. In the text, it says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hicimos una competici$\text{\'{o}}$n” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we made a contest"), which indicates a contest or tournament.:br

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:* F

* **F**: *proyecto* (*project*) is not correct because it refers to a plan or school work, not a *contest*.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *instituto* (*high school*) refers to the place where you study. In the text, it says $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{prepar\'{e}}$ mis cosas para la escuela” ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I prepared my things for school"), which indicates a place of study.:br

* *Opci$\textit{\'{o}}$n incorrecta:*

* **H**: *trabajo* (*work*) is not correct because it refers to a workplace, not a place where you study.
