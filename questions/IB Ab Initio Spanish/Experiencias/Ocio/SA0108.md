## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Bienvenidos al Estadio Nacional de Santiago". This tells us the match is being played in Santiago.

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

* **A**: The audio says *Santiago*, not Valpara$\text{\'{\i}}$so.

* **C**: The audio says *Santiago*, not Concepci$\text{\'{o}}$n.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$El partido empez$\text{\'{o}}$ hace diez minutos". This tells us the match has been going for ten minutes.:br

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, B

* **A**: The audio says *diez minutos*, not five. Note that five minutes is when Cat$\text{\'{o}}$lica had their chance, not how long the match has been going.

* **B**: The audio says *diez minutos*, not fifteen.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$el marcador est$\text{\'{a}}$ cero a cero". This tells us neither team has scored yet.:br

* *Opciones incorrectas:* A, C

* **A**: The audio says *cero a cero*, not one to zero.

* **C**: The audio says *cero a cero*, not one to one.

$4 - 6.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Las respuestas pueden estar escritas en cualquier orden.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, C, E }$        [3 marks]

* **B**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$sus delanteros son muy r$\text{\'{a}}$pidos y peligrosos". This tells us Universidad de Chile's forwards are very fast.

* **C**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la Universidad Cat$\text{\'{o}}$lica tuvo una gran oportunidad de gol en el minuto cinco". Note that*ocasi$\textit{\'{o}}$n de marcar* and *oportunidad de gol*have the same meaning. This tells us a Cat$\text{\'{o}}$lica player had a chance to score.

* **E**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$El ambiente en el estadio es incre$\text{\'{\i}}$ble". Note that*fant$\textit{\'{a}}$stico* and *incre$\textit{\'{\i}}$ble* have the same meaning. This tells us the atmosphere in the stadium is fantastic.

* *Opciones Incorrectas:*  A, D, F

* **A**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$La Universidad de Chile tiene m$\text{\'{a}}$s posesi$\text{\'{o}}$n del bal$\text{\'{o}}$n". This means the two teams do not have equal possession.

* **D**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$el portero la detuvo". This means the goalkeeper stopped the shot, the opposite of what statement D says.

* **F**: The audio says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hay m$\text{\'{a}}$s de cuarenta mil aficionados". This means there are more than forty thousand spectators, not fewer.
