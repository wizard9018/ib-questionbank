# SA0300

**Match each sentence to the correct form of *ir* + *a*. There are more options than sentences.** [3 marks]

|Sentence|Options|

|:--|:--|

|Este fin de semana yo **[-1-]** visitar un museo.|A.$\hspace{1em}$vamos a|

|¿Tú **[-2-]** hacer una excursión el sábado?|B.$\hspace{1em}$vas a|

|Mi hermana **[-3-]** viajar a Bogotá el domingo.$\hspace{3em}$|C.$\hspace{1em}$voy a|

| |D.$\hspace{1em}$va a|

| |F.$\hspace{1em}$vais a|

**Complete the sentences with the correct form of *ir* + *a*. Choose from the following options:**

*vamos a*, *vais a*, *van a*

$\hspace{1em}$Mañana mi hermano y yo **[-4-]** preparar la maleta.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$¿Vosotros **[-5-]** visitar el centro de la ciudad?    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Mis primos **[-6-]** jugar al voleibol con sus amigos.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- *Voy a* is correct because the subject is (*yo*). The structure *ir* + *a* + infinitive is used to express a future plan: *voy a visitar*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, D, E, F

- **A**: *Vamos a* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but the subject is (*yo*).

- **B**: *Vas a* is the (*tú*) form, but the subject is (*yo*).

- **D**: *Va a* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but the subject is (*yo*).

- **E**: *Van a* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but the subject is (*yo*).

- **F**: *Vais a* is the (*vosotros/as*) form, but the subject is (*yo*).

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- *Vas a* is correct because the subject is (*tú*). The structure *ir* + *a* + infinitive is used to express a future plan: *vas a hacer*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, C, D, E, F

- **A**: *Vamos a* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but the subject is (*tú*).

- **C**: *Voy a* is the (*yo*) form, but the subject is (*tú*).

- **D**: *Va a* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but the subject is (*tú*).

- **E**: *Van a* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but the subject is (*tú*).

- **F**: *Vais a* is the (*vosotros/as*) form, but the subject is (*tú*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- *Va a* is correct because *mi hermana* refers to (*ella*). The structure *ir* + *a* + infinitive is used to express a future plan: *va a viajar*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C, E, F

- **A**: *Vamos a* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but *mi hermana* refers to (*ella*).

- **B**: *Vas a* is the (*tú*) form, but *mi hermana* refers to (*ella*).

- **C**: *Voy a* is the (*yo*) form, but *mi hermana* refers to (*ella*).

- **E**: *Van a* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but *mi hermana* refers to one person.

- **F**: *Vais a* is the (*vosotros/as*) form, but *mi hermana* refers to (*ella*).

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{vamos a}$    [1 mark]

- *Mi hermano y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*), so the correct form is *vamos a*. The complete structure is *vamos a preparar*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{vais a}$    [1 mark]

- The subject is (*vosotros*), so the correct form is *vais a*. The complete structure is *vais a visitar*.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{van a}$    [1 mark]

- *Mis primos* refers to (*ellos*), so the correct form is *van a*. The complete structure is *van a jugar*.

**Grammar reminder:**

In Spanish, *ir* + *a* + infinitive is used to talk about future plans and intentions. The form of *ir* changes depending on the person. The infinitive does not change.

|Subject pronoun|*ir*|+ *a* + infinitive|

|:--|:--|:--|

|*yo*|*voy*|*a* + infinitive|

|*tú*|*vas*|*a* + infinitive|

|*él / ella / usted*|*va*|*a* + infinitive|

|*nosotros/as*|*vamos*|*a* + infinitive|

|*vosotros/as*|*vais*|*a* + infinitive|

|*ellos / ellas / ustedes*|*van*|*a* + infinitive|
