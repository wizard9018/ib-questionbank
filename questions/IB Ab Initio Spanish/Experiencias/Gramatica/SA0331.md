# SA0331

**Complete each sentence with the correct time expression. There are more options than gaps.**

$\hspace{1em}$Vivo en Costa Rica **[-1-]** tres años y todavía estoy aquí.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Voy a comenzar mi intercambio escolar **[-2-]** dos semanas.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$El verano pasado viví con una familia anfitriona **[-3-]** un mes.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$desde hace

- B.$\hspace{1em}$dentro de

- C.$\hspace{1em}$durante

- D.$\hspace{1em}$hace

- E.$\hspace{1em}$al cabo de

**Each underlined time expression is incorrect. Rewrite it correctly using an expression from the box. There are more options than gaps.** [4 marks]

Me mudé a Argentina **[-4-]** $\underline{\textrm{desde hace dos años}}$ y ahora vivo en Córdoba. Cuando llegué, estudié español **[-5-]** $\underline{\textrm{desde hace seis meses}}$ antes de empezar la universidad. Empecé la universidad y **[-6-]** $\underline{\textrm{al cabo cuatro meses}}$ conocí a mis mejores amigos. Ya tengo el billete para visitar a mi familia y voy a regresar a México **[-7-]** $\underline{\textrm{hace tres meses}}$.

- A.$\hspace{1em}$al cabo de

- B.$\hspace{1em}$dentro de

- C.$\hspace{1em}$durante

- D.$\hspace{1em}$hace

- E.$\hspace{1em}$desde

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- A. *Desde hace* is correct. It is used for an action that started in the past and is still happening: *vivo* and *todavía estoy aquí* show that I still live there.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:**

- B. *Dentro de* refers to the future, but *vivo* and *todavía estoy aquí* describe the present.

- C. *Durante* gives the length of a period, but it does not show that the action is still happening.

- D. *Hace* with a time means "ago" and goes with a completed past action, but *vivo* is in the present and the action continues.

- E. *Al cabo de* means "after" a period, but the sentence describes an action that is still happening.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- B. *Dentro de* is correct. It refers to a time in the future, and *voy a comenzar* shows that the exchange has not started yet.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:**

- A. *Desde hace* is used for an action that started in the past and is still happening, but the exchange has not started yet.

- C. *Durante* gives how long something lasts, not when it starts.

- D. *Hace* means "ago" and refers to the past, but *voy a comenzar* refers to the future.

- E. *Al cabo de* means "after" a period that follows another event. It does not count from now.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- C. *Durante* is correct. It gives the length of a completed period in the past: *viví* and *el verano pasado* show that the stay has ended.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:**

- A. *Desde hace* is used for an action that is still happening, but the stay ended last summer.

- B. *Dentro de* refers to the future, but *viví* is in the past.

- D. *Hace* means "ago" and tells you when something happened, but the sentence tells you how long the stay lasted.

- E. *Al cabo de* means "after" a period, but the sentence tells you how long the stay lasted.

**Model answer**: Vivo en Costa Rica desde hace tres años y todavía estoy aquí. Voy a comenzar mi intercambio escolar dentro de dos semanas. El verano pasado viví con una familia anfitriona durante un mes.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{hace dos años}$    [1 mark]

- *Hace dos años* is correct. *Me mudé* is a completed action in the past, and *hace* + time means "ago".

- *Desde hace* is incorrect because the move is finished and did not continue. It is only used for actions that are still happening, like *vivo en Córdoba desde hace dos años*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{durante seis meses}$    [1 mark]

- *Durante seis meses* is correct. *Estudié* and *antes de empezar la universidad* show a completed period in the past, and *durante* gives its length.

- *Desde hace* is incorrect because I am not studying Spanish now. That period ended before I started university.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{al cabo de cuatro meses}$    [1 mark]

- *Al cabo de cuatro meses* is correct. It means "after four months", counted from when I started university.

- The error is the missing *de*. The expression is always *al cabo de* + time.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{dentro de tres meses}$    [1 mark]

- *Dentro de tres meses* is correct. *Voy a regresar* and *ya tengo el billete* show a future plan, and *dentro de* refers to a time in the future.

- *Hace* is incorrect because it means "ago" and refers to the past.

**Model answer**: Me mudé a Argentina hace dos años y ahora vivo en Córdoba. Cuando llegué, estudié español durante seis meses antes de empezar la universidad. Empecé la universidad y al cabo de cuatro meses conocí a mis mejores amigos. Ya tengo el billete para visitar a mi familia y voy a regresar a México dentro de tres meses.
