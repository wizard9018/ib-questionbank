# SA0274

**Complete the paragraph with the correct present tense form of *ser* or *estar*.** [3 marks]

Este verano mi familia y yo **[-1-]** en Cartagena. Cartagena **[-2-]** una ciudad turística de Colombia. Hoy mi hermano **[-3-]** muy cansado después de una excursión.

**Put the words in the correct order. Choose and conjugate the correct verb. Begin with the subject and write the complete sentence.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$de Colombia / nuestra guía / (*ser* / *estar*)    [1 mark]

**The sentence contains $\underline{\textbf{one}}$ error in the use of *ser* or *estar*. Find the error and rewrite the complete sentence correctly.**

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$El hotel es cerca de la playa.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{estamos}$    [1 mark]

- *Mi familia y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*), so the correct form is *estamos*. We use *estar* to express location.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{es}$    [1 mark]

- *Cartagena* refers to (*ella*), so the correct form is *es*. We use *ser* to say what something is. *Cartagena* is a place, but this sentence describes the city, not where it is.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{est\'{a}}$    [1 mark]

- *Mi hermano* refers to (*él*), so the correct form is *está*. We use *estar* to express how someone feels.\

\

**Model answer**: Este verano mi familia y yo estamos en Cartagena. Cartagena es una ciudad turística de Colombia. Hoy mi hermano está muy cansado después de una excursión.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Nuestra gu\'{\i}a es de Colombia.}$    [1 mark]

- *Nuestra guía* is the subject and refers to (*ella*), so the correct form is *es*. We use *ser* with *de* to express origin. The origin *de Colombia* comes after the verb.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{El hotel est\'{a} cerca de la playa.}$    [1 mark]

- *Es* is a form of *ser*, but *cerca de la playa* expresses location, so we use *estar*. *El hotel* refers to (*él*), so the correct form is *está*.
