# SA0304

**Complete the sentences with the correct preterite form from the following verbs:**

*escribieron*, *$\textit{visit\'{e}}$*, *escribimos*, *$\textit{viaj\'{o}}$*, *$\textit{visit\'{o}}$*

$\hspace{1em}$Ayer yo **\[-1-]** el centro $\text{hist\'{o}rico}$.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$El verano pasado, Marta **\[-2-]** a Costa Rica.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Durante las vacaciones, mis hermanos **\[-3-]** una postal a nuestros abuelos.    [1 mark]

**Choose the correct preterite form.**

$\hspace{1em}$Anoche $\text{t\'{u}}$ **\[-4-]** en casa de tus abuelos.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}\text{com\'{\i}}$

* B.$\hspace{1em}$comiste

* C.$\hspace{1em}\text{comi\'{o}}$

* D.$\hspace{1em}$comimos

$\hspace{1em}$La semana pasada, mis amigos y yo **\[-5-]** el sol en la playa.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}\text{tom\'{o}}$

* B.$\hspace{1em}$tomaste

* C.$\hspace{1em}$tomaron

* D.$\hspace{1em}$tomamos

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{visit\'{e}}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is (*yo*). With (*yo*), regular *-ar* verbs in the preterite end in *$\textit{-\'{e}}$*, so the correct form is *$\textit{visit\'{e}}$*.

* The correct spelling is *$\textit{visit\'{e}}$*, with an accent on the *$\textit{\'{e}}$*. The mark is not lost for an accent error, but it is important to learn the correct spelling.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{viaj\'{o}}$    [1 mark]

* *Marta* refers to (*ella*). With (*ella*), regular *-ar* verbs in the preterite end in *$\textit{-\'{o}}$*, so the correct form is *$\textit{viaj\'{o}}$*.

* The correct spelling is *$\textit{viaj\'{o}}$*, with an accent on the *$\textit{\'{o}}$*. The mark is not lost for an accent error, but it is important to learn the correct spelling.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{escribieron}$    [1 mark]

* *Mis hermanos* refers to (*ellos*). With (*ellos*), regular *-ir* verbs in the preterite end in *-ieron*, so the correct form is *escribieron*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* *Comiste* is correct because the subject is (*$\textit{t\'{u}}$*). With (*$\textit{t\'{u}}$*), regular *-er* verbs in the preterite end in *-iste*.

* **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, C, D

* **A**: *$\textit{Com\'{\i}}$* is the (*yo*) form, but the subject is (*$\textit{t\'{u}}$*).

* **C**: *$\textit{Comi\'{o}}$* is the (*$\textit{\'{e}l/ella/usted}$*) form, but the subject is (*$\textit{t\'{u}}$*).

* **D**: *Comimos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form, but the subject is (*$\textit{t\'{u}}$*).

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *Tomamos* is correct because *mis amigos y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*). With (*nosotros*), regular *-ar* verbs in the preterite end in *-amos*. This is the same form as the present tense, so the time expression *la semana pasada* shows that the action is in the past.

* **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C

* **A**: *$\textit{Tom\'{o}}$* is the (*$\textit{\'{e}l/ella/usted}$*) form, but *mis amigos y yo* refers to (*nosotros*).

* **B**: *Tomaste* is the (*$\textit{t\'{u}}$*) form, but *mis amigos y yo* refers to (*nosotros*).

* **C**: *Tomaron* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but *mis amigos y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotros*).

**Grammar Reminder**:

The preterite is used for completed actions in the past. Time expressions such as *ayer*, *anoche* and *el $\textit{a\~{n}o}$ pasado* often indicate that the preterite is needed.

For regular verbs, remove the infinitive ending and add the correct preterite ending:

| $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{0.7em}$ $\textrm{Habl}$ar$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{0.7em}$ $\textrm{Com}$er$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{0.7em}$ $\textrm{Viv}$ir$\hspace{1em}$ |

| :- | :- | :- | :- |

| $\hspace{1em}$Yo | $\hspace{1em}$hab&#x6C;**$\textbf{\'{e}}$** | $\hspace{1em}$co&#x6D;**$\textbf{\'{\i}}$** | $\hspace{1em}$vi&#x76;**$\textbf{\'{\i}}$** |

| $\hspace{1em}\text{T\'{u}}$ | $\hspace{1em}$habl**aste** | $\hspace{1em}$com**iste** | $\hspace{1em}$viv**iste** |

| $\hspace{1em}\text{\'{E}l,}$ ella, usted | $\hspace{1em}$hab&#x6C;**$\textbf{\'{o}}$** | $\hspace{1em}$com&#x69;**$\textbf{\'{o}}$** | $\hspace{1em}$viv&#x69;**$\textbf{\'{o}}$** |

| $\hspace{1em}$Nosotros/as | $\hspace{1em}$habl**amos** | $\hspace{1em}$com**imos** | $\hspace{1em}$viv**imos** |

| $\hspace{1em}$Vosotros/as | $\hspace{1em}$habl**asteis**$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$com**isteis**$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$viv**isteis** $\hspace{1em}$ |

| $\hspace{1em}$Ellos, ellas, ustedes$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$habl**aron** | $\hspace{1em}$com**ieron** | $\hspace{1em}$viv**ieron** |

Some common verbs do not follow the regular preterite patterns. Their forms must be memorised. The forms below follow this order: *yo, $\textit{t\'{u}}$, $\textit{\'{e}l/ella/usted}$, nosotros/as, vosotros/as, ellos/ellas/ustedes*.

* *ir/ser*: fui, fuiste, fue, fuimos, fuisteis, fueron

* *estar*: estuve, estuviste, estuvo, estuvimos, estuvisteis, estuvieron

* *hacer*: hice, hiciste, hizo, hicimos, hicisteis, hicieron

* *tener*: tuve, tuviste, tuvo, tuvimos, tuvisteis, tuvieron
