# SA0364

**Match each sentence to the correct verb form in the preterite or the imperfect. There are more options than sentences.** [3 marks]

$\begin{array}{ll}

\textbf{Sentence}\hspace{2em} & \textbf{Options} \\

\text{El verano pasado yo } \textbf{[-1-]} \text{ a Perú.}\hspace{2em} & \text{A.}\hspace{1em}\text{viajaba} \\

\text{De niña, mi hermana } \textbf{[-2-]} \text{ el piano todos los días.}\hspace{2em} & \text{B.}\hspace{1em}\text{tocó} \\

\text{Cuando llegamos a la estación, } \textbf{[-3-]} \text{ mucho frío.}\hspace{2em} & \text{C.}\hspace{1em}\text{hacía} \\

 & \text{D.}\hspace{1em}\text{viajé} \\

 & \text{E.}\hspace{1em}\text{tocaba} \\

 & \text{F.}\hspace{1em}\text{hizo}

\end{array}$

**Choose the correct option.**

$\hspace{1em}$Cuando **[-4-]** diez años, vivía en Sevilla.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$tuve

- B.$\hspace{1em}$tenía

- C.$\hspace{1em}$tengo

- D.$\hspace{1em}$tenían

$\hspace{1em}$Ayer, después de clase, mis amigos y yo **[-5-]** al fútbol en el parque.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$jugábamos

- B.$\hspace{1em}$jugamos

- C.$\hspace{1em}$jugaron

- D.$\hspace{1em}$jugaba

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- D. *Viajé* is correct. *El verano pasado* refers to a specific, completed time in the past, so it needs the preterite. The (yo) form of *viajar* in the preterite is *viajé*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:**

- A. *Viajaba* is the imperfect of *viajar*. It is the right verb, but the wrong tense: the imperfect is for habits or descriptions, not for one completed trip.

- B. *Tocó* is the (él/ella/usted) preterite form of *tocar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for *yo*.

- C. *Hacía* is the imperfect of *hacer*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong tense.

- E. *Tocaba* is the imperfect of *tocar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong tense.

- F. *Hizo* is the (él/ella/usted) preterite form of *hacer*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for *yo*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- E. *Tocaba* is correct. *De niña* and *todos los días* describe a habit in the past, so it needs the imperfect. The (ella) form of *tocar* in the imperfect is *tocaba*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:**

- A. *Viajaba* is the imperfect of *viajar*. It is the right tense, but the wrong verb: you play the piano, you don't travel it.

- B. *Tocó* is the preterite of *tocar*. It is the right verb, but the wrong tense: the preterite is for one completed action, but *todos los días* shows a repeated habit.

- C. *Hacía* is the imperfect of *hacer*. It is the right tense, but the wrong verb.

- D. *Viajé* is the (yo) preterite form of *viajar*. It is the wrong verb, the wrong tense and the wrong form.

- F. *Hizo* is the preterite of *hacer*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong tense.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- C. *Hacía* is correct. *Mucho frío* describes the weather, the background situation when we arrived, so it needs the imperfect. Weather uses *hacer*: *hacía frío*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:**

- A. *Viajaba* is the imperfect of *viajar*. It is the right tense, but the wrong verb.

- B. *Tocó* is the preterite of *tocar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong tense.

- D. *Viajé* is the preterite of *viajar*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong tense.

- E. *Tocaba* is the imperfect of *tocar*. It is the right tense, but the wrong verb.

- F. *Hizo* is the preterite of *hacer*. It is the right verb, but the wrong tense: *llegamos* is the action, and the cold is the description of the scene, so it needs the imperfect.

**Model answer**: El verano pasado yo viajé a Perú. De niña, mi hermana tocaba el piano todos los días. Cuando llegamos a la estación, hacía mucho frío.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- B. *Tenía* is correct. Age in the past is a description, so it needs the imperfect. *Vivía* shows that the subject is *yo*, and the (yo) form of *tener* in the imperfect is *tenía*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:**

- A. *Tuve* is the preterite of *tener*. Age in the past is a description, not a completed action, so it needs the imperfect.

- C. *Tengo* is the present tense, but the sentence is about the past.

- D. *Tenían* is the (ellos/ellas/ustedes) form of the imperfect, but *vivía* shows that the subject is *yo*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- B. *Jugamos* is correct. *Ayer* refers to a specific, completed time, so it needs the preterite. *Mis amigos y yo* refers to *nosotros*, and the (nosotros/as) form of *jugar* in the preterite is *jugamos*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:**

- A. *Jugábamos* is the (nosotros/as) form of the imperfect. It is the right form, but the wrong tense: the imperfect is for habits, but *ayer* refers to one completed action.

- C. *Jugaron* is the (ellos/ellas/ustedes) form of the preterite. It is the right tense, but *mis amigos y yo* includes the speaker, so it refers to *nosotros*.

- D. *Jugaba* is the (yo) or (él/ella/usted) form of the imperfect. It is the wrong tense and the wrong form.

**Model answer**: Cuando tenía diez años, vivía en Sevilla. Ayer, después de clase, mis amigos y yo jugamos al fútbol en el parque.

**Grammar reminder**:

Both tenses talk about the past, but they do different jobs.

| | Preterite | Imperfect |

|---|---|---|

| Use it for | one completed action at a specific time | habits, descriptions, age, weather, background |

| Key words | ayer, el sábado pasado, en 2020, una vez | de niño/a, todos los días, siempre, normalmente |

**Regular endings**

| | -ar preterite | -er/-ir preterite | -ar imperfect | -er/-ir imperfect |

|---|---|---|---|---|

| yo | -é | -í | -aba | -ía |

| tú | -aste | -iste | -abas | -ías |

| él/ella/usted | -ó | -ió | -aba | -ía |

| nosotros/as | -amos | -imos | -ábamos | -íamos |

| vosotros/as | -asteis | -isteis | -abais | -íais |

| ellos/ellas/ustedes | -aron | -ieron | -aban | -ían |

**Watch out:** when one action interrupts a scene, the action is in the preterite and the scene is in the imperfect.

Examples: El sábado pasado visité a mis abuelos. De pequeño, mi primo nadaba en el río todos los veranos. Cuando salimos del cine, llovía.
