# SA0293

**Each sentence contains $\underline{\textbf{one}}$ error with a reflexive verb in the present tense. Find the error and write the correct form of the reflexive verb.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Yo se cepillo los dientes después de comer.    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Mis hermanas se peina antes de salir.    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Mi padre se afeitan todas las mañanas.    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Ana y yo nos maquilláis para la fiesta.    [1 mark]

**Match each sentence to the correct present tense form of the reflexive verb. There are more options than sentences.** [3 marks]

|Sentence|Options|

|:--|:--|

|Yo **[-5-]** con agua caliente por la noche.|A.$\hspace{1em}$se relajan|

|Tú **[-6-]** el pelo con un secador.|B.$\hspace{1em}$nos bañamos|

|Mis padres **[-7-]** en el sofá después de cenar.$\hspace{3em}$|C.$\hspace{1em}$te secas|

| |D.$\hspace{1em}$me baño|

| |E.$\hspace{1em}$os secáis|

| |F.$\hspace{1em}$se relaja|

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{me cepillo}$    [1 mark]

- *Se* does not match the subject (*yo*). The reflexive pronoun for (*yo*) is *me*, so the correct form is *me cepillo*.\

\

**Model answer**: Yo me cepillo los dientes después de comer.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{se peinan}$    [1 mark]

- *Se peina* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but *mis hermanas* refers to (*ellas*), so the correct form is *se peinan*.\

\

**Model answer**: Mis hermanas se peinan antes de salir.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{se afeita}$    [1 mark]

- *Se afeitan* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but *mi padre* refers to (*él*), so the correct form is *se afeita*.\

\

**Model answer**: Mi padre se afeita todas las mañanas.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{nos maquillamos}$    [1 mark]

- *Maquilláis* is the (*vosotros/as*) form, but *Ana y yo* includes the speaker and refers to (*nosotras*). The pronoun *nos* is correct, so the correct form is *nos maquillamos*.\

\

**Model answer**: Ana y yo nos maquillamos para la fiesta.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- *Me baño* is correct because the subject is (*yo*) and *con agua caliente* shows that the action is having a bath. The reflexive pronoun for (*yo*) is *me*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, C, E, F

- **A**: *Se relajan* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *relajarse*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*yo*).

- **B**: *Nos bañamos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form of *bañarse*, but the subject is (*yo*).

- **C**: *Te secas* is the (*tú*) form of *secarse*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*yo*).

- **E**: *Os secáis* is the (*vosotros/as*) form of *secarse*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*yo*).

- **F**: *Se relaja* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form of *relajarse*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*yo*).

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- *Te secas* is correct because the subject is (*tú*) and *con un secador* shows that the action is drying. The reflexive pronoun for (*tú*) is *te*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** A, B, D, E, F

- **A**: *Se relajan* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form of *relajarse*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

- **B**: *Nos bañamos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form of *bañarse*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

- **D**: *Me baño* is the (*yo*) form of *bañarse*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

- **E**: *Os secáis* is the (*vosotros/as*) form of *secarse*, but the subject is (*tú*).

- **F**: *Se relaja* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form of *relajarse*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*tú*).

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- *Se relajan* is correct because *mis padres* refers to (*ellos*) and *en el sofá después de cenar* shows that the action is relaxing. The reflexive pronoun for (*ellos*) is *se*.

- **Respuestas incorrectas:** B, C, D, E, F

- **B**: *Nos bañamos* is the (*nosotros/as*) form of *bañarse*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*ellos*).

- **C**: *Te secas* is the (*tú*) form of *secarse*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*ellos*).

- **D**: *Me baño* is the (*yo*) form of *bañarse*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*ellos*).

- **E**: *Os secáis* is the (*vosotros/as*) form of *secarse*. It is the wrong verb and the wrong form for (*ellos*).

- **F**: *Se relaja* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form of *relajarse*, but *mis padres* refers to more than one person.
