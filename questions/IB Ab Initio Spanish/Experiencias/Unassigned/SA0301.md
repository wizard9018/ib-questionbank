# SA0301

**Put the words in the correct order. Begin with the subject and write the complete sentence.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$voy / en tren / a / yo / viajar    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$va / Sara / una tarta / a / preparar    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$¿vas / tú / a / salir / esta noche?    [1 mark]

**Each sentence contains $\underline{\textbf{one}}$ error with *ir* + *a* + infinitive. Find the error and write the correct form.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Mañana yo voy jugar al tenis.    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$¿Tú vas a nadas en la piscina?    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Yo voy a viajar en tren.}$    [1 mark]

- The sentence begins with the subject *yo*. *Voy a viajar* stays together because it follows the structure *ir* + *a* + infinitive. *En tren* comes after the verb.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Sara va a preparar una tarta.}$    [1 mark]

- The sentence begins with the subject *Sara*. *Va a preparar* stays together because it follows the structure *ir* + *a* + infinitive. *Una tarta* comes after the verb.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{¿T\'{u} vas a salir esta noche?}$    [1 mark]

- The sentence begins with the subject *tú*. *Vas a salir* stays together because it follows the structure *ir* + *a* + infinitive. *Esta noche* comes after the verb.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{voy a jugar}$    [1 mark]

- The word *a* is missing. The structure is *ir* + *a* + infinitive, so *a* is needed between *voy* and *jugar*.\

\

**Model answer**: Mañana yo voy a jugar al tenis.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{vas a nadar}$    [1 mark]

- After *vas a*, the next verb stays in the infinitive, so *nadas* changes to *nadar*.\

\

**Model answer**: ¿Tú vas a nadar en la piscina?
