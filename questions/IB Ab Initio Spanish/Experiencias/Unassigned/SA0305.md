# SA0305

**Each sentence contains $\underline{\textbf{one}}$ error with the preterite. Find the error and write the correct form.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Ayer yo fue al cine con mis amigos.    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$El viaje fueron muy divertido.    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Mario y Ana fue de vacaciones a Santiago.    [1 mark]

**Put the words in the correct order and write the verb in the correct preterite form. Begin with the subject and write the complete sentence.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$una novela / yo / (*leer*) / en el avión    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$sobre la cultura local / Lucía / (*aprender*)    [1 mark]

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$muchos lugares / tú / (*visitar*) / durante las vacaciones    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{fui}$    [1 mark]

- *Fue* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but the subject is (*yo*), so the correct preterite form of *ir* is *fui*.\

\

**Model answer**: Ayer yo fui al cine con mis amigos.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{fue}$    [1 mark]

- *Fueron* is the (*ellos/ellas/ustedes*) form, but *el viaje* refers to (*él*), so the correct preterite form of *ser* is *fue*.\

\

**Model answer**: El viaje fue muy divertido.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{fueron}$    [1 mark]

- *Fue* is the (*él/ella/usted*) form, but *Mario y Ana* refers to (*ellos*), so the correct preterite form of *ir* is *fueron*.\

\

**Model answer**: Mario y Ana fueron de vacaciones a Santiago.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Yo le\'{\i} una novela en el avi\'{o}n.}$    [1 mark]

- The subject is (*yo*), so the correct preterite form of *leer* is *leí*.

- The sentence begins with the subject *yo*, followed by the verb *leí*. *Una novela* and *en el avión* come after the verb.

- The correct spelling is *leí*, with an accent on the *í*. The mark is not lost for an accent error, but it is important to learn the correct spelling.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Luc\'{\i}a aprendi\'{o} sobre la cultura local.}$    [1 mark]

- *Lucía* refers to (*ella*), so the correct preterite form of *aprender* is *aprendió*.

- The sentence begins with the subject *Lucía*, followed by the verb *aprendió*. *Sobre la cultura local* comes after the verb.

- The correct spelling is *aprendió*, with an accent on the *ó*. The mark is not lost for an accent error, but it is important to learn the correct spelling.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{T\'{u} visitaste muchos lugares durante las vacaciones.}$    [1 mark]

- The subject is (*tú*), so the correct preterite form of *visitar* is *visitaste*.

- The sentence begins with the subject *tú*, followed by the verb *visitaste*. *Muchos lugares* and *durante las vacaciones* come after the verb.
