# SA0365

**Complete the sentences. Conjugate the verb in brackets in the $\textbf{pret\'{e}rito}$ indefinido or the imperfecto.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Cuando era joven, mi abuelo normalmente **\[-1-]** (desayunar) pan con tomate.    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$El $\text{s\'{a}bado}$ pasado, $\text{Luc\'{\i}a}$ **\[-2-]** (comprar) un vestido para la fiesta.    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ Eran las ocho de la tarde y **\[-3-]** (llover) mucho.    [1 mark]

**Each sentence contains one error in the $\textbf{pret\'{e}rito}$ indefinido or the imperfecto. The error is underlined. Write the correct verb form.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Ayer mis padres $\underline{\textrm{llegaban}}$ a casa a las diez.    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Antes, mi familia $\underline{\textrm{fue}}$ de vacaciones a la playa todos los veranos.    [1 mark]

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$Primero me $\text{duch\'{e}}$, $\text{despu\'{e}s}$ $\underline{\textrm{me vest\'{\i}a}}$ y luego $\text{sal\'{\i}}$ de casa.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{desayunaba}$    [1 mark]

* *Desayunaba* is correct because *normalmente* and *cuando era joven* describe a habit in the past, which requires the imperfecto. *Desayunaba* is the *$\textit{\'{e}l}$* form of *desayunar*, matching *mi abuelo*.

* *$\textit{Opci\'{o}n}$ incorrecta:*

* *$\textit{Desayun\'{o}}$* is incorrect because the $\text{pret\'{e}rito}$ indefinido presents breakfast as a single completed action, but *normalmente* shows it was a habit.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Cuando era joven, mi abuelo normalmente desayunaba pan con tomate.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{compr\'{o}}$    [1 mark]

* *$\textit{Compr\'{o}}$* is correct because *el $\textit{s\'{a}bado}$ pasado* refers to a specific completed moment, which requires the $\text{pret\'{e}rito}$ indefinido. *$\textit{Compr\'{o}}$* is the *ella* form of *comprar*, matching $\text{Luc\'{\i}a}$.

* *$\textit{Opci\'{o}n}$ incorrecta:*

* *Compraba* is incorrect because the imperfecto describes a habit or an action in progress, but *el $\textit{s\'{a}bado}$ pasado* refers to one completed purchase. Remember the accent: *compro* without the accent is the *yo* form of the present tense.\

  \

  **Model answer**: El $\text{s\'{a}bado}$ pasado, $\text{Luc\'{\i}a}$ $\text{compr\'{o}}$ un vestido para la fiesta.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{llov\'{\i}a}$    [1 mark]

* *$\textit{Llov\'{\i}a}$* is correct because the sentence describes the weather at a moment in the past (*eran las ocho de la tarde*), which requires the imperfecto.

* *$\textit{Opci\'{o}n}$ incorrecta:*

* *$\textit{Llovi\'{o}}$* is incorrect because the $\text{pret\'{e}rito}$ indefinido presents the rain as a completed event, but here the sentence describes the scene at eight o'clock.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Eran las ocho de la tarde y $\text{llov\'{\i}a}$ mucho.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{llegaron}$    [1 mark]

* *Llegaron* is correct because *ayer... a las diez* refers to one completed action at a specific time, which requires the $\text{pret\'{e}rito}$ indefinido. *Llegaban* is the imperfecto, which describes habits or actions in progress. *Llegaron* is the *ellos* form, matching *mis padres*.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Ayer mis padres llegaron a casa a las diez.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{iba}$    [1 mark]

* *Iba* is correct because *antes* and *todos los veranos* describe a habit in the past, which requires the imperfecto. *Fue* is the $\text{pret\'{e}rito}$ indefinido and presents the holiday as a single completed event. *Iba* is the *ella* form of *ir*, matching *mi familia*.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Antes, mi familia iba de vacaciones a la playa todos los veranos.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{me vest\'{\i}}$    [1 mark]

* *Me $\textit{vest\'{\i}}$* is correct because *primero... $\textit{despu\'{e}s}$... luego* presents a sequence of completed actions, which requires the $\text{pret\'{e}rito}$ indefinido, like *me $\textit{duch\'{e}}$* and *$\textit{sal\'{\i}}$*. *Me $\textit{vest\'{\i}a}$* is the imperfecto, which would describe an action in progress instead of the next step in the sequence.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Primero me $\text{duch\'{e}}$, $\text{despu\'{e}s}$ me $\text{vest\'{\i}}$ y luego $\text{sal\'{\i}}$ de casa.
