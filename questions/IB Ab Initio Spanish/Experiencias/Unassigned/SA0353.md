# SA0353

**Complete the sentences. Use the correct imperfect form of each verb in brackets.**

$\hspace{1em}$De pequeño, yo **[-1-]** (montar) en bicicleta todos los veranos.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Durante las vacaciones, mis padres **[-2-]** (leer) en el jardín por la tarde.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$En verano, ¿tú **[-3-]** (escribir) postales a tus abuelos?    [1 mark]

**The sentences below each contain $\underline{\textbf{one}}$ imperfect verb error. The errors are underlined. Write the correct words.** 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Durante las vacaciones, mis hermanos $\underline{\textrm{sacaba}}$ muchas fotos.    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$De pequeña, yo $\underline{\textrm{jugabas}}$ en la playa todos los días.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{montaba}$    [1 mark]

- *Montaba* is correct because the subject is *yo*. *Montar* is an *-ar* verb, and the (*yo*) ending in the imperfect is *-aba*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{leían}$    [1 mark]

- *Leían* is correct because *mis padres* refers to more than one person (*ellos*). *Leer* is an *-er* verb, and the (*ellos*) ending in the imperfect is *-ían*, with an accent on the *í*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{escribías}$    [1 mark]

- *Escribías* is correct because the subject is *tú*. *Escribir* is an *-ir* verb, and the (*tú*) ending in the imperfect is *-ías*, with an accent on the *í*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{sacaban}$    [1 mark]

- *Sacaba* must change to *sacaban* because *mis hermanos* refers to more than one person (*ellos*). *Sacaba* is the (*yo*) or (*él/ella*) form. The (*ellos*) ending for *-ar* verbs in the imperfect is *-aban*.

- **Model answer:** Durante las vacaciones, mis hermanos *sacaban* muchas fotos.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{jugaba}$    [1 mark]

- *Jugabas* must change to *jugaba* because the subject is *yo*. *Jugabas* is the (*tú*) form. The (*yo*) ending for *-ar* verbs in the imperfect is *-aba*.

- **Model answer:** De pequeña, yo *jugaba* en la playa todos los días.
