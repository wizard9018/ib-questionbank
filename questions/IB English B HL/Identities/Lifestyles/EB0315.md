# EB0315

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a person talking about bouldering\*.**

:audio{#571c9181-04c9-4691-abdf-2f291005b383 aid="571c9181-04c9-4691-abdf-2f291005b383"}

**Choose the three true statements.**[3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Bouldering pushes you physically and mentally. 

- B.$\hspace{1em}$When you climb, you have to think and find solutions. 

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Each route is designed to follow exact steps. 

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Bouldering makes you more relaxed. 

- E.$\hspace{1em}$Climbers are very competitive. 

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Climbing is a solitary sport. 

**Choose the correct answer.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Bouldering is a great way to...    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$release stress.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$be balanced.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$improve your health.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Some people feel bouldering...    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$is a great pastime.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$is a part of their identity.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$is stressful.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, B, D}$        [3 marks]

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$It's a sport that challenges both your body and your mind.”\

The original sentence says the sport $\text{\textquotedblleft}$challenges both your body and your mind.” The new version uses *pushes* instead of *challenges* and replaces $\text{\textquotedblleft}$body and mind” with $\text{\textquotedblleft}$physically and mentally.”

- **B**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Bouldering teaches problem-solving. Each route is like a puzzle.”\

The new sentence replaces $\text{\textquotedblleft}$problem-solving” with $\text{\textquotedblleft}$think and find solutions.”

- **D**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$After a stressful day, climbing \

The new sentence simplifies this idea by using $\text{\textquotedblleft}$makes you more relaxed.”

- *Incorrect options:*  C, E, F

- **C**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Each route is like a puzzle, and you need to find the best way to reach the top.”\

The new sentence adds $\text{\textquotedblleft}$exact steps,” which is incorrect because the text suggests creativity, not fixed moves.

- **E**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Climbers support each other”\

The new sentence changes the meaning by saying $\text{\textquotedblleft}$competitive,” which is the opposite of $\text{\textquotedblleft}$support each other.”

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$It's a social sport”\

The new sentence uses $\text{\textquotedblleft}$solitary,” which is wrong because the text shows climbing is social.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$After a stressful day, climbing helps you focus and disconnect.” \

This clearly means it helps reduce or release stress.

- *Incorrect options:*  B, C

- Option **B** is confusing. The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Finally, bouldering gives you balance.” This refers to life balance. However, the statement is about what bouldering is good for.

- Option **C** is incorrect. The text mentions improved strength and flexibility, which are health benefits, so this is partly true, but stress relief is the most explicit benefit mentioned.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$it becomes part of who they are.” \

This option is correct as the phrase $\text{\textquotedblleft}$part of their identity” matches the original idea of becoming $\text{\textquotedblleft}$part of who they are”

- *Incorrect options:*  A, C

- Option **A** is incorrect.  The text mentions that bouldering is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$not just a hobby” or a *pastime*; it says it becomes something deeper than a hobby.

- Option **C** is incorrect. The text says the opposite: it helps after a stressful day, so this is wrong.
