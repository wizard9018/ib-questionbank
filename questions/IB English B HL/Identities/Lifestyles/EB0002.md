**Maximum mark: 6**

$\Large{\textrm{Is social media making you unhappy? The answer is not so simple}}$

Social media is a big part of many people's lives, but does it make us happier? Studies show the answer is not so clear. Some people feel worse when they spend a lot of time online, especially if they compare themselves to others. This can lead to feelings of envy and sadness. But the cause might not be social media itself. Instead, how you feel about using it matters.

If you feel like you are using social media in a way that works for you, it can be a good experience. For example, young people often use social media to stay connected with friends and express themselves. This can make them feel more positive.

On the other hand, if you feel like social media is controlling you, it can lead to negative feelings. It's important to remember that social media is just one part of your online life. It's best to use it in a balanced way and stay mindful of what you see and how it makes you feel. If social media starts making you unhappy, it might be time to change how you use it.

[Humphries, M., & Mitchell, L. (2024, June 25). Is social media making you unhappy? The answer is not so simple. University of Adelaide. The Conversation. https://www.theconversation.com/is-social-media-making-you-unhappy-the-answer-is-not-so-simple-232490. Accessed October 23, 2024.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 4.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers 1 to 4 can be written in any order.* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, D, F}$

- **A**. The text implies that frequent exposure to curated online content can influence how individuals define their own success and happiness. [1 mark]

- **C**. While not explicitly stated, the text implies that social media serves as a platform for self-discovery and expression, especially among young people. [1 mark]

- **D.** The text hints that increased interaction can lead to feelings of dissatisfaction, suggesting a disconnect between engagement and emotional fulfilment. [1 mark]

- **F**. The text highlights the importance of being mindful of social media's effect on emotions, suggesting that mindfulness can improve ones mental state.

    *Incorrect options*: B, E, G, and H.

- **B**. This is incorrect because the text indicates that many users may feel worse when comparing themselves to others rather than feeling empowered.

- **E**. This overlooks the text's indication that social media significantly affects emotional well-being, as it highlights both positive and negative influences.

- **G**. The text suggests that while social media can provide an escape, it also has the potential to create negative feelings, leading to a more complex relationship with emotional well-being.

- **H**. While some users might have varying experiences, the text suggests that changing how one uses social media can lead to a more positive emotional experience, making this statement potentially misleading.
