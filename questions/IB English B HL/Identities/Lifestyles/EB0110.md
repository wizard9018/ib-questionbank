# EB0110

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a short text on to-do-lists enthusiasts**

:audio{#1f39dffd-14a5-4ebb-883f-613f044ae8db aid="1f39dffd-14a5-4ebb-883f-613f044ae8db"}

**Answer the following questions:**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ What do to-do list enthusiasts like breaking into small, easy steps?     [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$How do list makers describe big tasks broken into smaller ones?    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$What feeling do list makers get when they complete a task?    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$What do the writers compare list makers' pens to?    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$How do list makers deal with stress and messiness?    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{their notebook (s) }$   [1 mark]

-   “Their notebooks are full of tasks, each one waiting to be ticked off.”

- The text points to notebooks as the place where to-do-lists enthusiasts tick off tasks.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{big jobs}$   [1 mark]

-   “These list lovers break big jobs into small, easy steps"

- Complicated tasks, big tasks that are broken into smaller ones are called “big jobs” in the text.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{feel like winners}$ or $\textrm{feel like true winners}$ or $\textrm{feel winners}$ [1 mark]

-   $\text{\textquotedblleft}$When they complete something, they feel like true winners"

- The act of breaking big tasks into smaller ones brings the users a feeling of achievement, closure, victory.

$4$.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{magic wands}$ [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Their pens are like magic wands"

- The text compares pens to magical instruments- wands.

$5$.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{smart planning}$ [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$These list makers are like heroes who beat messiness and stress with their smart planning"

- The text points to smart planning as a way of dealing with stress and messiness.
