# EB0276

**Maximum mark: 5**

**Cycling: Not Just a Hobby**

For many people, cycling is not something you do on the weekend; it's a way of life. I discovered this a few years ago when a friend invited me out to ride with a local cycling club. At the beginning, it was simply a way to get some exercise and enjoy the outdoors. But the longer I rode, the more it began to affect my health and social circle.

Cycling has several benefits. Physically, it makes me fit and agile. Mentally, it provides a feeling of freedom and allows me to let go of the tension of a long day. Cycling out in the open with nothing but the sound of the wind relaxes me.

It also introduced me to people with the same interests who enjoy the same hobby. We meet early on weekends, plan far-off tours, and encourage each other to perform better. I also learned about bike repair, safety on the road, and interesting cycling routes in my country.

What began as a hobby has become a way of life that fills my time and helps me feel energized. Cycling is not something I do—it's who I am.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The writer felt riding had a negative impact on their life.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$Cycling helps the writer stay in good physical shape.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Cycling helps the writer relax after a hard day.

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, E, F}$        [3 marks]

The writer mentions they have been cycling for some years, which is a long time.

- **E**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$it makes me fit and agile”\

The writer mentions that one of the advantages of cycling is being in good shape (fit and agile).

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$allows me to let go of the tension of a long day.”\

The writer says that one of the advantages of cycling is that it helps them *relax* (let go of the tension).

- *Incorrect options:*   B, C, D

- **C**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$At the beginning, it was simply a way to get some exercise and enjoy the outdoors.”\

The writer says that at first, it was a way to stay active and spend time outside.

- **D**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cycling has several benefits”\

The writer mentions there are benefits or advantages to cycling, which is the opposite of what the sentence says (a *negative impact*)
