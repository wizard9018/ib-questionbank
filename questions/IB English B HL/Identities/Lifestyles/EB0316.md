# EB0316

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a grandma talking about organizing Thanksgiving.**

:audio{#10a981f1-75c4-409d-b1ec-3f85d22fe5ac aid="10a981f1-75c4-409d-b1ec-3f85d22fe5ac"}

**Answer the following questions.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$What is the first step to organize Thanksgiving?     [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$How does the family ensure everything is organized?     [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Why does the speaker prepare extra food?     [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$What do they do if the weather is not nice?     [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$What does grandma say she likes most about this day?     [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{making a clear plan}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the starting point is making a clear plan”\

In the audio the speaker uses the phrase $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the starting point” which is similar in meaning to $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the first step.”

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{use a group chat (to share updates)}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$We use a group chat to share updates”\

The speaker mentions the group chat makes sure $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nothing is forgotten” which relates to the idea of organization.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{someone will bring a friend}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I always prepare extra food because someone will bring a friend!”\

Grandma knows that there will be extra people, so she prepares extra food.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{move inside}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$and if it's cold, we move inside”\

When the weather is not nice (it is cold) they move inside.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(take) a big family photo}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$we take a big family photo, and that's my favorite tradition”\

Grandma says that the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$big family photo” is her favorite tradition, or $\text{\textquotedblleft}$what she likes the most.”
