## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- “At first, I was excited about the unity it represented”
The speaker mentions feeling excited about the unity that taking her husband's surname represented.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **A**: is incorrect, as the speaker feels strongly about the change.
- **C**: is also incorrect, as the speaker seems just reflective and nostalgic, not regretful about the name change.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- “My maiden name held my family's history and my own identity”

- The text states that her maiden name held her family's history and her own identity

- *Incorrect options:*  B, C

- **B**: is incorrect, because it misrepresents the author's connection to her maiden name; it is tied to her own family, not her husband's.
- **C**: is also incorrect, as the text specifically connects her maiden name to her family's history rather than framing it as merely a new beginning.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- “Adopting his surname made me reflect on the balance between tradition and individuality”

- The speaker reflects on the balance between tradition and individuality in the context of changing her surname.

- *Incorrect options:*  A, B

- **A**: is incorrect, because the text does not focus on modernity but rather on balancing tradition with individuality.
- **B**: is also incorrect, as the text does not address social status as a theme.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- “Over time, she comes to see adopting his surname as a fusion of their stories.”

- *Incorrect options:*  A, C

- **A**: is incorrect,  because while she initially felt some loss, she ultimately views it positively as a fusion of their stories.
- **C**: is also incorrect, as it contradicts the author's reflective and positive perspective on adopting her husband's surname.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- “It's a reminder that while I cherish my roots, I'm also building a new legacy together”

- The author expresses that she cherishes her roots but is also building a new legacy together with her husband.

- *Incorrect options:*  B, C

- **B**: is incorrect, because the author explicitly states that she cherishes her roots even while embracing a new legacy.
- **C**: is also incorrect, as while she experiences some conflict, she ultimately finds value in both aspects of her identity.
