## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{chase}$   [1 mark]

- The speaker expresses that using English allows her to pursue her aspirations and goals. The word *chasing* conveys an active engagement with her dreams, highlighting the dynamic nature of his identity as he navigates both languages.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{vibrant}$   [1 mark]

- The word *vibrant* describes the rich and lively traditions that Spanish connects her to. This emphasizes the cultural significance of her  heritage and how it shapes her identity, reinforcing the idea that language is tied to personal and familial connections.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{juggling}$   [1 mark]

-  The term *juggling* illustrates the complexity of managing two identities simultaneously. It suggests a balance between different aspects of her life, reflecting the challenges and joys of being bilingual in a multicultural environment.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{blend}$   [1 mark]

- The word *blend* signifies the harmonious integration of her identities as she navigates both languages. This term captures the essence of her experience, suggesting that being bilingual creates a unique and cohesive sense of self.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{enrich}$   [1 mark]

- The verb *enrich* indicates that each language adds depth and value to her life experiences. This highlights the positive impact of bilingualism on her personal growth and understanding of the world around her.
