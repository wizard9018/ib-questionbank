# EB0095

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a person talking about a family custom connected with naming sons.**

:audio{#0bd33cf6-0140-4ece-8371-9223564a36fa aid="0bd33cf6-0140-4ece-8371-9223564a36fa"}

1 - 4.$\hspace{1em}$**Choose the five true statements.** [5 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker's grandfather, father, and brother are all named Richard.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$Grandpa Richard was a kind and humorous person.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$Richard Jr. is often stressed about work.
- D.$\hspace{1em}$Richard III is hardworking.
- E.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker is the only child in their family.
- F.$\hspace{1em}$The narrator felt lost and not unique growing up with so many Richards.
- G.$\hspace{1em}$The narrator's family has a strong tradition of naming their sons Richard.
- H.$\hspace{1em}$People aren't surprised when they find out the speaker's name is Richard.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 5.$\hspace{1em}$ *Answers 1 to 5 can be written in any order.* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, B, C, F, G}$   [5 marks]

- **A**. The text explicitly states the names of the three generations of Richards.

- **B**. Grandpa Richard is literally described as $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the kindest soul”

- **C**. Richard Jr. is characterized as a workaholic who is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$always stressed about deadlines."

- **F**. The narrator expresses feelings of being lost and not unique due to the shared name.

- **G**. as the consistent naming pattern suggests a strong family tradition.

- *Incorrect options*: D, E, H

- **D**.  Richard III is compared to a sloth, indicating laziness.

- **E**. The text mentions a little brother, Richard III, indicating that the narrator is not an only child.

- **H**. as the text mentions people are surprised by his name when meeting him.
