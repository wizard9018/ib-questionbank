# EB0371

**Maximum mark: 6**

Dear Tom,

You will not believe what I just did! You know I'm a Scrabble fanatic.  I've been playing for years here in our London Scrabble club!  But last weekend, I took part in a French Scrabble tournament in Lyon. You know I do not know French!! But four years of Latin at school gave me enough confidence to sign up - and it was amazing!

The competition itself was both terrifying and thrilling. My opponents were friendly and very confident as they quickly placed tiles to form words I could not pronounce!  I placed my own tiles relying entirely on my memory of Latin roots and the patterns I know that make up so many words in multiple languages. Needless to say, I lost every round I played!

In the end, what was really remarkable was how little spoken language mattered once the game began. Everyone understood the game - the rules and strategy - and it turns out the French give the same little smile when their opponent plays a great word as we do in London.  

I lost every game - and I can't wait to go back for the next competition!  Do you want to come with me?

Yours, James

**The following statements are either true or false. Write True or False, then justify it using words as they appear in the text. Both parts are required for [1 mark].** 

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$James is an experienced Scrabble player.    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$James speaks French fluently.    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$James studied Latin for four years.    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$James found the experience of competing both exciting and frightening.    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$James communicated with his opponents in French during the games.    [1 mark]

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$James is hoping to return to Lyon for another tournament.    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I've been playing for years" \

James tells his friend that he has been a member of a London Scrabble club $\text{\textquotedblleft}$for years”, which means a significant (long) period of time.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{False}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I do not know French" \

James admits that he does not know French, neither writing, nor speaking.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$four years of Latin at school”\

James states clearly that he studied Latin for four years before entering the tournament.  He believes his knowledge of Latin gives him a basic understanding of languages which stem from Latin, like French.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The competition itself was both terrifying and thrilling" \

James describes the experience using two contrasting emotions, one negative and one positive. Terrifying and frightening are synonyms and so are thrilling and exciting.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{False}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$how little spoken language mattered" \

James indicates that spoken language was not important during the games, because everyone knew the rules and how to play. And, when communicating with the other player, a smile worked perfectly - there was no need to speak at all.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I can't wait to go back for the next competition" \

James expresses a clear desire to return to Lyon for another French Scrabble tournament.
