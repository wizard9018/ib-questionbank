# EB0007

**Maximum mark: 5**

October 24 

Today was really tough. I had my math test, and I'd studied for days, but when I got to class, my mind went blank. The pressure to get a good grade started to creep in, and I felt panicked. Right then, I saw Paige next to me, with her paper at just the right angle—I could easily see her answers if I leaned just a bit. 

For a split second, it felt like my only option. Everyone always says how important grades are for our future, and I didn't want to mess up. But then I remembered what my parents and teachers always say about honesty and integrity. Would copying actually help me learn anything? And could I even feel proud if I did well that way? 

So I decided to stick with what I knew, even if it meant my grade wasn't perfect. It wasn't easy, and I felt disappointed leaving some answers blank. But when I handed in my paper, I felt surprisingly relieved. I guess I proved something to myself today. I want my grades to reflect my effort—not someone else's. Still nervous to see the result, but at least I can be proud of how I handled it.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 4.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers 1 to 4 can be written in any order.* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, D, F}$ [4 marks]

- **A:** $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I had my math test, and I'd studied for days...", so a few days/ several days.

- **C:** $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I saw Paige next to me, with her paper at just the right angle—I could easily see her answers if I leaned just a bit." The angle enabled the narrator to see the answers.

- **D:** $\text{\textquotedblleft}$But then I remembered what my parents and teachers always say about honesty and integrity."

- **F:** $\text{\textquotedblleft}$It wasn't easy, and I felt disappointed leaving some answers blank. But when I handed in my paper, I felt surprisingly relieved."

- *Incorrect options:* B, E, G, H

- **B**: The writer did not copy answers from Paige. While the writer did consider it briefly, they ultimately decided against cheating to stay true to their values: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$So I decided to stick with what I knew, even if it meant my grade wasn't perfect."

- **E**: The writer felt both disappointment and relief, not just disappointment. After submitting the paper, the writer reflects: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$But when I handed in my paper, I felt surprisingly relieved."

- **G**: The writer does not expect a perfect grade. Instead, they acknowledge leaving answers blank, which made them $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nervous to see the result," and focus more on pride in their integrity than on expecting a top score.

- **H**: The writer does not express being proud of their work. They mention being proud of the way they handled to whole situation. “I can be proud of how I handled it."

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- Each option touches on elements related to the text (eg. **Option A** mentions the impact of academic pressure on mental health, which is not the main topic or purpose of the text; **Option B** is wrong, as the text does not focus on the authors study habits; **Option D** concentrates on frustration, which again isn't the main point of the text), but the primary purpose remains the writer's internal reflection on maintaining personal integrity despite external pressures.
