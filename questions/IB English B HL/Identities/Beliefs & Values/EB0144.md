# EB0144

**Maximum mark: 7**

**You are going to hear a person explaining why they chose to become a vegetarian.**

:audio{#19324caf-8bc9-404d-abae-c2e85bb43c47 aid="19324caf-8bc9-404d-abae-c2e85bb43c47"}

**Answer the following questions.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$What event at school led the speaker to first question their diet?    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$What **three** resources does the speaker mention that meat production requires?    [3 marks]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$According to the speaker, what kind of choices have meaning?    [1 mark]

**Choose the correct answer.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$What made becoming a vegetarian difficult at first?  [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker didn't like vegetarian food
- B.$\hspace{1em}$Their family wasn't vegetarian
- C.$\hspace{1em}$They didn't believe it would make a difference

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$How does the speaker define making a difference?   [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Changing the entire world
- B.$\hspace{1em}$Stopping environmental harm alone
- C.$\hspace{1em}$Making a small positive change

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Reading about meat production}$  [1 mark]

- “Then, in school one day we read about how meat production harms the environment.”  The speaker had not considered how eating meat impacted the planet before this, and the knowledge made them reconsider what they chose to eat.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{water, land, and energy (in any order)}$ [1 mark]

- “I was shocked by how much water, land, and energy it uses.” The quantity of resources needed to produce meat surprised (“shocked”) the speaker.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{individual}$ [1 mark]

- “I believe individual choices matter…”
- *Matter* and *have meaning* are synonyms which show that something is of importance or has value.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- “My family wasn't vegetarian...”  The speaker had to overcome these challenges when they started a vegetarian diet.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **Option A**: the speaker tells us “...there were foods I really missed”, indicating that they missed eating meat.  However the text does not tell us that the speaker did not like vegetarian food.

- **Option C**: “Even if I can't change the world, I can change my actions.” The speaker did believe their choice could make a difference.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- “I can change my actions and make a small positive contribution to the health of our planet.”  The speaker feels that it is possible for one person's choice to make a difference in making the planet a little healthier.  *Contribute* means to give something to help achieve a goal or objective.

- *Incorrect options:* A, B

- **Option A**:  The speaker knows that one person cannot change or influence the whole world: “…even if I can't save the world alone, I can change my actions.” However, the speaker believes that “every small choice matters.”

- **Option B**: The speaker believes that they can “...make a small positive contribution to the health of our planet.”  The speaker believes that “...everyone plays a role in protecting the Earth” and they want to do their part to help.
