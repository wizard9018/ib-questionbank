# EB0145

**Maximum mark: 6**

**You are going to hear a person reflecting on overcoming self-doubt.**

:audio{#9765f2b9-9e44-4095-9ed7-f99bf54d6640 aid="9765f2b9-9e44-4095-9ed7-f99bf54d6640"}

1 - 3.$\hspace{1em}$**Choose the :u[three] true statements.** [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker never struggled with self-belief.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$Perseverance was important in helping the speaker build confidence.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker now sees failure as part of learning.
- D.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker immediately gained confidence after one success.
- E.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker believes only perfection leads to confidence.
- F.$\hspace{1em}$Lack of confidence was the biggest problem the speaker faced.

**Complete the following sentences using no more than three words from the text.** [3 marks]

The speaker used to question whether they were **[-4-]** to handle problems.\
The speaker **[-5-]** new things because they were scared of failing.\
Self-belief is not about perfection but about **[-6-]** that growth is possible.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 3.$\hspace{1em}$ *Answers can be written in any order.* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, C, F}$   [4 marks]

- **B**. The speaker tells us: “setting goals and sticking to them…made me stronger.”  *Perseverance* is the quality of never giving up and, in this case,  it it has a similar meaning to *sticking to* something.

- **C**. The speaker believes that “and even failure is just a step forward.” A *step forward* is another way of saying something can be learned if a mistake or failure happens.

- **F**. “self-doubt had been my biggest obstacle.”  *Obstacle* is a synonym for *problem* or *challenge*, something which must be overcome or solved.

- *Incorrect options*: A, D, E

- **A**.  The text begins with the statement: “For years, I doubted myself.”  This tells us that the speaker had struggled with self-belief.

- **D**. The text indicates that the process of becoming more confident was not immediate. “Each small success made me stronger” and “over time I realized…” show that it took a series of successes to help build the speaker’s self-belief

- **E**. “I didn’t need to be perfect…”  The text tells us that perfection is not necessary to have confidence, and making mistakes is part of the process of learning.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{good enough}$   [1 mark]

- “When I faced a challenge, I wondered if I was good enough to overcome it.”  *Wondered* and *questioned* are closely related in meaning and show that the speaker was worried about their ability to handle the problem.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{hesitated to try}$   [1 mark]

- “I hesitated to try new things, fearing I would fail.” *Hesitate* means to pause before doing something, usually because of fear or nervousness.  The speaker lacked self-confidence and was worried about failing if they tried something new.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{trusting}$   [1 mark]

- The speaker came to understand that “...I didn’t need to be perfect; it’s about trusting that I can learn and grow.”  Overcoming the fear of failing at something gave the speaker confidence and allowed them to try new things, even if they weren’t always successful (“...even failure teaches something important”).
