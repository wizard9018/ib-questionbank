# EB0143

**Maximum mark: 6**

**You are going to hear a person reflecting on time spent with their grandmother.**

:audio{#36e42810-f6ba-4026-a9bb-3c68ab93926a aid="36e42810-f6ba-4026-a9bb-3c68ab93926a"}

**Answer the following questions.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$The speaker identifies several activities done during the day by their grandmother.  Give **two** answers.     [2 marks]

2 - 4.$\hspace{1em}$**Choose the three true statements.** [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker’s parents worked from home every day.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker was regularly with their grandmother when he was younger.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker often felt bored when staying with their grandmother.
- D.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker learned about their family from their grandmother’s stories.
- E.$\hspace{1em}$The grandmother taught the speaker important life values.
- F.$\hspace{1em}$The grandmother enjoyed reading and did not like other activities.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$How does the speaker feel about their grandmother’s influence?  [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$grateful
- B.$\hspace{1em}$angry
- C.$\hspace{1em}$regretful

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1$.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order. Any two of the following:*

-   $\textrm{cooking (an evening meal)}$
-   $\textrm{cleaning}$
-   $\textrm{helping a neighbor}$
-   $\textrm{working in the garden}$
-   $\textrm{reading}$

- All options come from the following sentence: “She was tireless and would joyfully spend all day doing something: cooking a delicious evening meal for the family, cleaning, helping a neighbor, working in the garden, and, when she had time, she would sit in her favorite chair and read.” [2 marks]

2 - 4.$\hspace{1em}$ *Answers can be written in any order.* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, D, E}$   [4 marks]

- **B**. “When I was young, I spent a lot of time with my grandmother”

- The speaker mentions spending a lot of time with their  grandmother. The phrases *to spend a lot of time* and *to be regularly with someone* convey the same idea.

- **D**. “I learned about my relatives and the troubles and triumphs of their lives.”

- *Relatives* is a synonym for family.  *Troubles and triumphs* is another way of saying that the stories included the problems, *troubles*, that their relatives experienced, and the way they overcame them, *triumphs*.

- **E**. The speaker lists the personal values learned from their grandmother: “perseverance and kindness…the importance of family, and keeping a sense of humor…”.  The speaker recognizes how important the time spent with their grandmother was in shaping the values the adult speaker has.  *Perseverance* is the quality of not giving up when things become difficult.

- *Incorrect options*: A, C, F

- **A**.  The text states that the speaker’s parents “were at work.”  The text does not say where the parents were working, but it implies that the speaker and grandmother were the only ones at home during the day.

- **C**. The text shows us that the young speaker enjoyed the time spent with their grandmother: “As she worked and I followed her around, copying what she did…”  The speaker was as active as their grandmother, and was engaged in different activities during the day. There is no indication that the speaker remembers being bored.

- **F**. Although the grandmother did enjoy reading “when she had time, she would sit in her favorite chair and read”, the text clearly shows that she also did many other activities “joyfully”.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- The speaker says “. I am who I am… thanks to grandmother’s stories”, showing that they appreciated what they learned from her.  *Grateful* means being thankful for something done for you.

- *Incorrect options:* B, C

- **Option B**: There is no sign that the speaker was angry about spending time with their grandmother. The speaker uses positive images to describe the time they spent together: “joyfully,” “told me stories,” and “thanks to grandmother’s stories.”

- **Option C**: *Regret* means feeling sad or disappointed about something.  The speaker does not show regret for spending time with their grandmother and learning about values.  Instead, the speaker says they are grateful: “thanks to grandmother’s stories…”
