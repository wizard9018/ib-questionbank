$\Large{\textrm{MEANINGFUL WAYS TO CELEBRATE YOUR PATRIOTISM}}$

The 4th of July is a day to wave the American flag and celebrate our nation's independence, which was achieved over 200 years ago in 1776. Most people like to celebrate this holiday with backyard BBQs amongst friends and family. If you're looking for a few other ideas to show your patriotism, though, then you've come to the right place. 

- **[-1-]** 

- Invite friends, family, and neighbors to come together for a 4th of July celebration with plenty of food, drinks, and patriotic red, white, and blue decorations. To elevate the party's patriotic feel, include celebratory items like themed tableware, streamers, banners, flags, and several fresh floral arrangements in red, white, and blue to create a vibrant atmosphere.

- **[-2-]**

- Cities nationwide often host dazzling Independence Day parades honoring veterans and active service members. Bring your family to enjoy the display of patriotic floats and people, join in a lively and inspiring experience.

- **[-3-]**

- Offer your time or assistance to veterans in your area, or show appreciation with a patriotic flower arrangement or gift. If you don't know any veterans personally, reach out to a VA facility to learn ways to support veterans and their families.

- **[-4-]**

- Display the American flag as a symbol of patriotism. Raise it with care, ensuring it flies above other flags, never touches the ground, and is illuminated if displayed at night.

- **[-5-]**

- America's National Parks showcase some of the country's most incredible landscapes. Historic sites and monuments also offer rich experiences and a sense of pride. Many operate with limited funds, so consider visiting, joining a support group, or donating to help preserve them.

- **[-6-]**

- Strengthen your community by choosing locally-owned shops and American-made products when possible. Putting money back into the local economy is a meaningful way to show patriotism.
