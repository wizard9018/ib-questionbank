**You are going to hear a news reporter outside a school prom.**

**Complete the following gaps. Use no more than three words for each gap.**[4 marks]

**School Newsletter**

Each year, students in their final year of our school must fulfil a **[-1-]** to be admitted to the prom by finishing **[-2-]** of community service. Now running for **[-3-]**, the program was set up by the school board to strengthen community bonds. Seniors give their time in local organizations such as elderly care facilities and **[-4-]**.

**Choose the correct answer.**

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$How did students feel after completing the hours?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$stressed 

- B.$\hspace{1em}$satisfied

- C.$\hspace{1em}$comforted

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{requirement}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$every student had to complete a requirement”\

A requirement is something you have to do. Students had to complete their volunteer hours to go allowed to go to prom.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{one hundred hours}$    [1 mark]

- Also accept: 100 hours\

\

$\text{\textquotedblleft}$one hundred hours of community service”\

The text gives the exact number of hours required to be allowed to attend the prom.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{third year}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$now in its third year”\

The policy was introduced 3 years ago.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{local primary schools}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$local primary schools”\

Along with food banks and care facilities, primary schools are one of the named volunteering locations in the text.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the experience was fulfilling”\

Most students felt that the experience of serving the community was *fulfilling*. After, they felt *satisfied*. Remember, an experience can be fulfilling or satisfying, and a person feels fulfilled or satisfied.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **A**: The students felt stressed while doing the hours $\text{\textquotedblleft}$alongside their studies,” but not after they had finished.

- **C**: The students did not feel *comforted* after doing the hours. *Comforted* means to make someone feel better when they are sad or worried.
