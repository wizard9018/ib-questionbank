# EB0382

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a teenager reading a letter of apology.**

:audio{#5818791f-e6db-4ba5-b64b-3f82f93a31cf aid="5818791f-e6db-4ba5-b64b-3f82f93a31cf"}

**Choose the four true statements.** [4 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Sam came to school in traditional clothing.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Asher made the comment after school.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The class stayed quiet after the comment.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Asher's family wore traditional dress on the weekend.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$The grandmother's visit happened before Asher made the comment.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Asher had never found traditional clothing unusual at home.

- G.$\hspace{1em}$Asher understood what they had done wrong after talking with Sam.

- H.$\hspace{1em}$Asher's comment made Sam feel embarrassed about their culture.

**Choose the correct answer.**

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$What made the writer finally understand the impact of their actions?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Remembering that Sam was upset.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Wondering if Sam had spoken to other students about it.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Imagining a family member being made fun of.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, D, F, H}$        [4 marks]

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$When you wore your traditional dress to school last week"\

Sam was wearing traditional clothing on the day the incident happened at school.

- **D**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$She and my mom wore their traditional clothes..."\

Both Asher's grandmother and mother wore traditional dress - women wearing their cultural clothing was something Asher saw in his own home.

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$never once thought it was strange or old-fashioned"\

Traditional clothing had always been a completely normal part of life at home for Asher's family.

- **H**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I made you feel that who you are - your culture - was something to be ashamed of"\

Asher realized the comment had gone beyond the clothes themselves — it had attacked Sam's cultural identity directly.

- *Incorrect options:*  

- **B**: The text states the comment was made $\text{\textquotedblleft}$in front of the class" - not after school.

- **C**: The text says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$people laughed", which means that the class was not quiet.

- **E**: The school incident happened $\text{\textquotedblleft}$last week," and the grandmother's visit was $\text{\textquotedblleft}$yesterday." The visit came after the incident, not before.

- **G**: There was no conversation with Sam after the comment was made.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I imagined someone laughing at my grandmother in her dress"\

Picturing a family member being made fun of in the same way made Asher understand the real impact of what they had done to Sam.

- *Incorrect options:* A, B, C, D

- **A**: Sam's sadness is mentioned in paragraph 2, but this was not what led to Asher's realization - that came later, during the grandmother's visit.

- **B**: There is no mention in the text of Sam speaking to other students about the incident.
