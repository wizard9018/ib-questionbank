# EB0332

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a secondary school student talking about how they use music in their daily life.**

:audio{#958f0dd9-9ba6-45fc-879e-88ed40088a1c aid="958f0dd9-9ba6-45fc-879e-88ed40088a1c"}

**Answer the following questions.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$What kind of songs help the student wake up in the morning?    [1 mark]

**Find the four true statements.**[4 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The student listens to different playlists depending on the time of day.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Morning music is relaxed and quiet.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The student changes their school playlist every morning. 

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Music helps the student feel calmer before school. 

- E.$\hspace{1em}$The student uses a playlist to celebrate good feelings.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Music affects the student's thoughts and feelings. 

- G.$\hspace{1em}$When they are stressed, the student does not listen to music. 

- H.$\hspace{1em}$The student does not spend much time choosing music.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{fast beats}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Morning songs wake me up with fast beats and lots of energy.”\

The student explains that energetic music helps them wake up in the morning.

$2 - 5.$ $\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, D, E, F}$        [4 marks]

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I have a playlist for everything. Morning songs wake me up...”\

The student explains that they have different playlists for different situations, including mornings and the journey to school.

- **D**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$On the way to school, I play the same quiet tracks every day because they calm me...” \

The student states that listening to the same music daily before school helps them feel calm and ready to focus.

- **E**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I have a $`$happy' playlist that I use to celebrate when I'm feeling good” \

The student describes using a specific playlist to celebrate positive emotions.

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Music changes how I move, how I think and how I feel” \

The speaker explains that music affects their emotions, thoughts, and physical well-being.

- *Incorrect options:*  B, C, G, H 

- **B**: The student mentions that their morning playlist has $\text{\textquotedblleft}$fast beats and lots of energy,” which is the opposite of $\text{\textquotedblleft}$relaxed and quiet”.

- **C**: The student says they play $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the same tracks every day” on the way to school, not that they change the playlist daily.

- **G**: The student says he used music $\text{\textquotedblleft}$last week I used it to help me feel better when I was feeling stressed,” so they do listen to music when stressed.

- **H**: The student says they $\text{\textquotedblleft}$take time to make playlists,” which shows they do spend time choosing music.
