# EB0135

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a radio host introduce the idea of pet-ternity\* leave as a way of boosting employees' well-being.**

:audio{#e48458a3-a826-413f-bead-4afa5037072d aid="e48458a3-a826-413f-bead-4afa5037072d"}

**Answer the following questions.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$How much are you paid on pet-ternity leave? [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Who introduced this policy?  [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Who inspired the creation of this policy?     [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$What do supporters say the main benefit of this policy is?     [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$How does the text describe the public response to this idea?     [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{full pay}$

- *Accept any other answer with the same meaning* [1 mark]

- The second part of the text starts with this information exactly: “Imagine three weeks off work, full pay…"

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Derrick Moon (an IT boss)/ (an) IT boss}$

- *Also accept*: Derrick Moon (an IT boss) or An IT boss [1 mark]

- The text says that “Derrick Moon, an IT boss, is making it happen," so he is the person who introduced the policy.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{his wife}$ [1 mark]

- Derrick Moon was inspired by his wife, which is stated in the fragment, “After his wife took nine months to care for their dogs…"

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{great for mental health}$

- *Also accept*: reduces stress

- boosts happiness

- *Accept other words with similar meaning*. [1 mark]

- The text lists the main benefits of pet-ternity in the following sentence: “Advocates say it's great for mental health—caring for pets reduces stress and boosts happiness."

- The word *boost* means improve or enhance, in this case happiness.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{sparking plenty of debate}$

- *Accept other words with similar meaning.* [1 mark]

- The text concludes with the sentence describing the public response to the idea of pet-ternity leave , "Love it or not, it's sparking plenty of debate,” which clearly shows the idea is controversial, as the phrase *sparking debate* implies this topic is the source of arguments and divided opinions.
