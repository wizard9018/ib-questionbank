# EB0136

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a part of a programme on self-care and well-being promoting taking ‘ussies' instead of selfies.**

:audio{#87d5b63c-86dd-4770-b5dc-d5ac66d58ba3 aid="87d5b63c-86dd-4770-b5dc-d5ac66d58ba3"}

1 - 5.$\hspace{1em}$**Choose the 5 correct statements.** [5 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Ussies are photos taken with other people.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$The text suggests selfies can promote perfectionism.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$Ussies help combat feelings of isolation.
- D.$\hspace{1em}$Research shows social connections reduce stress levels.
- E.$\hspace{1em}$Ussies create lasting memories with others.
- F.$\hspace{1em}$The text claims “ussies” are always better than selfies.
- G.$\hspace{1em}$The text suggests never taking selfies again.
- H.$\hspace{1em}$The conclusion encourages including others in photos.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 5.$\hspace{1em}$ *Answers can be written in any order.* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, B, C, D, H}$   [4 marks]

- **A**. “photos taken with others"

- The text directly defines ussies as pictures with other people.

- **B**. “Unlike selfies, which can sometimes feed into perfectionism"

- The fragment shows that selfies can promote perfectionism, as the expression *feed into* in this context means *promote* and *strengthen*.

- **C**. “helping to combat feelings of isolation"

- It is directly stated in the fragment that ussies help overcome such feelings, and the word *combat* means fight, overcome

- **D**. “Research shows that social connections lower stress,"

- The text states explicitly that social connections have a beneficial influence on high stress levels. The word *lower* is a synonym of *reduce*.

- **H**. “invite someone into the frame",

- The conclusion explicitly calls for inviting others to join the picture, and the word frame in question is literally the photo frame.

- *Incorrect options*: E, F, G

- **E**.  “It's a simple way to make memories”

- While the text mentions making memories in the conclusion, it doesn't specifically claim they are “lasting", that is long-term memories - this would be an overstatement of what the text actually says.

- **F**. “Unlike selfies, which can sometimes feed into perfectionism or self-focus, ussies emphasize togetherness”

- The text doesn't make this absolute claim, only emphasizes the benefits of ‘ussies',  while the word ‘always' makes the statement in F sound so.

- **G**. “So, next time you reach for your phone, invite someone into the frame. “

- Text encourages listeners to take more ussies, not *eliminate* selfies (which is the synonym to *never taking selfies* again).
