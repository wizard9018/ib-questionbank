# EB0134

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to listen to part of a lecture by a professor of psychology on ambiverts.**

:audio{#bae868fd-a7bf-437f-a508-04c322f67859 aid="bae868fd-a7bf-437f-a508-04c322f67859"}

**Choose the correct answer.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$What is the primary characteristic of being an ambivert?  [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$They excel at communication only.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$Their ability to adapt to different social situations.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$They can build strong relationships.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$How do ambiverts manage their social interactions?  [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$They avoid all social situations.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$They balance social and quiet time effectively.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$They prefer constant social engagement.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$According to the text, what skill makes ambiverts good at interpersonal communication?  [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Always being talkative.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$Always being quiet.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$Knowing when to listen and when to speak.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$What does the professor say about ambiverts at work?  [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$They naturally manage work-life balance better.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$They only work well alone.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$They prefer working with large groups.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$The speaker's tone can best be described as:   [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Critical and judgemental
- B.$\hspace{1em}$Informative and balanced
- C.$\hspace{1em}$Overly enthusiastic

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The text states ambiverts, “adapt easily to both social and quiet settings" as their primary characteristic.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **Option A**: is incorrect because the text mentions communication as one of several skills, not the primary one.

- **Option C**: is incorrect, because building relationships is presented as a result of their communication skills, not the primary characteristic, which is the ability to adapt.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The text mentions they "avoid overstimulation or loneliness" by balancing their social energy. The word *overstimulation* can mean *hyperactive* or *overwhelmed*. Which means ambiverts avoid feeling like this in social interactions.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **Option A**: is incorrect, because the text specifically states they can adapt to social settings, so they don’t avoid them.
- **Option C**: is also incorrect, because the text emphasizes balance, rather than constant social engagement.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- The text directly states they excel at "knowing when to listen and when to assert themselves." The expression *assert themselve* means to behave in a way that expresses your confidence, importance or power in a way that earns you respect from others.

- *Incorrect options:* A, B

- **Option A**: is incorrect because the text emphasizes balance in communication, not constant talking.
- **Option B**:  is also incorrect, because the text emphasizes the importance of both listening and speaking.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- The text explicitly states, "Ambiverts are successful at managing personal and professional dynamics, often striking a healthy work-life balance." *Work-life balance* is a good replacement for *personal and professional dynamics*, as they both imply that ambiverts can effectively balance the public and private parts of their lives.

- *Incorrect options:* B, C

- **Option B**: is incorrect because the text mentions they can adapt to both social and quiet settings.
- **Option C**: is also  incorrect because the text doesn't mention preference for group size.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The text presents balanced, factual information without strong opinions.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **Option A**: is incorrect because there are no critical or negative judgements in the text.
- **Option C**: is also incorrect because the tone maintains professional objectivity without excessive enthusiasm.
