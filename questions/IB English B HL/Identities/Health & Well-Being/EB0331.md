# EB0331

**Maximum mark: 7**

**You are going to hear a university student reflecting on their experience of tracking health habits.**

:audio{#489edc2e-d907-4313-a12e-32dd25afb84b aid="489edc2e-d907-4313-a12e-32dd25afb84b"}

**Answer the following questions.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$What did the speaker install on their phone?    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Name **two** things the speaker tracked.    [2 marks]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$How often did the speaker check the app later on?    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$What did the speaker compare themselves to?    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$How did the speaker feel?    [1 mark]

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$What did the speaker finally do with the app?    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{a habit tracker}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Last year, I installed a habit tracker on my phone.”\

Also accept: app

- The speaker tells us that the app they installed was a habit tracker used to monitor health-related activities.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$*any two of the following answers:*  

- $\textrm{steps}$

- $\textrm{sleep}$

- $\textrm{water}$

- $\textrm{vegetables}$        [2 marks]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I counted steps, sleep, water, even how many vegetables I ate.”\

The speaker lists several habits they tracked. 

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{dozens of times (a day)}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I found myself checking the app dozens of times a day.”\

This shows that the speaker became overly focused on the app, checking it very frequently.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{“ideal” numbers}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I started comparing myself to $`$ideal' numbers.”\

The speaker explains that they judged their health by comparing their results to target numbers set by the app.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{anxious}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Instead of feeling healthy, I felt anxious.”\

The speaker directly contrasts how they expected to feel (happy) with how the habit tracking actually made them feel (anxious).

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{deleted the app}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I finally deleted the app.”\

The speaker explains that they stopped using the habit tracker completely by removing it from their phone.
