## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... is aiming for total safety always better for us?”\

The phrase *aiming* for means to pursue or strive toward something, which fits logically in this context. \

\

**Option F** is the distractor here, as you can *aim at* things (literally, for example, with a weapon), but contrary to *aiming for* things, which is trying to achieve something, it doesn't make sense here.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... it's easy to block out uncomfortable ideas.”\

*Block out* means to prevent something from entering or affecting you, fitting with the idea of avoiding uncomfortable ideas online.\

\

**Option E** is the distractor here, as although *block something off* sounds logical, it actually refers to closing a physical space (like *blocking off* a road), rather than filtering or ignoring information or ideas, which *block out* stands for.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... shield ourselves from distress... ”\

The correct preposition with *shield* is *from*, indicating what you protect yourself against.\

\

**Option B** is the distractor here, as, although it sounds quite logical to *shield somebody against something*, it is not grammatically correct.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Some psychologists warn that safeism can create a $`$fragile' mindset.”\

This matches well with $\text{\textquotedblleft}$adopting the principles of safeism may result in individuals lacking resilience,” which is what the metaphor *fragile mindset* stands for.\

\

**Option D** is the distractor, as, although it is related to resilience, the original statement is about the risks of safeism, not the difficulty of building resilience.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$If we never face difficulties, how do we learn to cope with stress or setbacks?”\

The phrases *to face difficulties* and *to experience challenges* are similar in meaning. The sentence expresses that without these challenges, there is no way to develop resilience ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$effective ways to manage pressure or obstacles”)\

\

**Option E** is the distractor here, as the original sentence is about developing coping skills, not about solving deeper problems.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$creating supportive environments is important, especially for those who truly need it.”\

The sentences are similar in meaning. $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Provide environments that support people" and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$create supportive environments" are similar in meaning in this context. \

\

**Option F** is the distractor here, as this phrase relates more to being unprepared for real life (previous sentence), not the need for support.
