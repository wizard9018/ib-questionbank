# EB0150

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a reporter explain the popularity of K-Pop.**

:audio{#c2e314d9-ffc5-4063-8551-eef1886e9157 aid="c2e314d9-ffc5-4063-8551-eef1886e9157"}

1 - 3.$\hspace{1em}$**Choose the three true statements.** [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$K-Pop fans are referred to as “stans”.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$K-Pop fans only support their favorite idols by attending concerts.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$Social media helps fans connect with artists.
- D.$\hspace{1em}$Some fans learn Korean to better understand lyrics.
- E.$\hspace{1em}$K-Pop music is only popular in Korea.
- F.$\hspace{1em}$K-Pop fan communities do not engage in charitable activities.

**Complete the following sentences using no more than three words from the text.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Fans can **[-4-]** in real-time on social media sites.[1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Fans around the world  **[-5-]** with others who also love the genre.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 3.$\hspace{1em}$ *Answers can be written in any order.* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, D}$   [4 marks]

- **A**. “K-Pop fans or “stans”...

- *Referred to* is a synonym for *called*.

- **C**. “Social media plays a huge role, allowing fans to interact with artists…”

- *Interact with* and *connect with* are synonyms in this context, showing how social media creates opportunities for fans and musicians to create relationships with each other.

- **D**. “Fan communities… study Korean…”

- K-Pop is a global *sensation* (a sensation in this context is a person or thing that raises a lot of interest), which means that many fans are not Korean.  Many fans enjoy the music without fully understanding the words (*lyrics*), but many others learn the language to better connect with the musicians and the community.

- *Incorrect options*: B, E, F

- **B**.  “They attend concerts, stream music, buy albums, and vote in competitions…”  Fans support their favorite artists in several different ways.

- **E**. “K-Pop, or Korean pop music, has become a global sensation.” It is popular with fans all around the world, not only in Korea.

- **F**. “Fan communities… even raise money for charity.”  Giving to charity in the name of their favorite star is another way fans show support. *Engage in charitable activities* means that fans are active in doing this.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Interact with artists}$

- *Accept other answers with similar meaning.*  [1 mark]

- “Social media plays a huge role, allowing fans to interact with artists in real-time.”  The spread of K-Pop from Korea to the world was helped by social media, by creating a direct connection between fans and the musicians themselves.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Form friendships (through K-Pop)}$

- *Accept other answers with similar meaning.*.  [1 mark]

- “Many fans find joy in the community, forming friendships with people worldwide who share their love for K-Pop.”
This means they might become friends online as they share similar interests.
