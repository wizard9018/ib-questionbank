## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- The text tells us the name: “sidewalk surfing” and explains that it described the purpose of the new activity: to give surfers (who usually needed to be in the water to practice), the chance to practice on dry land.

- *Incorrect options:* A, B

- **Option A** and **Option B** are both incorrect combinations of the words “skateboard” and “sidewalk”.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- “Skateboarders … had soon created their own culture, complete with music and fashion.”  As skateboarders moved from the ocean to land, the clothing style we now think of as ‘boarding developed.  This unique look, and the music they listened to while practicing, became part of the culture that teens take part in today.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **Option A**: “Although skateboarding is now a professional sport and an Olympic event”  Skateboarding began in the 1950s and the subculture began at that time: the text tells us that it became an Olympic sport sometime later.
- **Option C**: “became a subculture on the streets of Los Angeles” Skateboarding and the culture that grew from it started in Los Angeles.  *Banned* means officially stopped or not allowed to happen, which is the opposite of what the text states.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- “...many teens still find community in the lifestyle that ‘boarding represents…”  Skateboarding has grown in popularity over the years and become a professional sport, but it remains popular with teens because it still represents freedom and fun, more than 70 years later.

- *Incorrect options:* A, B

- **Option A**: “Although skateboarding is now a professional sport…” The text states that skateboarding has developed over time, but also tells us that it is still loved by teens, therefore, it is not only for professional athletes.
- **Option B**:  “...many teens still find community in the lifestyle that ‘boarding represents - freedom, and a love of movement.”  The lifestyle and culture surrounding skateboarding is still relevant for today's teens.

$4$.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

-   $\textrm{freedom}$
-   $\textrm{love of movement}$

- “... lifestyle that ‘boarding represents - freedom, and a love of movement.” Skateboarding culture reflected the California surfer lifestyle. Freedom was represented in clothing and music and could be easily adopted by anyone, anywhere.  Skateboards allow people to move easily, both as a form of transportation as well as a sporting activity, which many teens find attractive. [2 marks]

$5$.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

-   $\textrm{(It is now) A professional sport}$
-   $\textrm{(It is now) An Olympic event. }$

- Skateboarding began as a way for individuals to practice surfing and was not a competitive sport.  However, as it became more popular, it eventually became a professional and then an Olympic sport, both of which are competitive.  *Competitive* (from the verb *compete*) in this text describes  sports which require athletes to defeat or beat another to win or be the best.  [2 marks]
