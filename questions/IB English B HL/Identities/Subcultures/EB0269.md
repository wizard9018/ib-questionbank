# EB0269 

**Maximum mark: 5**

**Culture Uncovered: Therians**

Hey everyone, and welcome back to $`$Culture Uncovered'! Today, we're discovering the fascinating world of Therians!

A Therian is someone who identifies, on a deep, personal level, as a non-human animal. This isn't about dressing up - it's an internal identity, a core part of who they are. They might have a $\text{\textquotedblleft}$theriotype,” which is their specific animal, and experience $\text{\textquotedblleft}$shifts,” where they feel more connected to their animal side.

It's a vibrant, diverse community all about exploring identity beyond the human experience. From wolves to house cats, their experiences are totally unique.

So, what do you think? Have you ever heard of Therians before? Drop a comment and let me know! It's a truly wild and wonderful part of our world.

**Choose the three correct statements.** [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$A Therian views themselves as an animal.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Therians mostly dress up in animal costumes.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$When a Therian relates closely to their animal identity, they might feel changes.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$A $\text{\textquotedblleft}$theriotype” is the creature a Therian identifies with.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$The Therian community focuses on wild animals.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Therians feel their identity is a small part of who they are.

**Find the words that complete the following sentences. Answer using the words as they appear in paragraph 4.** [2 marks]

The Therian subculture is **[-4-]**, allowing its members to discover their own identity.

Every Therian's experience is personal and **[-5-]**.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 3.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, D}$        [3 marks]

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$A Therian is someone who identifies, on a deep, personal level, as a non-human animal."\

A Therian deep down views themselves or identifies as an animal. 

- **C**: They might [...] experience $\text{\textquotedblleft}$shifts,” where they feel more connected to their animal side.\

The text mentions Therians might experience shifts or changes when they are connected (relate closely) to their animal identity.

- **D**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$They might have a $`$theriotype', which is their specific animal.”

Theriotype is the given animal identity of a Therian.

- *Incorrect options:*   B, E, F

- **B**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$This isn't about dressing up - it's an internal identity."\

Being a Therian is not wearing animal costumes, but an integral part of some people's identity. 

- **E**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$From wolves to house cats, their experiences are diverse."\

The text mentions different types of animals, not just wild animals, and adds the experiences are diverse.

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... a core part of who they are."\

Being a Therian is a part of the core of their existence, not only a part of their identity.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{vibrant and diverse}$   [1 mark]

- Also accept: vibrant, diverse \

\

$\text{\textquotedblleft}$It's a vibrant, diverse community all about exploring identity beyond the human experience. $\text{\textquotedblleft}$\

The community is described as colourful and energetic - vibrant and diverse. Subculture is similar in meaning to community.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{unique}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Every Therian's experience is $`$totally unique' and meaningful." \

The word unique, one of a kind, fits the sentence both logically and grammatically.
