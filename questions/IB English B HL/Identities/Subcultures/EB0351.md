# EB0351

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a short news report about fan-subbers.**

:audio{#ed130164-5e83-457d-98a9-d0075b739c0b aid="ed130164-5e83-457d-98a9-d0075b739c0b"}

**Complete the gaps. Use no more than three words for each gap.** [5 marks]

Teens are becoming more active in a **[-1-]** called $`$fan-subbers'.

Fan-subbers translate foreign TV shows and series and add **[-2-]**. 

Creating subtitles is complicated and requires many skills, so fan-subbers work **[-3-]**.

Media companies argue that fan-subbers distribute **[-4-]** without permission. 

When the official subtitles are published, fan-subbers delete **[-5-]**.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{subculture}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$A growing online subculture is gaining attention among teenagers: fan-subbers.”\

A subculture is a group of people who share an interest in a very specific topic or activity.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{subtitles}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Fan-subbers translate foreign TV shows and series... and add subtitles”

The report defines $`$fan-subbers' by stating what they do.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{in teams}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$They work in teams, with different people translating, checking meaning, and timing the subtitles so they match the dialogue.”\

Making subtitles is not a simple process, so several people work together to make sure the subtitles match what the actors actually say.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{copyrighted content}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Media companies argue that fan-subbers share copyrighted content without permission.”\

The report identifies what companies claim is being distributed illegally.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{their (online) content}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$remove their online content once official subtitles become available.”\

Fan-subbers say that they are not doing anything illegal and, when official subtitles are released, they *remove* or *delete* the subtitles they created.
