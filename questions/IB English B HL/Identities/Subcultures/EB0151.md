$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Where can a new book be found after a popular BookTok video?    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- “A community of book lovers on TikTok…”  \
The text tells us what BookTok is in the opening sentence.

- *Incorrect options:* B, C

- **Option B**: the text states that “...a single viral video can push an unknown novel onto bestseller lists,” however that is not the purpose of the community. The group does not sell books, but their comments can encourage others to buy books with high recommendations.  A *viral video* is one that is seen by millions of people and can influence what people do.

- **Option C**: BookTok is on TikTok, it is not a new type of social media.

$2$.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

-   $\textrm{Book reviews }$
-   $\textrm{recommendations}$

- “Members post short videos reviewing books and sharing recommendations.” \
This quote explains the group's main purpose. [2 marks]

$3$.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(Onto) bestseller lists}$

- “a single viral video can push an unknown novel onto bestseller lists.” \
A *bestseller* is a book or other item that is very popular at a given time.  [1 mark]

$4$.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

-   $\textrm{responding to comments}$
-   $\textrm{creating content}$

- “Many authors interact with fans directly, responding to comments and creating content about their own books.” \
It is this connection between writer and reader that makes the community unique. [2 marks]

$5$.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{a dynamic, interactive way}$

- *Accept other words with similar meaning.*

- “It's more than just a social media trend—it's a dynamic, interactive way for people to experience stories together.” \
Having a community in which to discuss books, share opinions and interact with writers and other readers has created a lot of excitement for members of the group. [1 mark]
