# EB0352

**Maximum mark: 8**

**You are going to hear a podcast about teenagers who collect vinyl records.**

:audio{#4550ce56-0a4c-42e7-bb49-0fbad31b0a17 aid="4550ce56-0a4c-42e7-bb49-0fbad31b0a17"}

**Answer the following questions.** 

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$What do vinyl-teens prefer?     [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$What does buying vinyl offer that streaming does not?     [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Name **two** things teens like about vinyls.     [2 marks]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Name **one** way vinyl-teens save money in their collections.    [1 mark]

**Choose the three true statements.** [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Marco believes the growing interest in vinyl is mainly about music quality.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Marco says the interest in vinyl is not only about music.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Marco suggests that buying vinyl can help support artists and local shops.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Marco believes most teenagers prefer large online platforms.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$Marco says vinyl collecting can help build a sense of identity and community.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Marco believes teens feel isolated rather than connected through vinyl.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{records}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$young people choosing records”\

The podcast mentions $\text{\textquotedblleft}$vinyl-teens” choose or prefer records to streaming.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{physical interaction}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$buying vinyl offers teens a physical interaction that streaming cannot match”\

Marco believes that the teens like being able to physically interact with the music in a way they cannot do with streamed music.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*any two of the following answers:*   

- $\textrm{the cover art }$

- $\textrm{the printed lyrics}$    

- $\textrm{the posters}$        [2 marks]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$They love the cover art, the printed lyrics, even the posters...”\

Marco lists these as the physical extras that come with vinyl records that teens really enjoy.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*any one of the following answers:*   

- $\textrm{(they) buy second-hand }$

- $\textrm{(they) share collections }$        [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$many teenagers buy second-hand copies or share collections with friends”\

Marco mentions records are expensive, so teens find ways to save money when collecting by buying second-hand or sharing collections.

$5 - 8.$ $\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, C, E}$        [3 marks]

- **B**: Marco states that the interest $\text{\textquotedblleft}$isn't only about music.”

- **C**: Marco believes that part of the interest in buying vinyl comes from young people's interest in supporting local communities.

- **E**: The text explains that buying and collecting vinyl helps build $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a sense of identity and community with like-minded people.”

- *Incorrect options:*  A, D, F

- **A**: The text says the trend $\text{\textquotedblleft}$isn't only about music,” not mainly about music quality.

- **D**: The text suggests teens prefer to support local businesses and NOT buy from online platforms or large (global) companies.

- **F**: The text describes building community, not isolation.
