# EB0023

**Maximum mark: 5**

Good morning everyone,

Today, I want to shed light on a lesser-seen side of *voluntourism* *—the $`$dark side.' While volunteering abroad is often pursued with the best intentions, it can sometimes do more harm than good. In many cases, short-term volunteers without specialized skills are placed in roles that unintentionally disrupt local communities. Projects may be designed more to attract tourists than to meet real needs, providing a temporary $`$feel-good' experience for volunteers but little lasting benefit for those they aim to help.

Worse, voluntourism can create dependency on outside aid rather than empowering communities to be self-sustaining. In some cases, it even exploits vulnerable populations, especially children, to appeal to tourists. This is a call to rethink how we help- by partnering with local organizations, investing in skills training, and supporting sustainable development that truly benefits communities.

Let's remember that real change requires commitment and humility, not just adventure.

Thank you for your attention.

\*  *Voluntourism* is a portmanteau (a word combining two words and their meaning into a new meaning) of voluntarism and tourism
