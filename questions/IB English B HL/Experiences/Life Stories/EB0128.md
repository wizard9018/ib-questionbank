# EB0128

**Maximum mark: 6**

**You are going to hear a woman describe her childhood in a rural area.**

:audio{#35e7bb1b-62d0-4bc8-b7be-d0aa8224dc31 aid="35e7bb1b-62d0-4bc8-b7be-d0aa8224dc31"}

**Choose the correct answer.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$The speaker couldn't attend school because…    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$she had to take care of the farm animals.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$her house was in a remote location.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$she helped her mother mend clothes.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$How did the family mostly manage their food supply?     [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Her mother worked extra hours.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$The family raised their own animals.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$The family had a vegetable garden.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$One of the problems during the winter was that…     [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$the cold inside the house was unbearable.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$the water supply was too limited.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$they could not go outside the house.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$During the winter, the father…     [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$repaired the water pipes.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$worked more hours than usual.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$used his creativity to feed the family.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$As a child, the speaker learnt to value…     [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$a hot bath.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$her father’s hard work.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$the simple things in life.

**Answer the following question.**

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$According to the speaker, what did their mother teach them to do because they had little money?    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The woman says, “Our house was too far from the town, so my brother and I were homeschooled.”
- The word *remote* suggests the house was far away from the school, and the speaker and her brother didn’t go to school. That's why they were *homeschooled*, which means they were taught at home. thing.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **Option A**: is incorrect as the speaker says, “We didn't’ have cows or chickens…”

- **Option C**: is an incorrect answer since the speaker says it was her mother who mended their clothes; she doesn't suggest she helped her.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- The speaker says, “...we grew most of our food,” from which we infer they had a garden where they cultivated vegetables.

- *Incorrect options:* A, B

- **Option A**: is incorrect because the speaker mentions it was the father who had to work “extra shifts”; she doesn't mention that the mother works.
- **Option B**: is a wrong option since she says, “We didn't’ have cows or chickens…”

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The woman says, “In the winter, the bitter cold would freeze the garden and the water pipes, so we had less food and little water.”

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **Option A**: the word *unbearable* refers to something that is impossible to tolerate. The speaker says, “Our circumstances made us appreciate (...) a hot meal or a cosy blanket on a cold night,” which suggests they felt comfortable inside the house during winter.
- **Option C**: is not a correct option since she doesn't mention they had to stay inside the house.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The speaker says, “In the winter, the bitter cold would freeze the garden (...) we had less food and little water. But my father worked extra shifts to cover our basic needs.” This shows that due to the shortage of water and food during winter, the father had to do extra hours at his job to get more money to buy the food they couldn’t grow.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **Option A**: is incorrect as she doesn’t mention the father repairing or fixing the water pipes.
- **Option C**: is not correct, as she explains that it was the mother who taught them to be creative to compensate for what they didn't have or couldn’t buy.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- The speaker says, “Our circumstances made us appreciate the small joys in life, like a hot meal or a cosy blanket on a cold night.” These are simple things in life that bring people joy.

- *Incorrect options:* A, B

- **Option A**: is not correct, as the speaker never mentions a hot bath; she actually talks about the shortage of water in winter.
- **Option B**: the speaker mentions her father worked hard, but she doesn't specifically say that was something she valued, making option B incorrect.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{to rely on our creativity}$  *Also accept other words with similar meaning.*  [1 mark]

- The speaker says, “My mother taught us to rely on our creativity to make do with their limited resources.” The speaker mentions that their mother taught them to be creative to be able to “make do with their limited resources”. *To have limited resources* is, for example, to have little money.
