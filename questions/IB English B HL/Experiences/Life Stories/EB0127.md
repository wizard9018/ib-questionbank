# EB0127

**Maximum mark: 6**

**You are going to hear a former professional athlete talk about how an
injury changed his life forever.**

:audio{#c8873dcb-4a59-4a28-871b-64936b1ea953 aid="c8873dcb-4a59-4a28-871b-64936b1ea953"}

1 - 3.$\hspace{1em}$**Choose the 3 true statements.** [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$As a professional athlete, he wanted to be superior to any other competitor.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$When he competed professionally, he was not interested in being famous.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$The athlete's injury had no emotional impact on him.
- D.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker's previous athletic career made his recovery more difficult.
- E.$\hspace{1em}$Regaining his self-esteem was key in finding a new direction in life.
- F.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker finds coaching other athletes truly rewarding.

**Complete the following gaps using no more than three words for each answer.** [3 marks]

The speaker thinks his success as an athlete was the result of **[-4-]** and of challenging himself to the fullest. He believes that being **[-5-]** helped him go through the injury with strength and courage and that what happened to him taught him a valuable **[-6-]**.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 3.$\hspace{1em}$ *Answers can be written in any order.* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, E, F}$   [4 marks]

- **A**. The speaker says, “When I was a professional athlete, my life basically revolved around perseverance and pushing my limits to excel in races…” The word excel means to surpass others or be superior in some respect. In this case, the speaker expresses he wanted to be superior to others in races.

- **E**. The speaker says, “Slowly, I began to rebuild my confidence and to find a new purpose in life,” which means that the athlete's self-esteem had decreased after the injury, but regaining it was vital to discover he could train young athletes; that was a new purpose for his life.

- **F**. The speaker says, “Now, I train young athletes, and I have a renewed sense of fulfilment.” The phrase *renewed sense of fulfilment* suggests he found training young athletes *satisfying*, which is a synonym of the word *rewarding*.

- *Incorrect options*: B, C, D

- **B**.  The speaker says, “...my life basically revolved around (...) and be recognised worldwide for this,” which means the speaker wanted to be famous worldwide.

- **C**. The former athlete says, “However, an injury drastically altered my life, causing my entire world to crumble.”  *To crumble* refers to how his world of success collapsed due to the injury. Then he adds that being told he couldn't compete anymore was *devastating*, which adds to the idea that what happened to him had an emotional impact on him.

- **D**. The speaker says, “All those years of hard training and overcoming obstacles (...) helped me navigate my recovery smoothly…” The phrase “navigate my recovery smoothly”  refers to the speaker going through the recovery  without difficulty.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{perseverance}$   [1 mark]

- “When I was a professional athlete, my life basically revolved around perseverance…” which means he never gave up and continued towards his goal.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{resilient}$   [1 mark]

- “All those years of hard training and overcoming obstacles had made me really resilient and strong, which helped me navigate my recovery smoothly…” *Resilient* refers to someone who is strong and quick to recover after a hard time in life.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{lesson}$   [1 mark]

- “Lesson learnt? Facing adversity can lead to significant personal growth.”
