# EB0286 

**Maximum mark: 5**

Dear Sharon,

How are you? Sorry I've taken so long to write, but I've been swamped with exams! Algebra was a tough one - you know I don't get on well with figures, lol! Anyway, I thought I'd drop you a line to tell you about my new life at university. Classes are interesting and thought-provoking, and teachers always encourage us to give our best. The beginning of the term, however, was not fun. I had to fill in and sign so many forms and documents…#boring! So many administrative tasks and bureaucracy.

I also have a new roommate named Emily Chen. She is from the UK. However, her parents were born in China. The other day, they dropped by for a couple of days, and they were so nice that they invited me to eat out at a traditional restaurant with them! It was an awesome experience.

In other news, I truly miss you, but I guess I will see you soon!  Can't wait to hear from you!

Lots of love

Jo

**Choose the four true statements.** [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The writer of the letter is overwhelmed with assessments.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Jo is writing to tell her friend about her university life.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Lectures are sometimes boring.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Teachers do not give their best.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$When the term began, Jo felt happy and relaxed.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$The school required the writer to complete a lot of paperwork.

**Find the word or phrase in paragraph 2 which means the following:** 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$visited    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$authentic    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Las respuestas pueden estar en cualquier orden.*  

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, B, F}$        [3 marks]

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I've been swamped with exams”

The phrase *to be swamped* means to be *overwhelmed* by having too much of something, in this case, exams.

- **B**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I thought I'd *drop you a line* to tell you about my new life at university”\

The phrase drop a line means to contact someone by sending a message or email. Jo is telling her friend about her $\text{\textquotedblleft}$new life at university.”

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I had to fill in and sign so many forms and documents”\

The word *paperwork* refers to *signing forms and documents*, which are the requirements of the University at the beginning of the year. 

- *Opciones Incorrectas:*  C, D, E

- **Option C** is incorrect. The word *lecture* makes reference to classes at a University and the writer states that the *classes are thought-provoking*, which means they are really interesting. The writer does say that filling in and signing documents was boring.

- **Option D** is incorrect. The text mentions that *teachers encourage students*, which implies that they do their best and try to motivate the students.

- **Option E** is incorrect. The writer mentions  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The beginning of the term, however, was not fun.” The phrase *not fun* means that the writer did not enjoy the beginning of the school year, which is the opposite of *happy and relaxed*.   

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{dropped by}$    [1 mark]

- The phrasal verb *drop* by means to visit someone spontaneously. 

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{traditional}$    [1 mark]

- The words *traditional and authentic* can be considered synonyms in this context. The text makes reference to a restaurant where you can taste *authentic* Chinese food.
