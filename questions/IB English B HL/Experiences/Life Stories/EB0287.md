October 2nd            

**Overcoming adversity one movie at a time**\

By Susan Stone

Hi movie freaks! Long time no see! I'm writing today to tell you about a movie that really blew my mind! It's called $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The Blind Side”, and let me tell you, it is a must-see. One of the most astounding facts is that it is based on the true story of an American football player who went on to become an NFL superstar.

It stars Sandra Bullock as Leigh Anne Tuohy and Quinton Aaron as Michael Oher. He plays an African-American homeless boy who comes from an impoverished neighborhood and overcomes hardships thanks to the help of Tuohy's family. Leigh Ann is moved by compassion and decides to invite him to stay with them, and the whole family stands by Michael by providing tutors and study help and cheering him on when he plays football.

The movie is full of highs and lows, since Michael's life becomes overwhelming from time to time. The story is deeply heartfelt, and it is a clear example of how a person can overcome adversity with compassion and generosity.

I recommend having a packet of tissues while watching this masterpiece!

I'd love to read your comments about $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The Blind Side”!

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{blew my mind}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a movie that really blew my mind.”\

The phrase $\text{\textquotedblleft}$to blow somebody's mind” means to impress or to amaze someone. This means the reviewer was highly impressed by the movie.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{must-see}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$it is a must-see.”\

The compound word *must-see* means that the movie is essential to watch; therefore, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$movie-freaks” are advised not to miss it.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{true story}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$it is based on the true story $\text{\textquotedblleft}$\

The review mentions the movie is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$based on a true story.” When something is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$based on a true story,” it means it has derived from a true story. 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- The word *while* is used to pair the two roles in the story. \

Option **B** could be confusing in this space. *Meanwhile* introduces an action that occurs simultaneously with another, as described in the text. However, it would suggest that Sandra Bullock shifts to Quinton Aaron's role.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The word *because* is used to show cause and effect. In the text, the word *because* anticipates the reason why Anne decides to invite Michael to her house. \

Option **A**, *although*, could be confusing in this space, however, it indicates contrast, not cause and effect.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- The phrase *thanks to* is used to show that something happened *because of* something else. Michael could overcome his problems *thanks to* the help he received.\

Option **C**, *because* could be confusing in the space. However, the preposition *of* is missing for the sentence to be grammatically correct.\

Option **F** could be confusing in this space. *Without* indicates the absence of something, but the sentence indicates Michael succeeded because of the help he received.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I recommend having a packet of tissues while watching this masterpiece!”\

The writer recommends *a packet of tissues*, suggesting that you may cry during the movie. This implies that you may feel *moved* by the film. 

- *Opciones incorrectas:* B, C, D

- **Option B**, *entertained*, is confusing. The review talks about the movie as it might be entertaining but the closing refers to the emotional impact of the movie.

- **Option C**, *relaxed*, is incorrect. The movie is described as an emotional story, which suggests it is intense, not relaxing.

- **Option D** is incorrect. The review does not focus on how *curious* you might feel about the story.
