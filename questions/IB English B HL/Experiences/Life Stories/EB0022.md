# EB0022

**Maximum mark: 5**

$\LARGE{\textrm{SOLO IN THE ANTARCTIC!}}$

Interviewer: **[-0-]**  So, Mr. Thompson, after two years alone in the Antarctic, what was the toughest part for you?

Mr. Thompson: Honestly, it was a mixed bag. Some days were a walk in the park, while others felt like I was climbing a mountain. The isolation was the real kicker. It's like being in the middle of nowhere, with no one but yourself to talk to.

Interviewer: **[-1-]** 

Mr. Thompson: I had to dig deep, you know? Sometimes, I'd get caught up in my own thoughts, and it was a slippery slope. But I kept a diary, which was a lifesaver. Writing down my feelings was like venting to a friend. I also kept busy with research; it kept my mind from wandering.

Interviewer: **[-2-]** 

Mr. Thompson: Oh, there were a few hair-raising moments, for sure. Once, I nearly ran out of fuel for the generator. That would have been a nightmare. But I managed to scrape by, patching things up as I went along.

Interviewer: **[-3-]** 

Mr. Thompson: In a heartbeat. Despite the ups and downs, it was a once-in-a-lifetime experience. I came out stronger and with a fresh perspective on life.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$   [1 mark]

- The answer below gap 1 describes the ways Mr Thompson used to cheer himself up (*keep his spirits up*), for example, keeping a diary or keeping himself busy.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$   [1 mark]

- The paragraph below gap 2 talks about difficult and potentially dangerous moments (*close calls*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- Mr. Thompson's answer *In a heartbeat* confirms his eagerness to try such an adventure again.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C, F

- **A:** This question is a distractor for gap 2, which indeed describes the dangerous situations, but none of them threatens Mr Thompson's life.

- **C:** None of the answers is focused on one positive experience during the stay in Antarctic

- **F:** This question is a distractor for gap 1, as this paragraph mentions Mr Thompson $`$busy with research', yet it is not the essence of this fragment- not breaking down is. 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A walk in the park}$   [1 mark] 

- *A walk in the park* is a very easy task/activity, which is a synonym of *a piece of cake.*

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$  $\textrm{in the middle of nowhere}$   [1 mark]

- Also accept: Being in the middle of nowhere 

- *Being in the middle of nowhere* means being far and cut off from other people and places, in a remote location.
