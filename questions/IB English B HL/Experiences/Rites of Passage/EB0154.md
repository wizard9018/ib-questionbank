## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1$.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{traditions and rituals}$

- “...every culture has traditions and rituals which connect couples to their heritage, their families and each other.”  Weddings are important parts of every culture and serve as times of connection for people who may see each other only during occasional but important ceremonies. [1 mark]

$2$.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{honoring elders}$

- “In China, a tea ceremony honors the couple's elders.” The text gives three examples of important elements of wedding traditions, beginning with this example from China. [1 mark]

$3$.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{exchanging rings}$

- “Canadian weddings often feature couples exchanging rings, representing eternity…” The symbolism within weddings highlights the importance of physical elements (rings), as well as their culturally significant meaning (together for eternity). [1 mark]

$4$.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{guests throw rice}$

- “in Greece, guests throw rice to symbolize prosperity.” In a similar way to question 3, the physical and symbolic elements of wedding traditions are explained. [1 mark]

$5$.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{love, unity and new beginnings}$

- “... ceremonies that symbolize love, unity and new beginnings.” Weddings are highly important ceremonies which signify the end of one chapter of life  - as unmarried people - and the beginning of a new chapter lived as a couple.  [1 mark]
