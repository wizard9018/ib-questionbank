## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{coming-of-age}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$there is a coming-of-age tradition that has been passed down for generations"\

The text identifies the tradition specifically as a $\text{\textquotedblleft}$coming-of-age” ritual, meaning it marks the transition from childhood to adulthood.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{thirteen}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$When a teenager turns thirteen"\

The tradition begins at a specific age in this community - thirteen - which marks the beginning of adulthood.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{very young}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$He had been fishing with his father since he was very young"\

Marco was not new to fishing - he had years of experience working on his father's boat. This makes his first solo trip a meaningful step forward, not a first experience of the sea.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{captain}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$this was his first trip as the captain"\

Despite his experience, Marco had always been a passenger and learner. Taking the role of captain for the first time placed all responsibility on him alone.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{five in the morning}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$He left at five in the morning"\

The early departure time reflects the reality of fishing, but also the significance of the moment. His family was watching from the dock as he left before sunrise.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{four hours}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Four hours later, Marco returned with a small catch"\

The length of the trip is important - four hours alone at sea, responsible for the boat and his own safety, shows his ability to enter the community of adults in the village.
