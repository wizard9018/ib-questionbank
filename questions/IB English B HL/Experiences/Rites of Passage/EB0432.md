# EB0432

**Maximum mark: 5**

Hey Sis,

I know I promised to call, but it has been super busy and stressful here. Jordan signed the papers yesterday. She is officially the owner of the bee farm! Hives, equipment, supplier contracts, and all. Forty-three years after Dad started it, Jordan is taking over Honey Hill Farms.

I watched her do hive inspections on Saturday morning. She was calm and methodical, exactly the way Dad taught me, and exactly the way I taught her. She didn't need me standing next to her, and we both knew it. I felt so proud. I also felt completely unnecessary, which I wasn't expecting.

You know better than anyone how much she struggled in her first year of college. Beekeeping gave her the structure she needed. Weekends at home, gradually taking on the responsibility that came with the family business. I think the bees saved her from giving up on college, in a way.

She's ready. I know she's ready. The question I'm asking now is: am I ready to let go? I spent most of my life running this business and then teaching it to her. I wonder what comes next for me?

I'll figure it out. Probably.

I'll call you Sunday.

Diane x

**The following statements are either true or false. Write True or False, then justify it using words as they appear in the text. Both parts are required for [1 mark].**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Jordan has recently become the legal owner of the farm.    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Diane taught Jordan the same techniques she had learned from their father.    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Diane expected to feel unnecessary when Jordan took over.    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Jordan found beekeeping helpful during a difficult period at college.    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Diane is confident about what she will do next.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: Jordan signed the papers yesterday. She is officially the owner of the bee farm.\

\

The word $\text{\textquotedblleft}$yesterday" confirms this happened very recently, and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$officially" confirms the transfer of ownership is now legal and complete.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: exactly the way Dad taught me, and exactly the way I taught her\

Diane passed on to Jordan the same knowledge and techniques she had received from their father, which emphasizes the fact that the business has been in the family for generations.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{False}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: I also felt completely unnecessary, which I wasn't expecting.\

Diane was surprised by this feeling. The phrase $\text{\textquotedblleft}$which I wasn't expecting" makes clear that she had not anticipated feeling unnecessary when the moment finally arrived.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: Beekeeping gave her the structure she needed\

During her first difficult year at college, Jordan struggled. The routine and responsibility of beekeeping provided exactly the stability she needed to continue.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True False}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: I'll figure it out. Probably.\

The word $\text{\textquotedblleft}$probably" shows that Diane is NOT confident about her future.  She does not know what comes next and is honest about her uncertainty.
