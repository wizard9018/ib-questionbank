$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- “Although they differ from culture to culture,...these ‘coming of age' events … celebrate the end of childhood and the beginning …of adulthood.” \
The end of childhood is a special time and in many cultures the celebration of it is an important symbol of the child's readiness to accept more responsibilities.

- *Incorrect options:* B, C

- **Option B**: “The age at which one is considered an adult varies.”\
 In the text, three different examples are given for the age children transition into adulthood in different cultures. 16 years old  is the example given for the United States.

- **Option C**: “...usually the family and community of the young person participate in or observe…”\
  The text tells us that in some cultures the whole community is part of the celebration, which means that it is not private.  *Private* means personal and not to be shared, or something that is restricted to a specific group of people.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- “... the family and community of the young person participate in or observe these ‘coming of age' events…”\
  The text states that families are involved in these ceremonies, but without giving specific examples.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **Option A**: The participation of family is important in marking this transition in a young person's life.  The text does not mention that the family ignores the celebration.

- **Option C**: “The age at which one is considered an adult varies.”  The age is not decided by the parents, but by the community.
