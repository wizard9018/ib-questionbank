# EB0385

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a farmer talking about their sheepdog's first solo trial.**

:audio{#0554861d-dbe2-4b69-b711-c47f2b3dea08 aid="0554861d-dbe2-4b69-b711-c47f2b3dea08"}

**Answer the following questions.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$What breed of dog is Moss?    [1 mark] 

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$What must a herding dog do before it can work with sheep?    [1 mark] 

**Choose the correct answer.**

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$What is the final task in the trial?    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Moving the sheep through a series of gates.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Gathering the sheep from the far end of the field.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Separating two sheep from the rest of the group.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$How long does it usually take most dogs to reach trial level?    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Two years

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Several years

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Eight weeks

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$What is most impressive about Moss's performance?     [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$He completed the course without making any mistakes.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$He corrected his only mistake without being told to.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$He completed the course in under five minutes.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(Border) Collie}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$He's a two-year-old Border Collie”\

The text clearly identifies Moss's breed. Border Collies are well known as working sheepdogs.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{pass a trial}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Before a herding dog can be trusted to work with sheep, he has to pass a trial”\

The trial is a formal assessment, described in the text as a $\text{\textquotedblleft}$dog exam”, that tests that the dog has the knowledge needed to safely work with sheep.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$separate two from the group at the end”\

Separating two sheep is the final task in the trial. It comes after gathering and moving the flock through the gates.

- *Incorrect options:* A, B

- **A**: Moving through the gates is the second task in the trial, not the final one.

- **B**: Gathering the sheep from the far end of the field is the first task, not the last.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$It takes most dogs years to compete at this level”\

The text uses the word $\text{\textquotedblleft}$years” - plural - indicating it usually takes a long time.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **A**: Two years is Moss's age, not the typical training period for most dogs.

- **C**: Eight weeks is the age at which Moss began his training, not the time it takes to reach trial level.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$he corrected himself without any command from me”\

The most impressive detail is not the time or the mistake itself, but that Moss recognised and fixed his own error independently, showing true readiness to work alone.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **A**: The text states Moss made one mistake, so he did not complete the course without errors.

- **C**: While completing the course in four minutes twelve seconds is fast, the text does not present the time as the most significant achievement.
