**Maximum mark: 6**

Anna's fingers brushed against something small and smooth in the damp grass. She picked it up, surprised to find a flat, palm-sized rock painted with a bright red heart surrounded by tiny yellow stars. Beneath the design, a faint blue stamp read $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Kendal,” a town she hadn't thought about in years.

She turned the rock over in her hands, feeling its cool weight. It was silly, maybe, a painted stone in the middle of the park, yet it stirred a quiet ache inside her. Kendal had been her childhood home, a place she'd left behind long ago when life pulled her away to bigger cities and busier days. She imagined someone, perhaps a child, painting the rock and leaving it here with hopes it would find its way into someone's day.

Anna smiled, tucking the rock gently into her pocket, and let her gaze drift to the distant skyline. The moment felt like a gift, small yet perfectly timed. She decided she'd pass the kindness along—perhaps paint her own rock with colors and leave it somewhere for someone else to find, like a thread connecting her to strangers through tiny, unexpected acts of wonder.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.4.*Answers 1 to 3 can be written in any order.*

$\textrm{B, E, F, G}$    [4 marks]

- **B.** The quote $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Kendal had been her childhood home, a place she'd left behind long ago" directly supports the idea that the stone was a reminder of a forgotten memory from her childhood.

- **E.** The quote $\text{\textquotedblleft}$yet it stirred a quiet ache inside her" indicates a sense of nostalgia and longing for the past.

- **F.** The sentence $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The moment felt like a gift, small yet perfectly timed" - suggests a new appreciation for the small joys in life.

- **G.** The sentence $\text{\textquotedblleft}$She decided she'd pass the kindness along" directly supports the idea of wanting to share kindness with others.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C, D, H

- **A.** The quote $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a decision to move back to her hometown" implies the stone might evoke nostalgia, but there's no indication in the text that Anna plans to move back.

- **C.** The quote $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a feeling of loneliness and isolation" suggests the opposite - a sense of connection and warmth.

- **D.** As the quote $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a plan to become a professional artist" reads, while the stone might inspire creativity, there's no explicit mention of Anna pursuing a career in art.

- **H.** The phrase $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a commitment to living in the present moment" might be understood as a positive takeaway, yet the text primarily focuses on nostalgia and the desire to share kindness, rather than a specific commitment to mindfulness.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{gift}$    [1 mark]

- “The moment felt like a gift, small yet perfectly timed”\

The expression *(a moment) is a gift* is a metaphorical way of saying that a moment is precious and will not come back, so we should make the most of it.

The text mentions Anna tucked her rock and felt this little act, this moment, was a *gift*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{kindness}$    [1 mark]

- “She decided she’d pass the kindness along”\

To *pass (the) kindness along* means to do something nice for someone else because something nice has happened to you.

In this case, Anna feels grateful for the rock and decides to be kind to someone else in return.
