# EB0016

**Maximum mark: 5**

$\LARGE{\textrm{🍂🍁 The Chestnut Festival on Madeira 🍁🍂}}$ 

Every year, the picturesque village of Curral das Freiras on Madeira Island fills with the sweet aroma of roasted chestnuts as the locals celebrate the Chestnut Festival. This vibrant event is a testament to Madeira's deep agricultural roots and the community's love for this humble nut.

Held in November, the festival is more than just a celebration of chestnuts. It's an immersive experience, offering visitors a glimpse into the traditions and culture of Madeira. Stalls line the streets, selling a variety of chestnut-based delicacies, from chestnut cakes and soups to liqueurs and breads. There's even a chestnut beer! 

The festival also features music, dances, and traditional Madeiran crafts. One of the highlights is the chestnut harvest contest, where locals and visitors compete to gather the most chestnuts in a limited time.

Whether you're a foodie, a culture enthusiast, or simply someone who enjoys a good festival, the Chestnut Festival is a must-visit. The warm and welcoming atmosphere, combined with the stunning backdrop of Madeira's mountains, makes it an unforgettable experience. So, mark your calendars and join in the festivities—you'll leave with a newfound appreciation for chestnuts and the charming island of Madeira.

Curious yet? It's time to pack your bags and dive into the chestnut madness!

**The following statements are either true or false. Write True or False, then justify it using words as they appear in the text. Both parts are required for [1 mark]**. [4 marks]

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$This festival is a tribute to Madeira's farming traditions.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Attendees at the festival can enjoy a range of traditional food and beverages made from chestnuts.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$The Chestnut Festival features a contest where participants engage in various activities like singing and dancing.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Curral das Freiras is located in a mountainous region of Madeira, providing a picturesque setting for the festival.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{0.5em}\textrm{True}$ [1 mark]

- Justification:$\text{\textquotedblleft}$This vibrant event is a testament to Madeira's deep agricultural roots”

- *Agricultural roots* equals *farming traditions*, while *testament* is the synonym of *tribute*, so the statement is true.

$2.$ $\hspace{0.5em}\textrm{True}$ [1 mark]

- Justification:$\text{\textquotedblleft}$Stalls line the streets, selling a variety of chestnut-based delicacies... There's even a chestnut beer!"

- *A variety of chestnut-based delicacies* is the equivalent to *a range of traditional food and beverages made from chestnuts*, and beer is a beverage, so the statement is true

$3.$ $\hspace{0.5em}\textrm{False}$ [1 mark]

- Justification:$\text{\textquotedblleft}$One of the highlights is the chestnut harvest contest, where locals and visitors compete to gather the most chestnuts in a limited time”

- The contest is about the speed of chestnut picking, not singing and dancing.

$4.$ $\hspace{0.5em}\textrm{True}$ [1 mark]

- Justification:$\text{\textquotedblleft}$...combined with the stunning backdrop of Madeira's mountains, makes it an unforgettable experience."

- *Stunning backdrop* is the equivalent to *picturesque setting*,  so the statement is true.

$5.$ $\hspace{0.5em}\textrm{C}$ [1 mark]

- The main focus of the text is to highlight the Chestnut Festival's various activities, attractions, and overall atmosphere in an engaging way that encourages readers to participate. 

- *Incorrect options:* A, B, D

- **A**: The text mainly focuses on the present-day celebration and atmosphere of the festival rather than delving into the historical significance of chestnuts.

- **B**: The text mentions the variety of chestnut-based products available but doesn't provide any recipes.

- **D**: The text doesn't compare the Chestnut Festival to other festivals but rather focuses on encouraging participation in this specific festival.
