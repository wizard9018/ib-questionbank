# EB0334

**Maximum mark: 6**

**You are going to hear a university student describing a leisure activity they enjoy.**

:audio{#666fb6aa-9657-4eda-8adb-095013ee360b aid="666fb6aa-9657-4eda-8adb-095013ee360b"}

**Complete the following gaps using no more than three words for each answer.**[5 marks]

The speaker practises accents in their **[-1-]**.\

They copy voices they hear in a **[-2-]** or a podcast.\

To improve pronunciation, they repeat the same **[-3-]** many times.\

They never practise accents in front of **[-4-]**.\

Practising accents helps them notice new words and identify the way people **[-5-]**.

**Choose the correct answer.**

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$Why does the speaker say they mix accents or invent their own?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$It is strange

- B.$\hspace{1em}$It is fun

- C.$\hspace{1em}$It is like acting

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{free time}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$One thing I really enjoy doing in my free time is practising accents.”\

The speaker clearly states that this activity is done during their free time.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{film}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I hear a voice in a film or a podcast and try to copy it.”\

The speaker mentions using films and podcasts as sources to practice accents.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{sentence}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I repeat the same sentence again and again, just to get the sound right.”\

Repeating the same sentence helps the speaker focus on pronunciation and/or sound.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{other people}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I never do it in front of other people.”\

The speaker prefers to practise accents privately and not in front of others.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{really sound}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I notice words I never heard before and how people really sound”\

The activity increases the speaker's awareness of vocabulary and natural speech.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... it helps me switch off and have fun with language.”\

The speaker mentions $\text{\textquotedblleft}$practising accents” is a way to $\text{\textquotedblleft}$have fun with language”

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **Option A** is confusing. The speaker mentions practicing accents is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$strangely relaxing,” but not strange.

- **Option C** is incorrect. The speaker mentions $\text{\textquotedblleft}$It's not about acting”
