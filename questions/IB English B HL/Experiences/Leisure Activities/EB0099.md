# EB0099

**Maximum mark: 6**

**You are going to hear a teen offering suggestions to their parents for a
family activity.**

:audio{#41746127-8fe5-42df-a0b3-f60507aa555b aid="41746127-8fe5-42df-a0b3-f60507aa555b"}

**Choose the correct answer.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$What is the first activity the speaker suggests?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Playing board games
- B.$\hspace{1em}$Going hiking
- C.$\hspace{1em}$Sitting in the park

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Why does the speaker suggest going to the new park?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$It has amazing views and is good for exercise
- B.$\hspace{1em}$It is close to their house
- C.$\hspace{1em}$To eat a meal together

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$What does the speaker suggest they eat when playing board games?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Pizza
- B.$\hspace{1em}$Snacks
- C.$\hspace{1em}$Burgers

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$What is the speaker’s main reason for suggesting activities?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$To spend time outdoors
- B.$\hspace{1em}$To spend time as a family
- C.$\hspace{1em}$To avoid being bored

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$What is the speaker's attitude toward the activities?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$They prefer board games over hiking
- B.$\hspace{1em}$They are open to any activity as long as the family is together
- C.$\hspace{1em}$They want the family to choose hiking because of the new park

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The speaker starts the conversation with,”I have a few ideas to share…   we could go hiking this weekend…”

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **A**: Playing board games is the second option the speaker makes.
- **C**: The speaker suggests going to the park to hike, not to sit in the park.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- The park is supposed to have *wonderful views*. And the speaker also says, “ It would be a nice way to get outside and burn some calories, too.”

- *Incorrect options:* B, C

- **B**: The speaker says that the part is *new*, but there is no mention of where the park is.
- **C**: The suggestion to eat a meal together is made in the second suggestion, to have a board games night.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- *Incorrect options:* B, C

- The speaker mentions *snacks* first, then suggests *burgers*, but their final decision is, *“Homemade pizzas!”*

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The speaker says, “We could make homemade pizzas,... and then we can get the board set up to play.”

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **A**: The speaker suggests hiking outdoors as a way for the family to do something together.  Being outdoors is not the speaker’s main goal.
- **C**: There is no mention of being bored.  The speaker states that,”... it feels like we haven’t done anything fun together as a family in a long time…” However, this does not mean that the speaker has been feeling bored.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- “I just want to spend some time with you,” is what the speaker says at the end of the text.  They begin with,”  it feels like we haven’t done anything fun together as a family in a long time,” demonstrating that the speaker wants to spend time with their family.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **A**: The speaker states,”Honestly, I don’t mind what we pick—I just want to spend some time with you.  We do not know if the speaker prefers one activity over the other: the goal is to spend time together as a family.
- **C**: The speaker suggests going to the park because of the lovely view and the opportunity for the family to be outdoors, doing some exercise, together. There is no information that the speaker prefers to hike rather than play games (see option A above).
