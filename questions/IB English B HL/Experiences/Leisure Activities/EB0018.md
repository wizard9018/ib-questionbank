# EB0018

**Maximum mark: 7**

Subject: Quack-tastic News from Duckland! 🦆

Hey Sue!

Hope you're doing awesome! So, I've been meaning to tell you about my latest obsession: rubber ducks! 🦆✨ I know, I know, it sounds a bit quacky, but hear me out. I've started collecting these little guys from all over the world, and it's turning into a full-blown adventure!

I've got a pirate duck from the Caribbean that's ready to set sail, a sushi chef duck from Japan (complete with a tiny sushi roll!), and even a superhero duck that's convinced $\underline{\textrm{it}}$ can save the world one bath at a time. Seriously, who knew rubber ducks could have such diverse careers?

I'm on a mission to find a duck from every continent—yes, even Antarctica! Imagine a penguin-duck hybrid! 😂 If you come across any cool $\underline{\textrm{ones}}$ during your travels, you HAVE to let me know. 

We should totally have a rubber duck party soon! I'll bring my collection, and we can vote on which $\underline{\textrm{one}}$ deserves the title of $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ducky of the Year.” Spoiler alert: it's probably going to be the pirate duck. Arrr!

Can't wait to hear from you!

Quack you later,

Bill 🦆💛

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$:answer[**To find a rubber duck from every continent**]     [1 mark]

- Also accept: Finding a rubber duck from every continent

- The writer mentions they are $\text{\textquotedblleft}$on a mission”, which means to take on a task that they consider very important. In this context *a mission* works as a synonym for *a goal*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$:answer[**It is a penguin-duck hybrid**]    [1 mark]

- The writer mentions they want to find ducks from every continent and jokingly speculates about the possibility of a penguin-duck hybrid.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$:answer[**(a/the) superhero duck**]     [1 mark]

- The pronoun *it* refers back to the superhero duck mentioned earlier in the sentence.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$:answer[**ducks**]    [1 mark]
