# EB0335

**Maximum mark: 6**

**You are going to hear a young adult talking about a leisure activity they enjoy in their free time.**

:audio{#244a34d9-5b1f-477b-81c4-a33fd972db64 aid="244a34d9-5b1f-477b-81c4-a33fd972db64"}

**Find the four true statements.**  [4 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker explores his area on his bike. 

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker plans their destination before leaving home.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker gets on whatever public transport arrives first. 

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker gets off in the middle of the ride. 

- E.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker sometimes visits parts of the city they do not know. 

- F.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker takes photos during these trips. 

- G.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker usually travels very far from home. 

- H.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker enjoys travelling alone.

**Answer the following questions.**

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$What does the speaker do after arriving at the final stop? Name **one**.    [1 mark]

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$How does the speaker describe this activity, even though they are not travelling far?    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C, E, F, H }$        [4 marks]

- **C**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$get on the first train or bus that arrives” \

The speaker does not plan ahead and chooses the first available form of transport.

- **E**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Sometimes I end up in areas I've never seen before.” \

The activity leads the speaker to discover parts of the city they $\text{\textquotedblleft}$do not know” or $\text{\textquotedblleft}$have never seen before”.

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I walk around, take a few photos, and then go home.” \

Taking photos is part of the speaker's routine during these trips.

- **H**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I like being alone and traveling at my own pace” \

The speaker clearly states that they enjoy travelling alone.

- *Incorrect options:*  A, B, D, G 

- **A**: The speaker mentions using *public transportation*, not a bike.

- **B**: The speaker says, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I don't choose a place first.” This means they do not $\text{\textquotedblleft}$plan their destination”

- **D**: The speaker mentions waiting $\text{\textquotedblleft}$until the final stop,” not getting off in the middle of the ride.

- **G**: The speaker says, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I know I'm not travelling far.”

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$*any one of the following answers:*   

- $\textrm{walk around}$

- $\textrm{take photos}$        [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I walk around, take a few photos, and then go home.”\

After arriving, the speaker explores the area briefly before returning home.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{a mini adventure}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... it still feels like a mini adventure.”\

The speaker compares the experience to a small adventure despite staying local.
