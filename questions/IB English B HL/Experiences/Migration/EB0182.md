## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{their traditions}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$When people move, they bring their traditions with them”\

Immigrants bring their cultural traditions when they move to a new place, and over time, some of these traditions blend (become part of) into the local culture. The word *their* is key in this answer, as immigrants bring traditions that belong to their countries.

2.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{food}$        [1 mark]

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{music}$        [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$One of the most visible examples is food” and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$music is another area where cultures blend”\

These lines indicate two areas in which cultural influence is most visible  or seen.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{migration}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$many of the world's most popular dishes, like pizza, sushi, and tacos, spread due to migration”\

Although these foods may now be found all over the world, they began in a few specific locations or countries (Italy, Japan, and Mexico), and were taken to new places by immigrants.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(it) expands}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Rather than replacing local culture, migration expands it”\

The text tells us that the traditions that immigrants bring to their new home expands the local culture and does not replace it. For example, eating a pizza outside of Italy, for example in London, does not negatively impact the culture of the U.K. - their traditional foods still exist and people still love them.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{genres}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Genres like jazz, reggae, and hip-hop emerged from the mixing of different traditions.”\

A genre is a type or style of something, usually related to art, music, and literature.  When multiple cultures come into contact, these arts can blend (mix), creating something entirely new. Emerge means to come out of, so these new genres come from combining different elements of multiple cultures. 

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$*any one of the following answers:*   

- $\textrm{richer}$

- $\textrm{more diverse}$        [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Rather than replacing local culture, migration expands it, creating something richer and more diverse.”\

The text indicates that migration enriches - makes them richer -  local cultures and makes them more varied or diverse.
