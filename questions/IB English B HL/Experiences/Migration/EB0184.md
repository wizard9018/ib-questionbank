# EB0184

**Maximum mark: 6**

**You are going to hear a professional football player share their migration experience.**

:audio{#1eb84668-11a0-4146-863f-4bba23f014a9 aid="1eb84668-11a0-4146-863f-4bba23f014a9"}

**Choose the correct answer.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$What was the biggest challenge for the speaker after moving abroad?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The intense training sessions.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The difficulty of adapting to life outside of training.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The lack of proper training facilities.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Why was forming friendships a challenge for the speaker?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$They didn't speak the language well.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Their teammates were unfriendly.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$They had no free time.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Why did the speaker almost give up?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$They weren't enjoying the training sessions.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$They were injured during the trip abroad.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$They felt isolated and missed home.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ What motivated the speaker to change their mindset?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$A coach's words of encouragement.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$A visit from their family.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$A new teammate who spoke their language.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$What does the speaker mean by $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Slowly, things got better"?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$They decided to return home.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Their training sessions became easier.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$They gradually adapted to their new life.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$How did the speaker feel when looking back on their migration experience?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$They regretted the decision to move.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$They felt it was a positive experience.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$They wished they had moved to a different country.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$but the real challenge was off the field”\

The footballer didn't speak the language of their new country very well, and he felt lonely. The phrase off the field means that the challenges the footballer faced were not related to football but to their everyday life outside the field.

- *Incorrect options:*  A, C

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the training was intense”, which should be expected if you are training to be a professional player. But this was not the biggest problem for the player.

- **C**: There is no mention of what kind of facilities the new team had, but the training was intense, so there must have been some good-quality facilities (buildings, equipment or services needed for a particular purpose) for the players to train in.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I didn't speak the language well, and making friends was difficult.”\

This was one of the biggest challenges for the player.  Because he couldn't communicate well, he felt *isolated* (alone and lonely).

- *Incorrect options:*  B, C

- **B**: The speaker does not mention his new teammates, so we do not know if they were friendly or not.

- **C**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the training was intense" shows that the player was busy, but it does not mean that there was no free time for making friends.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Making friends was difficult. I missed home, my family, and my old teammates.”\

The player was having trouble adapting to the new environment.  He was lonely and isolated, which made him consider giving up and returning home.

- *Incorrect options:*  A, B

- **A**: The player calls the training intense, but that does not mean that they weren't enjoying the experience and instead wanted to go home.

- **B**: There is no mention of any kind of injury, so **option B** is incorrect.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$One day, a coach pulled me aside and said, $`$Struggles make champions.' That changed my mindset.”\

The player was thinking about leaving because he was having a hard time adapting to the new environment, but his coach told him that when one *struggles* (tries very hard to overcome a problem or difficulty), one becomes stronger. The player stopped thinking about his situation as a problem and began to see it as an opportunity to grow.

- *Incorrect options:*  B, C

- **B**: The player says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I missed home, my family”, but there is no mention that anyone from their family came to visit.

- **C**: There is no mention that any of their teammates spoke their language, therefore **option C** is incorrect.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I started working harder, both on and off the field. Slowly, things got better.”\

With the words of encouragement from their coach, the player began to work harder. The extra effort improved everything, and eventually the player began to feel more at home.

- *Incorrect options:*  A, B

- **A**: The player had considered returning home but in the end, decided to stay and work even harder. Therefore, **option A** is incorrect.

- **B**: The player says “I started working harder” but there is no mention that the training became easier.  Therefore, **option B** is incorrect.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Looking back, migration tested me in ways I never imagined—but it also made me stronger.\

The player felt that overcoming the challenges of migration had strengthened them as a person.  They were able to overcome loneliness and homesickness as well as the challenges that learning a language and forming new relationships presented, alongside becoming a better football player.

- *Incorrect options:*  A, C

- **A**: Although there were some hard times, the player does not regret (wish something had not happened) making the decision to move abroad.  With time, they were able to see the challenges they overcame as a positive aspect of the move.

- **C**:  The player does not mention wishing they had moved somewhere else.  Therefore, **option C** is incorrect.
