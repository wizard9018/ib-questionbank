# EB0033

**Maximum mark: 8**

I don't like cold. I have lived in Toronto for two years and am still not used to the cold weather. It's so different from Manaus, my hometown in Brazil, where the weather is always hot and rainy. I did not want to move, but my parents believed that studying abroad was better than the education we could get at home, so they worked very hard and saved up enough money for us to move to Canada.  

When I arrived in Canada, I did not speak any English. I was 13 years old and in school, I had to take special English classes for kids like me. At first, I felt quite isolated and overwhelmed by how hard it was to learn a new language. But soon I realized that in that class there were so many kids whose parents had made the same decision as mine, and we all had the same goal: to learn English as quickly as we could. We wanted to get into mainstream classes - the classes for students who spoke English - so we could $\text{\textquotedblleft}$fit in". So we studied hard and learned to enjoy life in this great city as we got better and better in our English. I made my first friends in that class - kids like me who were homesick and couldn't communicate well, but were kind and funny and, most importantly, determined to please our parents.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1$.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.* $\hspace{1em}$  [2 marks]

- $\textrm{isolated}$

- $\textrm{overwhelmed}$

- The text clearly states that the writer did not speak any English and felt “quite isolated and overwhelmed”.

$2$.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.* $\hspace{1em}$  [2 marks]

- $\textrm{get into mainstream classrooms}$

- The writer mentions all kids in the wanted to learn English “as quickly as they (we) could” and then mentions they all wanted to “fit in”

- $\textrm{please their parents}$

- At the end of the paragraph, the writer mentions that it was very important for them to “please their parents” making this a cleare reason to learn English quickly.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{hot and rainy}$   [1 mark]

- “It’s so different from Manaus, my hometown in Brazil, where the weather is always hot and rainy"

- The writer mentions they have lived in Toronto for two years but they are “still not used to the cold weather” which implies they still miss the weather in Manaus, their hometown.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{studying abroad was better}$   [1 mark]

- “my parents believed that studying abroad was better than the education we could get at home”

- The meaning of the phrase *my parents believed* is similar to the meaning of *the parents’ understanding* and they both imply the parents’ thought education in another country would be better than the education in their home country.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{could “fit in”}$   [1 mark]

- “We wanted to get into mainstream classes – the classes for students who spoke English - so we could ‘fit in’.”

- The writer mentions immigrant students made an effort to join their “local” peers. The phrase *fit in* means to belong or to be accepted.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{determined to please (our parents)}$   [1 mark]

- “kids like me … determined to please our parents.”

- The writer mentions immigrant kids were similar and they all were determined to please their parents which means to make them happy.
