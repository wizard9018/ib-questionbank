## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 4.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, C, E, G}$        [4 marks]

- **B.** $\text{\textquotedblleft}$They help new arrivals adjust to life in a foreign country”

\

The volunteers play an important role in helping newly arrived immigrants *adjust* to their new home. *Adapt* is a close synonym of *adjust*, which means to make changes to fit in a new situation or environment.

- **C.** $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I see firsthand how a simple conversation can change someone's outlook.”

\

The coordinator can see how conversations between volunteers and immigrants can help improve their *outlook* or *perspective*. To $\text{\textquotedblleft}$see something firsthand" is to experience it personally.

- **E.** $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Many immigrants arrive feeling lost, unsure of where to start.”  \

Immigrants face many challenges when they arrive in a new location and the number of things they need to do can be overwhelming.  But with the support and guidance of the *volunteers, they feel less *insecure and are able to begin to adapt. *Insecure* is similar in meaning to *unsure* or *lost*.

- **G.** $\text{\textquotedblleft}$they provide kindness. A smile, a shared meal, or even a listening ear can make an overwhelming situation feel manageable”\

An $\text{\textquotedblleft}$act of kindness" is something done for someone simply out of a feeling of care, support, or friendship. An example is opening the door for someone, especially if you don't know them. Kindness helps new immigrants begin to feel safe and secure in their new environment and allows them to begin to take the steps they need to settle in.

- *Incorrect options:*  A, D, F, H

- **A.** The immigration center does not provide housing. They do provide other services as the volunteers $\text{\textquotedblleft}$assist with paperwork, explain transportation systems, and even help children with school assignments”.  Providing housing - a place to live - is not mentioned in the text.

- **D.** The center does not specifically help new immigrants find jobs.  The text states that volunteers $\text{\textquotedblleft}$assist with paperwork”, but is not specific about what kind.

- **F.** The volunteers assist immigrants of all ages, helping adults with paperwork and understanding how to get around ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$explain transportation systems”), and some even help children with homework assignments.

- **H.** Volunteers are people who work without receiving payment for their services. Many people volunteer to contribute to their community or to be active in an organization they share values with.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$They help new arrivals adjust to life in a foreign country, offering language support, cultural guidance, and emotional encouragement.”\

In addition to the actions given above, volunteers provide support in other ways: they $\text{\textquotedblleft}$assist with paperwork, explain transportation systems, and even help children with school assignments”.

- *Incorrect options:*  A, C

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$They help new arrivals adjust to life in a foreign country.“ Although assisting with paperwork is one of the ways volunteers help immigrants, it is not the main purpose for them being present.

- **C**: The immigration center helps newly arrived immigrants.  They are assisting them in getting settled into their new location, not in assisting them in getting back to their home countries.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$More than anything, they provide kindness. A smile, a shared meal, or even a listening ear can make an overwhelming situation feel manageable.”\

The small *gestures* (non-spoken movements or actions that show a feeling or emotion) mentioned show the immigrants that the volunteers care about them. The volunteers are happy to share time ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$a listening ear means" that you are ready to listen and pay attention to a person), food (a *meal*) or just show that they care with a gentle smile.

- *Incorrect options:*  A, B

- **A**: All of the small gestures or acts of kindness shown by the volunteers are intended to *highlight* (emphasize or show the importance of something) the similarities between people, not the differences.  Everyone likes to be cared for, treated kindly, and encouraged when facing challenges, which is what the volunteers do for the newly arrived immigrants.

- **B**: None of the gestures mentioned requires the people involved to share a language.  While learning the language of the new location will be needed, it is not being emphasized (*highlighted*) by the gestures or acts of kindness mentioned.
