# EB0410

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a young woman talking about a traditional skill she has learned.**

:audio{#d5c1e3ac-af35-4e5c-8997-4cf696f982ed aid="d5c1e3ac-af35-4e5c-8997-4cf696f982ed"}

**Answer the following questions.** 

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$What has the speaker done every day since Kara arrived?    [1 mark] 

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$What is the wingspan of the speaker's eagle?    [1 mark] 

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$What does the speaker do when Kara lands?    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$In the speaker's community, eagle training had become...    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{fed her by hand}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I have fed her by hand every day since then"\

The speaker says Kara came to them as a chick, and she has fed her by hand every day.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{almost two meters}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$her wingspan is almost two meters"\

The speaker describes how big her eagle is. Kara weighs 5 kilograms and her wingspan - the distance from the tip of one wing to the tip of the other when they are stretched out - is almost two meters.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{stand (very) still}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I have learned to stand very still when she takes off and lands on me"\

The speaker mentions that when the eagle takes off and lands, she has to stay very still.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{c}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$eagles were only handled by men"\

The speaker states that the tradition was exclusively male. 

- *Incorrect options:* 

- **Option A** is incorrect.  The text says men and women worked with eagles only in the very old days, not something that was currently done. 

- **Option B** makes a mistake about the grandmother's role. She encouraged the speaker but did not teach the tradition to her.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$we are proud to be part of something very old that is becoming new again"\

This highlights the idea that the girls feel connected to an ancient tradition. 

- *Incorrect options:* 

- **Option B** contradicts the text - the speaker says they do not see themselves as unusual. 

- **Option C** is only true for the speaker, not the group.
