# EB0409

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a news feature about an unusual annual competition.**

:audio{#880c08f2-ccb1-42c0-a488-f6aebad97a0d aid="880c08f2-ccb1-42c0-a488-f6aebad97a0d"}

**Choose the five true statements.** [5 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The competition takes place on the same day every year.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Ashton is a large, well-known city in England.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Participants come to Ashton from all over the globe.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The championship has been running for less than four decades.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$Conkers are roughly the same size as a tennis ball.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$A match ends when one of the conkers can no longer continue. 

- G.$\hspace{1em}$Players must thread their conker onto a piece of string of a specific length.

- H.$\hspace{1em}$Participants are free to prepare their conker however they wish before competing.

- I.$\hspace{1.4em}$Both players strike at the same time during a match.

- J.$\hspace{1.2em}$The last competitor with an unbroken conker earns a special title.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 5.$ $\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, F, G, J}$        [5 marks]

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$On the first Sunday of every October"\

The text confirms the event always falls on the same day, making it a fixed annual date.

- **C**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$People from over thirty countries arrive"\

The text states that competitors travel from more than thirty different countries, showing the event has worldwide reach.

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The matches continue until only one conker is left"\

The text confirms that play continues until one conker is destroyed, meaning only the surviving conker advances to the next match.

- **G**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Each conker must be threaded onto 20 centimeters of string"\

The text specifies an exact string length, confirming that competitors must follow a precise measurement.

- **J**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the owner crowned the King - or Queen - Conker"\

The text states that the final winner is crowned as either the King or Queen, which makes this statement correct.

- *Incorrect options:*  B, D, E, H, I

- **B**: The text describes Ashton as a quiet village, not a city, so this statement is incorrect about its size.

- **D**: The competition started in 1965, which means it has been running for over five decades.

- **E**: The text compares conkers to a ping-pong ball, which is significantly smaller than a tennis ball.

- **H**: The text states that treating or hardening a conker before a match is not allowed, so competitors do not have free choice in how they prepare.

- **I**: The text explains that players take turns swinging, so they do not hit simultaneously.
