## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{count}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Swan Upping - an annual count of the swan population on the river"\ 

Swan Upping is described as an official count of the swan population on the Thames.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{twelfth century}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a tradition that has taken place every July since the twelfth century" \

The reporter tells us that Swan Upping has been happening since the 1100s, ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the twelfth century”), making it a very old tradition.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*any one of the following answers:* 

- $\textrm{catch}$

- $\textrm{check}$        [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Teams of people travel upriver to catch, check, and count every swan" \

The census requires the birds to be counted and to be checked to see if they are healthy.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{baby swan}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I'm watching my team lift a baby swan, out of the water" \

The reporter gets to watch the capture of a baby swan, and tells us what is happening in real time.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{weight}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$They're recording its weight and checking for injuries" \

Once the bird is out of the water, the team records how heavy it is and makes sure it is healthy and with no injuries before releasing it.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{five days}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The count runs for five days" \

The reporter states clearly that the entire Swan Upping count takes five days to complete.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{passed to (wildlife) vets}$    [1 mark]

- Accept other wording with similar meaning.\

\

$\text{\textquotedblleft}$those cases will be passed to wildlife vets" \

Swans found to be injured are not treated on the spot - they are referred to wildlife vets for professional care.
