## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{traffic}$   [1 mark]

- “booking a hotel, packing a suitcase, and then fighting traffic”
- The text mentions *fighting traffic* which is similar in meaning to *struggling in traffic*. Both expressions imply that traffic can be difficult and it might be hard to get to the airport, as there might be traffic jams.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{airport}$   [1 mark]

- “fighting traffic to get to an overcrowded airport”
- *Reach* and *get to* are synonyms, both meaning to arrive. The text mentions that getting to the airport con be stressful.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{daily life}$   [1 mark]

- “The stress of traveling to “get away” from your daily life can be more harmful than the rest you get while you’re away.”
- The phrasal verb *get away* is a synonym of *escape*. The text mentions you take holidays to escape your daily life.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- “you could set up a cozy outdoor picnic in your backyard.”

- The phrasal verb *set up* means to make arrangements so something can take place.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **Option A**: There is no mention of a road trip as part of a staycation.  “Fighting traffic” was given as a reason to not choose a traditional holiday that requires a drive to a busy airport.
- **Option C**: Staying in a hotel is what is done in a traditional holiday, but a staycation is a holiday you take in your own home.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- “…spend your time exploring neighborhoods you’ve never had time to see, or trying new restaurants…”

- The text mentions a “Staycation” is an opportunity to explore your own place, and discover new neighborhoods.

- *Incorrect options:* B, C

- **Option B**: Although the text suggests trying new restaurants, there is no mention of “exotic cuisines”.  There is also no mention of traveling to another country.
- **Option C**: The text focuses on activities in or near your home, not far “turn your home into an inviting retreat… a cozy outdoor picnic in your backyard.” It suggests “exploring neighborhoods you’ve never had time to see… or visiting the local museum”, both of which are close to home.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The text suggests both visiting museums and exploring new neighborhoods, but there is no mention of hosting dinner parties.  The text does suggest “an outdoor picnic in your backyard”, but that is not the same as a dinner party. As a party is a more sophisticated activity than a picnic.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **Option A**: One suggestion for things to do includes “visiting the local museum.”
- **Option C**: Another suggestion given in the text is “exploring neighborhoods you’ve never had time to see”

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The text suggests that stress can be a negative side-effect of traditional holidays that require travel, and lists several elements of planning a trip that may be stressful. “The stress of traveling to ‘’get away’’ from your daily life can be more harmful than the rest you get while you’re away.”

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **Option A**: Food is mentioned in the text twice: as a picnic, and as a suggestion to try local restaurants.  Food is something that will need to be bought wherever a person is, so a staycation does not remove the need to spend money on food.
- **Option C**: The text suggests several activities that would require a person on a staycation to leave their house: “exploring neighborhoods you’ve never had time to see, or trying new restaurants, or visiting the local museum.” A staycation does not mean that a person would never leave their home for the length of the holiday.

$8.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- After listing some of the stressors that may be associated with a traditional holiday, the staycation is introduced: “Maybe a “staycation” is a good option for you.”

- *Incorrect options:* A, B

- **Option A**: The text introduces only one alternative to traditional holidays, therefore it is not the main purpose of the text.
- ** Option B**: The text lists some of the inconvenient or unpleasant aspects of traditional holiday travel, including: “fighting traffic to get to an overcrowded airport.” However, the purpose of the text is not to stop people from going to airports, but to reconsider the purpose of their holiday.  If it is to relax and not be stressed, then the staycation is an appropriate alternative to traditional holidays.
