# EB0323 

**Maximum mark: 7**

**You are going to hear a TV presenter introducing a segment.**

:audio{#14d69ade-99be-4042-9b98-8f4b7718be87 aid="14d69ade-99be-4042-9b98-8f4b7718be87"}

**Answer the following questions.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Why is Tom Stuker famous for?    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$How does Tom Stuker fly first-class anywhere?    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Who else enjoys the flights with Tom?    [1 mark]

**Choose the four true statements.**[4 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Tom feels at home at airports. 

- B.$\hspace{1em}$He gets benefits every time he travels. 

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Sometimes he uses bonus points to buy cars. 

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Tom also pays for premium accommodation with his points. 

- E.$\hspace{1em}$He has bought luxury items with points. 

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Tom established a children's foundation. 

- G.$\hspace{1em}$Tom has always been an enthusiastic flyer. 

- H.$\hspace{1em}$There is a movie about him.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(he has) flown more than 23 million miles}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Tom Stuker, the man who has flown more than 23 million miles”\

The audio mentions this is what makes Tom's story $\text{\textquotedblleft}$fascinating.”

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(he invested 510.000 in) a lifetime pass}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Back in 1990, Stuker made a bold move: he invested \$510,000 in a lifetime pass”\

This is how he can fly $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ anywhere, anytime,” he has bought a pass that allows it.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{a companion}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$That golden ticket allows him and a companion”\

Tom can take a companion when he flies.

$4 - 7.$ $\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, B, D, H}$        [4 marks]

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$he's turned airports into his second home”\

The phrase $\text{\textquotedblleft}$second home” means Tom feels comfortable, as if he lived there.

- **B**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$earning millions of reward points along the way”\

$\text{\textquotedblleft}$Reward points” are benefits, an advantage you gain from something, in this case, flying.

- **D**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$He has used these points to hire cars, and pay for luxury hotels”\

*Luxury hotels* and *premium accommodation* are similar in meaning.

- **H**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$even inspired a Hollywood film”\

The text mentions he has $\text{\textquotedblleft}$inspired a Hollywood film.” This means that a film was created based on his story.

- *Incorrect options:*   C, E, F, G

- **C**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$He has used these points to hire cars”\

To *hire* a car is to rent it for some time, not buy it.

- **E**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$He has used these points to hire cars, and pay for luxury hotels”\

The text mentions he has used the points to pay for luxury hotels. Hotels are services, not items (things).

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Stuker has also used those points to support a children's cancer charity”\

Stuker supports a charity, but he has not funded or established it. 

- **G**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$He was once afraid of flying!”\

Stuker was not a fan of flying before.
