**Tips for a safari photography trip.**

Visiting South Africa is one of the most $\underline{\textrm{gripping}}$ experiences you can have in your entire life. Here are some $\underline{\textrm{pointers}}$ you can follow for taking the best photos while going on a safari trip.  

**[-3-]**\

This is essential because you will be in open areas in which a wide-angle lens can be the right choice for landscapes, or you will need a telephoto lens when you want to isolate an animal from its surroundings. Therefore, selecting the correct one is important. 

**[-4-]**\

Photographing animals in their natural habitat can be a challenge. Finding a spot and hiding are essential tips to avoid disturbing them. You must be ready to wait for long periods of time, often in silence. The most important thing is not to interfere with their natural behaviour. 

**[-5-]**\

The animal's environment is a crucial part of the story you want to convey in your photograph, so take it into consideration when framing your shot.

**[-6-]**\

Do not feed animals, shout, or get too close to them. Bear in mind that wild animals are territorial; therefore, getting too close may cause a direct attack.

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- The word *thrilling* is a synonym of the word *gripping*, meaning very exciting.\

\

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

**Option D** is incorrect because *clues* are hints, related to a mystery or problem to solve.
