# EB0325

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a person talking about their camping holidays.**

:audio{#55ba16e2-4cc8-49c4-b1cf-b7999fe06ca5 aid="55ba16e2-4cc8-49c4-b1cf-b7999fe06ca5"}

**Answer the following questions.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$What does the family do every year?    [1 mark]

**Choose the correct option.**

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$When they went to the mountains...    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$they prepared thoroughly.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$they felt exhausted.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$they were careful.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$However, as soon as they got there...    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$it had been raining.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$the weather was not ideal.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$it rained for 10 days.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$They stayed in the tent because...    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$they did not have dry wood.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$they were soaked and cold.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$they did not want to get dirty.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$The family feels...    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$this was a great camping experience.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$this was an interesting camping experience.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$this was the worst camping experience.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{buy new gear}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Year after year, we buy new gear”\

$\text{\textquotedblleft}$Year after year” means they do it every year. One possible mistake to look out for is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$going campling.” The speaker says they go camping for holidays but they do not mention it is every year.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$packed everything carefully”\

*Thoroughly* and *carefully* are synonyms. The speaker mentions they *packed* or *prepared* everything in detail.

- *Incorrect options:* B, C

- Option **B** is incorrect. The speaker says they were *excited*, not *exhausted*. 

- Option **C** is confusing. The speaker says they $\text{\textquotedblleft}$packed carefully,” but this does not mean they were *careful*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$On the first night, it started raining”\

The speaker mentions that night it began to rain and did not stop for 10 days.

- *Incorrect options:* A, B

- Option **A** is incorrect. It had not been raining before they got there. It started raining the night they arrived.

- Option **B** is confusing. This option is too vague and does not include the detail of 10 days of rain.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$our clothes were always wet, and we were freezing”\

The speaker mentions they were *wet* (*soaked*) and *freezing* (*cold*), so they stayed inside the tent.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- Option **A** is confusing. The speaker says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the firewood was soaked” but this alone is not the reason to stay in the tent. Because the wood was wet, they had wet clothes and were freezing.

- Option **C** is incorrect. The speaker mentions $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the ground was muddy,” but this is not the reason not to leave the tent.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$it was the worst weather, but the best family memories”\

The speaker mentions that, in spite of the bad weather, this camping created $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the best family memories”

- *Incorrect options:* B, C

- Option **B** is incorrect. When something is interesting, it catches your attention or curiosity. However, the text mentions that the trip was memorable.

- Option **C** is incorrect. Although it rained for 10 days, the trip created $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the best family memories” so it was not the worst overall.
