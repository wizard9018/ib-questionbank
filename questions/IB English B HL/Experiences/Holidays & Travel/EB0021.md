# EB0021

**Maximum mark: 7**

October 25th, 2022

Paradise Hotel\

Hotel Manager

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to express my absolute indignation with our recent stay at your hotel last week. 

Our room was located on the 7th floor, where there was a massive electrical failure shortly after arrival. Moreover, there were no well-trained hotel workers on duty to fix the issue for days. Hence we were moved to a first-floor room, as it was the only available accommodation in that hotel. However, what really caused our annoyance was that on the first floor there were construction works in progress in some rooms during the entire stay, and all day long, the sound was unbearable as well as the smell of freshly painted walls.

To make matters worse,  the front desk receptionists were visibly irritated and overwhelmed by the many complaints from other guests. Far from being resourceful, they were unresponsive when we expressed our displeasure. 

This chain of complications did not allow my wife and I to have a proper rest, and our exhaustion forced us to cancel some of the activities we had planned to do.

We demand a refund and we look forward to a prompt reply.

Faithfully,

Peter Bolt

**The following statements are either true or false. Write True or False, then justify your answer using words as they appear in the text. Both parts are required for [1 mark].**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Unfortunately, an electrical failure had occurred on the seventh floor before their arrival.    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$The hotel failed to take proactive measures to solve the electrical incident.    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$The noise from the repairs on the first floor significantly interfered with guests' relaxation.    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$The writer of the letter and his wife were the only guests who felt dissatisfied.    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$The hotel staff could not offer solutions to the writer of the letter and his wife's complaints.     [1 mark]

**Choose the correct answer.**

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$The writer expects the hotel to    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$apologise to him and his wife.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$compensate him with a new vacation stay.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$give back the money they paid for their stay.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$None of the above

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$What is the primary purpose of the letter?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$To praise the hotel's facilities

- B.$\hspace{1em}$To express his dissatisfaction with his stay at the hotel.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$To request a refund.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$To give details about his displeasure with his stay and to request a reimbursement.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{False}$   [1 mark]

- **Justification:** $\text{\textquotedblleft}$shortly after arrival"

- This means the electrical problem occurred a short time after the couple arrived at the hotel.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True}$   [1 mark]

- **Justification:** $\text{\textquotedblleft}$no well-trained hotel workers on duty to fix the issue for days."\

*well-trained workers* means workers with the skills or abilities to repair the problem. *On duty* is a key phrase since it means the workers were not currently at the hotel (their workplace) to do their job.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True}$   [1 mark]

- **Justification:** $\text{\textquotedblleft}$all day long, the sound was unbearable" \

*Unbearable* means that the noise from the construction works in progress was too loud, impossible to tolerate, and, as a result, the guests could not sleep, rest, or relax properly. 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{False}$   [1 mark]

- **Justification:**  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$by the many complaints from other guests"\

*Complaints* means expressing dissatisfaction. Many other people staying at the hotel were expressing this feeling to the hotel staff at the reception desk. 

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True}$   [1 mark]

- **Justification:** $\text{\textquotedblleft}$they were unresponsive"\

*Unresponsive* suggests the staff at the reception desk ignored the guests' complaints about the problems they were experiencing at the hotel. 

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- This is the only valid answer since when the writer closes the letter, he specifically says that he demands a *refund* which means to be given the money he paid for his stay back. 

- **Option A** is incorrect since, even though it is implied that the writer would like the hotel to apologise for the inconveniences, the writer does not explicitly mention this. As regards **option B**, there is no evidence in the letter that the writer wants to stay again at the hotel for a new vacation. 

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$   [1 mark]

- **Option D** is the correct option since, in the letter, the writer describes in detail all the problems he and his wife suffered during his stay at the hotel. Actually, he also specifically says he feels $\text{\textquotedblleft}$absolute indignation" and he demands a reimbursement when he uses the word *refund*. *Reimbursement* and *refund* are synonyms, and they mean *to be paid back*. 

- **Option A** is completely invalid as *praise* means *to express approval or admiration* and the writer clearly does not approve of any of the aspects of his stay at the hotel. \

**Option B** touches on one important purpose of the letter (to express his disapproval), but the writer also expects the hotel to return the money he paid for the stay since he is not satisfied, and this idea is left out of this option. **Option C** is wrong because it only focuses on the writer's demand for his money back.
