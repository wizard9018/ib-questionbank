# EB0272 

**Maximum mark: 6**

Dear Rachel,

I'm having a wonderful time in Scotland! The island of Skye is an astonishing place to visit and summer is the ideal time to come. The dramatic landscapes and sea cliffs create scenes that I believe are perfect for filming a movie or series, especially one of medieval times. Yesterday I visited the Old Man of Storr hill and I can tell you that going up was tiring, but unforgettable. The hike took about one hour or a bit more, because of the wind and drizzle. Let me tell you that it was exhausting!. Do you know why people call it $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Old Man of Storr”? It is crazy, but you can see the face of an old man if you look at it from the right angle. I couldn't see it; however, I liked the story. I can guarantee that this place is one of the most photographed spots in Scotland.

I'm staying at a gorgeous lodge. It is a historic country house, which I believe resembles the house in the movie Pride and Prejudice. In the interior, you can see pictures of the first owners and some antique furniture. It seems like a little museum!

Well, that's all for now. See you next week!

Love,

Mary

**1 to 4. Choose the four true statements.** [4 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Summer is the best season to visit the Island

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The island would make a great setting for movies due to its scenery.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Filming a movie would be an easy task in those landscapes.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The hike was less than one hour.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$The walk was tiring due to the weather.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$The legend states that the mountain resembles an old man's profile.  

- G.$\hspace{1em}$Mary could clearly identify the old man's features.

- H.$\hspace{1em}$The Old Man of Storr is the most visited place in Scotland. 

**Find the word or phrase in paragraph 2 which means the following:** 

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$inn    [1 mark]

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$vintage    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 4.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, B, E, F}$        [4 marks]

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... summer is the ideal moment to come.”\

The text states that the *ideal* moment to visit the island is summer; the word *ideal* suggests this is *the best* season to visit. 

- **B**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The dramatic landscapes and sea cliffs create scenes that I believe are perfect for filming a movie or series especially one of medieval times”\

The text mentions that the place would be ideal to film a movie (or series) set in medieval times. The exercise uses the more general word *scenery* to describe the *landscapes*.

- **E**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$going up was tiring [...] because of the wind and drizzle”\

The writer states that the walk was short; however, because of the bad weather conditions (wind and drizzle), it felt really long and tiring. 

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$...can see the face of an old man if you see it from the right angle.”\

The word *story* makes reference to the *legend* about the mountain. The word *profile* is similar to *face*. 

- *Incorrect options:*  C, D, G, H

- **C**: C is incorrect. The writer never mentions that filming a movie in that landscape would be easy or difficult.

- **D**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The hike took about one hour or a bit more.” \

The writer states that the trip took $\text{\textquotedblleft}$about” one hour or a little bit more. That means that the trip was not shorter than one hour. 

- **G**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$you can see the face of an old man [...] I couldn't see it”\

The writer states that she could not identify the *face* or *features* of the man.

- **H**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I can guarantee that this place is one of the most photographed spots in Scotland.”\

The writer states that the Old Man of Storr is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$one of the most photographed spots in Scotland.” However, that does not mean that it is the most visited place. 

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{lodge}$   [1 mark]

- The words *lodge* and *inn* are similar in meaning. A *lodge* is a place to stay and it is often linked to nature while *inn* is a small hotel.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{antique}$   [1 mark]

- The words *antique* and *vintage* are synonyms and both mean that the furniture comes from a past era.
