Flight attendants have many different responsibilities:

Greeting passengers and making them feel comfortable are examples of the **[-3-]** aspect of the job.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Safety procedures}$   [1 mark]

- “The crew takes time to carefully review safety procedures”
- Safety procedures are reviewed by the crew as part of the process of getting an airplane ready for flight before passengers arrive.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{crew}$   [1 mark]

- “The crew takes time to carefully review safety procedures”
- *Crew* is a word used to describe a group of people who work together, and is specifically used for the people who work on ships or airplanes.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{hospitality}$   [1 mark]

-  “much of my job is hospitality: greeting passengers, serving meals, and making sure everyone is comfortable”
- *Hospitality* is the act of being friendly and welcoming to guests and visitors. A part of the flight attendant's job is to ensure passengers are well taken care of.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{medical situations}$   [1 mark]

- “I am also trained to handle emergencies - from turbulence to medical situations”
- *Emergencies* and *crises* in this context have a similar meaning. The speaker mentions they are trained to deal with issues that might happen during flight.

$5$.$\hspace{1em}$*Answers can be written in any order.*

-   $\textrm{meeting new people}$
-   $\textrm{seeing new places}$

- “But I love meeting new people and seeing new places, so it’s the perfect job for me…”
- The speaker mentions at the end of the extract that meeting people and seeing new places makes this “the perfect job” which indicates these are aspects they enjoy.
