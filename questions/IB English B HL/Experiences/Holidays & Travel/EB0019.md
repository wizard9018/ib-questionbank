# EB0019

**Maximum mark: 6**

Going abroad on holidays is a once-in-a-lifetime chance to fully immerse oneself in a different culture. Unusual sights, sounds, and smells of another country greet passengers upon arrival. Experiencing local customs and traditions is one of the most rewarding benefits of traveling. For instance, trying traditional delicacies, attending a local festival, or simply being immersed in the everyday life, such experiences broaden your horizons and give you deeper insights into the world.

When it comes to cultural exposure, learning a few words in the right language may help to bridge the gap and allow you to get a deeper feeling for how someone lives. Even the courtesy of greeting and expressing gratitude may lead to a new friend or story.

Beyond the cultural panorama, travel offers a thrilling opportunity to explore marvellous landscapes, historical sites, and architectural wonders. Whether you prefer busy urban areas or the serenity of natural scenery, you can explore each differently. Sometimes, merely crossing the threshold into a new environment can change one's outlook on things. Do not underestimate the power of travelling; you will be flying back home in a state of awe and appreciation of life.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- In the text, “unusual sights, sounds, and smells” refers to things that are different from what travelers normally experience. *Unique* is a good replacement word because it focuses on distinctiveness.

- **Option D** is incorrect. *Strange* suggests something is weird. The text has a positive tone, so *strange* is too negative.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$   [1 mark]

- “Local customs and traditions” means traditions that belong to a specific area or region. Regional describes things connected to a particular place.

- **Option A** is confusing. *National* relates to a particular country, not a region. 

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- In the text, “rewarding benefits of traveling” refers to experiences that add value to a person’s life. *Meaningful* is a good replacement word becasue it reflects this benefit. 

- **Option F** is incorrect. *Entertaining* only focuses on the amusement, not the deeper benefits implied in *rewarding*.
