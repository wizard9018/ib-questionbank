# EB0383

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a nurse talking to a friend about their first solo night shift.**

:audio{#b7f46048-e474-4409-8b14-990a5af65d71 aid="b7f46048-e474-4409-8b14-990a5af65d71"}

**Choose the three true statements.** [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$It was the nurse's first time working alone.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The nurse was in charge of many patients.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The nurse felt relieved but still nervous.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Mrs. Banda was in bed six.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$The nurse asked for advice before changing the medicine.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Mrs. Banda felt better in less than an hour.

**Answer the following questions.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$What was the intern learning to do?    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$How does the nurse describe themselves professionally at the end of the shift?    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ A, C, F}$        [3 marks]

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$No supervisor — just me and twelve patients”\

The nurse was completely alone for the first time - there was no senior member of staff available to ask for help or rely on.

- **C**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I was happy to have been assigned to the small ward. But I was still nervous”\

A *ward* is a section of a hospital or clinic that serves one type of patient. Although the nurse was happy to be working in a smaller setting, the responsibility of being in charge on their own was still causing anxiety.

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$twenty minutes later she was comfortable”\

Twenty minutes is less than an hour, so this statement is true. The nurse acted independently and Mrs. Banda's condition improved quickly as a result.

- *Incorrect options:*  B, D, E

- **B**: The text states the nurse was assigned to a $\text{\textquotedblleft}$small ward”, and we are told there were 12 patients, which is not a large number.

- **D**: The text clearly states Mrs. Banda was in $\text{\textquotedblleft}$bed four,” not bed six.

- **E**: There is no mention of the nurse consulting anyone. The text makes clear there was no supervisor present, and the nurse acted independently.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{enter data}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$how to enter data into the system”\

The nurse took time during a quiet moment to guide the intern through the data entry process, already beginning to take on a mentoring role.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{a true professional}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Now I feel like a true professional”\

Having successfully managed the ward alone for the first time, the nurse felt they had experienced an important moment in their career.
