# EB0380

**Maximum mark: 7**

**You are going to hear a recorded phone message left by a school counselor for a student's parents.**

:audio{#be4f4d99-556c-484b-b2b4-38ee29a4317d aid="be4f4d99-556c-484b-b2b4-38ee29a4317d"}

**Choose the four true statements.** [4 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The caller works at Jordan's school.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The incident took place during a history examination.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The incident occurred during an afternoon examination.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Ms. Hartley personally witnessed the incident during the exam.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$A mobile device had been concealed during the exam.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Jordan was found to be copying from written materials during the exam.

- G.$\hspace{1em}$Jordan was uncooperative when asked about the incident.

- H.$\hspace{1em}$Jordan acknowledged responsibility for their actions. 

**Answer the following questions.**

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$What grade will Jordan receive for the history exam?    [1 mark]

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ When is the formal meeting scheduled?    [1 mark]

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$Name **one** possible consequence discussed at the meeting.     [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, B, E, H}$        [4 marks]

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am the student counselor at Redfield High School”\

Ms. Hartley identifies herself at the beginning of the message, confirming her role at the school.

- **B**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Jordan was involved in a serious incident during this morning's history exam”\

The text specifies that the exam subject was history, confirming that this is where the incident took place.

- **E**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$...Jordan using a hidden phone”\

The text states the phone was concealed, meaning it had been hidden on purpose during the exam.

- **H**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Jordan was cooperative and honest when we spoke and took full responsibility for what happened”\

Taking full responsibility means Jordan openly admitted to what they had done without attempting to deny it.

- *Incorrect options:*  C, D, F, G

- **C**: The incident took place during a morning exam, not an afternoon one. The text states, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$this morning's history exam.”

- **D**: The text states, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a teacher observed Jordan” - Ms. Hartley was not present in the exam room. She spoke with Jordan afterward.

- **F**: Jordan used a phone to photograph other students' answer sheets, not written materials such as notes or a textbook.

- **G**: The text states Jordan $\text{\textquotedblleft}$was cooperative and honest”, which is the opposite of uncooperative.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{a zero}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Jordan will receive a zero for this exam”\

Under the school's academic honesty policy, receiving a zero is the automatic consequence for cheating in an exam.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{next Tuesday at nine a.m./ 9:00 am}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a formal meeting next Tuesday at nine a.m.”\

The text gives both the day and the time of the meeting, and both are required for a complete answer.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$*any one of the following answers:*   

- $\textrm{a short suspension}$

- $\textrm{an honesty awareness workshop}$        [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$which may include a short suspension and an honesty awareness workshop”\

The text mentions two possible outcomes of the meeting. In addition to the poor mark, Jordan will have to face further consequences as a result of the cheating incident.
