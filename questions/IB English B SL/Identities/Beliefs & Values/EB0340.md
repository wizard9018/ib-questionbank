# EB0340

**Maximum mark: 5**

**The after-game group chat**

*Coach*: Good win today. I'm proud of the team's effort, but I'm going to say it again: we can't play politely and expect results. When the other team starts to lose focus, you press harder. That's not being cruel - that is competing. 

*Maya*: I agree with the pressure part, but some of the celebrating was embarrassing. When their striker missed that easy shot, half our bench laughed. I know it wasn't meant to be personal, but it was mean. If someone did that to us, we'd be furious. 

*Aiden*: That's bothered me, too, Coach. I want to win, and I'll work for it. But I don't want us to become the team that enjoys someone else failing. There's a difference between taking advantage of a mistake and cheering because a person made it. I didn't say anything because I didn't want to sound like I was judging everyone, but it's been stuck in my head since the game. 

*Coach*: I didn't mean to make any individual player feel bad about a mistake - mistakes are part of the game, too. But I hear what you're saying, and I will try to encourage the team in better ways from now on.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{press harder}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$When the other team starts to lose focus, you press harder.”\

The coach clearly states that the response is to increase pressure.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{laughed}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Half our bench laughed.”\

The sentence refers to the team's reaction when the player missed the shot. The text specifies laughter as the reaction.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{cheering}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$There's a difference between taking advantage of a mistake and cheering because a person made it.”\

The question contrasts using a mistake strategically with celebrating the person's failure. $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cheering” reflects the behavior Aiden criticises.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{since the game}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... it's been stuck in my head since the game.”

The sentence focuses on the fact that the concern continued after the match. 

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I will try to encourage the team in better ways from now on.”\

The coach acknowledges the concern and states that he will change how he encourages the team.

- *Incorrect options:*  A, B, D

- **A**: The coach does not defend the behavior; he says he hears the concern and will try to change.

- **B**: He does not blame anyone in his final message.

- **C**: He does not suggest reducing competitiveness, only changing the way he encourages the team.
