# EB0277

**Maximum mark: 5**

Good morning everyone,

Today I want to talk about something that affects us all: how we live and how it impacts our world. Sustainable living is not impossible; it can become a  lifestyle that fights to reduce harm to the environment while improving our health.

When I started to live more sustainably, I found it wasn't about making huge $\underline{\textrm{sacrifices}}$. I began to use $\underline{\textrm{reusable}}$ bags, cut down on plastics, and walk or take the bus instead of driving everywhere. At first, these small adjustments felt like small adjustments, but together, they had a big $\underline{\textrm{impact}}$.

Sustainable living is also about thinking about what we buy: where it comes from, how it is made, and if it'll last. I started to buy from local businesses and farmers, which not only helps the world but also contributes to my community.

This lifestyle choice has given me a sense of purpose. I feel more connected to the planet and more hopeful for the future.

I am not a perfectionist, and you don't have to be, either. But if we all make tiny, conscious steps, we can create a way of life that respects the earth and is healthy for all. Thank you for listening!

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- *Sacrifices* and *compromises* are similar in meaning; they both refer to giving up something valuable for the sake of another goal.\

\

**Option F**, *changes*, could fit Question 1. However, *changes* is a broad term and does not imply that something has been given up or sacrificed.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$   [1 mark]

- *Steps*  is a good replacement word for *adjustments* because it focuses on small actions toward a goal.

\

\

**Option A** is incorrect. *Arrangements* does not describe personal habit shifts or lifestyle decisions.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$   [1 mark]

- *Effect* is a good replacement word for *impact* as they both mean the result of an action, often suggesting a positive one.\

\

**Option C**, *repercussion* is a distractor for Question 3, however, it is usually associated with a negative result.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{more hopeful}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I feel more connected to the planet and more hopeful for the future.”\

\

The writer mentions that after their change of lifestyle, they feel more *hopeful* (optimistic) about the future.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{make tiny, conscious steps}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$But if we all make tiny, conscious steps, we can create a way of life that respects the earth and is healthy for all.”\

\

The writer explains that by taking small steps, everyone can contribute to a more sustainable way of life.
