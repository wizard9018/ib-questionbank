**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear somebody criticizing the phenomenon of  ‘kidulting’- adults behaving like a child.**

:audio{#69b025df-075c-49b1-a11e-7ea38a8ffa1b aid="69b025df-075c-49b1-a11e-7ea38a8ffa1b"}

**Choose the correct answer.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$What does the speaker seem to find most problematic about "kidults"? [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Their love of cartoon-themed merchandise
- B.$\hspace{1em}$Their reluctance to embrace adult responsibilities
- C.$\hspace{1em}$Their preference for fancy coffee

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Watch a lot of cartoons in their pajamas

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Always dress formally
- B.$\hspace{1em}$Take life seriously
- C.$\hspace{1em}$Never enjoy playful things.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$What does the speaker imply about previous generations? [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$They were more mature
- B.$\hspace{1em}$They didn't enjoy fun activities
- C.$\hspace{1em}$They never collected figurines

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$The speaker's tone can best be described as: [1 mark]

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Mildly frustrated and humorous

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- This matches the text's central criticism about avoiding responsibilities (eg $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Where's the dignity? ... Where's the responsibility?")

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **A**: is incorrect, as it is too specific and narrow.
- **C**: is a minor detail, not the main critique.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The text implies “kidults” choose childish clothing and accessories. The speaker mentions “cartoon pajamas” as well as “a Pikachu backpack.”

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- Both **Option A** and **C** are incorrect, as the text does not mention watching cartoons in pyjamas nor does it talk about professional cartoons design.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The speaker implies adults should approach life more seriously, the way they did “in their (my) time.” The speaker mentions “ In my time, we became adults at 18 and stayed that way.”

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **A**: is too narrow an interpretation.
- **C**: contradicts the speaker's overall message about maturity.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- The text suggests previous generations matured faster,  as the speaker mentions $\text{\textquotedblleft}$In my time, we became adults at 18 and stayed that way"

- *Incorrect options:* B, C

- **B**: is incorrect as the text doesn't suggest previous generations were joyless.
- **C**: is not addressed in the passage.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- The text uses humorous, exaggerated language showing mild frustration with phrases like $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Good grief" and “Kids these days, I tell you!​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​” and an overall exclamatory tone.

- *Incorrect options:* A, B

- **A**: is not correct as it suggests too much anger.
- **B**: is also wrong, as it implies sentimentality not present in the text.
