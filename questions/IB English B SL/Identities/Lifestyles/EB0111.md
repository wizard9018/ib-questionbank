# EB0111

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a person talking about “scrooging”- the
phenomenon of ending a relationship to save money on presents.**

:audio{#7ba04736-768a-493e-8757-35e8268296c2 aid="7ba04736-768a-493e-8757-35e8268296c2"}

1 - 5.$\hspace{1em}$**Choose 5 correct statements.** [5 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker suggests that modern dating is not cheap.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Scrooging" involves breaking up to save money on gifts.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$The text implies that all temporary relationships should end before gift-giving seasons.
- D.$\hspace{1em}$The speaker believes breaking up before Christmas is a form of financial self-defense.
- E.$\hspace{1em}$A Christmas present always costs hundreds of dollars.
- F.$\hspace{1em}$The text portrays breaking up as purely an economic decision.
- G.$\hspace{1em}$The author suggests that “scrooging” is a form of smart relationship management.
- H.$\hspace{1em}$The text argues that breaking up before gift-giving is emotionally cruel.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 5.$\hspace{1em}$ *Answers 1 to 5 can be written in any order.* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, B, D, F, G}$   [4 marks]

- **A**. is true, as in the first sentence it is stated directly that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$modern dating is expensive"

- **B**. is true, as the fragment  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Scrooging" is simply financial self-defense- Why invest in an elaborate gift when the relationship might be temporary?” captures the essence of $\text{\textquotedblleft}$scrooging”- breaking up to save on gifts.

- **D**. is true, as the fragment $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Breaking up strategically before gift-giving seasons is pure economic genius" highlights the economic motivation.

- **F**. is true,  as the fragment  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$split-ups are less about heartbreak and more about smart budgeting" emphasizes economic reasoning over emotional considerations.

- **G**. is true, as the fragment $\text{\textquotedblleft}$financially responsible relationship management" presents "scrooging" as a rational strategy.

- *Incorrect options*: C, E, H

- **C**. is false, as it overgeneralises the text's argument and no text evidence suggests ALL temporary relationships should end.

- **E**. is false, as the text does not specify an exact cost, it just says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a Christmas present can cost hundreds", so describes a possibility only.

- **H**. is false, as the text explicitly argues against the idea of being emotionally cruel, especially in the fragment  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$It's not cruel - it's calculated self-preservation".
