$\Large{\textrm{What Is Homeopathy?}}$

Homeopathy is a medical system based on the belief that the body can cure itself. This method, developed in the late 1700s in Germany, follows the principle $\text{\textquotedblleft}$like cures the like”. This idea suggests that a substance that triggers a certain disease in a healthy person can be used, in very small doses, to treat that disease. Unlike conventional medicine, which often focuses on directly targeting ailments, homeopathy aims to stimulate the body's self-healing mechanisms. The treatments are prepared from natural substances, including plants, minerals, and metals, diluted to ensure safety and minimize toxicity.

Homeopathy offers a holistic approach to health by treating both physical symptoms and emotional or mental conditions. One of its key advantages is that it can be used by a wide range of people, including infants, children, the elderly, and even pregnant women, without causing serious side effects.  Furthermore, many supporters appreciate how quickly it can work in some cases, offering fast relief.

Although homeopathy has been widely criticized by supporters of conventional medicine, it has gained global recognition. Many people choose it for its benign, non-invasive treatment methods and holistic approach to well-being.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$This idea suggests that a substance that triggers a certain disease in a healthy person can be used, in very small doses, to treat that disease."

- The text explains the basis of homeopathy as curing diseases by producing the same symptoms these diseases cause: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$like cures the like”

- *Incorrect options:* A, B, D

- **A**: The preposition $\text{\textquotedblleft}$unlike” suggests that it is different from conventional medicine.

- **B**: The preparation of homeopathic medicine includes not only plants but also some other elements such as metals, minerals, and others.

- **D**: Homeopathy is a holistic method; it treats not only physical but also emotional and mental illnesses.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- The text mentions several benefits of homeopathy, one of which is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$no side effects.” Theherefore the appearance of negative effects can not be considered a benefit.

- *Incorrect options:*  B, C, D

- **B**: The text mentions that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$many supporters appreciate how quickly it can work” implying it acts in a short period of time.

- **C**: The text lists the benefits of homeopathy covering all age ranges  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$infants, children, the elderly, and even pregnant women"

- **D**: The last benefit refers to the support and recognition homeopathy has gained at a global scale, which aligns with worldwide support.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ailments}$   [1 mark]

- *ailments* are *illnesses*, especially those which are not very serious. 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{stimulate}$   [1 mark]

- *Trigger* means incite, cause something to happen or to start, making this a good replacement word for *stimulate*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{side effects}$   [1 mark]

- *side effects* align with adverse reactions meaning an undesired effect caused by a drug or treatment.
