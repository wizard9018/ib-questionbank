# EB0330

**Maximum mark: 6**

**You are going to hear a teenager talking about health advice they receive from two sources.**

:audio{#7a2f5bac-6618-409b-81f4-d80903e7d64d aid="7a2f5bac-6618-409b-81f4-d80903e7d64d"}

**Complete the following gaps using no more than three words for each answer.**

The speaker feels caught between **[-1-]** because the advice they receive is different.    [1 mark]

Family members suggest simple habits, such as eating when you are **[-2-]** and sleeping enough.    [1 mark]

In contrast, online advice encourages strict control and waking up very **[-3-]**.    [1 mark]

The speaker decides to trust their **[-4-]** more than online trends.    [1 mark]

As a result, the speaker says they are already feeling **[-5-]**.    [1 mark]

**Choose the correct answer.**

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$What best describes the speaker's attitude toward online health advice now?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$They follow all online advice carefully.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$They completely stop using social media.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$They look at online tips but do not follow everything.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{two worlds}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I often feel caught between two worlds”\

The student uses this phrase to describe the conflict between family advice and social media advice.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{hungry}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$My grandmother says to eat when you're hungry.”\

This shows that the family's advice focuses on listening to natural signals rather than strict rules.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{early}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Online friends say wake up at 5 a.m. and exercise.”\

Waking up at 5 a.m. is an example of waking up very early, which reflects the pressure of online wellness culture.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{family's ideas / family}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I finally decided to trust my family's ideas and ignore most online trends.”\

The student states that they choose to follow their family's advice instead of online trends.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{healthier}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I'm already feeling healthier from listening to my family!”\

Following their family's advice has had a positive effect on their health.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$still look at online tips, of course, but I don't try to do everything”\

This shows that the student does not completely ignore online advice but is selective about what they follow.

- *Incorrect options:** A, B

- **A**: The speaker does not follow all online advice; they choose selectively.

- **B**: The speaker continues to use social media; they do not stop completely.
