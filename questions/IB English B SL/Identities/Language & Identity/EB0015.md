$\Large{\textrm{Why Language Matters: A Journey of Identity}}$

Have you ever considered how the languages we speak shape who we are? For many, language is more than just a means of communication; it's a key part of our identity, connecting us to our culture and heritage.

Growing up in a bilingual household, I often felt torn between two worlds. At home, I spoke Spanish with my family, while outside, I switched to English with my friends. It wasn't always easy, and sometimes I felt I had to choose one identity over the other. However, as I matured, I realised that navigating both languages enriched my life.

Language has a powerful ability to bring people together. When I started sharing my experiences in both Spanish and English, I noticed how others connected to my stories differently depending on the language I used. Each language carries its own unique perspective.

Now, I celebrate my bilingual identity. Switching languages fills me with pride in my heritage. Embracing both sides has helped me grow personally and connect with a wider community. So, next time you engage in conversation, remember: language is more than words; it's a bridge to understanding and connection.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$   [1 mark]

- *Incorrect options:* A, D, F

- **A**: The quote, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$navigating both languages enriched my life,” suggests that, while bilingualism may involve navigating two cultures, it is ultimately presented as a positive and enriching experience rather than one that creates a sense of division.

- **D**: The quote, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$language has a powerful ability to bring people together,” directly contradicts the idea of division. Instead, the text emphasises bilingualism's unifying effect, promoting connection rather than complication or separation.

- **F**: The quote, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$language is more than words; it's a bridge to understanding and connection,” clearly opposes the notion of language being $\text{\textquotedblleft}$merely a tool for communication.” The text stresses language's deeper role in shaping identity and building cultural connections.
