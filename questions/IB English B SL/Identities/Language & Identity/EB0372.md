# EB0372

**Maximum mark: 7**

**Lost in Translation...**

If you’ve been following my posts, you’ll know I’m always looking for ways to relax after school. A few weeks ago, I thought I’d found the perfect solution: joining the local knitting club. Peaceful. No Stress. Right?'

Wrong.

Within five minutes, everyone around me was “frogging their WIPs” and debating how to show off a FO. I smiled and nodded for two hours, trying to keep my yarn untangled while listening to the conversation and in the end - I understood absolutely nothing. I went home feeling more stressed than before!

So I did what any self-respecting teenager would do - I went online and looked up ‘knitting jargon’. Turns out “FO” means “finished object” and “frogging” means unravelling your work to start again? And WIP? Work in progress, of course!

Last week I went back, armed with needles and new words. And you know what? It felt completely different. Suddenly, I was part of the conversation - when my new friends gave me help, I could understand AND do cool new things! I love knitting!

Have you ever cracked the code of a new hobby? Tell me below!

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ways to relax}$    [1 mark]

- “I'm always looking for ways to relax after school”\

The writer was searching for activities that would help her unwind after school.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{a FO}$    [1 mark]

- “debating how to show off a FO”\

The writer heard club members discussing a term she did not yet understand - a FO, which she later discovered means a finished object.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{more stressed than before}$    [1 mark]

- “I went home feeling more stressed than before”\

The knitting club had the opposite effect to what the writer had hoped - rather than relaxing her, the experience increased her stress levels.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{knitting jargon}$    [1 mark]

- “I went online and looked up $`$knitting jargon'”\

*Jargon* is the specialised vocabulary used within a particular community. The writer needed to research this independently as no one at the club had explained the terms to her.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{frogging}$    [1 mark]

- “frogging means unravelling your work to start again”\

This term is specific to the knitting community.  It describes undoing stitches to correct a mistake, something the writer had never seen or heard of before joining the club.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{help}$    [1 mark]

- “when my new friends gave me help”\

On her return visit, fellow club members supported the writer. This help, combined with her new understanding of the vocabulary, allowed her to participate fully for the first time.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{do cool new things}$    [1 mark]

- “I could understand AND do cool new things”\

Understanding the jargon transformed the writer's experience. She went from confused outsider to engaged participant, able to both follow and take part in club activities.
