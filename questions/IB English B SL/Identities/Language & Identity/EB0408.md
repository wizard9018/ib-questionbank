# EB0408

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a student talking about a recent holiday.**

:audio{#a938ba9c-51e1-4576-8c8c-74687778556e aid="a938ba9c-51e1-4576-8c8c-74687778556e"}

**Choose the four true statements.** [4 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Pencarrow Cove is a small coastal village. 

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The student visited Pencarrow Cove the previous month. 

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Families in the village share one of four surnames.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The Pendrys arrived in the village in the 1900s. 

- E.$\hspace{1em}$The Trevean family owns the boatyard. 

- F.$\hspace{1em}$The boatyard has been owned by the same family for around two hundred years.

- G.$\hspace{1em}$The student's grandmother greeted someone in the bakery.

- H.$\hspace{1em}$The Hockings are bakers. 

**Answer the following question.**

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$How long have the Smyths lived in Pencarrow Cove?    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, E, G, H}$        [4 marks]

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a tiny fishing village on the coast of Cornwall" \

Pencarrow Cove is described as tiny, which means it is a *small* village, and it is on the coast, making it a *coastal* village.

- **E**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the boatyard has belonged to the Trevean family for around four hundred years" \

The text confirms that the boatyard is owned by and has belonged to the Trevean family for a very long time.

- **G**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$my grandmother waved at the woman in the bakery, Mrs. Hocking" \

The speaker's grandmother recognised Mrs. Hocking in the bakery and waved at her.  To greet in this context means to acknowledge.

- **H**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Her family... has been baking the village bread ever since" \

The Hocking family has been baking bread for the village for a long time. They are *bakers*.

- *Incorrect options:*  B, C, D, F

- **B**: The speaker says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$last week," not the previous month. 

- **C**: The text says three family names, not four.

- **D**: The text says the Pendrys came $\text{\textquotedblleft}$in the 1800s," not the 1900s. 

- **F**: The text says the Treveans have owned the boatyard for around four hundred years, not two hundred.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{fifty years}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$we Smyths have only been here fifty years" \

The grandfather confirms that the Smyth family has lived in Pencarrow Cove for fifty years, a very short time compared to the other village families.
