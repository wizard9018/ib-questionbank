# EB0350

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear an advertisement for student-made products.**

:audio{#7f7dfe67-b7ba-448d-976d-76413dd884e5 aid="7f7dfe67-b7ba-448d-976d-76413dd884e5"}

**Choose the four true statements.** [4 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The pop-up event will take place in the school gym.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Students will be selling brand-new clothing.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The group turns used clothes into new items.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$You and your friends can wear matching clothes.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$Nothing costs more than twenty dollars.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Students can pay only in cash.

- G.$\hspace{1em}$The money is used to help a local shelter.

- H.$\hspace{1em}$Students are accepting donations of unwanted clothes.

**Choose the correct option.**

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$What are students encouraged to do at the end of the ad?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Come shop and donate unwanted clothes.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Buy only second-hand jeans and jewelry.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Join the group only if they have experience.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, G, H}$        [4 marks]

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$come to the first ever Thrift-Flip Pop-Up, this Friday after school in the gym foyer.” \

The advertisement clearly states the event location.

- **C**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$students take second-hand clothes and redesign them into something totally new.” \

This explains that the group *redesigns* (*turns*) *second-hand* or *used* clothing into new items.

- **G**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$All profits go to a local shelter” \

The text mentions that *profits*, or the *money* that is made, will be given to a local shelter.

- **H**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$we'll also be collecting clean clothes to donate.” \

The advertisement explains that donations of clothes will be accepted.

- *Incorrect options:*  B, D, E, F

- **B**: The speaker describes the students as using $\text{\textquotedblleft}$second-hand clothes,” to create new items. They are not selling new clothes.

- **D**: The speaker says that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$every item is one-of-a-kind” and that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$no one at school will have the same look as you”, which is the opposite of this statement.

- **E**: The ad says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nothing costs more than fifteen,” not twenty.

- **F**: The ad says students can pay $\text{\textquotedblleft}$in cash or by card,” so cash is not the only option.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$come shop, clear out your closet, and support creative students”\

This line encourages students to shop and also donate clothes they no longer want.

- *Incorrect options:* B, C

- **B**: The ad gives examples of products (jeans into bags, jewellery into pins), but it does not say students should buy only those items.

- **C**: The ad says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$You don't need experience,” which is the opposite of this option.
