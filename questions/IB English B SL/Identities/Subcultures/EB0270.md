**Maximum mark: 5**

**Rising Tide of Hustle Culture Shapes a Generation**

In cities across the globe, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hustle culture" is rapidly growing among young professionals and students. Glorifying long work hours, hustle culture encourages relentless work and less downtime, as the key to personal and financial success.

Social media platforms such as TikTok and Instagram play a major role, with influencers documenting 16-hour days and constant side projects. Some celebrate the movement for promoting ambition and innovation. However,  mental health experts warn of its darker side, chronic stress, and a work-life balance that is often sacrificed in the name of achievement.

Organizations report many workers struggle to disconnect from work, with after-hours emails and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$productivity hacks” becoming the norm. Yet, for many young people, especially Gen Z and Millennials, the pressure to $\text{\textquotedblleft}$always be busy” defines $\underline{\textrm{their}}$ sense of identity and success.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hustle culture encourages relentless work and less downtime"\

The text mentions that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hustle culture” *encourages* or *promotes* relentless (non-stop) work over *time to rest* or *downtime*.

- *Incorrect options:*  A, c, D

- **A**: Less rest is mentioned as part of $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hustle culture” but this option is less precise than B, as it does not mention long work hours.

- **C**: The text does not mention team spirit.

- **D**: *Supporting*, in this case, means helping. $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hustle culture” does not help work-life balance; it harms it.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... with influencers documenting 16-hour days and constant side projects.”\

Influencers record and *show* (*document*) their extremely long days on social media platforms.

- *Incorrect options:*  B, C, D

- **B**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Side projects” are mentioned, but they are meant to be shown/documented, not acquired.

- **C**: The text mentions that TIkTok and Instagram play a major role, not the other way around as the sentence suggests.

- **D**: Influencers document their work-day, but the word *promote* is not used. 

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$mental health experts warn of its darker side: chronic stress..."

The text mentions a series of unwanted consequences of $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hustle culture” one of which is *chronic (long-term)* stress.

- *Incorrect options:*  A, C, D

- **A**: This is partially true but too extreme. The text does not say mental health is completely destroyed.

- **C**: The warning is about the negative impacts of overwork, not an obligation to work more.

- **D**: The text does not say $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hustle culture” discourages workers from taking breaks.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Organizations report many workers struggle to disconnect from work"\

The text mentions employees $\text{\textquotedblleft}$struggle to disconnect from work", which means they $\text{\textquotedblleft}$keep working after hours". 

- *Incorrect options:*  A, B, C

- **A**: The opposite is true; employees *struggle*, not find it easy, to disconnect.

- **B**: The text does not mention employee preferences but highlights difficulties in disconnecting.

- **C**: The text implies increased work and stress, not fewer hours.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{many young people}$   [1 mark]

- Also accept: young people, Gen Z and Millenials.\

\

$\text{\textquotedblleft}$Yet, for many young people, especially Gen Z and Millennials, the pressure to $\text{\textquotedblleft}$always be busy” defines $\underline{\textrm{their}}$ sense of identity and success."\

The word $\text{\textquotedblleft}$their” refers to the young people or Gen Z and Millenials mentioned previously in the sentence.
