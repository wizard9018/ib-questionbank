## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(fresh) seafood}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$there was an impressive variety, from fresh seafood to international dishes”\

The original text mentions seafood before international dishes.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{the chef}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the chef came out to greet all the customers”\

The speaker mentions they were able to *meet* and *greet* the chef in an intimate setting. Although *meet* and *greet* are not synonyms, in this context, they convey the experience of interacting with the chef.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{under the stars}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The highlight for me was the outdoor movie under the stars”\

The original sentence mentions a *movie* while the exercise refers to it as *cinema*. Both are similar in context.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{overcrowded}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$some activities were overcrowded”\

*Activities* is paraphrased with $\text{\textquotedblleft}$shows and attractions”

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{spotless}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$my cabin was always spotless”\

The text refers to the room as a *cabin*, which is a room in a cruise ship.
