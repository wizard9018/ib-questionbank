# EB0020

**Maximum mark: 7**

As you embark on your journeys around the globe, always keep in mind to tread respectfully on the Earth. Embracing sustainable initiatives is key to preserving the beauty and richness of our planet, along with safeguarding our legacy. To ensure enjoyment without causing unnecessary strain on visiting destinations and to make your travels have a positive impact on both the environment and the communities you visit, it is vital to be mindful of your actions.

Here are a few guidelines for travelling responsibly:  

**[-1-]**\

Seek out eco-friendly lodgings that minimise their environmental impact.

**[-2-]**\

Use sustainable means of transportation and be mindful of water usage. Besides, do not carry plastic use-and-throw items such as disposables. Instead, pack some reusables, like water bottles and shopping bags, so as to cut down the generation of waste.

**[-3-]**\

Purchase at local businesses, contributing to the economy of the local community.

**[-4-]**\

Observe the rules in national parks and wildlife reserves. Do not litter, and refrain from disturbing animals.

**[-5-]**\

Dress modestly, learn a few native words to show understanding and appreciation, and be sensitive to cultural norms and traditions. Avoid behaving intrusively and disrespectfully.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$   [1 mark]

- In the paragraph, *eco-friendly* means *sustainable* and *lodgings* is a synonym of *accomodations*. Looking for these types of places to stay minimises the impact of tourists on the environment.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$   [1 mark]

- The paragraph gives a few specific tricks (*hacks*) to travel responsibly. 

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- This is the only subheading that refers to the economy of local communities. 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The paragraph points out some ways in which travellers should behave and act to protect the natural habitat of the local wildlife. This part specifically deals with the natural spaces of the wildlife. Not bothering (*distubing*) animals is only one of the suggestions.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{G}$   [1 mark]

- According to this paragraph, travellers can show appreciation and respect for the local culture if they make an effort to communicate with locals in their native language and if they don't offend them by wearing inappropriate clothes, breaking their cultural norms, or mocking their traditions.

- *Incorrect options:* C, F, H, I

- **C**: The paragraph about showing appreciation and respect for local cultures offers more than one way in which this can be done. The idea of learning a bit about the language is only one suggestion to show appreciation for locals.

- **F**: The paragraph about *not disturbing animals* in their natural habitat does not say that the simple and only act of watching wildlife contributes to the preservation of natural environments. The text also suggests aspects related to respecting rules and not throwing rubbish to respect their natural habits.

- **H**: The paragraph about ways to reduce the impact on the environment when travelling makes suggestions about packing,   but not with the purpose of suggesting that travellers should carry less weight. It only mentions packing resusable items to contribute to preserving the environment. 

- **I**: The subheading “Choose lodgings according to your budget" means to stay at places you can afford according to the money you can spend on the trip. This idea of the traveller's budget does not connect directly with the concept of choosing a lodging to protect the environment. 

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{unnecessary strain}$   [1 mark]

- *Unjustified* is a synonym of *unnecessary* and *impact* is a good replacement for *strain* since this last word in the context refers to a strong and negative change that travellers may cause on a destination when entertaining themselves. 

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Mindful}$   [1 mark]

- *Conscious* is a synonym of *mindful*. Both mean *to be aware of something*.
