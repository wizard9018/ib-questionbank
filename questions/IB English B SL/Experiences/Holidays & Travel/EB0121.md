# EB0121

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a reporter explain the attraction of regional cuisines for food-loving travelers.**

:audio{#db2f3256-19c0-4a00-aa10-ef316d7e62d3 aid="db2f3256-19c0-4a00-aa10-ef316d7e62d3"}

1 - 4.$\hspace{1em}$**Choose the four correct statements.** [4 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Regional cuisine reflects the history and culture of the people in a location.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$Regional cuisine can be easily duplicated in other parts of the world.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$Many travelers revisit places specifically to enjoy the local food.
- D.$\hspace{1em}$Regional cuisine is influenced by the environment and local beliefs.
- E.$\hspace{1em}$Regional cuisine is unrelated to the cultural history of an area.
- F.$\hspace{1em}$Travelers do not consider regional food when planning a holiday to a new location.
- G.$\hspace{1em}$Regional cuisine is a blend of local traditions, produce, and residents' appreciation of history.
- H.$\hspace{1em}$The environment does not affect the flavors of regional cuisine.

**Choose the correct answer.**

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$What is one way food connects travelers to locals? [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Through the shared appreciation of the area’s natural resources and traditions
- B.$\hspace{1em}$By introducing travelers to foods that they can easily recreate at home
- C.$\hspace{1em}$By teaching travelers how to prepare traditional dishes

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 4.$\hspace{1em}$ *Answers can be written in any order.* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, D, G}$   [4 marks]

- **A**. “Regional cuisine – food that is based on the long history of the people who have lived in a specific location for generations – combines history, culture, and, of course, food…”

- The text mentions local cuisine is a reflection of the history and culture of the place.

- **C**. The text states that some travelers “…visit new places – or revisit old favorites – to savor the food to be found only there.”  To savor means to taste and eat food and drink with great enjoyment or pleasure.

- **D**. “The climate, local traditions and beliefs… unite to form the flavors of a region…” The unique aspects of a location, including environmental and cultural, contribute to the creation of a regional cuisine.

- **G**. Regional cuisine is made up of: “…food that is based on the long history of the people who have lived in a specific location for generations…”, “local traditions”, and foods that “thrive in the area.”

- *Incorrect options*: B, E, F, H

- **B**.  The text states: “…cannot be duplicated anywhere else in the world.” *Replicate* is a synonym of *duplicate*.

- **E**. Regional cuisine is dependent upon “…history, culture…” to shape its unique flavors.

- **F**. Many travelers choose a destination specifically to eat the local food. “Many travelers will tell you that the reason they visit new places – or revisit old favorites – is to savor the food to be found only there.”

- **H**. “The environment, local traditions and beliefs,... unite to form the flavors of a region.”

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$   [1 mark]

- “...long history of the people who have lived in a specific location for generations.” The use of words like “culture, history” and “traditions and beliefs” indicate that the food created in the area has important meaning for the local residents who make it.

- *Incorrect options:* B, C

- **B**: Regional food cannot be easily made at home: “Regional cuisine – food that is based on the long history of the people who have lived in a specific location for generations – combines history, culture, and, of course, food, in a way that cannot be duplicated anywhere else in the world.”
- **C**: Although taking cooking lessons from local chefs is a very popular activity for tourists, that is not mentioned in this text.
