# EB0433

**Maximum mark: 6**

**Walk a Mile in My Shoes**

**[-1-]**

Selin and I had been talking about doing a long-distance hike for years. Last spring, we finally committed to joining a group in Italy: eight strangers, 12 days, 300 kilometers. I signed up without hesitation. I train regularly, I eat well, and at 54, I still think of myself as someone who can do these kinds of things.

**[-2-]**

By day three, my left knee had opinions I hadn't asked for. By day five, I was taking the low road while the others scampered up hills. On day seven, it rained heavily and I stood at a fork in the trail watching the group move away, their voices echoing down the valley. Selin was with them, her red jacket fading into the mist. I turned around and started limping down to the next village.

**[-3-]**

Selin completed every kilometer. I completed most of them. I'm not sure what I expected to feel at the end of the hike… but it wasn't this. It isn't failure exactly. But clearly, I am no longer who I thought I was.  That's not a bad thing, I guess it is just a new road to walk down.

**Choose an appropriate question from the list that completes each gap in the text.** [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Was it worth it?

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Now what?

- C.$\hspace{1em}$What could go wrong?

- D.$\hspace{1em}$What was I thinking?

- E.$\hspace{1em}$Where was Selin?

- F.$\hspace{1em}$How hard can it be?

**Find a word in the text which means the following.** [3 marks]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$agreed    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$dashed    [1 mark]

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$disappearing    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I signed up without hesitation. I train regularly, I eat well, and at 54, I still think of myself as someone who can do these kinds of things."\

This section opens with confidence and enthusiasm: The writer has no doubts at this point, making $\text{\textquotedblleft}$What could go wrong?" the perfect humorous header for a tale that does not, actually, go very well.\

\

**Option F** is not correct. $\text{\textquotedblleft}$How hard can it be?" expresses a similar overconfidence, but it focuses specifically on physical difficulty. The paragraph is not only about the challenge ahead; it is about the writer's overall self-image. 

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$By day three, my left knee had opinions I hadn't asked for..."\

This section describes the physical and emotional reality of the hike, which was a sharp contrast with the confidence of the opening. $\text{\textquotedblleft}$What was I thinking?" reflects the writer's self-doubt as problems begin to arise.\

\

**Option E** is the distractor. Selin is mentioned in this section, but she is a detail within a larger picture of struggle and isolation. This section is about the writer's experience, not Selin's.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I'm not sure what I expected to feel at the end of the hike... but it wasn't this."\

This section is reflective, and there is no clear ending. The writer has discovered that they are not quite the person they used to be and must now transition into a new, unknown phase of life, making $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Now what?" the only heading that captures that open, uncertain mood.\

\

**Option A** does not fit well here. $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Was it worth it?" implies a conclusion or an ending has been reached. The writer managed to finish most of the hike, but this section deals with their internal struggles, not the hike itself.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{committed}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Last spring we finally committed to joining a group in Italy"\

Committed means to firmly agree or dedicate yourself to something. 

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{scampered}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I was taking the low road while the others scampered up hills"\

Scampered describes moving quickly and lightly. Dashed is a synonym that also captures the ease and speed with which the other hikers climbed.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{fading}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$her red jacket fading into the mist"\

Fading describes something gradually disappearing from view. Here, it captures the moment Selin and the group move beyond the writer's reach.
