# EB0027

**Maximum mark: 7**

$\Large{\textrm{La Quinceañera: A Rite of Passage among Latina Adolescents}}$

$\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hurry, *mamá*!” Laura begged her mom. She was hurriedly trying to unlace the glittery, bedazzled gown to put on her Jalisco dress to dance to $\text{\textquotedblleft}$El Son de la Negra” with her *corte de chambelanes y damas*, her court of young men and women who would accompany her. Amy, Laura's mom, clumsily helped Laura unlace the bodice of the dress, then helped Laura step into her Jalisco dress, careful not to get any of Laura's curls stuck in the zipper. The scene felt like a fiasco because the guests were waiting to see the *baile sorpresa* (surprise dance). It was Laura's quinceañera, the elaborate party and celebration of Laura turning 15 a few months ago, a ritual that many other Latina girls her age had gone through or were already planning for their birthdays. Laura had practiced the dance for months with her court members, and it was almost the moment to debut the choreographed and stylized dance for the party guests, a mixture of Laura's extended family members, friends, Amy's coworkers, and even guests that had flown to Chicago for the big event.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{unlace}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$unlace the glittery, bedazzled gown”

\

*To unlace* a dress, means to untie a dress. Laura asked her mom for help as she was in a hurry and needed to change her dress for the dance.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{court}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$her court of young men and women”

\

*Court* refers to the young men and women who would accompany Laura in her dance.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{curls}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$not to get any of Laura's curls stuck in the zipper”\

  A *curl* is a lock of hair in the form of a spiral. Laura's mother, who was in a hurry, made sure to prevent Laura's hair from being caught by the zipper. 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{turning}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$celebration of Laura turning 15”\

To *turn* a particular age means reaching that age, so *turn 15* means reaching the age of 15.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{extended family members}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a mixture of Laura's extended family members”\

 According to the text, the guests at the party were not only Laura's friends but also her distant relatives.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{False}$   [1 mark]

- Justification: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$guests that had flown to Chicago for the big event.”\

The party was held in Chicago, USA. The text mentions some guests had to fly to get there.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{False}$   [1 mark]

- Justification: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Laura's quinceañera, the elaborate party and celebration”\

The word *elaborate* indicates the party was carefully planned and had many details.
