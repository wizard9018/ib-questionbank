# EB0152

**Maximum mark: 6**

**You are going to hear a person’s experience of living alone for the first time.**

:audio{#128e8b89-9bb4-4193-aee2-78cca6469522 aid="128e8b89-9bb4-4193-aee2-78cca6469522"}

1 - 3.$\hspace{1em}$**Choose the three true statements.** [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Living alone is easy from the start.
- B.$\hspace{1em}$Paying rent and doing chores are part of living alone.
- C.$\hspace{1em}$Independence comes with no responsibilities.
- D.$\hspace{1em}$Most young adults never feel lonely after moving out.
- E.$\hspace{1em}$Over time, people learn to manage independence.
- F.$\hspace{1em}$Moving out of the family home is a stepping stone in becoming an adult.

**Complete the following sentences using no more than three words from the text.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$What are some responsibilities of living independently? Give **two** examples. [2 marks]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$What is one of the emotional challenges of living alone?    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 3.$\hspace{1em}$ *Answers can be written in any order.* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, E, F}$   [3 marks]

- **B**. The speaker tells us: “I soon learned that being independent also came with responsibilities: paying rent, doing laundry, and fixing problems”

- **E**. “...eventually, I learned to manage these challenges and my independence grew” The speaker recognized the benefits they received from being able to handle the responsibility of living alone, and felt that they were prepared for life as an adult.

- **F**. “Moving out of my family’s home was a major step into adulthood.” The speaker recognizes the importance of this move and the role it played in his development.  A *stepping stone* is a metaphorical expression that means an action that helps to make progress towards a goal. In this context, it is similar in meaning to a *major step*.

- *Incorrect options*: A, C, D

- **A**.  The speaker tells us that “...eventually, I learned to manage these challenges…”, showing that living alone was not easy from the start, but it did get better with time.

- **C**. The speaker tells us the opposite: “I soon learned that being independent also came with responsibilities.”

- **D**. We learn that the speaker did feel lonely: “I felt a bit lonely…” but we do not have any information on how other young people feel about their experience of living alone.

$4$.$\hspace{1em}$*Any two, in any order.*

-   $\textrm{paying rent}$
-   $\textrm{doing laundry}$
-   $\textrm{fixing problems}$

- “being independent also came with responsibilities: paying rent, doing laundry, and fixing problems (like a leaky sink).  The speaker learned that it wasn’t always fun being on their own, and that chores needed to be done.  [2 marks]

$5$.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{feeling lonely}$

- “I also discovered that I felt a bit lonely…” The text tells us about the challenges the speaker felt: paying the rent (economic challenge), doing laundry (physical challenge), fixing problems (problem-solving challenge), and feeling lonely (emotional challenge). [1 mark]
