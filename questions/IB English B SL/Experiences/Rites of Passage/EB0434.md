# EB0434

**Maximum mark: 6**

*Thursday*

Danny's getting married. The invitation arrived this morning. Spring wedding, plus one. I put it on the counter and went to check on the dogs.

There are eleven greyhounds on the farm now. They come from the track when their racing days are over, when someone decides they're too slow, too old. Sometimes they arrive injured. Most have never lived outside a cage. There's one I've named Ghost. It arrived so withdrawn that I wasn't sure he'd find his way back. He's fine now. More than fine.

My life doesn't look like much from the outside, I know. No wife, no children, no particular achievement or success to show. But I love this farm and the work I do on $\underline{\textrm{it}}$, and being able to give hope and comfort to these throw-away dogs gives me purpose.

I go back to the kitchen and respond to the invitation: just one person attending. Danny will be happy in his marriage, I hope so, anyway. I have 11 dogs who need feeding and $\underline{\textrm{who}}$ need me.  And $\underline{\textrm{that's}}$ enough for a Thursday.

**The following statements are either true or false. Write True or False, then justify it using words as they appear in the text. Both parts are required for [1 mark].** [4]

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$An invitation to an event arrived recently.    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$The dogs on the farm were previously used for sport.    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Dogs always arrive at the farm ready to be rehomed.    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Ghost found it easy to settle into his new life.    [1 mark]

**What do the following words refer to in the text?** [2]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... the work I do on $\underline{\textrm{it}}$...”    [1 mark]

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... $\underline{\textrm{who}}$ need feeding...”    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: The invitation arrived this morning\

The writer received the wedding invitation on the same day they were writing, confirming it arrived very recently.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: They come from the track when their racing days are over\

The greyhounds on the farm are all former racing dogs who have been retired from competition.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{False}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: Sometimes they arrive injured\

Not all dogs arrive healthy: the text makes clear that some animals end their racing lives due to injuries.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{False}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: arrived so withdrawn I wasn't sure he'd find his way back\

Ghost's adjustment was slow and uncertain, the opposite of easy. Withdrawn means unable or unwilling to communicate or interact with others; it took the writer a long time to earn Ghost's trust so that he could live like a normal dog.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{the farm}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$It" refers directly to the farm, which the writer mentions in the same sentence.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{the eleven dogs}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Who" refers to the eleven greyhounds the writer cares for.
