# EB0158

**Maximum mark: 5**

**You are going to hear a short text on greeting guests.**

:audio{#5a120cfe-47ff-4b3b-b700-60ff926aa0bb aid="5a120cfe-47ff-4b3b-b700-60ff926aa0bb"}

**Choose the three true statements.**[3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$In all cultures, greetings must include physical contact.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The level of eye contact in a greeting is influenced by societal norms.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Bowing is a universal way of greeting guests.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The formality of a greeting depends on the social rank of the person being welcomed.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$Stylized greeting gestures include bowing and handshakes.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$All cultures use the same type of hand gestures when greeting.

**Complete the following gaps using no more than three words for each answer.**

Greetings are intended to convey complex emotions such as **[-4-]**.    [1 mark]

A host's greeting should communicate **[-5-]**.    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1 - 3.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, D, E}$        [3 marks]

- **B**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$degree of eye contact is [are] dependent upon cultural expectation”\

*Societal norms* are the rules or expectations that define behavior in a group or society. They are a synonym for *cultural expectations*.

- **D**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Verbal greetings may be short and spontaneous, or formal and composed depending on the situation and the status of the person being welcomed.”\

*Status* is the social position of a person. Status may be based on their profession, family connection, wealth, etc. *Social rank* is a synonym for *status*. The level of formality in greetings will rise with higher levels of status of arriving guests.

- **E**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Many gestures are highly stylized, including bowing, kneeling, handshakes, ... kisses”\

The text provides a long list of gestures that may be included in greeting a guest but may vary (be different), depending on the societal norms of the culture.

- *Incorrect options:*   A, C, F

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$...dependent upon cultural expectation, as is the amount of physical contact.” \

Not all cultures include touching a guest as part of greeting them. Bowing and kneeling - two examples given in the text - do not include physical contact.

- **C**: Bowing is listed as one of the gestures which may be included in greeting a guest, but the text does not say it is universal (meaning that it is used everywhere and by all cultures).

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$...may include...handshakes, hand gestures...”

There are many types of hand gestures, with handshakes given as one specific type in the text, but the text also states $\text{\textquotedblleft}$may include", indicating that not all gestures are necessarily used by all cultures.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{warmth, joy and respect}$   [1 mark]

- *Convey* and *express* are synonyms which mean to show or demonstrate.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{genuine happiness}$   [1 mark]

- *Convey* and *communicate* are also synonyms.
