## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{predominant}$   [1 mark]

- *Predominant* means being the most noticeable or the largest in number.  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Most common" is a synonym.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{consumption}$   [1 mark]

- *To consume* means to eat or use up. *Consumption* in this text refers to the act of eating, and *intake* (take-in) is a close synonym which is the amount of something (food, air) taken into the body.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{embraced}$   [1 mark]

- *Embrace* means to accept or welcome, and *adopt* is a very close synonym, indicating a practice or habit that is used by many people within a culture.  

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{fosters}$   [1 mark]

- *To foster* means to encourage the growth or development of something. (for example: one could foster a child, or a good habit) To *cultivate* also means to try to improve or develop something (cultivate tolerance, for example).

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$   [1 mark]

- Personal preferences are not mentioned within the text as a reason for eating in a particular way.  The text states that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$historical, environmental, and cultural factors” are the main reasons we eat the way we do.

- *Incorrect options:* A, B

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$environmental factors” are given as a major influence in how we eat, and geographical location will greatly influence the environment in which a culture develops. Therefore, **A** is incorrect.

- **B**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$cultural factors” are stated as one of the most important elements in shaping how we eat, and societal influences is a synonym for that phrase. Therefore, **B** is incorrect.
