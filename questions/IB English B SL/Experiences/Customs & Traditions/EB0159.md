# EB0159

**Maximum mark: 6**

**You are going to hear a gallery employee discussing the modernization of traditional crafts.**

:audio{#4b530e7d-a65b-494d-bcaf-4be9c9d75041 aid="4b530e7d-a65b-494d-bcaf-4be9c9d75041"}

**Answer the following questions.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$The people producing traditional crafts are referred to in three different ways in the text.  Give **two** examples of the words used.    [2 marks]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$What innovation has ensured the continued success of traditional crafts in modern society?    [1 mark]

**Complete the following gaps using no more than three words for each answer.**

Artisans preserve the **[-3-]** of crafts.    [1 mark]

The blending of ancient craft with modern taste is referred to as **[-4-]**  in the text.    [1 mark]

The continued **[-5-]** of modern crafts has been enabled by online platforms.$    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$*any two of the following answers, in any order:* 

- $\textrm{artisans}$

- $\textrm{craftsmen}$

- $\textrm{makers}$        [3 marks]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$...artisans are blending time-honored techniques...”

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$...craftsmen adapting their work...”

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$...connect makers to a global audience...”\

\

The people engaged in producing crafts may be referred to in several ways, with three examples given in the text.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{digital platforms}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Digital platforms connect makers to a global audience, showcasing their work and ensuring the continued relevance and sustainability of these treasured creations.” \

\

By connecting to a larger (*global*) audience online, craftsmen demonstrate (*showcase*) their work and expand their customer base. This allows them to continue to produce work that people want and love, while maintaining their livelihood (having work and earning money). *Innovation* (in the question) is a noun referring to a new idea or product.  In this text, digital platforms are the innovation.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{history and symbolism}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$while preserving the history and symbolism of the original products.”\

\

Craftsmen are blending the meaning and tradition of products with the interests of a modern customer.  For example: patterns and colors which are symbolic of a culture could be used in a modern item, such as a mouse pad.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{fusion}$   [1 mark]

- *Fusion* is the process of joining two things together to form something new.  In this text, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$time-honored techniques” (the way things are made) are blended with $\text{\textquotedblleft}$modern design and expression” to create a new form of craft.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{relevance (and sustainability)}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Digital platforms connect makers to a global audience, showcasing their work and ensuring the continued relevance and sustainability of these treasured creations.”\

\

*Relevance* describes how meaningful or useful something is to what is happening at the present time.  Traditional crafts may not have as much relevance to a modern audience as they did to an audience 100 years ago, because society has changed.\

*Sustainability* is the ability to be kept (maintained) at a certain level or rate.\

With online marketing and sales, craftsmen can connect with customers and keep producing modern crafts.
