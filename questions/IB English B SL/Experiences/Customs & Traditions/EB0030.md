## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$   [1 mark]

- “If you love good food and good vibes”\

The original paragraph describes the food and atmosphere very positively, which is why the summary uses a strong adjective: *incredible* to keep the same enthusiastic tone.

- **Option A** is incorrect. *Interesting* suggests mild curiosity, while the original text shows strong, positive language.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$   [1 mark]

- “celebrates the vibrant flavors and culinary heritage of Jamaica.”\

The original paragraph highlights Jamaica’s heritage in food and culture, referring to customs and practices passed down over time, which aligns with the meaning of *traditional*.

- **Option B** is incorrect. *Well-known* only means familiar to many people and does not express cultural depth or importance.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$   [1 mark]

- “the diverse culinary traditions that make Jamaica’s food scene so unique.”\

The original paragraph clearly states that Jamaican cuisine includes many different traditions, which matches the meaning of *diverse* in the paraphrase.

- **Option D** is incorrect. *Special* is vague and does not clearly communicate the idea of diverse culinary influences.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Kingston Kitchen}$   [1 mark]

- *Also accept*: the festival

\

*It* refers back to Kingston Kitchen, the place introduced at the start of the paragraph.\

"The atmosphere" is confusing because it is the noun closest to the pronoun "it." However, in this paragraph, the writer is describing Kingston Kitchen as a whole, not just one feature of it. The atmosphere is one quality of the place.

- *Their* refers to the people listed immediately before in the sentence: the local chefs, street food truck owners, and young food enthusiasts who serve the dishes.
