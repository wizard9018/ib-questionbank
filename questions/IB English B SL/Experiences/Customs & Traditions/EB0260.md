# EB0260

**Maximum mark: 6**

Days before October 31st, children start getting anxious about an event that takes place every year on that precise date. Halloween was originally a Celtic celebration that was brought to America by the first European settlers. 

The Celtics used to believe that on October 31st, creepy ghosts and dead people came back to the living world; therefore, they dressed up like devils and put candles in front of their houses. This tradition was merged with the Christian celebration of All Saints, which is held on November 1st.

As time went by, people took up this custom and kept on dressing like evil creatures for fun. Nowadays, children do not just wear scary costumes; they go to fancy dress shops and choose among different characters, such as fairies, heroes, princesses, or even robots.

Families set up haunted house decorations like mysterious jack-o-lanterns, garlands, skeletons, enchanted cauldrons, and creepy tombstones. Once houses are decorated and children are dressed up in their costumes, the celebration starts. Everyone goes trick-or-treating and gets a ton of candy to celebrate Halloween.

**Find the word or phrase in the text which means the following:** 

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$frightening    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$combined    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$adopted    [1 mark]

**The following statements are either true or false. Write True or False, then justify it using words as they appear in the text. Both parts are required for [1 mark].**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Kids wear spooky costumes during Halloween.    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Houses are decorated with flags and garlands.    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$It is a custom to eat sweets on Halloween.   [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{creepy}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$creepy ghosts and dead people came back to the living world”\

The word *creepy* is a synonym of the word *frightening*, it means that the ghosts are scary.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{merged}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$This tradition was merged with the Christian celebration of All Saints”\

The word *merge* is a synonym of the word *combine*. This means that two or more things are joined together. In this context, the two things would be the Celtic tradition and Christian influences.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{took up}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$people took up this custom”\

The phrasal verb *take up* means to start doing a new hobby or habit. In this context people *adopted* or *started* celebrating this event. 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{False}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: children do not just wear scary costumes\

The text mentions that children not only wear *spooky* or *scary* costumes but also dress up as $\text{\textquotedblleft}$fairies, heroes, princesses, or even robots.” which are not scary.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{False}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: haunted house decorations\

The text offers a list of decorations used in houses: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$mysterious jack-o-lanterns, garlands, skeletons, enchanted cauldrons, and creepy tombstones.” Flags are not included in this list, and they are not scary.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{True}$    [1 mark]

- Justification: gets a ton of candy to celebrate Halloween.\

The text mentions that everyone gets candy to celebrate Halloween.
