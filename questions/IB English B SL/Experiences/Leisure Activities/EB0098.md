## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{10,000 years}$   [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Archery is one of the oldest sports in the world, dating back over *10,000 years*.”

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Bow and arrow}$   [1 mark]

- “Sports archers use a bow and arrow to shoot at targets…”

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Perfect timing}$   [1 mark]

- “...archers must focus their minds, steady their breathing, and release the arrow with *perfect timing*.”

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Sign up for}$   [1 mark]

- “I’m going to sign up for archery!”

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The text states:$\text{\textquotedblleft}$A key part of the sport is *precision*,... and release the arrow with perfect *timing*.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **A**: *Strength and speed* are not mentioned in the text.
- **C**: The size of bow each archer needs to use is not discussed in the text.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The speaker says, “I’m going to sign up for archery!”

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **A**: The text begins with a very short history of archery, but the speaker does not make any decisions until the end of the text.
- **C**: The text states that archery is an Olympic sport, but there is no mention of the speaker watching the Olympics.
