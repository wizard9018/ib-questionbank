# EB0333

**Maximum mark: 6**

**You are going to hear a young adult talking about their unusual hobby.**

:audio{#83aaa11f-59e4-4e19-a41f-050f45e1563b aid="83aaa11f-59e4-4e19-a41f-050f45e1563b"}

**Answer the following questions.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$How does the speaker describe moving through the supermarket?    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Name **two** things the speaker does with the products they see.    [2 marks]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Give one example of a “little challenge” the speaker creates.    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Why does the speaker like going to the supermarket alone? Name **one** reason.    [1 mark]

**Choose the correct answer.**

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$How does the speaker feel about going to the supermarket alone?    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Bored

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Excited

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Nervous

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{like a tourist}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I wander aisles like a tourist, checking unusual products.”\

To *wander* is to walk around, in the exercise the words *moving through* replace wander. The speaker uses the phrase $\text{\textquotedblleft}$like a tourist” to explain how they feel while exploring.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$*any two of the following answers:*   

- $\textrm{read labels}$

- $\textrm{imagine tasting}$

- $\textrm{(figure out) who might buy them}$        [2 marks]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I read the labels, imagine tasting them, and try to figure out who might buy them.”\

The speaker mentions these as some of the activities he engages in with the products.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*any one of the following answers:*   

- $\textrm{finding the cheapest chocolate}$

- $\textrm{finding the weirdest spice}$        [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Sometimes I create little challenges, like finding the cheapest chocolate or the weirdest spice.”\

The speaker adds playful challenges to make the supermarket experience more engaging

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*any one of the following answers:*   

- $\textrm{no one interrupts (me)}$

- $\textrm{(I) can take as long as I want}$        [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$No one interrupts me, and I can take as long as I want”\

The speaker frames solo supermarket shopping as a relaxing and quiet activity, without pressure to do anything quickly.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... It feels strangely exciting for such a normal place.”\

The supermarket is ordinary, but the speaker finds it exciting to explore alone.

- *Incorrect options:* A, C

- **A**: The speaker is clearly engaged and curious, not bored.

- **C**: The speaker does not feel nervous; the experience is described as relaxing and enjoyable.
