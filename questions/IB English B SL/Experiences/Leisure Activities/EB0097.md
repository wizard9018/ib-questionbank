# EB0097

**Maximum mark: 7**

**You are going to hear a student talking to their friend about a new free-time activity.**

:audio{#03519a53-7507-4b16-85f4-6c2720753fd8 aid="03519a53-7507-4b16-85f4-6c2720753fd8"}

**Choose an appropriate word from the text that completes each gap in the following sentences.**

The Youth Book Club meeting is at **[-1-]** on Saturday.    [1 mark]

The speaker joined the book club **[-2-]**. [1 mark]

The previous book the group read was a **[-3-]**.    [1 mark]

The book club is a great way to meet people who share the love of **[-4-]**. [1 mark]

The book club gives the speaker a break from **[-5-]**. [1 mark]

**Answer the following question.**

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$The speaker tells their friend the book club is not just about reading, and gives three reasons for the friend to join.  Give **two** reasons.    [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{three p.m.}$   [1 mark]

-   “Youth Book Club meeting at three p.m”

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{last month}$   [1 mark]

-   “I joined the club last month”

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{mystery story}$   [1 mark]

-   “and we read a great mystery story.”

$4$.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{reading}$ [1 mark]

- “to meet people who also love reading.”

$5$.$\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{school stress}$ [1 mark]

- “it's a little escape from school stress, too.”

$6$.$\hspace{1em}$*The answers can be written in any order. Any two of the following three:*

-   $\textrm{meet people}$
-   $\textrm{make new friends}$
-   $\textrm{escape (from) school stress}$

- The speaker says, “It's not just about reading, either, it's a great way to *meet people* - especially those who also love reading, and maybe *make new friends*, and it's a little *escape from school stress*, too.[2 marks]
