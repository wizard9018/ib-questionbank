# AI117

**Maximum mark: 6**

The diagram shows the straight line $L_1$, which intersects the $x$-axis
at A$(-8,0)$ and the $y$-axis at B$(0,4)$.

![c40083b445b05ffe171f553a310158b5de8ce983.svg](images/c40083b445b05ffe171f553a310158b5de8ce983.svg)

1.  Write down the coordinates of M, the midpoint of line segment
    \[AB\].[2 marks]

2.  Calculate the gradient of $L_1$. [2 marks]

The line $L_2$ is perpendicular to $L_1$ and passes through the point
P$(1,2)$.

3.  Find the equation of $L_2$. Give your answer in the form
    $y = mx + c$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  M:  $(-4,2)$

2.  The gradient of $L_1$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{11.9em} m_{L_1} &= \dfrac{4-0}{0-(-8)} \hspace{3em} [\text{by gradient formula}] \\[8pt]
    &= 0.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The gradient of $L_2$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8.1em} m_{L_2} &= -\dfrac{1}{m_{L_1}} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $L_2 \perp L_1$}] \\[5pt]
    &= -\dfrac{1}{0.5} \\[12pt]
    &= -2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the point-gradient formula with
    P$(1,2)$ and $m = -2$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y - y_1 &= m(x - x_1) \hspace{0.7em} \\[6pt]
    y - 2 &= -2\hspace{0.05em}(x-1) \\[6pt]
    y - 2 &= -2x + 2 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.5em} y = -2x + 4
    $$
