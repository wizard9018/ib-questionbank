# AI126

**Maximum mark: 17**

The diagram below shows the triangle ABC. The point C has coordinates
$(9,5)$ and the equation of the line (AB) is $x+4y = 12$.

![3d9c33de7f7eaf861d1fdfed39a4fd8d1cf5d629.svg](images/3d9c33de7f7eaf861d1fdfed39a4fd8d1cf5d629.svg)

1.  Find the coordinates of:
    1.  A;

    2.  B. [2 marks]

2.  Show that the length of \[AB\] is $12.4$ correct to three
    significant figures.[2 marks]

The point D lies on the line (AB). The line (CD) is perpendicular to the
line (AB).

3.  Find:
    1.  the gradient of (CD);

    2.  the equation of (CD). [5 marks]

4.  Find the coordinates of D. [3 marks]

5.  Calculate the length of \[CD\] correct to three significant
    figures. [2 marks]

6.  Calculate the area of triangle ABC. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Substituting $x = 0$ in the equation of (AB), we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        0+4y &= 12 \hspace{1.65em} \\[6pt]
        y &= 3
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the coordinates of A are
         $(0,3)$

    2.  Substituting $y = 0$ in the equation of (AB), we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        x+4(0) &= 12 \hspace{2.5em} \\[6pt]
        x &= 12
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the coordinates of B are
         $(12,0)$

2.  Using the distance b/n two points formula with A$(0,3)$ and
    B$(12,0)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.6em} \mathrm{AB} &= \sqrt{(12-0)^2 + (0-3)^2} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{12^2 + (-3)^2} \\[9pt]
    &\approx 12.4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.  If we rewrite the equation of (AB) in the form $y = mx + c$, we
        have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1em} x+4y &= 12 \\[12pt]
        4y &= -x + 12 \\[6pt]
        y &= -\dfrac{1}{4}x + 3
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the gradient
        of (CD) is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{10.9em} m_{(\mathrm{CD})} &= -\dfrac{1}{m_{(\mathrm{AB})}} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $(\mathrm{CD}) \perp (\mathrm{AB})$}] \\[6pt]
        &= -\dfrac{1}{(-1/4)} \\[12pt]
        &= 4
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the point-gradient formula with C$(9,5)$ and $m = 4$, we
        find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        y - y_1 &= m(x - x_1) \hspace{0.1em} \\[6pt]
        y - 5 &= 4(x - 9) \\[6pt]
        y - 5 &= 4x - 36 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        y = 4x - 31 \hspace{0.05em}
        $$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (\mathrm{AB}) &: \hspace{1.0em} x + 4y = 12  \\[6pt]
    (\mathrm{CD}) &: \hspace{1.0em} 4x - y = 31
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Solving the system of linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $x$ and $y$,
    we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}

    \hspace{12em} \begin{array}{rcl}
    x &=& 8 \\[6pt]
    y &=& 1
    \end{array} \hspace{4em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of D are
     $(8,1)$

5.  Using the distance b/n two points formula with C$(9,5)$ and
    D$(8,1)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \mathrm{CD} &= \sqrt{(8-9)^2 + (1-5)^2} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{(-1)^2 + (-4)^2} \\[9pt]
    &\approx 4.12
    \end{aligned}
    $$

6.  Using the area of a triangle formula with $b = 12.4$ and $h = 4.12$,
    we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} A &= \dfrac{1}{2}(b\times h) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(12.4)(4.12) \\[10pt]
    &= 25.544 \\[13pt]
    &\approx 25.5 \text{ units}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
