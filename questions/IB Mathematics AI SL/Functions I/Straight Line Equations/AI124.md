# AI124

**Maximum mark: 6**

The diagram below shows the straight lines $L_1$ and $L_2$.

![9ee6dfa84eb27bb1c8584bbd4318407cba979047.svg](images/9ee6dfa84eb27bb1c8584bbd4318407cba979047.svg)

1.  Find:
    1.  the gradient of $L_1$;

    2.  the equation of $L_1$, giving your answer in the form
        $y = mx + c$. [3 marks]

The equation of $L_2$ is $x - 2y = 0$.

2.  Find the area of the shaded triangle. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using the gradient formula with A$(0,6)$ and B$(8,0)$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        m_{L_1} &= \dfrac{0-6}{8-0} \hspace{3em} \\[6pt]
        &= -\dfrac{3}{4}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the point-gradient formula with A$(0,6)$ and $m = -3/4$,
        we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        y - y_1 &= m(x - x_1) \\[10pt]
        y - 6 &= -\dfrac{3}{4}(x-0) \hspace{1.8em} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        y = -\dfrac{3}{4}x+6 \hspace{0.4em}
        $$

2.  Substituting $x = 2y$ in the equation of $L_1$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10em} y &= -\dfrac{3}{4}(2y)+6 \\[6pt]
    y &= 2.4 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the area of a triangle formula with $b = 8$ and
    $h = 2.4$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.9em} A &= \dfrac{1}{2}(b\times h) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(8)(2.4) \\[10pt]
    &= 9.6  \text{ units}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
