# AI113

**Maximum mark: 6**

The diagram below shows a straight line $L_1$ which passes through
A$(0,-2)$ and B$(8,0)$.

![dd225af0bca38352f0853ac9a9eb9c1cd3829f36.svg](images/dd225af0bca38352f0853ac9a9eb9c1cd3829f36.svg)

1.  Write down the coordinates of the midpoint of line segment \[AB\].
    [2 marks]

Another line, $L_2$ , intersects the $y$-axis at
C$(0,3)$ and is parallel to $L_1$.

2.  Find the gradient of $L_2$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the equation of $L_2$, giving your answer in the form
    $y = mx+c$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $(4,-1)$

2.  The gradient of $L_2$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{12.2em} m_{L_2} &= m_{L_1}  \hspace{3em} [\text{since $L_2 \parallel L_1$}] \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{0-(-2)}{8-0} \hspace{3em} [\text{by gradient formula}] \\[8pt]
    &= 0.25
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the point-gradient formula with C$(0,3)$ and $m = 0.25$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y - y_1 &= m(x - x_1) \\[6pt]
    y - 3 &= 0.25\hspace{0.05em}(x-0) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{1.3em} y = 0.25x + 3
    $$
