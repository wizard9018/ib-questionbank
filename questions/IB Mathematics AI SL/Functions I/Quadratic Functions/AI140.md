# AI140

**Maximum mark: 6**

A factory produces bars in the shape of a cuboid, with a fixed height of
$30$ cm and varying base. The area, $A$, of each
bar base is described by the function
$$
\begin{aligned}
A(x) &= x(40 - x), \hspace{0.5em} 5 \leq x \leq 35,
\end{aligned}
$$
where
$x$ is the **length** of the bar base in centimetres.

1.  Bar P has a length of $10$ cm. Find the
    **volume** of P. [2 marks]

2.  Bar Q has the same volume as bar P, however has a different length.
    Find the length of Q. [2 marks]

3.  Find the value of $x$ that makes the bar's volume a maximum, and
    state this maximum volume. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The volume of a bar with base length $x$ cm
    is given by the function
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.95em} V(x) &= A(x)\times h \\[7pt]
    &= x(40-x)(30) \\[6pt]
    &= 1200x - 30x^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, evaluating $V(x)$ for
    $x = 10$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.85em} V(10) &= 1200(10) - 30(10)^2 \\[6pt]
    &= 9000 \text{ cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Solving the equation $V(x) = 9000$ for $x \neq 10$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    1200x - 30x^2 &= 9000 \hspace{6.7em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{9.85em} x = 30 \text{ cm} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
