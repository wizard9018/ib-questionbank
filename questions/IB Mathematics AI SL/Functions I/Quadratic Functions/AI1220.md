# AI1220

**Maximum mark: 8**

A drone is launched and follows a vertical path. Its height $h$, in metres, above the ground after $t$ seconds is modelled by the function

$h(t) = -5t^2 + 20t + 1$

1. Write down the initial height of the drone.[1 mark]

2. Find the maximum height the drone reaches and the time at which this occurs.[3 marks]

3. Determine how many seconds after it reaches its maximum height the drone will hit the ground. Give your answer correct to 2 decimal places.[3 marks]

4. State the domain of this function in the context of the flight.[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Initial height occurs when $t$ = 0, the moment the drone is launched

    

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    h(0) &= -5(0)^2 + 20(0) + 1 \\[6pt]

     &= 1\\[6pt]

    \text{Initial height} &=\boxed{1 \text{ metre}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. When the drone hits the ground,  $h(t) = 0$, so we can graph the function and find the $x$-intercepts

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    -5t^2 + 20t + 1 &= 0 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[4pt]

    t =  4.049...\text{ and } t &=-0.04989...\\[4pt]

    $$

    To find time after reaching maximum, we must subtract $t=2$

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    4.049... - 2 &= 2.049...\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
