# AI146

**Maximum mark: 6**

The graph of a quadratic function has a $y$-intercept at A$(0,24)$ and
**one** of its $x$-intercepts is B$(2,0)$.

The $x$-coordinate of the vertex of the graph is $4$.

The equation of the quadratic function is in the form $y = ax^2+bx+c$.

1.  Write down the value of $c$. [1 mark]

2.  Find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$. [4 marks]

3.  Write down the coordinates of the second $x$-intercept of the
    function. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $c = 24$

2.  Using the axis of symmetry formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7.4em} 4 &= -\dfrac{b}{2a} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $x = 4$}] \\[6pt]
    b &= -8a
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the coordinates of B, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0 &= a(2)^2 + b(2) + c \\[6pt]
    [\text{from $(1)$ \& part (a)}] \hspace{2em} 0 &= a(2)^2 + (-8a)(2) + 24 \hspace{3.6em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{8.1em} a = 2 \\[3pt] \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
    Hence, substituting $a = 2$ in the equation $(1)$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    b &= -8(2) \hspace{0.4em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    b = -16 \hspace{1.7em}
    $$

3.
    $(6,0)$
