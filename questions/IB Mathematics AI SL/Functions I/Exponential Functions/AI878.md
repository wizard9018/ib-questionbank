# AI878

**Maximum mark: 5**

An ice cube was dropped into a cup of warm water. The weight of the ice
cube, $W$, in grams, reduces as the ice cube dissolves in the water. The
weight can be modelled by the following function, $W(t)$, where $t$ is
the time in seconds after the dissolving starts.

$$
W(t) =28(0.98)^t,\quad t\geq 0
$$

1.  Find
    1.  the initial weight of the ice cube.

    2.  the percentage of the ice cube dissolving every $5$ seconds.
        [3 marks]

2.  Calculate the weight of the ice cube remaining after one minute.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  At $t=0$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        W(0)&=28(0.98)^0\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.5em}=28 \text{ g}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  At $t=5$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        W(5)&=28(0.98)^5\\[6pt]
        &=25.3 \text{ g}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        We can find the percentage
        decrease between $t=0$ and $t=5$, as follows
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Percentage decrease}&=\dfrac{28-25.3}{28}\times 100\%\hspace{1cm}\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.5em}=9.64\%
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  One minute is $60$ seconds. At $t=60$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1cm}W(60)&=28(0.98)^{60}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}=8.33 \text{ g}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
