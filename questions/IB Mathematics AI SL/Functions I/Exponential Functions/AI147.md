# AI147

**Maximum mark: 6**

After $1960$, the economy growth in Australia has grown in an
exponential fashion. Australian GDP per capita, $G(t)$, in United States
dollars (USD) is modelled by the function

$$
G(t) = 1806 \times (1.037)^t, \\[3pt]
$$
where $t$ is the number of
years since $1960$, and $t \geq 0$.

1.  Write down, in USD, Australian GDP per capita in $1960$. [1 mark]

2.  Find Australian GDP per capita in $1961$. [1 mark]

3.  Find the year for which Australian GDP per capita reaches
    $100\hspace{0.15em}000$ USD. Give your answer correct to **the
    nearest year**. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $1806 \text{ USD}$

2.  Evaluating $G(t)$ for $t = 1$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.7em} G(1) &= 1806 \times (1.037) \\[6pt]
    &= 1872.822 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 1870 \text{ USD}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $G(t) = 100\hspace{0.15em}000$ for $t$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} 1806 \times (1.037)^t &= 100\hspace{0.15em}000 \\[6pt]
    t &\approx 110 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence Australian GDP per capita reaches $100\hspace{0.15em}000$ USD
    in  $2070$
