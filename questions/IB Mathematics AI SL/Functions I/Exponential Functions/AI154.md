# AI154

**Maximum mark: 6**

A population of goldfish decreases exponentially. The size of the
population, $P$, after $t$ days is modelled by the function

$$
\begin{aligned}
P(t) = 8000\times2^{-t} + 100, \hspace{0.5em} t \geq 0. \\
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Write down the **exact** size of the initial population. [1 mark]

2.  Find the size of the population after $5$ days. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the time it will take for the size of the population to
    decrease to $120$.[2 marks]

The population will stabilize when it reaches a size of $n$.

4.  Write down the value of $n$. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $P(t)$ for $t = 0$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.7em} P(0) &= 8000\times2^0 + 100 \\[6pt]
    &= 8000 + 100 \\[6pt]
    &= 8100
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Evaluating $P(t)$ for $t = 5$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.3em} P(5) &= 8000\times2^{-5} + 100 \\[6pt]
    &= 350
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $P(t) = 120$ for $t$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    8000\times2^{-t} + 100 &= 120 \hspace{6.1em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{14.2em} t = 8.64 \text{ days} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{11.9em} \lim_{t\rightarrow\infty} P(t) &= \lim_{t\rightarrow\infty} \big[8000\times2^{-t} + 100\big] \\[6pt]
    &= 0+100 \hspace{2em} [\text{since $2^{-t} \rightarrow 0$ as $t \rightarrow \infty$}] \\[10pt]
    &= 100 \text{ days}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the value of $n$ is
     $100$
