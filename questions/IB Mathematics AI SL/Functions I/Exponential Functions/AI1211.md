# AI1211

**Maximum mark: 6**

The population $P$, of a bacteria culture grows according to the model

$P = 2\hspace{0.10em}500 \times e^{0.04t}$

where $P$ is the number of bacteria and $t$ is the time in hours.

1. Find the number of bacteria after 5 hours. Give your answer to the nearest whole number. [3 marks]

2. Find the time when the population reaches $10\hspace{0.10em}000$ bacteria. Give your answer to the nearest minute. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Substituting $t = 5$ into the model gives

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    P &= 2\hspace{0.10em}500 \times e^{0.04 \times 5} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[8pt]

    &= 3\hspace{0.10em}053.5...\tag*{\textbf{(A1)}} \\[8pt]

    P &=\boxed{3\,054}\hspace{1em}\text{\footnotesize{(nearest whole number)}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
