# AI855

**Maximum mark: 6**

The mass of a radioactive sample is given by

$$
M(t)=P+Q\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^\frac{t}{300}, \qquad t\geq 0 \text{ days.}
$$

where t is the number of days after the sample was collected and
measured.

The initial weight of the sample is $20 \text{ g}$. When each atom emits
its $\alpha$-particle, the mass of the atom is reduced by $3.2\%$.

1.
    1.  Explain why P is the final mass of the sample after every atom
        in the sample has emitted its $\alpha$-particle.

    2.  Hence, find the value of P, leaving your answer correct to two
        decimal places. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of Q. [2 marks]

3.  Estimate the mass of the sample after $2.5$ years (assume no leap
    years). [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        M(t)=P+Q\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^\frac{t}{300}
        $$

        ${\text{As } t\rightarrow \infty, \left(\dfrac{1}{2} \right)^\frac{t}{300} \rightarrow 0. \text{ Therefore }, M(\infty) \approx P.}$

    2.  Hence
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2cm}P &= 0.968\times 20 \qquad [\text{$96.8\%$ of $20$}]\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.35em} \approx 19.36 \text{ g}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  We have $M(0)=20$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    M(0) &= P+Q\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^0\\[6pt]
    20 &= 19.36+Q\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.25cm} Q = 0.64
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  $2.5$ years is $2.5\times 365 = 912.5 \text{ days}$. Hence, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    M(912.5) &= 19.36+0.64\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{\frac{912.5}{300}}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.35em}\approx 19.4  \text{ g}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
