# AI874

**Maximum mark: 6**

In a water filtration plant, the containers of water are filled and then
filtered, all by an automated system. The volume of water in a
container, $W(t)$, in litres, after $t$ seconds of the filling
commencing can be modelled by the function

$$
W(t) = V(1-e^{\frac{-t}{k}}),
$$
where $V$ is the maximum volume of the
water container and $k$ is a positive constant used to alter the rate at
which water is filling the containers.

The value of $k$ is currently set at 25.

1.  Find how long it takes, $t$, for a container to fill up to $50\%$ of
    its maximum volume. [2 marks]

2.  If one of the containers has a maximum volume of 20 litres, find the
    volume of water in this container after $22$ seconds. [2 marks]

The engineers of the plant want to alter the value of $k$ so that it
takes $20$ seconds to fill each container up to $95\%$ of their maximum
volumes.

3.  Find the value of $k$ required. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since the container is $50\%$ of its maximum volume,
    $W=\dfrac{V}{2}$. Hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{V}{2}&=V(1-e^{\frac{-t}{25}})\\[6pt]
    \dfrac{1}{2}&=1-e^{\frac{-t}{25}}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-0.5em}t\approx17.3 \text{ seconds} \qquad \qquad \qquad [\text{using GDC}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Substituting $V=20$ and $t=22$ into $W(t)$ function, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    W(t) &= V(1-e^{\frac{-t}{25}})\\[6pt]
    W(22)  &=20(1-e^{\frac{-22}{25}})\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}\approx11.7 \text{ litres} \qquad \qquad \qquad [\text{using GDC}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Since the containers are filled to $95\%$ of their maximum volume,
    $W=0.95V$. Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0.95V&=V(1-e^{\frac{-20}{k}})\\[6pt]
    0.95&=1-e^{\frac{-20}{k}}\\[6pt]
    e^{\frac{-20}{k}}&=0.05\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.5em}k\approx 6.68 \qquad \qquad \qquad [\text{using GDC}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
