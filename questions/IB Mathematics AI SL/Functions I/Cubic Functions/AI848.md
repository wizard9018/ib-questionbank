# AI848

**Maximum mark: 17**

The Burj Khalifa, located in Dubai, is the tallest building in the
world. It has a height of $830 \text{ m}$ and has a square base that
covers a floor area of $556 \text{ m} \times 556 \text{ m}$. A tourism
shop located near the building sells souvenirs of the tower, which sit
inside glass pyramids, as illustrated by the diagram below. The souvenir
tower is an accurate scale replica of the actual tower.

![75dbfe1df45464ae26235a84b47e84fedd3f43ea.svg](images/75dbfe1df45464ae26235a84b47e84fedd3f43ea.svg)

The scaled model of Burj Khalifa has a base area of
$20 \text{ cm} \times 20 \text{ cm}$. The height and base area
dimensions of the glass pyramid are 10% larger than the model.

1.
    1.  Find the height of the souvenir tower, in cm, correct to the
        nearest mm.

    2.  Find the volume of the glass pyramid, rounding your answer
        correct to the nearest cubic centimetre. [5 marks]

The shop owner aims to maximise profits from selling the souvenirs. The
more the owner orders from the manufacturer, the cheaper the souvenirs
are to buy. However, if too many are ordered, profits may decrease due
to surplus stock unsold.

The number of souvenirs ordered from previous years and the resulting
profits are shown in the following table.

| **Quantity** | **Profit(\$)** |
|:------------:|:--------------:|
|    $5000$    |   $35\,000$    |
|  $10\,000$   |   $95\,500$    |
|  $13\,000$   |   $116\,500$   |

The shop owner decides to fit a cubic model of the form

$$
P(x) = ax^3+bx^2+cx+d
$$
to model the profit, $P$, for $x$ thousand
souvenirs ordered.

2.  Explain why $d=0$.[1 mark]

3.  Construct three linear equations to solve for $a$, $b$ and $c$, and
    hence write down the completed function $P(x)$. [5 marks]

4.  Find $P'(x)$.[2 marks]

5.  Find, to the nearest hundred souvenirs, the optimal number of
    souvenirs the owner should buy to maximise profit, and the resulting
    profit from this number. [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  The height $(h_m)$ of the model can be calculated by
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        h_m&= \text \times \text{scale factor}\\[6pt]
        &= 83000 \times \dfrac{20}{55600}\hspace{4.1em} [\text{all values in cm}] \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 29.9 \text{ cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Let $x$ be the side length of the base and $h$ be the height of
        the glass pyramid. Since the dimensions of glass pyramid are
        $10\%$ larger than the model, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        x&= 20+20(0.1)\hspace{2cm} \\[6pt]
        &= 22 \text{ cm}\\[6pt]
        h&= 29.9+29.9(0.1)\\[6pt]
        &\approx 32.9 \text{ cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    ![c16aa6b415e3ff65a129bf040b3ec43c108d55ec.svg](images/c16aa6b415e3ff65a129bf040b3ec43c108d55ec.svg)

    The volume $(V)$ of the glass pyramid can therefore be calculated by

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V&= \dfrac{1}{3}\times x^2 \times h \\[6pt]
    &=\dfrac{1}{3}\times (22)^2 \times(32.9)\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 5308 \text{ cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  If zero souvenirs are ordered (i.e. $x=0$), there won't be any
    souvenirs to sell. Hence, the profit, $P$, will also be zero.

    $$
    P(0)=0, \,\text{ hence } \, d=0
    $$

3.  Since $x$ is in thousands, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    P(5)&=5^3a+5^2b+5c=35\, 000\\[6pt]
    P(10) &= 10^3a+10^2b+10c = 95\, 500\\[6pt]
    P(13) &= 13^3a+13^2b+13c = 116\, 500
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using
    G.D.C. to solve the equations $(1)$, $(2)$ and $(3)$, we get

    $$
    a=-\dfrac{2295}{26},\, b=\dfrac{47685}{26}, \text{ and } c=\dfrac{475}{13}
    $$
    Hence

    $$
    \hspace{0.3em}P(x) =-\dfrac{2295}{26}x^3+\dfrac{47685}{26}x^2+\dfrac{475}{13}x
    $$

4.  Differentiating $P(x)$ with respect to $x$, we get
    $$
    \hspace{0.3em}P'(x) =-\dfrac{6885}{26}x^2+\dfrac{47685}{13}x+\dfrac{475}{13}
    $$

5.  If we sketch the graph of $P(x)$, the maximum profit occurs at point
    A.

    ![5541c9fa3d0d1b43403859281edadfc0d340eef4.svg](images/5541c9fa3d0d1b43403859281edadfc0d340eef4.svg)

    Using G.D.C. to find the coordinates of A, we get

    $$
    x=13.9, \text{ and } P(x)\approx 118\,000
    $$
    Hence,

    The maximum profit will occur when the owner buys
    $\hspace{0.4em}13\,900\hspace{0.4em}$ souvenirs.
    The resulting profit will be approximately
    $$\hspace{0.35em}\$118\,000.$$
