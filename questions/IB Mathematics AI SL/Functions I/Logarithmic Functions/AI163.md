# AI163

**Maximum mark: 7**

The half-life, $T$, in years, of a radioactive isotope can be modelled
by the function
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(k) &= \dfrac{\ln 0.5}{\ln\left(1 - \frac{k}{100}\right)}, \hspace{0.5em} 0 < k < 100,
\end{aligned}
$$

where $k$ is the decay rate, in percent, per year of the isotope.

1.  The decay rate of Hydrogen-$3$ is $5.5$ %
    per year. Find its half-life.[2 marks]

The half-life of Uranium-$232$ (U-$232$) is $68.9$ years. A sample
containing $250$ grams of U-$232$ is obtained and stored as a side
product of a nuclear fuel cycle.

2.  Find the decay rate per year of U-$232$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the amount of U-$232$ left in the sample after:
    1.  $68.9$ years;

    2.  $100$ years. [3 marks]

    $\,$

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $T(k)$ for $k = 5.5$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    T(5.5) &= \dfrac{\ln 0.5}{\ln\left(1 - \frac{5.5}{100}\right)} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 12.3 \text{ years}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Solving the equation $T(k) = 68.9$ for $k$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\ln 0.5}{\ln\left(1 - \frac{r}{100}\right)} &= 68.9 \hspace{6.05em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{10.3em} k = 1.00\hspace{0.05em}\% \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

    2.  We have a geometric sequence with the first term $u_1 = 250$ and
        the $\text{common}$ ratio $r = (1 - 1.00/100) = 0.99$.

        Hence, using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1r^{n-1}$ with

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        u_{101} &= u_1r^{101-1} \\[6pt]

        \end{aligned}
