# AI1107

**Maximum mark: 8**

Jasper owns a business and has 7 employees. He can assign any number of employees to a task. The time the task takes varies inversely with the number of employees who are assigned to it. This is shown by the following:

$$

\begin{aligned}

t &\propto \dfrac{1}{x}

\end{aligned}

$$

Where $t$ is time a task takes in hours, and $x$ is the number of employees assigned to the task.

If one employee is assigned to a task, it will take 8 hours to complete the task.

1. Find an equation for $t$ in terms of $x$. [2 marks]

Jasper has a task that must be completed within three hours.

2. 1. Determine the minimum number of employees that should be assigned to the task to ensure it is completed within three hours.

    2. State the total amount of time the task will take. Give your answer to the nearest minute. [3 marks]

If a task does not take a whole number of hours, Jasper must round __up__ to the nearest whole hour to pay his employees.

3. Determine the optimal number of employees Jasper should assign to ensure the task is complete within three hours, while minimising the number of hours of work he needs to pay for. [3 marks]
