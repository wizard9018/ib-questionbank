# AI1177

**Maximum mark: 6**

A team of painters is hired to paint a house. The time $T$ (in hours) required to paint the house varies inversely with the number of painters, $P$. This can be expressed as

$$

\begin{aligned}

T &= \dfrac{k}{P}

\end{aligned}

$$

A team of 3 painters can paint the house in 12 hours.

1. Write an equation relating the time $T$ to the number of painters $P$.[2 marks]

The following diagram shows the graph of $T=P$ for $0 \leq P \leq 12$.

![AI1177a](images/AI1177a.JPG)

2. On the axes above, sketch the graph of $T(P)$ for $3 \leq P \leq 12$.[2 marks]

3. State the coordinates of the point of intersection.[1 mark]

4. State the meaning of the coordinates of this point in the context of the question. [1 mark]

    \begin{aligned} 

    12 &= \dfrac{k}{3} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    k &= 36

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore,

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &\boxed{T = \dfrac{36}{P}}\tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
