# AI068

**Maximum mark: 6**

At the beginning of a year, Jack has $$\$5000$$ in a savings account that pays a $4\hspace{0.05em}\%$ annual interest rate, **compounded quarterly**. At the end of each year, Jack plans to deposit a further $$\$5000$$ in the account.

1.  Find the number of whole years it will take until Jack has at least
    $$\$100\hspace{0.15em}000$$ in his account. [3 marks]

At the same time, John has $$\$6000$$ in a savings account that pays an annual interest rate that is **compounded semi-annually**. At the end of each year, John deposits a further $$\$6000$$ in the account. After $20$ years, John has $$\$200\hspace{0.15em}000$$ in his account.

2.  Find John's annual interest rate. [3 marks]
