# AI069

**Maximum mark: 6**

**In this question give all answers correct to the nearest whole
number.**

Michelle takes out a loan of $$\$12\hspace{0.15em}000$$. The unpaid
balance on the loan has an interest rate of
$4.3$ % per year, **compounded annually**.

1.  The loan is to be repaid in payments of $$\$1500$$ made at the end of
    each year.

    1.  Find the number of years it will take to repay the loan.
    2.  Calculate the total amount that has been paid in amortising
        the loan.[3 marks]

2.  The loan is to be amortised over $5$ years.
    1.  Find the annual payment made at the end of each year.

    2.  Calculate the total amount that has been paid in amortising
        the loan.[3 marks]
