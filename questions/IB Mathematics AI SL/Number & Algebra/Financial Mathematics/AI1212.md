# AI1212

**Maximum mark: 7**

A car loses $20\%$ of its value each year. The car initially costs $$\$30\,000$$.

1. 1. Write down an expression for the value of the car after $n$ years.

    2. Find the **exact** value of the car after 6 years. [3 marks]

2.  1. Find the total percentage loss in the value of the car after 6 years.

    2. Comment on whether the value is lost more rapidly in the early years or later years. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1. Let $V_n$ be the value of the car after $n$ years.

        Using the compound depreciation formula, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{FV} &= \text{PV}\bigg(1 - \dfrac{r}{100k}\bigg)^{kn} \\[5pt]
        V_n &= 30\hspace{0.25em}000\bigg(1 - \dfrac{20}{100(1)}\bigg)^{(1)n} \tag*{\textcolor{white}{{(M1)}}}\\[8pt]
        V_n &=\boxed{ 30\hspace{0.10em}000 \times 0.8^n} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2. Substituting $n = 6$ into our equation above
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        V_6 &= 30\hspace{0.10em}000 \times 0.8^6 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[4pt]
        V_6 &=\boxed{\$7\hspace{0.10em}864.32} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2. 1. Initial value = $$\$30\hspace{0.10em}000$$, value after 6 years = $$\$7\hspace{0.10em}864.32$$.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Loss}&= 30\hspace{0.10em}000 - 7\hspace{0.10em}864.32 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]
        &= 22\hspace{0.10em}135.68 \\[6pt]
        \text{Percentage loss} &= \frac{22\hspace{0.10em}135.68}{30\hspace{0.10em}000} \times 100   \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}} \\[12pt]
        &= 73.7856\% \\[6pt]
        \text{Percentage loss} &=\boxed{73.8\%}\,\,\text{(3 s.f.)}\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
    2. Considering the base value of the equation we found in part (a)(i)

        The exponential decay equation has a base value of 0.8, which leads to a rapid loss in the early years and progressively smaller losses in the later years.

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{R1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Examples to show loss in the early years
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Year 1:} &\quad 30\hspace{0.10em}000 \times 0.8      = 24\hspace{0.10em}000 \quad \text{(Loss: 6000)} \\
        \text{Year 2:} &\quad 24\hspace{0.10em}000 \times 0.8      = 19\hspace{0.10em}200 \quad \text{(Loss: 4800)} \\
        \text{Year 3:} &\quad 19\hspace{0.10em}200 \times 0.8      = 15\hspace{0.10em}360 \quad \text{(Loss: 3840)}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
