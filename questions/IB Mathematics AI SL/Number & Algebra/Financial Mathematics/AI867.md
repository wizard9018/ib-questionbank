# AI867

**Maximum mark: 19**

Nathan receives a lump sum inheritance of $$\$ 55\,000$$ and invests the
money into a savings account with an annual interest rate of $7.5 \%$,
compounded quarterly.

1.  Calculate the value of Nathan's investment after 5 years, rounding
    your answer to the nearest dollar. [3 marks]

After $m$ months, the amount in the savings account has increased to
more than $$\$70\,000$$.

2.  Find the minimum value of $m$, where $m\in N$.[4 marks]

Nathan is saving to purchase a property. The price of the property is
$$\$150\,000$$. Nathan puts down a $15\%$ deposit and takes out a loan for
the remaining amount.

3.  Write down the loan amount.[1 mark]

The loan duration is for eight years, compounded monthly, with equal
monthly payments of $$\$1500$$ made by Nathan at the end of each month.

4.  For this loan, find
    1.  the amount of interest paid by Nathan over the life of the loan.

    2.  the annual interest rate of the loan, correct to two decimal
        places. [5 marks]

After $5$ years of paying this loan, Nathan decides to pay the
outstanding loan amount in one final payment.

5.  Find the amount of the final payment after $5$ years, rounding your
    answer to the nearest dollar. [3 marks]

6.  Find the amount Nathan saved by making this final payment after $5$
    years, rounding your answer to the nearest dollar.[3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The compound interest formula is
    $$
    FV = PV\left(1+\dfrac{r}{100k}\right)^{kn}

    $$
    We have
    $PV=55000,\, r=7.5,\, k=4,$ and $n=5$.
    Substituting these values into the formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    FV &= 55000\left(1+\dfrac{7.5}{100(4)} \right)^{4(5)}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}= \$ 79\,747
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have $FV=70000,\,PV=55000,\, r=7.5,$ and $k=4$.
    Substituting these values into the compound interest formula, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    70000 &= 55000\left(1+\dfrac{7.5}{100(4)} \right)^{4(n)}\\[6pt]
    &\text{n}\approx 3.25 \text{ years}\hspace{2em}[\text{using GDC}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Converting to months, we get

    $$
    m=12\times 3.25 = 39 \text{ months}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Property price}&= 150\,000 \\[6pt]
    \text{Deposit} &= 15\% \\[6pt]
    \text{Loan amount} &= 150\,000 - (0.15\times 150\,000)\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}= \$ 127\,500
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.
    1.  For $8$ years, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Monthly payment}&= 1500 \\[6pt]
        \text{Total payments made}&= 1500\times 8 \times 12 \\[6pt]
        &= \$144 \, 000\\[6pt]
        \text{Interest paid} &= 144\,000 - 127\,500\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.5em}= \$ 16\,500
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the TVM Solver, we get

        The annual interest rate of the loan is
         $3.08 \%$

5.  Using the TVM Solver, we get

    ![405e9952db0082ca8955bbdaa92e691e8b526e72.svg](images/405e9952db0082ca8955bbdaa92e691e8b526e72.svg)

    The final payment after $5$ years is
     $$\$51\,533$$

6.  We can find the amount of interest Nathan saves by finding the
    difference between the total amount paid without the $5$ year final
    payment, and the total amount with making the $5$ year final
    payment, as follows
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Amount saved} &= (127\,500+ 16\,500)-[(5\times 12 \times 1500)+51\,533]\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}=\$2\,467
    \end{aligned}
    $$
