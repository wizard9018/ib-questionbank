# AI1131

**Maximum mark: 6**

On January the 1st 2017 Ji Sun had savings of $7\,380.

He decided to keep his money in a bank account that pays an annual interest rate of 4.7\% per year which is compounded monthly.

1. Ji Sun left the money in the bank account until January the 1st 2025.

    Calculate the value of his investment on this day, giving your answer to 2 decimal places. [2 marks]

After 2 complete years of earning interest in the bank account, Ji Sun decided to move his money into a savings account which paid a higher rate of yearly interest, compounded monthly.

2. On January the 1st 2025 he is projected to have  $11\,747.40.

    Determine the annual interest rate of the savings account. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The savings are invested for a total of $8$ years, hence we can write down the following

    ![a1131v1](images/a1131v1.png)

    Using the compound interest formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    FV&= 7380\bigg(1 + \dfrac{4.7}{100\times12}\bigg)^{12\times 8}\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[12pt]
    &\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{=\$10740.69}  \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  First we need to work out the value of Ji Sun's savings after $2$ complete years

    ![a1131v2](images/a1131v2.png)

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &= 7380\bigg(1 + \dfrac{4.7}{100\times12}\bigg)^{12\times 2} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[12pt]
    &=\$8105.88... \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    As this amount of money is invested for the remaining $6$ years, we can form the following equation in terms of the unknown interest rate $r$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    11747.40&= 8105.88...\bigg(1 + \dfrac{r} {100\times12}\bigg)^{12\times 6} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We solve this equation graphically

    ![a1131v3](images/a1131v3.png)

    Hence the value of $r$ is $6.20\% $
    $$
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    $$
