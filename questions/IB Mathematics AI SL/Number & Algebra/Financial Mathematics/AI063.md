# AI063

**Maximum mark: 6**

Ali bought a car for $$\$18\hspace{0.15em}000$$. The value of the car
depreciates by $10.5$ %
each year.

1.  Find the value of the car at the end of the first year. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of the car after $4$ years. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the number of years it will take for the car to be worth
    exactly half its original value. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have

    ![86d0641572fd7d8ca13b40e061d1374bca7391bc.svg](images/86d0641572fd7d8ca13b40e061d1374bca7391bc.svg)

    Hence, using the compound depreciation formula, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} \mathrm{FV} &= \text{PV}\bigg(1 - \dfrac{r}{100}\bigg)^n \\[6pt]
    &= 18\hspace{0.15em}000\bigg(1 - \dfrac{10.5}{100}\bigg)  \\[12pt]
    &= \$16\hspace{0.15em}110 \\[18pt]
    &\approx \$16\hspace{0.15em}100 \\[12pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have

    ![8adbc1baad593f942992356c96995063b6051eee.svg](images/8adbc1baad593f942992356c96995063b6051eee.svg)

    Hence, using the compound depreciation formula, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} \mathrm{FV} &= 18\hspace{0.15em}000\bigg(1 - \dfrac{10.5}{100}\bigg)^4  \\[12pt]
    &= \$11\hspace{0.15em}549.5 \\[18pt]
    &\approx \$11\hspace{0.15em}500
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have

    ![22cbaefd6c6344e284fd948d4606858070563a4e.svg](images/22cbaefd6c6344e284fd948d4606858070563a4e.svg)

    Hence, using the compound depreciation formula, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    9000 &= 18\hspace{0.15em}000\bigg(1 - \dfrac{10.5}{100}\bigg)^n \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{8em} n \approx 6.25 \text{ years} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
