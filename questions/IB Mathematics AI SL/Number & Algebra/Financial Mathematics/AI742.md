# AI742

**Maximum mark: 6**

Olivia invests an amount of USD into a savings account which pays $3.6\%$ p.a. interest, **compounded monthly**. After $6$ years, Olivia has \$$21$ $500$ in the account.

1.  Find the amount of USD initially invested. Give your answer to 2 decimal places.[3 marks]

With this money, Olivia purchases a used boat for \$$19$ $000$ and sells it $4$ years later for \$$15$ $200$.

2.  Find the rate at which the boat depreciates per year over the $4$-year period.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We can use Financial Solver on the GDC.

    | N | $72$ |
    |:---:|:---:|
    | I% | $3.6$ |
    | $\textcolor{#B52209}{\text{PV}}$ | $\textcolor{#B52209}{-17}$ $\textcolor{#B52209}{328.91\ldots}$ |
    | PMT | $0$ |
    | FV | $21$ $500$ |
    | P/Y | $12$ |
    | C/Y | $12$ |
    | PMT | END |

    Or using the **compound interest formula (1.4)** we have

    $$\\[-2.5em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{FV} &= \text{PV}\bigg(1 + \dfrac{r}{100k}\bigg)^{kn} \\[10pt]
        21\hspace{0.15em}500 &= \text{PV}\bigg(1 + \dfrac{3.6}{100 \times 12}\bigg)^{12 \times 6} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}} \\[10pt]
        \text{PV} &= 17\hspace{0.15em}328.911\ldots \\[6pt]
        \text{PV} &= \boxed{\$17\hspace{0.15em}328.91}\hspace{0.5em}\text{(2 d.p.)} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We can use Financial Solver on the GDC.

    | N | $4$ |
    |:---:|:---:|
    | $\textcolor{#B52209}{\text{I\%}}$ | $\textcolor{#B52209}{-5.425\ldots}$ |
    | PV | $-19$ $000$ |
    | PMT | $0$ |
    | FV | $15$ $200$ |
    | P/Y | $1$ |
    | C/Y | $1$ |
    | PMT | END |

    **Note: The $r$ value appears as a negative because the boat is depreciating in value.**

    Or using the **compound interest formula (1.4)** with $-r$ to model depreciation, we have

    $$\\[-2.5em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{FV} &= \text{PV}\bigg(1 + \dfrac{(-r)}{100}\bigg)^{n} \\[10pt]
        15\hspace{0.15em}200 &= 19\hspace{0.15em}000\bigg(1 + \dfrac{(-r)}{100}\bigg)^{4} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}} \\[10pt]
        r &= 5.425\ldots \\[6pt]
        r &= \boxed{5.43\%} \hspace{1em} \text{(3 s.f.)} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
