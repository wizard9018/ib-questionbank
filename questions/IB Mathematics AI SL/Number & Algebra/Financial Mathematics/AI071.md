# AI071

**Maximum mark: 6**

**In this question give all answers correct to two decimal places.**

Stella receives a loan of
€ $32\hspace{0.15em}000$ for her flower shop
business at an interest rate $5.29$ % per year,
**compounded monthly**. She agrees to pay back the loan in $60$ equal
installments, made at the end of each month over the next five years.

1.  Calculate the amount of monthly installment. [3 marks]

Four years after she starts repaying the loan, Stella decides to repay
the rest in a final single installment.

2.  Calculate the amount owing at the end of the four years.
    [3 marks]
