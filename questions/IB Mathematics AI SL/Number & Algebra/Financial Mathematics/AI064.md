# AI064

**Maximum mark: 6**

**In this question give all answers correct to the nearest whole
number.**

Benjamin spends € $32\hspace{0.15em}000$ buying
bitcoin mining hardware for his cryptocurrency $\text{mining}$
business. The hardware depreciates by $16$ %
each year.

1.  Find the value of the hardware after two years. [3 marks]

2.  Find the number of years it will take for the hardware to be worth
    less than $\text{\euro\hspace{0.05em}\(8000\)}$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have

    ![0586f73ff6162ac0d35f7aa7d72624e24f0856bf.svg](images/0586f73ff6162ac0d35f7aa7d72624e24f0856bf.svg)

    Hence, using the compound depreciation formula, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.9em} \mathrm{FV} &= \text{PV}\bigg(1 - \dfrac{r}{100}\bigg)^n \\[5pt]
    &= 32\hspace{0.15em}000\bigg(1 - \dfrac{16}{100}\bigg)^2 \\[12pt]
    &\approx \text{\euro\hspace{0.05em}}22\hspace{0.15em}579
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have

    ![532b7aee114d6a0a16e054edfa0e732a7eb37db7.svg](images/532b7aee114d6a0a16e054edfa0e732a7eb37db7.svg)

    Hence, using the compound depreciation formula, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.8em} 8000 &= 32\hspace{0.15em}000\bigg(1 - \dfrac{16}{100}\bigg)^n \\[8pt]
    n &\approx 7.95 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence it takes
     $8 \text{ years}$
    for the hardware to be worth less than
    € $8000$.
