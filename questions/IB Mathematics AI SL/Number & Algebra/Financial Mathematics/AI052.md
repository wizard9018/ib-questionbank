# AI052

**Maximum mark: 6**

Jeremy invests $$\$8000$$ into a savings account that pays an annual
interest rate of $5.5$ %, **compounded
annually**.

1.  Write down a formula which calculates that total value of the
    investment after $n$ years. [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the amount of money in the savings account after:
    1.  $1$ year;

    2.  $3$ years. [2 marks]

3.  Jeremy wants to use the money to put down a $$\$10\hspace{0.15em}000$$
    deposit on an apartment. Determine if Jeremy will be able to do this
    within a $5$-year timeframe.[2 marks]

    Hence, using the compound interest formula, we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{FV} &= \mathrm{PV}\bigg(1 + \dfrac{r}{100k}\bigg)^{kn} \\[5pt]
    &= 8000\bigg(1 + \dfrac{5.5}{100\times1}\bigg)^{1\times n} \\[12pt]
    &= 8000\hspace{0.05em}(1+0.055)^n
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  Substituting $n = 1$ in the formula $(1)$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{FV} &= 8000\hspace{0.05em}(1+0.055) \hspace{0.5em} \\[8pt]
        &= \$8440
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Substituting $n = 3$ in the formula $(1)$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{FV} &= 8000\hspace{0.05em}(1+0.055)^3 \\[8pt]
        &\approx \$9390
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  Substituting $n = 5$ in the formula $(1)$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.2em} \mathrm{FV} &= 8000\hspace{0.05em}(1+0.055)^5 \\[8pt]
    &\approx \$10\hspace{0.15em}456
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $\text{Yes, Jeremy will be able to do this within a \(5\)-year timeframe.}$
