# AI072

**Maximum mark: 7**

Ray takes out a loan of $$\$200\hspace{0.15em}000$$ to purchase a house.
He agrees to pay the bank $$\$1250$$ at the end of every month to amortise
the loan, and interest accumulates on the balance at a rate of
$3.79$ % per year, **compounded
monthly**.

1.  Find the number of years and months it takes to pay back the loan.
    [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the total amount that Ray has paid in amortising the loan.
    [2 marks]

3.  Ray decides to increase the monthly payment to $$\$1500$$. Justify
    this decision.[3 marks]
