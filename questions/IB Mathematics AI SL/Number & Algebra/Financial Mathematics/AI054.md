# AI054

**Maximum mark: 6**

**In this question give all answers correct to two decimal places.**

Mia deposits $4000$ Australian dollars (AUD) into a bank account. The
bank pays a nominal annual interest rate of
$6$ %, **compounded semi-annually**.

1.  Find the amount of **interest** that Mia will earn over the next
    $2.5$ years. [3 marks]

Ella also deposits AUD into a bank account. Her bank pays a nominal
annual $\text{interest}$ rate of $4$ %,
**compounded monthly**. In $2.5$ years, the total amount in Ella's
account will be $4000$ AUD.

2.  Find the amount that Ella deposits in the bank account.
    [3 marks]

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{FV} - \text{PV} &\approx 4637.10 - 4000 \hspace{3.7em} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 637.10 \text{ AUD}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
