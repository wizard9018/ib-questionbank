# AI002

**Maximum mark: 6**

The distance between two points with coordinates $(x_1,y_1)$ and
$(x_2,y_2)$ is equal to $\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}$.

1.  Calculate the distance between points A$(40,-100)$ and B$(1,-2)$.
    Give your answer correct to three significant figures. [3 marks]

2.  Give your answer from **part (a)** correct to one decimal place.
    [1 mark]

3.  Write the answer to **part (b)** in the form $a\times10^k$, where
    $1 \leq a < 10$, $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The distance between points A$(40,-100)$ and B$(1,-2)$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{AB} &= \sqrt{(1-40)^2+(-2-(-100))^2} \\[8pt]
    &\approx 105.475 \\[8pt]
    &\approx 105
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    $105.5$

3.
    $1.055\times10^2$
