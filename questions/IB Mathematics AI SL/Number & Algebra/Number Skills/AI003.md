# AI003

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $F = \dfrac{(4\sin 2z-1)(2\tan 3z+1)}{x^2-y^2}$, where $x = 12$,
$y = 8$ and $z = \ang{15}$.

1.  Calculate the **exact** value of $F$. [2 marks]

2.  Give your answer to $F$ correct to
    1.  two significant figures;

    2.  two decimal places. [2 marks]

Sasha estimates the value of $F$ to be $0.03$.

3.  Calculate the percentage error in Sasha's estimate.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting $x = 12$, $y = 8$ and $z = \ang{15}$ in the expression
    for $F$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.55em} F &= \dfrac{(4\sin \ang{30}-1)(2\tan \ang{45}+1)}{12^2-8^2} \\[6pt]
    &= 0.0375
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.
        $0.038$
    2.
        $0.04$

3.  Using the percentage error formula
    $\epsilon = \bigg|\dfrac{v_\mathrm{A} - v_\mathrm{E}}{v_\mathrm{E}}\bigg|\times 100\hspace{0.05em}\%$
    with $v_\mathrm{E} = 0.0375$ and $v_\mathrm{A} = 0.03$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \epsilon &= \bigg|\dfrac{0.03 - 0.0375}{0.0375}\bigg|\times 100\hspace{0.05em}\% \\[6pt]
    &= 20\hspace{0.05em}\%
    \end{aligned}
    $$
