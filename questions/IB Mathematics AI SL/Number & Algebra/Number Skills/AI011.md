# AI011

**Maximum mark: 6**

Four cement bags labelled, \"5 kg\", were
delivered to a customer. The customer measured each bag to check their
weights and recorded the following:
$$
\begin{aligned}
4.92,\hspace{0.3em} 4.95,\hspace{0.3em} 5.02,\hspace{0.3em}4.95 \\
\end{aligned}
$$

1.
    1.  Find the mean of the customer's measurements.

    2.  Calculate the percentage error between the mean and the stated,\
        approximate weight of $5$ kg.
        [3 marks]

2.  Calculate $\sqrt{2.15^8-5.12^{-0.8}}$, giving your answer
    1.  correct to the nearest integer;

    2.  in the form $a\times10^k$, where $1 \leq a < 10$ and
        $k\in \mathbb{Z}$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  If we calculate the mean of the measurements, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2.8em} \mu &=  \dfrac{4.92 + 4.95 + 5.02 + 4.95}{4} \\[6pt]
        &= 4.96
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the percentage error formula
        $\epsilon = \bigg|\dfrac{v_\mathrm{A} - v_\mathrm{E}}{v_\mathrm{E}}\bigg|\times 100\hspace{0.05em}\%$
        with $v_\mathrm{E} = 4.96$ and $v_\mathrm{A} = 5$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \epsilon &= \bigg|\dfrac{5 - 4.96}{4.96}\bigg|\times 100\hspace{0.05em}\% \\[6pt]
        &= 0.81\hspace{0.05em}\%
        \end{aligned}
        $$
