# AI1130

**Maximum mark: 5**

Jurgen is a scientist working for NASA. He maintains a telescope that is in orbit around the Earth.

To test the accuracy of the telescope he uses it to measure the radius of the Earth. The radius of the Earth is known to be $6\hspace{0.1em}378\hspace{0.1em}137$ metres.

Jurgen measures the radius of the Earth to be $6\hspace{0.1em}432\hspace{0.1em}400$ metres.

1. Calculate the percentage error in Jurgen's measurement. [2 marks]

Jurgen uses his measurement to calculate the circumference of the Earth. He assumes the Earth is spherical.

2.  1.  Determine the value for the circumference of the Earth that Jurgen obtains. Round your answer to $4$ significant figures.

    2.  Write down the answer to part (b)(i) in the form $a\times10^k$, where $1\leq a<10$ and $k\in \mathbb{Z}$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the formula we can write the percentage error in Jurgen's estimate as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \epsilon &=\bigg|\frac{6\hspace{0.1em}432\hspace{0.1em}400 - 6\hspace{0.1em}378\hspace{0.1em}137}{6378137}\bigg|\times 100 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
        &=0.8507656...\\[6pt]
        &=\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{0.851\%} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
2.  1.  As Jurgen has assumed the Earth is spherical we can use the formula for the circumference of a circle to find the circumference of the Earth. Hence
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
            C_{\text{Earth}}&=2\pi r \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\
            &=2\pi\times 6\hspace{0.1em}432\hspace{0.1em}400\\
            &=40\hspace{0.1em}415\hspace{0.1em}961\\[6pt]
            &=\hspace{0.275em}{40\,420\,000} \text{ metres} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
    2.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
            &=\hspace{0.275em}4.042 \times 10^7 \text{ metres} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
