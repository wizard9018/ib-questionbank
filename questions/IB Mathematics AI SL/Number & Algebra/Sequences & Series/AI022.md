# AI022

**Maximum mark: 6**

The third term, $u_3$, of an arithmetic sequence is $7$. The common
difference of\
the sequence, $d$, is $3$.

1.  Find $u_1$, the first term of the sequence. [2 marks]

2.  Find $u_{60}$, the $60$th term of sequence. [2 marks]

The first and fourth terms of this arithmetic sequence are the first two
terms\
of a geometric sequence.

3.  Calculate the sixth term of the **geometric** sequence.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_3 + (n-3)d$ with $n = 1$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_1 &= u_3 + (1-3)d \hspace{0.4em} \\[6pt]
    &= 7 + (1-3)(3) \\[6pt]
    &= 1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_3 + (n-3)d$ with $n = 60$, we
    find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_{60} &= u_3 + (60-3)d \\[6pt]
    &= 7 + (60-3)(3) \\[6pt]
    &= 178
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    \begin{aligned}
    u_6 &= u_1r^{6-1} \\[6pt]
    &= 1(10)^{6-1} \\[7pt]
