# AI037

**Maximum mark: 6**

The fifth term, $u_5$, of a geometric sequence is $375$. The sixth term,
$u_6$, of the sequence is $75$.

1.  Write down the common ratio of the sequence. [1 mark]

2.  Find $u_1$. [2 marks]

The sum of the first $k$ terms in the sequence is
$292\hspace{0.15em}968$.

3.  Find the value of $k$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The common ratio is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{u_6}{u_5} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{75}{375} \\[12pt]
    &= 0.2 \hspace{3.2em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_5r^{n-5}$ with $n = 1$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_1 &= u_5r^{1-5} \\[6pt]
    &= 375(0.2)^{1-5} \\[7pt]
    &= 234\hspace{0.15em}375
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $S_k = 292\hspace{0.15em}968$ for $k$, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{u_1(1-r^k)}{1-r} &= 292\hspace{0.15em}968 \hspace{9.3em} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{234\hspace{0.15em}375(1-(0.2)^k)}{1-0.2} &= 292\hspace{0.15em}968 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{6.6em} k = 8 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
