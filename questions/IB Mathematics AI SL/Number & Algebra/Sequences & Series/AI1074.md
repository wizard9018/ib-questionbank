# AI1074

**Maximum mark: 7**

Brendan is training for a long distance bike race.

In week $1$ of his training he cycled $22\,$km. In week $2$ he cycled $34\,$km. This pattern continues, with him cycling an extra $12\,$km per week.

The distances Brendan cycled in the first $5$ weeks of training is shown in the following table.

![Screenshot 2023-08-31 at 2.15.24 PM](<images/Screenshot 2023-08-31 at 2.15.24 PM.png>)

1. Calculate how far he cycles in the $17$th week of his training. [3 marks]

2. In total how far has he cycled after $17$ weeks? [2 marks]

3. Find the mean distance per week he cycled over 17 weeks. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using the formula for the $n$th term of an arithmetic sequence

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        u_{17}&=22+(17-1)\times 12\\[6pt]
        u_{17}&=214
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    He cycled $214\,\text{km}$ in the $17$th week.

2.  Using the formula for the sum of $n$ terms of an arithmetic sequence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        S_{17}&=\frac{17}{2}(2\times22+(17-1)\times12)\\[6pt]
        S_{17}&=2006
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    After $17$ weeks he cycled a total of $2006\,\text{km}$.

3.  Using the formula for the mean
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        &=\frac{22+34+46+...+214}{17}\\[6pt]
        &=\frac{2006}{17}\\[6pt]
        &=118
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    His mean distance per week over the $17$ weeks of training was $118 \text{ km}$ per week.
