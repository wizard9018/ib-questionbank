# AI027

**Maximum mark: 6**

The second and the third terms of a geometric sequence are $u_2 = 3$ and
$u_3 = 6$.

1.  Find the value of $r$, the common ratio of the sequence. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $u_6$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the largest value of $n$ for which $u_n$ is less than
    $10^4$.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The common ratio is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{u_3}{u_2} \hspace{2.15em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{6}{3} \\[12pt]
    &=2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_2r^{n-2}$ with $n = 6$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_6 &= u_2r^{6-2} \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
    &= 3(2)^{6-2} \\[7pt]
    &= 48
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8.5em} u_n &< 10^4 \\[6pt]
    u_2r^{n-2} &< 10^4 \\[6pt]
    3(2)^{n-2} &< 10^4 \\[7pt]
    n &< 13.7 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence
    the largest value of $n$ such that $u_n < 10^4$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    n = 13 \hspace{2.5em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
