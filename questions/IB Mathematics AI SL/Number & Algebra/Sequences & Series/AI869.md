# AI869

**Maximum mark: 15**

Towards the end of 2004, a theatre company upgraded their auditorium and
installed new comfortable ergonomic chairs for the audience.

After the redesign, there were $20$ seats in the first row and each
subsequent row had three more seats than the previous row.

1.  If the auditorium had a total of $16$ rows, find
    1.  the total number of seats in the last row.

    2.  the total number of seats in the auditorium. [5 marks]

The auditorium reopened for performances at the start of 2005. The
average number of visitors per show during that year was $500$. In 2006,
the average number of visitors per show increased by $5\%$.

2.  Find the average number of visitors per show in 2006. [1 mark]

The average number of visitors per show continued to increase by $5\%$
each year.

3.  Determine the first year in which the total number of visitors to a
    show exceeded the seating capacity of the auditorium. [5 marks]

The theatre company hosts $25$ shows per year.

4.  Determine the total number of visitors that attended the auditorium
    from when it opened in 2005 until the end of 2011. Round your answer
    correct to the nearest integer. [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  The $n$th term formula for an arithmetic sequence is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        u_n=u_1+(n-1)d\hspace{1.5cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        We have
        $u_1=20, n=16$, and $d=3$. Hence, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        u_{16} &= 20+ (16-1)(3) \hspace{0.75cm}\\[8pt]
        &\hspace{0.3em}= 65 \text{ seats}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The formula for the sum of $n$ terms of an arithmetic sequence
        is
        $$
        S_n=\dfrac{n}{2}[2u_1+(n-1)d]

        $$
        We have $u_1=20$, $n=16$
        and $d=3$. Hence, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1cm} S_{16} &= \dfrac{16}{2}[2(20)+(16-1)3]\\[8pt]
        &\hspace{0.3em}= 680 \text{ seats}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Increasing $500$ by $5\%$, we get
    $$
    500 \times 1.05\hspace{0.7em}= 525 \text{ visitors}
    $$

3.  The $n$th term formula for a geometric sequence is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_n=&u_1r^{n-1}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We have $u_1=500$ and $r=1.05$.
    Hence, an expression for the average number of visitors, $u_n$, for
    a given year $n$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_{n} &=500 (1.05)^{n-1}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The total seating
    capacity is $680$ (part a). The graph of $u_n$ is plotted below

    ![f454f823cc38ef44e946be77438706a366c95060.svg](images/f454f823cc38ef44e946be77438706a366c95060.svg)

    From the graph, $u_n > 680$ when $n>7.3$, which corresponds to the
    $8$th year.

    Hence, the average number of visitors will exceed the total capacity
    in  $2012$.

4.  The formula for the sum of $n$ terms in a geometric sequence is
    $$
    S_n=\dfrac{u_1(r^n-1)}{r-1}

    $$
    We have
    $u_1=500\times 25 = 12\,500$, $r=1.05$ and $n=7$. Hence, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1cm} S_{7} &= \dfrac{12\,500(1.05^{7}-1)}{1.05-1}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.3em}= 101\,775 \text{ visitors}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
