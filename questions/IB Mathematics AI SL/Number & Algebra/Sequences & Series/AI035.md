# AI035

**Maximum mark: 19**

On Wednesday Eddy goes to a velodrome to train. He cycles the first lap
of the track in $25$ seconds. Each lap Eddy cycles takes him $1.6$
seconds longer than the previous lap.

1.  Find the time, in seconds, Eddy takes to cycle his tenth lap.
    [3 marks]

Eddy cycles his last lap in $55.4$ seconds.

2.  Find how many laps he has cycled on Wednesday. [3 marks]

3.  Find the **total** time, in **minutes**, cycled by Eddy on
    Wednesday. [4 marks]

On Friday Eddy brings his friend Mario to train. They both cycled the
first lap of the track in $25$ seconds. Each lap Mario cycles takes him
$1.05$ times as long as his previous lap.

4.  Find the time, in seconds, Mario takes to cycle his fifth
    lap. [3 marks]

5.  Find the **total** time, in **minutes**, Mario takes to
    cycle his first ten laps. [3 marks]

Each lap Eddy cycles again takes him $1.6$ seconds longer that his
previous lap.\
After a certain number of laps Eddy takes less time per lap than Mario.

6.  Find the number of the lap when this happens. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have an arithmetic sequence with the first term $u_1 = 25$ and
    the common difference $d = 1.6$.

    Hence, using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with
    $n = 10$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_{10} &= u_1 + (10-1)d \\[6pt]
    &= 25 + (10-1)(1.6) \\[6pt]
    &= 39.4 \text{ seconds}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Solving the equation $u_n = 55.4$ for $n$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_1 + (n-1)d &= 55.4 \hspace{10.1em} \\[6pt]
    25 + (n-1)(1.6) &= 55.4 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{8.6em} n = 20 \text{ laps} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

3.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1 + (n-1)d)$ with $n = 20$, we get

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{5.1em} S_{20} &= \dfrac{20}{2}(2u_1 + (20-1)d) \\[6pt]
     &= \dfrac{20}{2}(2(25) + (20-1)(1.6)) \\[10pt]
     &= 804 \\[14pt]
     &= 13.4 \text{ minutes}
     \end{aligned}
     $$

4.  We have a geometric sequence with the first term $v_1 = 25$ and the
    common ratio $r = 1.05$.

    Hence, using the $n$th term formula $v_n = v_1r^{n-1}$ with $n = 5$,
    we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    v_5 &= v_1r^{5-1} \\[6pt]
    &= 25(1.05)^{5-1} \\[7pt]
    &\approx 30.4 \text{ seconds}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{v_1(r^n-1)}{r-1}$
    with $n = 10$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.6em} S_{10} &= \dfrac{v_1(r^{10}-1)}{r-1} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{25((1.05)^{10}-1)}{1.05-1} \hspace{0.7em} \\[12pt]
    &\approx 314.45 \\[15pt]
    &\approx 5.24 \text{ minutes}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

6.  Solving the inequality $u_n < v_n$ for the smallest value of $n$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_1 + (n-1)d &< v_1r^{n-1} \\[8pt]
    25 + (n-1)(1.6) &< 25(1.05)^{n-1} \\[9pt]
    11.7 &< n \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    n = 12 \text{th lap} \hspace{0.7em}
    $$
