# AI802

**Maximum mark: 6**

A geometric sequence has $u_1 =5$, $u_2 = -1$ and $u_3 = \dfrac{1}{5}$.

1.  Find the common ratio, $r$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the exact value of $u_{7}$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the exact value of $S_{7}$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The common ratio is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r&= \dfrac{u_2}{u_1} \hspace{1.5em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{-1}{5} \\[6pt]
    &= -\dfrac{1}{5}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 r^{n-1}$ with $n = 7$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.35em} u_{7} &= u_1 r^{7-1} \\[6pt]
    &= 5\Big(-\dfrac{1}{5}\Big)^6 \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{3\hspace{0.15em}125}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of the first $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{u_1(1-r^n)}{1-r}$ with $n = 7$, we get

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{1.7em} S_{7} &= \dfrac{u_1(1-r^{7})}{1-r} \\[6pt]
     &= \dfrac{5\Big(1-\Big(-\dfrac{1}{5}\Big)^{7}\Big)}{1-\Big(-\dfrac{1}{5}\Big)} \\[9pt]
     &=\dfrac{13\hspace{0.15em}021}{3\hspace{0.15em}125}
     \end{aligned}
     $$
