# AI032

**Maximum mark: 12**

The sum of the first $n$ terms of an arithmetic sequence,
$S_n = u_1 + u_2 + u_3 + \dots + u_n$, is given by $S_n = 2n^2 + n$.

1.  Write down the values of $S_1$ and $S_2$. [2 marks]

2.  Write down the values of $u_1$ and $u_2$. [2 marks]

3.  Find $d$, the common difference of the sequence. [1 mark]

4.  Find $u_{10}$, the tenth term of the sequence. [2 marks]

5.  Find the greatest value of $n$, for which $u_n$ is less than $100$.
    [3 marks]

6.  Find the value of $n$, for which $S_n$ is equal to $1275$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$

    \begin{array}{rlcrl}
    S_1 &= 2(1)^2 + 1 &\text{and}& S_2 &= 2(2)^2 + 2 \\[6pt]
    &= 3 &\,\hspace{5em}\,& &= 10
    \end{array} \hspace{2.2em}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$

    \begin{array}{rlcrl}
    u_1 &= S_1 &\text{and}& u_2 &= S_2 - S_1 \\[6pt]
    &= 3 &\,\hspace{5em}\,& &=10-3 \\[6pt]
    &&&&= 7
    \end{array}
    $$

3.  The common difference is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= u_2 - u_1 \\[6pt]
    &= 7 - 3 \\[6pt]
    &= 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with $n = 10$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.35em} u_{10} &= u_1 + (10-1)d \\[6pt]
    &= 3 + (10-1)(4) \\[6pt]
    &= 39
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8.7em} u_n &< 100 \\[9pt]
    u_1 + (n-1)d &< 100 \\[9pt]
    3 + (n-1)(4) &< 100 \\[10pt]
    n &< 25.3 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence
    the greatest value of $n$ such that $u_n < 100$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    n = 25 \hspace{2.3em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

6.  Solving the equation $S_n = 1275$ for $n$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1 + (n-1)d) &= 1275 \hspace{4em} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{n}{2}(2(3) + (n-1)(4)) &= 1275 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{13.9em} n = 25 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
