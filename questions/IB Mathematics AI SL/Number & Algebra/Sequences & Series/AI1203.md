# AI1203

**Maximum mark: 5**

A hexagon ($6$-sided polygon) has interior angles which form an arithmetic progression.

![Screenshot 2026-11-03 111121](<images/Screenshot 2026-11-03 111121.JPEG>)

If the smallest angle is a positive integer, find the largest possible value of $d$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let $u_1$ be the smallest interior angle. Then the six interior angles are $u_1,\, u_1+d,\, u_1+2d,\, u_1+3d,\, u_1+4d,\, u_1+5d$.

![Screenshot 2026-11-03 111143](<images/Screenshot 2026-11-03 111143.JPEG>)

Dividing the hexagon into triangles from one vertex gives $4$ triangles. Since the interior angles of each triangle sum to $180^\circ$, the sum of the interior angles of the hexagon is

$$

\begin{aligned}

    S_{\text{hex}} &= 4 \times 180 \\[6pt]

    &= 720^\circ \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

\end{aligned}

$$

Using the **arithmetic sum formula (1.2)**

$$S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}\big(2u_1 + (n-1)d\big)$$

with $n = 6$, we have

$$

\begin{aligned}

    S_6 &= \dfrac{6}{2}\big(2u_1 + (6-1)d\big) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]

    &= 3(2u_1 + 5d)

\end{aligned}

$$

Equating this to the sum of the interior angles of the hexagon, we have

$$

\begin{aligned}

    720 &= 3(2u_1 + 5d) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]

    240 &= 2u_1 + 5d

\end{aligned}

$$

To maximise $d$, we minimise $u_1$. Since $u_1$ represents the smallest interior angle and is a positive integer, the smallest value is $u_1 = 1$.

$$

\begin{aligned}

    240 &= 2(1) + 5d \tag*{\textbf{(R1)}} \\[6pt]

    5d &= 238 \\[6pt]

    d &= \boxed{47.6} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

\end{aligned}

$$
