# AI021

**Maximum mark: 6**

A geometric sequence has $20$ terms, with the first four terms given
below.
$$
\begin{aligned}
418.5,\hspace{0.25em} 279,\hspace{0.25em} 186,\hspace{0.25em} 124,\hspace{0.25em}\dots \\
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Find $r$, the common ratio of the sequence. Give your answer as a
    fraction. [1 mark]

2.  Find $u_5$, the fifth term of the sequence. Give your answer as a
    fraction. [1 mark]

3.  Find the smallest term in the sequence that is an **integer**.
    [2 marks]

4.  Find $S_{10}$, the sum of the first $10$ terms of the sequence. Give
    your answer correct to one decimal place. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have $u_1 = 418.5$ and $u_2 = 279$.
    Hence the common ratio is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{u_2}{u_1} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{279}{418.5} \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{2}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1r^{n-1}$ with $n = 5$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_5 &= u_1r^{5-1} \\[8pt]
    &= 418.5\bigg(\dfrac{2}{3}\bigg)^{5-1} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{248}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_5 &= \dfrac{248}{3},\hspace{0.3em}u_6 = \dfrac{496}{9},\hspace{0.3em}u_7 = \dfrac{992}{27},\hspace{0.3em}\dots
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the smallest integer term of the sequence is
     $u_4 = 124$

4.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{u_1(1-r^n)}{1-r}$
    with $n = 10$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.7em} S_{10} &= \dfrac{u_1(1-r^{10})}{1-r} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{418.5(1-(2/3)^{10})}{1-2/3} \\[9pt]
    &\approx 1233.7
    \end{aligned}
    $$
