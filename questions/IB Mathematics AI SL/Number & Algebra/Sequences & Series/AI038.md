# AI038

**Maximum mark: 6**

A battalion is arranged, per row, according to an arithmetic sequence.
There are $24$ troops in the third row and $42$ troops in the sixth row.

1.  Find the first term and the common difference of this arithmetic
    sequence. [3 marks]

There are $15$ rows in the battalion.

2.  Find the **total** number of troops in the battalion.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have $u_3 = 24$ and $u_6 = 42$.
    Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with $n = 3$ and
    $n = 6$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.75em} u_3 &= u_1 + (3-1)d \\[6pt]
    24 &= u_1 + 2d \tag*{$(1)$}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.75em} u_6 &= u_1 + (6-1)d \\[6pt]
    42 &= u_1 + 5d \tag*{$(2)$}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence $(1)$ & $(2)$ are
    simultaneous equations which can be solved for $u_1$ and $d$.

    If we subtract the equation $(1)$ from the equation $(2)$ and solve
    the resulting equation for $d$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.1em} 42-24 &= (u_1 + 5d) - (u_1 + 2d) \\[6pt]
    18 &= 3d \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d = 6 \hspace{4em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    \[$\text{This could have been solved using G.D.C.}$\]

    Hence, if we substitute $d = 6$ in the equation $(1)$ and solve the
    resulting equation for $u_1$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    24 &= u_1 + 2(6) \hspace{1.5em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    u_1 = 12 \hspace{4.5em}
    $$

2.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1 + (n-1)d)$ with $n = 15$, $u_1 = 12$ and
    $d = 6$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.35em} S_{15} &= \dfrac{15}{2}(2u_1 + (15-1)d) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{15}{2}(2(12) + (15-1)(6)) \\[11pt]
    &= 810 \text{ troops}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
