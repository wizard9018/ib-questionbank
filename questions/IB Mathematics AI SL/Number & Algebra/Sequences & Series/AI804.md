# AI804

**Maximum mark: 6**

The table below shows the first four terms of three sequences: $u_n$,
$v_n$, and $w_n$.

![6896afb03e54861ed9a71ba4f129a85ea32667d8.svg](images/6896afb03e54861ed9a71ba4f129a85ea32667d8.svg)

1.  State which sequence is
    1.  arithmetic;

    2.  geometric. [2 marks]

2.  Find the exact value of the sum of the first $35$ terms of the
    arithmetic\
    sequence. [2 marks]

3.  Find the exact value of the $10$th term of the geometric sequence.
    [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.

    2.
        $w_n$

2.  The common difference of the arithmetic sequence, $u_n$, is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= u_2 - u_1 \hspace{0.75em} \\[6pt]
    &= 9 - 5 \\[6pt]
    &= 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1 + (n-1)d)$ with
    $n=35$,

    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6em} S_{35} &= \dfrac{35}{2}(2u_1 + (35-1)d) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{35}{2}(2(5) + (35-1)(4)) \\[11pt]
    &= 2\hspace{0.15em}555
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The common ratio of the geometric sequence, $w_n$, is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{w_2}{w_1} \hspace{2.4em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{9}{-3} \\[12pt]
    &= -3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the $n$th term formula
    $w_n =w_1r^{n-1}$ with $n = 10$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.1em} w_{10} &= w_1r^{10-1} \\[6pt]
    &= (-3)\hspace{0.05em}(-3)^{10-1} \hspace{3.8em} \\[7pt]
    &= 59\hspace{0.15em}049
    \end{aligned}
    $$
