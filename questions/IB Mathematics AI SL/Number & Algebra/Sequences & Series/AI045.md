# AI045

**Maximum mark: 6**

The table shows the first four terms of three sequences: $u_n$, $v_n$,
and $w_n$.

![c39694c1cf7513ffce115791e6b0f1c54c230963.svg](images/c39694c1cf7513ffce115791e6b0f1c54c230963.svg)

1.  State which sequence is
    1.  arithmetic;

    2.  geometric. [2 marks]

2.  Find the sum of the first $50$ terms of the arithmetic sequence.
    [2 marks]

3.  Find the exact value of the $13$th term of the geometric sequence.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $v_n$

    2.
        $w_n$

2.  The common difference of the arithmetic sequence is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= v_2 - v_1 \hspace{0.75em} \\[6pt]
    &= 24 - 12 \\[6pt]
    &= 12
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2v_1 + (n-1)d)$ with
    $n=50$,

    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6em} S_{50} &= \dfrac{50}{2}(2v_1 + (50-1)d) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{50}{2}(2(12) + (50-1)(12)) \\[11pt]
    &= 15\hspace{0.15em}300
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The common ratio of the geometric sequence is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{w_2}{w_1} \hspace{2.4em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{24}{12} \\[12pt]
    &= 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the $n$th term formula
    $w_n = u_1r^{n-1}$ with $n = 13$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.1em} w_{13} &= u_1r^{13-1} \\[6pt]
    &= 12\hspace{0.05em}(2)^{13-1} \hspace{3.8em} \\[7pt]
    &= 49\hspace{0.15em}152
    \end{aligned}
    $$
