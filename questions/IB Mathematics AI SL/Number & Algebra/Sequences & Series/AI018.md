# AI018

**Maximum mark: 6**

A tennis ball bounces on the ground $n$ times. The heights of the
bounces, $h_1, h_2, h_3, \dots,h_n,$ form a geometric sequence. The
height that the ball bounces the first time, $h_1$, is
$80$ cm, and the second time, $h_2$, is
$60$ cm.

1.  Find the value of the common ratio for the sequence. [2 marks]

2.  Find the height that the ball bounces the tenth time, $h_{10}$.
    [2 marks]

3.  Find the **total distance** travelled by the ball during the first
    six bounces (up and down). Give your answer correct to $2$ decimal
    places. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The common ratio is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{h_2}{h_1} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{60}{80} \\[12pt]
    &= 0.75 \hspace{2.75em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $h_n = h_1r^{n-1}$ with $n = 10$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h_{10} &= h_1r^{10-1} \\[6pt]
    &= 80(0.75)^{10-1} \\[7pt]
    &\approx 6.01\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{h_1(1-r^n)}{1-r}$
    with $n=6$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{total distance} &= 2S_6 \\[12pt]
    &= 2\bigg[\dfrac{h_1(1-r^6)}{1-r}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= 2\bigg[\dfrac{80(1-0.75^6)}{1-0.75}\bigg] \hspace{2.4em} \\[12pt]
    &\approx 526.09\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
