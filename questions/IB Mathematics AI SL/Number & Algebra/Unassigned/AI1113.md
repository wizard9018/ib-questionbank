# AI1113

**Maximum mark: 6**

An owl takes off from from a tree branch and flies higher into the sky. Its height above the ground after $t$ seconds, where $t\geq 0$, is given by $s(t) = at^3 + bt^2 + ct+d$, where $a,b,c,d \in \mathbb{R}$ and $s(t)$ is measured in metres.

![owl](images/owl.png)

Initially the owl is $12\,$ metres above the ground.

1. Write down the value of $d$. [1 mark]

After $1$ second, the owl is $19.8$ m above the ground; after $2$ seconds, $34.5$ m; after $4$ seconds, $22.8$ m.

2.  1. Write down a system of three linear equations in terms of $a$, $b$ and $c$.

    2. Hence find the values of $a$, $b$ and $c$.  [3 marks]

After some time the owl reaches a maximum height. At this time it spots some prey at ground level and then immediately swoops down to catch it.

3.  1. Find the maximum height of the owl above the ground as it spots the prey.

    2. Find the time it catches the prey. [2 marks]

1.  $d=12$ m
$$
\begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
\end{aligned}
$$

2.  1.   We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \begin{array}{rcccccccl}
    a &+& b &+& c &+& 12&=& 19.8 \\[3pt]
    8a &+& 4b &+& 2c &+& 12&=& 34.5 \\[3pt]
    64a &+& 16b &+& 4c &+& 12&=& 22.8
    \end{array}
    \tag*{\textbf{M1A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    2. Using a G.D.C. we can find the values of the parameters, $a=-2.575$, $b=11.175$ and $c=-0.8$.\\[6pt]
These values can be rounded to three significant figures giving
       $a = -2.58, \hspace{0.25em} b = 11.2, \hspace{0.25em} c = -0.8 $
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  1.  The coordinates of the local maximum of when the owl spots the prey is $(2.857..., 40.96...)$.

        The $y-$coordinate of this local maximum gives the height of the owl as it spots the prey.\\[6pt]
        Hence the owl is at a height of $41.0\,\text{m}$.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
            \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The owl catches the prey when the height of the owl is zero, i.e. $s(t)=0$ when $t>0$. This can be found with a G.D.C., $(4.501..., 0)$. With the $x-$coordinate being the time.

        Hence the owl catches the prey at $t=4.50\,\text{s}$.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
            \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
