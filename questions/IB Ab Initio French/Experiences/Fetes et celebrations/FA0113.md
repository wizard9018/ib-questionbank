**Vous allez entendre Chlo$\textbf{\'{e}}$ parler de la F$\textbf{\^{e}}$te nationale de son pays.**

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(le) 21 juillet}$    [1 mark]

* Chlo$\text{\'{e}}$ states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le 21 juillet dernier, j'ai c$\text{\'{e}}$l$\text{\'{e}}$br$\text{\'{e}}$ la F$\text{\^{e}}$te Nationale avec toute ma famille $\text{\`{a}}$ Bruxelles, en Belgique." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Last July 21st, I celebrated National Day with all my family in Brussels, Belgium.") The date*le 21 juillet*answers when Chlo$\text{\'{e}}$ celebrated National Day.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(en) Belgique}$    [1 mark]

* Chlo$\text{\'{e}}$ states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... $\text{\`{a}}$ Bruxelles, en Belgique." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$... in Brussels, Belgium.") This confirms that Chlo$\text{\'{e}}$ lives in Belgium.

$3 - 5.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les r$\textit{\'{e}}$ponses peuvent $\textit{\^{e}}$tre $\textit{\'{e}}$crites dans n'importe quel ordre.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, D}$        [3 marks]

* **A**: Chlo$\text{\'{e}}$ mentions: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... avec toute ma famille..." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$... with all my family...") This shows that Chlo$\text{\'{e}}$ took part in the celebration with her family.

* **C**: Chlo$\text{\'{e}}$ adds: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$... des animations pour les enfants..." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$...activities for children...") This indicates that activities were planned for young people.

* **D**: Chlo$\text{\'{e}}$ explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mon fr$\text{\`{e}}$re et moi avons go$\text{\^{u}}$t$\text{\'{e}}$ une gaufre belge avant de regarder le feu d'artifice le soir." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$My brother and I tried a Belgian waffle before watching the fireworks in the evening.") This shows that Chlo$\text{\'{e}}$ tried a local specialty before the fireworks.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, E, F

* **B** : *Les repr$\textit{\'{e}}$sentations ont commenc$\textit{\'{e}}$ seulement dans la soir$\textit{\'{e}}$e* (*The performances only started in the evening*) is not correct because Chlo$\text{\'{e}}$ explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Pendant toute la journ$\text{\'{e}}$e, il y avait des concerts, des animations pour les enfants et des spectacles de rue." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Throughout the day, there were concerts, activities for children, and street performances.") Performances took place all day, not only in the evening.

* **E** : *Les rues $\textit{\'{e}}$taient presque vides pendant la c$\textit{\'{e}}$l$\textit{\'{e}}$bration* (*The streets were almost empty during the celebration*) is not correct because Chlo$\text{\'{e}}$ states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ce que j'ai pr$\text{\'{e}}$f$\text{\'{e}}$r$\text{\'{e}}$, c'$\text{\'{e}}$tait l'ambiance dans les rues, parce que tout le monde $\text{\'{e}}$tait souriant et partageait ce moment ensemble." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$What I preferred was the atmosphere in the streets, because everyone was smiling and sharing this moment together.") The streets were full of people enjoying the celebration together, not empty.

* **F** : *Cette f$\textit{\^{e}}$te lui a appris l'histoire de son pays* (*This celebration taught her the history of her country*) is not correct because Chlo$\text{\'{e}}$ concludes: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cette f$\text{\^{e}}$te m'a permis de mieux conna$\text{\^{\i}}$tre les traditions de mon pays." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$This celebration helped me get to know my country's traditions better.") The celebration helped her learn about traditions, not history.
