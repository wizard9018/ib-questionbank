## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les r$\textit{\'{e}}$ponses peuvent $\textit{\^{e}}$tre $\textit{\'{e}}$crites dans n'importe quel ordre.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, E}$        [3 marks]

* **A**: Bintou says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je suis all$\text{\'{e}}$e au carnaval de Martinique avec mes cousins." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I went to the Martinique carnival with my cousins.") The expression*avec mes cousins* (*with my cousins*) shows that she attended the celebration with members of her family.

* **C**: Bintou says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'ai $\text{\'{e}}$t$\text{\'{e}}$ impressionn$\text{\'{e}}$e par les couleurs et l'$\text{\'{e}}$nergie des danseurs" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I was impressed by the colours and the energy of the dancers"). The word*impressionn$\textit{\'{e}}$e* shows that she reacted very positively to what she saw. The phrase *l'$\textit{\'{e}}$nergie des danseurs* expresses the same idea as *l'enthousiasme des artistes*, because both describe the excitement and positive energy shown during the performance.

* **E**: Bintou says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{m\^{e}me}$ s'il faisait tr$\text{\`{e}}$s chaud." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$even though it was very hot.") The expression*la temp$\textit{\'{e}}$rature $\textit{\'{e}}$tait $\textit{\'{e}}$lev$\textit{\'{e}}$e* (*the temperature was high*) corresponds to the weather being hot.

* *Options incorrectes :*  B, D, F

* **B** : Bintou says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$il y avait de la musique et des costumes partout dans les rues." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$there was music and costumes everywhere in the streets.") This shows that the streets were lively and animated, not calm.

* **D**: Bintou says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nous avons... go$\text{\^{u}}$t$\text{\'{e}}$ des plats traditionnels d$\text{\'{e}}$licieux" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we tasted delicious traditional dishes"). The word*d$\textit{\'{e}}$licieux* shows that she enjoyed the local food.

* **F** : Bintou says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il y avait beaucoup de monde dans le centre-ville." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$There were a lot of people in the city centre.") The phrase *beaucoup de monde* shows that the city centre was crowded. The expression *peu de* means *not many* or *only a small number of*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{joyeuse}$    [1 mark]

* Bintou says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$l'ambiance $\text{\'{e}}$tait vraiment joyeuse." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the atmosphere was really joyful.") The adjective*joyeuse* (*joyful*) directly describes the atmosphere during the celebration.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{la culture martiniquaise}$    [1 mark]

* Bintou says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je pense que ce carnaval est important parce qu'il permet de d$\text{\'{e}}$couvrir la culture martiniquaise" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I think this carnival is important because it allows people to discover Martinican culture”). The verb*d$\textit{\'{e}}$couvrir* means to learn about or become familiar with something for the first time, so this shows that the event helps people better understand Martinican culture.
