## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les r$\textit{\'{e}}$ponses peuvent $\textit{\^{e}}$tre $\textit{\'{e}}$crites dans n'importe quel ordre.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, D, E}$        [3 marks]

* **A** : Sophie says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je cherche un costume pour participer au Carnaval de Nice en France." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am looking for a costume to take part in the Nice Carnival in France.") The expression *prendre part $\textit{\`{a}}$ une c$\textit{\'{e}}$l$\textit{\'{e}}$bration fran$\textit{\c{c}}$aise* (*to take part in a French celebration*) has the same meaning as *participer au Carnaval de Nice en France* in the audio.

* **D** : Sophie says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je veux simplement profiter de l'ambiance et passer du temps avec mes amis." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I simply want to enjoy the atmosphere and spend time with my friends.") The expression *avec ses proches* (*with people close to her*) has a similar meaning to *avec mes amis* (*with my friends*).

* **E**: Sophie says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les d$\text{\'{e}}$fil$\text{\'{e}}$s durent longtemps." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$The parades last a long time.") The expression*prennent beaucoup de temps* (*take a lot of time*) has the same meaning as *durent longtemps* in the audio.

* *Options incorrectes :*  B, C, F

* **B**: Sophie says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$quelque chose de color$\text{\'{e}}$ et d'original" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$something colourful and original"), so Sophie is not looking for a dark and simple outfit.

* **C** : Sophie says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mon objectif n'est pas de gagner un concours" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$My goal is not to win a competition"), so she does not want to be first in a competition.

* **F** : Sophie says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$pas trop cher" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$not too expensive"), so she does not want a very costly outfit.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{danser}$    [1 mark]

* Sophie says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Moi, j'aime surtout danser pendant cet $\text{\'{e}}$v$\text{\'{e}}$nement." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Personally, what I enjoy most is dancing during this event.") The verb*danser* (*to dance*) directly identifies the activity she enjoys during the carnival.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{sociable}$    [1 mark]

* Sophie says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je suis une personne sociable." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am a sociable person.") The adjective *sociable* (*sociable*) directly describes her personality.
