$\underline{\textrm{Marking Criteria Feedback}}$

**Criterion A (Language)**:

* Use a range of present and conditional structures to express ideas clearly: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$on a une super id$\text{\'{e}}$e", $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ce serait une excellente occasion", $\text{\textquotedblleft}$on a vraiment besoin de vous".

* Use infinitive structures: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$participer $\text{\`{a}}$ l'organisation", $\text{\textquotedblleft}$aider $\text{\`{a}}$ d$\text{\'{e}}$corer", $\text{\textquotedblleft}$organiser des activit$\text{\'{e}}$s".

* Use *afin que*+ subjunctive to express a purpose: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$organiser des activit$\text{\'{e}}$s afin que tout le monde profite de la soir$\text{\'{e}}$e".

* Use *contribuer $\textit{\`{a}}$*+ infinitive to express contribution: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$contribuer $\text{\`{a}}$ rendre cet $\text{\'{e}}$v$\text{\'{e}}$nement unique".

* Common errors include forgetting *$\textit{\`{a}}$* in key structures such as *participer $\textit{\`{a}}$* or *aider $\textit{\`{a}}$* and using incorrect infinitive structures after modal verbs (*vous pouvez aider d$\textit{\'{e}}$corer* instead of *vous pouvez aider $\textit{\`{a}}$ d$\textit{\'{e}}$corer*).

**Criterion B (Message)**:

* A good answer covers three content points:\

  $1.\hspace{1em}$presenting the idea (organising a school ball)\

  $2.\hspace{1em}$explaining why students should get involved\

  $3.\hspace{1em}$suggesting how to participate.

* The model answer clearly develops all three: (idea → $\text{\textquotedblleft}$organiser un bal... excellente occasion de c$\text{\'{e}}$l$\text{\'{e}}$brer...”; motivation → $\text{\textquotedblleft}$montrer votre cr$\text{\'{e}}$ativit$\text{\'{e}}$... travailler en $\text{\'{e}}$quipe... mieux se conna$\text{\^{\i}}$tre”; action → $\text{\textquotedblleft}$choix de la musique... d$\text{\'{e}}$corer la salle... organiser des activit$\text{\'{e}}$s... rendez-vous...”).

* Use *et* and *aussi* to add ideas, cependant to show contrast, *en plus* to add information, and *ou* to express an alternative.

* Weak answers only mention the idea without explaining why it is important, or give very vague suggestions without concrete examples.

**Criterion C (Conceptual understanding**):

* **Message publi$\textbf{\'{e}}$ dans les m$\textbf{\'{e}}$dias sociaux** is the appropriate text type. The purpose is to engage and motivate a large group of students quickly. A strong response includes all the conventions of a social media post, as in the model answer: a title (*Le bal de fin d'ann$\textit{\'{e}}$e*), a date and time reference (*18h00*), a username (*@bal\_lycee*), an opening salutation (*Salut !*), a message body presenting the idea and motivating students, a question to the recipient (*Alors, qui veut participer ?*), a comments section (*Laissez un commentaire !*), and hashtags (*#bal #lyc$\textit{\'{e}}$e #projet*) (optional).

* **Discours** is generally appropriate - it can motivate a group, but it requires a live speaking situation and a more structured oral format, which is not clearly implied here.

* **Journal intime** is generally inappropriate - it is a private text and does not match the purpose of communicating with and motivating a group.
