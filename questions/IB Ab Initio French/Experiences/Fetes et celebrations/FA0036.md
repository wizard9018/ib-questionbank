## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{le Festival au Désert}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1,  the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mon festival $\textrm{pr\'ef\'er\'e}$ est le Festival au $\textrm{D\'esert}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$My favourite festival is the Festival au $\textrm{D\'esert}$.") The adjective *$\textit{pr\'ef\'er\'e}$* (*favourite*) has the same meaning as *favorite* (*favourite*) used in the question, confirming that *le Festival au $\textit{D\'esert}$* is the answer.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{le désert}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 2, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ce festival a lieu dans le $\textrm{d\'esert}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$This festival takes place in the desert.") The expression *avoir lieu* (*to take place*) confirms the location of the event. The word *dans* (*in*) followed by *le $\textit{d\'esert}$* (*the desert*) directly identifies where it takes place.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{la musique traditionnelle et moderne}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 2, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Les gens viennent $\textrm{\'ecouter}$ de la musique traditionnelle et moderne." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$People come to listen to traditional and modern music.") The verb *viennent* (*come*) followed by the infinitive *$\textit{\'ecouter}$* (*to listen*) confirms the reason why visitors travel to the festival. The word *et* (*and*) shows that both types of music attract them.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(il y a) des concerts sous les étoiles}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 4, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le soir, il y a des concerts sous les $\textrm{\'etoiles}$." The time reference *le soir* (*in the evening*) confirms this happens when it gets dark, and the expression *sous les $\textit{\'etoiles}$* (*under the stars*) reinforces the night-time setting.

- In paragraph 6,  the text explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Pour moi, ce festival est $\textrm{sp\'ecial}$ parce qu'il montre la $\textrm{diversit\'e}$ culturelle du Mali." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$For me, this festival is special because it shows the cultural diversity of Mali.") The word *parce que* (*because*) directly introduces the reason why the festival is important to Awa. The expression *$\textit{diversit\'e}$ culturelle* (*cultural diversity*) confirms what makes it meaningful for her.
