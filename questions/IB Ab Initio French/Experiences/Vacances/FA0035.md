## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The word *$\textit{m\'emorable}$* is correct because in paragraph 2, the text describes the stay as $\text{\textquotedblleft}$un $\textrm{s\'ejour}$ inoubliable aux Seychelles." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$an unforgettable stay in the Seychelles.") The word *inoubliable* describes something that leaves a strong impression and cannot easily be forgotten, and *$\textit{m\'emorable}$* (*memorable*) expresses the same idea.

- *Option incorrecte :* D

- **D**: *Impressionnant* (*impressive*) is not correct because it describes something that has a strong immediate impact. In the text, *inoubliable* specifically refers to something that stays in the memory over time, which goes beyond simply being impressive.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- The word *vaste* is correct because in paragraph 2, the text mentions $\text{\textquotedblleft}$un $\textrm{h\'ebergement}$ spacieux dans un $\textrm{h\^otel}$ confortable." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$a spacious accommodation in a comfortable hotel.") The word *spacieux* describes a place with a lot of space, and *vaste* (*large/spacious*) expresses the same idea.

- *Option incorrecte :* A

- **A**: *Confortable* (*comfortable*) is not correct because it refers to the level of comfort rather than the size of a space. In the text, *spacieux* specifically describes how much space the accommodation offers, not how comfortable it is.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The word *$\textit{passionn\'e}$* is correct because in paragraph 2, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Que vous soyez amateur de $\textrm{d\'etente}$ ou d'aventure." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Whether you are a lover of relaxation or adventure.") In this context, *amateur* describes someone who enjoys and has a strong interest in an activity, and *$\textit{passionn\'e}$* (*passionate/enthusiastic*) expresses the same idea.

- *Option incorrecte :* F

- **F**: *$\textit{D\'ebutant}$* (*beginner*) is not correct because it refers to someone who lacks experience or skills. In the text, *amateur* describes someone who enjoys or is interested in an activity, not someone who is inexperienced at it.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{aux Seychelles}$    [1 mark]
