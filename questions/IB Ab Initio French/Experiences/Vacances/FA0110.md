## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Nice}$    [1 mark]

- Jean-Michel says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je suis $\textrm{all\'e}$ $\textrm{\`a}$ Nice." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I went to Nice.") The expression *s'est rendu $\textit{\`a}$* (*went to*) is equivalent to *je suis $\textit{all\'e}$ $\textit{\`a}$*. 

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{touristes}$    [1 mark]

- Jean-Michel says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$il y avait trop de touristes et les endroits $\textrm{\'etaient}$ souvent $\textrm{tr\`es}$ bruyants." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$there were too many tourists and the places were often very noisy.") The noun *touristes* confirms what made the places unpleasant.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{bruyants}$    [1 mark]

- Jean-Michel says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les endroits $\textrm{\'etaient}$ souvent $\textrm{tr\`es}$ bruyants." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the places were often very noisy.") The adjective *bruyants* describes how the locations felt during his stay.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.*  

- $\textrm{(les restaurants étaient) chers}$

- $\textrm{le service n'était pas toujours rapide}$        [2 marks]

- Jean-Michel says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les restaurants $\textrm{\'etaient}$ chers et le service $\textrm{n'\'etait}$ pas toujours rapide." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the restaurants were sometimes expensive and the service was not always quick.") The word *et* lists two different difficulties he encountered in restaurants.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{il faisait très chaud}$    [1 mark]

- Jean-Michel says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$il faisait $\textrm{tr\`es}$ chaud, ce qui rendait les visites un peu difficiles." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it was very hot, which made the visits a little difficult.") The expression *il faisait $\textit{tr\`es}$ chaud* identifies the weather condition he experienced.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{intéressante mais fatigante}$    [1 mark]

- Jean-Michel says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$c'est une destination $\textrm{int\'eressante}$ mais fatigante." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it is an interesting destination but tiring.") The two adjectives *$\textit{int\'eressante}$* and *fatigante* together describe how he found the trip.
