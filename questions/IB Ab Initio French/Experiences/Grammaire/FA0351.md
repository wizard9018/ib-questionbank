# FA0351

**Complete the sentences with the correct time expression. There are more options than gaps.**

Nous sommes $\text{arriv\'{e}s}$ $\text{\`{a}}$ la plage $\text{\`{a}}$ 10 h et nous y sommes toujours. Nous sommes $\text{\`{a}}$ la plage **\[-1-]** ce matin.    [1 mark]

Notre visite $\text{guid\'{e}e}$ commence $\text{\`{a}}$ 11 h, mais nous arrivons $\text{\`{a}}$ 10 h 45. Nous arrivons quinze minutes **\[-2-]** le $\text{d\'{e}but}$ de la visite.    [1 mark]

La $\text{soir\'{e}e}$ au camping commence $\text{\`{a}}$ 20 h. Elle continue **\[-3-]** minuit.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$de

* B.$\hspace{1em}$depuis

* C.$\hspace{1em}$avant

* D.$\hspace{1em}\text{apr\`{e}s}$

* E.$\hspace{1em}\text{jusqu'\`{a}}$

Le spectacle a lieu **\[-4-]**. $\text{\`{A}}$ 12:00, c'$\text{est-\`{a}-dire}$ **\[-5-]**, nous $\text{d\'{e}gustons}$ des $\text{sp\'{e}cialit\'{e}s}$ locales. Les $\text{festivit\'{e}s}$ continuent toute la $\text{journ\'{e}e}$ et se terminent $\text{\`{a}}$ 00:00, c'$\text{est-\`{a}-dire}$ **\[-6-]**.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* Use *depuis* with the present tense for a situation that began in the past and is still going on: we arrived this morning and are still at the beach.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D, E

* **A** : *De* gives a starting point.

* **C** : *Avant* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$before”

* **D** : *$\textit{Apr\`{e}s}$* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$after”.

* **E** : *$\textit{Jusqu'\`{a}}$* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$until”.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *Avant* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$before”: 10:45 is fifteen minutes before 11:00, when the guided tour begins.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D, E

* **A** : *De* gives a starting point.

* **B** : *Depuis* is used for something that started in the past and is still going on.

* **D** : *$\textit{Apr\`{e}s}$* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$after”, but we arrive before the tour begins.

* **E** : *$\textit{Jusqu'\`{a}}$* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$until”.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

* *$\textit{Jusqu'\`{a}}$* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$until”: the evening at the campsite continues until midnight.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C, D

* **A** : *De* gives a starting point.

* **B** : *Depuis* is used for something that started in the past and is still going on.

* **C** : *Avant* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$before”.

* **D** : *$\textit{Apr\`{e}s}$* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$after”.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *Le matin* is correct because the dance show starts at half past nine in the morning.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D

* **A** : *$\textit{\`{A}}$ minuit* means at midnight, but the show starts at half past nine in the morning.

* **B** : *$\textit{\`{A}}$ midi* means at noon, but the show starts at half past nine in the morning.

* **D** : *L'$\textit{apr\`{e}s-midi}$* means in the afternoon, but the show starts at half past nine in the morning.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* *$\textit{\`{A}}$ midi* is correct because we taste local specialities at noon.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D

* **A** : *$\textit{\`{A}}$ minuit* means at midnight, but we taste local specialities at noon.

* **C** : *Le matin* means in the morning, but we taste local specialities at noon.

* **D** : *L'$\textit{apr\`{e}s-midi}$* means in the afternoon, but we taste local specialities at noon, which is exactly 12:00.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *$\textit{\`{A}}$ minuit* is correct because the festivities end at midnight.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C, D

* **B** : *$\textit{\`{A}}$ midi* means at noon, but the festivities end at midnight.

* **C** : *Le matin* means in the morning, but the festivities end at midnight.

* **D** : *L'$\textit{apr\`{e}s-midi}$* means in the afternoon, but the festivities end at midnight.
