## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{rest\'{e}s}$    [1 mark]

* *Mes amis* refers to *ils*, so the past participle *$\textit{rest\'{e}s}$* must agree with the masculine plural subject.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{rentr\'{e}e}$    [1 mark]

* *Ma soeur* refers to *elle*, so the past participle *$\textit{rentr\'{e}e}$* must agree with the feminine singular subject.\
