# FA0309

**Complete the sentences. Choose the correct form of the verb in the perfect tense : *avons $\textbf{\textit{visit\'{e}}}$*, *a pris*, *ai $\textbf{\textit{regard\'{e}}}$*.**

$\hspace{1em}$Hier, j'**\[-1-]** un film $\text{fran\c{c}ais}$.

Samedi dernier, nous **\[-2-]** un $\text{mus\'{e}e}$ $\text{\`{a}}$ Paris.    [2 marks]

$\hspace{1em}$Pour aller au centre-ville, $\text{L\'{e}a}$ **\[-3-]** le bus.    [1 mark]

**Choose the correct form of the verb in the perfect tense.**

$\hspace{1em}$La semaine $\text{derni\`{e}re}$, tu **\[-4-]** beaucoup de photos.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$as pris

* B.$\hspace{1em}$a pris

* C.$\hspace{1em}$avons pris

* D.$\hspace{1em}$avez pris

$\hspace{1em}$Mes amis **\[-5-]** un $\text{tr\`{e}s}$ bon repas au restaurant.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$ont $\text{mang\'{e}}$

* B.$\hspace{1em}$avez $\text{mang\'{e}}$

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ai regard\'{e}}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *je* requires the *je* form of *avoir* in the present: *ai*. The past participle of *regarder* is *$\textit{regard\'{e}}$*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{avons visit\'{e}}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *nous* requires the *nous* form of *avoir*: *avons*. The past participle of *visiter* is *$\textit{visit\'{e}}$*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{a pris}$    [1 mark]

* *$\textit{L\'{e}a}$* refers to *elle*, so the correct form of *avoir* is *a*. The past participle of *prendre* is *pris*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *As pris* is the correct perfect tense form of *prendre* for *tu*.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C, D

* **B** : *A pris* is the *il/elle/on* form, not the *tu* form.

* **C** : *Avons pris* is the *nous* form, not the *tu* form.

* **D** : *Avez pris* is the *vous* form, not the *tu* form.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *Mes amis* refers to *ils*, so the correct perfect tense form of *manger* is *ont $\textit{mang\'{e}}$*.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C, D

* **B** : *Avez $\textit{mang\'{e}}$* is the *vous* form, not the *ils* form.

* **C** : *A $\textit{mang\'{e}}$* is the *il/elle/on* form, not the *ils* form.

* **D** : *Avons $\textit{mang\'{e}}$* is the *nous* form, not the *ils* form.

**Grammar Reminder**

The perfect tense (*$\textit{pass\'{e}}$ $\textit{compos\'{e}}$*) is used for completed actions in the past.

\

With *avoir*, the structure is: subject + *avoir* in the present + past participle.

| $\hspace{1em}$Subject pronoun$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$Avoir$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$Example: Visiter$\hspace{1.5em}$ |

| :- | :- | :- |

| $\hspace{1em}$J' | $\hspace{1em}$ai | $\hspace{1em}$j'ai $\text{visit\'{e}}$ |

| $\hspace{1em}$Tu | $\hspace{1em}$as | $\hspace{1em}$tu as $\text{visit\'{e}}$ |

| $\hspace{1em}$Il/elle/on | $\hspace{1em}$a | $\hspace{1em}$elle a $\text{visit\'{e}}$ |

| $\hspace{1em}$Nous | $\hspace{1em}$avons | $\hspace{1em}$nous avons $\text{visit\'{e}}$ |

| $\hspace{1em}$Vous | $\hspace{1em}$avez | $\hspace{1em}$vous avez $\text{visit\'{e}}$ |

| $\hspace{1em}$Ils/elles$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ont | $\hspace{1em}$ils ont $\text{visit\'{e}}$ |

For many regular verbs, the past participle is built like this:

* verbs ending in -er → $\text{-\'{e}}$: visiter → $\text{visit\'{e}}$

* verbs ending in -ir → often -i: finir → fini

* verbs ending in -re → often -u: vendre → vendu

Some common verbs have irregular past participles:

* avoir (to have) → eu

* faire (to do/make) → fait

* prendre (to take) → pris

* voir (to see) → vu

* lire (to read) → lu

* $\text{\'{e}crire}$ (to write) → $\text{\'{e}crit}$

* boire (to drink) → bu

* mettre (to put) → mis
