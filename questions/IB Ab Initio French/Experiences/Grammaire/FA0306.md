# FA0306

**The sentences below each contain one error related to *aller + infinitif*. Find the error and write the correct form.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Demain, tu allons visiter le centre-ville.    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Mon $\text{fr\`{e}re}$ va visite le $\text{mus\'{e}e}$ demain.    [1 mark]

:br**Put the words in the correct order. Write the complete sentence.**

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$vais / ce week-end / je / voyager / en train    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$va / Manon / $\text{diff\'{e}rentes}$ cultures / $\text{d\'{e}couvrir}$    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$vas / une excursion ? / tu / faire     [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{vas}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is *tu*, so the correct form is *vas*, not *allons*. The word *allons* is the *nous* form.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Demain, tu vas visiter le centre-ville.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{visiter}$    [1 mark]

* After *va*, the next verb stays in the infinitive. Therefore, *visite* changes to visiter.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Mon $\text{fr\`{e}re}$ va visiter le $\text{mus\'{e}e}$ demain.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Ce week-end, je vais voyager en train. / Je vais voyager en train ce week-end.      [1 mark]

* All answers are correct. The time expression *ce week-end* can appear in different positions in the sentence. The words *vais voyager* stay together because they follow the structure *aller + infinitif*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Manon va $\text{d\'{e}couvrir}$ $\text{diff\'{e}rentes}$ cultures.     [1 mark]

* The subject Manon comes before the verb phrase *va $\textit{d\'{e}couvrir}$*. The words *va $\textit{d\'{e}couvrir}$* stay together because they follow the structure *aller + infinitif*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Tu vas faire une excursion ? / Vas-tu faire une excursion ?     [1 mark]

* Both answers are correct. In French questions, the subject *tu* can appear before the verb, as an intonation question, or after it with a hyphen in an inversion question: *vas-tu*. The verb *faire* stays in the infinitive after *vas*.
