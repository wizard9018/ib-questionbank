# FA0314

**Complete the sentences. Use the correct perfect tense form of the verbs in brackets.**

$\hspace{1em}$Pendant les vacances, $\text{Chlo\'{e}}$ **\[-1-]** *(rester)* trois jours chez sa tante.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Hier matin, mes amies **\[-2-]** *(entrer)* dans le $\text{mus\'{e}e}$ $\text{\`{a}}$ dix heures.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Hier, Julie **\[-3-]** *(tomber)* pendant une promenade.    [1 mark]

**Put the words in the correct order. Write the complete sentence.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$est / $\text{apr\`{e}s}$ le concert / Paul / $\text{rentr\'{e}}$ / tard    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$en 2008 / est $\text{n\'{e}e}$ / ma cousine / $\text{\`{a}}$ $\text{Gen\`{e}ve}$    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{est rest\'{e}e}$    [1 mark]

* *$\textit{Chlo\'{e}}$* refers to *elle*, so the correct form of *$\textit{\^{e}tre}$* is *est*. The past participle *$\textit{rest\'{e}e}$* agrees with the feminine singular subject.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{sont entr\'{e}es}$    [1 mark]

* *Mes amies* refers to *elles*, so the correct form of *$\textit{\^{e}tre}$* is *sont*. The past participle *$\textit{entr\'{e}es}$* agrees with the feminine plural subject.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{est tomb\'{e}e}$    [1 mark]

* *Julie* refers to *elle*, so the correct form of *$\textit{\^{e}tre}$* is *est*. The past participle *$\textit{tomb\'{e}e}$* agrees with the feminine singular subject.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Paul est $\text{rentr\'{e}}$ tard $\text{apr\`{e}s}$ le concert. / $\text{Apr\`{e}s}$ le concert, Paul est $\text{rentr\'{e}}$ tard.     [1 mark]

* *Paul* is the subject, followed by the verb phrase *est $\textit{rentr\'{e}}$*. The past participle *$\textit{rentr\'{e}}$* agrees with the masculine singular subject. The time expression *$\textit{apr\`{e}s}$ le concert* can appear at the beginning or at the end of the sentence.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Ma cousine est $\text{n\'{e}e}$ $\text{\`{a}}$ $\text{Gen\`{e}ve}$ en 2008. / En 2008, ma cousine est $\text{n\'{e}e}$ $\text{\`{a}}$ $\text{Gen\`{e}ve}$.     [1 mark]

* *Ma cousine* is the subject, followed by the verb phrase *est $\textit{n\'{e}e}$*. The past participle of *$\textit{na\^{\i}tre}$* is *$\textit{n\'{e}}$*, and it becomes *$\textit{n\'{e}e}$* to agree with the feminine singular subject. The time expression *en 2008* can appear at the beginning or at the end of the sentence.
