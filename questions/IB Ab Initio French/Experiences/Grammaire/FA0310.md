# FA0310

**The sentences below each contain one error related to the perfect tense. Find the error and write the correct form.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Mes parents ont prend beaucoup de photos pendant le voyage.    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Nous avons fais une promenade dans le centre historique.    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Elle a $\text{\'{e}crire}$ des cartes postales à tous ses amis.    [1 mark]

**Put the words in the correct order. Write the complete sentence.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$avons / samedi dernier / un concert / vu / nous    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$une carte postale / a $\text{\'{e}crit}$ / pendant ses vacances / Sophie    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ont pris}$    [1 mark]

* The subject mes parents refers to *ils*. The correct past participle of *prendre* is *pris*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{avons fait}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *nous* requires *avons*. The past participle of *faire* is *fait*, not *fais*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{a \'{e}crit}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *elle* refers to one person. The correct past participle of *$\textit{\'{e}crire}$* is *$\textit{\'{e}crit}$*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Nous avons vu un concert samedi dernier. / Samedi dernier, nous avons vu un concert.     [1 mark]

* The subject *nou*s comes before the verb phrase *avons vu*. The form *avons* is the correct form of *avoir* for *nous*, and *vu* is the past participle of *voir*. The time expression *samedi dernier* can appear at the beginning or at the end of the sentence without changing the meaning.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Sophie a $\text{\'{e}crit}$ une carte postale pendant ses vacances. / Pendant ses vacances, Sophie a $\text{\'{e}crit}$ une carte\

$\phantom{4.}$ $\hspace{1em}$postale.     [1 mark]

* *Sophie* is the subject, so it comes before the verb phrase *a $\textit{\'{e}crit}$*. The form *a* is the correct form of *avoir* for Sophie, and *$\textit{\'{e}crit}$* is the past participle of *$\textit{\'{e}crire}$*. The phrase *pendant ses vacances* indicates when the action happened and can appear at the beginning or at the end of the sentence.
