## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* Use *depuis* with the present tense for an action or situation that began in the past and continues in the present.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C, D, E

* **B** : *Dans* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$in” for something that will happen later, but we are already waiting.

* **C** : *Pendant* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$for” for a completed period of time, but we are still waiting.

* **D** : *Il y a* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ago” for a moment in the past, but we are still waiting.

* **E** : *Au bout de* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$after” a period has passed, but we are still waiting.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* Use *dans* to indicate how much time remains before a future action.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D, E

* **A** : *Depuis* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$since” or $\text{\textquotedblleft}$for” for something still going on, but the boat has not left yet.

* **C** : *Pendant* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$for” for a completed period of time, but the boat leaves in ten minutes.

* **D** : *Il y a* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ago” for something in the past, but the boat leaves later.

* **E** : *Au bout de* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$after” a period has passed; it does not indicate how long until a future event.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* Use *pendant* to indicate the length of the completed visit.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D, E

* **A** : *Depuis* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$since” or $\text{\textquotedblleft}$for” for something still going on, but the visit ended at four o'clock.

* **B** : *Dans* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$in” for something that will happen later, but the visit happened yesterday.

* **D** : *Il y a* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ago”; *il y a deux heures* would say when the visit happened, not how long it lasted.

* **E** : *Au bout de* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$after”; it says what happens once a period ends, not how long the visit lasted.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{il y a trois ans}$ [1 mark]

 

 

* *Je suis parti* is in the $\text{pass\'{e} compos\'{e}}$ and describes a single past event. Use *il y a* to say how long ago it happened. *Dans* refers to the future.

 

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{pendant six mois}$ [1 mark]

 

 

* The French classes are finished, since they came before the studies began. Use *pendant* for a completed period of time. *Depuis* is for something that is still going on.

 

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{au bout de huit mois}$ [1 mark]

 

 

* Getting a diploma happens at one moment, after a period of study. Use *au bout de* to say $\text{\textquotedblleft}$after” a period has passed. *Pendant* describes how long an action lasts.

 

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{dans deux mois}$    [1 mark]

 

 

* The trip home to France will happen in the future. Use *dans* to say how much time is left before it. *Il y a* refers to the past. \

  \

  **Model answer**: Je suis parti vivre au Canada il y a trois ans et j'habite maintenant $\text{\`{a}}$ $\text{Montr\'{e}al}$. $\text{\`{A}}$ mon $\text{arriv\'{e}e}$, j'ai suivi des cours de $\text{fran\c{c}ais}$ pendant six mois avant de commencer mes $\text{\'{e}tudes}$. Ensuite, j'ai obtenu mon $\text{dipl\^{o}me}$ au bout de huit mois. Ma famille me manque donc je rentre en France dans deux mois. J'ai $\text{h\^{a}te}$ !
