## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des r$\textit{\'{e}}$ponses suivantes :*

* $\textrm{cinq heures}$

* $\textrm{5 heures}$        [1 mark]

* The speaker states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les adolescents passent en moyenne cinq heures par jour devant un $\text{\'{e}}$cran." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$teenagers spend an average of five hours a day in front of a screen.") The expression*cinq heures* gives the daily average.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{temps scolaire}$    [1 mark]

* The speaker adds: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$en dehors du temps scolaire." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$outside of school time.") The expression *temps scolaire* names what is excluded from this figure.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des r$\textit{\'{e}}$ponses suivantes :*

* $\textrm{sept sur dix}$

* $\textrm{7 sur 10}$        [1 mark]

* The speaker mentions: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$sept sur dix disent utiliser leur t$\text{\'{e}}$l$\text{\'{e}}$phone juste avant de dormir." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$seven out of ten say they use their phone just before sleeping.") The expression*sept sur dix* gives the proportion of teenagers concerned.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des r$\textit{\'{e}}$ponses suivantes :*

* $\textrm{(le) sommeil}$

* $\textrm{(la) concentration}$        [1 mark]

* The speaker says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$une habitude qui... perturbe le sommeil et la concentration." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$a habit which disrupts sleep and concentration.") The expressions *le sommeil* and *la concentration* name the two aspects affected by this habit.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* The speaker points out: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$certains $\text{\'{e}}$tablissements scolaires ont d$\text{\'{e}}$cid$\text{\'{e}}$ d'interdire les t$\text{\'{e}}$l$\text{\'{e}}$phones dans l'enceinte du lyc$\text{\'{e}}$e." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$some schools decided to ban phones within the school grounds.") This confirms that some schools banned phone use on their premises.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C

* **A** : *R$\textit{\'{e}}$duit le nombre de devoirs* (*reduced the amount of homework*) is not correct because homework is never mentioned in the report.

* **C** : *Ajout$\textit{\'{e}}$ des pauses plus longues* (*added longer breaks*) is not correct because the report discusses interaction during breaks, but never mentions making breaks longer.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* The speaker adds: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les $\text{\'{e}}$l$\text{\`{e}}$ves... interagissent davantage entre eux pendant les pauses." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$students interact more with each other during breaks.") The word*davantage* shows that interaction between students has increased.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C

* **B** : *Utilisent davantage leur t$\textit{\'{e}}$l$\textit{\'{e}}$phone* (*use their phone more*) is not correct because it contradicts both the phone ban and the increased face-to-face interaction reported.

* **C** : *Quittent l'$\textit{\'{e}}$tablissement plus t$\textit{\^{o}}$t* (*leave school earlier*) is not correct because this is never mentioned in the report.
