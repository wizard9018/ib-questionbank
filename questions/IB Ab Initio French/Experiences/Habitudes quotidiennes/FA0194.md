## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* Antoine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{C'\'{e}tait}$ une exp$\text{\'{e}}$rience tr$\text{\`{e}}$s enrichissante, mais aussi parfois d$\text{\'{e}}$routante." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$It was a very enriching experience, but also sometimes confusing.") This matches the idea of a positive experience that was sometimes disconcerting.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C

* **B** : *Facile et pr$\textit{\'{e}}$visible* (*Easy and predictable*) is not correct because Antoine describes the experience as sometimes *d$\textit{\'{e}}$routante* (*confusing*), not easy or predictable.

* **C** : *D$\textit{\'{e}}$cevante du d$\textit{\'{e}}$but $\textit{\`{a}}$ la fin* (*Disappointing from start to finish*) is not correct because Antoine calls the experience *enrichissante* (*enriching*), not disappointing.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* Antoine explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'ai d$\text{\^{u}}$ m'adapter $\text{\`{a}}$ un rythme tr$\text{\`{e}}$s diff$\text{\'{e}}$rent." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I had to adapt to a very different pace.") This shows that the schedule was what he had to adjust to.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C

* **B** : *$\textit{\`{A}}$ une nouvelle langue* (*To a new language*) is not correct because Antoine never mentions language as something he had to adapt to.

* **C** :  *$\textit{\`{A}}$ des cours plus longs* (*To longer lessons*) is not correct because Antoine says the breaks are shorter, not that the lessons themselves are longer.

:br$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{t\^{o}t}$[1 mark]

* Antoine mentions: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les cours commencent plus t$\text{\^{o}}$t." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$lessons start earlier.") The word*t$\textit{\^{o}}$t* completes how the school day begins in Quebec.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{courtes}$    [1 mark]

* Antoine adds: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les pauses sont plus courtes." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$breaks are shorter.") The word *courtes* completes how the breaks compare to those in France.

($\text{\textquotedblleft}$students eat quickly and go back to class after only twenty minutes.") The expression*vingt minutes* gives the length of the lunch break in Quebec.

:br$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(beaucoup plus) d\'{e}contract\'{e}e}$[1 mark]

* Antoine points out: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Elle est beaucoup plus d$\text{\'{e}}$contract$\text{\'{e}}$e qu'en France." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$It is much more relaxed than in France.") The word*d$\textit{\'{e}}$contract$\textit{\'{e}}$e* describes the relationship between teachers and students in Quebec.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{poser des questions (en plein cours)}$    [1 mark]
