## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ils partent $\textrm{\`a}$ $\textrm{l'\'ecole}$ $\textrm{\`a}$ pied." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$they go to school on foot.") The word *de* completes the expression *avant de se rendre* (*before going to*).

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- The text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{L'\'education}$ des enfants est une $\textrm{priorit\'e}$ du gouvernement $\textrm{b\'eninois}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$The education of children is a priority of the Beninese government.") The word *aux* completes the expression *$\textit{gr\^ace}$ aux efforts* (*thanks to the efforts*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

- The text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les $\textrm{\'etablissements}$ sont $\textrm{g\'en\'eralement}$ $\textrm{\`a}$ environ 1,5 km des habitations." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$schools are generally about 1.5 km from homes.") The word *des* completes the expression *$\textit{pr\`es}$ des habitations* (*near the homes*).

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D

- **A** : *$\textit{\`A}$* is not correct because it is used to indicate location or direction.

- **B** : *Dans* is not correct because it is used to indicate being inside something.

- **D** : *Parmi* is not correct because it is used to indicate being among a group.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{les enfants}$    [1 mark]

- The text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$le $\textrm{petit-d\'ejeuner}$ est pris $\textrm{\`a}$ $\textrm{l'\'ecole}$, $\textrm{o\`u}$ ils $\textrm{ach\`etent}$ aux vendeuses $\textrm{agr\'e\'ees}$ des plats traditionnels." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$breakfast is eaten at school, where they buy traditional dishes from approved vendors.") The pronoun *ils* refers back to *les enfants*, who are buying food at school.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(des/les) cantines (scolaires)}$    [1 mark]

- The text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$peu $\textrm{d'\'ecoles}$ disposent d'une cantine scolaire. Ainsi, le gouvernement cherche $\textrm{\`a}$ en $\textrm{d\'evelopper}$ davantage." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$few schools have a school canteen. The government seeks to develop more of them.") The pronoun *en* replaces *des cantines scolaires*, which is the object being developed.
