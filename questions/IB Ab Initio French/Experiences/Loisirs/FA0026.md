# FA0026

[Note maximale : 5\] 

Dimanche 20 avril

Cher journal,\

Je m'appelle Lucas et j'ai 16 ans. Pendant mon temps libre, j'aime jouer au football avec mes amis dans le parc. C'est amusant et je suis content. Quand il pleut, je reste $\textrm{\`a}$ la maison pour regarder des $\textrm{s\'eries}$ ou jouer aux jeux $\textrm{vid\'eo}$ avec mon $\textrm{fr\`ere}$. Le week-end, je vais au $\textrm{cin\'ema}$ avec ma $\textrm{s{\oe}ur}$. Nous aimons les films d'aventure et les $\textrm{com\'edies}$. Demain, je vais jouer au football avec mes amis.

Je $\textrm{t'\'ecrirai}$ demain. 

**Choisissez les quatre affirmations vraies.**  [4 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Lucas a plus de quinze ans.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Lucas pratique un sport avec ses amis.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Les copains de Lucas sont amusants.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Par mauvais temps, Lucas choisit souvent des $\textrm{activit\'es}$ $\textrm{\`a}$ la maison.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$Lucas passe $\textrm{g\'en\'eralement}$ ses week-ends seul au $\textrm{cin\'ema}$.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Les films d'aventure ne plaisent pas $\textrm{\`a}$ Lucas et $\textrm{\`a}$ sa $\textrm{s{\oe}ur}$.

- G.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{\`A}$ l'avenir, Lucas va faire une $\textrm{activit\'e}$ sportive.

- H.$\hspace{1em}$Lucas $\textrm{n'\'ecrira}$ plus dans son journal.

**Choisissez la bonne $\textbf{r\'eponse}$.**  

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Ce texte est...    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$un blog.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$une annonce.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$un journal intime.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$un article.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, B, D, G}$        [4 marks]

- **A**: Lucas writes: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'ai 16 ans." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am 16 years old.") This shows that Lucas is 16, which means he is older than fifteen.

- **B**: Lucas states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'aime jouer au football avec mes amis." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I like playing football with my friends.") The verb *jouer* (*to play*) followed by *avec mes amis* (*with my friends*) confirms that Lucas practises a sport together with his friends.

- **D**: Lucas explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Quand il pleut, je reste $\textrm{\`a}$ la maison pour regarder des $\textrm{s\'eries}$ ou jouer aux jeux $\textrm{vid\'eo}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$When it rains, I stay at home to watch series or play video games.") The word *quand* (*when*) followed by *il pleut* (*it rains*) confirms that bad weather leads Lucas to choose indoor activities.

- **G**: Lucas mentions: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Demain, je vais jouer au football." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Tomorrow, I am going to play football.") The construction *aller + infinitif* confirms a future plan, showing that Lucas will do a sporting activity the next day.

- *Options incorrectes :* C, E, F, H

- **C**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Les copains de Lucas sont amusants" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Lucas's friends are amusing") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$C'est amusant." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$It is fun.") Here, *amusant* (*fun*) describes the activity of playing football, not the friends themselves.

- **E**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Lucas passe $\textrm{g\'en\'eralement}$ ses week-ends seul au $\textrm{cin\'ema}$" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Lucas generally spends his weekends alone at the cinema") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le week-end, je vais au $\textrm{cin\'ema}$ avec ma $\textrm{s{\oe}ur}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$At the weekend, I go to the cinema with my sister.") The word *avec* (*with*) directly contradicts the idea that he goes alone.

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Les films d'aventure ne plaisent pas $\textrm{\`a}$ Lucas et $\textrm{\`a}$ sa $\textrm{s{\oe}ur}$" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Lucas and his sister do not like adventure films") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous aimons les films d'aventure et les $\textrm{com\'edies}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We like adventure films and comedies.") The verb *aimons* (*we like*) directly contradicts this statement.

- **H**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Lucas $\textrm{n'\'ecrira}$ plus dans son journal" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Lucas will no longer write in his diary") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je $\textrm{t'\'ecrirai}$ demain." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I will write to you tomorrow.") The future tense *$\textit{\'ecrirai}$* (*will write*) confirms that Lucas intends to write again the next day.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The text begins with a date and the greeting $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cher journal" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Dear diary"), and ends with $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je $\textrm{t'\'ecrirai}$ demain" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I will write to you tomorrow"). These features are typical of *un journal intime* (*a personal diary*), where someone records personal thoughts and daily activities in a private and informal way.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D

- **A**: *Un blog* (*a blog*) is not correct because a blog is written for a public audience and often invites comments or interaction. This text is addressed privately to the diary using $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cher journal", confirming it is not intended for a public readership.

- **B**: *Une annonce* (*an announcement*) is not correct because an announcement usually invites people to participate in an activity or provides practical information. This text only describes Lucas's personal hobbies and does not invite the reader to do anything.

- **D**: *Un article* (*an article*) is not correct because an article typically presents objective information to a general audience, for example in a newspaper or magazine. This text is personal, informal and addressed to a diary, which is the opposite of an article.
