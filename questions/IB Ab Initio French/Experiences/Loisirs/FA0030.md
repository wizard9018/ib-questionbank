## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{il pleuvait}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1, Mourad writes: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{M\^eme}$ s'il pleuvait, nous avons $\textrm{d\'ecid\'e}$ de faire une $\textrm{randonn\'ee}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Even though it was raining, we decided to go hiking.") The imperfect tense *pleuvait* (*it was raining*) describes the weather conditions at the time of the hike.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(ils) se reposaient}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 2, Mourad explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Tandis que mes cousins se reposaient, j'ai $\textrm{essay\'e}$ le kayak." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$While my cousins were resting, I tried kayaking.") The imperfect tense *se reposaient* (*were resting*) describes what the cousins were doing at the same time as Mourad tried kayaking. The word *tandis que* (*while*) confirms these actions were happening simultaneously after the picnic.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(c'était) spectaculaire}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 2, Mourad states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$finalement, j'ai $\textrm{ador\'e}$ cette $\textrm{exp\'erience}$. $\textrm{C'\'etait}$ spectaculaire !" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$in the end, I loved this experience. It was spectacular!") The adverb *finalement* (*in the end*) confirms that this is his final judgement of the activity, and the adjective *spectaculaire* (*spectacular*) expresses his very positive assessment.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Vrai}$    [1 mark]

- Justification : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'ai $\textrm{go\^ut\'e}$ des $\textrm{sp\'ecialit\'es}$ $\textrm{r\'egionales}$ pour la $\textrm{premi\`ere}$ fois." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I tasted regional specialities for the first time.")\

In paragraph 3, Mourad explains that he tried regional specialities for the first time during the evening meal. The phrase *pour la $\textit{premi\`ere}$ fois* (*for the first time*) directly confirms that this was a new experience for him.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Faux}$    [1 mark]

- Justification : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ces $\textrm{activit\'es}$ ne sont pas seulement amusantes ; elles permettent aussi de se $\textrm{d\'etendre}$ et de $\textrm{cr\'eer}$ des souvenirs." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$these activities are not only fun; they also allow people to relax and create memories.")\

In paragraph 4, Mourad clearly states that the activities are not only fun. The adverb *seulement* (*only*) followed by *aussi* (*also*) shows that he believes these activities have additional benefits beyond entertainment, which directly contradicts the statement.
