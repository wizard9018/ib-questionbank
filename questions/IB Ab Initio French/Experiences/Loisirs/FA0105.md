## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(à) un atelier de photographie}$    [1 mark]

- Thomas says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je suis $\textrm{all\'e}$ $\textrm{\`a}$ un atelier de photographie avec quelques amis." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I went to a photography workshop with some friends.") The expression *la semaine $\textit{derni\`ere}$* (*last week*) confirms this is a recent event.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(un peu) nerveux}$    [1 mark]

- Thomas says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Au $\textrm{d\'ebut}$, $\textrm{j'\'etais}$ un peu nerveux, parce que je ne connaissais personne." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$At the beginning, I was a little nervous because I did not know anyone.") The expression *au $\textit{d\'ebut}$* (*at the beginning*) confirms this describes Thomas's feeling at the start of the activity.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(vraiment) sympathique}$    [1 mark]

- Thomas says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$le professeur $\textrm{\'etait}$ vraiment sympathique." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the teacher was really friendly.") The noun *animateur du club* (*club leader*) refers to the same person as *professeur* (*teacher*) in the audio.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.*  

- $\textrm{la nature}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{des bâtiments de la ville}$        [1 mark]

- Thomas says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous avons appris $\textrm{\`a}$ prendre des photos de la nature et des $\textrm{b\^atiments}$ de la ville." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We learned to take photos of nature and buildings in the city.") The word *et* (*and*) lists two different things that the participants photographed during the session.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{créatives}$    [1 mark]

- Thomas says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$J'ai aussi $\textrm{rencontr\'e}$ des personnes $\textrm{tr\`es}$ $\textrm{cr\'eatives}$ qui aiment l'art et la musique." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I also met very creative people who like art and music.") The adjective *$\textit{cr\'eatives}$* (*creative*) describes the people Thomas met. The nouns *peinture* (*painting*) and *chansons* (*songs*) are used in the summary to refer to *art* and *musique* mentioned in the audio.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{relaxante}$    [1 mark]

- Thomas says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$c'est une $\textrm{activit\'e}$ amusante et relaxante." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$It is a fun and relaxing activity.") The adjective *relaxante* (*relaxing*) confirms that Thomas finds the activity enjoyable and restful.
