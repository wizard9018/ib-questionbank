## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* *$\textit{Lyc\'{e}e}$* is masculine despite ending in *-e*.

* *$\textit{R\'{e}ponses}$ incorrectes :* A, C, D

* **A**: *La* is a feminine article.

* **C**: *Les* is a plural article.

* **D**: *L'* is a contracted article used before a vowel or a mute h, for both masculine and feminine nouns.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *$\textit{Fr\`{e}res}$* is a plural noun (*-s* ending) so we use *des*.

* *$\textit{R\'{e}ponses}$ incorrectes :* A, B, D

* **A**: *Un* is masculine, but singular: *$\textit{fr\`{e}res}$* is plural.

* **B**: *Le* is masculine singular.

* **D**: *Une* is feminine singular.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

* *Les* is correct because *yeux* is a plural noun (the plural of *$\textit{\oe{}il}$*). Unlike *le* and *la*, *les* never changes to *l'* before a vowel: *les yeux*.

* *$\textit{R\'{e}ponses}$ incorrectes :* A, B, C, D, F

* **A**: *Le* is masculine singular.

* **B**: *La* is feminine singular.

* **C**: *Un* is masculine singular and indefinite.

* **D**: *Une* is feminine singular and indefinite.

* **F**: *L'* is only used before a singular noun beginning with a vowel sound; *yeux* begins with a vowel, but it is plural.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* *La* is correct because *main* is a feminine singular noun.

* *$\textit{R\'{e}ponses}$ incorrectes :* A, C, D, E, F

* **A**: *Le* is masculine singular.

* **C**: *Un* is masculine singular and indefinite.

* **D**: *Une* is feminine singular, but it is an indefinite article.

* **E**: *Les* is plural.

* **F**: *L'* is used before a vowel sound, but *main* begins with a consonant.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

* *Eau* is a feminine noun. The article is *l'* because it begins with a vowel.

* *$\textit{R\'{e}ponses}$ incorrectes :* A, B, C, D, E

* **A**: *Le* is masculine singular.

* **B**: *La* is the correct gender, but it must change to *l'* before a vowel.

* **C**: *Un* is masculine singular and indefinite.

* **D**: *Une* is feminine singular, but it is an indefinite article.

* **E**: *Les* is plural.

**Grammar reminder:**

**Nouns and gender: le / la / les / un / une / des**

Every French noun is either masculine or feminine.

Use definite articles when referring to specific people, places or things:

* *le* with masculine singular nouns: *le stylo*

* *la* with feminine singular nouns: *la table*

* *l'* before a vowel or a silent h: *l'$\textit{\'{e}cole}$, l'$\textit{h\^{o}tel}$*

* *les* with plural nouns: *les chocolats*

Use indefinite articles to refer to one or more people, places or things that are not specific:

* *un* with masculine singular nouns: *un stylo*

* *une* with feminine singular nouns: *une table*

* *des* with plural nouns (masculine or feminine): *des stylos, des tables*

**Common gender patterns:**

Although the gender of nouns designating objects can sometimes be unpredictable, the endings can help you guess whether the noun is masculine or feminine.

**Masculine**

Nouns ending in *-isme*, *-ment*, *-scope*, *-phone*, *-teur* and *-eau* are usually masculine:

* *le tourisme*

* *le monument*

* *le $\textit{t\'{e}l\'{e}scope}$*

* *le $\textit{t\'{e}l\'{e}phone}$*

* *l'ordinateur*

* *le bureau*

Exceptions: *l'eau, la peau*.

Nouns ending in *-age* are usually masculine:

* *le village*

* *le fromage*

Exceptions: *la plage, la page, l'image, la cage*.

Note: a lot of nouns ending in *-e* are masculine even though *-e* is usually an indicator of the feminine form:

* *le $\textit{syst\`{e}me}$*

* *le $\textit{probl\`{e}me}$*

**Feminine**

Nouns ending in *-ion*, *-esse*, *-ure*, *-otte*, *-ence*, *-ade* and *-ette* are usually feminine:

* *la natation*

* *la princesse*

* *la voiture*

* *la carotte*

* *la patience*

* *la salade*

* *la baguette*

Nouns ending in *$\textit{-t\'{e}}$* are usually feminine:

* *la $\textit{libert\'{e}}$*

* *la $\textit{sant\'{e}}$*

Exceptions: *le $\textit{c\^{o}t\'{e}}$, l'$\textit{\'{e}t\'{e}}$*.

Note: a lot of nouns ending in *-eur* are feminine:

* *la fleur*

* *la couleur*

* *la peur*

* *la valeur*
