# FA0328

**The paragraph below contains five possessive adjectives errors. Find the errors and write the correct words.** [5 marks]

Salut ! Nous, c’est Gwen et Cédric.

Dans votre famille, être en forme est très important. Ses parents disent que son médecin conseille de faire trois heures d’exercice par semaine, donc ils font une promenade chaque dimanche. En plus, nous mangeons mes cinq fruits et légumes par jour. Et vous, que faites-vous pour ta santé?

Au revoir!

**Complete the paragraph. Use the correct possessive adjective.** [4 marks]

**[-6-]** professeur de français nous a demandé de faire un exposé sur l’immigration. Alors, **[-7-]** camarades et moi, nous avons travaillé en groupe. Même si nous avons utilisé toutes **[-8-]** notes de français, le prof pense que **[-9-]** idées ne sont pas très intéressantes !

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{notre}$    [1 mark]

* *Notre* is used before a singular noun. The owner here is *Gwen* and *$\textit{C\'{e}dric}$* and it refers to *nous*. The noun *famille* is singular, so the correct answer is *notre*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{nos}$    [1 mark]

* *Nos* is used before a plural noun. The owner here is *Gwen* and *$\textit{C\'{e}dric}$* and it refers to *nous*. The noun parents is plural, so the correct answer is nos.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Leur}$    [1 mark]

* *Leur* is used before a singular noun when the owner is plural. The owner here is *nos parents* which refers to *ils*. The noun *$\textit{m\'{e}decin}$* is singular, so the correct answer is *leur*. \

  \

  *Son* is used before a singular noun when the owner is *il/elle*. In this paragraph, the owner is *ils*, not *il/elle*, so *son* is incorrect.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{nos}$    [1 mark]

* *Nos* is used before a plural noun. The owner here is *Gwen* and *$\textit{C\'{e}dric}$*, which refers to *nous*. In the phrase *fruits et $\textit{l\'{e}gumes}$* the nouns are plural, so the correct answer is *nos*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{votre}$    [1 mark]

* *Votre* is used before a singular noun when the owner is *vous*. In this paragraph, the question is directed at the reader (*vous*), and the noun *$\textit{sant\'{e}}$* is singular, so the correct answer is *votre*. \

  \

  **Model answer**: Salut ! Nous, c’est Gwen et $\text{C\'{e}dric}$. 

  Dans notre famille, $\text{\^{e}tre}$ en forme est $\text{tr\`{e}s}$ important. Nos parents disent que leur $\text{m\'{e}decin}$ conseille de faire trois heures d’exercice par semaine, donc ils font une promenade chaque dimanche. En plus, nous mangeons nos cinq fruits et $\text{l\'{e}gumes}$ par jour. Et vous, que faites-vous pour votre $\text{sant\'{e}}$ ?

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Notre}$    [1 mark]

* *Notre* is used before a singular noun. The owner here is *nous*, as it refers to the group of students who were asked to do the presentation. The noun *professeur* is singular, so the correct answer is *notre*.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{mes}$    [1 mark]

* *Mes* is used before a plural noun. The owner here is *moi*, as the speaker is referring to their own classmates. The noun *camarades* is plural, so the correct answer is *mes*.

$8.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{nos}$    [1 mark]

* *Nos* is used before a plural noun. The owner here is *nous*, as the notes belong to the group of students who worked together. The noun *notes* is plural, so the correct answer is *nos*.

$9.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{nos}$    [1 mark]

* *Nos* is used before a plural noun. The owner here is *nous*, as the ideas belong to the group of students who worked on the presentation. The noun *$\textit{id\'{e}es}$* is plural, so the correct answer is *nos*. \

  \

Alors, mes camarades et moi, nous avons $\text{travaill\'{e}}$ en groupe. $\text{M\^{e}me}$ si nous avons $\text{utilis\'{e}}$ toutes nos notes de $\text{fran\c{c}ais}$, le prof pense que nos $\text{id\'{e}es}$ ne sont pas $\text{tr\`{e}s}$ $\text{int\'{e}ressantes}$ !
