# FA0293

**Choose the correct form of the verb in the present tense.**

$\hspace{1em}$Je **\[-1-]** mon yaourt.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$finis

* B.$\hspace{1em}$finit

* C.$\hspace{1em}$finissons

* D.$\hspace{1em}$finissez

$\hspace{1em}$Tu **\[-2-]** une boisson.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$choisit

* B.$\hspace{1em}$choisissez

* C.$\hspace{1em}$choisissent

* D.$\hspace{1em}$choisis

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *Finis* is the correct form because the subject is *je*. Regular *-IR* verbs take the ending *-is* with *je*.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C, D

* **B** : *Finit* is used with *il/elle/on*, but the subject is *je*.

* **C** : *Finissons* is used with *nous*, but the subject is *je*.

* **D** : *Finissez* is used with *vous*, but the subject is *je*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *Choisis* is the correct form because the subject is *tu*. Regular *-IR* verbs take the ending *-is* with *tu*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C

* **A** : *Choisit* is the *il/elle/on* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **B** : *Choisissez* is the *vous* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **C** : *Choisissent* is the *ils/elles* form, but the subject is *tu*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* *$\textit{R\'{e}ussit}$* is the correct form because the subject is *elle*. Regular *-IR* verbs take the ending *-it* with *elle*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D, E, F

* **A** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussissons}$* is the *nous* form, but the subject is *elle*.

* **C** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussissent}$* is the *ils/elles* form, but the subject is *elle*.

* **D** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussis}$* is the *je/tu* form, but the subject is *elle*.

* **E** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussissez}$* is the *vous* form, but the subject is *elle*.

* **F** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussir}$* is the infinitive form, but the subject is *elle*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

* *$\textit{R\'{e}ussissez}$* is the correct form because the subject is *vous*. Regular *-IR* verbs take the ending *-issez* with *vous*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C, D, F

* **A** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussissons}$* is the *nous* form, but the subject is *vous*.

* **B** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussit}$* is the *il/elle/on* form, but the subject is *vous*.

* **C** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussissent}$* is the *ils/elles* form, but the subject is *vous*.

* **D** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussis}$* is the *je/tu* form, but the subject is *vous*.

* **F** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussir}$* is the infinitive form, but the subject is *vous*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *$\textit{R\'{e}ussissent}$* is the correct form because the subject is *ils*. Regular *-IR* verbs take the ending *-issent* with *ils*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D, E, F

* **A** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussissons}$* is the *nous* form, but the subject is *ils*.

* **B** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussit}$* is the *il/elle/on* form, but the subject is *ils*.

* **D** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussis}$* is the *je/tu* form, but the subject is *ils*.

* **E** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussissez}$* is the *vous* form, but the subject is *ils*.

* **F** : *$\textit{R\'{e}ussir}$* is the infinitive form, but the subject is *ils*.

**Grammar Reminder**

Regular *-IR* verbs such as *finir, choisir* and *$\textit{r\'{e}ussir}$* change their ending to agree with the subject.\

First, remove *-ir*. Then add the correct ending:

| $\hspace{1em}$Subject pronoun$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$-IR ending$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$Example: Finir$\hspace{1.5em}$ |

| :- | :- | :- |

| $\hspace{1em}$Je | $\hspace{1em}$-is | $\hspace{1em}$finis |

| $\hspace{1em}$Tu | $\hspace{1em}$-is | $\hspace{1em}$finis |

| $\hspace{1em}$Il/elle/on | $\hspace{1em}$-it | $\hspace{1em}$finit |

| $\hspace{1em}$Nous | $\hspace{1em}$-issons | $\hspace{1em}$finissons |

| $\hspace{1em}$Vous | $\hspace{1em}$-issez | $\hspace{1em}$finissez |

| $\hspace{1em}$Ils/elles$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$-issent | $\hspace{1em}$finissent |

**How can you recognize regular verbs in -ir?**

These verbs end in *-ir* in the infinitive and have an *-issons* form with *nous*.

Example: *finir* → *nous finissons* → regular *-IR* pattern.

Here is a list of common *-IR* verbs:

* finir: to finish

* choisir: to choose

* $\text{r\'{e}ussir}$: to succeed

* grandir: to grow up

* grossir: to gain weight

* maigrir: to lose weight

* $\text{gu\'{e}rir}$: to recover/to get better

* nourrir: to feed

* $\text{r\'{e}fl\'{e}chir}$: to think / to reflect

* remplir: to fill

**Common mistake**: Do not omit *-iss-* in the *nous, vous and ils/elles* forms of regular *-IR* verbs.\

For example, write *nous finissons*, not *nous finons*.
