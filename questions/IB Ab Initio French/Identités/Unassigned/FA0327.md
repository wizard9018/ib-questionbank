# FA0327

**Complete the paragraph. Use the correct possessive adjective.** [5 marks]

Je m'appelle $\text{B\'{e}a}$. **\[-1-]** famille habite $\text{\`{a}}$ Lyon, mais **\[-2-]** $\text{\'{e}cole}$ est $\text{\`{a}}$ Paris. J'ai un $\text{fr\`{e}re}$ et une soeur : **\[-3-]** grand $\text{fr\`{e}re}$ s'appelle Thomas et **\[-4-]** petite soeur s'appelle Chantal. **\[-5-]** chien s’appelle Toto et il est trop mignon.

**The sentences below contain each one possessive adjective error. Find the error and write the correct word.**

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$Leurs loisir $\text{pr\'{e}f\'{e}r\'{e}}$ est la $\text{randonn\'{e}e}$.    [1 mark]

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{Andr\'{e}}$ et son cousine sont tous les deux $\text{tr\`{e}s}$ bavards.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Ma}$    [1 mark]

* *Ma* is the correct answer because it is used in front of nouns that are feminine and singular. *Famille* is feminine and singular.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{mon}$    [1 mark]

* *Mon* is used before feminine and singular nouns which start with a vowel or a silent *h*. Therefore, even though the noun *$\textit{\'{e}cole}$* is a feminine and singular noun, mon is the correct possessive adjective.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{mon}$    [1 mark]

* *Mon* is used before masculine and singular nouns. *$\textit{Fr\`{e}re}$* is masculine and singular. Therefore *mon* is the correct answer.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ma}$    [1 mark]

* *Ma* is used before feminine and singular nouns. *Soeur* is feminine and singular. Therefore *ma* is the correct answer.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Notre}$    [1 mark]

* *Notre* is used when the owner is plural. Here the owners are $\text{B\'{e}a}$ and her siblings. It is used for singular nouns, and in this case, *chien* is a masculine and singular noun.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Je m'appelle $\text{B\'{e}a}$. Ma famille habite $\text{\`{a}}$ Lyon, mais mon $\text{\'{e}cole}$ est $\text{\`{a}}$ Paris. J'ai un $\text{fr\`{e}re}$ et une soeur : mon grand $\text{fr\`{e}re}$ s'appelle Thomas et ma petite soeur s'appelle Chantal. Notre chien s’appelle Toto et il est trop mignon.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Leur}$    [1 mark]

* *Leur* is used before singular nouns and refers to *ils/elles*, as in more than one person. The noun *loisir* is a singular noun, but is here associated with several people’s favourite hobby: *la $\textit{randonn\'{e}e}$*.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Leur loisir $\text{pr\'{e}f\'{e}r\'{e}}$ est la $\text{randonn\'{e}e}$.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{sa}$    [1 mark]

* *Sa* is used before feminine and singular nouns. Although the owner *$\textit{Andr\'{e}}$* refers to *il* and is masculine, the noun *cousine* is feminine and singular. \

  \

  **Model answer**: $\text{Andr\'{e}}$ et sa cousine sont tous les deux bavards.
