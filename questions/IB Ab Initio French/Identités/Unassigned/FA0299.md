# FA0299

**The sentence below contains one  verb error in the present tense. Find the error and write the correct word.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Ma soeur et moi attends le dessert $\text{apr\`{e}s}$ le $\text{d\'{e}jeuner}$.    [1 mark]

**Complete the sentences. Use the present tense form of each verb in brackets.**

Au $\text{march\'{e}}$, ma $\text{m\`{e}re}$ et toi **\[-2-]** *(vendre)* des tomates.    [1 mark]

Au restaurant, le serveur qui apporte les boissons **\[-3-]** (*$\textit{r\'{e}pondre}$*) aux clients.    [1 mark]

Après le $\text{d\'{e}jeuner}$, mes cousins et moi **\[-4-]** *(descendre)* en ville $\text{\`{a}}$ pied.    [1 mark]

Au restaurant, la soeur de mes amis **\[-5-]** *(attendre)* son dessert.    [1 mark]

Quand le serveur revient, je **\[-6-]** *(rendre)* le menu.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{attendons}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *ma soeur et moi* includes the speaker and corresponds to *nous*. The correct form of *attendre* is *attendons*, with the ending *-ons*.

  

  **Model answer**: Ma soeur et moi attendons le dessert $\text{apr\`{e}s}$ le $\text{d\'{e}jeuner}$.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{vendez}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *ma $\textit{m\`{e}re}$ et toi* includes the person being addressed and corresponds to *vous*. The correct form of *vendre* is *vendez*, with the ending *-ez*.

  

  **Model answer**: Au $\text{march\'{e}}$, ma $\text{m\`{e}re}$ et toi vendez des tomates.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{r\'{e}pond}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *le serveur qui apporte les boissons* is singular and corresponds to *il*. The words *qui apporte les boissons* describe the server. The subject is still *le serveur*, not *les boissons*. The correct form is *$\textit{r\'{e}pond}$*, with no added ending.

  

  **Model answer**: Au restaurant, le serveur qui apporte les boissons $\text{r\'{e}pond}$ aux clients.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{descendons}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *mes cousins et moi* includes the speaker and corresponds to *nous*. The correct form of *descendre* is *descendons*, with the ending *-ons*.

  **Model answer**: $\text{Apr\`{e}s}$ le $\text{d\'{e}jeuner}$, mes cousins et moi descendons en ville $\text{\`{a}}$ pied.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{attend}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is *la soeur de mes amis*. Its main noun, *soeur*, is singular and corresponds to *elle*. The plural noun *amis* does not determine the verb ending. The correct form is *attend*.

  **Model answer**: Au restaurant, la soeur de mes amis attend son dessert.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{rends}$    [1 mark]

* The subject of *rendre* is *je*. The correct form is *rends*.

  **Model answer**: Quand le serveur revient, je rends le menu.
