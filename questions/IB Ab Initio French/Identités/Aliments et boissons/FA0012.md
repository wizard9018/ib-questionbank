## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Certains choisissent des plats simples... d'autres $\textrm{pr\'ef\`erent}$... pizzas ou hamburgers." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Some choose simple dishes... others prefer... pizzas or burgers.") The sentence describes the types of food students choose, which matches the question $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Quels aliments les $\textrm{\'el\`eves}$ $\textrm{privil\'egient-ils}$ ?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$What foods do students prefer?") The verb *$\textit{privil\'egient}$* (*prefer/favour*) has the same meaning as *choisissent* (*choose*) and *$\textit{pr\'ef\`erent}$* (*prefer*) used in the text.

- *Option incorrecte :* A

($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Some prefer foods that are quicker to eat.") Here, *rapides* (*quick*) describes the food itself, not where it is bought or the reason for eating quickly.

The phrase *Selon plusieurs professeurs* (*According to several teachers*) clearly signals that the paragraph is about their views, not eating speed.
