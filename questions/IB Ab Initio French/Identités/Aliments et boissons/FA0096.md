## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(au) Sénégal}$    [1 mark]

- Francine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je vis au $\textrm{S\'en\'egal}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I live in Senegal.") The verb *vis* (*live*) directly identifies where she lives.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(du) pain }$    [1 mark]

- Francine says:  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le matin, je prends un petit $\textrm{d\'ejeuner}$ simple, souvent du pain avec du $\textrm{caf\'e}$." ( $\text{\textquotedblleft}$In the morning, I have a simple breakfast, often bread with coffee.") The time reference *le matin* (*in the morning*) confirms this is what she eats at the beginning of the day. The question uses *au $\textit{d\'ebut}$ de la $\textit{journ\'ee}$* (*at the beginning of the day*) which corresponds to *le matin* (*in the morning*) in the audio.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(le) riz avec du poisson}$    [1 mark]

- Francine says:  $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{\`A}$ midi, je mange un plat traditionnel comme le riz avec du poisson, qui est $\textrm{tr\`es}$ populaire ici." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$At midday, I eat a traditional dish such as rice with fish, which is very popular here.") The time reference *$\textit{\`a}$ midi* (*at midday*) confirms this is her lunchtime meal.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{les spécialités locales}$    [1 mark]

- Francine says:  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$J'aime $\textrm{d\'ecouvrir}$ les $\textrm{sp\'ecialit\'es}$ locales." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I like to discover local specialities.") The verb *$\textit{d\'ecouvrir}$* (*to discover*) has the same meaning as *explorer* (*to explore*) used in the question, confirming that local specialities are what Francine enjoys exploring.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{curieuse}$    [1 mark]

- Francine says:  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$parce que je suis curieuse." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$because I am curious.") The word *parce que* (*because*) directly introduces her reason for enjoying local specialities, and the adjective *curieuse* (*curious*) identifies her personality.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{d\'{e}licieuse et vari\'{e}e}$[1 mark]

* Francine says:  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je pense que la nourriture au S$\text{\'{e}}$n$\text{\'{e}}$gal est d$\text{\'{e}}$licieuse et vari$\text{\'{e}}$e." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I think that the food in Senegal is delicious and varied.") The expression *la cuisine au S$\textit{\'{e}}$n$\textit{\'{e}}$gal* (*the cuisine in Senegal*) in the question has a similar meaning to *la nourriture au S$\textit{\'{e}}$n$\textit{\'{e}}$gal* (*the food in Senegal*) used in the audio, confirming Francine’s opinion about the food.
