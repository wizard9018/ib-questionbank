$\underline{\textrm{Marking Criteria Feedback}}$

**Criterion A (Language)**:

* Use past tenses to describe what happened: perfect tense for completed actions (*j'ai d$\textit{\'{e}}$cid$\textit{\'{e}}$, j'ai choisi, j'ai commenc$\textit{\'{e}}$, j'ai compris*) and imperfect for descriptions (*c'$\textit{\'{e}}$tait, j'avais, je voulais*).

* Use the present tense for projection and general truth: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je veux continuer", $\text{\textquotedblleft}$D'habitude, je mange..."

* Use *commencer $\textit{\`{a}}$*+ infinitive to express the beginning of a change: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'ai commenc$\text{\'{e}}$ $\text{\`{a}}$ me sentir mieux".

* Use *d$\textit{\'{e}}$cider de*+ infinitive to express a decision: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'ai d$\text{\'{e}}$cid$\text{\'{e}}$ de changer mon alimentation".

* Use *avoir* + noun to express a physical state: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'avais souvent faim".

* Common errors include confusing perfect tense and imperfect (ask yourself if you are describing a situation (*c'$\textit{\'{e}}$tait difficile*) or a completed action (*j'ai d$\textit{\'{e}}$cid$\textit{\'{e}}$*) and missing *de* after verbs like *$\textit{d\'ecider}$* (*j'ai $\textit{d\'ecid\'e}$ changer* instead of *j'ai $\textit{d\'ecid\'e}$ de changer*).

**Criterion B (Message)**:

* A good answer covers three content points:\

    $1.\hspace{1em}$what you did: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'ai d$\text{\'{e}}$cid$\text{\'{e}}$ de changer mon alimentation... j'ai choisi de pr$\text{\'{e}}$parer mes repas”\

    $2.\hspace{1em}$the difficulties: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textit{c'\'{e}tait}$ vraiment difficile...je voulais manger des collations... je n'avais pas toujours le temps”\

    $3.\hspace{1em}$your opinion / result: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'ai commenc$\text{\'{e}}$ $\text{\`{a}}$ me sentir... j'ai compris que... maintenant, je veux continuer”.

* The model answer clearly develops all three ideas with detail and examples.

* Use *et* to add ideas, *mais* to show contrast, *par exemple* to illustrate, *de plus* to add information, and *cependant* to introduce a contrasting idea.

* Weak answers may only describe the experience without mentioning difficulties or forget the final reflection. Make sure you organise your ideas clearly in paragraphs.

 

**Criterion C (Conceptual understanding**):

- **Blog** is appropriate because you are sharing a personal experience with a broad, public audience, anyone can read a blog, which is exactly what the prompt asks for. You want to reach other students in general, not one specific person. A strong response includes all the conventions of a blog, as in the model answer: a blog URL (*www.monblogsante.com*), a title (*Une semaine pour mieux manger : mon $\textit{exp\'erience}$*), an author name with date and time (*Par : Alex_15 | 12 mars 2026 | 18h00*), an opening salutation (*Salut $\textit{\`a}$ tous !*), a text addressed to readers in the first person (*j'ai $\textit{d\'ecid\'e}$, j'ai compris*), a direct address to the reader (*Et vous, $\textit{\^etes-vous}$ $\textit{pr\^ets}$ $\textit{\`a}$ essayer ?*), a closing salutation (*$\textit{\`A}$ $\textit{bient\^ot}$ !*), and a comments section (*@MarieDu22 Super $\textit{id\'ee}$ !*). 

- **Courriel** is generally appropriate but less suitable because the message is not addressed to one specific person.

- **Journal intime** is generally inappropriate, as it is a private text and not intended for an audience.
