# FA0013

**Les repas en famille**

Chez moi, le repas est un moment $\textrm{tr\`es}$ important car nous mangeons toujours ensemble le soir. Mon $\textrm{p\`ere}$ adore cuisiner et $\textrm{pr\'epare}$ souvent des plats traditionnels $\textrm{fran\c{c}ais}$, comme le poulet $\textrm{r\^oti}$ ou la ratatouille.

Le week-end, nous allons parfois au $\textrm{march\'e}$ pour acheter des $\underline{\textrm{produits}}$ frais. $\textrm{L'ann\'ee}$ $\textrm{derni\`ere}$, nous avons aussi $\textrm{commenc\'e}$ $\textrm{\`a}$ cuisiner ensemble le dimanche.

Ma $\textrm{m\`ere}$ $\textrm{pr\'ef\`ere}$ les $\textrm{l\'egumes}$ tandis que mon $\textrm{fr\`ere}$ adore les $\underline{\textrm{desserts}}$, surtout les $\textrm{cr\^epes}$ et les $\textrm{g\^ateaux}$. Cependant, moi je $\textrm{pr\'ef\`ere}$ les plats simples comme les $\textrm{p\^ates}$ ou les salades.

Quand nous voyageons, j'essaie toujours de $\textrm{go\^uter}$ des $\underline{\textrm{spécialités locales}}$.

**$\textbf{R\'epondez}$ aux questions suivantes.**  

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Quand la famille se retrouve-t-elle pour partager un repas ?       [1 mark] 

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Qu'est-ce que le $\textrm{p\`ere}$ aime $\textrm{pr\'eparer}$ ?     [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{le soir}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nous mangeons toujours ensemble le soir." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we always eat together in the evening.") The adverb *toujours* (*always*) and the time reference *le soir* (*in the evening*) confirm that the evening is when the family regularly shares a meal together.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des $\textit{r\'eponses}$ suivantes :*

- $\textrm{des plats traditionnels français}$

- $\textrm{le poulet rôti}$    

- $\textrm{la ratatouille}$        [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mon $\textrm{p\`ere}$ adore cuisiner et $\textrm{pr\'epare}$ souvent des plats traditionnels $\textrm{fran\c{c}ais}$, comme le poulet $\textrm{r\^oti}$ ou la ratatouille." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$My father loves cooking and often prepares traditional French dishes, such as roast chicken or ratatouille.") The verb *adore* (*loves*) confirms that cooking is something the father genuinely enjoys. The word *comme* (*such as*) introduces two specific examples of the traditional dishes he prepares. All three answers are acceptable as they all appear in the text.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The word *aliments* is correct because paragraph 2 says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nous allons parfois au $\textrm{march\'e}$ pour acheter des produits frais." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we sometimes go to the market to buy fresh produce.) The word *produits* (*produce*) refers to fresh food items bought to be eaten, and *aliments* (*food items*) carries the same meaning in this context.

- *Option incorrecte :* D

- **D**: *Articles* (*articles/items*) is not correct because it is a more general word with several possible meanings and does not specifically refer to food items that can be bought at the market.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The word *$\textit{p\^atisseries}$* is correct because paragraph 3 says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$mon $\textrm{fr\`ere}$ adore les desserts, surtout les $\textrm{cr\^epes}$ et les $\textrm{g\^ateaux}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$my brother loves desserts, especially $\textrm{cr\^epes}$ and cakes.") The word *desserts* describes sweet foods eaten at the end of a meal, and *$\textit{p\^atisseries}$* (*pastries and sweet baked goods*) refers to the same type of sweet foods, as confirmed by the examples *$\textit{cr\^epes}$* and *$\textit{g\^ateaux}$* (*cakes*) given in the text.

- *Option incorrecte :* E

- **E**: *Repas* (*meal*) is not correct because it refers to a meal in general. In the text, *desserts* refers specifically to sweet foods eaten at the end of a meal, not to the meal as a whole.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

- The expression cuisine $\textrm{r\'egionale}$ is correct because in paragraph 4, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'essaie toujours de $\textrm{go\^uter}$ des $\textrm{sp\'ecialit\'es}$ locales." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I always try to taste local specialities.") The expression *$\textit{sp\'ecialit\'es}$ locales* describes typical dishes from a particular place, and *cuisine $\textit{r\'egionale}$* (*regional cuisine*) carries the same meaning as it refers to the typical food of a specific region.

- *Option incorrecte :* A

- **A**: *Plats traditionnels* (*traditional dishes*) is not correct because it refers generally to dishes that have been passed down over time without specifying a particular place. In the text, *$\textit{sp\'ecialit\'es}$ locales* refers more specifically to dishes typical of a particular location.
