## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{une nouvelle façon de manger}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je teste une nouvelle $\textrm{fa\c{c}on}$ de manger." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$this week, I am testing a new way of eating.") The verb *tester* (*to test*) confirms that the narrator is trying something new for a short period.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ni viande ni poisson}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1, the text explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je ne mange ni viande ni poisson." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I eat neither meat nor fish.") The double negative construction *ni... ni...* (*neither... nor...*) confirms that the narrator chooses not to consume either of these foods.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{un peu difficile}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1, the text mentions: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$c'est nouveau pour moi et un peu difficile." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it is new for me and a little difficult.") The expression *un peu difficile* (*a little difficult*) describes how the narrator personally experiences this change.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{de nouvelles recettes}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 2, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je $\textrm{pr\'epare}$ de nouvelles recettes avec ma famille." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I prepare new recipes with my family.") The adjective *nouvelles* (*new*) and the time reference *le week-end* (*at the weekend*) confirm that this is what the narrator cooks at the end of the week.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Noël}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 3, the text explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Demain, je $\textrm{pr\'epare}$ un repas $\textrm{sp\'ecial}$ pour $\textrm{No\"el}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Tomorrow, I am preparing a special meal for Christmas.") The word *demain* (*tomorrow*) confirms this refers to the following day, and *$\textit{No\"el}$* (*Christmas*) is the celebration being prepared for.
