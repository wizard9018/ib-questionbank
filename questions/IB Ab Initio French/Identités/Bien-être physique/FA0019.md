# FA0019

[Note maximale : 8\] 

**$\textbf{Am\'eliorer}$ son sommeil**

**[-1-]**\

Beaucoup de jeunes utilisent leur smartphone le soir. Ils consultent les $\textrm{r\'eseaux}$ sociaux et regardent des $\textrm{vid\'eos}$ en ligne. Ils se couchent donc $\textrm{tr\`es}$ tard. Par $\textrm{cons\'equent}$, ils dorment mal et sont $\textrm{fatigu\'es}$ le matin $\textrm{\`a}$ $\textrm{l'\'ecole}$.

**[-2-]**\

Pour $\textrm{am\'eliorer}$ le sommeil, il est $\textrm{conseill\'e}$ de changer certaines habitudes. Par exemple, lire un livre avant de dormir peut aider $\textrm{\`a}$ se $\textrm{d\'etendre}$. Il est aussi utile $\textrm{d'\'eviter}$ les $\textrm{\'ecrans}$ avant de se coucher. Ces actions permettent de $\textrm{r\'eduire}$ le stress.

**[-3-]**\

$\textrm{Gr\^ace}$ $\textrm{\`a}$ ces changements, les jeunes peuvent mieux dormir et avoir plus $\textrm{d'\'energie}$ pendant la $\textrm{journ\'ee}$. Ces habitudes sont $\textrm{b\'en\'efiques}$ pour la $\textrm{sant\'e}$ et le $\textrm{bien-\^etre}$. Il est donc important d'adopter une routine du soir plus calme.

**Choisissez un titre $\textbf{appropri\'e}$ de la liste pour remplir chaque blanc dans le texte.**     [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Organisation de la $\textrm{journ\'ee}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Solutions $\textrm{propos\'ees}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{R\'esultats}$ $\textrm{observ\'es}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Situation $\textrm{probl\'ematique}$

- E.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{Activit\'es}$ quotidiennes

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Habitudes des jeunes

**$\textbf{R\'epondez}$ $\textbf{\`a}$ la question suivante.** 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Quelle est la cause de la fatigue des jeunes ?    [1 mark]

**En vous basant sur les paragraphes 2–3, trouvez les mots qui ont la signification suivante :**  

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$se relaxer    [1 mark] 

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$diminuer    [1 mark] 

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$actions $\textrm{r\'ep\'et\'ees}$    [1 mark] 

$8.$ $\hspace{1em}$organisation quotidienne    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1, the text explains that young people use their phones late at night, go to bed very late and feel tired in the morning. The paragraph describes a problem affecting young people, which matches *Situation $\textit{probl\'ematique}$* (*Problematic situation*).

- *Option incorrecte :* F

- **F**: *Habitudes des jeunes* (*young people's habits*) is not correct because paragraph 1 does not only describe habits. Its main purpose is to highlight a problem caused by those habits, not simply list what young people do.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 2, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Pour $\textrm{am\'eliorer}$ le sommeil, il est $\textrm{conseill\'e}$ de changer certaines habitudes." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$To improve sleep, it is recommended to change certain habits.") The paragraph goes on to suggest reading and avoiding screens as concrete solutions, which matches *Solutions $\textit{propos\'ees}$* (*Proposed solutions*). The verb *$\textit{conseill\'e}$* (*recommended*) signals that the paragraph is offering advice.

- *Option incorrecte :* E

- **E**: *$\textit{Activit\'es}$ quotidiennes* (*Daily activities*) is not correct because paragraph 2 does not describe everyday routines. Instead, it suggests specific changes to improve sleep, making it a paragraph about solutions rather than daily habits.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 3, the text mentions: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les jeunes peuvent mieux dormir et avoir plus $\textrm{d'\'energie}$ pendant la $\textrm{journ\'ee}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$young people can sleep better and have more energy during the day.") The paragraph describes the positive outcomes of applying the suggested changes, which matches *$\textit{R\'esultats}$ $\textit{observ\'es}$* (*Observed results*).

- *Option incorrecte :* A

- **A**: *Organisation de la $\textit{journ\'ee}$* (*Organisation of the day*) is not correct because paragraph 3 focuses on the results of adopting better habits, not on planning or organising daily activities.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ils se couchent donc très tard}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1, the text explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ils se couchent donc $\textrm{tr\`es}$ tard. Par $\textrm{cons\'equent}$, ils dorment mal et sont $\textrm{fatigu\'es}$ le matin." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$They therefore go to bed very late. As a result, they sleep badly and are tired in the morning.") The connective *par $\textit{cons\'equent}$* (*as a result*) directly links going to bed late to feeling tired, confirming it as the main cause of their fatigue.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{se détendre}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 2, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$lire un livre avant de dormir peut aider $\textrm{\`a}$ se $\textrm{d\'etendre}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$reading a book before sleeping can help to relax.") The verb *se $\textit{d\'etendre}$* (*to relax*) means to unwind and release tension, which matches the meaning of *se relaxer* (*to relax*).

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{réduire}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 2, the text explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ces actions permettent de $\textrm{r\'eduire}$ le stress." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$These actions allow stress to be reduced.") The verb *$\textit{r\'eduire}$* (*to reduce*) means to make something smaller or less intense, which matches the meaning of *diminuer* (*to decrease*).

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{habitudes}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 2, the text mentions: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$il est $\textrm{conseill\'e}$ de changer certaines habitudes." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it is recommended to change certain habits.") The word *habitudes* (*habits*) refers to actions repeated regularly over time, which matches the meaning of actions *$\textit{r\'ep\'et\'ees}$* (*repeated actions*).

$8.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{routine}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 3, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$adopter une routine du soir plus calme." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to adopt a calmer evening routine.") The word *routine* refers to a regular and organised way of doing things each day, which matches the meaning of *organisation quotidienne* (*daily organisation*).
