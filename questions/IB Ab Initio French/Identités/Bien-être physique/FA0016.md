# FA0016

[Note maximale : 5\] 

**Une $\textbf{journ\'ee}$ active**

Le matin, Lucas fait de la marche $\text{\`{a}}$ pied et du v$\text{\'{e}}$lo. Apr$\text{\`{e}}$s l'école, il va $\text{\`{a}}$ la salle de sport. Il n'aime pas rester assis toute la journ$\text{\'{e}}$e. Le soir, il se repose. Il aime une routine simple au quotidien.

**R$\textbf{\'{e}}$pondez aux questions suivantes.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Citez les **deux** activit$\text{\'{e}}$s faites en d$\text{\'{e}}$but de journ$\text{\'{e}}$e.[2 marks]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{O\`{u}}$ se rend Lucas apr$\text{\`{e}}$s les cours ?[1 mark]

**Choisissez les deux affirmations vraies.** [2 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Lucas aime rester sans bouger.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Le soir est un moment calme. 

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Ses habitudes sont simples. 

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Sa routine change tous les jours.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des r$\textit{\'{e}}$ponses suivantes :*

* $\textrm{(de la) marche (\`{a} pied) }$

* $\textrm{(du) v\'{e}lo}$[2 marks]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le matin, Lucas fait de la marche $\text{\`{a}}$ pied et du v$\text{\'{e}}$lo." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$In the morning, Lucas goes walking and cycling.") The expression *d$\textit{\'{e}}$but de journ$\textit{\'{e}}$e* in the question refers to *le matin* in the text, confirming these activities take place early in the day.

:br$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(\`{a}) la salle de sport}$[1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{Apr\`{e}s}$ l'$\text{\'{e}}$cole, il va $\text{\`{a}}$ la salle de sport." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$After school, he goes to the gym.") The question uses the verb*se rend* ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$goes to"), which has the same meaning as *va* (*goes*) used in the text.

$3 - 4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les r$\textit{\'{e}}$ponses peuvent $\textit{\^{e}}$tre $\textit{\'{e}}$crites dans n'importe quel ordre.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, C}$        [2 marks]

* **B**: The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le soir, il se repose." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$In the evening, he rests.") The verb *se repose* (*rests*) confirms that the evening is a calm and quiet moment for Lucas.

* **C**: The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il aime une routine simple au quotidien." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$He likes a simple daily routine.") The adjective *simple* (*simple*) and the noun *routine* (*routine*) confirm that his daily habits are straightforward and uncomplicated.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, D

* **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Lucas aime rester sans bouger" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Lucas likes to stay still") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il n'aime pas rester assis toute la journ$\text{\'{e}}$e." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$He does not like to stay seated all day.") The negation *n'aime pas* (*does not like*) directly contradicts the idea that he enjoys being inactive.

* **D**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Sa routine change tous les jours" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$His routine changes every day") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il aime une routine simple au quotidien." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$He likes a simple daily routine.") The word *routine* (*routine*) implies consistency and regularity, which directly contradicts the idea that his habits change from one day to another.
