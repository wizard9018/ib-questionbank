## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{fille}$    [1 mark]

- Pierre says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'ai eu un rendez-vous avec une fille qui s'appelle Pauline." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I had a date with a girl called Pauline.") *Qui s'appelle Pauline* (*called Pauline*) identifies who he met, and *une fille* (*a girl*) describes who she is.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{au début}$    [1 mark]

- Pierre says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Au $\textrm{d\'ebut}$, elle semblait $\textrm{tr\`es}$ cool et amusante." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$At first, she seemed very cool and fun.") The expression *au $\textit{d\'ebut}$* (*at first/at the beginning*) introduces his initial positive impression of Pauline, before things changed.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{parle}$    [1 mark]

- Pierre explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Pauline parle tout le temps et elle $\textrm{n'\'ecoute}$ presque jamais." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Pauline talks all the time and she almost never listens.") The verb *parle* (*talks*) and the expression *tout le temps* (*all the time*) confirm she talks too much.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{elle s'énerve}$    [1 mark]

- Pierre says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$quand quelque chose ne lui $\textrm{pla\^{\i}t}$ pas, elle $\textrm{s'\'enerve}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$when something does not please her, she gets annoyed.") The word *quand* (*when*) introduces the trigger, and the verb *$\textit{s'\'enerve}$* (*gets annoyed/angry*) describes her reaction.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{les autres}$    [1 mark]

- Pierre says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$elle critique beaucoup les autres." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$she criticises other people a lot.") The verb *critique* (*criticises*) and the adverb *beaucoup* (*a lot*) confirm that Pauline regularly judges other people.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(avoir) un autre rendez-vous (avec elle)}$    [1 mark]

- Pierre says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je ne souhaite pas avoir un autre rendez-vous avec elle." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I do not wish to have another date with her.") The negation *ne souhaite pas* (*does not wish to*) and the expression *un autre rendez-vous* (*another date*) directly confirm what he does not want to repeat.
