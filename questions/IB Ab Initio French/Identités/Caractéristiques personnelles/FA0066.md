# FA0066

[Note maximale : 5\]

**Vous allez entendre un professeur qui se pr$\textbf{\'{e}}$sente $\textbf{\`{a}}$ la classe.**

:audio{#00300eef-cf26-41bf-b8ad-0b538d024485 aid="00300eef-cf26-41bf-b8ad-0b538d024485"}

**R$\textbf{\'{e}}$pondez aux questions suivantes.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Comment s'appelle le professeur ?    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Comment est le professeur $\text{\`{a}}$ l'$\text{\'{e}}$cole ?[1 mark]

**Choisissez la bonne r$\textbf{\'{e}}$ponse.**

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Comment est le professeur avec ses amis ?    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Calme.

* B.$\hspace{1em}\text{Agr\'{e}able}$.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Responsable.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Qu'est-ce que le professeur aime apprendre ?    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Des activit$\text{\'{e}}$s physiques.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Des langues diff$\text{\'{e}}$rentes.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Des programmes humoristiques.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Comment se sent le professeur en g$\text{\'{e}}$n$\text{\'{e}}$ral ?[1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Curieux.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Responsable.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Optimiste.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{François}$    [1 mark]

- The teacher says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je m'appelle $\textrm{Fran\c{c}ois}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$My name is $\textrm{Fran\c{c}ois}$.") This directly tells us his name.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{responsable}$    [1 mark]

- The teacher says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{\`A}$ $\textrm{l'\'ecole}$, je suis responsable, parce que je fais toujours mon travail $\textrm{\`a}$ temps." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$At school, I am responsible, because I always do my work on time.") He explains why he is responsible and links it specifically to school, confirming this is how he behaves in that context.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The teacher says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Avec mes amis, je suis sympathique et ouvert." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$With my friends, I am friendly and open.") He describes himself as *sympathique* (*friendly*) specifically when with his friends, and this has the same meaning as *$\textit{agr\'eable}$* (*pleasant*).

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C

- **A** : *Calme* (*Calm*) is not correct because the teacher says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je suis un professeur calme et $\textrm{organis\'e}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am a calm and organised teacher.") Here, *calme* describes how he is as a teacher, not how he is with his friends.

- **C** : *Responsable* (*Responsible*) is not correct because the teacher says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{\`A}$ $\textrm{l'\'ecole}$, je suis responsable." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$At school, I am responsible.") This refers to how he behaves at school, not with his friends.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The teacher says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$J'aime apprendre de nouvelles langues parce que je suis une personne curieuse." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I like learning new languages because I am a curious person.") He clearly says he enjoys learning de *nouvelles langues* (*new languages*) and gives his reason for it, confirming that different languages are what he likes to learn.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C

- **A** : *Des $\textit{activit\'es}$ physiques* (*Physical activities*) is not correct because the teacher says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je $\textrm{d\'eteste}$ le sport." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I hate sport.") He clearly dislikes physical activities.

- **C** : *Des programmes humoristiques* *(Comedy shows)* is not correct because the audio does not mention comedy shows at any point. The expression de *bonne humeur* (*in a good mood*) which appears later describes how he feels, not what he watches or enjoys.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The teacher says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je suis assez positif et je suis presque toujours de bonne humeur." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am quite positive and I am almost always in a good mood.") He describes himself as positive and almost always happy, which shows an optimistic outlook matching *optimiste* (*optimistic*).

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B

- **A** : *Curieux* (*Curious*) is not correct because the teacher says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je suis une personne curieuse." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am a curious person.") This describes his interest in learning new things, not how he feels in general.

- **B** : *Responsable* (*Responsible*) is not correct because the teacher says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{\`A}$ $\textrm{l'\'ecole}$, je suis responsable." This describes how he behaves professionally at school, not his general emotional state.
