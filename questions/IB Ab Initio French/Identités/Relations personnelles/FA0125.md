# FA0125

[Note maximale : 15\] 

**Utilisez, en fonction des propositions, le type de texte le plus $\textbf{appropri\'e}$. $\textbf{\'Ecrivez}$ entre 60 et 90 mots.**

Votre $\textrm{école}$ organise une $\textrm{journée}$ sur $\textrm{l'amitié}$. Le journal de $\textrm{l'école}$ publie des textes $\textrm{d'élèves}$ pour partager leurs $\textrm{idées}$. $\textrm{Écrivez}$ un texte pour le journal de votre $\textrm{école}$ pour $\textrm{présenter}$ un(e) ami(e) proche et expliquer pourquoi cette personne est importante pour vous.

|$\textrm{Article}$|$\textrm{Blog}$|$\textrm{Courriel}$|

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer:}}$

**Une amie importante**\

*12 mars*\

Par Sofia

$\underline{\textrm{Marking Criteria Feedback}}$

**Criterion A (Language)**:

- Use the present tense throughout: *elle a, elle est, elle aide, elle $\textit{\'ecoute}$*.

- Key structures to get right: *il/elle a + $\textit{\^age}$*; *il/elle est* + adjectif.

- Include simple vocabulary about relationships: *amie, $\textit{probl\`eme}$, conseils, $\textit{\'el\`eves}$*.

- Use *quand* + present tense to describe a situation: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$quand je suis $\textrm{stress\'ee}$ avant un $\textrm{contr\^ole}$, elle $\textrm{m'\'ecoute}$".

- Use toujours to express a consistent behaviour: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$elle est toujours $\textrm{l\`a}$ quand j'ai un $\textrm{probl\`eme}$".

- Use *on peut* + infinitive to express a general idea: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$une personne sur qui on peut compter".

- Common errors include repetition of *elle est*, missing agreement, and very short sentences.

**Criterion B (Message)**:

- A good answer covers two content points:\

    $1.\hspace{1em}$a description of the friend (age, personality, daily behaviour)\

    $2.\hspace{1em}$an explanation of why this person is important.

-  A strong answer includes a developed example to support ideas: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$quand je suis $\textrm{stress\'ee}$ avant un $\textrm{contr\^ole}$, elle $\textrm{m'\'ecoute}$ et elle me donne de bons conseils”. It can include a general statement to express a broader idea about friendship: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Pour moi, une bonne amie est une personne sur qui on peut compter”. 

-  Use *et* to add ideas, *parce que* to give a reason, and *par exemple* to illustrate. 

-  Weak answers describe the friend but do not explain why the person is important, or do not include an example. Make sure you organise your ideas clearly in paragraphs.

**Criterion C (Conceptual understanding**):

- **Article** is the most appropriate text type. You are writing for readers of a school publication. The purpose is to present a person and share ideas in a clear and organised way. A strong response includes all the conventions of an article, as in the model answer: a title (*Une amie importante*), a date (*12 mars*), and the name of the writer (*Par Sofia*). The text is organised in columns/paragraphs and the tone is neutral, without direct address to a specific person. 

-  **Blog** is generally appropriate. It allows personal expression and similar content, but in this task the text is written for a school publication, which makes an article more suitable.

-  **Courriel** is generally inappropriate. It is written to one specific person, which does not match the purpose of addressing a wider audience.
