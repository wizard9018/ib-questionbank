# FA0091

[Note maximale : 5\]

**Vous allez entendre Muhammad parler de son meilleur ami.**

:audio{#551e70a0-54b5-4de1-83d5-47a7eba5c7d4 aid="551e70a0-54b5-4de1-83d5-47a7eba5c7d4"}

**Choisissez les deux affirmations vraies.**    [2 marks]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Muhammad pense qu'il est utile d'avoir une personne tr$\text{\`{e}}$s proche.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Ali n'est pas dans la m$\text{\^{e}}$me $\text{\'{e}}$cole que Muhammad.

* C.$\hspace{1em}\text{Apr\`{e}s}$ les cours, les deux amis prennent souvent un repas ensemble.

* D.$\hspace{1em}$Ils ne discutent qu'au coll$\text{\`{e}}$ge.

**Choisissez la bonne r$\textbf{\'{e}}$ponse.**

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Comment Muhammad d$\text{\'{e}}$crit-il son ami ?[1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Il est triste.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Il est serviable.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Il est impatient.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Pourquoi Muhammad appr$\text{\'{e}}$cie-t-il son ami ?[1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 2.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C}$        [2 marks]

- **A** : Muhammad states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Pour moi, c'est important d'avoir un meilleur ami." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$For me, it is important to have a best friend.") The expression *c'est important* (*it is important*) confirms that Muhammad believes having a close friend is valuable.

- **C** : Muhammad states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{Apr\`es}$ $\textrm{l'\'ecole}$, nous mangeons." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$After school, we eat.") The time reference *$\textit{apr\`es}$ $\textit{l'\'ecole}$* (*after school*) confirms that the two friends regularly share a meal together after lessons.

- *Options incorrectes :*  B, D

- **B** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ali n'est pas dans la $\textrm{m\^eme}$ $\textrm{\'ecole}$ que Muhammad" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ali is not in the same school as Muhammad") is not correct because Muhammad says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$il est dans ma classe." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$he is in my class.") Being in the same class confirms they attend the same school.

- **D** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ils ne discutent qu'au $\textrm{coll\`ege}$" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$They only talk at school") is not correct because Muhammad says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{Apr\`es}$ $\textrm{l'\'ecole}$, nous mangeons et nous parlons." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$After school, we eat and we talk.") The verb *parlons* (*we talk*) confirms that they also discuss things after school, not only during school hours.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- Muhammad says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$il m'aide souvent." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$he often helps me.") The verb *aide* (*helps*) and the adverb *souvent* (*often*) confirm that Ali is described as someone who is regularly helpful, which matches *serviable* (*helpful/obliging*).

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C

- **A** : *Il est triste* (*He is sad*) is not correct because Muhammad says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Quand je suis triste, il me soutient." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$When I am sad, he supports me.") Here, *triste* (*sad*) describes Muhammad's own emotional state in certain moments, not a characteristic of Ali.

- **C** : *Il est impatient* (*He is impatient*) is not correct because Muhammad says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Moi, je suis impatient." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Me, I am impatient.") The pronoun *moi* (*me*) confirms that it is Muhammad who is impatient, not Ali.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- Muhammad says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Quand je suis triste, il me soutient." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$When I am sad, he supports me.") The word *quand* (*when*) introduces a difficult moment, and the verb *soutient* (*supports*) confirms that Muhammad appreciates his friend for being there for him in hard times.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B

- **A** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Parce qu'il l'aide en classe" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Because he helps him in class") is not correct because, although Muhammad says *il m'aide souvent* (*he often helps me*), the audio does not specify that this help takes place in class.

- **B** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Parce qu'il est toujours avec lui" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Because he is always with him") is not correct because the audio mentions specific moments they spend together, such as after school, but does not say that Ali is always with Muhammad.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- Muhammad says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Quand je suis avec lui, je suis toujours content." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$When I am with him, I am always happy.") The adjective *content* (*happy/pleased*) and the adverb *toujours* (*always*) directly confirm how Muhammad feels when he is with his friend.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B

- **A** : *Triste* (*Sad*) is not correct because, although Muhammad says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Quand je suis triste" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$When I am sad"), this describes a general situation where he needs support, not how he feels when he is with Ali. The audio clearly states $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je suis toujours content" when he is with his friend.

- **B** : *Impatient* (*Impatient*) is not correct because Muhammad says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Moi, je suis impatient." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Me, I am impatient.") This describes his own general personality trait, not the feeling he experiences when spending time with Ali.
