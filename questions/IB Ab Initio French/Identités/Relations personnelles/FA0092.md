# FA0092

[Note maximale : 5\]

**Vous allez entendre un jeune qui cherche une personne pour l'aider.**

:audio{#ea9c8f06-b87a-40c4-bb8a-972e63a55bc2 aid="ea9c8f06-b87a-40c4-bb8a-972e63a55bc2"}

**Choisissez les deux affirmations vraies.**    [2 marks]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Yassine voudrait trouver une personne pour le soutenir $\text{\`{a}}$ la maison.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Yassine n'a jamais de probl$\text{\`{e}}$mes avec ses parents.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Ses parents pensent qu'il ne fait pas assez d'efforts.

* D.$\hspace{1em}$Il ne fait pas ses devoirs quotidiennement.

**Choisissez la bonne r$\textbf{\'{e}}$ponse.**

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Quel type de personne Yassine cherche-t-il ?    [1 mark]

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Une personne peu compr$\text{\'{e}}$hensive.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 2.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C}$        [2 marks]

- **A** : Yassine states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je cherche une personne qui peut m'aider avec mes parents." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am looking for a person who can help me with my parents.") The verb *cherche* (*is looking for*) and the phrase *m'aider avec mes parents* (*help me with my parents*) confirm that Yassine wants support in dealing with his situation at home.

- **C** : Yassine states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mes parents pensent que je ne travaille pas assez." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$My parents think I do not work hard enough.") The verb *pensent* (*think*) confirms that this is his parents' opinion, showing they believe he is not making enough effort.

- *Options incorrectes :* B, D

- **B** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Yassine n'a jamais de $\textrm{probl\`emes}$ avec ses parents" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Yassine never has problems with his parents") is not correct because Yassine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'ai souvent des conflits $\textrm{\`a}$ la maison." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I often have conflicts at home.") The adverb *souvent* (*often*) confirms that he regularly has problems with his parents.

- **D** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il ne fait pas ses devoirs quotidiennement" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$He does not do his homework daily") is not correct because Yassine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je fais mes devoirs tous les jours." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I do my homework every day.") The expression *tous les jours* (*every day*) has the same meaning as *quotidiennement* (*daily*), confirming he does his homework regularly.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- Yassine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je voudrais parler avec une personne calme et $\textrm{compr\'ehensive}$. Cette personne peut $\textrm{m'\'ecouter}$ et me donner des conseils." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I would like to talk with a calm and understanding person. This person can listen to me and give me advice.") The adjectives *calme* (*calm*) and *$\textit{compr\'ehensive}$* (*understanding*), combined with *$\textit{m'\'ecouter}$* (*listen to me*), describe someone who is patient and attentive.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B

- **A** : *Une personne jeune* (*A young person*) is not correct because Yassine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je $\textrm{pr\'ef\`ere}$ quelqu'un qui $\textrm{conna\^{\i}t}$ les $\textrm{probl\`emes}$ des jeunes." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I prefer someone who knows the problems of young people.") This refers to someone who understands young people's issues, not necessarily someone who is young themselves.

- **B** : *Une personne peu $\textit{compr\'ehensive}$* (*A person who is not very understanding*) is not correct because Yassine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$une personne calme et $\textrm{compr\'ehensive}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$a calm and understanding person.") The adjective *$\textit{compr\'ehensive}$* (*understanding*) directly contradicts *peu $\textit{compr\'ehensive}$* (*not very understanding*).

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- Yassine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je parle parfois avec mes amis, mais ce n'est pas toujours facile." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I sometimes talk with my friends, but it is not always easy.") The word *mais* (*but*) introduces a reservation, and the expression *ce n'est pas toujours facile* (*it is not always easy*) suggests that Yassine finds it difficult to open up fully to his friends.

- *Options incorrectes :* B, C

- **B** : *Ses amis sont $\textit{compr\'ehensifs}$* (*His friends are understanding*) is not correct because Yassine describes the kind of person he is looking for as *calme et $\textit{compr\'ehensive}$.* This describes an ideal person he has not yet found, not his existing friends.

- **C** : *Ses amis connaissent bien ses $\textit{probl\`emes}$* (*His friends know his problems well*) is not correct because Yassine says he wants $\text{\textquotedblleft}$quelqu'un qui $\textrm{conna\^{\i}t}$ les $\textrm{probl\`emes}$ des jeunes." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$someone who knows the problems of young people.") This is a quality he is still searching for, not something he has found in his friends.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- Yassine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$La semaine prochaine, je vais essayer de rencontrer un conseiller $\textrm{\`a}$ $\textrm{l'\'ecole}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Next week, I am going to try to meet a school counsellor.") The expression *la semaine prochaine* (*next week*) and the near future construction *je vais essayer de* (*I am going to try to*) confirm that meeting a school counsellor is Yassine's plan for the near future.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C

- **A** : *Parler avec un ami de ses $\textit{probl\`emes}$* (*Talk with a friend about his problems*) is not correct because Yassine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je parle parfois avec mes amis." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I sometimes talk with my friends.") This describes something he already does occasionally, not a future intention.

- **C** : *Passer plus de temps $\textit{\`a}$ $\textit{l'\'ecole}$* (*Spend more time at school*) is not correct because Yassine mentions the school only in the context of meeting a counsellor. The audio does not say he plans to spend more time there in general.
