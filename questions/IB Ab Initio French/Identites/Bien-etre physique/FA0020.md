# FA0020

[Note maximale : 7\] 

**$\textbf{M\'edecin}$ en mission**

Dans cette interview, un $\textrm{m\'edecin}$ parle de son $\underline{\textrm{expérience}}$ dans des missions humanitaires et de l'$\underline{\textrm{impact}}$ de ce travail sur sa vie depuis plusieurs $\textrm{ann\'ees}$.

**Pourquoi avez-vous choisi de travailler dans ce $\underline{\textrm{domaine}}$ ?**\

J'ai toujours voulu aider les autres. Travailler dans $\textrm{diff\'erents}$ pays me permet de $\textrm{d\'ecouvrir}$ de nouvelles cultures et de me sentir utile. Cela me donne aussi une autre $\underline{\textrm{vision}}$ du monde et de ma propre vie. J'aime soigner les personnes qui sont dans le besoin et leur apporter du $\textrm{r\'econfort}$. Je comprends mieux les $\textrm{difficult\'es}$ des gens et cela me motive encore plus dans mon travail.

**Quelle est votre plus belle $\textbf{exp\'erience}$ dans ce travail?**\

Une de mes plus belles $\textrm{exp\'eriences}$ a $\textrm{\'et\'e}$ d'aider des patients dans un village $\textrm{isol\'e}$. Les conditions $\textrm{\'etaient}$ difficiles, mais les gens $\textrm{\'etaient}$ $\textrm{tr\`es}$ reconnaissants. Cette $\textrm{exp\'erience}$ m'a appris $\textrm{\`a}$ $\textrm{\^etre}$ plus patient et $\textrm{\`a}$ $\textrm{appr\'ecier}$ les choses simples. Elle m'a aussi $\textrm{montr\'e}$ l'importance de rester en bonne $\textrm{sant\'e}$ pour pouvoir aider les autres.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{H}$    [1 mark]

- The word *$\textit{v\'ecu}$* is correct because in the introduction, the text uses the word *$\textit{exp\'erience}$*. The word *$\textit{exp\'erience}$* refers to something a person has lived through, and *$\textit{v\'ecu}$* (*lived experience*) expresses the same idea in this context.

- *Option incorrecte :* C

- **C**: *Essai* (*attempt/test*) is not correct because it refers to trying something out. In the text, *$\textit{exp\'erience}$* describes something lived and experienced, not something attempted.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- The word *effet* is correct because in the introduction, the text uses the word *impact*. The word *impact* refers to the result or consequence of something, and *effet* (*effect*) expresses the same idea.

- *Option incorrecte :* G

- **G**: *Collision* (*collision/crash*) is not correct because it refers to a physical impact between objects. In the text, impact is used in an abstract sense to describe the influence of the work on the doctor's life, not a physical event.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

- The word *secteur* is correct because in the interview, the text uses the word *domaine*. The word *domaine* refers to a field or area of work, and *secteur* (*sector*) has the same meaning in this context.

- *Option incorrecte :* B

- **B**: *$\textit{Activit\'e}$* (*activity*) is not correct because it refers to a specific action or task. In the text, *domaine* describes a broader field of work, not a particular activity.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- The word *perception* is correct because in the interview, the doctor states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cela me donne aussi une autre vision du monde et de ma propre vie." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$This also gives me a different vision of the world and my own life.") The word *vision* refers to how someone understands or sees the world, and *perception* expresses the same idea.

- *Option incorrecte :* A

- **A**: *Opinion* (*opinion*) is not correct because it refers to a personal belief or judgement on a specific matter. In the text, *vision* describes a broader and more general way of seeing and understanding the world.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Vrai}$    [1 mark]

- Justification : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$dans un village $\textrm{isol\'e}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$in an isolated village.")\

\

In paragraph 3, the doctor explains that the experience took place in an isolated village. The word *$\textit{isol\'e}$* (*isolated*) confirms that the location was far from cities and not easily accessible, which matches the idea of a remote location.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Faux}$    [1 mark]

- Justification : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Les conditions $\textrm{\'etaient}$ difficiles." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$The conditions were difficult.")\

\

In paragraph 3, the text clearly states that the conditions were difficult. The aword *difficiles* (*difficult*) directly contradicts the statement that the environment was without difficulties.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Faux}$    [1 mark]

- Justification :  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cette $\textrm{exp\'erience}$ m'a appris $\textrm{\`a}$ $\textrm{\^etre}$ plus patient." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$This experience taught me to be more patient.")\

\

In paragraph 3, the doctor states that the experience taught him to be more *patient* (*patient/calm*). The word *patient* describes a calmer and more composed attitude, which directly contradicts the statement that the experience made the doctor more nervous.
