# FA0078

[Note maximale : 5\] 

**Vous allez entendre Val$\textbf{\'{e}}$rie parler de son mode de vie.**

:audio{#26672995-2b8d-47b9-a4a1-c24d28d76700 aid="26672995-2b8d-47b9-a4a1-c24d28d76700"}

**Choisissez les trois affirmations vraies.** [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{Val\'erie}$ $\textrm{s'entra\^{\i}ne}$ tous les jours pour rester en bonne condition physique.

- B.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{Val\'erie}$ consomme $\textrm{r\'eguli\`erement}$ de la nourriture peu saine.

- C.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{Val\'erie}$ fait attention $\textrm{\`a}$ $\textrm{\'eviter}$ les aliments riches en sucre et en $\textrm{mati\`eres}$ grasses.

- D.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{Val\'erie}$ ne consomme ni alcool ni tabac.

- E.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{Val\'erie}$ se couche rarement $\textrm{t\^ot}$.

- F.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{Val\'erie}$ a $\textrm{particip\'e}$ $\textrm{\`a}$ une $\textrm{comp\'etition}$ sportive $\textrm{r\'ecemment}$.

**Choisissez la bonne $\textbf{r\'eponse}$.**  

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Que va faire $\textrm{Val\'erie}$ pour se $\textrm{pr\'eparer}$ $\textrm{\`a}$ la $\textrm{comp\'etition}$ ?    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Garder le $\textrm{m\^eme}$ rythme.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Surveiller davantage son alimentation.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Faire du sport un jour sur deux.

**$\textbf{R\'epondez}$ $\textbf{\`a}$ la question suivante.** 

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}\textrm{O\`u}$ s'est-elle fait mal ?    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$

*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, D}$        [3 marks]

- **A** : $\textrm{Val\'erie}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je fais une heure de sport par jour pour garder la forme." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I do one hour of sport per day to stay in shape.") The expression *par jour* (*per day*) confirms she trains every day to stay in good physical condition.

- **C** : $\textrm{Val\'erie}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{J'\'evite}$ les graisses et les sucreries." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I avoid fats and sugary foods.") The verb *$\textit{\'evite}$* (*avoid*) confirms she is careful not to consume foods high in fat and sugar.

- **D** : $\textrm{Val\'erie}$ states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je ne bois pas d'alcool et ne fume pas." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I do not drink alcohol and do not smoke.") The double negation *ne... pas* confirms that she consumes neither alcohol nor tobacco.

- *Options incorrectes :* B, E, F

- **B** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{Val\'erie}$ consomme $\textrm{r\'eguli\`erement}$ de la nourriture peu saine" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{Val\'erie}$ regularly eats unhealthy food") is not correct because $\textrm{Val\'erie}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je ne mange pas de malbouffe." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I do not eat junk food.") The negation *ne... pas* confirms she avoids unhealthy food entirely.

- **E** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{Val\'erie}$ se couche rarement $\textrm{t\^ot}$" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{Val\'erie}$ rarely goes to bed early") is not correct because $\textrm{Val\'erie}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je me couche toujours $\textrm{t\^ot}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I always go to bed early.") The word *toujours* (*always*) confirms going to bed early is a consistent part of her routine, which directly contradicts *rarement* (*rarely*).

- **F** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{Val\'erie}$ a $\textrm{particip\'e}$ $\textrm{\`a}$ une $\textrm{comp\'etition}$ sportive $\textrm{r\'ecemment}$" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{Val\'erie}$ recently took part in a sporting competition") is not correct because $\textrm{Val\'erie}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$La semaine prochaine, je vais participer $\textrm{\`a}$ un tournoi important." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Next week, I am going to take part in an important tournament.") The expression *la semaine prochaine* (*next week*) and the near future construction *je vais participer* (*I am going to take part*) confirm the competition has not yet taken place.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{Val\'erie}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je vais manger encore plus $\textrm{\'equilibr\'e}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am going to eat even more healthily.") The words *encore plus* (*even more*) confirm she will increase her attention to her diet.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C

- **A**: *Garder le $\textit{m\^eme}$ rythme* (*Keep the same rhythm*) is not correct because, although $\textrm{Val\'erie}$ says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je vais continuer $\textrm{\`a}$ faire du sport tous les jours" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am going to continue doing sport every day"), the addition of $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je vais manger encore plus $\textrm{\'equilibr\'e}$" confirms she is actively improving her preparation, not simply maintaining it.

- **C**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Faire du sport un jour sur deux" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Do sport every other day") is not correct because $\textrm{Val\'erie}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je vais continuer $\textrm{\`a}$ faire du sport tous les jours." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am going to continue doing sport every day.") The expression *tous les jours* (*every day*) directly contradicts *un jour sur deux* (*every other day*).

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(au) bras}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{Val\'erie}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la semaine $\textrm{derni\`ere}$, je me suis $\textrm{bless\'ee}$ au bras." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$last week, I injured my arm.") The expression *se blesser au bras* (*to injure one's arm*) directly identifies the location of the injury. The time reference *la semaine $\textit{derni\`ere}$* (*last week*) confirms it happened recently.
