# FA0076

[Note maximale : 5\] 

**Vous allez entendre Hac$\textbf{\`{e}}$ne parler de son r$\textbf{\'{e}}$gime alimentaire.**

:audio{#1b3f576d-d4e8-4122-b15d-c82d41dc8b39 aid="1b3f576d-d4e8-4122-b15d-c82d41dc8b39"}

**$\textbf{R\'epondez}$ $\textbf{\`a}$ la question suivante.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ Citez **deux** aliments $\textrm{qu' Hac\`ene}$ consomme au petit $\textrm{d\'ejeuner}$.     [2 marks] 

**Choisissez la bonne $\textbf{r\'eponse}$.** 

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$O$\text{\`{u}}$  $\textrm{Hac\`ene}$ prend-il son $\textrm{d\'ejeuner}$ ?    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{\`A}$ la maison.

- B.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{\`A}$ la cantine du $\textrm{lyc\'ee}$.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Au restaurant.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Quelle boisson prend-il de temps en temps le soir ?    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$De l'eau.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Un chocolat chaud.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Un jus d'orange.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Quand boit-il beaucoup d'eau ?    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Quand il voyage.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Tous les jours.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Seulement le soir.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.*

- $\textrm{des tartines}$

- $\textrm{un jus d'orange}$        [2 marks]

- $\textrm{Hac\`ene}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le matin, je mange souvent des tartines et je bois un jus d'orange." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$In the morning, I often eat tartines and I drink an orange juice.") The time reference *le matin* (*in the morning*) confirms this refers to breakfast. The word *et* (*and*) introduces the two items he consumes.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{Hac\`ene}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{\`A}$ midi, $\textrm{\`a}$ la cantine du $\textrm{lyc\'ee}$, je prends du poulet avec du riz et une pomme." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$At midday, at the school canteen, I have chicken with rice and an apple.") The time reference *$\textit{\`a}$ midi* (*at midday*) confirms this is lunchtime, and *la cantine du $\textit{lyc\'ee}$* (*the school canteen*) directly identifies where he eats.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C

- **A** : *$\textit{\`A}$ la maison* (*At home*) is not correct because, although $\textrm{Hac\`ene}$ says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le soir, $\textrm{\`a}$ la maison, je mange une soupe ou une salade", the time reference *le soir* (*in the evening*) confirms this refers to dinner, not lunch.

- **C** : *Au restaurant* (*At a restaurant*) is not correct because the audio does not mention a restaurant at any point. The only location mentioned for a midday meal is *la cantine du $\textit{lyc\'ee}$* (*the school canteen*).

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{Hac\`ene}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Parfois, je bois un chocolat chaud." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Sometimes, I drink a hot chocolate.") The word *parfois* (*sometimes*) has the same meaning as *de temps en temps* (*from time to time*) used in the question, and the context of the evening meal confirms this is an occasional evening drink.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C

- **A** : *De l'eau* (*Water*) is not correct because, although $\textrm{Hac\`ene}$ says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je bois toujours beaucoup d'eau pendant la $\textrm{journ\'ee}$", the word *toujours* (*always*) and the expression *pendant la $\textit{journ\'ee}$* (*during the day*) confirm this is a regular daytime habit, not an occasional evening drink.

- **C** : *Un jus d'orange* (*An orange juice*) is not correct because $\textrm{Hac\`ene}$ says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le matin, je bois un jus d'orange." The time reference *le matin* (*in the morning*) confirms this is a breakfast drink, not an evening one.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{Hac\`ene}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je bois toujours beaucoup d'eau pendant la $\textrm{journ\'ee}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I always drink a lot of water during the day.") The adverb *toujours* (*always*) confirms this is a daily habit, which matches *tous les jours* (*every day*). The expression *pendant la $\textit{journ\'ee}$* (*during the day*) confirms it happens throughout each day.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C

- **A** : *Quand il voyage* (*When he travels*) is not correct because the audio does not mention travel at any point. 

- **C** : *Seulement le soir* (*Only in the evening*) is not correct because $\textrm{Hac\`ene}$ says *pendant la $\textit{journ\'ee}$* (*during the day*), which covers the whole day, not just the evening.
