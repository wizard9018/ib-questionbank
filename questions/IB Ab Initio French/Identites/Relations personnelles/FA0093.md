# FA0093

[Note maximale : 5\]

**Vous allez entendre une annonce dans un $\textbf{coll\`ege}$.**

:audio{#61183198-3199-47f3-9208-d49335177436 aid="61183198-3199-47f3-9208-d49335177436"}

**Choisissez les deux affirmations vraies.**     [2 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Une élève cherche le collège Jean-Moulin. 

- B.$\hspace{1em}$La jeune fille est nouvelle.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Samira connaît déjà très bien le collège.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$La personne qui fait l'annonce cherche à retrouver Samira.

**Remplissez les blancs suivants avec trois mots maximum pour chaque réponse.**     [3 marks]

**Une situation difficile**

Samira était censée être avec **[-3-]** Sa cousine a peur de la réaction de **[-4-]**. Si quelqu'un la voit, il peut informer un **[-5-]**.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 2.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B, D}$        [2 marks]

- **B** : The audio states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Samira vient d'arriver dans ce $\textrm{coll\`ege}$ aujourd'hui." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Samira has just arrived at this school today.") The expression *vient d'arriver* (*has just arrived*) and the time reference *aujourd'hui* (*today*) confirm that Samira is new to the school.

- **D** : The audio states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Une $\textrm{\'el\`eve}$ cherche sa cousine dans le $\textrm{coll\`ege}$ Jean-Moulin." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$A student is looking for her cousin at the $\textrm{Coll\`ege}$ Jean-Moulin.") The announcement then provides detailed information about Samira's appearance and clothing to help identify her, confirming that the purpose is to find Samira and reunite her with her cousin.

- *Options incorrectes :*  A, C

- **A** : *Une $\textit{\'el\`eve}$ cherche le $\textit{coll\`ege}$ Jean-Moulin* (*A student is looking for the $\textit{Coll\`ege}$ Jean-Moulin*) is not correct because the audio states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Une $\textrm{\'el\`eve}$ cherche sa cousine." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$A student is looking for her cousin.") The student is looking for a person, not for the school building.

- **C** : *Samira $\textit{conna\^{\i}t}$ $\textit{d\'ej\`a}$ $\textit{tr\`es}$ bien le $\textit{coll\`ege}$* (*Samira already knows the school very well*) is not correct because the audio states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$elle ne $\textrm{conna\^{\i}t}$ pas encore bien les lieux." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$she does not yet know the school well.") The word *encore* (*yet*) confirms she has not had time to familiarise herself with the school.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{sa cousine}$    [1 mark]

- The audio states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Sa cousine devait rester avec elle, mais elles se sont $\textrm{s\'epar\'ees}$ pendant la pause." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Her cousin was supposed to stay with her, but they got separated during the break.") The verb *devait* (*was supposed to*) confirms that the cousin was meant to accompany Samira, making her absence the reason the situation is difficult.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ses parents}$    [1 mark]

- The audio states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Elle est maintenant $\textrm{inqui\`ete}$, car ses parents ne vont pas $\textrm{\^etre}$ contents." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$She is now worried, because her parents are not going to be happy.") The word *$\textit{inqui\`ete}$* (*worried*) and the word *car* (*because*) directly link the cousin's anxiety to the anticipated reaction of Samira's parents.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(un) surveillant}$    [1 mark]

- The audio states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Si vous voyez Samira, merci de $\textrm{pr\'evenir}$ un surveillant." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$If you see Samira, please inform a supervisor.") *Si vous voyez* (*if you see*) and *$\textit{pr\'evenir}$* (*to inform*) confirm that anyone who spots Samira should alert a school supervisor who can then help reunite her with her cousin.
