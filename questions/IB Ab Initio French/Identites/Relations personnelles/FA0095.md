# FA0095

[Note maximale : 5\]

**Vous allez entendre un message vocal de Thomas $\textbf{\`{a}}$ son amie In$\textbf{\`{e}}$s.**

:audio{#daae5b2b-b29c-41d5-af5e-9d1bdc25c186 aid="daae5b2b-b29c-41d5-af5e-9d1bdc25c186"}

**$\textbf{R\'epondez}$ aux questions suivantes.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Qu'est-ce que Thomas demande $\textrm{\`a}$ $\textrm{In\`es}$ ?    [1 mark] 

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}\textrm{\`A}$ quel moment les proches intervient-elle dans une relation, selon $\textrm{L\'ea}$ ?    [1 mark] 

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Que pense Thomas des avis $\textrm{donn\'es}$ par les proches ?    [1 mark] 

**Choisissez la bonne $\textbf{r\'eponse}$.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Thomas $\textrm{\'etait}$ $\textrm{\'etonn\'e}$ car $\textrm{L\'ea}$...    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ne veut pas se marier.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$souhaite se marier $\textrm{t\^ot}$.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$parle beaucoup de sa famille.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Thomas $\textrm{h\'esite}$ car...    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$il ne s'entend pas bien avec $\textrm{L\'ea}$.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$il pense que $\textrm{L\'ea}$ n'est pas $\textrm{int\'eressante}$.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Ils ne voient pas le futur de la $\textrm{m\^eme}$ $\textrm{mani\`ere}$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(un) conseil}$    [1 mark]

- Thomas says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'ai besoin d'un conseil." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I need advice.") The expression *j'ai besoin de* (*I need*) directly identifies what Thomas is asking $\textrm{In\`es}$ for. The question uses *demander* (*to ask for*) while the audio uses *avoir besoin de* (*to need*), both confirming that he is seeking advice.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{quand la relation devient sérieuse}$    [1 mark]

- Thomas explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la famille qui aime $\textrm{\^etre}$ $\textrm{impliqu\'ee}$ quand la relation devient $\textrm{s\'erieuse}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the family who likes to be involved when the relationship becomes serious.") The word *quand* (*when*) directly introduces the moment at which family members intervene, according to $\textrm{L\'ea}$. The verb *$\textit{impliqu\'ee}$* (*involved*) has the same meaning as *intervient* (*intervenes*) used in the question.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(ils/elles) peuvent être utiles}$    [1 mark]

- Thomas states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les conseils de la famille peuvent $\textrm{\^etre}$ utiles." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the advice of the family can be useful.") The modal verb *peuvent* (*can*) and the adjective *utiles* (*useful*) confirm that Thomas thinks family opinions can have value, even though he ultimately believes each person should decide alone.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- Thomas says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ce qui m'a surpris, c'est qu'elle veut se marier assez jeune." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$What surprised me is that she wants to get married quite young.") The expression $\text{\textquotedblleft}$what surprised me" shows that $\textrm{L\'ea's}$ wish to get married young is what surprised him. 

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C

- **A** : *Ne veut pas se marier* (*does not want to get married*) is not correct because Thomas says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$elle veut se marier assez jeune." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$she wants to get married quite young.") $\textrm{L\'ea}$ clearly wants to get married, which is the opposite of this option.

- **C** : *Parle beaucoup de sa famille* (*talks a lot about her family*) is not correct because, although Thomas mentions that $\textrm{L\'ea's}$ family likes to be involved, the audio clearly states that what surprised him was specifically her wish to marry young, not how much she talks about her family.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- Thomas says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je me demande si on voit l'avenir de la $\textrm{m\^eme}$ $\textrm{fa\c{c}on}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I wonder if we see the future in the same way.") The verb *se demander* (*to wonder*) confirms his hesitation, and the expression de *la $\textit{m\^eme}$ $\textit{fa\c{c}on}$* (*in the same way*) shows that he is unsure whether they share the same vision for the future.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B

- **A** : *Il ne s'entend pas bien avec $\textit{L\'ea}$* (*He does not get along well with $\textit{L\'ea}$*) is not correct because Thomas says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$elle est $\textrm{tr\`es}$ $\textrm{int\'eressante}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$she is very interesting.") This positive description confirms that he gets along with her and finds her interesting.

- **B** : *Il pense que $\textit{L\'ea}$ n'est pas $\textit{int\'eressante}$* (*He thinks $\textit{L\'ea}$ is not interesting*) is not correct because Thomas explicitly says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$elle est $\textrm{tr\`es}$ $\textrm{int\'eressante}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$she is very interesting.") The word *$\textit{tr\`es}$* (*very*) confirms he finds her interesting.
