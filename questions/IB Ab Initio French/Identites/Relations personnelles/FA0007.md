# FA0007

[Note maximale : 5\] 

**Khadija**

Coucou ! Je vais vous parler de ma meilleure amie, Khadija. Je m'entends bien avec elle. Nous passons beaucoup de temps ensemble, surtout le week-end. Nous aimons aller au cin$\text{\'{e}}$ma, au parc ou $\text{\`{a}}$ la biblioth$\text{\`{e}}$que. Avec elle, on s'amuse toujours.

Et vous ? Qui est votre meilleur ami ? Laissez-moi un commentaire !

**Choisissez la bonne r$\textbf{\'{e}}$ponse.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Ce texte est...    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$un article de journal.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$une publicit$\text{\'{e}}$.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$un blog.

* D.$\hspace{1em}$une lettre formelle.

**R$\textbf{\'{e}}$pondez aux questions suivantes.**

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Avec qui la narratrice fait-elle des activit$\text{\'{e}}$s ?[1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Quand se retrouvent-elles principalement ?    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Citez **deux** endroits o$\text{\`{u}}$ elles vont ensemble.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* The text is written in a personal and informal style, using casual language such as *Coucou !* (*Hey!*) and direct address to the reader. The last line says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Laissez-moi un commentaire !" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Leave me a comment!") This invitation to interact with readers is a key feature of *un blog* (*a blog*), where the writer shares personal content and invites responses.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D

* **Option A**, *un article de journal* (*a newspaper article*) is not correct because a newspaper article uses formal language and presents factual information or news. This text is a personal, informal account of a friendship.

* **Option B**, *une publicit$\textit{\'{e}}$* (*an advert*) is not correct because an advert tries to sell or promote a product or service. This text does not promote anything, it simply describes a personal relationship.

* **Option D**, *une lettre formelle* *(a formal letter*) is not correct because a formal letter follows a specific structure and uses formal language. This text uses informal expressions such as *Coucou !* (*Hey!*) and *on s'amuse* (*we have fun*, which are typical of informal writing, not a formal letter.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$  *L'une des r$\textit{\'{e}}$ponses suivantes :*

* $\textrm{Khadija}$

* $\textrm{(ma) meilleure amie}$        [1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je vais vous parler de ma meilleure amie, Khadija." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am going to talk to you about my best friend, Khadija.") and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous passons beaucoup de temps ensemble." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We spend a lot of time together.") The pronoun *nous* (*we*) confirms that it is the narrator and Khadija who spend time together and do activities. Both Khadija and *ma meilleure amie* are acceptable as they refer to the same person.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{le week-end}$    [1 mark]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous passons beaucoup de temps ensemble, surtout le week-end." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We spend a lot of time together, especially at the weekend.") The adverb *surtout* (*especially*) indicates that the weekend is the main time they spend together, making *le week-end* the most precise and complete answer.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*La r$\textit{\'{e}}$ponse doit inclure deux des trois options indiqu$\textit{\'{e}}$es ci-dessous, dans n'importe quel ordre :*

* $\textrm{cin\'{e}ma}$

* $\textrm{parc}$

* $\textrm{(la) biblioth\`{e}que}$[2 marks]

* The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous aimons aller au cin$\text{\'{e}}$ma, au parc ou $\text{\`{a}}$ la biblioth$\text{\`{e}}$que." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We like to go to the cinema, the park or the library.") The verb*aller* (*to go*) followed by three locations introduced by *au* and *$\textit{\`{a}}$ la* provides a clear list of places they visit together. Any two of the three answers are acceptable.
