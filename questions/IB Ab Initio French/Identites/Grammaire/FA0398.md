# FA0398

**Complete the sentence. Use *ne* or *n'*.**

$\hspace{1em}$Mes cousins **\[-1-]** se disputent jamais.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Je **\[-2-]** invite plus mon voisin.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Vous **\[-3-]** dites rien $\text{\`{a}}$ votre $\text{fr\`{e}re}$.    [1 mark]

**Put the words in the correct order. Write the complete sentence.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$pas / ma / ne / voisine / sourit    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ne / soeur / m'/ $\text{\'{e}coute}$ / ma / pas    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ne}$    [1 mark]

* The next word is *se*, which starts with a consonant, so use *ne*. The reflexive pronoun stays between *ne* and *disputent*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{n'}$    [1 mark]

* *Invite* starts with a vowel, so *ne* becomes *n'* immediately before it.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ne}$    [1 mark]

* *Dites* starts with a consonant, so use *ne*. *Rien* is already after the verb and means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nothing”: *Vous ne dites rien $\textit{\`{a}}$ votre $\textit{fr\`{e}re}$.*

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Ma voisine ne sourit pas.}$    [1 mark]

* *Ma voisine* is the subject. Put *ne* before the conjugated verb *sourit* and *pas* after it.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Ma soeur ne m'\'{e}coute pas.}$    [1 mark]

* *Ma soeur* is the subject. Put *ne* before the object pronoun *m'* and *pas* after *$\textit{\'{e}coute}$*. Keep *ne*, because its next word begins with *m*, even though the verb starts with a vowel.
