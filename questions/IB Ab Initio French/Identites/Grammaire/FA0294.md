# FA0294

**Complete the sentences. Use the correct form of *choisir* in the present tense.**

$\hspace{1em}$Nous **\[-1-]** des fruits pour le $\text{go\^{u}ter}$.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Pour le $\text{d\'{e}jeuner}$, Amelle **\[-2-]** une salade.    [1 mark]

**Match each sentence to the correct form of the verb *finir* in the present tense. There are more options than sentences.**[3 marks]

| Sentence$\hspace{4em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$Options |

| :- | :- |

Tu **\[-3-]** ton sandwich. | $\hspace{3em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$finissons |

Mon $\text{p\`{e}re}$ **\[-4-]** son $\text{caf\'{e}}$. | $\hspace{3em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$finit |

Mes soeurs **\[-5-]** leur repas. | $\hspace{3em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$finis |

| $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$finissent |

| $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$E.$\hspace{1em}$finir |

| | $\hspace{3em}$F.$\hspace{1em}$finissez |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{choisissons}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is *nous*, so the correct form of *choisir* is *choisissons*. Regular *-IR* verbs take the ending *-issons* with *nous*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{choisit}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *Amelle* refers to one person and corresponds to *elle*. The correct form of *choisir* is therefore *choisit*, with the ending *-it*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is *tu*, so the correct form of *finir* is *finis*. Regular *-IR* verbs take the ending *-is* with *tu*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D, E, F

* **A** : *Finissons* is the *nous* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **B** : *Finit* is the *il/elle/on* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **D** : *Finissent* is the *ils/elles* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **E** : *Finir* is the infinitive; a conjugated form is *required*.

* **F** : *Finissez* is the *vous* form, but the subject is *tu*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *mon $\textit{p\`{e}re}$* is singular and corresponds to *il*. The correct form of *finir* is *finit*, with the ending *-it*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D, E, F

* **A** : *Finissons* is the *nous* form, but *mon $\textit{p\`{e}re}$* corresponds to *il*.

* **C** : *Finis* is the *je/tu* form, not the *il* form.

* **D** : *Finissent* is plural, but *mon $\textit{p\`{e}re}$* is singular.

* **E** : *Finir* is the infinitive; a conjugated form is required.

* **F** : *Finissez* is the *vous* form, but *mon $\textit{p\`{e}re}$* corresponds to *il*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *mes soeurs* is plural and corresponds to *elles*. The correct form of *finir* is *finissent*, with the ending *-issent*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C, D, E, F

* **A** : *Finissons* is the *nous* form; *mes soeurs* does not include the speaker.

* **B** : *Finit* is singular, but *mes soeurs* is plural.

* **C** : *Finis* is the *je/tu* form, but *mes soeurs* corresponds to elles.

* **E** : *Finir* is the infinitive; a conjugated form is required.

* **F** : *Finissez* is the *vous* form; the sentence refers to the sisters.
