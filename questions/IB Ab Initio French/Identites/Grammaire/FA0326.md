# FA0326

**Complete the sentences. Use the correct form of the possessive adjective that refers to the subject.** 

$\hspace{1em}$Je parle avec **[-1-]** frère tous les jours.     [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Tu rends visite à  **[-2-]** grands-parents chaque semaine.     [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Sandra et Joanna habitent **[-3-]** mère.    [1 mark]

**Put the words in the correct order. Write the complete sentence.** 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$sœur / les / a  / cheveux / ma / blonds    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$devons / nous / devoirs / nos / faire    [1 mark]

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$Olivia / café / son / boit    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{mon}$    [1 mark]

* *Mon* is correct because *je* is the owner and *$\textit{fr\`{e}re}$* is masculine and singular.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{tes}$    [1 mark]

* *Tes* is correct because *tu* is the owner and *grands-parents* is masculine and plural.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{leur}$    [1 mark]

* *Leur* is correct because *Sandra et Joanna* refer to *elles* and are the owners. *$\textit{M\`{e}re}$* is feminine and singular.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Ma soeur a les cheveux blonds.}$    [1 mark]

* *Ma* is used before the noun *soeur* because *soeur* is feminine and singular. The subject *ma soeur* comes before the verb *a*. The physical features *les cheveux bruns* come after the verb.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Nous devons faire nos devoirs.}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *nous* comes before the verb *devons*. The second verb, *faire*, comes after *devons* as it is an infinitive that follows that modal verb. *Nos* is used before *devoirs* because nous is the owner and *devoirs* is a masculine and plural noun. The structure *nos devoirs* comes after the verbs *devons faire* as it explains what *nous* has to do.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Olivia boit son caf\'{e}.}$    [1 mark]

* *Olivia* is the subject of *boit* and therefore placed before the verb. *Son* is used before the noun *$\textit{caf\'{e}}$* because the owner is Olivia, which refers to *elle*. The noun *$\textit{caf\'{e}}$* is masculine and singular. The structure *son $\textit{caf\'{e}}$* comes after the verb as it explains what Olivia is drinking.
