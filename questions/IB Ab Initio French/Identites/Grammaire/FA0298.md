# FA0298

**Complete the sentences. Use the correct form of *attendre* in the present tense.**

$\hspace{1em}$Nous **\[-1-]** l'heure du $\text{go\^{u}ter}$.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Au restaurant, Amelle **\[-2-]** sa soupe.    [1 mark]

**Match each sentence to the correct form of the verb *vendre* in the present tense. There are more options than sentences.** [3 marks]

| Sentence | $\hspace{4em}$Options |

| :- | :- |

| 3.$\hspace{1em}$Tu **\[-3-]** des boissons. | $\hspace{4em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$vends |

| 4.$\hspace{1em}$Ma tante **\[-4-]** du pain au $\text{march\'{e}}$. | $\hspace{4em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$vendent |

| 5.$\hspace{1em}$Mes voisins **\[-5-]** des $\text{l\'{e}gumes}$ frais. | $\hspace{4em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$vendons |

| | $\hspace{4em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$vendt |

| | $\hspace{4em}$E.$\hspace{1em}$vend |

| | $\hspace{4em}$F.$\hspace{1em}$vendez |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{attendons}$    [1 mark]

- The subject *nous* requires the ending *-ons*. Add it to the stem *attend-* to form *attendons*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{attend}$    [1 mark]

- The subject *Amelle* refers to one person and corresponds to *elle*. The correct form is the stem *attend-* with no added ending: *attend*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- The subject *tu* requires the ending *-s*. The correct form of *vendre* is therefore *vends*.

- *Options incorrectes :* B, C, D, E, F

- **B** : *Vendent* is the *ils/elles* form, but the subject is *tu*.

- **C** : *Vendons* is the *nous* form, but the subject is *tu*. 

- **D** : *Vendt* incorrectly adds *-t* to the stem *vend-*. This form is not part of the present-tense conjugation of *vendre*.

- **E** : *Vend* is the *il/elle/on* form, not the *tu* form.

- **F** : *Vendez* is the *vous* form, not the *tu* form.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- The subject *ma tante* is singular and corresponds to *elle*. The correct form of *vendre* is *vend*, with no added ending.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C, D, E, F

- **A** : *Vends* is the *je/tu* form, but *ma tante* corresponds to *elle*.

- **B** : *Vendent* is the *ils/elles* form, but *ma tante* is singular.

- **C** : *Vendons* is the *nous* form. *Ma tante* does not include the speaker.

- **D** : *Vendt* incorrectly adds *-t* to the stem *vend-*. This form is not part of the present-tense conjugation of *vendre*.

- **F** : *Vendez* is the *vous* form. The sentence refers to the aunt.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The subject *mes voisins* is plural and corresponds to *ils*. The correct form is *vendent*, with the ending *-ent*.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D, E, F

- **A** : *Vends* is the *je/tu* form, but *mes voisins* corresponds to *ils*.

- **C** : *Vendons* is the *nous* form. *Mes voisins* does not include the speaker.

- **D** : *Vendt* incorrectly adds *-t* to the stem *vend-*. This form is not part of the present-tense conjugation of *vendre*.

- **E** : *Vend* is the *il/elle/on* form, but *mes voisins* is plural and corresponds to *ils*.

- **F** : *Vendez* is the *vous* form. The sentence refers to the neighbours.
