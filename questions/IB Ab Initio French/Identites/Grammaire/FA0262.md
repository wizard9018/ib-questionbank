# FA0262

**Complete the sentences. Use the correct form of *$\textbf{\textit{\^{e}tre}}$* in the present tense.** [4 marks]

Ma sœur s'appelle $\text{L\'{e}a}$. Elle **\[-1-]** $\text{tr\`{e}s}$ sportive.

Paul et Karim **\[-2-]** mes meilleurs amis.

Vous **\[-3-]** $\text{tr\`{e}s}$ gentil, monsieur.

Toi et moi, nous **\[-4-]** belges !

**Put the words in the correct order. Write the full sentence.**

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$est / mon / sympathique / professeur    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{est}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is *Elle* so the correct form is *est*. We use *$\textit{\^{e}tre}$* to describe a person.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{sont}$    [1 mark]

* *Paul et Karim* refers to two people and can be replaced by *ils*, so the correct form is *sont*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{\^{e}tes}$    [1 mark]

* *Vous* requires the form *$\textit{\^{e}tes}$*. We use *$\textit{\^{e}tre}$* to describe a person.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{sommes}$    [1 mark]

* *Toi et moi* includes the speaker and therefore corresponds to *nous*. The correct form is *sommes*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Mon professeur est sympathique.}$    [1 mark]

* *Mon professeur* is a singular subject that corresponds to *il* so it is followed by *est*. The adjective *sympathique* comes after the verb.
