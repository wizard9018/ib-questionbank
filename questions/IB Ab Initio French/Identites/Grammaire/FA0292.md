# FA0292

**The paragraph below contains three errors related to verbs in the present tense. Find the errors and write the correct words in the order in which they appear.** [3 marks]

Dans ma famille, nous participons tous aux $\text{t\^{a}ches}$ $\text{m\'{e}nag\`{e}res}$. Ma soeur et mon petit $\text{fr\`{e}re}$ cuisine pendant que je $\text{pr\'{e}pares}$ la table. Ensuite, mes parents lave la vaisselle.

**Complete the paragraph with the present tense form of the verbs in brackets.** [5 marks]

Dans ma famille, nous **\[-4-]** (*passer*) beaucoup de temps ensemble. Mon $\text{fr\`{e}re}$ et moi **\[-5-]** (*aider*) souvent nos parents $\text{\`{a}}$ la maison et nous **\[-6-]** (*partager*) les $\text{m\^{e}mes}$ loisirs. Le week-end, mes parents **\[-7-]** (*$\textit{pr\'{e}parer}$*) un repas en famille et nous **\[-8-]** (*$\textit{\'{e}couter}$*) les histoires de nos grands-parents.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{cuisinent}$    [1 mark]

* *Ma $\textit{s\oeur}$ et mon petit $\textit{fr\`{e}re}$* refers to more than one person *ils*. With *ils*, regular *-er* verbs end in *-ent*, so the correct form is *cuisinent*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{pr\'{e}pare}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is *je*. With *je*, regular *-er* verbs end in *-e*, so the correct form is *$\textit{pr\'{e}pare}$*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{lavent}$    [1 mark]

* *Mes parents* refers to *ils*. With *ils*, regular *-er* verbs end in *-ent*, so the correct form is *lavent*.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Dans ma famille, nous participons tous aux $\text{t\^{a}ches}$ $\text{m\'{e}nag\`{e}res}$. Ma soeur et mon petit $\text{fr\`{e}re}$ cuisinent pendant que je $\text{pr\'{e}pare}$ la table. Ensuite, mes parents lavent la vaisselle.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{passons}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is *nous*. With *nous*, regular *-er* verbs end in *-ons*, so the correct form is *passons*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{aidons}$    [1 mark]

* *Mon $\textit{fr\`{e}re}$ et moi* includes the speaker and refers to *nous*. With *nous*, regular *-er* verbs end in *-ons*, so the correct form is *aidons*.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{partageons}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is *nous*. *Partager* is a *-ger* verb, so before *-ons* it takes an extra *e* to keep the soft \*$\text{\textquotedblleft}$j" sound: *partageons*.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{pr\'{e}parent}$    [1 mark]

* *Mes parents* refers to *ils*. With *ils*, regular *-er* verbs end in *-ent*, so the correct form is *$\textit{pr\'{e}parent}$*.

$8.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{\'{e}coutons}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is *nous*. With *nous*, regular *-er* verbs end in *-ons*, so the correct form is *$\textit{\'{e}coutons}$*.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Dans ma famille, nous passons beaucoup de temps ensemble. Mon $\text{fr\`{e}re}$ et moi aidons souvent nos parents $\text{\`{a}}$ la maison et nous partageons les $\text{m\^{e}mes}$ loisirs. Le week-end, mes parents $\text{pr\'{e}parent}$ un repas en famille et nous $\text{\'{e}coutons}$ les histoires de nos grands-parents.
