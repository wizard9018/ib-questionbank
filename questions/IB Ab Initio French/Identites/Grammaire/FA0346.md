# FA0346

**Complete the sentence. Use the correct question word.**

$\hspace{1em}$**\[-1-]** te disputes-tu avec ton $\text{fr\`{e}re}$ ? - Parce qu'il prend mes affaires.    [1 mark]

**Choose the correct question word or expression.**

$\hspace{1em}$**\[-2-]** retrouves-tu tes cousins ? - Le dimanche.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Pourquoi

* B.$\hspace{1em}\text{O\`{u}}$

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Quand

* D.$\hspace{1em}$Combien

$\hspace{1em}$Tu as **\[-3-]** $\text{fr\`{e}res}$ et soeurs ? - Deux.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$combien

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Pourquoi}$    [1 mark]

* *Parce qu'il prend mes affaires* gives the reason for the argument. *Pourquoi* asks for that reason.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *Le dimanche* identifies when the meetings take place. *Quand* asks about that time.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D

* **A** : *Pourquoi* asks for a reason, but the reply gives a day.

* **B** : *$\textit{O\`{u}}$* asks about a place.

* **D** : *Combien* asks about a quantity, not when the meetings take place.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *Deux* gives the number of brothers and sisters. Before the nouns *$\textit{fr\`{e}res}$ et soeurs*, the quantity expression must include *de*: *combien de $\textit{fr\`{e}res}$ et soeurs*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D

* **A** : *Combien* alone leaves out the required *de* before *$\textit{fr\`{e}res}$ et soeurs*.

* **B** : *Qui* asks about a person's identity.

* **D** : *Quand* asks about a time.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* *J'$\textit{\'{e}cris}$ $\textit{\`{a}}$ ma $\textit{grand-m\`{e}re}$* identifies the person receiving the message. Keep *$\textit{\`{a}}$* from *$\textit{\'{e}crire}$ $\textit{\`{a}}$ quelqu'un*: *Tu $\textit{\'{e}cris}$ $\textit{\`{a}}$ qui ?*

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D

* **A** : *Chez qui* asks at whose home something happens, but the reply names the recipient.

* **C** : *De qui* asks about whom, but the reply uses *$\textit{\`{a}}$ ma $\textit{grand-m\`{e}re}$* to identify the recipient.

* **D** : *Avec qui* asks who is writing together with the speaker, not who receives the message.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *Nous parlons de notre cousin* identifies the person being discussed. Keep *de* from *parler de quelqu'un*: *Vous parlez de qui ?*

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D

* **A** : *Chez qui* asks at whose home the conversation takes place; the reply gives its topic.

* **B** : *$\textit{\`{A}}$ qui* asks who is being spoken to, but the reply identifies the person being discussed.

* **D** : *Avec qui* asks who takes part in the conversation, but *de notre cousin* identifies its topic.
