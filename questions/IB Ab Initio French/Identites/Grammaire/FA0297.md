# FA0297

**Choose the correct form of the verb in the present tense.**

$\hspace{1em}$Je **[-1-]** des pommes.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$vend

- B.$\hspace{1em}$vendons

- C.$\hspace{1em}$vends

- D.$\hspace{1em}$vendent

$\hspace{1em}$Tu **[-2-]** ta bouteille d'eau.    [1 mark]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$perds

- B.$\hspace{1em}$perd

- C.$\hspace{1em}$perdez

- D.$\hspace{1em}$perdent

**Match each pronoun to the correct form of *attendre* in the present tense. There are more options than pronouns.** [3 marks] 

| Pronoun | $\hspace{4em}$Options |

| :- | :- |

| 3.$\hspace{1em}$Elle | $\hspace{4em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$attendons |

| 4.$\hspace{1em}$Vous | $\hspace{4em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$attend |

| 5.$\hspace{1em}$Ils | $\hspace{4em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$attendez |

| | $\hspace{4em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$attends |

| | $\hspace{4em}$E.$\hspace{1em}$attendent |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *je* requires the ending *-s*. Remove *-re* from *vendre* to get the stem *vend-*, then add *-s*: *vends*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D

* **A** : *Vend* is the *il/elle/on* form, but the subject is *je*.

* **B** : *Vendons* is the *nous* form, but the subject is *je*.

* **D** : *Vendent* is the *ils/elles* form. The plural object *des pommes* does not determine the verb ending; the subject is *je*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* The subject *tu* requires the ending *-s*. The stem of *perdre* is *perd-*, so the correct form is *perds*.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C, D

* **B** : *Perd* is the *il/elle/on* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **C** : *Perdez* is the *vous* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **D** : *Perdent* is the *ils/elles* form, but the subject is *tu*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* With *elle*, use the stem *attend-* with no added ending. The correct form is *attend*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D, E, F

* **A** : *Attendons* is the *nous* form, not the *elle* form.

* **C** : *Attendez* is the *vous* form, not the *elle* form.

* **D** : *Attends* is the *je/tu* form. No *-s* is added with *elle*.

* **E** : *Attendent* is the *ils/elles* form, but *elle* is singular.

* **F** : *Attendt* incorrectly adds *-t*. With *elle*, use *attend* with no added ending.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* The pronoun *vous* requires the ending *-ez*. The correct form is *attend- + -ez*: *attendez*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D, E, F

* **A** : *Attendons* is the *nous* form, not the *vous* form.

* **B** : *Attend* is the *il/elle/on* form, not the *vous* form.

* **D** : *Attends* is the *je/tu* form, not the *vous* form.

* **E** : *Attendent* is the *ils/elles* form, not the *vous* form.

* **F** : *Attendt* does not have the *vous* ending *-ez*. The correct form is *attendez*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

* The pronoun *ils* requires the ending *-ent*. The correct form is *attend- + -ent*: *attendent*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C, D, F

* **A** : *Attendons* is the *nous* form, not the *ils* form.

* **B** : *Attend* is the *il/elle/on* form, but *ils* is plural.

* **C** : *Attendez* is the *vous* form, not the *ils* form.

* **D** : *Attends* is the *je/tu* form, not the *ils* form.

* **F** : *Attendt* does not have the *ils/elles* ending *-ent*. The correct form is *attendent*.

**Grammar Reminder**

Regular *-RE* verbs follow the same pattern as *vendre* in the present tense. Remove *-re* from the infinitive. The remaining part is called the stem. Then add the ending that agrees with the subject.

For example: *vendre → vend-*.

| $\hspace{1em}$Subject pronoun$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$-RE ending$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$Example: Vendre$\hspace{1.5em}$ |

| :- | :- | :- |

| $\hspace{1em}$Je | $\hspace{1em}$-s | $\hspace{1em}$vends |

| $\hspace{1em}$Tu | $\hspace{1em}$-s | $\hspace{1em}$vends |

| $\hspace{1em}$Il/elle/on | $\hspace{1em}\text{-\o}$ | $\hspace{1em}$vend |

| $\hspace{1em}$Nous | $\hspace{1em}$-ons | $\hspace{1em}$vendons |

| $\hspace{1em}$Vous | $\hspace{1em}$-ez | $\hspace{1em}$vendez |

| $\hspace{1em}$Ils/elles$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$-ent | $\hspace{1em}$vendent |

Not all verbs ending in -re follow this pattern: *vendre* does, but *prendre* does not. These common verbs that follow the pattern:

* vendre: to sell

* attendre: to wait

* entendre: to hear

* $\text{r\'{e}pondre}$: to answer

* rendre: to give back

* perdre: to lose

* descendre: to go down

**Common mistake**: Do not add *-t* with *il/elle/on*. Write *il vend*, not *il vendt*. The *d* is already part of the stem *vend-*; no ending is added for these subjects.
