## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *Suis* is the correct form because the subject is *je*. The corresponding form of *$\textit{\^{e}tre}$* is *suis*.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C, D

* **B** : *Es* is used with *tu*.

* **C** : *Est* is used with *il/elle/on*.

* **D** : *Sont* is used with *ils/elles*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* *Sommes* is the correct form because the subject is *nous*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D

* **A** : *$\textit{\^{E}tes}$* is used with *vous*.

* **C** : *Sont* is used with *ils/elles*.

* **D** : *Suis* is used with *je*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* *Es* is the correct form because the subject is *tu*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D, E, F

* **A** : *Sommes* is the *nous* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **C** : *Sont* is the *ils/elles* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **D** : *Est* is the *il/elle/on* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **E** : *Suis* is the *je* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **F** : *$\textit{\^{E}tes}$* is the *vous* form, but the subject is *tu*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *Est* is the correct form because the subject is *elle*. The correct form for *il/elle/on* is est.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C, E, F

* **A** : *Sommes* is the *nous* form, but the subject is *elle*.

* **B** : *Es* is the *tu* form,  but the subject is *elle*.

* **C** : *Sont* is the *ils/elles* form,  but the subject is *elle*.

* **E** : *Suis* is the *je* form, but the subject is *elle*.

* **F** : *$\textit{\^{E}tes}$* is the *vous* form,  but the subject is *elle*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *Sont* is the correct form because the subject is *ils*. The correct form for *ils/elles* is *sont*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D, E, F

* **A** : *Sommes* is the *nous* form, but the subject is *ils*.

* **B** : *Es* is the *tu* form,  but the subject is *ils*.

* **D** : *Est* is the *il/elle/on* form, but the subject is *ils*.

* **E** : *Suis* is the *je* form, but the subject is *ils*.

* **F** : *$\textit{\^{E}tes}$* is the *vous* form,  but the subject is *ils*.

**Grammar reminder**

*$\textit{\^{E}tre}$* means *to be*. It is one of the most common verbs in French.

Use *$\textit{\^{e}tre}$* for:

* Identity: *Je suis Marie.*

* Relationship: *Tu es son amie ?*

* Nationality: *Nous sommes $\textit{fran\c{c}ais}$.*

* Profession: *Il est $\textit{m\'{e}decin}$.*

* Location: *Vous $\textit{\^{e}tes}$ $\textit{\`{a}}$ $\textit{Montr\'{e}al}$.*

* Personal qualities / characteristics: *Elles sont sympathiques.*

* Physical descriptions : *Il est grand.*

* Feelings / states: *Je suis $\textit{fatigu\'{e}}$.*

*$\textit{\^{E}tre}$* is an irregular verb, which means that its forms do not follow a regular conjugation pattern. Below is its present-tense conjugation:

| $\hspace{1em}$Subject pronoun | $\hspace{1em}\text{\^{E}tre}$ $\hspace{1.5em}$ |

| :- | :- |

| $\hspace{1em}$Je | $\hspace{1em}$suis |

| $\hspace{1em}$Tu | $\hspace{1em}$es |

| $\hspace{1em}$Il/elle/on | $\hspace{1em}$est |

| $\hspace{1em}$Nous | $\hspace{1em}$sommes |

| $\hspace{1em}$Vous | $\hspace{1em}\text{\^{e}tes}$ |

| $\hspace{1em}$Ils/elles$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$sont |

**Common mistake**: When the subject is a name or a noun phrase, first identify which subject pronoun it corresponds to. For example, *Paul et Karim = ils*, so we say *Paul et Karim sont amis*. Similarly, *ma $\textit{sœur}$ = elle*, so we say *ma $\textit{sœur}$ est sportive*.
