# FA0278

**Choose the correct plural noun form.**

$\hspace{1em}\text{Salom\'{e}}$ porte plusieurs **\[-1-]** en argent. *(bijou)*    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$bijous

* B.$\hspace{1em}$bijou

* C.$\hspace{1em}$bijoux

* D.$\hspace{1em}$bijouxs

$\hspace{1em}$Milan a les **\[-2-]** courts. *(cheveu)*    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$cheveux

* B.$\hspace{1em}$cheveus

* C.$\hspace{1em}$cheveu

* D.$\hspace{1em}$Cheveuxs

**The sentences below each contain one plural noun error. The errors are underlined. Write the correct words.**

$\hspace{1em}$Dans mon armoire, mes deux $\underline{\textrm{veste}}$ sont $\text{\`{a}}$ $\text{c\^{o}t\'{e}}$ de ma robe.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Sur cette photo, $\text{Zo\'{e}}$ montre ses deux $\underline{\textrm{brasx}}$.    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$Bastien porte des $\underline{\textrm{chaussettex}}$ jaunes.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- *Plusieurs* means more than one. *Bijou* is one of the nouns ending in *-ou* that take *-x*.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D

- **A** : *Bijous* uses the usual *-s* ending for nouns in *-ou*, but *bijou* is an exception.

- **B** : *Bijou* is singular and cannot follow plural *plusieurs* here.

- **D** : *Bijouxs* adds *-s* after the plural *-x*; only one plural ending is needed.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- *Les* and *courts* show that the noun is plural. *Cheveu* follows the usual *-eu → -eux* pattern: *cheveux*.

- *Options incorrectes :* B, C, D

- **B** : *Cheveus* adds *-s*, but *cheveu* takes *-x* in the plural.

- **C** : *Cheveu* is singular, whereas the sentence has plural *les* and *courts*.

- **D** : *Cheveuxs* adds an unnecessary *-s* after the plural *-x*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{vestes}$    [1 mark]

- *Mes deux* requires the plural of veste. Add regular *-s*: *vestes*. 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{bras}$    [1 mark]

- *Ses deux* requires the plural, but *bras* already ends in *-s* in the singular. Its plural spelling stays *bras*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{chaussettes}$    [1 mark]

- *Des* and *jaunes* show that the noun is plural. *Chaussette* takes regular *-s*.
