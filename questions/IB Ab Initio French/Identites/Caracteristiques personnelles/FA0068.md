# FA0068

[Note maximale : 6\]

**Vous allez entendre une annonce diffus$\textbf{\'{e}}$e dans un centre commercial.**

:audio{#6158dc17-a081-4b03-8e3d-16719b91d8f7 aid="6158dc17-a081-4b03-8e3d-16719b91d8f7"}

**Choisissez les trois affirmations vraies.** [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$On cherche un petit $\textrm{gar\c{c}on}$.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$On ne trouve pas l'homme adulte.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Paul a seize ans.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Paul n'est pas $\textrm{tr\`es}$ grand.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$Paul porte des $\textrm{v\^etements}$ $\textrm{d\'econtract\'es}$.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Paul a un sac blanc.

**Remplissez les blancs suivants avec trois mots maximum pour chaque $\textbf{r\'eponse}$.**[3 marks]

**$\textbf{O\`u}$ est Paul ?**

Paul est **[-4-]** dans le centre commercial Les Arcades. Si quelqu'un le voit, il peut informer un **[-5-]** du centre commercial. **[-6-]** essaient de le retrouver.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, D, E}$        [3 marks]

- **A** : The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Un enfant s'est perdu dans le centre commercial." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$A child has got lost in the shopping centre.") The noun *enfant* (*child*) and the description of Paul as *petit* (*small*) and *six ans* (*six years old*) confirm that people are looking for a young boy.

- **D** : The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il est petit." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$He is small.") The adjective *petit* (*small*) directly confirms that Paul is not very tall.

- **E** : The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$un tee-shirt rouge, un pantalon bleu et des baskets blanches." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$a red t-shirt, blue trousers and white trainers.") These items of clothing are all casual and informal, which matches *$\textit{v\^etements}$ $\textit{d\'econtract\'es}$* (*casual clothes*).

- *Options incorrectes :* B, C, F

- **B** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$On ne trouve pas l'homme adulte" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$An adult man cannot be found") is not correct because the announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Un enfant s'est perdu." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$A child has got lost.") The noun *enfant* (*child*) confirms it is a young child, not an adult man, who is missing.

- **C** :  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Paul a seize ans" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Paul is sixteen years old") is not correct because the announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$il a six ans." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$he is six years old.") The number six directly contradicts *seize* (*sixteen*).

- **F** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Paul a un sac blanc" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Paul has a white bag") is not correct because the announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$un petit sac $\textrm{\`a}$ dos vert." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$a small green backpack.") The adjective *vert* (*green*) directly contradicts *blanc* (*white*).

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{seul}$    [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$L'enfant est seul." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$The child is alone.") The adjective *seul* (*alone*) directly confirms that Paul is on his own in the shopping centre.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(un) employé}$    [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$merci de $\textrm{pr\'evenir}$ un $\textrm{employ\'e}$ du centre commercial." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$please inform an employee of the shopping centre.") The verb *$\textit{pr\'evenir}$* (*to inform*) and the noun *$\textit{employ\'e}$* (*employee*) confirm who should be contacted if Paul is spotted.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ses parents}$    [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ses parents le cherchent." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$His parents are looking for him.") The verb *cherchent* (*are looking for*) and the possessive *ses parents* (*his parents*) confirm who is trying to find Paul.
