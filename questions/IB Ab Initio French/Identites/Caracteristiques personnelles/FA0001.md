# FA0001

[Note maximale : 5\] 

**Portrait de mon meilleur ami**

Mon meilleur ami s'appelle Hugo. Il habite rue de Nantes, $\text{\`{a}}$ Rennes, dans le quartier de Villejean. Nous nous connaissons depuis longtemps. Nous $\text{\'{e}}$tudions ensemble au lyc$\text{\'{e}}$e Jeanne-d'Arc. D$\text{\`{e}}$s le premier jour, nous nous sommes tr$\text{\`{e}}$s bien entendus.

Hugo est formidable. Quand quelqu'un a un probl$\text{\`{e}}$me, il est toujours l$\text{\`{a}}$ pour aider. Beaucoup de gens l'appr$\text{\'{e}}$cient pour $\text{\c{c}}$a. Il est calme et adore lire des bandes dessin$\text{\'{e}}$es. Je l'admire parce qu'il est loyal.

Eug$\text{\`{e}}$ne

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Nantes

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Rennes

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Villejean

* D.$\hspace{1em}$Jeanne d'Arc

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Normale

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Mauvaise

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Bonne

* D.$\hspace{1em}$Excellente

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Que pensent beaucoup de personnes d'Hugo ?    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Il est tranquille.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Il se laisse aider.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Il est gentil.

* D.$\hspace{1em}$Il est ennuyeux.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* In paragraph 1, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il habite rue de Nantes, $\text{\`{a}}$ Rennes, dans le quartier de Villejean." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$He lives on rue de Nantes, in Rennes, in the Villejean neighbourhood.") This directly names the city where Hugo lives, which matches the question $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Dans quelle ville habite Hugo ?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$In which city does Hugo live?")

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D

* **A**: *Nantes* is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il habite rue de Nantes" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$He lives on rue de Nantes.") Here, *rue de Nantes* is the name of the street, not the city where Hugo lives.

* **C**: *Villejean* is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$dans le quartier de Villejean" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$in the neighbourhood of Villejean.") Here, *quartier* (*neighbourhood*) shows that Villejean is a district within the city, not the city itself.

* **D**:  *Jeanne d'Arc*is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous $\text{\'{e}}$tudions ensemble au lyc$\text{\'{e}}$e Jeanne-d'Arc." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We study together at Jeanne-d'Arc high school.") Here,*lyc$\textit{\'{e}}$e* (*high schoo*l) shows that *Jeanne-d'Arc* is the name of a school, not a city.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* In paragraph 1, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{D\`{e}s}$ le premier jour, nous nous sommes tr$\text{\`{e}}$s bien entendus." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$From the very first day, we got along very well.") This describes a relationship that has been excellent from the start, which matches the question $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Comment est la relation entre Eug$\text{\`{e}}$ne et Hugo ?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$What is the relationship between Eug$\text{\`{e}}$ne and Hugo like?") The opening*mon meilleur ami* (*my best friend*) and *formidable* (*wonderful*) further confirm this.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C

* **A**: The option *normale* (*normal*) is not correct because the text does not describe the relationship as ordinary. The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{D\`{e}s}$ le premier jour, nous nous sommes tr$\text{\`{e}}$s bien entendus." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$From the very first day, we got along very well.") Here,*tr$\textit{\`{e}}$s bien* (*very well*) shows the relationship is far above normal.

* **B**: The option *mauvaise*(bad) is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{D\`{e}s}$ le premier jour, nous nous sommes tr$\text{\`{e}}$s bien entendus." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$From the very first day, we got along very well.") The relationship is described as positive throughout, with no indication of conflict.

* **C**: The option *bonne* (*good*) is not correct because while the relationship is positive, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nous nous sommes tr$\text{\`{e}}$s bien entendus" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we got along very well.") The adverb*tr$\textit{\`{e}}$s* (*very*) elevates the relationship beyond merely good to excellent.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* In paragraph 2, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Quand quelqu'un a un probl$\text{\`{e}}$me, il est toujours l$\text{\`{a}}$ pour aider. Beaucoup de gens l'appr$\text{\'{e}}$cient pour $\text{\c{c}}$a." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$When someone has a problem, he is always there to help. Many people appreciate him for that.") This describes what many people think of Hugo, which matches the question $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Que pensent beaucoup de personnes d'Hugo ?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$What do many people think of Hugo?") The word*appr$\textit{\'{e}}$cient* (*appreciate*) shows they see him as *kind* (*gentil*).

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D

* **A**: The option $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il est tranquille" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$He is calm") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il est calme" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$He is calm.") Here, *calme* (*calm*) describes Hugo personally, but the text does not say this is specifically what *beaucoup de gens* (many people) appreciate him for.

* **B**: The option $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il se laisse aider" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$He lets himself be helped") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$il est toujours l$\text{\`{a}}$ pour aider" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$he is always there to help.") Here,*aider* (*to help*) shows Hugo helps other people, not that he accepts help from them.

* **D**: The option $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il est ennuyeux" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$He is boring") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hugo est formidable" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hugo is wonderful.") The text describes Hugo entirely positively, with no suggestion that he is boring.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{lire des bandes dessin\'{e}es}$[1 mark]

* In paragraph 2, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il est calme et adore lire des bandes dessin$\text{\'{e}}$es." $\text{\textquotedblleft}$(He is calm and loves reading comics.") The verb*adorer* (*to love/adore*) confirms that reading *bandes dessin$\textit{\'{e}}$es* (*comics*) is Hugo's favourite activity and directly answers what he likes to do.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(parce qu') il est loyal}$    [1 mark]

* In paragraph 2, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je l'admire parce qu'il est loyal." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I admire him because he is loyal.")  *Parce que* (*because*) directly links Eug$\text{\`{e}}$ne's admiration and respect to Hugo's quality of being*loyal* (*loyal*).
