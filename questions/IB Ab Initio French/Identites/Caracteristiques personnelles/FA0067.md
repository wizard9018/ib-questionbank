# FA0067

[Note maximale : 5\]

**Vous allez entendre une personne qui cherche un professeur de français.**

:audio{#b85b942c-5d17-41a7-8a2a-742e2f4b413a aid="b85b942c-5d17-41a7-8a2a-742e2f4b413a"}

**Choisissez les trois affirmations vraies.** [3 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Karine va étudier avec des amis.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Le français est une langue compliquée.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Karine veut améliorer son CV.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Karine veut rencontrer de nouvelles personnes.

- E.$\hspace{1em}$Apprendre une autre langue est divertissant.

- F.$\hspace{1em}$Karine cherche des personnes d'autres pays.

**Choisissez la bonne réponse.**  

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Quel type de professeur cherche Karine ?    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Actif.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Patient.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Responsable.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Comment est Karine ?    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Aimable et sociable.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Professionnelle et dynamique.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Intéressée et bavarde.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$

*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D, E, F}$        [3 marks]

- **D** : Karine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je veux simplement me faire des amis." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I simply want to make friends.") The verb *me faire des amis* (*to make friends*) confirms she wants to meet new people.

- **E** : Karine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je pense qu'apprendre une langue est amusant." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I think that learning a language is fun.") The adjective *amusant* (*fun*) has the same meaning as *divertissant* (*entertaining*) used in the statement.

- **F** : Karine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$J'aime beaucoup parler avec des personnes de cultures $\textrm{diff\'erentes}$ qui vivent dans d'autres endroits." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I really like talking with people from different cultures who live in other places.") The expression *d'autres pays* (*other countries*) confirms she is interested in people from different locations.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C

- **A** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Karine va $\textrm{\'etudier}$ seule" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Karine is going to study alone") is not correct because Karine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je cherche un professeur de $\textrm{fran\c{c}ais}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am looking for a French teacher.") She is actively looking for a teacher, confirming she does not want to study alone.

- **B** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le $\textrm{fran\c{c}ais}$ est une langue $\textrm{compliqu\'ee}$" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$French is a complicated language") is not correct because Karine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$une langue facile et internationale." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$an easy and international language.") The adjective *facile* (*easy*) directly contradicts *$\textit{compliqu\'ee}$* (*complicated*).

- **C** : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Karine veut $\textrm{am\'eliorer}$ son CV" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Karine wants to improve her CV") is not correct because Karine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mon objectif n'est pas professionnel." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$My goal is not professional.") The negation *n'est pas professionnel* directly confirms she has no professional motivation for learning French.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- Karine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'aimerais avoir un professeur dynamique." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I would like to have a dynamic teacher.") The adjective *dynamique* (*dynamic*) has the same meaning as *actif* (*active/energetic*), describing someone full of energy and enthusiasm.

- *Options incorrectes :* B, C

- **B** : *Patient* (*Patient*) is not correct because Karine says she wants a teacher who is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$dynamique, ouvert et sociable." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$dynamic, open and sociable.") The word *patient* does not appear in the audio as a desired quality.

- **C** : *Responsable* (*Responsible*) is not correct because the audio does not mention responsibility as a quality Karine is looking for in a teacher. The qualities she describes are *dynamique*, *ouvert* and *sociable*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- Karine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je suis une personne curieuse et communicative" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am a curious and communicative person") and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'aime participer et parler beaucoup en classe." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I like to participate and talk a lot in class.") She describes herself as curious and talkative, which matches *$\textit{int\'eress\'ee}$ et bavarde* (*interested and talkative*). The word *curieuse* (*curious*) has the same meaning as *$\textit{int\'eress\'ee}$* (*interested*), and communicative has the same meaning as *bavarde* (*talkative*).

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B

- **A** : *Aimable et sociable* (*Friendly and sociable*) is not correct because these words describe the type of teacher Karine is looking for. She says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je $\textrm{pr\'ef\`ere}$ aussi un professeur ouvert et sociable." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I also prefer an open and sociable teacher.") These qualities refer to her ideal teacher, not to Karine herself.

- **B** : *Professionnelle et dynamique* (*Professional and dynamic*) is not correct because Karine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mon objectif n'est pas professionnel." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$My goal is not professional.") She clearly states her motivation is personal, not professional. As for *dynamique* (*dynamic*), Karine says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'aimerais avoir un professeur dynamique." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I would like to have a dynamic teacher.") This describes the type of teacher she wants, not how she describes herself.
