# FA0097

[Note maximale : 5\]

**Vous allez entendre une professeure parler de ses habitudes alimentaires.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Quelle mati$\text{\`{e}}$re enseigne Madame Martin ?[1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Que prend-elle au d$\text{\'{e}}$but de la journ$\text{\'{e}}$e ?[1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(le) fran\c{c}ais}$[1 mark]

* Madame Martin says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je suis professeure de fran$\text{\c{c}}$ais." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am a French teacher.") This shows that the subject she teaches is French.

:br$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{un petit d\'{e}jeuner simple}$[1 mark]

* Madame Martin says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je prends toujours un petit d$\text{\'{e}}$jeuner simple." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I always have a simple breakfast.") This shows that she has a simple breakfast at the beginning of the day.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* Madame Martin says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je pr$\text{\'{e}}$f$\text{\`{e}}$re partager des repas avec mes coll$\text{\`{e}}$gues." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I prefer to share meals with my colleagues.") This confirms that she prefers to eat accompanied by others.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C

* **B** : *Seule* (*Alone*) is not correct because Madame Martin says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je n'aime pas manger seule." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I don't like eating alone.")

* **C** : *Sans ses coll$\textit{\`{e}}$gues* (*Without her colleagues*) is not correct because Madame Martin says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je pr$\text{\'{e}}$f$\text{\`{e}}$re partager des repas avec mes coll$\text{\`{e}}$gues." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I prefer to share meals with my colleagues.") She prefers to eat with her colleagues, not without them.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* Madame Martin says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je suis curieuse." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am curious.") This shows that she likes trying different dishes because she is curious.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B

* **A** : *Elle est difficile* (*she is fussy*) is not correct because Madame Martin says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je suis curieuse." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am curious.") Her curiosity, not fussiness, explains why she enjoys trying new dishes.

* **B** : *Elle est insatisfaite* (*she is dissatisfied*) is not correct because Madame Martin says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je suis presque toujours satisfaite de mon alimentation." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am almost always satisfied with my diet.") She describes herself as satisfied, not dissatisfied, with her diet.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* Madame Martin says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je fais des choix sains." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I make healthy choices.") This shows that she prefers meals that are good for her health.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C

* **B** : *Des plats gras* (*Fatty dishes*) is not correct because Madame Martin says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je n'aime pas les plats trop gras." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I don't like food that is too fatty.") She dislikes fatty dishes, so she would not prefer them.

* **C** : *Des frites* (*Fries*) is not correct because Madame Martin says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je n'aime pas les plats trop gras comme les frites." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I don't like food that is too fatty, like fries.") Fries are given as an example of food she dislikes, not prefers.
