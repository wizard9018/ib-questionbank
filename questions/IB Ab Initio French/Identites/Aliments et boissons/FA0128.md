$\underline{\textrm{Marking Criteria Feedback}}$

**Criterion A (Language)**:

- Use the present tense for general statements: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Beaucoup $\textrm{d'\'el\`eves}$ mangent trop de malbouffe", $\text{\textquotedblleft}$une bonne alimentation aide $\textrm{\`a}$ rester en forme".

- Use the imperative to give advice: *Mangez, Buvez, Ne grignotez pas !*

- Use *pour* + infinitive to express purpose: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$manger $\textrm{\'equilibr\'e}$ est $\textrm{tr\`es}$ important pour rester en bonne $\textrm{sant\'e}$".

- Use *$\textit{\`a}$ long terme* to express long-term consequences: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{\`A}$ long terme, de mauvaises habitudes alimentaires ont des $\textrm{cons\'equences}$ $\textrm{n\'egatives}$ sur la $\textrm{sant\'e}$".

- Use *$\textit{plut\^ot}$ que* to express a preference or better alternative: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Buvez de l'eau $\textrm{plut\^ot}$ que des sodas".

- Common errors include missing partitive articles (*buvez de l'eau, not buvez eau*) and confusing the imperative with the present tense (*mangez, not vous mangez*) when giving advice.

**Criterion B (Message)**:

- A good answer covers both content points:\

    $1.\hspace{1em}$why eating a balanced diet is important (health, energy, concentration, long-term effects): $\text{\textquotedblleft}$rester en bonne $\textrm{sant\'e}$, $\textrm{\'eviter}$ la fatigue, mieux se concentrer”\

    $2.\hspace{1em}$how to eat more healthily (food choices, drinks, habits): $\text{\textquotedblleft}$mangez des fruits... buvez de l'eau... ne grignotez pas”.

- Develop the two content points - make sure you develop each one with some detail rather than simply listing risks and pieces of advice.

- Use *et* to add ideas, and *pourtant* to show contrast. 

**Criterion C (Conceptual understanding**):

- **Brochure** is the appropriate text type. The audience is the whole school, the purpose is to inform and give advice, and the tone is direct and engaging. A strong response includes all the conventions of a brochure, as in the model answer: a title (*Mangez mieux, vivez mieux !*), an eye-catching layout with subheadings (*Pourquoi manger $\textit{\'equilibr\'e}$ ?, Comment s'alimenter plus sainement ?*), bullet points (*Mangez des fruits et des $\textit{l\'egumes}$ !, Buvez de l'eau..., Ne grignotez pas !*), a direct address to the reader (*Prenez soin de votre alimentation !*), contact information (*ecole-sante@email.com*), and columns to organise the content visually. 

- **Courriel** is generally appropriate, but less effective because the message is not addressed to one specific person.

- **Journal intime** is generally inappropriate, as it is a private text and not intended for an audience.
