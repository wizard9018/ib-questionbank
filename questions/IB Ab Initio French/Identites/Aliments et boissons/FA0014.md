**Un festival culinaire lyonnais**

Chaque printemps, la ville de Lyon organise un festival culinaire $\textrm{tr\`es}$ populaire. $\textrm{L'ann\'ee}$ $\textrm{derni\`ere}$, cet $\textrm{\'ev\'enement}$ a $\textrm{attir\'e}$ de nombreux visiteurs venus de toute la France. $\textrm{L'ann\'ee}$ prochaine, les organisateurs vont proposer encore plus $\textrm{d'activit\'es}$.

Pendant trois jours, les habitants et les touristes peuvent $\textrm{d\'ecouvrir}$ gratuitement des $\textrm{sp\'ecialit\'es}$ culinaires de $\textrm{diff\'erentes}$ $\textrm{r\'egions}$ francophones. Sur la place principale, des stands proposent des plats traditionnels, du fromage artisanal et des desserts faits maison. Certains visiteurs ont $\textrm{go\^ut\'e}$ de nouveaux plats.

Les visiteurs peuvent aussi assister $\textrm{\`a}$ des $\textrm{d\'emonstrations}$ de cuisine. Les chefs expliquent les recettes et donnent des conseils utiles. Pour beaucoup, ce festival est une excellente occasion de $\textrm{d\'ecouvrir}$ la culture $\textrm{fran\c{c}aise}$ et permettra de soutenir les producteurs locaux $\textrm{\`a}$ l'avenir.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.*

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, E, G}$        [4 marks]

- **A**: In paragraph 1, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Chaque printemps, la ville de Lyon organise un festival culinaire $\textrm{tr\`es}$ populaire." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Every spring, the city of Lyon organises a very popular culinary festival.") The expression *chaque printemps* (*every spring*) confirms that the event takes place at the same time each year, since spring is a fixed and recurring season.

- **C**: In paragraph 1, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$cet $\textrm{\'ev\'enement}$ a $\textrm{attir\'e}$ de nombreux visiteurs venus de toute la France." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$this event attracted many visitors from all over France.) The phrase de *nombreux visiteurs* (*many visitors*) confirms that a large number of people attended the previous edition. The use of the $\textrm{pass\'e}$ $\textrm{compos\'e}$ *a $\textit{attir\'e}$* (*attracted*) confirms this refers to the past edition.

- **E**: In paragraph 2, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les habitants et les touristes peuvent $\textrm{d\'ecouvrir}$ gratuitement des $\textrm{sp\'ecialit\'es}$ culinaires." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$residents and tourists can discover culinary specialities for free.") The adverb *gratuitement* (*for free*) clearly confirms that visitors do not need to pay to taste the food.

- **G**: In paragraph 2, the textsays: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$des stands proposent des plats traditionnels, du fromage artisanal et des desserts faits maison." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$stalls offer traditional dishes, artisan cheese and homemade desserts.") The list of three different types of food confirms that a variety of different foods is available on the main square.

- *Options incorrectes :* B, D, F, H

- **B**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{L'\'ev\'enement}$ se $\textrm{d\'eroule}$ dans plusieurs villes en France" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$The event takes place in several cities in France") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$la ville de Lyon organise." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the city of Lyon organises.") The singular *la ville de Lyon* (*the city of Lyon*) confirms the festival takes place in one specific city, not multiple locations.

- **D**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Les organisateurs $\textrm{pr\'evoient}$ de $\textrm{r\'eduire}$ les $\textrm{activit\'es}$ $\textrm{\`a}$ l'avenir" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$The organisers plan to reduce activities in the future") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les organisateurs vont proposer encore plus $\textrm{d'activit\'es}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the organisers are going to offer even more activities.") The expression *encore plus* (*even more*) directly contradicts the idea of a reduction.

- **F**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Les visiteurs n'ont pas la $\textrm{possibilit\'e}$ de $\textrm{d\'ecouvrir}$ de nouveaux plats" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Visitors do not have the opportunity to discover new dishes") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$peuvent $\textrm{d\'ecouvrir}$ gratuitement des $\textrm{sp\'ecialit\'es}$ culinaires de $\textrm{diff\'erentes}$ $\textrm{r\'egions}$ francophones." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$can discover culinary specialities from different French-speaking regions for free.") The verb *peuvent* (*can*) and the noun *$\textit{sp\'ecialit\'es}$* (*specialities*) confirm that visitors are able to discover new dishes.

- **H**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Tous les produits $\textrm{propos\'es}$ sont $\textrm{sucr\'es}$" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$All the products offered are sweet") is not correct because the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$des plats traditionnels, du fromage artisanal et des desserts faits maison." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$traditional dishes, artisan cheese and homemade desserts.") The inclusion of *plats traditionnels* (*traditional dishes*) and *fromage artisanal* (*artisan cheese*) shows that savoury items are also available, so not all products are sweet.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{producteurs locaux}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 3, the text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$permettra de soutenir les producteurs locaux $\textrm{\`a}$ l'avenir." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$will allow the support of local producers in the future.") The verb *permettra* (*will allow*) and the infinitive *soutenir* (*to support*) confirm that the festival will provide help to *les producteurs locaux* (*local producers*) in the future, which directly completes the sentence.
