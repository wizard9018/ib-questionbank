## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Paris}$    [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$un concours de cuisine $\textrm{\`a}$ Paris." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$a cooking competition in Paris.") The word *$\textit{\`a}$* (*in*) followed by Paris identifies the location of the competition.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des $\textit{r\'eponses}$ suivantes :*

- $\textrm{18 ans}$

- $\textrm{dix-huit ans}$        [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous cherchons des personnes entre 18 et 30 ans." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We are looking for people between 18 and 30 years old.") The number 18 identifies the minimum age required.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des $\textit{r\'eponses}$ suivantes :*

- $\textrm{30 ans}$

- $\textrm{trente ans}$        [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous cherchons des personnes entre 18 et 30 ans." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We are looking for people between 18 and 30 years old.") The number 30 identifies the maximum age required.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ponctuel}$    [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il est important $\textrm{d'\^etre}$ ponctuel, car les $\textrm{\'epreuves}$ commencent $\textrm{tr\`es}$ $\textrm{t\^ot}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$It is important to be punctual, because the tasks start very early.") The expression commencent *$\textit{tr\`es}$ $\textit{t\^ot}$* (*start very early*) has the same meaning as *$\textit{d\'ebutent}$ de bonne heure* (*begin early*) in the question.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(l')apparence}$    [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$L'apparence n'est pas $\textrm{tr\`es}$ importante." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Appearance is not very important.") The expression *$\textit{crit\`ere}$ non obligatoire* (*non-compulsory criterion*) in the question corresponds to *n'est pas $\textit{tr\`es}$ importante* (*is not very important*) in the audio.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des $\textit{r\'eponses}$ suivantes :*

- $\textrm{le 5 mai}$

- $\textrm{le cinq mai}$        [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous vous attendons le 5 mai au centre culinaire de Paris." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We expect you on 5 May at the Paris culinary centre.") The expression *nous vous attendons* (*we expect you*) confirms this is the date of the event.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(au) centre culinaire de Paris}$    [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous vous attendons le 5 mai au centre culinaire de Paris." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We expect you on 5 May at the Paris culinary centre.") The word *au* (*at*) followed by *centre culinaire de Paris* identifies the venue where participants must go.
