# FA0098

[Note maximale : 5\]

**Vous allez entendre C$\textbf{\'{e}}$line parler de ses habitudes alimentaires.**

:audio{#0bd8b127-7285-48e4-b8c8-b063a4936880 aid="0bd8b127-7285-48e4-b8c8-b063a4936880"}

**Choisissez les deux affirmations vraies.**  [2 marks]

- A.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{C\'eline}$ mange normalement au $\textrm{coll\`ege}$.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Elle $\textrm{pr\'ef\`ere}$ manger des $\textrm{prot\'eines}$.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Elle $\textrm{pr\'ef\`ere}$ manger de la nourriture grasse.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Quand elle n'est pas au $\textrm{coll\`ege}$, elle mange chez elle. 

**Remplissez les blancs suivants avec trois mots maximum pour chaque $\textbf{r\'eponse}$.**[3 marks]  

**L'alimentation de $\textbf{C\'eline}$** 

$\textrm{C\'eline}$ et ses proches cuisinent des **[-3-]**. Elle adore manger des **[-4-]**. Manger sain est important pour $\textrm{\^etre}$ **[-5-]**.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 2.$ $\hspace{1em}$

*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.* 

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, D}$        [2 marks]

- **A** : $\textrm{C\'eline}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$D'habitude, je mange $\textrm{\`a}$ la cantine de mon $\textrm{\'ecole}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Usually, I eat at my school canteen.") The adverb *d'habitude* (*usually*) confirms this is her regular habit, and *la cantine de mon $\textit{\'ecole}$* (*my school canteen*) confirms she normally eats at school. 

- **D** : $\textrm{C\'eline}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le week-end, je mange $\textrm{\`a}$ la maison avec ma famille." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$At the weekend, I eat at home with my family.") The time reference *le week-end* (*at the weekend*) confirms that when she is not at school, she eats at home.

- *Options incorrectes :* B, C

- **B** : *Elle $\textit{pr\'ef\`ere}$ manger des $\textit{prot\'eines}$* (*She prefers to eat protein*) is not correct because, although $\textrm{C\'eline}$ says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je choisis souvent du poulet" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I often choose chicken"" ), she also says $\text{\textquotedblleft}$parfois je prends des $\textrm{l\'egumes}$" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$sometimes I have vegetables"), showing she eats a variety of foods, not only protein.

- **C** : *Elle $\textit{pr\'ef\`ere}$ manger de la nourriture grasse* (*She prefers to eat fatty food*) is not correct because $\textrm{C\'eline}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je n'aime pas les plats trop gras." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I do not like dishes that are too fatty.") The negation *n'aime pas* (*does not like*) directly contradicts this option.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{plats traditionnels}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{C\'eline}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous $\textrm{pr\'eparons}$ des plats traditionnels." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We prepare traditional dishes.") The verb *$\textit{pr\'eparons}$* (*prepare*) has the same meaning as *cuisinent* (*cook*) used in the question, confirming that $\textrm{C\'eline}$ and her family cook traditional dishes together at the weekend.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{fruits}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{C\'eline}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$J'aime beaucoup les fruits, surtout les oranges." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I really like fruit, especially oranges.") The adverb *beaucoup* (*a lot*) confirms her strong preference for fruit, and *surtout les oranges* (*especially oranges*) gives a specific example.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{en bonne santé}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{C\'eline}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je pense que manger $\textrm{\'equilibr\'e}$ est important pour rester en bonne $\textrm{sant\'e}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I think that eating in a balanced way is important to stay in good health.") The verb *rester* (*to stay/remain*) and the expression *en bonne $\textit{sant\'e}$* (*in good health*) confirm that healthy eating helps maintain good health.
