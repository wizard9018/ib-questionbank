## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Vrai}$    [1 mark]

- Justification : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ma famille et moi participons au $\textrm{c\'el\`ebre}$ carnaval de $\textrm{l'\^{\i}le}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$my family and I take part in the famous carnival of the island.")\

In paragraph 2, the text states that $\textrm{J\'er\'emie}$ attends the carnival with his family. The expression *ma famille et moi* (*my family and I*) confirms that he goes with his relatives, which matches the idea of going with his *proches* (*loved ones*).

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Faux}$    [1 mark]

- Justification : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$le carnaval reste un moment essentiel pour moi." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the carnival remains an essential moment for me.")\

In paragraph 4, the text explains that the carnival is still very important to him. The verb *reste* *(remains*) and the adjective *essentiel* (*essential*) confirm that the festival has not lost its appeal.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Faux}$    [1 mark]

- Justification : $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ce qui me permettra de vivre cette $\textrm{exp\'erience}$ de $\textrm{mani\`ere}$ plus intense." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$which will allow me to experience this in a more intense way.")\

In paragraph 5, the text mentions that $\textrm{J\'er\'emie}$ plans to join a dance group next year. The future tense *permettra* (*will allow*) confirms that he intends to repeat and deepen this experience.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The text includes a full postal address, a date, the greeting $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cher $\textrm{K\'evin}$" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Dear $\textrm{K\'evin}$") and the closing $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{\`A}$ $\textrm{bient\^ot}$" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$See you soon"), which are all typical features of *une lettre* (*a letter*). The writer speaks directly to one person and shares personal memories and feelings throughout.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D

- **A**: *Une $\textit{publicit\'e}$* (*an advert*) is not correct because an advert tries to persuade or sell something. This text shares a personal experience of the carnival and does not promote or advertise anything.

- **B**: *Un article* (*an article*) is not correct because an article is usually formal and objective. This text uses *je* (*I*) throughout and expresses personal feelings and memories, which is the opposite of a formal article.

- **D**: *Un courriel* (*an email*) is not correct because, although similar in tone, an email does not usually include a full postal address and a formal layout. The presence of the address $\text{\textquotedblleft}$12 rue des Cocotiers, 97110 $\textrm{Pointe-\`a-Pitre}$" confirms this is a letter.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The text is written in the first person throughout, using *je*, *ma famille et moi* and *je voulais te parler*, and describes $\textrm{J\'er\'emie's}$ own memories of and participation in the carnival. This confirms that the main purpose is to share a personal experience, which matches raconter une *$\textit{exp\'erience}$ personnelle* (*to share a personal experience*).

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D

- **A**: *Promouvoir un $\textit{\'ev\'enement}$* (*to promote an event*) is not correct because the text does not try to advertise or encourage participation in the carnival. It simply describes $\textrm{J\'er\'emie's}$ personal relationship with it.

- **C**: *$\textit{D\'ecrire}$ un $\textit{\'ev\'enement}$ $\textit{pass\'e}$* (*to describe a past event*) is not correct because the text also refers to the present and future. In paragraph 4, the text states $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Aujourd'hui encore" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Even today") and in paragraph 5, $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{L'ann\'ee}$ prochaine" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Next year"), showing the text goes beyond describing only past events.

- **D**: *Expliquer l'importance des traditions* (*to explain the importance of traditions*) is not correct because, although traditions are mentioned in paragraph 4, this is not the main purpose of the text. The overall aim is to share a personal experience of the carnival.
