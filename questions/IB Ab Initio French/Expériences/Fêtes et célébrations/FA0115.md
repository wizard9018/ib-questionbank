## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{amis}$    [1 mark]

* Olivier says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Chaque hiver, je vais au Carnaval de Qu$\text{\'{e}}$bec avec mes amis." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Every winter, I go to the Quebec Carnival with my friends.") The word*amis* identifies who accompanies Olivier to the Carnival.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{course}$    [1 mark]

* Olivier explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous sommes arriv$\text{\'{e}}$s t$\text{\^{o}}$t pour assister $\text{\`{a}}$ une course de canots sur le fleuve Saint-Laurent." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We arrived early to watch a canoe race on the Saint Lawrence River.") The word*course* identifies the event they watched first.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{artistes}$    [1 mark]

* Olivier adds: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous avons admir$\text{\'{e}}$ des sculptures de glace r$\text{\'{e}}$alis$\text{\'{e}}$es par des artistes venus de plusieurs pays." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We admired ice sculptures made by artists from several countries.") The word*artistes* identifies who created the ice sculptures.

$4 - 5.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les r$\textit{\'{e}}$ponses peuvent $\textit{\^{e}}$tre $\textit{\'{e}}$crites dans n'importe quel ordre.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, B}$        [2 marks]

* **A**: Olivier mentions: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{Malgr\'{e}}$ le froid, les rues $\text{\'{e}}$taient anim$\text{\'{e}}$es et les visiteurs semblaient tr$\text{\`{e}}$s enthousiastes." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Despite the cold, the streets were lively and visitors seemed very enthusiastic.) This confirms that the bad weather did not discourage the visitors.

* **B**: Olivier adds: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous avons d$\text{\'{e}}$gust$\text{\'{e}}$ une boisson chaude traditionnelle avant d'$\text{\'{e}}$couter un concert en plein air." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We enjoyed a hot traditional drink before listening to an open-air concert."" ) This confirms that Olivier drank a typical warm drink before the concert.

* *Options incorrectes :*  C, D

* **C** : *Le concert s'est d$\textit{\'{e}}$roul$\textit{\'{e}}$ $\textit{\`{a}}$ l'int$\textit{\'{e}}$rieur* (*The concert took place indoors*) is not correct because Olivier explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$avant d'$\text{\'{e}}$couter un concert en plein air." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$before listening to an open-air concert.") The concert took place outdoors, not indoors.

* **D** : *Cette c$\textit{\'{e}}$l$\textit{\'{e}}$bration est uniquement faite pour divertir les participants* (*This celebration is only meant to entertain participants*) is not correct because Olivier states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cette f$\text{\^{e}}$te n'$\text{\'{e}}$tait pas seulement amusante : elle permettait aussi de mieux d$\text{\'{e}}$couvrir les coutumes qu$\text{\'{e}}$b$\text{\'{e}}$coises." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$This celebration wasn't just fun: it also helped people better discover Quebec customs.") The celebration serves more than one purpose, not just entertainment.
