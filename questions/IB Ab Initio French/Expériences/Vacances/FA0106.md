# FA0106

[Note maximale : 5\]

**Vous allez entendre $\textbf{Fran\c{c}oise}$ parler de ses vacances.**

:audio{#5e914897-77d8-41c9-96f1-4928d9dcb8ce aid="5e914897-77d8-41c9-96f1-4928d9dcb8ce"}

**$\textbf{R\'epondez}$ aux questions suivantes.** 

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Qui va voyager avec $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ ?    [1 mark] 

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Quelle est la destination de $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ ?    [1 mark] 

**Choisissez la bonne $\textbf{r\'eponse}$.**

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}\textrm{O\`u}$ va dormir $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ ?    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Dans un $\textrm{h\^otel}$ en ville.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Au bord de la mer.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Dans un $\textrm{h\^otel}$ $\textrm{pr\`es}$ des $\textrm{march\'es}$.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Que va-t-elle faire en $\textrm{d\'ebut}$ de $\textrm{journ\'ee}$ ?    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Visiter les $\textrm{march\'es}$.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Nager.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Prendre l'avion.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Comment se sent $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ par rapport $\textrm{\`a}$ ce $\textrm{s\'ejour}$ ?    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{Stress\'ee}$.

- B.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{Fatigu\'ee}$.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Ravie.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{des amis}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je pars en vacances avec des amis au Maroc." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am going on holiday with friends to Morocco.") The expression *des amis* confirms who is travelling with her.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{au Maroc}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je pars en vacances avec des amis au Maroc." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am going on holiday with friends to Morocco.") The expression *au Maroc* confirms her destination.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous allons rester dans un $\textrm{h\^otel}$ confortable $\textrm{pr\`es}$ de la plage." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We are going to stay in a comfortable hotel near the beach.") The words *$\textit{pr\`es}$ de la plage* (*near the beach*) show the hotel is by the sea, which matches *au bord de la mer* (*by the seaside*).

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C

- **A** : *Dans un $\textit{h\^otel}$ en ville* is not correct because $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{pr\`es}$ de la plage." The city is mentioned in the context of the markets, not the accommodation.

- **C** : *Dans un $\textit{h\^otel}$ $\textit{pr\`es}$ des $\textit{march\'es}$* is not correct because $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{pr\`es}$ de la plage." The markets are an afternoon activity, not the location of the hotel.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le matin, je vais faire de la natation." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$In the morning, I am going to swim.") The verb *nager* (*to swim*) has the same meaning as *faire de la natation*.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C

- **A** : *Visiter les $\textit{march\'es}$* is not correct because $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{l'apr\`es-midi}$, je vais visiter les $\textrm{march\'es}$ de la ville." Visiting the markets is an afternoon activity, not a morning one.

- **C** : *Prendre l'avion* is not correct because $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je n'aime pas prendre l'avion." Taking the plane is mentioned as something she dislikes, not as a morning activity.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je suis $\textrm{tr\`es}$ contente parce que le Maroc est un pays magnifique." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am very happy because Morocco is a magnificent country.) The adjective *ravie* (*delighted*) has the same meaning as *$\textit{tr\`es}$ contente* (*very happy*).

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B

- **A** : *$\textit{Stress\'ee}$* is not correct because $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je suis un peu $\textrm{stress\'ee}$ parce que je n'aime pas prendre l'avion." While she mentions feeling stressed, her overall feeling about the trip is one of happiness.

- **B** : *$\textit{Fatigu\'ee}$* is not correct because $\textrm{Fran\c{c}oise}$ says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je suis $\textrm{tr\`es}$ $\textrm{fatigu\'ee}$ $\textrm{apr\`es}$ un long voyage." The audio mentions tiredness after travelling, not before departure.
