# FA0031

[Note maximale : 5\] 

**Mes vacances aux Antilles**

Je vais vous raconter mes vacances $\textrm{d'\'et\'e}$. Comme d'habitude, je passe deux semaines aux Antilles avec mes parents. Nous $\textrm{s\'ejournons}$ sur une $\textrm{\^{\i}le}$ magnifique, sans touristes, car nous voulons $\textrm{d\'ecouvrir}$ la culture locale et ses traditions.

Chaque matin, nous nous promenons sur la plage ou dans les $\textrm{march\'es}$ locaux $\textrm{o\`u}$ l'on trouve des fruits exotiques et des produits artisanaux. $\textrm{L'apr\`es-midi}$, nous visitons des sites culturels ou nous nous reposons tranquillement. Le soir, nous $\textrm{d\'egustons}$ des $\textrm{sp\'ecialit\'es}$ locales dans des restaurants traditionnels.

Ces vacances sont inoubliables et $\textrm{tr\`es}$ enrichissantes. Je ne veux pas rentrer $\textrm{\`a}$ Paris demain.

Et vous ? $\textrm{O\`u}$ allez-vous en vacances ?

**Trouvez les mots qui $\textbf{compl\`etent}$ les phrases suivantes. Utilisez les mots tels qu'ils apparaissent dans les paragraphes 1-2.** 

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Je passe mes vacances $\textrm{accompagn\'e}$ de...    [1 mark] 

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$La famille souhaite mieux $\textrm{conna\^{\i}tre}$ la culture de la $\textrm{r\'egion}$ et...    [1 mark] 

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Au $\textrm{march\'e}$, il y a des fruits tropicaux...    [1 mark]

**Choisissez la bonne $\textbf{r\'eponse}$.** 

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Ce texte est...    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$un rapport.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$un $\textrm{d\'epliant}$.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$une annonce.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$un blog. 

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Le but principal de ce texte est...    [1 mark] 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$d'expliquer l'histoire des Antilles.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$de partager une $\textrm{exp\'erience}$.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$de donner des conseils pour voyager.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$de comparer $\textrm{diff\'erentes}$ destinations.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{mes parents}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je passe deux semaines aux Antilles avec mes parents." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I spend two weeks in the Antilles with my parents.") The word *avec* (*with*) directly identifies who accompanies the narrator on holiday.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{ses traditions}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1, the text explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nous voulons $\textrm{d\'ecouvrir}$ la culture locale et ses traditions." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we want to discover the local culture and its traditions.") The word *et* (*and*) introduces a second element that the family wants to learn about. The possessive *ses* (*its*) refers back to *la culture locale* (*the local culture*), showing that traditions are part of what they wish to explore.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{des produits artisanaux}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 2, the text mentions: $\text{\textquotedblleft}\textrm{o\`u}$ l'on trouve des fruits exotiques et des produits artisanaux." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$where one can find exotic fruits and artisan products.") The word *et* (*and*) confirms that handmade local products are available in addition to fruits, which directly completes the sentence.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

- The text says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je passe deux semaines aux Antilles avec mes parents" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I spend two weeks in the Antilles with my parents") and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ces vacances sont inoubliables" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$These holidays are unforgettable"), sharing personal experiences and feelings throughout. The closing question $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Et vous ? $\textrm{O\`u}$ allez-vous en vacances ?" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$And you? Where do you go on holiday?") invites the audience to respond, which is a typical feature of *un blog* (*a blog*).

- *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C

- **A**: *Un rapport* (*a report*) is not correct because *a report* is usually formal and objective. This text uses *je* (*I*) throughout and expresses personal opinions and feelings, which is the opposite of a formal report.

- **B**: *Un $\textit{d\'epliant}$* (*a leaflet*) is not correct because a leaflet is used to promote a place or service. This text focuses on describing what the narrator personally did and felt, rather than persuading the reader to visit.

- **C**: *Une annonce* (*an announcement*) is not correct because an announcement is usually short and informative. This text provides a detailed and personal description of a holiday experience, which goes beyond the purpose of an announcement. 

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

- The text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Chaque matin, nous nous promenons sur la plage" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Every morning, we walk on the beach") and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ces vacances sont inoubliables" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$These holidays are unforgettable"), showing that the text describes personal activities and impressions. The main aim is to share a personal experience, which matches *partager une $\textit{exp\'erience}$* (*to share an experience*).

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D

- **A**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$D'expliquer l'histoire des Antilles" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to explain the history of the Antilles") is not correct because, although paragraph 1 mentions $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nous voulons $\textrm{d\'ecouvrir}$ la culture locale et ses traditions" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we want to discover the local culture and its traditions"), this refers to personal cultural discovery and not to explaining historical facts to the reader.

- **C**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$De donner des conseils pour voyager" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to give travel advice") is not correct because, although the narrator describes activities such as $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nous nous promenons sur la plage" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we walk on the beach") and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nous visitons des sites culturels" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we visit cultural sites"), these are personal experiences shared with the reader, not advice given to them.

- **D**: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$De comparer $\textrm{diff\'erentes}$ destinations" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to compare different destinations") is not correct because the text only mentions les Antilles as a destination. No other place is introduced for comparison, so this cannot be the main purpose of the text.
