# FA0140

\[Note maximale : 15]

**Utilisez, en fonction des propositions, le type de texte le plus $\textbf{appropri\'{e}}$. $\textbf{\'{E}crivez}$ entre 60 et 90 mots.**

Vous $\textrm{\^etes}$ en vacances dans une ville $\textrm{\`a}$ $\textrm{l'\'etranger}$. Vous voulez partager votre $\textrm{exp\'erience}$ avec des jeunes de votre $\textrm{\'ecole}$. $\textrm{\'Ecrivez}$ un texte pour $\textrm{d\'ecrire}$ vos $\textrm{activit\'es}$ et expliquer ce que vous pensez de cette ville.

$\underline{\textrm{Marking Criteria Feedback}}$

**Criterion A (Language)**:

* Use the present tense throughout: *je suis, je visite, je d$\textit{\'{e}}$couvre*.

* Use opinion phrases: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je trouve que", $\text{\textquotedblleft}\text{\`{a}}$ mon avis".

* Use *chaque jour*to express a repeated action: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Chaque jour, je visite les march$\text{\'{e}}$s locaux".

* Use adjectives to describe the city: *vivante, agr$\textit{\'{e}}$able, anim$\textit{\'{e}}$es, accueillants*.

* Remember to use *en* with vacances and with feminine countries, and *$\textit{\`{a}}$* for cities (*je suis en vacances $\textit{\`{a}}$ Cotonou*). Also, be mindful of incorrect verb forms (*je visites*) and missing articles (*je visite march$\textit{\'{e}}$s*).

**Criterion B (Message)**:

* A good answer covers both content points:\

  $1.\hspace{1em}$activities during the holiday\

  $2.\hspace{1em}$opinion about the city.

* Both ideas must be developed in detail as in the model answer: (activities → $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je visite les march$\text{\'{e}}$s locaux... je d$\text{\'{e}}$couvre... j'aime go$\text{\^{u}}$ter la cuisine locale...” ; opinion → $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ville tr$\text{\`{e}}$s vivante... rues anim$\text{\'{e}}$es... gens accueillants... id$\text{\'{e}}$ale pour les vacances”).

* Use *et* and *aussi* to add ideas.

* Weak answers describe activities but do not clearly express an opinion, or give only a vague idea (*j'aime la ville*).

**Criterion C (Conceptual understanding**):

* **Blog** is the most appropriate text type. The audience is a broad group, and the purpose is to share a personal experience. A strong response includes all the conventions of a blog, as in the model answer: a blog URL (*www.voyageafricain.com*), a title (*Mes vacances $\textit{\`{a}}$ Cotonou*), an author and date (*Par : Lucas\_17 | 12 juillet*), an opening salutation (*Salut $\textit{\`{a}}$ tous !*), a text addressed to readers in the first person (*je visite, je d$\textit{\'{e}}$couvre*), a direct address to the reader (*Et vous, o$\textit{\`{u}}$ passez-vous vos vacances ?*), a closing salutation (*$\textit{\`{A}}$ bient$\textit{\^{o}}$t !*), and a comments section (*@Loulou1981 Je suis en Alg$\textit{\'{e}}$rie.*).

* **Courriel** is generally appropriate. It allows sharing a personal experience, but it is usually addressed to one specific person, which makes it less suitable for a wider audience.

* **Invitation** is generally inappropriate because the task does not involve inviting someone, but describing a personal experience.
