## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{participants}$    [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$On cherche des participants pour un tournoi de basket $\textrm{\`a}$ $\textrm{Montr\'eal}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We are looking for participants for a basketball tournament in Montreal.") The noun *participants* confirms that the event is looking for people to take part in the competition.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des $\textit{r\'eponses}$ suivantes :*  

- $\textrm{15 ans}$

- $\textrm{quinze ans}$        [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous recherchons des jeunes entre 15 et 25 ans." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We are looking for young people between 15 and 25 years old.") The number 15 identifies the minimum age required.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des $\textit{r\'eponses}$ suivantes :*  

- $\textrm{25 ans}$

- $\textrm{vingt-cinq ans}$        [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous recherchons des jeunes entre 15 et 25 ans." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We are looking for young people between 15 and 25 years old.") The number 25 identifies the maximum age required.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{professionnel}$    [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il n'est pas $\textrm{n\'ecessaire}$ $\textrm{d'\^etre}$ professionnel." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$It is not necessary to be a professional.") The negation *il n'est pas $\textit{n\'ecessaire}$* (*it is not necessary*) confirms that being a professional is not a requirement to participate. 

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{en équipe}$    [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$savoir travailler en $\textrm{\'equipe}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$to know how to work as a team.") The expression *en $\textit{\'equipe}$* (*as a team*) confirms that participants must be able to work collaboratively with others. 

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{tôt le matin}$    [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les matchs commencent $\textrm{t\^ot}$ le matin." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the matches begin early in the morning.") The expression *$\textit{t\^ot}$ le matin* (*early in the morning*) confirms when the matches begin each day. 

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des $\textit{r\'eponses}$ suivantes :*  

- $\textrm{le 18 juillet}$

- $\textrm{le dix-huit juillet}$        [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous vous attendons le 18 juillet au centre sportif de $\textrm{Montr\'eal}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We expect you on the 18th of July at the Montreal sports centre.") The expression *nous vous attendons* (*we expect you*) confirms this is the date of the tournament.
