## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{les Seychelles}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Venez y passer des vacances inoubliables !" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Come and spend unforgettable holidays there!") The pronoun *y* refers to a place already mentioned. It refers to *les Seychelles*, the destination introduced at the beginning of the text, and is used to avoid repetition of the place name.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{de nombreuses activités}$    [1 mark]

- In paragraph 1, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Les Seychelles offrent de nombreuses $\textrm{activit\'es}$ et les visiteurs en gardent souvent de $\textrm{tr\`es}$ beaux souvenirs." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$The Seychelles offer many activities and visitors often keep very beautiful memories of them.") The pronoun *en* replaces a noun already mentioned introduced by *de*. It refers to *de nombreuses $\textit{activit\'es}$* (*many activities*), confirming that visitors keep wonderful memories of these activities.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

- The word *$\textit{passionn\'es}$* is correct because in paragraph 2, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Les amoureux de la nature peuvent aussi explorer les parcs nationaux." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nature lovers can also explore the national parks.") The word *amoureux* describes people who have a deep love for nature, and *$\textit{passionn\'es}$* (*passionate/enthusiastic*) expresses the same idea of strong interest and dedication.

- *Option incorrecte :* F

- **F**: *Romantiques* (*romantic*) is not correct because it refers to love between people. In the text, *amoureux* is used to describe a strong interest in nature, not a romantic relationship between individuals.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

- The word *plats* is correct because in paragraph 3, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$vous pouvez $\textrm{d\'eguster}$ des $\textrm{sp\'ecialit\'es}$ locales dans les restaurants." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$you can taste local specialities in restaurants.") The verb *$\textit{d\'eguster}$* (*to taste*) confirms that *$\textit{sp\'ecialit\'es}$* refers to food, and *plats* (*dishes)* has the same meaning in this context.

- *Option incorrecte :* 

- **D**: *$\textit{Sp\'ecificit\'es}$* (*specificities/characteristics*) is not correct because it refers to particular features or traits of something. In the text, *$\textit{sp\'ecialit\'es}$* refers specifically to local food dishes, not general characteristics.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

- The word *$\textit{athl\'etiques}$* is correct because in paragraph 3, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Pour les visiteurs sportifs, il est possible de faire du kayak ou de la $\textrm{randonn\'ee}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$For sporty visitors, it is possible to go kayaking or hiking.") The word *sportifs* describes people who enjoy sport and physical activities, and *$\textit{athl\'etiques}$* (*athletic*) expresses the same idea of being physically active and fit.

- *Option incorrecte :* B

- **B**: *Physiques* (*physical*) is not correct because it simply refers to the body or something physical, and does not specifically describe people who do sports.
