## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{d'automne}$    [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le centre de loisirs est heureux de vous $\textrm{pr\'esenter}$ ses nouveaux ateliers pour la saison d'automne." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$The leisure centre is pleased to present its new workshops for the autumn season.") The noun *automne* (*autumn*) identifies the season of the new workshops.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des $\textit{r\'eponses}$ suivantes :*  

- $\textrm{16 ans}$

- $\textrm{seize ans}$        [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nous proposons des $\textrm{activit\'es}$ $\textrm{vari\'ees}$ pour les jeunes de 16 $\textrm{\`a}$ 30 ans." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we offer a variety of activities for young people aged 16 to 30.") The number 16 identifies the minimum age required.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des $\textit{r\'eponses}$ suivantes :*  

- $\textrm{30 ans}$

- $\textrm{trente ans}$        [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$nous proposons des $\textrm{activit\'es}$ $\textrm{vari\'ees}$ pour les jeunes de 16 $\textrm{\`a}$ 30 ans." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$we offer a variety of activities for young people aged 16 to 30.") The number 30 identifies the maximum age required.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{samedi matin}$    [1 mark]

- The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Les $\textrm{s\'eances}$ ont lieu tous les samedis matin $\textrm{\`a}$ partir de neuf heures." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Sessions take place every Saturday morning from nine o'clock.") The adverb *chaque* (*every*) in the question confirms the sessions take place every Saturday morning.

$5 - 7.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.*  

- $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, C, E}$        [3 marks]

- **A** : The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Les places sont $\textrm{limit\'ees}$, donc nous vous conseillons de vous inscrire rapidement." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Places are limited, so we advise you to register quickly.") The expression *nombre de places est restreint* in statement A reflects places *sont $\textit{limit\'ees}$* in the audio.

- **C** : The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nous cherchons des personnes $\textrm{motiv\'ees}$ et curieuses." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$We are looking for motivated and curious people.") The adjectives *$\textit{motiv\'ees}$* (*motivated*) and *curieuses* (*curious*) confirm this statement.

- **E** : The announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$qui souhaitent $\textrm{d\'ecouvrir}$ de nouvelles passions." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$who wish to discover new passions.") The expression *explorer de nouvelles passions* in statement E reflects *$\textit{d\'ecouvrir}$ de nouvelles passions* in the audio.

- *Options incorrectes :* B, D, F

- **B** : *Il est obligatoire d'avoir de $\textit{l'exp\'erience}$ pour participer* (*It is compulsory to have experience to participate*) is not correct because the announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$il n'est pas $\textrm{n\'ecessaire}$ d'avoir de $\textrm{l'exp\'erience}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$it is not necessary to have experience.") The negation *n'est pas $\textit{n\'ecessaire}$* directly contradicts this statement.

- **D** : *On peut s'inscrire uniquement par $\textit{t\'el\'ephone}$* (*You can only register by phone*) is not correct because the announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$rendez-vous $\textrm{\`a}$ l'accueil du centre ou envoyez un email." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$come to the centre reception or send an email.") Two options are mentioned, neither of which is by phone.

- **F** : *Le centre est ouvert tous les jours de la semaine* (*The centre is open every day of la semaine*) is not correct because the announcement states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$tous les samedis matin." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$every Saturday morning.") Only Saturdays are mentioned.
