# FA0302

**The sentences below contain one reflexive verb error. Find the error and write the correct verb.**

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$Myriam me $\text{r\'{e}veille}$ $\text{\`{a}}$ sept heures tous les jours.    [1 mark]

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$Mes parents se couche $\text{\`{a}}$ onze heures du soir.    [1 mark]

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$Tu se douches avant de prendre le $\text{petit-d\'{e}jeuner}$.    [1 mark]

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Ma soeur et moi nous habillez rapidement le matin.    [1 mark]

**Match each sentence to the correct reflexive verb. There are more options than sentences.**    [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{se r\'{e}veille}$    [1 mark]

* *Myriam* refers to *elle*. The reflexive pronoun must be *se*, so the correct form is *se $\textit{r\'{e}veille}$*.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Myriam se $\text{r\'{e}veille}$ $\text{\`{a}}$ sept heures tous les jours.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{se couchent}$    [1 mark]

* *Mes parents* refers to *ils*. The reflexive pronoun is already correct *se*, but the verb must agree: *se couchent*.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Mes parents se couchent $\text{\`{a}}$ onze heures du soir.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{te douches}$    [1 mark]

* The subject is *tu*. The reflexive pronoun must be *te*, so the correct form is *te douches*.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Tu te douches avant de prendre le $\text{petit-d\'{e}jeuner}$.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{nous habillons}$    [1 mark]

* *Ma soeur et moi* includes the speaker and refers to *nous*. The reflexive pronoun is already correct *nous*, but the verb must agree: *nous habillons*.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Ma soeur et moi nous habillons rapidement le matin.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *Me brosse* is correct because the subject is *je*. The reflexive pronoun is *me*, so the correct form is *me brosse*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C, D, E

* **A** : *Se brossent* is the *ils/elles* form and does not complete the sentence logically.

* **B** : *Nous $\textit{r\'{e}veillons}$* is the *nous* form, but the subject is *je*.

* **C** : *Se lave* is the *il/elle/on* form, but the subject is *je*.

* **D** : *Te $\textit{r\'{e}veilles}$* is the *tu* form, but the subject is *je*.

* **E** : *Se $\textit{l\`{e}vent}$* is the *ils/elles* form and does not complete the sentence logically.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

* *Te $\textit{r\'{e}veilles}$* is correct because the subject is *tu*. The reflexive pronoun is *te*, so the correct form is *te $\textit{r\'{e}veilles}$*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C, D, F

* **A** : *Se brossent* is the *ils/elles* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **B** : *Nous $\textit{r\'{e}veillons}$* is the *nous* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **C** : *Se lave* is the *il/elle/on* form, but the subject is *tu*.

* **D** : *Me brosse* is the *je* form and does not complete the sentence logically.

* **F** : *Se $\textit{l\`{e}vent}$* is the *ils/elles* form, but *tu* refers to one person.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{F}$    [1 mark]

* *Se $\textit{l\`{e}vent}$* is correct because *mes parents* refers to *ils*. The reflexive pronoun is *se*, so the correct form is *se $\textit{l\`{e}vent}$*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C, D, E

* **A** : *Se brossent* uses the correct pronoun, but it does not complete the sentence logically.

* **B** : *Nous $\textit{r\'{e}veillons}$* is the *nous* form, but *mes parents* refers to *ils*.

* **C** : *Se lave* is the *il/elle/on* form, but *mes parents* refers to *ils*.

* **D** : *Me brosse* is the *je* form and does not complete the sentence logically.

* **E** : *Te $\textit{r\'{e}veilles}$* is the *tu* form, but *mes parents* refers to *ils*.
