# FA0377

**Choose the correct form of the verb in the perfect or imperfect tense.**

$\hspace{1em}\text{\`{A}}$ midi, $\text{Zo\'{e}}$ **\[-1-]** encore sur la plage. Sa sieste n’$\text{\'{e}tait}$ pas $\text{termin\'{e}e}$.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$dormait

* B.$\hspace{1em}$a dormi

* C.$\hspace{1em}$dormaient

* D.$\hspace{1em}$a dormir

$\hspace{1em}$Hier, Karim **\[-2-]** une carte postale en cinq minutes.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}\text{\'{e}crivait}$

* B.$\hspace{1em}$ont $\text{\'{e}crit}$

* C.$\hspace{1em}$a $\text{\'{e}crit}$

* D.$\hspace{1em}$a $\text{\'{e}crire}$

**Match each sentence to the correct perfect or imperfect tense verb form. There are more options than sentences.** [3 marks]

**Sentence**

$\hspace{1em}\text{\`{A}}$ vingt heures, nous **\[-3-]** encore aux cartes au camping. La partie n’$\text{\'{e}tait}$ pas $\text{termin\'{e}e}$.

$\hspace{1em}$Hier, les enfants **\[-4-]** trois parties de tennis en une heure.

$\hspace{1em}$Quand je suis $\text{arriv\'{e}}$ au parc, Sandro **\[-5-]** au ballon depuis dix minutes.

**Options**

* A.$\hspace{1em}$a $\text{jou\'{e}}$

* B.$\hspace{1em}$jouions

* C.$\hspace{1em}$ont $\text{jou\'{e}}$

* D.$\hspace{1em}$jouait

* E.$\hspace{1em}$avons $\text{jou\'{e}}$

* F.$\hspace{1em}$jouaient

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *Sa sieste n'$\textit{\'{e}tait}$ pas $\textit{termin\'{e}e}$* shows that $\text{Zo\'{e}}$ was still sleeping at noon. Use the imperfect for this action in progress: *dorm-* + *-ait* for singular $\text{Zo\'{e}}$ gives *dormait*.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C, D

* **B** : *A dormi* presents the sleep as a completed event, whereas the sentence describes it in progress at noon.

* **C** : *Dormaient* is plural; $\text{Zo\'{e}}$ requires a singular form.

* **D** : *A dormir* uses an infinitive after *a*. The perfect tense would need a past participle, but this context requires the imperfect.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *En cinq minutes* shows that Karim finished writing the postcard in five minutes. Use the perfect tense for this completed past action: Karim takes *a*, and the past participle of *$\textit{\'{e}crire}$* is *$\textit{\'{e}crit}$*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D

* **A** : *$\textit{\'{E}crivait}$* describes writing in progress or a habit, whereas this sentence reports one postcard completed in five minutes.

* **B** : *Ont $\textit{\'{e}crit}$* is plural; the subject Karim is singular.

* **D** : *A $\textit{\'{e}crire}$* uses the infinitive instead of the past participle *$\textit{\'{e}crit}$*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* *La partie n'$\textit{\'{e}tait}$ pas $\textit{termin\'{e}e}$* shows that the game was still in progress. Use the imperfect tense for *nous*: *jouions*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D, E, F

* **A** : *A $\textit{jou\'{e}}$* is singular and presents a completed event; the subject is *nous* and the game is still in progress.

* **C** : *Ont $\textit{jou\'{e}}$* is used with *ils* or *elles*, not *nous*, and presents a completed event.

* **D** : *Jouait* is singular; *nous* needs *-ions*.

* **E** : *Avons $\textit{jou\'{e}}$* matches *nous*, but presents the game as completed rather than in progress at the stated time.

* **F** : *Jouaient* is used with *ils* or *elles*; *nous* needs *-ions*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *Trois parties* and *en une heure* present three completed games within one hour. Use the perfect tense. Plural *les enfants* takes *ont* + *$\textit{jou\'{e}}$*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D, E, F

* **A** : *A $\textit{jou\'{e}}$* is singular; *les enfants* requires *ont*.

* **B** : *Jouions* is used with *nous* and describes an ongoing or habitual activity.

* **D** : *Jouait* is singular and does not present the three games as completed.

* **E** : *Avons $\textit{jou\'{e}}$* is used with *nous*, not *les enfants*.

* **F** : *Jouaient* matches the subject, but describes an ongoing or habitual activity instead of the three completed games.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *Depuis dix minutes* shows that Sandro was already playing when the speaker arrived. Use the imperfect tense: *jou-* + singular *-ait* gives *jouait*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C, E, F

* **A** : *A $\textit{jou\'{e}}$* presents a completed event; here *depuis dix minutes* describes an activity still continuing at the arrival.

* **B** : *Jouions* is used with *nous*, not Sandro.

* **C** : *Ont $\textit{jou\'{e}}$* is plural and presents a completed event.

* **E** : *Avons $\textit{jou\'{e}}$* is used with *nous* and presents a completed event.

* **F** : *Jouaient* is plural; Sandro needs singular *-ait*.

**Grammar Reminder**

| Tense | Use | Example |

| :-: | :-: | :-: |

| imperfect tense | A past description | *Le village $\textit{\'{e}tait}$ calme.* (The village was quiet.) |

| imperfect tense | A past habit | *Chaque $\textit{\'{e}t\'{e}}$, nous campions.* (Every summer, we used to camp.) |

| imperfect tense | An action still in progress | *$\textit{\`{A}}$ huit heures, je $\textit{pr\'{e}parais}$ encore le $\textit{d\^{\i}ner}$.* (At eight, I was still preparing dinner.) |

| perfect tense | A completed past event | *J'ai $\textit{ferm\'{e}}$ la valise.* (I closed the suitcase.) |

| perfect tense | Events happening one after another | *J'ai $\textit{pay\'{e}}$, puis j'ai $\textit{quitt\'{e}}$ le camping.* (I paid, then left the campsite.) |

**Check the time clues**

* *Depuis* + a duration: the action had already been in progress for that long. If it was still continuing at the past moment, use the imperfect tense.

* *En* + a duration: the time taken to finish an action. *Elle a $\textit{r\'{e}par\'{e}}$ son $\textit{v\'{e}lo}$ en dix minutes.* (She repaired her bike in ten minutes.) The repair is completed so use the perfect tense.

**Common mistake**: Do not automatically put verbs in the perfect tense because the story is in the past.

*Je $\textit{pr\'{e}parais}$ le $\textit{d\^{\i}ner}$ depuis dix minutes quand le $\textit{t\'{e}l\'{e}phone}$ a $\textit{sonn\'{e}}$.* (I had been preparing dinner for ten minutes when the phone rang.)

*Depuis dix minutes* shows that the cooking was still in progress when the phone rang: use *$\textit{pr\'{e}parais}$*, not *ai $\textit{pr\'{e}par\'{e}}$*. The ringing is the completed event that happens during it: *a $\textit{sonn\'{e}}$*.
