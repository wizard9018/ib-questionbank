# FA0318

**Choose the correct perfect tense form of the verbs.**

$\hspace{1em}$La semaine $\text{derni\`{e}re}$, Emma **\[-1-]** chez sa tante.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$a $\text{rest\'{e}}$

* B.$\hspace{1em}$est $\text{rest\'{e}}$

* C.$\hspace{1em}$a $\text{rest\'{e}e}$

* D.$\hspace{1em}$est $\text{rest\'{e}e}$

$\hspace{1em}$Hier soir, mes $\text{fr\`{e}res}$ **\[-2-]** un film au camping.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$ont $\text{regard\'{e}}$

* B.$\hspace{1em}$sont $\text{regard\'{e}s}$

* C.$\hspace{1em}$ont $\text{regard\'{e}s}$

* D.$\hspace{1em}$a $\text{regard\'{e}}$

**Complete the sentence. Use the correct perfect tense form of the verb in brackets.** [1 mark]

Ce matin, nous **\[-3-]** *(acheter)* des cartes postales.

**The sentences below contain one perfect tense error. The error is underlined. Write the correct words.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}\text{Apr\`{e}s}$ la promenade, Hugo $\underline{\textrm{a revenu}}$ au chalet.    [1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Pour aller au village, tu $\underline{\textrm{es pris}}$ le bus.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{D}$    [1 mark]

* *Rester* is on the list of verbs that use *$\textit{\^{e}tre}$*, even though it means staying in a place. *Emma* refers to *elle*, so it requires *est*, and the past participle *$\textit{rest\'{e}e}$* must be feminine singular.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C

* **A** : *A $\textit{rest\'{e}}$* uses the wrong verb (*avoir*) and does not make the past participle feminine.

* **B** : *Est $\textit{rest\'{e}}$* has the correct verb (*$\textit{\^{e}tre}$*), but *$\textit{rest\'{e}}$* does not agree with *Emma*.

* **C** : *A $\textit{rest\'{e}e}$* has the feminine participle, but *rester* requires *$\textit{\^{e}tre}$*, not *avoir*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *Regarder* takes *avoir*. *Mes $\textit{fr\`{e}res}$* refers to *ils*, so it requires *ont*.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C, D

* **B** : *Sont $\textit{regard\'{e}s}$* uses *$\textit{\^{e}tre}$*, but *regarder* requires *avoir* in this sentence.

* **C** : *Ont $\textit{regard\'{e}s}$* incorrectly adds plural agreement with the subject. With *avoir*, the past participle does not agree with *mes $\textit{fr\`{e}res}$*.

* **D** : *A $\textit{regard\'{e}}$* is singular, but *mes $\textit{fr\`{e}res}$* is plural.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{avons achet\'{e}}$    [1 mark]

* *Acheter* takes *avoir*. The subject *nous* requires *avons*, and the past participle is *$\textit{achet\'{e}}$*.\

  \

  **Model answer**: Ce matin, nous avons $\text{achet\'{e}}$ des cartes postales.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{est}$    [1 mark]

* *Revenir* takes *$\textit{\^{e}tre}$* in the perfect tense. Hugo refers to *il*, so it requires *est*. The past participle *revenu* is already correct.\

  \

  **Model answer**: $\text{Apr\`{e}s}$ la promenade, Hugo est revenu au chalet.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{as}$    [1 mark]

* *Prendre* takes *avoir*. The subject *tu* requires *as*, so *as* replaces the underlined *es*. The past participle *pris* is already correct.\

  \

  **Model answer**:  Pour aller au village, tu as pris le bus.
