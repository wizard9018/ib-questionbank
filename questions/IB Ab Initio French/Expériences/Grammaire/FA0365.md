# FA0365

**Choose the correct imperfect tense verb form.**

$\hspace{1em}$Quand j’$\text{\'{e}tais}$ petit, je **\[-1-]** sur la plage chaque matin.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$joue

* B.$\hspace{1em}$jouais

* C.$\hspace{1em}$jouait

* D.$\hspace{1em}$jouions

$\hspace{1em}$Autrefois, le camping **\[-2-]** $\text{tr\`{e}s}$ calme.    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$est

* B.$\hspace{1em}\text{\'{e}taient}$

* C.$\hspace{1em}\text{\'{e}tait}$

* D.$\hspace{1em}\text{\'{e}tiez}$

**Match each sentence to the correct imperfect tense verb form. There are more options than sentences.**    [3 marks]

| Sentence$\hspace{4em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$Options |

| :- | :- |

| Nous **\[-3-]** un $\text{mus\'{e}e}$ tous les ans. | $\hspace{3em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$visitiez |

| Vous ne **\[-4-]** pas les $\text{m\^{e}mes}$ endroits chaque $\text{\'{e}t\'{e}}$. | $\hspace{3em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$visitais |

| Mes parents **\[-5-]** souvent des villages en montagne. | $\hspace{3em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$visitaient |

| $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$visite |

| $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$E.$\hspace{1em}$visitions |

| | $\hspace{3em}$F.$\hspace{1em}$visitait |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* *Quand j'$\textit{\'{e}tais}$ petit* and *chaque matin* describe a past habit. The imperfect of *jouer* uses the stem *jou-* and the ending *-ais* with *je*: *jouais*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C, D

* **A** : *Joue* is a present tense form.

* **C** : *Jouait* is the imperfect form for *il, elle* or *on*, but the subject is *je*.

* **D** : *Jouions* is the imperfect form for *nous*, but the subject is *je*.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *Autrefois* introduces a past description. *Le camping* is singular, like *il*. The imperfect of *$\textit{\^{e}tre}$* uses the stem *$\textit{\'{e}t-}$* and the ending *-ait*: *$\textit{\'{e}tait}$*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D

* **A** : *Est* is present tense, not imperfect.

* **B** : *$\textit{\'{E}taient}$* is plural, but *le camping* is singular.

* **D** : *$\textit{\'{E}tiez}$* is for *vous*, whereas *le camping* needs singular *$\textit{\'{e}tait}$*.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{E}$    [1 mark]

* *Nous* requires *-ions*. Add it to *visit-*, from *nous visitons*, to form *visitions*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, C, D, E, F

* **A** : *Visitiez* is the *vous* form, but the subject is *nous*.

* **B** : *Visitais* is the *je* or *tu* form, but the subject is *nous*.

* **C** : *Visitaient* is the *ils* or *elles* form, but the subject is *nous*.

* **D** : *Visite* is present tense.

* **F** : *Visitait* is the *il, elle* or *on* form, but the subject is *nous*.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* *Vous* requires *-iez*, so the correct form is *visitiez*.

* *Options incorrectes :* B, C, D, E, F

* **B** : *Visitais* is the *je* or *tu* form, but the subject is *vous*.

* **C** : *Visitaient* is the *ils* or *elles* form, but the subject is *vous*.

* **D** : *Visite* is present tense, not imperfect.

* **E** : *Visitions* is the *nous* form, but the subject is *vous*.

* **F** : *Visitait* is the *il, elle* or *on* form, but the subject is *vous*.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* *Mes parents* is plural, like *ils*. The imperfect therefore uses *visit-* + *-aient*: *visitaient*.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, B, D, E, F

* **A** : *Visitiez* is the *vous* form, but the subject is *Mes parents*.

* **B** : *Visitais* is the *je* or *tu* form, but the subject is *Mes parents*.

* **D** : *Visite* is present tense, not imperfect.

* **E** : *Visitions* is the *nous* form, but the subject is *Mes parents*.

* **F** : *Visitait* is singular, whereas *mes parents* is plural.

**Grammar Reminder**

* Use the imperfect to describe past situations, repeated past habits or actions that were in progress. \

  Examples: *La plage $\textit{\'{e}tait}$ vide.* (The beach was empty.) \

  *Chaque $\textit{\'{e}t\'{e}}$, Katia dormait dans une tente.* (Every summer, Katia used to sleep in a tent.)

* To form the imperfect

* $1.$ take the present-tense *nous* form, remove *-ons*

* $2.$ add the correct ending.

$\underline{\textrm{Step 1 : find the stem}}$

| $\hspace{1em}$Infinitive | $\hspace{1em}$Present nous form | $\hspace{1em}$Imperfect stem |

| :- | :- | :- |

| $\hspace{1em}$finir | $\hspace{1em}$nous finissons | $\hspace{1em}$finiss- |

| $\hspace{1em}$choisir | $\hspace{1em}$nous choisissons | $\hspace{1em}$choisiss- |

| $\hspace{1em}$prendre | $\hspace{1em}$nous prenons | $\hspace{1em}$pren- |

| $\hspace{1em}$faire | $\hspace{1em}$nous faisons | $\hspace{1em}$fais- |

| $\hspace{1em}$avoir | $\hspace{1em}$nous avons | $\hspace{1em}$av- |

| $\hspace{1em}$aller | $\hspace{1em}$nous allons | $\hspace{1em}$all- |

| $\hspace{1em}$voir | $\hspace{1em}$nous voyons | $\hspace{1em}$voy- |

* *$\textit{\^{E}tre}$* is the exception: use *$\textit{\'{e}t-}$* with the same endings. For example, *tu $\textit{\'{e}tais}$ $\textit{\`{a}}$ la plage* means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$you were at the beach”. Do not use the stem of *nous sommes*.

$\underline{\textrm{Step 2 : add the ending}}$

| $\hspace{1em}$Subject pronoun | $\hspace{1em}$Ending |

| :- | :- |

| $\hspace{1em}$Je | $\hspace{1em}$-ais |

| $\hspace{1em}$Tu | $\hspace{1em}$-ais |

| $\hspace{1em}$Il/elle/on | $\hspace{1em}$-ait |

| $\hspace{1em}$Nous | $\hspace{1em}$-ions |

| $\hspace{1em}$Vous | $\hspace{1em}$-iez |

| $\hspace{1em}$Ils/elles | $\hspace{1em}$-aient |

* With verbs ending in *-ger*, keep *e* after *g* for all pronouns except *nous* and *vous*. This keeps the same soft sound of *g*. \

  Example: *je voyageais /nous voyagions/vous voyagiez.*

* With verbs ending in *-cer*,  keep *$\textit{\c{c}}$* for all pronouns except *nous* and *vous*. This keeps the same soft sound of *c*. \

  Example: *je $\textit{lan\c{c}ais}$ /nous lancions/vous lanciez.*

* When the stem ends in *i*, keep that *i* before *-ions* and *-iez*. For *crier, nous crions* gives *cri-*; the imperfect forms are *nous criions* and *vous criiez* (we / you were shouting), with two consecutive *i*s.

| $\hspace{1em}$Subject pronoun | $\hspace{1em}$Voyager | $\hspace{1em}$Lancer | $\hspace{1em}$Crier |

| :- | :- | :- | :- |

| $\hspace{1em}$Je | $\hspace{1em}$voyageais | $\hspace{1em}\text{lan\c{c}ais}$ | $\hspace{1em}$criais |

| $\hspace{1em}$Tu | $\hspace{1em}$voyageais | $\hspace{1em}\text{lan\c{c}ais}$ | $\hspace{1em}$criais |

| $\hspace{1em}$Il/elle/on | $\hspace{1em}$voyageait | $\hspace{1em}\text{lan\c{c}ait}$ | $\hspace{1em}$criait |

| $\hspace{1em}$Nous | $\hspace{1em}$voyagions | $\hspace{1em}$lancions | $\hspace{1em}$ criions |

| $\hspace{1em}$Vous | $\hspace{1em}$voyagiez | $\hspace{1em}$lanciez | $\hspace{1em}$criiez |

| $\hspace{1em}$Ils/elles | $\hspace{1em}$voyageaient | $\hspace{1em}\text{lan\c{c}aient}$ | $\hspace{1em}$criaient |

**Common mistake**: Do not build the imperfect simply by removing the infinitive ending. - *nous finions* is not the correct imperfect of *finir*. Start from *nous finissons*, keep *finiss-* and add *-ions*: *nous finissions*.
