## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$   [1 mark]

- The email states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je vais $\textrm{\`a}$ $\textrm{l'\'ecole}$ entre 7h30 et 8h00." (I go to school between 7.30 and 8.00.) The word *entre* (between) confirms that Thomas arrives at school within this time range.

- *Options incorrectes :* A, C

- **A**: *Avant 7h30* is not correct because the email states:$\text{\textquotedblleft}$entre 7h30 et 8h00". The word *entre* confirms he does not arrive before 7.30.

- **C**: *$\textit{Apr\`es}$ 8h00* is not correct because the email states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$entre 7h30 et 8h00." The word *entre* confirms he arrives before 8.00, not after.

$2 - 4.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les $\textit{r\'eponses}$ peuvent $\textit{\^etre}$ $\textit{\'ecrites}$ dans n'importe quel ordre.* $\textrm{A, E, F}$ [3 marks]

- **E**: The email states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je rentre chez moi et je me repose." (I go home and I rest.) The verb *se repose* confirms he relaxes when he gets home.

- **F**: The email states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je vais aider mes parents $\textrm{\`a}$ $\textrm{pr\'eparer}$ le $\textrm{d\^{\i}ner}$." (I am going to help his parents prepare dinner.) The verb *aider* and the noun *$\textit{d\^{\i}ner}$* confirm he will help prepare the evening meal.

- *Options incorrectes :* B, C, D

- **B**: *Il n'aime pas ses cours $\textit{\`a}$ $\textit{l'\'ecole}$* is not correct because the email states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mon cours $\textrm{pr\'ef\'er\'e}$ est le sport." The expression *cours $\textit{pr\'ef\'er\'e}$* confirms he likes at least one subject.
