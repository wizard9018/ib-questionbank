# FA0134

[Note maximale : 15\] 

**Utilisez, en fonction des propositions, le type de texte le plus $\textbf{appropri\'e}$. $\textbf{\'Ecrivez}$ entre 60 et 90 mots.** 

Le club $\textrm{sant\'e}$ de votre $\textrm{\'ecole}$ organise un stage pour $\textrm{am\'eliorer}$ les habitudes quotidiennes des $\textrm{\'el\`eves}$. Il cherche $\textrm{\`a}$ attirer davantage de participants. $\textrm{\'Ecrivez}$ un texte pour $\textrm{pr\'esenter}$ ce stage et expliquer ce que les $\textrm{\'el\`eves}$ peuvent apprendre.

$\underline{\textrm{Marking Criteria Feedback}}$

**Criterion A (Language)**:

- Use the present tense and near future to describe the event: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$le club organise", $\text{\textquotedblleft}$tu vas apprendre".

- Use infinitive structures after *apprendre $\textit{\`a}$*: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$apprendre $\textrm{\`a}$ bien commencer", $\text{\textquotedblleft}$organiser", $\text{\textquotedblleft}$respecter".

- Use the imperative to engage the reader: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Profite", $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Inscris-toi", $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Change".

- Use *pour* + infinitive to express purpose: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Profite de conseils simples et motivants pour manger $\textrm{\'equilibr\'e}$, faire du sport et mieux dormir".

- Common errors include confusion between present and near future (*vous apprenez instead of vous allez apprendre*) and confusion between present and imperative (*tu changes instead of change !*).

**Criterion B (Message)**:

- A complete answer includes both content points:\

    $1.\hspace{1em}$presenting the work experience (what it is, when, where, for whom)\

    $2.\hspace{1em}$explaining what students can learn.

- The model answer also adds useful supporting details which makes the description more concrete.

- Use et to add ideas. 

- A weak response mixes both ideas without clarity or focuses only on one part (for example, only describing the work experience without explaining what students learn).

**Criterion C (Conceptual understanding**):

- **Annonce publicitaire** is the appropriate text type. The audience is the whole school, the purpose is to inform and persuade, and the tone is direct and engaging. A strong response includes all the conventions of an advert, as in the model answer: a catchy title (*Stage « Bien vivre au quotidien »*), a slogan (*Change ta routine !*), short structured sections (subtitle + bullet points), practical details *(lundi 17 novembre, salle de sport, clubsante@ecole.fr*), a call to action (*Inscris-toi vite !*) and images.

- **Dialogue en ligne** is generally appropriate. It allows interaction and can be used to share information informally with other students. However, it is usually less suitable for presenting clear, structured information to attract participants.

- **Rapport** is generally inappropriate - it is designed to report information objectively, not to engage or persuade a wide audience.
