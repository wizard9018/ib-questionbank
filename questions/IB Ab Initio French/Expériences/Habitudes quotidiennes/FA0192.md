# FA0192

[Note maximale : 7\]

**Vous allez entendre Sofia, jeune $\textbf{\'{e}}$tudiante qu$\textbf{\'{e}}$b$\textbf{\'{e}}$coise $\textbf{\`{a}}$ Toulouse, parler des diff$\textbf{\'{e}}$rences dans sa vie quotidienne en France.**

:audio{#1bc97edd-fcdd-437d-8c70-4949cc0163c2 aid="1bc97edd-fcdd-437d-8c70-4949cc0163c2"}

**Choisissez les trois affirmations vraies.** [3 marks]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Sofia habite en France depuis douze mois.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Dans son pays, elle rentrait chez elle pour d$\text{\'{e}}$jeuner.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Elle prenait son d$\text{\'{e}}$jeuner avant midi.

* D.$\hspace{1em}$En France, elle d$\text{\'{e}}$jeune $\text{\`{a}}$ la cantine.

* E.$\hspace{1em}$Au d$\text{\'{e}}$but, le changement lui semblait facile.

* F.$\hspace{1em}$Au d$\text{\'{e}}$but, elle connaissait tous ses camarades.

**R$\textbf{\'{e}}$pondez aux questions suivantes.**

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$Qu'a-t-elle maintenant $\text{\`{a}}$ l'$\text{\'{e}}$cole ?[1 mark]

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$Qu'est-ce qu'elle appr$\text{\'{e}}$cie surtout ?[1 mark]

**Choisissez la bonne r$\textbf{\'{e}}$ponse.**

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$Quel changement remarque-t-elle dans les magasins ?    [1 mark]

* A.$\hspace{1em}$Ils commencent la journ$\text{\'{e}}$e plus tard.

* B.$\hspace{1em}$Ils terminent la journ$\text{\'{e}}$e plus t$\text{\^{o}}$t.

* C.$\hspace{1em}$Ils restent ouverts plus tard que dans son pays.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$Comment r$\text{\'{e}}$agit-elle $\text{\`{a}}$ cette diff$\text{\'{e}}$rence ?[1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1 - 3.$ $\hspace{1em}$*Les r$\textit{\'{e}}$ponses peuvent $\textit{\^{e}}$tre $\textit{\'{e}}$crites dans n'importe quel ordre.*

* $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A, B, D}$        [3 marks]

* **A** : Sofia says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je vis en France depuis un an." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I have been living in France for a year.") The expression *depuis un an* shows that she has lived there for twelve months.

* **B**: Sofia explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je rentrais $\text{\`{a}}$ la maison le midi pour d$\text{\'{e}}$jeuner avec ma famille." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I used to go home at midday to have lunch with my family.") This shows that she used to go home for lunch in her home country.

* **D**: Sofia adds: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ici, je mange $\text{\`{a}}$ la cantine avec mes camarades." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Here, I eat at the cafeteria with my classmates.") This shows that she now has lunch at the cafeteria in France.

* *Options incorrectes :* C, E, F

* **C** : *Elle prenait son d$\textit{\'{e}}$jeuner avant midi* (*She used to have lunch before midday*) is not correct because Sofia says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je rentrais $\text{\`{a}}$ la maison le midi pour d$\text{\'{e}}$jeuner." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I used to go home at midday to have lunch.") She had lunch at midday, not before.

* **E** : *Au d$\textit{\'{e}}$but, le changement lui semblait facile* (*At first, the change seemed easy to her*) is not correct because Sofia says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Au d$\text{\'{e}}$but, c'$\text{\'{e}}$tait difficile." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$At first, it was difficult.") She found the change difficult, not easy.

* **F** : *Au d$\textit{\'{e}}$but, elle connaissait tous ses camarades* (*At first, she knew all her classmates*) is not correct because Sofia mentions: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je ne connaissais personne." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I didn't know anyone.") She knew no one at first, not everyone.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{de bons amis}$    [1 mark]

* Sofia adds: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$maintenant, j'ai de bons amis." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$now, I have good friends.") The expression *de bons amis* indicates what she now has at school.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{les repas}$    [1 mark]

* Sofia states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'adore les repas $\text{\`{a}}$ l'$\text{\'{e}}$cole qui sont vraiment de bonne qualit$\text{\'{e}}$." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I love the meals at school, which are really good quality.") The word *repas* indicates what she particularly appreciates about school.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* Sofia mentions: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$les magasins ferment plus t$\text{\^{o}}$t." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$the shops close earlier.") This shows that shops close earlier than in her home country.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C

* **A** : *Commencent la journ$\textit{\'{e}}$e plus tard* (*start the day later*) is not correct because Sofia only mentions closing times, never opening times.

* **C** : *Restent ouverts plus tard* (*stay open later*) is not correct because Sofia says the opposite: shops close earlier, not later.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* Sofia concludes: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je ne suis pas encore habitu$\text{\'{e}}$e, mais je m'adapte." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I'm not used to it yet, but I'm adapting.") This shows that she is gradually getting used to this difference.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C

* **A** : *Refuse de changer ses habitudes* (*refuses to change her habits*) is not correct because Sofia says she is adapting, not refusing to change.

* **C** : *D$\textit{\'{e}}$cide de ne plus faire les magasins* (*decides to stop going shopping*) is not correct because Sofia never mentions giving up shopping.
