## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* In paragraph 1, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$La journ$\text{\'{e}}$e d'un $\text{\'{e}}$l$\text{\`{e}}$ve commence t$\text{\^{o}}$t et avec un bon petit-d$\text{\'{e}}$jeuner, car il faut avoir une routine saine et $\text{\^{e}}$tre pr$\text{\^{e}}$t pour la journ$\text{\'{e}}$e." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$A student's day starts early and with a good breakfast, because you need to have a healthy routine and be ready for the day.") The expressions*bon petit-d$\textit{\'{e}}$jeuner* and *routine saine* confirm that the paragraph focuses on how to start the day well.

* *Option incorrecte :* A

* **A** : *Un d$\textit{\'{e}}$but de journ$\textit{\'{e}}$e difficile* is not correct because the paragraph does not describe the morning as difficult. It focuses on the importance of a healthy routine and a good breakfast, presenting the morning in a positive light.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* In paragraph 2, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$tous les travaux suppl$\text{\'{e}}$mentaires $\text{\`{a}}$ faire" ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$all the extra work to do") and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Il y a des tests toutes les semaines." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$There are tests every week.") The words*tous* and *toutes* confirm the heavy workload and regular assessments.

* *Option incorrecte :* E

* **E** : *Attention $\textit{\`{a}}$ la fatigue et au stress !* is not correct because although the text mentions students being tired (*on est souvent fatigu$\textit{\'{e}}$s*), the paragraph focuses on the volume of work and the need to keep up the pace, not on the effects of tiredness and stress.

* In paragraph 3, the text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$certains vont passer du temps avec leurs amis ou faire du sport, c'est important !" * **D** : *Profiter de la famille et des amis* is not correct because although the paragraph mentions spending time with family and friends, the main focus is on the importance of free time and stress relief, not simply on enjoying time with others.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{C}$    [1 mark]

* The text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$La journ$\text{\'{e}}$e d'un $\text{\'{e}}$l$\text{\`{e}}$ve commence t$\text{\^{o}}$t." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$A student's day starts early.") The verb*d$\textit{\'{e}}$bute* means *commence*.

* **F** : *Abandonne* is not correct because it means *to give up* or *abandon*, which is unrelated to the meaning of $\text{\textquotedblleft}$commence\* (*starts*).

* *Option incorrecte :* E

* **E** : *Progresse* is not correct because it means *to make progress* or *improve*, which implies a result rather than an attempt, unlike *essaie* which expresses the act of trying.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* The text states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le soir, on mange en famille." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$In the evening, we eat as a family.") The time reference *le soir* confirms that  *d$\textit{\^{\i}}$ne* is the correct equivalent of  *mange* in this context.

* *Option incorrecte :* D

* **D** : *D$\textit{\'{e}}$jeune* is not correct because it refers to a midday or morning meal. The time reference *le soir* confirms the meal takes place in the evening, not at lunchtime.
