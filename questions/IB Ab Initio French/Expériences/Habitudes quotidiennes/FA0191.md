## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$1.$ $\hspace{1em}$*L'une des r$\textit{\'{e}}$ponses suivantes :*

* $\textrm{seize ans}$

* $\textrm{16 ans}$        [1 mark]

* Ga$\text{\"{e}}$lle says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$J'ai seize ans." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am sixteen years old.") The number*seize* indicates her age.

$2.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{Strasbourg}$    [1 mark]

* Ga$\text{\"{e}}$lle mentions: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$j'habite $\text{\`{a}}$ Strasbourg." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I live in Strasbourg.") The town*Strasbourg* indicates where she lives.

$3.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{matin}$    [1 mark]

* Ga$\text{\"{e}}$lle explains: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Tous les matins, je me l$\text{\`{e}}$ve $\text{\`{a}}$ sept heures." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Every morning, I get up at seven o'clock.") The word*matin* indicates  when she gets up.

$4.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(des) tartines}$    [1 mark]

* Ga$\text{\"{e}}$lle adds: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Je mange des tartines." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I eat toast.") The word*tartines* shows what she eats for breakfast.

$5.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{(du) jus d'orange}$    [1 mark]

* Ga$\text{\"{e}}$lle states: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je bois du jus d'orange." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I drink orange juice.") The expression*jus d'orange* shows what she drinks for breakfast.

$6.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{B}$    [1 mark]

* Ga$\text{\"{e}}$lle says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Les cours commencent $\text{\`{a}}$ huit heures." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$Lessons begin at eight o'clock.") This shows that lessons start at eight o'clock.

* *Options incorrectes :* A, C

* **A**: *$\textit{\`{A}}$ sept heures* (*at seven o'clock*) is not correct because Ga$\text{\"{e}}$lle says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$je me l$\text{\`{e}}$ve $\text{\`{a}}$ sept heures." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$I get up at seven o'clock.") Seven o'clock is when she wakes up, not when lessons start.

* **C**: *Avant huit heures* (*before eight o'clock*) is not correct because Ga$\text{\"{e}}$lle says lessons star&#x74;*$\textit{\`{a}}$ huit heures* (*at eight o'clock*), not earlier.

$7.$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\textrm{A}$    [1 mark]

* Ga$\text{\"{e}}$lle says: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Le soir, je fais mes devoirs et je regarde un peu la t$\text{\'{e}}$l$\text{\'{e}}$vision." ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$In the evening, I do my homework and watch a little television.") This shows that she does her homework before watching television.
