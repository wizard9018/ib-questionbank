# BM0868

RideFast is a bicycle rental startup operating in Dublin. Its founder, Ciara, has written the following business objectives: Objective A: "We want to grow the business." Objective B: "We aim to increase the number of daily bicycle rentals by 25% within 12 months by expanding into two new city locations."

Using the SMART framework, explain two reasons why Objective B is more useful to RideFast than Objective A. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

One reason is that a SMART objective should be measurable, meaning it includes a clear target that can be tracked and assessed over time. Objective B aims to increase rentals by “25%”, allowing RideFast to monitor performance, whereas Objective A, “grow the business,” has no measurable target.

Another reason is that a SMART objective should be specific, meaning it clearly defines what the business aims to achieve. Objective B clearly states that RideFast will increase the “number of daily bicycle rentals” and expand into “two new city locations”, whereas Objective A, “grow the business,” is vague and unclear.
