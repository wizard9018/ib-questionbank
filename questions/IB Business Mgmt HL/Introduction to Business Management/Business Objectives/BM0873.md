# BM0873

Distinguish between profit maximisation and profit satisficing as business objectives. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

Profit maximisation means a business aims to achieve the highest possible level of profit, whereas profit satisficing means the business aims to make enough profit to satisfy stakeholders - such as shareholders - while also pursuing other objectives such as growth or ethical goals.

**Mark allocation:**

Award [1] for explaining profit maximisation.
Award [1] for explaining profit satisficing, with a clear contrast implied or stated.
