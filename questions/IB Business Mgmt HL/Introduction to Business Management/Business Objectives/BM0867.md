# BM0867

**Define** the term SMART objective. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

A SMART objective is a business goal that is clearly defined and structured, being Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

**Mark allocation:**

Award **[1]** for identifying that SMART is an acronym relating to the qualities of a well-written objective.

Award **[1]** for correctly identifying at least three of the five components: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.

N.B. Do not accept vague answers such as 'a clear goal' without reference to at least some of the SMART criteria.
