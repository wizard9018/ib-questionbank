# BM1081

FB Catering Ltd (FBC) is a private limited company based in Oldham, UK, specializing in mass-produced processed meals for major supermarkets. As the largest employer in Oldham, FBC is a vital part of the local community.

Recently, the government introduced strict "Healthy Living" guidelines following a national rise in diet-related illnesses. In response, a powerful pressure group, "CleanPlate UK," has launched a campaign against FBC, demanding they reduce the salt and sugar content in their meals.

To protect profit margins and stay competitive against lower-priced imports, FBC’s shareholders have approved a plan to shift to a capital-intensive production model. This involves investing in automated machinery that will lead to 200 redundancies. The factory employees have expressed significant resistance, fearing for their job security. Furthermore, many workers have lived in Oldham their entire lives and feel that FBC is prioritizing dividends over its corporate social responsibility (CSR) toward the town.

1. Define the term external stakeholder. [2 marks]

2. With reference to FBC, **explain** one point of conflict between shareholders and the local community. [2 marks]

3. **Explain** one reason why FBC should move to a capital-intensive approach and one reason why they should not. [4 marks]

[8 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. **Sample response:**

- An external stakeholder is an individual or group that does not form part of the organization but has a direct interest in its activities and decisions. Examples include customers, suppliers, the government, and the local community

- **Mark allocation:**

- Award **[1]** for a definition that shows partial understanding.
- Award **[2]** for a definition that shows understanding similar to the answer above.

- No application is required.

2. **Sample response:**

- The primary conflict at FBC is between profitability and local employment. Shareholders want to increase efficiency and dividends by investing in automated machinery, whereas the local community in Oldham wants to protect the 200 jobs at the factory to maintain the town's economic stability and prevent a rise in local unemployment.

- **Mark allocation:**

- Award **[1]** for a cause of conflict identified and an additional **[1]** for an explanation of the point of conflict in the context of the case study.

3.  **Sample response:**

- A reason for moving to a capital-intensive approach is that it increases long-term efficiency and consistency. Automated machinery can work 24/7 without fatigue, reducing the unit cost of processed meals and allowing FBC to compete with low-cost imports.

- A reason against this move is the negative impact on internal stakeholders. Implementing automation will lead to 200 redundancies in Oldham. This not only creates significant employee resistance and low morale among the remaining staff but also damages FBC’s reputation in the local community.

- **Mark allocation:**

- Award **[1]** for a reason for/against explained and an additional **[1]** for application to the case study.
