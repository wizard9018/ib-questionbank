# BM0878

**Define** the term pressure group in the context of business stakeholders. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

A pressure group is an organised group of individuals who share a common interest or cause  and seek to influence the decisions and behaviour of businesses or governments, for example through campaigns, protests, or lobbying.

**Mark allocation:**

Award **[1]** for identifying that a pressure group is an organised group that seeks to influence business or government behaviour.

Award **[1]** for clarifying that they do so in pursuit of a specific cause or shared interest.
