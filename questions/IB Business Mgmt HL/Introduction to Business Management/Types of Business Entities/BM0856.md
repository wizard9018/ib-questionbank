# BM0856

**Distinguish** between limited liability and unlimited liability. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

Limited liability means that owners are only financially responsible for business debts up to the amount they have invested, so their personal assets are protected, whereas unlimited liability means the owner is personally responsible for all business debts and personal assets such as savings or property can be seized to repay them.

**Mark allocation:**

Award **[1]** for explaining limited liability.\
Award **[1]** for explaining unlimited liability, with a clear contrast implied or stated.

N.B. A simple statement such as 'limited liability protects personal assets but unlimited liability does not' is sufficient for both marks if the contrast is clear.
