# BM1243

**Define** the term initial public offering. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

An initial public offering (IPO) is when a company sells shares to the public for the first time on a stock exchange. It allows the business to raise capital and become publicly traded.
