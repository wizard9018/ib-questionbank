# BM0994

CottonOn Ltd. is an Indian-based company operating across multiple sectors. The company began by cultivating cotton on its farms, focusing on sustainable and efficient farming practices that align with global environmental standards. Due to their 20 years of experience, they have developed a unique biological pest-control method that significantly reduces costs.

Currently, CottonOn Ltd. processes the harvested cotton into high-quality textiles and fabrics, leveraging advanced manufacturing techniques to ensure superior product quality. The company is now expanding by planning to open retail stores and an online platform to sell its products directly to consumers.

Additionally, CottonOn Ltd. is exploring opportunities in the quaternary sector by offering consultancy services on sustainable cotton farming and fabric production. However, the current management team consists entirely of agricultural scientists and factory supervisors, with no prior experience in client-facing service industries. Despite the growth potential, CottonOn Ltd. faces significant challenges. These include high initial capital requirements for store setups and intense competition from established brands in the retail industry.

1. **Define** the quaternary sector. [2 marks]

2. **Outline** one industry sector in which CottonOn Ltd operates apart from the quaternary sector. [2 marks]

3. **Explain** one challenge CottonOn Ltd. faces as it begins selling directly to consumers. [2 marks]

4. **Explain** one advantage and one disadvantage for CottonOn Ltd. if it decides to offer consultancy services. [4 marks]

[10 marks]

1. **Sample response:**

    The quaternary sector involves knowledge-based activities, including information technology, research and development, and consultancy services. This sector provides expertise, advice, and specialised skills to other businesses or industries.

    **Mark allocation:**

2. **Sample response:**

    One sector is the primary sector, which involves the extraction or cultivation of natural resources. In the case study, CottonOn Ltd. operates in this sector by cultivating cotton on its own farms.

3. **Sample response:**

   One significant challenge is the intense competition in the retail industry. CottonOn Ltd. is moving from a production-focused model to a consumer-facing one, where they must compete with established brands that already have strong customer loyalty and larger marketing budgets.

    **Mark allocation:**

    Award **[1]** for the identification of a relevant challenge, and an additional **[1]** for application to the case study.

4. **Sample response:**

    One benefit for CottonOn of offering consultancy services is it would allow for product differentiation and an additional revenue stream without the high costs of physical production. By using their 20 years of experience and unique pest-control methods, CottonOn Ltd. can provide specialized knowledge that other farms cannot easily replicate, increasing the company's overall brand value and profit margins.

   However, the company may face internal diseconomies of scale or management friction. Because the current leadership consists of scientists and factory supervisors, they likely lack the soft skills required for consultancy; this "management gap" could lead to poor service delivery and distract from the efficiency of their core farming and manufacturing operations.
