# BM0855

GreenGrove Bakery is a small artisan bread business founded by Priya, a former chef. She rents a commercial kitchen, employs three bakers, uses specialist ovens and mixing equipment, and draws on her own expertise and risk-taking to run the business.

**Describe** the four factors of production used by GreenGrove Bakery. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

Land: GreenGrove Bakery uses a commercial kitchen as the physical space for production.

Labour: GreenGrove Bakery employs three bakers to produce the bread.

Capital: GreenGrove Bakery purchased specialist ovens and mixing equipment to make production more efficient.

Enterprise: Priya, a former chef, uses her expertise and takes the risk of running GreenGrove Bakery.

**Mark allocation:**

Mark as **[1+1+1+1]** — one mark per factor correctly identified and applied to GreenGrove.

Award **[1]** per factor correctly named and **[1]** for application.

Do not award both marks without reference to GreenGrove.
