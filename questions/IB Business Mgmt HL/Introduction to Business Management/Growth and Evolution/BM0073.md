# BM0073

**Identify** the term for when an entire industry expands, leading to businesses enjoying reduced long-run average costs (LRAC)? [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
External economies of scale refer to cost advantages and efficiencies that firms within an industry experience as a result of the industry's overall growth and expansion. This can lead to lower production costs and increased competitiveness.

External economies of scale examples include:
1.    University research and development funding innovation
2.    Transport networks lowering network costs
3.    Relocation of suppliers to the centre of production
4.    Influx of human capital
