# BM0882

**Define** the term joint venture. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

A joint venture is a business arrangement in which two or more separate companies agree to work together on a specific project or in a specific market [1], sharing the costs, risks, and profits of that venture while each retaining its own independent legal identity [1].

**Mark allocation:**

Award **[1]** for identifying that a joint venture involves two or more businesses agreeing to collaborate on a specific project.
Award **[1]** for clarifying that each business retains its own separate legal identity while sharing costs, risks, and profits of the venture.
