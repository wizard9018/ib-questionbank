# BM0893

AutoDrive is a UK-based electric vehicle manufacturer. RoadMap is a Japanese technology company specialising in GPS navigation and autonomous driving software. Neither company has the resources or expertise to independently develop a fully autonomous vehicle. They have agreed to form a joint venture - DriveAI Ltd - to develop and commercialise autonomous vehicle technology, sharing development costs of £300 million equally.

**Explain** one advantage and one disadvantage for AutoDrive of entering the joint venture. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

An advantage of joint ventures is that they share costs, risks, and expertise, making large, complex projects more financially and operationally feasible. AutoDrive can split the significant development cost of “£300 million equally” with RoadMap while also gaining RoadMap's “autonomous driving software” expertise, reducing both financial risk and capability gaps.

A disadvantage is that joint ventures require businesses to share profits, which can reduce the overall financial return each business receives compared to operating independently. The profits will be “shared equally” with RoadMap, so AutoDrive receives only half the returns, reducing its financial benefit compared to developing the technology independently.
