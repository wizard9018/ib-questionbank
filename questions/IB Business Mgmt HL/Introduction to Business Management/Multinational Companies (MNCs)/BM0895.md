# BM0895

GlobalStitch is a UK-based clothing MNC that recently moved all of its manufacturing operations to Vietnam, where labour costs are approximately 80% lower than in the UK. As a result, GlobalStitch closed three factories in the North of England, making 1,400 workers redundant. The move has allowed GlobalStitch to significantly reduce its cost of production and increase profit margins.

**Explain** two impacts of GlobalStitch's decision on the UK as its home country. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Sample response:**

One impact on the UK is higher unemployment.   By closing its three UK factories, GlobalStitch has made 1,400 workers redundant, increasing unemployment in the North of England and reducing household incomes, which could lead to long term economic decline in communities that depended on its manufacturing operations.

A second impact on the UK is lower government tax revenue. By relocating manufacturing to Vietnam, GlobalStitch's UK operations are smaller, reducing the corporation tax paid to the UK government, while the 1,400 redundant workers no longer pay income tax, limiting the government's ability to fund public services.

**Mark allocation:**

**Mark as 2 + 2**

Award **[1]** for identifying the impact and **[1]** for application to GlobalStitch and the UK.

Accept other valid impacts e.g. negative multiplier effect on local supplier businesses, repatriation of profits to Vietnam rather than reinvestment in the UK.
