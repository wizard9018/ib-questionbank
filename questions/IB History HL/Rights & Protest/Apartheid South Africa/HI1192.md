# HI1192

What, according to Source N, are the benefits of having separate white and Bantu national territories in South Africa?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source N** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Hendrik Verwoerd's Senate speech advocating apartheid and the Bantustans, titled “They will become the conquerors of white South Africa.” Cape Town, South Africa, 1959. |

:box[If that will be the result, if, through the capabilities of the Bantu, it happens that here in South Africa there will be a White state, a big and strong White nation, along with various Bantu national units and areas (or states, if you like), how is that different from what we have in Europe? (…) Why is it so terrible that there are various nations and territories in South Africa, and even neighbouring states? These states can nevertheless have a bond, the bond of common interest. (…)  The ideal is to retain political independence while maintaining economic interdependence.\
\
Now I ask further: if such a division isn't possible, if having separate territories isn't a viable solution to political goals, what other option remains? The United Party [the opposition] has repeatedly stated: "Nothing but a common South Africa is possible, a multiracial country, even if numerically the Bantustans outnumber whites three or four times." I emphasize, honestly and in the best interests of the white people of South Africa, that I prefer a secure white state in South Africa, regardless of what happens in other areas, rather than my people being absorbed into an integrated state where the Bantustans eventually dominate. A Bantustan for all of South Africa is the unavoidable result of the United Party's policy. Therefore, characterizing partition and subdivision as an undesirable pattern is completely nonsense, because under both policies, there will be black areas. Under the apartheid policy, the white man will at least control his own area, no matter the difficulties and how hard it may be. At least he has a chance to protect himself, something he wouldn't have in a multiracially controlled state.
]

[Source: Verwoerd, H. (1959, May 20). *They will become the conquerors of white South Africa.* Speakola. Retrieved October 11, 2025, from https<nolinks>://speakola.com/political/hendrik-verwoerd-apartheid-senate-speech-1959. Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Separate territories allow each group to maintain political independence.
- Economic connections and cooperation can be maintained even with political separation.
- Separation provides a safe space for the white population to prevent being “absorbed” into an integrated country.
- Compares this separation to examples of nations in Europe, suggesting that distinct national territories are normal and natural, rather than problematic.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to this source, the advantages of separate white and Bantu national units or territories in South Africa include allowing each group to maintain its own political independence while remaining economically interconnected ($\checkmark$). Verwoerd also suggests that this arrangement would not be fundamentally different from the existence of various European nations, pointing to their successful coexistence and collaboration based on shared interests ($\checkmark$). Finally, he argues that such separation offers the white population a secure area under their own control, giving them a chance to protect themselves from potential domination in a multiracial state where they might be outnumbered ($\checkmark$).
