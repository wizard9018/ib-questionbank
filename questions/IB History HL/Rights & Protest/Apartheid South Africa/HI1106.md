# HI1106

What regulations, according to Source A, did the Population Registration Act establish in apartheid South Africa?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Excerpt from The Population Registration Act (Act No. 30 of 1950). The act was published and came into effect on July 7, 1950. |

[Source: Parliament of South Africa (1950, June). *Population Registration Act.* wikisource.org. Retrieved October 31, 2025, from https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Population_Registration_Act_1950. Public Domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** (or a combination) of the following:
- Every person listed in the register had to be classified by the Director.
- Authorities further categorized people of color and natives.
- The Act allowed for classifications to be changed if found to be incorrect.
- This classification system enforced racial categorization.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to this source, the Population Registration Act regulated that every person whose name was included in the register had to be classified by the Director as a white person, a coloured person, or a native ($\checkmark$). Furthermore, people of colour and natives were to be classified according to their specific ethnic or other group, as defined by the authorities ($\checkmark$). The Act also allowed for the classification to be changed if it was deemed incorrect, after providing notice and an opportunity to be heard ($\checkmark$).
