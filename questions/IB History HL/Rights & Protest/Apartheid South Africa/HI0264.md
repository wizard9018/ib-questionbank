# HI0264

Identify and explain **two** ways the Freedom Charter was significant in the anti-apartheid struggle.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

For identification, any **two** of the following: 

 - It provided a unifying vision

 - It became a symbol of resistance

 - It challenged the legitimacy of apartheid

 - It encouraged international support 

For explanation, any **two** of the following:

 

 - *Unifying vision*: The Freedom Charter articulated a shared vision for a non-racial, democratic South Africa, rallying various resistance groups under a common cause.

 - *Symbol of resistance*: The Charter served as an enduring symbol of the anti-apartheid movement, inspiring future initiatives like the drafting of the 1996 Constitution.

 - *Challenged legitimacy of apartheid*:  By declaring that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$South Africa belongs to all who live in it, black and white," the Charter rejected the racial segregation at the heart of apartheid.

 - *Encouraged global support*:  The principles of equality and democracy outlined in the Charter resonated with international audiences, increasing support for the movement.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Freedom Charter was significant because it provided a unifying vision for the anti-apartheid struggle ($\checkmark$). It declared that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$South Africa belongs to all who live in it, black and white," emphasising non-racialism and bringing together groups like the African National Congress (ANC) and the South African Indian Congress (SAIC) under a common goal ($\checkmark$). Furthermore, the Charter encouraged international support by aligning the anti-apartheid movement with global ideals of democracy and human rights ($\checkmark$), which attracted sympathy and backing from organizations such as the United Nations and international solidarity networks ($\checkmark$).
