## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Churches had an official policy of integration and tried to include all races in one fellowship.
- English-speaking Churches attempted to give shared worship spaces to both white and Black members.
- Churches tried to offer multi-racial welfare and service programmes.
- Some Churches attempted to maintain interracial contact despite apartheid's restrictions.
- Welfare organisations tried to remain multi-racial in structure and membership.
- Churches aimed to minister to people of different races as part of Christian unity.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source B shows that Churches tried to build interracial fellowship by preserving “inter-racial contact”, even though apartheid reduced these chances and made such interaction rare ($\checkmark$). Source C also explains that English-speaking Churches attempted unity through their “official policy of integration,” but this effort failed because Black members were not given equal participation ($\checkmark$).
