# HI1105

Describe **three** challenges faced by South African resistance movements in their efforts to combat apartheid.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- The apartheid government used harsh repressive measures.
- Activists and their families were often subject to harassment, imprisonment without trial, and even torture or death.
- Many resistance groups struggled with limited resources and had to operate underground.
- There were ideological and strategic differences among various groups, which sometimes led to divisions within the anti-apartheid movement.
- International isolation and the state's propaganda also hindered their efforts.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
South African resistance movements against apartheid encountered many major obstacles. The apartheid government employed harsh repressive actions, such as banning groups, detaining leaders, and using violence to suppress dissent ($\checkmark$). Many resistance groups faced limited resources and had to operate underground, which made communication and coordination difficult ($\checkmark$). Additionally, ideological and strategic differences existed among groups, such as the African National Congress (ANC), the Pan-Africanist Congress (PAC), and the United Democratic Front (UDF), which sometimes led to divisions within the broader anti-apartheid movement ($\checkmark$).
