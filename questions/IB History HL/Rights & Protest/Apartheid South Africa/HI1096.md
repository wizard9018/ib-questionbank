# HI1096

Define the concept of the Bantustan in apartheid South Africa.
[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **one** of the following (or a combination of them):
- “Bantu” means “people” in Bantu languages, and “stan” means “land” in the Persian language.
- It refers to the homelands of Black South Africans that were established by the all-white government of South Africa.
\
[1 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The concept of $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Bantustan" originated in the late 1940s, combining the term $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Bantu," which means $\text{\textquotedblleft}$people" in various Bantu languages, with the Persian suffix $\text{\textquotedblleft}$-stan," meaning $\text{\textquotedblleft}$land." It refers to the designated areas for Black South Africans (Bantu-speaking peoples) within South Africa's system of separate development ($\checkmark$).
