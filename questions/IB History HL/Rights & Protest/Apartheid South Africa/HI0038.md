# HI0038

Describe the purpose of the Freedom Charter.
[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any combination of the following:
- A statement of principles and goals for the various groups opposing apartheid.
- A document that set the ideological foundation for a future democratic South Africa.
- The document written at the Congress of the People that created the vision and ideals for ending apartheid and creating a more democratic and equal South Africa.
\
[1 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Freedom Charter was the document that set out a series of ideals and goals for South Africa's future including equal rights for white and non-white South Africans ($\checkmark$).
