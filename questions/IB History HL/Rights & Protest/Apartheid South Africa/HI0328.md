# HI0328

Describe **two** impacts of the Inkatha Freedom Party (IFP) on the anti-apartheid movement.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Advocated for the rights and culture of the Zulu people.
- Provided an alternative political voice for Zulu nationalism.
- Played a significant role in the negotiations that led to the end of apartheid.
- Served as a moderating force, advocating for federalism and regional autonomy.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The IFP played a crucial role in advocating for the political and cultural rights of the Zulu people, as well as in preserving and promoting Zulu cultural heritage and identity. It significantly contributed to the recognition of cultural diversity within South Africa's evolving democratic landscape ($\checkmark$).

The IFP emerged as a major political party and played a significant role in the multiparty negotiations that led to the end of apartheid. Its involvement ensured that the concerns of Zulu nationalists and regional interests were represented in the transition process, helping shape the new democratic framework ($\checkmark$).
