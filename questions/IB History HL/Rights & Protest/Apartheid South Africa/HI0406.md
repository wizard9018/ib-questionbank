# HI0406

With reference to content, analyze a value and limitation of Source A for an historian studying Grand Apartheid politics in South Africa.

[2 marks]

$\\$

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Excerpt from the article $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Forced Removals: Summary” in Overcoming Apartheid published by Michigan State University. |

:box[From 1960 to 1983, the apartheid government forcibly moved 3.5 million black South Africans in one of the largest mass removals of people in modern history. There were several political and economic reasons for these removals. First, during the 1950s and 1960s, large-scale removals of Africans, Indians, and Coloureds were carried out to implement the Group Areas Act, which mandated residential segregation throughout the country. More than 860,000 people were forced to move in order to divide and control racially-separate communities at a time of growing organized resistance to apartheid in urban areas; the removals also worked to the economic detriment of Indian shop owners. Sophiatown in Johannesburg (1955-63) and District Six in Cape Town (beginning in 1968) were among the vibrant multi-racial communities that were destroyed by government bulldozers when these areas were declared $\text{\textquotedblleft}$white." Blacks were forcibly removed to distant segregated townships, sometimes 30 kilometers (19 miles) from places of employment in the central cities. In Cape Town, many informal settlements were destroyed. In one incident over four days in 1985, Africans resisted being moved from Crossroads to the new government-run Khayelitsha township farther [sic] away; 18 people were killed and 230 were injured.]

[Source: *Forced Removals: Summary.* (No date). Overcoming Apartheid - Michigan State University. Accessed August 12, 2024 from https://overcomingapartheid.msu.edu/multimedia.php?kid=163-582-18. No known usage restrictions.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

For **value**, the following:

 - The source gives relevant, specific details about the impacts of Grand Apartheid policies on African, Indian, and Coloured communities.

For **limitation**, the following:

 - The source lacks detail about the motivation for the forced removals under Grand Apartheid.

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source A's content provides value because it provides specific details about the forced removals under Grand Apartheid, such as the specific communities and numbers of people they affected ($\checkmark$). However, despite the details provided, the source's content lacks information about what motivated these removals ($\checkmark$).
