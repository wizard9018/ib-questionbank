# HI1102

Describe the strategies used by the South African National Party to win the 1948 elections and secure long-term power.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following:
- The National Party won the 1948 election by manipulating electoral laws.
- Only white citizens were permitted to vote, which contributed to the National Party's victory.
- Starting in 1948, the National Party established a repressive system of racial segregation, stripping rights from most Black, Coloured, and Asian populations.
- Encouraged messaging that made the white population feel afraid or threatened by the black population.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
In the 1948 general elections, the radical National Party (NP) won by forming a coalition with the Afrikaner Party, led by Protestant pastor Daniel François Malan, who opposed more liberal candidates ($\checkmark$). Their victory was achieved through the manipulation of the electoral law, which gave them a majority despite receiving fewer votes than their rival, the United Party ($\checkmark$). Additionally, the NP used political propaganda to instill fear in the white population, strengthening support for their apartheid proposals ($\checkmark$).
