## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following: 

- Increased international attention. The campaign drew international recognition, with the United Nations acknowledging South Africa's racial policy as an international issue. This led to the establishment of a UN Commission to investigate apartheid.

- Mass mobilisation and jailings. More than 8,000 people were jailed for defying apartheid laws during the campaign, showing the extent of public resistance and willingness to challenge the regime.

- Growth in ANC membership .The African National Congress (ANC) saw a significant rise in membership

- Change in ANC strategy.\

Shifted from a more moderate approach to a more militant stance in its approach resisting apartheid

- Non-racial cooperation and organisational development.\

The Defiance Campaign marked the beginning of non-racial cooperation, strengthened with Congress Alliance in 1954, demonstrating the growing organisational power of African leadership.
