# HI1103

Explain the goals/objectives of Umkhonto we Sizwe (MK).
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** (or a combination) of the following:
- Umkhonto we Sizwe (MK), the armed wing of the African National Congress (ANC), aimed to fight against apartheid and end white minority rule in South Africa.
- Its primary objective was to achieve full political rights and equality for all South Africans.
- MK targeted government installations for sabotage to disrupt their function while intentionally avoiding human casualties.
- As the conflict intensified, a gradual shift toward guerrilla warfare tactics began to emerge.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Umkhonto we Sizwe (MK), established as the armed wing of the African National Congress (ANC), primarily aimed to fight apartheid and end white minority rule in South Africa ($\checkmark$). Its main goal was to secure full political rights and equality for all South Africans, regardless of race, and to end the system of apartheid and white minority rule through resistance ($\checkmark$). MK initially targeted government installations and symbolic sites for sabotage, to avoid unnecessary loss of life while substantially disrupting the apartheid government ($\checkmark$).
