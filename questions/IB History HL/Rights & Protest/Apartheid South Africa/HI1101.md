# HI1101

Describe **two** of the racial/ethnic definitions used during South Africa's apartheid policies.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Blacks: Largest group. Segregated. Removed from their homes.
- Whites: Ruling minority. Held power and privileges.
- Coloureds: Mixed race. Also segregated. With sub-classifications.
- Indians: Originally from India. Also segregated and classified.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Colored community, made up of people of mixed race, also faced segregation and discrimination during apartheid. Within this group, additional subcategories were created, such as $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cape Coloured,” $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cape Malay,” $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Griqua,” and “Other Coloured,” showing the government's effort to divide and control the population through strict racial classifications ($\checkmark$).

Indians, originally from India, were part of the groups classified under apartheid. Like other racially designated groups, they faced segregation and the denial of many rights. The government also labeled people of Asian descent with terms like $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Indian," $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Chinese," and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Other Asiatic," subjecting them to similar discriminatory policies and restrictions ($\checkmark$).
