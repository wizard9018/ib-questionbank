# HI0405

Describe **two** key elements of South Africa's Population Registration Act of 1950. 

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - It established a legal racial classification system in South Africa.

 - It used various physical and social factors to classify a person's race.

 - It was used to control access to different aspects of society based on race.

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Population Registration Act created a legal framework for racial classification in South Africa ($\checkmark$). It used physical features like skin color and social features like type of employment to determine an individual's racial classification ($\checkmark$), which then dictated other aspects of that individual's life.
