# HI0327

Describe **two** strategies Umkhonto we Sizwe (MK) used in apartheid resistance.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- As an armed resistance group, MK initially focused on sabotaging government infrastructure, targeting power plants and transportation networks (1960s).
- Later (1970s-1980s), the MK used ambushes and bomb attacks against military and police targets.
- Operated underground and relied on secret cells, including in other countries.
- Built a network of safe houses and secret training camps, both within South Africa and in neighbouring countries.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
To achieve its goals, MK initially depended on tactics focused on sabotage, targeting government infrastructure, power plants, and transportation networks to avoid loss of life ($\checkmark$). The organization operated underground, utilizing secret cells to execute its missions and evade government crackdowns through the use of safe houses ($\checkmark$).
