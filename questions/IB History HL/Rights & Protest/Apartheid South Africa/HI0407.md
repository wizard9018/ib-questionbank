## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Anti-apartheid activists defied apartheid laws in various parts of South Africa.

 - The South African government arrested many activists for minor offenses such as entering areas without permits, staying out past curfew, or using facilities labeled $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Europeans Only”.

 - Repercussions for activists included short-term imprisonment, fines, whippings, and, in some cases, acquittal. 

 - The campaigns made the South African government concerned about increasing resistance.

 - Some national leaders were arrested and charged under the Suppression of Communism Act and sentenced to longer terms.

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

According to Source B, the Defiance Campaign organized resistance to apartheid laws across South Africa, including many major urban areas ($\checkmark$). The South African government initially sentenced participants for minor crimes, even acquitting some ($\checkmark$). However, as the campaign grew, the government became more concerned and sentenced national organizers like Nelson Mandela to $\text{\textquotedblleft}$statutory communism” according to the Suppression of Communism Act ($\checkmark$).
