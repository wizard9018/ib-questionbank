# HI1008

Describe the role of the Federation of South African Women (FEDSAW) during apartheid.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Founded in 1954 in Johannesburg as part of the Congress Alliance.
- Brought together women of all races to fight for equality and social justice.
- Organised opposition to apartheid laws, especially the pass laws for women.
- Provided leadership through figures such as Lilian Ngoyi or Helen Joseph
- Mobilised grassroots campaigns in townships and trade unions, linking local struggles to national ones.
- Led the 20,000-strong Women's March on Pretoria in 1956, one of the largest demonstrations in South African history.
- Adopted the slogan “You strike a woman, you strike a rock” to symbolise women's strength in resistance.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Federation of South African Women played a key role by bringing together women of all races to resist apartheid laws, especially the pass laws ($\checkmark$), and by leading the 1956 Women's March of 20,000 women in Pretoria, which became one of the most powerful demonstrations against the regime ($\checkmark$).
