# HI0404

Distinguish between the terms $\text{\textquotedblleft}$petty apartheid” and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$grand apartheid”.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any of the following distinguishing characteristics:

- Petty apartheid segregated public facilities on a small scale, while grand apartheid created large-scale, political, and territorial segregation.

- Petty apartheid focused on limiting day-to-day social interactions between races, while grand apartheid enforced physical separation by creating designated areas for different races.

- Petty apartheid created separate public facilities, including buses, restaurants, schools, hospitals, beaches, and restrooms while grand apartheid created the Bantustans or black “homelands” across South Africa.

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Petty apartheid and grand apartheid were both components of South Africa's racial segregation system but differed in scope. Petty apartheid focused on enforcing racial separation in everyday life, restricting interactions in public spaces such as schools, transport, and restaurants through laws that segregated facilities and services ($\checkmark$). In contrast, grand apartheid was a broader, structural system designed to enforce long-term racial segregation by controlling land ownership, political rights, and residency, primarily through laws like the Group Areas Act and the establishment of Bantustans ($\checkmark$).
