# HI1083

Outline how African American citizens in the South were still economically restricted even after the end of slavery.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Landowners retained complete control over production, rents and wages and deliberately kept African-American citizens in debt
- African-Americans were arrested for minor crimes such as vagrancy and given a high fine or imprisonment
- Prisons rented out African-American labour to private companies at a cheap rate so that companies profited from the virtual slavery that was created
- Sharecropping resulting in chronic debt and dependence
- Violence and intimidation by the KKK
- Segregated schools hindering education and advancement
- Poll taxes
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Though black citizens had technically been granted equal rights, landowners in the South retained control over production and rents which enabled them to keep black citizens in debt ($\checkmark$). The justice system was used to further entrench debt and servitude by arresting blacks for minor crimes like vagrancy and giving them the choice of a high fine or imprisonment ($\checkmark$). Black prisoners were then used as a form of cheap labour, rented out by the prisons to profit private companies and producing a system akin to slavery ($\checkmark$).
