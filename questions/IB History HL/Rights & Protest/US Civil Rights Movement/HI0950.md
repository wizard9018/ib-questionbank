# HI0950

Analyse the role of the Ku Klux Klan (KKK) in maintaining white supremacy between 1954 and 1965.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **four** of the following:
- Engaged in terrorist acts (bombings, lynchings, beatings) targeting civil rights activists and Black communities.
- Sought to intimidate African Americans and white allies from participating in desegregation or voting rights efforts.
- Worked to suppress civil rights progress through fear, often with local law enforcement complicity.
- Had a resurgence in the 1950s and 60s in response to the Civil Rights Movement.
- Created a climate of fear that reinforced systemic racism.
- Held positions of power in local, state, and federal governments, allowing it to influence legislation.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Ku Klux Klan played a strategic role in reinforcing the social and political dominance of white Americans by acting as an unofficial enforcer of segregation and racial hierarchy ($\checkmark$). Through acts of terror such as lynchings, bombings, and intimidation, the KKK aimed to deter African Americans from asserting their civil rights, especially in areas like voting, education, and protest ($\checkmark$). This not only maintained the status quo of white supremacy but also undermined the legal efforts of the civil rights movement, making it dangerous for individuals to pursue equality ($\checkmark$). The Klan's actions contributed to a climate of fear that sustained systemic racism, even as federal laws and court rulings began to challenge segregation ($\checkmark$).
