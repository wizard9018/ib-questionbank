# HI1086

Explain the significance of the murders of James Chaney, Michael Schwerner, and Andrew Goodman to the Freedom Summer.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following:
- The murders became a symbol of sacrifices made by civil rights activists and drove further activism.
- The murders exposed the brutality of racism in the South.
- The murders drew national media attention and therefore increased visibility of the reality for Black Americans in the South.
- Because of increased visibility, the federal government became more involved, taking greater action and interest in state and local activities during the Civil Rights movement.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The murder of the three activists was a terrible blow to the morale of the movement as its brutality showed the reality of racism in the south ($\checkmark$). This was a significant blow to planning for Freedom Summer as they had been given the role of finding housing for volunteers and churches to be used as schools and showed the sacrifices required by civil rights activists ($\checkmark$).
