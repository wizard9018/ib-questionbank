# HI0046Q5

Describe the role of the Council of Federated Organizations (COFO) in the Freedom Summer of 1964.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any combination of **two** of the following:
- It coordinated the work of various civil rights groups in Mississippi including the Student Nonviolent Coordinating Committee (SNCC), the Congress of Racial Equality (CORE), and the National Association for the Advancement of Colored People (NAACP).
- It coordinated the volunteers working to register Black voters in Mississippi during Freedom Summer.
- It established Freedom Schools which offered alternative education to Black students by teaching subjects not typically taught in segregated schools and to encourage political participation.

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
COFO coordinated the work of other civil rights groups during the Freedom Summer including SNCC and the NAACP ($\checkmark$). As the primary organiser of the Freedom Summer, COFO helped establish Freedom Schools and lead Black voter registration campaigns ($\checkmark$).
