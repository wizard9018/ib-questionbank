# HI1093

According to Source C, what actions did President Kennedy take in response to the bombing of the 16th Street Baptist Church?
[2 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source C** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Statement made by President J.F. Kennedy expressing outrage and calling for peace and unity in the wake of the Sunday School bombing in Birmingham, 1963 |

[Source: Kennedy, J. F. (1963, September 16). *Statement to the Nation.* University of California, Santa Barbara. The American Presidency Project.. Retrieved October 14, 2025 from https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/statement-the-president-the-sunday-bombing-birmingham. Public Domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following:
- President Kennendy sent Assistant Attorney General Burke Marshall to Birmingham in person to lend assistance to the investigation.
- President Kennedy ensured that bomb specialists provided by the FBI were sent to aid in looking for those responsible for the crime.
- He publicly condemned the attack while appealing for peace and unity.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
President Kennedy announces that the Assistant Attorney General will personally support the investigation in Birmingham through his presence there ($\checkmark$). President Kennedy also states that specially trained FBI officers who are experts in bombs will support the investigation into the Sunday School bombing ($\checkmark$).
