# HI1095

With reference to its origin, purpose and content, analyse the value and limitations of Source C for an historian studying the response to the Sunday School bombing in Birmingham, 1963.
[4 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source C** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Statement made by President J.F. Kennedy expressing outrage and calling for peace and unity in the wake of the Sunday School bombing in Birmingham, 1963 |

[Source: Kennedy, J. F. (1963, September 16). *Statement to the Nation.* University of California, Santa Barbara. The American Presidency Project.. Retrieved October 14, 2025 from https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/statement-the-president-the-sunday-bombing-birmingham. Public Domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
A combination of the below that meets the following guidelines:
- Both values and limitations must be addressed. If only one is addressed, award a maximum of **two** marks.
- Origin, purpose and content must all be referenced at some point in the response (either as a value, limitation or both) to receive the full **four** marks.

For value, any of the following:
- As the source was written in 1963, it offers a primary source and insight from the time
- The purpose of the source is to address the nation and therefore it offers substantial value as a source of insight to the impact of the bombing
- The source focuses on the actions taken by law enforcement and provides useful details of concrete steps that were made.

For limitations, any of the following:
- The source was produced in the immediate aftermath of the bombing and does not therefore include the more thorough response after meeting with those involved locally.
- Kennedy's purpose is to provide a calming response to a national crisis and therefore the response may be overly positive in tone.
- The content of the source does not address the wider context of police brutality in Birmingham
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source C is valuable to historians studying the response to the Sunday School bombing as its origin is from the time, providing direct contemprary insight into the events ($\checkmark\, \text{VO}$). The source is also valuable due to its purpose, as it is designed to address the nation and therefore conveys important information about how the nation reacted to the tragedy ($\checkmark\, \text{VP}$).

Source C is also limited in terms of its origin, though, as it is from the immediate aftermath of the bombing and does therefore not include any reference to meetings with community leaders that took place before or after ($\checkmark\, \text{LO}$). It is also limited in that its purpose was to reassure the nation, so it may have left out important information in order to take on a calming and reassuring tone ($\checkmark\, \text{LP}$). Lastly, it is also limited in terms of its content as it makes no mention of the wider issue of policy brutality at the time ($\checkmark\, \text{LC}$).
