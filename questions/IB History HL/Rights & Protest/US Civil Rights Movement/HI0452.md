# HI0452

What does Source A suggest about the Great Migration between 1940 and 1970?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of **two** of the following or other valid observations shown on the map:

 - Large numbers of African Americans left the southern US.

 - It increased the African American population in many western, Midwestern and northeastern cities of the US increased substantially (10 percent or more).

 - Some southern cities still maintained large populations of African Americans or even grew their populations as well (e.g. New Orleans, LA).

 - Many southern cities lost substantial percentages (10 percent or more) of their black populations (e.g. Jacksonville, FL)

 - Most of the western interior of the United States was not impacted by the Great Migration

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The map suggests that the Great Migration between 1940 and 1970 caused significant increases, 10$\%$ or more, in the black population of many northern cities in the United States such as New York City, NY ($\checkmark$). The map also suggests that many of those migrants came from southern cities as many of them had substantial decreases in their black populations, though there are exceptions like Atlanta, GA whose black population increased during this period ($\checkmark$).
