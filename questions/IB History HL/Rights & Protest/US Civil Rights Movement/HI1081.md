# HI1081

Summarize the provisions of the 13th, 14th, and 15th amendments to the US Constitution.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following:
- The 13th Amendment abolished slavery.
- The 14th Amendment established citizenship for all people born or naturalized in the US (including former slaves) and guaranteed equal protection of law and due process.
- The 15th Amendment prohibited the denial of the right to vote based on race, color, or previous “condition of servitude,” functionally granting the right to vote to African Americans and former slaves
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The 13th Amendment abolished slavery in the USA ($\checkmark$). The 14th Amendment provided for “equal protection” of the law and defined citizenship at a national level ($\checkmark$). The 15th Amendment provided for voting rights and removed colour, race, or previous enslavement as a barrier to voting ($\checkmark$).
