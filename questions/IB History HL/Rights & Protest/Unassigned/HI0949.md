# HI0949

Explain **two** methods used to disenfranchise African American voters in the South before 1965.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following with explanation:
- Literacy tests: Required potential voters to pass reading or civics tests that were often unfair and selectively administered.
- Poll taxes: Imposed fees to vote, disproportionately affecting poor African Americans.
- Grandfather clauses: Allowed those whose grandfathers could vote before the Civil War to bypass restrictions, thus excluding Black citizens.
- Voter intimidation: Threats, violence, and job loss used to discourage voter registration or participation.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
First, the implementation of literacy tests required individuals to demonstrate reading and comprehension skills before being allowed to vote ($\checkmark$). In practice, the tests were applied selectively and unfairly, with white voters often being exempted or given easier versions, while Black voters faced complex or intentionally misleading questions. This created a significant barrier to voter registration ($\checkmark$).

Another method was the use of poll taxes, which required citizens to pay a fee in order to cast a ballot ($\checkmark$). These taxes disproportionately affected African Americans, who were more likely to live in poverty due to systemic economic discrimination. Since many could not afford to pay the tax, they were effectively excluded from voting ($\checkmark$).
