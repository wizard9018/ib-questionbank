# HI0400

What does the source suggest about the March on Selma?
[2 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Photograph by Stanley Wofson for the newspaper New York World Telegram & Sun, March 1965. This Photograph shows an African American man carrying a child on his shoulders with a placard telling President Johnson to go to Selma, Alabama. |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- It required the president`s help
- The situation was urgent as it calls for the president's action “now!”
- It was peaceful as there were young children participating
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The source suggests that marchers at Selma were requesting the president's help as we see one of them carrying a poster that says “President Johnson go to Selma now!”($\checkmark$). From the inclusion of the word “now!” in the phrase, we can also infer that the situation is urgent ($\checkmark$).
