# HI0396

Describe the Freedom Rides of 1961.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following (or a combination):

 - Small, racially integrated groups of activists travelled the South testing Supreme Court rulings in favor of desegregation on interstate transport and interstate bus facilities.

 - Initially organized by CORE (Congress of Racial Equality - an interracial American organization established by James Farmer in 1942 to improve race relations and end discriminatory policies through direct-action projects) but eventually included the participation of the  SCLC and SNCC as well

 - The Freedom Rides generated the violent response of white racism

 - They led Attorney General Robert Kennedy and the Interstate Commerce Commission to enforce the Supreme Court rulings on desegregated interstate travel in November 1961

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Freedom Rides of 1961 were a movement that consisted of small, racially integrated groups of students who traveled on buses throughout the Southern United States to test Supreme Court rulings in favor of desegregation on interstate transport and bus facilities ($\checkmark$). It was initially organized by the Congress of Racial Equality (CORE), but eventually had the support of other organizations like the Southern Christian Leadership Conference (SCLC) and the Student Nonviolent Coordinating Committee (SNCC) ($\checkmark$).
