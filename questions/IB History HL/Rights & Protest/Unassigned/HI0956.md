# HI0956

Identify and explain **three** main provisions of the Civil Rights Act of 1964.
[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** with explanation:
- Title II - Desegregation of Public Facilities. Outlawed segregation in public places (e.g., restaurants, hotels, theatres, parks).
- Title III - Desegregation of Public Facilities by Government Agencies. Authorised the federal government to enforce desegregation in public facilities owned or operated by state and local governments.
- Title IV - Desegregation of Public Schools. Directed the federal government to assist with the desegregation of schools, including legal support and funding.
- Title VI - Federal Funds Cut for Discrimination. Banned discrimination by government agencies and organisations that received federal funding; violators could lose funding.
- Title VII - Equal Employment Opportunity. Prohibited employment discrimination on the basis of race, colour, religion, sex, or national origin and established the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) to investigate complaints.
- Voting rights enforcement (while strengthened further in the Voting Rights Act of 1965, the Civil Rights Act provided some federal oversight for voting discrimination, such as prohibiting unequal application of voter registration requirements).
\
[6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Firstly, the Act outlawed segregation in public places, including restaurants, hotels, theatres, and transportation terminals ($\checkmark$). This meant that African Americans could no longer be legally denied service or access based on their race, helping to dismantle Jim Crow laws across the South ($\checkmark$).

Secondly, it banned employment discrimination based on race, colour, religion, sex, or national origin ($\checkmark$). This was enforced through the creation of the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC), which investigated cases of unfair hiring and workplace practices ($\checkmark$).

Thirdly, the Act barred discrimination by any institution receiving federal funds, including public schools and hospitals ($\checkmark$). This allowed the federal government to withhold funds from segregated institutions, putting pressure on local authorities to comply with desegregation laws ($\checkmark$).
