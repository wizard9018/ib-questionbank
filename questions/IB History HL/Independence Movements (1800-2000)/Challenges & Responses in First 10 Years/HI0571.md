# HI0571

Analyse the value and limitations of Source D for an historian researching the demographics of Cuba up to 1902.
[2 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source D** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  This map is from the United States Geological Survey conducted in 1902. It represents the proportion of the $\lq$coloured' to the total population of Cuba.  |

![HI0571](images/HI0571.png)

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
For value, any **one** of the following:
- It allows for a regional analysis of the “colored” population Cuba.
- It was compiled by the US Geological Survey and shows that the US was interested in mapping out the population of Afro-Cubans.

For limitations, any **one** of the following:
- The source and date of collection of the information used to create the map are not provided, limiting the utility of the map.
- The designation “colored” is vague and makes it unclear who is being considered in this category.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The source is valuable as it helps illustrate the distribution of the Afro-Cuban population of Cuba just at the time of independence ($\checkmark$). The source is also valuable because it suggests that the US had a particular interest in the racial makeup and distribution of Cuba ($\checkmark$). However, the source of the data in the map is unclear, and the criteria for what was considered “colored” by the USGS at the time limits the utility of the map itself ($\checkmark$).
