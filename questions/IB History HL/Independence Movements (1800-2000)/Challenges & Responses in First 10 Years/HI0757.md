# HI0757

According to Source B, what were the steps taken by the Indian Government to deal with the influx of refugees following Partition?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Ramchandra Guha, historian, environmentalist, and writer, in his book $\lq$India after Gandhi: The History of the World's Largest Democracy'. Published by *Pan Books*, 2008. |

[Source: Guha, R..(2008). *India after Gandhi: The History of the World's Largest Democracy.* Pan Books. Retrieved June 16, 2025 from https://archive.org/details/IndiaAfterGandhiByRamchandraGuha/page/n29/mode/2up. Public Domain Mark 1.0.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Refugee camps were set up to provide shelter, with tents being set up to house the migrants.
- The State provided basic staples of rice, flour, and lentils to help the refugees with food.
- The State also organised a network of Indian and foreign social workers under the umbrella of the United Council for Relief and Welfare.
- To help the refugees deal with the shock and trauma of dislocation, there were movies, including Disney cartoons shown every evening.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to Source B in order to deal with the influx of refugees following Partition, the Indian government set up camps with tents to house the migrants and to provide shelter ($\checkmark$). The government also ensured the provision of basic staples of rice, flour, lentils etc. to ensure the refugees were provided with food ($\checkmark$). To help them deal with and overcome the shock and trauma of the dislocation and slaughter, social work organisations screened movies every evening, including Disney cartoons, which provided a way for them to de-stress ($\checkmark$).
