## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The illustration presents Cuba as a tropical paradise, reinforcing the idea of Cuba as an exotic getaway.
- The woman, depicted in bright, flowing clothes and a seductive pose, suggests a romanticized and possibly idealized view of Cuban culture, catering to American fantasies of adventure and leisure.
- The image reflects how many Americans saw Cuba not as an independent nation but as a tourist escape.
- The phrase $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I'll See You in Cuba" implies that Cuba was a desirable location for Americans, particularly for leisure, business, or escape from restrictions in the U.S.
- The reference to the Greenwich Village Follies, a popular American revue, suggests that Cuban culture was being commercialized and marketed for American entertainment.
- The presence of Irving Berlin, a prominent American songwriter, highlights how Cuba was embedded in American popular culture, often depicted as a place for relaxation and enjoyment rather than a fully sovereign nation.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One message is that Cuban culture was being marketed to Americans as a source of entertainment and fantasy ($\checkmark$).  As the source is an advertisement for a theatre play, aspects of Cuban culture were being commercialized and adapted for American audiences. This reflects how Cuba was embedded in U.S. popular culture, often viewed as a place of leisure ($\checkmark$).
