## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Source B focuses on political instability after independence and Cuba's failure to solve its internal political problems, forcing the US to intervene, whereas Source C implies that Cuba never had true sovereignty.
- Source B focuses on the necessity of US intervention to restore peace in the country and prevent more violence post-independence. In contrast, Source C is more focused on the idea that US military intervention in Cuba was a threat if Cuba defied US will.
- Source B focuses on making sure that it is visually clear that Cuba maintains its own sovereignty at least on the surface “All the executive departments and the provincial and municipal governments, including that of the city of Habana, will continue to be administered as under the Cuban Republic.” whereas Source C implies that Cuba only had superficial sovereignty to begin with and that it ‘bows' to American will.
- Source B focuses on the challenge of restoring peace and stability whereas Source C looks more at political and economic possibilities for Cuba to resolve economic and political challenges to independence.
- Source B is a primary source from 1902 and states the US response to a the challenge of real political crisis in Cuba and Source C is a secondary source posing several hypotheticals and implications of a US dominated Cuba and the challenges it will bring.
- Source B is very early on in Cuban Independence whereas Source C has the benefit of almost 20 years of Cuban Independence to reflect upon various challenges to independence.
- Source B is focused on US military intervention to solve the challenges to peace in an independent Cuba and Source C poses hypothetical help from other foreign powers like France helping to resolve Cuba's challenges.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source B and Source C contrast in their opinions on the challenges posed to independent Cuba. Source B gives us the information that Cuba was allowed to keep its flag and other institutions indicating no challenge to independence whereas Source C implies that Cuba was only independent only superficially ($\checkmark$). Source B discusses the US maintaining peace and stability militarily in post independent Cuba while Source C focuses more on other foreign powers hypothetically becoming involved to aid an independent Cuba ($\checkmark$). Source B is written shortly after Cuba became independent and Source C has the advantage of being written twenty years after Cuban Independence ($\checkmark$).
