# HI0495

According to Source C, what did Marti believe the US’s intentions were for Cuba?
[2 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source C** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Excerpt from “Cuba in 1898” by Jose M. Hernandez. Published by the Library of Congress as a part of the presentation *World of 1898: International Perspectives on the Spanish American War.* |

:box[He [Marti] feared that if Cuba's struggle for independence continued indefinitely without the imminent prospect of success it would create conditions leading to U.S. intervention and ultimately to the annexation of the island. At one point he even came to believe, rightly or wrongly, that there existed an $\text{\textquotedblleft}$iniquitous plan to put pressure on the island and drive it to war so as to fabricate a pretext to intervene in its affairs and with the credit earned as guarantor and mediator keep it as its own." For this reason, he thought that Cubans had to achieve a quick victory and then present Washington with their political emancipation as an accomplished fact. Otherwise, they could very well shed their blood merely to exchange one master for another.]

[Source: Hernandez, J.M. (2023, February 28). *Cuba in 1898.* Library of Congress. Retrieved June 10, 2025 from https://guides.loc.gov/world-of-1898/cuba-overview. Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The US intended to intervene in Cuba and annex it.
- The US might use instability in Cuba as a pretext to involve itself in Cuban affairs, claiming to be a mediator or guarantor, to take power in Cuba..
- That the Spanish power would be replaced by US power
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Jose Marti believed that the US had a deliberate plan to annex Cuba ($\checkmark$).  He suggested they might try to act as a mediator between Cuba and other powers as a pretext to take control ($\checkmark$).
