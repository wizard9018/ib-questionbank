# HI0758

What does Source A suggest about the Indian foreign policy following independence?
[2 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  A political cartoon by K.Shankar Pillai titled $\lq$Devil and the Deep Sea' published in *Shanker's Weekly,* June 25, 1950. Nehru leads the Non-Aligned Movement as Truman and Stalin watch with  concern.|

![HI0758](images/HI0758.png)
[Source: Pillai, S.K. (1950) *Devil and the Deep Sea.* Shanker's Weekly. Retrieved June 13, 2025 from https://archive.org/details/dont-spare-me-shankar. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The new Indian state is faced with a difficult choice with regards to its foreign policy as it faces the pressure of having to align with either of the two major superpowers- neither choice being appealing. This is shown in the caption, $\lq$Devil and the Deep Sea'.
- India has embarked on an independent foreign policy choice of Non-Alignment with either of the two major superpowers. This is portrayed by Nehru leading other nations taking a similar stand and holding the flag, $\lq$No Alignment with Blocs'.
- Both the major superpowers disapprove of the Indian stand. This is shown by the disapproving and angry glares of Truman and Stalin.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source A suggests that India is faced with pressure  from both the USA and the USSR to align its foreign policies with that of either country. This is a difficult choice and not viewed as appealing as conveyed by the caption, $\lq$Devil and the Deep Sea', signifying the danger from both choices ($\checkmark$). The source also suggests that India has adopted an independent foreign policy of Non-Alignment with both the superpowers. This message is conveyed by Nehru leading other nations taking a similar stand and holding the flag, $\lq$No Alignment with Blocs'($\checkmark$).
