# HI0488

List **three** economic challenges to Cuban Independence in the first ten years following Cuban Independence. 

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Economic dependence on the US 

 - Fluctuations in the global price of sugar

 - Lack of industrial development

 - Foreign land ownership

 - High unemployment

 - Mismanagement of public funds

 - Limited infrastructure, which made trade difficult 

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The first ten years after Independence, Cuba faced many economic challenges.  One challenge was the dependence of Cuba on the US for trade ($\checkmark$).   Additionally, much of Cuba was still owned by foreigners ($\checkmark$).  Finally, there was a lack of industrialisation ($\checkmark$).
