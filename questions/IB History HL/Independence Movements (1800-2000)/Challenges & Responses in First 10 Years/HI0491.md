# HI0491

Examine the impacts of the Platt Amendment on Cuba following the first ten years of Cuban Independence. 

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **four** of the following: 

 - The status as a US $\text{\textquotedblleft}$protectorate" restricted Cuba's ability to govern independently 

 - Created a permanent US military presence in Cuba to enforce political stability

 - US Naval bases were built on a permanent $\text{\textquotedblleft}$lease" to the US (Guantánamo Bay most significantly)

 - Symbolised Cuba's dependency on the US, which led to social discontent

 - Nationalist movements were beginning to form, but had little to no impact on the first ten years of Independence

 - Insured a US-dominated economy, which made Cuba reliant on US funds and trade.

 - U.S. control over Cuban foreign policy and trade agreements meant that Cuba's economy remained tied to U.S. interests.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

One of its significant effects was that it made Cuba a U.S. protectorate, restricting its ability to govern freely ($\checkmark$).  Although Cuba was technically an independent nation, the U.S. retained the right to intervene in Cuban affairs, which weakened Cuba's ability to establish a stable government without external interference  ($\checkmark$). Another key impact was that Cuba had to give the U.S. land for naval bases, leading to the establishment of Guantánamo Bay Naval Base in 1903. This granted the U.S. a permanent military presence in Cuba and symbolized the country's lack of full sovereignty  ($\checkmark$). Additionally, this led to the beginnings of early nationalist movements, which were not yet successful in the first ten years ($\checkmark$).
