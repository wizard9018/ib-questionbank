# HI0575

Describe **two** goals of the *juntas* created in Bogota and Cartagena in 1810.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following goals:

 - Replace Spanish officials: the *juntas* aimed to remove Spanish-born authorities and replace them with local Creole leaders.

 - Protect the rights of Ferdinand VII: at first, the leaders of the *juntas* claimed loyalty to the king, who was currently imprisoned by Napoleon, to justify their actions and avoid being seen as rebels.

 - Gain political autonomy: they sought to govern themselves locally without interference from Spanish colonial rule.

 - Defend Creole interests: the *juntas* wanted to protect the political and economic privileges of the Creole elite.

\

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The *juntas* formed in Bogotá and Cartagena in 1810 mainly wanted to govern themselves and make local decisions without Spanish colonial officials' interference. Their goal was to have more control over political and economic matters in New Granada. ($\checkmark$) They also wanted to protect the Creole elite's interests, ensuring that important positions and privileges stayed in the hands of American-born Spaniards rather than Spanish-born authorities. ($\checkmark$)
