# HI0578

With reference to Source A, who was the intended audience, and why might it have been printed in Santa Fe de Bogota?
[2 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  An excerpt from a supplement to Issue 5 (1811) of “La Bagatela,” a political criticism newspaper founded by Antonio Nariño, a politician, soldier, and hero of Independence. |

[Source: Adapted from *La Bagatela - Suplemento al número 5.* (1811). Biblioteca Virtual de la Republica Retrieved May 11, 2025 from https://babel.banrepcultural.org/digital/collection/p17054coll26/id/1507. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **one** of each of the following:
- Intended audience:
    - Creole-born elites and educated urban readers who could access and spread printed pamphlets
    - Provincial leaders in New Granada sympathetic to independence
- Printing in Santa Fe:
    - Santa Fe de Bogotá was the administrative and political capital of New Granada
    - It housed the main printing press and a network for distributing revolutionary literature across the provinces
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source A was aimed at Creole leaders and other educated city residents who could read and share political leaflets ($\checkmark$). It was printed in Santa Fe de Bogotá because that city served as New Granada's political center ($\checkmark$).
