# HI0574

Identify and explain **two** political causes of the Colombian independence movement.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following with supporting explanation:

 - Bourbon Reforms: 

     - The Spanish Crown's centralization policies limited colonial autonomy and created resentment among Creoles.

 - Lack of Representation:

     - The colonies had no representation in the Spanish Cortes (parliament or representative assembly), leading to demands for self-governance.

- Napoleonic Invasion of Spain in 1808: 

     - The capture of King Ferdinand VII led to a power vacuum, prompting the colonies to question their allegiance to Spain.

 - Influence of the American and French Revolutions: 

     - These revolutions inspired ideas of liberty, equality, and republicanism among Creoles.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Two major political causes of Colombia's independence movement were the Bourbon Reforms and the lack of colonial representation in the Spanish *Cortes*. ($\checkmark\,\checkmark$) The Bourbon Reforms, introduced by the Spanish Crown, aimed to centralize power and increase royal control over the colonies, reducing local governments' autonomy and preventing Creoles from holding high-ranking political positions. This created growing frustration among Creole elites, who felt excluded from decision-making despite their economic and social influence. ($\checkmark$) At the same time, the colonies had no representation in the Spanish *Cortes*, meaning they had no voice in laws and policies that directly affected them. This exclusion fueled resentment and strengthened demands for self-governance, as many colonial leaders began to question their loyalty to Spain and seek greater political independence. ($\checkmark$)
