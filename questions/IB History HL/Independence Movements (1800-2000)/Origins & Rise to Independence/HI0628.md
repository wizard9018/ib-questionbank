What, according to Source C, was the impact of the Partition of Bengal on the Congress Party?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The partition led the Congress, dominated by the Extremists, to adopt a four-point program in the 1906 Calcutta session. The program emphasised Swaraj or self-governance, support of the boycott protests against the partition, and support for Swadeshi or the promotion of indigenous industries and national education.
- The differing reactions to the partition led to a formal split in the Congress at the 1907 Surat Session, with the Extremists leaving the organisation. The Moderates emphasised the path of gradual political reform leading to parliamentary government, and the Extremists asked for violent revolutionary action to achieve complete independence. The Moderates, led by Gokhale, prevailed over the Extremists led by Tilak.
- The opposition to the partition led to Tilak being sentenced to imprisonment in 1908 for six years, along with many Extremist leaders who were either jailed or deported.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Partition of Bengal led the Indian National Congress to adopt a more extreme, four-point program in 1906 which emphasised self-governance, support of the boycotts, the promotion of indigenous industries and a national education system ($\checkmark$). The Partition also led to a formal split in Congress between the Extremists and the Moderates ($\checkmark$).
