## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

- The race-based system remained largely unchanged during or after the wars of independence.

- Creoles retained positions of power in the military and the church.

- The economic, religious, and military spheres were still predominantly male domains.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Firstly, the source suggests that the colonial race-based system did not change significantly during or after the years of the Latin American wars of independence, at least in Perú. This is indicated by the caption of the image, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Types of Peruvian people,” which already highlights a division of social groups that had existed under Spanish rule as well. This idea is reinforced by the image itself, where individuals considered by the white hegemony at the time to belong to $\text{\textquotedblleft}$inferior races” (Indigenous, castas, and Black people), positioned at the center, are depicted with distinct social roles, as reflected in their attire ($\checkmark$). Secondly, the source suggests that Creoles (white people of Spanish descent born in the Americas) maintained positions of power in the military and the church. This is evidenced by the attire of the priest on the left and the two high-ranking military figures on the right, who are represented as white people ($\checkmark$).
