# HI0479

Identify **three** impacts of the Saint-Domingue Slave Rebellion (Haiti, 1791) on Cuban independence.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Created a sugar boom in Cuba to replace lost output from Saint-Domingue.

 -Increased fear of slave rebellions caused Spain to tighten its grip in Cuba.

 - Expanded/reinforced slavery in Cuba to support increased sugar production.

 - Pushed some wealthy white refugees from Saint-Domingue to Cuba who brought money, technical knowledge about sugar production, and anti-rebellion ideologies.

 - Served as inspiration for future rebellions in the Caribbean.

 - Pushed Cuban elites towards loyalty to Spain for protection out of fear of a rebellion 

 - Made both the elites and the colonial government resistant to liberal and revolutionary ideas until later in the 19th century.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Because of the rebellion, sugar production in Saint-Domingue declined, causing colonial powers to seek sugar elsewhere, and Cuba was able to fulfil this need ($\checkmark$).  As a result, slavery was significantly increased in Cuba to support new production levels ($\checkmark$). This also made Spain double their control over the island and pushed any Cuban elites towards Spain out of fear of a rebellion in Cuba ($\checkmark$).
