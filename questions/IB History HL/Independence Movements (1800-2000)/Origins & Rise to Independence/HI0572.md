# HI0572

Describe the significance of the Revolt of the Comuneros in 1781 to the independence movement of New Granada (Colombia).

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - The Revolt of the Comuneros (1781) was one of the first significant uprisings against Spanish rule in New Granada. It showed that people could unite and fight against unfair taxes and colonial control, inspiring future independence movements.

 - A source of inspiration when leaders like José Antonio Galán became symbols of resistance, inspiring revolutionary figures such as Simón Bolívar and Antonio Nariño.

 - As the Comuneros' revolt ended in betrayal when Spanish authorities revoked concessions and violently repressed the rebels, this event deepened colonial resentment, reinforcing the belief that true freedom could only come through complete independence from Spain.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Comuneros Rebellion of 1781 was one of the earliest large-scale challenges to Spanish authority in New Granada. It showed that diverse groups could oppose unjust taxes and colonial abuses, revealing the fragility of Spanish control and planting the seeds for future independence struggles. ($\checkmark$) Although the rebellion failed, José Antonio Galán's actions encouraged future leaders like Simón Bolívar and Antonio Nariño and helped grow the belief that real freedom was only possible through total independence from Spain. ($\checkmark$)
