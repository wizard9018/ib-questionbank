# HI0548

Describe the role and significance of Antonio Maceo in the Cuban War of Independence.
[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **one** or more of the following for role:
- He was a military commander for the Ten Years War and later for the Cuban War of Independence.
\
[1 marks]

Any **two** of the following for significance:
- He was a brilliant strategist in guerrilla warfare and led the famous Invasion from East to West (1895-1896)
- Was unhappy with the Pact of Zanjón and issued the Protest of Baraguá, calling for further resistance and full independence of Cuba.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Antonio Maceo was an Afro-Cuban military commander during the Cuban War of Independence ($\checkmark$). He was known to be an excellent military commander and expert in guerrilla warfare ($\checkmark$). He also vocally rejected the Pact of Zanjón, which he viewed as an inadequate compromise, and pushed for complete Cuban independence from Spain ($\checkmark$).
