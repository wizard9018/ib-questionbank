# HI0483

Describe **two** violent methods used to achieve Cuban Independence. 

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Both guerrilla and traditional open warfare: 

     - Cuban insurgents known as mambises used common guerrilla tactics, including hit-and-run, ambush, and sabotage against Spanish forces. They were noted for their $\text{\textquotedblleft}$machete charge”.

     - Cuban rebels also used traditional open battles when strategic opportunities arose. This combination allowed rebel forces to disrupt Spanish control across rural areas while avoiding prolonged direct confrontation, which favored the better-equipped colonial army.

 - Destruction of Property

     - Cuban Rebels deliberately burned sugar plantations, mills, and railroads, targeting the economic heart of Spanish power in Cuba. 

     - The goal was to undermine the profitability of the colony, discourage foreign investment (particularly American), and force Spain to abandon the island.

- Assassinations and Raids

     - Cuban Rebels Cuban rebels carried out assassinations of colonial officials, military officers, and loyalist collaborators.

     - Armed raids on towns, military outposts, and supply routes were also common, allowing rebels to capture weapons, provisions, and disrupt Spanish communications. 

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Outside the armed rebellions, there was sporadic guerrilla warfare whereby the Cuban rebels used hit-and-run tactics or ambushed Spanish forces ($\checkmark$).  Additionally, there was a clear attempt to destroy property in an effort to economically impact the country. Often, rebels raided towns and supply routes in an effort to disrupt trade ($\checkmark$).
