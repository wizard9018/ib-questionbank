$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The origin of the source is valuable as the author is a member of an academic organisation, the Institute of Pacific Relations. The publication date, 1942, also gives the source value as it captures a record of the events that would lead India to independence in 1947 ($\checkmark\, \text{VO}$). The purpose of the source is also valuable as it aims to provide a clear, critical analysis of the situation in the country and of the nationalist movement in 1942 following the outbreak of the Second World War ($\checkmark\, \text{VP}$). The content of the source also provides value as it provides specific examples of British actions in India that impacted the Nationalist movement ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$).

One limitation of the origin of the source is that, because it was published in 1942, it cannot provide an analysis of the later crucial events that contributed to Indian independence in 1947 ($\checkmark\, \text{LO}$). One limitation of the source is that its focus is on a broader history of India and thus may lack some detail with regards to the independence movement during the Second World War ($\checkmark\, \text{LP}$).
