# HI0487

Explain the role and significance of José Martí to the Cuban Independence Movement. 

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following with supporting explanation:

 - Political leadership

     - Founder of the Cuban Revolutionary Party in 1892 which served as the ideological motivation for the Independence movement

     - Promoted Cuban nationalism - wanted a $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Cuba for all Cubans" including Afro-Cubans and the working class

 - Writer and propagandist

     - His essays and speeches, like $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Nuestra América" (1891), called on Latin Americans to embrace their identity and resist both colonialism and U.S. imperialism.

     - Through newspapers like $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Patria”, he reached both Cuban exiles and readers on the island, denouncing Spanish abuses and arguing for unity, liberty, and justice.

 - Military role and martyrdom

     - He returned to Cuba in 1895 to fight despite lack of military experience because he believed it was important to set an example and fight physically, not just ideologically. 

     - He died in battle becoming a martyr for the cause

 - Secured international support for the movement

     - Martí mobilized Cuban exiles, especially in the United States, raising funds and building revolutionary networks.

     - He wrote for major newspapers abroad which brought international awareness to his cause

     - He traveled throughout Latin America building support for Cuban Independence

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Jose Marti is significant to the Cuban Independence Movement because of his role as the founder of the Cuban Revolutionary Party ($\checkmark$)  and his writings which were the ideological background to the Independence Movement. His role as a writer not only gave the party an ideological focus but it also helped get international funding for the revolution ($\checkmark$) by writing for foreign newspapers and traveling extensively throughout Latin America. His role as a martyr came after he returned to Cuba to lead the movement ($\checkmark$). He felt it was important to physically participate in the struggle which sadly cost him his life but his status as a martyr influenced and inspired the future revolutionaries who achieved independence ($\checkmark$).
