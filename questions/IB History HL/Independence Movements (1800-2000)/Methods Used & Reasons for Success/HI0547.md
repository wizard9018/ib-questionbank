# HI0547

Describe the role and significance of Carlos Manuel de Céspedes in the Cuban War for Independence.
[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **one** or more of the following for role:
- He is considered the father of the Cuban independence
- He is known for the Grito de Yara - a public call for Cuban Independence.
[1 marks]

Any **two** of the following for significance:
- He freed his slaves and tied the cause for independence with the abolition of slavery.
- Became a popular leader and revolution figure among Afro-Cubans who played a critical role in independence.
- He is known as the one who began the Ten Years War which, though it failed to achieve independence, laid the groundwork for future movements.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Carlos Manuel de Céspedes is known as the Father of Cuban independence for his leadership in the revolution and because he publicly called for Cuban Independence ($\checkmark$). He is also known for freeing his slaves ($\checkmark$), which was significant because he linked the abolition of slavery with independence in Cuba ($\checkmark$).
