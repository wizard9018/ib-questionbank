# HI0486

Explain the effect of **two** non-violent methods used to achieve independence in Cuba.

[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following methods with supporting discussion:

 - The establishment of the Cuban Revolutionary Party (Partido Revolucionario Cubano) in 1892.

     - Helped organize and unify the independence movement

     - Ensured that efforts to support Cuban independence inside and outside of Cuba were coordinated. 

     - This diplomatic work ultimately contributed to U.S. intervention in the Spanish-American War (1898), which resulted in Cuba's independence from Spain.

 - Propaganda

     - Through writings, speeches, and diplomatic efforts, Martí and others successfully rallied support in the United States and Latin America, influencing public opinion and securing funding for the revolutionary cause.

     - These publications created a strong sense of Cuban identity and convinced more people to join the independence cause.

     - By shaping public opinion, propaganda efforts weakened Spanish influence and increased the momentum for independence.

 - Boycotts

    - Calls for boycotting Spanish goods and refusing to cooperate economically with colonial authorities undermined Spain's control over the Cuban economy. 

     - By limiting Spain's ability to profit from its colony, economic resistance weakened the Spanish government's financial ability to sustain its rule.

 - Public protest

     - Public marches and rallies organized by Cuban nationalists kept the independence movement visible and pressured Spain to consider reforms. 

     - Protests helped to maintain morale, spread revolutionary ideas, and increase civilian involvement in the fight for independence.

 - Covert underground revolutionary groups

     - Plan uprisings, spread information, and mobilize resistance across Cuba. 

     - These networks provided essential coordination for rebel forces, ensuring that independence efforts were organized and sustained over time.

- International financial support

    - Cuban exiles, particularly in the United States, Mexico, and Latin America, played a key role in raising funds and securing weapons for the independence struggle. 

 - Rise of Cuban Nationalism

     - The promotion of Cuban art, music, and literature strengthened a shared national identity

     - Cultural nationalism inspired people to support independence and reinforced the idea that Cuba should be a sovereign nation, free from colonial control.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

One non-violent method contributing to Cuban independence was establishing the Cuban Revolutionary Party (PRC) in the United States ($\checkmark$). The PRC helped organise the Independence Movement both inside and outside of Cuba, drawing support not only from the US, but also from other Latin American countries that provided funds and donations used to wage war against the Spanish ($\checkmark$). Additionally, there were calls to boycott Spanish products and goods ($\checkmark$). This had the intention to undermine Spain's control over the Cuban economy and to weaken Spain in general ($\checkmark$).
