# HI0054

Describe the Lateran Treaty and discuss its impacts on Italy and the Catholic Church.

[5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **one** of the following points for description:

- A treaty between Italy (the Kingdom of Italy, under Mussolini) and the Catholic Church in 1929

- A treaty which settled the rift between fascist Italy and the Catholic Church which began after Italian unification (1870)

- A series of agreements between fascist Italy (Mussolini) and the Church (the Pope) that was beneficial to both parties.\

[1 marks]

Any combination of **four** of the following for discussion:

- Impact on fascist Italy:

  - Legitimacy and support of the Church.

    - This was important because many people in Italy were torn between allegiance to the state and/or the Church

    - Helped Mussolini gain more support of the people

    - Strengthened his rule as the Church supported many of Mussolini's domestic policies

- Impact on  the Catholic Church

  - Recognised the Church and the Pope's sovereignty in Vatican City.

  - Made Roman Catholicism the official state religion, therefore giving the Church more power in society - schools were run by the Church for example.

  - Financial compensation settled the 1870 dispute over the loss of the Papal States that occurred with the unification of Italy, as Italy agreed to pay the Church a sum of money.\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Lateran Treaty was an agreement between fascist Italy led by Mussolini and the Catholic Church in 1929 which was intended to benefit both Mussolini and the Church ($\checkmark$). 

Mussolini gained legitimacy in the eyes of the country and the international community after healing the conflict between the state and the church through the signing of the Lateran Treaty ($\checkmark$). This lessening of tension between the two subsequently made it easier for people to support the fascist regime as well ($\checkmark$).

The Church also gained several advantages from the treaty.  Firstly, there was financial compensation in which the Italian government agreed to compensate the Church for the loss of the Papal States in 1870 ($\checkmark$). The Roman Catholic Church was also given an official status as the state religion and this gave them control over many social aspects of society such as schools ($\checkmark$).
