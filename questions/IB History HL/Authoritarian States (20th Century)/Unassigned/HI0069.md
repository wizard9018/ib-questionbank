# HI0069

Describe the significance of the Reichstag Fire Decree in Hitler's consolidation of power.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three**  of the following:

- The Reichstag Fire Decree allowed for the suspension of fundamental civil rights such as freedom of speech, assembly, and the press. This suppression made it easier for the Nazis to silence critics and opposition, reducing the ability of any group to organise against them.

- The decree granted the Nazis the power to arrest and detain political opponents, particularly Communists and socialists, without trial or due process. This enabled Hitler to eliminate opposition parties and secure more control over the political landscape.

- By authorising the use of police and paramilitary forces like the SA and SS, the decree laid the foundation for a police state, allowing the Nazis to use force and intimidation to enforce their rule and maintain power.

- The decree created an environment of fear and eliminated political opposition, which paved the way for the passing of the Enabling Act in March 1933. This act effectively gave Hitler dictatorial powers, allowing him to legislate without Reichstag approval.

- The decree provided Hitler with a legal basis to act with emergency powers indefinitely, which he used to bypass democratic processes. This was a crucial step towards establishing his dictatorship and consolidating his control over Germany.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Reichstag Fire Decree was pivotal in consolidating Hitler's power by allowing him to arrest and imprison political rivals, especially Communists, without trial, thereby eliminating immediate threats to his authority ($\checkmark$). It also suspended basic civil liberties like freedom of speech, making it difficult for opposition groups to challenge the Nazi regime ($\checkmark$). Additionally, the decree created the conditions needed for the passing of the Enabling Act, which ultimately granted Hitler the dictatorial powers he needed to control Germany without interference from the Reichstag ($\checkmark$).
