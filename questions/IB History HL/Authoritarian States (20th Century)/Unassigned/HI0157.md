$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Sources A and C identify similar ideas when talking about the reasons for Hitler's rise ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$). They both identify economic factors as important in Hitler's rise. In Source A it says that “The greatest obstacle to Adolf's ascendancy, however, was the continued success of the (Weimar) Government in both political and economic directions.” So for Hitler the fact that the Weimar Republic was doing well economically, made his rise more difficult but, as Source C says, when this changed “as unemployment grew, and with it poverty and the fear of poverty, so grew the influence of the Nazi Party” ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$). They also both identify heavy industry as financing Hitler's emergence ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$). Source A says “The young Fuehrer won the admiration, goodwill and generous support of the outstanding leaders of heavy industry” while Source C says “Nazidom was financed as everyone knows by the heavy industry” ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$). Finally both sources identify messages of revenge as being used to persuade the German people ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$). Source A refers to the "November criminals" who had stabbed Germany in the back by signing the Treaty of Versailles and the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Jewish controlled Republic of Betrayal” and Source C mentions that the Nazis made a “ violent appeal to the revenges of a populace” ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).

However, the sources also present contrasts. Most notably, while Source C mentions the leaders of heavy industry as financing Hitler, it also says “the Junkers also contributed to the millions of marks which were paid to the leaders of the Nazi Party” while Source A ignores the role played by the big landowners ($\checkmark\, \text{difference}$$\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$). Moreover, Source A gives great importance to propaganda as a reason why Hitler became so influential while Source C points out his successes within the democratic process to explain how he reached a position of power ($\checkmark\, \text{difference}$$\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).
