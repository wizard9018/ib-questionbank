# HI0097

Outline **four** factors that helped Mussolini rise to power.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of any **four** of the following points:

- The war left Italy economically devastated, with high inflation and unemployment.

- Italians felt their demands at the Paris Peace Conference had not been met, as they were not granted the port of Fiume and Northern Dalmatia, leading to anger against the ruling liberal government.  

- The Italian government was threatened by the growing popularity of the Italian Socialist Party and the two years of industrial militant action - the *biennio rosso*. 

- Socialist action threatened the industrialists and middle class who turned to right wing groups such as Mussolini's *Fascio di Combattimento* to break up the strikes, leading to his increased popularity. 

- Mussolini was able to gain the support of the Catholic Church by removing left-wing elements from his party which helped secure the backing of the new Pope Pius XI. 

- He also gained the support of the Queen mother and the King's powerful cousin, the Duke of Aosta by ending his attacks on the monarch. 

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Mussolini rose to power as he was able to capitalize on the anger amongst the Italians who were suffering from both the death toll and the economic devastation that resulted from the First World War ($\checkmark$). The feeling of humiliation as a result of  the inability of the Italian government at the Paris Peace Conference, to get control of the port of Fiume and Dalmatia, also compounded the growing anger directed at the ruling liberal government ($\checkmark$).  In addition the Italians faced high inflation and unemployment and these economic hardships faced by the workers led to working class opposition that  manifested in strikes led by the Socialist Party during the two years of *biennio rosso* further weakening the government ($\checkmark$). This  helped Mussolini gain the support of industrialists and middle class as he offered the use of his Blackshirts to crush the workers movement, thus paving his way to power ($\checkmark$).
