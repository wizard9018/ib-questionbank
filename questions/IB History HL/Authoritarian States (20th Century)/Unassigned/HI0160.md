## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following:
- Hitler's presence in Hugenberg's newspaper and films made Hitler a familiar figure
- That same presence revealed to industrial leaders that Hitler was very good at propaganda and an agitator they could use.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Hugenberg allowed Hitler to become a well known figure among the German public thanks to his use of newspapers and films to spread his message ($\checkmark$). This media presence also allowed Hitler to be noticed by industrial leaders who used him as a political tool ($\checkmark$).
