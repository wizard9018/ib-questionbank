$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\\$

For comparison, any **two to three** of the following similarities:

- Both sources reflect Mussolini's desire to increase the population

- Both sources highlight the state's intervention in personal and family matters

- Both sources mention the ONMI as a key tool in achieving Mussolini's demographic goals

For contrast, any **two to three** of the following differences:

- Source B give more detailed information about the ONMI whereas Source D gives Mussolini's ideological point of view

- Source B focuses on the services provided to mothers and families while Source D focuses on the finances needed to provide these services

- Source B focuses more on assisting people, mostly women and children whereas Source D focuses on the racial purity and the need to grow the population

\

[6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Both sources discuss Mussolini's domestic aims and include his goal of increasing the population ($\checkmark$ similiarity). Source B discusses programs designed to cope with larger families and Source D is more clear as Mussolini refers to the need for a $\text{\textquotedblleft}$demographic boom” ($\checkmark$ evidence). Both sources also show that Mussolini's government should intervene in the personal lives of the people ($\checkmark$ similarity). Source B says that the ONMI is, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$...the highest expression of the State's care for Italian women and children.” and Source D elucidates the idea that the State should intervene in people's personal lives, for example suggesting taxes on certain family structures including bachelors and childless marriages ($\checkmark$ evidence). The idea that the ONMI was created to socially engineer Italy is expressed in both Source B and D ($\checkmark$ similarity). Source B is more concrete about the structures put in place via the ONMI and Source D is from Mussolini himself announcing the ONMI and his intentions for state involvement in the lives of the people ($\checkmark$ evidence).

In terms of contrasts, Source B is more explicit about the services being provided by the ONMI such as assistance to pregnant women, infants and children in need while Source D is more focused on ideological concepts like racial purity and linking the ONMI to that purpose ($\checkmark$ difference). Source D also gives a more focus on the financial aspects on how this project can be funded saying, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hence the tax on bachelors, and perhaps in the future there will be a tax on childless marriages.” Source B, in contrast, is not explicit about the funding but rather the purpose for the programs and is focused on the types of services being offered ($\checkmark$ difference and evidence).
