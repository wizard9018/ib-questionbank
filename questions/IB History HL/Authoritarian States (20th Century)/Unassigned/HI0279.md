# HI0279

According to Source D, what challenges did China's mass mobilization (the Great Leap Forward) still face in 1958?

[2 marks]

| |  | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source D** $\\$ $\hspace{3em}$ $\\$ $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |$\text{\textquotedblleft}$The Masses Can Do Anything” an extract from a statement by Mao Zedong to a reporter of the Hsin Hua Agency on 29 September 1958 following a tour of the country.|

[Source: Mao Tse-tung. (September 29, 1958). The Masses Can Do Anything. Selected Works of Mao-Tse-tun on Marxists.org. Retrieved December 12, 2024 from https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-8/mswv8_16.htm. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of **two** of the following:

- There were places where the work of mobilizing the masses had not been properly carried out and where the tasks required, the reasons for them and the methods had still not been made clear to the masses.

- There were a few Communist party leaders who were unwilling to undertake a large-scale mass movement in the industrial sphere and who disparaged the mass movement on the industrial front as $`$a rural style of work' and $`$a guerrilla habit'.

- To ensure that agriculture was not neglected or sacrificed while focusing on iron and steel production on a large scale.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

According to Source D, Mao stated that the Great Leap Forward mass mobilization faced challenges as there were places where the tasks required for the movement, the reasons for them, and the methods had not been made clear to the masses ($\checkmark$). Another challenge was that there were a few Communist party leaders who were unwilling to undertake a large-scale mass movement in the industrial sphere and who disparaged the mass movement on the industrial front as $`$a rural style of work' and $`$a guerrilla habit' ($\checkmark$).
