# HI0056

Why were the Anti-Semitic Laws of 1938 not strongly enforced until 1943? 

[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of the following:

- Many early leading Fascists were Jewish and about 30% of all Jewish Italians belonged to the Fascist Party

- Many local officials in charge of carrying out the laws simply ignored them

- The Catholic Church was publicly against the laws

\

[1 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Although Mussolini created the Anti-Semitic Laws in 1938 in an attempt to ally Italy more strongly with Hitler, the laws were mostly ignored by the local authorities since the Church came out publicly against these them ($\checkmark$).
