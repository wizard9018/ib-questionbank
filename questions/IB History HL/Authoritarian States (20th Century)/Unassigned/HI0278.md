For comparison, any **three** to **four** of the following **similarities**:

- Both sources argue that grain exports were emphasized during the Great Leap Forward.

- Both sources mention that the policies of the Great Leap Forward drove a famine and contributed to the deaths.

- Both sources state that farmers were forced to work on producing iron and steel and on growing grain.

Both sources state that millions of Chinese died as a result of the policies implemented during the Great Leap Forward.

For contrast, any **two** to **three** of the following differences:

- Source B states that the reduction in grain production was a result of $`$increasing output of iron and steel' while Source C states it was $`$due to the policy on communal dining halls, (as) peasants no longer had private plots to grow food'.

- Source C mentions accounts of cannibalism as a result of famine and the collapse of the healthcare system, which is not mentioned in Source B.

- Both Sources mention quantities of deaths caused by the Great Leap Forward, but Source B cites the number $\text{\textquotedblleft}$40 million” nationwide while Source C focuses on the quantities in Xinyang Region ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$over a million”) and Guangshan ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$one in four people”).

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Both sources mention the increased emphasis on small-scale steel production ($\checkmark$ similarity). Source B describes $\text{\textquotedblleft}$reduced grain production due to increasing output of iron and steel” in $\text{\textquotedblleft}$small blast furnaces,” while Source C says peasants worked “backyard furnaces to create steel” ($\checkmark$ evidence).

Both sources also state that grain export continued during the famine and contributed to the deaths ($\checkmark$ similarity). Source B mentions $\text{\textquotedblleft}$deficient food supply due to over-export,” while Source C states, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mao and members of the CCP were obsessed with their export markets and thus, even during widespread famine, continued to export most of their grain” ($\checkmark$ evidence).

Both sources also state that millions of Chinese died as a result of the policies implemented during the Great Leap Forward ($\checkmark$ similarity). Source B mentions $\text{\textquotedblleft}$over 40 million people starved to death statistically” while Source C states $\text{\textquotedblleft}$by 1960, over a million people were dead due to the widespread famine” ($\checkmark$ evidence). However, The focus of the deaths mentioned in both sources differs ($\checkmark$ difference). Source B cites the number of deaths at $\text{\textquotedblleft}$40 million” nationwide while Source C focuses on the quantities in Xinyang Region ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$over a million”) and Guangshan ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$one in four people”) ($\checkmark$ evidence).   

The sources disagree on what drove reduced grain production ($\checkmark$ difference). Source B states that the reduction in grain production resulted from $\text{\textquotedblleft}$increasing output of iron and steel” while Source C states it was $\text{\textquotedblleft}$due to the policy on communal dining halls, (as) peasants no longer had private plots to grow food” ($\checkmark$ evidence). Finally, Source C mentions accounts of cannibalism as a result of famine and the collapse of the healthcare system, which is not mentioned in Source B ($\checkmark$ difference).
