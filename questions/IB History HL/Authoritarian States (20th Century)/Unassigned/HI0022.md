# HI0022

Compare and contrast the economic policies of Nazi Germany under Adolf Hitler and Communist China under Mao Zedong.
[6 marks]

---

|Provides evidence from the text (SBQ) or own knowledge (SAQ) to validate arguments| ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$) |

\
$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
For comparison, any of the following similarities:
- Both regimes implemented state-controlled economic policies to rapidly transform their nations' economies.
- Both leaders pursued massive infrastructure projects to boost industrialization (e.g., Hitler's Autobahn; Mao's Great Leap Forward).
- Both economies emphasised the role of agriculture, with Hitler focusing on achieving autarky (self-sufficiency) and Mao attempting to collectivise agriculture.
- Both leaders used their economic policies to consolidate political power and strengthen the state.
- Both Hitler and Mao's policies led to widespread human suffering due to mismanagement and repression (e.g., famine in China, forced labour in Germany).

For contrast, any of the following differences:
- Hitler's economic policies aimed to prepare Germany for war and included rearmament, while Mao's policies were primarily focused on domestic transformation, particularly during the early years.
- Hitler's economic approach was capitalist but highly controlled by the state, allowing some private enterprise under strict state regulation. Mao's policies were rooted in communist ideology, focusing on complete state control of both agriculture and industry.
- Hitler's policies initially brought economic recovery and success in reducing unemployment, while Mao's Great Leap Forward led to economic disaster and widespread famine.
- Mao's economic reforms were more ideologically driven (aiming for true communism), while Hitler's were largely pragmatic, designed to strengthen the nation militarily and economically for future conflict.
[6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Both Nazi Germany under Hitler and Communist China under Mao Zedong implemented state-driven economic policies to rapidly transform their nations ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$). For example, both leaders launched major infrastructure projects, such as the Autobahn in Germany and Mao's Great Leap Forward, to stimulate industrial growth ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$). Additionally, both Hitler and Mao sought to restructure agriculture, although Hitler focused on self-sufficiency, while Mao pushed for collectivisation ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).

However, the economic goals of the two leaders differed. Hitler’s policies were designed to prepare Germany for war through rearmament and military expansion, while Mao’s early policies were centered on domestic reforms, particularly collectivization and industrialization ($\checkmark\, \text{difference}$). While Hitler’s approach allowed for some controlled private enterprise, Mao’s policies were based on strict communist principles, which rejected private ownership and sought complete state control over both agriculture and industry ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$) Additionally, while Hitler's policies initially led to economic growth and lower unemployment, Mao’s Great Leap Forward resulted in economic collapse and widespread famine ($\checkmark\, \text{difference and evidence}$).
