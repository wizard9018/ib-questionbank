# HI0846

Describe how Mussolini used the Black Shirts to create fear in the population.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The Black Shirts were used to attack political rivals/parties generally and directly (e.g. the assassinations of political rivals like Giacomo Matteotti)
- Mussolini called the Black Shirts out to suppress socialist activities and attack strikers
- The Black Shirts were prominent in The March on Rome in order to show power and intimidate
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Mussolini used the Black Shirts to attack other rival political parties and individuals, such as the assassination of socialist leader Giacomo Matteotti ($\checkmark$). He also used them to break up worker strikes and other activities that were deemed socialist or contrary to Fascist ideals ($\checkmark$).
