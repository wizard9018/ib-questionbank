# HI1112

Describe **two** ways the Chinese government's military spending contributed to its maintenance of power during the 1960s.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- It enabled the strengthening of the Chinese state's capabilities during the Cultural Revolution.
- It was supported by the Soviet Union's technical support, allowing it to advance more quickly.
- It helped the military forces regain control and restore social order in rural areas affected by rebels in 1967 and 1968.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
During the 1960s, the Chinese state increased military spending to strengthen its ability to address the country's political turmoil during the Cultural Revolution ($\checkmark$). Additionally, the strengthening of the military helped the public forces tackle public order issues in rural areas, which have worsened due to violent opposition from non-state actors ($\checkmark$).
