# HI0312

Describe **two** actions Fidel Castro took that helped consolidate his power in the first year of his government.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following actions with its supporting description:
- Creation of the National Institute of Agrarian Reform (INRA) in May 1959.
    - INRA absorbed the Office of Revolutionary Plans and Coordination and was directly subordinated to Castro, allowing him to control agrarian reforms and secure leadership over the provisional government.
- Suspension of free elections starting in 1959
    - Castro justified the suspension as a measure to prevent counterrevolution and protect the revolution. This eliminated potential challenges to his political authority.
- The establishment of revolutionary trials and public executions in 1959
    - According to Castro, these trials and executions ensured "revolutionary justice" and eliminated Batista's former allies, consolidating his authority and removing opposition to the regime.
\
[4 marks]

\
In May 1959, Fidel Castro created the National Institute of Agrarian Reform (INRA) ($\checkmark$). This key institution absorbed the Office of Revolutionary Plans and Coordination, ensuring Castro's direct control over agrarian reforms and the provisional government ($\checkmark$).

Additionally, in 1959, he suspended free elections ($\checkmark$) to prevent the risk of a counterrevolution, justifying this measure as necessary to protect the revolution. These decisions consolidated his power by eliminating political challenges and securing his leadership over reforms ($\checkmark$).
