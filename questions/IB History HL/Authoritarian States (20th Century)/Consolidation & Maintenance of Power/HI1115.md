# HI1115

Describe **two** ways the use of force by the Cuban state influenced its consolidation of power during the 1960s.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Suppression of opposition, including former officials from the Batista government and anti-communist groups, through execution and exile.
- Defence of the state from foreign influence in the face of the Bay of Pigs and Operation Mongoose.
- Establishment of labour camps for Cubans reluctant to enrol in the military (Military Units to Aid Production).
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Shortly after the initial triumph of the Cuban Revolution in 1959, the military suppressed any source of opposition from pro-Batista and pro-USA backgrounds through execution and exile ($\checkmark$). Moreover, the use of force was paramount for furthering the anti-imperialist revolutionary ideal. For instance, Castro's commitment to defending the state's sovereignty from foreign influence was successful in hindering the US-led attacks in the Bay of Pigs and through Operation Mongoose ($\checkmark$).
