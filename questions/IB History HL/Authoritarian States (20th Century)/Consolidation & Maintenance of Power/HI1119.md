# HI1119

Describe **two** ways the Soviet Union's economic assistance impacted Castro's maintenance of power in Cuba.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Open the market for sugar cane in the Soviet Union at convenient prices.
- Commercial treaty between the USSR and Cuba for the modernisation of the Cuban agricultural productive system (1966).
- Increased Cuba's economic dependence on the Soviet Union.
- Limitations of Cuba's single sugar product in the Soviet Union's market.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Soviet Union's and Cuba's ideological proximity allowed them to start building a strong economic bond in the early 1960s. This was mainly reflected in the opening of Soviet markets for Cuban sugar imports and the signing of a formal commercial treaty in 1966, which helped Castro's government reinvest the resources in the strengthening of the agricultural productive system and implementation of literacy education policies ($\checkmark$). However, Cuba heavily relied on the Soviet Union's assistance. As the agricultural production's boost of the 1960s slowed down in the 1970s, domestic policies did not meet the economic needs in the country. The Cuban state's capabilities were further weakened in the face of the limitations of exporting sugar as one of the only food products ($\checkmark$).
