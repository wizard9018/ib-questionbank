# HI1114

Describe **one** way Castro's education policies influenced his consolidation of power in Cuba.
[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Either of the following:
- The literacy program enhanced the state's presence in remote rural areas, which helped legitimise the Cuban state's commitment to context-based social policies.
- The increase in educated people allowed for upskilling the capabilities of the workforce and forming citizens in accordance with the Cuban Revolution's ideals.
\
[1 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Further to the ideals in his revolutionary addresses in the late 1950s, Castro's implementation of a literacy program allowed him to bridge the gap between people living in rural areas and the government, further legitimising his government ($\checkmark$).
