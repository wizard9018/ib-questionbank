# HI0200

Describe **four** of Mao's initial mass mobilisation campaigns to consolidate power between 1950 and 1952.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of **four** of the following:

- Resist America and Aid Korea Campaign in 1950 directed against foreigners resulting in their expulsion and the closing of Christian churches. 

- The Suppression of Counter-Revolutionaries Campaign from 1950-51 targeting the GMD, criminal gangs and religious sects with public executions.

- Speak Bitterness Campaign (Land Reform) in 1950 encouraged peasants to turn on landlords, subjecting them to a public 'trial' and executing 2 million of them. 

- The Three-Antis Campaign in 1951 against corruption, waste and obstruction that was directed against communist and non-communists, targeting state officials, police and communist party cadres. 

- The Five-Anti Campaign in 1952 against bribery, tax-evasion, theft of state property, cheating in government contracts and economic espionage which led to the death of 2-3 million people. [4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Mao's consolidation of power was the result of a series of mass mobilisation campaigns which used propaganda, self-criticism, rectification and purges. The first of these, the *Resist America and Aid Korea Campaign* in 1950 was directed against foreigners resulting in their expulsion and the closing of Christian churches.This ensured the country was closed to all foreigners except Russians ($\checkmark$) In the countryside, he launched the Speak Bitterness Campaign in 1950 which encouraged peasants to turn on landlords, subjecting them to a public 'trial' and executing 2 million of them. This ensured the removal of substantial opposition and gained him the support of the large rural peasant population of China ($\checkmark$). The period 1950-51 also saw the launch of *The Suppression of Counter-Revolutionaries Campaign* that targeted the Guomindang (GMD), criminal gangs and religious sects with denunciations and  public execution. This helped him remove the last remnants of the party controlling China before he came to power together with any religious opposition and ensuring law and order ($\checkmark$) Mao continued in 1951 with *The Three-Antis Campaign* against corruption, waste and obstruction. This was directed against communist and non-communists, targeting state officials, police and communist party cadres and ensured people were always careful to prove they were followers of Mao ($\checkmark$).
