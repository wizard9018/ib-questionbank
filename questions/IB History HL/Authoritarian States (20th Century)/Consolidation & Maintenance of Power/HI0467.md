# HI0467

Identify **two** strategies used by Castro to consolidate and maintain power according to Source B.

[4 marks]

$\\$

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source B** $\\$ $\hspace{5em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | The following extract is from a letter written by Ernesto $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Che” Guevara to Fidel Castro, which Castro publicly read during his speech on October 3, 1965, at the “Chaplin” Theater in Cuba.

[Source: Castro, F. (1965, October 3). *Speech delivered by Commander in Chief Fidel Castro Ruz during the presentation of the Central Committee of the Cuban Communist Party at the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Chaplin” Theater*. Retrieved  from https://fidelcastro.cu/en/discursos/speech-delivered-during-presentation-central-committee-cuban-communist-party-chaplin. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of **two** of the following, each with supporting evidence from the source: 

 - Building a cult of personality around Castro: Castro reads Guevara's words to present himself as a guiding figure and a role model, reinforcing his image as the central leader of the Revolution.

 - Expansion of the Revolution: The Revolution was not confined to Cuba; it was promoted as a global movement, solidifying Castro's leadership among international revolutionary groups

 - Encouraging absolute loyalty to the revolutionary cause: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Wherever I may end up I will feel the responsibility of being a Cuban revolutionary, and I will act as one.” This reinforces the idea of unwavering ideological commitment.

 - Portrayal of the state as the provider for the people: Guevara expresses confidence that the revolutionary state will provide for and educate his family, reinforcing the idea of a paternalistic government ensuring citizens' well-being.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source B reflects Castro's attempt to use Guevara's words to help build his cult of personality and maintain power ($\checkmark$). Guevara reinforces Castro's cult of personality by portraying him as a mentor and indispensable leader, stating, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I am thankful to you for your teachings and your example.” By publicly reading the letter, Castro strengthened his authority and secured loyalty among his followers ($\checkmark$). 

The letter also highlights Castro and Guevara's desire to expand the revolution globally, with Guevara saying, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$I have at all times been identified with the foreign policy of our Revolution.” ($\checkmark$) This underscores the Revolution's international scope, solidifying Castro's influence abroad while uniting Cubans under a broader ideological mission ($\checkmark$).
