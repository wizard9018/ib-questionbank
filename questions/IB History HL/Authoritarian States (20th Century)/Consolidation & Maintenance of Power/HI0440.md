# HI0440

State the impacts of the Treaty of Brest Litovsk to the Bolshevik's consolidation of power.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following: 

 - The Bolsheviks could focus on fighting against the White Army in the civil war.

 - The Bolsheviks were able to keep the promise in the April Theses by offering peace to the war weary citizens and soldiers. 

 - By signing the treaty with the Central Powers, the Bolsheviks established themselves as the legitimate successor government to the Provisional Government and Tsarist Government.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Treaty of Brest Litovsk was significant to the Bolshevik's consolidation of power due to several reasons. Firstly, by signing the treaty with the Central powers, the Bolshevhiks established themselves as the legitimate successor to the Provisional and Tsarist Government ($\checkmark$). By withdrawing from the war, the Bolsheviks could focus their resources in fighting against the White Army in the Russian Civil War ($\checkmark$).
