# HI1113

Describe **three** ways Chinese foreign policy affected its consolidation of power in the 1970s.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- China's confrontation with China in the context of the Sino-Soviet Split partly influenced Mao's violent undertakings during the Cultural Revolution against suspected anti-communist individuals and groups.
- China's foreign policy, centred on the balance of power and cooperation, paved the way for modernisation policies.
- China's foreign policy, focused on foreign investment, helped the state implement socioeconomic policies tailored to the peasants and reestablish its legitimacy in rural areas.
- China's alignment with the USA in the 1970s allowed it to attempt to exert control over Taiwan, but it had a limited impact on its consolidation of power.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
China's foreign policy focused on the balance of power in the Asian region, and transnational cooperation was translated into modernisation policies, thereby establishing conditions for establishing a most robust economic system ($\checkmark$). Moreover, the shift from ideological confrontation with the USA to economic measures linked to openness to trade allowed the Chinese regime to regain legitimacy amongst peasants ($\checkmark$). However, China's foreign policy also had shortcomings. Although the US support in the 1970s allowed China to resume its attempt to control Taiwan, it had a limited impact on its consolidation of power as the Taiwanese government remained strong ($\checkmark$).
