# HI1125

According to Source E, what was the significance of public health provision in the consolidation of power of the Cuban authoritarian state?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source E** | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Castro's speech on the role of doctors in the Cuban public medical service in the early 1960s. |

[Source: Adapted from Castro, F. (1964, September 11). *Cuba's Record in Medical Care.* Marxists.org. Retrieved October 15, 2025 from https://www.marxists.org/history/etol/newspape/world-outlook/v02n33-oct-02-1964-wo.pdf#page=6 p. 7-8. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Increased enrollment of doctors in the public health system.
- Establishment of the Rural Medical Service to address the needs of the rural population.
- Satisfaction of public health recipients across the country.
- Increase in the construction of public health facilities.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to the source, the public health provision in Cuba helped consolidate the power of the authoritarian state by strengthening the health system throughout the country. The needs of the population were met through the creation of a robust Rural Medical Service that reached people in distant rural areas ($\checkmark$). This was further enhanced through the expansion of the health system, particularly the construction of new hospitals and dispensaries, all of which were paramount for increasing the satisfaction of the services’ recipients and legitimising the public health policies of the Cuban state ($\checkmark$). The source also says that the provision helped increase the number of doctors available to provide services in the public health system ($\checkmark$).
