# HI0466

With reference to origin, purpose, and content, analyse the value and limitations of Source A for a historian studying the methods Fidel Castro used to maintain power in Cuba.

[4 marks]

$\\$

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Extract from the speech by Commander Camilo Cienfuegos at the Agramonte Theater in the city of Camagüey on October 21, 1959, to report on the betrayal of Commander Huber Matos Benítez.

[Source: Cienfuegos\, C. (1959, October 21). *Discurso del Comandante Camilo Cienfuegos en el Teatro Agramonte de la ciudad de Camagüey, para informar sobre la traición del Comandante Huber Matos Benítez.* Instituto de Historia de Cuba. ## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

A combination of the below that meets the following guidelines:

 - Both values and limitations must be addressed. If only one is addressed, award a maximum of **two** marks.

 - Origin, purpose and content must all be referenced at some point in the response (either as a value, limitation or both) to receive the full **four** marks.

For value, any of the following: 

 - Provides insight from Camilo Cienfuegos, a close ally of Castro who helped him maintain power.

 - The public speech's purpose is to demonstrate how $\text{\textquotedblleft}$betrayal” would be treated, highlighting methods used to reinforce Castro's authority.

 - Shows how Castro's government framed dissent as betrayal to maintain control.

For limitations, any of the following: 

 - Camilo Cienfuegos, not Castro himself, gave the speech, so it may not wholly reflect Castro's perspective.

 - The source shows only one example of how internal dissent was treated, thus limiting our understanding of the range of tactics used to maintain Castro's regime.

 - The source treats all disagreement as betrayal, making it hard to tell the difference between honest criticism and real acts of treason, and how Castro may have treated various cases differently.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source A provides insight from one of Castro's key supporters, providing insight into the role of Castro's closest allies in his maintenance of power ($\checkmark\, \text{VO}$). The content of the source provides insight into how Castro's government justified repression, portraying opposition as betrayal to solidify control ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$). It also shows how public rhetoric was used as a tool of legitimacy and to discourage dissent ($\checkmark\, \text{VP}$). 

The source provides insight into only one method, internal purges, and how they were used to maintain power. ($\checkmark\, \text{LP}$). Also, the source cites only two examples of internal dissent in Matos and Pedro Luis, so it cannot provide insight into the full extent of internal opposition that Castro was managing to maintain power ($\checkmark\, \text{LC}$).
