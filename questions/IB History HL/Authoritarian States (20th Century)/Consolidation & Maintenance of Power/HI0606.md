# HI0606

What is the message of Source B?
[2 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** $\hspace{3em}$ $\\$ $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  “Bumper harvest after bumper harvest, leap forward after leap forward!” Poster designed by Wu Linsheng and published in May 1959. |

![HI0606](images/HI0606.jpg)
[Source: Linsheng, W (1958, May). *Bumper harvest after bumper harvest, leap forward after leap forward!* Private collection hosted on ChinesePosters.net. Retrieved March 7, 2025 from https://chineseposters.net/posters/pc-1958-026. Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Substantial gains are being made in the wheat, cotton and corn harvests (as seen in the graph and the crops shown behind it)
- These gains will continue (bumper harvest after bumper harvest, great leap after great leap), also shown through the title and trajectory of the rocket
- Farmers in China should be optimistic about their future.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The message of the poster is that substantial gains are being made (as seen in the graph) ($\checkmark$). Furthermore, these gains will continue as shown in the caption “bumper harvest after bumper harvest, great leap after great leap” and also shown by the trajectory of the rocket ($\checkmark$).
