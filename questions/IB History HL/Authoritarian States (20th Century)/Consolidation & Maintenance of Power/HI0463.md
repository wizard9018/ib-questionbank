# HI0463

What does Source A suggest about the methods used by Castro to consolidate his power in Cuba?

[3 marks]

$\\$

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Extract from the speech by Commander Camilo Cienfuegos at the Agramonte Theater in the city of Camagüey on October 21, 1959, to report on the betrayal of Commander Huber Matos Benítez.

[Source: Cienfuegos\, C. (1959, October 21). *Discurso del Comandante Camilo Cienfuegos en el Teatro Agramonte de la ciudad de Camagüey, para informar sobre la traición del Comandante Huber Matos Benítez.* Instituto de Historia de Cuba. Retrieved February 17, 2025, from https://spreaker.com/episode/camilo-en-camaguey-denuncia-la-traicion-de-hubert-matos--19722615. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any of the following:

 - Portraying loyalty to Castro as a measure of revolutionary commitment. The speech frames betrayal of Castro as a betrayal of the Cuban Revolution, reinforcing his position as the sole legitimate leader.

 - Marginalizing political opposition - By labeling Huber Matos a traitor, the speech discourages dissent and strengthens Castro's control by eliminating potential rivals.

 - Emphasizing mass support for the revolution - Reference to $\text{\textquotedblleft}$massive demonstrations" legitimizes Castro's rule, portraying him as having the backing of the Cuban people.

 - Framing the revolution as a struggle between loyalty and treason - The idea that the revolution has only two paths, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$victory or death," discourages opposition and solidifies Castro's authority.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Camilo Cienfuegos' speech supports Castro's consolidation of power by portraying loyalty to Castro as essential to the revolution, marginalizing political opposition, and framing the revolution as a struggle between loyalty and treason ($\checkmark$). He presents the betrayal of Castro as a betrayal of the Cuban Revolution, reinforcing the idea that true revolutionaries must remain faithful to him. By labeling Huber Matos a traitor, he discredits political opposition and discourages dissent ($\checkmark$). Furthermore, his statement that the revolution has only $\text{\textquotedblleft}$two paths: victory or death" creates a sense of urgency and absolute commitment, making opposition appear disloyal and dangerous to the revolutionary cause ($\checkmark$).
