# HI0374

Explain the relationship between China's policies of benevolence and domestic policies in Source B.
[4 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Source B** | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Address by Mao Zedong on the importance of domestic policies in China's leading role in the Korean War. |

[Source: Adapted from Mao, Z. (1953, September 12). *Our Great Victory in the War to Resist U.S. Aggression and Aid Korea and Our Future Tasks.* Retrieved October 18, 2025 from https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-5/mswv5_34.htm. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- China's policies of benevolence under Mao's regime stressed an ideological principle of anti-imperialism against the historical legacy of Japanese invasion attempts and achievements. Chinese people were thus expected to contribute to a major cause of their own nation through hard work and high levels of productivity.
- China's policies of benevolence were established through communist alliances in the early 1950s, aligning with development programs aimed at modernising the domestic economic system (e.g., heavy industry and infrastructure construction).
- China's policies of benevolence supported nations affected by US imperialism and thus encouraged the Chinese people to support the efforts of the regional cause in the frame of the Korean War.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
China's policies of benevolence were deeply grounded in Mao's and the CCP's firm commitment to the ideological principles of anti-imperialism, communism and nationalism ($\checkmark$). This is reflected in the source's references to Mao's incitement of national unity and implementation of social policies of collectivisation of agricultural outputs aimed at outweighing the long-term impacts of past imperial legacies, such as the Japanese invasions ($\checkmark$).

Moreover, the commitment to communism positioned China's policies of benevolence at the centre of communist alliances at the international level ($\checkmark$). As illustrated in the source, China's active involvement in the Korean War led to the intensification of domestic policies for economic modernisation built on the equipment of heavy industry and the construction of infrastructure against the threats of the USA in the region ($\checkmark$).
