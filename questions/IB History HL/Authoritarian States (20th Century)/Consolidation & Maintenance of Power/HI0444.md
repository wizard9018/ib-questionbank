## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Russia must not be involved in a conflict far beyond her strength.
- Trotsky believes that Russia should buy time for the success of other socialist revolutions in the West and expects they would come to Russia's aid if the other revolutions were successful.
- The well-being of the revolution is the highest priority.
- The treaty would also allow the Bolsheviks to buy time to reconstruct their army and other internal issues.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source A suggests that Russia should not be involved in a conflict that is far beyond her own strength ($\checkmark$). Another message is that Trotsky believes that Russia should use the treaty to buy time in order for other countries' socialist revolutions to be successful, so that those countries could come to Russia's aid ($\checkmark$). Lastly, the source also believes that the revolution should take the highest priority ($\checkmark$).
