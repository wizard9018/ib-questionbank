# HI1121

According to Source A, what legal methods were used to consolidate power in the Chinese authoritarian state?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Address by Mao Zedong on the role of the Chinese Communist Party in the early 1950s. |

[Source: Adapted from Mao, Z. (1951, February 18). *Main Points of the Resolution Adopted at the Enlarged Meeting of the Political Bureau of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China.* Retrieved October 18, 2025 from https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-5/mswv5_12.htm. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Institutionalisation of party building.
- Concentration of power under the Central Committee.
- Training of officers for administrative tasks.
- Distinction of party building in cities and rural areas
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to the source, the power of the Chinese authoritarian state through legal methods was mainly achieved by institutionalising the party across the country ($\checkmark$). Moreover, the Chinese regime organised party units more consistently by controlling industrial workers in the cities and enrolling longstanding rural party members on the wider party structures ($\checkmark$). Finally, the source says that the concentration of power under the Central Committee allowed the implementation of top-down guidelines for organisations at different levels ($\checkmark$).
