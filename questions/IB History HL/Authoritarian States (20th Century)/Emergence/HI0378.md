# HI0378

Describe **two** causes of the Kornilov Affair.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Kornilov wanted to get rid of the Bolsheviks.
- Kornilov wanted to get rid of the Provisional Government.
- Kornilov wanted to restore order in the Russian army.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Support for the Bolshevik Party began to grow when Lenin argued that there should be a Socialist revolution. Kornilov, head of the army, was a conservative army officer. He ordered his troops to march on Petrograd to address the Bolshevik revolutionary threat ($\checkmark$).

Petrograd Soviet Order No. 1 called for each military unit to elect a committee to manage the unit. Soldiers' committees had weakened the authority of the officers, which led to desertions and ill-discipline. Kornilov sent troops to Petrograd to remove the Soviet and restore order in the army ($\checkmark$).
