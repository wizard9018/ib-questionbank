# HI1129

Explain **two** ways Goebbels' propaganda campaign contributed to Hitler's rise to power.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following with supporting explanation:
- Goebbels directed propaganda in accordance with people's social and economic interests.
    - Specific leaflets were produced for different social groups, and Nazi speakers paid particular attention to the worries and concerns of the individual clubs and societies they addressed.
- Goebbels exploited modern technology.
    - Goebbels hired airplanes for the practical purpose of transporting Hitler to as many places as possible, but also to project a statesman figure.
- Goebbels played on the idea of $\lq$scapegoats'.
    - Nazism focused on several identifiable groups which were denounced and blamed for Germany's suffering: the $\lq$November criminals', Communists, and Jews.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Goebbels tailored the Nazi propaganda message to fit a whole range of people ($\checkmark$). Specific leaflets were produced for different social groups, and Nazi speakers delivered messages tailored to appeal to their target audiences. For example, workers were promised jobs, farmers higher prices, and shopkeepers protection against competition. Nazi policies were flexible, so many Germans could find something that appealed to them ($\checkmark$). The German people supported the Nazis not because they shared Nazis' views but because they shared the Nazis' fears. This was achieved by Goebbels who played on the idea of $\lq$scapegoats' as Nazi propaganda denounced and blamed several groups for Germany's problems ($\checkmark$).Firstly, Nazi propaganda whipped up fear and hatred of the Communists. It was seen as a threat to traditional values and the ownership of property and wealth. Secondly, it exploited the long-established history of anti-Semitism in Germany and held the Jews responsible for all Germany's suffering. Thirdly, Nazism blamed the $\lq$November criminals' for German acceptance of the Armistice. These politicians became representative of all aspects associated with the Weimar Republic ($\checkmark$).
