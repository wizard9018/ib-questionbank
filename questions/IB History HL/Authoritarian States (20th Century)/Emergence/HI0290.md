## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - The image suggests the centrality of Plaza de Mayo as a political symbol in Argentine history.

 - The image suggests this was a large-scale, mass mobilization.

 - The image suggests that many of the attendees were young people.

 - The image suggests that participants viewed the demonstration as a patriotic event, evidenced by the flag present in the photo.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source D suggests that many people attended the gather in the Plaza de Mayo in support of Perón ($\checkmark$). The flag in the image also indicates that the participants felt their demonstration was a form of patriotism for Argentina ($\checkmark$).
