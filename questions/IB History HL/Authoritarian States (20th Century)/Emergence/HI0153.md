# HI0153

Describe **three** measures Perón implemented as Secretary of Labor which helped him rise to power.
\
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **3** of the following:
- Rural Workers' Code: established new living and working conditions for rural workers as well as medical care, paid annual vacation leave and adequate housing.
- Creation of Labor Courts: Employees now had a way to resolve their conflicts with employers legally.
- Paid annual vacation leave for all workers.
- One extra month's pay per year (*aguinaldo*).
- Government recognition of trade unions.
- Dismissal Law: established that every worker had the right to receive a compensation proportional to their seniority in the event of being dismissed without cause.
- Social Security in case of being unemployed.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
As Secretary of Labor during the dictatorship of the Grupo de Oficiales Unidos (GOU), Perón implemented a series of reforms that would garner him the support of the workers. Some of these were the introduction of paid annual vacation leave for all workers ($\checkmark$).  He also implemented a policy granting workers 13 monthly salaries a year ($\checkmark$). Furthermore, Perón formally recognized trade unions. This gave trade unions greater power of negotiation ($\checkmark$).
