# HI1133

Explain the significance of the Kornilov Affair in the Bolsheviks' rise to power in 1917.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following with supporting explanation:
- The Bolsheviks organised an army called the Red Guard.
    - Kornilov took troops to restore order by marching on Petrograd which panicked Kerensky. He released Bolshevik prisoners and organised an army, the Red Guards.
- The Bolsheviks kept their arms after the defeat of Kornilov.
    - Kerensky also armed the Bolshevik Red Guard with rifles and ammunition, which they kept. These weapons were used by the Bolsheviks in the November Revolution.
- The Affair increased political support for the Bolsheviks in the soviets.
    - The Bolsheviks removed the threat of Kornilov. By the end of September 1917, the Bolsheviks had control of the Petrograd Soviet and Leon Trotsky was its chairman.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
In September 1917, the army leader Kornilov decided to send troops into Petrograd to remove the Petrograd Soviet and the Provisional Government ($\checkmark$). Kerensky was forced to free imprisoned Bolshevik leaders and arm them to halt the coup. Trotsky, a recent recruit to the Bolsheviks, organized an armed Red Guard of 25,000 volunteer soldiers to defend Petrograd ($\checkmark$).

The Red Guard dug trenches and fortified the city. They also persuaded Kornilov's soldiers to stand down ($\checkmark$). The Bolsheviks gained credit for leading resistance among the workers. By the end of September, they had control of the Petrograd Soviet and Trotsky was elected chairman. He formed the Military Revolutionary Committee of the Petrograd Soviet to plan and execute the October Revolution. ($\checkmark$).
