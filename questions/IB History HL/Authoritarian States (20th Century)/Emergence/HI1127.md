# HI1127

Explain **one** reason the economy of the Weimar Republic collapsed after 1929.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **one** of the following with supporting explanation:
- The German economy depended on US loans.
    - In 1924 the Dawes Plan provided an American loan of 800 million gold marks to help kick-start the German economy. This initiative brought about a marked economic revival: inflation fell, industry expanded, and exports increased.
- The  US stock market crashed in 1929.
    - Wall Street, New York was the main American stock market where company shares were bought and sold. In October 1929 the panic selling of shares in this market led to a crash which marked the beginning of the Great Depression.
- The German economy depended on US loans.
    - American business failure put pressure on the banking system and many of the loans offered to Germany in the period since 1924 were recalled. This meant that the German economy suffered a severe blow.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
In 1924, through the Dawes Plan, Germany was loaned 800 million marks by the USA to invest in industry and commerce. The Dawes Plan was a success because the economy began to recover. By 1930, Germany was one of the world's leading exporters of manufactured goods. However, the German economy was dangerously dependent on the continuation of American loans ($\checkmark$). Shortly after the Wall Street Crash of October 1929, the American economy went into recession, triggering an economic crisis in Germany. Industrial production declined sharply because American loans and investment dried up, and the industrialised nations reduced their imports. Prices and wages fell, and the number of bankruptcies increased ($\checkmark$).
