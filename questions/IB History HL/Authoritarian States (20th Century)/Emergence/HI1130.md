# HI1130

Describe **two** impacts of the Great Depression on Germany.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Germany saw unemployment rocket to 25% of the working population.
- Increased homelessness and malnutrition.
- Portrayed Hitler as a saviour who would rebuild Germany.
- Increased anti-communist fears.
- Decrease in support for Weimar parties.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Germany's industrial recovery was built on borrowing from US banks. When the US stock market crashed in October 1929, the banks lending money to Germany needed it back, causing a lack of funds for Germany's postwar recovery ($\checkmark$). Many Germans lacked faith in the Weimar government, as it seemed to have no clear idea about how to address the problems of rising unemployment and growing poverty, increasing distrust in its leadership ($\checkmark$).
