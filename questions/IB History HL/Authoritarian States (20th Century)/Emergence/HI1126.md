# HI1126

Outline **three** methods used by the Nazis to increase their electoral support up to 1932.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- The Nazis made widespread use of propaganda.
- The Nazis blamed several groups for Germany's suffering.
- Hitler attacked the Treaty of Versailles.
- The Nazis used violence against the opposition.
- Hitler made public speeches across Germany.
- Hitler provided solutions to the problems of the Great Depression.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Firstly, the Great Depression significantly impacted the lives of the German people. In 1929, approximately 1.4 million Germans were unemployed. By 1933, this had increased to 6 million. The Nazis appealed to the unemployed by promising jobs in road-building and public works ($\checkmark$).

Secondly, the Nazis used propaganda to attack the government and criticise its inability to get Germany out of the depression. Radio broadcasts, millions of election posters, rallies, parades, and marches carried out the Nazi message in every town and home in Germany ($\checkmark$).

Finally, the Nazi leadership focused attention on the communist threat; as a result, they encouraged the use of violence against the communists. The *Sturmabteilung* (SA) frequently attacked communists and disrupted Communist Party meetings ($\checkmark$).
