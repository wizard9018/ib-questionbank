## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following ideas: 

 - Perón sees the movement as the rebirth of a workers' consciousness, which he believed was essential for the greatness and immortality of Argentina.

 - He sees the event as a sign of trust and loyalty and a rejection of those who thought “the people” would betray him and the movement.

 - Perón sees the movement as his acceptance as the leader of the working masses, showing a deep emotional connection by symbolically embracing them like family.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Perón sees the massive congregation in Plaza de Mayo in October 17, 1945, as the rebirth of workers' consciousness, which he believes is essential for Argentina's strength and success ($\checkmark$). He views the gathering as a sign of the workers' loyalty and trust, encouraging him forward as the leader of Argentina ($\checkmark$).
