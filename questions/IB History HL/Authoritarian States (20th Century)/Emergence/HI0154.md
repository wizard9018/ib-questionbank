# HI0154

Explain **one** of the ways in which World War II contributed to Perón's rise to power. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **1** of the following with its corresponding evidence:
- It generated political instability
    - WWII was one of the reasons for the 1943 coup that aimed at preventing Argentina´s declaration of war against the Axis powers. Perón was one of the leaders of the group that perpetrated the coup, the Grupo de Oficiales Unidos (GOU). Being a member of the GOU allowed him to pass measures that would garner him support. WWII created additional political instability from 1943-1945 due to US sanctions on the government to force it to declare war on the Axis. This instability facilitated Peron's rise.
- It allowed Perón to appeal to a nationalist ideology
    - After being faced with pressure from the US to declare war on the Axis powers, Perón appealed to Argentinian independence which  resonated with the population that resented the US's bullying tactics.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
WWII allowed Perón to appeal to the population`s nationalist sentiment ($\checkmark$). The population was resentful of U.S. pressures to declare war on the Axis powers. By resisting what many viewed as American bullying tactics, Perón positioned himself as a defender of Argentina’s sovereignty. These tactics included everything from a trade embargo to pressuring the governments of other Latin American countries as well as Great Britain to not recognize the government of President Farrell ($\checkmark$). This is clearly seen in one of his campaign slogans “Braden o Perón” referring to the name of the US ambassador to Argentina in 1945 ($\checkmark$) and asking people to vote for the US or for the representative of Argentinian interests.
