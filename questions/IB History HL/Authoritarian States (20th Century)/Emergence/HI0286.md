# HI0286

What does Source A suggest about Juan Domingo Perón?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - The poster suggests Perón would not back down, depicting he and his supporters during the mass demonstration on October 17, 1945, as unshakable, using the word $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Inconmovible” (unmovable).

 - The obelisk symbolizes Perón's firm and resistant leadership, while the waves represent opposition that cannot weaken him.

 - It suggests that Perón still has the loyalty of his supporters two years after the demonstrations of 1945 and marks $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Loyalty Day”, a key moment in the Peronist movement.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The poster emphasizes Perón's leadership as strong and unshakable by using the word $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Inconmovible,” which suggests that his rise to power and the movement's foundation cannot be weakened ($\checkmark$). Additionally, the obelisk serves as a symbol of stability and resilience, portraying Perón as a leader who stands firm despite opposition, shown by the waves, while reinforcing the idea that October 17, 1945, was a defining and enduring moment for the Peronist movement even two years later ($\checkmark$).
