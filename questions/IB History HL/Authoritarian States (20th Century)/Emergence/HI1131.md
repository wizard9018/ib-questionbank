# HI1131

Describe **four** factors that helped the Bolshevik seizure of power.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **four** of the following:
- The Provisional Government decided to continue fighting in the First World War.
- The Provisional Government did not address the land issue.
- Lenin's promises were more attractive than the Provisional Government's policies.
- The Petrograd Soviet undermined Kerensky's authority.
- Kerensky failed to deal with Kornilov, and this strengthened Lenin's position.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Russia's continued involvement in the First World War led to higher casualties, closure of factories and further food and fuel shortages. Lenin, leader of the Bolsheviks, was able to set the Bolsheviks up as the only anti-war party against the Provisional Government ($\checkmark$).

The Provisional Government refused to solve the land issue and asked peasants to wait for the election of the Constituent Assembly, which led to many peasants seizing land from their landlords. The Bolsheviks recognized the land seizures, which improved their previously weak support in the countryside ($\checkmark$).

Lenin offered an end to the unpopular war, the transfer of agricultural land from the nobility to the peasants and food for the hungry. These were issues which Kerensky failed to address. Support for the Bolsheviks increased quickly, particularly in the soviets and in the army ($\checkmark$).

The Petrograd Soviet controlled the armed forces. By September 1917, the Bolsheviks had control of the Petrograd Soviet, and Trotsky was its chairman. He formed the Military Revolutionary Committee and Red Guard units, which he used to seize power in November 1917 ($\checkmark$).
