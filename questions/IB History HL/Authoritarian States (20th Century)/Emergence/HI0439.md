# HI0439

Explain the reasons the Provisional Government was unpopular after it took power in Russia in March of 1917. \

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following reasons with supporting explanation:

 - Failure of the Russian offensive during WWI:

     - The Provisional Government continued to fight in the First World War to fulfill Russia's commitment in the Triple Entente. Moreover, the Kerensky government hoped to use the campaign to restore national unity and pride. However, the offensive failed since the Germans and Austrians were able to advance further into Russian territory and deliver more damage to the Russian forces. The failure of the offensive further discredited the Provisional Government as a result. 

 - Suppression of dissidents during the July Days

     - The July Days started spontaneously when soldiers and workers protested against the Provisional Government due to the failed Kerensky office and dissatisfaction against poor government administration. The Provisional Government then suppressed dissidents in the July Days, which further damaged the Provisional Government's credibility.  

 - Actions during the Kornilov Affair

     - The Kornilov Affair further revealed the incompetence of the Provisional Government when Kornilov brought his troops to Petrograd to restore order. The Provisional Government did not have enough troops to defend Petrograd so they asked the Bolsheviks for assistance and armed them. This shows that the Provisional Government was incapable of defending any threat, while the Bolsheviks gained more popularity as they were seen as heroes defending the revolution. 

 - Limited power during the Dual Power (Dvoyevlastiye) period

     - The Provisional Government was unable to resolve many socio-economic problems in Russia due to the dual-power structure where the Petrograd Soviet was more dominant and had the ability to enforce policies. 

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Provisional Government in Russia did not withdraw from the First World War, and the failure of the Kerensky Offensive in July 1917 was unpopular among the masses, especially the military ($\checkmark$). The Kerensky government hoped to use the campaign to restore national unity and pride. However, the offensive failed since the Germans and Austrians were able to advance further into Russian territory and deliver more damage to the Russian forces ($\checkmark$).

Moreover, the Provisional Government was incompetent, which was exemplified in the Kornilov Affair when Kornilov led his troops into Petrograd to restore order ($\checkmark$). The Provisional Government did not have enough troops to defend Petrograd, so they asked the Bolsheviks for assistance and armed them. This shows that the Provisional Government was incapable of defending any threat ($\checkmark$).
