# HI0282

Identify **two** reasons for the formation of the Grupo de Oficiales Unidos (GOU) in Argentina in 1943.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following: 

 - To remove the existing corrupt government led by Ramón Castillo

 - To challenge foreign economic dominance and promote Argentina's sovereignty

 - To address concerns about military vulnerability due to Brazil's growing power

 - To end communist influence in the labor movement

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The GOU was formed in 1943 to tackle two major issues. First, there was a growing concern over Argentina's military weakness, especially in comparison to Brazil's rising power ($\checkmark$). Second, there was a need to end the widespread political corruption that had dominated the country during the previous Infamous Decade ($\checkmark$).
