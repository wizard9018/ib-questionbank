# HI1149

Describe the aims of Castro's $\lq$Proclamation against discrimination' in 1959.
[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Include **both** of the following points:
- A policy aimed at reducing inequality between racial groups in Cuba.
- A policy to highlight the success of his new socialist state, where all races were included in his reforms.
\
[1 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Castro's $\lq$Proclamation against discrimination' in 1959 was aimed at reducing the social and economic gaps between racial groups. Because inequality contradicted his vision of a successful socialist state, he ensured that all races were included in his reforms. The inclusion of minorities also helped limit opposition to his rule ($\checkmark$).
