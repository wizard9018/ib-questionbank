# HI0240

Describe the features of Stalin's first 5-year plan (1928-1932). 

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Increased production of coal and iron.

 - There were major infrastructure projects completed, such as the White Sea-Baltic Canal and the Dnieprostroi Dam.

 - Major industrial areas were constructed, such as the Magnitogorsk Industrial city. 

 - Agricultural collectivisation was implemented to centralise the control of land resources and food production in order to facilitate industrial growth.

 - Forced labor stationed in gulags were used widely as extra manpower to help complete the projects in the 1st 5-year plan. 

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The USSR was able to rapidly industrialize its economy under the 1st 5-year plan. This included the increased production of coal and iron ($\checkmark$). There were also major construction projects involved, such as the White Sea-Baltic Canal and the Dnieprostroi Dam  ($\checkmark$). Lastly, major industrial areas such as Magitorgosk were built to facilitate more production of iron and steel ($\checkmark$).
