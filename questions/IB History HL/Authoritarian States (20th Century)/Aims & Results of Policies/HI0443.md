# HI0443

Explain two causes for the failure of War Communism.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Decline in industrial production

     - Industrial production fell by 20$\%$ from 1913 levels since the civil war brought damage to key industrial areas and conscription of men from both sides meant that there were fewer workers.

     - The Bolsheviks nationalised factories after they took power, but the management of the factories were inefficient, which led to poor productivity.

     - Moreover, due to war communism, most of the resources were diverted to support the military and military supplies were produced instead. Hence production of consumer goods declined and the availability of raw materials declined as a result.

 - Decline in agricultural production

     - The forced grain requisition discouraged peasants to produce food since they would be confiscated, which led to a drastic decline in agricultural production. For example, grain production fell by 40 million tons in 1920. 

     - The sharp decline in agricultural production also led to a massive famine that killed up to 5 million people across the country.

 - Urban Depopulation:

     - The civil war led to massive urban depopulation since the war greatly affected the urban areas and nearby countryside.  For example, Petrograd lost 70$\%$ of its population while Moscow lost 50% of its population. 

 - Mass Resistance 

     - The Kronstadt Rebellion erupted because sailors and soldiers at the Kronstadt naval base disagreed with war communism. Soldiers were dissatisfied since they received fewer food supplies under War Communism. Also, the Bolsheviks suppressed any dissidents during this time period, which further angered the soldiers that once supported the Bolsheviks. 

     - The forced grain requisition measures further alienated the peasants in the countryside, which led to many uprisings in the countryside such as the Tambov uprising. 

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

War Communism was a failure mainly due to the two following factors. Firstly, War Communism led to a decline in agricultural production ($\checkmark$). The forced grain acquisition measures used by the Bolsheviks only discouraged peasants to produce food since they would be confiscated. For example, grain production fell by 40 million tons in 1920 ($\checkmark$).

Secondly, War Communism also led to more mass resistance ($\checkmark$). One of the most notable resistant movements was the Kronstadt rebellion when soldiers at the Kronstadt naval base rebelled against the Bolsheviks due to their disagreement with the economic policies ($\checkmark$). The increase in mass resistance only showed that War Communism was becoming more unpopular over time.
