# HI0498

Describe **three** impacts of Castro's agrarian reforms in Cuba.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Reforms led to the rise of State Farms and Cooperatives: As the state centralized agriculture, Cuba saw the decline of individual smallholders
- Short-term increase in agricultural production: After the agrarian reforms, Cuba saw a rise in the production of sugarcane
- Increase in social justice and welfare: more people gained access to land due to land distribution reforms, leading to a reduction in rural poverty and an improvement in the standard of living.
- Long-term economic challenges: centralisation and strict state control led to inefficiencies and shortages of certain foods, as the working populace had few incentives to be productive.
- Economic isolation, caused by the removal of US influence from businesses, also led to supply problems.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The impact of Castro's agrarian reforms was that, as the state centralised agriculture, Cuba saw the decline of individual smallholders and instead a rise in State farms and Cooperatives ($\checkmark$). In the long term, this led to economic challenges, as strict state control resulted in inefficiencies and production shortages in certain areas ($\checkmark$).  The move to nationalism and the removal of US influences in Cuba also led to supply problems for certain goods and economic isolation for Cuba ($\checkmark$).
