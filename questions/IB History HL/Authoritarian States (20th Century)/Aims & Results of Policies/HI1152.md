# HI1152

What does Source B suggest about the benefits Cuba received from its relationship with the United States?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** | $\hspace{3em}$ | An excerpt from the article looking at the economic relationship between the United States and Cuba prior to the Cuban Revolution. |

[Source: Thomas, H. (1978, November).*The U.S. And Castro, 1959-1962.* American Heritage. Retrieved October 28, 2025 from https:www.americanheritage.com/us-and-castro-1959-1962. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Cuba gained freedom from Spain sooner due to American intervention in 1898
- The Cuban standard of living rose to half that of the United States by 1925
- Yellow fever was conquered through work of U.S. military doctors
- A railway was built along the length of the island by a U.S. company
- Upper-class Cuban children could be educated in the United States
- American investments transformed the Cuban economy
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to Source B, American investments transformed the Cuban economy. Cuba benefited from a rise in the standard of living to half that of the United States by 1925 ($\checkmark$). A second benefit was to the infrastructure of Cuba, as a US company built a railway along the length of the island ($\checkmark$). Finall,y the links between the two countries meant that the children of upper-class Cubans could be educated in the US ($\checkmark$).
