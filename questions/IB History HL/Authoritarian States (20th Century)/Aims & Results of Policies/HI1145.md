# HI1145

Explain why healthcare policies were a priority for Castro's government.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Supported policies of social justice and addressed inequality: Good healthcare and knowledge of basic hygiene were important to improve health issues in poorer/rural communities that previously had no access to healthcare.
- Comprehensive health care was made a part of every citizen's constitutional rights in 1979, so the government was expected to provide free health services, including preventative medical care.
- Improving public health: They were committed from the beginning to helping improve people's living standards, including vaccination campaigns and controlling infectious diseases.
- Political reasons: Delivering high-quality healthcare demonstrated the benefits of socialism and helped to maintain popular support.
- A healthy population was seen as essential for Cuba's economic development goals.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Castro's government was focused on removing social injustices and making healthcare accessible and free for all Cubans as a way of addressing some of the social inequalities in society ($\checkmark$). A healthy population was seen as essential for Cuba's economic development goals, as economic reforms and policies placed high importance on the rural workforce to aid Cuba's economic recovery ($\checkmark$). Politically, it was also a means of ensuring popular support as success in health care was equated with the success of Cuba's socialist system ($\checkmark$).
