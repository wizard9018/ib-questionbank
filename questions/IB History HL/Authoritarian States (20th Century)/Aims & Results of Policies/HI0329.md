# HI0329

Describe **one** of Fidel Castro's motivations to align Cuba with the Soviet Union.
[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **one** of the following:
- Strategic motivation: To protect Cuba against retaliation from the US after Castro nationalised foreign industries.
- Economic motivation: To provide Cuba with economic support after the US embargo on Cuban sugar, as sugar was Cuba's largest export.
- Ideological motivation: embrace of Marxist-Leninist principles, especially socialism and anti-imperialism.
\
[1 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Castro chose to align with the Soviet Union because he needed economic support. The nationalising of foreign industries has resulted in the US imposing an embargo on Cuban sugar which was Cuba's largest export commodity ($\checkmark$).
