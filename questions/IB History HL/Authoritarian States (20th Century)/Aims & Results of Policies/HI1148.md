# HI1148

Describe **two** aims of Fidel Castro's policies toward racial minorities in Cuba after 1959.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Aimed to reduce the social and economic gaps between racial groups
- Castro aimed to extend his Universal free education policy to include all minorities. Increased literacy helped people gain employment.
- He aimed to outlaw Racial discrimination in public places, education, and employment, promoting equal access to jobs and training for all races.
- He aimed to improve healthcare in marginalized communities.
- Aimed to promote equality in social programs, including housing and employment benefits for previously excluded groups.
- He aimed to increase the number of $\lq$non-whites' in positions of power, including government jobs.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The situation for racial minorities improved under Castro as he aimed for social equality and economic parity in Cuba. His educational policies ensured that minority groups had access to education, which helped increase literacy rates and provide people with more job opportunities ($\checkmark$). Jobs that had previously been deemed inaccessible to minority groups were now open, including jobs in the government and positions of power. This gave minority groups more political power and increased their awareness of their economic situation ($\checkmark$).
