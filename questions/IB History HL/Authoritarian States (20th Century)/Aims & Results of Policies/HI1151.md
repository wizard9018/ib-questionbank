# HI1151

According to Source B, how did the United States economically dominate Cuba?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B**  | $\hspace{3em}$ | An excerpt from the article looking at the economic relationship between the United States and Cuba prior to the Cuban Revolution. |

[Source: Thomas, H. (1978, November).*The U.S. And Castro, 1959-1962.* American Heritage. Retrieved October 28, 2025 from https:www.americanheritage.com/us-and-castro-1959-1962. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- 60% of Cuban sugar production was controlled by the US
- American companies controlled public utilities such as electricity and telephones
- U.S. companies had large landholdings in Cuba
- Cuban tobacco was marketed through U.S. merchants
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to Source B, the United States economically dominated Cuba as it controlled 60% of its sugar production  ($\checkmark$). Furthermore, American companies also controlled many of Cuba's public utilities, such as the electricity and phone companies ($\checkmark$). Finally, US companies also had large landholdings in Cuba, which gave them access to resources and greater economic control ($\checkmark$).
