# HI0445

What does Source B suggest about Stalin's view on the future of agriculture in the USSR?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B**  $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Speech by Joseph Stalin titled “Industrialisation of the country and the Right Deviation in the CPSU.” Published in November 19, 1928. |

[Source: Stalin, J (1928, November). *Industrialisation of the country and the Right Deviation in the C.P.S.U.(B.).* Marxists Internet Archive. Retrieved March 16, 2025 from http://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/stalin/works/1928/11/19.htm. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- The Soviet Union cannot exist on two foundations, with one foundation in modern large-scale industry and the other on backward agriculture.
- The Soviet Union would have to gradually and systematically transform agriculture to a scale equivalent to large-scale industries.
- The transformation of agriculture would ensure the victory of the Soviet Union.
- Stalin warned of the return of capitalism if the transformation of agriculture failed.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source B suggests that the existing problem of the Soviet Union is that it cannot exist on two foundations, with one foundation in modern large-scale industries and another foundations on small-scale backwards agricultural production for a long time ($\checkmark$). Stalin also stated that the Soviet union would have to gradually and systematically transform the country's agriculture on a new technical basis to reach the level of socialist industry  ($\checkmark$). Lastly, Stalin warned that capitalism would return if the transformation of agriculture fails  ($\checkmark$).
