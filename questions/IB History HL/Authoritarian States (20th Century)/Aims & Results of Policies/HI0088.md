# HI0088

Explain the impacts of **one** educational reform implemented in Cuba by Fidel Castro's government
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **one** of the following ideas with supporting details:

Literacy Campaign
- Mobilised volunteers to teach literacy in rural areas
- Literacy rose to over 96%, but education remained under state control which limited intellectual freedom.

Education Reform
- Broadened education access for many Cubans
- State curricula promoted socialism through propaganda which limited critical thinking
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One of Castro's most significant social reforms was the Literacy Campaign of 1961, which aimed to eliminate illiteracy by mobilising students and volunteers to teach in rural areas ($\checkmark$). The campaign was highly successful, raising literacy rates to over 96%. ($\checkmark$) However, education became state-controlled, promoting socialist ideology and limiting intellectual freedom. ($\checkmark$)
