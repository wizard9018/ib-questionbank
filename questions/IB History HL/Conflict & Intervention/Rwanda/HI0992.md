# HI0992

With reference to the origin, purpose and content, assess the value and limitations of Source B for a historian studying the international response to the Rwandan genocide
[4 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |A confidential telegram sent on 15 April 1994 by the US Department of State to its mission at the United Nations in New York. The telegram provided official guidance on the immediate withdrawal of UNAMIR personnel from Rwanda during the early days of the genocide. |

[Source: U.S. Department of State. (1994, April 15). *Talking Points for UNAMIR Withdrawal.* National Security Archive. Retrieved July 24, 2025, from https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu//NSAEBB/NSAEBB53/index.html. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
A combination of the ideas below that meets the following guidelines:
- Both values and limitations must be addressed. If only one is addressed, award a maximum of **two** marks.
- Origin, purpose and content must all be referenced at some point in the response (either as a value, limitation or both) to receive the full **four** marks.

For values, any combination of the following:
- The origin of the source is a confidential cable from the US Department of State and it offers a firsthand and contemporary view of American foreign policy during the genocide, revealing official attitudes at a critical moment.
- The purpose is to inform and instruct US diplomats at the United Nations and it provides insight into the diplomatic language used to justify peacekeeping withdrawal, which is valuable for understanding political decision-making.
- The content reveals the United States' firm opposition to maintaining UNAMIR and its prioritisation of troop safety, illustrating how national interest influenced international policy.

For limitations, any combination of the following:
- The origin of the source is US government communication and it reflects an official narrative and is unlikely to include criticism of US policy or acknowledgment of misjudgements.
- The purpose to justify troop withdrawal makes the source one-sided, as it seeks to legitimise a controversial stance and avoids deeper moral or legal accountability.
- The content omits any reference to the genocide itself, including the systematic targeting of Tutsi civilians, and avoids use of the word “genocide,” which significantly limits historical understanding.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The origin of the source, a confidential cable from the US Department of State written in April 1994, adds value as it provides a real-time reflection of the views and strategic thinking of a major global actor during the Rwandan genocide ($\checkmark\, \text{VO}$). The source's content illustrates how US national interests influenced its foreign policy decisions in the case of the genocide ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$).

However, the origin also limits the usefulness of the source, as it reflects the official position of the US government and is unlikely to include any criticism of American foreign policy or its failure to act ($\checkmark\, \text{LO}$). The purpose of the source is limited as it seeks to legitimise the actions of its allies rather than viewing their actions more objectively ($\checkmark\, \text{LP}$).
