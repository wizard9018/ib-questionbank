# HI0500

Identify **two** immediate outcomes of the assassination of President Habyarimana in 1994.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

The following: 

 - The assassination triggered the genocide in Rwanda, with mass killings starting almost immediately in Kigali. Roadblocks were set up, and Hutu militias began targeting Tutsi civilians and moderate Hutu within hours of the crash.

 - The interim government accused foreign powers, including Belgium and France, of involvement in the plane crash, causing diplomatic tension. 

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The assassination of President Habyarimana led directly to the outbreak of the Rwandan genocide, with violence erupting in Kigali almost immediately. Hutu militias quickly established checkpoints and began targeting Tutsi individuals and moderate Hutu ($\checkmark$

). Additionally, the transitional government accused external powers like Belgium and France of involvement, causing diplomatic tension ($\checkmark$

).
