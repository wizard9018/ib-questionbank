# HI0507

Identify **two** reasons Hutu extremists opposed the Arusha Accords.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The agreement allowed the return of Tutsi refugees, which extremists opposed because they feared it would shift the political balance or, in rural areas in particular, that Tutsi might reclaim land and resources previously low.
- They promoted a multiparty system that threatened the dominance of the Mouvement Révolutionnaire National pour le Développement (MRND).
- Some feared that integrating RPF forces would weaken Hutu control over the military.
- Propaganda portrayed the Accords as a foreign-imposed solution favouring the RPF and that President Juvénal Habyraimana had betrayed Hutu interests by signing them.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Hutu extremists opposed the Accords because they threatened Hutu political control both in their promotion of a multiparty system ($\checkmark$) and because they allowed Tutsi refugees to return, which they feared would lead to a shift in the economic and social balance of power ($\checkmark$).
