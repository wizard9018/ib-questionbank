# HI0506

Identify **three** ways the First Republic in Rwanda (1963-1973) discriminated against the Tutsis.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- The government restructured political power to ensure Hutu dominance, excluding Tutsis from leadership roles.
- Tutsis were discriminated against in employment, being denied access to key positions in government and other sectors.
- Ethnic violence targeted Tutsis, particularly in response to attacks from Inyenzi fighters (clarified from $\text{\textquotedblleft}$refugees"), leading to massacres, including violence in 1963–1964 following attacks from Burundi.
- Anti-Tutsi propaganda promoted Hutu superiority and justified their exclusion from society.
- The government did not intervene effectively to stop violence against the Tutsi, allowing massacres to take place.
- Education policies favoured Hutu students, limiting access to education for the Tutsi through ethnic quotas.
- The government redistributed land from Tutsi landowners to Hutu citizens, further economically marginalizing the Tutsi population.
- Tutsi refugees who fled violence were denied the right to return, effectively exiling them and preventing them from reclaiming property or rights.
- The Parmehutu (Party of the Hutu Emancipation Movement) played a significant role in reinforcing these discriminatory policies, advocating for Hutu supremacy in politics and society.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The First Republic disadvantaged the Tutsi in multiple ways. Firstly, the government restructured political institutions to ensure Hutu dominance, preventing Tutsis from holding significant positions ($\checkmark$). Secondly, Tutsis faced discrimination in employment, as many were excluded from government jobs and key professions ($\checkmark$). Moreover, violence against the Tutsi increased, particularly following attacks by Inyenzi fighters, leading to massacres that were either ignored or not effectively addressed by the authorities ($\checkmark$).
