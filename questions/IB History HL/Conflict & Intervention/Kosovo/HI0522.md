# HI0522

Describe the significance of the United Nations Interim Administration Mission in Kosovo (UNMIK) after the conflict ended in 1999.  [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

- Established Interim Governance - UNMIK took over the administration of Kosovo after Serbian withdrawal, providing an interim government that maintained law and order during the transitional period.

- Laid the Foundations for Self-Governance and Democracy - It helped to build democratic institutions, including establishing provisional governing bodies and organizing elections, which paved the way for Kosovo's future independence.

- Maintained Security and Stability - Alongside NATO's Kosovo Force (KFOR), UNMIK was responsible for maintaining peace and security, preventing renewed violence between ethnic groups.

- Protected Human Rights and Promoted Reconciliation - UNMIK was tasked with protecting minority rights, promoting reconciliation between Serbs and Albanians, and ensuring the rule of law in a deeply divided society.

- Facilitated Kosovo's Transition Toward Independence - UNMIK oversaw Kosovo's transition from a war-torn region under Serbian control to a territory with functioning self-governing institutions, ultimately leading to Kosovo's unilateral declaration of independence in 2008.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

UNMIK was significant because it established interim governance in Kosovo after the withdrawal of Serbian forces, providing stability and ensuring that essential administrative functions, such as law enforcement and civil services, continued during a highly unstable period ($\checkmark$). It also played a central role in building the framework for democratic self-governance by creating provisional institutions and organizing free elections, which were crucial steps toward Kosovo's political development and eventual declaration of independence ($\checkmark$). Furthermore, UNMIK worked to protect human rights and promote reconciliation between ethnic Albanians and Serbs by supporting legal reforms, rebuilding trust in public institutions, and addressing minority rights concerns in the post-conflict environment ($\checkmark$).
