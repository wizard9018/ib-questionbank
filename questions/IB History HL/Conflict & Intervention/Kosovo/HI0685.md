## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- After the bombings, there was nothing left for NATO to protect, though Kosovo was now autonomous.
- The bombings had a devastating impact on Kosovo.
- The area was left without a population.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source R suggests that, though the NATO bombings helped Kosovo defend its “autonomy”, as shown by the caption “An autonomous Kosovo protected by NATO troops” ($\checkmark$), the area was devastated by the bombings, shown by the troops standing in an empty area of burning rubble, so there was not much left to defend ($\checkmark$).
