# HI0520

Compare and contrast the roles of Slobodan Milošević and Ibrahim Rugova in the Kosovo conflict.

[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\

|:--|:--|

|Includes clear points of comparison (similarity)$\hspace{2em}$|($\checkmark$ similarity)|

|Includes clear points of contrast (difference)|($\checkmark$ difference)|

|Provides evidence from the text (SBQ) or own $\newline$ knowledge (SAQ) to validate arguments|($\checkmark$ evidence)|

For comparison, any of the following:

- Political Leadership - Both Milošević and Rugova were prominent political leaders during the Kosovo conflict, representing their respective ethnic groups.

- Influence Over Their Populations - Both exercised significant influence over their respective communities—Milošević over the Serbs and Rugova over Kosovar Albanians.

- Central Figures in the Conflict's Narrative - Both became symbolic leaders in the Kosovo conflict and were central to the way the conflict was perceived domestically and internationally.

- Long-Term Impact on Kosovo's Future - Both played significant roles in shaping the political outcomes of the Kosovo conflict, through war (Milošević) and post-war leadership (Rugova).

- Use of Nationalist Rhetoric - Both leaders appealed to national identity to gain support—Milošević promoted Serbian nationalism; Rugova championed Kosovar Albanian nationalism through calls for independence.

For contrast, any of the following:

- Approach to Achieving Political Goals - Milošević relied on military force, repression, and ethnic cleansing to maintain control over Kosovo. Rugova consistently advocated for nonviolent resistance and diplomatic solutions.

- Relationship with the Kosovo Liberation Army (KLA) - Milošević viewed the KLA as a terrorist group and sought to eliminate them through military means. Rugova initially distanced himself from the KLA's armed struggle, favoring peaceful negotiation, though later he recognized their role in the independence movement.

- Political Ideology - Milošević promoted authoritarianism and Serbian nationalist dominance, while Rugova emphasized democracy, self-determination, and peaceful coexistence.

- Impact on the Escalation or De-escalation of Conflict - Milošević's policies escalated the conflict through repression and war crimes. Rugova aimed to de-escalate tensions by promoting peaceful resistance.

- International Perception and Legitimacy - Milošević was widely condemned by the international community and eventually indicted for war crimes. Rugova was respected internationally for his commitment to peaceful solutions and was recognized as Kosovo's legitimate leader post-conflict.

- Outcomes After the Conflict - Milošević lost power in Serbia and was put on trial for war crimes by the ICTY. Rugova was elected Kosovo's president after the conflict and helped guide the territory toward statehood.

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Both Milošević and Rugova were central figures in the Kosovo conflict, each serving as the political leader of their respective ethnic groups ($\checkmark$ similarity). Milošević led Serbia, promoting Serbian nationalism, while Rugova headed the Democratic League of Kosovo (LDK), advocating for Albanian autonomy ($\checkmark$ evidence). Both leaders used national identity to unify their populations ($\checkmark$ similarity). Milošević invoked Serbia's historic and cultural claims to Kosovo, while Rugova promoted Kosovar Albanian identity through civil society and symbolic state-building ($\checkmark$ evidence). Both men played significant roles in shaping the trajectory of the conflict ($\checkmark$ similarity). Milošević's policies escalated tensions and drew NATO's attention, while Rugova's peaceful resistance gained international sympathy and diplomatic support ($\checkmark$ evidence).

Both leaders had a different approach to achieving political goals ($\checkmark$ difference). Milošević relied on military force, repression, and ethnic cleansing to maintain control over Kosovo. Rugova consistently advocated for nonviolent resistance and diplomatic solutions ($\checkmark$ evidence) Their relationships with the Kosovo Liberation Army (KLA) also differed ($\checkmark$ difference). Milošević targeted the KLA as a terrorist group and used violence to suppress it, while Rugova distanced himself from its armed tactics but later acknowledged its role in Kosovo's struggle ($\checkmark$ evidence) Their international reputations and outcomes also contrasted ($\checkmark$ difference). Milošević was indicted by the International Criminal Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia (ICTY), while Rugova was internationally recognized for his peaceful leadership and elected as Kosovo's first post-war president ($\checkmark$ evidence).
