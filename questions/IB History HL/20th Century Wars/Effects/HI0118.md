# HI0118

Explain **one** similarity between the political impact of WWI and WWII.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **one** of the following ideas with its corresponding evidence.
- The creation of international organisations for peace keeping: the League of Nations (1920) and the United Nations (1945)
- The end of an authoritarian system of government for some of the countries of Europe: Monarchy in Germany, Russia and the Austro-Hungarian Empire after WWI and Fascism and Nazism in Italy and Germany after WWII.
- The strengthening of Communism with the establishment of the first Communist State in the former Russian Empire after WWI and the establishment of Communist regimes in many countries of Europe after WWII (Poland, Czechoslovakia, Romania, Hungary, German Democratic Republic, Yugoslavia, Albania, Bulgaria)
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Both WWI and WWII had important political impacts. One of them was the realisation that a conflict of such magnitude should be avoided. To achieve this international organisations were created with the objective of solving conflicts peacefully with the principle of collective security ($\checkmark$). The League of Nations was created after WWI as stipulated in the Treaty of Versailles while the United Nations was created in 1945 a few months before the surrender of Japan ($\checkmark$).
