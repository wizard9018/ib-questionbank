# HI0742

Explain **three** ways World War One contributed to the collapse of political systems in Europe.

[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following with supporting evidence/explanation:

 - Military defeat of Central Powers. 

     - The defeat of Germany, Austria-Hungary, and the Ottoman Empire directly led to the fall of their monarchies. E.g. The abdication of Kaiser Wilhelm II in 1918 ended the German Empire and led to the Weimar Republic.

 - Economic collapse and hardship. 

     - Massive wartime spending caused inflation, food shortages, and unemployment, undermining public support for governments. In Russia, wartime food shortages and industrial breakdown caused mass unrest in urban centres like Petrograd. This economic crisis was a key factor behind the February Revolution of 1917, which forced Tsar Nicholas II to abdicate and ended centuries of Romanov rule.

 - Revolutionary movements. 

     - Discontent sparked socialist and communist revolutions, particularly in states that suffered heavy losses. In 1917, the Russian Revolution overthrew the Tsarist regime and eventually led to the rise of the Bolsheviks.

 - Ethnic nationalism and the breakup of empires. 

     - The Austro-Hungarian and Ottoman Empires disintegrated as ethnic groups demanded self-determination. The Austro-Hungarian Empire collapsed into separate nation-states like Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia, and Hungary.

 - Weakening of monarchies. 

     - Monarchies were blamed for the war and its devastating effects, leading to their collapse or reduction in power. The collapse of the Romanov dynasty in Russia marked the end of centuries of imperial rule.

 - Spread of democratic ideas and republicanism. 

     - War weariness and disillusionment with autocracy fostered support for parliamentary systems. The German Empire transitioned into the Weimar Republic, a democratic state, after 1918.

 - Failure of postwar settlements to ensure stability. 

     - The Treaty of Versailles and other peace treaties created resentment and a political vacuum rather than stability. Hungary lost two-thirds of its territory under the Treaty of Trianon, fueling political instability and irredentism.

\

[6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

World War One played a central role in the collapse of several long-standing monarchies ($\checkmark$). The military defeat of the Central Powers, which included Germany, Austria-Hungary, and the Ottoman Empire, directly triggered the downfall of their monarchies. In Austria, the empire's defeat led to the collapse of the Habsburg monarchy in 1918 and the disintegration of Austria-Hungary into a series of independent nation-states. This marked the end of centuries of imperial rule under the Habsburgs and the creation of the Austrian Republic ($\checkmark$).

Economic collapse further undermined political stability. The massive financial burden of prolonged warfare caused inflation, widespread unemployment, and food shortages, weakening public confidence in existing regimes ($\checkmark$). In Russia, these pressures sparked major unrest in cities like Petrograd, where shortages and industrial breakdown during the war played a major role in the February Revolution of 1917. This forced Tsar Nicholas II to abdicate and ended the Romanov dynasty, leading to a period of revolution and unrest ($\checkmark$).

Finally, World War One supercharged ethnic nationalism, particularly within the Austro-Hungarian and Ottoman Empires. As the war drew to a close, numerous ethnic groups within these multi-ethnic empires seized the opportunity to demand independence ($\checkmark$). This resulted in the break-up of Austria-Hungary into new states such as Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia, and Hungary, as well as the dismantling of the Ottoman Empire, further contributing to the collapse of imperial political systems in Europe ($\checkmark$).
