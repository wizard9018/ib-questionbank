# HI0120

Identify **two** territorial changes brought about by WWII.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Division of Germany between France, England, the USSR and the US which would lead to a long term division into the Federal Republic of Germany and the German Democratic Republic.
- Division of Korea into one controlled by the USSR and one controlled by the US which would lead to a long term division into the Democratic People's Republic of Korea and the Republic of Korea.
- US occupation of all of Japan until 1954 and of Okinawa until 1972.
- Redrawing of the Eastern frontiers of Germany.
- Redrawing of the frontiers of Poland and the USSR.
- USSR annexes the Southern Sakhalin and Kuril Islands from Japan
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Some territorial changes after WWII were the occupation of Germany by France, England, the USSR and the US which would lead to a long term division into the Federal Republic of Germany and the German Democratic Republic ($\checkmark$) and the fact that Germany lost territory to Poland while Poland ceded territory to the USSR  ($\checkmark$).
