# HI0611

Identify **two** ways that World War I impacted civilian life in Great Britain.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Women began to work in the positions that had previously been held by men.

 - The government imposed rations on the country in order to maintain food supplies.

 - Agriculture suffered, leading to price increases.

 - Conscription made it mandatory for men to serve in the armed forces.

Propaganda was used to encourage patriotism.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

World War I greatly impacted British civilians. In 1916, the government made conscription in the military mandatory for all men, causing a notable drop in the workforce ($\checkmark$). As a result, women were expected to work in the factories and farms in jobs that had previously been considered men's work ($\checkmark$).
