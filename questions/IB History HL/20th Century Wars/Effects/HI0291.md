# HI0291

Explain **two** economic impacts of WWI on Germany. 

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following with its corresponding evidence:

 - The War hurt Germany's trade. 

     - Germany imported about 30 percent of its food before the war but Britain's naval blockade meant that by  December 1915, Germany imported half of what it imported in 1913. During the war it lost most of its merchant ships and this hurt its post war international trade

 - The War hurt the German government's finances. 

     - Germany spent \$19.9 billion on the war effort. This caused a huge government debt. The German government's internal debt was the same as the German total national income. The Weimar Republic chose to print money to pay for the domestic debts it had incurred to pay for the war. In part, this led to the hyperinflation of 1923.

 - The Treaty of Versailles' clauses required Germany to lose areas with key resources.

     - Alsace-Lorraine and Upper Silesia were important industrial areas; the Saarland`s rich coal production was to go to France for 15 years. 

 - The Treaty of Versailles required Germany to pay reparations of £6.600 billion.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

WWI had adverse effects on the German government's finances ($\checkmark$). The government was left bankrupt as it had spent \$19.9 billion on the war effort, resulting in a domestic debt so big that it was equivalent to the nation's total income ($\checkmark$).

The economic consequences of the Treaty of Versailles further weakened the German economy ($\checkmark$). The Treaty called for key industrial areas to be transferred to other countries. For example, Alsace-Lorraine was returned to France and Upper Silesia was given to the newly created state of Czechoslovakia ($\checkmark$).
