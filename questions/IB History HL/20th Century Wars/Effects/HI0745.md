# HI0745

Outline the political repercussions of WWII for **three** countries.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Germany. Defeat led to occupation by Allied powers and eventual division. West Germany became a capitalist democracy (FRG), while East Germany became a Soviet-aligned socialist state (GDR), cementing Cold War divisions.

 - Soviet Union. Victory in WWII legitimized Stalin's regime and enabled Soviet domination of Eastern Europe, expanding communist ideology and creating the Eastern Bloc. The war increased the USSR's global influence.

 - United States. WWII marked the U.S.'s transition from isolationism to active global leadership, founding the UN and NATO. Domestically, the presidency gained power over foreign and defense policy.

 - Japan.The U.S. abolished Japan's military regime and imposed a pacifist constitution in 1947, ending imperial rule and initiating democratic government and economic recovery.

 - France. The Vichy regime's collaboration with Nazis discredited existing institutions. After liberation, Charles de Gaulle's leadership helped rebuild a new democratic structure (the Fourth Republic), though instability led to the Fifth Republic in 1958.

 - United Kingdom. The cost of war and global realignment sped up the process of decolonization. The Labour Party's victory in 1945 signaled public support for post-war reconstruction and led to the creation of the NHS and other social reforms signalling a political shift toward welfare state policies.

 - China. Resumption of civil war, leading to communist victory in 1949. The war with Japan weakened the Nationalist government and allowed Mao's communist forces to gain support and ultimately establish the People's Republic of China.

 - Italy. Mussolini's defeat and civil war between fascists and anti-fascists led to the fall of Fascism, the abolition of the monarchy and the creation of a democratic republic following a 1946 referendum.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

For the Soviet Union, victory significantly strengthened Stalin's political authority and lifted the USSR's status globally. The Soviet Union emerged as a superpower, using its military presence in Eastern Europe to establish pro-Soviet communist regimes. This led to the formation of the Eastern Bloc and expanded the reach of communist ideology, laying the groundwork for the Cold War. ($\checkmark$)

The United States shifted from a policy of isolationism to a central role in global affairs. It took the lead in establishing key international institutions such as the United Nations and the NATO. Domestically, the war expanded presidential powers, particularly in areas of defense and foreign policy, setting a precedent for future executive leadership in international matters. ($\checkmark$)

Following its defeat, Japan underwent major political change under American occupation. The US dismantled Japan's militarist government and introduced a new pacifist constitution in 1947, which renounced war and banned the maintenance of armed forces for offensive purposes. The emperor's role became purely symbolic, and Japan was rebuilt as a parliamentary democracy, which helped bring political stability and economic recovery after the war. ($\checkmark$)
