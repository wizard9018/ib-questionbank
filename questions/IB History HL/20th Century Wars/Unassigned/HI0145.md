# HI0145

Describe the advantages Germany gained by its involvement in the Spanish Civil War in 1936. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of **two** of the following:

 - Hitler used his involvement in the Spanish Civil war to test out new and improved armed forces.'

 - The fighting tactic of Blitzkrieg or lightning war was first tested in the Spanish Civil War. 

 - The Condor Legion of the Luftwaffe (German air force) tested out the effectiveness of civilian aerial bombing at Guernica. 

 - By helping Franco come to power, Germany could benefit from the presence of another right wing dictator in Europe.

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

German involvement in the Spanish Civil War provided Hitler with the advantage of testing out his new armed forces including Blitzkrieg tactics or $\text{\textquotedblleft}$lightning war" which was first tested during the fighting in the Spanish Civil War ($\checkmark$). The Condor Legion of the Luftwaffe (German air force) also tested the effectiveness of civilian aerial bombing at the infamous bombing of Guernica before its eventual use during the Second World War ($\checkmark$).
