## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Maintenance of foreign affairs, except with Russia

 - The elimination of the popular vote

 - To establish a new government based on totalitarian concepts

 - A revision of the tax system

 - Respect for the Catholic Church

 - Independence of the peasant

 - That labor would be guaranteed under his leadership

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

According to the US Consul to Seville, Franco was looking to establish a government based on totalitarian principles ($\checkmark$). As such, he wanted to eliminate the popular vote, saying that this would better serve the population ($\checkmark$). Also, Franco wanted to revise the tax system ($\checkmark$).
