# HI0533

What, according to Source B, were the reasons for the Republican defeat in the Spanish Civil War?

[3 marks]

$\\$

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source B**  $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Excerpt written by Leon Trotsky in 1939 about the results of the Spanish Civil War.

[Source: Trotsky, L. (1939, January). *The Tragedy of Spain.* Originally published in the “Russian Bulletin of the Opposition”. Retrieved February 21, 2025  from https://marxists.org/archive/trotsky/1939/01/spain.htm. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Franco had a clear plan throughout the war that the Spanish bourgeoisie could support. 

     - Evidence: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Franco's great superiority lies in this, that he has a clear and definite program: to safeguard and stabilize capitalist property, the rule of the exploiters, and the domination of the church; and to restore the monarchy.”

 - The Popular Front (Republicans) betrayed their own messaging, leading to distrust from the workers and peasants. 

     - Evidence: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$But the honorable Republicans did everything in their power to trample, to besmirch, or simply to drown in blood the cherished hopes of the oppressed masses. As a result, we have witnessed during the last two years the growing distrust and hatred of the Republican cliques on the part of the peasants and workers.”

 - The Republicans and their supporters became indifferent to the actions of their leadership. 

     - Evidence: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Despair or dull indifference gradually replaced revolutionary enthusiasm and the spirit of self - sacrifice. The masses turned their backs on those who had deceived and trampled upon them. That is the primary reason for the defeat of the Republican troops.”

 - By trusting Stalin and embracing capitalism, the Republicans betrayed their messaging and lost the support of the people. 

     - Evidence: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Under the label of the Popular Front, they set up a joint stock company. Under the leadership of Stalin, they have assured the most terrible defeat when all the conditions for victory were at hand.”

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

According to Leon Trotsky, the Republicans were defeated for betraying their own messaging, for losing the support of the people and because the Nationalists had clearer goals and messaging. The excerpt states that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$dull indifference gradually replaced revolutionary enthusiasm” among the Republicans and that this is because of their frustration with their leadership  ($\checkmark$). The leadership betrayed their own messaging by embracing capitalism and setting up “a joint-stock company.” This caused the peasants and workers to distrust the Republicans ($\checkmark$. In addition, Franco and the Nationalists remained clear and steady in their program. This led to capitalists supporting them as well as them getting the support of strong capitalist nations ($\checkmark$).
