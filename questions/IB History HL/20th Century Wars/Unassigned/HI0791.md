# HI0791

Using evidence from Sources A and B, describe **two** negative effects of imperialism in Asia and Africa.

[4 marks]

$\\$

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following with supporting evidence from the sources:

 - Source A shows that the colonists in the Congo were being mistreated by Belgium. This is an example of how the colonizing countries would sometimes exploit the colonists.

     - Evidence: The colonists look frightened with two running away in fear and one wrapped up in the snake's coils.

 - Source A refers to the importance of rubber. This implies that Belgium was using the Congo for their resources.

     - Evidence: The title mentions $\text{\textquotedblleft}$rubber coils” and the coils of a snake are wrapped around the Congolese worker.

 - Source B implies that China was not welcome at the table which is an example of how countries in Asia and Africa were left out of decision making. 

     - Evidence: The Chinese man is standing in the background with his hands in the air.

 - Source B suggests that there is tension between the nations sitting at the table which is consistent with the idea that imperialism was a cause of conflict.

     - Evidence: The Queen and the Kaiser are glaring at each other and seem to be fighting over territory.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source A implies that imperialism could lead to mistreatment of colonists in order to exploit them ($\checkmark$). This is represented by the colonists showing expressions of fear and running from the snake ($\checkmark$). Source B suggests that the countries who were colonizing land in Asia would fight over these territories ($\checkmark$). This can be clearly seen when you look at the cartoons representing Germany and England. They are looking at each other and seem to be fighting over the same piece of cake ($\checkmark$).
