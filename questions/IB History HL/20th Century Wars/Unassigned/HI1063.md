# HI1063

What does Source B suggest about Hitler's intentions for Germany's foreign policy in 1939?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Excerpt from a secret speech given by Adolf Hitler to his military commanders on February 10, 1939. |

[Source: Hitler, A. (10 February 1939). *Hitler's Secret Speech to Military Commanders.* Published in German History in Documents and Images. Retrieved 29 November 2025 from https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/nazi-germany-1933-1945/hitlers-secret-speech-to-military-commanders-february-10-1939 Public Domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Hitler intended for Germany to expand militarily.
He had been planning foreign policy moves well before 1939 — since 1933.
- The regime's previous actions (e.g., remilitarization, annexations) were part of a long-term strategy, not random or defensive.
- Despite public claims of peace, Hitler secretly prepared for war.
- He saw war as necessary or inevitable for Germany's future.
- He wanted the military commanders to understand and support the broader plan
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The source suggests that Hitler viewed Germany's foreign policy since 1933 as part of a calculated and long-term plan rather than a series of isolated decisions ($\checkmark$). It reveals that, despite public declarations of peaceful intentions, the regime was actively preparing for war in secret ($\checkmark$). Additionally, Hitler's aim in addressing his military commanders appears to be to justify his expansionist ambitions and secure their support for future military action ($\checkmark$).
