# HI0614

According to Source A, what were the conditions of the alliance between Austria-Hungary and Germany?    [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

- Germany and Austria-Hungary would fight for each other if either was attacked by Russia.

- Germany would not support another country that attacked Austria-Hungary, nor would Austria-Hungary support another country that attacked Germany, remaining at least neutral.

- If a country supported by Russia attacked Germany and Austria-Hungary, then Germany and Austria-Hungary would be obligated to support each other.

- They could reassess the terms of their alliance every five years and that if no further negotiations are requested in the fifth year, the treaty will automatically renew for three more years.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Dual Alliance between Germany and Austria-Hungary stated that Germany and Austria-Hungary would go to war for each other if either of them were attacked by Russia ($\checkmark$). They also agreed that they would remain neutral in the event that either of them were attacked by another European power ($\checkmark$). However, if Russia was supporting the country that attacked them, then Germany and Austria-Hungary would become involved to support the other ($\checkmark$).
