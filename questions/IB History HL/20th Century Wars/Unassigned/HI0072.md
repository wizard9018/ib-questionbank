# HI0072

What does the source suggest about the consequences of British and French actions for Czechoslovakia?

[3 marks]

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source** | $\hspace{3em}$ | A speech by Winston Churchill in the British House of Commons 5 October 1938.

[Source: Churchill, W. (5 October 1938). Disaster of the First Magnitude. America's National Churchill Museum. Retrived on 12 October 2024 from https://www.nationalchurchillmuseum.org/disaster-of-the-first-magnitude.html. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Churchill suggests that Czechoslovakia has been abandoned by the Western democracies, leading to its suffering.

 - He describes Czechoslovakia as being politically mutilated and economically devastated after the Munich Agreement.

 - Churchill predicts that Czechoslovakia will soon lose its independence and be absorbed into Nazi Germany.

 - He criticizes Britain and France for failing to stop the rise of Nazi power, which he believes directly contributed to Czechoslovakia's plight.

 - Churchill emphasizes that Britain and France's policy of appeasement has led to a major disaster, impacting Czechoslovakia and all of Central and Eastern Europe.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer:}}$

\

Churchill suggests that Czechoslovakia has suffered greatly because of the failure of the Western democracies, particularly Britain and France, to support it properly ($\checkmark$). He states that the country is now "politically mutilated" and facing economic devastation due to the loss of territory and resources ($\checkmark$). Additionally, Churchill predicts that Czechoslovakia will soon lose its independence entirely, being absorbed into the Nazi regime as a consequence of British and French inaction ($\checkmark$).
