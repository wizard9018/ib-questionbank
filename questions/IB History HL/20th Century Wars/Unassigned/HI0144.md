# HI0144

Describe the advantages Germany gained by signing the Nazi-Soviet Pact in 1939. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of **two** of the following:

- Allowed Germany to invade Poland without the risk of a two-front war 

- Received economic aid from the USSR which would negate the impact of any Anglo-French blockade 

- The control of Poland would be a launching pad for Hitler’s later goal of conquering the USSR

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The signing of the Nazi-Soviet Pact with Stalin permitted Germany to invade Poland without the risk of a two-front war as there would be no opposition from the Soviet Union ($\checkmark$). It also allowed Germany to receive economic aid from the USSR which would help negate the impact of any Anglo-French blockade ($\checkmark$).
