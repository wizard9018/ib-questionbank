# HI0782

Define imperialism and describe **three** ways that imperialism could strengthen an empire.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following or a similar description of imperialism:
- Imperialism is the policy of expanding a nation's power and influence by taking over other nations through diplomacy or force.
[1 marks]

Any **three** of the following:
- Empires looked for colonies that had access to natural resources that they were able to profit from.
- Empires used their colonies to expand their markets and make more money.
- Empires gained prestige for having control over large pieces of territory.
- Empires were able to build military bases in their colonies making it easier for them to protect their holdings and mobilize for war.
- Colonists were used for cheap labor or were recruited to the military.
- Empires were able to spread their cultures and way of thinking to many people around the world.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Imperialism is the expansion of an empire by taking over other territories ($\checkmark$). Imperialism can benefit industrial empires needing cheap raw materials ($\checkmark$). Empires also use the people living in their colonies for cheap labor ($\checkmark$). Colonies can also use empires to expand their military reach. They did this by building military and naval bases in their colonized areas ($\checkmark$).
