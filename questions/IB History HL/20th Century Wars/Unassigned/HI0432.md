# HI0432

Describe **two** reasons why the Republicans were defeated in the Spanish Civil War.    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

- Internal Divisions and Lack of Unity

    - The Republican side was politically fragmented, with communists, socialists, anarchists, and moderate Republicans frequently clashing.

    - The May Days of 1937 in Barcelona saw open fighting between communists and anarchists, severely weakening the Republican war effort.

    - Franco's Nationalists were united under a single military command, while the Republicans lacked centralised leadership.

- Limited and Unreliable Foreign Support

    - The Republicans relied heavily on Soviet aid, which was inconsistent and politically motivated.

    - Britain and France enforced the Non-Intervention Agreement, preventing democratic nations from assisting the Republic.

    - The International Brigades provided some support, but their numbers were insufficient to counter the Nationalists' foreign aid.

- Superior Nationalist Foreign Aid

    - Germany (Hitler) and Italy (Mussolini) provided Franco with planes, tanks, and troops, significantly strengthening the Nationalist forces.

    - Germany's Condor Legion carried out the bombing of Guernica (1937), showcasing the military superiority of the Nationalists.

    - Portugal's António Salazar supported Franco by allowing supplies and troops to pass through Portuguese territory.

- Military Weaknesses of the Republicans

    - The Republicans had fewer professional officers and lacked proper military coordination.

    - Many soldiers were militia fighters rather than trained military personnel.

    - The Nationalists had a more structured and disciplined army, led by experienced military leaders like Franco, Mola, and Yagüe.

- Economic Difficulties

    - The Republicans lacked access to crucial resources such as weapons, fuel, and food.

    - Nationalists controlled important agricultural and industrial areas, such as Andalusia (food supply) and the Basque Country (industry).

    - Inflation, shortages, and corruption further weakened the Republican economy.

- Effective Nationalist Propaganda and Organisation

    - Franco presented himself as the defender of Catholicism and Spanish tradition, gaining strong support from the Catholic Church and conservatives.

    - Nationalists controlled key media outlets and used propaganda to rally support, while the Republicans struggled to maintain a cohesive message.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

One reason for the Republican defeat in the Spanish Civil War was internal divisions among left-wing factions. The Republicans were split between communists, socialists, anarchists, and moderates, which led to infighting and weakened their ability to fight effectively ($\checkmark$).

Another reason was the lack of strong foreign support. While Franco received consistent aid from Nazi Germany and Fascist Italy, the Republicans relied on limited Soviet assistance and were further weakened by the Non-Intervention Agreement, which prevented democratic nations like Britain and France from helping them ($\checkmark$).
