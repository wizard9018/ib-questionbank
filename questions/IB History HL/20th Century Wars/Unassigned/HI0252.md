# HI0252

Define the Lend-Lease Policy and describe **two** of its features.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
**One** mark for definition similar to:
- The Lend-Lease Act set up a scheme through which the US sent aid to the Allies during the Second World War.

Any **two** of the following features:
- Immediate payment for war material was not required as the US was “lending” the materials to the Allies.
- The act that started the policy was passed in March of 1941, before the US officially entered WWII as a belligerent.
- It was introduced as a result of a conversation Churchill had with Roosevelt warning him that Britain could not keep paying for US war materials and would therefore not be able to continue to keep its stance against the Nazis.
- The US had Lend-Lease agreements with a number of Allied countries. For example, with the Soviet Union. It ended up supplying it with 18 000 airplanes; 1,500 tanks and more than 500 ships.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Lend-Lease policy was adopted by the US to be able to supply the Allies during WWII ($\checkmark$). The first act that created the policy was passed before the US entered the war as a belligerent  ($\checkmark$) but it allowed the British to be able to resist the Nazis without having to pay for American war materials ($\checkmark$).
