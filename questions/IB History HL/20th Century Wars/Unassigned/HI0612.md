# HI0612

Summarise the events of the July Crisis of 1914.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Germany offered Austria-Hungary its full support in the event of a war with Serbia (the “blank check”) (1-6 July).

 - Austria-Hungary sent Serbia an ultimatum, which included the provision that Austria-Hungary would be in charge of the investigation into the assassination (22 July).

 - Serbia agreed to most of Austria-Hungary's terms but did not want them in charge of the investigation (23 July).

 - Austria-Hungary mobilized for war after Serbia rejected the ultimatum (24-25 July).

 - Russia offered to support Serbia after Austria-Hungary mobilized (24-25 July).

 - Great Britain attempted to broker peace, but Germany and Austria-Hungary rejected the appeals (28 July).

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Following the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand, Europe faced a major diplomatic crisis that is referred to as the July Crisis. During this time, war could still have been avoided. However, diplomacy failed. Following the assassination, Germany agreed to support Austria-Hungary if they went to war with Serbia fully ($\checkmark$). This emboldened Austria-Hungary to send Serbia an ultimatum that they would likely reject as it would allow Austria-Hungary power in the assassination investigation ($\checkmark$). Austria-Hungary mobilized for war with Serbia after its ultimatum was rejected ($\checkmark$).
