# HI0785

Describe **three** reasons for the 1884 Berlin Conference.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- The Berlin Conference was held in order for the Europeans to avoid conflict over areas in Africa that multiple countries tried to claim.
- The Berlin Conference was held in order to maintain the balance of power in Europe and ensure that no one country became significantly more powerful than the others.
- The most powerful nations wanted to ensure that the country who gained a territory was able to control it.
- European nations wanted to clarify rules about free trade in Africa,
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Berlin Conference of 1884 was held in order to avoid conflict between European nations over land in Africa ($\checkmark$). Most European countries, and specifically Germany, wanted to make sure that Great Britain and France did not gain more power and influence in Africa, fearing a shift in the balance of power ($\checkmark$). The Europeans also wanted to clarify rules about free trade throughout Africa in order to ensure that no one nation controlled all of the waterways ($\checkmark$).
