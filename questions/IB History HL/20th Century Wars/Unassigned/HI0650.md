# HI0650

Describe the Entente Cordiale (1904) between Great Britain and France and explain its diplomatic significance.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following or similar:
- The Entente Cordiale, or the Anglo-French Agreement, was a diplomatic understanding between Great Britain and France to settle their colonial differences and encourage future cooperation between the nations.
\
[1 marks]

Any **three** of the following:
- France and Great Britain had been rivals for centuries. This agreement marked a shift in European diplomatic relations.
- This ended Great Britain's “splendid isolation” period which had limited its involvement in continental European affairs.
- With France and Great Britain on the same side, Germany could not benefit from their hostility.
- Paved the way for the Triple Entente as France already had an alliance with Russia.
- Allowed France to more effectively resist Germany when there was conflict in colonial issues.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Entente Cordiale was an agreement between Great Britain and France to encourage cooperation between their nations and to end their tensions over colonial holdings ($\checkmark$). This was a major shift in European diplomacy as the British and French had been rivals for centuries, fighting against each other in several major wars ($\checkmark$). Also, agreeing to the entente ended Britain's self-imposed isolation from continental European affairs, which they had been practicing since the end of the Napoleonic Wars ($\checkmark$). Finally, the easing of Anglo-French colonial tensions allowed France to focus its attention on its colonial conflicts with Germany, thus increasing tensions between the two major colonial powers ($\checkmark$).
