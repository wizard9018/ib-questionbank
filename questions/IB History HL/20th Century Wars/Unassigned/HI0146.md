# HI0146

What, according to Source B, was the British response to Mussolini’s actions in Abyssinia?  [3 marks] :br

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source B** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |Bertram Maxwell, a professor of history and political science in the academic book, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$International Relations (1939)", pp 395-396.

[Source: Maxwell, B. W. (1939). *International Relations*. HathiTrust Digital Library. Retrieved November 1, 2024  from https://hdl.handle.net/2027/uc1.$b589970. Public Domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - The British government prevented Abyssinia from acquiring arms, hoping to gain Mussolini's friendship. 

 - The British government hoped Mussolini would accept the Hoare-Laval Pact

 - The British people's reaction however was to support the principles of the League of Nations to help prevent Mussolini's aggression against Abyssinia. 

 - The British government hence were forced to reprimand Mussolini.

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

According to Source B, the British response to Mussolini's actions in Abyssinia was to prevent Abyssinia from acquiring arms in the hope of gaining Mussolini's friendship ($\checkmark$). They also put forward the Hoare-Laval pact which they hoped would help them deal with the crisis and appease Mussolini ($\checkmark$). However the British people's reaction was to support the principles put forward by the League and to help  prevent Mussolini's aggression against Abyssinia ($\checkmark$).
