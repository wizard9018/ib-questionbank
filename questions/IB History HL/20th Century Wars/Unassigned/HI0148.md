## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

A combination of the below that meets the following guidelines:

 - Both values and limitations must be addressed. If only one is addressed, award a maximum of **two** marks.

 - Origin, purpose and content must all be referenced at some point in the response (either as a value, limitation or both) to receive the full **four** marks.

For value, any combination of the following: 

 - The origin of the source is valuable as the author of Source C, George Martelli, is both a journalist and historian, hence trained in the skills of historical analysis. 

 - The purpose of the source is valuable as its focus is solely on the Abyssinian crisis as evident from the title of the book. 

 - The content, the source is valuable as it details the impact of public opinion on the British government which forced the government to abandon their initial appeasement of Mussolini. 

For limitations, any combination of the following: 

 - The purpose of the source is limited as the book focuses on the Abyssinian crisis and so does not discuss the wider impact of the crisis on the events in  - Europe, such as the remilitarization of the Rhineland that ultimately contributed to the Second World War. 

 - The origin of the source is limited as it was written in 1938 and so does not include the long term impact of the Abyssinian crisis on world peace. 

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The origin of Source C is valuable as the author, George Martelli, is both a journalist and historian. As a historian he is trained in the skills of historical analysis and as a journalist he would have access to policy makers who shaped the crisis ($\checkmark$ VO). The purpose of the source is also valuable as its focus is solely on the Abyssinian crisis as evident from the title of the book ($\checkmark$ VP). Lastly the content of the source holds value as it details the impact of public opinion on the British government which forced it to abandon the initial policy of appeasement of Mussolini ($\checkmark$ VC).

The purpose of the source is limited because it focuses on the Abyssinian crisis itself and so does not discuss the wider impact of the crisis on the events in Europe, such as the remilitarization of the Rhineland, that ultimately contributed to the Second World War ($\checkmark$ LP). In addition, the origin of the source is limited as it was written in 1938 and so does not include the long term impact of the Abyssinian crisis on world peace ($\checkmark$ LO).
