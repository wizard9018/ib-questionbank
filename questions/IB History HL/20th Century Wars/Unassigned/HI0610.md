# HI0610

Describe the Congress of Berlin and give **two** examples of how it shifted the balance of power in Europe.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

The following or similar description of the Congress of Berlin:

- The Congress of Berlin was a meeting of the most powerful nations in Europe to discuss the balance of power in Europe following the  Russo-Turkish War. The meeting happened in 1878 and was convened by German Chancellor Otto von Bismarck.

\

[1 marks]

Any **two** of the following:

 - The Treaty of Berlin was signed at the end of the conference and limited the amount of control Russia would have in the Balkans. This limited Russian access to the Black Sea and slowed any naval aspirations they may have had, shifting power away from Russia.

 - Nations such as Romania, Serbia and Montenegro were granted independence.

 - Bosnia and Herzegovina were occupied by Austria-Hungary, making Austria-Hungary more powerful and limiting Russian influence in the region.

 - The Ottoman Empire maintained its status as a sovereign empire which limited European expansion in the region.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Congress of Berlin was a meeting of the European powers in the late 19th century. The purpose of the meeting was to reassess the treaty that was made at the end of the Russo-Turkish War to maintain the balance of power in Europe ($\checkmark$). The Treaty of Berlin was signed at the end of the conference. Though Russia defeated the Ottoman Empire in the war, the conference limited Russia's access to the Balkan region. Specifically, Russia could not use the Black Sea to expand its military ($\checkmark$). In addition, several nations in the Balkans were granted their independence, but Austria-Hungary occupied Bosnia and Herzegovina ($\checkmark$). The treaty successfully limited the most powerful nations in Europe from gaining too much power at the expense of the Balkans and the Ottoman Empire.
