# HI0784

Describe **three** ways military conflict weakened the Ottoman Empire. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Conflicts with Russia and the Serbians led to a significant loss of territory in Europe and around the Black Sea. This led to a loss of trade routes and a decline in prestige, especially among the Europeans.
- Military losses contributed to frustrations on the homefront and internal conflict. This led to further distrust of the central government.
- Nationalist uprisings led to the loss of Greece, Serbia, and Bulgaria.
- Europeans purposely involved themselves in conflicts that involved the Ottoman Empire in order to weaken them and spread their influence in the region.
- Mistreatment of minorities led to uprisings and a distrust of authority.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Ottoman Empire engaged in conflicts with people within and outside of their empire that would significantly weaken their power and influence. Nationalist uprisings within the empire led to the loss of Greece, Serbia, and Bulgaria and weakened their influence within Europe ($\checkmark$). Also, the Ottoman government often mistreated minorities living in the empire which led to a distrust of authority ($\checkmark$). Military losses against Russia decreased their access to the Black Sea and important trade routes, hurting their wealth and military reach ($\checkmark$).
