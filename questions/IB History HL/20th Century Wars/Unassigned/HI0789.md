# HI0789

Using evidence from Source C, explain Rudyard Kipling's justification for imperialism.

[4 marks]

$\\$

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source C** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | An excerpt from the poem $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The White Man's Burden,” written by Rudyard Kipling in 1899.

:box[

Take up the White Man's burden--\

Send forth the best ye breed--\

Go bind your sons to exile\

To serve your captives' need;\

To wait in heavy harness,\

On fluttered folk and wild--\

Your new-caught, sullen peoples,\

Half-devil and half-child.\

\

Take up the White Man's burden--\

In patience to abide,\

To veil the threat of terror\

And check the show of pride;\

By open speech and simple,\

An hundred times made plain\

To seek another's profit,\

And work another's gain.\

\

Take up the White Man's burden--\

The savage wars of peace--\

Fill full the mouth of Famine\

And bid the sickness cease;\

And when your goal is nearest\

The end for others sought,\

Watch sloth and heathen Folly\

Bring all your hopes to nought.\

\

[…]\

\

Take up the White Man's burden--\

And reap his old reward:\

The blame of those ye better,\

The hate of those ye guard--\

The cry of hosts ye humour\

(Ah, slowly!) toward the light:--\

"Why brought he us from bondage,\

Our loved Egyptian night?"

]

[Source: Kipling\, R. (1899). *The White Man's Burden*. Retrieved May 15, 2025 from  from https://kiplingsociety.co.uk/poem/poems_burden.htm. Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **four** of the following:

 - It is the responsibility of the white man, or Europeans, to go to far away lands in order to help civilize people that they view as savage, wild or child-like.

     - Evidence: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Take up the White Man's burden--\

     Send forth the best ye breed--\

     Go bind your sons to exile\

     To serve your captives' need;\

     To wait in heavy harness,\

     On fluttered folk and wild--\

     Your new-caught, sullen peoples,\

     Half-devil and half-child

 - Going to these nations is not for the benefit of the Europeans but for the benefit of the people in these countries.

     - Evidence: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Take up the White Man's burden--\

     In patience to abide,\

     To veil the threat of terror\

     And check the show of pride;\

     By open speech and simple,\

     An hundred times made plain\

     To seek another's profit,\

     And work another's gain.”

 - Europeans should conquer these lands in order to make sure that the people are fed and healthy.

     - Evidence: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Take up the White Man's burden--\

     The savage wars of peace--\

     Fill full the mouth of Famine\

     And bid the sickness cease;”

 - Europeans will likely see their work squandered by the lazy people they are trying to help but it is the right thing to do.

     - Evidence: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$And when your goal is nearest\

     The end for others sought,\

     Watch sloth and heathen Folly\

     Bring all your hopes to nought.”

 - There is no real reward for saving the colonists as they will still yearn for their past, even though their lives are better.

     - Evidence: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Take up the White Man's burden--\

     And reap his old reward:\

     The blame of those ye better,\

     The hate of those ye guard.”

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source C promotes the idea that imperialism is justified because of the cultural supremacy of the Europeans, or white people ($\checkmark$), describing the people of other places as $\text{\textquotedblleft}$half-devil and half-child” to imply European superiority ($\checkmark$). He also frames imperialism as a sacrifice, saying that there is no benefit for the colonizers, while the places being colonized will be fed and cured of diseases ($\checkmark$). He also claims that their responsibility is thankless, as the colonists are too lazy to take advantage of all that they have to offer ($\checkmark$).
