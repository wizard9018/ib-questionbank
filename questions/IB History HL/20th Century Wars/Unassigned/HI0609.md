# HI0609

Describe the German policy of Weltpolitik and identify **two** of its goals.

[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

The following or similar description of Weltpolitik:

 - Weltpolitik was Kaiser Wilhelm II's goal to expand Germany's influence in the world. The policy reflected Wilhelm's belief that Germany should be considered a world power based on their military and industrial strength.

\

[1 marks]

Any **two** of the following:

 - Growth of the German colonial empire in Africa and Asia

 - Establishing economic spheres of influence in Africa and Asia

 - Expansion of the German navy

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Weltpolitik was a German foreign policy that was established in the 1890s. It was based on the belief that Germany should be a world power and needed to expand its influence ($\checkmark$). Kaiser Wilhelm II believed that in order to be a world power, the German's needed to expand their colonial holdings in Africa and Asia ($\checkmark$). Also, Germany made it a goal to expand their navy in order to better support their army and their international territories ($\checkmark$).
