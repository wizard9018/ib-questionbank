# HI0602Q3

With reference to its origin, purpose and content, analyse the value and limitations of Source D for a historian studying the causes of the Chinese Civil War.

[4 marks]

$\\$

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source D** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Telegram to the British Government reporting on an editorial titled $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Without Democracy There Will Not Be Peaceful Reconstruction” published in the Yenan newspaper Emancipation Daily in 1945, commemorating the 34th anniversary of the Chinese Revolution of 1911. Yenan (Yan'an) was the capital of the Communist-controlled area of China, and Emancipation Daily was a newspaper produced by the Chinese Communist Party.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

A combination of the ideas below that meets the following guidelines:

 - Both values and limitations must be addressed. If only one is addressed, award a maximum of **two** marks.

 - Origin, purpose and content must all be referenced at some point in the response (either as a value, limitation or both) to receive the full four marks.

For value, any of the following:

 - The source contains insight as it was made in 1945, when the Chinese Civil War started its second phase. (origin)

 - The source summarizes the ideas of an editorial in a newspaper of the Chinese Communist Party, letting the reader know the communist position regarding the future of the country. 

 - The source gives the Communist perspective, mentioning their desire for unity and a democratic government (content)

For limitations, any of the following:

 - The editorial is favorable to the Communist position, so it presents a one-sided view. (origin)

 - The source seeks to inform the British government of the most relevant ideas contained in the editorial, so it might leave out important information.  Since the editorial is one-sided it seeks to persuade readers that the Communist wants to fulfill the aims of the 1911 Revolution, leaving out communist ideology (purpose) 

 - The source does not provide the perspective of the GMD/KMT, and due to its date of publication, it also ignores the outcome of this conflict (content)

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source D's origin is valuable as it was created in 1945, which allows it to have insight, as this is the year the Chinese Civil War entered its second phase ($\checkmark\, \text{VO}$)

. The purpose is also valuable since it summarizes the ideas of an editorial from a newspaper affiliated with the Chinese Communist Party, giving readers a clear understanding of the Communist stance on the country's future ($\checkmark\, \text{VP}$). The content reflects the Communist perspective, emphasizing their desire for national unity and a democratic government ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$).

However, Source D also has some limitations. Its origin is limited as it is biased in favor of the Communist position, summarizing information belonging to a CCP newspaper ($\checkmark\, \text{LO}$).  Since it seeks to inform the British government about key ideas from the editorial, its purpose is also limited, since this desire to be concise may lead it to omit important details ($\checkmark\, \text{LP}$). Additionally, because the editorial is intended to persuade readers that the Communists aim to fulfill the goals of the 1911 Revolution, it leaves out aspects of Communist ideology that might have been more questionable to the Chinese people ($\checkmark\, \text{LP}$). The source also lacks the perspective of the GMD and, given its publication date, does not know that a war did end up breaking out ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$).
