# HI0602Q4

What, according to Source C, were the causes of the Chinese Civil War?

[3 marks]

$\\$

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source C** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Excerpt from a summary of the Chinese Civil War by the National Archives of Great Britain. 

[Source: *The Chinese Civil War*. The National Archives. Retrieved March 18, 2025 from https://nationalarchives.gov.uk/education/resources/the-chinese-civil-war/. UK Open Government Licence. ]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - There were ideological differences between the GMD and the CCP

 - The groups that controlled the GMD were afraid the CCP would eliminate their power. 

 - Peasants wanted land reform and better living conditions, indicating that there was an economic reason for the war

 - The rural masses had so far been ignored, and the CCP appealed to them. 

 - The defeat of the Japanese meant there was no longer an unifying factor

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

According to Source C, there were many causes for the Chinese Civil War. One of them was that peasants wanted land reform and better living conditions ($\checkmark$), something that had been ignored by the GMD but promised by the CCP ($\checkmark$). Moreover, once the Japanese were defeated, there was no other reason to remain united in the face of a common enemy ($\checkmark$).
