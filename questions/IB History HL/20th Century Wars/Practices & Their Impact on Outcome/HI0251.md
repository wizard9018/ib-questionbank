# HI0251

Describe the British Blockade and explain how it impacted the outcome of WWI.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
**One** mark for description of the blockade:
- Britain used its massive surface fleet to turn back all ships bound for Germany, forcing it to rely on its own production and that of the Austro-Hungarian Empire.

**Two** marks for explanation of impact:
- From 1914-1918, 800 000 German civilians died of undernourishment and related disorders. This made it difficult to sustain the army.
- Harsh living conditions incentivized revolution against the Kaiser which started in October 1918 with a sailors` mutiny. This political instability led the German leadership to ask for an armistice.
- Left the Army with limited supplies. The blockade cut supplies of nitrates to Germany which were vital for explosives for the army as well as fertilisers for the farmers.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The British blockade on Germany was one of the factors that determined the outcome of the war. Britain used its control of the seas to turn back all ships that wanted to supply Germany ($\checkmark$). This led to severe shortages that affected the German civilian population by causing around 800 000 from malnourishment and related illnesses. This meant German soldiers were also undernourished and did not have enough energy to fight properly ($\checkmark$). Food shortages also led to horrible living conditions that incentivized revolution against the Kaiser, the main proponent of the war. In October 1918 there was a mutiny of sailors that would later spread and end with the abdication of the Kaiser. This political instability led the German leadership to ask for an armistice ($\checkmark$).
