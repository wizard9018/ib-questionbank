# HI0744

Explain the role of foreign powers in the outcome of **two** 20th-century civil wars.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following with supporting explanation:

 - Russian Civil War (1917-1923). Britain, France, Japan, and the USA supported the anti-Bolshevik Whites. Intervention failed to unite or effectively support the Whites; the Bolsheviks discredited them as tools of foreign powers.

 - Spanish Civil War (1936-1939). Nazi Germany and Fascist Italy supported Franco's Nationalists, the USSR supported the Republicans. German and Italian air and ground support gave the Nationalists a decisive military edge, especially in air power and logistics.

 - Greek Civil War (1946-1949). Britain and later the USA supported the royalist government while the USSR and Yugoslavia indirectly aided communist forces. U.S. military and economic aid under the Truman Doctrine ensured government victory and containment of communism in Greece.

 - Chinese Civil War (1927-1949). USSR backed the CCP, especially after WWII while  the USA supported the Nationalists (Kuomindang). Soviet occupation of Manchuria post-WWII handed the CCP control of key areas and Japanese weapons. U.S. support was insufficient and undermined by Nationalist corruption.

 - Vietnam War (1955-1975). USA and other anti-communist nations supported South Vietnam while the USSR and China supported North Vietnam and the Viet Cong.

 - North Vietnamese victorious due to prolonged guerrilla warfare, strong local support, and continued Soviet/Chinese material aid. U.S. public opposition led to their withdrawal.

 - Angolan Civil War (1975-2002). USSR and Cuba supported MPLA while the USA and South Africa backed UNITA. Cuban troops and Soviet equipment enabled MPLA to gain and hold power. Cold War rivalry prolonged the war.

 - Afghan Civil War (1978-1992). USSR invaded in 1979 to support the communist government. The USA, Pakistan, and Saudi Arabia supported the Mujahideen.

 - U.S.-backed insurgency, with CIA and ISI support, ultimately forced Soviet withdrawal in 1989, contributing to the communist regime's collapse in 1992.

 - Nicaraguan Contra War (1981-1990). USA funded and trained Contra rebels to fight the Sandinista government while the USSR and Cuba supported the Sandinistas.

 - U.S. aid destabilized the Sandinista regime. The ongoing conflict led to economic hardship, influencing the 1990 election defeat of the Sandinistas.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

In the Russian Civil War (1917-1923), foreign powers including Britain, France, Japan, and the United States intervened to support the anti-Bolshevik White forces, providing limited military and financial support. ($\checkmark$) However, their involvement was poorly coordinated and failed to unify the White movement. The Bolsheviks portrayed the Whites as puppets of imperialist powers. This, combined with effective Bolshevik organization and control of central regions, contributed to Red Army victory and the establishment of the Soviet regime. ($\checkmark$)

In the Vietnam War (1955-1975), the United States, alongside other Western allies, supported the government of South Vietnam with troops, funds, and military equipment in an effort to contain communism. In contrast, the Soviet Union and China supplied North Vietnam and the Viet Cong with weapons, training, and logistical support. ($\checkmark$) Despite U.S. military superiority, North Vietnam's use of guerrilla tactics, its strong ideological motivation, and consistent foreign aid enabled it to persist. Increasing anti-war protests in the U.S. led to a gradual withdrawal, and in 1975, the North achieved reunification, marking a communist victory. ($\checkmark$)
