## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of **two** of the following:

Mao is worshipped as a deity by his followers in China. 

- The prayers and doctrine of this religion are recited from the book on Mao's Thought. 

- The symbols of the religion are the hammer and sickle that signify Communism.

- The entire nation, comprised of different sections of society, follow this religion.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source A suggests that Mao has been elevated to the status of a religious deity that is the subject of worship by all his followers in China ($\checkmark$). Mao's worshippers recite the prayers and doctrine of the religion he embodies from the book on Mao's Thought ($\checkmark$).
