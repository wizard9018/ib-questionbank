# HI0103

Explain **two** reasons for the improvement of Sino-American relations in the 1970's.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following ideas with their evidence:
- Sino-Soviet split: border clashes in 1969 signalled to the world, including the US, that the Communist block was not monolithic and it was to negotiate with its separate members.
- Fear of Soviet expansion: the Brezhnev Doctrine of 1968 stated that the USSR could intervene in any socialist state to “protect socialism”. This had been applied in Czechoslovakia in 1968 to quell the Prague Spring.
- To put an end to the Vietnam war: The US had been in a state of war against North Vietnam since 1964 and there was no victory in sight in part because the People's Republic of China supported North Vietnam.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Both the US and the People's Republic of China feared Soviet expansionism after the declaration of the Brezhnev Doctrine in 1968. The Brezhnev doctrine stated that the USSR could intervene in any socialist state to “protect socialism” ($\checkmark$). This could mean further Soviet occupation of Eastern Europe, like it had done in Czechoslovakia and possible intervention in China. China at the time was going through its Cultural Revolution and Mao feared Soviet intervention to put an end to the upheavals it had caused ($\checkmark$). The US was also eager to prevent Soviet expansionism and to negotiate with China a possible solution to the Vietnam War ($\checkmark$). There was domestic conflict because of the protracted war and Nixon had promised in his campaign to put an honorable end to US participation in Vietnam. For that, it needed to negotiate with North Vietnam's neighbour and main supplier, China ($\checkmark$).
