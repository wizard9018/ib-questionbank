# HI1158

Explain **three** effects of Mao's victory in the Chinese Civil War (1949) on the Cold War.
[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following with explanation:

Shift in the Global Balance of Power
- China's communist victory greatly expanded the reach of communism, shifting the balance toward the Soviet bloc.
- The “loss of China” shocked the West and deepened Cold War divisions between communist and capitalist powers.

Impact on U.S. Foreign Policy
- The U.S. faced domestic backlash for “losing China,” leading to stronger anti-communist sentiment and McCarthyism.
- America intensified its containment strategy in Asia, forming alliances and backing non-communist regimes in Korea, Vietnam, and Taiwan.

Impact on the Soviet Union and Communist Bloc
- The USSR gained a major new ally in Asia, forming the Sino-Soviet alliance (1950) and strengthening the communist front.
- China's revolution inspired other Asian communist movements, though rivalry over leadership later emerged between Moscow and Beijing.

Impact on the Asian Cold War
- Mao's victory made Asia a central arena of Cold War conflict, extending superpower competition beyond Europe.
- The U.S. adapted containment into a global policy, responding to rising communist influence in the region.

Long-Term Significance
- China's rise introduced a third major communist power, complicating the bipolar structure of the Cold War.
- Mao's revolutionary model inspired anti-colonial and communist movements worldwide, expanding the Cold War into Asia and Africa.
\
[6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Due to Mao's victory, there was a shift in the global balance of power ($\checkmark$) and the ideological reach of communism was dramatically expanded as the world's most populous nation came under communist control ($\checkmark$). Secondly, US foreign policy moved toward stronger containment in Asia ($\checkmark$). This can be seen in the formation of military alliances such as SEATO (1954) as well as support for anti-communist regimes in Korea, Vietnam, and Taiwan ($\checkmark$). Finally, the victory contributed to the spread of the Cold War ($\checkmark$), as it inspired communist movements in other countries in Asia, such as Vietnam, Malaya and Indonesia ($\checkmark$).
