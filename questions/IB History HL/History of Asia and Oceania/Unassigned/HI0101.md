# HI0101

Describe **two** reasons for strained relations between the USSR and China during the time of Stalin's rule.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Any **two** of the following:
- Limited Soviet support to the CCP during the Chinese Civil War.
- Ideological differences with Maoism's emphasis on the role of the peasants instead of the proletariat.
- Territorial disputes like the port of Vladivostok which had been taken by imperial Russia from China during the era of imperialism.
- Lack of the USSR's participation in the Korean War in defence of Communism while Stalin did pressure Mao into sending troops
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Sino-Soviet relations were strained from the start of the People's Republic of China. One stressor on the relationship was that the USSR favoured a united front against Japan instead of supporting the CCP during the Chinese Civil War ($\checkmark$). Furthermore, Soviet Communism and Chinese Communism were different ideologically with Mao having adapted Communism through the realities of China ($\checkmark$). Moreover, Mao was suspicious of the USSR`s motives in pressuring him to participate in the Korean War while the USSR took the time to rebuild itself after WWII ($\checkmark$).
