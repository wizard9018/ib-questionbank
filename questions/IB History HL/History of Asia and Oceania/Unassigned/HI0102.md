# HI0102

Explain **three** reasons for the Sino-Soviet split.
[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any of the following **three** reasons and their explanations:
- Ideological differences - any or all of the following:
    - Role of the peasants: Mao felt that the peasants should be the ones to drive the Communist revolution forward while Stalin was more orthodox in expecting the proletariat to be the drivers of revolution. This was significant in that the Soviet Union stressed the need to focus revolutionary activity on the cities while Mao favoured the creation of rural bases during the long Chinese Civil War.
    - Peaceful coexistence: Khruschev thought the USSR and the US could coexist peacefully despite having different ideologies while Mao thought it was a duty to advance the cause of Communism in the world.
    - Cult of personality: Khruschev criticised the cult of the leader done under Stalin's rule while Mao used the cult of personality to consolidate his own power.
- USSR's position in the Korean War: The USSR supported North Korea with material aid only.
- USSR's position in the war between India and China: the USSR did not even diplomatically condemn India in its border conflict with China
- Mao's idea that the USSR wanted to keep China as a junior partner: Mao resented receiving Soviet indications on how to achieve the victory of the Revolution during the period of the Chinese Civil War as well as Soviet indications on how to achieve development according to the Soviet model of the 5 Year Plans.
- The Soviet Union's limited support in helping the Chinese build a nuclear weapon.
- Soviet attempts to limit Mao's aggressive actions during the Taiwan Strait Crises in 1954-55 and 1958.
\
 [6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One of the most important reasons for the Sino-Soviet split were ideological differences ($\checkmark$). For example, Mao believed in the necessity of expanding the Revolution despite the cost and rejected Khrushchev's idea of “peaceful coexistence” with capitalist states ($\checkmark$).  Mao also identified Khruschev as a “revisionist” deviating from true Leninism with his criticism of the cult of personality during Stalin's time ($\checkmark$), a cult of personality which Mao greatly used in China to inspire the people to achieve the goals of the revolution and to consolidate his own power ($\checkmark$).

Mao was also angered by limited Soviet support in building a Chinese nuclear weapon as well as during the Taiwan Strait Crises ($\checkmark$). He was frustrated by  the USSR trying to restrain the CCP during the Taiwan Strait Crises because  Mao saw the opportunity to  achieve success for the Revolution in the whole of Chinese territory ($\checkmark$).
