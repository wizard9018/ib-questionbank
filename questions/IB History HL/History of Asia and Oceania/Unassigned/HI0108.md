# HI0108

Using Source B and your own knowledge, explain how the issue of “Two Chinas” was a problem for the normalization of Sino-American relations during the Cold War.
[4 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Excerpt from the Joint Statement Following Discussions With Leaders of the People’s Republic of China (also known as the Shanghai Communiqué). February 27th, 1972.

[Source: *Joint Statement Following Discussion With Leaders of the People's Republic of China*. (1972, February 27th). Office of the Historian. Retrieved October 6th, 2024 from https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v17/d203. Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **four** of the following:

Use of source:
- Source B explains the US view of the issue as a Chinese problem and recognizes the unity of the Chinese nation.
- Source B shows there are US military forces in Taiwan.

Own knowledge:
- The US recognized Taiwan's government as the legitimate government of all of China from 1949 until 1979.
- There were 2 crises in the 1950's involving the strait of Taiwan in which the US provided military support to the Taiwanese side.
- The situation of Taiwan was first dealt with diplomatically in the Shanghai Communiquè where Mao and Nixon agreed to the idea of “one China”.
- In 1979, the United States recognized the PRC and established diplomatic relations with it as the sole legitimate government of China. The United States then withdrew its diplomatic recognition of the government of Taiwan but continues to support it militarily.

To achieve full marks, there must be a reference to source in the answer.  References must be explicit, either by identifying the name of the source or its letter.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
From the end of the Chinese Civil War in 1949 the Chinese nation was ruled by 2 governments, one in Beijing controlling what is known as the People's Republic of China and one in Taiwan. Both presumed to rule over the whole Chinese nation and in that dispute, the US sided with the government of Taiwan and even stationed troops there as seen in Source B ($\checkmark\,  \text{OK and sources}$). In the 1950s, the desire of the People's Republic of China to recover Taiwan had led to crises in which the US had sided with the Taiwanese and committed itself to protecting Taiwan from an invasion ($\checkmark\, \text{OK}$).  We can see in Source B that this issue was dealt with in the Shanghai Communiquè where both sides agreed to recognize the existence of only one China ($\checkmark$ sources).
