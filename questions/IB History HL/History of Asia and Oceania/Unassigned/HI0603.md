# HI0603

Describe the 100 Flowers Campaign and explain how it facilitated the Great Leap Forward.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
For description, the following or similar:
- In the Hundred Flowers Campaign (1956-1957), Mao Zedong encouraged intellectuals to voice their opinions on government policies. Initially, there was little response; however, eventually, there was widespread criticism of the Communist Party.
\
[1 marks]

For explanation, any **three** of the following connections to the Great Leap Forward:
- Mao was able to identify critics
- It strengthened Mao's authority; critics were purged through the resultant Anti-Rightist campaign
- The suppression of perceived $\lq$rightists' set a precedent for the suppression of intellectuals
- The brief period of open discussion demonstrated the power of mass mobilisation, something Mao picked up on.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
In the Hundred Flowers Campaign (1956-1957), Mao encouraged intellectuals to voice their opinions on government policies. After a slow start, there was widespread criticism of the Communist Party ($\checkmark$). This facilitated the Great Leap Forward firstly by enabling Mao to identify his critics ($\checkmark$). Once identified, critics were purged through the Anti-Rightist campaign, leaving no one to protest against Mao's unrealistic goals in the GLF ($\checkmark$). Finally, the brief period of open discussion demonstrated the power of mass mobilisation in state-led initiatives, something Mao picked up on and developed during the GLF ($\checkmark$).
