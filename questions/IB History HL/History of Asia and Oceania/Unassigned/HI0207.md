# HI0207

With reference to its origin, purpose and content, analyse the value and limitations of Source D for an historian studying the impact of the Cultural Revolution. [4 marks]

$\\$

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source D** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Conversation between the Party of Labor of Albania Delegation, headed by Beqir Balluku, and Mao Zedong on 5 October 1968. |

[Source: *Conversation between the Party of Labor of Albania Delegation, headed by Beqir Balluku, and Mao Zedong*. (1968, October 5). Wilson Center Digital Archive. Retrieved November 29, 2024 from https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org/document/300036. Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

A combination of the below that meets the following guidelines:

- Both values and limitations must be addressed. If only one is addressed, award a maximum of **two** marks.

- Origin, purpose and content must all be referenced at some point in the response (either as a value, limitation or both) to receive the full **four** marks.

For value, any **one or two** of the following:

- The origin of the source is valuable as it is the recording of a conversation between China's Communist leader Mao Zedong and the leader of the visiting Albanian delegation in 1968, during the Cultural Revolution. 

- The purpose of the source is valuable as Mao's aim is to discuss and explain the reasons for and impact of the Cultural Revolution. 

- The content of the source is valuable as Mao shares details of infighting, at times involving family members and shares his reasoning regarding its revolutionary impact. 

For limitations, any **one or two** of the follwoing:

- The origin of the source is limited as the discussion in 1968 does not give the historian studying the Cultural Revolution an idea of how the Revolution impacted China in the years till the death of Mao Zedong in 1976. 

- The purpose of the source is limited as the discussion is between Mao who is the architect of the Cultural Revolution and hence would only present what he sees as the positive impact of the Revolution.

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The origin of Source D is valuable as it is the recording of a conversation between China's Communist leader Mao Zedong who was the main force behind the Cultural Revolution and the leader of the visiting Albanian delegation, recorded in 1968 just two years after the launch of the Cultural Revolution ($\checkmark$ VO). The purpose of the source is also valuable as Mao's aim is to discuss and explain the reasons for and impact of the Cultural Revolution ($\checkmark$ VP). Lastly, the content of the source holds value as Mao shares details of chaotic infighting, at times involving family members, and shares his reasoning regarding the necessity of the Cultural Revolution for Chinese society ($\checkmark$ VC). 

The origin of the source is limited as the discussion in 1968 does not give the historian studying the Cultural Revolution an idea of how it impacted China after 1968 and until the death of Mao Zedong in 1976 ($\checkmark$ LO). Furthermore, the origin and purpose of the source are limited because the discussion is with Mao himself and hence would tend to present the events of the Revolution in a positive light ($\checkmark$ LO and LP).
