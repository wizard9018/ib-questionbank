For **comparison** any **three** to **four** of the following **similarities**:

- Both Sources state that Liu Shaoqi had been overthrown or demoted during the Cultural Revolution.

- Both sources state that Mao was now aligned with Lin Piao

- Both sources mention the fighting and chaos that broke out during the Cultural Revolution.

- Both sources mention the attempts made to bring the chaos and disturbance under control.

- Both sources state that the military played a role in bringing the chaos under control.

- Both sources mention that despite the attempts to bring the chaos under control, the situation still is problematic.

- Both sources mention the importance of agricultural production.

For **contrast**, any **two** to **three** of the following **differences**:

- The Sources emphasize different impacts of the Revolution on leadership. Source C mentions that Chou Enlai retained his position which is not mentioned in Source B while Source B discusses how Liu and Teng lost power.

- Source C mentions the Red Guards and details the destruction caused by them. It also mentions the Red Guards were banished to the countryside in order to bring the chaos under control neither of which is mentioned in Source B.

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Both Source B and C state that Liu Shaoqi had been overthrown or demoted during the Cultural Revolution ($\checkmark$ similarity). Source B mentions that  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Liu Shaoqi, Deng Xiaoping, and others” were overthrown while Source C states that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$It became apparent that Liu and Teng had been demoted in party ranking at the Plenum” ($\checkmark$ evidence). 

Both sources also mention the fighting and chaos that broke out during the Cultural Revolution ($\checkmark$ similarity). Source B states $\text{\textquotedblleft}$a large number of organizations calling themselves the Mao-Lin faction repeatedly confronted and fought one another, whether within Party and government organizations or within those of workers and peasants,” while Source C mentions, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the attacks on persons regarded as bourgeois, the beating of aged persons in the streets, the seizure and destruction of works of art, the attacks on both Christian churches and Buddhist temples, the forced extortion of ancestral tablets from terrorized peasants, the desecration of graveyards” ($\checkmark$ evidence). 

Both sources state that the military played a role in bringing the chaos under control ($\checkmark$ similarity). Source B mentions, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the military, brought in to bring the situation under control,” while Source C mentions, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the formation of committees in the provinces to supervise their (Red Guard) activities was accelerated, with party and PLA [People's Liberation Army] representatives both playing key roles” ($\checkmark$ evidence).  

Both sources mention the need to maintain agricultural production as an issue that helped drive the reduction of the chaos of the Revolution ($\checkmark$ similarity). Source B emphasizes the value of agricultural production and the need to $\text{\textquotedblleft}$secure the autumn harvest” ($\checkmark$ evidence) and Source C explains that some Red Guards were even $\text{\textquotedblleft}$sent into the countryside to help the communes with the harvesting” ($\checkmark$ evidence). 

One significant contrast between Sources B and C is their emphasis on the impacts of the Red Guards. Source C describes the Red Guards and details the destruction caused by them as well as mentioning that they were sent to the countryside in order to bring the chaos under control while Source B does not mention the role of the Red Guards or their impact at all ($\checkmark$ difference). Lastly while both sources discuss the impacts of the Cultural Revolution on Chinese leadership, Source C simply mentions that Chou Enlai retained his position and alignment with Mao, while Source B does not mention Chou and instead emphasizes that Liu and Teng were $\text{\textquotedblleft}$overthrown” ($\checkmark$ difference).
