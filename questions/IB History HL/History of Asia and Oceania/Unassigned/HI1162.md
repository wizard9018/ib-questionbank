# HI1162

Describe two ways China's involvement in the Korean War affected its economic development.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- War diverted scarce resources away from domestic reconstruction after the civil war.
- Military mobilisation slowed industrial recovery and increased the strain on food and raw materials.
- Soviet military aid created short-term dependency and debt obligation.
- The conflict stimulated the development of China's defence industries and legitimised tighter central economic control.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
China's involvement in the Korean War diverted vital resources away from domestic recovery after the civil war, slowing economic reconstruction ($\checkmark$). However, the conflict also accelerated the growth of defence industries and justified greater central control over the economy, strengthening state planning and industrial capacity ($\checkmark$).
