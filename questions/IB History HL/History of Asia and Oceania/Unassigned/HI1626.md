## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Intellectuals should leave their privileged positions and work alongside workers and peasants.
- Physical labour would strengthen the revolutionary commitment of intellectuals by making them tougher and more dedicated to communist ideals through practical struggle.
- Intellectuals working alongside workers and peasants would help them understand ordinary people's lives and become more closely connected to the masses.
- The poster is throwing positive light on Mao's policy of sending educated people to the countryside to be re-educated by the masses.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The image caption indicates that man is an intellectual who has left his privileged position to work alongside workers and peasants in order to better understand their practical struggles and strengthen his commitment to communist ideals ($\checkmark$). By showing the challenging physical labour, it also implies that intellectuals have much to learn from the working class and peasants, which would help them become more integrated into socialist society ($\checkmark$). Finally, the red paper in the man's pocket indicates that he is undergoing reeducation in Maoist ideology ($\checkmark$).
