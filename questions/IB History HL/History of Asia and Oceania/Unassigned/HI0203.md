# HI0203

Describe actions taken by Mao to regain political control of China during the Cultural Revolution in 1966.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of **three** of the following:

- Mao encouraged the formation of the Red Guards consisting of bands of radical students.

- The Red Guards were ordered to attack the $`$four olds' - old thought, culture, practice and customs and remove $`$bad elements' from society.

- Mao removed his opponents from the party and retook absolute control of party hierarchy. 

- Among those targeted were Liu Shaoqi and Deng Xiaoping who ran the government since the time Mao had stepped down as Head of State. 

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The $`$Great Proletarian Cultural Revolution' marked Mao's attempt to regain political control following the failure of the Great Leap Forward and consequent weakening of his power. He initially encouraged the formation of the Red Guards consisting of bands of radical students who were ordered to attack the $`$four olds' - old thought, culture, practice and customs and remove $`$bad elements' from society ($\checkmark$). He also proceeded to remove his opponents from the party and retook absolute control of party hierarchy ($\checkmark$). Liu Shaoqi and Deng Xiaoping, who had run the government since the time Mao had stepped down as Head of State, were both removed from office amongst the chaos caused by the Red Guards ($\checkmark$).
