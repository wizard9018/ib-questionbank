$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source A is valuable in its origin since it is from 1973 and so has insight into the new relationship between the US and China ($\checkmark\, \text{VO}$). However, precisely because it is from 1973, it lacks hindsight and does not yet know about the official establishment of diplomatic relations with the US in 1979 ($\checkmark\, \text{LO}$).

Its purpose is valuable because it is a press release so it wants to inform the population of the conclusion of the Party Congress ($\checkmark\, \text{VP}$). However, the purpose is also limited since it seeks to persuade the population that China is in danger and they must be ready to defend it ($\checkmark\, \text{LP}$).

The content is valuable since it identifies China’s main enemies, the US and the USSR ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$). However, the content is also limited as it leaves out the fact that the relationship with the US is changing and improving ($\checkmark\, \text{LC}$).
