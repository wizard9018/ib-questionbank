# HI0105

List **two** of the people responsible for the improvement of Sino-American relations in the 1970s and describe their role.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Award only one mark if only names are listed with no description of role.

Any **two** of the following:

- Mao: leader of the People's Republic of China and one of the signatories of the Shanghai Communique.
- Nixon: president of the US and one of the signatories of the Shanghai Communique.
- Henry Kissinger: US Secretary of State in the 1970`s. He visited China  to iron out the difficulties in the establishment of relations between the US and the People's Republic of China prior to the visit of US president Nixon.
- Zhou Enlai: Prime Minister of China from 1949-1976. He met with Henry Kissinger prior to the visit of US president Nixon to iron out the difficulties in the establishment of relations between the US and the People's Republic of China.
- Glenn Cowan: member of the US Table Tennis team. His participation in the World Table Tennis Championship in 1971 and friendly encounter with Chinese counterpart Zhuang Zedong sparked the start of diplomatic relations between the 2 countries.
- Zhuang Zedong: member of the People's Republic of China`s Table Tennis team. His participation in the World Table Tennis Championship in 1971 and friendly encounter with American counterpart Glenn Cowan sparked the start of diplomatic relations between the 2 countries.
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Mao Zedong and Richard Nixon ($\checkmark$) were the leaders of the People's Republic of China and of the US respectively and they met to sign the Shanghai Communique reopening diplomatic relations between the countries while improving the security of Taiwan ($\checkmark$).
