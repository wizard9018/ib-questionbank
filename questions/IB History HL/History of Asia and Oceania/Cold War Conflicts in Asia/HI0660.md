# HI0660

Describe the content of NSC-68 and explain its influence on US foreign policy in Asia.

[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

For describe, the following or similar:

- A document created to report on US foreign policy which stated that Soviet expansionism was the cause of the continuation of the Cold War and reported that the USSR was aiming to dominate both Europe and Asia.

\

[1 marks]

Any **two** of the following for explanation:

 - Outlined possible courses of action against the perceived increase in threat from the USSR including: a return to isolationism, continued diplomatic negotiations with the USSR and a justification for the buildup of weapons to deter Soviet aggression and protect US interests.

 - Introduced the idea of $\text{\textquotedblleft}$rolling back" communism and a stronger response against communist expansion.

 - Increased US defense spending and influenced the expansion of long-term US military presence in Asia including bases in Japan, Korea and the Pacific

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

NSC-68 was the document created by the US National Security Council when asked to report on Cold War policy. The report looked at the role of the 2 superpowers and claimed that the USSR was aiming to extend its control to have domination over Europe and Asia ($\checkmark$). This fear of the increasing spread of communism made US foreign policy stress the need to ensure that non-communist regimes were a more attractive alternative than communism and that US policy should use military measures to combat the threat of communism ($\checkmark$). The policy marked a change from containing to rolling back communism as it meant the US was now prepared to push back communist groups trying to take over non-communist countries and, therefore, marked the beginning of a more aggressive US foreign policy in Asia ($\checkmark$).
