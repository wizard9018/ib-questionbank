# HI0662

Describe **three** ways the Korean War was significant in the context of the Cold War. 

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following: 

 - The Korean War intensified the Cold War and showed that the US was willing to use force to contain communism.

 - It became the first $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hotspot" of the Cold War: until this crisis, there had been no direct fighting between the superpowers.

 - It was the first time the Cold War had spread to Asia: up until this point, US policy had called for intervention in Japan and the Philippines but not Korea.

 - Managing the crisis and war became the first real test for the newly formed United Nations to resolve the situation effectively.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Cold War was a period of tension between nations that did not involve direct military action. The Korean War is significant because it marked a change in this status quo as Korea saw the first fighting in the Cold War. ($\checkmark$) It was led by the UN and was the first real test for the UN in its role as a peacekeeping organisation. ($\checkmark$) This crisis also showed how the Cold War had spread into Asia which was now seen as being a target for Soviet expansionism. ($\checkmark$)
