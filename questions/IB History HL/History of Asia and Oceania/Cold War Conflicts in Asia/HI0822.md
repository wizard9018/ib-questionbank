# HI0822

Identify **two** key causes of the Vietnam War.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The division of Vietnam along the 17th parallel (Geneva Accords, 1954) created a temporary North and South, which led to long-term political tension.
- The rise of communism in North Vietnam under Ho Chi Minh and the U.S. policy of containment led to ideological conflict.
- The South Vietnamese government under Ngo Dinh Diem alienated large portions of the population through political repression, nepotism, and anti-Buddhist policies, fueling support for the Viet Cong.
- U.S. support for Ngo Dinh Diem's anti-communist regime in South Vietnam, despite its unpopularity. The U.S. gradually escalated its involvement by sending advisors, weapons, and aid from 1955 onwards, leading to direct military engagement.
- Failure to hold national elections in 1956, as agreed in Geneva, prevented peaceful reunification. Elections were blocked by Diem and the U.S., fearing a likely communist victory, which escalated tensions.
- Colonial legacy and nationalism: Vietnamese resistance to French colonial rule evolved into a broader nationalist struggle for independence and unification, led by Ho Chi Minh. The First Indochina War (1946–1954) laid the groundwork for further conflict, as it failed to resolve the division or satisfy Vietnamese national aspirations.
- Ideological conflict- Capitalism vs Communism: The Cold War rivalry between the U.S. and USSR made Vietnam a proxy battleground between capitalist and communist ideologies.
- Influence of the Domino Theory: U.S. policymakers believed that the fall of South Vietnam to communism would lead to the spread of communism throughout Southeast Asia.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One key cause of the Vietnam War was the division of Vietnam at the 17th parallel following the Geneva Accords in 1954, which created political tension between the North and South ($\checkmark$).

A second cause was the rise of communism under Ho Chi Minh in the North, which clashed with the United States' policy of containment during the Cold War ($\checkmark$).
