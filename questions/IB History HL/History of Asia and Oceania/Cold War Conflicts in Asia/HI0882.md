# HI0882

Identify **three** causes of the Korean War.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following
- Korea was divided along the 38th parallel in 1945, with the USSR occupying the North and the U.S. the South, creating two rival governments with conflicting ideologies.
- Plans for nationwide elections failed in 1948, leading to the establishment of two separate states, communist North Korea and capitalist South Korea, each claiming legitimacy over the entire peninsula.
- The Korean peninsula became a Cold War flashpoint, as the U.S. and USSR promoted opposing systems, deepening hostility between the two Koreas and fuelling global ideological confrontation.
- Kim Il-Sung sought to reunify Korea through military force, believing the South Korean government was weak and that unification under communism was both achievable and inevitable.
- With backing from Stalin and later tacit support from Mao, North Korea gained confidence to attack, receiving weapons, training, and strategic approval to launch the invasion in 1950.
- The United States was determined to stop the spread of communism in Asia, fearing that a communist victory in Korea would lead neighbouring countries to fall, like dominoes, to communism
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One of the leading causes of the Korean War was the division of Korea along the 38th parallel at the end of World War II. This split the country into two zones, with the Soviets occupying the North and the Americans the South, setting the stage for growing political differences ($\checkmark$).

Another important factor was the failure to hold nationwide elections in 1948. Instead, two separate governments were established, North Korea became a communist state under Kim Il-Sung, while South Korea was led by Syngman Rhee and followed a capitalist model. Both sides claimed to be the rightful government of all Korea ($\checkmark$).

Finally, the broader Cold War rivalry between the United States and the Soviet Union added to the tension. With each superpower backing its respective Korean ally, the peninsula became a hotspot in the global struggle between communism and capitalism ($\checkmark$).
