# HI1163

Describe **three** political aims of the USSR's invasion of Afghanistan in December 1979.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Maintain a Pro-Soviet Regime
    - The USSR aimed to keep a friendly communist government in Afghanistan to secure its southern border and protect the Central Asian republics.
    - Intervention was meant to stop Western or Islamist influence and prevent civil war near Soviet territory.
- Contain Islamic and Western Influence
    - After the Iranian Revolution (1979), Moscow feared the spread of Islamic activism and Western-backed instability.
    - The invasion sought to suppress Islamist insurgency and defend socialist ideology from U.S. and regional influence.
- Assert Soviet Power and Credibility
    - The USSR wanted to show it could defend its allies and maintain control over the socialist bloc.
    - Reflected the Brezhnev Doctrine, justifying intervention to protect socialism from counterrevolution.
- Protect Strategic Interests
    - Afghanistan's position offered potential access to the Indian Ocean and Middle Eastern trade routes.
    - A stable, pro-Soviet Afghanistan would help safeguard Soviet geopolitical influence in Central Asia.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Politically, the invasion aimed to maintain a pro-Soviet regime on its southern border and prevent Afghanistan from collapsing into civil war or falling under Western or Islamist influence ($\checkmark$). Secondly, it aimed to contain the spread of Islamic and Western influence as Moscow feared a regional rise in Islamic activism that could inspire separatism within the Soviet Union's Muslim-majority republics ($\checkmark$). Finally, Brezhnev's leadership sought to project strength and control amid growing global and domestic challenges ($\checkmark$).
