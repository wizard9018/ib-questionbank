# HI1178

Explain **two** impacts of the Chinese intervention in the Korean War after October 1950.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following with explanation:

Immediate military effects
- The surprise Chinese offensives reversed UN gains dramatically.
- The First Phase Offensive (October-November 1950) halted the UN advance near the Yalu River.
- The Second Phase Offensive (November-December 1950) forced UN and South Korean troops into a rapid retreat southward.
- Key events:
    - The fall of Pyongyang (October 1950) was followed by its recapture by Chinese and North Korean forces in December.
    - The Battle of Chosin Reservoir (Nov-Dec 1950) resulted in a costly US withdrawal.
- By January 1951, the Chinese-North Korean forces had captured Seoul for the second time.

Change in the nature and scope of the war
- China's intervention transformed the conflict from a regional civil war into a major Cold War confrontation between the communist and capitalist blocs.
- The war became a stalemate rather than a swift UN victory.
- The intervention prevented the unification of Korea under a pro-Western government.
- It stabilised the northern regime, ensuring the survival of North Korea as a communist state.
- The entry of Chinese forces prolonged the war until 1953, resulting in heavy casualties on all sides.

Impact on US and UN strategy
- The intervention forced the US to abandon its goal of total victory and to adopt a limited war strategy focused on containment rather than rollback.
- President Truman rejected General MacArthur's proposal to bomb Chinese territory and use nuclear weapons.
- MacArthur was dismissed in April 1951 for insubordination, marking a turning point in US civil-military relations.
- The US increased military spending and reinforced alliances across Asia (e.g. Japan, SEATO, Taiwan).

Wider Cold War consequences
- The Chinese intervention intensified East-West tensions, reinforcing the global perception of a unified communist front (China, USSR, North Korea).
- It boosted China's international prestige as the first major communist power to confront the US militarily.
- The USSR supported China indirectly, providing weapons, pilots, and advisers but avoiding open conflict.
- The war contributed to the militarisation of the Cold War, including the rearmament of West Germany and formation of NATO's integrated military structure (1951).
- It solidified the division of Korea at the 38th parallel, which persists today.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
First, China's intervention reversed early UN and U.S. advances into North Korea which forced a retreat to below the 38th parallel and created a prolonged stalemate ($\checkmark$). This showed severe limits to American military power in Asia and marked the beginning of a costly, drawn-out war of attrition ($\checkmark$).

Second, Cold War divisions in Asia were intensified by the intervention  as the regional and global balance of power was altered ($\checkmark$). The United States increased its military presence across the Pacific and strengthened its alliances with Japan, South Korea, and Taiwan. For China, the war enhanced its prestige in the communist bloc, establishing it as a major power willing to confront the West ($\checkmark$).
