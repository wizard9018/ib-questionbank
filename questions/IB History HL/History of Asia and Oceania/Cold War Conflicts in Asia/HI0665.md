## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - The source shows that MacArthur has the power and influence to push the USA further into the conflict.

 - Pushing the US into further action in Korea could lead to a much more global conflict ($\text{\textquotedblleft}$World War II in Asia”). 

 - The caption/title suggests that MacArthur's actions were inappropriate and that he should not have the power to take any further military action.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source A suggests that General MacArthur, as an individual, could push America further into the conflict, shown by his ability to tip the hat, representing the US, over the cliff into $\text{\textquotedblleft}$World War III in Asia" ($\checkmark$). The cartoon also suggests that if he succeeds in pushing America further into the conflict, he will be responsible for starting $\text{\textquotedblleft}$World War III in Asia" as seen by the caption ($\checkmark$).
