## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

A combination of the ideas below that meets the following guidelines:

- To achieve full marks, the answer must include both a point of comparison (similarity) and a point of contrast (difference). 

- A minimum of two clear, valid arguments along with supporting evidence should be given to achieve the full **four** marks.

For comparison, any of the following similarities: 

- Both sources agree that the crisis could lead to a third World War.

    - Source A: ‘World War III in Asia’ is written in the cartoon

    - Source C: Truman states ‘‘We are trying to prevent a third world war…”

- Both sources suggest that the USA is able to influence events in Asia

    - Source A: MacArthur can be seen in the cartoon pushing the US over the edge towards war in Asia.

    - Source C: Truman is clear that the US should be involved when he say,s ‘the best way to meet the threat of aggression is for the peace-loving nations to act together’

For contrast, any of the following differences: 

- The sources disagree about the main aggressor in the conflict.

    - Source A suggests that it is the US and MacArthur as they are seen to be pushing for war

    - Source C suggests that it is the communists who are acting aggressively and a threat to peace.

- The sources disagree about who is responsible for the US’s involvement in Korea:

    - Source A suggests that it is MacArthur who is pushing the US further into the conflict

    - Source C suggests that the US is becoming involved in the conflict with the President’s support to protect itself

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Both sources A and C focus on the USA's role in the Korean Crisis. They both agree that the crisis could cause a ‘third World War’ ($\checkmark\,\text{similarity}$). Source A shows MacArthur potentially pushing the US into “World War III in Asia,” while in Source C, Truman states that the US is “trying to prevent a third world war” ($\checkmark\,\text{evidence}$).  

However, the two sources differ in their perspectives regarding who holds the most responsibility for this ($\checkmark\,\text{difference}$). Source A suggests that by giving MacArthur too much power, the US is becoming the main aggressor and pushing the US further into the conflict. In contrast, Source C argues that “the Communists in the Kremlin” are seeking to “stamp out freedom” and that this move in Korea “is the boldest and most dangerous” yet ($\checkmark\,\text{evidence}$).
