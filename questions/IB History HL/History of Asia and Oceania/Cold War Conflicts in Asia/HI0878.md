# HI0878

Identify **three** aims of the Malayan Communist Party (MCP) during the Malayan Emergency.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Establish a communist state in Malaya: The MCP aimed to overthrow the existing colonial government and replace it with a Marxist-Leninist regime modeled on the Chinese Communist Party.
- Remove British colonial rule: The MCP sought to end British imperial control over Malaya and achieve national independence through revolutionary means rather than negotiated reform.
- Gain control over trade unions and mobilize workers: By infiltrating and dominating trade unions, the MCP hoped to organize strikes and build a mass base of support among urban workers, crucial to their revolutionary strategy.
- Undermine capitalist and imperialist influences: The MCP opposed foreign-owned businesses and Western economic dominance, aiming to dismantle the capitalist system and redistribute resources among the local population.
- Inspire broader communist movements in Southeast Asia: The MCP's struggle was seen as part of a larger regional communist uprising; they hoped success in Malaya would encourage similar revolutions in neighbouring countries like Indonesia and Thailand.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Firstly, the MCP aimed to establish a communist state in Malaya by toppling the British-backed colonial government and replacing it with a Marxist-Leninist regime inspired by the Chinese Communist Party ($\checkmark$).

Secondly, the MCP sought to eliminate British colonial rule altogether, pursuing national independence through armed revolution rather than political negotiation ($\checkmark$).

Finally, the party aimed to gain control over trade unions and mobilize the working class by infiltrating labor movements, organizing strikes, and building widespread support among urban workers to strengthen their revolutionary campaign ($\checkmark$).
