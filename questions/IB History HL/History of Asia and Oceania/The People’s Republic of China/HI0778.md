# HI0778

What does Source B suggest about the role of private enterprises and capitalists in the country?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | An excerpt from Mao Zedong's work titled “The only road for the transformation of capitalist industry and commerce.” Published on September 7, 1953. |

[Source: Mao, Z.D. (1953, September). *The only road for the transformation of capitalist industry and commerce.* Marxists Internet Archive. Retrieved June 16, 2025 from https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-5/mswv5_33.htm. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Private enterprises should not be dismissed from state capitalism.
- Further study of private enterprises is required
- Private enterprises are an asset to the state and play a large part in the economy.
- Capitalists should be educated in patriotism.
- Capitalists could be trained to support the Communist Party.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The source suggests that private enterprises are an asset to the state since they could play an important part in the economy ($\checkmark$). Secondly, the source also mentions that capitalists could be educated in patriotism ($\checkmark$). Lastly, the source suggests that further study of the role of private enterprises in the state's economic development should be conducted ($\checkmark$).
