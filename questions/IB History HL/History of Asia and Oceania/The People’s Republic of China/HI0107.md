# HI0107

Identify **three** principles on which the USA and the People's Republic of China agreed according to Source B. [3 marks]

| | | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** $\\$  | $\hspace{3em}$ | Excerpt from the "Joint Statement Following Discussions With Leaders of the People’s Republic of China" (also known as the Shanghai Communiqué). Published February 27th, 1972.  |

[Source: No author. (1972, February 27th). *Joint Statement Following Discussion With Leaders of the People's Republic of China*. Office of the Historian. Retrieved October 6th, 2024 from https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v17/d203. Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Any **three** of the following:
-   they agreed that countries should respect each other's sovereignty by not attacking or becoming involved in the affairs of other states
-   the international situation is experiencing “important changes and great upheavals”
-   international disputes should be solved peacefully

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
According to Source B, the US and China agreed that countries should respect each other's sovereignty which includes no attacks or aggression toward other states ($\checkmark$) as well as not getting involved in the domestic affairs of other states ($\checkmark$). Moreover, both agree that disputes between countries should be solved peacefully ($\checkmark$).
