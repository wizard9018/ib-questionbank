# HI0780

What does Source D suggest about the communes' impact on the peasants during the Great Leap Forward?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source D** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Extract from the Memoirs of a Chinese Marshal - The Autobiographical Notes of Peng Dehuai (1898-1974). The autobiography was written in response during the interrogations he faced during the Cultural Revolution. In this extract, Peng Dehuai recalled his meeting at the Lushan Conference in 1959 that was called to review the Great Leap Forward. |

[Source: Peng, D.H. (1984). *Memories of a Chinese Marshal.* Marxists Internet Archive. Retrieved June 16, 2025 from https://www.marxists.org/archive/peng-dehuai/1984/memoirspengdehuai.pdf. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- The cadres wrongly believed that the communes were already considered an achievement of Communism.
- The peasants suffered in the people’s commune.
- The cadres were blindly collectivised from the peasants.
- Peasant households had to resort to selling their property to survive.
- The cadres exaggerated their achievements when reporting to the higher-level authorities.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The source shows peasants suffered greatly in the communes during the Great Leap Forward ($\checkmark$). The peasants suffered because the commune cadres collectivised aggressively from the peasants ($\checkmark$). To make matters worse, the cadres exaggerated their achievements of the communes when reporting to the higher-level authorities ($\checkmark$).
