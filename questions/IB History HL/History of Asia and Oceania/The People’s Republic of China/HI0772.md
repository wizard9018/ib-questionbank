# HI0772

Describe **three** key features of China's First 5-year plan from 1953 to 1957.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - The economy was directed by a centralized command system managed by teh State Planning Commission.

 - The government aimed to create heavy industry in China, which they believed was the foundation for a socialist society. The government increased the production of coal and steel. By 1957, 115 million tonnes of coal were produced, and 5.35 million tonnes of steel were produced. 

 - The government focused on developing the country's transportation infrastructure, such as constructing the Yangzi River Bridge at Nanjing.

 - The Soviet Union sent thousands of technical specialists to support industrial development. The government started 694 industrial projects, of which 150 industrial plants were built with Soviet aid.

 - The government created state-owned enterprises by nationalising or merging private enterprises with existing ones.

 - The government focused on industrial development by extracting resources from the agricultural sector, such as stockpiling food supplies by requiring peasants to sell grain at extremely low prices. 

 - Government budget focused on industrial development, while less funding was allocated to social welfare and defence. 

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The First 5-year plan of the PRC from 1953 to 1957 focused on industrial development. The government focused on developing the country's industry by cooperating with the Soviet Union, in which 150 of the 694 industrial projects were assisted by the Soviet Union ($\checkmark$). The government also abolished private enterprises and created state-owned enterprises to centralise industrial production ($\checkmark$). Moreover, the government also directed its budget to expand on the country’s transportation infrastructure, such as the construction of the Nanjing Yangzi River Bridge ($\checkmark$).
