# HI0775

Explain **two** consequences on China's economic development from the Sino-Soviet cooperation up to 1959.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Financial support from the Soviet Union.

     - The Soviet Union provided a loan of $\text{\textdollar}$60 million annually starting in 1950. 

     - The loan included the construction of railways, industrial infrastructure, and materials for reconstruction. 

 - Provision of Soviet specialists 

     - The Soviet Union provided up to 15000 technical advisors to China to support their industrial enterprises in 1954.

     - The Soviet Union assisted China in building its industrial infrastructure through the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$156 Projects,” which involved developing industrial plants in different Chinese cities. Some examples are the Anshan Steel Plant and Nanjing Machine Tool Plant.

     - The Soviet Union also assisted China in expanding its rail network, such as the Lanzhou-Xinjiang Railway, which connects Northwestern China, and the Trans-Siberian Railway in Central Asia. 

 - Transfer of ownership of industrial plants and resources in Northeastern China

     - The Soviet Union returned some of their industrial resources from the Japanese that were previously based in Manchuria to the Chinese government.

     - The Soviet Union renounced its rights to use Port Arthur, which was previously leased by the Russian Empire.

     - The Soviet Union returned the ownership of the Chinese Eastern Railway in 1953

 - Nuclear development

     - The Soviet Union provided nuclear energy engineers to assist China's development in nuclear research.

     - By 1958, nuclear energy development had become a top priority for the country. 

     - The Chinese government cooperated with the Soviet Union to establish the China Institute of Atomic Energy, which focused on designing nuclear power plants and nuclear fuel production. 

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

During the first decade of the PRC's establishment, the Soviet Union offered various forms of assistance to the PRC to assist in its economic development. During this period, the Soviet Union provided financial support by offering an annual loan of $\text{\textdollar}$60 million ($\checkmark$). The loan also included the construction of railways, industrial infrastructure, and reconstruction materials ($\checkmark$). 

In addition to providing financial support, the Soviet Union also sent technical specialists to assist in China's industrial development and economic recovery ($\checkmark$). For example, the Chinese government embarked on the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$156 Projects” that aimed to develop industrial plants in different major cities with Soviet technical assistance, one of these examples being the Anshan Steel Plant ($\checkmark$).
