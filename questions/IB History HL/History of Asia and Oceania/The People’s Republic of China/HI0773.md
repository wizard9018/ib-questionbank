# HI0773

Describe **two** methods used by the Communist Party to restore financial stability in China after the Chinese Civil War.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - The government banned the circulation of foreign currencies.

 - The supply of paper money was reduced to control inflation.

 - The government created a new unified monetary system to centralize government control, such as tightening government budget and credit control. 

 - A centralized banking system was created, the banking industry was nationalized, and only the People's Bank of China could operate in the country. 

 - The wage-point system was introduced as a new unified wage system for workers based on the price of basic items such as rice and oil. 

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The PRC inherited a country that was ravaged by the Second Sino-Japanese War and the Chinese Civil War, which left the country with a severely disrupted economy that included hyperinflation and low industrial and agricultural output. The government first controlled inflation by banning the circulation of foreign currencies and reducing the supply of paper money ($\checkmark$). This was followed by creating a new unified monetary system and a centralized banking system to tighten the control of the government budget and credit ($\checkmark$).
