# HI0781

With reference to its origin, purpose and content, analyse the value and limitations of Source D for an historian studying about the effects of the Great Leap Forward.
[4 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source D** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Extract from the Memoirs of a Chinese Marshal - The Autobiographical Notes of Peng Dehuai (1898-1974). The autobiography was written in response during the interrogations he faced during the Cultural Revolution. In this extract, Peng Dehuai recalled his meeting at the Lushan Conference in 1959 that was called to review the Great Leap Forward. |

[Source: Peng, D.H. (1984). *Memories of a Chinese Marshal.* Marxists Internet Archive. Retrieved June 16, 2025 from https://www.marxists.org/archive/peng-dehuai/1984/memoirspengdehuai.pdf. Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
A combination of the ideas below that meets the following guidelines:
- Both values and limitations must be addressed. If only one is addressed, award a maximum of two marks.
- Origin, purpose and content must all be referenced at some point in the response (either as a value, limitation or both) to receive the full four marks.

For value, any combination of the following:
- The origin of the source is written by Peng Dehuai himself, who was the defence minister of China and also a vocal critic of Mao's Great Leap Forward.
- The content shows Peng's observation of the dismal conditions in the countryside during his tour around China.
- The purpose of the source is to express his frustration towards the poor planning and execution of the Great Leap Forward. The extract reflects his emotion and opinion of the state of the countryside.

For limitations, any combination of the following:
- The memoir only showed Peng's dissatisfaction on the Great Leap Forward and did not mention other outcomes of the campaign or other party official's perspective.
- Peng wrote the autobiography during the Cultural Revolution as a response to his interrogation. This might suggest that the content may be affected by his emotion during his trial.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The source is valuable since it was written by Peng Dehuai himself, who was a vocal critic of Mao's Great Leap Forward policy and personally witnessed the disaster in the countryside when he toured around different provinces ($\checkmark\, \text{VO}$). The content and the purpose of the source is also a value because it shows Peng's emotions and insight on the outcomes of the Great Leap Forward that were unfolding in the countryside ($\checkmark\, \text{VC/VP}$).

The source has its limitations. In terms of the content, the memoir only showed Peng's dissatisfaction and his observations during the Great Leap Forward, other outcomes were not mentioned ($\checkmark\, \text{LC}$). Moreover, the origins could also be limited since Peng wrote this during the Cultural Revolution when he was subjected to continuous interrogation, in which his emotion and mental state might affect the accuracy of the memoir ($\checkmark\, \text{LO}$).
