# HI0646

Describe **four** key features of the Great Leap Forward from 1958 to 1962.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **four** of the following:

 - The Great Leap Forward aimed to boost China's steel production to surpass the UK and the US in the next 15 years. 

 - The government required the countryside to build backyard furnaces to increase steel production. 

 - The Chinese government also aimed to increase agricultural production dramatically by abolishing private ownership and forcing peasants into collective farms. 

 - The traditional social structure in the rural countryside was changed dramatically since all peasants were required to live in communes, in which they had to live a communal lifestyle. 

 - The Chinese government also conducted crop experiments by adopting Lysenkoism to increase the agricultural yield. 

 - Local officials exaggerated food production statistics due to fear of punishment. 

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Great Leap Forward aimed to boost China's steel production to surpass the UK and the US in the next 15 years ($\checkmark$). To reach this goal, the Chinese government mobilized the rural population to produce steel using backyard furnaces, but the steel produced was of poor quality ($\checkmark$). The Chinese government also aimed to increase agricultural production by forcing peasants to live in people's communes and abolishing private farming altogether ($\checkmark$). Due to this, the traditional social structure in the rural countryside was changed dramatically since all peasants were required to live in communes, in which they had to live a communal lifestyle ($\checkmark$).
