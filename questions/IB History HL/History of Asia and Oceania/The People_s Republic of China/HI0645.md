# HI0645

Explain **two** consequences of the PRC's first 5-year Plan from 1952 to 1957.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following reasons with supporting explanation:

Industrialization

 - China was able to develop key state industries by adopting the Soviet model and using the capitalists that did not flee China to contribute to China's economic development.

     - China developed its state-owned automobile industry, such as the First Automobile Works .

     - The government focused on developing coal and steel industries in Northeast China by using the abundant natural resources in the area and also the facilities left by the Japanese. 

     - By 1956, coal production increased by 98$\%$, industrial output increased by 25$\%$ 

 - Infrastructure development

     - The government completed various infrastructure projects under the support of the Soviet Union during this period, such as the Nanjing Yangtze River Bridge

     - The government also expanded its industrial capacity by constructing more industrial production facilities across the country, such as the Daqing Oilfield and Anshan Steel Plan. 

 - Impact on higher education in China

     - The 5-year plan required a large number of technical expertises. The government revamped the higher education program to focus on training more engineers and technicians. 

     - Due to the strong emphasis on technical and science education, the art subjects were discouraged.

 - Impact on Agriculture 

     - The government implemented the Agrarian Reform law that abolished land ownership by confiscating land from landlords, in which some of the landlords were executed in public trials. 

     - The confiscated land was redistributed to peasants. Around 300 million peasants received redistributed land by 1952.

     - Collective farms were established all across the country. By 1957, there were around 800,000 collective farms built. 

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The PRC's 1st 5-year plan had a significant impact on China's economy by laying down the foundation for China's industrial and agricultural development. Firstly, China was successful in creating different state-owned enterprises under the support of the Soviet Union, such as the First Automobile Works ($\checkmark$). During this period, the government was able to develop its coal and steel industries in Northeastern China by using the abundant natural sources and the resources left behind by the Japanese, which led to a significant increase in its overall industrial output by 25$\%$ ($\checkmark$). In addition to developing its foundation for its industries, the 1st 5-year plan also had a significant impact on China's agriculture. In order for the government to centralise its control on agricultural production, all land was confiscated from landlords and redistributed to the peasants, so that by 1952, around 300 million peasants received redistributed land ($\checkmark$). However, the government soon implemented collectivisation by having the state take over land ownership and requiring peasants to join collective farms ($\checkmark$).
