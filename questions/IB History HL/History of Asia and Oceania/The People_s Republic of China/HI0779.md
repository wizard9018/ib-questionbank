# HI0779

With reference to its origin, purpose and content, analyse the value and limitations of Source C for an historian studying the economic outcomes during the Great Leap Forward.
[4 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source C** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Speech at the 6th Plenum of the Eighth Central Committee (December 19, 1958). |

[Source: Mao, Z.D. (1958, December). Speech at the Sixth Plenum of the Eighth Central Committee. Marxists Internet Archive. Retrieved June 16, 2025 from https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-8/mswv8_23.htm. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
A combination of the ideas below that meets the following guidelines:
- Both values and limitations must be addressed. If only one is addressed, award a maximum of **two** marks.
- Origin, purpose and content must all be referenced at some point in the response (either as a value, limitation or both) to receive the full **four** marks.

For value, any combination of the following:
- The source comes from Mao himself, who was involved in most of China's economic planning in the 1950s.
- The content shows Mao's opinion on China's economic development during the 1st and 2nd 5-year plans.
- The content of the sources gives some data on China's economic development at that time.

For limitations, any combination of the following:
- The purpose of the speech is to sway the Communist Party members to support Mao's policies further; therefore, the language might be exaggerated, and the statistics exaggerated.
- The content of the source does not provide further information on the steel production other than the data mentioned in his speech.
- The content only addresses events up until 1958, while the events under the 2nd 5-year plan are still unfolding.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The source is valuable as the speech is directly from Mao himself, who played a significant role behind China's 5-year plans through the 1950s ($\checkmark\, \text{VO}$). The source's content also shows Mao's opinion on the 1st and 2nd 5-year plan ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$). However, the source also comes with limitations. Since the purpose of the speech is to rally more support within the Communist Party, the language might be emotive and the statistics used in the speech might be exaggerated ($\checkmark\, \text{LP}$). Moreover, the content does not provide more information about the outcome while the events under the 2nd 5-year plan were still taking place ($\checkmark\, \text{LC}$).
