# HI0776

Explain **two** causes for the failure of the Great Leap Forward in 1958.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Four Pests Campaign

     - The aim of the campaign is to eliminate rats, flies, mosquitoes and sparrows.

     - The government asked the people to kill any wild birds such as sparrows that ate crop seeds. 

     - The killing of sparrows led to an increase in locust and insect population that destroyed crops. 

 - Lysenkoism

     - The government was influenced by Lysenkoism and made it an official policy in 1958. Lysenkoism consisted of techniques that would increase rice and wheat production. However, Lysenko's theories were worthless and unsuitable for farming.

     - Some of the methods in Lysenkoism such as deep ploughing only hindered the yields in China.

 - Failure of the backyard furnaces

     - Most of the usable steel were only produced in industrial plants

     - The steel produced in the backyard furnaces were of poor quality.

     - Despite the poor quality steel being made, the local authorities did not report the problem and still continued to produce them to reach the quotas designated by the central government.

 - Role of local government officials

     - Many government officials covered up the low agricultural output and the famine, fearing that they might be purged. Instead, the officials lied to the central government that production targets were being met. 

     - Some local officials even placed crops alongside railway tracks when Mao's train passed through during his inspection. 

     - Anyone that dared speak up against the policies imposed by the government were denounced as $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Rightist”, as a result, no one dared to speak up despite the failures.

 - Natural Disasters

     - The Yellow River flooded in 1958 and affected the Henan and Shandong Region, in which over half a million acres of cultivated fields were destroyed. 

     - To help with the flooded areas, the officials assembled a rescue team consisting of farm labourers, which meant that the crops were left unattended and were left to rot. 

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Great Leap Forward resulted in an estimated 30 million people dead in China, making it one of the most devastating famines in modern history. The reason for the failure of the Great Leap Forward could be due to poor planning by local government officials combined with the natural disasters in 1958. Many local government officials during the Great Leap Forward were concerned with meeting the quotas assigned by the central government and therefore exaggerated the production result ($\checkmark$). Moreover, the local government officials did not dare speak up against the policies as anyone that went against the farming methods assigned by the state would be denounced as $\checkmark$Rightists” or “Class Enemies” ($\checkmark$). 

Another factor in the failure of the Great Leap Forward was the Yellow River Flood in 1958, which flooded over a million acres of cultivated land ($\checkmark$). The mismanagement of the flood only worsened the Great Leap Forward when officials diverted 2 million farmers from nearby provinces for the relief effort, which left the crops unattended and rotten, further decreasing the already low agricultural output ($\checkmark$).
