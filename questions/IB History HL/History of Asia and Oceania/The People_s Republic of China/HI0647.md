# HI0647

Explain **two** ways the failure of the Great Leap Forward impacted the leadership of the Chinese Communist Party.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Mao's retreat from politics

     - Mao decided to retreat from politics by stepping down as the President of China in 1959, but still retaining his position as the Chairman of the Communist Party of China. 

     - Mao conducted self-criticism on his policies in the 7000 Cadre Conference in 1962 due to the failure of the Great Leap Forward.  

 - Mao's leadership came under criticism and scrutiny

    - After Mao's resignation, he was criticized by General - Peng Dehuai for his failures in the Great Leap  Forward at the Lushan Conference. Mao later removed Peng from the Minister of National Defence post and installed Lin Biao as the new minister. 

- Rise of Deng Xiaoping and Liu Shaoqi

     - Liu and Deng took over Mao's position and started implementing policies to reverse the damage done by the Great Leap Forward.

     - Liu and Deng implemented policies such as re-introducing private farming and downsizing the people's communes. Peasants were allowed to trade at local markets to sell their surplus goods. 

     - Liu and Deng also imported grain from Australia, diverting food resources to affected regions. Grain exports were halted to direct resources to famine-affected regions.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The failure of the Great Leap Forward had different impacts on the CCP's leadership, such as Mao's leadership in the party. Due to the failure of the Great Leap Forward, Mao went through self-criticism and decided to step down as the President of China, but still kept his position as the Chairman of the CCP ($\checkmark$). Mao was also under criticism from General Peng Dehuai at the Lushan Conference  ($\checkmark$). 

Another impact of the Great Leap Forward on the CCP leadership was the rise of Deng Xiaoping and Liu Shaoqi. Deng Xiaoping and Liu Shaoqi took over Mao's duties and started to implement policies to reverse the damage brought by the Great Leap Forward, such as reintroducing private farming by allowing peasants to trade their surplus goods in local markets  ($\checkmark$). Moreover, they also imported grain from Australia to alleviate the damage in famine-affected regions ($\checkmark$).
