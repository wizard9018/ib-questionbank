# HI0644

Describe the features of the PRC's $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Reunification Campaigns" from 1949 to 1952. 

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - The PRC sent the PLA (People's Liberation Army) to Guangdong province, Tibet and Xinjiang in 1950 to assert their control.

 - Mao was worried that remote outlying regions would revolt against the CCP. For example, Guangdong province was once the origin and stronghold of the Nationalist Party. 

 - Mao feared that Xinjiang, which has a large Muslim population, would either declare independence or be taken by Mongolia or the Soviet Union.

 - Mao justified that Tibet was historically part of China. The PLA was sent to Tibet to take over the region and met minimal resistance due to the PLA having more men and experience in combat. 

 - Shortly after the reunification campaigns, the PRC conducted a "sinicization” campaign to forcefully assimilate Han culture into Tibet. 

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The PRC launched a series of $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Reunification Campaigns" from 1949 to 1952 by sending the PLA to different outlying regions to consolidate the PRC's dominance across China ($\checkmark$). Mao feared that the outlying regions may threaten the new government. For example, Mao feared that Xinjiang, which is a northwestern province dominated by a Muslim population, might be taken over by Mongolia or the Soviet Union ($\checkmark$). Mao also claimed that other outlying regions such as Tibet have been historically part of China, in which the PLA was dispatched to takeover the region without meeting any massive resistance ($\checkmark$).
