# HI0777

What does Source A suggest about the Land Reforms in China?
[2 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  “The life of the peasants is good after Land Reform” by Jin Meisheng, published in 1951. |

![HI0777](images/HI0777.jpg)
[Source: Jin, M.S. (1951). *The life of the peasants is good after Land Reform.* Chineseposters.net. Retrieved June 16, 2025 from https://chineseposters.net/gallery/e15-595. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The land reforms improved the living standard of peasants.
- Mao Zedong was shown as an influential figure behind the land reforms based on the portrait in the poster.
- The peasants are shown to be well-dressed and well-fed.
- The poster shows that the agricultural produce was plentiful.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The source shows that the land reforms brought improvements to peasants' living conditions in the countryside ($\checkmark$). Moreover, the source also seems to suggest that Mao was highly revered based on the portrait that was displayed at the center ($\checkmark$).
