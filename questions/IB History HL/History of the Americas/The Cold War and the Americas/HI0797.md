# HI0797

Using the information provided in Source C, identify **three** reasons for opposition to the McCarran Act.
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source C** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Address at the Dedication of the New Washington Headquarters of the American Legion Public Papers - Harry S Truman. Aug. 14, 1951 Excerpt. |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Because it violates people's rights, damaging democracy and civil liberties
- It violated the First Amendment, the right to free speech and assembly, by requiring groups considered Communist to register with the government and submit information about membership, finances, and activities.
- It violated the Fifth Amendment, which states that the government cannot compel individuals to provide incriminating information about themselves.
- Because it resembled policies of  Nazi Germany.
- Because it violated people's rights in similar ways to previous laws, such as the Fugitive Slave Act, which were also reversed by Presidents who “used their power under the Constitution to reverse the court's rulings”.
- It implies that registration as a Communist or Communist organization will lead to repression and wholesale persecution.
- The Act is based on the same scare tactics that were employed by Senator Joseph McCarthy
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The poster warns people of the dangers of the McCarran Act in that it is breaching American civil liberties, saying $\lq$preserve your democratic rights by registering protests not organizations' ($\checkmark$). The poster also compares the Act to the actions of the Nazis who started with $\lq$outlawing communists' and ended with $\lq$wholescale persecution' ($\checkmark$). Finally, the poster opposes the McCarran Act because it is a violation of the Fifth Amendment which protects individuals against self-incrimination ($\checkmark$).
