# HI0353

Describe **three** reasons for the tension between Truman and Stalin at the Potsdam Conference in 1945.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Differing Ideologies

     - Truman represented democratic capitalism, while Stalin represented communist authoritarianism, leading to clashing visions for post-war Europe.

 - Disagreement over Poland

     - Disagreements over Poland's future government heightened tensions, with Truman demanding free elections and Stalin insisting on a pro-Soviet regime.

 - Reparations

     - Stalin wanted heavy reparations from Germany to rebuild the Soviet Union, whereas Truman opposed excessive penalties to avoid economic instability.

 - Atomic Diplomacy

     - Truman revealed the successful testing of the atomic bomb to Stalin, which increased Soviet suspicions and set the stage for the arms race.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

One reason for tension at the Potsdam Conference was the disagreement over Poland, with Stalin insisting on a pro-Soviet government while Truman demanded free elections ($\checkmark$). Another source of conflict was Truman's decision to inform Stalin about the U.S. testing of the atomic bomb, which increased mistrust and highlighted U.S. military dominance ($\checkmark$). Lastly, there was significant tension over reparations, as Stalin sought heavy payments from Germany, whereas Truman opposed this to avoid economic instability ($\checkmark$).
