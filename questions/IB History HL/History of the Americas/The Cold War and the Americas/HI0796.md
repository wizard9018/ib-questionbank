# HI0796

Describe **two** of the $\lq$show trials' that took place as a part of HUAC's campaign to expose communists in America.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Alger Hiss: A high-level official in the US State Department accused of being a spy.  In January 1950, he was found guilty of perjury for passing classified US information to the Soviet Union during the late 1930s. Hiss was not tried for treason as it was outside the allowed time limit on when legal proceedings for a crime could begin. He was sentenced to five years in prison.
- Klaus Fuchs: was a physicist who worked on the Manhattan Project and was a spy for the Soviet Union, passing atomic secrets to them during the war. He was arrested in 1950, convicted of espionage, and sentenced to 14 years in prison.
- Julius and Ethel Rosenberg:worked on the US Manhattan Project, a US-led atomic research project set up in 1941. They were arrested on charges of stealing atomic secrets for the Soviets and executed in 1953.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Some of the most famous trials were linked to people who worked on the Manhattan Project, an atomic research project that was set up in 1941, such as Klaus Fuchs. Fuchs was the lead physicist on this project and was accused of passing atomic secrets to the USSR during the war and sentenced to 14 years in prison ($\checkmark$).

Julius and Ethel Rosenberg also worked on the US Manhattan Project with Klaus Fuchs. They were arrested on charges of stealing atomic secrets for the Soviets and executed in 1953 ($\checkmark$).
