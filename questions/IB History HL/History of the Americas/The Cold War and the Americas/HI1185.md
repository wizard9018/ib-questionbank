# HI1185

What does Source C suggest about the nature of the Cold War in Asia?
[2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:

Frames the Cold War as a popular, collective struggle:
- The phrase “19 million people united to struggle against $\lq$America's Diem' shows that the war is depicted as a mass movement of ordinary Vietnamese, rather than a conflict between great powers.
- This emphasises national unity and popular participation, implying that the Cold War in Asia was fought through people's revolutions, not just state policies.

Frames the Cold War as anti-imperialist resistance:
- “America's Diem” links the U.S. directly to the South Vietnamese regime, suggesting foreign control and interference.
- The image of a Viet Cong fighters against a warship labeled “USA” portrays America as a foreign invader, not a defender of freedom.
- The language and imagery implies the Cold War in Asia is a continuation of anti-colonial struggles, with the U.S. replacing former European empires as the main adversary.

Military success as proof of legitimacy
- The listing of precise statistics, “We fought 19,711 battles,” “killed 28,108 enemy including 222 American advisers,” “wounded 26,011 enemy including 102 American advisers,” “captured 5,118 enemy including 4 Americans”, conveys that local revolutionary forces were strong and effective.
- These numbers symbolise that the Vietnamese people were capable of defeating a global superpower, reinforcing an image of asymmetric but righteous resistance.

The Cold War as both local and international
- References to “American advisers” highlight direct U.S. involvement, showing that the Cold War in Asia was not abstract but physically fought on Asian soil.
- This conveys that regional conflicts like Vietnam were proxy arenas of the broader U.S.-Soviet rivalry.
- The flag-raising imagery symbolises national independence and socialist identity, suggesting the Cold War in Asia was inseparable from anti-colonial nationalism.

The moral and ideological dimension
- The triumphant tone of the statistics suggest that communist or revolutionary forces had moral superiority.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The image shows a Vietnamese fighter attacking a battleship marked “USA,” symbolising the Cold War in Asia as a direct, armed confrontation with American imperial power ($\checkmark$).

The text declares that “19 million people united to struggle against $\lq$America's Diem'” and that they “fought 19,711 battles,” presenting the conflict as a mass, united struggle for national liberation led by the Vietnamese people ($\checkmark$).
