# HI1181

According to Source A, what was the main purpose of the United States' involvement in Southeast Asia?
[3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- To promote and preserve a peaceful international order
- To defend nations in Southeast Asia from external aggression (“pressures from the North”)
- To uphold international law and protect U.S. rights
- To demonstrate U.S. credibility and deter further communist aggression
- To ensure stability and self-determination for Southeast Asian nations
- To prevent the escalation of communist influence (Domino Theory context)
- To align with postwar U.S. moral identity.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to Rusk, the primary purpose of U.S. involvement in Southeast Asia was to promote a peaceful international order upholding the post-World War II goal of building “a decent world community” where nations could coexist without threatening their neighbours ($\checkmark$).

He argued that American intervention aimed to defend Southeast Asian countries from external aggression, particularly communist pressure and interference from North Vietnam ($\checkmark$).

Rusk also justified U.S. military action as a matter of upholding international law and protecting American rights, explaining that attacks on U.S. ships in the Gulf of Tonkin occurred in international waters where the United States had every right to operate ($\checkmark$).
