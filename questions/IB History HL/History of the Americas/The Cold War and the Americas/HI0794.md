# HI0794

Describe the origins of the Red Scare before 1950.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Long history of opposition to communism: The first Red Scare began in the 1920s after the establishment of the first communist party in 1919 and the fear of increasing Communist influence in the US.
- Harry S. Truman was staunchly anti-communist and implemented policies to repress the spread of Communist and Socialist ideologies.
- Opposing economic ideology: The US viewed Communist economic policies as contrary to the ideas of a free market economy and freedom in general.
- The USSR was actively expanding its influence in Eastern Europe, and many European countries appeared attracted to communism.
- Propaganda: rumours of Soviet spies led to a culture of mistrust and fear among people
- China became communist in 1949: Senator Joseph McCarthy used this to promote fear further, asserting there were spies in the US Government and that anyone claiming to be liberal was supporting Communist ideals, creating paranoia and suspicion.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
After the Second World War, the USSR could be seen to expand their influence in Eastern Europe, which led to the belief that this would threaten American rights and freedoms ($\checkmark$). President Truman was staunchly anti-communist. He saw communism as a threat and implemented policies to repress the spread of Communist and Socialist ideologies ($\checkmark$). This fear was exacerbated by China becoming communist in 1949 and was subsequently used by Senator Joseph McCarthy to promote fear and suspicion about the influence and presence of Communism in the US government ($\checkmark$).
