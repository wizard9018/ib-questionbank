# HI0024

Outline **two** reasons why the Cuban Missile Crisis was significant in the context of the Cold War. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Any **two** of the following:
- It brought the United States and the Soviet Union to the brink of nuclear war, showcasing the real danger of Cold War tensions.
- The crisis led to the establishment of a direct communication line between Washington and Moscow, known as the “Hotline."
- It resulted in a diplomatic agreement where the Soviet Union agreed to remove its missiles from Cuba in exchange for the US promising not to invade Cuba and the removal of missiles from Turkey.
- The crisis demonstrated the importance of nuclear arms control, leading to future agreements such as the Nuclear Test Ban Treaty of 1963.
- It strengthened Fidel Castro's position in Cuba and solidified Cuba's role as a Soviet ally in the Western Hemisphere.

[2 marks]
$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

The Cuban Missile Crisis was significant because it brought the US and the Soviet Union to the brink of nuclear war, emphasizing the peril of Cold War confrontations ($\checkmark$). Moreover, it led to important diplomatic negotiations, such as the removal of US missiles from Turkey, marking a shift towards more cautious handling of nuclear weapons by both superpowers ($\checkmark$).
