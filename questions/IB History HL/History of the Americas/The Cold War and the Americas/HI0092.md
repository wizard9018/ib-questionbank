# HI0092

Explain the significance of the Truman Doctrine in the context of the Cold War.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any of the following:
- It marked the beginning of the U.S. policy of containment, which sought to stop the spread of communism during the Cold War, and laid the foundation for other US Cold War policies, such as the Marshall Plan.
- The doctrine essentially divided the world into two blocs: the capitalist West, led by the U.S., and the communist East, led by the Soviet Union, escalating tensions between the superpowers.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The significance of the Truman Doctrine is that it marked the start of the U.S. containment strategy  during the Cold War that aimed to stop the global spread of communism ($\checkmark$). It also laid the foundation for subsequent Cold War initiatives which sought to counter Soviet expansionism, such as the Marshall Plan ($\checkmark$).
