# HI0799

According to Source B, why was HUAC created?
[2 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  An excerpt from an overview of the House Un-American Activities Committee (HUAC) from the Harry S. Truman Presidential Library website. |

[Source: *House Un-American Activities Committee - Background.*  Harry S. Truman Library and Museum, National Archives. Retrieved 28 May 2025 from www.trumanlibrary.gov/education/presidential-inquiries/house-un-american-activities-committee. Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- To counter the $\lq$perceived threat posed by communism' that emerged after World War II
- To $\lq$identify Communist threats to the United States' as Cold War tensions increased.
- Some people believed it to have been created to protect and $\lq$secure national security' and others that it was a form of control and an attack on democracy.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to Source B HUAC was created to $\lq$identify Communist threats to the United States' resulting from increased Cold War tensions ($\checkmark$). However, some people also believed HUAC was created to $\lq$secure national security' ($\checkmark$).
