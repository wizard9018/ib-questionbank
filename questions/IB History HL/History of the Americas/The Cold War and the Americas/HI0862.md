# HI0862

Describe President Kennedy's Alliance for Progress and identify **two** challenges in implementing the plan.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following description or similar:
- President Kennedy's Alliance for Progress was a 10-year plan that promised aid to Latin American countries to help spur social change and commit to democracy.
[1 marks]

For challenges, any **two** of the following:
- The program expected change to happen too quickly which was a challenge for many Latin American governments.
- The wealthy in Latin American countries were resistant to change that might limit their wealth.
- Much of the funding did not reach the place it was supposed to reach because of corruption in the government.
- Latin American nationalists were resistant to the plan as it was seen as “too American.”
- US economic interests often conflicted with the goals of the Alliance for Progress.
- Many believed that the Alliance was just an anti-Communist tool of the United States
\
[2 marks]

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
President Kennedy's Alliance for Progress was meant to aid Latin American countries committed to democracy and social progress ($\checkmark$). The implementation of the program proved to be difficult in many Latin American countries. First, the program was seen by many as a way for America to push its anti-Communist beliefs onto other countries ($\checkmark$). Also, many of the elites in Latin America were resistant to change that would limit their wealth ($\checkmark$).
