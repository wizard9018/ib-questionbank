# HI0045

Summarize Kennan's perspective of the Soviet Union according to Source E.
[2 marks]

| |  | |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Kennan describes the Soviet Union as “fanatical” and disconnected from “reality” in its behaviors toward the US.
- Kennan suggests that the Soviet Union and the US cannot peacefully coexist.
- Kennan says that the Soviet Union will work to “disrupt” US society and “destroy” the American “way of life” in the name of its own security.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
In Source E, Kennan argues that the Soviet Union is “fanatical” in its beliefs and actions toward the US ($\checkmark$) and that as a result, the US and Soviet Union will never be able to permanently make peace with each other. ($\checkmark$)
