# HI0793

Define the term $\lq$dollar imperialism' and explain how it is connected to the Soviet Union's distrust of aid from the Marshall Plan.
[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
For definition, the following or similar:
- Dollar imperialism was a term used to describe US policy aimed at gaining influence and greater economic control in Europe. Typically used by the Soviet Union and other US rivals as a criticism of their aid policies.

For explanation, any **two** of the following:
- The USSR did not see it as a way of providing financial support to Europe but rather as exerting influence over Europe and making it more dependent on the USA.
- Communist countries were eligible to apply for Marshall Aid, but the conditions attached to it required them to allow the US access to their economic information and justify how they spent the aid. They feared this would give the US the upper hand in future negotiations.
- Both the USA and the USSR knew that economic strength was becoming a crucial factor in the Cold War, and the country with the strongest economy would have the advantage.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Dollar imperialism was a term used by Stalin and the Soviet Union to describe the US's approach to distributing foreign aid ($\checkmark$), which they distrusted because they viewed it as the US seeking economic control and gain in Europe rather than for the purposes of economic recovery after WWII ($\checkmark$).

The USSR also distrusted this policy, as to be eligible for Marshall Aid, they had to agree to certain conditions and allow the US access to their economic information ($\checkmark$).
