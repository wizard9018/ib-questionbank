# HI0792

Identify **three** key factors that led to the establishment of the Truman Doctrine in 1947.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- A desire to protect democracy in Europe
- To prevent countries with economic difficulties from turning to Communism as a way forward, for example, Greece and Turkey
- To take a leading role in world politics
- A means of establishing the USA as a leading economic power
- Growing fear and mistrust of the influence of Stalin and the USSR
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Truman Doctrine was established in 1947 because of the growing fear of the influence of Stalin and the USSR ($\checkmark$). The economic situation at the end of World War II meant that the US could no longer follow a policy of isolationism and was committed to protecting democracy in Europe ($\checkmark$). The US recognised that countries suffering from economic difficulties could be tempted to turn to Communism as it appeared to offer a way forward and a chance of renewed economic stability ($\checkmark$).
