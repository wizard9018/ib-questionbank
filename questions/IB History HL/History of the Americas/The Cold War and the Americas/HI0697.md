# HI0697

Explain **three** conditions for the emergence of Augusto Pinochet's dictatorship in Chile.
[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following with supporting explanation/evidence:
- Socialist and communist labels on Salvador Allende's government.
    - CIA's declassified documents reveal that US diplomats saw the weakening of conservative parties and rise of socialist movements in Chile (Popular Unity Party) as a similar process to the Cuban Revolution.
    - Ideological-based fear of the USA exacerbated by Henry Kissinger's resort to the $\lq$domino theory' and the strength of the constitutional process toward socialism in Chile.
- Active involvement of the USA in Chilean domestic affairs shortly before and after Allende was democratically elected.
    - Covert operations (1964-1972) under the CIA's 40 Committee during Richard Nixon's administration.
    - Track I: undermining of the Chilean political system through political means such as bribery of congresspeople and influence of public opinion through the control of media outlets (late 1960s).
    - Project FUBELT (Track II): promotion of coup of Allende through military means (1970).
- Short-term impact of economic policies implemented by Salvador Allende.
    - Inflation increased by nearly 400% between 1972 and 1973.
    - Wages decreased 38% by 1972.
- Chilean military's shifting alliances.
    - Military's protection of democratic process regardless of US economic offer (October 1970).
    - Failed coup attempt by anti-Allende military faction (June 1973).
    - Weakening of General Prats's influence over the military despite his firm commitment to an apolitical relationship with the elected President (August-September 1973).
\
[6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The emergence of Augusto Pinochet's military dictatorship in Chile may be attributed to both international and domestic factors. In terms of international factors, the broader Cold War context in the Americas, particularly the consolidation of Fidel Castro in Cuba and the US failed attempts to weaken his regime, created conditions for growing fear in the USA of the spread of communism ($\checkmark$). This was exacerbated by the weakening of US-aligned conservative parties in the face of the steady rise by the mid-1960s of socialist political movements such as the Popular Unity Party led by the figure of Salvador Allende ($\checkmark$). Moreover, the success of the latter in the presidential elections consolidated the move toward socialism against pro-capitalist interests of the USA in the country ($\checkmark$). The reaction of the CIA under Nixon's administration was firmly carried out through a combination of a $\lq$stick and carrots' approach such as bribery of congresspeople in 1970 and influence over the military between 1972 and 1973, which ultimately triggered a coup ($\checkmark$).

However, domestic factors also paved the way for the rise of Pinochet. For instance, the short-term impact of inclusive economic policies in employment and national production brought about nation-wide social discontent over and deligitmisation of the central government ($\checkmark$). By 1973, this was reflected in the increase of nearly 400% of inflation and the decrease in wages of 38%. This economic crisis contributed to widening the pre-existent domestic polarisation due to ideological disputes of communism and capitalism ($\checkmark$).
