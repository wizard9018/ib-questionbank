# HI0659

Describe **two** organisations or policies set up to counter communist threats in the US.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- House Un-American Activities Committee (HUAC), 1938: created to investigate alleged disloyalty and unpatriotic behaviour by any US citizen, employee or organisation suspected of Communist ties.
- National Security Act, 1947: set up to support the work of HUAC. It created the office of Secretary of Defense and also established the National Security Council and separate departments for each branch of the armed forces.
- McCarran Act, 1950 - gave the govt the power to investigate spies, and allowed for the investigation and deportation of people accused of promoting Communism or engaging in subversive activities.
- The Alien Registration Act, 1940: required all alien residents over 14 to file a record of their political beliefs. It also made it illegal to promote any ideas that may criticise the government or discuss overthrowing it.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
In 1938, the fear of increased communist influence in the US led to more focus being given to what was being referred to as $\lq$Un-American activities'.

The Alien Registration Act of 1940 was set up to counter communist threats. This required all alien residents over 14 to file a record of their political beliefs. It also made it illegal to promote any ideas that may criticise the government or talk about overthrowing the government ($\checkmark$). Similarly, the National Security Act was set up in 1947, which created the office of Secretary of Defense and also established the National Security Council and separate departments for each branch of the armed forces ($\checkmark$).
