# HI0863

Explain President Kennedy's reasons for creating the Peace Corps and give **two** reasons why it was or was not successful.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following reasons for creation or similar:
- President Kennedy designed the Peace Corps in order to help developing countries and to improve the international image of the United States.
\
[1 marks]

Any **two** of the following to assess its successes:
- Was successful:
    - The Peace Corps helped to develop successful educational systems and train teachers in countries such as India and Ghana.
    - The Peace Corps promoted a positive image of the United States in developing countries as most of them lived modestly.
    - Several newly independent nations benefited from the programs offered by the Peace Corps.
    - Volunteers came back to the United States more aware of global issues.
- Was NOT successful:
    - Volunteers were not always prepared or trained well for their work.
    - Critics saw this as a ploy to spread US influence and contain communism.
    - The success of the program was largely dependent on the reception of the host countries.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
President Kennedy created the Peace Corps to provide help and aid to developing countries while also showing the United States in a positive light ($\checkmark$). Like most programs, there were successes and failures within the program. The program was largely successful in helping the people of developing nations build education systems and learn English ($\checkmark$). However, the success of the Peace Corps often depended on how the host countries perceived the volunteers. Many countries did not embrace the Peace Corps, leaving the programs unsuccessful in some places ($\checkmark$).
