# HI0865

Summarize the reasons for and the effects of US intervention in Chile between 1970 and 1974.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **four** of the following with at least **one** reason and at least **two** effects:
- Reasons
    - The US foreign policy was still very focused on containment, especially following the Cuban Revolution
    - Marxist Salvador Allende's popularity was increasing, which concerned the Americans, as he would be the first democratically elected Marxist.
    - US multinational companies had less influence in Chile and would carry even less if Allende became their leader.
- Effects
    - The United States supported a destabilization campaign through the CIA to undermine Allende's leadership. This would lead to a coup where Allende committed suicide before he could be captured.
    - The military coup was led by Augusto Pinochet, who became a brutal totalitarian.
    - The coup strengthened distrust of America in Latin America, specifically because they were seen as hypocrites who undermined a democratically elected government.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Following the Cuban Revolution, the United States was very focused on stopping the spread of communism in Latin America ($\checkmark$). They were extremely concerned that Salvador Allende would win his election, becoming the first democratically elected Merxist ($\checkmark$). After he won, the United States supported a destabilization campaign that undermined Allende's leadership, leading to a Coup by the Chilean military leader, Augusto Pinochet ($\checkmark$). Pinochet was a violent totalitarian who was responsible for countless deaths in Chile ($\checkmark$).
