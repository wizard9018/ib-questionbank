# HI0852

Explain **two** reasons the United States supported the creation of the Organization of American States (OAS) in 1948.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following with supporting explanation:
- Containment of communism: This aligned with Truman's Cold War containment policy, encouraging regional unity against Soviet influence.
- Asserting US leadership in the Western Hemisphere:  Through the OAS, the US institutionalized its dominance and could influence political and diplomatic actions in the region.
- Creating a collective security framework: The OAS provided a structure for coordinating actions against instability, especially leftist movements or coups.
- Promoting political and economic cooperation: This helped counter Soviet influence by encouraging inter-American diplomacy and trade within a US-led bloc.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One reason the United States supported the creation of the OAS was to promote political and economic cooperation among the countries of the Americas ($\checkmark$). By building stronger diplomatic and economic ties, the US hoped to limit Soviet influence and create a more unified, pro-Western hemisphere ($\checkmark$).

Another reason was to assert greater US leadership in the region ($\checkmark$). The OAS gave the United States a formal mechanism to guide the political direction of the Americas and ensure regional policies aligned with US Cold War goals ($\checkmark$).
