# HI0864

Describe President Carter's beliefs regarding the Panama Canal.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- President Carter believed that the United States's control over the Panama Canal was leftover from the age of imperialism.
- Carter believed that it was important for the Canal to be neutral for international shipping regardless of who controlled the Canal. The Neutrality Treaty between Panama and the United States agreed to this.
- Carter believed that control of the Canal should be relinquished to Panama. The Panama Canal Treaty agreed that control of the canal would be handed over to Panama in 1999.
- He believed that giving control of the canal back to Panama would help to improve US relations in Latin America.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
President Carter believed that Panama should control the Panama Canal and that relinquishing control of the canal would improve US relations in Latin America ($\checkmark$). He also believed that the Canal should be neutral for international shipping, no matter who controlled the canal ($\checkmark$).
