# HI0866

Describe the Cuban Missile Crisis and its influence on the Cold War.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following description or similar:
- The Cuban Missile Crisis was a two week long standoff between the United States and the Soviet Union over the placement of ballistic missiles in Cuba. As a response to the discovery of the Missiles, President Kennedy initiated a naval blockade of Cuba.
\
[1 marks]

Any **three** of the following for influence on the Cold War:
- The Cuban Missile Crisis was the most significant threat of nuclear war to take place during the Cold War and both countries were able to see how quickly each side could escalate.
- Communication between Washington and Moscow improved following this crisis in order to more effectively avoid a larger conflict.
- Even though both sides made concessions to resolve the conflict, Nikita Khrushchev appeared to take the bigger political hit as he agreed to remove the missiles from Cuba publicly.
- President Kennedy quietly agreed to remove missiles from Turkey and redeemed some of his social credit after the disastrous Bay of Pigs invasion.
- Both Superpowers were motivated to pursue nuclear arms agreements.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Cuban Missile Crisis took place after the United States discovered Soviet ballistic missiles in Cuba. As a result of the missiles, the United States initiated a naval blockade of Cuba ($\checkmark$). This crisis came close to escalating the Cold War to a nuclear war and both sides were able to see how quickly escalation could occur ($\checkmark$). Because of this, the superpowers moved away from the policy of brinkmanship and began pursuing nuclear arms agreements  ($\checkmark$). Also, communication between the president of the United States and the First Secretary of the Soviet Union improved following the crisis ($\checkmark$).
