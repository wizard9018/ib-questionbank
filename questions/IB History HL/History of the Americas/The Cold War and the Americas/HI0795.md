# HI0795

How did the Truman Doctrine change American foreign policy?
[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following:
- The doctrine indicated  that the US was no longer following a policy of isolationism and were committed to supporting democratic countries who may be being threatened by communism
- In 1949, the U.S. and 11 other nations in North America and Europe formed NATO to guarantee security for its members
\
[1 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Truman Doctrine changed American foreign policy as it meant that the US was no longer following a policy of isolationism. Focus was now on protecting democratic countries in Europe that were under threat from the influence of communism ($\checkmark$).
