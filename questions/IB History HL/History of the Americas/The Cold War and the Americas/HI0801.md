Using the information in Source C, describe what principles constitute the idea of $\lq$Real Americanism'.
[3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- That freedom of speech should be protected regardless of whether or not we disagree with the opinions of others
- That fair play is the basic principle that underlies the American Constitution.
- That all accusations must be supported by evidence
- That people should believe in the system and that the government is made up of $\lq$the most loyal body of civil servants'
- That no one should live in fear of being falsely accused.
- That the innocent in society are protected.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source C highlights that the principles that make up the idea of $\lq$Real Americanism' are based on the ideas of fair play ($\checkmark$). That people should feel safe and not live in fear of persecution or of being falsely accused ($\checkmark$). The basic principle of Real Americanism is one based on freedom of speech, and this right should be defended $\lq$regardless of how much we may disagree with them' ($\checkmark$).
