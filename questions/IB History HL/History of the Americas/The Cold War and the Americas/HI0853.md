# HI0853

Describe **two** significant impacts of the House Un-American Activities Committee (HUAC) during the early Cold War.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Blacklisting in Hollywood: HUAC's investigations led to the blacklisting of actors, writers, and directors accused of communist ties.
- Limiting political expression: Created a culture of fear in which individuals avoided controversial political opinions to avoid suspicion.
- Reinforcing anti-communist legislation: HUAC's work supported laws like the Internal Security Act, increasing government control over leftist groups.
- Expanding federal loyalty programs: HUAC contributed to the creation of loyalty oaths and background checks for federal employees.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One significant impact of HUAC during the early Cold War was its investigation of the film industry, which resulted in the blacklisting of screenwriters, directors, and actors who were accused of having communist connections. Many of these individuals were unable to find work for years, even without clear evidence against them ($\checkmark$).

Another impact was that HUAC helped to create a political climate in which Americans became increasingly afraid to express dissenting views. People were cautious about attending certain meetings or criticizing the government because they feared being investigated or labeled un-American ($\checkmark$).
