## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
A combination of the ideas below that meets the following guidelines:
- To achieve full marks, the answer must include both a point of comparison (similarity) and a point of contrast (difference).
- A minimum of **two** clear, valid arguments along with supporting evidence should be given to achieve the full **four** marks.

For comparison, any of the following similarities:
- Both sources recognize the threat posed by Communism after the Second World War.
    - Source B: $\lq$the frenzy of perceived threat posed by Communism'
    - Source C: $\lq$a wave of fear and uncertainty.'
- Both look at the idea of civil liberties and the policies being put in place to deal with the threat of Communism.
    - Source B: $\lq$the lines between civil liberties and national security begging to blur'
    - Source C: $\lq$chipping away at our basic freedoms'
- Both sources agree that people were afraid to stand up for their rights.
    - Source B: $\lq$blacklisted', seen as $\lq$untrustworthy' and they could $\lq$lose their job.
    - Source C: describes the situation as a $\lq$dictatorship' where people are living in $\lq$terror of being denounced and slandered'

For contrast, any of the following differences:
- The sources differ in how they regard the impact of the threat.
    - Source B: considers the American government's response to the perceived threat of communism explaining about the creation of the House of Un-American Activities which was ‘charged with identifying Communist threats to the United States'
    - Source C focuses on criticising those in society who undermine the individual's rights in America and the $\lq$keystone of government' in America which is about $\lq$the liberty' of the individual and instead attacking with $\lq$unproven accusations'
- The sources focus on different perspectives of the idea of the perceived threat posed by communism.
    - Source B is informative and gives the background to the perceived threat and the idea of McCarthyism and his $\lq$campaign against alleged communist in the US government and other institutions'
    - Source C is very critical of McCarthyism and the way he is undermining the idea of Americanism whilst $\lq$proclaiming that they are its chief defenders'
- The sources also disagree on the level of communist influence and impact within the US government.
    - Source B suggests that the response by the government is $\lq$necessary to protect national security'
    - Source C states that the reaction is more of a witch hunt because in the US $\lq$we have the finest and most loyal body of civil servants in the whole world.
\
[6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Both Sources B and C show that the policies that are being put in place to deal with the threat of Communism are impacting people's civil liberties ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$). Source B talks about $\lq$the lines between civil liberties and national security beginning to blur' and Source C suggests that they are $\lq$chipping away at our basic freedoms' ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).

Both sources also agree that if a person has been suspected of Communist ties, they would be labeled and find it hard to have a livelihood ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$).
Source B talks about employees being $\lq$blacklisted', seen as $\lq$untrustworthy' and they could $\lq$lose their job. Source C talks about the $\lq$most irresponsible kinds of accusations' and $\lq$character assassination' ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).

The two sources differ in how they regard the impact of the threat ($\checkmark\, \text{difference}$). Source B considers the impact in terms of the response of the American government to the perceived threat of communism, explaining about the creation of the House of Un-American Activities which was $\lq$charged with identifying Communist threats to the United States' while Source C focuses on criticising those in society who are undermining the $\lq$keystone of government' in America which is about $\lq$the liberty' of the individual and instead attacking with $\lq$unproven accusations' ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).

The sources also disagree on the level of communist influence and impact within the US government ($\checkmark\, \text{difference}$). Source B highlights the period of 1950-1954, stating that McCarthyism was $\lq$necessary to secure national security.' In contrast, Source C states that $\lq$we have the finest and most loyal body of civil servants in the whole world' ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).
