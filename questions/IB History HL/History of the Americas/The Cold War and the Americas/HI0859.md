# HI0859

According to Source B, identify **three** priorities of U.S. Cold War strategy under the New Look policy.
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Excerpts from NSC 162/2 on 30 October 1953. This policy is the basis for what is known as Eisenhower's $\lq$New Look' Policy. |

[Source: National Security Council (October 30, 1953) *NSC 162/2 Note by the Executive Secretary to the National Security Council on Basic National Security Policy.* Retrieved July 16, 2025 from https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1952-54v02p1/d101. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Reliance on atomic capability to counterbalance Soviet power. The United States placed strategic emphasis on its atomic weapons as the primary means of deterring the Soviet Union.
- Dependence on allied cooperation to meet defense needs. U.S. defense strategy required active participation and political support from allied nations.
- Need for overseas bases to support strategic air power. Effective deployment of air power depended on securing military bases in allied countries.
- Emphasis on maintaining economic strength while defending against communism. Military planning was shaped by the need to avoid excessive spending that could weaken the U.S. economy.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to Source B, one priority of U.S. strategy was to use its nuclear arsenal as the foundation of its global defense plan ($\checkmark$). Another focus was ensuring close collaboration with friendly nations, since the U.S. couldn't maintain its position alone ($\checkmark$). A third goal was to secure access to foreign military bases in partner countries to make its air forces effective in any future conflict ($\checkmark$).
