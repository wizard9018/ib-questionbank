# HI0104

Describe the Cuban Missile Crisis.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Any **three** of the following:

- A crisis in 1962 involving the USSR, Cuba and the US.
- It was motivated by Castro's fear of a US invasion.
- It was motivated by Khrushchev's desire to establish a nuclear “balance” with US missiles set up in Turkey aiming at the USSR.
- Kennedy tried to solve it by imposing a naval blockade of Cuba.
- In the end, Khrushcev agreed to no nuclear missiles on Cuba if the US removed its missiles from Turkey and promised to not try to invade Cuba.
- An example of the Sino-Soviet split as Mao openly criticised Khrushchev for this handling of the crisis.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Cuban Missile Crisis is the closest the world has been to human made destruction. It happened when the USSR tried to set up nuclear missile bases in Cuba to counter the US  ($\checkmark$) threat to its own territory with American missile bases in Turkey ($\checkmark$). Cuba's Fidel Castro agreed to this since he was afraid the US would try to use force to bring down the revolution in Cuba after the failure of the Bay of Pigs Invasion ($\checkmark$). The situation was solved peacefully with Kennedy agreeing to remove the bases in Turkey and to not invade Cuba ($\checkmark$).
