# HI0996

Explain **two** reasons for Fidel Castro's visit to Chile during Salvador Allende's presidency.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following with supporting explanation:

 - Political solidarity: There was an ideological affinity between Castro and Allende, as both promoted socialist changes beyond the different paths by which they came to power.

 - Strengthening diplomatic relations: Until Allende took office, Cuba was isolated in the Americas, so it sought Chile's alliance, a feeling that was reciprocated, as Allende also needed allies on the continent.

 - Exchange of experiences on socialism: Castro was interested in seeing the development of the so-called "peaceful path" to socialism, and both wished to discuss strategies for its growth and for addressing potential future problems.

 - Symbolic and propaganda value: the meeting promoted regional unity of anti-imperialist forces to confront interference from foreign powers like the United States.

 - Regional implications: The meeting sought to inspire some leftist and anti-imperialist movements that were emerging in other Latin American countries, showing that socialism was possible on the continent.

 - Economic and trade cooperation: Both heads of state sought new allies and trade agreements to address the isolation that the United States was promoting toward both countries.

 - Popular participation and mass mobilization: Fidel Castro's extensive visit also sought to strengthen the support of workers, students, and peasants for the Allende government and the reforms it promoted.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

They need economic cooperation and the development of trade agreements due to the isolation promoted by the USA towards both countries as a strategy to undermine the socialist governments of Salvador Allende in Chile and Fidel Castro in Cuba. ($\checkmark$) They were facing delicate financial situations that limited their economic growth and political influence. During Salvador Allende’s presidency, the US sought to destabilize his socialist government through monetary measures. In the case of Cuba, the US imposed an economic embargo on Cuba. So they needed to establish alliances that would allow them to strengthen their economies and reduce their dependence on antagonistic foreign powers. ($\checkmark$)

The meeting also aimed to inspire emerging leftist and anti-imperialist movements across Latin America. ($\checkmark$) Both leaders wanted to send a message of hope and possibility, letting people know that creating a socialist society was realistic and achievable for Latin American nations, not just an abstract dream. This was an important message because numerous anti-imperialist movements marked the period between 1960 and 1970, challenged foreign influence—especially that of the United States—and sought to regain economic, political, and cultural sovereignty for their countries. ($\checkmark$)
