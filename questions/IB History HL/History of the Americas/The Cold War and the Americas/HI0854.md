# HI0854

Compare and contrast the “New Look” foreign policy of President Eisenhower with Truman's Cold War strategy in the Americas.
[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
For comparison, any of the following similarities:
- Both aimed to contain the spread of communism in the Western Hemisphere.
- Both used alliances and hemispheric cooperation (e.g., OAS) to support US influence in Latin America.
- Both supported authoritarian regimes if they aligned with US anti-communist goals.

For contrast, any of the following differences:
- Truman emphasized conventional military aid and troop deployments (e.g., Korean War), while Eisenhower's New Look emphasized nuclear deterrence and “massive retaliation.”
- Truman was more cautious, while Eisenhower significantly expanded CIA operations in Latin America (e.g., Guatemala 1954).
- Truman used broad economic aid programs (e.g., Point Four), while Eisenhower emphasized strategic spending and cost-cutting in defense.
\
[6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Both Truman and Eisenhower aimed to prevent the spread of communism in the Americas, aligning their foreign policies with the broader Cold War goal of containment ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$). Each president supported regimes that were anti-communist, even if they were authoritarian, as seen in US backing for conservative governments in countries like Cuba (pre-1959) and Nicaragua ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).

However, their strategies differed significantly. Truman relied more on conventional military approaches, such as providing direct military aid and deploying U.S. forces to resist communist expansion ($\checkmark\, \text{difference}$). In contrast, Eisenhower shifted toward a strategy of nuclear deterrence through the policy of “massive retaliation,” while also expanding the use of covert CIA operations to achieve U.S. objectives without large-scale military involvement. This is seen in the 1954 CIA-backed coup in Guatemala, which overthrew the democratically elected President Jacobo Árbenz ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).

They also differed in economic strategy. Truman promoted broader developmental goals through initiatives like the Point Four Program, which aimed to support modernization in Latin America ($\checkmark\, \text{difference}$). Eisenhower, however, prioritized economic restraint and tied foreign aid more directly to strategic objectives, such as securing military bases and backing anti-communist regimes that aligned with U.S. interests ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).
