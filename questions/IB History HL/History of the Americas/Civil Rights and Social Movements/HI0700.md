# HI0700

With reference to the expressions “RACIAL ACCORD” and “EXTREMISTS” in Source B, describe **two** characteristics of the US civil rights movement in the 1960s.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following alongside own knowledge of historical context:
- Racial accord may be understood as the initial progress of the US civil rights movement toward political equality.
- Racial accord may be associated with growing societal pressure from non-violent protests.
- Extremists may refer to the limited reach of legislative decisions beyond state-level.
- Extremists may reflect the persistent violence exerted by US authorities or organizations in an attempt to hinder civil rights entitlements for African American people.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
As seen in the growing flower in the cartoon, the engagement in non-violent demonstrations led by peaceful leaders such as Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. reflected initial progress toward pro-civil rights legislation for African-American people and wider social reach advocating for political equality ($\checkmark$).

Although peace initiatives initially gained traction in the legislature through anti-segregation agreements at the subnational level, persistent wider social violent actions such as riots and bombs by extremist groups further challenged the civil rights movement's aims ($\checkmark$).
