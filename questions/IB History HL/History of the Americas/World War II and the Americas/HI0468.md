# HI0468

Describe Franklin D. Roosevelt's Good Neighbor Policy.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

**One** mark for a definition similar to:  

 - No sovereign state in the region, including the United States, had the right to intervene in the affairs of other sovereign states.

Any **two** of the following additional details:

 - It was proposed by the US, under the presidency of Franklin D. Roosevelt,  at the 7th Pan American Conference held in Montevideo in 1933.

 - It was a departure from the former US policy of intervening militarily in Latin American countries to protect US interests or those of its citizens.

 - As it was put into effect, the US withdrew its marines from Haiti and Nicaragua, and eliminated the Platt Amendment that had limited Cuba`s sovereignty since its independence. 

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Good Neighbor Policy was declared at the 7th Pan American Conference held in Montevideo in 1933, by President Franklin D. Roosevelt ($\checkmark$). He proposed that no sovereign state in the region, including the United States itself, had the right to intervene in the affairs of other sovereign states ($\checkmark$). This was an important shift from the previous U.S. policy of military intervention in Latin American countries to protect American interests ($\checkmark$).
