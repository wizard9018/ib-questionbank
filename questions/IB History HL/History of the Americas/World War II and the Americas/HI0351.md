# HI0351

What, according to Source B, were the reasons for NOT using the atomic bombs on Japan?

[3 marks]

$\\$

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source B** $\\$ $\hspace{5em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Letter from President Harry S. Truman to Senator Richard Russell in response to his call for the further use of atomic weapons to destroy Japan, August 9, 1945.

[Source: Truman, H. S. (1945, August 9). *Correspondence Between President Harry S. Truman and Senator Richard Russell*. National Archives Catalog. Retrieved February 8, 2025 from https://catalog.archives.gov/id/40020053. Public Domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

The following:

 - Because the US should not act as the Japanese had done

 - Because Japan will surrender with the USSR`s entry into the war

 - Because Japan`s women and children are not responsible for the stubbornness of their leaders.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

According to Source B, the United States should not use atomic weapons to destroy whole populations of Japan since it should not behave as beastly as the Japanese had done ($\checkmark$). Truman recognizes that the ones guilty were the stubborn Japanese leaders and not the women and children, who would also suffer the effects of the atomic bombs ($\checkmark$). He also says it was not really necessary to use them as he thought Japan would surrender once the USSR entered into WWII ($\checkmark$).
