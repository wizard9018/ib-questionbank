# HI0591

Describe US participation in WWII.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Initially, the US participated by providing the Allies with supplies through the Cash and Carry policy and then the Lend-Lease policy. For example, the Lend-Lease Act granted the USSR 18,000 airplanes, 1,500 tanks, and more than 500 ships.

 - After 1941, the US was responsible for carrying out the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$island hopping” strategy in the Pacific to defeat Japan. 

 - In both the European theater and the Pacific theater, it was responsible for the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$carpet bombing” of major cities. Estimates about damage to urban areas in Japan vary, but many indicate well over 40$\%$ of urban areas were destroyed with a higher percentage in Tokyo. American troops participated in major battles on the Italian and Pacific fronts and in the Normandy invasion. In all, they sent about 16 million soldiers, 1 million of whom were casualties. 

 - The US was responsible for effectively ending the war with the use of atomic bombs against Japan. 

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The United States initially supported the Allies by supplying resources through the Cash and Carry policy and later the Lend-Lease Act. By the Lend-Lease Act, 18,000 airplanes, 1,500 tanks, and over 500 ships were provided to the USSR  ($\checkmark$). After officially entering the war in December of 1941, American forces were responsible for large-scale $\text{\textquotedblleft}$carpet bombing" campaigns in both Europe and the Pacific, with devastating effects. In Tokyo alone it is estimated that around 60$\%$ of the city was destroyed.  ($\checkmark$). Ultimately, the U.S. brought the war to an end by forcing Japan to surrender after the use of atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki ($\checkmark$).
