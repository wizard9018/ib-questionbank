# HI0347

What does Source A suggest about the impact of the atomic bomb on Nagasaki?

[2 marks]

$\\$

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Nagasaki, Japan after the atomic bomb detonation. Photograph taken March 17, 1948.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - The whole city was destroyed

 - The impact of the destruction was felt years after the end of the war

 - Civilian buildings were also destroyed 

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

From Source A we can learn that the atomic bomb had a long-lasting impact on Nagasaki as this photograph was taken in 1948 and the city is not rebuilt ($\checkmark$). It is evident from the tori (temple entrance) that even religious buildings were hit, not only military targets ($\checkmark$).
