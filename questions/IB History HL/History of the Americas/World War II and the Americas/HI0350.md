For comparison, any two to four of the following similarities: 

 - They both refer to the Japanese as beasts

     - Source B: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$because they are beasts” 

     - Source C: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$When you have to deal with a beast” 

 - Both refer to Japan`s brutality in war 

     - Source B: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Japan is a terribly cruel and uncivilized nation in warfare” 

     - Source C: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$unwarranted attack by the Japanese on Pearl Harbor and their murder of our prisoners of war”

For contrast, any two to three of the following differences:

 - The sources differ on whether the US should behave as brutally as Japan

     - In Source B Truman says, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ I can't bring myself to believe that, because they are beasts, we should ourselves act in the same manner.” But in Source C he says the opposite: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$When you have to deal with a beast you have to treat him as a beast”

 - The sources differ on the reasons the Japanese will surrender. 

     - Source B focuses on the participation of the USSR $\text{\textquotedblleft}$It is my opinion that after the Russians enter into the war the Japanese will very shortly fold up,” while Source C focuses on the use of bombs by the US: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The only language they seem to understand is the one we have been using to bombard them.$\text{\textquotedblleft}$ 

 - The sources differ in how they refer to the Japanese. While Source B identifies the 'pigheadedness' of the leaders of a nation$\text{\textquotedblleft}$ and recognizes the innocence of Japanese women and children, Source C just refers to the Japanese as a monolithic group.

 [6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Both Sources B and C reveal similar things about Truman's justification for the use of atomic weapons on Japan. One of the reasons that seem to validate the dropping of atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki is the consideration of the Japanese  $\text{\textquotedblleft}$beasts", which is how they are characterized both in Source B: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$because they are beasts” and in Source C: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$When you have to deal with a beast” ($\checkmark$ similarity and evidence ). In both sources, it is clear that they are considered beasts because of their behaviour as Source B says they are a $\text{\textquotedblleft}$terribly cruel and uncivilized nation in warfare," while Source B highlights the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$unwarranted attack by the Japanese on Pearl Harbor and their murder of our prisoners of war." ($\checkmark$ similarity and evidence). By bringing up this behavior, Truman justifies the use of atomic weapons.

 

However, despite coming from the pen of the same person, the sources also disagree on the reasons for the use of atomic weapons. A clear contrast concerns how the US should respond to Japanese brutalities ($\checkmark$ difference). While in Source B Truman can't believe $\text{\textquotedblleft}$that, because they are beasts, we should ourselves act in the same manner," in Source C, he says the opposite by stating, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$When you have to deal with a beast you have to treat him as a beast." ($\checkmark$ evidence) One of the reasons why they disagree is because they identify different groups of people as responsible for Japanese atrocities ($\checkmark$ difference). Source B identifies the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$'pigheadedness' of the leaders of a nation$\text{\textquotedblleft}$ and recognizes the innocence of Japanese women and children. In this view, the indiscriminate use of atomic weapons would be unjustified due to their impact on civilians. Source C just refers to the Japanese as a singular group and thus views the destruction of entire cities as valid ($\checkmark$ evidence). Moreover, the sources differ on what they believe will lead to Japan's surrender ($\checkmark$ difference). Source B suggests that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$after the Russians enter into the war the Japanese will very shortly fold up," therefore atomic weapons are not really needed while Source C emphasizes the impact of U.S. bombings, stating, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The only language they seem to understand is the one we have been using to bombard them." ($\checkmark$ evidence)
