# HI0250

"Nuclear weapons were necessary for the US to win the war with Japan." Provide **three** arguments that support this perspective.
[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following arguments with its supporting evidence.
- Nuclear weapons were necessary to avoid American casualties.
    - American leaders were afraid that an invasion of the Japanese home islands would require similar US casualties.
- Conventional strategic bombing was not enough to force Japan to surrender.
    - Conventional bombing against Japanese urban centers had caused huge losses to the Japanese, but it was not enough to cause their surrender.
- The use of atomic weapons would mean a faster end to the war.
    - The Japanese emperor surrendered 6 days after the bombing of Nagasaki.
- The use of atomic weapons would actually mean fewer overall casualties.
\
[6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Many at the time, including the US high command and President Truman argued that atomic bombs had to be used to end the war as quickly as possible to save American lives ($\checkmark$). The previous battle for Okinawa, the last stepping stone before reaching the Japanese home islands, had resulted in 65,000 U.S. casualties, or 35% of the American forces deployed. If those numbers held, it was expected an invasion of the Japanese home islands would result in around 250,000 American casualties  ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$). The death toll for the Japanese of the use of conventional weapons would not be much better ($\checkmark$). After only 1 air raid in March of 1945 over Tokyo the death toll had been 80.000 Japanese civilians ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$). The US government also feared that the Japanese would not surrender since they had not done so yet despite air raids that had destroyed 50% of Japanese urban areas ($\checkmark$). In this context, the use of atomic weapons appeared to offer a solution that minimized further loss of life for the Americans while delivering a shock that would make the Japanese surrender. In fact, the Japanese Emperor called for surrender on 15 August, 9 days after the bombing of Hiroshima ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).
