# HI0471

Identify the most important agreements reached at the Inter-American Conference for the Maintenance of Peace, Buenos Aires, 1936.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - The countries of the continent agreed to meet and decide on a joint action plan in case of a threat to the peace of the hemisphere

 - The countries of the continent agreed to remain neutral in case of extra continental conflicts

 - The nations condemned intervention by one State in the internal or external affairs of another State

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Since the end of the 19th century, the countries of the Americas met periodically in a series of Pan American Conferences to discuss issues that affected the region. One such conference was the one held in Buenos Aires in 1936. Some of the more important results of the conference were that the countries of the continent agreed to meet and decide on a joint action plan in case of a threat to the peace of the hemisphere ($\checkmark$). This was important as tensions were rising in Europe and Asia and there was the threat that they would spill over to the rest of the world. To prevent this, at least in the Americas, the countries of the region agreed to remain neutral in case of an extra continental war ($\checkmark$).
