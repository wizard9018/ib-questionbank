# HI0588

Describe the workings of one US government agency that helped organize the economy for the Second World War.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** details of **one** of the following government agencies:

 - Office of Production Management (OPM, 1941-1942)

     - Created by Roosevelt in January 1941, before the passage of the Lend-Lease Act and the declaration of war by the US. 

     - It aimed to regulate the production and supply of materials necessary for national defense.

     - It was not always successful in avoiding supply chain bottlenecks.

 - The War Production Board (WPB, 1942-1945)

     - It replaced the Office of Production Management after the Japanese attack on Pearl Harbour. 

     - Its job was to organize the production of war materials and ensure war industries had the necessary raw materials as well as to help .

     - It could not always ensure a constant flow of raw materials such as aluminium but it was partly responsible for a great increase in production of war materials. For example, production of aircraft in 1944 was 5 times that of 1941. 

 - War Manpower Commission (WMC, 1942-1945)

     - It was set up by President Roosevelt in1942 due to tensions between the military and the war industry. 

     - Its aim was to balance the manpower needs of agriculture, industry and the armed forces. 

     - It was responsible for publicity campaigns aimed at women to convince them to join the war industries or essential civilian services

     - It set up a Training Program to train unskilled workers to cover the shortages caused by those who had enlisted. 

     - It was able to train more than 1.5 million workers thus covering the shortage of skilled labor. 

 - Office of Price Administration (OPA, 1941-1947)

     - It was set up to control prices and rents to avoid inflation

     - It placed maximum prices on 90% of retail food prices and residential rents. 

     - It introduced rationing to control demand of scarce goods. It rationed things like tires, gasoline, fuel oil, coal, firewood, nylon, silk, and shoes.

     - Its measures resulted in an average inflation of 3.5% for the years 1942-1946. 

 - Office of War Mobilization (OWM, 1943-1945)

     - Managed the coordination among various wartime agencies to ensure smooth production.

     - Helped prioritize military contracts and ensure resources were used efficiently.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Office of Price Administration was established to control prices and rents in order to prevent inflation during World War II ($\checkmark$). It set maximum prices on 90$\%$ of retail food items and residential rents while also introducing rationing to manage the demand for scarce goods ($\checkmark$). Thanks to the action of the Office of Price Administration, the average inflation rate was only 3.5$\%$ between 1942 and 1946 ($\checkmark$).
