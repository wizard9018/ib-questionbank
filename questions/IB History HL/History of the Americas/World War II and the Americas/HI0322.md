# HI0322

Evaluate the impact of WWII on the Mexican economy.

[5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Since the command term is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$evaluate”, the response must contain both positive and negative results to reach more than four marks. To reach full marks, there must be a conclusion. To respond to an $\text{\textquotedblleft}$evaluate” command term, it is valid to say that the whole goal was not reached as something negative. 

Positive results:

 - US loan of $\text{\textdollar}$40 million to the Mexican government

 - US guaranteed to purchase Mexican silver 

 - The government encouraged the population to increase its productivity to assist the Allied cause. This led to an 8$\%$ increase in GDP per year between 1940 and 1945, 

 - The needs of the war and the war itself encouraged industrialization. Mexico replaced European and American production in Central America and the West Indies. For example, textile exports increased from less than 1$\%$ of Mexico's total exports in 1939 to 32$\%$ in 1945-46.

 - US assistance led to improved infrastructure (highways and ports) which in turn helped economic development. For example, in December 1942 the US sent a Technical Mission to help rehabilitate Mexico's railway system. 

 - Thanks to the Bracero Program, which was revived in 1942, Mexico received remittances from the Mexican citizens that were given short term work permits to the US. For example, in 1944, 62,200 Mexican citizens were allowed to work in the US thanks to this program. As part of their contracts, braceros agreed to have ten percent of their wages withheld and placed in a fund controlled by the Mexican government.

Negative results:

 - Inflation increased an average 18.5$\%$ during each of the war years. 

 - Large government contracts gave the opportunity for increased corruption

 - Mexico became dependent on the US market. By 1945 sent 90$\%$ of its exports to the United States.

\

[5 marks]

Concluding remark:

 - Responses are likely to conclude that the overall economic effects of WWII were positive for Mexico.

 - The strongest responses will mention the overall positive results while acknowledging the negative ones as well. 

\

[1 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

World War II presented significant economic benefits to Mexico. First, the US was willing to assist Mexico's economic development so it could produce the necessary war materials for the war effort. In that light, the US granted the Mexican government a loan of $\text{\textdollar}$40 million ($\checkmark$). The higher demand the war generated as well as the government's mobilization of the economic capabilities to support the war led to a significant growth in Mexico's economy. Between 1940 and 1945 it had a sustained 8% average annual GDP growth ($\checkmark$). A great part of this growth had to do with the industrial sector. As US and European consumer goods production was virtually halted, Mexico stepped in to cover that demand in the markets of Central America and the West Indies. This is evident in the fact that the textile industry was now a key component of Mexico's exports. Whereas in 1939 textile goods were only 1% of total exports, in 1945-1946 they were 32% of the total ($\checkmark$). 

However, the war also brought economic challenges. Like in most countries, war leads to inflation. This was also the case in Mexico, where the war years had an average inflation rate of 18.5$\%$ ($\checkmark$). Furthermore, Mexico became heavily dependent on the U.S. market, with 90% of its exports directed to the United States by 1945 ($\checkmark$). Therefore, its economic prosperity became tied to the United States.

Despite higher inflation and dependence, the overall economic impact of WWII on Mexico's economy was positive. The important growth in its GDP and the development of its industries outweigh the negative consequences ($\checkmark$ conclusion).
