# HI0292

Describe **three** economic impacts of WWII on the United States.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - WWII effectively ended the Great Depression in the United States. While the unemployment rate was 15$\%$ in 1940, it fell to 2$\%$ in 1945. 

 - The Price Administration Office limits the price of 90$\%$ of products and this results in an average inflation of only 3.5$\%$

 - A system of strict rationing was introduced in 1942 to keep up war production and supply of the Allies` troops

 - The US spent \$341 billion, including $50 billion for lend-lease supplies that went to its allies. 

 - The US economy emerged from the war as the dominant economy of the world. It held two-thirds of the world's gold supply and produced over 60$\%$ of the world's industrial output. 

 - America's economic influence during the war and immediately after it allowed the US to make its allies accept tariff and monetary agreements that opened much of the world, especially the colonial empires of Europe, to American trade and investment.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

WWII had a positive impact on the US economy. The necessities of war put an end to the decade-long Great Depression. Evidence of this is the fact that the unemployment rate was 15$\%$ in 1940, and it fell to $2\%$ in 1945 ($\checkmark$). The US also emerged from the war as the dominant economy of the world, producing about 60$\%$ of the world's industrial output by 1945 ($\checkmark$). This economic might and the fact that it had assisted its allies economically as well as militarily both during and after the war gave US leaders the possibility of influencing US aid recipients to accept tariff and monetary agreements that opened much of the world, especially the colonial empires of Europe, to American trade and investment ($\checkmark$).
