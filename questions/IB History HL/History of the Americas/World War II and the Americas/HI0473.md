# HI0473

What does Source A suggest about US involvement in WWII in 1940?
[2 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Cartoon by Clifford Berryman, September 2, 1940. Berryman titled this cartoon “Uncle Sam becomes Democracy's Arsenal,” and he made it for Labor Day. Uncle Sam comments, "every day will be Labor Day from now on." |

![HI0473](images/HI0473.jpg)
[Source: Berryman, C. K. (1940, September, 2). Uncle Sam becomes Democracy's Arsenal. National Archives NextGen Catalog. Retrieved February 4, 2025 from https://catalog.archives.gov/id/6012217. Public domain.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The US will switch its industrial production to meet the needs of war
- The US will make weapons for the democratic nations
- Since Uncle Sam says, “from now on,” we can infer that the US had not been producing weapons before this time
- The US will substantially increase weapon production.
- The weapons will be of good quality as they are tested
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
From Source A, we can learn that before 1940 the US was not getting ready to participate in WWII. In the cartoon, Uncle Sam says, “every day will be Labor Day from now on,” so we can infer that the United States had not been producing weapons before this time ($\checkmark$). The cartoon also suggests that weapons produced by the US will be of good quality, as depicted by the teddy bear testing the weapons ($\checkmark$).
