# HI0348

What, according to Source C, were the reasons for using the atomic bombs on Japan?

[3 marks]

$\\$

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source C** $\\$ $\hspace{5em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Letter from President Harry S. Truman to Samuel Cavert (General Secretary of the Federal Council of the Churches of Christ in America) in response to his call for an end to the use of atomic weapons, August 11, 1945.

[Source: Truman, H. S. (1945, August 11). *Correspondence Between Samuel Cavert and President Harry S. Truman*. National Archives Catalog. Retrieved February 8, 2025 from https://catalog.archives.gov/id/296762538. Public Domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

The following:

 - As revenge for the attack on Pearl Harbour and for Japan's treatment of American prisoners of war

 - It is the only way the Japanese will understand they need to surrender

 - The Japanese are beasts

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

From Source C, we can learn that, according to Truman, the US had to use atomic bombs against Japan since it was the only thing they would understand ($\checkmark$). Moreover, the President reminds Mr. Cavert of Japan's attack on Pearl Harbour and its treatment of American prisoners of war, showing his desire to avenge these horrors ($\checkmark$). Truman also refers to the Japanese as $\text{\textquotedblleft}$beasts,” using it as a justification to treat them as such ($\checkmark$).
