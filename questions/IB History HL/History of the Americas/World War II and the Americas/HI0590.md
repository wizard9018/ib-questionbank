# HI0590

Describe Brazilian participation in WWII.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Brazil's navy helped combat German U-boats in the south and central Atlantic Ocean. 

 - The USA built airfields in Brazil to be used for the war effort. For example, the US air base at Parnamirim was the largest single US air base outside its own territory. From the base, supplies were sent to North Africa or the China-Burma-India theatre. Because of its significant contribution to the war, the air base at Parnamirim was known as the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Trampoline to Victory."

 - Brazil provided key materials for the US war effort, such as iron ore, chrome, manganese, nickel, bauxite, and rubber.

 - Brazil was the only Latin American country to send part of its army, 25,000 men, into combat overseas. In 1944, the Brazilian Expeditionary Force (BEF) and the Air Force's First Fighter Group were sent to fight in Italy. 

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Since its entry into the war in 1942, Brazil played an important logistical role in the Allied war effort. The government of Vargas also allowed the US to build airfields in Brazil, including Parnamirim, which became the largest U.S. air base outside the continental United States. This base became known as the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Trampoline to Victory"  due to its importance for transporting supplies to North Africa and the Pacific theater ($\checkmark$). Additionally, Brazil provided essential raw materials to the US war effort, such as iron ore, chrome, manganese, nickel, bauxite, and rubber ($\checkmark$). Brazil was also the only Latin American country to deploy troops overseas, sending 25,000 Brazilian Expeditionary Force (BEF) soldiers to fight in Italy in 1944 ($\checkmark$).
