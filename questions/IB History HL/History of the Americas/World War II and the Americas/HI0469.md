# HI0469

Explain why Chile did not break diplomatic relations with the Axis powers after the Rio Conference of 1942.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following with evidence:

 - For domestic political reasons. 

     - Chile was going to have presidential election in 1942 and it did not want to have problems with its influential German population

     - The National Socialist Chilean Movement had gotten almost 5$\%$ of the votes in the municipal elections of 1938

     The National Socialist Chilean Movement was part of the main opposition coalition, the Alianza Popular Libertadora

 - For military reasons

     - Chile feared attacks by Japanese or German ships on its own territories. It did not have the necessary naval power to defend itself and it knew the US could not do so either due to the destruction of its navy at Pearl Harbor. 

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Chile did not break diplomatic relations with the Axis Powers after the Rio Conference of 1942 due to domestic political reasons. Since presidential elections were in that same year, the government did not want to alienate the influential German population or German origin population in the country ($\checkmark$). Moreover, the National Socialist Chilean Movement, originally inspired by the German Nazi Party, had some popular support. In the local elections of 1938 it had secured almost 5% of the votes, allowing it to become part of the main opposition coalition, the Alianza Popular Libertadora ($\checkmark$). Additionally,Chile feared attacks by Japanese or German ships on its territories ($\checkmark$). It knew it lacked the naval power to defend itself and Chile's authorities guessed that the US could not do so either because of the destruction of the US naval base at Pearl Harbor ($\checkmark$).
