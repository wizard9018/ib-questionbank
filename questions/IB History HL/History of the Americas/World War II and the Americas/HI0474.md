# HI0474

How, according to Source B, was the US participating in WWII by 1941?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Speech delivered by President Roosevelt at the White House Correspondents' Association Dinner in Washington, March 15, 1941. |

[Source: Roosevelt, F. D. (1941, March 15). Address to the White House Correspondents' Association. Internet Archive. Retrieved February 4, 2025 from https://web.archive.org/web/20161022223216/https://www<noklink>.mtholyoke.edu/acad/intrel/WorldWar2/fdr24.htm. Public Domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- It was sending overseas every possible instrument of war that it could spare.
- It was dedicating its total effort to the war
- It was building factories to produce war material as quickly as possible as well as the means to transport it.
- It was training millions of soldiers
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to Source B, by 1941 the United States was sending overseas every possible instrument of war which it could spare ($\checkmark$). It was also producing as much war material as possible as well as the way to transport it ($\checkmark$). Due to the decision of the American people, the US was not dedicating its total effort to winning the war ($\checkmark$).
