# EC0446

The demand for phosphates has fluctuated wildly due to changes in the demand for secondary  products for which phospates are a primary ingredient. It is predicted that the need for lithium-ion batteries may push phosphate prices higher.

Using a demand and supply diagram, explain how the increased need for lithium-ion batteries might impact the market for phosphates. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:

![image](images/image.png)

Suppliers of lithium-ion batteries prefer to use phosphates as a raw material in lithium-ion batteries. An increase in demand for lithium-ion batteries will increase demand (taste/preferences) for phosphates among suppliers of lithium-ion batteries. The result is a shift in demand from $\mathrm{D_1}$ to $\mathrm{D_2}$ and an increase in price from $\mathrm{P_1}$ to $\mathrm{P_2}$.

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

For drawing a **correctly labelled** demand and supply diagram showing:

- a rightward shift of demand ($\checkmark$)

- a new higher equilibrium price at $\mathrm{P_2}$ ($\checkmark$)

**and**

Explaining that the price of phosphates will increase because:

- phosphates are the preferred raw material in lithium-ion batteries (tastes and preferences) ($\checkmark$) 

- increased demand for lithium-ion batteries will result in increased demand for phosphates  ($\checkmark$)
