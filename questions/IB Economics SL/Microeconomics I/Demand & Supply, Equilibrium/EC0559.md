# EC0559

Company X is a leading manufacturer of consumer electronics, specialising in smartphones and tablets. Company Y is Company X’s main competitor.

Recently, Company Y has increased the price of its bestselling tablet.

**Figure 1** illustrates the market demand for Company Y’s tablets.

**Figure 1**
![EC0559](images/EC0559.png)

1. On  **Figure 1**, sketch the effect on the demand curve for Company X’s tablets due to the increase in price of Company Y’s tablets. [3 marks]

2. Outline whether Company X’s and Company Y’s products are substitutes or complements. [2 marks]

3. Calculate the change in sales revenue of Company Y after the change in price. [3 marks]

4. State whether **Figure 1** shows a decrease in the quantity demanded or a decrease in demand for Company Y. [1 mark]

5. Explain why **Figure 1** reflects the law of demand. [2 marks]

2. $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\
    Company X’s and Company Y’s products are substitutes, because when the price of Company Y’s tablets increase, more of Company X’s tablets are sold. Both products satisfy the same needs.

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:\
    Substitute products refer to goods or services that can be used as alternatives to each other for satisfying a particular need or desire of consumers. These products typically serve similar purposes and compete with each other in the marketplace.

    For identifying that they are substitutes ($\checkmark$) and outlining that these products typically serve similar purposes and compete with each other in the marketplace ($\checkmark$).

3. $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\
    Sales revenue 1 = 500 $\times$ 1500 = US$\text{\textdollar}$750 000

    Sales revenue 2 = 700 $\times$ 1200 = US$\text{\textdollar}$840 000

    Change in sales revenue = 840 000 $-$ 750 000 = US$\text{\textdollar}$90 000

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:\
    Sales revenue = P $\times$ Q

    Change in revenue = revenue 2 $-$ revenue 1

    For correct working of both sales revenues ($\checkmark\checkmark$)

    For the correct result: $\textbf{US\text{\textdollar}90 000}$ ($\checkmark$)

4. $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\
    Company Y experienced a decrease in the quantity demanded.

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:\
    For stating: a decrease in the quantity demanded  ($\checkmark$).

    There is a decrease in the quantity demanded because there is a movement along the curve, not a shift of the curve.
