# EC0569

Maté is a traditional South American drink, particularly popular in Argentina. It is made from the dried leaves of the yerba mate plant and is known for its stimulating and energising effects. The drink is traditionally served in a hollowed-out gourd (also called $\text{\textquotedblleft}$mate$\text{\textquotedblright}$) and sipped through a metal straw (bombilla).

1. Sketch a demand and supply diagram to show the effect of an increase in the price of mate gourds on the bombilla market. [3 marks]

2. Using your answer to part (a), outline why a change in the price of mate gourds impacts the bombilla market. [2 marks]

2. $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\
    Mate gourds and bombillas are complements, because they are jointly demanded as they are used together.

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:\
    Complementary goods are products or services that are often used together, where the consumption of one good enhances the consumption of the other. When the demand for one of these goods increases, the demand for its complement typically rises as well, and vice versa.
