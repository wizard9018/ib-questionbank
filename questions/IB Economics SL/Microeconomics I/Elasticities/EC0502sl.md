# EC0502sl

The price of a bushel of wheat in 2023 was \$6.08, and in that year 1.8 billion bushels were produced in the USA. 

A bushel is a unit of measurement equal to 35.2 litres.

[Economic Research Service, United States Department of Agriculture. 2024, March 27. Wheat Sector at a Glance. Retrieved May 20, 2024, from https://www.ers.usda.gov/topics/crops/wheat/wheat-sector-at-a-glance/.]

Calculate the number of bushels of wheat that would be produced if the price elasticity of supply of wheat is 0.2, and the price of wheat increases by 25\% to \$7.60 per bushel. [3 marks]

Explain **two** reasons why agricultural products like wheat tend to have a low PES. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

    $\dfrac{\%\Delta Qs}{\%\Delta P} = PES$

    $\dfrac{x}{25\%} = 0.2$

    $x = 0.2 \times 25\%$

    $x = 5\%$

    $1.8\ billion \times 1.05 = 1.89\ billion\ bushels$

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$: 

  

    $\dfrac{\%\Delta Qs}{\%\Delta P} = PES$

    $\%\Delta P \times PES = \%\Delta Qs$

    

    $\ new \ wheat \ production = 1.8\ billion \times \dfrac{(100 + \%\Delta Qs)}{100}$

    Showing correct calculations for:

    -   percentage change in quantity supplied ($\checkmark$)

    -   new quantity supplied of wheat ($\checkmark$)

    For the correct answer of :answer[**1.89 billion**] ($\checkmark$)

2.  $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

    The PES of agricultural products like wheat tends to be low because it takes a long time to increase output of a crop in response to increased prices. Producing crops takes a long time, as the field must be prepared, seed must be planted, and it takes time for the crop to grow. Consequently, farmers must wait until the following season to plant (and grow) more of a certain crop.

    Another reason is that suitable land for growing wheat may not be plentiful. Not all land is useful to grow every crop, so if most of the useful land for wheat is already being used to grow wheat, then it may not be possible to increase production in response to increased prices.

    

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:\

    For an explanation including **two** of the reasons below (a-e):

    a. It takes a long time to produce agricultural products ($\checkmark$) due to the length of the growing cycle ($\checkmark$).\

    **or**\

    b. Agricultural output tends to be perishable ($\checkmark$), so the PES for agricultural goods tends to be low as they cannot be stored when the price is low, and released to the market when the price increases ($\checkmark$).\

    **or**\

    c. Capital equipment in agriculture is specialised ($\checkmark$), making it more difficult to switch production from one crop to another ($\checkmark$). \

    **or**\

    d. Agricultural production is land-intensive ($\checkmark$). Land is a finite resource, so there may not be any additional land to use for increased production of a crop ($\checkmark$).
