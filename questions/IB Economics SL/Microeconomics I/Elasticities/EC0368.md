# EC0368

**Table 1- Economic data for Ireland**

| | 2016 | 2020 |

| --- | :-: | :-: |

| Real GNI per capita (US\$) | 51 740 | 66 010 |

| Population (millions) | 4.76 | 4.99 |

From 2016 to 2020, Ireland’s demand for new automobiles increased by nearly 38\%.

[$\text{\textquotedblleft}$Ireland .$\text{\textquotedblright}$ World Bank Open Data, data.worldbank.org/country/ireland. Accessed 28 Apr. 2024.]

1.  Using information from **Table 1**, calculate the income elasticity of demand (YED) for new automobiles in Ireland considering the change in income between 2016 and 2020. [3 marks]

2.  Using information from **Table 1**, state whether new automobiles are a normal or inferior good in Ireland. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

    $\dfrac{38}{27.5}$ = 1.38

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:

    YED = $\dfrac{\%\Delta Qd}{\%\Delta Y}$

    $\%\Delta Q_d = 38\%$

    $\%\Delta Y = \dfrac{2020\ GNI\ per\ capita - 2016\ GNI\ per\ capita}{2016\ GNI\ per\ capita} \times 100$

    $\%\Delta Y = \dfrac{66\ 010 - 51\ 740}{51\ 740} \times 100 = 27.5\%$ ($\checkmark$)

    YED = $\dfrac{38}{27.5}$ = :answer[**1.38**] 

    - *one mark for YED calculation* ($\checkmark$)

    - *one mark for correct answer* ($\checkmark$)

2.  $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

    New automobiles are a normal good in Ireland.

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:\

    Quantity demanded increases as income increases, therefore:

    -   new automobiles are a normal good ($\checkmark$)
