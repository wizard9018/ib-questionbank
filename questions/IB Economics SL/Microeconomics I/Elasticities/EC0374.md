# EC0374

**Figure 1: Market for Cell Phones**

![EC0374 fig1](<images/EC0374 fig1.png>)

Using **Figure 1**, calculate the price elasticity of supply for cell phones when price rises from US\$800 to US\$1 000. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

PES = $\dfrac{40}{25}$ = 1.6

$\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:

PES = $\dfrac{\%\Delta Quantity\ Supplied}{\%\Delta Price} = \dfrac{\dfrac{70\ 000-50\ 000}{50\ 000} \times 100}{\dfrac{1\ 000 - 800}{800} \times 100} = \dfrac{40\%}{25\%}$ = :answer[**1.6**]
