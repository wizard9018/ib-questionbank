# AI1286

**Maximum mark: 5**

A café sells drinks in two different cup sizes: regular and large.

The $2\times1$ matrix __P__ represents the prices of a regular drink and a large drink respectively.

The $2 \times 2$ matrix __S__ $ =
\begin{pmatrix}
118 & 84\\
66 & k
\end{pmatrix}$
represents the numbers of regular and large drinks sold on two different days, where $k\in\mathbb{R^+}$.

The total revenue matrix, __T__, can be found using __T = SP__.

1.    Find __S__$^{-1}$. [3 marks]

The manager of the café uses __S__$^{-1}$ and the daily revenue totals in __T__ to determine the drink prices in __P__.

She notices that when $k=a$, where $a \in \mathbb{Z}^+$, she is unable to use __S__$^{-1}$ to calculate the drink prices.

2.    Find $a$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the formula for the __determinant of a $\bm{2\times2}$ matrix (1.14)__,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \det
    \begin{pmatrix}
    a & b\\
    c & d
    \end{pmatrix}
    =ad-bc
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \det(\bm{\mathrm{S}})
    &=(118)(k)-(94)(59)\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    &=118k-5546\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the formula for the __inverse of a $\bm{2\times2}$ matrix (1.14)__,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \begin{pmatrix}
    a & b\\
    c & d
    \end{pmatrix}^{-1}
    =
    \dfrac{1}{\det(\bm{\mathrm{S}})}
    \begin{pmatrix}
    d & -b\\
    -c & a
    \end{pmatrix}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \bm{\mathrm{S}}^{-1}
    &=
    \hspace{0.525em}
    \boxed{
    \dfrac{1}{118k-5544}
    \begin{pmatrix}
    k & -94\\
    -59 & 118
    \end{pmatrix}}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
2. For $\mathbf{S^{-1}}$ to exist, the determinant of __S__ must be non-zero.
    Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    118k-5546 &\neq 0 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    118k &\neq 5546 \\[6pt]
    k &\neq 47
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    If $k=47$, then the manager would not be able to uniquely calculate the drink prices in __P__ from the total revenue matrix __T__.

    Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a = 47\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
