# AI1288

**Maximum mark: 5**

A factory produces three types of energy bars: oat, fruit and protein.

The matrix __F__ $=\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & 2\\
2 & 1 & 1\\
0 & 1 & 3
\end{pmatrix}$ represents the number of production batches completed at three factories.

The rows correspond to the factories and the columns correspond to oat, fruit and protein bar batches respectively.

The matrix __C__ $=\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 & 0\\
1 & 2 & 1\\
3 & 0 & 2
\end{pmatrix}$ represents the number of crates of oat, fruit and protein bars produced per batch for each factory.

The matrix __P__ represents the total production data for the factories such that __P = FC__.

1.    Find __P__$^{-1}$. [3 marks]

The company receives an order requiring: $309$ crates of oat bars, $316$ crates of fruit bars, and $460$ crates of protein bars.

If $a$ is the number of batches completed at factory A, $b$ is the number of batches completed at factory B, and $c$ is the number of batches completed at factory C then the following equation can be formed
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{P}
\begin{pmatrix}
a\\
b\\
c
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
309\\
316\\
460
\end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$

2.    Hence, find $a$, $b$ and $c$. [2 marks]
