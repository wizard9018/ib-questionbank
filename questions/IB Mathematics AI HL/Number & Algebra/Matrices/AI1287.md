# AI1287

**Maximum mark: 8**

A catering company provides three types of meals for events: standard, premium and deluxe.

The company charges fixed prices for each type of meal, regardless of the event.

The matrix $\mathbf{S}=\begin{pmatrix}
x\\
y\\
z
\end{pmatrix}$ represents the cost of each type of meal: standard ($x$), premium ($y$) and deluxe ($z$).

The numbers of meals ordered, together with the total catering costs for three events, are shown in the following table.

| **Event** | **Standard** | **Premium** | **Deluxe** | **Total Cost (£)** |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| Corporate Dinner | 4 | 6 | 10 | 1520 |
| Wedding | 3 | 8 | 7 | 1300 |
| Charity Gala | 5 | 4 | 12 | 1640 |

The $3 \times 3$ matrix __B__ represents the number of each meal type for the three events shown above and the $3 \times 1$ matrix __T__ represents the total cost for each of the three events.

1.    Write down

    1.    __B__;

    2.    __T__. [2 marks]

2.    Find __B__$^{-1}$. [1 mark]

3.    Hence, find the values of $x$, $y$ and $z$. [3 marks]

A fourth event orders $6$ standard meals, $9$ premium meals and $15$ deluxe meals.

4.    Find the total catering cost for this event. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1. The matrix $\mathbf{B}$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathbf{B}
        =

        \begin{pmatrix}4 & 6 & 10\\
        3 & 8 & 7\\
        5 & 4 & 12
        \end{pmatrix}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2. The matrix $\mathbf{T}$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathbf{T}
        =

        \begin{pmatrix}1520\\
        1300\\
        1640
        \end{pmatrix}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2. Using a GDC, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \bm{\mathrm{B}}^{-1}
    &=\begin{pmatrix}
    -\frac{34}{7} & \frac{16}{7} & \frac{19}{7}\\
    \frac{1}{14} & \frac{1}{7} & -\frac{1}{7} \\
    2 & -1 & -1
    \end{pmatrix}\\[16pt]
    &=\begin{pmatrix}
    -4.857... & 2.285... & 2.714...\\
    0.07142... & 0.1428... & -0.1428...\\
    2 & -1 & -1
    \end{pmatrix}\\[16pt]
    &=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{
    \begin{pmatrix}
    -4.86 & 2.29 & 2.71\\
    0.0714 & 0.143 & -0.143\\
    2 & -1 & -1
    \end{pmatrix}}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3. Since
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{BS}=\mathbf{T}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using our previous answer and a calculator we can rewrite the above to get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{B}\mathbf{B}^{-1}
    \mathbf{S}
    &=
    \mathbf{B}^{-1}\mathbf{T}\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[12pt]
    \mathbf{S}
    &=
    \mathbf{B}^{-1}\mathbf{T}\\[12pt]
    &=
    \begin{pmatrix}
    -\frac{34}{7} & \frac{16}{7} & \frac{19}{7}\\
    \frac{1}{14} & \frac{1}{7} & -\frac{1}{7} \\
    2 & -1 & -1
    \end{pmatrix}
    \begin{pmatrix}
    1520\\
    1300\\
    1640
    \end{pmatrix}\\[20pt]
    &=
    \begin{pmatrix}
    40\\
    60\\
    100
    \end{pmatrix}
    \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x &= 40\\[6pt]
    y &= 60\\[6pt]
    z &= 100\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4. Using our previous answer, the total catering cost is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    6(40)+9(60)+15(100)
    &=240+540+1500 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    &=2280

    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the total catering cost is $2280$ pounds.
    $$
    \\[-25pt]
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textcolor{black}{\textbf{A1}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
