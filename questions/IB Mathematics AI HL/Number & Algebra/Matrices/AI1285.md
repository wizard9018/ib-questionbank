# AI1285

**Maximum mark: 6**

A school canteen sells sandwiches and bottles of juice.

A sandwich costs  $£4.80$ and a bottle of juice costs $£2.30$.

On Monday, the canteen sells $325$ sandwiches and $184$ bottles of juice.

On Tuesday, the canteen sells $412$ sandwiches and $236$ bottles of juice.

The $2\times1$ matrix __P__ represents the prices and the $2\times2$ matrix __S__ represents the sales figures.

1.    Write down

    1.    __P__;

    2.    __S__. [2 marks]

2.    Find __SP__ and interpret the meaning of the resulting matrix. [2 marks]

3.    Find the total revenue from sales over the two days. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1. The prices matrix is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \bm{\mathrm{P}}=

        \begin{pmatrix}4.8\\
        2.3
        \end{pmatrix}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2. The sales matrix is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \bm{\mathrm{S}}=

        \begin{pmatrix}325 & 184\\
        412 & 236
        \end{pmatrix}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2. We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \bm{\mathrm{SP}}
    &=
    \begin{pmatrix}
    325 & 184\\
    412 & 236
    \end{pmatrix}
    \begin{pmatrix}
    4.8\\
    2.3
    \end{pmatrix} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[12pt]
    &=
    \begin{pmatrix}
    (325)(4.8)+(184)(2.3)\\
    (412)(4.8)+(236)(2.3)
    \end{pmatrix}\\[12pt]
    \bm{\mathrm{SP}}
    &=

    \begin{pmatrix}1983.2\\
    2520.4
    \end{pmatrix}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    This matrix represents the total revenue, in pounds, for Monday and Tuesday respectively.

3. To find the total revenue we must add the revenue for both days.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    1983.2+2520.4 &= 4503.6\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the total revenue over the two days is $£4503.6$.

    $$
    \\[-25pt]
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textcolor{black}{\textbf{A1}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
