# AI931

**Maximum mark: 5**

Phil's phone shop sells Azura smartphones for $$\$1\hspace{0.05em}499$$
and Bellson smartphones for $$\$850$$. It is expected that a Bellson
smartphone will depreciate at a rate of $20\hspace{0.05em}\%$ per
year.

After 2 years, an Azura smartphone is worth approximately $$\$735$$.

1.  Show that the expected annual depreciation rate of an Azura
    smartphone is 30%. [2 marks]

An Azura smartphone and a Bellson smartphone will have the same value
$n$ years after they were purchased.

2.  Find the value of $n$. [2 marks]

3.  Comment on the validity of your answer to part (b).
    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have

    ![b2a5ec580f342612071c0ff847247c8492db8bc3.svg](images/b2a5ec580f342612071c0ff847247c8492db8bc3.svg)

    Hence, using the compound depreciation formula, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{FV} &= \text{PV}\bigg(1 - \dfrac{r}{100}\bigg)^n \\[5pt]
    735 &= 1\hspace{0.05em}499\hspace{0.05em}\bigg(1 - \dfrac{r}{100}\bigg)^{2}  \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-0.5em}r \approx 30\% \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have

    ![a5751dd1907b71c5a82464012ef3bda35a63fb6f.svg](images/a5751dd1907b71c5a82464012ef3bda35a63fb6f.svg)

    Hence, using the compound depreciation formula, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{FV}_{\text{A}} &= \mathrm{FV}_{\text{B}} \\[12pt]
    1\hspace{0.05em}499\hspace{0.05em}\bigg(1 - \dfrac{30}{100}\bigg)^{n} &= 850\hspace{0.05em}\bigg(1 - \dfrac{20}{100}\bigg)^{n} \hspace{0.9em} \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-0.5em}n \approx 4.25 \hspace{3.5em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  For example,

    The calculation assumes that both smartphones will depreciate at a constant rate for over 4 years. This is unlikely given that new model smartphones are released regularly, and when new model smartphones are released, older phones are likely to depreciate in value more quickly.
