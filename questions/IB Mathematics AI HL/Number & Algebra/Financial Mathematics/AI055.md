# AI055

**Maximum mark: 6**

Isabella and Charlotte both receives $80\hspace{0.15em}000$ Australian
dollars (AUD) on their $18$th birthday to invest for later in their
life.

Isabella deposits her $80\hspace{0.15em}000$ AUD in a bank account that
pays a nominal annual interest rate of $x$ %,
**compounded monthly**.

1.  The amount in a bank account after $6$ years will be
    $100\hspace{0.15em}000$ AUD. Find the nominal annual interest rate.
    Give your answer correct to two decimal places.[3 marks]

Charlotte uses her $80\hspace{0.15em}000$ AUD to buy a house that
increases in value at a rate of $3$ % per year.

2.  Find the house price after $6$ years. [3 marks]
