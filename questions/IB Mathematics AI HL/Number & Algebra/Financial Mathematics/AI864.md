# AI864

**Maximum mark: 7**

Two college students, David and Lisa, decide to invest money they have
saved from working part-time jobs. David's investment strategy results
in an increase of his investment amount by $$\$ 1\,000$$ each year. Lisa's
investment strategy results in her investment amount increasing by
$5 \%$ each year.

At the start of the second year of investing, David's total investment
amount is $$\$21\,000$$ and Lisa's is $$\$11\,655$$.

1.  Calculate
    1.  the original amount David invested.

    2.  the original amount Lisa invested.[4 marks]

During a certain year, $n$, Lisa's investment amount becomes larger than
David's amount for the first time.

2.  Find the value of $n$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using the $n$th term formula for an arithmetic sequence with
        $u_2=21\,000$, $n=2$, and $d=1\,000$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        u_n &= u_1+(n-1)d\hspace{0.75cm}\\[6pt]
        21\,000 &= u_1+ (2-1)(1\,000) \hspace{0.75cm}\\[6pt]
        21\,000  &= u_1+ 1\,000 \hspace{0.75cm}\\[8pt]
        &\hspace{-0.5em}u_1 = \$20\,000
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the $n$th term formula for a geometric sequence with
        $v_2 = 11\,655$, $n=2$, and $r=1.05$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        v_n&=v_1r^{n-1}\hspace{0.75cm}\\[6pt]
        11\,655 &=v_1 (1.05)^{2-1}\hspace{0.75cm}\\[6pt]
        11\,655&=v_1(1.05) \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{-0.5em}v_1= \$ 11\,100
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  David's investment amount at the start of the $n$th year can be
    expressed as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_n &= u_1+(n-1)d\nonumber\\[6pt]
    &= 20\,000+(n-1)1\,000\nonumber\\[6pt]
    &=1\,000n+19\,000\nonumber
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Lisa's investment amount at the start of the $n$th year can be
    expressed as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    v_n &= v_1r^{n-1}\nonumber\hspace{1.3cm}\\[6pt]
    &= 11\,100(1.05)^{n-1}\nonumber
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Both investment amounts are plotted on the graph below, for a given
    start of year $n$

    ![8d89268e496fd951a9f86ddd8871c22f623a5d88.svg](images/8d89268e496fd951a9f86ddd8871c22f623a5d88.svg)

    Form the graph, $11\,100(1.05)^{n-1} > 1\,000n+19\,000$ when
    $n > 32.4$.

    Hence, Lisa's amount will become larger than David's during year $32$.
