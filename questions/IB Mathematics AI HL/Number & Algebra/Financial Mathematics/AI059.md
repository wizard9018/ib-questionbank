# AI059

**Maximum mark: 12**

Lily and Eva both receive $50\hspace{0.15em}000$ Australian dollars
(AUD) on their $18$th birthday. Lily deposits her $50\hspace{0.15em}000$
AUD into a bank account. The bank pays an annual interest rate of
$5$ %, **compounded yearly**. Eva invests her
$50\hspace{0.15em}000$ AUD into a high-yield mutual fund that returns a
fixed amount of $3000$ AUD per year.

1.  Calculate:
    1.  the amount in Lily's bank account at the end of the first year;

    2.  the total amount of Eva's funds at the end of the first year.
        [2 marks]

2.  Write down an expression for:
    1.  the amount in Lily's bank account at the end of the $n$th year;

    2.  the total amount of Eva's funds at the end of the $n$th year.
        [4 marks]

3.  Calculate the year in which the amount in Lily's bank account
    becomes\
    greater than the amount in Eva's fund. [2 marks]

4.  Calculate:
    1.  the interest amount that Lily earns if invested for $12$ years,
        giving your answer correct to **two decimal places**;

    2.  the amount of funds that Eva earns for her investment if
        invested for $12$ years. [4 marks]

        Hence, using the compound interest formula, we obtain

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3em} \mathrm{FV} &= 50\hspace{0.15em}000\bigg(1 + \dfrac{5}{100\times1}\bigg)^{1\times n} \\[6pt]
        &= \hspace{0.525em}50\hspace{0.15em}000\hspace{0.05em}(1 + 0.05)^n \hspace{0.35em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have

        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{FV} = \hspace{0.525em}50\hspace{0.15em}000 + 3000n \hspace{0.9em}

        $$

3.  Solving the inequality $(1) > (2)$ for $n$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    50\hspace{0.15em}000\hspace{0.05em}(1 + 0.05)^n &> 50\hspace{0.15em}000 + 3000n \hspace{1.2em} \\[6pt]
    50\hspace{0.05em}(1 + 0.05)^n &> 50+3n \\[6pt]

    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence this will happen in
