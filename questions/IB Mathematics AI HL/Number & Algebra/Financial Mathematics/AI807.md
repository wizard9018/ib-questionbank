# AI807

**Maximum mark: 6**

Michael buys a second hand Tesla car for $$\$18\hspace{0.15em}000$$. The
value of the car depreciates by $10\%$ each
year.

1.  Find the total amount the car will depreciate after 5 years, giving
    your answer correct to the nearest dollar. [3 marks]

The price of a different used car depreciates by $5\%$ each year.

2.  Find the value reduction of this car after $4$ years as a
    percentage, when compared to the original purchase price.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have

    ![6462608efbf9ba0d371a0ca7fdfe6437d1874af0.svg](images/6462608efbf9ba0d371a0ca7fdfe6437d1874af0.svg)

    Using the compound depreciation formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \mathrm{FV} &= \text{PV}\bigg(1 - \dfrac{r}{100}\bigg)^n \\[5pt]
    &= 18\hspace{0.25em}000\bigg(1 - \dfrac{10}{100}\bigg)^{5}  \\[12pt]
    &\approx \$10\hspace{0.15em}629
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, after $5$
    years the car has depreciated by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.1em} \mathrm{PV} - \text{FV} &= 18\hspace{0.15em}000 - 10\hspace{0.15em}629 \\[6pt]
    &=\$\, 7\hspace{0.15em}371
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have

    ![16c3205d28d13971f8ad1ff276a51f080dad38f6.svg](images/16c3205d28d13971f8ad1ff276a51f080dad38f6.svg)

    Using the compound depreciation formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \mathrm{FV} &= \text{PV}\bigg(1 - \dfrac{r}{100}\bigg)^n \\[5pt]
    &= \text{PV}\bigg(1 - \dfrac{5}{100}\bigg)^{4}  \\[12pt]
    &=\text{PV}\bigg(\dfrac{19}{20}\bigg)^{4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore,
    the reduction in price is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.1em} \mathrm{PV} -\text{PV}\bigg(\dfrac{19}{20}\bigg)^{4} &=\Big(1-\bigg(\dfrac{19}{20}\bigg)^{4}\Big) \, \mathrm{PV} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.185 \, \mathrm{PV}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the reduction in
    price after $4$ years as a percentage is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.1em} 0.185\times 100\% \,\,\hspace{0.525em} =18.5\%
    \end{aligned}
    $$
