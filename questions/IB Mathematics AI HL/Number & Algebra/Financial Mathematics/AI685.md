# AI685

**Maximum mark: 5**

Alex rolls his \$$900$ $000$ of savings into an annuity account. He wants the money to last for $20$ years.

1.  Given that Alex wants to withdraw \$$5$ $742.54$ each month, find the annual interest rate, compounded monthly, of the account.[2 marks]

2.  Given that Alex withdraws only \$$5$ $000$ each month, find the additional length of time his money will last, rounded to the nearest month.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the G.D.C. (Financial Solver) to solve for I%.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    | N | $240$ |
    |:---:|:---:|
    | $\textcolor{#B52209}{\text{I\%}}$ | $\textcolor{#B52209}{4.599\ldots}$ |
    | PV | $900$ $000$ |
    | PMT | $-5$ $742.54$ |
    | FV | $0$ |
    | P/Y | $12$ |
    | C/Y | $12$ |
    | PMT | END |

    Therefore, the annual interest rate is $\boxed{4.60\%}$ (to $3$ s.f.).
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  With I% $= 4.60$, use the G.D.C. (Financial Solver) to solve for the new N when the withdrawal is reduced to \$$5$ $000$ per month.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    | $\textcolor{#B52209}{\text{N}}$ | $\textcolor{#B52209}{306.111\ldots}$ |
    |:---:|:---:|
    | I% | $4.60$ |
    | PV | $900$ $000$ |
    | PMT | $-5$ $000$ |
    | FV | $0$ |
    | P/Y | $12$ |
    | C/Y | $12$ |
    | PMT | END |

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        N &= 306.111\ldots \text{ months} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Keeping the unrounded N from the solver and subtracting from the original $240$ months.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        306.111\ldots - 240 &= 66.111\ldots \\[6pt]
        &= \boxed{66 \text{ months}} \hspace{0.75em}\text{(to nearest month)}\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
