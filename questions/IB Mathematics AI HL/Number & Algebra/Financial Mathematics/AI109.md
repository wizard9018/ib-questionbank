# AI109

**Maximum mark: 6**

Melinda has $$\$300\hspace{0.15em}000$$ in a private foundation. Each year
she donates $10\hspace{0.05em}\%$ of the money remaining in her private
foundation to charity.

1.  Find the maximum number of years Melinda can donate to charity while
    keeping at least $$\$100\hspace{0.15em}000$$ in the private
    foundation. [3 marks]

Bill invests $$\$400\hspace{0.15em}000$$ in a bank account that pays a
nominal interest rate of $4$ %, **compounded
quarterly**, for ten years.

2.  Calculate the value of Bill's investment at the end of this
    time. Give your answer correct to **the nearest dollar**.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We can form an inequality using the compound interest formula, with a $\textcolor{red}{\text{minus}}$ sign instead of a plus sign, to show that Melinda's money is decreasing instead of increasing:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{FV} &\leq \mathrm{PV}\bigg(1 \textcolor{red}{-} \dfrac{r}{100k}\bigg)^{kn}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We need to find the largest value $n$ so that the RHS is greater than or equal to the LHS. We have

    ![ai109a](images/ai109a.PNG)

    Hence we must solve the inequality
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    100\,000 &\leq 300\,000\bigg(1 - \dfrac{10}{100}\bigg)^{n} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{1}{3} &\leq 0.9^n
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Sketching the graphs of $y=\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $y=0.9^x$ and finding the point of intersection, we have

    ![ai109b](images/ai109b.PNG)

    Hence we have $n \leq 10.427...$ and given that $n$ must be a whole number, the maximum number of years is $10$

2.  We have

    ![d87692941dc43b1f610dcca79c498cfb3a932d8a.svg](images/d87692941dc43b1f610dcca79c498cfb3a932d8a.svg)

    Hence, using the compound interest formula, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{FV} &= \mathrm{PV}\bigg(1 + \dfrac{r}{100k}\bigg)^{kn} \\[5pt]
    &= 400\hspace{0.15em}000\bigg(1 + \dfrac{4}{100\times4}\bigg)^{4\times10} \\[12pt]
    &\approx \$595\hspace{0.15em}545
    \end{aligned}
    $$
