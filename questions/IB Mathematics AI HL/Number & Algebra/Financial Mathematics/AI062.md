# AI062

**Maximum mark: 6**

Hannah buys a car for $$\$24\hspace{0.15em}900$$. The value of the car
depreciates by $16$ %
each year.

1.  Find the value of the car after $10$ years. [3 marks]

Patrick buys a car for $$\$12\hspace{0.15em}000$$. The car depreciates by
a fixed amount each year, and after $6$ years it is worth $$\$6200$$.

2.  Find the annual rate of depreciation of the car.
    [3 marks]

---

    Hence, using the compound depreciation formula, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \mathrm{FV} &= \text{PV}\bigg(1 - \dfrac{r}{100}\bigg)^n \\[5pt]
    &= 24\hspace{0.25em}900\bigg(1 - \dfrac{16}{100}\bigg)^{10}  \\[12pt]
    &\approx \$4360
    \end{aligned}
    $$
