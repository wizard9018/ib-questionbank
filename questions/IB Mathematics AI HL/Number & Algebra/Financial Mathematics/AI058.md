# AI058

**Maximum mark: 6**

Julia wants to buy a house that requires a deposit of
$74\hspace{0.15em}000$ Australian dollars (AUD).

Julia is going to invest an amount of AUD in an account that pays a
nominal annual interest rate of $5.5$ %,
**compounded monthly**.

1.  Find the amount of AUD Julia needs to invest to reach
    $74\hspace{0.15em}000$ AUD after $8$ years. Give your answer correct
    to **the nearest dollar**. [3 marks]

Julia's parents offer to add $5000$ AUD to her initial investment from
part (a), however, only if she invests her money in a more reliable bank
that pays a nominal annual interest rate only of
$3.5$ %, **compounded quarterly**.

2.  Find the number of years it would take Julia to save the
    $74\hspace{0.15em}000$ AUD if she accepts her parents money and
    follows their advice. Give your answer correct to **the nearest
    year**. [3 marks]
