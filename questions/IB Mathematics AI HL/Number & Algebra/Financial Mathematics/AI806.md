# AI806

**Maximum mark: 14**

**In this question, give all answers correct to the nearest whole
number.**

Ann is considering investing $$\$85\hspace{0.15em}000$$ into a term
deposit in one of two banks. The first bank offers an annual interest
rate of $3$ %, compounding monthly. The second
bank offers a fixed payment amount of $$\$250$$ per month.

1.  Calculate:
    1.  the amount that would be in the account in the first bank at the
        end of the first year;

    2.  the amount that would be in the account in the second bank at
        the end of the first year. [4 marks]

2.  Write down an expression for:
    1.  the amount in the account in the first bank at the end of the
        $n$th year;

    2.  the amount in the account in the second bank at the end of the
        $n$th year. [4 marks]

3.  Calculate the year in which the amount in the first bank account
    becomes\
    greater than the amount in the second bank. [2 marks]

4.  Calculate:
    1.  the interest that Ann would earn if she invested in the first
        bank for $10$ years;

    2.  the interest that Ann would earn if she invested in the second
        bank for $10$ years. [4 marks]
