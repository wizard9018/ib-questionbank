# AI715

**Maximum mark: 6**

Alex invests an amount of USD into a savings account which pays 3.3%
(p.a.) interest, compounded monthly. After 5 years Alex has
$8\hspace{0.15em}000$ USD in the account.

1.  Find the amount of USD initially invested, rounding your answer to
    two decimal places.[3 marks]

With this money, Alex purchases a used car for $5\hspace{0.15em}000$
dollars, and sells it 3 years later for $4\hspace{0.15em}200$.

2.  Find the rate at which the car depreciates per year over
    the 3 year period.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have

    ![02190c699d42d109e659d8b5742f5cdb828d8e79.svg](images/02190c699d42d109e659d8b5742f5cdb828d8e79.svg)

    Using the compound interest formula, we can solve for PV

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    8\hspace{0.15em}000 &= \text{PV}\bigg(1 + \dfrac{3.3}{100\times 12}\bigg)^{12(5)} \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 6\hspace{0.15em}784.69 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have

    ![6f2b95ec07474eac9d47b28cc09e2eb63167e4c5.svg](images/6f2b95ec07474eac9d47b28cc09e2eb63167e4c5.svg)

    We can use the compound depreciation formula to solve for $r$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{FV} &= \text{PV}\bigg(1 - \dfrac{r}{100}\bigg)^n \rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{13mm}\\
    4\hspace{0.15em}200&= 5\hspace{0.25em}000\bigg(1 - \dfrac{r}{100}\bigg)^{3}  \rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{13mm}\\
    &\hspace{-0.5em}r\approx 5.65 \rule[0mm]{0pt}{6mm}\hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
