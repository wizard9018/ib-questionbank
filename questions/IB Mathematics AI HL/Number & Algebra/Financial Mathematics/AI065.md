# AI065

**Maximum mark: 6**

**In this question give all answers correct to two decimal places.**

Elena invests in a retirement plan. She initially invests €$1500$, then invests a further
€$1500$ at the end of each year.
Interest is earned on each payment at a rate of
$2.49$ % per year, **compounded annually**.

1.  Find the value of the investment after $25$ years. [3 marks]

2.  Find the amount of interest Elena will earn on the investment over
    $25$ years.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the finance solver on G.D.C., we have

    ![elena 1](<images/elena 1.PNG>)

    Hence the value of the investment after $25$ years is
     $\text{\euro\hspace{0.05em}}53\hspace{0.15em}944.48$

2.  The amount of interest earned on the investment over $25$ years is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{FV} - 1500\times26 &= 53\hspace{0.15em}944.48 - 39\hspace{0.15em}000 \hspace{3em} \\[6pt]
    &= \text{\euro\hspace{0.05em}}14\hspace{0.15em}944.48
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    **Note that Elena has made an initial investment, followed by 25 end-of-year payments, so there are 26 payments of €$\bm{1500}$ altogether.**
