# AI061

**Maximum mark: 6**

**In this question give all answers correct to two decimal places.**

Charlie deposits $8000$ Canadian dollars (CAD) into a bank account. The
bank pays a nominal annual interest rate of
$5$ %, **compounded semi-monthly**.

1.  Find the amount of **interest** that Charlie will earn over the next
    $2$ years. [3 marks]

Oscar also deposits CAD into a bank account. His bank pays a nominal
annual interest rate of $6$ %, **compounded
quarterly**. In $2$ years, the total amount in Oscar's account will be
$$\$8000$$ CAD.

2.  Find the amount that Oscar deposits in the bank account.
    [3 marks]
