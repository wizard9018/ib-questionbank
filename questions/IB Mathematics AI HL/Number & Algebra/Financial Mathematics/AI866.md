# AI866

**Maximum mark: 6**

Smith has saved $$\$5\,000$$ from working a part-time job and wants to
invest this money so that it grows over time. His bank offers a savings
account that pays an annual interest rate of $4.2\%$, compounded
quarterly.

1.  Find how many years it will take for Smith's investment amount to
    double in value, rounding your answer to the nearest integer.
    [3 marks]

Smith wants his money to grow faster than this first option. His wants
to invest the money so that it will double in value in $5$ years. He
considers an high-growth, higher-risk option, which pays an annual
interest of $r\%$, compounding half-yearly.

2.  Determine the value of $r$ required in this option, rounding your
    answer to two decimal places. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The compound interest formula is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    FV &= PV\left(1+\dfrac{r}{100k}\right)^{kn}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We
    have $FV=2\times 5000 = 10000,\, PV=5000,\, r=4.2,$ and $k=4$.
    Substituting these values into the formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    10000 &= 5000\left(1+\dfrac{4.2}{100(4)} \right)^{4n}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.4em}\text{n}= 17 \qquad \qquad [\text{using GDC}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have $FV=10000, \,PV=5000,\, k=2,$ and $n=5$.
    Substituting these values into the compound interest formula, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    10000 &= 5000\left(1+\dfrac{r}{100(2)} \right)^{2(5)}\\[6pt]
    2 &= \left(1+\dfrac{r}{200} \right)^{10}\\[6pt]
    \sqrt[10]{2}&=1+\dfrac{r}{200}\\[6pt]
    \sqrt[10]{2}-1&=\dfrac{r}{200}\\[6pt]
    (\sqrt[10]{2}-1)\times200&=r\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.3em}\text{r}= 14.35 \%
    \end{aligned}
    $$
