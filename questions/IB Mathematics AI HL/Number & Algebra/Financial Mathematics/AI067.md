# AI067

**Maximum mark: 6**

Charles plans to invest in a retirement plan for $30$ years. In this
plan, he will deposit $1000$ British pounds (GBP) at the end of every
month and receive a $6.5\hspace{0.05em}\%$ interest rate per annum,
**compounded monthly**.

1.  Find the future value of the investment at the end of the $30$
    years. Give your answer correct to **the nearest pound**.[3 marks]

After the $30$-year period, Charles will start receiving regular monthly
payments of $1500$ GBP.

2.  Calculate the number of years it will take Charles's
    monthly retirement $\text{payments}$ to match the total
    amount originally invested. [3 marks]

2.  The amount that Charles invests over $30$ years is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.2em} 1000\times360 &= 360\hspace{0.15em}000 \text{ GBP}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence Charles breaks even with the amount he invested after

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{360\hspace{0.15em}000}{1500\times12} &=\hspace{0.525em}20 \text{ years}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
