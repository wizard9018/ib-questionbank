# AI070

**Maximum mark: 6**

Olivia takes a mortgage (loan) of $$\$250\hspace{0.15em}000$$ to buy an
apartment in Sydney. $\text{Interest}$ on the loan accumulates
at a rate of $3.49\%$ per year, **compounded
semi-annually**. Olivia agrees with the bank to amortise the loan in
monthly payments, made at the end of each month.

1.  Given that the loan is to be amortised over $30$ years, find:
    1.  the monthly payment amount;

    2.  the total amount paid in amortising the loan. [4 marks]

2.  Olivia has the capacity to increase her monthly payments by $$\$85$$. Justify to Olivia why this may be a smart financial choice.
    [2 marks]
