# AI682

**Maximum mark: 6**

Harry has \$ $950$ $000$ in his savings fund. He rolls his money into an annuity fund which returns $4.4\%$ p.a. compounded monthly. He wants to withdraw $$\$8\,000$$ each month to live on.

1.  Find the length of time Harry's money will last, rounding your answer to the nearest month.[3 marks]

2.  Given that Harry only withdraws $$\$7\,000$$ each month, find the additional length of time his money will last, rounding your answer to the nearest month.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the G.D.C. (Financial Solver),

    $$\\[-2.3em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{(M1})}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    | $\textcolor{#B52209}{\text{N}}$ | $\textcolor{#B52209}{156.1\ldots}$ |
    |:---:|:---:|
    | I% | $4.4$ |
    | PV | $950$ $000$ |
    | PMT | $-8$ $000$ |
    | FV | $0$ |
    | P/Y | $12$ |
    | C/Y | $12$ |
    | PMT | END |

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        N &= 156.1\ldots \text{ months} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Therefore, the money will last $\boxed{156 \text{ months}}$ (or $13$ years).
    $$\\[-2.3em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the G.D.C. (Financial Solver) with the lower monthly withdrawal.

    | $\textcolor{#B52209}{\text{N}}$ | $\textcolor{#B52209}{188.088\ldots}$ |
    |:---:|:---:|
    | I% | $4.4$ |
    | PV | $950$ $000$ |
    | PMT | $-7$ $000$ |
    | FV | $0$ |
    | P/Y | $12$ |
    | C/Y | $12$ |
    | PMT | END |

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        N &= 188.088\ldots \text{ months} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Keeping the unrounded N values from both parts and subtracting.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        188.088\ldots - 156.194\ldots &= 31.893\ldots \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}\\[6pt]
        &= \boxed{32 \text{ months}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    (equivalently, $2$ years and $8$ months longer).
