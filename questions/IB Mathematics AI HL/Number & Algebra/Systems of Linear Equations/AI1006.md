# AI1006

**Maximum mark: 8**

A healthcare company is designing a new deodorant stick that can be modelled by the shape of a parabola as the top, vertical lines as the sides and a horizontal line as the base, on the $x$-axis. The parabola has end points at $(0,7)$ and $(5,7)$, and vertex at $(2.5,8)$.

This design is shown in the diagram below. All distances are measured in centimetres.

![AI1006a](images/AI1006a.PNG)

The quadratic curve can be expressed in the form $y=ax^2+bx+c$ for
$0\leq x \leq 5$.

1.
    1.  Find the value of $c$.

    2.  Using the points $(2.5,8)$ and $(5,7)$, write two equations in $a$ and $b$.

    3.  Hence, find the equation of the quadratic curve. [5 marks]

2.  Find the area of the shaded region in the deodorant
    design.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  The value of $c$ is the value of the $y$ intercept. Hence,
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        &\hspace{-0.44em}c=7
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  As the curve passes through points $(2.5,8)$ and $(5,7)$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        a(2.5)^2 + b(2.5) + 7 &= 8 \\[12pt]
        &\hspace{-1.84cm}6.25a + 2.5b = 1  \hspace{3em}{(1)}\\
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        and
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        a(5)^2 + b(5) + 7 &= 7\\[12pt]
        &\hspace{-1.3cm}25a+5b = 0 \hspace{4.55em}{(2)}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3.  Solving the linear system (1) and (2), using G.D.C., we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        a &= -0.16 \\[12pt]
        b &= 0.8
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Therefore, the quadratic function is
        $y=-0.16 x^2 + 0.8x +7$

2.  Using the formula for the area enclosed by a function and the
    $x$-axis, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Area} &= \int_0^5 \big(-0.16 x^2 + 0.8x +7\big)\, \mathrm{d}x \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-2.4em}\text{Area} \approx 38.3\, \text{cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
