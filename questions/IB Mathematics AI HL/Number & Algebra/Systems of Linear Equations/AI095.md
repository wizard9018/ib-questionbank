# AI095

**Maximum mark: 6**

Taste of Home Magazine recommends using a combination of Cheddar, Brie
and Swiss when putting together cheese boards for parties. The
recommended total cheese board size for a party of
$10$ - $15$ people is
$1$ kilogram. The table below shows the weight, in hundred of grams, of
each kind of cheese required to make one kilogram of cheese combination,
and the cost of making each combination.

![1f95e531ff9a99fac83619dc0ed06ffca82c640c.svg](images/1f95e531ff9a99fac83619dc0ed06ffca82c640c.svg)

1.  By setting up a system of linear equations and using matrices, find\
    the price per kilogram of each type of cheese. [4 marks]

John prepares a cheese board with proportion of each cheese type, in
hundred grams, as shown in the table below.

![585de61d3f014a0443069387501b6401e1d28016.svg](images/585de61d3f014a0443069387501b6401e1d28016.svg)

2.  Calculate the amount of money John spent on cheese.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Letting $x$, $y$ and $z$ represent the cost per kilogram of Cheddar,
    Brie and Swiss respectively, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}

    \begin{array}{rcccccl}
    0.4x &+& 0.3y &+& 0.3z &=& 13.50 \\[3pt]
    0.2x &+& 0.6y &+& 0.2z &=& 17.30 \\[3pt]
    0.5x &+& 0.2y &+& 0.3z &=& 12.30
    \end{array} \hspace{0.75em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    If we express
    the system of linear equations $(1)$ as a matrix equation, we have

    ![2039bef8f45a8040f036a4cc94b8dcaf5b977a13.svg](images/2039bef8f45a8040f036a4cc94b8dcaf5b977a13.svg)

    Hence, if we solve the matrix equation $(2)$, we get

    ![730c2a8cba4ad3132e7b19b40901796479a3143d.svg](images/730c2a8cba4ad3132e7b19b40901796479a3143d.svg)

    Hence the prices of the three cheeses are

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Cheddar} &:\hspace{0.75em} \hspace{0.525em}\$10.5 \text{ per kg} \\[6pt]
    \text{Brie} &:\hspace{0.75em} \hspace{0.525em}\$22.5 \text{ per kg} \\[6pt]
    \text{Swiss} &:\hspace{0.75em} \hspace{0.525em}\$8.50 \text{ per kg}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The amount John spent on cheese is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0.4\times10.5 + 0.2\times22.5 + 0.4\times8.50 &= \hspace{0.525em}\$12.1
    \end{aligned}
    $$
