# AI097

**Maximum mark: 6**

When a ball is thrown from the top of a tall building, its height above
the ground after $t$ seconds is given by\
 $s(t) = at^2 + bt + c\,,$ where
$a,b,c \in \mathbb{R}$ and $s(t)$ is measured in metres. After $1$
second, the ball is $84.3$ m above the ground;
after $2$ seconds, $93.9$ m; after $8$ seconds,
$42.3$ m.

1.
    1.  Write down a system of three linear equations in terms of $a$,
        $b$ and $c$.

    2.  Use matrices to find the values of $a$, $b$ and $c$. [3 marks]

2.  Find the height of the building. [1 mark]

3.  Find the time it takes for the ball to hit the ground. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}

        \begin{array}{rcccccl}
        a &+& b &+& c &=& 84.3 \\[3pt]
        4a &+& 2b &+& c &=& 93.9 \\[3pt]
        64a &+& 8b &+& c &=& 42.3
        \end{array}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  If we express the system of linear equations $(1)$ as a matrix
        equation, we have

        ![b40b58be9e04029307e50815631013b0e434f45c.svg](images/b40b58be9e04029307e50815631013b0e434f45c.svg)

        Hence, if we solve the matrix equation $(2)$, we get

        ![04caede963f7db2aac1bc0812ea44cf5b6a29875.svg](images/04caede963f7db2aac1bc0812ea44cf5b6a29875.svg)

        Hence we find
         $a = -2.6, \hspace{0.25em} b = 17.4, \hspace{0.25em} c = 69.5$

2.  Evaluating $s(t)$ at $t = 0$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7.2em} s(0) &= -2.6(0)^2 + 17.4(0) + 69.5 \\[6pt]
    &= 69.5 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $s(t) = 0$ for $t$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -2.6t^2 + 17.4t + 69.5 &= 0 \hspace{10.8em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{13.6em} t \approx 9.50 \text{ seconds} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
