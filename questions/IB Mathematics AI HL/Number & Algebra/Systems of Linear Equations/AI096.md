# AI096

**Maximum mark: 6**

The Brown, Miller and Taylor families pay utility bills for their houses
each month. The table below shows the amount of electricity, water and
gas consumed during January by each family, and the total cost of the
utilities.

![4c79f485c80ba0239ecc4cfcc637b51df6e48bea.svg](images/4c79f485c80ba0239ecc4cfcc637b51df6e48bea.svg)

Let $x$, $y$ and $z$ represent the prices, in dollars, for
$1$ kWh of electricity,
$1$ m$^3$ of water and
$1$ m$^3$ of gas, respectively.

1.  Write down a system of three linear equations in terms of $x$, $y$
    and $z$\
    that represents the information in the table above. [2 marks]

2.  Using matrices, find the price for each of the utility. [2 marks]

The Smith family also pay utility bills each month. The table below
shows the amount of electricity, water and gas consumed during January
by the Smith family.

![00ca88f1397a994795e3b84ae8c62e66b719b0b0.svg](images/00ca88f1397a994795e3b84ae8c62e66b719b0b0.svg)

3.  Calculate the total cost of the utilities for the Smiths.
    [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}

    \begin{array}{rcccccl}
    950x &+& 72y &+& 448z &=& 343.59 \\[3pt]
    788x &+& 88y &+& 356z &=& 314.68 \\[3pt]
    532x &+& 49y &+& 298z &=& 219.43
    \end{array}  \hspace{0.6em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  If we express the system of linear equations $(1)$ as a matrix
    equation, we have

    ![30358a75db00011708b84ec520ef55df879b2a52.svg](images/30358a75db00011708b84ec520ef55df879b2a52.svg)

    Hence, if we solve the matrix equation $(2)$, we get

    ![d50fe173dd0736bf21c2972d4d6e6feec7a7d478.svg](images/d50fe173dd0736bf21c2972d4d6e6feec7a7d478.svg)

    Hence the prices for the utilities are

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Electricity} &:\hspace{0.75em} \hspace{0.525em}10.5 \text{ cents per kWh} \\[6pt]
    \text{Water} &:\hspace{0.75em} \hspace{0.525em}1.24 \text{ dollars per m$^3$} \\[6pt]
    \text{Gas} &:\hspace{0.75em} \hspace{0.525em}34.5 \text{ cents per m$^3$}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The total cost of the utilities for the Smiths is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    1124\times0.105 + 115\times1.24 + 598\times0.345 &= \$466.93 \approx \hspace{0.525em}\$467
    \end{aligned}
    $$
