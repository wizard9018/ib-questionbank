# AI098

**Maximum mark: 6**

When a coin is thrown from the top of a skyscraper, its height above the
ground after $t$ seconds is given by $s(t) = at^2 + bt + c$, where
$a,b,c \in \mathbb{R}$ and $s(t)$ is measured in metres. After $1$
second, the coin is $179.3$ m above the ground;
after $2$ seconds, $188.2$ m; after $6$ seconds,
$159.8$ m.

1.
    1.  Write down a system of three linear equations in terms of $a$,
        $b$ and $c$.

    2.  Hence find the values of $a$, $b$ and $c$. [3 marks]

2.  Find the height of the skyscraper. [1 mark]

3.  Find the time it takes for the coin to hit the ground. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}

        \begin{array}{rcccccl}
        a &+& b &+& c &=& 179.3 \\[3pt]
        4a &+& 2b &+& c &=& 188.2 \\[3pt]
        36a &+& 6b &+& c &=& 159.8
        \end{array}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  If we express the system of linear equations $(1)$ as a matrix
        equation, we have

        ![13c88ece23638748c2e63548748180dced5a9e26.svg](images/13c88ece23638748c2e63548748180dced5a9e26.svg)

        Hence, if we solve the matrix equation $(2)$, we get

        ![8c737a936777a47edd03c6c7de7ba744071b76ba.svg](images/8c737a936777a47edd03c6c7de7ba744071b76ba.svg)

        Hence we find
         $a = -3.2, \hspace{0.25em} b = 18.5, \hspace{0.25em} c = 164$

2.  Evaluating $s(t)$ at $t = 0$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.9em} s(0) &= -3.2(0)^2 + 18.5(0) + 164 \\[6pt]
    &= 164 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $s(t) = 0$ for $t$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -3.2t^2 + 18.5t + 164 &= 0 \hspace{10.6em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{13.5em} t \approx 10.6 \text{ seconds} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
