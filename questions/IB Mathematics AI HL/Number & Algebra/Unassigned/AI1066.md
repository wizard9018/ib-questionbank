# AI1066

**Maximum mark: 8**

Consider two power sources with voltages $V_1$ and $V_2$ respectively, where $\omega$ is the frequency of the power source in Hertz (Hz) and $t$ is time in seconds. 

It is given that

$$

\begin{aligned}

    V_1 = 161\sin(\omega t + 1.047)\,.

\end{aligned}

$$

When the two power sources are combined, the resultant voltage $V_1 + V_2$ is given by

$$

\begin{aligned}

    V_T = 240\sin(\omega t + 1.883)\,.

\end{aligned}

$$

1. Find $V_2$. Give your answer in the form $a\sin(\omega t + b)$, where $a,b \in \mathbb{R}$.[6 marks]

2. Hence, determine the difference in phase shift between $V_1$ and $V_2$.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  When adding sinusoidal functions, writing the functions in Euler's form makes sense as it allows use to easily add and subtract them.

    $V_1$ is the imaginary part of the complex number $z_1$ such that

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    z_1 &= 161\left(\cos(\omega t + 1.047)+\mathrm{i} \sin(\omega t + 1.047)\right) \\[6pt]

    &= 161e^{\mathrm{i}(\omega t + 1.047)}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    And $V_T$ is the imaginary part of the complex number $z_2$ such that

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    z_2 &= 240\left(\cos(\omega t + 1.883)+\mathrm{i} \sin(\omega t + 1.883)\right) \\[6pt]

    &= 240e^{\mathrm{i}(\omega t + 1.883)}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Given that $V_2 = V_T - V_1$, $V_2$ is the imaginary part of $z_2 - z_1$. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    z_2 - z_1 &= 240e^{\mathrm{i}(\omega t + 1.883)} - 161e^{\mathrm{i}(\omega t + 1.047)} \\[6pt]

    &= 240\left(e^{\mathrm{i}\omega t}\right)\left(e^{1.883\mathrm{i}}\right) - 161\left(e^{\mathrm{i}\omega t}\right)\left(e^{1.047\mathrm{i}}\right) \\[6pt]

    &= e^{\mathrm{i}\omega t}\left(240e^{1.883\mathrm{i}}-161e^{1.047\mathrm{i}}\right) \\[6pt]

    &= e^{\mathrm{i}\omega t}\left(178.1e^{2.618\mathrm{i}}\right) \hspace{3em}[\text{by G.D.C.}] \\[6pt]

    &= 178e^{\mathrm{i}(\omega t + 2.62)}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Converting to polar form, we have 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    V_2 &= \mathrm{Im}\left(178\left(\cos(\omega t + 2.62)+\mathrm{i} \sin(\omega t + 2.62)\right)\right) \\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-1em}\boxed{V_2 = 178\sin(\omega t + 2.62)}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The phase shift of $V_1$ is $1.047$ and the phase shift of $V_2$ is $2.62$. Hence the difference is given by

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \boxed{2.62 - 1.047 \approx 1.57}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
