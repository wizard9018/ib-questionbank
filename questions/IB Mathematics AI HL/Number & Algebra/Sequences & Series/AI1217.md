# AI1217

**Maximum mark: 7**

The cost of a membership at a gym increases each year to form an arithmetic sequence.

In the first year, the membership costs $\euro\,450$. Each year, the cost increases by $\euro\,22.50$.

1. Find the cost of membership in the 7th year. [2 marks]

2. Write down the sum of the costs over the 7 years using sigma notation. [2 marks]

3. Hence, find the total amount a member would pay for 7 years of membership. [1 mark]

4. A different gym has a total 7-year membership cost of $\euro\,3750$. State which gym is cheaper and by how much. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Cost in the 7th year occurs when $n=7$

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    u_n &= u_1  + (n - 1)d \\[4pt]

    u_7 &= 450 + (7 - 1)(22.5) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    u_7 &=\boxed{\text{\euro}\,585} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Sigma notation for the total cost is written as

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &\sum_{n=1}^{7} (450 + 22.5(n - 1))\\[6pt]

    &=\boxed{\displaystyle{\sum_{n=1}^{7}} (427.5 + 22.5n)} \tag*{\textbf{A2}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. The total sum of the first 7 years is found using the formula from part (b).

    

    We can type this into the G.D.C. directly

    

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    S_n &= \sum_{n=1}^{7} (427.5 + 22.5n)\\[10pt]

    S_7 &=\boxed{\text{\euro}\,3\hspace{0.1em}622.50} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
