# AI1021

**Maximum mark: 13**

On September 1st, an orchard commences the process of harvesting $36$ hectares of apple trees. At the end of September 4th, there were $30$ hectares remaining to be harvested, and at the end of September 8th, there were $24$ hectares remaining. Assuming that the number of hectares harvested each day is constant, the total number of hectares remaining to be harvested can be described by an arithmetic sequence.

1. Find the number of hectares of apple trees that are harvested each day. [3 marks]

2. Determine the number of hectares remaining to be harvested at the end of September 1st. [1 mark]

3. Determine the date on which the harvest will be complete. [2 marks]

In 2021 the orchard sold their apple crop for $$\$220\,000$$. It is expected that the selling price will then increase by $3.2\%$ annually for the next $7$ years.

4. Determine the amount of money the orchard will earn for their crop in 2026. Round your answer to the nearest dollar. [3 marks]

5. 1. Find the value of $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^8 \big(220\hspace{0.15em}000 \times 1.032^{n-1}\big)$. Round your answer to the nearest integer.

    2. Describe, in context, what the value in part (e) (i) represents. [3 marks]

6. Comment on whether it is appropriate to model this situation in terms of a geometric sequence. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The formula for the $n$th term of an arithmetic sequence is

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    u_n &= u_1+(n-1)d\\[-12pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Taking $u_1$ as the number of hectares remaining to be harvested at the end of September 1st, we have $u_4=30$, and $u_8=24$. Hence, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    u_1 + 3d &= 30 \\[11pt]

    u_1 + 7d &= 24 \\[-12pt]  \nonumber

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Hence subtracting (1) from (2), we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    4d &= -6 \\[8pt]

    d &= -1.5 \\[-12pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore, $$\text{the orchard harvests $1.5$ hectares of apple trees per day.}$$

2. There are 36 hectares of apple trees remaining to be harvested at the start of September 1st, and from part (a) we know that they are being harvested at 1.5 hectares per day. 

    Therefore, at the end of September 1st, there are $$\text{$34.5$ hectares}\,$$remaining to be harvested.

3. We must find the value of $n$ such that $u_n = 0$. We have $u_1 = 34.5$ and $d = -1.5$. Therefore, using the formula for the $n$th term of an arithmetic sequence, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    34.5 + (n-1)(-1.5) &= 0\\[7pt]

    34.5 -1.5n +1.5 &= 0\\[11pt]

    36 - 1.5n &= 0 \\[11pt]

    -1.5n &= -36 \\[11pt]

    n &= \dfrac{-36}{-1.5}\\[11pt]

    n &= 24

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore, $\text{the harvest will be complete at the end of September 24th.}$

4. From $2021$ until $2028$, the selling price of the apple crop can be modelled by a geometric sequence with $u_1 = 220\hspace{0.15em}000$ and $r = 1.032$. The selling price in $2026$ will be term $u_6$ in this sequence.

    Using the formula for the $n$th term in a geometric sequence, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    u_n &= u_1 \times r^{n-1} \\[11pt]

    u_6 &= 220\hspace{0.15em}000 \left(1.032\right)^{6-1} \\[11pt]

    &= 257526\\[-12pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    In $2026$, the orchard will earn $$\$257\,526\,$$ for their crop.
