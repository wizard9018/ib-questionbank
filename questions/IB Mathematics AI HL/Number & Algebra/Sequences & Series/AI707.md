# AI707

**Maximum mark: 16**

The number of seats in an auditorium follows a regular pattern where the
first row has $u_1$ seats, and the amount increases by the same amount,
$d$, each row. In the fifth row, there are $62$ seats and in the
thirteenth row there are $86$ seats.

1.  Write down an equation, in terms of $u_1$ and $d$, for the amount of
    seats

    1.  in the fifth row.
    2.  in the thirteenth row.[2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $u_1$ and $d$.[2 marks]

3.  Calculate the total number of seats if the auditorium has 20
    rows.[3 marks]

The cost of the ticket for a musical held at the auditorium is inversely
proportional to the seat's row. The price for a seat in the first row is
\$120 dollars, and the price decreases $3\%$ each row. Thus, the value
of the ticket for seats in the second row is \$116.40 and \$112.91 in
the third one, etc.

4.
    1.  Find the price of the ticket for a seat in the fifth row,
        rounding your answer to two decimal places.

    2.  Find the row of the seat at which the price of a ticket first
        falls below \$70.

    3.  Find the total revenue the auditorium generates by tickets sales
        if 40 seats in each of the 20 rows are sold. Give your answer
        rounded to the nearest dollar.[9 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The $n$th term of an arithmetic sequence can be found by
    $u_n=u_1+(n-1)d$. Hence, we get

    1.  $u_5=u_1+4d$
    2.  $u_{13}=u_1+12d$

2.  Substituting the values of $u_5=62$ and $u_{13}=86$ into the
    equations found in part (a), we get
    $$
    \begin{cases}
    u_1+4d = 62 \\
    u_1+12d= 86
    \end{cases}
    $$

    Using G.D.C. to solve, we get
    $u_1=50$
     $\text{ and }d=3$

3.  Using the formula for the sum of terms of an arithmetic sequence, we
     get
     $$
     \begin{aligned}
        S_{n}
        &= \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1+(n-1)d)\\[6pt]
        S_{20} &= \dfrac{20}{2}(2(50)+(19)3)\\[8pt]
        &\hspace{0.3em}= 1570
        \end{aligned}
    $$

4.
    1.  Let $p_n$ be the price of the ticket in the $n$-th row. Using
        the formula for the $n$-th term in a geometric sequence, we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
            p_n &= u_1r^{n-1} \\[7pt]
            p_5 &= 120(0.97)^4 \\[8pt]
            &\hspace{0.3em}=\text{\$}106.24
            \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Solving for the nearest integer the equation for the $n$-th
            term, we get
        $$
            \begin{aligned}
            70 &= 120(0.97)^{n-1} \\[7pt]
            n &\approx 18.70
            \end{aligned}
        $$
        Therefore, the price of a
        ticket first falls below \$70 in the
        $19\text{th row}$.

    3.  The sum of terms in a geometric sequence can be found by
            $s_n=\dfrac{u_1(1-r^n)}{1-r}$. Hence, 1 ticket sold in each row
            can calculated by
        $$
            \begin{aligned}
            s_{20} &=\dfrac{120(1-0.97^{20})}{1-0.97} \\[7pt]
            &\approx 1824.82
            \end{aligned}
        $$

        40 tickets sold in each row can therefore be calculated by

        $$
            \begin{aligned}
            40 \times s_{20} &=40 \times 1824.82 \\[9pt]
            &\hspace{0.3em}\text{\$}72\text{,}993
            \end{aligned}
        $$
