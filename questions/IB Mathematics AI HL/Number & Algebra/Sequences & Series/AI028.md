# AI028

**Maximum mark: 6**

On the first day of September, $2019$, Gloria planted $5$ flowers in her
garden. The number of flowers, which she plants at every day of the
month, forms an arithmetic sequence. The number of flowers she is going
to plant in the last day of September is $63$.

1.  Find the common difference of the sequence. [2 marks]

2.  Find the **total** number of flowers Gloria is going to plant during
    September.[2 marks]

3.  Gloria estimated she would plant $1000$ flowers in the month of
    September. Calculate the percentage error in Gloria's estimate.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have $u_1 = 5$ and $u_{30}=63$.
    Hence, using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with
    $n=30$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_{30} &= u_1 + (30-1)d \\[6pt]
    63 &= 5 + (30-1)d
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{5.8em}d = 2 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

2.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1+(n-1)d)$
    with $n = 30$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.5em} S_{30} &= \dfrac{30}{2}(2u_1+(30-1)d) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{30}{2}(2(5)+(30-1)(2)) \\[12pt]
    &= 1020
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the percentage error formula
    $\epsilon = \bigg|\dfrac{v_\mathrm{A} - v_\mathrm{E}}{v_\mathrm{E}}\bigg|\times 100\hspace{0.05em}\%$
    with $v_\mathrm{E} = 1020$ and $v_\mathrm{A} = 1000$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.4em} \epsilon &= \bigg|\dfrac{1000 - 1020}{1020}\bigg|\times 100\hspace{0.05em}\% \\[6pt]
    &= 1.96\hspace{0.05em}\%
    \end{aligned}
    $$
