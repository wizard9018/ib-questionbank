# AI934

**Maximum mark: 7**

Elon is challenged to a speed climb at a local mountain. He has to reach
a height of $400$ metres above the ground within four hours.

Elon knows he can climb $150$ metres in the first hour. Due to
increasing tiredness, each hour he can only climb $75\hspace{0.05em}\%$
of the height climbed in the previous hour.

1.  Verify that Elon reaches his target height of $400$ metres in four
    hours.[2 marks]

The mountain has a height of $650$ metres. Elon decides to attempt to
climb to the summit.

2.  Determine whether he can reach the summit of the mountain
    if he continues climbing, given his increasing tiredness. Justify
    your answer.[2 marks]

On a different day, Elon climbs with energy snacks, which help to reduce
his tiredness as he climbs. On this day, Elon again climbs 150 metres in
the first hour, but then $k\hspace{0.05em}\%$ of the height he climbed
in the previous hour, where $k > 75$.

3.  Calculate the minimum value of $k$, given that on this day
    Elon is able to reach the summit. Give your answer as a percentage,
    to the nearest whole number.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have a geometric sequence with first term $u_1 = 150$ and common
    ratio $r = 0.75$. Hence, using the formula for the sum of the first
    $n$ terms, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_{n} &= \dfrac{u_1(1-r^n)}{1-r} \\[12pt]
    S_{4} &= \dfrac{150(1-0.75^{4})}{1-0.75} \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-1em}S_{4} \approx 410 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m} > 400 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the formula for the sum to infinity of a geometric
    sequence, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_\infty &= \dfrac{150}{1 - 0.75}  \hspace{0.1em} \\[12pt]
    &= 600 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $\text{Elon can't reach the summit since \(600 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m} < 650 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m}\).}\hspace{-0.15em}$

3.  Using the formula for the sum to infinity of a geometric
    sequence, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_\infty &= \dfrac{150}{1 - r}  \hspace{0.1em} \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{150}{1 - r} &\geq 650 \hspace{0.1em} \\[12pt]
    r &\geq 0.76923... \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $\text{The minimum value of k is 77\%.}$
