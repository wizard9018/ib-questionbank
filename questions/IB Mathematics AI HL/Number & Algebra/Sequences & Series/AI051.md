# AI051

**Maximum mark: 6**

A bouncy ball is dropped out of a second story classroom window,
$5$ m off the ground. Every time the ball hits
the ground it bounces $89$ % of its previous
height.

1.  Find the height the ball reaches after the $11$th bounce.
    [3 marks]

2.  Find the total distance travelled by the ball until it comes to
    rest. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have

    ![8f1fbf3b941881b0c3497fa818d77dc32a216ccc.svg](images/8f1fbf3b941881b0c3497fa818d77dc32a216ccc.svg)

    Hence the height of successive bounces of the ball form a geometric
    sequence with the first term $u_1 = 5(0.89)$ and the common ratio
    $r = 0.89$.

    Hence, using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1r^{n-1}$ with
    $n = 11$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_{11} &= u_1r^{11-1} \\[6pt]
    &= [5(0.89)](0.89)^{11-1} \\[7pt]
    &\approx 1.39\text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the sum of an infinite geometric sequence formula
    $S_{\infty} = \dfrac{u_1}{1-r}$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.8em} [\text{total distance travelled}] &= 5 + 10(0.89) +  10(0.89)^2 + 10(0.89)^3 + \cdots \\[11pt]
    &= 5 + 2[5(0.89) +  5(0.89)^2 + 5(0.89)^3 + \cdots] \\[12pt]
    &= 5 + 2S_{\infty} \hspace{3em} [u_1 = 5(0.89) \text{ \& } r = 0.89] \\[6pt]
    &= 5 + 2\bigg[\dfrac{5(0.89)}{1 - 0.89}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &\approx 85.9 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
