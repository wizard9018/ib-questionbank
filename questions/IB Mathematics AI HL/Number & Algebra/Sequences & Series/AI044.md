# AI044

**Maximum mark: 6**

**In this question give all answers correct to the nearest whole
number.**

A population of goats on an island starts at $232$. The population is
expected\
to increase by $15$ % each year.

1.  Find the expected population size after:
    1.  $10$ years;

    2.  $20$ years. [4 marks]

2.  Find the number of years it will take for the population to reach
    $15\hspace{0.15em}000$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have

    ![537894a1e06c34454736a91f01647c46ca2f4fa3.svg](images/537894a1e06c34454736a91f01647c46ca2f4fa3.svg)

    Hence the population size is expected to increase according to a
    geometric sequence with the first term $u_1 = 232(1.15)$ and the
    common ratio $r = 1.15$.

    1.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1r^{n-1}$ with $n = 10$,
        we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3.1em} u_{10} &= u_1r^{10-1} \\[6pt]
        &= [232(1.15)](1.15)^{10-1} \\[7pt]
        &\approx 939
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1r^{n-1}$ with $n = 20$,
        we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3.1em} u_{20} &= u_1r^{20-1} \\[6pt]
        &= [232(1.15)](1.15)^{20-1} \\[7pt]
        &\approx 3797
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Solving the equation $u_n = 15\hspace{0.15em}000$ for $n$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_1r^{n-1} &= 15\hspace{0.15em}000 \\[6pt]
    \hspace{1.5em} [232(1.15)](1.15)^{n-1} &= 15\hspace{0.15em}000 \\[7pt]
    n &\approx 29.8 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence it will take
     $30 \text{ years}$
    for the population of goats to reach $15\hspace{0.15em}000$.
