# AI049

**Maximum mark: 6**

Peter is playing on a swing during a school lunch break. The height of
the first swing was $2$ m and every subsequent
swing was $84$ % of the previous one. Peter's
friend, Ronald, gives him a push whenever the height falls below
$1$ m.

1.  Find the height of the third swing. [2 marks]

2.  Find the number of swings before Ronald gives Peter a push.
    [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the total height of swings if Peter is left to swing until
    coming\
    to rest. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have a geometric sequence with the first term $u_1 = 2$ and the
    common ratio $r = 0.84$.

    Hence, using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1r^{n-1}$ with $n = 3$,
    we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_3 &= u_1r^{3-1} \\[6pt]
    &= 2\hspace{0.05em}(0.84)^{3-1} \\[7pt]
    &\approx 1.41 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Solving the inequality $u_n < 1$ for $n$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_1r^{n-1} &< 1 \\[6pt]
    \hspace{3em} 2\hspace{0.05em}(0.84)^{n-1} &< 1 \\[7pt]
    4.98 &< n \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
       Hence
    the swing needs a push after
     $5$
    swings.

3.  Using the sum of an infinite geometric sequence formula
    $S_{\infty} = \dfrac{u_1}{1-r}$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_{\infty} &= \dfrac{2}{1-0.84} \hspace{1.3em} \\[12pt]
    &= 12.5 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
