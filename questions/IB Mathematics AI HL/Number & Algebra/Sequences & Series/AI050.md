# AI050

**Maximum mark: 6**

The sides of a square are $8$ cm long. A new
square is formed by joining the midpoints of the adjacent sides and two
of the resulting triangles are shaded as shown.

![727d93a86010415452fdb83a6ee2e0781e163bf4.svg](images/727d93a86010415452fdb83a6ee2e0781e163bf4.svg)

This process is repeated
$5$ more times to form the diagram below.

![90943a34ec184ff8999f55ac9b28e149e8510dc3.svg](images/90943a34ec184ff8999f55ac9b28e149e8510dc3.svg)

1.  Find the total area of the shaded region in the right hand diagram
    above. [3 marks]

2.  Find the total area of the shaded region if the process is repeated
    indefinitely.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The side length of the first pair of shaded triangles is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{side length} &= \dfrac{8}{2} \\[6pt]
    &= 4\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm} \hspace{2.9em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence
    their total area is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.9em} \text{area} &= 2\left[\dfrac{1}{2}(4)(4)\right] \\[6pt]
    &= 16\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Similarly, we have

    ![e6f45d593aa89a3c2cdb821ce1c78f8d901f645f.svg](images/e6f45d593aa89a3c2cdb821ce1c78f8d901f645f.svg)

    Hence the area of successive pairs of shaded triangles form a
    geometric $\text{sequence}$ with the first term $u_1 = 16$
    and the common ratio $r = 1/2$.

    Hence, using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{u_1(1-r^n)}{1-r}$ with $n = 6$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} S_6 &= \dfrac{u_1(1-r^6)}{1-r} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{16\hspace{0.05em}(1-(1/2)^6)}{1-1/2} \\[12pt]
    &\approx 31.5 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the sum of an infinite geometric sequence formula
    $S_{\infty} = \dfrac{u_1}{1-r}$, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_{\infty} &= \dfrac{16}{1-1/2} \hspace{0.5em} \\[12pt]
    &= 32 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
