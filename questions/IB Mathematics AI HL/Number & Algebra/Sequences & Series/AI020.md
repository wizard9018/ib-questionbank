# AI020

**Maximum mark: 6**

An arithmetic sequence has $u_1 = 12$, $u_2 = 21$, $u_3 = 30$.

1.  Find the common difference, $d$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $u_{10}$. [2 marks]

3.  Find $S_{10}$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The common difference is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= u_2 - u_1 \hspace{1.5em} \\[6pt]
    &= 21 - 12 \\[6pt]
    &= 9
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with $n = 10$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.35em} u_{10} &= u_1 + (10-1)d \\[6pt]
    &= 12 + (10-1)(9) \\[6pt]
    &= 93
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(u_1+u_n)$
    with $n=10$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_{10} &= \dfrac{10}{2}(u_1+u_{10}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{10}{2}(12+93) \\[10pt]
    &= 525
    \end{aligned}
    $$
