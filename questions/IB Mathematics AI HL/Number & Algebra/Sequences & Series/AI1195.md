# AI1195

**Maximum mark: 16**

Bill is planning to hike the Appalachian trail, a $3\hspace{0.1em}600$ kilometre long walking track along the east coast of the USA.

 

Bill plans to hike $2.1$ kilometres on the first day, then increase his distance by $200$ metres every day, so that on day $2$ he will hike $2.3$ kilometres, on day $3$ he will hike $2.5$ kilometres and so on.

1. Calculate how far Bill will hike on day 90. [2 marks]

2. Determine how many days it will take Bill to hike the entire Appalachian trail. [3 marks]

3. Hence determine how far Bill will have to hike on the final day. [1 mark]

Bill can hike at an average speed of 5 kilometres per hour.

4. Determine how long Bill will have to hike for on the final day.[2 marks]

Bill’s friend Stephen starts hiking the Appalachian trail on the same day but uses a different strategy to Bill.

 

Stephen will hike 1 kilometre on the first day, then increase his distance by $3\%$ per day, so that on day 2 he will hike 1.03 kilometres, on day 3 he will hike 1.0609 kilometres and so on.

5. Calculate how much further Bill hikes than Stephen on day 90. [3 marks]

6. Determine the day on which Stephen passes Bill. [3 marks]

7. Calculate how many days earlier Stephen finishes the entire trail than Bill.   [2 marks]
