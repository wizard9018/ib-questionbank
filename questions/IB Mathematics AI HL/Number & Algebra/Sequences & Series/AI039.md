# AI039

**Maximum mark: 6**

Emily starts reading Leo Tolstoy's War and Peace on the $1$st of
February. The number of pages she reads each day increases by the same
number on each successive day.

![c94a768fb53af8987d3e1115bdd47ee0b1976776.svg](images/c94a768fb53af8987d3e1115bdd47ee0b1976776.svg)

1.  Calculate the number of pages Emily reads on the $14$th of February.
    [3 marks]

2.  Find the exact total number of pages Emily reads in the $28$ days of
    February.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have an arithmetic sequence with the first term $u_1 = 8$ and the
    common difference $d = 3$.

    Hence, using the $n$the term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with
    $n = 14$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_{14} &= u_1 + (14-1)d \hspace{1.75em} \\[6pt]\
    &= 8 + (14-1)(3) \\[6pt]
    &= 47 \text{ pages}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1 + (n-1)d)$ with $n = 28$, we obtain

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{1.7em} S_{28} &= \dfrac{28}{2}(2u_1 + (28-1)d) \\[6pt]
     &= \dfrac{28}{2}(2(8) + (28-1)(3)) \\[11pt]
     &= 1358 \text{ pages}
     \end{aligned}
     $$
