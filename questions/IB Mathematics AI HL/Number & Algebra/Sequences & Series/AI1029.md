# AI1029

**Maximum mark: 5**

A bouncy ball is dropped out of a second story classroom window,  $5\,$m off the ground. Every time the ball hits the ground it bounces $89\,$% of its previous height.

1. Find the height the ball reaches after the $2$nd bounce. [2 marks]

2. Find the total distance the ball has travelled when it hits the ground for the $5$th time. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The height of the ball after each bounce follows a geometric sequence, with first term $u_1 = 5(0.89)$ and common ratio $r = 0.89$. 

    Using the formula for the $n$th term of a geometric sequence, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    u_{2} &= u_1r^{2-1} \\[6pt]

    &= [5(0.89)](0.89)^1 \\[7pt]

    &\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{\approx 3.96\text{ m}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The total distance travelled by the ball is the sum of two separate geometric sequences.

    The first, when the ball is travelling downwards, has first term $u_1=5$ and common ratio $r=0.89$. When the ball hits the ground for the $5$th time, it has travelled downwards a distance that is equivalent to the sum of the first five terms of this sequence. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    S_n &= \dfrac{u_1(1-r^n)}{1-r} \\[6pt]

    S_5 &= \dfrac{5(1-0.89^5)}{1-0.89} \\[7pt]

    &\approx 20.07 \,\text{m}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    The second, when the ball is travelling upwards, has first term $u_1=5(0.89)$ and common ratio $r=0.89$. When the ball hits the ground for the $5$th time, it has travelled upwards a distance that is equivalent to the sum of the first four terms of this sequence. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    S_n &= \dfrac{u_1(1-r^n)}{1-r} \\[6pt]

    S_4 &= \dfrac{5(0.89)(1-0.89^4)}{1-0.89} \\[7pt]

    &\approx 15.07 \,\text{m}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    The total distance travelled is given by $20.07 + 15.07$.

    The total distance travelled by the ball is approximately $\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{35.1\,\text{metres}}$.
