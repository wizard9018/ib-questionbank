# AI031

**Maximum mark: 15**

A ball is dropped from the top of the Eiffel Tower, $324$ metres from
the ground. The ball falls a distance of $4.9$ metres during the first
second, $14.7$ metres during the next second, $24.5$ metres during the
third second, and so on. The distances that the ball falls each second
form an arithmetic sequence.

1.
    1.  Find $d$, the common difference of the sequence.

    2.  Find $u_5$, the fifth term of the sequence. [2 marks]

2.  Find $S_6$, the sum of the first $6$ terms of the sequence.
    [2 marks]

3.  Find the time the ball will take to reach the ground. Give your
    answer in seconds correct to one decimal place. [3 marks]

Assuming the ball is dropped another time from a much higher height than
of the Eiffel Tower,

4.  find the distance the ball travels from the start of the
    $10$th second to the end of the $15$th second. [3 marks]

The Eiffel Tower in Paris, France was opened in $1889$, and $1.9$
million visitors ascended it during that first year. The number of
people who visited the tower the following year ($1890$) was $2$
million.

5.  Calculate the percentage increase in the number of visitors
    from $1889$ to $1890$. Give your answer correct to one decimal
    place. [2 marks]

6.  Use your answer to **part (e)** to estimate the number of
    visitors in $1900$, assuming that the number of visitors continues
    to increase at the same percentage rate. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have $u_1 = 4.9$ and $u_2 = 14.7$.
        Hence the common difference is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        d &= u_2 - u_1 \hspace{1.4em} \\[6pt]
        &= 14.7 - 4.9 \\[6pt]
        &= 9.8\hspace{0.25em}\text{m}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with $n = 5$,
        we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2.4em} u_5 &= u_1 + (5-1)d \\[6pt]
        &= 4.9 + (5-1)(9.8) \\[6pt]
        &= 44.1\hspace{0.25em}\text{m}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1 + (n-1)d)$ with $n = 6$, we get

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{7.4em} S_6 &= \dfrac{6}{2}(2u_1 + (6-1)d) \\[4pt]
     &= \dfrac{6}{2}(2(4.9) + (6-1)(9.8)) \\[9pt]
     &= 176\hspace{0.25em}\text{m}
     \end{aligned}
     $$

3.  Solving the equation $S_n = 324$ for $n$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1 + (n-1)d) &= 324 \\[3pt]
    \dfrac{n}{2}(2(4.9) + (n-1)(9.8)) &= 324 \\[6pt]
    n &\approx 8.13 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.45em} n \approx 8.1\text{ seconds}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1 + (n-1)d)$ with $n = 9$ and $n=15$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_{15} - S_9 &= \bigg[\dfrac{15}{2}(2u_1 + (15-1)d)\bigg] - \bigg[\dfrac{9}{2}(2u_1 + (9-1)d)\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= \bigg[\dfrac{15}{2}(2(4.9) + (15-1)(9.8))\bigg] - \bigg[\dfrac{9}{2}(2(4.9) + (9-1)(9.8))\bigg] \\[11pt]
    &= 705.6\hspace{0.25em}\text{m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Using the percentage increase formula
    $\%\hspace{0.2em}\textbf{increase} = \dfrac{v_\mathrm{E}-v_\mathrm{S}}{v_\mathrm{S}}\times 100\hspace{0.05em}\%$
    with $v_\mathrm{S} = 1.9$ and $v_\mathrm{E} = 2.0$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \%\hspace{0.2em}\textbf{increase} &= \dfrac{2.0-1.9}{1.9}\times100\hspace{0.05em}\% \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 5.3\hspace{0.05em}\%
    \end{aligned}
    $$

6.  We have a geometric sequence with the first term $v_1 = 1.9$ million
    and the common ratio $r =  1.0526$.

    Hence, using the $n$th term formula $v_n = v_1r^{n-1}$ with
    $n = 12$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.8em} v_{12} &= v_1r^{12-1} \\[6pt]
    &= (1.9\text{ million})(1.0526)^{12-1} \\[7pt]
    &= 3.34 \text{ million}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
