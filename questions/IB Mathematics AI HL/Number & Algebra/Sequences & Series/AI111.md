# AI111

**Maximum mark: 15**

Charles has a New Years Resolution that he wants to be able to complete
$100$ pushups in one go without a break. He sets out a training regime
whereby he completes $20$ pushups on the first day, then adds $5$
pushups each day thereafter.

1.  Write down the number of pushups Charles completes
    1.  on the $4$th training day;

    2.  on the $n$th training day. [3 marks]

On the $k$th training day Charles completes $100$
pushups for the first time.

2.  Find the value of $k$. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the total number of pushups Charles completes on
    the first $10$ training days. [4 marks]

Charles is also working on improving his long distance swimming in
preparation for an Iron Man event in $12$ weeks time. He swims a total
of $10\hspace{0.15em}000$ metres in the first week, and plans to
increase this by $10$ % each week up until the
event.

4.  Find the distance Charles swims in the $6$th week of
    training. [3 marks]

5.  Calculate the total distance Charles swims until the event.
    [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have an arithmetic sequence with the first term $u_1 = 20$ and
    the common difference $d = 5$.

    1.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with $n=4$, we
        have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        u_4 &= u_1 + (4-1)d \\[6pt]
        &= 20 + (4-1)(5) \\[6pt]
        &= 35
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        u_n &= 20 + (n-1)(5) \\[6pt]
        &= 20+5(n-1)
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Solving the equation $u_k = 100$ for $k$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    20+5(k-1) &= 100 \hspace{7.5em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7.9em} k = 17 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1 + (n-1)d)$ with $n = 10$, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.6em} S_{10} &= \dfrac{10}{2}(2u_1 + (10-1)d) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{10}{2}(2(20) + (10-1)(5)) \\[12pt]
    &= 425
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  We have a geometric sequence with the first term
    $v_1 = 10\hspace{0.15em}000$ and the $\text{common}$ ratio
    $r = 1.1$.
    Hence, using the $n$th term formula $v_n = v_1r^{n-1}$ with $n=6$,
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} v_6 &= v_1r^{6-1} \\[6pt]
    &= 10\hspace{0.15em}000\hspace{0.05em}(1.1)^{6-1} \\[7pt]
    &= 16105.1 \\[7pt]
    &\approx 16\hspace{0.15em}100\text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{v_1(r^n-1)}{r-1}$
    with $n=12$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.9em} S_{12} &= \dfrac{v_1(r^{12}-1)}{r-1} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{10\hspace{0.15em}000\hspace{0.05em}(1.1^{12}-1)}{1.1-1} \\[12pt]
    &\approx 213\hspace{0.15em}842.84 \\[16pt]
    &\approx 214\hspace{0.15em}000 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
