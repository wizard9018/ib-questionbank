# AI046

**Maximum mark: 6**

A bouncy ball is dropped from a height of $2$ metres onto a concrete
floor. After hitting the floor, the ball rebounds back up to
$80$ % of its previous height, and this pattern
continues on repeatedly, until coming to rest.

1.  Show that the total distance travelled by the ball until coming to
    rest can be expressed by

    $2 + 4(0.8) + 4(0.8)^2 + 4(0.8)^3 + \cdots$[2 marks]

2.  Find an expression for the total distance travelled by the ball, in
    terms of the number of bounces, $n$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the total distance travelled by the ball. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have

    ![866fc824efe482d8b182d4c5b9e109a2fdbd4aca.svg](images/866fc824efe482d8b182d4c5b9e109a2fdbd4aca.svg)

    Hence the total distance travelled by the ball until coming to rest
    is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2 + 4(0.8) + 4(0.8)^2 + 4(0.8)^3 + \cdots
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{u_1(1 - r^n)}{1-r}$
    for a geometric sequence

    with $u_1 = 4$ and $r = 0.8$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} [\text{sum of $n$ terms of $(1)$}] &= 2 + 4(0.8) + 4(0.8)^2 + \cdots + 4(0.8)^{n-1} \\[14pt]
    &= -2 + \big[4 + 4(0.8) + 4(0.8)^2 + \cdots + 4(0.8)^{n-1}\big] \hspace{1.3em} \\[8pt]
    &= -2 + \bigg[\dfrac{4(1 - 0.8^{n})}{1 - 0.8}\bigg] \\[8pt]
    &= -2 + 20(1 - 0.8^{n}) \\[8pt]
    &= -2+20-20(0.8)^n\\[8pt]
    &= 18 - 20(0.8)^n
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of an infinite geometric sequence formula
    $S_{\infty} = \dfrac{u_1}{1-r}$ with

    $u_1 = 4$ and $r = 0.8$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &= 2 + 4(0.8) + 4(0.8)^2 + 4(0.8)^3 + \cdots \\[14pt]
    &= -2 + \big[4 + 4(0.8) + 4(0.8)^2 + 4(0.8)^3 + \cdots\big] \\[8pt]
    &= -2 + \bigg[\dfrac{4}{1 - 0.8}\bigg] \\[8pt]
    &= 18 \text{ metres}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
