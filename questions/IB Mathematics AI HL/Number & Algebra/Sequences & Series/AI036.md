# AI036

**Maximum mark: 6**

The $15$th term of an arithmetic sequence is $21$ and the common
difference is $-4$.

1.  Find the first term of the sequence. [2 marks]

2.  Find the $29$th term of the sequence. [2 marks]

3.  Find the sum of the first $40$ terms of the sequence. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_{1} + (n-1)d$ with $n = 15$,
    and solving for $u_1$ we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    21 &= u_{1} + (15-1)d \\[6pt]
    u_1&= 21 - (14)(-4) \\[6pt]
    &= 77
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with $n = 29$, we
    find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_{29} &= u_1 + (29-1)d \\[6pt]
    &= 77 + (29-1)(-4) \hspace{0.4em} \\[6pt]
    &= -35
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1 + (n-1)d)$ with $n = 40$, we obtain

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{2.8em} S_{40} &= \dfrac{40}{2}(2u_1 + (40-1)d) \\[6pt]
     &= \dfrac{40}{2}(2(77) + (40-1)(-4)) \\[11pt]
     &= -40
     \end{aligned}
     $$
