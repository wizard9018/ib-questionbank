# AI088

**Maximum mark: 5**

Two voltage sources are connected to a circuit. At time $t$ milliseconds
(ms), the voltage from the first source is $V_1(t) = 12\cos(20t)$ and
the voltage from the second source is $V_2(t) = 18\cos(20t+5)$, where
both $V_1(t)$ and $V_2(t)$ are measured in
volts.

1.  Write, in the form $V(t) = A\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\omega t+\varphi)$,
    an expression for the total voltage in the circuit at time $t$ ms.
    [4 marks]

2.  Hence write down the highest voltage in the circuit. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The total voltage in the circuit at time $t$ ms is given by
    $V(t) = V_1(t) + V_2(t)$.

    Hence we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} V(t) &= 12\cos(20t) + 18\cos(20t+5) \\[7pt]
    &= \mathrm{Re}\big[12\mathop{\mathrm{cis}}(20t) + 18\mathop{\mathrm{cis}}(20t+5)\big] \\[6pt]
    &= \mathrm{Re}\big[12e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}20t} + 18e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(20t+5)}\big]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we further have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    12e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}20t} + 18e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(20t+5)} &= e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}20t}\big[12 + 18e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}5}\big] \\[6pt]
    &\approx e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}20t}\big[17.1 - 17.3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\big] \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\[6pt]
    &\approx e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}20t}\big[24.3e^{-{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}0.790}\big] \hspace{3.1em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\[6pt]
    &\approx 24.3e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(20t-0.790)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V(t) &\approx \mathrm{Re}\big[24.3e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(20t-0.790)}\big] \\[6pt]
    &\approx 24.3\hspace{0.05em}\cos(20t-0.790) \hspace{3em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Hence the highest voltage in the circuit is
     $24.3\hspace{0.25em}\text{volts}$
