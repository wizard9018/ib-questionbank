# AI085

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $z = re^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\frac{\pi}{6}}$ where
$r \in \mathbb{R}^+$.

1.  For $r = \sqrt{3}$,
    1.  express $z^2$ and $z^3$ in the form
        $a+b{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ where
        $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$;

    2.  draw $z^2$ and $z^3$ on the following Argand diagram.
        [4 marks]

        ![633c15de281dbaeb852d4279b825abc3a94854e8.svg](images/633c15de281dbaeb852d4279b825abc3a94854e8.svg)

2.  Given that the integer powers of
    $w = \dfrac{z}{6+2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}}$ lie on a
    unit circle centred

    at the origin, find the value of $r$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  If we express $z^2$ and $z^3$ in Cartesian form, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{4em} z^2 &= 3e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\frac{\pi}{3}} \\[10pt]
        &= 3\Big[\hspace{-0.1em}\cos\Big(\frac{\pi}{3}\Big) + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\sin\Big(\frac{\pi}{3}\Big)\Big] \\[4pt]
        &=\dfrac{3}{2} + \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        and
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{5.3em} z^3 &= 3\sqrt{3}e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\frac{\pi}{2}} \\[10pt]
        &= 3\sqrt{3}\Big[\hspace{-0.1em}\cos\Big(\frac{\pi}{2}\Big) + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\sin\Big(\frac{\pi}{2}\Big)\Big] \\[10pt]
        &=0+3\sqrt{3}{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  If we draw $z^2$ and $z^3$ on the following Argand diagram, we
        have

        ![ecdfaa8e1f1031e684d45d25638e79596842fb1a.svg](images/ecdfaa8e1f1031e684d45d25638e79596842fb1a.svg)

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |6+2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}| &= \sqrt{6^2 + 2^2} \hspace{2.2em} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{40} \\[6pt]
    &= 2\sqrt{10}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the properties of modulus,
    we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |w^k| &= 1 \hspace{3em} [\text{for all $k \in \mathbb{Z}$}]  \\[16pt]
    |w|^k &= 1 \\[17pt]
    |w| &= 1 \\[15pt]
    \hspace{3.9em} \Big|\dfrac{z}{6+2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}}\Big| &= 1 \hspace{3em} [w = z/(6+2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu})] \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{|z|}{|6+2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}|} &= 1 \\[9pt]
    \dfrac{r}{2\sqrt{10}} &= 1 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    r = 2\sqrt{10} \hspace{1.3em}
    $$
