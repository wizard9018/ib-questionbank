# AI081

**Maximum mark: 6**

A circle is drawn on an Argand diagram as shown below. The tangent to
the circle from the point B$(0,9)$ meets the circle at the point A as
shown. Let $w = \vec{\mathrm{OA}}$.

![756bc10d49856365edc8188e18631d432452e02d.svg](images/756bc10d49856365edc8188e18631d432452e02d.svg)

1.  Show that $|w| = 3\sqrt{3}$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $\arg w$. [2 marks]

3.  Hence write $w$ in the form
    $a+b{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ where
    $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have

    ![be4795ddca85773f31f1a7dd616d51f34bbef9b7.svg](images/be4795ddca85773f31f1a7dd616d51f34bbef9b7.svg)

    Using the right triangle ABC, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.4em} \cos(\text{A\^{C}B}) &= \dfrac{3}{6} \hspace{3em} \left(=\dfrac{1}{2}\right) \\[6pt]
    \text{A\^{C}B} &= \dfrac{\pi}{3} \\[6pt]
    \text{A\^{C}O} &= \dfrac{2\pi}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the
    cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8.4em} |w|^2 &= 3^2 + 3^2 - 2(3)(3)\cos \Big(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\Big) \\[3pt]
    |w|^2 &= 18 - 18\hspace{-0.1em}\left[-\dfrac{1}{2}\right] \\[6pt]
    |w|^2 &= 27 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |w| = 3\sqrt{3} \hspace{0.9em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    ![be4795ddca85773f31f1a7dd616d51f34bbef9b7.svg](images/be4795ddca85773f31f1a7dd616d51f34bbef9b7.svg)

2.  Using the isosceles triangle OAC, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \text{A\^{O}C} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\hspace{0.05em}(\pi - \text{A\^{C}O}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\bigg[\pi - \dfrac{2\pi}{3}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\pi}{6}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the argument of $w$ is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} \arg w &= \dfrac{\pi}{2} - \text{A\^{O}C} \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{\pi}{2} - \dfrac{\pi}{6} \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{\pi}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  If we write $w$ in Cartesian form, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.4em} w &= 3\sqrt{3}\mathop{\mathrm{cis}}\left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) \\[9pt]
    &= 3\sqrt{3}\bigg[\hspace{-0.1em}\cos \left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\sin \left(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\hspace{-0.2em}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= 3\sqrt{3}\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2} + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\bigg] \\[7pt]
    &= \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} + \dfrac{9}{2}{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
