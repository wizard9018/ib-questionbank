# AI083

**Maximum mark: 6**

Points A and B represent the complex numbers
$z_1 = \sqrt{3} - {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ and
$z_2 = -3 - 3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ as shown on an
Argand diagram below.

![b9acbcc9be3dbe232f939a960c2a1907744b9b96.svg](images/b9acbcc9be3dbe232f939a960c2a1907744b9b96.svg)

1.  Find the angle AOB. [2 marks]

2.  Find the argument of $z_1z_2$. [1 mark]

3.  Given that the real powers of $pz_1z_2$, for $p > 0$, all lie on a
    unit circle centred at the origin, find the exact value of $p$.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \arg z_1 &= \tan^{-1}\left(\hspace{-0.2em}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\hspace{-0.1em}\right) \\[6pt]
    &= -\dfrac{\pi}{6}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    and
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \arg z_2 &= \tan^{-1}(1) \hspace{0.9em} \\[12pt]
    &= \dfrac{\pi}{4} - \pi \\[8pt]
    &= -\dfrac{3\pi}{4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.3em} \text{A\^{O}B} &= |\arg z_2| - |\arg z_1| \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{3\pi}{4} - \dfrac{\pi}{6} \\[7pt]
    &= \dfrac{7\pi}{12}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The argument of $z_1z_2$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \arg(z_1z_2) &= \arg z_1 + \arg z_2 \\[10pt]
    &= -\dfrac{\pi}{6} - \dfrac{3\pi}{4} \\[7pt]
    &= -\dfrac{11\pi}{12}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.2em} |z_1| &= \textstyle \sqrt{(\sqrt{3})^2 + (-1)^2} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{4} \\[8pt]
    &= 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.1em} |z_2| &= \sqrt{(-3)^2 + (-3)^2} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{18} \\[6pt]
    &= 3\sqrt{2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the properties of modulus,
    we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.7em} |(pz_1z_2)^x| &= 1 \hspace{3em} [\text{for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$}] \\[12pt]
    |pz_1z_2|^x &= 1 \\[12pt]
    |pz_1z_2| &= 1 \\[12pt]
    |p||z_1||z_2| &= 1 \\[12pt]
    p\hspace{0.05em}(2)(3\sqrt{2}) &= 1  \hspace{3em} [\text{since $p > 0$}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    p = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{12} \hspace{2.6em}
    $$
