# AI076

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $z = 2+{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ and
$w = 1-2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$.

1.  Find $zw$. [2 marks]

2.  Illustrate $z$, $w$ and $zw$ on the same Argand diagram. [3 marks]

3.  Let $\theta$ be the angle between $zw$ and $w$. Find $\theta$,
    giving your answer in radians.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The product of $z$ and $w$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    zw &= (2+{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu})(1-2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}) \\[6pt]
    &= 2-4{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}+{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}+2 \\[6pt]
    &= 4-3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \arg w &= \tan^{-1}((-2)/1) \\[6pt]
    &= -1.107148\dots \hspace{1.3em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \arg zw &= \tan^{-1}((-3)/4) \\[6pt]
    &= -0.643501\dots \hspace{1.8em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8em} \theta &= |\arg w| - |\arg zw| \\[6pt]
    &= (1.107148\dots) - (0.643501\dots) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.464
    \end{aligned}
    $$
