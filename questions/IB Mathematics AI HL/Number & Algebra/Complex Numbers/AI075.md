# AI075

**Maximum mark: 5**

**In this question give all angles in radians.**

Let $z = 1 + 2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ and
$w = 4 + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$.

1.  Find $z+w$. [1 mark]

2.  Find:
    1.  $|z+w|$;

    2.  $\arg(z+w)$. [2 marks]

3.  Find $\theta$, the angle shown on the diagram below. [2 marks]

    ![9f1b9b167af97e4b2c9ee84b7c9014f2cdaf63c7.svg](images/9f1b9b167af97e4b2c9ee84b7c9014f2cdaf63c7.svg)

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    z+w &= (1+2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}) + (4 + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}) \\[6pt]
    &= (1+4) + (2+1){\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\\[6pt]
    &= 5 + 3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  The modulus of $z+w$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        |z+w| &= \sqrt{5^2 + 3^2} \hspace{6.3em} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 5.83
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The argument of $z+w$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \arg(z+w) &= \tan^{-1}(3/5) \hspace{7.25em} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.540
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7.7em} \theta &= \arg z - \arg (z+w) \\[6pt]
    &= \tan^{-1}(2/1) - \tan^{-1}(3/5) \\[6pt]
    &= (1.107148\dots) - (0.540419\dots) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.567
    \end{aligned}
    $$
