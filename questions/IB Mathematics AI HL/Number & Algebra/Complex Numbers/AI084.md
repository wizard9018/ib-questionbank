# AI084

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $z = re^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\frac{\pi}{3}}$ where
$r \in \mathbb{R}^+$.

1.  For $r = \sqrt{2}$,
    1.  express $z^2$ and $z^3$ in the form
        $a+b{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ where
        $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$;

    2.  draw $z^2$ and $z^3$ on the following Argand diagram.
        [4 marks]

        ![7e711daec273ec9c0f630b852aae229a5a09d558.svg](images/7e711daec273ec9c0f630b852aae229a5a09d558.svg)

2.  Given that the integer powers of
    $w = (3-3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu})\hspace{0.05em}z$ lie
    on a unit circle centred at the origin, find the value of $r$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  If we express $z^2$ and $z^3$ in Cartesian form, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{5.5em} z^2 &= 2e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\frac{2\pi}{3}} \\[9pt]
        &= 2\Big[\hspace{-0.1em}\cos\Big(\frac{2\pi}{3}\Big) + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\sin\Big(\frac{2\pi}{3}\Big)\Big] \\[9pt]
        &=-1 + \sqrt{3}{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        and
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3em} z^3 &= 2\sqrt{2}e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\pi} \\[9pt]
        &= 2\sqrt{2}\Big[\hspace{-0.1em}\cos \pi + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\sin \pi \Big] \\[9pt]
        &=-2\sqrt{2} + 0{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  If we draw $z^2$ and $z^3$ on the following Argand diagram, we
        have

        ![ee3e2d0e8670f88c39e4d0221f05849bcdba3ebd.svg](images/ee3e2d0e8670f88c39e4d0221f05849bcdba3ebd.svg)

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |3-3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}| &= \sqrt{3^2 + (-3)^2} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{18} \\[6pt]
    &= 3\sqrt{2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the properties of modulus,
    we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |w^k| &= 1 \hspace{3em} [\text{for all $k \in \mathbb{Z}$}]  \\[13pt]
    |w|^k &= 1 \\[14pt]
    |w| &= 1 \\[14pt]
    \hspace{3.1em} |(3-3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu})z| &= 1 \hspace{3em} [w = (3-3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu})z] \\[14pt]
    |3-3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}||z| &= 1 \\[12pt]
    3\sqrt{2}\hspace{0.05em}r &= 1 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    r = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{6} \hspace{1.4em}
    $$
