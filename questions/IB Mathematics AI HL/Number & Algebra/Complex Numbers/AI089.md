# AI089

**Maximum mark: 6**

The revenues of a four seasons hotel can be modelled by the function

$\mathrm{R}(t) = 58.2\sin\hspace{0.05em}(0.0172t - 1.25) + 204$,

where $t$ is the number of days after midnight on $31$ December.

In a similar way, the operating costs of the hotel can be modelled by
the function

$\mathrm{C}(t) = 31.4\sin\hspace{0.05em}(0.0172t + 1.14) + 85.0$.

Both $\mathrm{R}(t)$ and $\mathrm{C}(t)$ are measured in thousand
dollars.

1.  Show that the profits of the hotel can be modelled by the function
    $\mathrm{P}(t) = 83.9\sin\hspace{0.05em}(0.0172t-1.51) + 119$.
    [3 marks]

2.  According to the model, find:
    1.  the highest profit the hotel will make;

    2.  the **date** on which the highest profit will occur. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(t) &= \mathrm{R}(t) - \mathrm{C}(t) \\[9pt]
    &= (58.2\sin\hspace{0.05em}(0.0172t-1.25) + 204) - (31.4\sin\hspace{0.05em}(0.0172t+1.14) + 85.0) \\[9pt]
    &= \big[58.2\sin\hspace{0.05em}(0.0172t-1.25) - 31.4\sin\hspace{0.05em}(0.0172t+1.14)\big] + (204 - 85.0) \\[9pt]
    &= \mathrm{Im}\big[58.2\mathop{\mathrm{cis}}\hspace{0.05em}(0.0172t-1.25) - 31.4\mathop{\mathrm{cis}}\hspace{0.05em}(0.0172t+1.14)\big] + 119 \\[8pt]
    &= \mathrm{Im}\big[58.2e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(0.0172t-1.25)} - 31.4e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(0.0172t+1.14)}\big] + 119
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we further have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    58.2e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(0.0172t-1.25)} - 31.4e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(0.0172t+1.14)} &= e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}0.0172t}\big[58.2e^{-{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}1.25} - 31.4e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}1.14}\big] \\[8pt]
    [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{3em} &\approx e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}0.0172t}\big[5.24-83.8{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\big] \\[8pt]
    [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{3em} &\approx e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}0.0172t}\big[83.9e^{-{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}1.51}\big] \\[8pt]
    &\approx 83.9e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(0.0172t-1.51)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(t) &\approx \mathrm{Im}\big[83.9e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(0.0172t-1.51)}\big] + 119 \\[8pt]
    &\approx 83.9\sin\hspace{0.05em}(0.0172t-1.51) + 119
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  According to the model, the highest profit is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P_{max}} &\approx 83.9 + 119 \\[8pt]
        &\approx \$203 \text{ thousand}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  If we sketch the graph of $y = \mathrm{P}(t)$ \[by using
        G.D.C.\], we have

        ![543d375a9ce4d0222b972c2179c98d4a685548a5.svg](images/543d375a9ce4d0222b972c2179c98d4a685548a5.svg)

        Hence the highest profit occurs on $\text{day } 179$ or
         $\text{June } 28$
