# AI082

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let
$z_1 = 2\sqrt{3}\mathop{\mathrm{cis}}\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{7\pi}{12}\Big)$,
$z_3 = 2\mathop{\mathrm{cis}}\theta$, and $z_2 = z_1 + z_3$ be
represented by the points

A, B and C on an Argand diagram as shown below.

![f4ed9162ce8f5fbe06e47de0f00519455d24ad0d.svg](images/f4ed9162ce8f5fbe06e47de0f00519455d24ad0d.svg)

The shape OABC is a rectangle.

1.  Show that $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{12}$.
    [1 mark]

2.  Find $\arg\hspace{0.05em}(z_1 - z_2)$. [1 mark]

3.  Express $z_2$ in modulus-argument form. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  From the diagram, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \theta &= \arg z_1 - \text{A\^{O}C} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{7\pi}{12} - \dfrac{\pi}{2} = \hspace{0.525em}\dfrac{\pi}{12} \hspace{1em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  If we illustrate $z_1 - z_2$ on the same Argand diagram, we have

    ![61b40c253f2d12de7c4ab40eafed5e3eb9ac59d0.svg](images/61b40c253f2d12de7c4ab40eafed5e3eb9ac59d0.svg)

    Hence the argument of $z_1 - z_2$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \arg\hspace{0.05em}(z_1 - z_2) &= \text{C\^{O}D} - \arg z_3 \hspace{0.75em} \\[14pt]
    &= \pi - \dfrac{\pi}{12} \\[8pt]
    &= -\dfrac{11\pi}{12}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    ![61b40c253f2d12de7c4ab40eafed5e3eb9ac59d0.svg](images/61b40c253f2d12de7c4ab40eafed5e3eb9ac59d0.svg)

3.  Using the right triangle OBC, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} |z_2|^2 &= |z_1|^2 + |z_3|^2 \\[6pt]
    |z_2|^2 &= (2\sqrt{3})^2 + 2^2 \hspace{0.2em} \\[6pt]
    |z_2|^2 &= 16 \\[7pt]
    |z_2| &= 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the right triangle OBC again, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.4em} \cos(\text{B\^{O}C}) &= \dfrac{|z_3|}{|z_2|} \\[6pt]
    \cos(\text{B\^{O}C}) &= \dfrac{2}{4} \hspace{3em} \left(=\dfrac{1}{2}\right) \\[9pt]
    \text{B\^{O}C} &= \dfrac{\pi}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \arg z_2 &= \arg z_3 + \text{B\^{O}C} \\[12pt]
    &= \dfrac{\pi}{12} + \dfrac{\pi}{3} \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{5\pi}{12}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, if we write $z_2$ in
    modulus-argument form, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} z_2 = 4\mathop{\mathrm{cis}}\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{5\pi}{12}\Big)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
