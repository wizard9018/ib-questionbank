# AI004

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $A = \sqrt{\dfrac{\sin \alpha - \sin \beta}{x^2 + 2y}}$, where
$\alpha = \ang{54}$, $\beta = \ang{18}$, $x = 24$ and $y = 18.25$.

1.  Find the value of $A$. Write down your full calculator display.
    [2 marks]

2.  Give your answer to **part (a)** correct to
    1.  three significant figures;

    2.  three decimal places. [2 marks]

3.  Give the answer to **part (b) (i)** in the form $a\times10^k$, where
    $1 \leq a < 10$, $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting $\alpha = \ang{54}$, $\beta = \ang{18}$, $x = 24$ and
    $y = 18.25$ in the expression for $A$, we get

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     A &= \sqrt{\dfrac{\sin \ang{54} - \sin \ang{18}}{24^2 + 2(18.25)}} \\[6pt]
     &\approx 0.028571
     \end{aligned}
     $$
