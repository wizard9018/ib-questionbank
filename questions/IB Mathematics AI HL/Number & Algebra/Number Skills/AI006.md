# AI006

**Maximum mark: 8**

A cuboid has the following dimensions: $\text{length} = 9.6\hspace{0.25em}$cm, $\text = 7.4\hspace{0.25em}$cm, and $\text = 5.2\hspace{0.25em}$cm, measured correct to the **nearest millimetre**.

1.  Using these measurements, calculate the volume of the cuboid, in cm$^3$. Give your answer to two decimal places. [2 marks]

The lower and upper bounds for the length of the cuboid can be expressed as $9.55 \leq l < 9.65$.

2.  Write similar expressions for
    1. the width;

    2.  the height. [2 marks]

3. Hence, calculate the **minimum** volume of the cuboid. Give your answer to three significant figures. [2 marks]

4.  Write your answer to **part (c)** in the form $a\times10^k$, where $1 \leq a < 10$ and $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the volume of a cuboid formula $V = l \times w \times h$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= 9.6\times7.4\times5.2 \\[6pt]
    &= 369.408 \\[6pt]
    &= 369.41\hspace{0.25em}\mathrm{cm}^3 \text{ (2 d.p.)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.
        $7.35 \leq w < 7.45$
    2.
        $5.15 \leq h < 5.25$

3. Using the lower bound for each dimension, and the volume formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= 9.55\times7.35\times5.15 \\[6pt]
    &= 361.491... \\[6pt]
    &\boxed{= 361\hspace{0.25em}\mathrm{cm}^3 \text{ ($3$ s.f.)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
