# AI015

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows a rectangle with sides of length
$7.6\times10^2$ cm and
$1.5\times10^3$ cm.

![95dfeaca3907795a90048551ee5f6c3d7e41c444.svg](images/95dfeaca3907795a90048551ee5f6c3d7e41c444.svg)

1.  Write down the area of the rectangle in the form $a\times10^k$,
    where\
    $1 \leq a < 10$ and $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. [3 marks]

Natalie estimates the area of the rectangle to be
$1\hspace{0.1em}200\hspace{0.15em}000$ cm$^2$.

2.  Find the percentage error in Natalie's estimate.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The area of the rectangle is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \big(7.6\times10^2\big)\hspace{-0.05em}\times\big(1.5\times10^3\big) \\[8pt]
    &= 1.14\times10^6\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the percentage error formula
    $\epsilon = \bigg|\dfrac{v_\mathrm{A} - v_\mathrm{E}}{v_\mathrm{E}}\bigg|\times 100\hspace{0.05em}\%$
    with $v_\mathrm{E} = 1\hspace{0.1em}140\hspace{0.15em}000$ and
    $v_\mathrm{A} = 1\hspace{0.1em}200\hspace{0.15em}000$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.75em}\epsilon &= \bigg|\dfrac{1\hspace{0.1em}200\hspace{0.15em}000 - 1\hspace{0.1em}140\hspace{0.15em}000}{1\hspace{0.1em}140\hspace{0.15em}000}\bigg|\times 100\hspace{0.05em}\% \\[6pt]
    &\approx 5.26\hspace{0.05em}\%
    \end{aligned}
    $$
