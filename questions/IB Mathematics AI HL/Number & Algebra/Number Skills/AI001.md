# AI001

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $Q = \dfrac{(\sin 2x + b)(2\sin x - 1)}{a^2 - 4\tan x}$, where
$x = \ang{45}$, $a = 18$ and $b = \sqrt{2}$.

1.  Find the **exact** value of $Q$. [2 marks]

2.  Give your answer to **part (a)** correct to
    1.  three decimal places;

    2.  three significant figures. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the percentage error if $Q$ is given to three decimal
    places. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting $x = \ang{45}$, $a = 18$ and $b = \sqrt{2}$ in the
    expression for $Q$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    Q &= \dfrac{(\sin \ang{90} + \sqrt{2})(2\sin \ang{45} - 1)}{18^2 - 4\tan \ang{45}} \\[6pt]
    &= 0.003125
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.
        $0.003$
    2.
        $0.00313$

3.  Using the percentage error formula
    $\epsilon = \bigg|\dfrac{v_\mathrm{A} - v_\mathrm{E}}{v_\mathrm{E}}\bigg|\times 100\hspace{0.05em}\%$
    with $v_\mathrm{E} = 0.003125$ and $v_\mathrm{A} = 0.003$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \epsilon &= \bigg|\dfrac{0.003 - 0.003125}{0.003125}\bigg|\times 100\hspace{0.05em}\% \\[6pt]
    &= 4\hspace{0.05em}\%
    \end{aligned}
    $$
