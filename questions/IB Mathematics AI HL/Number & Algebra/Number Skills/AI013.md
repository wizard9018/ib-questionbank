# AI013

**Maximum mark: 6**

Given that $z = \dfrac{10\sin \alpha}{3x+y}$, where $\alpha = \ang{30}$,
$x = 6$ and $y = 46$.

1.  Find the **exact** value of $z$. [2 marks]

2.  Write your answer to part (a)
    1.  correct to $2$ decimal places;

    2.  correct to $3$ significant figures;
    3.  in the form $a\times10^k$, where $1 \leq a < 10$ and
        $k\in \mathbb{Z}$.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting $\alpha = \ang{30}$, $x = 6$ and $y = 46$ in the
    expression for $z$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    z &=  \dfrac{10\sin \ang{30}}{3(6)+46} \\[6pt]
    &= 0.078125
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.
        $0.08$
    2.
        $0.0781$
    3.
        $7.81\times10^{-2}$
