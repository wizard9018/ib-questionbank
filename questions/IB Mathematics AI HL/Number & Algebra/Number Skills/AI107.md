# AI107

**Maximum mark: 6**

The volume of a hemisphere, $V$, is given by the formula

$$
V = \sqrt{\dfrac{4S^3}{243\pi}}\hspace{0.05em},
$$
where $S$ is the
total surface area.

The total surface area of a given hemisphere is
$529$ cm$^2$.

1.  Calculate the volume of this hemisphere in cm$^3$. Give your answer
    correct to **one decimal place**. [3 marks]

2.  Write down your answer to part (a) correct to **the nearest
    integer**. [1 mark]

3.  Write down your answer to **part (b)** in the form $a\times10^k$,
    where $1 \leq a < 10$ and $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting $S = 529$ in the volume of a hemisphere formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \sqrt{\dfrac{4(529)^3}{243\pi}} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 880.7\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    $881\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}^3$

3.
    $8.81\times10^2\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}^3$
