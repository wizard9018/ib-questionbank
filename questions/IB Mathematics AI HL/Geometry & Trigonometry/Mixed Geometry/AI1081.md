# AI1081

**Maximum mark: 17**

Yoon works in graphical design and she is designing a company logo.

She has made a preliminary sketch and she wants the logo to look like the following

![logo_q](images/logo_q.png)

In this question you will explore the measurements of the logo to help Yoon decide how much paint she will need to colour the logo as shown.

1. To begin with Yoon draws a kite with vertices $ABCD$.

    ![angles_q](images/angles_q.png)

    The sides $AB=AD=23$ mm and $CB=CD$. The angle $\angle{BAD}= 43^{\circ}$ and angle $\angle{BDC}= 31^{\circ}$.

    1. Find the side length $BD$.

    2. Find the angle $\angle{BCD}$.

    3. Hence or otherwise, find the side length $BC$.

    4. Hence or otherwise, find the area of $\triangle BCD$.[6 marks]

2. Next, Yoon is trying to work out how much red paint she needs for the logo.

    She draws on top of the shape she created in part (a) and produces the following

    ![arc_q1](images/arc_q1.png)

    Where $BD$ is an arc from vertex $B$ to $D$ centred at $C$.

    1. Help Yoon by finding the perimeter of the shape $BDC$.

    She then adds the following shape on top of her diagram

    ![arc_q2](images/arc_q2.png)

    Where the new arc $BD$ from vertex $B$ to $D$ is centred at $A$.

    2. Help Yoon by finding the perimeter of the shape $BDA$.

    3. Hence, what is the total length of the red lines and arcs in the logo? [5 marks]
3. Now, Yoon will work out how much blue paint she needs.

    She begins by filling in the segment shown below generated from the sector described in part (b).

    ![seg_q1](images/seg_q1.png)

    1. Find the area of the sector $BCD$.

    2. Hence, find the area of the blue segment. [3 marks]

4. To finish with, Yoon shades the final segment on the logo.

    ![seg_q2](images/seg_q2.png)

    Help Yoon by finding the total area of the blue segments in the completed logo.[3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 1. Use the Cosine rule.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        BD^2&=23^2+23^2-2\times23\times23\times\cos(43)\\[3pt]
        BD &= 16.85905...
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        The length BD is $16.9\,\text{mm}$

    2. $\triangle BCD$ is an isosceles.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \angle BCD &= 180 - 2\times31\\[3pt]
        \angle BCD &= 118^{\circ}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    3. Use the Sine rule.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \frac{BC}{\sin(31)}&=\frac{16.85905...}{\sin(118)}\\[3pt]
        BC &= 9.8341...
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        The length BC is $9.83\, \text{mm}$

    4. Use the area of a triangle formula.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \triangle BCD &= \frac{1}{2}\times 9.8341...^2\times \sin(118)\\[3pt]
        \triangle BCD &=42.695...
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        The area of $BCD$ is $42.7 \, \text{mm}^2$

2.  1. Consider the sector $BDC$

        ![ans_1](images/ans_1.png)

        Use the formula for arc length to find $BD$.
        Hence, the perimeter of $BDC$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        &=BC + CD + BD\\[6pt]
        &= 2\times 9.8341... + 2\pi \times 9.8341...\times \frac{118}{360}\\[6pt]
        &= 39.92169...\\[6pt]
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        The perimeter of $BDC$ is $39.9\, \text{mm}$

    2. Consider the new sector generated by Yoon

        ![ans_2](images/ans_2.png)

        The perimeter of $BDA$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        &=AB + AD + BD\\[6pt]
        &= 2\times 23 + 2\pi \times 23\times \frac{43}{360}\\[6pt]
        &= 63.2613....\\[6pt]
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        The perimeter of $BDA$ is $63.3\, \text{mm}$

    3. Therefore the total length of the red lines and arcs is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        &=63.2613....+39.92169...\\[3pt]
        &=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{103 \, \text{mm}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3. 1. Use the area of a sector formula
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        &=\pi\times\ 9.8341...^2\times \frac{118}{360}\\[6pt]
        &=99.58747...\\[3pt]
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        The area of the sector is $99.6\, \text{mm}^2$

    2. Now subtract the area of the triangle we found in part (a) (iv), from the answer above.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        &=99.58747... -42.695...\\[6pt]
        &=56.8921...
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        The area of the blue segment is $56.9\, \text{mm}^2$

4. First we need to find the area of the sector $ABD$ then subtract from it the area of $\triangle ABD$. Then finally we add this to the previous answer.

    The area of the remaining segment
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        &=\pi\times 23^2\times\frac{43}{360} - \frac{1}{2}\times 23^2\times\sin(43)\\[6pt]
        &=18.116...
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The area of both blue segments
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        &=56.8921...+18.116...\\[6pt]
        &=75.0 \, \text{mm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
