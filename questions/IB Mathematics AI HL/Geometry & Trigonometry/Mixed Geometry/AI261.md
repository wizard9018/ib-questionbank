# AI261

**Maximum mark: 6**

The diagram below shows a triangular park ABC. Alice is in the park with
her dog. She stops at the corner B of the park to make a phone call. The
dog's leash is $12$ metres long.

![a520b8da5eea1b7db73fbb8b01e2237d525e6cde.svg](images/a520b8da5eea1b7db73fbb8b01e2237d525e6cde.svg)

1.  Find the area of the park that the dog cannot reach when attached to
    its leash. [4 marks]

2.  Determine the percentage of total park area that the dog can reach
    while attached to its leash [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The area that the dog cannot reach, $A_{\textrm{shaded}}$, is the
    area of the triangular park, $A_{\triangle \text{ABC}}$, subtract
    the area of the unshaded sector, $A_{\textrm {sec}}$.

    First we find $A_{\triangle \text{ABC}}$. Using the area of a
    triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle \text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2} (\mathrm{BC})(\mathrm{AB})\sin(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C})\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 (12)(20)\sin(\ang{65})\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & \approx  108.8\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {m}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Next, we calculate the area of the unshaded sector. Using the area
    of a sector formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{sec}} &= \dfrac \theta {360} \, \pi r^2\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {65}{360} \, \pi (12^2)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & \approx  81.7\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A _{\textrm{shaded}}    & = A_{\triangle \text{ABC}} - A_{\textrm{sec}} \rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = 108.8 - 81.7\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = 27.1\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The percentage of total park area that the dog can reach is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Percentage}
    & = \dfrac {A_{\text{sec}}} {A_{\triangle \text{ABC}}}\times 100\%\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {81.7} {108.8} \times 100\%\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = 75.1\%\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
