# AI260

**Maximum mark: 6**

The diagram below shows a triangular backyard ABC. A dog is on a leash
attached to a pole at point A. The length of the leash is $10$ metres.

![ed28544ace3ad868a95a2d89c46741931994b663.svg](images/ed28544ace3ad868a95a2d89c46741931994b663.svg)

1.  Find the area of the backyard that the dog cannot reach. [4 marks]

The owner of the dog estimates that the area the dog cannot reach is
$50$ m$^2$.

2.  Calculate the percentage error in the dog owner's estimate.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  First, let's find the total area of the triangle backyard, ABC.
    Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle \text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2} (\mathrm{AC})(\mathrm{AB})\sin(\mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{C})\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 (10)(23) \sin(\ang{50})\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & \approx  88.1\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {m}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Next, let's calculate the area of the sector that the dog can reach
    (the unshaded sector). Using the area of a sector formula, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{unshaded}} &= \dfrac \theta {360} \, \pi r^2 \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {50}{360} \, \pi (10^2)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & \approx  43.6\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, the area of backyard that the dog cannot reach can be found
    by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A _{\textrm{shaded}}    & = A_{\triangle \text{ABC}} - A_{\textrm{unshaded}} \rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = 88.1 - 43.6\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & \approx 44.5\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}^2\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the percentage formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \epsilon &= \left|\dfrac{v_A - v_E} {v_E}\right|\times 100\%\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    & = \left|\dfrac{50 - 44.5} {44.5}\right|\times 100\%\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    & = 12.4\%\rule[-0mm]{0pt}{6mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
