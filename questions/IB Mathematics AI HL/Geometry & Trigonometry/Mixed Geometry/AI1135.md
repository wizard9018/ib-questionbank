# AI1135

**Maximum mark: 17**

The diagram shows a football pitch from above, as Oliver is about to take a free kick. Oliver is at point O, and is aiming to kick the football between the goalposts, indicated by point A and point B. The width of the goal is $7.32$ metres. $\mathrm{OB}=20.62$ metres and $\mathrm{B\hat{A}O}$ is $58.37^{\circ}$.

![ai1135a](images/ai1135a.PNG)

1. Calculate the size of $\mathrm{A\hat{O}B}$. Give your answer to **2 decimal places.** [3 marks]

2. Calculate the area of triangle $\mathrm{AOB}$.[4 marks]

At the moment the free kick is taken, no opposing player is allowed within $10$ metres of the point from which the free kick is taken. The opposition decide to attempt to block Oliver's shot by lining up players between Oliver and the goal. This is shown in the following diagram as the arc CD.

![AI1135b](images/AI1135b.PNG)

3. Calculate the length of arc CD.[2 marks]

Each player in the defending team is approximately $80$ centimetres wide.

4. Determine the minimum number of players that should be positioned on arc CD to fully block Oliver's shot.[2 marks]

5. Calculate the area ABCD, the area that can be defended between Oliver and the goal.[4 marks]

There are 11 players on a football team. Apart from the players lined up on arc CD, the remaining players will defend area ABCD.

6. Determine the amount of area each of these remaining players will be responsible for defending individually.[2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The information provided means the sine rule can be applied. 

    We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{\mathrm{sin}(\mathrm{A\hat{O}B})}{7.32} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{sin}\left(58.37^{\circ}\right)}{20.62} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[10pt]

    \mathrm{sin}(\mathrm{A\hat{O}B}) &= \dfrac{7.32 \times \mathrm{sin}\left(58.37^{\circ}\right)}{20.62} \\[10pt]

    \mathrm{A\hat{O}B} &= \mathrm{sin}^{-1}\left(\dfrac{7.32 \times \mathrm{sin}\left(58.37^{\circ}\right)}{20.62}\right) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[10pt]

    &\hspace{-2.55em}\boxed{\mathrm{A\hat{O}B} = 17.59^{\circ} (2\,\mathrm{d.p.})} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. To apply the area rule for triangles, we require two side lengths and the angle between them. We have $\mathrm{B\hat{A}O}=58.37^{\circ}$ (given) and $\mathrm{A\hat{O}B}=17.59^{\circ}$ (from part (a)). 

    Given that the angle sum of a triangle is $180^{\circ}$, we can work out $\mathrm{A\hat{B}O}$. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{A\hat{B}O} &= 180 - \mathrm{A\hat{O}B} - \mathrm{B\hat{A}O} \\[8pt]

    &= 180 - 17.59 - 58.37 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]

    \mathrm{A\hat{B}O} &= 104.04^{\circ} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore, applying the area rule for triangles, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{A} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(7.32)(20.62)\mathrm{sin}(104.04) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-0.9em}\boxed{A \approx 73.2\hspace{0.1em}\mathrm{m}^2} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. We have $\theta = 17.59^{\circ}$ (from part (a)) and $r=10$. Using the formula for the length of an arc, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    l &= \dfrac{\theta}{360} \times 2 \pi r \\[8pt]

    &= \dfrac{17.59}{360} \times 2 \pi \times 10 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-0.7em}\boxed{l \approx 3.07\hspace{0.1em}\mathrm{m}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

4. Taking the length of arc CD and dividing by the width of each player in metres, $0.8$, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{3.07}{0.8} &= 3.8375 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    $\text{A minimum of 4 players should be positioned on arc CD.}$ 

    $$                    

    \begin{aligned}            

    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}    

    \end{aligned}

    $$

5. The area ABCD is the difference between the area of triangle AOB and the area of sector COD. We have the area of triangle AOB from part (b).Using the formula for the area of a sector, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A_{\mathrm{ABCD}} &= A_{\mathrm{AOB}} - A_{\mathrm{COD}} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]

    &= 73.2 - \dfrac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]

    &= 73.2 - \dfrac{17.59}{360} \times \pi \times 10^2 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-1em}\boxed{A \approx 57.8\hspace{0.1em}\mathrm{m}^2} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{57.8}{7} &\approx 8.257 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
