# AI270

**Maximum mark: 6**

A lawn sprinkler is setup in the corner of a rectangular lawn which is
enclosed by a fence. The sprinkler has a maximum spray distance of
$5$ m. The lawn has side lengths
$4$ m and $10$ m. This
situation is illustrated in the diagram below, where the sprinkler is
positioned in the top right corner.

![be86456e817673fe5e6dbd0536e7ce66decd6af8.svg](images/be86456e817673fe5e6dbd0536e7ce66decd6af8.svg)

1.  Find the angle $\alpha$ in the diagram, leaving your answer in
    degrees. [2 marks]

2.  Find the area of grass that is currently being watered by the
    sprinkler\
    in this position. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's consider the triangular ABC, shown below.

    ![5bba2af41fa3f54627dbccb44dfa6ab56e5c1bc4.svg](images/5bba2af41fa3f54627dbccb44dfa6ab56e5c1bc4.svg)

    From the diagram we see that
    $\alpha =\mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{C}$ (alternate angles).
    Hence:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \sin (\mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{C}) & = \dfrac 4 5\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    \mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{C}    & = \sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac 4 5\right)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    &\hspace{-2.5mm}\alpha \approx \ang{53.1}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The area of the grass able to be watered with the sprinkler in this
    position is the sum of the area of triangle ABC and area of sector
    BAD.

    ![fe3e4f48c878007a892a6e5485bec5e97ab38940.svg](images/fe3e4f48c878007a892a6e5485bec5e97ab38940.svg)

    First, let's find the area of the triangle ABC. Using Pythagoras
    theorem we can find length AC
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{AB}^2     & = \text{AC}^2 + \text{AB}^2\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{1.5mm}\\
    5^2             & = \text{AC}^2 + 4^2\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    \text{AC}^2     & = 5^2 - 4^2\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    \text{AC}       & = \sqrt{5^2 - 4^2}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = 3\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the area of triangle ABC is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\text{ABC}}  & = \dfrac 1 2 (\text{AC})(\text{BC})\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 (3)(4)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = 6\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Next we find the area of sector BAD. Using the area of a sector
    formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac \theta {360} \pi r^2\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    A_{\text{BAD}}  & = \dfrac {53.1} {360} \pi (5^2)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & \approx 11.6\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the total area the sprinkler is currently watering is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\text{total}}        & = A_{\text{ABC}} + A_{\text{BAD}}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = 6 + 11.6\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    &  \approx 17.6\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4.25mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
