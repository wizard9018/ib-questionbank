# AI712

**Maximum mark: 6**

A motion sensing security light is installed in the corner of a front
garden. The sensor can detect any movement up to $7$ meters. The garden
has boundaries of two $12$ m long parallel
walls, and the house facade and a wooden fence which are
$6$ m long. This creates to a rectangular area
as shown in the follow diagram, where the motion light is positioned in
the top right corner.

![47cef28620c353c05f8f45a03a7c19166c9f643b.svg](images/47cef28620c353c05f8f45a03a7c19166c9f643b.svg)

1.  Find the angle $\alpha$ in the diagram, leaving your answer in
    degrees. [2 marks]

2.  Find the area of the front garden that is currently being monitored
    by the motion sensing system. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's consider the triangular ABC, shown below.

    ![ec361deef2f672fdb685da570b10038ec3a7600a.svg](images/ec361deef2f672fdb685da570b10038ec3a7600a.svg)

    From the diagram we see that
    $\alpha =\mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{C}$ (alternate angles).
    Hence:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \sin (\mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{C}) & = \dfrac 6 7\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    \mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{C}    & = \sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac 6 7\right)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    &\hspace{-2.5mm}\alpha \approx \ang{59}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The area of the front garden being monitored is the sum of the area
    of triangle ABC and area of sector BAD.

    ![332b94efbad57ce2c3c58703bcd7a8c08dd84ff0.svg](images/332b94efbad57ce2c3c58703bcd7a8c08dd84ff0.svg)

    First, let's find the area of the triangle ABC. Using Pythagoras
    theorem we can find length AC
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{AB}^2     & = \text{AC}^2 + \text{AB}^2\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{1.5mm}\\
    7^2             & = \text{AC}^2 + 6^2\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    \text{AC}^2     & = 7^2 - 6^2\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    \text{AC}       & = \sqrt{7^2 - 6^2}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & \approx 3.61\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the area of triangle ABC is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\text{ABC}}  & = \dfrac 1 2 (\text{AC})(\text{BC})\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 (3.61)(6)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = 10.83\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Next we find the area of sector BAD. Using the area of a sector
    formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\text{BAD}} &= \dfrac \theta {360} \pi r^2\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {59} {360} \pi (7^2)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & \approx 25.23\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the total area of garden being monitored is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\text{total}}        & = A_{\text{ABC}} + A_{\text{BAD}}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = 10.83 + 25.23\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    &  \approx 36.1\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4.25mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
