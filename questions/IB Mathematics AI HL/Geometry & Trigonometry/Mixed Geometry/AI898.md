# AI898

**Maximum mark: 6**

A plot of farmland is shown by the shaded region in the diagram below.
The plot has a curved border which can be described as an arc, PQ, of a
circle with centre O and radius, $r=500 \text{ m}$, such that PÔQ
$=\ang{80}$. The straight border of the farmland is defined by the chord
PQ.

![fadbc7106cb5ccc27a8dc2731ca1036dc72b3668.svg](images/fadbc7106cb5ccc27a8dc2731ca1036dc72b3668.svg)

The farm owner wishes to construct a fence along the curved border of
the plot.

1.  Find the length of the fence to be built. [2 marks]

2.  Find the area of the farmland, rounding your answer to the nearest
    m$^2$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the formula for the length of an arc, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l&=\dfrac{\theta}{\ang{360}}\times 2\pi r \\[6pt]
    &=\dfrac{\ang{80}}{\ang{360}}\times 2\pi (500) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}\approx698\text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the formulas for areas of a sector and triangle, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A&=\text{(Area of sector)}-\text{(Area of triangle)}\\[6pt]
    &=\left(\dfrac{\theta}{\ang{360}}\times \pi r^2\right)-\left(\dfrac{1}{2} a b \sin C \right) \\[6pt]
    &= \left(\dfrac{\ang{80}}{\ang{360}}\times \pi (500)^2\right)-\left(\dfrac{1}{2} (500) (500) \sin \ang{80}\right)\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}\approx51\,432\text{ m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
