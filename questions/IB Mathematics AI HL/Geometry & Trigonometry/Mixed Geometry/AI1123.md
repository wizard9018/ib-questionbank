# AI1123

**Maximum mark: 7**

A chocolate company is designing new packaging.

The marketing team want to create a cone obtained after removing a sector from a circle of radius $5$ centimetres.

The sector has arc length $\ell$ and the angle $\theta$ is subtended by $\ell$ as shown in the following diagram.

![q1123](images/q1123.png)

Let $h$ be the height and $r$ be the radius of the resulting cone.

The volume of the cone is given by the formula $V = \dfrac{\pi\rule[-1pt]{0pt}{9pt}}{3} (25h-h^3)$

The marketing team want the packaging to be as efficient as possible.

1.  Find the value of $h$ that maximises the volume of the packaging. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $r$ for this maximum.[1 mark]

3. Hence, find
    $\\[6pt]$
    1.  $\ell$

    2.  $\theta$[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. We can sketch the function $V$ on a G.D.C.
and identify the maximum point
    $$
    \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    $$

    ![a1123](images/a1123.png)

    The $x$-coordinate is the value of $h$ that maximises the volume.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        h&=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{2.89 \text{ cm}}\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
2.  Using the Pythagorean theorem, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        5^2 &= r^2 + 2.886...^2\\
        r^2 &=5^2- 2.886...^2\\
        r&= 4.0824...\\[6pt]
        &=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{4.08 \text{ cm}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  1.  The circumference of the circle is equal to $\ell$ added to the circumference of the base of the cone
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
             2\pi\times 5 &=\ell + 2\pi\times 4.0824...\\
        \ell&=2\pi\times 5 - 2\pi\times 4.0824...\tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\
        &=5.7654...\\[6pt]
        &=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{5.77 \text{ cm}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    2.  We can use the formula for arc length to write the following equation
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
          \frac{\theta}{360}\times 2 \pi \times 5&=5.7654...\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Solving this on the G.D.C. gives
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
            \theta &= 66.0672...\\[6pt]
            &=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{66.1^\circ} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
