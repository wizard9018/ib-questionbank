# AI224

**Maximum mark: 14**

A toy robot has four main parts: two cylinders (legs), a hemisphere
(body), and a right cone (head). The robot is represented by the diagram
below.

The legs have heights of $10$ cm and volume
$80$ cm$^3$ each.

The hemisphere has a diameter $30$ cm.

The cone has a radius $5$ cm.

The toy robot's total height is $32$ cm.

![6ecced3d7a45be651e5e47caf90f54af76676dfa.svg](images/6ecced3d7a45be651e5e47caf90f54af76676dfa.svg)

1.  Calculate the distance between the robot's legs. [3 marks]

2.  Calculate the volume of the hemisphere, correct to the nearest
    cm$^3$. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the volume of the cone. [3 marks]

4.  Calculate the total surface area of the toy robot. Give your answer
    to the nearest cm$^2$. [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The distance between legs is $D = 30 - (2\cdot L)$, where $L$ is the
    leg diameter.

    First, let's find $L$. Using the volume formula for a cylinder, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{\textrm{cyl}} &= \pi r^2 h \\[6pt]
    80  & = \pi r^2 (10)\\[3pt]
    r^2 & = \dfrac {80}{10 \pi}\\[3pt]
    r & = \sqrt{2.546}  \\[8pt]
    & \approx 1.596\hspace{0.25em} \mbox{cm}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence
    $L = 2 r = 3.191$ cm.
    Next we find the distance between legs
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{11mm}D &= 30 - (2\cdot L)\\[5pt]
    & = 30 - (2 \cdot 3.191) \\[7pt]
    &\approx 23.6\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the formula of a hemisphere volume
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1mm}V_{\textrm{hsp}} &= \dfrac 1 2 V_{\textrm{sp}}\\[1pt]
    &=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3 \right)\\[0pt]
    &= \dfrac{2}{3} \pi \left(\dfrac{30}{2}\right)^3\\[7pt]
    &\approx 7\hspace{0.25em}069\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The height of the cone can be obtained as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h_{\text{cone}} &= \text{total height $-$ (leg height $+$ radius of a hemisphere)}\\[2pt]
    &= 32 - (10+15) \\[2pt]
    &= 7\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Thus, the volume of
    the cone is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3mm}V_{\textrm{cone}} &= \dfrac 1 3 \pi r^2 h \\[4pt]
    & = \dfrac 1 3 \pi (5^2)(7) \hspace{1.4cm} \\[9pt]
    & \approx 183\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The total surface area ($A_{\textrm{total}}$) consists of the area
    of the legs ($A_{\textrm{legs}}$), the body ($A_{\textrm{body}}$),
    and the head ($A_{\textrm{head}}$).

    The area of the legs consists of area of two cylinders. Using the
    area formula for a cylinder, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{legs}} & = 2 A_{\textrm{cyl}} \\[3pt]
    &= 2 \big(2\pi (1.596)(10)\big)\\[4pt]
    & \approx 201\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {cm}^2.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The
    area of the body consists of the area of the hemisphere and the area
    of the circular base. Using the area formula for a hemisphere
    ($A_{\textrm{hsp}}$) and the area formula for a circle
    ($A_{\textrm{circ}}$), we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{body}}   & =  A_{\textrm{hsp}} + A_{\textrm{circ}}\hspace{10mm} \\[6pt]
    &= 2\pi r^2 + \pi r^2 \\[4pt]
    & = 2 \pi (15)^2 + \pi (15)^2\\[6pt]
    & \approx 1\,413.72 + 706.89\\[6pt]
    & \approx 2\,121\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The
    area of the head consists of the area of the curved surface of the
    cone and the area of the circular base. The area of the curved
    surface of the cone is $A_{\textrm{cone}} =  \pi r L$. We know
    $r = 5$ cm. Let's find the length of the slant $L$. Consider the
    right-angled triangle

    ![cc2fde1bab1076600a8862739285600bd7772a85.svg](images/cc2fde1bab1076600a8862739285600bd7772a85.svg)

    Utilizing Pythagorean theorem, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    L^2 &= r^2+h^2 \\[4pt]
    L^2         & =  5^2 + 7^2\hspace{13mm}\\[6pt]
    L       & \approx 8.60\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{head}}   & = A_{\textrm{cone}} + A_{\textrm{circ}}\\[6pt]
    &= \pi r L + \pi r^2 \\[5pt]
    & = \pi (5)(8.60) + \pi (5^2)\hspace{6mm} \\[5pt]
    & \approx 214\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Finally, we find the total area
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{total}}  & = A_{\textrm{legs}} + A_{\textrm{body}} + A_{\textrm{head}}  \\[7pt]
    & = 201 + 2\,121 + 214\\[7pt]
    & = 2\,536\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
