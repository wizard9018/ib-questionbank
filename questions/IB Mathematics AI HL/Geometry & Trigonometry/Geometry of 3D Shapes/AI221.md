# AI221

**Maximum mark: 6**

An ice cream cup is designed in the form of a right circular cone that
has a volume of $150$ cm$^3$ and a vertical
height of $14$ cm.

![2652874d6fbd39d68962e69b78a1fbd14f1d7eb2.svg](images/2652874d6fbd39d68962e69b78a1fbd14f1d7eb2.svg)

1.  Find the radius, $r$, of the circular base of the cone. [2 marks]

2.  Find the slant height, $l$, of the cone. [2 marks]

3.  Find the curved surface area of the cone. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the volume formula for a cone, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \dfrac 1 3 \pi r^2 h\\[4pt]
    150     \, & = \dfrac 1 3 \pi r^2 (14)\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.45em}r \approx 3.20\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}\qquad\text{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Utilizing Pythagorean theorem, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l^2 &= h^2 + r^2 \\[3pt]
    l^2 & = 14^2 + 3.20^2 \\[2pt]
    l &= \sqrt{14^2 + 3.20^2}\hspace{22mm} \\[5pt]
    &\approx 14.4\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  By the formula of a curved surface area of a cone, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \pi r l \\[3pt]
    A & = \pi (3.20)(14.4)\hspace{22mm}\\[7pt]
    &\approx 145\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
