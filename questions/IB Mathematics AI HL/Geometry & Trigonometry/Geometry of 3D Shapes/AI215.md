# AI215

**Maximum mark: 6**

The surface area of a baseball is made up of two equal leather strips.
The height of the baseball laying on the ground is
$73$ mm. Assuming the surface of the baseball is
a sphere:

1.  Find the area of one leather strip used to make the baseball in
    mm$^2$. Give your answer correct to one decimal place. [4 marks]

2.  Find the circumference of the baseball. Give your answer in mm
    correct to three significant figures. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Notice that the diameter of the ball is equal to its height:
    $73$ mm.

    ![0df97ccd201542f8b7527c1dd73a7a9be5dd771a.svg](images/0df97ccd201542f8b7527c1dd73a7a9be5dd771a.svg)

    Hence, the radius is equal to $(73/2)$ mm.

    Using the area formula for a sphere, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.7mm}A_{\text{sphere}} &= 4\pi r^2 \\[0pt]
    &= 4\pi \left(\dfrac{73}{2}\right)^2 \\[3pt]
    & \approx 16\ 741.5 \hspace{0.25em}\text{mm}^2.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Since the ball is made up of two equal leather strips, the area of
    one of them is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac {A_{\text{sphere}}}{2}    & \approx  \dfrac {16\, 741.5} 2\\[10pt]
    & \approx 8\hspace{0.25em}370.8\hspace{0.25em}\text{mm}^2\hspace{2.3mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the circumference formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1mm}C &= 2\pi r \\[3pt]
    &= 2 \pi  \left(\dfrac {73} 2 \right)\\[6pt]
    &\approx 229\hspace{0.25em}\text{mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
