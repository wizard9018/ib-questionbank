# AI217

**Maximum mark: 6**

The height of a baseball laying on the ground is
$73$ mm.

1.  Assuming that the baseball is a perfect sphere, calculate its volume
    in mm$^3$. Give your answer in the form $a\times 10^k$, where
    $1 \leq a <10$ and $k \in \mathbb Z$, correct to three significant
    figures. [3 marks]

The volume of a volleyball is
$4.64 \times 10^6$ mm$^3$.

2.  Calculate how many times greater the volume of the
    volleyball is when compared to the baseball. Give your answer
    correct to the nearest whole number.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Notice that the diameter of the ball is equal to its height:
    $73$ mm.

    ![0df97ccd201542f8b7527c1dd73a7a9be5dd771a.svg](images/0df97ccd201542f8b7527c1dd73a7a9be5dd771a.svg)

    Hence, the radius is equal to $(73/2)$ mm.

    Using the volume formula for a sphere, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{\text{baseball}} &= \dfrac 4 3 \pi r^3 \\[1pt]
    & = \dfrac 4 3 \pi \left(\dfrac{73}{2}\right)^3 \\[4pt]
    & = 203\hspace{0.25em}689\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{mm}^3 \\[8pt]
    & \approx 204\, 000 \mbox{ mm}^3\\[8pt]
    &= 2.04 \times 10^5 \hspace{0.25em}\mbox{mm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $V_{\text{volleybal}}$ be the volume of the voleyball and $x$
    the ratio we want to find. Thus, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{\text{baseball}} \times x &= V_{\text{volleyball}} \\[5pt]
    x &= \dfrac{V_{\text{volleyball}}}{V_{\text{baseball}}} \\[0pt]
    &= \dfrac{4.64\times 10^6 }{2.04\times 10^5}\hspace{13.8mm}\\[7pt]
    &\approx 23
    \end{aligned}
    $$
