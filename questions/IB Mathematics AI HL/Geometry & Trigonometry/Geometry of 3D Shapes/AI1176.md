# AI1176

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following diagram shows a circle of radius 12 metres, with centre at point $\mathrm{O}$. The angle $\mathrm{A\hat{O}B}$ is $141^{\circ}$.

![AI1176a](images/AI1176a.JPG)

1. Find the area of sector $\mathrm{AOB}$.[2 marks]

2. Hence, find the shaded area.[3 marks]

The shaded area from the above diagram is to be the front of an aircraft hangar of uniform cross-section, as shown below. The length of the hangar is $h$ metres.

![AI1176b](images/AI1176b.JPG)

3. Given that the volume of the hangar is to be at least $1.975 \times 10^4\, \mathrm{m}^3$, find the minimum value of $h$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using the sector area formula, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A &= \dfrac{\theta}{360}\times \pi\,r^2 \\[6pt]

    &= \dfrac{141}{360}\times \pi \times 12^2 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    &= 177.1...\\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-1.1em}\boxed{A = 177\,\mathrm{m}^2\,(3\,\mathrm{s.f.})} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The shaded area is the difference between the area of sector AOB and triangle AOB. 

    Using the formula for the area of a triangle, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A_{\Delta\text{AOB}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\,ab\,\sin C \\[6pt]

    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\times 12 \times 12 \times \sin 141 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    A_{\text{shaded}} &= 177.1... - \dfrac{1}{2}\times 12 \times 12 \times \sin 141 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    &= 131.8...\\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-3.2em}\boxed{A_{\text{shaded}} = 132\,\mathrm{m}^2\,(3\,\mathrm{s.f.})} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. Using the formula for the volume of a prism, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    V &= Ah \\[6pt]

    &= 131.8...\times h

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Our volume must be at least $1.975 \times 10^4\, \mathrm{m}^3$, therefore we form the inequality

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    V &\geq 1.975 \times 10^4 \\[6pt]

    131.8...\times h &\geq 1.975 \times 10^4  \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    h &\geq \dfrac{1.975 \times 10^4}{131.8...} \\[6pt]

    &\geq 149.7...

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    $$\boxed{\text{The minimum value of $h$ is $150$ metres $(3\,\mathrm{s.f.})$.}}$$

    $$                    

    \begin{aligned}            

    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}    

    \end{aligned}

    $$
