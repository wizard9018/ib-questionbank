# AI216

**Maximum mark: 6**

Peter has two water tanks with goldfish inside. The first tank is in the
shape of a cylinder with diameter $40$ cm and
height $45$ cm. The second tank is in the shape
of a cuboid with length $40$ cm, width
$32$ cm, and height
$42$ cm.

![49e9edbe836c7e88ce9407ebfc706e2dc0ca9e31.svg](images/49e9edbe836c7e88ce9407ebfc706e2dc0ca9e31.svg)

1.  Calculate the volume, giving your answer in cm$^3$ correct to three
    significant figures,

    1.  of the first water tank;
    2.  of the second water tank. [4 marks]

Each goldfish requires
$15\hspace{0.25em}000$ cm$^3$ of fresh water for
a comfortable life.

2.  Calculate the number of goldfish Peter can safely put into
    his tanks. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using the volume formula for a cylinder, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        V_{\text{cylinder}} &= \pi r^2 h \\[4pt]
        & = \pi (20^2)(45) \\[5pt]
        &\approx 56\hspace{0.25em}500\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the volume formula for a cuboid, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2mm} V_{\text{cuboid}} & = Lwh\\[4pt]
        &=40 (32) (42)\\[4pt]
        & =  53\, 760\\[5pt]
        &\approx 53\hspace{0.25em}800\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  To find the number of goldfish in the first tank we divide $V_1$ by
    $15\hspace{0.25em}000$ and get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{9mm}\dfrac {V_1} {15\hspace{0.25em}000}     & = \dfrac {56\hspace{0.25em}549} {15\hspace{0.25em}000}\\[6pt]
    & = 3.77\\[7pt]
    & =3\text{ goldfish}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    To find the number of goldfish in the second tank we divide $V_2$ by
    $15\hspace{0.25em}000$ and get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac {V_2} {15\hspace{0.25em}000}     & = \dfrac {53\hspace{0.25em}760} {15\hspace{0.25em}000}\\[6pt]
    & = 3.58\\[7pt]
    & =3\text{ goldfish}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $\text{Hence, the total number of goldfish that Peter can safely  put in the tanks is 6 goldfish.}$
