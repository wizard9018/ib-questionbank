# AI225

**Maximum mark: 18**

A plastic toy is in the shape of a right pyramid. The pyramid has a
square base with the sides $4$ cm long. The
diagram below represents this pyramid, labelled VABCD. V is the vertex
of the pyramid. O is the center of the base ABCD. M is the midpoint of
BC. The angle $\mathrm{V}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C}$ is equal to
$\ang{75}$.

![395b74bb942eb0ccc8e448d9bc624f2780a2b130.svg](images/395b74bb942eb0ccc8e448d9bc624f2780a2b130.svg)

1.  Calculate the length of VM. [3 marks]

2.  Calculate the height of the pyramid, VO. [2 marks]

3.  Find the volume of the pyramid, in cm$^3$. Give your answer correct
    to one decimal place. [2 marks]

4.  Write down your answer to part (c) in the form $a \times 10^k$,
    where $1 \leq a \leq 10$ and $k \in \mathbb Z$. [1 mark]

Another plastic toy is in the shape of a right cone. The height of the
cone is equal to the height of the pyramid, and the diameter of the
cone's base is equal to
$4$ cm.

5.  Determine whether the amount of plastic used to manufacture
    the pyramid is also enough to manufacture this cone. [4 marks]

After heating, the pyramid deforms in such a way that the angle
$\mathrm{V}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C}$ decreases by $20\%$. The side VC
decreases in length during this deformation, however sides VB and BC
stay the same length.

6.  Calculate
    1.  The new distance from the pyramid vertex V to point M.

    2.  The new area of the pyramid's side VBC. [6 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's consider the right-angled triangle BMV.

    ![63adbe95a6d4a1ab0372e07f24ffa1c0d4a5ce15.svg](images/63adbe95a6d4a1ab0372e07f24ffa1c0d4a5ce15.svg)

    Using the definition of the tangent, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tan \theta &= \dfrac {\textrm{opp}} {\textrm{adj}}  \\[2pt]
    \tan (\ang{75}) & = \dfrac {\textrm{VM}} 2 \\[9pt]
    \textrm{VM} & = 2 \tan(\ang{75})\\[13pt]
    &\approx 7.46\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let's consider the right-angled triangle OMV.

    ![2f5acd99283d94d2eca3bfbad63a387b90d6e93a.svg](images/2f5acd99283d94d2eca3bfbad63a387b90d6e93a.svg)

    Utilizing Pythagorean theorem, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm{MV}^2 &= \textrm{VO}^2 + \textrm{MO}^2 \\[5pt]
    \textrm{VO}^2 & =7.46^2 - 2^2 \\[5pt]
    \textrm{VO} & = \sqrt{7.46^2 - 2^2}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.25em}\approx7.19 \hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the volume formula for a pyramid, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3mm}V_p &= \dfrac 1 3 A h \\[5pt]
    & = \dfrac 1 3 (4^2)(7.19) \\[9pt]
    &\approx 38.3\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  $3.83 \times 10^1\hspace{0.25em} \mbox{cm}^3$

5.  To answer this question we need to find the volume of the cone
    ($V_c$). Given cone has $r = 2$ cm and $h = \textrm{VO} = 7.19$ cm,
    hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_c &= \dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h \\[3pt]
    V_c & = \dfrac 1 3 \pi (2^2)(7.19)\\[6pt]
    & \approx 30.1\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

6.  Since the angle $\mathrm{V}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C}$ decreases by
    $20\%$, we get that the deformed angle is
    $\ang{75} - (\ang{75} \cdot 0.2)  = \ang{60}$.
    Consider triangles BVC: original and deformed.

    ![343d78260130cf97f0d5d1caee81266ff421a109.svg](images/343d78260130cf97f0d5d1caee81266ff421a109.svg)

    1.  From the original triangle BVM, using the definition of the
        cosine, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \cos (\mathrm{V}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C} )&= \dfrac {\textrm{adj}} {\textrm{hyp}} \\[2pt]
        \cos (\ang{75}) & = \dfrac 2 {\textrm{VB}}\\[5pt]
        \textrm{VB} & = \dfrac 2 {\cos (\ang{75})}\\[8pt]
        & \approx 7.73\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}.
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Utilizing the cosine formula for the deformed triangle BVM, we
        obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \textrm{VM}^2   & = \textrm{BM}^2 + \textrm{VB}^2 - 2 (\textrm{BM}) (\textrm{VB}) \cdot \cos (\mathrm{V}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{M})\\[6pt]
        \textrm{VM}^2   & = 2^2 + 7.73^2  - 2(2)(7.73) \cdot \cos (\ang{60})\\[8pt]
        &\hspace{-6mm}\textrm{VM} \approx 6.95\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the area formula for a triangle, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        A &= \dfrac{1}{2} (VB)(BC) \sin(\mathrm{V}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C}) \\[4pt]
        & = \dfrac 1 2 (7.73)(4) \cdot \sin(\ang{60})\\[8pt]
        &  \approx 13.4\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2
        \end{aligned}
        $$
