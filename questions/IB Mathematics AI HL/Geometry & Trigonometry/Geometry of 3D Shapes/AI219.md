# AI219

**Maximum mark: 6**

A right cylinder, as shown in the diagram, has a base diameter of
$0.4$ m and a height of
$2.6$ m.

![cc2e4d82f3e639dd0473269e9c9314ac2899d6d3.svg](images/cc2e4d82f3e639dd0473269e9c9314ac2899d6d3.svg)

1.  Write down the radius, **in cm**, of the base of the cylinder.
    [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the area, **in m$^2$**, of the base of the cylinder.
    [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the area, **in m$^2$**, of the curved surface of the
    cylinder. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  First we obtain the value of the radius from the diameter (in cm)
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2r &= d \\[3pt]
    r &= \dfrac{d}{2} \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{40}{2} \\[4pt]
    &= 20\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the area formula for a circle (in $m^2$), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.75mm}A_{\text{base}} &= \pi r^2\\[3pt]
    &= \pi (0.2)^2 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.126 \mbox{ m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the area formula for the curved surface of a cylinder, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.6mm}A_{\text{curved}} &= 2\pi r h \\[3pt]
    & = 2 \pi (0.2) (2.6) \\[5pt]
    &\approx 3.27\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
