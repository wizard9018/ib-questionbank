# AI894

**Maximum mark: 6**

A bakery has designed a box to deliver their speciality birthday cakes.
The box has a rectangular prism base, with a rectangular pyramid top, as
illustrated in the diagram below with dimensions shown.

After the cake is put into the box, the outside of the box is completely
wrapped in colourful gift paper.

![b19713d6a0f67fa2d4ca219ca9ca0c25b6ad8859.svg](images/b19713d6a0f67fa2d4ca219ca9ca0c25b6ad8859.svg)

Point O shown on the diagram is the midpoint of the base of the pyramid.

1.  Find the value of $a$ shown in the diagram; the slant height of one
    of the pyramid faces. [2 marks]

2.  Find the total outer surface area of the box to be wrapped in gift
    paper. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since O is the midpoint of the pyramid base,
    $l=\dfrac{42}{2}=21 \text{ cm}$.

    Using the Pythagorean theorem, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a^2 &= 28^2+l^2\\[6pt]
    a &= \sqrt{28^2 + 21^2}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}=35 \text{ cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $A_1$ and $A_2$ be the outer facing surface areas of the prism
    base and the pyramid top respectively. The total box surface area
    will be $A= A_1+A_2$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_1&=2(42\times 20)+2(15\times 20)+(42\times 15)\\[6pt]
    &= 2\,910 \text{ cm}^2\\[6pt]
    %
    \end{aligned}
    % \begin{aligned}
    A_2&= 2\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\times15\times35\right)+2\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\times42\times\sqrt{28^2+\left(\dfrac{15}{2}\right)^2}\right)\\[6pt]
    &\approx1\,742\text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence,

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= A_1+A_2\\[6pt]
    &=2\,910+1\,742\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}\approx4\,652 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
