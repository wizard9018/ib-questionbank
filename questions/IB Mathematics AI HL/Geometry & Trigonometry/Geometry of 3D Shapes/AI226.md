# AI226

**Maximum mark: 16**

The following diagram shows a toy spinning top made up of a plastic
cylinder and a plastic cone (tip).\
The diameter of the cylinder is $0.6$ cm.\
The height of the cylinder is $2.5$ cm.\
The radius of the base of the cone is $2$ cm.\
The slant height of the cone is $2.8$ cm.

![615d11ffdee83fcdce3fb6976eac6abfc6b2903f.svg](images/615d11ffdee83fcdce3fb6976eac6abfc6b2903f.svg)

1.
    1.  Calculate the vertical height of the cone, in cm. Give your
        answer correct to three significant figures.

    2.  Use your answer from the part (i) to calculate the total volume
        of the spinning top. Give your answer correct to two decimal
        places. [6 marks]

One spinning top was deemed as defected after the manufacturing process
due to insufficient amount of plastic. As a result, the stem was shorter
than usual.

2.  Find the height of this short stem, given that the amount
    of plastic used for this spinning top was
    $8.5$ cm$^3$. [3 marks]

The paint used for colouring the toy has layer thickness of
$0.04$ cm.

3.  Calculate the **total** surface area of a non-defective
    spinning top. [4 marks]

4.  Calculate the volume of paint needed to dye
    $1\hspace{0.15em}000$ non-defective spinning tops. Give your answer
    correct to one decimal place. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using Pythagorean theorem, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        L^2 &= h^2+r^2\\[5pt]
        h^2     & = L^2 - r^2\\[5pt]
        h       & = \sqrt{L^2 - r^2}\\[5pt]
        h       & = \sqrt{(2.8)^2 - 2^2} \\[8pt]
        &\approx 1.96\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The total volume is
        $V = V_{\textrm{cylinder}} + V_{\textrm{cone}}$. Using a volume
        of a cylinder formula, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        V_{\textrm{cylinder}} &= \pi r^2 h\\[6pt]
        V_{\textrm{cylinder}}& = \pi (0.3^2) (2.5)\\[6pt]
        & \approx 0.71\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3.\hspace{14mm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Using a volume of a cone formula, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        V_{\textrm{cone}} &= \dfrac 1 3 \pi r^2 h\\[5pt]
        V_{\textrm{cone}} & = \dfrac 1 3 \pi (2^2) (1.96)\\[8pt]
        & \approx 8.21\,\mbox{cm}^3.\hspace{10mm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{5mm}V &= V_{\textrm{cylinder}} + V_{\textrm{cone}}\\[5pt]
        V   &  = 0.71 + 8.21\\[6pt]
        & \approx 8.92 \hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Let's find the volume of the defected stem. The total volume of the
    spinning top is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= V_{\textrm{cylinder}} + V_{\textrm{cone}}\\[6pt]
    8.5 & = V_{\textrm{cylinder}} + 8.21 \qquad \text{[\,from (a)(ii)\,]}\hspace{-3cm}\\[6pt]
    V_{\textrm{cylinder}} & = 0.29\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the volume formula for a cylinder, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{\textrm{cylinder}} &=  \pi r^2h \hspace{1.6cm} \\[8pt]
    0.29 & = \pi (0.3^2) h\\[4pt]
    h & = \dfrac {0.29} {\pi (0.3^2)}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 1.03\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The total surface area $A$ consists of the cone and cylinder curved
    surface areas, and the area of the cone base (because the circular area on the top of the step is equal to the circular area where the cone base and stem connect).

    Using the formula of the curved surface area of a cone, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{cone}} &= \pi r L \\[4pt]
    A_{\textrm{cone}}   & = \pi (2)(2.8) \\[4pt]
    & \approx 17.5929\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Then we find the area of the cone base using the formula of the area
    of a circle
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2mm}A_{\textrm {cb}} &= \pi r^2\\[4pt]
    A_{\textrm{cb}}  & =  \pi(2^2)\\[5pt]
    & \approx 12.5664\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the curved surface area formula for a cylinder, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{cylinder}} &=
    2 \pi r h \\[4pt]
    A_{\textrm{cylinder}}   & = 2 \pi (0.3) (2.5)\\[4pt]
    & \approx 4.7124\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2.\hspace{33mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Finally, the total area is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= A_{\textrm{cone}}+A_{\textrm {cb}}+A_{\textrm{cylinder}}\\[6pt]
    &  = 17.5929 + 12.5664 + 4.7124\\[6pt]
    & = 34.8717\\[6pt]
    & \approx34.9 \hspace{0.25em} \mbox{cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Here we need to find the answer up to one decimal place, for that we
    use the total area rounded to four decimal places.

    The total volume of paint in terms of the total area of the toy $A$,
    the thickness of the paint layer $Th$ and the number of toys $N$ is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= A \cdot Th \cdot N \\[5pt]
    &  = (34.8717) (0.04) (1\,000)\hspace{7mm}\\[5pt]
    & =1\hspace{0.25em}394.9 \hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
