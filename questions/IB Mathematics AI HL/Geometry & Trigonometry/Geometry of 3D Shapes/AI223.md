# AI223

**Maximum mark: 6**

A container has the shape of a right circular cylinder, as shown in the
left diagram below. The height of the container is
$5$ cm, and the diameter of the cylinder's base
is $8$ cm.

![2e2095fccd4f0f4cf7168e7ea1447df0a0b1ac00.svg](images/2e2095fccd4f0f4cf7168e7ea1447df0a0b1ac00.svg)

1.  Find the volume of the container, $V$. [2 marks]

A cup full of water has the shape of hemisphere, as shown in the right
diagram above. The water from the cup is poured into the container and
fills one third of the container.

2.  Find the radius of the cup, $r$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Notice the radius of the base of the cylinder is half the diameter:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{d}{2} \\[1pt]
    &= \dfrac{8}{2} \\[4pt]
    &= 4\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}\hspace{3.3mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the volume formula for a cylinder, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_c &= \pi r^2 h\\[4pt]
    &= \pi (4^2) (5)\\[5pt]
    &\approx 251\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Since the cup's volume is one third of the volume of the container,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3mm}V_{hs} &= \dfrac 1 3 V_c \\
    &= \dfrac 1 3 (251) \\[4pt]
    & \approx 83.7\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {cm}^3.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The volume of a hemisphere is half the volume of a sphere. Hence we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{hs} &= \dfrac 1 2 V_s \\
    V_{hs} &= \dfrac 1 2 (\dfrac 4 3 \pi r^3)\\[2pt]
    V_{hs} &= \dfrac 2 3 \pi r^3\\[2pt]
    83.7 & = \dfrac 2 3 \pi r^3 \\[4pt]
    40  & = r^3\\[8pt]
    r & = \sqrt[3]{40}\\[10pt]
    &\approx 3.42\,\mbox{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
