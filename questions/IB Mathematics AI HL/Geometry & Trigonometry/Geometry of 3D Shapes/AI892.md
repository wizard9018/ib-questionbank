# AI892

**Maximum mark: 5**

A zipline is installed on a mountain range as a tourist attraction. The
locations of the stops along the zipline can be described by coordinates
(in metres) in reference to the $x$, $y$, and $z$-axes, where the $x$
and $y$ axes are in the horizontal plane and $z$-axis is in the vertical
plane.

Stop G, at ground level, has coordinates $(1000,20,0)$. Stop, T, located
near the top of the mountain, has coordinates $(10,15,550)$.

![f29427798fee5d75d1bbf4b54f5a25fdfa2bc4ea.svg](images/f29427798fee5d75d1bbf4b54f5a25fdfa2bc4ea.svg)

1.  Find the distance between stops G and T, rounding your answer to the
    nearest metre. [2 marks]

A new stop, M, is built exactly half-way between stops G and T.

2.  Find the coordinates of stop M. [2 marks]

3.  Write down the height of stop M, in metres, above the ground.
    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the formula for the distance between two points, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2+(z_2-z_1)^2}\\[6pt]
    &=\sqrt{(10-1000)^2+(15-20)^2+(550-0)^2}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}\approx1\,133 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the midpoint formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{M}&=\left(\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{2},\dfrac{y_1+y_2}{2},\dfrac{z_1+z_2}{2}\right)  \\[6pt]
    &= \left(\dfrac{1000+10}{2},\dfrac{20+15}{2},\dfrac{0+550}{2}\right)\hspace{1.8cm}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}=\left(505,17.5,275\right)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The vertical height of M corresponds to the $z$-coordinate of M.\
    Hence, the height of M is
     $275 \text{ m}$
