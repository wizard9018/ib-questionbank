# AI1034

**Maximum mark: 5**

A traffic cone consists of a frustum sitting on a square base. A solid right cone has a base radius of $25$ cm and a slant height of $65$ cm. A smaller right cone with a base radius of $5$ cm and a slant of $13$ cm is then removed from the top of the larger cone to form the frustum, as shown in the diagram.

![ai1034a](images/ai1034a.PNG)

1. Calculate the height of the cone which has been removed, $h$ in the diagram.

[2 marks]

The frustum has a height of $48\,$cm.

2. Calculate the volume of the frustum.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Consider the following right-angled triangle:

    ![ai1034b](images/ai1034b.PNG)

    Using the Pythagorean theorem, $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, with $a = 5$ cm, $b = h$ and $c = 13$ cm,  we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    5^2 + h^2 &= 13^2 \\[6pt]

    h^2     & = 13^2 - 5^2\\[6pt]

    h         & = \sqrt{169 - 25}\\[6pt]

        & \hspace{-0.8em}\boxed{h=12\,\mbox{cm}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The volume of the frustum is the difference between the volume of a right cone with base radius $r_2=25\,$cm and height $h_2=\sqrt{65^2-25^2}=60\,$cm, and a right cone with base radius $r_1=5\,$cm and height $h_1=12\,$cm. Using the formula for the volume of a cone, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    V &= \dfrac{1}{3}\pi {r_2}^2 h_2 - \dfrac{1}{3}\pi {r_1}^2 h_1 \\[6pt]

    &= \dfrac{1}{3}\pi (25)^2(60) - \dfrac{1}{3}\pi (5)^2(12) \\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-1.2em}\boxed{V \approx 39\,000\,\text{cm}^3}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
