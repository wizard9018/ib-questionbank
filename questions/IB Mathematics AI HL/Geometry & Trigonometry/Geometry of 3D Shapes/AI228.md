# AI228

**Maximum mark: 6**

A birthday party cap is made in the form of a right circular cone that
has volume $950$ cm$^3$ and vertical height
$20$ cm.

![0f59653270a69372f276957ae88ddd1dc0073105.svg](images/0f59653270a69372f276957ae88ddd1dc0073105.svg)

1.  Find the radius, $r$, of the circular base of the cone. [2 marks]

2.  Find the slant height, $l$, of the cone. [2 marks]

3.  Find the curved surface area of the cone. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the volume formula for a cone, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \dfrac 1 3 \pi r^2 h \\[4pt]
    950 & = \dfrac 1 3 \pi r^2 (20)\\[10pt]
    &\hspace{-0.37em}r \approx 6.73\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}\qquad\quad
    \textrm{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Consider the following right-angled triangle:

    ![29e439dc809b634c9b9dde896b07bd2694537101.svg](images/29e439dc809b634c9b9dde896b07bd2694537101.svg)

    Using Pythagorean theorem, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l^2 & = 6.73...^2 + 20^2\\[6pt]
    l & = \sqrt{445.293}\\[8pt]
    &\approx 21.1\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Utilizing the area formula for a cone curved surface, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \pi r L \\[7pt]
    A & = \pi (6.73...) (21.1...) \\[7pt]
    &\approx 446\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
