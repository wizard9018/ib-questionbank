# AI218

**Maximum mark: 6**

A water storage tank has a cylindrical shape. The diameter of the base
of the tank is $0.568$ m. The height of the tank
is $0.855$ m. This is shown in the following
diagram.

![c030bb330e0b8b3949dc9101547fb4534d88d274.svg](images/c030bb330e0b8b3949dc9101547fb4534d88d274.svg)

1.  Write down the radius, in m, of the base of the tank. [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the area of the base of the tank. [2 marks]

George is going to paint the curved surface and the base of the water
storage tank.

3.  Calculate the area to be painted. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  First we obtain the value of the radius from the diameter
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.5mm}2r &= d \\[2pt]
    r &= \dfrac{d}{2} \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.568}{2} \\[4pt]
    &= 0.284\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the area formula for a circle, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1mm}A_{\text{base}} &= \pi r^2 \\[1pt]
    &= \pi (0.284)^2 \\[3pt]
    &\approx 0.253\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the area formula for the curved surface of a cylinder, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10mm}A_{\text{curved}} &= 2\pi r h \\[1pt]
    &= 2 \pi (0.284) (0.855) \\[1pt]
    & \approx 1.526 \hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}^2.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence,
    the total area to be painted is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A _ {\text{base}} + A_{\text{curved}}   & = 0.253 + 1.526 \hspace{9mm}\\[6pt]
    &\approx 1.78 \mbox{ m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
