# AI511

**Maximum mark: 14**

Nikola is testing his new autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) for diving
and surfacing in the Black Sea.

After submersion in the sea, the AUV begins traveling with a speed of
$1.5$ m s$^{-1}$ in the
direction $2\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} - 2\mathbf{k}$, where the unit
vector $\mathbf{i}$ is due east, $\mathbf{j}$ is due north and
$\mathbf{k}$ is perpendicular to the sea surface. The time is measured
in seconds and distances are measured in metres.

1.
    1.  Find the velocity vector of the AUV.

    2.  Write down the diving speed of the AUV. [3 marks]

Nikola's yacht is at O$(0,0,0)$. The AUV passes under the yacht at time
$t = 0$ and its position vector at this moment is $-30\mathbf{k}$.

2.  Find the displacement of the AUV relative to the yacht one
    minute later. [2 marks]

At time $t = 60$, the AUV turns $90$ $\text{\textdegree{}}$ in the
clockwise direction, maintaining the same horizontal speed, and begins
surfacing at
$0.75$ m s$^{-1}$.

3.
    1.  Find the velocity vector of the AUV at the moment it begins
        surfacing.

    2.  Hence, write down the displacement of the AUV relative to the
        yacht at time $t'$ seconds, where $t' = 0$ is the time at which
        the AUV begins surfacing.[5 marks]

4.  Find the time it takes for the AUV to reach the sea surface
    from the moment it begins surfacing. [2 marks]

5.  Find the distance of the AUV from the yacht when it reaches
    the sea surface.[2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  The velocity vector of the AUV is given by
        $\mathbf{v}_\text{d} = k(2\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} - 2\mathbf{k})$, for $k > 0$.

        Using the magnitude of a vector formula, we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        |\mathbf{v}_\text{d}| &= \text{speed} \\[7pt]
        |k(2\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} - 2\mathbf{k})| &= 1.5 \\[6pt]
        k\sqrt{2^2 + 1^2 + (-2)^2} &= 1.5 \\[7pt]
        k &= 0.5 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence the velocity vector is
         $\mathbf{v}_\text{d} = \mathbf{i} + 0.5\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}$
    2.
        $1\text{\hspace{0.25em}m\hspace{0.15em}s}^{-1}$

2.  The displacement of the AUV relative to the yacht at time $t$ sec is
    given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} \mathbf{r}_\text{d} &= (-30\mathbf{k}) + t(\mathbf{i} + 0.5\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k})
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, evaluating $\mathbf{r}_\text{d}$ at $t = 60$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.6em} \mathbf{r}_\text{d} &= (-30\mathbf{k}) + 60(\mathbf{i} + 0.5\mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k}) \\[6pt]
    &= 60\mathbf{i} + 30\mathbf{j} - 90\mathbf{k}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.  Let $\theta$ be the bearing of the AUV before it begins
        surfacing.

        We have

        ![70746a9e825bc7d2033e269f1a164a7017f5e9b7.svg](images/70746a9e825bc7d2033e269f1a164a7017f5e9b7.svg)

        Hence the velocity vector is
         $\mathbf{v}_\text{s} = 0.5\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 0.75\mathbf{k}$
    2.
        $\mathbf{r}_\text{s} = (60\mathbf{i} + 30\mathbf{j} - 90\mathbf{k}) + t'(0.5\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 0.75\mathbf{k})$

4.  The depth at which the AUV travels at time $t'$ seconds is given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    z &= -90 + 0.75t'
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Substituting $z = 0$
    into equation $(1)$ and solving for $t'$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0 &= -90 + 0.75t' \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{10.25em} t' = 120\text{ seconds} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

5.  Evaluating $\mathbf{r}_\text{s}$ at $t' = 120$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.1em} \mathbf{r}_\text{s} &= (60\mathbf{i} + 30\mathbf{j} - 90\mathbf{k}) + 120(0.5\mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 0.75\mathbf{k}) \\[6pt]
    &= 120\mathbf{i} - 90\mathbf{j}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the
    magnitude of a vector formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} |\mathbf{r}_\text{s}| &= \sqrt{120^2 + (-90)^2 + 0^2} \\[6pt]
    &= 150 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
