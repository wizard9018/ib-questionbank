# AI954

**Maximum mark: 18**

Adriano is riding a skateboard in a parking lot. His position vector
from a fixed origin O at time $t$ seconds is modelled by

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} \raisebox{0.06em}{$x$} \\[2pt] \raisebox{0.175em}{$y$} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.15em} = \hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} \raisebox{0em}{$a\ln{(t+b)}\cos t$} \\[2pt] \raisebox{0em}{$a\ln{(t+b)}\sin t$} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)
\end{aligned}
$$

where $a$ and $b$ are non-zero constants to be determined. All distances
are in metres.

1.  Find the velocity vector at time $t$. [3 marks]

2.  Given that $a > 0$, show that the magnitude of the velocity vector
    at time $t$\
    is given by $a\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{(t+b)^2}+(\ln{(t+b)})^2}$.

    [4 marks]

At time $t = 0$, the velocity vector is
 $\bigg(\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{matrix} 2 \\[2pt] 2.773 \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)$ .

3.  Find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$. [3 marks]

4.  Find the magnitude of the velocity vector when $t = 3$.
    [2 marks]

At point P, Adriano is riding parallel to the $y$-axis for the first
time.

5.  Find \|OP\|. [6 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we differentiate $x$ with respect to $t$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x' &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\big[a\ln{(t+b)}\cos t\big] \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{a}{t+b}\cos t - a\ln{(t+b)}\sin t \hspace{2em}[\text{by product rule}] \\[8pt]
    &= a\bigg[\dfrac{\cos t}{t+b} - \ln{(t+b)}\sin t\bigg] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    If we differentiate $y$ with respect to $t$, we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y' &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\big[a\ln{(t+b)}\sin t\big] \\
    &= \dfrac{a}{t+b}\sin t + a\ln{(t+b)}\cos t \hspace{2em}[\text{by product rule}] \\
    &= a\bigg[\dfrac{\sin t}{t+b} + \ln{(t+b)}\cos t\bigg]\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{-8.4mm}\left(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {x'} \\ {y'} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\right)\hspace{-0.15em} = \hspace{-0.1em}\Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {a\bigg[\dfrac{\cos t}{t+b} - \ln{(t+b)}\sin t\bigg]} \\[8pt] {a\bigg[\dfrac{\sin t}{t+b} + \ln{(t+b)}\cos t\bigg]} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg) \hspace{3.1em} \tag*{$(1)$}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the magnitude of a vector formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\bigg|\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {x'} \\[2pt] {y'} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg| = \sqrt{[\hspace{0.05em}x']^2 + [\hspace{0.05em}y']^2} \\[8pt]
    %
    &= \sqrt{a^2\bigg[\dfrac{\cos t}{t+b} - \ln{(t+b)}\sin t\bigg]^2 + a^2\bigg[\dfrac{\sin t}{t+b} + \ln{(t+b)}\cos t\bigg]^2} \hspace{3em} {[\text{by $(1)$}]} \\[8pt]
    %
    %&= |r|\sqrt{\bigg[\dfrac{\cos ^2 t}{(t+k)^2} - \cancel{\dfrac{2\ln{(t+k)}\cos t \sin t}{t+k}} + (\ln{(t+k)})^2\sin^2 t\bigg] + \bigg[\dfrac{\sin ^2 t}{(t+k)^2} + \cancel{\dfrac{2\ln{(t+k)}\sin t \cos t}{t+k}} + (\ln{(t+k)})^2\cos^2 t\bigg]} \\[12pt]
    %
    &= |a|\sqrt{\bigg[\dfrac{\cos ^2 t}{(t+b)^2} + (\ln{(t+b)})^2\sin^2 t\bigg] + \bigg[\dfrac{\sin ^2 t}{(t+b)^2} + (\ln{(t+b)})^2\cos^2 t\bigg]} \\[8pt]
    %
    &= a\sqrt{\dfrac{\cos^2 t + \sin^2 t}{(t+b)^2} + (\ln{(t+b)})^2(\cos^2 t + \sin^2 t)}  \hspace{3em} {[\text{since $a > 0$}]}\\[8pt]
    %
    &\bigg|\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {x'} \\[2pt] {y'} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg| = a\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{(t+b)^2}+(\ln{(t+b)})^2} \hspace{3em} {[\text{by Pythagorean identity}]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Substituting $t = 0$ into velocity vector $(1)$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {x'} \\[8pt] {y'} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg|_{t=0} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {a\bigg[\dfrac{1}{b} - 0\bigg]} \\[8pt] {a[0 \hspace{0.025em}+\hspace{0.025em}\ln{b}]} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg) \\[10pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {\dfrac{a}{b}} \\[6pt] {a\hspace{0.2em}\ln{b}} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \bigg(\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{matrix} 2 \\[2pt] 2.773 \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.15em} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {\dfrac{a}{b}} \\[6pt] {a\hspace{0.2em}\ln{b}} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg) \hspace{0.25em} \\[10pt]
    \Rightarrow \hspace{1em} a = 4\hspace{0.525em}&\text{ and }\hspace{0.525em}b = 2 \hspace{3em} \text{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using part (b), we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \bigg|\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {x'} \\[2pt] {y'} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg| &= 4\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{(t+2)^2}+(\ln{(t+2)})^2} \hspace{2.0em} \tag*{$(2)$}\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, if we evaluate expression $(2)$ for $t = 3$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \bigg|\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {x'} \\[2pt] {y'} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg| &= 4\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{(3+2)^2}+(\ln{(3+2)})^2} \hspace{1.3em} \\[10pt]
    %
    &\hspace{-3em}\bigg|\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {x'} \\[2pt] {y'} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg| \approx 6.49 \text{ m\hspace{0.05em}s}^{-1}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Solving the equation $x' = 0$ for the smallest $t > 0$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    4\bigg[\dfrac{\mathrm{cos}\, t}{t+2} - \mathrm{ln}{(t+2)}\mathrm{sin}\, t\bigg] &= 0 \\[10pt]
    t &\approx 0.4330 \text{ seconds} \hspace{3em}\text{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Evaluating $x$ for $t = 0.4330$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x &= 4\ln{(0.4330+2)}\cos(0.4330) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 3.228 \text{ m} \hspace{3em} \text{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Evaluating $y$ for $t = 0.4330$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 4\ln{(0.4330+2)}\sin(0.4330) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 1.492 \text{ m} \hspace{3em} \text{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the magnitude of a vector formula, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |\text{OP}| &= \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \\[10pt]
    &= \sqrt{(3.228)^2 + (1.492)^2} \\[10pt]
    &\hspace{-2em}|\text{OP}| \approx 3.56 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
