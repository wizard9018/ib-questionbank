# AI503

**Maximum mark: 15**

The displacement, from a fixed origin at sea level, of a passenger
riding on a gondola ski lift is modelled by the vector equation

$$
\mathbf{r} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 244 \\ 12 \\ 1650 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}t\hspace{-0.1em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2.4 \\ 5.2 \\ 1.8 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}, \hspace{1em}
$$

where $t$ is the travel time, in seconds, starting at the base station
and ending at the top station. Distances are
measured in metres.

Bill boards the ski lift from the base station and travels to the top
station.

1.  Write down Bill's :
    1.  initial position;

    2.  velocity vector. [2 marks]

2.  Find the speed, in metres per second, of the gondola ski lift.
    [2 marks]

It takes $k$ minutes for Bill to reach the top station, which has a
vertical height of $2352$
metres.

3.  Find the value of $k$. [2 marks]

4.  Find:
    1.  Bill's terminal position;

    2.  the length of Bill's ride. [4 marks]

5.  Find Bill's position when he is closest to a skier resting
    on a hill at the point P$(610,962,2020)$.[5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $\text{A}(244, 12, 1650)$

    2.
        $\mathbf{v} = \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2.4 \\ 5.2 \\ 1.8 \end{pmatrix*}$

2.  Using the magnitude of a vector formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.3em} \text{speed} &= |\mathbf{v}| \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{(2.4)^2 + (5.2)^2 + (1.8)^2} \\[6pt]
    &= 6.00 \text{ m\hspace{0.15em}s$^{-1}$}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The height of a passenger above sea level at time $t$ seconds,
    $t \geq 0$, is given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    z &= 1650 + 1.8t \hspace{1.9em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting $z = 2352$ into equation $(1)$ and solving for $t$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.9em} 2352 &= 1650 + 1.8t \\[6pt]
    t &= 390 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Converting seconds to minutes, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.3em} 390 \text{ seconds} &= 6.5 \text{ minutes} \hspace{3em} [= 390/60]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we find
     $k = 6.5$

4.
    1.  Evaluating $\mathbf{r}$ at $t = 390$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathbf{r} &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 244 \\ 12 \\ 1650 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}390\hspace{-0.1em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2.4 \\ 5.2 \\ 1.8 \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
        &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1180 \\ 2040 \\ 2352 \end{pmatrix*} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence Bill's terminal position is at
         $\text{B}(1180,2040,2352)$

    2.  Using the distance between two points formula, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{AB} &= \sqrt{(1180 - 244)^2 + (2040 - 12)^2 + (2352-1650)^2} \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 2340 \text{ m}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

5.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.1em} \mathbf{r} - \vec{\text{OP}} &= \hspace{-0.15em}\left[\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 244 \\ 12 \\ 1650 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}t\hspace{-0.1em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2.4 \\ 5.2 \\ 1.8 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}\right]\hspace{-0.15em} - \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 610 \\ 962 \\ 2020 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2.4t - 366 \\ 5.2t-950 \\ 1.8t-370 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Bill is closest to the resting skier when
    $\mathbf{v} \perp (\mathbf{r} - \vec{\text{OP}}) = 0$.

    Hence, solving the equation
    $\mathbf{v} \cdot (\mathbf{r} - \vec{\text{OP}}) = 0$ for $t$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (2.4)(2.4t - 366)+(5.2)(5.2t-950)+(1.8)(1.8t-370) &= 0 \\[6pt]
    [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{3em}  t &\approx 180
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Evaluating $\mathbf{r}$ at $t = 180$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{r} &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 244 \\ 12 \\ 1650 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}180\hspace{-0.1em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2.4 \\ 5.2 \\ 1.8 \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 676 \\ 948 \\ 1974 \end{pmatrix*} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence Bill is closest to the resting skier at
     $\text{Q}(676,948,1974)$
