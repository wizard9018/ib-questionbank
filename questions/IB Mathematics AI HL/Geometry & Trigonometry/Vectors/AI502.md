# AI502

**Maximum mark: 15**

WaveRunner X leaves from A$(-265,141)$ and travels with velocity vector
$10\mathbf{i} - 4\mathbf{j}$. At the same time, WaveRunner Y leaves from
B$(-180,-103)$ and travels at
$\text{\(19.5\)\hspace{0.25em}m\hspace{0.15em}s\(^{-1}\)}$ in the
direction $5\mathbf{i} + 12\mathbf{j}$. Times and distances are measured
in seconds and metres respectively.

1.  Determine if the two WaveRunners are travelling perpendicular to
    each other.[2 marks]

2.  Find, in terms of $\mathbf{i}$ and $\mathbf{j}$,
    the :

    1.  velocity vector of WaveRunner Y.
    2.  position vector of WaveRunner Y after $t$ seconds, $t \geq 0$.
        [4 marks]

3.  Find the point at which the paths of the WaveRunners intersect.
    [4 marks]

4.  Calculate the distance between WaveRunners X and Y when
    WaveRunner X passes through the intersection
    point. [2 marks]

5.  Find the time, in seconds, when WaveRunner X is closest to
    WaveRunner Y's starting point. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{v}_{\text{X}} &= 10\mathbf{i}-4\mathbf{j} \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
    \mathbf{d}_{\text{Y}} &= 5\mathbf{i}+12\mathbf{j}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the scalar product formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.7em} \mathbf{v}_{\text{X}} \cdot \mathbf{d}_{\text{X}} &= (10\mathbf{i}-4\mathbf{j})\cdot(5\mathbf{i}+12\mathbf{j}) \\[6pt]
    &= (10)(5) + (-4)(12) \\[6pt]
    &= 2 \hspace{2.2em}  [\neq 0]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the two
    WaveRunners are not travelling perpendicular to each other.

2.
    1.  The velocity vector of WaveRunner Y is given by
        $\mathbf{v}_{\text{Y}}\hspace{-0.05em} = k(5\mathbf{i}\hspace{0.125em}+\hspace{0.025em}12\mathbf{j})$, $\hspace{0.1em}k\hspace{-0.05em}>\hspace{-0.05em}0$.
        Using the magnitude of a vector formula, we have

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        |\mathbf{v}_{\text{Y}}| &= \text{speed} \\[6pt]
        \hspace{0.3em} |k(5\mathbf{i} + 12\mathbf{j})| &= 19.5 \\[6pt]
        k\textstyle \sqrt{5^2 + 12^2} &= 19.5 \\[6pt]
        k &= 1.5 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence the velocity vector is
         $\mathbf{v}_{\text{Y}} = 7.5\mathbf{i} + 18\mathbf{j}$
    2.  The position vector of WaveRunner Y after $t$ seconds,
        $t \geq 0$, is given by
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathbf{r}_\text{Y} &= \vec{\text{OB}} + t\mathbf{v}_{\text{Y}} \\[6pt]
        &= (-180\mathbf{i} - 103\mathbf{j}) + t(7.5\mathbf{i} + 18\mathbf{j}) \\[6pt]
        &= (-180 + 7.5t)\mathbf{i} + (-103 + 18t)\mathbf{j} \hspace{2em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  The position vector of WaveRunner X after $s$ seconds, $s \geq 0$,
    is given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{r}_\text{X} &= \vec{\text{OA}} + s\mathbf{v}_{\text{X}} \\[6pt]
    &= (-265\mathbf{i} + 141\mathbf{j}) + s(10\mathbf{i} - 4\mathbf{j}) \\[6pt]
    &= (-265 + 10s)\mathbf{i} + (141 - 4s)\mathbf{j}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the vector equation
    $\mathbf{r}_\text{X} = \mathbf{r}_\text{Y}$, we deduce
    $$
    \begin{array}{rcccccl}
    \hspace{9.5em} -265 &+& 10s &=& -180 &+& 7.5t \\[6pt]
    141 &-& 4s &=& -103 &+& 18t \\[9pt]
    &&&\text{OR}&&& \\[8pt]
    -10s &+& 7.5t &=& -85 && \hspace{12.5em}(1) \\[6pt]
    4s &+& 18t &=& 244 && \hspace{12.5em}(2)
    \end{array}
    $$
    Solving the system of linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for
    $s$ and $t$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8em} s = 16 \hspace{0.525em}\&\hspace{0.525em} t = 10 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, evaluating $\mathbf{r}_\text{X}$ at $s = 16$, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.4em} \mathbf{r}_\text{X} &= (-265 + 10(16))\mathbf{i} + (141 - 4(16))\mathbf{j} \\[6pt]
    &= -105\mathbf{i} + 77\mathbf{j}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore the paths
    of WaveRunners X and Y intersect at
     $\text{P}(-105,77)$

4.  Evaluating $\mathbf{r}_\text{Y}$ at $t = 16$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.2em} \mathbf{r}_\text{Y} &= (-180 + 7.5(16))\mathbf{i} + (-103 + 18(16))\mathbf{j} \\[6pt]
    &= -60\mathbf{i} + 185\mathbf{j}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence WaveRunner Y
    is at Q$(-60,185)$ when WaveRunner X is at P.

    Therefore, using the distance between two points formula, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} \text{PQ} &= \sqrt{(-60-(-105))^2 + (185-77)^2} \\[8pt]
    &= 117 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{r}_\text{X} - \vec{\text{OB}} &= ((-265 + 10s)\mathbf{i} + (141 - 4s)\mathbf{j}) - (-180\mathbf{i} - 103\mathbf{j}) \\[6pt]
    &= (-85 + 10s)\mathbf{i} + (244 - 4s)\mathbf{j}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    WaveRunner X is closest to B when
    $\mathbf{v}_\text{X} \perp (\mathbf{r}_\text{X} - \vec{\text{OB}})$.

    Hence, solving the equation
    $\mathbf{v}_\text{X} \cdot (\mathbf{r}_\text{X} - \vec{\text{OB}}) = 0$
    for $s$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (10\mathbf{i}-4\mathbf{j})\cdot((-85 + 10s)\mathbf{i} + (244 - 4s)\mathbf{j}) &= 0 \hspace{4.6em} \\[6pt]
    (10)(-85 + 10s) + (-4)(244 - 4s) &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{6.5em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{3em} s \approx 15.7 \text{ seconds}
    $$
