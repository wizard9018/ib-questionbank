# AI510

**Maximum mark: 6**

A land surveyor is measuring the area of a triangular plot of land on a
smooth hill. She uses a theodolite to determine the coordinates of the
corners of the land and finds them to be $(-8,20,10)$, $(16,8,7)$ and
$(-20,-8,3)$, where the coordinates are in metres from a fixed origin
where she stands.

Using vectors, determine the area of the land.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

If we draw the land, we have

![7d4761e51a411b45b483dbcaa5a6565761d9d764.svg](images/7d4761e51a411b45b483dbcaa5a6565761d9d764.svg)

Using the position vectors of A and B, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2.4em} \vec{\text{AB}} &= \vec{\text{OB}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[6pt]
&= (16\mathbf{i} + 8\mathbf{j} + 7\mathbf{k}) - (-8\mathbf{i} + 20\mathbf{j} + 10\mathbf{k}) \\[6pt]
&= 24\mathbf{i} - 12\mathbf{j} - 3\mathbf{k}
\end{aligned}
$$
Similarly,
using the position vectors of A and C, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{3.25em} \vec{\text{AC}} &= \vec{\text{OC}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[6pt]
&= (-20\mathbf{i} - 8\mathbf{j} + 3\mathbf{k}) - (-8\mathbf{i} + 20\mathbf{j} + 10\mathbf{k}) \\[6pt]
&= -12\mathbf{i} - 28\mathbf{j} - 7\mathbf{k}
\end{aligned}
$$
Using the
vector product formula, we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{7.9em} \vec{\text{AB}} \times \vec{\text{AC}} &= \left|\begin{matrix*}[c] \hspace{0.25em}\textbf{i} & \textbf{j} & \hspace{0.25em}\textbf{k} \\ \phantom{-}24 & \hspace{-0.25em}-12 & \hspace{-0.25em}-3 \\ -12 & \hspace{-0.25em}-28 & \hspace{-0.25em}-7 \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.25em}\right| \\[6pt]
&= [84-84]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{i} - [-168-36]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{j} + [-672-144]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{k}  \\[10pt]
&= 204\textbf{j} - 816\textbf{k} \hspace{2.8em}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, using the area of a triangle formula, we find

 $$
 \begin{aligned}
 \hspace{1.2em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big|\vec{\text{AB}} \times \vec{\text{AC}}\big| \\[6pt]
 &= \dfrac{1}{2}|204\textbf{j} - 816\textbf{k}| \\[6pt]
 &= \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{0^2 + 204^2 + (-816)^2} \\[9pt]
 &\approx 421 \text{ m}^2
 \end{aligned}
 $$
