# AI499

**Maximum mark: 14**

The US Defense Force is testing a new drone. The drone is controlled by
a remote ground control system. The drone's
position is given by the coordinates $(x, y, z)$, where $x$ and $y$ are
the drone's displacement east and north of an airbase,
and $z$ is the height of the drone above the
ground. All displacements are given in kilometres.

The drone's velocity is given by the vector
$\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -80 \\ -240 \\ -15 \end{pmatrix*}$
km h$^{-1}$.

At 15 : 00 the remote
ground control system detects the drone at a position
$32$ km east and
$96$ km north of the airbase, and at a height of
$8$ km. Let $t$ be the
length of time, in hours, from
15 : 00.

1.  Write down a vector equation for the drone's displacement,
    $\mathbf{r}$, in terms of $t$.[2 marks]

2.  Given that the drone continues to fly at the same velocity,
    1.  verify that it will pass directly over the airbase;

    2.  find the time at which this happens;
    3.  calculate its height at this point. [4 marks]

The drone continues to fly at the same velocity and descends to a height
of $5$ km.

3.  Find the time at which this happens. [2 marks]

4.  Calculate the direct distance of the drone from the airbase
    at this point. [3 marks]

After descending to a height of $5$ km, the
drone continues to fly on the same bearing but
adjusts the angle of descent so that it will land at the point
Q$(0,0,0)$.

The drone's velocity, after the adjustment of the angle of descent, is
given by

the vector $\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -80 \\ -240 \\ q \end{pmatrix*}$
km h$^{-1}$.

5.  Find the value of $q$. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\mathbf{r} = \hspace{-0.2em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 32 \\ 96 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em} + \hspace{0.1em} t\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -80 \\ -240 \\ -15 \end{pmatrix*}$

2.
    1.  Using the equation from part (a), we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        x &= 32 - 80t  \hspace{2.5em} \\[6pt]
        y &= 96 - 240t  \\[6pt]
        z &= 8 - 15t
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Substituting $x = 0$ into
        equation $(1)$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{5.95em} 0 &= 32 - 80t \\[6pt]
        t &= 0.4 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Evaluating $(2)$ for $t = 0.4$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        y &= 96 - 240(0.4) \hspace{0.25em}\\[6pt]
        &= 0
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence $x = y = 0$ when $t = 0.4$.
        Therefore the drone will pass directly over the airbase.

    2.  Converting hours to minutes, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        t &= 0.4 \text{ hours} \\[6pt]
        &= 0.4(60) \\[6pt]
        &= 24 \text{ minutes} \hspace{1.1em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the
        drone will pass directly over the airbase at
         $\text{15\hspace{0.025em}:\hspace{0.025em}24}$
    3.  Evaluating equation $(3)$ for $t = 0.4$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        z &= 8 - 15(0.4) \hspace{1.2em} \\[6pt]
        &= 2 \text{ km}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  Substituting $z = 5$ into equation $(3)$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8.2em} 5 &= 8 - 15t \\[6pt]
    t &= 0.2 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Converting hours to minutes, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.2em}  t &= 0.2 \text{ hours} \\[6pt]
    &= 0.2(60) \\[6pt]
    &= 12 \text{ minutes}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the drone is
    $5$ km above the ground at
     $\text{15\hspace{0.025em}:\hspace{-0.025em}12}$

4.  Evaluating $\mathbf{r}$ for $t = 0.2$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.1em} \mathbf{r} &= \hspace{-0.2em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 32 \\ 96 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em} + \hspace{0.1em} 0.2\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -80 \\ -240 \\ -15 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.2em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 16 \\ 48 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the magnitude of a vector formula, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.8em} |\mathbf{r}| &= \sqrt{16^2 + 48^2 + 5^2} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 50.8 \text{ km}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  If we sketch the test flight from
    15 : 00 until the
    drone lands at Q, we have

    ![155a611aa306c37ea911aba59fc5477a4dff2a65.svg](images/155a611aa306c37ea911aba59fc5477a4dff2a65.svg)

    Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.9em} q &= -\dfrac{\text}{\text{time to land}} \\[6pt]
    &= -\dfrac{5}{0.2} \\[10pt]
    &= -25
    \end{aligned}
    $$
