# AI766

**Maximum mark: 14**

A submarine is moving in a straight line such that $x=5+12\hspace{0.1em}t$ and $y=-10-18\hspace{0.1em}t$, where $t$ is the travel time, in minutes, after 3:40 pm. $x$ represents displacement due east, and  $y$ represents displacement due north, relative to an origin O. Distances are measured in metres.

1. Find the coordinates of the submarine's position at 4:25 pm. [2 marks]

2. 1. Write down the velocity vector of the submarine.

    2. Hence find the speed of the submarine. [3 marks]

An iceberg is located at P$(1500,-1400)$.

3. Find the distance from the submarine to the iceberg at 4:55 pm. [3 marks]

4. Find the time, to the nearest minute, when the submarine is closest to the iceberg. [3 marks]

5. If the submarine changed course at the time found in part (d), to go directly underneath the iceberg, determine the time at which it would be directly underneath. [3 marks]

2. 1. We could write a vector equation for the path of the submarine. This would represent the displacement of the submarine from the origin O at any time $t$. We have

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        r &= \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 5 + 12t \\ -10-18t  \end{pmatrix*} \\[8pt]
        &= \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 5 \\ -10  \end{pmatrix*} + t\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 12 \\ -18  \end{pmatrix*}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        The velocity vector of the submarine is $\mathbf{v} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 12 \\ -18  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}$
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2. Using the magnitude of a vector formula, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{speed} &= |\mathbf{v}| \\[6pt]
        &= \textstyle \sqrt{12^2 + (-18)^2} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
        &= 21.63... \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0em}\boxed{= 21.6 \text{ m\hspace{0.15em}{\text{min}}}^{-1}\,\text{(3 s.f.)}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}\hspace{4.1em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3. Evaluating $x = 5+12\hspace{0.1em}t$ for $t = 75$, we obtain
