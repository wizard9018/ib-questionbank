# AI761

**Maximum mark: 6**

An amusement park is recording a promotional video. For this, they will release a drone from the highest point in the park.  The drone moves horizontally in a straight line for the first $12.5$ seconds, then descends vertically.

The velocity vector of the drone, $v$ m s$^{-1}$, at time $t$ seconds, is given by
$$
\begin{aligned}
    v(t) = \begin{pmatrix}
-0.08\hspace{0.1em}t^2+t \\
0 \\
\end{pmatrix} \text{ for } 0 \leq t \leq 12.5
\end{aligned}
$$
And
$$
\begin{aligned}
    v(t) = \begin{pmatrix}
0 \\
e^{25-2t} - \frac{12.5}{t} \\
\end{pmatrix} \text{ for } t > 12.5
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Calculate the distance travelled by the drone in the first $12.5$ seconds. [2 marks]

The velocity of the drone when it reaches the ground is exactly $-0.25$ m s$^{-1}$.

2.  Given that the drone is airborne for more than 20 seconds, determine the height from which the drone was released. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We only need to consider the horizontal component of the drone's velocity vector. Using the formula for distance travelled from time $t_1$ to time $t_2$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= \int_{t_1}^{t_2} |v(t)|\, \textrm dt \\[12pt]
    & =  \int_0^{12.5} |-0.08\hspace{0.1em}t^2+t|\, \textrm dt \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[12pt]
    &= 26.04...\quad \quad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}]\\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-0.7em}\boxed{d =  26.0 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m (3 s.f.)}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We can use the same formula we used in part (a) with the vertical component of the velocity vector for $t>12.5$. $t_1$ will be $12.5$, because this is the time that the drone starts descending. However, we need a value for $t_2$, the time at which the drone reaches the ground.

    Our first step therefore is to determine the time at which the drone reaches the ground. Solving the equation $v(t) = -0.25$ for $t$ (and noting that $t$ must be greater than $20$), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    e^{25-2t} - \frac{12.5}{t} & =  -0.25 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
    t &= 50 \hspace{0.25em}\text{s} \quad \quad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We can now find the total distance travelled by the drone vertically between $t_1=12.5$ and $t_2=50$ seconds.

    We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= \int_{t_1}^{t_2} |v(t)|\, \textrm dt \\[8pt]
    & =  \int_{12.5}^{50} |e^{25-2t} - \frac{12.5}{t}|\, \textrm dt \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
    &= 16.82...\quad \quad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The drone was released from a height of $$\boxed{16.8\,\text{m (3 s.f.)}}$$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
