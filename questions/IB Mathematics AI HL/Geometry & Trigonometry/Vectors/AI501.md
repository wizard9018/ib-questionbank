# AI501

**Maximum mark: 13**

Grace and Henry are playing on a youth soccer field. Grace kicks a ball
from A$(4,3)$ with velocity vector $-5\mathbf{i} + 10\mathbf{j}$. At the
same time, Henry kicks a ball from B$(24,13)$ at
$15$ m s$^{-1}$ in the
direction $-4\mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j}$. The times and distances are
measured in seconds and metres respectively.

1.  Find, in terms of $\mathbf{i}$ and $\mathbf{j}$,
    the :

    1.  velocity vector of Henry's ball;
    2.  position vector of Henry's ball after $t$ seconds, $t \geq 0$.
        [4 marks]

2.  Find the distance between the two balls after $2$ seconds.
    [4 marks]

3.
    1.  Calculate the minimum distance between the balls.

    2.  Determine the position of Grace's ball when this occurs.
        [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  The velocity vector of Henry's ball is given by
        $\mathbf{v}_{\text{H}} = k(-4\mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j})$,
        $k > 0$.

        We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        |\mathbf{v}_{\text{H}}| &= \text{speed} \\[6pt]
        |k(-4\mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j})| &= 15 \\[6pt]
        \hspace{6.5em} k\textstyle \sqrt{(-4)^2 + 3^2} &= 15 \\[6pt]
        k &= 3 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence the velocity vector is
         $\mathbf{v}_{\text{H}} = -12\mathbf{i} + 9\mathbf{j}$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathbf{r}_\text{H} &= \vec{\text{OB}} + t\mathbf{v}_{\text{H}} \\[6pt]
        &= (24\mathbf{i} + 13\mathbf{j}) + t(-12\mathbf{i} + 9\mathbf{j}) \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
        &= (24 - 12t)\mathbf{i} + (13 + 9t)\mathbf{j}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{r}_\text{G} &= \vec{\text{OA}} + t\mathbf{v}_{\text{G}} \\[6pt]
    &= (4\mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j}) + t(-5\mathbf{i} + 10\mathbf{j}) \hspace{0.8em} \\[6pt]
    &= (4 - 5t)\mathbf{i} + (3 + 10t)\mathbf{j}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Evaluating $\mathbf{r}_\text{H}$ at $t = 2$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} \mathbf{r}_\text{H} &= (24 - 12(2))\mathbf{i} + (13 + 9(2))\mathbf{j} \\[6pt]
    &= 31\mathbf{j}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Evaluating $\mathbf{r}_\text{G}$ at
    $t = 2$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{r}_\text{G} &= (4 - 5(2))\mathbf{i} + (3 + 10(2))\mathbf{j} \hspace{0.1em} \\[6pt]
    &= -6\mathbf{i} + 23\mathbf{j}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the
    magnitude of a vector formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |\mathbf{r}_\text{H} - \mathbf{r}_\text{G}| &= |31\mathbf{j} - (-6\mathbf{i} + 23\mathbf{j})| \\[8pt]
    &= |6\mathbf{i} + 8\mathbf{j}| \\[6pt]
    &= \textstyle \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} \\[8pt]
    &= 10 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathbf{r}_\text{H} - \mathbf{r}_\text{G} &= [(24 - 12t)\mathbf{i} + (13 + 9t)\mathbf{j}] - [(4 - 5t)\mathbf{i} + (3 + 10t)\mathbf{j}] \hspace{1em} \\[8pt]
        &= [(24 - 12t) - (4 - 5t)]\mathbf{i} + [(13 + 9t) - (3 + 10t)]\mathbf{j} \\[8pt]
        &= (20 - 7t)\mathbf{i} + (10 - t)\mathbf{j} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    Hence the distance b$/$n the balls after $t$ seconds, $t \geq 0$, is
    given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} d &= |\mathbf{r}_\text{H} - \mathbf{r}_\text{G}| \\[6pt]
    &= \textstyle \sqrt{(20 - 7t)^2 + (10 - t)^2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    If we
    draw the graph of $d$ versus $t$ for $0 \leq t \leq 6$
    \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\], we have

    ![4ac87e7655deb49a55c90f9e54e964c1c3714450.svg](images/4ac87e7655deb49a55c90f9e54e964c1c3714450.svg)

    Hence the minimum distance b$/$n the balls is
     $d_{\text{min}} \approx 7.07\text{ m}$
    2.  Evaluating $\mathbf{r}_\text{G}$ at $t = 3$, we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{r}_\text{G} &= (4 - 5(3))\mathbf{i} + (3 + 10(3))\mathbf{j} \hspace{0.1em} \\[6pt]
    &= -11\mathbf{i} + 33\mathbf{j}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence Grace's ball
    is at
     $(-11,33)$
    when the distance b$/$n the balls\
    is at a minimum.
