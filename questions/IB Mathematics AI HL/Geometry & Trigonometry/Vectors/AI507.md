# AI507

**Maximum mark: 14**

Aircraft X is flying south-east on a bearing of
$135$ $\text{\textdegree{}}$ at a speed of
$920$ km h$^{-1}$ and is
ascending at a rate of
$1.2$ km h$^{-1}$. At
16 : 00 the aircraft is
directly above a tracking station at an $\text{altitude}$ of
$8.8$ km.

1.  Find an expression for the displacement of Aircraft X from the
    tracking station at time $t$ hours after
    16 : 00. [4 marks]

Aircraft Y is flying on a bearing of $120$ $\text{\textdegree{}}$ at a
speed of
$840$ km h$^{-1}$ and is
descending at a rate of
$2.4$ km h$^{-1}$. At
16 : 45 the aircraft is
directly above a tracking station at an altitude of
$12.4$ km.

2.  Find an expression for the displacement of Aircraft Y from
    the tracking station at time $t$ hours after
    16 : 00. [4 marks]

At time $k$ hours after
16 : 00 the two
aircrafts reach the same cruising altitude.

3.  Find the value of $k$. [2 marks]

At $t = k$, Aircraft X is at point A and Aircraft Y is at point B.

4.  Find the coordinates of :
    1.  A;

    2.  B. [2 marks]

5.  Hence, find the distance between the two aircrafts when
    they reach the same cruising altitude. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The initial tracking position vector is
    $\textbf{p}_{\text{X}}=\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ 0 \\ 8.8 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}$.

    Given the aircraft is flying south-east at
    $920$ km h$^{-1}$, we
    deduce

    ![85614f48c6dc16aa88722594e2515b4650480189.svg](images/85614f48c6dc16aa88722594e2515b4650480189.svg)

    Hence the velocity vector is
    $\mathbf{v}_{\text{X}} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 460\sqrt{2} \\ \hspace{-0.25em}-460\sqrt{2} \\ 1.2\phantom{\hspace{1.0em}} \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$.
    Therefore we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{r}_{\text{X}} &= \mathbf{p}_{\text{X}} + t\mathbf{v}_{\text{X}} \\[9pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ 0 \\ 8.8 \end{pmatrix*} + \hspace{0.1em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 460\sqrt{2} \\ \hspace{-0.25em}-460\sqrt{2} \\ 1.2\phantom{\hspace{1.0em}} \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{2.4em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The initial tracking position vector is
    $\textbf{p}_{\text{Y}}=\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ 0 \\ 12.4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}$.

    Given the aircraft is flying on the bearing of
    $120$ $\text{\textdegree{}}$ at
    $840$ km h$^{-1}$, we
    deduce

    ![9f5f7e86c2815c49e3eecfe35e42be0f3a2914d5.svg](images/9f5f7e86c2815c49e3eecfe35e42be0f3a2914d5.svg)

    Hence the velocity vector is
    $\mathbf{v}_{\text{Y}} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 420\sqrt{3} \\ -420\phantom{\hspace{0.5em}} \\ -2.4\phantom{\hspace{0.6em}} \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$.
    Therefore we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} \mathbf{r}_{\text{Y}} &= \mathbf{p}_{\text{Y}} + t\mathbf{v}_{\text{Y}} \\[9pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ 0 \\ 12.4 \end{pmatrix*} + \hspace{0.1em}(t-0.75)\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 420\sqrt{3} \\ -420\phantom{\hspace{0.5em}} \\ -2.4\phantom{\hspace{0.6em}} \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} 8.8 + k(1.2) &= 12.4 + (k-0.75)(-2.4) \\[6pt]
    8.8 + 1.2k &= 14.2 - 2.4k \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{8.35em} k = 1.5 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

4.
    1.  Evaluating $\mathbf{r}_{\text{X}}$ at $t = 1.5$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathbf{r}_{\text{X}} &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ 0 \\ 8.8 \end{pmatrix*} + \hspace{0.1em}1.5\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 460\sqrt{2} \\ \hspace{-0.25em}-460\sqrt{2} \\ 1.2\phantom{\hspace{1.0em}} \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{3.8em} \\[6pt]
        &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 690\sqrt{2} \\ \hspace{-0.25em}-690\sqrt{2} \\ 10.6\phantom{\hspace{0.75em}} \end{pmatrix*}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence the coordinates of A are
         $(690\sqrt{2}, -690\sqrt{2}, 10.6)$
    2.  Evaluating $\mathbf{r}_{\text{Y}}$ at $t = 1.5$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathbf{r}_{\text{Y}} &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ 0 \\ 12.4 \end{pmatrix*} + \hspace{0.1em}(1.5-0.75)\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 420\sqrt{3} \\ -420\phantom{\hspace{0.5em}} \\ -2.4\phantom{\hspace{0.6em}} \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
        &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 315\sqrt{3} \\ -315\phantom{\hspace{0.5em}} \\ 10.6\phantom{\hspace{0.5em}} \end{pmatrix*}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence the coordinates of B are
         $(315\sqrt{3}, -315, 10.6)$

5.  Using the distance between two points formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.75em} \text{AB} &= \sqrt{(690\sqrt{2}-315\sqrt{3})^2 + (-690\sqrt{2}-(-315))^2 + (10.6-10.6)^2} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 789 \text{ km}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
