# AI504

**Maximum mark: 7**

The displacement of a remote-controlled toy yacht is modelled by the
vector equation

$$
\mathbf{r} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -5 \\ 12 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{0.1em}t\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1.5 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em},
$$

where $t$ is the sail time, in seconds, $t \geq 0$, and distances are
measured in metres.

Ali is standing on a boat dock at O$(0,0)$ and controls the yacht.

1.  Write down the position of the yacht at :
    1.  $t = 0$ ;

    2.  $t = 3$. [2 marks]

2.  Determine the speed the yacht is travelling. [1 mark]

3.  Determine :
    1.  the distance between Ali and the yacht at $t = 0$;

    2.  an expression for the distance between Ali and the yacht for
        $t \geq 0$. [2 marks]

4.  Hence, or otherwise, determine :
    1.  the minimum distance of the yacht from Ali;

    2.  the time when this occurs. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $\text{A}(-5,12)$

    2.
        $\text{B}(-0.5,6)$

2.  The velocity vector of the yacht is
    $\mathbf{v} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1.5 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$.

    Hence, using the magnitude of a vector formula, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{speed} &= |\mathbf{v}| \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{(1.5)^2 + (-2)^2} \hspace{3em} \\[7pt]
    &= 2.5 \text{\text{ m\hspace{0.15em}s$^{-1}$}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.  At $t = 0$, we have
        $\mathbf{r} = \vec{\text{OA}} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -5 \\ 12 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$.

        Using the magnitude of a vector formula, we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        d &= |\vec{\text{OA}}| \\[6pt]
        &= \sqrt{(-5)^2 + 12^2} \hspace{4.4em} \\[8pt]
        &= 13 \text{ m}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Similarly, using the magnitude of a vector formula, we deduce
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.3em} d &= |\mathbf{r}| \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.272em}= \sqrt{(-5+1.5t)^2 + (12-2t)^2}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
