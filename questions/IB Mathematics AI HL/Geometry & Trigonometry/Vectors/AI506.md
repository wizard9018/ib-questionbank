# AI506

**Maximum mark: 6**

A golf buggy drives in a straight path on a wide fairway. The buggy's
path is given by the parametric equations $x = 2t + 11$ and
$y = 3t - 5$, where $t$ is the drive time in seconds ($t \geq 0$) and
distances are measured in meters.

1.
    1.  Write down the initial position of the buggy.

    2.  Find the position of the buggy after $30$ seconds. [3 marks]

2.
    1.  Write down the velocity vector of the buggy.

    2.  Find the speed of the buggy. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $\text{A}(11,-5)$

    2.  Evaluating $x = 2t+11$ for $t = 30$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        x &= 2(30)+11 \\[6pt]
        &= 71
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Evaluating $y = 3t-5$ for $t = 30$, we
        obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        x &= 3(30)-5 \\[6pt]
        &= 85
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the position of the buggy after $30$
        seconds is
         $\text{B}(71,85)$

2.
    1.
        $\mathbf{v} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 3  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}$
    2.  Using the magnitude of a vector formula, we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{speed} &= |\mathbf{v}| \\[6pt]
        &= \textstyle \sqrt{2^2 + 3^2} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 3.61 \text{ m\hspace{0.15em}s}^{-1} \hspace{1.4em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
