# AI508

**Maximum mark: 5**

A military helicopter flies from an airbase at A to a battlefield at B
and then to a hospital at H. The routes taken by
the helicopter are given by the vectors

$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec{\text{AB}} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] \hspace{-0.1em}-140 \\ 70\phantom{0} \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{0.5em}\text{and}\hspace{0.85em} \vec{\text{BH}} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 165 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-20  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Distances are measured in kilometres.

1.  Determine the vector $\vec{\text{AH}}$. [2 marks]

After evacuating wounded soldiers from the battlefield to the front-line
hospital, the helicopter returns back to the
airbase.

2.  Write down the vector that describes this return flight.
    [1 mark]

3.  Find the direct distance from the hospital to the airbase.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{AH}} &= \vec{\text{AB}} + \vec{\text{BH}} \\[8pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] \hspace{-0.1em}-140 \\ 70\phantom{0} \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 165 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-20  \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{2.5em} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 25 \\ 50 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    $\vec{\text{HA}} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-25 \\ \hspace{-0.1em} -50  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}$

3.  Using the magnitude of a vector formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |\vec{\text{HA}}| &= \sqrt{(-25)^2 + (-50)^2} \hspace{2.7em} \\[8pt]
    &\approx 55.9 \text{ km}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
