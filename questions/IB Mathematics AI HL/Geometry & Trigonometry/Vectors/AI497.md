# AI497

**Maximum mark: 6**

A particle P moves with velocity
$\mathbf{v} = \hspace{-0.2em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ -5 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*}$
in a magnetic field,
$\mathbf{B} = \hspace{-0.2em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] a \\ 0 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}$ , $a \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  Given that $\mathbf{v}$ is perpendicular to $\mathbf{B}$, find the
    value of $a$. [2 marks]

The force, $\mathbf{F}$, produced by P moving in the magnetic field is
given by the vector equation
$\mathbf{F} = b\hspace{0.025em}\mathbf{v}\times\mathbf{B}$,
$b \in \mathbb{R}^+$.

2.  Given that $|\mathbf{F}| = 18$, find the value of $b$.
    [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the scalar product formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{B} &= 0 \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\mathbf{v} \perp \mathbf{B}$}] \hspace{1.6em} \\[6pt]
    \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ -5 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*}\cdot\begin{pmatrix*}[c] a \\ 0 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*} &= 0 \\[6pt]
    (2)(a) + (-5)(0) + (4)(2) &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{11.45em} a = -4 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

2.  Using the vector product formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.15em} \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B} &= (2\textbf{i} - 5\textbf{j} + 4\textbf{k})\times(-4\textbf{i} + 2\textbf{k}) \\[8pt]
    &= \left|\begin{matrix*}[r] \textbf{i} & \textbf{j} & \hspace{0.8em}\textbf{k} \\ 2 & -5 & 4 \\ -4 & 0 & 2 \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.25em}\right| \\[8pt]
    &= [-10-0]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{i} - [4-(-16)]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{j} + [0-20]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{k}  \\[9pt]
    &= -10\textbf{i} - 20\textbf{j} - 20\textbf{k} \\[9pt]
    &= -10(\textbf{i} + 2\textbf{j} + 2\textbf{k}) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the vector equation
    $\mathbf{F} = b\hspace{0.025em}\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}$, we
    find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.5em} |\mathbf{F}| &= |b\hspace{0.025em}\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}| \\[9pt]
    18 &= |\hspace{-0.15em}-\hspace{-0.25em}10b(\textbf{i} + 2\textbf{j} + 2\textbf{k})| \\[9pt]
    18 &= |\hspace{-0.15em}-\hspace{-0.25em}10||b||\textbf{i} + 2\textbf{j} + 2\textbf{k}| \\[7pt]
    18 &= 10b\sqrt{1^2 + 2^2 + 2^2} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $b > 0$}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{1.8em} b = 0.6 \hspace{3.5em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
