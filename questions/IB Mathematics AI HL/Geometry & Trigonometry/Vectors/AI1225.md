# AI1225

**Maximum mark: 7**

Wayne is throwing a boomerang into the air from a position on a flat field. If thrown correctly, the boomerang will return directly to the point from which he throws it.

The displacement of the boomerang relative to Wayne's position on the ground, at time $t$ seconds, is given by the vector

$$

\begin{aligned}

    \bm{s} &= \begin{pmatrix} 8\,\sqrt{2 \cos \left(\frac{t}{4}\right)\,\sin \left(\frac{t}{2}\right)} \\[10pt] 4\,\sqrt{2 \cos \left(\frac{t}{4}\right)\,\sin \left(\frac{t}{2}\right)\,\sin \left(\frac{t}{4}\right)} \\[10pt] 11.8 - \frac{10(t-\pi)^2}{\pi ^2} \end{pmatrix} \hspace{1em}\text{for}\hspace{1em} 0 \leq t \leq 7

\end{aligned}

$$

Where the $x$ component is distance east, the $y$ component is distance north and the $z$ component is vertical distance above the ground, all measured in metres.

1. Determine the height from which Wayne throws the boomerang. [2 marks]

2. Calculate the maximum distance of the boomerang from Wayne's position on the ground at any time. [3 marks]

If thrown correctly, the boomerang will return to Wayne for the first time after $k$ seconds.

3. Determine the value of $k$. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Wayne will throw the boomerang at time $t=0$, hence we must substitute the value $t=0$ into the displacement vector. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \bm{s} &= \begin{pmatrix} 8\,\sqrt{2 \cos \left(\frac{0}{4}\right)\,\sin \left(\frac{0}{2}\right)} \\[6pt] 4\,\sqrt{2 \cos \left(\frac{0}{4}\right)\,\sin \left(\frac{0}{2}\right)\,\sin \left(\frac{0}{4}\right)} \\[6pt] 11.8 - \frac{10(0-\pi)^2}{\pi ^2} \end{pmatrix} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[30pt]

    &= \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\[6pt] 0 \\[6pt] 1.8 \end{pmatrix} 

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    The $z$ component of the displacement vector describes the height of the boomerang at time $t$ seconds.

    Wayne throws the boomerang from a height of $1.8\,\text{m}$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The distance of the boomerang from Wayne at any time $t$ is given by the magnitude of the displacement vector. Using the formula for the magnitude of a vector, We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    |\bm{s}| &= \sqrt{s_1^2 + s_2^2 + s_3^2} \\[8pt]

    &= \sqrt{\scriptstyle{\left(8\,\sqrt{2 \cos \left(\tfrac{t}{4}\right)\,\sin \left(\tfrac{t}{2}\right)}\right)^2 + \left(4\,\sqrt{2 \cos \left(\tfrac{t}{4}\right)\,\sin \left(\tfrac{t}{2}\right)\,\sin \left(\tfrac{t}{4}\right)}\right)^2 + \left(11.8 - \tfrac{10(t-\pi)^2}{\pi ^2}\right)^2}} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} 

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. From part (a), our initial displacement vector is 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \bm{s} &= \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\[6pt] 0 \\[6pt] 1.8 \end{pmatrix} 

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    This means that the initial distance of the boomerang from Wayne's position on the ground is $1.8\,\text{m}$. This is the height from which Wayne throws the boomerang.

    When the boomerang returns to Wayne, the distance will again be $1.8\,\text{m}$. However, it is important that $x$ component and $y$ component are both $0$, and the $z$ component is $1.8$. Using our distance graph from part (b), we can add the function $|\bm{s}|=1.8$ and find the first point of intersection such that $t>0$. 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    ![ai1225b](images/ai1225b.png)

    The boomerang is $1.8\,\text{m}$ from Wayne after $6.283...$ seconds, as it was initially.

    Substituting $t=6.283...$ into our displacement vector, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \bm{s} &= \begin{pmatrix} 8\,\sqrt{2 \cos \left(\frac{6.283...}{4}\right)\,\sin \left(\frac{6.283...}{2}\right)} \\[10pt] 4\,\sqrt{2 \cos \left(\frac{6.283...}{4}\right)\,\sin \left(\frac{6.283...}{2}\right)\,\sin \left(\frac{6.283...}{4}\right)} \\[10pt] 11.8 - \frac{10(6.283...-\pi)^2}{\pi ^2} \end{pmatrix} \\[45pt]

    &= \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ 1.8\end{pmatrix} 

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    This verifies that after $6.283...$ seconds, the boomerang has returned to the exact point it started from.

    $\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{k=6.28\,\text{(3 s.f.)}}$ 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
