# AI940

**Maximum mark: 6**

The Great Pyramid of Giza can be modelled by a right-pyramid with a
square base. A scale model of the Great Pyramid of Giza is shown below.

![4abc6a4f018c2d92a630babb30f95c228896a1b6.svg](images/4abc6a4f018c2d92a630babb30f95c228896a1b6.svg)

The vertices A, B and C have coordinates A$(1,5.5,0)$, B$(6.5,0,7)$ and
C$(12,5.5,0)$ relative to a fixed
origin O near the pyramid. All distances are measured in
centimetres.

1.
    1.  Find $\vec{\text{AB}}$.

    2.  Find $\vec{\text{AC}}$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $\vec{\text{AB}} \times \vec{\text{AC}}$. [2 marks]

3.  Hence, or otherwise, find the surface area of the scale model, **not
    including** the base.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using the position vectors of A and B, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \vec{\text{AB}} &= \vec{\text{OB}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[12pt]
        &= (6.5\mathbf{i} + 7\mathbf{k}) - (\mathbf{i} + 5.5\mathbf{j}) \hspace{0.1em} \\[12pt]
        &\hspace{-1.45em}\vec{\text{AB}} = 5.5\mathbf{i} - 5.5\mathbf{j} + 7\mathbf{k}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the position vectors of A and C, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \vec{\text{AC}} &= \vec{\text{OC}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[9pt]
        &= (12\mathbf{i} + 5.5\mathbf{j}) - (\mathbf{i} + 5.5\mathbf{j}) \hspace{0.4em} \\[9pt]
        &\hspace{-1.45em}\vec{\text{AC}} = 11\mathbf{i}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Using the vector product formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{AB}} \times \vec{\text{AC}} &= \left|\begin{matrix*}[c] \textbf{i} & \hspace{0.6em}\textbf{j} & \hspace{0.75em}\textbf{k} \\ 5.5 & \hspace{-0.25em}-5.5 & \hspace{0.75em}7 \\ 11 & \hspace{0.6em}0 & \hspace{0.75em}0 \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.25em}\right| \\[9pt]
    &= [0-0]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{i} - [0-77]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{j} + [0-(-60.5)]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{k} \\[9pt]
    &\hspace{-4.1em}\vec{\text{AB}} \times \vec{\text{AC}} = 77\textbf{j} + 60.5\textbf{k}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S.A. &= 4A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} \\[9pt]
    &= 4\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}\left\lvert\vec{\text{AB}} \times \vec{\text{AC}}\right\rvert\bigg] \\[9pt]
    &= 4\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}\big|77\textbf{j} \hspace{0.05em}+\hspace{0.05em} 60.5\textbf{k}\big|\bigg] \\[9pt]
    &= 2\sqrt{0^2 + 77^2 + (60.5)^2} \hspace{1.0em} \\[9pt]
    &\hspace{-1.9em}S.A. \approx 196 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
