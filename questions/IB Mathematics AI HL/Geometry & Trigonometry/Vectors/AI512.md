# AI512

**Maximum mark: 6**

A camel walks in a straight line on flat grassland. The camel's path is
given by the parametric equations $x = 3t - 2$ and

$y = 1+4t$, where $t$ is the walk time, in hours, $t \geq 0$. Distances are measured in kilometres.

1.
    1.  Write down the initial position of the camel.

    2.  Find the position of the camel after $2$ hours. [3 marks]

2.
    1.  Write down the velocity vector of the camel.

    2.  Find the speed of the camel. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $\text{A}(-2,1)$

    2.  Evaluating $x = 3t-2$ for $t = 2$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        x &= 3(2)-2 \\[6pt]
        &= 4
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Evaluating $y = 1+4t$ for $t = 2$, we obtain

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        x &= 1+4(2) \\[6pt]
        &= 9
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the position of the camel after $2$
        hours is
         $\text{B}(4,9)$

2.
    1.
        $\mathbf{v} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 4  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}$
    2.  Using the magnitude of a vector formula, we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{speed} &= |\mathbf{v}| \\[6pt]
        &= \textstyle \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} \\[6pt]
        &= 5 \text{ km\hspace{0.15em}h}^{-1} \hspace{1.4em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
