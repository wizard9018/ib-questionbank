# AI513

**Maximum mark: 15**

A sailboat is traveling in a straight line given by the parametric
equations $x = 6+4.5t$ and $y = 9t-11$,
where $t$ is the travel time, in hours, after
16:30.
The positive $x$-axis is due east and the positive $y$-axis is due
north. Distances are measured in
kilometres.

1.  Write down the position of the sailboat at
    16:30.
    [1 mark]

2.
    1.  Write down the velocity vector of the sailboat.

    2.  Find the speed of the sailboat. [3 marks]

A lighthouse is located at P$(22,0)$.

3.  Find the distance of the sailboat from the lighthouse at
    19:30.
    [3 marks]

4.  Find the time, to the nearest minute, when the sailboat
    is:

    1.  closest to the lighthouse;
    2.  directly to the north of the lighthouse. [8 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\text{A}(6,-11)$

2.
    1.
        $\mathbf{v} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4.5 \\ 9  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}$
    2.  Using the magnitude of a vector formula, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{speed} &= |\mathbf{v}| \\[6pt]
        &= \textstyle \sqrt{4.5^2 + 9^2} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 10.1 \text{ km\hspace{0.15em}h}^{-1} \hspace{4.1em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  Evaluating $x = 6+4.5t$ for $t = 3$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x &= 6+4.5(3) \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
    &= 19.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Evaluating $y = 9t-11$ for $t = 3$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 9(3)-11 \hspace{1.3em} \\[6pt]
    &= 16
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the position of the sailboat at
    19 : 30 is
    Q$(19.5,16)$.
    Using the distance between two points formula, we find

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{2.3em} \text{PQ} &= \sqrt{(19.5-22)^2 + (16-0)^2} \\[6pt]
     &\approx 16.2 \text{ km}
     \end{aligned}
     $$

4.  If we draw the path travelled by the sailboat on the $xy$-plane, we
    have

    ![bd77611d6e29ffa6f6f3e60a8aa893616695cb13.svg](images/bd77611d6e29ffa6f6f3e60a8aa893616695cb13.svg)

    Using the position vectors of B and P, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{BP}} = \vec{\text{OP}} - \vec{\text{OB}} &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 22 \\ 0  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} - \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 6+4.5t_1 \\ 9t_1-11  \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 16-4.5t_1 \\ 11-9t_1  \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Similarly, using the position vectors of C and P, we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{CP}} = \vec{\text{OP}} - \vec{\text{OC}} &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 22 \\ 0  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} - \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 6+4.5t_2 \\ 9t_2-11  \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 16-4.5t_2 \\ 11-9t_2  \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

1.  The sailboat is closest to the lighthouse when
    $\mathbf{v} \perp \vec{\text{BP}}$.

    Hence, solving the equation $\mathbf{v} \cdot \vec{\text{BP}} = 0$
    for $t_1$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4.5 \\ 9  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} \cdot \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 16-4.5t_1 \\ 11-9t_1  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} &= 0 \\[6pt]
    (4.5)(16-4.5t_1) + (9)(11-9t_1) &= 0 \\[6pt]
    t_1 &\approx 1.6889 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{2.2em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Converting hours to minutes, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0.6889 \text{ hours} &= 0.6889(60) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 41 \text{ minutes} \hspace{2.4em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore
    the sailboat is closest to the lighthouse at
     $\text{18\hspace{0.025em}:\hspace{-0.05em}11}$

2.  The sailboat is directly on the north of the lighthouse when
    $\mathbf{i} \perp \vec{\text{CP}}$.

    Hence, solving the equation $\mathbf{i} \cdot \vec{\text{CP}} = 0$
    for $t_2$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 0  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} \cdot \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 16-4.5t_2 \\ 11-9t_2  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} &= 0 \\[6pt]
    (1)(16-4.5t_2) + (0)(11-9t_2) &= 0 \\[6pt]
    t_2 &\approx 3.5556 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{2em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Converting hours to minutes, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0.5556 \text{ hours} &= 0.5556(60) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 33 \text{ minutes} \hspace{3em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore
    the sailboat is directly on the north of the lighthouse at
     $\text{20\hspace{-0.01em}:\hspace{-0.01em}03}$
