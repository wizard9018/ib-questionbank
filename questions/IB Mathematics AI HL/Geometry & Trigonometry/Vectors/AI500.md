# AI500

**Maximum mark: 9**

A lighthouse positioned at O$(0,0)$ is located at the centre of a ring
shaped coral reef. A sailboat nearby, initially outside the reef,
travels at a constant speed of
$8\sqrt{5}$ km h$^{-1}$
in the direction $\mathbf{d} = \mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j}$. Distances are
measured in kilometres.

1.  Find the velocity vector of the sailboat, in terms of $\mathbf{i}$
    and $\mathbf{j}$. [2 marks]

Initially, the sailboat is at the point A$(4,-12)$.

2.  Find, in terms of $\mathbf{i}$ and $\mathbf{j}$, its:
    1.  initial position vector;

    2.  position vector after $t$ hours, $t \geq 0$. [3 marks]

3.  Find the time when the sailboat is closest to the
    lighthouse. [2 marks]

The ring-shaped coral reef has a radius of
$10$ km.

4.  Determine whether the sailboat will hit the reef. Justify
    your answer. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The velocity vector of the sailboat is given by
    $\mathbf{v} = k(\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j})$, for $k > 0$.

    Using the magnitude of a vector formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |\mathbf{v}| &= \text{speed} \\[6pt]
    |k(\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j})| &= 8\sqrt{5} \\[6pt]
    \hspace{6em} k\textstyle \sqrt{1^2 + 2^2} &= 8\sqrt{5} \\[8pt]
    k &= 8 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence
    the velocity vector is
     $\mathbf{v} = 8\mathbf{i} + 16\mathbf{j}$

2.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathbf{a} &= \vec{\text{OA}} \\[6pt]
        &= 4\mathbf{i} - 12\mathbf{j} \hspace{5.1em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathbf{r} &= \mathbf{a} + t\mathbf{v} \\[6pt]
        &= (4\mathbf{i} - 12\mathbf{j}) + t(8\mathbf{i} + 16\mathbf{j}) \\[6pt]
        &= (4 + 8t)\mathbf{i} + (-12 + 16t)\mathbf{j} \hspace{2em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  The sailboat is closest to the lighthouse when
    $\mathbf{r} \perp \mathbf{d}$.

    Hence, solving the equation $\mathbf{r} \cdot \mathbf{d} = 0$ for
    $t$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    ((4 + 8t)\mathbf{i} + (-12 + 16t)\mathbf{j}) \cdot (\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j}) &= 0 \hspace{7em} \\[6pt]
    (4 + 8t)(1) + (-12 + 16t)(2) &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{4.25em} t = 0.5 \text{ hours} \hspace{0.7em}
    $$

4.  Evaluating $\mathbf{r}$ at $t = 0.5$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.7em} \mathbf{r} &= (4 + 8(0.5))\mathbf{i} + (-12 + 16(0.5))\mathbf{j} \\[6pt]
    &= 8\mathbf{i} - 4\mathbf{j}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the magnitude of
    a vector formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |\mathbf{r}| &= \sqrt{8^2 + (-4)^2} \hspace{5em} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 8.94  \text{ km}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $\text{Since \(8.94\hspace{0.25em}\text{km} < 10\hspace{0.25em}\text{km}\), the sailboat will hit the reef as it sails by.}$
