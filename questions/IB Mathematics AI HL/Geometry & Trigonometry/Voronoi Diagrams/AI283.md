# AI283

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the Voronoi diagram below with sites A, B, C and D, and
intersections E and F.

![dc0ad7cbb5108122ca451576ddfd73489762b391.svg](images/dc0ad7cbb5108122ca451576ddfd73489762b391.svg)

1.  Determine the site closest to the coordinate $(9,6)$. [1 mark]

2.  Determine the coordinates of the centre of the largest
    empty circle. [3 marks]

3.  Calculate the area of the largest empty circle [2 marks]

2.  The largest empty circle will be centered at either at E or F, as
    these intersections are the furthest distance from neighboring
    sites.

    Let's check intersection E and its distance from site C. From the
    diagram we see that E has coordinates $(2,1)$ and C has coordinates
    $(-2,2)$. Hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm C \textrm E     & = \sqrt {(-2-2)^2 + (2 - 1)^2}\\[6pt]
    & \approx 4.12
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Next, let's check intersection F and its distance from site C. From
    the diagram we see that F has coordinates $(2,4)$ and C has
    coordinates $(-2,2)$. Hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm C \textrm F     & = \sqrt {(-2-2)^2 + (2 - 4)^2}\\[6pt]
    & \approx 4.47
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Since $4.47 > 4.12$, the largest empty circle is centered at
      $\textrm F(2,4)$

3.  We found in (b) that the largest empty circle is located at
    intersection F and has the radius $4.47$. Using the area of a circle
    formula $A = \pi r^2$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A   & = \pi (4.47)^2\hspace{1.8cm}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} \approx 62.8 \,\mbox {units}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
