# AI275

**Maximum mark: 6**

Points A$(1,5)$, B$(5,9)$, C$(7,7)$, D$(9,3)$ and E$(13,11)$ represent
feeding-racks for deer in the Teutoburg Forest in Germany. The
feeding-racks are illustrated in the following coordinate axes.

Horizontal scale: $1$ unit represents $1$ km.

Vertical scale: $1$ unit represents $1$ km.

![23149fbffe62a6b61c2f25830428a5c615d32044.svg](images/23149fbffe62a6b61c2f25830428a5c615d32044.svg)

1.  Calculate the gradient of the line segment CE. [2 marks]

Three straight lines are drawn on the coordinate axes to form an
incomplete Voronoi diagram.

![84816af918e46094f30197a823a0a13090809053.svg](images/84816af918e46094f30197a823a0a13090809053.svg)

2.  Find the equation of the line that would complete the
    Voronoi cell containing site C. Give your answer in the form\
    $ax + by + d = 0$ where $a, b, d\in \mathbb Z$. [3 marks]

3.  In the context of the question, explain the meaning of the
    Voronoi cell containing site C. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the gradient formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m_{\textrm{CE}} &= \dfrac {y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {11 - 7} {13 - 7}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 2 3\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.

    ![daaa7f5e5c468599e615815ec1507a781cada12e.svg](images/daaa7f5e5c468599e615815ec1507a781cada12e.svg)

    Let's call the line that completes the Voronoi cell containing site
    C as $L$ (shown in red). Since $L$ is perpendicular CE, the gradient
    of $L$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m_L         & = -\dfrac 1 m_{\textrm{CE}}\\[6pt]
    & = -\dfrac 3 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    So far the equation of $L$ is
    $y = -\dfrac 3 2 x + c$. Since $L$ completes the Voronoi diagram, it
    passes through the midpoint of CE, $M_{CE}$. Using the coordinates
    of a midpoint formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    M_{CE} &= \left(\dfrac{x_1 + x_2} 2,\dfrac{y_1 + y_2} 2\right)  \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \left(\dfrac {13+7} 2,\dfrac{7+11}2\right)\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = \left(10,9\right)\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting coordinates of $M_{CE}$ into the equation for $L$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    9   & = -\dfrac 3 2 (10) + c\\
    c   & = 9 + 15\\
    & = 24
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the equation of $L$ in
    slope-intercept from, we get:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y   & = -\dfrac 3 2 x + 24
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Converting this equation
    into standard form:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
     3x +2y - 48  = 0
     \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  :::answer
    Every point within the cell containing site C is closer to the feeding-rack at site C than any other feeding-rack.
