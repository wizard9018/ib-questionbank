# AI1193

**Maximum mark: 17**

A company has four restaurants in a city, located at points $A(2,3)$, $B(8,3)$, $C(6,7)$ and $D$, relative to the city centre at $(0,0)$. All units are in kilometres. 

The following axes show the Voronoi diagram for the four restaurants, the restaurant at point $D$ is missing from the diagram.

The axes also show a road (in red) that runs directly from the restaurant at $A$ to the restaurant at $D$, intersecting the edges of the Voronoi diagram at points $E$ and $F$. The equation of the road is $y = 0.5\hspace{0.05em} x + 2$.

![1193a](images/1193a.JPG)

1. State the coordinates of the restaurant at point $D$. [1 mark]

2. Calculate the distance from the restaurant at point $A$ to the restaurant at point $D$. Give your answer in kilometres, to $4$ significant figures. [2 marks]

3. Find the coordinates of the midpoint between the restaurant at point $A$ and the restaurant at point $C$. [2 marks]

4. Hence, find the equation of the perpendicular bisector of [$AC$].[4 marks]

5. 1. State the coordinates of point $F$.

    

   2. Find the coordinates of point $E$.[4 marks]

Juan has been asked to take some supplies from the restaurant at $A$ to the restaurant at $D$.

6. Determine the percentage of Juan’s journey during which he will be closer to the restaurant at $C$ than any other restaurant.[4 marks]

1. Because the edge that lies between points $C$ and $D$ is vertical, those points must have the same $y$ coordinate. And, because point $C$ is two units to the left of this edge, point $D$ must be two units to the right.

    Therefore, $$\text{the coordinates of point $D$ are $(10,7)$.}$$ 

    $$                    

    \begin{aligned}            

    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}    

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. An updated Voronoi diagram including point $D$ is shown below.

    ![1193b](images/1193b.JPG)

    Using the formula for the distance between two points, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    d &= \sqrt{(x_1-x_2)^2+(y_1-y_2)^2} \\[6pt]

    &= \sqrt{(10-2)^2+(7-3)^2} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-0.8em}\boxed{d = 8.944\,\text{km}\,(4\,\mathrm{s.f.})} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. Using the formula for the midpoint, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    M &= \left(\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{2}\,,\,\dfrac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right) \\[8pt]

    &= \left(\dfrac{2+6}{2}\,,\,\dfrac{3+7}{2}\right) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-1.4em}\boxed{M = (4,5)} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

4. The perpendicular bisector of [$AC$] is a straight line that passes through the point $(4,5)$, as we found in part (c). We now need the gradient, and this will be the negative reciprocal of the gradient of the line passing through points $A$ and $C$. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    m &= \dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1} \\[6pt]

    m_{AC} &= \dfrac{7-3}{6-2} \\[6pt]

    m_{\text{perpendicular}} &= -\dfrac{6-2}{7-3} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    &= -1 \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    And, using the formula for the equation of a straight line with $m=-1$ and $(x_1,y_1)=(4,5)$, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    y - y_1 &= m(x - x_1) \\[6pt]

    y - 5 &= -(x-4) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-0.9em}\boxed{y = -x + 9} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

6. When Juan is inside the Voronoi cell for site $C$, he is closer to restaurant $C$ than any other. This occurs between points $E$ and $F$. This is shown in $\color{green}{\text{green}}$ in the following diagram, with other information updated from part (e).

    ![1193c](images/1193c.JPG)

    We know from part (b) that the total distance of Juan's journey is $8.944\,\text{km}$. We now need to calculate the distance from point $E$ to point $F$.

    Using the formula for the distance between two points, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    d &= \sqrt{(x_1-x_2)^2+(y_1-y_2)^2} \\[6pt]

    &= \sqrt{\left(8-\tfrac{14}{3}\right)^2+\left(6-\tfrac{13}{3}\right)^2} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    d &= 3.726...\,\text{km} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Hence the percentage of Juan’s journey during which he will be closer to the restaurant at $C$ than any other restaurant is given by

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{3.726...}{8.944}\times 100 &= \hspace{0.525em}\boxed{41.7\%\,(3\,\mathrm{s.f.)}} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
