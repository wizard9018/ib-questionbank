# AI279

**Maximum mark: 6**

The sites A, B, C and D (not shown) in the Voronoi diagram below
represent the location of wind speed recording gauges on a parcel of
farm land on the Northern Island of New Zealand.

Horizontal scale: $1$ unit represents $1$ km.

Vertical scale: $1$ unit represents $1$ km.

![2e4bc1f167fae984508bdfc8655ec9e3dc31f646.svg](images/2e4bc1f167fae984508bdfc8655ec9e3dc31f646.svg)

1.  Site D is missing from the diagram. Write down its coordinates.
    [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the area of the cell B. [2 marks]

3.  Find the distance from point E$(12,4)$ to the nearest wind speed
    recording gauge. [2 marks]

The average wind speed at each site is given in the table below.

![a4da06b1280f4c30be27af25a94691bdc38c424d.svg](images/a4da06b1280f4c30be27af25a94691bdc38c424d.svg)

4.  Estimate the wind speed at point F$(4,2)$. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Sites B and D are separated by the perpendicular bisector BD. Hence,
    site D is located at:

    $\text{D (7,1)}$

2.  From the diagram we see that the base is
    $6$ km and the high is
    $5$ km. Using the area of a triangle
    formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac 1 2 b h\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 \,(6)(5)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = 15\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{km}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The nearest wind speed recording gauge to point E is site C. Using
    the distance between two points formula between E and site C we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= \sqrt{(x_1 - x_2)^2  + (y_1 - y_2)^2}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \sqrt{(12 - 8)^2 + (4 - 6)^2}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = \sqrt {20}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & \approx 4.47\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {km}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The nearest site to the point F(4,2) is site D. Using nearest
    neighbour interpolation, the wind speed can be estimated as the same
    as D. Hence, we can estimate the wind speed at F to be:

    $$
    9.2\,\mbox {ms}^{-1}
    $$
