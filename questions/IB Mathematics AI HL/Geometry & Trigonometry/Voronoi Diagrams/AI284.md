# AI284

**Maximum mark: 13**

A town currently has four Italian pizza restaurants. A famous chef
wishes to open a new pizza restaurant in the town but wants it to be
located as far away as possible from the four current restaurants.

The Voronoi diagram below showing the positions of the four current
restaurants on a set of coordinates axes: A$(2,3)$, B$(8,4)$, C$(7,1)$,
and D$(2,1)$, where one unit represents $1$ km.

![ae627e16c68b506e076335c4f545f0dbee0d2b20.svg](images/ae627e16c68b506e076335c4f545f0dbee0d2b20.svg)

1.  Write down the coordinates of P. [1 mark]

2.  Show that the coordinates of Q are $(5.03,3.32)$, correct to three
    significant figures. [6 marks]

3.  Determine where the famous chef should position their new
    restaurant. [4 marks]

The table below shows the average weekly number of pizzas sold at each
of the four current pizza restaurants.

![1bafeae4200d71aa78120d774062db4613437d2e.svg](images/1bafeae4200d71aa78120d774062db4613437d2e.svg)

4.  Determine the estimated average weekly number of pizzas
    this new shop will sell. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\textrm P(4.5,2)$

2.  We firstly need to find the equations of the perpendicular bisectors
    PB(A,B) and PB(B,C). First let's find PB(A,B). Using the gradient
    formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m_{AB} &= \dfrac {y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {4 - 3} {8 - 2}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 6\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the
    gradient of the normal to AB is $-6$ and PB(A,B) has the equation
    $y = -6 x + c$.

    Let's find the midpoint of AB, $M_{AB}$. Using the coordinates of a
    midpoint formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    M_{AB} &= \left(\dfrac{x_1 + x_2} 2,\dfrac{y_1 + y_2} 2\right) \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = \left(\dfrac {8 + 2} 2,\dfrac{4 + 3}2\right)\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = \left(5,3.5\right)\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting coordinates of $M_{AB}$ into the equation of PB(A,B),
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    3.5 & = -6(5) + c\\[7pt]
    c   & = 33.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Finally the equation of PB(A,B) is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= -6 x  + 33.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Next we find find the equation of PB(B,C). Using the gradient
    formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m_{BC}  &= \dfrac {y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {4 - 1} {8 - 7}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = 3\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the gradient of the normal to BC is $-\dfrac 1 3$ and PB(B,C)
    has the equation $y = -\dfrac 1 3 x + c$.

    Let's now find the midpoint of BC, $M_{BC}$. Using the coordinates
    of a midpoint formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    M_{BC} &= \left(\dfrac{x_1 + x_2} 2,\dfrac{y_1 + y_2} 2\right)\rule[4mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \left(\dfrac {8 + 7} 2,\dfrac {4 + 1}2\right)\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = \left(7.5,2.5\right)\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting the coordinates of $M_{BC}$ into the equation of
    PB(B,C), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2.5 & = -\dfrac 1 3 (7.5) + c\\[6pt]
    c   & = 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the equation of PB(B,C) is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= -\dfrac 1 3 x  + 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The coordinates of $Q$ can be
    found by solving the following system of linear equations

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{-7.5mm}\begin{cases}
    y = -6 x  + 33.5\\
    y = -\dfrac 1 3 x  + 5
    \end{cases}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    By using G.D.C. we get

    $$
    \textrm Q(5.03,3.32)
    $$

3.  The new pizza restaurant should be located at either intersections Q
    or P, as these locations are the furthest distance away from each of
    the neighboring sites. We need to determine which of these points is
    the furthest from its closest site. Let's compare the distances of Q
    and P from site A.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    QA  & = \sqrt{(5.03 - 2)^2 + (3.32 - 3)^2}\\[6pt]
    & \approx 3.05\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{km} \qquad\qquad \mbox{[by using G.D.C]}\\[6pt]
    PA  & = \sqrt{(4.5 - 2)^2 + (2- 3)^2}\\[6pt]
    & \approx 2.69\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{km} \qquad\qquad \mbox{[by using G.D.C]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Since QA \> PA, the new restaurant should be located at
      $\textrm Q(5.03,3.32)$

4.  Q is equidistant from sites A, B and C. Using nearest neighbour
    interpolation, we can estimate the average weekly number of pizzas
    this new restaurant will sell by finding the average of the 3
    neighbouring sites
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac {1634 + 1752 + 1791} 3   & = 1725.67\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}\approx 1730\,\mbox{pizzas}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
