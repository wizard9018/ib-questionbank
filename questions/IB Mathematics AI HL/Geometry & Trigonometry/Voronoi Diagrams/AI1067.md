# AI1067

**Maximum mark: 13**

The location of six cabins (labelled A, B, C, D, E and F) within a National Park are shown, along with the Voronoi diagram. All distances are measured in kilometres. Every summer, a park ranger is assigned to each cabin to monitor the area within the Voronoi cell surrounding their cabin.

![ai1067](images/ai1067.PNG)

Cabin A is at (3,7), cabin B is at (7,7) and cabin C is at (9,3). The perpendicular bisector of AB has equation $x=5$.

1. 1.  State the coordinates of the midpoint of BC.

    2. Find the gradient of BC.

    3. Hence find the equation of the perpendicular bisector of BC.[5 marks]

The rangers from cabins A and C are going to meet at point G.

2. Determine how far each ranger needs to walk.[4 marks]

Cabin D is at (5,11).

3. Show that the cell containing cabin B is a trapezoid.[4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 1. Using the formula for the midpoint, we have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \mathrm{M}_{BC} &= \left(\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{2},\dfrac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right) \\[8pt]

        &= \left(\dfrac{7+9}{2},\dfrac{7+3}{2}\right) \\[8pt]

        &\hspace{-2.4em}\boxed{\mathrm{M}_{BC} = (8,5)}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

    2. Using the formula for the gradient, we have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        m &= \dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1} \\[8pt]

        &= \dfrac{3-7}{9-7} \\[8pt]

        &\hspace{-1.2em}\boxed{m = -2}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

    3. The gradient of the perpendicular bisector is the negative reciprocal of the gradient of BC. $m = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Using the formula for the equation of a straight line, we have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        y - y_1 &= m(x-x_1) \\[8pt]

        y - 5 &= 0.5(x - 8) \\[8pt]

        &\hspace{-0.9em}\boxed{y = 0.5x + 1}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

2. Point G is at the intersection of the perpendicular bisector of AB, $x=5$, and the perpendicular bisector of BC, $y=0.5x+1$. The $x$-coordinate of point G is therefore 5, and substituting this value into $y=0.5x+1$ we find the $y$-coordinate is $3.5$.

    The coordinates of point G are $(5,3.5)$. Given that point G is at the vertex where the edges of the Voronoi cells for cabin A and C meet, point G is equidistant from points A and C.

    Hence, we can find the distance from either A or C to G. We will choose A(3,7). Using the formula for the distance between two points, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    d &= \sqrt{(x_1-x_2)^2+(y_1-y_2)^2} \\[8pt]

    &= \sqrt{(5-3)^2+(3.5-7)^2} \\[8pt]

    d &= 4.031\ ...

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    $$\boxed{\text{Each ranger needs to walk approximately $4.03\,$ km.}}$$

3. A trapezoid is a quadrilateral that has one pair of parallel sides. We note that the cell containing cabin B is a quadrilateral, and from part (a) (iii) we have that the gradient of one side (the perpendicular bisector of BC) is $0.5$. Given that the coordinates of D are $(5,11)$, we can find the gradient of the opposite side. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    m_{BD} &= \dfrac{11-7}{5-7} \\[8pt]

    &= -2

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    The perpendicular bisector of BD therefore has a gradient of $0.5$. Hence we have a pair of parallel sides. 

    $\boxed{\text{The cell containing cabin B is a trapezoid.}}$
