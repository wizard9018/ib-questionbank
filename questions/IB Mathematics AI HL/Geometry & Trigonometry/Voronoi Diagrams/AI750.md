# AI750

**Maximum mark: 9**

Consider the Voronoi diagram below for a town centre that contains four coffee shops A, B, C, and D.

![750](images/750.JPEG)

The equation of the perpendicular bisector between sites B and C is
$y = 3x-17$. The coordinates of the midpoint between C and D is (7,4).

1.   Determine the equation of the perpendicular bisector between C and D. [3 marks]

2.  Hence, determine the coordinates of the intersection point V$_2$. [3 marks]

The perpendicular bisectors of AB and AD intersect at the point V$_1$(4,5), which is $2.236\,$km from location D, correct to 4 significant figures. A new coffee shop will be built at either V$_1$ or V$_2$.

3.  Given that the new shop is to be as far away as possible from an existing coffee shop, determine which of the locations the new coffee shop should be built at. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  First we find the gradient of CD.
    Using the gradient formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m_{\text{CD}} &=     \dfrac {y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {3 - 5}{5-9} \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\
    & = \dfrac{1}{2}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the gradient of the perpendicular bisector to CD is $-2$.

    Substituting the coordinates of the given midpoint (7,4) and the slope -2 into the equation of a line, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    4     & = -2 (7) + c \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    c     & = 18
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the equation of the perpendicular bisector CD is
    $$
    \begin{equation*}
    \boxed{y = -2x  + 18}\hspace{6mm}\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{equation*}
    $$

2.  The coordinates of $\text{V}_2$ can be found by finding the coordinates where perpendicular bisector of BC and the perpendicular bisector of CD intersect. Hence, letting these two perpendicular bisectors be equal to each other, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    3x-17     & = -2 x  + 18 \tag*{\hspace{-0.2em}{\textbf{(M1)}}}\\[6pt]
    x     & = 7 \tag*{\hspace{-0.2em}{\textbf{(A1)}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence $y = 4$ and therefore the coordinates of V$_2$ are $$\boxed{(7,4)}$$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
     \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The new coffee shop must be at V$_1$ or V$_2$, as these locations are the furthest distance away from each of the sites. We need to determine which of these points is the furthest from its closest site.

    Given V$_1$ and its distance to D
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm{V}_1\textrm{D}     & = \ 2.236\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {km}\,\text{(4 s.f.)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Consider V$_2$ and its distance to D
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm V_2\textrm D     & = \sqrt{(7-5)^2 + (4-3)^2} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    & = \ 2.2360...\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {km} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The intersections V$_1$ and V$_2$ are at the same distance from any of its nearby coffee shops, and hence the optimal location for the new coffee shop is at either
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
     \tag*{\textbf{R1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\boxed{\textrm V_1(4,5) \text{ or } \textrm V_2(7,4)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
