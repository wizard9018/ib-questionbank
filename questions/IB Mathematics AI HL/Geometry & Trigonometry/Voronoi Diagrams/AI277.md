# AI277

**Maximum mark: 6**

The Voronoi diagram below shows the fitness studios A, B and C, located
in a particular district.

![b3aaeee21fcd45406c5c929ca6b8501265c0c1b5.svg](images/b3aaeee21fcd45406c5c929ca6b8501265c0c1b5.svg)

1.  On the diagram, shade the cell with the missing fitness studio.
    [2 marks]

2.  Determine the coordinates of the missing fitness studio, D.
    [2 marks]

3.  Jennifer lives an equal distance from fitness studios A and D. Find
    the shortest possible distance Jennifer's house
    could be from fitness studios A and D.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.

    ![73889d0a0a0383f7b650f1da42b30eebb68bd17a.svg](images/73889d0a0a0383f7b650f1da42b30eebb68bd17a.svg)

2.  The missing studio D will be the same distance from the line $x = 2$
    as studio B, since the perpendicular bisector BD is vertical. The
    distance between $x = 2$ and B is $3$ km.
    Hence the missing fitness studio D is located at

    $$
    \text D(-1,4)
    $$

3.  Jennifer's house is at the midpoint of \[AD\]. Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= \dfrac 1 2 [\text{AD}]\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 \sqrt{(x_1 - x_2)^2  + (y_1 - y_2)^2}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{1.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 \sqrt{(-3 - (-1))^2 + (1 - 4)^2}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 \sqrt {4 + 9}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & \approx 1.80\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {km}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
