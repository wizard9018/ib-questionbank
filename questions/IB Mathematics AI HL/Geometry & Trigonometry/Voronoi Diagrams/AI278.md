# AI278

**Maximum mark: 6**

The Voronoi diagram below shows the location of fire stations in outback
Queensland, a North-Eastern state of Australia.

Horizontal scale: $1$ unit represents $100$ km.

Vertical scale: $1$ unit represents $100$ km.

![69048454d8fed10b53cd670a0d7ba601ab5f7c04.svg](images/69048454d8fed10b53cd670a0d7ba601ab5f7c04.svg)

1.  Identify the nearest fire-station to houses located at
    1.  $(300,300)$

    2.  $(-200,100)$ [2 marks]

A house located at $(200,200)$ has an internal fire and requires
assistance.

2.  Determine which fire-stations the house is closest to.
    [2 marks]

3.  Determine how far the house is from these two
    fire-stations. Give your answer to the nearest kilometre.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  $\text A$

    2.  $\text D$

2.  $\text{A and C}$

3.  The house is the equal distance from fire stations A and C. Let's
    use the distance between two points formula to find the distance
    from fire station A to the house. We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= \sqrt{(x_1 - x_2)^2  + (y_1 - y_2)^2}  \rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = \sqrt{(100 - 200)^2 + (500-200)^2}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = \sqrt {100\hspace{0.15em}000}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = 316.2...\\
    & =  316\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {km}\hspace{0.5em}\text{(3 s.f.)}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
