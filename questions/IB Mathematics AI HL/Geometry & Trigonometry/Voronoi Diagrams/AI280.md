# AI280

**Maximum mark: 6**

The sites A, B (not shown), C, and D in the Voronoi diagram below
represent the location of rainfall recording gauges on a large corn farm
in Texas, USA.

Horizontal scale: $1$ unit represents $1$ km.

Vertical scale: $1$ unit represents $1$ km.

![48edf3eec6ac0a57254e540da8b74e8897e3c38b.svg](images/48edf3eec6ac0a57254e540da8b74e8897e3c38b.svg)

1.  Site B is missing from the diagram. Write down its coordinates.
    [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the area of the cell C. [2 marks]

3.  Find the distance from point E$(9,8)$ to the nearest rainfall
    recording gauge. [2 marks]

The table below shows measurements of the average monthly rainfall (mm)
of each of the recording gauges.

![f47fc4920502443be484f99b587174bf55adce1c.svg](images/f47fc4920502443be484f99b587174bf55adce1c.svg)

4.  Estimate the average monthly rainfall at point F$(1,6)$
    in mm. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Sites B and C are evenly separated by the perpendicular bisector BC.
    Hence, site B is located at:
    $$
    \text{B (5,7)}
    $$

2.  From the diagram we see that cell C is in the shape of a triangle
    with a base of $6$ km and a height of
    $4$ km. Using the area of a triangle
    formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac 1 2 b h\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 \,(6)(4)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = 12\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{km}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The nearest rainfall recording gauge to point E is site B. Using the
    distance between two points formula between E and B, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= \sqrt{(x_1 - x_2)^2  + (y_1 - y_2)^2}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \sqrt{(9 - 5)^2 + (8 - 7)^2}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = \sqrt {17}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & \approx 4.12\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {km}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The nearest site to the point F is site A. Using nearest neighbour
    interpolation, the average monthly rainfall can be estimated as the
    same as A. Hence, we can estimate the average monthly rainfall at F
    to be:

    $$
    43\,\mbox {mm}
    $$
