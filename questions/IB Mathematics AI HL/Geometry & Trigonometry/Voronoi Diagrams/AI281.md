# AI281

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the Voronoi diagram below for a city that contains the towns A,
B, C, and D.

![3a7a3f9e315b54f19926d7481becc661100f4e83.svg](images/3a7a3f9e315b54f19926d7481becc661100f4e83.svg)

The equation of the perpendicular bisector between sites A and D,
PB(A,D), is found to be
$$
\begin{aligned}
y       &= x - 25
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Determine the equation of the perpendicular bisector between C and
    D, PB(C,D). [2 marks]

2.  Hence, determine the coordinates of the intersection V$_2$.
    [2 marks]

3.  Determine the optimal position for a toxic waste dump in this city,
    such that it is as far away from a town as possible. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  First we find the gradient of CD. Using the gradient formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m_{CD} &=   \dfrac {y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {-26 - 14}{29 - 39} \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = 4\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the gradient of
    the normal to CD is $-\dfrac 1 4$ and PB(C,D) has the equation
    $y = -\dfrac 1 4 \,x + c$.

    Let's find the midpoint of CD. Let's call this midpoint M. Using the
    coordinates of a midpoint formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm M &= \left(\dfrac{x_1 + x_2} 2,\dfrac{y_1 + y_2} 2\right)\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = \left(\dfrac {39 + 29} 2,\dfrac{14 + (-26)}2\right)\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = \left(34,-6\right)\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.25mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting the coordinates of M into the equation of PB(C,D), we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -6  & = -\dfrac 1 4 (34) + c\\[6pt]
    c   & = \dfrac 5 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the equation of PB(C,D) is

    $$
    y = -\dfrac 1 4 \, x  + \dfrac 5 2\hspace{6mm}
    $$

2.  The coordinates of $V_2$ can be found by finding the coordinates
    where PB(A,D) and PB(C,D) intersect. Hence we let these two
    perpendicular bisector equations be equal to each other.

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x - 25  & = -\dfrac 1 4 \, x  + \dfrac 5 2\\[6pt]
    x       & = 22 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence $y = -3$ and therefore the coordinates of V$_2$
    are  $ (22,-3)$

3.  The location of the toxic waste dump must be at either V$_1$ or
    V$_2$, as these locations are the furthest distance away from each
    of the sites. We need to determine which of these points is the
    furthest from its closest site.\
    Consider V$_1$ and its distance to A
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm{V}_1\textrm{A}  & = \sqrt{(-1-18)^2 + (4 - 13)^2}\\[6pt]
    & \approx 21.02\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {km}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Consider V$_2$ and its distance to A
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm V_2\textrm A    & = \sqrt{(-1-22)^2 + (4 - (-3)^2}\\[6pt]
    & \approx 24.04\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {km}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The intersection V$_2$ is the furthest from any of its nearby towns,
    and hence the optimal location for the toxic waste dump is at

    $$
    \textrm V_2(22,-3)
    $$
