# AI1033

**Maximum mark: 6**

The sites A, B, C (not shown) and D in the Voronoi diagram below represent the location of rain gauges in a wildlife sanctuary. 

Horizontal scale: $1$ unit represents $1$ km.

Vertical scale: $1$ unit represents $1$ km.

![ai1033a](images/ai1033a.PNG)

1. Site C is missing from the diagram. Write down its coordinates.

[1 mark]

2. Calculate the area of the cell containing site B.

[2 marks]

3. Find the distance from point E$(14,4)$ to the nearest rain gauge.

[2 marks]

The average monthly rainfall at each site is given in the table below.

![ai1033b](images/ai1033b.PNG)

4. Estimate the average monthly rainfall at point F$(2,4)$.[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Site A has coordinates $(3,8)$. Sites A and C are separated by the perpendicular bisector AC. Perpendicular bisector AC is vertical (equation $x=7$) and site A is 4 units to the left. Site C is therefore 4 units to the right, with the same $y$-coordinate. Hence, site C is located at $\boxed{\text{C}(11,8)}$

2. From the diagram we see that the cell containing site B is triangular with a base of $12$ km and a height of $6$ km.

Using the area of a triangle formula $A = \dfrac 1 2 b h$, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A         & = \dfrac 1 2 \,(12)(6)\\[6pt]

        &\hspace{-1em}\boxed{A = 36\,\mbox{km}^2}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. The nearest rain gauge to point E is site C. Using the distance between two points formula  $d = \sqrt{(x_1 - x_2)^2  + (y_1 - y_2)^2}$ between E(14,4) and site C(11,8) we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    d     & = \sqrt{(14 - 11)^2 + (4 - 8)^2}\\[6pt]

     & = \sqrt {25}\\[6pt]

     & \hspace{-0.8em}\boxed{d=5\,\mbox {km}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

4. The nearest site to the point F(2,4) is site A. Using nearest neighbour interpolation, the average monthly rainfall can be estimated as the same as A. Hence, we can estimate the average monthly rainfall at F to be $\boxed{210\,\mbox {mm}}$
