# AI1215

**Maximum mark: 9**

A city is planning to build three new public libraries located at the points

$\text{A}(3,2) \hspace{5em}\text{B}(9,6) \hspace{5em}\text{C}(5,10)$

The Voronoi diagram for these three libraries is shown below.

![VD3](images/VD3.png)

1. Find the equation of the perpendicular bisector of AB. Give your answer in the form  $$y = mx + c$$. [4 marks]

The city council is considering building a new library at point D $$(12,4)$$.

2. Determine which existing library (A, B, or C) the point D is currently closest to. Justify your answer. [2 marks]

3. Each library offers free Wi-Fi within a radius of 5 km. A student is located at point (6,5).
State whether the student can access free Wi-Fi, and if so  from which of the  libraries. Justify your answer. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using the midpoint formula, with $(x_1,y_1)=\text{A}(3,2)$ and $(x_2,y_2)=\text{B}(9,6)$, we can find the midpoint of AB
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Midpoint}  &=
    \left( \frac{x_{1} + x_{2}}{2}, \frac{y_{1} + y_{2}}{2} \right) \\[8pt]
    &= \left( \frac{3 + 9}{2}, \frac{2 + 6}{2} \right) \\[8pt]
    \text{Midpoint} &= (6, 4)\tag*{\textbf{(A1)}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Now substituting the coordinates of A and B into the gradient formula, we can find the gradient of AB
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m &= \frac{y_{2} -y_{1} }{x_{2} - x_{1}}\\[8pt]
    &= \frac{6 - 2}{9 - 3}\\[8pt]
    m_{\text{AB}} &= \frac{2}{3} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}\\[8pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The negative reciprocal is the perpendicular gradient to AB
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m &= -\frac{3}{2} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[8pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Now we can substitute the perpendicular gradient and the coordinates of the midpoint into the equation of a line formula
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y - y_{1} &= m(x - x_{1}) \\[8pt]
    y - 4 &= -\frac{3}{2}(x - 6) \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-0.7em}\boxed{y = -\frac{3}{2}x + 13} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. Considering the cell that point D(12,4) lies in, it is the same cell containing the point B(9,6), therefore

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.275em}\boxed{\text{B is closest, since it is within the same cell as D}}\tag*{\textbf{A1R1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3. Using the formula to find the distance from (6,5) to each of the libraries, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= \sqrt{(x_1-x_2)^2+(y_1-y_2)^2} \\[8pt]
    \text{Distance to A } &= \sqrt{(6 - 3)^2 + (5 - 2)^2} \\[8pt]
        &= \sqrt{18} = 4.242... \\[8pt]
    \text{Distance to B } &= \sqrt{(6 - 9)^2 + (5 - 6)^2} \\[8pt]
     &= \sqrt{10} = 3.162... \\[8pt]
    \text{Distance to C } &= \sqrt{(6 - 5)^2 + (5 - 10)^2}  \\[8pt]
     &= \sqrt{26} =5.099...
     \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
     \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{2.5em}\text{The student is within 5 km of A and B, but not C.} \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{\text{Yes, the student can access free Wi-Fi from Library A and B}} \tag*{\textbf{A1A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
