# AI889

**Maximum mark: 7**

Cellular phone towers in a town are represented by the points P, Q, R,
and S (not shown) in the partially complete Voronoi diagram shown below.
The regions created by the diagram represent the phone coverage provided
by each tower.

![2a7c46476c8509aa442b7f02044f64514f5bf4a9.svg](images/2a7c46476c8509aa442b7f02044f64514f5bf4a9.svg)

1.  Write down the coordinates of the missing phone tower S. [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the coverage area provided by phone tower Q. [2 marks]

3.  Find the gradient of the edge of Voronoi diagram between towers Q
    and R.[2 marks]

The phone towers provide different data download speeds, as per the
table below.

| Tower | Data download speed |
|:-----:|:-------------------:|
|   P   |      $25$ MBps      |     |
|   R   |      $30$ MBps      |     |
| Q & S |      $20$ MBps      |     |

4.  A customer is located at $(4,10)$. Determine the download speed she
    receives. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Tower S will be the same distance from the line $y=3$ as tower Q,
    since the perpendicular bisector of QS is horizontal. The vertical
    distance between Q and the line $y=3$ is $2 \text{ km}$. Hence,
    tower S is located at
    $$
    (7,1)
    $$

2.  Using the formula for the area of a triangle, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac{1}{2}\times \text{base}\times \text \\[6pt]
    &=\dfrac{1}{2}(10-4)(8-3)\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}=15\text{ km}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the gradient formula to calculate the gradient of the line
    connecting Q$(7,5)$ and R$(8,6)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m_{QR}  & = \dfrac {6-5}{8-7} \\[6pt]
    & = 1
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The gradient of the edge between Q and R is
    therefore
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m_{e,QR} &= -\dfrac{1}{m_{QR}}\hspace{1cm}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.35em} = -1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The diagram below shows the location of the customer, C, at
    $(4,10)$. The towers nearest to C are P and R. Let $d_1$ and $d_2$
    be the distances from C to P and C to R respectively, as shown.

    ![50a5faae8e1af07fd9973ec8ce9cd78ca2e1f280.svg](images/50a5faae8e1af07fd9973ec8ce9cd78ca2e1f280.svg)

    Using the distance formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d_1 &= \sqrt{(4-2)^2+(10-6)^2}\\[6pt]
    &\approx 4.47 \\[6pt]
    d_2 &=\sqrt{(4-8)^2+(10-6)^2}\\[6pt]
    &\approx5.66
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Since $d_1<d_2$, the customer is
    nearest to tower P.

    Hence, the customer will get
     $\text{25} \text{ MBps data download speed.}$
