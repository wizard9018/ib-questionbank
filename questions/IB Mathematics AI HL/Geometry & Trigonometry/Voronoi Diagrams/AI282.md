# AI282

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the Voronoi diagram below that shows the location of convenient
stores within a town. The convenient stores are labelled A, B, C, and D.

The equation of the perpendicular bisector between sites A and D,
PB(A,D), is found to be
$$
\begin{aligned}
y       = -x + 6
\end{aligned}
$$

![644afabe646f4c28f745560ecd152df984ab1c38.svg](images/644afabe646f4c28f745560ecd152df984ab1c38.svg)

1.  Determine the equation of the perpendicular bisector between sites C
    and D, PB(C,D). [2 marks]

2.  Hence, determine the coordinates of the intersection V$_1$.
    [2 marks]

A company that opens and manages convenient stores wants to open a new
store in this town.

3.  Determine the optimal position for this new store, so that
    it is as far as possible from its nearest store. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  First we find the gradient of CD. Using the gradient formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m &= \dfrac {y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {2 - (-8)} {-6 - 0}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = -\dfrac 5 3\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the
    gradient of the normal to CD is $\dfrac 3 5$ and PB(C,D) has the
    equation $y = \dfrac 3 5 \,x + c$.

    Let's find the midpoint of CD. Let's call this midpoint M. Using the
    coordinates of a midpoint formula M, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm M &= \left(\dfrac{x_1 + x_2} 2,\dfrac{y_1 + y_2} 2\right)\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \left(\dfrac {0 - 6} 2,\dfrac{-8 + 2}2\right)\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = \left(-3,-3\right)\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting the coordinates of M into the equation of PB(C,D), we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -3  & = \dfrac 3 5 (-3) + c\\[6pt]
    c   & = -\dfrac 6 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the equation of PB(C,D) is

    $$
    y = \dfrac 3 5\, x  - \dfrac 6 5\hspace{8mm}
    $$

2.  The coordinates of V$_1$ can be found by finding the coordinates
    where PB(A,D) and PB(C,D) intersect. Hence we let these two
    perpendicular bisector equations be equal to each other.

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -x + 6  & = \dfrac 3 5 \, x  - \dfrac 6 5\\[6pt]
    x       & = \dfrac 9 2 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence $y = \dfrac 3 2$ and therefore the coordinates of V$_1$ are
      $ \left(\dfrac 9 2,\dfrac 3 2\right)$

3.  The location of this new store should be at V$_1$ or V$_2$, as these
    locations are the furthest distance away from each of the
    neighboring sites. We need to determine which of these points is the
    furthest from its closest site.\
    Consider V$_1$ and its distance to C
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm V_1\textrm C    & = \sqrt{\left(0 - \dfrac 9 2\right)^2 + \left(- 8 - \dfrac 3 2 \right)^2}\\[6pt]
    & \approx 10.51\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {km}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Consider V$_2$ and its distance to C
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm V_2 \textrm C   & = \sqrt{(0- 7)^2 + (-8-1)^2}\\[6pt]
    & \approx 11.40\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {km}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The intersection V$_2$ is the furthest from any of the nearest
    stores, and therefore the optimal location for this new store is at

    $$
    \textrm V_2(7,1)
    $$
