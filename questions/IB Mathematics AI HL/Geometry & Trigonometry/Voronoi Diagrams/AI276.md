# AI276

**Maximum mark: 6**

Points A$(-3,5)$, B$(5,3)$, C$(3,9)$, D$(3,-3)$ and E$(11,5)$ represent
coffee shops in a city. The coffee shops are illustrated on the
following coordinate axes.

Horizontal scale: $1$ unit represents $1$ km.

Vertical scale: $1$ unit represents $1$ km.

![c9d9dfbd3e124a4769965e862fda5c8ffbed9dad.svg](images/c9d9dfbd3e124a4769965e862fda5c8ffbed9dad.svg)

1.  Calculate the gradient of the line segment BE. [2 marks]

The diagram below shows three straight lines that form an incomplete
Voronoi diagram.

![8f78725f0ab7a84074a9d4c10793ae75d0ecd81e.svg](images/8f78725f0ab7a84074a9d4c10793ae75d0ecd81e.svg)

2.  Find the equation of the line completing the Voronoi cell
    containing site B. Give your answer in the form $ax + by + d = 0$
    where $a, b, d\in \mathbb Z$. [3 marks]

3.  In the context of the question, explain the meaning of the
    Voronoi cell containing site B. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the gradient formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m_{\textrm{BE}} &= \dfrac {y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {5 - 3} {11 - 5}\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 3\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.

    ![27454fedb787ade666f270940249af4debb07fc9.svg](images/27454fedb787ade666f270940249af4debb07fc9.svg)

    Let's call the line that completes the Voronoi cell containing site
    B as $L$ (shown in red). Since $L$ is perpendicular to BE, the
    gradient of $L$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m_L     & = -\dfrac 1 m_{\textrm{BE}}\\[6pt]
    & = - 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    So far the equation of $L$ is
    $y = -3 x + c$. Since $L$ completes the Voronoi diagram, it passes
    through the midpoint of BE. Let's call this midpoint $M_{BE}$. Using
    the coordinates of a midpoint formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    M_{BE} &=   \left(\dfrac{x_1 + x_2} 2,\dfrac{y_1 + y_2} 2\right)\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \left(\dfrac {5+11} 2,\dfrac{3+5}2\right)\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = \left(8,4\right)\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting coordinates of $M_{BE}$ into the equation for $L$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    4   & = -3 (8) + c\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    c   & = 4 + 24\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = 28\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the equation of
    $L$ in slope-intercept form is:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y   & = -3 x + 28
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Converting this equation into
    standard form, we get:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
     3x + y - 28 = 0
     \end{aligned}
    $$
