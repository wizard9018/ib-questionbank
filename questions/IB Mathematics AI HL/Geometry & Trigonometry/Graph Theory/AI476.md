# AI476

**Maximum mark: 6**

A postman starts at the Post Office located at O and needs to travel
along every road illustrated by the edges in the graph below. The
numbers on the graph represent distances in kilometres of the roads.

![4f7c5504d56b4457091d152cafd037713c507b75.svg](images/4f7c5504d56b4457091d152cafd037713c507b75.svg)

1.  Find a solution to the Chinese Postman Problem for the graph.
    [4 marks]

2.  Write down the length, in kilometres, of an optimal Chinese postman
    route. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The odd vertices are A,B, E and F.
    There are three ways of pairing them together.

    1: A&B and E&F.  2: A&E and B&F.
     3: A&F and B&E.

    If we search the graph for the shortest path for each pairing, we
    get

    ![2b492e768ec9710ab91f175bb5f0e8a3e693fde4.svg](images/2b492e768ec9710ab91f175bb5f0e8a3e693fde4.svg)

    If we duplicate the edges of the paths AB and ECF, we obtain

    ![b6045e4ffe9f10e9163018e5df5921b37634838d.svg](images/b6045e4ffe9f10e9163018e5df5921b37634838d.svg)

    Hence, if we search the graph above for an Eulerian circuit, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{OABDEFCECABCFO}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  If we sum the road length of the Eulerian circuit in part (a), we
    get  $73\text{ km}$
