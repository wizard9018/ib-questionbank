# AI483

**Maximum mark: 12**

The weights of the edges in the complete graph $\bm{G}$ are given in the
following table.

![351d314e638411a639f017a6d0402c542a9bb6df.svg](images/351d314e638411a639f017a6d0402c542a9bb6df.svg)

1.  Starting at vertex A, use the nearest-neighbour algorithm to find an
    upper bound for the travelling salesman problem for graph $\bm{G}$.
    [5 marks]

2.  By first deleting vertex A, use the deleted vertex algorithm
    together with Kruskal's algorithm to find a lower bound for the
    travelling salesman $\text{problem}$ for $\bm{G}$.
    [7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we use the nearest-neighbour algorithm, we get

    ![84ef9811491553a0a944422cf4a202fdf3391025.svg](images/84ef9811491553a0a944422cf4a202fdf3391025.svg)

    Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Upper bound} &= \text{weight of Hamiltonian cycle ABCEDFA} \\[6pt]
    &= 3 + 1 + 2 + 9 + 8 + 7 \\[6pt]
    &= 30
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Deleting the row & column of the table that correspond to vertex A,
    we have

    ![3f0564a52755d4d03ca2473fe582da38819310b7.svg](images/3f0564a52755d4d03ca2473fe582da38819310b7.svg)

    Now using Kruskal's algorithm to find the minimum spanning tree, we
    get

    ![a7de4dc9c18ee3369c54f33831f7bf8b7c7e2ae5.svg](images/a7de4dc9c18ee3369c54f33831f7bf8b7c7e2ae5.svg)

    ![7b49011bfdea861706f328d18d3da6a7ac837838.svg](images/7b49011bfdea861706f328d18d3da6a7ac837838.svg)

    Reconnecting vertex A with the two edges of least weight, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.25em} \text{Lower bound} &= \text{weight of AB \& AD} + \text{weight of min span tree} \\[6pt]
    &= 3 + 5 + (1 + 2 + 4 + 8) \\[6pt]
    &= 23
    \end{aligned}
    $$
