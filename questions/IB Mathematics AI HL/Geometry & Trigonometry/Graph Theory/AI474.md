# AI474

**Maximum mark: 6**

A local government is planning to connect nine nearby mountain villages
(A-I) to a new local airport (O) with roads, as illustrated on the graph
below.

The numbers on the graph edges represent the distance in kilometres
between the villages and airport.

![b57cc46ce260d490ed3846b021b22eb01eae65c2.svg](images/b57cc46ce260d490ed3846b021b22eb01eae65c2.svg)

Every village does not need to be connected directly, however it must be
possible to traverse to the airport from any village.

1.  Determine the most efficient way to connect the villages to the
    airport. [4 marks]

2.  Determine the total length of road the local government will need to
    build. [2 marks]
