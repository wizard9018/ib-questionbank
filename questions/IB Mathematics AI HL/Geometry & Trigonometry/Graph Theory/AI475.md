# AI475

**Maximum mark: 6**

Omni Communications Inc. is planning to connect six cities (A-F) with a
fibre optic network. Due to the high speed of information transfer over
a fibre optic network, each city does not need to be connected directly
to each other.

The numbers on the graph below indicate the cost, in thousands of
dollars, for each possible connection between the cities.

![3c845dccd82424b5013332016a17b76f4aa2aaf5.svg](images/3c845dccd82424b5013332016a17b76f4aa2aaf5.svg)

Determine the network of cables that will minimize the total cost of
connecting these six cities to the fibre optic network, and the
associated cost.
