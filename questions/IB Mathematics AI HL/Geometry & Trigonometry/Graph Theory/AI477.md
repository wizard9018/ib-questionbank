# AI477

**Maximum mark: 6**

A postman starts at the Post Office located at O and needs to travel
along every road illustrated by the edges in the graph below. The
numbers on the graph represent distances in kilometres.

![6807521ee6f303e3e71640a6461ec0a0f4553447.svg](images/6807521ee6f303e3e71640a6461ec0a0f4553447.svg)

1.  Find a solution to the Chinese Postman Problem for the graph.
    [4 marks]

2.  Write down the length, in kilometres, of an optimal Chinese postman
    route. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The odd vertices are B and E.
    There is only one way of pairing them together: B&E.

    If we search the graph for the shortest path between B&E, we get

    ![b257929b1aaad8097edbfd2cd1cc169d9133f62b.svg](images/b257929b1aaad8097edbfd2cd1cc169d9133f62b.svg)

    If we duplicate the edges of the path BCE, we obtain

    ![75c58424bbf331ead351deb653fad9c6961fdba4.svg](images/75c58424bbf331ead351deb653fad9c6961fdba4.svg)

    Hence, if we search the graph above for an Eulerian circuit, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{OABCDEOBCECADO}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  If we sum the road length of the Eulerian circuit in part (a), we
    get  $71\text{ km}$
