# AI478

**Maximum mark: 6**

Peter is a salesman who works for a skin care products company. He
travels to a nearby city to find new customers. He researches six beauty
salons on Yelp and pins their locations on Google Map.

The numbers on the graph below indicate the travel times in minutes
between Peter's hotel (at A) and the beauty salons (B-G).

Peter is currently located at his hotel and wants to visit each beauty
salon once, before returning to the hotel.

![03eccc51b0bedb43cf6c315c4959bdd71b39011e.svg](images/03eccc51b0bedb43cf6c315c4959bdd71b39011e.svg)

1.  Write down the term which is used to describe this type of route.
    [1 mark]

Peter decides to visit beauty salon E first.

2.  Find the route that he can take. [3 marks]

3.  Calculate the total travel time, in hours and minutes, for
    his route. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\text{Hamiltonian cycle}$

2.  If we search the graph for a possible route that Peter can take, we
    find

    ![0fe7eb945f41dcd187873182eee16290a97d3001.svg](images/0fe7eb945f41dcd187873182eee16290a97d3001.svg)

    Hence
     $\text{AEDCBGFA}$
    is the only route that Peter can take if he visits beauty

    salon E first.

3.  If we add weights of the edges of the cycle AEDCBGFA, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{total travel time} &= 19 + 25 + 16 + 20 + 32 + 40 + 33 \\[6pt]
    &= 185 \\[6pt]
    &= 3 \text{ hours } 5 \text{ minutes}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
