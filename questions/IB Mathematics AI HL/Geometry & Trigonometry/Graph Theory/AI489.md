# AI489

**Maximum mark: 6**

The complete graph $\bm{K}$ has the following weighted adjacency table.

![1b50e022f65e5b2cf435e9b6936ad532cbac65f0.svg](images/1b50e022f65e5b2cf435e9b6936ad532cbac65f0.svg)

Consider the travelling salesman problem for $\bm{K}$.

1.  By first finding a minimum spanning tree on the subgraph of $\bm{K}$
    formed by deleting vertex A and all edges
    connected to A, find a lower bound to this
    problem. [4 marks]

2.  Find the total weight of the cycle ABCEDA. [1 mark]

3.  State a conclusion from your results found in part (a) and part (b).
    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Deleting the row & column of the table that correspond to vertex A,
    we have

    ![227c59913e865d11d23f31df77b67fac0ee73c03.svg](images/227c59913e865d11d23f31df77b67fac0ee73c03.svg)

    Hence, using Kruskal's algorithm, we get

    ![5a7cdffc22f2a448c27bfc20bf2bbcf7f4fe5a0c.svg](images/5a7cdffc22f2a448c27bfc20bf2bbcf7f4fe5a0c.svg)

    ![8623b7d7e72947389e1bf5ee36ca23efae389c9c.svg](images/8623b7d7e72947389e1bf5ee36ca23efae389c9c.svg)

    Reconnecting vertex A with the two edges of least weight, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.25em} \text{Lower bound} &= \text{weight of AB \& AD} + \text{weight of min span tree} \\[6pt]
    &= (14 + 16) + (11 + 13 + 15) \\[6pt]
    &= 69
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{weight of ABCEDA} &= 14 + 11 + 13 + 15 + 16 \\[6pt]
    &= 69
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  :::answer
    ABCEDA is a Hamiltonian cycle of $\bm{K}$ of least weight. ABCEDA is a solution to the travelling salesman problem for $\bm{K}$.
