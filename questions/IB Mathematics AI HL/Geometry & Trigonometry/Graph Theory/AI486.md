# AI486

**Maximum mark: 10**

The weighted graph $\bm{H}$, representing the travelling costs between
five customers, has the following weighted adjacency table.

![6f5a17dc5b9121870dac2663e58c9e6139ab63c4.svg](images/6f5a17dc5b9121870dac2663e58c9e6139ab63c4.svg)

1.  Draw a possible graph of $\bm{H}$. [2 marks]

2.  Starting from customer E, use the nearest-neighbour algorithm to
    determine an upper bound to the travelling salesman problem for
    $\bm{H}$. [4 marks]

3.  By removing customer A, use the method of vertex deletion to
    determine a lower bound to the travelling salesman problem for
    $\bm{H}$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  A potential graph of $\bm{H}$ is shown

    ![390ab0a508f5cbd7964485a8a1c1592779eed536.svg](images/390ab0a508f5cbd7964485a8a1c1592779eed536.svg)

2.  If we use the nearest-neighbour algorithm, we get

    ![0c8f56b942cddb8e8b66deca40fffb8b65e89f5d.svg](images/0c8f56b942cddb8e8b66deca40fffb8b65e89f5d.svg)

    Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.5em} \text{Upper bound} &= \text{weight of Hamiltonian cycle ECDABE} \\[6pt]
    &= 7 + 3 + 4 + 2 + 9 \\[6pt]
    &= 25
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Deleting the row & column of the table that correspond to vertex A,
    we get

    ![b9588a056f32f9472712f9de571bdfa372f093d7.svg](images/b9588a056f32f9472712f9de571bdfa372f093d7.svg)

    Using Kruskal's algorithm, we obtain

    ![497779562de814bdc506c14d6736527a1428d62f.svg](images/497779562de814bdc506c14d6736527a1428d62f.svg)

    ![81dcb850c0bf9468dfa26fe1f2290a6d8cd4a171.svg](images/81dcb850c0bf9468dfa26fe1f2290a6d8cd4a171.svg)

    Reconnecting vertex A with the two edges of least weight, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.25em} \text{Lower bound} &= \text{weight of AB \& AD} + \text{weight of min span tree} \\[6pt]
    &= (2 + 4) + (3 + 6 + 7) \\[6pt]
    &= 22
    \end{aligned}
    $$
