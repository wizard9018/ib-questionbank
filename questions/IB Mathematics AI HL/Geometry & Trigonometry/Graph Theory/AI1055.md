# AI1055

**Maximum mark: 5**

Darwin is at a theme park that has five attractions. The graph below shows the location of the main gate, the attractions, and the walking paths that exist between them. Darwin is currently at the main gate, point A, and is planning to meet his friend Diogo at the Ferris Wheel, located at point F.

![ai1055a](images/ai1055a.PNG)

1. Complete the adjacency matrix, $\bm{M}$, for the theme park graph.[2 marks]

    ![ai1055b](images/ai1055b.PNG)

2. Determine the number of different ways that Darwin can walk from the main gate to the Ferris Wheel using a maximum of 3 paths.[3 marks]
