# AI1153

**Maximum mark: 7**

The graph below shows the roads connecting $5$ small towns.

![q1153](images/q1153.png)

1.  Construct the adjacency matrix $\bm{M}$ for this network. [3 marks]

2.  Evaluate $\bm{M}+\bm{M^2}$. [1 mark]

Hueng-min lives in one of the towns and works in a different one. Every weekday morning he drives from his home to work and then in the evening back to his home.

When driving to work Hueng-min will use **at most** $2$ different roads. Similarly when driving home he will use **at most** $2$ different roads.

When driving to work Hueng-min has exactly $3$ different routes he can take.

3.  Hence, identify the towns Hueng-min could live in. [1 mark]

It is known that Hueng-min works in town $B$.

4.  1.  Identify which town he lives in.

    2.  Hence, identify all of the routes Hueng-min can take to drive home in the evening. [2 marks]
