# AI760

**Maximum mark: 10**

An insurance salesman has four meetings scheduled with some prospective clients (A, B, C and D), and is planning the order in which to visit them, starting from his home at E and returning there afterwards.

The distances between each home, in km, are shown in the following weighted adjacency table. Each distance is a positive integer.

![grapha](images/grapha.JPG)

1. Starting from E, use the nearest neighbour algorithm to determine an upper bound for the salesman's journey, in terms of $x$. [3 marks]

2. 1. By removing client D, determine a minimum spanning tree for the remaining subgraph. Use Prim's algorithm, starting from vertex E.

    2. Hence use the deleted vertex algorithm to find two possible expressions for the lower bound.[4 marks]

3. Given that the lower bound is exactly $1\,\text{km}$ less than the upper bound, find the range of possible values for $x$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The order in which we must choose the edges is as follows.

    - We must first choose the edge of least weight connected to vertex E. This is edge AE, which has a weight of 6.

    - From A, we must choose edge AC, which has a weight of 3.

    - From C, we must choose edge CD, which has a weight of 4.

    - From D, we must choose edge BD, which has a weight of $x$.

    - From B, we must choose edge BE, which has a weight of 9.

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{(M2)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Therefore

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Upper Bound} &= 6 + 3 + 4 + x + 9 \\[8pt]
    &= \hspace{0.525em}\boxed{(22+x)\,\text{km}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. 1. According to Prim's algorithm, we first select the edge of least weight. Starting from vertex E, we select edge AE, which has a weight of 6.

        We now select the next edge of least weight that is incident to the tree (i.e. it must connect to vertex A or vertex E). We therefore select edge AC, which has a weight of 3.

        We now only need to connect vertex B in order to complete our MST. The edge of least weight is AB, which has a weight of 8.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Our minimum spanning tree is therefore

        ![graphb](images/graphb.JPG)

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2. We must now reconnect vertex D to the MST using the two edges of least weight. We have 4 options:

        - AD - weight 7
        - BD - weight $x$
        - CD - weight 4
        - DE - weight 8

        Edge CD will definitely be included and edge DE will definitely be excluded.

        If $x < 7$, then edge BD will be included.

        If $x > 7$, then edge AD will be included.

        If $x=7$, then either AD or BD can be included.

        Hence our first lower bound is $6+8+3+4+7=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{28\,\text{km}}\,\,$ (if $x \geq 7$).
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Our second lower bound is $6+8+3+4+x=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{(21+x)\,\text{km}}\,\,$ (if $x < 7$).
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3. We have our upper bound as $(22+x)\,\text{km}$. We now consider separately our two possible lower bounds, given that they must be exactly $1\,\text{km}$ less than the upper bound.

    Firstly, we have a lower bound of $28\,\text{km}$ if $x \geq 7$. In this case the lower bound will only be 1 less than the upper bound if the upper bound is $29\,\text{km}$. In this case we must have $x=7$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Secondly, we have a lower bound of $(21+x)\,\text{km}$ if $x < 7$.

    $21 + x$ will always be exactly one less than $22 + x$ hence this holds for all valid values of $x$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We were given initially that $x$ is a positive integer, hence our range of possible values for $x$ is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\boxed{1 \leq x \leq 7\,,\,x \in \mathbb{Z}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
