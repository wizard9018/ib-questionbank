# AI492

**Maximum mark: 6**

George, an experienced civil engineer, is designing a high-speed railway
network to connect seven cities (A-G), as illustrated in the graph
below. The possible railways and costs of building them are represented
by the graph edges, where costs are in billions of dollars.

![60e1dd78ce3b74b4ebbf4719f6ece9e7c7dfed9a.svg](images/60e1dd78ce3b74b4ebbf4719f6ece9e7c7dfed9a.svg)

George needs to design the lowest cost high-speed railway network that
will connect the seven cities. Each city does not need to directly
connect with all other cities, however each city must have a connection
to the rail network.

1.  Name an algorithm which will allow George to find the lowest cost
    high-speed railway network. [1 mark]

2.
    1.  Find and list the railway connections of the lowest cost
        high-speed railway network.

    2.  Hence, write down the cost of building this network. [5 marks]
