# AI252

**Maximum mark: 6**

The quadrilateral ABCD has side lengths AB
$= 17$ cm, BC $= 12$ cm,
and CD $= 11$ cm. The size of angle
$\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{D}}\mathrm{C}$ is $\ang{105}$ and the size of
angle $\mathrm{C}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{D}$ is $\ang{25}$.

![a39940c9c9074118b0cb97e73ce025287a1e34da.svg](images/a39940c9c9074118b0cb97e73ce025287a1e34da.svg)

1.  Find the length of AC in centimetres. [3 marks]

2.  Find the size of angle $\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C}$.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the sine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac {\text{AC}} {\sin(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{D}}\mathrm{C})} &= \dfrac {\text{DC}}
    {\sin (\mathrm{C}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{D})}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \dfrac {\text{AC}} {\sin(\ang{105})} & = \dfrac {11} {\sin(\ang{25})}\hspace{2cm}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \text{AC}   & = \dfrac {11 \sin(\ang{105})} {\sin(\ang{25})}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &\approx  25.1\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{AC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{BC}^2 - 2 (\text{AB})(\text{BC})\cos(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C})\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    25.1^2  & = 17^2 + 12^2 - 2(17)(12) \cos(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C})\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    \cos(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C})  &  = \dfrac {17^2 + 12^2 - 25.1^2} {2(17)(12)}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    & \approx  -0.483\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    \mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C} & = \cos^{-1}(-0.483)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    & \approx  \ang{119}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{6.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
