# AI253

**Maximum mark: 6**

The diagram below shows quadrilateral ABCD with side lengths of AD
$= 12$ m, DC $= 7$ m and
BC $= 5$ m. Angle
$\mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{D}= \ang{50}$ and angle
$\mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{C}}\mathrm{D}= \ang{100}$.

![adc3c8b854d650925a4d1529c42f7b19e72aab2d.svg](images/adc3c8b854d650925a4d1529c42f7b19e72aab2d.svg)

A new line is drawn from B to D direct.

1.  Calculate the length of this new line, BD. [3 marks]

2.  Calculate the size of angle $\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{D}$.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{BD}^2 &= \mathrm{CD}^2+\mathrm{BC}^2-2(\mathrm{CD})(\mathrm{BC})\cos(\mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{C}}\mathrm{D})\rule[-2mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
    \text{BD}^2     & = 7^2 + 5^2 - 2(7)(5)\cos(\ang{100})\rule[-2mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
    \text{BD}       & = \sqrt{7^2 + 5^2 - 2(7)(5)\cos(\ang{100})}\rule[-2mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
    & \hspace{.275em}\approx  9.28\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the sine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac {\text{AD}}{\sin(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{D})} &= \dfrac {\text{BD}}{\sin(\mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{D})}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \dfrac {12}{\sin(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{D})}     & = \dfrac {9.28}{\sin(\ang{50})}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \sin(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{D})  & = \dfrac {12 \sin(\ang{50})}{9.28}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & \approx  0.9906 \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{D}    & \approx \sin^{-1}(0.9906)\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &\hspace{.275em} \approx  \ang{82.1}      \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
