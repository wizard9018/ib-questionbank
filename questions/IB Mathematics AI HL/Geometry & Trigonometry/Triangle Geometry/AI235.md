# AI235

**Maximum mark: 6**

Daniela (D) stands near the window on the second floor of the six-story
apartment building and can see Andrea (A), who is
$14$ m from the base of the building. The angle
of depression from Daniela to Andrea is $\ang{20}$.

![b3ba81d67276c1e138ce6b6fc346ca2c4fb7ab1a.svg](images/b3ba81d67276c1e138ce6b6fc346ca2c4fb7ab1a.svg)

1.  Calculate the distance from Daniela to Andrea, AD. [2 marks]

Clara (C) is standing at the window of the sixth floor, three times
higher from the ground than Daniela.

2.  Calculate the angle of depression from C to A. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's consider the right-angled triangle ABD, shown in the diagram.

    ![2413bc7b0bb209cc72be6457d9994bcad8968cd8.svg](images/2413bc7b0bb209cc72be6457d9994bcad8968cd8.svg)

    The angle BAD is equal to the angle of depression from Daniela to
    Andrea. Hence, $\textrm{B}\hat{\textrm{A}}\textrm{D} = \ang{20}$.
    Using the definition of the cosine, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos(\textrm{B}\hat{\textrm{A}}\textrm{D}) &= \dfrac{\textrm{AB}}{\textrm{AD}}\\[5pt]
    \cos (\ang{20}) & = \dfrac {14} {\textrm{AD}} \\[6pt]
    AD  & = \dfrac {14}{\cos (\ang{20})}\\[8pt]
    &\approx 14.9 \hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  First, we find BD.

    ![bc6bbcfb425c54d979aea5631ce8dd476c468aba.svg](images/bc6bbcfb425c54d979aea5631ce8dd476c468aba.svg)

    Applying Pythagorean theorem to the triangle ABD, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{13mm}\textrm{AD}^2  & = \textrm{AB}^2 + \textrm{BD}^2\\[6pt]
    \textrm{BD}^2   & = 14.9^2 - 14^2\\[6pt]
    \textrm{BD}         & = 5.1\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm{BC} &= 3\textrm{BD} \\[3pt]
    &= 3(5.1) \\[3pt]
    &= 15.3\hspace{0.25em}\textrm{m}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    By the
    definition of the tangent we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{.5mm}\tan (\textrm{A}\hat{\textrm{C}}\textrm    {B}) & = \dfrac {\textrm{AB}} {\textrm{BC}}\\[2pt]
    & = \dfrac {14} {15.3}\\[2pt]
    \textrm{A}\hat{\textrm{C}}\textrm{B}    & = \tan^{-1} \left(\dfrac {14} {15.3}\right)\\[4pt]
    & = \ang{42.5}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the angle of depression
    $\alpha$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8mm}\alpha  & = \ang{90} - \ang{42.5} \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{47.5}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
