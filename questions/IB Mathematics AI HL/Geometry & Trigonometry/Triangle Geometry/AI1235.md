# AI1235

**Maximum mark: 21**

A school is building a new chicken house in their eco-garden. The structure has a triangular prism-shaped roof, built from sustainable materials. The roof has a base width of 3.6 metres, and a base length of 5.5 metres. This is shown in the following diagram.

![ai1235aa](images/ai1235aa.png)

The triangular cross-section is not isosceles, as one side is extended to accommodate solar panels which need a minimum width of 2.1 metres. The vertical height of the roof is shown by [CD]. The longer sloped side, [BC], is 2.2 metres long, to fit the solar panels, and B$\hat{\text{A}}$C is $32.7^\circ$. This is shown in the following diagram.

![ai1235bb](images/ai1235bb.png)

1. Using the cosine rule, calculate the length of the shorter sloped side, [AC], giving your answer to $3$ significant figures. [3 marks]

2. Calculate the vertical height of the roof, [CD].[2 marks]

The triangular cross-section of the roof, as shown in diagram, will be painted once the chicken house is built. The cost of painting is \$18.50 per square metre.

3. 1. Find the area of the triangular cross-section.

    2. Hence, calculate the total cost of painting the front triangular cross-section.[4 marks]

4. Calculate the total surface area of the two sloped roof panels (the rectangular panels along the length). Give your answer to the nearest square metre.[4 marks]

5. Find the volume of space enclosed by the roof. Give your answer to the nearest cubic metre.[2 marks]

For the solar panels to fit, one side of the roof is built longer than the other. However, the builder can only construct the roof panels at angles between $30^\circ$ and $50^\circ$.

6. Determine whether both roof angles, B$\hat{\text{A}}$C and A$\hat{\text{B}}$C in the triangular cross-section, can be constructed by the builder. Justify your answer.[3 marks]

Alexander believes the side BC needs to be reduced by $5\,\text{cm}$ from $2.2\,\text{m}$ to $2.15\,\text{m}$ so that all requirements to fit the solar panels are met. This can be achieved by reducing the base width of the roof so that the roof height, CD, doesn't change.

His classmate Coady disagrees and says that BC needs to be increased by $5\,\text{cm}$ to $2.25\,\text{m}$, which can be achieved by increasing the base width of the roof so that the roof height, CD, doesn't change.

7. Determine if Alexander or Coady is correct. Justify your answer.[3 marks]
