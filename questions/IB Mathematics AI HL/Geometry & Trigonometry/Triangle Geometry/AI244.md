# AI244

**Maximum mark: 15**

The quadrilateral ABCD is shown below, where AB
$= 56$ m, AD $=122$ m,
CD $=40$ m, and the angles
$\mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{D}$ and
$\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{D}}\mathrm{C}$ are $\ang{68}$ and $\ang{80}$
respectively.

![9ac9c49d04ad3013029a2ec78343fd814fb2d6eb.svg](images/9ac9c49d04ad3013029a2ec78343fd814fb2d6eb.svg)

1.  Find the length of line AC. [3 marks]

2.  Find the area of triangle ACD. [3 marks]

3.  Find the angle $\mathrm{D}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{C}$ . Give your
    answer in degrees correct to one\
    decimal place. [3 marks]

4.  Find the shortest distance between point B and the line AC.
    [2 marks]

5.  Find the length of the perimeter of triangle ABC. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm{AC}^2 &= \mathrm{AD}^2 + \mathrm{CD}^2 - 2(\mathrm{AD})(\mathrm{CD})\cos(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{D}}\mathrm{C}) \\[5pt]
    & = 122^2 + 40^2 - 2(122)(40)\cos (\ang{80})\\[8pt]
    & \hspace{-5.5mm}\textrm{AC} \approx 122\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac{1}{2} (\mathrm{AD})(\mathrm{CD}) \sin(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{D}}\mathrm{C})\\[6pt]
    & = \dfrac 1 2 (122)(40) \sin(\ang{80})\\[11pt]
    & \approx 2\hspace{0.25em}400\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{CD}^2 &= \mathrm{AC}^2+\mathrm{AD}^2-2(\mathrm{AC})(\mathrm{AD})\cos(\mathrm{D}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{C})\\[9pt]
    40^2 &= 122^2+122^2-2(122)(122)\cos(\mathrm{D}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{C})\\[5pt]
    \cos (\mathrm{D}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{C})     & = \dfrac {122^2 + 122^2 - 40^2} {2(122)(122)}\\[4pt]
    \mathrm{D}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{C}& = \cos^{-1}(0.946)\\[12pt]
    & \approx \ang{18.9}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The shortest distance to B on line AC will lie perpendicular to B.
    Let's call this point H. Let's now consider the triangle ABH

    ![9cb1584bba8ded07c5e002352c81a1b4bc9677f5.svg](images/9cb1584bba8ded07c5e002352c81a1b4bc9677f5.svg)

    Following on from part (c) we can find angle
    $\mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{H}$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{H} & = \mathrm{D}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{B} - \mathrm{D}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{C}\\[6pt]
    & = \ang{68} - \ang{18.9}\\[6pt]
    & = \ang{49.1}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Since ABH is a right angled
    triangle, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \sin(\mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{H}) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{BH}}{\mathrm{AB}}\\[4pt]
    \sin(\ang{49.1})    &  = \dfrac {\textrm{BH}} {56}\\[10pt]
    \textrm{BH} & = 56 \sin(\ang{49.1}) \\[15pt]
    &\approx 42.3\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Using the cosine rule to find BC, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{BC}^2 &= \mathrm{AB}^2+\mathrm{AC}^2 -2 (\mathrm{AB})(\mathrm{AC})\cos(\mathrm{B}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{H})\\[6pt]
    \textrm{BC}^2   & = 56^2 + 122^2 - 2(56)(122)\cos (\ang{49.1})\\[8pt]
    \textrm{BC}     & \approx 95.3\,\mbox m \qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The perimeter of ABC can be found by summing AB, BC and AC:

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    p   & = 56 + 95.3 + 122\\[5pt]
    &\approx 273\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m
    \end{aligned}
    $$
