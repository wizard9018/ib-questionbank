# AI236

**Maximum mark: 5**

From a sailboat moored at sea, the angle of elevation to the top of a
$20$ m high cliff is $\ang{22}$. On the cliff
top there is a coconut tree. The angle of elevation to the coconut tree
is $\ang{29}$.

1.  Draw a diagram representing the situation. [2 marks]

2.  Find the height of the coconut tree. [3 marks]

2.  First, we find the distance from the boat to the cliff bottom (BO).
    Using the definition of the tangent, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tan(\textrm{C}\hat{\textrm{B}}\textrm{O}) &= \dfrac{\textrm{CO}}{\textrm{BO}} \\[3pt]
    \tan (\ang{22}) &  = \dfrac {20} {\textrm{BO}}\hspace{1.38cm}\\[5pt]
    \textrm{BO} & = \dfrac {20} {\tan (\ang{22})}\\[6pt]
    & \approx 49.5\hspace{0.25em} \mbox{m}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Next,
    using the definition of the tangent of
    $\textrm{T}\hat{\textrm{B}}\textrm{O}$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tan(\textrm{T}\hat{\textrm{B}}\textrm{O})&= \dfrac{\mathrm{TO}}{\mathrm{BO}} \\[2pt]
    \tan (\ang{29}) &  = \dfrac {\textrm{TO}} {49.5}\\[7pt]
    \textrm{TO}     & = 49.5  \tan (\ang{29})\\[9pt]
    & \approx 27.4\hspace{0.25em} \mbox{m}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence,
    the hight of the coconut tree, TC, is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.35mm}\textrm{TC}  &  = \textrm{TO} - \textrm{CO}\\[6pt]
    & = 27.4 - 20\\[6pt]
    & =7.4 \hspace{0.25em} \mbox{m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
