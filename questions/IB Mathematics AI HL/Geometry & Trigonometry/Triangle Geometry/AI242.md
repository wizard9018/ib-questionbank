# AI242

**Maximum mark: 6**

Triangle Island is a small island located off the north-western tip of
British Columbia, Canada. Two side lengths of Triangle Island are equal
to $1600$ and $1800$ metres and the angle between these two sides is
equal to $\ang{78}$.

![d3642e090b1ba3b74f787c051ac0689334753283.svg](images/d3642e090b1ba3b74f787c051ac0689334753283.svg)

1.  Calculate the length of the third side of Triangle island.
    [3 marks]

2.  Find the area of a Triangle Island in m$^2$. Give your answer in the
    form $a\times 10^k$, where $1 \leq a < 10$ and $k \in \mathbb Z$.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm{BC}^2 &= \textrm{AC}^2+\textrm{AB}^2-2(\textrm{AC})(\textrm{AB})\cos(\mathrm{C}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{B}) \\[5pt]
    \textrm{BC}^2   & = 1\hspace{0.25em}800^2 + 1\hspace{0.25em}600^2 - 2(1\hspace{0.25em}800)(1\hspace{0.25em}600)\cos(\ang{78})\\[5pt]
    \textrm{BC}^2   & = 4\hspace{0.25em}602\hspace{0.25em}428.66\\[9pt]
    &\hspace{-5mm}\textrm{BC} \approx 2\hspace{0.25em}150\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m
    \qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the formula for the area of a triangle, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac 1 2 (\mathrm{AC})(\mathrm{AB}) \sin(\mathrm{C}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{B})\\[1pt]
    A & = \dfrac 1 2 (1\hspace{0.25em}800)(1\hspace{0.25em}600)\sin (\ang{78})\hspace{1cm}\\[6pt]
    & = 1\hspace{0.25em}408\hspace{0.25em}533\\[11pt]
    & \approx 1\hspace{0.25em}410\hspace{0.25em}000\\[10pt]
    & = 1.41\times10^6\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
