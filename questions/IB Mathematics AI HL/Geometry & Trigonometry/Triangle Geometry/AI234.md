# AI234

**Maximum mark: 6**

BT represents the height of a skyscraper, with its base at B and top at
T. Jack has an apartment on the $32$nd floor at C, three fifths of the
way to the top of the building.

![b832a6db273a071310c61198869f97c5a0de4f09.svg](images/b832a6db273a071310c61198869f97c5a0de4f09.svg)

From a point A, on horizontal ground $200$ m
from B, the angle of elevation to C is $\ang{38}$.

1.  Calculate BC, the height of Jack's apartment above the ground.
    [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the angle of depression from T to A. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the definition of the tangent, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tan(\ang{38})  & = \dfrac {\textrm{BC}} {200} \\[6pt]
    \textrm{BC} & = 200 \tan(\ang{38})\\[11pt]
    &\approx 156\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The angle of depression from T to A is $\beta$, shown in the diagram
    below.

    ![46371d013970152b99d90907b011f94c2f9033be.svg](images/46371d013970152b99d90907b011f94c2f9033be.svg)

    First, let's find the length of BT. The $32$nd floor (BC) is three
    fifths of the building height (BT). Hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm{BC} &= \dfrac{3}{5} \textrm{BT} \\[3pt]
    \textrm{BT} & = \dfrac 5 3 (156)\\[6pt]
    & = 260\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Next, consider the right-angled triangle ABT. Utilizing the
    definition of the tangent we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7mm}\tan \alpha &= \dfrac{\textrm{AB}}{\textrm{BT}}\\[4pt]
    \tan \alpha & = \dfrac {200}{260} \\[3pt]
    \alpha      & = \tan^{-1} \left(\dfrac {200}{260}\right)\\[8pt]
    & \approx \ang{37.6}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8mm}\beta & = \ang{90} - \alpha \\[6pt]
    & = \ang{90} - \ang{37.6}\\[6pt]
    &\approx \ang{52.4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
