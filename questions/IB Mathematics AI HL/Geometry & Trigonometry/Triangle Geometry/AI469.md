# AI469

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $\cos \theta = - \dfrac{5}{6}$, for $\dfrac{\pi}{2} < \theta < \pi$.
Find the exact value of:

1.  $\sin \theta$; [3 marks]

2.  $\tan \theta$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we draw a unit circle, the angle $\theta$ & the value of
    $\cos \theta$ on the $x$-axis, we have

    ![bc2cc2cbb7066ea9f9eab4feabbf10d917a6fcd4.svg](images/bc2cc2cbb7066ea9f9eab4feabbf10d917a6fcd4.svg)

    Hence the value of $\sin \theta$ is positive.

    Using the Pythagorean identity, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos^2 \theta + \sin^2 \theta &= 1 \\[9pt]
    \bigg(\hspace{-0.25em}-\dfrac{5}{6}\bigg)^2\hspace{-0.15em} + \sin^2 \theta &= 1 \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{25}{36} + \sin^2 \theta &= 1 \\[9pt]
    \sin^2 \theta &= \dfrac{11}{36} \hspace{5em} \\[8pt]
    \sin \theta &= \pm \dfrac{\sqrt{11}}{6} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{10.75em} \sin \theta = \dfrac{\sqrt{11}}{6} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\sin \theta > 0$}]
    $$

2.  Using the tangent identity, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6em} \tan \theta &= \dfrac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} \\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{\sqrt{11}/6}{(-5/6)} \hspace{3em} [\text{from part (a)}] \\[9pt]
    &= -\dfrac{\sqrt{11}}{5}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
