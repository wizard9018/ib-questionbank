# AI466

**Maximum mark: 6**

In a triangle ABC, $\text{AB} = 12$ cm and
$\text{AC} = 15$ cm. The area of the
triangle is $\text{\(80\)\hspace{0.25em}cm\(^2\)}$.

1.  Find the two possible values for angle BÂC. [3 marks]

2.  Given that BÂC is obtuse, find the length of side BC. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(\text{AC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \hspace{3.25em} \\[6pt]
    80 &= \dfrac{1}{2}(12)(15) \sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{3.6em} \text{B\^{A}C} \approx \ang{62.7},\hspace{0.25em} \ang{117} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

2.  Using the cosine rule with BÂC equal to 117°, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.2em} \text{BC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{AC}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{AC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6pt]
    \text{BC} &= \sqrt{12^2 + 15^2 - 2(12)(15)\cos\ang{117}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{BC} \approx 23.1 \text{ cm} \hspace{7.6em}
    $$
