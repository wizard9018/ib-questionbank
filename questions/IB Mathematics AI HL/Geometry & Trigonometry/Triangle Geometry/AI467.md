# AI467

**Maximum mark: 6**

A triangle ABC has side lengths $a = 10.2$ cm,
$b= 17.5$ cm and an area of
$32$ cm$^2$. Find the largest possible perimeter
of triangle ABC.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the area of a triangle formula, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{8.4em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(a)(b)\sin\hspace{-0.05em}\text{\^{C}} \\[6pt]
32 &= \dfrac{1}{2}(10.2)(17.5)\sin\hspace{-0.05em}\text{\^{C}} \\[10pt]
\text{\^{C}} &\approx \ang{21.0},\hspace{0.25em} \ang{159} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, using the cosine rule, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2em} c^2 &= a^2 + b^2 - 2(a)(b)\cos\hspace{-0.05em}\text{\^{C}} \\[6.5pt]
c &= \sqrt{(10.2)^2 + (17.5)^2 - 2(10.2)(17.5)\cos\hspace{-0.05em}\smash{\raisebox{-0.05em}{$\text{\^{C}}$}}} \\[8pt]
c &\approx 8.78\text{ cm},\hspace{0.25em} 27.3\text{ cm} \hspace{3em} [\text{for $\text{\^{C}} \approx \ang{21.0},\hspace{0.25em} \ang{159}$}]
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence the (largest possible) perimeter of triangle ABC is

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1em} P_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= a + b + c_{\text{max}} \\[6pt]
&= 10.2 + 17.5 + 27.3 \\[6pt]
&= 55 \text{ cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
