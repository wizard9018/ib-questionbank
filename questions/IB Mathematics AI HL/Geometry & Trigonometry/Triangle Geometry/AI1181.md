# AI1181

**Maximum mark: 7**

Olivia is attempting to fly two drones at the same time on horizontal ground.

From her location on the ground, the first drone, $D_1$, is 5 metres due west and 13 metres away from her in a straight line.

The second drone, $D_2$, is due east from Olivia's position, 6 metres above the ground and at an angle of elevation of $50^{\circ}$.

This information is shown in the following diagram.

![1181a](images/1181a.JPG)

1. Find the angle of elevation from Olivia's position to drone 1.     [2 marks]

2. Find the distance from  Olivia's position to drone 2.     [2 marks]

3. Hence find the distance between the two drones.     [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using the cosine ratio in the right-angled triangle shown below, we can find the angle of elevation, labelled $\theta$:

    ![1181b](images/1181b.JPG)

    We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \cos \theta &= \dfrac{5}{13} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    \theta &= \cos ^{-1}\left(\dfrac{5}{13}\right) \\[6pt]

    &= 67.38... \\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-0.8em}\boxed{\theta \approx 67.4^{\circ}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Using the sine ratio in the right-angled triangle shown below, we can find the length $\mathrm{O}D_2$:

    ![1181c](images/1181c.JPG)

    We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \sin 50^{\circ} &= \dfrac{6}{\mathrm{O}D_2} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    \mathrm{O}D_2 &= \dfrac{6}{\sin 50^{\circ}} \\[6pt]

    &= 7.832... \\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-2.3em}\boxed{\mathrm{O}D_2 \approx 7.83 \text{ metres}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. The following diagram shows the information we were given, plus our answers from parts (a) and (b). We have also calculated the angle $\angle{D_1\mathrm{O}D_2}$, by using the fact that angles on a straight line must add up to $180^{\circ}$:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    67.38... + \angle{D_1\mathrm{O}D_2} + 50 &= 180 \\[6pt]

    \angle{D_1\mathrm{O}D_2} &= 62.6...^{\circ} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    ![1181d](images/1181d.JPG)

    We have enough information to use the cosine rule to find length $D_1D_2$. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \left(D_1D_2\right)^2 &= 13^2 + 7.832...^2 - 2(13)(7.832...)\,\cos 62.6...^{\circ} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    D_1D_2 &= \sqrt{13^2 + 7.832...^2 - 2(13)(7.832...)\,\cos 62.6...^{\circ}} \\[6pt]

    &= 11.69... 

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    $$\boxed{\text{The distance between the drones is approximately $11.7$ metres.}}$$ 

    $$                    

    \begin{aligned}            

    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}    

    \end{aligned}

    $$
