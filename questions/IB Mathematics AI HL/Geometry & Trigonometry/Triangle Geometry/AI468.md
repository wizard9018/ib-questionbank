# AI468

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $\sin \theta = - \dfrac{3}{5}$, for
$\pi < \theta < \dfrac{3\pi}{2}$. Find the exact value of:

1.  $\cos \theta$; [3 marks]

2.  $\tan \theta$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we draw a unit circle, the angle $\theta$ & the value of
    $\sin \theta$ on the $y$-axis, we have

    ![b3becd4517266b3d3cf813c644fe65370fa96562.svg](images/b3becd4517266b3d3cf813c644fe65370fa96562.svg)

    Hence the value of $\cos \theta$ is negative.

    Using the Pythagorean identity, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos^2 \theta + \sin^2 \theta &= 1 \\[9pt]
    \cos^2 \theta + \hspace{-0.15em}\bigg(\hspace{-0.25em}-\dfrac{3}{5}\bigg)^2\hspace{-0.15em} &= 1 \\[6pt]
    \cos^2 \theta + \dfrac{9}{25} &= 1 \\[9pt]
    \cos^2 \theta &= \dfrac{16}{25} \hspace{5em} \\[9pt]
    \cos \theta &= \pm \dfrac{4}{5} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{10.15em} \cos \theta = - \dfrac{4}{5} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\cos \theta < 0$}]
    $$

2.  Using the tangent identity, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6em} \tan \theta &= \dfrac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} \\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{(-3/5)}{(-4/5)} \hspace{3em} [\text{from part (a)}] \\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{3}{4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
