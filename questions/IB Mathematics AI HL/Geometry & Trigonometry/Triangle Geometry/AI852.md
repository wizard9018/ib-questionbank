# AI852

**Maximum mark: 8**

Bus-1 and Bus-2 leave a terminal, T, at the same time. Bus-1 travels
$65 \text{ km}$ on a true bearing of $\ang{140}$ to point U, then a
further $40 \text{ km}$ on a true bearing $\ang{235}$ to point V. Bus-2
travels directly from the terminal to point V.

![56d80536a77436a60b7d4fee4deee77d34df7e3c.svg](images/56d80536a77436a60b7d4fee4deee77d34df7e3c.svg)

1.  Find the distance, $d$, travelled by Bus-2. [3 marks]

2.  Find the true bearing on which Bus-2 travels. [2 marks]

Bus-1 averages a speed of $55 \text{ km/h}$ and Bus-2 averages a speed
of $36 \text{ km/h}$.

3.  Determine which bus arrives at point V first. Justify your answer.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Consider the angles $\alpha$ and $\theta$ on the diagram below.

    ![9067a79ab68d8d4a6452cc9e02a8e84376b48852.svg](images/9067a79ab68d8d4a6452cc9e02a8e84376b48852.svg)

    Using the diagram and co-interior angles, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \alpha &= \ang{180}-\ang{140}\hspace{3cm}\\[6pt]
    &=\ang{40}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Also
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{T\^{U}V} &= \ang{360}-\ang{40}-\ang{235}\hspace{3cm}\\[6pt]
    &=\ang{85}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the cosine rule, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d&=\sqrt{\text{UT}^2+\text{UV}^2-2(\text{UT})(\text{UV})\cos \text{T\^{U}V}}\\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{65^2+40^2-2(65)(40)\cos \ang{85}} \hspace{0.5cm}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.35em} \approx 73.3 \text{ km}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the sine rule, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\sin\theta}{\text{UV}}&=\dfrac{\sin\text{T\^{U}V}}{d}\hspace{0.5cm}\\[6pt]
    \dfrac{\sin\theta}{40} &= \dfrac{\sin{\ang{85}}}{73.3} \hspace{0.5cm}\\[6pt]
    \sin\theta &= \dfrac{\sin{\ang{85}}}{73.3}\times 40 \hspace{0.5cm}\\[6pt]
    \theta &\approx \ang{32.9}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, Bus-2 travels on a
    true bearing of $\ang{140}+\ang{32.9}$
     $ \approx \ang{173}$

3.  Let $t_1$ and $t_2$ be the total trip times taken by Bus-1 and Bus-2
    respectively.

    Since
    $$
    \text{time}=\dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}

    $$
    Then

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    t_1 &= \dfrac{65+40}{55} \hspace{0.5cm}\\[6pt]
    &= 1.91 \text{ hours}\\[6pt]
    t_2 &= \dfrac{73.3}{36}\\[6pt]
    &= 2.04 \text{ hours}\\[6pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Since $t_2 > t_1$, Bus-1's travel time is less, and hence will arrive at V first.
