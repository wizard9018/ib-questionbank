# AI903

**Maximum mark: 13**

A large triangular garden, ABC, is shown in the diagram below. A walking
path passes through the garden at point D and leaves at point E, such
that DE is parallel to the garden boundary BC.

![8f64ef57974c5c16521a9a2823135472d702f64d.svg](images/8f64ef57974c5c16521a9a2823135472d702f64d.svg)

Triangles ABC and ADE are found to be similar (same shape, but side
lengths can vary). Angles AD̂E = AB̂C $=\theta$ and AÊD = AĈB $=\alpha$.

1.  Find the value of
    1.  $x$.

    2.  $y$. [4 marks]

2.  Find the value of
    1.  $\alpha$.

    2.  $\theta$.
    3.  $\gamma$. [6 marks]

3.  Find the area of the triangle ABC, rounding your answer to the
    nearest m$^2$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  With the similarities of triangles ABC and ADE, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{\text{AC}}{\text{AE}} &=\dfrac{\text{BC}}{\text{DE}}\hspace{2cm} \\[8pt]
        \text{AC}&=\dfrac{\text{BC}}{\text{DE}}\times \text{AE}\\[8pt]
        45+x&=\dfrac{52}{30}\times 45\\[8pt]
        &\hspace{-0.5em}x=33\text{ m}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Likewise to find $y$
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{\text{AB}}{\text{AD}} &=\dfrac{\text{BC}}{\text{DE}}\hspace{2cm} \\[8pt]
        \text{AB}&=\dfrac{\text{BC}}{\text{DE}}\times \text{AD}\\[8pt]
        32+y&=\dfrac{52}{30}\times 32\\[8pt]
        y&=23.46...\\[6pt]
        y&=23.5\text{ m}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    1.  Using the cosine rule with triangle ADE, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \cos \alpha &= \dfrac{\text{AE}^2+\text{DE}^2-\text{AD}^2}{2(\text{AE})(\text{DE})}\\[8pt]
        \alpha &=\cos^{-1}\left(\dfrac{45^2+30^2-32^2}{2(45)(30)} \right)\\[8pt]
        &=45.24...\\[6pt]
        \alpha&=\ang{45.2}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the cosine rule with triangle ADE, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \cos \theta &= \dfrac{\text{AD}^2+\text{DE}^2-\text{AE}^2}{2(\text{AD})(\text{DE})}\\[8pt]
        \theta &=\cos^{-1}\left(\dfrac{32^2+30^2-45^2}{2(32)(30)} \right)\\[8pt]
        &=93.01...\\[6pt]
        \theta&=\ang{93.0}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3.  The sum of the angles in a triangle is $\ang{180}$. In triangle
        ADE, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \theta+\alpha + \gamma &= \ang{180}\hspace{4cm}\\[6pt]
        \gamma &= \ang{180}-\ang{45.24...}-\ang{93.01...}\\[6pt]
        &=41.73...\\[6pt]
        \gamma&=\ang{41.7}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle\text{ABC}}&=\dfrac{1}{2}(\text{BC})(\text{AB})\sin \theta\\[6pt]
    &=\dfrac{1}{2}(52)(32+23.5)\sin \ang{93}\\[6pt]
    &=1441.0...\\[6pt]
    A&=1\,441\text{ m}^2\hspace{1em}\text{(nearest m$^2$)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
