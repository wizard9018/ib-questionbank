# AI991

**Maximum mark: 8**

Melody is camping with her friends in a forest. After setting up the tent, she goes for a walk to see the nearby surroundings. She  walks $200$ m on a bearing of $\ang{280}$, changes direction, then walks another $150$ m on a bearing of $\ang{035}$.

1.  Find the distance Melody is from the tent at this point. [4 marks]

2.  Determine the bearing Melody should walk on to return to the tent.
    [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Consider the angle $\alpha$ in the diagram below

    ![AI991_part_a](images/AI991_part_a.PNG)

    Looking at the angles marked at T, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \alpha = \ang{360}-\ang{280}=\ang{80}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    **Note that $\bf{\alpha}$ is $\underline{\textbf{not}}$ the angle in the triangle, $\textbf{A\^TB}$**

    Now consider the angles at A. These angles together are co-interior to $\alpha$, and hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \ang{35}+\theta+\alpha & = \ang{180} \hspace{5cm}\\[6pt]
    \theta &= \ang{180}-\ang{35}-\ang{80}\\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{0.275em}\ang{65}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the cosine
    rule, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{BT}^2 &= \text{AT}^2+\text{AB}^2-2(\text{AT})(\text{AB})\cos \theta \\[6pt]
    \text{BT} &= \sqrt{200^2+150^2-2(200)(150)\cos \ang{65}}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-1.75em}\boxed{\text{BT} \approx 193 \text{ m}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the sine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\sin \text{A\^{B}T}}{\text{AT}} &= \dfrac{\sin \theta}{\text{BT}} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{\sin \text{A\^{B}T}}{200} &= \dfrac{\sin \ang{65}}{193} \\[6pt]
    \sin \text{A\^{B}T} &= \dfrac{200\sin \ang{65}}{193} \\[6pt]
    \text{A\^{B}T} &\approx\ang{69.91}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Consider angles $\beta$ and $\phi$ marked on the diagram below, where $\phi$ represents the bearing from Melody's position to the tent.

    ![AI991_part_b](images/AI991_part_b.PNG)

    $\beta$ is co-interior to $\ang{35}$, therefore $\beta = \ang{145}$.

    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \phi &= \ang{360}-\ang{69.91}-\beta \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{360}-\ang{69.91}-\ang{145} \\[6pt]
    \phi &= \ang{145.09}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The bearing from Melody's position to the tent is $\ang{145}$
