# AI249

**Maximum mark: 6**

A national park is in the form of a triangle, ABC, as shown in the
following diagram. Side length AB is $49$ km and
side length BC is $66$ km. Angle
$\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C}$ is $58$ $\text{\textdegree{}}$.

![883eee01912d7a6754025841f36293ea55bb652c.svg](images/883eee01912d7a6754025841f36293ea55bb652c.svg)

1.  Calculate the side length AC in km. [3 marks]

2.  Calculate the area of the park. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{AC}^2 &= (\mathrm{AB})^2 + (\mathrm{BC})^2 - 2 (\mathrm{AB})(\mathrm{BC}) \cos(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C})\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = 49^2 + 66^2 - 2(49)(66) \cos (\ang{58})\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    \text{AC}   & = \sqrt{49^2 + 66^2 - 2(49)(66) \cos (\ang{58})}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & \approx  57.7\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {km}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A   &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\mathrm{AB})(\mathrm{BC})\sin(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C})\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    &= \dfrac 1 2 (49)(66) \sin (\ang{58})\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    &  \approx 1\hspace{0.15em}370\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{km}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
