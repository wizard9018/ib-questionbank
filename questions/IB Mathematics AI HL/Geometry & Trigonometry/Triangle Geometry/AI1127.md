# AI1127

**Maximum mark: 7**

A clothes shop is designing an advertising board for an upcoming sale.

![q1127](images/q1127.png)

It is the shape of a triangular-based prism and made from wooden panels.

The panels are machine cut such that the lengths are exact. $BC=2.3$ metres, $DC=2$ metres and $EA=EB=1.6$ metres.

A wooden supporting rod, connecting points $E$ to $X$, is used to strengthen the advertising board.

1. Calculate the height, $EX$, of the advertising board.[2 marks]

A workman hand-cuts the rod to a length $1.26$ metres, **but** this is only accurate to the nearest centimetre.

2.  1. Determine the upper and lower bound for supporting rod.

    2. Justify that the rod will not fit. [2 marks]

The workman decides to **precision cut** a new supporting rod that will connect $E$ to $C$.

3. Find the angle the new supporting rod makes with the base of the prism.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The cross-sectional area of the prism is formed by the isosceles triangle $AEB$.

    $X$ is the mid-point of $AB$, therefore $XB=1\hspace{0.1em}\mathrm{m}$.

    ![a1127v1](images/a1127v1.png)

    Using Pythagoras' theorem we can find $EX$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        EX&=\sqrt{1.6^2-1^2}\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
        &\approx\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{1.25 \text{ metres}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
2.  1. Recall that $1\,$cm $=0.01\,$m.
        Hence if $L=1.26$ metres to the nearest cm then the upper and lower bounds are given by
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
            \hspace{0.525em}\boxed{1.255\hspace{0.1em}\mathrm{m}\leq L < 1.265\hspace{0.1em}\mathrm{m}}\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  $$
        \text{The minimum length possible ($1.255\hspace{0.1em}\mathrm{m}$) is larger than $EX$ ($1.25\hspace{0.1em}\mathrm{m}$)}.\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        $$

3. The projection of $EC$ in the base of the prism is shown by the line $XC$.

    ![a1127v2](images/a1127v2.png)

    The angle $EC$ makes with the base is the angle between $EC$ and $XC$, shown as $\theta$.

    We need to find either $EC$ or $XC$. In this solution we will find $XC$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        XC^2&=2.3^2+1^2\\
        XC&=2.5079... \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We can now use the tangent ratio to find $\theta$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \tan{\theta}&=\frac{EX}{XC}\\
        \theta&=\mathrm{tan}^{-1}\left({\frac{1.25}{2.5079...}}\right) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\
        &=26.492...\\[6pt]
        &\approx\hspace{0.525em}26.5^\circ \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
