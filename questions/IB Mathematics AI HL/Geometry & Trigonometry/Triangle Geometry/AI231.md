# AI231

**Maximum mark: 6**

A toy house has a roof in the shape of a right-angled isosceles
triangle, ABC.\
AB $= 6$ cm and angle
$\textrm{A}\hat{\textrm{C}}\textrm{B}= \ang{90}$.

![6df3defc1eabfef2a86919ee71982529c3d265ad.svg](images/6df3defc1eabfef2a86919ee71982529c3d265ad.svg)

1.  Find the length of AC. [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the area of triangle ABC. [2 marks]

The garage of the toy house also has a roof in the shape of right-angled
triangle, LMN. , angle
$\textrm{L}\hat{\textrm{M}}\textrm{N}= \ang{56}$ and angle
$\textrm{N}\hat{\textrm{L}}\textrm{M}= \ang{90}$.

3.  Calculate the length of MN. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using Pythagorean theorem, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm{AB}^2 &= \textrm{AC}^2 + \textrm{CB}^2 \\[5pt]
    6^2     & = 2 \textrm{AC}^2 \\[6pt]
    \textrm{AC}^2   & = 18\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-5mm}\textrm{AC} \approx 4.24\,  \mbox{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Consider the triangle ABC

    ![f7850ead1457a17bfe4c27e24b080afa8be0281a.svg](images/f7850ead1457a17bfe4c27e24b080afa8be0281a.svg)

    ABC is a right-angled isosceles triangle, therefore the height CH
    splits it into two right-angled isosceles triangles. Utilizing the
    area formula for a triangle, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle \textrm{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\textrm{AB})(\textrm{CH}) \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(6)(3) \\[8pt]
    &= 9\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  By the definition of the sine for angle
    $\textrm{L}\hat{\textrm{M}}\textrm{N}$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \sin(\textrm{L}\hat{\textrm{M}}\textrm{N}) & = \dfrac{\textrm{NL}}{\textrm{MN}} \\[5pt]
    \sin \ang{56} & = \dfrac{3.2}{\textrm{MN}} \\[5pt]
    \textrm{MN} & = \dfrac {3.2} {\sin \ang{56}}\\[11pt]
    &\approx 3.86\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
