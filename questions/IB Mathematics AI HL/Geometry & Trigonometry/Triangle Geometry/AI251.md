# AI251

**Maximum mark: 14**

A picnic area is shaped like a quadrilateral, ABCD, where AB
$= 10$ m andCD
$= 17.5$ m. Three of the internal angles have
been measured; angle $\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{D}}\mathrm{C}= \ang{51}$,
angle $\mathrm{C}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{D}= \ang{70}$ and angle
$\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{C}}\mathrm{B}= \ang{42}$. This information is
represented in the following diagram.

![9bde76ca633ea2626fe6f221f136dbf74a912806.svg](images/9bde76ca633ea2626fe6f221f136dbf74a912806.svg)

1.  Calculate the distance AC. [3 marks]

2.  Calculate the angle $\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C}.$
    [3 marks]

There is a lamp post at A, perpendicular to the ground. The angle of
elevation to the top of the lamp post from B is $\ang{32}$.

3.  Calculate the height of the lamp post. [2 marks]

Patrick's family is having a barbecue party on the picnic area. The
family brings a $1.5$ litre bottle of lemonade and **cone-shaped** paper
cups.

Each cup has a vertical height of $8$ cm and the
top of the cup has a diameter of $5$ cm.

4.  Calculate the volume of one paper cup. [3 marks]

5.  Calculate the maximum number of cups that can be completely
    filled\
    with the $1.5$ litre bottle of lemonade. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the sine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac {\text{DC}} {\sin (\mathrm{C}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{D})} &= \dfrac {\text{AC}} {\sin(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{D}}\mathrm{C})}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \dfrac {17.5} {\sin(\ang{70})}  & = \dfrac {\text{AC}}{\sin(\ang{51})}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \text{AC} & = \dfrac {17.5 \sin(\ang{51})} {\sin(\ang{70})}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &  \approx  14.5\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m    \rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the sine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac {\text{AB}} {\sin (\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{C}}\mathrm{B})} &= \dfrac {\text{AC}} {\sin(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C})}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \dfrac {10} {\sin(\ang{42})}
    & = \dfrac {14.5}{\sin(\text{A\^BC})}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \sin(\text{A\^BC})      & = \dfrac {14.5 \sin(\ang{42})} {10}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \text{A\^BC} & \approx \sin^{-1} (0.970)\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & \approx  \ang{76}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The height of the lamp post, $h$, can be illustrated in the diagram
    below.

    ![29a83562587a6ddc3804de4af3e3b55bbfd65578.svg](images/29a83562587a6ddc3804de4af3e3b55bbfd65578.svg)

    $h$ can be found by:

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tan(\hat{\mathrm{B}}) &= \dfrac{h}{\mathrm{AB}}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    \tan(\ang{32})  & = \dfrac h {10}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    h   & = 10 \tan(\ang{32})\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &\approx  6.25\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m\rule[0mm]{0pt}{6mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using the volume of a cone formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V   & = \dfrac 1 3 \pi r^2 h\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    &=\dfrac 1 3 \pi \left(\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^2(8)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    &\approx  52.4\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {cm}^3\rule[0mm]{0pt}{6.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  The number of cups ($N$) can be calculated by dividing the total
    volume by the volume of each cup. Note, 1L of liquid is equal to
    $1,000 cm^3$. Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    N   & = 1\hspace{0.15em}500 \div 52.4\\[6pt]
    & \approx  28.6\\[6pt]
    & = 28 \mbox{ completely filled cups}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
