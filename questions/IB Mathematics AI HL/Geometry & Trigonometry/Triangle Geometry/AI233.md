# AI233

**Maximum mark: 6**

A right pyramid has apex $V$ and square base ABCD. The vertical height
of the pyramid, VM, is $5$ cm. The sloping edges
are $8$ cm long.

![ae2458c1e0123f19fc6f4d687f0b95c44643ec9b.svg](images/ae2458c1e0123f19fc6f4d687f0b95c44643ec9b.svg)

1.  Calculate the length of MC. [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the size of the angle that sloping edge VC makes with the\
    vertical height of the pyramid. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the area of the triangle ABC. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Consider the right-angled triangle VMC.

    ![108aca5ad060fa89d9b3b3a2e211e22df1fba845.svg](images/108aca5ad060fa89d9b3b3a2e211e22df1fba845.svg)

    Using Pythagorean theorem, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm{VC}^2 &= \textrm{VM}^2 + \textrm{MC}^2 \\[4pt]
    8^2         & =  5^2 + \textrm{MC}^2\\[5pt]
    \textrm{MC}^2        & = 8^2 - 5^2\\[5pt]
    \textrm{MC}         & = \sqrt{64 - 25}\\[6pt]
    &\approx 6.24\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  To find the angle $\theta$ (illustrated in the diagram from part
    (a)) we apply the definition of cosine
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos \theta &= \dfrac {\textrm{MV}} {\textrm{VC}}\\[2pt]
    \cos \theta & = \dfrac 5 8 \\[1pt]
    \theta  & = \cos^{-1} \left(\dfrac 5 8\right)\hspace{2.05mm}\\[5pt]
    &\approx \ang{51.3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  ABC is a right-angled isosceles triangle, therefore the height MB
    splits it into two right-angled isosceles triangles. Utilizing the
    area formula for a triangle, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.8cm}A &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\textrm{AC})(\textrm{MB}) \\
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(2\textrm{MC})(\textrm{MC})  \qquad \text{[AC = 2MC, MB = MC]} \\[4pt]
    &= (6.24)^2 \qquad \text{[from part (a)]}\\[8pt]
    &\approx 39.0 \hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
