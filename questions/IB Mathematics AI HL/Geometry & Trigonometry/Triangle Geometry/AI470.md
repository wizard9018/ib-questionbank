# AI470

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $\tan \theta = - \dfrac{3}{2}$, for
$\dfrac{3\pi}{2} < \theta < 2\pi$. Find the exact value of:

1.  $\cos \theta$; [4 marks]

2.  $\sin \theta$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we draw a unit circle and the angle $\theta$, we have

    ![9f616a79d0964e589b70f309433a9322f51866ef.svg](images/9f616a79d0964e589b70f309433a9322f51866ef.svg)

    Hence the value of $\cos \theta$ is positive and the value of
    $\sin \theta$ is negative.

    Using the tangent identity, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tan \theta &= \dfrac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} \\[4pt]
    -\dfrac{3}{2} &= \dfrac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} \\[4pt]
    \sin \theta &= -\dfrac{3}{2}\cos \theta
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the Pythagorean identity and expression $(1)$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos^2 \theta + \sin^2 \theta &= 1 \\[9pt]
    \cos^2 \theta + \hspace{-0.15em}\bigg(\hspace{-0.25em}-\dfrac{3}{2}\cos \theta\bigg)^2\hspace{-0.15em} &= 1 \\[6pt]
    \cos^2 \theta + \dfrac{9}{4}\cos^2 \theta &= 1 \\[9pt]
    \dfrac{13}{4}\cos^2 \theta &= 1 \\[9pt]
    \cos^2 \theta &= \dfrac{4}{13} \\[9pt]
    \cos \theta &= \pm \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}} \hspace{5.45em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{10.8em} \cos \theta = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\cos \theta > 0$}]
    $$

2.  Substituting $\cos \theta = 2/\hspace{-0.05em}\sqrt{13}$ into
    expression $(1)$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \sin \theta &= -\dfrac{3}{2}\bigg(\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{13}}\bigg) \hspace{0.5em} \\[6pt]
    &= -\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{13}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
