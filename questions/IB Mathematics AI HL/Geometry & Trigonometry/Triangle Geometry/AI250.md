# AI250

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two hot air balloons, located at points A and B, float in the sky
$50$ m apart from each other on the same height
above a flat (horizontal) ground. Two straight ropes, AP and BP, are
attached to the same point, P, on the ground. The length of AP is\
$36$m and angle
$\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{P}$ is $\ang{45}$.

![eb6d9b378bacb00b3469a8e0a3816c18c3938047.svg](images/eb6d9b378bacb00b3469a8e0a3816c18c3938047.svg)

1.  On the diagram, draw and label with an $\alpha$ the angle of
    elevation of A
    [1 mark]

Angle APB is acute.

2.  Find the size of angle
    $\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{P}}\mathrm{B}$. [3 marks]

3.  Find the size of the angle of elevation of A from P.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.

    ![0b080a1f1303dd103ba3b0cc1c4657313d09ad53.svg](images/0b080a1f1303dd103ba3b0cc1c4657313d09ad53.svg)

2.  Using the sine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac {\textrm {AB}} {\sin (\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{P}}\mathrm{B})} &= \dfrac {\textrm{AP}} {\sin(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{P})}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \dfrac {50} {\sin (\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{P}}\mathrm{B})}   & = \dfrac {36}{\sin(\ang{45})}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \sin (\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{P}}\mathrm{B}) & = \dfrac{50\sin(\ang{45})}{36}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & \approx 0.982\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{P}}\mathrm{B}    & \approx \sin^{-1} (0.982)\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & \approx  \ang{79.1}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Since the ground is horizontal, the ground and AB are parallel.
    Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \alpha  & = \mathrm{P}\hat{\mathrm{A}}\mathrm{B} \qquad\quad \mbox{[alternate angles]}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4.5mm}\\
    & = \ang{180} - \mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{P} - \mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{P}}\mathrm{B}\\
    & = \ang{180} - \ang{45} - \ang{79.1}\\
    \alpha  \,\, & \approx  \ang{55.9}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
