# AI241

**Maximum mark: 6**

A triangular plot of farming land is shown in the diagram below. The
longest side is $120$ m, with the two adjoining
angles to this length being $\ang{35}$ and $\ang{25}$.

![4a1119a091f06b4845181c5e3a189a3f0aa885ee.svg](images/4a1119a091f06b4845181c5e3a189a3f0aa885ee.svg)

1.  Find the lengths of two other sides of this plot of land.
    [4 marks]

2.  Find the area of the plot of farming land. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  First, let's introduce some notation on the diagram

    ![222397793fb3c0afbc0d528426bba7e7006b37e0.svg](images/222397793fb3c0afbc0d528426bba7e7006b37e0.svg)

    Notice that angle $\hat{\mathrm{C}}$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hat{\mathrm{C}} & = \ang{180} - \ang{25} - \ang{35}\\[5pt]
    & = \ang{120}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    To find $a$, we can use the sine rule. We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac a {\sin \hat{\mathrm{A}}} &= \dfrac c {\sin \hat{\mathrm{C}}} \\[5pt]
    \dfrac a {\sin \ang{25}}    & =\dfrac {120}{\sin \ang{120}}\\[6pt]
    a & = \dfrac {120} {\sin \ang{120}} \cdot\sin \ang{25}\\[13pt]
    &\hspace{-1.8mm} a \approx 58.6\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    To find $b$, we can again use the sine rule. We get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac b {\sin \hat{\mathrm{B}}} &= \dfrac c {\sin \hat{\mathrm{C}}}\\[5pt]
    \dfrac b {\sin \ang{35}} & =\dfrac {120}{\sin \ang{120}}\\[6pt]
    b   & = \dfrac {120} {\sin \ang{120}} \cdot\sin \ang{35}\\[13pt]
    &\hspace{-1.6mm}b \approx 79.5\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the formula for the area of a triangle, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac 1 2 a b \sin \hat{\mathrm{C}} \\[5pt]
    A & = \dfrac 1 2 (58.6) (79.5) \sin \ang{120}\\[10pt]
    &\approx 2020\hspace{0.25em}\mbox m^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
