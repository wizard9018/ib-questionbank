# AI992

**Maximum mark: 8**

On a trip to Morocco, a group of tourists experience a horse trek through the Saharan desert. They start from a horse farm, moving on a bearing of $0\ang{75}$ for $3.2\,$km to reach an oasis. After taking a rest there, they take another ride on a bearing of $\ang{210}$ for $2.1\,$km to visit the Draa valley.

1.  Find the distance that they need to cover to get back to the farm.
    [4 marks]

2.  Determine the bearing they should ride on to get back to the farm.
    [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Consider the angle $\beta$ on the diagram below

    ![AI992a](images/AI992a.PNG)

    We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7em} \beta + \ang{75} &= \ang{180}\hspace{3em} [\text{co-interior angles on parallel lines}] \\[6pt]
    \beta &= \ang{180}-\ang{75}\\[6pt]
    &= \ang{105}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Looking at the angles marked
    at O, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \alpha &= \ang{360}-\ang{210} - \ang{105} \hspace{2.25em}\\[6pt]
    &= \ang{45}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the cosine rule, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em}\text{DF}^2 & = \text{OF}^2+\text{OD}^2-2(\text{OF})(\text{OD})\cos \alpha \\[6pt]
    \text{DF} &= \sqrt{3.2^2+2.1^2-2(3.2)(2.1)\cos \ang{45}}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-1.75em}\text{DF} \approx 2.27 \text{ km}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the sine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\sin \text{O\^{F}D}}{\text{OD}} &= \dfrac{\sin \alpha}{\text{DF}} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{\sin \text{O\^{F}D}}{2.1} &= \dfrac{\sin \ang{45}}{2.27} \\[6pt]
    \sin \text{O\^{F}D} &= \dfrac{2.1\sin \ang{45}}{2.27} \\[6pt]
    \text{O\^{F}D} &\approx\ang{40.86}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Now consider
    angle $\phi$ marked on the diagram below.

    ![AI992b](images/AI992b.PNG)

    We see that the angle $\phi$ is co-interior to $\ang{75} + \ang{40.86}$. Hence we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \phi + \ang{75} + \ang{40.86} &= \ang{180} \hspace{3.5em}\\[6pt]
    \phi &= \ang{64.14}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    And the bearing is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \ang{360} - \phi &= \ang{360} - \ang{64.14} \\[6pt]
    &=\ang{295.9}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The bearing from the Draa valley to the horse farm is $\ang{296}$
