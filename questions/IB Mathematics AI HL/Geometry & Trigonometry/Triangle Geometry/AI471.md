# AI471

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $\tan \theta = \dfrac{3}{4}$, for $0 < \theta < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
Find the exact value of:

1.  $\cos \theta$; [4 marks]

2.  $\sin \theta$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we draw a unit circle and the angle $\theta$, we have

    ![8b1bedf34bca085a901af6d794535a8fd16a5399.svg](images/8b1bedf34bca085a901af6d794535a8fd16a5399.svg)

    Hence the value of $\cos \theta$ and the value of $\sin \theta$ are
    positive.

    Using the tangent identity, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tan \theta &= \dfrac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} \\[4pt]
    \dfrac{3}{4} &= \dfrac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} \\[4pt]
    \sin \theta &= \dfrac{3}{4}\cos \theta \hspace{0.7em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the Pythagorean identity and expression $(1)$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos^2 \theta + \sin^2 \theta &= 1 \\[9pt]
    \cos^2 \theta + \hspace{-0.15em}\bigg(\dfrac{3}{4}\cos \theta\bigg)^2\hspace{-0.15em} &= 1 \\[6pt]
    \cos^2 \theta + \dfrac{9}{16}\cos^2 \theta &= 1 \\[9pt]
    \dfrac{25}{16}\cos^2 \theta &= 1 \\[9pt]
    \cos^2 \theta &= \dfrac{16}{25} \\[9pt]
    \cos \theta &= \pm \dfrac{4}{5} \hspace{5.8em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{9.5em} \cos \theta = \dfrac{4}{5} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\cos \theta > 0$}]
    $$

2.  Substituting $\cos \theta = 4/5$ into expression $(1)$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \sin \theta &= \dfrac{3}{4}\bigg(\dfrac{4}{5}\bigg) \hspace{2.6em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{3}{5}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
