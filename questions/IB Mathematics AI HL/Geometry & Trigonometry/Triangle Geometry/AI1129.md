# AI1129

**Maximum mark: 5**

Jane is learning how to ice skate.

She enters the ice rink and skates $4$ metres, on a bearing of $130^\circ$. She then skates a further  $6$ metres, on a bearing of $\alpha^{\circ}$ where $270^{\circ}<\alpha<310^{\circ}$.

Jane is $2.92$ metres from her starting point and she decides to head back.

On what bearing must she travel?

Use the Cosine rule to find $\angle CAB$
$$
\begin{aligned}
    \cos{\angle CAB}&=\frac{4^2+2.92^2-6^2}{2\times4\times2.92} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\\\[6pt]
    \angle CAB&=119.417...\tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\
\end{aligned}
$$

Now using angle reasoning we can find the bearing $\theta$
$$
\begin{aligned}
    \theta&=180-(360-130-119.417...)\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    &=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{069^\circ} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
\end{aligned}
$$
