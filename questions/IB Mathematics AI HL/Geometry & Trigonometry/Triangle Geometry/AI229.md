# AI229

**Maximum mark: 6**

Mary (M) sits on the top of the Eastern side of the Grand Canyon. Below
her stands Peter (P), at the bottom of the canyon. It is known that the
depth of the Grand Canyon is $1\hspace{0.15em}850$ meters at this point.
Nathan (N) stands on the Western side of the bottom of the Canyon, with
a distance of $2.5$ km to Peter.

![dbfc65aca8b1a391acee022e2f009a3db23375f5.svg](images/dbfc65aca8b1a391acee022e2f009a3db23375f5.svg)

1.  Write down the depth of the Grand Canyon, MP, in kilometers.
    [1 mark]

2.  On the diagram, label the angle of elevation from N to M with an
    $x$. [1 mark]

3.  Find the size of the angle of elevation from N to M. [2 marks]

4.  Find MN, the distance in **kilometers** between Mary and Nathan.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\textrm{MP} = 1.85\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{km}$

    ![319eaa2fc18e3e2bfc460af084fc38020895c6c3.svg](images/319eaa2fc18e3e2bfc460af084fc38020895c6c3.svg)

3.  Utilizing the definition of the tangent, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tan x &= \dfrac {\mbox{MP}} {\mbox{NP}} \\[4pt]
    \tan x  & = \dfrac {1.85}{2.5} \\[5pt]
    x & = \tan^{-1} \left(\dfrac {1.85}{2.5}\right)\\[7pt]
    &\approx \ang{36.5}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using Pythagorean theorem, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{NM}^2& = 2.5^2 + 1.85^2\hspace{3mm} \\[6pt]
    \mathrm{NM} & = \sqrt{9.6725}\\[8pt]
    &\approx 3.11\,\mbox{km}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
