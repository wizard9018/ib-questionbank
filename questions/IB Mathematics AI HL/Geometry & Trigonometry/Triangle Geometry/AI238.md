# AI238

**Maximum mark: 6**

A right pyramid has apex V and square base ABCD with the base diagonal $\text{AC}=4\,$cm. The sloping edges are
$7$ cm long.

![a98d55859cf37d2bfe06b0c518823537449f00ff.svg](images/a98d55859cf37d2bfe06b0c518823537449f00ff.svg)

1.  Calculate the size of the angle that sloping edge VC makes with\
    the pyramid base. [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the vertical height of the pyramid, VO. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the volume of the pyramid. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Consider the triangle AVC:

    ![39268b98d9a941b5547e00c99eeb48b0ba8b0e13.svg](images/39268b98d9a941b5547e00c99eeb48b0ba8b0e13.svg)

    To find the angle $\theta$ we apply the definition of the cosine of
    $\theta$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos\theta &= \dfrac{\textrm{OC}}{\textrm{VC}} \\[4pt]
    \cos \theta & = \dfrac 2 7 \\[4pt]
    \theta      & = \cos^{-1} \left(\dfrac 2 7\right)\\[7pt]
    &\approx \ang{73.4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Applying Pythagorean theorem to the triangle OVC (shown in part a),
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1mm}\textrm{VO}^2   & = \textrm{VC}^2 - \textrm{OC}^2\\[4pt]
    & = 7^2 - 2^2\\[4pt]
    \textrm{VO}     & = \sqrt{7^2 - 2^2}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em}\approx 6.71\,\mbox{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  First, let's find the length of a side of the square base.

    ![7350a0926c38999b48b72dc9ac847cc3905c5621.svg](images/7350a0926c38999b48b72dc9ac847cc3905c5621.svg)

    By Pythagorean theorem, from the right-angled triangle ABC, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm{AC}^2   & = \textrm{AB}^2 + \textrm{BC}^2\\[10pt]
    4^2 & = 2 (\mathrm{AB})^2\\[1pt]
    \mathrm{AB}& = \sqrt{\dfrac{4^2}{2}}%\\[7pt]
    %\textrm{AB} & \approx  2.83\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    and therefore, the area of the base is
    $A=(\mathrm{AB})^2=8$ cm$^2$.

    Next, using the volume formula of a pyramid, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \dfrac{1}{3} A\cdot h\\[4pt]
    V & = \dfrac 1 3 (8)(6.71)  \\[10pt]
    &\approx 17.9\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
