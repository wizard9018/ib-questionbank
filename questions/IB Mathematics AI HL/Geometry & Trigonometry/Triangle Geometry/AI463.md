# AI463

**Maximum mark: 6**

ABC is a triangle of area $32$ cm$^2$. The sides
AB and BC have lengths $7$ cm
and $12$ cm
respectively. Find the two possible lengths of the side AC, giving
your answers correct to $3$ significant
figures.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the area of a triangle formula, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(\text{BC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[6pt]
32 &= \dfrac{1}{2}(7)(12)\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[6pt]
\hspace{4.3em} \sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) &= \dfrac{16}{21}
\end{aligned}
$$

Using the Pythagorean identity, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos^2(\text{C\^{B}A}) + \sin^2(\text{C\^{B}A}) &= 1 \\[8pt]
\cos^2(\text{C\^{B}A}) + (16/21)^2 &= 1 \\[6pt]
\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) &= \pm\sqrt{1 - (16/21)^2} \hspace{4.4em} \\[7pt]
\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) &\approx \pm0.64769
\end{aligned}
$$

Using the cosine rule, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{3em} \text{AC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{BC}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{BC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[7pt]
\text{AC}^2 &= 7^2 + 12^2 - 2(7)(12)(\pm0.64769) \\[6pt]
\text{AC} &= \sqrt{7^2 + 12^2 - 2(7)(12)(\pm0.64769)} \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\text{AC} \approx 9.18 \text{ cm} \hspace{0.25em} \text{and} \hspace{0.25em} 17.4 \text{ cm} \hspace{4.8em}
$$
