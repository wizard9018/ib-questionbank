# AI237

**Maximum mark: 5**

There are two traffic lights on a road below a
120 m high skyscraper. From the top of the
skyscraper, the traffic lights can be seen at angles of depression of
$\ang{35}$ and $\ang{65}$ on each side of the skyscraper, as illustrated
in the diagram below.

![6b05816f591a3f607c81207fe8cd47c0ed87a26c.svg](images/6b05816f591a3f607c81207fe8cd47c0ed87a26c.svg)

Find the distance between the two traffic lights, correct to the nearest
whole metre.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Consider the following triangles:

![9159d818c9068ef1ebe1a7e36d4e5419fc0543c4.svg](images/9159d818c9068ef1ebe1a7e36d4e5419fc0543c4.svg)

From the right-angled triangle ABH, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\tan(\textrm{B}\hat{\textrm{A}}\textrm{H}) &= \dfrac{\textrm{BH}}{\textrm{AH}}\\[3pt]
\tan (\ang{65})     & = \dfrac {120}{\textrm{AH}}\\[3pt]
\textrm{AH}         & = \dfrac {120}{\tan (\ang{65}) }\\[6pt]
& \approx 56.0 \,\mbox{m}.
\end{aligned}
$$

From the right-angled triangle HBC, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\tan(\textrm{B}\hat{\textrm{C}}\textrm{H}) &= \dfrac{\textrm{BH}}{\textrm{CH}}\\[3pt]
\tan (\ang{35})     & = \dfrac {120}{\textrm{CH}}\\[3pt]
\textrm{CH}         & = \dfrac {120}{\tan (\ang{35}) }\\[6pt]
& \approx 171 \hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1.2cm}
\textrm{AC} & = \textrm{AH} + \textrm{CH} \\[6pt]
& = 56 + 171\\[7pt]
&\approx 227\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}
\end{aligned}
$$
