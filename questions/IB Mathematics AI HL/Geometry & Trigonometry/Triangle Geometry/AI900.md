# AI900

**Maximum mark: 16**

Consider the analogue clock below, which has a circular face with centre at point O. The minute hand of the clock, OP, has a length of $12 \text{ cm}$ and the hour hand, OQ, has a length of $10 \text{ cm}$.

![AI900a](images/AI900a.PNG)

In Figure: 1 the time on the clock is 7:00$\,$pm.

1. For Figure: 1, find

    1. the size of the reflex angle, $\mathrm{P\^OQ}$, in degrees. 

    2. the distance between the ends of the hour and minute hands.[4 marks]

In Figure: 2, the time is now 7:18$\,$pm, and as such, the end point of the minute hand has rotated through an angle, $\theta$, covering an arc length of $l$.

2. For figure: 2, find

    1. the size of the angle $\theta$ in degrees.

    2. the arc length $l$.

    3. the area of the shaded sector. [6 marks]

Another circular analogue clock, shown below, has a face radius of $14 \text{ cm}$, and minute and hour hands of length $14 \text{ cm}$. The current time shown on the clock is 6:00$\,$pm. The bottom of the clock face is $4 \text{cm}$ above the base of the frame holding the clock.

![ai900b](images/ai900b.PNG)

The height, $h$, of the end point of the minute hand above the base of the frame is modelled by the function

$$h(\theta)=14\cos \theta + 18$$

where $\theta$ is the angle rotated by the minute hand after 6:00$\,$pm.

3. Find the value of $h$ when $\theta = \ang{170}$ [2 marks]

The height, $g$, of the end point of the hour hand above the base of the frame is modelled by the function

$$g(\theta)=-14\cos\left(\dfrac{\theta}{12}\right) + 18$$

where $\theta$ is the angle rotated by the minute hand after 6:00$\,$pm.

The end points of the minute and hour hands have the same height from the base of the frame for the first time when $\theta=k$.

4. Find the value of $k$. [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1. The size of the reflex angle $\mathrm{P\^OQ}$ is

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \mathrm{P\^OQ}&= \dfrac{7}{12}\times \ang{360}\hspace{2cm}\\[6pt]

        &\hspace{0.5em}\boxed{=\ang{210}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

    2. Using the cosine formula and the obtuse $\mathrm{P\^OQ}$ angle of $\ang{150}$, we get

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \mathrm{PQ}^2&=\mathrm{PO}^2+\mathrm{OQ}^2-2(\mathrm{PO})(\mathrm{OQ})\cos \mathrm{P\^{O}Q}\\[6pt]

        \mathrm{PQ}&=\sqrt{(12)^2+(10)^2-2(12)(10)\cos\ang{150}}\\[6pt]

        &\hspace{0.5em}\boxed{\approx21.3 \mathrm{ cm}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

2. 1. The size of angle $\theta$ is

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \theta&= \dfrac{18}{60}\times \ang{360}\hspace{2cm}\\[6pt]

        &\hspace{0.5em}\boxed{=\ang{108}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

   2. Using the length of an arc formula with a radius of $12\text{ cm}$, we get

        

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        l&= \dfrac{\theta}{\ang{360}}\times 2\pi r\hspace{2cm}\\[6pt]

        &=\dfrac{\ang{108}}{\ang{360}}\times 2\pi(12)\\[6pt]

        &\hspace{0.5em}\boxed{\approx22.6 \text{ cm}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

   3. Using the area of a sector formula, we get

        

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        A&= \dfrac{\theta}{\ang{360}}\times \pi r^2\hspace{2cm}\\[6pt]

        &=\dfrac{\ang{108}}{\ang{360}}\times \pi(12)^2\\[6pt]

        &\hspace{0.5em}\boxed{\approx136 \text{ cm}^2}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

3. The height of the minute hand at $\theta=\ang{170}$ is

$$

\begin{aligned}

h(\ang{170})&=14\cos\ang{170}+18 \\[6pt]

        &\hspace{0.5em}\boxed{\approx4.21 \text{ cm}}

\end{aligned}

$$

4.    The graphs of $h(\theta)$ and $g(\theta)$ are plotted below

      ![AI900c](images/AI900c.PNG)

        From the graph, $h(\theta)=g(\theta)$ when $\theta=\ang{166}$. 

        Hence, $\boxed{k=\ang{166}}$
