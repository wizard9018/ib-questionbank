# AI232

**Maximum mark: 6**

A right pyramid has apex $V$ and square base ABCD, with AB
$= 3$ cm. The vertical height of the pyramid,
VM, is $12$ cm.

![8ce4509b82dfe469f3b2b98ac660f4aa263e6210.svg](images/8ce4509b82dfe469f3b2b98ac660f4aa263e6210.svg)

1.  Calculate the length of VA. [4 marks]

2.  Calculate the volume of the pyramid. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  First, let's find MA. Consider the square base

    ![423443c9e38160733ed3f4f8ffa8eacb5acf05bd.svg](images/423443c9e38160733ed3f4f8ffa8eacb5acf05bd.svg)

    By Pythagorean theorem from the right-angled triangle AMN, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textrm{AM}^2   & = \textrm{MN}^2 + \textrm{AN}^2\\[6pt]
    & = 2 (1.5^2)\\[6pt]
    & = 4.5\\[7pt]
    \textrm{AM}     & \approx   2.12\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Next, applying Pythagorean theorem to the right-angled triangle AVM,
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.2mm}\textrm{VA}^2     & = \textrm{AM}^2 + \textrm{VM}^2\\[6pt]
    & = 2.12^2 + 12^2\\[6pt]
    & \approx 148.49\\[9pt]
    & \hspace{-4.8mm}\textrm{VA} \approx 12.2 \hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Utilizing the volume formula for a pyramid, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \dfrac 1 3 A h \\
    V & = \dfrac 1 3 (9)12\hspace{3mm}\\[6pt]
    & = 36 \hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
