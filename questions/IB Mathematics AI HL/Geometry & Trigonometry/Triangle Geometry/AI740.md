# AI740

**Maximum mark: 8**

A walking trail surrounding a man-made lake in a park is in the shape of a triangle, ABC, as shown in the following diagram. The side length AB is $400\,$m. Angle $\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C}$ is $52^\circ$ and angle $\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{C}}\mathrm{B}$ is $41.5^\circ$.

![lake](images/lake.JPG)

1. Calculate the side length BC. [5 marks]

2. Hence, calculate the area of the man-made lake surrounded by the walking trail, rounding your answer $\textbf{to the nearest square metre}$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The angle sum of a triangle is $180^{\circ}$. Hence we can work out $\mathrm{B\hat{A}C}$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{B\hat{A}C} &= 180 - \mathrm{A\hat{C}B} - \mathrm{A\hat{B}C} \\[6pt]
    &= 180 - 41.5 - 52 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    \mathrm{B\hat{A}C} &= 86.5^{\circ} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Therefore using the sine rule, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\text{BC}}{\sin \left(86.5^{{\circ}}\right)} &= \dfrac{400}{\sin \left(41.5^{\circ}\right)} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
    \mathrm{BC} &= \dfrac{400 \times \mathrm{sin}\left(86.5^{\circ}\right)}{\mathrm{sin}\left(41.5^{\circ}\right)} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
    &= 602.5...\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-1.6em}\boxed{\text{BC} = 603\,\text{m} \,(3\,\mathrm{s.f.} ) }\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  From part (a), we have the length BC, and we were given length AB and angle $\mathrm{A\hat{B}C}$ initially. We have an angle and the two adjacent sides, hence we can use the area of a triangle formula. We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A     &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\mathrm{AB})(\mathrm{BC})\sin(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{B}}\mathrm{C})\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\[5pt]
    &= \dfrac 1 2 (400)(602.5...) \sin (\ang{52})\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[10pt]
    &=  94\hspace{0.15em} 961.32... \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}\\[8pt]
    & \hspace{0.275em}\boxed{= 94\hspace{0.15em}961\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}^2\,\text{ (nearest square metre)}}\tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\[10pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
