# AI230

**Maximum mark: 6**

A ladder $3.7$ m long leans against a vertical
wall. The distance from the top of the ladder to the ground is
$3.1$ m.

![b2d163545a6a77affd33b3cb66aa0089157bff91.svg](images/b2d163545a6a77affd33b3cb66aa0089157bff91.svg)

1.  Represent this information on a diagram in the space provided above.
    Show the ground, the ladder and the wall as the labelled line
    segments. [1 mark]

2.  Find the distance from the bottom of the ladder to the wall.
    [2 marks]

3.  Write down the **acute** angle made by the ladder with the wall.
    [3 marks]

    ![34299aa7bdb7b8ab70b94358fdb6a8aaa7910e99.svg](images/34299aa7bdb7b8ab70b94358fdb6a8aaa7910e99.svg)

2.  Using Pythagorean theorem, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    c^2 &= a^2+b^2 \\[6pt]
    3.7^2 & = 3.1^2 + b^2 \\[6pt]
    b^2 & = 3.7^2 - 3.1^2\\[5pt]
    b & = \sqrt{3.7^2 - 3.1^2}\\[9pt]
    &\approx 2.02\, \mbox{m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Utilizing the definition of the cosine, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos \theta &= \dfrac {\mbox{adj}} {\mbox{hyp}} \\[4pt]
    \cos \theta & = \dfrac {3.1}{3.7} \\[4pt]
    \theta      & = \cos^{-1} \left(\dfrac {3.1}{3.7}\right)\hspace{1mm}\\[9pt]
    &\approx \ang{33.1}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
