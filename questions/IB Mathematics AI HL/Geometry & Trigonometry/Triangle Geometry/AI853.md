# AI853

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two drones are being flown in a park. Drone A starts at point P and
travels towards point Q in a straight line on a true bearing of
$\ang{115}$ at a speed of $2.5 \text{ ms}^{-1}$. The distance from P to
Q is 800m.

Drone B starts at point Q and travels towards point R in a straight line
on a true bearing of $\ang{215}$ at a speed of $7.5 \text{ ms}^{-1}$.

These two paths are shown on the diagram below.

![689b942c97010e73a4ea1acaededfa45d09e9926.svg](images/689b942c97010e73a4ea1acaededfa45d09e9926.svg)

1.  Find the acute angle $\text{P\^{Q}R}$ [2 marks]

2.  Find the distance between Drone A and Drone B after $4$ minutes,
    rounding your answer to the nearest metre. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Consider the angle $\theta$ marked on the diagram below.

    ![3e79ff69a8eb3d40a21630ce4053ea089e0f44ef.svg](images/3e79ff69a8eb3d40a21630ce4053ea089e0f44ef.svg)

    Looking at the angles marked at P, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \theta &= \ang{180}-\ang{115}\hspace{5cm}\\[6pt]
    &=\ang{65}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, looking at the angles at Q, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{P\^{Q}R} &= \ang{360}-\ang{215}-\theta \hspace{5cm}\\[6pt]
    &=\ang{360}-\ang{215}-\ang{65}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.35em} =\ang{80}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Distance $(d_1)$ covered by Drone A after $4$ minutes is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d_1 &= 2.5 \times 240\qquad \quad [\text{4 minutes = 240 seconds}] \hspace{0.5cm}\\[6pt]
    &= 600\text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    After $4$ minutes, Drone A will
    be at $800-600=200\text{ m}$ from point Q.
    $$

    $$
    Distance $(d_2)$
    covered by Drone B after $4$ minutes is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d_2 &= 7.5 \times 240 \hspace{0.5cm}\\[6pt]
    &= 1800 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The diagram below illustrates
    the updated positions $\text{A}'$ and $\text{B}'$ for Drone A and
    Drone B respectively, after $4$ minutes.

    ![21fab3f4e044399edb4dc24a59c033e13026b1ed.svg](images/21fab3f4e044399edb4dc24a59c033e13026b1ed.svg)

    Let $(d)$ be the distance between Drone A and Drone B. Using the
    cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d&=\sqrt{\text{($\text{A}'$Q})^2+(\text{Q$\text{B}'$})^2-2(\text{$\text{A}'$Q})(\text{Q$\text{B}'$})\cos\text{$\text{A}'$\^{Q}$\text{B}'$}}\hspace{0.5cm}\\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{200^2+1800^2-2(200)(1800)\cos\ang{80}}\hspace{0.5cm}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.35em} \approx 1776 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
