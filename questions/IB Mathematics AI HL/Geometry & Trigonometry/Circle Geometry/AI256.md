# AI256

**Maximum mark: 7**

On the diagram below, points X and Y are the centres of two circles
containing the two arcs from A to B.

![0fd4845b4e2c438d70e90376c767a9d7d1c0fa7c.svg](images/0fd4845b4e2c438d70e90376c767a9d7d1c0fa7c.svg)

1.  Find the length from point A to point Y. [2 marks]

2.  Find the perimeter of the shaded region. [2 marks]

3.  Find the area of the shaded region. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Consider the right-angled triangle AYX. Using the sine ratio, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \sin(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{X}}\mathrm{Y}) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{AY}}{\mathrm{AX}}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \sin(\ang{30}) & = \dfrac {\text{AY}} {25}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \text{AY} & = 25 \sin(\ang{30})\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &\approx  12.5\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{mm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The perimeter consists of two arcs. Let's first find $l_X$, the
    length of the arc AB with centre at X (lower arc). Using the length
    of an arc formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l_{\mathrm{X}} &= \dfrac \theta {360} 2 \pi r \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    l_{\text X} & = \dfrac {60}{360} 2\pi (25)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & \approx  26.2\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {mm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Next, let's find $l_Y$, the length of the arc AB with centre at Y
    (upper arc). Using the length of an arc formula, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l_{\mathrm{Y}} &= \dfrac \theta {360} 2 \pi r \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    l_{\text Y}     & = \dfrac {180}{360}\, 2\pi (12.5)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = 39.3\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {mm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the perimeter of the shaded region can be found by:

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    p   & = l_{\text X} + l_{\text Y}\rule[-2mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
    & = 26.2 + 39.3\rule[-2mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
    &\approx  65.5\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{mm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The area of the shaded region is the area of a half disc with the
    center at Y and the radius AY without the area of the red region
    (shown below).

    ![51f05b553dd8193e99485e33665d2833f28fb1db.svg](images/51f05b553dd8193e99485e33665d2833f28fb1db.svg)

    The area of the half disc is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\text{half disc}} &= \dfrac 1 2 \pi r^2\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 \pi (12.5^2)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & \approx  245\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {mm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The area of the red region is the area of the sector XAB without the
    area of the triangle XAB. Using the area of a sector formula, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{sector}} &= \dfrac \theta {360} \pi r^2\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {60} {360} \pi (25^2)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & \approx  327\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{mm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{triangle}} & = \dfrac 1 2 (\mathrm{AX})(\mathrm{BX})\sin(\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{X}}\mathrm{B})\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm} \\
    &=\dfrac 1 2 (25^2) \sin(\ang{60})\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & \approx  271\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {mm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A   & = A_{\text{half disc}} - (A_{\textrm{sector}} - A_{\textrm{triangle}} )\\
    & = 245 - (327 - 271)\\
    &  \approx  189\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{mm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
