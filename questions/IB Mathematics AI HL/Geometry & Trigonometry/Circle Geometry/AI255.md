# AI255

**Maximum mark: 7**

John is building furniture using cylindrical logs of length
$1.8$ m and radius
$9.2$ cm. A wedge is cut from one log and the
cross-section of this log is illustrated in the following diagram.

![ac3a5d5b9ae3076486d87f71bc26393d414e7b90.svg](images/ac3a5d5b9ae3076486d87f71bc26393d414e7b90.svg)

1.  Find the length of the wedge arc, ABC. [2 marks]

2.  Find the area of the empty sector, OABC. [2 marks]

3.  Find the volume of each log. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the length of an arc formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l &= \dfrac \theta {360} 2 \pi r \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    l & = \dfrac {100} {360} 2 \pi (9.2) \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &\approx  16.1\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the area of a sector formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A  &= \dfrac \theta {360} \pi r^2\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm} \\
    & = \dfrac {100} {360} \pi (9.2^2)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &\approx  73.9\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  First we find the area of a log's sector. Using the area of a sector
    formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac \theta {360} \, \pi r^2\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &= \dfrac{360 - 100}{360} \, \pi (9.2^2)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {260} {360} \, \pi (9.2^2)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & \approx 192\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The volume of the log can now be found by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= A\hspace{0.25em}l \rule[-2mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
    V  & = 192(180)\rule[-2mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
    &\hspace{.275em} \approx 34\hspace{0.15em}600\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
