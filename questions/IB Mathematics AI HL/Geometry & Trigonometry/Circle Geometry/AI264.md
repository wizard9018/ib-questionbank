# AI264

**Maximum mark: 7**

Cylindrical logs of length $2.7$ m and radius
$7.5$ cm are used to build a wooden cabin. A
wedge is cut from one log and the cross-section of this log is
illustrated in the following diagram.

![f60b75b2f3c1a0c5e84ac7bd76c0dc2292e3c18a.svg](images/f60b75b2f3c1a0c5e84ac7bd76c0dc2292e3c18a.svg)

1.  Convert 108 degrees into radians, leaving your answer in exact
    values. [1 mark]

2.  Find the length of the missing arc ABC. [2 marks]

3.  Find the area of the missing sector ABCO. [2 marks]

4.  Find the volume of this log. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \theta  & = 108 \,\dfrac \pi {180}\\[6pt]
    &  = \dfrac{3\pi} 5\,\mbox{radians}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the length of an arc formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l &= r\, \theta\rule[-3.5mm]{1pt}{0mm}\\
    & = (7.5)\left(\dfrac{3\pi} 5\right) \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &\approx  14.1\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.75mm}\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the area of a sector formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac 1 2 r^2 \theta\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{0mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2  (7.5^2) \left(\dfrac{3\pi} 5\right)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & \approx  53.0\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.75mm}\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  First we find the area of a log's sector. Using the area of a sector
    formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac 1 2 \, r^2 \theta\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{0mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2\,(7.5^2)\left(2\pi-\dfrac {3\pi} 5\right)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2\,(7.5^2)\left(\dfrac {7\pi} 5\right)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & \approx 123.7\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The volume of the log can be found by

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     V &= A l \\
     & = (123.7)(270)\\
     &\hspace{.275em} \approx 33\hspace{0.15em}400\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3
     \end{aligned}
     $$
