# AI263

**Maximum mark: 6**

In a school playground, the grass is watered by lawn sprinklers that are
set to spray at an arc of $\ang{220}$ with a maximum spray distance of
$5$ metres, as shown in the diagram below.

![f7bf214c46ae1ce3087f0696019c94f6a30e6fdf.svg](images/f7bf214c46ae1ce3087f0696019c94f6a30e6fdf.svg)

1.  Calculate the area of grass that is watered by this sprinkler.
    [3 marks]

2.  Calculate the length of the sprinkler arc edge, where the maximum\
    spray distance lands. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the area of a sector formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac \theta {360}\,\pi r^2 \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {220}{360} \, \pi (5^2)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & \approx  48.0\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the length of an arc formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l &= \dfrac \theta {360}\, 2 \pi r \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {220}{360} \,2 \pi (5)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    &  \approx  19.2\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {m}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
