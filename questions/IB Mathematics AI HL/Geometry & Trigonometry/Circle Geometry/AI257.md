# AI257

**Maximum mark: 6**

In the shaded region shown below, the radius of the smaller arc is
$6$ cm and the distance between the two arcs is
$4$ cm.

![6e96a6fc9b9dd3120651100dfc86294bf80998b8.svg](images/6e96a6fc9b9dd3120651100dfc86294bf80998b8.svg)

1.  Find the perimeter of the shaded region. [3 marks]

2.  Find the area of the shaded region. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The perimeter of the shaded region consists of two arcs (small and
    big) and two line segments of $4$ cm each.
    First we find the length of the small arc. Using the length of an
    arc formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l_{\textrm{small}} &= \dfrac{\theta}{360} \, 2\pi r \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {140}{360} \,2 \pi (6)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    & \approx  14.7\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {cm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Next we find the length of the bigger arc. The radius of the big arc
    is $r = 6+4 = 10$ cm. Again, using the length of an arc formula, we
    get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l_{\textrm{big}} &= \dfrac \theta {360}\, 2 \pi r\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {140}{360} \,2 \pi (10)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & \approx  24.4\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {cm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the perimeter of the shaded region can be calculated by:

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    p   & = 14.7 + 24.4 + 2(4)\\[6pt]
    & = 47.1\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The area of the shaded region is the area of the small sector
    subtracted from the area of the big sector. Using the area of a
    sector formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{small}}  &= \dfrac \theta {360}\,\pi r^2\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {140}{360} \, \pi (6^2)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & \approx  44.0\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the same formula with $r = 10$cm, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{big}} &= \dfrac \theta {360}\,\pi r^2\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {140}{360} \, \pi (10^2)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & \approx  122.2\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, the area of the shaded region is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A & = 122.2 - 44.0\\[6pt]
    &=  78.2\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
