# AI259

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the analogue clock below. The clock has a diameter of
$30$ cm and is shaded between 1 and 6.

![1dda735d26c19e7135044a10956fc300e214d33e.svg](images/1dda735d26c19e7135044a10956fc300e214d33e.svg)

1.  Find the length of the arc between $1$ and $6$, rounded to the
    nearest cm. [3 marks]

2.  Find the area of the sector between $1$ and $6$, rounded to the
    nearest\
    integer. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's first find the angle of the sector.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \theta  & = \dfrac 5 {12} \,\ang{360}\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \ang{150}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the
    length of an arc formula with a radius of 15 cm, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l &= \dfrac \theta {360}\, 2 \pi r \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {150}{360} \,2 \pi (15)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    &  \approx 39\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {cm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the area of a sector formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac \theta {360}\,\pi r^2\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {150}{360} \, \pi (15^2)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & \approx  295\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
