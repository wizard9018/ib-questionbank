# AI254

**Maximum mark: 4**

Cylindrical logs of length $2.2$ m and radius
$7.6$ cm are used to construct a fence line.
Part of the logs are removed, as illustrated in the diagram below.

![7ab1702add176182b785d942e7d7230ac6752635.svg](images/7ab1702add176182b785d942e7d7230ac6752635.svg)

Find the volume of each log.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

First we find the area of the sector remaining. Using the area of a
sector formula, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
A   &= \dfrac \theta {360} \, \pi r^2 \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
& = \dfrac {360-60} {360} \, \pi (7.6^2)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
& = \dfrac {300} {360} \, \pi (7.6^2)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
& \approx 151.2\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

The volume of the log can now be found by
$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= A\hspace{0.15em}l\rule[-2mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
& = 151.2 (220)\rule[-2mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
&\hspace{.275em} \approx 33\hspace{0.15em}300\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^3\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
\end{aligned}
$$
