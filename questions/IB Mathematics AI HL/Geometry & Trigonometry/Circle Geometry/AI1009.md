# AI1009

**Maximum mark: 5**

A rotating sprinkler waters part of a lawn, shown as the shaded area in the diagram below. The sprinkler covers a distance of $13\hspace{0.25em}\mathrm{m}$, reaching a maximum distance of $17\hspace{0.25em}\mathrm{m}$ and covering an angle of $130^\circ.$

![AI1009a](images/AI1009a.PNG)

1. Calculate the length of the outer arc the sprinkler can reach, the arc from point A to point B. [2 marks]

2. Find the area the sprinkler can cover. Give your answer to the nearest square metre. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using the length of an arc formula with $\theta=130^\circ$ and $r=17$, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    l     & = \dfrac {130}{360} \times 2 \pi \times 17\\[11pt]

    &\hspace{-0.575em}\boxed{l \approx  38.6\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {m}}\hspace{2em}(3 \text{ s.f.})

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Let $A_1$ be the area of the circular sector with radius 17.

    Let $A_2$ be the area of the circular sector with radius 4.

    Using the formula for the area of a sector, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A_1    & = \dfrac {130}{360} \times \pi \times (17)^2\\[11pt]

    &\approx 327.9\hspace{0.25em}\mathrm{m}^2\\[11pt]

    A_2    & = \dfrac {130}{360} \times \pi \times (4)^2\\[11pt]

    &\approx 18.15\hspace{0.25em}\mathrm{m}^2

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore, the area of the region shaded is

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A & = A_1 - A_2 \\[9pt]

    &\approx 327.9 - 18.15 \\[9pt]

    &\hspace{-1em}\boxed{A \approx 310\hspace{0.25em}\mathrm{m}^2}\hspace{2em}(3 \text{ s.f.})

    \end{aligned}

    $$
