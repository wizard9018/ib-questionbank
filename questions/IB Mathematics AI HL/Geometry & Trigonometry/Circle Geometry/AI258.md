# AI258

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the shape shown below (shaded region). The radius of the longer
arc is $12$ cm and the distance between the arcs
is $3$ cm.

![a27c941e1caacd3c268ea0e11dcefcb5caaa5225.svg](images/a27c941e1caacd3c268ea0e11dcefcb5caaa5225.svg)

1.  Find the perimeter of the shape. [3 marks]

2.  Find the area of the shape. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The perimeter of the shape consists of two arcs (big and small) and
    two line segments, $3$ cm each. First we
    find the length of the small arc. Using the length of an arc
    formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l_{\textrm{small}} &= \dfrac \theta {360}\, 2 \pi r \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {70}{360} \,2 \pi (12-3)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & \approx  11.0\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {cm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Next, let's find the length of the big arc. Again, we use the length
    of an arc formula
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l_{\textrm{big}} &= \dfrac \theta {360}\, 2 \pi r\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {70}{360} \,2 \pi (12)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & \approx  14.7\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {cm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The perimeter can now be calculated by:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    p   & = 11.0 + 14.7 + 2(3)\\[6pt]
    & =  31.7\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The area of the shape can be calculated by subtracting the area of
    the small sector from the big sector. Using the area of a sector
    formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{small}} &= \dfrac \theta {360}\,\pi r^2\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {70}{360} \, \pi ((12-3)^2)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & \approx  49.48\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the same formula with $r = 12$ cm, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\textrm{big}} &= \dfrac \theta {360}\,\pi r^2\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {70}{360} \, \pi (12^2)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & \approx  87.96\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The area of the shape (shaded region) can now be calculated by

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A      & = 87.96 - 49.48\\[6pt]
    & \approx 38.5\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
