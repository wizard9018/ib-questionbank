# AI262

**Maximum mark: 6**

A lawn sprinkler watering a garden sprays at an arc of $\ang{140}$ with
a maximum spray distance of $3.5$ metres, as shown in the diagram below.

![e3d75f6f7537ff19de86825d07d2f6d9eb07390f.svg](images/e3d75f6f7537ff19de86825d07d2f6d9eb07390f.svg)

1.  Calculate the area of lawn that is watered by the sprinkler.
    [3 marks]

2.  Calculate the length of the outer arc, where the maximum spray\
    distance lands. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the area of a sector formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac \theta {360}\,\pi r^2 \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {140}{360} \, \pi (3.5^2)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & \approx  15.0\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the length of an arc formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l &= \dfrac \theta {360}\, 2 \pi r\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {140}{360} \,2 \pi (3.5)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}\\
    &  \approx  8.55\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {m}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
