# AI266

**Maximum mark: 5**

Two concentric circles with centre O are shown in the diagram below. The
radius of the smaller circle is $5$ cm and the radius of the larger
circle is $7$ cm. Points A and B lie on the
circumference of the larger circle such that angle
$\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{O}}\mathrm{B}$ is $\dfrac \pi 5$ radians.

![5381fcfc739adad425aa88426f6c2242a7ffc44d.svg](images/5381fcfc739adad425aa88426f6c2242a7ffc44d.svg)

1.  Find the length of the short arc AB. [2 marks]

2.  Find the area of the shaded region. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the length of an arc formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l &= r\, \theta \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = 7 \left(\dfrac \pi 5\right)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm} \\
    &\approx  4.40\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The area the shaded region is the area of the large circle sector
    subtract the area of the small circle sector. Using the area of the
    sector formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A   & = A_{\textrm{large circle sector}} - A_{\textrm{small circle sector}} \rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = [\dfrac 1 2 (7^2) \dfrac \pi 5]  - [\dfrac 1 2 (5^2) \dfrac \pi 5]\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & \approx  7.54\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
