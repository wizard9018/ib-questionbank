# AI1223

**Maximum mark: 6**

A garden is designed in the shape of a sector of a circle with a radius of $12\,$m and a central angle of $150^{\circ}$.

The garden is entirely covered with grass, except for a flower bed of width $2\,$m that runs along the curved edge of the sector, as shown in the diagram below.

![ai1223](images/ai1223.png)

Find the area of the flower bed.

Give your answer correct to two decimal places.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let $R = 12$ be the outer radius, including grass area and flower bed.

Let $r = 10$ be the inner radius, grass area only.

Let $\theta = 150^{\circ}$, given in question.

Finding the Total area of sector

$$

\begin{aligned}

A &= \frac{\theta}{360} \cdot \pi \cdot r^2 \\[6pt]

A_{\text{total}} &= \frac{150}{360} \cdot \pi \cdot 12^2 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    &= 60\pi \text{ or } {188.49...\,\text{m}^2} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

\end{aligned}

$$

Finding area of the inner sector, grass area only

$$

\begin{aligned}

A_{\text{grass}} &= \frac{150}{360} \cdot \pi \cdot 10^2 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

&= \frac{125\pi}{3} \text{ or } {130.89...\,\text{m}^2} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

\end{aligned}

$$

Now subtracting to find the area of flower bed only

$$

\begin{aligned}

A_{\text{flower bed}} &= A_{\text{total}} - A_{\text{grass}} \\[6pt]

&= 60\pi - \frac{125\pi}{3}\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

&= \frac{55\pi}{3} \text{ or } 57.59...\\[6pt]

A_{\text{flower bed}} &= \boxed{57.60\,\text{m}^2 }\hspace{0.5em}\text{ (2 d.p.) }\tag*{\textbf{A1}}

\end{aligned}

$$
