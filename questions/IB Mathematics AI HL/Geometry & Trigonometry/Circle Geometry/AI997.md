# AI997

**Maximum mark: 5**

Joe creates a small baseball field in his backyard to play with his children. The field is covered by grass in the shape of a sector of a circle with radius $8$ metres, as shown in the following diagram. The angle at the centre of the sector is $\theta^{\circ}$.

![AI997a](images/AI997a.PNG)

A total of $48$ m$^2$ of grass was used to cover
the field.

1.  Find the value of $\theta$. [2 marks]

Joe asks his son, Lancer, to run around the field twice to warm up
before playing a game.

2.  Find the total distance that Lancer runs. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the area of a sector formula $A = \dfrac \theta {360}\,\times\,\pi r^2$ where
 $r = 8\,$m, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    48    & = \dfrac {\theta}{360} \,\times\, \pi (8^2)\\[6pt]
    \theta &= \dfrac{48 \cdot360}{64\pi} \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-0.75em}\boxed{\theta \approx \ang{85.9}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $l$ be the length of the arc of the sector.

    Using the length of an arc formula
$l = \dfrac \theta {360}\,\times\, 2 \pi r$ where $\theta = \ang{85.9}$ and $r = 8$ m, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l     & = \dfrac {85.9}{360} \,\times\,2 \pi (8)\\[6pt]
    & \approx  11.99\,\mbox {m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the distance that Lancer runs is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= 2(r + r + l) \\[6pt]
    &= 2(8 + 8 + 11.99) \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-0.75em}\boxed{d = 56.0\,\mbox {m}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
