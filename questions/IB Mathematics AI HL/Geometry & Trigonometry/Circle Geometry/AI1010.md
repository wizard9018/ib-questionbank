# AI1010

**Maximum mark: 5**

A surveillance camera records the area in front of a warehouse, shown as the shaded area in the following diagram. The camera's field of view has a radial distance of $15\hspace{0.25em}\mathrm{m}$, reaching a maximum distance of $21\hspace{0.25em}\mathrm{m}$ away from the camera, and covering an angle of $150^\circ.$

![AI1010a](images/AI1010a.PNG)

1. Calculate the length of the outer arc the camera can record, the arc from point A to point B. [2 marks]

2. Find the area the camera can record. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using the length of an arc formula with $\theta = 150^\circ$ and $r=21$, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    l     & = \dfrac {150}{360} \times 2 \pi \times 21\\[11pt]

    &\hspace{-0.575em}\boxed{l \approx 55.0\hspace{0.25em}\mathrm{m}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Let $A_1$ be the area of the circular sector with radius 21.

    Let $A_2$ be the area of the circular sector with radius 6.

    Using the formula for the area of a sector, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A_1    & = \dfrac {150}{360} \times \pi \times (21)^2\\[11pt]

    &\approx 577.3\hspace{0.25em}\mathrm{m}^2 \\[11pt]

    A_2    & = \dfrac {150}{360} \times \pi \times (6)^2 \\[11pt]

    &\approx 47.12\hspace{0.25em}\mathrm{m}^2

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore, the area of the region shaded is

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A & = A_1 - A_2 \\[9pt]

    &\approx 577.3 - 47.12 \\[11pt]

    &\hspace{-1em}\boxed{A \approx 530\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {m}^2} 

    \end{aligned}

    $$
