# AI901

**Maximum mark: 6**

During a hiking trip, Jim stopped and checked his compass for directions. The compass has a circular face with centre at O and the diameter of $20 \text{ cm}$. The needle of the compass is at an angle of $\theta$ to the East of North, covering an arc length of $l$, as illustrated in the diagram below.

![AI901](images/AI901.PNG)

1. Determine the circumference of the face of the compass. [1 mark]

2. Using the diagram, write down the size of the angle $\theta$ in degrees. [1 mark]

3. Calculate the arc length, $l$. [2 marks]

4. Calculate the total area of the shaded sectors on the diagram. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The circumference of the compass face is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    C&=2\pi r\hspace{2cm}\\[6pt]
    &=2\pi (10)\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}\approx 62.8 \text{ cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  By inspection, we see
    $$
    \theta=\ang{50}\hspace{2cm}
    $$

3.  Using the length of an arc formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l&= \dfrac{\theta}{\ang{360}}\times 2\pi r\hspace{2cm}\\[6pt]
    &=\dfrac{\ang{50}}{\ang{360}}\times 2\pi(10)\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}\approx8.73 \text{ cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using the area of a sector formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A&=2\left( \dfrac{\theta}{\ang{360}}\times \pi r^2\right)\hspace{2cm}\\[6pt]
    &=2\left(\dfrac{\ang{50}}{\ang{360}}\times \pi(10)^2\right)\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}\approx87.3 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
