# AI265

**Maximum mark: 5**

Paper party cups in the shape of a cone are formed from a sector of
paper material. The diagram below left shows the finished paper cup. The
diagram below right shows the original paper sector used to create the
cup. When the cones are formed, the straight edges of the sector join
without any overlap.

![19151462df82e042cfe680d98e5bbdbb9045524b.svg](images/19151462df82e042cfe680d98e5bbdbb9045524b.svg)

1.  Find the slant height of the cups, $L$. [1 mark]

2.  Find the arc length of the sector. [2 marks]

3.  Find the angle $\theta$ in radians. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using Pythagoras theorem, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    L^2 & = 8^2 + 3^2\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    L & = \sqrt{8^2 + 3^2}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    &\approx  8.54\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{cm}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The arc length of the sector is equal to the circumference of a
    circular base of the cone. Using the circumference of a circle
    formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l &= 2\pi r\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    & = 2 \pi (3)\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\\
    &\approx  18.8\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {cm}\rule[-1.5mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the length of an arc formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l &= r\, \theta\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    18.8 & =8.54\, \theta\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    \theta  & = \dfrac {18.8} {8.54}\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    &\approx  2.20\ \mbox{radians}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
