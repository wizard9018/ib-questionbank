# AI267

**Maximum mark: 6**

Kindergarten students are creating cone-shaped birthday hats from pieces
of colorful paper circles. The first step is to cut out a sector of the
paper, as illustrated by sector OABC in the diagram below. The paper
circles have centre O and . The length of arc
ABC is $4\pi$ cm and angle
$\mathrm{A}\hat{\mathrm{O}}\mathrm{C}= \dfrac {2\pi} 7$.

![7be9547705f10f010c3e340ba66bb78adcd7114d.svg](images/7be9547705f10f010c3e340ba66bb78adcd7114d.svg)

1.  Find the radius of the paper circles, $r$. [3 marks]

2.  Find the area of the sector the students cut out, OABC. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the length of an arc formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l &= r \, \theta\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    4 \pi       & = r \left(\dfrac {2\pi} 7\right)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    r       & = \dfrac {4 (7) \pi} {2 \pi}\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = 14\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {cm}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the area of a sector formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac 1 2 r^2 \theta\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 (14^2) \left(\dfrac {2\pi} 7\right)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & \approx  88.0\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {cm}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
