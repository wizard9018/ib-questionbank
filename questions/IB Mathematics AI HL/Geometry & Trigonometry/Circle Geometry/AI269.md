# AI269

**Maximum mark: 6**

A windscreen wiper blade has a length of
$0.45$ m. When it's raining and the blade is in
motion, it moves through an arc angle of $\dfrac {3\pi} 4$ radians.

![5082bbe392cebb27115fda455ea9bead5eccf9db.svg](images/5082bbe392cebb27115fda455ea9bead5eccf9db.svg)

1.  Determine the area of the windscreen wiped by the blade. [3 marks]

2.  Determine the arc length travelled by the outer edge of the blade.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the area of a sector formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac 1 2 r^2 \theta\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 (0.45^2) \dfrac {3\pi} 4\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & \approx  0.239\hspace{0.25em}\mbox {m}^2\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the arc length formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l &= r \theta\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    & = 0.45 \,\dfrac {3\pi} 4 \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    &\approx  1.06\hspace{0.25em}\mbox{m}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
