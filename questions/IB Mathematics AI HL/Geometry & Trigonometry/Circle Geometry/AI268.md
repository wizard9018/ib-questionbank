# AI268

**Maximum mark: 6**

A camping tent in the shape of a cone is constructed from a large
circular sheet of polyester with a sector cut out, as illustrated in the
diagram below. The circular polyester sheet has a centre O and radius
$r$. The area of sector cut-out, OABC, is
$\pi$ m$^2$ and the length of the arc ABC is
$\dfrac {2\pi} 3$ m.

![cbc1724c95d439d8e58e2fc129378d864353d39d.svg](images/cbc1724c95d439d8e58e2fc129378d864353d39d.svg)

1.  Determine the slant height of the tent, $r$. [3 marks]

2.  Determine the angle of the sector OABC, $\theta$, leaving your
    answer in radians with exact values. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the the area of a sector formula and substituting in the
    length of an arc formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac 1 2 r^2 \theta\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 r r\theta\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = \dfrac 1 2 r l\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $A = \pi$ m$^2$ and $l = \dfrac {2\pi}3$.
    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \pi & = \dfrac 1 2\, r\, \left(\dfrac {2\pi}3\right)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    r & = \dfrac {2(3)\pi}{2\pi}\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = 3 \hspace{0.25em}\mbox m\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Substituting the area and radius value into the area of a sector
    formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \dfrac 1 2 r^2 \theta\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    \pi & = \dfrac 1 2 (3^2) \theta\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    \theta  & = \dfrac {2\pi} {3^2}\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    & = \dfrac {2\pi} 9 \,\mbox{radians}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
