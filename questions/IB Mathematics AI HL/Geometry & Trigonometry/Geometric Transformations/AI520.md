# AI520

**Maximum mark: 7**

The geometric transformation
$\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] x' \\ y' \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 & \hspace{-0.25em}-3 \\ 5 & \hspace{-0.25em}-2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] x \\ y \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$
maps points

with coordinates $(x,y)$ to points with coordinates $(x',y')$.

1.  Find the coordinates of the image of the
    point :

    1.  A$(1,-6)$;
    2.  B$(3b,b)$ where $b \in \mathbb{R}$. [4 marks]

2.  Determine the coordinates of point P whose image is
    P $^\prime(11,22)$; [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Substituting $x = 1$ and $y = -6$ into the transformation, we
        get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \begin{pmatrix*}[c] x' \\ y' \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 & \hspace{-0.25em}-3 \\ 5 & \hspace{-0.25em}-2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-6 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{2em} \\[5pt]
        &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 20 \\ 17 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*} \\[5pt]
        &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 16 \\ 18 \end{pmatrix*}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence the coordinates of the image of A are
         $(16,18)$

    2.  Substituting $x = 3b$ and $y = b$ into the transformation, we
        get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \begin{pmatrix*}[c] x' \\ y' \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 & \hspace{-0.25em}-3 \\ 5 & \hspace{-0.25em}-2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3b \\ b \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{2em} \\[5pt]
        &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3b \\ 13b \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*} \\[5pt]
        &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3b - 4 \\ 13b + 1 \end{pmatrix*}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence the coordinates of the image of B are
         $(3b-4,13b+1)$

2.  Substituting $x' = 11$ and $y' = 22$ into the transformation, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.2em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 11 \\ 22 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 & \hspace{-0.25em}-3 \\ 5 & \hspace{-0.25em}-2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] x \\ y \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*} \\[5pt]
    \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 11 \\ 22 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2x - 3y \\ 5x - 2y \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}  \\[5pt]
    \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 15 \\ 21 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2x - 3y \\ 5x - 2y \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the vector equation $(1)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2x - 3y &= 15 \hspace{0.3em}  \\[6pt]
    5x - 2y &= 21
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Solving the system of linear
    equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $x$ and $y$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{9em} x = 3 \hspace{0.75em}\&\hspace{0.75em} y = -3 \hspace{2em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Therefore the coordinates of P are
     $(3,-3)$
