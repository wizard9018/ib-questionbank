# AI514

**Maximum mark: 6**

A triangle with vertices A$(6,6)$, B$(3,1)$ and C$(1,3)$ is transformed
by $\mathbf{x}' = \mathbf{A}\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{b}$

where
$\mathbf{A} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}$
and
$\mathbf{b} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-6 \\ 3  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$.

1.  On the following grid, draw the image of the triangle ABC.
    [3 marks]

    ![70ce9488efbebd59eceae34e68c389afbd115760.svg](images/70ce9488efbebd59eceae34e68c389afbd115760.svg)

2.  Given the area of the triangle ABC is $16$ units$^2$, find the area
    of its image. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let A $^\prime$, B$^\prime$,
    C $^\prime$ be the image points of A, B and
    C.

    Using the position vector of A, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{OA\hspace{-0.1em}$^\prime$}} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 1  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\vec{\text{OA}} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-6 \\ 3  \end{pmatrix*} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 1  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 6 \\ 6  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-6 \\ 3  \end{pmatrix*} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-3  \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of A $^\prime$ are
    $(0,-3)$.

    Using the position vector of B, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{OB$^\prime$}} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 1  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\vec{\text{OB}} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-6 \\ 3  \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{0.25em} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 1  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 1  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-6 \\ 3  \end{pmatrix*} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-7 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2  \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of B$^\prime$ are $(-7,-2)$.

    Using the position vector of C, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{OC\hspace{0.05em}$^\prime$}} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 1  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\vec{\text{OC}} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-6 \\ 3  \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{0.5em} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 1  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 3  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-6 \\ 3  \end{pmatrix*} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 \\ 4  \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of C $^\prime$ are
    $(-1,4)$.

- If we draw the triangle
    A $^\prime$B$^\prime$C $^\prime$
    on the following grid, we get

    ![34aaca7c4adb4724ff4c5f454988c6de97d85ef0.svg](images/34aaca7c4adb4724ff4c5f454988c6de97d85ef0.svg)

2.  Using the formula for the determinant of a $2\times2$ matrix, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{det}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{A} &= \left|\begin{matrix*}[c] -1 & 2 \\ -2 & 1 \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.25em}\right| \\[6pt]
    &= (-1)(1) - (2)(-2) \\[15pt]
    &= 3 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle\text{A\hspace{-0.1em}$^\prime$B$^\prime$C\hspace{0.05em}$^\prime$}} &= |\text{det}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{A}| \times A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} \hspace{2.2em} \\[7pt]
    &= (3)(16) \\[6pt]
    &= 48 \text{ units}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
