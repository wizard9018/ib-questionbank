# AI516

**Maximum mark: 7**

A triangle with vertices A$(3,5)$, B$(5,2)$ and C$(3,2)$ is rotated
$90$ $\text{\textdegree{}}$ anticlockwise about O$(0,0)$ and then
reflected in the line $y = -x$. The rotation is represented by matrix
$\mathbf{A}$ and reflection by matrix $\mathbf{B}$.

1.  Write down the matrix :
    1.  $\mathbf{A}$;

    2.  $\mathbf{B}$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the matrix that represents the rotation followed by the
    reflection. [2 marks]

3.  On the following grid, draw the image of the triangle ABC.
    [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $\mathbf{A} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 & \hspace{-0.25em}-1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix*}$
    2.
        $\mathbf{B} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 & \hspace{-0.25em}-1 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 0 \end{pmatrix*}$

2.  The rotation followed by the reflection is represented by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em} \mathbf{B}\mathbf{A} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 & \hspace{-0.25em}-1 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 & \hspace{-0.25em}-1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Let A $^\prime$, B$^\prime$,
    C $^\prime$ be the image points of A, B and
    C.

    Using the position vector of A, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{OA\hspace{-0.1em}$^\prime$}} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\vec{\text{OA}} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 5  \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{1.2em} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-3 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of A $^\prime$ are
    $(-3,5)$.

    Using the position vector of B, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{OB$^\prime$}} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\vec{\text{OB}} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 5 \\ 2  \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{1.2em} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -\hspace{-0.1em}5 \\ 2  \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of B$^\prime$ are $(-5,2)$.

    Using the position vector of C, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{OC\hspace{0.05em}$^\prime$}} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\vec{\text{OC}} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 2  \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{1.2em} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-3 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of C $^\prime$ are
    $(-3,2)$.
