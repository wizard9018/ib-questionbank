# AI522

**Maximum mark: 6**

A circle defined by the equation
$\hspace{0.1em}x^2 + y^2 = \dfrac{9}{4}\hspace{0.1em}$ is vertically
stretched by a scale

factor of $\hspace{0.1em}\dfrac{10}{3}\hspace{0.1em}$ and then
translated by the vector
$\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.15em}-3 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$.

1.  On the following grid, draw the image of the circle. [3 marks]

    ![7246b6c543337c944be5cfdc8ceaf66af6f75629.svg](images/7246b6c543337c944be5cfdc8ceaf66af6f75629.svg)

2.  Find the area of the image of the circle, giving your answer in
    terms of $\pi$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The radius of the circle is $r = 3/2$.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{scale factor}\times r &= \left[\dfrac{10}{3}\right]\hspace{-0.2em}\left[\dfrac{3}{2}\right] \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
    &= 5 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    If we draw the image of the circle, we get

    ![99e394303418e8c391c7c183f33acaad49becffb.svg](images/99e394303418e8c391c7c183f33acaad49becffb.svg)

2.  The matrix
    $\mathbf{S} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*} 1 & \hspace{-0.15em}0 \\ 0 & \hspace{-0.25em}\frac{10}{3} \hspace{-0.1em}\end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$
    represents the vert stretch w$/$ a scale
    factor of $10\hspace{-0.05em}/3$.
    Using the formula for the determinant of a $2\times2$ matrix, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.3em} \text{det}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{S} &= \left|\hspace{0.15em}\begin{matrix*} 1 & \phantom{\hspace{0.75em}}0 \\ 0 & \hspace{-0.25em}\frac{10}{3} \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.15em}\right| \\[6pt]
    &= (1)(\frac{10}{3}) - (0)(0) \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{10}{3} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the area of a circle
    formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.0em} A_{\text{circle}} &= \pi r^2 \hspace{3em} [r = 3/2] \\[8pt]
    &= \pi\hspace{-0.1em}\left[\dfrac{3}{2}\right]^2 \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{9\pi}{4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     A_{\text{image}} &= |\text{det}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{S}| \times A_{\text{circle}} \hspace{1em} \\[10pt]
     &= \left[\dfrac{10}{3}\right]\hspace{-0.2em}\left[\dfrac{9\pi}{4}\right] \\[5pt]
     &= \dfrac{15\pi}{2}
     \end{aligned}
     $$
