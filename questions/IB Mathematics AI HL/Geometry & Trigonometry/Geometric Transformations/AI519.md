# AI519

**Maximum mark: 6**

Trapezoid PQRS is translated by the vector
$\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-7 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$,
then vertically stretched

by a scale factor of
 $\dfrac{5}{3}$  and then
reflected in the $x$-axis.

1.  Determine a single transformation that maps the trapezoid
    PQRS to its image. Give your
    answer in the form
    $\mathbf{x}' = \mathbf{A}\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{b}$ [4 marks]

The area of the image is equal to $k$ times the area of the trapezoid
PQRS.

2.  Find the value of $k$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The effect of translating the trapezoid by the vector $\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -7 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$ can be shown as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{x} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -7 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The matrices
    $\mathbf{S} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & \frac{5}{3} \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$
    and
    $\mathbf{R} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 1 & 0 \\ 0 & \hspace{-0.25em}-1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$
    represent the vertical stretch

    with a scale factor of $5/3$ and the reflection in the $x$-axis
    respectively.

    The vertical stretch followed by the reflection is represented by

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{R}\mathbf{S} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 1 & 0 \\ 0 & \hspace{-0.25em}-1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\begin{pmatrix*} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & \frac{5}{3} \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{1.7em} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 1 & 0\phantom{\hspace{0.5em}} \\ 0 & \hspace{-0.25em}-\frac{5}{3} \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we deduce
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{x}' &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em} 1 & 0 \\ \hspace{0.5em}0 & \hspace{-0.25em} -\frac{5}{3} \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\left(\mathbf{x} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -7 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}\right) \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em} 1 & 0 \\ \hspace{0.5em}0 & \hspace{-0.25em} -\frac{5}{3} \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\mathbf{x} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em} 1 & 0 \\ \hspace{0.5em}0 & \hspace{-0.25em} -\frac{5}{3} \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -7 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    And therefore
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \boxed{\mathbf{x}' = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 1 & 0\phantom{\hspace{0.5em}} \\ 0 & \hspace{-0.25em}-\frac{5}{3} \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\mathbf{x} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -7 \\ -\frac{10}{3} \end{pmatrix*}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  For a transformation $\mathbf{T}$, we can use the formula
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Area}_{image} = |\text{det}(\mathbf{T})| \times \text{Area}_{pre-image}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We know that in this case, $\text{Area}_{image} = k \times \text{Area}_{pre-image}$ and our transformation matrix is $\mathbf{R}\mathbf{S}$.

    Hence $k = |\text{det}(\mathbf{R}\mathbf{S})|$.

    Using the formula for the determinant of a $2\times2$ matrix, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{det}(\mathbf{R}\mathbf{S}) &= \left|\hspace{0.25em}\begin{matrix*}[r] 1 & 0\phantom{\hspace{0.5em}} \\ 0 & \hspace{-0.25em}-\frac{5}{3} \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.15em}\right| \\[6pt]
    &= (1)(-\frac{5}{3}) - (0)(0) \\[10pt]
    &= -\dfrac{5}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     k &= |\text{det}(\mathbf{R}\mathbf{S})| \hspace{0.4em} \\[10pt]
     &= \dfrac{5}{3}
     \end{aligned}
     $$
