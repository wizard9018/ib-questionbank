# AI953

**Maximum mark: 7**

A geometric transformation
$T: \hspace{-0.15em}\bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix}{x} \\[2pt] {y} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg) \hspace{-0.15em}\mapsto \hspace{-0.15em} \bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {x'} \\[2pt] {y'} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.1em}$
is defined by
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix}{x'} \\[6pt] {y'} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)\hspace{-0.05em} &= \Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} \mathrm{cos}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) & \mathrm{sin}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) \\[6pt] \mathrm{sin}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) & \mathrm{cos}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)\hspace{-0.15em}\Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {x} \\[6pt] {y} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)\hspace{-0.05em} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} 1 \\[6pt] 2 \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)\hspace{-0.1em}. \\
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Find the coordinates of the image of the point
    P$(1,-\hspace{0.2em}\sqrt{3})$. [2 marks]

2.  Given that
    $T: \hspace{-0.15em}\bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {a} \\[2pt] {b} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg) \hspace{-0.15em}\mapsto \hspace{-0.05em} \dfrac{1}{2}\hspace{-0.05em} \bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {a} \\[2pt] {b} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.1em}$,
    find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$. [3 marks]

A rhombus $R$ with vertices lying on the $xy$-plane is transformed by
$T$.

3.  Show that the area of the image is half the size of $R$.
    [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting $x = 1$ and $y = -\hspace{0.2em}\sqrt{3}$ into the
    transformation definition, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {x'} \\[6pt] {y'} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)\hspace{-0.05em} &= \Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} \mathrm{cos}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) & \mathrm{sin}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) \\[6pt] \mathrm{sin}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) & \mathrm{cos}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)\hspace{-0.15em}\Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {1} \\[6pt] {-\hspace{0.2em}\sqrt{3}} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)\hspace{-0.05em} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} 1 \\[6pt] 2 \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)\hspace{-0.1em}. \\[8pt]
    %
    &= \bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} -1 \\[2pt] 0 \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.05em} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} 1 \\[2pt] 2 \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg) \\[8pt]
    %
    &= \Bigg(\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{matrix} -1 - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \\[6pt] -\hspace{0.2em}\sqrt{3} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of the image of P are
     $\Bigg(-1 - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2},-\hspace{0.2em}\sqrt{3}\hspace{0.2em}\Bigg)$

2.  Substituting $x = a$ and $y = b$ into the transformation, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{1}{2}\bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {a} \\[2pt] \raisebox{0em}{b} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.05em} &= \Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} \mathrm{cos}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) & \mathrm{sin}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) \\[6pt] \mathrm{sin}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) & \mathrm{cos}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)\hspace{-0.15em}\Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} {a} \\[6pt] {b} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)\hspace{-0.05em} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\Bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} 1 \\[6pt] 2 \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg)\hspace{-0.1em}. \\[8pt]
    %
    \bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} \raisebox{0em}{$0.5a$} \\[2pt] \raisebox{0em}{$0.5b$} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.05em} &= \bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} \raisebox{0em}{$0.5a + 0.866b$} \\[2pt] \raisebox{0em}{$0.866a + 0.5b$} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.05em} - \bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} 0.866 \\[2pt] 1.732 \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg) \\[8pt]
    %
    \bigg(\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{matrix} 0.866 \\[2pt] 1.732 \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.05em} &= \bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} \raisebox{0em}{$0.866b$} \\[2pt] \raisebox{0em}{$0.866a$} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg) \hspace{3em}{(1)}\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using vector equation $(1)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a = 2 \hspace{0.7em}&\hspace{0.3em}\&\hspace{1.0em}b = 1 \hspace{3em}[\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the formula for the determinant of $2\times2$ matrix, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \det\hspace{0.05em}(T)\hspace{-0.05em} &= \Bigg|\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} \mathrm{cos}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) & \mathrm{sin}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) \\[6pt] \mathrm{sin}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) & \mathrm{cos}\big(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\big) \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\Bigg| \\[8pt]
    %&= (8)(-1) - (3)(-2) \\[6pt]
    &= -\dfrac{1}{2} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the formula relating
    the area of the rhombus with its image,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{area}\hspace{0.05em}(T(R)\hspace{-0.05em})\hspace{-0.05em} &= |\hspace{-0.1em}\det\hspace{0.05em}(T)|\hspace{-0.05em}\times\text{area}\hspace{0.05em}(R)\\[12pt]
    %\text{area}\hspace{0.05em}(T(R)\hspace{-0.05em})\hspace{-0.05em} &= |\hspace{-0.15em}-2|\hspace{-0.05em}\times\text{area}\hspace{0.05em}(R) \\
    &\hspace{-4.5em}\text{area}\hspace{0.05em}(T(R)\hspace{-0.05em}
    =\dfrac{1}{2}\times\text{area}\hspace{0.05em}(R\hspace{-0.05em})
    \end{aligned}
    $$
