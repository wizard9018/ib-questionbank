# AI515

**Maximum mark: 6**

A triangle with vertices A$(2,5)$, B$(6,-5)$ and C$(-4,-5)$ is
transformed by $\mathbf{x}' = \mathbf{A}\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{b}$

where
$\mathbf{A} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.2 \\ 0.5 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}$
and
$\mathbf{b} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$.

1.  On the following grid, draw the image of the triangle ABC.
    [3 marks]

    ![26689391defa22f5dea13844b8d4df0b44f2a556.svg](images/26689391defa22f5dea13844b8d4df0b44f2a556.svg)

2.  Find the area, in square units, of the image of the triangle ABC.
    [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let A $^\prime$, B$^\prime$,
    C $^\prime$ be the image points of A, B and
    C.

    Using the position vector of A, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{OA\hspace{-0.1em}$^\prime$}} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.2 \\ 0.5 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\vec{\text{OA}} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2  \end{pmatrix*} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.2 \\ 0.5 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 5  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2  \end{pmatrix*} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-3 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of A $^\prime$ are
    $(0,-3)$.

    Using the position vector of B, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} \vec{\text{OB$^\prime$}} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.2 \\ 0.5 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\vec{\text{OB}} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2  \end{pmatrix*} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.2 \\ 0.5 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 6 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-5  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 \end{pmatrix*} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 6 \\ 3  \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of B$^\prime$ are $(6,3)$.

    Using the position vector of C, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} \vec{\text{OC\hspace{0.05em}$^\prime$}} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.2 \\ 0.5 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\vec{\text{OC}} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2  \end{pmatrix*} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.2 \\ 0.5 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-4 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-5  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em} + \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2  \end{pmatrix*} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-4 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of C $^\prime$ are
    $(-4,-2)$.

2.  Using the formula for the determinant of a $2\times2$ matrix, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.3em} \text{det}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{A} &= \left|\hspace{0.25em}\begin{matrix*}[c] 1 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.2 \\ 0.5 & \hspace{-0.25em}-0.4 \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.25em}\right| \\[6pt]
    &= (1)(-0.4) - (-0.2)(0.5) \\[15pt]
    &= -0.3 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the area of a triangle formula, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{base})(\text) \hspace{1.9em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(10)(10) \\[10pt]
    &= 50 \text{ units}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle\text{A\hspace{-0.1em}$^\prime$B$^\prime$C\hspace{0.05em}$^\prime$}} &= |\text{det}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{A}| \times A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} \hspace{2.2em} \\[7pt]
    &= (0.3)(50) \\[6pt]
    &= 15 \text{ units}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
