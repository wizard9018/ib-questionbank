# AI517

**Maximum mark: 7**

A triangle with vertices A$(-3,3)$, B$(3,2)$ and C$(-1,1)$ is rotated
$90$ $\text{\textdegree{}}$ clockwise
about O$(0,0)$ and then enlarged by a scale factor of $2$.

The matrix
$\mathbf{E} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$
represents the enlargement with a scale factor of $2$.

1.  On the following grid, draw the image of the triangle ABC.
    [4 marks]

    ![40db0c784fec1bdfe7970e6373418b59c0a6225f.svg](images/40db0c784fec1bdfe7970e6373418b59c0a6225f.svg)

2.  Given the area of the triangle ABC is $5$ units$^2$, find the area
    of its image. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The matrix
    $\mathbf{R} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 & 1 \\  \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$
    represents the CW rotation about O through
    $90$ $\text{\textdegree{}}$.

    The rotation followed by the enlargement is represented by

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.6em} \mathbf{E}\mathbf{R} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 & 1 \\  \hspace{-0.1em}-1 & 0 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 0 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Let A $^\prime$, B$^\prime$,
    C $^\prime$ be the image points of A, B and
    C.

    Using the position vector of A, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{OA\hspace{-0.1em}$^\prime$}} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\vec{\text{OA}} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-3 \\ 3  \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{0.4em} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 6 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of A $^\prime$ are
    $(6,6)$.

    Using the position vector of B, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{OB$^\prime$}} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\vec{\text{OB}} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 2  \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{1.2em} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-6 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of B$^\prime$ are $(4,-6)$.

    Using the position vector of C, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{OC\hspace{0.05em}$^\prime$}} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}\vec{\text{OC}} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 & 2 \\ \hspace{-0.1em}-2 & 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em}\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] \hspace{-0.1em}-1 \\ 1  \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{0.5em} \\[2pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 2  \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of C $^\prime$ are
    $(2,2)$.

    If we draw the triangle
    A $^\prime$B$^\prime$C $^\prime$
    on the following grid, we have

    ![a9886c0429614e58afba95dd49e631919ae51d11.svg](images/a9886c0429614e58afba95dd49e631919ae51d11.svg)

2.  Using the formula for the determinant of a $2\times2$ matrix, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{det}(\mathbf{E}\mathbf{R}) &= \left|\begin{matrix*}[r] 0 & 2 \\ -2 & 0 \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.25em}\right| \\[6pt]
    &= (0)(0) - (2)(-2) \hspace{2.2em} \\[15pt]
    &= 4 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle\text{A\hspace{-0.1em}$^\prime$B$^\prime$C\hspace{0.05em}$^\prime$}} &= |\text{det}(\mathbf{E}\mathbf{R})| \times A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} \hspace{0.7em} \\[7pt]
    &= (4)(5) \\[6pt]
    &= 20 \text{ units}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
