# AI521

**Maximum mark: 6**

A circle defined by the equation
$\hspace{0.1em}x^2 + y^2 = \dfrac{25}{4}\hspace{0.1em}$ is horizontally
stretched by a scale

factor of $\hspace{0.1em}\dfrac{8}{5}\hspace{0.1em}$ and then translated
by the vector
$\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ \frac{5}{2} \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$.

1.  On the following grid, draw the image of the circle. [3 marks]

    ![f2b1acdfbc60295332800e31df6d9cc483be1cae.svg](images/f2b1acdfbc60295332800e31df6d9cc483be1cae.svg)

2.  Find the area of the image of the circle, giving your answer in
    terms of $\pi$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The radius of the circle is $r = 5/2$.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{scale factor}\times r &= \left[\dfrac{8}{5}\right]\hspace{-0.2em}\left[\dfrac{5}{2}\right] \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
    &= 4 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    If we draw the image of the circle, we get

    ![7a411a20acdc3e5921cb9af2340a018c0a3422c7.svg](images/7a411a20acdc3e5921cb9af2340a018c0a3422c7.svg)

2.  The matrix
    $\mathbf{S} = \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*} \frac{8}{5} & \hspace{-0.25em}0 \\ 0 & \hspace{-0.25em}1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em}$
    represents the horz stretch w$/$ a scale factor of $8/5$.

    Using the formula for the determinant of a $2\times2$ matrix, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.9em} \text{det}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{S} &= \left|\begin{matrix*} \frac{8}{5} & 0 \\ \phantom{\hspace{0.5em}}0 & 1 \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.15em}\right| \\[6pt]
    &= (\frac{8}{5})(1) - (0)(0) \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{8}{5} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the area of a circle
    formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.1em} A_{\text{circle}} &= \pi r^2 \hspace{3em} [r = 5/2] \\[8pt]
    &= \pi\hspace{-0.1em}\left[\dfrac{5}{2}\right]^2 \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{25\pi}{4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     A_{\text{image}} &= |\text{det}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{S}| \times A_{\text{circle}} \hspace{1em} \\[8pt]
     &= \left[\dfrac{8}{5}\right]\hspace{-0.2em}\left[\dfrac{25\pi}{4}\right] \\[8pt]
     &= 10\pi
     \end{aligned}
     $$
