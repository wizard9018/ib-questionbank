# AI691

**Maximum mark: 7**

Jack, a volleyball player, serves the ball with a trajectory modelled by the function
$$
h_1(x)=-0.12x^2+x+0.725
$$
where $h$ is the height of the ball above the ground, in metres, and $x$ is the horizontal distance from the serving point, in metres.

1. Find the height of the ball when Jack makes his serve (i.e. the height above the ground at the point where he connects with the ball).[2 marks]

Unfortunately, the serve is short and misses. For his next attempt, Jack will serve the ball $c$ metres higher at the serving point, so that the ball has the same trajectory (only the vertical height changes, increasing by $c$ metres).

2. Write down a second function, $h_2(x)$, modelling the new path of the ball in terms of $x$ and $c$.[1 mark]

3. 1. Calculate  the value of $c$, if the horizontal distance is $10$ metres from the serving point to the point where the ball would hit the ground.

    2. Determine the vertical height of the ball from which Jack makes his successful serve.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Letting $x=0$ and solving for $h_1(0)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h_1(x) &= -0.12x^2+x+0.725 \\[6pt]
     h_1(0) &= -0.12(0)^2+(0)+0.725 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    &= 0.725 \\[6pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Therefore the ball was served at a height of $$\text{$0.725$ metres}$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. As $c$ is a vertical height, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{-0.3em}\boxed{h_2(x)=-0.12x^2+x+0.725+c} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3. 1. Given that the distance is $10$ metres, we have $h_2(10)=0$, thus
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        h_2(10) &= 0 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
        -0.12(10)^2+10+0.725+c&=0 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{-0.9em} \boxed{c = 1.275} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2. Height of ball at  service point = 0.725 + 1.275
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        &\hspace{-0.3em}\boxed{{\text}}\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
