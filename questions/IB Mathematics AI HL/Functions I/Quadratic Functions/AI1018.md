# AI1018

**Maximum mark: 5**

The cross-section of a ship's hull below the surface of the ocean can be modelled by a parabola. The depth of the boat's hull, $d$ metres, is given by $d(x) = 0.5x^2-6.4x$, where $x$ is the horizontal distance from the left-hand side of the hull at the surface, also in metres, as shown in the following diagram. The $x$-axis represents the ocean surface.

![AI1018a](images/AI1018a.PNG)

1. Find the equation of the axis of symmetry of the parabola that models the hull of the ship. [2 marks]

The ship has a horizontal storage deck at a depth of $10$ metres, with a ceiling at surface level. The storage deck is used to transport containers in the shape of cuboids.

2. Determine the maximum width of a container that the ship can transport on the storage deck, given that the container is $10$ metres high. Give your answer to the nearest metre. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using the formula for the axis of symmetry of a quadratic function, with $a=0.5$ and $b=-6.4$, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    x &= - \dfrac{b}{2a} \\[11pt]

    &= - \dfrac{-6.4}{(2)(0.5)} \\[11pt]

    & \hspace{-0.55em}\boxed{x = 6.4}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
