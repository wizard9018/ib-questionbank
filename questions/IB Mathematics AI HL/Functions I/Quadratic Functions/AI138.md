# AI138

**Maximum mark: 6**

The diagram below shows the graph of a quadratic function
$f(x) = 2x^2 + bx + c$.

![75e0c507b59edc93cc9f79e429c580e90c045817.svg](images/75e0c507b59edc93cc9f79e429c580e90c045817.svg)

1.  Write down the value of $c$. [1 mark]

2.  Find the value of $b$ and write down $f(x)$. [3 marks]

3.  Calculate the coordinates of the vertex of the graph of $f$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $c = -30$

2.  The graph of $f$ passes through the point P$(3,0)$.
    Hence, using the coordinates of P, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(3) &= 0 \hspace{3em} \\[6pt]
    2(3)^2 + b(3) - 30 &= 0 \\[6pt]
    18 + 3b - 30 &= 0 \\[6pt]
    3b - 12 &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{4em} b = 4
    $$
    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.4em} f(x) = 2x^2 + 4x - 30
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The axis of symmetry of the graph of $f$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em}  x &= -\dfrac{b}{2a} \\[6pt]
    x &= -\dfrac{4}{2(2)} \\[8pt]
    x &= -1
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Evaluating $f(x)$ for $x = -1$, we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.65em} f(-1) &= 2(-1)^2 + 4(-1) - 30 \\[6pt]
    &= -32
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the coordinates of the vertex are
     $(-1,-32)$
