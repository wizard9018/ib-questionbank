# AI1075

**Maximum mark: 5**

An eagle is sitting in her nest and scanning the area for prey.

![eagle](images/eagle.png)

The height, $h$, of the eagle above the ground, in metres, is modelled by the following quadratic function.

$h(t)=1.8t^2-8.7t+14$, $t \geq 0$

where $t$ is the time in seconds after the eagle begins to move.

1. Write down the height above the ground of the eagles nest. [1 mark]

2. The eagle sees a dormouse and swoops down to try and catch it, however, the dormouse runs away and the eagle returns back to the nest.
    1. How high above the ground was the eagle when it turned around and went back to the nest?

    2. How long does it take for the eagle to return to the nest, from the time it left the nest? [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The eagle is initially in her nest, that is at $t=0$. Therefore her height is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        h(0)&=1.8\times(0)^2-8.7\times(0)+14\\
        &=14
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The nest is $14\,\text{m}$ above the ground.
2. 1. The eagle turns around and returns to the nest at the **minimum** point of $h(t)$ with the $y$-coordinate being the height above the ground at this moment.

        ![para1](images/para1.png)

        The eagle was $3.49\,\text{m}$ above the ground when it turned around.

    2. The nest is $14$m above the ground. By solving $h(t)=14$ we can find the time the eagle arrives back at the nest.

        ![para2](images/para2.png)

        The eagle took $4.83\,\text{s}$ to return to the nest.
