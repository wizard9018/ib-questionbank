# AI143

**Maximum mark: 6**

A right cylinder has height $h$ mm and diameter $x$ mm. The volume of
this cylinder is equal to $45$ mm$^3$.

![9aebb1d3e12c9fb1c552ed279ae0c28d1ca125c3.svg](images/9aebb1d3e12c9fb1c552ed279ae0c28d1ca125c3.svg)

The total surface area, $A$, of the cylinder can be expressed as
$A = \dfrac{\pi}{2}x^2 + \dfrac{k}{x}$.

1.  Find the value of $k$. [4 marks]

2.  Find the value of $x$ that makes the total surface area a minimum.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the volume of a right cylinder formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \pi r^2h \\[6pt]
    45 &= \pi\bigg[\dfrac{x}{2}\bigg]^2\hspace{-0.2em}h \\[3pt]
    45 &= \dfrac{\pi x^2h}{4} \\[6pt]
    h &= \dfrac{180}{\pi x^2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the total surface area is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8em} A(x) &= [\text{top \& bottom area}] + [\text{curved surface area}] \\[16pt]
    &=  2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh \\[9pt]
    &= 2\pi\bigg[\dfrac{x}{2}\bigg]^2 + 2\pi\bigg[\dfrac{x}{2}\bigg]\bigg[\dfrac{180}{\pi x^2}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\pi}{2}x^2 + \dfrac{180}{x}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find
     $k = 180$

2.  If we sketch the graph of $y = A(x)$, for $x > 0$ \[by using
    G.D.C.\], we have

    ![10ad2caa42e46f8e9db41f9ae6642694c0b81ac1.svg](images/10ad2caa42e46f8e9db41f9ae6642694c0b81ac1.svg)

    Hence
     $x \approx 3.86 \text{  mm}$
    makes the total surface area a minimum.
