# AI1112

**Maximum mark: 6**

A toy rocket is fired, from a platform, vertically into the air, its height above the ground after $t$ seconds is given by $s(t) = at^2 + bt + c$, where $a,b,c \in \mathbb{R}$ and $s(t)$ is measured in metres.

![rocket](images/rocket.png)

After $2$ second, the rocket is $28.3$ m above the ground; after $4$ seconds, $25.6$ m; after $5$ seconds, $14.7$ m.

1.  1.  Write down a system of three linear equations in terms of $a$, $b$ and $c$.

    2. Hence find the values of $a$, $b$ and $c$. [3 marks]

2. Find the height, above the ground, of the platform. [1 mark]

3. Find the time it takes for the rocket to hit the ground. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1. We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \boxed{\begin{array}{rcccccl}
    4a &+& 2b &+& c &=& 28.3 \\[3pt]
    16a &+& 4b &+& c &=& 25.6 \\[3pt]
    25a &+& 5b &+& c &=& 14.7
    \end{array}}
    \tag*{\textbf{A2}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    2. Using a G.D.C. we find $a = -3.18, \hspace{0.25em} b = 17.8, \hspace{0.25em} c = 5.53$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. Evaluating $s(t)$ at $t = 0$, we obtain the height of the platform
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{7.2em} s(0) &= -3.18(0)^2 + 17.8(0) + 5.53 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\[6pt]
&\hspace{0.275em}= 5.53 \text{ m}
\end{aligned}
$$

3. Solving the equation $s(t) = 0$ for $t$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -3.18t^2 + 17.8t + 5.53 &= 0 \hspace{10.8em}\tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\
    t&=-0.295..., 5.8925...\hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The value of $t$ must be positive
    $$
    \begin{equation*}
    t =5.89 \text{ seconds} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{equation*}
    $$
