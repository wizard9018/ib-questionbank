# AI779

**Maximum mark: 6**

A water tank has a float valve that allows water to enter while the tank is not full.  Over time, the float valve reduces the water flow until the tank is almost at its maximum capacity.

The amount of water in the tank, in litres, is given by the function $$W(t)=5\,000-C\left(1.04^{-t}\right)$$

where $t$ is minutes after the tank starts to fill.

![779](images/779.JPEG)

1.  Given that the tank was initially empty, find the value of $C$.[2 marks]

2.  State what the horizontal asymptote represents in the context of the question.[1 mark]

3.  Find the total amount of water in the tank after one and half hours. Give your answer in cubic metres. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting $t=0$, and letting $W(0)=0$, to solve for $C$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0 &= 5\hspace{0.15em}000 - C(1.04^{-0}) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[5pt]
    &= 5\hspace{0.15em}000-C \\[7pt]
    &\hspace{-0.8em}\boxed{C = 5\hspace{0.15em}000} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.

    The horizontal asymptote represents the maximum capacity of the tank, which is $5\,000$ litres.

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{R1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  First, we must convert the given time to minutes, because minutes is the unit used in the question.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        1.5\text{ hours}&= 90\text{ minutes}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Then the amount of water, in litres, after 90 minutes is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        W(90) &= 5\,000-5\,000\left(1.04^{-90}\right) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[5pt]
        &= 4\hspace{0.15em}853.45... \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Now we must convert from litres to cubic metres, using the fact that $1\,000$ litres $=1\,\text{m}^3$. We can divide the amount in litres by $1\,000$.

    Expressed in cubic metres, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    4\hspace{0.15em}853.45\text{ litres} &= \enskip\, \boxed{4.85\hspace{0.25em}\textrm{m}^3\,(3\,\text{s.f.)}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
