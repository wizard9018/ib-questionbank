# AI156

**Maximum mark: 11**

Peter wants to reach a rock, located $5$ km away
from his current position, using the strategy outlined below.

Every hour Peter approaches the rock by a fixed percent of the remaining
distance to the rock.

The distance, $D$, in kilometres, Peter has travelled from his original
position, can be modelled by the exponential function
$$
\begin{aligned}
D(t) = a + b\big(k^{-t}\big), \hspace{0.5em} t\geq 0,
\end{aligned}
$$

where $t$ is the time, in hours, since Peter started moving.

![819ba2cfeb3383629fe25649fde3301bcf8c76d4.svg](images/819ba2cfeb3383629fe25649fde3301bcf8c76d4.svg)

1.  Explain why $a= 5$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $b$. [2 marks]

After one hour, the distance from Peter to the rock is
$3.75$ km.

3.  Find the value of $k$. [2 marks]

4.  Find the distance between Peter and the rock after $4$
    hours. [2 marks]

Peter can be heard when shouting from $200$ metres away.

5.  Calculate the time it takes until someone standing on the
    rock can hear Peter shouting. Round your answer correct to **the
    nearest hour**.\
    [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have

    ![284ba0b0f6db44c91e038d4ec5486789b5846da8.svg](images/284ba0b0f6db44c91e038d4ec5486789b5846da8.svg)

    On the other hand, we know Peter will not travel more than
    $5$ km.

    Hence we must have $a = 5$.

2.  Solving the equation $D(0) = 0$ for $t$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    5 + b\big(k^{0}\big) &= 0 \hspace{1.6em} \\[6pt]
    5 + b &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{2.8em} b = -5
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $D(1) = 5 - 3.75 = 1.25$ for $k$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    5 - 5\big(k^{-1}\big) &= 1.25 \hspace{2.35em} \\[10pt]
    5 - \dfrac{5}{k} &= \dfrac{5}{4} \\[6pt]
    1 - \dfrac{1}{k} &= \dfrac{1}{4} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{3}{4} &= \dfrac{1}{k} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.8em} k = \dfrac{4}{3}
    $$

4.  Evaluating $D(t)$ for $t = 4$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.4em} D(4) &= 5 - 5\hspace{-0.15em}\left(\dfrac{4}{3}\right)^{-4} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 3.42
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the distance between Peter and
    the rock after $4$ hours is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    5  - D(4) &= 5 - 3.42 \\[6pt]
    &= 1.58 \text{ km} \hspace{1.4em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Solving the equation $D(t) = 5 - 0.2 = 4.80$ for $t$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.8em} 5 - 5\big(k^{-t}\big) &= 4.80 \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
    t &\approx 11.2 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence it takes
     $11 \text{ hours}$
    until someone on the rock can hear Peter shouting.
