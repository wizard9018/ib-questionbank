# AI877

**Maximum mark: 5**

The amount of water, $W$, in litres, remaining in a cooking pot after it
is placed onto a hot stove is given by the function

$$
W(t) = 40(0.75)^t,\quad t\geq 0
$$
where $t$ is the time in hours after
the pot is placed on the stove.

1.  Find
    1.  the initial amount of water in the pot.

    2.  the percentage of water that leaves the pot in the form of steam
        each hour. [3 marks]

2.  Calculate the amount of water remaining in the pot after $5$ hours.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  At $t=0$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        W(0)&=40(0.75)^0\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.5em}=40 \text{ litres}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  At $t=1$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        W(1)&=40(0.75)^1\\[6pt]
        &=30 \text{ litres}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        We can find the percentage
        decrease between $t=0$ and $t=1$, as follows
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Percentage decrease}&=\dfrac{40-30}{40}\times 100\%\hspace{1cm}\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.5em}=25\%
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  At $t=5$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5cm}W(5)&=40(0.75)^5\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}=9.49 \text{ litres}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
