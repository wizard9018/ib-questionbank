# AI157

**Maximum mark: 6**

In an ecology experiment, a number of mosquitoes are placed in a
container with water and vegetation. The population of mosquitoes, $P$,
increases and can be modelled by the function

$$
P(t) = 24\times 4^{0.385t}, \hspace{0.5em} t \geq 0,
$$
where $t$ is
the time, in days, since the mosquitoes were places in the
container.

1.  Find the number of mosquitoes:
    1.  initially placed in the container;

    2.  in the container after $5$ days. [4 marks]

    $\,$

$$\\[-2em]$$

The maximum capacity of the container is $5000$ mosquitoes.

2.  Find the number of days until the container reaches its
    maximum capacity. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Evaluating $P(t)$ for $t = 0$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2.3em} P(0) &= 24\times 4^{0.385(0)} \\[6pt]
        &= 24
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Evaluating $P(t)$ for $t = 5$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2.3em} P(5) &= 24\times 4^{0.385(5)} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 346
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Solving the equation $P(t) = 5000$ for $t$, find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    24\times 4^{0.385t} &= 5000 \hspace{1.9em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{15.3em} t \approx 10.0 \text{ days} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
