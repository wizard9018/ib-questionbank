# AI114

**Maximum mark: 6**

The equation of a line $L_1$ is $y = 0.5x + p$. The point A$(2,-1)$ lies
on $L_1$.

1.  Find the value of $p$. [2 marks]

A second line, $L_2$ , is perpendicular to $L_1$
and intersects $L_1$ at point A.

2.  Find the gradient of $L_2$. [1 mark]

3.  Find the equation of $L_2$. Give your answer in the form
    $y = mx+c$. [2 marks]

4.  Write your answer to **part (c)** in the form
    $ax + by + d = 0$, where $a, b, d \in \mathbb{Z}$.[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting $x = 2$ and $y = -1$ in the equation of $L_1$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.2em}  -1 = 0.5(2) + p \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    p = -2
    $$

2.  The gradient of $L_2$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{9.8em} m_{L_2} &= -\dfrac{1}{m_{L_1}} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $L_2 \perp L_1$}] \\[6pt]
    &= -\dfrac{1}{0.5} \\[12pt]
    &= -2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the point-gradient formula with A$(2,-1)$ and $m = -2$, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y - y_1 &= m(x - x_1) \\[6pt]
    y - (-1) &= -2\hspace{0.05em}(x-2) \hspace{0.25em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{2.2em} y = -2x + 3
    $$

4.
    $2x + y - 3 = 0$
