# AI125

**Maximum mark: 6**

The straight line, $L_1$, has the equation $y = \dfrac{1}{3}x-5$.

1.  Write down the $y$-intercept of $L_1$. [1 mark]

2.  Write down the gradient of $L_1$. [1 mark]

The line $L_2$ is perpendicular to $L_1$ and passes through the point
A$(2,4)$.

3.  Find the gradient of $L_2$. [1 mark]

4.  Find the equation of $L_2$, giving your answer in the form
    $ax + by + d = 0$,\
    where $a,b,d \in \mathbb{Z}$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\mathrm{B}(0,-5)$

2.
    $m_{L_1} = 1/3$

3.  The gradient of $L_2$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8.8em} m_{L_2} &= -\dfrac{1}{m_{L_1}} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $L_2 \perp L_1$}] \\[6pt]
    &= -\dfrac{1}{1/3} \\[10pt]
    &= -3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using the point-gradient formula with A$(2,4)$ and $m = -3$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y - y_1 &= m(x - x_1) \\[6pt]
    y - 4 &= -3(x-2) \\[6pt]
    y - 4 &= -3x+6 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    3x + y - 10 = 0 \hspace{6.3em}
    $$
