# AI1001

**Maximum mark: 7**

A small town is planning the construction of a new road. The new road will pass through the point A$(0,5)$ and will be perpendicular to the road connecting the points B$(3,0)$ and C$(6,6)$. This information is shown in the following diagram.

![55d55a043dfcb136022e8a71a28efdbe577c0832.svg](images/55d55a043dfcb136022e8a71a28efdbe577c0832.svg)

1.  Find the gradient of the line through points B and C. [2 marks]

2.  Hence, state the gradient of the line through points A and D. [1 mark]

3.  Find the equation of the line through A and D. Give your answer in the form $y=mx+c$.[2 marks]

4.  Point D lies on the $x$-axis. Find the coordinates of point D.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the gradient formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m_{\mathrm{BC}} &= \dfrac{6-0}{6-3} \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-2.2em}\boxed{m_{\mathrm{BC}} = 2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Since A and D lie on a perpendicular line to the line through B and
    C we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m_{\mathrm{AD}} &= -\dfrac{1}{m_{\mathrm{BC}}} \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-2.25em}\boxed{ m_{\mathrm{AD}} = -\dfrac{1}{2}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The $y$-intercept of the line through points A and D is $(0,5)$, therefore we have $c = 5$. Using the gradient-intercept formula with $c = 5$ and $m=-\dfrac{1}{2}$, we get
    $m=-\dfrac{1}{2}$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x + 5}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  As point D lies on the $x$-axis, it has a $y$-coordinate of $0$. Using the solution from part (c)  and substituting in $y = 0$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0 &= -\dfrac{1}{2}x + 5 \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{1}{2}x &= 5 \\[12pt]
    x &= 10
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore, point D has coordinates
    $(10,0)$
