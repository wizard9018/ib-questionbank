# AI875

**Maximum mark: 6**

The security personnel at the entry of a rural campground activate a
siren to alert campers when a wild animal is spotted nearby. The sound
intensity, $I$, of the siren varies inversely with the square of the
distance from the siren, $d$. When initially testing the siren, the
security personnel found that at a distance of $3$ metres from the
siren, the sound intensity is $5$ watts per square metre
$(\text{Wm}^{-2})$.

1.  Show that $I=\dfrac{45}{d^2}$. [2 marks]

2.  Sketch the curve of $I$ for $d>0$, labelling the point $(3,5)$.
    [2 marks]

The campers can only hear the siren if the sound intensity at their
location is greater than $1.2\times10^{-5} \text{Wm}^{-2}$.

3.  Find the minimum distance, in kilometers, from the siren where the
    campers can no longer hear the siren. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since $I$ varies inversely with the square of $d$, we can state 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    I&=\dfrac{k}{d^2} \quad \text{[where k is a constant]}
    \end{aligned}

    $$

    We have $I=5 \text{ Wm}^{-2}$ when $d=3\text{ m}$. We can use these to find $k$:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    5 &= \dfrac{k}{3^2}\hspace{3.8cm}\\[6pt]

    k&=45
    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Hence, we have shown

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \boxed{I=\dfrac{45}{d^2}}
    \end{aligned}

    $$

2.  Using GDC, we can plot the curve of $I=\dfrac{45}{d^2}$ for $d>0$, as shown

    ![AI875](images/AI875.PNG)

3.  Campers can only hear the siren when $I>1.2\times 10^{-5}$. Therefore

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    I &> 1.2\times 10^{-5} \\[6pt]

    \dfrac{45}{d^2} &> 1.2\times 10^{-5}\\[6pt]

    \dfrac{45}{1.2\times 10^{-5}}&>d^2\\[6pt]

    d &< \sqrt{\dfrac{45}{1.2\times 10^{-5}}}\\[6pt]

    d &< 1936.49
    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Hence, the minimum distance from the siren is $1.94 \text{ km}$
