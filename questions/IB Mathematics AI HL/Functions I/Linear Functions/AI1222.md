# AI1222

**Maximum mark: 6**

A taxi company charges based on a fixed booking fee and a rate per kilometre, depending on how far the journey is

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\bullet\;\$3.50\ \text{ per kilometre for trips up to } 4\,\text{km in distance, with a fixed booking fee of }\$8\\
&\bullet\;\$2.50\ \text{ per kilometre for trips that exceed } 4\,\text{km, with a fixed booking fee of }\$12
\end{aligned}
$$

Let $C(x)$ be the cost (in dollars) of a trip of $x$ kilometres.

1.  Write a piecewise function for $C(x)$, clearly defining its domain.[2 marks]

2.  Arnold orders a taxi and pays \$18.15 for his trip. Find the total distance Arnold travelled.[2 marks]

3.  Another customer Eddie, travels twice the distance Arnold did for their trip. Find the total cost of Eddie`s trip.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Writing the function, $C(x)$, considering the two different pricing models
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \boxed{C(x) =\begin{cases}
    3.5x + 8, & \text{if } 0 \leq x \leq 4 \\
    2.5x + 12, & \text{if } x  > 4
    \end{cases}}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Cost is \$18.15, therefore we use, $C(x) = 3.5x + 8$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    3.5x + 8 &= 18.15 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[4pt]
    x &= \boxed{2.9\text{ km}}\hspace{0.1em}\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Distance travelled is 5.8\,km, so we use $C(x) = 2.5x + 12$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    C(x) &= 2.5x + 12\\[4pt]
    C(5.8) &= 2.5(5.8) + 12\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[4pt]
    \text{Cost} &=\boxed{\$26.50} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
