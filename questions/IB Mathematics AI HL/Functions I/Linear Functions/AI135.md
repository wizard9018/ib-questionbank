# AI135

**Maximum mark: 5**

Two carpet cleaning companies have different pricing structures.
CarpetShine charges $1.80$ AUD per square metre, plus an upfront
scheduling fee of $50$ AUD. CleanMaster has no scheduling a service fee,
but the rate is $2.50$ AUD per square metre. Let $C_1(x)$ and $C_2(x)$
be the costs, in AUD, of cleaning $x$ square metres of carpet area by
CarpetShine and CleanMaster respectively.

1.  Write down expressions for the costs, $C_1$ and $C_2$, of cleaning
    $x$ square metres of carpet area. [2 marks]

2.  Find the least number of whole square metres of carpet area cleaned
    by $\text{CarpetShine}$ for its $\text{services}$ to
    be a cheaper option. [3 marks]

---

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{CarpetShine}:&\hspace{0.75em}\hspace{0.525em} C_1(x) = 1.80x + 50 \hspace{0.6em}   \\[8pt]
    \text{CleanMaster}:&\hspace{0.75em}\hspace{0.525em} C_2(x) = 2.50x
    \end{aligned}
    $$
