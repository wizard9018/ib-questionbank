# AI127

**Maximum mark: 6**

The total cost of a gym membership, $C$, in Australian dollars (AUD), in
John's Gym can be modelled by the function
$$
\begin{aligned}
C = 65t +30,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $t$ is the time in months.

1.  Calculate the gym membership cost over a $6$ month duration.
    [1 mark]

2.  On the grid below, sketch the graph of the function $C = 65t + 30$,
    for $t\geq0$. [2 marks]

    ![40ef97be540273524df8765426fe4afbd3aa99f1.svg](images/40ef97be540273524df8765426fe4afbd3aa99f1.svg)

3.  Calculate the time, $t$, in months, when the total cost reach $290$
    AUD. [1 mark]

In the neighbouring Jetts Gym, the initial payment is $$\$75$$ higher than
in John's Gym, however the monthly fee is $$\$35$$ lower per month.

4.  Find the number of months it takes for the total cost to be
    less by attending Jetts Gym in comparison to John's Gym. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $C = 65t + 30$ for $t = 6$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.9em} C &= 65(6) + 30 \\[6pt]
    &= 420 \text{ AUD}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  If we sketch the graph of $C = 65t + 30$, for $t \geq 0$, on the
    grid below, we have

    ![ffcb3caa4ff34c194a63936e0581df79e4c6a499.svg](images/ffcb3caa4ff34c194a63936e0581df79e4c6a499.svg)

3.  Solving the equation $C = 290$ for $t$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    65t + 30 &= 290 \hspace{3.9em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{12.5em} t = 4 \text{ months} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{John's Gym}:& \hspace{1.0em} C = 65t + 30  \\[6pt]
    \text{Jetts Gym}:& \hspace{1.0em} C = 30t + 105
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    If we sketch the graphs of $(1)$ and $(2)$, for $t \geq 0$ \[by
    using G.D.C.\], we have

    ![502b76a7a4ee55ede1c0b292e93ff534a6d4ff0f.svg](images/502b76a7a4ee55ede1c0b292e93ff534a6d4ff0f.svg)

    Hence it takes
     $\text{\(2.14\) months}$
    for the total cost to be less by attending Jetts
