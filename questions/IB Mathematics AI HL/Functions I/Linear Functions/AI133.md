# AI133

**Maximum mark: 5**

The data below shows the price, $y$, in Kazakhstani tenge (KZT), that
each bag of wheat flour can be purchased for at a wholesale market in
Almaty if $x$ bags are ordered.

![5a253ce60e250108cf649fd1f8dc000abcca0657.svg](images/5a253ce60e250108cf649fd1f8dc000abcca0657.svg)

1.  Find an expression for the price, $y$ KZT, of each bag purchased in
    terms of the number of bags, $x$, ordered. [2 marks]

2.  Hence find the exact total cost of purchasing $250$ bags of wheat
    flour. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  From the data we see that $y$ is determined by the linear equation
    $y= mx + c$.

    Hence we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    50m + c &= 6500   \\[6pt]
    100m + c &= 6000 \hspace{0.75em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Solving
    the system of linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $m$ and $c$, we get

     $$
     \begin{aligned}

     \hspace{8.9em} \begin{array}{rcl}
     m &=& -10 \\[6pt]
     c &=& 7000
     \end{array} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
     \end{aligned}
     $$

    Hence we find
     $y = -10x + 7000$

2.  Evaluating $y = -10x + 7000$ for $x = 250$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.8em} y &= -10(250) + 7000 \\[6pt]
    &= 4500 \text{ KZT}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the total cost of
    purchasing $250$ bags of wheat flour is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Total cost} &= 4500(250) \\[6pt]
    &= 1\hspace{0.15em}125\hspace{0.15em}000 \text{ KZT} \hspace{1em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
