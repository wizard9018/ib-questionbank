# AI132

**Maximum mark: 6**

Mehmet organises a graduation party on a boat in the Strait of Istanbul
for his peers from college. The cost to rent a large boat for the
evening is $5000$ Turkish liras (TRY) and the cost to hire a DJ is
$1500$ TRY. The cost for food and drinks is estimated to be $125$ TRY
per person.

1.  Find an expression for the total cost, $y$ TRY, of the evening in
    terms of the number of peers, $x$, attending the party. [2 marks]

2.  Find the total cost of the evening if $40$ peers attend the party.
    [2 marks]

3.  Given that Mehmet decides to collect $250$ TRY from each peer
    attending the party, find the least number of peers he has to invite
    to be able to cover the total cost of the evening. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.8em} \text{Total cost} &= \text{Fixed costs} +  \text{Variable costs} \\[6pt]
    y &= (5000+1500) + 125x \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    y = 6500 + 125x \hspace{1.2em}
    $$

2.  Evaluating $y = 6500 + 125x$ for $x = 40$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 6500 + 125(40) \\[6pt]
    &= 11\hspace{0.15em}500 \text{ TRY}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Total cost} &= 250x \hspace{9.2em} \\[6pt]
    6500 + 125x &= 250x \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{5.1em} x = 52 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
