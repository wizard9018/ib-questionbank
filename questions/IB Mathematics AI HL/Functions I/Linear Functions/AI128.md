# AI128

**Maximum mark: 6**

The amount of fuel in a car's tank, $V$, in litres, can be modelled by a
linear function, $V = mx + c$, where
$x$ is the distance driven measured in kilometres.

After driving $100$ km, the amount of fuel in
the tank is $33$ litres.

1.  Write down an equation that describes this information. [1 mark]

After driving $250$ km, the amount of fuel in
the tank is $22.5$ litres.

2.  Write down an equation that describes this second piece of
    information. [1 mark]

3.  Calculate the amount of fuel in the tank after driving
    $50$ km. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting $x = 100$ and $V = 33$ in the equation $V = mx + c$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    33 = m(100) + c \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    33 = 100m+c \hspace{0.8em}
    $$

2.  Substituting $x = 250$ and $V = 22.5$ in the equation $V = mx + c$,
    we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    22.5 = m(250) + c \hspace{0.8em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    22.5 = 250m+c \hspace{1.6em}
    $$

3.  From parts (a) and (b), we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    100m + c &= 33   \\[6pt]
    250m + c &= 22.5 \hspace{0.75em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Solving
    the system of linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $m$ and $c$, we get

     $$
     \begin{aligned}

     \hspace{8.5em} \begin{array}{rcl}
     m &=& -0.07 \\[6pt]
     c &=& 40
     \end{array} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
     \end{aligned}
     $$

    Hence, evaluating $V = -0.07x + 40$ for $x = 50$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.9em} V &= -0.07(50) + 40 \\[6pt]
    &= 36.5 \text{ litres}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
