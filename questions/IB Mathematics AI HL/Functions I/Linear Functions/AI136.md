# AI136

**Maximum mark: 6**

The total cost of a ride, $C$, in British pounds (GBP), on BlackCabs can
be $\text{modelled}$ by the function

$$
C = 1.75x + 3.00, \\[3pt]
$$
where $x$ is the distance travelled in
kilometres.

1.  Calculate the total cost of an $8$ kilometre ride in a BlackCab.
    [1 mark]

2.  On the grid below, sketch the graph of the
    function $\text{\(C\hspace{-0.1em}=\hspace{-0.1em}1.75x + 3.00\)}$, for $\text{\(x\hspace{-0.1em}\geq\hspace{-0.1em}0\)}$.[2 marks]

    ![c07ee70da58c7beac03974a72c1af86381c22994.svg](images/c07ee70da58c7beac03974a72c1af86381c22994.svg)

3.  Calculate the distance travelled, $x$, in kilometres, when the total
    cost of a ride reach $45$ GBP. [1 mark]

The starting fare of MiniCabs is $2.00$ GBP higher than of BlackCabs,
however the ride fare is lower at $1.40$ GBP per kilometre travelled.

4.  Find the least number of whole kilometres required to
    travel for the total cost to be less on a MiniCab ride in comparison
    to a BlackCab ride. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $C = 1.75x + 3.00$ for $x = 8$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.6em} C &= 1.75(8) + 3.00 \\[6pt]
    &= 17 \text{ GBP}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  If we sketch the graph
    of $\text{\(C\hspace{-0.15em}=\hspace{-0.15em}1.75x\hspace{-0.15em}+\hspace{-0.15em}3.00\)}$, for $\text{\(x\hspace{-0.15em}\geq\hspace{-0.15em}0\)}$, on the grid $\text{below}$, we have

    ![0fbc572f5386caf92a0b3a37fe873d0c550a8cee.svg](images/0fbc572f5386caf92a0b3a37fe873d0c550a8cee.svg)

3.  Solving the equation $C = 45$ for $x$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    1.75x + 3.00 &= 45 \hspace{6.95em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{10.2em} x = 24 \text{ km} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{BlackCab}:& \hspace{1.0em} C = 1.75x + 3.00  \\[6pt]
    \text{MiniCab}:& \hspace{1.0em} C = 1.40x + 5.00
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    If we sketch the graphs of $(1)$ and $(2)$, for $x \geq 0$ \[by
    using G.D.C.\], we have

    ![e7c289a4de8f1b0042a6607943738c5b0d6aeabd.svg](images/e7c289a4de8f1b0042a6607943738c5b0d6aeabd.svg)

    Hence
     $\text{at least \(6\) km}$
    is required to travel for the total cost of the ride to be

    less on a MiniCab than on a BlackCab.
