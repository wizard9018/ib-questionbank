# AI1231

**Maximum mark: 8**

Amia believes that the depletion of a particular chemical in a reaction can be modelled by the equation $V=a \times b^t$, where $V$ milligrams is the quantity of chemical remaining at time $t$ minutes, and $a$ and $b$ are positive constants.

1. Show that $V$ and $t$ are related by the equation $\ln V = t \ln b + \ln a$.[2 marks]

Amia sketches a graph of $\ln V$ against $t$ and is satisfied that it is a straight line. Amia's graph is shown below.

![ai1231a](images/ai1231a.png)

2. Determine the gradient of the line.[1 mark]

3. Hence find, to three significant figures,

    1. The value of $a$,

    2. The value of $b$. [3 marks]

4. Hence determine the amount of chemical remaining after 2 minutes. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. By applying the natural log function to both sides of the equation, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \ln V &= \ln \left(a \times b^t\right) \tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[8pt]

    &= \ln a + \ln b^t \hspace{3em}\text{[Because $\ln (xy) = \ln x + \ln y$]} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\[8pt]

    &= \ln a + t \ln b \hspace{3em}\text{[Because $\ln x^m = m\ln x$]} \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-2.2em}\boxed{\ln V = t \ln b + \ln a}\hspace{1em}...\text{as required.} \tag*{\textbf{AG}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Using the formula for the gradient of a straight line, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    m &= \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} \\[8pt]

    &= \frac{0 - 0.693}{3.11 - 0} \\[8pt]

    &= -0.2228...\\[8pt]

    m &= \boxed{-0.223}\hspace{1em}\text{(3 s.f.)} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. Our equation from part (a), $\ln V = t \ln b + \ln a$, is a straight line equation where $\ln a$ represents the $y$-intercept, and $\ln b$ represents the gradient (which we found in part (b)).

    1. We have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \ln a &= 0.693 \\[8pt]

        a &= e^{0.693} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]

        &= 1.999... \\[8pt]

        a &= \boxed{2.00}\hspace{1em}\text{(3 s.f.)} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

    

    2. We have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \ln b &= -0.2228... \\[8pt]

        b &= e^{-0.2228...} \\[8pt]

        &= 0.8002... \\[8pt]

        b &= \boxed{0.800}\hspace{1em}\text{(3 s.f.)} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

4. We have the equation $V=2\times0.8^t$. Substituting in the value $t=2$, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    V(2) &= 2 \times 0.8^2 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]

    &= 1.28

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    $$\boxed{\text{After 2 minutes, there is 1.28 milligrams of chemical remaining.}}$$

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
