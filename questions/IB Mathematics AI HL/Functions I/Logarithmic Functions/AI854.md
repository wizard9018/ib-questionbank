# AI854

**Maximum mark: 5**

The orbital velocity, $v$, of a spacecraft in km/s is given by

$$
v = -0.0098t + c \ln R,
$$
where $t$ is the firing time in seconds, $c$
is the exhaust velocity in km/s and $R$ is the mass ratio.

If $R=30$, find

1.  The orbital velocity, $v$, after a firing time of 150 seconds and an
    exhaust velocity of 3 km/s. [2 marks]

2.  The exhaust velocity, $c$, of the spacecraft traveling at an orbital
    velocity of $9.5 \text{ km/s}$ after a firing time of 150 seconds.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting in $t=150$, $c=3$ and $R=30$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    v &= -0.0098(150) + (3) \ln (30)\hspace{0.5cm}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.35em} \approx 8.73 \text{ km/s}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Substituting in $v=9.5$, $t=150$ and $R=30$, we can solve for $c$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    9.5 &= -0.0098(150) + c \ln (30)\hspace{0.5cm}\\[8pt]
    c \ln (30) &= 9.5 + 0.0098(150)\\[8pt]
    c &= \dfrac{9.5+0.0098(150)}{\ln (30)}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.35em} \approx 3.23 \text{ km/s}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
