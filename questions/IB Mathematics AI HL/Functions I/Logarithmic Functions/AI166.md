# AI166

**Maximum mark: 14**

A new class of antibiotics is studied in the medical research center.
Antibiotics are applied to a pure culture of bacteria. The number of
bacteria, $N$, present in the culture $t$ hours after the start of the
experiment are recorded. The results are shown in the table below.

![e1fb081fc41a9cc621eeb3b2ff28f126bbc93514.svg](images/e1fb081fc41a9cc621eeb3b2ff28f126bbc93514.svg)

1.  Complete the third row of the table above. [2 marks]

2.  Draw a scatter diagram of $\log N$ versus $t$, scaling the axes if
    needed. [3 marks]

3.  State the type of model that best fits the data displayed on your
    scatter\
    diagram from **part (b)**. [1 mark]

A researcher in the center claims that the number of bacteria, $N$, left
in the culture $\text{after}$ applying antibiotics can be
modelled by an exponential function of the form $N(t) = a\times b^t$,
where $a$ and $b$ are positive constants.

4.  Explain why the researcher is correct. [4 marks]

5.  Find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$. [4 marks]

4.  Using the properties of exponents, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{9.2em} \log N(t) &= mt + c \hspace{3em} [\text{by parts (b) and (c)}] \\[8pt]
    10^{mt+c} &= N(t) \hspace{3em} [\text{by definition of log}] \\[8pt]
    (10^{mt})(10^c) &= N(t) \\[8pt]
    N(t) &= (10^c)(10^{m})^t
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we obtain
    $N(t) = a\times b^t$ for $a = 10^c$ and $b = 10^m$.

5.  The equation of the regression line of $\log N$ on $t$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{11em} \log N(t) &= -0.13765t + 4.84028 \hspace{2em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using part (d), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.2em} a &= 10^{4.84028} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 69\hspace{0.15em}200
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    and
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.9em} b &= 10^{-0.13765} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.728
    \end{aligned}
    $$
