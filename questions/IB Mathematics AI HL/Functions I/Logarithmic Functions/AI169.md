# AI169

**Maximum mark: 19**

John operates a small clothing factory that manufactures jeans. John
observes that the weekly total production cost, $C$, in Australian
dollars (AUD), and the number of jeans produced per week, $N$, can be
related by the equation
$$
C = aN^b + K,

$$
where $a,b$ and $K$ are
positive constants.

John estimates that the weekly total fixed cost of
$\text{operating}$ the $\text{factory}$ is $7500$ AUD.

1.  Write down the value of $K$. [1 mark]

After analysing the financial accounting records of a particular month,
John finds the data given below.

![fbdd8eb79d6d3959b803e0d70b8e5774db6d8585.svg](images/fbdd8eb79d6d3959b803e0d70b8e5774db6d8585.svg)

2.  Draw a scatter diagram of $\ln(C-K)$ versus $\ln N$,
    scaling and shifting the axes if needed. [4 marks]

3.  State the type of model that best fits the data displayed
    on your scatter diagram from **part (b)**. [1 mark]

4.  Write down the equation of the regression line of
    $\ln(C-K)$ on $\ln N$. [2 marks]

5.  Hence find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$.
    [4 marks]

John wants to increase the production rate of jeans up to $1000$ pairs
per week.

6.  Using John's equation, estimate the weekly total cost of
    producing $1000$ jeans.[2 marks]

7.  State whether it is valid to use John's equation to
    estimate the weekly total cost of producing $1000$ jeans. Give a
    reason for your answer. [1 mark]

8.
    1.  Describe how the data must be entered into your G.D.C. to
        determine John's equation using power regression method.

    2.  Hence verify your answers to **part (e)**. [4 marks]

4.
    $\ln(C-7500) = 0.806\ln N + 5.14$ \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

5.  Using the properties of logarithms, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \ln(C-7500) &= 0.806\ln N + 5.14 \\[6pt]
    \ln(C-7500) &= \ln N^{0.806} + \ln e^{5.14} \\[6pt]
    \ln(C-7500) &= \ln (171N^{0.806}) \hspace{3em} [e^{5.14} \approx 171] \\[6pt]
    C - 7500 &= 171N^{0.806} \\[6pt]
    C &= 171N^{0.806} + 7500
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find
     $a = 171$
    and  $b = 0.806$

6.  Evaluating $C$ when $N = 1000$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} C &= 171(1000)^{0.806} + 7500 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 52\hspace{0.15em}300 \text{ AUD}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
