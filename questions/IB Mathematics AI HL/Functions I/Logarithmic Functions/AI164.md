# AI164

**Maximum mark: 7**

The half-life, $T$, in days, of a radioactive isotope can be modelled by
the function
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(k) &= \dfrac{\ln 0.5}{\ln\left(1 - \frac{k}{100}\right)}, \hspace{0.5em} 0 < k < 100,
\end{aligned}
$$

where $k$ is the decay rate, in percent, per day of the isotope.

1.  The decay rate of Gold-$196$ is $6.2$ % per
    day. Find its half-life.[2 marks]

The half-life of Phosphorus-$32$ (P-$32$) is $14.3$ days. A sample
containing $120$ grams of P-$32$ is produced and stored in a
biochemistry laboratory.

2.  Find the decay rate per day of P-$32$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the amount of P-$32$ left in the sample after:
    1.  $14.3$ days;

    2.  $50$ days. [3 marks]

    $\,$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $T(k)$ for $k = 6.2$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    T(6.2) &= \dfrac{\ln 0.5}{\ln\left(1 - \frac{6.2}{100}\right)} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 10.8 \text{ days}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Solving the equation $T(k) = 14.3$ for $k$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\ln 0.5}{\ln\left(1 - \frac{r}{100}\right)} &= 14.3 \\
    k &= 4.73\hspace{0.05em}\% \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.
        $60 \text{ grams}$

    2.  We have a geometric sequence with the first term $u_1 = 120$ and
        the $\text{common}$ ratio $r = (1 - 4.73/100) = 0.9527$.

        Hence, using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1r^{n-1}$ with
        $n=51$, we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.8em} u_{51} &= u_1r^{51-1} \\[6pt]
        &= 120\hspace{0.05em}(0.9527)^{51-1} \\[7pt]
        &\approx 10.6 \text{ grams}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
