# AI159

**Maximum mark: 4**

The sound intensity level, $D$, in decibels (dB), can be modelled by the
function
$$
D(I) = 10\log_{10}(I) + 120, \hspace{0.5em} I \geq 0,

$$
where
$I$ is the sound intensity, in watts per square meter
(W $/$m$^2$).

1.  A vacuum cleaner has a sound intensity of $1.6\times10^{-5}$
    W $/$m$^2$. Calculate the intensity level of
    the vacuum cleaner. [2 marks]

2.  A fire truck siren has an intensity level of $124$ dB. Find the
    sound intensity of the siren. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $D(I)$ for $I = 1.6\times10^{-5}$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.2em} D\left(1.6\times10^{-5}\right) &= 10\log_{10}\left(1.6\times10^{-5}\right) + 120 \\[8pt]
    &\approx 72.0 \text{ dB}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Solving the equation $D(I) = 124$ for $I$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    10\log_{10}(I) + 120 &= 124 \hspace{5.8em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{15em} I \approx 2.51 \text{ W\hspace{-0.1em}$/$m$^2$} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
