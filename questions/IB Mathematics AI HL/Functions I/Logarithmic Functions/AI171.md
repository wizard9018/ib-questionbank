# AI171

**Maximum mark: 8**

Radiocarbon dating can be used to estimate the age of an artifact made
of wood. Archeologists take a sample of wood from the artifact and
measure the ratio, $r$, of Carbon-$14$ in the sample to Carbon-$14$
in a living wood. The age, $t$, in years, is then estimated by using the
equation
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{14.5em} t &= -\dfrac{6000}{\ln 2}\times\ln r, \hspace{8em} [\text{Age Model}]
\end{aligned}
$$

where $0.01 < r < 1$.

A team of underwater archeologists found an ancient wooden shipwreck
deep down in the Black Sea. A sample of wood is taken from the shipwreck
to determine its age.

1.  Find the age of the shipwreck if $r = 0.75$. [2 marks]

In all wood samples, Carbon-$14$ decays over time. If $r < 0.01$, there
is insufficient amount of Carbon-$14$ left in the sample wood for its
age estimate calculated by the age model to be reliable.

2.  Find the oldest sample of wood for its age estimate found
    by the age model would just cease to be reliable. Give your answer
    to the nearest $10\hspace{0.15em}000$ years.[2 marks]

3.  Using the age model, express $r$ in terms of $t$.
    [2 marks]

4.  Hence find $r$ when $t = 7500$, giving your answer correct
    to $2$ decimal places. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $t$ when $r = 0.75$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.9em} t &= -\dfrac{6000}{\ln 2}\times\ln 0.75 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 2490 \text{ years}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Evaluating $t$ when $r = 0.01$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.9em} t &= -\dfrac{6000}{\ln 2}\times\ln 0.01 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 40\hspace{0.15em}000 \text{ years}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the age model, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    t &= -\dfrac{6000}{\ln 2}\times\ln r \hspace{1.7em} \\[6pt]
    -\dfrac{\ln 2}{6000}\times t &= \ln r \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.4em}r = e^{-\frac{\ln 2}{6000}\times t}
    $$

4.  Hence evaluating $r$ when $t = 7500$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.65em} r &= e^{-\frac{\ln 2}{6000}\times t} \\[6pt]
    &= e^{-\frac{\ln 2}{6000}\times 7500} \\[8pt]
    &\approx 0.42
    \end{aligned}
    $$
