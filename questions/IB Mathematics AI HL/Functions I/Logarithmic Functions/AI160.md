# AI160

**Maximum mark: 6**

The size of the trunk diameter of a chestnut oak tree, $d$, in
centimetres, can be modelled by the function

$$
d(t) = 9\ln(0.25\hspace{0.05em}t), \hspace{0.5em} t \geq 5, \\[3pt]
$$

where $t$ is the age, in years, of the tree.

1.  Find the size of the trunk diameter of a $10$-year-old chestnut oak
    tree.[2 marks]

2.  The size of the trunk diameter of a chestnut oak tree is
    $15$ cm. Find its age. [2 marks]

A chestnut oak tree can be harvested for wood when the size of its trunk
diameter reaches $20$ cm.

3.  Find the age of the chestnut oak tree when it is old enough
    to be harvested for timber. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $d(t)$ for $t = 10$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d(10) &= 9\ln(0.25(10)) \\[6pt]
    &= 8.25 \hspace{0.25em}\text{ cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Solving the equation $d(t) = 15$ for $t$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    9\ln(0.25\hspace{0.05em}t) &= 15 \hspace{7em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{11.1em} t = 21.2 \text{ years} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $d(t) = 20$ for $t$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    9\ln(0.25\hspace{0.05em}t) &= 20 \hspace{7em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{11.1em} t = 36.9 \text{ years} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
