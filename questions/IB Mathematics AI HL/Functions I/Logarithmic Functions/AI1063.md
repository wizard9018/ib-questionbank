# AI1063

**Maximum mark: 8**

John is investigating a relationship between two sets of data, $X$ and $Y$. After creating a scatter plot with $\ln Y$ on the $y$-axis and $\ln X$ on the $x$-axis, John notices what appears to be a strong negative linear correlation. The line of best fit passes through the points $(1,3.6)$ and $(6,1.6)$, as shown in the following diagram.

![ai1063](images/ai1063.PNG)

1. Find an equation for $Y$ in terms of $X$.[4 marks]

John also has some data relating the same variable $Y$, with another variable $Z$. He thinks the data can be modelled by $\log_{10}Y = m\log_{10}Z + c$. Upon investigation, John does find a linear relationship between $\log_{10}Y$ and $\log_{10}Z$, with $m=0.6$ and $c=0.2$.

2. Find an equation for $X$ in terms of $Z$.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. First, we form an equation of the form $\ln Y = m\ln X + c$, where $m$ is the gradient and $c$ is the $y$-intercept. For the gradient, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    m &= \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} \\[8pt]

    &= \dfrac{1.6 - 3.6}{6 - 1} \\[8pt]

    m &= -0.4

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Using the formula for the equation of a straight line, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    y - y_1 &= m(x - x_1) \\[8pt]

    \ln Y - 3.6 &= -0.4(\ln X - 1) \\[8pt]

    \ln Y &= -0.4\ln X + 4

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Now we make $Y$ the subject of the equation:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \ln Y &= \ln X^{-0.4} + 4 \hspace{3em}[a\ln b = \ln b^a]\\[8pt]

    Y &= e^{\ln X^{-0.4} + 4} \\[8pt]

    &= X^{-0.4} \cdot e^4 \hspace{3em}[a^{m+n} = a^m \cdot a^n] \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-0.7em}\boxed{Y = 54.6\,X^{-0.4}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. We have $\log_{10}Y = 0.6\log_{10}Z + 0.2$. Therefore,

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \log_{10}Y &= \log_{10}Z^{0.6} + 0.2 \\[8pt]

    Y &= 10^{\log_{10}Z^{0.6} + 0.2} \\[8pt]

    Y &= 1.585\,Z^{0.6}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    From part (a) we have $Y = 54.6\,X^{-0.4}$. Hence, equating the right hand side of each equation,

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    1.585\,Z^{0.6} &= 54.6\,X^{-0.4} \\[8pt]

    1.585\,Z^{0.6} &= \dfrac{54.6}{X^{0.4}} \\[8pt]

    X^{0.4} &= \dfrac{54.6}{1.585\,Z^{0.6}} \\[8pt]

    &= 34.45\,Z^{-0.6} \\[8pt]

    \left(X^{0.4}\right)^{2.5} &= \left(34.45\,Z^{-0.6}\right)^{2.5} \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-0.7em}\boxed{X = 6970\,Z^{-1.5}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
