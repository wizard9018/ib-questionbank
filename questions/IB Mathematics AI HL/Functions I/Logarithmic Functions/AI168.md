# AI168

**Maximum mark: 11**

The sound intensity level (SIL), $L$, in decibels (dB), of the sound
intensity, $I$, in watts per square meter, is given by the equation

$$
\begin{aligned}
L &= 10\log\left(\dfrac{I}{I_0}\right),
\end{aligned}
$$
where $I_0$ is
the reference sound intensity.

1.  Two sounds have intensities $I_1$ and $I_2$ with $I_1 < I_2$. Show
    that the difference

    in SIL between the two sounds is
    $\triangle L = 10\log\left(\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}\right)$. [3 marks]

2.  A train rumble has the SIL of $82$ dB and a dishwasher noise has the
    SIL of $52$ dB. Find the number of times the train rumble is more
    intense than the dishwasher noise. [3 marks]

Jack is attending a rock concert where the SIL of the music reaches
$110$ dB. During the concert, Jack's mom calls him to find out if he is
safe and everything is going well. The SIL of his cell phone ring is
$70$% of the SIL of the rock music.

3.  The perceived loudness of a sound **doubles** for every
    $10$ dB increase in SIL.

    1.  Write down the SIL of Jack's cell phone ring.

    2.  Jack perceives the rock music $k$ times as loud as his cell
        phone ringing. Find the value of $k$, giving your answer correct
        to $3$ significant figures.

    3.  Hence, decide whether Jack's mom will reach him during the
        concert if Jack carries his cell phone in the pocket and turned
        vibrating alert off. [5 marks]

    $\,$

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the properties of logarithms, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7.3em} \triangle L  &= L_2 - L_1 \\[6pt]
    &= 10\log\left(\dfrac{I_2}{I_0}\right) - 10\log\left(\dfrac{I_1}{I_0}\right) \\[6pt]
    &= 10\left[\log\left(\dfrac{I_2}{I_0}\right) - \log\left(\dfrac{I_1}{I_0}\right)\right] \\[6pt]
    &= 10\log\left(\dfrac{I_2}{I_0} \div \dfrac{I_1}{I_0}\right) \\[6pt]
    &= 10\log\left(\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}\right)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  If we let $I_1$ and $I_2$ be the intensities of the dishwasher noise
    and the train rumble, we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \triangle L &= 82 - 52 \\[10pt]
    \hspace{1.3em} 10\log\left(\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}\right) &= 30 \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[5pt]
    \log\left(\dfrac{I_2}{I_1}\right) &= 3 \\[5pt]
    10^3 &= \dfrac{I_2}{I_1} \\[10pt]
    I_2 &= 1000I_1 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the train rumble is
     $1000 \text{ times}$
    more intense than the dishwasher noise.

3.
    1.  The SIL of Jack's cell phone ring is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        L &= 110(0.70) \hspace{1.7em} \\[6pt]
        &= 77 \text{ dB}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{\triangle L}{10} &= \dfrac{110 - 77}{10} \hspace{2.9em} \\[6pt]
        &= 3.3
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        k &= 2^{3.3} \\[8pt]
        &\approx 9.85 \hspace{3.9em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3.  :::answer
        Jack's mom will not reach him during the concert b/c the rock music is too loud to hear cell phone ringing
