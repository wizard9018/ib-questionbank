# AI192

**Maximum mark: 14**

The position, $s$, in metres, of a cyclist on a straight track can be
modelled by the piecewise function
$$
s(t) = \left\{\begin{array}{lcl}
at^2, && 0 \leq t < 6.5, \\[6pt]
0.102t^2+2.48t-12, && 6.5 \leq t < 10, \\[6pt]
mt-62, && t \geq 10,
\end{array} \right. \\[4pt]
$$
where $t$ is the time, in seconds, and
$a, m \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  Show that $a = 0.1995$ correct to $4$ significant figures.
    [2 marks]

2.  Find the exact value of $m$. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the time it takes for the cyclist to travel:
    1.  $15$ m;

    2.  $200$ m. [4 marks]

4.  Draw the graph of $s$ versus $t$, showing the position of the
    cyclist during the first $20$ seconds. [3 marks]

5.  Determine the time interval when the cyclist is travelling fastest.
    [1 mark]

6.  Hence find its maximum speed, giving your answer in **kilometres per
    hour**.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The piecewise function $s$ must be continuous at $6.5$.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} a(6.5)^2 &= 0.102(6.5)^2+2.48(6.5)-12 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{4.9em} a \approx 0.1995 \hspace{2em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

2.  The piecewise function $s$ must be continuous at $10$.
    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.6em} m(10)-62 &= 0.102(10)^2+2.48(10)-12 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{6em} m = 8.5 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

3.  Evaluating $s(t)$ for $t = 6.5$ and $t = 10$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.5em} s(6.5) &= 0.102(6.5)^2+2.48(6.5)-12 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 8.43 \text{ m} \\[9pt]
    s(10) &= 8.5(10)-62 \\[6pt]
    &= 23 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    1.  Hence solving the equation $s(t) = 15$ for $6.5 < t < 10$, we
        get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        0.102t^2+2.48t-12 &= 15 \hspace{15em} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \hspace{8.4em} t \approx 8.15 \text{ seconds} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        $$

    2.  Hence solving the equation $s(t) = 200$ for $t > 10$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        8.5t-62 &= 200 \hspace{9.7em} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \hspace{8.4em} t \approx 30.8 \text{ seconds} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        $$

    using G.D.C.\], we have

5.  The graph of $s$ versus $t$ is steepest (highest speed) on the
    interval  $t \geq 10$

6.  Hence the maximum speed of the cyclist is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10.2em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}s}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\big[8.5t-62\big] \\[6pt]
    &= 8.5 \text{ m sec$^{-1}$} \\[9pt]
    &= 8.5\times3.6 \text{ km h$^{-1}$} \hspace{2em} [1 \text{ m sec}^{-1} = 3.6 \text{ km h}^{-1}] \\[9pt]
    &= 30.6 \text{ km h$^{-1}$}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
