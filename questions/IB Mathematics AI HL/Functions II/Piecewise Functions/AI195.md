## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating tax payable for the income of $$\$200\hspace{0.15em}000$$,
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.7em} \text{Tax payable} &=  54\hspace{0.15em}096 + 0.45(200\hspace{0.15em}000 - 180\hspace{0.15em}000) \\[6pt]
    &= \$63\hspace{0.15em}096
    \end{aligned}
    $$
