# AI212

**Maximum mark: 6**

A government has a piecewise function, $T$, to calculate the amount of
taxes a citizen has to pay quarterly, according to their quarterly
income, $x$, given by
$$
T(x)=\begin{cases}
0 & x \leq 5\hspace{0.1em} 000 \\[3pt]
\dfrac{(x-5\hspace{0.1em}000)^2}{50\hspace{0.1em}000}  & 5\hspace{0.1em}000 <x \leq 15\hspace{0.1em}000 \\[6pt]
\dfrac{1}{5}x-1\hspace{0.1em}000 & 15\hspace{0.1em}000<x\\&
\end{cases}
$$

1.  Find the quarterly taxes a citizen has to pay for incomes
    of

    1.  \$9 000
    2.  \$16 000[2 marks]

The government is considering increasing the tax rates and is
contemplating the following two options:

$\,$\
Option 1: Increase taxes by \$100 on all levels, and then by a factor of 1.15\
$\hspace{-7.2em}\text{Option 2: Increase taxes using the model } T(1.4x+1\hspace{0.1em}000)$

2.  Determine functions to model the taxes for each of these
    options.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating each income value in the corresponding component of the
    function, we get

    1.  $\begin{aligned}
            T(9\hspace{0.1em}000) &= \dfrac{(9\hspace{0.1em}000-5\hspace{0.1em}000)^2}{50\hspace{0.1em}000} \qquad [\text{as }5\hspace{0.1em}000<9\hspace{0.1em}000<15\hspace{0.1em}000]\\[9pt]
            &\hspace{0.3em}=320 \\
        \end{aligned}$

    2.  $\begin{aligned}
            T(16\hspace{0.1em}000) &= \dfrac{1}{5}(16\hspace{0.1em}000)-1\hspace{0.1em}000 \hspace{1.45em}[\text{as }15\hspace{0.1em}000<16\hspace{0.1em}000]\\[9pt]
            &\hspace{0.3em}=2\hspace{0.1em}200  \\
        \end{aligned}$

2.  Let's denote $T_1(x)$ the tax function for option 1 and $T_2(x)$ the
    function for option 2.

    Using the appropriate stretches and translations to the tax function
    $T(x)$, we get

    $$

    T_1(x)=\begin{cases}
    115 & x \leq 5\hspace{0.1em} 000 \\[6pt]
    1.15\left[\dfrac{(x-5\hspace{0.1em}000)^2}{50\hspace{0.1em}000}+100\right]  & 5\hspace{0.1em}000 <x \leq 15\hspace{0.1em}000 \\[9pt]
    1.15\left[\dfrac{1}{5}x-900\right] & 15\hspace{0.1em}000<x\\&
    \end{cases}

    $$
    and

    $$

    T_2(x)=\begin{cases}
    0 & x \leq 5\hspace{0.1em} 000 \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{(1.4x-4\hspace{0.1em}000)^2}{50\hspace{0.1em}000}  & 5\hspace{0.1em}000 <x \leq 15\hspace{0.1em}000 \\[9pt]
    \dfrac{1.4x-4\hspace{0.1em}000}{5}\hspace*{1.92cm} & 15\hspace{0.1em}000<x\\&
    \end{cases}

    $$
