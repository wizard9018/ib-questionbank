# AI193

**Maximum mark: 14**

The most popular mobile phone plans in $2019$ from a major mobile
service provider in Australia are summarised in the table below.

![4b6cfffce9db227a1223cd6215b239f9cf62d983.svg](images/4b6cfffce9db227a1223cd6215b239f9cf62d983.svg)

1.  The total monthly cost, $C$, in AUD, of the Optimal Data Plan can
    be $\text{modelled}$ as a
    piecewise linear function of the amount of data, $d$, in
    GB, used. Determine the model.
    [4 marks]

2.  Find down the total monthly cost of the Optimal Data Plan if:
    1.  $8$ GB of data is used;

    2.  $12$ GB of data is used. [3 marks]

3.  Determine how many GB of data is used when the Optimal Data
    Plan $\text{becomes}$ more
    expensive than the Massive Data Plan. [2 marks]

Daniela is using the Optimal Data Plan and is thinking of changing it
to the $\text{Massive}$ Data
Plan. It is the evening of December $7$, $2019$ and she
has already used $3.5$ GB of data so
far. Assume that her data usage continues
at the same constant rate during
December and the next year.

4.  Estimate Daniela's data usage in December. [2 marks]

5.  Hence explain why Daniela should change her plan.
    [1 mark]

6.  Find the amount of money Daniela will save each month in
    $2020$ if she changes her Optimal
    Data Plan to the Massive Data Plan.[2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \answer{C(d) = \left\{ \begin{array}{lcl}
    39, && 0\leq d \leq 10 \\[6pt]
    39 + 10(d-10), && d > 10
    \end{array} \right.}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.
        $39 \text{ AUD}$

    2.  Evaluating $C(d)$ for $d = 12$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.1em} C(12) &= 39 + 10(12-10) \\[6pt]
        &= 59 \text{ AUD}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  Solving the equation $C(d) = 79$ for $d > 10$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.6em} 39 + 10(d-10) &= 79 \\[6pt]
    d &= 14 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence
    Optimal Data Plan becomes more expensive than Massive Data Plan

    $\text{when more than \(14\) GB of data is used}$

4.  Daniela's approximate data usage in December is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{3.5}{7} \times 31 &= \hspace{0.525em}15.5 \text{ GB} \hspace{1em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.
    $\text{Because her current plan is more expensive than Massive Data Plan}$

6.  Evaluating $C(d) - 79$ for $d = 15.5$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    C(15.5) - 79 &= [39 + 10(15.5-10)] - 79\\[6pt]
    &= 94 - 79 \\[6pt]
    &= 15 \text{ AUD}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
