# AI196

**Maximum mark: 6**

For $2019$, the income tax rates for a resident in UK are shown on the
graph below.

![b0ad0a092577a73313e3e5cec3d37079a38fab29.svg](images/b0ad0a092577a73313e3e5cec3d37079a38fab29.svg)

1.  Write down the highest amount of income that is free of tax.
    [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the amount of tax payable on the first
    £$50\hspace{0.15em}000$ of income.[2 marks]

3.  Theresa earns £$148\hspace{0.15em}500$ in $2019$. Find the amount of
    her income tax payable.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\text{\pounds}12\hspace{0.15em}500$

2.  Evaluating tax payable for the income of £$50\hspace{0.15em}000$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} \text{Tax payable} &= 0 + 0.2(50\hspace{0.15em}000 - 12\hspace{0.15em}500) \\[6pt]
    &= \text{\pounds}7500
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Evaluating tax payable for the income of £$148\hspace{0.15em}500$,
    we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \text{Tax payable} &= 7500 + 0.4(148\hspace{0.15em}500 - 50\hspace{0.15em}000) \\[6pt]
    &= \text{\pounds}46\hspace{0.15em}900
    \end{aligned}
    $$
