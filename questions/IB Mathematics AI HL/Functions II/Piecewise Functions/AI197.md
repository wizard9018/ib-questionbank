# AI197

**Maximum mark: 14**

The downward speed, $V$, in metres per second, of a skydiver $t$ seconds
after a jump from a plane is modelled by the piecewise function

$$
\begin{aligned}
V(t) &= \left\{ \begin{array}{lcl}
60 - \dfrac{K}{t+1}, && 0 \leq t < T, \\[15pt]
12 + \dfrac{46}{t-28}, && t \geq T.
\end{array} \right.
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Show that $K = 60$.  [2 marks]

2.  Draw the graphs of $y = 60 - \dfrac{60}{t+1}$, for $t \geq 0$, and
    $y = 12 + \dfrac{46}{t-28}$, for $t > 28$,

    on the grid below. [4 marks]

    ![82e862a110791fe827f0532a883ee15963d674c3.svg](images/82e862a110791fe827f0532a883ee15963d674c3.svg)

3.  Find the time $T$ at which the skydiver opened their parachute.
    [2 marks]

4.  Calculate the speed of the skydiver after:
    1.  $10$ seconds;

    2.  $32$ seconds. [4 marks]

Assume that the skydiver's parachute failed to open.

5.  Write down the terminal downward speed of the skydiver.
    [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Solving the equation $V(0) = 0$ for $K$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    60 - \dfrac{K}{0+1} &= 0 \hspace{3.6em} \\[6pt]
    60 - K &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{11.3em} K = 60 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

2.  If we draw the graphs of $y = 60 - \dfrac{60}{t+1}$, for $t \geq 0$,
    and $y = 12 + \dfrac{46}{t-28}$, for $t > 28$, on the grid below, we
    have

    ![f1e6feb2dcd8269a36b82b80c11b2c1941011f79.svg](images/f1e6feb2dcd8269a36b82b80c11b2c1941011f79.svg)

3.  The piecewise function $V$ must be continuous at $T$.
    Hence we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.1em} 60 - \dfrac{60}{T+1} &= 12 + \dfrac{46}{T-28} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{15.15em} T = 29 \text{ seconds} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\[6pt]
    $$
    Note that the value of $T$ is the
    $x$-coordinate of the point A$(29,58)$ from part (b).

4.
    1.  Evaluating $V(t)$ for $t = 10$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{9em} V(10) &= 60 - \dfrac{60}{10+1} \hspace{2em} [\text{since $t = 10 < T = 29$}] \\[6pt]
        &\approx 54.5 \text{ m sec$^{-1}$}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Evaluating $V(t)$ for $t = 32$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{9.4em} V(32) &= 12 + \dfrac{46}{32-28} \hspace{2em} [\text{since $t = 32 > T = 29$}] \\[6pt]
        &= 23.5 \text{ m sec$^{-1}$}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

5.
    $60 \text{ m sec\(^{-1}\)}$
