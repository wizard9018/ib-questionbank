# AI213

**Maximum mark: 12**

A company that produces electronic components is investigating the
heating properties of a particular component. The company finds that the
temperature, $T$, of the component can be modelled by the function

$$
T(t)=-40(0.9)^t+90
$$
where $T$ is measured in degrees Celsius and $t$
is measured in minutes of use.

1.  Write down the initial temperature of the component.[1 mark]

2.  Sketch a graph of $T$ against $t$ for $0\leq t \leq 90$.[3 marks]

3.  Find the temperature of the component after 15 minutes.[1 mark]

4.  Find the steady-state temperature of the component after a long
    duration of use.[1 mark]

To slow down the heating speed of the component, the company increases
the size of the component in such a way that the component heats
$\dfrac{3}{4}$ times as fast as the initial component.

5.  Find a model for the temperature $T_2$ of the modified
    component.[2 marks]

Another option to reduce the rate of heating - instead of increasing the
size of the component - is to add a thermal pad to the component. In
this case the component heats half as fast as the initial component.

6.  Find a model for the temperature $T_3$ of the component
    with the thermal pad.[2 marks]

7.  Find the time taken for the component with the thermal pad
    to reach $80^\circ$ C.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The initial temperature of the component is given by $T(0)$.
    Hence, the initial temperature is
    $ 50^\circ\hspace{0.25em}\text{C}$

2.  The graph of $T$ versus $t$ \[by using G.D.C.\] for
    $0\leq t \leq 90$ is shown below

    ![1ea4bfa73ce4f9b86bdbec79080f39dd70e04a16.svg](images/1ea4bfa73ce4f9b86bdbec79080f39dd70e04a16.svg)

3.  The temperature after $15$ minutes is given by $T(15)$. We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    T(15) &= -40(0.9)^{15}+90 \\[9pt]
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 81.8^\circ\hspace{0.25em}\text{C} \hspace{2.15cm} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  From the graph in part (a) we can see that the steady-state
    temperature after a long duration of use is:

    $$
    90^\circ\hspace{0.25em}\text{C}
    $$

5.  The new function $T_2(t)$ is found by transforming the graph of
    $T(t)$ by a stretch with scale factor $\dfrac{4}{3}$ and $y$-axis
    invariant. Therefore, we get

    $$
    T_2(t)=-40(0.9)^{3t/4}+90
    $$

6.  The function $T_3(t)$ can be found by transforming the graph of
    $T(t)$ by a stretch with scale factor $2$ and $y$-axis invariant.
    Therefore, we get
    $$
    T_3(t)=-40(0.9)^{t/2}+90
    $$

7.  The graph of $T_3$ versus $t$ and $y=80$ if shown \[by using
    G.D.C.\]

    ![787dac570c08164c400ae6f2d2de11249fcfecc8.svg](images/787dac570c08164c400ae6f2d2de11249fcfecc8.svg)

    Therefore, the component reaches a temperature of
    $80^\circ$ C after

    $$
    26.3 \text{ minutes}
    $$
