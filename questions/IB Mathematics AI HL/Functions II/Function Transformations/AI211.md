# AI211

**Maximum mark: 6**

A soccer ball is kicked from the ground such that its path can be
modelled by the function
$$
\begin{aligned}
h(d) = d-\dfrac{d^2}{6}
\end{aligned}
$$
where $h$ is the height above
ground level in meters, and $d$ is the horizontal distance the ball
travels, also in meters.

1.  Find the distance travelled by the ball when it hits the
    ground.[2 marks]

2.  Find the maximum height the ball reaches.[2 marks]

The soccer player now practices kicking the ball shorter, so that it
travels half the vertical height and half the horizontal distance as the
previous kick.

3.  Determine the height function, $h_2$, that describes this
    new trajectory.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we sketch the graph of $h(d)$, for $d \geq 0$ \[by using
    G.D.C.\], we get

    ![dc03a8c401d7ff9b93bbae51a1b1a543d21e61fa.svg](images/dc03a8c401d7ff9b93bbae51a1b1a543d21e61fa.svg)

    Hence the distance travelled by the ball is:
    $6\hspace{0.25em}\text{m.}$

2.  From part (a) we see the maximum height is:
    $1.5\hspace{0.25em}\text{m}.$

3.  The trajectory $h_2$ is an horizontal stretch of $h(d)$ by a scale
    factor of $2$ and a vertical stretch of $h(d)$ by a scale factor of
    $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

    Hence, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h_2(d) &= \dfrac{1}{2}\left((2d)-\dfrac{(2d)^2}{6}\right) \\[10pt]
    &\hspace{-2em}h_2(d)=d-\dfrac{d^2}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
