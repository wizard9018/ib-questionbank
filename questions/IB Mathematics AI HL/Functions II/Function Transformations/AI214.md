# AI214

**Maximum mark: 12**

A scientist is studying the growth of a population of bacteria under
different environments. In environment $A$ bacteria grows according to
the function
$$
G(t)=-700(0.95)^t+750

$$
where $G$ is the population of
bacteria and $t$ is the time in minutes since placed in the environment.

1.  Find the initial population of bacteria.[1 mark]

2.  Sketch a graph of $G$ against $t$ for $0\leq t \leq 800$.[3 marks]

3.  Find the population of bacteria after 20 minutes.[1 mark]

4.  Find the steady-state population of bacteria after a long period of
    time.[1 mark]

To slow down the growth of bacteria, the scientist creates a new
environment, $B$, by reducing the pH level of the environment. The
scientist finds that the bacteria now grow half as fast as in
environment $A$.

5.  Determine a model for the growth of bacteria $G_2$ for this
    modified environment.[2 marks]

The scientist creates a another environment, $C$, which is rich in
proteins. The scientist finds that the bacteria grows twice as fast as
in the original environment, $A$.

6.  Determine a model for the growth, $G_3$, of bacteria in
    this protein-rich environment.[2 marks]

7.  Find out how long it takes for the population of bacteria
    to reach $600$ in environment $C$.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The initial population of bacteria is given by $G(0)$.
    Therefore, the initial population is
    $ 50\hspace{0.25em}\text{bacteria}$

2.  The graph of $G$ versus $t$ for $0\leq t \leq 800$ is shown below
    \[by using G.D.C.\]

    ![6c875d0f390136f8009b8bc56f5fb4337484b581.svg](images/6c875d0f390136f8009b8bc56f5fb4337484b581.svg)

3.  The population after $20$ minutes is given by $G(20)$. We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    G(20) &= -700(0.95)^{20}+750 \\[9pt]
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 499\text{ bacteria} \hspace{2.15cm} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  From the graph in part (a) we can see that the steady-state
    population of bacteria after a long period of time is

    $$
    750\hspace{0.25em}\text{bacteria}
    $$

5.  The new function $G_2(t)$ is found by transforming the graph of
    $G(t)$ by a stretch with scale factor $2$ and $y$-axis invariant.
    Therefore, we get
    $$
    G_2(t)= -700(0.95)^{t/2}+750
    $$

6.  The function $G_3(t)$ can be found by transforming the graph of
    $G(t)$ by a stretch with scale factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $y$-axis
    invariant. Therefore, we get

    $$
    G_3(t)= -700(0.95)^{2t}+750
    $$

7.  If we draw the graph of $G_3$ versus $t$ and $y=600$ \[by using
    G.D.C.\], we have

    ![99ecd8a838edd38c55b6acb6559f25b64ad90f4d.svg](images/99ecd8a838edd38c55b6acb6559f25b64ad90f4d.svg)

    Therefore, the population of bacteria in environment $C$ reaches 600
    after
    $$
    15 \text{ minutes}
    $$
