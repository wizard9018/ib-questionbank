# AI210

**Maximum mark: 6**

Jessica hits a tennis serve and the path of the ball can be modelled by
the function
$$
\begin{aligned}
h(d) = 2+d-\dfrac{d^2}{5}
\end{aligned}
$$
where $h$ is the height
above ground level in meters, and $d$ is the horizontal distance the
tennis ball travels, also in meters.

1.  Find the horizontal distance travelled by the ball when it hits the
    ground.[2 marks]

2.  Find the maximum height the ball reaches.[2 marks]

On her next serve, Jessica hits the ball harder and flatter, so that the
ball reaches the same height as the previous serve, but travels twice as
far.

3.  Determine a new height function, $h_2$, that describes this
    trajectory.[2 marks]

---

1.  If we sketch the graph of $h(d)$, for $d \geq 0$ \[by using

    Hence the horizontal distance travelled by the ball is:
    $6.53\hspace{0.25em}\text{m.}$

2.  From part (a) we see the maximum height is:
    $3.25\hspace{0.25em}\text{m}.$

3.  The trajectory $h_2$ is an horizontal stretch of $h(d)$ by a scale
    factor of $2$.

    Therefore, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h_2(d) &= 2+\dfrac{d}{2} - \dfrac{(d/2)^2}{5} \\[10pt]
    &\hspace{-2em}h_2(d)=2+\dfrac{d}{2}-\dfrac{d^2}{20}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
