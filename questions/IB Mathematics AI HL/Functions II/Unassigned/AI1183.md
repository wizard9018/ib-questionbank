# AI1183

**Maximum mark: 8**

Hayley is investigating the growth of a bacteria. She records the approximate number of bacteria, $N$, after $t$ hours.

After creating a scatterplot with t on the $x$-axis and $\ln(N)$ on the $y$-axis, Hayley notices a strong linear correlation during the first 5 hours. The line of best fit, shown on the following diagram, passes through the points $(0,3.054)$ and $(5, 8.094)$.

![1183](images/1183.JPG)

1. Find an equation for $N$ in terms of $t$. Give your answer in the form $N(t)=a(b)^t$, where $a,b \in \mathbb{R}$.     [3 marks]

2. Hence state the values of

    1. $N(0)$

    2. $N(5)$ [2 marks]

After tracking her data over several more hours, Hayley notices that the number of bacteria never reaches 10,000 and realises a more appropriate model would be of the form

$$

\begin{aligned}

N(t)=\dfrac{10\,000}{1+Ce^{-kt}},\enskip \text{ where }\enskip C,k>0.

\end{aligned}

$$

3. Using part (b), find appropriate values for $C$ and $k$.      [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Because we have a linear relationship between $\ln N$ and $t$, we can express this as $\ln N = mt + c$.

    The $y$-intercept is $(0,3.054)$, therefore $c=3.054$. To find the gradient, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    m &= \dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1} \\[6pt]

    &= \dfrac{8.094-3.054}{5-0} \\[6pt]

    &= 1.008

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    We can now convert this linear equation into the form required:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \ln N &= 1.008t + 3.054 \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}\\[6pt]

    N &= e^{1.008t + 3.054} \\[6pt]

    &= e^{1.008t}\cdot e^{3.054} \hspace{3.5em}[e^{m+n}=e^m \cdot e^n] \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    &= e^{3.054}\left(e^{1.008}\right)^t \hspace{3em}[e^{m\times n}=\left(e^m\right)^n]\\[6pt]

    &= 21.199...(2.7401...)^t \\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-2.25em}\boxed{N(t) = 21.2(2.74)^t\,(3\,\mathrm{s.f.})} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. 1. We have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        N(0) &= 21.199...(2.7401...)^0 \\[6pt]

        &= 21.199... \\[6pt]

        &\hspace{-2.5em}\boxed{N(0) = 21.2\,(3\,\mathrm{s.f.})} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

    2. We have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        N(5) &= 21.199...(2.7401...)^5 \\[6pt]

        &= 3274.7...\\[6pt]

        &\hspace{-2.5em}\boxed{N(5) = 3270\,\text{(3 s.f.)}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

    \end{aligned}

    $$
