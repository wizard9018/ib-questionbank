# AI090

**Maximum mark: 6**

Ali is swimming in a public pool with some of his friends. At time $t$
seconds, he $\text{encounters}$ some waves with height
$h_1(t) = 0.15\sin\hspace{0.05em}(3t)$ from big Bobby jumping into the
pool, and waves of height $h_2(t) = 0.08\sin\hspace{0.05em}(3t+1.25)$
from small Suzie jumping into the pool. Both $h_1(t)$ and $h_2(t)$ are
measured in metres.

1.  Write, in the form $h(t) = A\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\omega t+\varphi)$,
    an expression for the total height of the waves Ali encounters at
    time $t$ seconds. [3 marks]

2.  Find the times in the first $5$ seconds when Ali isn't affected by
    any waves. [2 marks]

3.  Find the first time when the waves reaching Ali has maximum height.
    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h(t) &= h_1(t) + h_2(t) \\[9pt]
    &= 0.15\sin\hspace{0.05em}(3t) + 0.08\sin\hspace{0.05em}(3t+1.25) \\[8pt]
    &= \mathrm{Im}\big[0.15\mathop{\mathrm{cis}}\hspace{0.05em}(3t) + 0.08\mathop{\mathrm{cis}}\hspace{0.05em}(3t+1.25)\big] \\[8pt]
    &= \mathrm{Im}\big[0.15e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}3t} + 0.08e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(3t+1.25)}\big]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we further have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0.15e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}3t} + 0.08e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(3t+1.25)} &= e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}3t}\big[0.15 + 0.08e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}1.25}\big] \\[8pt]
    &\approx e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}3t}\big[0.175+ 0.0759{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\big] \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\[8pt]
    &\approx e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}3t}\big[0.191e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}0.409}\big] \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\[8pt]
    &\approx 0.191e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(3t+0.409)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h(t) &\approx \mathrm{Im}\big[0.191e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(3t+0.409)}\big] \\[8pt]
    &\approx 0.191\sin\hspace{0.05em}(3t+0.409)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
