# AI202

**Maximum mark: 5**

The temperature, $T$, measured in degrees Celsius, above ground level in
Bagan, Myanmar, can be modelled by the function

$$
T(H)=37-\dfrac{H}{160}
$$
where $H$ is the height above ground,
measured in metres.\
The height, $H$, of a hot air balloon carrying tourists on a particular
day in Bagan is given by the function
$$
H(t) = 40+10t

$$
where $H$ is in
metres, and $t$ is the time in minutes after reaching cruising
height.

1.  Find the composite function $T\circ H$ and explain what it means in
    the\
    context of this question.[3 marks]

2.  Find $(T\circ H)(36)$ and explain what it means in the context of
    this\
    question.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The composite function can be expressed as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (T\circ H) (t) &= T(H(t)) \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{4mm}\\
    &= T(40+10t)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &= 37-\dfrac{40+10t}{160} \rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &= 37-\dfrac{40}{160}-\dfrac{10t}{160} \rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &\hspace{0.28em}= 36.75-\dfrac{t}{16}\rule[-5.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    This composite function represents the temperature outside the hot air balloon after $t$ minutes of the balloon cruising.

2.  Substituting $t=36$ in (1), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (T\circ H)(36)&=36.75-\dfrac{36}{16}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{4mm}\\
    &\hspace{0.28em}=34.5\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    It means that after 36 minutes of the hot air balloon cruising, the temperature outside the balloon is 34.5 degrees Celcius.
