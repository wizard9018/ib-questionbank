# AI203

**Maximum mark: 11**

A conical funnel is used to add water to a scientific experiment. The
funnel initially contains 3 L
of water, and the water flows out of the funnel at a rate of
40 mL per minute.

![8c377cf98298a7a684cfd677cfc233747ca8bc06.svg](images/8c377cf98298a7a684cfd677cfc233747ca8bc06.svg)

1.  Determine a function for the volume $V$ (in millilitres) of water
    remaining in the funnel after $t$ minutes.[2 marks]

2.  If there is $V$ mL of water in the funnel, show that the height of
    the water can be expressed as\
    $h(V)=3 \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{V}{\pi}}$ cm.

    [4 marks]

3.  Find the composite function $h\circ V$, and interpret what it means
    in the context of this question.[3 marks]

4.  Find $(h\circ V)(15)$ and explain what it means in the context of
    this question.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The volume of water starts at 3000 mL and decreases 40 mL per
    minute. Hence, we get

    $$
    V(t)=3\hspace{0.15em}000-40\hspace{0.1em}t
    $$

2.  Let's consider a cross-sectional view of the right part of the
    funnel, as shown in the figure

    ![1fa1545b27c671db53373226d01df2aa7fd58824.svg](images/1fa1545b27c671db53373226d01df2aa7fd58824.svg)

    From the diagram, using similarity, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{r}{10} &= \dfrac{h}{30} \\[7pt]
    r &= \dfrac{h}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the volume formula for a cone, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \dfrac{1}{3} \pi \left(\dfrac{h}{3}\right)^2 h\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm} \\
    V &= \dfrac{1}{27} \pi h^3 \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    h^3 &= 27\dfrac{V}{\pi} \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    &\hspace{-0.3em}h = 3 \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{V}{\pi}}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{6.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The composite function can be expressed as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (h\circ V)(t) &= h(3000-40\hspace{0.1em}t) \\[7pt]
    &\hspace{0.3em}= 3 \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{3000-40\hspace{0.1em}t}{\pi}}\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    This composite function represents the height of water remaining in the funnel after $t$ minutes.

4.  Evaluating $t=15$ in (1), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (h\circ V))(15) &= 3 \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{3000-40\hspace{0.1em}(15)}{\pi}} \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 27.4\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}\hspace{4em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{It means that after 15 minutes the height of water remaining in the funnel is $27.4$ cm.}
    $$
