# AI172

**Maximum mark: 6**

The water depth, $D$, in metres, in a harbour on a particular day can be
modelled by the equation

$$
D = 2.5\cos\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{30}\hspace{-0.15em}\times t) + 5, \hspace{0.5em} 0 \leq t \leq 24, \\[3pt]
$$

where $t$ is the elapsed time, in hours, since midnight.

1.  Draw the graph of $D$ versus $t$ on the grid below. [2 marks]

    ![19fd3a817fff397619c38bc3f6d8f3794089c50d.svg](images/19fd3a817fff397619c38bc3f6d8f3794089c50d.svg)

2.  Find the lowest and highest depths of water in the harbour, and the
    times when they occur. [2 marks]

3.  A large yacht has a draught of $3$ metres. Decide whether the yacht
    will be able to enter the harbour at $5$ pm. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we draw the graph of $D$ versus $t$ on the grid below, we have

    ![99ad31616997cbd93f3b2bcca5b2e21cec1a56b5.svg](images/99ad31616997cbd93f3b2bcca5b2e21cec1a56b5.svg)

2.  From the graph of $D$ versus $t$ on the grid above, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    D_{\text{min}} = 2.5 \text{ m}\hspace{0.525em} &\text{ when }\hspace{0.525em}t = 6, 18 \\[6pt]
    \hspace{4.2em} D_{\text{max}} = 7.5 \text{ m}\hspace{0.525em} &\text{ when }\hspace{0.525em}t = 0, 12, 24
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Evaluating $D$ when $t = 17$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} D &= 2.5\cos\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{30}\hspace{-0.15em}\times 17) + 5 \\[6pt]
    &= 2.5\cos 5\ang{10} + 5 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 2.83\text{ m} \\[6pt]
    &< 3 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the yacht
     $\text{will not be able}$
    to enter the harbour at $5$ pm.
