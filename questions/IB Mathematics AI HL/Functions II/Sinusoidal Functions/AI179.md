# AI179

**Maximum mark: 6**

The tip of a large wind turbine's blade rotates at a constant speed on a
windy day. Its height, $H$ , in metres, above
the ground is modelled by the function

$$
H(t) = 52\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{60}\hspace{-0.15em}\times t) + 80,
$$

where $t$ is the elapsed time, in seconds, since the turbine was
accelerated to its max speed.

1.  Write down the minimum height of the blade's tip above the ground.
    [2 marks]

2.  Find the height of the blade's tip above the ground after $8$
    seconds. [2 marks]

3.  Find the time it takes for the blade's tip to complete one full
    revolution. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The min value of the sine function is $-1$.
    Hence the min height of the blade's tip is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    H_{\text{min}} &= 52(-1) + 80 \hspace{1.7em} \\[6pt]
    &= 28\text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Evaluating $H(t)$ for $t = 8$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em} H(8) &= 52\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{60}\hspace{-0.15em}\times 8) + 80 \\[6pt]
    &= 52\sin \ang{480} + 80 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 125\text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  If we draw the graph of $y = D(t)$ \[by using G.D.C.\], we have

    ![279de316931380f9a5da6848695c261465d527a8.svg](images/279de316931380f9a5da6848695c261465d527a8.svg)

    Hence it takes
     $6 \text{ seconds}$
    for the blade's tip to complete one full revolution.
