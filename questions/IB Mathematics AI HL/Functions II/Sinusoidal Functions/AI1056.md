# AI1056

**Maximum mark: 6**

A Ferris wheel rotates at a constant rate in a clockwise direction. The height of a passenger car above the ground, $h$ metres, at time $t$ minutes, can be modelled by

$$

\begin{aligned}

    h(t)=a \sin (bt) + c \, , \hspace{3em}\text{where $a, b, c \in \mathbb{R}$.}

\end{aligned}

$$

![ai1056](images/ai1056.PNG)

When $t=0$, the passenger car is $75$ metres above the ground.

1. State the value of $c$.[1 mark]

The passenger car first reaches its maximum height of $135$ metres when $t=6$.

2. Find the value of

    1. $a$.

    2. $b$.[3 marks]

A ride on the Ferris wheel consists of two full rotations.

3. Calculate the how long it takes to ride on the Ferris wheel.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. We have $h(0)=75$. Substituting these values into our equation, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    h(t)&=a \sin (bt) + c \\[8pt]

    h(0)&=a \sin (b\times0) + c \\[8pt]

    75 &= a \sin(0) + c \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-0.8em}\boxed{c = 75}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. 1.  We have $h(6)=135$. Substituting these values into our equation, we have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        h(t)&=a \sin (bt) + 75 \\[8pt]

        h(6)&=a \sin (6b) + 75 \\[8pt]

        135&=a \sin (6b) + 75 \\[8pt]

        a \sin (6b) &= 60

        \end{aligned}

        $$

        Note that at its maximum, $\sin (6b)$ must equal 1. Hence $\boxed{a=60}$

    2. Following on from part (b) (i), the maximum value of a sine function first occurs when the argument is $90^{\circ}$. Hence we have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        6b &= 90 \\[8pt]

        &\hspace{-0.8em}\boxed{b=15}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

3. The period of a sine function is given by

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \text{period}&=\dfrac{360}{b} \\[8pt]

    &= \dfrac{360}{15} \\[8pt]

    &= 24

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    One full rotation takes 24 minutes. Given that a ride on the Ferris wheel consists of two full rotations, 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \boxed{\text{A ride on the Ferris wheel takes $48$ minutes.}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
