# AI180

**Maximum mark: 14**

The water depth, $D$, in metres, in a boating marina on a particular day
can be modelled by the function
$$
\begin{aligned}
D(t) &= 4\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{30}\hspace{-0.15em}\times t) + 12.5, \hspace{0.5em} 0 \leq t \leq 24,
\end{aligned}
$$

where $t$ is the elapsed time, in hours, since midnight.

1.  Write down the depth of water at midnight. [1 mark]

2.  The cycle of water depths repeats every T hours. Find the value
    of T. [2 marks]

3.
    1.  Write down the minimum and maximum depths of water
        during the day.

    2.  Find the times at which the minimum and maximum depths of water
        occur during the day.[4 marks]

4.  Draw the graph of $D$ versus $t$ on the grid below.[3 marks]

    ![65ae508d3aaa92f2c9dd08b381c4298f16693fe9.svg](images/65ae508d3aaa92f2c9dd08b381c4298f16693fe9.svg)

5.  A ship with $8$ m draught is allowed to
    enter the marina if the depth of water is greater than
    $10$ m. Find the time interval **before**
    midday, in hours and minutes, during which the ship is **not**
    allowed to enter the marina. [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $12.5\text{ m}$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{T} &= \dfrac{360}{30} \hspace{0.1em} \\[6pt]
    &=12
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.  The min and max values of the sine function are $-1$ and $+1$.
        Hence the min and max depths of water are
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        D_{\text{min}} &=  4(-1)+12.5 \hspace{0.2em} \\[6pt]
        &= 8.5\text{ m} \\[15pt]
        D_{\text{max}} &=  4(+1)+12.5 \\[6pt]
        &= 16.5\text{ m}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Solving the equation $D(t) = 8.5$ for $t$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2.9em} 4\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{30}\hspace{-0.15em}\times t) + 12.5 &= 8.5 \\[6pt]
        t &= 9,\hspace{0.25em} 21 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence the min depth of water occurs at
         $9 \text{ am}$
        and
         $9 \text{ pm}$

        Similarly, solving the equation $D(t) = 16.5$ for $t$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2.8em} 4\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{30}\hspace{-0.15em}\times t) + 12.5 &= 16.5 \\[6pt]
        t &= 3,\hspace{0.25em} 15 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence the max depth of water occurs at
         $3 \text{ am}$
        and
         $3 \text{ pm}$

5.  Solving the equation $D(t) = 10$ for $t < 12$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7.8em} 4\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{30}\hspace{-0.15em}\times t) + 12.5 &= 10 \\[6pt]
    t &\approx 7.289,\hspace{0.25em} 10.711 \hspace{2.8em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0.289(60) &= 17.34 \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
    0.711(60) &= 42.66
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the ship is not allowed to
    enter the marina
     $\text{between 7:17 am and 10:43 am}$
