# AI177

**Maximum mark: 13**

The mean depth, $D$, in metres, of a mountain lake fluctuates in a
yearly cycle and can be modelled by the function

$$
D(t) = a\cos\hspace{0.15em}(k t) + b,
$$
where $t$ is the elapsed time,
in months, since the beginning of an autumn season.

The mean depth of the lake on month $1$ is
$33.2$ m and on month $5$ is
$22.8$ m.

1.  Find the value of $k$, in degrees. [2 marks]

2.  Set up a pair of equations and find the value of $a$ and the value
    of $b$. Give your answers correct to **the nearest integer**.
    [3 marks]

3.  Hence find the mean depth of the lake on month $8$. [2 marks]

4.  Draw the graph of $y = D(t)$ on the grid below, for one full year,
    indicating clearly the minimum and maximum points.\
    [4 marks]

    ![22e9fb4574cbc4ff36d7eb0e402d77e50876a875.svg](images/22e9fb4574cbc4ff36d7eb0e402d77e50876a875.svg)

5. Determine the total amount of time in one year that the mean depth is expected to be lower than 26 metres. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The period of $D$ is $12$ months.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    k &= \dfrac{\ang{360}}{12} \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{30}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    D(1) &= 33.2 \hspace{3em} \\[6pt]
    a\cos\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{30}\hspace{-0.15em}\times 1) + b &= 33.2 \\[6pt]
    0.866a + b &= 33.2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
       and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    D(5) &= 22.8 \hspace{3em} \\[6pt]
    a\cos\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{30}\hspace{-0.15em}\times 5) + b &= 22.8 \\[6pt]
    -0.866a + b &= 22.8
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, solving the
    system of linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $a$ and $b$, we obtain

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{11.8em} a \approx 6 \hspace{1em}\text{ and }\hspace{1em} b \approx 28 \hspace{3.8em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
     \end{aligned}
     $$

3.  Evaluating $D(t)$ for $t = 8$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    D(8) &= 6\cos\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{30}\hspace{-0.15em}\times8) + 28 \hspace{0.7em} \\[6pt]
    &= 25 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The min and max values of the cosine function are $-1$ and $+1$.
    Hence the min and max mean depths of the lake during the year are

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10.2em} D_{\text{min}} &= 6(-1)+28 \\[6pt]
    &= 22 \text{ m} \hspace{1em} [\text{when } t = (0.5)12 = 9] \\[15pt]
    D_{\text{max}} &= 6(+1)+28 \\[6pt]
    &= 34 \text{ m} \hspace{1em} [\text{when } t = (0)12 = 0\hspace{0.25em}\&\hspace{0.25em}t = (1)12 = 12]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    If we draw the graph of $y = D(t)$ on the grid below, for
    $0 \leq t \leq 12$, indicating the points A$(0,34)$, B$(12,34)$ and
    C$(6,22)$, we have

    ![73e28209b510fb17f449ac18e686993a5d03e3ef.svg](images/73e28209b510fb17f449ac18e686993a5d03e3ef.svg)

5. Adding the graph of $y=26$ to our solution from part (d), we can find the points of intersection:

    ![ai177a](images/ai177a.PNG)

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    8.351-3.649 &= 4.702
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The mean depth of the lake is lower than $26$ metres for approximately $$\text{$4.70$ months.}$$
