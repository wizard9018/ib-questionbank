# AI178

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two identical water waves pass a sensor in an oceanography laboratory.
As the waves pass the sensor, the
depth, $D$, in metres, of water is recorded
and modelled by the function

$$
D(t) = 3.21-0.45\cos\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{24.2}\hspace{-0.15em}\times t)
$$

where $t$ is the elapsed time, in seconds, since the first wave hit the
sensor.

1.  Find the minimum and maximum depths of the water as the two waves
    pass the sensor. [3 marks]

2.  Find the first time after $16$ seconds at which the depth of water
    reaches $\text{\(3.5\)\hspace{0.25em}m}$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The min and max values of the cosine function are $-1$ and $+1$.
    Hence the min and max depths of water are
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    D_{\text{min}} &= 3.21-0.45(+1) \\[6pt]
    &= 2.76 \text{ m} \\[15pt]
    \hspace{1em} D_{\text{max}} &= 3.21-0.45(-1) \\[6pt]
    &= 3.66 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Solving the equation $D(t) = 3.5$ for first $t > 16$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    3.21-0.45\cos\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{24.2}\hspace{-0.15em}\times t) &= 3.5 \hspace{13em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{8.6em} t \approx 20.3 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
