# AI1213

**Maximum mark: 12**

The water depth, $w(t)$, measured in metres in a harbour is modelled by a sinusoidal function of the form

$w(t) = a\sin(bt) + d\, ,$ for $a, b$ and $d > 0$

where $t$ is the number of hours after midnight.

The maximum depth is 6 metres and the minimum depth is 0.5 metres.
The time between consecutive high tides is 12 hours.

1.  Find the values of

    1.   $a$;
    2.   $d$;
    3.   $b$. [5 marks]

2. Hence, write down the equation of the function $w(t)$.   [1 mark]

Jacinta is a member of the harbour's  boating club, which only opens between 8:00$\,$am and 8:30$\,$pm.
Her boat can only leave the harbour when the club is open **and** the water depth is less than 1.25 metres.

3. Determine the time(s) during which Jacinta can take her boat out on a given day.
Give your answer to the nearest minute. [4 marks]

The club decides to stay open later during July and August due to the longer daylight hours and now closes at 9:45$\,$pm.

4. Given these new hours, determine whether Jacinta would have more time in the **morning** or **late evening** to take her boat out in July and August. Justify your answer. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1. Finding the value of $a$, the amplitude of the function
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        a &= \frac{\text{max} - \text{min}}{2} \\[6pt]
        a &= \frac{6 - 0.5}{2}  \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}    \\[6pt]
        a &= \boxed{2.75} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2. Finding the value of $d$, the midline of the function
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        d &= \frac{\text{max} + \text{min}}{2}     \\[6pt]
        d &= \frac{6 + 0.5}{2}     \\[6pt]
        d &= \boxed{3.25} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3. Finding the value of $b$, using the period formula
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Period} &= \frac{360}{b} \\[6pt]
        b &= \frac{360}{12}    \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]
        b &=\boxed{30} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2. $\boxed{w(t) = 2.75 \sin(30t) + 3.25}$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    Using our G.D.C., we can find the time intervals where the water level falls below $1.25\,$metres,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Solving } w(t) &< 1.25 \\[6pt]
        2.75 \sin(30t) + 3.25 &< 1.25 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\textcolor{green}{t = 7.555\ldots} \quad (\text{opening is\,} 8{:}00\,\text{am}) \text{ to } \\[4pt]
    &\textcolor{green}{t = 10.444\ldots} \tag*{\textbf{M1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Converting to hours and minutes
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{-5em}\text{ Time} =10 \text{ hours} + (0.444\ldots \times 60)\text{ minutes} \\[4pt]

    \end{aligned}

4.  Updated opening hours for the club are now opening at $t = 8$ and closes at  $t = 21.75$.

    Morning interval, which represents the first interval on the graph $\color{green}{\text{(in green)}}$ doesn't change

    From part (c) the morning time interval we get is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    10:26 - 8:00 &= 2:26\\
    \text{Morning interval length}&= 2 \text{ hours } 26 \text{ minutes}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Evening interval which represents the second interval on the graph (in black) is  extended due to later opening hours.

    Therefore taking evening interval from part (c) and taking into account later opening, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    21.75 - 19.555... &= 2.194...\,\text{hours}\\[2pt]
    \text{Evening interval length} &= 2 \text{ hours} + (0.195...\times 60)\text{ minutes} \\[4pt]
    \hspace{2em}&= 2 \text{ hours } 11.66... \text{ minutes} \tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[4pt]
    \hspace{2em}&= 2 \text{ hours } 11 \text{ minutes}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Jacinta has more time available in the morning interval as $2$ hours $26$ minutes is longer than $2$ hours $11$ minutes.

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
