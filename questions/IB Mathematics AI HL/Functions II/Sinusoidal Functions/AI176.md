# AI176

**Maximum mark: 13**

The amount of water, $A$, in thousands of litres, available in a water
tank located on a farm fluctuates in a yearly cycle and can be modelled
by the function
$$
A(t) = a\sin\hspace{0.15em}(k t) + b,
$$
where $t$ is the elapsed time, in weeks, since the start of the
year.

The amount of water available in the tank on week $6$ is $24$ thousand
litres and on week $31$ is $9.2$ thousand
litres.

1.  Find the value of $k$, in degrees, assuming there are $52$ whole
    weeks in a year.[2 marks]

2.  Set up a pair of equations to find the value of $a$ and the value of
    $b$. Give your answers correct to **the nearest integer**.
    [3 marks]

3.  Hence find the amount of water available in the tank in week $42$.
    [2 marks]

4.  Draw the graph of $y = A(t)$ on the grid below, for one full year,
    indicating clearly the minimum and maximum points.\
    [4 marks]

    ![a35842049aa426b4bf5aefe8b287a6da9db53a2d.svg](images/a35842049aa426b4bf5aefe8b287a6da9db53a2d.svg)

5. Determine the total number of weeks that the amount of water in the tank is at least $24\,000$ litres. Give your answer to **the nearest week**. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The period of $A$ is $52$ weeks.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    k &= \dfrac{\ang{360}}{52} \\[6pt]
    &\approx \ang{6.92}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A(6) &= 24 \hspace{3em} \\[6pt]
    a\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{6.92}\hspace{-0.15em}\times 6) + b &= 24 \\[6pt]
    0.663a + b &= 24
    \end{aligned}
    $$
       and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A(31) &= 9.2 \hspace{3em} \\[6pt]
    a\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{6.92}\hspace{-0.15em}\times 31) + b &= 9.2 \\[6pt]
    -0.567a + b &= 9.2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, solving the
    system of linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $a$ and $b$, we obtain

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{11.5em} a \approx 12 \hspace{1em}\text{ and }\hspace{1em} b \approx 16 \hspace{3.5em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
     \end{aligned}
     $$

3.  Evaluating $A(t)$ for $t = 42$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} A(42) &= 12\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{6.92}\hspace{-0.15em}\times42) + 16 \\[6pt]
    &= 4.77 \text{ thousand litres}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The min and max values of the sine function are $-1$ and $+1$.
    Hence the min and max amounts of water in the tank available during
    the year are
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{9.3em} A_{\text{min}} &= 12(-1)+16 \\[6pt]
    &= 4 \text{ thousand litres} \hspace{2.5em} [\text{when } t = (0.75)52 = 39] \\[15pt]
    A_{\text{max}} &= 12(+1)+16 \\[6pt]
    &= 28 \text{ thousand litres} \hspace{2em} [\text{when } t = (0.25)52 = 13]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    If we draw the graph of $y = A(t)$ on the grid below, for
    $0 \leq t \leq 52$, indicating the points P$(13,28)$ and Q$(39,4)$,
    we have

    ![8814e237c9bc7b3142ac40e7f50be90d39e62a05.svg](images/8814e237c9bc7b3142ac40e7f50be90d39e62a05.svg)

5. Adding the graph of $y=24$ to our solution from part (d), we can find the points of intersection:

    ![ai176a](images/ai176a.PNG)

    The amount of water in the tank is at least $24\,000$ litres for $$\text{$14$ weeks}$$ (to the nearest week).
