# AI185

**Maximum mark: 9**

Alexandros monitors the temperature during a particular day in
Athens.

The table below shows some of his findings.

![063047b1151b8885e1ce8bf5005a5b210fdafca9.svg](images/063047b1151b8885e1ce8bf5005a5b210fdafca9.svg)

Based on his observations, Alexandros concludes that the temperature,
$T$, in $\text{\textdegree{C}}$, during the day can be modelled by the
equation
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= a\sin\hspace{0.1em}(b\hspace{0.1em}(t-c)) + d, \hspace{0.5em} 0 \leq t \leq 24,
\end{aligned}
$$

where $t$ is the elapsed time, in hours, since midnight.

1.  Write down the value of:
    1.  $a$;

    2.  $d$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $b$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the smallest possible value of $c$, given $c > 0$. [2 marks]

4.  Find the time, in hours and minutes, when the temperature first
    reaches
    $35\text{\textdegree{C}}$ during the day. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.4em} a &= \dfrac{T_{\text{max}} - T_{\text{min}}}{2} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{39.2 - 25.4}{2} \\[10pt]
        &= 6.9
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.4em} d &= \dfrac{T_{\text{max}} + T_{\text{min}}}{2} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{39.2 + 25.4}{2} \\[10pt]
        &= 32.3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  The period of $T$ is $24$ hours.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    b &= \dfrac{2\pi}{24} \\[10pt]
    &\approx 0.262
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The max temperature occurs at $4$:$12$ pm ($t = 16.2$) and the mean
    temperature occurs $6$ hours earlier.

    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.75em} t_{\text{max}} &= t_{\text{mean}} + 6 \\[6pt]
    16.2 &= c + 6 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    c = 10.2
    $$

4.  If we draw the graph of
    $T = 6.9\sin\hspace{0.1em}(0.262\hspace{0.1em}(t-10.2)) + 32.3$
    versus $t$, we have

    ![be78b702b9ccf256126ee3383eee8affe77f9461.svg](images/be78b702b9ccf256126ee3383eee8affe77f9461.svg)

    If we write $0.735$ hour in whole minutes, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0.735(60) &= 44.1 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 44 \text{ minutes} \hspace{0.65em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the
    temperature first reaches $35$ $\text{\textdegree{C}}$ during the
    day at
     $\text{\(11\):\(44\) am}$
