# AI825

**Maximum mark: 7**

The average daily hours of sunlight, $S$, during a 12-month period in a
particular town is given by the function
$$
S(t)=a\cos(bt)+d,

$$
where
time, $t$, is measured in months, $a$ and $d$ are constants, and $b$ is
measured in degrees.

The graph of $S$ versus $t$ is shown in the diagram below.

![aff649079ad71d825a6dc0770f6728fb76b7cce5.svg](images/aff649079ad71d825a6dc0770f6728fb76b7cce5.svg)

1.  Find the value of
    1.  $a$;

    2.  $d$. [3 marks]

2.  Find the value of $b$. [2 marks]

3.  Write down the values of $t$ when the average daily sunlight is $10$
    hours. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  The amplitude of the function is equal to $|a|$.

        Using the graph of $S$ versus $t$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{amplitude} &=\dfrac{S_\text{max}-S_\text{min}}{2} \\[6pt]
        &=  \dfrac{16-4}{2} \\[6pt]
        \text{amplitude} & =6 \\[6pt]
        |a| &= 6 \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        If a cosine function has a positive coefficient, it will start and end one period at its maximum value, reaching its minimum value halfway through the period. However, we can see from our graph that the function starts and ends at its minimum value, reaching its maximum halfway through the period. Therefore this cosine function must have a negative coefficient.

        Hence, $a=-6$

        $$\\[-2.5em]$$
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The value of $d$ gives the principal axis (midline) of the function.

        Using the graph of $S$ versus $t$, we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{midline value} &=\dfrac{S_\text{max}+S_\text{min}}{2} \\[6pt]
        &=  \dfrac{16+4}{2} \\[6pt]
        \text{midline value} &= 10 \\[6pt]
        d &=10 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  From the graph, one full cycle runs from t = 0 to t = 12, so the period of S is 12 months.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    b &=\dfrac{\ang{360}}{\text{period}} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    &=  \dfrac{\ang{360}}{12} \\[6pt]
    b & =30 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The graph of $S$ versus $t$ shows that $S(3) =10$ and $S(9) =10$.

    $$\\[-2.5em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, the average number of daily sunlight hours is 10 for
     $t=3$ and $t=9$.

     $$\\[-2.5em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
