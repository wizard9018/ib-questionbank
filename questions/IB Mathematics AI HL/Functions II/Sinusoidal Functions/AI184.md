# AI184

**Maximum mark: 13**

The temperature, $T$, in degrees Celsius, in Istanbul during a
particular winter day is modelled by the equation
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= a\sin\hspace{0.1em}(b\hspace{0.1em}(t-c))+d, \hspace{0.5em} 0 \leq t \leq 24,
\end{aligned}
$$

where $a,b,c,d \in \mathbb{R}$ and $t$ is the elapsed time, in hours,
since midnight.

The graph of $T$ versus $t$ is shown below.

![6960bc2052fe8ed1bdd6d4f86c2a3bd840872a33.svg](images/6960bc2052fe8ed1bdd6d4f86c2a3bd840872a33.svg)

The lowest temperature was recorded at $3$:$30$ am and the highest
$\text{temperature}$ was recorded at $3$:$30$ pm.

1.  Write down the value of:
    1.  $a$;

    2.  $d$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $b$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the smallest possible value of $c$, given $c > 0$. [2 marks]

The temperature, $T$, in degrees Celsius, in Istanbul during a
particular summer day is modelled by the equation
$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= 5\sin\hspace{0.1em}(0.262\hspace{0.1em}(t-8))+25, \hspace{0.5em} 0 \leq t \leq 24,
\end{aligned}
$$

where $t$ is the elapsed time, in hours, since midnight.

4.  Find the time, in hours and minutes, when the temperature:
    1.  reaches its maximum;

    2.  first drops below $24$ $\text{\textdegree{C}}$. [5 marks]

5.  The temperature is below $24$ $\text{\textdegree{C}}$ for
    $h$ hours and $m$ minutes. Find\
    the value of $h$ and the value of $m$.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        a &= \dfrac{T_{\text{max}} - T_{\text{min}}}{2} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{10 - 2}{2} \\[10pt]
        &= 4
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        d &= \dfrac{T_{\text{max}} + T_{\text{min}}}{2} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{10 + 2}{2} \\[10pt]
        &= 6
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  The period of $T$ is $24$ hours.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    b &= \dfrac{2\pi}{24} \\[10pt]
    &\approx 0.262
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The max temperature occurs at $3$:$30$ pm ($t = 15.5$) and the mean
    temperature occurs $6$ hours earlier.

    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.75em} t_{\text{max}} &= t_{\text{mean}} + 6 \\[6pt]
    15.5 &= c + 6 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    c = 9.5 \hspace{0.5em}
    $$

4.  If we draw the graph of $T$ versus $t$ \[by using G.D.C.\], we have

    ![22498aef2f9240c0f54bbcd77edc60575ac3345f.svg](images/22498aef2f9240c0f54bbcd77edc60575ac3345f.svg)

    1.  If we write $0.995$ hour in whole minutes, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1em} 0.995(60) &= 59.7 \\[6pt]
        &\approx 60 \text{ minutes}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the temperature
        reaches maximum at
         $\text{\(2\):\(00\) pm}$

    2.  If we write $0.759$ hour in whole minutes, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1em} 0.759(60) &= 45.54 \\[6pt]
        &\approx 46 \text{ minutes}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the temperature
        first drops below $24$ $\text{\textdegree{C}}$ at
         $\text{\(8\):\(46\) pm}$

5.  Using the $x$-coordinates of B and C, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    24 - (20.759-7.231) &= 10.472 \text{ hours} \hspace{1.2em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    If we write $0.472$ hour in whole minutes, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.3em} 0.472(60) &= 28.32 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 28 \text{ minutes}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find
     $h = 10$
    and  $m = 28$
