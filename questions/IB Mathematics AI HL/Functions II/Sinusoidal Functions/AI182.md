# AI182

**Maximum mark: 5**

PM$2.5$ particles are tiny air pollutants with a diameter of $2.5$
micrometres or less. In comparison, the average diameter of a human hair
is about $70$ $\text{micrometres}$. Air pollution from diesel &
petrol engines form part of PM$2.5$ emissions and
of $\text{particular}$ concern due to the
health impacts.

![2e913d65859b2219fd828cae63e39f9922b8ba1c.svg](images/2e913d65859b2219fd828cae63e39f9922b8ba1c.svg)

During a particular working day, the rate of PM$2.5$ emissions, in grams
per second, from all road vehicles in Beijing, China is modelled by the
function

$$
E(t) = 6\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}t - \dfrac{5\pi}{6}\right) + 9, \hspace{0.5em} 0 \leq t \leq 24,
$$

where $t$ is the elapsed time, in hours, since midnight.

1.  Find the maximum rate of PM2.5 emissions during the day in Beijing,
    China.[2 marks]

2.  Find the times at which the maximum rate of PM2.5 emissions occur
    during the day. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The max value of the sine function is $1$.
    Hence the max rate of PM2.5 emissions during the day is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.55em} E_{\text{max}} &= 6(1) + 9 \\[6pt]
    &= 15 \text{ g$/$sec}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Solving the equation $E(t) = 15$ for $t$, we have

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{3em} 6\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}t - \dfrac{5\pi}{6}\right) + 9 &= 15 \\[4pt]
     t &= 8,\hspace{0.25em} 20 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
     \end{aligned}
     $$

    Hence the max rate of PM2.5 emissions occurs at
     $8 \text{ am}$
    and  $8 \text{ pm}$
