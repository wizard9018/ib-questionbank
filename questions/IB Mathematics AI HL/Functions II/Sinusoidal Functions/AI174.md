# AI174

**Maximum mark: 8**

The average petrol price, $P$, in cents per litre, in Australia $2019$
can be modelled by the function

$$
\hspace{1em} P(t) = a\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{10}\hspace{-0.15em}\times t) + b, \hspace{0.5em} 0 \leq t \leq 365, \\[3pt]
$$

where $t$ is the elapsed time, in days, since New Year's Eve $2018$.

Part of the graph of $y = P(t)$ is shown below.

![3686d49d230056274ce98d6aa0d4dd1872a85c82.svg](images/3686d49d230056274ce98d6aa0d4dd1872a85c82.svg)

1.  Write down the value of $b$. [1 mark]

The average price of petrol on the $21$st of January, $2019$ was $139$
cents per litre.

2.  Show the value of $a$ is equal to 12. [2 marks]

3.  Hence calculate:
    1.  the average petrol price on the $10$th of February, $2019$;

    2.  the minimum and maximum average prices of petrol in Australia
        $2019$.[3 marks]

The cycle of average petrol prices repeats every T days.

4.  Determine the value of T.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $145$

2.  Solving the equation $P(21) = 139$ for $a$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{10}\hspace{-0.15em}\times 21) + 145 &= 139 \hspace{7.4em} \\[6pt]
    a\sin 2\ang{10} + 145 &= 139 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{11.2em} a = 12 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

3.
    1.  Evaluating $P(t)$ for $t = 31+10 = 41$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        P(41) &= 12\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{10}\hspace{-0.15em}\times 41) + 145 \hspace{1.8em} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 154 \text{ cents per litre}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The min and max values of the sine function are $-1$ and $+1$.
        Hence the min and max average prices are
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        P_{\text{min}} &= 12(-1)+145 \\[6pt]
        &\approx 133 \text{ cents per litre} \hspace{3em} \\[15pt]
        P_{\text{max}} &= 12(+1)+145 \\[6pt]
        &\approx 157 \text{ cents per litre}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

4.  Using G.D.C., we have

    ![30c06c40c5cf2c5d8e88197edf49a8900fddc76e.svg](images/30c06c40c5cf2c5d8e88197edf49a8900fddc76e.svg)

    Hence we find
     $\text{T} = 36$
