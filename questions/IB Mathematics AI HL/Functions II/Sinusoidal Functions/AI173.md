# AI173

**Maximum mark: 6**

The water depth, $D$, in metres, in a harbour on a particular can be
modelled by the equation

$$
D = 3\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{30}\hspace{-0.15em}\times t) + 5, \hspace{0.5em} 0 \leq t \leq 24, \\[3pt]
$$

where $t$ is the elapsed time, in hours, since midnight.

1.  Draw the graph of $D$ versus $t$ on the grid below. [2 marks]

    ![bc607b763c6a7abff0abf4c7db110b192240d1a3.svg](images/bc607b763c6a7abff0abf4c7db110b192240d1a3.svg)

2.  Find the lowest and highest depths of water in the harbour, and the
    times when they occur. [2 marks]

3.  A large boat has a draught of $2.5$ metres. Decide whether the boat
    will be able to enter the harbour at $8$ pm. [2 marks]

2.  From the graph of $D$ versus $t$ on the grid above, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    D_{\text{min}} = 2 \text{ m}\hspace{0.525em} &\text{ when }\hspace{0.525em}t = 9, 21 \\[6pt]
    \hspace{1.35em} D_{\text{max}} = 8 \text{ m}\hspace{0.525em} &\text{ when }\hspace{0.525em}t = 3, 15
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Evaluating $D$ when $t = 20$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em} D &= 3\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{30}\hspace{-0.15em}\times 20) + 5 \\[6pt]
    &= 3\sin 600\text{\textdegree} + 5 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 2.40\text{ m} \\[6pt]
    &< 2.5 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the boat
     $\text{will not be able}$
    to enter the harbour at $8$ pm.
