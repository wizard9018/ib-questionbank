# AI186

**Maximum mark: 8**

The tides at the Port of Bristol, UK, were observed by a student.

On a particular day, the range between the lowest and highest tides is
$10.6$ m and the time
difference between **high tides** is $12.2$ hours.
The first highest tide occurs at $8$:$12$ am and is
$12.5$ m high.

The height, $H$, in metres, of the tides can be modelled by the equation

$$
\begin{aligned}
H &= a\sin\hspace{0.1em}(b\hspace{0.1em}(t-c)) + d, \hspace{0.5em} 0 \leq t \leq 24,
\end{aligned}
$$

where $t$ is the elapsed time, in hours, since midnight.

1.  Write down the value of:
    1.  $a$;

    2.  $d$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $b$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the smallest possible value of $c$, given $c > 0$. [2 marks]

4.  Hence draw the graph of $H$ versus $t$, for $0 \leq t \leq 24$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        a &= \dfrac{10.6}{2} \hspace{3.4em} \\[6pt]
        &= 5.3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3.4em} d &= \dfrac{12.5+(12.5-10.6)}{2} \\[6pt]
        &= 7.2
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  The period of $H$ is $12.2$ hours.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.8em} b &= \dfrac{2\pi}{12.2} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.515
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Solving the equation
    $a\sin\hspace{0.1em}(b\hspace{0.1em}(8.2-c)) + d = 12.5$ for the
    smallest $c > 0$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    5.3\sin\hspace{0.1em}(0.515\hspace{0.1em}(8.2-c)) + 7.2 = 12.5 \hspace{10.4em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{12em} c \approx 5.15 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
