# AI183

**Maximum mark: 7**

The frequency, $f$, in hertz (Hz), and the period, $T$, in seconds, of a
wave are related by the equation

$$
\hspace{-2em} f = \dfrac{1}{T}  \\[3pt]
$$
An electronic signal is sent
out with amplitude $3$ and frequency $200$
Hz. The strength of this signal at time
$t$ seconds is given by
$$
S_1 = 3\sin(bt)
$$

1.  Find the value of $b$, giving your answer in terms of $\pi$.
    [2 marks]

A second signal of equal strength is sent out with a time delay of $2$
milliseconds. The strength of the second signal at time $t$ seconds is
given by
$$
S_2 = 3\sin(b\hspace{0.05em}(t - c)) \hspace{1em}
$$

2.  Write down the value of $c$. [1 mark]

The strength of the sum of the two signals at time $t$ seconds is given
by
$$
\hspace{3.5em} S_3 = 3\sin(bt) + 3\sin(b\hspace{0.05em}(t - c))
$$

3.  Draw the graphs of $S_1$, $S_2$ and $S_3$ versus $t$, for
    $0 \leq t \leq 0.01$, on the same axes.[4 marks]

    \end{aligned}
