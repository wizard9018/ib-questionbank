# AI1007

**Maximum mark: 19**

The temperature in New York ranges from $-4^\circ\,\text{C}$ to $22^\circ\,\text{C}$ throughout the course of a year. The temperature, $T$, can be modelled by the function

$T(t) = -a\hspace{0.15em}\text{cos}(b\hspace{0.15em}t)+d,$

Where $t$ is time measured in months from the beginning of the year, and $a$, $b$ and $d \in \mathbb{Z}^{+}$.

1. Show that

    1. $a=13$;

    2. $b=30$;

    3. $d=9$. [6 marks]

2. Sketch the function $T(t)$ for $0 \leq t \leq 12$, clearly labelling the coordinates of the maximum and minimum points. [3 marks]

3. Find the temperature when $t=4$. [2 marks]

4. 1. Determine the number of months in a year that the temperature is above $9^\circ\,\text{C}$.

    2. Find the probability that on a randomly chosen day of the year, the temperature is above $9^\circ\,\text{C}$. [5 marks]

5. If the temperature in New York ranged from $-7^\circ\,\text{C}$ to $25^\circ\,\text{C}$ instead of $-4^\circ\,\text{C}$ to $22^\circ\,\text{C}$, describe how this would affect the probability found in (d) part (ii). [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1. The maximum temperature is $22^\circ\,$C and the minimum temperature is $-4^\circ\,$C. Using the formula for the amplitude, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        a &= \dfrac{T_{\max} - T_{\min}}{2}\\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{22 - (-4)}{2} \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{-0.8em}\boxed{a = 13}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2. The period is $12$ months. Using the formula for the period we have,
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        b &= \dfrac{360}{\text{period}} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{360}{12} \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{-0.8em}\boxed{b = 30}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3. The maximum temperature is $22^\circ\,$C and the minimum temperature is $-4^\circ\,$C. Using the formula for the principal axis, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        d &= \dfrac{T_{\min} + T_{\max}}{2} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{-4 + 22}{2} \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{-0.8em}\boxed{d = 9}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
