# AI175

**Maximum mark: 8**

The average gasoline price, $P$, in cents per gallon, in USA $2019$ can
be modelled by the function

$$
\hspace{1em} P(t) = a\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{15}\hspace{-0.15em}\times t) + d, \hspace{0.5em} 0 \leq t \leq 365,
$$

where $t$ is the elapsed time, in days, since New Year's Eve $2018$.

Part of the graph of $y = P(t)$ is shown below.

![eba99471e7227e6ca3549b0274fdd498563d4121.svg](images/eba99471e7227e6ca3549b0274fdd498563d4121.svg)

1.  Write down the value of $d$. [1 mark]

The average price of gasoline at the end of $14$th of January, $2019$ was $328$
cents per gallon.

2.  Show that the value of $a$ is $-56$. [1 mark]

3.  Hence calculate

    1.  the average gasoline price on the $14$th of February, $2019$;

    2.  the minimum and maximum average prices of gasoline in USA
        $2019$. [4 marks]

The cycle of average gasoline prices repeats every T days.

4.  Determine the value of T.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The value of $d$ gives the principal axis (midline) of the function.

    From the graph we can see that $d=300$

    $$\\[-2.3em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have been given that $P(14)=328$.

    Solving the equation $P(14) = 328$ for $a$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{15}\hspace{-0.15em}\times 14) + 300 &= 328 \tag*{\textbf{M1}} \\[6pt]
    a\sin \ang{210} + 300 &= 328 \\[6pt]
    a&=-56 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \tag*{\textbf{AG}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.  There are 31 days in January, so February 14th equates to $t=31+14=45$. That is, 45 days after New Year's Eve.

        Evaluating $P(45)$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        P(45) &= -56\sin\hspace{0.15em}(\ang{15}\hspace{-0.15em}\times 45) + 300 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
        &= 339.5... \\[6pt]
        P(45)&=340 \text{ cents per gallon} \hspace{0.5em}\text{(3 s.f.)} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We found in part (a) that the midline value is $d=300$, and in part (b) that the amplitude is 56 (note that the amplitude is always positive; it is **not** $-56$).

        The minimum value and maximum value will be given by
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{minimum} &= \text{midline value} - \text{amplitude} \\
        \text{maximum} &= \text{midline value} + \text{amplitude}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        P_{\text{min}} &= 300-56 \\[6pt]
        &=244 \text{ cents per gallon} \tag*{\textbf{A1}} \\[15pt]
        P_{\text{max}} &= 300+56 \\[6pt]
        &=356 \text{ cents per gallon} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

4.  The graph we are given shows a duration of 60 days, and we can see that the function has gone through exactly 2.5 periods in that time. Therefore

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{T} &= \dfrac{60}{2.5} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    &= 24 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
