# AI1564

**Maximum mark: 15**

The concentration of a drug in a patient's bloodstream, $t$ hours after a single dose is administered, is modelled by

$$

C(t) = 12t\,\mathrm{e}^{-0.5t},\quad t \geq 0,

$$

where $C$ is measured in milligrams per litre (mg/L). The graph of $C$ is shown on the following axes.

![AI1564q](images/AI1564q.JPEG)

1.  Find $C(0)$ and $C(3)$.[2 marks]

2.  Find the maximum concentration of the drug, and the time at which this occurs.[3 marks]

3.  1. Describe the long-term behaviour of $C(t)$.

    2. Hence state the range of $C(t)$ for $t \geq 0$.[2 marks]

The drug is considered to be effective while $C(t) \geq 4$.

4.  Find the interval of time during which the drug is effective.[3 marks]

In some patients, the drug causes mild side effects whenever the concentration exceeds $2$ mg/L.

5.  Find the total length of time during which the patient could experience side effects.[3 marks]

The patient may take another dose of the drug once the concentration falls to $1$ mg/L.

6.  Find the time at which the patient may take another dose.[2 marks]
