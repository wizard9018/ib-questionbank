# AI188

**Maximum mark: 8**

The bench press world record weight, $W$, in kilograms, is modelled by
the function
$$
W(t) = \dfrac{M}{1 + Ce^{-0.04t}},

$$
where $t$ is the
number of years that have passed since $1953$.

The first bench press world record of
$227.27$ kg was set in $1953$. According
to bench press experts, the record weight will eventually reach a limit
of $350$ kg.

1.
    1.  Write down the value of $M$.

    2.  Find the value of $C$ , giving your
        answer correct to $2$ decimal places. [3 marks]

2.  The current unbeaten bench press world record weight is
    $337$ kg. Find the
    year in which this record was set. [2 marks]

3.  A young and promising powerlifter claims that he is going to break
    any bench press world record performed until $2025$. Find the weight
    that he has to bench press in $2025$ to set a new bench press world
    record according to the model. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $M = 350$

    2.  Solving the equation $W(0) = 227.27$ for $C$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{350}{1 + Ce^{-0.04(0)}} &= 227.27 \hspace{5.6em} \\[6pt]
        \dfrac{350}{1 + C} &= 227.27 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \hspace{9.6em} C \approx 0.54 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        $$

2.  Solving the equation $W(t) = 337$ for $t$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.9em} \dfrac{350}{1 + 0.54e^{-0.04t}} &= 337 \\[6pt]
    t &\approx 66 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we have

    ![3995fc167568b86787ae79ed98ca134320f14178.svg](images/3995fc167568b86787ae79ed98ca134320f14178.svg)

    Hence the current unbeaten bench press world record was set in
     $2019$

3.  We have

    ![00dae124260e7339716684c3dd5812e8ff1deace.svg](images/00dae124260e7339716684c3dd5812e8ff1deace.svg)

    Hence, evaluating $W(t)$ for $t = 72$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.7em} W(72) &= \dfrac{350}{1 + 0.54e^{-0.04(72)}} \\[9pt]
    &\approx 340 \text{ kg}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
