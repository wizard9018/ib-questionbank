# AI191

**Maximum mark: 7**

A population of $40$ rabbits are brought to an island at the end of
December, $2018$.

The population, $P$, of rabbits on the island can be modelled by the
logistic function
$$
\begin{aligned}
P(t) &= \dfrac{M}{1 + Ce^{-0.3t}}\hspace{0.05em},
\end{aligned}
$$
where
$t$ is the number of months that have passed since New Year Eve $2019$.

After $12$ months, the population of rabbits on the island is increased
to $1423$.

1.  Find the value of $M$ and the value of $C$. [4 marks]

2.  Write down the largest population of rabbits that the island will
    ever be able to accommodate.[1 mark]

3.  Hence calculate the number of months it takes for the population of
    rabbits to reach half of its largest
    population.\
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we solve the equation $P(0) = 40$ for $M$ (in terms of $C$), we
    have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{M}{1 + Ce^{-0.3(0)}} &= 40 \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{M}{1 + C} &= 40 \\[12pt]
    M &= 40(1+C) \hspace{2.5em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, solving the equation $P(12) = 1423$ for $C$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.6em} \dfrac{M}{1 + Ce^{-0.3(12)}} &= 1423 \\[4pt]
    \dfrac{40(1+C)}{1 + Ce^{-0.3(12)}} &= 1423 \hspace{3em} [\text{by $(1)$}] \\[10pt]
    C &\approx 1236.65 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    C \approx 1240 \hspace{0.8em}
    $$

    Hence, substituting $C \approx 1236.65$ in the equation $(1)$, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.7em} M &= 40(1+1236.65) \\[6pt]
    &= 49\hspace{0.15em}506 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 49\hspace{0.15em}500
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    $49\hspace{0.15em}500$

3.  Solving the equation $P(t) = 49\hspace{0.15em}500(0.5)$ for $t$, we
    find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{49\hspace{0.15em}500}{1 + 1240e^{-0.3t}} &= 24\hspace{0.15em}750 \hspace{5.8em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{13.6em}  t = 23.7 \text{ months} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
