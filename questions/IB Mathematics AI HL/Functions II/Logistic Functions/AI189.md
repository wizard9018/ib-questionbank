# AI189

**Maximum mark: 8**

An elephant raised in a wildlife sanctuary. Her height, $H$, in metres,
is modelled by the logistic function
$$
\begin{aligned}
H(t) &= \dfrac{2.5}{1+1.5e^{-0.25t}}\hspace{0.05em},
\end{aligned}
$$

where $t$ is the number of years that have passed since her birth.

1.  Find the elephant's height:
    1.  when she was born;

    2.  on her $5$th birthday. [4 marks]

The elephant's height approaches a limit of $M$ metres as she ages.

2.  Write down the value of $M$. [1 mark]

The elephant reaches a height of $2$ m before
her $n$th birthday.

3.  Find the smallest value of $n$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Evaluating $H(t)$ for $t = 0$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.2em} H(0) &= \dfrac{2.5}{1+1.5e^{-0.25(0)}} \\[9pt]
        &= 1 \text{ m}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Evaluating $H(t)$ for $t = 5$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.2em} H(5) &= \dfrac{2.5}{1+1.5e^{-0.25(5)}} \\[9pt]
        &\approx 1.75 \text{ m}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    $M = 2.5$

3.  Solving the equation $H(t) = 2$ for $t$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.1em} \dfrac{2.5}{1+1.5e^{-0.25t}} &= 2 \\[6pt]
    t &\approx 7.17 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we find
     $n = 8$
