# AI190

**Maximum mark: 12**

Farmer Jimmy bought $50$ goats and brought them to his farm. He
determined that $10$ of these new goats have a viral disease. The total
number of goats on Jimmy's farm is now $600$.

The spread of the viral disease on Jimmy's farm is modelled by the
logistic function
$$
\begin{aligned}
S(d) &= \dfrac{M}{1 + Ce^{-kd}}\hspace{0.05em},
\end{aligned}
$$
where
$S(d)$ is the number of infected goats after $d$ epidemic days.

1.
    1.  Write down the value of $M$.

    2.  Find the value of $C$. [3 marks]

2.  Given that quarter of the goats are infected after $12$ days, find
    the value of $k$, giving your answer correct to $4$ significant
    figures.[2 marks]

3.  Find the number of days it takes for $90$% of the goats to get
    infected. [2 marks]

4.  Draw the graph of $y= S(d)$ on the axes below, indicating clearly
    the point of inflexion and any asymptotes. [5 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $M = 600$

    2.  Solving the equation $S(0) = 10$ for $C$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{600}{1 + Ce^{-k(0)}} &= 10 \hspace{8.9em} \\[6pt]
        \dfrac{600}{1 + C} &= 10 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \hspace{6.3em} C = 59 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        $$

2.  Solving the equation $S(12) = 600(0.25)$ for $k$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{600}{1 + 59e^{-k(12)}} &= 150 \hspace{6.8em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{10.6em} k \approx 0.2482 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $S(d) = 600(0.9)$ for $d$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{600}{1 + 59e^{-0.2482d}} &= 540 \hspace{7.6em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{11.9em} d \approx 25.3 \text{ days} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

4.  The $d$-coordinate of the point of inflexion of the graph of
    $y = S(d)$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.1em} d &= \dfrac{1}{k}\times\ln\hspace{-0.1em}\left(\dfrac{M-S_0}{S_0}\right) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{0.2482}\times\ln\hspace{-0.1em}\left(\dfrac{600-10}{10}\right) \\[12pt]
    &\approx 16.4
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The $y$-coordinate of the point of
    inflexion of the graph of $y = S(d)$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= \dfrac{M}{2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{600}{2} \\[11pt]
    &= 300 \hspace{3em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $y= S(d)$ on the axes below, indicating clearly the point
    P$(16.4,300)$ and the horizontal asymptote $y = 600$, we have
