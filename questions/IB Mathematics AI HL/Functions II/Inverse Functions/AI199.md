# AI199

**Maximum mark: 5**

The perimeter, $P$, of a given rectangle can be represented by the
function
$$
\begin{aligned}
P(A) &= 6\sqrt{\dfrac{A}{2}}, \hspace{0.5em} A \geq 0,
\end{aligned}
$$

where $A$ is the area of the rectangle.

The graph of the function $P$, for $0 \leq A \leq 24$, is shown below.

![13be64c3a363091e834dcc3848fda5575c4c9b07.svg](images/13be64c3a363091e834dcc3848fda5575c4c9b07.svg)

1.  Find the value of $P(24)$. [1 mark]

2.  On the grid above, draw the graph of the inverse function, $P^{-1}$.
    [3 marks]

3.  In the context of the question, explain the meaning of
    $P^{-1}(12) = 8$. [1 mark]
