# AI200

**Maximum mark: 5**

The circumference, $C$, of a given circle can be represented by the
function
$$
\begin{aligned}
C(A) &= 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{A}{\pi}}, \hspace{0.5em} A \geq 0,
\end{aligned}
$$

where $A$ is the area of the circle.

The graph of the function $C$, for $0 \leq A \leq 24$, is shown below.

![3388e210bb4d6f02426816cb2651396f2822c4c1.svg](images/3388e210bb4d6f02426816cb2651396f2822c4c1.svg)

1.  Find the value of $C(24)$. [1 mark]

2.  On the grid above, draw the graph of the inverse function, $C^{-1}$.
    [3 marks]

3.  In the context of the question, explain the meaning of
    $C^{-1}(2\pi) = \pi$. [1 mark]
