# AI736

**Maximum mark: 8**

Consider the following composite shape, consisting of a rectangle of
length 50 cm and varying width
$x$ cm, and three-quarter circle with its center
at a vertex of the rectangle and radius equal to the rectangle width,
$x$ cm.

![9ed23f8a95d65fb8498a74d7b3a0d8921a8b6a79.svg](images/9ed23f8a95d65fb8498a74d7b3a0d8921a8b6a79.svg)

1.  Determine a function, $L$, in terms of $x$, for the total length of
    line seen in the diagram. This consists of both the perimeter of the
    rectangle and circumference of the three-quarter circle.[2 marks]

Constraints are placed on the dimensions of the composite shape. The
total length and width of the composite shape cannot exceed
$100$ cm and $80$ cm
respectively.

2.  Determine the domain and range of $L$, taking into
    consideration these constraints.[2 marks]

3.  Find an equation for the inverse function $L^{-1}(x)$.
    Express your answer in the form $L^{-1}(x)=mx+c$.[2 marks]

Suppose $L$ can only be in multiples of $100$ cm
(i.e., $100$ cm,
$200$ cm, $300$ cm,
\...).

4.  Determine the maximum length of $L$ that satisfies the
    constraints.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The total length of the line, $L$, is the sum of the rectangle
    perimeter and $3/4$'s of the circumference of a circle of radius
    $x$. We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    L(x) &= 2(50) + 2x + \dfrac{3}{4}(2\pi x) \\[7pt]
    &= 100 + 2x + \dfrac{3\pi x}{2} \\[7pt]
    &\hspace{-2.2em}L(x) = 100+\left(2+\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\right)x
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Since the total length cannot exceed
    $100$ cm, we get $50+x\leq 100$. Similarly,
    the total width cannot exceed $80$ cm,
    therefore $2x\leq 80$. Hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Domain: } 0 < x \leq 40.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The
    function $L(x)$ is increasing, $L(0)=100$, and $L(40)=180+60\pi$.
    Hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Range: } 100 < L(x) \leq 180+60\pi.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Let's denote $L(x)$ by $y$. To find the inverse we can interchange
    the variables $x$ and $y$ and rearrange for $y$, as follows

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 100+\left(2+\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\right)x\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
    x &= 100+\left(2+\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\right)y\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    y &= \dfrac{2(x-100)}{4+3\pi}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &\hspace{-3.2em}L^{-1}(x) = \dfrac{2x}{4+3\pi}-\dfrac{200}{4+3\pi}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  For each of the possible values of $L$, we can find the radius value
    $x$ since $x=L^{-1}(L)$. The length and width of the composite shape
    can then be calculated and compared against the constraints, as
    shown in the following table.

    ![881fb34d4c0132d6f520788601f45a7a6a476a82.svg](images/881fb34d4c0132d6f520788601f45a7a6a476a82.svg)

    From the table, we can see that the largest line length of the
    options that satisfies the constraints is

    $$
    L=300\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}.
    $$
