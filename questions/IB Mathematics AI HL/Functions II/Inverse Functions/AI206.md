# AI206

**Maximum mark: 6**

James goes on a cross-country bike ride which comprises of
8 km of flat terrain followed by
12 km of constant uphill sloping terrain. We
know James can cycle at a speed of 24 km/hr in
flat terrain, however we don't know his speed on sloping uphill terrain,
and therefore denote this by $x$ km/hr.

1.  Show his average speed over the entire ride, in terms of $x$, can be
    expressed as[2 marks]
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S(x)=\dfrac{60x}{x+36}\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Find $S^{-1}(x)$.[2 marks]

3.  Hence find the speed he needs cycle over the sloping uphill terrain
    in order to obtain an average speed of
    20 km/hr for the whole ride.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's denote by $A$ the flat path of 8 km
    and by $B$ the sloping path of 12 km as in
    the figure.

    ![51afddbd3335432582ce0a7448d0ba409510d7ad.svg](images/51afddbd3335432582ce0a7448d0ba409510d7ad.svg)

    First, we find the time it takes James to cycle the path $A$. From
    the speed formula in terms of distance and time, we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    t_A &= \dfrac{d_A}{V_A} = \dfrac{8}{24} = \dfrac{1}{3}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    \intertext{Similarly, the time required to cycle $B$ is}
    t_B &= \dfrac{d_B}{V_B} = \dfrac{12}{x}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    \intertext{Finally, we can find the average speed in terms of $x$ as follows}
    S(x) &= \dfrac{d_A+d_B}{t_A+t_B}\rule[-5.5mm]{0pt}{13mm}\\
    &= \dfrac{20}{\mbox{\small$\dfrac{1}{3}$}+\mbox{\small$\dfrac{12}{x}$}} \rule[-7.5mm]{0pt}{13mm}\\
    &= \dfrac{20}{\mbox{\small$\dfrac{x+36}{3x}$}}\rule[-7.5mm]{0pt}{13mm}\\
    &\hspace{-2em}S(x)= \dfrac{60x}{x+36}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We can find the inverse of S(x) by using expression (1), changing
    $S(x)$ to $y$, interchanging $x$ and $y$, and then rearranging for
    $y$. We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= \dfrac{60x}{x+36}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{3.5mm}\\
    x &= \dfrac{60y}{y+36}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    xy+36x &= 60y \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    60y-xy &= 36x \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    y&= \dfrac{36x}{60-x}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \intertext{Hence, the inverse function is}
    &\hspace{-3em}S^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{36x}{60-x}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The speed for the uphill sloping terrain required to obtain an
    average speed of 20 km/hr for the whole ride
    can be found by $S^{-1}(20)$. We get

    $$
    S^{-1}(20) = \dfrac{36(20)}{60-20} = 18.
    $$
    Therefore, James needs
    to cycle at a speed of
    $18\hspace{0.25em}\text{km/hr}$
    over the uphill sloping terrain.
