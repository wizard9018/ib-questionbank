# AI882

**Maximum mark: 5**

A function is defined by $f(x)=\sqrt{x+5}$, $x\geq -5$

1.  Determine the domain and range of $f$. [3 marks]

2.  Find the value of $f^{-1}(\sqrt{6})$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The graph of $f(x)=\sqrt{x+5}$ is plotted below.

    ![1805e337e073512237286acb5583906bdf31bb84.svg](images/1805e337e073512237286acb5583906bdf31bb84.svg)

    From the graph, the domain of $f$ is
    $$
    [-5,\infty)
    $$

    and the range of $f$ is
    $$
    [0,\infty)
    $$

2.  $f^{-1}(\sqrt{6})$ corresponds to the value of $x$ when
    $f(x)=\sqrt{6}$.

    ![7c19742e74bbe79b3eeb3b8509335ac2df5057a0.svg](images/7c19742e74bbe79b3eeb3b8509335ac2df5057a0.svg)

    From the graph, $f(x)=\sqrt{6}$ when $x=1$. Hence,

    $$
    f^{-1}(\sqrt{6})=1
    $$
