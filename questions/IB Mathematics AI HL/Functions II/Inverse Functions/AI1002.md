# AI1002

**Maximum mark: 7**

The radius of a cylindrical container with height
$x$ cm is
$$
\begin{aligned}
r(x) = \dfrac{56}{x},\qquad 4\leq x\leq 16
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Find the range of $r$.[3 marks]

The function $r^{-1}$ is the inverse function of
$r$.

2.
    1.  Find $r^{-1}(8)$.

    2.  Interpret the answer to **part (b) (i)** in
        context.

    3.  Find the range of $r^{-1}$.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  A sketch of $r(x)$ shows that the minimum value is found where $x=16$ and the maximum value is found where $x=4$.

    ![AI1002a](images/AI1002a.PNG)

    Evaluating $r$ at the endpoints of its domain, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r(4) &= \dfrac{56}{4} = 14 \\[11pt]
    r(16) &= \dfrac{56}{16} = 3.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Therefore the range of $r$ is $3.5 \leq r \leq 14$

2. 1. Let $r^{-1}(8)=a$. This implies that $r(a)=8$.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{56}{a} &= 8 \\[11pt]
        a &= \dfrac{56}{8} \\[11pt]
        a &= 7
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $r(7)=8$ and therefore $r^{-1}(8) = 7$

    2.  :::answer
        When the radius of the container is $8\,$cm the height is $7\,$cm

    3. Using the definition of a function inverse, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{range of $r^{-1}$} &= \text{domain of $r$}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Therefore the range of $r^{-1}$ is $4 \leq r^{-1} \leq 16$
