# AI881

**Maximum mark: 5**

A function is defined by $g(x)=3-\dfrac{12}{x+3}$ for $-9\leq x\leq 9$,
$x\neq -3$

1.  Find the range of $g$. [3 marks]

2.  Find the value of $g^{-1}(0)$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The graph of $g(x)=3-\dfrac{12}{x+3}$ is plotted below.

    ![AI881a](images/AI881a.PNG)

    From the graph, the range of $g$ is $(-\infty,2]\cup[5,\infty)]$

2. $g^{-1}(0)$ corresponds to the value of $x$ when $g(x)=0$.

    ![AI881b](images/AI881b.PNG)

    From the graph, $g(x)=0$ when $x=1$. Hence,

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    g^{-1}(0)=1

    \end{aligned}

    $$
