# AI883

**Maximum mark: 6**

A function is defined by $f(x)=\dfrac{\sqrt{x+1}}{x^2-4}$, for the
domain $-1\leq x\leq 5$, $x\neq 2$

1.  Find the range of $f$. [4 marks]

2.  Find the value of $f^{-1}\left(\dfrac{2}{5}\right)$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The graph of $f(x)=\dfrac{\sqrt{x+1}}{x^2-4}$ is plotted below

    ![AI883a](images/AI883a.PNG)

    From the graph, the range of $f$ is

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &(-\infty,0]\cup[0.117,\infty)])

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. $f^{-1}\bigg(\dfrac{2}{5}\bigg)$ corresponds to the value of $x$ when $f(x)=\dfrac{2}{5}$.

    ![AI883b](images/AI883b.PNG)

    From the graph, $f(x)=\dfrac{2}{5}$ when $x=3$. Hence,

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &f^{-1}\bigg(\dfrac{2}{5}\bigg)=3

    \end{aligned}

    $$
