# AI207

**Maximum mark: 6**

Emily is training for a triathlon and cycles at a speed of
20 km/hr for 15 minutes followed by $t$ hours
running at a speed of 15 km/hr.

1.  Show that a function to model her average speed $S(t)$ is given by
    [2 marks]
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S(t)=\dfrac{20+60t}{1+4t}.\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Find $S^{-1}(t)$.[2 marks]

3.  Hence find the time, $t$, if the average speed Emily travels at is
    16 km/hr.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's denote the path she cycles at 20 km/hr
    by $A$, and the running path at 15 km/hr by
    $B$.

    ![18f8e72209382b1575d513cf1f3df1aca73bd68e.svg](images/18f8e72209382b1575d513cf1f3df1aca73bd68e.svg)

    The distance Emily travels during the cycle ($A$) in km can be found by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d_A &= V_A t_A = (20)\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)=5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Similarly, the distance Emily travels during the run ($B$) can be expressed as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d_B &= V_B t_B = 15\hspace{0.05em}t\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, we can find the average speed in terms of $t$ as follows
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S(t) &= \dfrac{d_A+d_B}{t_A+t_B}\rule[-5.5mm]{0pt}{13mm}\\
    &= \dfrac{5+15\hspace{0.05em}t}{\mbox{\small$\dfrac{1}{4}$}+t} \rule[-7.5mm]{0pt}{13mm}\\
    &= \dfrac{5+15\hspace{0.05em}t}{\mbox{\small$\dfrac{1+4t}{4}$}}\rule[-7.5mm]{0pt}{13mm}\\
    &\hspace{-2em}S(t)= \dfrac{20+60\hspace{0.05em}t}{1+4t}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}\hspace{3em}{(1)}
    \end{aligned}

2.  We can find the inverse of S(t) by using expression (1), changing
    $S(t)$ to $y$, interchanging $t$ and $y$, and then rearranging for
    $y$. We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= \dfrac{20+60t}{1+4t}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{3.5mm}\\
    t &= \dfrac{20+60y}{1+4y}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{3.5mm}\\
    20+60y &= t+4ty\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    (60-4t)y &= t-20 \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    y&= \dfrac{t-20}{60-4t}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    \intertext{Hence, the inverse function is}
    &\hspace{-3em}S^{-1}(t)=\dfrac{t-20}{60-4\hspace{0.05em}t}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The time $t$ she has to run to get an average speed of
    16 km/hr is $S^{-1}(16)$. Hence, we get

    $$
    S^{-1}(16) = \dfrac{16-20}{60-4(16)} = 1.
    $$
