# AI104

**Maximum mark: 14**

Zoologists have been collecting data about the migration habits of a
particular species of mammals in two regions; region X and region Y.
Each year $\text{\(30\)\hspace{0.05em}\%}$ of the mammals move
from region X to region Y and $15$ % of the
mammals move from region Y to region X. Assume that there are no mammal
movements to or from any other neighboring regions.

1.  Write down a transition matrix $\bm{T}$ representing the movements
    between the two regions in a particular year. [2 marks]

2.
    1.  Find the eigenvalues of $\bm{T}$.

    2.  Find a corresponding eigenvector for each eigenvalue of
        $\bm{T}$.

    3.  Hence write down matrices $\bm{P}$ and $\bm{D}$ such that
        $\bm{T} = \bm{PDP}^{-1}$. [6 marks]

Initially region X had $12\hspace{0.15em}600$ and region Y had
$16\hspace{0.15em}200$ of these mammals.

3.  Find an expression for the number of mammals living in
    region Y after\
    $n$ years, where $n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$. [5 marks]

4.  Hence write down the long-term number of mammals living in
    region Y. [1 mark]
