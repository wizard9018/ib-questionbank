# AI529

**Maximum mark: 8**

Austin allocates a portion of his employment salary each month to
investing and invests this money into two stock funds: A and B. He
adjusts his investment portfolio each month according to the following
transition diagram.

![d757d471211a2e088d1ccda7cdf3618fbe7eb4c2.svg](images/d757d471211a2e088d1ccda7cdf3618fbe7eb4c2.svg)

1.  Construct a transition matrix $\bm{T}$ with elements in decimal
    form. [2 marks]

2.  Interpret the meaning of the elements with values
    1.  $0.1$

    2.  $0.7$ [2 marks]

  The initial state of his
investment portfolio is $100\%$ in stock fund B.

3.
    1.  Find the investment proportion in stock fund A after $3$ months.

    2.  Determine the long term steady state proportion of his
        investment between the two stock funds. [4 marks]

        This gives us the system of equations:

        $$
        \left \{ \begin{aligned}
        0.9a + 0.3b &= a \\
        0.1a + 0.7b &= b
        \end{aligned} \right.
        $$

        $$
        \left \{\begin{aligned}
        -0.1a + 0.3b &= 0 \\[3pt]
        0.1a - 0.3b &= 0 \phantom{\hspace{0.9em}}
        \end{aligned}\right.
        $$

        Solving the first equation will yield $a = 3b$. So we get

        ![a41b647c952aff4659f7a65f53883e552b3219bd.svg](images/a41b647c952aff4659f7a65f53883e552b3219bd.svg)

        Since $\bm{s}$ represents the percentages, its elements add up
        to $1$. Hence
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        3b + b &= 1 \\[3pt]
        4b &= 1 \\[3pt]
        b &= 0.25 \\[3pt]
        a &= 0.75
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Therefore, the long term steady state
        proportions between the two stock funds is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Stock fund A} &:\hspace{0.75em} \hspace{0.525em}0.75 \hspace{3em} \\[6pt]
