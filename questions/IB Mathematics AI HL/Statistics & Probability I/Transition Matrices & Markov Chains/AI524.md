# AI524

**Maximum mark: 6**

The vegetables sold at supermarkets in a town are supplied by three
major retail suppliers: A, B and C. According to an analysis report,
supplier A retains $80$ % of their customers
each year and lose $15$ % to supplier B and
$5$ % to supplier C. Meanwhile, supplier B
retains $70$ % of their customers each year and
lose $20$ % to supplier A and
$10$ % to supplier C. Supplier C retains
$75$ % of their customers each year and lose
$10$ % to supplier A and
$15$ % to supplier B.

The report also shows that suppliers A, B and C currently hold a market
share of $50$ %, $25$ %
and $25$ %,
respectively.

1.  Find the market share held by each supplier after three years.
    [4 marks]

2.  Determine the steady state market share held by each supplier if the
    same trend remains unchanged. [2 marks]
