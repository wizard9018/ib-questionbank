# AI769

**Maximum mark: 15**

A water park lends life jackets to its visitors. The park has two stations, $A$ and $B$, where clients can take a life jacket and they can return the life jacket to either of the two stations. It is found that at the end of a day, $81$\% of the life jackets are returned to the same station they were taken from, while the other $19$\% are returned to the other station.

1. Write down a transition matrix $\bm{T}$ representing the proportion of the life jackets moving between the two stations. [2 marks]

On February 1st, before opening the park, the staff counted the number of life jackets and found there were $400$ life jackets at station $A$ and $500$ at station $B$.

2. Determine the number of life jackets expected to be at each station when the park opens on February 5th. Give your answer to the nearest whole number.[2 marks]

3. 1. Show that the eigenvalues of $\bm{T}$ are $\lambda_1 = 1$ and $\lambda_2 = 0.62$.

    2. Find a corresponding eigenvector for each eigenvalue from $\textbf{part (c) (i)}$.

    3. Hence express $\bm{T}$ in the form $\bm{T} = \bm{PDP}^{-1}$. [6 marks]

4. Show that $\bm{T}^n = \dfrac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix} 1+ (0.62)^n &  1 - (0.62)^n \\ 1-(0.62)^n & 1+(0.62)^n \end{bmatrix}$, where $n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$. [2 marks]

5. Hence, find an expression for the number of life jackets in station $A$ after $n$ days, where $n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$ [3 marks]
