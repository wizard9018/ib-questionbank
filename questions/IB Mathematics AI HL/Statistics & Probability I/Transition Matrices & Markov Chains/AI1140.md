# AI1140

**Maximum mark: 15**

A University offers undergraduate programmes in the Arts, Business Studies and Science. Every year, some students change their programme of study, and the university are investigating these trends in order to plan correctly for future numbers in their programmes.

It is noted that in general the following annual changes occur:

- From Arts, $1.5\%$ change their major to Business Studies and $3\%$ change their major to Science.

- From Business Studies, $2.5\%$ change their major to Arts and $4\%$ change their major to Science.

- From Science, $1\%$ change their major to Arts and $2\%$ change their major to Business Studies.

1. Represent the given information in a transition matrix $\textbf{\textit{T}}$.[3 marks]

At the start of the current academic year, there are $5\hspace{0.1em}000$ students enrolled in Arts programmes, $18\hspace{0.1em}000$ students enrolled in Business Studies programmes and $7\hspace{0.1em}000$ students enrolled in Science programmes.

2. Use $\textbf{\textit{T}}$ to find the expected number of students enrolled in Business Studies programmes $5$ years from now. [4 marks]

For the transition matrix there exists a steady state vector $\textbf{\textit{v}}$ with components $a, b \text{ and } c$ that give the proportions of students that study Arts, Business Studies and Science programmes respectively. This steady state vector may be found by solving a system of linear equations.

3. 1. Write down the $\textbf{four}$ equations that are to be solved.

    2. Hence find $\textbf{\textit{v}}$.

    3. Using your answer to part (c)$\hspace{0.15em}$(ii), determine the long-term number of students expected to be taking Business Studies programmes.[6 marks]

The University realises that the number of students taking their undergraduate programmes will grow over time, and have modelled that growth using the function

$$

\begin{aligned}

P(t) &= \dfrac{60\hspace{0.1em}000}{1+e^{-0.15t}}

\end{aligned}

$$

Where $P$ is the total number of students taking their undergraduate programs in the Arts, Business Studies and Science, $t$ years after the current academic year.

4. Using this model, and your answer from part (c)$\hspace{0.15em}$(ii), determine a better estimate for the long-term number of students expected to be taking Business Studies programmes. [2 marks]

3. 1. Firstly, because the components represent a proportion of the total, our first equation must be

        $$

        Secondly, by its nature, a steady state vector does not change when a transition matrix is applied to it. Therefore we have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \bm{T} \times v &= v \\[8pt]

        \begin{bmatrix} 0.955  & 0.025 & 0.01 \\ 0.015  & 0.935 & 0.02 \\ 0.03 & 0.04 & 0.97  \end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix} a \\ b \\ c \end{bmatrix} &= \begin{bmatrix} a \\ b \\ c \end{bmatrix} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

       $0.955a+0.025b+0.01c=a$
