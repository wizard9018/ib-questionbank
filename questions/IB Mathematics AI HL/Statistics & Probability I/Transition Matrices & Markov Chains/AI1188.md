# AI1188

**Maximum mark: 6**

Ali is training an AI powered model car in decision making. The training track is shown in the diagram below. $$\textbf{The car starts at point $\bm{A}$}$$ and can move along any edge to a subsequent point.

![1188a](images/1188a.JPG)

Ali writes a set of rules for the car to follow, based on the alphabet:

- Never go to a point that is earlier in the alphabet than the current point, unless there is no other option. For example, if the car is at point $C$, the car can move to point $D$ or point $F$, but not point $B$. If the car is at point $F$, it can move to point $B$, point $C$ or point $E$.

     

- Choose the next point with random probability. For example, if the car is at point $C$, there is a $50\%$ chance it will move to point $D$ next and a $50\%$ chance it will move to point $F$. If the car is at point $F$, the probability it will move to point $B, C$ or $E$ are all $\frac{1}{3}$.

 

A partially complete transition matrix, $\bm{T}$, is shown below.

![1188b](images/1188b.JPG)

1. On the transition matrix above, complete the columns for $C$ and $D$.         [2 marks]

2. Calculate the probability that after three moves, the car is at point $C$.    [2 marks]

Ali instructs the car to make as many moves as possible over the course of an hour, then stop at the point it has reached.

3. State the most likely point for the car to be at when Ali returns. Justify your answer.         [2 marks]
