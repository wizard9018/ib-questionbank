# AI527

**Maximum mark: 6**

A discrete dynamical system is described by the following transition
matrix, $\bm{T}$,

![cf10a27b504c9599c9adc42101e9bc3c0f6527ac.svg](images/cf10a27b504c9599c9adc42101e9bc3c0f6527ac.svg)

The state of the system is defined by the proportions of population with
a particular characteristic.

1.  Use the characteristic polynomial of $\bm{T}$ to find its
    eigenvalues. [2 marks]

2.  Find the corresponding eigenvectors of $\bm{T}$. [2 marks]

3.  Hence find the steady state matrix $\bm{s}$ of the system.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The characteristic polynomial of $\bm{T}$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{det}(\bm{T} - \lambda \bm{I}) &=
    \begin{vmatrix} \hspace{0.05em} 0.3 - \lambda & 0.8 \\ 0.7 & 0.2 - \lambda \hspace{0.25em} \end{vmatrix} \\[4pt]
    &= (0.3 - \lambda)(0.2 - \lambda) - 0.7 \cdot 0.8\\[3pt]
    &= \lambda^2 - 0.5\lambda - 0.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
     Solving the
    characteristic equation $\mathrm{det}(\bm{T} - \lambda \bm{I}) = 0$,
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \lambda^2 - 0.5\lambda - 0.5 &= 0 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-2.15cm}\lambda_1 = -0.5,\,\lambda_2 = 1 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
