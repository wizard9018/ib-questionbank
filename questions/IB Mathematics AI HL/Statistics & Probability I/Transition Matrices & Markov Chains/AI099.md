# AI099

**Maximum mark: 6**

Three Internet Service Providers (ISPs) are available in a small town.
During the year, ISP A is expected to retain
$85$ % of its customers;
$10$ % will be lost to ISP B and
$5$ % to ISP C. ISP B is expected to retain
$80$ % of its customers;
$10$ % will be lost to each of the other two
ISPs. ISP C is expected to retain $75$ % of its
customers; $15$ % will be lost to ISP A and
$10$ % to ISP B.

1.  Write down a transition matrix that describes the exchange of market
    shares between the three ISPs during the year. [2 marks]

The current market share held by ISP A is $0.2$, by ISP B is $0.3$ and
by ISP C is $0.5$.

2.  Find the market share held by each ISP after one year.
    [2 marks]

3.  Find the market share held by each ISP after five years if
    the same trend of market share exchanges between the three ISPs
    continues. [2 marks]
