# AI298

**Maximum mark: 18**

The cumulative frequency curve below shows the serving speed, in km/h,
of a sample of $80$ randomly chosen serves by Roger Federer, the famous
Swiss professional tennis player.

![d9c34f2295f6b298e37c4d97868d2eada53ac0e5.svg](images/d9c34f2295f6b298e37c4d97868d2eada53ac0e5.svg)

Give your answers to the following correct to **the nearest whole
number**,\
wherever applicable.

1.  Estimate the minimum and maximum speeds of these serves. [2 marks]

2.  Estimate the median speed of these serves. [2 marks]

3.  Estimate the lower and upper quartiles. [2 marks]

4.  Calculate the interquartile range. [1 mark]

5.  Estimate the number of serves that had speed of more than $215$
    km/h. [2 marks]

The table below shows speeds of these serves.

![f9c36617121c1f85554fd285e46b0c965a5fe926.svg](images/f9c36617121c1f85554fd285e46b0c965a5fe926.svg)

6.  Find the values of $x$ and $y$. [2 marks]

7.  Write down
    1.  the modal class;

    2.  the mid-interval value of the modal class. [2 marks]

8.  Estimate the below using your Graphic Display Calculator.
    1.  the mean speed of these serves;

    2.  the standard deviation. [3 marks]

9.  Estimate the percentage of the serves where the serving
    speed differs from the mean by no more than $20$ km/hr. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\text{Min \(=182\) km/h, \, Max \(=230\) km/h}$

2.  Using the graph, we find the point at which the cumulative frequency
    is half of the total number of data values. In this case, look for
    the point where the cumulative frequency is $40$ (half of $80$). The
    graph shows that this is $209$ km/h.

    Thus,
     $\text{median \(=209\) km/h.}$

3.  The lower and upper quartiles are the points at which the cumulative
    frequencies are $20$ and $60$ as they represent $25\%$ and $75\%$ of
    the data set, respectively. The graph shows that the values are
    $199$ km/h and $216$ km/h.

    Thus,
     $\text{\(Q_1=199\) km/h, \, \(Q_3 = 216\) km/h.}$

4.  The interquartile range is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{IQR} &= Q_3 - Q_1 \\
    &= 216 - 199 \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}= 17
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  The graph shows that the cumulative frequency of the speed of $215$
    km/h is $58$. Hence the number of serves of that had speed of more
    than $215$ km/h is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[number of serves]} &= 80 - 58 \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}= 22
    \end{aligned}
    $$

6.  The graph shows that the frequency of the class $180 \leq v < 190$
    is $6$ while the frequency of the class $210 \leq v < 220$ is
    $69 - 48 = 21$.

    Thus,
     $x = 6, \, y = 21.$

7.
    1.  The modal class is the one which has the greatest frequency.
        Using the table, we can see that the greatest frequency is $27$,
        which corresponds to the class $200 \leq v < 210$.

        $\text{Hence the modal class is } 200 \leq v < 210$.

    2.  $\begin{aligned}
                     \text{[Mid-interval value]} &= \dfrac{200 + 210}{2} \\[6pt]
                                        &\hspace{0.525em}= 205
                    \end{aligned}$

8.
    1.
        $\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mux\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu = 207 \text{ km/h}$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    2.
        $s_x = 11 \text{ km/h}$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

9.  The interval where the speed of the serves differs from the mean by
    no more than $20$ km/h is $(207 - 20, 207 + 20)$, or $(187, 227)$.
    Using the graph, we see that the cumulative frequencies at these two
    endpoints are $4$ and $77$. Hence the percentage of such the serves
    are
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[percentage]} &= \dfrac{77-4}{80} \times 100\% \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}= 91 \%
    \end{aligned}
    $$
