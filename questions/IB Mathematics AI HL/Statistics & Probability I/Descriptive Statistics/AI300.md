# AI300

**Maximum mark: 7**

A group of iPhone users participated in a research survey and the ages
of participants were recorded in the following table.

![d2f0625d7bbbd1cc448d9f913ab408a57eb34d65.svg](images/d2f0625d7bbbd1cc448d9f913ab408a57eb34d65.svg)

It is known that $56<x<100$.

1.  Write down
    1.  the modal class;

    2.  the mid-interval value of the modal class. [2 marks]

2.  Determine the class in which the lower quartile lies. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the average age of participants between the ages of $45$
    and $74$. [2 marks]

The participants in this survey were chosen by randomly selecting people
walking past a stand in a shopping mall. However, to be polite, the
surveyor only asked people who were by themselves and looked to not be
in a hurry.

4.  Write down the type of sampling method used. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  The modal class is the one which has the greatest frequency.
        Since $x > 56$, it has the greatest frequency in the data
        table.

        $\text{Hence the modal class is \(35\)-\(44\) years old.}$

    2.  $\begin{aligned}
                            \text{Mid-interval} &= \dfrac{35+44}{2} \\[6pt]
                           & \hspace{0.525em}=39.5
                        \end{aligned}$

2.  Let's create a cumulative frequency table for this data set in the
    cases where $x$ could be on the lower or upper boundaries in the
    given interval (meaning $x=57$ or $x=99$).

    ![f6fa14675b5460d1c0fce0c2db9619cd240fe510.svg](images/f6fa14675b5460d1c0fce0c2db9619cd240fe510.svg)

    For $x=57$, the lower quartile is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    Q_1 &= \dfrac{1}{4} (n+1) \, \text{th} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{4} (205+1) \, \text{th} \\[6pt]
    &= 51.5 \, \text{th}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    For $x=99$, the lower quartile is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    Q_1 &= \dfrac{1}{4} (n+1) \, \text{th} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{4} (247+1) \, \text{th} \\[6pt]
    &= 62 \, \text{nd}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $\text{We see that the lower quartile always lies in the class of \(35\)-\(44\) years old.}$

3.  The last three rows of the table represent those between the ages
    $45$ and $74$. So filter these rows and identify the mid-interval
    values.

    ![0beb875193367c631b18714f35db91772441eb78.svg](images/0beb875193367c631b18714f35db91772441eb78.svg)

    Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[average age]} &= \dfrac{(49.5 \times 56) + (59.5 \times 24) + (69.5 \times 18)}{56+24+18} \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 55.6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.
    $\text{Convenience sampling}$
