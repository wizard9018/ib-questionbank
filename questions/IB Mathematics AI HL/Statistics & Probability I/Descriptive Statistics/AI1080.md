    3. The estimate for the mean of $W$ is $35.2\,\text{g}$. &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; [by using G.D.C.]

3. The median is $35\,\text{g}$.

4.  The upper quartile ($Q_3$) represents the $75$th percentile, this means $75$\% of the weights are less than this. $a=37\,\text{g}$.

5. Use the formula to find the interquartile range.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        IQR &= Q_3 - Q_1\\[3pt]
        &= 37-34 \\[3pt]
        &= 3\,\text{g}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
6. Use the formula to find the upper fence.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \text{Upper fence} &= Q_3 + 1.5 \times IQR\\[3pt]
        &= 37+1.5 \times 3 \\[3pt]
        &= 41.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    $\text{This means that any value over 41.5 g is classed as an outlier.}$\
    Therefore $42$ g is an outlier.
7.  1.  $\text{The median for Tuesday is higher than Monday}$, this supports Annabelle's claim.

        $\text{The maximum value for Tuesday is lower than the maximum value for Monday}$, this refutes Annabelle's claim.

    2. $\text{Monday's distribution is more likely to follow a normal distribution as is it symmetrical.}$

        Tuesday's distribution shows a negative skew which would not be exhibited by a normal distribution.
