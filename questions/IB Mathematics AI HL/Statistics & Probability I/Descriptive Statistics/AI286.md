# AI286

**Maximum mark: 6**

The column graph below shows the number of wet days per week recorded
for a period of time in Melbourne, Australia.

![74734100a77b8396208ce74aaaa2d09f5d724a3d.svg](images/74734100a77b8396208ce74aaaa2d09f5d724a3d.svg)

1.  Write down how many weeks were recorded. [1 mark]

2.  Write down the modal number of wet days per week. [1 mark]

3.  Calculate the mean number of wet days per week. [2 marks]

4.  Determine the percentage of weeks which had more than 2 wet days.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $11$

2.  The mode in a data set is the number that appears at the highest
    frequency. From the graph, we see that the highest frequency is $4$,
    first column. The number of wet days that corresponds to that column
    is zero.\
    $\text{ Thus, the mode is zero wet days per week.}$

3.  Based off the graph, we see that the total number of weeks that were
    recorded is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    n &= 4 + 1 + 3 + 2 + 1 \\
    &= 11.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence to find the mean,

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mux\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu &= \dfrac{(0 \times 4) + (1 \times 1) + (2 \times 3) + (3 \times 2)+ (5 \times 1)}{11} \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}=1.64
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  By inspection, we can see that there are $2$ weeks that had $3$ wet
    days and $1$ week that had $5$ wet days during those $11$ weeks.
    Hence the percentage of weeks which had more than $2$ wet days is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[percentage]} &= \dfrac{2+1}{11} \times 100\% \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}=27.3\%\hspace{0.525em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
