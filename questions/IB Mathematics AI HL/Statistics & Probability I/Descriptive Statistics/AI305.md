# AI305

**Maximum mark: 6**

The lifetime, in thousand of hours, of LED bulbs at a manufacture plant
were recorded for a periodic quality check. The data is illustrated in
the box-and-whisker diagram shown below.

![6e64588c54d68511b0fe63032b6a8fcf435f0e4d.svg](images/6e64588c54d68511b0fe63032b6a8fcf435f0e4d.svg)

1.  Write down the median lifetime of these LED bulbs. [1 mark]

2.  Write down the upper quartile. [1 mark]

3.  Find the interquartile range. [2 marks]

The lifetimes of these LED bulbs are normally distributed.

4.  Find the longest lifetime of an LED bulb that is still not
    considered an outlier. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's label the five summary numbers on the given box-and-whisker
    diagram.

    ![2b9be433ba959e4abee194e833ae3a2b48b2158a.svg](images/2b9be433ba959e4abee194e833ae3a2b48b2158a.svg)

    Hence,
     $ \text{median = \(40\hspace{0.15em}600\) hours.}$

2.
    $Q_3 = 42\hspace{0.15em}000 \text{ hours}$

3.  From the labelled box-and-whisker diagram, we can also see that the
    lower quartile is $Q_1 = 38\hspace{0.15em}400$ hours. Hence the
    interquartile range is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{IQR} &= Q_3 - Q_1 \\
    &= 42\hspace{0.15em}000 - 38\hspace{0.15em}400 \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}= 3600
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  A data value is identified as an outlier if it falls outside the
    interval
    $(Q_1 - 1.5 \times \text{IQR}, \, Q_3 + 1.5 \times \text{IQR})$.
    Testing this interval, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    Q_3 + 1.5 \times \text{IQR} &= 42\hspace{0.15em}000 + 1.5(3600) \\
    &= 47\hspace{0.15em}400 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, the maximum lifetime for a LED bulb, whilst still not considered to be an outlier, is $47\hspace{0.15em}400$ hours.
