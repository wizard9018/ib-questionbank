# AI285

**Maximum mark: 6**

The number of phone calls per hour that store managers receive during
the day are shown below.

$3 \hspace{1em} 5 \hspace{1em} 6 \hspace{1em} 2 \hspace{1em} 4 \hspace{1em} 7 \hspace{1em} 4 \hspace{1em} 9$

1.  Find the median number of calls per hour. [2 marks]

2.  Write down the value of
    1.  the mode;

    2.  the upper quartile. [2 marks]

3.  Find the probability that managers received no more than 5 calls in
    a randomly chosen hour. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  First, rearrange the data in ascending order:
    $2 \hspace{1em} 3 \hspace{1em} 4 \hspace{1em} 4 \hspace{1em} 5 \hspace{1em} 6 \hspace{1em} 7 \hspace{1em} 9$

    Then we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{median} &= \dfrac{4+5}{2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac 9 2 \\[6pt]
    &= 4.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  The number 4 appears more frequently than the other numbers in
        the data set. Therefore, it is the mode.

        Thus,
         $\text{mode}=4$

    2.  The upper quartile $Q_3$ is the median of the upper half of a
        data set. The upper half of the data set is $5, 6, 7, 9$. Hence,

         $$
         \begin{aligned}
         Q_3 &= \dfrac {6+7} 2 \\[6pt]
         &= \dfrac{13}{2} \\[6pt]
         &= 6.5
         \end{aligned}
         $$

3.  There are eight hours recorded in all, of which five have 5 calls or less.

    $\begin{aligned}
            \text{P(no more than 5 calls)} &= \dfrac{\text{Number of hours recorded with 5 calls or less}}{\text{Total number of hours that were recorded}} \\[6pt]
                    &= \dfrac 5 8 \\[6pt]
                    &= 0.625
    \end{aligned}$
