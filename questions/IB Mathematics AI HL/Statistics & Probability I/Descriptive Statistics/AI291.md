# AI291

**Maximum mark: 8**

The distribution of tomato sales in a grocery store over $100$ days is
displayed in the following box-and-whisker diagram.

![e8bd54b1dc04594495fb2b2ab906e6763c882596.svg](images/e8bd54b1dc04594495fb2b2ab906e6763c882596.svg)

1.  Write down the median tomato sales.[1 mark]

2.  Write down the minimum tomato sales.[1 mark]

3.  Find the interquartile range.[2 marks]

4.  Write down the number of days the tomato sales will be
    1.  between $42$ kg and $50$ kg;

    2.  between $26$ kg and $55$ kg. [2 marks]

Another day's sales were recorded. It was a very quiet day due to bad
weather and only $8$ kg of tomatoes were sold.

5.  Determine if this day would be considered an outlier.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We label the five number summary on the box-and-whisker diagram as
    follows. Note that they divide the whole range of data into four
    intervals of the same percentage.

    ![32bd70c5485eddaa1088e9962a6f7f8bb40d54c8.svg](images/32bd70c5485eddaa1088e9962a6f7f8bb40d54c8.svg)

    $\text{The median = } 42 \text{kg}.$

2.
    $\text{Min}=13 \text{kg}$

3.  The interquartile range is:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{IQR} &= \text{Q}_3 - \text{Q}_1 \\[3pt]
    &= 50 - 26 \\[3pt]
    & =24
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.
    1.  $42$ kg and $50$ kg are the median and the upper quartile of the
        data. The percentage of data that fall in this interval is
        $25\%$. 100 days in total were recorded. Hence the number of
        days tomato sales are between $42$ kg and $50$ kg is

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{[number of days]} &= 25\% \times 100 \\[6pt]
        & = 25
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  $26$ kg and $55$ kg are the lower quartile and the maximum of
        the data. The percentage of data that fall in this interval is
        $75\%$. Similarly, the number of days tomato sales are between
        $26$ kg and $55$ kg is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{[number of days]} &= 75\% \times 100 \\[6pt]
        & = 75
        \end{aligned}
        $$

5.  A point is considered as an outlier if it falls outside the
    interval\
    $(Q_1-1.5 \times \text{IQR}, \, Q_3+1.5 \times \text{IQR})$. Testing
    this interval, we get:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    Q_1 - 1.5 \times \text{IQR} &= 26 - 1.5(24) \\
    &= -10\\
    Q_3 + 1.5 \times\text{IQR} &= 50 + 1.5(24) \\
    &= 86 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $\text{Since the \(8\) kg belongs to the interval \((-10,86)\), it is not an outlier.}$
