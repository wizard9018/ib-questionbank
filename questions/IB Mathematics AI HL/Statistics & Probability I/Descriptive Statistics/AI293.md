# AI293

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following cumulative frequency diagram shows the distance students
need to travel to get to school.

![03439bda8db314adc9c2b710fcb3499973f1049f.svg](images/03439bda8db314adc9c2b710fcb3499973f1049f.svg)

1.  Find the median distance a student travels to school. [2 marks]

2.  Find the number of students that travel between $2$ km and $4$ km to
    get to school. [3 marks]

3.  Find the percentage of students that travel more than $4.5$ km to
    get to school. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The median is the point at which the cumulative frequency is half of
    the total number of data values. There are $160$ students in total.
    Therefore, the median occurs when the cumulative frequency is $80$.
    The graph shows that this is $3$ km.

    Thus,
     $\text{median}=3 \text{ km}$.

2.  The graph shows that $130$ students travel less than $4$ km to get
    to school. Meanwhile, $30$ students travel less than $2$ km to get
    to school. Hence the number of students that travel between $2$ km
    and $4$ km is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Number of students} &= 130 - 30 \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}=100
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The graph shows that $140$ students travel less than $4.5$ km to get
    to school. Recall, there are $160$ students in total. Hence the
    percentage of students who travel more than $4.5$ km to get to
    school is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[percentage]} &= \dfrac{160-140}{160} \times 100 \% \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}=12.5 \%
    \end{aligned}
    $$
