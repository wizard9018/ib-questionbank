# AI307

**Maximum mark: 6**

At the end of a working day, a survey was conducted at a company head
office asking employees how frequently they take sick leave days per
year.

The data is shown in the following table.

![6667877fb702938478e1714b66c77714ad1049f0.svg](images/6667877fb702938478e1714b66c77714ad1049f0.svg)

1.  State whether the data is discrete or continuous. [1 mark]

The mean number of sick leave days per year is $4$.

2.  Find the value of $k$. [4 marks]

In this survey, these employees were arranged in alphabetical order and
every $5$th person was asked for the number of sick leaves per year.

3.  Identify the sampling technique used in the survey.
    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\text{The data is discrete.}$

2.  The formula for the mean is
    $$
    \mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mux\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu = \dfrac 1 n \sum_{i=1}^{n} f_i x_i.
    $$
    We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    n &= 12 + 22 + 24 + 15 + k + 3 + 1 \\[5pt]
    &= 77 + k \\
    \sum_{i=1}^{n} f_i x_i &= (2\times 12) + (3 \times 22) + (4 \times 24) + (5 \times 15) + (6 \times k) + (7 \times 3) + (8 \times 1) \\
    &= 290 + 6k
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mux\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu &= \dfrac {290 + 6k} {77 + k}  \\[8pt]
    4   &= \dfrac {290 + 6k} {77 + k} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7.3em}k = 9 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    $\text{Systematic sampling}$
