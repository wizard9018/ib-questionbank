# AI295

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two groups of $60$ students each were asked how many movies they have
watched in the last month.

The results for **the first group** are shown in the following table.

![8bf9f17038341113fb7f52de37190c9f9f7d58e7.svg](images/8bf9f17038341113fb7f52de37190c9f9f7d58e7.svg)

The quartiles for these results are $4$ and $6$.

1.  Determine down the value of the median for these results.
    [2 marks]

2.  Draw a box-and-whisker diagram for these results on the following
    grid. [2 marks]

![e28b812631a11b62e8b601f9c9e8520629c72a9a.svg](images/e28b812631a11b62e8b601f9c9e8520629c72a9a.svg)

The results for **the second group** are shown in the following
box-and-whisker diagram.

![dd147d9ef4cb0f78f934ac71c26961e0da6d2679.svg](images/dd147d9ef4cb0f78f934ac71c26961e0da6d2679.svg)

'

3.  Estimate the number of students **in the second group** who
    have watched at least $7$ movies. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The median of a data set is the value in the middle when the data is
    sorted in ascending order. Since there are $60$ data values in
    total, the median is the average number of the $30$th and $31$st
    data values.

    The table gives us that the cumulative frequency of $4$ movies
    watched is $7 + 12 = 19$ and that of $5$ movies watched is
    $7+12 + 18 = 37$. This means the values from the $20$th to $37$th
    positions are all $5$. Hence the $30$th and $31$st values are $5$
    both. Therefore,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[median]} &= \dfrac{5+5}{2} \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}=5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have the five-number summary of the given data set as follows.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Min} = 3, \quad \,\,  Q_1 = 4, \quad \, \,  \text{Median} = 5, \quad \, \, \,  Q_3 = 6,  \quad \,\,   \text{Max} = 10
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Based off the summary above, we can draw the box-and-whisker diagram
    as follows.

    ![f227830c208557e511eca3bfa4a0ad0d9c3130b5.svg](images/f227830c208557e511eca3bfa4a0ad0d9c3130b5.svg)

3.  We label the five number summary on the box-and-whisker diagram as
    follows. Note that they divide the whole range of data into four
    intervals of the same percentage.

    ![9047929782dfbfc3384fa9c8883ae67d4dc11183.svg](images/9047929782dfbfc3384fa9c8883ae67d4dc11183.svg)
    '

    $25\%$ of the students in the second group have watched at least 7
    movies. Hence the number of such students is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[number of students]} &= 25 \% \times 60 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}=15
    \end{aligned}
    $$
