# AI296

**Maximum mark: 6**

The cumulative frequency curve below shows the number of hours spent by
$60$ students on their IB homework during one week.

![e4f96999c2aac8ece4e4ff865f8aa1d973517b8b.svg](images/e4f96999c2aac8ece4e4ff865f8aa1d973517b8b.svg)

1.  Write down the median value of this data. Give your answer correct
    to **the nearest hour**. [1 mark]

2.  The upper quartile of this data is $10.1$. Find the interquartile
    range, giving your answer correct to **one decimal place**.
    [2 marks]

3.  The minimum number of hours spent by students on their homework is
    zero. The maximum number hours spent by students is $14$. Draw a
    box-and-whisker diagram to represent this data. [2 marks]

4.  The $60$ students sampled were chosen by randomly sampling $30$
    students from Diploma Program Year $1$ and $30$ students from
    Diploma Program Year $2$. Both the Year $1$ and Year $2$ cohorts had
    the same number of total students. Write down the sampling method
    used. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The median is the point at which the cumulative frequency is half of
    the total number of data values. There are $60$ students in total.
    Therefore, we find the point at which the cumulative frequency is
    $30$. The graph shows that this is $7$ hours.

    Thus,
     $\text{median}=7 \text{ hours}$.

2.  To find the interquartile, we first need to find the lower quartile,
    $Q_1$. This is the point at which the cumulative frequency is $25\%$
    of the total number of data values. $25\%$ of $60$ = $15$. The graph
    shows that this is $4.8$ hours. Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{IQR} &= Q_3 - Q_1 \\
    &= 10.1 - 4.8 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}= 5.3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have the five-number summary of the given data set as follows.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Min} = 0, \quad \,\,  Q_1 = 4.8, \quad \, \,  \text{Median} = 7, \quad \, \, \,  Q_3 = 10.1,  \quad \,\,   \text{Max} = 14
    \end{aligned}
    $$
