# AI303

**Maximum mark: 6**

The histogram below shows the weights of $40$ Honeydew Melons, each
measured correct to the nearest kg.

![fd1586f7683d5502f5ec9b73b08c212d4209a384.svg](images/fd1586f7683d5502f5ec9b73b08c212d4209a384.svg)

1.  Write down the modal weight of the melons. [1 mark]

2.  Find the median weight of the melons. [3 marks]

The lower quartile is $3$ kg.

3.  Calculate
    1.  the upper quartile;

    2.  the interquartile range. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The modal value is the one that occurs at the highest frequency.
    Based off the histogram, we see that the highest column is $13$,
    which is the frequency of $4$ kg of melons. Hence,

    $$
    \hspace{0.525em}\text{Modal weight} = 4 \text{ kg}
    $$

2.  Using the given histogram, we create a cumulative frequency table
    for this data as follows.

    ![e5efdca30de2dc99ec5376e41fa9d3bd01ef7919.svg](images/e5efdca30de2dc99ec5376e41fa9d3bd01ef7919.svg)

    Using the formula
    $\text{Median} = \dfrac 1 2 (n+1) \text{ th value}$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[Median]} &= \dfrac 1 2 (40+1) \text{ th value} \\
    &= 20.5 \text{th value} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Therefore, the Median is the average of the $20$th and $21$st
    values. The cumulative frequency table above shows that they are
    both $4$ kg. Hence,

    $$
    \hspace{0.525em}\text{Median weight} = 4 \text{ kg}.
    $$

3.
    1.  Using the formula $Q_3 = \dfrac 3 4 (n+1) \text{ th value}$, we
        get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        Q_3 &= \dfrac 3 4 (40+1) \text{ th value} \\
        &= 30.75 \text{th value}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        So it is the average of the $30$th
        and $31$st values. The cumulative frequency table shows that
        they are $4$ kg and $5$ kg. Hence,
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        Q_3 &= \dfrac{4+5}2 \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}= 4.5 \text{ kg}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The interquartile range is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{IQR} &= Q_3 - Q_1 \\
        &= 4.5 - 3\\[6pt]
        & \hspace{0.525em}=1.5
        \end{aligned}
        $$
