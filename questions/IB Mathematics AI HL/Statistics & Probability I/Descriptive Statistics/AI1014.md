# AI1014

**Maximum mark: 5**

The manager of a small movie theatre recorded the number of tickets purchased each day for $14$ days. The number of tickets purchased each day were:

31, 36, 37, 17, 25, 27, 32, 54, 37, 21, 19, 26, 26, 29

For these data, the lower quartile is $25$ and the upper quartile is $36$.

1. Show that the value 54 would be considered an outlier.[3 marks]

The box and whisker diagram for tickets purchased is given below.

![AI1014a](images/AI1014a.PNG)

The manager of a second small theatre also recorded the number of tickets purchased for the same 14 days, giving the following box and whisker diagram:

![AI1014b](images/AI1014b.PNG)

The manager of the second theatre claims that, in general, his theatre has more customers than the first theatre.

2. By comparing the two box and whisker diagrams, give one piece of evidence that supports this claim and one that may refute it. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The interquartile range is:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \text{IQR} &= \text{Q}_3 - \text{Q}_1 \\[11pt]

    &= 36 - 25 \\[11pt]

    &= 11\\[-12pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    A point is considered as an outlier if it falls outside the interval $(Q_1-1.5 \times \text{IQR}, \, Q_3+1.5 \times \text{IQR})$. Testing this interval, we get:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    Q_1 - 1.5 \times \text{IQR} &= 25 - 1.5 \times 11 \\[11pt]

    &= 8.5\\[11pt]

    Q_3 + 1.5 \times\text{IQR} &= 36 + 1.5 \times 11 \\[11pt]

    &= 52.5 \\

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    $\text{Since the value 54 does not belong to the interval (8.5 , 52.5), it is an outlier.}$

2.  :::answer

    In support: The median for the second theatre is greater than the median for the first theatre.

    $\\$

    In opposition: The lower quartile is lower for the second theatre. The first theatre can say that they sell at least 25 tickets on 75\% of days, but the second theatre cannot match this.
