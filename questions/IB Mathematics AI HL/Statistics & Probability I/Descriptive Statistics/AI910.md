# AI910

**Maximum mark: 5**

The number of weeks spent in a hospital by a sample of lung cancer
patient during a course of chemotherapy treatment was recorded. The data
is illustrated in the box-and-whisker diagram shown below.

![dacfcff07e0e19ec1d1e500bae0e0338c5308eaf.svg](images/dacfcff07e0e19ec1d1e500bae0e0338c5308eaf.svg)

1.  For this data, write down

    1.  the maximum number of weeks spent during a course of treatment.

    2.  the upper quartile.

    3.  the median. [3 marks]

Dr. Buckley interprets this box-and-whisker diagram and claims that the
percentage of patients spending less than $13$ weeks during a treatment
is less than the percentage of those who spend more than $25$ weeks.

2.  State whether Dr. Buckley's interpretation is correct.
    Justify your answer. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's label the five summary numbers on the given box-and-whisker
    diagram.

    ![04b3964d1445d216121ecf861ccae5b9e885051b.svg](images/04b3964d1445d216121ecf861ccae5b9e885051b.svg)

    Hence, we have

    1.
        $ \text{Max} = 38 \text{ weeks}$

    2.
        $Q_3 = 25 \text{ weeks}$

    3.
        $\text{Med} = 22 \text{ weeks}$

2.  From part (a)(ii), we have $Q_3 = 25$ weeks. So $25\%$ of the
    patients spent more than $25$ weeks during the course of treatment

    From the labelled box-and-whisker diagram in part (a), we can also
    see that the lower quartile, $Q_1$, is $13$ weeks. So $25\%$ of the
    patients spent less than $13$ weeks during the course of treatment.

    The percentages are equal, hence Dr. Buckley's interpretation is incorrect.
