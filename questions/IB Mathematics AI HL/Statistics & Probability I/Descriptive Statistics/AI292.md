# AI292

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following cumulative frequency diagram shows the lengths of 80
pieces of string, in cm.

![5589f350d0090e985f920bb4d1af1efbce7f3504.svg](images/5589f350d0090e985f920bb4d1af1efbce7f3504.svg)

1.  Find the median length. [2 marks]

The following frequency table also gives the lengths of the $80$ pieces
of string.

![ed2392b971b19715451c3173194c2fc1c347a5d6.svg](images/ed2392b971b19715451c3173194c2fc1c347a5d6.svg)

2.
    1.  Write down the value of $q$.

    2.  Find the value of $p$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The median is the point at which the cumulative frequency is half of
    the total number of data values. There are $80$ pieces of string in
    total. Therefore, we find the point at which the cumulative
    frequency is $40$. The graph shows this is $3.6$ cm.
    Thus,
     $\text{median}=3.6 \text{ cm}$.

2.
    1.  The graph shows that the cumulative frequency for the lengths
        less than $4$ cm is $47$.

        Thus,  $q = 47$.

    2.  The total frequency is $80$. Hence,
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        p &= 80 - 47 - 26 \\[6pt]
        & =7
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        *Note: Answers of q = $46$ or $48$ and p
        = $6$ or $8$ would also be acceptable.*
