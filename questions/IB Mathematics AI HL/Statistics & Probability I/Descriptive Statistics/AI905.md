# AI905

**Maximum mark: 5**

Aaron lives in a remote country town in outback Australia. He uses
Amazon.com to purchase everyday supplies. Due to his remote location, it
can take numerous days for deliveries to arrive. Aaron records the
number of days for an order to arrive on his next ten orders, with the
results as follows.
$$
4, \,5, \,3, \,2, \,6, \,11, \,4, \,4, \,6, \,7
$$
For this data set, find the value of

1.  the median; [2 marks]

2.  the mean; [2 marks]

3.  the standard deviation. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we arrange the data in ascending order, we have
    $$
    2, \,3, \,4, \,4, \,4, \,5, \,6, \,6, \,7, \,11

    $$
    Since there are
    $10$ data values, the median will be the $5.5$th value. Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{median} &= \dfrac{5^\text{th}\text{ term} + 6^\text{th}\text{ term}}{2} \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{4 + 5}{2} \\[10pt]
    &= 4.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{mean} &= \dfrac{\displaystyle \sum x_i}{n} \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{2 + 3 + 4 + 4 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 6 + 7 + 11}{10} \\[8pt]
    &= 5.2 \hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    $\sigma = 2.4$
    \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]
