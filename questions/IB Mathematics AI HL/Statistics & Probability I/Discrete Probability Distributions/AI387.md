# AI387

**Maximum mark: 6**

Nancy is initiating a bake sale for charity at her school. The price of
each item is labelled in front of the counter. In the first week, she
records the relative frequencies of selling items of each type. She then
uses this to estimate the probability of selling an item of a certain
type.

![54de26c1e1dcce65ed2650630cb32605223d6467.svg](images/54de26c1e1dcce65ed2650630cb32605223d6467.svg)

1.  Determine the exact value of $p$. [1 mark]

2.  Find the expected cost of an item sold. Round your answer to the
    nearest [2 marks] cent.

Two students come in together, and Nancy sells one item to each student.

3.  Find the probability that Nancy earns exactly \$10 from
    selling the two items. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since the probabilities must add to $1$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac 1 {15} + \dfrac{1}{5} + p + \dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{5} +  \dfrac{2}{15} &= 1 \\[4pt]
    p + \dfrac{14}{15} &= 1 \\[8pt]
    p & = \dfrac{1}{15}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $X$ be the cost of an item sold. The completed probability
    distribution of $X$ is as follows.

    ![5fcf1325956d2befba8f6bfb0c8e045036294bf3.svg](images/5fcf1325956d2befba8f6bfb0c8e045036294bf3.svg)

    Using the formula of expected value
    $\mathrm{E}(X) = \displaystyle{\sum_{i = 1}^n x_i} \cdot \text{Pr}(X = x_i)$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{E}(X) &= (7 \times \dfrac{1}{15}) + (2 \times \dfrac{1}{5}) + (3 \times \dfrac{1}{15}) + (1 \times \dfrac{1}{3}) + (1 \times \dfrac{1}{5}) + (3.50 \times \dfrac{2}{15}) \\[8pt]
    &=\$2.07
    \hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  All of the outcomes for the total amount that she earns from selling
    those two items are illustrated in the following table. The
    combinations of amounts that total $$\$10$$ are highlighted in red.

    ![5fe3b057c318e9e8f67e1d8357b92fa3e16e1292.svg](images/5fe3b057c318e9e8f67e1d8357b92fa3e16e1292.svg)

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{P(total of $10$)} &= 2  \left[\text{P}(X = 7) \cdot \text{P}(X = 3) \right]\\[3pt]
    &= 2 \left(\dfrac{1}{15} \right)\left(\dfrac{1}{15}\right)  \\[6pt]
    &=0.00889\hspace{0.5em}\text{(3 s.f.)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
