# AI390

**Maximum mark: 6**

The faces of a fair six-sided die are numbered $2,4,4,4,6,6$. Let $X$ be
the discrete random variable that models the sum of scores obtained when
two such dice are rolled.

1.  Complete the probability distribution table for $X$. [3 marks]

    ![a8b845224b4fd3248d6157c2c0aa381c4628c690.svg](images/a8b845224b4fd3248d6157c2c0aa381c4628c690.svg)

2.  Find the expected value of $X$, the expected score when rolling two
    such dice. [3 marks]

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{E}[X] &= \sum x_i\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{Pr}(X=x_i) \\[10pt]

    \end{aligned}
