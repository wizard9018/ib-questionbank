# AI392

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows an archery target which is divided into
three regions A, B and C.

![091cb5327e00b25c5b64048cb35e8bab5848f215.svg](images/091cb5327e00b25c5b64048cb35e8bab5848f215.svg)

A contest consists of an archer shooting one arrow at the target. The
probability of hitting each region is given in the following table.

![6313a406c3aa82abc8eb508c344ca936eb2e4293.svg](images/6313a406c3aa82abc8eb508c344ca936eb2e4293.svg)

1.  Find the probability that the arrow does **not** hit the target.
    [2 marks]

An archer scores points on the contest as shown in the following table.

![694ffce2a56841f609dba228efef5a736468d2a3.svg](images/694ffce2a56841f609dba228efef5a736468d2a3.svg)

2.  Given that the contest is fair, find the value of $k$.
    [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{Pr}(\text{A}) + \mathrm{Pr}(\text{B}) + \mathrm{Pr}(\text{C}) + \mathrm{Pr}(\text{Missed Target}) &= 1 \hspace{3.6em} \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{1}{24} + \dfrac{4}{24} + \dfrac{7}{24} + \mathrm{Pr}(\text{Missed Target}) &= 1 \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{12}{24} + \mathrm{Pr}(\text{Missed Target}) &= 1 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{6.9em} \mathrm{Pr}(\text{Missed Target}) = \dfrac{1}{2}
    $$

2.  Let $X$ be the number of points scored by an archer on the contest.
    Hence, using the formula for the expected value of $X$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{E}(X) &= 0 \hspace{2em} [\text{fair contest}] \\[12pt]
    \hspace{5em}10\times\dfrac{1}{24} + 6\times\dfrac{4}{24} + k\times\dfrac{7}{24} + (-4)\times\dfrac{12}{24} &= 0 \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{10 + 24 + 7k - 48}{24} &= 0 \\[12pt]
    7k - 14 &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{13.65em} k = 2
    $$
