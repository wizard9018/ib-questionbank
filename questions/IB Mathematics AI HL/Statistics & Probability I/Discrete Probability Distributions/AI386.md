# AI386

**Maximum mark: 6**

The table below represents the number of vehicles and the associated
probability of an individual household having that number of vehicles.

![851d9c7603013036375d430362ec78441c9e4aa6.svg](images/851d9c7603013036375d430362ec78441c9e4aa6.svg)

1.  Determine the value of $p$. [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the expected number of vehicles in a household.
    [2 marks]

Sam lives next to his grandparents' house.

3.  Find the probability that Sam's household, and his
    grandparent's household, [3 marks] have a total of $7$ vehicles
    combined.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since the total probability must be $1$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0.05  +   p  +  0.24   +    0.32  +   0.17 + 0.05 &= 1 \\[3pt]
    p + 0.83 &= 1 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.5em}p = 0.17
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  From part (a), we obtain the complete table as follows.

    ![9dadff6215521b2fcbe920730c619f6c5f0a3d69.svg](images/9dadff6215521b2fcbe920730c619f6c5f0a3d69.svg)

    Using the formula
    $E(X) = \sum_{i=1}^{k} x_i \cdot \text{Pr}(X = x_i)$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    E(X) &= (0 \times 0.05) + (1 \times 0.17) + (2 \times 0.24) + (3 \times 0.32) \\[3pt]
    & \quad \,+  (4 \times 0.17) + (5 \times 0.05) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}= 2.54
    \hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  All of the outcomes for the total number of vehicles are illustrated
    in the following table. The combinations of vehicles that total $7$
    are highlighted in red.

    ![611c8a51fcaf3c0c0eef61c56663e4104b61d7a6.svg](images/611c8a51fcaf3c0c0eef61c56663e4104b61d7a6.svg)

    Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(total of $7$)} &= 2  \left[\text{Pr}(X = 2) \cdot \text{Pr}(X = 5) \right]+ 2 \left[ \text{Pr}(X = 3) \cdot \text{Pr}(X = 4)\right]\\[3pt]
    &= 2(0.24)(0.05) + 2(0.32)(0.17)  \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.133
    \end{aligned}
    $$
