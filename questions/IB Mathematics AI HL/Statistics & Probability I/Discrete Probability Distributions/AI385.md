# AI385

**Maximum mark: 6**

Laura's school is holding an outdoor activities celebration event for
its $20$th anniversary. Laura decides to participate in the Best Striker
football game.

In this game, the player shoots at goal which is split into $6$ regions
assigned with the scores, $X$, as shown in the diagram below.

![c067090886e86d59cab42a6aa9242f43668b40ba.svg](images/c067090886e86d59cab42a6aa9242f43668b40ba.svg)

If the shot goes outside of the mini goal, the player receives a score
of $0$.

The following table shows the probability distribution for $X$.

![2a53fed9175666411007fe32b9f843ecf78d1cc2.svg](images/2a53fed9175666411007fe32b9f843ecf78d1cc2.svg)

1.  Find the exact value of $p$. [1 mark]

Laura plays the game once.

2.  Calculate the expected score. [2 marks]

Laura plays the game twice.

3.  Find the probability that she has a total score of $5$
    after two rounds. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since the total of the probabilities must add to $1$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{3}{12} + \dfrac{1}{12} + \dfrac{4}{12} + \dfrac{2}{12} + p + \dfrac{1}{12} &= 1 \\[3pt]
    p + \dfrac{11}{12} &= 1 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.5em}p = \dfrac{1}{12}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  From part (a), we obtain the complete table as follows.

    ![40e071d434fa43456425dd1a16460863f0114221.svg](images/40e071d434fa43456425dd1a16460863f0114221.svg)

    Using the formula
    $E(X) = \sum_{i=1}^{k} x_i \cdot \text{Pr}(X = x_i)$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    E(X) &= \left(-3 \times \dfrac{3}{12}\right) +  \left(-1 \times \dfrac{1}{12}\right) +  \left(0 \times \dfrac{4}{12}\right) +  \left(1 \times \dfrac{2}{12}\right) \\[3pt]
    & \hspace{0.5em} +  \left(2 \times \dfrac{1}{12}\right) +  \left(4 \times \dfrac{1}{12}\right) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}= -\dfrac{1}{6}
    \hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  All of the outcomes for the total score are shown in the following
    sample space diagram. The combination of scores that total $5$ are
    highlighted in red.

    ![4cc031f3559f4e64e9c2afe8c2184c585e0448db.svg](images/4cc031f3559f4e64e9c2afe8c2184c585e0448db.svg)

    Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(total of $5$)} &= 2 \cdot \text{Pr}(X = 1) \cdot \text{Pr}(X = 4) \\[3pt]
    &= 2 \cdot \dfrac{2}{12} \cdot \dfrac{1}{12} \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.0278
    \end{aligned}
    $$
