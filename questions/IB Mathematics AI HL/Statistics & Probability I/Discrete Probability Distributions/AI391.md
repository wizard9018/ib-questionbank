# AI391

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two unbiased, standard, 6 sided dice are thrown and the scores are
recorded. Let the random variable $X$ be the minimum of these two
scores. The probability distribution of $X$ is given in the following
table.

![7b8039a8716eab46adfe0a4927410dcb7e953a07.svg](images/7b8039a8716eab46adfe0a4927410dcb7e953a07.svg)

1.  Find the value of $p$ and the value of $q$. [3 marks]

2.  Find the expected value of $X$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The possible outcomes with the minimum score of $2$ are
    $$
    \begin{array}{rcccl}
    (2,2) & (2,3) & (2,4) & (2,5) & (2,6) \\[6pt]
    & (3,2) & (4,2) & (5,2) & (6,2)
    \end{array}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} p &= \mathrm{Pr}(X = 2) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{9}{36}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The possible outcomes with the minimum score of $4$ are
    $$
    \begin{array}{rcccl}
    (4,4) & (4,5) & (4,6) \\[6pt]
    & (5,4) & (6,4)
    \end{array}
    $$
    Hence we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} q &= \mathrm{Pr}(X = 4) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{5}{36}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the formula for the expected value of $X$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \mathrm{E}[X] &= \sum x\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{Pr}(X = x) \\[10pt]
    &= 1\times\dfrac{11}{36} + 2\times\dfrac{9}{36} + 3\times\dfrac{7}{36} + \cdots \\[10pt]
    &\phantom{= 1\times\dfrac{11}{36}}\hspace{0.28em} + 4\times\dfrac{5}{36} + 5\times\dfrac{3}{36} + 6\times\dfrac{1}{36} \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{91}{36}
    \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
