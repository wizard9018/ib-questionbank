# AI817

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two unbiased, standard, 6 sided dice are thrown and the scores are
recorded. Let the random variable $X$ be the sum of the two scores. The
probability distribution of $X$ is given in the following table.

![a53ea4403d79d319ae6a8ea24264e2854b228774.svg](images/a53ea4403d79d319ae6a8ea24264e2854b228774.svg)

1.  Find the value of $p$ and the value of $q$. [4 marks]

2.  Find the expected value of $X$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The possible outcomes with the sum of scores of $5$ are
    $$
    \begin{array}{rcccl}
    (1,4) & (2,3) \\[6pt]
    (4,1) & (3,2)
    \end{array}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} p &= \mathrm{Pr}(X = 5) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{4}{36}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The possible outcomes with the sum of scores of $8$ are
    $$
    \begin{array}{rcccl}
    (2,6) & (3,5) & (4,4) \\[6pt]
    (6,2) & (5,3)&
    \end{array}
    $$
    Hence we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} q &= \mathrm{Pr}(X = 8) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{5}{36}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the formula for the expected value of $X$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    %\hspace{2em}
    \mathrm{E}[X] &= \sum x\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{Pr}(X = x) \\[10pt]
    &= 2\times\dfrac{1}{36} + 3\times\dfrac{2}{36} + 4\times\dfrac{3}{36} + 5\times\dfrac{4}{36} \\
    &\phantom{= 2\times\dfrac{11}{36}}\hspace{0.28em}+ 6\times\dfrac{5}{36}
    + 7\times\dfrac{6}{36} + 8\times\dfrac{5}{36}  \\
    &\phantom{= 2\times\dfrac{11}{36}}\hspace{0.28em}+ 9\times\dfrac{4}{36}+10\times\dfrac{3}{36} + 11\times\dfrac{2}{36} + 12\times\dfrac{1}{36}\\[10pt]
    &=7
    \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
