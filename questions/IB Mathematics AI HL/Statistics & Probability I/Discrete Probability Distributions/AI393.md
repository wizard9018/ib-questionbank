# AI393

**Maximum mark: 5**

A biased coin has a probability of showing "heads" as $0.7$. Jim pays
$$\$20$$ to play a game where he flips the coin. He gets no money if he
flips a "heads" and gets $$\$k$$ if he flips a "tails". The game is fair.
Determine the value of $k$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let $X$ be the amount of money that Jim gets after playing the game.

The probability distribution for $X$ is

![2678236a94f7c8bbf19d4999709f0669366bcf73.svg](images/2678236a94f7c8bbf19d4999709f0669366bcf73.svg)

Hence, using the formula for the expected value of $X$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{E}[X] &= 20 \hspace{3em} [\text{fair game}] \\[6pt]
(0)(0.7) + (k)(0.3) &= 20 \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hspace{11.7em} k \approx \$66.70 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
$$
