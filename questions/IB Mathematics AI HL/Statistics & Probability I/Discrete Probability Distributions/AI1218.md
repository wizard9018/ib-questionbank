# AI1218

**Maximum mark: 6**

A juice bar runs a loyalty program that rewards customers with free juices. The number of free juices, $x$, a customer receives in a month is a discrete random variable, with the following probability distribution

| $x$ | &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;$0$&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| &nbsp;$\mathrm{P}(X=x)$&nbsp; | $p$ | &nbsp;$0.25$&nbsp; | &nbsp;$0.35$&nbsp; | &nbsp;$0.10$&nbsp; |

The juice bar has determined that a maximum of 3 free juices can be given per customer each month.

1. Find the value of $p$. [2 marks]

2. Calculate the expected number of free juices a customer receives in a month. [2 marks]

The juice bar loses \$4 for every free juice given. Let $y$ represent the juice bar's monthly loss per customer.

3. Express $y$ in terms of $x$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Total of all probabilities must sum to $1$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    p + 0.25 + 0.35 + 0.10 &= 1 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    p &=\boxed{0.30} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. To calculate the expected value of $x$, we must sum the values of $x$ times its given probability
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{E}(X) &= 0(0.30) + 1(0.25) + 2(0.35) + 3(0.10) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]
    &= 0 + 0.25 + 0.70 + 0.30  \\[6pt]
    \mathrm{E}(X)&=\boxed{1.25}\tag*{\textbf{A1}} \\[6pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3. Since the juice bar loses \$4 per free juice ($x$) customers receive
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{-7.9em} \boxed{y = -4x} \hspace{4.35em}\tag*{\textbf{A2}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
