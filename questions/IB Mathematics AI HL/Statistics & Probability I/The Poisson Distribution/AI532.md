# AI532

**Maximum mark: 7**

The number of drivers detected using their mobile phones while driving
per square kilometre in a district can be modelled by a Poisson
distribution with a mean of $0.352$ drivers per hour.

1.  Find the probability that no driver is detected using their phone
    while driving in a $10$ km$^2$ area of the
    district in one hour. [2 marks]

2.  Find the probability that at least $15$ drivers are detected using
    their phones while driving in a $20$ km$^2$
    area of the district in a day (24 hrs). [2 marks]

3.  Find the probability that there are fewer than $8$ drivers detected
    using phones while driving per day (24 hrs) in a
    $1$ km$^2$ area for at least six days of a
    week. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  In a $10$ km$^2$ area of the district, there
    are $m = 0.352 \cdot 10 = 3.52$ drivers using phones while driving
    per hour on average.

    Let $X$ be the number of drivers detected for using their phones
    while driving in a $10$ km$^2$ area of the
    district in one hour. We have $X \sim \rm{Po}(3.52)$.
    Using GDC, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X = 0) &= \rm{poissonPdf}(3.52,0) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.0296
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  In a $20$ km$^2$ area of the district, there
    are $m = 0.352 \cdot 20 \cdot 24 = 168.96$ drivers using phones
    while driving per day on average.

    Let $Y$ be the number of drivers detected for using their phones
    while driving in a $20$ km$^2$ area of the
    district in a day. We have $Y \sim \rm{Po}(168.96)$.
    Using GDC, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(Y \geq 15) &= \rm{poissonCdf}(168.96,15,500) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  In a $1$ km$^2$ area of the district, there
    are $m = 0.352 \cdot 24 = 8.448$ drivers using phones while driving
    per day on average.

    Let $Z$ be the number of drivers detected for using their phones
    while driving in a $1$ km$^2$ area of the
    district per day. We have $Z \sim \rm{Po}(8.448)$.
    Using GDC, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(Z < 8) &= \text{Pr}(Z \leq 7) \\[3pt]
    &= \rm{poissonCdf}(8.448,0,7) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.3924
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Let $T$ represent the number of days for which
    there are fewer than $8$ drivers detected for using phones while
    driving in a $1$ km$^2$ area of the
    district. We have $T \sim \rm{B}(7,0.3924)$.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(T \geq 6) &= \rm{binomcdf}(7,0.3924,6,7) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.017 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
