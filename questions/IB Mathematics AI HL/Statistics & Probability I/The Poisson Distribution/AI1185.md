# AI1185

**Maximum mark: 6**

A customer service centre receives an average of 4 phone calls per hour. It can be assumed that the number of calls follows a Poisson distribution.

1. Calculate the probability that there are more than 40 calls during an eight hour working day.[3 marks]

The number of calls received on any given day is independent of the number of calls received on any other day.

2. Calculate the probability that, in a 5 day working week, there are less than 2 days on which more than 40 calls were received.     [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Given that the number of phone calls per hour follows a Poisson distribution, the number of calls per eight hour working day will also follow a Poisson distribution. There will be an average of $4 \times 8 = 32$ calls per day.

    Let the discrete random variable $C$ represent the number of calls received during an eight hour working day. We have $C \sim \mathrm{Po}(32)$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(C > 40) &= 1 - \mathrm{P}(C \leq 40) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    &= 1 - \text{poissCdf}(32,0,40) \\[6pt]

    &= 0.07066...\\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-4.9em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(C > 40) = 0.0707\,(3\,\mathrm{s.f.})} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Firstly we can note that all the criteria for a binomial distribution are met.

    - There are a fixed number of trials (5 days in the working week)

    - Each trial is independent (given)

    - Each trial has two outcomes ($>40$ calls or $\leq 40$ calls)

    - The probability is constant (from part (a), $\mathrm{P}(C > 40) = 0.07066...$)

    Let the discrete random variable $X$ represent the number of days in a five day working week on which more than 40 calls are received. 

    We have $X \sim \mathrm{B}(5,0.07066...)$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(X<2) &= \text{binomCdf}(5,0.07066...,0,1) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-4.5em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(X<2) = 0.957\,(3\,\mathrm{s.f.})} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
