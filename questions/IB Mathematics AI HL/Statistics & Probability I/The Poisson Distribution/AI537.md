# AI537

**Maximum mark: 8**

The number of cars sold per day by a local Audi car dealership follows a
Poisson distribution with mean $1.2$.

1.  On a randomly chosen day, find the probability that
    1.  there are no sales;

    2.  there are at least five cars sold. [2 marks]

2.  On a randomly chosen five-day week, find the probability that there
    are no sales. [2 marks]

3.  On a randomly chosen day, determine the most likely number of cars
    sold. Justify your answer. [2 marks]

The car dealership starts an advertising campaign to increase the number
of sales. The number of cars sold per day now follows a Poisson
distribution with mean of $\lambda$.

On a randomly chosen day, the probability that there are no sales is now
$0.13$.

4.  Find the value of $\lambda$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X \sim \mathrm{Po}(1.2)$.
    1.  $\begin{aligned}
                \mathrm{P}(X = 0) & = \mathrm{poissonPdf}(1.2,0) \\[6pt]
                                &\approx 0.301 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
                \end{aligned}$

    2.  $\begin{aligned}
                \mathrm{P}(X \geq 5) & = \mathrm{poissoncdf}(1.2,5,100) \\[6pt]
                                    &\approx 0.00775 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
                \end{aligned}$

2.  $\begin{aligned}
            \mathrm{P}(\text{no sales in a five-day week}) & = \left[\mathrm{P}(X = 0)\right]^5 \\[3pt]
            & \approx [0.301]^5  \hspace{3em} [\text{from part (a)}]\\[6pt]
            & \approx 0.00247 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
            \end{aligned}$

3.  Recall that for a random variable following a Poisson distribution
    with mean $\lambda$, its mode is also $\lambda$.

    Hence the most likely number of cars sold on a particular day is $1$.

4.  Letting $Y \sim \mathrm{Po}(\lambda)$ and solving the following
    equation for $\lambda$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(Y = 0) & = e^{-\lambda} \\[3pt]
    0.13 & = e^{-\lambda} \\[3pt]
    \ln (0.13) & = -\lambda \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{-0.6em}\lambda \approx 2.04 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
