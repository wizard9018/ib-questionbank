# AI540

**Maximum mark: 8**

The random variable $X$ represents the number of parcels that Jonathan,
a clerk at the local Post Office, receives from customers during a day.
It is observed that $X$ follows a Poisson distribution with a mean of
$25$.

1.  Find the probability that, on a particular day, Jonathan will
    receive fewer than $20$ parcels. [2 marks]

2.  Find the probability that, on a particular day, Jonathan will
    receive more than $30$ parcels. [2 marks]

3.  Find the probability that Jonathan will receive $110$ parcels or
    less in a particular five-day work week. [2 marks]

The number of parcels, $Y$, that Rachel, another clerk at the Post
Office, receives during a day also follows a Poisson distribution with a
mean of $35$.

4.  Find the probability that, on a particular day, the total
    number of parcels received by both Jonathan and Rachel is $60$ or
    less. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since $X \sim \mathrm{Po}(25)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{Pr}(X < 20) &= \mathrm{Pr}(X \leq 19) \\[3pt]
    &= \mathrm{poissoncdf}(25,0,19) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.134 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Likewise, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{Pr}(X > 30) &= \mathrm{Pr}(X \geq 31)  \\[3pt]
    &= \mathrm{poissoncdf}(25,31,500) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.137 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Letting $Z$ represent the number of parcels that Jonathan receives
    during a five-day work week, we have $Z\sim \mathrm{Po}(125)$ where
    $125 = 25 \cdot 5$. Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{Pr}(Z \leq 110) &= \mathrm{poissoncdf}(125,0,110) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.0954 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Let $T$ be the total number of parcels received by both Jonathan and
    Rachel on a particular day. So $T = X + Y$. Since
    $X\sim \mathrm{Po}(25)$ and $Y\sim \mathrm{Po}(35)$, then we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} T &\sim \mathrm{Po}(25 + 35) \\[6pt]
    T &\sim \mathrm{Po}(60)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the probably of the total number of
    parcels received by both Jonathan and Rachel being $60$ or less is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6em}\mathrm{Pr}(T \leq 60) &= \mathrm{poissoncdf}(60,0,60) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.534 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
