# AI957

**Maximum mark: 4**

Simon loads his truck with cartons of eggs and transports them from a
farm directly to a local market. In his experience, the mean number of
eggs broken while transporting a load is $3.5$. The number of eggs
broken while transporting a load is assumed to follow a Poisson
distribution.

On a day in which Simon has to transport $4$ separate loads to the
market, find the probability that there will be less than $10$ eggs
broken in total.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let $X$ be the total number of eggs broken on the day and let $\lambda$
be the mean number of eggs broken on the day.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda &= 4 \times 3.5 \\[12pt]
&= 14\\
\end{aligned}
$$
So we have $X \sim \rm{Po}(14)$.

Using the G.D.C., we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1.25em} \text{P}(X < 10) &= \text{P}(X \leq 9) \\[12pt]
&= \text{PoissonCdf}(14,9) \\[12pt]
&\hspace{-4.7em}\text{P}(X < 10) \approx 0.109
\end{aligned}
$$
