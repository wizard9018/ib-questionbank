# AI531

**Maximum mark: 5**

At a large dental clinic in Austin, Texas USA, patients arrive at an
average rate of $10$ patients every hour during the opening hours of 8am
to 5pm.

1.  Find the probability that during opening hours exactly $8$ customers
    will arrive in the next hour. [2 marks]

  The dental clinic manager
decides that if the probability that more than $2$ patients arrive
during a $15$ minute period is greater than $90\%$, she should employ an
extra dentist.

2.  Determine whether the manager should employ an extra
    dentist. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X$ be the number of patients who will arrive in the next hour.
    We have $X \sim \rm{Po}(10)$.

    The following diagram shows the central part of this distribution.

    ![3298f37f444c18871676473c50b294b31058679b.svg](images/3298f37f444c18871676473c50b294b31058679b.svg)

    Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X = 8) &= \rm{poissonPdf}(10,8) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.113
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $Y$ be the number of patients arriving during a $15$ minute
    period. $15$ minutes is a quarter of an hour, therefore the average
    number of patients who arrive at the clinic in a $15$ minute period
    is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \lambda &= \dfrac{10}{4} \\[3pt]
    &= 2.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we have $Y \sim \rm{Po}(2.5)$. The
    probability that more than $2$ patients will arrive during $15$
    minutes of peak period is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(Y > 2) &= 1 - P(Y \leq 2) \\[3pt]
    &= 1 - \rm{poissonCdf}(2.5,0,2) \\[3pt]
    &\approx 1 - 0.544 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.456  \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $\text{Since 0.456 < 0.9, the manager should not employ an extra dentist}$
