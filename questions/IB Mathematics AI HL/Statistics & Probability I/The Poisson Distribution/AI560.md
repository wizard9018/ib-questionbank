# AI560

**Maximum mark: 6**

Chang is hired as a digital marketer to increase traffic to two
particular YouTube channels; A and B. The number of views per hour on
channel A follows a Poisson distribution with a mean of $125$. On
channel B, the number of views per hour follows a Poisson distribution
with a mean of $180$.

Let $X$ be the total number of views per hour recorded on both YouTube
channels.

1.  Determine the distribution of $X$. [3 marks]

2.  Find the probability that, on a given hour, there are more than
    $350$ views across both YouTube channels. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X_1$ and $X_2$ be the numbers of views per hour on channels A
    and B, respectively, and $X$ be the total number of views on both
    channels per hour. $X = X_1 + X_2$, where
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    X_1 &\sim \rm{Po}(125)  \\[3pt]
    X_2 &\sim \rm{Po}(180)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    X &\sim \rm{Po}(125 + 180) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.6em} X \sim \rm{Po}(305)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using GDC, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \rm{Pr}(X > 350) &= \rm{Pr}(X \geq 351) \\[3pt]
    &= \text{poissonCdf}(305, 351, 1000) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.275em} = 0.00533
    \end{aligned}
    $$
