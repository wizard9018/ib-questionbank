# AI1139

**Maximum mark: 15**

Ahmed is interested in the number of goals that professional football teams in his country score per game. During a 38 game season, Ahmed records the number of goals his local team scores in each game, as shown in the following table.

![ai1139a](images/ai1139a.PNG)

The mean number of goals per game for Ahmed's team in this particular season is $1.95$.

1. Calculate the unbiased estimate of the population variance of goals per game.[3 marks] 

Ahmed believes that the mean number of goals per game by professional football teams in his country follows a Poisson distribution.

2. State one reason why Ahmed might believe this.[1 mark] 

Ahmed decides to see if his belief is correct by conducting a test at the $10\%$ significance level. His null hypothesis is $G \sim \mathrm{Po}(\lambda)$, where the random variable $G$ is defined as the number of goals per team per game.

Ahmed uses the mean for his team, $1.95$, as an estimate for the parameter $\lambda$. He calculates the expected number of goals per game for a complete 38 game season. These are shown in the following table, correct to two decimal places.

![ai1139b](images/ai1139b.PNG)

3. Find the value of

    1. $a$;

    2. $b$.[5 marks]

4. State why Ahmed needs to combine groups before conducting his test.[1 mark]

5. 1. Write down the number of degrees of freedom for the test.

    2. Find the $p$-value for the test.

    3. State the conclusion to the test, giving a reason for your answer.[5 marks]
