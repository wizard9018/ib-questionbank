# AI561

**Maximum mark: 6**

Max and Jeffrey are parking inspectors that issue parking tickets for
cars that have parked for longer than allowed in a car spot in the city
of Brisbane, Australia. The number of parking tickets issued by Max
follows a Poisson distribution with a mean of $4$ tickets every $30$
minutes. The number of parking tickets issued by Jeffrey follows a
Poisson distribution with a mean of $6$ tickets per hour.

Max and Jeffrey both begin their working shifts at
$9$ am, have two 30 minute breaks per shift, and
finish at $6$ pm. Determine the probability that
there are at least $100$ parking tickets issued in total on a given day.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

The average number of tickets Max issues per hour is 8.

Let $X_1$ and $X_2$ be the number of parking tickets issued by Max and
Jeffrey per hour respectively. Let $X$ be the total number of tickets
issued by both per hour. $X = X_1 + X_2$ where
$$
\begin{aligned}
X_1 &\sim \rm{Po}(8)  \\[3pt]
X_2 &\sim \rm{Po}(6)
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
X &\sim \rm{Po}(8 + 6) \\[6pt]
&\hspace{-0.6em} X \sim \rm{Po}(14)
\end{aligned}
$$

Each shift has $8$ hours of working time ($9$ hours total subtract the
two $30$ min breaks). If we let $T$ represent the total number of
parking tickets issued by both during the shift, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
T &\sim \rm{Po}(14 \cdot 8) \\[3pt]
T &\sim \rm{Po}(112)
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence the probability of the combined number of tickets issued being at
least $100$ can be calculated as
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rm{Pr}(T \geq 100) &= \text{poissonCdf}(112, 100, 1000) \\[6pt]
&\hspace{0.275em} = 0.883
\end{aligned}
$$
