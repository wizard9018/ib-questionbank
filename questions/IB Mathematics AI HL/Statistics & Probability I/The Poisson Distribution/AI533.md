# AI533

**Maximum mark: 6**

Philippines is a country subject to tropical cyclones, with a year-round
average frequency of $21$ typhoons per year.

1.  Find the probability that there are exactly $2$ typhoons next month.
    [2 marks]

2.  Find the probability that there are no typhoons next month.
    [2 marks]

3.  Find the probability that there is at least one typhoon next month.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X$ be the number of typhoons in a month in Philippines. Since
    there are $21$ typhoons per year on average, the expected number of
    typhoons to occur per month can be calculated by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m &= \dfrac{21}{12} \\[3pt]
    &= 1.75
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    So we have $X \sim \rm{Po}(1.75)$.
    For part (a), by using GDC, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X = 2) &= \rm{poissonPdf}(1.75,2) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.266
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Following on from part (a), using GDC, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X = 0) &= \rm{poissonPdf}(1.75,0) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.174
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Following on from part (a), using GDC, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X \geq 1) &= \rm{poissonCdf}(1.75,1,100) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.826
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    *Or alternately, we could have used
    1-poissonPdf(1.75,0) from part (b)*
