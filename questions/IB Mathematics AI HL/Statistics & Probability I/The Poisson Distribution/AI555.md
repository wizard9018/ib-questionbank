# AI555

**Maximum mark: 6**

The number of customers entering two cafes each day both follow Poisson
distributions and are independent of each other. The 'Paris Cafe' has a
mean number of daily customers of $210$, while the 'Sens Cafe' has a
mean number of daily customers of $295$.

Let $T$ be the total number of customers entering both cafes daily.

1.  Determine the distribution of $T$. [3 marks]

2.  Find the probability that the combined total number of customers for
    the two cafes on a particular day is less than $500$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X_1$ and $X_2$ be the mean daily customers for the Paris Cafe
    and the Sens Cafe respectively. Hence $T = X_1 + X_2$ where

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    X_1 &\sim \rm{Po}(210)  \\[3pt]
    X_2 &\sim \rm{Po}(295)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    T &\sim \rm{Po}(210 + 295) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.6em} T \sim \rm{Po}(505)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using GDC, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \rm{Pr}(T < 500) &= \rm{Pr}(T \leq 499) \\[3pt]
    &= \text{poissonCdf}(505, 0, 499) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.275em} \approx 0.406
    \end{aligned}
    $$
