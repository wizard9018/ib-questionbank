# AI538

**Maximum mark: 6**

Charlie installs a new messaging app on his smartphone to chat with his
friends. It is observed that the daily number of messages Charlie
receives from his friends follows a Poisson distribution with mean
$m = 20$.

1.
    1.  Find the probability that on a certain day Charlie receives more
        than $25$ messages.

    2.  Determine the expected number of days in a week that Charlie
        receives more than $25$ messages. [4 marks]

2.  Find the probability that Charlie receives fewer than $125$ messages
    during a week. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Let $X$ be the number of messages that Charlie receives on a
        certain day. Since $X \sim \text{Po}(20)$, we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{Pr}(X > 25) &= \mathrm{Pr}(X \geq 26) \\[3pt]
        &= \mathrm{poissoncdf}(20,26,500) \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.112 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Let $Y$ be the number of days in a week on which Charlie
        receives more than $25$ message. We have
        $Y \sim \text{B}(7, 0.112)$ and hence
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{E}[Y] & = np \\[3pt]
        & \approx 7\cdot 0.112 \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.785
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Let $Z$ represent the number of messages that Charlie receives
    during a week. We have $Z \sim \text{Po}(140)$ where
    $140 = 7\cdot20$. Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{Pr}(Z < 125) & =\mathrm{Pr}(Z \leq 124) \\[3pt]
    & = \mathrm{poissoncdf}(140,0,124) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.0933 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
