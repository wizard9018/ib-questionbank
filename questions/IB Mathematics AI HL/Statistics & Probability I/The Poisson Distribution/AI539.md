# AI539

**Maximum mark: 13**

The number of planes landing at Istanbul Ataturk Airport can be modelled
by a Poisson distribution. During busy periods of the day, planes land
at a mean rate of $10.4$ planes every $20$ minutes.

Let T represent a random $20$ minute busy period.

1.
    1.  Find the probability that exactly $8$ planes land during T.

    2.  Find the most likely number of planes that would land during T.
    3.  Given that more than $10$ planes land during T, find the
        probability that exactly $12$ planes land during T. [7 marks]

During the quiet periods of the day, planes land at a mean rate of $3.2$
planes every $20$ minutes.

2.  Find the probability that during a period of $30$ minutes,
    of which the first $20$ minutes is busy and the
    next $10$ minutes is quiet, that exactly $6$
    planes land. [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Letting $X$ be the number of planes that land during T, we have
    $X \sim \mathrm{Po}(10.4)$.

    1.  Hence the probability that exactly $8$ planes land during T is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3em} \mathrm{P}(X = 8) &= \mathrm{poissonPdf}(10.4,8) \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.103 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Recall that for a random variable following a Poisson
        distribution with mean $\lambda$, its mode is also $\lambda$.
        Hence, the most likely number of planes that would land during T
        is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{10.6em} \mathrm{mode} &=10 \hspace{3em} [\text{the integer part of the mean}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3.  If we calculate the conditional probability, we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{Pr}(X = 12\,|\,X > 10) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{Pr}(X = 12)}{\mathrm{Pr}(X > 10)} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{\mathrm{Pr}(X = 12)}{1 - \mathrm{P}(X \leq 10)} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{\mathrm{poissonPdf}(10.4,12)}{1-\mathrm{poissoncdf}(10.4,0,10)} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.218 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{1.25em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Letting $Y$ be the number of planes that land during a random $10$
    minute quiet period, we have
    $Y \sim \mathrm{Po}\big(\textstyle \frac{3.2}{2}\big)$, that is
    $Y \sim \mathrm{Po}(1.6)$.

    Letting $Z = X + Y$ be the sum of two independent Poisson random
    variables $X$ and $Y$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} Z &\sim \mathrm{Po}(10.4 + 1.6) \\[6pt]
    Z &\sim \mathrm{Po}(12)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the probability that exactly $6$
    planes land during the period of $30$ minutes is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6em}\mathrm{Pr}(Z = 6) &= \mathrm{poissonPdf}(12,6) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.0255 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
