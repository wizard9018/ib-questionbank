# AI343

**Maximum mark: 14**

$125$ sports fans were interviewed and asked what types of sport they
have been to in the last year from a choice of soccer $(S)$, rugby $(R)$
or baseball $(B)$. The following information was obtained.

$$
\begin{aligned}
74 & \text{ had been to soccer} \\[-0.3em]
36 & \text{ had been to rugby} \\[-0.3em]
66 & \text{ had been to baseball} \\[-0.3em]
20 & \text{ had been to soccer and rugby} \\[-0.3em]
27 & \text{ had been to soccer and baseball} \\[-0.3em]
24 & \text{ had been to rugby and baseball} \\[-0.3em]
15 & \text{ had been to all three types of sport}
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Draw a Venn diagram to show the above information. [4 marks]

2.  Find the number of sports fans who, in the last year, had been to
    1.  baseball only;

    2.  both rugby and baseball but not by soccer;
    3.  at least two types of sport;

    4.  none of the three types of sport. [6 marks]

A sports fan is selected at random from those who were interviewed.

3.  Find the probability that the sports fan had been to only
    one type of sport [2 marks] in the last year.

4.  Given that the sports fan had been to only one type of
    sport in the last year, [2 marks] find the
    probability that the sports fan had been to rugby.

3.  The region shaded below represents the event that the sports fans
    had been to only one type of sport.

    ![f581456a1618181fc2183e4448912c986558b034.svg](images/f581456a1618181fc2183e4448912c986558b034.svg)

    So the probability of having been to only one type of sport is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr($1$ sport only)} &= \dfrac{42 + 7 + 30}{125} \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}=0.632
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using the Conditional Probability rule
    $\text{Pr}(A \hspace{0.1em} | \hspace{0.1em} B) = \dfrac{\text{Pr}(A \cap B)}{\text{Pr}(B)}$,
    we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Pr($R$ | $1$ sport only)} &= \dfrac{\text{Pr}(R \cap \text{($1$ sport only)})}{\text{Pr($1$ sport only)}} \\[3pt]
&= \dfrac{7}{42 + 7 + 30}\\[6pt]
&\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.0886
\end{aligned}
$$
