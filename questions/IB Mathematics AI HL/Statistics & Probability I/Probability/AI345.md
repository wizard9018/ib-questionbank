# AI345

**Maximum mark: 16**

50 people waiting in line at a movie cinema were asked their preference
of movie genres: action $(A)$, comedy $(C)$ or drama $(D)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5 & \text{ prefer all three} \\[-0.3em]
12 & \text{ prefer action and comedy} \\[-0.3em]
7 & \text{ prefer action and drama} \\[-0.3em]
9 & \text{ prefer comedy and drama} \\[-0.3em]
8 & \text{ prefer action \textbf{only}} \\[-0.3em]
3 & \text{ prefer drama \textbf{only}}
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Draw a Venn diagram to represent the information provided so far.
    [4 marks]

2.  Write down the number of people who prefer action but not drama.
    [1 mark]

There are $22$ people **in total** who prefer comedy.

3.
    1.  Calculate the number of people who prefer comedy **only**.

    2.  Find the number of people who prefer none of these three genres.
        [4 marks]

A person is chosen at random from the 50 people that were waiting in
line.

4.  Find the probability that this person
    1.  prefers action;

    2.  prefers comedy and drama but not action;
    3.  does not prefer either action or comedy;

    4.  does not prefer action given that the person does not prefer
        comedy. [7 marks]

3.
    1.  Let $a$ be the number of people who prefer comedy only. The
        region shaded below represents all those who prefer comedy.

        \begin{aligned}

    2.  Let $b$ be the number of people who prefer none of these three
        genres. The region shaded in the Venn diagram below represents

        ![a67b4a17ded0da3b26fcc1617a6ce5ed10f4bd89.svg](images/a67b4a17ded0da3b26fcc1617a6ce5ed10f4bd89.svg)

        Summing all of these numbers would give us a total of $50$:

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        b + 8 + 7 + 6 + 5 + 2 + 4 + 3 &= 50 \\

        \end{aligned}

4.
    1.  Summing all the numbers in set $A$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        n(A) &= 8 + 7 + 5 + 2 \\
        &= 22.
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        That gives

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(A) &= \dfrac{22}{50}\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}= 0.44
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The region shaded on the Venn diagram below represents those who
        prefer comedy and drama but not action.

        ![7adc316fb56aab94e20facb07867c88db366d2d6.svg](images/7adc316fb56aab94e20facb07867c88db366d2d6.svg)

        Hence,
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{P}(C \cap D \cap A') &= \dfrac 4 {50} \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}= 0.08
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3.  The region shaded below represents those who do not prefer
        either action or comedy.

        ![e8a8083149c2ae9af420af5dec7176b66f2f5f02.svg](images/e8a8083149c2ae9af420af5dec7176b66f2f5f02.svg)

        Hence we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{P}(A\cup C)' &= \dfrac{3+15}{50} \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}0.36
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    4.  Using the Conditional Pobability rule
        $\text{P}(A \hspace{0.1em} | \hspace{0.1em} B) = \dfrac{\text{P}(A \cap B)}{\text{P}(B)}$,
        we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{P}(A' \hspace{0.1em} | \hspace{0.1em} C') &= \dfrac{\text{P}(A' \cap C')}{\text{P}(C')} \\[3pt]
        &=\dfrac{\text{P}(A \cup C)'}{\text{P}(C')} \hspace{3em} [A' \cap C' = (A \cup C)'] \\[3pt]
        &= \dfrac{3+15}{8+2+3+15}\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.643
        \end{aligned}
        $$
