# AI346

**Maximum mark: 6**

Adam has an unbiased octahedral (eight faced) die which has the numbers
$1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8$.

Bella has an unbiased pentahedral (five faced) die which has the numbers
$1, 3, 5, 7, 9$.

1.  Complete the Venn diagram with the numbers written on Adam's die
    ($A$) [2 marks] and Bella's die ($B$).

    ![0441ffc31df02334107bc52d9648d34dabf00dcd.svg](images/0441ffc31df02334107bc52d9648d34dabf00dcd.svg)

2.  Find $n(A' \cup B')$. [2 marks]

Adam and Bella are each going to roll their die once only. Charlie says
the probability that each die will show the same number is
$\dfrac 1 {10}$.

3.  Determine whether Charlie is correct. Give a reason.
    [2 marks]

3.  All of the outcomes can be illustrated in the following table.

    ![398fcb1295a78e7a8bc73bf6185975ef276f71c1.svg](images/398fcb1295a78e7a8bc73bf6185975ef276f71c1.svg)

    There are $4$ favorable outcomes (shown in red) for the same number
    out of $8 \cdot 5 = 40$ outcomes in total. Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(same number)} &= \dfrac{4}{40} \\[4pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{10}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $\text{Hence, Charlie is correct.}$
