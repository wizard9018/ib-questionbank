# AI354

**Maximum mark: 6**

$106$ runners at a marathon event were asked through which media
channels they received information about the marathon. The summary shows
that $52$ runners answered \"TV advertising\", $64$ answered \"social
media\" and $14$ answered \"others\". Find the probability that a runner
selected at random from the marathon received information about the
marathon through:

1.  both TV advertising and social media; [3 marks]

2.  only TV advertising. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $T$ and $S$ be the events that the runner received information
    about the marathon through TV advertising and social media
    respectively.

    Since $14$ runners answered \"others\", we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    n(T \cup S) &= 106 - 14 \\[3pt]
    &= 92
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the Combined Events
    rule $n(T \cup S) = n(T) + n(S) - n(T \cap S)$ gives

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    92 &= 52 + 64 -  n(T \cap S) \\[3pt]
    n(T \cap S) &= 24
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Based off these information, we can draw a Venn diagram as follows.

    ![48ba0975ee893e452b6dce89997872659dc88f91.svg](images/48ba0975ee893e452b6dce89997872659dc88f91.svg)

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(T \cap S) &= \dfrac{24}{106} \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.226
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The region shaded below represents the event that this runner
    received information about the marathon via TV advertising only.

    ![f17fb47152b63f20eba3948c17b7eedb5b7742fa.svg](images/f17fb47152b63f20eba3948c17b7eedb5b7742fa.svg)

    So we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(T \cap S') &= \dfrac{28}{106} \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.264
    \end{aligned}
    $$
