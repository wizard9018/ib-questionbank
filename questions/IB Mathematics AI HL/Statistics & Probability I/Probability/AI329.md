# AI329

**Maximum mark: 6**

There are $3$ pairs of white coloured socks and $4$ pairs of red
coloured socks in a drawer. Anne picks **one sock** from the drawer at
random and puts it on. She then picks another sock from the drawer at
random and puts it on.

1.  Complete the following tree diagram. [3 marks]

    ![90f2cc4155b94167a1ab8289ec324979d7d337da.svg](images/90f2cc4155b94167a1ab8289ec324979d7d337da.svg)

2.  Find the probability that Anne goes for a walk in socks of the
    same color. [3 marks]

2.  Let $W_1$ = {$1$st sock picked is white},
    $W_2$ = {$2$nd sock picked is white},

    $R_1$ = {$1$st sock picked is red},

    $R_2$ = {$2$nd sock picked is red}.

    The outcome that Anne picks two socks of the same color can be
    illustrated by the paths highlighted in red in the following tree
    diagram.

    ![e7e556a3d5c7906406dcddbd4b28ab1f355ba743.svg](images/e7e556a3d5c7906406dcddbd4b28ab1f355ba743.svg)

    Based off the tree diagram above, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(same color)} &= \text{Pr}(W_1) \cdot \text{Pr}(W_2|W_1) + \text{Pr}(R_1) \cdot \text{Pr}(R_2|R_1) \\[4pt]
    &= (\dfrac{3}{7} \cdot \dfrac{5}{13}) + (\dfrac{4}{7} \cdot \dfrac{7}{13}) \\[7pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.473
    \end{aligned}
    $$
