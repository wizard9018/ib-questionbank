# AI349

**Maximum mark: 18**

A group of chemistry students at Indiana University Bloomington were
surveyed. They were asked which of the following textbook formats:
e-textbook ($E$), paperback ($P$) or hardcover ($H$), they used in their
courses.

The following results were obtained:
$$

\begin{array}{rl}
108 & \text{students used paperback} \\[-0.25em]
64 & \text{students used hardcover} \\[-0.25em]
45 & \text{students used paperback and hardcover} \\[-0.25em]
68 & \text{students used e-textbook and paperback} \\[-0.25em]
49 & \text{students used e-textbook and hardcover} \\[-0.25em]
35 & \text{students used all three textbook formats} \\[-0.25em]
18 & \text{students used e-textbook \textbf{only}} \\[-0.25em]
15 & \text{students used none of the three textbook formats}
\end{array}
$$

1.  Use the above information to complete a Venn diagram. [5 marks]

2.  Calculate the number of students who were surveyed. [2 marks]

3.
    1.  On your Venn diagram, shade the set $(E\cup P)\cap H'$.

    2.  Find $n\big((E\cup P)\cap H'\big)$. [3 marks]

4.  A chemistry student who was surveyed is chosen at random. Find the
    probability that

    1.  the student used an e-textbook;
    2.  the student used a hardcover, given that they had not used an
        e-textbook;

    3.  the student used at least two of the textbook formats.
        [6 marks]

The Chemistry Department at IU Bloomington has $2560$ students.

5.  Find the expected number of students in the Chemistry
    Department that [2 marks] used e-textbooks.

        $$
