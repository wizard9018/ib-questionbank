# AI334

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two bags of marbles contain green and orange marbles. Bag A contains $7$
green marbles and $5$ orange marbles, while Bag B contains $3$ green
marbles and $9$ orange marbles.

One marble is selected from Bag A and then Bag B.

1.  Determine the probability of selecting two marbles of the same
    colour. [3 marks]

Both marbles from **part (a)** are put back into the bags they came
from. This time, an unbiased coin is flipped to determine which bag to
select a single marble from. If the coin flips heads, a marble is
selected from Bag A. If the coin flips tails, a marble is selected from
Bag B.

2.  Determine the probability of flipping tails and then
    selecting a green marble. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $G_A$ = {selecting green marble from Bag A},
    $O_A$ = {selecting orange marble from Bag A},

    $G_B$ = {selecting green marble from Bag B},

    $O_B$ = {selecting orange marble from Bag B},

    The event of selecting two marbles of the same colour can be
    illustrated by the paths highlighted in red on the tree diagram
    below.

    ![9e322fec019fae2ba9b49b87431a04a300aea10b.svg](images/9e322fec019fae2ba9b49b87431a04a300aea10b.svg)

    Hence the probability of selecting two marbles of the same colour is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(same colour)} &= \text{Pr}(G_A) \cdot \text{Pr}(G_B|G_A) + \text{Pr}(O_A) \cdot \text{Pr}(O_B|O_A)\\[3pt]
    &= (\dfrac{7}{12} \cdot \dfrac{3}{12}) + (\dfrac{5}{12} \cdot \dfrac{9}{12})\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.458
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The paths highlighted in red on the following tree diagram represent
    the event of flipping tails and then selecting a green marble.

    ![e54009b2664f346822aa7156037e4aca49720a9e.svg](images/e54009b2664f346822aa7156037e4aca49720a9e.svg)

    So we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(T \cap G_B) &= \text{Pr}(T) \cdot \text{Pr}(G_B|T) \\
    &= \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{3}{12}\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}= 0.125
    \end{aligned}
    $$
