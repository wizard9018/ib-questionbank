# AI327

**Maximum mark: 6**

The probability that a soccer team scores in the first half is $0.6$. If
the team scores in the first half, the probability that it will score in
the second half is $0.3$. If the team does not score in the first half,
the probability it will not score in the second half is $0.2$.

1.  Using the information given, complete the following tree diagram.
    [3 marks]

    ![f7e46b9d250ef40b5a80fae22e5b1e7d5ffc6742.svg](images/f7e46b9d250ef40b5a80fae22e5b1e7d5ffc6742.svg)

2.  Calculate the probability that the team will score in the second
    half. [3 marks]

2.  Let $S_1$ = {the team scores in the $1$st half},
    $S_2$ = {the team scores in the $2$nd half}.

    The outcome that the team will score in the second half can be
    illustrated by the paths highlighted in red in the following tree
    diagram.

    ![a9c3cf6eee65524b0476affbd1872160e09d9178.svg](images/a9c3cf6eee65524b0476affbd1872160e09d9178.svg)

    Using the Law of Total Probability, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(S_2) &= \text{Pr}(S_1) \cdot \text{Pr}(S_2|S_1) + \text{Pr}(S_1') \cdot \text{Pr}(S_2|S_1') \\[3pt]
    &= (0.6 \cdot 0.3) + (0.4 \cdot 0.8) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}=0.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
