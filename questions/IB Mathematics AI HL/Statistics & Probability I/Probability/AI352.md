# AI352

**Maximum mark: 6**

At a travel agency, a survey conducted on the $32$ agents shows that
$18$ agents can speak Spanish fluently, $12$ agents speak French
fluently and $8$ neither Spanish nor French.

1.  Find the number of agents who can speak both Spanish and French
    fluently. [3 marks]

2.  Find the probability that an agent chosen at random from this travel
    agency [3 marks] speaks exactly one of these
    two languages fluently.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $x$ be the number of agents who can speak both Spanish and
    French fluently. Subtracting $x$ from $18$ and $12$ will give us the
    numbers of agents who speak only Spanish and only French fluently,
    respectively. Based off these information, we can draw a Venn
    diagram as follows.

    ![6f1bc13ce47dc1c61f0abd14febe868ef36a81c9.svg](images/6f1bc13ce47dc1c61f0abd14febe868ef36a81c9.svg)

    Summing all of the numbers on the diagram gives a total of $32$.
    Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (18-x) + x + (12-x) + 8 &= 32 \\[3pt]
    38 - x &= 32 \\[6pt]
    x &= 6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $\text{Hence there are \(6\) agents who can speak both Spanish and French fluently.}$

2.  Provided $x = 6$ found in part (a), we have a completed Venn diagram
    below. The shaded region represents those who speaks exactly one of
    these two languages fluently.

    ![024bc0f5c51bfb7017c2a0bb24f9ff0131ede93f.svg](images/024bc0f5c51bfb7017c2a0bb24f9ff0131ede93f.svg)

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(exactly one of two)} &= \dfrac{12 + 6}{32} \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.563
    \end{aligned}
    $$
