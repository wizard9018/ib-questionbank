# AI335

**Maximum mark: 6**

The first two characters of a student ID number is composed by the
following rules:

-   :item one letter is chosen from student's first name at random;

-   :item one digit is chosen from student's date of birth at random
    (dd/mm/yyyy).

Bob Stewart was born on the $12$th of January, $2001 (12/01/2001)$. Find
the probability that

1.  Bob's ID starts with \"B1\". [2 marks]

2.  Bob's ID starts with \"B\" **or** its second character is \"$2$\".
    [2 marks]

3.  Bob's ID starts with \"O\" **given that** the second character **is
    not** $0$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  His first name is Bob. So the probability that his ID starts with
    \"B\" would be $\dfrac 2 3$. In addition, his date of birth is
    $12/01/2001$. Hence the probability of the next character being a
    \"$1$\" is $\dfrac 3 8$. These two events can be illustrated in the
    following tree diagram.

    ![973c979adeab95ef92b24a4319898e3c4c022c76.svg](images/973c979adeab95ef92b24a4319898e3c4c022c76.svg)

    Since these two events are independent, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr($B1$)} &= \text{Pr($B$)} \cdot \text{Pr($1$)} \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac 2 3 \cdot \dfrac 3 8 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}= 0.25
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the Combined Events Rule
    $\text{Pr}(A \cup B) = \text{Pr}(A) + \text{Pr}(B) - \text{Pr}(A \cap B)$,
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(B \cup 2) &= \text{Pr}(B) + \text{Pr}(2) - \text{Pr}(B \cap 2) \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac 2 3 + \dfrac 2 8 - (\dfrac 2 3 \cdot \dfrac 2 8 )\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}= 0.75
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the Conditional Probability Rule
    $\text{Pr}(A|B) = \dfrac{\text{Pr}(A \cap B)}{\text{Pr}(B)}$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(\text{O}\hspace{0.2em}| \hspace{0.2em}\text{Not zero}) &= \dfrac{\text{Pr}(\text{O} \cap \text{Not zero})}{\text{Pr}(\text{Not zero})} \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{\dfrac 1 3 \cdot \dfrac 5 8}{\dfrac 5 8} \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.333
    \end{aligned}
    $$
