# AI738

**Maximum mark: 4**

Oliver has 8 pens in his desk drawer, three black, three red and two blue. He randomly selects two pens, one after the other, from the drawer. The following tree diagram shows all the possible outcomes of taking the two pens.

![ollya](images/ollya.JPG)

1. Find the probability that the second pen is red.[2 marks]

2. Given that the second pen is red, find the probability that the first pen is $\textbf{not}$ red. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. There are three ways in which the second pen could be red, as shown by the red branches in the following tree diagram.

    ![ollyb](images/ollyb.JPG)

    Using the tree diagram above, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(R_2) &= \frac{3}{8} \times \frac{3}{7} + \frac{3}{8} \times \frac{2}{7} + \frac{2}{8} \times \frac{3}{7} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0em}\boxed{=\dfrac{21}{56}\hspace{1em}\left(=\frac{3}{8}\right)} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. Using the conditional probability formula, we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(R_1' | R_2) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(R_1' \cap R_2)}{\mathrm{P}(R_2)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    In the denominator, we have $\mathrm{P}(R_2)$, which we know to be $\frac{21}{56}$ from part (a).

    In the numerator, we have $\mathrm{P}(R_1' \cap R_2)$ which is the same as selecting a black pen followed by a red pen, or selecting a blue pen followed by a red pen.

    We actually have both of these as the first and third terms of our working in part (a).

    This is shown below, with the relevant parts in red:

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(R_2) &= \color{red}{\frac{3}{8} \times \frac{3}{7}} \color{darkblue}{+ \frac{3}{8} \times \frac{2}{7} + }\color{red}{\frac{2}{8} \times \frac{3}{7}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the calculation we require is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(R_1' | R_2) &= \dfrac{3/8 \times 3/7 + 2/8 \times 3/7}{21/56} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Given that the second pen is red, the probability that the first pen is $\textbf{not}$ red is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\boxed{\mathrm{P}(R_1' | R_2) = \dfrac{15}{21} \hspace{1em}\left(=\frac{5}{7}\right)} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
