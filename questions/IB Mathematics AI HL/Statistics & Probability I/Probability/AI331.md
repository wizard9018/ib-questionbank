# AI331

**Maximum mark: 6**

A bag contains $8$ blue marbles and $4$ red marbles.

Ray selects two marbles from the bag in succession, **without
replacement**.

1.  Complete the missing probabilities on the tree diagram below.
     [2 marks]

    ![7916935d3ad4a54ba27494d71ca4e50fd127fc2a.svg](images/7916935d3ad4a54ba27494d71ca4e50fd127fc2a.svg)

2.  Determine the probability of:

    1.  Ray selecting two red marbles.

    2.  Ray selecting two marbles of different colour. [4 marks]

2.
    1.  Let $B_1$ = {$1$st marble is blue},
        $R_1$ = {$1$st marble is red},

        $B_2$ = {$2$nd marble is blue},

        $R_2$ = {$2$nd marble is red}.

        The paths highlighted in red on the tree diagram below represent
        the outcome that Ray selects two red marbles.

        ![8ee74b27a1680445bc6a262586c4a687de21599b.svg](images/8ee74b27a1680445bc6a262586c4a687de21599b.svg)

        Hence the probability of Ray selecting two red marbles is

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr(two red marbles)} &= \text{Pr}(R_1) \cdot \text{Pr}(R_2|R_1) \\
        &= \dfrac{4}{12} \cdot \dfrac{3}{11}\\[6pt]
        & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.0909
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The outcome that Ray selects two marbles of different colour is
        represented by the paths highlighted in red on the following
        tree diagram.

        ![9346b34d733d19267207f748fe535baa5989c43c.svg](images/9346b34d733d19267207f748fe535baa5989c43c.svg)

        Based off this tree diagram, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr(different colour)} &= \text{Pr}(B_1) \cdot \text{Pr}(R_2|B_1) + \text{Pr}(R_1) \cdot \text{Pr}(B_2|R_1)\\
        &= (\dfrac{8}{12} \cdot \dfrac{4}{11}) + (\dfrac{4}{12} \cdot \dfrac{8}{11})\\[6pt]
        & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.485
        \end{aligned}
        $$
