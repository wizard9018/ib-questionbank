# AI328

**Maximum mark: 6**

Samantha collects a basket of apples, however only $80\%$ of them are
ripe. The probability that a ripe apple has a worm inside the apple is
$p$. The probability that an unripe apple has a worm inside the apple is
$0.1$.

1.  Complete the following tree diagram. [2 marks]

    ![0a82a80da851206114f7908f2d3650e68bb7dea3.svg](images/0a82a80da851206114f7908f2d3650e68bb7dea3.svg)

2.  Find the value of $p$, assuming that exactly half of all apples have
    worms inside. [4 marks]

2.  Let $R$ = {the apple chosen is ripe},
    $W$ = {the apple has a worm inside}.

    The assumption that half of all apples have worms inside implies
    that the probability that an apple randomly chosen has a worm inside
    will be $0.5$, or $\text{Pr}(W) = 0.5$. This event can be
    illustrated by the paths highlighted in red in the following tree
    diagram.

    ![bae6ae08b65c895b7ea02ca1c3ef428c51f3ec5e.svg](images/bae6ae08b65c895b7ea02ca1c3ef428c51f3ec5e.svg)

    Using the Law of Total Probability, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(W) = \text{Pr}(R) \cdot \text{Pr}(W|R) + \text{Pr}(R') \cdot \text{Pr}(W|R')
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting $\text{Pr}(W) = 0.5$ and other given values into the
    formula above, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0.5 &= 0.8 \cdot p + 0.2 \cdot 0.1 \\[3pt]
    0.5 &= 0.8p + 0.02 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{5.5em} p = 0.6  \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
