# AI338

**Maximum mark: 6**

A bag contains $6$ white and $4$ orange table tennis balls. Jack selects
a ball at random from the bag and then, afterwards, John selects a ball
at random from the bag.

1.  Complete the tree diagram. [3 marks]

    ![4833dbdc60abfa0bd07aaf9560193c577886f077.svg](images/4833dbdc60abfa0bd07aaf9560193c577886f077.svg)

2.  Find the probability that John chooses a white ball. [3 marks]

2.  Let $W_1$ = {Jack takes a white ball},  $O_1$ = {Jack takes an
    orange ball},

    $W_2$ = {John takes a white ball},  $O_2$ = {John takes an orange
    ball}.

    The event that John chooses a white ball can be illustrated by the
    paths highlighted on the tree diagram below.

    ![71f021dfad5636bd378edeec86f457adfd29e048.svg](images/71f021dfad5636bd378edeec86f457adfd29e048.svg)

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(W_2) &= \text{Pr}(W_1) \cdot \text{Pr}(W_2\hspace{0.1em}|\hspace{0.1em}W_1) + \text{Pr}(O_1) \cdot \text{Pr}(W_2\hspace{0.1em}|\hspace{0.1em}O_1) \\[6pt]
    &= \left(\dfrac{3}{5} \cdot \dfrac{5}{9}\right) + \left(\dfrac{2}{5} \cdot \dfrac{2}{3}\right) \\[12pt]
    &=0.6
    \end{aligned}
    $$
