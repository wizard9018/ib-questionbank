# AI326

**Maximum mark: 6**

A lego storage box is left with $4$ blue and $7$ red bricks. Max takes a
brick from the box at random and attaches it to his new castle. He then
chooses another brick from the same box at random.

1.  Complete the following tree diagram. [3 marks]

    ![1b542676ffda4969a3e248786ba7a2b435ee7cac.svg](images/1b542676ffda4969a3e248786ba7a2b435ee7cac.svg)

2.  Calculate the probability that both bricks chosen by Max are of the
    same color. [3 marks]

2.  Let $B_1$ = {$1$st brick chosen is blue},
    $B_2$ = {$2$nd brick chosen is blue},

    $R_1$ = {$1$st brick chosen is red},

    $R_2$ = {$2$nd brick chosen is red}.

    The event that both bricks chosen by Max are of the same color can
    be illustrated by the paths highlighted in red in the following tree
    diagram.

    ![e065c7c318a959891289f508cc6cca7418046fc6.svg](images/e065c7c318a959891289f508cc6cca7418046fc6.svg)

    Based off the tree diagram above, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(same color)} &= \text{Pr}(B_1) \cdot \text{Pr}(B_2|B_1) + \text{Pr}(R_1) \cdot \text{Pr}(R_2|R_1) \\[4pt]
    &= (\dfrac{4}{11} \cdot \dfrac{3}{10}) + (\dfrac{7}{11} \cdot \dfrac{6}{10}) \\[7pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.491
    \end{aligned}
    $$
