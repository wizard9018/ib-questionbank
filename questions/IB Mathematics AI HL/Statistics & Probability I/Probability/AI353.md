# AI353

**Maximum mark: 6**

A seafood restaurant conducts a promotional campaign during the month of
April. Over the $30$ days, there will be $15$ days with a $50\%$
discount on drinks and $10$ days with a $50\%$ discount on meals.
However, on $8$ of the days there will be no discount on either drinks
or meals.

1.  Calculate the number of days there will be a $50\%$ discount for
    both drinks and meals. [3 marks]

2.  Find the probability that, for a guest coming to the restaurant for
    dinner on a random day in April, the guest will receive a $50\%$
    discount on either drink or food, but not both.  [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $x$ be the number of days that both drinks and food are
    discounted. Subtracting $x$ from $15$ and $10$ will give us the
    numbers of days that drinks only, or meals only, are discounted
    respectively.

    Let $D$ and $M$ be the days that drinks and meals are discounted
    respectively. Based off this information, we can draw a Venn diagram
    as follows.

    ![b10f6aceba656b72bcc13e59ec949831508b45d0.svg](images/b10f6aceba656b72bcc13e59ec949831508b45d0.svg)

    Summing all of the numbers on the diagram gives a total of $30$.
    Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (15-x) + x + (10-x) + 8 &= 30 \\[3pt]
    33 - x &= 30 \\[3pt]
    x &= 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $\text{Hence there are \(3\) days where both drinks and food are discounted.}$

2.  Provided $x = 3$ found in part (a), we can draw a completed Venn
    diagram illustrating the scenario. The region shaded represents the
    days that one of either drink or food is discounted, but not both.

    ![0d62e2e136acb952e03bdb6651955235c5dfe489.svg](images/0d62e2e136acb952e03bdb6651955235c5dfe489.svg)

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(one of drinks or food is discounted)} &= \dfrac{7 + 12}{30} \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.633
    \end{aligned}
    $$
