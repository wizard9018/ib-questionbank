# AI397

**Maximum mark: 5**

There is a lucky gift machine placed in a shopping mall. In order to
purchase an unknown gift, customers must pay $$\$10$$ and then press a
number to choose a gift box. This gift box will be replaced by another
one of the same value after being picked. There are $15$ gift boxes in
total to choose from, $6$ of which have valuable gifts inside. Six
customers at the shopping mall are waiting in line for their turn to use
the machine.

1.  Find the probability that a customer gets a valuable gift.
    [1 mark]

2.  Find the probability that exactly three of the six customers get a
    valuable [2 marks] gift.

3.  Find the probability that between two and four of the customers get
    a [2 marks] valuable gift.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since there are $6$ valuable gift boxes out of a total of $15$ gift
    boxes, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(get a valuable gift box)} &= \dfrac{6}{15} \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}= 0.4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $X$ be the number of customers who get a valuable gift box out
    of the $6$ customers.

    From part (a), we know that the probability of such a box being
    chosen is $p = 0.4$. Hence, we get
    $X \sim \mathrm{B}\left(6, 0.4 \right)$. This gives

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X = 3) &= \text{BinomPdf}\left(6, \,0.4,\,3 \right) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.276 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  As $X \sim \mathrm{B}\left(6, 0.4 \right)$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(2 \leq X \leq 4) &= \text{BinomCdf}\left(6, \,0.4,\,2, \,4 \right) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.726 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
