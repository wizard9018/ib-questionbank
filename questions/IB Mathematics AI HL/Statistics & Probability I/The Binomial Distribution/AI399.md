# AI399

**Maximum mark: 5**

Terry purchases small pack of Narcissus poeticus seeds. The seed
packaging claims that the germination rate of the seeds is $75\%$.

Terry sows $5$ seeds selected at random in his backyard.

1.  Find the probability that at least one seed will germinate.
    [3 marks]

2.  Find the probability that exactly $3$ seeds will germinate.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X$ be the number of seeds that will germinate. Then we have
    $X~\sim~\mathrm{B}\left(5, 0.75 \right)$. This gives

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X \geq 1) &= 1 - \text{Pr}(X = 0) \\[3pt]
    &= 1 - \text{BinomPdf}\left(5, \,0.75,\,0 \right) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.999 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The probability that exactly $3$ seeds will germinate is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X = 3) &= \text{BinomPdf}\left( 5, \,0.75,\,3 \right) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.264 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
