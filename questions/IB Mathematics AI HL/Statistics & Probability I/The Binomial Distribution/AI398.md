# AI398

**Maximum mark: 6**

Xavier is a very talented high-jumper. When training, he can clear a
$2.1$ metre bar at a constant probability of $\dfrac 3 4$. During one
training session, Xavier attempts this height $7$ times. Let $X$ be the
number of times he clears the bar.

1.  Calculate the probability that Xavier clears the bar exactly $5$
    times. [2 marks]

2.  Find the probability that Xavier clears the bar three or more times.
    [2 marks]

Xavier's training partner, Aaron, can clear the bar of the same height
level at a constant probability of $\dfrac 1 3$. During this training
session, Aaron also attempts this height 7
times.

3.  Find the probability that both Xavier and Aaron clear the
    $2.1$ metre bar [2 marks]three or more times.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since $X \sim \mathrm{B}\left(7, \dfrac 3 4 \right)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X = 5) &= \text{BinomPdf}\left(7, \,\dfrac 3 4,\,5 \right) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.311 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The probability that he clears the bar three or more times is given
    by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X \geq 3) &= \text{BinomCdf}\left(7, \,\dfrac 3 4 ,\,3,\,7\right) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.987 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Let $Y$ be the number of times that Aaron clears the bar in his 7
    attempts. Then we have
    $Y \sim \mathrm{B}\left(7, \dfrac 1 3 \right)$.

    The probability that Aaron clears the bar three or more times is
    given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(Y \geq 3) &= \text{BinomCdf}\left(7, \,\dfrac 1 3 ,\,3,\,7\right) \\[6pt]
    & \approx 0.429 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the probability that both Xavier and Aaron clear the bar three
    or more times can be found by:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{Pr}(X \cap Y \geq 3) &= \mathrm{Pr}(X \geq 3, Y \geq 3) \\[6pt]
    &= (0.987)(0.429) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.423
    \end{aligned}
    $$
