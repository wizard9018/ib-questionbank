# AI837

**Maximum mark: 6**

Jessica is practicing her basketball free throws. Her coach estimates
that her success rate is $60\%$ from each attempt. Jessica practices
with $20$ throws. Let the random variable $X$ be the number of
successful throws she makes out of the $20$.

1.  Write down
    1.  the distribution of the random variable used to model this
        situation.

    2.  the assumptions you are making to use the model. [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the probability that Jessica successfully makes
    1.  exactly $12$ throws.

    2.  $10$ or more throws. [2 marks]

3.  Find
$\\[6pt]$
    1.  $\text{E}$($X$)

    2.  $\text{Var}$($X)$ [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Since we have a set of number of trials (number of throws,
        $n=20$), and a probability of success and failure (probability
        of successful throw, $p=0.6$), we can use the binomial
        distribution. Hence,

        $$
        X \sim \mathrm{B}\left(20,\, 0.6\right)
        $$

    2.  When using a binomial distribution to model a situation, we
        assume the trials are independent of each other.

        Hence, we assume the probability of making a free throw are independent in each throw.

2.  Since $X \sim \mathrm{B}\left(20,\, 0.6 \right)$, we get

    1.  $\text{Pr}(X = 12) = \text{BinomPdf}\left(20, \,0.6,\,12 \right)\hspace{3cm}$\
        $$
        \hspace{-2.5cm}\approx 0.18 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        $$

    2.  $\text{Pr}(X > 10) = \text{Binomcdf}\left(20, \,0.6,\,10,20 \right) \hspace{3cm}$\
        $$
        \hspace{-2.5cm}\approx 0.872 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        $$

3.  Since $X \sim \mathrm{B}\left(20,\, 0.6 \right)$, then

    1.  The mean is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{E}(X)&=np\hspace{4cm}\\[6pt]
        &=20\times 0.6 \\[8pt]
        & \hspace{0.3em}=12
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The variance is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Var}(X)&= np(1-p)\hspace{3cm}\\[6pt]
        &= (20)(0.6)(1-0.6)\\[8pt]
        & \hspace{0.3em} =4.8
        \end{aligned}
        $$
