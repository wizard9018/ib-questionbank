# AI690

**Maximum mark: 7**

A'ja is a keen basketball player. Each time A'ja attempts a free throw, she has a $75\%$ chance of scoring. Suppose she practises 120 free throws and counts the total number of free throws she makes. It can be assumed that the probability of scoring on any given free throw is independent of her other previous free throw attempts.

1.  1.  Write down the mean number of free throws she makes.

    2.  Calculate the variance of the number of free throws she makes. [4 marks]

2.  Find the probability that the number of free throws she makes is less than one standard deviation away from the mean. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $T$ be the number of free throws A'ja makes when practicing $120$ free throws.

    We have $T \sim \mathrm{B}(120,0.75)$.

    1.  Hence, using the formula for the expected value of $T$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{E}(T) &= np \\[6pt]
    &= (120)(0.75) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{= 90} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    2.  Using the formula for the variance of $T$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} \mathrm{Var}(T) &= np(1-p) \\[6pt]
    &= (120)(0.75)(1-0.75) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{= 22.5} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the formula for the standard deviation of $T$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} \sigma &= \sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(T)} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{22.5} \\[7pt]
    &= 4.743...\tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.4em} \mathrm{P}(|T - 90| < 4.743...) &= \mathrm{P}(90-4.743... < T < 90+4.743...) \\[8pt]
    &= \mathrm{P}(85.25... < T < 94.74...) \\[8pt]
    &= \mathrm{P}(86 \leq T \leq 94) \\[8pt]
    &= \text{binomCdf}(120,0.75,86,94) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{= 0.657 \, (3\,\text{s.f.})} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
