# AI727

**Maximum mark: 13**

A university library has two sections, one for books and the other for
journals. The probability that a randomly observed visitor accesses the
books section is 0.85, and the probability that the visitor accesses the
journals section is 0.3. Assume that in each visit, visitors access at
least one of the two sections.

1.  Find the probability that a randomly chosen visitor
    1.  Accesses both the books and journals section,

    2.  Only accesses the journals section. [4 marks]

On a typical day, $120$ visitors visit the
library.

2.  On a typical day, find
    1.  The expected number of visitors to access the books section,

    2.  The probability that more than 40 visitors access the journals
        section.[4 marks]

It is found that $70\%$ of the visitors to the library are students, and
that $82\%$ of these students access the book's section when they visit
the library.

3.
    1.  A library visitor is chosen at random. Find the probability that
        the visitor is a student and accesses the book's section.

    2.  A non-student visitor is chosen at random. Find the probability
        that the visitor accesses the book's
        section.[5 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $B = \{$The visitor accesses the book's section$\}$,
    $J = \{$The visitor accesses the journal's section$\}$.

    1.  Using the combined events formula, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(B\cap J) &= \mathrm{P}(B)+\mathrm{P}(J)-\mathrm{P}(B\cup J) \rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
        &= 0.85+0.3-1 \rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{7mm}\\
        &= 0.15\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{7mm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the result from part (a)(i) we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(B'\cap J) &= \mathrm{P}(J)-\mathrm{P}(B\cap J) \rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
        &=0.3-0.15\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{7mm}\\
        &=0.15\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{7mm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    1.  Let $X$ be the number of visitors to access the books section on
        a typical day. Then $X\sim \mathrm{B}(120,0.85)$ and, therefore,
        the expected number is calculated by
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{E}[X] &= (120)(0.85)\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
        &= 102\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Let $Y$ be the number of access to the journals section on a
        typical day. Then $Y\sim \mathrm{B}(120,0.3)$. Hence the
        probability of more than 40 visitors accessing the journal
        section can be found by
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(Y>40) &= \mathrm{binomCdf}(120,0.3,41,120)\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
        &\approx 0.184\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\hspace{3em}[\text{ using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.
    1.  Using the conditional probability formula, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(B\,|\,S) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(B\cap S)}{\mathrm{P}(S)} \rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
        0.82&=\dfrac{\mathrm{P}(B\cap S)}{0.7}\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
        \mathrm{P}(B\cap S) &= (0.70)(0.82)\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
        &= 0.574\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the conditional probability formula, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(B\,|\,S') &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(B\cap S')}{\mathrm{P}(S')} \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
        &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(B)-\mathrm{P}(B\cap S)}{1-\mathrm{P}(S)}\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
        &=\dfrac{0.85-0.574}{1-0.7} \rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
        &=0.92\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
