# AI1236

**Maximum mark: 18**

At an international airport, passengers are either frequent flyers or not. Recently the airport has installed a new passenger screening security machine. Data from the passenger screening numbers shows the following:

-  40\% of all passengers are frequent flyers.

-  Frequent flyers have a 10\% chance of being selected for random screening.

- Other passengers have a 25\% chance of being selected.

- If selected, there is a 5\% chance that a passenger triggers a false alert.

- If not selected, the chance of a false alert is 0\%.

1. Draw a labelled tree diagram to represent this situation, showing all probabilities.[4 marks]

2. Find the probability that a randomly chosen passenger is selected for screening.[2 marks]

3. Find the probability that a randomly selected passenger triggers a false alert.[2 marks]

4. Given that a passenger is selected, find the probability that they were a frequent flyer.[2 marks]

The airport screens an average of 3200 passengers per day.

5. Estimate how many passengers are expected to trigger a false alert each day.[2 marks]

A company needs to send an employee on five work trips, passing through the international airport in the next month. Each trip is independent of each other.

6. The company wants to know the following probabilities with respect to the upcoming trips.

    1. Using your answer from part (c),  find the probability they trigger **at least one** false alert on their 5 trips this month.

    2. Find the probability they trigger **exactly three** false alerts. Give your answer in the form $a \times 10^k$ where $1 \leq a < 10 \text{ and } k \text{ is an integer}$. [6 marks]

4. We are given that the passenger is selected, which we found the probability of in part (b). 

    When we know some information about the outcome, it is conditional probability. Using the appropriate formula, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(\text{Frequent} \mid \text{Selected}) 

    &= \frac{\mathrm{P}(\text{Frequent} \cap \text{Selected})}{\mathrm{P}(\text{Selected})} \\[6pt]

    &= \frac{0.4 \times 0.10}{0.19} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    &= 0.2105\ldots\\[6pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$
