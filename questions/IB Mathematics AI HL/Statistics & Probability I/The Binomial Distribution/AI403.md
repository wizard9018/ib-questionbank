# AI403

**Maximum mark: 6**

In the Monty Hall game show, there are three doors that hide either a
goat or a car. Two of the doors hide a goat and the remaining door hides
the car. In the game, each player is asked to choose one door at random.
Suppose that there are $15$ players playing the game in a particular
month.

1.  Find the expected number of players who win a car. [2 marks]

2.  Find the probability that
$\\[6pt]$
    1.  exactly five players win a car;

    2.  more than five players win a car. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X$ be the number of players winning a car.
    We have $X \sim \mathrm{B}\left(15,\dfrac 1 3\right)$.

    Hence, using the formula for the expected value of $X$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{E}[X] &= np \\[3pt]
    &= 15\times\dfrac{1}{3} \hspace{3em} \\[6pt]
    &= 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  The probability that exactly five players win a car is given by
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(X = 5) &= \text{binomPdf}\left(15,\,\dfrac 1 3,\,5\right) \hspace{1.35em} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.214\hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The probability that more than five players win a car is given
        by
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.15em} \mathrm{P}(X > 5) &= \text{binomCdf}\left(15,\,\dfrac 1 3,\,6,\,15\right) \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.382\hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$
