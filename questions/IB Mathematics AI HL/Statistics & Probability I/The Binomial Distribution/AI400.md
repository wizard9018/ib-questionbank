# AI400

**Maximum mark: 5**

Alex works as a health insurance agent for Medical Benefits Fund. The
probability that he succeeds in selling an insurance policy to a given
customer aged $25$ or older is $0.45$.

On a given day, he interacts with $8$ customers in this age range. Find
the probability that he will sell

1.  at least one insurance policy on this day; [3 marks]

2.  exactly $2$ insurance policies on this day. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X$ be the number of insurance policies that Alex sells on this
    day. Then we have $X~\sim~\mathrm{B}\left(8, 0.45 \right)$. This
    gives
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X \geq 1) &= 1 - \text{Pr}(X = 0) \\[3pt]
    &= 1 - \text{BinomPdf}\left(8, \,0.45,\,0 \right) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.992 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The probability that he will sell exactly $2$ insurance policies on
    the day is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X = 2) &= \text{BinomPdf}\left(8, \,0.45,\,2 \right) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.157 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
