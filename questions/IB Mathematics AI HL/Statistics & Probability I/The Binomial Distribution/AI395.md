# AI395

**Maximum mark: 6**

Jennifer has a collection of $15$ pairs of shoes, $5$ of which are sport
shoes, which she uses for exercise. Every working day she picks a pair
of shoes by random to go to work.

1.  Find the probability that on a given day Jennifer chooses a pair of
    sports shoes to go to work.[1 mark]

Jennifer has to work for $24$ days next month. Find the probability that
she will choose

2.  a pair of sports shoes $10$ times; [2 marks]

3.  a pair of non-sports shoes at least $16$ times. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since there are $5$ pairs of sports shoes out of $15$ pairs in her
    collection, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(chooses sports shoes)} &= \dfrac{5}{15} \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.333
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $X$ be the number of times that Jennifer will choose a pair of
    sports shoes in those $24$ days. From part (a), we know that the
    probability that she will pick a pair of sports shoes on a given day
    is $p = \dfrac 1 3$.

    Hence, we get $X \sim \mathrm{B}\left(24, \dfrac 1 3 \right)$.
      This
    gives
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X = 10) &= \text{BinomPdf}\left(24, \,\dfrac 1 3, \,10 \right) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.114 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the result of part (a), we can deduce
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(choose non-sports shoes)} &= 1 - \dfrac 1 3 \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac 2 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Let $Y$ represent the number of times Jennifer
    picks a pair of non-sports shoes. Then we have
    $Y \sim \mathrm{B}\left( 24, \dfrac 2 3\right)$. This gives

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(Y \geq 16) &= \text{BinomCdf}\left(24, \,\dfrac 2 3 ,\,16,\,24\right) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.594 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
