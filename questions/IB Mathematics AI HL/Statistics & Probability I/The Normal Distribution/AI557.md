# AI557

**Maximum mark: 6**

The amount of time it takes Jenny to drive from Adelaide to the Flinders
Ranges can be modelled by a normal distribution with a mean of $6.5$
hours and a standard deviation of $0.4$ hours. She is about to do a
round trip, starting at Adelaide, travelling to Flinders Ranges, and
then back to Adelaide.

Find the probability that Jenny will spend $12$ hours or less driving
throughout the round trip. State any assumptions that you make.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let $T_1$ and $T_2$ be the time it takes Jenny to complete the departure
and return trips, respectively. Let $T$ be the total time taken for the
whole round trip.

By
 $\text{assuming the independence of } T_1 \text{ and } T_2$,
 we get
 $$
 T = T_1 + T_2

 $$
 Hence,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\rm{E}(T) &= \rm{E}(T_1) + \rm{E}(T_2) \\[3pt]
&= 6.5 + 6.5 \\[3pt]
&= 13 \text{ hours}
\end{aligned}
$$
and,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Var}(T) &=  \rm{Var}(T_1) + \rm{Var}(T_2) \\[3pt]
&= 0.4^2 + 0.4^2 \\[3pt]
&= 0.32
\end{aligned}
$$
The standard deviation of $T$ is
$\sigma_T = \sqrt{0.32} \approx 0.5657$.
Hence,
$$
T \sim \rm{N}(13, 0.5657^2)
$$

The probability that Jenny spends less than $12$ hrs driving on the
round trip is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rm{Pr}(T \leq 12) &= \text{normCdf}(0,12,13,0.5657)  \\[6pt]
&\approx 0.0386 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
\end{aligned}
$$

$\text{We also are assuming that Jenny does not stop or take rest breaks during the trip}$
