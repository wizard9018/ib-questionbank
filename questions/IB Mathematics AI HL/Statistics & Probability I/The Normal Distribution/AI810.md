# AI810

**Maximum mark: 6**

The weights of $800$ students are normally distributed with a mean of
$66$ kilograms and a standard deviation of $5$ kilograms.

1.
    1.  On the following diagram, shade the region representing the
        probability that a randomly selected student weighs more than
        $66$ kilograms.

        ![34c25e6975274ad43272fcca3293718be7d03b31.svg](images/34c25e6975274ad43272fcca3293718be7d03b31.svg)

    2.  Write down the weight that is three standard deviations below
        the mean weight.[2 marks]

2.  Find the probability that a randomly selected student weights
    between 65 and 75 kilograms. [2 marks]

3.  Estimate the number of the students with a weight greater than 72
    kilograms.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.

        ![1773f68f6f95a8a7d5adfc3c152ffb231916cc44.svg](images/1773f68f6f95a8a7d5adfc3c152ffb231916cc44.svg)

    2.  As the mean is 66 kg and the standard deviation is 5 kg, the
        weight corresponding to three standard deviations below the mean
        is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        66-3(5) \hspace{0.525em}\enskip=51\text{ kg}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Let $W$ be the weight of the student. We have
    $W \sim \mathrm{N}(66,5^2)$. Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    P(65<W<75) &= \text{normalCdf}(65,75,66,5) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx  0.543 \hspace{3em} [\text{using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    P(W>72) &=P(72<W<+\infty) \\[6pt]
    &= \text{normalCdf}(72,1000,66,5) \\[6pt]
    &\approx  0.1151 \hspace{3em} [\text{using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Thus, the number of students with weight greater than 72 kg is
    estimated as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    800 (0.1151) \hspace{0.525em}\enskip=92
    \end{aligned}
    $$
