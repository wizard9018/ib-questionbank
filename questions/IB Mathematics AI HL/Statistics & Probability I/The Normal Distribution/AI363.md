# AI363

**Maximum mark: 14**

Students at Sydney High School took an examination. The examination
scores, $S$, are normally distributed with mean of $72$ and standard
deviation of $8$.

1.  Sketch the normal distribution diagram of $S$. Shade the region
    representing [2 marks] examination scores between $70$ and $80$.

2.  A student is chosen at random. Calculate the probability that their
    examination score is:

    1.  less than $80$
    2.  higher than $70$

    3.  between $70$ and $80$ [6 marks]

3.  Given that $12\%$ of students' scores are higher than $x$, calculate
    the value [2 marks] of $x$.

4.  It is found that $7$ students' scores are higher than $85$. Estimate
    how many students:
    $\\[6pt]$
    1.  scored less than $59$

    2.  took the test [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The normal distribution of $S$ is illustrated as follows. The shaded
    region represents the examination scores between $70$ and $80$.

    ![68b5ed7305f242063fccf4dcea906ef52fcf98ef.svg](images/68b5ed7305f242063fccf4dcea906ef52fcf98ef.svg)

2.
    1.  The shaded region on the diagram below represents those who
        score less than $80$.

        ![d55fb608b3b95c5e749d22b099a15b247a124df1.svg](images/d55fb608b3b95c5e749d22b099a15b247a124df1.svg)

        Using G.D.C, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr}(S < 80) &= \text{NormCdf}(0,\,80,\,72,\,8) \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.841
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The shaded region on the diagram below represents those who
        score higher than $70$.

        ![dcb57724cc120834c4125bc65d11c1a6f0c7f8b2.svg](images/dcb57724cc120834c4125bc65d11c1a6f0c7f8b2.svg)

        Using G.D.C, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr}(S > 70) &= \text{NormCdf}(70,\,200,\,72,\,8) \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.599
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3.  The shaded region on the diagram sketched in part (a) represents
        those who score between $70$ and $80$.

        Using G.D.C, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr}(70 < S < 80) &= \text{NormCdf}(70,\,80,\,72,\,8) \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.440
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  Those who score higher than $x$ are represented by the region shaded
    below.

    ![09db149e4f4890ad197bdb6d889f048f28f702b4.svg](images/09db149e4f4890ad197bdb6d889f048f28f702b4.svg)

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x &= \text{InvNorm}(1-0.12,\, 72, \, 8) \\[3pt]
    &= \text{InvNorm}(0.88,\, 72, \, 8) \\[3pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 81.4 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.
    1.  Those whose scores are higher than $85$ and those whose scores
        are less than $59$ are represented by the shaded regions below.

        ![c412febb38eede81c383a33cb78d965580157964.svg](images/c412febb38eede81c383a33cb78d965580157964.svg)

        The values $59$ and $85$ are symmetric about the mean value
        (they are both $13$ units from $72$). Hence the probabilities at
        two tails are equal and so are the numbers of students
        represented by these regions.

        $\text{Hence, it is estimated that \(7\) students scored less than \(59\).}$

    2.  Let $N$ be the number of students who took the test. Then we
        have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr}(S < 59) = \dfrac{7}{N}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        where

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr}(S < 59) &= \text{NormCdf}(0,\,59,\,72,\,8) \\[3pt]
        &= 0.0521  \hspace{3em}[\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        0.0521 &= \dfrac{7}{N} \\[6pt]
        \hspace{-4em}&N \approx 134
        \end{aligned}
        $$
