# AI994

**Maximum mark: 15**

The lifespans of a new model of smart television are normally distributed with a mean of $8.3$ years and a standard deviation of $2.2$ years.

1.  A customer buys a television of this model. Find the probability that the television lasts longer than $5$ years.
    [2 marks]

2.  $10\%$ of televisions of this model have a lifespan of less than $m$ years. Find the value of $m$.
    [2 marks]

The manufacturer offers a five-year warranty for this television model. Eight smart televisions of this model are sold on a certain day.

3.  Find the probability that at most one of them will be
    claimed for warranty. [4 marks]

4.  Find the probability that the eighth television sold will be the second one to be claimed for warranty. [3 marks]

As company policy, televisions with a lifespan of less than $3$ years will be replaced with a new one of the same model without repairing.

5.  Find the probability that a television will be replaced with a new one, given that it is claimed for warranty. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $T$ be the lifespan of a television of this model.

    We have $T \sim \mathrm{N}(8.3,2.2^2)$. Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(T > 5) &= \text{normCdf}(5,\infty, 8.3,2.2) \\[6pt]
    &= 0.93319... \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-4.35em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(T > 5) \approx 0.933}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(T < m) &= 0.1 \\[6pt]
    m &=  \text{invNorm}(0.1, 8.3,2.2) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-1.1em}m \approx 5.48 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the answer to part (a), we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(T \leq 5) &= 1 - 0.93319...\\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.06681
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Let $X$ be the
    number of televisions that will be claimed for warranty among the
    eight televisions sold.

    We have $X \sim \mathrm{B}(8, 0.06681)$. Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X \leq 1) &= \text{binomCdf}(8,0.06681,0,1) \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
                    &\hspace{-4.5em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(X \leq 1) \approx 0.905} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
                    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Let $Y$ be the number of televisions that will be claimed for
    warranty among the first seven televisions sold.

    We need to find the probability that exactly one television will be claimed from the first $7$. Hence $Y=1$.

    We have $Y \sim \mathrm{B}(7, 0.06681)$. Hence, the probability that
    the last television sold is the second one claimed for warranty is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    p &= \mathrm{P}(Y = 1) \cdot 0.06681 \\[6pt]
      &= \text{binomPdf}(7, 0.06681, 1) \cdot 0.06681 \\[6pt]
      &= 0.3089 \cdot 0.06681\hspace{0.5em}  \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]\\[6pt]
      &\hspace{-0.75em}\boxed{p \approx 0.0206}
      \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Using the formula for conditional probability, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(T < 3 \hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}T < 5) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(T < 3)}{\mathrm{P}(T < 5)} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\text{normCdf}(-\infty,3,8.3,2.2)}{0.06681} \\[6pt]
    &=\dfrac{0.007996}{0.06681} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-7.4em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(T < 3 \hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}T < 5) \approx 0.120}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
