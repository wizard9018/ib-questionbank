# AI365

**Maximum mark: 6**

The delivery time (in minutes) for a pizza from Pizzeria da Michele
follows a normal distribution with a mean of $30$ and standard deviation
of $5$.

On a particular day, $12$ % of the pizzas were
delivered in more than $k$ minutes.

1.  Find the value of $k$. [2 marks]

As an advertising campaign, Pizzeria da Michele is offering customers a
free pizza coupon if their pizza is not delivered in $37.5$ minutes or
less.

2.  Find the probability that a randomly chosen customer will
    get a free pizza [2 marks] coupon.

3.  Given that Vito gets a free pizza coupon from Pizzeria da
    Michele, find [2 marks] the probability that
    his pizza was delivered in $40$ minutes or more.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $T$ be the delivery time (in minutes) for a pizza. Then we're
    given $T \sim \mathrm{N}(30,\hspace{0.05em}5^2)$. The region shaded
    on the following normal distribution diagram represents the
    percentage of the pizzas delivered in more than $k$ minutes.

    ![53779f1b3bc57091ee2482f861415174ec4f6347.svg](images/53779f1b3bc57091ee2482f861415174ec4f6347.svg)

    So we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(T < k) &= 1 - 0.12 \\[3pt]
    &= 0.88 \\[6pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using G.D.C, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    k &= \text{InvNorm}(0.88,\hspace{0.05em} 30, \hspace{0.05em} 5) \\[3pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 35.9 \text{ min}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  A customer will get a free pizza coupon if his pizza is delivered in
    more than $37.5$ minutes. This probability is represented by the
    shaded region below.

    ![9b05f8b5ae7e7d9a42b7504b005180df2db87f5e.svg](images/9b05f8b5ae7e7d9a42b7504b005180df2db87f5e.svg)

    Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(T > 37.5) &= \text{NormCdf}(37.5,\,200,\,30,\,5) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.0668
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Let $F =$ {Vito gets a free pizza coupon}.
    Using the Conditional Probability rule
    $\text{Pr}(A\hspace{0.1em}|\hspace{0.1em}B) = \dfrac{\text{Pr}(A \cap B)}{\text{Pr}(B)}$,
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}((T > 40)\hspace{0.1em}|\hspace{0.1em}F) &= \dfrac{\text{Pr}((T > 40) \cap F)}{\text{Pr}(F)} \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{\text{Pr}(T > 40)}{\text{Pr}(F)} \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{\text{NormCdf(40,\,200,\,30,\,5)}}{0.067}   \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.0228}{0.067} \hspace{3em}[\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.34
    \end{aligned}
    $$
