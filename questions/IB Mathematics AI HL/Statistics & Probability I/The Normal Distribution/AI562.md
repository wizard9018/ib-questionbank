# AI562

**Maximum mark: 6**

A Japanese sushi restaurant purchases two species of tuna from a seafood
supplier; Yellowfin tuna and Bluefin tuna. The weights of these two tuna
species, in kilograms, are independent and are both normally distributed
with $Y \sim \rm{N}(110, 9)$ for Yellowfin tuna, and
$B \sim \rm{N}(240, 25)$ for Bluefin tuna.

1.  One tuna of each species is selected at random. Find the probability
    that the Bluefin tuna weighs less than two times the weight of the
    Yellowfin tuna. [3 marks]

2.  A Bluefin tuna and two Yellowfin tunas are selected at random. Find
    the probability that the Bluefin tuna weighs less than the total
    weight of the two Yellowfin tunas. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $D$ be equal to the weight of a Bluefin tuna subtract the weight
    of two Yellowfin tunas; $D = B - 2Y$. We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \rm{E}(D) &= \rm{E}(B - 2Y) \\[3pt]
    &= \rm{E}(B) - 2\rm{E}(Y) \\[3pt]
    &= 240 - 2(110) \\[3pt]
    &= 20
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and since the weights of the tunas are
    independent,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \rm{Var}(D) &= \rm{Var}(B - 2Y) \\[3pt]
    &= \rm{Var}(B) + 2^2\rm{Var}(Y) \\[3pt]
    &= 25 + 4(9) \\[3pt]
    &= 61
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we have $D \sim \rm{N}(20,61)$, and therefore

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \rm{Pr}(B < 2Y) &= \rm{Pr}(B - 2Y < 0) \\[3pt]
    &= \rm{Pr}(D < 0) \\[3pt]
    &= \text{normCdf}(-1000,0,20,\sqrt{61}) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.275em} = 0.00522
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $Y_1$ and $Y_2$ represent the weights of the two Yellowfin tunas
    and $S = B - (Y_1 + Y_2)$. We get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \rm{E}(S) &= \rm{E}[B - (Y_1 + Y_2)] \\[3pt]
    &= \rm{E}(B) - [\rm{E}(Y_1) + \rm{E}(Y_2)] \\[3pt]
    &= 240 - (110 + 110) \\[3pt]
    &= 20
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and since the weights of the tunas are
    independent,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \rm{Var}(S) &= \rm{Var}[B - (Y_1 + Y_2)] \\[3pt]
    &= \rm{Var}(B) + \rm{Var}(Y_1) + \rm{Var}(Y_2) \\[3pt]
    &= 25 + 9 + 9 \\[3pt]
    &= 43
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we have $S \sim \rm{N}(20,43)$, and
    therefore
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \rm{Pr}(B < Y_1 + Y_2) &= \rm{Pr}[B - (Y_1 + Y_2) < 0] \\[3pt]
    &= \rm{Pr}(S < 0) \\[3pt]
    &= \text{normCdf}(-1000,0,20,\sqrt{43}) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.275em} = 0.00114
    \end{aligned}
    $$
