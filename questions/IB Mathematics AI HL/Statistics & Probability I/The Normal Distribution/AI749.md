# AI749

**Maximum mark: 6**

The number of hours that a fully charged laptop battery lasts is normally distributed with a mean of 10 hours and a standard deviation of 1.5 hours.

1. On the following diagram, shade the region representing the probability that after a full charge, the battery lasts less than 8 hours.[1 mark]

    ![norma](images/norma.JPG)

The value $a$ hours is exactly two standard deviations below the mean.

2. 1. Write down the value of $a$.

    2. Find the probability that a full charge lasts between $a$ hours and 8 hours.[3 marks]

The manufacturer states that 25\% of batteries will last at least $x$ hours when fully charged.

3.  Find the value of $x$.[2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. We have

    ![normb](images/normb.JPG)

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. 1. As the mean is 10 hours and the standard deviation is 1.5 hours, the number of hours corresponding to two standard deviations below the mean is $7\text{ hours.}$
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2. Let $H$ be the number of hours the battery lasts. We have $H \sim \mathrm{N}(10,1.5^2)$. Hence we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        P(7<H<8) &= \text{normalCdf}(7,8,10,1.5) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
        &= 0.06846... \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{-6.4em}\boxed{P(7<H<8) = 0.0685\,\text{(3 s.f.)}} \hspace{4em} [\text{using G.D.C.}]\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3. This is the minimum number of hours the longest lasting $25\%$ of batteries last. Therefore $75\%$ of batteries last $\textbf{less}$ than this. Using G.D.C., we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    P(H<x) &= 0.75 \\[6pt]
    x &= \text{InvNorm}(0.75,10,1.5)\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
      &= 11.01... \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.9em}\boxed{x= 11.0\,\text{hours (3 s.f.)}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}\hspace{3em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
