# AI1077

**Maximum mark: 5**

Darwin is a fisherman. He catches different types of fish.

After years of recording his catches he notes that the weights of the fish are normally distributed with a mean weight of $2.45\,$kg and a standard deviation of $0.545\,$kg.

A _Lunker_ is a fish that weighs over $3.5\,$kg.
1. Find the probability the next fish he catches is a _Lunker_. [2 marks]

A small fish is called a _Minnow_ and it weighs less than $w$ kg. Darwin expects  $3$  _Minnows_ out of every $60$ fish he catches.

2.  Find the value of $w$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Let the continuous random variable $W$ represent the weight of the fish. $W$ is measured in kilograms.

    As the weights are normally distributed it can be said that $W  \sim \mathrm{N}(2.45,\, 0.545^2)$.

    A _Lunker_ weighs over $3.5\,$kg, which is represented by the shaded region on the diagram below.

    ![norm1](images/norm1.png)

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(W>3.5) &= \text{normCdf}(3.5,\,\infty,\, 2.45,\, 0.545)\\
        &=0.0270
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The probability the next fish he catches is a _Lunker_ is $0.0270$

2. The probability he will catch a _Minnow_ is $\dfrac{3}{60}=\dfrac{1}{20}$.

    ![norm2](images/norm2.png)

    We need to use the inverse normal function to find the value of $w$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(W<w)&=\dfrac{1}{20}\\
        w&=\text{invNorm}\left(\frac{1}{20}, 2.45, 0.545\right)\\
        &=1.5535...
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The value of $w$ is $1.55\, \text{kg}$
