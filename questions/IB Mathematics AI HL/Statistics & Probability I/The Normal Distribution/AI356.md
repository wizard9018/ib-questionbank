# AI356

**Maximum mark: 6**

The length of newborn babies is normally distributed with a mean of $51$
cm and standard deviation $2$ cm.

![fc55f92651c428eb4a1e38be9287f6381645f4b9.svg](images/fc55f92651c428eb4a1e38be9287f6381645f4b9.svg)

1.  On the normal distribution diagram, shade the region representing
    the [2 marks] probability that a baby, chosen
    at random, is less than $55$ cm long.

2.  A baby is chosen at random.
    1.  The probability that the length of the baby is greater than $X$
        cm is equal to the probability that the length of the baby is
        less than $55$ cm. Calculate the value of $X$.

    2.  Calculate the probability that the length of the baby is greater
        than [4 marks] one standard deviation below
        the mean.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The region shaded below represents the probability that the baby is
    less than $55$ cm long.

    ![4248d13fbda1d3443ba0b25b04a5c5fc4bfe649e.svg](images/4248d13fbda1d3443ba0b25b04a5c5fc4bfe649e.svg)

2.
    1.  The difference between $55$ and $51$ is $4$ cm, which is $2$
        standard deviations from the mean. Hence $X$ must be $2$
        standard deviations below the mean for equal probabilities.

        ![4822bd90fd0b12c9ef30fd3a1d9e1d66c0ed05ba.svg](images/4822bd90fd0b12c9ef30fd3a1d9e1d66c0ed05ba.svg)

        Hence,
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        X = 47 \text{ cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The length of a baby that is one standard deviation below the
        mean is $51 - 2 = 49$ cm. The region shaded below represents the
        probability that the length of the baby is greater than one
        standard deviation below the mean.

        ![7f37c5b3f3b776414c195c6baa96a7ae38234ae4.svg](images/7f37c5b3f3b776414c195c6baa96a7ae38234ae4.svg)

        Using G.D.C, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr(length of baby} > 49 \text{cm}) &= \text{NormCdf}(49, 100, 51, 2) \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.841
        \end{aligned}
        $$
