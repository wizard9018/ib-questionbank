# AI717

**Maximum mark: 6**

A company produces automatic activation devices for parachutes. The
devices are designed to open the parachutes at a certain altitude above
ground level. The altitude at which they open is found to be normally
distributed with a mean of $200$ meters and a standard deviation of $10$
meters.

1.
    1.  On the following diagram, shade the region representing the
        probability that a randomly selected device opens the parachutes
        at less than 200 meters above ground level.

        ![91fac421c74aa8c1a557a1247b641a808c03e610.svg](images/91fac421c74aa8c1a557a1247b641a808c03e610.svg)

    2.  Write down the altitude that is two standard deviations above
        the mean altitude.[2 marks]

2.  Find the probability that a randomly selected device opens the
    parachute between 195 and 208 meters above ground level.[2 marks]

A customer wants to purchase devices whose automatic altitude activation
is in the highest 20% of the device population.

3.  Find the minimum elevation at which a device activates to
    sell to this customer.[2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.

        ![88898b288d66aa3f490ee1f146e84ae66264f416.svg](images/88898b288d66aa3f490ee1f146e84ae66264f416.svg)

    2.  As the mean is 200 m and standard deviation is 10 m, the
        altitude corresponding to two standard deviations above the mean
        is $220\text{ m}$

2.  Let $H$ be the height at which the device opens the parachute. We
    have $H \sim \mathrm{N}(200,10^2)$. Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    P(195<H<208) &= \text{normalCdf}(195,208,200,10) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 0.48 \hspace{3em} [\text{using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The minimum elevation for the highest $20\%$ is the same as the
    highest elevation for the lowest $80\%$. Hence, using G.D.C., we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    P(H<x) &= 0.8 \\[6pt]
    x &= \text{InvNorm}(0.8,200,10)\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 208
    \end{aligned}
    $$
