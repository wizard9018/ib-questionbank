# AI558

**Maximum mark: 6**

Stephen owns two sports accessories shops and one fitness centre that
operate independently. The monthly revenue, in thousands of USD, from
the two sports accessories shops are both normally distributed with
$S \sim \rm{N}(18,1.8^2)$ and the monthly revenue from the fitness
centre is normally distributed with $F \sim \rm{N}(15,1.2^2)$.

Consider the total monthly revenue of the three businesses,
$T = S_1 + S_2 + F$.

1.  For the distribution of $T$, determine
    1.  the mean;

    2.  the variance. [4 marks]

2.  Find P$(T \geq 50)$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using $\rm{E}(a_1 X_1 + a_2 X_2 + ... +a_n X_n) = a_1 \rm{E}(X_1) + a_2 \rm{E}(X_2) + ... + a_n \rm{E}(X_n)$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \rm{E}(T) &= \rm{E}(S_1 + S_2 + F)  \\[3pt]
                 &= \rm{E}(S_1) + \rm{E}(S_2) + \rm{E}(F) \\[3pt]
                 &= 18 + 18 + 15 \\[6pt]
        &= 51 \text{ thousand USD}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Since $S_1$, $S_2$ and $F$ are independent, we have

        $\rm{Var}(a_1 X_1 + a_2 X_2 + ... + a_n X_n) = a_1^2 \rm{Var}(X_1) + a_2^2 \rm{Var}(X_2) + ... + a_n^2 \rm{Var}(X_n)$, and hence
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \rm{Var}(T) &= \rm{Var}(S_1 + S_2 + F)  \\[3pt]
        &= \rm{Var}(S_1) + \rm{Var}(S_2) + \rm{Var}(F) \\[3pt]
        &= 1.8^2 + 1.8^2 + 1.2^2 \\[6pt]
        &= 7.92
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  From part (a) we have determined that $T \sim \rm{N}(51,7.92)$. Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \rm{P}(T \geq 50) &= \text{normCdf}\left(50, \infty, 51, \sqrt{7.92}\right)  \\[3pt]
                &= \text{normCdf}\left(50, \infty, 51, 2.814\right)  \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.639
    \end{aligned}
    $$
