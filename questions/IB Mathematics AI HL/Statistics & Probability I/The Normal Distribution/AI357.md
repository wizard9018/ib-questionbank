# AI357

**Maximum mark: 6**

The runtime, $R$, of lithium batteries made by a battery manufacturing
company follows a normal distribution. $R$ is measured in hours of
continuous use in a camping head lamp.

The normal distribution curve of $R$ is shown below.

![ea19a30bee9041db718ef8c59822d9d75879bbb1.svg](images/ea19a30bee9041db718ef8c59822d9d75879bbb1.svg)

1.  Write down the mean runtime of the batteries. [1 mark]

The standard deviation of the runtime of the batteries is $12$
hours.

2.  Find the probability that $85 \leq R \leq 100$, for a
    randomly chosen battery. [2 marks]

The company states that $95\hspace{0.1em}\%$ of the batteries have a
runtime of at least $n$ hours.

3.  Find the value of $n$. Give your answer correct to the
    nearest integer. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5muR\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu = 98$

2.  The probability that $85 \leq R \leq 100$ is represented by the
    shaded region below.

    ![5a1d37283f927555435f8560fcc1f059467fa551.svg](images/5a1d37283f927555435f8560fcc1f059467fa551.svg)

    Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(85 \leq R \leq 100) &= \text{NormCdf}(85,100,98,12) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.427
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The shaded region below represents $95\%$ of the batteries having a
    runtime of at least $n$ hours.

    ![eab37a177e78174d252603c86b8f16383579b175.svg](images/eab37a177e78174d252603c86b8f16383579b175.svg)

    The unshaded region represents a probability of $1 - 0.95 = 0.05$.
    Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    n &= \text{InvNorm}(0.05,98,12) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 78
    \end{aligned}
    $$
