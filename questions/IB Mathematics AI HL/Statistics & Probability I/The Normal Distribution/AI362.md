# AI362

**Maximum mark: 6**

The amount of sugar in $100$ grams of grapes, $S$, follows a normal
distribution with a mean of $16$ grams and standard deviation $1$ gram.

1.  Find the probability that $100$ grams of grapes chosen at random
    contains [2 marks] between $15.5$ grams and $16.2$ grams of sugar.

2.  $90\%$ of randomly chosen $100$ gram portions of grapes contain less
    than $k$ [2 marks] grams of sugar. Calculate the value of $k$.

3.  For $100$ grams of grapes chosen at random,
    $\mathrm{Pr}(a < S < b) = 0.98$. If $16 - a$ [2 marks] is equal to
    $b - 16$, calculate the values of $a$ and $b$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The normal distribution of amount of sugar in $100$ grams of grapes
    is illustrated as follows. Note that the shaded region represents
    the probability that $100$ grams of grapes contains between $15.5$
    grams and $16.2$ grams of sugar.

    ![8e106188a23f9822abdb2a09590500a692331720.svg](images/8e106188a23f9822abdb2a09590500a692331720.svg)

    Using G.D.C., we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(15.5 < S < 16.2) &= \text{NormCdf}(15.5,\,16.2, \,16, \,1) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.271
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The shaded region below represents the percentage of $100$ gram
    portions of grapes that contain less than $k$ grams of sugar.

    ![9c9e5e95a3f26fbfaae85092261771b4598f2d41.svg](images/9c9e5e95a3f26fbfaae85092261771b4598f2d41.svg)

    Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    k &= \text{InvNorm}(0.9,\, 16, \, 1) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 17.3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    16 - a &= b - 16 \\[3pt]
    b + a &= 32 \\[3pt]
    \dfrac{a+b}{2} &= 16
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    So $16$ is the midpoint between $a$ and $b$.
    Let's illustrated this on the normal distribution diagram below.

    ![ab37891fcaa378afa8b441d9cabd4cc77be7353f.svg](images/ab37891fcaa378afa8b441d9cabd4cc77be7353f.svg)

    Since the area in the middle is $98\%$, the area on each tail will
    be

    $(100\% - 98\%)/2 = 1\%$.

    Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a &= \text{InvNorm}(0.01,\, 16, \, 1) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 13.7 \\[8pt]
    b &= \text{InvNorm}(1-0.01,\, 16, \, 1) \\[3pt]
    &= \text{InvNorm}(0.99,\, 16, \, 1) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 18.3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
