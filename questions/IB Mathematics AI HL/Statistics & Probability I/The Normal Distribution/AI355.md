# AI355

**Maximum mark: 6**

The weight ($W$) of tomatoes grown by a farmer follows a normal
distribution. $W$ is measured in grams. The normal distribution curve of
$W$ is shown below.

![ae75b3a281927c87d492ded306bf629d12509b03.svg](images/ae75b3a281927c87d492ded306bf629d12509b03.svg)

1.  Write down the mean weight of tomatoes. [1 mark]

The standard deviation of the weights of tomatoes is $30$ grams.

2.  The farmer sorts the tomatoes before selling. He chooses to
    group[2 marks] Medium sized tomatoes, with a weight in the range
    of $100 \leq W \leq 150$. Estimate the percentage of tomatoes that
    are classified as Medium.

3.  The farmer produces tomato juice from the Small sized
    tomatoes, [3 marks] weights: $W \leq A$. Find the value of $A$ if
    $10\%$ of all tomatoes are used for juice production. Give your
    answer correct to two decimal places.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The mean value is in the middle of the range of the normal
    distribution, which is also the $x$-value at which the curve reaches
    its highest level. By inspection we can see that

    $$
    \mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5muW\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu = 120 \text{ grams}
    $$

2.  The region shaded below represents the percentage of Medium sized
    tomatoes.

    ![a3ae36f4e7d7de5915e7e20cb770d1a7e2465c41.svg](images/a3ae36f4e7d7de5915e7e20cb770d1a7e2465c41.svg)

    Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(100 \leq W \leq 150) &= \text{NormCdf}(100,\,150,\,120,\,30) \\[3pt]
    &\approx 0.5888... \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 58.9\%
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The region shaded below represents the percentage of Small sized
    tomatoes.

    ![9d21e21741b3bef1d68058ea683a4a3fc58a0e96.svg](images/9d21e21741b3bef1d68058ea683a4a3fc58a0e96.svg)

    Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \text{InvNorm}(0.1,\,120,\,30) \\[6pt]
    &= 81.55...\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 81.55
    \end{aligned}
    $$
