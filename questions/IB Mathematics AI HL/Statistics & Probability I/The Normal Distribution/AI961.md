# AI961

**Maximum mark: 6**

The time it takes to prepare a large pizza at Pizzeria Fratelli Salvo
follows a normal distribution with a mean of $15.3$ minutes and a
standard deviation of $1.8$ minutes. Diego comes to the restaurant and
orders $4$ large pizzas to take away. Due to the oven's small capacity,
the pizzas are prepared one by one, and the time it takes to make each
pizza is independent of another.

1.  Find the mean time, in minutes, for four large pizzas to be
    prepared. [2 marks]

2.  Find the standard deviation of the time, in minutes, it takes to
    prepare four large pizzas. [2 marks]

Diego has requested that his order be ready in one hour.

3.  Find the probability that the restaurant fulfills Diego's
    order in time.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $T$ be the time, in minutes, it takes to make a large pizza,\
    and $S$ be the time, in minutes, it takes to make four large
    pizzas.\
    We have $T \sim \text{N}(15.3, 1.8^2)$ and $S = T_1 + T_2 + T_3 + T_4$.
    Hence, using the properties of the expectation, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{E} (S) &= \text{E}(T_1 + T_2 + T_3 + T_4)  \hspace{2em} \\[12pt]
    &= \text{E} (T_1) + \text{E} (T_2) + \text{E} (T_3) + \text{E} (T_4) \\[12pt]
    &=  15.3 + 15.3 + 15.3 + 15.3\\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-2.2em}\text{E} (S) = 61.2 \text{ minutes}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Since the time it takes to make each pizza is independent, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Var}(S) &=  \text{Var}(T_1 + T_2 + T_3 + T_4) \\[12pt]
    &= \text{Var}(T_1) + \text{Var}(T_2) + \text{Var}(T_3) + \text{Var}(T_4) \\[12pt]
    &= 1.8^2 + 1.8^2 + 1.8^2 + 1.8^2 \\[12pt]
    &= 12.96\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the standard
    deviation of $S$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \sigma_S &= \sqrt{12.96} \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-1.2em}\sigma_S = 3.6 \text{ minutes}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Since $T$ is normally distributed, $S$ is also normally distributed
    with  $S \sim \text{N}(61.2, 3.6^2)$.

    Using $1$ hour $= 60$ minutes, the probability of fulfilling Diego's
    order in time is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{P}(S \leq 60) &= \text{NormalCdf}(0,60,61.2, 3.6)  \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-4.5em}\text{P}(S \leq 60) \approx 0.369
    \end{aligned}
    $$
