# AI1008

**Maximum mark: 13**

The amount of time a fully charged battery lasts in a particular model of wireless headphones is normally distributed with a mean time of $280$ minutes and a standard deviation of $40$ minutes.

1.  Find the probability that
    1.  A full charge lasts more than $300$ minutes.

    2.  A full charge lasts less than $230$ minutes.[3 marks]

Every full charge is independent of another.

2.  Find the probability that two consecutive charges both last less
    than $230$ minutes.[2 marks]

Charlotte has this particular model of headphones. She fully charges her headphones each day and uses them until the battery fully discharges. She keeps a record of the number of days the charge lasts less than $230$ minutes.

3.  Find the probability that the battery lasts less than $230$ minutes on exactly four days in a 7 day week.[3 marks]

Charlotte will replace the headphones if the charge lasts less than $230$ minutes at least three times in a week.

4.  Find the probability that Charlotte will replace the headphones. [2 marks]

5. Find the probability that Charlotte will replace the headphones, given that the battery lasts less than $230$ minutes at least one day in a week.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 1. Let $D$ be the duration of a full charge in minutes.

        Hence $D \sim \mathrm{N}(280,40^2)$, and we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(D>300) &= \mathrm{normCdf}(300,\infty,280,40)\\[8pt]
        &\hspace{-5.4em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(D>300) \approx 0.309}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2. Using again that $D \sim \mathrm{N}(280,40^2)$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(D<230) &= \mathrm{normCdf}(-\infty,230,280,40)\\[8pt]
        &\hspace{-5.4em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(D<230) \approx 0.106}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2. Let $D_1$ be the duration of the first charge.

    Let $D_2$ be the duration of the second charge.

    Using the formula for independent events, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(D_1 < 230 \cap D_2 < 230) &= \mathrm{P}(D_1 < 230) \times \mathrm{P}(D_2 < 230)  \\[12pt]
    &= 0.106 \times 0.106 \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-11em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(D_1 < 230 \cap D_2 < 230) \approx 0.0112}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3. Let $X$ represent the number of days during a 7 day week that the battery lasts less than $230$ minutes. From part (a) (ii) we have $\mathrm{P}(D<230) = 0.106$.

    Therefore $X \sim \mathrm{B}(7,0.106)$ and we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X=4) &= \mathrm{binomPdf}(7,0.106,4)\\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-4.4em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(X=4) \approx 0.00316}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4. Using again that $X \sim \mathrm{B}(7,0.106)$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X \geq 3) &= \mathrm{P}(3 \leq X \leq 7) \\[12pt]
    &= \mathrm{binomCdf}(7,0.106,3,7)\\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-4.4em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(X \geq 3) \approx 0.0300}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5. Using the formula for conditional probability, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X \geq 3 | X \geq 1)&=\dfrac{\mathrm{P}(X \geq 3 \cap X \geq 1)}{\mathrm{P}(X\geq 1)}\\[12pt]
    &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(X \geq 3)}{\mathrm{P}(X\geq 1)}\\[12pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.0300}{\mathrm{binomCdf}(7,0.106,1,7)}\\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-7.5em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(X \geq 3 | X \geq 1) \approx 0.0552}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
