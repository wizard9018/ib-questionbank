# AI358

**Maximum mark: 13**

The time, $T$, spent by students on their homework on a weekly basis is
normally distributed with mean of $8$ hours and standard deviation of
$2$ hours.

1.  Sketch the normal distribution diagram of $T$. Label the mean, then
    label one and two standard deviations above and below the mean. [2 marks]

2.  State how many standard deviation $T = 12$ is away from the mean.
    [1 mark]

3.  A student is chosen at random. Calculate the probability that this
    student spends more than $11$ hours on his homework for the week.[2 marks]

4.
    1.  A student is chosen at random. Calculate
        $\text{P}(7 \leq T \leq 10)$.

    2.  Estimate how many students in group of $150$ students spend
        between $7$ hours to $10$ hours on their homework on a weekly basis.[4 marks]

5.  $90\%$ of students spend less than $x$ hours on their homework, calculate the value of $x$.
    [2 marks]

6.  Find the values of $a$ and $b$ in the diagram below. [2 marks]

3.  The region shaded below represents the probability that this student
    spent more than $11$ hours on his homework for the week.

    ![e594f5a492b7aa001f06101871360fda2f213c4b.svg](images/e594f5a492b7aa001f06101871360fda2f213c4b.svg)

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(T > 11) &= \text{NormCdf}(11,100,8,2) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.0668  \hspace{3em}[\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.
    1.  The region shaded below represents the probability for
        $7 \leq T \leq 10$.

        ![c06aa49ba7a7e86d564eff9f90d19d9deb15fb2b.svg](images/c06aa49ba7a7e86d564eff9f90d19d9deb15fb2b.svg)

        We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr}(7 \leq T \leq 10) &= \text{NormCdf}(7,10,8,2) \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.533  \hspace{3em}[\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Let $Y$ be the number of students who spent between $7$ hours to
        $10$ hours in the group.

        We have $Y \sim \mathrm{B}(150, 0.533)$. That gives
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        E(Y) &= np \\[3pt]
        &= 150 \times 0.533 \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 80
        \end{aligned}
        $$

5.  The region shaded below represents $90\%$ of students spend less
    than $x$ hours on their homework.

    ![e5893e8c87abaa974b2a392a46f12fafbb97e5e6.svg](images/e5893e8c87abaa974b2a392a46f12fafbb97e5e6.svg)

    Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x &= \text{InvNorm}(0.9,8,2) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 10.6 \text{ hours}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

6.  Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a &= \text{InvNorm}(0.25,8,2) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 6.65 \text{ hours} \\
    \\
    b &= \text{InvNorm}(1-0.25,8,2) \\
    &= \text{InvNorm}(0.75,8,2) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 9.35 \text{ hours}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
