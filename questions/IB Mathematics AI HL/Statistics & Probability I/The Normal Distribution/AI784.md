# AI784

**Maximum mark: 17**

Professor Smith teaches a calculus course to a group of students in a
school. He has noticed that the length of the lectures he gives follows
a normal distribution with mean $m$ minutes.

There is a 20% chance that any lecture lasts at most $48.6$ minutes.

1.  Sketch a diagram to represent this probability.[2 marks]

There is also a 20% chance that any lecture lasts longer than $55.4$
minutes.

2.  Show that $m=52$.[2 marks]

The standard deviation of the number of minutes a lecture lasts is $4$
minutes.

3.  Find the probability that a
    lecture

    1.  lasts between $45$ and $51$ minutes;
    2.  lasts more than $55$ minutes.[4 marks]

There is a 70% chance that a lecture lasts less than $x$
minutes.

4.  Find the value of $x$.[2 marks]

Professor Smith delivers one lecture per day to this group of students, Monday to Friday. It can be assumed that the length of each lecture is independent.

5. Find the probability that the lecture on Monday lasts between $45$ and $51$ minutes but the lecture on Tuesday lasts more than $55$ minutes.[3 marks]

6. Calculate the probability that during a given week, at least 3 lectures last more than 55 minutes.[4 marks]

2. If we consider the symmetry of the normal distribution curve, we can see that the values $48.6$ and $55.4$ must be equidistant from the mean:

    ![ai784bb](images/ai784bb.JPG)

    Hence, the mean of the distribution, $m$, will be equal to the mean of $48.6$ and $55.4$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{(R1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m &= \frac{48.6+55.4}{2} \tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-1.1em}\boxed{m = 52} \tag*{\textbf{AG}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3. 1. Let the continuous random variable $X$ represent the length of a randomly selected lecture.

        We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}\left(52,4^2\right)$. Hence we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(45<X<51) &= \text{normalCdf}(45,51,52,4) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
        &=0.3612... \\[8pt]
        &\hspace{0em}\boxed{= 0.361\,\text{(3 s.f.)}} \hspace{3em} [\text{using G.D.C.}] \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2. Again using the G.D.C., we have

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(X>55) &= \text{normalCdf}(55,\infty,52,4) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
        &= 0.2266... \\[8pt]
        &\hspace{0em}\boxed{= 0.227\,\text{(3 s.f.)}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

4. Using the G.D.C., we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X<x) &= 0.7 \\[8pt]
    x &= \text{InvNorm}(0.7,52,4)\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
    &= 54.09... \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0em}\boxed{= 54.1\,\text{(3 s.f.)}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5. Taking the results in part (c) (i) and (ii), we can use the formula for independent events. We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(45<\mathrm{M}<51\,\cap\,\mathrm{T}>55) &= \mathrm{P}(45<\mathrm{M}<51) \times \mathrm{P}(\mathrm{T}>55) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
    &= 0.3612...\,\times 0.2266...\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
    &= 0.08186...\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0em}\boxed{= 0.0819\,\text{(3 s.f.)}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

6. Let $L$ represent the event that a randomly selected lecture during a given week lasts more than 55 minutes. We can say that $L$ follows a binomial distribution, because

    - We have a fixed number of trials ($n=5$ lectures per week)
    - Each trial is independent (given information)
    - Each trial has two outcomes (a lecture must last either more than 55 minutes, or less than or equal to 55 minutes)
    - We have a constant probability (from (c) (ii), $p=0.2266...$)

    The criteria for a binomial distribution are met, therefore $L \sim \mathrm{B}(5,0.2266...)$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{(R1A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We require $\mathrm{P}(L \geq 3)$. We have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(L \geq 3) &= \mathrm{binomCdf}(5,0.2266...,3,5) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
    &= 0.08041... \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0em}\boxed{= 0.0804\,\text{(3 s.f.)}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
