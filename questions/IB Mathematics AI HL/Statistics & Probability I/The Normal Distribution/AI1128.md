# AI1128

**Maximum mark: 6**

Yejin works in an office with a room temperature that is approximated by a normally distributed with a mean of $19.5^\circ\,C$  and a standard deviation of $0.75^\circ\, C$.

1. Calculate the probability that on any particular day the temperature will be above  $20^\circ\,C$.[1 mark]

It is known that $30\%$ of the days are less than $t^\circ\,C$.

2. Find the value of $t$.[2 marks]

Yejin finds the temperature in the office favourable for $80\%$ of the days.

3. If the upper limit of $T$ for favourable days is $21^\circ\,C$ then what is the lower limit? [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We can define the distribution such that the CRV $T$ represents the temperature of the office on a particular day. $T \sim \mathrm{N}(19.5,0.75^2)$.

1.  Hence, the probability the temperature is above $20^\circ\,C$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(T>20)&=\text{normCdf}(20, \infty, 19.5, 0.75) \\
        &=0.25249...\\[6pt]
        &=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{0.252} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We need to use the inverse normal function as we need to find the $T$ value that gives us the shaded area of $0.3$

    ![ans1](images/ans1.png)

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(T<t)&=0.3 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\
        t&=\text{inverseNorm}(0.3, 19.5, 0.75)\\
        &=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{19.1^\circ\,C} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The blue area represents the favourable days ($80\%$).

    ![ans2](images/ans2.png)

    We can find the sum of the red area and the blue area as follows
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        A_{\text{red and blue}} &= \mathrm{P}(T<21)\\
        &= \text{normCdf}(-\infty,21, 19.5, 0.75)\\
        &=0.97724... \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    That means the red area is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        A_\text{red}&=0.97724...-0.8\\
        &=0.17724...\tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We can now find the lower limit of $T$ using the inverse normal function
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(T<t_{\text{lower limit}})&=0.17724...\\
        T_\text{lower limit}&=\text{inverseNorm}(0.17724..., 19.5, 0..75)\\
        &=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{18.8^\circ\,C} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
