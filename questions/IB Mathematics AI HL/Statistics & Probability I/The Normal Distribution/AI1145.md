# AI1145

**Maximum mark: 6**

A fast food restaurant sells two sizes of drink, large and small. The large drinks are normally distributed with a mean volume of $500$ millilitres and a standard deviation of $15$ millilitres. The small drinks are also normally distributed, with a mean volume of $335$ millilitres and a standard deviation of $10$ millilitres. It can be assumed that the volume of each drink is independent.

Lisa buys two large drinks and her friend Sam buys three small drinks. Calculate the probability that the total volume of Lisa's drinks exceeds the total volume of Sam's drinks.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let the random variable $L$ represent the volume of a randomly selected large drink. We have that $L \sim \mathrm{N}(500,15^2)$.

Let the random variable $S$ represent the volume of a randomly selected small drink. We have that $S\sim \mathrm{N}(335,10^2)$.

Let the random variable $T$ represent the difference in volume between two randomly selected large drinks and three randomly selected small drinks. Therefore

$$

\begin{aligned}

T &= (L_1 + L_2) - (S_1 + S_2 + S_3) \\[8pt]

T &= L_1 + L_2 - S_1 - S_2 - S_3 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}

\end{aligned}

$$

We require $\mathrm{P}(T>0)$. Note that $T$ is a linear combination of 5 independent random variables.

Because these 5 independent random variables are normally distributed, $T$ will also be normally distributed.

$$

\begin{aligned}

\tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

\end{aligned}

$$

Using the formula for the expectation of a linear combination of random variables, we have

$$

\begin{aligned}

\mathrm{E}(T) &= \mathrm{E}(L_1 + L_2 - S_1 - S_2 - S_3) \\[6pt]

&=  \mathrm{E}(L_1) +  \mathrm{E}(L_2) -  \mathrm{E}(S_1) -  \mathrm{E}(S_2) -  \mathrm{E}(S_3) \\[6pt]

&= 500 + 500 - 335 - 335 - 335 \\[6pt]

\mathrm{E}(T) &= -5 \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

\end{aligned}

$$

Using the formula for the variance of a linear combination of random variables, we have

$$

\begin{aligned}

\mathrm{Var}(T) &= \mathrm{Var}(L_1 + L_2 - S_1 - S_2 - S_3) \\[6pt]

&=  \mathrm{Var}(L_1) +  \mathrm{Var}(L_2) +  \mathrm{Var}(S_1) +  \mathrm{Var}(S_2) +  \mathrm{Var}(S_3) \\[6pt]

&= 15^2 + 15^2 + 10^2 + 10^2 + 10^2 \\[6pt]

\mathrm{Var}(T) &= 750 \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

\end{aligned}

$$

Hence $T \sim \mathrm{N}\left(-5,\sqrt{750}^{\hspace{0.1em}2}\right)$ and therefore

$$

\begin{aligned}

\mathrm{P}(T>0) &= \text{normCdf}\left(0,\infty,-5,\sqrt{750}\right) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]

&\approx 0.428

\end{aligned}

$$

The probability that the total volume of Lisa's drinks exceeds the total volume of Sam's drinks is approximately 0.428.

$$

\begin{aligned}

\tag*{\textbf{A1}}

\end{aligned}

$$
