# AI364

**Maximum mark: 12**

Fuel consumption of the $2019$ model Toyota Corolla (small car) in
litres per $100$ km is normally distributed with a mean of $7.6$ and
standard deviation of $0.8$.

1.  Sketch the normal distribution diagram that shows this information.
    [2 marks]

2.  One of the cars is chosen at random. Calculate the probability that
    the fuel  [2 marks] consumption of this car is between $7.2$ and
    $8$.

3.  $200$ of these car types are involved in a test. Estimate the number
    of cars  [2 marks] that will have a fuel consumption between $7.2$
    and $8$.

4.  One of these cars, that currently has $14$ litres of fuel in the
    tank, needs to [3 marks] travel $200$ km to reach the desired
    destination. Estimate the probability that this car will not reach
    the destination point without refueling.

5.  $200$ of these cars were involved in a test and each car's tank has
    the same [3 marks] amount of fuel. Only $30$ of the cars were able
    to pass the test distance of $100$ km without refueling. Estimate
    the initial fuel amount in the tank of each car.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $F$ be the fuel consumption in litres per $100$ km. The normal
    distribution of $F$ is illustrated as follows.

    ![937e93f0a391c1b13c6bcaa6a5be40e650c70b40.svg](images/937e93f0a391c1b13c6bcaa6a5be40e650c70b40.svg)

2.  The probability of the fuel consumption of this car being between
    $7.2$ and $8$ is represented by the region shaded below.

    ![7e6122f823665f53f200626dc9072f606aa26218.svg](images/7e6122f823665f53f200626dc9072f606aa26218.svg)

    Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(7.2 < F < 8) &= \text{NormCdf}\hspace{0.1em}(7.2,\, 8,\, 7.6,\, 0.8) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.383
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Let $Y$ be the number of cars out of these $200$ cars that have a
    fuel consumption between $7.2$ and $8$ litres. Then we have
    $Y \sim \mathrm{B(200,\hspace{0.2em}0.383)}$ and get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{E}(Y) &= np \\[3pt]
    &= 200 \times 0.383 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}= 76.6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Note that $200 \text{ km} = 2 \times 100 \text{ km}$.
    To reach the destination point without refueling, the car must
    consume less than $14/2 = 7$ litres per $100$ km. So the probability
    of not reaching the destination is represented by the region shaded
    below.

    ![c9379484655ab74c84fe34be26d4e4d273f905e8.svg](images/c9379484655ab74c84fe34be26d4e4d273f905e8.svg)

    Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(F > 7) &= \text{NormCdf}\hspace{0.1em}(7,\, 100,\, 7.6,\, 0.8) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.773
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Let $x$ be the initial fuel amount in the tank of each of the cars
    that pass the test distance of $100$ km without refueling (ie.
    consumes no more than $x$ litres per $100$ km). The percentage of
    such cars is $30/200 \times 100\% = 15\%$. Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(F < x) &= 0.15 \\[3pt]
    x &= \text{InvNorm}(0.15,\, 7.6,\, 0.8) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 6.77 \text{ litres}  \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
