# AI359

**Maximum mark: 6**

The daily number of visitors to Hagia Sophia Museum in June is normally
distributed with mean of $5000$ and a standard deviation of $750$
people.

1.  Calculate the probability that the number of visitors on a randomly
    chosen [2 marks] day in June is more than
    $4000$ people.

2.  Calculate the expected number of days in June during where the
    number of [2 marks] visitors will be less than
    $4000$ people.

On a randomly chosen day in June, the probability that the number of
visitors is less than $N$ people is $0.25$.

3.  Find the value of $N$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The region shaded below represents the probability that the number
    of visitors is more than $4000$ people.

    ![2fe06134d437585f4a4e4d729b8791a749fc212d.svg](images/2fe06134d437585f4a4e4d729b8791a749fc212d.svg)

    Let $X$ be the number of visitors on a randomly chosen day. Given
    that $X \sim {N}(5000, 750^2)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{Pr}(X > 4000)  &=  \text{NormCdf}(4000,\,100\hspace{0.1em}000,\,5000,\,750) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.909 \hspace{3em}[\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X < 4000) & =  1 -  \mathrm{Pr}(X > 4000)\\[3pt]
    &= 1- 0.909 \\[6pt]
    &= 0.091
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Let $Y$ be the number of days in June during which the number of
    visitors are less than $4000$. Since there are $30$ days in June, we
    have $Y \sim \mathrm{B}(30,0.0912)$. That gives
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{E}[Y] & =  np \\[3pt]
    & =  30 \times 0.091 \\[3pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}= 2.73 \text{ (days)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The probability that the number of visitors is less than $N$ people
    is represented by the region shaded below.

    ![7adbf6fc8e2b5b6cdb6d96b6219b183033b374a0.svg](images/7adbf6fc8e2b5b6cdb6d96b6219b183033b374a0.svg)

    We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    N &= \text{InvNorm}(0.25,\,5000,\,750) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 4490  \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
