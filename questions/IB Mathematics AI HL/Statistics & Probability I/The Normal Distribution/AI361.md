# AI361

**Maximum mark: 10**

John decided to build a stone fence around his house and lay a stone
walkway. John ordered a large bag of stones and first chose a random a
sample of $50$ stones for measuring. He measured the diameters of the
stones correct to the nearest centimetre. The following table shows the
frequency distribution of these diameters.

![f5ad380a25b825292a91df056b8f78926724fddd.svg](images/f5ad380a25b825292a91df056b8f78926724fddd.svg)

1.  Find the value of
    1.  the mean diameter of these stones;

    2.  the standard deviation of the diameters of these stones.
        [2 marks]

John assumes that the diameters of all the stones from this bag are
normally distributed with a mean $16$ cm and a standard deviation of
$1.1$ cm. John sorts all stones by their diameter size.

2.  John selects the largest stones, with diameters of $18$ cm
    or greater, to use [2 marks] for the stone walkway. Estimate the
    percentage of stones used.

3.  John selects small stones, which have a diameter less than
    $x$ cm to use for [2 marks] a walkway border. The small stones
    account for $5\%$ of the total number of stones. Calculate the value
    of $x$.

4.  John selects medium stones to use the stone fence. Estimate
    the percentage [2 marks] of medium stones with diameter more than
    $x$ cm from part (c) and less than $18$ cm.

5.  John estimates that there are about $10\hspace{0.2em}000$
    stones in total. Estimate the [2 marks] number of large stones.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X$ be the diameter of a stone. We get
    1.
        $\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mux\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu \approx 15.9 \text{ cm}$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    2.
        $s_x \approx 1.1 \text{ cm}$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

2.  The distribution of the diameters of these stones can be illustrated
    as follows. Note that the shaded region represents the percentage of
    stones with diameters of at least $18$ cm.

    ![8cb53dc2e29be326431b0e55dd3affecfd5b664b.svg](images/8cb53dc2e29be326431b0e55dd3affecfd5b664b.svg)

    Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X \geq 18) &= \text{NormCdf}(18,\,100,\,16,\,1.1) \\[3pt]
    &\approx 0.0345 \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 3.45\%
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The region shaded below represents the percentage of stones with
    diameters of less than $x$ cm.

    ![146a18b3dff68cc6718157ba9f1ed00005e32f1b.svg](images/146a18b3dff68cc6718157ba9f1ed00005e32f1b.svg)

    Using G.D.C, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x &= \text{InvNorm}(0.05,\, 16, \, 1.1) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 14.2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  $\begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr(medium)} &= \text{Pr}(x < X < 18) \\[3pt]
                        &= 100\% - \text{Pr}(X \leq x) - \text{Pr} (X \geq 18)  \\[3pt]
                        &= 100\% - 5\% - 3.45\% \\[6pt]
                        &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 91.6\%
    \end{aligned}$

5.  $\begin{aligned}
        n(\text{large stones}) &= 10\hspace{0.2em}000 \times \text{Pr}(X \geq 18) \\[3pt]
            &= 10\hspace{0.2em}000 \times 0.0345 \\[6pt]
            &\hspace{0.525em}= 345
    \end{aligned}$
