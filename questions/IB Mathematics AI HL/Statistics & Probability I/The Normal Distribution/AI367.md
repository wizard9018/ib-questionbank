# AI367

**Maximum mark: 6**

Avocado Oil Factory opens at
$06\text{\hspace{0.05em}:\hspace{0.05em}}00$ every morning.

The daily arrival times of the employees at the Avocado Oil Factory
follow a normal distribution. The mean arrival time is $42$ minutes
after the factory opens and the standard deviation is $6$
minutes.

1.  Find the probability that a randomly chosen employee
    1.  arrives at least $50$ minutes after the factory opens;

    2.  arrives between $30$ minutes and $45$ minutes after the factory
        opens. [4 marks]

2.  Given that, on one morning, $33$ employees arrive at least $50$
    minutes after [2 marks] the factory opens,
    estimate the total number of employees at the Avocado Oil Factory.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  The region shaded in the following diagram represents the
        probability that an employee arrives at least $50$ minutes after
        the factory opens.

        ![a56e0282ca6d327e23ffc69c158592e0652fec3c.svg](images/a56e0282ca6d327e23ffc69c158592e0652fec3c.svg)

        Let $T$ be the daily arrival time, in minutes after the factory
        opens, of an employee. We are given $T \sim {N}(42, 6^2)$.
        Hence,
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr}(T \geq 50) &= \text{NormCdf}(50,\,200,\,42,\,6) \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.0912  \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The region shaded on the diagram below represents the
        probability that an employee arrives between $30$ and $45$
        minutes after the factory opens.

        ![20e51e4486451e23b38490e7a1a95f85beba8c8a.svg](images/20e51e4486451e23b38490e7a1a95f85beba8c8a.svg)

        Using G.D.C, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr}(30 < T < 45) &= \text{NormCdf}(30,\,45,\,42,\,6) \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.669
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Let $N$ be the number of employees at the Olive Oil Factory. From
    part (a), we get $\text{Pr}(T \geq 50) = 0.0912$. Hence,

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{33}{N} &= 0.0912 \\[6pt]
    \hspace{3em}&N \approx 362
    \end{aligned}
    $$
