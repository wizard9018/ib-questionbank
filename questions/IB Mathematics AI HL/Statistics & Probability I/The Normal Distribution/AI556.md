# AI556

**Maximum mark: 6**

At the 'Magic Hand Carwash', the time taken for a car to be cleaned can
be modelled by a normal distribution with a mean of $50$ minutes and a
standard deviation of $4.5$ minutes. Two cars come and use the service,
one after each other.

Determine the probability that the total time taken to have both cars
cleaned is $1.5$ hours or less. State any assumptions that you have
made.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let $T_1$ and $T_2$ be the time taken for the first and second cars to
be cleaned respectively. Let $T$ to be the total time taken to have both
cars cleaned.

By
 $\text{assuming the independence of } T_1 \text{ and } T_2$,
 we get
 $$
 T = T_1 + T_2

 $$
 and,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\rm{E}(T) &= \rm{E}(T_1) + \rm{E}(T_2) \\[3pt]
&= 50 + 50 \\[3pt]
&= 100
\end{aligned}
$$
and,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Var}(T) &=  \rm{Var}(T_1) + \rm{Var}(T_2) \\[3pt]
&= 4.5^2 + 4.5^2 \\[3pt]
&= 40.5
\end{aligned}
$$

The standard deviation of $T$ is $\sigma_T = \sqrt{40.5} \approx 6.364$.
Hence,
$$
T \sim \rm{N}(100, 6.364^2)
$$

$1.5$ hours $= 90$ minutes. Hence the probability that the total time is
less than $1.5$ hours is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\rm{Pr}(T < 90) &= \text{normCdf}(0,90,100,6.364)  \\[6pt]
&\approx 0.0581 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
\end{aligned}
$$
