# AI650

**Maximum mark: 6**

A piece of jewellery is being designed for an auction. Its shape is based on the graph of

$y=\dfrac{x}{50} \sqrt{625-x^2} \quad\text{for } 0 \leq x \leq 25$

which is revolved $360^\circ$ about the $x$-axis, where $x$ and $y$ are measured in mm. 

![AI650](images/AI650.PNG)

1. Find the area bounded by the graph and the $x$-axis. [2 marks]

2. Hence or otherwise, find the volume of the jewel. [2 marks]

3. If $1$cm$^3$ of silver weighs $10.5$ grams, and $1$ gram of silver costs \$1.50, find the cost of the silver used for the jewel to the nearest dollar. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the formula for the area below a curve, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Area} &= \int_0^{25} \left(\dfrac{x}{50}\sqrt{625-x^2}\right)\, \mathrm{d}x \rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 104\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the formula for the volume of a solid generated by rotation
    about the $x$-axis, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \pi \int_0^{25} \left(\dfrac{x}{50}\sqrt{625-x^2}\right)^2\, \mathrm{d}x \rule[-5mm]{0pt}{5mm}\\
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 1\hspace{0.05em}640\hspace{0.15em}\text{mm}^3\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Applying the appropriate conversions we obtain the cost of gold used
    for the jewel.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Cost} &=
    (1\hspace{0.15em}640\hspace{0.15em}\text{mm}^3)\left(\dfrac{1\hspace{0.15em}\text{cm}}{10\hspace{0.15em}\text{mm}}\right)^3 \left(\dfrac{10.5\hspace{0.15em}\text{g}}{1\hspace{0.15em}\text{cm}^3}\right)\left(\dfrac{\$\hspace{0.05em}1.50}{1\hspace{0.15em}\text{g}}\right)\rule[-7mm]{0pt}{5mm}\\
    &=(1\hspace{0.15em}640\hspace{0.15em}\cancel{\text{mm}^3})\left(\dfrac{1\hspace{0.15em}\cancel{\text{cm}^3}}{1\hspace{0.15em}000\hspace{0.15em}\cancel{\text{mm}^3}}\right) \left(\dfrac{10.5\hspace{0.15em}\cancel{\text{g}}}{1\hspace{0.15em}\cancel{\text{cm}^3}}\right)\left(\dfrac{\$\hspace{0.05em}1.50}{1\hspace{0.15em}\cancel{\text{g}}}\right)\rule[-7mm]{0pt}{5mm}\\
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx \$26\rule[-5mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
