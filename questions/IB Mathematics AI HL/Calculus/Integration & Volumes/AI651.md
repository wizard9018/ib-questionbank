# AI651

**Maximum mark: 4**

The following diagram shows the graph of the function
$f(x)=\dfrac{b}{a}\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$

![7ae49d531f1131c093048903bf6ddc6622d61ac9.svg](images/7ae49d531f1131c093048903bf6ddc6622d61ac9.svg)

Find an expression, in terms of $a$ and $b$, for the volume of the solid
formed by rotating the curve $360^\circ$ about the $x$-axis.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the formula for the volume of a solid generated by rotation around
the $x$-axis, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= \pi \int_0^a y^2\, \mathrm{d}x \rule[-7.5mm]{0pt}{7.5mm}\\
&= \pi \int_0^a \left(\dfrac{b}{a}\sqrt{a^2-x^2}\right)^2\, \mathrm{d}x \rule[-7.5mm]{0pt}{7.5mm}\\
%&= \pi \int_0^a \dfrac{b}{a}\sqrt{a^2-x^2}\, \mathrm{d}x \\
&= \dfrac{\pi b^2}{a^2} \int_0^a \left(a^2-x^2\right) \, \mathrm{d}x \rule[-7.5mm]{0pt}{7.5mm}\\
&= \dfrac{\pi b^2}{a^2} \left[a^2x-\dfrac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^a \rule[-7.5mm]{0pt}{7.5mm}\\
&= \dfrac{\pi b^2}{a^2} \left(a^3-\dfrac{a^3}{3}\right) \rule[-7.5mm]{0pt}{7.5mm}\\
&= \dfrac{\pi b^2}{a^2} \left(\dfrac{2a^3}{3}\right) \rule[-7.5mm]{0pt}{7.5mm}\\
&\hspace{0.3em} = \dfrac{2\pi ab^2}{3}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{7.5mm}
\end{aligned}
$$
