# AI635

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $f(x) = x(x - 3)^2$, for $0 \leq x \leq 4$. The following graph
shows $f$.

![73357d47f8356c984a1f6f8487656309e75d281e.svg](images/73357d47f8356c984a1f6f8487656309e75d281e.svg)

Let $R$ be the region enclosed by $f$, the $x$-axis and the lines $x=0$,
$x=3$.

1.  Show that
    $\int f(x)\, \textrm dx = \dfrac{x^4}{4}-2x^3+\dfrac{9x^2}{2}+C$.[2 marks]

2.  Find the volume formed when $R$ is rotated $\ang{360}$ about the
    $x$-axis.[2 marks]

3.  The diagram below shows part of the graph of the quadric function\
    $g(x) = x(a - x)$. The graph of $g$ crosses the $x$-axis when
    $x = a$.

    ![24a3dcfcd5f84e0e10dbbdadd1ce6ea632e48932.svg](images/24a3dcfcd5f84e0e10dbbdadd1ce6ea632e48932.svg)

    The area enclosed by the graph of $g$ is equal to the area of $R$.
    Find the\
    value of $a$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{-2cm}
    \int f(x)\, \mathrm{d}x &= \int x(x-3)^2\, \mathrm{d}x \\
    &= \int x(x^2-6x+9) \, \mathrm{d}x \\
    &= \int (x^3-6x^2+9x) \, \mathrm{d}x\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em}= \dfrac{x^4}{4}-2x^3+\dfrac{9x^2}{2}+C
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The volume is given by the integral
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V   & = \pi \int_0^3 (x(x - 3)^2)^2\, \textrm d x\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em}= 65.4\qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Firstly, we find the area of $R$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_R     & = \int_0^3 (x(x - 3)^2)\, \textrm d x = 6.75
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We can now solve for $a$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_0^a x(a - x)\,\textrm d x  & = 6.75\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-1.5mm} a  = 3.43
    \qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \hspace{2cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
