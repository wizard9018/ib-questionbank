# AI642

**Maximum mark: 7**

The diagram below shows part of the graphs of $y=(x+2)^2$ and
$y=(6-x)^2$.

![bc878d7844233f0151603278826c7c50df5af2fd.svg](images/bc878d7844233f0151603278826c7c50df5af2fd.svg)

1.  Find the coordinates of the points P, Q and R.[3 marks]

The shaded region is rotated through $360^\circ$ about the
$x$-axis.

2.  Calculate the volume of the solid obtained.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using our G.D.C., we can get the following sketch

    ![8cf116b664a5bcf3de05afb06236c3d6017dd37d.svg](images/8cf116b664a5bcf3de05afb06236c3d6017dd37d.svg)

    Therefore, the labeled points are:
    $\mathrm{P}(0,4)$ $\mathrm{Q}(2,16)$ $\mathrm{R}(4,4)$

2.  Splitting the shaded region into two sub-regions as seen in the
    diagram in part (a) and using the formula for the volume of a solid
    generated by rotation about the $x$-axis, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V&= \text{Volume generated by region A}+\text{Volume generated by region B} \rule[-2mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
    &= \pi \displaystyle \int_0^2 \big[((x+2)^2)^2-4^2\big] \, \mathrm{d}x + \pi \int_2^4 \big[((6-x)^2)^2-4^2\big]\, \mathrm{d}x \rule[-5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &\hspace{0.3em}= \dfrac{1664\pi}{5}\approx 1\hspace{0.05em}050\rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\hspace{3em}[\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
