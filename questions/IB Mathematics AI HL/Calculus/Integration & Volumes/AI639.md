# AI639

**Maximum mark: 6**

The diagram below shows the graphs of functions $y=4-2x^2$ and $y=2x^3$.

![ce052e5c9c29c473be9def7891dc8f9bbc457822.svg](images/ce052e5c9c29c473be9def7891dc8f9bbc457822.svg)

1.  Find the volume resulting from a rotation of the shaded region shown
    in the diagram through $2\pi$ about the $x$-axis.[2 marks]

2.  Find the volume resulting from a rotation of the region shown in the
    diagram through $2\pi$ about the $y$-axis.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  First, let's determine the intersection point of the two graphs.
    Using G.D.C., we get

    ![4d4d554f9c447a30725ab62a0f5991e46afd24d7.svg](images/4d4d554f9c447a30725ab62a0f5991e46afd24d7.svg)

    Now, using the formula for the volume of a solid obtained by
    rotating a region between two curves about the $x$ axis, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V&=\pi \int_0^{1} (4-2x^2)^2\, \mathrm{d}x - \pi \int_0^{1} (2x^3)^2\, \mathrm{d}x\rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    %&=\pi \int_0^{1} \left[16-16x^2+4x^4-4x^6\right]\,\mathrm{d}x\rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    %&= \pi \big[16x-\dfrac{16}{3}x^3+\dfrac{4}{5}x^5+2x+\sin(2x)\big]_0^{1.06}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    &\approx 34.2\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\hspace{3em}[\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.

    ![cba797befe92cd403520206e36da4585178b7025.svg](images/cba797befe92cd403520206e36da4585178b7025.svg)

    Splitting the shaded region into two sub-regions as seen in the
    diagram above, where the region $A$ is bounded by the graph of
    $2x^3$ and the $y$-axis while region $B$ is bounded by the graph of
    $4-2x^2$ and the $y$-axis, we obtain the volume using the formula
    for the volume of a solid generated by rotation about the $y$ axis.

    First, we express $x$ as a function of $y$ as follows:

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 2x^3 \rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
    x &= \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{y}{2}}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{6.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    and
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 4-2x^2\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{5mm}\\
    %x^2 &= \dfrac{4-y}{2} \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    x &= \sqrt{2-\dfrac{y}{2}}\rule[0mm]{0pt}{6.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Now, using the formula for the volume of a solid obtained by
    rotation, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V&= \text{Volume generated by region A}+\text{Volume generated by region B}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &= \pi\int_0^{2} \left(\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{y}{2}}\right)^2 \, \mathrm{d}y+\pi \int_{2}^{4} \left(\sqrt{2-\dfrac{y}{2}}\right)^2 \,\mathrm{d}y \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &\approx3.77 + 3.14\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{12mm} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 6.91\rule[0mm]{0pt}{8mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
