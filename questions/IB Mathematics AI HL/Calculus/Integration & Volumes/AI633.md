# AI633

**Maximum mark: 13**

**All lengths in this question are in metres.**

Consider the function $f(x) = \sqrt{\dfrac{16-4x^2}{7}}$,
for $-2 \leq x \leq 2$. In the following diagram, the shaded
 region is enclosed by the graph of $f$ and
the $x$-axis.

![b44e497d31bf5071c65617d06bb35c85c800702b.svg](images/b44e497d31bf5071c65617d06bb35c85c800702b.svg)

A rainwater collection tank can be modelled by revolving this region by
$360$ $\text{\textdegree{}}$ about the
$x$-axis.

1.  Find the volume of the tank. [3 marks]

Rainwater in the tank is used for drinking, cooking, bathing and other
needs during the week.

The volume of rainwater in the tank is given by the function $g(t)$, for
$0 \leq t \leq 7$, where $t$ is measured in days and $g(t)$ is measured
in m$^3$. The rate of change of the volume of rainwater in the tank is
given by $g'(t) = 1.5-4\cos(0.12\hspace{0.025em}t^2)$.

2.  The volume of rainwater in the tank is increasing only when
    $a < t < b$.

    1.  Find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$.
    2.  During the interval $a < t < b$, the volume of rainwater in the
        tank increases by $\text{\(d\)\hspace{0.25em}m\(^3\).}$ Find
        the value of $d$. [5 marks]

When $t=0$, the volume of rainwater in the tank is
$8.2$ m$^3$. It is known that the tank is never
completely full of rainwater during the $7$ day period.

3.  Find the minimum volume of empty space in the tank during
    the $7$ day period. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The volume is given by the following integral
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V   & = \pi\int_{-2}^2 \dfrac {16 - 4 x^2} 7 \,\textrm d x\\[10pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}  = 19.1\,\mbox{m}^3 \qquad\quad [\mbox{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  The volume of rainwater is increasing when $g'(t) > 0$,
        $0\leq t \leq 7$. First, let's solve $g'(t) = 0$, we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        1.5 - 4 \cos (0.12 t^2)     & = 0\\[3pt]
        t                   & = 3.14,\,6.52\,\mbox{days}\qquad\quad [\mbox{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Using the ID Test, we have

        ![84db364e5158f46802db700af9ea7e270c462097.svg](images/84db364e5158f46802db700af9ea7e270c462097.svg)

        Hence
          $\text{a = 3.14} \mbox { days and } \text{b = 6.52} \mbox{ days}$
    2.  The value of $d$ is given by the integral
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        d   & = \int_{3.14}^{6.52} (1.5 - 4 \cos(.12 t^2))\textrm d t\\[8pt]
        &\hspace{.275em}  = 11.1\,\mbox{m}^3\qquad\quad
        \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  The initial volume is $V(0) = 8.2$ m$^3$. Then the volume decreases
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V(3.14) & = 8.2 + \int_{0}^{3.14} (1.5 - 4 \cos(0.12 t^2))\textrm d t\\[6pt]
    & = 8.2 - 6.2\\[8pt]
    & = 2\,\mbox{m}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    After that the volume
    increases by $11.1$ (part (ii))
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V(6.52) & = 2 + 11.1\hspace{3.7cm}\\[6pt]
    & = 13.1\,\mbox{m}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Then the volume
    decreases
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3mm}
    V(7)        & = 13.1 + \int_{6.52}^{7} (1.5 - 4 \cos(0.12 t^2))\textrm d t\\[6pt]
    & = 13.1 - 0.588\\[8pt]
    & = 12.512\,\mbox{m}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the minimum
    volume of empty space is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{\min}    & = 19.1 - 13.1\hspace{2.9cm}\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}   = 6\,\mbox{m}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
