# AI640

**Maximum mark: 6**

The diagram below shows the graphs of functions $y=5-3x^3$ and $y=2x^4$.

![7e539a64423905dbc9970ce254cc0d530dec6962.svg](images/7e539a64423905dbc9970ce254cc0d530dec6962.svg)

1.  Find the volume resulting from a rotation of the region shown in the
    diagram through $2\pi$ about the $x$-axis.[2 marks]

2.  Find the volume resulting from a rotation of the region shown in the
    diagram through $2\pi$ about the $y$-axis.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  First, let's determine the intersection point of the two graphs.
    Using G.D.C., we get

    ![2377f9646f6481b8299803730766ae3e7c797665.svg](images/2377f9646f6481b8299803730766ae3e7c797665.svg)

    Now, using the formula for the volume of a solid obtained by
    rotation about the $x$ axis of a region between two curves, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V&=\pi \int_0^{1} (5-3x^3)^2\, \mathrm{d}x - \pi \int_0^{1} (2x^4)^2\, \mathrm{d}x \rule[-5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    %&=\pi \int_0^{0.94} \left[9\cos^2x-4x^4\right]\,\mathrm{d}x\rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    %&= \pi \left[\dfrac{9}{2}x+\dfrac{9}{4}\sin (2x)-\dfrac{4}{5}x^5\right]_0^{0.94}\rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &\approx 57.6\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\hspace{3em}[\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.

    ![a452b430be21f10fc054a48d8f328b971fefa010.svg](images/a452b430be21f10fc054a48d8f328b971fefa010.svg)

    Splitting the shaded region into two sub-regions as seen in the
    diagram above, where the region $A$ is bounded by the graph of
    $2x^4$ and the $y$-axis while region $B$ is bounded by the graph of
    $5-3x^3$ and the $y$-axis, we obtain the volume using the formula
    for the volume of a solid generated by rotation about the $y$ axis.

    First, we express $x$ as a function of $y$ as follows:

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 2x^4 \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    x &= \sqrt[4]{\dfrac{y}{2}}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    and
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y&= 5-3x^3 \rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{7mm}\\
    x &= \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{5-y}{3}}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Now, using the formula for the volume of a solid obtained by
    rotation, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V&= \text{Volume generated by region A}+\text{Volume generated by region B}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &= \pi \int_0^{2} \left(\sqrt[4]{\dfrac{y}{2}}\right)^2 \,\mathrm{d}y + \pi\int_{2}^5 \left(\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{5-y}{3}}\right)^2 \, \mathrm{d}y \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &\approx 4.19 + 5.65\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{12mm} \hspace{3em}[\text{by using G.D.C.}]\\
    &\approx 9.84\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
