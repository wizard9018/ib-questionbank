# AI636

**Maximum mark: 5**

The shape of a bowl is given by revolving the curve
$y=\dfrac{1}{3}x^{\frac{7}{4}}$, $0\leq x \leq 6$ through $360^\circ$
about the $y$-axis. The units are in centimetres.

![59fc18b70b168ee97500668d8ebc3b842b8280b6.svg](images/59fc18b70b168ee97500668d8ebc3b842b8280b6.svg)

1.  Find the height of the bowl.[2 marks]

The bowl is completely filled with water.

2.  Find the volume of water in the bowl.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The height of the bowl is the maximum $y$ value on the curve for the
    given domain. As the function is clearly increasing, the maximum $y$
    value is reached at its right endpoint. Therefore, the height, $h$,
    can be found
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h&= \dfrac{1}{3}(6)^{\frac{7}{4}}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{5mm}\\
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 7.67 \hspace{0.2em} \text{cm}\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{7mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  First, we find $x$ as a function of $y$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= \dfrac{1}{3} x^{\frac{7}{4}}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    3y &= x^\frac{7}{4} \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    (3y)^\frac{4}{7} &= x\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Now we can find the volume by integrating the curve around the
    $y$-axis.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \pi \int_0^{7.67} x^2 \, \mathrm{d}y\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &= \pi \int_0^{7.67} (3y)^\frac{8}{7} \, \mathrm{d}y\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 405 \hspace{0.2em} \text{cm$^3$}\text{}\rule[-2mm]{0pt}{7mm}\hspace{4em}[\text{by using G.D.C}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
