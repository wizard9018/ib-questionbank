# AI969

**Maximum mark: 13**

During a trip to a forest to forage for mushrooms, Viviane finds a giant
mushroom. She decides to model the shape of the mushroom to find its
volume.

![98032f9279caa2e292b2032c4c132798b858d8f7.svg](images/98032f9279caa2e292b2032c4c132798b858d8f7.svg)

After taking a photo of the mushroom and zooming in to get its real
size, she rotates the photograph and estimates that the cross-section
passes through the points $(0,3)$, $(15,3)$, $(15,15)$, $(22, 10)$,
$(23.5, 6)$ and $(24, 0)$, where all measurements are in centimetres.
The cross-section is symmetrical about the $x$-axis, as shown below.

![2679426554600d5654bf660c65793c7964833f3d.svg](images/2679426554600d5654bf660c65793c7964833f3d.svg)

Viviane models the section from $(0,3)$ and $(15,3)$ with a straight
line.

1.  Write down the equation of the line that passes through these
    points. [1 mark]

Next, Viviane models the section that passes through the points
$(15,15)$, $(22, 10)$, $(23.5, 6)$ and $(24, 0)$ with a quadratic
curve.

2.
    1.  Use your G.D.C. to find the equation of this quadratic curve.

    2.  By considering the gradient of the curve at the point $(15,15)$,
        explain why this may not be a good model. [3 marks]

Viviane thinks she can obtain a better model if a quadratic passing
through the point $(24,0)$ with a maximum point at $(15,15)$ is
used.

3.  Find the equation of this model, in the form
    $y = a(x-h)^2 + k$. [4 marks]

Using this new model, Viviane proceeds to estimate the volume of the
mushroom by finding the volume of revolution about the
$x$-axis.

4.
    1.  Write down an expression for her estimate as a sum of two
        integrals.

    2.  Find the volume of the mushroom estimated by Viviane.
        [5 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $y = 3$

2.
    1.
        $y \approx -0.442x^2 + 15.7x - 121$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    2.  By using the G.D.C. we find that the gradient of the curve at
        the point $(15,15)$ is $2.44$.\
        According to the diagram, the gradient at this point should be
        $\leq 0$.

        Since the gradient at $(15,15)$ is not $\leq 0$, the quadratic curve is not a good model.

3.  Let the equation of the new model be $y = f(x)$ where
    $f(x) = a(x-h)^2 + k$.

    Since the quadratic has a maximum at $(15,15)$, we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h &= 15 \\[12pt]
    k &= 15\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore, $y = a(x-15)^2 + 15$.\
    Additionally, since the quadratic passes through the point $(24,0)$,
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0 &= a(24-15)^2 + 15 \hspace{4.25em} \\[11pt]
    %81a &= 15 \\[12pt]
    a &= -\dfrac{15}{81}\\[11pt]
    a &\approx -0.185 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore, the equation of
    the model is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{-0.52em}y \approx -0.185(x-15)^2 + 15
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.
    1.  The expression for the volume of revolution is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \\
        V \approx \int_{0}^{15} \pi(3)^2 \,\text{d}x + \int_{15}^{24} \pi\left[ -0.185(x-15)^2 + 15 \right]^2 \,\text{d}x
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using G.D.C. to evaluate $V$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        &\hspace{-0.75em}V \approx 3820 \hspace{0.2em}\text{cm}^3
        \end{aligned}
        $$
