# AI608

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $f(x) = 9 - 2\ln(x^2 + 4)$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$. The graph of $f$
passes through the point $(p,3)$, where
$p > 0$.

1.  Find the value of $p$. [2 marks]

The following diagram shows part of the graph of $f$.

![6016001b65155100f0dc6eb98ff244252ca74b02.svg](images/6016001b65155100f0dc6eb98ff244252ca74b02.svg)

The region enclosed by the graph of $f$, the $x$-axis and the lines
$x = -p$ and $x = p$ is rotated $360^\circ$ about the $x$-axis.

2.  Find the volume of the solid formed. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(p)                & = 3\\[6pt]
    9 - 2\ln(p^2 + 4)   & = 3\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{-2mm} p = 4.01\qquad\quad
    \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}\hspace{1cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The volume of revolution is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.1cm}
    V       & = \pi \int_{-4.01}^{4.01} (9 - 2 \ln(x^2 + 4))^2\,\textrm d x\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = 605\qquad\quad
    \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
