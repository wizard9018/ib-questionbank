# AI663

**Maximum mark: 12**

**Note: In this question, distance is in metres and time is in
seconds.**

An experiment in a particle accelerator moves a particle $P$ in a
straight line for six seconds. Its acceleration during this period is
given by $a(t) = -2t^2 + 13 t - 15$, for $0 \leq t \leq 6$.

1.  Write down the values of $t$ when the particle's acceleration is
    zero.[2 marks]

2.  Hence or otherwise, find all possible values of $t$ for which the
    velocity of $P$ is increasing. [2 marks]

The particle has an initial velocity of $7$ ms$^{-1}$.

3.  Find an expression for the velocity of $P$ at time
    $t$.[4 marks]

4.  Find the total distance travelled by $P$ when its velocity
    is decreasing. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -2t^2 + 13 t - 15   & = 0\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-1.5mm} t = 1.5,\,5\ \mbox{sec}
    \qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The velocity is increasing on   $1.5 < t < 5$

3.  The velocity is given by the integral
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    v(t)    & = \int a(t)\,\textrm d t\\[6pt]
    & = \int (-2t^2 + 13 t - 15)\,\textrm d t\\[6pt]
    & = -\dfrac {2 t^3} 3 + \dfrac {13t^2} 2 - 15 t + C\hspace{.5cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Since $v(0) = 7$ we have $C = 7$.\
    Hence, we get
      $v(t) = -\dfrac {2 t^3} 3 + \dfrac {13t^2} 2 - 15 t + 7$

4.  From (b) we know that the velocity is decreasing on $0 < t < 1.5$
    and $5 < t < 6$. Hence the distance is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.3cm}
    d       & = \int_0^{1.5} |v(t)|\,\textrm d t +  \int_5^{6} |v(t)|\,\textrm d t\\[10pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = 13.6\,\mbox{m} \qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
