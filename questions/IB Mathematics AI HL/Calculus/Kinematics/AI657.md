# AI657

**Maximum mark: 6**

A particle is moving along a straight line. Its velocity can be modelled
by the function $v(t) = 2 t - 0.3 t^3 + 2$, for $t \geq 0$, where $v$ is
in ms$^{-1}$ and $t$ in seconds.

1.  Find the acceleration of the particle after $2.2$ seconds.
    [2 marks]

2.
    1.  Find the time when the particle's acceleration is zero.

    2.  Find the particle's velocity when its acceleration is zero.
        [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a(t)        & = v'(t)\\[6pt]
    & = 2 - 0.9 t^2\\[6pt]
    a(2.2)  & = 2 - 0.9(2.2^2)\hspace{7mm}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em}  = -2.36\,\mbox{ms}^{-2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  $a(t) = 0 \,\Longrightarrow \,v'(t) = 0$, hence
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{7mm}
        2 - 0.9 t^2 & = 0\\[6pt]
        & \hspace{-1.5mm}  t = 1.49\,\mbox{s}\qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  By part (i), we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        v(1.49)     & = 2(1.49) - 0.3(1.49^3) + 2\\[6pt]
        & \hspace{.275em} = 3.99\,\mbox {ms}^{-1}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
