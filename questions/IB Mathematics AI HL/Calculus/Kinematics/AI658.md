# AI658

**Maximum mark: 6**

A freight lift moves along a vertical straight line so that its
velocity, $v$ ms$^{-1}$, after $t$ seconds is
given by $v(t) = 1.5^t - 4.9$, for $0 \leq t \leq 6$.

1.  Find when the lift is at rest. [2 marks]

2.  Find the acceleration of the lift when $t = 3$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the total distance travelled by the lift. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The lift is at rest when $v(t) = 0$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    1.5^t - 4.9     & = 0\\[3pt]
    & \hspace{-1.5mm}t = 3.92\,\mbox{s}\qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The acceleration is $a(t) = v'(t)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5cm}
    a(3) & = v'(3)\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}= 1.37\,\mbox{ms}^{-2}\qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The total distance travelled by the lift is given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5cm}
    d   & =\int_0^6|1.5^t - 4.9| \,\textrm dt\\[6pt]
    %   & = \left[\dfrac {1.5^t} {\ln1.5} - 4.9 t\right] \Bigg|_0^6\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}= 15.4\,\mbox{m}\qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
