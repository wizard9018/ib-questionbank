# AI660

**Maximum mark: 8**

A particle $P$ starts from a fixed point $O$ and moves along a
horizontal straight line. Its velocity $v$ ms$^{-1}$ after $t$ seconds
is given by
$$
\begin{aligned}
v(t) =
\begin{cases}
3t + 1, &\textrm {for } 0 \leq t \leq 1\\
2 \sqrt t - \dfrac 1 {t^2} + 3,& \textrm {for } 1 \leq t \leq 8
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$

The following diagram shows the graph of $v$.

![81dd5c33bf6112288e7fb373e8824b738373898c.svg](images/81dd5c33bf6112288e7fb373e8824b738373898c.svg)

1.  Find the initial velocity of $P$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the acceleration of $P$ during the first second.[2 marks]

3.  Write down the number of times the particle changes direction in the
    first $8$ seconds. Justify your answer. [2 marks]

4.  Find the total distance travelled by the particle in the first $8$
    seconds.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    v(0)        & = 3(0) + 1\hspace{2cm}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{.275em}= 1\,\mbox {ms}^{-1}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a(t)        & = v'(t)\hspace{2.2cm}\\[5pt]
    & = 3\\[5pt]
    &\hspace{-6.5mm}a(0\rightarrow1) = 3\,\mbox{ms}^{-2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The particle changes direction when $v(t) = 0$. From the diagram we
    see

    that $v(t)$ does not intercept the $x$-axis, hence
      $\mbox{no change in direction}$

4.  The total distance is given by the sum of integrals
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.4cm}
    d       & = \int_0^1 (3t + 1)\,\textrm dt
    + \int_1^8 \left(2 \sqrt t - \dfrac 1 {t^2} + 3\right)\textrm d t\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = 51.5\,\mbox m
    \qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
