# AI666

**Maximum mark: 8**

John drops a stone from the top of a cliff which is $h$ metres above sea
level. The stone strikes the water surface after 9 seconds.

The velocity of the falling stone,
$v$ m s$^{-1}$, $t$
seconds after John releases it, can be modelled by the function

$$
v(t) = \left\{\begin{array}{clc}
9.8t, &\text{for}& 0 \leq t \leq 9, \\[6pt]
88.2e^{\left(3-\frac{t}{3}\right)}, &\text{for}& t > 9.
\end{array}\right.
$$

1.  Find the velocity of the stone when $t = 12$, giving your answer to
    the\
    nearest m s$^{-1}$. [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the value of $h$, giving your answer to the nearest metre.
    [2 marks]

The velocity of the stone when it reaches the bottom of sea is
$10$ m s$^{-1}$.

3.  Determine the depth of sea near the cliff, giving your
    answer to the\
    nearest metre. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The velocity of the stone when $t = 12$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    v(12) & =  88.2e^{\left(3-\frac{12}{3}\right)} \\[9pt]
    & =  \dfrac{88.2}{e} \\[9pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} \approx  32 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m\hspace{0.1em}s}^{-1} \quad \quad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The height of the cliff above sea level is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h & =  \int_0^9 9.8 t\, \textrm dt \\[8pt]
    %& =  \left(4.9\,t^2\right)\big|_0^9 \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} \approx  397 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m} \quad \quad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $v(t) = 10$ for $t$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    88.2e^{\left(3-\frac{t}{3}\right)} & =  10 \\[9pt]
    t & \approx  15.531 \hspace{0.25em}\text{s}. \quad \quad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the depth of sea near the cliff is given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d & =  88.2\int_9^{15.531} e^{\left(3-\frac{t}{3}\right)}\textrm dt \\[8pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}\approx  235 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m} \quad \quad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
