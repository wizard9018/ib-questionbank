# AI423

**Maximum mark: 5**

The daily cost of producing car tires at a manufacturing plant can be
modelled by the following cost function

$$
C(x) = 5000 + 65x + 0.1x^2 - 0.0005x^3, \hspace{1em}0 \leq x \leq 250
$$

where $x$ is the number of car tires produced and $C$ the cost in USD.

1.  Write down the daily cost to the company if no car tires are
    produced. [1 mark]

The marginal cost of production is the cost of producing one additional
unit. This can be approximated by the gradient of the cost function.

2.  Find an expression for the marginal cost, $C'(x)$, of
    producing $x$ car tires. [2 marks]

3.  Find the marginal cost of producing

    1.  $100$ car tires;
    2.  $200$ car tires. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting $x = 0$ into the cost function, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    C(0) &=  5000 + 65(0) + 0.1(0)^2 - 0.0005(0)^3 \\[6pt]
    &= \text{US}\$ 5000
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let's differentiate the cost function to find the marginal cost of
    producing $x$ car tires
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    C(x) &= 5000 + 65x + 0.1x^2 - 0.0005x^3 \\[6pt]
    C'(x) &= 0 + 65(1) + 0.1(2x) - 0.0005(3x^2) \\[6pt]
    &= 65 + 0.2x - 0.0015x^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.  The marginal cost of producing $100$ car tires is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        C'(100) &= 65 + 0.2(100) - 0.0015(100)^2 \\[6pt]
        &= \text{US}\$ 70
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The marginal cost of producing $200$ car tires is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        C'(200) &= 65 + 0.2(200) - 0.0015(200)^2 \\[6pt]
        &= \text{US}\$ 45
        \end{aligned}
        $$
