# AI612

**Maximum mark: 8**

A small population of rabbits in a forest is observed. After $t$ weeks
the population is modelled by

$$
P(t)=\dfrac{15\hspace{0.1em}000}{1+50 e^{-0.6t}}\hspace{0.3em},\hspace{0.1em} \text{where $0\leq t \leq 30$}.
$$

1.  Find $P'(t)$.[2 marks]

2.  Find the rate at which the population is increasing after $10$
    weeks.[2 marks]

3.  Determine the time(s) at which the population is increasing at 1860
    rabbits per week. Round your answer(s) to the nearest
    integer.[2 marks]

4.  During which week does the rate at which the population is
    increasing reach its maximum.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Taking the derivative of $P$ with respect to $t$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    P'(t) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t} \left(\dfrac{15\hspace{0.1em}000}{1+50 e^{-0.6t}}\right)\rule[-5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    &= -\dfrac{15\hspace{0.1em}000((-0.6)(50)e^{-0.6t})}{(1+50e^{-0.6t})^2}\rule[-5.5mm]{0pt}{14mm}\\
    &\hspace{-2mm}= \dfrac{450\hspace{0.1em}000e^{-0.6t}}{(1+50e^{-0.6t})^2}\rule[-5mm]{0pt}{13mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Evaluating $P'(t)$ at $t= 10$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    P'(10) &= \dfrac{450\hspace{0.1em}000e^{-0.6(10)}}{(1+50e^{-0.6(10)})^2} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{11mm}\\
    &\approx 883 \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{11mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore the
    rate of change after $10$ weeks is
    $\text{883}\text{ rabbits per week}$
