# AI631

**Maximum mark: 6**

Points $A$ and $B$ lie on a circle with centre O and radius
$6$ cm. Suppose the angle $\theta=A\widehat{O}B$
is increasing at a rate of $0.5$ radians per
second.

![2e11143c0c1197bd8583762c46fca5be1f66b8fa.svg](images/2e11143c0c1197bd8583762c46fca5be1f66b8fa.svg)

Find the rate at which the area of the shaded region is increasing with
respect to time, at the instant $\theta = 2$ radians.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the area formula for a circular sector and a triangle, we can find
an expression for the area of the shaded region.
$$
\begin{aligned}
A &= \dfrac{r^2 \cdot \theta }{2} - \dfrac{r^2 \cdot \sin\theta}{2} \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
A &= \dfrac{6^2}{2} \left(\theta-\sin\theta\right) \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
A &= 18 \left(\theta-\sin\theta\right) \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Using the chain rule, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}\theta} \times \dfrac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\mathrm{d}t} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Using $A(\theta)$, we can find
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}\theta} &= 18-18\cos\theta \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, we now have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}t} &= (18-18\cos\theta) \times \dfrac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\mathrm{d}t} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, substituting $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\mathrm{d}t}=0.5$ and
$\theta=2$, we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}t} &= (18-18\cos(2)) \times (0.5) \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}t} &\approx 12.7453 \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the shaded area is increasing at a rate of
$12.7\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}^2\hspace{0.05em}\text{/sec}$
