# AI417

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the function $f(x) = 2x^4+1$.

1.  Find $f'(x)$. [1 mark]

2.  Find the gradient of the graph of $f$ at $x = -1$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the $x$-coordinate of the point at which the normal to the
    graph of $f$ has a gradient of $-8$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Differentiating $f(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \, \hspace{1em}
    f'(x) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}(x)}(2x^4 + 1) \\[3pt]
    &= 2(4x^{3}) + 0 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}= 8x^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Substituting $x = -1$ into $f'(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \, \hspace{-5em}
    f'(-1) &= 8(-1)^3 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}= -8
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The gradient of the normal, $m_N$, is equal to $-8$. Hence, we can
    find the gradient of the tangent, $m_T$, at this point using the
    relationship of the gradients of perpendicular lines

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \, \hspace{1.125em}
    m_T &= \dfrac{-1}{m_N} \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{-1}{-8} \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac 1 8
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We can now find the $x$-coordinate of this point
    using the derivate function, $f'(x)$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f'(x) &= \dfrac 1 8 \\[3pt]
    8x^3 &= \dfrac 1 8 \\[3pt]
    x^3 &= \dfrac 1 {64} \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.525em}x = \dfrac 1 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$
