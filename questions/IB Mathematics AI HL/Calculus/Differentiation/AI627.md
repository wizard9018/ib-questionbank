# AI627

**Maximum mark: 7**

A ladder that is $6$ m long rests against a wall
with its feet on horizontal ground $x$ m from
the wall, as illustrated.

![04db1ac2887f921a5ccf2d3196d257cfb00d1292.svg](images/04db1ac2887f921a5ccf2d3196d257cfb00d1292.svg)

1.  Show that the height the ladder reaches up the wall, $y$, can be
    expressed by the equation,
    $$
    y=\sqrt{36-x^2}

    $$
    where $x$ is the
    horizontal distance in metres from the wall to the
    ladder.[2 marks]

The bottom of the ladder is then pulled away from the wall at a constant
speed of $3$ m s$^{-1}$.

2.  Calculate the speed the top of the ladder is moving down
    the wall at the instant when the bottom of the ladder is 4m away
    from the wall.[5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The ladder together with the wall and the floor forms a right
    triangle, let $y$ be the height of the other end.

    ![58fa3b296b5f20ec8af4eb5131b7338c03629d73.svg](images/58fa3b296b5f20ec8af4eb5131b7338c03629d73.svg)

    Using the Pythagorean theorem, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y^2 + x^2 &= 6^2 \rule[-2mm]{0pt}{2mm}\\
    y^2 &= 6^2 - x^2 \rule[-2mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
    &\hspace{-0.45em}y = \sqrt{36-x^2} \rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the chain rule, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} \times \dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \left(\dfrac{1(-2x)}{2\sqrt{36-x^2}}\right) \dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}&=-\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{36-x^2}}\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} \rule[0mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting $x=4$ and
    $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}=3$ m s$^{-1}$
    into equation (2), we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &=-\dfrac{12}{\sqrt{36-(4)^2}}(3)\rule[-5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    &\approx  -2.68\rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the
    top of the ladder is moving at a speed of
     $2.68\hspace{0.25em}\text{m}\hspace{0.05em}\text{/s}$
      down the\
    wall at the instant when the bottom of the ladder is 4m away from
    the wall.
