# AI409

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the function $f(x)  = 2x^2 - b x +c$

1.  Find $f'(x)$. [1 mark]

The equation of the tangent line to the graph of $y = f(x)$ at $x = -1$
is $y = 2x - 7$.

2.  Calculate the value of $b$. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the value of $c$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We differentiate
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.6cm}
    f'(x) & = 2(2x^{1}) - b(1x^{0}) + 0\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = 4 x - b
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Since the gradient of the tangent at $x = -1$ is $2$, $f'(-1) = 2$.
    Using part (a), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2 & = f'(-1)\\[6pt]
    2 & = 4 (-1) - b\\[6pt]
    2 & = -4 - b\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-1mm} b = -6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Substituting $x = -1$ into $y = 2 x - 7$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y & = 2(-1) - 7\\[6pt]
    y & = -9
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    From part (b) we now have
    $f(x) = 2 x^2 + 6x + c$. Substituting $(-1,-9)$ into $f(x)$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.3cm}
    -9 & = f(-1)\\[6pt]
    -9 & = 2 (-1)^2 + 6(-1) + c\\[6pt]
    -9 & = -4 + c\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{-1.4mm} c = -5
    %\qquad\qquad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
