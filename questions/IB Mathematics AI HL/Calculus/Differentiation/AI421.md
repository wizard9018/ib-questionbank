# AI421

**Maximum mark: 5**

The daily cost of production of a company producing lithium batteries is
modelled by the cubic function

$$
C(x) = 900 + 18x - 0.01x^2 - 0.0002x^3, \hspace{1em}0 \leq x \leq 150
$$

where $x$ is the number of lithium batteries produced and $C$ the cost
in USD.

1.  Write down the daily cost to the company if no lithium batteries are
    produced.[1 mark]

The marginal cost of production is the cost of producing one additional
unit. This can be approximated by the gradient of the cost function.

2.  Find an expression for the marginal cost, $C'(x)$, of
    producing $x$ lithium batteries.  [2 marks]

3.  Find the marginal cost of producing

    1.  $50$ lithium batteries;
    2.  $100$ lithium batteries. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting $x = 0$ into the cost function, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    C(0) &= 900 + 18(0) - 0.01(0)^2 - 0.0002(0)^3 \\[6pt]
    &= \text{US}\$ 900
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let's differentiate the cost function to find the marginal cost of
    producing $x$ lithium batteries.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    C(x) &= 900 + 18x - 0.01x^2 - 0.0002x^3 \\[6pt]
    C'(x) &= 0 + 18(1) - 0.01(2x) - 0.0002(3x^2) \\[6pt]
    &= 18 - 0.02x - 0.0006x^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.  The marginal cost of producing $50$ lithium batteries is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        C'(50) &= 18 - 0.02(50) - 0.0006(50)^2 \\[6pt]
        &= \text{US}\$ 15.50
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The marginal cost of producing $100$ lithium batteries is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        C'(100) &= 18 - 0.02(100) - 0.0006(100)^2 \\[6pt]
        &= \text{US}\$ 10
        \end{aligned}
        $$
