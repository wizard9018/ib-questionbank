# AI611

**Maximum mark: 7**

The concentration in mg of a certain medicine $t$ hours after
administration can be modeled by
$C(t)=1\hspace{0.1em}000\hspace{0.05em}te^{-1.25t}$ ,
$t\geq0$.

1.  Find the rate of change of the concentration with respect to time.
    [3 marks]

2.  Find the value of $t$ when the rate of change of $C$ is
    zero.[2 marks]

3.  Determine if the time found in **part (b)** is a local minima or
    maxima for $C$. Justify your answer.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the product rule to take the derivative of $C$ with respect to
    $t$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    C'(t) &=\dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t} \big[ 1\hspace{0.1em}000\hspace{0.05em}te^{-1.25t}\big] \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    &= 1\hspace{0.1em}000e^{-1.25t}+1\hspace{0.1em}000(-1.25)te^{-1.25t}\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    &\hspace{0.29em}= 1\hspace{0.1em}000 e^{-1.25t}(1-1.25\hspace{0.05em}t) \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{10mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Letting $C'(t)=0$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    %\\
    1\hspace{0.1em}000 e^{-1.25t}(1-1.25\hspace{0.05em}t) &= 0 \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{6.5mm}\\
    1-1.25\hspace{0.05em}t&= 0 \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{6.5mm}\\
    &\hspace{-1.4mm}t = 0.8 \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{6.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the ID Test, we have

    ![5054d264a3d2a5693de4f995278b5a9c5d0829f1.svg](images/5054d264a3d2a5693de4f995278b5a9c5d0829f1.svg)

    The sign of $C'$ changes from positive to negative at $t=0.8$.
    Therefore, by the First Derivative Test, $C$ has a local maximum at
    $t=0.8$.
