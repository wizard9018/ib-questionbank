# AI628

**Maximum mark: 6**

A trough, containing water, of length $8$ m has
a uniform cross-section in the shape of a trapezoid, with side lengths
shown below.

![bf531c0e18355493509c7d7793811ec4d87f00dd.svg](images/bf531c0e18355493509c7d7793811ec4d87f00dd.svg)

When the water is $h$ m deep, the volume of
water in the trough is given by
$$
V(h)=16h+\dfrac{8h^2}{\sqrt{3}}
$$
Water leaks from the bottom of the trough at a constant rate of
$3.1$ m$^3$ per minute. Find the rate at which
the height of the water level $h$ is falling at the instant when the
water has $50$ cm of depth.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the chain rule, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}h}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}h}{\mathrm{d}V} \times \dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Using $V(h)$, we can find
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}h} &= 16+\dfrac{16h}{\sqrt{3}} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
&= \dfrac{16\sqrt{3}+16h}{\sqrt{3}} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
\therefore \hspace{1em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}h}{\mathrm{d}V} &= \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{16\sqrt{3}+16h} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, we now have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}h}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{16\sqrt{3}+16h} \times \dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, substituting $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t}=-3.1$ and $h=0.5$,
we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}h}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{16\sqrt{3}+16(0.5)} \times (-3.1) \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}h}{\mathrm{d}t} &\approx -0.150348 \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the height of the water level is falling at a rate of
$\text{15}\text{ cm per min}$
