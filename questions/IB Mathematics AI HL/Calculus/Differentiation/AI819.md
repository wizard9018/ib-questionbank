# AI819

**Maximum mark: 14**

A function, $f$, is given by

$$
f(x)= \dfrac{1}{3}\cdot 2^{-2x} + \dfrac 12x -1.
$$

1.  Calculate $f(-2)$. [2 marks]

2.  Use your graphic display calculator to solve $f(x) = 0$. [2 marks]

3.  Sketch the graph of $y = f(x)$ for $-2 \leq x \leq 5$ and
    $-1 \leq y \leq 4$. Clearly label the coordinates of the $x$ and $y$
    intercepts. [4 marks]

The function $f$ is the derivative of the function $g$. It is known that
$g(0) = 1$.

4.
    1.  Calculate $g'(0)$.

    2.  Find the equation of the tangent of the graph of $y = g(x)$ at
        the point $(0,1)$. Give your answer in the
        form $y =mx+c$. [6 marks]

1.  Substituting $x = -2$ into $f(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(-2) &= \dfrac{1}{3}\cdot 2^{-2(-2)} + \dfrac 12(-2) -1 \\[3pt]
    &=  \dfrac{16}{3}-1 - 1 \\[3pt]
    %    &=  \dfrac{769}{768} \\[3pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}=\dfrac{10}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We want to solve for $x$ for when $f(x) = 0$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(x) &= \dfrac{1}{3}\cdot 2^{-2x} + \dfrac 12x -1 \\[3pt]
    0    &= \dfrac{1}{3}\cdot 2^{-2x} + \dfrac 12x -1
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using GDC, we get
     $x = -1.11 \text{ or }  x = 1.96$

4.
    1.  Since $g'(x) = f(x)$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        g'(0) &= f(0) \\[3pt]
        &= \dfrac{1}{3}\cdot 2^{-2(0)} + \dfrac 12(0) -1 \\[3pt]
        &= \dfrac{1}{3}-1 \\[3pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}= -\dfrac{2}{3}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The gradient of the tangent ($m_T$) of $g(x)$ at $x=0$ is
        $-{2}/{3}$. Using the point-slope form of a straight line where
        $(x_1, y_1) = (0,1)$ and $m =-{2}/{3}$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        y - y_1 &= m(x-x_1) \\[3pt]
        y - 1&= -\dfrac{2}{3}(x - 0) \\[3pt]
        y - 1 &= -\dfrac{2}{3} x
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Rearranging into gradient-intercept form, we
        get   $y = -\dfrac{2}{3} x+1$
