# AI632

**Maximum mark: 7**

The volume $V$ of a cube increases at a rate of
$4$ cm$^3$ sec$^{-1}$.
Find the rate of change of the surface area of the cube, with respect to
time $t$, at the instant when the volume of the cube is
$8$ cm$^3$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Letting $l$ be the side length of the cube, we know that

$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= l^3 \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
V^{\frac{1}{3}} &= l \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
A &= 6 \times l^2 \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
\therefore \hspace{0.4em} A &= 6 \times V^{\frac{2}{3}} \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, we can find
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}V} \times \dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Using $A(V)$, we can find
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}V} &= 4 \times V^{-\frac{1}{3}} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, we now have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}t} &= (4 \times V^{-\frac{1}{3}}) \times \dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Now, substituting $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t}=4$ and $V=8$, we
obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}t} &= (4 \times (8)^{-\frac{1}{3}}) \times (4) = 8\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, the surface area is increasing at a rate of
$8\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}^2\hspace{0.05em}\text{/sec}$
