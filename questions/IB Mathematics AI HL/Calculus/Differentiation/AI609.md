# AI609

**Maximum mark: 7**

Tom uses a compass to draw a circle. The arm of the compass with the
needle is 7 cm long, and the arm with the pencil
is 8 cm long. The angle between the arms is
$\theta$ as shown in the diagram below.

![7cffc88fe839e61e7b8b00816659b1757515673d.svg](images/7cffc88fe839e61e7b8b00816659b1757515673d.svg)

1.  Find an expression for the radius of the circle to be drawn in terms
    of $\theta$.[3 marks]

2.  Hence, find the rate of change in $r$ with respect to $\theta$ when
    $\theta =30^\circ$.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r^2 &= 7^2+8^2-2(7)(8)\cos\theta \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{5mm}\\
    &\hspace{-1.8mm}r = \sqrt{113-112\cos\theta} \rule[0mm]{0pt}{5.5mm} \qquad [\text{Since }r>0]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Taking the derivative of $r$ with respect to $\theta$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}\theta} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}\theta} \Big[\sqrt{113-112\cos\theta} \,\Big]\rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{5mm}\\
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}  \Big[\sqrt{113-112\cos\theta} \,\Big]^{-\frac{1}{2}}(112\sin\theta) \rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &= \dfrac{56\sin\theta}{\sqrt{113-112\cos\theta}} \rule[0mm]{0pt}{7.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    As the rate of change formula is given in terms of the angle
    $\theta$ in radians, first we convert $\theta=30^\circ$ to radians,
    as follows
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \left(\ang{30}\right)\left(\dfrac{\pi\,\text{radians}}{\ang{180}}\right) = \dfrac{\pi}{6}\,\text{radians}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Finally, evaluating the derivative of $r$ with respect to $\theta$
    at $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}\theta}\bigg\lvert_{\footnotesize\mbox{$\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{6}$}} &= \dfrac{56\sin \dfrac{\pi}{6}}{\sqrt{113-112\cos \dfrac{\pi}{6}}} \rule[-5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &\approx 6.99887 \rule[-5mm]{0pt}{12mm} \hspace{4em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Therefore, the rate of change is
    $7.00\text{ cm per radian}$
