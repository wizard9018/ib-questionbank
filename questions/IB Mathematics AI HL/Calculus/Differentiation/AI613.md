# AI613

**Maximum mark: 6**

The number of bacteria in a culture is given by the function
$n(t)=310e^{kt}$, where $t$ is the time in minutes after the start of
the culture and $k$ is a constant. It is known that the number of
bacteria doubles every 10 minutes.

1.  Find the exact value of $k$.[3 marks]

2.  Find the rate of change of the number of bacteria, $n'(t)$, when
    $t=11$. Round your answer to the nearest integer.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Given that the number of bacteria doubles every 10 minutes, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    310\hspace{0.05em}e^{(10)k} &= 2\cdot310e^{(0)k}\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    e^{10k} &= 2\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    10k &= \ln 2 \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    &\hspace{-2mm}k = \dfrac{\ln 2}{10} \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Substituting the value of $k$ found in part (a), and taking the
    derivative with respect to $t$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    n(t) &= 310 e^{\frac{\ln 2}{10}t} \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{11mm}\\
    n'(t) &= 310 \left(\dfrac{\ln 2}{10}\right)e^{\frac{\ln 2}{10}t} \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{11mm}\\
    &= 31\cdot \ln 2 \hspace{0.05em} e^{\frac{\ln 2}{10}t}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{11mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Evaluating $n'(t)$ at $t=11$, we get

    $$
    n'(11) = 31\cdot \ln 2 \hspace{0.05em} e^{\frac{\ln 2}{10}(11)} \approx 46.0596 \qquad [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    $$
    Therefore, at the instant when $t=11$, the number of bacteria in
    the culture is increasing at a rate of
     $\text{46}\text{ bacteria per minute}$
