# AI630

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows the graph of $f'$, the derivative of $f$.

![5fc6d51a5be97f36e791638f80bec9eadd8df94b.svg](images/5fc6d51a5be97f36e791638f80bec9eadd8df94b.svg)

The graph of $f'$ has a local maximum at A, a local minimum at B and
passes through P$(2,\hspace{-0.1em}-3)$.

1.  The point Q$(2,6)$ lies on the graph of the function $f$.
    1.  Write down the gradient of the curve of $f$ at Q.

    2.  Find the equation of the normal to the curve of $f$ at Q.
        [4 marks]

2.  Determine the concavity of the graph of $f$ when $2 < x < 3$ **and**
    justify your answer. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  The gradient of the curve of $f$ at Q is
          $f'(2) = -3$

    2.  The slope of the normal to the curve at Q$(2,6)$ is
        $m = \dfrac 1 3$ (from part (i)) and the equation of the normal
        is given by
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        y - y_1 & = m (x - x_1)\\[5pt]
        y - 6       & = \dfrac 1 3(x - 2)\\[5pt]
        &\hspace{-2mm}y = \dfrac 1 3 x + \dfrac {16} 3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  From the diagram, we see that $f'$ is decreasing on $(2,3)$, hence
    $f''(x) < 0$ on this interval, therefore we conclude that $f$ is
    concave down on $(2,3)$.
