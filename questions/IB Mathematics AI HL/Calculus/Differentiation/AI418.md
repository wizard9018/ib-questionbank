# AI418

**Maximum mark: 15**

Sandy is on a holiday in Hawaii and takes a parasailing ride on a beach.
She is towed behind a motor boat and attached to the parasail. The
vertical height of the parasail over the first part of the ride can be
modelled by the equation

$$
h(x) = \dfrac{2x^3}{45} - \dfrac{17x^2}{15} + 8x
$$
for
$0 \leq x \leq 15$, where $x$ m is the
horizontal distance from the start, and $h$ m is
the vertical height.

1.  Find $h'(x)$. [2 marks]

2.  Solve $h'(x) = 0$.[2 marks]

3.  Using your answer to part (b), find the coordinates of the turning
    points of $h(x)$. [4 marks]

4.  Sketch the graph of $h(x)$ for $0 \leq x \leq 15$, labelling the
    turning points. [3 marks]

5.  If the parasail reaches a height level of
    $12$ m, Sandy might suffer from vertigo.
    Find the $x$ value for which this first happens. [2 marks]

6.  When the gradient is negatively steeper than $-\dfrac 3 2$, Sandy
    screams. Find the $x$ value for which this first happens.
    [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Differentiating $h(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h'(x) &= \dfrac{2}{45}\left( 3x^{2}\right) - \dfrac{17}{15} \left( 2x^{1}\right) + 8x^{0} \\[6pt]
    & = \dfrac{2}{15}x^2 - \dfrac{34}{15}x + 8
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Solve $h'(x) = 0$ for $x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{2}{15}x^2 - \dfrac{34}{15}x + 8 &= 0 \\[3pt]
    2x^2 - 34x + 120 &= 0 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-1.6cm}x = 5 \text{ or } x = 12 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  From part (b) we found the $x$ values for which $h'(x) = 0$. These
    are the $x$-coordinates of the stationary points. Let's substitute
    them into $h(x)$ to find the corresponding $y$-coordinates of these
    points.

    Substituting $x = 5$ into $h(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h(5) &= \dfrac{2(5)^3}{45} - \dfrac{17(5)^2}{15} + 8(5) \\[3pt]
    &=\dfrac{155}{9}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting $x = 12$ into $h(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h(12) &= \dfrac{2(12)^3}{45} - \dfrac{17(12)^2}{15} + 8(12) \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{48}{5}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of the two turning points are:

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     &  \left(5, \dfrac{155}{9}\right)\left(12, \dfrac{48}{5}\right) \\[12pt]
     &\hspace{0.275em}
     \end{aligned}
     $$

5.  Solving for $h(x) = 12$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \phantom{\hspace{3cm}} \dfrac{2x^3}{45} - \dfrac{17x^2}{15} + 8x &= 12 \\[3pt]
    \dfrac{2x^3}{45} - \dfrac{17x^2}{15} + 8x -12 &= 0 \\[3pt]
    x \approx 2.04 \text{ or } x \approx 9.38 \text{ or } x &\approx 14.1  \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence this first happens at
     $x \approx 2.04$

6.  Setting $h'(x) = -\dfrac 3 2$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \phantom{\hspace{3.4cm}}
    \dfrac{2}{15}x^2 - \dfrac{34}{15}x + 8 &= -\dfrac 3 2 \\[3pt]
    \dfrac{2}{15}x^2 - \dfrac{34}{15}x + \dfrac {19} 2 &= 0 \\[3pt]
    x = 7.5 \text{ or } x &= 9.5  \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence this first happens at
     $x = 7.5$
