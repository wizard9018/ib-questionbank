# AI1152

**Maximum mark: 5**

A cylindrical shaped air drum, with a fixed height of $0.8\,$ metres, has a changing radius such that the volume of air being **pushed out** per second is a constant $45\,$ m$^3$s$^{-1}$.

1.  Find the radius of the drum when the volume is $180\pi\,\text{m}^3$.  [2 marks]

2.  For the value found in part (a) find the rate of change of the radius at this instance.  [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We can write an equation for the volume of the cylinder using the formula and the given fact that $h=0.8$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        V&=\pi r^2 h\\[6pt]
        180\pi&=\pi r^2 \times 0.8  \tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[6pt]
        r&=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{15\,\text{m}}\hspace{2em}[\text{by using G.D.C.}]   \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
2.  Using the chain rule we can write
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \frac{\text{d}r}{\text{d}t}&=\frac{\text{d}r}{\text{d}V}\times \frac{\text{d}V}{\text{d}t}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We know that $\frac{\text{d}V}{\text{d}t}=-45$ as the air is being pushed out and the volume is decreasing. Hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \frac{\text{d}r}{\text{d}t}&=\frac{\text{d}r}{\text{d}V}\times -45 \tag*{\textbf{M1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We want to known $\frac{\text{d}r}{\text{d}V}$ when $r=15$.

    Recall
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        V&=\pi r^2 h\\
        &=0.8\pi r^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We can differentiate this with respect to $r$ and evaluate the derivative when $r=15$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \frac{\text{d}V}{\text{d}r}&=1.6\pi r\\
        &=1.6\pi \times 15\\
        &=75.398...\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Note $\tfrac{\text{d}r}{\text{d}V}=\tfrac{1}{\tfrac{\text{d}V}{\text{d}r}}$

    Hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \frac{\text{d}r}{\text{d}t}&=\frac{1}{75.398...}\times -45\\[6pt]
        &=-0.59683...\\[6pt]
        &=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{-0.597\,\text{m}\,\text{s}^{-1}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
