# AI605

**Maximum mark: 6**

A particle $P$ moves so that its velocity, in
m s$^{-1}$, at time $t$ seconds can be described
by the function $v(t)=2\cos t$, where $t\geq0$. The kinetic energy of
the particle, in joules (J), is given by the function
$E(v)=3v^2$.

1.  Find an expression for $E$ as a function of time.[2 marks]

2.  Hence, find $E'(t)$.[2 marks]

3.  Hence or otherwise, find the first time at which the kinetic energy
    is changing at a rate of
    4 J s$^{-1}$.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We find an expression for $E$ as a function of time by taking the
    composite $E\circ v$, given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    E(t) &= E\circ v(t) \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{1mm}\\
    &= E(2\cos t)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    &= 3\left(2 \cos t\right)^2 \\
    &\hspace{-2.2em}E(t) = 12 \cos^2 t\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Differentiating $E$ with respect to $t$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    E'(t) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}\hspace{1pt}t} \bigg[ 12 \cos^2 t \bigg] \rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{13mm}\\
    &= 12\cdot (2\cos t)(-\sin t) \rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{11mm}\\
    &\hspace{-2em}E'(t) = -24 \cos t\sin t\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The kinetic energy is changing at a rate of
    $4$ J s$^{-1}$ when
    $E'(t) = 4$.

    If we draw the graph of $E'(t)$ versus $t$ and $y=4$ \[by using
    G.D.C.\], we have

    ![753e849435f4f04a15e5faa577c767617465d567.svg](images/753e849435f4f04a15e5faa577c767617465d567.svg)

    By inspection, we find the first time at which the kinetic energy is
    changing at a rate of
    $4$ J s$^{-1}$:

    $$
    t=1.74\hspace{0.25em}s
    $$
