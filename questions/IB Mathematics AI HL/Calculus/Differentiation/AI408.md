# AI408

**Maximum mark: 6**

The equation of the curve $C$ is
$y = \dfrac 1 {45} x^5 - \dfrac 2 3 x^3 + 6x$. A section of the curve
$C$ is shown on the diagram below.

![9f504b0f6d94aa31f52e8cd80117bf729e835568.svg](images/9f504b0f6d94aa31f52e8cd80117bf729e835568.svg)

1.  Find $\dfrac {\text d y}{\text dx}$. [2 marks]

There are two points, A and B, in the domain of the curve shown at which
the gradient of the tangent line to the curve $C$ is equal to $-3$.

2.  Calculate the $x$-coordinates of points A and B.
    [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Differentiating $y$ with respect to $x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac {\mathrm d y}{\mathrm d x} & = \dfrac {\mathrm d}{\mathrm d x} \left( \dfrac 1 {45} x^5 - \dfrac 2 3 x^3 + 6x \right) \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac 1 {45} (5x^{4})
    - \dfrac 2 3 (3x^{2})
    + 6x^{0}\\[8pt]
    & = \dfrac 1 9\,x^4 - 2 x^2 + 6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  By letting $\dfrac {\text d y}{\text d x}  = -3$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5mm}
    -3 & = \dfrac 1 9 x^4 - 2 x^2 + 6\\[6pt]
    0 & = \dfrac 1 9 x^4 - 2 x^2 + 9\\[6pt]
    x & = -3\ \ \mbox{and}\ \ x = 3 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the $x$-coordinates of points A and B are
      $-3\ \mbox{and}\ 3$
     .
