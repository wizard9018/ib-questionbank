# AI407

**Maximum mark: 17**

Consider the function $f(x) = \dfrac 1 {2x} + \dfrac {x^4} 8 - 1.625$,
$x \neq 0$.

1.  Find $f'(x)$. [3 marks]

2.  Calculate $f(1)$. [2 marks]

3.  Find $f'(1)$. [2 marks]

4.  Sketch the graph of $f(x)$, for $-3\leq x \leq 3$ and
    $-3\leq y \leq 7$. [4 marks]

5.  Draw the tangent line, $A$, to the graph of $y = f(x)$ at the point
    $x = 1$. [2 marks]

6.  Find the equation of the tangent line $A$. [2 marks]

7.  The graph of $y = f(x)$ and the tangent line $A$ have a second
    intersection at point K. Use your graphic
    display calculator to find the $x$-coordinate
    of K. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  By rewriting $f(x)$ as
    $f(x) = \dfrac 1 2x^{-1} + \dfrac 1 8 x^4 - 1.625$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6mm}
    f'(x) & = \left(\dfrac 1 2x^{-1} + \dfrac 1 8 x^4 - 1.625 \right)'\\[3pt]
    & = \dfrac 1 2(-1x^{-2}) + \dfrac 1 8(4x^3) - 0\\[3pt]
    & = -\dfrac 1 2 x^{-2} + \dfrac{1}{2} x^3\\[8pt]
    & = -\dfrac 1 {2x^2} + \dfrac {x^3} 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Substituting $x = 1$ into $f(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1cm}
    f(1) & = \dfrac 1 {2(1)} + \dfrac {1^4} 8 - 1.625\\[6pt]
    & = -1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Substituting $x = 1$ into $f'(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f'(1) & = -\dfrac 1 {2(1)^2} + \dfrac {1^3} 2\\[6pt]
    & = 0
    \end{aligned}
    $$
