# AI643

**Maximum mark: 15**

Let $f(x) = x^3 + 3x^2 - 9x + k$. Part of the graph of $f$ is shown
below. The graph of $f$ has a local maximum at A, a local minimum at B
and a point of inflection at C.

![82b794a48ab65bd95cc0c0e1dcc2dc5ac6c03744.svg](images/82b794a48ab65bd95cc0c0e1dcc2dc5ac6c03744.svg)

1.
    1.  Find $f'(x)$.

    2.  Find $f''(x)$. [4 marks]

2.  Find the $x$-coordinate of the point of inflection at C. [2 marks]

Given that $f(-1) = 14$.

3.
    1.  Find $f(0)$.

    2.  Hence, find the coordinates of the local maximum A$(x,y)$ and
        justify your answer. [7 marks]

4.  Write down in order from least to greatest $f''(\mbox B)$,
    $f'(\mbox B)$, $f(\mbox B)$. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $$
        f'(x) = 3x^2 + 6x - 9\hspace{1cm}
        $$

    2.
        $$
        f''(x) = 6x + 6\hspace{2cm}
        $$

2.  A point of inflection occurs when $f''(x) = 0$, hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    6x + 6  & = 0\hspace{2.2cm}\\[5pt]
    & \hspace{-1.9mm}x = -1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.  Substituting $f(-1) = 14$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.8cm}
        14  & = (-1)^3 + 3(-1)^2 - 9(-1) + k\\[6pt]
        14  & = -1 + 3 + 9 +k\\[6pt]
        k   & = 3
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{.6cm}
        f(0)    & = (0)^3 + 3(0)^2 - 9(0) + 3\\[10pt]
        &\hspace{-6.4mm}f(0)  = 3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  A local maximum occurs when $f'(x) = 0$, hence
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        3x^2 + 6x - 9   & = 0\\[5pt]
        x^2 + 2x - 3    & = 0\\[5pt]
        (x + 3)(x - 1)  & = 0\\[5pt]
        x           & = -3,\,1
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Substituting $x = -3$ into $f''(x)$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        f''(-3)     & = 6(-3) + 6\\[5pt]
        & = -12
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Since $f''(-3) < 0$, we conclude
        that $f(x)$ has a local maximum at $x= -3$. Then

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        f(-3)   & = (-3)^3 + 3(-3)^2 - 9(-3) + 3\\[5pt]
        & = 30
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Finally
          $\mbox A(-3,30)$

4.  We see from the diagram that $f''(\mbox B) > 0$, $f'(\mbox B) = 0$,
    and $f(\mbox B) < 0$, hence\
    $f(\mbox B), \ f'(\mbox B),\ f''(\mbox B)$
