# AI820

**Maximum mark: 12**

Consider the region $R$ enclosed by the curve $y = \dfrac12(x+2)(x-3)$
and the $x$-axis.

1.  Sketch the curve and shade the region $R$ on the following
    axes. [3 marks]

    ![352305c4cadaa31ce194cbf092e685ba89eda799.svg](images/352305c4cadaa31ce194cbf092e685ba89eda799.svg)

2.
    1.  Write down an integral that represents the area of region $R$.

    2.  Find the area of region $R$. [4 marks]

3.  Estimate the area of region $R$ using five trapezoids. [4 marks]

4.  Find the percentage error between the exact value found in part
    (b)(ii) and the estimation in part (c). [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.

    ![a5ff776db9844abd20f9ab24d17b0d07d3d94a0e.svg](images/a5ff776db9844abd20f9ab24d17b0d07d3d94a0e.svg)

2.
    1.  Since the region is below the $x$-axis, the area of region $R$
        is determined by
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        A =-\int_{-2}^3 \dfrac12(x+2)(x-3) \, \mathrm{d}x
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Evaluating the integral in part (b)(i), we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        A &=- \int_{-2}^3 \dfrac12(x+2)(x-3) \\[3pt]
        &= -\dfrac12\int_{-2}^3 (x^2 - 3x +2x -6)\,\, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
        &=- \dfrac12\int_{-2}^3 (x^2 - x -6) \,\mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
        &= -\dfrac12\left[\dfrac 1 3 x^3 - \dfrac 1 2 x^2  -6x \right]_{-2}^3 \\[3pt]
        &= -\dfrac12\left[\left(\dfrac 1 3 (3)^3 - \dfrac12 (3)^2 -6(3)\right) - \left(\dfrac 1 3 (-2)^3 - \dfrac12 (-2)^2 -6(-2)\right) \right] \\[6pt]
        &\approx 10.4 \text{ units}^2 \hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  Since five trapezoids are used to estimate the area of region $R$,
    the strip width is $h = \dfrac{3-(-2)}{5} = 1$. Let's find the
    $x$-values of the subintervals of the trapezoids and their
    corresponding $y$-values, as follows.

    ![629a6253932ac7a9f7fa6be73ed0ce1f34a79f22.svg](images/629a6253932ac7a9f7fa6be73ed0ce1f34a79f22.svg)

    These trapezoids are illustrated as follows.

    ![bfab42ac8f83dcb47f07d8347363fb40e1dd0d38.svg](images/bfab42ac8f83dcb47f07d8347363fb40e1dd0d38.svg)

    Using the trapezoidal rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_{-2}^3 |f(x)| \, \mathrm{d}x &\approx -\dfrac{1}{2}h \left( (y_0 + y_n) + 2(y_1 + y_2 + \dots + y_{n-1} ) \right) \\[3pt]
    &\approx -\dfrac{1}{2}(1) \left[ (0 + 0) + 2((-2) + (-3) +(-3) + (-2))  \right] \\[6pt]
    &\approx 10\text{ units}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The percentage error between the approximation in part (c), $V_A$,
    and the exact value in part (b)(ii), $V_E$, can be found using the
    percentage error formula, as follows
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[\% error]} &= \left|\dfrac{V_A-V_E}{V_E}\right| \times 100\% \\[3pt]
    &= \left|\dfrac{10 - 10.4}{10.4}\right| \times 100\% \\[6pt]
    &\approx 3.85 \%
    \end{aligned}
    $$
