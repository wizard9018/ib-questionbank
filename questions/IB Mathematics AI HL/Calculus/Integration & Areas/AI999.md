# AI999

**Maximum mark: 6**

On the Persian Gulf, there is a small peninsula that can be modelled by the equation
$y = -0.2x^3+1.2x^2-x$ where $x$ and $y$ are measured in kilometres, as shown in the following diagram. Points P and Q are on the coastline.

![AI999a](images/AI999a.PNG)

 1. Find the coordinates of P and Q. [2 marks]

 2. Find the furthest distance that the land protrudes from the coastline. [2 marks]

 3. Find the area of the peninsula. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. By using the GDC, we find that the zeros of the function are $(1,0)$ and $(5,0)$, as shown in the following diagram.

    ![AI999b](images/AI999b.PNG)

    Hence, $\text{P}(1,0)$ and $\text{Q}(5,0)$

2. By using  the GDC, we find that the maximum point of the graph is $\text{M}(3.53,2.63)$, as shown in the following diagram.

    ![AI999c](images/AI999c.PNG)

    Hence, the furthest distance that the land protrudes from the coastline is $2.63\,$km.

3. The area of the peninsula is given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5em} A &= \int_1^5 (-0.2x^3+1.2x^2-x) \, \mathrm{d}x\\[8pt]
      &\hspace{-0.75em}\boxed{A = 6.4\hspace{0.2em}\text{km}^2} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
