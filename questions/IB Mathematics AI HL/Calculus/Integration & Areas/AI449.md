# AI449

**Maximum mark: 6**

The equation of a curve is $y = -x^2 + 8x - 12$. A section of the curve
is shown on the diagram below, with the two $x$-intercepts labelled.

![c2fbee5ada527ee7ac66bd331318622398a5d354.svg](images/c2fbee5ada527ee7ac66bd331318622398a5d354.svg)

1.  Find $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}$. [2 marks]

2.  Write down the coordinates of the local maximum. [1 mark]

3.  Write down an integral representing the area of the shaded region.
    [1 mark]

4.  Find the area of the shaded region. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Differentiating $y$ with respect to $x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\left( -x^2 + 8x - 12 \right) \\[6pt]
    &= -2x + 8
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Setting $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = 0$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -2x + 8 &= 0 \\[3pt]
    x &= 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    This $x$-value is the $x$-coordinate of the
    turning point on the diagram.

    ![3487bef2d5b7655088b25dc1a2feda40debe6db3.svg](images/3487bef2d5b7655088b25dc1a2feda40debe6db3.svg)

    Substituting $x = 4$ into $y$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= -(4)^2 + 8(4) - 12 \\[3pt]
    &= 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the local maximum is located at
     $\text{(4, 4)}$

3.  Using the diagram, we can see that the shaded region is bounded
    between $x = 2$ and $x = 6$. Hence, its area is determined by

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     A = \int_2^6 (-x^2 + 8x - 12) \, \mathrm{d}x
     \end{aligned}
     $$

4.  Evaluating the integral in part (c), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \int_2^6 (-x^2 + 8x - 12) \, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac 1 3 x^3 + 8 \left( \dfrac 1 2 x^2 \right) - 12x\right]_2^6 \\[3pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac 1 3 x^3 +  4 x^2 - 12x\right]_2^6 \\[3pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac 1 3 (6)^3 +  4 (6)^2 - 12(6)\right] - \left[-\dfrac 1 3 (2)^3 +  4 (2)^2 - 12(2)\right] \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{32}{3} \text{ units}^2 \hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
