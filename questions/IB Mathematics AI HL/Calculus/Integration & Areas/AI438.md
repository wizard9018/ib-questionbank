# AI438

**Maximum mark: 6**

A fire truck is pumping water at a rate of $r = \dfrac{2t^2}{35}$ where
$r$ is in litres per second and $t$ is the time measured in seconds.

The graph of $r$ is shown below.

![076099b48b7c8dbffc4ab1bab71e00e34b954017.svg](images/076099b48b7c8dbffc4ab1bab71e00e34b954017.svg)

1.  Write down an integral that represents the area of the shaded
    region. [1 mark]

2.
    1.  Find the area of the shaded region, rounding your answer to the
        nearest whole number.

    2.  Interpret the meaning of the shaded area in the context of this
        question. [3 marks]

3.  Calculate the number of seconds it takes for the fire truck to pump
    $1000$ L of water. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The area of the shaded region can be calculated by the integral
    $$
    A = \int_0^{120} \dfrac {2t^2}{35}\, \mathrm{d}t
    $$

2.
    1.  Evaluating the integral in part (a), we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \int_0^{120} \dfrac {2t^2}{35}\, \mathrm{d}t &=  \left[\dfrac{2}{35} \left( \dfrac{t^3}{3}\right)\right]_0^{120} \\[3pt]
        &= \left[\dfrac{2t^3}{105}\right]_0^{120} \\[3pt]
        &= \dfrac{2(120)^3}{105} - \dfrac{2(0)^3}{105} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 32\hspace{0.2em}914  \hspace{0.25em}\text{L}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The area under the curve represents the total volume of water
        pumped from the fire truck for a given number of seconds, $s$.\
        $\text{Hence, \(32\hspace{0.2em}914\) litres of water has been pumped from the truck after \(2\) min.}$

3.  Let $s$ be the number of seconds it takes the fire truck to pump
    $1000$ L of water. We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_0^{s} \dfrac {2t^2}{35}\, \mathrm{d}t  &= 1000 \\[3pt]
    \left[\dfrac{2}{35} \left( \dfrac{t^3}{3}\right)\right]_0^{s} &= 1000 \\[3pt]
    \left[\dfrac{2t^3}{105}\right]_0^{s} &= 1000 \\[3pt]
    \dfrac{2s^3}{105} &= 1000 \\[3pt]
    s^3 &= 52500 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.68em}s \approx 37.4 \hspace{0.25em}\text{s}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
