# AI434

**Maximum mark: 6**

A reservoir of water has a cross-sectional area shown in the shaded
region of the diagram below, where $x$ and $y$ values are in metres. The
bottom of the reservoir (the curve shown) has an equation in the form

$$
y = k (x-8)^2
$$

![3e6a7015e8369a3cc24b8ab582e9056c9efba0e1.svg](images/3e6a7015e8369a3cc24b8ab582e9056c9efba0e1.svg)

1.  Find the area of the rectangle OMNP. [1 mark]

2.  Determine the value of $k$.[2 marks]

3.  Find the cross-sectional area of the reservoir. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The area of the rectangle OMNP is determined by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_1 &= OP \times OM \\[3pt]
    &= 12 \times 6 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}= 72 \text{ m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  From the diagram we can see that the coordinates of point M are
    $(0,6)$. Since the curve passes though point M, its coordinates must
    satisfy the equation of the curve. Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    6 &= k(0 - 8)^2 \\[3pt]
    6 &= 64k  \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.6em}k = \dfrac 3 {32}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Let's first find the area between the curve and the $x$-axis. Using
    $k = \dfrac{3}{32}$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_2 &= \int_0^{12}  \dfrac{3}{32} (x-8)^2 dx \\[3pt]
    &=  \left[ \dfrac 3 {32} \cdot \dfrac 1 3 (x-8)^3 \right]_0^{12} \\[3pt]
    &= \left[ \dfrac{1}{32} (x-8)^3 \right]_0^{12} \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{32} \left[(12-8)^3 - (0-8)^3 \right] \\[6pt]
    &= 18 \text{ m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The cross-sectional area (shaded region) can now be found by

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[Cross-sectional area]} &= A_1 - A_2 \\[3pt]
    &= 72 - 18 \\[6pt]
    &= 54 \text{ m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
