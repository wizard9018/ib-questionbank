# AI445

**Maximum mark: 6**

The area $A$ is defined as the region bounded by the curve
$y = -\dfrac{x^2}{5}(2x - 5)$ and the $x$-axis for
$0 \leq x \leq \dfrac 5 2$.

1.  Sketch the curve on the diagram below, shading the area
    $A$. [3 marks]

    ![3393e1cf3d29ee22b0566fe9782c48dcdf499067.svg](images/3393e1cf3d29ee22b0566fe9782c48dcdf499067.svg)

2.  Write down a definite integral that represents area $A$. [1 mark]

3.  Find the area of $A$. [2 marks]

2.  Since the area $A$ is the region bounded by the curve and the
    $x$-axis for $0 \leq x \leq \dfrac 5 2$, we can write

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[Area }A] &= \int_0^{5/2} -\dfrac{x^2}{5}(2x - 5) \,\mathrm{d}x
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Evaluating the integral in part (b), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[Area }A] &= \int_0^{5/2} -\dfrac{x^2}{5}(2x - 5) \, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= -\dfrac 1 5 \int_0^{5/2} \left(2x^3 - 5x^2\right) \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= -\dfrac 1 5 \left[2\left(\dfrac 1 4 x^4\right) - 5\left(\dfrac 1 3 x^3\right)\right]_0^{5/2}\\[3pt]
    &= -\dfrac 1 5 \left[\dfrac 1 2 x^4 - \dfrac 5 3 x^3\right]_0^{5/2} \\[3pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac 1 {10} x^4 + \dfrac 1 3 x^3\right]_0^{5/2} \\[3pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac 1 {10} \left(\dfrac 5 2\right)^4 + \dfrac 1 3 \left(\dfrac 5 2\right)^3\right] - \left[-\dfrac 1 {10} (0)^4 + \dfrac 1 3 (0)^3\right] \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{125}{96} \text{ units}^2\hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
