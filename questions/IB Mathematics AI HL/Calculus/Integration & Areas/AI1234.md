# AI1234

**Maximum mark: 13**

A company produces and sells a product. The weekly revenue is modelled by the function

$R(x) = -2x^2 + 16x\,, \text{ for }\,\, 0 \leq x \leq 8$

where $x$ is the number of weeks since the product launched.

Revenue and costs are both in hundreds of dollars.

1. Sketch the graph of the function $R(x)$.

Label the vertex and any axis intercepts.[3 marks]

2. 1. Write down an integral that represents the area enclosed by the curve and the $x$-axis.

    The area enclosed by the curve and the $x$-axis represents the total revenue earned over the 8 week period.

    2. Calculate this amount, giving your answer to the nearest dollar.[3 marks]

The company incurs weekly costs modelled by the function

$C(x) = 4x + 8$

The area enclosed by the function and the $x$-axis represents the total costs earned over the 8 week period. 

3.  Calculate the total costs incurred over the 8-week period. Give your answer in dollars.[2 marks]

The company's profit, $P$, is given by $P=\text{Total Revenue} - \text{Total Costs}$.

4. Determine the company's  profit over the 8-week period.[2 marks]

The company's weekly profit, $P(x)$, is given by $P(x)=R(x)-C(x)$.

5. Determine the week in which the company's profits are maximised.[3 marks]
