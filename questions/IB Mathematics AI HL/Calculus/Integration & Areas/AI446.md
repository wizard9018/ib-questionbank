# AI446

**Maximum mark: 5**

The following diagram shows part of the graph of
$f(x) = (16-x^2)(x+3), \, x \in \mathbb{R}$. The shaded region $R$ is
bounded by the $x$-axis, $y$-axis and the graph of $f$.

![3751391507994630eeac54a189163b7a9bf8ff4b.svg](images/3751391507994630eeac54a189163b7a9bf8ff4b.svg)

1.  Write down an integral for the area of region $R$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the area of region $R$. [1 mark]

A trapezoid ABCD has four vertices with the coordinates shown as below.

![7483ada6476d727e0afb4f73aa114d5cd94fa693.svg](images/7483ada6476d727e0afb4f73aa114d5cd94fa693.svg)

3.  Find the value of $a$, the $x$-coordinate of point D, such
    that the area of the trapezoid is equal to the area of region $R$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  First, we need to find the $x$-intercept on the right hand side of
    region $R$. Letting $f(x) = 0$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (16-x^2)(x+3) &= 0 \\[3pt]
    x = -4 \text{ or } x = -3 \text{ or } x &=4 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Thus, the point where the curve first crosses
    the $x$-axis on the right edge of region $R$ is $(4,0)$. Hence $R$
    is bounded by the $x$-axis, $y$-axis and the curve for
    $0 \leq x \leq 4$ and its area represented by the integral

    $$
    \text{[Area }R] = \int_0^4 (16-x^2)(x+3) \,\mathrm{d}x
    $$

2.  Evaluating the integral in part (a), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[Area }R] &= \int_0^4 (16-x^2)(x+3) \, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &=  \int_0^4 (16x + 48 - x^3 - 3x^2) \, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &=  \int_0^4 (- x^3 - 3x^2 + 16x + 48) \, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &=  \left[-\dfrac 1 4 x^4 - 3 \left(\dfrac 1 3 x^3\right) + 16\left(\dfrac 1 2 x^2\right) +48x \right]_0^4 \\[3pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac 1 4 x^4 - x^3 + 8 x^2 +48x \right]_0^4 \\[3pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac 1 4 (4)^4 - (4)^3 + 8 (4)^2 +48(4) \right] - \left[-\dfrac 1 4 (0)^4 - (0)^3 + 8 (0)^2 +48(0) \right]\\[6pt]
    &= 192 \text{ units}^2\hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Since points B and C have the same $y$-coordinate, $12$, we can
    conclude that the height of the trapezoid is $h = 12$. In addition,
    we also get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    BC  &= x_C - x_B \\[3pt]
    &= 17 - 5 \\[3pt]
    &= 12
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    AD &= a - 0 \\[3pt]
    &= a
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the formula for the area of a trapezoid,
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[Area of ABCD]} &= \dfrac 1 2 (BC + AD)h \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac 1 2 (12 + a)(12) \\[3pt]
    &= 6(12 + a)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Since the area of the trapezoid ABCD is
    equal to the area of region $R$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    6(12 + a) &= 192 \\[3pt]
    12 + a &= 32 \\[3pt]
    &\hspace{-0.6em}a = 20
    \end{aligned}
    $$
