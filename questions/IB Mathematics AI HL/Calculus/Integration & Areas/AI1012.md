# AI1012

**Maximum mark: 7**

The graphs of $y=-0.5x+2.5$ and $y=0.5x^2+0.5x+1$ intersect at $(-3,4)$ and $(1,2)$, as shown in the following diagrams.

![AI1012a](images/AI1012a.PNG)

1. Calculate the shaded area in diagram 1, the area enclosed by the lines $y=-0.5x+2.5$, $x=-3$, $x=1$ and the $x$-axis. [2 marks]

2. 1. Write down an integral for the shaded area in diagram 2, the area enclosed by the curve $y=0.5x^2+0.5x+1$, the lines $x=-3$ and $x=1$, and the $x$-axis.

    2. Calculate the area of this shaded region. [3 marks]

3. Hence, determine the area enclosed by the line $y=-0.5x+2.5$ and the curve $y=0.5x^2+0.5x+1$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The shaded area in diagram 1 is a trapezoid. The parallel sides, $a$ and $b$, are represented by the lines $x=-3$ and $x=1$ respectively and $h$, the perpendicular distance between them, is parallel to the $x$-axis.

    ![AI1012b](images/AI1012b.PNG)

    Using the coordinates of the points $(-3,4)$ and $(1,2)$, we have $a=4$, $b=2$ and $h=4$.

    

    Let $A_1$ be the shaded area in diagram 1. Using the formula for the area of a trapezoid, we have

    

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A_1 &= \dfrac{1}{2} (4+2)\times 4 \\[11pt]

    &\hspace{-1.45em}\boxed{A_1 = 12 \text{ units}^2}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. 1. Let $A_2$ be the shaded area in diagram 2. Using the formula for the area enclosed by a curve and the $x$-axis, we have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        &\hspace{1.15em}\boxed{A_2 = \displaystyle{\int_{-3}^1} \left( 0.5x^2+0.5x+1 \right) \mathrm{d}x}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

    2. Evaluating the integral in part (b)(i), we get

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        A_2 &= \int_{-3}^1 \left( 0.5x^2+0.5x+1 \right) \mathrm{d}x \\[9pt]

        &\hspace{-1.45em}\boxed{A_2 \approx 6.67 \text{ units}^2}\hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]

        \end{aligned}

        $$

3. The area between the curves is given by the difference $A_1-A_2$:

   $$

   \begin{aligned}   

    A &= A_1 - A_2 \\[11pt]

    &\approx 12 - 6.67\\[11pt]

    &\hspace{-1em}\boxed{A \approx 5.33 \text{ units}^2}

   \end{aligned}

   $$
