# AI437

**Maximum mark: 6**

Water is pumped from a river basin into an irrigated field at a rate of
$r = \dfrac{t^2}{25}$ where $r$ is measured in cubic metres of water per
minute and $t$ is the time measured in

minutes. The graph of this is shown below.

![b36f036b2f3c610eead266e47f6c4a839afe29a8.svg](images/b36f036b2f3c610eead266e47f6c4a839afe29a8.svg)

1.  Write down an integral that represents the area of the shaded
    region. [1 mark]

2.
    1.  Find the area of the shaded region.

    2.  Interpret the meaning of the shaded area in the context of this
        question. [3 marks]

3.  Find the number of minutes it takes to pump
    $500$ m$^3$ of water into the
    field. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The area of the shaded region can be calculated by the integral
    $$
    A = \int_0^{60} \dfrac {t^2}{25}\, \mathrm{d}t
    $$

2.
    1.  Evaluating the integral in part (a), we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \int_0^{60} \dfrac {t^2}{25}\, \mathrm{d}t &=  \left[\dfrac{1}{25} \left( \dfrac{t^3}{3}\right)\right]_0^{60} \\[3pt]
        &= \left[\dfrac{t^3}{75}\right]_0^{60} \\[3pt]
        &= \dfrac{(60)^3}{75} - \dfrac{(0)^3}{75} \\[6pt]
        &= 2880 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m}^3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The area under the curve represents the total volume of water
        pumped from the river for a given number of minutes, $t$.\
        $\text{Hence, \(2880\)\hspace{0.25em}m\(^3\) of water has been pumped out of the river after \(1\) hour.}$

3.  Let $m$ be the number of minutes it takes to pump
    $500$ m$^3$ into the field. We get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_0^{m} \dfrac {t^2}{25}\, \mathrm{d}t  &= 500 \\[3pt]
    \left[\dfrac{1}{25} \left( \dfrac{t^3}{3}\right)\right]_0^{m} &= 500 \\[3pt]
    \left[\dfrac{t^3}{75}\right]_0^{m} &= 500 \\[3pt]
    \dfrac{m^3}{75} &= 500 \\[3pt]
    m^3 &= 37500 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.67em}m \approx 33.5 \hspace{0.25em}\text{min}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
