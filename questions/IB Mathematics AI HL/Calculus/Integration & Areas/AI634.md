# AI634

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following diagram shows the graph of $f(x) = \dfrac {4x}{x^2 + 1}$,
for $0 \leq x \leq 6$, and the line $x = 6$.

![2c4cfb84f6a2023641db5a3e6fb90100b872411a.svg](images/2c4cfb84f6a2023641db5a3e6fb90100b872411a.svg)

Let $R$ be the region enclosed by the graph of $f$, the $x$-axis and the
line $x = 6$.

1.  Show that $\int f(x)\, \textrm dx = 2\ln(x^2+1)+C$. [4 marks]

2.  The exact value for the area of the region $R$ is $\ln k$, where $k$
    is a positive integer. Find the value of $k$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Integrating by substitution with $u = x^2 + 1$, we get\
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{-3em}
    \int f(x)   & = \int \dfrac {4x}{x^2 + 1} \,\textrm dx\\[8pt]
    & =  \int \dfrac {2}{u} \,\textrm du\\[10pt]
    & =  2 \ln (u)+C\\[12pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = 2 \ln (x^2 + 1)+C
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The area of $R$ is given by the integral
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_R         & = \int_0^6 f(x)\,\textrm dx\\[8pt]
    & = \int_0^6 \dfrac {4x} {x^2 + 1}\,\textrm d x\\[8pt]
    & =  2 \ln (x^2 + 1)\Big|_0^{6}\\[8pt]
    & = 2 \ln(37) - 2 \ln(1)\\[10pt]
    & = \ln(37^2)\\[10pt]
    & = \ln(1369)\\[12pt]
    \therefore \hspace{1em} &k = 1369
    \end{aligned}
    $$
