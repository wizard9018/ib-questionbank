# AI456

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following diagram shows a section of a curve $y = f(x)$ and the
coordinates of six points that lie on the curve.

![c58b9a14e039e96d28ad3f6401e250d4314623e5.svg](images/c58b9a14e039e96d28ad3f6401e250d4314623e5.svg)

1.  Estimate the area of the shaded region, giving your answer to two
    decimal places. [3 marks]

The equation of the curve was found to be $y = 2x^2 - 4x + 20$.

2.  Write an integral that represents the area of the shaded
    region. [1 mark]

3.  Find the area of the shaded region, giving your answer in
    exact values. [2 marks]

4.  Find the percentage error between the estimation in
    part (a) and the exact value found in part (c). [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's partition the shaded region into trapezoids using the given
    points as follows.

    ![197d35a79f9e8bb88ee32643d3b9eb35d456f47f.svg](images/197d35a79f9e8bb88ee32643d3b9eb35d456f47f.svg)

    From the diagram, we see that the strip width is
    $h = 1.8 - 1 = 0.8$. Using the trapezoidal rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_1^5 y \, \mathrm{d}x &\approx \dfrac{1}{2}h \left( (y_0 + y_n) + 2(y_1 + y_2 + \dots + y_{n-1} ) \right) \\[3pt]
    &\approx \dfrac{1}{2}(0.8) \left[ (18 + 50) +  2 \left( 19.28 + 23.12 + 29.52 + 38.48\right)  \right] \\[6pt]
    &\approx 115.52 \text{ units}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The area of the shaded region is determined by

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     A = \int_1^5 (2x^2 - 4x + 20) \, \mathrm{d}x
     \end{aligned}
     $$

3.  Evaluating the integral in part (b), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \int_1^5 (2x^2 - 4x + 20)\, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= \left[2 \left( \dfrac 1 3 x^3\right) -4 \left(\dfrac 1 2 x^2 \right) + 20x \right]_1^5 \\[3pt]
    &= \left[\dfrac 2 3 x^3 - 2x^2 + 20x \right]_1^5 \\[3pt]
    &= \left[\dfrac 2 3 (5)^3 - 2(5)^2 + 20(5) \right] - \left[\dfrac 2 3 (1)^3 - 2(1)^2 + 20(1) \right] \\[6pt]
    & = \dfrac{344}{3} \text{ units}^2 \hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The percentage error between the estimation in part (a) and the
    exact value in part (c) can be found using the percentage error
    formula
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[\% error]} &= \left|\dfrac{V_A-V_E}{V_E}\right| \times 100\% \\[3pt]
    &= \left|\dfrac{ 115.52- \dfrac{344}{3}}{\dfrac{344}{3}}\right| \times 100\% \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.744 \%
    \end{aligned}
    $$
