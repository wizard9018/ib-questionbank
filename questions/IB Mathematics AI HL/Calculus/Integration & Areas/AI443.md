# AI443

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows the parabolic shape of a gateway arch that
has a span of $12$ metres and a maximum height of $8$ metres.

![c7526c95183b578105a5079f11677bc8cf86a397.svg](images/c7526c95183b578105a5079f11677bc8cf86a397.svg)

The curve has an equation in the form $y = k(x - 6)^2 + 8$.

1.  Determine the value of $k$. [2 marks]

2.  Write down an integral that represents the cross sectional area
    under the arch shown as OMN.  [2 marks]

3.  Find the cross sectional area under the arch. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since the curve passes through the origin O$(0,0)$, these
    coordinates must satisfy the equation of the curve. Hence,

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0 &= k(0 - 6)^2 + 8 \\[3pt]
    0 &= 36k + 8 \\[3pt]
    &\hspace{-0.6em}k = -\dfrac{2}{9}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  From part (a), we obtain the complete equation of the curve as
    below:
    $$
    y = -\dfrac{2}{9}(x-6)^2 + 8

    $$
    Hence, the cross sectional
    area under the arch is determined by

    $$
    A = \int_0^{12} \left[-\dfrac{2}{9}(x-6)^2 + 8\right] \mathrm{d}x
    $$

3.  Evaluating the integral in part (b), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \int_0^{12} \left[-\dfrac{2}{9}(x-6)^2 + 8\right] \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= \int_0^{12} \left[-\dfrac{2}{9}(x^2 - 12x + 36) + 8\right] \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= \int_0^{12} \left(-\dfrac{2}{9}x^2 + \dfrac{8}{3}x - 8 + 8\right) \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= \int_0^{12} \left(-\dfrac{2}{9}x^2 + \dfrac{8}{3}x\right) \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac{2}{9} \left(\dfrac 1 3 x^3 \right) +  \dfrac{8}{3}\left(\dfrac 1 2 x^2 \right)  \right]_0^{12} \\[3pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac{2}{27} x^3 +  \dfrac{4}{3}x^2 \right]_0^{12} \\[3pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac{2}{27} (12)^3 +  \dfrac{4}{3}(12)^2 \right] -  \left[-\dfrac{2}{27} (0)^3 +  \dfrac{4}{3}(0)^2 \right]\\[6pt]
    &= 64\hspace{0.2em} \text{m}^2 \hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
