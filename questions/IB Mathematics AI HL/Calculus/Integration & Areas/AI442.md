# AI442

**Maximum mark: 7**

A section of the curve with equation $y = 2(x - 3)(x+8)$ is shown.

![7606bb82ea7867dff3e9e9998d9b320edfa7a429.svg](images/7606bb82ea7867dff3e9e9998d9b320edfa7a429.svg)

1.  Write an integral for the shaded area A. [2 marks]

2.  Find the area of A. [2 marks]

3.  Find the area of B. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The diagram shows that the shaded area A is bounded by the curve and
    the $x$-axis from $3$ to $6$. Hence,

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[Area A]} = \int_3^6 2(x - 3)(x+8) \mathrm{d}x
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Evaluating the integral in part (a), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[Area A]} &= \int_3^6 2(x - 3)(x+8)  \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= \int_3^6 2(x^2 + 8x - 3x - 24)  \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= \int_3^6 2(x^2 + 5x - 24)  \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= 2\left[\dfrac 1 3 x^3 + 5\left(\dfrac 1 2 x^2\right) - 24x\right]_3^6 \\[3pt]
    &= 2\left[\dfrac 1 3 x^3 + \dfrac 5 2 x^2 - 24x\right]_3^6 \\[3pt]
    &= 2\left[\left(\dfrac 1 3 (6)^3 + \dfrac 5 2 (6)^2 - 24(6)\right) - \left(\dfrac 1 3 (3)^3 + \dfrac 5 2 (3)^2 - 24(3)\right)\right] \\[6pt]
    &= 117 \text{ units}^2\hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Area A and area B add up to the area of the rectangle as shown in
    the diagram. To determine the rectangle's height, we substitute
    $x = 6$ into the equation of the curve:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 2(6 - 3)(6 + 8) \\[3pt]
    &= 84
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    So the dimensions of the rectangle are $6$ by
    $84$. Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[Area B]} &= \text{[Area of the rectangle]} - \text{[Area A]} \\[3pt]
    &= (6 \times 84) - 117\\[6pt]
    &= 387 \text{ units}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
