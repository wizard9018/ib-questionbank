# AI448

**Maximum mark: 6**

The equation of a curve is $y = -x^3 + 4x^2 + x - 4$. A section of the
curve is shown on the diagram below, with the three $x$-intercepts
labelled.

![8d404fa2ccfe40545665dda05f6b194b571fddc7.svg](images/8d404fa2ccfe40545665dda05f6b194b571fddc7.svg)

1.  Find $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}$. [2 marks]

2.  Write down the coordinates of the local maximum. [1 mark]

3.  Write down an integral representing the area of the shaded region.
    [1 mark]

4.  Find the area of the shaded region. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Differentiating $y$ with respect to $x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\left( -x^3 + 4x^2 + x - 4 \right) \\[3pt]
    &= -3x^2 + 4(2x) + 1 \\[6pt]
    &= -3x^2 + 8x + 1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Setting $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = 0$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -3x^2 + 8x + 1 &= 0 \\[3pt]
    x \approx -0.12 \text{ or } x &\approx 2.786 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    These two $x$-values are the $x$-coordinates of
    the two turning points on the diagram. Using the given diagram, we
    can see that the local maximum is located between $x = 1$ and
    $x = 4$. So we take $x = 2.786$.

    ![bb2411b5a1cf350f5d6dda24089a6cc1a59471aa.svg](images/bb2411b5a1cf350f5d6dda24089a6cc1a59471aa.svg)

    Substituting $x = 2.786$ into $y$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= -(2.786)^3 + 4(2.786)^2 + 2.786 - 4 \\[3pt]
    &\approx 8.21
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the local maximum is located at
     $\text{(2.79, 8.21)}$

3.  Using the diagram, we can see that the shaded region is bounded
    between $x = 1$ and $x = 4$. Hence, its area is determined by

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     A = \int_1^4 (-x^3 + 4x^2 + x - 4) \, \mathrm{d}x
     \end{aligned}
     $$

4.  Evaluating the integral in part (c), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \int_1^4 (-x^3 + 4x^2 + x - 4) \, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac 1 4 x^4 + 4 \left( \dfrac 1 3 x^3 \right) + \dfrac 1 2 x^2 - 4x\right]_1^4 \\[3pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac 1 4 x^4 +  \dfrac 4 3 x^3 + \dfrac 1 2 x^2 - 4x\right]_1^4 \\[3pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac 1 4 (4)^4 +  \dfrac 4 3 (4)^3 + \dfrac 1 2 (4)^2 - 4(4)\right] - \left[-\dfrac 1 4 (1)^4 +  \dfrac 4 3 (1)^3 + \dfrac 1 2 (1)^2 - 4(1)\right] \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{63}{4} \text{ units}^2 \hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
