# AI624

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $f(x) = 2 x e^x$ and $g(x) = - 2 f(x) -1$.

The graphs of $f$ and $g$ intersect at $x = a$ and $x = b$, where
$a< b$.

1.  Find the value of $a$ and of $b$. [3 marks]

2.  Hence, find the area of the region enclosed by the graphs of $f$ and
    $g$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's find $g$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    g(x)    & = -2 f(x) - 1\hspace{1cm}\\[10pt]
    & = -2 (2 x e^x) - 1\\[10pt]
    & = -4 x e^x - 1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Next, we find where $f$ and $g$ intersect
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3cm}
    2 x e ^ x   & = -4 x e^x - 1\\[6pt]
    x       & = -2.83,\,-0.204\qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence,   $a = -2.83\ \mbox{and } b = -0.204$

2.  The area is given by the integral
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.4cm}
    A       & = \int_a^b (g(x) - f(x))\,\textrm d x\\[10pt]
    & = \int_{-2.83}^{-0.204} (-4x e^x - 1 - 2 x e ^ x)\,\textrm d x\\[12pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}= 1.91\qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
