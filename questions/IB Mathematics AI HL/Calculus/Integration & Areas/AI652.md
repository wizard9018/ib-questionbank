# AI652

**Maximum mark: 6**

A wood sculptor is going to replicate a ship whose hull has the shape of
a prism. The sculptor notices that the cross-sectional area of the hull
can be modelled by the region enclosed by the functions

$$
f(x)=0.05x^2+10 \quad \text{and} \quad g(x)=0.04x^2+11
$$
for the
domain $-10\leq x \leq 10$ where $x$ is given in centimetres.

1.  Sketch the functions $y=f(x)$ and $y=g(x)$ on the same set of axes
    and shade in the area that represents the cross-sectional area of
    the hull.[2 marks]

2.  Calculate, to $2$ decimal places, the area of the shaded region that
    represents the cross-sectional area of the hull.[2 marks]

3.  The replica ship is $60$ cm long. Find the
    volume of the original ship hull given that the replica is 1:20 in
    scale.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using G.D.C., we get

    ![924d2fcee2e52f3ff8203951642d670f7585466e.svg](images/924d2fcee2e52f3ff8203951642d670f7585466e.svg)

2.  Using the formula for the area bounded by two curves, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Area} &= \int_{-10}^{10} \big[g(x)-f(x)\big] \, \mathrm{d}x \rule[-7.5mm]{0pt}{7.5mm}\\
    &= \int_{-10}^{10} \big[(0.04x^2+11)-(0.05x^2+10)\big] \, \mathrm{d}x \rule[-7.5mm]{0pt}{7.5mm}\\
    &= \int_{-10}^{10} \big[ -0.01x^2+1 \big] \rule[-7.5mm]{0pt}{7.5mm}\\
    &\hspace{0.3em}\approx 13.33\hspace{0.15em}\text{cm}^2\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{7.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  As the cross-sectional area is constant, the volume of the scaled
    hull, $V_s$, is

    $$
    \text{V}_s = \text{Area}\times 60 = (13.33\hspace{0.15em}\text{cm}^2)\times (60\hspace{0.15em}\text{cm}) \approx 800\hspace{0.15em}\text{cm}^3
    $$
    Now, converting cm$^3$ to m$^3$ and using the scale factor, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{V} &= 800\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}^3 \left(\dfrac{1\hspace{0.15em}\text{m}}{100\hspace{0.15em}\text{cm}}\right)^3 \underbrace{\left(\dfrac{20}{1}\right)^3}_{\text{scale factor}} \rule[-2.5mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\
    &\hspace{0.3em}= 6.4\hspace{0.15em} \text{m}^3\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
