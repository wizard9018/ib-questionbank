# AI1079

**Maximum mark: 8**

A wave power generator moves up and down in the sea and generates electricity.

The surface of the sea is at $0\,$m and the generator is moving below the surface.

It is known the depth $D$, in metres in relation to sea level, is described by the following equation

$\dfrac{\mathrm{d}D}{\mathrm{d}t}=\dfrac{324}{t^3}-4t$,  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;   $0 \leq t \leq 10$

Where $t$ is measured in hours.
1. Determine after how many hours the depth of the generator is closest to the surface of the sea. [2 marks]

It is known that after $1.5$ hours the generator is at $-80.5$ m in relation to the surface of the sea.

2. Show that the closest the generator gets to the surface of the sea is $40\,$m. [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The generator is closest to the surface of the sea when its depth is at a maximum value. That is, it is _least_ negative.

    Therefore
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{\mathrm{d}D}{\mathrm{d}t}&=0\\
        0&=\dfrac{324}{t^3}-4t\\[6pt]
        4t&=\dfrac{324}{t^3}\\[6pt]
        t^4&=81\\
        t&=\pm 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The value of $t$ must be in $0 \leq t \leq 10$, therefore $t=$ $\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{3\, \text{hours}}\hspace{0.525em}$
2. To find $D(t)$ we must integrate the RHS, because $D(t)=\displaystyle{\int}\dfrac{\mathrm{d}D}{\mathrm{d}t}\mathrm{d}t$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        D(t)&=\int \left(\dfrac{324}{t^3}-4t\right) \mathrm{d}t\\[10pt]
        &=\int \left(324t^{-3}-4t\right)\mathrm{d}t\\[10pt]
        &=\dfrac{324t^{-2}}{-2} - \dfrac{4t^2}{2} + C\\[10pt]
        &= -\dfrac{162}{t^2}-2t^2+C
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Where $C$ is the constant of integration.

    To find $C$ use the information given. $D(1.5)=-80.5$. Therefore
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        D(1.5)&=-\dfrac{162}{(1.5)^2}-2(1.5)^2+C\\[6pt]
        -80.5&=-\dfrac{162}{(1.5)^2}-2(1.5)^2+C\\[6pt]
        C &= -4  &[\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Here is the complete function $D(t)=-\dfrac{162}{t^2}-2t^2-4$.

    From part a) at time $t=3$ hours the generator is closest to the surface. Therefore
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        D(3)&=-\dfrac{162}{(3)^2}-2(3)^2-4\\[6pt]
        &=-40
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The closest the generator gets to the surface is $40\, \text{m}$ as required.
