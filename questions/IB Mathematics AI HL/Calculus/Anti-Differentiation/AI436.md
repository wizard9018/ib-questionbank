# AI436

**Maximum mark: 6**

The water in a pool is being pumped out into a nearby lake at a rate of
$r$ litres per second where $r = \dfrac{2t}{275}$ and $t$ is time
measured in seconds. The graph of this is shown below.

![f2ed86d6fe210f5c31ef202ce41d1d2686ec1c4e.svg](images/f2ed86d6fe210f5c31ef202ce41d1d2686ec1c4e.svg)

1.  Find out how much water has been pumped out after $1$ minute.
    [3 marks]

2.  Determine an expression for the volume of water that has been pumped
    out of the pool for any given number of seconds,
    $s$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The volume of water that has been pumped out of the pool is equal to
    the area under the curve for a given number of seconds, as
    illustrated below (note, $1$ minute = $60$
    seconds)

    ![e4e9415b1af906a30875c8114211521de519210e.svg](images/e4e9415b1af906a30875c8114211521de519210e.svg)

    Hence, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \int_0^{60} \dfrac {2t}{275}\, \mathrm{d}t \\[6pt]
    &= \left[ \dfrac{2}{275} \left( \dfrac 1 2 t^2\right)\right]_0^{60} \\[6pt]
    &= \left[ \dfrac 1 {275}  t^2 \right]_0^{60} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac 1 {275} \left(  60^2 - 0^2 \right) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 13.1 \text{ L}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The volume of water that has been pumped out of the pool after $s$
    seconds can be expressed as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \int_0^{s} \dfrac {2t}{275}\, \mathrm{d}t \\[6pt]
    &= \left[ \dfrac{2}{275} \left( \dfrac 1 2 t^2\right)\right]_0^{s} \\[6pt]
    &= \left[ \dfrac 1 {275}  t^2 \right]_0^{s} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac 1 {275} \left(  s^2 - 0^2 \right) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac 1 {275} s^2 \text{ (L)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
