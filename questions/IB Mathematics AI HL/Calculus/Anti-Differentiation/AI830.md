# AI830

**Maximum mark: 8**

In a memory competition, the rate at which English speaking participants
memorise French vocabulary can be modelled by the equation

$$
\dfrac{dM}{dt}=1-0.01t,\quad t\geq 0,
$$
where $M(t)$ is the number of
words memorised in $t$ minutes.

The number of words participants have memorised at the beginning of the
competition, $t=0$, is zero.

1.  Find the equation for the number of memorised words at time $t$.
    [4 marks]

  The competition ends when
students get so tired that the number of additional memorised words per
minute becomes zero.

2.  Find the value of $t$ when the competition will end.
    [2 marks]

3.  Determine
    1.  the domain of $M(t)$;

    2.  the range of $M(t)$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Integrating $\dfrac{dM}{dt}$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.6cm}   M(t)&= \int (1-0.01t) \, \mathrm{d}t \\[3pt]
    &= 1(t)-0.01\left(\dfrac 1 2 t^2 \right) + C \\[3pt]
    &= t -0.005t^2 + C \hspace{3em} (\text{where $C$ is a constant})
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Since $M(0)=0$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0&= M(0) \\[3pt]
    0 &= 0-0.005(0)^2 + C\\[3pt]
    C &= 0
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, the equation for $M(t)$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    & M(t) = t -0.005t^2 + 0 \\[6pt]
    & M(t) =t -0.005t^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  To find when the competition will end, we let the derivative
    $\dfrac{dM}{dt}$ be equal to zero and then solve for $t$. Solving
    the equation $\dfrac{dM}{dt} = 0$ for $t > 0$, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.2cm}\dfrac{dM}{dt}&=0\\[6pt]
    1-0.01t&= 0  \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{-0.5em} t = 100 \text{ minutes} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  From part (b), the competition will end after $100 \text{ minutes}.$
    If we sketch the graph of $y = M(t)$, for $0\leq t\leq 100$, we get

    ![86ab1d7777692c66882ad70876f81d758205cab5.svg](images/86ab1d7777692c66882ad70876f81d758205cab5.svg)

    Using the graph we can see that the maximum number of memorised
    words is 50 and it is reached after 100 minutes.

    1.  Hence the domain of $M(t)$ is
         $[0, 100]$

    2.  Hence the range of $M(t)$ is
         $[0,50]$
