# AI925

**Maximum mark: 8**

Kai launches stones from a slingshot into the air. Each time he launches
a stone, he records the angle, $\theta$, in degrees, at which the stone
is launched, and the horizontal distance, $L$, in metres, which the
stone travels from his shooting position to where it first lands. The
diagram below illustrates how the stones travel in the air when launched
at different angles.

![2dfa3cfcc7c3b779931c7b63f92855c7468db96f.svg](images/2dfa3cfcc7c3b779931c7b63f92855c7468db96f.svg)

Kai analyses his results and concludes:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}L}{\mathrm{d}\theta} = -0.08\theta+3.3, \hspace{1.0em} \ang{0} \leq \theta \leq \ang{90}.
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Determine whether the graph of $L$ versus $\theta$ is increasing or
    decreasing
    at $\theta = \ang{50}$. [3 marks]

Kai observes that when the angle is $\ang{30}$, the sling stone will
travel a horizontal distance of
$90$ m.

2.  Find an expression for $L$ in terms of $\theta$.
    [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We know the gradient of the graph of $L$ versus $\theta$ is given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7.4em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}L}{\mathrm{d}\theta} = -0.08\theta+3.3 \hspace{3em} [\ang{0} \leq \theta \leq \ang{90}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting $\theta = \ang{50}$ into equation (1), we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10.2em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}L}{\mathrm{d}\theta} &= -0.08(50) + 3.3 \\[9pt]
    &= -0.7 \hspace{3em} [\text{$\Rightarrow$ the gradient is negative}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Therefore, the graph of $L$ versus $\theta$ is
     $\text{decreasing}$
    at $\theta = \ang{50}$.

2.  If we integrate both sides of equation $(1)$ with respect to
    $\theta$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{-1.3em} \int \hspace{-0.05em}\bigg[\dfrac{\mathrm{d}L}{\mathrm{d}\theta}\bigg] \hspace{0.025em} \mathrm{d}\theta &= \int \hspace{-0.05em}\big[\hspace{-0.15em}-0.08\hspace{0.025em}\theta+3.3\big]\hspace{0.075em} \mathrm{d}\theta \\[9pt]
    L &= -0.04\hspace{0.025em}\theta^2 + 3.3\hspace{0.025em}\theta + C
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    for some $C \in \mathbb{R}$.

    We know $L = 90$ m when $\theta = \ang{30}$.

    Hence, substituting $L = 90$ m and
    $\theta = \ang{30}$ into equation $(2)$, we deduce

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{4.6em} 90 &= -0.04(30)^2 + 3.3(30) + C \\[9pt]
     90 &= 63 + C \\[9pt]
     C &= 27 \\
     \end{aligned}
     $$
     Therefore we find
     $L = -0.04\hspace{0.025em}\theta^2 + 3.3\hspace{0.025em}\theta + 27$
