# AI927

**Maximum mark: 7**

Bellatrix Aviation manufactures business jets. The profit the company
makes, $P$, in millions of dollars,
changes based on the number of jets, $x$, they
manufacture per quarter.

The rate of change of the profit made from manufacturing $x$ jets is
modelled by
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}P}{\mathrm{d}x} &= -0.4x + 16, \hspace{1.0em} x \geq 0.
\end{aligned}
$$

In the previous quarter, the company made a profit of $165$ million
dollars from manufacturing $20$ jets.

1.  Find an expression for $P$ in terms of $x$. [5 marks]

The company has the capacity to increase the number of jets they
manufacture.

2.  Describe how the profit changes if the company increases
    manufacturing to over $40$ jets
    per quarter and up to $75$ jets per quarter. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}P}{\mathrm{d}x} &= -0.4x + 16 \hspace{3.5em} [x \geq 0]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    If we integrate both sides of equation $(1)$ with respect to $x$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{-1.3em} \int \hspace{-0.05em}\bigg[\dfrac{\mathrm{d}P}{\mathrm{d}x}\bigg] \hspace{0.025em} \mathrm{d}x &= \int \hspace{-0.05em}\big[\hspace{-0.15em}-0.4x+16\big]\hspace{0.075em} \mathrm{d}x \\[9pt]
    P &= -0.2x^2 + 16x + C
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    for some
    $C \in \mathbb{R}$.

    We know $P = 165$ million dollars when $x = 20$ jets.

    Hence, substituting $P = 165$ and $x = 20$ into equation $(2)$, we
    deduce
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.05em} 165 &= -0.2(20)^2 + 16(20) + C \\[6pt]
    165 &= 240 + C \\[6pt]
    C &= -75 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore we obtain
     $P = -0.2x^2 + 16x - 75$
