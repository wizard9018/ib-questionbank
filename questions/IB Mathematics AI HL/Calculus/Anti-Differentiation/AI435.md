# AI435

**Maximum mark: 6**

Rain is filling an empty water reservoir at a rate of $r$ litres per
minute, where $r = \dfrac{t}{5}$ and $t$ is the time measured in
minutes. The graph of this is shown below.

![8c03e733910059f9ace3de1e60425da7178d5fd4.svg](images/8c03e733910059f9ace3de1e60425da7178d5fd4.svg)

1.  Find how much water has filled the reservoir after 1 hour.
    [3 marks]

2.  Determine an expression for the amount of water that has filled the
    reservoir for any given number of minutes, $m$.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The volume of water that has filled the reservoir is equal to the
    area under the curve for a given number of minutes, as illustrated
    below (note, $1$ hour = $60$ minutes)

    ![fe85991ee945b4bebc431fdbf2c9f2b9ae5681d9.svg](images/fe85991ee945b4bebc431fdbf2c9f2b9ae5681d9.svg)

    Hence, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \int_0^{60} \dfrac t 5\, \mathrm{d}t \\[6pt]
    &= \left[ \dfrac 1 5 \left( \dfrac 1 2 t^2\right)\right]_0^{60} \\[6pt]
    &= \left[ \dfrac 1 {10}  t^2 \right]_0^{60} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac 1 {10} \left(  60^2 - 0^2 \right) \\[6pt]
    &= 360 \text{ L}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The volume of water that has filled the reservoir after $m$ minutes
    can be expressed as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \int_0^{m} \dfrac t 5\, \mathrm{d}t \\[6pt]
    &= \left[ \dfrac 1 5 \left( \dfrac 1 2 t^2\right)\right]_0^{m} \\[6pt]
    &= \left[ \dfrac 1 {10}  t^2 \right]_0^{m} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac 1 {10} \left(  m^2 - 0^2 \right) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac 1 {10} m^2 \text{ (L)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
