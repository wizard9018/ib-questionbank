# AI1171

**Maximum mark: 6**

Hanna has created a simple design for her bedroom wall using the equation $y=0.1x\,(x-5)^2$. Hanna's bedroom wall is $5$ metres wide and $3$ metres high. She plans to paint the area below the curve gray, and the area above the curve white.

This is shown in the following diagram.

![ai1171a](images/ai1171a.JPG)

Hanna measures the height of the curve $(y)$ at several points along the width of the bedroom $(x)$. Hanna's measurements are shown in the following table.

![ai1171b](images/ai1171b.JPG)

1. Use the trapezoidal rule with $5$ intervals to estimate the area that Hanna will paint $\text{\bf{gray}}$. [3 marks]

2. 1. Write down an equation that will enable Hanna to calculate the exact area that is to be painted $\text{\bf{white}}$.

    2. Hence determine the exact area that is to be painted $\text{\bf{white}}$. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using the trapezoidal rule with five intervals, we have $a = 0$, $b = 5$, $n = 5$ and therefore

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

     h &= \dfrac{5-0}{5} \\[7pt]

     h &= 1

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Our $y$ values are $y_0=0$, $y_1=1.6$, $y_2=1.8$, $y_3=1.2$, $y_4=0.4$ and $y_5=0$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore, using the trapezoidal rule, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \displaystyle{\int}_a^b\,y\,\mathrm{d}x &\approx \frac{1}{2}h\left[\left(y_0+y_5\right)+2\left(y_1+y_2+y_3+y_4\right)\right] \\[10pt]

    \displaystyle{\int}_0^5\,y\,\mathrm{d}x &\approx \frac{1}{2}(1)\left[\left(0+0\right)+2\left(1.6+1.8+1.2+0.4\right)\right] \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[10pt]

    &= 5

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Using the trapzoidal rule with five intervals, the approximate area that is to be painted gray is $\hspace{0em}\boxed{5\,\mathrm{m}^2}$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. 1. The area that is to be painted white $(A_W)$ is the difference between the total area of the wall $(A_T)$ and the area that is to be painted gray $(A_G)$.

        The wall is a rectangle, therefore $A_T = 3\times 5$.

        The area to be painted gray is the area underneath the curve, therefore $A_G=\displaystyle{\int_0^5}\,0.1x(x-5)^2\,\mathrm{d}x$. Hence we have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        A_W &= A_T - A_G \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[7pt]

        &\hspace{-2em}\boxed{A_W = 15 - \displaystyle{\int_0^5}\,0.1x(x-5)^2\,\mathrm{d}x} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$
