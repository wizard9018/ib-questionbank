# AI455

**Maximum mark: 11**

Consider the region $R$ enclosed by the curve $y = -3(x-2)(x-8)$ and the
$x$-axis.

1.  Sketch the curve and shade the region $R$ on the following
    axes. [3 marks]

    ![50da159a8b98222746ff9038d302b09b67a7f4a8.svg](images/50da159a8b98222746ff9038d302b09b67a7f4a8.svg)

2.
    1.  Write down an integral that represents the area of region $R$.

    2.  Find the area of region $R$. [3 marks]

3.  Estimate the area of region $R$ using five trapezoids. [4 marks]

4.  Find the percentage error between the exact value found in part
    (b)(ii) and the estimation in part (c). [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.

    ![e3c6d8a748abf0c6c7d21295e7b381c95432b372.svg](images/e3c6d8a748abf0c6c7d21295e7b381c95432b372.svg)

2.
    1.  The area of region $R$ is determined by

         $$
         \begin{aligned}
         A = \int_2^8 -3(x-2)(x-8) \, \mathrm{d}x
         \end{aligned}
         $$

    2.  Evaluating the integral in part (b)(i), we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        A &= \int_2^8 -3(x-2)(x-8)\, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
        &= -3\int_2^8 (x^2 - 8x - 2x + 16)\,\, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
        &= -3\int_2^8 (x^2 - 10x + 16) \,\mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
        &= -3\left[\dfrac 1 3 x^3 - 10 \left( \dfrac 1 2 x^2 \right) +16x \right]_2^8 \\[3pt]
        &= -3\left[\dfrac 1 3 x^3 - 5 x^2 +16x \right]_2^8 \\[3pt]
        &= -3\left[\left(\dfrac 1 3 (8)^3 - 5 (8)^2 +16(8)\right) - \left(\dfrac 1 3 (2)^3 - 5 (2)^2 +16(2)\right) \right] \\[6pt]
        & = 108 \text{ units}^2 \hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  Since five trapezoids are used to estimate the area of region $R$,
    the strip width is $h = \dfrac{8-2}{5} = 1.2$. So we obtain the
    $x$-values of the subintervals of the trapezoids and their
    corresponding $y$-values as follows.

    ![8e2a7816c364a81a5e7109e69b3b7c86326bf242.svg](images/8e2a7816c364a81a5e7109e69b3b7c86326bf242.svg)

    These trapezoids can be illustrated as follows.

    ![a16085cd00689b6892f954a083c409a583ac4506.svg](images/a16085cd00689b6892f954a083c409a583ac4506.svg)

    Using the trapezoidal rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_2^8 y \, \mathrm{d}x &\approx \dfrac{1}{2}h \left( (y_0 + y_n) + 2(y_1 + y_2 + \dots + y_{n-1} ) \right) \\[3pt]
    &\approx \dfrac{1}{2}(1.2) \left[ (0 + 0) + 2(17.28 + 25.92 + 25.92 + 17.28)  \right] \\[6pt]
    &\approx 104 \text{ units}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The percentage error between the estimation in part (c) and the
    exact value in part (b)(ii) can be found using the percentage error
    formula
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[\% error]} &= \left|\dfrac{V_A-V_E}{V_E}\right| \times 100\% \\[3pt]
    &= \left|\dfrac{104 - 108}{108}\right| \times 100\% \\[6pt]
    &= 3.7 \%
    \end{aligned}
    $$
