# AI774

**Maximum mark: 5**

A town's water distribution system supplies water from a lake.  The operating cost is $$\$3.25$$ per cubic metre of water. The following table shows an estimate of the areas of horizontal cross-sections at $50$-metre intervals, of the water in the lake, where the depth is measured in metres below the ground level.

![intlake](images/intlake.JPG)

1. Use the trapezoidal rule to find an estimate of the volume of water in the lake. [3 marks]

2. Hence or otherwise, estimate the operating cost of extracting all the water.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Let $x$ be the depth below the ground level. Thus, the volume of water is the integral from 0 to 300 of the area of the horizontal cross-sections multiplied by the differential $\mathrm{d}x$.

    According to the trapezoidal rule, $h=\frac{b-a}{n}$. In this case we have a minimum depth of 0 metres, a maximum depth of 300 metres, and 6 intervals. Therefore

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h &= \frac{300-0}{6} \\[6pt]
    &= 50
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the trapezoidal rule we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \displaystyle \int _0^{300} A(x)\, \mathrm{d}x \rule[-5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &\approx \dfrac{h}{2}\big(623+494+2(592+560+568+537+519)\big)\\
    & \approx \dfrac{50}{2}\big(623+494+2(592+560+568+537+519)\big) \rule[-5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\tag*{\textbf{(A1)(A1)}}\\
    & \approx 166\,725 \text{ m}^3\rule[-5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    & \hspace{0em}\boxed{= 167\,000 \text{ m}^3 \text{ (3 s.f.)}} \rule[-2mm]{0pt}{7mm}\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. The operating cost of extracting all the water is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (166\,725)(3.25) &=\enskip \$\,541\hspace{0.1em}856.25 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[12pt]
    &\hspace{0em} \boxed{ = \$\hspace{0.1em}542\,000 \text{ (3 s.f.)}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
