# AI1003

**Maximum mark: 5**

The following table shows the rate, $R$, at which rubbish is produced by a town, in tonnes per day. $t$ represents the time in days since the rate of rubbish production was first recorded.

![d0773d1458207e9a25a6519f86415b84187f0fcf.svg](images/d0773d1458207e9a25a6519f86415b84187f0fcf.svg)

The total amount of rubbish produced by the town over the 3-day period is equal to the area underneath the graph of the curve $R(t)$.

1.  Use the trapezoidal rule with 6 intervals to estimate the total amount of rubbish produced by the town over the 3-day period.[3 marks]

The actual amount of rubbish produced over the 3 days was $191\,$tonnes.

2.  Find the percentage error between the estimate in part (a) and the actual amount of rubbish produced. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  An estimate of the area under the curve over the interval $0 \leq t \leq 3$ is illustrated by the shaded region in the diagram below.

    ![AI1003a](images/AI1003a.PNG)

    We can approximate this area using the trapezoidal rule:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_ a^b R(t) \, \mathrm{d}t &\approx \dfrac{1}{2}(h) \left[ (R_0 + R_6) + 2(R_1 + R_2 + R_3 + R_4 + R_5) \right]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Where $a=0$, $b=3$ and $h=\dfrac{3-0}{6}=0.5$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_ 0^3 R(t) \, \mathrm{d}x &\approx \dfrac{1}{2}(0.5) \left[ (51 + 61) + 2(60 + 57 + 63 + 46 + 72) \right] \\[12pt]
    &\approx 177
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The total amount of rubbish produced is approximately $177 \text{ tonnes}$

2.  The percentage error between the estimate in part (a) and the exact value can be found using the percentage error formula
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \epsilon &= \left|\dfrac{V_A-V_E}{V_E}\right| \times 100\% \\[12pt]
    &= \left|\dfrac{177-191}{191}\right| \times 100\% \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-0.4em}\boxed{\epsilon \approx 7.33 \%}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
