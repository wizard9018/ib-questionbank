# AI453

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following table shows the $x$ and $y$ coordinates of five points
that lie on a curve $y = f(x)$.

![13e7f4b049e5d3bdaa2312a42db35bbad0a20808.svg](images/13e7f4b049e5d3bdaa2312a42db35bbad0a20808.svg)

1.  Estimate the area under the curve over the interval $1 < x < 6$.
    [2 marks]

The equation of the curve was found to be
$y = -\dfrac{1}{10}(4x+5)(x-8)$.

2.  Find the exact value of the area under the curve over the
    interval $1 < x < 6$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the percentage error between the estimation in
    part (a) and the exact value in part (b). Provide a reason for the
    difference. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  An estimation of the area under the curve over the interval
    $1 < x < 6$ is illustrated by the shaded region in the diagram
    below.

    ![573e12933523f36b69178f909ad6217c2cd2a4ee.svg](images/573e12933523f36b69178f909ad6217c2cd2a4ee.svg)

    We can approximate this area using the trapezoidal rule

    $$
    \int_a^b y \, \mathrm{d}x \approx \dfrac{1}{2}h \left( (y_0 + y_n) + 2(y_1 + y_2 + \dots + y_{n-1} ) \right)
    $$
    In this case, $h = 2.25 - 1 = 1.25$ and $f(x_i) = y_i$ as given in
    the second row of the table. Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_1^6 y \, \mathrm{d}x &\approx \dfrac{1}{2}(1.25) \left[ (6.3 + 5.8)+ 2(8.05 + 8.55 + 7.8)\right] \\[6pt]
    &\approx 38.1 \text{ units}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The exact value of the area under the curve over the interval
    $0 < x < 2$ is determined by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= \int_1^6 -\dfrac{1}{10}(4x+5)(x-8) \, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= -\dfrac{1}{10} \int_1^6 (4x^2 - 32x + 5x - 40) \, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= -\dfrac{1}{10} \int_1^6 (4x^2 -27x - 40) \, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= -\dfrac{1}{10} \left[4\left( \dfrac 1 3 x^3\right) -27 \left(\dfrac 1 2 x^2\right) - 40x)\right]_1^6 \\[3pt]
    &= -\dfrac{1}{10} \left[\dfrac 4 3 x^3 -\dfrac {27} 2 x^2 - 40x\right]_1^6\\[3pt]
    &= -\dfrac{1}{10} \left[\left(\dfrac 4 3 (6)^3 -\dfrac {27} 2 (6)^2 - 40(6)\right) - \left(\dfrac 4 3 (1)^3 -\dfrac {27} 2 (1)^2 - 40(1)\right) \right]\\[6pt]
    &=  \dfrac{463}{12} \text{ units}^2 \hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The percentage error between the estimation in part (a) and the
    exact value in part (b) can be found using the percentage error
    formula
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[\% error]} &= \left|\dfrac{V_A-V_E}{V_E}\right| \times 100\% \\[3pt]
    &= \left|\dfrac{38.1 - \dfrac{463}{12}}{\dfrac{463}{12}}\right| \times 100\% \\[6pt]
    &\approx 1.25 \%
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The difference between the estimation and the exact value occurs as we use straight line segments (tops of the trapezoids) to approximate the curve. There are error regions between the straight lines and the curve.
