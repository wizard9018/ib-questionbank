# AI645

**Maximum mark: 7**

Consider the region enclosed by the graph of the function
$f(x)=e^{x/2}$, the $x$-axis and the vertical lines $x=0$ and
$x=3$.

1.  Sketch the function $f$ and shade in the region.[2 marks]

    ![f1b644b2fe2c5a061d9283ea9bad66c749302510.svg](images/f1b644b2fe2c5a061d9283ea9bad66c749302510.svg)

2.
    1.  Write down a definite integral that represents the shaded
        region.

    2.  Calculate the area of the shaded region.[2 marks]

3.  Use the trapezoidal rule with $n=6$ trapeziums to estimate
    the area of the region.[3 marks]
