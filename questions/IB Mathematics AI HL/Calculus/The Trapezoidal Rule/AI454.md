# AI454

**Maximum mark: 14**

A function is given by $f(x) = 4x^3 - x$ for
$-\dfrac 1 2 \leq x \leq \dfrac 1 2$.

1.  Sketch the graph of $f$ for the given domain, labelling all
    intercepts and stationary points. [4 marks]

2.  Find an approximation for
    $I = {\displaystyle \int_0^{1/2}} f(x)\, \mathrm{d}x$ using the
    trapezoidal rule with a strip width of $0.1$. [4 marks]

3.  Find the exact value of $I$ in part (b). [2 marks]

4.  Explain why the approximation found in part (b) is larger than the
    exact value found in part (c). [2 marks]

5.  Write down the value of
    $J = {\displaystyle  \int_{-1/2}^{1/2}} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x$ without
    calculation. Give a reason for your answer. [2 marks]

2.  Since the strip width is $h = 0.1$, we start with $x=0$ and increase
    by an increment of $0.1$ to get the other $x$-values. These
    $x$-values and their corresponding $y$-values are calculated and
    shown in the table below.

    ![34d13a45dabb01b1c21f46b9c273978b426f6552.svg](images/34d13a45dabb01b1c21f46b9c273978b426f6552.svg)

    Using the trapezoidal rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_0^{1/2} y \, \mathrm{d}x &\approx \dfrac{1}{2}h \left( (y_0 + y_n) + 2(y_1 + y_2 + \dots + y_{n-1} ) \right) \\[3pt]
    &\approx \dfrac{1}{2}(0.1) \left[ (0 + 0) + 2 \left((-0.096) + (-0.168) + (-0.192) + (-0.144) \right)  \right] \\[6pt]
    &\approx -0.06
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The exact value of $I$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    I &= \int_{0}^{1/2} \left( 4x^3 - x\right) \mathrm{d}x  \\[3pt]
    &= \left[ 4 \left(\dfrac 1 4 x^4 \right) - \dfrac 1 2 x^2\right]_0^{1/2}  \\[3pt]
    &= \left[ x^4 - \dfrac 1 2 x^2\right]_0^{1/2}  \\[3pt]
    &= \left[ \left(\dfrac 1 2 \right)^4 - \dfrac 1 2 \left(\dfrac 1 2 \right)^2\right] - \left[ (0)^4 - \dfrac 1 2 (0)^2\right]  \\[6pt]
    &=  -0.0625 \hspace{3em} [\text{or by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The shaded region below represents an approximation of the value of
    $I$ in part (b).

    ![fe340aca6deda6454dcc6192ad470f045c2c3d12.svg](images/fe340aca6deda6454dcc6192ad470f045c2c3d12.svg)

    As we can see, the total area of the shaded trapezoids is less than the area of the region enclosed by the curve and the $x$-axis from $0 < x < \dfrac 1 2$. And since the values are negative, the approximation value is larger than the exact value of $I$.

5.  The diagram below shows us that the areas of the regions bounded by
    the curve and the $x$-axis on the left and right of the $y$-axis are
    equal, yet opposite to each other.

    ![163d9e83b2056fcd42e13d72c2cd708234d6f4fc.svg](images/163d9e83b2056fcd42e13d72c2cd708234d6f4fc.svg)

    Hence we get

    $$
    {\displaystyle  \int_{-1/2}^{0}} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x = - {\displaystyle  \int_{0}^{1/2}} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x
    $$
    Therefore,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    J &= {\displaystyle  \int_{-1/2}^{1/2}} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= {\displaystyle  \int_{-1/2}^{0}} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x + {\displaystyle  \int_{0}^{1/2}} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= -{\displaystyle  \int_{0}^{1/2}} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x + {\displaystyle  \int_{0}^{1/2}} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
    &= -(-0.0625)+(-0.0625) \\[3pt]
    &= 0
    \end{aligned}
    $$
