# AI641

**Maximum mark: 5**

A company is going to make an excavation to access a mine. The cost of
excavating is $$\$ 12$$ per m$^3$. The following table shows an estimate
of the areas of horizontal cross-sections, at $5$-metre intervals, of
the underground to be removed where the height is measured in metres
below the ground level.

![6874d5a844183f44e95c188424d4576450fb7622.svg](images/6874d5a844183f44e95c188424d4576450fb7622.svg)

1.  Use the trapezoidal rule to find an estimate of the volume of ground
    to be removed. [3 marks]

2.  Hence, or otherwise, estimate the total cost of
    excavation.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $h$ be the height below the ground level. Thus, the volume of
    ground to be removed is the integral from 0 to 30 of the area of the
    horizontal cross-sections multiplied by the differential
    $\mathrm{d}h$, thus using the trapezoidal rule we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \displaystyle \int _0^{30} A(h)\, \mathrm{d}h \rule[-5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &\approx \dfrac{\Delta h}{2}\big(115+67+2(105+90+75+72+69)\big) \rule[-5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    & \approx \dfrac{5}{2} \big(115+67+2(105+90+75+72+69)\big) \rule[-5mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    & \hspace{0.3em}\approx 2\hspace{0.1em}510 \text{ m}^3 \rule[-2mm]{0pt}{7mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The total cost of excavation is
    $$(2\hspace{0.1em}510)(12) =\enskip  \$\hspace{0.1em} 30\hspace{0.1em}120$$
