# AI1228

**Maximum mark: 8**

Consider the system of coupled differential equations given by

$$

\begin{aligned}

\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} &= 2x-9y \\[8pt]

\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= 2y-x

\end{aligned}

$$

The characteristic polynomial of the matrix of coefficients is $p(\lambda)= \lambda^2 - 4\lambda - 5$.

It is given that one of the eigenvalues is $\lambda_1=5$ and its associated eigenvector is $\bm{p}_1=\begin{pmatrix} -3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$.

1. Find

    1. $\lambda_2$

    2. $\bm{p}_2$ [5 marks]

2. Sketch a phase portrait for the general solution to the coupled system on the following axes. [3 marks]

    ![ai1228a](images/ai1228a.png)

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 1. The eigenvalues are the solutions to the equation $p(\lambda)=0$. Using the G.D.C., we can find the solutions to $\lambda^2 - 4\lambda - 5=0$. 

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

  

        We find two solutions, $\lambda = 5$ and $\lambda = -1$. We have already been told that $\lambda_1=5$, therefore

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \boxed{\lambda_2=-1} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

    2. $\bm{p}_2$ is the solution to $(\textbf{M}-\lambda_2 \textbf{I})\bm{p}_2=0$. We have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 3 & -9 \\ -1 & 3 \end{pmatrix*}\begin{pmatrix*} x \\ y \end{pmatrix*} &= \begin{pmatrix*} 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]

        -x + 3y &= 0 \hspace{3em}\text{[from row 2]} \\[8pt]

        3y & = x \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

        Choose $x=3$ and $y=1$.

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \boxed{\bm{p}_2=\begin{pmatrix*} 3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

        **Note: there are an infinite number of correct eigenvectors. Any vector that is parallel to the vector $\begin{pmatrix*} 3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}$ is correct.**
