# AI105

**Maximum mark: 6**

The rates of change of the area covered by two types of mould, A and B,
on the surface of a bathroom ceiling are given by the following
differential equations, where $x$ is the area covered by A and $y$ is
the area covered by B.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} &= 4x - 3y \\[8pt]
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= 2x - 3y \\
\end{aligned}
$$
The matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 4 & -3 \\ 2 & -3 \end{pmatrix}$ has eigenvalues of $3$ and $-2$ with corresponding eigenvectors $\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}$.

The initial areas covered by A and B are
$x = 5$ cm$^2$ and
$y =5$ cm$^2$.

1.  Find the value of
     $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}$
    when $t = 0$. [2 marks]

2.  On the axes below, sketch a possible trajectory for the growth of
    the areas covered by the two moulds, making clear any asymptotic
    behaviour. [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \bigg[\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}\bigg]\bigg|_{t=0} &= \bigg[\dfrac{2x-3y}{4x-3y}\bigg]\bigg|_{x=5,\hspace{0.1em} y=5} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{2(5)-3(5)}{4(5)-3(5)} \\[12pt]
    &= -1
    \end{aligned}
