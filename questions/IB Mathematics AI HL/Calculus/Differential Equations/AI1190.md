# AI1190

**Maximum mark: 6**

The displacement, $x$ metres, of a particle from a fixed origin $O$ at time $t$ seconds is given by the differential equation

$$

\begin{aligned}

2\dfrac{\mathrm{d}^2x}{\mathrm{d}t^2}+3\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}-x=5t+2

\end{aligned}

$$

The initial displacement of the particle is $5$ metres, and its initial velocity is $-2\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}$. Use Euler’s method for coupled systems with a step size of $0.1$ to find the approximate displacement $\textbf{and}$ velocity of the particle after $1$ second.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

By making the substitution $y = \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}$, we can rewrite the second order differential equation as a coupled system and hence use Euler's method.

First, we can note that if $y = \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}$, then $\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} = \frac{\mathrm{d}^2x}{\mathrm{d}t^2}$.

Substituting these two expressions into the original equation, we have

$$

\begin{aligned}

    2\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}+3y-x&=5t+2

\end{aligned}

$$

Rearranging to make $\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}$ the subject, we have

$$

\begin{aligned}

    \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \dfrac{5t + 2 +x - 3y}{2}

\end{aligned}

$$

We now have expressions $f_1(x_n,y_n,t_n)$ and $f_2(x_n,y_n,t_n)$ which is what we require to use Euler's method for coupled systems.

Recall that, for Euler's method, $f_1(x_n,y_n,t_n)$ is the value of $\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}$ at the previous set of values. 

Given that $\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}=y$, $f_1(x_n,y_n,t_n)=y_n$.

$$

\begin{aligned}

\tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

\end{aligned}

$$

Recall also that $f_2(x_n,y_n,t_n)$ is the value of $\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}$ at the previous set of values. We have

$$

\begin{aligned}

    \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \dfrac{5t + 2 +x - 3y}{2} \\[6pt]

    \implies f_2(x_n,y_n,t_n) &= \dfrac{5t_n + 2 + x_n - 3y_n}{2} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

\end{aligned}

$$

We were told that the initial displacement of the particle is $5$ metres, and its initial velocity is $-2\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}$. Velocity $=\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}=y$. This tells us the following:

$$

\begin{aligned}

    t_0 = 0\,, \enskip x_0 = 5\,, \enskip y_0 = -2

\end{aligned}

$$

And given that $h=0.1$, we have our three equations that are required for Euler's method for coupled systems:

$\hspace{2em}\bullet$ $x_{n+1} = x_n + 0.1y_n$

$\hspace{2em}\bullet$ $y_{n+1} = y_n + 0.1\left(\dfrac{5t_n + 2 + x_n - 3y_n}{2}\right)$

$\hspace{2em}\bullet$ $t_{n+1} = t_n + 0.1$
