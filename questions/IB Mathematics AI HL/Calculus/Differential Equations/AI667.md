# AI667

**Maximum mark: 6**

An experiment is conducted on a gas confined in a container. The
pressure $P$ measured in Pascals, and the volume V measured in cm$^3$
satisfy the differential equation

$$
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}P}{\mathrm{d}V}=-\dfrac{P}{V}
$$

Initially, the gas has a pressure of 20 000
Pascals and it is confined in a container with a volume of
100 cm$^3$.

1.  Solve the differential equation to show that
    $$
    P=\dfrac{2\hspace{0.15em}000\hspace{0.15em}000}{V}
    $$
    [4 marks]

2.  Calculate the pressure required to compress the gas to a
    different container that has a volume of
    $50$ cm$^3$.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Solving the differential equation by separating variables, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}P}{\mathrm{d}V} &= -\dfrac{P}{V}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    \int \dfrac{\mathrm{d}P}{P} &= -\int \dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{V} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    \ln P &= -\ln V + C \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    \ln P + \ln V &= C \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    \ln PV &= C \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    PV &= K \hspace{3em} [\text{by letting  $K=e^C$}]\rule[-2mm]{0pt}{8mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    using that $P=20\hspace{0.1em}000$ when $V=100$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (20\hspace{0.15em}000)(100) &= K \rule[-2mm]{0pt}{6mm}\\
    K &= 2\hspace{0.15em}000\hspace{0.15em}000\rule[0mm]{0pt}{4mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Therefore, an expression for the pressure in terms of the volume is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    & \hspace{-0.82em}P = \dfrac{2\hspace{0.15em}000\hspace{0.15em}000}{V}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Substituting $V=50$ into equation (1), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    P &= \dfrac{2\hspace{0.15em}000\hspace{0.15em}000}{50} \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-0.82em}P = 40\hspace{0.15em}000 \hspace{0.25em}\text{Pa}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
