# AI670

**Maximum mark: 5**

The population $P$ of bears in a forest is expected to grow according to
the model

$$
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}P}{\mathrm{d}t} = 0.2P\left(1-\dfrac{P}{2\hspace{0.1em}500}\right)
$$

where $t$ is the time given in years from the beginning of 2020.

The slope field for this differential equation is as
follows:

![08b4f6cc4c85e4347a20ab95f71de8f17c4fcd67.svg](images/08b4f6cc4c85e4347a20ab95f71de8f17c4fcd67.svg)

1.  Given that the population at the beginning of 2020 is
    $1\hspace{0.1em}00$, sketch the particular solution
    curve.[3 marks]

2.  Find the value at which the population approaches as time
    increases.[2 marks]
