# AI1142

**Maximum mark: 15**

Chrissy's solar powered car has experienced a fault with its steering, but is still in motion.

Its position at time $t$ seconds is defined by

$$

\begin{aligned}

\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}=3x-5y, \hspace{1em}&\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}=5x-3y, 

\end{aligned}

$$

where $x$ metres is the distance east and $y$ metres is the distance north of a fixed point O.

The position of the car can be defined by the equation $\dot{\textbf{x}}=\bf{\textit{A}}\hspace{0.1em}\textbf{x}$, where $\bf{\textit{A}}$ is a $2 \times 2$ matrix.

1. 1. Write down the matrix $\bf{\textit{A}}$.

    2. Find the eigenvalues of $\bf{\textit{A}}$.[4 marks]

At time $t=0$, the car has position $(0,10)$.

2. At this time, find the value of

    1. $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}$

    2. $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}$[5 marks]

3. Hence describe the path of the car.[3 marks]

4. Sketch the path of the solar powered car.[3 marks]
