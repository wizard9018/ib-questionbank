# AI977

**Maximum mark: 5**

The diagram below shows the slope field for the differential equation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{3.9em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = \cos(x-y), \hspace{0.75em}-6.5\leq x \leq 4.5, \hspace{0.75em}0 \leq y \leq 5.5. \\
\end{aligned}
$$

The graphs of the two solutions to the differential equation passing
through points P$(0,1)$ and Q$(0,3)$ are drawn over the slope field.

![90e5f091d5b901c342c544173a01c19a09907f69.svg](images/90e5f091d5b901c342c544173a01c19a09907f69.svg)

For the two graphs given, the local maximum points lie on the straight
line $L_1$.

1.  Find the equation of $L_1$, giving your answer in the form
    $y = mx+c$. [3 marks]

For the two graphs given, the local minimum points lie on the straight
line $L_2$.

2.  Find the equation of $L_2$, giving your answer in the form
    $y = mx+c$. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Note that at a local maximum, $\dfrac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} = 0$. 

    Hence, we must solve the equation $\cos{(x-y)}=0$. 

    

    A cosine function is equal to zero only if the argument is an odd multiple of $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Therefore,

$x - y = n\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big)$ for some odd value of $n \in \mathbb{Z}$. Hence the equation of $L_1$ is of the form $y = x - n\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big)$. We must find the value of $n$.

    ![AI977a](images/AI977a.PNG)

    If we label the local maximum points on the two graphs given, and sketch the lines for $n=-5$, $n=-3$ 

and $n=-1$, we have

    ![AI977b](images/AI977b.PNG)

    Given that the $y$-intercept for $y=x+5\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big)$ is too large, and the $y$-intercept for $y=x+\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big)$ is too small, we must have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{y = x + \dfrac{3\pi}{2}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Note that at a local minimum, $\dfrac{\text{d}y}{\text{d}x} = 0$.

    Hence, we must solve the equation $\cos{(x-y)}=0$

    A cosine function is equal to zero if and only if the argument is an odd multiple of $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Therefore,

$x - y = n\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big)$ for some odd value of $n \in \mathbb{Z}$. Hence the equation of $L_2$ is of the form $y = x - n\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big)$. We must find the value of $n$.

    ![AI977c](images/AI977c.PNG)

    If we label the local minimum points on the two graphs given, and sketch the lines for $n=-3$, $n=-1$ and $n=1$, we have

    ![AI977d](images/AI977d.PNG)

    Given that the $y$-intercept for $y=x+3\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big)$ is too large, and the $y$-intercept for $y=x-\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big)$ is too small, we must have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{y = x + \dfrac{\pi}{2}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
