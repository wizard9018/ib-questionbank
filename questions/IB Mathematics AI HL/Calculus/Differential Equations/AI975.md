# AI975

**Maximum mark: 8**

A tabletop water purifier is filled with
$2\hspace{0.15em}500$ ml of water. It takes the
purifier $90$ seconds to filter $900$ ml of
water.

The volume of water, $V$ ml, remaining
unfiltered in the purifier after $t$ seconds, can be modelled by the
differential equation
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t} &= -q\hspace{0.025em}\sqrt{V},
\end{aligned}
$$

where $q$ is a positive constant.

1.  Show that
    $V = \Big(50 - \dfrac{t}{9}\Big)^2$ .
    [6 marks]

2.  Find the time it takes for the purifier to filter all the water.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Separating variables and integrating both sides, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{V}}\mathrm{d}V &= -q\,\mathrm{d}t \\[8pt]
    %\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\sqrt{V}} &= -q\,\mathrm{d}t \\[10pt]
    V^{-\frac{1}{2}}\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{d}V &= -q\,\mathrm{d}t \hspace{5em}  \\[8pt]
    \int V^{-\frac{1}{2}}\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{d}V &= \int \hspace{-0.25em}-q\,\mathrm{d}t \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{V^{\frac{1}{2}}}{\frac{1}{2}} &= -qt + C \hspace{3em} [\text{for some $C \in \mathbb{R}$}] \\[6.5pt]
    V^{\frac{1}{2}} &= -\dfrac{qt}{2} + K \hspace{2.825em} [\text{where $K = \textstyle\frac{1}{2}C$}] \\[8pt]
    V &= \Big(K - \dfrac{qt}{2}\Big)^2 \hspace{3em}{(1)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We know:

    (2) $V = 2500$ ml when $t = 0$
        seconds;

    (3) $V = 1600$ ml when $t = 90$
        seconds.

    Substituting initial values $(2)$ into equation $(1)$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2500 &= (K - 0)^2 \\[12pt]
    K &= 50 \hspace{3.748em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting values $(3)$ into equation $(1)$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    1600 &= \Big(50 - \dfrac{90q}{2}\Big)^2 \\[10pt]
    q &= \dfrac{2}{9} \hspace{4em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we deduce
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{-0.8em} V = \Big(50 - \dfrac{t}{9}\Big)^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Solving the equation $V = 0$ for $t$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \Big(50 - \dfrac{t}{9}\Big)^2 &= 0 \\[10pt]
    t &= 450 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $\text{It takes \(450\) seconds to filter all the water.}$
