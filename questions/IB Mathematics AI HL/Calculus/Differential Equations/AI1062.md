# AI1062

**Maximum mark: 5**

Consider the following system of coupled differential equations.

$$

\begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} &= 3y \\[8pt]

    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= -3x

\end{aligned}

$$

The system can be written in the form 

$$

\begin{aligned}

\bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} \dot{x} \\[2pt] \dot{y} \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg) &= A\hspace{0.2em}\bigg(\hspace{0.1em}\begin{matrix} x \\[2pt] y \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)

\end{aligned}

$$

where $A$ is a $2\times2$ matrix.

1. Show that the eigenvalues of $A$ are $\lambda_1 = -3\mathrm{i}$ and $\lambda_2 = 3\mathrm{i}$.[3 marks]

It is given that at the point $(0,1)$, $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}>0$ and $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}=0$.

2. Sketch a phase portrait for the system on the following set of axes. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} &= 0x + 3y \\[8pt]

    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= -3x + 0y

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    And hence $A = \bigg(\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{matrix} \,\,\,\,\,0 & 3 \\[2pt] -3 & 0 \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)$

    If we solve the equation $\det(A - \lambda I) = 0$ for $\lambda$, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \bigg|\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{matrix} \,\,\,\,-\lambda & \hspace{0.65em}3 \\[10pt] \hspace{0.65em}-3 & -\lambda \end{matrix}\hspace{0.1em}\bigg)\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg| &= 0 \\[10pt]

    \lambda^2 + 9 &= 0 \\[8pt]

    \lambda^2 &= -9 \\[8pt]

    \lambda &= \pm\sqrt{-9} \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-4.75em}\boxed{\lambda_1 = -3\mathrm{i} \text{ and } \lambda_2 = 3\mathrm{i}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. When the eigenvalues are imaginary, the phase portrait will consist of concentric circles or ellipses about the origin.
