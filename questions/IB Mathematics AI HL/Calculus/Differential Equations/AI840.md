# AI840

**Maximum mark: 8**

Lisa continuously invests money into an account at a rate of 1500 euros
per year. The account has an annual interest rate of 8%. The amount of
money, $A$, in the account after $t$ years satisfies the following
differential equation:
$$
\dfrac{dA}{dt}=0.08 A+1500

$$
The initial amount
of money Lisa deposited into the account when it was set up was 730
euros.

1.  Find an expression for $A$, in terms of $t$. [5 marks]

2.
    1.  Find $\displaystyle{\int_0^6}(0.08 A+1500)\, \textrm d t$;

    2.  State what this value represents. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Solving the differential equation by separating variables, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{dA}{dt}&=0.08 A+1500\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    \int \dfrac{dA}{0.08 A+1500}&= \int \textrm d t\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    12.5\ln (0.08A+1500) &= t+ C \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    0.08A+1500 &= e^{0.08t+ 0.08C} \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{10mm} \hspace{3em}\\
    A&= Ke^{0.08t}-18750 \hspace{3em} [\text{by letting $K=12.5e^{0.08C}$}]\rule[-2mm]{0pt}{8mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using $A=730$ when $t=0$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    730 &=  Ke^{0.08(0)}-18750\\[6pt]
    730 &=  K-18750\\[6pt]
    K &=19480
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore, an expression for $A$ in
    terms of $t$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    & \hspace{-0.82em}A(t)=  19480\,e^{0.08t}-18750
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  Using the expression for $A(t)$ found in part (a), we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \int_0^{6}(0.08 A(t)+1500)\, \textrm d t    & =\int_0^{6}(0.08 (19480\,e^{0.08t}-18750)+1500)\, \textrm d t\\[8pt]
        &\hspace{.275em} \approx 12000 \hspace{3em} [\text{using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.

        This value represents the increase in the account value between $t=0$ and $t=6$. Hence, during the first six years, the amount of money in the account increases by 12000 euros.
