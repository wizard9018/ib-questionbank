# AI106

**Maximum mark: 6**

The rates of change of the area covered by two types of mould, X and Y,
on the surface of a rotten fruit are given by the following differential
equations, where $x$ is the area covered by X and $y$ is the area
covered by Y.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} &= 2x - 5y \\[8pt]
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= x - 4y \\
\end{aligned}
$$
The matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -5 \\ 1 & -4\end{pmatrix}$ has eigenvalues of $1$ and $-3$ with corresponding eigenvectors $\begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$.

The initial areas covered by X and Y are
$x = 6$ cm$^2$ and
$y = 2$ cm$^2$.

1.  Find the value of
     $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}$
    when $t = 0$. [2 marks]

2.  On the axes below, sketch a possible trajectory for the growth of
    the areas covered by the two moulds, making clear any asymptotic
    behaviour. [4 marks]

    &= \dfrac{6-4(2)}{2(6)-5(2)} \\[12pt]
