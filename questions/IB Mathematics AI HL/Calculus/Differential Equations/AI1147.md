# AI1147

**Maximum mark: 6**

A particle moves such that its displacement (in centimetres) from an origin O at time $t$ seconds is given by the differential equation

$$

\begin{aligned}

\dfrac{\mathrm{d}^2x}{\mathrm{d}t^2} + 5\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} + 4x &= 3t + 1

\end{aligned}

$$

When $t=0$, $x=3$ and $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}=-3$.

Using Euler's method with a step size of $0.2$ seconds, estimate the value of $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}$ when $t=1$.

---
