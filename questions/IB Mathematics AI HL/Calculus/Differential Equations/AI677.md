# AI677

**Maximum mark: 5**

On an island, there is a population of cats that like to hunt a certain species of birds.

The populations of birds and cats can be modelled by the coupled system

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{lcl}
x'(t)=2x-xy\\[8pt]
y'(t)=2xy-y
\end{array} \right. \\[4pt]
$$

Where $x$ is the number of birds and $y$ is the number of cats, both measured in hundreds, at time $t$ years. Initially there were $70$ birds and $40$ cats.

1.  Find the non-zero equilibrium point for the system. [2 marks]

2.  Using Euler's method with a step size of $0.1$, estimate the population of birds and cats after $1$ year. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  At an equilibrium point, both $x'(t)$ and $y'(t)$ must equal zero.

    If we factorise both expressions and set them equal to zero, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        x(2-y)&= 0  &&& y(2x-1)=0 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Given that $x,\,y \neq 0$ for a non-zero equilibrium point, the solution to the first equation is $y=2$ and the solution to the second equation is $x=\frac{1}{2}$.

    Therefore, the non-zero equilibrium point is $$\boxed{\text{$\left(\tfrac{1}{2},2\right)$}}$$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the formulae for Euler's method for coupled systems, with $h=0.1$, we can generate expressions for $x_{n+1}$, $y_{n+1}$ and $t_{n+1}$. We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x_{n+1} &= x_n + h\times f_1\left(x_n,y_n,t_n\right) \\[6pt]
    &= x_n + 0.1\left(2x_n- x_n y_n\right)\\[6pt]
    y_{n+1} &= y_n + h\times f_2\left(x_n,y_n,t_n\right) \\[6pt]
    &= y_n + 0.1\left(2x_n y_n - y_n\right) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    t_{n+1} &= t_n + h \\[6pt]
    &= t_n + 0.1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We also have initial values of $x_0 = 0.7$ and $y_0 = 0.4$ because $x$ and $y$ are measured in hundreds.
