# AI1031

**Maximum mark: 8**

In a rural area the population of rabbits, in thousands, and foxes, in hundreds, are described by the coupled system

$$

\begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}R}{\mathrm{d}t} &= R - 2FR \\[8pt]

    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}F}{\mathrm{d}t} &= 1.5FR - 0.8F

\end{aligned}

$$

Where $t$ represents time in years. It is noted that initially there are 700 rabbits and 70 foxes.

1. Use Euler's method with a step size of $0.5$ to estimate the population of both rabbits and foxes after 5 years. [5 marks]

2. On the grid below, sketch a graph of $F$ versus $R$ for $0 \leq t \leq 7$. [2 marks]

![ai1031a](images/ai1031a.PNG)

3. Hence, determine the approximate amount of time in the first seven years that the population of rabbits and foxes are both increasing.[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using Euler's method for coupled systems with $h=0.5$, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    R_{n+1} &= R_n + 0.5 \times f_1(R_n,F_n,t_n) \\[8pt]

    F_{n+1} &= F_n + 0.5 \times f_2(R_n,F_n,t_n) \\[8pt]

    t_{n+1} &= t_n + 0.5

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Where $f_1(R_n,F_n,t_n) = R_n - 2F_n R_n$ and $f_2(R_n,F_n,t_n) = 1.5F_n R_n -0.8F_n$. Hence,

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    R_{n+1} &= R_n + 0.5(R_n - 2F_n R_n) \\[8pt]

    F_{n+1} &= F_n + 0.5 (1.5F_n R_n -0.8F_n) \\[8pt]

    t_{n+1} &= t_n + 0.5

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Given that the rabbit population is measured in thousands and the fox population is measured in hundreds, we have initial values of $t_0=0$, $R_0=0.7$ and $F_0=0.7$. Using the G.D.C., we compile the following table:

    ![ai1031b](images/ai1031b.PNG)

    Recalling that the rabbit population is measured in thousands and the fox population is measured in hundreds, after five years we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    R &= 0.5296 \times 1000 \\[8pt]

    &\approx 530 \\[8pt]

    F &= 0.1466 \times 100 \\[8pt]

    &\approx 15

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    After five years, the rabbit population is approximately $530$ and the fox population is approximately $15$.
