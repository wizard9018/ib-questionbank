# AI137

**Maximum mark: 13**

A cannon-ball is fired from the top of a tower. The height, $h$, in
metres, of the cannon-ball above the ground is modelled by the function

$$
\begin{aligned}
h(t) = -2t^2 + 20t + 8, \hspace{0.5em} t \geq 0,
\end{aligned}
$$
where
$t$ is the time, in seconds, since the moment the cannon-ball was fired.

1.  Write down the height of the tower. [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the height of the cannon-ball $5$ seconds after it was
    fired. [2 marks]

The cannon-ball hits its target on the ground $n$ seconds after it was
fired.

3.  Find the value of $n$. [2 marks]

4.  Find $h'(t)$. [2 marks]

5.  Calculate the maximum height reached by the cannon-ball,
    and
    write down the corresponding time $t$. [3 marks]

6.  Determine the total time the cannon ball was above the
    height of the tower.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $h(t)$ for $t = 0$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.7em} h(0) &= -2(0)^2+20(0)+8 \\[6pt]
    &= 8 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Evaluating $h(t)$ for $t = 5$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.7em} h(5) &= -2(5)^2+20(5)+8 \\[6pt]
    &= 58 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $h(n) = 0$ for $n > 0$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -2n^2+20n+8 &= 0 \hspace{9em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{9.2em} n \approx 10.4 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

4.  If we differentiate $h(t)$ with respect to $t$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.2em} h'(t) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\big[-2t^2+20t+8\big] \\[6pt]
    &= -4t+20
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  If we sketch the graph of $y = h(t)$, for $t \geq 0$ \[by using
    G.D.C.\], we have

    ![397bd75d8b517ae440b92c4aa69a8d23b9a43a67.svg](images/397bd75d8b517ae440b92c4aa69a8d23b9a43a67.svg)

    Hence the maximum height is
     $58 \text{ m}$
    and it is reached after
     $5 \text{ seconds}$

6.  Solving the equation $h(t) = 8$ for $t > 0$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -2t^2+20t+8 &= 8 \hspace{8.6em} \\[6pt]
    -2t^2+20t &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{12.2em} t = 10 \text{ seconds} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
