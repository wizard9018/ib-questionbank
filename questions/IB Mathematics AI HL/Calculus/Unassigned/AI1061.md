# AI1061

**Maximum mark: 5**

Evan has inserted a photo of a bridge into graphing software. The photo shows a suspension cable hanging between two vertical suspenders. The part of the cable between the vertical suspenders in the photo has equation

$$

\begin{aligned}

    h(x) = \dfrac{7}{53}x^2 + 2 \, , \, -4.6 \leq x \leq 4.6

\end{aligned}

$$

Where $h$ and $x$ are measured in centimetres. The bridge deck lies on the $x$-axis.

![ai1061](images/ai1061.PNG)

The photo is an enlargement of the actual bridge with a scale factor of $\dfrac{1}{125}$ and centre $(0,0)$.

1. Find the equation of the actual suspension cable between the vertical suspenders. Give your answer in the form $f(x)=ax^2+b$, where $a,b\,\in\mathbb{R}$.[3 marks]

2. Hence, calculate the area bound by the vertical suspenders, the bridge deck and the suspension cable. Give your answer in m$^2$.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Let the equation of the actual suspension cable be $f(x)$. Therefore, using both a vertical enlargement and horizontal enlargement of scale factor $\dfrac{1}{125}$ we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    h(x) = \dfrac{1}{125}\,f(125x)

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Expressing $f(x)$ in terms of $h(x)$ we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    f(x) &= 125\,h\left(\dfrac{x}{125}\right) \\[8pt]

    &= 125\left(\dfrac{7}{53}\left(\dfrac{x}{125}\right)^2 + 2\right) \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-1.9em}\boxed{f(x) = 0.001057x^2 + 250\,,\,-575 \leq x \leq 575}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The area required is given by the integral

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A &= \displaystyle\int_{-575}^{575}\,0.001057x^2 + 250\,\mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]

    &\approx 421\,000\,\mathrm{cm}^2

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    We can convert area in $\mathrm{cm}^2$ to area in $\mathrm{m}^2$ by dividing by $100^2$.

    The area bound by the vertical suspenders, the bridge deck and the suspension cable is approximately $42.1\,\text{m}{^2}$.
