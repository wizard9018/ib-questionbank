# AI1032

**Maximum mark: 6**

A food company produces mozzarella sticks that can be modelled by a cuboid, with length $y$ cm, width $x$ cm and height $x$ cm, as shown below.

![ai1032a](images/ai1032a.PNG)

The volume of cheese in each mozzarella stick is $81\,\text{cm}^3$ and hence the total surface area is given by 

$$

\begin{aligned}

    A &= \dfrac{324}{x} + 2x^2

\end{aligned}

$$

1. Find $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}x}$. [3 marks]

The surface area is to be minimized in order to reduce the cooling rate of the mozzarella stick.

2. Using your answer to part (a), calculate the value of $x$ that minimizes the surface area of the mozzarella stick.  [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Our expression for $A$ can be rewritten as

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A &= 324x^{-1} + 2x^2

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    And hence we can differentiate term by term:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}x} &= -324x^{-2} + 4x \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-1.85em}\boxed{\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}x} = -\dfrac{324}{x^2} + 4x}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The minimum (or maximum) of a function occurs when the derivative is equal to zero. Therefore we must solve

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}x} &= 0 \\[8pt]

    -\dfrac{324}{x^2} + 4x &= 0 \\[8pt]

    x &\approx 4.327

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    To verify that this $x$-value represents a minimum and not a maximum, we can inspect the graph of $A$ on the G.D.C.:

    ![ai1032b](images/ai1032b.PNG)

    The point represents a minimum. Therefore,

    $$\boxed{\text{when $x=4.33$\hspace{0.15em}cm, the surface area of the mozzarella stick is minimized.}}$$
