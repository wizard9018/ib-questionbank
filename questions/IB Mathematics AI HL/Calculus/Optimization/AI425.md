# AI425

**Maximum mark: 6**

A cement silo made of steel consists of a cylinder with height $h$ and
radius $r$ and a cone at the bottom of the cylinder's base. The total
surface area of the cement silo is given by
$$
\begin{aligned}
A = \dfrac{48 \pi}{r} + 3\pi r^2,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $r$ is the radius of the cylinder, in metres.

The silo is designed such that the total cost for the steel used is
minimized. This is the same as minimizing the total surface area of the
silo.

1.  Find $\dfrac{dA}{dr}$. [3 marks]

2.  Calculate the value of $r$ that minimizes the total surface area of
    the silo. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Differentiating $A$ with respect to $r$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}r} &=  \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}r}\left(\dfrac{48 \pi}{r} + 3\pi r^2\right) \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}r}\left(48 \pi r^{-1} + 3\pi r^2\right) \\[3pt]
    &= 48 \pi (-1r^{-2}) + 3\pi(2r^{1}) \\[3pt]
    &= -48 \pi r^{-2} +  6\pi r  \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}=  -\dfrac{48\pi}{r^2} + 6 \pi r
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The graph of the total surface area, $A$, is shown below.

    ![2b02d279d79fb5affedafa35ab036e8b1785aadf.svg](images/2b02d279d79fb5affedafa35ab036e8b1785aadf.svg)

    The total surface area $A$ is minimized when
    $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}r} = 0$. We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0 &= -\dfrac{48\pi}{r^2} + 6 \pi r  \\[3pt]
    0 &=  -48 \pi + 6\pi r^3 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.525em}r = 2 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
