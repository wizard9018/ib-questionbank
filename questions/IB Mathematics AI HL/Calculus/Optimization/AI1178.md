# AI1178

**Maximum mark: 8**

The following diagram shows a rectangular garden of area $80\,\mathrm{m}^2$, width = $y$ metres, length = $x$ metres. There is a path around the garden with a uniform width of 2 metres.

![1178a](images/1178a.JPG)

1. Write an expression for $y$ in terms of $x$. [2 marks]

2. Hence show that the total area of the garden and path combined can be expressed as $A=96 + 4x +\dfrac{320}{x}$. [3 marks]

3. Find the minimum area of the path. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using the formula for the area of a rectangle, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A &= xy \\[6pt]

    80 &= xy \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{y = \frac{80}{x}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The combined area of the garden and path is a rectangle, with width $(y+4)$ metres, and length $(x+4)$ metres.

    $$                    

    \begin{aligned}            

    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}    

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Using our result from part (a), we can write an expression for the area in terms of $x$ only:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A &= (y+4)(x+4) \\[6pt]

     &= \left(\frac{80}{x}+4\right)\left(x+4\right) \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Expanding the brackets, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A &= 80 + \dfrac{320}{x} + 4x + 16 \tag*{\textbf{M1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Simplifying, we obtain $\boxed{A = 96 +4x + \frac{320}{x}\,,\text{ as required.}}$ 

    $$                    

    \begin{aligned}            

    \tag*{\textbf{AG}}    

    \end{aligned}

    $$
