# AI426

**Maximum mark: 6**

Mustafa is an ice sculptor. In an ice and snow festival, he is about to
build an ice tent in the shape of a cylinder with a cone of the same
radius at the top. The total surface area of the tent is in m$^2$ and
given by
$$
\begin{aligned}
A = \dfrac{18\pi}{r} + \pi r^2 - 2,
\end{aligned}
$$
where $r$ is the radius of the cylinder, in metres.

In order for his work to last as long as possible, Mustafa aims to
reduce the evaporation rate and hence minimize the total surface area of
the ice tent.

1.  Find $\dfrac{dA}{dr}$. [3 marks]

2.  Determine the value of $r$ that minimizes the total surface area of
    the ice tent. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Differentiating $A$ with respect to $r$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}r} &=  \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}r}\left(\dfrac{18\pi}{r} + \pi r^2 - 2\right) \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}r}\left(18 \pi r^{-1} + \pi r^2 - 2\right) \\[3pt]
    &= 18 \pi (-1r^{-2}) + \pi(2r^{1}) - 0 \\[3pt]
    &= -18 \pi r^{-2} +  2\pi r  \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}=  -\dfrac{18\pi}{r^2} + 2 \pi r
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The graph of the total surface area, $A$, is shown below

    ![3c06b6460e1b03a978fd046361f998f908a327b2.svg](images/3c06b6460e1b03a978fd046361f998f908a327b2.svg)

    The total surface area $A$ is minimized when
    $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}r} = 0$. We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0 &= -\dfrac{18\pi}{r^2} + 2 \pi r  \\[3pt]
    0 &=  -18 \pi + 2\pi r^3 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.525em}r \approx 2.08 \text{ m} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
