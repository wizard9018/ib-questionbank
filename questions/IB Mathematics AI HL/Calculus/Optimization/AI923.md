# AI923

**Maximum mark: 8**

Olivia designs a logo for a mountain camping club. The logo is in the
shape of a right-angled triangle, ABC, which
represents a mountain. A rectangular section, ADEF, is inscribed inside
the triangle to create a view of two smaller mountains. The lengths of
BD, DE, EF and FC are $p$ cm,
$4$ cm, $6$ cm and
$q$ cm respectively.

![291e1ac59f96803c42d18626ff6a359562c52722.svg](images/291e1ac59f96803c42d18626ff6a359562c52722.svg)

The total area of the logo is $A$ cm$^2$.

1.
    1.  Find $A$ in terms of $p$ and $q$, giving your
        answer in the form $\text{\(A=ap+bq+c\)}$.

    2.  Show that $A = \dfrac{48}{q} + 3q + 24$. [4 marks]

2.  Find $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}q}$. [2 marks]

Olivia wishes to find the value of $q$ that will minimize the area of
the club logo.

3.
    1.  Write down an equation Olivia could solve to find this value of
        $q$.

    2.  Hence, or otherwise, find this value of $q$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using the formulas for the area of a triangle and a rectangle,
        we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3.1em} A &= A_{\triangle\text{DBE}} + A_{\square\text{ADEF}} + A_{\triangle\text{FEC}} \\[12pt]
        &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\mathrm{BD})(\mathrm{DE}) + (\mathrm{DE})(\mathrm{EF}) + \dfrac{1}{2}(\mathrm{EF})(\mathrm{FC}) \\[9pt]
        &= \dfrac{1}{2}(p)(4) + (4)(6) + \dfrac{1}{2}(6)(q) \\[12pt]
        &= 2p + 3q + 24
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the similarity of triangles DBE and FEC, we get

         $$
         \begin{aligned}
         \dfrac{\mathrm{BD}}{\mathrm{EF}} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{DE}}{\mathrm{FC}} \\[9pt]
         \dfrac{p}{6} &= \dfrac{4}{q} \\[9pt]
         p &= 6\bigg[\dfrac{4}{q}\bigg] \hspace{3em} \\[9pt]
         p &= \dfrac{24}{q}
         \end{aligned}
         $$
         Substituting
        expression $(2)$ into equation $(1)$, we obtain

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        A &= 2\bigg[\dfrac{24}{q}\bigg] + 3q + 24 \hspace{3em} \\[9pt]
        &= \dfrac{48}{q} + 3q + 24
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  If we differentiate $A$ with respect to $q$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}q} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}q}\bigg[\dfrac{48}{q} + 3q + 24\bigg] \\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}q}\big[48q^{-1}\big] + \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}q}\big[3q\big] + \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}q}\big[24\big] \\[14pt]
    &= -48q^{-2} + 3 + 0 \\[15pt]
    &= -\dfrac{48}{q^2} + 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.
        $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}q} = 0$

    2.  Hence, solving the equation
        $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}q} = 0$ for $q$, we find

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        -\dfrac{48}{q^2} + 3 &= 0 \hspace{5.1em} \\[-1.2em]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \hspace{6.6em} q = 4 \hspace{3em} [\text{using GDC}]
        $$
