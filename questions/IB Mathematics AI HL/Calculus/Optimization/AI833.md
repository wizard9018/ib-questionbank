# AI833

**Maximum mark: 19**

A company that manufactures and sells cardboard boxes has a box with an
open-top design. This box is constructed from a rectangular cardboard
sheet with a length of 2 meters and a width of 1.2 meters, as
illustrated in the diagram below. The box is formed by cutting squares
of equal side length ($x$ meters) from each corner and folding up the
sides.

![3c496bb4d6576d3c5d758772b923f361dffa658e.svg](images/3c496bb4d6576d3c5d758772b923f361dffa658e.svg)

1.  Show that the volume of the box can be described by the function
    $V(x)=4x^3-6.4x^2+2.4x$. [3 marks]

2.
    1.  Find $V'(x)$;

    2.  Hence or otherwise, find the value for $x$ that maximises the
        volume of the box;

    3.  Hence, find the maximum volume of the box. [6 marks]

3.  Sketch the graph of $V(x)$ on the axes below for the domain
    $0<x<0.6$. [2 marks]

    ![cb22e8b54e351e6ffa8f03d2f697cbec0f531faf.svg](images/cb22e8b54e351e6ffa8f03d2f697cbec0f531faf.svg)

4.
    1.  Write down an integral representing the area between the graph
        of $V(x)$ and the $x$-axis;

    2.  Hence, find this area between the graph of $V(x)$ and the
        $x$-axis. [4 marks]

Let $A(x)$ be a function representing the outside surface area of the
box.

5.  Determine the function $A(x)$. [2 marks]

6.  Given that the volume of the box is maximised, find the
    outside surface area of the box. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  When the corners of the cardboard sheet are cut out and the box
    constructed, it forms a rectangular prism with the following
    dimensions:

    ![020abeb07d1b7975e40f5c9cf1c23db6786ea12a.svg](images/020abeb07d1b7975e40f5c9cf1c23db6786ea12a.svg)

    We can find the volume of a rectangular prism by multiplying the
    length, width and height dimensions. Hence, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V(x)&=(2-2x)(1.2-2x)x\\[6pt]
    &=(2.4-4x-2.4x+4x^2)x\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}= 4x^3-6.4x^2+2.4x
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  Differentiating $V$ with respect to $x$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        V'(x)&=4(3)x^{3-1}-6.4(2)x^{2-1}+2.4(1)x^{1-1}\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}=12x^2-12.8x+2.4
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The volume of the box is maximised when $V'(x) = 0$. Hence, we
        get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        12x^2-12.8x+2.4&=0\\[6pt]
        x&=0.243\text{ or }0.824\hspace{3em} [\text{using GDC}]\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{-0.425em}x=0.243\,\text{m}\hspace{4em} [\text{since x < 0.6}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3.  Substituting $x=0.243$ into $V(x)$ to find the corresponding
        volume, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        V(0.243)&=4(0.243)^3-6.4(0.243)^2+2.4(0.243),\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}= 0.263\,\text{m}^3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  Using GDC, we can we can sketch the graph of $V(x)$, for $0<x<0.6$

    ![61f6e1b462f24ba2b10671119fecab5dbbd34327.svg](images/61f6e1b462f24ba2b10671119fecab5dbbd34327.svg)

4.
    1.  The area is the region bounded by the curve $V(x)$ and the
        $x$-axis for $0\leq x\leq 0.6$. An integral describing this area
        is as follows
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{[Area}] =\int_0^{0.6} (4x^3-6.4x^2+2.4x)  \,\mathrm{d}x
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Evaluating the integral in part (d)(i), we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{[Area}] &= \int_0^{0.6} (4x^3-6.4x^2+2.4x)  \,\mathrm{d}x\\[3pt]
        &= \left[x^4-\dfrac{6.4}{3}x^3+1.2x^2\right]_0^{0.6}\\[3pt]
        &= \left[(0.6)^4-\dfrac{6.4}{3}(0.6)^3+1.2(0.6)^2\right] - \left[(0)^4-\dfrac{6.4}{3}(0)^3+1.2(0)^2\right] \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.101 \text{ units}^2\hspace{3em} [\text{or by using GDC}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

5.  The outside area of the box consists of a rectangular base and two
    sets of equal rectangular sides. Hence, the total outside area can
    be expressed as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A(x)& = (2-2x)(1.2-2x) + 2(2-2x)x+2(1.2-2x)x\\[6pt]
    & = (2.4-4x-2.4x+4x^2)+(4x-4x^2)+(2.4x-4x^2)\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em} = -4x^2+2.4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    ![020abeb07d1b7975e40f5c9cf1c23db6786ea12a.svg](images/020abeb07d1b7975e40f5c9cf1c23db6786ea12a.svg)

6.  The maximum volume occurs when $x = 0.243$ m (from part (b)(ii)).
    Substituting $x = 0.243$ into $A(x)$ to find the
    corresponding area, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A(0.243) & = -4(0.243)^2+2.4\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em} \approx 2.16\,\text{m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
