# AI432

**Maximum mark: 18**

Jeremy is making an open-top rectangular box as part of a science
project. He makes the box from a rectangular piece of cardboard, $30$ cm
x $18$ cm. To construct the box, Jeremy cuts off squares of side length
$x$ cm from each corner, as shown in the following diagram.

![6d12a40a71db94b14f1df4255e7d32a2e82c8487.svg](images/6d12a40a71db94b14f1df4255e7d32a2e82c8487.svg)

After removing the squares, Jeremy folds up the edges to form the box as
shown.

![3fb118d9dcdb42b91fbeb7a7bb558bfe715937e7.svg](images/3fb118d9dcdb42b91fbeb7a7bb558bfe715937e7.svg)

1.  Write down, in terms of $x$, expressions for the length and width of
    the box. [2 marks]

2.
    1.  State whether $x$ can have a value of $10$. Give a reason for
        your answer.

    2.  Write down the interval for the possible values of $x$.
        [3 marks]

3.  Show that the volume, $V$ cm$^3$, of the box
    is given by [2 marks]
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= 4x^3 - 96x^2 + 540x.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Find $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}x}$.
    [2 marks]

5.  Using your answer from part (d), find the value of $x$ that
    maximizes the volume of the box. [3 marks]

6.  Calculate the maximum volume of the box. [2 marks]

7.  Sketch the graph of $V$, for the possible values of $x$ found in
    part (b)(ii), and $0 \leq V \leq 1000$. Label the maximum point.
    [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The length and width of the gift box can be expressed as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l &= 30 - x - x \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.35em}l = 30 - 2x
    \end{aligned}
    \phantom{\hspace{3cm}}
    \begin{aligned}
    w &= 18 - x - x \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.7em}w = 18 - 2x
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  If $x = 10$, then
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        w &= 18 - 2(10) \\[3pt]
        &= -2
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence, $x$ cannot have a value of $10$ as the width of the box must be positive.

    2.  Since the dimensions of the box must be positive, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        l &> 0  \\[3pt]
        30 - 2x &> 0 \\[3pt]
        30  &> 2x  \\[3pt]
        x  &< 15
        \end{aligned}
        \phantom{\hspace{3cm}}
        \begin{aligned}
        w &> 0 \\[3pt]
        18 - 2x &> 0 \\[3pt]
        18  &> 2x \\[3pt]
        x  &< 9
        \end{aligned}\phantom{\hspace{2.8cm}}
        $$
        Combining
        $x < 15$ and $x < 9$, we get
         $0 < x < 9$
         .

3.  The box has a height of $x$ cm. Using the
    formula for the volume of a rectangular prism, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= wlh \\[3pt]
    &= (18-2x)(30-2x)x \\[3pt]
    &= (540 - 36x - 60x + 4x^2)x \\[3pt]
    &= (540 - 96x + 4x^2)x \\[6pt]
    &= 4x^3 - 96x^2 + 540x \hspace{3em} [\text{shown}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Differentiating $V$ with respect to $x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}x} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x} \left( 4x^3 - 96x^2 + 540x \right) \\[3pt]
    &= 4(3x^2) - 96(2x) + 540  \\[6pt]
    &= 12x^2 - 192x + 540
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Solving the equation $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}x} = 0$ for $x$,
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    12x^2 - 192x + 540 &= 0 \\[3pt]
    x \approx 3.64 \text{ or }  x &\approx 12.4 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Since the possible values of $x$ is $x < 9$ as
    outlined in part (b)(ii), we can reject $x = 12.4$.\
    Hence the volume of the gift box is maximized at
     $x = 3.64\hspace{0.25em} \text{cm}$.

6.  Substituting $x = 3.64$ into our volume expression, $V$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= 4(3.64)^3 - 96(3.64)^2 + 540(3.64) \\[6pt]
    &  \approx 887 \hspace{0.25em} \text{cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
