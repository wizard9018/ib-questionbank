# AI1026

**Maximum mark: 16**

The owner of a bakery has found that the profit obtained from selling $x$ cakes is given by the function

$P(x) = \dfrac{x}{20} \left(600 - \dfrac{x^2}{2k^2}\right)$

where $k$ is a positive constant and $x\geq 0$.

1. Find an expression for $P\,{'}(x)$ in terms of $k$ and $x$. [3 marks]

2. Hence, find the maximum value of $P$ in terms of $k$. [4 marks]

The owner knows that the bakery makes a profit of $$\$873$$ when they sell $30$ cakes.

3. Find the value of $k$. [3 marks]

4. Determine how many cakes the bakery should sell to maximize their profit. [1 mark]

5. Sketch the graph of $P$, labelling the maximum point and $x$-intercepts. [3 marks]

6. Determine the maximum number of cakes the bakery can sell before they start losing money. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Expanding the brackets we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    P(x) &= \dfrac{600 x}{20} - \dfrac{x^3}{40k^2}\\[11pt]

    &= 30x - \dfrac{x^3}{40k^2}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Differentiating term by term we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &P\,{'}(x) = 30 - \dfrac{3x^2}{40k^2}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The maximum value of a function is found where the derivative is equal to zero. Therefore

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    P\,{'}(x) &= 0 \\[11pt]

    30 - \dfrac{3x^2}{40k^2} &= 0 \\[11pt]

    30 &= \dfrac{3x^2}{40k^2} \\[11pt]

    1200k^2 &= 3x^2 \\[11pt]

    400k^2 &= x^2 \\[11pt]

    x & = 20k

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    The maximum value of $P$ occurs when $x = 20k$. Substituting this value into $P(x)$ we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    P(x) &= \dfrac{x}{20} \left(600 - \dfrac{x^2}{2k^2}\right) \\[11pt]

    P(20k) &= \dfrac{20k}{20} \left(600 - \dfrac{400k^2}{2k^2}\right) \\[11pt]

    &= k(600 - 200) \\[11pt]

    &= 400k

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    The maximum value of $P$ is $400k$

3. We have $P(30)=873$. Solving for $k$, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    P(x) &= \dfrac{x}{20} \left(600 - \dfrac{x^2}{2k^2}\right) \\[10pt]

    P(30) &= \dfrac{30}{20} \left(600 - \dfrac{30^2}{2k^2}\right) \\[10pt]

    \dfrac{30}{20} \left(600 - \dfrac{30^2}{2k^2}\right) &= 873 \\[10pt]

    1.5 \left(600 - \dfrac{450}{k^2}\right) &= 873 \\[10pt]

    600 - \dfrac{450}{k^2} &= 582 \\[10pt]

    18 &= \dfrac{450}{k^2} \\[10pt]

    k^2 &= \dfrac{450}{18} \\[10pt]

    k^2 &= 25 \\[10pt]

    &\hspace{-0.55em}\boxed{k = 5}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

4. In part (b) it was found that the maximum occurs when $x=20k$. From part (c) we have $k = 5$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    x &= 20 \times 5 \\[10pt]

    &= 100

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    The bakery should sell $100 \text{ cakes}\,$  to maximise their profit.
