# AI424

**Maximum mark: 6**

A food company produces ice creams in the shape of a cone with a
hemisphere on top. Each ice cream consists of a cone base with height
$h$ and radius $r$, and a hemisphere on top of the cone's base, also
with a radius of $r$. The total surface area of the ice cream cone is in
cm$^2$ and is given by the formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
A = 2 \pi r^2 + \dfrac{60 \pi}{r},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $r$ is the radius of the cone, in cm.

The ice cream designers of the company have been instructed to minimize
the surface area of the cone in order to reduce the melting rate of ice
cream.

1.  Find $\dfrac{dA}{dr}$. [3 marks]

2.  Calculate the value of $r$ that minimizes the total surface area of
    the ice [3 marks] cream cone.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Differentiating $A$ with respect to $r$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}r} &=  \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}r}\left(2 \pi r^2 + \dfrac{60 \pi}{r}\right) \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}r}\left(2 \pi r^2 + 60 \pi r^{-1}\right) \\[3pt]
    &= 2\pi (2r^{1}) + 60 \pi (-1r^{-2}) \\[3pt]
    &= 4\pi r - 60 \pi r^{-2} \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}= 4 \pi r -  \dfrac{60 \pi}{r^2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let's sketch the graph of $A$ to visualise the function.

    ![a344c3545abaaf3c0318613fa09afe2ff396050d.svg](images/a344c3545abaaf3c0318613fa09afe2ff396050d.svg)

    The total surface area $A$ is minimised when
    $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}r} = 0$. This gives
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0 &= 4 \pi r -  \dfrac{60 \pi}{r^2}  \\[3pt]
    0 &= 4\pi r^3 - 60 \pi \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.525em}r = 2.47 \hspace{0.25em}\text{cm} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
