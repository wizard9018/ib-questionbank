# AI431

**Maximum mark: 18**

Charlotte decides to build a storage box with an open top from a
rectangular piece of cardboard, $45$ cm by
$24$ cm. She removes squares with side length
$x$ cm from each corner, as shown in the
following diagram.

![19bc1780d89ee75c969de5b9c719ca50a9cec817.svg](images/19bc1780d89ee75c969de5b9c719ca50a9cec817.svg)

After the corner squares are removed, the remainder of the cardboard is
folded up to form the storage box as shown in the following diagram.

![3fb118d9dcdb42b91fbeb7a7bb558bfe715937e7.svg](images/3fb118d9dcdb42b91fbeb7a7bb558bfe715937e7.svg)

1.  Write down, **in terms of $x$**, the length and the width of the
    storage box. [2 marks]

2.
    1.  State whether $x$ can have a value of $12$. Give a reason for
        your answer.

    2.  Write down the interval for the possible values of $x$.
        [3 marks]

3.  Show that the volume, $V$ cm$^3$, of this
    storage box is given by [2 marks]
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= 4x^3 - 138x^2 + 1080x.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Find $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}x}$.
    [2 marks]

5.  Using your answer from part (d), find the value of $x$ that
    maximizes the volume of the storage box. [3 marks]

6.  Calculate the maximum volume of the storage box. [2 marks]

7.  Sketch the graph of $V$ for the possible values of $x$ found in part
    (b)(ii), and $0 \leq V \leq 2500$. Label the maximum point.
    [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The length and width of the storage box are determined as follows.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l &= 45 - x - x \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.35em}l = 45 - 2x
    \end{aligned}
    \phantom{\hspace{3cm}}
    \begin{aligned}
    w &= 24 - x - x \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.7em}w = 24 - 2x
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  If $x = 12$, then
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        w &= 24 - 2(12) \\[3pt]
        &= 0
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        So $x$ cannot have a value of $12$ as the width of the box must be positive.

    2.  Since the dimensions of the box must be positive, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        l &> 0  \\[3pt]
        45 - 2x &> 0 \\[3pt]
        45  &> 2x  \\[3pt]
        x  &< 22.5
        \end{aligned}
        \phantom{\hspace{3cm}}
        \begin{aligned}
        w &> 0 \\[3pt]
        24 - 2x &> 0 \\[3pt]
        24  &> 2x \\[3pt]
        x  &< 12
        \end{aligned}\phantom{\hspace{2.8cm}}
        $$
        Combining
        $x < 22.5$ and $x < 12$, we get
         $0 < x < 12$
         .

3.  The box has a height of $x$ cm. Using the
    formula for the volume of a rectangular prism, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= wlh \\[3pt]
    &= (24-2x)(45-2x)x \\[3pt]
    &= (1080 - 48x - 90x + 4x^2)x \\[3pt]
    &= (1080 - 138x + 4x^2)x \\[6pt]
    &= 4x^3 - 138x^2 + 1080x \hspace{3em} [\text{shown}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Differentiating $V$ with respect to $x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}x} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x} \left( 4x^3 - 138x^2 + 1080x \right) \\[3pt]
    &= 4(3x^2) - 138(2x) + 1080  \\[6pt]
    &= 12x^2 - 276x + 1080
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Solving the equation $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}x} = 0$ for $x$,
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    12x^2 - 276x + 1080 &= 0 \\[3pt]
    x = 5 \text{ or }  x &= 18 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Since the possible values of $x$ is $x < 12$ as
    shown in part (b)(ii), we reject $x = 18$.\
    Hence the volume of the storage box is maximized at
     $x = 5\hspace{0.25em} \text{cm}$.

6.  Substituting $x = 5$ into our volume expression, $V$ (part (c)), we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= 4(5)^3 - 138(5)^2 + 1080(5) \\[6pt]
    &  = 2450 \hspace{0.25em} \text{cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
