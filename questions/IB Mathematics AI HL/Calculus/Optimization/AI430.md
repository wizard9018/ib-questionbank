# AI430

**Maximum mark: 16**

A greenhouse is made in the shape of a half cylinder. It is constructed
from a galvanized steel frame with a multiwall polycarbonate sheeting.
The steel frame consists of a rectangular base, four semicircular arches
and three further support rods, as shown in the following diagram.

![09f9eb44377ee714e0a529816bc71bab2407becd.svg](images/09f9eb44377ee714e0a529816bc71bab2407becd.svg)

The semicircular arches have radius $r$ and the support rods each have
length $l$.

Let $S$ be the total length of steel used in the frame of the
greenhouse.

1.  Write down an expression for $S$ in terms of $r, l$ and $\pi$.
    [3 marks]

The volume of the greenhouse is $37.5$ m$^3$.

2.  Write down an equation for the volume of the greenhouse in
    terms of $r, l$ and $\pi$.[2 marks]

3.  Show that $S = \dfrac{375}{\pi r^2} + 4r(1 + \pi)$.
    [3 marks]

4.  Find $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}S}{\mathrm{d}r}$. [2 marks]

The greenhouse is designed so that the length of the steel used in the
frame is minimized.

5.  Show that the value of $r$ for which $S$ is a minimum is
    $2.43$ m, correct to $3$ significant figures. [2 marks]

6.  Calculate the value of $l$ for which $S$ is a minimum.
    [2 marks]

7.  Calculate the minimum value of $S$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's find the lengths used in each part of the frame.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[rectangular base]} &= 2(\text{l + w}) \phantom{\hspace{4.5cm}a}\\[3pt]
    &= 2(l + 2r) \\[3pt]
    &= 2l + 4r \\[6pt]
    \text{[$4$ semicircular arches]} &= 4\left(\dfrac 1 2 \right)2\pi r \\[3pt]
    &= 4\pi r \\[8pt]
    \text{[$3$ support rods]} &= 3l
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the total length of steel can be expressed
    as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S &= 2l + 4r + 4\pi r + 3l \\[6pt]
    &= 5l + 4r + 4\pi r
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the volume of a cylinder formula $V = \pi r^2 h$, we get
    $$
    \hspace{-2.8cm}\dfrac{1}{2}\pi r^2 l = 37.5
    $$

3.  Rearranging our expression for the volume (part (b)) to be in terms
    of $l$, we get
    $$
    l = \dfrac{75}{\pi r^2}

    $$
    Substituting this into
    the expression for $S$ from part (a), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S &= 5\left(\dfrac{75}{\pi r^2}\right) + 4r + 4\pi r \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{375}{\pi r^2} + 4r(1 + \pi)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Differentiating $S$ with respect to $r$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}S}{\mathrm{d}r} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}r} \left(\dfrac{375}{\pi r^2} + 4r(1 + \pi) \right) \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}r} \left(\dfrac{375}{\pi} r^{-2} + 4(1 + \pi)r \right) \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{375}{\pi} (-2r^{-3}) + 4(1 + \pi)(1r^{0})  \\[6pt]
    &= -\dfrac{750}{\pi r^3} + 4(1 + \pi)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  The graph of $S$, in metres, for a given radius $r$, is shown below

    ![884bb83929006782110b465794643888377de32f.svg](images/884bb83929006782110b465794643888377de32f.svg)

    $S$ is a minimum when solving the equation
    $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}S}{\mathrm{d}r} = 0$ for $r$. Hence, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -\dfrac{750}{\pi r^3} + 4(1 + \pi) &= 0 \\[3pt]
    &\hspace{-0.6em}r \approx 2.43 \text{\hspace{0.2em}m} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

6.  Substituting $r = 2.43$ into $l = \dfrac{75}{\pi r^2}$ (from part
    (c)), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.275em} l &= \dfrac{75}{\pi (2.43)^2} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 4.04 \text{\hspace{0.2em}m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

7.  Using the expression found in part (a), the minimum value of $S$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_{\text{min}} &= 5(4.04) + 4(2.43) + 4\pi(2.43) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 60.5 \text{\hspace{0.2em}m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
