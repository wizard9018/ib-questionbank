# AI607

**Maximum mark: 6**

The export fee per tonne of apples, $E$ (in thousand of dollars), when
$x$ tonnes of apples are exported is given by

$$
E(x)=5x+\dfrac{147}{x}, \hspace{0.5em} x>0.
$$

1.  Find an expression for $E'(x)$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $x$ for which the export fee per tonne is a
    minimum.[2 marks]

3.  Find the value of the minimum export fee.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Taking the derivative of $E$ with respect to $x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    E'(x) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x} \left[5x+\dfrac{147}{x}\right] \rule[-5.5mm]{0pt}{5mm}\\
    &\hspace{-2em}E'(x) = 5-\dfrac{147}{x^2} \rule[0mm]{0pt}{7.5mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We can find the local minimum point of $E$ by letting $E'(x) = 0$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    E'(x) &= 0 \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    5-\dfrac{147}{x^2} &= 0 \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    x &= \sqrt{\dfrac{147}{5}}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\
    &\hspace{-2em}x \approx 5.42 \rule[0mm]{0pt}{6.5mm} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We can find the minimum export fee by calculating
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    E(5.42217) &\approx 54.2 \hspace{3em} [\text{or found on graph}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $\text{Therefore, the minimum export fee}\hspace{0.05em} \approx 54\hspace{0.15em}200\text{ dollars}$
