# AI1221

**Maximum mark: 7**

A teacher investigates whether attending after-school revision sessions helps improve students’ test performance.

The scores (out of 100) from a recent test are shown below for the students who attended after-school revision, and for those who did not.

A two-sample $t$-test was performed using technology. The $p$-value for the test was given as $0.000267$, correct to 3 significant figures.

1. Suggest one assumption that is required in order for this test to be valid.[1 mark]

2. State whether a one-tailed or two-tailed test is appropriate in this context. Justify your answer. [2 marks]

3. Write down the null and alternate hypotheses for this test.[2 marks]

4. State the conclusion of the test at the 1\% significance level. Justify your answer.[2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. For the test to be valid, one assumption required is

    The samples are drawn from populations that are approximately normally distributed (with the same variance).

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Since the teacher is investigating specifically for improvement, not just a difference, then

    A one-tailed test is appropriate because the teacher is testing for improvement.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{A2}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. Null and alternate hypotheses for this test are

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \text{H}_0 &\colon \mu_1 = \mu_2 && \text{(no difference in mean test scores)} \\

    \text{H}_1 &\colon \mu_1 > \mu_2 && \text{(students who attended revision score higher)}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \boxed{\text{H}_0\!:\text{ }    \mu_1 = \mu_2, \quad \text{H}_1\!:\text{ }   \mu_1 > \mu_2} \tag*{\textbf{A1A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

4. Given in the question that

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    p &= 0.000267 \\[4pt]

    0.000267 &< 0.01 \\ 

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Since the $p$-value is less than the 1\% significance level, there is sufficient evidence to reject the null hypothesis. Students who attend revision are more likely to score higher.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{R1A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
