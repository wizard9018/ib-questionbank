# AI1121

**Maximum mark: 6**

A music producer wonders how the average duration of songs has changed over the decade from 2010 to 2020.

To compare the average length, he takes a random sample of the most popular songs in 2010 and 2020.

The following table shows the data in seconds for the two samples.

![ai1121q](images/ai1121q.png)

The producer is interested to see whether the mean length of the songs in these two groups is significantly different.

At the $5\%$ level of significance, a $t$-test was used to compare the means.

Assume that the data is normally distributed and the standard deviations between the two groups are equal.

1.  1. State the null hypothesis.

    2. State the alternative hypothesis. [2 marks]

2.  Determine the $p$-value for this test. [2 marks]

3.  State and justify the conclusion of the test in the context of the question. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Let $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ be the mean length of the songs released in 2010 and 2020, respectively. We are using the $t$-test to compare the means of two groups. Hence

    1. $H_0: \mu_1 = \mu_2 $
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2. $H_1: \mu_1 \neq \mu_2 $
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  $p\text{-value} \approx 0.0541$ $\hspace{2em}$ [by using G.D.C.]

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A2}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Recall, we reject the null hypothesis $H_0$ if $p$-value is less than the significance level. In this case, we are given that the significance level is $0. 05$.

    Since the $p$- value is greater than the significance level $(0.0541 > 0.05)$, there is insufficient evidence to reject the null hypothesis.

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We therefore conclude that there is no evidence that the average duration of the songs has changed over the decade.

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
