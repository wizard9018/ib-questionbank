# AI1076

**Maximum mark: 5**

A pharmaceutical company has developed a new drug which they claim can lower blood sugar levels in diabetic patients.

Dr. Brown wants to test this claim. She did this by splitting a group of diabetes patients into two groups.

Group A, with 7 participants, were given the old drug and group B, with 8 participants, were given the new drug.

Dr. Brown found the blood sugar levels (mg/dL) for each patient after taking a course of the drug. The data is shown in the table below.

![ai1076](images/ai1076.PNG)

She decided to conduct a two-sampled $t$-test at a $10$\% significance level to decide if the new drug is better at **reducing** blood sugar levels than the old drug.

The null hypothesis is $H_0: \mu_A = \mu_B$, where $\mu_A$ is the mean blood sugar level of group A patients and $\mu_B$ is the mean blood sugar level of group B patients.

1. State the alternate hypothesis for the test. [1 mark]

2. Find the $p$-value for the test. [2 marks]

Dr. Brown concludes that there is sufficient evidence to reject the null hypothesis.

3. State whether Dr. Brown is correct and give a reason for your answer. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Dr. Brown wants to know if $\mu_B$ is less than $\mu_A$ i.e. it is more effective at reducing blood sugar level.
Therefore the alternate hypothesis is $H_1: \mu_A > \mu_B$

2. The $p$-value is $0.119$ [by using G.D.C.]

3. $\text{Dr. Brown is incorrect. The p-value is larger than the significance level,}$ this means the set of results are not unlikely enough to suggest the null hypothesis should be rejected.
