# AI595

**Maximum mark: 6**

It is believed that smokers will gain weight after quitting smoking
for one month. To test this hypothesis, Claire asks five smokers chosen
at random to give up smoking for a month. Their weights, in kilograms,
were recorded before and after quitting smoking.

![497f5270843c2b06b07ed885427ca58fe8ce77f4.svg](images/497f5270843c2b06b07ed885427ca58fe8ce77f4.svg)

1.  Write down the null and alternative hypotheses. [2 marks]

2.  Find the $p$-value of her test. [2 marks]

3.  State the conclusion to the test at the $5\%$ significance level.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Let $D$ be the difference between the weights before and after quitting smoking. We conduct a paired $t$-test in which the hypotheses are

    

    $\boxed{\text{H}_0:  \mu_D= 0} $

    

    $\boxed{\text{H}_1: \mu_D > 0}$

2. The difference $D$ between the weights before and after quitting smoking are:

    ![AI595a](images/AI595a.PNG)

    Given that the population standard deviation is unknown, using the GDC, we conduct a $t$-test and obtain

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        &\boxed{p \text{-value} = 0.399}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. The $p$-value is greater than the significance level.

    Since $0.399 > 0.05$, we do not reject the null hypothesis. Therefore, there is insufficient evidence to conclude that smokers will gain weight after quitting smoking for one month at the $5\%$ significance level.
