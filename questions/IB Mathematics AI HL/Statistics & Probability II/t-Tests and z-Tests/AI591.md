# AI591

**Maximum mark: 7**

A restaurant's website claims that the average wait time for a table
without a reservation during dinner hours is $18.5$ minutes. It is known
that this wait time, $X$, is normally distributed with a standard
deviation of $2.8$ minutes.

Bella is a regular guest of this restaurant. She believes that the
average wait time without a reservation is longer than what the
restaurant claims and so she conducts a test by recording the wait time
of her next five visits. The results of the recordings are shown in the
table below.

![09b572951c30aef217efb611b5532c1675614c3e.svg](images/09b572951c30aef217efb611b5532c1675614c3e.svg)

1.  For Bella's test, state
    1.  the null hypothesis H$_0$,

    2.  the alternative hypothesis H$_1$. [2 marks]

2.  Determine the $p$-value of Bella's test. [2 marks]

3.  Bella decides to validate the claim using a $10\%$ level of
    significance.

    1.  state the conclusion of Bella's test,
    2.  find the critical region of $z$-statistic for this test.
        [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  By letting $\mu$ be the mean wait time, Bella is testing

    1.
        $\text{H}_0: \mu = 18.5$
    2.
        $\text{H}_1: \mu > 18.5$

2.  Using GDC, we obtain the statistic $z = 0.431$ and
     $p \text{-value} = 0.333$

3.
    1.

        Since $p$-value $= 0.333 > 0.1$, Bella should not reject the null hypothesis. Therefore, there is insufficient evidence (at a $10\%$ level of significance) for Bella to conclude that the mean wait time is longer than $18.5$ minutes.

    2.  At a $10\%$ significance level, $z_\text{crit} = 1.28$ for a
        right-tailed test. Hence the critical region
        is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        &Z > 1.28
        \end{aligned}
        $$
