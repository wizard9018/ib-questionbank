# AI590

**Maximum mark: 6**

The latest model of laptop released from Microsoft is stated to have a
battery life of 8 hours, with a standard deviation of $0.5$ hours
(battery life of all laptops follow a normal distribution). TechCrunch,
a technology news publication, decided to test the accuracy of this
claim and conducted a study of $36$ of these laptops and found the mean
to battery life to be $7.8$ hours. The lead journalist from TechCrunch
decided to assess the validity of the claim using a 5% level of
significance.

1.  State
    1.  the null hypothesis H$_0$,

    2.  the alternative hypothesis H$_1$. [2 marks]

2.
    1.  Determine the $p$-value for the test.

    2.  State the conclusion of the test. [2 marks]

3.  Find the critical region for this test and hence verify your result
    in part (b)(ii). [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Letting $\mu$ be the population mean of the battery life of these
    laptops, we are testing

    1.
        $\text{H}_0: \mu = 8$

    2.
        $\text{H}_1: \mu < 8$

2.
    1.  Using GDC, we obtain the statistic $z = -2.40$ and
         $p \text{-value} = 0.00820$
    2.

        Since the $p$-value $= 0.00820 < 0.05$, we reject the null hypothesis. There is sufficient evidence to conclude that the mean battery life of these laptops is less than $8$ hours.

3.  At a $5\%$ significance level, $z_\text{crit} = - 1.64$ for a
    left-tailed test. Hence the critical region is

    $$
    Z < -1.64
    $$

    From part (c)(i) we obtained $z = -2.40$. Hence, this value belongs to the critical region and thus confirms the result of the test.
