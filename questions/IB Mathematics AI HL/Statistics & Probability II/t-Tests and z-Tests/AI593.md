# AI593

**Maximum mark: 6**

An investigation is undertaken into the annual incomes of two IT companies. A sample of 14 employees is taken from company A and a sample of 12 employees is taken from company B.

![AI593a](images/AI593a.PNG)

To find out if there is any difference between the income levels of these two companies, a $t$-test is conducted at the 5\% significance level.

It can be assumed that the annual incomes at each company are normally distributed with the same variance.

1.  Write down the null and alternative hypotheses. [2 marks]

2.  Find the $p$-value of this test. [2 marks]

3.  State, with a reason, the conclusion to the test.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ be the mean incomes at company A and company B respectively. We are testing

    $\text{H}_0: \mu_1 = \mu_2$

     $\text{H}_1: \mu_1 \neq \mu_2$

2.  Since the population variances are assumed to be equal, we use a pooled 2-Sample $t$-test in this case. Using the GDC we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &p \text{-value} = 0.969
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The $p$-value is greater than the significance level.

    Since $0.969 > 0.05$, we do not reject the null hypothesis. There is insufficient evidence to conclude that there is a difference between the income levels of these two teams at the $5\%$ significance level.
