# AI378

**Maximum mark: 6**

Mustafa is studying the weights of newborns in two groups of mothers, a
group who smoke cigarettes and a group who do not. His data is shown in
the table below.

![09b6d8e28bda3debc66035c54b0e84e50495ef57.svg](images/09b6d8e28bda3debc66035c54b0e84e50495ef57.svg)

Mustafa is interested to see whether the mean weights of newborns in
these two groups are significantly different. At the $10\%$ level of
significance, a $t$-test was used to compare the means. The data is
assumed to be normally distributed and the standard deviations are equal
between the two groups.

1.
    1.  State the null hypothesis.

    2.  State the alternative hypothesis. [2 marks]

2.  Determine the $p$-value for this test. [2 marks]

3.  State, giving a reason, whether Mustafa should accept the null
    hypothesis. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ be the mean weights of the newborns in the
    group of mothers who smoke cigarettes and the group who do not,
    respectively. We are using the $t$-test to compare the means of two
    groups. Hence,

    1.
        $H_0: \mu_1 - \mu_2 = 0$

    2.
        $H_1: \mu_1 - \mu_2 \neq 0$

2.
    $p\text{-value} \approx 0.0434$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

3.  Recall we reject the null hypothesis $H_0$ if $p$-value is less than
    the significance level. In this case, we are given that the
    significance level is $0.1$.

    Since $p$-value $= 0.0434 < 0.1$, there is sufficient evidence for Mustafa to reject the null hypothesis at the $10\%$ level of significance. We therefore reject that there is no difference in the mean weights of the babies
