# AI377

**Maximum mark: 6**

Susan has read a scientific paper which states that there is a
significant difference in body temperature between non-pregnant women
and pregnant women. She is interested in verifying this result and
decides to measures the body temperature of two groups: a group of $7$
non-pregnant women and a group of $8$ pregnant women at weeks $8$ to
$12$ of their pregnancy. The results are shown below.

![5088af5c79c2275ae9de7cb47f2e302966e3356c.svg](images/5088af5c79c2275ae9de7cb47f2e302966e3356c.svg)

At the $10\%$ level of significance, a $t$-test was used to compare the
means of two groups. The data is assumed to be normally distributed and
the standard deviations are equal between the two groups.

1.
    1.  State the null hypothesis.

    2.  State the alternative hypothesis. [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the $p$-value for this test. [2 marks]

3.  State, giving a reason, whether Susan's conclusion agrees with the
    finding of [2 marks] the scientific paper.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ be the mean body temperatures of
    non-pregnant women and pregnant women, respectively. We are using
    the $t$-test to compare the means of two groups. Hence,

    1.
        $H_0: \mu_1 - \mu_2 = 0$

    2.
        $H_1: \mu_1 - \mu_2 \neq 0$

2.
    $p\text{-value} \approx 0.0196$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

3.  Recall that we reject the null hypothesis $H_0$ if $p$-value is less
    than the significance level. In this case, we are given that the
    significance level is $0.1$.

    Since $p$-value $= 0.0196 < 0.1$, we reject $H_0$. Thus, Susan's conclusion does agree with the finding of the scientific paper at a significance level of $10\%$.
