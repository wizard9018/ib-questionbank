# AI594

**Maximum mark: 6**

Dr Eric wants to find out whether there is a difference in terms of age between female and male patients diagnosed with Brugada syndrome, a genetic disorder in which the electrical activity within the heart is abnormal. A group of each gender is chosen at random with their ages shown in the table below.

![AI594a](images/AI594a.PNG)

Dr Eric performs a $t$-test at the 10\% significance level to determine whether males are diagnosed at a younger age than females.

It can be assumed that the ages of males and females are normally distributed with the same variance.

1.  Write down the null and alternative hypotheses. [2 marks]

2.  Find the $p$-value of this test. [2 marks]

3.  State, with a reason, the conclusion to the test.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ be the mean ages of male and female
    patients, respectively. We are testing

    $\text{H}_0: \mu_1 = \mu_2$

    $\text{H}_1: \mu_1 < \mu_2$

2.  Since the population variances are assumed to be equal, we use a 2-Sample $t$-test in this case. Using the GDC we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &p \text{-value} = 0.0949
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The $p$-value is less than the significance level.

    Since $0.0949 < 0.10$, we reject the null hypothesis. There is significant evidence at the $10\%$ significance level that males are diagnosed at a younger age than females.
