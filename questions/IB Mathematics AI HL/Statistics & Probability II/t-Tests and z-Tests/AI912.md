# AI912

**Maximum mark: 6**

A study was conducted looking at if zinc deficiency contributes to hair
loss in male adults. The zinc concentration, measured in $\mu$g/dl, in
the hair strands of ten male adults experiencing hair loss and ten that
are not were recorded. The data from this sample is shown in the
following table.

![119eed94c6cf064f8b72a453d473c55f0efc3209.svg](images/119eed94c6cf064f8b72a453d473c55f0efc3209.svg)

The aim of the study was to determine if male adults experiencing hair
loss have less zinc concentration in their hair strands than male adults
who are not experiencing hair loss. A $t$-test was performed at a $5\%$
significance level. It is assumed that the zinc concentrations in the
two groups are normally distributed and have equal variances.

1.  Write down the null and alternative hypotheses for this test.
    [2 marks]

2.  Find the $p$-value for this test. [2 marks]

3.  State the conclusion to the test. Justify your answer. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ be the mean zinc concentration in hair
    strands in male adults who experience hair loss, and those who
    don't, respectively. Since the study aims to determine if those
    experiencing hair loss have a lower zinc concentration, our
    hypotheses are
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    & H_0: \mu_1 - \mu_2 = 0 \\[10pt]
    &H_1: \mu_1 - \mu_2 < 0
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    $p\text{-value} \approx 0.0326$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

3.  Recall that we reject $H_0$ if the $p$-value is less than the
    significance level. In this test, the significance level is set at
    $0.05$.

    Since the $p$-value $= 0.0326 < 0.05$, we reject the null hypothesis. Therefore, there is sufficient evidence to conclude that the zinc concentration in hair strands of male adults experiencing hair loss is less than male adults not experiencing hair loss.
