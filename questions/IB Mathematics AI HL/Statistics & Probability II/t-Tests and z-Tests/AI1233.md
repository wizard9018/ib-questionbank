# AI1233

**Maximum mark: 16**

An IB school surveys all 100 of its students from Year 11 and all 100 of its students from Year 12 to study the relationship between co-curricular club participation and homework habits. Each student reports

- number of co-curricular clubs attended per week

- frequency of homework completion as either Always, Sometimes, or Rarely

The following table shows the number of clubs attended each week by the ten students in class 11A and the ten students in class 12A.

**Table 1 - Clubs Attended per Week**

| $\text{Class}$ | $\text{Clubs Attended per Week}$ |

| :--- | :---: |

| 11A | 2, 2, 3, 2, 3, 2, 2, 3, 2, 3 |

| 12A | 3, 3, 4, 2, 4, 2, 3, 3, 4, 3 |

1. Calculate the mean and standard deviation of the number of clubs attended per week for the ten students in class 11A, and the ten students in class 12A.[4 marks]

The school decides to perform a $t$-test at the 1\% significance level to investigate whether Year 12 students participate in more clubs than Year 11 students.

2. 1. State the null and alternate hypotheses for the test.

    2. Find the $p$-value for the test.

    3. State your conclusion to the test and justify your answer.[6 marks]

The following table shows the homework frequency for all 100 Year 11 and all 100 Year 12 students.

| $\text{Frequency}$ | $\text{Year 11}$ | $\text{Year 12}$ |

| :--- | :---: | :---: |

| Always | 60 | 30 |

| Sometimes | 30 | 50 |

| Rarely | 10 | 20 |

The school wants to performs a $\chi^2$ test for independence at the 10\% significance level to determine whether there is an association between year group and homework completion frequency.

3. 1. State the null and alternative hypotheses for the test.

    2. Find the $p$-value for the test.

    3. State your conclusion to the test and justify your answer.[6 marks]
