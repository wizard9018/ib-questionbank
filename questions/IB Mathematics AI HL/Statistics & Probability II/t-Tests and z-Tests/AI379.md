# AI379

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two groups of the same elementary mathematics course are taught by two
different teachers: Teacher A & Teacher B. The final grades out of 100
of the students were recorded as follows:

![27f3c2ce58378a13804773696f6ba2e979e92786.svg](images/27f3c2ce58378a13804773696f6ba2e979e92786.svg)

Assuming that the data is normally distributed and the standard
deviations are equal between the two groups, conduct a $t$-test to see
whether there is a significant difference in the average grades given by
these two teachers. Use a $0.05$ level of significance.

1.
    1.  State the null hypothesis.

    2.  State the alternative hypothesis. [2 marks]

2.  Find the $t$-statistic and $p$-value for this test. [2 marks]

3.  Write down the conclusion to the test. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ be the average scores of the students in the
    sections taught by teachers A and B, respectively. We are using the
    $t$-test to compare the average grades of the students in these two
    groups. Hence,

    1.
        $H_0: \mu_1 - \mu_2 = 0$

    2.
        $H_1: \mu_1 - \mu_2 \neq 0$

2.
    $t\text{-statistic} \approx 0.61$

    $p\text{-value} \approx 0.547$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

3.  The rejection rule is that we reject the null hypothesis $H_0$ if
    $p$-value is less than the significance level. In this case, the
    significance level is given as $0.05$.

    Since $p$-value $= 0.548 > 0.05$, we do not reject the null hypothesis. Therefore, there is insufficient evidence to conclude that the average grades of these two groups are different at the $5\%$ level of significance.
