# AI1180

**Maximum mark: 6**

A company making lithium-ion batteries believe that their new ``ARC'' technology has improved the life of their batteries. In order to test this, they take 8 of their original batteries (L) and 8 of their new batteries (A) and record the time to failure for each in minutes. The results are shown in the following table.

![1180](images/1180.JPG)

They wish to determine, at the $5\%$ significance level, whether there is any evidence that the new batteries have a longer time to failure than the original batteries.

 

It can be assumed that the time to failure of both the original batteries and the new ARC technology batteries follows a normal distribution with the same variance.

1. State the null and alternate hypotheses.     [2 marks]

2. Find the $p$-value for the test.     [2 marks]

3. State the conclusion to the test. Justify your answer.    [2 marks]
