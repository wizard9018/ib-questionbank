# AI380

**Maximum mark: 6**

McDonald's, the global fast-food restaurant chain, observed that the
wait time for customers ordering was increasing, which is not good for
customer satisfaction and therefore their business. They decided to
trial a self-ordering kiosk at one of their restaurants to resolve this
issue. The table below shows the waiting time, in minutes, of $20$
customers chosen by random, $10$ before and $10$ after the introduction
of the self-ordering kiosk.

![580032584d7b7ef3f27c9b18c6253b6ee12e3a53.svg](images/580032584d7b7ef3f27c9b18c6253b6ee12e3a53.svg)

The trial supervisor was interested to understand whether the kiosk
reduced the waiting time during the ordering process and performed a
$t$-test using a $5\%$ level of significance.

1.  Write down
    1.  the null hypothesis;

    2.  the alternative hypothesis. [2 marks]

2.  Find the $t$-statistic and $p$-value for this $t$-test. [2 marks]

3.  State the conclusion to the test and give a reason for your answer.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ be the waiting time, in minutes, of
    customers at the restaurant before and after the kiosk equipment,
    respectively. Since the trial supervisor is interested to see the
    kiosk equipment has reduced the waiting time, the test hypotheses
    are as follows.

    1.
        $H_0: \mu_1 - \mu_2 = 0$

    2.
        $H_1: \mu_1 - \mu_2 > 0$

2.
    $t\text{-statistic} \approx 2.96$

    $p\text{-value} \approx 0.00422$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

3.  Recall that we reject the null hypothesis if the $p$-value is less
    than the significance level. In this case, the significance level is
    given as $0.05$.

    Since $p$-value $= 0.00422 < 0.05$, we reject the null hypothesis. Therefore, there is sufficient evidence to conclude that the waiting time during the ordering process has been reduced after the self-ordering kiosk equipment at the $5\%$ level of significance.
