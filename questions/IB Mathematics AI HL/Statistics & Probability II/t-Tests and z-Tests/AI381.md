# AI381

**Maximum mark: 6**

At a horticultural lab, the sizes of Malus Domestica (a type of apple)
are studied to determine the effectiveness of the new fertilizer, B, in
increasing the sizes of apples compared with the original fertilizer, A.
In a $200$-m$^2$ yard, $20$ apple trees are planted in two separated
areas, $10$ fertilized with A and $10$ fertilized with B.

At the time of harvesting, the researchers at the lab collected apples
at random from each tree and measured their diameters in cm. The data
are shown below:

![3229a20c5d18bb5ccf5b09ad3fc35abe4d74603a.svg](images/3229a20c5d18bb5ccf5b09ad3fc35abe4d74603a.svg)

A $t$-test is performed using a $5\%$ level of significance.

1.  Write down
    1.  the null hypothesis;

    2.  the alternative hypothesis. [2 marks]

2.  Find the $t$-statistic and $p$-value for this $t$-test. [2 marks]

3.  State the conclusion to the test and give a reason for your answer.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $\mu_1$ and $\mu_2$ be the diameters of the apples collected
    from the trees fertilized with A and B, respectively. Since the
    researchers are interested to see whether the new fertilizer, B,
    produces larger apple sizes, the test hypotheses are as follows.

    1.
        $H_0: \mu_1 - \mu_2 = 0$

    2.
        $H_1: \mu_1 - \mu_2 < 0$

2.
    $t\text{-statistic} \approx -2.43$

    $p\text{-value} \approx 0.0129$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

3.  Recall that we reject the null hypothesis if the $p$-value is less
    than the significance level. In this case, the significance level is
    given as $0.05$.

    Since $p$-value $= 0.0129 < 0.05$, we reject the null hypothesis. Therefore, at the $5\%$ level of significance, there is sufficient evidence to conclude that the new fertilizer yields larger size for this apple variety.
