# AI906

**Maximum mark: 6**

Munir owns and operates a pearl farm, where he grows and harvests South
Sea pearls and Tahitian pearls. Munir believes the mean size of South
Sea pearls is greater than the mean size of Tahitian pearls.

Munir selects a random sample of each type of pearl and measures their
diameters, in millimetres. The results of these samples are shown in the
table below.

![6d4184636ab8f7e4b3e4a34db678273993d43589.svg](images/6d4184636ab8f7e4b3e4a34db678273993d43589.svg)

Munir performs a $t$-test at a $10\%$ level of significance to validate
his belief. It is assumed that the sizes of pearls are normally
distributed with equal variances.

1.  State, in words, the null and alternative hypotheses. [2 marks]

2.  Find the $p$-value for the test. [2 marks]

3.  State whether the conclusion of the test supports Munir's belief.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Munir is testing whether the mean size of South Sea pearls is
    greater than the mean size of Tahitian pearls. Hence,

    H$_0$: The mean size of South Sea pearls is equal to the mean size of Tahitian pearls.

    H$_1$: The mean size of South Sea pearls is greater than the mean size of Tahitian pearls.

2.
    $p\text{-value} \approx 0.12$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

3.  Recall we reject the H$_0$ if the $p$-value is less than the
    significance level. In this case, we are given a significance level
    of $0.1$.

    Since the $p$-value $= 0.120 > 0.1$, there is insufficient evidence for Munir to reject the null hypothesis at a $10\%$ level of significance. Therefore, the result of the test does not support Munir's belief.
