# AI1132

**Maximum mark: 6**

Greta works in the HR department of a large department store.

She is analysing the results of a recent customer survey and is trying to find a link between customer satisfaction, on a scale from $1$ to $10$, and the corresponding amount of money the customer spent, in dollars.

She takes a sample of $10$ customers and the results are shown below.

![q1132v1](images/q1132v1.png)

Greta can't decide whether to calculate Spearman's rank correlation coefficient, $r_s$, or Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient, $r_p$.

1.  State one mathematical reason why Greta might choose to calculate $r_s$. [1 mark]

She decides to calculate $r_s$.

2.  Complete the table of ranks [2 marks]

![q1132v2](images/q1132v2.png)

3. Calculate $r_s$. [2 marks]

After finding $r_s$, Greta then finds $r_p$ to be $0.918$.

4.  Comment on why the values of $r_p$ and $r_s$ are similar. [1 mark]
