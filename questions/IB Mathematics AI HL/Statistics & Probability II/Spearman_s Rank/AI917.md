# AI917

**Maximum mark: 16**

The London National Theatre is presenting the famous Shakespearean play
Hamlet. Matthew is interested in the age range of the audience attendees
and collects data by standing at the entrance and sampling every fifth
audience member as they enter the theatre. Using this data, he
constructs a box-and-whisker diagram to represent the data, as shown
below.

![214e13d2bd404f805b93b34f9ffdc8ddf7466491.svg](images/214e13d2bd404f805b93b34f9ffdc8ddf7466491.svg)

1.  State the sampling method Matthew used in his study. [1 mark]

2.  Write down the median age of the sample. [1 mark]

3.  Calculate the interquartile range. [2 marks]

The youngest audience member sampled, Rosie, is just $15$ years old.
Matthew believes that Rosie is an outlier and is considering excluding
her from the sample data set.

4.  Determine if Matthew is correct. Give a reason to your
    answer. [2 marks]

When asking their ages, Matthew also asks how many other Shakespearean
plays they have watched in their lifetime ($y$). The following scatter
diagram shows this data, paired with their age ($x$).

![fd8440d3d0deba7fb541ed341c2c0809e1c04f77.svg](images/fd8440d3d0deba7fb541ed341c2c0809e1c04f77.svg)

5.  Describe the correlation between the two variables.
    [1 mark]

Matthew used the data to calculate the equation of the regression line
$y$ on $x$ as
$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= 0.458x - 5.76
\end{aligned}
$$
Matthew used this equation to
estimate the number of Shakespearean plays a $65$ year old audience
attendee would have previously watched.

6.
    1.  Determine Matthew's estimate.

    2.  State whether it is valid to use the line of regression equation
        for this estimate. Give a reason for your answer. [4 marks]

Matthew, an avid Shakespearean fan, wants to understand if there is a
relationship between his favourite Shakespearean plays and their length,
measured by total run time in minutes. He creates a table, shown below,
and ranks $8$ Shakespearean plays (A-H), their run time length, and his
rating out of all the Shakespearean plays he has seen ($1$ being his
favourite).

![ae257aea9fde7c3eb6cfc7a29660444d53dc7a3e.svg](images/ae257aea9fde7c3eb6cfc7a29660444d53dc7a3e.svg)

For this analysis, Matthew uses the Spearman's rank correlation
coefficient, $r_s$ to understand the relationship between the variables.
A rank table is created.

![b2fcf75c081643d05aab07dafff1f32112d203a9.svg](images/b2fcf75c081643d05aab07dafff1f32112d203a9.svg)

8.
    1.  Calculate the value of $r_s$.

    2.  Interpret the result.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\text{Systematic sampling}$

2.  Let's label the five summary numbers on the given box-and-whisker
    diagram.

    ![84919e7d717d429dcd580e1317ea23bb22242003.svg](images/84919e7d717d429dcd580e1317ea23bb22242003.svg)

    Hence,
     $ \text{median = \(32\) years old}$

3.  From the labelled box-and-whisker diagram, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    Q_1 &= 28 \hspace{2em}\\[6pt]
    Q_3 &= 38
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the interquartile range is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.25em} \text{IQR} &= Q_3 - Q_1 \\[6pt]
    &= 38 - 28 \\[8pt]
    & = 10 \text{ years}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  A data value is identified as an outlier if it falls outside the
    interval
    $(Q_1 - 1.5 \times \text{IQR}, \, Q_3 + 1.5 \times \text{IQR})$.
    Testing the lower interval, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Lower limit} &= Q_1 - 1.5 \times \text{IQR} \\[6pt]
    &= 28 - 1.5(10) \\[6pt]
    &= 13 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Since $15$ years old is within the interval, Rosie is not considered an outlier. Thus, Matthew's belief is incorrect.

5.
    $\text{Strong, positive correlation}$

6.
    1.  Evaluating $y$ for $x = 65$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        y &= 0.458(65) - 5.76 \\[8pt]
        &\approx 24
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.

        It is not valid to use the line of regression equation to estimate the value, as $x = 65$ is outside the data range used to generate the equation (extrapolation).
