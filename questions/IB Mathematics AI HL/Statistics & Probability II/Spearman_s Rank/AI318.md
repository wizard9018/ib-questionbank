# AI318

**Maximum mark: 17**

The Elite Traveller and Condé Nast Traveller are two magazines that
review and rank the best restaurants in Paris. Based on different sets
of criteria, scores out of $10$ are assigned to $8$ restaurants in Paris
(labelled A to H). The raw data is collated in the following table.

![d1035194399985c232b56ad8f6022a02ac901dae.svg](images/d1035194399985c232b56ad8f6022a02ac901dae.svg)

1.
    1.  Find the Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient , $r$,
        for this data.

    2.  Using the value of $r$, interpret the relationship between the
        Elite Traveller's score and the Condé Nast Traveller' score.
        [4 marks]

2.  Find the equation of the line of regression, $y$ on $x$. [2 marks]

3.
    1.  Use your regression equation from **part (b)** to estimate the
        Condé Nast Traveller's score when the Elite Traveller awarded a
        perfect $10$.

    2.  State whether this estimate is reliable. Justify your answer.
        [4 marks]

4.  Complete the two empty rank rows in the table above. [2 marks]

5.
    1.  Find the value of the Spearman's rank correlation coeffcient,
        $r_s$.

    2.  Comment on the result obtained for $r_s$. [4 marks]

The Director of the Elite Traveller Magazine believes that the score for
restaurant E was too high and decreased the score from $9.7$ to $9.2$.

6.  Explain why the value of the Spearman's rank correlation
    coefficient $r_s$ does not change. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $r \approx 0.961$
        \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    2.

        Since the correlation coefficient is positive and close to $1$, there is a very strong positive correlation between the Elite Traveller's score and Condé Nast Traveller' score.

2.
    $y = 0.685x + 2.38$
    \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

3.
    1.  Substituting $x = 10$ into the equation of the regression line,
        we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        y &= 0.685(10) + 2.38 \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 9.23
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.

        This estimate is not reliable as $x=10$ falls out of the data range $[5.6, 9.7]$.

4.  Rearranging the scores of these restaurants in descending order, we
    obtain their ranks for each magazine in the following table.

    ![1d7a143db77f00d281597bd952aa9a86372add53.svg](images/1d7a143db77f00d281597bd952aa9a86372add53.svg)

5.
    1.
        $r_s \approx 0.97$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    2.

        Since the Spearman's rank correlation coefficient is positive and close to $1$, both the Elite Traveller and Condé Nast Traveller Magazines strongly agree on the ranking of the restaurants.

6.

    Although the score for the restaurant E decreased from $9.7$ to $9.2$, the rank of this restaurant still remain the same (remains 1) and hence the Spearman's rank correlation coefficient does not change.
