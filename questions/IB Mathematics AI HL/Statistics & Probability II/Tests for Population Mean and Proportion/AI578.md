# AI578

**Maximum mark: 12**

An automobile company is reviewing two potential advertising campaigns,
A and B, for the launch of their new vehicle. The head of marketing of
the company decides to conduct a poll of $16$ employees within the
company to determine if campaign A is preferred over campaign B at a
$5\%$ significance level.

Let $X$ be the number of employees in the sample who prefer campaign A
and $p$ be the proportion of employees in the sample who prefer campaign
A.

1.  State the null and alternative hypotheses. [2 marks]

2.  Determine the critical region for the test. [3 marks]

3.  Find the least possible significance level of the test. [2 marks]

The result of the poll shows that $14$ of the $16$ employees prefer
campaign A.

4.  State the conclusion of the test. [2 marks]

5.
    1.  Find Pr$(X \geq 14)$ under the assumption that the null
        hypothesis is true.

    2.  Explain why this justifies your answer to part (d). [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\text{H}_0: p = 0.5$

    $\text{H}_1: p > 0.5$

2.  Let $r$ be the critical value for this binomial test. Since the
    significance level is $5\%$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \rm{Pr}(X \geq r) &\leq 0.05 \\[3pt]
    \rm{Pr}(X \leq r - 1) &\geq 0.95 \\[3pt]
    r-1 &= \text{binomInv}(0.95,16,0.5)  \\[3pt]
    r - 1 &= 11 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\[3pt]
    r &= 12
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $\text{Hence the critical region is } X \geq 12.$

3.  From part (b), we found that the critical region is $X \geq 12$.
    Hence, the least possible significance level would be

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X \geq 12) &= \text{binomCdf}(16,0.5,12,16) \\[3pt]
    &\approx 0.0384 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the least possible significance level is 3.84\%.

4.

    Since $14$ lies in the critical region, we reject the null hypothesis. Hence, campaign $A$ is preferred by the sample of employees.

5.
    1.  If the null hypothesis is true (i.e. $p = 0.5$), we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr}(X \geq 14) &= \text{binomCdf}(16,0.5, 14, 16) \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.0021 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.

        If there is an even preference between campaigns by all employees in the company (ie, the null hypothesis is true), there would only be a $0.21\%$ possibility of $14$ or more of the $16$ employees in the sample choosing A, which is lower than the $5\%$ significance level. Hence, this supports the claim in part (d) to reject the null hypothesis in favor of the alternative hypothesis.
