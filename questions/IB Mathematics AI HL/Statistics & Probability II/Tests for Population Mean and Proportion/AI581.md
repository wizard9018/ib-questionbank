# AI581

**Maximum mark: 7**

A nutritionist claims that a strict following of the ketogenic diet can
help to lose at least $8$ kg of weight for
$65\%$ of people who follow the diet for $6$ weeks. To test this claim,
$18$ volunteers particpated in the program, following the diet for a $6$
week period. At the end of the $6$ weeks, it was found that $5$ of the
volunteers lost $8$ kg or more.

Let $p$ represent the proportion of people who follow the ketogenic diet
for $6$ weeks and lose at least $8$ kg of
weight.

1.  State the null and alternative hypotheses for the test. [2 marks]

2.  Find the critical region for the test at a $5\%$ significance level.
    [3 marks]

3.  State the conclusion of the test. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\text{H}_0: p = 0.65$

    $\text{H}_1: p < 0.65$

2.  Let $X$ represent the number of people who follow the diet for $6$
    weeks and lose at least $8$ kg, and $r$ be
    the critical value for this binomial test. The critical region is
    $X < r$.

    Since the significance level is $5\%$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \rm{Pr}(X < r) &\leq 0.05 \\[3pt]
    r &= \text{binomInv}(0.05,18,0.65)  \\[3pt]
    r &= 8 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $\text{Hence the critical region is } X < 8, \text{ or } X \leq 7.$

3.  From part (b), we found that the critical region is $X \leq 7$.

    Since $5$ falls within the critical region, we can reject the null hypothesis. Hence, there is sufficient evidence to reject the claim that the ketogenic diet can help to lose at least $8\hspace{0.25em}$kg of weight for $65\%$ of people who follow it.
