# AI959

**Maximum mark: 7**

A telecom company purchases one thousand wifi access points from a
wireless equipment manufacturer, who claim that just $0.9\%$ of the
access points produced give a weak signal.

To verify this claim, an inspector from the company numbers each access
point from 1 to 1000 and checks every tenth unit for their signal
strength. Of the one hundred units in the sample, four are found to give
weak signals.

1.  State the sampling method used by the inspector. [1 mark]

The inspector decides to perform a one-tailed hypothesis test, at the
$5\%$ significance level, to determine whether the manufacturer's claim
is reasonable. It is assumed that the weak signals found in the access
points occur independently.

2.  Write down the null and alternative hypotheses. [2 marks]

3.  Find the $p$-value for the test. [2 marks]

4.  State the conclusion of the test. Justify your answer.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\text{Systematic sampling}$

2.  Let $p$ be the population proportion of the wifi access points
    giving weak signals. We are testing
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{-3em}\text{H}_0 : p = 0.009 \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-3em}\text{H}_1 : p > 0.009
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The $p$-value is equal to the probability that 4 or more access
    points give weak signals.\
    $X \sim \rm{B}(100,0.009)$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{P}(X \geq 4) &= 1 - \text{P}(X \leq 3) \\[12pt]
    &= 1 - \text{BinomialCdf}(100,0.009,0,3) \\[12pt]
    &\approx 0.0130 \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-3.08em}p\text{-value} \approx 0.013
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  We reject the null hypothesis H$_0$ if the $p$-value is less than
    the significance level. In this case, we are given that the
    significance level is $0.05$.

    Since the $p$-value of $0.013 < 0.05$, there is sufficient evidence to reject the null hypothesis at the $5\%$ significance level. Hence, we reject the statement from the wireless equipment manufacturer that only 0.9\% of access points give a weak signal.
