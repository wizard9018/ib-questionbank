# AI1065

**Maximum mark: 6**

A store owner is worried that only 20\% of people in her town are aware of her upcoming winter sale, so decides to implement a marketing campaign to raise awareness. She then tests whether the campaign has raised awareness about the sale, assuming that a person's knowledge of the sale is independent of other people knowing about it. Her null and alternate hypotheses are:

$$

\begin{aligned}

    H_0&: p = 0.2 \\[8pt]

    H_1&: p > 0.2

\end{aligned}

$$

The store owner surveys a random sample of 100 people from the town and will reject her null hypothesis if more than 25 people know about the sale.

1. Find the probability that the store owner makes a Type I error.[3 marks]

The marketing campaign has in fact led to 35\% of people in the town knowing about the sale.

2. Find the probability that the store owner makes a Type II error.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Let $T$ represent the total number of people from the survey who know about the sale. assuming the null hypothesis to be true, we have:

    ![ai1065](images/ai1065.PNG)

    Hence $T \sim \mathrm{B}(100,0.2)$. Recall that a type I error occurs when a null hypothesis is rejected, given that the null hypothesis is actually true. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(\text{type I error}) &= \mathrm{P}(T > 25\,|\,p =0.2) \\[8pt]

    &= \mathrm{P}(T \geq 26\,|\,p =0.2) \\[8pt]

    &= \text{binomCdf}(100,0.2,26,100) \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-6.7em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(\text{type I error})\approx0.0875}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Recall that a type II error occurs when a null hypothesis is not rejected, given that the null hypothesis is actually false. We have $p=0.35$. Therefore

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(\text{type II error}) &= \mathrm{P}(T \leq 25\,|\,p =0.35) \\[8pt]

    &= \text{binomCdf}(100,0.35,0,25) \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-7em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(\text{type II error})\approx0.0211}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
