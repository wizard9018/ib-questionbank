# AI582

**Maximum mark: 5**

The number of bushfires per year in the Northern Territory of Australia
can be modelled by a Poisson distribution with a mean of $15$. A
FireSmart governance system was introduced throughout the region to help
reduce the rate of bushfires. A test was then conducted to determine if
this system did in fact reduce the number of bushfires, at a $5\%$
significance level.

Three years after the implementation of the system, the total number of
bushfires recorded ($X$) over the three year period was $32$.

1.  Find the critical region for the test. [3 marks]

2.  Determine if the number of bushfires in the region has been
    significantly reduced at a $5\%$ level of significance. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since the expected number of bushfires per year is $15$, we expect
    that the number of bushfires in three years would be
    $15 \times 3 = 45$.

    Let $\lambda$ be the mean number of bushfires three years after the
    system is introduced. We are testing
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{H}_0: \lambda &= 45 \\[3pt]
    \text{H}_1: \lambda &< 45
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Let $X$ represent the number of bushfires in
    three years and $r$ be the critical value for this test. If the null
    hypothesis is true, by using GDC, we can find the cumulative
    probability of $X$ as shown in the table below.

    ![8f9ee1aed09520248ac01a8ae046f66c931b9765.svg](images/8f9ee1aed09520248ac01a8ae046f66c931b9765.svg)

    The critical value ($r$) is the largest value of $X$ for which
    $\rm{Pr}(X \leq r) < 0.05$. Based on the table, we can see that
    $r = 33$.

    $\text{Hence the critical region is } X \leq 33.$

2.  From part (a), we found that the critical region is $X \leq 33$.

    Since $32$ is within the critical region, we can reject the null hypothesis. Hence there is sufficient evidence to conclude that the number of bushfires in the region has been significantly reduced at a $5\%$ level of significance.
