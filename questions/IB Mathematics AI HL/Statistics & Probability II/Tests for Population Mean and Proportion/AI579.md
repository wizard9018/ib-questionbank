# AI579

**Maximum mark: 12**

A mattress store in Liverpool, UK, places two types of new mattresses,
\"Silent Night Onyx\" and \"Sherman\", next to each other in its
showroom. The Silent Night Onyx is a deluxe style mattress and the store
manager would like to understand whether customers preferred this model
at a $5\%$ significance level. A random sample of $22$ customers were
asked to try both mattresses without labels or prices shown and then
vote for which mattress they thought was most comfortable.

Let $X$ be the number of the customers who show their preference for
Silent Night Onyx, and $p$ be the population proportion of those who
prefer Silent Night Onyx.

1.  State the null and alternative hypotheses. [2 marks]

2.  Determine the critical region for the test. [3 marks]

3.  Find the least possible significance level of the test. [2 marks]

The result of the survey shows that $18$ out of $22$ customers sampled
voted for Silent Night Onyx.

4.  State the conclusion of the test. [2 marks]

5.
    1.  Find P$(X \geq 18)$ under the assumption that the null
        hypothesis is true.

    2.  Explain why this justifies your answer to part (d). [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\text{H}_0: p = 0.5$

    $\text{H}_1: p > 0.5$

2.  Let $r$ be the critical value for this binomial test. Since the
    significance level is $5\%$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \rm{Pr}(X \geq r) &\leq 0.05 \\[3pt]
    \rm{Pr}(X \leq r - 1) &\geq 0.95 \\[3pt]
    r-1 &= \text{binomInv}(0.95,22,0.5)  \\[3pt]
    r - 1 &= 15 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\[3pt]
    r &= 16
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $\text{Hence the critical region is } X \geq 16.$

3.  From part (b), we found that the critical region is $X \geq 16$.
    Hence, the least possible significance level would be

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X \geq 16) &= \text{binomCdf}(22,0.5,16,22) \\[3pt]
    &\approx 0.0262 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the least possible significance level is 2.62\%.

4.

    Since $18$ lies in the critical region, we reject the null hypothesis. Hence, we can claim that customers prefer the Silent Night Onyx model.

5.
    1.  If the null hypothesis is true (i.e. $p = 0.5$), then we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{Pr}(X \geq 18) &= \text{binomCdf}(22,0.5, 18, 22) \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.00217 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.

        If there is an even preference between mattresses by all customers (ie, the null hypothesis is true), there would only be a $0.217\%$ possibility of $18$ or more of the $22$ customers sampled preferring the Silent Night Onyx, which is lower than the $5\%$ significance level. Hence, this supports the claim in part (d) to reject the null hypothesis in favor of the alternative hypothesis.
