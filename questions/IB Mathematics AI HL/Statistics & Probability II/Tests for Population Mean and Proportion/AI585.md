# AI585

**Maximum mark: 6**

At a hospital in Manila, Philippines, the number of patients arriving
with emergency conditions each hour is known to follow the Poisson
distribution. The doctors responsible for emergency cases claim that the
rate is $8$ patients per hour and have requested for additional doctors
to be added on each shift. The hospital manager suspects that the rate
of patient arrivals is less than the doctors claim. The manager conducts
a test over a $5$ hour period, decides to use a $5\%$ level of
significance, and records $28$ patients arrive.

1.  State the null and alternative hypotheses for the test. [2 marks]

2.  Find the $p$-value for the test. [2 marks]

3.  State the conclusion of the test. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since the doctors claim that the number of emergency patients
    arriving each hour is $8$, we expect that the number in $5$ hours to
    be $8 \times 5 = 40$.

    Let $\lambda$ be the mean number of emergency patients arriving in a
    $5$ hour period. We are testing
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\text{H}_0: \lambda = 40 \\[6pt]
    &\text{H}_1: \lambda < 40
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $X$ represent the number of emergency patients arriving in a $5$
    hour period. If the null hypothesis is true, then
    $X \sim \text{Po}(40)$. Hence the $p$-value for the test is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr}(X \leq 28) &= \text{poissonCdf}(40,0,28) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.0294 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.

    Since $p$-value $= 0.0294 < 0.05$, we reject the null hypothesis. Thus, there is sufficient evidence to conclude that the rate of emergency patients arriving each hour is less than the $8$ that the doctors claim.
