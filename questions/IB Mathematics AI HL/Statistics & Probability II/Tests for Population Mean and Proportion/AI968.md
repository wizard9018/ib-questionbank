# AI968

**Maximum mark: 7**

Counting of fetal movements such as kicks, flutters, or rolls, is a way
to check if an unborn baby is growing healthily.

Emma is pregnant in her third trimester. The number of movements per
hour during her $32$ weeks of gestation is assumed to be modelled by a
Poisson distribution with a mean of $5$.

Emma was told by her healthcare provider that babies tend to be more
active during the last period of pregnancy. The mean number of movements
will significantly increase during the final weeks of gestation. To test
whether this is true for her baby, she decides to record the number of
movements over a single $4$-hour period and carry out a hypothesis test
at a $1\%$ level of significance.

1.  State the null and alternative hypotheses for her test. [1 mark]

Let $X$ be the number of movements during the 4-hour period.

2.  Show that the critical region for the test is $X \geq 32$.
    [3 marks]

Emma initially made a miscalculation, and discovers that the mean number
of movements per hour during her pregnancy so far was in fact 7, not 5.

3.  Calculate the probability of a type II error in the
    hypothesis test. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $m$ denote the average number of movements of the baby during
    her $32$ weeks of gestation. We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\text{H}_0 : m = 5 \\[12pt]
    &\text{H}_1 : m > 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have $\text{E}(X) = 4 \times 5 = 20$. Hence,
    $X \sim \text{Po}(20)$.

    Using the G.D.C., we evaluate $\text{P}(X \geq 31)$ and
    $\text{P}(X \geq 32)$ to verify that $X \geq 32$ is the first time
    that the probability is less than $0.01$, as shown below.

    ![5821584d60156d8f4005f8b6ba95202b6ce72325.svg](images/5821584d60156d8f4005f8b6ba95202b6ce72325.svg)

    Hence,
     $\text{the critical region is \(X \geq 32\)}$

3.  Recall in hypothesis testing that a type II error occurs when the
    null\
    hypothesis is accepted when it is actually false. In this case, we
    know that\
    $\text{E}(X) = 4 \times 7 = 28$. Hence, $X \sim \text{Po}(28)$.\
    From part (b) we know that we will accept $\text{H}_0 \text{ if }$
    $X \leq 31$.\
    Thus we must calculate
    $P(X \leq 31 \hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em} \lambda = 28)$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    P(X \leq 31 \hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em} \lambda = 28)= 0.752
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $\text{Given that \(m=7\), the probability of a type II error is \(0.752\).}$
