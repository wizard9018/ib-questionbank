# AI574

**Maximum mark: 14**

The following table lists the gold medal winners in the male javelin
throwing competition in a youth athletics event. The table shows the
winner in each age category and their associated longest javelin throw.

![bcb4495dfacf2d0e1c4b82ae3ade1e5aa8f8111a.svg](images/bcb4495dfacf2d0e1c4b82ae3ade1e5aa8f8111a.svg)

1.  Sketch a scatter diagram for the data on the axes below and describe
    it. [3 marks]

    ![1e932acbc41df75d33e0481034da673bf1d3d548.svg](images/1e932acbc41df75d33e0481034da673bf1d3d548.svg)

2.  Determine an appropriate non-linear model using linearisation with
    logarithms to model distance thrown, $d$, in terms of age, $a$.
    [5 marks]

3.  Find the coefficient of determination for your model and interpret.
    [2 marks]

4.  Using your model, determine the athlete(s) who, given their age,
    threw the javelin

    1.  longer than expected.
    2.  shorter than expected. [4 marks]
