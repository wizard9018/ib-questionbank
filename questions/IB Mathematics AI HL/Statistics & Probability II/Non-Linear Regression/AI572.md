# AI572

**Maximum mark: 6**

Kristina recently opened a painting exhibition in Berlin. She wants to
estimate the average number of guests coming to the gallery per day
after the opening. The table below shows some data of days that Kristina
did record how many guests came to the gallery.

![b982f624e50c0c483400b32075006f2769c24a19.svg](images/b982f624e50c0c483400b32075006f2769c24a19.svg)

1.  Find a linear model of $\ln(G)$ in terms of $d$. [2 marks]

2.  Hence or otherwise, find an exponential model of $G$ in terms of
    $d$. [2 marks]

3.  Using one of your models, estimate how many guests came to the
    gallery on the opening day and explain why this estimate could be
    inaccurate. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using GDC, we can re-express the data in logarithm form as follows.

    ![64413131bdce4551929eaaae2c5aff3413549026.svg](images/64413131bdce4551929eaaae2c5aff3413549026.svg)

    Using GDC, we can find the linear model connecting $\ln(G)$ and $d$:

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\ln(G) = -0.0724(d) + 5.11
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the linear model found in part (a), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \ln(G) &= -0.0724(d) + 5.11 \\[3pt]
    G &= e^{-0.0724(d) + 5.11} \\[3pt]
    G &= e^{-0.0724(d)} \cdot e^{5.11} \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.75em}G \approx 166 \, (0.93)^d
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Substituting $d = 0$ (opening day) into the model from part (b), we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    G &=   165.7 \, (0.93)^0 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 166
    \end{aligned}
    $$

This estimate could be inaccurate as $d=0$ is outside the data range used to create the exponential model (extraploation).
