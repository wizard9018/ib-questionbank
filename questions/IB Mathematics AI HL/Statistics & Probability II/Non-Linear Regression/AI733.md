# AI733

**Maximum mark: 14**

The table below shows the average number of words of a child's
vocabulary as age increases on a monthly basis. The first row shows the
child's age in months ($t$), and the second row shows the average number
of words, $W$, in hundreds.

![74d633d85b33654fe66ac357f0b97fb4fff11ad5.svg](images/74d633d85b33654fe66ac357f0b97fb4fff11ad5.svg)

1.  Generate a scatter diagram for this data on the axes below and
    describe the trend.[3 marks]

    ![8d0831be3ad127175e6b7015a40759279358f764.svg](images/8d0831be3ad127175e6b7015a40759279358f764.svg)

2.  Linearise the data and determine an appropriate linear model for the
    number of words, $W$, in terms of the months, $t$. [5 marks]

3.  Using your model, predict the approximate number of words in the
    vocabulary of a child who has just turned 6 years old. [2 marks]

The rate of change for the number of words a child learns per month can
be estimated by $W'(t)$.

4.  Estimate the month at which the child learns 100 words per
    month. [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The scatter diagram is shown below.

    ![73d78a0453a53351170c494332cfc35e580a3c4a.svg](images/73d78a0453a53351170c494332cfc35e580a3c4a.svg)

    The scatter plot shows that there appears to be a logarithmic pattern in the number of words added to a child's vocabulary as they increase in age.

2.  The data can be linearised by taking the log of $t$ and creating a
    table of log($t$) vs $W$, as follows.

    ![122a8b1364f05060502da21765712b548a3d0034.svg](images/122a8b1364f05060502da21765712b548a3d0034.svg)

    Using GDC, we can now find a linear model connecting log($t$) vs $W$

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     &W = 103 \,\rm{log}(t) -135
     \end{aligned}
     $$

3.  $6$ years correspond to $m = 6 \times 12 = 72$ months. Hence, we
    substitute $72$ into the model and get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    W_{\mathrm{pred}} &= 103 \, \mathrm{log}(72) -135 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 56.3\text{, or }5\hspace{0.1em}630\,\text{ words}\\[6pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  We can use the graphing section of the GDC to find the solution for
    when $W'(t)=1$ (since $W$ is in hundreds of words). We get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    W'(t) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t} \left(103 \,\rm{log}(t) -135\right) \\[7pt]
    1 &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t} \left(103 \,\rm{log}(t) -135\right) \\[7pt]
    t &\approx 44.7 \\[7pt]
    &\hspace{-0.3em}\approx \text{month }44
    \end{aligned}
    $$
