# AI576

**Maximum mark: 7**

A household appliance manufacturing company uses the following model to
predict the quantity of output, $Q$ (in thousands of items), from the
quantity of labour, $l$ (in hundreds of hours).
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q(l) &= al e^{bl}, \,a, \,b \in \mathbb{R}
\end{aligned}
$$
The
production analysts in the company are trying to decide between two
options for the values of $a$ and $b$, depending on which historical
time period to use as their data set. The two options, Models $A$ and
$B$ are as follows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Model A: }& a = 4, \, b = -0.3 \\[3pt]
\text{Model B: }& a = 3.5, \, b = -0.2
\end{aligned}
$$
To test
whether model A or B has better capacity of forecasting output, the
company records the quantity of output for three different quantities of
labour, as shown in the table below.

![67c16b603caa8f038bb2adeee08be2036f2da611.svg](images/67c16b603caa8f038bb2adeee08be2036f2da611.svg)

Determine the model that the firm should choose based on the least sum
of square of residuals.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let's substitute the values of $l$ into each model to estimate the
corresponding quantities of output $Q$ at each workplace.

Model A: $Q(l) = 4l e^{-0.3l}$

![d86a53d48fe17b5123522f5ac777110eb3b7a18e.svg](images/d86a53d48fe17b5123522f5ac777110eb3b7a18e.svg)

The sum of squares of residuals for model A is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{SS}_{\text{res}} &= (2.9633 - 2.9)^2 + (3.8258 - 3.7)^2 +  (4.3905 - 4.5)^2 \\[3pt]
&\approx 0.0318
\end{aligned}
$$

Model B: $Q(l) = 3.5l e^{-0.2l}$

![b10fb11fc072f422a7f7176a656ffd83ce2cbbbb.svg](images/b10fb11fc072f422a7f7176a656ffd83ce2cbbbb.svg)

Then the sum of squares of residuals for model B is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{SS}_{\text{res}} &= (2.8656 - 2.9)^2 + (3.8893 - 3.7)^2 +  (4.6922 - 4.5)^2 \\[3pt]
&\approx 0.0740
\end{aligned}
$$

Since model A has a smaller sum of squares of residuals, the production analysts should choose model A.
