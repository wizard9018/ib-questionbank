# AI575

**Maximum mark: 15**

The table below shows the daily revenue, $R$, in thousands of USD, of a
toy store during the month of December. The first row of the table shows
the day of the month ($t$) and the second row the revenue of that day in
thousands of USD.

![bc6e37c79ee8f4030acc882a02b8d5f16770af14.svg](images/bc6e37c79ee8f4030acc882a02b8d5f16770af14.svg)

1.
    1.  Generate a scatter diagram for this data on the axes below.

        ![33483a8a81508eda71cfe03baec2df075a08f1e0.svg](images/33483a8a81508eda71cfe03baec2df075a08f1e0.svg)

    2.  Identify a possible outlier and give a possible reason for this
        outlier;

    3.  Give a reason why plotting $R$ vs log($t$) is likely to be the
        best linearisation method. [6 marks]

2.  Linearise the data and determine an appropriate linear model for the
    daily revenue $R$ in terms of day of the month in December, $t$.
    [3 marks]

3.  Using your model, predict the revenue after $3$ weeks in December.
    [2 marks]

4.  The total revenue for the month of December can be estimated by
    $\displaystyle\int_a^b R\, \rm{d} t$.

    1.  Write down the values of $a$ and $b$.
    2.  Estimate the total revenue in December using your model.
        [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Scattergram for the data:

        ![de4a5615a54d4a14f6a6acb9ab2b013aa0af7dd1.svg](images/de4a5615a54d4a14f6a6acb9ab2b013aa0af7dd1.svg)

    2.  $t=24$ appears to be an outlier.

        There appears to be an outlier at $t = 24$ (i.e. December $24$th) with higher revenues compared with the pattern of the other days. This could be due to higher volumes of shopping on Christmas Eve.

    3.

        The scatter plot shows that there appears to be a logarithmic pattern in the daily revenue against time.

2.  Since we are expecting a logarithmic relationship between $R$ and
    $t$, let's create a table of log($t$) vs $R$ after removing the
    outlier.

    ![5281e21d08a91a316ce41b212dd7fb8beaf2e7fd.svg](images/5281e21d08a91a316ce41b212dd7fb8beaf2e7fd.svg)

    Using GDC, we get the log model:

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &R = 10.9 \,\rm{log}(t) + 7.24
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  $3$ weeks correspond to $t = 3 \times 7 = 21$ days. Hence, we
    substitute $21$ into the model and get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    R_{\text{pred}} &= 10.9 \, \rm{log}(21) + 7.24 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 21.7 \text{ thousands of USD}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.
    1.
        $a = 0$

        $b = 31$

    2.  The total revenue, $TR$, for the month of December can be
        estimated by
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        TR &= \displaystyle \int_0^{31} \left[ 10.9 \, \rm{log}(t) + 7.24 \right] \rm{d}t \\[6pt]
        &\approx 582 \text{ thousands of USD} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$
