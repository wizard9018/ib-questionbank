# AI577

**Maximum mark: 7**

Phillip, an IB math teacher, has formulated a mathematical model to
predict the average mark on the final exam of the students, $M$, from
the time allowed to complete the exam, $h$, in hours. The model is
described as follows.
$$
\begin{aligned}
M(h) &= ch e^{dh}, \,c, \,d \in \mathbb{R}
\end{aligned}
$$
Phillip
is trying to determine the values for $c$ and $d$ and two options,
depending on which prior class data to use. His options are Model $A$ or
Model $B$, as follows:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Model A: }& c = 102, \, d = -0.48 \\[3pt]
\text{Model B: }& c = 110, \, d = -0.55
\end{aligned}
$$
To determine
whether Models $A$ or $B$ is more accurate in predicting the average
exam mark, Philip conducts three trial examinations with his class with
different exam lengths. The results are shown in the table below.

![3b7fa06ba720c4d90cc35d9d6c38a2f3cdd4be87.svg](images/3b7fa06ba720c4d90cc35d9d6c38a2f3cdd4be87.svg)

Phillip decides to choose the model with the smallest value of the sum
of square of residuals. Determine the model that he should choose.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let's substitute the values of $h$ into each model to estimate the
corresponding average exam mark of the students in trial.

Model A: $M(h) = 102h e^{-0.48h}$

![4ee9724722173cc939ee1d05b9eccaa167cc4a43.svg](images/4ee9724722173cc939ee1d05b9eccaa167cc4a43.svg)

Then the sum of squares of residuals for model A is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{SS}_{\text{res}} &= (74.4731 - 73.1)^2 + (78.1101 - 76.5)^2 +  (76.8045 - 69.8)^2 \\[3pt]
&\approx 53.5408
\end{aligned}
$$

Model B: $M(h) = 110h e^{-0.55h}$

![d840273f90480568c4bb368abcc51943e021ece1.svg](images/d840273f90480568c4bb368abcc51943e021ece1.svg)

Then the sum of squares of residuals for model B is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{SS}_{\text{res}} &= (72.3088 - 73.1)^2 + (73.2316 - 76.5)^2 +  (69.5309 - 69.8)^2 \\[3pt]
&\approx 11.3809
\end{aligned}
$$

Since model B has a smaller sum of squares of residuals, Phillip should choose model B.
