# AI571

**Maximum mark: 5**

Particulate Matter (PM) is a measure of air pollution. PM2.5
concentration, measured in $\bm{\mu}$g/m${^3}$, is a measure of the
amount of fine, inhalable particles with diameters of $2.5$ micrometres
or smaller in the air. The PM2.5 levels were measured at various times
on a particular day in Delhi, India, with the results shown in the table
below. The max and min PM2.5 levels were recorded and are included
in the data.

![afac0a239c933cf76b03c91800cd7a4fec734d45.svg](images/afac0a239c933cf76b03c91800cd7a4fec734d45.svg)

![6b5c823d7ded142371ed761039fb5f1800a5c8a9.svg](images/6b5c823d7ded142371ed761039fb5f1800a5c8a9.svg)

1.  Determine an appropriate model for the PM2.5 concentration, $P$, in
    terms of the number of hours after midnight, $t$. [3 marks]

On this particular day, the Central Pollution Control Board of Dehli
alerts citizens that the air quality will be \"very poor\" between
$09$:$15$ to $10$:$15$ and recommends that the citizens should minimise
being outside during these times of the day.

2.  Use your model from part (a) to estimate the PM2.5 level
    that the Central Pollution Control Board considers to be \"very
    poor\". [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  There appears a sinusoidal association between PM$_{2.5}$
    concentration, $P$, and time after midnight, $t$.

    ![d8cc16ab43a26bf9f1835d212eabbce896ac2503.svg](images/d8cc16ab43a26bf9f1835d212eabbce896ac2503.svg)

    Using GDC and finding a sinusoidal regression for the data, we get

    $$
    P(t) = 31.7 \sin(0.496t -2.93) + 92.5
    $$

2.  The PM2.5 level is first considered very poor at $09$:$15$. The
    number of hours after midnight of $09$:$15$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    t &= 9 + \dfrac{15}{60} \\[3pt]
    &= 9.25
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Substituting $t = 9.25$ into the model, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    P &= 31.7 \sin(0.496(9.25) -2.93) + 92.5 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 124\hspace{0.25em} \mu\text{g/m}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
