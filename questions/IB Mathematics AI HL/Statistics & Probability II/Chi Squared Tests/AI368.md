# AI368

**Maximum mark: 6**

A group of adults who use either Mac or PC computers were surveyed. The
results were recorded according to their age group and are shown
below.

![51bbc9904e0e59fa0275f7796f1a147eb674ba30.svg](images/51bbc9904e0e59fa0275f7796f1a147eb674ba30.svg)

1.  Write down the total number of computer users surveyed. [1 mark]

A $\chi^2$ test was conducted at the $5\%$ significance level.

2.  Write down the null hypothesis for this test. [1 mark]

3.  Using your graphics display calculator, find the $\chi^2$
    statistic. [2 marks]

The critical value at the $5\%$ significance level is $5.99$.

4.  State the conclusion of this test. Give a reason for your
    answer. [2 marks]
