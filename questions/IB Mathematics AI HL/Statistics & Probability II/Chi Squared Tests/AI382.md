# AI382

**Maximum mark: 6**

Sport4Fit is fitness nutrition company, selling products such as
vitamins, protein supplementation and amino acids. It has a media
advertising budget distributed according to the percentages below.

![71d194af42676898b88ef104ed86aaae18f51e3a.svg](images/71d194af42676898b88ef104ed86aaae18f51e3a.svg)

In a recent survey, $120$ randomly chosen customers were asked how they
found out about the products of the company. Their responses were
summarized in the following table.

![71d0a2394cfb8940c69871e81d656fe98d5f1ba6.svg](images/71d0a2394cfb8940c69871e81d656fe98d5f1ba6.svg)

To determine whether the advertising effectiveness is consistent with
the distribution of spend on advertising, a $\chi^2$ goodness of fit is
performed at a $5\%$ significance level.

1.  Write down
    1.  the null hypothesis;

    2.  the number of degrees of freedom. [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the $p$-value for the test. [2 marks]

3.  Determine whether the company should change the distribution of
    budget on advertising media. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $p_1, \,p_2,\,p_3,\,p_4$ and $p_5$ be the population proportions
    of customers who find out about the products of the company through
    TV media, radio, social media, newspaper and magazine, and others
    respectively.

    1.
        $H_0: p_1 = 0.3,\,p_2 = 0.1,\,p_3 = 0.3,\,p_4 = 0.2,\, p_5 = 0.1$

    2.  Using the formula $df = k - 1$ where $k$ is the number of
        categories (in this case, number of media types), we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        df &= 5 - 1 \\[6pt]
        &= 4
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    $p\text{-value} \approx 0.0129$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

3.  Recall that we reject the null hypothesis if the $p$-value is less
    than the significance level. In this case, the significance level is
    given as $0.05$.

    Since $p$-value $= 0.0129 < 0.05$, we reject $H_0$ in favour of $H_1$ at a $5\%$ level of significance. So there is sufficient evidence to conclude that the advertising effectiveness is inconsistent with the budget distribution on advertising. Hence, the company should change its budget distribution or perhaps allocate more budget to the media type that yields out of proportion results.
