# AI1173

**Maximum mark: 6**

A researcher is studying the relationship between exercise frequency and stress levels among a group of 100 adults. The data collected is summarized in the following table.

![1173a](images/1173a.JPG)

An adult is chosen at random from the 100 in the group.

1. Find the probability that this adult’s stress level is not high, given that they exercise regularly.     [2 marks]

A chi-squared test at the $5\%$ significance level is undertaken to determine if stress level and exercise frequency are independent.

The null and alternate hypotheses are:

$\hspace{5em}\text{H}_0$: stress level and exercise frequency are independent.

$\hspace{5em}\text{H}_1$: stress level and exercise frequency are not independent.

2. Find the $p$-value for this test. [2 marks]

3. State the conclusion for this test. Justify your answer.[2 marks]
