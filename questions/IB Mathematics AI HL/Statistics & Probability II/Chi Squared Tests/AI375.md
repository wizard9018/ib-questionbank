# AI375

**Maximum mark: 6**

In a kindergarten, children aged $3$ to $6$ years old were asked to
select their preferred toy. The choices were wooden blocks, puzzles and
board games. The data obtained was organized in the following table.

![21ee8b75d4c8d790868a96f64e3ed23e7269053c.svg](images/21ee8b75d4c8d790868a96f64e3ed23e7269053c.svg)

A $\chi^2$ test is carried out at the $5\%$ significance level with
hypotheses:
$$

\begin{array}{l}
\text{H$_0$: the preferred toy is independent of the age} \\[-0.1em]
\text{H$_1$: the preferred toy is not independent of the age}
\end{array}
$$
The $\chi^2$ critical value for this test is
$12.6$.

1.  Find the value of $k$. [1 mark]

2.  Determine the number of degrees of freedom for this test.
    [1 mark]

3.  Use your graphic display calculator to find the $\chi^2$ statistic
    for this test. [2 marks]

4.  State the conclusion for this test. Give a reason for your answer.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Summing all the numbers of games from ages $3$ to $6$ in the puzzles
    column will give us a total of $45$. So we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    8 + k + 16 + 15 &= 45 \\[3pt]
    k + 39 &= 45 \\[6pt]
    \hspace{2.5em} &k = 6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The degrees of freedom in a test of independence is given by
    $df = (r-1)(c-1)$, where $r$ is the number of rows and $c$ is the
    number of columns. From the table we see that there are $4$ rows and
    $3$ columns. Therefore,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    df &= (4-1)(3-1) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}= 6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    $\chi^2 \approx 18.1$
    \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

4.  Recall that we would reject the null hypothesis $H_0$ if
    $\chi^2 > \chi^2_{\text{crit}}$. In this case, we are given
    $\chi^2_{\text{crit}} = 12.6$.

    Since $\chi^2 = 18.15 > 12.6$, we reject $H_0$. So at the $5\%$ significance level, there is sufficient evidence to conclude that the preferred toy is not independent of the age.
