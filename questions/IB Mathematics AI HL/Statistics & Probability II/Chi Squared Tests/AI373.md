# AI373

**Maximum mark: 6**

A survey was conducted among $1000$ Australians and their preferred
overseas holiday destination. The purpose of the survey was to establish
a relationship between the level of education and the preferred holiday
destination. The education levels and top five holiday destinations are
shown in the following tables.

Level of Education:

![67e61fdf9bacab14602f412a87b7bb6cd9a2aaf9.svg](images/67e61fdf9bacab14602f412a87b7bb6cd9a2aaf9.svg)

Preferred overseas holiday destination:

![ad88f125a398c9e1a6be0b2482a80f15b277b4ae.svg](images/ad88f125a398c9e1a6be0b2482a80f15b277b4ae.svg)

A $\chi^2$-test was performed at the $5\%$ significance level.

1.  Write down the null hypothesis. [1 mark]

2.  Find the number of degrees of freedom. [2 marks]

The critical value of this $\chi^2$-test is $21.026$.

3.  Use this information to write down the values of the
    $\chi^2$ statistic for which  [1 mark] the null hypothesis is
    rejected.

From the travellers taking part in the survey, $580$ have a Bachelor's
degree and $671$ recorded New Zealand as their preferred
destination.

4.  Calculate the expected number of travellers who preferred
    New Zealand and  [2 marks] have a Bachelor's degree.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.

    $H_0$: The preferred holiday destination is independent of the level of education

2.  The given tables reveal that the contingency table for this data has
    $4$ rows and $5$ columns. Using the formula $df = (r-1)(c-1)$ where
    $r$ and $c$ represent the number of rows and columns, respectively,
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    df &= (4-1)(5-1) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}= 12
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Recall that we reject the null hypothesis $H_0$ if
    $\chi^2 > \chi^2_{\text{crit}}$. In this case, we are given
    $\chi^2_{\text{crit}} = 21.026$.
    $\text{Hence the null hypothesis will be rejected if \(\chi^2 > 21.026\)}$

4.  Let's fill in the contingency table using the given information.

    ![0d0f3ff7ee99265cee58473b7622f15b886f6adc.svg](images/0d0f3ff7ee99265cee58473b7622f15b886f6adc.svg)

    So the expected number of travellers who preferred New Zealand and
    have bachelor degree is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    E_{31} &= \dfrac{671 \times 580}{1000} \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 389
    \end{aligned}
    $$
