# AI369

**Maximum mark: 6**

A survey was conducted on a random sample of families who own cats. The
cats were classified by breed and by gender. Survey results for some of
the breeds are shown in the following table.

![2e515543725e9b5ccfa80d1a8f642c89024d2135.svg](images/2e515543725e9b5ccfa80d1a8f642c89024d2135.svg)

1.  Write down the expected number of Female Persian cats. [2 marks]

A $\chi^2$-test was performed at the $5\%$ significance level. It is
used to determine whether breed preference is independent of cat gender.

2.  Use your graphic display calculator to find the $p$-value
    for this test. [2 marks]

3.  State whether breed preference is independent of cat
    gender. Provide [2 marks] a reason for your
    answer.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's add a gender total column to the table. The totals of Female
    Persian cats are highlighted in red.

    ![cfdc24aa4683d4565f8470fa7987591d223983ee.svg](images/cfdc24aa4683d4565f8470fa7987591d223983ee.svg)

    The expected number of Female Persian cats is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    E_{22} &= \dfrac{31 \times 82}{174} \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 14.6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    $p\text{-value} \approx 0.346$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

3.  We perform the test of independence using the $\chi^2$-test for the
    following hypotheses.

    $H_0$: Breed preference is independent of
    gender

    $H_1$: Breed preference is not independent of gender

    Since the test is performed at the $5\%$ ($0.05$) significance
    level, we reject the null hypothesis, $H_0$, if the $p$-value \<
    $0.05$.

    Since the $p$-value $= 0.346 > 0.05$, we do not reject the $H_0$. Hence, the breed preference is independent of the gender.
