# AI703

**Maximum mark: 14**

250 households in a town were surveyed asking how many video streaming
services the household subscribes to. The outcome of the survey is shown
in the following table.

![25d9b125b89b0a862e6a604f92dfd1d568a0634b.svg](images/25d9b125b89b0a862e6a604f92dfd1d568a0634b.svg)

1.  Find the number of households that subscribe to more than $2$
    services.[1 mark]

2.  Find the modal number of streaming services per
    household.[1 mark]

3.  From the data, find
    1.  the median number of streaming services;

    2.  the lower quartile;
    3.  the upper quartile.[3 marks]

A different survey conducted by an entertainment blog asked 150 survey
participants of their preference between two video streaming services;
Netflix and YouTube TV. The results of this survey data is summarized in
the following table.

![3c608aa4e9fbe2de1a9d71117bce03e058096250.svg](images/3c608aa4e9fbe2de1a9d71117bce03e058096250.svg)

A $\chi^2$ test of independence is carried out at the $10\%$
significance level.

4.  State
    1.  the null hypothesis;

    2.  the alternative hypothesis.[2 marks]

5.  Write down the number of degrees of freedom for this
    test.[1 mark]

6.  Calculate the expected number of people between 30--45 that
    prefer Netflix. [2 marks]

7.  Find the $p$ value for this test.[2 marks]

8.  State the conclusion for this test.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $46 + 36 + 32 + 21 = \hspace{0.7em}135$

2.  The modal number of services is the value most common. Hence,
     $2$

3.
    1.  The median value is the average of the numbers in the 125 and
        126 position. Thus, the median value is
        $3$

    2.  The lower quartile $Q_1$ is the number that corresponds to the
        position $63$. Thus, the lower quartile is
        $2$

    3.  The upper quartile $Q_3$ is the number that corresponds to the
        position $188$. Thus, the upper quartile is
        $4$

4.  The hypotheses are
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\text{H}_0 :\ \text{Preference of streaming service and age are independent of each other.} \\[8pt]
    &\text{H}_1 :\ \text{Preference of streaming service depends on age.}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  The number of degrees of freedom can be found by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    df &= (r-1)(c-1) \\[6pt]
    &= (4-1)(2-1) \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}= 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

6.  The expected number of people aged 30-45 that prefer Netflix can be
    found by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    E_{3,1} &= \dfrac{45 \times 79}{150} \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 23.7
    \end{aligned}
    $$

7.  The $p$ value
    is $\approx0.812$
    \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

8.  We reject the null hypothesis, $H_0$, if the $p$-value is less than
    the significance level. In this case, we are given that the
    significance level is $0.1$.\
    $\text{Since p-value = 0.812 > 0.1, there is insufficient evidence to reject \(H_0\).}$\
    Therefore, we can conclude that the preference of the two video
    streaming services is independent of the age of the viewer.
