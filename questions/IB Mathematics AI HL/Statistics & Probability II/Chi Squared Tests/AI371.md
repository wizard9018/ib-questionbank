# AI371

**Maximum mark: 6**

A survey was conducted at a New York high school on the preferred sports
of a cohort of students from the options of of football, basketball,
athletics or swimming. The results are shown in the table below, with
results categorised by gender.

![f927fd4558a0d486c785e348d70360dcb0a58221.svg](images/f927fd4558a0d486c785e348d70360dcb0a58221.svg)

A $\chi^2$-test was performed at the $5\%$ significance level to
determine if sport preference is independent of
gender.

1.  State the null hypothesis for this test, $H_0$. [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the expected number of boys who preferred basketball.
    [2 marks]

3.  Use your graphic display calculator to find the $p$-value for this
    test. [2 marks]

4.  State whether a students preferred sport is independent
    of [1 mark] their gender. Give a reason for
    your answer.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $H_0: \text{ Preferred sport and gender are independent of each other}$

2.  Let's complete the contingency table with the marginal frequencies.
    The marginal frequencies of boys who prefer basketball are
    highlighted in red.

    ![5554cced1252d4781548ba4adcf590f52cd67cc9.svg](images/5554cced1252d4781548ba4adcf590f52cd67cc9.svg)

    So the expected number of boys to prefer basketball is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    E_{23} &= \dfrac{336 \times 660}{1171} \\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 189
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    $p\text{-value} \approx 0.982$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

4.  Recall that, for a test of independence using $\chi^2$-test, we
    reject the null hypothesis $H_0$ if $p$-value \<
    $0.05$.

    In this case, since $p$-value $= 0.982 > 0.05$, we do not reject $H_0$. Hence, a students preferred sport is independent of gender.
