# AI376

**Maximum mark: 6**

Alexandra carries out a $\chi^2$ test at the
$5$ % significance level to determine whether a
person's gender impacts their chosen professional field. She focuses on
the fields of accounting, sales and marketing, and web design. She
surveyed $230$ people and the results are shown in the table below.

![ac2a0c3bea69cc7059a50b074451a286410e866e.svg](images/ac2a0c3bea69cc7059a50b074451a286410e866e.svg)

1.  State the null hypothesis, $H_0$, for this test. [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the expected number of female web designers. [2 marks]

3.  Find the $p$ -value for this test.
    [2 marks]

Alexandra accepts $H_0$.

4.  State a reason why Alexandra is incorrect in doing so.
    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $H_0:  \text{ The professional field is independent of the gender}$

2.  Let's complete the contingency table with the marginal frequencies
    as follows. The marginal frequencies of female web designers are
    highlighted in red.

    ![12372bfdba4e04b8ab7c9162a5a8d46e2dbd0690.svg](images/12372bfdba4e04b8ab7c9162a5a8d46e2dbd0690.svg)

    Hence the expected number of female web designers is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    E_{23} &= \dfrac{45 \times 115}{230} \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}= 22.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    $p\text{-value} \approx 0.0273$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

4.  Recall that for a test of independence, we reject the null
    hypothesis $H_0$ if the $p$-value is less than the significance
    level.

    In this case, since $p$-value $= 0.0273 < 0.05$, we reject $H_0$. Therefore Alexandra is incorrect in accepting $H_0$.
