# AI598

**Maximum mark: 12**

A game producer designs an online game in which players are supposed to walk through seven levels. For each level passed, they will be offered a diamond token. The following frequency table shows the results of $620$ players in terms of number of diamond tokens they collect after the game.

| **Number of diamonds** | **Frequency** |
|:---:|:---:|
| $0$ | $7$ |
| $1$ | $32$ |
| $2$ | $99$ |
| $3$ | $175$ |
| $4$ | $178$ |
| $5$ | $108$ |
| $6$ | $13$ |
| $7$ | $8$ |

1.  The producer claims that these levels are all designed such that to be fair in terms of winning probability (i.e. $50\%$ chance of winning). A suitable $\chi^2$ test is hence conducted to test this claim.

    1. State the null and alternative hypotheses.

    2. Find the $p$-value.

    3. Write down your conclusion.[8 marks]

2.  Comment on how to reduce the probability of committing a type I error.[2 marks]

3.  1. State the meaning of a type II error.

    2. Write down how to proceed if it is required to reduce the probability of committing both a type I and type II error.[2 marks]

1.  1. The null and alternative hypotheses are

        \begin{aligned}
            &\boxed{\text{H}_0: \text{ all levels are fair (or the data are represented by B}(7,\,0.5))} \\[8pt]
