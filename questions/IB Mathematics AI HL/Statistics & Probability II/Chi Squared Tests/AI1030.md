# AI1030

**Maximum mark: 6**

400 people were asked to select their preferred streaming service, among four options. The age bracket of each person was recorded. The results of the survey are shown below.

![ai1030](images/ai1030.PNG)

A $\chi^2$ test is carried out at the $5\%$ significance level with hypotheses: 

$$

\begin{aligned}

        &\text{H$_0$: the preferred streaming service is independent of age bracket} \\[-0.1em]

        &\text{H$_1$: the preferred streaming service is not independent of age bracket}

\end{aligned}

$$ 

The $\chi^2$ critical value for this test is $16.9$.

1. Find the value of $a$. [1 mark]

2. Determine the number of degrees of freedom for this test. [1 mark]

3. Use your graphic display calculator to find the $\chi^2$ statistic for this test. [2 marks]

4. State the conclusion for this test. Justify your answer. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Looking at the row for Age $40-49$, we have 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    30 + 32 + 23 + a &= 100 \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-0.57em}\boxed{a = 15} 

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The degrees of freedom in a test of independence is given by $v = (r-1)(c-1)$, where $r$ is the number of rows and $c$ is the number of columns. From the table we see that there are $4$ rows and $4$ columns. Therefore,

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    v &= (4-1)(4-1) \\[6pt]

    & \hspace{-0.57em}\boxed{v = 9}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. $$\boxed{\chi^2 \approx 21.2} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]$$

4. Recall that we would reject the null hypothesis $H_0$ if $\chi^2 > \chi^2_{\text{crit}}$. In this case, we are given $\chi^2_{\text{crit}} = 16.9$.

    Since $\chi^2 = 21.2 > 16.9$, we reject $H_0$. So at the $5\%$ significance level, there is sufficient evidence to conclude that the preferred streaming service is not independent of age bracket.
