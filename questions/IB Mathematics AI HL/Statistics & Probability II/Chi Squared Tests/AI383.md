# AI383

**Maximum mark: 8**

The call centre at the Austin Police Department receives hundreds of
calls for service from citizens every week. To determine whether the
phone calls are uniformly distributed among the days in the week, a
random sample of $147$ calls are studied for their days of occurrence.
The results are shown below.

![859c478853e3d164f0130cd9b3ee942615422e3d.svg](images/859c478853e3d164f0130cd9b3ee942615422e3d.svg)

1.  Complete the table below with the expected frequencies, on the
     [2 marks] assumption that each day should be
    equally proportional in calls received.

    ![c32e6e4b625e6190a8a5696a7dc6f31411511593.svg](images/c32e6e4b625e6190a8a5696a7dc6f31411511593.svg)

2.  A $\chi^2$ goodness of fit test is performed at a $5\%$ significance
    level.

    1.  State the null and alternate hypotheses.
    2.  Write down the number of degrees of freedom.

    3.  Using your graphics display calculator, find the
        $\chi^2$-statistic and p-value. [4 marks]

3.  The critical value is $12.592$. Determine the conclusion for the
    test, providing [2 marks] a reason.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since each day is equally likely, the expected proportion of each
    day is $p_{0i} = \dfrac 1 7$. Using the formula of expected
    frequency $E_i = np_{0i}$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    E_i &= 147 \times \dfrac{1}{7} \\[3pt]
    &= 21
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    So the expected frequencies are as follows.

    ![3f15445d1d685b3ab523b09e359ff125487b3306.svg](images/3f15445d1d685b3ab523b09e359ff125487b3306.svg)

2.
    1.

        $H_0$: Calls are evenly proportioned over the 7 days of the week. \newline $H_1$: Calls are not evenly proportioned over the 7 days of the week.

    2.  Using the formula $df = k - 1$ where $k$ is the number of
        categories (in this case, the number of days in the week), we
        get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        df &= 7-1 \\[3pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}= 6
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3.
        $\chi^2 = 2.95$

        $p\text{-value} \approx 0.815$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

3.  Recall that we reject the null hypothesis if the $p$-value is less
    than the significance level or if $\chi^2 > \chi^2_{\text{crit}}$.
    In this case, the significance level is given as $0.05$ and the
    critical value is $12.592$.

    Since $p$-value $= 0.8151 > 0.05$ or $\chi^2 = 2.95 < 12.592$, we do not reject $H_0$. Hence, there is insufficient evidence to conclude that the calls are not evenly proportioned over the 7 days of the week.
