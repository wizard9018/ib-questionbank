**Maximum mark: 7**

The total sales of soup ($S$) in US dollars and temperature ($T$) in degrees celsius  follow a bivariate normal distribution with PMCC $\rho$. 

The following table shows the values of eight random observations of $(S,T)$.

![ai1144](images/ai1144.PNG)

A test is to be conducted at the $5\%$ significance level to determine whether there is a linear correlation between $S$ and $T$.

1. State the null and alternate hypotheses for the test.[2 marks]

2. Determine the value of

    1. the product-moment correlation coefficient, $r$, of the sample.

    2. the corresponding $p$-value.[3 marks]

3. State whether there is sufficient evidence to claim whether $S$ and $T$ are not linearly correlated. Justify your answer. [2 marks]
