# AI1230

**Maximum mark: 6**

Anita has been on a driving tour around the United States and thinks she noticed that the higher the altitude a city was above sea level, the colder it was. She decides to test whether there is a negative linear relationship between the height above sea level and average annual temperature for cities in the United States. 

Anita visited 10 cities on her travels and decides to use these as a sample for her test. She collects the following data

| City | Height above sea level (m) | Average annual temperature ($^\circ$C) |

| :--- | :---: | :---: |

| Atlanta | 230 | 17 |

| Chicago | 180 | 10 |

| Dallas | 130 | 19 |

| Denver | 1600 | 10 |

| Detroit | 200 | 10 |

| Las Vegas | 610 | 20 |

| Los Angeles | 90 | 18 |

| New York | 10 | 13 |

| Phoenix | 330 | 24 |

| Salt Lake City | 1290 | 12 |

1. State the sampling method used by Anita.[1 mark]

2. Write down the null and alternate hypotheses for Anita's test.[1 mark]

Anita conducts her test at the 10\% significance level.

3. Calculate the $p$-value for the test.[2 marks]

4. State the conclusion to the test. Justify your answer.[2 marks]
