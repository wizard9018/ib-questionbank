# AI586

**Maximum mark: 6**

A bank conducts a study to determine if the amount of customers loans
and the number times they are late making their monthly repayments are
related. Eight customers were sampled at random and the data collected
for the previous financial year, as shown on the table below.

![ea59ae5de4c9314f3ac1b2831ed51f3952d902da.svg](images/ea59ae5de4c9314f3ac1b2831ed51f3952d902da.svg)

A test at the 5% significance level is conducted to determine if there
is a linear relationship between the variables.

1.  State the
    1.  null hypothesis, H$_0$,

    2.  alternative hypothesis, H$_1$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the $p$-value for the test and hence the conclusion of the
    test. [2 marks]

3.  Find the sample correlation coefficient and interpret the value.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Letting $\rho$ be the population correlation coefficient, we are
    testing

    1.
        $\text{H}_0: \rho = 0$

    2.
        $\text{H}_1: \rho \neq 0$

2.
    $p \text{-value} = 0.221$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    Since $p$-value = 0.221 > 0.05, we do not reject H$_0$. Thus, there is insufficient evidence to conclude that there is a linear relationship between the loan amount and the number of times customers are late making their monthly payments.

3.
    $r = 0.4874$
    \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    The sample correlation coefficient of $0.4874$ implies moderate evidence for a linear relationship, however a sample size of 8 is small, so it is recommended to sample more customers to gain confidence in the evidence of a linear relationship.
