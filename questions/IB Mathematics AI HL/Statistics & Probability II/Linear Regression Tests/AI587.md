# AI587

**Maximum mark: 7**

An aeronautical engineering student conducts a study to determine it
heavier aircrafts require a longer takeoff runway length. The student
takes a sample of $10$ aircrafts, the runway length required to takeoff
from the ground, and their total aircraft weight. The results of this
study are shown in the table below.

![e501bcb6a0f1f933aaf1073333c7af15e0a45663.svg](images/e501bcb6a0f1f933aaf1073333c7af15e0a45663.svg)

The student decided to use a 5% significance level to determine if there
is a positive correlation between the takeoff weight and the takeoff
length.

1.  For this test,
    1.  state the null and alternative hypotheses, H$_0$ and H$_1$,

    2.  find the $p$-value,
    3.  state the conclusion. [4 marks]

2.
    1.  Find the line of regression equation to estimate the takeoff
        length, $l$, for a given takeoff weight, $w$.

    2.  Hence, estimate the takeoff distance for an aircraft weighing
        $58$ tonnes, rounded to the nearest meter. [2 marks]

3.  Explain why the line of regression equation found in part (b) has
    limitations when estimating the takeoff distance for an aircraft
    weighing $70$ tonnes. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $\rho$ be the correlation coefficient between takeoff weight and
    takeoff length. We are testing

    1.
        $\text{H}_0: \rho = 0$

        $\text{H}_1: \rho > 0$

    2.
        $p \text{-value} = 0.00135$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    3.

        Since $p$-value = 0.00135 < 0.05, we reject the null hypothesis. Therefore, there is sufficient evidence to conclude that there is a positive correlation between takeoff weight and takeoff length.

2.
    1.  Using GDC, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        &l = 35.2\,w - 857
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Substituting $w = 58$ into the regression model, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        l &=  35.2(58) - 857\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}= 1185 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.

    A weight of $70$ tonnes is outside the data range used to generated the line of regression equation, it is not suitable to extrapolate it.
