# AI600

**Maximum mark: 16**

The altitude at origin, in metres, and the amount of variation in timing of bud flush of *Pinus sylvestris*, in days, at nine distinct regions in Aberdeen, Scotland, are shown in the table below. These data are assumed to be a random sample taken from a bivariate normal distribution, with correlation coefficient $\rho$.

| **Altitude ($\boldsymbol{x}$)** | $252$ | $84$ | $175$ | $437$ | $350$ | $243$ | $146$ | $378$ | $221$ |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Variation in timing ($\boldsymbol{y}$)** | $8$ | $5$ | $9$ | $12$ | $10$ | $7$ | $8$ | $9$ | $6$ |

A phenologist wishes to use these data to determine whether there is a positive correlation between altitude and amount of variation in timing of bud flush.

1.  State the null and alternative hypotheses.[2 marks]

2.  Determine the product moment correlation coefficient for these data.[2 marks]

3.  Determine the associated $p$-value and state the conclusion in the context of the question.[2 marks]

4.  Find the equation of the regression line of $y$ on $x$.[2 marks]

5.  Estimate the amount of variation in bud flush timing in a region at a $400$ m altitude.[2 marks]

6.  Determine the angle between the regression line of $y$ on $x$ and that of $x$ on $y$. Give your answer to $3$ significant figures.[6 marks]

        &\boxed{\text{H}_1: \rho > 0}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using GDC, the product moment correlation coefficient is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        r &= 0.8011\ldots \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]
        r &= \boxed{0.801} \hspace{1em} (3\text{ s.f.}) \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using GDC, the $p$-value is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        p\text{-value} &= 0.004726\ldots \\[6pt]
        p\text{-value} &= \boxed{0.00473} \hspace{1em} (3\text{ s.f.}) \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $\begin{array}{l} \text{Since } p\text{-value } = 0.00473 < 0.05 \text{, there is sufficient evidence to reject } \text{H}_0 \text{. Hence we conclude that there} \\ \text{is a positive correlation between altitude and amount of variation in bud flush timing.} \end{array}$

    $$\\[-0.6em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{R1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using GDC, the equation of the regression line of $y$ on $x$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        y &= 0.01466\ldots x + 4.497\ldots \\[6pt]
        &\boxed{y = 0.0147x + 4.50} \tag*{\textbf{A2}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Substituting $x = 400$ into the regression line from part (d).
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        y &= 0.01466\ldots(400) + 4.497\ldots \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]
        &= 10.36\ldots \\[6pt]
        y &= \boxed{10.4 \text{ days}} \hspace{1em} (3\text{ s.f.}) \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

6.  Using GDC, the equation of the regression line of $x$ on $y$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        x &= 43.76\ldots y - 105.8\ldots \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]
        x &= \boxed{43.8y - 106} \hspace{1em} (3\text{ s.f.}) \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The gradient of the regression line of $x$ on $y$, expressed as $y$ on $x$, is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        m_1 &= \dfrac{1}{43.76\ldots} \\[10pt]
        &= 0.02285\ldots \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    From part (d), the gradient of the regression line of $y$ on $x$ is $m_2 = 0.01467\ldots$

    The angle each line makes with the positive $x$-axis is found using $\arctan$ of its gradient.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \alpha_1 &= \arctan(0.01467\ldots) = 0.8405\ldots^{\circ} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]
        \alpha_2 &= \arctan(0.02285\ldots) = 1.309\ldots^{\circ} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The angle between the two lines is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \theta &= |\alpha_2 - \alpha_1| \\[6pt]
        &= |1.309\ldots - 0.8405\ldots| \\[6pt]
        &= 0.4685\ldots^{\circ} \\[6pt]
        \theta &= \boxed{0.469^{\circ}} \hspace{1em} (3\text{ s.f.}) \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
