# AI170

**Maximum mark: 19**

Sandra operates a pizzeria that has $24$ hours delivery service. Sandra
thinks that the daily total production cost, $C$, in euros (EUR), and
the number of pizzas made per day, $N$, can be related by the equation

$$
C = aN^b + K,
$$
where $a,b$ and $K$ are positive constants.

Sandra estimates that the daily total fixed cost of
$\text{operating}$ the $\text{pizzeria}$ is $1000$ EUR.

1.  Write down the value of $K$. [1 mark]

After analysing the bookkeeping records of a particular working week,
Sandra finds the data given below.

![7bb8aad63a8010d3b18aca0ec2b8af0e9af7f108.svg](images/7bb8aad63a8010d3b18aca0ec2b8af0e9af7f108.svg)

2.  Draw a scatter diagram of $\log_2(C-K)$ versus $\log_2 N$,
    scaling and shifting the axes if needed. [4 marks]

3.  State the type of model that best fits the data displayed
    on your scatter diagram from **part (b)**. [1 mark]

4.  Write down the equation of the regression line of
    $\log_2(C-K)$ on $\log_2 N$. [2 marks]

5.  Hence find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$.
    [4 marks]

Sandra wants to increase the selling rate of pizzas up to $800$ items
per day.

6.  Using Sandra's equation, estimate the daily total cost of
    producing $800$ $\text{pizzas}$.[2 marks]

7.  State whether it is valid to use Sandra's equation to
    estimate the daily total cost of producing $800$ pizzas. Give a
    reason for your answer. [1 mark]

8.
    1.  Describe how the data must be entered into your G.D.C. to
        determine Sandra's equation using power regression method.

    2.  Hence verify your answers to **part (e)**. [4 marks]

4.
    $\log_2(C-1000) = 0.853\log_2 N + 3.02$ \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

5.  Using the properties of logarithms, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.5em} \log_2(C-1000) &= 0.853\log_2 N + 3.02 \\[6pt]
    \log_2(C-1000) &= \log_2 N^{0.853} + \log_2 2^{3.02} \\[6pt]
    \log_2(C-1000) &= \log_2 (8.11N^{0.853}) \hspace{3em} [2^{3.02} \approx 8.11] \\[6pt]
    C - 1000 &= 8.11N^{0.853} \\[6pt]
    C &= 8.11N^{0.853} + 1000
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find
     $a = 8.11$
    and  $b = 0.853$

6.  Evaluating $C$ when $N = 800$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.6em} C &= 8.11(800)^{0.853} + 1000 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 3430 \text{ EUR}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
