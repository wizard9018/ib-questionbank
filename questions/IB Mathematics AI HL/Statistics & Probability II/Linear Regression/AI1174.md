# AI1174

**Maximum mark: 7**

A real estate agent is studying the relationship between the size of a house (in square meters) and its selling price (in thousands of dollars). The data collected for 8 houses is shown in the table below:

![1174](images/1174.JPG)

1. Find the equation of the regression line $y$ on $x$.[2 marks]

2. Explain, in context, the meaning of the coefficient of $x$ in your equation.[2 marks]

The real estate agent is listing a house for sale that has an area of 72 square metres.

3. Estimate the likely selling price of this house. Give your answer correct to $\bm{2}\,\mathrm{\bold{significant\,figures.}}$[2 marks]

The real estate agent has just sold a house for $$\$150\,000$$.

4. Explain why it would not be appropriate to use the equation found in part (a) to estimate the size of this house.[1 mark]
