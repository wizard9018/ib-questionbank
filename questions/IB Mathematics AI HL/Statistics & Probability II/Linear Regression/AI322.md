# AI322

**Maximum mark: 7**

Gippsland in Victoria, Australia, is an important milk-producing
farmland area. Some people might argue that, because of transportation
costs, the cost of milk increases with the distance of markets from
Gippsland. The table below shows the milk price in eight markets the
Gippsland farmers supply to.

![d0c84ebcca93ee01309e5890c2b205e2b0035d5f.svg](images/d0c84ebcca93ee01309e5890c2b205e2b0035d5f.svg)

1.  Find the Pearson's product moment correlation coefficient for this
    data, $r$. [2 marks]

2.  Comment on the result obtained for $r$. [1 mark]

The equation of the regression line for this data is in the form
$y = mx + c$.

3.
    1.  Find the value of $m$ and comment on its meaning.

    2.  Find the value of $c$ and interpret its meaning if appropriate.
        If not appropriate, explain why. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $r \approx 0.941$
    \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

2.

    The correlation coefficient is positive and close to $1$. We can conclude that there is a strong, positive linear relationship between the distance from Gippsland and the milk price.

3.
    1.  Using G.D.C, we obtain the equation of the regression line
        below.
        $$
        y = 0.0385x + 2.16
        $$

        $m = 0.0385$. This means the cost of milk will increase by about $4$ cents for every additional $100$ km distance from Gippsland.

    2.

        $c = 2.16$. It is appropriate to interpret the meaning of $c$ as a distance of $x=0$ implies a market within the Gippsland region, and this means the cost of milk at Gippsland is about $2.16$ AUD per $2$-litre bottle.
