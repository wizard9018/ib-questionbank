2. 1. Interpret the meaning of the gradient in the context of the question.

    2. State appropriate units for the gradient. [2 marks]

3. Find the equation of the line. Give your answer in the form $y=mx+c$.[2 marks]

4. Use Adam's model to estimate the population of Philadelphia in the year 2000.[2 marks]

Chuck obtains a model for the data by using linear regression.

5. 1. Find the equation of the regression line $y$ on $x$.

    2. Find the value of Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient, $r$. [3 marks]

6. Use Chuck's model to estimate the population of Philadelphia in the year 2000.[2 marks]

Chuck uses his model to predict the year when the population of Philadelphia will first reach $2\hspace{0.1em}500\hspace{0.1em}000$.

7. State two reasons why Chuck's prediction may not be valid.[2 marks]
