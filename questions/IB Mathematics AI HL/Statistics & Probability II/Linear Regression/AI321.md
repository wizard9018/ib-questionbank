# AI321

**Maximum mark: 7**

A clothes tailoring shop received an order for pants of different waist
and length sizes. The made-to-order measurements for this order were
recorded and are shown in the following table.

![44cd736f021d1a2b3d07511eb548249a3fedb4d9.svg](images/44cd736f021d1a2b3d07511eb548249a3fedb4d9.svg)

1.  Find the Pearson's product moment correlation coefficient, $r$, for
    this data. [2 marks]

2.  Comment on the result found for $r$. [1 mark]

The equation of the regression line is in the form $y = mx + c$.

3.
    1.  Find the value of $m$ and comment on its meaning.

    2.  Find the value of $c$ and interpret its meaning if appropriate.
        If not appropriate, explain why. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $r \approx 0.92$
    \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

2.

    The correlation coefficient is positive and close to $1$. We can conclude that there is a strong, positive linear relationship between waist circumference and length of the pants.

3.
    1.  Using G.D.C, we obtain the equation of the regression line
        below.
        $$
        y = 0.787x + 41.097
        $$

        $m = 0.787$. This means the length of a pant will increase by $0.787$ cm for every additional $1$ cm of waist circumference.

    2.

        $c \approx 41.1$. It is not appropriate to interpret the meaning of $c$ as the waist circumference ($x$) cannot be zero.
