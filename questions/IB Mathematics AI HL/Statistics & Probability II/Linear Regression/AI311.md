# AI311

**Maximum mark: 7**

A fast food store owner recorded the daily number of burgers and hot
dogs sold in her restaurant. The following table shows this data for the
last eight days. This data is also shown on the scatter diagram below.

![1e241dcf40b5a4598dd6736180afc7e2c83cacbb.svg](images/1e241dcf40b5a4598dd6736180afc7e2c83cacbb.svg)

![e113c84d2472153181cf3d36acaf766c66c7a429.svg](images/e113c84d2472153181cf3d36acaf766c66c7a429.svg)

The number of burgers sold varies linearly with the number of hot dogs
sold.

1.  Find
    1.  the mean number of burgers, $\bar{x}$ ;

    2.  the mean number of hot dogs, $\bar{y}$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the line of regression equation, $y$ on $x$, and plot on the
    scatter diagram above. [3 marks]

3.  Use the line of regression equation, $y$ on $x$, to estimate the
    number of hot dogs sold on a day when $22$ burgers are sold.
    [2 marks]

---

    1.

        \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    2.
        $\bar{y} = 41.3$
        \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

2.  The equation of the regression line and its plot are as following.
    $$
    y = 1.43x + 2.06 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

    ![6874fb7e7bdfa94d9932a9ac3dec42f3ee800b8a.svg](images/6874fb7e7bdfa94d9932a9ac3dec42f3ee800b8a.svg)

3.  Substituting $x = 22$ into the equation of the regression line, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 1.43(22) + 2.06 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 33.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
