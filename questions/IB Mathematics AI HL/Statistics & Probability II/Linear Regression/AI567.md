# AI567

**Maximum mark: 6**

Wynn conducts an experiment to investigate the rate of photosynthesis,
measured by oxygen production (mL), of an algae species over time. Wynn
puts six pieces of the algae in six different tubes filled with water
and places them at the same distance from the light source. A sample of
the oxygen production readings of these tubes after some time are shown
in the table below.

![fe648101db96c3c687742a1e2373c08593e5aa97.svg](images/fe648101db96c3c687742a1e2373c08593e5aa97.svg)

1.
    1.  Find the line of regression equation for the oxygen production,
        $P$, in terms of minutes exposed to the light, $t$.

    2.  Interpret your model in the context of this question.
        [2 marks]

2.
    1.  Find the coefficient of determination, $r^2$.

    2.  Interpret the $r^2$ in the context of this question. [2 marks]

3.  Use the model found in part (a) to predict the oxygen produced after
    $25$ minutes exposed to the light. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using GDC, we get.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        &P = 0.00414\,t + 0.0132
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.

        For every minute the algae is exposed to the light, the rate of oxygen production increases by $0.0046\,$mL on average.

2.
    1.  Using GDC, we get
         $r^2 \approx 0.88$
    2.

        About 88\% of the variation in oxygen production by the algae can be attributed to the time the algae has been exposed to the light source.

3.  Substituting $t = 25$ into the line of regression equation, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    P &= 0.00414(25) + 0.0132 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.117 \hspace{0.25em}\text{mL}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
