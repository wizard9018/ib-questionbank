# AI320

**Maximum mark: 6**

At Changi Airport in Singapore, six passengers were surveyed on the
number of stores they had shopped in, and the waiting time for the
flight, in minutes. The data is shown in the scatter diagram below,
where $x$ represents the waiting time for their flight and $y$
represents the number of stores they had shopped in.

![9c25f6d44db984786cf449a60f8dd3a59c0eadda.svg](images/9c25f6d44db984786cf449a60f8dd3a59c0eadda.svg)

1.  Use the scatter diagram above to complete the missing cells in the
    table below. [2 marks]

    |$\hspace{2em}x\hspace{2em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}50\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}35\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}50\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}40\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}75\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}65\hspace{0.5em}$|
    |:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
    |$y$||1|3|||||

2.  Find the Pearson's product moment correlation coefficient, $r$, for
    this data. [2 marks]

3.  Give two reasons why it would not be valid to use this scatter
    diagram to predict the number of stores a passenger will shop in,
    who has a $120$ minute wait time. [2 marks]
