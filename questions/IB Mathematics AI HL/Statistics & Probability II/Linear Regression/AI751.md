# AI751

**Maximum mark: 7**

An e-commerce company has eight distribution centres and wants to know if there is a relationship between the number of employees at each centre and the average delivery time of sales. The data involved are summarized in the following table.

![biv](images/biv.JPG)

1. From the data for these eight centres

    1. Calculate the Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient, $r$.

    2. Describe the correlation between the number of employees and the average delivery time. [3 marks]

2. Find the equation of the regression line, $y$ on $x$, in the form $y = mx + c$. Give the values of $m$ and $c$ to $3$ significant figures. [2 marks]

The company opened a new distribution centre recently with 425 employees.

3. Use your regression line, the solution to part (b), to estimate the average delivery time for this new distribution centre. [2 marks]

---

1. 1. $r = -0.922 \,\text{ (3 s.f.)}$ $\hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]$
        $$

        \tag*{\textbf{A2}}
        \end{aligned}

    2. :::answer
        Since the correlation coefficient is negative and close to $1$, there is a (very) strong negative linear correlation between the number of employees and the average delivery time.

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{R1}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2. $y = -0.116\hspace{0.05em}x + 115 \,\text{ (3 s.f.)} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3. Substituting $x = 425$ into the equation of the regression line, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= -0.116(425) + 115 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
            &\hspace{0em}\boxed{= 65.7} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
