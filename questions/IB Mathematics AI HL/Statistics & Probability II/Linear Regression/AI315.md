# AI315

**Maximum mark: 19**

A group of eight undergraduate engineering students were surveyed to
determine their satisfaction with their semester 1 mathematics course.
The following table shows their responses using a seven-point scale, and
their final grades for this course.

![b7f23c88bdb56f48a9323440437fc96d1781cef1.svg](images/b7f23c88bdb56f48a9323440437fc96d1781cef1.svg)

1.  On the axes below, draw a scatter diagram for this data. Use a scale
    of $1$ unit to represent ratings on the $x$-axis and $1$ unit to
    represent $5$ grade percent on the $y$-axis. [3 marks]

    ![4aab8d86f17c014ab9b3f3d13af1b19c252979e7.svg](images/4aab8d86f17c014ab9b3f3d13af1b19c252979e7.svg)

2.
    1.  Calculate the Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient
        for this data, $r$.

    2.  Describe the correlation between students' mathematics course
        grades and survey results. [4 marks]

3.  Calculate
    1.  the mean rating, $\bar{x}$;

    2.  the mean grade, $\bar{y}$. [2 marks]

4.  Plot the point M$(\bar{x}, \bar{y})$ on your scatter diagram.
    [2 marks]

5.  Find the line of regression equation, $y$ on $x$. [2 marks]

6.  Draw the regression line from part (e) on your scatter diagram.
    [2 marks]

7.  Use the line of the regression to estimate the final grade for the
    student who gave a $7$ rating for the course. [2 marks]

8.  State whether your estimate is reliable and justify your answer.
    [2 marks]
