# AI1028

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following table shows the total revenue, $y$, in Australian dollars (AUD), obtained daily during the first week of January $2020$, by Peppy's Pizza restaurant and the number of guests, $x$, served.

|$\hspace{0.5em}\text{Number of Guests }(x)\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}45\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}54\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}39\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}51\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}89\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}83\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}90\hspace{0.5em}$|

|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|

|Revenue in AUD $(y)$|410|516|423|558|906|843|940|

1. 1. Calculate the Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient, $r$, for this data.

    2. Hence comment on the result. [3 marks]

2. Write down the equation of the regression line $y$ on $x$. [1 mark]

3. Use the equation of the regression line to estimate the revenue of serving $70$ guests. Give your answer correct to $\textbf{the nearest AUD}$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 1. $\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{r = 0.991}\hspace{2em}[\text{by using G.D.C.}]$

    2. $\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{\text{Strong, positive correlation between number of guests and revenue}}$

2. $\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{y = 10.4x - 14.3}\hspace{3em}[\text{by using G.D.C.}]$

3. Evaluating $y = 10.4x - 14.3$ for $x = 70$, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned} 

    y &= 10.4(70) - 14.3 \\[6pt] 

    &= 713.7 \\[6pt]

    &\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{\approx 714 \text{ AUD}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
