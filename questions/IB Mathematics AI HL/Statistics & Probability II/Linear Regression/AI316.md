# AI316

**Maximum mark: 17**

The following table shows the average height, $x$, and the average trunk
diameter, $y$, of eight species of tree.

![6d2fc28957de85629a1171c6a238d611a6f0c8f9.svg](images/6d2fc28957de85629a1171c6a238d611a6f0c8f9.svg)

1.  Find the range of the average heights for these eight species of
    tree, correct to two decimal places. [2 marks]

2.  From the data from of these eight species of tree
    1.  calculate the Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient,
        $r$;

    2.  describe the correlation between the average height and the
        average trunk diameter. [4 marks]

3.  Write down the line of regression equation, $y$ on $x$, in the form
    $y = mx + c$. [2 marks]

The average height of another tree species, Black Ash, is $15.2$ m.

4.  Use your regression line to estimate the average trunk
    diameter of Black Ash. [2 marks]

The average diameter of Black Ash is found to be $0.445$ m.

5.  Find the percentage error in your estimate in part (d).
    [2 marks]

The average height of another tree species, Alpine Ash, is $87.5$ m.

6.  State whether it is valid to use the regression line to
    estimate the average trunk diameter of Alpine Ash. Give a reason for
    your answer. [2 marks]

7.  Complete the two empty rank columns in the table and state
    why the Spearman's rank correlation coefficient could be calculated
    by inspection. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The range of the average heights for these eight species of tree is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{[range of $x$]} &= \text{Max} - \text{Min} \\
    &= 32.6 - 8.25 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}24.35
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.
        $r \approx 0.959$
        \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    2.

        Since the correlation coefficient is positive and close to $1$, there is a very strong positive correlation between the average height and the average trunk diameter.

3.
    $y = 0.0551x - 0.364$
    \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

4.  Substituting $x = 15.2$ into the equation of the regression line, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 0.0551(15.2) - 0.364 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.474 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Using the formula for the percentage error
    $\epsilon = \left| \dfrac{\text{V}_\text{A} - \text{V}_\text{E}}{\text{V}_\text{E}}\right| \times 100 \%$
    with $\text{V}_\text{A} = 0.474$ and $\text{V}_\text{E} = 0.445$, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \epsilon &= \left| \dfrac{0.474 - 0.445}{0.445}\right| \times 100\%  \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx  6.52\%
    \end{aligned}
    $$

6.

    It is not valid to use the regression line to estimate the average trunk diameter of Alpine Ash as $x = 87.5$ falls outside the data range $[8.25, 32.6]$. Hence, this would be an extrapolating estimator.

7.  Ranking the $x$ and $y$ values in descending order, we obtain the
    $X$ ranks and $Y$ ranks shown in the table below.

    ![c6580bef8349795eba9994ac82050715586915f2.svg](images/c6580bef8349795eba9994ac82050715586915f2.svg)

    By inspection, the Spearman's rank correlation coefficient will be $1.0$ as there is a direct match in the $X$ and $Y$ ranks.
