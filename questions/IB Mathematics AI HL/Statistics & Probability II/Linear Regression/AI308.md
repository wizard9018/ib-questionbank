# AI308

**Maximum mark: 6**

The number of daily visitors to the famous 'Bondi Beach' in Sydney,
Australia, and the daily temperature on those days, were recorded for
eight days in February. The table below shows this data and has been
ordered in ascending order by temperature.

![6c40b1beafba3626a3fe2e08a7b2d90049b49a23.svg](images/6c40b1beafba3626a3fe2e08a7b2d90049b49a23.svg)

The number of visitors to Bondi Beach varies linearly with the
temperature.

1.  Find
    1.  Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient,
        $r$ ;

    2.  the equation of the regression line $y$ on $x$. [4 marks]

2.  Use the equation of the regression line $y$ on $x$ to estimate the
    number of visitors to Bondi Beach during a day the temperature is
    $26^\circ$C. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $r \approx 1$
        \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    2.
        $y = 40.3 x - 403$
        \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

2.  Substituting $x = 26$ into the equation of the regression line, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 40.3(26) - 403 \\[5pt]
    &\approx 645
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    There will be approximately
     $645 \text{ visitors}$
    to Bondi Beach on a $26^\circ$C temperature day.
