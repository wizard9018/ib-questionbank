# AI1214

**Maximum mark: 9**

A researcher investigates the relationship between the average daily temperature, in degrees Celsius (°C) and the amount of electricity used, in kilowatt-hours (kWh) for air conditioning in a Barcelona cafe during the summer. The data collected over six days is shown below

$\\[1em]$

| $\textbf{Temperature} \,$ ($\bm{^{\circ}\text{C}}$) | $24$ | $26$ | $25$ | $30$ | $32$ | $31$ |

| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |

| $\textbf{Electricity Use} \,$ ($\textbf{kWh}$) | &nbsp;&nbsp;120&nbsp;&nbsp; | &nbsp;&nbsp;135&nbsp;&nbsp; | &nbsp;&nbsp;132&nbsp;&nbsp; | &nbsp;&nbsp;161&nbsp;&nbsp; | &nbsp;&nbsp;183&nbsp;&nbsp; | &nbsp;&nbsp;178&nbsp;&nbsp; |

1. 1. Find the Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient, $r$, for this data.

    2. Interpret the value of  $r$  in context.[4 marks]

2. Find the equation of the regression line $y$ on $x$. Give your answers for the gradient and intercept correct to 2 decimal places.[2 marks]

3. Using your regression line from part (b), estimate the electricity use on a day with an average temperature of 39°C.[2 marks]

4. The researcher wants to use the regression model to predict electricity usage on a day when the temperature is 39°C. Comment on whether this prediction is reliable.[1 mark]
