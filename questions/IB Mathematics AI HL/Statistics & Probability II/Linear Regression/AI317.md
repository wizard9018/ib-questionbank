# AI317

**Maximum mark: 14**

A health study was conducted on $8$ male participants, measuring their
average daily sugar intake (in grams) and their body fat percentage.
This data were used to determine whether sugar intake influences male
body fat levels. The results are shown in the table.

![b63b644c9bc5cc36d695e9ca4e715825f3158f7b.svg](images/b63b644c9bc5cc36d695e9ca4e715825f3158f7b.svg)

1.  Use your graphic calculator to find
    1.  $\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mux\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu$,
        the mean sugar intake;

    2.  $\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5muy\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu$,
        the mean body fat percentage;

    3.  $r$, the Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient.
        [4 marks]

The equation of the regression line $y$ on $x$ is in the form
$y = mx + c$.

2.
    1.  Find the values of $m$ and $c$ for this data.

    2.  Show that the point M$(\bar{x},\bar{y})$ lies on the regression
        line $y$ on $x$. [4 marks]

A ninth male participant, Daniel, has the average daily sugar intake of
$32$ grams.

3.
    1.  Use the regression line $y$ on $x$ to estimate Daniel's body fat
        percentage.

    2.  Justify whether it is valid to use the regression line $y$ on
        $x$ to estimate Daniel's body fat percentage. [4 marks]

Daniel's body fat percentage is measured to be
$12$ %.

4.  Calculate the percentage error in Daniel's estimated body
    fat percentage, found in part (c). [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mux\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu = 65.5$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    2.
        $\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5muy\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu = 21$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    3.
        $r \approx 0.972$
        \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

2.
    1.  Using G.D.C, we get the equation of the regression line as
        follows.

        $$
        y = 0.238x + 5.4 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        $$
        Thus,

        $m = 0.238, \, c = 5.4.$
    2.  Substituting $x = 65.5$ and $y = 21$ into the equation of the
        regression line, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        21 &= 0.238(65.5) + 5.4 \\
        21 &= 21
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the point $M(65.5, 21)$, which is
        $M(\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mux\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu, \mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5muy\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu)$,
        lies on the regression line.

3.
    1.  Substituting $x = 32$ into the equation of the regression line,
        we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        y &= 0.238(32) + 5.4 \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.525em}\approx 13\%
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.

        It is valid to use the regression line to estimate Daniel's body fat percentage~as $x=32$ falls within the data range $[24,120]$.

4.  Using the formula for the percentage error
    $\epsilon = \left| \dfrac{\text{V}_\text{A} - \text{V}_\text{E}}{\text{V}_\text{E}}\right| \times 100 \%$
    with $\text{V}_\text{A} = 13$ and $\text{V}_\text{E} = 12$, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \epsilon &= \left| \dfrac{13 - 12}{12}\right| \times 100\% \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{0.525em}\approx  8.33\%
    \end{aligned}
    $$
