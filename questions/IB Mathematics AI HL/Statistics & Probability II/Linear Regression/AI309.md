# AI309

**Maximum mark: 12**

The average price for one square metre of land is recorded for seven
different locations in Melbourne, Australia. The following table shows
the average prices for these locations and the distances from the centre
of the Melbourne city. This data is also shown on the scatter diagram
below.

![c9208260dce54ccda835e0f42abc7ec9f9a8aae5.svg](images/c9208260dce54ccda835e0f42abc7ec9f9a8aae5.svg)

![a2e3345359dc176d2f32a342db6cecece57ae579.svg](images/a2e3345359dc176d2f32a342db6cecece57ae579.svg)

1.  Use the scatter diagram to find the value of $a$ and $b$ in the
    table. [2 marks]

2.  Calculate

    1.  the mean distance from the Melbourne city centre,
        $\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mux\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu$ ;

    2.  the mean $1$ m$^2$ price, in thousands
        AUD,
        $\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5muy\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu$ ;

    3.  the Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient, $r$.
        [4 marks]

3.  Write down the equation of the regression line $y$ on $x$.
    [2 marks]

4.  Use the line of the regression equation to estimate the price of $1$
    m$^2$ of the land $4$ km from the Melbourne city centre. State
    whether your estimate is reliable and justify your answer.
    [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let's identify the points on the scatter diagram of which the
    coordinates are undefined in the table.

    ![fa32e2df418efff6ee45c1e98504ffad2052050e.svg](images/fa32e2df418efff6ee45c1e98504ffad2052050e.svg)

    By inspection, we see that    $a = 2.4$    and
      $b = 0.9.$

2.
    1.
        $\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mux\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu \approx 3.33 \text{ km}$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    2.
        $\mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5muy\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu \approx 1.43  \text{ thousands AUD}$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    3.
        $r \approx -0.981$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

3.
    $y = -0.392x + 2.74$
    \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

4.  Substituting $x = 4$ into the line of regression equation
    $y = -0.392x + 2.74$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= -0.392(4) + 2.74 \\[5pt]
    &\approx 1.17 \text{ thousands AUD}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, the price of land $4$ km away from the centre Melbourne city centre is approximately \$$1,170$ per m$^2$.

    The estimate is reliable as the magnitude of the correlation coefficient is close to $-1$ (which implies a strong correlation between the distance and the price), and $4$ km lies within the range of data used to calculate the line of regression equation (interpolation).
