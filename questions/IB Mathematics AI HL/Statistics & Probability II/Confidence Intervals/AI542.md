# AI542

**Maximum mark: 6**

A quality control manager at a pharmaceutical company carries out a
weight variation test on Chlorpheniramine tablets, an anti-allergy drug,
produced on a certain day. The manager randomly samples $18$ tablets and
finds the mean weight of the sample is $3.97$ mg
and the standard deviation of the sample is
$0.152$ mg.

1.  Find the unbiased estimate for the standard deviation, $s_{n-1}$.
    [2 marks]

2.  Find a $95\%$ confidence interval of the population mean, correct to
    $2$ decimal places. [3 marks]

These tablets are packaged into bottles labelled as Chlorpheniramine
$4$ mg.

3.  Comment on the accuracy of the labelled weights of the
    tablets with reference to your findings in part (b). [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using $s_{n-1} = \sqrt{\dfrac{n}{n-1}}\,s_n$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    s_{n-1} &= \sqrt{\dfrac{18}{18-1}} \cdot 0.152 \\[10pt]
    &= 0.1564...\\[6pt]
    s_{n-1} &=0.156 \hspace{0.5em}\text{(3 s.f.)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Since the population variance is unknown, we calculate a $t$-interval, not a $z$-interval. Because we do not have a set of data, we must do this using statistics.

    Inputting the values $\overline{x}=3.97$, $s_{n-1}=0.1564...$, $n=18$ and $\text{C Level}=0.95$ into the G.D.C.,

    We find that $\text{C Lower}=3.892...$ and $\text{C Upper}=4.047...$

    Thus the $95\%$ confidence interval of the
    population mean correct to $2$ decimal places is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    3.89 < \mu < 4.05
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.

    Since $\mu_0 = 4$ is within the $95\%$ confidence interval of the population mean $\mu$ $(3.89, 4.05)$ we do not reject the claim that $\mu = 4\,$mg as labelled.
