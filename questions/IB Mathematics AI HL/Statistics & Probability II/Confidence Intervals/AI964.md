# AI964

**Maximum mark: 6**

A large flower export company in the Netherlands packs tulips in
fibreboard boxes for transportation. It is claimed by the owner of the
company that each box contains an average of $78$ tulips.

Yashada, a client in Japan, chooses $25$ boxes at random from a
container shipped to him and records the number of tulips in each box to
check the claim. Given that the number of tulips is represented by $x$,
he found that
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum x &= 1962\text{ and } \sum x^2 = 154\hspace{0.15em}090.
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Find an unbiased estimate for the mean number ($\mu$) of tulips per
    box. [1 mark]

To find an unbiased estimate for the standard deviation of the number of
tulips per box, Yashada uses the formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
\\
s_{n-1} = \sqrt{\dfrac{\sum x^2 - \dfrac{(\sum x)^2}{n}}{n-1}}
\end{aligned}
$$

2.  Determine the unbiased estimate for the standard deviation
    found by Yashada. [2 marks]

3.  Find a $99\%$ confidence interval for $\mu$, assuming the
    conditions for a normally distributed population has been met.
    [2 marks]

4.  Based on the result in part (c), state a valid conclusion
    that Yashada could make. Justify your answer. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the formula $\bar{x} = \dfrac{\sum x}{n}$, an unbiased
    estimate for the mean number of tulips per box is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \bar{x} &= \dfrac{1962}{25} \\[12pt] &\hspace{-0.55em}\bar{x} \approx 78.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the formula for the unbiased estimate for the standard
    deviation, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    s_{n-1} &= \sqrt{\dfrac{ 154090 - \dfrac{1962^2}{25}}{25-1}}  \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-2.05em}s_{n-1} \approx 2.16
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Since the population variance is unknown, we calculate a
    $t$-interval.\
    $\bar{x}=78.5\,;  s_{n-1}=2.16\,;
     n=25$
    $\text{ and the confidence level is }$ $0.99$.\
    $\text{Thus the }$ $99\%$
    $\text{ confidence interval for }$ $\mu$
    $\text{is }$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{-2em}77.3 < \mu < 79.7 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4. :::answer
    Since $\mu_0 = 78$ is within the 99% confidence interval for $\mu$, (77.3, 79.7), Yashada could conclude that there is no reason to believe that the owner's claim is incorrect.
