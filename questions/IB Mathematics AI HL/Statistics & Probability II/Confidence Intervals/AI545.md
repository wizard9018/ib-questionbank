# AI545

**Maximum mark: 6**

The heights of all players in the United States National Basketball
Association (NBA) is known to vary with a standard deviation of
$9.85$ cm.

1.  A player statistician selects $30$ players at random from the NBA
    and researches their recorded height. The mean height of this sample
    is $201.4$ cm. Calculate a $95\%$ confidence
    interval for the mean heights of all the players in the NBA, correct
    to the nearest mm. [3 marks]

2.  Another statistician is about to create a random sample $n$ players
    from the NBA. Find the minimum value of $n$ needed for the width of
    a $95\%$ confidence interval to be less than
    $4$ cm. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since the population standard deviation is known, we use
    $z$-critical values. Given $1-\alpha = 0.95$, we get $\alpha = 0.05$
    and so $\dfrac{\alpha}{2} = 0.025$.
    Hence the margin of error is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{ME} &= Z_{\textstyle \frac{\alpha}{2}} \cdot \dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}} \\[3pt]
    &= Z_{0.025} \cdot \dfrac{9.85}{\sqrt{30}} \\[3pt]
    &= \text{normInv}(0.975) \cdot \dfrac{9.85}{\sqrt{30}} \\[3pt]
    &= 1.96 \cdot \dfrac{9.85}{\sqrt{30}} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\[6pt]
    &\approx 3.52
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     Thus the $95\%$ confidence interval of the
    population mean correct to the nearest mm ($1$ decimal place) is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mux\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu - \text{ME} &< \mu < \mkern 1.5mu\overline{\mkern-1.5mux\mkern-1.5mu}\mkern 1.5mu + \text{ME} \\[3pt]
    201.4 - 3.52 &< \mu < 201.4 + 3.52 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-2.2em}197.9 < \mu < 204.9
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Since the width of the confidence interval is required to be less
    than 4 cm, we can solve for $n$ for a margin
    of error less than 2 cm
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    Z_{0.025} \cdot \dfrac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}} &< 2 \\[3pt]
    1.96 \cdot \dfrac{9.85}{\sqrt{n}} &< 2 \\[3pt]
    \sqrt{n} &> \dfrac{1.96 \cdot 9.85}{2} \\[3pt]
    n &> \left( \dfrac{1.96 \cdot 9.85}{2}\right)^2 \\[3pt]
    n &> 93.18
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $\text{Therefore, the minimum value of \(n\) is \(94\).}$
