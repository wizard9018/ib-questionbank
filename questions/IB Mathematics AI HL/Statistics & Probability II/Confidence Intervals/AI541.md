# AI541

**Maximum mark: 6**

A quality control test was conducted at a brewery to assess the accuracy
of the labelled volume of beer in each bottle. A sample of $25$ beer
bottles were randomly chosen and it was found that the mean volume of
beer in the sample was $331.25$ ml and the
standard deviation of the sample was $3.264$ ml.

1.  Find the unbiased estimate for the standard deviation, $s_{n-1}$.
    [2 marks]

2.  Find a $95\%$ confidence interval for the population mean, correct
    to $2$ decimal places. [3 marks]

Each bottle of beer produced is labelled as having a volume of
$330$ ml.

3.  Comment on this claim with reference to your finding in
    part (b). [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using $s_{n-1} = \sqrt{\dfrac{n}{n-1}}\,s_n$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    s_{n-1} &= \sqrt{\dfrac{25}{25-1}} \cdot 3.264 \\[10pt]
    &= 3.331...\\[6pt]
    s_{n-1} &= 3.33\hspace{0.5em}\text{(3 s.f.)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Since the population variance is unknown, we calculate a $t$-interval, not a $z$-interval. Because we do not have a set of data, we must do this using statistics.

    Inputting the values $\overline{x}=331.25$, $s_{n-1}=3.331...$, $n=25$ and $\text{C Level}=0.95$ into the G.D.C.,

    We find that $\text{C Lower}=329.874...$ and $\text{C Upper}=332.625...$

    Thus the $95\%$ confidence interval of the
    population mean correct to $2$ decimal places is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    329.87 < \mu < 332.63
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.

    Since $\mu_0 = 330$ is within the $95\%$ confidence interval of the population mean $\mu$ $(329.87, 332.63)$ we do not reject the claim that $\mu = 330\,$ml, as labelled on the bottles.
