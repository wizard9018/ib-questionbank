# AA163

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows part of the graph of a quadratic function
$f$.

![1964f1685a23a24125bfe5568ac8db4fc2c5ff72.svg](images/1964f1685a23a24125bfe5568ac8db4fc2c5ff72.svg)

The vertex is at point A and the $x$-intercepts are at points B and C.

The function $f$ can be written in the form $f(x) = (x - h)^2+k$.

1.  Write down the values of $h$ and $k$. [2 marks]

The function $f$ can also be written in the form
$f(x) = (x + p)(x - q)$, where $p,q \in \mathbb{Z^+}$.

2.  Write down the values of $p$ and $q$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the $y$-intercept of the graph of $f$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $h = 2$  and
     $k = -9$

2.
    $p = 1$  and
     $q = 5$

3.  Evaluating $f(x) = (x+1)(x-5)$ for $x = 0$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(0) &= (0+1)(0-5) \\[6pt]
    &= (1)(-5) \\[6pt]
    &= -5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the point
     $\mathrm{D}(0,-5)$
    is the $y$-intercept of the graph of $f$.
