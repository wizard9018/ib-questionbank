# AA162

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = x^2 - 2x - 8$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  Write down the $y$-intercept of the graph of $y = f(x)$. [1 mark]

2.  Solve the equation $f(x) = 0$. [3 marks]

3.  Find the equation of the axis of symmetry of the graph of
    $y = f(x)$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $y = f(x)$ for $x = 0$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6em} y &= f(0) \\[6pt]
    &= 0^2 - 2(0) - 8 \\[6pt]
    &= -8
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the point
     $\mathrm{P}(0,-8)$
    is the $y$-intercept of the graph of $y = f(x)$.

2.  Solving the equation $f(x) = 0$ for $x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x^2 - 2x - 8 &= 0 \hspace{4.8em} \\[6pt]
    (x + 2)(x - 4) &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{2.8em} x = -2,\hspace{0.25em} 4
    $$

3.  Using the method of completing the square, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.7em} f(x) &= x^2 - 2x - 8 \\[6pt]
    &= \big[(x - 1)^2 - 1\big] - 8 \\[6pt]
    &= (x - 1)^2 - 9
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the axis of symmetry of the
    graph of $y = f(x)$ is
     $x = 1$
