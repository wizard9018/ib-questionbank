# AA164

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $f(x) = (x - 3)(x + 1)$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

For the graph of $f$, find:

1.  the $y$-intercept; [2 marks]

2.  the $x$-intercepts; [2 marks]

3.  the coordinates of the vertex. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $f(x) = (x - 3)(x + 1)$ for $x = 0$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4em} f(0) &= (0 - 3)(0 + 1) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    &= (-3)(1) \\[6pt]
    &= -3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the $y$-intercept is
     $\mathrm{A}(0,-3)$

    $$\\[-2.5em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Solving the equation $f(x) = 0$ for $x$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (x - 3)(x + 1) &= 0 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    x &= -1,\hspace{0.25em} 3 \hspace{1.5em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the
    $x$-intercepts are
     $\mathrm{B}(-1,0)$
    and
     $\mathrm{C}(3,0)$

    $$\\[-2.5em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the method of completing the square,

    $$\\[-2.5em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.8em} f(x) &= (x - 3)(x + 1) \\[6pt]
    &= x^2 - 2x - 3 \\[6pt]
    &= \big[(x-1)^2 - 1\big] - 3 \\[6pt]
    &= (x-1)^2 - 4 \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the coordinates of the vertex
    are  $(1,-4)$

    $$\\[-2.5em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
