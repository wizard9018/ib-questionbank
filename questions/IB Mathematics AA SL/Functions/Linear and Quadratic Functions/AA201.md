# AA201

**Maximum mark: 15**

Let $f(x) = 2(x-1)^2-8$, for $x\in\mathbb{R}$.

1.  Show that $f(x) = 2x^2-4x-6$. [2 marks]

2.  For the graph of $f$:
    1.  write down the coordinates of the vertex;

    2.  write down the $y$-intercept;
    3.  find both $x$-intercepts. [7 marks]

3.  Hence sketch the graph of $f$. [3 marks]

Let $g(x) = 6x^2$, for $x\in\mathbb{R}$.

The graph of $f$ may be obtained from the graph of $g$ using the
following three transformations:

 $$
 \begin{aligned}
 \hspace{1em} & \bullet\text{ a compression of scale factor $a$ in the $y$-direction, followed by} \\
 & \bullet\text{ a horizontal translation of $h$ units, followed by} \\
 & \bullet\text{ a vertical translation of $k$ units.}
 \end{aligned}
 $$

4.  Find the values of $a, h$ and $k$. [3 marks]

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.9em} f(x) &= 2(x-1)^2-8 \\[6pt]
    &= 2(x^2-2x+1)-8 \\[6pt]
    &= 2x^2-4x-6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.
        $(1,-8)$

    2.
        $\text{P}(0,-6)$

    3.  Solving the equation $f(x) = 0$ for $x$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        2x^2-4x-6 &= 0 \\[6pt]
        x^2-2x-3 &= 0 \\[6pt]
        \hspace{0.8em} (x+1)(x-3) &= 0 \\[6pt]
        x &= -1,\hspace{0.25em}3
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the $x$-intercepts
        are
         $\text{A}(-1,0)$
        and
         $\text{B}(3,0)$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(x) &= ag(x-h)+k \\[6pt]
    2(x-1)^2 - 8 &= a\big[6(x-h)^2\big]+k \\[6pt]
