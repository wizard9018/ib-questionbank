# AA744

**Maximum mark: 5**

The ocean pressure, $P$, under sea level can be modelled by the function

$$
P(D) = \dfrac{D}{10}+1
$$
where $D$ is the distance in metres below sea
level and $P$ is measured in atmospheres.\
A submarine cruising near the surface is submerged according to the
function
$$
D(t) = 10+5t

$$
where $t$ is measured in minutes and $D$ is
the distance the submarine is below sea level, measured in
metres.

1.  Find the composite function $P\circ D$ and explain what it means in
    the\
    context of this question[3 marks]

2.  Find and interpret $(P\circ D)(10)$.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The composite function can be expressed as
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (P\circ D) (t) &= P(D(t)) \rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{4mm}\\
    &= P(10+5t)\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &= \dfrac{10+5t}{10}+1 \rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &\hspace{0.28em}= 2+\dfrac{t}{2}\rule[-5.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}\hspace{6em}{(1)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{This composite function represents the ocean pressure (in atmospheres) outside the submarine at time $t$ minutes.}
    $$

2.  Substituting $t=10$ in (1), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (P\circ D)(10)&=2+\dfrac{10}{2}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{4mm}\\
    &\hspace{0.28em}=7\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{This result means that after 10 minutes, the ocean pressure outside the submarine is 7 atmospheres.}
    $$
