# AA177

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider $f(x) = x^2 + bx + c$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$, where
$b,c \in \mathbb{Z}$. The graph of $f$ has a local minimum when $x = 2.5$.
The distance between the two $x$-intercepts of the graph of $f$ is $7$.

1.  Find the coordinates of the two $x$-intercepts. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $b$ and the value of $c$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The axis of symmetry of the graph of $f$ is $x = 2.5$.
    Hence the coordinates of the two $x$-intercepts are

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \bigg(2.5-\frac{7}{2},0\bigg) = \hspace{0.525em}(-1,0)\hspace{0.525em} \hspace{1em}\text{and}\hspace{1em}
    \bigg(2.5+\frac{7}{2},0\bigg) = \hspace{0.525em}(6,0)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The roots of the quadratic function $f(x) = x^2 + bx + c$ are
    $x = -1$ and $x = 6$.

    Hence, using the factor theorem, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.4em} f(x) &= (x + 1)(x - 6) \\[6pt]
    &= x^2 - 5x - 6
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find
     $b = -5$
    and  $c = -6$
