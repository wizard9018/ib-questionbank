# AA134

**Maximum mark: 5**

1.  Express $2x^2 - 8x + 9$ in the form $a(x+b)^2 + c$ where
    $a,b,c \in\mathbb{Z}$. [2 marks]

2.  Given that $f(x) = x - 2$ and $(g\circ f)(x) = 2x^2 - 8x + 9$, find
    $g(x)$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the method of completing the square, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2x^2 - 8x + 9 &= 2\big[x^2 - 4x\big] + 9 \\[6pt]
    &= 2\big[(x-2)^2 - 4\big] + 9 \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
    &= 2(x-2)^2 - 8 + 9 \\[6pt]
    &= 2(x-2)^2 + 1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8.3em} (g\circ f)(x) &= 2x^2 - 8x + 9 \\[6pt]
    g(f(x)) &=  2(x-2)^2 + 1 \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[6pt]
    g(x-2) &= 2(x-2)^2 + 1 \hspace{3em} [\text{since $f(x) = x - 2$}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    g(x) = 2x^2 + 1 \hspace{3.4em}
    $$
