# AA179

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $f(x) = ax^2 - 24x + c$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$, where
$a, c \in \mathbb{Z}$.

1.  A horizontal line, $L$, intersects the graph of $y = f(x)$ at
    $x = 1$ and $x = 7$.

    1.  Find the equation of the axis of symmetry of the graph of
        $y = f(x)$.

    2.  Hence show that $a = 3$. [4 marks]

2.  The equation of $L$ is $y = 6$. Find the value of $c$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  The equation of the axis of symmetry is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        x &= \dfrac{1 + 7}{2} \hspace{0.2em} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        x= 4 \hspace{2.2em}
        $$

    2.  Using the axis of symmetry formula, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        x &= -\dfrac{b}{2a} \\[6pt]
        4 &= -\dfrac{(-24)}{2a} \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[12pt]
        \hspace{8.8em} 8a &= 24 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        a = 3 \hspace{2.1em}
        $$

2.  Since the line $y = 6$ intersects the graph of $y = f(x)$ at
    $x = 1$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(1) &= 6 \hspace{6.7em} \\[6pt]
    3(1)^2 - 24(1) + c &= 6 \\[7pt]
    3 - 24 + c &= 6 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.8em} c = 27
    $$
