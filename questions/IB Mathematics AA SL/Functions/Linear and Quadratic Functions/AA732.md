# AA732

**Maximum mark: 6**

The graph shows the curve of a quadratic function of the form
$f(x) = ax^2 + bx + 90$.

![e02d6931ce58420fa7c9cbe422473aefce55e151.svg](images/e02d6931ce58420fa7c9cbe422473aefce55e151.svg)

1.  Write down the equation for the axis of symmetry of the curve.
    [2 marks]

2.  Hence, or otherwise, find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$.
    [3 marks]

3.  Find the $y$-coordinate of the vertex of the curve. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The axis of symmetry intersects the $x$-axis midway between the
    $x$-intercepts.

    Hence we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.15em} x &= \dfrac{-5+9}{2} \\
    \tag*{\textbf{{(M1)}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.4em} x = 2
    \tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}
    $$

2.  Substitute the given coordinates into $f(x)=ax^2+bx+90.$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textcolor{white}{placeholder}\tag*{{\textbf{M1}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the coordinates of A, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(-5) &= 0 \\[6pt]
    a(-5)^2 + b(-5) + 90 &= 0 \\[6pt]
    25a - 5b &= -90 \hspace{1em} \hspace{6em}(1)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the
    coordinates of B, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(9) &= 0 \\[6pt]
    \hspace{0.6em} a(9)^2 + b(9) + 90 &= 0 \\[6pt]
    81a + 9b &= -90 \hspace{7.5em}(2)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, solving the system
    of linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $a$ and $b$ [by using G.D.C.].

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textcolor{white}{placeholder}\tag*{{\textbf{(M1)}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10.4em} a = -2\hspace{0.75em}\text{ and }\hspace{0.75em}b = 8\hspace{3em}
    \tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The vertex of the curve lies on the axis of symmetry.\
    Hence, evaluating $f(x)$ for $x = 2$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.8em} f(2) &= -2(2)^2 + 8(2) + 90 \\[6pt]
    &= 98
    \end{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}
    $$
