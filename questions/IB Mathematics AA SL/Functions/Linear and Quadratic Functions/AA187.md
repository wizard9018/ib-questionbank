# AA187

**Maximum mark: 15**

Let $f(x) = 2x^2 + 4x + p$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$, where
$p \in \mathbb{Z}$.

1.  The equation $f(x) = 0$ has two equal roots.
    1.  Write down the value of the discriminant of $f$.

    2.  Show that $p = 2$. [3 marks]

2.  For the graph of $f$, find:
    1.  the equation of the axis of symmetry.

    2.  the coordinates of the vertex; [4 marks]

3.  Write down the solution to the equation $f(x) = 0$. [1 mark]

4.  The function $f$ can be written in the form $f(x) = a(x-h)^2 + k$.
    Find\
    the values of $a$, $h$ and $k$. [5 marks]

5.  The graph of a function $g$ is obtained from the graph of $f$ by a
    reflection\
    in the $x$-axis. Find the coordinates of the vertex of the graph of
    $g$. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $\triangle  = 0$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2em} \triangle  &= b^2 - 4ac \\[4pt]
        0 &= 4^2 - 4(2)(p) \hspace{0.5em} \\[5pt]
        0 &= 16 - 8p \\[5pt]
        16 &= 8p \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        p = 2 \hspace{2.9em}
        $$

2.
    1.  Using the axis of symmetry formula, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        x &= -\dfrac{b}{2a} \\[6pt]
        x &= -\dfrac{4}{2(2)} \hspace{1.4em} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        x = -1 \hspace{2.9em}
        $$

    2.  Evaluating $f(x)=2x^2 + 4x + 2$ for $x = -1$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2.2em} f(-1) &= 2(-1)^2 + 4(-1) + 2 \\[8pt]
        &= 0
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the coordinates of the vertex are
         $(-1,0)$

3.
    $x=-1$

4.  Using the method of completing the square, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} f(x) &= 2x^2 + 4x + 2 \\[6pt]
    &= 2\big[x^2 + 2x\big] + 2 \\[6pt]
    &= 2\big[(x+1)^2 - 1\big] + 2 \\[6pt]
    &= 2(x+1)^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find
     $a = 2,\hspace{0.25em} h = -1$
    and  $k = 0$

5.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.2em}  g(x) &= -f(x) \\[6pt]
    &= -2(x+1)^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the coordinates of the vertex of
    the graph of $g$ are
     $(-1,0)$
