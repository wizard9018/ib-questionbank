# AA180

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows the graph of $y = f(x)$, for
$-1 \leq x \leq 2$.

![ccc9d5942ccfefc2e6313a34922fed1ada13594f.svg](images/ccc9d5942ccfefc2e6313a34922fed1ada13594f.svg)

1.  Write down the value of:
    1.  $f(1)$;

    2.  $f^{-1}(-2)$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $(f\circ f)(1)$. [2 marks]

3.  Sketch the graph of $y = f(-x)$ on the same grid above. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $f(1) = 2$

    2.
        $f^{-1}(-2) = -1$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (f\circ f)(1) &= f(f(1)) \\[6pt]
    &= f(2) \\[6pt]
    &= 6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The graph of $y = f(-x)$ is a reflection of the graph of $y = f(x)$
    in the $y$-axis.
