# AA734

**Maximum mark: 6**

Atmospheric pressure, $P$, in kPa, decreases exponentially with
increasing height above sea level, $h$. The atmospheric pressure can be
modelled by the function
$$
\begin{aligned}
P(h) &= 101\times\bigg(\dfrac{25}{22}\bigg)^{-h},
\end{aligned}
$$
where
$h$ is the height above sea level in kilometres.

1.  Write down the exact atmospheric pressure at sea level, in kPa.
    [1 mark]

Mount Kosciuszko is the highest mountain in Australia with a height of
$2228$ $\text{metres}$ above sea level at the top.

2.  Calculate the atmospheric pressure at the top of the Mount
    Kosciuszko. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the height where the atmospheric pressure is
    equal to $10$ kPa. [3 marks]
