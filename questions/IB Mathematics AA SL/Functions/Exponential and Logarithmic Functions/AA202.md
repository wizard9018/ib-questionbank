# AA202

**Maximum mark: 8**

A population of endangered snow leopards, $P$, can be modelled by the
equation
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_t = P_0e^{kt},
\end{aligned}
$$
where $P_0$ is the initial population,
and $t$ is measured in years.

1.  After one year, it is estimated that $\dfrac{P_1}{P_0} = 0.93$.
    1.  Find the value of $k$.

    2.  Interpret the meaning of the value of $k$. [3 marks]

2.  Find the least number of **whole** years for which
    $\dfrac{P_t}{P_0} < 0.50$. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Substituting $t = 1$ in the equation $P_t = P_0e^{kt}$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        P_1 &= P_0e^{k(1)} \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
        \dfrac{P_1}{P_0} &= e^k \\[6pt]
        \hspace{0.1em} 0.93 &= e^k \\[11pt]
        k &= \ln 0.93 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \hspace{0.8em} k \approx -0.0726
        $$

    2.
        $\text{The rate at which the population of leopards exponentially decrease}$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{P_t}{P_0} &< 0.50 \\[8pt]
    e^{-0.0726t} &< 0.50 \\[12pt]
    -0.0726t &< \ln 0.50 \\[6pt]
    t &> -\dfrac{\ln 0.50}{0.0726} \\[8pt]
    t &> 9.55 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the least number of whole years
    for which $\dfrac{P_t}{P_0} < 0.50$ is
     $t = 10$
