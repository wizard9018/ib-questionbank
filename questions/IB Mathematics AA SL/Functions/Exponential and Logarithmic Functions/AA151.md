# AA151

**Maximum mark: 8**

1.  Write down the domain and range of the logarithmic function
    $y = \log_a x$\
    where $a > 0$ and $a \neq 1$. [2 marks]

2.  Given that $\log_{x^2} y = 9 \log_y (x^2)$, find all the possible
    expressions\
    of $y$ as a function of $x$. [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{domain}:\hspace{0.75em}&\hspace{0.525em}(0,\infty) \hspace{1.8em} \\[8pt]
    \text{range}:\hspace{0.75em}&\hspace{0.525em}\mathbb{R}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the properties of logarithms, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \log_{x^2} y &= 9 \log_y (x^2) \\[11pt]
    \hspace{4em} \dfrac{\ln y}{\ln (x^2)} &= \dfrac{9\ln (x^2)}{\ln y} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{\ln y}{2\ln x} &= \dfrac{18\ln x}{\ln y} \\[12pt]
    (\ln y)^2 &= (6\ln x)^2 \\[16pt]
    \ln y &= \pm 6\ln x \\[16pt]
    \ln y &= \ln (1/x^6)\hspace{0.25em}\text{ or }\hspace{0.25em}\ln (x^6) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{2.2em} y = \dfrac{1}{x^6}\hspace{0.25em}\text{ or }\hspace{0.25em}x^6
    $$
