# AA178

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $f(x) = e^x - 2$ and $g(x) = 3x + k$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$, where
$k$ is a constant.

1.  Find $(g\circ f)(x)$. [2 marks]

2.  Given that
    $\displaystyle \lim_{x \rightarrow -\infty} (g\circ f)(x) = -4$,
    find the value of $k$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.8em} (g\circ f)(x) &= g(f(x)) \\[6pt]
    &= g(e^x - 2) \\[6pt]
    &= 3(e^x - 2) + k \\[6pt]
    &= 3e^x - 6 + k
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \lim_{x \rightarrow -\infty} (g\circ f)(x) &= -4 \hspace{6.9em} \\[4pt]
    \lim_{x \rightarrow -\infty}\hspace{-0.2em}\big[3e^x - 6 + k\big] &= -4 \\[4pt]
    3(0) - 6 + k &= -4 \\[9pt]
    - 6 + k &= -4 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.2em} k = 2
    $$
