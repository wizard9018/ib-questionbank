# AA197

**Maximum mark: 5**

Two functions, $f$ and $g$, are defined in the following table.

![f16cb92d520a6fc6d08cc849fc6b256348e1335f.svg](images/f16cb92d520a6fc6d08cc849fc6b256348e1335f.svg)

1.  Write down the value of $f(2)$. [1 mark]

2.  Find the value of $(g\circ f)(2)$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the value of $g^{-1}(5)$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $f(2) = 0$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (g\circ f)(2) &= g(f(2)) \\[6pt]
    &= g(0) \\[6pt]
    &= -1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (g\circ g^{-1})(5) &= 5 \hspace{3.7em} \\[6pt]
    g(g^{-1}(5)) &= 5 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{9.7em} g^{-1}(5) = -4 \hspace{3em} [\text{since $g(-4) = 5$}]
    $$
