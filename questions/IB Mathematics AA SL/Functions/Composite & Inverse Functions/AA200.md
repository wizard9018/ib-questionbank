# AA200

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $f(x) = 2x$, $g(x) = 4x + 6$ and $h(x) = (f\circ g)(x)$, for
$x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  Find $h(x)$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $h^{-1}(x)$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.3em} h(x) &= (f\circ g)(x) \\[6pt]
    &= f(g(x)) \\[6pt]
    &= f(4x+6) \\[6pt]
    &= 2(4x+6) \\[6pt]
    &= 8x+12
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  If we solve the equation $(h\circ h^{-1})(x) = x$ for
    $y = h^{-1}(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h(h^{-1}(x)) &= x \\[12pt]
    h(y) &= x \\[12pt]
    8y+12 &= x \\[12pt]
    8y &= x-12 \\[6pt]
    y &= \dfrac{x-12}{8} \hspace{1.3em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    h^{-1}(x) = \dfrac{x-12}{8}
    $$
