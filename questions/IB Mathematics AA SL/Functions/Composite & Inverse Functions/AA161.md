# AA161

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = x^2 + 2$ and $g(x) = x - 3$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  Find $f(3)$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $(g\circ f)(3)$. [2 marks]

3.  Find $g^{-1}(x)$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $f(x) = x^2 + 2$ for $x = 3$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.6em} f(3) &= 3^2 + 2 \\[6pt]
    &= 9 + 2 \\[6pt]
    &= 11
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Evaluating $(g\circ f)(x) = g(f(x))$ for $x = 3$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.4em} (g\circ f)(3) &= g(f(3)) \\[6pt]
    &= g(11) \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[6pt]
    &= 11 - 3 \\[6pt]
    &= 8
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  If we solve the equation $(g\circ g^{-1})(x) = x$ for
    $y = g^{-1}(x)$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    g(g^{-1}(x)) &= x \\[6pt]
    g(y) &= x \\[6pt]
    y - 3 &= x \\[6pt]
    y &= x + 3 \hspace{2.1em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    g^{-1}(x) = x+3 \hspace{0.8em}
    $$
