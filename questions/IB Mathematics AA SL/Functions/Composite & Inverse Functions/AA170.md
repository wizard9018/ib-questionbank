# AA170

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $f(x) = 4x - 3$ and $g(x) = 2x$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  Write down the value of $g(5)$. [1 mark]

2.  Find $(f\circ g)(x)$. [2 marks]

3.  Find $(f\circ g)^{-1}(x)$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $g(5) = 10$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (f\circ g)(x) &= f(g(x)) \\[6pt]
    &= f(2x) \\[6pt]
    &= 4(2x) - 3 \\[6pt]
    &= 8x - 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Let $h(x) = (f\circ g)(x)$.
    Hence $h^{-1}(x) = (f\circ g)^{-1}(x)$.

    If we solve the equation $(h\circ h^{-1})(x) = x$ for
    $y = h^{-1}(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h(h^{-1}(x)) &= x \\[12pt]
    h(y) &= x \\[12pt]
    8y - 3 &= x \\[12pt]
    8y &= x + 3 \\[6pt]
    y &= \dfrac{x + 3}{8} \hspace{1.8em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    (f\circ g)^{-1}(x) = \dfrac{x + 3}{8} \hspace{2.7em}
    $$
