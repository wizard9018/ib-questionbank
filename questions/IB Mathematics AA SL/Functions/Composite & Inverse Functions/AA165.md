# AA165

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = x^3$ and $g(x) = 2x - 1$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  Find $g^{-1}(x)$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $(g\circ f)(x)$. [2 marks]

3.  Solve the equation $(g\circ f)(x) = 0$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we solve the equation $(g\circ g^{-1})(x) = x$ for
    $y = g^{-1}(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    g(g^{-1}(x)) &= x \\[12pt]
    g(y) &= x \\[12pt]
    2y - 1 &= x \\[12pt]
    2y &= x + 1 \\[6pt]
    y &= \dfrac{x + 1}{2} \hspace{1.9em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    g^{-1}(x) = \dfrac{x+1}{2} \hspace{0.6em}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (g\circ f)(x) &= g(f(x)) \\[6pt]
    &= g(x^3) \\[6pt]
    &= 2x^3-1 \hspace{1.1em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $(g\circ f)(x) = 0$ for $x$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.5em} 2x^3-1 &= 0 \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (b)}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{12.2em} x \approx 0.794 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
