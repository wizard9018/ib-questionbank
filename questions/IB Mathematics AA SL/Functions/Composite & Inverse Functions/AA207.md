# AA207

**Maximum mark: 5**

The functions $f$ and $g$ are defined such that $f(x) = \dfrac{x-2}{3}$
and $g(x) = 12x+4$.

1.  Show that $(g\circ f)(x) = 4x-4$. [2 marks]

2.  Given that $(g\circ f)^{-1}(a) = 10$, find the value of $a$.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.8em} (g\circ f)(x) &= g\hspace{0.05em}(f(x)) \\[9pt]
    &= g\Big(\dfrac{x-2}{3}\Big) \\[5pt]
    &= 12\Big(\dfrac{x-2}{3}\Big)+4 \\[9pt]
    &= 4\hspace{0.05em}(x-2) + 4 \\[13pt]
    &= 4x-4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.25em} (g\circ f)^{-1}(a) &= 10 \\[6pt]
    (g\circ f)(10) &= a \\[6pt]
    4(10)-4 &= a \hspace{3em} [\text{by part $(a)$}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    a = 36
    $$
