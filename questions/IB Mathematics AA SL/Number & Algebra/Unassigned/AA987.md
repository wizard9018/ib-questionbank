# AA987

**Maximum mark: 7**

A species of bird, thought to be extinct, was rediscovered on 14 March 1973. Through a careful protection and breeding program, the population has continued to increase since then.

The Department of conservation proposes that the population of birds $t$ years after 14 March 1973 can be modelled by the function $P(t)=10e^{kt}$, where $0 < k < 1$ and $t \geq 0$.

On 14 March 1993 the known population of birds was 50.

1. Use the model to estimate the population of birds on 14 March 2023.[4 marks]

In fact, the known population of birds on 14 March 2023 was 502. A new model is proposed in which

* The population on March 14 1973 was 10.

* The population on March 14 2023 was 502.

* The population increased at a constant $r\%$ per year.

2. Find the value of $r$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 14 March 1993 is 20 years after 14 March 1973, therefore $P(20)=50$. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    P(20) &= 10e^{20k} \\[8pt]

    50 &= 10e^{20k} \\[8pt]

    e^{20k} &= 5

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Using the numerical solver on the G.D.C. we find that $k\approx 0.08047$. Hence our model is $P(t)=10e^{0.08047t}$.

    14 March 2023 is 50 years after 14 March 1973, hence we calculate $P(50)$:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    P(50) &= 10e^{0.08047 \times 50} \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-2.4em}\boxed{P(50)\approx559}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. If something increases at a constant rate, the compound interest formula applies. Using the formula for compound interest with $PV=10$, $FV=502$, $k=1$ and $n=50$, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    FV &= PV \times \left(1 + \dfrac{r}{100k}\right)^{kn} \\[8pt]

    502 &= 10 \times \left(1 + \dfrac{r}{100}\right)^{50}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Using the numerical solver on the G.D.C. we find that $\boxed{r\approx 8.15}$
