# AA631

**Maximum mark: 6**

Landmarks are placed along the road from London to Edinburgh and the
distance between each landmark is $16.1$ km. The
first landmark placed on the road is $124.7$ km
from London, and the last landmark is near Edinburgh. The length of the
road from London to Edinburgh is $667.1$ km.

1.  Find the distance between the fifth landmark and London. [3 marks]

2.  Determine how many landmarks there are along the road. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have an arithmetic sequence with $u_1 = 124.7$ and $d = 16.1$.
    Hence, using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with
    $n = 5$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.6em} u_5 &= u_1 + (5-1)d \\[6pt]
    &= 124.7 + (5-1)(16.1) \\[6pt]
    &= 189
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the distance between the fifth
    landmark and London is
     $189\hspace{0.25em}\text{km}$

2.  If we solve the inequality $u_n < 667.1$ for the largest value of
    $n$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_1 + (n-1)d &< 667.1 \\[6pt]
    124.7 + (n-1)(16.1) &< 667.1 \\[6pt]
    n &< 34.7 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
    n &= 34 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence there are
     $34$
    landmarks along the road.
