# AA023

**Maximum mark: 8**

The first three terms of a geometric sequence are $\ln x^9$, $\ln x^3$,
$\ln x$, for $x > 0$.

1.  Find the common ratio. [3 marks]

2.  Hence, solve $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^\infty 3^{3-k}\ln x = 27$.
    [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have $u_1 = 9\ln x$ and $u_2 = 3\ln x$.
    Hence the common ratio is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{u_2}{u_1} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{3\ln x}{9\ln x} \hspace{2.9em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The $n$th term of the geometric sequence is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_n &= u_1r^{n-1} \\[6pt]
    &= 9\ln x\bigg(\dfrac{1}{3}\bigg)^{n-1} \\[8pt]
    &= 3^{3-n}\ln x
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_{\infty} &= \sum_{k=1}^\infty 3^{3-k}\ln x \hspace{1em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, solving the equation $S_{\infty} = 27$ for $x$, we get

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \dfrac{u_1}{1-r} &= 27 \hspace{2.8em} \\[6pt]
     \dfrac{9\ln x}{1 - 1/3} &= 27 \\[6pt]
     \dfrac{27\ln x}{3-1} &= 27 \\[12pt]
     \ln x &= 2 \\
     \end{aligned}
     $$

    $$
    x = e^2
    $$
