# AA010

**Maximum mark: 7**

The first three terms of a geometric sequence are $u_1 = 0.8$,
$u_2 = 2.4$, $u_3 = 7.2$.

1.  Find the value of the common ratio, $r$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $S_8$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the least value of $n$ such that $S_n > 35\hspace{0.15em}000$.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The value of $r$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{u_2}{u_1} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{2.4}{0.8} \\[13pt]
    &= 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{u_1(r^n-1)}{r-1}$
    with $n=8$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.8em} S_8 &= \dfrac{u_1(r^8 - 1)}{r-1} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.8(3^8 - 1)}{3-1} \\[12pt]
    &= 2624
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_n &> 35\hspace{0.15em}000 \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{u_1(r^n-1)}{r-1} &> 35\hspace{0.15em}000 \\[4pt]
    \hspace{8.1em} \dfrac{0.8(3^n-1)}{3-1} &> 35\hspace{0.15em}000 \\[8pt]
    0.4(3^n-1) &> 35\hspace{0.15em}000 \\[13pt]
    n &> 10.358 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the least value of $n$ such that $S_n > 35\hspace{0.15em}000$
    is  $n=11$
