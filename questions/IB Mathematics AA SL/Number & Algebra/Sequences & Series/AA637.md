# AA637

**Maximum mark: 5**

A bouncy ball is dropped from a height of $2$ metres onto a concrete floor. After hitting the floor, the ball rebounds back up to $80$\% of it's previous height, and this pattern continues on repeatedly, until coming to rest.

1.  Show that the total distance travelled by the ball until coming to rest can be expressed by

    $$
    2 + 4(0.8) + 4(0.8)^2 + 4(0.8)^3 + \cdots
    $$

    $$\\[-2.5em]$$
    [2 marks]

2.  Find the total distance travelled by the ball.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have

    | |$n$|$\hspace{1.5em}0\hspace{1.5em}$|$\hspace{1.5em}1\hspace{1.5em}$|$\hspace{1.5em}2\hspace{1.5em}$|$\hspace{1.5em}3\hspace{1.5em}$|
    |:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
    |distance travelled in metres|$n$th fall|2|$2(0.8)$|$2(0.8)^2$|$2(0.8)^3$|
    | |$n$th rebound|0|$2(0.8)$|$2(0.8)^2$|$2(0.8)^3$|

    $$\\[-1.5em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{M1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Combining the fall distances (top row) with the non-zero rebound distances (bottom row) gives the total distance travelled by the ball.
    $$\\[-2.5em]$$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        &\boxed{2 + 4(0.8) + 4(0.8)^2 + 4(0.8)^3 + \cdots}\hspace{1em}\text{... as required.} \tag*{\textbf{AG}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Applying the **sum of infinite geometric series (1.3)**, with $u_1 = 4$ and $r = 0.8$, to find the total distance travelled by the ball.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        S &= 2 + 4(0.8) + 4(0.8)^2 + 4(0.8)^3 + \cdots
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Adding and subtracting $2$ regroups the leading term as $4$, allowing the series to be rewritten as a single geometric series starting from $n=0$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        S &= \textcolor{blue}{(-2+2)} + 2 + 4(0.8) + 4(0.8)^2 + 4(0.8)^3 + \cdots \\[12pt]
        &= -2 + \left[4 + 4(0.8) + 4(0.8)^2 + 4(0.8)^3 + \cdots\right] \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[12pt]
        &= -2 + \left[\dfrac{4}{1 - 0.8}\right] \tag*{\textbf{A1}} \\[12pt]
        S &= \boxed{18 \text{ metres}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
