# AA624

**Maximum mark: 6**

Only one of the following four sequences is arithmetic and only one of
them is geometric.
$$
\begin{array}{rcccccl}
a_n &=& \dfrac{1}{3},\,\dfrac{1}{4},\,\dfrac{1}{5},\,\dfrac{1}{6},\,\dots &\,\hspace{4em}\,& c_n &=& 3,\,1,\,\dfrac{1}{3},\,\dfrac{1}{9},\,\dots \\[12pt]
b_n &=& 2.5,\,5,\,7.5,\,10,\,\dots &\,\hspace{4em}\,& d_n &=& 1,\,3,\,6,\,10,\,\dots
\end{array}
$$

1.  State which sequence is arithmetic and find the common difference of
    the sequence. [2 marks]

2.  State which sequence is geometric and find the common ratio of the
    sequence.[2 marks]

3.  For the **geometric** sequence find the **exact** value of the sixth
    term. Give your answer as a fraction. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$

    \begin{array}{rclc|crcl}
    a_2-a_1 &\neq& a_3-a_2 &\,\hspace{0.5em}\,&\,\hspace{0.5em}\,& c_2-c_1 &\neq& c_3-c_2 \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{3} &\neq& \dfrac{1}{5}-\dfrac{1}{4} &&& 1-3 &\neq& \dfrac{1}{3}-1 \\[12pt]
    -\dfrac{1}{12} &\neq& -\dfrac{1}{20} &&& -2 &\neq& -\dfrac{2}{3} \\[12pt] \hline
    &&&&&&& \\
    b_2-b_1 &=& b_3-b_2 &&& d_2-d_1 &\neq& d_3-d_2 \\[12pt]
    5-2.5 &=& 7.5-5 &&& 3-1 &\neq& 6-3 \\[12pt]
    2.5 &=& 2.5 &&& 2 &\neq& 3
    \end{array}
    $$
    Hence
     $b_n$
    is arithmetic and
     $d = 2.5$

2.  We have
    $$

    \begin{array}{rclc|crcl}
    \dfrac{a_2}{a_1} &\neq& \dfrac{a_3}{a_2} &\,\hspace{0.5em}\,&\,\hspace{0.5em}\,& \dfrac{c_2}{c_1} &=& \dfrac{c_3}{c_2} \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{1/4}{1/3} &\neq& \dfrac{1/5}{1/4} &&& \dfrac{1}{3} &=& \dfrac{1/3}{1} \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{3}{4} &\neq& \dfrac{4}{5} &&& \dfrac{1}{3} &=&\dfrac{1}{3} \\[12pt] \hline
    &&&&&&& \\
    \dfrac{b_2}{b_1} &\neq& \dfrac{b_3}{b_2} &&& \dfrac{d_2}{d_1} &\neq& \dfrac{d_3}{d_2} \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{5}{2.5} &\neq& \dfrac{7.5}{5} &&& \dfrac{3}{1} &\neq& \dfrac{6}{3} \\[12pt]
    2 &\neq& 1.5 &&& 3 &\neq& 2
    \end{array}
    $$
    Hence
     $c_n$
    is geometric and
     $r = \dfrac{1}{3}$

3.  Using the $n$th term formula $c_n = c_1r^{n-1}$ with $n = 6$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    c_6 &= c_1r^{6-1} \\[8pt]
    &= 3\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{6-1} \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{81}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
