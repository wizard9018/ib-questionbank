# AA024

**Maximum mark: 6**

The seventh term of an arithmetic sequence is equal to $1$ and the sum
of the first $16$ terms is $52$.

Find the common difference and the first term.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_7 + (n-7)d$ with $n = 1$ and
$n = 16$, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
u_1 &= u_7 + (1-7)d \\[6pt]
&= 1-6d
\end{aligned}
$$
and

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_{16} &= u_7 + (16-7)d \\[6pt]
&= 1+9d
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the sum of $n$ terms formula
$S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(u_1 + u_n)$ with $n=16$, we get

 $$
 \begin{aligned}
 \hspace{4em}  S_{16} &= \dfrac{16}{2}(u_1 + u_{16}) \\[6pt]
 52 &= \dfrac{16}{2}((1-6d) + (1+9d)) \\[6pt]
 52 &= \dfrac{16}{2}(2 + 3d) \\[10pt]
 52 &= 16 + 24d \\[14pt]
 36 &= 24d \\
 \end{aligned}
 $$

$$
d = 1.5 \hspace{4em}
$$
Hence
we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
u_1 &= u_7-6d \\[6pt]
&= 1 - 6(1.5) \hspace{1.5em}  \\[6pt]
&= -8
\end{aligned}
$$
