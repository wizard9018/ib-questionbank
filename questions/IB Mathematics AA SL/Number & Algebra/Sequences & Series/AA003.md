# AA003

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the infinite geometric sequence $4480$, $-3360$, $2520$,
$-1890,\dots$

1.  Find the common ratio, $r$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the $20$th term. [2 marks]

3.  Find the **exact** sum of the infinite sequence. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have $u_1 = 4480$ and $u_2 = -3360$.
    Hence the common ratio is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{u_2}{u_1} \\[6pt]
    &= -\dfrac{3360}{4480} \\[12pt]
    &= -0.75
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 r^{n-1}$ with $n = 20$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4em} u_{20} &= u_1 r^{20-1} \\[6pt]
    &= (4480)(-0.75)^{20-1} \\[7pt]
    &\approx -18.9
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of an infinite geometric sequence formula
    $S_{\infty} = \dfrac{u_1}{1-r}$, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.3em} S_{\infty} &= \dfrac{4480}{1-(-0.75)} \\[8pt]
    &= 2560
    \end{aligned}
    $$
