# AA013

**Maximum mark: 6**

The first term and the common ratio of a geometric series are denoted,
respectively, by $u_1$ and $r$, where $u_1,r \in \mathbb{Q}$. Given that
the fourth term is $64$ and the sum to infinity is $625$, find the value
of $u_1$ and the value of $r$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
u_4 &= 64 \hspace{4em} \\[6pt]
u_1r^{4-1} &= 64 \\[6pt]
u_1r^3 &= 64
\end{aligned}
$$
and

$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{\infty} &= 625 \\[6pt]
\dfrac{u_1}{1-r} &= 625 \\[5pt]
u_1 &= 625(1-r)
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_1r^3 &= 64 \\[6pt]
625(1-r)r^3 &= 64  \hspace{6.45em}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hspace{14.4em}r = 0.8 \hspace{2em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{0.5em} [\text{since $r\in \mathbb{Q}$}]
$$

and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2.15em} u_1 &= 625(1-r) \\[7pt]
&= 625(1-0.8) \\[7pt]
&= 125
\end{aligned}
$$

**Note:** Solving $625(1-r)r^3 = 64$ on the G.D.C. gives two solutions, $r = 0.8$ and $r = 0.695\ldots$, both of which satisfy $|r| < 1$. However, only $r = 0.8$ is rational, so the second solution must be rejected since we are given $r \in \mathbb{Q}$ in the question.
