# AA627

**Maximum mark: 6**

The fifth term, $u_5$, of a geometric sequence is $125$. The sixth term,
$u_6$, is $156.25$.

1.  Find the common ratio of the sequence. [2 marks]

2.  Find $u_1$, the first term of the sequence. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the sum of the first $12$ terms of the sequence.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The common ratio is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{u_6}{u_5} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{156.25}{125} \\[11pt]
    &= 1.25 \hspace{3.2em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_5r^{n-5}$ with $n = 1$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_1 &= u_5r^{1-5} \\[6pt]
    &= 125(1.25)^{1-5} \\[6pt]
    &= 51.2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{u_1(r^n-1)}{r-1}$
    with $n = 12$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} S_{12} &= \dfrac{u_1(r^{12}-1)}{r-1} \\[12pt]
    &= \dfrac{51.2((1.25)^{12}-1)}{1.25-1} \\[12pt]
    &=2775.4...\\[6pt]
    &\approx 2780\hspace{3em}\text{rounded to 3 s.f.}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
