# AA004

**Maximum mark: 7**

An arithmetic sequence is given by $3$, $5$, $7,\dots$

1.  Write down the value of the common difference, $d$. [1 mark]

2.  Find
    1.  $u_{10}$;

    2.  $S_{10}$. [4 marks]

3.  Given that $u_n = 253$, find the value of $n$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have $u_1 = 3$ and $u_2 = 5$.
    Hence the value of $d$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= u_2 - u_1 \\[6pt]
    &= 5 - 3 \\[6pt]
    &= 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with $n = 10$,
        we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.1em} u_{10} &= u_1 + (10-1)d  \\[6pt]
        &= 3 + (10-1)(2) \\[6pt]
        &= 21
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula
        $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(u_1 + u_n)$ with $n=10$, we find

         $$
         \begin{aligned}
         S_{10} &= \dfrac{10}{2}(u_1 + u_{10}) \\[6pt]
         &= \dfrac{10}{2}(3 + 21) \\[11pt]
         &= 120
         \end{aligned}
         $$

3.  Substituting $u_n = 253$, $u_1 = 3$ and $d = 2$ in
    $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ and solving the resulting equation for $n$, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.6em} 253 &= 3 + (n-1)(2) \\[6pt]
    253 &= 2n+1 \\[6pt]
    252 &= 2n
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    n = 126\hspace{1.1em}
    $$
