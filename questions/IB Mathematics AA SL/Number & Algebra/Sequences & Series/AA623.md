# AA623

**Maximum mark: 6**

Only one of the following four sequences is arithmetic and only one of
them is geometric.
$$
\begin{array}{rcccccl}
a_n &=& 1,\,5,\,10,\,15,\,\dots &\,\hspace{4em}\,& c_n &=& 1.5,\,3,\,4.5,\,6,\,\dots \\[12pt]
b_n &=& \dfrac{1}{2},\,\dfrac{2}{3},\,\dfrac{3}{4},\,\dfrac{4}{5},\,\dots &\,\hspace{4em}\,& d_n &=& 2,\,1,\,\dfrac{1}{2},\,\dfrac{1}{4},\,\dots
\end{array}
$$

1.  State which sequence is arithmetic and find the common difference of
    the sequence. [2 marks]

2.  State which sequence is geometric and find the common ratio of the
    sequence.[2 marks]

3.  For the **geometric** sequence find the **exact** value of the
    eighth term. Give your answer as a fraction. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$

    \begin{array}{rclc|crcl}
    a_2-a_1 &\neq& a_3-a_2 &\,\hspace{0.5em}\,&\,\hspace{0.5em}\,& c_2-c_1 &=& c_3-c_2 \\[12pt]
    5-1 &\neq& 10-5 &&& 3-1.5 &=& 4.5-3 \\[12pt]
    4 &\neq& 5 &&& 1.5 &=& 1.5 \\[12pt] \hline
    &&&&&&& \\
    b_2-b_1 &\neq& b_3-b_2 &&& d_2-d_1 &\neq& d_3-d_2 \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{2}{3}-\dfrac{1}{2} &\neq& \dfrac{3}{4}-\dfrac{2}{3} &&& 1-2 &\neq& \dfrac{1}{2}-1 \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{1}{6} &\neq& \dfrac{1}{12} &&& -1 &\neq& -\dfrac{1}{2}
    \end{array}
    $$
    Hence
     $c_n$
    is arithmetic and
     $d = 1.5$

2.  We have
    $$

    \begin{array}{rclc|crcl}
    \dfrac{a_2}{a_1} &\neq& \dfrac{a_3}{a_2} &\,\hspace{0.5em}\,&\,\hspace{0.5em}\,& \dfrac{c_2}{c_1} &\neq& \dfrac{c_3}{c_2} \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{5}{1} &\neq& \dfrac{10}{5} &&& \dfrac{3}{1.5} &\neq& \dfrac{4.5}{3} \\[12pt]
    5 &\neq& 2 &&& 2 &\neq& 1.5 \\[12pt] \hline
    &&&&&&& \\
    \dfrac{b_2}{b_1} &\neq& \dfrac{b_3}{b_2} &&& \dfrac{d_2}{d_1} &=& \dfrac{d_3}{d_2} \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{2/3}{1/2} &\neq& \dfrac{3/4}{2/3} &&& \dfrac{1}{2} &=& \dfrac{1/2}{1} \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{4}{3} &\neq& \dfrac{9}{8} &&& \dfrac{1}{2} &=& \dfrac{1}{2}
    \end{array}
    $$
    Hence
     $d_n$
    is geometric and
     $r = \dfrac{1}{2}$

3.  Using the $n$th term formula $d_n = d_1r^{n-1}$ with $n = 8$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d_8 &= d_1r^{8-1} \\[8pt]
    &= 2\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{8-1} \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{64}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
