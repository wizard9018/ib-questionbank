# AA1023

**Maximum mark: 6**

The fourth term of an arithmetic sequence is equal to $13$ and the sum of the first $10$ terms is $55$.

1.  Find the common difference and the first term. [4 marks]

2.  Determine the greatest value of $n$ such that the $n$th term is positive. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with $n = 4$ and $u_4 = 13$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_4 &= u_1 + 3d \\[6pt]
    13 &= u_1 + 3d \tag*{\textcolor{green}{[1]}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1 + (n-1)d)$ with $n=10$ and $S_{10}=55$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_{10} &= \dfrac{10}{2}(2u_1 + 9d) \\[6pt]
    55 &= 5(2u_1 + 9d) \\[6pt]
    11 &= 2u_1 + 9d \tag*{\textcolor{green}{[2]}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Solving the system formed by equations [1] and [2] we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2u_1 + 6d &= 26 \hspace{3em} \text{(equation \textcolor{green}{[1]} $\times$ 2)} \tag*{\textcolor{green}{[3]}}\\[6pt]
    2u_1 + 9d &= 11 \tag*{\textcolor{green}{[4]}}\\[6pt]
    3d &= -15 \hspace{2.5em}\text{(equation \textcolor{green}{[4]} - equation \textcolor{green}{[3]})}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.8em}d = -5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_1 &= 13-3d \\[6pt]
    &= 13 - 3(-5) \hspace{1.5em}  \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-1.2em}u_1 = 28
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We must have $u_n > 0$. Using the $n$th term formula with $u_1=28$ and $d=-5$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_n &> 0 \\[6pt]
    u_1 + (n-1)d &> 0 \\[6pt]
    28 - 5(n-1) &> 0 \\[6pt]
    28 - 5n + 5 &> 0 \\[6pt]
    33 &> 5n \\[6pt]
    n &< \dfrac{33}{5}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    $n$ must be an integer, and the greatest integer less than $\dfrac{33}{5}$ is $6$.

    Hence, the greatest value of $n$ such that $u_n$ is positive is $n=6$.
