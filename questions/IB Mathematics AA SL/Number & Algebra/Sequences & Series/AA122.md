# AA122

**Maximum mark: 6**

The sum of an infinite geometric sequence is $27$. The second term of
the sequence is $6$. Find the possible values of $r$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have $S_{\infty} = 27$ and $u_2 = 6$.

Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1r^{n-1}$ with $n=2$, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_2 &= u_1r^{2-1} \\[12pt]
6 &= u_1r \\[6pt]
u_1 &= \dfrac{6}{r}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the sum of an infinite
geometric sequence formula
$S_{\infty} = \dfrac{u_1}{1-r}$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
27 &= \dfrac{6/r}{1-r} \\[5pt]
27-27r &= \dfrac{6}{r} \\[10pt]
9r - 9r^2 &= 2 \\[10pt]
0 &= 9r^2 - 9r + 2
\end{aligned}
$$
Using the quadratic formula to find the roots of the equation, we
obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{9.6em} r &= \dfrac{-(-9)\pm\sqrt{(-9)^2 - 4(9)(2)}}{2(9)} \\[9pt]
r &= \dfrac{6}{18},\hspace{0.25em}\dfrac{12}{18} \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
r = \dfrac{1}{3},\hspace{0.25em}\dfrac{2}{3} \hspace{0.3em}
$$
