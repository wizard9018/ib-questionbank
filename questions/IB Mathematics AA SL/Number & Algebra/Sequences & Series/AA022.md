# AA022

**Maximum mark: 15**

The first two terms of an infinite geometric sequence are $u_1 = 20$ and
$u_2 = 16\sin^2 \theta$, where $\text{\(0 < \theta < 2\pi\)}$, and
$\theta \neq \pi$.

1.
    1.  Find an expression for $r$ in terms of $\theta$.

    2.  Find the possible values of $r$. [5 marks]

2.  Show that the sum of the infinite sequence is
    $\dfrac{100}{3 + 2\cos 2\theta}$.
    [4 marks]

3.  Find the values of $\theta$ which give the greatest value of the
    sum. [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  The common ratio is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        r &= \dfrac{u_2}{u_1} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{16\sin^2 \theta}{20} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{4\sin^2 \theta}{5}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Since $0 < \theta < 2\pi$ and $\theta \neq \pi$, we have
        $\sin \theta \neq 0$.

        Hence we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        0 < \sin^2 \theta &\leq 1 \\[9pt]
        0 < \dfrac{4\sin^2 \theta}{5} &\leq \dfrac{4}{5} \hspace{2.8em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        0 < r \leq \dfrac{4}{5}
        $$

2.  Using the double-angle formula for cosine, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos 2\theta &= 1 - 2\sin^2\theta \\[6pt]
    2\cos 2\theta &= 2 - 4\sin^2\theta \\[6pt]
    3 + 2\cos 2\theta &= 5 - 4\sin^2\theta \hspace{4em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the sum of an infinite geometric sequence formula
    $S_{\infty} = \dfrac{u_1}{1-r}$,

    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_{\infty} &= \dfrac{20}{1 - \dfrac{4\sin^2 \theta}{5}} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{100}{5-4\sin^2 \theta} \\[15pt]
    &= \dfrac{100}{3 + 2\cos 2\theta}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The greatest value of the sum is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{100}{3 + 2(-1)} &= 100 \hspace{6.8em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence,
    solving the equation $\cos 2\theta = -1$ for $\theta$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2\theta &= \pi,\, 3\pi \hspace{2.5em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \theta = \dfrac{\pi}{2},\, \dfrac{3\pi}{2} \hspace{1.5em}
    $$
