# AA111

**Maximum mark: 6**

1.  Show that $(2n-1)^3 + (2n+1)^3 = 16n^3+12n$ for $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.
    [3 marks]

2.  Hence, or otherwise, prove that the sum of the cubes of any two
    consecutive odd integers is divisible by four. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (2n-1)^3 &= (2n)^3 - 3(2n)^2 + 3(2n) - 1 \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
    &= 8n^3 - 12n^2 + 6n - 1
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (2n+1)^3 &= (2n)^3 + 3(2n)^2 + 3(2n) + 1 \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
    &= 8n^3 + 12n^2 + 6n + 1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Summing our expressions for $(2n-1)^3$ and $(2n+1)^3$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (2n-1)^3 + (2n+1)^3 &= 8n^3 - \cancel{12n^2} + 6n - \cancel{1} + 8n^3 + \cancel{12n^2} + 6n + \cancel{1}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (2n-1)^3 + (2n+1)^3 = 16n^3 + 12n \hspace{2em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $2n-1$ and $2n+1$ be two consecutive odd integers for some
    $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

    The sum of the cubes of $2n-1$ and $2n+1$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.4em} (2n-1)^3 + (2n+1)^3 &= 16n^3 + 12n \hspace{2em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[6pt]
    &= 4n(4n^2 + 3) \hspace{2.22em} [\text{multiple of $4$}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the sum of the cubes of any two consecutive odd integers is
    divisible by four.
