# AA117

**Maximum mark: 8**

It is known that the number of trees in a small forest will decrease by
$5$ % each year unless some new trees are
planted. At the end of each year, $600$ new trees are planted to the
forest. At the start of $2021$, there are $8200$ trees in the forest.

1.  Show that there will be roughly $9060$ trees in the forest at the
    start of $2026$. [4 marks]

2.  Find the approximate number of trees in the forest at the start of
    $2041$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The number of trees in the forest at the start of $2022$ will be
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{T}_1 &= 8200\times0.95 + 600 \hspace{2.8em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The number of trees in the forest at the start of $2023$ will be

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.65em} \mathrm{T}_2 &= \mathrm{T}_1\times0.95 + 600 \\[7pt]
    &= \left(8200\times0.95 + 600\right)0.95 + 600 \\[6pt]
    &= 8200\times0.95^2 + 600(0.95 + 1)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the number
    of trees in the forest at the start of $2026$ will be

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{T}_5 &= 8200\times0.95^5 + 600\hspace{0.05em}(0.95^4 + 0.95^3 + 0.95^2 + 0.95 + 1) \\[8pt]
    &\approx 9060
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The approximate number of trees in the forest at the start of $2041$
    will be
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{T}_{20} &= 8200\times0.95^{20} + 600\bigg[\sum_{k=0}^{19}0.95^k\bigg] \\[2pt]
    &= 8200\times0.95^{20} + 600\bigg[\dfrac{1 - 0.95^{20}}{1 - 0.95}\bigg] \\[12pt]
    &\approx 10\hspace{0.15em}638
    \end{aligned}
    $$
