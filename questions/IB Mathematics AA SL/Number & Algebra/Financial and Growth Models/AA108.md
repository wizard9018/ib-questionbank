# AA108

**Maximum mark: 6**

On $1$st of January $2022$, Grace invests $$\$P$$ in an account that pays
a nominal annual interest rate of $6$ %,
**compounded quarterly**.

The amount of money in Grace's account **at the end of each year**
follows a geometric sequence with common ratio, $\alpha$.

1.  Find the value of $\alpha$, giving your answer to four significant
    figures. [3 marks]

Grace makes no further deposits or withdrawals from the account.

2.  Find the year in which the amount of money in Grace's
    account will become **triple** the amount she invested. [3 marks]
