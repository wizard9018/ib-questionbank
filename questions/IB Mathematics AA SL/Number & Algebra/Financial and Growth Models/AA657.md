# AA657

**Maximum mark: 6**

On $1$st of January $2021$, Fiona decides to take out a bank loan to
purchase a new Tesla electric car. Fiona takes out a loan of $$\$P$$ with
a bank that offers a nominal annual interest rate of
$2.6\hspace{0.05em}\%$, **compounded monthly**.

The size of Fiona's loan **at the end of each year** follows a geometric
sequence with common ratio, $\alpha$.

1.  Find the value of $\alpha$, giving your answer to five significant
    figures. [3 marks]

The bank lets the size of Fiona's loan increase until it becomes
**triple** the size of the original loan. Once this happens, the bank
demands that Fiona pays the entire amount back to close the loan.

2.  Find the year during which Fiona will need to pay back the
    loan. [3 marks]
