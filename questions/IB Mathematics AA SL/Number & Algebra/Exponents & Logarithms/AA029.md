# AA029

**Maximum mark: 5**

Solve $\log_6(x) + \log_6(x-5) = 2$, for $x > 5$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the properties of logarithms, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\log_6(x) + \log_6(x-5) &= 2 \\[6pt]
\log_6(x(x-5)) &= \log_6 6^2 \\[5pt]
\log_6 (x^2-5x) &= \log_6 36 \\[5pt]
x^2-5x &= 36 \\[5pt]
x^2 - 5x - 36 &= 0 \\[6pt]
(x + 4)(x - 9) &= 0 \\[6pt]
x - 9 &= 0 \hspace{3em} [\text{since $x + 4 > 0$}]
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
x = 9 \hspace{1.6em}
$$
