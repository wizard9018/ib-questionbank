# AA751

**Maximum mark: 6**

1.  Write the expression $4\ln 2 - \ln 8$ in the form of $\ln k$, where
    $k \in \mathbb{Z}$. [3 marks]

2.  Hence, or otherwise, solve $4\ln 2 - \ln 8 = -\ln (2x)$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the properties of logarithms, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    4\ln 2 - \ln 8 &= \ln \big(2^4\big) - \ln 8 \\[12pt]
    &= \ln 16 - \ln 8 \\[6pt]
    &= \ln\Big(\dfrac{16}{8}\Big) \\[9pt]
    & = \ln 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Replacing the left hand side of the equation with $\ln 2$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    4\ln 2 - \ln 8 &= -\ln (2x) \\[12pt]
    \ln 2  &= -\ln(2x) \hspace{2.2em} \\[12pt]
    -\ln 2 &= \ln(2x) \\[12pt]
    \ln \big(2^{-1}\big) &= \ln(2x) \\[12pt]
    2x &= 2^{-1} \\[12pt]
    2x &= \dfrac 1 2 \\[13pt]
    &\hspace{-0.525em} x = \dfrac{1}{4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
