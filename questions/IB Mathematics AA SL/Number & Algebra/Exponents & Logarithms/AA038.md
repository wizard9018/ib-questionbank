# AA038

**Maximum mark: 6**

Given that $\log_b 3 = 10$.

1.  Find the exact value of $\log_b 81$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the exact value of $\log_{b^2} 3$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the value of $b$, giving your answer correct to $3$ significant
    figures. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the properties of logarithms, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \log_b 81 &= \log_b 3^4 \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
    &= 4 \log_b 3 \\[6pt]
    &= 4(10) \\[6pt]
    &= 40
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the properties of logarithms, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \log_{b^2} 3 &= \dfrac{\log 3}{\log b^2} \hspace{2.1em} \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{\log 3}{2\log b} \\[5pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\log_b 3 \\[7pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(10) \\[10pt]
    &= 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the definition of logarithm, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \log_b 3 &= 10 \hspace{3.3em} \\[6pt]
    b^{10} &= 3 \\[4pt]
    b &= 3^\frac{1}{10} \\[4pt]
    &=1.116...
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    b \approx 1.12 \hspace{0.6em}
    $$
