# AA1315

**Maximum mark: 4**

It is given that $\log_k{\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)}=\dfrac{1}{5}$, where $k>0$.

Find the value of

1.    $\log_k{16}$; [2 marks]

2.    $\log_{k^2}{\left(\dfrac{1}{16}\right)}$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Let's begin by looking at the given relationship and expression

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \log_k{\left(\textcolor{red}{\dfrac{1}{4}}\right)}&=\dfrac{1}{5}\log_k{\textcolor{red}{16}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Notice that $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $16$ share the same base of $4$, this means we can rewrite $\log_k{16}$ in the terms of $\log_k{\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)}$.

    

    To do that we first write down

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    16&=\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{a}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    where $a$ is to be determined.

    Using index laws we can simplify the L.H.S. and the R.H.S. of above to get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    4^2&=4^{-a}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore $a=-2$.

    Using that we can rewrite the given expression in terms of $\log_k{\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)}$

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \log_k{16}&= \log_k{\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{-2}}\tag*{\textbf{{(M1)}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Using the logarithmic power law we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &= -2\log_k{\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)}\\[9pt]

    &=-2 \times \frac{1}{5}\\[9pt]

    &=-\dfrac{2}{5}\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Note that the base of the expression is now $k^2$, hence we need to use the change of base formula

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \log_{a}x&=\frac{\log_{b}x}{\log_{b}a}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    to change the base to $k$ so it matches the given relation

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \log_{k^2}{\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)}&=\frac{\log_{k}\left(\frac{1}{16}\right)}{\log_{k}k^2}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Note that $\frac{1}{16}=16^{-1}$ and $\log_{k}k^2=2$ hence we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &=\frac{\log_{k}\left(16\right)^{-1}}{2}\tag*{\textbf{{(M1)}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Using the logarithmic power law and our answer from part (a) we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &=-\frac{1}{2}\log_{k}\left(16\right)\\[9pt]

    &=-\frac{1}{2} \times -\frac{2}{5}\\[9pt]

    &=\dfrac{1}{5}\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
