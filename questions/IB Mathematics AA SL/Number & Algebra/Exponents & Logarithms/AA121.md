# AA121

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $a = \log_5b$, where $b > 0$. Write down each of the following
expressions\
in terms of $a$.

1.  $\log_5b^4$ [2 marks]

2.  $\log_5 (25b)$ [2 marks]

3.  $\log_{25}b$ [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the properties of logarithms, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \log_5b^4 &= 4\log_5b \\[6pt]
    &= 4a
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the properties of logarithms, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.5em} \log_5 (25b) &= \log_5 \left(5^2\hspace{-0.2em}\cdot b\right) \\[6pt]
    &= 2\log_5 5 + \log_5 b \\[6pt]
    &= 2 + a
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the properties of logarithms, we obtain

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{0.3em} \log_{25}b &= \dfrac{\log_5 b}{\log_5 25} \\[6pt]
     &= \dfrac{\log_5 b}{2\log_5 5} \\[10pt]
     &= \dfrac{a}{2}
     \end{aligned}
     $$
