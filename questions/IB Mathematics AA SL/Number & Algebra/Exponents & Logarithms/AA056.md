# AA056

**Maximum mark: 6**

Find the value of

1.  $\log_5 75 - \log_5 3$; [2 marks]

2.  $25^{\log_5 8}$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the properties of logarithms, we get

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \log_5 75 - \log_5 3 &= \log_5\Big(\dfrac{75}{3}\Big) \hspace{3.1em} \\[6pt]
     &= \log_5 25 \\[10pt]
     &= \log_5\big(5^2\big) \\[12pt]
     &= 2 \log_5 5 \\[12pt]
     &= 2
     \end{aligned}
     $$

2.  Using the properties of exponents and logarithms, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    25^{\log_5 8} &= \big(5^2\big)^{\log_5 8} \\[8pt]
    &= 5^{2\log_5 8} \\[6pt]
    &= 5^{\log_5 (8^2)} \\[7pt]
    &= 5^{\log_5 64} \\[9pt]
    &= 64
    \end{aligned}
    $$
