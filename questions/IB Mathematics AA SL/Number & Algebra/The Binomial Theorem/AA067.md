# AA067

**Maximum mark: 5**

In the expansion of $(2x + 1)^n$, the coefficient of the term in $x^2$
is $40n$, where $n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$. Find $n$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the binomial theorem, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
40nx^2 &= \binom{n}{2}\big[1^{n-2}\big]\big[(2x)^2\big]
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, solving the equation $(1)$ for $n$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
40nx^2 &= \bigg[\dfrac{n!}{2!(n-2)!}\bigg]\big[4x^2\big] \hspace{0.3em} \\[18pt]
40n &= \bigg[\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}\bigg]\big[4\big] \\[18pt]
20n &= n(n-1) \\[18pt]
0 &= n(n - 21) \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
n =21 \hspace{5.1em}
$$
