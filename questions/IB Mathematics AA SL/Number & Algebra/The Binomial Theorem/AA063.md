# AA063

**Maximum mark: 7**

1.  Write down the quadratic expression $3x^2 + 5x - 2$ in the form
    $(ax-b)(x+c)$.[2 marks]

2.  Hence, or otherwise, find the coefficient of the term in $x^9$ in
    the expansion\
    of $(3x^2+5x-2)^5$. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $(3x - 1)(x + 2)$

2.  Using the binomial theorem, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (3x^2+5x-2)^5 &= (3x-1)^5(x+2)^5 \\[6pt]
    &= \left[\sum_{k = 0}^5 \binom{5}{k}\big[(3x)^{5-k}\big]\big[(-1)^k\big]\right]\left[\sum_{k=0}^5\binom{5}{k}\big(x^{5-k}\big)\big(2^k\big)\right]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coefficient of the term in $x^9$ in the expansion of
    $(3x^2+5x-2)^5$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a_9 &= \left[\binom{5}{0}\big[3^{5-0}\big]\big[(-1)^0\big]\right]\left[\binom{5}{1}\big(2^1\big)\right] + \left[\binom{5}{1}\big[3^{5-1}\big]\big[(-1)^1\big]\right]\left[\binom{5}{0}\big(2^0\big)\right] \\[9pt]
    &= \big[(1)(3^5)(1)\big]\big[(5)(2)\big] + \big[(5)(3^4)(-1)\big]\big[(1)(1)\big] \\[15pt]
    &= 2025
    \end{aligned}
    $$
