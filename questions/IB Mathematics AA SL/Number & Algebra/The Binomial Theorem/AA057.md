# AA057

**Maximum mark: 4**

Expand $(2x - 3)^4$ in descending powers of $x$ and simplify your
answer.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using Pascal triangle

![d6143401ad9783a3554c63b7ab804d89d1d7b5f1.svg](images/d6143401ad9783a3554c63b7ab804d89d1d7b5f1.svg)

we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2em} (2x - 3)^4 &= (2x)^4 + 4(2x)^3(-3) + 6(2x)^2(-3)^2 + 4(2x)(-3)^3 + (-3)^4 \\[9pt]
&= 16x^4 + 4(8x^3)(-3) + 6(4x^2)(9) + 4(2x)(-27) + 81 \\[9pt]
&= 16x^4 - 96x^3 + 216x^2 - 216x + 81
\end{aligned}
$$
