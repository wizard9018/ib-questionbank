# AA071

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the expansion of $\bigg(x^3+\dfrac{2}{x}\bigg)^8$.

1.  Write down the number of terms in this expansion. [1 mark]

2.  Find the coefficient of the term in $x^4$. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The number of terms in this expansion is
     $9$

2.  The term in $x^4$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \binom{8}{5} \big[x^3\big]^{8-5} \bigg[\dfrac{2}{x}\bigg]^5&= 56\big[x^9\big]\bigg[\dfrac{32}{x^5}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= 1792x^4
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the coefficient of the term in $x^4$
    is  $1792$
