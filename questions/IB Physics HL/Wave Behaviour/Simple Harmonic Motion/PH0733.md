# PH0733

Jenny is standing on a rotating roundabout and the sun is casting her shadow onto a nearby wall. Jenny is in the position on the diagram when the roundabout starts rotating. The motion of her shadow approximates to simple harmonic.

What is the most likely position of her shadow after the turntable has moved through an angle of $35.5 \pi$?

![PH0733a](images/PH0733a.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From the databooklet:

$\hspace{1em}$ $1\  \ce{radian} \left(\ce{rad}\right)=\dfrac{180\degree}{pi}\ $

Every $2\ \pi$ radians she returns to the same position, so $\dfrac{35.5\ \pi}{2\ \pi}=17.75$ rotations

Jenny completes 17 full circles, then another 0.75 of a rotation.

$\hspace{1em}$ $0.75\ \times \left(360\degree\right)=270\degree$

Therefore, Jenny moves an extra $270\degree$ from her starting position after 17 rotations, so her shadow will be at position **B**, and her actual position will be $270\degree$ counter-clockwise from her starting position.
