# PH0953

Pendulum A has a length $l$ and a bob of mass $m$. Pendulum B has a length $\dfrac{l}{2}$ and a bob of mass $\dfrac{m}{2}$.

What is the ratio

- $\dfrac {\text{Frequency of oscillations of pendulum A}}{\text{Frequency of oscillations of pendulum B}}$ ?

- A. $\hspace{1em} $ $\dfrac{1}{2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em} $ $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt 2}$

- C. $\hspace{1em} $ 1

- D. $\hspace{1em} $ $\sqrt 2$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $T = 2 \pi \sqrt {\dfrac{l}{g}} \hspace{1em}$ and $\hspace{1em} f = \dfrac{1}{T}$ so we may write

- $f = \dfrac{1}{2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{l}{g}}} = \dfrac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{l}}$

$g$ is constant therefore

- $f$ $\alpha$ $\sqrt \dfrac{1}{l}$

(Note: frequency is independent of mass)

- $\dfrac{f}{\sqrt\dfrac{1}{l}}$ = constant

-  $f\sqrt l$ = constant

- $f_{A}\sqrt l_{A} = f_{B}\sqrt l_{B}$

- $\dfrac {f_{A}}{f_{B}} = \dfrac {\sqrt l_{B}}{\sqrt l_{A}} = \dfrac {\sqrt \dfrac {l_A}{2}}{l_A} = \sqrt \dfrac {l_A}{2l_A} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt 2}$
