# PH0481

**Maximum mark: 7**

The graph below shows the variation with displacement of kinetic energy of a 0.20 kg object which is performing simple harmonic motion.

![PH0481aaa](images/PH0481aaa.png)

1. Use the graph to determine the total mechanical energy of the object [1 mark]

2. On the graph, draw a dotted line that shows the variation with displacement of potential energy of the object. [2 marks]

3. Use conservation of energy to show that the speed of the object is approximately $1.2\,\textrm{m }\textrm{s}^{-1}$ when the displacement is 10 cm. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. $E_k$ max = $E_{total}$ = :answer[$160\,\textrm{mJ}$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    The graph shows that the kinetic energy as the object passes through the equilibrium point is $160\,\textrm{mJ}$. At this point, the potential energy (as shown by the dashed line) is zero, therefore $160\,\textrm{mJ}$ is also equal to the total energy of the system.

2. $\hspace{1em}$

    ![PH0481bb](images/PH0481bb.png)

    Dotted line passes through the origin $\checkmark$

    max value = 160 mJ at extreme points $\checkmark$

3. $E_k + E_p = E_{total}$

    $E_p=\dfrac{1}{2} kx^2$ or $E_p\propto x^2$ $\checkmark$

    $E_p = 160\,\textrm{mJ}$ when $x = 40\,\textrm{cm}$

    So if $x$ becomes $4$ times smaller, then the $E_p$ will become 16 times smaller. 

    So $E_p = 10\,\textrm{mJ}$ when $x = 10\,\textrm{cm}$ $\checkmark$

    $E_k = 160 - 10 = 150\,\textrm{mJ}\ = 0.150\textrm{ J}$ $\checkmark$

    $E_k=\dfrac{1}{2} mv^2$

    $v^2 = \dfrac{(2)(0.150)}{0.2}$

    $v=\ $:answer[$1.22\,\textrm{m}\,\textrm{s}^{-1}$]$\ \approx 1.2\,\textrm{m}\,\textrm{s}^{-1}$ $\checkmark$ (extra significant figure must be shown in calculation)
