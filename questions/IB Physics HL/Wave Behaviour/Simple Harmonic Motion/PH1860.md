## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The magnitude of the particle's velocity reaches its maximum values at $t=0$ s, 1 s, 2 s, 3 s...

At these times, the oscillating particle is passing through the equilibrium position, which means that the potential energy must be zero at these times.

Graph B correctly shows the potential energy of the particle which is what we were asked.

Graph A would correspond to the kinetic energy of the particle.
