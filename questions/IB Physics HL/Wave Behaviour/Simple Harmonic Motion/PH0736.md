# PH0736

A $5.0$ kg mass hanging from a spring with a spring constant ($k$) of $300\, $N m$^{-1}$ is in simple harmonic motion. If the maximum kinetic energy of the mass is $13.5\, $J, what is the maximum acceleration of the mass?

![PH0736](images/PH0736.jpg)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $2\, $m s$^{-2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $6\, $m s$^{-2}$
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $18\, $m s$^{-2}$
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $60\, $m s$^{-2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From the databooklet:

$\hspace{1em}$ $E=\dfrac{1}{2}k \Delta {x}^2$

Re-arrange:

$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta x=\ \sqrt{\dfrac{2E}{k}}$

$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta x= \sqrt{\dfrac{2(13.5)}{300}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{27}{300}} = \sqrt{0.09} = 0.3$ m

Since the restoring force ($F$) is proportional to the displacement ($x$):\
$\hspace{1em}$ $F= -k x$

$\hspace{1em}$ and

$\hspace{1em}$ $F=m a$

so:

$\hspace{1em}$ $a=\ \dfrac{k x}{m}$

Substituting gives

- $a=-\dfrac{(300)(0.3)}{5}=18 $ m s$^{-2}$
