# PH0731

Mario is bouncing on a trampoline such that the amplitude of his oscillations matches that of the trampoline mat. His motion is simple harmonic with an amplitude of 0.25 m. At the top of the motion, the force between Mario and the mat approaches zero.

What is the **approximate** frequency of his motion?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.1 Hz

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.5 Hz
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 1.0 Hz
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 10.0 Hz

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From the databooklet:

$\hspace{1em}$ $a_{max}=\ -\omega^2x$

Because the normal force on Mario approaches zero at the top of his motion, his acceleration must be equal to that of gravity, $10\, \ce{m}\, \ce{s}^{-2}$

$\hspace{1em}$ $-10={-\omega}^2 (0.25)$

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{-10}{0.25}=-\omega^2$

$\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{40}=\omega$

$\hspace{1em}$ $\omega=6.3\,\ce{rad}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$ (approx.)

From the data booklet:

- $T = \dfrac{1}{f} =\dfrac{2\pi }{\omega}$

- $T=\dfrac{2\pi}{6.3}=\dfrac{6.3}{6.3}=1.0\,$ Hz (approx.)
