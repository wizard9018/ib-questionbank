# PH0739

A 600 g mass is hanging from a frictionless spring. A force of 12 N is then applied by pulling the mass down by 20 cm and it oscillates in simple harmonic motion.

What is the maximum velocity the mass will achieve?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $1.0\, \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $1.2\, \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $2.0\, \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $2.2\, \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

To find the spring constant,\
From the databooklet:\
$\hspace{1em}$ $F=\ -kx$

$\hspace{1em}$ $k=\ \dfrac{12}{0.2} = 60\, \ce{N}\,\ce{m}^{-1}$

Find the potential energy in the spring:\
From the databooklet:\
$\hspace{1em}$ $E_p=\ \dfrac{1}{2}\ k\ x^2$

$\hspace{1em}$ $E_p= (\dfrac{1}{2})(60)(0.2)^2=1.2$ J

Find the maximum kinetic energy of the mass,\
From the databooklet:\
$\hspace{1em}$ $E_k=\ \dfrac{1}{2\ }m v^2$

$\hspace{1em}$ $E_k=\ \dfrac{1}{2}(0.6)v^2$

The maximum potential energy will equal the maximum kinetic energy:

$\hspace{1em}$ $E_p=E_k$

$\hspace{1em}$ $1.2=\ \dfrac{1}{2}(0.6)v^2$

$\hspace{1em}$ $1.2=0.3 v^2$

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{1.2}{0.3}=v^2$

$\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt4=v$

$\hspace{1em}$ $v=2.0\, \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1} $
