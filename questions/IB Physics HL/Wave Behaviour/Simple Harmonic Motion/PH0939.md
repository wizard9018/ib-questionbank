## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

- $a=-\omega^2x$

Graph of $x$ will have same shape as graph of $a$ but will be negative and scaled parallel to the $y$-axis.

The maximum amplitude and displacement are related in the following way

- $a_{max}=\omega^2 x_{max}$

- $x_{max}=\dfrac{a_{max}}{\omega^2}=\dfrac{a_{max}}{(\frac{2\pi}{T})^2}=\dfrac{a_{max}T^2 }{4\pi^2}$
