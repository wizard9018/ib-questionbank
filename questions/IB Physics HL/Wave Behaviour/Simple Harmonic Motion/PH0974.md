# PH0974

Below are three statements about a mass spring system moving in simple harmonic motion

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | When the velocity of the mass is maximum its acceleration is zero.   |
  | II. | The velocity of the mass is inversely proportional to its displacement from the equilibrium position. |
  | III. | The acceleration of the mass is always directed towards a fixed point. |

Which of the statements are correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

I is correct - Acceleration is zero when the mass is at the equilibrium position ($a\propto -x$ and $x=0$ at equilibrium by definition). Velocity is maximum at this point.

II is incorrect - $v=\pm\omega\sqrt{x_0^2-x^2}$. This is not a relation of inverse proportion between $v$ and $x$. Inverse proportion would imply $v$ is infinite when $x=0$, but we see that $v=\omega x_0$ when $x=0$.

III is correct - From the definition of simple harmonic motion, acceleration is always directed towards the equilibrium position.
