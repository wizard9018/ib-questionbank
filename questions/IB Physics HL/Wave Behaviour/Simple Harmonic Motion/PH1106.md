# PH1106

A simple pendulum A of length $L$ is set in motion from its lowest point so that it oscillates freely. When Pendulum A reaches maximum height, another simple pendulum B of length $4L$ begins to oscillate from its lowest point, with the same initial direction of motion as Pendulum A.

Which graph illustrates the angular displacement $x$ of each pendulum at time $t$?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The period of a simple pendulum is given by

- $T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{l}{g}}$

Hence, the period of Pendulum B can be calculated using the equation

- $T_B=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{4L}{g}}=2\Bigl(2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{L}{g}}\Bigr)=2T_A$

The frequency of Pendulum B is therefore half that of Pendulum A.

Finally, it is stated that Pendulum B commences its motion when Pendulum A is at maximum displacement, and swings in the same initial direction as Pendulum A. Thus, the $x$ value of Pendulum B increases at the beginning of its motion.
