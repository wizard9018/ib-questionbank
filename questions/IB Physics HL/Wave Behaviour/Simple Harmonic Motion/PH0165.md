# PH0165

A metal container filled with sand is stationary on a frictionless surface. It is connected to a wall with a horizontal spring. 

![PH0165-1](images/PH0165-1.png)

The container is pulled in one direction and released. It starts to execute simple harmonic motion with a period of $T$ and a maximum velocity of $v$. 

Sand is then removed from the container. Which of the following graphs correctly shows the possible variation of velocity with time for the new situation? The period $T$ and maximum velocity $v$ from the initial situation are shown on the graphs.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

The period of the oscillation decreases with the decrease of the mass according to the formula; 

- $T=2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$

Maximum velocity can be found by the following equation;

- $v_{max} = \omega x_0 =\dfrac{2\pi}{T} x_0 \hspace{2em}$ 

where $x_0$ is the amplitude.

Since amplitude is only affected by the initial displacement, the amplitude is constant. Therefore maximum velocity increases with the decrease of the period. The only graph that satisfies these two conditions is D.
