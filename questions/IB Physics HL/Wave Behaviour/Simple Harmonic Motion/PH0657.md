## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In the diagram shown, the release point is at the maximum displacement in the positive $x$ direction. The acceleration is proportional to but in the opposite direction to the displacement, therefore the acceleration-time graph should start with a maximum negative acceleration. Only graph D shows this.
