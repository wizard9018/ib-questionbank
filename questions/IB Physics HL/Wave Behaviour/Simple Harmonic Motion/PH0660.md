## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Spring $B$ accelerates less, therefore has a smaller restoring force for the same displacement i.e. $F=-kx$, so smaller $k$. Since potential energy is $E_H=\dfrac{1}{2}kx^2$, but $x$ remains constant for both springs, and $k$ for spring $B$ is smaller, the graph for $B$ must be under curve $A$. Since the maximum acceleration for $B$ is roughly half that of $A$, the spring constant is roughly half the value, hence for any given extension, the graph for the potential energy must be around half the height.
