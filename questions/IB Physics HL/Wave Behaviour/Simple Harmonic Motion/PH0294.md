## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Since frequency is 0.05 Hz, $T= \dfrac{1}{f} = \dfrac{1}{0.05} =$ 20 s 

The amplitude is 4 cm on either side of the equilibrium position because in one full cycle it travels four amplitudes $\bigg(\dfrac{16}{4} = 4 \bigg)$.
