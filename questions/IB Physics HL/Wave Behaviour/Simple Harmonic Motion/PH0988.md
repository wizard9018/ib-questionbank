# PH0988

A mass is placed on the end of a spring of natural length 5 cm causing it to oscillate in simple harmonic motion with an angular frequency $\omega$. The length of the spring when the speed of the mass is maximum is 15 cm

What is $\omega$? Assume $g=10\,\ce{N}\,\ce{kg}^{-1}$.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\pi \, \text{rad} \, \ce{s}^{-1}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $5 \, \text{rad} \, \ce{s}^{-1}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $2\pi \, \text{rad} \, \ce{s}^{-1}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $10 \, \text{rad} \, \ce{s}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

$\omega = \dfrac{2\pi}{T}$ so we must determine $T$

- $T=2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$

We have not been given $m$ or $k$ but we know for a mass on a spring.

- $F=mg=kx$

Where $x$ is the extension of the spring in its equilibrium position when the mass is hanging and at rest.

- $\dfrac{m}{k}=\dfrac{x}{g}$

- $T=2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{x}{g}}$ (note the similarity between this and the equation for the time period of a pendulum)

The spring is in its equilibrium position when the speed is maximum so the extension $x=\text{length in equilibrium}-\text{natural length}= 15\, \ce{cm}-5\,\ce{cm}=10\,\ce{cm}=0.1\,\ce{m}$.

- $T=2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{x}{g}}=2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{0.1}{10}}$

- $=2\pi\sqrt{0.01}$

- $=2\pi (0.1)=0.2\pi$

- $\omega = \dfrac{2\pi}{T}=\dfrac{2\pi}{0.2\pi}=10\,\text{rad}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$
