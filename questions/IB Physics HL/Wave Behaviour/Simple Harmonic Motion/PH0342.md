   $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
The net force on the metal block is the sum of force due to spring and friction between the surface and the metal block. Even though the force of spring changes with displacement, friction force does not change as the block slides on the surface. So direct proportionality between net force (and acceleration) and displacement is not satisfied. Additionally, there are consequences of the conditions for executing simple harmonic motion. One of them is having constant amplitude during the motion. It is also violated.
