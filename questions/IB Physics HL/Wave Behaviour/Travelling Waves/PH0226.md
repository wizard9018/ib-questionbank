# PH0226

Water drips onto a pond every 3.0 seconds. The speed of water waves in the pond has been measured to be $1.2 \, \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$. What is the wavelength of water waves generated because of the drops? 

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.4 m

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 1.6 m

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 3.6 m

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 4.8 m

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

If water drips onto the pond every 3 seconds, then we know that the period of water waves is $3 \, \ce{s}$.

The frequency $f$ of the water waves is 

$f = \dfrac{1}{T} = \dfrac{1}{3} = 0.33\, \ce{Hz}$ 

The speed of the waves $v$ is therefore:

$\begin{aligned}

v &= \lambda f\\

\hspace{1em}\\

\lambda &= \frac{v}{f}\\

\hspace{1em}\\ 

&= \frac{1.2}{0.33}\\

\hspace{1em}\\  

&= 3.6\, \ce{m}

\end{aligned}$

Alternatively, 

- $T=3.0\, \ce{s}$ and $v=1.2\, \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$

- $v=\dfrac{\lambda}{T}$ and $\lambda=vT$

- $\lambda = (1.2)(3)=3.6\, \ce{m}$
