# PH1158

Which of the following can NOT transmit both longitudinal and transverse waves?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Water

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Vacuum

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Air

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Glass

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The only type of waves that can travel in vacuum is electromagnetic waves and they are transverse.

The other three media can all transmit longitudinal waves in the form of sound, and transverse waves in the form of electromagnetic radiation.
