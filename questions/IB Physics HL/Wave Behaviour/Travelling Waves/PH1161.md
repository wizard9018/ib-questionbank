# PH1161

Which of the following statements is/are correct for electromagnetic waves?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | The frequency of ultraviolet radiation is greater than the frequency of green light.  |
  | II. | The wavelength of infrared radiation is greater than X-rays.  |
  | III. | Gamma rays travel faster than radio waves in a vacuum. |

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and II only

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Among the given statements, the first two are accurate. Ultraviolet light indeed has a higher frequency than green light, making statement I true. Additionally, infrared waves possess longer wavelengths compared to X-rays, as stated in statement II. However, the third statement is incorrect. In a vacuum, all electromagnetic waves, including gamma rays and radio waves, propagate at the speed of light, which remains constant for all of them. Thus, statement III is inaccurate.
