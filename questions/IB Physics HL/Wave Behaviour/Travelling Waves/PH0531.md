# PH0531

**Maximum mark: 6**

Waves travel through a wave tank. A small bob on the surface of the water is attached to an elastic rope which is fixed to the base of the tank.

![PH0531](images/PH0531.png)

The graph below shows the variation of vertical displacement of the bob with time.

![PH0531-1](images/PH0531-1.png)

$\footnotesize{\textrm{[Source: Created with GeoGebra by Tom Walsh- https://www.geogebra.org/m/jrWMapS7]}}$

1. Use the figure and the graph above to determine:

    1. the frequency of the water wave. [2 marks]

    

    2. the speed of the water wave. [2 marks]

2. State two times when the bob is at a crest of the water wave and two times when the bob is at the trough of the water wave. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 

    1. T = 50 ms. $\checkmark$

        $f = \dfrac{1}{T} = \dfrac{1}{(50\,\times\,10^{-3})}$

        $f$ = :answer[$20\,\ce{Hz}\,(\ce{s}^{-1})$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        The frequency of the oscillation of the bob is equal to the frequency of water wave  .

        

        The period can be determined by the time from peak to peak on the graph. The period is therefore T = 50 ms. Taking the reciprocal of the period, and account for the units, we find the final frequency:

        $f = \dfrac{1}{T} =\dfrac{1}{(50\,\times\,10^{-3})}$

        $f = 20\, \ce{Hz}\,(\ce{s}^{-1})$ 

    2. $\lambda = 2.5 \ce{m}$ $\checkmark$ 

        $v=\lambda f$

        $v = 2.5(20) =$ :answer[$50\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        From the figure, we know that the wavelength of the wave is 2.5 m. We can then use the wave equation $v=\lambda f$ to determine the velocity by multiplying by the frequency in the previous answer, giving a speed of $50\, \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$.

2. The crest is where the bob is at the maximum positive displacement.\

    At $t = 12.5\,\ce{ms}$, and $62.5\,\ce{ms}$. $\checkmark$

    The trough is where the bob is at the maximum negative displacement.\

    At $t = 37.5\,\ce{ms}$ and $87.5\,\ce{ms}$. $\checkmark$
