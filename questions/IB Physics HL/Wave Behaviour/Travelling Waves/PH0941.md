# PH0941

Below are three statements about sound waves.

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Sound waves can be diffracted.  |
  | II. | Sound waves can be refracted. |
  | III. | Oscillations of particles are perpendicular to the direction of energy transfer.|

Which of the statements are correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

I is correct – Sound can be diffracted. Sound waves change direction as they pass around the edge of an object.

II is correct – Sound can be refracted. The speed of a sound wave depends on the medium – for example sound travels much faster in water than it does in air.

III is incorrect – this is the definition of a transverse wave e.g., light. Sound waves are longitudinal. Oscillations of particles are parallel to direction of energy transfer.
