# PH1109

Waves of light and sound are transmitted through a glass box filled with air. Two experiments are carried out to investigate the effects of box contents on the wave speed $v$ in each case. In Experiment 1, air is removed from the box to create a vacuum. In Experiment 2, the box is instead filled with water.

![PH1109](images/PH1109.png)

Which line of the table correctly describes the outcomes of the two experiments?

$\\$

| | Light | Light | Sound | Sound |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| | $\hspace{1.5em}$ Experiment 1 $\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$ Experiment 2 $\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$ Experiment 1 $\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$ Experiment 2 $\hspace{1.5em}$ |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $v$ unchanged | $v$ unchanged | $v$ unchanged | $v$ unchanged |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $v$ unchanged | $v$ unchanged | $v$ is zero | $v$ unchanged |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $v$ increases | $v$ decreases | $v$ unchanged | $v$ increases |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $v$ increases | $v$ decreases | $v$ is zero | $v$ increases |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In a vacuum, there are no particles to transfer the mechanical vibrations needed for sound to travel. Hence, the speed of sound in Experiment 1 is zero. Conversely, light consists of electromagnetic waves that can travel through a vacuum, and the absence of particles enables them to travel more rapidly as the waves undergo no interactions with matter during travel (the refractive index of air is greater than one).

Due to its greater particle density, sound travels faster in water than in air as collisions between the particles occur more rapidly. By contrast, the speed of light decreases when the air is replaced with water, as the light interacts with a greater number of particles during travel (the refractive index of water is greater than that of air).
