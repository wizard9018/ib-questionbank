# PH1111

Three antennae are designed to transmit electromagnetic waves to a receiver located east of the transmission location. The designs are viewed from above as shown. Design A consists of an oscillating electric current $I$ directed horizontally in the east-west direction. In Design B, electric current instead oscillates horizontally in the north-south direction. Finally, Design C consists of a continuous electric current travelling in a horizontal circle.

![PH1111](images/PH1111.png)

Which design should be selected?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Design A

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Design B

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Design C

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ None of the designs are appropriate

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Electromagnetic waves are instigated by the oscillation of an electrical charge, and they propagate perpendicular to the direction of oscillation. Therefore, transmission from west to east requires the electric current to oscillate in the north-south direction.
