# PH1163

The diagram shows the side view of a wave generated on a rope at a specific time. Which of the marked points on the rope have maximum speed at this instant?

![Screenshot 2024-10-26 at 12.00.12 PM](<images/Screenshot 2024-10-26 at 12.00.12 PM.JPEG>)

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ W only

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ W and Y

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ X and Z only

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ W, X, Y and Z

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The wave on the rope is a transverse wave, where particles oscillate perpendicular to the direction of the wave. The motion of particles can be viewed as simple harmonic motion, with particles reaching maximum speed at equilibrium points, such as points X and Z.
