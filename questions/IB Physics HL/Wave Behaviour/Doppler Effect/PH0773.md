# PH0773

An observer is moving at $20\%$ of the speed of sound away from a sound source generating a frequency $f_0$. What is the correct expression for the perceived frequency in terms of $f_0$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{5}{6}f_0$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{6}{5}f_0$
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{5}{4}f_0$
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{4}{5}f_0$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Set the speed of the observer as:

$\hspace{1em}$ $u=\dfrac{2}{10}v$

Using the Doppler equation for a moving observer:

$\hspace{1em}$ $f=f_0\left (\dfrac{v-u}{v} \right) \to f= f_0 \left (\dfrac{v-\dfrac{2}{10}v}{v} \right)= \dfrac{8}{10}f_0= \dfrac{4}{5}f_0$
