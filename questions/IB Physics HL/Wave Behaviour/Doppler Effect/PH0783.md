2. A stationary observer measures the frequency and the speed of the wave radiated from a stationary loudspeaker as $f_{0}$ and $v_{0}$ respectively.

    Complete the table below to compare how the observer's measured frequency and wave speed values change in the following situations of relative motion between the source and observer.  [2 marks]

    | | Measured frequency | Measured wave speed |
    | --- | :-: | :-: |
    | Moving source towards the stationary observer |  | Same as $v_{0}$ |
    | Moving observer towards the stationary source | Higher than $f_{0}$ |  |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 1. Explanation using the Doppler effect $\checkmark$

        moving away $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The term redshift means that the observed wavelength is longer than the wavelength originally emitted by the source. Due to the Doppler effect, when a wave source is moving away the spacing of wavefronts is increased because the movement of the source itself causes each new wave to be created at a new location that is in the opposite direction from where the wavefront is travelling.

1. 2. Straight line $\checkmark$

        passing through origin

        **OR**

        extension of straight line passing through origin $\checkmark$

        ![P0783Ansb](images/P0783Ansb.png)

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        By using the formula of the Doppler effect:

        - $\dfrac{\Delta \lambda}{\lambda} \approx \dfrac{v}{c}$

        and the fact that the wavelength of the emission and speed of light is constant, it can be concluded:

        - $\Delta \lambda \propto v$

        This relationship can be expressed as a straight line passing through the origin.

2. :::tableoptions
    | | Measured frequency | Measured wave speed |
    | --- | :-: | :-: |
    | Moving source towards the stationary observer | Higher than $f_{0} \checkmark$   | Same as $v_{0}$ |
    | Moving observer towards the stationary source | Higher than $f_{0}$ | Greater than  $v_{0} \checkmark$ |

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    For the top left box:

    Due to the loudspeaker moving towards the observer, the wavelength experiences a decrease in wavelength and an increase in frequency..

    For the right bottom box:

    When the loudspeaker remains stationary and the observer is moving towards the source, the observed wavelength remains equal to the emitted wavelength, while the observed frequency becomes higher than the source frequency. This situation implies an increase in speed which occurs due to the observer moving relative to the medium, in accordance with the wave speed formula:

    - $v = \lambda f$
