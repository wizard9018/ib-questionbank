# PH0778

A person is standing on the side of the road while a car races past them at velocity $v$, while emitting a frequency $f$. The person remains stationary some distance away from the road at all times.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

At a large distance away from the person, the car is approaching in what can be perceived as a straight line. Hence there will be a Doppler shift, i.e. increase in frequency. As the car approaches the person, at the point where the car is passing the observer, the relative change in distance between the car and the observer is almost zero, hence there will be no Doppler shift and the frequency heard will be the same as emitted. The frequency must therefore decrease to equal $f$ at the origin.

As the car moves away from the person, again, the Doppler shift will come into effect as the car begins to move away from the person in an approximate straight line. Because the car is moving away from the observer, the observed frequency will be less than the emitted frequency.
