# PH0774

Two high speed trains pass each other, each with a speed of $60\ \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$. Just after the trains pass each other, the first train blows a whistle of frequency 2000 Hz. What is the apparent frequency of the whistle as heard by the passenger of the other train? The speed of sound in air is $340\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$.

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ 700 Hz

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ 1200 Hz

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ 1400 Hz

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ 1700 Hz

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

*** Note that the new syllabus states that questions where both the source and observer are moving will not be tested. We have left this question in the questionbank for extra challenge.

This question involves a double Doppler shift. The first train is considered to be a moving source (moving away), and the passenger is a moving observer (also moving away). The two Doppler effect formulae can then be combined as follows:

- $f=f_0\Bigl(\dfrac{v-u_r}{v+u_s}\Bigr) \rightarrow f=2000\Bigl(\dfrac{340-60}{340+60}\Bigr)=2000\Bigl(\dfrac{280}{400}\Bigr)=1400$ Hz
