# PH0320

A radio is placed on a turntable. The turntable rotates anticlockwise with constant angular frequency and the frequency of the generated sound waves is $f$. The frequency of sound waves is measured with a detector. The graph shows how the measured frequency $f^{\prime}$ varies with time t.

![PH0320-1a](images/PH0320-1a.png)

The diagram below shows four possible starting positions of the radio. Which letter gives the position of the radio at $t=0\, s$?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

According to the graph, The frequency at $t=0$ is $f$. It is the same with generated frequency.  Therefore, there must be no relative approach or receding between the source of the wave and the detector so that there is no Doppler effect.

Even though the radio has non zero speed for all the points, there will be no component of velocity towards or away from the detector on point A or C.
