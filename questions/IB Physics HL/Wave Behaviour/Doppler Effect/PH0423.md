## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1.  Doppler effect $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Due to the Doppler effect, the observed frequency is different from the emitted frequency when there is relative motion between the source of the wave and the observer. The frequency emitted from the galaxies is redshifted. It indicates the receding of the galaxies from the observatory at an increasing speed.

    2.  .

        ![PH0423-4](images/PH0423-4.png)

        Straight line $\checkmark$

        Passing through the origin $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Observed frequency and speed relationships in radio waves;

        - $\dfrac{\Delta f}{f}≈\dfrac{v}{c}$

        Since $\dfrac{v}{c}$ is constant, ∆ff must be constant. This indicates direct proportionality between $\Delta f$ and $f$. Direct proportionality can be shown with a straight line that passes from the origin of the graph.

    3. Speed of the galaxy  $\ll$  speed of light /c $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        - $\dfrac{\Delta f}{f}≈\dfrac{v}{c}$

        This equation can be used for the sources which move with a very small velocity compared to the speed of the wave generated. Since radio waves move with the speed of light, the speed of the galaxy must be small compared to the speed of light.

2.  Receding /going/moving away $\checkmark$

    At a constant velocity/speed at first $\checkmark$

    Then at an increasing velocity/speed $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:
\
    Observed frequency and speed relationships in sound waves;

    - $f^\prime=f\dfrac{v}{v\pm u_s}$

    Since observed/recorded frequency is lower than emitted frequency according to the graph, the equation should be;

    - $f^\prime=f\dfrac{v}{v+u_s}$

    This is the equation for the receding source. The graph shows that the observed frequency is constant initially. Moreover, $f$  and $v$ are constant during the motion. Hence, to get a constant observed frequency, the speed of the ambulance should be constant. Then the observed frequency decreases with time. This indicates an increase in the receding speed of the ambulance according to the formula.
