# PH0771

An ambulance is moving relative to a stationary observer with a constant velocity. The siren on the ambulance emits a sound wave with a frequency of 1600 Hz. If the observed frequency is measured to be 1700 Hz, what is the velocity of the ambulance if the speed of sound is $340\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$ Magnitude $\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{3.0em}$ Direction $\hspace{3.0em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| $20\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$ | Towards observer |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $21\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$| Towards observer |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $20\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$ | Away from observer |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $21\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$ | Away from observer |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $f=f_0\Bigl(\dfrac{v}{v-u}\Bigr) \rightarrow 1700=1600\Bigl(\dfrac{340}{340-x}\Bigr)$

- $340-x=\dfrac{1600}{1700}(340) \rightarrow x=340-\dfrac{16}{17}(340)=340-(16)(20)=20\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$

As the detected frequency is higher than the emitted frequency, the ambulance must be moving toward the observer.
