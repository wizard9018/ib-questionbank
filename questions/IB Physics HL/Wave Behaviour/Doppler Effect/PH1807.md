# PH1807

A supersonic aircraft travels at a speed 50% greater than that of sound in air. The aircraft flies past a siren emitting a sound of frequency $f$.

![PH1807q](images/PH1807q.JPEG)

What is the frequency of the sound detected by an observer on the aircraft once it has passed the siren?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{f}{3}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{2f}{5}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{f}{2}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{5f}{2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

For a moving observer, observed frequency $f'$ of stationary source is given by:

- $f'=f\left(\dfrac{v\pm u_o}{v}\right)$,

Where $f$ is the frequency measured by a stationary observer, $v$ is the velocity of sound in air, and $u_o$ is the velocity of the observer.

Note that the sign used in the equation is positive when the observer is moving towards the source and negative when the observer is moving away from the source.

Here we have $u_o=(v+50\%)=1.5v$

Substituting this into the equation we have

- $f'=f\left(\dfrac{v-1.5v}{v}\right)=f\left(\dfrac{-0.5v}{v}\right)=\dfrac{-f}{2}$,

Now frequency is a scalar, so we can ignore the negative sign: $f'=\dfrac{f}{2}$

Note: The negative sign in front of the indicates that the aircraft is moving faster than the sound.
