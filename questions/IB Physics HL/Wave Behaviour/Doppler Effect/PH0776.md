# PH0776

Two submarines are engaged in a military exercise. The first uses sonar to detect enemy vessels underwater. While moving at a speed of $20\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$, it emits an ultrasonic sound, which is reflected off the stationary enemy submarine directly ahead and is detected.

![image](images/image.png)

The emitted frequency is 148000 Hz. The speed of sound underwater is $1500\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$. What is the detected frequency?

- A. $\hspace{1.5em}$ 150 kHz

- B. $\hspace{1.5em}$ 152 kHz

- C. $\hspace{1.5em}$ 150000 kHz

- D. $\hspace{1.5em}$ 152000 kHz

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Since the submarine is moving while emitting a sound, this will increase the frequency. However, the reflected sound will also be detected by the moving submarine, so we have to use the Doppler equation for both moving source and observer:

- $f = f_0 \Bigl(\dfrac{v+u}{v-u}\Bigr) \rightarrow f=(148000)\Bigl(\dfrac{1500+20}{1500-20}\Bigr)$

- $\therefore f=(148000)\Bigl(\dfrac{1520}{1480}\Bigr)=152000$ Hz

So the answer is 152 kHz.
