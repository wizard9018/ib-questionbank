# PH0770

A firework flies towards a stationary observer at half the speed of sound, while emitting a sound of frequency 600 Hz. The speed of sound in air is 340 $\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}.$ What frequency does the observer hear?

- A.    $\hspace{1em}$ 300 Hz

- B.    $\hspace{1em}$ 400 Hz

- C.    $\hspace{1em}$ 900 Hz

- D.    $\hspace{1em}$ 1200 Hz

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $f=f_0\Bigl(\dfrac{v}{v-u}\Bigr)=600\Bigl(\dfrac{340}{340-170}\Bigr)=1200$ Hz
