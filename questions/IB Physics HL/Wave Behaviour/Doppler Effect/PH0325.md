# PH0325

A car drives along a straight road. Radio waves of frequency $f_o$  emitted from an antenna located at one end of the road are received by the car. The graph shows how the observed frequency $f$ by the car varies with time $t$.

![PH0325a](images/PH0325a.png)

What can be stated for the motion of the car?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Stationary then approaches the antenna at a constant speed.

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Moving at constant speed towards the antenna then stationary

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Accelerates towards the antenna then moves with constant speed.

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Accelerates towards the antenna then stationary.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The observed frequency and speed relationship for radio waves is given by

- $\dfrac{\Delta f}{f_o}≈\dfrac{v}{c}$

Since the observed frequency is greater than emitted frequency according to the graph;

- $\dfrac{\lvert f_o-f \rvert}{f_o}\approx\dfrac{v}{c}$

The graph shows that the observed frequency initially increases. Then the observed frequency does not change. This indicates an increase in speed at the first part of the motion, then constant speed. Since the observed frequency is greater than the emitted frequency, the car can not be stationary.
