# PH0772

A loudspeaker emits a frequency $f_0$ in a stadium. An observer is standing on the other side of the stadium. What can be concluded about the observed frequency $f$, if a constant wind is blowing from the speaker to the observer?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $f$ is greater than $f_0$.

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $f$ is less than $f_0$.

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $f$ will change depending on the wind direction.

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $f$ does not change.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

As per the Doppler equation:

$f=f_0\Bigl(\dfrac{v+u_r}{v+u_s}\Bigr)$

The frequency of sound is affected by the motion of the observer and/or the motion of the source relative to the medium. Because both observer and source have the same speed relative to the medium, there is no shift in the frequency.

This can also be considered in the context of the frame of reference. This situation with the medium moving relative to both source and observer is equivalent to a situation where the medium is at rest (i.e. there is no wind) and both source and observer moving at the same speed relative to the medium.
