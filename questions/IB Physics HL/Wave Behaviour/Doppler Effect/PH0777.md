# PH0777

A source and receiver are both moving to the right with velocity $u$ towards a stationary observer, with the source in front of the receiver. The source emits a continuous sound at frequency $f_0$.

![Screenshot 2023-11-28 at 1.59.34 PM](<images/Screenshot 2023-11-28 at 1.59.34 PM.png>)

What can be said about the frequency detected by the receiver and observer?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

For the stationary observer:

- $f=f_0 \Bigl(\dfrac{v}{v-u}\Bigr) \rightarrow f>f_0$

Since the denominator is less than the numerator.

For the moving receiver:

- $f=f_0 \Bigl(\dfrac{v+u_o}{v+u_s}\Bigr) \rightarrow f=f_0$

Since the receiver is moving TOWARDS the source sound wave, we use a + sign in the denominator. However, since the source is moving AWAY from the receiver, we also use a plus sign in the numerator. The speed of source and receiver are the same so the equation collapses as above.
