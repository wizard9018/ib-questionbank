# PH0184

In Young’s double-slit experiment, monochromatic light is incident on two slits and a diffraction pattern is observed on a distant screen. The graph shows the variation of intensity with distance from the centre of the pattern.

![PH0184](images/PH0184.png)

The slits are replaced by two slits with negligible width. What is the effect of this change on the interference pattern?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Central peak intensity increases and all maxima have the same intensity as the central peak

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Central peak intensity increases and greater spacing of maxima

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Central peak intensity decreases and all maxima have the same intensity as the central peak

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Central peak intensity decreases and greater spacing of maxima

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The interference pattern is not modulated by the single slit diffraction when slit width is negligible. Therefore, the intensities for all maxima should be the same. A decrease in slit width results in a decrease of maximum intensity. Therefore the peak values should be smaller.
