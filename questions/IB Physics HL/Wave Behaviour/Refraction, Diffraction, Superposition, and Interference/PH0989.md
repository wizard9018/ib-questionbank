## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

The central maximum will have the greatest intensity. Therefore, we can discount $C$ and $D$.

This is because a large number of rays passing through the center of the slit are responsible for this maximum. The central maximum is due to constructive interference of rays diffracted on the two edges of the slit.

For single slit diffraction $\theta = \dfrac{\lambda}{b}$

Where $\theta$ is the angle between the first order minimum and the central maximum. $\lambda$ is the wavelength of the light and $b$ is the slit width.

- $\theta = \dfrac{500\ \ce{nm}}{2.5\, \ce{\mu} \ce{m}}=\dfrac{500\times 10^{-9}}{2.5\times 10^{-6}}=200\times 10^{-3}=0.2\,\text{rad}$

So the first order minima are at $\pm0.2$ rad - the answer is $B$.
