# PH0177

In a single slit diffraction experiment, incident light passes through an aperture and a diffraction pattern is observed on a distant screen. $X$ is a point on the screen between the second-order minimum and the first-order minimum. $Y$ is a point at the centre of the principal maximum.

![Screenshot 2025-05-27 at 8.06.51 AM](<images/Screenshot 2025-05-27 at 8.06.51 AM.png>)

Which one of the following could be the ratio of the intensity of light on $X$ to the intensity of light on $Y$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.05

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.25

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.50

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.75

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Intensity falls off significantly from the central maximum to the secondary maxima. It decreases to approximately 5% of the initial value - this is information that should be memorised.  Therefore, the ratio should be equal to or less than 0.05.
