**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

As per the equation:

- $\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{b}$

Increasing the slit width causes the angle at which the first minimum occurs to decrease, hence the diffraction pattern becomes narrower but also lets more light through, increasing intensity.
