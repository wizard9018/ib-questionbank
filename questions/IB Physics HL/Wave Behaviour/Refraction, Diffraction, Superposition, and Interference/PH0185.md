# PH0185

Light with wavelength λ is incident on a diffraction grating. The maximum number of bright fringes that can be observed on the screen is 9. Which of the following gives the number of slits per cm in the grating?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$     $\dfrac{1}{400\lambda}{\ \ce{cm}}^{-1}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$     $\dfrac{1}{4\lambda}{\ \ce{cm}}^{-1}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$     $4\lambda\ {\ \ce{cm}}^{-1}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$     $400\lambda\ {\ \ce{cm}}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

To observe the maximum number of bright fringes,  the full diffraction angle of 90° must be observed.

The expression for the diffraction grating is

- $d\ \ce{sin}\ \theta\ =n\lambda $ , where d is the slit spacing.

There must be 4 maxima on each side of the central maximum to observe 9 total maxima. θ = 90° for the maximum number of bright fringes.

- $d\ \ce{sin}\ 90\degree =4λ    $

- $$

Number of slits per cm (N);

- $N=\dfrac{1.0\times{10}^{-2}}{d}=\dfrac{1.0\times{10}^{-2}}{4\lambda}=\dfrac{1}{400\lambda}{\ \ce{cm}}^{-1}$
