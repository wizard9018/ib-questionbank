# PH0762

What happens to the width of the central maximum of a diffraction pattern when the width of the slit through which light passes is increased?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ The width of the central maximum increases.

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ The width of the central maximum decreases.

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ The width of the central maximum remains constant.

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ The change in the width of the central maximum cannot be determined.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The angle to the first minimum of a single slit pattern is given by the formula

- $\theta = \dfrac{\lambda}{b}$

Where $b$ is the slit width.

Increasing the slit width decreases the distance to the first minima, which is twice the central maximum.
