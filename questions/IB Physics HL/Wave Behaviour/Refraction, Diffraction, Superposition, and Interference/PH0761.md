# PH0761

A beam of light with wavelength 600 nm is diffracted by a single slit of width 0.06 mm. The diffraction pattern is observed on a screen 2.0 m away from the slit. What is the distance between the central maximum and the first minimum of the pattern?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$  1.0 mm

- B. $\hspace{1em}$  2.0 mm

- C. $\hspace{1em}$  1.0 cm

- D. $\hspace{1em}$  2.0 cm

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Using the formula

- $\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{b} $

we obtain an angle of

- $\theta=\dfrac{600\times{10}^{-9}}{6\times{10}^{-5}}={10}^{-2}\ \ce{rad}$

From the diagram:

![triangle3](images/triangle3.png)

Applying the small angle approximation:

- $\ce{tan}\ \theta=\dfrac{d}{D}\ \rightarrow\theta\approx\dfrac{d}{D}$

- $d=\theta D={10}^{-2}\times2=0.02\, \ce{m}$
