# PH0362

Microwaves generated by a microwave transmitter are diffracted while passing through a slit. A receiver is positioned at a long distance of $D$ from the slit. The receiver is moved between point $A$ and point $C$.

![PH0362a](images/PH0362a.png)

1. Outline what is meant by diffracted microwaves.  [2 marks]

2. The receiver records the intensity of the microwave for various positions between $A$ and $C$. It measures zero intensity two times while moving from $A$ to $B$.

    1. On the axes below, draw the variation of measured intensity $I$ with the position $x$ while moving from $A$ to $C$. (There is no need to add values to the axes)            [3 marks]

    ![PH0362b](images/PH0362b.png)

    2. The slit used in the arrangement is replaced by one of greater width. State and explain the changes in the intensity-position graph you have drawn in (b)(i) [3 marks]

2. 1. $\hspace{1em}$

        Central maxima have twice the width of secondary maxima $\checkmark$

        The intensity of secondary maxima is less than 20% of central maxima by eye $\checkmark$

        Two minima on each side $\checkmark$

        (A and C do not need to have minimum intensity)

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        In a single slit diffraction pattern, central maxima have twice the width of secondary maxima and fifth times greater intensity than secondary maxima at least.

        Additionally, there must be two minima on each side according to the condition stated in the question.

    2.  Narrower central maximum/pattern compressed horizontally/more minima on each side $\checkmark$

        Fringe separation $∝ \dfrac{1}{Slit\ width}/ S ∝ \dfrac{1}{b}$ $\checkmark$

        Greater intensity/Higher amplitude $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Greater slit width allows more light to reach the screen. This increases the intensity. Therefore the peaks will be higher.  The width of maxima/fringe separation is proportional with $\dfrac{\lambda}{b}$.

        An increase in slit width $b$ results in a decrease in the width of the maxima. Therefore, maxima will be narrower.
