# PH0122

A monochromatic beam of light is incident on a double slit arrangement where the slit separation is $9.0 \times 10^{-5}\,\ce{m}$ and the slit-screen distance is 1.0 m. The graph below shows the relative intensity of the light reaching the screen with respect to the distance from the central point of the pattern.

![PH0122](images/PH0122.png)

$\footnotesize{\textrm{[Source: Created with GeoGebra - https://www.geogebra.org/graphing?lang=en]}}$

What is the wavelength of the light?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $9.0 \times 10^{-7}\,\ce{m}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $3.0 \times 10^{-7}\,\ce{m}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $4.5 \times 10^{-5}\,\ce{m}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $4.5 \times 10^{-7}\,\ce{m}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

The fringe separation is the product of wavelength of the waves to the slit-screen distance divided by the slit width.  

$s=\dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$

$\lambda=\dfrac{sd}{D}$

The distance between bright fringes on the graph is 0.5 cm.

$\lambda =\dfrac{(0.005)(9 \times 10^{-5})}{1}$

$\lambda = 4.5 \times 10^{-7}\,\ce{m}$
