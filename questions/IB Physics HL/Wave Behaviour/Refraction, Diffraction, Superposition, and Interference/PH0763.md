# PH0763

A monochromatic beam of light with wavelength $\lambda$ is diffracted by a single slit of width $a$. The diffraction pattern is observed on a screen located at a distance $D$ from the slit. Which of the following statements is correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ All maxima will have the same intensity.

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ The distance between the first minimum and the central maximum is proportional to $\dfrac{\lambda}{D}$.

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ The width of the central maximum is proportional to $\lambda$.

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ The width of the central maximum is proportional to $a$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

As per the equation:

- $\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{b}$

Where $b$ is replaced by $a$, we see that the angle will increase with $\lambda$.

The width on the screen is proportional to the distance away $D$, and so B is incorrect. It is also inversely proportional to slit width $a$ and so D is incorrect.
