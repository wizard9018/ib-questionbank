# PH1816

The diagram below shows the path followed by a light ray travelling between two media of refractive indices $n_1$ and $n_2$.

![Screenshoot 2025-03-25 at 1.58.06 PM](<images/Screenshoot 2025-03-25 at 1.58.06 PM.JPEG>)

What is the critical angle between the media?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{1}{n_2}\right)$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{1}{n_1}\right)$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{n_1}{n_2}\right)$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{n_2}{n_1}\right)$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From the diagram we see that light travelling from medium $n_1$ to medium $n_2$ bends towards from the normal. Therefore $n_1\lt n_2$.

The critical angle occurs when the angle of refraction for light travelling from a slow medium into a faster medium, which in this case is medium $n_2$ to medium $n_1$, is $90\degree$. Using Snell's law we have:

- $\dfrac{n_1}{n_2}=\dfrac{\sin\theta_2}{\sin\theta_1}$

- $\dfrac{n_1}{n_2}=\dfrac{\sin\theta_{critical}}{\sin(90\degree)}$

- $\dfrac{n_1}{n_2}=\textrm{sin}(\theta_{critical})$

- $\theta_{critical}=\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{n_1}{n_2}\right)$
