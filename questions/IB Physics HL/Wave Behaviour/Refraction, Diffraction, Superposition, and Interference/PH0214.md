# PH0214

A coherent light is incident on a double slit that has slit separation of $d$ and slit width of $b$. An interference pattern on a screen is observed. The graph shows the variation of intensity with distance from the centre of the pattern.

![PH0214](images/PH0214.png)

$S_1,\ S_2 $ and $S_3$ are the separations between various points on the pattern and the center of the pattern. The distance between the screen and slits is $D$. What is the wavelength of the incident light?

- A. $\hspace{1em}      \dfrac{bS_3}{D}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}      \dfrac{dS_3}{D}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}     \dfrac{bS_1}{D} $

- D. $\hspace{1em}      \dfrac{bS_2}{D}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

For constructive interference in the interference pattern;

- $d\ \ce{sin\theta}=n\lambda \hspace{2em}$ where $\hspace{2em}\ce{sin\theta}=\dfrac{s}{D}$, so;

- $n\lambda=\dfrac{ds}{D}$

For the first maxima, $n =1$ and $s = S_1$;

- $\lambda=\dfrac{dS_1}{D}$   but it is not one of the answers.

For single slit destructive interference in the diffraction pattern;

- $\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{b}$   where $\theta=\dfrac{s}{D}$, so;
$\lambda=\dfrac{bs}{D}$

For the first minimum, $s = S_2$ which gives
$\lambda=\dfrac{bS_2}{D}$

Therefore, the answer is $D$.
