# PH0215

Monochromatic light of wavelength λ falls normally on a diffraction grating. The maximum number of maxima that can be observed using this setup is 9. Which of the following can be the slit separation of the diffraction grating?

- A. $\hspace{1em}      1.5\ \lambda$

- B. $\hspace{1em}      3\ \lambda$

- C. $\hspace{1em}      4.5\ \lambda$

- D. $\hspace{1em}      6\ \lambda$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

For constructive interference;

- $d\ce{sin\theta}=n\lambda  $

After passing through a diffraction grating, the diffraction angle of the light cannot exceed or equal to $90\degree$ to be seen on a screen and 9 maxima occur when there is a central maximum and 4 maxima on either side of the central maxima.

Since $n$ is integer and is 4 in this case.

- $d\ce{sin{90\degree}}>4λ$  and $d\ce{sin{90\degree}}<5λ$

- $\ 4\lambda<d<5\lambda  $

Therefore, the answer is C.
