# PH0179

A beam of parallel light with a wavelength of λ is incident on a narrow slit. A diffraction pattern is formed on a distant screen. The angle of diffraction between the central maximum and the first minimum is θ. What would be the angle of the first diffraction minimum for the light with a wavelength of 3λ?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 3$\theta$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 2$\theta$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\theta$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{\theta}{3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The angle of diffraction for the first minimum when the wavelength is λ;

- $\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{b}$

The angle of diffraction for the first minimum $\theta_x$ when the wavelength is $3λ$.

- $\theta_x=\dfrac{3\lambda}{b}=3\theta$
