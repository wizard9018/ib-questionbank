## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 1. Constructive interference $\checkmark$

        Path difference $=n\lambda$ where $n$ is an integer

        **OR**

        Phase difference =  0

        **OR**

        Waves are in phase. $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        When light passes through the two slits, it diffracts and overlaps, forming an interference pattern. At points of maximum intensity, the waves arriving from each slit are in phase, meaning their peaks and troughs align perfectly. This occurs when the path difference is $n\lambda$, where $n$ is an integer (0, 1, 2, ...).

    2. $\ll$The distance between adjacent maxima$\gg$ increases as $s\propto D$/from $s=\dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Fringe separation $s$ is given by:

        - $s=\dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$

        Where $\lambda$ is wavelength, $D$ is the distance between the slits and the screen and $d$ is the separation between the slits.

        $d$ and $\lambda$ are constant so we have:

        - $s\propto D$

        If $D$ increases $s$ will also increase.

2. 1. Single slit modulation/effects of the slit width $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        In a double-slit experiment, each individual slit acts as a single slit diffraction source. This means that in addition to the interference pattern, there is an envelope pattern caused by diffraction from each slit. As the angle increases, the intensity of the maxima decreases due to this single slit diffraction effect.

    2. ::indent
        - $b=\dfrac{\lambda}{\theta}=\dfrac{580\times10^{-9}}{8.0\times10^{-2}}=$ :answer[$7.25\times10^{-6}$ m] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        For a single slit, slit width $b$ is given by:

        - $b=\dfrac{\lambda}{\theta}$

        Where $\lambda$ is wavelength and $\theta$ is the angle of the first intensity minimum in radians.

        The first intensity minimum of the **single slit envelope** occurs at $8.0\times10^{-2}$ radians.

        Substituting our values (converting $\lambda$ from nm to m) we have:

        - $b=\dfrac{\lambda}{\theta}=\dfrac{580\times10^{-9}}{8.0\times10^{-2}}=7.25\times10^{-6}$ m

    3. Any one from:

        Greater intensity of central maximum

        Greater average/overall intensity

        $\ll$Subsidiary/extra$\gg$ maxima in between the bright fringes

        Sharper maxima $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Adding a third slit increases the number of interfering waves. This creates more points of constructive interference which leads to a secondary maximum appearing between the bright fringes.

        The fringes become narrower and more defined, as more slits cause the interference pattern to sharpen.

        The overall intensity increases, especially at the central maximum, due to more light contributing to constructive interference.
