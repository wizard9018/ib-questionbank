# PH0765

A beam of monochromatic light is incident on a single slit and a diffraction pattern forms on the screen. The angle $\theta_1$ represents the angle at which the first minimum occurs.

![PH0765aaa](images/PH0765aaa.png)

What change will increase $\theta_1$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Increasing the width of the slit.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Decreasing the width of the slit.
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Increasing the distance between the slit and the screen.
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Decreasing the distance between the slit and the screen.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
According to $\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{b}$, decreasing slit width increases the angle. Changing the distance to the screen doesn't affect the angle.
