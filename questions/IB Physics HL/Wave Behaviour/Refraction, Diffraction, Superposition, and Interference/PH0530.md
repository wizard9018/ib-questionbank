# PH0530

**Maximum mark: 7**

A speaker emits sound waves toward a boundary between dry, cool air and moist, hot air. The speed of sound in the dry air is $340\ \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$ and the speed of sound in the moist air is $375\ \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$. Three wavefronts enter the boundary with an angle of 51$\ce{\degree}$ as shown.

![Screenshot 2026-04-22 at 8.51.05 AM](<images/Screenshot 2026-04-22 at 8.51.05 AM.png>)

1. Draw lines to show wavefronts in the moist air. [3 marks]

2. For the sound traveling in the moist air, calculate the angle between the wavefronts and the boundary. [2 marks]

3. Now the moist air region has been replaced with another medium $X$. Determine the minimum speed of sound in the medium $X$ above which sound will not cross the boundary for this angle of incidence. [2 marks]

2. $\dfrac{\sin(51\degree)}{\sin\theta}=\dfrac{340}{375}$ $\checkmark$

    $\theta$ = :answer[$59\degree$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    Note that the angle between the wavefront and the boundary is equal to the angle between the ray and the normal. Thus Snell's Law can be used with the given angle to find the angle that the wavefront makes with the boundary after refraction.

    Using Snell's law $\dfrac{\sin\theta _1}{\sin\theta _2}=\dfrac{v_1}{v_2}$. 

    $\dfrac{\sin(51\degree)}{\sin\theta}=\dfrac{340}{375}$ $\checkmark$

    $\theta$ = :answer[$59\degree$] 

3. $\dfrac{\sin(51\degree)}{\sin(90\degree)}=\dfrac{340}{v}$ $\checkmark$

    $v$ = :answer[$437\ \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    For the waves not to be able to enter, the angle of incidence must be equal to the critical angle or higher. At the critical angle, the angle of refraction will be $90\degree$.

    Substituting into the Snell's Law equation

    $\dfrac{\sin\theta _1}{\sin\theta _2}=\dfrac{v_1}{v_2}$. 

    $\dfrac{\sin(51\degree)}{\sin(90\degree)}=\dfrac{340}{v}$

    $v = 437\ \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$
