# PH0064

In a two slit interference experiment, light of wavelength 600 nm is sent to slits with a width of $b$. An intensity pattern is observed in a narrow range as shown below.

![PH0064](images/PH0064.png)

The distance between the slits is $d$. Calculate the ratio of  $\ \dfrac{b}{d}$.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 1

- B. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{1}{5}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 5

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ infinite

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{b} $ for single slit diffraction

$d\ce{sin\theta}=n\lambda$ for two slit interference.

The fifth maximum for two slit interference disappeared due to the single slit diffraction effect.

For small $\theta$,  $\ce{sin\theta} \approx\ \theta\ $, thus for the double slit pattern $\theta=\dfrac{5\lambda}{d}$

Using n = 5 and setting $\theta$ equal to one another gives

- $\dfrac{\lambda}{b}=\dfrac{5\lambda}{d}$

- $\dfrac{b}{d}=\dfrac{1}{5}$
