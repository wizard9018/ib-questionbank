## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 

    1. Ratio of the speed of waves in two different media. $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        Normally, the refractive index gives the ratio of the speed of light in a vacuum to the speed of light in a medium ($n = \dfrac{c}{v}$). More generally, however, refractive index can refer to the ratio of speeds at a boundary, ($n = \dfrac{v_1}{v_2}$).

    2. Any ray closer to the normal. $\checkmark$

        ![PH0576-2](images/PH0576-2.png)

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        Light has a higher speed in air than that in glass.

        The refractive index of air is 1; while the refractive index of glass is greater than one since

        $n=\dfrac{c}{v}$

        According to Snell's law

        $\dfrac{n_1}{n_2} =\dfrac{\sin(\theta_2)}{\sin(\theta_1)}$

        Then the angle of refraction is less than the angle of incidence and the refracted ray is closer to the normal.

    3. $\ll \dfrac{n_1}{n_2} = \dfrac{\sin (\theta_2)}{\sin(\theta_1)}\gg$

        $\sin(\theta_2)=\dfrac{\sin (40)}{1.5}$ $\checkmark$

        $\theta_2$ = :answer[$25^o$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    Using Snell's law 

    - $\dfrac{n_1}{n_2} = \dfrac{\sin (\theta_2)}{\sin(\theta_1)}$

    for air, n = 1

    Substituting gives

    - $\sin(\theta_2)=\dfrac{\sin (40)}{1.5}$ 

    - $\theta_2$ = $25^o$

    

2. $\ll \dfrac{(n_1)}{(n_2)} = \dfrac{\sin\,(\theta_2)}{\sin\,(\theta_1)}\gg$

    $\sin \ (\theta_c)=\dfrac{1}{1.5}$  $\checkmark$

    $\theta_c$ = :answer[$41.8\degree$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    When light travels from a medium of higher reflective index; the angle of refraction is greater than the angle of incidence according to Snell's law

    - $\dfrac{n_1}{n_2} = \dfrac{\sin\,\theta_2}{\sin\,\theta_1}$

    The critical angle is the angle of incidence that corresponds to the angle of refraction of $90\degree$.

    Then

    

    - $\dfrac{n_{glass}}{n_{air}} =\dfrac{\sin\,(90)}{\sin\,\theta_c}$

    - $\sin(\theta_c)=(\dfrac{1}{1.5})$

    

    - $\theta_c=42\degree$
