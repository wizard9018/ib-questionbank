# PH0189

Light of wavelength $\lambda_1$ and $\lambda_2$ is incident on a diffraction grating with the slit spacing of $d$. A series of bright lines are formed on a screen.

![PH0189](images/PH0189.png)

What is the ratio  $\dfrac{\ce{sin}\ \theta_1\ }{\ce{sin}\ \theta_2\ }\ $?

- A. $\hspace{1em}    \dfrac{2\lambda_1}{\lambda_2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}    \dfrac{2\lambda_2}{\lambda_1}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}    \dfrac{\lambda_1}{2\lambda_2}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}    \dfrac{\lambda_2}{2\lambda_1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

For a diffraction grating;

- $d\ce{sin}\ \theta\ =n\lambda$

for $\ce{sin}\theta_1\ $ when $\lambda{=\lambda}_1$ and $n=1$;

- $d\ce{sin}\theta_1=1\times\lambda_1$

- $\ce{sin}\theta_1=\dfrac{\lambda_1}{d}$

for $\ce{sin}\theta_2\ $ when ${\lambda=\lambda}_2$ and $n=2$;

- $d\ce{sin}\theta_2=2\times\lambda_2$

- $\ce{sin}\theta_2=\dfrac{{2\lambda}_2}{d}$

Therefore;

- $\dfrac{\ce{sin}\theta_1}{\ce{sin}\theta_2}=\dfrac{\frac{\lambda_1}{d}}{\frac{{2\lambda}_2}{d}}=\dfrac{\lambda_1}{{2\lambda}_2}$
