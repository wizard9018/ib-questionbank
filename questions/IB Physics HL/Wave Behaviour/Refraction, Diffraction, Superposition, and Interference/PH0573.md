## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Two or more waves overlap / **OWTTE** $\checkmark$

    The resultant displacement at any point is found by adding the displacements produced by each individual wave / **OWTTE** $\checkmark$

2. 

    1. The overlap is in the first half of the waves only $\checkmark$

        Maximum displacement of the overlapped part is positive and less than the amplitudes of both waves $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        At $t = 0.75$ s each wave will have moved one-quarter of their wavelength, which means that the front half of each wave will overlap.
