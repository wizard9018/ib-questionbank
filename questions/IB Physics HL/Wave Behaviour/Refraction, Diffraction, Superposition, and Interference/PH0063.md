# PH0063

Three monochromatic light sources, $K$, $L$, and $N$, are incident on a single slit separately. The diagram shows the diffraction patterns obtained on the screens for each light source along with the relative widths of their central maxima.

![PH0063](images/PH0063.png)

In which option are the possible colors of the light sources given correctly?

| | K | L |N|
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| red | green |$\hspace{1.5em}$ violet$\hspace{1.5em}$|

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $\theta=\dfrac{\lambda}{b}$

as wavelength increases angular separation increases, that is $\lambda_N>\lambda_L>\lambda_K$

- $\lambda_{red}>\lambda_{green}>\lambda_{violet}$
