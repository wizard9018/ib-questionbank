# PH0401

A 50 cm string that is fixed at both ends produces a standing wave in the third harmonic. The speed of the wave on the string is $200\ \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$

What is the frequency of oscillation?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 6 Hz

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 40 Hz

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 300 Hz

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 600 Hz

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The frequency of a string fixed at both ends in the third harmonic: 

![PH0401](images/PH0401.png)

$L = \dfrac{3}{2}(\lambda)$

$\lambda = (\dfrac{2}{3})L$

$v = f\lambda$

$f = \dfrac{v}{\lambda} = \dfrac{(3v)}{(2L)} = \dfrac{(3)(200)}{(2)(0.5)} = 600$ Hz
