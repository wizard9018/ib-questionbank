## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 1. Incident wave is reflected at the open end/far end of the tube  $\checkmark$

        Incident and reflected wave interfere/superpose/ combine  $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The sound wave travels through the tube and is partially reflected at the open end due to a difference in pressure inside and outside of the tube. The incident and reflected waves then superpose to form a standing wave with nodes and antinodes.

    2. Parallel  $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Sound waves are longitudinal. Particle motion is parallel to the direction of propagation of the wave. Here we have a stationary wave but the superposed waves are propagating parallel to the direction of the tube.

2. 1. $x_0=5\times 10^{-6} \, \ce{m}$   $\checkmark$

        $\omega=\dfrac{2\pi}{T}=\dfrac{2\pi}{0.02}=100\pi$   $\checkmark$

        $v_{max}=\omega x_0=(100\pi)(5\times 10^{-6})= 1.6\times 10^{-3}\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$  $\checkmark$

        Alternatively:

        Tangent line drawn on graph at $t=5\,\ce{ms}$ or $t=15\, \ce{ms}$ \
        OR \
        statement that gradient = velocity  $\checkmark$

        Attempt to find max magnitude of gradient  $\checkmark$

        Gradient (velocity) value between $1.5\times 10^{-3} \, \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$ and $1.7\times 10^{-3} \,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$  $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        Particle $P$ moves in simple harmonic motion.

        - $v=\pm \omega \sqrt{x_0^2-x^2}$

        Velocity of $P$ is maximum at the equilibrium position (when $x=0$). So, we may write:

        - $v_{max} = \omega \sqrt{x_0^2-(0)^2}=\omega x_0$

        From graph: amplitude $x_0=5\mu \ce{m}= 5\times 10^{-6}\, \ce{m}$

        From graph: time period $T=20\, \ce{ms}=0.02\, \ce{s}$

        - $\omega = \dfrac{2\pi}{T}=\dfrac{2\pi}{0.02}=100\pi$

        - $v_{max}=\omega x_0=(100\pi)(5\times 10^{-6})= 1.6\times 10^{-3}\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$

    2. Maximum velocity if $Q$ is zero/ velocity of $Q$ is always zero  $\checkmark$

        Maximum velocity of $R$ is greater than that of $P$  $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        $\bold{Q}$ is a node - particles at node do not oscillate so their displacement velocity and acceleration is always zero.

        $\bold{R}$ is an antinode, so amplitude of oscillation centered at $R$ is greatest. Maximum amplitude means that (magnitude of) displacement, velocity and acceleration of particle centered at $R$ is greater than any other point on the standing wave at all times.

    3. $\lambda=vT=(330)(0.02)=6.6\, \ce{m}$    $\checkmark$

        $l=\dfrac{\lambda}{2}=\dfrac{6.6}{2}=3.3\, \ce{m}$  $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        One full oscillation of the standing wave occurs in the same time as a full oscillation for particle $P$. So, time period of the standing wave is 20 ms or 0.02 s (from graph of $P$).

        - $v=f\lambda$ and $f=\dfrac{1}{T}$ so $v=\dfrac{\lambda}{T}$ (alternatively we can think of this equation as speed = distance/ time for a wave)

        Rearranging $v=\dfrac{\lambda}{T}$ for $\lambda$ gives

        - $\lambda=vT=(330)(0.02)=6.6$ m

        For the first harmonic of a tube open at both ends $l=\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$

        ![image](images/image.png)

        - $l=\dfrac{\lambda}{2}=\dfrac{6.6}{2}=3.3$ m
