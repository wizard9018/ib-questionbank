# PH0527

**Maximum mark: 8**

A thin tube with a length of $L$ is immersed in water and fixed to the base of a container of liquid by a string. The length of the tube above the surface of the water is $X$ and inside the water is $Y$.

![PH0527](images/PH0527.png)

A speaker is emitting a single-frequency sound above the tube. For particular values of $X$, a standing wave is established in the tube.

1. Explain how a standing wave is formed in this tube. [2 marks]

2. The frequency of the sound is 200 Hz. It is found that the smallest value of $X$ for which a standing wave is established in the tube is 42.0 cm. Estimate the speed of sound in the tube. [3 marks]

3. The string is cut and the tube rises in the container, decreasing the length of tube $Y$ inside the water. When $Y$ is 22 cm, a standing wave is next formed in the tube. Calculate the total length of the tube. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Sound waves move down the tube and are reflected from the closed end of the tube/surface of the water $\checkmark$

    then incident waves interfere/superpose with the reflected waves $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    Standing waves occur when two identical waves travel in opposite directions in a medium. This situation arises here because of the reflection of the waves from the closed end.

2. $X = (\dfrac{1}{4})\lambda$ $\checkmark$

    $v=\lambda f$ $\checkmark$

    $v = 4Xf = (4)(0.42)(200)$

    $v$ = :answer[$336\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: 

    ![PH0527-1](images/PH0527-1.png)

    For the first resonant length, the distance $X$ will be equal to $\dfrac{\lambda}{4}$.

    Thus $\lambda = 4X = 4(0.42) = 1.68$ m

    And knowing that the frequency $f =$ 200 Hz

    Therefore $v = f\lambda = (200)(1.68) = 336\ \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$

3. $X = (\dfrac{3}{4}) \lambda$ $\checkmark$

    $X = (0.75)(1.68) = 1.26$ m $\checkmark$

    $L = 0.22 + 1.26 = 1.48$ m $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: 

    ![PH0527-2](images/PH0527-2.png)

    The next resonant length occurs when $X$ has lengthened to ($\dfrac{3}{4}$) $\lambda$

    The wavelength has already been calculated, therefore $X = (\dfrac{3}{4})\lambda = (0.75)(1.68)=1.26$ m

    It is given that the section in the water has a length of $22$ cm

    Therefore the total length is $1.26 + 0.22 = 1.48$ m
