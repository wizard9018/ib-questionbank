# PH0816

A loudspeaker is placed next to a tube $AB$ which is open at both ends as shown in the diagram. The loudspeaker plays sound of constant frequency causing a standing wave of the second harmonic to be established inside the tube.

![stadningtube](images/stadningtube.png)

Below are three statements about the standing wave.

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | The standing wave is formed from the superposition of two waves travelling in opposite directions.   |
  | II. | The sound wave is partially reflected at $B.$ |
  | III. | The wavelength of the sound wave is equal to the length of the tube. |

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

I is correct. Standing waves are formed when two identical progressive waves are travelling in opposite directions.

II is correct. The wave moving to the right is produced from the loudspeaker and the wave traveling to the left is due to partial, in-phase, reflection of the traveling wave at the open end of the tube. This wave is of the same frequency as the original wave.
