# PH0399

A string fixed at both ends oscillates in a standing wave as shown with a wavelength of 400 mm.

![PH0399](images/PH0399.png)

What is the length of the string?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 600 mm

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 800 mm

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 1200 mm

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 2400 mm

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

The distance between two successive nodes in a standing wave is half a wavelength. There are 6 such sections, therefore 3 full wavelengths. 

- $(3)(400\, \ce{mm}) = 1200$ mm

Alternative:

From the diagram, the standing wave is in the sixth harmonic.

For a string fixed from both ends

- $\lambda=\dfrac{2L}{n}$

- $400 \times 10^{-3}=\dfrac{2L}{6}$

- $L=\dfrac{(6)(400\times 10^{-3})}{2}=1.2\, \ce{m}=1200$ mm
