# PH0945

Particle A is travelling in a magnetic field with a speed $v$. Particle A makes an angle of 30$\text{\textdegree}$ to the field. Particle B is travelling in the same field and makes an angle of 60$\text{\textdegree}$ to the field. This is illustrated below.

![PH0945](images/PH0945.png)

It is given that both particles have the same charge and experience the same force. What is the speed of particle B?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $v\,\sin (30\text{\textdegree})$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $v\,\sin (60\text{\textdegree})$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$  $\dfrac{v\,\sin\, (60\text{\textdegree})}{\sin\, (30\text{\textdegree})}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{v\,\sin\, (30\text{\textdegree})}{\sin\, (60\text{\textdegree})}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$F=Bqv \sin\,\theta$

Where $\theta$ is the angle between the velocity of the particle and the field (such that $v\,\sin\,\theta$ is the component of the velocity that is perpendicular to the field).

- $v\,\sin\,\theta=\dfrac{F}{q\,B}$

$F,\ B$ and $q$ are constant so we may write.

- $v\,\sin\,\theta=constant$

Therefore

- $v_A\,\sin\,\theta_A=v_B\,\sin\,\theta_B$

- $v\,\sin(30\text{\textdegree})=v_B\,\sin(60\text{\textdegree})$

- $v_B= \dfrac {v\,\sin(30\text{\textdegree})}{\sin(60\text{\textdegree})}$
