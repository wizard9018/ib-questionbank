# PH0802

An alpha particle is accelerated horizontally from rest through a potential difference $V$. Following its acceleration, the alpha particle immediately enters a magnetic field of magnetic flux density $B$. The magnetic field lies in the same plane as the paper and makes an angle $\theta$ with the vertical. The alpha particle is seen to follow a helical path of radius $r$ in the magnetic field.

![PH0802](images/PH0802.png)

Which of the following is a correct expression for the angle $\theta$?

($u$ is the unified atomic mass unit and $e$ is the elementary charge)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ ${\sin}^{-1}\left(\dfrac{2}{Br}\sqrt{\dfrac{uV}{e}\ }\right)$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ ${\sin}^{-1}\left(\dfrac{4}{Br}\sqrt{\dfrac{uV}{2e}\ }\right)$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ ${\cos}^{-1}\left(\dfrac{2}{Br}\sqrt{\dfrac{uV}{e}\ }\right)$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ ${\cos}^{-1}\left(\dfrac{4}{Br}\sqrt{\dfrac{uV}{2e}\ }\right)$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

An alpha particle has a mass of $4u$ and a charge of $2e$.

As the alpha particle accelerates electric potential energy is converted into kinetic energy.

Work done by the electric field as alpha particle is accelerated across the potential difference is equal to kinetic energy gained by the alpha particle:

- $q\,V=\dfrac{1}{2\,}mv^2$

- $2\,e\,V=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(4u\right)v^2 $

Where $v$ is the velocity of the alpha particle as it exits the electric field and enters the magnetic field.

Simplifying gives:

- $eV=uv^2$

Rearranging for $v$ gives:

- $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{e\,V}{u}\ }$

The angle $\theta$ is such that $B\,\cos\,\theta$ gives us the component of the magnetic force acting perpendicular to the velocity of the alpha particle (upwards). So magnetic force acting on the alpha particle is given by $Bqv\cos\theta$.

(The formula in your formula book $F=Bqv\sin\theta$ refers to the case where the angle $\theta$ is the angle between the velocity and the field lines)

As the alpha particle enters the magnetic field it undergoes circular motion. The magnetic force is equal to centripetal force:

- $Bqv\cos\,\theta=\dfrac{mv^2}{r}$

- $Bq\cos\,\theta=\dfrac{mv}{r}$

- $B\left(2e\right)\cos\,\theta=\dfrac{\left(4u\right)v}{r}$

Substituting expression for $v$ from above gives:

- $Be\,\cos\,\theta=\dfrac{2u}{r}\sqrt{\dfrac{eV}{u}\ }$

- $\cos\, \theta=\dfrac{2u}{rBe}\sqrt{\dfrac{eV}{u}\ }$

Taking $\dfrac{u}{e}$ inside the square root gives:

- $\cos\, \theta=\dfrac{2}{Br}\sqrt{\left(\dfrac{u^2}{e^2}\right)\dfrac{eV}{u}}$

- $\cos\, \theta=\dfrac{2}{Br}\sqrt{\dfrac{uV}{e}}$

- $\theta={\cos}^{-1}\left(\dfrac{2}{Br}\sqrt{\dfrac{uV}{e}}\right)$
