# PH0804

Two parallel wires **A** and **B** are separated by a distance of $1.2\ $cm as shown in the diagram. Both wires have a length of $80$ cm and wire **A** carries a current of $10\, $A . It is observed that the wires experience a force of $1.6 \times{10}^{-3}\, $N towards each other.

What is the magnitude of the current in wire **B**?

![Screenshot 2024-12-26 at 10.42.25 AM](<images/Screenshot 2024-12-26 at 10.42.25 AM.JPEG>)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $6.0\, $A

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $8.0\,$A
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $10\, $A
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $12\, $A

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{F}{L}=\mu_0\dfrac{I_1I_2}{2\pi r}$

Rearranging for $I_2$ gives:

$\hspace{1em}$ $I_2=\dfrac{2\pi rF}{I_1\mu_0L}$

Substituting values gives:

$\hspace{1em}$ $I_2=\dfrac{2\pi(0.012)(1.6\times{10}^{-3})}{(10)(4\pi\times{10}^{-7})(0.8)}=\ 12\, $A
