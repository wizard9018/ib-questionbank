# PH0787

A proton with a speed of $v$ enters a region in a vacuum where there is a magnetic field perpendicular to the page and an electric field due to oppositely charged plates. The proton does not deflect.

![PH0787aa](images/PH0787aa.png)

Consider the following particles travelling in the same region in the same direction.

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | A neutron with the speed of $2v$   |
  | II. | A proton with the speed of $2v$ |
  | III. | An electron with the speed of $v$ |

Which of the particles would not be deflected?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

As the proton passes through the region, it experiences two forces: one magnetic $F_B$ and one electric $F_E$. To enable the proton to travel without deviating from its path, these two forces must have equal magnitudes but act in opposite directions, effectively cancelling each other out:

- $F_B=F_E$

- $qvB=qE$

- $v=\dfrac{E}{B}$

The deduced relationship shows that the path followed by a charged particle does not depend on its charge. Moreover, the magnitude of the electric field and the magnetic flux density are constant. Hence, the only criteria for the charged particle to follow a specific path is that it must be moving with a specific speed, $v$.

Therefore, while an electron with the speed of $v$ satisfies this criterion, a proton with the speed of $2v$ does not. A neutron is a chargeless particle. Hence, it will not experience any force on itself due to electric and magnetic fields. It is also undeflected.
