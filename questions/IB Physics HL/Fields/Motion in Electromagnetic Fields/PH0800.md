# PH0800

A wire of length 0.4 m lies in a horizontal magnetic field of magnetic flux density $50\, \mu$ T as shown. The wire is in the same plane as the paper and makes an angle of 30$\text{\textdegree}$ with the field.

![PH0800](images/PH0800.png)

A current of 0.7 A is passed through the wire. What is the magnitude of the magnetic force acting on the wire?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $3.8\times{10}^{-6}$ N

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $7.0\times{10}^{-6}$ N

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $1.2\times{10}^{-5}$ N

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $1.4\times{10}^{-5}$ N

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$F=BIL\sin\theta$ where $\theta$ is the angle between the wire and the field lines (so that $I(\sin\theta)$  gives the component of the current traveling perpendicular to the field)

Given that $\sin \left(30\right)=0.5$

- $F=\left(50\times{10}^{-6}\right)\left(0.7\right)\left(0.4\right)\sin \left(30\right)\ $

- $\hspace{1em} =\left(50\times{10}^{-6}\right)\left(0.7\right)\left(0.4\right)\left(0.5\right)$

- $\hspace{1em} =7.0\times{10}^{-6}$ N
