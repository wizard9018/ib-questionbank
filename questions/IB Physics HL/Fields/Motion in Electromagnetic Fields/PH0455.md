# PH0455

A charged particle is moving horizontally when it enters an electric field between two parallel plates. The path followed by the particle when it passes through the plates is shown. Assume gravitational effects to be negligible.

![PH0455](images/PH0455.png)

Consider the following statements about the charged particle.

- I.$\hspace{1em}$ The particle is positively charged.

- II.$\hspace{1em}$ A magnetic field in a direction into the page could cause the charged particle to move horizontally without deflection.

- III.$\hspace{1em}$ A magnetic field in a direction out of the page could cause the charged particle to move horizontally without deflection.

Which statements are correct?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Only II

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Neither

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- I.$\hspace{1em}$ Correct: Since the particle is deflected toward the negative plate it is positively charged.

- II.$\hspace{0.7em}$ Correct: In order not to be deflected magnetic force acting on the particle should be upwards. According to the right hand rule the magnetic field should be into the page.

- III.$\hspace{0.45em}$ Incorrect: In order not to be deflected the magnetic force acting on the particle should be upwards. According to the right hand rule magnetic field should be into the page.
