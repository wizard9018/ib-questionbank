# PH0784

Two parallel wires, each carrying a current $I$, experience an electromagnetic force denoted as $F$, and they have a length $L$ with a separation distance of $r$ between them. The force between the wires is doubled when

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Only $I$ is doubled

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Only $r$ is doubled

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Only $L$ is doubled

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $L$ and $r$ are both doubled

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The force F between two parallel wires:

- $F=L\mu_0\dfrac{I_1I_2}{2\pi r}$

When given values are substituted:

- $F=L\mu_0\dfrac{I^2}{2\pi r}$

Therefore:

- $F\propto\ L\ $

- $F \propto\ I^2\ $

- $F\propto\ \dfrac{1}{r}$

Therefore, when length is doubled force is also doubled.
