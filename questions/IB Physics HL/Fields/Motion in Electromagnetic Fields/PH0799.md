# PH0799

The diagram below shows the path of a charged particle as it travels through a magnetic field directed into the plane of the page.

![PH0799](images/PH0799.png)

Below are three statements about the particle inside the magnetic field.

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | The kinetic energy of the particle remains constant.   |
  | II. | The force acting on the particle does not change. |
  | III. | The particle is negatively charged. |

Which of the statements are correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

I is correct as the speed of the particle is constant (uniform circular motion) the mass is also unchanged so $E_k=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$ remains constant.

II is incorrect as the force changes direction as the velocity changes. The force remains perpendicular to velocity.

III is correct. Using Fleming's left-hand rule it can be deduced that the direction of the current (rate of flow of positive charge) is in the opposite direction to the path shown meaning the charge must be negative.
