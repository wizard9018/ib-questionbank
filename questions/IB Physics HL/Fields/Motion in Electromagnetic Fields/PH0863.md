# PH0863

Two square coils, coil **A** and coil **B** are placed in a magnetic field.  Both coils carry a current. The plane of coil **A** is perpendicular to the magnetic field and the plane of coil **B** is parallel to the magnetic field as shown in the diagram below.

![PH0863ccc](images/PH0863ccc.png)

Which row correctly describes the motion of each of the coils due to their interaction with the magnetic field?

$\\$

| | Coil A | Coil B
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
$\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Rotation | Linear acceleration
$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Linear acceleration | Rotation
$\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | No acceleration | Linear acceleration
$\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | No acceleration | Rotation

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Current will flow around the loop and have the same magnitude everywhere.

Using Fleming's left-hand rule $\text{\textendash}$ the force on each side of coil **A** is such that the net force and net torque on the coil are zero. Therefore there is no acceleration. The forces acting on coil **A** are illustrated below.

![PH0863msaa](images/PH0863msaa.png)

There is no force acting on the sides of coil B where the current is parallel to the field.

Using Fleming's left-hand rule $\text{\textendash}$ the force on the sides of coil **B** that are perpendicular to the field are equal and opposite so net force is zero meaning no linear acceleration. However, the lines of action of the forces do not meet so there is a net torque on the coil $\text{\textendash}$ coil will rotate. The forces acting on coil **B** are illustrated below.
