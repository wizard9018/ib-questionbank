# PH0458

**Maximum mark: 8**

A charged particle $x$ enters a region of magnetic field $B$ of 2.0 T with a speed of $3.5 \times 10^6\, \ce{m\, s^{-1}}$ in the shown direction. The magnitude of the charge on the particle is $1.6 \times 10^{-19}$ C.

![PH0458a](images/PH0458a.png)

1. Determine whether the particle is positive or negative. Justify your answer.  [2 marks]

2. Calculate the magnitude of the force $F$ on this particle when it enters the magnetic field.  [2 marks]

A particle $y$ with an equal but opposite charge enters the same magnetic field $B$ as shown.

![PH0458b](images/PH0458b.png)

3.
    1. If the ratio $\dfrac{v_x}{v_y}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, calculate the magnitude of the magnetic force on charge $y$.    [1 mark]

    2. Draw an arrow indicating the direction of the magnetic force on this particle when it just entered the magnetic field.    [1 mark]

    3. Particle $y$ has the same mass as particle $x$, draw the path of particle y as it moves through the field.   [2 marks]

1. Positive. $\checkmark$

    Using the right hand rule/ force is directed upwards. $\checkmark$

2. $F = \ll qvB \gg$

    $F =  (1.6 \times 10^{-19})(3.5\times 10^6) (2)\,\checkmark $

    $F = 1.12 \times 10^{-12} \ll \ce{N} \gg\,\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$
    The magnetic force on a charged particle $F$

    $
    \begin{aligned}
    F &= qvB \\
    F &= (1.6 \times 10^{-19})(3.5\times 10^6)(2)\\
    &= 1.12 \times 10^{-12}\, \ce{N}
    \end{aligned}
    $
