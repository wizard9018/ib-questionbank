# PH1821

Which of the following was a key finding of Millikan's oil drop experiment?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Electrons exhibit wavelike behavior

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Electric charge is quantised

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Light energy is quantised

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ The size of the atomic nucleus is small compared to the size of the atom

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Millikan's oil drop experiment (1909) measured the charge of individual oil droplets suspended in an electric field. By adjusting the field and observing the droplets' motion, he determined that charge comes in discrete units—multiples of a fundamental charge, which he identified as the charge of a single electron $1.6\times10^{-19}\,$C.

Option A is incorrect because wavelike behaviour of electrons was demonstrated in the electron diffraction experiments by Davisson and Germer.

Option C is incorrect as light quantization was introduced by Planck and later explained by Einstein in the photoelectric effect.

Option D is incorrect because the discovery of the small atomic nucleus was made in Rutherford's gold foil experiment.
