# PH0429

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two positive charges $2Q$ and $Q$ are located as shown in the diagram. Some of the electric field lines due to the charges are shown. Points $P$ and $ S$ are two points as located on the diagram.

![PH0249](images/PH0249.png)

1.  Define the term electric potential.    [2 marks]

2. Sketch the equipotential line that passes through point $P$. [2 marks]

3. A positive charge is moved from point $P$ to point $S$. State and explain how the electric potential energy changes due to this move.   [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Work done per unit charge. $\checkmark$

   To bring a small/test charge to a location from a point of zero potential. $\checkmark$

2. One complete loop. $\checkmark$

   perpendicular to the electric field lines.  $\checkmark$
(roughly “peanut shape” as below)

   ![PH0429-1](images/PH0429-1.png)

   $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
   Equipotential lines must always be perpendicular to electric field lines, therefore the answer can be obtained by continuing to adjacent field lines, always crossing at 90°.

3. the electric potential energy increases $\checkmark$

   $S$ is at higher potential/positive charge is repelled therefore it requires more energy at $S$/the displacement is against the direction of the electric force so positive work is done on the charge.  $\checkmark$

   $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
   Moving positive charges against the direction of field lines requires work to be done, therefore there is an increase in the electrical potential energy.
