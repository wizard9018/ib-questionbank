# PH0218

The diagram shows the equipotential surfaces of two charges.

![PH0218](images/PH0218.png)

Which of the following can be the directions of the electric field at points A and B?

$\\$

| | Direction at point A | Direction at point B |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| $\uparrow$ | $\uparrow$ |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\uparrow$ | $\downarrow$ |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\gets$ | $\gets$ |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\gets$ | $\rightarrow$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The pattern of equipotential surfaces belongs to two like charges. That indicates that field direction at points A and B should be in the same direction. Additionally, field lines and equipotential surfaces are at right angles to each other. The only answer that satisfies these conditions is A. Therefore, the answer is A.
