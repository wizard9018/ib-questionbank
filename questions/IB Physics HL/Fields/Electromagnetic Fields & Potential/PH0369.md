# PH0369

A positive charge Q and four points on equipotential lines are represented in the diagram shown.

A negative ion is moved from location 1 to 2, then to 3 and finally to 4.
Which of the following gives the correct nature of the net work done on the ion during each section of this movement?

![PH0369](images/PH0369.png)

| | 1 to 2| 2 to 3 | 3 to 4|
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| $\hspace{1.5em}$negative$\hspace{1.5em}$ |$\hspace{1.5em}$ positive $\hspace{1.5em}$|$\hspace{1.5em}$positive$\hspace{1.5em}$|
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | positive | negative |negative|
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | negative | zero |positive|
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | positive| zero |negative|

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The negative ion is attracted by the positive charge, therefore its energy at the inner equipotential line is less than the outer one.

Moving from 1 to 2, it moves to a lower energy, therefore work is negative

Moving from 2 to 3, it moves along the same equipotential line, therefore no work done

Moving from 3 to 4, it moves to a higher energy, therefore positive work is done.
