# PH1270

For a point charge, the variation of potential with distance around the charge is known. Which of the following quantities can be determined?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Sign of the point charge   |
  | II. | Strength of electric field created by the point charge at a given distance |
  | III. | Work done to move another charge from an infinite distance to a specific point |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Only I

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Only II

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Only I and II

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Using the potential difference equation, both the magnitude and sign of the charge can be determined.

With the known values of charge and distance, the electric field strength can also be calculated from the gradient of a plot of the variation of potential with distance.

However, to determine the work done to move a charge to a specific point, the magnitude of the charge being moved must also be known.
