# PH0364

Two parallel plates are separated by a distance d and connected to a battery of potential difference V as shown in the diagram. A proton released from the positive plate gains a speed v when it reaches the opposite plate.

The separation of the plates is decreased to $\dfrac{d}{2}$.

What would be the speed gained by an alpha particle released from the positive plate, when it reaches the opposite plate?

![PH0364aa](images/PH0364aa.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$     $\dfrac{v}{\sqrt2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$     $\dfrac{v}{2}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$     $v$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$     $v\sqrt2$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The charge of a proton is $e$ and its mass is $m$

Using $\frac{1}{2}mv^2=$ eV

- $ v=\sqrt{\dfrac{2eV}{m}}$

An alpha particle has mass $4m$ and charge $2e$. The voltage is maintained at $V$,  therefore changing the separation has no impact on the energy imparted.

- $\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{2}(4m)v_{alpha}^2&=(2e)V\\
\hspace{1em}\\
v_{alpha}&=\sqrt{\dfrac{eV}{m}}\\
\hspace{1em}\\&=\dfrac{v}{\sqrt2}\\
\end{aligned}$
