# PH0990

The diagram below shows a massive negatively charged object.

![image](images/image.png)

The magnitude of the gravitational potential difference between point A and B is $\Delta V_g$ and the magnitude of the electric potential difference between points A and B is $\Delta V_e$.

What is the external work done moving a small object of mass $m$ and charge $-q$ from point A to point B?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $-m\Delta V_g - q\Delta V_e$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $m\Delta V_g - q\Delta V_e$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $q\Delta V_e-m\Delta V_g$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $q\Delta V_e+m\Delta V_g$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Gravitational force is attractive so moving objects away from each other requires external work. Work done against gravitational force $= m\Delta V_g$ is positive.

Electric force between two negatively charged objects is repulsive so moving objects away from each other does not require external work (work is done by the field). Work done against electric force $=-q\Delta V_e$ is negative.

Therefore the correct answer is

- $ m\Delta V_g-q\Delta V_e$
