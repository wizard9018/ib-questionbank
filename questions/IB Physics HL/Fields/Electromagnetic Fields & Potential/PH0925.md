# PH0925

A spherical oil drop of diameter $d$, density $\rho$, and charge $q$ is suspended between two horizontal parallel plates separated by a distance $x$ as shown in the diagram below.

![image](images/image.png)

The drop is stationary. Which of the following is a correct expression for the potential difference between the plates?

Assume buoyant force is negligible.

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{3q}{4\pi \rho gxd^3}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{6q}{\pi \rho gxd^3}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{\pi}{6q}\rho gxd^3$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{4\pi}{3q}\rho gxd^3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

For the drop to be stationary, it must be in equilibrium. The two forces acting on the drop are weight $=mg$ (downwards) and electric force $=Eq$ (upwards). The free body diagram is as follows:

![image](images/image.png)

Equating weight and electric force:

- $mg=Eq$

- (Volume)$\rho g=\dfrac{V}{x} q$

Assuming drop is spherical:

- $\Bigl(\dfrac{4}{3}\pi \Bigl(\dfrac{d}{2}\Bigr)^3 \Bigr) \rho g=\dfrac{V}{x} q $

- $\dfrac{\pi}{6} \rho gd^3=\dfrac{V}{x} q$

- $V=\dfrac{\pi}{6q} \rho gxd^3$
