# PH1046

Three negative charges are arranged in an equilateral triangle as shown below.

A small positive test charge is placed near the charges. Which of the following shows a path that could be taken by the positive charge along which no work is done?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

B shows a line of equipotential around the charges. The electric field strength is the same for all points along this line. This cannot be said for the lines in A, C and D.

No work is done moving a test charge along lines of equipotential.

B shows one of these lines.

A, C and D involve cutting across these equipotential lines so work must be done (either by the field or the particle).
