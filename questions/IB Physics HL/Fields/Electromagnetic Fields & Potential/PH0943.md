# PH0943

An electron is accelerated through a potential difference of $300\, $V. The final kinetic energy of the electron is $6.4\times{10}^{-17}\ $J. What is the initial kinetic energy of the electron?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $100\, $eV

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $200\, $eV

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $300\, $eV

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $400\, $eV

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The electric potential energy gained by an electron accelerating through a potential difference of $300\ $V is $300\ $eV

Converting final kinetic energy into electrons:

- $6.4\times{10}^{-17}$ J $=\dfrac{6.4\times{10}^{-17}}{1.6\times{10}^{-19}}=400\ $eV

Initial kinetic energy + electric potential energy = final kinetic energy

Initial kinetic energy + 300 eV $=  400 \ $eV

Initial kinetic energy = $100\ $eV
