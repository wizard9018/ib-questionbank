# PH0927

In an experiment, oil drops are passed through a thin tube of air. As they move, the oil droplets become negatively charged.

Which of the following rows correctly identifies the name of the process and the direction of electron transfer?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$Name of process$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$Direction of electron transfer$\hspace{1.5em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Charging by friction |Air molecules to oil drop |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Charging by friction | Oil drop to air molecules  |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$Charging by electrostatic induction$\hspace{1.5em}$  | Air molecules to oil drop |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Charging by electrostatic induction  | Oil drop to air molecules  |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Electrons are transferred to the oil drops (we know this as the question states that the oil drops become negatively charged) as they move past air molecules. This is an example of transfer of charge via friction.
