# PH0417

Two positive charges $q_1$ and $q_2$ are separated by a distance $d$. A third positive charge $q$ is placed at a distance $\dfrac{d}{4}$ from $q_1$ so that the charge $q$ is in equilibrium.

![PH0417](images/PH0417.png)

What is the ratio $\dfrac{q_1}{q_2}$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 16

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 4

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{1}{9}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 3

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

For charge $q$ to be in equilibrium, the magnitude of forces from $q_1$ and $q_2$ must be equal

![PH0417-1](images/PH0417-1.png)

- $\begin{aligned}

F_1&=(F_2)\\

\hspace{1em}\\

\frac{kq_1 q}{(\dfrac{d}{4})^2} &=\frac{kqq_2}{(d-\dfrac{d}{4})^2}\\

\hspace{1em}\\

\frac{q_1}{(\dfrac{d}{4})^2} &= \frac{q_2}{(\dfrac{3d}{4})^2}\\

\hspace{1em}\\

\frac{q_1}{q_2} &=\dfrac{\dfrac{d^2}{16}}{\dfrac{9d^2}{16}} =\frac{1}{9}\\

\end{aligned}$
