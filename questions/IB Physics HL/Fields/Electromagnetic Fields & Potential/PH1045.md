## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Potential at a point is given by the sum of  $\dfrac{kq}{r}$ where $r$ is the distance from each charge.

Consider the midpoint of the top left and bottom left charges.

![image](images/image.png)

As we move to the left of this midpoint, we are further away from all of the charges so potential must decrease.

Hence, we can rule out B, C and D because they show horizontal equipotential lines, implying that potential is constant for anywhere to the right and left of this point.

B shows equipotential for top left and bottom right charges being equal and opposite to top right and bottom left charges.

C shows equipotential for top two charges being equal and opposite to bottom two charges.
