# PH0370

The diagram shows equipotential lines due to a point charge.

A second point charge of $0.8\,\mu$ C is moved from X to Y.

What is the work done on this charge?

![PH0370](images/PH0370.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}16 \,\mu$ eV

- B. $\hspace{1em}10^{14}$ J

- C. $\hspace{1em}10^{14}$ eV

- D. $\hspace{1em}10^{13}$ eV

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\begin{aligned}
Work = q\Delta \ce{V} &= (0.8 \,\times\,10^{-6}\, \ce{C})[-30 - (-50)]\, \ce{V} \\
&= (0.8 \,\times\, 10^{-6})(20)\, \ce{J} \\
&= 16 \,\times\, 10^{-6}\, \ce{J}
\end{aligned}$

This may appear as the answer but the units need to be converted.

Converting to eV

- $\dfrac{16\times{10}^{-6}}{1.6\times{10}^{-19}}=10\times{10}^{13}={10}^{14}$ eV
