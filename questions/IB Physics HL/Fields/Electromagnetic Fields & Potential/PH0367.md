## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The electric field vector inside a hollow conductor is zero. Due to the lack of field inside the sphere, the electric potential is constant because no work is required to move a charge within the sphere. At the surface, the potential can be determined by the equation

- $V=\dfrac{kQ}{r}$

This will also be the constant potential inside the sphere.

Outward from the surface of the sphere, the electric field varies with the $\underline{\textrm{inverse}}$ square of the distance from the center, following the equation

- $E=\dfrac{kQ}{r^2}$

while electric potential decreases inversely with the distance from the center using the equation given previously.
