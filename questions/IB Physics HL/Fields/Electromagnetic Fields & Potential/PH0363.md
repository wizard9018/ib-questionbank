# PH0363

The graph shows the variation of the electric potential due to a point charge as a function the distance $r$ from its center.

![potentialaaa](images/potentialaaa.png)

Which of the following gives the work done $\underline{\textrm{by the electric field}}$ on a charge of $-3\, \mu \ce{C}$ when it is moved from $r_1$ to $r_2$ ?

- A. $\hspace{1em} -3\,\mu$C divided by the shaded area

- B. $\hspace{1em}-3\,\mu$C multiplied by the shaded area

- C. $\hspace{1em} -60\, \mu$J

- D. $\hspace{1em} +60\, \mu$J

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $\begin{aligned}
\Delta E &= q\Delta V\\
&= q ( V_{final} - V_{initial})\\
 &= (-3\mu \ce{C})(-30+50)\\
 &=-60\,\mu \ce{J}\\
 \end{aligned}$

Thus the change in position in the field has caused a loss of electric potential energy. However, the question asks for the work done by the field. Since the move is in the direction of the force on the charge, the field does positive work. This gives answer D.
