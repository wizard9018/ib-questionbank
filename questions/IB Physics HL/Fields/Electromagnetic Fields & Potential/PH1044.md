# PH1044

The diagram below shows equipotential lines for the arrangement of four identically charged conducting spheres P, Q, R and S.

![image](images/image.png)

P is positively charged. Which of the following shows the sign of each of the charges R, Q and S?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$Positive$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$Negative$\hspace{1.5em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| None | Q, R and S |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Q | R and S |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | S | Q and R |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Q, R and S | None |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Potential at a point is given by the sum of $\dfrac{kq}{r}$ where $r$ is the distance from each charge.

Along the central vertical line, P and Q are equidistant, as are R and S. The vertical line can only be equipotential if the charges P and Q are equal and opposite, and the charges R and S are equal and opposite. This gives a potential of zero along the central vertical line.

Similarly, the central horizontal equipotential line implies that P and R and Q and S are equal and opposite.

So, the only possible configuration involves P and S having equal and opposite charge to R and Q.

The answer is C.
