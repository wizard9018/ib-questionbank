# PH1047

The diagram below shows some equipotential lines around two charged objects A and B.

![image](images/image.png)
$\footnotesize{\textrm{[Copyright © 2012 - Matplotlib Development Team; All Rights Reserved]}}$

A has a charge of $-Q$.

Which of the following is correct about the magnitude and sign of charge B?

$\\$

| | Magnitude | Sign |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Less than $Q$ |$\hspace{1.5em}$Positive$\hspace{1.5em}$ |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Less than $Q$ | Negative |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Greater than $Q$ | Positive |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$Greater than $Q$ $\hspace{1.5em}$ | Negative |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Equipotential lines are perpendicular to electric field lines. The diagram suggests that we should have a field line connecting A and B. Therefore, they are of opposite charge. (For charges of the same sign we expect equipotential loops that enclose both charges.)

Magnitude of electric potential at a point is given by the sum of $\dfrac{kq}{r}$ at a point. From the diagram, we can see that an equipotential line at the midpoint between A and B will curve towards B so the magnitude of the charge on B must be greater. (A smaller change in distance from B is necessary to produce the same change in potential from B.)

Because we have determined that the charge on B is greater than Q and it is also positive, this corresponds to answer C.
