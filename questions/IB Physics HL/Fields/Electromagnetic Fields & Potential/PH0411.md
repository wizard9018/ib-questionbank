# PH0411

**Maximum mark: 8**

A non-conducting sphere with radius R carries a uniformly distributed positive charge with charge per unit volume $\rho$.

Point $P$ is at a distance $r$ from the center of the sphere.

![PH0411aaaa](images/PH0411aaaa.png)

The electric field outside this sphere is given by

- $E_{out}=\dfrac{\rho R^3}{3\varepsilon_0 r^2}$

While the electric field inside the sphere is given by

- $E_{in}=\dfrac{\rho r}{3\varepsilon_0}$

Where $\varepsilon_0$ is the free space permittivity.

The radius of the sphere is 2.0 cm. 

1. Calculate $\rho$ when the charge on the sphere is 0.1 $\mu \ce{C}$. [2 marks]

2. Determine the electric field at point $P$, when $r = 3$ cm. [2 marks]

3. In the space provided, sketch the variation of electric field $E$ with the distance from the center of the sphere $r$. [2 marks]

![Screenshot 2026-09-19 at 5.51.35 AM](<images/Screenshot 2026-09-19 at 5.51.35 AM.png>)

A positive charge is released from the surface of the sphere.

4. Describe the motion of this released charge. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. ::indent

    - $\rho=\dfrac{0.1\,\times \,10^{-6}}{(\frac{4}{3})(\pi)(0.02^3)}$ $\checkmark$

    

    - $\rho =$ :answer[$2.98 \times 10^{-3}\,\ll \ce{C\,m^{-3}}\gg$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    $\rho$ is the charge per unit volume,

    - $\rho=\dfrac{Q}{V}$

    The volume of a sphere is $\frac{4}{3} \pi R^3$

    - $\rho=\dfrac{Q}{\frac{4}{3} \pi R^3}=\dfrac{0.1\,\times\, 10^{-6}}{(\frac{4}{3})(\pi)(0.02^3)}=2.98\times 10^{-3}\, \ce{C\,m^{-3}}$

2. ::indent

    - $E=\dfrac{(2.98\,\times \,10^{-3})(0.02^3)}{(3)(8.85\,\times \,10^{-12})(0.03)^2}$ $\checkmark$

    - $E =$ :answer[$9.98\times 10^5\ll \ce{N\,C^{-1}}\gg$] $\checkmark$

    

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    When $r = 3$ cm, this means that point $P$ is outside the sphere, since $r$ > $R$.

    Therefore,

    - $E_{out}=\dfrac{\rho R^3}{3\varepsilon_0 r^2}$

    

    - $E=\dfrac{(2.98\,\times\, 10^{-3})(0.02^3)}{(3)(8.85\,\times\, 10^{-12})(0.03)^2}=9.98\,\times\, 10^5\, \ce{N\,C^{-1}}$

3. Line with positive gradient from the origin till $r$ = $R$. $\checkmark$

    Decreasing curve for $r$ > $R$. $\checkmark$

    ![Screenshot 2026-09-19 at 5.53.38 AM](<images/Screenshot 2026-09-19 at 5.53.38 AM.png>)

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    For $r \leq R$,

    - $E_{in}=\dfrac{\rho r}{3\varepsilon_0}$

    Electric field $E$ is directly proportional to $r$.

    The graph should be a line from the origin with a positive gradient.

    For $r > R$,

    - $E_{out}=\dfrac{\rho R^3}{3\varepsilon_0 r^2}$

    This is a reciprocal squared function, so the graph will be a decreasing curve since the electric field $E$ is inversely proportional to the square of $r$.

4. Accelerated motion away from the sphere $\checkmark$

    The acceleration decreases with time/distance away from the sphere $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    When a positive charge is released from the sphere, there will be an electrostatic force between the sphere and this charge, so the charge will accelerate away from the sphere.

    Since the force is inversely proportional to the square of the separating distance,

    - $F=k \dfrac{(q_1 q_2)}{r^2}$

    The force will decrease when the separating distance increases, accordingly the acceleration of the charge will decrease.

    Recall that $F=ma$
