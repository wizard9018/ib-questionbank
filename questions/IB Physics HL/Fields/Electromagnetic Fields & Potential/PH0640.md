# PH0640

**Maximum mark: 9**

An atom is being modeled with an electron assumed to be orbiting the nucleus in the first energy level with a speed of $2.18 \times 10^6$ m s$^{-1}$.

The orbital radius of the ground state, in m, is given by

- $r=(0.53\times{10}^{-10})n^2$

Where

- $n$ = order of the energy level.

1. Determine the charge of the nucleus. [3 marks]

2.
    1. The electron moves to the second energy level where it orbits with a kinetic energy of $5.44 \times 10^{-19}$ J.

        Explain whether the centripetal acceleration increases, decreases or stays the same. [3 marks]

    2. Calculate the orbital period of the electron in the second energy level. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The centripetal force is the electrostatic force between the electron and the nucleus:

    $\hspace{2em}$ $k\dfrac{eq}{r^2}=\dfrac{mv^2}{r}$ $\checkmark$

    $\hspace{2em}$ $q = \ \ll\dfrac{mv^2r}{ek}\gg =\dfrac{\left(9.11\times{10}^{-31}\right)\left(2.18\times{10}^6\right)^2\left(0.53\times{10}^{-10}\right)}{\left(1.6\times{10}^{-19}\right)\left(8.99\times{10}^9\right)}$ $\checkmark$

    $\hspace{2em}$ $q$  = :answer[$1.6\times{10}^{-19}\ \ll\ \ce{C}\gg$] $\checkmark$

2.
    1. The kinetic energy in level 2 (5.44 × 10⁻¹⁹ J) is less than in level 1 $\ll$so speed decreases$\gg$ $\checkmark$\
    The orbital radius increases $\ll$ as $r \propto n^2$ $\gg$ $\checkmark$\
    Centripetal acceleration $a = \dfrac{v^2}{r}$ decreases $\ll$since $v$ decreases and $r$ increases$\gg$ $\checkmark$

        (some justification using equations must be shown to receive full marks)

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The kinetic energy in the first energy level is

        - $E_K = \dfrac{1}{2}mv^2 = \dfrac{1}{2}(9.11 \times 10^{-31})(2.18 \times 10^6)^2 = 2.17 \times 10^{-18}$ J

        Since $5.44 \times 10^{-19}$ J $< 2.17 \times 10^{-18}$ J, the kinetic energy — and therefore the orbital speed — is smaller in the second energy level.

        The orbital radius is given by $r = (0.53 \times 10^{-10})n^2$, so the radius increases from level 1 to level 2.

        The centripetal acceleration is

        - $a = \dfrac{v^2}{r}$

        Since $v$ has decreased and $r$ has increased, the centripetal acceleration decreases.

    2.  ::indent
        - $r= \ \ll\left(0.53\times{10}^{-10}\right)\left(2\right)^2\gg \ =2.12\times{10}^{-10}\ll$ m $\gg$ $\checkmark$

        - $v=\ll\sqrt{\dfrac{2E_K}{m}}\gg \ = \ \ll\sqrt{\dfrac{\left(2\right)\left(5.44\times{10}^{-19}\right)}{\left(9.11\times{10}^{-31}\right)}}\gg \ = \ 1.09\times{10}^6\ \ll$ m s$^{-1}\gg$ $\checkmark$

        - $T=\ll\dfrac{2\pi r}{v}\gg \ = \ \ll\dfrac{\left(2\pi\right)\left(2.12\times{10}^{-10}\right)}{1.09\times{10}^6}\gg $ =:answer[$1.22\times{10}^{-15}\ \ll$ s $\gg$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        For the second energy level:

        - $r=\left(0.53\times{10}^{-10}\right)n^2$

        - $r=\left(0.53\times{10}^{-10}\right)\left(2\right)^2=2.12\times{10}^{-10}$ m

        And

        - $\begin{aligned}
        E_K&=\frac{1}{2}mv^2\\
        v&=\sqrt{\frac{2E_K}{m}}\\
        &=\sqrt{\frac{\left(2\right)\left(5.44\times{10}^{-19}\right)}{\left(9.11\times{10}^{-31}\right)}}\\
        &=1.09\times{10}^6\textrm{ m s}^{-1}
        \end{aligned}$

        To find the orbital period,

        - $v=\dfrac{2\pi r}{T}$

        - $T=\dfrac{2\pi r}{v}=\dfrac{\left(2\pi\right)\left(2.12\times{10}^{-10}\right)}{1.09\times{10}^6}=1.22\times{10}^{-15}$ s
