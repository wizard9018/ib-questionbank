# PH0862

The diagram below shows a series of equipotential lines in a gravitational field, along with the potentials of the lines. Points **X**, **Y** and **Z** lie on these equipotential lines.

![Screenshot 2026-04-06 at 7.57.53 AM](<images/Screenshot 2026-04-06 at 7.57.53 AM.png>)

Three statements about **X**, **Y** and **Z** are:

- I. $\hspace{1em}$ No work is done transporting a particle from point **X** to point **Y**
- II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Positive work is done by the field when a mass is transported from point **Z** to point **X**.
- III. $\hspace{.4em}$ A mass placed at point **Y** will accelerate towards **Z**.

Which of the statements are correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only
- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

**Statement I** is correct.\
Work done = change in potential energy $\Delta E_p=m\Delta V_g$. \
Change in potential $\Delta V_g=0$ so $\Delta E_p=0$ no work is done.

**Statement II** is incorrect.\
Gravitational potential is defined as having a value of 0 at infinity. A mass in a gravitational field will move from high to low potential $\text{\textendash}$ low meaning more negative. Positive work will be done by the field moving a mass from **X** to **Z**. Moving a particle from **Z** to **X** requires doing work against the field (so requires an external force), and the field does negative work against this external force.

**Statement III** is correct.\
The mass will accelerate from high to low potential. Force will be perpendicular to the equipotential line.
