## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The electric potential between the charges:

- $E_p=k\dfrac{q_1q_2}{r}$

The work done to assemble the system from an infinite separation is the sum of the electric potential energies between the charges. To achieve a higher electric potential energy, the charges should be closer together and have larger magnitudes. In option $B$, there are two charges of $2q$, and all the charges are positioned at the smallest separation compared to the other options.
