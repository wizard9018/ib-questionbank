# PH0067

Four identical negatively charged objects move in an electric field along paths shown as arrows below. Equipotential lines for the field are shown. Initially, all four objects have the same kinetic energy.

![PH0067](images/PH0067.png)

What is the relationship between the kinetic energies of the objects at the end of their paths?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I = IV > III > II

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I > IV = II > III

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ I = II = IV >III

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I > IV > III > II

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $\Delta E_p=q \Delta V$.

For I, III and IV the change in potential energy is negative because $\Delta V$ is positive. This energy is converted to kinetic energy, therefore kinetic energy increases.

For II the change in potential energy is positive because $\Delta V$ is negative, therefore kinetic energy decreases.

For I and IV, ∆V is equal so the increase in kinetic energy is the same, and this increase is larger than that for III.
