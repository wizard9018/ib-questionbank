# PH1006

**Maximum mark: 7**

1. State Newton\'s law of Gravitation. [2 marks]

2. A geostationary orbit is a special orbit in which satellites follow a circular path and remain above a fixed point on the Earth\'s surface. A satellite orbits in geostationary orbit at a constant speed of $v$ and orbital radius of $R$. The mass of the Earth is $M$.

    1. Show that $v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{R}}$ [2 marks]

    2. The radius of the satellite\'s orbit is 4.23 $\times 10^7\, \ce{m}$. Use this information to estimate mass of the Earth. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The attractive force is proportional to the product of masses $\checkmark$

    Inversely proportional to the square of the distance between masses $\checkmark$

    *Accept formula if all terms are defined*

2.
    1. Statement that centripetal force is equal to gravitational force $\checkmark$

        - $Fc\ =Fg$

        - $\dfrac{mv^2}{R}=\ \dfrac{GMm}{R^2}$

        With correct rearrangement to obtain result $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        When in orbit, the centripetal force on the satellite is provided by the gravitational force from the planet. To find the orbital speed, the two quantities can be set equal to one another and rearranged.

        - $\dfrac{mv^2}{R}=\ \dfrac{GMm}{R^2}$

        Giving

        - $v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{R}}$

    2. The period of the satellite must be 1 day $\ll$ to stay above a fixed point $\gg$ $\checkmark$

        - $v = \dfrac{circumference}{period}$ or $\dfrac{2\pi R}{T}$ or $\dfrac{2\ \pi\ (4.23\ \times\ {10}^7) }{(24)(60)(60)}$ $\checkmark$

        - $\ll M = \dfrac{v^2R}{G} = \dfrac{(3076)^2(4.23\times10^7)}{\ 6.67\ \times\ {10}^{-11}} \gg$

        - $6.00 \times {10}^{24} \ll \ce{kg} \gg $ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The period of the satellite\'s orbit must match that of the Earth\'s rotation, therefore the period is 24 hours.

        One day can be converted to seconds through $24 \times 60 \times 60$

        The speed of the orbit is

        - $Speed\ =\ \dfrac{distance}{time}=\dfrac{circumference}{period}=\dfrac{2\pi R}{T}$

        $\hspace{4.7em} =\dfrac{2\ \times\ \pi\ \times\ \ 4.23\ \times\ {10}^7\ }{24\ \times\ 60\ \times60}=3076\ \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$

        Rearranging the formula

        - $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{R}}$

        to solve for mass gives

        - $M = \dfrac{v^2R}{G} = \dfrac{{3076}^2\ \times\ {4.23\ \times10}^7}{\ 6.67\ \times\ {10}^{-11}} = 6.00 \times {10}^{24}\ \ce{kg}$
