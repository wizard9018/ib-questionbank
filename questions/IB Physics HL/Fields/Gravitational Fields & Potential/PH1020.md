# PH1020

Planet Y has a mass three times greater than planet X. The gravitational field strength on planet Y is six times greater than the gravitational field strength on planet X. If the density of planet X is $\rho$, what is the density of planet Y?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $2\sqrt2 \rho$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $3 \rho$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $6\sqrt2 \rho$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $12 \rho$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $g=\dfrac{GM}{r^2}$

- $g\ \propto\dfrac{M}{r^2}$

- $r\ \propto\ \sqrt{M/g}$

- $r\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{M}}=constant$

- $r_X\sqrt{\dfrac{g_X}{M_X}}=r_Y\sqrt{\dfrac{g_Y}{M_Y}}$

- $r_X\sqrt{\dfrac{g_X}{M_X}}=r_Y\sqrt{\dfrac{6g_X}{3M_X}}$

- $r_X\sqrt{\dfrac{g_X}{M_X}}=r_Y\sqrt{\dfrac{{2g}_X}{M_X}}$

- $r_X\sqrt{\dfrac{g_X}{M_X}}=r_Y\sqrt2\sqrt{\dfrac{g_X}{M_Y}}$

- $r_X=\sqrt2r_Y$

Now density $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$ and $V=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi\ r^3$ giving $V\ \propto\ r^3$ so we may write

- $\rho\ \propto\dfrac{m}{r^3}$

- $\dfrac{\rho r^3}{m}=constant$

- $\dfrac{\rho_Xr_X^3}{M_X}=\ \dfrac{\rho_Yr_Y^3}{M_Y}$

- $\dfrac{\rho_X\left(\sqrt2r_Y\right)^3}{M_X}=\dfrac{\rho_Yr_Y^3}{{3M}_X}$

- $\left(3\right)\left(2\sqrt2\right)\dfrac{\rho_Xr_Y^3}{M_X}=\dfrac{\rho_Yr_Y^3}{M_X}$

- $6\sqrt2\rho_X=\rho_Y$

Density of planet Y = $6\sqrt2\rho$
