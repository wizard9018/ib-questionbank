# PH0388

Planet $X$ has half the mass and twice the diameter of planet $Y$.
The escape speed from the surface of planet $X$ is given by $v$. What is the escape speed from the surface of planet $Y$  ?

![PH0388](images/PH0388.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}    \dfrac{v}{2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}    \dfrac{v}{\sqrt2}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}     v\sqrt2$

- D. $\hspace{1em}     2v$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
escape speed from X $=\sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{r}}=v$

$\begin{aligned}
\textrm{escape speed from Y   }&=\sqrt{\dfrac{(2G)(2M)}{\frac{r}{2}}}\\
&=v\sqrt4\\
&=2v\\
\end{aligned}$

 therefore the answer is D
