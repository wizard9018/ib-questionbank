# PH1809

Planet $A$ has a surface escape speed $v$ and a mass $m$. Planet $B$ of the same radius has a surface escape speed $1.2v$. What is the mass of planet $B$ in terms of $m$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{m}{1.2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{1.2}m$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $1.2m$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $1.44m$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Escape speed from the surface of a planet $v_{esc}$ is given by:

- $v_{esc}=\sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{r}}$

Where $M$ is the mass of the planet, $G$ is the gravitational constant and $r$ is the radius of the planet.

Here $r$ is constant so we have:

- $v_{esc}\propto\sqrt{m}$

- $\dfrac{v_B}{v_A}=\sqrt{\dfrac{m_B}{m_A}}$

- $\dfrac{1.2v}{v}=\sqrt{\dfrac{m_B}{m}}$

- $1.2=\sqrt{\dfrac{m_B}{m}}$

- $1.44=\dfrac{m_B}{m}$

- $m_B=1.44m$
