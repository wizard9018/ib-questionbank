# PH0676

Spaceship **A** orbits Earth with orbital speed $v_A$ and orbital radius $r$. Spaceship **B** orbits planet **X**  with the same orbital radius r. The mass of planet **X** is twice the mass of the Earth.

What is the orbital speed of spaceship B?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{v_A}{2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{v_A}{\sqrt2}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt2v_A$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $2v_A$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Spaceship **A** orbits Earth, therefore the centripetal force is due to the gravitational field strength of the Earth at its location.

- $\dfrac{mv^2}{r}=G\dfrac{m_1m_2}{r^2}$

The mass of the spaceship can be divided out of both sides of the equation giving an equation containing the mass of the Earth

- $\dfrac{v^2}{r}=G\dfrac{M_E}{r^2}$
- $v^2=G\dfrac{M_E}{r}$

- $v=\sqrt{G\dfrac{M_E}{r}}$

The mass of the planet **X** is twice that of the Earth.

Then

- ${v_B}=\sqrt{G\dfrac{(2M_E)}{r}}$

- ${v_B}=\sqrt2 \sqrt{G\dfrac{M_E}{r}}$

-  $v_B=\sqrt2v$
