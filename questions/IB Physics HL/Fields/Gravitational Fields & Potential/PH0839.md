# PH0839

Some data is collected regarding the orbital period $T$ and orbital radius $r$ of some planets orbiting a nearby star of mass $M$.

Which of the following shows a correct graph relating $T$ and $r$ that will have a gradient of $\dfrac{GM}{4\pi ^2}$ ?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Gravitational force is equal to centripetal force so

- $\dfrac{GmM}{r^2}=m{\omega}^2 r$

Where $m$ is the mass of the planet.

Cancelling $m$ gives:

- $\dfrac{GM}{r^2} = {\omega}^2 r$

$\omega$ can be written in terms of the time period $\omega = \dfrac{2\pi}{T}$, substituting this into the equation above gives:

- $\dfrac{GM}{r^2} = \Bigl(\dfrac{2\pi}{T}\Bigr)^2 r$

- $\dfrac{GM}{r^2} = \dfrac{4\pi ^2 r}{T^2}$

- $r^3=\dfrac{GM}{4\pi ^2} T^2$

Comparing this to $y=mx$ tells us that a graph of $r^3$ ($y$ axis) plotted against $T^2$ ($x$ axis) will have a gradient of $\dfrac{GM}{4\pi ^2}$.
