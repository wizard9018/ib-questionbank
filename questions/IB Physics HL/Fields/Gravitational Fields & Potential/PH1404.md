# PH1404

A satellite of mass $m$ orbits a planet of mass $M$ at a distance $r$ from the centre of the planet and is positioned at point $P$.

![Screenshot 2024-11-28 at 12.33.55 PM](<images/Screenshot 2024-11-28 at 12.33.55 PM.JPEG>)

Given that the gravitational potential at $P$ is $V$, what is the change in gravitational potential when the satellite is raised in its orbit by an additional height $2r$ to $Q$?

- A. $-\dfrac{2}{3}V$

- B. $-\dfrac{1}{3}V$

- C. $\dfrac{1}{3}V$

- D. $\dfrac{2}{3}V$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

At $r$, $V(r)=-G\dfrac{M}{r}=V$

At $3r$, $V(3r)=-G\dfrac{M}{3r}=\dfrac{1}{3}(-G\dfrac{M}{r})=\dfrac{1}{3}V$

The change in gravitational potential, $\Delta V=\dfrac{V}{3}-V=V(\dfrac{1}{3}-1)=-\dfrac{2}{3}V$
