# PH0384

A satellite is in an orbit around a planet of radius $R$. The graph shows the variation of different energy quantities against r, which is the distance from the center of the planet. The graph origin represents the surface of the planet.

![PH0384](images/PH0384.png)

Which one of the following statements may be correct about the energies of this satellite?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | A shows the variation of kinetic energy of the satellite. |
  | II. | B shows the variation of the total energy of the satellite.  |
  | III. | C shows the variation of the sum of kinetic and potential energies of the satellite |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Only I

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

I: A can only be kinetic energy as kinetic energy is always positive. The formula for kinetic energy of a satellite can be shown to be $E_K = \dfrac{GMm}{2r}$

II: B is the total energy as it has the same magnitude as the kinetic energy but is negative, and the sum of the value of graph A and graph C at all times. The equation for total energy is $E_{Total} = -\dfrac{GMm}{2r}$

III: C is the gravitational potential energy as given in the data booklet ($E_p = -\dfrac{GMm}{r}$), therefore the statement is wrong
