# PH0069

A spaceship orbits a planet at a distance of $d$ from the center of the planet. Later the spaceship moves and settles into a new orbit of $\dfrac{d}{2}$. Which of the following describes the change in kinetic energy, potential energy, and total energy of the spaceship?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{2em}$Kinetic Energy$\hspace{2em}$ | $\hspace{2em}$Potential Energy$\hspace{2em}$ |$\hspace{2em}$Total Energy$\hspace{2em}$|
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Decrease | Increase | No Change|
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Increase| Decrease | No Change|
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Increase | Decrease |Decrease|
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Decrease | Increase |Decrease|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $v_{orbit}=\sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{r}}$

As radius of orbit decreases, the orbital velocity increases so the kinetic energy of the spaceship will also increase.

- $E_{potential}=-\dfrac{GMm}{r}\hspace{2em}$ and $\hspace{2em}E_{total}=-\dfrac{GMm}{2r}$

As radius of orbit decreases, the potential and total energy decreases.
