# PH0677

The diagram shows the gravitational field between two masses $m_1$ and $m_2$.

![PH0677zzzz](images/PH0677zzzz.png)

Which of the following is correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ The two masses are equal

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ The two masses repel each other

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $m_1<m_2$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $m_2<m_1$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The diagram shows position of zero gravitational field near $m_2$.

At that location, $r_1>r_2$

Then,

$\hspace{1em}$ $g_1=g_2$

$\hspace{1em}$ $G\dfrac{m_1}{{r_1}^2}=G\dfrac{m_2}{{r_2}^2}$

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{m_1}{m_2}=\dfrac{{r_1}^2}{{r_2}^2}$

This means that

$\hspace{1em}$ $m_2<m_1$
