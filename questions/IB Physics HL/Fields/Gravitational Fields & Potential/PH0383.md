# PH0383

The escape speed from the surface of a planet of mass $M$ and radius $r$ is given by $v_{esc}$.

What is the orbital speed of a satellite of mass $m$, at a height of $2r$ from the surface of the same planet?

- A. $\hspace{1em}    \dfrac{v_{esc}}{3}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{v_{esc}}{\sqrt6}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}    \dfrac{v_{esc}}{2}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}    {\sqrt6v}_{esc}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The escape speed from the surface of the planet is given by $v_{esc}=\sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{r}}$

At a height $2r$ from the surface, the distance from the center will be $3r$

Therefore the expression for the orbital speed is $\sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{3r}}$

To write this expression in terms of $v_{esc}$, the ratio is

$\dfrac{\sqrt{\frac{1}{3}}}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt6}$

Therefore the answer is B.
