# PH0982

Planet $Y$ has a mass three times greater than planet $X$. The gravitational field strength at the surface of planet $Y$ is six times greater than the gravitational field strength on planet $X$. The radius of planet $X$ is $r$. What is the radius of planet $Y$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{r}{2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{r}{\sqrt{2}}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $r$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $r\sqrt{2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

Knowing that

- $g = \dfrac{GM}{r^2} \hspace{3em}$ gives $\hspace{3em} g \propto \dfrac{M}{r^2}$

And solving for $r$ leads to

- $r \propto \sqrt{\dfrac{M}{g}} \hspace{3em}$ and $ \hspace{3em}r\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{M}} = constant$

Using $X$ and $Y$ to represent the different planets,

$\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1.5em} r_X\sqrt{\dfrac{g_X}{M_X}} &= r_Y\sqrt{\dfrac{g_Y}{M_Y}}\\
\hspace{1em}\\
r_X\sqrt{\dfrac{g_X}{M_X}} &= r_Y\sqrt{\dfrac{6g_X}{3M_X}}\\
\hspace{1em}\\
r_X\sqrt{\dfrac{g_X}{M_X}} &= r_Y\sqrt{\dfrac{2g_X}{M_X}}\\
\hspace{1em}\\
r_X\sqrt{\dfrac{g_X}{M_X}} &= r_Y\sqrt{2}\sqrt{\dfrac{g_X}{M_X}}\\
\hspace{1em}\\
r_X &= \sqrt{2} r_Y\\
\hspace{1em}\\
r_Y &= \dfrac{r_X}{\sqrt{2}}\\
\end{aligned}$
