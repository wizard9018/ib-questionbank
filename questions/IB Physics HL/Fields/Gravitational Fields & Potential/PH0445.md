# PH0445

The following statements are given about the forces acting on a satellite in orbit around a planet.

-  I. The gravitational force on the satellite is proportional to the mass of the planet and the mass of the satellite.
- II. The strength of the gravitational field is equal to the weight of the satellite.
- III. The centripetal force acting on the satellite is provided by the gravitational force between the planet and the satellite.

Which of these statements are correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Only I
- B. $\hspace{1em}$    Only III
- C. $\hspace{1em}$    I and III
- D. $\hspace{1em}$    I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
I is correct as the gravitational force is given by $F=\dfrac{GMm}{r^2}$

II is incorrect because gravitational field strength is the gravitational acceleration, which is not equivalent to the force.

III is correct because the gravitational force is the only force acting on the satellite and it provides the centripetal force.
