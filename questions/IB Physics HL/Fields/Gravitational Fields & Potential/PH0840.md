## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Kepler's second law states:

A line segment joining a planet and star that it orbits sweeps out equal areas in equal time intervals.

This is illustrated in B.

(Note that A illustrates Kepler's first law:
The orbit of every planet is an ellipse with the Sun at one focus.)
