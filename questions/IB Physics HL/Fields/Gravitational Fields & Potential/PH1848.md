# PH1848

Planet $X$ and planet $Y$ follow circular orbits around a star. Planet $Y$ is half the mass of planet $X$ and its orbital speed is 1.5 times the orbital speed of planet $X$.

What is the ratio $\dfrac{\textrm{Orbital radius of planet }Y}{\textrm{Orbital radius of planet }X}$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{2}{9}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{4}{9}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{3}{2}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{9}{4}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The orbital speed is given by

- $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{GM}{r}}$

Where $M$ is the mass of the star being orbited.

Solving for radius of orbit gives

- $r=\dfrac{GM}{v^2}$

Note that $M$ in the equation is the central mass, and so the mass of the orbiting planet has no effect on its orbital speed

- $\dfrac{\textrm{Orbital radius of planet }Y}{\textrm{Orbital radius of planet }X}=\dfrac{\frac{GM}{(v_y)^2}}{\frac{GM}{(v_x)^2}}=\dfrac{(v_x)^2}{(v_y)^2}=\left(\dfrac{v_x}{v_y}\right)^2=\left(\dfrac{1}{1.5}\right)^2=\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^2=\dfrac{4}{9}$
