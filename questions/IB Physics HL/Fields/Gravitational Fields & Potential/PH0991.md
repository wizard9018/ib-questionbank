# PH0991

A rock of mass $m$ has a weight $W$ on the surface of a planet of radius $r$. What is the minimum speed at which the rock must be projected off the surface of the planet for it to completely escape the planet's gravitational field?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{Wr}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{2Wr}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{\dfrac{Wr}{m}}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{\dfrac{2Wr}{m}}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Escape velocity $v_{esc}=\sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{r}}$ (this can be shown by equating potential energy of the surface of the planet to kinetic energy)

Surface potential on the planet $= V_g=-\dfrac{GM}{r}$

Surface potential is also given by $V_g=-g\Delta r=-\dfrac{F}{m}\Delta r=-\dfrac{Wr}{m}$

Where $g=\dfrac{W}{m}$ is the gravitational field strength on the planet

So we have $\dfrac{GM}{r}=\dfrac{Wr}{m}$

- $v_{esc}=\sqrt{\dfrac{2Wr}{m}}$
