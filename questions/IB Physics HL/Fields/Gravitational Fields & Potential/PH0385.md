# PH0385

A satellite is orbiting a planet at a height $h$ from the surface of the planet. The satellite settles on a lower orbit.

What is correct about the changes to its kinetic energy and the centripetal force acting on the satellite in its new orbit?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
At a level closer to the surface of the planet, the centripetal force increases. In a circular orbit, the centripetal force is provided by the gravitational force, which   is proportional to the inverse square of the distance from the center of the planet.

The kinetic energy in a closer orbit is larger since the centripetal force is larger. Making the speed (given as $v_{orbit} = \sqrt{(\dfrac{GM}{r})}$ in the data booklet) larger is necessary to maintain the orbit, which therefore increases the kinetic energy.
