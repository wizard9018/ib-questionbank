# PH0053

Planet $X$ has radius $R$ and density $\rho$. The gravitational field strength at the surface of Planet $X$ is $g$.

What is the ratio of the gravitational field strength at the surface of planet Y, which has radius 0.25$R$ and density 3$\rho$, to that at the surface of Planet X?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{3}{4}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 4
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 16
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 48

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $g=G\dfrac{M}{r^2}$

where

- $M = \rho V$ and $\ V=\ \dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$

- $g=G\bigg(\dfrac{4\rho \pi r^3 }{3r^2}\bigg)=\dfrac{4}{3}G\rho\pi r$

Which gives

- $g \propto \rho r$

Therefore,

$\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1.5em} \frac{g_y}{g_x} &=\ \frac{3\rho(0.25r)}{\rho r} \\
\hspace{1em}\\
g_y&= \frac{3}{4}g_x
\hspace{1em}\\
\end{aligned}$
