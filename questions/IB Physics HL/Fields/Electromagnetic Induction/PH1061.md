# PH1061

A coil of wire rotates in a uniform magnetic field with an angular frequency of $\omega$ and produces an induced emf, $\varepsilon$, as shown in the graph below:

![Screenshot 2026-02-04 at 12.53.12](<images/Screenshot 2026-02-04 at 12.53.12.JPEG>)

If the angular frequency is increased to $2\omega$, select from the table below, the new values of the maximum emf, ${\varepsilon}_{max}$, and the period, $T$.

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$ ${\varepsilon}_{max}$ $\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$ $T$ $\hspace{1.5em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| 2.5 V | 10 ms |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 2.5 V | 40 ms |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 10 V | 10 ms |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 10 V | 40 ms |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From Faraday's Law, $\varepsilon = -N\dfrac{\Delta \phi}{\Delta t}$, an increase in the rate of change of flux will increase the induced emf, $\varepsilon$.

Increasing the angular frequency from $\omega$ to $2\omega$, will double the rate of change of flux and, therefore, double the induced emf, $\varepsilon$.

Therefore, the maximum emf, ${\varepsilon}_{max}$, will double from 5 V to 10 V.

In one full rotation (period), the emf from the coil will have an output equivalent to one full period of a sine wave. Increasing the angular  frequency from $\omega$ to $2\omega$, will halve the period, $T$, i.e. from 20 ms to 10 ms.
