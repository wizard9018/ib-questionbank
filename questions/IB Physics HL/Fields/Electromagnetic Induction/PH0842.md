A permanent magnet is attached to a circular platform which rotates about its centre with a frequency $f$. A solenoid is placed next to the rotating platform so that there is an induced electromotive force in the solenoid.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Two different changes have been introduced.

We are first told that the number of turns in the solenoid is halved. According to Faraday's law of induction, $\left (\varepsilon =-N \dfrac{\Delta \Phi}{\Delta t} \right ) $ the induced emf is proportional to the number of turns in the solenoid, thus the induced emf should also be halved.

However, the frequency of rotation of the magnet is doubled which doubles the rate of change of magnetic flux. Doubling the frequency of rotation also doubles the induced emf.

Both changes cancel out so amplitude of the induced (AC) emf remains the same except that the frequency is doubled (period is halved).

Graph **B** matches this description.
