# PH0024

There are four conducting coils $K$, $L$, $M$, and $N$. Each loop moves from a region where the magnetic field is zero into a uniform magnetic field, inducing either a clockwise or counterclockwise current in the loops. The speed of each coil is given, and the dimensions can be seen from the grid.

![PH0024](images/PH0024.png)

Below are three statements about the instant when the loops enter the magnetic field.

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | The magnitude of induced emf in the coil $K$ equals the emf in coil $M$.  |
  | II. | The magnitude of induced emf in coil $M$ equals the emf induced in coil $N$. |
  | III. | The induced current in coil $K$ is in the opposite direction to the induced current in coil $L$. |

Which statements are correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

I.    Correct

The magnitude of induced emf in coil $K$ equals that induced in the coil $M$. \
emf = $Blv$   where $B$ is magnetic field strength, $l$ is the length of the leading edge and $v$ is the speed of the coil. $B$ is the same for both coils.
For coil $K$ induced emf is proportional to $2v\times6\left(squares\right)\ $
For coil $M$ induced emf is proportional to $2v\times6\left(squares\right)$ thus equal.

II.    Correct

The magnitude of induced current in coil $M$ equals the coil $N$. \
emf $= Blv$   where $B$ is magnetic field strength, $l$ is the length of the leading edge and $v$ is the speed of the coil. $B$ is the same for both coils
For coil $M$ the rate of change in area is $2v\times6\left(squares\right)\ $
For coil $N$ the rate of change in area is $3v\times4\left(squares\right)$ thus equal.

III.    Incorrect

According to Lenz' law, induced emf and so the induced current are in a direction to oppose the changes. As both coil  $K$ and coil $L$ move, the magnetic flux increases; that is, the induced current will be in the same direction to create a magnetic field in the same direction to oppose the change in flux.
