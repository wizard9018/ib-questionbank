# PH0028

A conducting rod is moving in a magnetic field which is in the direction of out of the page as shown. It is moved in the direction of Side C (opposite to side A) by a force $F$

![PH0028](images/PH0028.png)

What is the direction of the electric field established in the conducting rod?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ From Side A to Side C

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ From Side C to Side A

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ From Side B to Side D

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ From Side D to Side B

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

According to Fleming’s left hand rule, the electrons in the rod experience a force towards Side B at the top of the rod (or from the rule, positive charges would experience a force towards Side D at the bottom of the rod).  The flow of electrons (negative charges) towards the top end of the rod will stop when the electrons at that end are numerous enough to push new electrons back by electrostatic repulsion. That is, an electric field is established in the rod where the top is negative and the bottom is positive. The corresponding field is from the bottom to the top (Side D to Side B)
