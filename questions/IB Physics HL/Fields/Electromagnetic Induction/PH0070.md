# PH0070

A conducting rod with a length of $l$ is rotating around vertical axis with constant angular velocity $\omega$  in a region of uniform vertical magnetic field $B$.

![PH0070](images/PH0070.png)

What is the emf between the ends of the rod (O and X)?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  B\omega l$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  B\omega^2l^2$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  \dfrac{B\omega l^2}{2}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ zero

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $\varepsilon=Bvl$

and for different points of the rod,

- $v=\omega r $

 Where $r_{average}=\dfrac{l}{2}$

Therefore;

- $\varepsilon=\dfrac{B\omega l^2}{2}$
