# PH0022

A loop of area $5.0 \,\ce{cm^2}$ is placed in a uniform magnetic field of 10 mT. The loop rotates around the vertical axis with a period of 4.0 s.

![PH0022](images/PH0022.png)

What is the magnitude of the change in magnetic flux linkage after 2.0 s?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ 0

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $     $0.5\, \ce{\mu Wb}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $ 5.0\,\ce{\mu Wb}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $    10\, \ce{\mu Wb}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- ${\Phi}=BA\cos\theta$

Initially,

- $\mathrm{\Phi}=(10\times\ {10}^{-3})(5 \times {10}^{-4})\cos(0)=50\ \times{ 10}^{-7}$Wb

After 2 seconds,

- $\mathrm{\Phi}=(10 \times {10}^{-3})(5 \times {10}^{-4})\cos(180) = - 50 \times{10}^{-7}$Wb

Therefore,

- $\Delta Φ= (-50 - 50)\times10^{-7}=- 100\times10^{-7}$ Wb

- $\hspace{0.7cm} =10\,\ce{μWb}$ (negative sign can be disregarded here as the question is asking for magnitude)
