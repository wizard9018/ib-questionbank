# PH1823

Four conducting rods $P$, $Q$, $R$ and $S$, are shown in the diagram. The length of rods $P$ and $R$ is $l$. Rod $P$ moves the right with a speed $v_P$ while rod $R$ moves to the left with a speed $v_R$ where $v_P\lt v_R$. The magnetic flux density inside the loop is $B$.

![PH1823q](images/PH1823q.JPEG)

At the instant shown, the rods are separated by a distance $x$. What is the magnitude of the emf induced inside the loop at this instant?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $Bl(v_R-v_P)$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $Bl(v_R+v_P)$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $Bx(v_R-v_P)$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $Bx(v_R+v_P)$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The emf induced inside a straight-line conductor moving perpendicular to a magnetic field is given by:

- $\varepsilon=Blv$

$B$ is the magnetic flux density, $l$ is the length of the conductor, and $v$ is the speed of the conductor.

Both rods induce a clockwise current inside the loop because both are acting to decrease the flux through the loop. The total emf is given by:

- $\varepsilon=Blv_P+Blv_R=Bl(v_P+v_R)$

Alternatively, we can see $v_P+v_R$ as the relative speed between the rods.
