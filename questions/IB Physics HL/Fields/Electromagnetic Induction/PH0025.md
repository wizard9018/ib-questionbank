# PH0025

A bar magnet is aligned along the axis of a conducting loop. The magnet and the loop move with velocities as given in situations $K, L,$ and $M$. The distance between the bar and loop is the same in all scenarios.

![PH0025](images/PH0025.png)

Which is correct about the magnitudes of induced emf, $\varepsilon$, at the instant shown in each situation?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  \varepsilon_K=\varepsilon_L>\varepsilon_M$

- B. $\hspace{1em}      \varepsilon_L>\varepsilon_K=\varepsilon_M$

- C. $\hspace{1em}      \varepsilon_M>\varepsilon_K>\varepsilon_L$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  \varepsilon_K>\varepsilon_L>\varepsilon_M$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Induced emf is proportional with rate of change in magnetic flux.  As the distance between the bar magnet and loop magnetic flux changes and the rate of change increases as the relative velocity increases ($v_L>v_K=v_M$).

Therefore, $\varepsilon_L>\varepsilon_K=\varepsilon_M$
