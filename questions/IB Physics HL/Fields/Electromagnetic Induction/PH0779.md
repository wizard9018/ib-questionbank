# PH0779

A $\lq$C' shaped conductor lies in a magnetic field of magnetic flux density 40 mT. The conductor is stationary and perpendicular to the field.

A conducting rod of length 30 cm is slid horizontally along the $\lq$C' shaped conductor at a constant speed for 0.60 s. During this time an $emf$ of 3.0 mV is induced across the rod causing a current to flow counterclockwise around the loop.

![PH0779](images/PH0779.png)

Which row correctly identifies $d$, the displacement between the initial and final positions of the rod (shown as dashed lines) and the direction of motion of the rod?

$\\$

| | $d$ / cm | Direction |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| 15 |$\hspace{1.5em}$ To the right $\hspace{1.5em}$|
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 15 | To the left |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 25 | To the right |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 25 | To the left |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Rod is moving perpendicular to field so

-  $\varepsilon=Blv$

Rearranging gives

-  $v=\dfrac{\varepsilon}{Bl}=\dfrac{3\times{10}^{-3}}{(40\times{10}^{-3})(0.3)}=0.25\ \ce{m\,s^{-1}}$

The rod travels at a constant speed therefore

-  $d=vt=(0.25)(0.6)=0.15\ \ce{m}=15$ cm.

By the right hand rule a counter-clockwise current is causing an upward magnetic flux inside the loop.

Emf induced opposes the change that caused it (Lenz’s law) suggesting the moving rod caused a decrease in upward flux (due to external field) inside the loop.

Therefore the area of the loop must have decreased during the motion – the rod moved to the left.
