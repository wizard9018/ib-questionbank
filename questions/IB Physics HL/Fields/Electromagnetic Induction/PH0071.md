# PH0071

A conducting rod of length $L$ is moved in a region of uniform magnetic field $B$ to light the filament bulb with a resistance of $R$. The direction of the magnetic field is out of plane.

![PH0071](images/PH0071.png)

What is the maximum output power of the bulb?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  \dfrac{BvL}{R}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  \dfrac{B^2v^2L^2}{R}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  \dfrac{B^2v^2L^2}{R^2}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ zero

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $\varepsilon=BvL$

- power = $\dfrac{\varepsilon^2}{R}=\dfrac{\left(BvL\right)^2}{R} = \dfrac{B^2v^2L^2}{R}$
