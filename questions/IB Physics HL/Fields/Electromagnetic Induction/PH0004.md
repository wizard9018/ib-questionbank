# PH0004

A conductor coil is rotating around a fixed axis in a magnetic field. As a result, an alternating current is generated. The graph below shows the variation of output power with time.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

As the frequency of rotation of the coil is doubled, the rate of change of the magnetic flux will also double. This causes the maximum value of generated emf to double. Because the current is related to the emf through the equation

- $I = \dfrac{V}{R}$

The maximum current will also double.

Since $P_{max}=(V_{max}{)\ (I}_{max})$ the maximum power generated will be four times greater.

AND

As the frequency is doubled, the peak power is reached with double the frequency and so the period of one cycle will be halved.
