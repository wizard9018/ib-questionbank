A circular disc magnet and a conducting ring are located as shown.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $I_{ind}=\dfrac{{\varepsilon}_{induced}}{R}$,  (resistance formula)

- $\varepsilon_{induced}=\ -N \dfrac{\Delta \Phi}{ \Delta t} $   (Faraday's Law)

where $\Phi$ is maximum at $t=0$ and changes sinusoidally as it rotates.

Since rate of change in flux (slope of $\Phi$ vs $t$ graph) is zero at $t=0$ , the induced emf and the induced current are zero initially.

Another explanation is that at time $t = 0$, flux has reached a maximum and is about to decrease, resulting in a change in direction of induced current. Thus, current is zero at the start.

For one revolution of the magnet there is one period of
 sinusoidal induced current in the ring.
