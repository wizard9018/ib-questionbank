# PH1037

In a fission reactor, which of the following is correct for the main role of the control rods and the heat exchangers?

$\\$

| | Control Rods  | $\hspace{1.5em}$Heat Exchangers$\hspace{1.5em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| $\hspace{1.5em}$Absorb neutrons$\hspace{1.5em}$ |$\hspace{1.5em}$Transfer heat from moderator to boiler$\hspace{1.5em}$ |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Absorb neutrons | Transfer heat from boiler to moderator |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$Slow down neutrons$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Transfer heat from moderator to boiler |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Slow down neutrons | Transfer heat from boiler to moderator |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In a fission reactor, control rods regulate the rate of the nuclear chain reactions by absorbing neutrons while heat exchangers transfer the heat stored in the moderator (mostly water) to the boiler for generating electricity.
