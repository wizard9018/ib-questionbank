# PH1042

Which of the following, on its own, would be least likely to lead to a meltdown in a nuclear reactor?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Absence of heat exchanger

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Absence of moderator

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Absence of control rods

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Absence of coolant

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In the absence of a moderator, the chain reaction becomes difficult to sustain, leading to the reactor's shutdown without achieving a high temperature.
