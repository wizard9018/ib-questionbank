# PH1041

Which of the following is correct for neutron-induced fission?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Heavier nuclei split into smaller nuclei on its own

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Lighter nuclei combine into a heavier nucleus on its own

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Heavier nuclei split into smaller nuclei after absorption of a particle

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Lighter nuclei combine into a heavier nucleus after absorption of a particle

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Neutron-induced fission occurs when a heavy nucleus absorbs a neutron, triggering its disintegration into smaller fragments accompanied by the release of a tremendous amount of energy.
