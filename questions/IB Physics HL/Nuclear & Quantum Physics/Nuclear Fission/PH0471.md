# PH0471

**Maximum mark: 10**

Uranium-235 ($\ce{_{92}^{235}U}$) has a nuclear mass of $235.044u$.

1. 1. State what is meant by mass defect. [1 mark]

    2. Calculate the mass defect of uranium-235. [2 marks]

2.
    1. Calculate the binding energy per nucleon of uranium-235. [2 marks]

    2. On the axes given, sketch the variation of the binding energy per nucleon with the nucleon number. Label the position of uranium-235. There is no need to add numbers to the axes. [3 marks]

3. Determine which nuclide has a higher binding energy per nucleon. Use the graph to justify your answer.  [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1. The mass of a nucleus is always less than the total mass of its constituent nucleons. The difference in mass is called the mass defect. $\checkmark$

    2. ::indent
        - $\delta=\ll Zm_p+\left(A-Z\right)m_n-M_{nucleus}\gg$

        - $\delta=(92)(1.007276)+(143)(1.008665)-235.044$ $\checkmark$

        - $\delta=$ :answer[1.86 $\ll u\gg$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$ \
Here the proton number (92) and the neutron number (143) are multiplied by their respective particle masses. The mass of the nucleus is then subtracted from this number.

        The non-rounded value is 1.8644487.

2.
    1. ::indent
        - $\ll \textrm{binding energy}=\delta\ c^2\gg$

        - binding energy $=(1.86)(931.5) =1737\ \ll\ \ce{MeV}\gg$ $\checkmark$

        - binding energy per nucleon $=\ll\dfrac{1737}{235}=\gg$ :answer[7.39 $\,\ll \ce{MeV}\gg$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$ \
        The binding energy will be the mass defect in amu multiplied by the mass-energy equivalence of an atomic mass unit.

        - binding energy $=(1.864487)(931.5)=1737$

        (Using the rounded value gives 1732.59)

        To find binding energy per nucleon we divide by 235.

        - binding energy per nucleon $=\dfrac{1737}{235}=7.39$ MeV

        (Using the rounded value gives 7.37 MeV)
