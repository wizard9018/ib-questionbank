# PH1036

Which of the following methods is suitable for the management of waste produced from fuel rods in a nuclear reactor?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Storing them in well-suited containers at ground level.   |
  | II. | Storing them in underground vaults such as disused mines. |
  | III. | Burying them in the ground. |

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Only I and II

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Only I and III

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Only II and III

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

High-level radioactive waste from nuclear reactors poses a unique environmental hazard due to its long-lasting radioactivity and potential to contaminate the food chain. Unlike low-level waste, it cannot be buried safely, requiring specialized disposal methods to prevent environmental harm.
