# PH0091

Which of the following correctly shows the change in total binding energy per nucleon in nuclear fission and nuclear fusion?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1em}$ Nuclear fission $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ Nuclear fusion$\hspace{1em}$  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Increases | Increases |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Increases | Decreases |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Decreases | Increases |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Decreases | Decreases |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Energy is released in both nuclear fission and nuclear fusion. Hence the total nuclear mass in both cases decreases, which corresponds to greater mass defect and greater binding energy per nucleon.
