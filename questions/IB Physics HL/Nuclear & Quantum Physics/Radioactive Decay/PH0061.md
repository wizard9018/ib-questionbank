# PH0061

In the absence of the strong nuclear force, a large nucleus would be even more unstable than a small nucleus. Which one of the following is the best explanation?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ A nucleus with fewer protons will more likely stay together due to the gravitational attraction between the nucleons.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ The more protons in the nucleus, the greater the repulsive force would be to make it unstable.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ A nucleus with fewer neutrons will more likely stay together due to the gravitational attraction between the nucleons.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ The more neutrons there are in the nucleus, the larger the separation of the protons which weakens the electrostatic repulsion between the protons.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

In the absence of the strong nuclear force there is no force to overcome the repulsive electrostatic force between protons. Therefore, more protons in a large nucleus results in more repulsive electrostatic force.
