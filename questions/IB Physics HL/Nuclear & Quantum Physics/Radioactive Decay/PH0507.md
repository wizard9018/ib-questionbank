# PH0507

Which of the following physical quantities has the same SI unit as the radioactive decay constant?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $Frequency

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $Time period

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $Wavelength

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $Wave speed

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The SI unit of radioactive decay constant is s$^{-1}$, which is the same as that of frequency.
