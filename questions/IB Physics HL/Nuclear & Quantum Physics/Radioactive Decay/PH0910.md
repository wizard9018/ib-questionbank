# PH0910

In a Rutherford scattering experiment, an aluminium foil is used instead of gold foil. The graph below shows the variation of intensity of alpha particles at fixed angle $I$ with the initial energy of alpha particle $E$.

![image](images/image.png)

The difference between predicted and observed scattering can be explained by

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Existence of a strong nuclear force

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Existence of electromagnetic force

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Existence of weak force

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Existence of gravitational force

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Aluminium nuclei have fewer positive charges than gold nuclei, which allows alpha particles to get closer to them due to the weaker electromagnetic repulsion. When alpha particles are close enough, the impact of the strong force causes the deviations from the predicted scattering that would occur due to electromagnetic forces alone. As a result, the intensity of alpha particles deflected at the expected angles drops dramatically.
