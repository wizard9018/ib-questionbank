# PH0911

Carbon-14 decays into nitrogen-14 by emitting a beta particle and an electron anti-neutrino. Which of the following is correct for the ratio of neutrons to protons in carbon-14 and the electromagnetic force between its protons?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$The ratio of neutrons to protons$\hspace{1.5em}$  | $\hspace{1.5em}$Electromagnetic force between protons$\hspace{1.5em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Greater than nitrogen-14 | Not balanced with strong nuclear force |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Greater than nitrogen-14 | Balanced with strong nuclear force |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Less than nitrogen-14 | Not balanced with strong nuclear force |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Less than nitrogen-14 | Balanced with strong nuclear force |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The decay can be represented by the following reaction:

- $\ce{^{14}_{6}C} \rarr \ce{^{14}_{7}N}+\ce{^{0}_{-1}e}+\overline{\nu}$

Carbon-14 has more neutrons than protons, while nitrogen-14 has an equal number of protons and neutrons. Therefore the neutron-proton ratio is larger in carbon-14. Since carbon-14 decays to nitrogen-14, it must be unstable. This is because the electromagnetic force between the protons in carbon-14 is not balanced by the strong nuclear force between the nucleons.
