# PH0916

Which of the following is the most accurate statement about the decay constant?

- A. $\hspace{1em} $ The decay constant equals the probability of decay in unit time regardless of the value of
$\lambda t$

- B. $\hspace{1em} $ The decay constant equals the probability of decay in unit time only in the limit of a sufficiently small value of $\lambda t$

- C. $\hspace{1em} $ The decay constant equals the probability of decay in unit time only in the limit of sufficiently large value of $\lambda t$

- D. $\hspace{1em} $ The decay constant is proportional to the half-life

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The decay constant is the probability of decay in unit time only in the limit of a sufficiently small value of $\lambda t$.

The rate of decay (activity) is proportional to the number of undecayed nuclei at a given time through the relationship $A = \lambda N$. If either the time interval or the decay constant is large, a significant proportion of nuclei could decay in the time interval.

Whereas the activity $\lambda N$  depends on the number of nuclei present at the start of the interval ($N$) for larger values of $\lambda t$ the value of $N$ will change during the time interval and make the assumption inaccurate.

Answer D is incorrect because the decay constant is inversely proportional to the half-life.
