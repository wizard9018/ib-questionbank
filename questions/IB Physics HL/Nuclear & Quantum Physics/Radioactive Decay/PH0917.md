# PH0917

A radioactive isotope has a decay constant of $\lambda$ and the initial number of nuclei is $N_0$. Determine the ratio of the number of decayed nuclei after 1 second to the initial number of nuclei, $\dfrac{N_{\textrm{decayed}}}{N_o}$

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $e^{-\lambda}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $e^{\lambda}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $1-e^{-\lambda}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $1-e^{\lambda}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

By the equation of radioactivity:

- $N=N_0 e^{-\lambda t}$

At $t=1$

- $N=N_0 e^{-\lambda}$

The number $N_d$ of decayed nuclei:

- $N_d=N_0-N_0 e^{-\lambda}=N_0 (1-e^{-\lambda})$

The ratio:

- $\dfrac{N_d}{N_0} =\dfrac{N_0 (1-e^{-\lambda})}{N_0} =1-e^{-\lambda}$
