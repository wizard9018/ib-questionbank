# PH0915

A pure sample of a nuclide has a decay constant of $\lambda$ and undergoes decay over time. The initial number of undecayed nuclides is $N_0$ and the number of undecayed nuclides is $N$ after some time. Which of the following graphs is the variation of the natural logarithm of $\dfrac{N_0}{N}$ in the sample with time $t$?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

By rearranging the equation of radioactivity:

$\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2em} N &= N_0 e^{-\lambda t}\\
\hspace{2em}\\
\dfrac{1}{e^{-\lambda t}} &= \dfrac{N_0}{N}\\
\hspace{2em}\\
e^{\lambda t} &= \dfrac{N_0}{N}\\
\hspace{2em}\\
\ln{e^{\lambda t}} &= \ln{\dfrac{N_0}{N}}\\
\hspace{2em}\\
\lambda t &= \ln{\dfrac{N_0}{N}}
\end{aligned}$

This relationship can be modeled as

- $y=mx$

Where $y$ is $\ln{\dfrac{N_0}{N}}$, $m$ is $\lambda$ and $x$ is $t$.

Therefore, the graph should be a straight line with a positive gradient.
