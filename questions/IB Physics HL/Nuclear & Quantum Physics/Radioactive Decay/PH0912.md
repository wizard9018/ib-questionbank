# PH0912

Consider the following statements regarding how the average binding energy per nucleon varies with nucleon number (A):

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | For A < 6, the binding energy per nucleon is greater than that for nuclides where A > 60   |
  | II. | For A > 120 a fission reaction will release energy|
  | III. | The binding energy per nucleon is approximately constant for A > 60 |

Which of the statements are correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em} $ Only I and II

- B. $\hspace{1em} $ Only I and III

- C. $\hspace{1em} $ Only II and III

- D. $\hspace{1em} $ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

![PH0912](images/PH0912.png)

According to the graph:

The binding energy per nucleon is lower for nucleon numbers below 6 compared to those exceeding 60.

In nuclear reactions, the products exhibit greater stability in terms of higher binding energy per nucleon when compared to the reactants. Consequently, for nucleon numbers exceeding 120, greater stability can be achieved in the products by reducing their nucleus size, which is achievable through nuclear splitting, leading to fission and the release of energy.

As can be seen on the graph, after increasing significantly for smaller nuclei, the binding energy per nucleon remains relatively constant when the nucleon number is approximately 60.
