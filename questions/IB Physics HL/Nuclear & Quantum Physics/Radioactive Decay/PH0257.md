# PH0257

Which of the following is correct for an electron neutrino?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ An electron neutrino has a continuous distribution of energies when produced in radioactive decay.

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ The charge of an electron neutrino is $1.6 \times 10^{-19}$ C.

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ The speed of an electron neutrino is $3.0 \times10^8 \,\ce{m} \,\ce{s}^{-1}$.

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Electron neutrinos are produced during alpha decays.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Neutrinos are chargeless particles. Although they have almost zero mass, their speed is less than the speed of light. They can be produced only in beta decays. Since beta particles can have a continuous distribution of energies in beta decays, electron neutrinos also have similar distribution due to energy conservation in the decay. Therefore, the answer is A.
