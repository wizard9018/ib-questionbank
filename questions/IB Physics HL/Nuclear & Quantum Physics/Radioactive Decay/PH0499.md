# PH0499

**Maximum mark: 12**

1. Define decay constant.        [1 mark]

2. Mushrooms contain a radioactive element called caesium-137, $\ce{^{137}_{55}Cs}$ . The element has a half-life of 30 yr and a sample of living mushrooms has a constant decay rate of 2000 Bq for every kg of mass. A sample of  50 g  is extracted from a dried  mushroom and the activity is measured as 0.80 Bq.

    1. Caesium-137 decays to barium-137,  $\ce{^{137}_{56}Ba}$, by beta emission. Complete the nuclear reaction equation of this decay, showing both particles emitted in the decay.

        $\ce{^{137}_{55}Cs}$ →  ______  + $\ce{^{137}_{56}Ba}$ +  ______  [2 marks]

    2. Show that the decay constant of caesium-137 is about 0.023 yr$^{-1}$. [2 marks]

    3. Given that the amount of caesium in the mushroom stops being replenished once the mushroom is no longer living, determine the age of the dried mushroom.        [3 marks]

3.  Some other radioactive substances have half-lives of thousands of years.   Outline the method used for determining the half-life of these kinds of substances.    [4 marks]

2.  1. $\ce{^{0}_{-1}\beta}$/$\ce{^{0}_{-1}e}$  $\checkmark$

        $\ce{^{0}_{0}\overline{\nu}}$   $\checkmark$  (zeroes need not be present for this marking point)

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    To balance the equation, beta minus should be emitted. The beta minus is accompanied by antineutrino to conserve lepton number during the reaction. The neutrino/antineutrino was originally postulated to account for an observed lack of conservation of energy and momentum in beta decays.

    2. Usage of $\lambda=\dfrac{\ln{2}}{T_\frac{1}{2}}$ $\checkmark$

        - $\lambda=\ll\dfrac{\ln{2}}{30}=\gg $:answer[0.0231 yr$^{-1}$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The half-life $T_\frac{1}{2}$ of any radioactive substance;

        - $T_\frac{1}{2}=\dfrac{\ln{2}}{\lambda}  \hspace{1em}$ where  $\hspace{1em}T_\frac{1}{2}$ = 30 years

        When it is rearranged;

        - $\lambda=\dfrac{\ln{2}}{T_\frac{1}{2}}=\dfrac{\ln{2}}{30}=0.0231\ \ce{yr}^{-1}$

    3. Activity of mushroom if it was living now $A_0=\ll(2000)(50 \times {10}^{-3})=\gg100\ \ce{Bq}$ $\checkmark$

        Usage of $A=A_0e^{-\lambda t}$, with $A =0.80\, \ce{Bq}$ and $A_0 = 100$ $\checkmark$

        - $t=\ln\Bigl(\dfrac{A_0}{A}\Bigr)\times\dfrac{1}{\lambda}=\dfrac{4.83\ }{0.023}=$:answer[$210\, \textrm{years}$ ] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        If it was a living mushroom, it would have an activity $A_0$;

        - $A_0=(2000)(50 \times {10}^{-3})=100\ \ce{Bq}$

        The equation of decay;

        - $A=A_0e^{-\lambda t}$  where $A=0.80\ \ce{Bq}, A_0=100\ \ce{Bq}$ and $\lambda=0.023\ \ce{yr}^{-1}$

        The equation can be rearranged as;

        $\begin{aligned}
        A&=A_0e^{-\lambda t}\\
        \hspace{1.3cm}\\
        0.8&=100e^{-0.023t}\\
        \hspace{1.3cm}\\
        0.008&=e^{-0.023t}\\
        \hspace{1.3cm}\\
        \ln(0.008) &= -0.023t\\
        \hspace{1.3cm}\\
        t  &= 210\, \textrm{years}\\
        \end{aligned}$

3.  Measurement of mass of the substance and calculation of number of nuclei from $N=\dfrac{\textrm{mass of the substance}}{\textrm{molar mass}}$ $\checkmark$

    Measurement of the activity of the substance $\ll$using a Geiger-Müller tube/counter$\gg$   $\checkmark$

    Calculation of decay constant from $\lambda=\dfrac{A}{N}$ $\checkmark$

    Calculation of half-life from ${\ T}_{1/2}=\dfrac{\ln{2}}{\lambda}$ $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    The number of nuclei $N$ inside the substance can be found by;

    - $N=\dfrac{\textrm{mass of the substance}}{\textrm{molar mass}}$

    Therefore, the mass of the substance should be measured initially. Then the activity $A$ of the substance should be measured. The decay constant $\lambda$ can be found by;

    - $\lambda=\dfrac{A}{N}$

    The half-life $T_\frac{1}{2}$ of the substance can be found by;

    - $T_\frac{1}{2}=\dfrac{\ln{2}}{\lambda}$
