# PH0918

A sample consists of $N_{0}$ undecayed nuclei of a nuclide with a decay constant of $\lambda$, where $\lambda$ is large. Estimate the activity of the sample after 1 s.

- A. $\hspace{1em} $ $\lambda N_{0}$

- B. $\hspace{1em} $ $\lambda N_{0}e{^{-\lambda}}$

- C. $\hspace{1em} $ $\lambda N_{0} (1 - e{^{-\lambda}})$

- D. $\hspace{1em} $ $\lambda N_{0} (1 - \lambda)$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Initial number of nuclei is $N_{0}$. After 1 s, the number of nuclei remaining is $N_{0}e{^{-\lambda}}$

Activity is given by $\lambda N$

Therefore the activity after 1 s $= \lambda N_{0}e{^{-\lambda}}$
