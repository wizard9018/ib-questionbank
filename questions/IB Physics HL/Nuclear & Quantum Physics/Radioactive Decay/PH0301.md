# PH0301

A nucleus $_{}^{A}\textrm{X}$ has a mass defect of $N$ unified atomic mass units, $Nu$. What is the correct expression for its binding energy per nucleon?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $931.5\frac{\ N}{A}$ MeV

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $931.5\frac{N}{A}$ MeV $c^{-2}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $931.5\frac{\ A}{N}$ MeV

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $931.5\frac{\ A}{N}$ MeV $c^{-2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
For one atomic mass unit ($u$), the mass defect is 931.5 MeV $c^{-2}$ \
Total mass defect = $Nu$ = 931.5 N MeV $c^{-2}$ \
This means the binding energy is 931.5 MeV

For binding energy per nucleon, dividing by nucleon number A gives $931.5\frac{\ N}{A}$ MeV. \
Note that the units of MeV $c^{-2}$ are mass units and not energy units.
