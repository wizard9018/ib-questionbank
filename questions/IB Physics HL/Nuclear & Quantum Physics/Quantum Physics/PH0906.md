# PH0906

A series of X-ray photons are scattered by a carbon target.

The following graphs show the variations of relative intensity with wavelength detected for four different scattering angles. The wavelength of the incident photon is $\lambda_{i}$ and of the scattered photon $\lambda_{f}$. Which graph represents detected intensities for the largest scattering angle?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The Compton shift is given by

- $\lambda_{f} - \lambda_{i} = \dfrac{h}{m_{e}c}(1 - \cos \theta)$

The difference between the final and initial wavelengths increases as the angle of scattering increases.

Graph D represents the greatest difference between final and initial wavelength.
