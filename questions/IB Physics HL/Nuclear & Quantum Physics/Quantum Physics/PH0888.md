# PH0888

A photon is scattered when hitting an electron.

Three statements are given about the scattering of photons:

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | This is evidence that light behaves as a particle  |
  | II. | The wavelength of the photon shortens |
  | III. | The total momentum of the photon and the electron is conserved |

Which of these statements is correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em} $ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em} $ I and II only

- C. $\hspace{1em} $ I and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em} $ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The collision of the photon with the electron causes the scattering of the photon obeying the law of conservation of momentum, which is evidence of the particle properties of light. This means statement I is correct.

When a photon hits an electron, it is scattered with a higher wavelength. This means that statement II is incorrect.

When the photon and electron collide, due to the Law of Conservation of Momentum their total momentum is conserved before and after collision. This means that statement III is correct.
