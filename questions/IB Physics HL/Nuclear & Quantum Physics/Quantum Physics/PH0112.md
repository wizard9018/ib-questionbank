# PH0112

Light from a sodium lamp of wavelength is directed towards a metal surface and electrons are emitted. A stopping potential of $V_s$ is applied, dropping the photocurrent to zero.

The wavelength of the incident light is then halved. Which of the following corresponds to the new value of the required stopping potential?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  \dfrac{V_s}{2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  \dfrac{V_s}{2} - \dfrac{\phi}{e}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  2V_s$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  2V_s+ \dfrac{\phi}{e}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\begin{aligned}
{eV}_s&=hf-\ \phi\\
\hspace{2.3cm}\\
V_{s1}&=\frac{h}{e}f-\ \frac{\phi}{e}\\
\hspace{2.3cm}\\
{2V}_{s1}&=\frac{2h}{e}f-\ \frac{2\phi}{e}\\
\end{aligned}$

For doubled frequency,

$\begin{aligned}
{E}_{max}&=2hf-\ \phi\\
\hspace{2.3cm}\\
{eV}_{s_2}&=2hf-\ \phi\\
\hspace{2.3cm}\\
V_{s_2}&=\frac{2h}{e}f-\ \frac{\phi}{e}\\
\hspace{2.3cm}\\
V_{s_2}&=\frac{2h}{e}f-\ \frac{2\phi}{e}+\
\frac{\phi}{e}\\
\hspace{2.3cm}\\
V_{s_2}&={2V}_{s_1}+\ \frac{\phi}{e}\\
\end{aligned}$
