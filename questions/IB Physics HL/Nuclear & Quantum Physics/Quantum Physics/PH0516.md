    2. Violet photons have higher frequency/energy than red photons. $\checkmark$

        Electrons are emitted when energy of photon > the work function. $\checkmark$

        Energy of violet > work function **AND** energy of red < work function $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Violet photons have higher frequency than red photons. Since $E=hf$, The violet photons will have higher energy as well.

       The photoelectric circuit will emit electrons when the frequency of the photons is greater than the critical frequency. In this case, the energy of a photon is greater than the work function $\phi$.

       The ammeter detects current when electrons are emitted from the photoelectric surface.

       This means that the violet photons have higher energy than the work function of the photoelectric surface, while red photons have lower energy than the work function of the photoelectric cell.
