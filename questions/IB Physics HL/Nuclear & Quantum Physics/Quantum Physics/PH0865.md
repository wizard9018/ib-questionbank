# PH0865

Below are three physical phenomena:

- I. $\hspace{1em}$ Electron diffraction
- II. $\hspace{.7em}$ The photoelectric effect
- III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Beta decay

Which of the physical phenomena combine to give evidence for wave-particle duality?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only
- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Electron diffraction shows how electrons (usually thought of as particles) can exhibit wavelike behavior (diffraction and interference).

The photoelectric effect shows how light (usually thought of as a wave) can exhibit particle-like behavior. Its energy is delivered in discrete packets.

Beta decay is a process by which a nucleus emits an electron or positron $\text{\textendash}$ it does not showcase any wave-particle duality.
