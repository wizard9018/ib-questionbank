# PH0887

An incident X-ray photon of wavelength $\lambda_i$  is scattered at 90$\degree$. The scattered photon wavelength is 1.1$\lambda_i$.

Which of the following gives an expression for the wavelength of the scattered photon?

- A. $\hspace{1em} $ $\dfrac{0.1h}{m{_e}c}$

- B. $\hspace{1em} $ $\dfrac{1.1h}{m{_e}c}$

- C. $\hspace{1em} $ $\dfrac{10h}{m{_e}c}$

- D. $\hspace{1em} $ $\dfrac{11h}{m{_e}c}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The difference between the incident and scattered wavelength of a photon is given by

- $\lambda_{f}-\lambda_{i}=\dfrac{h}{m_{e}c}(1-\cos \theta)$

Since

- $\lambda_{f}=1.1\lambda_{i}$

and

- $\theta$ = 90$\degree$

Then

- $1.1\lambda_{i}-\lambda_{i}=\dfrac{h}{m_{e}c}(1-0)$

- $0.1\lambda_{i}=\dfrac{h}{m_{e}c}$

- $\lambda_{i}=10\dfrac{h}{m_{e}c}$

Then

- $\lambda_{f}=(1.1)(10)\dfrac{h}{m_{e}c}$

- $\lambda_{f}=11\dfrac{h}{m_{e}c}$
