## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Electrons are released from the metal plate due to the photoelectric effect.

- $E_{max}=hf-\phi$

- $E_{max}=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}-\phi$

When the voltage in the circuit is zero the electrons (with maximum kinetic energy $E_{max}$) travel across the vacuum completing the circuit.

When the battery is set to a positive potential difference, it will be more difficult for the electrons to move across the evacuated tube as the potential difference will cause them to lose energy (decelerate).

If the voltage is set such that

- $E_{max}=\text{electric potential energy}=eV$

Then electrons will have zero kinetic energy by the time they reach the plate at the other side of the tube $-$ hence no current will flow.

- $eV=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}-\phi$

- $V=\dfrac{hc}{e}\Bigl(\dfrac{1}{\lambda}\Bigr)-\dfrac{\phi}{e}$

Graph of $V$ against $\dfrac{1}{\lambda}$ is a straight line with a positive gradient and a negative intercept on the vertical axis.
