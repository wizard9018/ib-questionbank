# PH0309

Two photosensitive plates A and B with threshold frequencies $f_A >f_B$ are illuminated in a vacuum with an electromagnetic radiation of wavelength λ such that photoelectrons are emitted from both plates. The corresponding stopping voltages are recorded to be $V_A$ and $V_B$ respectively.

The ratio $\dfrac{V_B-V_A}{f_A-f_B}$ is an estimate of:

- A. $\hspace{1em}  hc$

- B. $\hspace{1em}      \dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}      h$

- D. $\hspace{1em}      \dfrac{h}{e}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The photoelectric effect equation is given by

- $E_{max}=hf - \Phi$

The energy lost to the stopping voltage is equal to $E_{max}$. And the work function $\Phi$ is equal to the energy of the photons at the threshold frequency, $hf_t$. Therefore

- $eV_s=hf - hf_t$

Rearranging gives

- $hf=hf_t + eV_s $

For the first plate: $hf=hf_A+eV_A$.

For the second plate: $hf=hf_B+eV_B$.

Equating the two:

$\begin{aligned}
hf_A+eV_A&=hf_B+eV_B\\
\hspace{2.3cm}\\
e(V_A-V_B)&=h(f_B-f_A)\\
\hspace{2.3cm}\\
\frac{V_B-V_A}{f_A-f_B}&=\frac{h}{e}\\
\end{aligned}$
