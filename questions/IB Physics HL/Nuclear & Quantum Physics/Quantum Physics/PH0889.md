# PH0889

A photon is scattered when hitting an electron. The wavelengths of the incident and scattered photons are represented by $\lambda_{i}$ and $\lambda_{f}$ respectively.

Which of the following is an expression for the maximum energy that can be given to the electron, resulting in $\lambda_{f_{max}}$?

- A. $\hspace{1em} $ $\dfrac{h{^2}}{m_{e}\lambda_{i}\lambda_{f_{max}}}$

- B. $\hspace{1em} $ $\dfrac{2h{^2}}{m_{e}\lambda_{i}\lambda_{f_{max}}}$

- C. $\hspace{1em} $ $\dfrac{m_{e}c{^2}}{2}$

- D. $\hspace{1em} $ $\dfrac{2h{^2}}{m_{e}}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The energy given to the electron is the difference between the initial and final energies of the photon

- $E_{e_{max}} = E_{i}- E_{f}$

The maximum energy given to the electron is at the maximum Compton shift of the photon at 180$\degree$ and $\cos(180 \degree)=-1$

- $\Delta \lambda_{max} = \dfrac{h}{m_{e}c}(1 - \cos (180\degree))= \dfrac{2h}{m_{e}c}$

Then

- $E_{e_{max}} = E_{i}- E_{f_{min}}$

Since

- $E = \dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$

Then

- $E_{e_{max}} = \dfrac{hc}{\lambda_{i}} - \dfrac{hc}{\lambda_{f_{max}}} = \dfrac{hc(\lambda_{f_{max}} - \lambda_{i})}{\lambda_{i}\lambda_{f_{max}}} $

Since

- $\Delta \lambda_{max} = \lambda_{f_{max}} - \lambda_{i}$

Then

- $E_{e_{max}} = \dfrac{hc\Bigl(\dfrac{2h}{m_{e}c}\Bigr)}{\lambda_{i}\lambda_{f_{max}}} $

- $E_{e_{max}} = \dfrac{2h{^2}}{m_{e}\lambda_{i}\lambda_{f_{max}}} $
