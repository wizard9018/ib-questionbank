# PH0110

The graph below shows the maximum kinetic energy ($E_{max})$ of the emitted electrons against incident frequency ($f$) plotted by a student investigating the work function for a particular metal.

![Screenshot 2026-01-11 at 7.30.31 AM](<images/Screenshot 2026-01-11 at 7.30.31 AM.png>)

Which of the following can be used by the student to get an approximate value for the work function of the metal in question?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $Intercept X

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $Intercept W

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $Gradient at Y

- D. $\hspace{1em}      \dfrac{\textrm{Speed of Light     }}{\textrm{Intercept W     }}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The photoelectric equation is given by

- $E_{max}=hf-\ \Phi$

Where

- $\Phi $ = work function
- $h = $ Planck's constant
- $f$ = frequency

For a plot of $E_{max}$  against $f$, the gradient will be $h$ and the work function will be the negative of the intercept with the $E_{max}$ axis.
