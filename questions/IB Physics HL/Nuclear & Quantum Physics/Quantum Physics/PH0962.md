# PH0962

A beam of light is incident upon a metal surface and photoelectrons are emitted. The work function of the metal is $E$ and the energy of each photon is $3E$.

What is the ratio $\,\,\dfrac{\textrm{Maximum kinetic energy of a photoelectron}}{\textrm{Work function of the metal}}$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{1}{3}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{1}{2}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ 2

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ 3

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $E_{max} = hf - \phi$

Where $E_{max}$ is the maximum kinetic energy of the photoelectron,  $hf$ is the photon energy, $\phi$ is the work function energy.

Given that

- $hf=3E$ and $\phi=E$

Substituting into the equation gives

- $E_{max}=3E-E=2E$

Therefore

- $\dfrac{E_{max}}{\phi}=\dfrac{2E}{E}=2$
