# PH0333

Which statement describes a possible nuclear fusion reaction?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Hydrogen nuclei join together to form a helium nucleus.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ A helium nucleus joins with a hydrogen nucleus to form an alpha particle.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Uranium nuclei split and produce high energy neutrons causing a chain reaction.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Helium nuclei join to form hydrogen nuclei.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
A represents the best conditions for this, in that hydrogen nuclei are smaller than helium nuclei. \
B shows at least one reactant particle identical to the only product particle. This is not balanced. \
C shows a larger particle splitting into smaller ones. This is called fission. \
D is also unbalanced because 2 larger atoms would not combine to form a smaller one.
