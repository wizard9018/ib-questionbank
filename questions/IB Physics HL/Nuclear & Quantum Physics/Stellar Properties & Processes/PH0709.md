# PH0709

In the core of a star, a proton-proton chain reaction takes place.

$4\ce{^{1}_{1}H}\rightarrow\ce{^{4}_{2}He}+2\ce{^{0}_{+1}\beta}^{+} +2\nu_e+2\gamma$

After the initial stage of the reaction, two positrons annihilate with electrons present in the surroundings. Which of the following correctly shows the nature of the chain reaction and the total energy released due to annihilation?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$Nature of the chain reaction$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$Energy released$\hspace{1.5em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Fusion |$\left(2\right)\left(0.511\right)$ MeV |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Fusion| $\left(4\right)\left(0.511\right)$ MeV |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Fission | $\left(2\right)\left(0.511\right)$ MeV |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Fission | $\left(4\right)\left(0.511\right)$ MeV |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In the given reaction, four protons combine to form the nucleus of a helium atom. This process is known as fusion, where smaller nuclei merge to create a larger one. During the annihilation process, two positrons and two electrons interact and transform into gamma rays. Therefore, the energy they stored due to their masses is released. The energy released can be calculated by using the following formula:

- $E=mc^2$

Since the mass of the positron and electron are the same:

- $E=4m_ec^2=\left(4\right)\left(\dfrac{0.511\,\ce{MeV}}{c^2}\right)c^2=\left(4\right)\left(0.511\right)\,\ce{MeV}$
