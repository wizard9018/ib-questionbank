# PH1827

**Maximum mark: 7**

The star Betelgeuse is a red supergiant in the Orion constellation. Betelgeuse has a surface temperature of approximately 3500 K and a diameter of $1.2\times10^9$ km.

![PH1827q](images/PH1827q.JPEG)

1. Calculate the luminosity of Betelgeuse. [2 marks]

2. Betelgeuse will continue to fuse heavier elements together until iron is produced. Explain why iron production signifies the end of energy production in the star. [2 marks]

3. Explain the next stages in the evolution of Betelgeuse after the production of iron in the star's core. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. ::indent

    - $(1.2\times10^9$ km $ =1.2\times10^{12}$ m)
    - $A=4\pi r^2=4\pi\left(\dfrac{1.2\times10^{12}}{2}\right)^2=4.5\times10^{24}$ m$^2$ $\checkmark$

    - $L=\sigma AT^4=(5.67\times10^{-8})(4.5\times10^{24})(3500)^4= $ :answer[$3.8\times10^{31}$ W] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    To find the luminosity of the star, we use the Stefan-Boltzmann law:

    - $L=\sigma AT^4$

    The diameter of Betelgeuse is:

    - $d=1.2\times10^9$ km $ =1.2\times10^{12}$ m

    Now, we can calculate the surface area, $A$, by dividing the diameter by two in order to find the radius:

    - $A=4\pi r^2=4\pi\left(\dfrac{1.2\times10^{12}}{2}\right)^2=4.5\times10^{24}$ m$^2$

    Substituting the values into the equation for $L$:

    - $L=\sigma AT^4=(5.67\times10^{-8})(4.5\times10^{24})(3500)^4=3.8\times10^{31}$ W

2. Fusion of elements lighter than iron releases energy due to increase in binding energy per nucleon $\checkmark$

    Fusion of elements heavier than iron consumes energy due to decrease in binding energy per nucleon $\checkmark$

    OWTTE

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    Fusing elements lighter than iron together into heavier ones releases energy due to the resulting nucleus having a higher binding energy per nucleon. Elements heavier than iron have a lower binding energy per nucleon and so energy is required (no energy would be released) to fuse these heavier elements together. This is an unsustainable energy source for the star which is why iron production signifies the end of energy production.

3. Gravity causes core to collapse due to lack of outward pressure from fusion and so core temperature will rise $\checkmark$

    Outer layers collapse into the core and increase the temperature, causing a type-II supernova $\checkmark$

    Resulting in a neutron star or a black hole $\checkmark$

    OWTTE

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    Once iron is produced in the core and there is no nuclear fuel left, gravity will cause the core to collapse in on itself due to the lack of outward pressure from fusion. The temperature of the core will increase and allow for iron to fuse and consume more energy. The collapse causes protons to combine with electrons to produce neutrons. This process will continue until the core contains only neutrons. The collapse occurs so quickly that a gap is left between the core and outer layers. The outer layers fall into the gap and cause a rapid increase in temperature, resulting in a large explosion that blows everything away except the core — this is known as a type-II supernova. The result is either a neutron star or a black hole.
