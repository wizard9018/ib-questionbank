# PH0708-3

**Maximum mark: 7**

1. A star X is burning carbon in its core. The reaction is given by:

    - $\ce{^{12}_{6}C}+\ce{^{12}_{6}C}\rightarrow{_{12}^{24}}Mg  $

    The following data are available for the reaction.

    - Rest mass of the $\ce{^{12}_{6}C}                     =12.0111507 \,u$

    - Rest mass of the ${_{12}^{24}}Mg                    =23.9909630\, u$

    Determine, in MeV, the energy released in the reaction. Be sure to express your answer to the correct number of significant figures                            [3 marks]

2.  The star $Y$ is a red supergiant.  Describe the next stage in the evolutionary sequence of the star $Y$. [2 marks]

3. Outline why the Sun is not expected to undergo carbon fusion.                                     [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  ::indent
    - $\Delta m=(2)(12.0111507 u)-23.9909630 u=0.0313384 u$ $\checkmark$

    - $(0.0313384)(931.5)= $ :answer[$29.19\, MeV$] $\checkmark$

    - 4 s.f. on answer    $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    The mass defect $\Delta m$ in the reaction:

    - $\Delta m=(2)(12.0111507 u)-23.9909630 u=0.0313384 u$

    The energy $\Delta E$ is released from the reaction:

    - $\Delta E=\Delta mc^2=(0.0313384)(931.5)=29.19\, MeV$

    Because there are six significant figures on the mass defect and four significant figures on the mass to energy conversion, the final answer will have the lesser of the two, which results in four significant figures on the answer.

2.  It undergoes Supernova. $\checkmark$

    Becoming a neutron star or black hole. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    A red supergiant star undergoes a supernova explosion, leaving behind its core. Depending on the mass of the core, it can either form a neutron star or a black hole.

3.  To initiate carbon fusion, there is not enough pressure and temperature in the core $\checkmark$

    Due to having not enough mass $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    To have carbon fusion, there must be more pressure and temperature inside the core. To make it happen, the Sun should be more massive.
