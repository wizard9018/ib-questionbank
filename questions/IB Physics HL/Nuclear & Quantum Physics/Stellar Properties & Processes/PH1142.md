# PH1142

The Hertzsprung-Russell diagram shown below represents the surface temperature and luminosity for different stars.

![PH1142](images/PH1142.png)

One of the stars is labelled X. Which of the following best describes star X?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Red giant

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Red supergiant

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ White dwarf

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Main sequence star

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Star X is located within the group of stars with higher temperatures and low luminosities on the HR diagram.

These stars must then be relatively small so that their luminosity is low and are known as white dwarfs.
