# PH1140

Star M has a radius that is about 640 times that of the Sun and its surface temperature is about 3100 K.

Given that the surface temperature of the Sun is about 5800 K, which of the following best describes the luminosity of Star M?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $1700L_\odot$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $33000L_\odot$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $42000L_\odot$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $740000L_\odot$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

According to Stefan-Boltzmann law the luminosity is given by:

- $L=4\pi\sigma R^2T^4$

Using ratios we get:

- $\dfrac{L_M}{L_\odot}=\Bigl(\dfrac{R_M}{R_\odot}\Bigr)^2\Bigl(\dfrac{T_M}{T_\odot}\Bigr)^4$

- $\dfrac{L_M}{L_\odot}=(640)^2\Bigl(\dfrac{3100\,\ce{K}}{5800\,\ce{K}}\Bigr)^4\approx33426$

Leading to:

- $L_M\approx33000L_\odot$
