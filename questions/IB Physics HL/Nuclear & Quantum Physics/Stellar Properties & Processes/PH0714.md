# PH0714

The luminosity of the Sun is $L_\odot$. A main sequence star with twice the radius of the Sun and twice the temperature of the Sun has a luminosity of $L$. What is the ratio of $\dfrac{L}{L_\odot}$ ?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  4$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  16$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  32$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  64$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The luminosity of a star:

- $L=\sigma AT^4=\sigma 4 \pi r^2T^4$

Luminosity $L$ of a star is directly proportional to the product of the square of the radius and the fourth power of temperature.

For the Sun

- $L_\odot\propto{r_\odot}^2{T_\odot}^4$

For the star

- $L\propto\left(2r_\odot\right)^2\left(2T_\odot\right)^4=64{r_\odot}^2{T_\odot}^4$

- $\dfrac{L}{L_\odot}=\dfrac{64{r_\odot}^2{T_\odot}^4}{{r_\odot}^2{T_\odot}^4}=64$
