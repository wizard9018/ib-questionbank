# PH0128

Which value below gives the closest approximation for the diameter of a uranium-238 nucleus?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  10^{-16}$ m

- B. $\hspace{1em}  10^{-14}$ m

- C. $\hspace{1em}  10^{-12}$ m

- D. $\hspace{1em}  10^{-10}$ m

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The size of a nucleus is approximately 1 Fermi, which is equivalent to $10^{-15}$ m.

The radius of the a nucleus is given by  $R = R_0A^{\frac{1}{3}}$

Where $R_0$ is a constant equivalent to  $1.2 \times 10^{-15}$ m, and $A$ is the nucleon number.

Substituting 238 into $A^{\frac{1}{3}}$ gives

- $238^{\frac{1}{3}} \approx 6 $

- and $R_0  = 1.2\times 10^{-15}$ m

Therefore

- $R = (1.2\times 10^{-15})(6) = 7.2\times 10^{-15}$ m

Giving

- $D =2R =(2)(7.2\times 10^{-15}) = 1.4\times 10^{-14} \approx 10^{-14}$ m
