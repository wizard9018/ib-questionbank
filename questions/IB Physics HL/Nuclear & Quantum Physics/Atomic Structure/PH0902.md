# PH0902

An electron in a hydrogen atom falls from its first excited state to the lowest energy state.

Which of the following correctly describes the wavelength of the electromagnetic radiation emitted?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{1}{4} \left(\dfrac{13.6\,\text{eV}}{hc}\right)$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{3}{4} \left(\dfrac{13.6\,\text{eV}}{hc}\right)$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{4}{3} \left(\dfrac{hc}{13.6\,\text{eV}}\right)$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $4 \left(\dfrac{hc}{13.6\,\text{eV}}\right)$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The energy of an electron in the first excited state ($n=2$) is given by

- $-\dfrac{13.6}{2^2}\,\text{eV}=-\dfrac{1}{4}\,13.6\,\text{eV}$.

The fundamental energy state ($n=1$) is defined as having an energy of

- $-\dfrac{13.6}{1^2}\,\text{eV}=-13.6\,\text{eV}$.

The difference between these two states is then given by:

- $\Delta E = -13.6\,\text{eV} - \Bigl(-\dfrac{1}{4}\,13.6\,\text{eV}\Bigr)=-\dfrac{3}{4}\,(13.6\,\text{eV})$

This energy corresponds energy lost by the electron which is (in magnitude) equal to the energy of the emitted photon which will have a frequency $f$ given by

- $\Delta E = hf$

- $\Delta E = h\dfrac{c}{\lambda}$

This can be solved for $\lambda$ leading to:

- $\lambda = \dfrac{hc}{\Delta E} = \dfrac{hc}{\dfrac{3}{4}\,(13.6\,\text{eV})} = \dfrac{4}{3}\left(\dfrac{hc}{13.6\,\text{eV}}\right)$
