## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Density is defined as the ratio of mass to volume.

- $\rho_x=\dfrac{\text{mass}}{\text{vol}}$

The mass is given by the nucleon number $A_x$ times the average mass of each nucleon $m_n$, and the volume is given by $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi R^3$ where $R$ is the nuclear radius.

- $\rho_x=\dfrac{A_x m_n}{\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3}$

The radius can then be expressed as $R_0 A_x^{\frac{1}{3}}$.

- $\rho_x=\dfrac{A_x m_n}{\frac{4}{3}\pi \Bigl(R_0 (A_x)^{\frac{1}{3}}\Bigr)^3}=\dfrac{A_x m_n}{\frac{4}{3}\pi (R_0)^3 A_x}$

This expression can be simplified further into:

- $\rho_x=\dfrac{m_n}{\frac{4}{3}\pi (R_0)^3}$

which is independent of $A_x$. The graph of $\rho_x$ against $A_x$ should therefore be constant.
