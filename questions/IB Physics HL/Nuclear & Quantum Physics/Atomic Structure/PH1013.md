# PH1013

**Maximum mark: 5**

A gold nucleus has a nucleon number of 197.

1. Estimate the nuclear radius of gold. [2 marks]

2. Assuming that the nucleus is spherical, show that the density is the same for all nuclides. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. ::indent
    - $\ll R=R_0 A^{\frac{1}{3}}\gg$

    - $R=(1.2\times 10^{-15})(197^{\frac{1}{3}})$  $\checkmark$

    - $R=6.98\times 10^{-15} \ll \text{m}\gg$  $\checkmark$

2. ::indent
    - $\rho=\dfrac{Au}{\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3}$  $\checkmark$

    - $\rho =\dfrac{u}{\frac{4}{3}\pi (R_0)^3}$  $\checkmark$

    Density is independent on the nucleon number.  $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    - $\rho=\dfrac{m}{V}$

    - $\rho = \dfrac{Au}{\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3}$

    Where $A$ is the nucleon number and $u$ is the mass of the unified atomic mass unit.

    Since

    - $R=R_0 A^{\frac{1}{3}}$

    Then

    - $\rho = \dfrac{Au}{\frac{4}{3}\pi (R_0 A^{\frac{1}{3}})^3}=\dfrac{u}{\frac{4}{3}\pi (R_0)^3}$

    This means that the density of the nucleus is independent on the nucleon number and its value is the same for all nuclides.
