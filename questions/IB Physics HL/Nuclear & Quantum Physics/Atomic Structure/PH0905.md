# PH0905

The orbital radius of the electron in the first excited state ($n=2$) in the Bohr model of the hydrogen atom is 4 times greater than the orbital radius of the electron in the ground state ($n=1$).

Which of the following corresponds to the ratio

$\dfrac{\text{speed of the electron in the first excited state}}{\text{speed of the elecron in the ground state}}$

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{1}{16}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{1}{8}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{1}{4}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{1}{2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In Bohr's model for the hydrogen atom, the quantization of angular momentum is given by

- $mvr=\dfrac{nh}{2\pi}$

This expression can be written as

- $\dfrac{vr}{n} = \dfrac{h}{2\pi m}$

Given that the right-hand side of the previous equation is constant, the left-hand side should also be constant. This leads to:

- $\dfrac{v_{(n=2)}\,r_{(n=2)}}{2}=\dfrac{v_{(n=1)}\,r_{(n=1)}}{1}$

Which can be re-arranged into:

- $\dfrac{v_{(n=2)}}{v_{(n=1)}}=\dfrac{2\,r_{(n=1)}}{1\,r_{(n=2)}}=2\Bigl(\dfrac{1}{4}\Bigr)=\dfrac{1}{2}$

Therefore:

- $\dfrac{v_{(n=2)}}{v_{(n=1)}} = \dfrac{1}{2}$
