# PH0504

The nucleon numbers of elements X and Y are $A$ and $8A$ respectively. Their radii are $R_X $ and $R_Y$, and their densities are $\rho_X$ and $\rho_Y$.

Which of the following shows the correct values  of the ratios $\dfrac{R_Y}{R_X}$ and $\dfrac{\rho_Y}{\rho_X}$?

$\\$

| | $\dfrac{R_Y}{R_X}$ | $\dfrac{\rho_Y}{\rho_X}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| $\hspace{1.5em}$ 2 $\hspace{1.5em}$|$\hspace{1.5em}$ 1 $\hspace{1.5em}$|
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 8| 2 |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 2 | 2 |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 8 | 1 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Nuclear radius is proportional to the cube root of nucleon number:

- $R_X={R_0A}^\frac{1}{3}$.

Thus, when $A$ increases 8 fold, the radius is doubled:

- $R_{new}=R_0(8A)^\frac{1}{3}=2R_0A^\frac{1}{3}=2R$.

Nuclear density is a constant.

- Density $\rho=\dfrac{\text{Mass}}{\text{Volume}}=\dfrac{A}{\bigg(\dfrac{4\pi R^3}{3}\bigg)}=\dfrac{A}{\bigg(\dfrac{4\pi (R_0A^\frac{1}{3})^3}{3}\bigg)}=\dfrac{3}{4\pi (R_0)^3}.$

It is independent of nucleon number, radius and volume.
