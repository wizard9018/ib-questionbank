# PH0961

Consider a nucleus of tritium $\ce{^{3}_{1}H}$ and nucleus X, which has double the diameter of tritium. In nucleus X the number of protons equals the number of neutrons. The tritium nucleus experiences an acceleration of $a$ when placed in an electric field.

What is the acceleration of nucleus X when placed in the same electric field?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{1}{8}\,a$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{1}{2}\,a$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{2}{3}\,a$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{3}{2}\,a$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $R = R_0 \, A^{\frac{1}{3}}$

Where $R$ is the radius of the nucleus, $R_0$ is a constant and $A$ is the nucleon number. $2R=d$ so

- $d \propto A^{\frac{1}{3}}\\$

Therefore

- $\dfrac{d}{A^{\frac{1}{3}}} = \text{constant} \hspace{1em}$ and  $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{d^3}{A} = \text{constant}$

Giving

$\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1em} \dfrac{(d_{tritium})^3}{A_{tritium}} &= \dfrac{(d_X)^3}{A_X}\\
\hspace{1em}\\
\dfrac{d^3}{3} &= \dfrac{(2d)^3}{A_X}\\
\hspace{1em}\\
\dfrac{1}{3} &= \dfrac{8}{A_X}\\
\hspace{1em}\\
\dfrac{3}{1} &= \dfrac{A_X}{8}\\
\hspace{1em}\\
A_X &= 24\\
\end{aligned}$

Nucleon number of X is $24$ so X has a mass $\dfrac{24}{3}=8$ times greater than tritium.

X has $\dfrac{24}{2}=12$ protons $\text{\textemdash}$ X has a charge $12$ times greater than tritium.

- $F = Eq = ma$

Where $E$ is the strength of the electric field, $q$ is the charge of tritium and $m$ is the mass of tritium.

- $a = \dfrac{Eq}{m}$

Nucleus X has a charge $12q$ and a mass of $8m$.

Acceleration of nucleus X = $\dfrac{E(12q)}{8m} = \dfrac{3}{2} \Bigl(\dfrac{Eq}{m}\Bigr) = \dfrac{3}{2}a$
