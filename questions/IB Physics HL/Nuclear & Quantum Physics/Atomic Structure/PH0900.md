# PH0900

An alpha particle is moving with a speed $v_1$ along a path that leads towards a distant gold ($\ce{^{197}_{79}Au}$) nucleus.
The alpha particle reaches a distance of closest approach, $r_1$, to the nucleus before being repelled backwards along the same path.

If the experiment is repeated with the initial velocity being halved, which of the following correctly describes the distance of closest approach to the nucleus?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\sqrt{2}\,r_1$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $2r_1$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $2\sqrt{2}\,r_1$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $4r_1$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Given that the alpha particle is initially distant from the nucleus, the initial potential energy can be considered as zero.

At the distance of closest approach, the potential energy will be equal to the initial kinetic energy.

- $E_k$ (initial)$=E_p$ (at the point of closest approach)

- $\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2=\dfrac{kq_1q_2}{r}$

- $\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2=\dfrac{k(2e)(79e)}{r}$

Solving for $r$ leads to:

- $r=\dfrac{2k(2e)(79e)}{mv^2}$

This means that $r\propto\dfrac{1}{v^2}$  so if the initial speed is halved, the radius distance of closest approach is quadrupled.
