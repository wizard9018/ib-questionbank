# PH1072

An electron absorbs a photon and makes a transition between energy levels that have an energy difference of $E$. Then it relaxes to its initial state. Which is correct for absorbed and emitted photons during this transition?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$ Energy of Absorbed Photon $\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$ Energy of Emitted Photon $\hspace{1.5em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Greater than E | Equal to E |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Greater than E | Less than E |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Equal to E | Equal to E |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Equal to E | Less than E |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

An electron can absorb photon energy only when it matches the energy difference of the energy levels it transitions between. In the relaxation period, a photon is emitted with the same energy as the difference between the energy levels.
