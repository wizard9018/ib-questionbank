# PH0054

The diagram gives energy levels of a single-electron atom. 

![Screenshot 2026-01-06 at 6.02.39 PM](<images/Screenshot 2026-01-06 at 6.02.39 PM.png>)

How many different wavelengths can be detected in the emission spectrum of the atom?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 4

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 5

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 9

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 10

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

With five energy levels there are 10 possible transitions, but in this case the transitions from $n=5$ to $n=3$ and $n=3$ to $n=2$ are identical in energy difference (1.3 eV). Therefore, there are 9 different transitions with different wavelengths.
