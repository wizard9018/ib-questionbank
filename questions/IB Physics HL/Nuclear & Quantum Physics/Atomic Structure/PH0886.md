# PH0886

The diagram below shows three energy levels within a hydrogen atom. An electron in energy state $E_1$ makes a transition to one of the other energy states shown. This transition involves a photon of the lowest possible frequency.

![Screenshot 2026-01-05 at 12.31.21 PM](<images/Screenshot 2026-01-05 at 12.31.21 PM.png>)

Which row is correct about the photon associated with this transition?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$ Frequency of the photon $\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$ Photon absorbed/emitted $\hspace{1.5em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| $\dfrac{(E_2-E_1)}{h}$ |Absorbed |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\dfrac{(E_2-E_1)}{h}$ | Emitted |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\dfrac{(E_3-E_1)}{h}$ | Absorbed |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\dfrac{(E_3-E_1)}{h}$ | Emitted |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The energy of the photon is given by $E=hf $

$E \propto f$ so, the lowest frequency transition will be one with lowest energy difference involving $E_1$. This is the difference between $E_1$ and $E_2$. All energy states are negative and $E_1$ is the lowest. So $E_2-E_1$ gives a positive value.

- $f=\dfrac{(E_2-E_1)}{h}$

Electron jumps to a higher energy level, so photon is absorbed (energy of the photon is converted into electric potential energy of electron).
