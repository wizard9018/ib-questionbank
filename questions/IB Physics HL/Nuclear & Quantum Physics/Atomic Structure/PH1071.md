# PH1071

Which of the following is the evidence for discrete atomic energy levels?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Discrete energy of alpha emission from a nucleus.

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Discrete X-ray lines are produced by high-speed electrons striking a metal target.

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Emission spectra of gases.

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ The blackbody spectrum produced by incandescent bulbs.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The presence of discrete energy levels in alpha particles emitted from the nucleus is evidence of the quantized energy levels within the nucleus.

Discrete X-ray lines are generated, among other processes, by the sudden deceleration of electrons during collisions.

Incandescent bulbs produce a blackbody spectrum due to the thermal  radiation from heated filaments, covering a broad range of wavelengths.

Conversely, the emission spectrum of gases is a direct result of electrons transitioning between quantized energy levels within atoms. Therefore, C is the correct answer here.
