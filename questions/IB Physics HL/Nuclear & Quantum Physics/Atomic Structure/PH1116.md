# PH1116

Infra-red radiation is absorbed by a gas molecule, causing it to transition from energy level $E_1$ to energy level $E_2$. The absorptivity of the molecule is tested with infra-red radiation of energy $E_{in}$.

![Screenshot 2026-01-05 at 11.27.46 AM](<images/Screenshot 2026-01-05 at 11.27.46 AM.png>)

Which of the following conditions maximises absorption of the test radiation?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $E_{in}$ is equal to $E_2$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $E_{in}$ is equal to $E_2-E_1$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $E_{in}$ is much less than $E_2-E_1$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $E_{in}$ is much greater than $E_2-E_1$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The molecule absorbs infra-red radiation of energy that exactly matches the energy difference between levels $E_1$ and $E_2$.
