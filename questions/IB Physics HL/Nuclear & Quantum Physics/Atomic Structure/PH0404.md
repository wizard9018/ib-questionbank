# PH0404

Which of the following is **not** true about Bohr's model of the atom?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $It explains the stability of electrons within their energy levels.

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $It correctly calculates the energies of electrons in all atoms.

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $It assumes the quantisation of the energy of hydrogen atoms.

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $It assumes that the angular momentum of electrons is quantised.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

A: From his postulates, Bohr showed that the energy of electrons is quantised. Hence, this explained why they don't lose energy continuously. This explained why electrons don't spiral into the nucleus. So, A is correct.

B: Bohr's model works only for atoms and ions with single electrons. B is not true. Hence this is the incorrect statement and should be selected as the response for this question.

C and D: Based on the assumption that angular momentum of atomic electrons is quantised, Bohr's model also assumes that energies and orbital sizes are also quantised. So, C and D are correct also.
