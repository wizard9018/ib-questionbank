# PH1910

A motor with a power output of 6.0 W is used to lift a 2.0 kg object vertically at constant speed for 10 seconds. What is the best estimate, in metres, for the maximum vertical height the object can be raised in this time?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.3

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 1

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 3

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 10

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The power output is equal to work done per unit time:

- $P=\dfrac{\Delta W}{\Delta t}$

The work done lifting an object of mass
$m$ by a height $\Delta h$ is given by:

- $E_p=mg\Delta h$

Where $g$ is the acceleration due to gravity $\approx10$ m s$^{-2}$

Combining these equations and rearranging for height we have:

- $P=\dfrac{mg\Delta h}{\Delta t}$

- $\Delta h=\dfrac{P\Delta t}{mg}$

Substituting in our values we have:

- $\Delta h=\dfrac{(6)(10)}{(2)(10)}=3$ m
