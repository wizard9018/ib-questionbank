# PH0264

The graph shows the variation of length $l$ of a spring of original length $L_0$ as it is stretched to length $L$ by a variable force $F$.

![Screenshot 2025-05-27 at 4.11.33 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-05-27 at 4.11.33 PM.JPEG>)

The work done against the spring is represented by which of the following?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ The area marked X.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ The area marked Y.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ The area marked Z.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ The sum of the areas marked Y and Z

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Since $W = F s$, work can be found from areas on the graph. Normally, the graph of a spring being stretched has force on the vertical axis and extension or length on the horizontal axis and the work done is the area beneath the graph. Because the axes are switched, and the area must still be found between using the length axis as the base, we use the area to the left of the graph. Only the triangular area contained between the graph and the length axis from $L$ to $L_0$ represents the work done.
