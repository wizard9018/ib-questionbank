# PH0722

Which of the following postulates has the consequence of time and space not being absolute?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $Newton's laws of motion are the same in all reference frames

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $The relative velocity of two inertial frames of reference is constant

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $The laws of physics are the same in all inertial reference frames

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $The speed of light in a vacuum is constant in all inertial reference frames

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The speed of light is a fundamental constant according to the postulate in D. It does not change for the observers that move at different velocities. Consequences of this include time dilation and length contraction. This indicates there is no single universal reference frame in which time and space are absolute.
