# PH0666

**Maximum mark: 10**

1. In a thought experiment, a spacecraft recedes away from the Earth at a constant speed of  0.92$c$ in the reference frame of Earth. The length of the spacecraft is measured as 35 m by an observer in the spacecraft.

    ![Screenshot 2025-06-02 at 8.43.02 AM](<images/Screenshot 2025-06-02 at 8.43.02 AM.png>)

    1. State the differences between Galilean and special relativity with regards to the speed of light and time. [2 marks]

    2. Calculate the length of the spacecraft measured by an observer on Earth. [2 marks]

2. After some time, the spacecraft encounters a rocket that moves at a speed of 0.75$c$ relative to Earth in the opposite direction.

    1. Show that, relative to the spacecraft, the speed of the rocket according to the Galilean transformation is around 1.6$c$. [2 marks]

    2. Calculate, relative to the spacecraft, the speed of the rocket according to the Lorentz transformation. [2 marks]

    3. According to postulates of special relativity, outline which of your calculations in (b)(i) and (b)(ii) related to the speed of the rocket relative to the spacecraft is valid. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1. The speed of light is constant in special relativity $\checkmark$

        Time is relative in special relativity $\checkmark$

        *Accept reverse arguments*

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The speed of light is the same for all inertial reference frames in special relativity while it is not constant in Galilean relativity.

        Time is absolute in Galilean relativity but it is relative in special relativity.

    2. ::indent
        - $\gamma=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-\dfrac{v^2}{c^2}}}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-\dfrac{{(0.92)}^2c^2}{c^2}}}=2.55\ = 2.6$  $\checkmark$

        - $L=\dfrac{L_0}{\gamma}=\dfrac{35}{2.55}=$  :answer[14 m] $\checkmark$

        *Accept 13 m*

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The Lorentz factor $\gamma$ is

        - $\gamma=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-\dfrac{v^2}{c^2}}}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-\dfrac{{(0.92)}^2c^2}{c^2}}}=2.6$

        The proper length is the length measured by an observer in the spaceship, 35 m.

        Use the formula that links the proper length $L_0$, contracted length $L$ and Lorentz factor $\gamma$:

        - $L_0=\gamma\ L$

        - $L=\dfrac{L_0}{\gamma}=\dfrac{35}{2.55}=14$ m

2. 1. $\hspace{1.8em}$ $u^\prime=0.75c-(-0.92c)$ $\checkmark$

        - $\hspace{1em}$= :answer[1.67$c$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        - $u^\prime=u-v$

        Using the direction of the rocket as the positive direction, the spacecraft is moving in the opposite direction, so it is assigned a negative velocity

        - $u^\prime=0.75c-(-0.92c) $

        - $u^\prime=1.67\ c$

    2. $\hspace{1.8em}$ $u^\prime=\dfrac{-0.75c-0.92c}{1-\dfrac{(-0.75c)(0.92c)}{c^2}}$ $\checkmark$

        - $\hspace{1em}$= :answer[0.99$c$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The relationship between the relative speed of the rocket $u^\prime$, the speed of the rocket relative to the earth $u$, and the speed of the spacecraft $v$:

        - $u^\prime=\dfrac{u-v}{1-\dfrac{uv}{c^2}}$

        - $u^\prime=\dfrac{-0.75c-0.92c}{1-\dfrac{(-0.75c)(0.92c)}{c^2}}=0.99c$

    3. The calculation with Lorentz transformation $\checkmark$

        A consequence of the postulate that the speed of light is constant in all inertial frames of reference is that speed of the rocket cannot be greater than the speed of light $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        According to special relativity, the speed of light is the maximum possible speed. Therefore, the speed of the rocket cannot be bigger than the speed of light. Hence, the calculation with Lorentz transformation is more likely to be correct.
