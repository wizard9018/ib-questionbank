# PH0725

In a landing zone designed for spaceships that can move at relativistic speeds, a sequence of warning lights A, B, C, and D are used to guide landings. The spacetime diagram of this system shows the events of switching on the lights  in the landing zone reference frame $ct$ and the worldline $ct'$ of a spaceship approaching the landing zone.

![Screenshot 2025-03-22 at 2.17.26 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-03-22 at 2.17.26 PM.JPEG>)

Which of the following is the order of the lights reaching an observer on the spaceship from warning lights?

- A. $\hspace{1em}      A,\ D,\ C,\ B$

- B. $\hspace{1em}      D,\ C,\ B,\ A$

- C. $\hspace{1em}      B,\ C,\ A,\ D$

- D. $\hspace{1em}      A,\ C,\ B,\ D$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Photon worldlines should be added to the diagram to determine the order of the lights' arrival. These lines can be drawn from the position of each warning light as straight lines that are $45°$ to the axis.

![Screenshot 2025-03-22 at 2.26.02 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-03-22 at 2.26.02 PM.JPEG>)

The intersection of the worldlines of the photons with $ct^\prime$ shows the time of arrival of the light coming from related lights. Therefore, the order is $A, C, B, D$.
