# PH1878

The spacetime diagram shows the worldlines of rockets $A$ and $B$ in the reference frame of an observer $O$.

![Screenshot 2025-08-26 at 3.32.06 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-08-26 at 3.32.06 PM.png>)

What is the speed of rocket $B$ in the reference frame of rocket $A$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{2}{9}c$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{2}{3}c$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{3}{4}c$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{6}{7}c$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The angle $\theta$ of an object's worldline and the $ct$ axis in a frame $S$ is related to the speed of the object $v$ in the frame $S$ as follows:

- $\tan\theta=\dfrac{v}{c}$

Where $c$ is the speed of light.

We also know that:

- $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\textrm{opposite}}{\textrm{adjacent}}$

So, we can use the graph to determine the velocities of $A$ and $B$ relative to $O$.

Velocity of $A$:

- $\dfrac{v_A}{c}=\tan\theta_A=\dfrac{\textrm{opposite}}{\textrm{adjacent}}=\dfrac{-2}{4}=-\dfrac{1}{2}$

- $v_A=-\dfrac{c}{2}$

Velocity of B:

- $\dfrac{v_B}{c}=\tan\theta_B=\dfrac{\textrm{opposite}}{\textrm{adjacent}}=\dfrac{1}{4}$

- $v_B=\dfrac{c}{4}$

We must then use the relativistic velocity addition formula to find the velocity of $B$ in the reference frame of $A$:

- $u'=\dfrac{u-v}{1-\dfrac{uv}{c^2}}$

Substituting $u=v_B=\dfrac{c}{4}$ and $v=v_A=-\dfrac{c}{2}$:

- $u'=\dfrac{\dfrac{c}{4}-\left(-\dfrac{c}{2}\right)}{1-\dfrac{\left(\dfrac{c}{4}\right)\left(-\dfrac{c}{2}\right)}{{c^2}}}=\dfrac{\dfrac{c}{4}+\dfrac{c}{2}}{1+\dfrac{\left(\dfrac{c^2}{8}\right)}{c^2}}=\dfrac{\dfrac{3c}{4}}{1+\dfrac{1}{8}}=\dfrac{\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)}{\left(\dfrac{9}{8}\right)}c=\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)\left(\dfrac{8}{9}\right)c=\dfrac{2c}{3}$
