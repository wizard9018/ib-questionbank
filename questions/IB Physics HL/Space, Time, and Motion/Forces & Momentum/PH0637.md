# PH0637

A roller coaster car of mass $m$ approximates circular motion at point $A$ as shown in the diagram.

![PH0637](images/PH0637.png)

The circular radius of the track curvature at point $A$ is $r$ and the track exerts a normal reaction force $F_N$ on the car.

Which expression gives the linear speed of the car at point $A$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{2rg}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{\dfrac{r(F_N-mg)}{m}}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{\dfrac{r(mg-F_N)}{m}}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The centripetal force at point $A$ is the vector sum of the weight of the car and the normal reaction force of the track

$\hspace{2em}$ $F_{centripetal}=mg-F_N$

And

$\hspace{2em}$ $F_{centripetal}=\dfrac{mv^2}{r}$

Then

$\hspace{2em}$ $\dfrac{mv^2}{r}=mg-F_N$

$\hspace{2em}$ $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{r(mg-F_N)}{m}}$
