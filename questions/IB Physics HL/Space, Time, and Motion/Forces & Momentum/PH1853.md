# PH1853

Two objects with masses $3m$ and $2m$ are attached by an inextensible string with negligible mass. Both objects are placed on a frictonless incline at an angle $\theta$ above the horizontal.

![ph1853q](images/ph1853q.png)

A force parallel to the incline is pulling the system at constant speed up the incline.

Which of the following correctly describes the magnitude of the tension in the string connecting the objects?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $mg\sin\theta$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $2mg\sin\theta$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $3mg\sin\theta$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $5mg\sin\theta$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

To find the magnitude of the tension we must write Newton's 2$^{\textrm{nd}}$ Law for the object with a mass $3m$ along with an axis that is parallel to the incline.

Along this direction, the object is acted upon by the tension force and the component of the weight which is parallel to the incline.

![Screenshot 2025-09-06 at 5.27.37 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-09-06 at 5.27.37 PM.png>)

Given that the system moves at constant speed, the total force must be zero. Resolving the forces in the direction parallel to the incline gives

- $T-3mg\sin\theta=0$

Therefore, the magnitude of the tension on the string is

- $T=3mg\sin\theta$
