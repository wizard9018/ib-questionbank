# PH0146

A football player bounced the ball off their head while diving sideways, as shown:

![PH0146-1](images/PH0146-1.png)

Which of the following gives a Newton's Third Law pair of forces that applies to this situation?

$\\$

| | Force 1 | Force 2 |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Force of the ball on the player | Force of the ball on the earth |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Force of the player on the earth | Force of the ball on the player |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Force of the earth on the ball | Force of the ball on the earth |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Force of the earth on the player | Force of the player on the ball |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The only combination of answers which mentions the correct Newton's Third Law  force pair is C. In Newton's Third Law force pairs always occur between the same two objects.

In this case, because the ball and Earth are not in contact, it is the gravitational force that is equal and opposite here.
