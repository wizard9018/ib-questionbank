## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The area underneath the force-time graph gives the change in momentum of the box. Since the area increases with time at a constant rate, the momentum should increase at a constant rate. The only graph that satisfies this condition is the graph in A.

Therefore, the answer is A.

Alternatively, the impulse equation gives $\Delta p = F \Delta t$ .

As $F$ is a constant,  $\Delta p$ is directly proportional to $t$ and a straight line through the origin is expected.
