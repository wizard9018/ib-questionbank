# PH1030

A firework is dropped from a bridge and explodes a few seconds after release, splitting into three fragments. Which of the following vector diagrams could represent the momenta of the fragments just after the explosion?

|  | ![image](images/image.png)  |  |  |![image](images/image.png)|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The total momentum of the firework will increase as it falls towards the Earth, so that the firework will have downwards momentum before the explosion. Due to conservation of momentum, the sum of the momenta of the fragments should therefore also be downwards. In summing the momentum vectors, we are looking for a net momentum towards the Earth's surface.

![image](images/image.png)

A has a net momentum towards Earth.

B has a net momentum towards Earth and to the right – there cannot be net momentum in the horizontal direction as there is no external force pulling the firework horizontally.

C has net momentum away from Earth.

D has net momentum of zero.
