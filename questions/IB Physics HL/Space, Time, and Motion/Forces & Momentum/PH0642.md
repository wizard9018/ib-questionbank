A conical pendulum consists of a mass $m$ tied to a $22$ cm string that makes an angle $\theta$ with the vertical as shown in the diagram.

![PH06421a](images/PH06421a.png)

- Angle with the vertical $\theta = 20\degree$

1. Draw the free body diagram of the bob in the given position.            [2 marks]

2. 1. Show that angular velocity $\omega$ is given by
$\omega=\sqrt{\dfrac{g\tan\theta\ }{r}}$
[2 marks]

    2. Calculate the linear velocity of the bob. [2 marks]

    3. Determine the tension in the string.                    [2 marks]

3.  1. Explain the effect of increasing the angular speed ω on the angle θ.     [3 marks]

    2. The maximum tension the string can provide is $1.5$ N.

        Determine the maximum angular speed. [3 marks]

3. 1. The vertical component of the tension force remains constant, as it is equal to the weight of the bob. $\checkmark$

        The horizontal component of the tension provides the centripetal force, which increases as $\omega$ increases. $\checkmark$

        Because the horizontal component increases while the vertical component stays constant, the angle $\theta$ will increase with $\omega$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        ![Screenshot 2025-06-02 at 8.21.52 AM](<images/Screenshot 2025-06-02 at 8.21.52 AM.png>)

        - $T\cos\theta=mg$

        and

        - $T\sin\theta=F_c$

        Therefore,

        - $tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}=\dfrac{F_c}{mg}$

        Because $mg$ is constant and $F_c$ is increasing as ω increases,

        $\tan\theta$ and therefore $\theta$ increase as $\omega$ increases.

   2. $mg = (0.008)(9.8) = 0.08$ N

        0.08 N $\ll$ 1.5 N

        Therefore centripetal force is equal to the tension force/rope is almost horizontal/θ very close to $90\degree$. $\checkmark$

        - $\omega=\ll\sqrt{\dfrac{T}{mr}}\gg$

        - $\omega=\sqrt{\dfrac{1.5}{\left(8\times{10}^{-3}\right)\left(22\times{10}^{-2}\right)}}$ $\checkmark$

        - $\omega=$ :answer[29.2] $\ll\textrm{ rad s}^{-1}\gg$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Gravitational force is

        - $mg = (0.008)(9.8) = 0.08$ N

        The maximum tension is 1.5 N, which is the resultant of the vertical component of tension (mg) and the horizontal component of the tension, $F_c$.

        Therefore,

        - ${1.5}^2={0.08}^2+\ {F_c}^2$

        Solving gives

        - $F_c=\ 1.49787\ =\ 1.5$ N

        This shows that the centripetal force is effectively equal to the tension force and the string is very close to horizontal.

        ![Screenshot 2025-07-08 at 3.58.49 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-08 at 3.58.49 PM.png>)

        - $m\omega^2r=T$

        - $\omega=\sqrt{\dfrac{T}{mr}}$

        - $\omega=\sqrt{\dfrac{1.5}{(8\times{10}^{-3})(22\times{10}^{-2})}}=29.2$ rad s$^{-1}$
