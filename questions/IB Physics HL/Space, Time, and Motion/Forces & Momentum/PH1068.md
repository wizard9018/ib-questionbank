# PH1068

**Maximum mark: 7**

A solid sphere of mass $m$ is released from rest below the surface of oil and moves downwards.

1. Draw the free body diagram of the sphere at the instant it was released. [2 marks]

  ![Screenshot 2025-07-03 at 4.33.43 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-03 at 4.33.43 PM.png>)

2. 1. Show that the terminal speed of the sphere is given by:

        $v=\dfrac{2r^2 g(\rho_s-\rho_o)}{9\eta}$

        where $\rho_s$ is the density of the sphere and $\rho_o$ is the density of oil. [2 marks]

    2. Another sphere from the same material but with mass $8m$ is released in the oil. Determine the terminal velocity of the new sphere in terms of the terminal velocity of the smaller sphere, $v$. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Weight downwards and buoyant force upwards  $\checkmark$\
    Weight arrow is longer than the buoyant force arrow.  $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
    The sphere is accelerating downwards. This is because the gravitational force on the sphere is bigger than the buoyant force. Note that at the instant of release, there is no velocity and therefore no drag force.

    ![Screenshot 2025-06-03 at 4.44.43 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-06-03 at 4.44.43 PM.png>)

2. 1.  $mg=\rho_o Vg+6\pi \eta rv$  $\checkmark$ \
        Correct steps to reach $v=\dfrac{2r^2 g(\rho_s-\rho_o)}{9\eta}$  $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
        When the sphere reaches terminal speed, its weight is equal to the sum of the buoyant and drag forces.

        - $F_g=F_b+F_d$

        - $mg=\rho_o Vg+6\pi \eta rv$

        - $\rho_s Vg=\rho_o Vg+6\pi \eta rv$

        - $v=\dfrac{\rho_s Vg-\rho_o Vg}{6\pi \eta r}$

        - $v=\dfrac{Vg(\rho_s-\rho_o)}{6\pi \eta r}$

        - $v=\dfrac{\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 g(\rho_s-\rho_o)}{6\pi \eta r}$

        - $v=\dfrac{2r^2 g(\rho_s-\rho_o)}{9\eta}$

    2. $r_2=2r$  $\checkmark$\
        $v \propto r^2$  $\checkmark$\
        $v_2=4v$  $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
        Since the new sphere is from the same material as that in part (b) (i), then:

        - $\dfrac{m_2}{V_2} = \dfrac{m}{V}$

        - $\dfrac{8m}{V_2} = \dfrac{m}{V}$

        - $V_2=8V$

        - $(r_2)^3=8r^3$

        - $r_2=2r$

        From the relation in (b)(i)

        - $v \propto r^2$

        Then

        - $v_2=4v$
