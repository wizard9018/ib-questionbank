Two objects $A$ and $B$ of the same mass undergo an elastic collision. Which of the following shows the correct velocity-time graph for $A$ and $B$?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

During any collision between two objects, the rate of change of momentum of the objects $\dfrac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$ must be equal and opposite (Newton's third law).

$\Delta p=\Delta(mv)$
and $A$ and $B$ have the same mass, so in this case the rate of change of velocity of the objects must be equal and opposite. We can therefore rule out options $C$ and $D$ as the gradients of the graphs during the collision are not equal and opposite.

Kinetic energy is conserved in an elastic collision. In option $B$ both of the objects have less kinetic energy after the collision than before the collision, so the correct answer is option $A$.
