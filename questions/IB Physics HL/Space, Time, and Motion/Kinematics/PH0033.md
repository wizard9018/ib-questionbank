## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The cargo starts with large negative (downward) acceleration because the instant that it is released from the plane its acceleration is equal to the acceleration due to gravity.

 As the cargo container falls, it is speeding up; as it is speeding up, drag force increases, so the acceleration decreases until the cargo container reaches a terminal speed where acceleration is zero. After the parachute is opened, drag force will increase, which is greater than the weight of the cargo container, causing an opposite, upwards, acceleration (deceleration).

 As the cargo container slows down the drag force decreases, so the net force decreases, and acceleration decreases again until the cargo container reaches a lower terminal speed where acceleration is zero and the forces are balanced.
