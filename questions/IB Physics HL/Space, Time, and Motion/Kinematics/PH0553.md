# PH0553

**Maximum mark: 9**

The graph shows how the speed *v* of a car varies with time *t*.

![Screenshot 2025-07-03 at 5.18.15 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-03 at 5.18.15 PM.png>)

1. State the difference between average and instantaneous accelerations. [2 marks]

2. Calculate the instantaneous acceleration of the car at $t=1.0$ s. [2 marks]

3. Determine, with explanation, whether the car covers more distance before or after the car reaches a constant speed. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Average acceleration is the change in velocity over a period of time $\checkmark$

    Instantaneous acceleration is the acceleration at a particular instant/point in time $\checkmark$

2. Reference to the gradient of the graph = instantaneous acceleration $\checkmark$

    :answer[$7.9\pm0.5\ll\textrm{m s}^{-2}\gg$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    The gradient of the graph at the interested time gives the instantaneous acceleration of the car. To find the gradient, the tangent line should be drawn. To do this, we draw a line that represents the gradient of the curve at the exact moment when $t=1$ s. Note that this line needs to be estimated by eye, and the mark scheme will normally have an allowed range of values to account for different answers.

    ![Screenshot 2025-07-03 at 5.21.16 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-03 at 5.21.16 PM.png>)

    The gradient of the tangent line $= \dfrac{\textrm{rise}}{\textrm{run}}=\dfrac{26.0-11.0}{1.9-0}=7.9\textrm{ m s}^{-2}$

3. Time of reaching constant speed = 2.7 or 2.8 s $\checkmark$

    Any evidence of distance covered = area under the line $\checkmark$

    The distance covered at constant speed  $= \ \ll(25.0)(5.0-2.8)=\ \gg$ :answer[55]$\ \ll\textrm{m}\gg$ $\checkmark$

    *Accept 58 m*

    Any sensible estimate of the area to determine displacement before reaching a constant speed $\checkmark$

    After $\checkmark$

    Note that if plausible calculations are performed so tha the results are equal or that the distance moved before is larger, full marks will be awarded for a conclusion that matches the calculated result.

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    The car reaches the constant speed of $25.0$ m s$^{-1}$ at $t=2.8$ s. This is the point where the graph becomes horizontal. The area under the speed-time graph equals the car\'s distance travelled. The distance $d_1$ while moving at non-constant speed can be found by the method of small square approximation.

    The area of one small square =$(1.0\ \ce{m s}^{-1})(0.1\ \ce{s}) = 0.1\textrm{ m}$  and the number of small squares $= 524$

    Alternatively, a larger square has an area = $(5\ \ce{m s}^{-1})(0.5\ \ce{s})=$ 2.5 m. Counting roughly, there are about 22 larger squares

    $d_1=\left(524\right)\left(0.1\right)=52.4\ $m $\hspace{3em}$ or $d_1=\left(22\right)\left(2.5\right)=$ 55 m

    The distance $d_2$ at a constant speed \
$d_2=(25)(5.0-2.8)=55$ m

    $d_2$ > $d_1$  therefore, the car covers more distance after reaching the constant speed.

    Or the distances are equal. (suitable conclusion based on the calculation performed)
