# PH0552

**Maximum mark: 9**

A ball of mass $1.6$ kg is dropped from a very high building. The only forces acting on the ball are the force of gravity and air resistance.  The graph shows the variation of the vertical velocity $v$ of the ball with time $t$.

![Screenshot 2025-07-02 at 1.41.16 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-02 at 1.41.16 PM.png>)

1. 1. Define terminal speed. [1 mark]

    2. State the terminal speed of the ball.  [1 mark]

2.  Estimate the vertical displacement of the ball during the $10.0$ s of motion. [2 marks]

3.  1. Calculate the instantaneous acceleration of the ball at $t=2.0$ s. [2 marks]

    2. Determine the magnitude of the vertical component of the force of air resistance at $t=2.0$ s [3 marks]

1.  1.  The maximum speed/velocity  obtained by an object during  motion in a fluid/gas/air/liquid $\checkmark$

    2. :answer[18] $\ll\textrm{ m s}^{-1}\gg$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        According to the graph, the velocity of the ball increases and then stays constant at 18 m s$^{-1}$.

2. Any reference to the area under the graph being equal to the displacement. $\checkmark$

    :answer[$125\pm10$] $\ll\textrm{m, downwards}\gg$ $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    The area under the velocity-time graph is equal to the ball's displacement. The area can be calculated by using the small square approximation. The area $A_s$ of each small square:

    - $A_s=(0.2)(1)=0.2$ m

    There are approximately 625 small   squares. Therefore, the total area A or displacement:

    - $A=(625)(0.2)=125$ m

    Note that displacement is a vector quantity and so a direction should be stated. In this case, the ball is falling so the direction of the displacement is downwards.

        :answer[$2.8\ \pm\ 0.5$] $\ll\textrm{m s}^{-2}\gg $ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The gradient of the graph at a given time gives the instantaneous acceleration of the ball. To find the gradient, the tangent line should be drawn.

    2.  $mg-ma=$ air resistance $\checkmark$

        $(1.6)(9.81)-(1.6)(2.8)=$ air  resistance $\checkmark$

        :answer[11] $\ll\textrm{N}\gg$ $\checkmark$

        ECF for the use of the answer of (c) (i) to determine the acceleration

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The vertical resultant force $F_{net}$ acting on the mass:

        - $F_{net}$ = Weight - Air Resistance

        or

        - $F_{net} = F_g - F_d$

        and

        - $F_{net}$ = $ma$

        Therefore

        - $ma = F_g - F_d$

        - $F_d = mg - ma$

        - $F_d=(1.6)(9.81)-(1.6)(2.8)$

        - $F_d = 11.3$ N
