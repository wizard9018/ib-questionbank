## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1. Correct usage of Newton\'s 2nd law $\checkmark$

        - $T_1=\left(2.0\right)\left(2.3\right)+\left(2.0\right)\left(9.81\right)=$ :answer[24 N] $\checkmark$

        - $T_2=\left(4.0\right)\left(-2.3\right)+\left(4.0\right)\left(9.81\right)=$ :answer[30 N] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Two forces are acting on the mass of $2.0$ kg as tension $T_1$ and weight $W_1$. By using Newton\'s second law:

        - $T_1-W_1=ma$

        where $m$ is 2.0 kg and $a$ is $2.3$ m s$^{-2}$

        - $T_1-(2.0)(9.81)=(2.0)(2.3)$

        - $T_1=\left(2.0\right)\left(2.3\right)+\left(2.0\right)\left(9.81\right)=24.2$ N

        Since the two masses are connected, they should have the same magnitude of acceleration. There are two forces acting on the mass of $4.0$ kg as tension $T_3$ and weight $W_1$. By using Newton\'s second law and a sign convention where upwards is positive:

        - $T_2-W_1=m(-a)$

        where $m$ is $4.0$ kg

        - $T_2-(4.0)(9.81)=(4.0)(-2.3)$

        - $T_2=\left(4.0\right)\left(-2.3\right)+\left(4.0\right)\left(9.81\right)=30.0$ N

    2. ::indent
        - $\alpha=\dfrac{a}{r}=\dfrac{2.3}{0.25}$ $\checkmark$

        - $\alpha= $ :answer[$9.2$ rad s$^{-2}$] $\checkmark$\
        *An extra significant figure should be shown to obtain the second marking point.*

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The relationship between angular acceleration $\alpha$ and linear acceleration $a$:

        - $\alpha=\dfrac{a}{r}=\dfrac{2.3}{0.25}$

        - $\alpha=9.2{\ \text{rad}\,s}^{-2}$

    3. ::indent
        - $\omega_f=0+\left(9.2\right)\left(3.0\right)$ $\checkmark$

           $\hspace{1.5em}$ =  :answer[$28$ rad s$^{-1}$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The angular speed $\omega_f$ at an instant under the constant angular acceleration $\alpha$:

        - $\omega_f=\omega_i+\alpha\ t$ \
        where $\omega_i$ is the initial angular speed

        - $\omega_f=0+\left(9.2\right)\left(3.0\right)=27.6$ rad s$^{-1}$

    4.  ::indent
        - Torque $=\left(30-24\right)\left(0.25\right)=1.5$ N m $\checkmark$

        - $I=\dfrac{1.5}{9.2}$ $\checkmark$

        - $\hspace{1em} =$ :answer[$0.16$ kg m$^2$] $\checkmark$

        *Accept different answers due to error carried forward from (i)*

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The net torque  $\tau $ acting on the pulley:

        - $\tau  = (T_2 - T_1)r$

        - $\tau  = (30 - 24)(0.25) = 1.5$ N m

        The relationship between the torque and the moment of inertia I of the pulley:

        - $\tau  = I \alpha$

        - $I = \dfrac{\tau}{\alpha}  = \dfrac{1.5}{9.2} = 0.16$ kg m$^2$

    5. Non-zero torque/non-zero angular acceleration

        Not in rotational equilibrium $\checkmark$

        Zero resultant force/ zero acceleration ($\checkmark$) in translational equilibrium ($\checkmark$).

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Rotational equilibrium occurs when the resultant torque is zero or angular acceleration is zero. Due to the existence of non-zero resultant torque and non-zero rotational acceleration, the pulley is not in rotational equilibrium.

        Since the pulley is fixed in place, the upwards force supporting the pulley must be equal to the sum of the downwards forces from tensions in the strings. Since this is true, the acceleration of the pulley is zero and it is in translational equilibrium.
