# PH1891

A constant torque of 3.0 N m is applied to a system with a moment of inertia of 5.0 kg m$^2$. The system is initially at rest and the torque acts for 10 s.

What is the rotational kinetic energy gained by the system?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 6.0 J

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 12 J

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 90 J

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 180 J

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The change in angular momentum of the object is given by:

- $\Delta L=\tau\Delta t=(3.0)(10)=30$ kg m$^2$ s$^{-1}$

This is also the final angular momentum of the system as it started from rest.

The kinetic energy gained by the system is given by:

- $E_k=\dfrac{L^2}{2I}=\dfrac{30^2}{2(5)}=\dfrac{900}{10}=90$ J
