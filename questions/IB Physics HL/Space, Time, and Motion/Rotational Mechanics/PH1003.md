# PH1003

The tip of the seconds hand of a clock moves from the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$12$\text{\textquotedblright}$ marking to the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$6$\text{\textquotedblright}$ marking with a tangential speed of $2$ cm s$^{-1}$.

Which of the following is correct about the angular speed and the centripetal acceleration?

| |$\hspace{1em}$ Angular speed$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$Centripetal acceleration $\hspace{1em}$|
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| $\dfrac{\pi}{60} \textrm{ rad s}^{-1}$ | $\dfrac{\pi}{30} \textrm{ cm s}^{-2}$ |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$| $\dfrac{\pi}{60} \textrm{ rad s}^{-1}$ | $\dfrac{\pi}{15} \textrm{ cm s}^{-2}$ |
|$\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$| $\dfrac{\pi}{30} \textrm{ rad s}^{-1}$ | $\dfrac{\pi}{30} \textrm{ cm s}^{-2}$ |
|$\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$| $\dfrac{\pi}{30} \textrm{ rad s}^{-1}$ | $\dfrac{\pi}{15} \textrm{ cm s}^{-2}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The seconds hand of a clock makes a complete rotation in 60 s.

The angular speed $\omega=\dfrac{\Delta \theta }{\Delta t}$

- $\omega=\dfrac{2\pi}{T}=\dfrac{2\pi}{60}=\dfrac{\pi}{30}$ rad s$^{-1}$

To find the centripetal acceleration we need to calculate the radius of the circular path.

- $v=\omega\ r$

- $r=\dfrac{2}{\frac{\pi}{30}}=\dfrac{60}{\pi}$ cm

Then the centripetal acceleration

- $a=\dfrac{v^2}{r}$

- $a=\dfrac{4}{\frac{60}{\pi}}=\dfrac{\pi}{15}$ cm s$^{-2}$
