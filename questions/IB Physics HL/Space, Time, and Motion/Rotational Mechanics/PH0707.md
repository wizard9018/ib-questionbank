# PH0707

The graph shows the variation of torque on an object with time.

![Screenshot 2025-06-02 at 8.59.14 AM](<images/Screenshot 2025-06-02 at 8.59.14 AM.JPEG>)

The moment of inertia is $0.25$ kg m$^2$.

Assuming the object starts from rest, what is the final angular speed?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  8$ rad s$^{-1}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  12$ rad s$^{-1}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  20$ rad s$^{-1}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  80$ rad s$^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Given the equation

- $\Delta L=\tau \Delta t$

The area under the graph is the change in angular momentum

- $\Delta L=\dfrac{1}{2} (8+12)(2)=20$ N m s

The change in angular momentum is given by

- $\begin{aligned}
\ \Delta L&=\Delta (I\omega)\\
\ 20&=(0.25)\omega\\
\ \omega&=80\textrm{ rad s}^{-1}\\
\end{aligned}$
