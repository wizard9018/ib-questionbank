# PH1060

A uniform solid beam with a length of $2.00$ m and a weight of $800$ N is placed horizontally and hinged at a wall at point O. It is supported by string AB which is attached to the beam at A and to the wall at B.

![Screenshot 2025-06-03 at 4.33.06 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-06-03 at 4.33.06 PM.png>)

Which of the following correctly describes the magnitude of the tension on the string?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $200$ N

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $400$ N

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $600$ N

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $800$ N

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The beam is in equilibrium which means that the total torque (from any point) must be zero.

With respect to the hinge (O) there are two forces exerting a torque: weight and tension. These two torques must be balanced.

- ${\tau}_{weight}={\tau}_{tension}$

- (1.00 m)(800 N) = (2.00 m)(T$\sin{30\degree})$

- $T=\dfrac{(1.00\textrm{ m})(800\textrm{ N})}{(2.00\textrm{ m})(\sin{30\degree})}$

- $T=800$ N
