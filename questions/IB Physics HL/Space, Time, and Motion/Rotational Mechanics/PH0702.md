# PH0702

Two masses $M$ and $m$ are connected by a rod of negligible mass. They rotate around a vertical axis as shown in the diagram.

![PH0702](images/PH0702.png)

The ratio

- $\hspace{5em}\dfrac{\textrm{Distance\ between\ M\ and\ the\ axis}}{\textrm{Distance\ between\ m\ and\ the\ axis} }=\dfrac{1}{2}$

What is the moment of inertia?

- A. $\hspace{1em}      \dfrac{L^2 (M+m)}{4}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  \dfrac{L^2 (M+4m)}{9}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  L^2 (M+m)$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  L^2 (M+4m)$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Since

- $\dfrac{\textrm{Distance\ between\ M\ and\ the\ axis}}{\textrm{Distance\ between\ m\ and\ the\ axis} }=
\dfrac{1}{2}$

Then

- Distance between $M$ and the axis = $\dfrac{L}{3}$

and

- Distance between $m$ and the axis = $\dfrac{2L}{3}$

The moment of inertia is given by

- $I=\Sigma mr^2$

- $I=M\dfrac{L^2}{9} + m\dfrac{4L^2}{9}$

- $\dfrac{L^2(M+4m)}{9}$
