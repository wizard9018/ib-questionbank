# PH0615

**Maximum mark: 9**

In an amusement park, children enjoy a carousel ride that has a rotating platform and seats in the shape of horses on the platform. Some of the horses are closer to the centre of the platform. A top view of the ride is shown below.

![PH0615](images/PH0615.png)

After accelerating to a certain angular velocity, the torque of the motor that rotates the carousel is equal to the torques of the opposing forces.

1. The carousel makes 20 revolutions in $5.0$ min. Determine the angular speed of the carousel. [2 marks]

2.  1. State the law of conservation of angular momentum. [2 marks]

    2. During the ride, some children on the outer horses move to horses that are closer to the centre. Explain the effect this could have on the angular velocity of the carousel. [3 marks]

3. Another carousel is moving with an angular velocity of $0.035$ rad s$^{-1}$. In an instant, the magnitude of the rate of change of moment of inertia due to a child moving towards the centre is $24$ kg m$^2$ s$^{-1}$. Determine the net torque acting on the carousel during the motion of the child. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.   frequency of the carousel = $\dfrac{n}{t}=\dfrac{20}{(5.0)(60)}=0.067$ Hz $\checkmark$

        - $\omega=2\pi f=(2)(\pi)\left(0.067\right)$ = :answer[$0.42$ rad s$^{-1}$] $\checkmark$

        **OR**

        Period of the carousel = $\dfrac{t}{n}= \dfrac{(5.0\textrm{ min})(60\textrm{ s min}^{-1})}{20}=15$ s

        - $\omega=\dfrac{2\pi}{T}=\dfrac{2\pi}{15}=0.42$ rad s$^{-1}$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The relationship between frequency $f$ of the carousel, number $n$ of revolution and time $t$;

        - $f=\dfrac{n}{t}=\dfrac{20}{(5.0)(60)}=0.067$ Hz

        The angular velocity $\omega$;

        - $\omega=2\pi f=\left(2 \pi\right)\left(0.067\right)=0.42$ rad s$^{-1}$

2. 1. If the net torque acting on the system is zero/in the absence of external torques $\checkmark$

        The angular momentum of the system does not change $\checkmark$

    3. The moment of inertia decreases $\checkmark$

        angular momentum is conserved due to no net torque/isolated system $\checkmark$

        Angular velocity increases $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        The moment of inertia $I$ of the children is;

        - $I=\mathrm{\Sigma m}r^2$

        Since the distance between children and the centre of the carousel $r$ decreases, the moment of inertia $I$ decreases.

        There is no external net torque acting on the system. Therefore, the angular momentum $L$ of the system must be constant.

        - $L=I\omega$

        If the angular momentum is to remain constant, a decrease in moment of inertia must be compensated with an increase in angular velocity.

3. ::indent
    - $\dfrac{\omega \Delta I}{t} = (0.035)(24)$ $\checkmark$

    - = :answer[$0.84$ N m] $\checkmark$

    -

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
    The net torque $\tau$ acting on the carousel is equal to the rate of change of angular momentum

    - $\tau = \dfrac{\Delta L}{t} = \dfrac{\Delta (I\omega)}{t} = \omega \dfrac{\Delta I }{t} = (0.035)(24) = 0.84$ N m
