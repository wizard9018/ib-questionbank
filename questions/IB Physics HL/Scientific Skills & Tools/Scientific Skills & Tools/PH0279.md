# PH0279

A student models the relationship between velocity v of a moving charge in a magnetic field, and the force $F$ acting on it as $F = kv$, with $k$ being a constant

What are the units for $k$ expressed in fundamental SI units?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ m s

- B. $\hspace{1em} \ce{kg \,s^{-1}}$

- C. $\hspace{1em} \ce{kg \,s^{-2}}$

- D. $\hspace{1em} \ce{m \,s^{-2}}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The units for $k$ must be the unit of force divided by the unit of velocity, ($\dfrac{\ce{kg \,m \,s^{-2}} }{ \ce{m \,s^{-1}}}$ ) therefore  $\ce{kg \,s^{-1}}$
