# PH1757

Reynolds number, $Re$, is a dimensionless number (a value with no units) used in fluid mechanics to predict the nature of flow of a fluid. It is given by:

$Re=vl\rho^\alpha\eta^\beta$

where $v$ is the velocity of the flow, $l$ is length, $\rho$ is the density of the fluid and $\eta$ its viscosity. What are the exponents $\alpha$ and $\beta$?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$ $\alpha$ $\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$ $\beta$ $\hspace{1.5em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $-1$ | $-1$ |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $-1$ | 1 |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 1 | $-1$ |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 1 | 1 |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Using the given formula, we compare the powers of the quantities on both sides. By dimensional analysis, we ensure the equation is dimensionally consistent.

First, we need to find the units of all the quantities in the formula. Viscosity $\eta$ is a bit tricky so this will be determined first:

Viscosity $\eta$ appears in the equation of drag force $F_d$:

- $F_d=6\pi\eta rv$

Where $r$ is radius and $v$ is velocity.

The units for $F_d$ are N = kg m s$^{-2}$\
The units for $r$ are m\
The units for $v$ are m s$^{-1}$\
6 and $\pi$ are dimensionless quantities

Substituting units into the equation we have:

- $\hspace{0.1cm}$kg m s$^{-2}$ = \[units of $\eta$\]\[m\]\[m s$^{-1}$\]

- units of $\eta$ = $\dfrac{[\ce{kg}\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-2}]}{[\ce{m}][\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}]}$ = kg m$^{-1}$ s$^{-1}$

So now we have:

The units for $v$ are m s$^{-1}$\
The units for $l$ are m\
The units for $\rho$ are kg m$^{-3}$\
The units for $\eta$ are kg m$^{-1}$ s$^{-1}$\
$Re$ is dimensionless

Substituting this into our equation we have:

- $Re=vl\rho^\alpha\eta^\beta$

- $\hspace{0.27cm}$1 = \[m s$^{-1}$\]\[m\]\[kg m$^{-3}$\]$^\alpha$\[kg m$^{-1}$ s$^{-1}$\]$^\beta$

- $\hspace{0.27cm}$1 = m$^{(2-3\alpha-\beta)}$ s$^{(-1-\beta)}$ kg$^{(\alpha+\beta)}$

Each of the powers must be equal to zero to give a dimensionless result. Therefore, we have:

$\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2.3cm}\\2-3\alpha-\beta&=0 \hspace{2.3cm} (1)
\\-1-\beta&=0 \hspace{2.3cm} (2)
\\\alpha+\beta&=0 \hspace{2.3cm} (3)
\end{aligned}$

(1) $\implies\beta=-1$\
(2) $\implies-1+\alpha=0\implies\alpha=1$

Therefore, $\alpha=1$ and $\beta=-1$.
