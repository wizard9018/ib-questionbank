# PH0486

Which of the following is an estimate of the order of magnitude of the radius of the Earth?

- A. $\hspace{1em}10^{4}$ m

- B. $\hspace{1em}10^{7}$ m

- C. $\hspace{1em}10^{10}$ m

- D. $\hspace{1em}10^{13}$ m

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The radius of the earth is approximately 10 000 km, which is $10^7 \,$m. All other values are significantly different from this value.
