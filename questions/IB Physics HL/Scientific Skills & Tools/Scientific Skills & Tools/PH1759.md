# PH1759

Two forces A and B are acting on an object. The magnitude of force A is 3.0 N. Below shows a scale diagram representing forces A and B.

![PH1759](images/PH1759.JPEG)

What is the net force acting on the object?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ 3.0 N

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ 4.0 N

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ 5.0 N

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ 6.0 N

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The net force acting on the object is the vector sum of A and B shown below as F:

![PH1759MS](images/PH1759MS.JPEG)

Using a ruler to measure the sizes of A and F. We see that the length of F is double the length of A. Therefore, the magnitude of F is:

- $F=2(3)=6$ N.

Alternatively, we can see that vector A has components of 2 units in one direction and one unit in the other. Vector F is made of components of 4 units in one direction and two in the other, and therefore is twice the size of vector A.
