# PH0849

The absolute uncertainty in the radius $r$ of a sphere is $\Delta r$.

What is the fractional uncertainty in the volume of the sphere?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\left ( \dfrac{\Delta r}{r} \right )^3$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $3 \dfrac{\Delta r}{r}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{4}{3} \pi \left( \dfrac{\Delta r}{r} \right)^3$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $4 \pi \dfrac{\Delta r}{r}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The formula for the volume of a sphere is

$\hspace{1em}$ $V=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$

The rule for propagating uncertainty is that the fractional uncertainties are added when quantities are multiplied together.

In this case, the fractional uncertainty on $r$ will add together three times:

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{\Delta r}{r} + \dfrac{\Delta r}{r} + \dfrac{\Delta r}{r} = 3\dfrac{\Delta r}{r}$
