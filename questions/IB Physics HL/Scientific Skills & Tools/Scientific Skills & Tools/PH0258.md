# PH0258

In finding the area of a circular metal plate, a student measures the radius $R$ of the plate and calculates the area to be $A \pm a$, where $a$ is the absolute uncertainty on the area. Which of the following would give the fractional uncertainty on $R$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{\dfrac{a}{A}}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{a}{2A}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{a}{2\pi A}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{\dfrac{a}{2}}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Assuming the measured radius was $R ± r$, 

Because $A = \pi r^2$

The fractional uncertainty on $A$ will be twice the fractional uncertainty on $r$, 

Thus

$\dfrac{a}{A} = 2(\dfrac{r}{R})$

Giving the absolute uncertainty on $A$ to be $a = 2(\dfrac{r}{R})A$

The question is asking for the fractional uncertainty on $R$ which is ($\dfrac{r}{R}$)

Solving the equation above gives the answer $\dfrac{r}{R} = \dfrac{a}{2A}$
