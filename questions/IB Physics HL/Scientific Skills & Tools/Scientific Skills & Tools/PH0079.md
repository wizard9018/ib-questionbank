# PH0079

The volume of a cylinder is calculated from the product of its base area and height. The measurement of the height has an uncertainty of 5% and the uncertainty of the diameter is 1%. What is the uncertainty of the volume of the cylinder?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 1%

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 5%

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 6%

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 7%

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The volume of a cylinder is calculated by Base Area $\times$ Height

$V=AH=\dfrac{\pi}{4}(D)(D)(H)$

The uncertainty of volume can be calculated as the following, where the relative uncertainties of each term are added when quantities are multiplied together

$\cfrac{\Delta V}{V}=\cfrac{\Delta D}{D}+\cfrac{\Delta D}{D}+\cfrac{\Delta H}{H}$

Hence the percentage of the volume uncertainty is just the sum of the percentage uncertainty of base area and height.

$1\%+1\%+5\%=7\%$.
