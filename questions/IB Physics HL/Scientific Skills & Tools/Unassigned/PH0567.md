# PH0567

Electrons are accelerated between two conducting plates that have a potential difference of 100 V. Estimate the total energy that would be delivered by the conducting plates to ${10}^{21}$ electrons.

- A. $\hspace{1em}      {10}^5\ $J

- B. $\hspace{1em}      {10}^4\ $J

- C. $\hspace{1em}      {10}^3\ $J

- D. $\hspace{1em}      {10}^2\ $J

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The energy $E$ is given to each electron in J:

- $E=q\Delta V=(1.6 \times 10^{-19})(100)=1.6 \times10^{-17}\,$J

The total energy $E_T$ is given to the electrons in J:

- $E_T=({10}^{21})(1.6\times{10}^{-17})$

- $E_T=1.6\times{10}^4\,$ J
