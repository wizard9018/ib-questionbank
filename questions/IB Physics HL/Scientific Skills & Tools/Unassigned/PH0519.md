    2. To decrease power dissipated in the wires **OR** to prevent heating of the wire **OR** to reduce current in the wire. $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    When a resistor is connected in series with the wire, it increases the overall resistance of the circuit and thus decreases the current. A lower current decreases the power dissipated in the wire as seen in the relationship $P=I^2R$, thus preventing the wire from heating up, which may affect the value of its resistance.

2. Percentage uncertainty of $A=2\bigg(\dfrac{0.01}{0.58}\bigg)(100 \%)$ $\checkmark$

    $\hspace{4.4cm}=$:answer[$0.0344$] OR :answer[$3.44\%$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    - $A=\pi\ (r^2)=\pi{(\dfrac{d}{2})}^2$

    When values with absolute uncertainty are multiplied, the sum of the relative uncertainties is used to find the new uncertainty. In this case, the relative uncertainty of the area is equal to twice the relative uncertainty on the diameter (which is also equal to twice the relative uncertainty of the radius)

    - $A=2(\dfrac{0.01}{0.58})(100)=0.0344 \ll=3.44\% \gg$
