# PH0050

A circuit of a cell of electromotive force (emf) 18.0 V and negligible internal resistance is connected to three resistors each of resistance $6.0\,\Omega$.

![PH0050](images/PH0050.png)
$\footnotesize{\textrm{[Source: Created with GeoGebra, - https://www.geogebra.org/m/JbAKa82Y}]}$

The ammeter is ideal. What is the reading of the ammeter?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 1.0 A

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 2.0 A

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 3.0 A

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 6.0 A

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$R_{eq}=6+\left(\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{6}}\right)=9\,\Omega$

Current in the circuit, $I=\dfrac{V}{R_{eq}}=\dfrac{18}{9}=2$ A

The current will split evenly between the two resistors as their resistances are equal.

Therefore the ammeter reads 1 A
