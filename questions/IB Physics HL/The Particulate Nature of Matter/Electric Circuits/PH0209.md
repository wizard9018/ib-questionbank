# PH0209

A 6 V battery with an internal resistance of $2\,\Omega$ is connected to a $1\,\Omega$ external resistor. Which statement is correct when the $1\,\Omega$ resistor is replaced with a $2\,\Omega$ resistor?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The potential difference across the terminals of the battery is increased.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The current in the battery is increased.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The power dissipated in the battery is increased.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The total resistance of the circuit is decreased.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The total resistance of the circuit is increased from $3\,\Omega$ to $4\,\Omega$ due to the series connection of internal and external resistors. The current of the circuit is decreased with the increase of total resistance.

The power dissipated inside the battery;

$P=i^2 r$

The dissipated power is decreased due to a decrease in current and constant internal resistance.

On the other hand, the potential difference across the terminal of the battery;

$V=\varepsilon - ir$

The emf of the battery ($\varepsilon$) and internal resistance ($r$) is constant while the current is decreased. Therefore, potential difference ($V$) is increased. The answer is A.
