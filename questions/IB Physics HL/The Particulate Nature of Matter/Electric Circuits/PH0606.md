# PH0606

Consider the circuits below that are located side by side as shown. One of these circuits includes a thermistor, which decreases in resistance as it increases in temperature.

![PH0606](images/PH0606.png)

If the switch in the circuit on the left is closed, what will happen to the brightness of the lamp over time, and why?

$\\$

| | Lamp brightness | Reason |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Increases | Current in lamp circuit increases |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Decreases | Current in lamp circuit decreases |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Increases | Current in lamp circuit decreases |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Decreases | Current in lamp circuit increases |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Once the switch is closed, the heating element in the circuit on the left ![PH0606-i](images/PH0606-i.png) increases in temperature.

This increase affects the thermistor ![PH0606-ii](images/PH0606-ii.png)by **decreasing** its resistance. As a result the current in the circuit on the right increases.

The power in the lamp is given by the equation

- $P=I^2R$

Therefore the increase in current causes the lamp\'s power and brightness to increase.
