# PH0415

An electric circuit is connected as shown. An electric current of 2 A flows through the electric cell with an internal resistance $r$.

![PH0415](images/PH0415.png)
$\small{\textrm{[Source: Created with GeoGebra, - https://www.geogebra.org/m/JbAKa82Y}]}$

What is the value for $r$ ?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $1\, \Omega$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $2\, \Omega$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $0.5\, \Omega$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $0.25\, \Omega$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The $26\, \Omega$ resistors are connected in parallel, their total resistance is,

$\dfrac{1}{R_{total}}=\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{2}{6}=\dfrac{1}{3}$

$R_{total}=3\,\Omega$

These 2 resistors are connected in series with the $2\, \Omega$ resistor,

$R=3+2=5\, \Omega$

The emf $\varepsilon$,

$\varepsilon=I(R+r)$

$12=2(5+r)$

$r=\dfrac{12}{2}-5=6-5=1\, \Omega$
