# PH0979

The diagram shows a circuit containing eight resistors connected to a cell of emf 12 V and negligible internal resistance.

![image](images/image.png)

A voltmeter is connected between two points in the circuit. Which of the following connections will result in a potential difference reading of the largest magnitude?

$\\$

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ P to Q

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Q to R

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Q to S

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ R to S

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The potential difference across each of the four sections of the parallel combination will be 12 V.

Resistors in series have the same current, so for each pair of resistors $V\propto R$

Ratio of resistances = Ratio of potential difference across those resistors

For section containing $P$: Ratio of potential difference across the resistors $= 1:1=6:6$

For section containing $Q$: Ratio of potential difference across the resistors $= 1:5=2:10$

For section containing $R$: Ratio of potential difference across the resistors $= 2:1=8:4$

For section containing $S$: Ratio of potential difference across the resistors $= 1:11$

For each final ratio above, the first term gives the potential difference across the first resistor.

If we let the potential either side of the cell be 0 V and 12 V we may write the potentials at $P$, $Q$, $R$ and $S$ as follows

![image](images/image.png)

(Note: We can choose any arbitrary values for the potentials either side of the cell - as long as they have a difference of 12 V and the positive side of the cell has a larger potential)

The largest difference here is $(11-4)$ between points $R$ and $S$.
