# PH0608

An electric motor lifts containers through a vertical distance of 30 m. Each container weighs 200 N and takes 60 seconds to lift.

If the motor operates at 100 V and has an efficiency of 40\%, what current must be delivered to the motor?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.25 A

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.4 A
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 1.0 A
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 2.5 A

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

A motor efficiency of $40\%$ means that $0.4$ =$\dfrac{Useful\ power\ out}{Total\ power\ in}$.

The useful power out $P_{out}=\dfrac{E}{t}=\dfrac{\left(F\right)\left(d\right)}{t}=\dfrac{\left(200\ \ce{N}\right)\left(30\ \ce{m}\right)}{60\ \ce{s}}=100\ \ce{W}$.

Total power in $P_{in}=\ \dfrac{100\ \ce{W}}{0.4}=250$ W.

Since this is electrical power, $P_{in}=250\ \ce{W}=VI=\left(100\ \ce{V}\right)\left(I\right)$.

Therefore $I=\dfrac{250\ \ce{W}}{100\ \ce{V}}=2.5$ A.
