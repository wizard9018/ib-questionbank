# PH0609

Two resistors, $P$ and $Q$, demonstrate the following relationships between current $I$ and potential difference $V$.

![PH0609aa](images/PH0609aa.png)

The resistors are arranged as follows.

![PH0609b](images/PH0609b.png)

If the ammeter reads a current of 3.0 A, what is the potential difference across the cell?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.75 V

- B. $\hspace{1em}$    3.0 V
- C. $\hspace{1em}$    5.0 V
- D. $\hspace{1em}$    7.0 V

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Because the components are in parallel, the current read by the ammeter is the sum of the currents through the resistors *P* and *Q*.

From the graph we see their combined current is 3.0 A at a potential difference of 5.0 V.

This must be the potential difference that the cell supplies to the circuit.
