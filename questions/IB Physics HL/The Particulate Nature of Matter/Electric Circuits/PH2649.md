2. A second circuit is constructed as shown below. The resistance characteristics of components **Y** and **Z** are shown in the graph.

    ![PH2649ci](images/PH2649ci.png)
    ![PH2649cii](images/PH2649cii.png)

    Determine which resistor has greater resistance.  [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. $R_{total}= \dfrac{5000\ \Omega}{N}$ $\checkmark$

     $\hspace{1em}$ $I = \dfrac{V}{R_{total}}=\dfrac{24\  (N)}{5000\ }\le2.0$ A $\checkmark$

     $\hspace{1em}$ $N\le\dfrac{10000}{24}\ \Rightarrow$ :answer[$N=416$]  $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
Using the equation $\dfrac{1}{R_{total}}=\dfrac{1}{R_1}+\dfrac{1}{R_2}+\ldots$ for the total circuit resistance of identical resistors in parallel can be used to find $R_{total}$.

      $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{1}{R_{total}}=N\lparen \dfrac{1}{R_{total}})$

      $\hspace{1em}$ $R_{total}=\dfrac{R}{N}=\dfrac{5000}{N}$

      Using this value in the resistance equation indicates that the maximum circuit current

     $\hspace{1em}$ $I_{max}=\dfrac{V}{R}=\dfrac{24(N)}{5000}$

     $\hspace{1em}$ $2\ =\dfrac{24(N)}{5000}$

     Rearranging to solve for $N$ gives a value of 416 (Note that the number is rounded down. To round up would make the current over the allowed limit).

2.  At $6 \ \ce{V}, R_Z\approx\dfrac{6\ \ce{V}}{1.8\ \ce{A}}=3.3$  while $R_{Y\ }\approx\ \dfrac{6\ \ce{V}}{4.6\ \ce{A}}=1.3\ \Omega$ $\checkmark$

    Component **Z** has greater resistance $\checkmark$

    **OR**

    From the graph, $I_Y > I_Z$ at all potential differences and $R=\dfrac{V}{I}$ $\checkmark$

    Component Z has greater resistance    $\checkmark$

     $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
Using the resistance formula $R = \dfrac{V}{I}$ to find the resistance of each resistor when $V = 6$ V.

     Taking approximate values from the graph, at 6 $V$ the current in **Y** is roughly 4.6 A, while the current through **Z** is about 1.8 A.

     $R=\dfrac{V}{I}$, therefore the resistor with the least current at 6 V will have the greatest resistance. At this potential difference, resistor **Z** has less current than resistor **Y**, and therefore a greater resistance.

     Alternatively,

     It can be seen from the graph that $I_Y > I_Z$ at all potential differences (although 6 V is the potential difference it is operating at in this situation). Through the definition of resistance
$R = \dfrac{V}{I}$

    We can see that if $V$ is the same (which it is in the parallel arrangement here), larger currents reflect smaller resistances.

    Because **Y** has a larger current than **Z**, it must have a smaller resistance. **Z** has greater resistance than **Y**.
