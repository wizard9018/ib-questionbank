# PH0104

In the circuit shown, a resistor of $5\,\Omega$ is connected to a long resistive wire with resistance of $15\,\Omega$ through a sliding contact that can move along the entire length of the wire. Both are connected to a power supply with 15 V  of emf and negligible internal resistance.

![Screenshot 2025-04-18 at 10.12.23 AM](<images/Screenshot 2025-04-18 at 10.12.23 AM.JPEG>)
$\small{\textrm{[Source: Created with GeoGebra, - https://www.geogebra.org/m/JbAKa82Y}]}$

What is the maximum and minimum values of current through the power supply?

$\\$

| | Maximum current / A | Minimum current / A |
| :--   | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | $3.0$ | $0.75$ |
| B. | $4.0$ | $0.75$ |
| C. | $3.0$ | $1.0$ |
| D. | $4.0$ | $1.0$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

When the sliding contact is placed at the end of the resistive wire, it is a parallel circuit.

$\dfrac{1}{R_T}=\dfrac{1}{15}+\dfrac{1}{5}= \dfrac{4}{15}\longrightarrow R_T = \dfrac{15}{4} \, \Omega$

Hence by $V=IR$, the current through the power supply is 4.0 A.

When the sliding contact is placed at the start of the resistive wire, the potential difference across the $5\,\Omega$ resistor is 0 V. Hence there is only current through $15\,\Omega$ resistive wire. Hence the current through the power supply is 1.0 A.
