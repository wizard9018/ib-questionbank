# PH0600

If the radius of a segment of wire is doubled, and its length decreases by half, by what factor does the wire\'s resistance change?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 1

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 2

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 4

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 8

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- $\rho=\dfrac{RA}{L}$, so $R=\dfrac{\rho L}{A}$.

For a wire with circular cross-section, $A=\pi\ r^2$.

If $r\rightarrow2r$, then $A\rightarrow\left(2^2\right)A=4A$.

Also, $L \rightarrow \dfrac{L}{2}$

If the initial resistance of the wire is $R_1$, and the final resistance is $R_2$, then $\dfrac{R_1}{R_2}=\dfrac{\frac{\rho L}{A}}{\frac{\rho(\frac{L}{2})}{(4A)}}=8$.
