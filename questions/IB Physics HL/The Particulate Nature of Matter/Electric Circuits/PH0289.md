# PH0289

A battery of emf $\varepsilon$ and an internal resistance $r$ is connected across a resistance $R$. The total power dissipated in the circuit is $P$.

An identical resistor and an identical battery are added in series to the circuit. What is the new total power dissipated in this circuit?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $P$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $2P$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $4P$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $8P$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Using the equation

- $P=\dfrac{V^2}{R}$

the total power in the first circuit is

- $P=\dfrac{V^2}{R+r}$.

When the new battery is added, the total emf becomes 2 V and the internal resistance becomes $2r,$ so the new power calculation is

- $P_{\text{new}}=\dfrac{(2\ce{V})^2}{2R+2r}=\dfrac{4V^2}{2(R+r)}=2P_\text{old}$.
