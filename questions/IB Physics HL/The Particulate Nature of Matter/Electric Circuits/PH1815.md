# PH1815

The diagram shows two identical cells, each with an emf of $3\,$V and internal resistance $r$, are connected in a circuit with a $3.0\,\Omega$ resistor.

![PH1815q](images/PH1815q.JPEG)

The ammeter reads $1.5$ A. What is $r$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $0.25\,\Omega$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $0.50\,\Omega$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $0.75\,\Omega$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $1.0\,\Omega$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The total emf of the circuit is $3+3=6$ V as both cells work to push the current in the same direction around the circuit.

The total resistance of the circuit is $3+2r$.

Using $V_{total}=IR_{total}$ we have:

- $6=1.5(3+2r)$

- $4=3+2r$

- $1=2r$

- $r=0.5\,\Omega$
