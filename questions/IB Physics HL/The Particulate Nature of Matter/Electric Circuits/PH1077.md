    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    An ohmic conductor is defined as having a constant current to potential difference ratio (a constant resistance).

    The graph here is curved meaning that the resistance changes. Hence *L* is not ohmic.

    Filament lamps undergo a significant increase in temperature when current is increased. A higher temperature means faster atomic vibrations leading to increased resistance.

**OR**\
both emfs positive on different sides of the equation $\checkmark$\
**OR**\
correct resistances, i.e. $I\ (1\ +\ 2)$ $\checkmark$

    $\hspace{1em}$ $10\ -\ 6\ =I\ (1\ +\ 2)$\
    **OR**\
    $\hspace{1em}$ $I\ =\dfrac{4}{3}$ A seen $\checkmark$

    $\hspace{1em}$ $V\ =\ \left( \dfrac{4}{3} \right)\ (2)\ =$ :answer[$2.7\ \ce{V}$] $\checkmark$

    Allow any valid method.

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    For a series circuit, we know that the sum of all voltage rise and drop around any closed loop is equal to zero (due to conservation of energy).

    Total voltage rise $=\ 10\ -\ 6\ =\ 4\,\ce{V}$ because the cells are working in opposite directions (The 10 V cell alone would cause a clockwise current and the 6 V cell alone would cause an anticlockwise current. Together the cells cause a clockwise current because the 10 V cell is larger)

    Total voltage drop $=\ IR_{total}\ =\ I(1\ +\ 2)\ =\ 3I$ (both resistances are positive because current is flowing clockwise through them both)

    Therefore $4\ =\ 3I$

    $\hspace{1em}$ $I\ =\ \dfrac{4}{3}$

    Voltage across $2\ \Omega$ resistor $=\ IR\ =\ \left( \dfrac{4}{3} \right)\ (2)\ =\ \dfrac{8}{3}\ =\ 2.7$ V
