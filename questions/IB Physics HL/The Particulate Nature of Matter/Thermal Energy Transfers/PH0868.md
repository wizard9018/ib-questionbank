# PH0868

A steel ball is dropped into a body of water. The water is then cooled until it freezes. The thermal conductivity of ice is approximately four times higher than the thermal conductivity of water.

Which line of the table correctly describes how freezing affects the rates of heat transfer out of the ball by conduction and convection?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$Conduction $\hspace{1.5em}$| $\hspace{1.5em}$Convection$\hspace{1.5em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Decreases |Decreases |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Decreases | Increases |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Increases| Decreases|
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Increases | Increases |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

When the water freezes to ice, the rate of heat conduction increases because the thermal conductivity of ice is greater than that of water. However, heat transfer by convection ceases because the heat can no longer be transferred via motion of the water molecules.
