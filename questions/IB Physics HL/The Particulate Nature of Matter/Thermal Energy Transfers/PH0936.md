## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The internal energy of a substance (total energy) is the sum of kinetic and potential energies of the particles.

Temperature is directly proportional to the average kinetic energy of particles in a substance.

During change of state, heat energy is transferred into potential energy, so temperature does not change. This corresponds to $\lq$flat' regions on a graph of $T$ against $Q$.

When heat energy is added and there is no change of state - energy is transferred to kinetic energy - so temperature increases in proportion to heat added. This corresponds to a sloped straight line on a graph of $T$ against $Q$.
