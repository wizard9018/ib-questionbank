# PH0857

A solid material is heated at a constant rate.

The graph shows the variation of its temperature $T$ with time $t$.

![Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 5.01.15 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 5.01.15 PM.png>)

Three statements are given about this material:

- I. $\hspace{1em}$ The solid state has the highest specific heat capacity
- II. $\hspace{.7em}$ The specific latent heat of vaporisation $L_v$ is greater than the specific latent heat of fusion $L_f$
- III. $\hspace{.4em}$ The liquid state has the highest specific heat capacity

Which of these statements is correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The steepness of the line in the solid state shows that it takes the least time to rise in temperature; this means it has the lowest specific heat capacity. Statement **I** is incorrect.

From the graph we can see that vaporisation takes more time than melting, therefore more energy is required for vaporisation and $L_v>L_f$.  Statement **II** is correct.

The liquid state is less steep than the solid state, indicating that it takes the longest time to rise in temperature; this means it has the highest specific heat capacity. Statement **III** is correct.
