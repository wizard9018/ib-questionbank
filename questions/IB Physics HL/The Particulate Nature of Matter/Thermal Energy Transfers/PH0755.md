# PH0755

One mole of $\ce{N2}$ ($28$ g mol$^{-1}$) reacts with one mole of $\ce{O2}$ ($32$ g  mol$^{-1}$) at constant temperature to form two moles of $\ce{NO}$ ($30$ g mol$^{-1}$).

Which line of the table correctly describes the ratios between the mean velocities of the gas molecules?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em} \dfrac{\textrm{Mean\ N}_2\ \textrm{velocity}}{\textrm{Mean\ NO}\ \textrm{velocity}} \hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em} \dfrac{\textrm{Mean\ O}_2\ \textrm{velocity}}{\textrm{Mean\ NO\ velocity}} \hspace{1.5em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Less than 1| Less than 1|
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Less than 1| Greater than 1|
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Greater than 1| Less than 1|
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Greater than 1| Greater than 1|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The mean velocity of the $\ce{N2}$ molecules is given by the equation:

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{m_{\left(N_2\right)}v_{\left(N_2\right)}^2}{2}=\dfrac{3k_BT}{2}$

$\hspace{1em}$ $\therefore\ v_{\left(N_2\right)}=\sqrt{\dfrac{3k_BT}{m_{\left(N_2\right)}}}$

where $m_{(N_2)}$ is the molar mass, $R$ is the molar gas constant and $T$ is temperature.

Similarly,

$\hspace{1em}$ $v_{\left(O_2\right)}=\sqrt{\dfrac{3k_BT}{m_{\left(O_2\right)}\ }}$

Once converted to NO, the mean velocity of the molecules becomes

$\hspace{1em}$ $v_{\left(NO\right)}=\sqrt{\dfrac{3k_BT}{m_{\left(NO\right)}}}$

Thus, the velocity ratios can be expressed as

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{v_{\left(N_2\right)}}{v_{\left(NO\right)}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{m_{\left(NO\right)}}{m_{\left(N_2\right)}}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{30}{28}}>1$

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{v_{\left(O_2\right)}}{v_{\left(NO\right)}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{m_{\left(NO\right)}}{m_{\left(O_2\right)}}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{30}{32}}<1$

Note that the number of moles of particles of each gas is not relevant here, as we are looking at individual particle masses and speeds.
