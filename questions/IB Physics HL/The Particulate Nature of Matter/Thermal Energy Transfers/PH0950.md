# PH0950

A box containing an ideal gas is heated evenly from all sides. It is found that the temperature of the gas closest to the ground is the lowest. This is due to

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Conduction

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Convection

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Condensation

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Radiation

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Convection is a movement of groups of atoms or molecules in fluids, that arises from change in density.

Convection here occurs because hot gas molecules are less dense so will rise up against gravity. Cooler gas molecules are more dense so will fall to bottom of the box.
