# PH0951

The graph below shows wavelength against intensity for a black body.

![Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 5.16.47 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 5.16.47 PM.png>)

How would the shape of the graph change if the surface of the black body were at a higher temperature?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Peak becomes lower and shifts to the left

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Peak becomes lower and shifts to the right

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Peak becomes higher and shifts to the left

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Peak becomes higher and shifts to the right

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The peak wavelength is given by the equation

- $\lambda_{max}\,T=2.9\times{10}^{-3}$ K m

Therefore, as temperature increases, the peak wavelength decreases and will shift to the left

And the power is given by

- $P=\sigma AT^4$

The higher temperature of a black body, the higher the total energy of radiation given off. This will cause the area under the graph, which is representative of the amount of power emitted, to increase and the peak to go higher.
