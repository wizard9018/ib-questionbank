# PH0120

![PH0120zzz](images/PH0120zzz.png)

$\hspace{1em}$

A hot sphere of temperature $T$ and surface area $A_1$ radiates towards a metal plate of area $A_2$ that is a very large distance $D$ away. Which of the following expressions gives the rate at which radiant energy is received by the plate? 

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{\sigma A_1 A_2 T^4}{4{\pi}D}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{\sigma A_1 T^4}{D^2}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{\sigma A_1 A_2 T^4}{4{\pi}D^2}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{\sigma A_2 T^4}{D^2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

The power radiated by the sphere is $P_1=\sigma A_1 T^4$

The intensity received by the plate is 

- $b = \dfrac{L}{4\pi r^2}= \dfrac{P_1}{4\pi D^2} =\dfrac{\sigma A_1 T^4}{4{\pi}D^2}$ 

The power received by the plate is 

- $P_2 = b A_2$

Therefore combining the formulae, the power received by the plate is 

- $\dfrac{\sigma A_1 A_2 T^4}{4{\pi}D^2}$
