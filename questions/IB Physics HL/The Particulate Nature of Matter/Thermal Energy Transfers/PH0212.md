# PH0212

A spherical object of surface area $A$ and absolute temperature $T$ can be regarded as a black body. The power of the radiation emitted from the object is $P$. What would be the power of the radiation emitted by an object with an absolute temperature of $2T$ and a surface area of $\dfrac{A}{2}$? 

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $P$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $2P$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $4P$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $8P$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Power of the emission for the initial conditions; 

- $P= \sigma AT^4$

Power of the emission for the final conditions; 

- $P_x = \sigma \bigg(\dfrac{A}{2}\bigg) (2T)^4 $

- $= \sigma \bigg(\dfrac{A}{2}\bigg) 16T^4 = 8\sigma AT^4$

Therefore 

- $P_x= 8P$
