## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Ice has half the specific heat capacity of liquid water and the rate of energy loss is constant. Therefore, after freezing the temperature of the system decreases at twice the rate compared to when cooling in the liquid state.
