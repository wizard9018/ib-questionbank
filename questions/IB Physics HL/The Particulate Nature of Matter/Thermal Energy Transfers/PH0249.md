# PH0249

A material with a mass of m is heated. The graph shows the variation of the temperature $T$ of the material with the energy supplied $E$. 

![Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 4.36.39 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 4.36.39 PM.png>)

Which one of the following gives the specific heat capacity of the material?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{2E_0m}{T_2-T_1}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{E_0m}{T_1}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{2E_0}{m(T_2-T_1)}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{E_0}{m(T_2-T_1)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Rearranging the equation for specific heat capacity gives

- $c =\dfrac{Q}{m\Delta T}$ 

The specific heat capacity equation applies to the sloped part of the graph, where temperature is changing. 

- $c = \dfrac{2E_0-E_0}{m(T_2-T_1)}$ 

- $c = \dfrac{E_0}{m(T_2-T_1)}$
