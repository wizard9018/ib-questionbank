# PH0347

A sealed container contains a mixture of hydrogen and nitrogen gas. 

The mass ratio $\dfrac{\textrm{mass of a hydrogen molecule}}{\textrm{mass of a nitrogen molecule}}=\dfrac{1}{7}$

The ratio $\dfrac{\textrm{average kinetic energy of hydrogen}}{\textrm{average kinetic energy of nitrogen}}$ is

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 7

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{1}{7}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 1

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Dependent on the concentration of each gas.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

The container is sealed.

The two gases are mixed together and therefore are in thermal equilibrium, which means they have the same temperature.

The temperature is a measure of the average kinetic energy, so both gases have the same average kinetic energy, which makes the ratio of their average kinetic energies to be 1.
