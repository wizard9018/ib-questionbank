## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

373 K is the boiling point of water. Temperature does not change until all water is boiled. B and C show temperature change at $t=0$. D is not compatible with the question. 

You might have seen graphs that resemble options B and D in this context, they are there to distract you.
