# PH0856

Four insulated containers contain the same liquid with different masses and at different temperatures as shown in the diagram.

![Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 4.59.39 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 4.59.39 PM.png>)

The four are then mixed in another insulated container.

What is the final temperature of the mixture?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $45\degree$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $55\degree$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $60\degree$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $180\degree$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The four containers are filled with the same liquid then they have the same specific heat capacity

The net heat lost for the system is zero.

Using the formula
$Q=mc \Delta T$ for each container gives:

$\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2em} 0&=3mc(T-80)+2mc(T-60)+2mc(T-40)+mc(T-0)\\
0&=(3T-240)+(2T-120)+(2T-80)+T\\
8T&=440\\
T&=55\degree
\end{aligned}$
