# PH0239

Several energy conversions occur in power stations. Examples of electricity generation that both involve energy transfer from thermal to electrical are 

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Coal-fired and hydroelectric

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Coal-fired and nuclear

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Solar and natural gas

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Solar and wind

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Both in coal-fired and nuclear, thermal energy is converted to electrical energy. Therefore, the answer is B.
