# PH0059

The orders of energy transformations in three power stations are given;

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}

  | | |

  | :-- | :-- |

  | I. | chemical $\rightarrow$ thermal $\rightarrow$ kinetic $\rightarrow$ electrical |

  | II. | kinetic $\rightarrow$ kinetic $\rightarrow$ electrical |

  | III. | gravitational potential $\rightarrow$ kinetic $\rightarrow$ electrical |

Which row correctly matches the type of power station with the energy transformations?

$\\$

| |  $\hspace{6em}$I$\hspace{6em}$  |  $\hspace{5em}$ II $\hspace{5em}$ |  $\hspace{4em}$III $\hspace{4em}$ |

| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$ A. $\hspace{1.5em}$ | Coal power station | Hydroelectric power station | Wind power station |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$ B. $\hspace{1.5em}$ | Coal power station | Wind power station | Hydroelectric power station |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$ C. $\hspace{1.5em}$ | Solar power station | Hydroelectric power station | Coal power station |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$ D. $\hspace{1.5em}$ | Nuclear power station | Wind power station | Coal power station |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

I. $\hspace{1em}$ Chemical energy in fossil fuel is transformed into thermal energy by burning and thermal energy is used to move (kinetic energy) steam that rotates the turbine and generator (also kinetic energy). Finally, electric energy is produced in generators.

II.    $\hspace{1em}$ The kinetic energy of wind rotates the blades (kinetic energy) and rotates the generator. Finally, electric energy is produced in generators.

III. $\hspace{1em}$ The gravitational potential energy of water behind a dam converts into kinetic energy as water falls down and rotates the turbine and generator. Finally, electric energy is produced in generators.
