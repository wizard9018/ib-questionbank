# PH0952

Light is incident on a patch of fresh snow. It is found that for every photon absorbed by the snow, four photons are reflected. What is the albedo value of the fresh snow?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.2

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.25

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.75

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.8

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

- Albedo $= \dfrac{\textrm{Total scattered power}}{\textrm{Total incident power}}$

We are told that for every absorbed photon, four are scattered. This means that out of five incident photons, four will be scattered and one absorbed so the ratio is

- $\dfrac{\textrm{ Number of scattered photons}}{\textrm{Total number of incident photons}} = \dfrac{4}{5} = 0.8$

The scattering rate of  the photons is equivalent to the scattering rate of the incident light and therefore the albedo.

- Albedo $= 0.8$
