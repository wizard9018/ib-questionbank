# PH0134

Solar radiation is incident on a planet, whose surface absorbs 65% of incident intensity. What is the albedo of the planet? 

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.25

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.35

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.65

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 1

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Albedo = $\dfrac{\textrm{reflected power}}{\textrm{incident power}}$

If the planet absorbs 65%, it reflects 35%

Giving albedo = $\dfrac{35}{100}=0.35$
