# PH1788

**Maximum mark: 3**

The diagram below shows a simple energy balance model for the Earth.

![Screenshot 2025-03-21 at 10.17.17 AM](<images/Screenshot 2025-03-21 at 10.17.17 AM.JPEG>)

1.    Determine the intensity of the incoming radiation absorbed by the atmosphere. [1 mark]

2. Use the diagram to deduce the albedo of the Earth. [1 mark]

3. Explain in terms of energy balance how a decrease in the average albedo value of the Earth would cause the Earth to reach a new equilibrium temperature. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. ::indent
    - $340-170-85-15=$ :answer[70 W m$^{-2}$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    Conservation of energy tells us that all of the incoming solar radiation is either reflected or absorbed by the Earth's atmosphere and surface. We therefore have:

    - $85+15+170+X=340$

    - $340-170-85-15=70$ W m$^{-2}$

2. ::indent
    - $\dfrac{15+85}{340}=$ :answer[0.29] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    Albedo is given by:

    - $\textrm{albedo}=\dfrac{\textrm{total scattered power}}{\textrm{total incident power}}$

    In the diagram, 15 W m$^{-2}$ is reflected by the atmosphere, and 85 W m$^{-2}$  is reflected by the surface. The total scattered power is $15+85=100$ W m$^{-2}$. The total incident radiation is 340 W m$^{-2}$.

    Substituting these values into our equation we have:

    - $\textrm{albedo}=\dfrac{\textrm{total scattered power}}{\textrm{total incident power}}=\dfrac{100}{340}=0.29$

3. $\ll$A decrease in albedo would result in$\gg$ more solar radiation being absorbed by the Earth, $\checkmark$

    Earth's temperature will increase until emitted radiation = absorbed radiation $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    Albedo represents the reflectivity of the Earth. A lower albedo means less solar radiation is reflected and more is absorbed by the Earth's surface and atmosphere.

    To achieve energy balance, the radiated energy must equal the absorbed energy. Radiated energy is given by the equation

    - $L=\sigma AT^4$

    And so the temperature $T$ must increase so that $L$ can match the radiation absorbed.
