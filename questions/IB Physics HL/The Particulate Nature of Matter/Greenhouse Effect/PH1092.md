# PH1092

Which of the following statements is correct for the albedo of a particular area of the Earth's surface?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | The albedo remains constant for the same area throughout the year. |
  | II. | The albedo tends to increase when the area is covered by snow. |
  | III. | The albedo is generally lower for areas covered with trees compared to those covered with concrete.  |

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Only I

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Only II

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Only III

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Only II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The albedo of land varies due to several factors. Cloud formations, which change daily, significantly influence albedo by affecting the amount of incoming solar radiation reflected into space. Additionally, the presence of snow tends to increase albedo, as snow is a highly reflective surface. Conversely, areas covered with trees tend to have lower albedo because trees absorb a significant amount of sunlight for photosynthesis, reducing the overall reflectivity of the land.
