# PH1091

A system with an albedo of 0.3 scatters radiation that is incident on it. The power of scattered radiation is $1200$ W. Determine the power absorbed by the system.

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $840$ W

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $1200$ W

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $2800$ W

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $4000$ W

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

- Albedo $=\dfrac{\textrm{Total scattered power}}{\textrm{Total incident power}}$

- Total incident power $=\dfrac{\textrm{Total scattered power}}{\textrm{Albedo}} = \dfrac{1200}{0.3} = 4000$ W

- Absorbed power $=4000$ W$-1200$ W $=2800$ W
