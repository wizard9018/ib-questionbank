# PH1096

Consider the following statements about the behaviour of greenhouse gases.

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | They absorb radiation that matches their natural frequency.   |
  | II. | They absorb radiation with frequencies matching the energy difference between molecular energy levels. |
  | III. | They reach high temperatures after absorbing radiation, warming the Earth. |

Which statements are correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Only I

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Only II

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Only I and II

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Only II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The absorption of radiation emitted by the Earth and the atmosphere by greenhouse gases can be explained by both resonance and energy differences between molecular energy levels.

Because the photon energies match the differences in the energy levels, the photons are absorbed and the molecules enter excited energy states.

The resonance model states that the frequency of the radiation matches the resonant frequencies of the molecules, causing them to oscillate in a resonant state.

On absorption of the radiation, the molecule energies increase slightly, but it is the re-radiation of the photons back to Earth that causes the Earth’s temperature to increase.
