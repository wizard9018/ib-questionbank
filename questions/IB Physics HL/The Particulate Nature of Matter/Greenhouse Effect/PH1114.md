# PH1114

The production rates of greenhouse gases from various sources within a country are measured over a period of thirty years. The graph shows how the measured production rates vary with time.

![PH1114](images/PH1114.png)

Which of the following statements are correct?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Contributions to the enhanced greenhouse effect decrease after 15 years |
  | II. | Total contributions to the enhanced greenhouse effect exceed $8$ Mt per year at 15 years |
  | III. | The enhanced greenhouse effect accounts for a lower proportion of total global warming at the end of the measurement period than at the start |

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and II

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and III

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ II and III

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The enhanced greenhouse effect refers to warming due to human sources of greenhouse gases. Consequently, it is true that contributions to the enhanced greenhouse effect decrease after 15 years, even though contributions from natural sources continue to increase during this period; hence, Statement I is correct.

The sum of all contributions to the greenhouse effect surpasses $8$ Mt per year after 15 years, but human sources do not; therefore, Statement II is incorrect.

At the start of the measurement period, emissions from human sources occur at an approximately five times higher rate than those from natural sources. By comparison, the emission rate from human sources after 30 years is around double the rate of natural emissions. Hence, human sources account for a greater proportion of emissions at the start of the measurement period than at the end, so Statement III is correct.
