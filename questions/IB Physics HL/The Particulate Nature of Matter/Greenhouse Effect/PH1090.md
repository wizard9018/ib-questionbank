# PH1090

A surface with an area $A_S$ emits electromagnetic radiation at a rate of $P_S$. An ideal blackbody with the same temperature and area $A_B$ emits radiation at a rate of $P_B$. What is the emissivity of the surface?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{P_SA_B}{P_BA_S}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{P_SA_S}{P_BA_B}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{P_BA_B}{P_SA_S}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{P_BA_S}{P_SA_B}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

By using Stefan Boltzmann's Law for both bodies:

- $P_S=\epsilon \sigma A_S T^4$

Since the emissivity of a black body is 1:

- $P_B=\sigma A_B T^4$

Dividing the two equations:

- $\dfrac{P_S}{P_B}=\dfrac{\epsilon \sigma A_S T^4}{\sigma A_B T^4}=\epsilon \dfrac{A_S}{A_B}$

- $\epsilon=\dfrac{P_S A_B}{P_B A_S}$
