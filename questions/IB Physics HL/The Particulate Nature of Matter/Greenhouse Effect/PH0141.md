# PH0141

An area on the Earth's surface receives an average solar intensity of 250 W m$^{-2}$. What is the minimum area of photovoltaic cells needed to generate 2 kW of electrical power if the photocells operate at 40% efficiency? 

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.5 m$^2$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 1.0 m$^2$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 2.0 m$^2$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 20 m$^2$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

For the solar cells functioning at 40% efficiency, the product of efficiency, power input per square meter and the area of the solar cells the output energy will be 2 kW. 

Hence, $P=(0.40)(250\textrm{ W m}^{-2})(A)$

- $A=\dfrac{2000\textrm{ W}}{100\textrm{ W m}^{-2}}=20$ m$^2$
