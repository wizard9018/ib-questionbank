# PH0149

An ideal gas is enclosed in a container of fixed volume. When the temperature of the gas is $27\degree$C, its pressure is $p$. Calculate the pressure in the gas when it is heated to $54\degree$C.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $p$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $1.09p$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $1.5p$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $2p$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Temperature appears to be doubled; but that is using the celsius scale. Absolute temperature is not doubled. So, $D$ is incorrect. 

Volume cannot change, since the container's volume is fixed. 

The number of moles cannot change since the gas is enclosed in the container. 

Hence, pressure is proportional to absolute temperature:

 $\dfrac{p_2}{p}=\dfrac{273+54}{273+27}\Rightarrow p_2=1.09p$.
