# PH0086

An enclosed container with an internal volume V contains $3.0 \times 10^{23}$ helium atoms at a Kelvin temperature $T$. What is the pressure of the helium gas?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $(3.0 \times 10^{23}) \dfrac{RT}{V}$  

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $3.0 \dfrac{k_B T}{V}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{3.0}{6.02}\dfrac{RT}{V}$  

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{3.0}{6.02}\dfrac{k_B T}{V}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Apply $PV=nRT$, in which $n$ is the number of moles of gas molecules. Using 

$n=\dfrac{N}{N_A} = \dfrac{3.0}{6.02}$ arrives at the solution.
