# PH0042

The following graph shows the relationship between temperature and volume for a real gas at constant pressure. What must be the conditions for pressure and density of the gas?

![PH0042](images/PH0042.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Low pressure and low density

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Low pressure and high density

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ High pressure and low density

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ High pressure and high density

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

The graph shows Charles' Law for an ideal gas. A real gas may be approximated by an ideal gas under low pressure and when the density is low. At higher pressures and larger densities, the effects of intermolecular forces become significant. 

For example, when the particles are under high pressure and pushed close together (high density), the intermolecular forces between the gas particles become significant and a real gas will no longer approximate an ideal gas.
