# PH1523

Two containers each hold a different gas. The first gas has a density $\rho$ and a pressure $2P$, with an average translational speed $v$. The second gas has a density $2\rho$ and a pressure $P$.

![Screenshot 2025-03-24 at 5.37.42 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-03-24 at 5.37.42 PM.JPEG>)

What is the average translational speed of the gas molecules in the second container?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{v}{2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{v}{\sqrt{2}}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $2v$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{2}v$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The average translational speed v using the equation given in the data booklet:

- $P=\dfrac{1}{3}\rho v^2$

- $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{3P}{\rho}}$

For the first container:

- $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{3(2P)}{\rho}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{6P}{\rho}}$

For the second container:

- $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{3P}{2\rho}}=\dfrac{v}{2}$
