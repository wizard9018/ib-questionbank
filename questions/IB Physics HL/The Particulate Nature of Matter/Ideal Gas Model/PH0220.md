# PH0220

A fixed mass of ideal gas is trapped in a closed container. The graph shows how two variables related to the gas vary.

![PH0220](images/PH0220.png)

The gradient of the graph is $kV$ while $k$ is constant and $V$ is the volume of the container. 

In the table below, which row correctly describes quantities X and Y? 

$\\$

| | $\hspace{6em}$ X $\hspace{6em}$ | $\hspace{6em}$ Y $\hspace{6em}$ |

| :-: | :-: | :-: |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Temperature in Celsius | Pressure |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Temperature in Kelvin | Pressure |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Pressure | Temperature in Celsius |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Pressure | Temperature in Kelvin |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

From the ideal gas law, 

$pV = nRT$ therefore, 

- $T = \dfrac{V}{nR}P$

This equation would lead to a graph of $T$ vs $P$ that passes through the origin, however the given graph does not. This can be accounted for by a conversion from absolute temperature to Celsius temperature through a shift of 273.

$\underline{\textrm{Alternatively}}$ \

 A graph of pressure against temperature will give a straight line.

The vertical (X) axis has negative values. This means it must be temperature in Celsius, as neither pressure nor Kelvin temperature can have a negative value. Thus, the horizontal (Y) axis is pressure and the correct answer is A.
