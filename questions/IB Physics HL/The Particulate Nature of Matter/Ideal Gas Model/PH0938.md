# PH0938

Two cubic containers $A$ and $B$ each contain a mass $m$ of the same ideal gas. The same force per unit area is exerted on the walls inside the containers by the ideal gas molecules.

Container $A$ has side lengths $x$. The absolute temperature of the ideal gas inside container $A$ is $T_A$ and the average translational speed of the molecules is $v_A$.

Container $B$ has side lengths $2x$ Which row correctly identifies the absolute temperature of the gas and the average translational speed of the molecules in container $B$?

$\\$

| | Temperature  | Average translational speed |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| $2T_A$  |$\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}v_A $ |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $2T_A$  | $2\sqrt2v_A $   |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $8T_A$  | $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}v_A$ |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $8T_A$  | $2\sqrt2v_A$  |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Volume of container $A = x^3$

Volume of container $B = \left(2x\right)^3=8x^3$

So, container $B$ has a volume eight times greater than container $A$.

- $\dfrac{PV}{T}=constant\  $

Pressure is the same for each container so we may write:

- $\dfrac{V}{T}=constant\  $

- $\dfrac{V_B}{T_B}=\dfrac{V_A}{T_A} $

- $\dfrac{8V_A}{T_B}=\dfrac{V_A}{T_A}  $

- $\dfrac{T_B}{8}=T_A $

- $T_B=8T_A $

Now average $E_k=\dfrac{3}{2}k_BT \hspace{1.5em}$ and $\hspace{1.5em}E_k=\dfrac{1}{2}mv^2$

- $E_k\ \alpha\ T\ \alpha\ v^2 $

We may write:

- $\dfrac{T}{v^2}=constant\  $

- $\dfrac{T_A}{(v_A)^2}=\dfrac{T_B}{(v_B)^2}$

- $\dfrac{T_A}{(v_A)^2}=\dfrac{8T_A}{v_B^2} $

- $\dfrac{v_B^2}{8}=(v_A)^2 $

- $v_B=\sqrt8v=2\sqrt2v_A $
