# PH0480

**Maximum mark: 6**

1. Define one mole. [2 marks]

2. An ideal gas is heated in a closed container of volume 2.5 m$^3$. The variation of the pressure of the gas $p$ with temperature $T$ is shown on the graph below. 

    ![PH0480](images/PH0480.png)

    1. Determine the amount of gas, in mol, in the closed container. [2 marks]

    2. State and explain how the gradient of the graph would change if the amount of gas in the container was increased. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Amount of a substance $\checkmark$

    That contains a number of particles equal to Avogadro's number/the same number of particles as 12 g of Carbon-12 $\checkmark$

2. 

    1. Usage of $n=\dfrac{pV}{RT}$ $\checkmark$

        $n=\ll\dfrac{(3.0\,\times \,10^3)(2.5)}{(8.31)(360)}=\gg$ :answer[$2.5$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        By using the ideal gas law, the number of moles n can be found as;

        $n=\dfrac{pV}{RT}$

        Any point on the graph can be employed to substitute for p and V.

        $n=\dfrac{(3.0\,\times\, 10^3)(2.5)}{(8.31)(360)}= 2.5$

    

    2. Greater/steeper $\checkmark$

        $\dfrac{p}{T}=\dfrac{nR}{V}$

        $\dfrac{p}{T}$ = gradient of the graph and $\dfrac{R}{V}$ = constant

        $\ll$ Therefore; $\gg$

        Gradient $\propto n$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        The ideal gas law;

        - $pV=nRT$

        When it is rearranged;

        - $\dfrac{p}{T}=\dfrac{nR}{V}$

        - $\dfrac{p}{T}$ = gradient of the graph and $\dfrac{R}{V}$ = constant

        Therefore;

        - Gradient $\propto n$

        An increase in the number of moles increases the gradient of the graph.
