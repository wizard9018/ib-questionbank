# PH0876

The internal energies $U$ of two ideal gases are measured with respect to temperature $T$. Gas A contains 0.25 moles of gas molecules, whilst Gas B comprises $10^{23}$ gas molecules.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The internal energy of Gas A is given by

- $U_A=\dfrac{3}{2} n_A RT$

whereas the internal energy of Gas B is best expressed as

- $U_B=\dfrac{3}{2} N_B k_B T$

Taking the ratio of these quantities for the same temperature gives

- $\dfrac{U_A}{U_B} =\dfrac{n_A R}{N_B k_B}\approx \dfrac{(0.25\, \text{mol})(8.3\,\ce{J\,K^{-1}}\text{mol}^{-1})}{(10^{23})(1.4\times 10^{-23}\,\ce{J\,K}^{-1})}\approx 1.5$

Hence, the gradient of the $U$ vs $T$ graph for Gas A is 1.5 times higher than the gradient of the corresponding graph for Gas B.
