2.
    1. The unit of $P_A=\ll\dfrac{\ce{kg m s}^{-2}}{\ce{m}^2}=\gg\ \ce{kg}\,\ce{m}^{-1}\ce{s}^{-2}$ $\checkmark$

        The fundamental SI units of $k=  \ll\ (\ce{kg}\,\ce{m}^{-1}\ce{s}^{-2})(\ce{m}^3) \gg =\ \ce{kg}\,\ce{s}^{-2}\ce{m}^2$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The unit of atmospheric pressure $P_A$ is Pascals. The formula for pressure is

        - $P = \dfrac{F}{A}$ and also $F=ma$.

        Pascals can then be expressed in SI fundamental units as follows;

        - The unit of $P_A=\dfrac{\ce{kg}\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-2}}{\ce{m}^2}=\ce{kg}\,\ce{m}^{-1}\ce{s}^{-2}$

        - The unit of $\dfrac{mg}{A}$  is the same as the unit of $P_A$

        SI fundamental units of $k$ is therefore pressure units multiplied by volume units:

        - $=\ (\ce{kg}\,\ce{m}^{-1}\ce{s}^{-2})(\ce{m}^3)  =\ \ce{kg}\,\ce{s}^{-2}\ce{m}^2$

    2. ::indent
        - $V=2.9\pm0.2\ {\ce{cm}}^3$ $\checkmark$

        - $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V} \times 100 \% = \dfrac{0.2}{2.9} \times 100\% = $ :answer[$6.9\%$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
The volume of the trapped gas  *V* when $m=700$ g;

        - $V=2.9\pm0.2\ \ce{cm}^3$

        Therefore, percentage uncertainty;

        - $\dfrac{\Delta V}{V} \times 100 \% = \dfrac{0.2}{2.9} \times 100\% = 6.9\%$

3. Correct calculation of individual percentage uncertainties $\checkmark$

    - for mass = $\dfrac{\Delta m}{m} \times 100 \% = \dfrac{0.005}{0.700} \times 100 \% = 0.7\%$

    - for area = $\dfrac{\Delta A}{A} \times 100 \% = \dfrac{0.00001}{0.00010} \times 100 \% = 10\%$

    - for gravity = $\dfrac{\Delta g}{g} \times 100 \% = \dfrac{0.01}{9.81} \times 100\% = 0.1\%$

    The percentage uncertainty of $\dfrac{mg}{A} = \ \ll \dfrac{\Delta m}{m}  + \dfrac{\Delta g}{g}  + \dfrac{\Delta A}{A}  = 0.7\% + 0.1\% + 10\% \gg \ = 10.8\%$ $\checkmark$

    - $\dfrac{mg}{A}=\dfrac{(0.700)(9.81)}{0.00010}=68670\ $Pa

    - $\Delta (\dfrac{mg}{A}) = \dfrac{(0.700)(9.81)}{0.00010} \times \dfrac{10.8}{100} = 7416 = $ :answer[$7000 \ \ce{Pa}$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    - For $\dfrac{mg}{A}$ with its absolute uncertainty:

    - $\dfrac{mg}{A} \pm \Delta(\dfrac{mg}{A}) = \dfrac{(0.700)(9.81)}{0.00010} \pm \dfrac{(0.700)(9.81)}{0.00010} \times \dfrac{10.8}{100} = 68670 \pm 7416$

    With correct rounding of the uncertainty to 1 significant figure and matching precision in the calculated value, it would be $69000 \pm 7000$ Pa
