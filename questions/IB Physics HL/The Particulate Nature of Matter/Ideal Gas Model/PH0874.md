# PH0874

An expanding balloon contains $10^{-3}$ moles of ideal gas molecules at a constant pressure of 100 kPa. The graph shows how the average molecular velocity $v$ changes with the internal volume $V$ of the balloon.

![Screenshot 2026-08-10 at 7.57.38 AM](<images/Screenshot 2026-08-10 at 7.57.38 AM.png>)

What is the molar mass of the gas ?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $15\,\ce{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $30\,\ce{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $45\,\ce{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $60\,\ce{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The pressure $P$ inside the balloon is given by

- $P=\dfrac{\rho v^2}{3}$

Expressing the density $\rho$ in terms of the gas volume $V$, the molar quantity $n$ of gas, and the molar mass $M$, one obtains

- $P=\dfrac{nMv^2}{3V}$

Rearrangement yields

- $v^2=\dfrac{3P}{nM} V$

Thus, the gradient $k$ of a plot of $v^2$ vs $V$ can be expressed as

- $k=\dfrac{3P}{nM}$

- $\therefore M = \dfrac{3P}{nk}$

The gradient of the graph is

- $k= \dfrac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} = \dfrac{200\times 10^3\,\ce{m^2\,s^{-2}}}{40\times 10^{-6}\,\ce{m^3}}=5\times 10^9\,\ce{m^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$

Inserting the values given and the measured gradient of the plot gives

- $M = \dfrac{3(10^5\,\ce{Pa})}{(10^{-3}\,\text{mol})(5\times 10^9\,\ce{m^{-1}\,s^{-2}})}=0.06\,\ce{kg}\,\text{mol}^{-1}=60\,\ce{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$
