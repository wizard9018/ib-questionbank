# PH0126

A vessel of volume $1.0 \times 10^{-3}$ m$^3$ contains neon gas at a pressure of $2.0 \times 10^5$ Pa, when the temperature is 200 K. The molar gas constant is approximately $8.0$ J mol$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$ and the molar mass of neon is $20$ g mol$^{-1}$. 

What is the approximate mass of the neon in the vessel?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.1 g

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.4 g

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 2.5 g

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 5.0 g

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

The number of moles can be found by dividing the mass ($m$) by the molar mass ($M$), 

- $n=\dfrac{m}{M}$

Using $PV=nRT$, $\hspace{1em} PV=\frac{m}{M} RT$   

- $\begin{aligned}

m&=Mn\\

\hspace{1em}\\

&= \frac{PVM}{RT}\\

\hspace{1em}\\

&= \frac{(2.0 \times 10^5\textrm{ Pa})(1.0 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{ m}^3)(20\textrm{ g mol}^{-1})}{(8.0\textrm{ J mol}^{-1}\textrm{ K}^{-1})(200\textrm{ K})}\\

\hspace{1em}\\

&= \frac{4000}{1600}\\

\hspace{1em}\\

&= 2.5\textrm{ g}

\end{aligned}$
