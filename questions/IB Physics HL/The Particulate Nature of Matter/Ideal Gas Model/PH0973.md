# PH0973

A box of volume $V$ contains $n$ moles of an ideal gas. A student varies the temperature of the gas and measures how the pressure inside the box varies.

The student plots the results on a graph with pressure in Pa against temperature in $\degree $C. The student then draws a line of best fit extrapolating the data until the line crosses the temperature axis.

Which row correctly shows the gradient and temperature axis intercept of the graph?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$Gradient$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$ Temperature Axis Intercept / $\degree $C $\hspace{1.5em}$|
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| $\dfrac{nR}{V}$ |-273 |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\dfrac{nR}{V}$ | 0 |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\dfrac{V}{nR}$ | -273 |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\dfrac{V}{nR}$ | 0 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Because temperature is the independent variable, it will be plotted on the horizontal axis. Pressure is the dependent variable and will be plotted on the vertical axis.

- $PV=nRT$

Where the temperature will be the absolute temperature in Kelvin.

- Gradient $= \dfrac{P}{T} = \dfrac{nR}{V}$

The temperature axis intercept will be absolute zero (0 kelvin) because when pressure = 0 temperature is as low as possible.

Absolute zero is $-273\degree$C. So the temperature axis intercept for this question is $-273\degree$C.
