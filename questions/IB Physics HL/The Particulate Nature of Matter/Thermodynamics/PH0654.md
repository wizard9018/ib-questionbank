## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. the Clausius form: Heat cannot be transferred from a cold to a hot body without any external work being done $\checkmark$

    the Kelvin form: It is impossible to have a 100\% efficient heat engine $\checkmark$

    in terms of entropy: Any (irreversible) process that occurs increases the entropy of the system or surroundings $\checkmark$

2.
    1. $Q$ is positive when heat is transferred from the surroundings to the system $\checkmark$

        $\Delta U$ is positive when there is an increase in internal energy $\checkmark$

        $W$ is positive when the system does work on the surroundings $\checkmark$

        *Accept reverse statements*

    2. (During the isothermal process) the change in internal energy  $\Delta U$ is zero $\checkmark$

        - $W =$ :answer[$250 \ \ce{J}$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        During the isothermal process, the change in internal energy  $\Delta U$ is zero.

        - $Q = \Delta U + W$

        - $250=0+W$

        - $W=250 \ $J

    3. $\Delta S = \dfrac{\Delta Q}{T} = \dfrac{250}{275}$ $\checkmark$

        $\hspace{1.6em}$ = :answer[$0.91 \, \ce{J K}^{-1}$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        During the isothermal process, change in entropy $\Delta S$  is related to the heat transferred $Q$ and the temperature $T$ at which the change takes place:

        $\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta S = \dfrac{\Delta Q}{T} = \dfrac{250}{275} = 0.91\, \ce{J K}^{-1}$
