# PH0828

The $p-V$ diagram shows 2 possible changes, one adiabatic and one isothermal, of an ideal gas from a common initial state.

Select from the choices the correct description for the processes taking place and the change experienced by the gas.

![image](images/image.png)

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$Process #1$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$Process #2$\hspace{1.5em}$ |$\hspace{1.5em}$Change of the gas$\hspace{1.5em}$|
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Adiabatic | Isothermal | Compression|
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Adiabatic| Isothermal | Expansion|
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Isothermal | Adiabatic |Expansion|
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Isothermal | Adiabatic |Compression|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Because volume is decreasing, we know that both processes are compressions.

In an isothermal compression, temperature remains constant because heat is exchanged with the surroundings. The graph of this change will move along an isotherm.

In an adiabatic compression, there is no exchange of heat with the surroundings and so a temperature increase takes place. This increase in temperature will result in a higher pressure increase than in an isothermal compression to the same volume. The curve will also move to a higher isotherm.

It can be seen on the graph that Curve #1 is steeper than Curve #2, and so #1 is an adiabatic change and #2 is an isothermal change.
