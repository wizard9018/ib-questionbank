# PH1859

The diagram below shows one cycle of operation of a diesel heat engine with an overall efficiency of 40%.

![ph1859q](images/ph1859q.png)

The heat energy transferred to the environment during one cycle of operation of the engine is 1200 J.

Which of the following best describes the area inside the loop shown on the graph?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 480 J

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 720 J

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 800 J

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 940 J

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The area inside the loop corresponds to the useful work done by the engine during one cycle.

The efficiency corresponds to the ratio between the useful work done and the total input energy. This leads to:

- $\eta=\dfrac{\textrm{ useful work}}{\textrm{input energy}}=0.40$

Within a cycle, the internal energy must remain constant, which means that the input energy is equal to the sum of the work done and the 1200 J of heat that was removed from the system.

- $\dfrac{W}{W+1200\textrm{ J}}=0.40$

We can now solve this equation for W.

- $\begin{aligned}
\ W&=0.40(W+1200\textrm{ J})\\
\\\
\ W&=0.40W+0.40(1200\textrm{ J})\\
\\\
\ W-0.40W&=0.40(1200\textrm{ J})\\
\\\
\ W&=\dfrac{0.40(1200\textrm{ J})}{1-0.40}\\
\\\
\ W&=\dfrac{0.40(1200\textrm{ J})}{0.60}\\
\\\
\ W&=800\textrm{ J}\\
\end{aligned}$
