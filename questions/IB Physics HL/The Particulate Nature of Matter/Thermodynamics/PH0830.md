# PH0830

An ideal gas is in a container with a piston. As energy $Q$ is transferred into the gas, the gas expands isothermally.

Which of the following statements are correct?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | The temperature $T$ is constant.  |
  | II. | $\Delta U > 0$ |
  | III. | The work done by the gas is equal to $Q$. |

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and II.

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and III.

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ II and III.

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ III only.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The process is isothermal, so $T =$ constant.

It follows that because temperature remains constant and

- $\Delta U=\dfrac{3}{2}nR \Delta T$

- $\Delta U = 0$

And since $Q = \Delta U + W$ we must have $W = Q$.

So, the work done by the gas in this case is equal to the energy $Q$ supplied to it.
