# PH0834

Figures #1 and #2 show 2 boxes each having 2 imaginary compartments of equal size inside: X and Y respectively. There are 6 molecules in total ($N = 6$).

The least likely probability of the number of molecules per compartment is shown in figure #1, with the total number of microstates being = 1.

The most likely probability of the number of molecules per compartment is shown in figure #2, with the total number of microstates being = 20.

What is the change in entropy from figure #1 to figure #2?

![image](images/image.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ 0

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $(1.38\times 10^{-23})\,(\ln{20})$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $(1.38\times 10^{-23})\,[(\ln{20})-1]$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $(1.38\times 10^{-23})\,(\ln{19})$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From the data booklet:

- $S = k_b \ln{\Omega}$

Initial entropy (figure #1):

- $S_i = k_b \ln{\Omega}_i$

- $S_i = 1.38\times10^{-23} (\ln{1})$

And (ln 1) = 0, therefore

- $S_i=  0$

Final entropy (figure #2):

- $S_i = k_b \ln{\Omega}_f$

- $S_f = 1.38\times 10^{-23}[\ln{20}]$

For change in entropy

- $\Delta S = S_f - S_i$

- $\Delta S = 1.38\times 10^{-23}[\ln{20}] - 0$

- $\Delta S = 1.38\times 10^{-23}[\ln{20}]$
