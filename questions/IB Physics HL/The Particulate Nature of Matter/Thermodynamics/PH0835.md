# PH0835

The engine in the diagram has $T_H$ of 600 K and $T_C$ of 400 K. From the 100 J of energy taken from the hot reservoir, 75 J are delivered to the cold reservoir.

![PH0835xxx](images/PH0835xxx.png)

Which of the following give the correct values for the efficiency of the engine and the efficiency that a Carnot engine would have when operating between these two reservoirs?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$Original engine efficiency$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$Carnot engine efficiency$\hspace{1.5em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| 25% | 50% |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 75%| 33% |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 25% | 33% |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 75% | 50% |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From the data booklet:

The engine in the diagram has an efficiency of:

- Efficiency $=\dfrac{\textrm{useful work out}}{\textrm{total energy in}}=\dfrac{25}{100}=25\%$

To determine the efficiency of the Carnot engine:

- Carnot Efficiency  $= 1-\dfrac{T_C}{T_H}=1-\dfrac{400}{600}=33\%$

In addition, we know that the actual efficiency cannot be greater than the Carnot efficiency. C is the only response that satisfies this condition.
