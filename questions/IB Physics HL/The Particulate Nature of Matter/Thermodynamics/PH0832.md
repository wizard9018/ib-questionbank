# PH0832

The graph shows a pressure-volume diagram for an ideal gas undergoing a cyclic process. The diagram shows two adiabatic curves, an isovolumetric and an isobaric process making up the loop DEFG. The arrows show the direction of the cycle.

![image](images/image.png)

Between which pair of letters is heat supplied to the gas ($Q_{in}$)?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ D and E

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ E and F

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ F and G

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ G and D

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From the data booklet:

- $Q=\Delta U+W$

There is no heat transferred along the adiabatic expansion EF and adiabatic compression GD.

Along DE, work is done by the gas, so $W > 0$, and $\Delta U > 0$ since the temperature at E must be higher than that at point D.

Hence $Q > 0$ and heat is supplied to the gas ($Q_{in}$).

Along FG, $W = 0$ (the volume does not change), and $\Delta U < 0$ because the temperature at G is lower than that at F.
Hence Q < 0 and heat is removed from the gas ($Q_{out}$).
