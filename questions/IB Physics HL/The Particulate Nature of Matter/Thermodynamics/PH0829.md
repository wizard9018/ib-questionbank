# PH0829

The $p-V$ diagram shows a gas expanding.

![PH0829xxxx](images/PH0829xxxx.png)
$\footnotesize{\textrm{[Source: Created with GeoGebra - https://www.geogebra.org/graphing?lang=en]}}$

How much work is done by the gas as it expands?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $120$ J

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $200$ J

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $400$ J

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $720$ J

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From the data booklet:

- $W=p\Delta V$

which is the area under the curve.

![PH0829xxxy](images/PH0829xxxy.png)
$\footnotesize{\textrm{[Source: Created with GeoGebra - https://www.geogebra.org/graphing?lang=en]}}$

Using the area of the trapezoid formula:

- $A = \bigg(\dfrac{b_1+b_2}{2}\bigg)h$

- $W=\bigg(\dfrac{(1.4+2.6)\times 10^5}{2}\bigg)( 2.0\times 10^{-3})$

- $W=(2.0\times 10^5)(2.0 \times 10^{-3})$

- $W=400$ J
