# PH0826

An ideal gas of volume $100\,\ce{cm^3}$ is trapped by a piston with a cross-sectional area of $10\, \ce{cm^2}$ in a cylinder as shown in the diagram. There is negligible friction between the piston and the cylinder. The initial external atmospheric pressure acting on the piston is 100 kPa.

The gas expands slowly when heat is supplied by an electric heater inside the cylinder.

![image](images/image.png)

How far does the piston move if the gas does 5.0 J of work?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ 0.20 cm

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ 0.50 cm

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ 2.0 cm

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ 5.0 cm

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From the data booklet:

- $W=p\Delta V$

and Work done = Force x distance moved:

- $W=F\Delta x$

and Force = Pressure x Area

- $W=pA\Delta x$

Area of the piston $=\dfrac{100}{10}=10\,\ce{cm^2}=10\times 10^{-4}\, \ce{m}^2$

The pressure remains constant, substituting all values into the equation gives

- $5.0=(100\times 10^3)(10\times 10^{-4})(\Delta x)$

- $\Delta x=\dfrac{5.0}{100}=0.05\,\ce{m}=5.0$ cm
