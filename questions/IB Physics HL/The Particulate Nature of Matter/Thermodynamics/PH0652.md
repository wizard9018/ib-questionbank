1. A Carnot Cycle heat engine operates between temperatures of 700 K and 300 K. The monatomic gas inside the engine undergoes four thermal processes:

    $A \rightarrow B$ isothermal expansion

    $B \rightarrow C$ adiabatic expansion

    $C \rightarrow D$ isothermal compression

    $D \rightarrow A$ adiabatic compression.

2. At the beginning of the adiabatic expansion, the volume of the gas is $4.5 \times {10}^{-2}\ \ce{m}^3$. During the expansion, the pressure of gas decreased from  $9.2\times{10}^5\ $Pa to $1.1\times{10}^5\ $Pa. Calculate the volume following this process. [2 marks]

3. Determine the efficiency of the heat engine. [2 marks]

4. In the adiabatic process, there is no heat transferred into or out of the system. Outline how adiabatic processes must be performed. [1 mark]

    Correct direction of arrows $\checkmark$

    Two adiabatic processes and two isothermal processes $\checkmark$

    The adiabatic processes have greater slopes than the isothermal processes $\checkmark$

2. ::indent
    - $(9.2\times{10}^5){(4.5\times{10}^{-2})}^\frac{5}{3}=(1.1\times{10}^5)\ {V_2}^\frac{5}{3}$ $\checkmark$

    - $V_2= $ :answer[$1.6\times{10}^{-1}\ \ce{m}^3$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    During the adiabatic process pressure and volume are related as the following:

    $\hspace{1em}$ $pV^\frac{5}{3}= $ Constant

    So:

    $\hspace{1em}$ $p_1{V_1}^\frac{5}{3}=p_2{V_2}^\frac{5}{3}$

    $\hspace{1em}$ $(9.2\times{10}^5){(4.5\times{10}^{-2})}^\frac{5}{3}=(1.1\times{10}^5)\ {V_2}^\frac{5}{3}$

    $\hspace{1em}$ $V_2=1.6\times{10}^{-1}\ \ce{m}^3$

3. ::indent
    - $\eta=1-\dfrac{300}{700}$ $\checkmark$

    - $\hspace{1em}$ = :answer[0.57] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
The efficiency $\eta$ of the heat engine in the Carnot cycle can be found by the following formula:

    - $\eta=1-\dfrac{T_c}{T_h}=1-\dfrac{300}{700}=0.57$

4. Compression or expansion should be done quickly $\checkmark$

    **OR**

    system should be isolated from the surrounding $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
To eliminate heat transfer between the system and the surroundings, the compression and expansion should be done very quickly or the system should be insulated.
