# PH0746

An isothermal change of a gas at $63.0\degree$C occurs by transferring $3360\, $J of heat energy to the surrounding medium.

What is the change of entropy of the gas during the process?

- A. $\hspace{1em}      -53.3 \, \ce{J K}^{-1}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}      -10.0 \, \ce{J K}^{-1}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  10.0 \, \ce{J K}^{-1}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  53.3
\, \ce{J K}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The entropy is given by

- $S=\dfrac{\Delta Q}{T}$

Since the heat energy is leaving the gas

Then

- $S=-\dfrac{3360}{63+273}=-\dfrac{3360}{336}$

- $\hspace{1em}=-10\, \ce{J K}^{-1}$
