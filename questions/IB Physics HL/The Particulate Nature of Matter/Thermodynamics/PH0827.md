# PH0827

An ideal gas expands adiabatically from an initial state to a new volume 8 times larger.

What will be the ratio of the final pressure to the initial pressure $p_2:p_1$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ 1:64

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ 1:32

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ 1:16

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ 1:8

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From the databooklet:

$\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1em} pV^{\frac{5}{3}} &= constant \\
\hspace{1em}\\
\hspace{1em} p_1(V_1)^{\frac{5}{3}} &= p_2(V_2)^{\frac{5}{3}}
\hspace{1em}\\
\end{aligned}$

and $V_2= 8 V_1$

- $p_1(V_1)^{\frac{5}{3}} = p_2(8V_1)^{\frac{5}{3}}$

- $\dfrac{p_1(V_1)^{\frac{5}{3}}}{(8.0V_1)^{\frac{5}{3}}}=p_2$

- $\Bigl(\dfrac{p_1}{\sqrt[3]{8^5}}\Bigr) = p_2$

- $p_1 \Bigl(\dfrac{1}{32}\Bigr) = p_2$

- $\dfrac{p_2}{p_1}=\dfrac{1}{32}$
