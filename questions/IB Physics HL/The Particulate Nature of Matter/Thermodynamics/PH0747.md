# PH0747

An engine operates at hot reservoir temperature of 500 K and cold reservoir temperature of 300 K. The output work of the engine is 100 J and its efficiency is half that of a Carnot engine operating at the same temperatures.

What is the quantity of heat supplied by the hot reservoir?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  20\ $J

- B. $\hspace{1em}  200\ $J

- C. $\hspace{1em}  333\ $J

- D. $\hspace{1em}  500\, $J

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The efficiency of a Carnot engine is given by

- $\eta_{Carnot}=1-\dfrac{T_C}{T_H}$

- $\eta_{Carnot}=1-\dfrac{300}{500}=\dfrac{2}{5}$

The efficiency of the engine is given by

- $\eta_{engine}=\dfrac{W}{Q_H}$

Since the efficiency of the engine is half that of the Carnot engine

- $\eta_{engine}=\dfrac{1}{2}\eta_{Carnot}$

Then

- $\dfrac{W}{Q_H}=\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(\dfrac{2}{5}\right)$

- $\dfrac{100}{Q_H}=\dfrac{1}{5}$

- $Q_H=500\ $ J
