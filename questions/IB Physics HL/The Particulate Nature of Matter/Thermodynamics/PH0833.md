# PH0833

The unit of entropy is:

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ J K

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\ce{J^{-1}}\,\ce{K}^{-1}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\ce{J}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\ce{J\,K^{-1}}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From the data booklet:

- $\Delta S=\dfrac{\Delta Q}{T}$

where,

$\Delta Q =$ required heat per mol (Joules)

$T=$ constant absolute temperature (Kelvin).

- $S= \text{Joules} \times  \dfrac{1}{\text{Kelvin}}$

Unit of entropy is $\ce{J\,K^{-1}}$.
