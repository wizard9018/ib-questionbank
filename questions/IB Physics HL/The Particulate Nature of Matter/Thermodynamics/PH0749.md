# PH0749

The work done when 1 mole of a gas expands through an adiabatic process is 120 J.

What is the change in temperature of the gas?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$  -9.6 K

- B. $\hspace{1em}$  Zero

- C. $\hspace{1em} $ 9.6 K

- D. $\hspace{1em}$  120 K

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

For an adiabatic process

- $Q=0$

The work done by the gas when expanding is positive

From the first law of thermodynamics

- $Q=\Delta U+W$

Then

- $\Delta U=-W=-120$ J

Since

- $∆U=\dfrac{3}{2}nR\Delta T$

Then

- $-120=(\dfrac{3}{2})(8.31)\Delta T$

- $\Delta T=-9.6$ K
