# PH0744

Heat engine $X$ takes heat energy $Q_H$ from the hot reservoir to deliver work $W$ and reject heat energy $Q_C$ in the cold reservoir. Another heat engine $Y$ takes heat energy $5Q_H$ to deliver $2W$ and reject $7Q_C$.

What is the efficiency of engine $Y$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  3.6 \% $

- B. $\hspace{1em}  16 \%$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  22.2 \%$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  40 \%$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The efficiency of  an engine is given by

- $\eta=\dfrac{useful\ work}{input\ energy}$

For engine $Y$

Work done = $2W$

Input energy = $5Q_H$

- $\eta_Y=\dfrac{2W}{5Q_H}$

For engine $X$

- $W=Q_H-Q_C$

Then

- $Q_C=Q_H-W$

For engine $Y$

- $2W=5Q_H-7Q_C$

- $2W=5Q_H-7\left(Q_H-W\right)$

- $2W=5Q_H-7Q_H+7W$

- $5W=2Q_H$

- $\dfrac{W}{Q_H}=\dfrac{2}{5}$

Then

- $\eta_Y=\dfrac{2W}{5Q_H}$

- $\eta_Y=\left(\dfrac{2}{5}\right)\dfrac{W}{Q_H}$

- $\hspace{1.3em}=\left(\dfrac{2}{5}\right)\left(\dfrac{2}{5}\right)=\dfrac{4}{25}=16\%$

Alternative solution

- $\eta=1-\frac{Q_C}{Q_H}$

- $\eta_Y=1-\frac{7Q_C}{{5Q}_H}$

For engine $X$

- $W=Q_H-Q_C$

For engine $Y$

- $2W=5Q_H-7Q_C$

For engine $Y$

- $2(Q_H-Q_C)=5Q_H-7Q_C$

- ${2Q}_H-2Q_C=5Q_H-7Q_C$

- $3Q_H=5Q_C$

- $\dfrac{Q_C}{Q_H}=\dfrac{3}{5}$

Then

- $\eta_Y=1-\dfrac{7Q_C}{{5Q}_H}$

- $\eta_Y=1-\left(\dfrac{7}{5}\right)\dfrac{Q_C}{Q_H}$

- $\hspace{1.4em}=1-\left(\dfrac{7}{5}\right)\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)=1-\dfrac{21}{25}=\dfrac{4}{25}=16\%$
