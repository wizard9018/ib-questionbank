# PS0235

In the study by Mueller and Oppenheimer (2014) on typewriting vs. handwriting notes, the results of the study supported __________.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ The positive effects of technology

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ The negative effects of technology
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Technology does not influence cognition in any way
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ The effect of technology depends on the way it is used

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The results of the study by Mueller and Oppenheimer indicated that students who took notes by hand did significantly better in their comprehension skills than those who took notes with a laptop. This shows a negative effect of technology on cognition.

The other options are incorrect as the results of the study did not support it.

Mueller, P., & Oppenheimer, D. M. (2014). The pen is mightier than the keyboard. *Psychological Science*, *25(6)*, 1159–1168. https://doi.org/10.1177/095679761452458
