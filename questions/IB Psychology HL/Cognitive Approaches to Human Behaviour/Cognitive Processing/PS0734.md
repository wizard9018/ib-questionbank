# PS0734

Describe the working memory model's concepts of the phonological store and the articulatory process.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\

The phonological store is often referred to as the inner ear. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) It listens to external sounds and then stores them temporarily. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) Allowing auditory information to be briefly retained before being processed further or forgotten. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) Whereas the articulatory process is often referred to as the inner voice. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) The articulatory process allows you to mentally/internally rehearse words and information. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) This allows you to maintain auditory information in working memory.(:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

$\underline{\textrm{Marking Guidance}}$:\

Similarly worded accurate statements should also be awarded marks. A maximum of **two (2) marks** should be awarded for the description of the phonological store. A maximum of **two (2) marks** should be awarded for the description of the articulatory process.
