# PS0307

Explain how one research method is used in studying emotion and cognition.
[9 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\

A maximum of **9 marks** are awarded if the responses include all of the following:

$\textcolor{#000000}{\textbf{1. Direct Answer}\ [\textbf{DA}]}$\

A direct answer to the question is provided in the first few sentences (*e.g. “One research method used in studying the processes involved in emotion and cognition are questionnaires. This method collects data by asking individuals a set of standardised questions, often in written form.” / “This answer will focus on the questionnaire method. It is a common method to obtain data from participants where the questions can be open*$\text{\textemdash}$*ended or not.” / One study that used this method is Neisser & Harsch (1992) who examined participants’ memories of the Challenger Space Shuttle explosion.*)

$\textcolor{#F04438}{\textbf{2. Key Terms}\ [\textbf{KT}]}$\

Accurate knowledge and understanding of research methods. The response is focused on the command term ‘explain’, which asks to give a detailed account, including reasons and causes of one research method in studying emotion & cognition. (*e.g. Define and explain the research method in focus such as interviews, experiments, questionnaires, case studies, observation etc. / “A questionnaire is a method used to obtain data from participants where the questions can be open*$\text{\textemdash}$*ended or close*$\text{\textemdash}$*ended.” / “The experiment method is commonly used to study the relationship between the IV and DV. It helps to establish a cause*$\text{\textemdash}$*and*$\text{\textemdash}$*effect relationship between the two variables”*).

$\textcolor{#008c3a}{\textbf{3. Study}\ [\textbf{S}]}$\

A clear focus on describing one study – including the aim, procedure, results, and conclusion, where the study’s aim is linked to research methods on the topic of emotion & cognition or the effect of emotion on cognitive processes.\

*Relevant studies include but are not limited to:*

- Using questionnaires, Neisser and Harsch (1992) investigated the role of flashbulb memory.

- Talarico and Rubin (2003) studied flashbulb memory and its effect on emotions and its consistency through questionnaires 

- Sharot et al.’s (2004) quasi$\text{\textemdash}$experiment investigated the role of biological factors on flashbulb memories.

$\textcolor{#4472C4}{\textbf{4. Link}\ [\textbf{L}]}$\

An explicit link connecting the results of the study to the topic (e.g. *With the help of the questionnaire method, all participants were able to give data at the same time and the results from both questionnaires were compared for a better understanding of the recall accuracy in case of flashbulb memory theory.*).

[9 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Researchers use a variety of methods to obtain and analyse data. There are two main types of research, namely qualitative research such as interviews and case studies, and quantitative research, such as experiments, correlational research, and survey questionnaires.$\textcolor{#F04438}{[\textbf{KT}]}$

This answer will focus on the questionnaire method.$\textcolor{#000000}{[\textbf{DA}]}$ It is a common method to obtain data from participants where the questions can be open or closed$\text{\textemdash}$ended. It is a self$\text{\textemdash}$report technique that is cost$\text{\textemdash}$effective as it can be administered to multiple participants at the same time.$\textcolor{#F04438}{[\textbf{KT}]}$ One study that used this method is Neisser & Harsch (1992) who examined participants’ memories of the Challenger Space Shuttle explosion.$\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$  Questionnaire was given to 106 participants the day after the incident asking them about the circumstances when they heard the news. They had to fill a similar questionnaire after the incident and another 3 years after the incident. They were also asked how accurate their memories were on a scale of 1 to 5.$\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$ 

The results of the study showed that only 11 out of 44 participants remembered to have filled this questionnaire before. The consistency of the responses was very low. Participants correctly answered only 2.95 questions out of 7 (42%). $\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$ 

With the help of the questionnaire method, all participants were able to give data at the same time and the results from both questionnaires was compared for better understanding of the recall accuracy in case of flashbulb memory theory.$\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$ $\textcolor{#4472C4}{[\textbf{L}]}$
