# PS0819

Define anchoring bias and explain how it can lead to errors in thinking and decision-making.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Anchoring bias occurs when an individual relies too heavily on the first piece of information presented to them  when making judgements. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) The initial information acts as a reference point, forming the basis of the decision-making process, (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) even if the initial information is misleading, inaccurate, or not significant. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) The relevance or importance of the initial piece of information is not necessarily significant; it is the placement of the information at initial introduction that helps it become a framework around which understanding is built.  (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
