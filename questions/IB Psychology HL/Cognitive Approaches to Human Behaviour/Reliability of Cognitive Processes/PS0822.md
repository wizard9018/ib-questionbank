# PS0822

Explain two potential causes of, or reasons for anchoring bias.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Anchoring bias might be caused by selective attention. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) When focusing on the anchor, people might selectively attend to this information and ignore other information. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

Anchoring bias might be caused by using system 1 thinking, which often relies on heuristics (mental shortcuts).  (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) Using the first piece of information as a starting point for providing an estimate or making a decision can act as a mental shortcut, which aims to speed up the decision-making process. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
