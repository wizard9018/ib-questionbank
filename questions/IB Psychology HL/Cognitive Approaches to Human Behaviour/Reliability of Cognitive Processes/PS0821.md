# PS0821

Explain one reason why it might be difficult to avoid anchoring bias.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Anchoring bias is considered to be an unconscious bias. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) As such, it is difficult to avoid something that you are not aware is impacting your decision-making processes. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
