# PS0815

Define confirmation bias and explain how it can lead to errors in thinking and decision-making. 

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Confirmation bias is a cognitive bias that causes us to actively seek out or search for evidence or examples that confirm our pre-existing beliefs or ideas. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) It also involves a failure to search for contradictory evidence. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) In doing so, our currently held beliefs are strengthened, even if these beliefs are false. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) This usually occurs as a shortcut in the thinking process, which reduces mental effort, but can lead to skewed perspectives and faulty decision-making. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
