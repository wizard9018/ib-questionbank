# PS0818

Explain two factors that might contribute to confirmation bias.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Cognitive effort may be a factor that contributes to confirmation bias. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)  People may avoid seeking out contradictory evidence, as this process requires greater mental effort. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

Emotional attachment may also contribute the confirmation bias. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) If people are emotionally invested in a particular belief or point of view they will have less desire to seek out contradictory evidence. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
