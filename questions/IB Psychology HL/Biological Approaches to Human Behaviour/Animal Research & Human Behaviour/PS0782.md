# PS0782

Describe the procedure of Rogers & Kesner's (2003) study on Acetylcholine and memory.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Rogers & Kesner (2003) had 30 rats learn a maze with food placed in one corner. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) The rats were then randomly assigned to receive either scopolamine (which blocks acetylcholine receptors) or a saline solution (placebo) ten minutes before running the maze. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) They measured memory by counting the number of errors in the first and last five trials of Day 1 for encoding and the first five trials of Day 2 for retrieval. Rats who received scopolamine took longer and made more errors in learning the maze. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

- 1 mark for explaining the maze learning procedure and the rat groups.

- 1 mark for describing the injections and their purpose.

- 1 mark for explaining the memory assessment and findings.
