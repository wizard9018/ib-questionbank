# PS0809

The use of animal research must be justified, meaning that the potential benefits of the research must outweigh the harm caused to the animals. Researchers must demonstrate that the knowledge gained from the study cannot be obtained without using animals. 

Identify and briefly explain three ethical guidelines that researchers must follow when conducting animal research. 

[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

**Replacement**\

Researchers must consider alternatives to animal testing whenever possible, such as using in vitro (cell culture) or computational models. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) If animals are used, alternatives should be prioritized to reduce the number of animals involved. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

**Refinement**\

Researchers should refine their experimental procedures (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) to reduce the suffering and distress animals experience. This includes providing proper care and minimising any distress caused by procedures. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

**Reduction**\

Reducing the number of animals used minimizes harm by ensuring only the essential sample size is used. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) This helps avoid unnecessary suffering while still providing valid results, balancing ethics with scientific rigor. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

**Ethical Review Process**\

Any research involving animals must be approved by an ethics committee (e.g., an Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee). (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) This committee assesses the scientific value and ethical aspects of the research to ensure compliance with ethical standards. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

$\underline{\textrm{Marking Guidance}}$:

- 1 mark for identifying the ethical guideline (e.g. replacement, refinement, reduction, ethical review).

- 1 mark for explaining why the researcher must follow the guidelines (e.g. reduce animal suffering, ensuring proper care is maintained).

- Marks should be awarded for any similarly worded answer to a maximum of 6 marks.
