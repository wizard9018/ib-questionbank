# PS0778

Identify the two groups in Meaney et al.'s (1988) study and explain their treatment conditions. 

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

The treatment group consisted of rats handled daily by researchers from birth to weaning, with 15 minutes of grooming to simulate maternal care. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) The control group was taken away from their mothers but not handled by the researchers, serving as the baseline condition. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

$\underline{\textrm{Marking Guidance}}$:

- 1 mark for describing the treatment groups and their specific handling.

- 1 mark for describing the control group and their lack of handling.

- Marks should be awarded for any similarly worded explanations to a maximum of 2 marks.

Reference/s:\

Meaney, M. J., Aitken, D. H., van Berkel, C., Bhatnagar, S., & Sapolsky, R. M. (1988). Effect of neonatal handling on age-related impairments associated with the hippocampus.
