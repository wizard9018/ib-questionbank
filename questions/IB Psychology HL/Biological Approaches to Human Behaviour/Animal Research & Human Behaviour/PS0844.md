# PS0844

Mice and rats constitute about 95% of all animals used in laboratories, with mice being the most frequently used species in research. There are several reasons for their widespread use in psychological research. 

Explain two reasons why mice are predominantly used in research. 

[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

**Genetic Similarity to Humans**\

Mice share many genetic, physiological, and behavioral similarities with humans. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) This makes them ideal models for studying human diseases, brain functions, and psychological processes, such as memory and stress. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) 

**Small Size and Manageability**\

Mice are small and easy to handle in a lab setting. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) Their size makes them less expensive to house and care for, and they require less space compared to larger animals. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) 

**Short Lifespan and Rapid Reproduction**\

Mice have short lifespans (2-3 years) and reproduce quickly. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) This allows researchers to observe changes across generations in a short period, making them useful for studying aging, development, and the long-term effects of stress or treatment. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

**Established research models**\

Mice are widely studied, with extensive knowledge of their biology. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) This makes it easier to design experiments, manipulate genetics, and compare results across studies, making them versatile for researching various psychological and medical conditions. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) 

**Ethical and practical considerations**\

Mice are considered ethically acceptable for many types of research. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) They are seen as having lower moral status compared to higher-order animals like primates, making their use more widely accepted in research, though ethical guidelines still apply. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) 

**Availability and cost**\

Mice are easy to obtain and inexpensive to maintain. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) Their widespread availability in labs and low cost make them practical choices for a wide range of experiments. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
