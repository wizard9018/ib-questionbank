# PS0783

How did Rogers & Kesner (2003) ensure that confounding variables did not influence the results of their study?
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Rogers & Kesner controlled for confounding variables by using a placebo group (saline injection) to rule out the possibility that the injection itself was responsible for changes in memory. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) Additionally, the injections were made directly into the hippocampus, ensuring that the effects were related to acetylcholine and not other factors. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

Marking Guidance:

- 1 mark for mentioning the placebo condition.

- 1 mark for explaining how the injection into the hippocampus controls for confounding variables.

- Marks should be awarded for any similarly worded explanations to a maximum of 3 marks.
