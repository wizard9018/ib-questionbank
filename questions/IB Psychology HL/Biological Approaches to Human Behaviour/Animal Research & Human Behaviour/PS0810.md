# PS0810

Explain two advantages of using animals with shorter lifespans in research on the effects of early-life stress.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

**Observe the long term effects**\

First, animals with shorter lifespans allow researchers to observe the long-term effects of early-life stress over a much shorter period of time, which is crucial when studying developmental or aging-related changes. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) For example, researchers can track the effects of childhood stress on aging rats and see how it impacts their cognitive function and physical health as they grow older. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) 

**Genetic inheritance**\

Second, the use of animals that breed quickly enables researchers to study the genetic transmission of stress effects across generations, providing insights into how environmental factors influence genetics. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) This allows for a better understanding of the hereditary and biological factors that might contribute to stress-related disorders in humans. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

$\underline{\textrm{Marking Guidance}}$:

- 1 mark for identifying the advantage of using animals (e.g. long term effects, genetic inheritance). 

- 1 mark for explaining how this advantage helps in research on early-life stress (e.g. allows researchers to track the effects of childhood stress).

- Marks will also be awarded for any similarly worded explanations to a maximum of 4 marks.
