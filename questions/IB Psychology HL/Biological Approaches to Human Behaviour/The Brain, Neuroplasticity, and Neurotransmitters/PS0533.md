# PS0533

Explain localisation of brain function.
[9 marks]

---

A direct answer to the question provided in the first few sentences (e.g. *“Cognitive functions are localized in specific areas of the brain as it helps to improve brain processing while allowing brain plasticity….”)*

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \

Researchers have been studying the brain since earlier times to gain a better understanding of how it works. One of the emerging theories that aids in this is the localization of brain function. This refers to the idea that different areas of the brain are responsible for different functions $\textcolor{#000000}{[\textbf{DA}]}$. In addition, there has been evidence suggesting that functions of one area of the brain can be overtaken by another in case of trauma, injury or illness further supporting the theory of localization. One of the most important functions of the brain include language processing and language centres in the brain are located in the left hemisphere near to the frontal lobe $\textcolor{#F04438}{[\textbf{KT}]}$. One study that supports localization of the language function in the brain is by Tiernay et. al (2001). $\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$

Tiernay et. al conducted a case study on patient MA to study the language centres of the brain. MA had a lesion on his left frontal lobe in infancy but had grown to become fluent in the spoken language as well as the sign language as his parents were deaf. $\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$  PET scan imaging was used to compare the brain areas of MA to 12 control participants who were also fluent in sign and spoken language. $\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$  The participants were asked to recount an incident while being scanned. Results from the scans revealed that the left hemisphere was primarily more active compared to other brain regions for all the control participants. However, it was interesting to note that for patient MA the right hemisphere was more active indicating that this part of the brain had overtaken the language functions due to the injury on the left hemisphere. $\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$  Therefore, it can be concluded that normally it is the left hemisphere of the brain which is responsible for language functions, however in MA's case, it was on the right due to his childhood injury. $\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$

The results of this study give evidence to the theory of localization and explains how it occurs as all the control participants had an active left hemisphere during the language tasks but MA had an active right hemisphere due to his injury. $\textcolor{#4472C4}{[\textbf{L}]}$  Therefore, we can understand that different brain areas are associated with specific cognitive functions but this is not fixed and one area can overtake another area's functions as seen in MA. As this was a case study, it was also noted that MA had reduced visuo-spatial abilities - functions which are associated with the right hemisphere. This gives further explanation to localization as when the right hemisphere overtook the tasks of the left hemisphere, its own functions were somewhat compromised helping us to understand better how localization of brain function works. $\textcolor{#4472C4}{[\textbf{L}]}$
