# PS0512

Outline the role of one gene on human behaviour using a relevant study.
[9 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

A maximum of **9 marks** are awarded if the response includes all of the following:

$\textcolor{#000000}{\textbf{1. Direct Answer}\ [\textbf{DA}]}$\

The response should have a direct answer to the question within the first few lines.

*(e.g.  One gene that may influence human behaviour is the 5HTT gene……*). As the question clearly refers to the role of genes on human behaviour, it should be identified and referred to in the first few lines. A specific gene affecting a behaviour which is being investigated through the study must be mentioned, however as genetics is a vast area of study with different techniques used, it must be clearly mentioned how research on this gene is conducted. (*e.g. With the advent of new scientific methods, techniques such as gene mapping have advanced to a significant extent helping scientists to…*)

$\textcolor{#F04438}{\textbf{2. Key Terms}\ [\textbf{KT}]}$\

There should be accurate knowledge and understanding of the topic by using all or some key terms, such as:

-    Genes - Genes are made up of DNA and are the functional unit of heredity which determines factors affecting your physical and personality features. 

-    Genetic Inheritance - a process by which genetic information is passed through genes from parent to child.

-    Gene mapping - the process of understanding the position of each gene on the chromosomes

-    Epigenetics - an area of study that looks at how the interaction between genetics and environment determines the expression of genes.

The command term outline seeks to provide a summary or a brief account. Therefore, while the explanation of the effect of genes on human behaviour is essential, there should also be a focus on meeting the command term. There should be a clear summary of how the genetic influence occurs with sufficient details.

$\textcolor{#008c3a}{\textbf{3. Study}\ [\textbf{S}]}$\

A clear focus on describing one study - including the aim, procedure, results, and conclusion. *Relevant studies include but are not limited to:*

-    Caspi et. al (2009) on the role of epigenetics on major depressive disorder. 

-    Sorensen et.al  (1992) on the role of genes in obesity 

-    Bouchard et. al (1990) on the role of genetics on intelligence 

$\textcolor{#4472C4}{\textbf{4. Link}\ [\textbf{L}]}$\

There should be a clear link between the study results and the topic using one of the key terms mentioned in the introduction. (*e.g. This shows a gene-environment interaction, which suggests that the short 5HTT allele, when combined with stressful life events, may influence behaviour, in this case, depression*).

**Marking limits:**

-    Focussing on the biological aspect of genetics and not its influence on behaviour. (*a maximum of 2 marks may be deducted*)

-    A relevant study is described, but no clear links to the effect of genetics on behaviour are made as it can be tricky to make explicit links. (*a maximum of 5 marks will be awarded*)

-    No explanation of the method used to study genetics in the study cited. (*a maximum of 2 marks may be deducted*)

-    If the question is on $`$one' gene, then care should be taken to ensure that only one gene, such as the 5HTT gene as identified by Caspi, is used (*a maximum of 2 marks may be deducted*).

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \

One gene that may influence human behaviour is the 5HTT gene, also known as the serotonin transport gene, which is believed to influence depression. $\textcolor{#000000}{[\textbf{DA}]}$

 Genes are inherited from our parents and are considered the basic building blocks of a person. Genes contain DNA and tell our cells what to do and how to express different traits. These traits can affect our body's functions and even influence our behaviour. Depression is a mental health condition that can cause persistent feelings of sadness and loss of interest in things that used to bring joy. $\textcolor{#C34A2C}{[\textbf{KT}]}$

The 5HTT gene, also known as the serotonin transport gene regulates serotonin levels, and serotonin is found to be crucial for mood regulation. Alterations in serotonin levels or functioning have been implicated in research on depression. Specifically, mutations in the 5HTT gene, such as short alleles, may affect serotonin reuptake.$\textcolor{#C34A2C}{[\textbf{KT}]}$

This was studied by Caspi & colleagues in 2003. They wanted to see if the 5HTT gene plays a role in depression.$\textcolor{#056608}{[\textbf{S}]}$ They studied 800-plus individuals and split them into groups according to their allele types (short 5HTT alleles vs long 5HTT alleles vs long & short alleles combined.$\textcolor{#056608}{[\textbf{S}]}$ They assessed their level of depression regarding stressful life events and found that the participants with shorter 5HTT alleles encountered more stress and had a higher vulnerability to developing depression. This suggests short 5HTT gene alleles may predispose individuals to be vulnerable to depression under adverse conditions. $\textcolor{#056608}{[\textbf{S}]}$ $\textcolor{#1974D2}{[\textbf{L}]}$

This study highlights a gene-environment interaction, which suggests that the short 5HTT allele, when combined with stressful life events, may influence behaviour, in this case, depression. $\textcolor{#1974D2}{[\textbf{L}]}$

This shows the importance of looking at genetic influences and environmental factors when analysing behaviour.
