# PS0305

Explain the effect of one pheromone on one human behaviour.

[9 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\

A maximum of **9 marks** are awarded if the responses include all of the following:

$\textcolor{#000000}{\textbf{1. Direct Answer}\ [\textbf{DA}]}$\

The first few sentences provide a direct answer to the question (*e.g. “Pheromones can influence human behaviour by triggering subconscious responses such as attraction, arousal, or social bonding.” / “Pheromones can impact human behaviour by subtly influencing attraction and mate selection through subconscious chemical signals.”*)

$\textcolor{#F04438}{\textbf{2. Key Terms}\ [\textbf{KT}]}$\

Accurate knowledge and understanding of what pheromones are and how they affect human behaviour by linking pheromones to a specific behaviour. The answer is focused on the command term **‘explain’**, which seeks to give a detailed account including reasons and causes related to *pheromones*. (e.g. *Definition of pheromones, the role of pheromones to any human behaviour such as attraction*).

Examples of some chemicals linked to pheromones are MHC or major histocompatibility complex (linked to mate selection, body odour preferences & immune system response), AND or androstadienone (*linked to elevated mood & arousal, attention to emotional cues*) & estratetraenol (*linked to attraction, trust, and social bonding*). It is best to add information on the ambiguous nature of pheromones.

$\textcolor{#008c3a}{\textbf{3. Study}\ [\textbf{S}]}$\

A clear focus on describing one study – including the aim, procedure, results, and conclusion, where the study’s aim is linked to pheromones.\

*Relevant studies include but are not limited to:*

- McCoy & Pitino (2002) study on whether synthesised female pheromones increase sociosexual behaviour of women.

- Lundstrom & Olsson (2005) studied the effects of androstadienone and its effect on women’s mood.

- Cutler, Friedman, & McCoy’s (1998) research focused on investigating whether synthesised male pheromones increase sociosexual behaviour of men. 

- Wedekind’s (1995) research on the effect of MHC and the role it plays in mate selection in humans.

$\textcolor{#4472C4}{\textbf{4. Link}\ [\textbf{L}]}$\

An explicit link connecting the results of the study described to pheromones (*e.g. ‘The results of the research showed that the androstadienone increased women’s mood in the presence of a male experimenter and it had no effect when the experimenter was a female. The research clearly shows that androstadienone as a pheromone intensifies women’s reactions to men and also signals sexual attractiveness towards the opposite gender.’*)

[9 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Pheromones are chemical messengers that carry stimulation and play an important role in mating behaviour for mammals.$\textcolor{#000000}{[\textbf{DA}]}$ There are still debates about the existence of pheromones in human behaviour with supporting and non supporting evidence.$\textcolor{#F04438}{[\textbf{KT}]}$ Many pheromones usually have a distinct smell. Mammals have a separate structure called the vomeronasal organ and is located in the anterior nasal cavity. This organ is missing in most humans and hence the debate with the existence of pheromones. $\textcolor{#F04438}{[\textbf{KT}]}$ One example of a chemical associated with pheromones is AND or androstadienon.$\textcolor{#000000}{[\textbf{DA}]}$ 

One study conducted by Lundstrom and Olsson (2005) studied the effects of androstadienone.$\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$ Androstadienone is a chemical component of sweat and a derivative of testosterone.$\textcolor{#F04438}{[\textbf{KT}]}$  The participants were 37 females who were exposed to two conditions, condition 1 being exposed to androstadienone and condition 2 being a control condition. In both conditions the participants were in front of a male or a female experimenter. The experimenters used questionnaires to measure the mood of the participants.$\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$ 

The results of the research showed that the androstadienone increased women’s mood in the presence of a male experimenter and it had no effect when the experimenter was a female.$\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$ 

The research clearly shows that androstadienone as a pheromone intensifies women’s reactions to men and also signals sexual attractiveness towards the opposite gender.$\textcolor{#4472C4}{[\textbf{L}]}$
