# PH1005

**Maximum mark: 7**

A roller coaster car with a mass of 80 kg moves on the frictionless track shown in the diagram starting from point A.

![PH1005_a](images/PH1005_a.png)
$\footnotesize{\textrm{[Source: Image created using Images by OpenClipart-Vectors from Pixabay - https:// pixabay.com]}}$

At points B and C, the track approximates a circular path so that the radii at B and C are 12 m and 18 m respectively.

1. Compare the normal force exerted by the track on the car at points B and C. [2 marks]

2. Draw and label the free body diagram of the car at point C. [2 marks]

![Screenshot 2025-07-04 at 1.08.24 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-04 at 1.08.24 PM.png>)

3. Calculate the maximum speed of the car at point C so that it can remain on the track. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. $N_B=\dfrac{mv^2}{r_B}+mg$ **AND** $N_C=mg-\dfrac{mv^2}{r_C}$ $\checkmark$

    The normal force at B is greater than the normal force at C. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    Resolving the forces at B

    - $\dfrac{mv^2}{r_B}=N_B-mg$

    - $N_B=mg +\dfrac{mv^2}{r_B}$

    While at C,

    - $\dfrac{mv^2}{r_C}=mg-N_C$

    - $N_C=mg-\dfrac{mv^2}{r_C}$

    The normal force at B is greater than the normal force at C.

2.  :::center
    ![Screenshot 2025-07-04 at 1.11.27 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-04 at 1.11.27 PM.png>)

    Upward normal force **AND** downward gravitational force. $\checkmark$

    The downward arrow is bigger than the upward arrow. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
    Because the car is at the top of a curve, the centripetal acceleration must be towards the centre which is in the downwards direction. The acceleration must also therefore be downwards, so the gravitational force must be greater than the upwards normal force.

3. The maximum speed is when the normal force is zero. $\checkmark$

    - $v=\ll\sqrt{r_cg}\gg=\sqrt{\left(18\right)(9.81)}$ $\checkmark$

    - $v=13\  \textrm{m s}^{-1}\ \checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    The maximum speed at point C is when the normal force of the track on the car is zero.

    The normal force at C

    - $F_N=mg-\dfrac{mv^2}{r_c}$

    When $F_N = 0$

    - $mg=\dfrac{mv^2}{r_c}$

    Using the given value of $r=18$ m

    - $v=\sqrt{r_cg}=\sqrt{(18)(9.81)}=13.29\textrm{ m s}^{-1}$
