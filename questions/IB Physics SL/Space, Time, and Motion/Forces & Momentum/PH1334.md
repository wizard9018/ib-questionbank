# PH1334

Two balls $A$ and $B$ are moving towards each other along the same line. Ball A is moving rightwards with a speed of 9 m s$^{-1}$ and ball B is moving leftwards with a speed of 2 m s$^{-1}$.

![Screenshot 2025-06-04 at 7.11.49 AM](<images/Screenshot 2025-06-04 at 7.11.49 AM.png>)

They collide elastically and after the collision ball $A$ rebounds to the left with a speed of 12 m s$^{-1}$.

What is the speed and direction of ball $B$ after the collision?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $5$ m s$^{-1}$ to the left

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $1$ m s$^{-1}$ to the left

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $1$ m s$^{-1}$ to the right

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $5$ m s$^{-1}$ to the right

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

During a perfectly elastic collision the relative speed between the colliding objects remains constant.

Before the collision, A was moving to the right at $9$ m s$^{-1}$ and B was moving to the left at $2$ m s$^{-1}$ which means that both objects were moving towards each other at a relative speed of $11$ m s$^{-1}$.

After the collision they must then be moving away from each other at the same relative speed.
Given that A is moving leftwards at a speed of $12$ m s$^{-1}$, B must be moving leftwards at a speed of $1$ m s$^{-1}$ and so the relative speed between them would still be $11$ m s$^{-1}$.
