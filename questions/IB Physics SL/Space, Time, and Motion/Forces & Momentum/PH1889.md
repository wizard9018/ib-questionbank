# PH1889

Two objects of mass $m$ and $2m$ are stacked vertically as shown. A horizontal force $F$ is applied to the lower object, causing the system to accelerate. When the acceleration reaches 1 m s$^{-2}$ the top object just begins to slide.

![ph1889q](images/ph1889q.png)

What is the coefficient of static friction between the objects?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.05

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.1

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.2

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.4

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The maximum frictional force between the two objects is what keeps the top object from sliding. This friction provides the only horizontal force and therefore the net force acting on the top object.

- $F_f=(2m)a$

When the top object just begins to slide, static friction is at its maximum value:

- $F_f=\mu_sF_N=\mu_s(2m)g$

- $\mu_s(2m)g=2ma$

- $\mu_sg=a$

- $\mu_s=\dfrac{a}{g}=\dfrac{1}{10}=0.1$
