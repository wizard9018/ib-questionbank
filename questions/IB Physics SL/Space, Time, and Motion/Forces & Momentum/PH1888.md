# PH1888

Spheres $A$ and $B$ are made of the same material that sinks in water. Both spheres are small, and $A$ has twice the radius of $B$. Both are dropped into water and reach terminal velocity.

What is the ratio $\dfrac{\textrm{terminal velocity of object }A}{\textrm{terminal velocity of object }B}$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{1}{2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 2

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 4

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 8

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

When the objects enter the water, the weight downwards is opposed by the upwards forces of buoyancy and drag:

- $F_{net}=ma$

- weight $-$ buoyancy $-$ drag $=ma$

At terminal velocity the net force is zero, so we have:

- weight $-$ buoyancy = drag

- $mg-\rho_wVg=6\pi\eta rv$

- $\rho_sVg-\rho_wVg=6\pi\eta rv$

Where $\rho_s$ is the density of the sphere, $\rho_w$ is the density of water, $V$ is the volume of the sphere, $\eta$ is the viscosity of water, $r$ is the radius of the sphere, and $v$ is the velocity of the sphere.

Substituting the volume of the sphere as $V=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$ and factorizing, we have:

- $\rho_s\left(\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3\right)g-\rho_w\left(\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3\right)g=6\pi\eta rv$

- $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3g(\rho_s-\rho_w)=6\pi\eta rv$

Finally cancelling a factor of $r$:

- $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^2g(\rho_s-\rho_w)=6\pi\eta v$

Comparing spheres $A$ and $B$ we have:

- $\begin{aligned}
\ r^2&\propto v\\
\\\
\ \dfrac{r^2}{v}&=\textrm{ constant}\\
\\\
\ \dfrac{(r_A)^2}{v_A}&=\dfrac{(r_B)^2}{v_B}\\
\\\
\ \dfrac{v_A}{v_B}&=\dfrac{(r_A)^2}{(r_B)^2}\\
\\\
\ \dfrac{v_A}{v_B}&=\left(\dfrac{r_A}{r_B}\right)^2\\
\\\
\ \dfrac{v_A}{v_B}&=\left(\dfrac{2}{1}\right)^2\\
\\\
\ \dfrac{v_A}{v_B}&=4\\
\end{aligned}$
