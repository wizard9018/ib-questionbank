# PH0658

A mass of $400$ g is connected to a spring on a frictionless surface.

![PH0658-3](images/PH0658-3.png)

The spring constant is $0.06\ $N cm$^{-1}$. What is the magnitude of the acceleration when the mass is 20 cm from equilibrium?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $0.03\ $m s$^{-2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $0.06\ $m s$^{-2}$
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $0.6\ $m s$^{-2}$
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $3\ $m s$^{-2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Note that because the length units on the spring constant (cm) match the units on the extension of the spring (cm), the force from the spring in N can be found by simply multiplying the two terms.

$\hspace{1em}$ $F_{net}=F_H = kx = (.06\,$N cm$^{-1})(20\,\ce{cm})= 1.2\, $N

$\hspace{1em}$ $a = \dfrac{F_{net}}{m}=\dfrac{1.2\,\ce{N}}{0.40\,\ce{kg}}=3\, $m s$^{-2}$
