## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The only forces acting are the weight and the tension. These forces must be balanced in the vertical direction, but provide a net horizontal force when added together.

B is the only diagram that shows weight and tension, with the vertical component of tension equal in magnitude to the weight vector. Upwards and horizontal forces on the mass are only provided by the tension force.
