## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Even though the instantaneous speed of the body is unknown, average speeds for each interval can be compared by using the distances covered for the same time interval. Initially, its average speed increases, then stays constant and finally decreases. Also, the object does not change direction, therefore it does not have negative velocity.  The only graph that satisfies these conditions is C.
