# PH1331

A solid block accelerates uniformly down an inclined plane passing through points
A, B and C. It takes $2.00\,s$ to move from A to B and also from B to C.

![PH1331](images/PH1331.png)

The distance between A and B is $2.00$ m and between B and C is $3.00$ m.

Which of the following best describes the acceleration of the block?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $0.125$ m s$^{-2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $0.250$ m s$^{-2}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $0.375$ m s$^{-2}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $0.625$ m s$^{-2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The average speed between points A and B is given by:

- $v_\textrm{average}\textrm{(A to B)} = \dfrac{2.00\textrm{ m}}{2.00\textrm{ s}}=1.00$ m s$^{-1}$

This corresponds to the instantaneous speed of the block $1.00\,s$ after passing through A which occurs before passing through the midpoint between A and B.

Similarly, the average speed between points B and C is given by:

- $v_\textrm{average}\textrm{(B to C)} = \dfrac{3.00\textrm{ m}}{2.00\textrm{ s}}=1.50$ m s$^{-1}$

Again, this corresponds to the instantaneous speed of the block $1.00$ s after passing through B (before passing through the midpoint between B and C).

Therefore, it takes $2.00$ s for the speed of the block to change from $1.00$ m s$^{-1}$ to $1.50$ m s$^{-1}$.

The acceleration is then:

- $a=\dfrac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{1.50\textrm{ m s}^{-1}-1.00\textrm{ m s}^{-1}}{2.00\textrm{ s}}=0.250$ m s$^{-2}$
