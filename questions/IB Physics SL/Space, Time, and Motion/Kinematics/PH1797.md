# PH1797

The diagram shows the path of a football kicked from the ground.

![Screenshot 2025-03-24 at 5.29.42 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-03-24 at 5.29.42 PM.JPEG>)

Air resistance is negligible. Which of the arrows $A$, $B$, $C$, or $D$ represent the direction of the acceleration of the ball at the instant in time shown?

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In the absence of air resistance, gravity is the only force acting on the object so its acceleration acts vertically downwards.

At the point of time shown in the diagram, the vertical velocity of the ball is decreasing whilst its horizontal velocity remains the same.
