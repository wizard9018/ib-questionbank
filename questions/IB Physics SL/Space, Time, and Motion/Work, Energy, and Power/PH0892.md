# PH0892

Below shows a Sankey diagram for an LED.

![Screenshot 2025-07-02 at 2.58.46 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-02 at 2.58.46 PM.JPEG>)

Which of the following shows the percentage efficiency of the LED and the total power input?

$\\$

| | Percentage efficiency | Total power input |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $40\%$ | $4$ W |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $40\%$ | $20$ W |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $60\%$ | $4$ W |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $60\%$ | $20$ W |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Total power input = total power output = $12\ +\ 8\ =\ 20$ W

For an LED useful energy is visible light energy:

Percentage efficiency = $\dfrac{\textrm{ Useful energy output}}{\textrm{ Total energy input}}\ (100\%)\ =\ \dfrac{8}{20}\ (100\%)\ =\ 40\%$
