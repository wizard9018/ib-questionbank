# PH0125

A builder uses a block and tackle pulley system to lift a bundle of materials of mass 15 kg to the top of a building of height 6 m in 9 s. What is the power delivered to the bundle? Assume $g=10$ N kg$^{-1}$.

![PH0125](images/PH0125.png)
$\footnotesize{\textrm{[Image Source: Block-and-tackle-in-use.svg by Themightyquill licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0}]}$

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 10 W

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 17 W

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 100 W

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 900 W

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\begin{aligned}
\textrm{Power} &= \dfrac{\textrm{Work done}}{\textrm{Time}}\\
\hspace{2.3cm}\\
\end{aligned}$

In this case the work done is giving gravitational potential energy to the object. Change in gravitational potential energy is given by $\Delta E_p=mg\Delta h$

$\begin{aligned}
\textrm{Power} &= \dfrac{mgh}{t}\\
\hspace{2.3cm}\\
&= \dfrac{(15\textrm{ kg})(6\textrm{ m})(10\textrm{ N kg}^{-1})}{9\textrm{ s}}\\
\hspace{2.3cm}\\
 &= 100\textrm{ W}\
 \end{aligned}$
