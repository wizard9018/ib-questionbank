# PH0464

**Maximum mark: 5**

A box half filled with sand is pulled by a motor at a constant speed of $2.0$ m s$^{-1}$ on a frictionless surface. During the motion, sand is added to the box from above at a constant rate of $\sigma$ kg s$^{-1}$. 

![Screenshot 2025-05-29 at 1.45.240 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-05-29 at 1.45.240 PM.JPEG>)

1. State the condition related to the mass of an object in order for the equation $F_{net}=ma$ to be equivalent to the equation $F_{net}=\dfrac{\Delta p}{t}$ . [1 mark]

2. The force applied by the motor on the box is $0.11$ N. Determine $\sigma$. [2 marks]

3. The motor has an efficiency of 75%. Calculate the total power provided to the motor. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Mass of the object should be constant $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    Net force is equal to the rate of change of momentum

    - $F_{net}=\dfrac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}=\dfrac{\Delta (mv)}{\Delta t}$    

    Therefore $F_{net}$ can depend on the rate of change of mass or the rate of change of velocity or both.

    The equation 

    - $F=ma$    

    is equivalent to 

    

    - $F=m\dfrac{\Delta v}{t}$

    and therefore assumes that mass is constant and that only velocity is changing with time.

2. ::indent

    - $F=\dfrac{\Delta p}{t}=\dfrac{\Delta(mv)}{t}$

    - $\dfrac{F}{v}=\dfrac{\Delta m}{t}$

    - $\dfrac{F}{v}=\sigma$ $\checkmark$

    - $\sigma=\dfrac{0.11}{2.0}= $ :answer[$0.055\textrm{ kg s}^{-1}$]  $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

     The force $F$ applied to the box;

    - $F=\dfrac{\Delta p}{t}=\dfrac{\Delta(mv)}{t}$

    Since $v$ is constant;

    - $F=\dfrac{v\Delta m}{t}$

    When $\dfrac{\Delta m}{t}$ is replaced with $\sigma$;

    - $F=v\sigma$

    - $\dfrac{F}{v}=\sigma$ where $F=0.11$ N and $v=2.0$ m s$^{-1}$

    - $\sigma=\dfrac{0.11}{2.0}=0.055$ kg s$^{-1}$

3. Useful power output of the motor $=\ll Fv=(0.11)(2.0)=\gg 0.22$ W $\checkmark$

    The power provided to the motor $=\ll \dfrac{0.22}{75\%} \times 100\% = \gg $ :answer[ 0.29 W] $ \checkmark$

    

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    The useful power output $P_o$ of the motor;

    - $P_o=Fv=(0.11)(2.0)=0.22$ W

    The efficiency of the motor;

    - Efficiency = $\dfrac{\textrm{Useful power output}}{\textrm{Input power}} \times 100\%$

    So the input power;

    - Input power = $\dfrac{\textrm{Useful power output}}{\textrm{Efficiency}} \times 100\%$ 

    Substitute the given and calculated values;

    - Input power = $\dfrac{0.22}{75\%} \times 100\% = 0.29\,W$
