# PH1136

The third stage of the proton-proton cycle is described by the equation below:

$\ce{^{3}_{2}He}+\ce{^{3}_{2}He} \rightarrow \ce{^{4}_{2}He}+2\ce{^{1}_{1}H}$

The binding energies per nucleon are given in the following table:

| $\hspace{1em}$Nuclide$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$Binding energy per nucleon$\hspace{1em}$ |
| :-: | :-: |
| $\ce{^{3}_{2}He}$ | 2.573 MeV |
| $\ce{^{4}_{2}He}$ | 7.074 MeV |

Which of the following best describes the energy released in this stage?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ 4.50 MeV

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ 5.50 MeV

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ 7.95 MeV

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ 12.9 MeV

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The energy released can be calculated as follows:

Energy to split two $\ce{^{3}_{2}He}$ into its constituent nucleons: $(2)(3)(2.573\,\ce{MeV})=15.438\,\ce{MeV}$

Energy released when $\ce{^{4}_{2}He}$ is formed: $(4)(7.074\,\ce{MeV})=(28.296\,\ce{MeV})$

- $E=28.296\,\ce{MeV}-15.438\,\ce{MeV}=12.858$ MeV
