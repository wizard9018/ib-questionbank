# PH0716

Which of the following is correct about a red giant star?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | The orbits of the inner planets of the solar system could fit into its volume   |
  | II. | Cooler than the Sun |
  | III. | Have higher luminosity than the Sun |

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and II only

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

By considering the position of the red giants and the Sun on an H-R diagram, it is easy to say that red giants have higher luminosity and lower temperature than the Sun. They can also be large enough to have a volume that could include the Sun and inner planets inside.
