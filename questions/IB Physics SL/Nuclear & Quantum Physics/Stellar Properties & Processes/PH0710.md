# PH0710

Which of the following is correct for a stable star?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$  Inward force due to radiation pressure equals the outward force due to gravitational attraction

- B. $\hspace{1em} $ Inward force due to radiation pressure is greater than the outward force due to gravitational attraction

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Inward force due to gravitational attraction equals the outward force due to radiation pressure

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Inward force due to gravitational attraction is greater than the outward force due to radiation pressure

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

To be a stable star, outwards and inwards forces must be balanced. Outwards force is the result of the pressure of radiation and temperature while the inwards force is formed due to gravitational attraction.
