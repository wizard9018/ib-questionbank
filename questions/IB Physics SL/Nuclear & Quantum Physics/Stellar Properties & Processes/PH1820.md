## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The Hertzsprung-Russell (H-R) diagram is a fundamental tool in astrophysics used to classify stars based on their luminosity and temperature.

The horizontal axis of the diagram represents the temperature of the star, but it is plotted in a decreasing order, meaning hotter stars (higher temperature) are on the left, and cooler stars (lower temperature) are on the right.

The vertical axis represents the luminosity, which is the total energy output of a star per second. Stars with higher luminosities are positioned higher on the diagram, while stars with lower luminosities are lower.
