    2. ::indent
        - $R=\dfrac{R_\odot 5800^2}{3 800^2}  \sqrt{\dfrac{2.7\times10^5 L_\odot}{L_\odot }}$ $\checkmark$

        - $\hspace{1em}= 1200R_\odot$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        By using the Stefan-Boltzmann constant for a black body:

        - $L=\sigma AT^4$

        The following relation can be obtained for the ratio of the luminosities of two stars:

        - $\dfrac{L_1}{L_2} =\dfrac{\sigma A_1 (T_1)^4}{\sigma A_2 (T_2)^4 }$

        - $\hspace{1.6em} =\dfrac{\sigma 4\pi (R_1)^2 (T_1)^4}{\sigma 4\pi (R_2)^2 (T_2)^4 }$

        - $\hspace{1.6em} =\dfrac{(R_1)^2 (T_1)^4}{(R_2)^2 (T_2)^4} $

        - $R_2=\dfrac{R_1 (T_1)^2}{(T_2)^2}  \sqrt{\dfrac{L_2}{L_1 }}$

        - $R=\dfrac{R_\odot (T_\odot)^2}{T^2}  \sqrt{\dfrac{L}{L_\odot}}$

        - $R=\dfrac{R_\odot 5800^2}{3 800^2 } \sqrt{\dfrac{2.7\times10^5 L_\odot}{L_\odot }}$

        - $R=1200R_\odot$

2.  1. Nuclear fusion generates an outward force/radiation pressure. $\checkmark$

       The gravitational inward pull is more than the outward force. $\checkmark$

       $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Nuclear fusion generates an outward force/radiation pressure, which is counterbalanced by the inward force of gravity. When a star exhausts a lot of its hydrogen fuel, the outward force can no longer counteract gravity's inward pull. As a result, the star's core begins to contract and heat up, causing the outer layers of the star to expand and cool down.
