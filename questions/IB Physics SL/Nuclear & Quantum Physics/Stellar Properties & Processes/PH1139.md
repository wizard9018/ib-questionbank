# PH1139

At the core of stars P and Q different elements are fused together.
- Star P fuses Hydrogen creating Helium at its core.
- Star Q fuses Helium nuclei to produce Beryllium.

Which row correctly describes the relative temperature and pressure at the core of these stars?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1em}$Temperature is higher$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$Pressure is higher$\hspace{1em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| P | P |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | P| Q |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Q | P |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Q | Q |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

At the core of star Q larger nuclei are fused. The electrostatic repulsion between these nuclei is higher due to the larger number of positively charged protons.

For these nuclei to fuse and to overcome the Coulomb repulsion, higher kinetic energies  will be required which represents higher temperatures. The pressure at its core will also be higher given that stronger forces are needed.

Hydrogen fusing stars do not need such high temperatures and pressures, given that the Coulomb repulsion between hydrogen nuclei is, in comparison, relatively small.
