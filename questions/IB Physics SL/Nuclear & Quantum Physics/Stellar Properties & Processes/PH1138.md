# PH1138

The equation below describes the fusion of Deuterium $\ce{^{2}_{1}H}$ with Tritium $\ce{^{3}_{1}H}$.

$\ce{^{2}_{1}H}+\ce{^{3}_{1}H}\rightarrow \ce{^{4}_{2}He} + \ce{^{1}_{0}n}$

During this reaction 17.59 MeV of energy are released.

Consider the following statements for this reaction:

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | The total binding energy increases.   |
  | II. | The total mass increases. |
  | III. | Binding energy per nucleon remains constant. |

Which of the statements are true?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Only I

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Only II

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and II

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

When two small nuclei fuse together energy is released, this process results in a loss of mass in the system.\
The product of this reaction will then reach a lower energy state which correspond to a higher magnitude of the total binding energy.\
The binding energy per nucleon will also increase.
