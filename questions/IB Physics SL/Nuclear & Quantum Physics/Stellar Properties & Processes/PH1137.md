# PH1137

Star X has a surface temperature that is twice that of the Sun ($T_X=2T_\odot$) and its luminosity is 49 times greater than the luminosity of the Sun ($L_X=49L_\odot$).

Which of the following best describes the ratio $R_X:R_\odot$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ 1.8

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ 3.5

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ 8.4

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ 9.1

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

According to Stefan-Boltzmann law the luminosity is given by:

- $L=4\pi\sigma R^2T^4$

Using ratios we get:

- $\dfrac{L_X}{L_\odot}=\Bigl(\dfrac{R_X}{R_\odot}\Bigr)^2\Bigl(\dfrac{T_X}{T_\odot}\Bigr)^4$

Which can be rearranged into:

- $\Bigl(\dfrac{R_X}{R_\odot}\Bigr)^2=\Bigl(\dfrac{L_X}{L_\odot}\Bigr)\Bigl(\dfrac{T_\odot}{T_X}\Bigr)^4$

Leading to:

- $\dfrac{R_X}{R_\odot}=\sqrt{\Bigl(\dfrac{L_X}{L_\odot}\Bigr)\Bigl(\dfrac{T_\odot}{T_X}\Bigr)^4} = \Bigl(\sqrt{\dfrac{L_X}{L_\odot}}\Bigr)\Bigl(\dfrac{T_\odot}{T_X}\Bigr)^2 = (\sqrt{49})\Bigl(\dfrac{1}{2}\Bigr)^2 = 1.75 \approx 1.8$
