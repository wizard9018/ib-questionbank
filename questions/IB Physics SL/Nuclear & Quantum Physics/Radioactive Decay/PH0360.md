# PH0360

The binding energy of $_{2}^{4}\textrm{He}$ is 28 MeV. What is its binding energy per nucleon?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 45 $\times$ $10^{-13}$ J

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 22 $\times$ $10^{-13}$ J

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 11 $\times$ $10^{-13}$ J

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 7.5 $\times$ $10^{-13}$ J

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
To get the value of the binding energy per nucleon, the binding energy is divided by the number of nucleons

$\hspace{3em}$ $\dfrac{28}{4}=7$ MeV

Since 1 eV $= 1.6 \times$ $10^{-19}$ J, converting to joules gives

$\hspace{3em}$ ($7\times{10}^6)(1.6\times{10}^{-19})=(7)(1.6)\times{10}^{-13}=11.2\times{10}^{-13}$ J

To estimate such a value without calculator, first add the powers of 10: $6 -19= -13$ \
Then, $(7)(1.6)$ = 11.2 is less than $(7)(2)=14$, so the nearest answer to this from the choices will only be C.
