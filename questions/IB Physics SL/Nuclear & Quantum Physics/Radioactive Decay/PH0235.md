# PH0235

The binding energy per nucleon of a $_{Z}^{A}\textrm{X}$ nucleus is $E$. What is the mass defect for the formation of X  from its constituent nucleons?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{AE}{c^2}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{ZE}{c^2}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{E}{Ac^2}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{E}{Zc^2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Binding energy of X:

- $(A)(E) = AE$

By using $E = mc^2$, mass defect:

- $m =$ $\dfrac{AE}{c^2}$

Therefore the answer is A.
