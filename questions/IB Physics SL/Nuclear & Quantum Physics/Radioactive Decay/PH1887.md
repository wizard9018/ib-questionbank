# PH1887

A radioactive source emits two types of radiation. Most of the radiation is stopped by a thin sheet of paper and all of it is stopped by around a 0.5 cm sheet of aluminum. Which best describes the nature of the radiation?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Mostly alpha with some beta

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Mostly alpha with some gamma

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Mostly beta with some alpha

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Mostly gamma with some alpha

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Alpha radiation is easily stopped by a thin sheet of paper (or even a few centimetres of air), so if most of the radiation is blocked by paper, the source must be emitting mostly alpha particles.

Beta radiation can pass through paper but is stopped by materials like aluminium a few millimetres thick. All of the remaining radiation is stopped by a 0.5 cm sheet of aluminium, the second type must be beta particles.

Gamma radiation, on the other hand, is much more penetrating and would not be completely stopped by such a thin aluminium sheet.

Therefore, the source is emitting mostly alpha radiation, with some beta radiation.
