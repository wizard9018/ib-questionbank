# PH0983

An atom of $\ce{^{A}_{Z}X}$ follows a sequence of three decays: beta plus, alpha, beta plus.

What is the number of neutrons in the final nucleus produced?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $A$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $A-Z$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $A-4$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $A-Z-4$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

During beta plus decay a proton in the nucleus decays into a neutron (a positron and an electron neutrino). Neutron numbers increase by 1.

During alpha decay the nucleus loses 2 protons and 2 neutrons (an alpha particle), so neutron number decreases by 2.

$A$ is the nucleon number (number of protons + neutrons) and $Z$ is the proton number.

Initially the nucleus has $A-Z$ neutrons.

2 neutrons are lost (in alpha decay) and 2 are gained (one in each beta decay) so overall neutron number does not change. Final neutron number is $A-Z$.

This can be also shown by looking at each stage of the decay process:

- After decay 1 (beta plus):
Neutron number $= A-Z+1$

- After decay 2 (alpha):
Neutron number $= A-Z+1-2=A-Z-1$

- After decay 3 (beta plus):
Neutron number $= A-Z-1+1=A-Z$
