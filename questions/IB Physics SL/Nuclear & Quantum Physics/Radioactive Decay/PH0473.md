2. At the maximum point of the curve. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
Nuclear reactions occur such that there is an increase in binding energy per nucleon. The most stable nuclide will have the highest binding energy per nucleon.

3.
    1. X is a neutron $\checkmark$ \
Mass number $A=1$ **AND** atomic number $Z=0$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Charge and mass must be conserved.
        Therefore the mass numbers on each side must total 4, and the charge numbers must total 2.

        $\hspace{2em}$ $_{1}^{2}$H + $_{1}^{2}$H $\rightarrow$ $_{2}^{3}$He + $_{0}^{1}$n

    2. ::indent
        - $\Delta m=(2)(2.014)-(3.016+1.008665)=0.0033\ll \ce{u}\gg$ $\checkmark$
        - $E=\ll\Delta mc^2\gg$
        - $E=(0.0033)(931.5)$ $\checkmark$
        - $E=3.07\ll\ \ce{MeV}\gg$ $\checkmark$

    3. ::indent
        - $p_{He}=p_n$ $\checkmark$

        - $E_n=\dfrac{3}{4}\times\ E$ $\checkmark$

        - $v_n=\ll\sqrt{\dfrac{(\frac{3}{4})(3.07\times{10}^6)(1.6\times{10}^{-19})}{(\frac{1}{2})(1.675\times{10}^{-27})}}\gg=2.09\times{10}^7\ll\ $m s$^{-1}\gg$ $\checkmark$

        **OR**

        - $p_{He}=p_n$ $\checkmark$

        - $p=\ll\sqrt{\dfrac{2E}{\left(\frac{1}{m_{He}}+\frac{1}{m_n}\right)}}\gg=\sqrt{\dfrac{(2)(3.07\times{10}^6)(1.6\times{10}^{-19})}{\left(\frac{1}{(3.016)(1.661\times{10}^{-27})}+\frac{1}{1.675\times{10}^{-27}}\right)}}$ $\checkmark$

        - $v_n=\ll\dfrac{p}{m_n}\gg=2.09\times{10}^7\ll \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}\gg$ $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    Energy released is the sum of kinetic energies of He-3 and the neutron.

    $\hspace{1em}$ $E=E_{He}+E_n$

    Due to conservation of momentum, and since the initial momentum before the reaction is zero, then the momentum of He-3 is equal in magnitude to the momentum of the neutron and opposite in direction

    - $p_{He}=p_n$

    Because $E=\dfrac{p^2}{2m}$, the ratio between the kinetic energy of the neutron to that of the He-3 is:

    - $\dfrac{E_n}{E_{He}}=\dfrac{m_{He}}{m_n}=\dfrac{3.016}{1.008665}\approx\dfrac{3}{1}$

    To calculate the velocity,

    - $E_n=\dfrac{3}{4}E=\dfrac{1}{2}m_n{v_n}^2$

    - $\dfrac{3}{4}(3.07\times{10}^6)(1.6\times{10}^{-19})=\dfrac{1}{2}(1.675\times{10}^{-27}){v_n}^2$

    - $v_n=2.09\times{10}^7\,$m s$^{-1}$

    **OR**

    - $E=\dfrac{p^2}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{m_{He}}+\dfrac{1}{m_n}\right)$

    - $(3.07\times{10}^6)(1.6\times{10}^{-19})=\dfrac{p^2}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{(3.016)(1.661\times{10}^{-27})}+\dfrac{1}{1.675\times{10}^{-27}}\right)$

    - $p=m_nv_n$

    therefore,

    - $v_n=2.09\times{10}^7$ m s$^{-1}$
