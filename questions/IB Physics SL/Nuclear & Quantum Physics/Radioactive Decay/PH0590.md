# PH0590

Estimate the speed of an alpha particle that has accelerated from rest in a uniform electric field $E = 50\,$N C$^{-1}$ over a distance of 2.0 m.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $10^3 \ $m s$^{-1}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $10^4 \ $m s$^{-1}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $10^5 \ $m s$^{-1}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $10^9 \ $m s$^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Using $E=\ \dfrac{F}{q}$ from the data booklet, as well as $v^2=u^2+2as$ and $F=ma$,

$\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2em}v^2&=u^2+2as \\
v&=\sqrt{2as} \\
\hspace{2em}\\
F&=ma \\
a&=\ \frac{F}{m} \\
\end{aligned}$

Giving
$v=\sqrt{2\dfrac{F}{m}s}$

And $F=qE$

Therefore, $v=\sqrt{2\dfrac{qE}{m}s}$

the speed of the alpha particle is

- $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{2Eqs}{m}}\approx\sqrt{\dfrac{2(50\,\ce{N C}^{-1})(3.2 \times  {10}^{-19}\,\ce{C})(2\ \ce{m})}{6.4 \times  {10}^{-27}\,\ce{kg}}}$

Therefore

- $v=\sqrt{\dfrac{6.4\ \times \ {10}^{-17}\ \ce{ N m}}{6.4\ \times \ {10}^{-27}\ \ce{kg}}}=\sqrt{{10}^{10}\ \dfrac{\ce{m}^2}{\ce{s}^2}}={10}^5\ $m s$^{-1}$
