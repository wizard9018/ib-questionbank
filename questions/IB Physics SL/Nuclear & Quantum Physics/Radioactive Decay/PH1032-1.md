1. 1. Determination of 126 neutrons  $\checkmark$

        $84m_p+126m_n-3.4203\times 10^{-25}=84(1.673\times 10^{-27})+126(1.675\times 10^{-27})-3.4203\times 10^{-25}=9.552\times 10^{-27}\,kg$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
    The mass of a nucleus is always less than the total mass of its constituent nucleons, the difference in mass is called the mass defect.

        $\ce{^{210}_{84}Po}$ has 84 protons and $(210-84=)126$ neutrons so we may write:

        - Mass defect $=84m_p+126m_n-3.4203\times 10^{-25}$

        The masses of a proton and a neutron can be found in the data booklet.

        - $84(1.673\times 10^{-27})+126(1.675\times 10^{-27})-3.4203\times 10^{-25}=9.552\times 10^{-27}\,kg$

    2. $\ce{^{210}_{84}Po} \rightarrow \ce{^{206}_{82}Pb} + \ce{^{4}_{2}\alpha}$ $\checkmark$

        Allow He for $\alpha$ \
        Ignore gamma

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        An alpha particle is a nucleus of helium. Comprised of two protons and two neutrons.

        The top left number (mass number) and bottom left number (atomic number) are conserved.

        $\ce{^{210}_{84}Po}$ nucleus loses 4 nucleons so mass number decreases by 4: $210-4=206$

        $\ce{^{210}_{84}Po}$ nucleus loses 2 protons so atomic number decreases by 2: $84-2=82$

    3. $\ce{^{206}_{82}Pb}$/ Lead has a higher binding energy $\bold{per\,\,nucleon}$ than $\ce{^{210}_{84}Po}$ / Polonium

        OR

        $\ce{^{206}_{82}Pb}$/ Lead and alpha combined have a higher binding than that of $\ce{^{210}_{84}Po}$ / Polonium  $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        Binding energy is the energy required to separate a nucleus into its constituent nucleons. It is also the energy released when the completely separated nucleons combine to form that nucleus.

        Energy is released when the total binding energy of the decay products is greater than that of the original nucleus.

        Below is a curve showing binding energy per nucleon against mass number.

        ![2032ccc](images/2032ccc.png)

        $\ce{^{206}_{82}Pb}$ has a greater binding energy per nucleon than $\ce{^{210}_{84}Po}$. So total binding energy of $\ce{^{206}_{82}Pb}$ and alpha is greater than that of $\ce{^{210}_{84}Po}$ alone.
