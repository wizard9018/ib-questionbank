# PH0332

The diagram below shows the graph of binding energy per nucleon vs. the mass (nucleon) number of some elements.

![Screenshot 2026-04-27 at 7.02.35 AM](<images/Screenshot 2026-04-27 at 7.02.35 AM.png>)

Which is the most stable nucleus?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $_{}^{238}\textrm{U}$
- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $_{}^{2}\textrm{H}$
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $_{}^{4}\textrm{He}$
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $_{}^{32}\textrm{S}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The binding energy per nucleon indicates the energy needed to remove a particle from the nucleus. The more energy needed to remove a particle from a nucleus, the more stable a nucleus is.

Of the choices listed, $_{}^{32}\textrm{S}$ has a binding energy per nucleon slightly greater than 8 MeV. $_{}^{238}\textrm{U}$ is slightly less than 8 MeV. The values for $_{}^{2}\textrm{H}$ and $_{}^{4}\textrm{He}$ are much lower.
