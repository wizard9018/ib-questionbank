# PH0998

Below are three statements about the Rutherford-Geiger-Marsden experiment:

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | It provided evidence that the atom is mostly empty space   |
  | II. | It provided evidence that atoms have a small positively charged center  |
  | III. | It provided evidence that electrons exist in discrete energy levels |

Which of the statements are correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In this experiment, alpha particles were fired at a thin sheet of gold to investigate the structure of the atom.

I is correct $-$ Most alpha particles passed straight through the gold sheet providing evidence that the atom is mostly empty space.

II is correct $-$ Some alpha particles (which were known to be positively charged) were deflected at large angles, providing evidence the atom contains a small concentration of positive charge.

III is incorrect $-$ The electron had not been discovered at the time of this experiment, work involving energy levels came later.
