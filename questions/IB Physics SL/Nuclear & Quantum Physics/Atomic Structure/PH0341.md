# PH0341

The electron in the ground state of a hydrogen atom absorbs a certain amount of energy. The diagram shows some of the energy levels of the hydrogen atom. 

![PH0341](images/PH0341.png)

At which state would the electron be if it absorbs 12.09 eV of energy?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ At ground state.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ First excited state.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Second excited state.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Third excited state.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The electron is in ground state, which is $n=1$. \

It absorbs 12.09 eV of energy, which is the exact difference between $n=1$ and $n=3$ on the diagram. Therefore, the electron will be in $n=3$, which is the second excited state.
