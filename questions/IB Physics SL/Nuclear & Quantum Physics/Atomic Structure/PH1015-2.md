# PH1015-2

**Maximum mark: 4**

The diagram shows the emission spectrum of hydrogen atoms when electrons make a transition from different excited states to the second excited state.

![1015aa copy](<images/1015aa copy.png>)

1. Outline how the emission spectrum of hydrogen provides evidence for the discrete energy levels of electrons.[2 marks]

2. Determine, from the information given in the diagram,  the energy difference between the 3rd  and 2nd  excited states of the electron. Give your answer in units of joules. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Emitted photons reflect the energy difference between electron energy levels $\checkmark$

   The wavelengths and therefore energy of emitted photons is discrete $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Photons are emitted when electrons transition between energy levels. The wavelength of the emitted photon is directly related to its energy. Because the emitted wavelengths are quantized, the energy of a photon is also quantized. Therefore, there are distinct energy differences between energy levels.

2. The energy difference should be smallest

    $656\,nm$ is the longest wavelength that gives lowest energy difference $\checkmark$

    $\dfrac{(6.63\times 10^{-34})(3.00\times 10^8)}{656\times 10^{-9}}=3.03\times 10^{-19}\,J$ $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    The energy difference between the excited states corresponds to the energy of the emitted photon when electrons transition between those states. Energy $E$ of the electron:

    - $E=hf=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$

    The photon possesses its minimum energy when the wavelength is at its maximum. Specifically, the energy difference between the 3rd  and the 2nd energy states should be the smallest among all other transitions from various excited states to the 2nd state. Consequently, a photon with a wavelength of $656\,nm$ is expected to be emitted during this particular transition.

    - $E=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}=\dfrac{(6.63\times 10^{-34})(3.00\times 10^8)}{656\times 10^{-9}}=3.03\times 10^{-19}\,J$
