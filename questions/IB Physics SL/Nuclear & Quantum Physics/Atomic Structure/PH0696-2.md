# PH0696-2

**Maximum mark: 5**

On a sunny day, a house is powered by a solar panel. In order to run home appliances, the incident power of sunlight on the solar panel must be 10 kW.

1. The average surface temperature of the Sun is 5850 K. Show that the peak wavelength of the emission of the sun is around 500 nm. [2 marks]

2. It can be assumed that 7.5$\%$ of the energy from incident sunlight consists of peak wavelength photons that are converted to electrical energy by the panel. Calculate the number of 500 nm photons that must strike the solar panel per second in order to meet the power requirement of the appliances. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{2.90\times{10}^{-3}}{5850}$ $\checkmark$

    $\hspace{1em}$ $496\, \ll$nm$\gg\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
By using Wien\'s displacement law,

    $\hspace{1em}$ $\lambda_{max}=\dfrac{2.90\times{10}^{-3}}{5850}=496\, $nm

2. Energy of the 500 nm photons in one second
$=\left(10000\right)\left(0.075\right)\left(1\right)=750\, $J $\checkmark$

    Energy per 500 nm photon=$\dfrac{(6.63\times{10}^{-34})(3.0\times{10}^8)}{500\times{10}^{-9}}=3.98\times{10}^{-19}\, $J $\checkmark$

    $\hspace{1em}$ $n=\dfrac{750}{(3.98\times{10}^{-19})}=$ :answer[$1.9\times{10}^{21}$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        Total power of the 500 nm photons = $\left(10000\right)\left(0.075\right)=750\, $W

    The energy $E$ received by the solar panel due to 500 nm photons in one second:

    $\hspace{1em}$ $E=Pt=\left(750\right)\left(1\right)=750\, J$

    The energy $E_p$ of one 500 nm photon:

    $\hspace{1em}$ $E_p=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda}=\dfrac{(6.63\times{10}^{-34})(3.0\times{10}^8)}{500\times{10}^{-9}}=3.98\times{10}^{-19}\ $J

    The number $n$ of 500 nm photons:

    $\hspace{1em}$ $n=\dfrac{750}{(3.98\times{10}^{-19})}=19\times{10}^{20}$
