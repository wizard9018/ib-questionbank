# PH1152

The diagram shows the emission spectrum received from a star and the emission spectra of hydrogen, helium, oxygen, and carbon.

![Screenshot 2026-01-01 at 12.39.32 PM](<images/Screenshot 2026-01-01 at 12.39.32 PM.png>)

Which of the following is correct about the chemical composition of this star?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Hydrogen and helium only

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Hydrogen, helium and carbon

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Hydrogen, carbon and oxygen

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Helium, carbon and oxygen

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

When we examine the pattern of the emission spectra of the star and compare it to the individual spectrum of each element, we’ll find that it matches the composition of hydrogen and helium only.

For example, if we consider the smallest wavelength of the spectrum coming from the star, we’ll find that it’s the smallest wavelength of hydrogen. And the largest wavelength coming from the star is the same as the largest wavelength of helium.
