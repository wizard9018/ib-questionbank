# PH1076

Which of the following is directly used in identifying the chemical composition of a star in astronomy?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Doppler shift in spectral lines

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Emission spectra

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Wien's law

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Luminosity of stars

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Each element has a unique emission spectrum, like a fingerprint. By comparing the emission spectrum of a star to the emission spectra of elements on Earth, astronomers can determine the composition of the star.

Doppler shift makes use of spectral lines, but is used to determine the motion of a star relative to the Earth.

Wien's law is used to determine the temperature of a star using its blackbody spectrum.

Stellar luminosity is a measure of the power emitted by a star, but is not a direct  indicator of the chemical composition.
