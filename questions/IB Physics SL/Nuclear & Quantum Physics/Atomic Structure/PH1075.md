# PH1075

In the diagram below, arrows indicate the transitions made by electrons, and corresponding wavelengths of the emitted photons are also marked.

![Screenshot 2026-01-01 at 12.28.46 PM](<images/Screenshot 2026-01-01 at 12.28.46 PM.png>)

What is the ratio $\dfrac{\lambda_1}{\lambda_2}$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{1.7}{0.8}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{0.8}{1.7}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{1.3}{0.8}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{0.8}{1.3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The energy $E_1$ emitted  by the photon that has a wavelength of $\lambda_1$:

- $E_1=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda_1} =-0.2E-(-E)$

- $\lambda_1=\dfrac{hc}{-0.2 E+E}=\dfrac{hc}{0.8 E}$

The energy $E_2$ emitted  by the photon that has a wavelength of $\lambda_2$:

- $E_2=\dfrac{hc}{\lambda_2} =-0.2E-(-1.9E)$

- $\lambda_2=\dfrac{hc}{-0.2E+1.9 E}=\dfrac{hc}{1.7 E}$

- $\dfrac{\lambda_1}{\lambda_2} =\dfrac{\frac{hc}{0.8 E}}{\frac{hc}{1.7 E}}=\dfrac{1.7}{0.8}$
