# PH1039

The diagram shows a pressurized water reactor. Which part of the diagram is responsible for protecting workers in a nuclear power station?

![PH1039](images/PH1039.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

A reactor must be equipped with a protective shield that effectively absorbs all the harmful radiation emanating from its core. This shield must be sufficiently thick and encompassing to effectively prevent radiation leakage. Option C is the only option that meets this requirement.
