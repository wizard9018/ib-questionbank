# PH0472

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following reaction takes place in a nuclear reactor:

$\hspace{4em}$ $\ce{^{235}_{92}U}$ + $\ce{^{1}_{0}\textit{n}}$ $\rightarrow$ $\ce{^{144}_{56}Ba}$ + $\ce{^{89}_{36}Kr}$ + 3$\ce{^{1}_{0}\textit{n}}$

1. 1. Differentiate between fission and fusion reactions. [3 marks]

    2. Determine whether this reaction is a nuclear fission or nuclear fusion. [1 mark]

The following data is given for this reaction:

- The binding energy per nucleon of $\ce{^{235}U}$ = 7.59 MeV
- The binding energy per nucleon of $\ce{^{144}Ba}$ = 8.27 MeV
- The binding energy per nucleon of $\ce{^{89}Kr}$ = 8.62 MeV

2. Calculate the energy released from this reaction. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1. Fission is the process of splitting a large nucleus into smaller nuclei. $\checkmark$ \
Fusion is the joining together of small nuclei to give larger ones. $\checkmark$ \
Energy is released in both. $\checkmark$ \

    2. Fission $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
    The larger uranium nucleus breaks into two smaller daughter nuclei.

2. Binding energy of $\ce{^{235}U}$ = 1783.65 MeV\
    **AND** \
    Binding energy of $\ce{^{144}Ba}$ = 1190.88 MeV \
    **AND** \
    Binding energy of $\ce{^{89}Kr}$ = 767.18 MeV $\checkmark$

    - $E=1783.65-\left(1190.88+767.18\right)$ $\checkmark$

    - Energy released =:answer[($-$)174.41 $\ll\ \ce{MeV}\gg$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    To find the binding energies of the nuclides involved, multiply the binding energy per nucleon by the number of nucleons

    - Binding energy of $\ce{^{235}U}$ = (235)(7.59) = 1783.65 MeV
    - Binding energy of $\ce{^{144}Ba}$ = (144)(8.27) = 1190.88 MeV
    - Binding energy of $\ce{^{89}Kr}$ = (89)(8.62) = 767.18 MeV

    - $\Delta$E = Binding energy of original nucleus $-$ Sum of binding energy of products

    - $\Delta E=1783.65-\left(1190.88+767.18\right)$

    - $\Delta E = -174.41$ MeV

    This negative result shows that the binding energy of the products is greater than that of the original uranium nucleus. As there is an increase in total binding energy, there is a release of energy in this reaction.
