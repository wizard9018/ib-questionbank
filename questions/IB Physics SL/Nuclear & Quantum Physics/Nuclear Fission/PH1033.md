# PH1033

Energy is released in nuclear reactors by

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Neutron-induced fission

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Spontaneous fission

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Random fission

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Fusion

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Nuclear power plants harness the energy released through nuclear fission. Fission can occur spontaneously, without any external stimulus, but this is a rare and inefficient process that is not utilized in nuclear power plants. Instead, nuclear power plants employ neutron-induced fission, which involves bombarding a heavy nucleus with a neutron, causing it to split and release energy. This induced fission can be controlled in nuclear reactors by using control rods to block given proportions of neutrons, allowing for a sustained release of energy that can be used to generate electricity.
