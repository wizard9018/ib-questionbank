# PH1035

In a nuclear reactor, the purpose of moderators is

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Exchanging heat

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Slowing down neutrons

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Absorbing neutrons

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Shielding

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The neutrons released during nuclear fission are extremely fast, travelling at speeds that are too high to be captured by the nuclei of the fuel atoms. To make these neutrons more likely to be captured and sustain a chain reaction, they need to be slowed down. This is achieved by passing the neutrons through a material called a moderator, which is typically a light element like water, graphite, or heavy water.
