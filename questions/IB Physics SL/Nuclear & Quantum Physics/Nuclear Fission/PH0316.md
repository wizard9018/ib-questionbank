# PH0316

**Maximum mark: 10**

A nuclear power plant operating at an overall efficiency of 40% produces energy at a rate of 950 MW. The reaction responsible for generating this energy is given by:

$\ce{_92^235U + _0^1n -> _56^141Ba + _36^92Kr + 3 _0^1n}$ 

The atomic masses of the different nuclides are:

$\begin{aligned}

\ce{_92^235U} = 235.0444u\ \hspace{5em}

\ce{_56^141Ba} &= 140.9144u\ \hspace{5em}

\ce{_36^92Kr} = 91.9262u\\

\end{aligned}$

1. 

    1. Show that approximately 170 MeV of energy is released in one fission reaction. [2 marks]

    2. Using the information given, determine the energy density of uranium-235 in units of J kg$^{-1}$. [3 marks]

    3. Assuming that all of the uranium-235 undergoes fission, calculate the mass of uranium needed to operate the plant for one year. [3 marks]

2. The nuclear reactor consists of several features responsible for the smooth functioning of the reactor. State the function of the following features  in the reactor:

    

    1. The control rods [1 mark]

    2. The moderator [1 mark]

    3. The heat exchanger [1 mark]

        At this point the extra significant figures need to be shown since it's a proof.

    2. ::indent

    

        - $m=235.0444u = (235.0444)(1.661 \times 10^{-27}) = 3.90 \times 10^{-25}$ kg $\checkmark$

        - $E = 173.7\,\ce{MeV} = 2.78 \times 10^{-11}\,\ce{J}$ $\checkmark$

        - $E_S$ = $\dfrac{E}{m} = \dfrac{2.78\, \times \,10^{-11}}{3.90 \,\times\, 10^{-25}} = $ :answer[$7.13 \times 10^{13}\,\ce{J kg}^{-1}$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        The specific energy of the uranium atom is the energy released per unit mass. 

        Mass of one uranium atom = $235.0444u = (235.0444)(1.661 \times 10^{-27}) = 3.90 \times 10^{-25}\,\ce{kg}$ 

        Energy from one atom = $(173.7 \times 10^6 $ eV$)(1.6 \times 10^{-19}$ J eV$^{-1}$) = $2.78 \times 10^{-11}$ J 

        Specific energy = $\dfrac{\textrm{Energy released}}{\textrm{mass}} = \dfrac{2.78 \times 10^{-11}}{3.90 \times 10^{-25}} = 7.13 \times 10^{13}$ J kg$^{-1}$

    3. ::indent

        - $P_{in}=\dfrac{950\,\ce{MW}}{0.4}=2375\,\ce{MW}$ $\checkmark$

        - $E_{year}=(2375\times 10^6)(365)(24)(60)(60) = 7.49\times 10^{16}\,\ce{J}$ $\checkmark$

        - $m = \dfrac{7.49\,\times\, 10^{16}\,\ce{J}}{7.13 \times 10^{13}\,\ce{J}\,\ce{kg}^{-1}}= $ :answer[1050 kg] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\

        $Power_{in}=\dfrac{950\,\ce{MW}}{0.4}=2375$ MW

        Energy in 1 year, $E_{year}=(2375\times 10^6)(365)(24)(60)(60) = 7.49\times 10^{16}$ J 

        Mass needed = $\dfrac{\textrm{Yearly energy}}{\textrm{specific energy}} = \dfrac{7.49\,\times \,10^{16}\,\ce{J}}{7.13 \times 10^{13}\,\ce{J}\,\ce{kg}^{-1}}=1052$ kg
