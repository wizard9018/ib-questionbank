# PH0719

Which of the following incorrectly matches a component of a nuclear power plant with its function?

$\\$

| | Component | Function |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| $\hspace{1.5em}$Heat Exchanger$\hspace{1.5em}$ |Prevents overheating |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Moderator | Slows down electrons |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Control rods | Absorbs neutrons |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Shielding | $\hspace{1.5em}$Prevents any leakage of radiation$\hspace{1.5em}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Moderators slow down the speed of the neutrons (not electrons) so that they can be captured by nuclei of fuel and initiate further fission reactions.
