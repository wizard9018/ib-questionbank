# PH0452

Two identical lamps $L_1$ and $L_2$ are connected to a cell with no internal resistance as shown below. Resistors $R_1$ and $R_2$ are also identical. Switch $S$ controls part of the circuit.

![PH0452](images/PH0452.png)
$\footnotesize{\textrm{[Source: Created with GeoGebra, - https://www.geogebra.org/m/JbAKa82Y}]}$

Which of the following statements about the brightness of the lamp $L_2$ are correct?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | If the resistance of $R_2$ increases, the brightness of $L_2$ increases. |
  | II. | If the resistance of $R_1$ increases, the brightness of $L_2$ decreases. |
  | III. | If switch $S$ is opened, the brightness of $L_2$ does not change. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Only I

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- I. $\quad$ Incorrect: The potential difference between $L_2$ plus $R_1$ is equal to the potential difference of the battery. As the battery does not change the current passing through / the power of / brightness of the lamp stays constant.

- II. $\enskip$ Correct: The potential difference between $L_2$ plus $R_1$ is equal to the potential difference of the battery. As $R_1$ increases the current passing through / power of / brightness of the lamp decreases.

- III. $\,$ Correct: The potential difference between $L_2$ plus $R_1$ is equal to the potential difference of the battery. As the battery does not change the current passing through / the power of / brightness of the lamp stays constant.
