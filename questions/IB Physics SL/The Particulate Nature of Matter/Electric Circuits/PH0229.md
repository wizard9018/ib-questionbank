# PH0229

In the circuits below, all resistors have the same resistance, and the cells do not have internal resistance. The emf of the cell in circuit 1 is $E_1$ while it is $E_2$ in circuit 2.

![PH0229](images/PH0229.png)
$\small{\textrm{[Source: Created with GeoGebra, - https://www.geogebra.org/m/JbAKa82Y}]}$

All resistors dissipate the same power. Which of the following gives the ratio $\dfrac{E_1}{E_2}$?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $2$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $1$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{1}{2}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{1}{4}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The potential difference across each resistor in circuit 1 is $\dfrac{E_1}{2}$ due to series connection while it is $E_2$ in circuit 2 due to parallel connection. The power dissipated in the resistors is equal, and for each resistor the dissipated power is given by

- $P = \dfrac{V^2}{R}$

- $\dfrac{(\dfrac{E_1}{2})^2}{R} = \dfrac{(E_2)^2}{R}$

- $\dfrac{(E_1)^2}{2^2} = \dfrac{(E_2)^2}{1}$

- $\dfrac{(E_1)^2}{(E_2)^2} = \dfrac{4}{1}$

- $\dfrac{E_1}{E_2} = \dfrac{2}{1} = 2$
