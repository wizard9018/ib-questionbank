## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1. When current flows through the battery $\checkmark$

        There is a potential difference across the internal resistance $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
When there is a current flow through the cell, there is a potential difference across the internal resistance, and this results in a reduction in the potential difference across the terminals of the cell.

    2. Total V across variable resistance and internal resistance $=12-8.2=$ $3.8$ V $\checkmark$

        The current flow through the circuit = $\dfrac{3.8}{2.5+0.025} =$ $1.5$ A $\checkmark$

        *OR*

        $R=\dfrac{(8.2)(2.5+0.025)}{3.8}=5.4\ \mathrm{\Omega}$

        $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{5.4}{9.4} =$ :answer[$0.58\ \mathrm{\Omega}\,\ce{m^{-1}}$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
Within the circuit, the sum of potential differences across the resistors will be equal to the emf of the cell:

        $\hspace{1em}$ $12=8.2+V_{r+R}$

        Where $V_{r+R}$ is the sum of the potential difference across variable resistance and the internal resistance.

        $\hspace{1em}$ $V_{r+R}=12-8.2=3.8$ V

        The current flow through the circuit:

        $\hspace{1em}$ $I=\dfrac{V_{r+R}}{R+r}=\dfrac{3.8}{2.5+0.025}=1.5$ A

        By using $R=\dfrac{V}{I}$ across the coil:

        $\hspace{1em}$ $R=\dfrac{V}{I}=\dfrac{8.2}{1.5}=5.4\ \mathrm{\Omega}$

        *OR*

        The potential difference is shared among the resistors in proportion to their resistance values:

        $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{3.8}{2.5+0.025}=\dfrac{8.2}{R}$

        $\hspace{1em}$ $R=\dfrac{(8.2)(2.5+0.025)}{3.8}=5.4\ \mathrm{\Omega}$

        The resistance per meter = $\dfrac{5.4}{9.4}=0.58\ \mathrm{\Omega}\,\ce{m^{-1}}$

    3. The current flows clockwise in the circuit $\checkmark$

        The direction of magnetic field at point $P$ is downwards
or towards the bottom of the page $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
The current flows clockwise in the circuit. When the right-hand grip rule is applied to the current passing through the coil, the magnetic field direction at point $P$ can be found towards the bottom of the page. Since the compass needle aligns with the direction of the magnetic field, the compass needle should be pointing to the bottom of the page.

2. Straight line with negative gradient $\checkmark$

    No y or x intercepts $\checkmark$

    ![PH0670xxxx](images/PH0670xxxx.png)

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
The potential difference across the battery $V$:

    $\hspace{1em}$ $V=\varepsilon-Ir$

    Since the emf $\varepsilon$ and internal resistance $r$ do not change, this relationship can be modelled as the following:

    $\hspace{1em}$ $y=c-mx$

    where $x$ is $I$, $m$ is $r$, $c$ is $\varepsilon$ and $y$ is $V$

    This relationship indicates a straight line with a negative gradient. It is a closed circuit with emf. Therefore, the current will never be zero and the potential difference cannot be zero accordingly. Hence, the line does not have y and x-intercepts.
