# PH1812

The diagram shows a combination of resistors:

![Screenshot 2025-03-25 at 1.39.12 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-03-25 at 1.39.12 PM.JPEG>)

What is the effective resistance between points $A$ and $B$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{12}{5}\ \Omega$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{5}{2}\ \Omega$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{50}{11}\ \Omega$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $10\ \Omega$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

For resistors $R_1$, $R_2$, $R_3$ in series we have:

- $R_{total}=R_1+R_2+R_3+...$

For resistors $R_1$, $R_2$ in parallel we have:

- $\dfrac{1}{R_{total}}=\dfrac{1}{R_1}+\dfrac{1}{R_2}$

With respect to points $A$ and $B$, the $1\,\Omega$, $2\,\Omega$ and $3\,\Omega$ are in series so their combined resistance is given by:

- $R=1+2+3=6\,\Omega$

Between $A$ and $B$, we then have a parallel combination with $6\,\Omega$ and $4\,\Omega$, therefore:

- $\dfrac{1}{R_{total}}=\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{3}{12}+\dfrac{2}{12}=\dfrac{5}{12}$

- $R_{total}=\dfrac{12}{5}\Omega$
