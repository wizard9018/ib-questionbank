# PH0270

The following graph shows the relationship between current and potential difference across an unknown component in a series circuit.

![PH0270](images/PH0270.png)

Which of the following is true about the component?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The component is not a resistor.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The component is made of non-conducting material.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The component is a non-ohmic resistor.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The component is an ohmic resistor.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ All electrical components are resistors to some extent. Even the best conductors will take up some amount of energy as electricity flows through them.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ While all materials are conducting given the appropriate voltage, at voltages as low as those shown on the graph, no current would flow if the material was not a good conductor of electricity.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ According to the graph, the ratio $V$/$I$ is not constant. By definition, that indicates a non-ohmic resistor. C is the correct answer.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The voltage-current graph of an ohmic resistor is a straight line whose gradient represents a constant resistance.
