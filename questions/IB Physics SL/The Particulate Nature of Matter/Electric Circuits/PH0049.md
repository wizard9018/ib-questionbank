## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The voltage across $AB$ is also equal to the terminal voltage. For very low values of $R$, the terminal voltage will be zero. As $R$ increases and the current drops, the terminal voltage will approach the emf of the cell. Thus the correct graph will show voltage starting from zero and then leveling off to a fixed value.

Mathematically:

Terminal voltage is given by the equation $V_T = \varepsilon-Ir$, where $r$ is the internal resistance of the cell.

The current at any time is given by the formula $I=\dfrac{\varepsilon}{R+r}$

Combining the equations gives $V_T = \varepsilon-\dfrac{\varepsilon r}{R+r}$

Which factors to give $V_T = \varepsilon \left(1-\dfrac{r}{R+r}\right)$
