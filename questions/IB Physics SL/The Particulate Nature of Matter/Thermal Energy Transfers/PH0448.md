## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Average kinetic energy increases as the temperature increases and stays constant during the vapourisation process. Moreover, average kinetic energy is zero at absolute zero ($-273\degree$C) temperature, which is not the case for water ($0 - 100\degree$C). Therefore we need to choose an answer that has a non-zero intercept on the temperature axis.
