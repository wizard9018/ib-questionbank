# PH0346

In order to calculate the specific latent heat of fusion of ice, a mass $m$ of ice at a temperature of -2˚C was left inside a beaker until it changed into water at 20˚C.

![Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 4.43.52 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 4.43.52 PM.png>)

$\footnotesize{\textrm{[Source: Created with Chemix, https://chemix.org/]}}$

 The specific heat capacity of ice is $c_i$, the specific heat capacity of water is $c_w$, and the specific latent heat of fusion of ice is $L_F$.

If $q$ is the energy needed to make this change for 1 kilogram of ice, which expression gives the specific latent heat of fusion of ice $L_F$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $q-2c_i-20c_w$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $q-22c_i$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $q-22c_w$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $q-\dfrac{2c_i-20c_w}{m}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

The energy needed to change ice from -2$\degree$C to 20$\degree$C can be divided into:

- The energy needed to increase the temperature of ice from -2$\degree$C to 0$\degree$C: \

$mc_i \Delta T=mc_i (0-(-2))=2mc_i$

- The energy needed to change ice to water, without change in temperature: \

$mL$

- The energy needed to change water from 0$\degree$C to 20$\degree$C: \

$mc_w \Delta T=mc_w (20-0)=20mc_w$

The total energy needed = $2mc_i+mL+20mc_w$

Since $q$ is the energy for 1 kg, therefore the total energy needed = $mq$

So, to calculate L

- $mq=2mc_i+mL+20mc_w$  

- $L=\dfrac{mq-2mc_i-20mc_w}{m}$  

- $L=q-2c_i-20c_w$
