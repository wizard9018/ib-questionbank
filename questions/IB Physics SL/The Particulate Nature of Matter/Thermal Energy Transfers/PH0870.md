# PH0870

An insulated metal rod has cross-sectional area $10$ cm$^2$ and length $80$ cm. A power of $25$ W is supplied to the top face of the rod, whilst the bottom face is held at a constant temperature of $20\degree$C. Assume that the thermal conductivity of the metal is $400$ W m$^{-1}\degree$C$^{-1}$.

![Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 5.05.09 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 5.05.09 PM.png>)

What is the equilibrium temperature of the top face?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $22\degree$C

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $40\degree$C

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $50\degree$C

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $70\degree$C

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The rate of heat transfer from the top to bottom face is given by

- $\dfrac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t} = kA\dfrac{\Delta T}{\Delta x}=kA\dfrac{(T_{top}-T_{bottom})}{L}$

where $A$ is the cross-sectional area, $k$ is the thermal conductivity, $L$ is the length, and $\Delta T$ is the temperature difference between the top and bottom faces, which are at temperatures $T_{top}$ and $T_{bottom}$, respectively.

In equilibrium, the rate of heat transfer is equal to the power $P$ supplied to the top face:

- $P=kA\dfrac{T_{top}-T_{bottom}}{L}$

Rearranging to make $T_{top}$ the subject of the equation gives

- $\dfrac{LP}{kA}=T_{top}-T_{bottom}$

- $\therefore T_{top}=\dfrac{LP}{kA}+T_{bottom}$

Inserting the values given (remembering to convert to SI units) yields

$T_{top}=\dfrac{(0.8\,\ce{m})(25\,\ce{W})}{(400\,\ce{W}\,\ce{m}^{-1}\degree \ce{C}^{-1})(0.001\ \ce{m}^2)}+20\degree \ce{C}= 70\degree \ce{C}$
