# PH0869

Two vertical cylinders are filled with water. The cylinders are identical and do not lose heat to their surroundings. Heat is supplied to the top face of Cylinder **A** at a rate $P$, and the temperature change of the bottom face is measured by Temperature Sensor **A**. The bottom face of Cylinder **B** is also heated at a rate $P$, and Temperature Sensor **B** measures the temperature change of the top face.

![PH0869aaa](images/PH0869aaa.png)

Which temperature sensor measures the higher rate of temperature change?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Temperature Sensor **A**

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Temperature Sensor **B**
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ The rates are equal
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ The relative rates depend on the value of $P$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In Cylinder **A**, heat is transferred from the top face to the bottom face by thermal conduction and radiation. However, convection to the bottom face does not occur because the heated water at the top of the tube is less dense than the cooler water below.

In Cylinder **B**, the rates of conduction and radiation are identical to those in Cylinder **A**, but heated water at the bottom of the tube is displaced upward by cooler, denser water from above. Hence, the upward transference of heat in Cylinder **B** is greater than the downward transference of heat in Cylinder **A**.
