# PH1802

Which of the following best describes the phenomenon of convection?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Transfer of heat by electromagnetic waves.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Transfer of heat through direct contact between particles.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Transfer of heat through the vibration of particles in a solid.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Transfer of heat through the movement of fluid due to density differences.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Convection is the transfer of heat through the movement of a fluid (liquid or gas) due to differences in density. When a fluid is heated, it expands, becomes less dense, and the hot fluid rises, while cooler, denser fluid sinks, creating a convection current.

A is incorrect: This describes radiation, where heat energy is transferred by electromagnetic waves.

B is incorrect: This describes conduction, where heat transfers through direct contact between particles.

C is incorrect: This also describes conduction, particularly in solids, where heat spreads via particle vibrations without bulk movement.
