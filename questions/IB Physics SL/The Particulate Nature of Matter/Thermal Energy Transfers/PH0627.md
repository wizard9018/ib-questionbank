# PH0627

A chemical reaction occurs inside a solid of mass $m$ and specific heat capacity $c$. The initial temperature of the solid is $50$ K.

What is the minimum energy that the reaction must release to increase the temperature of the solid to 100$\degree$C?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $50 mc$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $100 mc$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $323 mc$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $373 mc$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The temperature of the solid is 373 K (100 + 273) after the reaction takes place, so a temperature change of $(373-50)=323$ K is required to achieve the target temperature.

The energy $Q$ needed for this change is related to the temperature change $\Delta T$ by the expression

- $Q =mc\Delta T=323mc$.
