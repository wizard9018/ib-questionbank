1. Define the specific latent heat of fusion. [2 marks]

An ice cube of temperature $-20\degree$C and mass $45$ g is placed in a bottle which is half filled with water of temperature $40\degree$C and mass $1.2$ kg. Energy transfer only happens between the water and the ice.   

The following data are available:

Specific latent heat of fusion of ice $= 3.3 \times 10^5$ J kg$^{-1}$

Specific heat capacity of ice $= 2.2 \times 10^3$ J kg$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$

Specific heat capacity of water $= 4.2 \times 10^3$ J kg$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$

2. 

    1. Thermal energy lost of water / $Q=m_w c_w\Delta T_w=(1.2)(4.2 \times 10^3)(40-T)=201600-5040T$ $\checkmark$

        The energy needed to increase the temperature of the ice from $-20°C$ to $0°C$ / $Q_{ice}=m_ic_i\Delta T_i=(45 \times 10^{-3})(2.2 \times 10^3)(20)=1980$ J

        **and**

        The energy needed to melt ice at $0°C$ / $Q_{melting}=m_iL=(45 \times 10^{-3})(3.3 \times 10^5)=14850$ J $\checkmark$

        The energy is needed to increase the temperature of melted ice (now it is water) from $0°C$ to $T$ / $Q_{iw}=m_ic_w\Delta T_{iw}=(45 \times 10^{-3})(4.2 \times 10^3)T=189\,T$ $\checkmark$

        Recognition of energy lost by water is transferred to ice to reach a common temperature in the form of water / $Q=Q_{ice}+Q_{melting}+Q_{iw}$ $\checkmark$

        

        - $\begin{aligned}

        201600-5040T&=1980+14850+189T\\

        \ 184770&=5229T\\

        \end{aligned}$

        

        - $\hspace{6.2em} T=$ :answer[$35\degree$C]

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        By assuming the final temperature of the water as $T$, the thermal energy lost $Q$ the water; 

        - $Q=m_w c_w\Delta T_w=(1.2)(4.2 \times 10^3)(40-T)=201600-5040T$

        The energy $Q_{ice}$ is needed to increase the temperature of the ice from $-20\degree$C to $0\degree$C.  

        - $Q_{ice}=m_ic_i\Delta T_i=(45 \times 10^{-3})(2.2 \times10^3)(20)=1980$ J

        The energy $Q_{melting}$ is needed to melt ice at $0\degree$C;

        - $Q_{melting}=m_iL=(45 \times 10^{-3})(3.3 \times 10^5)=14850$ J 

        The energy $Q_{iw}$ needed to increase the temperature of melted ice (now it is water) from $0\degree$C to $T$

        - $Q_{iw}=m_ic_w\Delta T_{iw}=(45 \times 10^{-3})(4.2 \times 10^3)T=189T$

        Since energy lost by water is taken by ice;

        $\begin{aligned}

        Q&=Q_{ice}+Q_{melting}+Q_{iw}\\

        201600-5040T&=1980+14850+189T\\

        184770&=5229T\\

        T&=35\degree\textrm{C}

        \end{aligned}$
