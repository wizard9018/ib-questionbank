# PH1095

Greenhouse gases absorb particular wavelengths of radiation and re-radiate the radiation as they transition back to lower energy states. Which is most correct for the type of radiation absorbed and the direction in which the radiation is emitted?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$Type of radiation$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$Direction of emission$\hspace{1.5em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Ultraviolet | All |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Ultraviolet | Towards the earth |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Infrared | All |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Infrared | Towards the earth |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Greenhouse gases in the Earth's atmosphere interact with infrared radiation. When these gases absorb infrared radiation from the Earth's surface and atmosphere, their molecules undergo energy changes, increasing their internal energy. As a result of the relaxation process, the molecules release the absorbed energy in the form of radiation. This emitted radiation is primarily in the form of infrared and can be scattered in any direction, not just towards the Earth. Therefore, the correct answer is that greenhouse gases emit infrared radiation in all directions during the relaxation process.
