# PH1094

Methane $CH_4$, carbon dioxide $CO_2$ and water vapor $H_2 O$ are three gases present in the atmosphere. Which of these gases are naturally occurring greenhouse gases?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Methane and carbon dioxide

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Carbon dioxide and water vapor

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Methane and water vapor

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Methane, carbon dioxide, and water vapor

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Methane is a naturally occurring greenhouse gas. It can be emitted from natural sources such as wetlands, as well as human activities like agriculture and the production of fossil fuels. Carbon dioxide is another naturally occurring greenhouse gas. It is emitted from natural sources like volcanic eruptions, respiration, and decay of organic matter. Water vapor is the gas form of water and is the most abundant greenhouse gas in the Earth's atmosphere. It is naturally present in the atmosphere and plays a crucial role in the Earth's climate system.
