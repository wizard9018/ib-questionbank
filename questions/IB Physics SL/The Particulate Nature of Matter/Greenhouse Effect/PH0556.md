# PH0556

**Maximum mark: 11**

1. 

    1. Define the albedo of a body [1 mark]

    2. It was suggested that painting rocky mountain tops with a white chalk-based paint could help reduce the impact of global warming. In terms of albedo, explain why this could work. [2 marks]

2. 

    1. Given that the radius of the Earth is $6.4 \times 10^6$ m, show that at any given time, the total amount of energy incident on the Earth's surface is approximately $1.8 \times 10^{17}$ J per second. [2 marks]

    2. The average albedo of the Earth is 0.30. What is the rate at which the Earth absorbs energy from the Sun? [1 mark]

    3. Using your answer from (b)(ii) and ignoring atmospheric effects, estimate the average temperature of the Earth for which equilibrium would be established. Assume the Earth's average emissivity is 0.80 [2 marks]

    4. Greenhouse gases in the atmosphere elevate the average temperature of the Earth such that it is higher than the answer found in (b)(iii). Explain the mechanism by which this occurs. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 

    1. Albedo is the fraction of the incident light that is reflected by a surface. $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        Normally expressed as a decimal, it is found from $\dfrac{\textrm{Power}_{\textrm{reflected}}}{\textrm{Power}_{\textrm{incident}}}$

    2. White is more reflective therefore has a higher albedo $\checkmark$

        Less radiation absorbed will result in less warming $\checkmark$

2. 

    1. $\hspace{1.8em}$ $A = \pi r^2 = \pi(6.4 \times 10^6)2 = 1.29 \times 10^{14}$ $\checkmark$

        - $P=AS$

        - $\hspace{0.8em}$ $=(1.29\times10^{14})(1.36\times10^3)$

        - $\hspace{0.8em}$ $=$ :answer[$1.75\times10^{17}$ W]

        

        (must show 3 or more significant figures to receive the second mark)

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        The shape that blocks the sunlight is a circle with the radius of the Earth. 

        

        - $A = \pi r^2 = \pi(6.4 \times 10^6)2 = 1.29 \times 10^{14}$

        That area multiplied by the solar constant will give the total incident energy.

        - $P=AS$

        - $\hspace{0.8em}$ $=(1.29\times10^{14})(1.36\times10^3)$

        - $\hspace{0.8em}$ $=1.75\times10^{17}$ W

        

    2. Power absorbed = $(1-0.3)(1.75 \times 10^{17})$ = :answer[$1.2 \times 10^{17}\,W$] (or $1.3\times10^{17}$  if $1.8 \times 10^{17}$ is used)

    3. $P = e \sigma A T^4$ , where $A = 4\pi(6.4 \times 10^6)^2$ $\checkmark$

        $T = \sqrt[4]{\dfrac{1.2 \,\times\, 10^{17}}{(0.80)(5.67\, \times\, 10^{-8})(4)(\pi)(6.4 \,\times \,10^6)^2}}$ = :answer[$268$ K] $\checkmark$

    4. Greenhouse gases absorb infrared radiation released by the Earth. $\checkmark$

        This radiation is re-radiated in all directions by the greenhouse gases. $\checkmark$

        With about 50% of this radiation returning back to the Earth's surface $\checkmark$
