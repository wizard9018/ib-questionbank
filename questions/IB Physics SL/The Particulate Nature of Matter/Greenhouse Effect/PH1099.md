# PH1099

Which of the following is **not** a reason why the solar constant is regarded as an average value rather than a fixed value?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Elliptical orbit of the Earth

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Solar flares on the Sun

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Variations in atmospheric composition

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Solar cycles

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Variations in atmospheric composition do not directly influence the calculation of the solar constant. The solar constant primarily represents the average amount of solar radiation received per unit area at a distance of one astronomical unit (AU) from the Sun. While changes in atmospheric composition can affect how much solar radiation is absorbed or reflected by Earth's atmosphere, they are not a direct factor in determining the solar constant itself. Therefore, option C is the correct choice as it is not one of the reasons why the solar constant is regarded as an average value rather than an absolute value.
