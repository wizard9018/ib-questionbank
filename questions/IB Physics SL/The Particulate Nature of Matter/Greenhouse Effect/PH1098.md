# PH1098

A grey body of emissivity $e$ and albedo $1-e$ has a total surface area of $A$ cm$^2$. The absolute temperatures of the body and the surroundings are $T$ and $\dfrac{T}{2}$ respectively. The surroundings can be considered as a black body. The net power, in watts, transferred from the body to the surroundings is

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{1}{16}eA\sigma T^4$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{1}{16}e(A\times10^{-4})\sigma T^4$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{15}{16}eA\sigma T^4$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $\dfrac{15}{16}e(A\times10^{-4})\sigma T^4$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The power emitted $P_e$ by the body:

- $P_e=e(A\times10^{-4})\sigma T^4$

The albedo is given as $1 - e$, so the fraction of incident power absorbed will be $e$.

The power received $P_r$ by the body from surroundings:

- $P_r=e(A\times10^{-4})\sigma\Bigl(\dfrac{T}{2}\Bigr)^4=e(A\times10^{-4})\sigma \dfrac{T^4}{16}$

The net power $P$ transferred:

- $P=P_e-P_r=e(A\times10^{-4})\sigma T^4-e(A\times10^{-4})\sigma \dfrac{T^4}{16} = \dfrac{15}{16} e(A\times10^{-4})\sigma T^4$
