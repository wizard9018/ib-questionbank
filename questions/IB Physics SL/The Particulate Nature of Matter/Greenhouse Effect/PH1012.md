# PH1012

**Maximum mark: 11**

1. A house uses a solar heating panel with an area of $1.7$ m$^2$. The panel receives an average solar intensity of $240$ W m$^{-2}$. The albedo of the solar panel is 0.30.

    1. State the name of the type of solar panel that generates electricity. [1 mark]

    2. Show that the absorbed power by the solar heating panel is around $290$ W. [1 mark]

    3.     The solar panel only transfers $73$ J of heat to the water that circulates just below it every second. Determine the efficiency of the panel. [1 mark]

2. The water has a specific heat capacity of $4200$ J kg$^{-1}$ K$^{-1}$ and its temperature rises by $32\degree$ C.

    1. Calculate the mass of the water that circulates in one minute. [3 marks]

    2. Many solar panels use a mixture of propylene glycol and water to prevent water from freezing. Propylene glycol has a higher molar mass than water. Compare the mean square speed of the molecules of propylene glycol to the speed of the molecules of water. [2 marks]

3. Outline one advantage and disadvantage of using solar photovoltaic cells  to generate electricity compared to nuclear power stations. [2 marks]

    2. Incident power on the solar panel $=(240)(1.7)=410$ W $\checkmark$

        - $(0.7)(410 )=286$ W $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The power $P$ of incident light on the solar heating panel:

        - $P=IA=(240)(1.7)=410$ W

        The power absorbed $P_0$ by the solar heating panel:

        - $P_0=(0.7)(410 )=286$ W

    3. $\hspace{1.8em}$ $\dfrac{73}{290}= $ :answer[0.25] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        The efficiency $\eta$:

        - $\eta=\dfrac{\textrm{useful  power out}}{\textrm{total power in}} =\dfrac{73}{290}=0.25$

2. 1. Heat supplied in one minute $=(73)(60)=4400$ J $\checkmark$

        - $m=\dfrac{4400}{(4200)(32)}$ $\checkmark$

        - $\hspace{0.9em}$ = :answer[$3.3\times10^{-2}$ kg] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        The heat $Q$ supplied to the water in one minute:

        - $Q= P\,t=(73)(60)=4400$ J

        - $Q=mc\Delta T$

        - $m=\dfrac{Q}{c\Delta T}=\dfrac{4380}{(4200)(32)}=3.3\times 10^{-2}$ kg

    2. Both of the liquids have the same temperature at any time so the average kinetic energy of the molecules is the same

        Greater mass results in lower mean square speed therefore propylene glycol molecules are slower/water molecules are faster $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        Both of the liquids have the same temperature at any time. By using the following formula:

        - $E_k=\dfrac{3}{2}k_bT$

        They should have the same average kinetic energy for their molecules. By using the formula of kinetic energy:

        - $E_k=\dfrac{1}{2}m<c^2>$

        Greater mass results in a smaller mean square speed. Therefore, propylene glycol molecules will be slower than water molecules.
