1.    An object $X$ of surface area $24.3$ cm$^2$ emits $300$ W of radiation when its temperature is $1215$ K.

    1. State what is meant by emissivity. [1 mark]

    2. Show that this object is a blackbody radiator. [3 marks]

2. Calculate the peak wavelength of the emitted radiation. [2 marks]

3. The object $X$ is left to cool down until it reaches $800$ K. On the axes given, sketch the variation of intensity with wavelength of the radiation of object $X$ at both the original $1215$ K and the cooler temperature of $800$ K. Label the graphs. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 

    1. The ratio of the power emitted by a surface to the power emitted by a black body of the same dimensions at the same temperature. $\checkmark$

    2. $\hspace{1.8em}$ $\ll P = e\sigma AT^4\gg$

        - $e=\dfrac{300}{(5.67\times 10^{-8})(24.3\times 10^{-4})(1215)^4}$ $\checkmark$

        - $e\approx 1$ $\checkmark$

        The emissivity of a blackbody is 1 / the object is a blackbody since emissivity is **OWTTE** $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        The power of the radiation is given by

        - $P=e\sigma AT^4$

        Solving for emissivity ($e$) gives

        - $e=\dfrac{300}{(5.67\,\times \,10^{-8})(24.3\,\times\, 10^{-4})(1215)^4}\approx 1$

        The emissivity of a blackbody is 1

        This means that this object is a blackbody. 

2. $\hspace{1.8em}$ $\ll \lambda_{max}=\dfrac{2.90\times 10^{-3}}{T}\gg$

    - $\lambda_{max}=\dfrac{2.90 \times 10^{-3}}{1215}$ $\checkmark$

    - $\lambda_{max}$ = :answer[$2.39\times 10^{-6}\ll\textrm{ m}\gg$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    The peak wavelength can be found by Wein's displacement law, where $T$ is the kelvin temperature.

    - $\lambda_{max}=\dfrac{2.90 \times 10^{-3}}{T}$

    - $\lambda_{max}=\dfrac{2.90 \times 10^{-3}}{1215}=2.39 \times 10^{-6}$ m
