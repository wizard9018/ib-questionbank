# PH0561

**Maximum mark: 9**

The Sun radiates energy to the surface of the Earth. Some of this energy is scattered and the rest reaches the Earth's surface.

![PH0561b](images/PH0561b.png)

1. Differentiate between heat transfer methods: conduction, convection and thermal radiation. [3 marks]

2. At any given time, the power of the radiation of the Sun absorbed by the Earth's surface is $P$. $P$ is given by

   $\hspace{1em}P=(1-\alpha)S\pi R^2$

   Where $\alpha$ is the albedo of the Earth's surface.

    

    1. Show that the average intensity of radiation $I_{avg}$ that is absorbed by the Earth's surface is given by $I_{avg}=\frac{(1-\alpha)S}{4}$ [2 marks]

    2. Calculate the average intensity of the radiation that is absorbed by the Earth's surface. The Earth's albedo is 0.3. [1 mark]

Some of the infrared radiation from the Earth is absorbed and reradiated by the greenhouse gases.

3. Explain this absorption and emission of radiation in terms of molecular energy levels. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Conduction is the transfer of thermal energy through atomic vibrations $\checkmark$

    Convection is the transfer of thermal energy resulting from the mass movement of groups of atoms or molecules in fluids $\ll $often arising  from differences in density in a gravitational field. $\gg$ $\checkmark$

    Thermal radiation is the transfer of thermal energy by electromagnetic radiation. $\checkmark$

2. 

    1. $\hspace{1.8em}$ $I=\dfrac{Power}{Area}$ / intensity is power divided by area $\checkmark$

        - $I_{avg}=\dfrac{(1-\alpha)S\pi R^2}{4\pi R^2}$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        The intensity of radiation can be found from

        - $I=\dfrac{Power}{Area}$

        Considering the Earth's surface to be spherical, then the average intensity of radiation absorbed is

        - $I_{avg}=\dfrac{(1-\alpha)S\pi R^2}{4\pi R^2}$

        

        - $I_{avg}=\dfrac{(1-\alpha)S}{4}$

    2. $\hspace{1.8em}$ $\ll I_{avg}=\dfrac{(1-\alpha)S}{4}\gg$

        - $\ll I_{avg}=\dfrac{(1-0.3)(1.36\times 10^3)}{4}\gg$ 

        - $I_{avg}$ = :answer[$238\ll\textrm{ W m}^{-2}\gg$] $\checkmark$

3. Energy difference between molecular energy levels matches the energy of an infrared photon. $\checkmark$

    Infrared photons are absorbed. $\checkmark$

    Excited gas molecules return to their lower energy states and emit the absorbed photons. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    The different energy states of the greenhouse gas molecules also correspond to the resonant frequencies of the molecules. The emitted infrared photons match these resonant frequencies and are absorbed by the greenhouse gases. The photons are then re-emitted when the greenhouse gas molecules exit their resonant state.
