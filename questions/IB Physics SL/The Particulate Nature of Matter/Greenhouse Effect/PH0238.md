# PH0238

Which of the following is a non-renewable energy resource?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Nuclear

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Wind

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Solar

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Geothermal

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Non-renewable sources are sources where the rate of replenishment is much longer than a human lifespan. Examples include nuclear, coal, oil, and natural gas. Therefore, the answer is A.
