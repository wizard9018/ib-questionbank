# PH0140

On a certain day, the solar intensity on the Earth's surface is 300 W m$^{-2}$. Photovoltaic cells which have an efficiency of 20% are used to convert solar energy to electrical energy. What should be the minimum area of the photovoltaic cells to produce an output of 6.0 kW assuming that the incident light is normal to the surface of the panel?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 20 m$^2$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 40 m$^2$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 100 m$^2$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 3000 m$^2$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Efficiency is the ratio of power output to power input expressed as a percentage. 

- Efficiency = $\dfrac{\textrm{Power output}}{\textrm{Power input}} \times 100$

- Power output = 6000 W

- Power input = (300)(Area)

- $0.2=\dfrac{6000}{300(A)}$

    

- $A=\dfrac{6000}{(0.2)(300)}$

   

- $A=100$ m$^2$
