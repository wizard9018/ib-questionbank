# PH1113

Which of the following effects would result in an increase in the average temperature of the Earth?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Increased surface albedo   |
  | II. | Increased atmospheric transmission of visible light |
  | III. | Increased atmospheric absorption of infra-red radiation |

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and II

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and III

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ II and III

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Surface albedo refers to the proportion of incident radiation reflected by the Earth’s surface. Increasing the albedo decreases the amount of energy absorbed by the surface and subsequently re-emitted as infra-red radiation that can be trapped by the atmosphere; thus, increasing albedo reduces the average temperature of the Earth.

Increasing the atmospheric transmission of visible light enables more energy to reach the Earth’s surface, contributing to an increase in the Earth’s temperature. Subsequent thermal emissions from the surface are predominantly in the infra-red region of the electromagnetic spectrum, meaning that visible light transmittivity has negligible impact on the trapping of radiation by greenhouse gases.

Finally, increasing atmospheric absorption of infra-red radiation enhances the greenhouse effect, as this change increases the proportion of thermal emissions from the Earth’s surface that become trapped due to absorption and subsequent re-emission by greenhouse gases.
