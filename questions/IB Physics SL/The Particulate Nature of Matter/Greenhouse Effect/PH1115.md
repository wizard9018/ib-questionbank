# PH1115

An experiment is carried out to investigate the absorption of electromagnetic radiation by four gases of equal molar concentration. The proportion A of energy absorbed is plotted versus spectral frequency $f$ in each case. Which of the four gases would make the greatest contribution to the greenhouse effect?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Greenhouse gases contribute to the greenhouse effect by absorbing and then re-emitting thermal radiation from the Earth's surface, which is predominantly in the infra-red portion of the electromagnetic spectrum. Consequently, the gases that produce the greatest greenhouse effect are those that absorb most strongly at infra-red frequencies. Whilst Gas B and Gas C both absorb a large total amount of radiation over the measured range of frequencies, very little absorption occurs in the infra-red range. Similarly, despite Gas D absorbing nearly all UV radiation, it absorbs only a small quantity of infra-red waves. By contrast, Gas A absorbs the highest quantity of infra-red radiation, even though it produces the lowest total absorption over the entire electromagnetic spectrum.
