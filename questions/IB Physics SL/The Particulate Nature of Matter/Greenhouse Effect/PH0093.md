# PH0093

Which of the following energy resources is non-renewable?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Biomass

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Solar

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Wind

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Uranium-235

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Uranium-235 is a rare radioactive isotope on Earth, extracted from uranium ores. The available uranium is limited and will be depleted in time. Biomass will replenish in a human lifetime, so is considered renewable.
