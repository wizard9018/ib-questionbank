# PH0060

An object  has a surface area of $2.0$ m$^2$ and emissivity $e=0.5$. The object emits electromagnetic radiation of peak wavelength $2.90 \times 10^{-5}$ m. What is the best estimate for the energy radiated by the object in one second?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $1.4$ J

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $2.8$ J

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $5.7$ J

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $11.3$ J

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

From the data booklet, $\lambda T=2.9\times 10^{-3}$

Which gives $T=\dfrac{2.9\,\times\,10^{-3}}{2.9\,\times\,10^{-5}}=100\,K$

Also from the data booklet $P=e\sigma AT^4$

$P=(0.5)(5.67\times 10^{-8})(2)(100)^4=5.67\,W\cong 5.7\,$J in one second
