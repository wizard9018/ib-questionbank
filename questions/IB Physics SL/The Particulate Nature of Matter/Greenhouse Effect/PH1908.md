# PH1908

The diagram below shows a simple energy balance model for the Earth.

![Screenshot 2025-09-24 at 10.18.04 AM](<images/Screenshot 2025-09-24 at 10.18.04 AM.png>)

The intensity of radiation reflected by the clouds is half the intensity of the radiation absorbed by the clouds. What is the intensity of the radiation absorbed by the clouds?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 10 W m$^{-2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 15 W m$^{-2}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 20 W m$^{-2}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 30 W m$^{-2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In the energy balance model, the total energy absorbed and reflected is equal to the incoming energy.

Let the intensity reflected by clouds be $X$ and the intensity absorbed by the clouds be $Y$.

- $340=20+50+X+Y+70+170$

- $X+Y=30$

The intensity of radiation reflected by the clouds is half the intensity of the radiation absorbed by the clouds so $X=\dfrac{Y}{2}$.

- $\dfrac{Y}{2}+Y=30$

- $\dfrac{3}{2}Y=30$

- $Y=20$ W m$^{-2}$
