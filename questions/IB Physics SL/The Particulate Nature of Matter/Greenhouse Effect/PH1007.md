# PH1007

**Maximum mark: 7**

The government of a medium sized city plans to replace coal power plants with photovoltaic cells to power the city.

The following data are available:

- $\bullet \hspace{1em}$ Total area available for photovoltaic cells    = 62.6 km$^2$
- $\bullet \hspace{1em}$ Maximum intensity of light incident on photovoltaic cells = $1.36 \times 10^3$ W m$^{-2}$
- $\bullet \hspace{1em}$ Average efficiency of energy conversion in photovoltaic cells = 20 $\%$
- $\bullet \hspace{1em}$ Hours of sunshine in one month    = 150 hours

1. Explain why the government\'s choice to reduce its reliance on coal would be beneficial to the Earth\'s climate. [2 marks]

2. Outline, in terms of energy changes, how electrical energy is generated from coal.  [3 marks]

3. Using the data given, calculate in GJ the maximum expected energy generation for one month. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Burning coal emits carbon dioxide (CO$_2$) $\checkmark$

    Which is a greenhouse gas/traps heat radiated by the Earth $\checkmark$

2. The chemical energy of coal is transferred to the internal energy of the water/steam $\checkmark$

    The internal energy of the water gives kinetic energy to rotate a turbine  $\checkmark$

    The kinetic energy of the turbine is transferred to electrical energy in the generator/dynamo $\checkmark$

3. $(1.36 \times 10^3 )(62.6 \times 10^6 )(150)(3600)(0.20)$ $\checkmark$

    :answer[$9.2 \times 10^6  \ll\textrm{GJ} \gg$]  $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
    150 hours = $(150)(3600) = 540 \ 000 \textrm{ s}$

    $62.6 \textrm{ km}^2 = 62.6 \times 10^6 \textrm{ m}^2$

    $\begin{aligned}
    \textrm{Total Incident Energy} &= \textrm{Energy Intensity} \times \textrm{Area} \times \textrm{Time} \\
    &= (1.36 \times 10^3 )(62.6 \times 10^6 )( 540 \ 000 ) \\
    &= 4.6 \times 10^{16}\textrm{ J}
    \end{aligned}$

    $\begin{aligned}
    \textrm{Electrical energy obtained} &= \textrm{Total energy } \times \textrm{Efficiency} \\
    &= (4.6 \times 10^{16})(0.20)\\
    &= 9.2 \times 10^{15}\textrm{ J}\\
    &= 9.2 \times 10^6\textrm{ GJ}
    \end{aligned}$
