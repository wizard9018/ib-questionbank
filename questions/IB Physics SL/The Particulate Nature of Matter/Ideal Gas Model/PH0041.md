# PH0041

Ideal gases obey the assumptions in the kinetic model. The behaviour of real gases is different from ideal gases. Which of the following statements about an ideal gas is **not** correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Forces between ideal gas molecules are negligible when not undergoing a collision.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ All molecules in an ideal gas have negligible volume.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Ideal gases can be liquefied.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Collisions of ideal gas molecules with each other are elastic.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

A gas can be liquefied if there are forces of attraction acting on gas molecules. According to the kinetic model, forces between gas molecules are negligible. Therefore, ideal gases can not be liquefied.
