# PH0032

A quantity of 2000 kg Carbon Dioxide ($\ce{CO2}$) is maintained in a container tank. The molar mass of Carbon Dioxide ($\ce{CO2}$) is 44 g mol$^{-1}$. Determine the correct estimate of the number of Carbon Dioxide ($\ce{CO2}$) molecules in the container tank.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $10^{22}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $10^{25}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $10^{28}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $10^{31}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

$N=N_A \times \dfrac{m}{M}$

$N=(6.02 \times 10^{23}) (\dfrac{2000 \times 10^3}{44}) \approx 300 \times 10^{26} \approx 10^{28}$
