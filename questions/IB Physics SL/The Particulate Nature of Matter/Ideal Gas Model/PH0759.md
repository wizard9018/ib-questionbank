# PH0759

A 10 L cylinder containing nitrogen gas at a pressure of $20$ MPa is used to fill a $20$ L cylinder that initially contains nitrogen gas at a pressure of $2$ MPa. The two cylinders are at equal temperature.

![PH0759a](images/PH0759a.png)

What is the final pressure inside the two cylinders?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $8$ MPa

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $11$ MPa

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $14$ MPa

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $22$ MPa

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The number of molecules in Cylinders 1 and 2 are respectively given by:

$\hspace{1em}$ $N_1=\dfrac{P_1V_1}{k_BT}$

$\hspace{1em}$ $N_2=\dfrac{P_2V_2}{k_BT}$

The final number of molecules in the two cylinders must equal the sum of these two values; hence,

$\hspace{1em}$ $N_f=\dfrac{P_f\left(V_1+V_2\right)}{k_BT}=N_1+N_2$

$\hspace{1em}$ ${\therefore P}_f\left(V_1+V_2\right)=P_1V_1+P_2V_2$

$\hspace{1em}$ ${\therefore P}_f=\dfrac{P_1V_1+P_2V_2}{V_1+V_2}=\dfrac{\left(20\textrm{ MPa}\right)\left(10\textrm{ L}\right)+\left(2\textrm{ MPa}\right)\left(20\textrm{ L}\right)}{\left(10\textrm{ L}+20\textrm{ L}\right)}=8$ MPa

(Answer **B** results from averaging the two starting pressures without taking the cylinder volumes into account.

Answer **C** is obtained by mistakenly switching the volumes of the two cylinders.

Answer **D** is equal to the sum of the two initial pressures.)
