# PH0098

Which of the following real gases at atmospheric pressure would be least likely to approximate to an ideal gas? 

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Helium gas at temperature of 56 K

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Oxygen gas at temperature of 500 K

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Nitrogen at temperature of 374 K

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Hydrogen gas at temperature of 22 K

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Ideal gas approximation is only applicable for low pressure and high temperature. This is because when the particles are close together (high pressure) or interact with slower speeds (low temperatures), intermolecular forces start to play a role in their interactions. One of the assumptions of ideal gases is that there are no intermolecular forces.

For the gases listed, the assumption is least applicable for hydrogen gas at 22 K.
