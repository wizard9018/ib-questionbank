# PH0757

A piston contains ideal gas in a volume of $100$ cm$^3$ at a pressure of $100$ kPa. The piston is compressed until it has a volume of $40$ cm$^3$ and a pressure of $125$ kPa.

![PH0757aaaa](images/PH0757aaaa.png)

What is the ratio of the final temperature to the initial temperature?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.32

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.50

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 1.00

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 2.00

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The temperature of the ideal gas is given by

$\hspace{1em}$ $T=\dfrac{PV}{nR}$

Because $nR$ is constant for both gases, the ratio of the final temperature to the initial temperature can be expressed as

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{T_f}{T_i}=\dfrac{\left(125\textrm{ kPa}\right)\left(40\textrm{ cm}^3\right)}{\left(100\textrm{ kPa}\right)\left(100\textrm{ cm}^3\right)}=\dfrac{5000}{10000}=0.50$

(Answer **A** is obtained by mistakenly switching the initial and final pressure values.

One arrives at answer **C** by invoking an incorrect assumption that no temperature change can occur during ideal gas compression.

Answer **D** results from calculating the reciprocal ratio.)
