# PH0859

The mass of a nucleus of $\ce{^{12}_{6}C}$ in g is $m_c$. $N_A$ is Avogadro's constant in $\ce{mol^{-1}}$.

Which of the following is equivalent to 1 g?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{N_A m_c}{12}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $N_A m_c$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{N_A}{12m_c}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{N_A}{m_c}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The unified atomic mass unit $u$ is defined as $\dfrac{1}{12}$ of the mass of a carbon-12 atom. So $u=\dfrac{m_c}{12}$

Avogadro's constant, $N_A$ is the number of molecules in one mole of a substance (which is also the number of unified atomic mass units in 1 g)

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{N_Am_c}{12}=N_Au=1$ g
