# PH0872

A cylindrical piston of internal volume $V$ is filled with $N$ identical gas molecules at temperature $T$. Three changes are proposed to **increase** the pressure inside the piston:

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Increase $V$ without changing $N$ or $T$      |
  | II. | Increase $N$ without changing $V$ or $T$ |
  | III. | Increase $T$ without changing $N$ or $V$|

Which of the proposed changes would work?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Option I causes pressure $P$ to decrease as $V$ is increased without changing temperature $T$ or molecular quantity $N$:

- $P=\dfrac{Nk_B T}{V}    ∝ \dfrac{1}{V} \hspace{2em}$ (constant $N$ and $T$)

Conversely, Option II results in increased $P$ because $N$ is increased

- $P=\dfrac{Nk_B T}{V}  ∝ N \hspace{2em}$(constant $T$ and $V$)

Finally, from the same expression for $P$, it follows that Option III also yields an increase in $P$ when $T$ is increased:

- $P=\dfrac{Nk_B T}{V} ∝T\hspace{2em}$ (constant N and V )

Therefore options II and III will result in an increase in pressure.
