# PH0322

Ultrasound incident on a moving blood cell is reflected and received by a detector.

 ![PH0322](images/PH0322.png)

The frequency of an incident wave is $f$ and the measured frequency by the detector is $f^{\prime}$. The angle between the directions of the reflected wave and blood velocity $v$ is $\theta$. Which of the following quantities is $f^{\prime}$ dependent on?

- A. $\hspace{1em}       v $ only

- B. $\hspace{1em}       v $ and $f\ $only

- C. $\hspace{1em}       \theta $ and $f$ only

- D. $\hspace{1em}       v,\ \theta$ and $f $

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

This is the method used for measuring the flow rate of blood by using the Doppler effect. According to the Doppler effect, the observed frequency is dependent on emitted frequency $f$, the component of speed $v$ towards the observer. Since the change in angle $\theta$ or $v$ may result in a change in the component of $v$ towards the observer, they both affect $f^{\prime}$.
