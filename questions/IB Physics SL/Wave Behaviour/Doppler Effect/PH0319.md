## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

When there is a relative approach between the source of the wave and the receiver, the observed frequency ($f$) is higher than the emitted frequency ($f_0$) from the source due to the doppler effect. The produced wave is light. Therefore, the relationship between speed and the change in frequency;

- $\dfrac{\Delta f}{f}≈\dfrac{v}{c}$

Since $f>f_0, \hspace{2em} \Delta f=f-f_0 $

- $\dfrac{f-f_0\ }{f_0}\approx\dfrac{v}{c}$

This relationship suggests that an increase in $v$ will result in an increase in $f$ at a constant rate. So the graph should be a straight line. Additionally, when the speed is zero, the observed frequency must be equal to the emitted frequency of light. Therefore, the graph should start from a non-zero y-intercept. The only graph that satisfies these conditions is C.
