# PH0775

Hubble's law states that the speed at which galaxies recede from Earth is proportional to their distance from Earth. The velocities of galaxies can be determined by their Doppler redshift.

A certain galaxy is moving away from Earth with a velocity of $10^6\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$. If a particular detected wavelength line is 600 nm, what is the magnitude of the Doppler shift in the wavelength?

- A. $\hspace{1.5em}$ 0.2 nm

- B. $\hspace{1.5em}$ 2 nm

- C. $\hspace{1.5em}$ 10 nm

- D. $\hspace{1.5em}$ 20 nm

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

- $\dfrac{\Delta\lambda}{\lambda} \approx \dfrac{v}{c} $

- $\Delta\lambda = \lambda \Bigl(\dfrac{v}{c}\Bigr) = (600 \times10^{-9})\Bigl(\dfrac{10^6}{(3 \times 10^8)}\Bigr) $

- $\hspace{1.8em} = 2 \times10^{-9}\,\ce{m} = 2$ nm
