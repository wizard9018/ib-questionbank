# PH0999

A theory to describe observed changes in the pitch of fast-moving sources of sound was first developed in 1842. This same theory later played an important role in interpreting evidence for which of the following?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ the equivalence of the electric and magnetic forces

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ the particulate nature of light

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ the wave-like nature of particles

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ the expansion of the universe

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

The Doppler effect was first analyzed in the context of sound waves. Later it was found that this same effect applies to light waves. Light from distant galaxies in all directions appears $\lq$redshifted' (longer wavelength than it should) which tells us these galaxies are moving away from us. From this it can be concluded that either, we are at the center of the universe (very unlikely), or the universe is expanding!
