# PH0241

Incident light of wavelength $600\,\ce{nm}$ is emitted towards two slits separated by $10\, \ce{\mu m}$. The interference pattern is observed on a wall. The distance between two adjacent maxima is 0.12 m. What is the distance of the wall from the slits?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 2.0 mm

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 2.0 cm

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 2.0 dm

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 2.0 m

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

For the distance of the wall;

- $D = \dfrac{sd}{\lambda}$

- $D = \dfrac{(10 \,\times \,10^{-6}\,\ce{m})(0.12\,\ce{m})}{600 \,\times \,10^{-9}\,\ce{m}}$

- *D* = 2 m
