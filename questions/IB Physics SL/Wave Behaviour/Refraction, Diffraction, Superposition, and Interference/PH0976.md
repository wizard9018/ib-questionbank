# PH0976

A microwave has a wavelength of 1 cm when it is traveling through a medium of refractive index 2.0. What is the nature of the wave and the number of oscillations per second?

$\\$

| | $\hspace{1.5em}$Nature of the wave$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $\hspace{1.5em}$Number of oscillations per second$\hspace{1.5em}$  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Longitudinal | $1.5\times10^{10}$ |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Longitudinal | $6.0\times10^{10}$ |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Transverse | $1.5\times10^{10}$ |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Transverse | $6.0\times10^{10}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

A microwave is an electromagnetic wave. All electromagnetic waves are transverse - oscillations are perpendicular to the direction of energy transfer. (Electromagnetic waves are transverse oscillations in the electromagnetic field).

Number of oscillations per second is frequency.

- $v=f\lambda$

- $f=\dfrac{v}{\lambda}$

Speed of microwave in a vacuum is the speed of light $=3\times 10^8\ \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$.

- $\dfrac{n_{vacuum}}{n_{medium}}=\dfrac{v_{medium}}{v_{vacuum}}$

- $\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{v_{medium}}{3\times 10^8}$

- $v_{medium}=\dfrac{3\times 10^8}{2}=1.5\times 10^8$

- $f=\dfrac{v}{\lambda}=\dfrac{1.5\times 10^8}{1\times 10^{-2}}=1.5\times 10^{10}$
