# PH0525

**Maximum mark: 9**

Two speakers emit identical sound waves. A sound sensor moves along the line $XY$. As the sound sensor moves it detects locations where there is almost no sound. Some distances between the speakers and points $A$ and $B$ are given on the diagram.

![PH0525](images/PH0525.png)

1. Explain why there are quiet locations. [3 marks]

2. The speed of sound is $340\ \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$. Point $O$ is where the highest intensity is received by the sound sensor. Point $A$ is the first quiet spot as the sensor moves from $O$ to $X$ and point $B$ is the second quiet spot as the sensor moves from $O$ to $Y$.

    1. Show that the frequency of the sound is approximately 140 Hz. [3 marks]

    2.     Calculate the distance $|S_1-B|$, distance between Speaker 1 and point $B$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Sound waves from Speaker 1 and Speaker 2 superpose/interfere with each other. $\checkmark$

    At some points path difference is half integer of wavelength of sound wave/waves arrive out of phase $\checkmark$

    So destructive interference occurs $\checkmark$

2. 

    1. Path difference for point $A$ $\hspace{1em} 3.4 - 2.2 = 1.2\ \ce{m}$  $\checkmark$

        - $\Delta s=(n+\dfrac{1}{2})\lambda$, $\hspace{1em} n=0$ $\checkmark$

        - $\lambda=(2)(1.2)=2.4\ \ce{m}$

        - $\lambda f=v$

        - $f=\dfrac{340}{2.4}=141.6\approx 140\ \ce{Hz}$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        At the first minimum away from the central maximum, the path length difference between the two waves will be half a wavelength which will mean that the two waves arrive out of phase with one another at that point.

        The path length difference can be found from $3.4 - 2.2 = 1.2\ \ce{m}$

        The wavelength is therefore $2(1.2) = 2.4\ \ce{m}$

        Then using the universal wave equation, 

        - $f=\dfrac{v}{λ}=\dfrac{340}{2.4}=141.6\approx 140\ \ce{Hz}$ 

    2.  ::indent

        - $\Delta s=(n+\dfrac{1}{2})\lambda, \hspace{1em} n=1$ $\checkmark$

        - $\Delta s=(\dfrac{3}{2})\lambda = 3.6\ \ce{m}$ $\checkmark$

        - $\Delta s = |S_1-B| - |S_2-B|$

        - $|S1-B| = 3.6 + 2.1$ = :answer[$5.7\ \ce{m}$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        As point $B$ is the second location of destructive interference (on the second nodal line), the path length difference will be $(\frac{3}{2})\lambda = 3.6\ \ce{m}$

        As the distance from $B$ to $S_2$ is known to be $2.1\ \ce{m}$, the distance from $B$ to $S_1$ must be $3.6 + 2.1 = 5.7\ \ce{m}$
