## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

We know that particles in solids are more tightly bound to one another than particles in the gas. As a result, each particle will move faster back to equilibrium upon being disturbed, and so the speed of sound in the solid will be greater than the speed of sound in the gas.

The frequency, or rate of oscillations stays constant when the wave travels from the solid to the gas. 

The wave equation: $v = \lambda f \rightarrow \lambda = \dfrac{v}{f}$

Therefore, if the wave speed increases in the solid relative to the gas, the wavelength increases as well.

The wavelength of the original wave is approximately 6, which eliminates choices A and C.

The particles in solids are far closer to each other than that in the gas. So, the particles displacement will be less in magnitude than that in the gas, and the graph will have decreased amplitude. This means that the only reasonable choice is Option D.
