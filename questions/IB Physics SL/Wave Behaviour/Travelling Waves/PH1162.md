# PH1162

Which of the following is NOT true for both mechanical and electromagnetic waves?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ They can be transverse

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ They transfer energy

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ The wavelength is inversely proportional to the frequency of the wave in the same medium

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ They can be longitudinal

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

 Electromagnetic waves are always composed of transverse oscillations of electric and magnetic fields, with the planes of oscillation of the two fields perpendicular to one another.

All other statements are true for both types of wave.
