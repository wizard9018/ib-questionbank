# PH0376

A radio wave of frequency 90 MHz travels a distance $d$, which is equivalent to 6000 wavelengths of the wave. What is d?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $1.8\,\ce{km}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $3.3\,\ce{km}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $20\,\ce{km}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $200\,\ce{km}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

First, we determine the wavelength using the wave equation:

- $c=f\lambda$

and radio waves travel at the speed of light, $3 \times 10^8\, \ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$

- $c=f\lambda$ 

- $\lambda = \dfrac{c}{f}$

- = $\dfrac{3\,\times \,10^8}{90\,\times \,10^6}=\dfrac{10}{3}\,\ce{m}$

The number of waves $N$ can be found by dividing the total distance d by the wavelength:

- $N = \dfrac{d}{\lambda}$

giving

- $\begin{aligned}

d &= N \lambda\\

&=6000( \frac{10}{3})\,\ce{m}\\

&=20000\,\ce{m}\\

&=20\,\ce{km}

\end{aligned}$
