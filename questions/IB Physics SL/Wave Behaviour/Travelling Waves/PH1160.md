# PH1160

Which of the following statements is correct for the sound waves?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | The vibration of the particles is parallel to the direction of the motion of the sound waves |
  | II. | They travel faster in liquids than in gases |
  | III. | They cannot travel in a vacuum |

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Sound waves are classified as longitudinal waves, whereby the vibration of particles occurs parallel to the direction of propagation of the wave. Moreover, they typically travel faster in solids than in liquids, and faster in liquids than in gases. In addition, sound waves cannot propagate in a vacuum due to the absence of a medium necessary for their transmission. Therefore all three statements are correct.
