# PH1166

The graph shows the displacement $s$ of particles in a longitudinal wave as a function of distance $d$. Positive displacements are in a direction to the right of a particle's equilibrium position. Which letter, as labeled on the graph, shows the location of a particle at the centre of a compression?

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanations}}$:

Points A and C represent positions where particles are displaced towards point B. Therefore, point B is the position where the particle is at the centre of the compression.
