## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Water waves propagate at varying speeds in different depths, but the frequency of the wave remains constant regardless of the depth of the water. Because frequency is not dependent on the medium. It follows that wavelength and speed are directly proportional.

- $v=\lambda f$

- $v \propto \lambda$

Among the provided graphs, only graph C aligns with this deduction.
