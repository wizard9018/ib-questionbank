# PH1164

Which of the following statements are not always true?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Along one wavelength of a travelling wave, no two points have the same displacement from the equilibrium position |
  | II. | The wave generated on a spring is longitudinal |
  | III. | The energy of an electromagnetic wave is proportional to the wavelength of the wave |

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ I and II only

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Within a wavelength, two points will have the same displacement from equilibrium, although their velocities will be opposite in direction. The first statement is incorrect.

Waves generated on a spring can also be transverse waves. The second statement is not always true.

The energy of an electromagnetic wave is proportional to frequency and inversely proportional to the wavelength. This statement is incorrect.

Therefore, all of the statements are not necessarily true.
