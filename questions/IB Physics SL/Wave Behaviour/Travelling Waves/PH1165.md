# PH1165

Which of the following combinations correctly matches the type of wave with its typical usage application?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ Radio waves $\text{\textemdash}$ Wi-Fi

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ Gamma rays $\text{\textemdash}$ Remote controller

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ Microwaves $\text{\textemdash}$ Cancer treatment

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ Infrared $\text{\textemdash}$ TV broadcasting

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Radio waves are commonly used for Wi-Fi. (This is the correct answer)

Remote controllers typically use infrared radiation.

Gamma rays are used for cancer treatment

Radio waves are used for TV broadcasting.
