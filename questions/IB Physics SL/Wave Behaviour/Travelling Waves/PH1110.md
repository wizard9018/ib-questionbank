# PH1110

An electromagnetic wave travels in the $x$-direction with its electric component oscillating in the $y$-direction. Which line of the table is correct?

![PH1110](images/PH1110.png)

$\\$

| |  Direction of magnetic field oscillation  |  Direction of energy transfer  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Parallel to $y$ axis | Parallel to $x$ axis |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Parallel to $y$ axis | Parallel to $y$ axis |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Parallel to $z$ axis | Parallel to $x$ axis |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Parallel to $z$ axis | Parallel to $y$ axis |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The magnetic component of an electromagnetic wave is always perpendicular to the electric component and the direction of travel, meaning that it is orientated in the $z$-direction in this case.

The wave transfers energy in the direction of travel, which is in the $x$-direction.
