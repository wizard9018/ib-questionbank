# PH0940

The diagram below shows a wave of frequency 300 Hz travelling along a string $AB$. The speed of the wave is $90\,$m s$^{-1}$.

![image](images/image.png)

Two points $P$ and $Q$ lie on the wave such that the phase difference between them is $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. Which of the following could be the displacement between $P$ and $Q$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 10 cm

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 15 cm

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 25 cm

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 36 cm

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The wavelength of the wave is given by

- $\dfrac{v}{f} = \dfrac{90}{300} = 0.3\,$m = 30 cm

Points that are $\dfrac{\frac{\pi}{3}}{2\pi}(30)=\dfrac{1}{6}(30)=\ce{5\,cm}$ away from each other will have a phase difference of $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$.

So a point that is 5 cm ahead of point P or 5 cm behind point P will have this phase difference.

Because 5 cm is not an answer choice, we will need to look at other possibilities.

Because the point 5 cm ahead of our point is not an option answer, we realise that a point 25 cm behind our point will be in phase with a point 5 cm ahead of our point, and so 25 cm is the correct answer here.

In more general terms:

Two points that are separated by an integer number of wavelengths, $n\lambda$, will have a phase difference of 0.

All points that are a distance $n\lambda \pm \left( \dfrac{\lambda}{6} \right) $ from each other will have a phase difference of $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$

So, all points that are a distance

- $n(30) \pm 5 = 30n \pm 5$
away from $P$ where $n$ is an integer will be $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ out of phase with $P$.

Answer is $C$ ($30-5=25\,$cm)
