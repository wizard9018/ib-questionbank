# PH1008-1

**Maximum mark: 5**

A longitudinal wave is travelling in a medium from left to right. The diagram below is a snapshot of plane wave fronts of showing position of particles $X$ (center of compression) and $Y$ (center of rarefaction) in the medium at $t =0 \ s$.

![Screen Shot 2023-04-07 at 7.12.02 PM](<images/Screen Shot 2023-04-07 at 7.12.02 PM.png>)

1. Distinguish between a transverse wave and a longitudinal wave. [2 marks]

2. In the graph below, the solid line shows the variation of displacement $s$ of particle $X$ with time, and the dotted line shows the variation of displacement of particle $Y$ with time.

    ![image](images/image.png)

    1.     State the phase difference between particles $X$ and $Y$. [1 mark]

    2. Calculate the speed of the wave in the medium. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. For transverse wave; the displacement of particles/ movement of particles / oscillations of particles is **perpendicular** to the direction of energy transfer/ wave movement / wave travel. $\checkmark$

    For longitudinal wave; the displacement of particles/ movement of particles / oscillations of particles is **parallel** to the direction of energy transfer/ wave movement / wave travel. $\checkmark$

2.
    1. half cycle/ $\pi / \dfrac{1}{2}$  $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        **Alternative 1**\
        At any instant of time magnitudes of displacement of $X$ and $Y$ are same, but in opposite direction. That is phase difference is  half cycle/ $\pi / \dfrac{1}{2}$

        **Alternative 2**\
        $\phi=\dfrac{\Delta t}{T}\times2\pi=\ <\dfrac{0.8}{1.6}\times2\pi>\ =\ \pi$

    2. $\lambda = 8 \ cm$  and  $T =1.6 \ s$. $\checkmark$

        So; $v=\dfrac{\lambda}{T}\ =\dfrac{8}{1.6}\ =5.0\ cm\ s^{-1}$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        From the figure, the wavelength is two times distance between the center of a rarefaction and the center of the next compression, $\lambda =8 \ cm$

        Period of the wave from the graph, $T= 1.6 \ s$

        $v=\dfrac{\lambda}{T}\ =\dfrac{8}{1.6}\ =5.0\ cm\ s^{-1}$
