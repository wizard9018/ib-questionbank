# PH0062

An object with a mass of $m$ is connected to a spring on a frictionless horizontal surface.

![PH0062](images/PH0062.png)

The object is released from point $A$ which is at a displacement $x$ from its equilibrium point $O$ and executes simple harmonic motion with a period of $T$. Consider the following statements about the situation if the object is released from displacement $2x$.

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}

  | | |

  | :-- | :-- |

  | I. | The angular frequency of the oscillation will be less. |

  | II. | Total energy of the object will be greater. |

  | III. | The average speed of the object will not change. |

Which of the statements is/are correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

I. $\hspace{1em}$ Incorrect. $\omega = \dfrac{2\pi}{T}$, and period is found from $T=2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}}$. This equation shows that period is independent of amplitude $x_0$

II. $\hspace{1em}$ Correct. $E_{Total} = E_{H{max}} = \dfrac{1}{2} kx^2$. Therefore energy has increased due to the increase in amplitude.

III. $\hspace{1em}$ Incorrect. Average speed = $\dfrac{4x_0}{T}$. The period $T$ does not change but average speed increases due to the increase in the amplitude.
