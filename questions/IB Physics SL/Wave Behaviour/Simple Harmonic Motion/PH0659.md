# PH0659

A system experiences simple harmonic motion when a restoring force acts on an object displaced from an equilibrium position. Which of the following is $\textbf{NOT}$ true for simple harmonic motion?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $The restoring force is proportional to the displacement from equilibrium.

- B. $\hspace{1em} $The kinetic energy of the system is maximum at maximum displacement.

- C. $\hspace{1em} $The maximum speed is proportional to the maximum displacement from equilibrium.

- D. $\hspace{1em} $The restoring force is always in the opposite direction to the displacement from equilibrium.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

- A: $\hspace{1em}  $This is the standard defining feature of SHM i.e.  $a=-\omega^2x$.

- C: $\hspace{1em} $ maximum kinetic energy is equal to the maximum potential energy. Therefore

- $\hspace{6em} \dfrac{1}{2}m(v_o)^2=\dfrac{1}{2}k(x_o)^2$

- $\hspace{2.4em}$ and so $(v_o)^2 \propto(x_o)^2$ and $v_o \propto x_o$

- $\hspace{2.4em}$where $x_0$ is the maximum displacement, and $v_o$ is the maximum speed.

- D: $\hspace{1em} $This is one of the principal tenets of SHM. The restoring force always acts to bring an object back to the equilibrium position.

- B: $\hspace{1em} $The kinetic energy is always maximum at zero displacement from equilibrium, it is the POTENTIAL energy that is maximum at maximum displacement. This is incorrect, so B is the answer to select here.
