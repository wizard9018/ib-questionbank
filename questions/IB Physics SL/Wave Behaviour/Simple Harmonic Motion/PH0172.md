# PH0172

The graph below shows the variation with displacement $x$ of the acceleration $a$ from the equilibrium position of a particle that undergoes simple harmonic motion.

![PH0172](images/PH0172.png)

The magnitude of the slope of the graph is directly proportional to

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ The square of the oscillation frequency

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ The oscillation frequency

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ The square of the oscillation period

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ The oscillation period

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The slope of the graph is

slope = $\frac{a}{x}$

By using the relationship between acceleration and displacement;

$a=ω^2x$ where $ω=2πf$

$a=4\pi^2f^2x$

$\dfrac{a}{x}=4\pi^2f^2$    ,    where $4\pi^2$ is constant

therefore $\dfrac{a}{x} ∝ f^2$
