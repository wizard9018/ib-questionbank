# PH0044

Two identical objects $P$ and $Q$ are connected to identical springs. Both $P$ and $Q$ are performing simple harmonic motion (SHM) around equilibrium level $O$.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Potential energy is in its maximum value at boundaries (extreme points), kinetic energy at a maximum value at equilibrium point. So at $t = 0$, for $P$ kinetic energy is a maximum, for $Q$ potential energy is a maximum but kinetic energy is zero. For both masses, the kinetic energy will maximise every half cycle.

At  $t = \dfrac{T}{2}$ again for $P$ kinetic energy is a maximum, for $Q$ potential energy is a maximum.
