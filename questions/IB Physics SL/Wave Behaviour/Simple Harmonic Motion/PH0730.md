# PH0730

The diagram shows a mass tied to a piece of string which is attached to the ceiling. The mass acts as a simple pendulum. It is released from position **A** and moves through its equilibrium position to position **B**.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From the databooklet:\
$\hspace{1em}$ $a=\ -\omega^2x$

The acceleration is directly proportional to $x$, so it must be a straight-line graph passing through the origin. In addition, the gradient must be negative due to the acceleration and the displacement being in opposite directions, represented by the negative sign in the defining equation.

Hence the gradient is:\
 $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{a}{x}=-\omega^2$
