# PH0664

Consider the following statements about the motion of a simple harmonic oscillator:

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | The amplitude of oscillation is always equal to the maximum displacement from equilibrium.   |
  | II. | The period of oscillation always depends on the mass of the oscillator. |
  | III. | The speed of the oscillator is maximum at the equilibrium position. |

Which of the statements are true?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

I: In principle, this is true.

II: False, the period of oscillation can also depend on other factors, dependent on the nature of the oscillation (for example, for a pendulum the mass  is irrelevant).

III: This is true as per equation $v=\pm \omega(x_0^2-x^2)$, $v$ is greatest when the terms in the bracket produce the biggest difference, i.e. when $x$ is at a maximum.

Therefore, B is the correct answer.
