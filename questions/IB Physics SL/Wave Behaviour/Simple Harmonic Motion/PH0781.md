## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. $g$ and $l$ are constant so acceleration/$a$ is proportional to displacement/$x$ $\checkmark$

    negative sign indicates acceleration and displacement are in opposite directions $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
    To classify a harmonic motion as a simple harmonic, the acceleration of the object must be directly proportional to the displacement of the object from the mean position but in opposite directions. Given that $\dfrac{g}{l}$ is constant, the following relationship can be inferred:

    - $a\propto -x$

    This relationship shows that acceleration is directly proportional to displacement but they are in opposite directions (due to the negative sign).

2. Identification of $\omega =\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{l}}$  or ${\omega}^2=\dfrac{g}{l}$       $\checkmark$

    Using $\omega=\dfrac{2\pi}{T}$   $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
    Acceleration a of the oscillation:

    - $a=-{\omega}^2 x=-\dfrac{g}{l} x$

    By cancelling out $x$ on both sides:

    - ${\omega}^2=\dfrac{g}{l}$

    - $\omega = \sqrt{\dfrac{g}{l}}$

    By substituting $\omega=\dfrac{2\pi}{T}$ into the equation:

    - $\dfrac{2\pi}{T}=\sqrt{\dfrac{g}{l}}$

    - $T=2\pi \sqrt{\dfrac{l}{g}}$

4. ::indent
    - $\dfrac{1}{2\pi} \sqrt{\dfrac{9.7}{2.4}}$ $\checkmark$

    - = $0.32\,\ce{Hz}$  $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
    The frequency $f$ of the simple pendulum:

    - $f=\dfrac{1}{T}=\dfrac{1}{2\pi} \sqrt{\dfrac{g}{l}}$

    - $f=\dfrac{1}{T}=\dfrac{1}{2\pi} \sqrt{\dfrac{9.7}{2.4}}=0.32\, \ce{Hz}$

5. Air resistance is always in the opposite direction to the motion / Air resistance increases with the speed $\checkmark$

    The resultant force is no longer directly proportional with displacement / opposite direction to the motion $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
    Air resistance (drag) consistently opposes the motion's direction, contradicting the principle that net force should be in the opposite direction to displacement. Additionally, air resistance increases with the square of the speed, violating the direct relationship between forces and displacement.
