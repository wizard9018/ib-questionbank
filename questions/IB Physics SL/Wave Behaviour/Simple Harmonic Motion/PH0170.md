# PH0170

A mass-spring system executes simple harmonic motion with a period $T$ in a vertical plane. What is the period when the oscillating mass is doubled and the system is brought to a planet where the gravitational field strength at the surface is halved?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$     $2T$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$     $\sqrt2T$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$     $T$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$     $\dfrac{T}{2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Period of a mass-spring system;

$T=2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}} $    where $m$ is mass and $k$ is the spring constant.

For the new condition;

Mass is $2m$ and the spring constant does not change. Additionally, the gravitational field strength does not affect the period.

By substituting new values in the equation;

$\begin{aligned}
T_{new}&=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{2m}{k}}\\
&=(\sqrt2)(2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}})\\
&=\sqrt2T\\
\end{aligned}$
