## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The sum of the values of the potential energy ($E_p$) and the kinetic energy ($E_k$) is always equal to the maximum energy.

In answers **B** and **C** the sum of the values of the potential energy ($E_p$) and the kinetic energy ($E_k$) at any point in time does not always equal the maximum energy value.

In answers **A** and **D** the kinetic energy ($E_k$) starts at zero and the potential energy ($E_p$) starts at a maximum value, and throughout the motion the sum of the 2 values at any point in time equals the maximum, so only answer **A** or **D** can be correct.

The time period ($T$) is given to be 1 second. In that time period the object will pass through the equilibrium position twice, so the kinetic energy must reach a maximum value twice, and the potential energy will have 3 peak values.

Answer **A** shows the correct energy of motion for the potential energy ($E_p$) and the kinetic energy ($E_k$).
