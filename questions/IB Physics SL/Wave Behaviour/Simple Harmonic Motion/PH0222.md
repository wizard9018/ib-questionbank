# PH0222

For a body executing simple harmonic motion, which statement is **not** possible? 

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Acceleration and velocity are in the opposite direction 

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Acceleration and velocity are in the same direction

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Resultant force and velocity are in the same direction

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Resultant force and acceleration are in the opposite direction

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

From Newton's Second Law

- $F_{net}=ma$

Acceleration is proportional to the resultant force and will also be in the same direction. Therefore, it is not possible for the net force and the acceleration to be in opposite directions.

In simple harmonic motion, it is the acceleration and the displacement from the equilibrium position that are in opposite directions.
