## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Occur when two identical waves traveling in opposite directions superpose/when a wave interferes with its own reflection in a medium **OWTTE**. $\checkmark$

2. 

    1. Sound is reflected at the barrier. $\checkmark$

        Standing wave is formed. $\checkmark$

        Loud sound at antinodes/points of constructive interference **and** low intensity sound at nodes/points of destructive interference. $\checkmark$

    2. Node/zero amplitude at wall $\checkmark$

        Three antinodes shown. $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        At the wall, the sound reflects out of phase which results in destructive interference and therefore a node. Antinodes occur where there are loud sounds, so three must be shown. There is no specific end condition required for the wave at the speaker.

        ![PH0577-2](images/PH0577-2.png)

    3. $\lambda = \ll(2)(0.60)\gg$ 

        $\lambda=1.2\ll \ce{m}\gg$ $\checkmark$

        $f=\dfrac{340}{1.2}$ = :answer[$283\ll \ce{Hz}\gg$] $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

        The wavelength is double the distance between two antinodes.

        $\lambda=(2)(0.6)=1.2\,\ce{m}$

        Then the frequency is

        

        $f=\dfrac{v}{\lambda}=\dfrac{340}{1.2}=283.33\,\ce{Hz}$

3. Distance moved = $\ll\dfrac{\lambda}{4}\gg=\dfrac{1.2}{4}$

    **OR**

    Distance moved = $\ll\dfrac{1}{2}$ distance between two antinodes $\gg =\dfrac{0.6}{2}$ $\checkmark$

    

    Distance moved = :answer[$0.3\, \ce{m}$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \

    Moving the barrier will shift the whole wave pattern to the right. Changing from a loud area to a quiet area involves moving from an antinode to a node. A node and an antinode are separated by $\dfrac{\lambda}{4}$
