# PH0977

The shortest distance between a node and an antinode on a standing wave is 10 cm. The frequency of the wave is 100 Hz. What is the speed of the wave?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ $2.5\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ $10\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ $20\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ $40\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The shortest distance between a node and an antinode is $\dfrac{\lambda}{4}$. This is illustrated below.

![image](images/image.png)

- $\dfrac{\lambda}{4} = 10\,\ce{cm} = 0.1\, \ce{m}$

- $\lambda = (4)(0.1) = 0.4\, \ce{m}$

- $v=f\lambda = (100)(0.4) = 40\,\ce{m}\,\ce{s}^{-1}$
