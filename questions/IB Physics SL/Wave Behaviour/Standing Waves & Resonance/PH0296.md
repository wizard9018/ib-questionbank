# PH0296

A tuning fork is held above a hollow pipe which is in a long cylinder filled with water as shown in the diagram below. 

Initially, the pipe is fully immersed in the water. The pipe and the vibrating tuning fork are slowly lifted upward from the surface of the water, creating an air column inside the pipe above the water surface.  

A maximum intensity of the sound is first heard when the air column reaches a length $a$ and again at length $b$.

![PH0296](images/PH0296.png)

Consider the following statements about the lengths $a$ and $b$, and the wavelength $\lambda$

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}

  | | |

  | :-- | :-- |

  | I. | $\hspace{1em}$ $b=3a$ |

  | II. | $\hspace{1em}$ $\lambda=\dfrac{3b}{4}$ |

  | III. | $\hspace{1em}$ $\lambda=4a$ |

Which of the statements are correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Only I

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Only III

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The first harmonic will be found when length is equal to $\dfrac{\lambda}{4}$, which is $a$. 

The next harmonic at $b$, is when the length is equal to $\dfrac{3\lambda}{4}$.

 

Therefore, statements I and III are correct.
