# PH0942

The diagram below shows a loudspeaker above a cylindrical tube of length $l$. When the height of the water is $h$ a resonant sound of the first harmonic is heard.

![PH0942ooo](images/PH0942ooo.png)

Water is then drained slowly from the bottom of the tube. What is the height of the water when the next resonant sound is heard?

- A. $\hspace{1em} $ 2$h - l$

- B. $\hspace{1em} $ 3$h - l$

- C. $\hspace{1em} $ 2$h - 2l$

- D. $\hspace{1em} $ 3$h - 2l$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The open end will be an antinode and the water surface will be a node. First harmonic may be represented as follows

![PH0942Ans](images/PH0942Ans.png)

From this we can see that

- $l - h = \dfrac {\lambda}{4}$.

- $\lambda = 4(l - h)$

Third harmonic will occur when the water level has fallen a distance of $\dfrac {\lambda}{2}$. This may be represented as follows

![PH0942AnsB](images/PH0942AnsB.png)

Therefore the new height is given by

- $h - \dfrac {\lambda}{2} = h - \dfrac {4(l - h)}{2} $
- $\hspace{3em} =h - 2(l - h) $
- $\hspace{3em}=h - 2l + 2h $
- $\hspace{3em}=3h - 2l$
