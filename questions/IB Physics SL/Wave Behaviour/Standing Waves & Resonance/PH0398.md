# PH0398

A pipe of length $L_1$ is open at one end and closed at the other. It has a first harmonic frequency equal to that of the third harmonic frequency in a pipe with length $L_2$  that is closed at both ends.

What is the ratio $\dfrac{L_1}{L_2}$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 6

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 3

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{1}{3}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{1}{6}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

For the pipe that is opened from one end and closed from the other

- $f=\dfrac{nv}{4L_1}$

at the first harmonic

- $f= \dfrac{v}{4L_1}$

For the pipe that is closed from both ends

- $f=\dfrac{nv}{2L_2}$

at the third harmonic

- $f= \dfrac{3v}{2L_2}$

Since both have the same frequency

- $\dfrac{v}{4L_1}=\dfrac{3v}{2L_2}$

- $\dfrac{L_1}{L_2} = \dfrac{1}{6}$
