# PH0813

A tall building is fitted with a stabilizing mechanism to prevent swaying during earthquakes. The stabilizing mechanism is a damped harmonic oscillator that transfers vibration energy into thermal energy.

Below are three statements about the stabilizing mechanism.

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | The natural frequency of the harmonic oscillator should be much greater than the natural frequency of oscillation of the building during the largest expected earthquake.   |
  | II. | The thermal energy absorbed by the oscillator will be at a maximum when the driving force provided by the swaying building causes the oscillator to resonate. |
  | III. | The stabilizing mechanism will be most effective when the oscillator exhibits critical damping. |

Which of the statements are correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

I is incorrect. The natural frequency of the oscillator should be approximately equal to the natural frequency of oscillation of the building during the largest expected earthquake. This will allow for resonance and therefore maximum absorption of energy (during the most dangerous of situations).

II is correct. Maximum energy absorption occurs at resonance.

III is correct. Critical damping will mean that amplitude of vibration falls to zero in the shortest possible time.
