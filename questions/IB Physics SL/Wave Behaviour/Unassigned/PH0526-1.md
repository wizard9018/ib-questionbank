    2. $\lambda=\dfrac{(3.2\times{10}^{-3})(0.15\times{10}^{-3})}{1.3}$

        $\lambda=369.2\times{10}^{-9}\ll\ \ce{mm}\gg$ $\checkmark$

        $\Delta \lambda = 50.3 \times 10^{-9}\ll\ \ce{m}\gg$ $\checkmark$

        $\lambda=$ :answer[$370\times{10}^{-9}\ \pm\ 50\ \times{10}^{-9}$ nm]  $\checkmark$ ($369\times{10}^{-9}\ \pm\ 50\ \times{10}^{-9}$ also accepted if 2 significant figures are kept on the uncertainty)

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The wavelength can be calculated from the formula

         - $s=\dfrac{\lambda D}{d}$

        - $\lambda=\dfrac{sd}{D}$

        - $\lambda=\dfrac{(3.2\times{10}^{-3})(0.15\times{10}^{-3})}{1.3}=369$.$23\times{10}^{-9}\ce{m}=369.23 \ \ce{nm}$

        To calculate the fractional uncertainty in the wavelength, we add the fractional uncertainty of $s$, $d$, and $D$

        - $\dfrac{\Delta \lambda}{\lambda} = \dfrac{\Delta s}{s} + \dfrac{\Delta d}{d} + \dfrac{\Delta D}{D}$

        - $\dfrac{\Delta \lambda}{\lambda} = \dfrac{0.1}{3.2} + \dfrac{0.01}{0.15} + \dfrac{0.05}{1.3} = 0.13$

        The uncertainty in wavelength $\Delta \lambda$ can be found from
$\Delta \lambda = (0.13) \lambda =(0.13)(369.23 \times 10^{-9})=50.3 \times 10^{-9} \ \ce{m} = 50 \ \ce{nm}$

        Therefore

         - $\lambda=370\pm50 \ $nm

         where 369 has been rounded to match the 1 significant figure precision of the uncertainty. As 2 significant figures are also permissible on uncertainty,

         - $\lambda=369\pm50 \ $nm

         is also acceptable here.
