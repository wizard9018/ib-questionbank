# PH0883

Planet $X$ has five times the mass and eight times the volume of Earth.

What is the gravitational field strength on the surface of planet $X$?

Assume that both the Earth and planet $X$ are spherical.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{5}{8}\ g$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{5}{4}\ g$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{5}{2}\ g$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $5\ g$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Gravitational field strength is given by:

$\hspace{1em}$ $g\ =\ \dfrac{G\ M}{r^{2}}$

So, we must find the relationship between the radii of the two planets. Planets are spherical so:

$\hspace{1em}$ $V\ =\ \dfrac{4}{3}\ \pi\ r^{3}$

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{V}{r^{3}}\ =\ constant$

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{V_{Earth}}{r^{3}_{Earth}}\ =\ \dfrac{V_{X}}{r^{3}_{X}}$

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{ V_{Earth}}{ r^{3}_{Earth}}\ =\ \dfrac{8\ V_{Earth}}{r^{3}_{X}}$

$\hspace{1em}$ $r^{3}_{X}\ =\ 8 r^{3}_{Earth}$

$\hspace{1em}$ $r_{X}\ =\ 2 r_{Earth}$

Now:

$\hspace{1em}$ $g\ =\ \dfrac{G M_{Earth}}{r^{2}_{Earth}}$

Where $g$ is the acceleration of freefall on Earth's surface.

Now let the gravitational field strength on the surface of planet $X$ be $g_{X}$:

$\hspace{1em}$ $g_{X}\ =\ \dfrac{G\ (5 M_{X})}{(2 r_{X})^{2}}\ =\ \dfrac{G\ (5 M_{Earth})}{(2 r_{Earth})^{2}}\ =\ \dfrac{5 G M_{Earth}}{4 r^{2}_{Earth}}\ =\ \dfrac{5}{4}\ g$
