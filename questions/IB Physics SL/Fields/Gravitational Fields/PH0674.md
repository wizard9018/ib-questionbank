# PH0674

Two point masses 4m and m separated by 30 cm as shown.

![Screenshot 2026-04-06 at 7.54.57 AM](<images/Screenshot 2026-04-06 at 7.54.57 AM.png>)

At point **A**, the net gravitational field due to the point masses is zero.

What is the distance between 4m and **A**?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ 10 cm

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$  15 cm

-    C. $\hspace{1em}$  20 cm

-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ 30 cm

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The net gravitational field is zero when the two gravitational fields of the two masses are equal in magnitude

$\hspace{1em}$ $\begin{aligned}
g_1 &= g_2\\
G\dfrac{4m}{{r_1}^2} &= G\dfrac{m}{{r_2}^2}\\
\dfrac{4}{x^2} &= \dfrac{1}{{(30-x)}^2}\\
4\left(30-x\right)^2 &= x^2\\
2\left(30-x\right) &= x\\
60-2x &= x\\
x &= 20\ \ce{cm}
\end{aligned}$
