# PH1808

The minimum distance between two planets $X$ and $Y$ is $d$. Planet $Y$ has an orbital period eight times that of planet $X$.

![Screenshot 2025-03-11 at 10.27.15 AM](<images/Screenshot 2025-03-11 at 10.27.15 AM.JPEG>)

What is the orbital radius of planet $X$ in terms of $d$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{d}{5}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{d}{4}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{d}{3}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{d}{2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Kepler's third law states that orbital period squared $T^2$ is directly proportional to orbital radius cubed $r^3$:

- $T^2\propto r^3$

So, we have:

- $\dfrac{(T_Y)^2}{(T_X)^2}=\dfrac{(r_Y)^3}{(r_X)^3}$

Substituting $r_Y=r_X+d$ and $T_Y=8T_X$:

- $\dfrac{(8T_X)^2}{(T_X)^2}=\dfrac{(r_X+d)^3}{(r_X)^3}$

- $64=\left(\dfrac{r_X+d}{r_x}\right)^3$

- $4=\dfrac{r_X+d}{r_X}$

- $4r_X=r_X+d$

- $3r_X=d$

- $r_X=\dfrac{d}{3}$
