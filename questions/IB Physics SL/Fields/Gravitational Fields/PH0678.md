# PH0678

Which of the following is correct about the gravitational field strength at a point near a planet?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ It is the force of gravity acting on an object.

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ It is the force per unit mass acting on a point mass at that point

-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ It is inversely proportional to the distance between the centre of the planet and that point

-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ It is independent of the mass of the planet.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Choice **A** is incorrect as it equates the gravitational field strength with the gravitational force and these two are two different physical quantities.

Choice **B** is the correct answer, since the gravitational field strength equals to force per unit mass;
$g=\dfrac{F}{m}$.

Choice **C** is incorrect, because the gravitational field strength is inversely proportional to the square of the distance between the center of mass of the planet and that point;
$g=G\dfrac{M}{r^2}$.

Choice **D** is incorrect, because the gravitational field strength depends on the mass of the planet;
$g=G\dfrac{M}{r^2}$.
