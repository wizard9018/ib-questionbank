# PH1847

The period of a planet's orbit about the Sun is approximately 2 years. If the radius of the Earth's orbit is $R$, what is the orbital radius of the other planet in terms of $R$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt[3]{2}R$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{2}R$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt[3]{4}R$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{8}R$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From Kepler's Third Law

- $T^2\propto r^3$

Then

- $\dfrac{(T_{planet})^2}{(T_{Earth})^2}=\dfrac{(r_{planet})^3}{(r_{Earth})^3}$

- $\dfrac{2^2}{1}=\dfrac{(r_{planet})^3}{R^3}$

- $r_{planet}=\sqrt[3]{4}R$
