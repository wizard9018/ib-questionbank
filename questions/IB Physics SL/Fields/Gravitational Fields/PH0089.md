# PH0089

A satellite $X$ is in a circular orbit of radius $R$ with a period $T$ around Earth.

What will be the orbital radius of satellite $Y$, travelling in a circular orbit with a period of $8T$ around Earth?

- A. $\hspace{1em} 16R$

- B. $\hspace{1em}    8 R$
- C. $\hspace{1em}    4 R$
- D. $\hspace{1em}    2 R$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Two formulae are needed here.

Gravitational field strength: $g=\dfrac{GM}{r^2}$

Centripetal acceleration: $a=\dfrac{v^2}{r}=\dfrac{4\pi^2r}{T^2}$

Rearrangement gives Kepler's third law, which relates orbital period to radius.

- $\begin{aligned}
g &= a \\
\hspace{.1em} \\
T^2 &=\frac{4\pi^2}{GM}\ r^3 \\
\hspace{.1em} \\
\left(\frac{T_Y}{T_X}\right)^2 &= \left(\frac{R_Y}{R_X}\right)^3 \\
\hspace{.1em} \\
\left(8\right)^2 &=\left(\frac{R_Y}{R}\right)^3 \\
\hspace{.1em} \\
R_Y &=(\sqrt[3]{64}) R \\
\hspace{.1em}
\end{aligned}$

    $\hspace{3.3em}$ = :answer[4 $R$]

Hence C is the correct answer option.
