# PH0836

Which of the following is $\bold{not}$ one of Kepler's laws of planetary motion?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ The square of a planet's orbital period around a star is directly proportional to the cube of the distance between the planet and the star.

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ The force of gravity between two objects is inversely proportional to the square of the separation of their centers and directly proportional to the product of their masses.

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ The orbit of every planet is an ellipse with the sun at one focus.

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ A line segment joining a planet and star that it orbits sweeps out equal areas in equal time intervals.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

A is Kepler's third law.

B is Newton's law of gravitation.

C is Kepler's first law.

D is Kepler's second law.
