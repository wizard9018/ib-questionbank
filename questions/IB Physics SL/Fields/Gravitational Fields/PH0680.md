# PH0680

Two objects **A** and **B** are separated by a distance $r$. The mass of **A** is much larger than that of **B**.

Which of the following is correct about magnitude and direction of the gravitational force exerted by **A** on **B** compared to that exerted by **B** on **A**?

$\\$

| | Magnitude | Direction |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Same | Same |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Same | Opposite |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Bigger | Same |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Bigger | Opposite |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
According to Newton\'s third law, the two objects exert equal and opposite forces on each other.

The equation

- $F_g = \dfrac{GMm}{r^2}$

also shows that the forces on the planets are equal in magnitude.
