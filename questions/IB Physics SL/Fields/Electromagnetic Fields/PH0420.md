# PH0420

A point charge is moved between two parallel plates separated by a distance of 5 cm. The potential difference between the points is 50 V. 

What is the average electric field $E$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 1000 $\ce{N\,C^{-1}}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 250 $\ce{N\,C^{-1}}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 10 $\ce{N\,C^{-1}}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 2.5 $\ce{N\,C^{-1}}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

The potential difference V,

- $V=\dfrac{W}{q}$

The work done $W$,

- $W=Fs$

And

- $F=Eq$

Substituting in the first equation,

- $F=Eq$

- $V=\dfrac{Eqs}{q}=Es \hspace{1em}$

- $E=\dfrac{V}{s}=\dfrac{50}{5 \times 10^{-2}}=1000\, \ce{NC^{-1}}$
