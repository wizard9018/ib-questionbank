# PH0922

Which of the following was the subject of an experiment conducted by Robert Millikan?

- A. $\hspace{1em}  $ The wave-like behavior of particles.

- B. $\hspace{1em}  $ The magnitude of the elementary charge.

- C. $\hspace{1em}  $ The existence of the nucleus.

- D. $\hspace{1em}  $ The particle-like behavior of photons.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In 1909, Robert Millikan and his PhD student Harvey Fletcher conducted the “oil drop” experiment to determine the elementary charge (they also determined Avogadro's constant in the same experiment).
