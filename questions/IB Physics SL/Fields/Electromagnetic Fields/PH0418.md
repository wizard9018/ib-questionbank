# PH0418

Two charges separated by a distance $r$ are placed in a medium with permittivity $\varepsilon$. The charges are then moved to air so that they have the same force between them as that in the medium.

What is the distance between the charges in air, knowing that $\varepsilon=\varepsilon_r\varepsilon_0$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\varepsilon_rr$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{r}{\varepsilon_r}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{r}{\sqrt{\varepsilon_r}}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $r\sqrt{\varepsilon_r}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

The electric force in the medium

- $F=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon} \dfrac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}$ 

The electric force in air

- $F=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \dfrac{q_1 q_2}{r_2\textrm{}^2}$ 

Since the two forces are equal

- $\begin{aligned}

\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon} \frac{q_1 q_2}{r^2} &= \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{q_1 q_2}{r_2\textrm{}^2}\\

\hspace{1em}\\ \frac{1}{\varepsilon_r \varepsilon_0 r^2} &= \frac{1}{\varepsilon_0 r_2\textrm{}^2}\\

\hspace{1em}\\ \frac{1}{\varepsilon_r r^2} &= \frac{1}{r_2\textrm{}^2}\\ \hspace{1em}\\r_2&=r\sqrt{\varepsilon_r}

\hspace{1em}\\

\end{aligned}$
