# PH0108

Two positive charges of magnitude $2\,$C and $8\,$C respectively are placed 9 cm apart. A third charge is introduced between the two charges as shown below;

![PH0108-1aa](images/PH0108-1aa.png)

At what separation, X, from the 2 C charge is the electric field strength equal to zero?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ $0.19\,$cm

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ $1.70\,$cm

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ $3.00\,$cm

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ $4.50\,$cm

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

![PH0108MS](images/PH0108MS.png)

The charge experiences a net zero force when $E_1=E_2$

$\begin{aligned}

E&=\frac{kq}{r^2}\\

\hspace{1em}\\

\frac{2}{x^2} &= \frac{8}{(9-x)^2}\\

\hspace{1em}\\

(9-x)^2&=4x^2\\

\hspace{1em}\\

9-x&=2x\\

\hspace{1em}\\

3x&=9\\

\hspace{1em}\\

x&=3.00\,\ce{cm}\\

\end{aligned}$
