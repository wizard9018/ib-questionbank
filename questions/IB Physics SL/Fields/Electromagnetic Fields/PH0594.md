# PH0594

A positive and a negative charge of equal magnitude are located on the same horizontal line.

![PH0594_q](images/PH0594_q.png)

What is the direction of the net electric field at the point marked X, equidistant from the two charges?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ To the right

- B. $\hspace{1em}$    To the left

- C. $\hspace{1em}$    Up

- D. $\hspace{1em}$    Down

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The electric field at X due to -Q points toward -Q. The field at X due to +Q points away from +Q, with the same magnitude at X as the field from -Q. Adding the electric field vectors gives a vector pointing to the left.
