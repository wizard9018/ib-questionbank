# PH0598

In the following diagram, the size of the charges is small compared to the distance *R*.

![PH0598](images/PH0598.png)

What is the direction of the force on the charge in the center?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Up

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ To the right

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ To the left

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ The charge does not move

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Knowing that $F=k\dfrac{q_1q_2}{r^2}$, we analyze the effect of pairs of surrounding charges on the center charge.

In the vertical direction, $\dfrac{(Q)(Q)}{R^2}=\dfrac{(4Q)(Q)}{(2R)^2}$, so there is no net force on the center charge.

In the horizontal direction, $\dfrac{(3Q)(Q)}{(3R)^2}<\dfrac{(2Q)(Q)}{(2R)^2}$, so there is a net force on the center charge, and it points to the left.
