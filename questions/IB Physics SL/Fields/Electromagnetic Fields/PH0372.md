# PH0372

A hydrogen nucleus is held stationary in a uniform vertical electric field, represented by the arrows. Which of the paths shown would require the largest postive work to be done by an external force on the hydrogen nucleus?

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The hydrogen nucleus is a proton, so it has positive charge.

The electric field lines are pointed upwards, meaning that the electric force on the nucleus due to the field is upwards.

The question asks for the largest positive work done, therefore we are looking for the largest displacement opposite to the direction the field lines, against the electric force. This is shown in D.
