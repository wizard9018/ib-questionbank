## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Curved field lines entering and passing through the solenoid and looping around the outside.  $\checkmark$

    Field line direction shown to be to the left within the solenoid and to the right when looping back.  $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    ![PH0461a](images/PH0461a.png)
    $\footnotesize{\textrm{[Source: Created with chemix - https://chemix.org/]}}$

    Using the right hand solenoid rule: when the fingers of the right hand are wrapped around the coil to match the direction of current flow, the right thumb will point in the direction of the field lines within the solenoid.

2. 1.  Into the page. $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$
    The velocity of the charge can be split into two components, perpendicular and parallel to the field. The charge experiences no force due to the interaction of the parallel component of the velocity of the charge with the magnetic field, but does experience a force due to the interaction of the perpendicular component of the velocity with the field. As this direction is up the page, we can apply the right hand rule to determine that the magnetic force is into the page.

    2.  $\mbox{}$
        $
        \begin{aligned}
        F &= (1.6\times 10^{-19})(5.6 \times 10^6)(4.2\times 10^{-3})(\sin 35) \,\checkmark \\[6pt]
        F &= 2.16 \times 10^{-15} \,\ll \ce{N} \gg \\[6pt]
        \end{aligned}
        $

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$
        $
        \begin{aligned}
        F &= qvb \sin\theta  \rule[-0pt]{0pt}{4mm}\\
        F &= (1.6\times 10^{-19})(5.6 \times 10^6)(4.2\times 10^{-3})(\sin 35) \\
        F &= 2.16 \times 10^{-15}\, \ce{N}
        \end{aligned}
        $

    3. Helix/$\text{\textquotedblleft}$spring-like” shape $\checkmark$

        (Component of) velocity parallel to the direction of the field is unchanged $\checkmark$

        (Component of) velocity perpendicular to the direction of the field causes the proton to move in a circular path. $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$: \
        The component of the particle's velocity along the direction of the field lines is unaffected by the field, however the field does cause a circular motion in the plane perpendicular to the page. These combine to form a helical shape, where the circular path is “stretched out” due to the parallel velocity component. Note that “spiral” is an incorrect description here, as spirals have decreasing radius.

    3. No. $\checkmark$

        No force/ velocity parallel to the magnetic field/ velocity is in the same direction as the magnetic field. $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$
        The proton in this case will experience zero magnetic force, because it is parallel to the magnetic field.

        Also using the equation and setting $\theta = 0\degree$ gives $F = 0$.
