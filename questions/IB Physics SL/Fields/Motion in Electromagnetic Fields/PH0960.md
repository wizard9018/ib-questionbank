# PH0960

The diagram below shows a square coil ABCD in a magnetic field. The plane of the coil is parallel to a magnetic field of magnetic flux density $1$ mT. The area of the coil is $25\, \ce{cm^2}$. A clockwise current of 1 A flows through the coil.

![PH0960aa2](images/PH0960aa2.png)

What is the magnitude and direction of the force acting on side AB?

$\\$

| | Magnitude of force / N | Direction of force on AB|
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| $2.5\times{10}^{-6}$  | Into the page |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $$2.5\times{10}^{-6}$$   | Out of the page |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $5\times{10}^{-5}$    | Into the page |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $5\times{10}^{-5}$   | Out of the page |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$F=BIL\,\sin\theta=BIL$ (as field and AB are perpendicular)

- $B=1\times{10}^{-3}$ T
- $I=1$ A

Length of one side of the coil = $\sqrt{25\ \ce{cm^2}}=5\ \ce{cm=0.05}\ \ce{m}$

- $L=0.05$ m

- $F=BIL=\left(1\times{10}^{-3}\right)\left(1\right)\left(0.05\right)=\ 5\times{10}^{-5}$ N

Using Fleming's left-hand rule:

Current flows from B to A - magnetic field is to the right - so force is into the page.
