# PH0291

An electron enters a region of magnetic field with a velocity as shown in the diagram. The path followed by the electron is shown by the dotted line.
What is the direction of the magnetic field in this region?

![PH0291](images/PH0291.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Out of the plane of the paper

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Into the plane of the paper

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Towards the right

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Towards the left

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The force acting on the electron is downward at entry and perpendicular to the velocity, and the electron has negative charge. Using conventional methods (right-hand/left-hand) the magnetic field vector points into the plane of the paper
