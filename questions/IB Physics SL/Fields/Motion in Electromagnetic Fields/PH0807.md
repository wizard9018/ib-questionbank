# PH0807

A wire is placed between the poles of a large magnet and sits on a mass balance as shown in the diagram. The wire is perpendicular to the magnetic field. The mass of the magnet is $M$,  the magnetic flux density between the poles is $B$ and the length of wire that lies in the field is $L$.

![PH0807aaa](images/PH0807aaa.png)

The graph below shows how the mass reading $m$ varies as the current in the wire $I$ is varied.

![PH0807bbb](images/PH0807bbb.png)

Which row gives the correct expressions for the gradient and y-intercept of the graph?

$\\$

| | Y-intercept | Gradient|
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Mg  | $\dfrac{BL}{g}$ |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Mg  | $BL$  |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $M$ | $\dfrac{BL}{g}$ |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | $M$ | $BL$   |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The reading shown on the mass balance multiplied by $g$ (acceleration due to gravity) is the force exerted on the balance by the magnet.

Force exerted on the balance (by magnet) = weight of magnet + Magnetic force exerted on magnet by wire

- $mg=Mg+B\,I\,L$

- $m=M+\dfrac{B\,I\,L}{g}$

Comparing this to the equation of a straight line. $m$ is analogous to $y$ as it is the variable on the vertical axis and $I$ is analogous to $x$ as it is the variable on the horizontal axis.

- $y=\left(gradient\right)x+y\ intercept$

- $m=\left(\dfrac{BL}{g}\right)I+\ M$

We can see that $\dfrac{BL}{g}$ is analogous to the gradient and M is analogous to the y-intercept.
