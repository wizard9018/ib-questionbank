# PH1870

A charged particle moves at a constant velocity through a region where the electric and magnetic fields are perpendicular to each other and to the direction of motion. The magnitude of the electric field strength in N C$^{-1}$ is 1000 times greater than that of the magnetic field strength in T.

![ph1870](images/ph1870.png)

What is the magnitude of the particle's velocity?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.001 m s$^{-1}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 1 m s$^{-1}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 1000 m s$^{-1}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ More information is needed

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In order for the particle to travel at a constant velocity (constant speed and no deflection) the net force acting on it must be zero. This means that the magnetic and electric forces must be equal and opposite.

Equating the electric and magnetic forces we have:

- $Bqv=Eq$

Cancelling a factor of $q$ and rearranging for $v$ we have:

- $Bv=E$

- $v=\dfrac{E}{B}$

The question tells us that $E$ (in N C$^{-1}$) is 1000 times greater than $B$ (in T) so we have:

- $v=\dfrac{E}{B}=1000$ m s$^{-1}$
