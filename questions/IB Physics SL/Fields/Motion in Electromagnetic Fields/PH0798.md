# PH0798

Two parallel wires are separated by a distance of 0.015 m and each carry a current of 3.0 A in opposite directions. Each wire has a mass per unit length of $0.2\, \ce{g\, cm^{-1}}$.

What is the magnitude and direction of the acceleration of the wires?

$\\$

| | Magnitude / ${10}^{-3}\ \ce{m\, s^{-2}}$  | Direction |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| 6 | Towards each other |
|$\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 60 | Towards each other |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 6 | Away from each other |
| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | 60 | Away from each other |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Converting to correct units:

$\hspace{1em}$ $0.2\ \ce{g\ cm^{-1}} =\left(0.2\times{10}^{-3}\ \ce{kg}\right)\left({10}^{-2}\ \ce{m}\right)^{-1}=0.02\ \ce{kg\ m^{-1}}$

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{F}{L}=\mu_0\dfrac{I_1I_2}{2\pi r} $

Substituting $F=ma$ gives

$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{ma}{L}=\mu_0\dfrac{I_1I_2}{2\pi r}$

Substituting values  $(\dfrac{m}{L}=0.02\ \ce{kg\ m^{-1}})$

$\hspace{1em}$ $(0.02)a=\dfrac{(4\pi\times{10}^{-7})(3)(3)}{(2\pi)(0.015)}$

$\hspace{1em}$ $a=\dfrac{(4\pi\times{10}^{-7})(3)(3)}{(2\pi)(0.015)(0.02)}=0.006\ \ce{ms^{-2}}$

The wires feel a repulsive force. This can be determined by considering how the magnetic field from one of the wires affects the other. Using the right-hand rule to determine the direction of the field from wire 1. Then using the left-hand rule to determine the direction of the force on wire 2 caused by this field and the current in wire 2.
