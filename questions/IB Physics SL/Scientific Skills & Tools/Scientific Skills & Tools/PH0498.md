# PH0498

A rectangle has length $L = (2\,\pm\,0.1)\,$cm and width $W = (3\,\pm\,0.2)\,$cm. 

What is the uncertainty of the perimeter of the rectangle?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.08 cm

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.2 cm

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.3 cm

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.6 cm

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

Perimeter = 2$(L+W)$

uncertainty of perimeter will be calculated by adding the uncertainties of the four sides of the rectangle which  gives $(0.1+0.1+0.2+0.2)=  0.6 $ cm
