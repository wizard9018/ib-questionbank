# PH0966

Which of the following quantities has the same units as force per unit charge?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Electric current

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Electric field strength

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Electric potential difference

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Electric potential energy

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$E=\dfrac{F}{q}$ so electric field strength has same units as ‘force per unit charge’.

Electric field strength at a point is defined as:

The force per unit charge exerted on a small positive test charge at that point.
