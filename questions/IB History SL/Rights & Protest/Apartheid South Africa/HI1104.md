# HI1104

Identify **one** key figure of the Black Consciousness Movement and explain its contributions to the anti-apartheid movement.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
For identify, any of the following:
- Steve Biko - leader and founder of BCM
- Mamphela Ramphele - worked with the South African Students' Organisation (SASO) and the Black Community Programs (BCP) for black community development
- Barney Pityana - founding member of SASO and black theologian
\
[1 marks]

For explanation, any combination of the following:
- Promoted black pride, self-reliance, and psychological liberation from apartheid oppression.
- Mobilized students and youth, inspiring resistance and raising awareness of the injustices of apartheid.
- Promoted unity among all oppressed groups in South Africa who were labeled “non-white” under apartheid laws.
- Helped mobilize young people for key events such as the Soweto Uprising in 1976, drawing global attention.
\
[3 marks]
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The key figure of the Black Consciousness Movement (BCM) was Steve Biko, its most prominent leader and founder ($\checkmark$). The BCM's main achievements included promoting black pride, self-reliance, and psychological liberation from apartheid oppression ($\checkmark$). It mobilized students and youth, inspiring resistance and raising awareness about the injustices of apartheid ($\checkmark$). Additionally, the BCM encouraged the formation of grassroots organizations and community projects, which empowered local communities to challenge apartheid policies and fostered a sense of unity and purpose among oppressed South Africans ($\checkmark$).
