# HI1013

According to Source A, what problems were caused by the pass laws?
[2 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  An extract from a speech delivered by Dora Tamana in Langa Township on January 4, 1953, during a protest against the new pass laws. Tamana was a member of the ANC Women's League and later the Federation of South African Women (FSAW). |

[Source: South African History Online. (2015, May 13). Found in the article *The 1956 Women's March in Pretoria.* South African History Online. Retrieved February 10, 2025, from https://sahistory.org.za/article/1956-womens-march-pretoria  Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Unemployment
- Risk of imprisonment for the women
- Lack of accommodation
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to Source A, the pass system restricted people's ability to work, as Tamana explains that “we have seen unemployment… because of passes” ($\checkmark$). It also disrupted family life, since the source states that passes led to “families broken” and even the risk of mothers being jailed “for a small technical offence - not having a pass,” leaving their children without care ($\checkmark$).
