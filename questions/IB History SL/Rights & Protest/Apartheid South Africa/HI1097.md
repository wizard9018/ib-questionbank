# HI1097

Describe Henrik Verwoerd's role and significance in apartheid South Africa.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following for the **role**:
- Between 1950 and 58, Hendrik Verwoerd served as Minister of Education, creating a separate and inferior education system for non-white students.
- As prime minister (1958-1966), Verwoerd promoted apartheid as the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$policy of separate development."

Any **two** of the following (or a combination of the following) for his **significance:**
- Key figure in implementing $\text{\textquotedblleft}$grand apartheid” using education to reinforce the Black South African students' subordinate status to whites.
- Official establisher of the concept of Bantustan and main responsible for its lasting effects.
- Principal architect of the compelled allocation of each African (Bantu) group to poor land territories (Bantustans).
- He reserved cities, industrial zones, and mineral-rich lands for whites.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Between 1950 and 1958, Hendrik Verwoerd served as Minister of Education and created a separate and inferior education system for non-white students; later, as prime minister from 1958 to 1966, he promoted apartheid as the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$policy of separate development" ($\checkmark$).

Verwoerd was the official establisher of the Bantustan concept and played a key role in implementing $\text{\textquotedblleft}$grand apartheid," using education to reinforce the subordinate status of Black South African students to whites, leaving lasting effects on the country ($\checkmark$).

As the principal architect of the forced allocation of each African (Bantu) group to impoverished land territories known as Bantustans, Verwoerd also ensured that cities, industrial zones, and mineral-rich areas were reserved exclusively for whites. His leadership solidified apartheid, enforcing racial segregation and increasing economic inequality in South Africa. His legacy is characterized by the systematic marginalization of Black South Africans with enduring social and economic consequences ($\checkmark$).
