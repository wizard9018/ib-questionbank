# HI0806

Define the term “pass laws” and explain why they were the focus of the protest actions during apartheid.
[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
For the definition of “pass laws,” the following or similar:
- The pass laws restricted where black South Africans could live, work, and travel. Black South Africans had to carry a pass book which employers had to sign giving them permission to be in urban areas
\
[1 marks]

For explanation, any **three** of the following:
- Pass laws were seen as dehumanizing as they reduced black South Africans to carrying identification documents at all times or face arrest
- The enforcement of pass laws resulted in frequent arrests and harassment by police, creating widespread anger among the black population
- Pass laws were a visible and tangible symbol of apartheid oppression that ordinary people encountered regularly in their daily lives
- The PAC specifically chose to target pass laws as they believed this would mobilize mass support due to the universal hatred of the system
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The pass laws were introduced to limit black South Africans' access to whites-only urban areas. They required black South Africans to carry a pass book which gave them permission to work in these areas ($\checkmark$).

Pass laws were chosen as the focus of protest because they controlled where black South Africans could live and work, creating daily restrictions that led to deep resentment ($\checkmark$). The constant police enforcement of these laws led to regular arrests and harassment, generating widespread anger, resentment, and humiliation amongst black South Africans ($\checkmark$). The PAC strategically selected pass laws as their target because they recognized that the universal hatred of this system could lead to massive popular participation in the protest ($\checkmark$).
