# HI0228Q6

Describe **two** examples of increasing repression and/or violence by the anti-apartheid movement between 1960 and 1965.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Formation of uMkhonto weSizwe (aka MK or $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Spear of the Nation”), 1961

     - As a direct response to the government's actions at Sharpeville, the ANC formed a military wing, the MK, led by Nelson Mandela. It announced in June 1961 that it would begin a sabotage campaign if the government did not agree to reforms and began that campaign in late 1961. The organization also began the planning of a broader campaign known as “Operation Mayibuye” which was never carried out.

 - Sabotage campaigns (1961-1963)

     - Beginning with the bombing of an electrical substation in New Brighton, Eastern Cape, nearly 200 acts of sabotage are recorded between October 1961 and July 1963. No deaths or injuries are recorded as these attacks were focused on “hard targets” and intended to destroy government property/infrastructure rather than individuals. Attacks were typically carried out at times when these places would be unoccupied to avoid loss of life intentionally.  

 - Formation of Poqo (1961)

    - The Pan Africanist Congress (PAC) formed its armed wing, Poqo, in response to state violence but took a far more radical approach by targeting white civilians, police officers, and police informants. The organization carried out many targeted attacks in both the Western Cape (Paarl and Langa) and Transkei (Eastern Cape). After a violent assault in Paarl (1962) and the murder of a family camping near the Bashee River (1963), the government carried out mass arrests and executions of Poqo members. This, combined with a lack of mass support for its violent methods, largely destroyed the organization.  

 - International military training (early 1960s)

     - Many ANC members traveled abroad for military training in the early 1960s in various places, including the Soviet Union, China, Egypt, Algeria and Tanzania. Notable examples from the ANC include: 

     - Chris Hani - received military training in the Soviet Union and served in the Zimbabwean War of Liberation. 

     - Joe Modise - trained in the Soviet Union, former Czechoslovakia and Cuba. He also lived in Tanzania and trained MK members there while also establishing bases in Angola and Zambia as well.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Both of the major anti-apartheid groups, the ANC and the PAC formed their military wings between 1960 and 1965 ($\checkmark$ $\checkmark$). The ANC formed the MK and began sabotage tactics against the apartheid government, and the PAC formed Poqo, which carried out targeted violence against both civilians and police officers ($\checkmark$). While it was never carried out, the MK began planning a major campaign of resistance known as $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Operation Mayibuye” and sent ANC and MK members overseas for military training in preparation ($\checkmark$).
