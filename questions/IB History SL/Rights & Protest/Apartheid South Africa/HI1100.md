# HI1100

Describe the significance of the Transkei Bantustan to apartheid South Africa.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Transkei was the first Bantustan established under South Africa's apartheid policy of $\text{\textquotedblleft}$separate development" and served as a trial model.
- The Transkei Territorial Authority was established in 1959; its territory was defined in 1962 and officially established in 1963.
- It was the largest Bantustan, covering 42,000 km², with a population of 4.75 million, and it was assigned to the Xhosa ethnic group.
- In 1976, after the Soweto uprising, Transkei declared nominal independence and established its own government led by Kaiser Matanzima.
- International recognition was very limited; only Uruguay, then under military rule, acknowledged Transkei's independence.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Transkei is significant as the first Bantustan created under South Africa's apartheid policy of $\text{\textquotedblleft}$separate development." The government intended Bantustans to be designated homelands for Black South Africans, thereby enforcing racial segregation and restricting Black political rights. Transkei served as a test case for this system ($\checkmark$). Covering 42,000 km², Transkei was the largest and most populous of the Bantustans, with a population of 4.75 million. The South African government specifically designated Transkei for the Xhosa ethnic group, aiming to isolate them from areas set aside for whites and other groups. This separation was meant to reinforce the apartheid ideology ($\checkmark$).

In 1976, following the Soweto uprising, Transkei declared nominal independence. It established its own government, led by Kaiser Matanzima, but remained economically reliant on South Africa, and its autonomy was largely symbolic. Transkei's existence highlights the failures and injustices of the Bantustan system, which was used to justify excluding Black South Africans from citizenship and rights, helping the white minority maintain power ($\checkmark$).
