# HI1109

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** or a combination of the following:
- Both sources are central to the establishment of apartheid in South Africa. Source A provides the legal framework, and Source D outlines the ideological justification.
- Both emphasize dividing the population into racial categories (white, coloured, native/non-European).
- Both prioritize protecting the white population as a distinct and dominant group.
- Both advocate for the separation of racial groups rather than integration. Source A does this through administrative classification; Source D through the political ideology of “separate development.”
- Both give power to officials: Authority figures decide how groups are defined and managed.
- Both reject the idea of racial equality.
- Both present apartheid as a system that supposedly benefits all racial groups by preserving their “character” and “future.”
- Both sources see apartheid as essential for the future stability of South Africa. Source A establishes the legal infrastructure, and Source D provides the ideological rationale before the law's enactment.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Both Source A and Source D highlight the division of the South African population into separate racial groups. Each source emphasizes the separation of whites, coloureds, and natives/non-Europeans as a key principle, making racial classification the basis of apartheid. This similarity points to their shared view of a society built on strict racial boundaries rather than integration ($\checkmark$).

Another clear similarity is their support for the separation of racial groups. Source A enforces this through administrative classification, and Source D promotes it through the ideology of “separate development” ($\checkmark$). Despite different approaches, both sources agree on the same goal: keeping racial communities apart. This shared stance shows their united effort to make apartheid not just a legal system but also a political philosophy guiding their actions ($\checkmark$).

Finally, both sources depict apartheid as essential for South Africa's future stability. Source A provides the legal framework for enforcing the system, and Source D offers the ideological reasoning that justifies it ($\checkmark$).
