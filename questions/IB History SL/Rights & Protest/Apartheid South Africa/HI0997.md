# HI0997

What, according to source B, were the proposals of the Freedom Charter to achieve equality for all races and national groups?

[2 marks]

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source B** $\\$ $\hspace{5em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | An excerpt adapted from $\textit{\textquotedblleft}$*The Freedom Charter,”* signed in Kliptown, South Africa on June 26, 1955.

[ Source: Congress of the People. (1955, June 26). *The Freedom Charter*. Retrieved August 15, 2025, https://anc1912.org.za/the-freedom-charter-2/ Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Equal rights, including for all racial groups and in all sectors of society.

 - Equal opportunities for all people without discrimination of race, religion, or beliefs.

 - Socioeconomic equality for all sectors and social classes.

 - Educational equality for all children, regardless of their national group, race, or religion.

 - Political equality for all inhabitants of South Africa, without discrimination based on race, colour, or sex. 

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

To achieve equal rights, the Freedom Charter proposes that all laws that discriminate based on race or belief are to be repealed, ensuring equality before the law. Besides, the Charter shall guarantee equal human rights, that is, the right to speak, publish, preach, worship, etc., irrespective of race or belief.  ($\checkmark$)

The Freedom Charter guarantees educational equality by declaring that education shall be free, compulsory, universal, and equal for all children, without discrimination. It emphasizes that education aims to instill a love of culture, honor for liberty and peace, and to end adult illiteracy through a mass state education plan. By making education accessible and equitable for everyone, regardless of race or background, the Charter seeks to ensure that all individuals have the opportunity to learn and thrive equally. ($\checkmark$)
