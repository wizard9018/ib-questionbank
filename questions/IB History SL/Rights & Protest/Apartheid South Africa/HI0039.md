# HI0039

Explain the role and significance of Albert Luthuli in the anti-Apartheid movement.
[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following (or minor variation) for role:

- Leader (President-General) of the African National Congress beginning in 1952 who emphasised the concept of Ubuntu and non-violent resistance in his anti-apartheid activism.

Any **two** of the following (or combination of the following) for significance:

- Public supporter and participant in the Defiance Campaign in June of 1952 which helped dramatically increase overall membership of the ANC which represented the first large-scale, multiracial anti-apartheid resistance.
- Rooted his resistance and philosophy of non-violence in his Christianity, creating a wider audience and appeal for the ANC and anti-apartheid movement.
- Nobel Peace Prize winner who helped draw significant international attention to the anti-apartheid struggle in South Africa.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Albert Luthuli was the national leader of the African National Congress (ANC) beginning in 1952 until his death in 1967 ($\checkmark$). He was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize in 1960 for his deep commitment to nonviolence in the anti-apartheid movement ($\checkmark$). He led the ANC during a time where its membership was growing dramatically after growth in popularity during the Defiance Campaign in 1952 and was a renowned orator who inspired many subsequent anti-apartheid activism ($\checkmark$).
