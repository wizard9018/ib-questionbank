# HI0808

Identify **two** consequences of the Rivonia Trial for the anti-apartheid movement.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The imprisonment of key ANC leaders, including Nelson Mandela, Walter Sisulu, and Govan Mbeki, for life sentences
- The effective decapitation of the ANC's leadership structure and organizational capacity within South Africa
- The forced exile of the remaining ANC leadership and operations to other African countries
- The creation of martyrs and symbols of resistance that inspired future anti-apartheid activism
- The international attention and sympathy generated for the anti-apartheid cause through media coverage of the trial
- The weakening of Umkhonto we Sizwe (MK) as an armed resistance organization due to the loss of key leaders
- The shift of anti-apartheid resistance leadership to younger activists and external organizations
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Rivonia Trial resulted in the imprisonment of key ANC leaders, including Nelson Mandela, for life sentences, effectively removing the organization's leadership ($\checkmark$). The trial forced the remaining ANC leadership into exile in other African countries, weakening the organization's domestic operations ($\checkmark$).
