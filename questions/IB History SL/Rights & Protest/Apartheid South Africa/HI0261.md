# HI0261

Identify **two** reasons why the ANC split in 1959.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any two of the following: 

 - Dissatisfaction with ANC leadership under Albert Luthuli, who was seen by some as too moderate

 - Critics within the ANC emphasised African nationalism, advocating for a more radical, militant and African-centered approach to resistance and for a departure from non-racial principles

 - Some members of the ANC believed the ANC was overly influenced by communism and too focused on alliances with non-African groups, such as Indians and whites.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Firstly, some members, including leaders like Robert Sobukwe, were dissatisfied with the leadership of Albert Luthuli, viewing him as too moderate in his approach to opposing apartheid ($\checkmark$). Second, critics within the ANC emphasised African nationalism and an African-centered resistance including more radical, militant action as well as rejecting the ANC's non-racial principles ($\checkmark$).
