# HI0040

Describe **one** of the events leading to the Sharpeville Massacre in 1960.
[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **one** of the following (or a combination of them):
- A common form of protest was the burning of “pass books” (or “dompas”) which were used to control movement and employment of Black South Africans and thus were a point of great resentment.
- A large group (5000-10000) of anti-apartheid demonstrators gathered at the police station in Sharpeville to protest the enforcement of “pass laws” by intentionally not carrying their “pass books” as was required by law.
\
[1 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
A large group of demonstrators gathered at the police station to protest the use and enforcement of “pass books” which controlled various aspects of Black South African’s lives. The demonstrators were intentionally not carrying their pass books which was required by the law. The protest escalated, though it is unclear why, and led to the policy opening fire on the demonstrators ($\checkmark$).
