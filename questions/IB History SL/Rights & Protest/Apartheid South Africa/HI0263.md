# HI0263

Define $\text{\textquotedblleft}$non-racialism” and explain its relevance in the context of the anti-apartheid struggle in the 1950s.

[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

For definition, the following: 

 - Non-racialism refers to the principle of rejecting racial discrimination and advocating for equality and unity among all racial groups.

\

[1 marks]

For explanation, any of the following: 

 - In the context of the 1950s anti-apartheid struggle, it was a key ideology of the African National Congress (ANC) and its allies, emphasising that South Africa belonged to all who lived in it, regardless of race.

 - Promoted collaboration between racial groups to oppose apartheid.

 - Central to documents such as the Freedom Charter (1955), which declared a vision of an inclusive and democratic South Africa.

\

[1 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Non-racialism refers to belief in eliminating racial discrimination and promoting equality and unity among people of all racial backgrounds ($\checkmark$). In the context of the 1950s anti-apartheid struggle, non-racialism was a central principle espoused by African National Congress (ANC) and its allies, who called for South Africa to be a shared nation for all inhabitants, irrespective of race ($\checkmark$).
