# HI0260

Define $\text{\textquotedblleft}$petty apartheid' and describe **two** examples of how it was enforced legally.

[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

For definition, **any** combination of the following: 

 - Petty apartheid refers to the system of racial segregation in South Africa during apartheid (1948-1994) that regulated everyday life.

 - Petty apartheid was the system of racial segregation that focused on the separation of races in public spaces, transportation, schools, hospitals, and other facilities. 

\

[1 marks]

For examples, any **two** of the following laws and their descriptions: 

 - Separate Amenities Act which mandated separate and unequal facilities for different races.

 - The Group Areas Act which divided residential and business districts by race 

 - The Prohibition of Mixed Marriages Act and/or the Immorality Act which outlawed interracial relationships 

 - The Pass Laws which required black citizens to carry passbooks to restrict their movement in areas designated for white people 

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Petty apartheid refers to the racial segregation laws and practices in South Africa during the apartheid era (1948-1994) that dictated the everyday lives of citizens ($\checkmark$). One example of how petty apartheid was enforced is the Reservation of Separate Amenities Act. This law mandated that all public spaces and facilities, such as beaches, buses and benches, be segregated by race ($\checkmark$). Another example was the Group Areas Act, which segregated both residential and business districts by race ($\checkmark$).
