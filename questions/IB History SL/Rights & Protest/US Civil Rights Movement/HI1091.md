# HI1091

According to Source D, what was the purpose of the Inter-Citizens Committee?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source D** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Extract from a letter sent to President Kennedy by Reverend C. Herbert Oliver detailing the violence against the Negro community that has taken place in Birmingham, 1963. |

[Source: Oliver, C. H. (1963, September 19). *HU: 2: ST 1 (Alabama): General, 1963: 1 July-9 December JFKWHCSF-0367-008-p0008.* JFK Library Retrieved October 14, 2025 from https://www.jfklibrary.org/asset-viewer/archives/jfkwhcsf-0367-008#?image_identifier=JFKWHCSF-0367-008-p0008. Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- To document cases of police brutality
- To secure remedial justice for victims of injustice
- To assist the victims of brutality
- To awaken the nation to the scope of injustice
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source D states that the purpose of the Inter-Citizens Committee was to ensure that cases of police brutality went on the record ($\checkmark$).  Secondly, Source D mentions that the Committee existed to support those who had suffered police brutality ($\checkmark$). Lastly, it states that the wider purpose of the Committee and its work was to create awareness across the nation of how stark the realities of injustice and police brutality were in the area ($\checkmark$).
