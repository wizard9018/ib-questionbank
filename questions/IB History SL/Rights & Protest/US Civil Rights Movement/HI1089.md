# HI1089

Describe **two** reasons the arrest of Dr Martin Luther King Jr. in Birmingham in 1963 was significant.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- King's arrest was significant as it demonstrated his willingness to break the law for the cause of civil rights and helped pressure the federal government to act.
- His arrest was captured on camera, making it significant as it helped publicize the movement's goals and the need for change.
- While in prison, Dr King wrote “A Letter from Birmingham Jail,” which clearly explained his rationale for action and became an essential piece of literature in the civil rights movement.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The arrest of Dr King in Birmingham in 1963 was significant as it was captured on camera and drew national attention to the goals of the civil rights movement and the need for change ($\checkmark$). During his imprisonment in solitary confinement, Dr King wrote “A Letter from Birmingham Jail” which explained in detail his rationale for action and became a celebrated and important piece of writing within the civil rights movement ($\checkmark$).
