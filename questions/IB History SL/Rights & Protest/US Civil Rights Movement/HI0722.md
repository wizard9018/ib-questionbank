## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Legal and legislative issues were obstacles to improvements to the US education system.

 - Education should be at the centre of the legislative processes in the late 1950s.

 - The education system was struggling to meet the needs of students in the USA.

\

[2 marks]

$\\$

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The references to literacy and numeracy, misspelled as $\text{\textquotedblleft}$readin' ”, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ritin' ” and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$rithmatic”, in the image portray education as both an area of US society that needs improvement and an important issue that the US Congress should prioritise during the late 1950s. ($\checkmark$) Further, the word $\text{\textquotedblleft}$smothered” in the title and the layout of documents on top of schoolbooks suggest that the wider sociopolitical system did not allow for progress in the education system. ($\checkmark$)
