## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The Freedom Riders faced violent resistance, shown by the burning bus.
- They encountered physical danger, including smoke/fire, implying an attack.
- The group appears distressed or disoriented, suggesting sudden or severe hostility.
- The Riders were unprotected, with no visible law enforcement assisting them.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Freedom Riders faced violent and dangerous resistance, shown by the burning bus and riders' escape from the smoke ($\checkmark$). It also indicates that they were left vulnerable, as no authorities appear to protect them, highlighting that their non-violent protest was met with serious hostility ($\checkmark$).
