# HI0994

Describe the role of **two** prominent leaders of the Civil Rights Movement in the United States.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Martin Luther King Jr. - Baptist minister, activist, and important leader in the nonviolent protest movement for racial equality.

 - Rosa Parks was an activist who played a crucial role in sparking the Montgomery Bus Boycott.

 - Malcolm X - A Muslim leader advocating for Black empowerment and self-defense.

 - John Lewis - Leader of the Student Nonviolent Coordinating Committee (SNCC) and later a U.S. Congressman.

 - Bayard Rustin - An essential organizer and advisor. One of the strategists behind the 1963 March on Washington.

 - Ella Baker - Organizer and leader, pivotal in SNCC and other organizations.

 - Thurgood Marshall - First Black Supreme Court Justice and important NAACP lawyer in cases like Brown v. Board of Education.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Thurgood Marshall was a lawyer, a Supreme Court Justice, and a prominent civil rights advocate. He was also notable for being the first African American to serve on the Supreme Court. He led the NAACP Legal Defense and Education Fund. He won important Supreme Court cases, including the landmark Brown v. Board of Education, which declared racial segregation in public schools unconstitutional.  ($\checkmark$)

Ella Baker was a prominent African-American civil rights activist, notable for her low-profile organizing work, which focused on empowering African-American communities through education and training.  ($\checkmark$)
