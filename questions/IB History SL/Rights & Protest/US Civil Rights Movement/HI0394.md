# HI0394

Identify three central beliefs of the members of the Nation of Islam about the issue of racial segregation in the United States.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any three of the following:

 - Elijah Muhammad was a prophet of God (Allah)

 - African Americans should aim for separation, not integration, with the white Americans

 - Elijah Muhammad preached that white people are evil 

 - Divine intervention would resolve race problems

 - Was against the the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$turn the other cheek" philosophy of Christianity as a perpetuation of enslavement

 - Stressed racial pride and economic self-help as a way to improve the lives of African Americans

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Nation of Islam was an organization that mixed ideas of black nationalism with elements of Islam. Some of its beliefs included the idea that its leader, Elijah Muhammad was a prophet of Allah (God)($\checkmark$). Elijah Muhammad preached that African Americans should aim for separation instead of integration ($\checkmark$) since he considered white people as evil ($\checkmark$).
