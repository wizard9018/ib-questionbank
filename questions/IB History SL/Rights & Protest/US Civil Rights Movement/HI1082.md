# HI1082

Identify the doctrine established by the *Plessy vs Ferguson* Supreme Court decision of 1896 and explain its significance.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
For identify, the following or similar:
- It established the legality of racial segregation based on the doctrine of $\lq$separate but equal' in the United States.
\
[1 marks]

For explanation of significance, any **two** of the following:
- Gave legal approval for the establishment and continuation of Jim Crow laws in the southern US.
- Set the precedent for broader, legalized racial segregation at various levels of social, economic, and political life in the US, which would stand until 1954.
- Sparked some local resistance in New Orleans (where Homer Plessy was from) via the Citizens' Committee of New Orleans.
\
[2 marks]
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Plessy vs Ferguson decision of 1896 was a key moment in civil rights in the USA as it firmly established the doctrine of $\lq$separate but equal' which allowed facilities to be racially segregated as long as they were “equal” ($\checkmark$).

This led to Jim Crow laws, like those that established racially segregated public facilities, having legal approval and continuing to be used in the southern US ($\checkmark$). These segregated facilities, such as schools, resulted in very different outcomes and opportunities for black Americans, further widening the socioeconomic gap ($\checkmark$).
