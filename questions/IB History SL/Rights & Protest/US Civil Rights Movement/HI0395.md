# HI0395

Describe the Montgomery bus boycott (1955-1956).

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - The objective was to end segregation on buses in Montgomery, Alabama, the state capital.

 - It was initially organized by the local branch of the NAACP, and then by the newly created Montgomery Improvement Association (MIA), led by Martin Luther King. 

 - It started just after the arrest of Rosa Parks, secretary of the local branch of the NAACP, for refusing to give up her seat to a white passenger on a crowded bus. 

 - Most of the African American community in Montgomery, roughly 50,000 people, refused to use buses until their demands were met. 

 - As a result, Montgomery buses were desegregated after the results of the federal court case Browder v. Gayle took effect,

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The main goal of the Montgomery bus boycott was to end racial segregation on Montgomery's public buses in Alabama ($\checkmark$). The boycott was initially organized by the local NAACP branch but gained strength under the organization of the newly established Montgomery Improvement Association (MIA), headed by Martin Luther King Jr. ($\checkmark$). Partly as a result of the boycott, Montgomery buses were desegregated although it took the decision of the federal courts with the case Browder v. Gayle for desegregation to officially take effect ($\checkmark$).
