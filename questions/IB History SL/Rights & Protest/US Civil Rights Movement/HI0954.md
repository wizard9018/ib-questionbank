# HI0954

Identify **three** forms of nonviolent protest used during the Civil Rights Movement between 1954 and 1965.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Bus boycotts - e.g., Montgomery Bus Boycott (1955-56)
- Sit-ins - e.g., Greensboro sit-ins (1960)
- Freedom Rides - to test desegregation of interstate buses (1961)
- Marches and demonstrations - e.g., March on Washington (1963), Selma to Montgomery marches (1965)
- Voter registration drives - e.g., Mississippi Freedom Summer (1964)
- Legal challenges - e.g., NAACP-supported court cases like Brown v. Board of Education (1954)
- Use of media/publicity - strategic use of television, newspapers, and photographs to raise awareness
- Economic boycotts - e.g., boycotting segregated businesses
- Church-led activism and prayer pilgrimages - often coordinated by leaders like MLK
- Non-violent civil disobedience - refusing to obey unjust laws peacefully
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Firstly, bus boycotts, most famously seen in the Montgomery Bus Boycott of 1955-56, placed economic pressure on the bus system and contributed to the Supreme Court ruling that bus segregation was unconstitutional ($\checkmark$).

Secondly, sit-ins such as the Greensboro sit-in of 1960 challenged the legality and morality of segregation in public places ($\checkmark$).

Thirdly, Freedom Rides drew national attention to the continued defiance of federal laws and helped prompt stricter enforcement of desegregation ($\checkmark$).
