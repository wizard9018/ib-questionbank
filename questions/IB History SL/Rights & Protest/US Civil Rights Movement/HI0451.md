# HI0451

Describe the concept of the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Great Migration” in the 20th-century United States.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of the following:

 - The large-scale movement of African Americans from the southern US to the northern US and Midwest

 - African Americans moving away from the South for better economic opportunities

 - African Americans moving away from the South to escape racial violence and discrimination

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Great Migration was the mass movement of African Americans out of the southern United States and into other regions, especially cities in the Midwest (e.g. Chicago, Illinois) and Northeast (e.g. Philadelphia, Pennsylvania) ($\checkmark$). Many of these migrants were motivated to move by higher paying jobs in factories of major cities compared to their wages working on farms in the South ($\checkmark$).
