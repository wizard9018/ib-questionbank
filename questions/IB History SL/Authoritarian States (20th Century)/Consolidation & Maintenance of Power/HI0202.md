# HI0202

Describe **three** impacts of the failure of the Great Leap Forward in 1958 on Mao's maintenance of power.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of **three** of the following:

The failure of the Great Leap Forward resulted in the death of 20 to 50 million Chinese. 

- Mao faced mild criticism from his Defence Minister, Peng Dehuai who was dismissed. 

- The crisis weakened Mao who would then step down as Head of State in 1959 but continue as Head of the Communist Party. 

- The aftermath of the crisis was now handed to Liu Shaoqi and Deng Xiaoping who introduced some elements of private ownership of land,  going against Mao's Communist teachings.[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The failure of the Great Leap Forward resulted in the death of 20 to 50 million Chinese but Mao refused to admit responsibility ($\checkmark$). Internal criticism was minimal, such as from his Defence Minister, Peng Dehuai, who was simply dismissed ($\checkmark$). The crisis, however, weakened Mao who stepped down as Head of State in 1959 but continued as Head of the Communist Party, thus maintaining substantial power in China ($\checkmark$).
