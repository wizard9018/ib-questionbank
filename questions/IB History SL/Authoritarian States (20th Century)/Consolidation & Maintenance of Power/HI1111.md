# HI1111

Describe **three** impacts of the Soviet Union's economic assistance to Mao's maintenance of power in China.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Reconstruction of the country after the war through loans and supplies.
- Implementation of the Five-Year Plan aimed at fast-paced economic development.
- Mao's prioritisation of political developments aligned with communist principles limited the progress of the Soviet Union's economic assistance in the long-term.
- Soviet Union planners did not allocate resources on needs-based, thereby paving the way for inefficient administration.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Through direct loans and the provision of essential supplies, the Chinese state was able to enhance the reconstruction of the country after the war ($\checkmark$). This paved the way for a robust economy, which Mao could use to further his political revolutionary agenda ($\checkmark$). However, the inefficient administration of resources by Soviet Union planners in China, coupled with Mao's disagreement over ideological principles in the USSR, challenged the extent of the alliance between both nations and limited the reach of the economic recovery of the country in the long-term ($\checkmark$).
