# HI0605

Explain the significance of the Lushan Conference of 1959 to Mao's consolidation of power.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Peng and his supporters were purged.
- Opposition to Mao's policies were practically eliminated from the party as criticism of Mao could lead to persecution.
- The Great Leap Forward continued unchecked, with disastrous effects.
- Lin Biao, a staunch supporter of Mao, replaced Peng as Minister of Defence. With Lin Biao in charge of the military, the military and ideology took priority over pragmatism, also confirming the PLA as a key power base for Mao.
- Lin Biao's growing influence would lead to the militarisation of Mao's Cult of Personality.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Lushan Conference was significant as opposition to Mao's policies were practically eliminated from the party, as criticism of Mao could lead to persecution ($\checkmark$). Thus, the Great Leap Forward was allowed to continue unchecked, leading to the escalation of famine and economic collapse ($\checkmark$). Finally, Lin Biao, a loyal supporter of Mao, replaced Peng as Minister of Defence. With Lin Biao in charge of the military, the military and ideology took precedence over pragmatism and cemented the People's Liberation Army as a key power base for Mao ($\checkmark$).
