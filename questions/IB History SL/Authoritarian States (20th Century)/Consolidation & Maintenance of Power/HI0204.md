# HI0204

Describe how Mao’s Continuous Revolution Theory helped him retain power.  [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of the following:

- The concept of continuous revolution emphasises revolution as a process that continues even after a party achieves power in order to ensure a constant process of renewal to avoid complacency and corruption. 

- Mao used this theory of a constant process of renewal to attack opponents on the basis of complacency and corruption. 

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Mao's theory of continuous revolution was a cornerstone of his thought and stated that revolution was as a process that continues even after a party achieves power as there needed to be constant renewal to avoid complacency and corruption ($\checkmark$). This was the theory used by Mao to justify his many initial mobilisation campaigns and later the purges carried out in the Hundred Flowers Campaign and the Cultural Revolution ($\checkmark$).
