# HI0767

What is the message of Source A?

[3 marks]

$\\$

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- | 

|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |Excerpt from $\textit{\textquotedblleft}$A Letter To The Red Guards Of Tsinghua University Middle School,” sent August 1, 1966, by Mao Zedong. Originally published in *Long Live Mao Tse-tung Thought*, a Red Guard publication. 

[Source: Mao\, Z. (1966, August 1). *A Letter To The Red Guards Of Tsinghua University Middle School.* Retrieved May 12, 2025 from https://marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-9/mswv9_60.htm. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Mao enthusiastically supports the Red Guards' denunciations of landlords, bourgeois elements, imperialists, and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$revisionists” as enemies of the revolution. 

 - Mao encourages mass action against perceived enemies of socialism. He reinforces the slogan $\text{\textquotedblleft}$It is right to rebel against reactionaries”. 

 - Mao calls for unity and re-education. Whilst supporting rebellion, Mao cautions the Red Guards to unite with others who can still be part of the revolution. 

 - Mao frames the the Cultural revolution as part of a universal proletarian mission, referencing Marx's idea that $\text{\textquotedblleft}$the proletariat must emancipate not only itself but all mankind,”

 - By addressing the students directly, Mao signals that they are now the vanguard of the revolution. (Thus bypassing traditional party structures)

 - By endorsing the Red Flag Combat Group and specific posters, Mao validates extreme, sometimes violent methods as legitimate revolutionary acts. Thus radicalism is not only permitted, it is praised by the highest authority.

 - The source contains an implied criticism of the CCP establishment. In referring to $\text{\textquotedblleft}$landlords, bourgeois, imperialists, revisionists and their running dogs” Mao is suggesting the existing party leadership is suspect, and he is placing his trust in mass mobilisation rather than official structures.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The first message of Source A is that Mao strongly endorses the Red Guards' denunciation of landlords, bourgeois elements, imperialists, and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$revisionists,” whom he identifies as enemies of the revolution. He expresses approval of their big-character posters and revolutionary speeches, signaling his personal support for their militant approach. ($\checkmark$) Secondly, he actively encourages mass action against perceived enemies of socialism, reaffirming the slogan, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$It is right to rebel against reactionaries.” By doing so, Mao grants the Red Guards legitimacy to continue their revolutionary efforts, thereby intensifying the Cultural Revolution. ($\checkmark$) Finally, Mao urges unity and the re-education of those who have made errors. While backing rebellion, he advises the Red Guards to work with all those who can still contribute to the revolution. He emphasizes that individuals who have made serious mistakes should be guided toward ideological reform and $\text{\textquotedblleft}$become new men,” reflecting a Marxist view that transformation, not just punishment, is the ultimate goal. ($\checkmark$)
