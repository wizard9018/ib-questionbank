# HI0201

Outline the goals of the Hundred Flowers Campaign launched in 1956 and explain its impact on Mao's maintenance of power.[5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of the following for the goals of the Campaign:

- Mao lifts censorship restrictions and encourages criticism of the Communist Party.

- February 1957 he announced $`$Let a Hundred Flowers blossom and a  

hundred schools of thought contend' as a policy to encourage constructive criticism.[1 mark]

Any combination of the following for impact on maintenance of power. 

- The Central government receives a deluge of letters and complaints. 

- Criticism appears in magazine articles, in rallies in the street and on posters on the $`$Democracy Wall' in Beijing University.

- Mao calls off the campaign and reimposes censorship. 

- He launched the Anti-Rightist Campaign in 1957 where half a million Chinese were subject to persecution, imprisonment, public shooting and re-education in labour camps. Removing another layer of opposition. 

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Having established his power by 1956 Mao launched the Hundred Flowers Campaign with the goal of encouraging constructive criticism of the working of the Communist Party. He said $`$letting a Hundred Flowers blossom and a hundred schools of thought contend' would promote progress and a flourishing socialist culture ($\checkmark$). 

The impact of the Hundred Flowers Campaign was a deluge of criticism in letters sent to the Central government ($\checkmark$). This was followed by criticism of the privileged lifestyle of party members which was just one amongst other issues that appeared in magazine articles, on rallies in the street and from students in Beijing University who expressed their opinion on posters on the $`$Democracy Wall' ($\checkmark$). All this was deemed by Mao to go beyond $`$healthy criticis' and he called off the campaign and reimposed censorship ($\checkmark$). This was followed in 1957 by the Anti-Rightist Campaign in 1957 where half a million Chinese were subject to persecution, imprisonment, public shooting and re-education in labour camps and helped remove another layer of opposition ($\checkmark$).
