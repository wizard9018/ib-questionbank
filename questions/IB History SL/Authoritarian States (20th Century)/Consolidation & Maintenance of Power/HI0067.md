# HI0067

Identify and explain one legal method used by Hitler to consolidate power in Germany. 

[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

One mark for identifying any of the following:

- The Enabling Act (1933)

- The Reichstag Fire Decree (1933)

- The Law Against the Formation of New Parties (1933)

[1 marks]

One mark for a correct explanation of the legal method used

- The Enabling Act allowed Hitler to pass laws without Reichstag approval, effectively bypassing the democratic process and giving him dictatorial powers.

- The Reichstag Fire Decree suspended civil liberties and enabled the arrest of political opponents, particularly Communists, thereby eliminating opposition to Nazi rule.

- The Law Against the Formation of New Parties made the Nazi Party the only legal political party in Germany, ensuring that no other parties could challenge Hitler's power and solidifying a one-party state.

\

[1 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

One method used by Hitler to consolidate power was the Enabling Act ($\checkmark$). It was a law that allowed him to bypass the Reichstag entirely, enabling him to create and enforce laws independently, which quickly dismantled democratic structures and solidified his control over Germany ($\checkmark$).
