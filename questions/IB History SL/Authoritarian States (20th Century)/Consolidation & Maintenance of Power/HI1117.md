# HI1117

Describe **two** ways the Cuban authoritarian state treated its opposition during the 1960s.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Suppression of anti-communist rebels early on during the Castro regime (e.g. Escambray Rebellion).
- Execution in the early 1960s of former members of the Batista regime.
- The systematic control over political prisoners from opposition groups within and outside the regime.
- The institutionalisation of a one-party system with the Constitution of 1976.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Cuban authoritarian state engaged in the suppression of opposition groups in violent confrontations and the execution of Batista officials in the early 1960s ($\checkmark$). Additionally, the Cuban state systematically hindered elections and banned political parties from participating in politics throughout the 1960s. This was further materialised with the establishment of a one-party system under the 1976 Constitution ($\checkmark$).
