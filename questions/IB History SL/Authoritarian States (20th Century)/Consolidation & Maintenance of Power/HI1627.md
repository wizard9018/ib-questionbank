# HI1627

What does Source B suggest about Mao's view of the challenges facing socialist China in 1957?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| $\textbf{Source B}$ |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| | | $\mbox{}\\[-30pts]$This excerpt is taken from a speech by Mao Zedong at the Chinese Communist Party's National Conference on Propaganda Work in 1957, later published in *Quotations from Chairman Mao Tse-Tung*. It was delivered during the Hundred Flowers Campaign (1956-1957), when Mao initially encouraged criticism of the Communist Party before reversing course and launching the Anti-Rightist Campaign. The source reflects Mao's belief that bourgeois and anti-Marxist ideas remained a threat to socialism and justified the continued ideological struggle to strengthen Communist Party control. |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Mao believed that bourgeois and petty-bourgeois ideas continued to exist within Chinese society and threatened the development of socialism.
- He argued that economic transformation alone was not enough, as the struggle between socialist and capitalist ideas was still ongoing.
- Mao felt that political and ideological victory had not yet been secured, even though socialist ownership of the means of production had been established.
- He warned that anti-Marxist beliefs could spread and weaken the socialist system if they were not actively challenged.
- Mao believed that constant criticism and ideological education were necessary to ensure that communist principles remained dominant and that China stayed on the socialist path
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source B suggests that Mao believed the main challenge facing socialist China was ideological rather than economic ($\checkmark$). Although socialism had largely transformed ownership of the means of production, Mao argued that bourgeois and anti-Marxist ideas still survived in society ($\checkmark$). He therefore believed that a long-term ideological struggle was necessary to ensure that socialist and communist values would prevail ($\checkmark$).
