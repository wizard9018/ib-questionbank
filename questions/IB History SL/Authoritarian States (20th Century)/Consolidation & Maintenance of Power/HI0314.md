# HI0314

Describe **three** key features of Castro's Rectification Campaign *(rectificación de errores y tendencias negativas)* in 1986.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following features:
- A rejection of economic liberalization that Castro felt had gone too far and was undermining centralized planning.
- The campaign banned material incentives, increased state oversight, and reinforced the ideological commitment to labor to curb corruption and restore centralized control.
- Increased government oversight of production and distribution to reduce reliance on market mechanisms and enforce socialist principles.
- Promoted voluntary labor, moral incentives, and socialist values to strengthen ideological commitment and unity.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Rectification Campaign of 1986 was characterized by a rejection of economic liberalization, an anti-corruption drive, and a push for greater labor discipline in Cuba. Castro sought to reverse market-oriented policies that had weakened centralized planning, emphasizing a return to socialist economic principles ($\checkmark$). Additionally, the government cracked down on corruption among officials who had exploited market freedoms for personal gain, reinforcing state control and ideological purity ($\checkmark$). Lastly, the campaign encouraged voluntary labor and moral incentives to boost productivity and foster revolutionary commitment among workers ($\checkmark$).
