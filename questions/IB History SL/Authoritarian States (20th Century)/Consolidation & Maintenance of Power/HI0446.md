# HI0446

With reference to its origin, purpose, and content, analyse the value and limitations of Source A for a historian studying the state of the Russian Revolution up to 1918.
[4 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ $\\$ $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  An excerpt from $\text{\textquotedblleft}$History of the Russian Revolution to Brest-Litovsk," written by Trotsky, published in 1918. |

[Source: Trotsky, L (1918, February). *History of the Russian Revolution to Brest-Litovsk.* Marxists Internet Archive. Retrieved February 10, 2025 from https://www.marxists.org/archive/trotsky/1918/hrr/ch04.htm. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
A combination of the ideas below that meets the following guidelines:
- Both values and limitations must be addressed. If only one is addressed, award a maximum of **two** marks.
- Origin, purpose and content must all be referenced at some point in the response (either as a value, limitation or both) to receive the full **four** marks.

For value, any combination of the following:
- The origin of the source is a top-level Bolshevik member that was directly involved in many events of the Russian Revolution, providing direct insight into the state of the Revolution.
- The content of the source provides insight into the Bolshevik leadership's perspective on the events leading up to the treaty.

For limitations, any combination of the following:
- The content of the source provides only Trotsky's perspective and thus provides no insight into alternative or contrasting Bolshevik views of the treaty.
- The content only provides information up to 1918 as the events are still unfolding.
- Trotsky is trying to justify his decision to sign the treaty, which suggests that his analysis might be subjective.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The source is valuable as it was written by Trotsky himself, a top Bolshevik leader who participated in the events and thus had access to inside information during the Russian Revolution ($\checkmark\, \text{VO}$).  The source's content offers insight into the Bolshevik Party's perspective on the events leading up to the signing of the Treaty of Brest Litvosk ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$).

The source's content is limited because it is strictly from Trotsky's perspective, which implies that other Bolshevik leaders' perspectives are not shown ($\checkmark\, \text{LC}$). The source's purpose is also limited as Trotsky is a firm believer in World Revolution, so his documentation of the events may have a very subjective narrative and be used to promote his beliefs ($\checkmark\, \text{LP}$).
