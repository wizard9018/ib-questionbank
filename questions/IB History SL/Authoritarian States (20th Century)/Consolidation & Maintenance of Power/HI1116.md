# HI1116

Describe three ways *Radio Rebelde* was significant in the consolidation of the Cuban authoritarian state.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Radio Rebelde was state-controlled by 1962.
- Radio Rebelde was a platform for propaganda, including Castro's speeches, the government's successes and the revolution's values.
- As infrastructure in remote rural areas improved, Radio Rebelde's reach increased.
- Radio Rebelde was one of the only state-approved media outlets through which official narratives were broadcast and views from the opposition were banned.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Because it was fully controlled by the Castro regime by 1962, Radio Rebelde helped establish a state-led propaganda platform through which Castro could disseminate his ideas and the achievements of his government ($\checkmark$). Moreover, Castro built on the legacies of early Cuban Revolution stages in Sierra Maestra to position Radio Rebelde as one of the few official media outlets ($\checkmark$). In doing so, the Cuban state enhanced its consolidation process through the broadcast of the regime's announcements and propaganda and the banning of media companies that opposed the government ($\checkmark$).
