# HI0604

Outline the events of the Lushan Conference of 1959.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- The conference was called to address growing concerns within the Party over agricultural failures, food shortages and economic problems as a result of collectivisation and unrealistic production targets.
- Peng Dehuai (Minister of Defence) wrote a letter to Mao outlining the problems that he saw with the Great Leap Forward.
- Rather than addressing Peng's criticisms, Mao attacked Peng (and his supporters) accusing him of rightist and bourgeois tendencies.
- Most key leaders, fearing for their positions, sided with Mao, and the Great Leap Forward was allowed to continue unchecked.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Lushan conference was called to address growing concerns within the Party over agricultural failures, food shortages and economic problems as a result of collectivisation and unrealistic production targets ($\checkmark$). Peng Dehuai (Minister of Defence) wrote a letter to Mao outlining the problems that he saw with the Great Leap forward ($\checkmark$). Rather than addressing Peng's criticisms, Mao attacked Peng (and his supporters) accusing him of rightist and bourgeois tendencies ($\checkmark$). As a result, the Great Leap Forward was allowed to continue unchecked.
