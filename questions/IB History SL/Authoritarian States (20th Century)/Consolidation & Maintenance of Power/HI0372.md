# HI0372

With reference to its origin, purpose and content, analyse the value and limitations of Source A for an historian studying the consolidation of power of the Chinese authoritarian state.
[4 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source A** | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Address by Mao Zedong on the role of the Chinese Communist Party in the early 1950s. |

[Source: Adapted from Mao, Z. (1951, February 18). *Main Points of the Resolution Adopted at the Enlarged Meeting of the Political Bureau of the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China.* Retrieved October 18, 2025 from https://www.marxists.org/reference/archive/mao/selected-works/volume-5/mswv5_12.htm. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
A combination of the ideas below that meets the following guidelines:
- Both values and limitations must be addressed. If only one is addressed, a maximum of **two** marks is awarded.
- Origin, purpose and content must all be referenced at some point in the response (either as a value, limitation or both) to receive the full **four** marks.

For value, any of the following:
- The source is valuable in terms of origin because it is an official address by Mao himself as the leader of the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) and the regime.
- The value of the source's purpose is that Mao's purpose is to inform both high-ranking officials and members of the CCP about the measures the regime is taking to strengthen the party internally.
- The content is valuable because it clearly outlines the main policy actions for the CCP's consolidation.

For limitations, any of the following:
- It is limited in terms of origin because it is a speech shortly after the CCP's rise to power, which does not allow for tracing the effects of the policy.
- The source's purpose is limited to debriefing members of the CCP on the policy plan for consolidation and does not account for an evaluation of the scope of the policy.
- The content is limited to a policy outline on party building and consolidation and does not account for its actual impact in cities and rural towns.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The source is valuable in terms of origin because it is an internal speech by Mao himself as head of state and the leader of the Chinese Communist Party (CCP), which positions him as an authoritative actor in the consolidation of the Chinese authoritarian state  ($\checkmark\, \text{VO}$). With reference to the purpose, the source is valuable because it showcases the direction in which the Chinese regime is planning to strengthen the CCP  ($\checkmark\, \text{VP}$). Mao's firsthand account makes the source's content valuable as it contains the layout of the policy for the party's buildup and consolidation  ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$).

However, the source's origin is limited because it is historically framed shortly after the CCP rose to power  ($\checkmark\, \text{LO}$). Along these lines, the content is limited because Mao's address does not account for either the implementation or the impacts of the policy in urban centres and rural towns ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$).
