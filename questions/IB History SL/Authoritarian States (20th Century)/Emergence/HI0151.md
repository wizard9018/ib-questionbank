# HI0151

Compare and contrast the use of force in Castro and Hitler's rise to power.  [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
If only similarities or contrasts are identified, 4 is the highest mark.

Any **6** of the following:

For compare:
- Both attempted coups unsuccessfully: Munich Putsch of 1923 against the government of Bavaria and 26 July 1953 on the Moncada Barracks against the dictatorship of Batista.
- Both used armed groups to help them get to power: the Sturmabteilung (SA) and the Schutzstaffel (SS) were paramilitary groups controlled by Hitler which terrorized political opponents while the guerrilla group 26th of July Movement was Castro's guerrilla army during the civil war.
- In both cases, the failed coups pushed both leaders into notoriety.
- The trials they were both subjected to as a result of the failed coups provided a useful platform from which to explain their ideas.

For contrast:
- Hitler got to power through legal means by being appointed chancellor by President Hindenburg while Castro was victorious in a civil war.
- Hitler's violence was directed against a democratic government that guaranteed people's liberties while Castro's was directed against the dictatorship of Batista that had been brutalizing the population of Cuba since the 1930's.
\
[6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
In both Hitler and Castro's rise the use of force was an important factor . Both leaders tried unsuccessfully to overthrow the government to get to power ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$). Hitler did so in 1923 during the Munich Putsch against the government of Bavaria while Castro tried to control the Moncada Barracks on the 26th of July, 1953 as the first step in his plan to replace the dictatorship of Batista ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$). In both cases, although they were unsuccessful in their initial goals, the coups made them household names  and allowed them a platform from which to express their ideas ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$). In the trials that followed, Hitler was able to defend himself by declaring the treachery of the Weimar Republic and his fervent nationalism while Castro presented his Moncada Program during his defense ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$). After the failure of the coups, both leaders relied on armed groups to help them get to power ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$): the SA and the SS were paramilitary groups controlled by Hitler which terrorized political opponents while the guerrilla group 26th of July Movement was Castro's guerrilla army during the civil war ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).

However, there were also some differences regarding their use of force. Most notably, Hitler's use of force was not decisive in his getting to power as he eventually did so legally while in the case of Castro his victory in the civil war is what placed him in the position of leader of Cuba ($\checkmark\, \text{difference}$). In part this had to do with the fact that Hitler's violence was used against a democratic government that defended people's rights while Castro's was directed against the dictatorship of Batista that had been brutalizing the population of Cuba since the 1930s. Therefore, in the case of Hitler, the use of force did not greatly garner him the people's support while Castro's did ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).
