# HI0152

Compare and contrast Castro's Moncada Programme with Hitler's 25 Points.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

|Top Markband Descriptors (5-6 marks)|Labels |
| :-- | :-- |
|Includes clear points of comparison| ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$) |
|Includes clear points of contrast| ($\checkmark\, \text{difference}$) |
|Provides evidence from the text (SBQ) or own knowledge (SAQ) to validate arguments| ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$) |

\
If only similarities or contrasts are identified, 3 is the highest mark.

For **compare**:
- Land reforms:
    - Castro: The ceding of land to tenant farmers, sharecroppers, and squatters who occupy small parcels of land.
    - Hitler: farmers should be given their land.
- Company Profits:
    - Castro: Profit sharing for industrial workers.
    - Hitler: Division of profits in heavy industries.
- Corruption:
    - Castro: End of corruption and confiscation of property from those found guilty of it.
    - Hitler: We demand struggle without consideration against those whose activity is injurious to the general interest. Common national criminals are to be punished with death.

For **contrast**:
-   Form of government:
    -   Castro's demand for the re-establishment of the democratic and republican Constitution of 1940 in contrast to Hitler's idea of Führer - the idea that there should be a single leader with complete power.
- Form of proposal:
    - Castro proposed the laws during his defense trial after the attack on the Moncada Barracks of 26 July, 1953 while Hitler's 25 Points were the Nazi Party's program.

[4 marks]
\
$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Much of the ideology that helped Castro gain power was detailed in his Moncada Program. The same is true for Hitler and the Nazi Party's 25 Point program. Though the 2 leaders had radically different ideas in some aspects, they were similar in others.

Both proposals emphasize the need for land redistribution to farmers ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$)
and profit sharing for industrial workers ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$). Both ideologies also advocate for the elimination of corruption in the government ($\checkmark\, \text{similarity}$).
 They differ however, in the punishment that those guilty of corruption should receive ($\checkmark\, \text{difference}$). While Castro calls for the confiscation of their property, the Nazis call for capital punishment ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).

However, a stark difference exists in what the two leaders believed the future government should look like ($\checkmark\, \text{difference}$). Castro advocated for a return to the democratic and republican Constitution of 1940. In contrast, the Nazi Party Program promoted the concept of a Führer, or a single leader with authoritarian power, rejecting democratic principles ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).
