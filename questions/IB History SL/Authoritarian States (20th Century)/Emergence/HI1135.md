# HI1135

Outline **three** key events leading to the Bolshevik seizure of power in November 1917.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- On November 2, the Petrograd Soviet sets up a Military Revolutionary Committee.
- On November 6, Red Guards took control of Petrograd's post offices, bridges and the State Bank.
- On November 7, Red Guards took control of railways
- On November 7, the Bolsheviks stormed the Winter Palace.
- On November 8, an announcement was made to the Russian people.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
On November 6, the Red Guard, led by Leon Trotsky, took control of post offices, bridges and the State Bank ($\checkmark$).

On 7 November, without much opposition, the Bolsheviks stormed the Winter Palace and arrested the ministers of the Provisional Government ($\checkmark$).

On 8 November, the Petrograd Soviet proclaimed the Provisional Government was replaced by an administration headed by Lenin ($\checkmark$).
