# HI0844

Outline **two** consequences of the First World War that helped Mussolini come to power.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- After the war, massive inflation, debt, and unemployment created widespread public discontent. Mussolini promised to resolve these issues.
- The war normalised violence to some extent, and Mussolini was able to exploit this with the rise of his fascist squads (Blackshirts)
- Italy's minimal gains from the Treaty of Versailles led to discontent, which Mussolini exploited by appealing to those who wanted to $\lq$restore' Italy's greatness.
- The war made people lose faith in the liberal government, which made Mussolini's call for strong, authoritarian leadership more appealing.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Mussolini was able to exploit several results of WWI. First, Italy was disappointed in the amount of land it gained in the Treaty of Versailles, feeling it was disproportionately less than other countries. This allowed Mussolini to use this to appeal to people's pride and promise a return to a $\lq$great' Italy ($\checkmark$).  Additionally, after WWI, people began to lose faith in the government due to its failures, making Mussolini's call for authoritarian rule seem to offer people a change ($\checkmark$).
