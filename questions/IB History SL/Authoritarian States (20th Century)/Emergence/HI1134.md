# HI1134

Describe **two** ways Trotsky contributed to the downfall of the Provisional Government.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Chaired the Petrograd Soviet from September 1917.
- Sided with Lenin in favour of an armed uprising against the Provisional Government.
- Used the Military Revolutionary Committee to organise the seizure of power.
- Ordered the Red Guards to take control of Petrograd's bridges and railway stations.
- Led the counterattacks against Cossack regiments after the insurrection.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Trotsky, as chairman of the Petrograd Soviet, took the initiative in the formation of a Military Revolutionary Committee of the Petrograd Soviet. He used this organisation to carry out an armed insurrection against the Provisional Government ($\checkmark$).

Trotsky, as chairman of the Petrograd Soviet, organised the Red Guards, an armed workers' group. On 6 and 7 November, the Red Guards led by Trotsky took control of Petrograd's post offices, bridges, railways and the State Bank ($\checkmark$).
