# HI0283

Describe **two** key events between 1943 and 1946 in Juan Domingo Perón's political career that led him to power.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

The following: 

 - Perón emerged during the military coup of June 4, 1943, which overthrew President Ramón Castillo. Initially, General Arturo Rawson briefly took power but was soon replaced by Pedro Pablo Ramírez, under whose government Perón emerged as a key figure. He became part of the military government as a member of the Grupo de Oficiales Unidos (GOU), founded on March 10th, 1943 by 19 military members including Coronel Peron.

 - Perón was appointed to key positions, including Secretary of Labor and Social Welfare in 1943-1944. He gained popularity by improving workers' rights, and introducing social reforms like wage increases, pensions, and paid vacations.

 - When Ramírez was removed in early 1944, Edelmiro Farrell took over as President, and Perón rose to Vice President and Minister of War, consolidating his influence in the military and among workers.

 - After being forced to resign on October 9th and briefly imprisoned in October 1945,  large-scale, worker-led demonstrations (17 de Octubre de 1945) demanded his release. This event solidified Perón's support from labor unions. He won the 1946 presidential election as the candidate of the Labor Party, beginning his first term as President.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Juan Domingo Perón's political career between 1943 and 1946 began with the military coup of June 4, 1943, which removed President Ramón Castillo from power. Perón emerged as a key figure within the new government through his role in the Grupo de Oficiales Unidos (GOU) ($\checkmark$). Another key event came in October 1945 after Perón's brief imprisonment sparked massive worker-led protests on October 17, solidifying his position as a popular leader. This growing support culminated in his victory in the 1946 presidential election as the candidate of the Labor Party, beginning his first term as President ($\checkmark$).
