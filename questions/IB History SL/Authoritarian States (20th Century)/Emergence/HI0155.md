# HI0155

Explain **one** of the ways in which WWII contributed to Mao's rise to power. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **1** of the following with its corresponding evidence:
- It weakened the Guomindang (GMD) economically, militarily and morally which contributed to the Chinese Communist Party (CCP)'s victory during the Chinese Civil War.
- It allowed Mao to seem like the liberator of the nation since the CCP fought a guerrilla war against the Japanese invaders.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Guomindang (GMD) were Mao`s enemy during the Chinese Civil War and the Japanese invasion during WWII seriously weakened it leaving Mao with a discredited adversary ($\checkmark$). WWII meant the GMD had to retreat from the coastal cities, the wealthiest parts of China which provided the most revenue. As a result, the GMD was forced to increase taxation on the peasants and to print money to finance itself. This caused resentment among the peasants and an inflation rate of about 25% per month during 1944 ($\checkmark$).
