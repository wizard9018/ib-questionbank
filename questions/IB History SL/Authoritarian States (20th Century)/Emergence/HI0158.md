## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
If only values or limitations are discussed, the student cannot get more than **two** marks. There must be at least one reference to origin, purpose and content in either value or limitations to reach **four** marks.

 |  | $\ce{VALUE}$ |$\ce{LIMITATIONS}$|
 | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| ORIGIN | Since the source was published in 1945 and the author lived in Central Europe at the time of Hitler's rise, he has insight into the situation. $\\$ He is a journalist so is  knowledgeable about the topic. | Because the author was placed in a concentration camp,  he is writing from an anti-Nazi, anti-Hitler perspective.|
| PURPOSE| He wants to explain why the Weimar Republic was replaced by Hitler. As a journalist, his aim is to inform. | |
| CONTENT | He gives data proving how the Nazis increased support after the Great Depression. He explains the connection between economic problems and descent into despotism. He explains the role of big industrialists and the Junkers in helping Hitler`s rise.  |He uses strong language that proves he is exaggerating his point like when he writes the Germans were “condemned to hear only a malignant and distorted explanation.”|

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The source has value in its origin because it was published in 1945 and the author lived in Austria and Germany. As a result, he has insight into the situation ($\checkmark\, \text{VO}$). However, a limitation of the origin is that Heilig is against Hitler as we can see from the fact that he was put in a concentration camp ($\checkmark\, \text{LO}$).

The value of the purpose is that Heilig is a journalist who wants to explain to the public how Germans went from democracy to Hitler's rule ($\checkmark\, \text{VP}$).

The value of the content is that the source gives data proving how support for the Nazis increased due to the economic crisis. This data is used to prove his explanation of the connection between economic problems and descent into despotism ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$). However, a limitation of the content has to do with the fact that, as the writer experienced the whole process, he uses emotional language like: “condemned to hear only a malignant and distorted explanation” . This emotional language limits objectivity. ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$)
