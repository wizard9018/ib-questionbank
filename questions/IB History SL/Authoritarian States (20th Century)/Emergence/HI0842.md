# HI0842

List **three** weaknesses of recently unified Italy (1870).
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Regional economic differences, wealthy north and poorer south
- Weak, liberal democracy run by elites
- Lack of a national identity as $\lq$Italians'.
- The new state caused conflict between government and the Church
- Centralization and elite rule during unification set a precedent for top-down governance
- Lack of modernisation
- Limited suffrage - only wealthy literate men can vote
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
When Italy was unified in 1870, it became a country, but the people lacked an identity as “Italians” ($\checkmark$).  Additionally, there was a lack of industrialisation and economic development, especially in the southern part of the country ($\checkmark$). This led to economic disparity in which southern Italy was much poorer ($\checkmark$).
