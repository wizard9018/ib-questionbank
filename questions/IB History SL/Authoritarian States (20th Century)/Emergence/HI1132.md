# HI1132

Describe **three** consequences of the July Days on the rule of the Provisional Government.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Kerensky used troops to crush the rebellion.
- Kerensky took over the Provisional Government.
- Kerensky arrested Bolshevik leaders.
- Kerensky reintroduced the death penalty.
- Kerensky appointed Kornilov as the head of the army.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
In July 1917 workers and soldiers of Petrograd staged armed demonstrations against the Provisional Government. The government was forced to bring in troops to end the riots. The July Days resulted in the formation of a stronger Provisional Government led by Kerensky, aimed at restoring order ($\checkmark$).

To restore military discipline and crush revolutionary elements, the Provisional Government became more hardline in approach. Kerensky, the new leader, reintroduced the death penalty on the front lines for rebelling, deserting and disorderly soldiers ($\checkmark$).

In August 1917, Prime Minister Kerensky appointed Kornilov as Supreme Commander of the Russian army.  Led to the Kornilov Affair. In September 1917, Kornilov attempted a military coup against the Provisional Government and the Petrograd Soviet ($\checkmark$).
