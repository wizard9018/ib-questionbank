# HI0379

Explain **two** challenges facing the Provisional Government after March 1917.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following with supporting explanation:
- The issue of dual power with the Petrograd Soviet.
    - The Provisional Government was obliged to share power with the Petrograd Soviet, which had the support of workers and soldiers. The Petrograd Soviet published Order No. 1, which gave it control over the army.
- The failure of the Summer Offensive led to the July Days.
    - In June 1917, Kerensky ordered the Summer Offensive, which was a massive failure and defeat. It sparked armed demonstrations and strike action in Petrograd against the Provisional Government.
- Peasants demanded that land was redistributed.
    - The Provisional Government refused to give land to the peasants and asked them to wait for the election of the Constituent Assembly. However, some soldiers returned and used their weapons to seize land from the landlords.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Provisional Government was not against the idea of land redistribution but wanted to do it within the framework of the law. Therefore, they asked the peasants to wait for the elections of the Constituent Assembly. However, many of the peasants who deserted from the army returned home and seized land from the landlords ($\checkmark$). The government sent out punishment brigades to try to stop the land seizures but failed to control the peasants ($\checkmark$).

Two Russian governments coexisted as a result of the February Revolution, the Provisional Government and the Petrograd Soviet, which represented workers, sailors and soldiers ($\checkmark$). The Petrograd Soviet issued Soviet Order Number 1 in March 1917 which ordered soldiers and sailors to not follow orders from the Provisional Government if they contradicted the Soviet ($\checkmark$).
