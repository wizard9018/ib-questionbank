# HI1144

Identify **two** economic problems Cuba faced by 1962.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Industrialisation had not been as effective as hoped: Factories had been built to produce goods, but many could have been imported more cheaply, so industrial production was inefficient.
- In the countryside, peasants resisted cooperative farming. Farmers were forced to sell their products to the state at low prices and thus had little incentive to produce a surplus.
- Lack of support from the middle class: policies that promoted equality between the classes led to many people with specialised skills leaving the workforce, which had a negative impact on Castro's economic plans.
- By 1962 industrial and agricultural production was not as successful as Castro had aimed for, leaving Cuba in an economic crisis and the nation suffering from food shortages.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
By 1962, Castro realised that some of his plans for improving the Cuban economy had not been as effective as hoped. Industrialization had brought about change, but the cost of building factories outweighed the cost of importing certain products from abroad ($\checkmark$). Furthermore, the redistribution of land had caused dissatisfaction amongst the middle classes as it did not economically benefit them, and this led to approximately 250,000 Cubans leaving the island. This resulted in a reduction in knowledge and specialised skills in the workforce ($\checkmark$).
