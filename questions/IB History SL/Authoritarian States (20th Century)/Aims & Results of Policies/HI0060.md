# HI0060

With reference to its **content**, analyse the **value** of Source C for an historian researching the impacts of Mussolini's policies on families. 

[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source C** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Excerpt from $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Mussolini’s Speech of the Ascension" from May 26, 1927.|

[Source: Mussolini, Benito, (1925, May 26th).  *Speech of the Ascension*. Biblioteca Fascista. Retrieved October 12th, 2024 from https://bibliotecafascista.blogspot.com/2012/03/speech-of-ascension-may-26-1927.html. No known copyright restrictions.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

- The source announces the creation of a National Agency to protect women and children. This is a value as it gives the historian a concrete date from which to date their research on the effectiveness of this agency.

- The source indicates Mussolini's intentions in protecting the family and $`$race' which is a value to the historian as it gives insight into Mussolini's motivations for these policies. 

- The source also gives the historian information about the former and current overseers of the agency, Minister of the Interior, Federzoni, and Blanc, who were appointed by Mussolini to manage the agency and its policies. This is a value to the historian in establishing cooperation between Mussolini and his Ministers. 

- The source also gives an indication about how the government dissolved other agencies and could be a value to the historian in establishing the ways in which Mussolini took over the government and consolidated power. 

- The source also gives the information about the financial situation at the time and indicates steps Mussolini would take to address the financial need. This is useful to the historian evaluating the impact of Mussolini's policies concerning women and children on the Italian economy and finances. 

- The source may also indicate the pressure that would be put on women in the future to have children through taxes on “bachelors” and “childless marriages”. 

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The content of this speech is of value to the historian as it hints at Mussolini's ideas on race and on how the fascist government could protect the future of Italy ($\checkmark$). The source also gives the historian valuable information about the lack of money the state had and Mussolini's solutions as to how to raise money by taxing bachelors and childless couples ($\checkmark$).  Finally, the source may be valuable as it indicates the pressure women were put under by the fascist state to have children ($\checkmark$).
