# HI0275

What, according to Source B, were the reasons that prompted Mao to launch the Great Leap Forward? [3 marks]

| |  | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source B** $\\$ $\hspace{3em}$ $\\$ $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |Research report titled *Causes, Consequences and Impact of the Great Leap Forward in China* published by Canadian Center of Science and Education. July 10, 2019|

[Source: Jung, Hsiung-Shen & Chen, Jui-Lung. (2019, July 10). *Causes, Consequences and Impact of the Great Leap Forward in China*.Asian Culture and History - . Canadian Center of Science and Education. Retrieved December 14, 2024 from https://doi.org/10.5539/ach.v11n2p58 . CC-BY 4.0. ]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of **three** the following:

- The Great Leap Forward was a result of the deterioration of the relations between the Communist Parties of China and the Soviet Union. 

- Mao launched the Great Leap Forward as he initially wanted to catch up with the United States in iron and steel production in 50 to 60 years.

- However after meeting Krushchev in 1957 he decided that China could surpass Britain in 15 years. 

- He was also convinced that China's economy would develop faster and better than that of the Soviet Union. 

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

According to Source B, Mao launched the Great Leap Forward as a result of the deteriorating relations between the Communist Parties of China and the Soviet Union and as a way to  lessen Chinese dependance on the Soviet Union ($\checkmark$). He was also initially prompted by the desire to catch up with the United States in iron and steel production in 50 to 60 years ($\checkmark$). After meeting Krushchev in 1957, Mao decided that China could surpass Britain in 15 years with the mass mobilisation of the Great Leap Forward ($\checkmark$).
