# HI0052

Describe **two** of the aims of Mussolini's ‘Battle for Births' policy.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

- A policy to increase the population of Italy 20 million by 1950.

- A policy to increase the population to make more soldiers and workers

- A policy to produce a new generation faithful to fascist Italy. 

- A policy to increase the population in order to populate Italy's colonial holdings in Africa.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The ‘Battle for Births' was a policy created by Mussolini to increase the population of Italy with a target of 20 million by 1950 ($\checkmark$). The motivations for this increased population was to supply workers for industry and agriculture ($\checkmark$) as well as to populate new colonial holdings in Africa ($\checkmark$).
