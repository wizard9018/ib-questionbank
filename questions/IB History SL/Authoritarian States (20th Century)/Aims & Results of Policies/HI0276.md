## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

- The Chinese people have been hard hit by the Great Leap Forward.

- Many deaths have resulted from hunger.

- Mao and his colleagues are cynical and unconcerned about the human cost of the program and are still promoting it. 

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source A shows that the Chinese people have suffered as a result of the Great Leap Forward as many deaths have resulted from hunger as evidenced by the graves and the words $\text{\textquotedblleft}$victims of hunger” ($\checkmark$). The source also shows that Mao and his colleagues are cynical and unconcerned about the human cost of the program and are still promoting it as the title of the cartoon reads, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$By government decree every member of the commune is entitled to a private lot,” with reference to plots for the graves ($\checkmark$).
