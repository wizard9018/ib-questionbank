# HI1142

Outline **three** short-and long-term impacts of Castro's nationalisation policy for foreign investors.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following, including at least one short-term and one long-term impact:
- **Short-term impacts:**
    - Castro took control of foreign owned businesses and property by passing laws that took away the legal rights of foreigners. They could no longer benefit from their assets in Cuba. Affected land, banks, oil refineries, commercial enterprises.
    - This caused a diplomatic crisis especially with the USA and an embargo on the import of sugar from Cuba.
- **Long-term impacts:**
    - Deterred any new foreign investment in Cuba - If no legal protection of assets existed investment was considered too high risk. Led to Cuba becoming isolated
    - Loss of foreign investment and expertise.
    - Economic sanctions imposed by the USA also discouraged other International investors as they feared loss of US support and markets.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One short-term impact of Castro's policy of nationalisation on foreign investors was that American and European businesses no longer had any legal rights in Cuba and could no longer benefit from their assets ($\checkmark$). This led to an immediate diplomatic crisis, especially in the USA, and they placed an embargo on the import of sugar from Cuba ($\checkmark$). In the long term, this led to economic isolation as it deterred any new foreign investments in Cuba, as there was no legal protection for their assets ($\checkmark$).
