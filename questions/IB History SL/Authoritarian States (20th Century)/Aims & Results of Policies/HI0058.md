# HI0058

According to Source B, what were the aims of the ONMI?

[2 marks]

| |  | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source B** $\\$ $\hspace{3em}$ $\\$ $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Excerpt from “Italian Social Policy for Mother and Child during the World War II (1943–1945)” written by Domenica la Banca, an historian and political scientist, and published in 2016 in the *Prague Economic and Social History Papers (Prager wirtschafts- und sozialhistorische Mitteilungen)*. |

:box[One of the welfare institutions the regime was most proud about was [The National Agency for Maternity and Childhood or Opera nazionale per la protezione della maternità e dell’infanzia, ONMI]. It was considered as the highest expression of the State’s care for Italian women and children. The ONMI, founded in 1925 and located within the Ministry of Interior for the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$physical and moral improvement of the race”, was mandated to: protect and assist pregnant women, especially those who were poor or single; nursing and weaned infants up to five years old from needy families; children who are physically or psychologically abnormal; and minors under eighteen years of age who are materially and morally abandoned, deviant or delinquent.]

[Source: La Banca, D. (2016). *Italian Social Policy for Mother and Child during the World War II (1943-1945)*. CEJSH The Central European Journal of Social Sciences and the Humanities. Retrieved October 11th, 2024, from https://dspace.cuni.cz/bitstream/handle/20.500.11956/97014/1513250_domenica_la_banca_35-49.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y. Creative Commons.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

- To improve the moral and physical conditions of Italians

- To protect women and children

- To take care of babies and small children from poor families

- To take care of children who are physically or mentally disabled

- To protect minors under 18 who are poor, abandoned or “delinquent”

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The ONMI was a welfare institution whose aim was to take care of women and children. They set up organisations to take care of pregnant women and babies ($\checkmark$). Additionally, they aimed to take care of children living in poverty ($\checkmark$).
