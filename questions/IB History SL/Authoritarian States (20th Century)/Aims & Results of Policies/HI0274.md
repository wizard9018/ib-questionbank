# HI0274

Describe **two** impacts of Mao's Communist Revolution on minority groups within China. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following with supporting details:

- Annexation of Tibet and subsequent famine:

    - Tibet was forcibly made part of the People's Republic of China, and Chinese economic policies were imposed.

    - These new economic policies resulted in a famine in which a quarter of the Tibetan population died. This was because of Chinese policies that forced the Tibetans to grow wheat and maize, which was not their staple diet. The high ground used by the Tibetans for grazing their yak was forcibly farmed, resulting in a shortage of milk, cheese, and yak hair, which were required for clothes and tents. 

- Supervision of ethnic minorities and culture:

    - In 1950, ethnic minorities were asked to self-identify and were promised autonomy. 400 ethnic groups that did so were organised into 50 groups and placed under military supervision. 

- Religious suppression:

    - Buddhist culture was suppressed in Tibet. In 1959 the uprising in Tibet against the outlawing of the Lama Faith and Tibetan culture was brutally crushed. 

    - In 1974 during the Cultural Revolution, the People's Liberation Army forced mosques to close and burnt religious books.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Mao's communist revolution resulted in the suppression of Tibet's Buddhist culture, and Tibet was forcibly made part of the People's Republic of China ($\checkmark$). The Lama Faith was outlawed, and the resultant uprising against this in 1959 was brutally crushed ($\checkmark$).

Chinese economic policies in Tibet resulted in a famine in which a quarter of the Tibetan population died ($\checkmark$). This was because Chinese policies forced the Tibetians to grow non-staple crops like wheat and because grazing land was forcibly farmed, resulting in a shortage of milk, cheese and yak hair needed for clothes and tents ($\checkmark$).
