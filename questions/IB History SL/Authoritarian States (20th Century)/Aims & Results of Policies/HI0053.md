# HI0053

Explain **two** methods used to achieve the goals of Mussolini’s ‘Battle for Births’.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following numbered points with at least 1 bullet point included:

$1.$ Incentives and Rewards from the government

- Families with many children were given tax breaks

- Fathers with more than six children were given medals

- Mothers were given ‘honorary titles’ 

- Mothers were seen as ‘heroines of the nation’

- Marriage was encouraged with financial incentives

$2.$ Penalties

- Higher taxes on unmarried men and childless couples

- Lack of promotions in jobs or limited career potential

- Educational opportunities limited for women

$3.$ Social pressure/loss of social status

- Propaganda stereotyped woman’s place in the house - pressure to conform

- Unmarried or childless women seen as ‘less than’ 

$4.$ Reproductive Rights limited

- Abortion illegal

- Birth control illegal

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

There were several methods which Mussolini used to achieve his ‘Battle for Births’. The first approach was through incentives and/or rewards ($\checkmark$) from the Italian government including tax breaks for families with many children ($\checkmark$). 

Mussolini also used punitive measures to make people afraid of not having children ($\checkmark$). In contrast to the incentives given to married couples with many children, unmarried people and childless couples paid higher taxes ($\checkmark$). Job promotions and careers were often given to men who had many children ($\checkmark$) and women were punished by limiting their educational opportunities in order to encourage them to get married and have children ($\checkmark$).
