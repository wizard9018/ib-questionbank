# HI0442

Describe the features of War Communism.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following points:

 - Peasants were required to give their agricultural produce to the state.

 - Forced grain requisition was conducted when peasants could not hand over food supplies to the state.

 - Peasants that resisted the requisition squads were executed.

 - Food rationing was introduced to control food supplies to ensure that the Red Army has more of the food supplies in order to defeat the White Army.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The first feature of War Communism is that food rationing was implemented to ensure that food supplies were given to the Red Army in order to defeat the White Army ($\checkmark$). To enable this, peasants were required to give their agricultural produce to the state ($\checkmark$). Lastly, force grain requisition was implemented as a last report if peasants could not give in their food supplies to the state ($\checkmark$).
