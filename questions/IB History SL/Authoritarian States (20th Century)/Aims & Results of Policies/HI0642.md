# HI0642

Explain how Source C conveys socialist ideals promoted by the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) during the 1950s.
[4 marks]

| |  | |

|**Source C** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Chinese propaganda poster from 1953. The caption reads, “A free and independent marriage is good, there is great happiness in unified production." |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following with supporting evidence/explanation:
- Emphasis on greater gender equality and women's liberation:
    - The phrase “A free and independent marriage is good” reflects the Marriage Law of 1950, which aimed to eliminate forced marriages, arranged marriages, and feudal family structures. The CCP promoted marriage based on mutual love and equality, aligning with its broader goal of gender equality and women's liberation.
- Connects marriage to the collective good of society:
    - The phrase, “There is great happiness in unified production” ties marriage to communal labor and socialist production, reinforcing the idea that happiness comes from working together for the collective good.
- Emphasizes the role of productivity of both men and women together:
    - The Great Leap Forward (1958-1962) emphasized collective farming and industrial production, and propaganda often depicted men and women working enthusiastically in agriculture and industry.
- The critical role of agricultural laborers
    - Agricultural labor was a key theme in Communist propaganda, portraying work in the fields as fulfilling and joyful rather than labor-intensive and harsh. The smiling young man and woman harvesting represents the CCP's vision of a harmonious, productive socialist society.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The poster reflects key socialist ideals, beginning with the promotion of free and independent marriage ($\checkmark$). The phrase “A free and independent marriage is good” aligns with the Marriage Law of 1950, which sought to abolish forced and arranged marriages, as well as feudal family structures. The CCP emphasized marriage based on mutual love and equality, supporting its broader vision of gender equality and women's liberation ($\checkmark$). Additionally, the poster highlights economic productivity and collective labor ($\checkmark$). The phrase “There is great happiness in unified production” connects marriage to communal labor and socialist production, reinforcing the notion that true happiness comes from working together for the benefit of society ($\checkmark$).
