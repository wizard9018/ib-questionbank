# HI0273

Outline **four** steps Mao took to support women who, as he stated, $`$hold up half the sky'.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of **four** of the following:

- In 1950 Female infanticide was abolished, and foot binding was outlawed. 

- The 1950 Marriage Law forbade arranged and child marriages, the payment of dowry, and concubinage. Official marriage registration was introduced, and divorce by mutual consent or on the complaint of either party was permitted. Those subjected to arranged marriage were allowed to petition for divorce. 

- In 1950 women were given the same property rights as men.

- 1953 Election Law gave women the right to vote. 

- Women were encouraged to train for jobs, and some joined the government and the People's Liberation Army. 

- During the Great Leap Forward communal nurseries, communal eating and shared cleaning supported women's entry into the workforce. 

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Mao famously proclaimed, $`$women held up half the sky', to emphasise the equal role played by women in achieving the goals of the Chinese Revolution. To ensure equal status for women, the Communist state banned female infanticide and foot binding in 1950 ($\checkmark$).

The 1950 Marriage Law further forbade arranged and child marriages, the payment of dowry, and concubinage. Official marriage registration was introduced, and divorce by mutual consent or on the complaint of either party was permitted. Those subjected to arranged marriage were allowed to petition for divorce, and in 1950, women were also given the same property rights as men ($\checkmark$).

In 1953, the Election Law gave women the right to vote, leading to greater participation in political matters ($\checkmark$). 

During the Great Leap Forward, the establishment of communal nurseries, shared eating arrangements for all commune members and shared, centralised cleaning of all living areas lessened the domestic burden placed on women and supported their entry into the workforce, leading to greater independence ($\checkmark$).
