# HI1143

Describe **two** social policies implemented by Fidel Castro to improve the rights of women in Cuba.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Federation of Cuban Women (1960):The main organisation for promoting women's rights, gender equality, integrating women into the workforce, expanding educational opportunities, and promoting legal reforms for equality.
- Maternity Law of 1963:Ensured that women were paid maternity leave and their jobs were protected during maternity leave.
- National Literacy Campaign 1961: Although not exclusively a women's policy, this campaign significantly helped women overcome illiteracy and participate more fully in education and skilled work.
- Free and Universal Healthcare Policies: Included reproductive health rights such as contraception and abortion services, crucial for women's autonomy and health.
- The Family Code (1975): A legal code that covers marriage, divorce, paternity, adoption and parental responsibilities. It suggested that men and women were to take equal responsibility for the upbringing of children and domestic labour.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One social policy that was introduced to improve the rights of women in Cuba was the establishment of the Federation of Cuban Women in 1960. The organisation was set up to promote women's rights and gender equality. Castro believed in the importance of integrating women into the workforce, expanding their educational opportunities, and promoting legal reforms for equality ($\checkmark$). The Maternity Law of 1963 helped women retain equality in the workforce as it ensured that they were paid maternity leave and their jobs were protected during maternity leave ($\checkmark$).
