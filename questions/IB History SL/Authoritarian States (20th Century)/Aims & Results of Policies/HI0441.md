# HI0441

Describe how the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$New Economic Policy” (NEP) helped the Bolsheviks consolidate their power.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - The NEP allowed Russia's economy to recover from the devastation caused by War Communism.

 - The NEP restored support for the Bolsheviks from the peasants due to the ending of forced grain acquisition.

 - However, the NEP also introduced NEPmen, which were people that benefited from the private trade during the NEP period, some Bolsheviks saw this as a step backwards from the revolution. 

 - Some Bolsheviks also saw the emerging NEPmen as a challenge to the Bolshevik state. 

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The NEP, to a more considerable extent, was successful in helping the Bolsheviks to consolidate their power. One of the notable successes of the NEP is that it restored Russia's economy, which had been devastated by War Communism in the early phases of the Russian Civil War ($\checkmark$). Since the NEP was able to briefly restore the economy and especially end forced grain acquisition in the countryside, the NEP helped to restore support for the Bolseviks in the countryside ($\checkmark$). Despite the successes of the NEP, it also met some challenges both within and outside the party. Some Bolsheviks, such as Trotsky, criticized the NEP for taking a step backwards from the revolution ($\checkmark$).
