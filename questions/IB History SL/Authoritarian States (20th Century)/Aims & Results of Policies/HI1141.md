# HI1141

Explain **three** aims of Castro's domestic economic policies.
[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following with supporting explanations:
- To secure economic independence for Cuba: Castro wanted Cuba to be free of foreign influence, especially from the USA, so he aimed to nationalise key Cuban industries like sugar production.
- To develop a centrally planned economy: Castro wanted his government to control the economic decisions to ensure they served national interests and the Cuban people. They aimed to establish agriculture under state or cooperative ownership.
- To promote rapid economic growth: Castro wanted the economy to be more modernised and industrialized, so he invested money in manufacturing and technology. This would help to improve productivity in agriculture and sugar exports.
- To improve the standard of living of all Cubans: Implemented policies of land reform, redistribution of wealth, and access to free healthcare and education for all.
\
[6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One of the most important aims of Castro's economic policies was to secure economic independence for Cuba ($\checkmark$). In order to be independent Castro wanted Cuba to be free of foreign influence, especially from the USA and so he aimed to nationalise key Cuban industries like sugar production ($\checkmark$). He aimed to improve the standard of living of all Cubans to remove the inequalities that had existed under the Batista regime ($\checkmark$). To do this, he implemented land reform policies and redistribution of wealth, and aimed to ensure access to free healthcare and education for all Cubans ($\checkmark$). A further aim was to drive rapid economic growth ($\checkmark$). As such, Castro invested in manufacturing and technology to improve agricultural and sugar production, which was Cuba's primary source of wealth ($\checkmark$).
