# HI1147

Describe **three** aims of Fidel Castro's Agrarian Reform Act of May 1959.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- To redistribute land to peasants and have more equality in land ownership
- To increase the living standards of the rural poor - land ownership titles meant that they no longer had to pay rent
- To promote further agricultural development - the act encourages cooperation, and intensive production to increase productivity.
- Encouraging growth of the agriculture industry to reduce Cuba's reliance on sugar production
- To increase the government's control over resources  - the law banned foreign ownership of rural property.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Agrarian Reform Act of 1959 was implemented to improve welfare in Cuba. Land redistribution was a key factor of the reform act and this gave the peasants greater equality in society ($\checkmark$). Peasants were no longer expected to pay rent on land so it also improved the standard of living for many of the rural poor in Cuba ($\checkmark$). Agricultural development was prioritised and working cooperatively in order to achieve intense production and increased productivity which in turn was also linked to an increase in living standards ($\checkmark$).
