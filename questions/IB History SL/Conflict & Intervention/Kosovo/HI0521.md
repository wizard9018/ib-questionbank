# HI0521

Describe **three** reasons the Kosovo Liberation Army (KLA) gained support among Kosovar Albanians. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

- Many Kosovar Albanians became frustrated with Ibrahim Rugova's policy of nonviolent resistance. After years of peaceful protests and diplomacy, there was little progress toward independence, making armed struggle seem like a more effective option.

- Widespread repression by Serbian police and military forces, including arbitrary arrests, killings, and the destruction of villages, led many Albanians to believe they needed armed protection. The KLA was seen as the only group actively resisting Serbian aggression and defending Albanian communities.

- The KLA promoted Kosovar Albanian nationalism and the goal of establishing an independent Kosovo. Their commitment to achieving self-determination through direct action appealed to many Albanians who wanted to break away from Serbian rule.

- As Serbian forces escalated violence, the KLA gained support by presenting itself as the protector of Albanian civilians.  Their image as defenders against oppression made them heroes in the eyes of many Albanians. 

- The KLA's early successes in guerrilla attacks against Serbian targets raised their profile. These actions demonstrated that armed resistance could challenge Serbian forces and gave many Albanians hope for achieving political change.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Many Kosovar Albanians began to support the KLA because they had become disillusioned with Ibrahim Rugova's nonviolent strategies after peaceful resistance failed to stop Serbian repression ($\checkmark$). The KLA also gained popularity by presenting itself as a force capable of protecting the Albanian population from the violence carried out by Serbian security forces ($\checkmark$). Finally, the KLA gained some support after its early success against Serbian targets using guerrilla tactics ($\checkmark$).
