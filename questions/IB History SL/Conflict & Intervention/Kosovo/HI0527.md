# HI0527

Using Source C, describe three reasons President Clinton gives to justify international action in Kosovo.
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source C** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Excerpt from United States President Bill Clinton, March 19, 1999, White House Press Conference. |

[Source: *White House Press Conference* (March 19, 1999). US Department of State. Retrieved April 25, 2025 from https://1997-2001.state.gov/policy_remarks/1999/990319_clinton_kosovo.html. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- The memory of past atrocities such as the massacre at Racak must not be repeated. Clinton describes Racak as the murder of civilians based on their identity.
- Serbian forces are currently mobilizing near Kosovo, posing a direct threat. Clinton references 40,000 troops massing near villages like Racak.
- The Kosovar Albanians have already chosen peace, but remain vulnerable. He says they are “without protection” despite their peaceful choice.
- Inaction will lead to further massacres. “If we and our allies do not have the will to act, there will be more massacres.”
- Strong international resolve can stop violence and save lives. “Action and resolve can stop armies.”
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source C shows that President Clinton believed immediate international intervention was necessary to prevent further atrocities like those seen in Racak, where civilians were brutally killed ($\checkmark$). He also emphasizes that Kosovar Albanians had committed to peace, yet remained exposed to violence from Serbian forces ($\checkmark$). Clinton argues that only decisive action from the international community could halt future bloodshed and protect innocent lives from a repeat of past horrors ($\checkmark$).
