# HI0686

With reference to its origin, purpose and content, analyse the value and limitations of Source T for an historian studying international responses to the crisis in Kosovo.
[4 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source T** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\\$ |  Excerpt from “The situation in and around Kosovo” a statement issued at the Extraordinary Ministerial Meeting of the North Atlantic Council held at NATO Headquarters, Brussels, April 12, 1999. |

[Source: North Atlantic Treaty Organization. (1999, April 12). *The situation in and around Kosovo: Statement issued at the Extraordinary Ministerial Meeting of the North Atlantic Council held at NATO Headquarters*, Brussels. Retrieved August 10, 2025 from https://www.nato.int/cps/en/natohq/official_texts_27435.htm. Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
A combination of the ideas below that meets the following guidelines:
- Both values and limitations must be addressed. If only one is addressed, award a maximum of **two** marks.
- Origin, purpose and content must all be referenced at some point in the response (either as a value, limitation or both) to receive the full **four** marks.

For value, any of the following:
- Provides direct insight into the perspective of NATO's response.
- Reveals how NATO sought to present its role and actions to the international community.
- It provides concrete evidence of NATO's stated objectives in its actions and its interpretation of “international community” goals.

For limitations, any of the following:
- Only represents NATO's perspective, not other international responses or their perspective on NATO's actions.
- The portrayal of the crisis is selective and self-justifying to support NATO's actions.
- Omits mention of criticism from UN member states, humanitarian NGOs critical of bombing, or countries advocating alternative solutions.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source T comes from NATO, one of the most critical international organizations responding to the crisis in Kosovo, providing direct insight into their actions ($\checkmark\, \text{VO}$). However, this could also be considered a limitation, because it provides information only about NATO's response, not other international responses ($\checkmark\, \text{LO}$).

A limitation of the source's purpose is that NATO is attempting to justify its actions, which likely indicates that they are painting their decision in a positive light, biasing their account ($\checkmark\, \text{LP}$). That being said, the purpose is to provide NATO's final rationale for its actions in response to the crisis, providing insight into their decision-making ($\checkmark\, \text{VP}$).

The source's content is limited because it does not represent, share, or respond to the criticisms of its actions at the time, such as those from the UN ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$).
