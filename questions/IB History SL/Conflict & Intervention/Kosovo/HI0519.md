# HI0519

Define the term “ethnic cleansing” and describe how it was applied during the Kosovo conflict.  [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

For definition, the following or similar:

- The systematic and forced removal of an ethnic or religious group from a specific area.

- A policy aimed at removing an ethnic group through methods such as intimidation, violence, murder, or deportation in order to achieve ethnic homogeneity.

- The deliberate expulsion or elimination of a particular ethnic group from a territory.\

[1 marks]

For description, and **two** of the following:

- Serbian forces carried out the mass expulsion of Kosovar Albanians from their homes and villages.

- Many Kosovar Albanians were forcibly deported to neighboring countries such as Albania, Macedonia, and Montenegro.

- Villages, homes, and places of cultural or religious significance (e.g., mosques) were deliberately destroyed to prevent return.

- The Serbian military and paramilitary forces committed widespread human rights abuses, including killings and sexual violence, as part of the ethnic cleansing campaign.

- The campaign led to a massive refugee crisis, with approximately 800,000 Kosovar Albanians fleeing Kosovo.

- Intimidation and fear tactics were used to terrorize Kosovar Albanians into abandoning their homes.

- Some populations were concentrated in controlled areas before being expelled.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Ethnic cleansing is when an ethnic group is systematically removed from a particular area, often by using violence or threats ($\checkmark$). In Kosovo, Serbian forces drove hundreds of thousands of Kosovar Albanians from their homes, forcing them to cross into neighboring countries and turning them into refugees ($\checkmark$). Furthermore, predominantly Kosovar Albanian villages and homes were targeted and destroyed to discourage a return in the future ($\checkmark$).
