# HI0515

Identify **two** actions taken by the Kosovo Liberation Army (KLA) during the Kosovo conflict.  [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

- Guerrilla attacks were carried out against Serbian police and military forces.

- Serbian officials and Albanians accused of collaboration were targeted for assassination.

- International recognition and military support were actively sought, particularly from NATO.

- Key regions of Kosovo were seized in an attempt to establish territorial control.

- Kosovar Albanians, including those from the diaspora, were recruited to expand the KLA's forces.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

KLA fighters launched guerrilla attacks against Serbian security forces to challenge Serbian rule ($\checkmark$). They also sought international support from NATO to strengthen their position in the conflict ($\checkmark$).
