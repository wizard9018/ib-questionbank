# HI0684

What, according to Source T, were the reasons for NATO's military actions in Kosovo?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source T** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\\$ |  Excerpt from “The situation in and around Kosovo” a statement issued at the Extraordinary Ministerial Meeting of the North Atlantic Council held at NATO Headquarters, Brussels, April 12, 1999. |

[Source: North Atlantic Treaty Organization. (1999, April 12). *The situation in and around Kosovo: Statement issued at the Extraordinary Ministerial Meeting of the North Atlantic Council held at NATO Headquarters*, Brussels. Retrieved August 10, 2025 from https://www.nato.int/cps/en/natohq/official_texts_27435.htm. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- It felt that the actions of the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia (FRY) had violated UN resolutions and undermined its values.
- It was acting in support of the goals of the international community.
- The actions of the FRY and President Milosevic created a massive humanitarian catastrophe and threatened to destabilise the surrounding region.
- The Yugoslav government had committed violations of human rights and used indiscriminate force, pushing NATO to act.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to Source T, NATO intervened in the Kosovo conflict because it was concerned about the potential humanitarian consequences of the conflict ($\checkmark$). NATO also intervened because it felt that the FRY had committed human rights violations ($\checkmark$). Finally, NATO took action in Kosovo because it felt it was acting in support of the views of the international community ($\checkmark$).
