# HI0518

Outline Slobodan Milošević's significance in the Kosovo conflict.   [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

- Milošević revoked Kosovo's autonomy in 1989, removing political rights from Kosovar Albanians and increasing tensions that led to demands for independence.

- He ordered military crackdowns on Kosovar Albanian civilians and the Kosovo Liberation Army (KLA), escalating the conflict into widespread violence. The brutality of Serbian forces contributed to NATO's decision to intervene.

- Milošević oversaw ethnic cleansing campaigns targeting Kosovar Albanians, resulting in mass displacement and widespread human rights abuses.

- His refusal to accept peace proposals at the Rambouillet talks in 1999 led directly to NATO's military intervention.

- Milošević was indicted for war crimes by the International Criminal Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia (ICTY), marking a key moment in holding political leaders accountable for crimes committed during conflict.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Milošević was significant in the Kosovo conflict because he revoked Kosovo's autonomy in 1989, which increased ethnic tensions and helped trigger the conflict ($\checkmark$). Shortly after, he ordered brutal crackdowns against Kosovar Albanians with Serbia's armed forces ($\checkmark$). He also refused to agree to the peace terms at the Rambouillet negotiations, leading to NATO's bombing campaign against Serbia in 1999 ($\checkmark$).
