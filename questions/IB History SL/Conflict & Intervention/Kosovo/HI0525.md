# HI0525

Using Source B, describe three purposes of the obligations outlined in the Kumanovo Agreement.
[3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- To stop fighting and prevent further conflict by restricting FRY and Serbian forces from entering Kosovo or designated safety zones without permission from KFOR.
- To authorize KFOR to use force if necessary to enforce the agreement and maintain security.
- To protect KFOR personnel during their mission in Kosovo.
- To support the international civil presence by helping create a stable environment.
- To permit only local police in the Ground Safety Zone.
- To clarify that any future return of Serbian personnel would require a separate agreement.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source B explains that one purpose of the agreement was to bring an end to hostilities by ensuring that Serbian and FRY troops could not return to Kosovo or nearby security zones unless they received direct approval from KFOR ($\checkmark$). It also gave KFOR the authority to take “necessary force” to make sure the terms of the agreement were followed, showing that peacekeepers were meant to have active enforcement powers ($\checkmark$). Another goal was to help establish conditions in which international civilian organizations could safely begin their work in Kosovo, such as rebuilding governance and supporting displaced populations ($\checkmark$).
