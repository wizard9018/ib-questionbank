# HI0047

Describe **two** consequences of the Rwandan Genocide.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Approximately 800,000 to 1 million Tutsis and moderate Hutus were killed in just 100 days, leading to widespread trauma and long-term demographic impacts.

 - A refugee crisis emerged as hundreds of thousands of Hutus fled to neighbouring countries, such as the Democratic Republic of Congo, fearing retaliation from the new government. Many refugee camps became bases for armed groups, prolonging regional instability.

 - Infrastructure was destroyed, agricultural production was disrupted, and a significant portion of the working-age population was killed, leaving Rwanda's economy in ruins.

 - Trust between ethnic groups was shattered, making reconciliation efforts difficult and prolonging tensions in Rwandan society.

 - The International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda (ICTR) was established by the United Nations to prosecute key figures responsible for genocide, setting an international legal precedent for addressing crimes against humanity.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

One major consequence of the Rwandan Genocide was the massive loss of life, with approximately 800,000 to 1 million Tutsis and moderate Hutus killed over a span of just 100 days. This large-scale violence devastated families and communities across the country ($\checkmark$). 

Another consequence was a severe refugee crisis, as hundreds of thousands of Hutus fled to neighbouring countries such as the Democratic Republic of Congo, fearing retaliation from the new Tutsi-led government. Many of these refugee camps later became bases for armed groups, further destabilising the region ($\checkmark$).
