# HI0987

Identify **two** ways in which the withdrawal of UNAMIR troops in April 1994 worsened the genocide.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The withdrawal reduced the number of peacekeepers able to protect civilians.
- It left vulnerable people, such as the Tutsi at the ETO school and Don Bosco School, unprotected.
- It signalled to the perpetrators that the international community would not intervene.
- It allowed the killers to operate with greater freedom and less fear of being stopped.
- It showed international priorities lay in evacuating foreigners, not protecting Rwandans.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The withdrawal of UNAMIR troops meant that safe zones were no longer secure for civilians because of fewer peacekeepers ($\checkmark$). It also gave the impression that the international community had abandoned Rwanda ($\checkmark$).
