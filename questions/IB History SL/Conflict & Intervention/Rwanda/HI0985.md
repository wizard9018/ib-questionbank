# HI0985

Explain **two** ways the international response to President Habyarimana's death contributed to the worsening crisis in Rwanda.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Delayed international reaction allowed extremist violence to escalate unchecked in the critical early hours and days.
- Belgian peacekeepers were quickly disarmed and later withdrawn, weakening the UN presence (UNAMIR) and sending a message that international forces would not intervene.
- France's role at the crash site raised suspicions, with allegations that French troops interfered with the investigation, which fuelled mistrust and diverted attention.
- No credible investigation followed, due to the disappearance of the black box and political sensitivities, which allowed extremist narratives to dominate.
- UN's restricted mandate (under Chapter VI) meant peacekeepers were not authorised to use force to stop the violence.
- International debate over responsibility (RPF vs Hutu extremists) created division and inaction among global actors, including at the UN Security Council.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One way the international response worsened the crisis was through inaction ($\checkmark$). The UN and foreign governments hesitated to react quickly after the crash, giving extremists time to organise and begin the killings without resistance ($\checkmark$).

Another problem was the withdrawal of Belgian peacekeepers after some of their soldiers were killed ($\checkmark$). This significantly weakened the UN's ability to maintain order and sent a message that the international community would not intervene ($\checkmark$).
