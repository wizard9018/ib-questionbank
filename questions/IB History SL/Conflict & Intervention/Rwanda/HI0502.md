# HI0502

Describe **two** reasons France supported the Hutu-led government during the genocide.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following: 

 - France aimed to maintain its influence in the region and feared a victory by the Tutsi-led RPF, which they associated with English-speaking powers like Uganda, the UK and the USA.

 - The French government viewed the Rwandan Patriotic Front (RPF), led by Paul Kagame, as an extension of Anglophone influence, given its connections to Uganda and support from the United States and the UK. France saw this as a direct challenge to Francophone hegemony in the region.

 - France sought to maintain its historical and geopolitical influence in Central Africa, particularly in Rwanda, as part of its Miterrand's Françafrique policy, which sustained French dominance over its former colonies and allies and ensured they remained within the Francophone sphere.

 - The Hutu-led government under President Juvénal Habyarimana had long-standing ties to France. France provided economic aid, military training, and weapons to the regime, reinforcing its authority and ensuring Rwanda remained aligned with French interests.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

France supported the Hutu-led government because it sought to preserve its influence in the region and prevent the rise of the Tutsi-led RPF, which was seen as aligned with English-speaking countries like Uganda and the USA  ($\checkmark$). The French government viewed the RPF as part of an "Anglophone invasion" that could undermine Francophone dominance in Africa ($\checkmark$).
