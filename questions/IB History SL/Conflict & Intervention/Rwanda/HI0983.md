# HI0983

Outline the role played by the Interahamwe between 1990 and 1994.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The Interahamwe functioned as a paramilitary militia loyal to the ruling MRND party, formed in the early 1990s.
- They were involved in targeted attacks against Tutsi civilians and political opponents even before the official start of the genocide.
- The group helped intimidate moderate Hutus, opposition parties, and anyone supporting the Arusha Accords.
- They played a key role in training and arming local youth for future mass violence.
- They were central to spreading Hutu Power ideology, participating in rallies and distributing propaganda.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One of the roles played by the Interahamwe was to spread extremist propaganda ($\checkmark$). The Interahamwe participated in rallies and worked alongside media outlets like RTLM to promote Hutu Power ideology and increase ethnic tension in the lead-up to the genocide ($\checkmark$).
