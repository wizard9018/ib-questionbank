# HI0984

Explain **two** consequences of the assassination of President Habyarimana in April 1994.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The assassination triggered the start of the Rwandan Genocide, with mass killings beginning in Kigali just hours after the plane was shot down.
- It led to the murder of moderate Hutu politicians, including Prime Minister Agathe Uwilingiyimana, who was assassinated the following day.
- The event provided Hutu Power extremists with a pretext to implement their pre-planned genocide, as it created chaos and removed the president who had signed the Arusha Accords.
- It caused a breakdown in the peace process outlined in the Arusha Agreement, which was abandoned immediately after the assassination.
- It led to conflicting international responses, with suspicion cast on different actors, including the RPF, French forces, and Hutu extremists.
- The incident sparked diplomatic fallout, including tensions between France and Rwanda over responsibility for the crash and the failure to prevent escalation.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One major consequence of the assassination was the outbreak of the genocide itself ($\checkmark$). Within hours of the crash, killings of Tutsi civilians and moderate Hutus began in Kigali, marking the start of mass violence across the country ($\checkmark$).

Another consequence was the complete breakdown of the Arusha peace process ($\checkmark$). The assassination removed the president who had supported the negotiations, allowing Hutu extremists to reject power-sharing and pursue violent control instead ($\checkmark$).
