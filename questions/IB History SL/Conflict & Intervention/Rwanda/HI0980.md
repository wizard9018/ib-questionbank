# HI0980

Explain **two** reasons why the Rwandan government considered the formation of RTLM *(Radio Télévision Libre des Mille Collines)* in 1993 to be a strategic political tool.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following with supporting explanation:
- RTLM was used to spread anti-Tutsi propaganda, framing the Tutsi minority as a threat to national security and encouraging fear and hatred among the Hutu population.
- The station was a platform to promote the ideology of Hutu Power, reinforcing ethnic divisions and attacking political moderates and peace efforts such as the Arusha Accords.
- It provided a means of communication to a largely illiterate population, making it an effective tool to reach rural communities with state-sanctioned messaging.
- It disguised anti-Tutsi propaganda as entertainment through music, satirical political commentary, and conspiracy theories, making the content more accessible, especially among the less-educated portions of the population.
- RTLM helped the government counteract opposition media, such as the pro-RPF newspaper Kanguka, ensuring state control over public discourse.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One reason the Rwandan government considered RTLM as a political tool was to disseminate anti-Tutsi propaganda that incited fear and hatred ($\checkmark$). By consistently portraying Tutsi civilians as enemies and traitors, RTLM helped psychologically prepare the Hutu population to accept, support, or even participate in ethnic violence ($\checkmark$).

A second reason was that radio was the most accessible medium for the majority of the Rwandan population, especially those who were illiterate ($\checkmark$). The government used RTLM to promote Hutu Power ideology broadly and to control the national narrative, effectively countering opposition voices such as those from RPF-aligned media ($\checkmark$).
