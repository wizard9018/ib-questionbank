# HI0988

Identify **two** short-term consequences of the assassination of Prime Minister Agathe Uwilingiyumana in April 1994.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Collapse of the constitutional line of succession
- Extremists could install an interim government
- Sparked further killings of moderate Hutus
- Showed that even high-level officials weren't safe from violence
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The assassination of Prime Minister Agathe Uwilingiyumana allowed extremist leaders to form an interim government without opposition ($\checkmark$). It also sent a clear message that even senior Hutu politicians who opposed violence could be targeted ($\checkmark$).
