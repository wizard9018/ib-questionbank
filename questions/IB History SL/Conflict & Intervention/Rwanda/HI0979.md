# HI0979

Identify **two** terms of the Arusha Peace Agreement signed in August 1993.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Integration of RPF into national armed forces (50% Tutsi)
- Establishment of broad-based transitional government (BBTG, Hutu-Tutsi power-sharing), including reduction in the powers of the president and the creation of a national assembly
- Ceasefire between RPF and government forces
- Repatriation and resettlement of refugees
- Rule of law to be established based on democracy and pluralism with guarantee of human rights and civil liberties as well as an independent judiciary
- UN to oversee the transition (UN Assistance Mission for Rwanda, UNAMIR) and implementation
- Establishment of Commissions for National Unity and Reconciliation, Reform of the Judiciary and Demobilization and Reintegration of former combatants
- The transitional period would end after two years with free and fair democratic elections.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
One term of the Arusha Peace Agreement was the integration of the Rwandan Patriotic Front (RPF) into the national armed forces, with 50% of the positions allocated to Tutsi members ($\checkmark$). Another term was the establishment of a broad-based transitional government that would include power-sharing between Hutu and Tutsi representatives ($\checkmark$).
