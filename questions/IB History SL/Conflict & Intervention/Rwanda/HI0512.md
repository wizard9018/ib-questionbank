# HI0512

Using the information in Source B, identify and explain **two** ways in which Hutu people were encouraged to exclude or discriminate against Tutsi people.
[4 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  An excerpt from the “Hutu Ten Commandments,” published in 1990 in the Rwandan extremist magazine *Kangura*. |

[Source: Wikipedia (n.d). *Hutu Ten Commandments.* Retrieved April 30, 2025 from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hutu_Ten_Commandments. CC BY-SA 4.0.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
-  Marriage and personal relationships:
    - Hutu men were warned not to marry or even employ Tutsi women (as secretaries or concubines).
    - Any Hutu who did so was labelled a “traitor,” framing personal relationships with Tutsi women as acts of betrayal.
- Business and economic activity:
    - Hutu were forbidden from going into business with Tutsi — including partnerships, loans, or investing in Tutsi-owned businesses.
    - Helping Tutsi get public contracts, bank loans, or import licences was also condemned. This aimed to cut Tutsi out of the economy.
- Gender roles and loyalty:
    - Hutu women were urged to be vigilant and to influence their husbands, brothers, and sons to follow anti-Tutsi ideology.
    - This extended the ideology into private and family life.
- Employment and power:
    - Strategic jobs (political, military, education) were to be reserved for Hutu only, excluding Tutsi from public and professional life.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source A shows that Hutu were told not to marry Tutsi women ($\checkmark$). Any Hutu who did so was labelled a traitor, which discouraged personal relationships and promoted ethnic separation ($\checkmark$).

It also states that Hutu should not go into business with Tutsi or help them get contracts or loans ($\checkmark$). This was meant to push Tutsi out of the economy and prevent them from gaining financial power ($\checkmark$).
