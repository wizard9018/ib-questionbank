# HI0503

Describe **three** ways in which the international community failed to respond effectively to the Rwandan Genocide.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - UNAMIR was given a limited mandate that focused on maintaining peace rather than taking offensive actions to stop the killings. This lack of authority prevented the mission from intervening effectively.

 - Key international actors were fully informed of the atrocities occurring in Rwanda but chose not to classify it as $\text{\textquotedblleft}$genocide" to avoid legal obligations to act under the UN Convention on Genocide.

 - Countries like the USA, France, and the UK showed a lack of political will to commit troops or resources, fearing the political and military costs of involvement.

 - France was not only reluctant to intervene but also actively supported the Hutu-led government during the genocide, contributing to the problem rather than resolving it.

 - The United States and other countries cited the financial burden of potential interventions, such as the high cost of jamming hate radio broadcasts.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The international community failed to act effectively during the Rwandan genocide in several key ways. The UN Security Council reduced the size of UNAMIR from 2,500 to a token force of 270 peacekeepers, even after General Dallaire had warned of impending genocide ($\checkmark$). Major powers, including the USA, France, and the UK, showed a lack of commitment, avoiding intervention due to concerns over political repercussions, financial costs, and potential military risks ($\checkmark$). Additionally, the refusal to classify the violence as genocide allowed countries to avoid their legal obligations under the UN Genocide Convention, further delaying any meaningful action to stop the killings ($\checkmark$).
