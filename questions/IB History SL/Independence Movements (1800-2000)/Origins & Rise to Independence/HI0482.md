# HI0482

Describe the impact of the Ten Years' War (1868-1878) on Cuban Independence.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

Economic impact: 

 - Thousands of plantations were burned

     - Trade routes were disrupted

     - Cuba's economic recovery remained under Spain's exploitative control

 - Political impact:

     - The failure of the war to secure independence fueled further discontent with Spanish rule

     - Politicised a new generation of Cubans

     - Gave an opportunity for future leaders to learn from the mistakes

 - Social impact:

     - Cuba's economic recovery remained under Spain's exploitative control

     - Increased the involvement of Afro-Cubans as some enslaved people were promised freedom in exchange for military service, and thousands joined the rebel forces

     - Brought an awareness of the potential power the Afro-Cubans could have in helping Creole Cubans seek independence from Spain

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The prolonged conflict devastated Cuba's economy as many sugar plantations were burned in the course of the war  ($\checkmark$).  The Ten Years War was unsuccessful and the issues which prompted the war remained; however, it gave future leaders the opportunity to learn from their mistakes ($\checkmark$). The devastation of the economy led to more social unrest which would allow for a second attempt at independence which would integrate a broader base of opposition as during the Ten Years War, some enslaved people were offered freedom if they took up arms with rebel forces. While this led to more social and racial unrest, it also showed future leaders the  potential impact the enslaved peoples could have on a Cuban Independence Movement ($\checkmark$).
