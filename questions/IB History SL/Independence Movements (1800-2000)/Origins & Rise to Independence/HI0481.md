# HI0481

Describe **two** economic impacts the abolition of slavery in 1886 had on Cuba.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following: 

 - Plantations were forced to hire wage labour, mostly rehiring their former slaves

 - These newly freed slaves often became tenant farmers/sharecroppers, but were still reliant on the plantation owner economically.

 - The cost of production increased because owners now had to pay wages, provide housing, or offer contracts.

 - Some plantation owners began to import workers from other countries, most commonly China, to save money on paying wages to former slaves.

 - The sudden increase of new immigrant workers created unrest in the countryside, which led to social unrest that had the potential to disrupt the economy.

 - As opposed to decreasing the size of plantations, it increased them as many smaller plantation owners sold their estates to foreigners (mainly US investors or other Spanish-owned plantations), which led to larger sugar-producing estates.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The abolition of slavery disrupted Cuba's plantation economy, forcing a transition to wage labor, which had consequences for former slaves because they were often rehired and became tenant farmers still dependent on the landowner for income ($\checkmark$).  Additionally, many plantation owners could not economically survive having to pay wages, so they sold their land to foreign investors, thus creating even more extensive plantations in the hands of non-Cubans ($\checkmark$).
