With reference to origin, purpose and content, analyse the value and limitations of Source A for an historian studying the significance of race in the Latin American wars of independence.

[4 marks]

|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Excerpt from the article $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Las conceptas de Pasto defensoras de la justa causa durante la guerra de Independencia, 1812-1824.” In English, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The Nuns of Pasto: Defenders of the Just Cause During the War of Independence, 1812-1824," written by Rosa Isabel Zarama, Colombian historian and professor, published in the academic journal *Memoria y Sociedad: Revista de Historia* from the Javeriana University in Colombia, in 2018.

:box[From the beginning of the dispute, the Indigenous people from the twenty-one Indian towns surrounding the city [of Pasto, New Granada], as well as those from other nearby settlements, became involved in the conflict. According to Marcela Echeverri, they supported what she calls $\text{\textquotedblleft}$popular royalism" because, starting in 1809, various authorities formed alliances with the natives. In exchange for their support in the confrontation—primarily serving as porters—the authorities offered to abolish the tribute, which was an important source of colonial revenue.

\

\

This position was not static: between 1815 and 1820, with the arrival of the reconquistador Pablo Morillo in New Granada and the return to authoritarianism in Spain, the Indigenous people lost these benefits. This was because the caciques, seeking to regain their power within the framework of the Crown, decided that their communities would continue paying the tribute. The natives became allies because they felt that Independence threatened the social and cultural structure in which they lived: the communal lands [resguardos], brotherhoods [cofradías], chieftaincies [cacicazgos], and a certain protection from the king through the protectors of the Indians.
