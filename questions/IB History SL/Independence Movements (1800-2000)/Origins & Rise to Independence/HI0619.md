# HI0619

Outline the initial aims and makeup of the Indian National Congress founded in 1885.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any combination of the following:
- The Indian National Congress was a movement by educated Indians to ensure greater political representation.
- The founding members were all university-educated, English-speaking, and initially saw themselves as a bridge between the Indian masses and the colonial power.
- The Congress called for greater Indian representation in the legislative councils, easier access to the Indian Civil Service, and less expenditure on the army.
- Membership consisted of both Hindus and Muslims, and the organisation felt the interests of the Indian nation were primary rather than a focus on caste or religion.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Indian National Congress, founded in 1885, initially asked for greater representation of Indians in the Legislative Councils ($\checkmark$). They also wanted Indians to have easier access to positions in the Indian Civil Service and less expenditure spent on maintaining the army. They aimed to represent the interests of the entire nation rather than any particular group ($\checkmark$).
