# HI0579

What is the message conveyed by Source A?
[2 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  An excerpt from a supplement to Issue 5 (1811) of “La Bagatela,” a political criticism newspaper founded by Antonio Nariño, a politician, soldier, and hero of Independence. |

[Source: Adapted from *La Bagatela - Suplemento al número 5.* (1811). Biblioteca Virtual de la Republica Retrieved May 11, 2025 from https://babel.banrepcultural.org/digital/collection/p17054coll26/id/1507. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The colonies should remain vigilant and united because the next phase of Spanish domination would be subtle manipulation, not military violence.
- Colonial citizens should distrust Spanish promises
- Spanish policies held a double standard between European Spain and colonial Spain.
- There is some collaboration between the Catholic clergy in the Americas and the Spanish government, and thus, certain church figures should not be trusted.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The source conveys a powerful message: do not be deceived by words or rewards, remember past atrocities, and stay together in the fight for independence ($\checkmark$). Another message in the source is that some church figures should not be trusted because they are collaborating with the elites to help maintain Spanish domination of the colonies ($\checkmark$).
