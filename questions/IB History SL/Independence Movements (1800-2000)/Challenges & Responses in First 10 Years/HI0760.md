According to Source C, what were the main reasons for the adoption of the Five-Year Plan in post-Independence India?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **four** of the following:
- It was adopted to mobilize the country's available resources and raise the living standards of the large population.
- Ensure that the state implements extensive developmental programs.
- To ensure improvements to the economy without undermining the democratic institutions set up by the Constitution.
- To supplement and support economic development, as private capital was limited.
- To ensure that the electorate's demands, which expected a speedy improvement in its conditions of chronic poverty, are met.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
India adopted economic planning based on Five-Year Plans, for the government to mobilize and use the country's resources and raise the living standards of its large population ($\checkmark$). This also ensured the government could implement and spearhead extensive developmental programs in specific areas that would be implemented by the state ($\checkmark$). It was also felt that government leadership was necessary for economic development, as private capital for investment was limited in newly independent India ($\checkmark$). Lastly, government guidance was to ensure economic development without undermining the democratic institutions set up by the Constitution ($\checkmark$).
