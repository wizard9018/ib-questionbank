# HI1040

Describe **two** impacts of the Platt Amendment in Cuba.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- The Platt Amendment authorized future US intervention should it be felt necessary by the USA.
- The US was granted two naval bases, including the one at Guantanamo Bay.
- The Platt Amendment made the Cuban economy heavily dependent on the United States.
- Cuba was not allowed to make alliances without US approval.
- Cuba fell under the US sphere of influence by including the Amendment in the Cuban Constitution.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The 1903 Commercial Reciprocity Treaty incorporated the Platt Amendment into law, granting duty reductions on certain goods exchanged between the USA and Cuba. Cuban sugar and tobacco were tied to US markets through preferential tariffs, while US goods entered Cuba at reductions varying from 25 to 40 percent ($\checkmark$).

The Platt Amendment limited Cuban sovereignty by granting the United States rights to intervene in Cuba's affairs. This provision was applied in 1906 when Cuba was occupied by US forces after the collapse of Cuban president Tomás Estrada Palma's government ($\checkmark$).
