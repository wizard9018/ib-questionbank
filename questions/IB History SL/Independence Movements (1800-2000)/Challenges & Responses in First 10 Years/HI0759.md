limitations of Source B for an historian studying the impact of the Partition on post-Independence India

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The origin of Source B is valuable as its author Guha is a well-known historian trained in the skills of historical analysis and specialising in Modern Indian history. The date of publication in 2008 is also valuable as the author would have accessed documents in archives that would not have been available to historians writing closer to the event ($\checkmark\, \text{VO}$). The purpose of the source holds value as it offers a comprehensive account of India's political and social evolution after independence, highlighting how democracy took root in India following the trauma of partition ($\checkmark\, \text{VP}$). The content also holds value as it details the steps taken by the Indian state in the immediate aftermath of the crisis caused by Partition and records the trauma of people caught up in events beyond their control ($\checkmark\, \text{VC}$).

However, the origin of the source is limited in relation to its discussion of events during the later years following independence, due to the proximity of these events to the publication of the book in 2008. As the long-term consequences of these events were still unfolding, this could lead to a reliance on preliminary judgements in analysis ($\checkmark\, \text{LO}$).
