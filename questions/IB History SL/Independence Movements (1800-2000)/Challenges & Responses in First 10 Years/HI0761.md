# HI0761

According to Source C, what was the impact of the expansion of the public sector on India's private sector?
[2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- There was less freedom for private enterprise as the Indian government now exercised a greater measure of regulation over the private sector of the economy at strategic points.
- The private sector was disadvantaged because the growth and expansion of the public sector had been far more rapid than that of the private sector.
- The Five-Year Plan emphasised the Indian government's socialistic outlook, which could be detrimental to the growth of the private sector.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
According to Source C, due to the expansion of the public sector, India's private sector now found that the State exercised greater regulation, leaving less freedom for the private sector to expand and flourish ($\checkmark$).

The rapid growth and expansion of the public sector as a result of the Five-Year Plan further disadvantaged the private sector and hampered its economic interests ($\checkmark$).
