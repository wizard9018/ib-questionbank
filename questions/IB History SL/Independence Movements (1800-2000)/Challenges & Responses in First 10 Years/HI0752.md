# HI0752

Describe **four** immediate problems resulting from the Partition of India in August 1947.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **four** of the following:
- Indian independence was accompanied by the partition of the country into two independent states - secular India and a Muslim state Pakistan. The borders of the new states, the Radcliffe Line, was only announced two days after independence, causing chaos. 10-15 Million Hindus, Muslims and Sikhs found themselves on the wrong side of the border.
- Partition resulted in the division of the states of Punjab in the west and Bengal in the east of India. The new state of Pakistan was thus separated by 1600 kms of Indian territory.
- The announcement of the borders led to desperate migration of people leading to a complete breakdown of law and order and the slaughter of a million people in communal attacks. Infrastructure collapsed under the pressure; trains and roads were overwhelmed, and there was little food, shelter, or sanitation.
- The resettlement of refugees resulted in a huge financial burden for the new states. Homes, land, and businesses left behind by migrants were often looted or seized.Property disputes and compensation became major administrative burdens.
- Partition involved dividing the British Indian civil service, military, railways, postal systems, and treasury. The division of assets (e.g., cash reserves, military equipment) created tensions; notably, Gandhi's insistence that Pakistan receive its agreed share of cash led to political crisis. New governments struggled with a lack of experienced administrators, especially in Pakistan.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The celebrations accompanying the independence of India on August 15, 1947 were quickly drowned in the chaos and slaughter that accompanied the partition of the country in the states of India and Pakistan. The Radcliffe Line that was to form the border between the new states was announced two days after independence. This caused chaos as 10-15 million Hindus, Muslims and Sikhs found themselves on the wrong side of the border ($\checkmark$).

The announcement led to desperate migration of these  people, resulting in a complete breakdown of law and order, with  a million people  dying in communal attacks. The new states faced a collapse of Infrastructure with  trains and roads being overwhelmed, and migrants facing a scarcity of food, shelter, or sanitation ($\checkmark$).

The new state of Pakistan was separated by 1600 kms of Indian territory as partition resulted in the formation of the new country with the the division of the states of Punjab in the west of India and Bengal in the east of India ($\checkmark$).

The division of assets including cash reserves and military equipment together with a division of the British Indian civil service, railways and postal systems created further tensions. The new governments, especially in Pakistan, struggled with a lack of experienced administrators, and Gandhi's insistence that Pakistan receive its agreed share of cash led to a political crisis in India ($\checkmark$).
