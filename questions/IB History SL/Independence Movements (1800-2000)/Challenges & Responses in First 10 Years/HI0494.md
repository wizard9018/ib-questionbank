# HI0494

Based on the text of Source A, list three things the Platt Amendment prohibits Cuba from doing.
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Text of the Platt Amendment, initially introduced in the Army Appropriations Bill of 1901 and approved by the US Congress on May 22, 1903 and signed as a permanent treaty between the United States and Cuba.|

[Source: Adapted from *Platt Amendment* (1903). Milestone Documents. National Archives. Retrieved February 14th, 2025 from https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/platt-amendment. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Cuba could not sign any treaty or agreement that might allow a foreign power to gain control or influence over any part of the island (Clause I).
- Cuba was forbidden from permitting any foreign country to establish military bases, colonies, or other forms of control on its territory (Clause I).
- Cuba was prohibited from contracting public debt that exceeded its ability to repay using ordinary revenues after covering government expenses (Clause II).
- The U.S. had the right to intervene in Cuba's affairs whenever necessary to preserve Cuban independence, maintain order, and protect life, property, and liberty (Clause III).
- All acts, laws, and rights established by the U.S. during its occupation of Cuba were permanently validated and could not be overturned (Clause IV).
- Cuba was required to implement and extend the U.S.-designed sanitation plans to prevent the spread of diseases, protecting both Cuban and U.S. economic interests (Clause V).
- The sovereignty of the Isle of Pines was left to future negotiation through a treaty, meaning Cuba could not claim it as part of its national territory (Clause VI).
- Cuba was required to lease or sell land to the U.S. for the establishment of coaling or naval stations(Clause VII).
- Cuba was obligated to formally incorporate the provisions of the Platt Amendment into a permanent treaty with the U.S.(Clause VIII).
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Cuba was not allowed to enter into agreements with other nations that could threaten its independence or allow foreign influence over its territory ($\checkmark$). Cuba could not take on financial obligations that it could not repay using its regular government income ($\checkmark$). Cuba was forbidden from allowing any foreign country other than the US  to establish military bases ($\checkmark$).
