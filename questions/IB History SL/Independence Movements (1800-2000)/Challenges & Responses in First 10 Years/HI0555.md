$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following, with reference to origin, purpose **and** content:
- The source was written in 1923, a time of strong US influence in Cuba, which could impact the author's perspective.
- The author was a known proponent of Latin American independence who wanted Latin America to be free of foreign influence.
- The author's goal is to highlight the negatives of US control and to encourage pan-hispanism in Central and South America
- The scenario described in the text is hypothetical, meant to provoke debate or discussion, and therefore does not reflect historical realities.
- The tone of the text is meant to be persuasive and biased against perceived American imperialism
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source C reflects the author's ideological belief that Central and South America should be unified, limiting his perspective ($\checkmark$). The text is also limited by the time at which it was published (1923), when the US was heavily invested in and involved in Cuba, thus influencing the negative interpretation by the author ($\checkmark$). Finally, the content of the text is hypothetical, imagining a situation for Cuba which did not exist and therefore is limited in its usefulness to an historian researching challenges to Cuban Independence ($\checkmark$).
