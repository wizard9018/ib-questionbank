# HI0490

Outline **two** key provisions of the Platt Amendment (1901).

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Made Cuba unable to sign treaties with other countries without US approval

 - Prevented Cuba from incurring debts to pay $\text{\textquotedblleft}$ordinary revenues”

 - Gave the US the right to intervene in Cuban affairs militarily

 - Required Cuba to execute sanitation plans to protect the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$people and commerce” of Cuba

 - Cuba had to give the US land for naval bases, establishing Guantánamo Bay Naval Base in 1903

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Platt Amendment was a law passed by the U.S. Congress in 1901 that established the terms under which the U.S. would end its military occupation of Cuba following the Spanish-American War  ($\checkmark$). It was later incorporated into the Cuban Constitution of 1901, effectively limiting Cuba's sovereignty  ($\checkmark$).
