## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **four** of the following:
- The British government's actions that declared India a belligerent nation in the Second World War and the The Defense of India Ordinance of September 3, 1939 that allowed the British government to rule by decree, prohibit meetings and to arrest without warrant created opposition and political tensions in India.
- The Congress protested against these British actions while the Muslim League gave assurances of cooperation in the British war effort, provided that Moslem interests were guaranteed against encroachment by the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hindu majority".
- The British used the differences in the Nationalist Movement as an excuse for deferring any concrete concessions, on the grounds that Congress and the League must first arrive at a solution of their differences.
- Following the White Paper issued by the Viceroy Linlithgow, which implicitly accepted the League's claim to speak for the Moslems of India, the Congress asked their Provincial Ministers to resign as they felt cooperation with Great Britain would be an endorsement of their imperialist policy.
- Jinnah at the annual meeting of the Muslim League put forward his $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Pakistan” plan for the partition of India into Moslem and non-Moslem states. Much of northern India would be separated into a separate Muslim region including the Northwest Frontier Province (92% Moslem), Baluchistan (87%), the Punjab (56%), the State of Jimmu and Kashmir (77%), and Bengal (55%).

\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The British declaration of India as a belligerent nation at the outbreak of the Second World War without the consultation of Indian representatives created opposition and raised political tensions. The British government tightened its hold over India with the passing of the Defense of India Ordinance of September 3, 1939 that gave them the power to rule by decree,to prohibit meetings and to arrest without warrant, in an attempt to prevent any opposition to their actions ($\checkmark$).

The Congress refused to accept the decision taken to bring the nation into the war, without the consultation of Indian representatives  and protested against this.  The Muslim League put forward an assurance of cooperation with the British war effort, provided the British ensured that Moslem interests were guaranteed against encroachment by the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Hindu majority". This move heightened the tensions between the two parties ($\checkmark$).

The Viceroy Linlithgow issued the White Paper  which implicitly accepted the League's claim to speak for all the Moslems of India leading the Congress to ask their Provincial Ministers to resign on the grounds that any cooperation with Great Britain would be an endorsement of their imperialist policy ($\checkmark$).

Bolstered by British implicit support, Jinnah and the Muslim League, put forward a $\lq$Pakistan' plan for the partition of India into Moslem and non-Moslem states. The Moslem regions included much of northern India with the Northwest Frontier Province (92% Moslem), Baluchistan (87%), the Punjab (56%), the State of Jimmu and Kashmir (77%), and Bengal (55%). This furthered the political tensions between the two major nationalist parties ($\checkmark$).
