# HI1039

List **two** terms of the Treaty of Paris of 1898.
[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Ended the Spanish-American War.
- Spain gave up its claim to Cuba.
- Cuba became independent but would be occupied by the USA.
- Spain gave up Puerto Rico to the USA.
\
[1 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Spanish were required to withdraw from Cuba ($\checkmark$), while another allowed for the temporary occupation of Cuba by the US ($\checkmark$).
