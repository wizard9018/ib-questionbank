# HI0550

Discuss the significance of Tomas Estrada Palma's contributions to the Cuban Independence Movement.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following arguments supporting the significance of his contributions:
- Unity and continuity: Palma took over leadership of the Cuban Revolutionary Party after Martí died in 1895, which helped keep unity and continuity in the independence movement.
- Organisation of various peoples: Palma was able to unify the various factions of the Cuban population (class, race, and religious differences)  under the cause of independence.
- Coordination and organisation: Palma mobilized the Cuban exiles in the United States, which helped coordinate fundraising from abroad and build relationships with key American political figures.
- Appointment of good military leadership: Palma was able to link strong military leaders (Gomez and Garcia) to the Cuban Revolutionary Party, and they were instrumental in helping win independence.
- International relationships: Palma maintained a good relationship with the US, which led to the Spanish-American War, which ended Spain's colonial rule in Cuba.

Any **two** of the following counterclaims rejecting the significance of his contributions:
- Failure to achieve Cuban sovereignty: Palma governed under the Platt Amendment, which allowed U.S. intervention in Cuban affairs. His acceptance of U.S. control over Cuban policies and economy contradicted the revolutionary vision of full independence.
- Foreign influence: His presidency set a precedent for foreign influence in Cuban politics, undermining long-term nationalist goals.
- Distance from the people: Palma, based in the United States during most of the war, meant he was not on the ground fighting in Cuba, which many who were fighting resented.
- Perception as a bureaucrat: Many Cubans, especially Afro-Cubans and rural rebels, viewed him as more of a bureaucratic figure than a revolutionary leader.
- Favored the elites: His policies were seen to be advantageous to the Cuban elite as they aligned closely with the U.S. rather than radical reform or social inclusion.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Some of Palma's contributions were significant in Cuban Independence. He took over leadership of the Cuban Revolutionary Party after Marti's death, which helped maintain consistency and leadership of the independence movement ($\checkmark$). Palma also maintained a good relationship with the United States, which later led to the US's involvement in helping end Spanish colonialism and the subsequent Cuban Independence ($\checkmark$).

However, some of Palma's contributions were less than significant.  As a result of the Platt Amendment, which allowed for US intervention, Cuba was unable to achieve absolute independence ($\checkmark$). Although Palma's relationship with the US helped achieve independence, it became a liability in his leadership immediately after independence ($\checkmark$).  He became the first president of Cuba, but he did not have the support of many who fought on the ground for independence.
