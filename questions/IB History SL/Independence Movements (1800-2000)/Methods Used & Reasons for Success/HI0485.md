# HI0485

Describe the role and significance of the Cuban Revolutionary Party (PRC) in the Wars for Cuban Independence.

[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **one** of the following roles with relevant arguments for significance from below:

 - Organise Cuban Independence into a single movement

 - Unify all Cubans across class, race, and region

 - Provide a cohesive national structure

 - Coordinate military 

For significance, any **two** of the following: 

 - Promoted a vision of a democratic, inclusive republic for all Cubans

 - Mobilized the Cuban exile community, especially in the United States 

 - Through the PRC, trusted military leaders like Máximo Gómez and Antonio Maceo were appointed, linking the party's political aims with experienced military leadership.

 - Allowed for a cohesive political structure that functioned to lead the movement for independence

 - Created an organisation that could function to raise money and support for the movement both inside Cuba and internationally.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Cuban Revolutionary Party's establishment allowed Cuban Independence to be more effectively organised ($\checkmark$). This was significant in that it allowed for a political structure which could not only lead the movement ($\checkmark$) but also legitimised the independence movement, enabling the rebels to look for support abroad ($\checkmark$).
