## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - The cartoon conveys that Nazi Germany, represented by the soldier, is increasingly pressuring neighboring countries, foreshadowing Germany's plans for territorial expansion. This is particularly relevant to Austria and Czechoslovakia, which were soon to fall under German influence.

 - The cartoon criticizes Britain and France's policy of appeasement, suggesting that by failing to take action, they allow Hitler to apply unchecked pressure on European states, ultimately enabling Nazi aggression.

 - The cartoon warns of an inevitable escalation toward conflict due to Germany's actions. The increasing pressure implies that continued appeasement may lead to war, as Hitler's ambitions will not be satisfied without aggressive expansion, which will destabilize Europe.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The cartoon by David Low conveys the message that Germany, symbolized by the large soldier pushing against smaller nations, is aggressively exerting pressure on neighboring European countries. This illustrates Nazi Germany's expansionist ambitions, particularly aimed at countries like Austria and Czechoslovakia, and shows the growing dominance and threat posed by Hitler's regime ($\checkmark$). Additionally, the British figures on the left, holding a basket labeled $\text{\textquotedblleft}$British Empire" and discussing the issue as something distant, suggest a critique of British inaction. This implies Low's disapproval of the British policy of appeasement, as they watch passively instead of intervening, which ultimately allows Hitler to continue his aggressive tactics unchecked ($\checkmark$).
