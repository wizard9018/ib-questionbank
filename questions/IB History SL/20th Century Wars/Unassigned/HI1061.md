# HI1061

What does Source A suggest about the reaction of the local population to the German army entering the Sudetenland?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | Excerpt from the diary of Wehrmacht soldier Herbert Wetzig, October 10, 1938. Wetzig describes the takeover of the Sudetenland in Czechoslovakia. |

[Source: Wetzig, H. (1938, October 10) *Soldier's Diary Entries on the Takeover of the Sudetenland (1938)*, published in German History in Documents and Images. Retrieved 21 October 2025 from https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/nazi-germany-1933-1945/soldiers-diary-entries-on-the-takeover-of-the-sudetenland-1938 Public Domain]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- The local population welcomed the German soldiers with joy and enthusiasm.
- People threw flowers and gave food, drink, and cigarettes to the soldiers.
- The reception was described as deeply emotional and genuine, not forced.
- The local people expressed a sense of relief and happiness (“true joy,” “indescribable joy”).
- There was a sense of unity between the German army and local Sudeten Germans (“brothers and sisters of ours”).
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The source shows that the local people were excited to see the German soldiers. They gathered in crowds and cheered so loudly that the music could barely be heard ($\checkmark$). People also gave the soldiers food, drinks, and cigarettes, which suggests they were welcoming and generous ($\checkmark$). The writer says the joy was “true” and unlike anything they had experienced before, which shows the emotional response of the population ($\checkmark$).
