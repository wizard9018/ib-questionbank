# HI0215

What does Source B suggest about China's role in the Allied war effort and its relationship with the United States?
[2 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Excerpt from “All we are and all we have” by  Chiang Kaishek in a message to  President Franklin D. Roosevelt on the day after Pearl Harbor, dated December 9, 1941. |

[Source: Kai-shek, C . (1942). *All We Are and All We Have* Internet Archive. Retrieved November 18, 2024, from https://archive.org/details/AllWeAreAndAllWeHaveSpeechesAndMessagesSincePearlHarbor/page/n4/mode/1up. Public domain.]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- China is fully committed to fighting alongside the United States and the Allies against the Axis powers, showing its dedication to the shared war effort.
- Emphasizes the strong bond between China and the U.S., with China expressing gratitude for American support, which was vital in resisting Japan.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source B suggests that China was fully committed to fighting with the US against Japan who they viewed as a “common” enemy ($\checkmark$). In Source B Chiang also expresses China's gratitude for the help the U.S. had given. This highlights especially, the U.S. support that helped China resist Japan before America joined the war ($\checkmark$).
