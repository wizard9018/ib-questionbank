## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Germany's defence spending rose dramatically, especially after 1933.
- By 1938, Germany had the highest military expenditure among the countries shown.
- Japan's spending peaked around 1936, then decreased.
- The USSR's spending steadily increased and remained high throughout the period.
- The United Kingdom's defence spending began rising more noticeably after 1935.
- Most countries increased their defence budgets in the late 1930s, reflecting growing tensions.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The chart shows that Germany's defence spending rose sharply after 1933, surpassing all other countries by 1938 ($\checkmark$). It also indicates that most major powers, including the United Kingdom and the USSR, began significantly increasing their military budgets in the mid- to late 1930s, likely in response to growing international tensions ($\checkmark$).
