# HI0147

What does Source A reveal about the impact of the Abyssinian crisis? [2 marks]

| | | |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

|**Source A** $\\$ $\hspace{5em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |A political cartoon titled $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Abyssinian Breezes” by David Low, January 1936.  The caption reads: “It's an ill wind that blows nobody good.” 

![HI0147](images/HI0147.png)

[Source: Low, D. (1936, January 20). *Abyssinian Breezes* from $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Low Again." Internet Archive. Retrieved November 1, 2024 from https://archive.org/details/371201-low-again-1930s-political-satirical-cartoons. Public Domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any combination of the following:

 - The Abyssinian crisis or the Abyssinian breeze is encouraging the growth of Japanese and German militarism depicted by the Japanese and German soldiers marching through the windmills. 

 - The Abyssinian breeze is “an ill wind that blows nobody good” implying that what is happening in Abyssinia is bad for everyone.

 - The Abyssinian breeze is threatening to destabilize peace which is depicted by the threat to the kite being flown peacefully by the woman. 

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source A shows that the Abyssinian crisis or the Abyssinian breeze is encouraging the growth of Japanese and German militarism. This is depicted by marching Japanese and German soldiers going through two windmills that are fueled by the Abyssinian breeze ($\checkmark$).  Source A also shows that the Abyssian breeze or the Abyssinian crisis  is threatening peace as it is $\text{\textquotedblleft}$an ill wind that blows nobody good." This is shown by the destabilizing of the kite being peacefully flown by the Abyssinian breeze ($\checkmark$).
