# HI0433

Outline **two** ways German and Italian support helped Franco's Nationalists during the Spanish Civil War. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

- Germany (Hitler) provided aircraft, tanks, and troops, giving Franco air superiority. The Condor Legion (Germany) bombed key Republican cities, including Guernica (April 1937), damaging morale and infrastructure.

- Italy (Mussolini) sent 70,000 troops, artillery, and air support, which helped Franco win major battles like Guadalajara (1937).

- Portugal's Salazar provided logistical support and allowed Nationalist troops to move freely through Portugal.

- Germany and Italy provided military supplies on credit, ensuring Franco had continuous access to weapons.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

One way foreign intervention affected the outcome of the Spanish Civil War was through Germany and Italy's military support for Franco's Nationalists. Germany's Condor Legion provided advanced aircraft and carried out devastating bombings, most famously the destruction of Guernica in April 1937, which damaged Republican morale and infrastructure ($\checkmark$). Italy sent approximately 70,000 troops, tanks, and artillery, helping the Nationalists win critical battles such as Guadalajara ($\checkmark$). This consistent and large-scale support gave Franco a significant military advantage.
