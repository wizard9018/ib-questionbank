# HI0635

Evaluate the claim that “Chinese Communist Party (CCP) members were the true defenders of China during the Sino-Japanese War” (1937-45).
[5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Since the command term is “evaluate,” the response must contain both agreement and disagreement to reach more than three marks. To reach full marks, there must be a conclusion.

The following ideas may be present in support of the claim:
- The CCP's guerrilla tactics disrupted Japanese operations, stretched their forces thin, and helped them resist complete occupation, which significantly contributed to China's defenses.
- Land reform and promises to peasants won the CCP's widespread rural support, boosting recruitment, intelligence, and resources for resisting Japan.
- The CCP joined the KMT in the Second United Front, where they set aside ideological conflict to coordinate effective resistance against Japan.
- CCP control in rural areas allowed for agricultural stability, militia organization, and sustained resistance in Japanese-occupied zones.

The following ideas may be present to reject the claim:
The KMT defended China's key cities and industrial centres, preserving vital infrastructure despite heavy losses.
- The KMT secured major U.S. support through diplomacy, gaining aid and international recognition as a key Allied power, securing China's role as one of the "Big Four" Allied powers.
\
[4 marks]

Concluding remark:
- The strongest answers are likely to argue that both the CCP and KMT fulfilled crucial yet distinct roles in China's defense.
\
[1 marks]
\
[5 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The claim that the CCP were the true defenders of China during the Sino-Japanese War has merit. First, their effective guerrilla tactics disrupted Japanese operations in rural areas, tying down large enemy forces ($\checkmark$). Second, the CCP's land reform policies gained peasant support, allowing them to recruit fighters and gather crucial intelligence ($\checkmark$). Third, their willingness to cooperate with the KMT through the Second United Front showed a commitment to defending China over party rivalry ($\checkmark$). However, the KMT also played a critical role in China's defense by securing vital international aid through diplomatic channels, gaining U.S. military support, and boosting China's global standing ($\checkmark$). Overall, while the CCP made significant contributions to resistance efforts, both parties played essential but different roles in defending China ($\checkmark$).
