# HI0343

Describe the Manhattan Project.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- President Roosevelt assembled the greatest minds in physics and chemistry in 1942 so they would discover how to initiate the chain reaction of nuclear fission and harness it into a weapon,
- The scientific efforts were directed by Dr Robert Oppenheimer and included scientists from around the world.
It employed over 125,000 people and cost about 2 billion dollars.
- By the summer of 1945 the project test an atomic weapon in New Mexico.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
In 1942, President Roosevelt brought together the greatest minds in physics and chemistry to discover how to initiate a nuclear fission chain reaction and harness it as a weapon ($\checkmark$). This was a secret endeavor known as the Manhattan Project. Over the years, it employed over 125,000 people and cost approximately 2 billion dollars ($\checkmark$). By the summer of 1945, the project had successfully tested an atomic weapon in New Mexico, paving the way for the use of atomic bombs against Japan ($\checkmark$).
