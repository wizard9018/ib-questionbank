# HI0654

What does Source A suggest about the impact of Russia's revolution on the war effort?
[3 marks]

$\\$

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source A** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  A cartoon titled “Kerensky to the Rescue” by American political cartoonist Clifford Berryman in 1918. |

![HI0654](images/HI0654.png):IDXXXXXXXXXX
[Source: Berryman, C. K. (1917, November 13). *Kerensky to the Rescue*. National Archives PICRYL. Retrieved from https://picryl.com/media/kerensky-to-the-rescue-a14bb6 on March 25, 2025. Public domain.]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- The Bolsheviks were trying to pull the Russians away from the fighting.
- The title and the drawing suggest that Kerensky's provisional government was looking to continue the fighting on the eastern front.
- The leash represents Germany's influence on the Bolsheviks. This suggests that Germany is supporting the Bolsheviks because they know it will weaken the Allies in the war.
- The bear looks confused, showing that the people of Russia may be confused on the best way forward.
- The bear is holding onto a sign that reads “Liberty” and it may be worried about whether Kerensky or the Bolsheviks are their best option for keeping liberty.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Source A shows a bear labeled Russia being dragged away from the fighting by the Bolsheviks. This suggests that if the communists were to win the revolution, the Russians would be leaving the war ($\checkmark$). The cartoon also shows the leash on the Russians being held by the Bolsheviks has a tag on it that reads “Made in Germany.” This suggests that the Bolsheviks are supported by the Germans and that they would benefit from the Bolsheviks winning and Russia being removed from the war ($\checkmark$). The cartoon also shows Kerensky, the leader of the Russian provisional government, chasing after the bear with worry. This, along with the cartoon's title, suggests that if Kerensky's side wins the revolution, they will continue to fight ($\checkmark$).
