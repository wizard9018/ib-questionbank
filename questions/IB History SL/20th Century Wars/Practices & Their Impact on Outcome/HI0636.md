# HI0636

Describe the Xi'an Incident of December 1936 and explain its importance in the outcome of the Chinese Civil War.
[5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
For description, the following or similar:
- The Xi'an Incident of December 1936 was a pivotal moment in the Chinese Civil War. Prior to the event, Chiang Kai-shek and the Kuomintang (KMT) were more focused on eradicating the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) than resisting Japanese aggression. However, frustration within the KMT, in particular General Zhang Xueliang, led to Chiang's dramatic kidnapping while he was in Xi'an. This forced Chiang to negotiate with the CCP to change his priorities. The result was the formation of the Second United Front: a temporary alliance between the KMT and CCP aimed at resisting Japanese invasion.
\
[1 marks]

For importance, any **two** of the following:
- The ending (or at least limiting) of KMT-CCP hostilities (for a time) and formation of a united front against the Japanese took the pressure off CCP at Yan'an. This allowed the CCP to expand its influence, gaining credibility by allowing them to be recognized as a partner in the fight against the Japanese. The temporary ceasefire between the KMT and CCP allowed the communists to rebuild and strengthen their position.
- Mao's position within the CCP became more solidified as he was seen as a key player in pushing for the alliance with the KMT. With a stronger leadership position and growing popularity, Mao was able to strengthen the CCP's organizational capabilities and expand its base of support. Mao's vision of a peasant-based revolutionary struggle began to take hold, and this contributed to the CCP's eventual success in the civil war.
- The KMT's internal divisions were exacerbated by the Xi'an Incident. Chiang's leadership was weakened, and although he remained in power, his ability to effectively lead the KMT against both the CCP and Japan was reduced. As a result, the KMT was less united and less effective in the years following the incident.
- The CCP used the time during the Second United Front to further strengthen its position and prepare for the resumption of the civil war after the defeat of Japan. The strategic and political gains made by the CCP during the united front were crucial in its ability to eventually defeat the KMT and establish the People's Republic of China in 1949.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Xi'an Incident was a major turning point in 1936 when Chiang Kai-shek was kidnapped by his own generals because they were frustrated that he wasn't doing enough to stop the Japanese. Before this, the KMT was more focused on fighting the CCP than dealing with Japan. Instead of killing Chiang, the CCP used the situation to push for an alliance, the Second United Front, so both sides could fight Japan together. ($\checkmark$) This event had significant consequences. The ceasefire between the two parties allowed the CCP to regroup and expand its base from Yan'an, gaining valuable time to build popular support through land reforms and resistance efforts. ($\checkmark$) Additionally, the CCP gained political legitimacy by being recognized as a co-defender of the nation. ($\checkmark$) Meanwhile, Chiang's forced compromise exposed internal divisions within the KMT and undermined his leadership authority. Although he remained in power, his ability to maintain a united front within his own party weakened, affecting the KMT's effectiveness in the later stages of the war. ($\checkmark$)
