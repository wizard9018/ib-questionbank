# HI0326

Describe **three** diplomatic impacts of World War II on Canada.

[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Relations between the United States and Canada became closer. The Permanent Joint Board on Defense was created in 1940 to formulate a joint strategy for the military defence of North America. 

 - While it had traditionally been represented by British diplomats (as Canada was part of the Commonwealth) during the war, Canada established formal, independent relations with Brazil, Argentina, and Chile.

 - The people of Canada made a tremendous contribution to the war effort which raised Canada's international profile. Almost 10% of the Canadian population had served in the Armed Forces during the war. 

 - Canada made a significant contribution to the war effort. For example, more than half of its production of war material went to the UK. Because of this contribution, Canada was given a place as one of the founding members of the United Nations after the war ended.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Relations between the United States and Canada grew closer during World War II. In 1940, a Permanent Joint Board on Defense was set up so that both countries could coordinate their military strategy for North America ($\checkmark$). The significant contributions of the Canadian people to the war effort, such as the fact that almost 10$\%$ of the whole population served in the Armed Forces,  improved the country's international profile. Many now saw it as second only to the great superpowers and thus a key global player. Evidence of this is the fact that it would become a founding member of the United Nations ($\checkmark$). This increased international role of Canada translated itself into more diplomatic representation. Before the war, it had been mainly represented by British diplomats as it was part of the Commonwealth. However, during the war, it established independent diplomatic missions in Brazil, Argentina, and Chile ($\checkmark$).
