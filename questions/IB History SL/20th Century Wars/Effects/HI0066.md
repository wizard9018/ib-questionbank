# HI0066

Based on the information in the source, identify **three** reasons why Senate Republicans rejected the Treaty of Versailles.
[3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Wilson's decision to exclude senators from the Paris Peace Conference angered Senate Republicans, particularly Lodge.
- Despite Lodge's committee making reservations to the treaty, Wilson refused to compromise, further alienating Senate Republicans.
- Wilson and Lodge's long-standing dislike for each other contributed to Lodge's opposition.
- Wilson's idealistic approach of a “peace without victory” contrasted with Lodge's desire for Germany's unconditional surrender, creating division.
- Wilson's open hostility towards Lodge and other Senate Republicans, calling them "contemptible, narrow, selfish, poor little minds," likely deepened the animosity and reduced the chances of cooperation, contributing to the rejection of the treaty.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Senate Republicans rejected the Treaty of Versailles mainly due to three factors. First, the long-standing personal rivalry between Wilson and Lodge fueled mistrust and hindered cooperation ($\checkmark$). Second, Wilson's exclusion of senators from the peace negotiations frustrated the Senate, particularly when he made the results public without consulting them ($\checkmark$). Lastly, their differing visions for post-war peace, with Wilson seeking a more lenient settlement and Lodge demanding stricter terms, widened the divide, leading to the treaty's rejection ($\checkmark$).
