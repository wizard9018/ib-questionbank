# HI0531

Describe **two** domestic political effects of the Spanish Civil War on Spain.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Francisco Franco emerged from the war as the victor and developed a military dictatorship with fascist leanings. This led to a foreign policy that was sympathetic to the Axis powers, though Spain remained neutral in World War II.

 - Franco claimed that Nationalist victory was a victory against communism and waged a domestic battle against republicans who survived the war. Many were killed in the years following the war.

 - The 1939 Law of Political Responsibility allowed the government to seize the property and land of former republicans.

 - The Church saw a resurgence in power because they were allied with the Nationalists and all citizens were required to assimilate to Nationalist views.

 - Labor unions were dismantled and, as a result, many people saw their wages reduced.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Francisco Franco's victory in the Spanish Civil War led to the development of a military dictatorship with fascist leanings. This led to a domestic battle against Republicans who survived the war. In the years following the Nationalist victory, many Republicans were killed or forced to assimilate to Nationalist values ($\checkmark$). In addition, the Church saw a resurgence in power in Spain under Franco's leadership and often teamed with him to espouse Nationalist and Catholic ideals for Spain ($\checkmark$).
