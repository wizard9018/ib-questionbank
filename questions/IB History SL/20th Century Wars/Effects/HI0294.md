# HI0294

Describe **two** of the main clauses of the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Russia agreed to end its participation in WWI. 

 - Russia recognized the independence of Ukraine, Georgia and Finland; gave up Poland and the Baltic states of Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia to Germany and Austria-Hungary; and ceded Kars, Ardahan and Batum to Turkey. These lands comprised 34$\%$ of the former empire's population, 54$\%$ of its industrial land, 89$\%$ of its coalfields, and 26% of its railways. 

 - It required Russia to pay Germany war reparations of 6 billion German marks. 

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

With the Treaty of Brest Litovsk, Russia agreed to end its participation in WWI ($\checkmark$). It also lost substantial territory, having to recognize the independence of Ukraine, Georgia and Finland and giving up its Polish lands and the Baltic states to Germany and Austro-Hungary ($\checkmark$).
