# HI0117

Describe the immediate political impacts of WWI.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Russian Revolutions: Russia's poor performance in WWI and the impact the war had on civilians was a catalyst for the February/March Revolution that brought down the tsar as well as for the October Revolution that brought down the Provisional Government
- Establishment of the Weimar Republic in Germany: faced with Germany`s imminent defeat, the Kaiser decided to abdicate and a Republic was set up to replace him. The German ruling class was hoping to negotiate a lenient peace now that the man responsible for many of the factors that led to WWI was no longer in power.
- Break up of the Austro-Hungarian Empire: the strain of war proved too much for an already embattled multiethnic empire. The Paris Peace Conference and the treaties that followed with the defeated Austria and Hungary included the creation of new countries from the lands that had formerly been ruled by the Habsburg monarch.
- Creation of Yugoslavia, Czechoslovakia and Poland: following Wilson's 14 points, the Paris Peace Conference decided to create these 3 nation-states based on the principle of self-determination.
- Creation of the League of Nations as an organisation that would solve future conflicts. This was established in the Treaty of Versailles.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Great War, as WWI was known before WWII, had many immediate political effects. One of the most important for the future of the world was the October Revolution in Russia where the Bolsheviks were able to bring down the Provisional Government and eventually set up the world's first Communist state ($\checkmark$).

Another big change was the breakup of the strong power of central Europe, the Austro-Hungarian Empire. The strain of war proved too much for an already embattled multiethnic empire ($\checkmark$). The Paris Peace Conference decided to create new nation-states with some lands from the former Austro-Hungarian Empire as well as some land taken from the German Empire. These were the countries of Czechoslovakia, Poland and Yugoslavia ($\checkmark$).
