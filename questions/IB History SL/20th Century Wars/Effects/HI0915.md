# HI0915

What is the message of Source B?
[3 marks]

| |  | |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
|**Source B** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ |  Kim Il Sung. $\text{\textquotedblleft}$On a Historic Victory in the Fatherland Liberation War and the Tasks of the People's Army." Speech before the Officers and Men of Unit No.256 of the Korean People's Army. October 23, 1953. |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following
- Kim calls the war a “great victory,” claiming North Korea forced the U.S. to sign the armistice, redefining success as resistance rather than reunification.
- The army “repulsed the enemy's invasion.” Kim labels the U.S. the “ringleader of world imperialism,” and says the war “shattered the myth of the might of U.S. imperialism.”
- Kim says the war “tempered” the people and state, presenting it as a unifying struggle that built resilience and loyalty under the Workers' Party.
- The speech claims a “great contribution to global peace and security,” asserting that North Korea's struggle boosted its international reputation and moral standing.
- The mention of “tasks” suggests the fight isn't over. The tone promotes readiness, discipline, and long-term ideological commitment by the army and people.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The message of Source A is that North Korea emerged from the Korean War with honour and strength. Although Kim Il-Sung acknowledges that reunification was not achieved, he describes the war as a “great victory” because North Korea resisted the combined forces of the United States and its allies and forced them to agree to an armistice ($\checkmark$).

Secondly, Kim frames the conflict as part of a larger anti-imperialist struggle. He claims the Korean People's Army “repulsed the enemy's invasion” and portrays the United States as the “ringleader of world imperialism.” According to Kim, the war exposed U.S. aggression and destroyed its reputation for military superiority ($\checkmark$).

Finally, the speech praises the unity and endurance of the North Korean people, military, and state institutions. Kim argues that the war strengthened national resilience and loyalty under the leadership of the Workers' Party, presenting the conflict as a transformative moment for domestic unity ($\checkmark$).
