## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following with supporting evidence from the sources:

Contrasts:
- In Source A, decentralization was done to make sure there was “stability of the occupation by leaving the day-to-day running to those who were seen by the local population as legitimate rulers” while in Source B decentralization was inteded to “prevent the establishment of a strong central government dominating the German people instead of being responsible to their democratic will”
- In Source A it says that the Allies allowed the “continuity in authoritarian personnel of the state administrations,” while in Source B Byrnes says “The Allies could not leave the leaders or minions of Nazism in key positions”
- In Source A it says that the Allies had “The assumption that both Italians and Germans were destined to live under authoritarian rule” while in Source B Byrnes promises democracy when he says “it never was the intention of the American Government to deny to the German people the right to manage their own internal affairs as soon as they were able to do so in a democratic way”
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Both sources A and B talk about how the Allied powers tried to get democracy established in Germany after the war. However, they have very different ideas. Source A says that decentralization was needed to make sure there was “stability of the occupation by leaving the day-to-day running to those who were seen by the local population as legitimate rulers” while in Source B decentralization was done to “prevent the establishment of a strong central government dominating the German people instead of being responsible to their democratic will” ($\checkmark$). Also, Source A says that to facilitate this stability they had to allow the “continuity in authoritarian personnel of the state administrations,” which is contradicted in Source B when Byrnes says “The Allies could not leave the leaders or minions of Nazism in key positions” ($\checkmark$). This difference can be explained by the perceptions the Sources say the Allies had of the Germans. While Source B says that Germans would be able to manage themselves in a “democratic way” eventually, Source A says the Allies assumed “that both Italians and Germans were destined to live under authoritarian rule” ($\checkmark$).
