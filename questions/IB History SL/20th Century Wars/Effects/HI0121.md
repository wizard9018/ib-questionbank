# HI0121

Provide **two** arguments to justify the following statement: “the results of WWI were the main cause of WWII”.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Arguments need evidence to back them up. **Two** of the following arguments with its supporting evidence:
- The Treaty of Versailles left Germans humiliated and with a deep resentment towards the Allies. Evidence of this is the fact that part of Hitler's popularity before and during his government was due to the fact that he promised to destroy the Treaty of Versailles and in fact did so in the years prior to the start of WWII.
- The Paris Peace Conference created a power vacuum in central Europe with the creation of new small states. Evidence of this is that it was very easy for Nazi Germany to annex Austria, conquer Czechoslovakia and aim for Poland which was the short term cause of the war. With each new conquest, Germany became stronger and thus ready for war.
- Japan was not satisfied by the gains it had obtained from the Paris Peace Conference. It desired further territory in China. Evidence: Japan invaded China during the Second Sino-Japanese War which would become the Pacific Front during WWII.
- The horrors of the Great War made many politicians in France and the UK willing to use the policy of appeasement with Hitler. Evidence: in the Munich Agreement, also known as the Munich Betrayal, France, Italy, and the UK agreed Germany could take part of Czechoslovakia. Hitler was encouraged by this Agreement and kept trying to undo the loss of land imposed by the Treaty of Versailles by taking the Polish Corridor and thus sparking WWII.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The horrors of the Great War made many politicians in France and the UK willing to use the policy of appeasement with Hitler thus encouraging him to be more ambitious ($\checkmark\, \text{argument}$). We can see this in the Munich Agreement, also known as the Munich Betrayal, where France, Italy, and the UK agreed Germany could take part of Czechoslovakia. Hitler was encouraged by this Agreement and kept trying to undo the loss of land imposed by the Treaty of Versailles by taking the Polish Corridor and thus sparking WWII ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).
Another reason why the impact of WWI was one of the main causes of WWII is because the Paris Peace Conference created a power vacuum in central Europe. The fact that the Austro-Hungarian Empire was replaced by three new and small states made Germany's expansion very easy ($\checkmark\, \text{argument}$).  We can see this in the fact that Hitler annexed Austria in 1938 without firing a single shot and the same is true for the conquest of Czechoslovakia. By taking over these countries, Germany became stronger and thus more capable of conquering lands ($\checkmark\, \text{evidence}$).
