## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Wilson wanted a $\text{\textquotedblleft}$peace without victory.”

 - Great Britain, France, and Italy wanted revenge and to protect themselves from potential future German aggressions

 - Great Britain, France, and Italy wanted to strengthen their colonial empires 

 - Great Britain and France wanted monetary reparations and German lands. Italy also wanted more territory in Europe

 - Japan wanted to get concessions in Asia, 

 - Fear of Russia's Bolshevik revolution made them want to secure their strategic interests

 - Except for Wilson, they did not have $\text{\textquotedblleft}$world peace” as an objective

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

From Source B, we can learn that the men at the Paris Peace Conference had different objectives. In the case of Wilson, his priority was to obtain peace ($\checkmark$). In the case of Great Britain, France and Italy, they wanted to get revenge on the Germans ($\checkmark$) and to increase their colonial empires at the expense of Germany ($\checkmark$).
