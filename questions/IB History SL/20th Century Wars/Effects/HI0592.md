## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Whole families were confined to camps

 - Even though Japanese Americans were willing to risk their lives for their country, they were still treated as criminals. 

 - Even harmless and innocent people were confined. 

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

From Source A, we can learn that whole families were confined to camps, even with their little children, such as the one portrayed in the photograph ($\checkmark$). We can also learn the unfairness of the situation as, despite the willingness of Japanese Americans to risk their lives for their country, people like Army Sergeant Kazuo Komoto, awarded the Purple Heart, were still treated as threats or criminals ($\checkmark$).
