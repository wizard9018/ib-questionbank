# HI0602Q6

Describe the United States' economic influence in Cuba in the 1950s.

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - By the late 1950s, U.S. financial interests had a great share in Cuba`s production, including 90 percent of Cuban mines, 80 percent of its public utilities, 50 percent of its railways, and 40 percent of its sugar production.

 - US capital was also important in the financial sector. It represented 25 percent of its bank deposits, roughly $1 billion in total. 

 - A 1934 trade agreement with the USA eliminated all tariffs on US goods. This made the development of domestic industry impossible for Cuba.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The US practically dominated the Cuban economy in the 1950s. U.S. capital owned 90$\%$ of its mines, 80$\%$ of its public utilities, 50$\%$ of its railways, and 40$\%$ of its sugar production ($\checkmark$). It was difficult for Cuba to liberate itself from US economic influence due to a 1934 trade agreement with the United States that eliminated all tariffs on U.S. goods, making it nearly impossible for Cuba to develop its own industries ($\checkmark$).
