# HI0301

Identify **two** social divisions in Spain before the outbreak of the Spanish Civil War and explain how they contributed to the conflict.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any two of the following for identification:

- The deep divide between rural peasants and landowners

- Tensions between urban working classes and conservative elites

- Conflict between the Catholic Church and anti-clerical movements

\

[2 marks]

Any of the following explanations (must correspond to the identification given):

- The resentment of rural peasants toward wealthy landowners created support for leftist factions, polarizing society further.

- Urban workers' demands for rights clashed with conservative elites who supported the Nationalists, deepening class divides.

- Anti-clerical policies from the left alienated conservative and Catholic groups, consolidating their support for the Nationalists.

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

One social division before the Spanish Civil War was the tension between rural peasants and wealthy landowners ($\checkmark$). Many peasants resented the lack of land reforms and poor working conditions, which pushed them to support leftist factions advocating for redistribution. This increased polarization as landowners aligned themselves with the Nationalists to protect their interests ($\checkmark$).

Another key division was between the Catholic Church and anti-clerical factions ($\checkmark$), as the Church was closely aligned with conservative landowners, while leftist groups saw it as an institution that upheld inequality. The increasing hostility between these groups contributed to the polarization that led to civil war ($\checkmark$).
