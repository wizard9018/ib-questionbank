# HI0786

Identify **two** effects of the 1884 Berlin Conference.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **two** of the following:
- Africa was divided up by the European powers who did not consider previously standing ethnic boundaries.
- Many African kingdoms and nations ceased to exist without the input of the people who lived there.
- The boundaries that were established put together people of different ethnicities and cultures. This led to conflict and war in some areas.
\
[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The Berlin Conference had a profound effect on Africa and its people. The Europeans created boundaries that had not previously existed. This forced people who had different languages and cultures into one area, causing conflict ($\checkmark$). Also, many African kingdoms ceased to exist without the input of the people ($\checkmark$).
