# HI0783

Describe how Germany's industrial growth after 1871 influenced its domestic and foreign policy.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any **three** of the following:
- Industrial growth led to the need for more resources and markets which led to Germany looking to increase their colonial holdings.
- Germany used the influx of money from their industrial growth to fund their expanding military, specifically their navy. Germany was looking to compete with Great Britain’s superior navy.
- At home, Germany saw the creation of workers’ unions and social welfare programs in response to rapid urbanization and difficult working conditions.
- The middle class gained some political power while the Junkers saw a decrease in power.
- Germany's increase in global power led to more conflict with other European powers, such as Great Britain, France, and Russia. As a result, Germany became involved in conflicts that offered a chance to undermine their enemies.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Germany grew as an industrial power after its unification and this led to significant changes in their domestic life and foreign policies. They saw the rise in power of unions and social programs to help the working class ($\checkmark$). Also, Germany used their excess wealth to invest in their military in order to compete with the strength of the British Navy ($\checkmark$). Germany also became more interested in expanding their empire in order to reach new markets and gain cheaper natural resources to further their industrial growth ($\checkmark$).
