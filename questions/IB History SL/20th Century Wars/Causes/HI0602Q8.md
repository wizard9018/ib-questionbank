# HI0602Q8

Explain the impact of the Second Sino-Japanese War on the GMD/KMT government in China.

[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following, each with supporting explanation/evidence:

 - Tax shortages due to the loss of control in wealthier coastal cities.

     - Since Japan controlled the wealthy coastal cities, peasants had to pay for the war with higher taxes. Because this is not enough, the government prints money, generating inflation. 

 - The GMD presents almost no resistance while the CCP fights against the invaders. 

     - Faced with the Japanese invasion, the GMD retreated to Chongqing, in central China. At the same time, the CCP stronghold was established in Yanan, next to the Japanese controlled areas of northern China, from where the Communists used their guerrilla tactics to weaken the invaders.

 - Lost of GMD/KMT control in Northern China

     - Since the GMD is under attack by Japan, the CCP takes advantage of the situation and starts controlling most of Northern China.

 - Changing perception of Jiang Jieshi after decision to retreat

     - The GMD/KMT's decision to retreat makes Jiang seem like a traitor and the CCP the defender of the nation.

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

The Second Sino-Japanese War weakened the GMD so much that it made the second phase of the Chinese Civil War possible. Since Japan's attack led to its control of the wealthy coastal cities, the GMD had to raise taxes on the peasants to finance itself and the war ($\checkmark$).  However, as this revenue was insufficient due to the poverty of the rural sector, the government resorted to printing money, leading to severe inflation, which reached 234$\%$ in 1944 alone ($\checkmark$). 

GMD policy of retreating to the interior and moving the capital to Chongqing also hurt the legitimacy of the government ($\checkmark$). While the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) was actively resisting the Japanese invasion through guerrilla warfare, the Nationalist Government appeared as having given up on its duty to defend the nation against foreign aggression ($\checkmark$).
