# HI0079

Explain why Jiang Jieshi said the phrase, “The Japanese are a disease of the skin; the Communists are a disease of the heart.”
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
Any combination of the following ideas:
- He thought that the Japanese were not a real threat to China, the real problem were the Communists.
- He felt it was not necessary to fight the Japanese, they would go away on their own. The GMD must eliminate the CCP first.
- He said this because the Japanese had invaded Manchuria in 1931 and the GMD had not tried to repel the invasion.
- He said this because he was trying to eliminate the CCP during the first phase of the Chinese Civil War (1927-1937).
- He said this to justify the GMD`s inaction in the face of Japanese aggression.
- He had received military training in the USSR and knew that Communism posed a threat to Chinese traditional culture.
\
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
Jiang Jieshi decided to concentrate his efforts on fighting the CCP ($\checkmark$) during the first phase of the Chinese Civil War ($\checkmark$) even though the Japanese had invaded Manchuria in 1931 and were extending their control ($\checkmark$). This is because he thought the real threat to China's existence were the Communists ($\checkmark$) since they wanted to change Chinà`s social structure ($\checkmark$). His policy of eliminating the CCP instead of fighting the Japanese brought him widespread criticism so with this phrase he was trying to explain his priorities ($\checkmark$).
