## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following with at least **one** reference to each document:

- Russia's victory against the Ottoman Empire in 1878 inspired the Dual Alliance between Austria-Hungary and Germany which states that they would come to each other's aid if Russia attacked either of them.

    - Source A: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Should, contrary to their hope, and against the loyal desire of the two High Contracting Parties, one of the two Empires be attacked by Russia the High Contracting Parties are bound to come to the assistance one of the other with the whole war strength of their Empires, and accordingly only to conclude peace together and upon mutual agreement.”

- Germany and Austria-Hungary were so concerned about the possibility of war with Russia that they included a clause in their Dual Alliance that if Russia were to support any nation that might invade or attack them, then they would need to come to each other's aid as well.

    - Source A: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Should, however, the attacking party in such a case be supported by Russia, either by an active cooperation or by military measures which constitute a menace to the Party attacked, then the obligation stipulated in Article 1 of this Treaty, for reciprocal assistance with the whole fighting force, becomes equally operative, and the conduct of the war by the two High Contracting Parties shall in this case also be in common until the conclusion of a common peace.”

- Germany and Austria Hungary invested millions of dollars and added troops to their militaries and Russia responded by doing the same.

    - Source C: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$\$67,290,000.00, in addition to the regular funds, will be expended by Austria-Hungary on her military service, and her army increased by 31,000 within five years. It has been already raised by 58,000 officers and men during the past two years, within which period Germany has added over 38,000 to her military forces with the official proposal to recruit 136,000 more officers and men inside of the next few years. Russia and France are planning to equalize these additions by similar increases in their own armies.”

- Germany and Austria-Hungary's policies were inspired by the presence of Slavic regiments that were supported by Russia.

    - Evidence: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Among the considerations which moved the statesmen of Berlin and Vienna to adopt this new military policy, none was more potent than the fact that the presence of a Slavic alliance in the Balkans sustained by Russia will hereafter neutralize a large portion of the Austrian army in the event of the outbreak of hostilities between the great powers of Europe, for a number of regiments will have perforce to be permanently stationed near the southern boundary of the Austrian Empire.”

\

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source A implies growing tensions between Germany and Russia, saying that if $\text{\textquotedblleft}$one of the two Empires be attacked by Russia" or if the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$attacking party" was $\text{\textquotedblleft}$supported by Russia", both Germany and Austria-Hungary would be required to come to each other's aid ($\checkmark$). This alliance for mutual defense suggests growing tensions in that both empires felt that an attack by Russia was highly likely, if not imminent ($\checkmark$). 

Source C states that much of Germany and Austria-Hungary's military growth was inspired by growing presence of Russia in the Balkans and specifically the fear that these Slavic regiments would be able to quickly mobilize along the Austro-Hungarian border ($\checkmark$). During this period, Germany and Russia also saw significant growth in their military. Russia had been behind the other European powers for a long time, but following their victory in the Ottoman Empire and their increased influence in the Balkans, they began to increase the numbers of their standing army. However, after the Balkan Wars in 1912, Germany and Austria-Hungary added troops and money to their military budget. According to Source C, Russia and France would $\text{\textquotedblleft}$equalize these additions by similar increases in their own armies” ($\checkmark$).
