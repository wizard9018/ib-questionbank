## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **two** of the following:

 - Some people in Havana were very poor.

 - There was a stark contrast between the wealth of the buildings, like casinos and baseball stadiums, and the living conditions of many Cubans.

 - There was a US economic presence in Cuba (Westinghouse logo).

\

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

From Source B, we can see that a sector of the Cuban population was ready for revolution as their living conditions were horrible while others were enjoying great wealth. This is evident in the shanties people live in compared to the grand building of the baseball stadium  ($\checkmark$).  Additionally, U.S. economic influence is evident, as seen in the Westinghouse logo, showing how foreign investment was not benefiting the average people's lives  ($\checkmark$).
