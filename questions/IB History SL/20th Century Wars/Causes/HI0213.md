# HI0213

Describe the relationship between the *zaibatsu* (Japanese business conglomerates) and the rise of Japanese militarism.
[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
\
The following **three** relationships should be described:
- Economic Backing
    - The zaibatsu provided key economic support for Japan's military buildup, enabling rapid industrialization and armament.
- Mutual Benefit
    - They gained from imperial conquests, reinforcing their support for militarist policies and fueling Japan's expansionist ambitions.
- Accelerated Militarism
    - Their support for the military accelerated Japan's aggressive expansionist policies in Asia, contributing to the outbreak of World War II.
\
[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:
\
The connection between the zaibatsu and the military was significant in mainly three ways. First, financial support, as the zaibatsu helped fund Japan's military development and industrial growth, strengthening its armed forces ($\checkmark$). Second, shared interests, as the zaibatsu gained profits from Japan's territorial expansions while backing militaristic policies that aligned with their economic goals ($\checkmark$). Lastly, increased militarism, as the zaibatsu's wealth allowed Japan to pursue aggressive territorial expansion in Asia, which played a key role in the onset of World War II ($\checkmark$).
