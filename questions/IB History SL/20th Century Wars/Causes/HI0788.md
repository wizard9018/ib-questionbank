# HI0788

What does Source B suggest about how Europeans viewed imperialism in China?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - China is a $\text{\textquotedblleft}$treat” to be divided among the powerful countries sitting at the table, represented by the slicing of a cake.

 - China is helpless against the others, represented by the Chinese character standing in the background, looking panicked or helpless.

 - Britain may be worried about Germany infringing on its colonial holdings, represented by Queen Victoria protectively holding her piece while staring at Germany.

 - Germany is looking to gain control over territory in China and is worried about Great Britain's reaction, represented by Kaiser Wilhelm II cutting himself a slice while staring at Great Britain. 

 - France, Russia, and Japan are interested in gaining more influence in China, but also that they may be more passive than Germany and Great Britain, as they are simply looking on rather than taking action

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source B suggests that the Europeans see China as an opportunity for them to grow their empires. This is implied by the presence of France, Russia, Germany and Great Britain at the table ($\checkmark$). The cartoon also suggests that Germany and Great Britain were fighting over territory ($\checkmark$). The cartoon also suggests that China was helpless in stopping these countries from carving them up ($\checkmark$).
