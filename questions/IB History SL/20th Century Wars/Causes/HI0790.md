# HI0790

Contrast the views of imperialism shown in Sources A and C.

[3 marks]

|**Source C** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ | An excerpt from the poem $\text{\textquotedblleft}$*The White Man's Burden,”* written by Rudyard Kipling in 1899.

:box[

Take up the White Man's burden--\

Send forth the best ye breed--\

Go bind your sons to exile\

To serve your captives' need;\

To wait in heavy harness,\

On fluttered folk and wild--\

Your new-caught, sullen peoples,\

Half-devil and half-child.\

\

Take up the White Man's burden--\

In patience to abide,\

To veil the threat of terror\

And check the show of pride;\

By open speech and simple,\

An hundred times made plain\

To seek another's profit,\

And work another's gain.\

\

Take up the White Man's burden--\

The savage wars of peace--\

Fill full the mouth of Famine\

And bid the sickness cease;\

And when your goal is nearest\

The end for others sought,\

Watch sloth and heathen Folly\

Bring all your hopes to nought.\

\

[…]\

\

Take up the White Man's burden--\

And reap his old reward:\

The blame of those ye better,\

The hate of those ye guard--\

The cry of hosts ye humour\

(Ah, slowly!) toward the light:--\

"Why brought he us from bondage,\

Our loved Egyptian night?"

\

]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

 - Source C implies that the colonists would benefit from being colonized while Source A shows that the people are mistreated.

     - Source A shows a Congolese worker struggling against the coils of Belgium. 

     - Source C: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Go bind your sons to exile To serve your captives' need”

 - Source A implies that Belgium is using the Congo in order to benefit from their natural resources, specifically rubber. Source C implies that the white man would not benefit at all.

     - Source A refers to the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$rubber coils” implying the importance of that resource to Belgium

     - Source C: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$To seek another's profit, And work another's gain.”

 - Source A shows that the colonists feared the Belgians, specifically King Leopold II, because of their mistreatment. Source C says that the colonists would be resentful because the Europeans were their betters.

     - Source A shows colonists running away in fear

     - Source C: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$The blame of those ye better, The hate of those ye guard-”

 - Source A does not show any benefits to colonization while Source C says that the colonizers would help stop famine.

     - Source A only focuses on the negative aspects of imperialism in the Congo.

     - Source C: $\text{\textquotedblleft}$Fill full the mouth of Famine And bid the sickness cease.”

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

Source C portrays a selfless version of imperialism where Western nations are colonizing countries for their own good while Source A shows that nations were using their colonies for resources ($\checkmark$). Also Source A implies that colonists are being mistreated, as evidenced by the snake coiling around the worker, while Source C only focuses on the benefits of imperialism ($\checkmark$). Source C focuses on the fact that the colonists were likely to resent westerners for trying to modernize them. This is shown when Kipling writes that white men will get the $\text{\textquotedblleft}$hate of those they guard.” However,  Source A shows that the colonists feared the westerners who would mistreat them, illustrated by the fearful looks of the workers as they ran from the snake ($\checkmark$).
