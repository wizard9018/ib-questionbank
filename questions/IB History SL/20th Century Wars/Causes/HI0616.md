According to Source C, how did the Balkan wars influence militarism in Europe?  [3 marks]

$\\$

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

\

Any **three** of the following:

- Austria-Hungary added 31,000 people to their military in five years.

- Austria-Hungary and Germany added over $67 million to their military budget.

- Germany recruited more than 38,000 people to the military and promised to add 136,000 more.

- France and Russia vowed to increase their military by similar amounts.

\

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

\

According to Document C, the Balkan Wars inspired Austria- Hungary to increase their military by over 30,000 people ($\checkmark$). Germany also added more than 38,000 soldiers to their ranks with the promise of adding thousands more ($\checkmark$). France and Russia planned to add to their ranks in order to keep up with Austria-Hungary and Germany ($\checkmark$).
