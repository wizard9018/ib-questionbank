1. Explain how the properties of water allow it to be transported in the xylem.  [3 marks]

2. Describe a physical property of water, other than those that are important for the movement of water through the plant, that makes water essential for the survival of plants.    [1 mark]

3. Outline why plants cannot use water to transport lipids.    [1 mark]

4. If the temperature drops to -1:sup[o]C for a few hours suggest a reason why the water in xylem may not freeze.    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ water is cohesive **OR** hydrogen bonding between water molecules **OR** strong intermolecular forces so can form a continuous column of water **OR** counteract gravity     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water is adhesive **OR** water molecules can adhere to other polar surfaces **OR** water adheres to walls of xylem vessels **OR** to lignin and cellulose of xylem vessels, so prevent collapse of water column **OR** separation from xylem vessel walls     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ capillary action occurs as water moves upwards against gravity in the narrow xylem vessels         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the hydrogen bonds between water molecules mean that as water evaporates  from the leaf  negative pressure **OR** tension occurs, so water moves upwards against gravity         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ low viscosity reduces resistance to flow within the xylem         [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
Water can be transported efficiently through the xylem as the water molecules adhere to the inside of the xylem vessels ($\checkmark$) and cohere to each other, therefore forming a continual column of water ($\checkmark$). As the water evaporates from the leaves, the hydrogen bonds between the molecules mean that a negative pressure occurs at the top of this column of water, enabling the water to move upwards, against gravity ($\checkmark$).  This is the cohesion-tension theory.

2. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ water is a solvent so can dissolve nutrients which can be used by the plant **OR** provides a medium for various biochemical reactions     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ high specific heat capacity prevents extreme temperature fluctuations that could damage plant tissues **OR** maintain optimal enzyme activity and metabolic processes in plants     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ transparency allows light to penetrate and reach submerged plants for photosynthesis         [1 mark]

    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Water is known as a universal solvent, so nutrients can be dissolved in it and be transported to where they are needed ($\checkmark$).

 3. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ lipids are hydrophobic **OR** non-polar, so cannot dissolve in water     [1 mark]

    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Water cannot be used to transport lipids in plants as they are hydrophobic  ($\checkmark$).

 4. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ water has a high specific heat capacity, **OR** it has to lose a lot of energy to freeze     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ due to the high solute concentration of the contents of the xylem         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increase in pressure in the xylem raises the freezing point of water             [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ high specific heat capacity means water can release stored heat slowly, maintaining a stable temperature above freezing point                 [1 mark]

    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Water in the xylem may not freeze when the temperature drops below -1:sup[o]C, as it has a high specific heat capacity  ($\checkmark$).
