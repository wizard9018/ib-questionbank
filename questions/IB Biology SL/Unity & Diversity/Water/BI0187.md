2. Describe, using an example, the importance of water as a solvent in living organisms. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$  cohesion, **OR** cohesive properties, of water is responsible [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  water molecules bind to other water molecules [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  water molecules form intermolecular hydrogen bonds [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  cohesion results in high surface tension of water [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  legs of the insect are hydrophobic [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    **Cohesion**($\checkmark$) is the water property that is responsible for this phenomenon. Water **molecules bind with other water molecules** ($\checkmark$) through **hydrogen bonds** ($\checkmark$)to create **high surface tension**($\checkmark$). The legs of the water strider are covered in a secreted wax, making them **hydrophobic**($\checkmark$), which allows the water strider to move on top of the water.

2. Any **three** of the following (maximum of 2 marks if no example is provided):

    $\ce{ -> }$  metabolic reactions can only happen in solutions [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  water dissolves hydrophilic substances needed for life processes [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  substances can be transported through the water-based solutions [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  blood plasma dissolves and transports sodium chloride **OR** glucose **OR** amino acids **OR** any other valid example in animals [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  water dissolves and transports nutrients **OR** minerals needed by plants **OR** any other valid example in plants [1 mark]

    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    **Water dissolves hydrophilic substances**($\checkmark$)
required for **metabolic reactions** ($\checkmark$) which can only occur in solution.  Dissolved substances can react with each other and also be **transported**($\checkmark$)to other parts of an organism. For example, water dissolves and transports glucose in the **blood plasma**($\checkmark$).
