# BI1479

What makes up the genome of all eukaryotes but not prokaryotes?

- I. $\hspace{1.6em}$ Chromosomes and mitochondrial DNA

- II. $\hspace{1.3em}$ Chloroplast DNA

- III. $\hspace{1em}$ Plasmids and ribosomal DNA

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

The genome is the whole of the genetic information of an organism. In eukaryotes, chromosomes and mitochondrial DNA (**Statement I**) make up the genome. **Choice A**  is correct.

Chloroplast DNA is not found in all eukaryotes (**Statement II**). It is primarily found in plant cells but not animal cells; **Statement II** is incorrect. Plasmids are found in prokaryotes but not eukaryotes, and ribosomes do not contain DNA, **Statement III**  is incorrect. **Choices B, C** and **D**  are incorrect as they all contain **Statements II** and/or **III**.
