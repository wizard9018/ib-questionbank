# BI2321

Whole genome sequencing has been used to sequence the genomes of many species. Which of the following are potential future uses of whole genome sequencing?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Identifying disease-causing alleles.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Sequencing the genomes of bacteria and fungi.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Advancing personalised medicine.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Identifying evolutionary relationships between species.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because personalised medicine involves using an individual's entire genetic profile to predict their response to specific drugs and tailor treatments accordingly. While the technology exists, its integration into routine clinical practice for most patients is considered a primary future goal of genomics.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the identification of disease-causing alleles (such as the BRCA1 gene or those for cystic fibrosis) is a well-established current use of genome sequencing.

**Choice B** is incorrect because the sequencing of prokaryotes (bacteria) and fungi was among the earliest successes of the Human Genome Project era and is currently performed routinely in pathology and research labs.

**Choice D** is incorrect because comparing whole genomes to construct phylogenetic trees and determine evolutionary relationships is a standard contemporary method in modern cladistics.
