# BI0908

The table shows the number of tree species in two similarly sized forests. Which statement about the two communities is correct?

| | Tree species | Forest 1 | Forest 2
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$ | *Quercus alba* | 30 | 25 |
| $\hspace{1em}$ | *Acer rubrum* | 12 | 25 |
| $\hspace{1em}$ | *Fagus sylvatica*| 50 | 25 |
| $\hspace{1em}$ | *Betula occidentalis* | 8 | 25|
| $\hspace{1em}$ | **Total number of trees** | 100 | 100|

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Forest 2 has the same species richness as Forest 1

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Forest 1 has a higher species evenness than Forest 2

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Forest 1 has a higher species richness than Forest 2

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Forest 2 has an equal species evenness to Forest 1

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Species richness is the number of different species present in an area. There are 4 species present in both forests so **Choice A** is correct.

Species evenness is the relative abundance of different species in an area. **Choices B** and **D** are incorrect as Forest 2 has a higher species evenness than Forest 1 as there is an equal number of each tree species. **Choice C** is incorrect as species richness is the same in both forests.
