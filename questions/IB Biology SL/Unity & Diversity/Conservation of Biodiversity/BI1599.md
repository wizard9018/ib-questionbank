# BI1599

**Maximum mark: 3**

Conservationists must consider the size and arrangement of in situ nature parks when creating and managing them. The diagram below shows the theoretical design of three sets of nature reserves.

![nature reserve design BI1599ii](<images/nature reserve design BI1599ii.png>)

For each pair of designs labelled A and B, compare the two and determine which design is better suited for the long-term preservation and management of animal populations. For each comparison, provide a reason to justify your choice. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**AI vs BI**

any **one** of the following:

*AI is better than BI sincee a larger reserve*:

$\ce{ -> }$ supports more species **OR** a wider variety of species [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ ensures a more stable food chain **OR** provides more resources [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ reduction in the chance of inbreeding **OR** extinction [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ can better buffer species against environmental changes because it includes a variety of microhabitats **OR** climates [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ more likely to support keystone species, critical for structure of community [1 mark]

**AII vs BII**

any **one** of the following:

*AII is better than BII since a less fragmented reserve*:

$\ce{ -> }$ has better connectivity for breeding **OR** gene flow [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ has reduced edge effect so fewer disturbances **OR** predators **OR** invasive species **OR** reduces habitat degradation preserving the core habitat for sensitive species [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ provides enough resources to sustain viable populations in the long term [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ might reduce human-wildlife conflict, as animals less likely to stray into human-populated areas [1 mark]

**AIII vs BIII**

any **one** of the following:

*AIII is better than BIII since corridors*:

$\ce{ -> }$ facilitate movement of animals **OR** migration which is crucial for breeding **OR** finding food **OR** escaping predators **OR** for maintaining genetic diversity [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ can make the reserve more accessible for maintenance and management purposes [1 mark]

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
**AI is better** than BI because the **larger reserve** area in AI can **support more species**, as it provides **more resources** ($\checkmark$).
**AII is better** than BII because it is **less fragmented**. This **minimises** the **edge effect**, which **reduces exposure** to threats such as **poaching**, **predation**, and **invasive species**. ($\checkmark$).
**AIII is better** than BIII because the **corridors** in AIII **facilitate animal movement**, which is essential for **maintaining genetic diversity**, accessing **resources**, and **escaping predators**. ($\checkmark$).
