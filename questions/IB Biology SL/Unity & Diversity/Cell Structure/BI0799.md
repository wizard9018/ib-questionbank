# BI0799

**Maximum mark: 4**

The micrograph below shows *E. coli* cells.

![BI0799A](images/BI0799A.png)
[Source: Adapted from Highton, P., 1968. *E.coli image.jpeg*. Wikimedia Commons. Retrieved 29th January 2023 from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:E.coli_image.jpg. Copyright under Creative CommonsCC0 1.0]

1. Identify the structure labelled I in the micrograph. [1 mark]

2. If the magnification of the image is x36,000 and the length of cell II in the image is 54 mm, calculate the actual length of cell II in micrometres (µm). [2 marks]

3. State **one** feature, other than the structure labelled I, that can be used to identify the cells in this micrograph as prokaryotic cells. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ Nucleoid **OR** region of circular DNA **OR** region of bacterial chromosome **OR** circular DNA    [1 mark]
[1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:  \
$\hspace{1em}$ Structure I is the **nucleoid** ($\checkmark$).

2.

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ = $\left(\dfrac{\text{54000 µm}}{\text{36000}}\right) $
[1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ = 1.5µm    [1 mark]
[2 marks]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:  \
$\hspace{1em}$ Magnification = x36000

$\hspace{1em}$ Length in image = 54mm x 1000 = 54000 µm

$\hspace{1em}$ Actual length

$\hspace{1em}$ = $\left(\dfrac{\textbf{54000 µm}}{\textbf{36000}}\right) $  ($\checkmark$)

$\hspace{1em}$ = **1.5 µm** ($\checkmark$)

3. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ cell wall of peptidoglycan    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ cell in binary fission        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ no compartments in cell **OR** no membrane bound organelles **OR** no nucleus    [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:  \
    The cell does **not have** a nucleus, or any other **membrane bound organelles** ($\checkmark$).
