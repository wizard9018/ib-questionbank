# BI0560

Palisade mesophyll cells produce and release large amounts of carbohydrates. Which characteristics would you expect to observe in such cells?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ A high density of mitochondria and a large central vacuole.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ A high density of chloroplasts and an extensive Golgi apparatus.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ A high density of chloroplasts and a large central vacuole.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ A high density of mitochondria and an extensive Golgi apparatus.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because palisade mesophyll cells are the primary site of photosynthesis. A high density of chloroplasts is required to capture light energy. The large central vacuole is essential because it pushes the chloroplasts to the edges of the cell, minimising the distance carbon dioxide must diffuse and maximising light absorption.

**Choices A** and **D** are incorrect because while palisade cells contain mitochondria for cellular maintenance, their dominant characteristic is the machinery for production (chloroplasts), not just the breakdown of energy.

**Choice B** is incorrect because although the Golgi apparatus is involved in cell wall carbohydrate packaging, the central vacuole is a more significant and defining structural adaptation for the specific function of light-dependent carbohydrate production in the mesophyll.
