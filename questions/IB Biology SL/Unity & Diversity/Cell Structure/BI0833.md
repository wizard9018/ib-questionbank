## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **four** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ surface area affects the rate at which materials enter and leave the cell [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ volume affects the rate at which materials are used and produced by the cell [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the surface area to volume ratio affects the rate of metabolism in a cell [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the bigger the cell, the smaller the SA:V ratio is [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ cells having a low SA:V ratio cannot exchange materials fast enough [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ some cells, **OR** motor neurons, **OR** muscle cells, **OR** other named example in humans, are long and thin, which maintains a high SA:V ratio [1 mark]
 [4 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
 The **surface area** of a cell affects the rate at which materials **enter and leave cells** ($\checkmark$). On the other hand, the cell’s **volume** affects the rate at which materials are **used and produced** ($\checkmark$). A small surface area to volume ratio is not ideal for human cells because it **limits the cell’s ability to export waste from the cytoplasm** rapidly enough to meet the **cytoplasm’s metabolic needs, such as the availability of oxygen** ($\checkmark$). **A large cell has a small surface area to volume ratio** ($\checkmark$). Therefore, the transport of the substances is more efficient in small cells.

2. Any **four** of the following (function of life must be mentioned when explaining how *Paramecium* performs this, to award mark):

 $\ce{ -> }$ functions of life are metabolism **AND** growth **AND** reproduction **AND** nutrition **AND** homeostasis **AND** movement **AND** excretion **AND** response to stimuli, **OR** sensitivity [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ movement in *Paramecium* is demonstrated by cilia used to propel themselves through water [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ response is through reacting to stimuli in the environment **OR** avoidance of light [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ homeostasis is demonstrated by keeping the internal conditions within set limits **OR** excess water is expelled by the contractile vacuole [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ metabolism is carried out by enzymes in the cytoplasm [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ growth is an irreversible increase in size **OR** dry mass and paramecium achieve this through accumulating organic matter **OR** nutrients from food [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ reproduction function in *Paramecium* is demonstrated by asexual division through binary fission, **OR** mitosis or micronucleus and amitotic division of macronucleus **OR** sexually through conjugation [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ excretion is the removal of metabolic waste products from the cell **OR** diffusion of CO:sub[2] out of the cell [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ nutrition is performed by ingesting smaller organisms and digesting them in vesicles **OR** through endocytosis [1 mark]
 [4 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
 *Paramecium* acquires **nutrients** by ingesting bacteria and **digesting them in vesicles**($\checkmark$). It exhibits **growth** when it **irreversibly increases in size** by accumulating organic matter from ingested **food** ($\checkmark$). *Paramecium* **responds** to different environmental stimuli. For example, it **changes** the **direction** of its movement once it **touches** a solid object ($\checkmark$). **Excretion** in *Paramecium* occurs when waste products such as **carbon dioxide diffuse out of the cell** ($\checkmark$). *Paramecium* **reproduces** both asexually by **binary fission** ($\checkmark$).
