Eukaryotic and prokaryotic cells both have **a plasma membrane** ($\checkmark$) and **free ribosomes** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **two** of the following:

    *One difference and one similarity are needed for full marks*

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** consist of flattened membrane areas                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ rough endoplasmic reticulum has ribosomes bound to it **WHEREAS**  the Golgi apparatus doesn't [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ Golgi apparatus has shorter cisternae **THAN** rough endoplasmic reticulum  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ part of the rough endoplasmic reticulum is continuous with the nuclear membrane **WHEREAS** the Golgi apparatus is not  [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
The Golgi apparatus and rough endoplasmic reticulum (RER) are both made up of **flattened membrane areas** ($\checkmark$), however the RER has ribosomes bound to the outside of it, whereas the Golgi apparatus **does not** ($\checkmark$). Also, the Golgi apparatus has **shorter cisternae** ($\checkmark$).
