# BI1197

**Maximum mark: 8**

The micrograph below shows *Cyanobacteria*, which contain chlorophyll.

![BI1197-SL](images/BI1197-SL.png)

1. Using your knowledge and the micrograph, state two features that would indicate that this is a bacterium.     [2 marks]

2. Outline the structural differences between animal and plant cells.  [3 marks]

3. Explain how light energy is used in photosynthesis to carry out anabolic reactions. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **two** of the following

    $\ce{ -> }$ cell wall of peptidoglycan            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ naked DNA **OR** DNA not associated with histones **OR** proteins    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ nucleoid **OR** DNA free in cytoplasm                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ no membrane bound organelles                    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ 70s ribosomes                                    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ cell smaller than 10μm                            [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Two features that would indicate that this is a bacterium are it has **no membrane-bound organelles** ($\checkmark$) and **70s ribosomes** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ plant cells have a cell wall, **WHEREAS** animal cells do not    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ plant cells generally have a central vacuole **WHEREAS** animal cells do not        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ plant cells have chloroplasts **WHEREAS** animal cells do not    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ animal cells may have lysosomes **WHEREAS** plant cells do not    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ animal cells may have cilia **OR** flagella **WHEREAS** plant cells do not    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ animal cells have centrioles **WHEREAS** plant cells do not        [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Plant cells have a **cell wall** ($\checkmark$), **central vacuole** ($\checkmark$) and **chloroplasts**, whereas **animal** cells **do not** ($\checkmark$) .

3. any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ light energy is absorbed by chlorophyll            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ light energy is transferred to electrons **OR** is used to excite electrons    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ light energy splitting water molecules **OR** photolysis releases electrons for the electron transport chain **OR** hydrogen ions for use in ATP production    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ light energy is used to synthesise ATP from ADP and inorganic phosphate **OR** NADPH from electrons and one proton            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ NADPH **OR** ATP is used to make larger organic compounds **OR** glucose **OR** starch **OR** other named organic compound from carbon dioxide        [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Light energy is **absorbed** by **chlorophyll** ($\checkmark$) and is used to **excite electrons** ($\checkmark$).  These electrons reduce **NADP:sup[+]** to form **NADPH**, which is an anabolic reaction ($\checkmark$).  Energy present in **NADPH** is then used to **produce glucose** from **carbon dioxide** ($\checkmark$).  This is also an anabolic reaction.
