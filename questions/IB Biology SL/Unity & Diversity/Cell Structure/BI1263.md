# BI1263

**Maximum mark: 16**

1. Outline how some eukaryotic cells have an atypical cell structure.            [3 marks]

2. Use named examples to explain how unicellular organisms carry out processes of life.    [5 marks]

3. Compare and contrast the structure of prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells.        [8 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **three** from the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ mature red blood cells do not have a nucleus    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ striated muscle cells have many nuclei    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ aseptate fungal hyphae **OR** fungal hyphae **OR** *Mucor* are multinucleate     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ phloem sieve tube elements lack nuclei **OR** do not have many organelles **OR** have continuous cytoplasm flowing through adjacent cells **OR** have sieve tube elements to provide metabolic support **OR** are interconnected cells.    [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Mature red blood cells **do not have a nucleus** ($\checkmark$), mature sieve tube elements **do not have a nucleus** ($\checkmark$) and striated muscle cells and fungal hyphae have **many nuclei** ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **five** of the following characteristics and a correct way the chosen unicellular organism carries out that process:

    $\ce{ -> }$ cilia allow movement in *Paramecium* **OR** flagella allow movement in *Chlamydomonas* **OR** any other suitable mode of movement relevant to a named unicellular organism    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ contractile vacuole helps to regulate water potential of the cell **OR** contractile vacuole has a role in homeostasis in *Paramecium* **OR** *Chlamydomonas* **OR** any other mechanism of homeostasis that is relevant to a named unicellular organism    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ enzyme-catalysed metabolic reactions such as respiration occur in the cytoplasm and mitochondria of *Paramecium* **OR** photosynthesis occurs in the chloroplast of *Chlamydomonas* **OR** correct example of metabolism for a named unicellular organism    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ oral groove allows nutrition in *Paramecium* **OR** nutrition is by photosynthesis in *Chlamydomonas* **OR** correct mode of nutrition for a named unicellular organism    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ *Paramecium* can reproduce asexually by binary fission **OR** *Paramecium* can reproduce by conjugation **OR** *Chlorella* reproduces asexually by mitosis    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ metabolic wastes such as CO$_2$ and ammonia diffuse out across the cell membrane in *Paramecium* **OR** metabolic wastes diffuse out across the cell membrane in *Chlamydomonas* **OR** correct mode of excretion for a named unicellular organism (do not accept removal of undigested food through the anal pore, as this is egestion)    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ growth of *Paramecium* occurs after cell division **OR** *Chlamydomonas* increases in size by synthesising new cell components between divisions **OR** correct mode of growth for a named unicellular organism    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ *Paramecium* sense their environment and respond by movement due to the movement of cilia **OR** *Chlamydomonas* has an eyespot to detect light and responds by swimming towards it using its flagella (phototaxis) **OR** correct example of sensitivity for a named unicellular organism    [1 mark]
    [5 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    *Paramecia* are unicellular organisms that can carry out all functions of life. *Paramecia* have **cilia that allow movement** ($\checkmark$). **Metabolic reactions** ($\checkmark$) happen in mitochondria and the cytoplasm. The contractile vacuole helps to **regulate the water concentration** ($\checkmark$) of *Paramecium* by releasing excess water. The oral groove takes in food, so it is responsible for **nutrition** ($\checkmark$). *Paramecium* reproduces asexually by **binary fission** ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **eight** of the following: \
*Answer **must** include at least **one** similarity and **one** difference to gain full marks*

    **Similarities:** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic **AND** eukaryotic cells have plasma membranes    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic **AND** eukaryotic cells have cytoplasm        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic **AND** eukaryotic cells have ribosomes        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic **AND** eukaryotic cells have genetic material **OR** DNA **OR** RNA    [1 mark]

    **Differences:** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic cells are not compartmentalised  **BUT** eukaryotic cells are compartmentalised    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic cells do not have membrane-bound organelles **BUT** eukaryotic cells do have membrane-bound organelles            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic cells have DNA in a loop **OR** plasmid **BUT** eukaryotes have linear DNA **OR** chromosomes         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic cells have 70S ribosomes **BUT** eukaryotic cells have 80S ribosomes    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic cells do not have a true nucleus **OR** have a nucleoid region **BUT** eukaryotic cells do have a true nucleus    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ all prokaryotic cells have a cell wall **BUT** not all eukaryotic cells have a cell wall    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic DNA is naked **OR** not associated with proteins **BUT** eukaryotic DNA is associated with histone proteins        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic cells are smaller **THAN** eukaryotic cells        [1 mark]
    [8 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells have **genetic material** ($\checkmark$). Prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells have **plasma membranes** ($\checkmark$). Prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells have **cytoplasm** ($\checkmark$). Prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells have **ribosomes** ($\checkmark$), but **prokaryotes have 70S ribosomes, whereas eukaryotic cells have 80S ribosomes** ($\checkmark$). Prokaryotic cells **do not have a true nucleus**, but eukaryotic cells do have a true nucleus ($\checkmark$). Prokaryotic cells do not have membrane-bound organelles but **eukaryotic cells do have membrane-bound organelles** such as mitochondria ($\checkmark$). Prokaryotic cells have **DNA in a loop** but eukaryotic cells have linear DNA ($\checkmark$). Not all eukaryotic cells have a cell wall but **all prokaryotic cells do have a cell wall** ($\checkmark$).
