# BI3206

The diagram below shows a replication fork with exposed bases on one template strand.

![BI3206](images/BI3206.png)

What is the sequence of the new DNA strand shown by the empty boxes, written from 5' to 3'?

A. $\hspace{1em}$ 5'–ATGCCT–3'

B. $\hspace{1em}$ 5'–TACGGA–3'

C. $\hspace{1em}$ 5'–UACGGA–3'

D. $\hspace{1em}$ 5'–AUGCCU–3'

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The template strand runs from 3' to 5' and has the sequence 3'–TACGGA–5'. Complementary base pairing in DNA places A opposite T and G opposite C. Because the new strand is antiparallel to the template, its sequence is 5'–ATGCCT–3'. Therefore, **Choice A** is correct.

**Choice B** is incorrect because it repeats the template sequence rather than providing its complementary sequence.

**Choice C** is incorrect because it is not complementary to the template and contains uracil, which occurs in RNA rather than DNA.

**Choice D** is incorrect because it is a complementary RNA sequence, as shown by the presence of uracil; it is not the new DNA strand produced during DNA replication.
