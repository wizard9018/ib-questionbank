# BI3204

A single, linear RNA polymer contains 300 nucleotide monomers.

How many condensation reactions were required to form the polymer, and how many water molecules were released?

A. $\hspace{1em}$ 300 condensation reactions; 300 water molecules

B. $\hspace{1em}$ 300 condensation reactions; 600 water molecules

C. $\hspace{1em}$ 150 condensation reactions; 150 water molecules

D. $\hspace{1em}$ 299 condensation reactions; 299 water molecules

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Joining *n* monomers into a single linear polymer requires *n−1* bonds to be formed. Therefore, joining 300 nucleotides requires 299 condensation reactions. In the simplified condensation model, each reaction releases one water molecule, so 299 water molecules are released. **Choice D** is correct because 299 links are required, resulting in 299 condensation reactions and 299 water molecules.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the number of links formed is one fewer than the number of monomers.

**Choice B** is incorrect because each condensation reaction releases one water molecule in this model, not two.

**Choice C** is incorrect because there is no reason to divide the number of nucleotides by two.
