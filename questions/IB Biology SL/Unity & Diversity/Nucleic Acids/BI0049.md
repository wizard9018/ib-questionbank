2. Compare and contrast the structures of DNA and RNA.  [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ two DNA strands run in opposite directions **OR** one strand is oriented 5' to 3', while the other is 3' to 5' [1 mark]

    [1 mark]

    :u[Sample answer]:

   Antiparallel is the arrangement of the **two strands** in the **double helix**, where they run in **opposite directions**, with **one strand oriented 5' to 3'** and the **other 3' to 5'**  ($\checkmark$).

2. any **four** of the following:

    *At least **one** statement for compare and **one** statement for contrast*

    **compare**

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** are nucleic acids **OR**  composed of nucleotides [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** contain adenine **OR** A **AND** cytosine **OR** C **AND** guanine **OR** G [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have a sugar-phosphate backbone [1 mark]

    **contrast**

    $\ce{ -> }$ DNA is double-stranded **WHEREAS** RNA is single-stranded [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ DNA contains thymine **OR** T  **WHEREAS** RNA has uracil **OR** U [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ DNA has deoxyribose **WHEREAS** RNA contains ribose [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ structure of DNA is more stable **THAN** RNA [1 mark]
[4 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    **Both** DNA and RNA are **nucleic acids** composed of **nucleotides**  ($\checkmark$) and they both have a **sugar-phosphate backbone** ($\checkmark$). They also contain the nitrogenous bases **adenine** (A), **cytosine** (C), and **guanine** (G) ($\checkmark$), however, **DNA** contains **thymine** (T), while **RNA** has **uracil** (U) ($\checkmark$). **DNA** is **double-stranded**, whereas **RNA** is **single-stranded**  ($\checkmark$).
