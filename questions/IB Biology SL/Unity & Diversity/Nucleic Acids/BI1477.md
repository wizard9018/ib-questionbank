## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

RNA is a single strand of nucleotides (Uracil, Guanine, Adenine and Cytosine), bonded together by a phosphodiester backbone. Each nucleotide consists of a phosphate group (represented by a circle), joined to a ribose sugar (represented by a pentagon) joined to a base (represented by a rectangle). Each nucleotide is joined to another by a covalent bond between the ribose sugar of one nucleotide and the phosphate of the next. **Choice B** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect as RNA is single stranded, not double stranded. **Choice C** is incorrect as it shows a hexose sugar rather than a pentose sugar. **Choice D** is incorrect as the pentose sugars and phosphate groups are in the wrong positions.
