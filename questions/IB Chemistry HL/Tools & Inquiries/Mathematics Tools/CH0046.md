# CH0046

Which of the following is most likely to be the diameter of a carbon nucleus, if the diameter of a particular carbon atom is measured as 140 pm?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$12 amu

- B.$\hspace{1em}$0.0012 mm

- C.$\hspace{1em}$0.001 nm

- D.$\hspace{1em}$0.005 pm

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The dimension of an atom nucleus is on the order of 10$^{5}$  smaller than the diameter of the atom. Converting the three lengths to picometers:

$0.0012 \textrm{\ mm}$ |$\xrightarrow{\times 10^9}$ | $1200000 \textrm{\ pm}$
:--|:-:|:--
0.001 nm |$\xrightarrow{\times 10^3}$ | 1 pm
0.005 pm|$\xrightarrow{\times 1}$ | 0.005 pm

The best answer is 0.005 pm.
