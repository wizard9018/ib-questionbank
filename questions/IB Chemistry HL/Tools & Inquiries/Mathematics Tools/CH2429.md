# CH2429

A solution is prepared by dissolving $18.37 \pm 0.02\textrm{ g}$ of solute in $100.00 \pm 0.10\textrm{ cm}^3$ of solution. What concentration should be reported in $\textrm{g}\textrm{ cm}^{-3}$?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}0.1837 \pm 0.0001$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}0.1837 \pm 0.0004$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}0.184 \pm 0.001$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}0.18 \pm 0.01$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The concentration is the mass divided by the volume:

$c = \dfrac{18.37\textrm{ g}}{100.00\textrm{ cm}^3} = 0.1837\textrm{ g cm}^{-3}$

When multiplying or dividing, percentage uncertainties are added. The percentage uncertainty in the mass is:

$\%\textrm{ unc. in mass} = \dfrac{0.02 \textrm{\ g}}{18.37 \textrm{\ g}} \times 100 \approx 0.109\%$

The percentage uncertainty in the volume is:

$\%\textrm{ unc. in volume} = \dfrac{0.10 \textrm{\ cm}^3}{100.00 \textrm{\ cm}^3} \times 100 = 0.100\%$

The total percentage uncertainty in the concentration is:

$\%\textrm{ unc. in  concentration} = 0.109 + 0.100 = 0.209\%$

Converting back to an absolute uncertainty:

$\Delta c = 0.209\% \times 0.1837 \textrm{\ g cm}^{-3}\approx 0.0004\textrm{ g cm}^{-3}$

The concentration is therefore reported as $0.1837 \pm 0.0004\textrm{ g cm}^{-3}$.
