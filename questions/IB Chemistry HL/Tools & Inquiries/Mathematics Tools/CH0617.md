# CH0617

A student recorded the volume of a gas as 0.04056 m$^3.$ How many significant figures does the recorded value have?     

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$3

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$4

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$5

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$6

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Leading zeroes are not significant while non-zero digits are significant (4,5,6), and zeroes between two non-zero digits are significant. Hence, 0.04056 has 4 significant figures.
