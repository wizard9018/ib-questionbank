# CH2430

Hydrogen peroxide is decomposed, and the volume of oxygen given off is recorded, as shown below.

![CH2430_volume_time](images/CH2430_volume_time.png)

What is the instant rate at 24 s?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}0.30\textrm{ cm}^3\textrm{ s}^{-1}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}0.18\textrm{ cm}^3\textrm{ s}^{-1}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}0.01\textrm{ cm}^3\textrm{ s}^{-1}$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}0.11\textrm{ cm}^3\textrm{ s}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The instantaneous rate at a particular time is the gradient of the tangent to the curve at that point. A tangent is a straight line that just touches the curve at the point of interest and has the same slope as the curve there.

At $t = 24\textrm{ s},$ the tangent can be drawn and extended so that two well$-$separated points can be read, as shown below.

![CH2430_volume_time_ms](images/CH2430_volume_time_ms.png)

Choosing convenient points on the tangent line, for example $(16,\;10.0)$ and $(34,\;12.0):$

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\textrm{instant rate} = \dfrac{\Delta V}{\Delta t} = \dfrac{(12.0 - 10.0)\textrm{\ cm}^3}{(34 - 16)\textrm{\ s}} = \dfrac{2.0\textrm{\ cm}^3}{18\textrm{\ s}} = 0.11\textrm{\ cm}^3\textrm{ s}^{-1}$
