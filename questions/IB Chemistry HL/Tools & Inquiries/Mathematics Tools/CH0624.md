# CH0624

The initial mass of a mortar containing a solid was recorded to be 109.34 g. The mortar was heated, and the final mass was recorded to be 81.92 g. What value should be recorded for the mass lost in grams?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$27.42 g

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$27.4 g

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$27 g

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$27.420 g

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

In addition and subtraction, the answer should be rounded to the least number of decimal places.

In this case, both masses recorded show two decimal places.

Therefore, the answer to $(109.34 - 81.92)$ should be choice letter A.
