# CH0625

The length of a thread was being measured using a 50 cm wooden ruler with a 1 mm increment. The reading for the beginning of the thread was 12.6 cm while the reading for the end of the thread was 45.3 cm. Which of the following is the largest possible length of the thread?    

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$32.7 cm

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$32.8 cm

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$33.7 cm

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$34.7 cm

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

For a ruler with a 1 mm increment, the uncertainty for each reading is $\pm 0.5$ mm.

Thus, the uncertainty of the measurement, which is made up of two readings, is double the uncertainty of a single reading *i.e.* $\pm 1 \textrm{\ mm} = \pm 0.1 \textrm{\ cm.}$

Hence, the length of the thread is 

$45.3 \textrm{\ cm} - 12.6 \textrm{\ cm} = 32.7 \pm 0.1 \textrm{\ cm}$

Thus, the maximum possible length is 32.8 cm.
