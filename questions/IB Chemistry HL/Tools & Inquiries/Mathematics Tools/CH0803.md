# CH0803

Which of the below gives the correct average titre with its uncertainty?

-   :::tableoptions{}
    | $\text{Initial volume } \pm 0.05\,\text{cm}^3$ | $0.00$ | $23.15$ | $0.00$ |
    |:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
    | $\text{Final volume } \pm 0.05\,\text{cm}^3$ | $23.15$ | $46.25$ | $23.20$ |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}23.15 \pm 0.05\,\text{cm}^3$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}23.2 \pm 0.1\,\text{cm}^3$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}23.15 \pm 0.1\,\text{cm}^3$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}23.15 \pm 0.10\,\text{cm}^3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The titres are 23.15, 23.10 and 23.20 respectively all given to two decimal places due to subtraction operation. The average of these three values is equation = 23.15 given to 4 significant figures.

Uncertainties are added for the values used giving a total uncertainty of 0.10 cm$^3$ given to two decimal places as addition operation is used and to align with the precision of the average value itself.
