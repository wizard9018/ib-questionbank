# CH0632

A student is measuring the temperature change of an endothermic reaction.

Calculate the temperature change and identify the corresponding error. 

![11.1-632](images/11.1-632.png)

Temperature decrease: _______ $^\circ$C ± ______$^\circ$C

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

8 $^\circ$C $\checkmark$ ± 2 $^\circ$C $\checkmark$

*All values to be left to 1 sf.*

$\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

Readings are taken from the bottom of the meniscus giving 24 $^\circ$C and 16 $^\circ$C.

The uncertainty of each reading is $\pm$ 1 $^\circ$C (half of the smallest increment on the thermometer). As both readings are used to give the temperature decrease, the uncertainties are added together.
