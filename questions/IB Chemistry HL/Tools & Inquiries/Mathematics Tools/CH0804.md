# CH0804

A student dissolves $2.00 \pm 0.02\textrm{ g}$ of $\ce{NaClO_3}$ in distilled water and makes the solution up to $2.0 \pm 0.5\textrm{ dm}^3.$

Which row correctly shows the concentration and its absolute uncertainty in $\textrm{g dm}^{-3}$?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  | | Concentration $\mathbf{/ \textbf{ g dm}^{-3}}$ | Absolute uncertainty $\mathbf{/ \textbf{ g dm}^{-3}}$ |
  | :-: | :-: | :-: |
  |A.$\hspace{1em}$| 1.00 | $\pm\textrm{ }0.25$ |
  |B.$\hspace{1em}$| 1.0$\textcolor{white}{0}$ | $\pm\textrm{ }0.3\textcolor{white}{0}$ |
  |C.$\hspace{1em}$| 1.00 | $\pm\textrm{ }0.26$ |
  |D.$\hspace{1em}$| 1.0$\textcolor{white}{0}$ | $\pm\textrm{ }0.25$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The concentration is found by dividing mass by volume:

$c = \dfrac{2.00\textrm{\ g}}{2.0\textrm{\ dm}^3} = 1.0\textrm{\ g dm}^{-3}$

The result is reported to two significant figures because the volume (2.0) has only two significant figures, and in division the answer takes the fewest significant figures of the input values.

For the uncertainty, the percentage uncertainties of each measurement are added:

$\%\textrm{ unc. mass} = \dfrac{0.02\textrm{\ g}}{2.00\textrm{\ g}} \times 100 = 1\%$

$\%\textrm{ unc. volume} = \dfrac{0.5\textrm{\ dm}^3}{2.0\textrm{\ dm}^3} \times 100 = 25\%$

$\%\textrm{ unc. concentration} = 1\% + 25\% = 26\%$

Converting back to an absolute uncertainty:

$\Delta c = \dfrac{26}{100} \times 1.0\textrm{\ g dm}^{-3} = 0.26\textrm{\ g dm}^{-3}$

The uncertainty is rounded to one significant figure to match the precision of the concentration: $d = 1.0 \pm 0.3\textrm{\ g dm}^{-3}.$
