# CH2433

Which temperature measurement was obtained with the most precise thermometer?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}98 \pm 2\textrm{\ }{}^\circ\textrm{C}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}10.12 \pm 0.05\textrm{\ }{}^\circ\textrm{C}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}20.20 \pm 0.10\textrm{\ }{}^\circ\textrm{C}$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}8 \pm 1\textrm{\ }{}^\circ\textrm{C}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The precision of an instrument is reflected in the size of its absolute uncertainty. A smaller absolute uncertainty means the instrument can distinguish between values that are closer together, so it is more precise.

Comparing the four uncertainties, the smallest absolute uncertainty is $\pm 0.05\textrm{\ }{}^\circ\textrm{C},$ so $10.12 \pm 0.05\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}$ was measured with the most precise thermometer.

Note that precision depends only on the absolute uncertainty of the instrument, not on the magnitude of the reading. A thermometer reading $98\textrm{\ }{}^\circ\textrm{C}$ with $\pm 2\textrm{\ }{}^\circ\textrm{C}$ uncertainty has a lower percentage uncertainty, but it is less precise because its absolute uncertainty is larger.
