# CH0361

**Maximum mark: 6**

The relationship between pressure and temperature of a known volume and amount of gas can be described using Gay$-$Lussac's law which states that pressure is directly proportional to temperature.

$P \propto T$

Where $P$ is pressure in Pa, and $T$ is temperature in K.

![11.2-CH0361](images/CH0361.png)

1.  On the axes above, sketch the graph of $P \propto T$. [2 marks]

2.  State two features that would need to be added to your sketched graph to turn it into a drawn graph. [2 marks]

3.  The proportionality can be shown in an equation by incorporating a constant, $k.$ The equation becomes $P = kT$, and follows the format of the equation of a straight line.

    1.  State the feature of a straight line that $k$ represents in this context. [1 mark]

    2.  Deduce the $y-$intercept of the straight line. [1 mark]
