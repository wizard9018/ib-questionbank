# CH1087

A student processed the data collected for an unknown liquid. The graph is shown below.

![CH1087_Image1](images/CH1087_Image1.png)

What is the density, in $\text{g cm}^{-3},$ of the unknown liquid?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}1.26$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}0.0172$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}58.1$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}0.791$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Density is the ratio between mass and volume of a specific substance. It can be obtained from this graph's gradient ($m$).

$m = \dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \dfrac{\Delta mass}{\Delta volume}$

The gradient is the change in mass per unit of change in volume.

From the linear equation given in the graph, we get $y = 1.2644 x - 0.0172$. The gradient is $1.2644$. the units for this gradient come from the units of the $y$ and $x$ variables, so the gradient is in $\text{g cm}^{-3}.$

Considering the masses and volumes informed, the density is given with three significant figures. So rather than $1.2644\,\text{g cm}^{-3},$ we express it as $1.26\,\text{g cm}^{-3}.$

This question is categorized as medium difficulty; students must interpret the meaning of the gradient in this processed data.
