# CH0623

A piece of sodium metal has a mass of 13.02 g and a volume of 13.4 cm$^3.$ What is the most precise density that can be reported?        

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$1 g cm$^{-3}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$0.97 g cm$^{-3}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$0.972 g cm$^{-3}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$0.9716 g cm$^{-3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

$\textrm{density} = \dfrac{\textrm{mass}}{\textrm{volume}}$

The volume has a smaller number of significant figures of 3.

Since the final answer should follow the smaller significant figure for multiplication and division operations, the most precise density that can be reported is 0.972 g cm$^{-3}.$
