# CH0359

Which statement best describes the relationship between the rate and $\ce{H+}$ concentration, in a graph with the equation: rate $= k[\ce{H+}]^2$?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Rate vs $\ce{[H+]}$ graph shows a straight line passing through the origin

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Rate and $k$ are directly proportional

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The relationship between rate and $\ce{[H+]}$ could be linear

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$There is positive correlation between rate and $\ce{[H+]}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Rate is directly proportional to and would give a linear relationship with $\ce{[H+]}$ not $\ce{[H+]^2}$.

When $rate = k\ce{[H+]}^2$, the rate is directly proportional to the **square** of the concentration; it increases with increasing $\ce{[H+]}$, and so there is a positive correlation between the two.
