# CH2437

Molten lead(II) bromide was electrolysed and the mass of lead produced was recorded. Which mass corresponds to the greatest number of electrons transferred?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}531\textrm{ mg}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}7.44 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{ g}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}3.02 \times 10^{-4}\textrm{ kg}$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}6.98 \times 10^{3}\ \mu\textrm{g}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The reduction half$-$equation is

$\ce{Pb^2+}\textrm{(l)} + 2\,\ce{e^-} \longrightarrow \ce{Pb}\textrm{(l)}$

The greater the mass of lead produced, the more moles of lead are deposited, and therefore the more electrons are transferred. Convert each option to grams to identify the largest mass of lead.

$531\textrm{ mg} \times \dfrac{1 \textrm{\ g}}{1000 \textrm{\ mg}}= 0.531\textrm{ g}$

$7.44 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{ g} = 0.00744\textrm{ g}$

$3.02 \times 10^{-4}\textrm{ kg} \times \dfrac{1000 \textrm{\ g}}{1 \textrm{\ kg}} = 0.302\textrm{ g}$

$6.98 \times 10^{3}\ \mu\textrm{g} \times \dfrac{1 \textrm{\ g}}{1 \times 10^6\ \mu\textrm{g}} = 0.00698\textrm{ g}$

0.531 g is the largest mass, so it corresponds to the greatest number of electrons transferred.
