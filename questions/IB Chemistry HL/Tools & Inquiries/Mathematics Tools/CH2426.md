# CH2426

A sample of $50.0\textrm{\ cm}^3$ of oil is weighed. The mass of the empty graduated cylinder is $35.95\textrm{\ g},$ and the mass of the graduated cylinder with the oil is $78.46\textrm{\ g}.$ What density should be reported for this oil in $\textrm{g cm}^{-3}$?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$0.8502

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$0.850

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$0.85

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$0.9

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The mass of the oil is found by difference:

$m = 78.46\textrm{\ g} - 35.95\textrm{\ g} = 42.51\textrm{\ g}$

This result has four significant figures. The volume is $50.0\textrm{\ cm}^3,$ which has three significant figures. The density is:

$d = \dfrac{42.51\textrm{\ g}}{50.0\textrm{\ cm}^3} = 0.8502\textrm{\ g cm}^{-3}$

When multiplying or dividing, the final answer must be rounded to match the quantity with the fewest significant figures. Since the volume has three significant figures, the density is reported as $0.850\textrm{\ g cm}^{-3}.$

The trailing zero after the 5 is significant and must be included to show that the result is known to three figures, not two.
