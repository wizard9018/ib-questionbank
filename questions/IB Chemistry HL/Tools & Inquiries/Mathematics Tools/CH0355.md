# CH0355

In a first order reaction, the concentration $[A]$ of a given reactant can be determined at time $t$, according to the equation below, where $[A]_o$ is the initial concentration of $A$ and $k$ is the rate constant.

$\ln[A] = - kt + \ln[A]_o$

![11.2CH0355](images/CH0355.png)

For the graph above of $\ln[A]$ vs $t$, which feature is equal to $\ln[A]_o$?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$Independent variable

- B. $\hspace{1em}$Dependent variable

- C. $\hspace{1em}y$-intercept

- D. $\hspace{1em}$Gradient

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The equation of a straight line is $y = mx + c,$ where $m$ is the slope and $c$ is the $y$-intercept.

In this case, $\ln[A]_o$ is $c$.
