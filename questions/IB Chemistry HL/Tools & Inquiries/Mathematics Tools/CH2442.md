# CH2442

Copper foil is placed in a ceramic boat inside a glass tube and heated in a stream of air. The ceramic boat and its contents are weighed before and after heating. The following measurements are recorded.

| | Mass / g |
|---|---|
| ceramic boat | $15.01 \pm 0.01$ |
| ceramic boat and copper before heating | $16.34 \pm 0.01$ |
| ceramic boat and copper after heating | $16.66 \pm 0.01$ |

What is the correct processed data for the mass of oxygen gained?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

|$\hspace{0.95em}$||$\hspace{0.1em}$| Mass of oxygen / g | Absolute uncertainty / g | Percentage uncertainty |
:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:
|| A. || 0.32 | 0.01 | 3% |
|| B. || 0.32 | 0.02 | 6% |
|| C. || 1.65 | 0.01 | 0.6% |
|| D. || 1.65 | 0.02 | 1% |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
When copper is heated in air, it reacts with oxygen to form copper(II) oxide:

$\ce{2Cu(s) + O2(g) -> 2CuO(s)}$

The mass of oxygen gained is the difference between the mass after heating and the mass before heating:

$m(\ce{O}) = m(\textrm{boat after heating}) - m(\textrm{boat before heating})$

$m(\ce{O}) = 16.66 \textrm{\ g} - 16.34\textrm{\ g} = 0.32\textrm{ g}$

When subtracting two measured values, the absolute uncertainties are added:

$\Delta m(\ce{O}) = 0.01 \textrm{\ g}+  0.01\textrm{\ g} = 0.02\textrm{ g}$

The percentage uncertainty is then:

$\%\textrm{ unc.} = \dfrac{0.02 \textrm{\ g}}{0.32\textrm{\ g}} \times 100 = 6.25\% \approx 6\%$

The mass of oxygen gained is $0.32 \pm 0.02\textrm{ g}$ with a percentage uncertainty of 6%.
