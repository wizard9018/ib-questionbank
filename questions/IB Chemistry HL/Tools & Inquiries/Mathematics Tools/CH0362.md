# CH0362

**Maximum mark: 6**

The relationship between the concentration of a reactant, $A$, and the time, for a second-order reaction can be expressed using the equation below:

$\dfrac{1}{[A]}=kt+\dfrac{1}{[A]_o}$

Where $[A]$ is the concentration of reactant $A, \, [A]_o$ is the initial concentration of reactant $A, \, t$ is the time in seconds, and $k$ is a constant.

![11.2-CH0362](images/CH0362.png)

1. Calculate the gradient of the line and state the correct units. [2 marks]

2. Use the graph to determine $[A]_o$ (also known as the initial concentration of $A$). [2 marks]

3. Determine the time at which the concentration of $A$ is equal to an eighth of its initial concentration. [2 marks]

 *(If you were unable to complete parts (a), and (b), you may use $k = 0.02$, and $A_o = 42)$*.
