# CH0626

A student recorded the mass of solid NaCl as 1200 g. Which of the following is correct about the number of significant figures recorded?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The mass was recorded to 2 significant figures

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The mass was recorded to 4 significant figures

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The mass could have been recorded to either 2, 3, or 4 significant figures

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$None of the above

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Depending on the raw data, the mass could have been recorded to either 2, 3, or 4 significant figures.

1201 g would have been recorded as 1200 g when recorded to 2 or 3 significant figures while 1200.4 g would also have been recorded as 1200 g when recorded to 4 significant figures.

Since we do not have information about the raw data, the mass could have been recorded to 2, 3, or 4 significant figures.
