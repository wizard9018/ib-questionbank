# CH0628

**Maximum mark: 3**

A student is determining the rate of the reaction between $\ce{MgCO3}$ and HCl. List 3 sources of random errors.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Imprecise or unreliable measuring equipment $\checkmark$, human error $\checkmark$, varying environmental conditions $\checkmark$ and other acceptable answers.

$\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

Random error refers to the random fluctuations of the measured data above and/or below the true value.
