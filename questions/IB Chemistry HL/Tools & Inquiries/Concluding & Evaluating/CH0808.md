# CH0808

25.00 cm$^3$ of 0.100 mol dm$^{-3}$ $\ce{HNO3}$ was titrated against 0.200 mol dm$^{-3}$ $\ce{NaOH}$. The volumes of NaOH delivered for the three trials 14.00 cm$^3$, 14.05 cm$^3$ and 14.00 cm$^3$.

Which statement below best describes these results?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The results are not accurate due to random error.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The results are not precise due to random error.
-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The results are not accurate due to systematic error.
-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The results are not precise due to systematic error.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
$\ce{HNO3}$ and NaOH react in a $1:1$ ratio, therefore the expected volume of NaOH required is $12.50 \textrm{\ cm}^3$ (due to it being double the concentration of the $\ce{HNO3}).$

The values obtained are close to each other showing high precision but are not close to the true value $(12.50 \textrm{\ cm}^3)$ and so are not accurate. This is indicative of a systematic error as they are shifted from the true value consistently each time.
