# CH1085

**Maximum mark: 2**

A student recorded the following data for three substances.

| Substance | Dissolves in water | Melting point $\mathbf{(^\circ\textbf{C})}$ | Electrical conductivity in solid state | Electrical conductivity in molten form |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |:-: |
| X | Yes | $186$ | Low | Low |
| Y | No| $801$ | Low | High |
| Z | No | $907$ | High | High |

1.  Which substance is most likely formed through the electrostatic attraction between positive and negative ions? [1 mark]

2.  Which substance is most likely formed through the electrostatic attraction between positive ions and delocalized electrons? [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substance **Y** $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    Ionic compounds form through the electrostatic attraction between positive and negative ions. An ionic compound may or may not dissolve in water, but it will only conduct electricity in a molten state and not in a solid state as its ions are not free to move. The data of substance **Y** most closely aligns with this; therefore, we can infer that substance **Y** is most likely to be an ionic compound i.e. the substance formed through the electrostatic attraction of positive and negative ions.

2.  Substance **Z** $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    Metals are formed through the electrostatic attraction between positive ions and delocalized electrons. Substance **Z** does not dissolve in water, has a comparatively higher melting point than the others, and conducts electricity in solid and molten states. Based on this, we can infer that substance **Z** is a metal and hence the substance formed through the electrostatic attraction of positive ions and delocalized electrons.
