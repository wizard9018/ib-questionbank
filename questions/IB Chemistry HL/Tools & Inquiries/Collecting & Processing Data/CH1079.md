# CH1079

Based on the below calibration curve for standard solutions of $\ce{KMnO4}$, what is the number of moles of oxygen in a $25\,\text{cm}^3$ solution of $\ce{KMnO4}$ of an unknown concentration, having an absorbance of 0.35?

![CH1079_Image1](images/CH1079_Image1.png)

-   A. $\hspace{1em}0.35$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}0.035$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}0.33$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}0.033$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

-   Step 1: Use the graph to determine the concentration of the solution.

    When absorbance is $0.35$, the concentration of the solution $= 0.33 (0.34)$

-   Step 2: Calculate the moles in $\ce{KMnO4}$; concentration of the solution $= \dfrac{moles}{volume}$

    $Moles = concentration \times volume\ (in\ \text{dm}^3)$

    $Moles = (0.33) (0.025) = 8.25\times 10^{-3}$

-   Step 3: Equate ratios

    1 mole of $\ce{KMnO4}$ : 4 moles of O

    So, moles of oxygen $= 4 (8.25\times 10^{-3}) = 0.033$
