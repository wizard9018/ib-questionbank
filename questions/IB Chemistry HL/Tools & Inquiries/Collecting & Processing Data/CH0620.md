# CH0620

A thermometer was used to record the temperature of pure boiling water at sea level three times and the values were: 85.6 $^\circ$C, 85.7 $^\circ$C, 85.5 $^\circ$C. What can be said about the accuracy and precision of these values?            

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The values are both accurate and precise

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The values are accurate, but not precise

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The values are precise, but not accurate

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The values are neither accurate nor precise

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The values are precise because they are close to each other, but inaccurate as they are far from the temperature of boiling water (100 $^\circ$C).
