# CH1078

Which of the following aspirin samples is most likely to have the highest impurity, considering the theoretical melting point of aspirin is $136\,{}^\circ\text{C}$?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Sample 1: $132\,{}^\circ\text{C}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Sample 2: $130\,{}^\circ\text{C}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Sample 3: $138\,{}^\circ\text{C}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Sample 4: $135\,{}^\circ\text{C}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The aspirin sample with the melting point farthest from the theoretical melting point of $130\,{}^\circ\text{C}$ will likely contain the most impurities.

Lower purity generally corresponds to a melting point further to the theoretical value due to the presence of impurities that could lower the melting point.
