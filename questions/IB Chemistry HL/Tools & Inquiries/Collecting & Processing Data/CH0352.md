# CH0352

Which statement best describes the relationship between $x$ and $y$ on the graph below?

![CH0352](images/CH0352.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}x$ and $y$ are directly proportional

- B. $\hspace{1em}$There is a positive correlation between $x$ and $y$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$There is a negative correlation between $x$ and $y$

- D. $\hspace{1em}x$ is dependent on $y$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$y$ increases with $x$, and so, there is positive correlation between them with $y$ being dependent on $x.$

It is not directly proportional as it is not a straight line that starts at the origin.
