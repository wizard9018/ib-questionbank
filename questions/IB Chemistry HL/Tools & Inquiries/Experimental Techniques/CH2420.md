# CH2420

A lab technician prepares a set of standard solutions for a calibration curve. A stock solution of $4.00\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ is diluted in a $50.0\textrm{\ cm}^3$ volumetric flask by adding aliquots of stock solution with a pipette and filling to the mark with distilled water.

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

| Concentration / $\textbf{mol dm}^\mathbf{-3}$ | Volume of stock solution / $\textbf{cm}^\mathbf{3}$ |
|:-:|:-:|
| 0.160 | 2.00 |
| 0.240 | 3.00 |
| 0.400 | $X$ |
| 0.560 | $Y$ |
| 0.720 | $Z$ |

Which combination of $X,$ $Y$ and $Z$ is correct?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

| $\hspace{1.em}$||| $X$ / $\textbf{cm}^\mathbf{3}$| $Y$ / $\textbf{cm}^\mathbf{3}$| $Z$ / $\textbf{cm}^\mathbf{3}$ |
|:-: |:-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
|| A. | | 5.00 | 8.00 | 10.0 |
|| B. | | 5.00 | 7.00 | 9.00 |
|| C. | | 4.00 | 5.00 | 6.00 |
|| D. | | 4.00 | 7.00 | 9.00 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The amount of solute is conserved during a dilution. The stock solution provides the solute, and the added distilled water simply increases the total volume without changing the amount of solute present. This relationship is expressed as:

$C_i V_i = C_f V_f$

where $C_i$ and $V_i$ are the concentration and volume of the stock solution, and $C_f$ and $V_f$ are the final concentration and volume after dilution. Rearranging for the volume of stock solution needed:

$V_i = \dfrac{C_f \times V_f}{C_i}$

For the $0.400\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ standard:

$V_i = \dfrac{0.400\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \times 50.0\textrm{\ cm}^3}{4.00\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}} = 5.00\textrm{\ cm}^3$

Repeating for the remaining two concentrations:

$V_i = \dfrac{0.560\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \times 50.0\textrm{\ cm}^3}{4.00\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}} = 7.00\textrm{\ cm}^3$

$V_i = \dfrac{0.720\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \times 50.0\textrm{\ cm}^3}{4.00\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}} = 9.00\textrm{\ cm}^3$

So $X = 5.00 \textrm{\ cm}^3,$ $Y = 7.00 \textrm{\ cm}^3,$ $Z = 9.00 \textrm{\ cm}^3.$
