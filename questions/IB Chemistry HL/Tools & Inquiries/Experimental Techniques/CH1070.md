# CH1070

Based on the chromatogram of this particular plant pigment, which inference is considered inaccurate?

![CH1070_Image1](images/CH1070_Image1.png)

$\small{\textrm{[https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:T.baccata-paper-chromatography.jpg]}}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The plant pigment is a mixture

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The plant pigment is made of at least four compounds

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Chl a has a greater retention factor than Chl b

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Xan has a greater retention factor than Car

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Paper chromatography is a technique used to separate components in mixtures, and since the chromatogram shows four labels, it indicates that this mixture is a combination of at least four different substances. Thus, options A and B are inferences that can be drawn from this chromatogram.

The retention factor is the ratio of the distance traveled by the substance to the distance traveled by the solvent front, and because Chl a travels further than Chl b, Chl a has a greater retention factor than Chl b, and Car has a greater retention factor than Xan. This indicates that option D is not an inference that can be made from this chromatogram.
