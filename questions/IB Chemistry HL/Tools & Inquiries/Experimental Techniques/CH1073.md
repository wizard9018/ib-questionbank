# CH1073

Below are four glassware with a maximum capacity of $25\,\text{cm}^3.$ Which of these is the most suitable for measuring $25.0\,\text{cm}^3$ of $1\,\text{M}$ acetic acid solution?

![CH1073_Image1](images/CH1073_Image1.png)

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Glassware W

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Glassware X

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Glassware Y

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Glassware Z

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Glassware **W** is a beaker with a $25\,\text{mL}$ capacity. The markings on the exterior of the beaker could be more precise for liquid volume measurement.

Glassware **X**, a $25\,\text{mL}$ gas syringe, is not intended for liquid volume measurement.

Glassware **Y** is a $25\,\text{mL}$ pipette designed for liquid volume measurement. It is highly precise and straightforward to obtain the desired liquid volume.

Glassware **Z** is a $25\,\text{mL}$ glass (test) tube that holds liquids.
