# CH2414

A food colouring was analysed using thin$-$layer chromatography to check for the presence of a banned substance. The result is shown below.

![CH2414](images/CH2414.png)

Which statement can be deduced from the chromatogram?

-   I.$\hspace{1em}$The banned substance is present in the food colouring sample, and it is more polar than the other components of the sample.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The banned substance has $R_\textrm{f} = 0.85$.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$The food colouring sample is a mixture of at least three substances.

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$I, II and III

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$II only

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$I and III only

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$I only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
In thin$-$layer chromatography, components of a mixture are separated based on their relative affinity for the stationary phase (silica gel) and the mobile phase (solvent). Polar substances are adsorbed more strongly onto the polar stationary phase and travel a shorter distance, giving a smaller $R_\textrm{f}$ value.

The chromatogram shows three spots in the food colouring lane. The lowest spot matches the position of the reference spot in the banned substance lane, confirming that the banned substance is present in the food colouring. Because this spot has the smallest $R_\textrm{f}$ of the three, the banned substance is the most polar component. Statement I is correct.

![CH2414ms](images/CH2414ms.png)

The retention factor is defined as

$R_\textrm{f} = \dfrac{\textrm{distance travelled by the spot}}{\textrm{distance travelled by the solvent front}}$

From the chromatogram, the banned substance spot has travelled approximately $0.75\textrm{\ cm}$ while the solvent front has travelled approximately $5.0\textrm{\ cm}$, giving $R_\textrm{f} = \dfrac{0.75}{5.0} = 0.15.$ The value $0.85$ is closer to the $R_\textrm{f}$ of the top spot in the food colouring lane. Statement II is incorrect.

Three distinct spots are visible in the food colouring lane. If separation were incomplete, two or more components could overlap in the same spot, so the number of spots represents a minimum. The food colouring contains at least three substances. Statement III is correct.
