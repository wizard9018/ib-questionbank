# CH0629

**Maximum mark: 3**

In conducting a titration, a 25 cm$^3$ volumetric pipette and 50 cm$^3$ graduated burette are used.  Explain important technique required when using these pieces of volumetric glassware to reduce sources of error.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Any three of:

-   make sure glassware is clean before use, rinse with distilled water $\checkmark$

-    rinse with solution before filling $\checkmark$

-   remove air bubbles from tip $\checkmark$

-   read volume at meniscus at eye level $\checkmark$

-   place tip against flask to transfer last drop of solution $\checkmark$

-   *other acceptable answers*

$\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\

Rinsing the burette/pipette with water first removes soluble impurities.\

Rinsing again with the solution reduces the chance that any dilution of the solution will occur when subsequently added.
