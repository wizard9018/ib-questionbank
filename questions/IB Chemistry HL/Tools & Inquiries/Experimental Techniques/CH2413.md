# CH2413

An impure copper(II) sulfate sample is recrystallized. Which statements are correct?

-   I.$\hspace{1em}$A hot, unsaturated solution of $\ce{CuSO_4}\textrm{(aq)}$ is prepared.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$Two filtrations are performed, and the residue collected on the filter paper is discarded as impurities each time.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$Crystals are washed with a small amount of ice$-$cold water.

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$I, II and III

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$I and III only

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$II and III only

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Recrystallization purifies a solid by exploiting the temperature dependence of solubility. The procedure has three key stages: dissolving, filtering, and washing.

The impure solid is dissolved in the minimum volume of hot solvent to produce a hot saturated solution. A saturated solution ensures that the maximum amount of solute is dissolved at high temperature; when the solution cools, the solubility drops and pure crystals precipitate. Statement I is incorrect.

Two filtrations are performed, but they serve different purposes. The first filtration of the hot solution removes insoluble impurities as the residue, while the filtrate (the hot saturated solution) is retained. After cooling, pure crystals form and a second filtration collects these crystals as the residue. This time the residue is the purified product, not waste. Soluble impurities remain dissolved in the filtrate and are discarded with it. Statement II is incorrect.

The crystals are then washed with a small amount of ice$-$cold water to rinse away any adhering soluble impurities. The water must be ice$-$cold to minimise the amount of product that redissolves. Statement III is correct.
