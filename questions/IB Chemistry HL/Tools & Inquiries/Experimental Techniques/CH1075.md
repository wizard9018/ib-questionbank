# CH1075

A student obtained titre readings of $14.25$, $13.69$, $13.65$, $13.75$, and $13.65\,\text{cm}^3$ in a series of five titrations. What is the most precise titre value that can be used in any calculation?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}13.65\,\text{cm}^3$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}13.69\,\text{cm}^3$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}13.685\,\text{cm}^3$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}13.650\,\text{cm}^3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The average of $13.69$, $13.65$, $13.75$, and $13.65$ is $13.685$, which must be rounded to $13.69$ to match the significant figures of the titer readings. The titre $14.25$ is an outlier and will not be included when calculating the average.
