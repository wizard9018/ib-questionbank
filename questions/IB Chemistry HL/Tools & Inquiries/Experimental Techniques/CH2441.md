# CH2441

Solid potassium reacts vigorously with water, producing a gas. Which hazard symbol does $\textbf{not}$ correspond to the gaseous product formed?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The reaction between potassium and water produces hydrogen gas and potassium hydroxide:

$2\,\ce{K}\textrm{(s)} + 2 \ce{\,H2O}\textrm{(l)} \longrightarrow 2 \,\ce{KOH}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{H2}\textrm{(g)}$

Hydrogen gas is highly flammable (it ignites in the presence of oxygen in air) and can be explosive when mixed with air in the right proportions. It is also classified as harmful through asphyxiation in enclosed spaces. However, hydrogen gas is not an oxidising agent. Hydrogen acts as a reducing agent. The oxidising hazard symbol applies to substances that provide oxygen or otherwise promote combustion of other materials, which hydrogen does not do.
