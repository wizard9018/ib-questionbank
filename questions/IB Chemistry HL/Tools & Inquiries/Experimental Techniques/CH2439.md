# CH2439

What is the correct reading of the burette shown below?

![CH2439](images/CH2439.png)

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}49.2 \pm 0.1\textrm{ cm}^{3}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}49.22 \pm 0.05\textrm{ cm}^{3}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}48.78 \pm 0.05\textrm{ cm}^{3}$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}48.8 \pm 0.1\textrm{ cm}^{3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
A burette is read at the bottom of the meniscus for an aqueous solution. The smallest division on this burette is $0.1\textrm{ cm}^{3},$ so the absolute uncertainty is half the smallest division:

$\textrm{absolute uncertainty} = \frac{1}{2} \times 0.1 \textrm{\ cm}^3 = 0.05\textrm{ cm}^{3}$

The bottom of the meniscus lies between the $48.7\textrm{ cm}^{3}$ and $48.8\textrm{ cm}^{3}$ minor marks, closer to $48.8\textrm{ cm}^{3}.$ Estimating the last digit gives a reading of $48.78\textrm{ cm}^{3}.$ Because the uncertainty is $\pm 0.05\textrm{ cm}^{3},$ the reading must be recorded to two decimal places so that the last digit matches the precision of the uncertainty.
