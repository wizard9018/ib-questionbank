# CH2423

Which statement is correct for the use of a separating funnel?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$It separates miscible liquids; the denser liquid is collected first from the bottom.

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$It separates immiscible liquids; the denser liquid is collected first from the bottom.

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$It separates miscible liquids; the less dense liquid is collected first from the bottom.

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$It separates immiscible liquids; the less dense liquid is collected first from the bottom.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
A separating funnel is used to separate immiscible liquids, that is, liquids that do not mix and instead form distinct layers. When left to settle, the denser liquid sinks to the bottom and the less dense liquid floats on top. Opening the stopcock allows the lower (denser) layer to drain into a collection vessel. Once the boundary between the two layers reaches the stopcock, it is closed and a fresh vessel is placed underneath to collect the upper layer.

Miscible liquids form a single homogeneous phase and cannot be separated by a separating funnel. Fractional distillation is the appropriate technique for separating miscible liquids with different boiling points.
