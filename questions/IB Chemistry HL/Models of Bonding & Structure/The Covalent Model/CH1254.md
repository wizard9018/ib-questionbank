# CH1254

Which molecule presents a $\pi$ bond and an $sp^3$ hybridization in its central atom?

- A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH_2O}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{O_3}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH_4}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{POCl_3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The Lewis structure for each molecule allows us to identify every single bond as $\sigma$ bond and double bonds as one $\sigma$ and one $\pi$ bond.

![CH1254](images/CH1254.png)
![Mock-set1-2025- hlp1a- 1254](<images/Mock-set1-2025- hlp1a- 1254.png>)

$\ce{CH_2O}$ is trigonal planar, and the carbon is $sp^2.$

$\ce{O_3}$ is V-shaped, and the oxygen in the middle is $sp^2.$

$\ce{CH_4}$ is tetrahedral, and the carbon is $sp^3.$

$\ce{POCl_3}$ is tetrahedral, and the P is $sp^3.$

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as the Lewis structures and molecular geometries must be analyzed to deduce the hybridization and the presence of $\sigma$ and $\pi$ bonds.
