# CH1185

Which is the correct combination for benzene?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
 | | Number of $\sigma$ bonds|Number of $\pi$ bonds | Hybridization of carbon |
 | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
 | A. | 6 | 3 | $sp$ |
 | B. | 6 | 3 | $sp^2$ |
 | C. | 12 | 6 | $sp^2$ |
 | D. | 12 | 3 | $sp^2$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The benzene ring structure is shown below.

![CH1185](images/CH1185.png)

There are six $\sigma$ bonds between carbons, six $\sigma$ bonds between carbon and hydrogen and three $\pi$ bonds between carbons.

![CH1185B](images/CH1185B.png)

Every carbon presents a trigonal planar geometry and shows three electron domains in an $sp^2$ hybridization.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as sigma, pi bonds, and carbon hybridization should be distinguished in the benzene structure.
