# CH0951

In paper chromatography, components of a mixture are separated according to which property?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Solubility

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Melting point

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Atomic radius

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Electronegativity

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
In paper chromatography, water adsorbed onto the cellulose
fibers acts as the stationary phase. Components of the
mixture partition between the stationary phase and the
mobile phase (solvent) based on their relative solubility
in each. Components that are more soluble in the mobile
phase travel further up the paper and have a higher
$R_f$ value, while those with greater affinity for the
stationary phase lag behind. The underlying reason for
these differences in solubility is the strength of the
intermolecular forces each component can form with each
phase.

This question is characterized as easy because the student
only needs to recall basic concepts of chromatography.
