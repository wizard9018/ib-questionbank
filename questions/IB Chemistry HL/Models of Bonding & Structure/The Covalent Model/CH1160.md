# CH1160

A black marker was tested through chromatography. The chromatography paper obtained is shown below.

![CH1160](images/CH1160.png)

Which statement is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The black marker tested is a mixture of only five colours.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Spot $\textbf{t}$ has the smallest retardation factor.

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Spot $\textbf{p}$ has a retardation factor of 0.17.

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Spot $\textbf{r}$ has a retardation factor of 0.50.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Paper chromatography is a technique used to separate coloured mixtures. This technique allows separation, and different spots can be interpreted as the minimum number of components of the mixture. However, if the observation of five spots takes place, the mixture could have 5 or more components. Some components might not have been separated effectively. As a result, we can only verify the minimum amount of components through it.

In addition, the retardation factor $(R_f)$ can be determined as shown below:

Retardation factor = $\dfrac{\text{distance travelled by the spot}}{\text{distance travelled by the solvent}}$

The retardation factor is greater as the spot is higher and closer to the solvent front. Spot $\textbf{p}$ has the smallest retardation factor and spot $\textbf{t}$ has the highest retardation factor here.

In addition, we can determine the retardation factor for the spots $\textbf{r}$ and $\textbf{t}$ as shown below:

$\begin{aligned}
{\textbf{r}} &= \dfrac {3.25 - 0.50}{6.00-0.50} = 0.50 \\\\
{\textbf{p}} &= \dfrac {1.00 - 0.50}{6.00-0.50} = 0.091
\end{aligned}$

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students are expected to interpret a paper chromatography, calculate the $R_f$ and interpret the result.
