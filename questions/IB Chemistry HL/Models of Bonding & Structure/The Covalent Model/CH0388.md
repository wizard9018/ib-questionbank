# CH0388

The Lewis structure of the nitrite ion is shown.

![CH0388](images/CH0388.png)

What is the hybridization of the N atom and the most likely value of the bond angle in the nitrite ion?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  | | Hybridization | Bond angle |

  | :-: | :-: | :-: |

  | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | $sp^2$ | 125$\degree$

  | B.$\hspace{1em}$ | $sp^2$ | 115$\degree$

  | C.$\hspace{1em}$ | $sp^3$ | 112$\degree$

  | D.$\hspace{1em}$ | $sp^3$ | 107$\degree$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

The N atom has three electron domains so is $sp^2$ hybridized. The $sp^2$ orbitals are occupied by two bonding pairs and one nonbonding pair of electrons. The repulsion between the nonbonding and bonding pairs is greater than that between the two bonding pairs, so the bond angle is less than the expected value of $120\degree$; $115^\circ$ is the most likely of the two options given.

This question is hard as the student must correctly identify the hybridization of an unfamiliar molecule, in which one $sp^2$ orbital is occupied by nonbonding electrons rather than a bonding pair, and apply VSEPR principles to deduce the likely bond angle.
