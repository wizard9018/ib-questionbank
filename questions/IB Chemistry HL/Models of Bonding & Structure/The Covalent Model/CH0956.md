## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
To answer this question, the student must start by assessing the paper chromatogram, and comparing it to the chemical structures.

Both dibenzofuran and pyrene are nonpolar, but the oxygen on dibenzofuran provides a location for minor dipole$-$dipole interactions. Since both compounds are nonpolar, the solvent will either be hexane or diethyl ether (since water and methanol are polar).

Hexane is incapable of dipole-dipole forces since there are no electronegative atoms on the molecule, and it is nonpolar. Therefore, diethyl ether is the correct choice.

This question is difficult because there are many concepts that must be understood to answer the question. Matching structure to intermolecular forces based on polarity and atom type must first be considered, followed by understanding how polarity affects component movement in TLC.
