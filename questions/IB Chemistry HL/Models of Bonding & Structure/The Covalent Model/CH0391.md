# CH0391

**Maximum mark: 8**

Hydrogen cyanide consists of a carbon atom bonded to one hydrogen atom and one nitrogen atom. 

1. Draw the Lewis structure of hydrogen cyanide. [2 marks]

2. The valence electron configuration of the carbon atom before hybridization is shown.

   ![14.2-392](images/14.2-392.png)

   Hybridization produces a new configuration of valence orbitals, each containing one unpaired electron.

   Draw a similar diagram illustrating the valence electron configuration of the carbon atom after hybridization, including labels for the valence orbitals. [3 marks]

3. Hydrogen cyanide contains both $\sigma$ and $\pi$ bonds.

   1. State the type of bond formed by the hybrid orbitals of the carbon atom. [1 mark]

   2. Explain how the $\pi$ bonding in hydrogen cyanide is produced by the overlap of valence orbitals. [2 marks]
