# CH0709

The structural formulas of three organic compounds, I$-$III, are shown.

![4-709](images/4-709.png)

In which of the following are the compounds listed in order of decreasing boiling point?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$II > I > III

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$II > III > I

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$III > II > I

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$III > I > II

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Molecules III contains a hydrogen atom attached to a highly electronegative atom (O) with lone pairs, so can form hydrogen bonds.

Molecule I contains a polar $\ce{C-Cl}$ bond, so the molecules can form dipole-dipole interactions.

Molecule II contains no polar bonds, so the molecules can only interact via weak London (dispersion) forces.

  

The strength of these interactions decreases in the order hydrogen bonds > dipole$-$dipole forces > London forces.

Weaker intermolecular interactions can be broken by a smaller thermal energy input, resulting in a lower boiling point. Therefore, the order of boiling points for the molecules matches the order of the intermolecular interaction strengths.

  

This question is moderately difficult, as the student must recall the relative strengths of intermolecular forces and identify the interactions displayed by several molecules based only on their structural formulas.
