# CH0348

**Maximum mark: 8**

The formate or methanoate ion, $\ce{HCO2-}$, is commonly found in living systems, and is part of the synthesis of many amino acids.

1.  Determine the number of valence electrons in the methanoate ion. [1 mark]

2.  Draw a Lewis structure for the methanoate ion. Include resonance structures if resonance is present in the structure [3 marks]

3.  State the molecular geometry AND the bond angles of the methanoate ion. [2 marks]

4.  Justify the existence of a dipole moment in the molecular structure of the methanoate ion. [2 marks]

---

1.  18 valence electrons. $\checkmark$

   

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    Valence electrons can be determined by the group number of each element. H( group 1), C(droup 14) and O( group 16). The ion has an overall negative charge, so one extra electron must be added.

    $1 + 4 + 2(6) + 1 = 18$
