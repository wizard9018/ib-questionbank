# CH2013

What is the hybridization of carbon in graphene?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}sp$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}sp^2$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}sp^3$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}sp^2$ and $sp^3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand the structure of graphene as a single sheet of graphite, shown below.

![CH2013](images/CH2013.png)

It is a two$\text{- -}$dimensional structure. Every carbon is bonded to three other carbons in a covalent bond and is part of a trigonal planar geometry. Delocalized $\pi\text{--}$bonds extend over the entire sheet making all bonds the same strength, all $\ce{C-C}$ bonds the same length, and all of the bond angles 120$^\circ.$ Each carbon has hybridization $sp^2$ because there are three electron domains.

This question is categorized as a medium as the student must recall the structure of graphene and its carbon hybridization.
