# CH1033

In the following paper chromatogram which component of the mixture is the most polar if the mobile phase was water?

![CH0951](images/CH0951.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

To answer this question, the student must recall that components of a mixture with a greater affinity for the mobile phase will travel furthest from the starting line.

Since the mobile phase in this question is water and water is polar, then those components of the mixture that travel the furthest are the most polar.

This question is characterized as easy because the student needs only to recall the basic concepts of chromatography.
