# CH0507

Molecules of liquid bromine trifluoride $\ce{(BrF3)}$ react with each other to form $\ce{BrF2+}$ and $\ce{BrF4-}$ ions. Which equation shows the molecular and electron domain geometries of the reactant and products?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The $\ce{Br}$ atom in $\ce{BrF3}$ has two lone pairs. The electron domain geometry of $\ce{BrF3}$ is trigonal bipyramidal, and the lone pairs occupy equatorial positions to minimize repulsion with each other and with the bonding electron pairs. Thus, the molecular geometry is T-shaped.

$\ce{BrF4-}$ has four bonding pairs and two nonbonding pairs of electrons, so the molecular geometry is square planar and the electron domain geometry is octahedral.

This question is medium-difficult, as the student must deduce both the number of non-bonding electrons on the Br atom and the geometry that will minimize repulsion between lone and bonding electron pairs.
