# CH0512

The structural formula of the sulfite ion ($\ce{SO3^{2-}}$) is shown.

![CH0512](images/CH0512.png)

The $\ce{S-O}$ bonds in the sulfite ion form a delocalised $\pi-$system.  What is the bond order of each $\ce{S-O}$ bond in the sulfite ion?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}2$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{3}{2}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{4}{3}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{2}{3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
There are four bonding pairs of electrons divided between three $\ce{S-O}$ bonds, so the bond order of each bond is $\dfrac{4}{3}$.

This question is of moderate difficulty: the student must determine the number of bonding electron pairs and perform the bond order calculation correctly, but is aided by a structural formula and guided to consider delocalization of the $\pi$ electrons.
