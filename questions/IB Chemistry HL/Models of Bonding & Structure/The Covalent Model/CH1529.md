# CH1529

Which statement is correct about $\ce{OCN-}?$ 

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$It has three resonance structures. 

-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The preferred Lewis structure is 

    $\mbox{}\\[-20pts]$

    ![CH1529image5v2](images/CH1529image5v2.png) 

-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$  The ion has two $\sigma\text{--}$bonds and two $\pi\text{--}$bonds.

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The student must be able to work out all of the possible Lewis structures of $\ce{OCN-}$ and use formal charge to distinguish the most stable one. The student must also recognize that single bonds are $\sigma\text{--}$bonds and that multiple bonds are $\sigma\text{--}$bonds and $\pi\text{--}$bonds.

This ion has 16 electrons (6 valence electrons from oxygen, 4 valence electrons from carbon, 5 valence electrons from nitrogen and an extra electron as it has a negative charge). Three different resonance structures can be proposed, as shown below.

![CH1529image2v2](images/CH1529image2v2.png)

Statement I is correct, there are three valid resonance structures.

The preferred Lewis structure has formal charges closest to zero, as the electron distribution in that case is best.  It will also place a negative change on the most electronegative atom (and a positive charge on the least electronegative atom). The formula below assigns formal charge (FC).

$\text{FC} = \text{valence e}^- - \dfrac{1}{2} \times\text{bonding e}^- - \text{nonbonding e}^-$

Calculations for each resonance structure are presented below.

|   | ![CH1529image3v2](images/CH1529image3v2.png) |   ![CH1529image4v2](images/CH1529image4v2.png) | ![CH1529image5v2](images/CH1529image5v2.png)

| :-: | :-: |:-: | :-: | 

|   O|$6 - \frac{1}{2}\times 2 - 6 = -1$| $6- \frac{1}{2}\times 4 - 4 = 0$|$6 - \frac{1}{2} \times 6 - 2 = 1$| 

|   C|$4 - \frac{1}{2} \times 8 - 0 = 0$|$4 - \frac{1}{2}\times 8 - 0 = 0$|$4 - \frac{1}{2} \times 8 - 0 = 0$| 

|   N|$5 - \frac{1}{2} \times 6 - 2 = 0$| $5 - \frac{1}{2} \times 4 - 4 = -1$|$5 - \frac{1}{2} \times 2 - 6 = -2$| 

The last resonance structure turns out to be the least stable as it has the largest formal charges, and statement II is incorrect. The preferred resonance structure is the middle resonance structure placing the $-1$ formal charge on the more electronegative oxygen atom.

Single bonds consist of one $\sigma\text{--}$bond; double bonds consist of a $\sigma\text{--}$ and a $\pi\text{--}$bond, and triple bonds consist of a $\sigma\text{--}$bond and two $\pi\text{--}$bonds. The $\ce{OCN-}$ ions contain two $\sigma\text{--}$ and two $\pi\text{--}$bonds in all three resonance structures. Statement III is correct.

This question is categorized as hard as the student must calculate the possible resonance structures of the ions, calculate the formal charge, and identify $\sigma\text{--}$ and $\pi\text{--}$bonds.
