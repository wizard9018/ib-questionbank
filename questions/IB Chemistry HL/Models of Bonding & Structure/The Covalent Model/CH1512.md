# CH1512

Which of the following molecules has a trigonal planar molecular geometry?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{PH3}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{BF3}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{SiH4}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H2S}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

For a trigonal planar geometry, a molecule should have three electron domains around the central atoms and all domains should be bonding domains.

![CH1512_v2](images/CH1512_v2.png)

All molecules except $\ce{BF3}$ have four electron domains which give them tetrahedral electron geometries. $\ce{BF3}$ has three electron domains all of which are bonded electrons. The repulsion is equal, giving rise to a trigonal molecular geometry.
