# CH0281

**Maximum mark: 6**

Dimethyl ether and ethanol are both organic liquids that are soluble in water. Dimethyl ether has limited solubility, whereas ethanol is soluble in water in all proportions.

![CH0281](images/CH0281.png)

1. Use the diagrams above to explain the differences in solubility of dimethyl ether and ethanol in water. [4 marks]

2. Predict and explain the relative solubility of chloroethane in water, compared to dimethyl ether and ethanol. [2 marks]

2. Chloroethane will be less soluble than ethanol and dimethyl ether$\.\checkmark$

 Not able to form hydrogen bonds with water molecules (dipole$-$dipole interactions are weaker than hydrogen bonds)$\.\checkmark$

 $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

 Chloroethane $\ce{CH3CH2Cl}$ cannot form hydrogen bonds with water molecules. Therefore, it is less soluble than the other compounds.
