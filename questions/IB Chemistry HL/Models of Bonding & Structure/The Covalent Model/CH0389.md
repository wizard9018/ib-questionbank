# CH0389

**Maximum mark: 8**

The structural formula of methane is shown.

![14.2-389](images/14.2-389.png)

1. The valence orbitals of the carbon atom must be hybridized to produce the bond angle $\theta$.

   1. State the hybridization of the carbon atom in methane. [1 mark]

   2. State the value of $\theta$ in degrees. [1 mark]

   3. The angle between the p orbitals of the carbon atom **before** hybridization is **not** equal to $\theta$.

   Suggest why the **molecular** geometry of methane requires the p orbitals of the carbon atom to be hybridized. [2 marks]

2. Methane contains four $\ce{C-H}$ bonds.

   1. State how the electron density is distributed within each $\ce{C-H}$ bond. [1 mark]

   2. State how the orbitals of the carbon and hydrogen atoms overlap to produce a $\ce{C-H}$ bond. [1 mark]

   3. Explain how hybridization affects the pairing and relative energies of the carbon valence electrons to enable the formation of four **equivalent** $\ce{C-H}$ bonds. [2 marks]
