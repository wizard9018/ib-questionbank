# CH0510

Which terms best describe the $\textbf{electron domain}$ geometry of $\ce{BrCl5}$?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Square pyramidal

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Trigonal bipyramidal

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Pentagonal planar

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Octahedral

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The Br atom in $\ce{BrCl5}$ has one nonbonding and five bonding electron pairs. The electron domain geometry for six electron pairs is octahedral. The bonding pairs form a square pyramidal geometry, but this is the molecular rather than the electron domain geometry.

This question is categorized as very hard, as the student must deduce the geometry without the aid of a Lewis structure and also differentiate between molecular and electron domain geometries.
