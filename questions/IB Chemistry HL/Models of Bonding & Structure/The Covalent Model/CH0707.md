# CH0707

The structural formula of borane ($\ce{BH3}$) is shown.

![PE-707](images/PE-707.png)

Which of the following statements is/are true?

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$The electron domain geometry of borane is trigonal planar. 

-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The bonds in borane are dative covalent (coordination) bonds. 

-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$The boron atom in borane is electron$-$deficient. 

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

The boron atom in borane shares three bonding electron pairs with the hydrogen atoms and has no lone electron pairs. Therefore, the electron domain and bonding geometries are trigonal planar.

The boron atom is electron-deficient because it has only six electrons in its valence shell rather than a complete octet. Because it is electron-deficient, borane can accept a donated lone pair of electrons from a nucleophile, such as water or a fluoride ion, to form a dative covalent (coordination) bond. However, this dative covalent bond (coordination) is not present in borane itself.
