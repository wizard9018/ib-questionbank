# CH1530

Which statement is correct about ClF$+\atop2$?

$\\[10pts]$

| $\hspace{1em}$  |   | Hybridization of Cl | Geometry of Ion| 

| :-- | :-- | :-: | :-:| 

|| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | $sp^3$  | bent |

|| B.$\hspace{1em}$ |$sp^3$  | linear|

| | C.$\hspace{1em}$ |$sp^2$  | bent |

||D.$\hspace{1em}$ | $sp^2$ |linear |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

 

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The student must be able to deduce the Lewis structure of the ion, as shown below. 

![CH1530](images/CH1530.png)

The Lewis structure has 20 electrons (7 from each chlorine and fluorine minus 1 electron lost due to the positive charge). Chlorine is surrounded by four electron domains, two bonding pairs and two nonbonding pairs. This results in a tetrahedral electron geometry to maximize the distance between the valence electrons.  The geometry of the ion is bent.

In addition, when chlorine combines with fluorine, hybridization occurs. This is the mixing of four atomic orbitals on chlorine to produce new $sp^3$ hybrid orbitals that are tetrahedrally arranged.

This question is categorized as easy as the student must work out the Lewis structure of the ion, the VSEPR shape, and identify the hybridization of the central atom.
