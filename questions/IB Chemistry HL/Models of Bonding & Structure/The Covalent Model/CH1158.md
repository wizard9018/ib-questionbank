# CH1158

Which molecule has the shortest carbon$-$to$-$carbon bond?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH2CH2CH2CH3}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH2CHCH2CH2CH3}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CHCCH2CH2CH3}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH2CHCH2CHCH2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The structural formulae are given below.

|$\hspace{8em}$|$\ce{CH3CH2CH2CH2CH3}$ $\newline \ \newline$ $\textrm{pentane}$ |![CH1158a](images/CH1158a.png)| $\hspace{8em}$
:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:
||$\ce{CH2CHCH2CH2CH3}$ $\newline \ \newline$ $\textrm{pent--1--ene}$ |![CH1158b](images/CH1158b.png)
||$\ce{CHCCH2CH2CH3}$ $\newline \ \newline$ $\textrm{pent--1--yne}$ | ![CH1158c](images/CH1158c.png)
||$\ce{CH2CHCH2CHCH2}$  $\newline \ \newline$ $\textrm{penta--1,4--diene}$ | ![CH1158d](images/CH1158d.png)

Pent$-$1$-$yne has a $\ce{C#C}$ bond which is shorter than the single and double bonds in the other molecules.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must interpret the condensed formulae, compare the structures, identify multiple bonds, and compare their lengths.
