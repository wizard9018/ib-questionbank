# CH0286

Carbon tetrachloride has covalent bonding. Which of the following clearly describes this type of bonding?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The electrostatic force of attraction by oppositely charged ions
- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The force of attraction between the electron donor and electron acceptor

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The intermolecular force between hydrogen and most electronegative elements

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The electrostatic force of attraction between the shared pairs of electrons and the positively charged nuclei

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Choice A refers to ionic bonding;

Choice B could refer to ionic bonding or specifically coordinate (dative) bonding;

Choice C refers to hydrogen bonding.
