# CH0338

![ch0388](images/ch0388.png)

The bond angles surrounding atoms 1, 2, and 3 are:

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  |   |Atom 1|Atom 2|Atom 3|

  |:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|

  |A.$\hspace{1em}$|90$\degree$|120$\degree$|120$\degree$

  |B.$\hspace{1em}$|109.5$\degree$|120$\degree$|109.5$\degree$

  |C.$\hspace{1em}$|107$\degree$|120$\degree$|109.5$\degree$

  |D.$\hspace{1em}$|109.5$\degree$|120$\degree$|107$\degree$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Atom 1 has four electron domains and so a tetrahedral shape (109.5$\degree$).

Atom 2 has three electron domains and so a trigonal planar shape (120$\degree$).

Atom 3 has four electron domains of which one is a lone pair giving it a trigonal pyramidal shape (107$\degree$).
