# CH2038

Which statement about coordinate covalent bonds is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$A coordinate covalent bond involves the transfer of electrons from one atom to another.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Both atoms in a coordinate covalent bond contribute one electron to the shared bonding electrons.

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$A coordinate covalent bond is weaker than a regular covalent bond.

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$A coordinate covalent bond forms when one atom donates both electrons in a shared pair.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

A coordinate covalent bond occurs when one atom in a covalent bond contributes both of the electrons instead of one electron being shared from each atom of the bond. Importantly, the electrons are not transferred, but they are shared, resulting in a covalent bond with a similar strength to a regular covalent bond.
