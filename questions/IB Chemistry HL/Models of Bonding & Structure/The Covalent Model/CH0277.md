# CH0277

Based on their structures, which of the substances below is likely to be the most volatile?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{HF}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{HCl}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{H2SO4}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{HNO3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The other three in theory, can form hydrogen bonds due to $\ce{O-H}$ and $\ce{F-H}$ bonds present.
