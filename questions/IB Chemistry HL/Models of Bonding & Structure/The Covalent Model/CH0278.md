# CH0278

Which of the below gives the intermolecular forces in water in the correct order from weakest to strongest?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\text{London} < \text{dipole-dipole} <\text{hydrogen bonding}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\text{Dipole-dipole} < \text{London} <\text{hydrogen bonding}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\text{Hydrogen bonding} < \text{dipole-dipole} <\text{London}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\text{London} < \text{hydrogen bonding} < \text{dipole-dipole}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Recall that hydrogen bonds are the strongest intermolecular forces, and London forces are the weakest.
