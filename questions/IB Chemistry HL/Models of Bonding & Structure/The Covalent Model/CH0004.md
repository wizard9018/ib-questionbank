# CH0004

What name is given to a compound composed of two non-metals?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Polyatomic ions

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Ionic

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Molecular

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Alloy

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
Compounds consisting of non-metals are molecular or covalent compounds.

  Ionic compounds comprise a cation (metal) and anion (non-metal).

   Alloys are mixtures of 2 or more metals.

   Polyatomic ions are charged, while compounds are neutral.
