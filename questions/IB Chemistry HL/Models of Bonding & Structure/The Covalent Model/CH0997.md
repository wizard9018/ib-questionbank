# CH0997

Which statement(s) is(are) correct about $\ce{IF+2}?$

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :- | :- |
    | I. | There are two bonding pairs of electrons on iodine. |
    | II. | There are two nonbonding pairs of electrons on iodine. |
    | III. | The electron domain geometry is tetrahedral. |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$I only

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$II only

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The electron dot/Lewis structure of $\ce{IF+2}$ is shown below:

![CH0997](images/CH0997.png)

Iodine is the central atom and presents 4 bonding electrons (two pairs of bonding electrons) and 4 non-bonding electrons (two pairs of non-boding electrons). There are 4 electron domains around, resulting in a tetrahedral electron domain geometry.

This question is categorized as very hard as the student must draw the Lewis structure of the ion, apply the VSEPR theory principles, determine the electron domains around the central atom, and determine the electron domain geometry in an unusual cation.
