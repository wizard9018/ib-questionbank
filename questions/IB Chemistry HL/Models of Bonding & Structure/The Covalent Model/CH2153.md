# CH2153

What is the number of sigma $(\sigma)$ and pi $(\pi)$ bonds in $\ce{(CH3)2C=C(CN)2}?$

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | $\mathbf{\sigma-}$bonds | $\mathbf{\pi-}$bonds |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    |A.$\hspace{1em}$ | 13 | 5 |
    |B. | 5 | 13 |
    |C. | 14 | 4 |
    |D. | 16 | 2 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The Lewis structure of the molecule is

![CH2153](images/CH2153.png)

Each single bond $(\ce{C-H}, \ce{C-C}, \ce{C-N},$ etc.) contains one $\sigma-$bond, each double bond $(\ce{C=C})$ contains one $\sigma-$bond and one $\pi-$bond, and each triple bond $(\ce{C#N})$ contains one $\sigma-$bond and two $\pi-$bonds.  There are 13 $\sigma-$bonds and 5 $\pi-$bonds.
