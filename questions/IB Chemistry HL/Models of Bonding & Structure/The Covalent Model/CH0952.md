# CH0952

In a paper chromatography experiment, a component of the mixture traveled $2.0\,\text{cm}$ and the $R_f$ value was 0.60. How far did the solvent travel?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}1.4\,\text{cm}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}3.3\,\text{cm}$
-   C.$\hspace{1em}0.30\,\text{cm}$
-   D.$\hspace{1em}1.2\,\text{cm}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
To answer this question, the student must use the following equation:

$R_f$ $= \dfrac{d_{spot}}{d_{solvent}}$

where $d_{spot}$ is the distance traveled by the spot and $d_{solvent}$ is the distance traveled by the solvent, as measured from the starting line.
Therefore:

$d_{solvent} = \dfrac{d_{spot}}{{R_f}} = \dfrac{2.0 \textrm{\ cm}}{0.60} = 3.3 \textrm{\ cm}$

This question is of medium difficulty because the student must first recall the equation to calculate $R_f$ and then rearrange the formula in order to calculate the distance traveled by the solvent.
