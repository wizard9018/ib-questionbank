# CH2014

What is the correct overall number of $\sigma\text{--}$ and $\pi\text{--}$bonds in the limonene structure?

![CH2014](images/CH2014.png)

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | $\mathbf{\sigma-}$bonds | $\mathbf{\pi-}$bonds |
    | :-- | :-: | :-: |
    | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | 10 | 2 |
    | B. | 8 | 4 |
    | C. | 26 | 2 |
    | D. | 24 | 4 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The student must be able to interpret the skeletal structure given and identify all the single and double bonds.  Drawing the full structural formual from the skeletal formula can help:

![CH2014ans](images/CH2014ans.png)

Every single bond is a $\sigma\text{--}$bond, and every double bond is one $\sigma$ and one $\pi\text{--}$bond.

There are sixteen $\ce{C-H}$ single bonds, two $\ce{C=C}$ bonds and eight $\ce{C-C}$ bonds. The overall number is 26 $\sigma\text{--}$bonds and 2 $\pi\text{--}$bonds.

This question is categorized as a medium as the student must interpret the skeletal structure to deduce the number of sigma and pi bonds.
