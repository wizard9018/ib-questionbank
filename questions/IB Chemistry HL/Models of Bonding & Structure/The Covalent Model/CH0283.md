# CH0283

Which bond is more polar?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{C-C}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{C-N}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{C-F}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{C-Br}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Fluorine is the most electronegative element on the periodic table. Therefore, the greatest difference in electronegativity between $\ce{C}$ and $\ce{F}$ results in the most polar bond.
