# CH0295

**Maximum mark: 5**

There are many different molecules with both carbon and oxygen.

1. Draw the Lewis structures for carbon monoxide, carbon dioxide, and methanol. [3 marks]

2. Arrange in increasing order of bond length the $\ce{C-O}$ bonds found in the three molecules from part (a). [1 mark]

3. Outline why the individual carbon-oxygen bond is stronger in carbon monoxide than in carbon dioxide.  [1 mark]
