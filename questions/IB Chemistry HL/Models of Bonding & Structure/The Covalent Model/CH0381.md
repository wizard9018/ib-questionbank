# CH0381

The Lewis structure of ammonia is shown.

![14.2-381](images/14.2-381.png)

How is the electron domain geometry of ammonia related to the hybridization of the N atom?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The $sp^2$ hybridization produces a trigonal pyramidal electron domain geometry

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The $sp^2$ hybridization produces a tetrahedral electron domain geometry

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The $sp^3$ hybridization produces a trigonal pyramidal electron domain geometry

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The $sp^3$ hybridization produces a tetrahedral electron domain geometry

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The N atom in ammonia has three bonds and one lone pair, producing a tetrahedral *electron domain geometry*. The hybridization of the N atom is $sp^3$. 

This question is moderately difficult as the student must be able to both determine the hybridization of a molecule from its Lewis structure and understand the difference between molecular and electron domain geometries.
