# CH0282

**Maximum mark: 6**

The halogens can be found in group 17 and exist as diatomic molecules.

1. State the intermolecular forces present in all of the halogens in their standard states. [1 mark]

2. Describe and explain the trend in boiling point down group 17 by referring to the relative strengths of their intermolecular forces. [2 marks]

3. Iodine monochloride ($\ce{ICl}$) has a very similar molecular mass to bromine ($\ce{Br2}$).

    1. State the intermolecular force present in $\ce{ICl}$ that is not present in $\ce{Br2}$ .  [1 mark]

    2. Based on your answer to part (a) of this question, suggest with a reason whether the boiling point of $\ce{ICl}$ will be higher, lower, or very similar to that of $\ce{Br2}$ .  [2 marks]
