# CH0339

Which of the following statements regarding the Lewis structure of BF$_3$ is correct? 

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$The structure contains a net dipole moment.

-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The structure would have bond angles of 120$\degree$ with one double bond.

-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$The structure is considered to be electron deficient.

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$ II only

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$ III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Boron trifluoride has $3 + 3 \times 7 = 24$ valence electrons in total. The Lewis structure places three bonding pairs of electrons around the central boron atom, with no lone pairs on boron. 

![CH0339lewis](images/CH0339lewis.png)

Importantly, boron ends up surrounded by only six electrons, which means it does not complete an octet and is therefore described as electron deficient.  Statement III is correct.

 This Lewis structure supports a trigonal planar arrangement with bond angles of $120^\circ$. 

![CH0339vsepr](images/CH0339vsepr.png)

Because the molecule is symmetrical, the bond dipoles cancel and there is no overall dipole moment, even though each $\ce{B-F}$ bond is polar. Statement I is incorrect. 

Elements such as beryllium, boron, and sometimes aluminum often form stable compounds that do not obey the octet rule. Proposals to add a double bond to fluorine are not correct, because that would assign an unrealistic positive formal charge to highly electronegative fluorine and a negative charge to boron, which is much less electronegative. Statement II is incorrect. 

![CH0339formal](images/CH0339formal.png)

***incorrect***
