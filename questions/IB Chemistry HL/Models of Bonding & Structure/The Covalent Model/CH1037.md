# CH1037

Which combination is correct for $\ce{SF4}$?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | Electron domain geometry | Molecular geometry | $\mathbf{F-\hat{S}-F}$ angles |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | A. | Triangular bipyramidal | See$-$saw | $88^\circ$ and $117^\circ$ |
    | B. | Triangular bipyramidal | See$-$saw | $180^\circ$, $120^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ |
    | C. | Tetrahedral | Tetrahedral | $90^\circ$ |
    | D. | Tetrahedral | Tetrahedral | $109.5^\circ$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The student must be able to draw the Lewis (electron dot) structure of $\ce{SF4}$ and deduce the number of electron domains to apply the VSEPR theory.

Sulfur has six valence electrons and fluorine seven: there are 34 electrons in the molecule.  The Lewis structure requires an expanded octet around the sulfur atom:

![CH1037lewis](images/CH1037lewis.png)

There are five electron domains around sulfur, so the electron domain geometry is trigonal bipyramidal. Four correspond to bonding electron domains and one to a nonbonding electron domain.
The molecular geometry is known as an unsymmetrical tetrahedron or see$-$saw.

![CH1037vsepr](images/CH1037vsepr.png)

Angles should be $120\,{}^\circ$ and $90^\circ$, but the repulsion exerted by the equatorial lone pair reduces the $120^\circ$ angle to $117^\circ$ and the $90^\circ$ angle to $88^\circ$.

This question is categorized as hard. The student must apply the VSEPR theory and provide electron geometry, molecular geometry, and angles.
