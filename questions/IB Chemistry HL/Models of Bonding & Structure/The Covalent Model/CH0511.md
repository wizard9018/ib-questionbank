# CH0511

Two Lewis structures of gaseous beryllium difluoride $\ce{(BeF2)}$ are shown.

![14.1-CH0511](images/CH0511.png)

Which of the following statements is true?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Structure **A** is preferred because $\ce{Be}$ has a stable octet of electrons

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Structure **A** is preferred because $\ce{F}$ has fewer nonbonding electron pairs

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Structure **B** is preferred because $\ce{F}$ forms no $\pi$ bonds

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Structure **B** is preferred because $\ce{Be}$ and $\ce{F}$ have zero formal charge

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The preferred Lewis structure is that which minimizes the total magnitude of formal charges, *i.e.*

the value $\Delta \ce{FC} =$ sum of positive charges $-$ sum of negative charges. This is the structure in which $\ce{Be}$ has an incomplete octet of valence electrons.

In Structure **A**, F has a $+1$ formal charge and Be has a $-2$ formal charge.

$\ce{FC} = \textrm{valence\ } \ce{e-} - \textrm{nonbonding\ } \ce{e-} - \dfrac{1}{2} \times \textrm{bonding\ } \ce{e-}$

$\ce{FC}_\ce{F} = 7 - 4 - \dfrac{1}{2}\times4 = +1$

$\ce{FC}_\ce{Be} = 2 - 0 - \dfrac{1}{2}\times 8 = -2$

In Structure **B**, Be does not complete its octet but the formal charge of all atoms is 0.

$\ce{FC}_\ce{F} = 7 - 6 - \dfrac{1}{2}\times2 = 0$

$\ce{FC}_\ce{Be} = 2 - 0 - \dfrac{1}{2}\times 4 = 0$

This question is categorized as moderately difficult: although it addresses a principle that is covered extensively in the syllabus, the example used will be unfamiliar to most students and distractor A could seem a plausible response.
