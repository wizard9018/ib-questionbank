# CH0271

Which statement correctly explains why the boiling point of 2-methylpentane is lower than that of hexane?

![4.4-271](images/4.4-271.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 2-methylpentane has fewer electrons than hexane

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 2-methylpentane has a smaller $M_r$ than hexane

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 2-methylpentane has more branching than hexane

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 2-methylpentane has lower electron density than hexane

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

2$-$methylpentane is branched and therefore has a lower surface area. The lower the surface area, the weaker the London (dispersion) forces and the lower the boiling point.
