# CH0513

The molecule $\ce{O2}$ contains one $\sigma$ and one $\pi$ bond. How do $p$ orbitals in the $\ce{O}$ atoms overlap to produce the $\pi$ bond?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$End-to-end overlap results in high electron density along the bond axis

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Sideways overlap results in high electron density along the bond axis

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$End-to-end overlap results in high electron density above and below the bond axis

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Sideways overlap results in high electron density above and below the bond axis

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The student must recall the appearance of a π bond and the way in which it is formed. This information is stated clearly in the syllabus, so the question is categorized as easy.

Option A describes the formation of a $\sigma$ bond.
