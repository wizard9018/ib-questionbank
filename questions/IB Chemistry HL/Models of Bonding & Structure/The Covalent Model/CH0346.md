# CH0346

Which molecule would be considered polar covalent?           

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$CO$_2$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$NH$_3$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$CH$_4$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$SO$_3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The bonds between N and H are polar due to the difference in electronegativity.

The overall shape of the NH$_3$ is asymmetrical (trigonal pyramidal), resulting in a molecule with a net dipole moment.

$\ce{CO2}$ and $\ce{SO3}$ both have polar bonds, but the molecules are symmetrical. Therefore, they do not have net dipole moment.

The C$-$H bonds are not polar due to a small difference in electronegativity.
