# CH0515

**Maximum mark: 6**

Ozone $\ce{(O3)}$ and molecular oxygen $(\ce{O2})$ both contain $\pi$ bonds.

1.  The ozone molecule has the following Lewis structure:

    ![14.1-CH0515a](images/CH0515a.png)

    The $\pi$ bonding electrons in the ozone molecule are delocalized.

    1.  Explain how a $\pi$ bond is formed from the orbitals of two O atoms. [2 marks]

    2.  State the formal charge of the central $\ce{O}$ atom in $\ce{O3}$. [1 mark]

    3.  State the bond order of the $\ce{O-O}$ bonds in $\ce{O3}$. [1 mark]

2.  The UV$-$visible absorption spectrum of ozone is shown.

    ![14.1-CH0515b](images/CH0515b.png)
    [Source: approximated from data at pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14664632/]

    Explain why the absorption spectrum of molecular oxygen would differ from that of ozone. [2 marks]

    2.  +1 $\checkmark $

        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

        Formal charge (FC) = valence electrons $-$ nonbonding electrons $- \ \frac{1}{2}$bonding electrons

        Oxygeb has 6 valence electrons, therefore in ozone

        FC = $6 - 2 - \frac{1}{2} \times6 = +1$.

    3.  1.5  $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
        There are 3 bonding pairs of electrons divided between two $\ce{O-O}$ bonds, so the bond order of each bond is $\frac{3}{2}$.
