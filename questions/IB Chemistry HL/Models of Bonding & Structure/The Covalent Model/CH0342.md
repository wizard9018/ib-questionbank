# CH0342

Which correctly states the relative size of the bond angles for $\ce{H2O}$, $\ce{NH3}$, and $\ce{CH4}$? 

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH4} < \ce{NH3} < \ce{H2O}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H2O} < \ce{NH3} < \ce{CH4}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H2O} < \ce{CH4} < \ce{NH3}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{NH3} < \ce{CH4} < \ce{H2O}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

All three central atoms (N, C, and O) have four electron domains. An increasing number of lone pairs reduces the angle between the bonding pairs.

In $\ce{CH4}$ there are no lone pairs and it has a tetrahedral shape with bond angles of 109.5$\degree$.

In $\ce{NH3}$ there is one lone pair and it has a trigonal pyramidal shape with bond angles of 107$\degree$.

In $\ce{H2O}$, there are two lone pairs, and it has a bent shape with bond angles of 104.5$\degree$.
