# CH0705

Ammonium nitrate is a compound commonly used in fertilizers and explosives.

What is the bond angle of the cation in ammonium nitrate?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}104.5^\circ$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}107.0^\circ$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}109.5^\circ$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}120.0^\circ$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

The cation in ammonium nitrate is the ammonium ion, which has the formula $\ce{NH4^+}$. There are four bonds and no lone pairs, so the ion is tetrahedral with a bond angle of approximately 109.5 $^\circ$.

Options B and D correspond to the neutral trigonal pyramidal ammonia molecule and trigonal planar nitrate anion, respectively, while option A is the bond angle of water.

 

This question is categorized as hard, as the student must correctly identify the cation in a salt, recall the structure of the ammonium ion, and identify the bond angle of a tetrahedral species with little guidance.
