# CH1038

What is the correct hybridization of nitrogen in the ammonium ion?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}sp$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}sp^2$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}sp^3$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}sp^3d$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
To determine the hybridization around the central nitrogen atom in the ammonium ion, $\ce{NH4+}$, start with the Lewis structure.  There are eight valence electrons in the ions, so the structure is:

![CH1038Lewis](images/CH1038Lewis.png)

There are four electron domains around the central atom so the nitrogen a tetrahedral electron domain geometry and is $sp^3$ hybridized.

Hybridized orbitals involve mixing $s$ and $p$ atomic orbitals to form new hybrid atomic orbitals that orient themselves at $109.5^\circ$. Each hybrid orbital in nitrogen overlaps with the atomic orbitals of hydrogen, forming four $\sigma$ bonds (direct overlap).

![CH1038](images/CH1038.png)

This question is categorized as medium. The student must connect the number of electron domains to the hybridization around the central atom.
