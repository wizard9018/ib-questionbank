## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
A $\sigma$ bonding orbital is produced by end$-$to$-$end overlap of atomic orbitals.  A $\pi-$bond requires side$-$to$-$side overlap as shown in option C. Options A and D would not produce a bond.
