# CH0343

What is the correct number of total valence electrons and electron domains on the central atom for the methanal molecule, $\ce{CH2O}$?            

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$8 valence electrons, 3 electron domains

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$16 valence electrons, 3 electron domains

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$8 valence electrons, 4 electron domains

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$16 valence electrons, 4 electron domains

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The central atom is carbon with two $\ce{C-H}$ single bonds and one $\ce{C=O}$ bond. This gives 3 electron domains and 8 valence electrons.
