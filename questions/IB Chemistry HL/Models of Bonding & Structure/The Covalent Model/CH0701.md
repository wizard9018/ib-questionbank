# CH0701

Which of the following is the correct Lewis structure of the carbonate ion?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$ ![PE-701a](images/PE-701a.png)

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$ ![PE-701b](images/PE-701b.png)

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$ ![PE-701c](images/PE-701c.png)

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$ ![PE-701d](images/PE-701d.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

The molecular formula of the carbonate ion is $\ce{CO3^{2-}}$.

The carbon atom forms two single bonds and one double bond, giving it an octet of valence electrons. Each oxygen atom also has an octet of valence electrons: the singly bonded oxygen atoms have three lone pairs, while the doubly bonded oxygen atom has two.

In reality, the bonds are equivalent due to resonance, but this effect is not represented in the Lewis structure.

This question is moderately difficult as the student must recall the formula of the carbonate ion and correctly interpret the structures given.
