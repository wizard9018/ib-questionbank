# CH0509

The $\ce{Xe}$ atom in $\ce{XeF2}$ has three nonbonding pairs of valence electrons and shares two bonding pairs of electrons with $\ce{F}$ atoms.

Which of the following statements are true?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :- | :- |
    | I. | $\ce{XeF2}$ has a bent molecular geometry. |
    | II. | The $\ce{Xe}$ atom has an expanded octet of valence electrons. |
    | III. | The formal charge of the $\ce{Xe}$ atom is zero. |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\ce{XeF2}$ has a trigonal bipyramidal electron domain geometry, as there are two bonds and three lone pairs around the central $\ce{Xe}$ atom. The molecular geometry is linear, not bent, as the lone pairs occupy equatorial positions to minimize repulsion.

![CH0509](images/CH0509.png)

The bonds and lone pairs total 10 valence electrons, meaning that the $\ce{Xe}$ has an expanded octet.

The oxidation state of the $\ce{Xe}$ atom is +2. To calculate the formal charge:

FC = 8 valence $\ce{e-}$ $-$ 6 nonbonding $\ce{e-} - \dfrac{1}{2} \times 4$ bonding $\ce{e-} = 0$
