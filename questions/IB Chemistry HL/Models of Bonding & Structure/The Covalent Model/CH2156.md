# CH2156

What is the hybridization of the central carbon atom in propanone, $\ce{CH3\textbf{\underline{C}}OCH3}?$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}sp$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}sp^2$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}sp^3$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}2p$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The hybridization of an atom is determined by the number of electron domains around it. Considering the Lewis structure:

![CH2156](images/CH2156.png)

there are three electron domains around the central carbon. According to VSEPR theory, three electron domains correspond to a trigonal planar shape which requires $sp^2$ hybridization. When carbon is $sp^2$ hybridized, a singly$\text{--}$occupied, unhybridized $2p$ orbital can form a $\pi\text{--}$bond giving rise to the double bond with oxygen.
