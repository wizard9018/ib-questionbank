## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1.  Octahedral. $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

        There are 6 bonding pairs and no lone pairs.

    2.  $\ce{S-F}$ bonds drawn in correct positions; $\checkmark$

        One lone pair drawn in correct positions. $\checkmark$

        ![14.1-CH0518i](images/CH0518i.png)

        *Ignore labelled formal charges;*\
        *Ignore labelled non-bonding electrons on F atoms;*\
        *Accept structures with bonding electrons drawn next to bonds or between atoms;*\
        *Do **not** award M2 if distinction between bonding and non-bonding electrons is ambiguous;*\
        *Do **not** award M2 if distinction between non-bonding electrons of $\ce{S}$ and $\ce{F}$ is ambiguous.*

        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

        There are 5 electron domains around the $\ce{S}$ atom and so, the shape is based on the trigonal bipyramidal geometry. To minimize repulsion, the lone pair takes an equatorial position giving it a $`$see-saw' geometry.

    3.  Lone / non-bonding (electron) pair occupies an equatorial position **OR** does not occupy an axial position **OR** is positioned $120^\circ$ to two $\ce{S-F}$ bonds / bonding pairs; $\checkmark$

        as this minimizes repulsion **OR** because lone pairs repel bonding pairs more strongly than bonding pairs repel each other. $\checkmark$

        *Award M1 if the positions of bonding pairs are described instead.*

        Formal charge $= 0. \, \checkmark$

        *Accept structures with formal charge of 0 labelled on third $\ce{O}$ atom;*\

        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$
