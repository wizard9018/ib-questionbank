# CH0392

**Maximum mark: 8**

Ethanol can be oxidized to ethanoic acid.

![14.2-392](images/14.2-392.png)

1.  State how the hybridization of the oxygen atom changes when the $\ce{C-O}$ bond of ethanol 

is converted to the $\ce{C=O}$ bond of ethanoic acid. [1 mark]

2.     State how the electron domain geometry of the oxygen atom changes when the $\ce{C-O}$ bond of ethanol is converted to the $\ce{C=O}$ bond of ethanoic acid. [2 marks]

3.  Ethanol and ethanoic acid can react to form ethylethanoate. The Lewis structure of ethylethanoate is shown.

    ![14.2-392c](images/14.2-392c.png)

    - The $\ce{C-O-C}$ bond angle is 115.7 $^\circ$. $\ \newline \ $

    

    1.  Suggest how the $\ce{C-O-C}$ bond angle indicates that the hybridization of the O atom is between $sp^2$ and $sp^3$. [2 marks]

    2.  Draw a resonance structure of ethylethanoate to show why the hybridization of the O atom in the $\ce{C-O-C}$ linkage is partly $sp^2$. Include in your structure any non$-$zero formal charges and nonbonding electron pairs. [3 marks]
