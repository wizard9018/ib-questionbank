# CH1184

Which species has the longest carbon-to-oxygen bond?

- A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CO2}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CO}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3COO-}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CO3^2-}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The structural formulae are given below.

![CH1184](images/CH1184.png)

Option A presents double bonds, option B presents a triple bond, option C presents an intermediate bond between a single and double bond, and option D presents an intermediate bond between **two** single and a double bond with a greater contribution of the single bond.

The bond length is a measure of the distance between two bonded nuclei.

Since multiple bonds have more shared pairs of electrons between the atoms, a stronger attraction between bonded nuclei results in stronger and, hence, shorter bonds.

Option D presents fewer electrons taking part in the bonding. As a result, the longest bond is between C and O atoms in option D.

This question is categorized as hard as bonds must be compared, taking into account the resonance structures of the ions presented.
