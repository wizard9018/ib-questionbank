Which two resonance structures best illustrate the bonding and formal charges of nitrous oxide?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

To answer this question, consider the expression for formal charge:

$\ce{FC}$ = valence $\ce{e-} -$ nonbonding $\ce{e-} - \dfrac{1}{2} \times$ bonding $\ce{e-}$

Choice A gives the correct formal charges and has molecules that can be linked by resonance.

The student must be able to deduce the formal charges of the N and O atoms from their bonds and lone pairs, and correctly determine which pairs of bonding arrangements could be linked by resonance. The question is hard as it involves a compound that may be unfamiliar to students and requires a good understanding of multiple topics.
