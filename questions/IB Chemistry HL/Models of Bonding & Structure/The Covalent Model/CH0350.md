# CH0350

**Maximum mark: 5**

Graphite is an allotrope of carbon.

1.  State two other allotropes of carbon. [1 mark]

2.  State the bond angle in graphite and describe the resulting structure. [2 marks]

3.  State two properties of graphite resulting from the structure. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\textit{Any additional two allotropes:}$\

    diamond, graphene, Fullerene.$\checkmark$

2.  Bond angle is 120$\degree$ $\checkmark$

    Hexagonal, parallel layers. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    Each carbon in graphite has three electron domains giving them trigonal planar arrangement.

3.  $\textit{Any two additional properties:}$\

    Good electrical conductor, soft, slippery, brittle, high melting point.$\checkmark$ $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    Graphite has delocalized electrons and can therefore conduct electricity. The weak London forces between the layers allow them to slide over each other, making them soft and slippery.

    Graphite has a giant covalent structure held together by many strong covalent bonds, leading to a high melting point.
