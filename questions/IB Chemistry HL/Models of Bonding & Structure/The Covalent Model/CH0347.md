# CH0347

Which molecule does **NOT** have a linear shape?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CS2}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{BeCl2}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{C2H2}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{SO2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

To answer this question, the Lewis structures of each molecule must be drawn and then the VSEPR rules must be applied.  If there are two electron domains around the central atom, the molecule will be linear.

In $\ce{SO2}$, the sulfur is bound to two oxygen atom and has one nonbonding pair of electrons giving it three electron domains in total.  The molecular shape is bent. $\ce{CS2}$, $\ce{BeCl2}$, and $\ce{C2H2}$ have two electron domains on their central atoms and have linear shapes.
