## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must be able to deduce the Lewis structure of this compound. There are $8 + 4\times 7 = 36$ valence electrons and krypton must expand its octet to accommondate the fluorine bonds.  With the octet filled on each fluorine, there are four remaining electrons that appear as lone pairs on the central atom.

![CH2012e](images/CH2012e.png)

Krypton has six electron domains. Four of the electron domains are bonding pairs, and two are nonbonding pairs. The electronic geometry is octahedral. To minimize the repulsion between the lone pairs, they are placed as far apart as possible according to VSEPR theory: the molecular geometry is square planar.

![CH2012f](images/CH2012f.png)

This question is categorized as medium as the student must work out the molecular geometry for a molecule with a noble gas and an expanded octet.
