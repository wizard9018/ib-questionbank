# CH2036

What is the shape of $\ce{PCl3}?$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$linear

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$trigonal pyramidal

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$tetrahedral

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$trigonal planar

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

To answer this question, the Lewis structure of $\ce{PCl3}$ needs to be drawn:

![CH2036a](images/CH2036a.png)

This reveals that the electron domain geometry of the molecule is tetrahedral since there are four regions where there are either nonbonding electrons or atoms attached to the central atom. The VSEPR geometry for a molecule with a tetrahedral electron domain geometry where one of the domains is nonbonding electrons is trigonal pyramidal:

![CH2036b](images/CH2036b.png)
