# CH0269

Which of the below shows the boiling points in the correct ascending order?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{(CH3)4C} < \ce{CH3CH2CH2CH2CH3} < \ce{CH3CH2CH(CH3)CH3}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{(CH3)4C} < \ce{CH3CH2CH(CH3)CH3} < \ce{CH3CH2CH2CH2CH3}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{CH3CH2CH2CH2CH3} < \ce{CH3CH2CH(CH3)CH3} < \ce{(CH3)4C}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{CH3CH2CH(CH3)CH3} < \ce{CH3CH2CH2CH2CH3} < \ce{(CH3)4C}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The most branched compound will have the lowest boiling point due to a lower surface area.
