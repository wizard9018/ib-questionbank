# CH0285

In a $\ce{CO2}$ molecule, how many lone and bonding pairs of electrons are present in the Lewis-dot structure?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | Lone pairs of electrons | Bonding pairs |
    | --- | :-: | :-: |
    |$\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$ | 4 | 4 |
    |$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$  | 2 | 6 |
    |$\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$  | 4 | 2 |
    |$\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$  | 2 | 8 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Two $\ce{C=O}$ bonds and, therefore, four bonding pairs. Each oxygen atom has four electrons not involved in bonding; therefore, two lone pairs per oxygen atom (four total).
