# CH0275

Which statement explains the high boiling point of $\ce{NH3}$?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Strong London forces

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Strong $\ce{N-H}$ covalent bonds

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Strong hydrogen bonds

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Strong dipole-dipole interactions

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\ce{NH3}$ has all of the bonds/forces named above, but its high boiling point is due to the presence of hydrogen bonds which are the strongest intermolecular forces. Hydrogen bonding is possible due to the presence of $\ce{N-H}$ bonds.
