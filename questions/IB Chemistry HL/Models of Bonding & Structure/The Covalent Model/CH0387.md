# CH0387

The structural formula of beryllium hydride is shown.

![14.2-387](images/14.2-387.png)

Which diagram best illustrates the valence electron configuration of the beryllium atom after hybridization?

-   A. ![14.2-387a](images/14.2-387a.png)

-   B. ![14.2-387b](images/14.2-387b.png)

-   C. ![14.2-387c](images/14.2-387c.png)

-   D. ![14.2-387d](images/14.2-387d.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

The beryllium atom in $\ce{BeH2}$ is $sp$ hybridized, with two $sp$ orbitals of equal energy and two higher energy $p$ orbitals. Each $sp$ orbital contains an unpaired electron, while the two $p$ orbitals are unoccupied. The $sp$ orbitals are positioned at $180\degree$, producing a linear geometry, while the $p$ orbitals are perpendicular to each other and to the $\ce{Be-H}$ bonds.

This question is hard as it requires the student to determine the hybridization of an unfamiliar molecule and correctly recall the orbital energy diagram.
