# CH0383

The tetrafluoroborate ion ($\ce{BF4-}$) is produced when boron trifluoride ($\ce{BF3}$) reacts with a fluoride ion.

![14.2-383](images/14.2-383.png)

What is the hybridization of the boron atom in $\ce{BF3}$ and $\ce{BF4-}$?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  | |$\mathbf{BF_3}$| $\mathbf{BF_4^-}$|

  |:-:|:-:|:-:|

   A.$\hspace{1em}$|$sp^3$|$sp^2$

   B.$\hspace{1em}$|$sp^2$|$sp^3$

   C.$\hspace{1em}$|$sp^2$|$sp^2$

   D.$\hspace{1em}$|$sp^3$|$sp^3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

$\ce{BF3}$ is trigonal planar, with an unoccupied p orbital perpendicular to the plane of the molecule. The hybridization of B is $sp^2$.

$\ce{BF4-}$ is tetrahedral and the B atom has $sp^3$ hybridization.

This question is moderately difficult as the student must consider the hybridization of a non-hydrocarbon, recognizing that the B atom in $\ce{BF3}$ has only three electron domains and is therefore analogous to the C atom of ethene.
