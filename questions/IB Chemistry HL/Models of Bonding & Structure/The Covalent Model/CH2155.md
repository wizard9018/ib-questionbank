## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

Formal charge is the charge an atom would have if all the bonding electrons in the molecule were shared equally, and if its nonbonding electrons were not shared at all. Formal charge is typically used to choose the best Lewis structure when several are possible.

Formal charge can be calculated as:

$\text{FC} = \text{VE} - \text{NBE} - \dfrac{1}{2}\text{ BE}$

where the formal charge (FC) is equal to the number of valence electrons (VE) minus the number of nonbonding electrons (NBE) minus one half of the number of bonding electrons (BE). The sum of the formal charges of all atoms in a molecule or ion should be equal to the overall charge of that molecule or ion.

***Formal Charge of Sulphur:***

$\text{FC} = 6 - 0 - \frac{1}{2}\times 12 = 0$

***Formal Charge of Double$-$Bonded Oxygen:***

$\text{FC} = 6 - 4 - \frac{1}{2}\times 4 = 0$

***Formal Charge of Single$-$Bonded Oxygen:***

$\text{FC} = 6 - 6 - \frac{1}{2}\times 2 = -1$
