# CH1230

What is the reason behind the trend in the boiling points of noble gases?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$London dispersion forces are stronger as the number of electrons decreases

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The electron cloud is more easily distorted as the atomic number increases

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Permanent dipoles get stronger as the atomic number increases

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Permanent dipoles get stronger as the atomic number decreases

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The boiling points increase down a group (you can refer to the chemistry Data Booklet for that).

Noble gases are monoatomic and no permanent dipoles exist (Options C and D are incorrect).

Only London dispersion forces, known as instantaneous dipoles, are present. These are induced and are temporary. Their strength depends on the number of electrons involved; the greater the number of electrons in the electron cloud, the greater the strength of the London dispersion force. Option A is incorrect.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must interpret the trend in the boiling point of gases under the light of the LDF (London dispersion forces).
