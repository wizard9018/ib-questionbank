# CH1229

Which statement is correct for $\ce{N2H4}?$

- A. $\hspace{1em}$The geometry around nitrogen is trigonal planar and the $H\hat{N}H$ angle is $120\,{}^\circ$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$The geometry around nitrogen is trigonal planar and the $H\hat{N}H$ angle is $180\,{}^\circ$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$The geometry around nitrogen is pyramidal and the $H\hat{N}H$ angle is $109.5\,{}^\circ$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$The geometry around nitrogen is pyramidal and the $H\hat{N}H$ angle is $107\,{}^\circ$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The Lewis structure of hydrazine is shown below:

![CH1229](images/CH1229.png)

Four electron domains are around each nitrogen, 3 bonding pairs and one non-bonding pair, so a pyramidal geometry applies.

![CH1229B](images/CH1229B.png)

According to the VSEPR theory, the lone pair in the nitrogen exerts a greater repulsion than bonding pairs. As a result, the $H\hat{N}H$ angle is expected to be smaller than $109.5\,{}^\circ.$

![CH1229C](images/CH1229C.png)

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must deduce the Lewis structure, apply the VSEPR model principles, and figure out geometry and bond angles.
