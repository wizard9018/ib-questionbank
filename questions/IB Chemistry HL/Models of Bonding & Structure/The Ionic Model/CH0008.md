# CH0008

Which of the following polyatomic ion formulas is incorrect?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{NO^-_3}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{OH^-}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{SO^{2-}_4}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{CO^{2-}_4}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
There is no polyatomic ion with the formula of $\ce{CO^{2-}_4}$. The correct formula for the carbonate ion is $\ce{CO^{2-}_3}$. The formulas and charges of the other ions are correct.
