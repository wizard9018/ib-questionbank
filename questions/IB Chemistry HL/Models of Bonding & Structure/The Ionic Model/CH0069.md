# CH0069

**Maximum mark: 7**

P, Q, R and S are elements with consecutive atomic numbers in period 3 of the periodic table. There are 3 electrons in the valence shell of element P.

1. Identify the elements P, Q, R and S. [1 mark]

2. Using the relevant symbols for the elements concerned, suggest the formula for the ionic compound formed involving P and S.  [2 marks]

3. Explain why the melting point of the compound formed between elements P and S is likely to be relatively high. [2 marks]

4. The Pauling electronegativity values of elements P and R are 1.5 and 2.1 respectively. State whether the bonding found in the compound of P and R is likely to be ionic or not. Explain your choice. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. P = aluminium, Q = silicon, R = phosphorus, S = sulfur  $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
   Period 3 is the third row of the periodic table. The elements in groups 13, 14, 15, and 16 have 3, 4, 5, and 6 electrons in their outer shell, respectively.

2. $\ce{Al2S3}\:\checkmark$\
     $\ce{Al^3+}$ and  $\ce{S^2-} \: \checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
   In the ionic compound, the charges of the positive and negative ions must cancel out to give a neutral compound. Al is in group 13; its ion has a charge of $3+$.  S is in group 16; its ion has a charge of $2-$. Two $3+$ ions balance the charges of three $2-$ ions.
