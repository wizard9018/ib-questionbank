# CH0700

The reaction of solid magnesium with gaseous oxygen is shown.

$\ce{Mg(s) + \frac{1}{2}O2(g) ->  MgO(s)}$

Which of the following correctly describes the process of MgO(s) formation?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Magnesium atoms lose electrons to produce magnesium cations

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Magnesium atoms lose electrons to produce magnesium anions

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Magnesium atoms gain electrons to produce magnesium cations

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Magnesium atoms gain electrons to produce magnesium anions

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Oxidation reaction; Magnesium is much less electronegative than oxygen, so the Mg atoms lose electrons to the O atoms during the reaction. This process forms positive $\ce{Mg^{2+}}$ ions, termed cations, and negative $\ce{O^{2-}}$ ions, termed anions.

This question is categorized as easy, as the student must recall two pieces of information that are covered extensively in the syllabus.
