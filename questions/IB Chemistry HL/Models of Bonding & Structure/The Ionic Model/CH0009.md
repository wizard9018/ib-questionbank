# CH0009

Which of the following would be the products of the double replacement reaction below?
$\ \newline \ $

$
\ce{Pb(NO_3)_2 + KI -> \qquad ?}
$

- A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{K(NO_3)_2}$ and $\ce{PbI}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{K_2NO_3}$ and $\ce{PbI_2}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{PbI}$ and $\ce{KNO_3}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{KNO_3}$ and $\ce{PbI_2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
In a double replacement reaction, two reactants exchange ions to form two new compounds. $\ce{Pb^{2+}}$ will combine with iodide ($\ce{I^-}$), forming $\ce{PbI_2}$, and $\ce{K^+}$ will combine with nitrate ($\ce{NO^{-}_3}$), forming $\ce{KNO_3}$. The charges within the compound have to cancel each other out to form a neutral compound.
