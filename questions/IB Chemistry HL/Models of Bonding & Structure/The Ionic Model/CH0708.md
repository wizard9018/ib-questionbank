# CH0708

A powdered solid sample is found to contain two ionic compounds.

Which of the following are possible formulas for these compounds?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{NH4SO4}$ and $\ce{Ca3(PO4)2}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Ca(CO3)2}$ and $\ce{K2HCO3}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Al2(CO3)3}$ and $\ce{(NH4)2PO4}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{K2SO4}$ and $\ce{Al(HCO3)3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The formulas of the potassium, calcium, aluminum, and ammonium cations are $\ce{K+}$, $\ce{Ca^{2+}}$, $\ce{Al^{3+}}$, and $\ce{NH4+}$, respectively.

The formulas of the nitrate, carbonate, hydrogen carbonate, sulfate, and phosphate ions are $\ce{NO3-}$, $\ce{CO3^{2-}}$, $\ce{HCO3-}$, $\ce{SO4^{2-}}$, and $\ce{PO4^{3-}}$, respectively.

The incorrect formulas in A, B, and C are $\ce{NH4SO4}$ (should be $\ce{(NH4)2SO4)}$, $\ce{Ca(CO3)2}$ (should be $\ce{CaCO3}$), $\ce{K2HCO3}$ (should be $\ce{KHCO3}$), and $\ce{(NH4)2PO4}$ (should be $\ce{(NH4)3PO4}$).

This question is categorized as moderately difficult, as the student must recall the charges of multiple ions and calculate the correct stoichiometries of the ions in a range of salts.
