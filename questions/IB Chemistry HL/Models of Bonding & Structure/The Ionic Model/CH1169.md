# CH1169

What chemical equation represents the lattice enthalpy of potassium fluoride?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{K+(g) + F-(g) -> KF(s)}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{KF(s) -> K+(g) + F-(g)}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{K(s) + \frac{1}{2} F2(g) -> KF(s)}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{KF(s) -> K(s) + \frac{1}{2} F2(g)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The lattice enthalpy is the energy change when one mole of a solid ionic compound is separated into gaseous ions under standard conditions. In this case, potassium fluoride is broken to yield potassium ions $\ce{(K+)}$ and fluoride ions $\ce{(F-)}$ in the gaseous state.
