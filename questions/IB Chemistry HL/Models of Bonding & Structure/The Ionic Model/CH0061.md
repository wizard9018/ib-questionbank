# CH0061

Sodium fluoride contains ionic bonding. Which statement correctly describes ionic bonding?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The electrostatic attraction between positive ions, arranged in a lattice, and delocalized electrons

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ A force of attraction that operates between molecules in a lattice

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The electrostatic attraction between a shared pair of electrons and positively charged nuclei

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Oppositely charged ions attract, it is an ionic bonding;

Choice A describes metallic bonding;

Choice B describes a simple molecular lattice;

Choice D describes a covalent bond.
