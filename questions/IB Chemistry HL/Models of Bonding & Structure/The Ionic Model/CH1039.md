# CH1039

Which equation represents the lattice enthalpy of $\ce{MgO}$?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MgO}\textrm{(s)} \longrightarrow \ce{Mg^2+}\textrm{(g)}+ \ce{O^2-}\textrm{(g)}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Mg^2+}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{O^2-}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow \ce{MgO}\textrm{(s)}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MgO}\textrm{(s)} \longrightarrow \ce{Mg}\textrm{(g)} + \frac{1}{2}\ce{O2}\textrm{(g)}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MgO}\textrm{(s)} \longrightarrow \ce{Mg}\textrm{(s)} + \frac{1}{2}\ce{O2}\textrm{(g)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The lattice enthalpy expresses the enthalpy change for forming gaseous ions from one mole of a solid crystal (ionic compound) at standard conditions.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty. Students must recall and apply the lattice enthalpy definition to magnesium oxide.
