# CH2098

Which statement about ionic compounds is true?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions holds the lattice together.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The melting point is low due to the weak forces between ions in the solid state.

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Ionic compounds conduct electricity in the solid state because ions are free to move.

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Ionic compounds are malleable and ductile because their ions can slide past each other easily.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$
Ionic bonding is the electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions. This strong attraction holds the ions together in a rigid, repeating three$-$dimensional lattice. The strength of these ionic bonds gives ionic compounds several characteristic properties, including high melting points, brittle structures, and the formation of crystalline solids.

The other options are incorrect due to common misconceptions.
-   Ionic compounds generally have high melting points, not low ones, precisely due to the strong ionic attractions in the lattice.

-   Ionic compounds do not conduct electricity in the solid state$\text{--}$ions are fixed in place within the lattice so there are no mobile charge carriers. They only conduct electricity when molten or dissolved in water, when the ions are free to move.

-   Ionic compounds are brittle, not malleable or ductile. When stress is applied, the alignment of like charges causes repulsion and the lattice shatters. This contrasts with the behavior of metals, where delocalized electrons allow layers of atoms to slide past each other without breaking bonds.
