# CH0067

In which instance is ionic bonding most likely to occur?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ When two metals are chemically combined

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ When a non-metal in group 17 reacts with a non-metal from a different group

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ When a metal and a non-metal react with one another

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ When a noble gas reacts with the most reactive element in group 1

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Metals and non-metals have the greatest differences in electronegativity and so ionic bonds are formed.
