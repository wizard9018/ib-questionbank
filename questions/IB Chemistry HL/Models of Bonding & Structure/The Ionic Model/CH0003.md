# CH0003

Which of the following is incorrect?

| | Formula | Name |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
|$\hspace{1em}$ A.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\ce{PO^{3-}_3}$ | phosphate ion |
|$\hspace{1em}$ B. | $\ce{Mg^{2+}}$ | magnesium ion |
|$\hspace{1em}$ C. | $\quad\ce{CH_3COO^-}\quad$ | $\quad$ethanoate ion$\quad$ |
|$\hspace{1em}$ D. | $\ce{Fe^{3+}}$ | iron (III) ion |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
The correct formula of the phosphate ion is $\ce{PO^{3-}_4}$. The formulas and names of the other ions are correct.
