# CH0059

A white solid has a melting point of $2072\,{}^\circ \mathrm{C}$. It does not conduct electricity in the solid state, but does so in the molten state. Which type of structure is found in the solid material?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Metallic

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Simple molecular

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Ionic

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Giant molecular

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
 Ionic compounds have high melting points due to strong electrostatic forces of attraction between the oppositely charged ions.

Ionic compounds do not conduct electricity when solid as the ions are in fixed positions. When molten, the ions are free to move.
