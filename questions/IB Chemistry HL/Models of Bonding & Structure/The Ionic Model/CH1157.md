# CH1157

What is the name of $\ce{Al2(SO4)3}?$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$Aluminium sulfate

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$Aluminium sulfide

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$Aluminium sulfite

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$Aluminium (III) sulfide

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
$\ce{Al2(SO4)3}$ is made of $\ce{Al^3+}$ and $\ce{SO4^2-}.$

The cation should be named first. The Roman number III is unnecessary as aluminium has a unique oxidation state when combined, it is always $+3.$

$\ce{SO4^2-}$ is sulfate ($\ce{SO3^2-}$ is sulfite and $\ce{S^{2-}}$ is sulfide).

This question is categorized as medium difficulty, as students must identify the ions involved to name the ionic substance.
