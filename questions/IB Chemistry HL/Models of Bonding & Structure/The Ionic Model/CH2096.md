# CH2096

What is the formula of calcium phosphate(V)?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Ca3P2O5}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Ca3(PO3)2}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Ca3P2}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Ca3(PO4)2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

In this ionic compound, calcium forms a $+2$ cation and phosphate (V) is the anion $\ce{PO4^3-}.$ To balance charge, the formula for the compound is $\ce{Ca3(PO4)2}.$

The roman numeral (V) signifies the oxidation state of phosphorus in the anion. There are four oxygens with oxidation state $-2$ and an overall charge of $3-$ so,

$x + 4(-2) = -3\ ; \qquad  x = +5$
