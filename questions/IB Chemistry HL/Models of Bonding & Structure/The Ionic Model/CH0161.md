# CH0161

What is the correct order of decreasing lattice enthalpy?

- A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MgF2} > \ce{MgO} > \ce{LiF} > \ce{NaF}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MgF2} > \ce{MgO} > \ce{NaF} > \ce{LiF} $

- C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MgO} > \ce{MgF2} > \ce{NaF} > \ce{LiF}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MgO} > \ce{MgF2} > \ce{LiF }> \ce{NaF}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Lattice enthalpy is dependent on the charge of the constituent ions and their sizes. $\ce{Mg^2+}$ and $\ce{O^2-}$ have the highest charges and, therefore, the highest lattice enthalpy.

$\ce{Na+}$ has a larger ionic radius than $\ce{Li+}$ (with the same charge) and will form ionic compounds with lower lattice enthalpies.
