# CH2097

Which of the following ionic compounds is expected to have the highest melting point?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$NaF

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$NaBr

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$LiI

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$LiF

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Two factors affect the strength of ionic bonds: the charge of the ions and the distance between them.  This can be seen in Coulomb's Law which shows the force of the attraction between ions to be

$F \propto \dfrac{q_1q_2}{r^2}$

where $q_1$ is the charge of the cation, $q_2$ the charge of the anion, and $r$ the sum of the ionic radii, or the distance between the ions.

In these compounds, Group 1 and Group 17 atoms are combining so the charges are the same: 1+ for the cations and $1-$ for the anions.  Ionic size increases down the group, $r_\ce{Li+} < r_\ce{Na+}$ and $r_\ce{F-} < r_\ce{Br-} < r_\ce{I-}.$ The smallest distance will cause the greatest attraction between the ions: LiF has he highest melting point.

***Alternate approach:***\
Lattice enthalpy, $\Delta H_{latt},$ is the energy required to separate one mole of an ionic compound into its gaseous ions.The greater the value of the lattice enthalpy, the more energetically stable the crystal lattice structure and the higher the melting point.

Section 16 of the IB Chemistry data booklet provides the lattice enthalpies for ionic substances, particularly for Group 1 and Group 2 metal halides.

| Compound | $\mathbf{\Delta H_{latt}} \\ \mathbf{(kJ\ mol^{-1})}$ |
| :-: | :-: |
| NaF | 930 |
| NaBr | 754 |
| LiI | 764 |
| LiF | 1049 |

The compound with the highest lattice enthalpy is LiF. Since lattice enthalpy is directly related to the strength of ionic bonds, LiF is also the compound most likely to have the highest melting point.
