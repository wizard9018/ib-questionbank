# CH0058

Which properties are typical of ionic compounds?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  |     | Volatility | Solubility in polar solvents |
  | :-: | :-: | :-: |
  |A.$\hspace{1em}$ | High | Good |
  |B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Low  | Good |
  |C.$\hspace{1em}$ | High | Poor |
  |D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Low  | Poor |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Volatility refers to the ease at which a substance vaporizes. Ionic bonds are very strong and, therefore, a lot of energy is required to turn ionic compounds into gases.

Polar solvents are able to form electrostatic forces of attraction with ions.
