# CH0150

Which of the following reactions represents esterification?      

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$Methanol + ethanoic acid → methyl ethanoate + water

-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$Methane + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water

-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$Ethanol + propanoic acid → ethyl propanoate + water 

-   IV.$\hspace{0.5em}$None of the above

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$IV only

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$II only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

An ester and water are formed from the condensation reaction between a carboxylic acid and an alcohol in the presence of a catalyst, normally concentrated sulfuric acid.

Methanol + ethanoic acid → methyl ethanoate + water 

***esterification:*** $\ce{CH3OH} + \ce{CH3COOH} \longrightarrow \underset{\normalsize\textrm{ester}}{\ce{CH3COOCH3}} + \ce{H2O}$

Methane + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water 

***combustion:*** $\ce{CH4} + 2 \ce{\,O2} \longrightarrow \ce{CO2} + 2 \ce{\,H2O}$

Ethanol + propanoic acid → ethyl propanoate + water 

***esterification:*** $\ce{CH3CH2OH} + \ce{CH3CH2COOH} \longrightarrow \underset{\normalsize\textrm{ester}}{\ce{CH3CH2COOCH2CH3}} + \ce{H2O}$
