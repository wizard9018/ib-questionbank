# CH1000

Which statement about alloys is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Their structure is a lattice of cations and delocalized anions.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$They are solutions of metals.

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$They are stronger than pure metals because the atoms form covalent bonds with each other.

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$They do not contain nonmetals.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
An alloy is a homogeneous mixture of two or more elements in which the major component is a metal. The atoms of the added element occupy either regular lattice sites (substitutional alloy) or interstitial gaps (interstitial alloy) within the parent metal's metallic lattice. This makes an alloy a solid solution of metals, with constituent atoms distributed uniformly at the atomic level.

Metallic bonding does involve a lattice of positive metal ions held together by a sea of delocalised electrons, but those electrons are not anions. An anion is a negatively charged ion with its own nucleus.

Alloys are generally harder and stronger than the parent pure metal, but this is because the differently sized atoms disrupt the regular packing of layers and prevent them from sliding over one another. The bonding in an alloy remains metallic, not covalent.

Alloys can and do contain nonmetals. Steels contain carbon, stainless steels contain nitrogen, and titanium alloys often include small amounts of oxygen to strengthen the lattice.
