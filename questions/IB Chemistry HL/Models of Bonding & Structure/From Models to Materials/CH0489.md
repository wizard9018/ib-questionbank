# CH0489

**Maximum mark: 7**

Polyvinyl acetate (PVA) is a polymer commonly used in glues. A potential synthetic route to PVA is shown.

![20.2-CH0489](images/CH0489.png)

1.  PVC is synthesized via the addition polymerization of monomer **A**. Draw the structural formula of monomer **A**. [1 mark]

2.  PVC could also be synthesized from polyethene, as shown.

    ![20.2-CH0489-1](images/CH0489-1.png)

    1.  Suggest a reagent for this reaction and the reaction conditions required. [2 marks]

    2.  Suggest one way this reaction could produce polymers other than PVC. [1 mark]

3.  Polymer **B** is produced from PVC via a nucleophilic substitution reaction.

    1.  Draw the structural formula of the repeat unit in polymer **B**. [1 mark]

    2.  Name a reactant that could be used to produce polymer **B** from PVC. [1 mark]

4.  The final step of the reaction is an esterification. Name the reactant that could be used in this reaction. [1 mark]
