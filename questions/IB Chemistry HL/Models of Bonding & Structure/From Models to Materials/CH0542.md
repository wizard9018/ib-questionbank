# CH0542

Which statement regarding stainless steel, an important industrial alloy, is $\textbf{NOT}$ correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The alloy is more resistant to corrosion than iron

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The alloy is formed from a mixture of iron and zinc

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The alloy has different strength than its component elements

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The alloy is used for pots and pans for cooking due to its heat conductivity and low reactivity

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Stainless steel is a mixture of predominantly iron and chromium.
