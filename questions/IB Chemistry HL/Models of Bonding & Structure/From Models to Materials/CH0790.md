# CH0790

An ester **E** is synthesised from the condensation reaction (esterification) between 4-fluorohexanoic acid and methanol.

What is the structural formula of ester **E**?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The carboxylic acid 4$-$fluorohexanoic acid contains a chain of six carbon atoms. The first carbon atom is part of the carboxylic acid ($\ce{COOH}$) group and is labelled carbon 1.

The fluoro group is attached to carbon 4, so is separated from the COOH group by two carbon atoms.

When the ester is formed, the OH group of the carboxylic acid is replaced by the $\ce{OCH3}$ of methanol.
