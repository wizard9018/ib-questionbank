## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In an addition polymerisation, alkene C atoms are linked together to form a chain of $\ce{C-C}$ single bonds. The groups attached to the alkene C atoms become the side chains of the product polymer.

In 3-methylpent-2-ene, the $\ce{C=C}$ bond begins at position 2, i.e. between C atoms 2 and 3. The methyl ($\ce{CH3}$) group is attached to C atom 3. In the polymer backbone, one C atom is attached to a $\ce{CH3}$ group (C atom 1) and the neighbouring C atom is attached to a $\ce{CH2CH3}$ group (C atoms 4 and 5) and a $\ce{CH3}$ group (the 3-methyl group).

In a real polymer, the repeat units may not be linked in a regular pattern, as an alkene C atom in one molecule can bond to either of the alkene C atoms in another.
