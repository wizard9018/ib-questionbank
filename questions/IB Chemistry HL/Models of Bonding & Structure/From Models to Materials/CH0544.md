# CH0544

\[Maxium mark: 5\]

Alloys are considered a solid solution of two or more metals.  

1.  Describe the general properties of an elemental metal and how those properties can be altered in a specific alloy of that metal. [3 marks]

2.  In terms of metallic bonding, outline a justification for the change in the properties of an alloy compared to a pure metal. [2 marks]
