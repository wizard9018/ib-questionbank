# CH1065

The product of a condensation reaction has the following skeletal structural formula.

![CH1065_Image1](images/CH1065_Image1.png)

What are the IUPAC names of the reactants in this condensation reaction?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Butan-2-ol and 3-methyl butanoic acid

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Butan-2-ol and 2-methyl butanoic acid

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Butan-3-ol and 3-methyl butanoic acid

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Butan-3-ol and 2-methyl butanoic acid

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The condensation reaction produces an ester from an alcohol and a carboxylic acid.

The alcohol contains four carbon atoms in a single chain, so it must be an isomer of butanol. The position of the hydroxyl group is indicated by a number before the -ol suffix, representing the number of carbon atoms between the hydroxyl group and the nearest end of the chain. The alcohol is therefore butan-2-ol, not butan-3-ol.

The carboxylic acid contains a chain of four carbon atoms, so the compound is a derivative of butanoic acid. An additional methyl group is attached to the second carbon atom from the end of the chain. However, when numbering the position of the methyl group, the carbon atom of the carboxylic acid group is given the label $1$. Thus, the methyl group is in position $3$, and the compound is named 3-methylbutanoic acid, not 2-methylbutanoic acid.
