# CH1162

Which statement about aluminum nitride is correct?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$It can be classified as a pure covalent substance in the triangular bonding diagram

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$It can be classified as a highly ionic substance in the triangular bonding diagram

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$It has some covalent properties and some ionic characteristics

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$Its covalent percentage is near 20% according to the triangular bonding diagram

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Using  Section 9 in the Data Booklet, the electronegativity of aluminum is 1.6 and that of nitrogen is 3.0. To determine the position of a compound in the bonding triangle, we need these calculations:

$\text{Electronegativity difference} = 3.0 - 1.6 = 1.4$\
$\text{Average electronegativity} = \dfrac{(3.0 + 1.6)} {2} = 2.3$

We can see that AlN lies near the limit between the ionic and polar covalent regions in the van Arkel$-$Ketelaar triangle in Section 17. This substance has a partial ionic and a partial covalent character, nearly 50$-$50.
