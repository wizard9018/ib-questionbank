# CH1064

The structural formulas of compounds **X** and **Y** are shown.

![CH1064_Image1](images/CH1064_Image1.png)

A condensation reaction occurs when compounds **X** and **Y** are mixed.

Which row of the table is correct?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | Nucleophile | Electrophile | By-product |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | A. | X | Y | $\ce{NH3}$ |
    | B. | Y | X | $\ce{NH3}$ |
    | C. | X | Y | $\ce{HCl}$ |
    | D. | Y | X | $\ce{HCl}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The nucleophile is the reactant that donates an electron pair during the reaction, while the electrophile is the reactant that accepts the electron pair.

In this reaction, the non-bonding electron pair of the nitrogen atom is donated to the carbon atom of the acyl chloride $\ce{(-COCl)}$ group, so **X** is the nucleophile, and **Y** is the electrophile. The reaction produces an amide group, with HCl eliminated as a by-product:
