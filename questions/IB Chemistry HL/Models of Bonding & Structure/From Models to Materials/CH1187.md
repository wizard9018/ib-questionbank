# CH1187

Which monomer is missing in the formation of this condensation polymer?

![Screenshot 2025-10-07 at 2.31.39 PM](<images/Screenshot 2025-10-07 at 2.31.39 PM.png>)

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH2CH2}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH2OH}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{HOCH2CH2OH}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
A condensation polymer forms when two different monomers each have **two** reactive functional groups that can join together while eliminating a small molecule such as water. In this polyester, the two monomers must be ethanedioic acid $\ce{(HOOC-COOH)}$ and a diol, or molecule with two hydroxy groups.

![CH1187ms-2](images/CH1187ms-2.png)

The diol in this reaction is 1,2$-$ethanediol.
