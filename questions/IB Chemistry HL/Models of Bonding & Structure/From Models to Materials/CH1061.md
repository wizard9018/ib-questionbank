## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

An amido $\ce{-CONH-}$ group is produced by a condensation reaction between a carboxyl $\ce{(-COOH)}$ and amino $\ce{(-NH2)}$ group.

An ether $\ce{-COC-}$ group and is produced by a condensation reaction between two hydroxy $\ce{(-OH)}$ groups.

The polymer repeat unit contains one amide and one ether, so the monomers must together contain one carboxyl, one amino, and two hydroxy groups.

One C atom neighboring the amido group is attached to a methyl $\ce{(-CH3)}$ group, so one of the monomers must feature a methyl group next to an amino or carboxyl group.
