## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

When an alkene is polymerized, a radical initiator enables the double bond to break and link a second alkene to the original chain. The second alkene bond breaks and this molecule reacts with another alkene, and so on, forming a chain (the polymer). To identify the polymer repeating unit, the double bond is simply removed, and a bond is extended from each carbon of the original double bond. These extended bonds show where the repeating unit connects to another monomer.
