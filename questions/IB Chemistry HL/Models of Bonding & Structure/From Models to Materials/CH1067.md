# CH1067

In the production of butyl ethanoate, it is useful to remove water from the reaction mixture by distillation.

Why does distillation increase the yield of butyl ethanoate?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Concentrating the solution increases the rate at which reactants collide

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Concentrating the solution favors reactions that reduce the number of molecules

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Removal of a polar solvent lowers the solubility of the reactants

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Removal of a reaction product moves the position of equilibrium to the right

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The formation of the ester is a reversible reaction, as the product can react with water to regenerate the starting materials:

![CH1067_Image1](images/CH1067_Image1.png)

If water, the by-product of esterification is removed from the reaction mixture, the position of equilibrium moves to counteract the change in accordance with Le Chatelier's principle. Thus, the position of equilibrium moves to the right, increasing the yield of the reaction.

Removing water also concentrates the reaction mixture, but dilution affects the rates of both the forward and backward reactions, so it does not affect the ester yield.
