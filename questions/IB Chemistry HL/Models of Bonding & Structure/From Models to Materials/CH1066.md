# CH1066

Amylose is a polymeric sugar found in plants. It is produced via the condensation of polymerization of glucose, as shown:

![CH1066_Image1](images/CH1066_Image1.png)

What is the molar mass of an amylose molecule produced from *n* molecules of glucose?

$M_r (glucose) = 180.2\,\text{g mol}^{-1}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}180.2 n - 18.0 (n-1)$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}180.2 n - (2) (18.0) (n-1)$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}180.2 n - 18.0 (n-2)$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}180.2 n - (2) (18.0) (n-2)$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

When two molecules of glucose react to produce an ether group, one molecule of water is produced. Each additional molecule of glucose forms one more ether group and one more molecule of water.

Thus, if *n* is the number of glucose molecules reacted, the number of water molecules produced is $(n - 1)$.

The total mass is *n* times the molar mass of glucose $(180.2)$ minus $(n - 1)$ times the molar mass of water $(18.0)$.
