## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Expanation:}}$

The student must recognize that forming a condensation polymer requires each monomer to have two functional groups.  The carboxyl and amino groups in this molecule react to form an amido group and water.  This molecule is an example of a polyamide, or a polypeptide.

![CH2015ans](images/CH2015ans.png)

This question is categorized as hard as the student must determine the condensation between the $\ce{-COOH}$ and the $\ce{-NH2}$ to form the amide bond and successfully represent the repeat unit.
