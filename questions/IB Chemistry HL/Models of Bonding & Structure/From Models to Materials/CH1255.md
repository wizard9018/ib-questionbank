# CH1255

Synthetic polymers have been widely used since the Second World War, replacing traditional natural materials. For example, fabric coatings can be made with polychloroethene, a synthetic polymer known as polyvinylchloride (PVC).

Which statement is correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$PVC is made from one monomer, chloroethene, and it is classified as an addition polymer

- B. $\hspace{1em}$PVC is made from one monomer, chloroethene, and it is classified as a condensation polymer

- C. $\hspace{1em}$PVC is made from two monomers, chloro and ethene, and it is classified as an addition polymer

- D. $\hspace{1em}$PVC is made from two monomers, chloro and ethene, and it is classified as a condensation polymer

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Synthetic polymers are produced from monomers. They should understand how addition polymerization occurs.

The polymer is always made from a monomer. In this case, the monomer contains chlorine and is chloroethene. The addition polymerization is described below.

![CH1255](images/CH1255.png)

As shown above, the addition polymerization involves breaking the carbon-to-carbon double bond to form large chains. No water is lost in this process, as this is not a condensation polymerization.

Condensation polymerization occurs with the chemical reaction of two functional groups in the monomers that allow the release of a small molecule, such as water.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must figure out the name of a possible monomer and recall the addition polymerization process.
