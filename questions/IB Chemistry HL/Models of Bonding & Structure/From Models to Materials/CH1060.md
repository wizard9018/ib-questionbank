# CH1060

The structural formula of glycolic acid is shown.

![CH1060](images/CH1060.png)

In the presence of sulfuric acid, glycolic acid reacts to produce a polymer.

Which of the following statements are true?

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$The number of sulfuric acid molecules decreases during polymerization.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The number of water molecules increases during polymerization.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$The number of $\ce{C-O}$ bonds does not change during the polymerization.

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Glycolic acid is bifunctional: every molecule carries one hydroxyl group and one carboxyl group. When the $\ce{-OH}$ of one molecule reacts with the $\ce{-COOH}$ of another, they undergo a condensation reaction and form and ester linkage.

![CH1060reaction](images/CH1060reaction.png)

After this first link forms, each glycolic acid unit still has an unreacted functional group at its opposite end, so it can react again to extend the chain; repeated linking of thousands of units produces the polyester poly(glycolic acid).

![CH1060poly](images/CH1060poly.png)

The total number of $\ce{C-O}$ bonds is conserved: two $\ce{C-OH}$ bonds in the monomers are simply rearranged into one $\ce{C-O-C}$ bond in the polymer. The sulfuric acid catalyst is regenerated in every step, so it is neither consumed nor produced during the reaction.
