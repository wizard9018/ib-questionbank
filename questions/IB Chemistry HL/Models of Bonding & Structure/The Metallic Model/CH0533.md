# CH0533

Which of the following is **NOT** true of metals?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Metals tend to have higher ionization energies than nonmetals across the same period

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Metals have a small number of electrons in their valence energy level

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Metals are found on the left side of the periodic table

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Metals tend to form cations in ionic bonding

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Across the same period, metals have lower ionization energies than non-metals due to lower nuclear charges with the same number of shells (shielding effect).
