# CH2161

Which combination corresponds to a strong metallic bond?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2=hide}
    | || Charge of the metal ion | Radius of the ion |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-:
    | A. | $\hspace{0.em}$ | large | large |
    | B. || large | small |
    | C. || small | small |
    | D. || small | large |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

Metallic bonds arise from the attraction between the positively charged lattice of cations and the delocalized electron sea.  The strength of a metallic bond is determined by three main factors: the number of delocalized electrons, the charge of the metal cation and the radius of the metal cation.

Metallic bond strength increases with a higher ionic charge and decreases with a larger ionic radius. Thus, a strong metallic bond occurs when metal ions have a high positive charge and a small radius. A higher charge results in a stronger electrostatic attraction between the positively charged metal ions and the delocalized sea of electrons. A smaller radius concentrates the positive charge over a smaller area, strengthening the attraction even more.
