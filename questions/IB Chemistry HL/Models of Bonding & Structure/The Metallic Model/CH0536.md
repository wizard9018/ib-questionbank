# CH0536

Which is $\textbf{NOT}$ a characteristic property of metals resulting from metallic bonding?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Good thermal conductivity

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Low melting points

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Malleability

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Good electrical conductivity

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Metals characteristically have high melting points due to strong metallic bonds.
