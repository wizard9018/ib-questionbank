$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The metallic structure can be described as shown below.

![CH1231](images/CH1231.png)

Metals form a lattice of cations in a sea of delocalized electrons. As electrons are free to move, metals are good conductors of heat and electricity as solids or liquids.

In addition, their boiling point is usually high, as strong electrostatic attractions should be overcome to melt their structure.

This question is categorized as easy as different properties should be compared to deduce the metal.
