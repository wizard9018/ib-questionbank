# CH1513

Which of the following statements about metallic bonding is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Electrons are shared equally between metallic atoms.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Electrons are transferred from one atom to another.

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Electrons move freely throughout the structure.

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Electrons are localized between specific pairs of metallic atoms.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In metallic bonding electrons are neither shared nor localized. They are also not transferred between atoms. Instead they are delocalized and move freely within the metallic structure.
