# CH2175

Which factor contributes most to the strength of the metallic bond in transition metals?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Low ionization energy of $d$ block elements

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$High electronegativity of transition metals

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Delocalization of both $s$ and $d$ electrons in the metal lattice

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$High number of neutrons in the atomic nucleus

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The strength of the metallic bond is due to the electrostatic attraction between the metal cations and the sea of delocalized electrons. In transition metals, both $s$ and $d$ electrons are delocalized, increasing the electron density and strengthening the bond.
