# CH1186

Which of the following statements is correct about Zinc?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$It is a transition metal.

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$It has a high melting point.

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$It is a bad conductor of heat.

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$It can act as a catalyst due to the presence of unpaired electrons.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Transition metals are found in the $d-$block of the periodic table and are defined as the ones that have atoms or produce ions with incomplete d sublevels.

Zinc has an atomic number of 30.

Its electron configuration is $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6\,4s^2\,3d^{10}.$

Its ion is $\ce{Zn^2+},$ and its electron configuration is $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6\,3d^{10}.$

None of these two present incomplete d sublevels, so Zn is not a transition metal. Option A is incorrect.

Zinc is a metal, though; like most metals, it has a high melting point. Its structure is described as a lattice of cations with free and delocalized electrons. The electrostatic forces of attraction between cations and electrons are the reason behind the high melting point of most metals. Option B is correct.

As mentioned above, metals have delocalized electrons in their lattices and are good conductors of heat and electricity. Option C is incorrect.

Finally, Zinc does not present unpaired electrons (Neither as Zn or $\ce{Zn^2+}),$ so option D is incorrect.

This question is categorized as hard as students must understand what transition metals mean and be able to recall some metallic properties.
