# CH0539

Which statements regarding metallic bonding are correct?

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$The higher the charge on the ion of a metallic element, the weaker the bond.

-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$A metallic element with a greater ionic radius will have a weaker metallic bonding. 

-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$Metallic bond strength increases with the number of delocalized electrons. 

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$I only

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$II only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Greater ionic radii result in weaker electrostatic forces of attraction between the nuclei of the positive metal ions and the delocalized electrons.
