# CH0537

Based on periodic trends, which metallic element would be expected to have the highest boiling point?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$Lithium

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$Beryllium

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Potassium

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Calcium

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Beryllium forms ions with the highest charge density as it has a 2+ charge and the smallest ionic radius (compared to the 1+ charge of potassium and lithium, and the two additional shells of calcium). The greater the charge density, the stronger the metallic bonds and the higher the melting/boiling points.
