# CH0543

**Maximum mark: 7**

Metallic bonding characterizes the bonding present in the elemental form of the metals on the periodic table.

1.  Describe features of metallic bonding. [2 marks]

2.  Describe 3 common properties of metals that result from metallic bonding. [3 marks]

3.  Solid sodium chloride is brittle while sodium metal is not. Explain this difference in properties. [2 marks]
