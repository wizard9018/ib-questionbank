# CH0534

Metallic bonding is best characterized by the following statement:

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Valence electrons of a metallic element are shared between the adjacent positive ions

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Valence electrons of a metallic element are lost by the nuclei to become ions

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Valence electrons of a metallic element are localized, remaining closely associated with a positive lattice of ions

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Valence electrons of a metallic element are delocalized and held loosely by a positive lattice of ions

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Recall of description of metallic bonding as positive ions surrounded by delocalized electrons.
