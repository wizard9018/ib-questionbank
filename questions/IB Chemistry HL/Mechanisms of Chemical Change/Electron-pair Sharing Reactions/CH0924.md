# CH0924

An alcohol can be produced by two synthetic routes, as shown.

![CH0924_Image1](images/CH0924_Image1.png)

Which of the following statements are true?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Reactions 1 and 3 are both substitutions.|
  | II. | Reactions 2 and 3 both involve tertiary carbocation intermediates.|
  | III. | All of the reactions involve more than one elementary step. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\
Reactions 1 and 3 are both substitutions because they involve two reactants and two products. In reaction 1, one H atom in the alkane is replaced with a Cl atom from $\ce{Cl2}$ via a free radical substitution reaction, producing HCl as a byproduct.

In reaction 3, the $\ce{C-Cl}$ bond breaks and the C atom forms a new bond with the O atom of the hydroxide ion, producing a chloride ion as a by-product.

Reaction 3 occurs via an $S_N 1$ mechanism, because the alcohol is tertiary: the $\ce{C-Cl}$ bond breaks to produce a carbocation intermediate, which then reacts with the nucleophile.

Reaction 2 also involves a carbocation intermediate: the $\ce{C=C}$ bond breaks as the $\ce{C-H}$ bond is formed, and the chloride ion from $\ce{H-Cl}$ then reacts with the positively charged tertiary carbon atom.

Since they involve intermediates, reactions 2 and 3 both occur via two elementary steps. Reaction 1 is also a multistep process. In the initiation step, the $\ce{Cl-Cl}$ bond breaks to produce $\ce{Cl}\text{\char"A78F}$ radicals. In the propagation step, a $\ce{Cl}\text{\char"A78F}$ radical reacts with the alkane to produce the $\ce{(CH3)3C}\text{\char"A78F}$ radical and HCl. Finally, in the termination step, the $\ce{(CH3)3C}\text{\char"A78F}$ and $\ce{Cl}\text{\char"A78F}$ radicals react to produce the chloroalkane.
