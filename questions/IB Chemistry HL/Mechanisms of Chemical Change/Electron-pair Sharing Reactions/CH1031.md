# CH1031

**Maximum mark: 4**

The nucleophilic substitution of iodoethane with hydroxide ions is described below:

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{CH3CH2I + OH- -> CH3CH2OH + I-}$

1.  Explain why $\ce{OH-}$ acts as a nucleophile. [1 mark]

2.  Draw, using curly arrows to show the movement of electron pairs, the mechanism for this nucleophilic substitution reaction. [2 marks]

3.  State the name of the alcohol produced. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Hydroxide ion donates electron pairs. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    

    The student must understand that the hydroxide ion is an electron-rich reactant that forms a bond with its reaction partner (iodoethane) by donating electrons. The hydroxide ion is electron-rich due to non-bonding electrons in its structure.

The hydroxide forms a new bond through its nonbonding electrons (as shown in the diagram above), and the $\ce{C-I}$ bond breaks up in heterolytic fission so that iodide forms (the leaving group).
