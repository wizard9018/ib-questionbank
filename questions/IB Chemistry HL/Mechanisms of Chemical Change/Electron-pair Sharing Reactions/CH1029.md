# CH1029

Consider the reaction between chloropropane and hydroxide ions.

$\ce{CH3CH2CH2Cl + OH- -> CH3CH2OH + Cl-}$

Which species acts as a leaving group?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH2CH2Cl}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{OH-}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Cl-}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH2CH2OH}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\ce{Cl-}$ is the leaving group. The chlorine breaks its bond with carbon (in heterolytic fission), producing chloride as a leaving group.
