# CH1556

Which of these compounds would react most readily in an $S_N2$ mechanism?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}2\text{--}$bromo$\text{--}$2$\text{--}$methylbutane

-   B.$\hspace{1em}1\text{--}$bromobutane

-   C.$\hspace{1em}2\text{--}$chloro$\text{--}$2$\text{--}$methylbutane

-   D.$\hspace{1em}1\text{--}$chlorobutane

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\

The student must understand the difference between $S_N1$ and $S_N2.$ $S_N1$ is an unimolecular nucleophilic substitution favoured for tertiary halogenoalkanes where the carbocation intermediate is stabilized by the positive inductive effect. $S_N2$ is a bimolecular nucleophilic substitution favoured by primary halogenoalkanes where there is ample space to form the five$\text{--}$member transition state.

The rate of the nucleophilic substitution depends on breaking the $\ce{C-X}$  bond, where X represents a halogen. The halogen has an effect on the rate of nucleophilic substitution as the $\ce{C-F}$ bond is harder to break than the $\ce{C-Cl}$ bond, etc. If we compare $\ce{C-Cl}$ and $\ce{C-Br},$ $\ce{C-Br}$ is easier to break, and the substitution will occur faster.

As a result, we must choose the primary halogenoalkane substituted with bromine.

![CH1556v2](images/CH1556v2.png)

So, 1$\text{--}$bromobutane is the correct option.

This question is categorized as a medium as the student must understand the reactant that favours $S_N1$ and $S_N2,$ be able to work out the structural formulas to figure out the nature of the halogenoalkane (primary or tertiary), and recall the effect of the halogen on the rate of substitution.
