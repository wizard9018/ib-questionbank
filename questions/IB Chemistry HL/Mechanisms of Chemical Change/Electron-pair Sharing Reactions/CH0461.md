The Brønsted$-$Lowry and the Lewis theories are two different theories of classifying acids and bases.

1. 

    1. Distinguish between the Brønsted$-$Lowry theory and the Lewis theory. [1 mark]

    2. Define incomplete octet. [1 mark]

Consider the dimerization of $\ce{AlCl3}$:

 $\,\ce{2AlCl3 -> Al2Cl6}$

2. 

    1. Draw structural formulas to represent this reaction. [2 marks]

    2. Explain how this is an acid$-$base reaction under the Lewis theory but not the Brønsted$-$Lowry theory. [2 marks]

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        The aluminium atoms have an incomplete octet (six valence electrons), and so, can accept a lone pair of electrons from the chlorine atoms.

    2.  This is not an acid-base reaction under the Brønsted-Lowry theory because there are no H atoms so there is no transfer of protons. $\checkmark$

        It is, however, an acid-base reaction under the Lewis theory because $\ce{AlCl3}$ has an incomplete octet, so it can accept a lone pair of electrons (Lewis acid) from one of the Cl atoms on the other molecule (Lewis base). $\checkmark$
