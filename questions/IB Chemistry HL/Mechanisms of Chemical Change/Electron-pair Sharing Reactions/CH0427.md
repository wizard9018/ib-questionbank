# CH0427

The first step of a nucleophilic substitution reaction is shown.

![20.1-CH0427](images/CH0427.png)

Which of the following statements is true?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The reaction is a heterolytic fission and the curly arrow indicates transfer of 1 electron

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The reaction is a heterolytic fission and the curly arrow indicates transfer of 2 electrons

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The reaction is a homolytic fission and the curly arrow indicates transfer of 1 electron

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The reaction is a homolytic fission and the curly arrow indicates transfer of 2 electrons

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Heterolytic fission is the unequal splitting of the covalent bond where one atom keeps both electrons from the bond. A curly arrow represents the movement of a pair of electrons. A fish hook arrow would show the movement of a single electron.

The student must recall the definition of heterolytic fission and understand the meaning of the curly arrows used in mechanisms. The question is of moderate or hard difficulty as the student must recall two details and recognize mechanistic details which rarely appear in the syllabus.
