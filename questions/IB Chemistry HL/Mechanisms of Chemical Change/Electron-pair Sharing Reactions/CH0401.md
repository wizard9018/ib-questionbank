# CH0401

Which gives the correct formula of the complex formed between $\ce{Fe^3+}$ and the hexadentate (six$-$coordinate) $\ce{EDTA^4-}$ ligand?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{[Fe(EDTA)]^3+}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{[Fe(EDTA)6]}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$ Fe(EDTA)

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{[Fe(EDTA)]^1-}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
One EDTA ligand will form six coordinate bonds with the central  $\ce{Fe^3+}$ ion. The charges combine leading to a complex with an overall $1-$ charge.
