# CH1204

Consider the chemical reaction below:

$\ce{AlCl3 + Cl2 -> AlCl4- + Cl+}$

Which statement is correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$Aluminium acts as a Lewis acid and a coordinate bond is formed between aluminium and chlorine in $\ce{AlCl4-}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$Aluminium acts as a Lewis base and a coordinate bond is formed between aluminium and chlorine in $\ce{AlCl4-}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}$Aluminium acts as a Lewis acid and an ionic is formed between aluminium and chlorine in $\ce{AlCl4-}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$Aluminium acts as a Lewis base and an ionic is formed between aluminium and chlorine in $\ce{AlCl4-}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Lewis acids are electron-pair acceptors (as they are electron deficient). They usually form coordination bonds with Lewis bases (electron-rich /electron-donor species).

In this case the reaction can be described as shown below.

![CH1204](images/CH1204.png)

Aluminium forms a covalent coordinate bond with chlorine when $\ce{AlCl4-}$ is formed. Aluminium (acting as a Lewis acid) accepts electrons from chlorine (acting as a Lewis base).

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students should have a deep understanding of Lewis acids and bases, apply their knowledge to a specific reaction, and identify the formation of coordinate bonds.
