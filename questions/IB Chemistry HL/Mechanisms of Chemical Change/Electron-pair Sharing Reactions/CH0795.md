# CH0795

Compound **C** is produced from ethane in three reaction steps. The conditions used in the three steps are shown.

*$\ce{K2Cr2O7}$ and $\ce{H2SO4}$ are oxidizing agents in step 3.*

![CH0795](images/CH0795.png)

Which row of the table is correct?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  | | HCl is produced | The product is distilled |
  | :-: | :-: | :-: |
  |A.$\hspace{1em}$| Step 1 | Step 2 |
  |B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Step 2 | Step 2 |
  |C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Step 1 | Step 3 |
  |D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Step 2 | Step 3 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Step 1 is a free radical substitution, converting the alkane to a chloroalkane and producing HCl as a by-product.

Step 2 is a nucleophilic substitution, in which the $\ce{C-Cl}$ bond breaks and is replaced by a hydroxyl ($\ce{C-OH}$) group; the products are an alcohol and sodium chloride.

Step 3 is an oxidation. To prevent the aldehyde from being oxidised further to a carboxylic acid, the product must be removed by distillation as it forms.
