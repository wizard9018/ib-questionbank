# CH2029

What is the major product of the reaction of HCl with $3\text{--methylpent--}2\text{--ene}?$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\text{1--chloro--3--methylpentane}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\text{3--chloro--2--methylpentane}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\text{3--chloro--3--methylpentane}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\text{2--chloro--3--methylpentane}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student may consider the mechanism for the electrophilic addition of HCl with $\text{3--methylpent--2--ene}$ comparing the stability of the carbocation intermediates or apply Markovnikov's rule.

Markovnikov's rule states that the hydrogen will add to the carbon with more hydrogens. Drawing out the full structural formula of $\text{3--methylpent--2--ene}$ shows that carbon 2 has one hydrogen and carbon 3 has no hydrogens.

![CH2029struct](images/CH2029struct.png)

The chlorine will add to carbon 3 and the hydrogen will add to carbon 2. The product is $\text{3--chloro--3--methylpentane}.$

![CH2029structans](images/CH2029structans.png)

Alternatively, the electrophilic addition mechanism allows the formation of two carbocations. The tertiary carbocation is more stabilized than the secondary due to the positive inductive effect. As a result, the major product is obtained through the tertiary carbocation.

![CH2029mechans](images/CH2029mechans.png)

The major product is $\text{3--chloro--3--methylpentane}.$

This question is categorized as medium as the student must deduce the major product of an asymmetric electrophilic addition reaction.
