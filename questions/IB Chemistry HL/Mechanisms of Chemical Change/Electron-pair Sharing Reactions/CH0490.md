# CH0490

**Maximum mark: 5**

Lactic acid is released by muscles during strenuous exercise. The structural formula of lactic acid is shown.

![CH0490](images/CH0490.png)

Lactic acid can also be produced synthetically. One synthetic route uses propene as a starting material, and consists of four reaction steps.

1.  In the first step of the reaction, propene is reacted with bromine.
Draw the structural formula of the product.[2 marks]

2.  For the second, third and fourth steps show structural formulae for the reactants and products and name the type of reaction that is occurring. [3 marks]

A mild reducing agent is then chosen athat will reduce the ketone back to the alcohol but will not reduce the carboxylic acid.
