## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\
In the transition state, a $\ce{C-C}$ bond is forming while a $\ce{C-Cl}$ bond is breaking. The nucleophile reacts directly with the electrophile in a single reaction step. The reaction is therefore a bimolecular nucleophilic substitution $(S_\ce{N}2)$ reaction.

![CH0922ms](images/CH0922ms.png)

The $\ce{C-C}$ bond forms from a lone pair on the cyanide $\ce{C}$ atom, and the $\ce{C-Cl}$ bond breaks when the bonding electron pair is transferred to the $\ce{Cl}$ atom to produce a chloride ion.

The transfer of an electron pair is indicated by a full curly arrow. The half arrow, or fishhook arrow, indicates the transfer of a single electron, as in a free radical reaction.
