$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{C4H8 + Br2 -> C4H8Br2}$

1. Explain why $\ce{Br2}$ acts as an electrophile. [1 mark]

2. State the name of the product formed. [1 mark]

3. Using the structural formulas of reactants and products, represent the electrophilic addition of bromine to but$-$2$-$ene. [1 mark]

4. Name the product formed in the chemical reaction in (c). [1 mark]
