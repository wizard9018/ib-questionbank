# CH1026

Consider the reaction between ethene and hydrogen bromide:

$\ce{C2H4 + HBr -> C2H5Br}$

Which species acts as an electrophile?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{C2H4}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{HBr}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{C2H5Br}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Br-}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The student must recognize $\ce{HBr}$ as a neutral electrophile. It forms a covalent bond with ethene by accepting both electrons.

This question is categorized as medium as the student must recall the electrophile concept and is expected to apply it in this specific electrophilic addition.
