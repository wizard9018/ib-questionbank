# CH0394

Which of the below gives the correct charges of the ligand and the overall complex for the $\ce{Fe^3+}$ salt, $\ce{Na3[Fe(C2O4)3]}$?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  | | $\enskip$Charge of $\ce{C2O4}$ ligand$\enskip$ | $\enskip$Charge of $\ce{[Fe(C2O4)3]}$ complex ion$\enskip$ |
  | :-: | :-: | :-: |
  | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | $2-$ | $3-$ |
  | B.$\hspace{1em}$ | $2-$ | $3+$ |
  | C.$\hspace{1em}$ | $6-$ | $3-$ |
  | D.$\hspace{1em}$ | $3-$ | $3+$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Sodium is a group one metal, and so forms $1+$ ions. This leads to an overall complex charge of $3-$ to obtain a neutral compound.

As Fe has a $3+$ charge, the total ligand charge must be $6-$. There are 3 ligands, each having a charge of $2-$.
