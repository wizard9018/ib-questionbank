# CH1273

Which statements about the nitration of benzene are correct?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | $\ce{NO2+}$ acts as an electrophile  |
  | II. | A mixture of sulfuric and concentrated nitric acid is used |
  | III. | It is an electrophilic addition reaction |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The reaction between benzene and nitronium $(\ce{NO2+})$ in nitration is shown below.

$\ce{H2SO4 + HNO3 -> HSO4- + H2NO3+}$

$\ce{H2NO3+ -> H2O + NO2+}$

$\ce{C6H6 + NO2+ -> C6H5NO2 + H+}$

$\ce{NO2+}$ is the electrophile, and it is formed with a mixture of $\ce{H2SO4}$ and $\ce{HNO3(c)}.$ Statements I and II are correct.

This reaction is classified as an electrophilic substitution. So, statement III is incorrect.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must identify the type of reaction and the conditions required for the nitration to occur as the electrophile.
