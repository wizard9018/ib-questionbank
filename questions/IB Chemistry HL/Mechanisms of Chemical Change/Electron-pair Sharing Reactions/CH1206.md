# CH1206

Which statement for the chemical reaction between 3-methyl pent-2-ene and hydrogen bromide is correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$3-bromo-3-methyl pentane is the major product, and a tertiary carbocation is involved in its formation

- B. $\hspace{1em}$3-bromo-3-methyl pentane is the major product, and a secondary carbocation is involved in its formation

- C. $\hspace{1em}$2-bromo-3-methyl pentane is the major product, and a tertiary carbocation is involved in its formation

- D. $\hspace{1em}$2-bromo-3-methyl pentane is the major product, and a secondary carbocation is involved in its formation
