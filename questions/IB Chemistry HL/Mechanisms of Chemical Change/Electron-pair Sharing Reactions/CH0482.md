# CH0482

Which of the following best describes the route for converting compound **X** to compound **Y**?

![20.2-CH0482](images/CH0482.png)

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The oxidation of **X** is followed by a reduction and then a nucleophilic substitution

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The reduction of **X** is followed by an oxidation and then a nucleophilic substitution

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The nucleophilic substitution of **X** is followed by an oxidation and then a reduction

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The nucleophilic substitution of **X** is followed by a reduction and then an oxidation

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The student must correctly identify the reaction type of each synthetic step and remember to reverse the order of steps in the retro-synthesis to obtain the synthetic route.

The question is at the lower end of the “hard” category due to the number of reactions that must be considered and the potential for confusion when interpreting a retro-synthesis.

The steps in retro-synthesis must be reversed to obtain the synthetic route. The chloroalkane first reacts with $\ce{NaOH}$ in a nucleophilic substitution, the resultant primary and secondary alcohols are oxidized, followed by a reduction of the ketone.
