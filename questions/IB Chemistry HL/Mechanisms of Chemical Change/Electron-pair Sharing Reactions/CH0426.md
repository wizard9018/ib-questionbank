$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The transition state has an overall negative charge, with weak partial bonds (dotted lines) to both the incoming nucleophile and the leaving group.

This question is hard as the structures of the reactants and products are not given, and the student must recall the structural details of an $S_N2$ transition state.
