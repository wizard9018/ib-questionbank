## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In the rate-determining step of this electrophilic addition reaction, the $\ce{C=C}$ bond breaks and a $\ce{C-Br}$ bond is formed.

The alkene group reacts with the Br atom, rather than the Cl atom, because the Cl atom is more electronegative than the Br atom.

The Cl atom attracts the bonding electrons in the $\ce{Br-Cl}$ bond more strongly towards itself, gaining a $\delta^-$ charge and producing a $\delta^+$ charge on the Br atom. The $\delta^+$ Br atom is electrostatically attracted to the bonding electron density of the $\ce{C=C}$ bond, and therefore acts as the electrophile.
