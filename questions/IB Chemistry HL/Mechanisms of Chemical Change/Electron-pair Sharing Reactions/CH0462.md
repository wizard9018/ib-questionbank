# CH0462

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the following reaction: 

$\ce{Co^2+ (aq) + 6H2O (l) -> [Co(H2O)6]^2+ (aq)}$

1.  

    1.  Identify the nucleophile and electrophile in this reaction. [2 marks]

    

    2.  State the type of bond and species formed in this reaction. [2 marks]

2.  Explain why this can be considered an acid-base reaction. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  

    1.  Nucleophile: $\ce{H2O}$ $\checkmark$

        Electrophile: $\ce{Co^2+}$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        $\ce{H2O}$ is an electron-pair donor,  and $\ce{Co^2+}$ is an electron pair acceptor.

    2.  Coordination bond $\checkmark$

        

        Complex ion $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        The $\ce{H2O}$ molecules form bonds by donating a pair of electrons to the central metal ion.

2.  $\ce{Co^2+}$ accepts a lone pair from the $\ce{H2O}$ meaning it acts as a Lewis acid. $\checkmark$

    $\ce{H2O}$ donates a lone pair to the $\ce{Co^2+}$ ion meaning it acts as a Lewis base. $\checkmark$ (Hence, this is an acid-base reaction.)
