# CH0432

**Maximum mark: 8**

Nitration reactions often involve the formation of the nitronium ion, $\ce{NO2+}$. The structure of the nitronium ion is shown.

![20.1-CH0432](images/CH0432.png)

1.  State the reagents used to generate the nitronium ion. [1 mark]

2.  The formation of the nitronium ion involves the intermediate $X$.

    The Lewis structure of intermediate $X$ is shown.

    ![20.1-CH0432-1](images/CH0432-1.png)

    Draw **two** curly arrows to show how the nitronium ion forms from intermediate X. [1 mark]

3.  The nitronium ion can react with toluene to produce 4-nitrotoluene.

    ![CH0432-2](images/CH0432-2.png)

    Draw the mechanism of this nitration reaction. [4 marks]

4.  The reduction of 4-nitrotoluene requires two reaction steps. The product of the first step is compound $Y$.

    ![20.1-CH0432-3](images/CH0432-3.png)

    Draw the structure of compound $Y$. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Nitric acid **OR** $\ce{HNO3}$;

    *AND* sulfuric acid **OR** $\ce{H2SO4}\,. \checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    $\ce{HNO3 + H2SO4 -> NO2+ + HSO4- + H2O}$

2.  Arrow drawn from lone pair of $\ce{O-}$ to $\ce{N-O-}$ bond;

    *AND* arrow drawn from $\ce{N-OH2+}$ bond to $\ce{O}$ of $\ce{OH2+}$ leaving group. $\checkmark$

    ![20.1-CH0432a](images/CH0432a.png)
    

    *Do not award [1] if start and end positions of arrows are ambiguous.*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    Curly arrows indicate the movement of a pair of electrons from an electron rich to an electron poor area.

3.  Arrow from benzene ring pi system to $\ce{N}$ of nitronium ion; $\checkmark$

    Arrow from $\ce{N=O}$ bond to $\ce{O}\,; \checkmark$

    Arrow from $\ce{C-H}$ bond to benzene ring OR pi system; $\checkmark$

    Carbocation intermediate correctly drawn with positive charge and partial pi system; $\checkmark$

    ![20.1-CH0432b](images/CH0432b.png)
    

    *Accept Kekulé structure for benzene ring;\
    Accept answers with $\ce{CH3}$ group omitted;\
    Do not accept arrows with ambiguous start or end positions;\
    Do not award M4 if charge omitted or complete pi system drawn.*

4.  Benzene ring with molecular formulas of substituents correct; $\checkmark$

    Ammonium group with positive charge; $\checkmark$

    ![20.1-CH0432c](images/CH0432c.png)
    

    *Accept $\ce{-NH3Cl}$ with no charges drawn;\
    Accept $\ce{-NH3}$ with positive charge and no counterion;\
    Accept Kekulé structure for benzene ring;\
    Award $[1]$ if $\ce{-NH2}$ drawn;\
    Award [1] if $\ce{CH3}$ omitted but diagram otherwise correct*.

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    $\ce{Sn}$ and $\ce{HCl}$ are used to reduce nitrobenzene to the phenylammonium ion. $\ce{NaOH}$ must the be added to deprotonate the ion and produce the amine.
