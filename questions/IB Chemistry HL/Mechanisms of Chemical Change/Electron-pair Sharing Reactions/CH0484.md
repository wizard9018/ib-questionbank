## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Propene would first form 2-chloropropane via the more stable secondary carbocation intermediate. This is followed by nucleophilic substitution to form propan-2-ol and finally esterification with propanoic acid.

The student must recall Markovnikov's rule to deduce the regioselectivity of the first step, and then determine how this selectivity is reflected in the structure of the final product. The structural formula of propanoic acid must also be identified correctly.

The question is at the higher end of the hard-difficulty category as it requires good mechanistic knowledge in addition to recall of functional group transformations.
