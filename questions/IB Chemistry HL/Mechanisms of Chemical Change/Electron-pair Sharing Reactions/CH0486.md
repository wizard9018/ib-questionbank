# CH0486

In the first step of a three$-$step synthetic route, ethane is reacted with chlorine gas under ultraviolet light. The resulting compound is heated under reflux with aqueous sodium hydroxide. In the final step, acidified potassium dichromate(VI), an oxidizing agent, is added, and the initial product of the reaction is isolated by distillation.

Which of the following reaction routes best matches this experimental procedure?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Ethane is an alkane and reacts with chlorine in the presence of UV light to form a halogenoalkane in a free radical substitution reaction. The chloroalkane reacts with $\ce{NaOH}$ to form an alcohol in a nucleophilic substitution reaction. The alcohol produced is a primary alcohol and, when oxidized in a distillation apparatus, only undergoes partial oxidation to the aldehyde (and not complete oxidation to the carboxylic acid).

The student must convert the written experimental procedure into equations involving structural formulas and correctly identify the products of steps 1, 2, and 3 as a chloroalkane, an alcohol, and an aldehyde, respectively (note that step 3 does not produce a carboxylic acid due to the use of distillation).

The need to recall three functional group transformations and recognize the importance of distillation in the final step places this question in the “hard” category.
