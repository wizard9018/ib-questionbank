## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The intermediate of the reaction contains a $\ce{C-Br}$ bond as $\ce{Br}$ is less electronegative than $\ce{Cl}$, so is attacked more readily by the electron-rich $\ce{C=C}$ group. The secondary carbocation is more stable, resulting in the product shown.

The true mechanism involves a triangular bromonium intermediate but a simplified carbocation version is described in the IB syllabus. The question is “hard” as the student must understand the reactivity of interhalogens (not discussed elsewhere in the course), recall multiple details of the electrophilic addition mechanism and work out which of the two carbocation intermediates is most stable.
