# CH1051

Both 1$-$bromopropane and 2$-$bromopropane can be produced from the reaction between propene and hydrogen bromide.  Which of the statements below about this reaction are correct?

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$It is an addition reaction.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The major product is 2$-$bromopropane.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$The electrons from the $\ce{C=C}$ bond in propene react with the hydrogen in $\ce{H-Br}.$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$I only

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The reaction is an addition reaction as the $\ce{C=C}$ double bond of an alkene is converted into a $\ce{C-C}$ single bond. Statement I is correct.

The electrophilic addition mechanism behind this chemical reaction is shown:

![CH1051ms](images/CH1051ms.png)

The $\ce{C=C}$ bond acts as the nucleophile and the partially positive hydrogen acts as the electrophile.  Statemet III is correct.

The mechanism depicted shows the carbocation formation in the first step. The secondary carbocation is shown as it is the major product due to its stability (primary carbocations are less stable than secondary carbocations). As a result, the 2$-$bromopropane is the major product. Statement II is correct.

This question is categorized as hard as students must recall electrophilic additions and correctly assign stabilities for possible carbocations and the major product of the chemical reaction.
