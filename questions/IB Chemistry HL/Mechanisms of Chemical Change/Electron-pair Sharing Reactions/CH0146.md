# CH0146

Which of the following reagents is used to differentiate between $\ce{C3H8}$ and $\ce{C3H6}$ and what change of color would be observed?               

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{Br2(aq)}$; change from brown color to colorless

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Water; change from white to brown color

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{AgNO3(aq)}$; change from colorless to brown color

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{NH3(aq)}$; change from colorless to pink color

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

$\ce{C3H8}$ is a saturated alkane and $\ce{C3H6}$ is an unsaturated alkene. The test of unsaturation using $\ce{Br2(aq)}$ can demonstrate the presence or absence of a carbon-carbon double bond.

In the presence of a double bond, $\ce{Br2(aq)}$ can add across the double bond in the alkene producing a dibromo-alkane, and a change of color from brown to colorless will be observed.

In the case of the absence of a double bond (saturated hydrocarbon), no color change would occur.
