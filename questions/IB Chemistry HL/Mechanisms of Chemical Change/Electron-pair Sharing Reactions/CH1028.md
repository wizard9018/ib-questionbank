# CH1028

Consider the reaction between 2,2-dimethyl-chloroethane and hydroxide ions.

$\ce{(CH3)3CCl + OH- -> (CH3)3COH + Cl-}$

Which species acts as a nucleophile?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3.}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{OH-}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Cl-}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{(CH3)3CCl}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\ce{OH-}$ is a negatively charged, electron-rich species that acts as a nucleophile. The hydroxide ion is attracted to groups which can accept electron pairs.

In this case, it is attracted to the carbon next to the chlorine ($\ce{C-Cl}$) in the nucleophilic substitution, as shown below. This carbon presents a partial positive ($\delta^+$) charge due to bond polarity in the $\ce{C-Cl}$ bond.
