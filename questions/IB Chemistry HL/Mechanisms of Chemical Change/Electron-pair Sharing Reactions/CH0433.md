# CH0433

**Maximum mark: 6**

A chloroalkane reacts with sodium iodide in propanone, a polar aprotic solvent.

![20.1-CH0433](images/CH0433.png)

The reaction occurs via an $S_N2$ mechanism and generates sodium chloride as a solid byproduct.

1.  Name a technique that could be used to separate the sodium chloride byproduct from the reaction mixture. [1 mark]

2.  Draw the structure of product $A$, using wedge$-$dash notation. [2 marks]

3.  When the polar protic solvent formamide is used instead of propanone, an $S_N1$ reaction mechanism is favoured. Draw the mechanism for the $S_N1$ reaction of the chloroalkane with sodium iodide. [3 marks]
