# CH0454

What feature must all Lewis bases have?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$An incomplete octet

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$A lone pair of electrons

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$A negative charge

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$A neutral charge

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Lewis bases are electron pair donors and so, must have a lone (non-bonding) pair of electrons.
