# CH0151

Which type of reaction is shown below?

$\ce{C6H6 + Br2 ->[FeBr3] C6H5Br + HBr}$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$Electrophilic substitution of benzene

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$Addition reaction of benzene

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$Nucleophilic substitution of benzene

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$Substitution of hexane

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

$\ce{C6H6}$ is the formula for benzene thus ruling out choice D.

This is a substitution reaction as an H atom on the benzene is being replaced by a Br atom. $\ce{Br2}$ (in the presence of a $\ce{FeBr3}$ catalyst) acts as an electrophile as it can accept a pair of electrons from the electron$-$dense $\pi$ electrons of benzene.

Electron$-$poor electrophiles are attracted to the $\pi$ electrons in the aromatic benzene ring.
