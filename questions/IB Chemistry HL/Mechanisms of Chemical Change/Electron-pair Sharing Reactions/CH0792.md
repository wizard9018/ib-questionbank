# CH0792

Compound **B** has the following skeletal structural formula.

![CH0792](images/CH0792.png)

When compound **B** reacts with a hydroxide ion, the $\ce{C-Br}$ bond is broken.  Which of the following statements are true?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Compound **B** is a secondary bromoalkane and acts as a nucleophile

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Compound **B** is a secondary bromoalkane and acts as an electrophile

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Compound **B** is a tertiary bromoalkane and acts as a nucleophile

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Compound **B** is a tertiary bromoalkane and acts as an electrophile

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
In this nucleophilic substitution reaction, the hydroxide donates a lone pair to break the C-Br bond and form a new C-O bond. The C atom of compound **B** accepts the electron pair, so is an electrophile.

The compound is a secondary bromoalkane, as the C atom of the C-Br bond is attached to two C atoms.
