## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

1-bromobutane is a primary halogenoalkane. Its reaction with NaOH is a bimolecular nucleophilic substitution, as shown below.

![CH1205Ans](images/CH1205Ans.png)

The concerted one-step reaction must have a transition state, with the nucleophile getting close to the carbon in the C-Br bond from the opposite side of the leaving group (the bromine).

This question is categorized as medium difficulty, as students should recall how $S_N2$ occurs and identify the correct representation of the transition state.
