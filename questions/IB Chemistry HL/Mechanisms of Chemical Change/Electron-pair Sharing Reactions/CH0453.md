# CH0453

Which of the following equations is not an acid-base reaction according to the Bronsted-Lowry theory?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{2H2O(l) <=> H3O+(aq) + OH-(aq)}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{NaOH(aq) + HCl(aq) <=> NaCl(aq) + H2O(l)}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{NH3(aq) + H+(aq) <=> NH4+(aq)}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{Co^2+(aq) + 6H2O(l) <=> [Co(H2O)6]^{2+}(aq)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Bronsted-Lowry acid-base theory involves the transfer of protons ($\ce{H+}$).

Reaction D follows the Lewis acid-base theory where the Co$^{2+}$ is the Lewis acid (electron pair acceptor), and $\ce{H2O}$ is the Lewis base (electron pair donor).
