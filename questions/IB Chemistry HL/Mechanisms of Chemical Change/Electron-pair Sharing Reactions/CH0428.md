## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The reaction rate is greatest for tertiary carbocations, and the $\ce{C-Br}$ bond breaks more easily than the $\ce{C-Cl}$ bond as the $\ce{C-Br}$ bond enthalpy is lower.

This question is hard as the student must compare correctly and recall two trends in reactivity and ignore other structural differences, such as the presence of a cyclohexane ring in B and D.
