# CH0429

Some halogenoalkanes can undergo both $\ce{S_N1}$ and $\ce{S_N2}$ reactions with sodium hydroxide.

Which of the following statements are true?

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$The $\ce{S_N2}$ reaction includes a carbocation intermediate.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The $\ce{S_N2}$ reaction is stereospecific.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$The $\ce{S_N2}$ reaction rate is proportional to the nucleophile concentration.

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Statement I is incorrect. The carbocation intermediate is a feature of the $\textrm{S}_\textrm{N}\textrm{1}$ mechanism, not $\textrm{S}_\textrm{N}\textrm{2.}$ The $\textrm{S}_\textrm{N}\textrm{2}$ mechanism proceeds in a single concerted step through a five$-$coordinate transition state, no intermediate is formed.

Statement II is correct. In the $\textrm{S}_\textrm{N}\textrm{2}$ mechanism, the nucleophile attacks at $180\degree$ to the leaving group (backside attack), causing inversion of configuration at the stereogenic centre. This makes the reaction stereospecific.

Statement III is correct. The rate$-$determining step in an $\textrm{S}_\textrm{N}\textrm{2}$ reaction is bimolecular, involving both the halogenoalkane and the nucleophile. The rate law is first order with respect to each, so the rate is directly proportional to the nucleophile concentration.
