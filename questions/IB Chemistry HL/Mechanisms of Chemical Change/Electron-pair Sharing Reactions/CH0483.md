# CH0483

Propene is completely reacted with elemental bromine, followed by excess aqueous sodium hydroxide.  What is the final product of this two-step reaction?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Propane$-$1,2$-$diol

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Propane$-$1,3$-$diol

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$2$-$Bromopropan$-$1$-$ol

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$1$-$Bromopropan$-$2$-$ol

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The alkene is dibrominated in the first step, and the nucleophilic substitution in the second step replaces the adjacent bromo groups with alcohol groups.

The student must be able to deduce the product's structure without the assistance of structural formulas and correctly name the product using IUPAC conventions.

The question is hard due to the absence of structural formulas and the need to understand both organic nomenclature and reaction mechanisms.
