# CH0452

Which row of the table correctly classifies the following organic base?

![18.1-452](images/18.1-452.png)

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  | |Bronsted-Lowry Base| Lewis Base

  |:-:|:-:|:-:|

  A.$\hspace{1em}$|Yes|Yes

  B.$\hspace{1em}$|Yes|No

  C.$\hspace{1em}$|No|Yes

  D.$\hspace{1em}$|No|No

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

The nonbonding electron pair on the nitrogen atom can accept a proton and be donated to another species.

Bronsted$-$Lowry bases are $\ce{H+}$ acceptors while Lewis bases are electron$-$pair donors.
