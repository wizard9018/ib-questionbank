## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
In step 1, $\ce{NaOH}$ acts as a nucleophile and substitutes the $\ce{Cl}$ leaving group to form an alcohol. The aldehyde group is not affected by $\ce{NaOH}$ under these conditions. This eliminates the options showing a sodium carboxylate salt $(\ce{-COO^{-}Na^{+}}).$

The remaining two options differ only in the stereochemistry at the chiral centre. $\ce{NaOH}$ is a strong nucleophile, favouring an $\textrm{S}_\textrm{N}\textrm{2}$ mechanism. An $\textrm{S}_\textrm{N}\textrm{1}$ pathway would produce a racemic mixture rather than a single stereoisomer, which is inconsistent with the answer options. The $\textrm{S}_\textrm{N}\textrm{2}$ backside attack at $180°$ to the leaving group results in inversion of configuration at the stereocenter.
