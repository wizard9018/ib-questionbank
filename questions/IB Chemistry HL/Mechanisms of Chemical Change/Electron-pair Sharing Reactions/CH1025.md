# CH1025

Consider the reaction between bromobutane and hydroxide ions:

$\ce{CH3CH2CH2CH2Br + OH- -> CH3CH2CH2CH2OH + Br-}$

Which species acts as a nucleophile?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH2CH2CH2Br}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{OH-}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Br-}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Br}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

The student must recognize $\ce{OH-}$ as a negatively charged electron$-$rich species that acts as a nucleophile. The hydroxide ion is attracted to groups which can accept electron pairs.

In this case, it is attracted to the carbon next to the bromine ($\ce{C-Br}$) in the nucleophilic substitution, as shown below. This carbon presents a partial positive ($\delta^+$) charge due to bond polarity in the $\ce{C-Br}$ bond.

![CH1025](images/CH1025.png)
