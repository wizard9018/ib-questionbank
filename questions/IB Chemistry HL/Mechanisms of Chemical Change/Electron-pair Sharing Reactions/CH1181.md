# CH1181

Which reactant yields 2$-$methylbutane after its hydrogenation?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$but$-$1$-$ene

- B. $\hspace{1em}$but$-$2$-$ene

- C. $\hspace{1em}$2,3$-$dimethyl$-$but$-$2$-$ene

- D. $\hspace{1em}$2$-$methylbuta$-$1,3$-$diene

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Alkenes undergo electrophilic addition in the carbon-to-carbon double bond.
The hydrogenation of but-1-ene and but-2-ene will yield butane.

![CH1181](images/CH1181.png)

**OR**

![CH1181B](images/CH1181B.png)

The hydrogenation of 2,3-dimethyl-but-2-ene yields 2,3-dimethylbutane.

![CH1181C](images/CH1181C.png)

The hydrogenation of 2-methylbuta-1,3-diene yields 2-methylbutane.
