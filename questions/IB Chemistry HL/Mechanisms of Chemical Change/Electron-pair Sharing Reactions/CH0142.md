# CH0142

Which of the following describe a nucleophile?   

- I.$\hspace{1em}$Electron$-$rich species\

II.$\hspace{0.7em}$Have a lone pair of electron\

III.$\hspace{0.4em}$Donate an electron pair to an electron$-$deficient compound\

IV.$\hspace{0.4em}$Is electron$-$deficient species itself

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$III and IV only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$All of the above

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

A nucleophile is a species that is electron$-$rich and an electron pair donor.

An electrophile is an electron deficient species.
