# CH0450

Which statement about acids and bases is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$All Lewis acids are nucleophiles

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$All Bronsted-Lowry bases are Lewis bases

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$All Lewis acids are Bronsted-Lowry acids

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$All Lewis bases are electrophiles

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Bronsted-Lowry bases are proton acceptors. Lewis bases are electron-pair donors (nucleophiles). When a Bronsted-Lowry base accepts a proton, it forms a coordinate covalent bond with the $\ce{H+}$ ion. This can be viewed as the base donating a lone pair to the $\ce{H+}$ ion: All Bronsted-Lowry bases are also Lewis bases.

Lewis acids are electrophiles (electron-pair acceptors). While all Bronsted-Lowry acids are Lewis acids, the opposite is not true. Metal cations and electron-deficient compounds like $\ce{BF3}$ can accept electron pairs and behave as Lewis acids but do not have protons to donate so cannot act as Bronsted-Lowry acids.
