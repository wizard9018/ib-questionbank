$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The $S_N1$ mechanism has a unimolecular rate determining step with heterolytic fission of the $\ce{C-Cl}$ bond producing a carbocation intermediate.

Students must recall the mechanism of the $S_N1$ reaction and be able to identify the RDS. Difficulty is increased by the use of a specific reaction example and the need to recall two reaction details: the mechanism of the $S_N1$ reaction and the identity of the RDS.
