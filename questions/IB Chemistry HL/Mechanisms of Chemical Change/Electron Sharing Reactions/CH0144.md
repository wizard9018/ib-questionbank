# CH0144

Which of the following reactions is an example of a free$-$radical substitution reaction in alkanes? 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$combustion

- B.$\hspace{1em}$polymerization

- C.$\hspace{1em}$halogenation 

- D.$\hspace{1em}$addition

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Homolytic fission of a halogen molecule produces free radicals that can react with otherwise unreactive alkanes.
