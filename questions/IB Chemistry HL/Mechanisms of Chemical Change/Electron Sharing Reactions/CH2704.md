# CH2704

Ethane reacts with chlorine in the presence of sunlight. Which statements are correct?

-   I.$\hspace{1em}$Ultraviolet light is needed for the propagation steps.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The reaction is a free$-$radical substitution.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}1,2-$dichloroethane is a possible product.

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$I, II and III

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$I and II only

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$I and III only

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Statement I is incorrect. Ultraviolet light is required for the initiation step, not the propagation steps. In the initiation step, UV light supplies the energy needed for homolytic fission of the $\ce{Cl-Cl}$ bond:

$\ce{Cl2 ->[\textrm{UV}] 2 Cl}$ꞏ

Once chlorine radicals have been generated, the propagation steps proceed without further input of UV light. The chain reaction sustains itself because each propagation step regenerates a radical.

Statement II is correct. The reaction of ethane with chlorine is a free$-$radical substitution. A hydrogen atom on ethane is replaced by a chlorine atom through a chain mechanism involving radicals:

$\ce{C2H6 + Cl}$ꞏ$\ce{ \ -> C2H5}$ꞏ$\ce{ + HCl}$

$\ce{C2H5}$ꞏ$ \ce{+ Cl2 -> C2H5Cl + Cl}$ꞏ

Statement III is correct. The initial product, chloroethane, still contains $\ce{C-H}$ bonds that can undergo further substitution. A chlorine radical can abstract a hydrogen atom from chloroethane to produce $\ce{C2H4Cl}\text{ꞏ},$ which then reacts with $\ce{Cl2}$ to form $1,2-$dichloroethane (or $1,1-$dichloroethane, depending on which hydrogen is abstracted). Multiple substitution is characteristic of free$-$radical mechanisms.
