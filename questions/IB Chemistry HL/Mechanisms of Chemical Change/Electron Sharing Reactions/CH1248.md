# CH1248

Ozone depletion in the stratosphere can be catalyzed by CFCs. Part of the mechanism involved is described below.

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | Reaction 1: $\hspace{2em}$ | $\ce{CF2Cl2 ->[h\nu] CF_2Cl}$• + Cl•|
  | Reaction 2: | $\ce{O3 + Cl}$• $\ce{->  ClO}$• $\ce{+ O2}$ |
  | Reaction 3: | $\ce{ClO}$• $\ce{+ O3 -> 2 O2 + Cl}$• |

Which statement is correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$Reaction 1 is an initiation step, and reaction 2 is a propagation step.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$Reaction 1 is an initiation step, and reaction 3 is a termination step.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$Reaction 1 involves heterolytic fission to produce highly reactive radicals.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$Reaction 3 is a termination step as radicals are consumed.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Radicals are entities with unpaired electrons that are highly reactive (represented as R•).

Reaction 1 is an initiation step (as radicals are formed from molecules), and reactions 2 and 3 are propagation steps (as radicals form other radicals). Each of these steps require homolytic fission, where the two electrons in a bond are split evenly between the bonding atoms.

This question is categorized as easy as students must understand radical forms and identify initiation, propagation, and termination steps.
