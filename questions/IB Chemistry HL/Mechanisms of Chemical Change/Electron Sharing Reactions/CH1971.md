# CH1971

The radical substitution of ethane with chlorine produces chloroethane. Which pair of equations represents the propagation steps?

$\mbox{}\\[-10pts]$

| $\hspace{1.em}$||| Equation 1 | Equation 2 |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
|| A. || $\ce{Cl2} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,Cl}$• | $\ce{C2H6} + \ce{Cl}$• $\longrightarrow \ce{C2H5Cl} + \ce{H}$• |
|| B. || $\ce{C2H6} + \ce{Cl}$• $\longrightarrow \ce{C2H5}$• $+ \ce{HCl}$ | $\ce{C2H5}$• $+ \ce{Cl2} \longrightarrow \ce{C2H5Cl} + \ce{Cl}$• |
|| C. || $\ce{C2H5}$• $+ \ce{Cl}$• $\longrightarrow \ce{C2H5Cl}$ | $\ce{Cl}$• $+ \ce{Cl}$• $\longrightarrow \ce{Cl2}$ |
|| D. || $\ce{Cl2} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,Cl}$• | $\ce{C2H5}$• $+ \ce{Cl}$• $\longrightarrow \ce{C2H5Cl}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Propagation steps are the repeating cycle of a chain reaction in which radicals are both consumed and regenerated. Each step must begin with a radical and produce a new radical, sustaining the chain.

The first step consumes a chlorine radical and produces an ethyl radical:

$\ce{C2H6} + \ce{Cl}$• $\longrightarrow \ce{C2H5}$• $+ \ce{HCl}$

The second step consumes the ethyl radical and regenerates a chlorine radical:

$\ce{C2H5}$• $+ \ce{Cl2} \longrightarrow \ce{C2H5Cl} + \ce{Cl}$•

The regenerated $\ce{Cl}$• feeds back into the first step, creating a self$-$sustaining cycle. This is the defining feature of propagation.

This question is categorised as medium. The student must distinguish between initiation, propagation, and termination steps, and recognise the self$-$sustaining nature of the propagation cycle.
