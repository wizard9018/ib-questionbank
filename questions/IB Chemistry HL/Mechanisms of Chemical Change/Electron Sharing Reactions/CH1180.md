# CH1180

Which statement(s) is (are) correct about this chemical reaction?

$\ce{Cl2 -> 2 Cl\textrm{\char"A78F}}$

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$It is a heterolytic fission.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$It can represent an initiation step.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$Radicals are consumed in this reaction.

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$II only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Homolytic fission occur when radicals are formed; a bond is broken, and the two electrons are split evenly between the two atoms involved, as shown below.

![CH1180](images/CH1180.png)

This reaction shows homolytic fission, so statement I is incorrect. In addition, radicals are formed, so statement III is incorrect.

This reaction requires UV light and initiates radical substitution reactions of alkanes with chlorine. Statement II is correct.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must understand how initiation steps occur, identifying this process as homolytic fission that causes radical formation.
