# CH1024

Which statement is correct about homolytic fission?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$It is more common where a bond is polar 

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$It is more likely to occur in a bond between identical atoms

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$It does not form free radicals

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$When the bond breaks, both shared electrons go to one of the atoms in that bond

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Students must compare ways of breaking bonds.

They must recall that homolytic fissions form free radicals as shared electrons split once the bond breaks. In contrast, heterolytic fissions produce positively and negatively charged particles (ions) as both shared electrons go to just one of the atoms in the bond.

In addition, students must identify that polar bonds usually undergo heterolytic fissions and nonpolar bonds (such as the one between identical atoms) undergo homolytic fissions.

  

This question is categorized as hard as students must evaluate the options in light of breaking bonds and bond polarity.
