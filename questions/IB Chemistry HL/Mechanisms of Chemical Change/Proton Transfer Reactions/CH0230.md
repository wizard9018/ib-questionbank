# CH0230

10 cm$^3$ of an aqueous solution of $\ce{HCl}$ with $\ce{pH}$ 1.0 is mixed with 990 cm$^3$ of water. The $\ce{pH}$ of the formed solution is:

- A.$\hspace{1em}$3.0

- B.$\hspace{1em}$2.0

- C.$\hspace{1em}$2.5

- D.$\hspace{1em}$1.5

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
A dilution from 10 cm$^3$ to 1000 cm$^3$ is a $100-$fold dilution of $\ce{H+}$ which increases the $\ce{pH}$ by a factor of 2.

Mathematically, we can use the dilution formula

$C_1V_1 = C_2V_2$

Rearranging and substituting in the known values

$C_2 = \dfrac{C_1V_1}{V_2} = \dfrac{(0.1 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3})(10 \textrm{\ cm}^3)}{1000 \textrm{\ cm}^3} = 1 \times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

The pH can be calculation:

$\textrm{pH} = -\log [\ce{H^+}] = 3$
