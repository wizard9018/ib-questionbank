# CH0411

Which statement is correct for the following reaction?

$\ce{H2SO4(l) + HNO3(l) -> H2NO3^+(l) + HSO4^-(l)}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{H2SO4}$ has a higher ${K_a}$ than $\ce{HNO3}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{HNO3}$ has a higher ${K_a}$ than $\ce{H2SO4}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$ Both $\ce{H2SO4}$ and $\ce{HNO3}$ are acting as acids

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{H2SO4}$ is acting as a base

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Stronger acids have larger ${K_a}$ values (release more $\ce{H+}$ into solution).

As $\ce{H2SO4}$ is acting as the acid by donating a proton to $\ce{HNO3}$, $\ce{H2SO4}$ must be the stronger acid.
