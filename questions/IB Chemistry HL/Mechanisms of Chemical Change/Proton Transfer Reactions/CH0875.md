# CH0875

What is the pH of a solution when 100 cm$^{3}$ of 0.1 mol dm$^{-3}$ of $\ce{HNO3}$ is diluted with 900 cm$^{3}$ of water?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$1

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$2

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$3

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$4

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The concentration of $\ce{H+}$ ions in the original solution is 0.1 mol dm$^{-3}$.

This corresponds to a pH $ = -\log_{10}\ce{[H+]} = -\log_{10}0.1 = 1$.

When the water is added, this dilutes the solution 10-fold, which corresponds to a 1 pH point increase to 2.
