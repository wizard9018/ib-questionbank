# CH0239

Which of the following is correct?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$A weak acid is a proton donor and it has good conductivity in solution

- B.$\hspace{1em}$A weak acid is a proton donor and it has poor conductivity in solution

- C.$\hspace{1em}$A weak acid is a proton acceptor and it has good conductivity in solution

- D.$\hspace{1em}$A weak acid is a proton acceptor and it has poor conductivity in solution

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

By definition an acid is a proton donor. Weak acids only partially dissociate in aqueous solution leading to poor conductivity.
