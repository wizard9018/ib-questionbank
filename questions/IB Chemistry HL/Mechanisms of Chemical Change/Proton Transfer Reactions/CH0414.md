# CH0414

A solution of 1.0 mol dm$^{-3}$ HCl is diluted to 0.1 mol dm$^{-3}.$ Which statement is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The $\ce{pH}$ increases by a factor of 10

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The $\ce{pH}$ decreases by a factor of 10

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The $\ce{pH}$ increases by 1

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The $\ce{pH}$ decreases by 1

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

A tenfold decrease in $\ce{[H+]}$, leads to a $\ce{pH}$ increase of 1.
