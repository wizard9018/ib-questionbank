# CH0225

When wine is exposed to air, the ethanol is slowly converted to ethanoic acid. The $\ce{pH}$ of a
bottle of wine changes from 6.5 to 4.5. The concentration of $\ce{[H+]}$ is?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Decreased by 100

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Decreased by 3

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Increased by 3

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Increased by 100

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The pH decreases by $2$ units, from $6.5$ to $4.5.$ A lower pH means a higher $[\ce{H^{+}}],$ so the concentration of $\ce{H^{+}}$ ions increases. This eliminates options A and B.

Since $\textrm{pH} = -\log[\ce{H^{+}}],$ the relationship between the two $[\ce{H^{+}}]$ values is:

$\dfrac{[\ce{H^{+}}]_{\textrm{final}}}{[\ce{H^{+}}]_{\textrm{initial}}} = \dfrac{10^{-4.5}}{10^{-6.5}} = 10^{(-4.5) - (-6.5)} = 10^{2} = 100$

Each decrease of one $\textrm{pH}$ unit corresponds to a tenfold increase in $[\ce{H^{+}}],$ so a decrease of two units corresponds to $10 \times 10 = 100.$ The concentration increases by a factor of $100.$
