# CH0231

Which one of the following statements about acids is/are true?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Acids will neutralize bases to form salts |
  | II. | Acids produce solutions with pH greater than 7 |
  | III. | Acids ionize to form $\ce{H+}$ ions when dissolved in water |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$I only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Statement II is incorrect as acids produce solutions with $\ce{pH}$ lower than 7.
