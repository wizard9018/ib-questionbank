# CH0235

**Maximum mark: 5**

Water dissociates partially and the value of the ionic product constant of water at 25 $^\circ \mathrm{C}$ is $1.00 \times10^{-14}.$

1.  State an equation for the dissociation of water. [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the $\ce{pH}$ of a 0.0100 mol dm$^{-3}$ of lithium hydroxide at 298 K. [2 marks]

3.  Explain whether a 0.0100 mol dm$^{-3}$ solution of calcium hydroxide will have a higher or lower $\ce{pH}$ than a 0.0100 mol dm$^{-3}$ solution of lithium hydroxide at the same temperature. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\ce{2H2O (l) <=> H3O+ (aq) + OH- (aq)}$

    $\textbf{OR}$

    $\ce{H2O (l) <=> H+ (aq) + OH- (aq)}\, \checkmark$

    $\textit{Reversible arrow required}$\
    $\textit{State symbols not required}$
2. $\begin{aligned}\\
  \ce{[H+]} &= \dfrac{1 \times10^{-14}}{0.01}  = 1.00 \times 10^{-12}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3 }\,\checkmark \\
  \ce{pH} &= -log \ce{[H+] }= 12.0\,\checkmark
  \end{aligned}$

  $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

  $\ce{LiOH(aq) -> Li^{1+} (aq) +  OH^{1-} (aq)}$

  $\ce{LiOH}$ is a strong base, therefore fully dissociates in water and [LiOH] = $\ce{[OH-]}= 0.0100\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

  $K_W = \ce{[H+][OH-]}$

3.  For $\ce{Ca(OH)2}$ , $\ce{[OH-]} = (2)(0.0100\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}) = 0.0200\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\checkmark$

    A higher $\ce{[OH-]}$ in $\ce{Ca(OH)2}$ will lead to a lower $\ce{[H+]}$ and consequently a higher $\ce{pH}.\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    For the same concentration of both bases, more OH$^-$ is produced for $\ce{Ca(OH)2}$ and therefore higher pH.

    $\ce{Ca(OH)2(aq) -> Ca^{2+}(aq) + 2 OH-(aq)}$
