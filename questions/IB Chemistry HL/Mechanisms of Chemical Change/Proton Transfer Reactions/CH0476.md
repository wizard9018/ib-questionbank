## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\ce{HIn <=> H+ + In-} \,\checkmark$ (or specific example accepted);

    Acid-base indicators are weak acids or weak bases that are different colours in acidic $\ce{(HIn)}$ and alkaline $\ce{(In^-)}$ conditions. $\checkmark$

    From Le Chatelier's principle, when acid $\ce{(H+)}$ is added, the equilibrium shifts to the left ($\ce{HIn}$ colour is observed) and when base is added, (accepts/removes $\ce{H+}$) the equilibrium shifts to the right (In$^-$ colour is observed). $\checkmark$

2.  1.  $pOH = -\log_{10}[\ce{OH-}] =-\log_{10}(1.0) =0$

        $pH =14-pOH = 14-0 = 14\, \checkmark$

        **OR**

        $K_w = \ce{[H+][OH-] \Longrightarrow [H+]} = \dfrac{1\times10^{-14}}{1.0}= 1\times10^{-14}$

        $pH = -\log_{10}(1\times 10^{-14}) = 14\, \checkmark$

    2.  $pH = -\log_{10}[\ce{H+}] = -\log_{10}0.5 = 0.3 \, \checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

        $\ce{HNO3}$ is a strong acid that dissociates completely in water.

    3.  Bromothymol blue. $\checkmark$ *Accept phenol red*.

        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

        This is a titration between a strong acid and strong base. The $pH$ at the equivalence point will be approximately 7.
