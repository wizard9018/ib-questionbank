# CH0907

For which of the following acid-base pairs does the equivalence point occur at the **highest** pH value?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  | | $\mathbf{pK_a}$ of acid | $\mathbf{pK_b}$ of base |
  | :-: | :-: | :-: |
  | A. | $-6.7$ | $-0.4$ |
  | B. | $-1.5$ | $9.4$ |
  | C. | $4.8$ | $3.4$ |
  | D. | $6.4$ | $-1.0$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The value of $pK_a$ increases as the strength of an acid decreases. Similarly, weaker bases display higher values of $pK_b$.

Therefore, options **A, B, C, and D** correspond to strong acid-strong base, strong acid-weak base, weak acid-weak base, and weak acid-strong base reactions, respectively. The stronger an acid (or base), the weaker its conjugate base (or acid).

Thus, the equivalence point occurs at the highest pH when a strong base reacts with a weak acid to produce a weak conjugate acid and a strong conjugate base.
