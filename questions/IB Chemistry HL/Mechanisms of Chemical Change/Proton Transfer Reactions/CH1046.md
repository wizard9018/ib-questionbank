# CH1046

What is the correct expression for the acid dissociation constant, $K_a,$ of the ammonium ion?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{\ce{[NH3][H3O+]}}{\ce{[NH4+]}}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{\ce{[NH4+]}}{\ce{[NH3][H3O+]}}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{\ce{[NH3][H3O+]}}{\ce{[NH4+][H2O]}}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{\ce{[NH4+][H2O]}}{\ce{[NH3][H3O+]}}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Ammonium acts as an acid, as shown below.

$\ce{NH4+}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{H2O}\textrm{(l)} \rightleftharpoons  \ce{NH3}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{H3O+}\textrm{(aq)}\qquad$ :answer[$K = \dfrac{\ce{[NH3][H3O+]}}{\ce{[NH4+]}}$]

Water is omitted from the expression because it is a liquid.  Only aqueous and gaseous species are included in the equilibrium constant expression.

This question is categorized as medium as students must generate the chemical reaction for the acid$-$behavior of ammonium ion and then formulate the expression for the equilibrium constant.
