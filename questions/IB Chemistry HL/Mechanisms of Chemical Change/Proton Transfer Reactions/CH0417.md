# CH0417

**Maximum mark: 6**

This question is about the weak acid, carbonic acid $\ce{H2CO3}$ ($pK_{a1} = 6.35$).

1.  

    1.  Determine the pH of a 0.175 mol dm$^{-3}$ solution of carbonic acid. [3 marks]

    2.  State one assumption made in your answer to (a)(i). [1 mark]

Carbonic acid is a diprotic acid so can undergo two dissociations. The second dissociation has a $pK_{a2}$ of 10.33.

2.  

    1.  State the equation for the second dissociation of carbonic acid. [1 mark]

    2.  Comment on the degree of dissociation for this second proton, based on the value of $pK_{a2}$. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  

    1.  $\hspace{1em}$ $\checkmark$

        | $\hspace{7em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ $\ce{H2CO3}$ $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{HCO^-3}$ $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{H+}$ $\hspace{1em}$ |

        | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |

        | Initial | 0.175 | 0 | 0 |

        | Change | $- x$ | $+ x$ | $+ x$ |

        | Equilibrium | $0.175-x\approx 0.175$ | $x$ | $x$ |

        $\dfrac{x^2}{0.175} = 10^{-6.35} \Rightarrow x = 2.80 \times 10^{-4}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3};$ $\checkmark$

        $pH = - \log[H^+] = -\log(2.80 \times 10^{-4}) = 3.55\,\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        $K_{a1} =\dfrac{[\ce{H^+}][\ce{HCO3^-}]}{[\ce{H2CO3}]}$,  and $K_{a1} = 10^{-pK_{a1}}$.

    2.  $[\ce{H2CO3}]_{initial} \approx [\ce{H2CO3}]_{equilibrium}$ / the degree of dissociation is small/negligible. $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        Weak acids only partially dissociate. It can be assumed that this dissociation is so small that the concentration of the undissociated acid at equilibrium is approximately the same as its initial concentration.

2.  

    1.  $\ce{HCO^-3 (aq) + H2O (aq) <=> CO^{2-}3 (aq) + H3O+ (aq)}$ $\checkmark$

        **OR**

        $\ce{HCO^-3 (aq) <=> CO^{2-}3 (aq) + H+ (aq)}$

    2.  The $pK_{a2}$ value is very high which means that very little $\ce{HCO^-3}$ dissociates. $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        High $pK_a$ values correspond to low $K_a$ values. This suggests equilibrium lies significantly over to the left-hand side with the undissociated weak acid.
