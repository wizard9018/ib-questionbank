# CH1596

Sulfuric acid completely ionizes in water.

$\ce{H2SO4 + 2 H2O -> 2 H3O+ + SO4^2-}$

What is the pH of a $5.0 \times 10^{-5}\text{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ solution of sulfuric acid?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$1.7

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$1.4

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$4.0

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$2.0

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

From the chemical equation given in the question, it must be assumed that one mole of sulfuric acid will completely dissociate to produce two moles of $\ce{H+}$ ions. Thus, when the  concentration of acid is $5.0 \times 10^{-5}\text{\ mol dm}^{-3},$ the concentration of $\ce{H+}$ ions will be double, *i.e.*, $[\ce{H+}] = 1.0 \times 10^{-4}.$

$\text{pH} = -\log[\ce{H+}] = -\log (1.0 \times 10^{-4}) = 4.0$
