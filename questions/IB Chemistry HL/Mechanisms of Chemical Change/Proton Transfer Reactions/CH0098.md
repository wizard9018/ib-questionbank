# CH0098

**Maximum mark: 5**

Alkali solutions can be made by dissolving a basic substance in water.

1.  Determine the concentration of a solution of NaOH made by carefully dissolving 5.00 g of NaOH in sufficient distilled water to make 250.0 cm$^3$ total solution in a volumetric flask. [2 marks]

2.  An indicator was added to the solution made in (a), and it turned red. Using section 22 (*Section 18- 2025 syllabus*) in the Data booklet, state a possible indicator used. [1 mark]

3.  The temperature of a 50.0 cm$^3$ sample of the solution containing the indicator from (b) was found to be 22.0 °C. Suggest with a reason, two possible observations if a slightly larger quantity of HCl was added and stirred. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. $n = \dfrac{m}{M} = \dfrac{5.00\,\text{g}}{40.00\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^-1}= 0.125\,\text{mol}\,\checkmark$

   $C = \dfrac{n}{V} = \dfrac{0.125\,\text{mol}}{250\times 10^{-3}\,\text{dm}^3} = 0.500\,\text{mol}\,\text{dm}^{-3}\,\checkmark$

   $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

   $M(\ce{NaOH}) = 22.99 + 16.00 + 1.01 = 40.00\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$

2. Phenol red $\checkmark$

   $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

   Solution will be an alkali (NaOH is a strong base), indicator that is red in alkaline solution is phenol red.

3. Acid-base reactions are exothermic; therefore temperature will increase/rise; $\checkmark$

   Indicator (phenol red) will change color (from red to yellow) since pH will be reduced/lowered; $\checkmark$
