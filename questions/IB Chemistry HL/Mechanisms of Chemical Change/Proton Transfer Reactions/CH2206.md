# CH2206

Which species is the conjugate acid of the oxide ion, $\ce{O^2-}?$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{O2}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{OH-}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H2O}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H3O+}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand that a Bronsted$\text{--}$Lowry base can accept a hydrogen ion to produce its conjugate acid, which is the species formed after gaining $\ce{H+}.$

$\ce{\underset{\large base}{O^2-(aq)} + H+(aq) -> \underset{\large conjugate acid}{OH-(aq)}}$

The conjugate acid of the oxide ion is the hydroxide ion, as shown above.

This question is categorized as easy as the student must identify conjugate pairs.
