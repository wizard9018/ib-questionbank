# CH0097

**Maximum mark: 6**

1. State the complete balanced equation for the reaction between sulphuric acid and copper (II) oxide. [2 marks]

2. State the complete balanced equation for the reaction between ethanoic acid and potassium carbonate. [2 marks]

3. Outline two ways to monitor the rate of reaction for reaction (b) above. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. $\ce{H2SO4(aq) + CuO(s) -> CuSO4(aq) + H2O(l)}\,\checkmark\,\checkmark$

   $\textit{Award 1 mark for correct products, 1 mark for balancing}$

    $\textrm{\underline{Further Explanation:}}$\
    The reaction of an acid with a metal oxide forms water and salt in a double displacement reaction.  The copper (II) ion and the sulfate ion with its $2-$ charge make a $1:1$ compound, $\ce{CuSO4}.$

    acid + metal oxide $\ce{->}$ salt + water

2. $\ce{2 CH3COOH(aq) + K2CO3(s) -> 2 CH3COOK(aq) + CO2(g) + H2O(l)}\,\checkmark\,\checkmark$

   $\textit{Award 1 mark for correct products, 1 mark for balancing}$

   $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
    The reaction of an acid with a metal carbonate forms a salt and carbonic acid in a double displacement reaction. Carbonic acid is quite unstable and immediately decomposes to form water and carbon dioxide.

    acid + metal carbonate $\ce{->}$ salt + water + carbon dioxide

3. $\textit{Any two from the below:}$

    -   volume of gas produced per time using water displacement  $\checkmark$\
    pressure of gas produced per time using pressure sensor $\checkmark$\
    mass of gas lost per unit time with balance $\checkmark$\
    pH change over time with indicator / pH probe $\checkmark$

   $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
   Rate is determined as change in concentration/pressure/mass of reactant or product over time.

   As a gas is being produced, this could be collected and its volume or pressure measured or allowed to escape with decrease in mass measured.

   The pH of the solution would be expected to increase as the H$^+$ ions in CH$_3$COOH solution are used up.
