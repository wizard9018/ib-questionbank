# CH0869

A well$-$mixed solution is analysed and found to contain the following concentrations: $[\ce{HCl}]= 0.25 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3},$ $[\ce{HNO3}] = 0.25 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3},$ $[\ce{Sr(OH)2}] = 0.50 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}.$

What is the pH of the solution?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}-\log_{10} 0.50$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}7$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}14+\log_{10} 0.25$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}14+\log_{10} 0.50$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Because $\ce{HCl}$ and $\ce{HNO3}$ are strong monoprotic acids, each dissociates completely to contribute $0.25 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ of $\ce{H+}$ ions, giving a combined acidic concentration of $0.50 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}.$

$\ce{Sr(OH)2}$ is a strong diprotic base; every mole furnishes two moles of $\ce{OH-},$ so the stated $0.50 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ supplies $1.00 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ of hydroxide ions.

The $\ce{H+}$ and $\ce{OH-}$ ions react quantitatively to form water, neutralising each other in a $1 : 1$ ratio. Consuming $0.50 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ of $\ce{OH-}$ to neutralise the $0.50 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ of $\ce{H+}$ leaves an excess of $0.50 \ce{\ mol dm}^{-3}\ce{\ OH-}.$

The pOH of the mixture is therefore

$\textrm{pOH} = -\log_{10}(0.50)$
