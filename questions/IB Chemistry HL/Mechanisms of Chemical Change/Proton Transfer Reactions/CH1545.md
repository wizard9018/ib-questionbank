# CH1545

Which is correct about a $1\times 10^{-8}\text{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ HCl(aq) solution at $25^\circ\text{C}?$ 

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\text{pH} = 8.00$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\text{pH} = 6.00$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\text{pH}$ is near 7.00

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\text{pH}$ cannot be determined

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The student must understand that strong acids will always produce an acidic solution, meaning that $[\ce{OH-}] < [\ce{H+}].$ However, when acids are very dilute, the hydrogen ion concentration from the autoionization of water must be included in the calculation.

Recall that pure water at $25^\circ\text{C}$ has a $\text{pH} = 7.00,$ so the concentration of hydrogen ion in pure water is

$[\ce{H+}] = 10^{-\text{pH}}$

$[\ce{H+}] = 10^{-7.00} = 1.00 \times 10^{-7}\text{\ mol dm}^{-3}$

In this case with a small addition of acid, we can add the concentration of naturally occurring $\ce{H+}$ to the concentration added so

$[\ce{H+}] \sim 1 \times 10^{-8}\text{\ mol dm}^{-3} + 1 \times 10^{-7}\text{\ mol dm}^{-3}$

$[\ce{H+}] \sim 1.1 \times 10^{-7}\text{\ mol dm}^{-3}.$

The resulting pH can be calculated as:

$\ce{pH} = -\log [\ce{H+}]$ 

 

$\ce{pH} = - \log (1.1 \times 10^{-7}) = 6.96$ 

or, without a calculator, estimated to be just smaller than 7.

This question is categorized as hard as the student must think in detail about this diluted solution to figure out the resulting pH.
