# CH0229

In order to prepare a solution of 0.1 mol dm$^{-3}$ with the highest $\ce{pH}$, substance $X$ is dissolved in water. Substance $X$ is:

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{HCl}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{NaOH}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{H2SO4}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{NaCl}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\ce{NaOH}$ is a strong base and so would release a high concentration of $\ce{OH-}$ ions in water. Choices A and C are incorrect as $\ce{HCl}$ and $\ce{H2SO4}$ are strong acids and would give low $\ce{pH}$s. $\ce{NaCl}$ is a neutral salt.
