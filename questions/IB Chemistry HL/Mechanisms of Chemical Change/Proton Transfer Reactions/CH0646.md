# CH0646

**Maximum mark: 10**

Zinc(II) oxide reacts with hydrochloric acid to produce an aqueous solution of zinc and chloride ions.

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{ZnO(s) + 2 HCl(aq) ->  Zn^{2+}(aq) + 2 Cl-(aq) + H2O(l)}$

1. HCl is described as an acid because it is a hydrogen ion donor. State the term used to describe acidity of this type. [1 mark]

2. State the conjugate base of HCl in this reaction, giving a reason for your answer. [2 marks]

3. An aqueous solution of HCl does not contain molecules of HCl. Draw the structural formula of the positively charged molecule formed in the solution, including the positive charge. [2 marks]

4. Zinc(II) oxide is amphoteric.

    $\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

   1. Define the term amphoteric. [1 mark]

   2. Suggest a reaction that could be performed to confirm that zinc oxide is amphoteric, giving your reasoning. [2 marks]

   3. Explain why zinc(II) oxide is not **amphiprotic.** [2 marks]
