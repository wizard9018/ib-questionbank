# CH0086

What are the correct products of the reaction of nitric acid and magnesium metal?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$MgNO$_3$ and H$_2$O

- B.$\hspace{1em}$MgNO$_3$ and H$_2$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Mg(NO$_3$)$_2$ and H$_2$O 

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Mg(NO$_3$)$_2$ and H$_2$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Metals and acids react to produce a salt and hydrogen gas. The formulae of the ions are $\ce{Mg^{2+}}$ and NO$_3^{-}$, therefore for a neutral salt, two nitrate ions are required for each magnesium ion.
