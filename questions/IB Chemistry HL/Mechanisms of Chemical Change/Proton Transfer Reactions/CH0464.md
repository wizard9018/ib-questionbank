# CH0464

Which combination of acid and base will typically have the lowest pH at the equivalence point in a titration?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Strong acid and strong base

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Strong acid and weak base

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Weak acid and strong base

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Weak acid and weak base

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Strong acids and weak bases react to produce acidic salts $(\ce{pH}<7)$ as the conjugate acid to a weak base is strong.
