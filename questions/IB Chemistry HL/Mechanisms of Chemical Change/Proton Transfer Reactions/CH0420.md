# CH0420

**Maximum mark: 6**

A student reacts a 15 cm$^3$ sample of 0.10 mol dm$^{-3}$ methylamine, $\ce{CH3NH2} (K_b = 4.47 \times 10^{-4})$, with 10 cm$^3$ of 0.10 moldm$^{-3}\,\ce{HNO3}$.

1.  

    1.  Calculate the initial number of moles of methylamine in the sample. [1 mark]

    

    2.  Calculate the final number of moles of methylamine after the neutralization reaction. [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the pOH of the final solution. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  

    1.  $n(\ce{CH3NH2})_{initial} = C(\ce{CH3NH2})\times V(\ce{CH3NH2}) = \left(15\times 10^{-3}\,\text{dm}^3\right)\left(0.10\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\right) = 1.5\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}\,\checkmark$

    2.  $n(\ce{CH3NH2})_{reacted} = n(\ce{HNO3})_{initial} = C(\ce{HNO3})\times V(\ce{HNO3}) = \left(10\times 10^{-3}\,\text{dm}^3\right)\left(0.10\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\right) = 1.0\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}\,\checkmark$

        $n(\ce{CH3NH2})_{final} = n(\ce{CH3NH2})_{initial} - n(\ce{CH3NH2})_{reacted} = 1.5 \times 10^{-3} - 1.0 \times 10^{-3} = 5.0 \times 10^{-4}\,\text{mol}\,\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        $\ce{CH3NH2 + HNO3 -> CH3NH3^+ + NO3^-}$

        $\ce{CH3NH2}$ reacts with $\ce{HNO3}$ in a $1:1$ ratio, and $\ce{HNO3}$ is the limiting reactant.

2.  $K_b = \dfrac{\ce{[CH3NH^+3][OH-]}}{\ce{[CH3NH2]}}$

    $\Longrightarrow \ce{[OH-]} = \dfrac{\ce{[CH3NH2]}}{\ce{[CH3NH^+3]}} \times K_b$ $\checkmark$

    $\ce{[CH3NH^+3]} = \ce{[CH3NH2]}_{reacted}$,

    $\ce{[CH3NH2]} = \ce{[CH3NH2]}_{final}$

    $\ce{[OH-]} = \dfrac{\dfrac{5.0 \times 10^{-4}}{15 \times 10^{-3} + 10 \times 10^{-3}}}{\dfrac{1.0 \times 10^{-3}}{15 \times 10^{-3} + 10 \times 10^{-3}}} \times 4.47 \times 10^{-4} = 2.24 \times 10^{-4}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\checkmark$

    pOH =$- log[\ce{OH-}] =- log(2.24 \times 10^{-4}) = 3.65\,\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    $\ce{CH3NH2}$ reacts with $\ce{HNO3}$ to produce $\ce{CH3NH3+}$. Therefore, $[\ce{CH3NH^+3}] = [\ce{CH3NH2}]_{reacted}$.

    | Change|${-1.0 \times 10^{-3}}$|${- 1.0 \times 10^{-3}}$|${+1.0 \times 10^{-3}}$|${+1.0 \times 10^{-3}}$
