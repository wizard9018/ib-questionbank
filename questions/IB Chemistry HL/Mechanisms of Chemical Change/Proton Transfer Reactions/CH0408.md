# CH0408

Which expression represents $K_a$ for $\ce{CH3COOH}$?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{\ce{[CH3COO^-][H^+]}}{\ce{[CH3COOH]}}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{\ce{[CH3COOH]}}{\ce{[CH3COO^-][H^+]}}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{\ce{[CH3COO^-]}}{\ce{[CH3COOH][OH^-]}}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{\ce{[CH3COOH][OH^-]}}{\ce{[CH3COO^-]}}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

For the reaction $\ce{HA <=> H+ + A-}$, $K_a = \dfrac{[\ce{H^+}][\ce{A^-}]}{[\ce{HA}]}$.
