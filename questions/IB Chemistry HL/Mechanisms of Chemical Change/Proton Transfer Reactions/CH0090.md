# CH0090

Which of the following reactions is $\textbf{not}$ a neutralization reaction?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Pb(NO$_3$)$_2$ + 2KI → 2KNO$_3$ + PbI$_2$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$CuO + 2HNO$_3$ →  Cu(NO$_3$)$_2$ + H$_2$O

- C.$\hspace{1em}$HCl + NaHCO$_3$ → NaCl + CO$_2$ + H$_2$O

- D.$\hspace{1em}$NH$_4$OH + HNO$_3$ →NH$_4$NO$_3$ + H$_2$O

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Neutralization involves acid and base reacting to form a salt and water. Reaction A does not show those criteria to be categorized as a neutralization reaction.

$\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

Reaction B shows the neutralization reaction of a strong acid with a (basic) metal oxide to form a metal salt and water.

Reaction C shows the neturalization reacton of a strong acid with a (basic) metal hydrogen carbonate to form a metal salt, carbon dioxide, and water.  Hydrogen carbonate ion is the conjugate base to unstable carbonic acid, $\ce{H2CO3}$, which decomposes into water and carbon dioxide.

Reaction D is the neutralization reaction of the weak base ammonium hydroxide (the solvated form of ammonia) with a strong acid to form a salt and water.
