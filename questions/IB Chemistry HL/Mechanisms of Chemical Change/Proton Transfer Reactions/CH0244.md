# CH0244

Which statement is true for the phosphoric $\ce{H3PO4}$ and nitric $\ce{HNO3}$ acids?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Both acids ionize completely when dissolved in water

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The conjugate base of phosphoric acid is a stronger base than the conjugate base of nitric acid

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Phosphoric acid is a better electrolyte than nitric acid

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The conjugate base of nitric acid has a better conductivity in solution than the conjugate base of phosphoric acid

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Phosphoric acid is a weak acid, and so only partially ionizes when dissolved in water.

The weaker the acid, the stronger its conjugate base.

Choice C is incorrect as weak acids are poor conductors of electricity, unlike strong acids.
