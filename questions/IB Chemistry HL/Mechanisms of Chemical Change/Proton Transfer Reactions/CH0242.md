# CH0242

Equimolar solutions of sulfuric acid $\ce{H2SO4}$ and sulfurous acid $\ce{H2SO3}$ are made to react with $0.2 \textrm{\ g}$ of magnesium carbonate. Which of the following statements is (are) $\textbf{not}$ true?

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$The volume of carbon dioxide produced at the end of both reactions is the same.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The reaction with sulfurous acid produces more carbon dioxide per unit of time.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$Both reactions proceed with equal rates.

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

- C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Both acids are diprotic and so contain the same number of moles of $\ce{H+}$ ions, so the neutralization reaction will produce the same number of moles of carbon dioxdie.  The concentration of $\ce{H+}$ ions will be lower at the outset of the reaction with a weak acid so the reaction with sulfurous acid will occur more slowly.
