# CH0249

**Maximum mark: 6**

Commercial vinegar contains a small percentage of ethanoic acid. A student wishes to determine the concentration of ethanoic acid in vinegar. She titrates a solution of ethanoic acid against a standard solution of potassium hydroxide of concentration $8.0\times 10^{-4} \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}.$ The equation for the reaction is:

$\ce{CH3COOH (aq) + KOH (aq) -> CH3COOK (aq) + H2O (l)}$

A volume of $25.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3$ of vinegar was diluted with distilled water and made up to a volume of $250.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3$ in a volumetric flask. A burette was filled with the diluted solution.  $25.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3$ of potassium hydroxide solution was added to a conical flask and three drops of phenolphthalein indicator added.

1. Ethanoic acid is considered a weak acid. Define the term weak acid. [1 mark]

2. The results of the titration are shown in the table below:

    | $\enskip$Trial$\enskip$ | $\enskip$Volume of Titre$\mathbf{\ (cm^3)}\enskip$ |
    | :---: | :---: |
    | 1 | 21.0 |
    | 2 | 21.2 |
    | 3 | 20.8 |

    1. Using Section 18 of the IB data booklet, identify the color at the endpoint. [1 mark]

    2. Calculate the concentration of dilute ethanoic acid. [3 marks]

    3. Calculate the concentration of ethanoic acid in vinegar. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  dissociates / ionizes partially / incompletely in water $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
    A weak acid is a poor proton donor so the reaction

    $\ce{CH3COOH} + \ce{H2O} \rightleftharpoons \ce{CH3COO-} + \ce{H3O+}$

    achieves an equilibrium that favors the reactant side.

2.  1.  colorless $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
        Phenolphthalein is pink in basic solution and colorless in acidic solution.  In this experiment, the conical flask is filled with KOH and the indicator, so will appear pink.  The ethanoic acid is titrated into the basic solution until it becomes colorless.

    2.  $n = 8.0 \times 10^{-4} \times 0.025 = 2.0 \times 10^{-5} \textrm{\ mol\ } \ce{CH3COOH} \: \checkmark$\
    $\mbox{}\\[-11pts]$
        $V =\dfrac{21.0+21.2+20.8}{3}=21.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3  \, \checkmark$\
        $\mbox{}\\[-11pts]$
        $C = \dfrac{n}{V} = 9.52 \times 10^{-4} \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \: \checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
        First, find the amount of the potassium hydroxide in moles using the relationship $n = CV,$ where $C$ is the concentration $8.0 \times 10^{-4}\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ given in the question statement and $V$ is the volume of KOH added to the flask, $25 \textrm{\ cm}^3$, in $\textrm{dm}^3:$

        $n_\ce{KOH} = 8.0 \times 10^{-4}\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \times 0.025 \textrm{\ dm}^3 = 2.0 \times 10^{-5}\textrm{\ mol}$

        At the equivalence point, in each titration, the moles of potassium hydroxide are equal to the moles of ethanoic acid because there is a $1:1$ mole ratio in the neutralization reaction.

        $\ce{KOH} + \ce{CH3COOH} \longrightarrow \ce{KCH3COO} + \ce{H2O}$

        $n_\ce{CH3COOH} =2.0 \times 10^{-5}\textrm{\ mol}$

        On average, the volume  of ethanoic acid used to reach the equivalence point is

        $V =\dfrac{21.0+21.2+20.8}{3}=21.0  \textrm{\ cm}^3$

        So the concentration of the dilute ethanoic acid is

        $C = \dfrac{n}{V}=\dfrac{2.0 \times 10^{-5} \textrm{\ mol}}{21.0 \times 10^{-3} \textrm{\ dm}^3}$ = :answer[$9.52 \times 10^{-4} \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$]

    3.  $C = 9.52 \times 10^{-3} \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \:\checkmark$

        $\textrm{\underline{Further Explanation:}}$\
        The vinegar was diluted 10 fold $(25 \textrm{\ cm}^3 \rightarrow 250 \textrm{\ cm}^3);$ the original concentration is therefore ten times more concentrated:

        $C= 9.52 \times 10^{-4} \times 10 \ $= :answer[$9.52 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$]
