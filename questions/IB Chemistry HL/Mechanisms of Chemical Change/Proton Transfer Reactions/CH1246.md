# CH1246

An unknown substance of concentration $3.00\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$ has a pH = 3.76 at $25\,{}^\circ\text{C}.$

Which statement is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The substance is a strong acid and $\ce{[OH-]} > 10^{-7}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The substance is a strong acid and $\ce{[OH-]} < 10^{-7}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The substance is a weak acid and $\ce{[OH-]} > 10^{-7}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The substance is a weak acid and $\ce{[OH-]} < 10^{-7}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

pH = 3.76 corresponds to an acidic medium.

A strong acid will fully dissociate, so the pH should be $\text{pH} = -\log (3.00\times 10^{-3}) = 2.52.$

However, the pH here is higher, suggesting only a partial dissociation for this acid. As a result, we can conclude it is a weak acid. Options A and B are disregarded.

In addition, $K_w = \ce{[H+][OH-]}$ and $K_w = 10^{-14}$ at $25\,{}^\circ\text{C}.$

As the pH is informed, we can deduce the $\ce{[H+]}.$

$\ce{[H+]} = 10^{-\text{pH}} = 10^{-3.76} = 1.74\times 10^{-4}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

So, we can deduce $\ce{[OH-]}.$

$\ce{[OH-]} = \dfrac{K_w}{[\ce{H+}]} = \dfrac{10^{-14}}{1.74\times 10^{-4}} = 5.74\times 10^{-11}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3} < 10^{-7}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must deduce the partial dissociation of the acid from the given data and estimate that the hydroxide ion concentration will be lower than $10^{-7}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}.$
