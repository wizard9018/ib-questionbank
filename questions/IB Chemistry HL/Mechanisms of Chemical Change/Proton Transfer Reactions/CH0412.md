# CH0412

The $\text{p}K_b$ of $\ce{NH3}$ is 4.75 at 298 K. What is the ${K_a}$ of the conjugate acid at the same temperature?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$ $10^{-4.75}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$ $10^{4.75}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$ $10^{-9.25}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$ $10^{9.25}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

$\begin{aligned}

pK_a + pK_b &= 14 \\

pK_a &= 14 - 4.75 = 9.25 \\

K_a &= 10^{-pK_a} = 10^{-9.25}

\end{aligned}$
