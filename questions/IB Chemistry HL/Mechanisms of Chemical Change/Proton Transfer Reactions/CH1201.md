# CH1201

Consider the titration curve below.

![CH1201](images/CH1201.png)

Which statement is correct?

- $pK_a (bromophenol\ blue) = 4.2$
- $pK_a (phenolphthalein) = 9.6$

- A. $\hspace{1em}$It is a strong acid titrated with a strong base, and bromophenol blue can be used as an indicator

- B. $\hspace{1em}$It is a strong acid titrated with a strong base, and phenolphthalein can be used as an indicator

- C. $\hspace{1em}$It is a weak acid titrated with a strong base, and bromophenol blue can be used as an indicator

- D. $\hspace{1em}$It is a weak acid titrated with a strong base, and phenolphthalein can be used as an indicator

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The equivalent point is above pH 7.

![CH1201b](images/CH1201b.png)

In addition, a buffer zone is observed.

![CH1201C](images/CH1201C.png)

This is consistent with the titration of a weak acid with a strong base.

A good indicator will present a $pK_a$ near the equivalent point.

Bromophenol blue has a $pK_a$ of 4.2, and phenolphthalein has a $pK_a$ of 9.6. Phenolphthalein is adequate for this titration.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must interpret the titration curve, deduce the type of titration and nature of the acid and base combined, and the characteristic $pK_a$ of the suitable acid-base indicator.
