# CH0878

**Maximum mark: 6**

Nick and Anya are chemistry students arguing about the definition of Brønsted-Lowry acids and bases.

Nick argues that all acids contain $\ce{H+}$ ions and all bases contain $\ce{OH^{-}}$ ions.

Anya argues that $\ce{H2O}$ is the only amphoteric species because it contains $\ce{H+}$ and $\ce{OH^{-}}$ ions and, therefore, is the only species that can act as both an acid and a base.

1.
    1.  Discuss, providing a relevant example, why Nick's statement is incorrect. [2 marks]

    2.  Discuss, providing a relevant example, why Anya's statement is incorrect. [2 marks]

2.  Suggest a qualitative method and a quantitative method that could be used to distinguish between an acidic or basic chemical solution and the associated observations. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
   1. An acid is a proton donor, whereas a base is a proton acceptor - they are not defined by $\ce{H+}$ and $\ce{OH^{-}}$ ions. $\checkmark$

      One contradiction to Nick's statement is $\ce{NH3}$, which is a Brønsted-Lowry base but does not contain $\ce{OH^{-}}$ ions. $\checkmark$

   2. While it is true that $\ce{H2O}$ is an amphoteric species, it is not the only one. An amphoteric species does not need to contain an $\ce{H+}$ and $\ce{OH^{-}}$ ion, but can act as an acid and as a base. $\checkmark$

      For example, $\ce{Al2O3}$ is an amphoteric oxide with an acid and base behavior. It doesn't contain $\ce{H+}$ and $\ce{OH^{-}}$ ions. $\checkmark$

2. Qualitative method: add universal indicator to the solutions. An acidic solution will turn red, whereas a basic solution will turn purple. $\checkmark$

   *Other answers with litmus paper, phenolphthalein, and methyl orange are acceptable with the appropriate observations.*

   Quantitative method: measure the pH. An acidic solution will have a pH $< 7$, and a basic solution will have a pH $> 7$. $\checkmark$

   $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

   A qualitative method involves looking at observations, such as observing color changes, whereas a quantitative method involves looking at data, such as pH values.
