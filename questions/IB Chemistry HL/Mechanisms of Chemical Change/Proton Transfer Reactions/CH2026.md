# CH2026

Which aqueous solution would have a pH below 7 at 298 K?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Sodium carbonate

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Potassium nitrate

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Lithium ethanoate

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Aluminium chloride

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must be able to identify the acid$\text{--}$base behaviour of the ions in the aqueous solution. If the parent acid or parent base of the ions is weak, the ions will hydrolyze, and the pH will be different from $\text{pH}=7.$

***Sodium carbonate: $\ce{Na2CO3}$***\
Carbonate ions come from carbonic acid (a weak parent acid). As a result, the carbonate ions might take hydrogen ions, resulting in a $\text{pH} > 7.$

$\ce{CO3^2- + H2O -> HCO3- + OH-}$

***Potassium nitrate: $\ce{KNO3}$***\
Potassium ions come from a strong parent base, KOH, and nitrate ions come from a strong parent acid, $\ce{HNO3}.$ As a result, these ions remain aqueous with no acid$\text{--}$base interaction, and the resulting solution has $\text{pH} = 7.$

***Lithium ethanoate: $\ce{LiCH3COO}$***\
Lithium ions come from a strong parent base, LiOH, and ethanoate ions come from a weak parent acid, $\ce{CH3COOH}.$ As a result, lithium ions remain aqueous with no acid$\text{--}$base interaction, but ethanoate ions undergo a protonation resulting in a solution with $\text{pH} > 7.$

$\ce{CH3COO- + H2O -> CH3COOH + OH-}$

***Aluminium chloride: $\ce{AlCl3}$***\
Chloride ions come from a strong parent acid, HCl, and aluminium ions come from a  parent base, $\ce{Al(OH)3}.$ Aluminium ions are trivalent and have a very positive charge. $\ce{Al^3+}$ acts as a Lewis acid and so, the resulting pH is below 7.

$\ce{Al^3+ + 6 H2O  -> Al(H2O)6^3+}$

$\ce{Al(H2O)6^3+ + H2O -> Al(H2O)5(OH)^2+ + H3O+}$

This question is categorized as hard as the student must identify the ions and understand their acid$\text{--}$base behaviour.
