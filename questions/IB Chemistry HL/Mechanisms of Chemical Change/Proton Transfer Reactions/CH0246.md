# CH0246

Which statement about a strong acid and its conjugate base is correct?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The stronger the acid, the stronger its conjugate base

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The stronger the acid, the less concentrated the conjugate base

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The stronger the acid, the weaker its conjugate base

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The stronger the acid, the more concentrated its conjugate base.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

By definition, the more readily an acid releases a proton, the less likely the conjugate base is to accept a proton.
