## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand that nitric acid is a strong acid and sodium hydroxide is a strong base. The equivalence point will occur at $\text{pH} = 7.$

If $0.0100\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$ NaOH(aq) is used as a titrant, and the equivalence point volume is given (it is $25.0\text{ cm}^3$ in all these options), we can deduce the concentration of the nitric acid.

$C_\ce{HNO3}\times V_\ce{HNO3} = C_\ce{NaOH}\times V_\ce{NaOH}$

$C_\ce{HNO3}\times 25.0\text{ cm}^3 = 0.0100\text{ mol dm}^{-3}\times 25.0\text{ cm}^3$

$C_\ce{HNO3} = 0.0100\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$

We can deduce the initial pH as it corresponds to the initial nitric acid concentration.

$\text{pH} = -\log 0.0100 = 2.0$

This question is categorized as medium as the student must interpret the titration and must be able to deduce features of a titration of a strong acid with a strong base.
