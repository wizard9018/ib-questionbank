# CH0233

A minimal quantity of concentrated nitric acid is gradually added to $150 \textrm{\ cm}^3$ of distilled water at $25\,{}^\circ \textrm{C}$. In the resulting solution, which is correct for the values of $K_w$, $\ce{[H3O+]}$ and $\ce{[OH-]}$?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    |  $\hspace{1em}\mbox{A}.\hspace{1em}$ | $\enskip K_w  = 10^{-14} \enskip$ | $\enskip \ce{[H3O+]  =  10^{-7}}\enskip $ |$\enskip \ce{[OH-]  =  10^{-7}}\enskip $ |
    | :-- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
    |  $\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | $ K_w  = 10^{-14} $ | $\ce{[H3O+]  >  10^{-7}}$ | $\ce{[OH-]  <  10^{-7}}$ |
    |  $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | $K_w  = 10^{-14}$ | $\ce{[H3O+]  <  10^{-7}}$ | $\ce{[OH-]  >  10^{-7}}$ |
    |  $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | $K_w  < 10^{-14}$ | $\ce{[H3O+]  <  10^{-7}}$ | $\ce{[OH-]  <  10^{-7}}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The autoionization of water

$\ce{H2O} + \ce{H2O} \rightleftharpoons \ce{H3O+} + \ce{OH-}$

is governed by the equilibrium constant expression $K_w = \ce{[H3O+][OH-]} = 1.0\times 10^{-14}$ at 298 K.

In neutral solutions, $\ce{[H3O+]}=\ce{[OH-]} = 10^{-7} \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}.$ Adding nitric acid would increase the $\ce{H3O+}$ concentration, shift the equilibrium toward the reactant side, and decrease the $\ce{OH-}$ concentration.

The value of $K_w$ is only affected by temperature.
