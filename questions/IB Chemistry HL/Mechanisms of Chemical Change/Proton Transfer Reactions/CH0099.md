# CH0099

**Maximum mark: 4**

Aqueous ammonia solutions are commonly used to clean in houses, particularly glass such as windows. There are many safety warnings on these products.

1. Ammonia mixed in water forms ammonium hydroxide. State the equation for this process. [2 marks]

2. One concern is that ammonia solutions could mix with acidic cleaning products, such as drain cleaner with sulphuric acid.

    1. Determine the balanced equation for ammonium hydroxide reacting with sulphuric acid. [1 mark]

    2. Suggest a reason this reaction could be dangerous. [1 mark]
