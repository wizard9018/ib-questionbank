# CH0466

Place the following $1.0 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ solutions in order of increasing $\ce{pH}$:
$\ce{NaOH, Na2CO3, NaCl}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{NaOH}<\ce{Na2CO3}<\ce{NaCl}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{NaCl}<\ce{Na2CO3}<\ce{NaOH}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Na2CO3}<\ce{NaCl}<\ce{NaOH}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{NaOH}<\ce{NaCl}<\ce{Na2CO3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\ce{NaCl}$ is a neutral salt, $\ce{Na2CO3}$ is a basic salt, and $\ce{NaOH}$ is a strong base.
