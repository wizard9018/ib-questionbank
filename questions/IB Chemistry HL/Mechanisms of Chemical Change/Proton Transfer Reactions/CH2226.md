# CH2226

What is the pOH for a solution with $[\ce{H+}] = 5.0\times 10^{-8}\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$ at $25\ ^\circ\text{C}?$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$6.70

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$7.30

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$8.00

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$6.00

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\textrm{\underline{Explanations:}}$

Using the relationships $\text{pH} + \text{pOH} = 14$ at $25\ ^\circ\text{C}$ and the definition of the pH, $\text{pH} = -\log [\ce{H+}],$ the pOH can be determined.

$\text{pH} = -\log 5.0\times 10^{-8} = 7.30$

$\text{pOH} = 14 - 7.30 = 6.70$

This question is categorized as easy as the student must understand the relationship between pH and pOH at a given temperature.
