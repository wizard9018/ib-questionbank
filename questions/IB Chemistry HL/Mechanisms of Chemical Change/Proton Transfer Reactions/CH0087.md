# CH0087

A reaction produces carbon dioxide gas and a solution of water with a dissolved salt. What could be the reactants?     

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Sulphuric acid and ammonia

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Ethanoic acid and sodium carbonate

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Nitric acid and magnesium hydroxide 

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Hydrochloric acid and calcium metal

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Acids react with carbonates to produce a salt, water, and carbon dioxide.
