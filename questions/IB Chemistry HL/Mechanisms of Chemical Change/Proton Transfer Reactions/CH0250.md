# CH0250

**Maximum mark: 6**

Carbonic acid is a diprotic acid and its dissociation reactions are shown below:

$\begin{aligned}
\text{Step 1}&: \ce{H2CO3 (aq) <=> H+(aq) + HCO^-3 (aq)}    &\quad    &K_1 \\[3pt]
\text{Step 2}&: \ce{HCO^-3 (aq) <=> H+(aq) + CO3^2- (aq)}    &\quad    &K_2 \\
\end{aligned}$

$K_1$ and $K_2$ are the equilibrium constants for steps 1 and 2 respectively.

1.  1. State the overall equation for the dissociation of carbonic acid. [1 mark]

    2. State an expression for the overall equilibrium constant $K_o$ in terms of $K_1$ and $K_2.$ [1 mark]

    3. State and explain the effect of adding $\ce{Ba(OH)2}$ to the position of the equilibrium for the overall reaction. [2 marks]

2. State, giving a reason in each case, $\textbf{two}$ methods that could be used to distinguish between equimolar solutions of carbonic acid and sulphuric acid. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1.  $\ce{H2CO3 (aq) <=>  2H+(aq) + CO3^2- (aq)\, \checkmark}$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
        Combining the two steps by adding reactants and products yields the equilibrium above.

    2.  $K_o = K_1 \times K_2 \: \checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
        When equations are summed their equilbrium constants are multiplied:

         $\left(\dfrac{\ce{[H+]\cancel{[\ce{HCO3^-}]}}}{\ce{[H2CO3]}}\right)\left(\dfrac{\ce{[H+][CO3^2-]}}{\ce{\cancel{[\ce{HCO3^-}]}}}\right) =\dfrac{\ce{[H+]^2[CO3^2-]}}{\ce{[H2CO3]}}$

    3.  equilibrium will shift to the right $\checkmark $ \
        $\ce{[H+]}$ removed by neutralization  $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
        $\ce{Ba(OH)2}$ being a strong base will dissociate releasing $\ce{OH-}$ ions: $\ce{Ba(OH)2(aq) -> Ba^2+(aq) + 2OH-(aq)}$.

        The $\ce{OH-}$  ions can react with $\ce{H+}$ ions to form water. Therefore, the $\ce{[H+]}$ will decrease.

2.  *Any two from the below:*
    - Conductivity - carbonic acid being a weaker acid, its conductivity will be lower since lower ion concentration will be found in solution. $\checkmark$
    - Reaction with metal / metal carbonate / metal hydrogen carbonate: carbonic acid will react slower/less vigorously because lower $\ce{[H+]}$ / less dissociated. $\checkmark$
    - $\ce{pH}$: carbonic acid will have a higher $\ce{pH}$ because lower $\ce{[H+]}$ / less dissociated. $\checkmark$
