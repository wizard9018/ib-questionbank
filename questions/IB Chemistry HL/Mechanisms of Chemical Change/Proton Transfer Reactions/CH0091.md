# CH0091

Given the following indicators and their behaviours, what will be the correct colours of a solution of 1.0 mol dm$^{-3}$ KOH tested with each indicator? 

| | Indicator | pH range | Colour change (Acid- alkali) |

| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |

| $\hspace{1em}$ | Methyl orange | 3.1 $\text{--}$ 4.4 |Red – Orange

| | Bromocresol green | 3.8 $\text{--}$ 5.4  |Yellow - Blue

| | Phenolphthalein  |8.3 $\text{--}$ 10.0 |Colourless - Pink

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  | | Methyl orange | Bromocresol green | Phenolphthalein

  | --- | :-: | :-: |:-: 

  | A.$\hspace{1em}$| Red| Yellow |Colourless|

  | B.$\hspace{1em}$  | Orange | Blue | Pink |

  | C.$\hspace{1em}$  | Orange | Blue | Colourless |

  | D.$\hspace{1em}$  | Orange| Yellow | Pink|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

A 1.0 mol dm$^{-3}$ solution of KOH would have a pH of 14. Methyl orange, bromocresol green, and phenolphthalein would be orange, blue, and pink respectively at this pH.
