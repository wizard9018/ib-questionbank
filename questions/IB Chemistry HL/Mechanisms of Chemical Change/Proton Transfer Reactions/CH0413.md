# CH0413

Which of the following statements about $\ce{pH}$ is incorrect?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{pH}$ cannot be negative

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$ The $\ce{pH}$ of pure water changes with temperature

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$As the $\ce{pH}$ increases, the concentration of $\ce{H+}$ decreases

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$There is a non-linear relationship between $\ce{pH}$ and $\ce{[H+]}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

$\ce{pH}$ = $-\log_{10}\ce{[H+]}$.

For concentrations of $\ce{H+}$  greater than 1 mol dm$^{-3}$, this would result in a negative $\ce{pH}$ value.
