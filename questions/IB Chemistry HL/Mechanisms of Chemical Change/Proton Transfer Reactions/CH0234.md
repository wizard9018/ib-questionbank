# CH0234

Which of the following solutions will produce a neutral solution when reacted with 0.05 mol of sulfuric acid?

- A.$\hspace{1em}50.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3 \text{ of }\,  1.00 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \ce{\ NaOH}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}100.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3 \text{ of }\,  2.00 \textrm{\ mol\, dm}^{-3} \ce{\ NaOH}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}100.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3 \text{ of }\,  2.00 \textrm{\ mol\, dm}^{-3}  \ce{\ Ca(OH)2}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}25.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3 \text{ of }\,  2.00\textrm{\ mol\, dm}^{-3}  \ce{\ Ca(OH)2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Sulfuric acid is diprotic and so reacts in a $1:1$ ratio with $\ce{Ca(OH)2}$ and a $1:2$ ratio with $\ce{NaOH}.$

$n=CV$ where $n$ is the amount in moles, $C$ is the concentration and $V$ is the volume in dm$^3.$

In D, $n =0.025 \textrm{\ dm}^3 \times 2.00 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} = 0.05 \textrm{\ mol}$
