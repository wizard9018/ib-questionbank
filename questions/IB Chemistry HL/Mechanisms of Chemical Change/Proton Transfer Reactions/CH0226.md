# CH0226

Which of the following statements concerning acids and bases is $\textbf{not}$ true?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The product of the $\ce{[H+]}$and $\ce{[OH-]}$ in a solution at a particular temperature is constant

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The concentration of hydroxide ions is high in a very acidic solution

- C.$\hspace{1em}$At 298 K, a neutral solution has a $\ce{pH}$ equal to 7

- D.$\hspace{1em}$An increase of $\ce{pH}$ by 3 units gives a decrease of $\ce{[H+]}$ by 1000

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Acidic solutions have high $\ce{H+}$concentrations. Bases have a high concentration of $\ce{OH-}$ ions
