# CH0906

Compound HX undergoes the following 

acid$-$base reaction.

$\ce{HX}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{H2O}\textrm{(l)} 

\rightleftharpoons \ce{X-}\textrm{(aq)}  + \ce{H3O}^+\textrm{(aq)}$

Which of the following statements is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The $K_a$ of HX is small because 

    the equilibrium lies to the right

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The $K_a$ of HX is small because 

    the conjugate base readily accepts a proton

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The $K_a$ of HX is large because 

    the conjugate base readily accepts a proton

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The $K_a$ of HX is large because 

    the equilibrium lies to the left

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The use of the equilibrium symbol ($\rightleftharpoons$)
indicates that HX does not fully dissociate and is
therefore a weak acid with a small $K_a$ value.

The conjugate base of HX ($\ce{X-}$) has a strong
tendency to accept a proton and re$\text{-}$form the
acid, which drives the equilibrium to the left. A small
$K_a$ corresponds to an equilibrium that lies to the
left, not the right; these are contradictory and cannot
both be true in the same statement. Similarly, a large
$K_a$ indicates extensive dissociation with the
equilibrium to the right, not a conjugate base that
readily re$\text{-}$accepts a proton.

This question tests the inverse relationship between
acid strength and conjugate base proton affinity: the
weaker the acid, the more readily its conjugate base
re$\text{-}$accepts a proton.
