# CH1048

Consider the acid$-$base titration curve below.

![CH1048](images/CH1048.png)

Which statement about the titration is correct?

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$Methyl orange can be used as an indicator $(\textrm{p}K_a = 3.7).$
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The pH at the equivalence point is 6.1.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$The buffer region is centered at $\text{pH} \approx 4.3.$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$I only

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
On a titration curve, the equivalence point is found at the steepest section of the graph. In this case, it occurs at $V = 25 \textrm{\ cm}^3$ with a pH of about 8.5, which is consistent with titrating a weak acid with a strong base. Statement II is incorrect.

At the half$-$equivalence point, the concentrations of the acid and its conjugate base are equal, so
$\textrm{pH} = \textrm{p}K_a.$ For this acid, $\textrm{p}K_a = 4.3,$ and this point represents the center of the buffer region where the pH changes only gradually as base is added. Statement III is correct.

Finally, an indicator must undergo its color change close to the pH at equivalence. Methyl orange, with $\textrm{p}K_a = 3.7,$ changes too early and would not provide a sharp endpoint here. Thus, it is not suitable for this titration. Statement I is incorrect.
