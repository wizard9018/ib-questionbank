# CH1550

Which of these acids is the strongest?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Butanoic acid $(\textrm{p}K_a = 4.83)$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$2,2$\text{--}$dimethyl propanoic acid $(\textrm{p}K_a = 5.03)$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$hydrofluoric acid $(K_a = 6.76 \times 10^{-4})$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$hydrogen cyanide $(K_a = 6.30 \times 10^{-10})$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\

 The student must understand that the strongest acid undergoes the greatest dissociation. That means that the strongest acid has the greatest $K_a$ or the smallest $\textrm{p}K_a$ (as $\textrm{p}K_a = -\log K_a$).

In this case we should compare by estimation. We can estimate the $\textrm{p}K_a$ for hydrofluoric acid and hydrogen cyanide.

-   HF should have a $\textrm{p}K_a$ between 3 and 4.

-   HCN should have a $\textrm{p}K_a$ between 9 and 10.

Comparing them all, HF is the one with the smallest $\textrm{p}K_a$ and so the one with the greatest $K_a$ and the strongest acid out of the ones considered.

This question is categorized as a medium as the student must compare the strength by estimating the magnitudes of $K_a$ and $\textrm{p}K_a$.
