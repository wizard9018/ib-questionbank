# CH0227

An aqueous solution of nitric acid $\ce{HNO3}$ has a $\ce{pH}$ of 4. How many moles of this acid are found in $1 \textrm{\ dm}^3$ of the solution?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $10^{-4} \textrm{\ mol}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $4 \textrm{\ mol}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $10^{4} \textrm{\ mol}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $1 \textrm{\ mol}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\mathrm{pH} = -\log_{10}\ce{[H+]} \text{ and } \ce{[H+]} = 10^{-\mathrm{pH}}\ce{* [H+] }= 10^{-4}$

$\ce{HNO3}$ is a strong acid that dissociates completely in water.
