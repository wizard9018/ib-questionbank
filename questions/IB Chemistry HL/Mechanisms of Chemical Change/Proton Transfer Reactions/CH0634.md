# CH0634

Four features of the $\ce{HNO3}$ molecule are labelled a-d.

![8.1-634](images/8.1-634.png)

Why is one mole of $\ce{HNO3}$ able to react with one mole of NaOH?           

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Bond a can break to produce a hydrogen ion

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Bond b can break to produce a hydroxide ion

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The charged nitrogen atom c can bond to a hydroxide ion

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The charged oxygen atom d can bond to a hydrogen ion

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

NaOH is a base so reacts with a hydrogen ion. $\ce{HNO3}$, nitric acid, releases a hydrogen ion through the breaking (heterolysis) of the O$-$H bond.

This question is moderately difficult as the student must recognise that NaOH is a base and HNO$_3$ an acid and correctly interpret a structural formula that may be unfamiliar.
