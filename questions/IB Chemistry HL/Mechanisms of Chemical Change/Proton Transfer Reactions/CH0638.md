# CH0638

When powdered calcium oxide is added to dilute hydrochloric acid in a conical flask, the products are water, positive calcium ions and negative chloride ions.

![8.1-638](images/8.1-638.png)

Which species is the conjugate base of hydrochloric acid in this reaction?              

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Calcium oxide

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Water

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Calcium ion

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Chloride ion

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The conjugate base of HCl is the compound produced when a hydrogen ion is removed. The conjugate base is therefore the chloride ion, $\ce{Cl-}$.

This question is hard as the student must link the names of chemicals to their formula and identify a conjugate base without the aid of an explicit reaction equation.
