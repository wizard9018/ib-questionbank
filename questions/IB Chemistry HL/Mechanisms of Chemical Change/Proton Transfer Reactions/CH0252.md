# CH0252

**Maximum mark: 5**

Methanoic acid HCOOH is a weak acid.

1.  State the formula of the conjugate base of $\ce{HCOOH}.$ [1 mark]

2.  $\ce{HCl}$ is a strong acid and dissociates in water as shown below:

    $\ce{HCl (aq) -> H+ (aq) + Cl- (aq)}$

    1.  Suggest the $\ce{pH}$ of the solution formed when $\ce{HCl}$ dissolves in water. [1 mark]

    2.  Compare the relative strengths of the respective conjugate bases of $\ce{HCOOH}$ and $\ce{HCl}.$ [1 mark]

3.  0.01 mol dm$^{-3}$ of a given monoprotic acid $\ce{HA}$ has a $\ce{pH}$ of 3.

    $\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$
    1.  Determine whether this acid is weak or strong. [1 mark]

    2.  Compare the electrical conductivity of $\ce{HA (aq)}$ and $\ce{HNO3 (aq)}$, giving a reason for your choice. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.   $\ce{HCOO-}\,\checkmark$

     $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

     A conjugate base has one fewer proton $\ce{(H+)}$ to the acid.

2.   1.   $\ce{pH}$ value less than 7. $\checkmark$

          $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

          An acidic solution has a pH less than 7.

     2.   The conjugate base of HCl is weaker than the conjugate base of HCOOH. $\checkmark$

          $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

          The stronger the acid, the weaker its conjugate base is.

3.   1.   $\ce{HA}$ is a weak acid. $\checkmark$

          $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

          Strong acids fully dissociate and so $\ce{[HA] = [H+]}.$ Weak acids only partially dissociate and so $\ce{[HA] > [H+]}$.

          A strong monoprotic acid of 0.01 M will have a $\ce{pH}$ of $2 = (-\log [\ce{H+}])$.

     2.   $\ce{HNO3 (aq)}$ has a higher electrical conductivity since it is a strong acid. $\checkmark$

          $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

          Strong acids fully dissociate/ionise in water giving a high concentration of ions in solution.
