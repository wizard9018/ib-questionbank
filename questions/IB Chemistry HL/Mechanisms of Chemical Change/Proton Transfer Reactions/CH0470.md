# CH0470

Which statement about the equivalence point of acid-base titrations is incorrect?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The $\ce{pH}$ at the equivalence point depends on the strength of the acid and base

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The gradient at the equivalence point is the steepest section on a titration curve

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The equivalence point occurs when the stoichiometrically correct ratio of moles of acid and base have reacted

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The $\ce{pH}$ at the equivalence point depends on the concentrations of acid and base

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The concentrations of the acid and base will determine the volumes at which the equivalence point is reached but not the resultant $\ce{pH}$.
