# CH2221

Which compounds can be mixed as solutions of equal concentration and volume to prepare a buffer solution?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$ammonia and hydrochloric acid

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$ammonia and sodium hydroxide

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$ammonia and ammonium chloride

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$ammonia and methylamine

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

A buffer solution requires the presence of a weak acid$\text{--}$base conjugate pair. When equal concentrations of a weak base such as ammonia and its salt with a strong acid, ammonium chloride, are mixed, both ammonia and ammonium ions are present in comparable amounts. This equilibrium resists changes in pH upon addition of small amounts of acid or base.

By contrast, mixing a weak base with a strong acid in equal amounts results in complete neutralization, leaving no buffer system. A weak base with another weak base, or with a strong base, does not provide the necessary conjugate pair.
