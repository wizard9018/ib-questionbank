# CH0637

The structure of the hydrogen phosphate ion is shown.

![8.1-637](images/8.1-637.png)

How many $\ce{H+}$ ions can react with one hydrogen phosphate ion?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$1

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$2

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$3

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$4

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The $\ce{HPO4^-}$ ion contains two negatively charged oxygen atoms which can bond to hydrogen ions, producing phosphoric acid ($\ce{H3PO4}$).

The $\ce{HPO4^-}$ ion can therefore react with two $\ce{H+}$ ions.

This question is moderately difficult as the student must recognise that H$^+$ ions bond to the negatively charged oxygen atoms, or that $\ce{HPO4^-}$ is derived from $\ce{H3PO4}$.
