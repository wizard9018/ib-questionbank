## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must remember the ion product constant for water, $K_w,$ relates the concentrations of hydroxide and hydrogen ions in aqueous solution.

$K_w= [\ce{H+}][\ce{OH-}]$

These two concentrations show an inverse relationship and appear as a hyperbola.

This question is categorized as easy as the student must remember the ion product of water and the relationship between the hydrogen and the hydroxide ion concentration.
