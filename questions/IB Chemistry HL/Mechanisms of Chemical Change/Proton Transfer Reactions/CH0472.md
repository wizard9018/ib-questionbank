# CH0472

Which combination of acid and base should be reacted to obtain a basic salt?

 | $\hspace{0.9em}$| | Acid | Base |
 |:-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
  || A.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\enskip$Strong$\enskip$ | $\enskip$Strong$\enskip$ |
 | | B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Strong | Weak |
 | | C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Weak | Strong |
 | | D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Weak | Weak |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
When a strong base is mixed in equal amount with a weak acid, a basic salt is formed.

Consider the reaction of sodium hydroxide with ethanoic acid:

$\ce{NaOH} + \ce{CH3COOH} \longrightarrow \ce{NaCH3COO} + \ce{H2O}$

The product, sodium ethanoate, dissociates in water to give sodium ions and ethanoate ions.
The sodium ion has no significant acid$-$base behaviour, but the ethanoate ion is the conjugate base of the weak acid, ethanoic acid. The $\ce{CH3COO-}$ ion reacts with water in hydrolysis:

$\ce{CH3COO-} + \ce{H2O} \rightleftharpoons \ce{CH3COOH} + \ce{OH-}$

This equilibrium produces hydroxide ions, making the solution basic. Therefore, salts of strong bases and weak acids hydrolyse to give solutions with pH greater than 7.
