# CH2213

Which product is formed when ethanoic acid reacts with magnesium?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$carbon dioxide

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$water

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$magnesium ethanoate

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$magnesium oxide

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must deduce the chemical reaction involved.

$\ce{Mg(s) + 2 CH3COOH(aq) -> Mg(CH3COO)2(aq) + H2(g)}$

The products are hydrogen gas and magnesium ethanoate.

This question is categorized as a medium as the student must identify the product of the chemical reaction proposed.
