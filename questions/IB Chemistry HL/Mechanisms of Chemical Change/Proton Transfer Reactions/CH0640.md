# CH0640

The reversible reaction between $\ce{NH3 and H2SO4}$ is:

$\ce{NH3 + H2SO4 <=> NH4+ + HSO4-}$

Which compounds act as bases in the forward and backward reactions?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  ||Base in forward reaction|Base in backward reaction

  |:-:|:-:|:-:|

  |A.$\hspace{1em}$|$\ce{NH3}$|$\ce{NH4+}$

  |B.$\hspace{1em}$|$\ce{NH3}$|$\ce{HSO4-}$

  |C.$\hspace{1em}$|$\ce{H2SO4}$|$\ce{NH4+}$

  |D.$\hspace{1em}$|$\ce{H2SO4}$|$\ce{HSO4-}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

A base accepts a hydrogen ion, whereas an acid donates it. The base in the forward and backward reactions are therefore the species which gain hydrogen ions during the reaction: $\ce{NH3}$ and $\ce{HSO4^-}$, respectively.

This question is moderately difficult as the student must recall the definition of a base and identify specific examples of bases in a reaction equation.
