# CH2225

A weak base of concentration $0.100\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$ has $K_b = 1.0\times 10^{-7}.$ What is the approximate pOH of this solution?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}1$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}4$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}10$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}13$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

A generic weak base, B, partially hydrolyzes water to form its conjugate acid and hydroxide ion.  To determine the amount of hydroxide at equilibrium, and the pOH, we use a RICE table:

| Reaction: | $\ce{B}$ | $+$ | $\quad \ce{H2O} \quad$ | $\ce{<=>}$ | $\quad \ce{HB+} \quad$ | $+$ | $\quad \ce{OH-} \quad$ |
| :-- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\textbf{Initial:}$ | 0.100    ||||    0 ||    0
| $\textbf{Concentration:}$ | $-x$ ||||$+x$ ||$+x$
| $\textbf{Equilibrium:}$ | $0.100-x$   ||||$x$ || $x$

$\ $

$K_b = 1.0\times 10^{-7} = \dfrac{\ce{[HB+]}\ce{[OH-]}}{\ce{[B]}} = \dfrac{x^2}{0.100-x}$

Since $K_b$ is very small, $x$ is very small. Very little of the base reacts so

$\dfrac{x^2}{0.100}\approx 1.0\times 10^{-7}$

$x\approx \sqrt{1.0\times 10^{-7}\times 0.100} = 1.0\times 10^{-4}$

Since $x$ represents the concentration of hydroxide ion,

$\text{pOH} =-\log 1.0\times 10^{-4} = 4$

This is medium difficulty question, students must recall the definition of $K_b,$ set up and solve the equilibrium expression using the small$-x$ approximation, and connect $[\mathrm{OH-}]$to pOH accurately.
