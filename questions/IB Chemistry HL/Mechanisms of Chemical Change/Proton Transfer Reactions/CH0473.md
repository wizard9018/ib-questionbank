# CH0473

**Maximum mark: 6**

1.
    1.  Differentiate between a strong acid and a weak acid. [1 mark]

    2.  State a balanced equation for the reaction between $\ce{KOH}$ and $\ce{HNO3}$.  [1 mark]

Measuring the $\ce{pH}$ can be useful when trying to differentiate between different solutions.

A student has three unlabelled bottles of white powders. They know that the bottles contain either $\ce{KOH, KOOCCH3}$ or $\ce{KNO3}$. The student has access to a pH meter and distilled water.

2.  Outline how the student can determine which powder is inside each of the three unlabelled bottles. Include equations where relevant. [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  A strong acid completely ionises in water whereas a weak acid only partially ionises in water. $\checkmark$

    2.  $\ce{KOH}(aq)+\ce{HNO3}(aq) \ce{->KNO3}(aq)+\ce{H2O}(l)\,  \checkmark$\
        *State symbols not required*

2.  The student can dissolve a similar amount of each of the white powders in water and test the $pH$ of the subsequent solutions. $\checkmark$

    $\ce{KOH}$ is a strong base so it will have the highest $pH$. $\checkmark$

    The $\ce{CH3COOK}$ will produce $\ce{CH3COO-}$ and $\ce{K+}$ ions in water. The $\ce{CH3COO-}$ ion is the conjugate base of a weak acid so it will react with water to form a basic solution/ so will have the next highest $\ce{pH}$:

    $\ce{CH3COO-(aq) + H2O(l) <=> CH3COOH + OH-}\,\checkmark$

    $\ce{KNO3}$ is a salt formed from the reaction between a strong acid and a strong base so when dissolved in water, the $\ce{K+}$ and $\ce{NO3-}$ ions should not react so the $pH$ will be around 7. $\checkmark$
