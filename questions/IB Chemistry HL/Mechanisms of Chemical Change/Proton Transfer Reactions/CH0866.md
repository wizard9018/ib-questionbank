# CH0866

Which of the following statements about the pH scale is incorrect?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$pH can be negative

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$A pH vs $\ce{[H+]}$ graph has a negative logarithmic relationship

-    C. $\hspace{1em}$A weak acid can have a lower pH than a strong acid

-    D. $\hspace{1em}$An acid with a lower pH requires more moles of base to neutralise compared to the same number of moles of an acid with a higher pH

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\text{pH} = -\log_{10}\ce{[H+]}$

Generally, pH is measured on a scale of $0$ to $14$, but the equation above shows that pH can be negative (e.g., a 2 M solution of sulfuric acid). Statement A is correct.

The equation also shows a negative logarithmic relationship between pH and $\ce{[H+]}$. Statement B is correct.

The difference between a weak and strong acid is that a weak acid only partially ionizes in water, whereas a strong acid is assumed to ionize in water fully.

As pH only depends on the concentration of $\ce{H+}$, a very concentrated solution of a weak acid could have a lower pH than a very dilute solution of a strong acid; therefore, statement C is correct.

The number of moles of base required for neutralization depends on the stoichiometric coefficients of the balanced chemical reaction and has nothing to do with the pH of the acid.

For example, sulfuric acid is a strong diprotic acid $\ce{(H_2SO_4\+2NaOH \to Na_2SO_4\+ 2H_2O)}$, so it would need twice as much base to neutralize as the same number of moles of ethanoic acid – a weak, monoprotic acid $\ce{(CH_3COOH\+ NaOH \to CH_3COONa\+ H_2O)}$ so statement D is incorrect.
