# CH1920

A student prepares four solutions of equal concentration $(0.10\text{ mol dm}^{-3}):$ $\ce{HCl},$ $\ce{H2SO4},$ $\ce{NH3},$ and $\ce{NaOH}.$ Which order correctly ranks these solutions from lowest to highest pH?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{HCl} < \ce{H2SO4} < \ce{NH3} < \ce{NaOH}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{H2SO4} < \ce{HCl} < \ce{NH3} < \ce{NaOH}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{HCl} < \ce{NH3} < \ce{H2SO4} < \ce{NaOH}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{NH3} < \ce{H2SO4} < \ce{HCl} < \ce{NaOH}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
$\ce{H2SO4}$ and HCl are strong acids, but $\ce{H2SO4}$ is diprotic. The concentration of $\ce{H+}$ ions will be greater than $0.10\textrm{ mol dm}^{-3}$ in the sulfuric acid solution and equal to $0.10\textrm{ mol dm}^{-3}$ in the hydrochloric acid solution.  $\ce{H2SO4}$ will have the lowest pH. $\ce{NH3}$ (weak base) will be basic but less so than NaOH (strong base), which will have the highest pH.
