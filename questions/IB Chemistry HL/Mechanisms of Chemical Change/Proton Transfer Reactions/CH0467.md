# CH0467

Which indicator would be most suitable for a titration between sodium hydroxide and ethanoic acid?

| $\enskip$Indicator$\enskip$ | $\enskip \textbf{p}$***K***$_\mathbf{a}$ $\enskip$ |
| :---: | :---: |
| Methyl orange | 3.7 |
| Bromocresol green | 4.7 |
| $\enskip$Bromothymol blue$\enskip$ | 7.0 |
| Phenolphthalein | 9.6 |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Methyl orange

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Bromocresol green

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Bromothymol blue

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Phenolphthalein

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Ethanoic acid is a weak acid and sodium hydroxide is a strong base. At the equivalence point, $\ce{pH}>7$, therefore the indicator chosen must have an endpoint $ > 7 $ (as determined by its $pK_a$).
