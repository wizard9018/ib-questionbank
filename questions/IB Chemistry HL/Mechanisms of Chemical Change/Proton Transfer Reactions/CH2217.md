# CH2217

Consider the titration curve below.

![CH2217](images/CH2217.png)

Which statement is correct?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :- | :- |
    | I. | It is a weak acid titrated against a strong base. |
    | II. | The indicator should have $\text{p}K_a \approx 5$ for an adequate detection of the end point. |
    | III. | The volume at the buffer zone is $25\text{ cm}^3.$ |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$I only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

Consider the annotated titration curve:

![CH2217ms](images/CH2217ms.png)

The equivalence point is identified by the midpoint of the near$-$vertical rise and lies close to pH 9. Because a strong acid/strong base titration gives an equivalence pH of 7, a value well above 7 indicates that the analyte is a weak acid neutralised by a strong base. Statement I is correct.

For reliable visual detection, an indicator must change colour within $\pm 1$ pH unit of the equivalence pH. An end point near pH 9 requires an indicator whose $\text{p}K_a$ is likewise around 9. Statement II is incorrect.

The vertical section occurs at about $25\text{ cm}^3,$ so the half$\text{--}$equivalence (and hence the center of the buffer region) is at roughly $12.5\text{ cm}^3,$ not $25\text{ cm}^3.$ Statement III is incorrect.
