# CH0641

In the autoionisation of water, a reversible reaction, two molecules of water react together to produce an acidic species A and a basic species B.

$\ce{2 H2O(l) <=> A(aq) + B(aq)}$

Which of the following statements are true?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}

    | | |

    | :- | :- |

    | I. | A and B are a conjugate acid$-$base pair. |

    | II. | A contains more hydrogen atoms than  B. |

    | III. | The reaction demonstrates that water is both amphoteric and amphiprotic. |

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

$\ce{H2O}$ molecules react to produce $\ce{H3O+}$ (A) and OH$^-$ (B). A contains more hydrogen atoms than  B, as it is produced by the transfer of a hydrogen ion from one water molecule to another.

This reaction demonstrates that water is amphiprotic, as it can both donate and accept a hydrogen ion. It is also amphoteric, as all amphiprotic molecules are also amphoteric (able to act as both an acid and a base) by definition.

A and B are not a conjugate acid$-$base pair, however, as they differ by more than one hydrogen ion: in fact, water is the conjugate base of A and the conjugate acid of B.

This question is medium as the student must recall multiple pieces of information from the syllabus and correctly identify an unknown acid and base.
