# CH0247

The correct sequence in decreasing order of $\ce{pH}$ for equimolar solutions is:

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{CH3COOH} > \ce{HCl} > \ce{NH3} >  \ce{NaOH}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{HCl} > \ce{CH3COOH} > \ce{NaOH} > \ce{NH3}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{NaOH} > \ce{NH3}> \ce{HCl} > \ce{CH3COOH}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{NaOH}  >\ce{NH3} > \ce{CH3COOH}  > \ce{HCl}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\ce{NaOH}$ is a strong base and has a high $pH,\,\ce{NH3}$ is a weak base with a lower $pH$ than $\ce{NaOH}.\,
\ce{CH3COOH}$ is a weak acid, $\ce{HCl}$ is a strong acid and so has the lowest $pH$.
