# CH2212

A $50.0\text{ cm}^3$  sample of $\ce{HNO3(aq)}$ with $\text{pH} = 2.00$ is diluted to a final volume of $500\text{ cm}^3.$ Which statement is correct?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :- | :- |
    | I. | The amount, in mol, of $\ce{HNO3}$ is the same in the initial and final solution. |
    | II. | The diluted solution has $\text{pH} = 1.00.$ |
    | III. | $[\ce{NO3-}] = 0.00100\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$ in the diluted solution. |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$I only

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand that the dilution of an acid is a procedure that consists of adding water. The amount of acid remains constant (as only solvent is added) even though its concentration will be lowered. Statement I is correct.

The dilution of $50.0\text{ cm}^3$ of $\ce{HNO3(aq)}$ to $500\text{ cm}^3$ means the volume increases by a factor of 10. The concentration decreases by the same factor of 10. The initial nitric acid solution has $\text{pH} = 2.00.$ As this is a strong acid, the initial concentration of the acid was $1.0\times 10^{-2}\text{ mol dm}^{-3}.$ After the dilution, the concentration is ten times smaller, $1.0\times 10^{-3}\text{ mol dm}^{-3}.$ The resulting pH is 3.0. Statement II is incorrect.

The nitrate ion results from the complete dissociation of the nitric acid. In the diluted solution, we can assume that the nitric acid is fully dissociated into hydrogen and nitrate ions. So, statement III is correct.

This question is categorized as a medium, as the student must understand how a dilution affects the pH of an acid.
