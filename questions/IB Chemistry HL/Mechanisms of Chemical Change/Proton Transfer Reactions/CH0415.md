# CH0415

The pH of a $0.1 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ solution of phenol is 5.0. What is the approximate $K_a$ of phenol?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$9

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$10

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$10$^{-9}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$10$^{-10}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\

Phenol is a weak acid that dissociates according to:

$\ce{C6H5OH}\textrm{(aq)} \rightleftharpoons \ce{H^{+}}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{C6H5O^{-}}\textrm{(aq)}$

From the pH: $[\ce{H^{+}}] = 10^{-5} \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}.$ By stoichiometry, $[\ce{C6H5O^{-}}] = 10^{-5} \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}.$

| | $\ce{C6H5OH}$ | $\ce{H^{+}}$ | $\ce{C6H5O^{-}}$ |

|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|

| $\textrm{Initial}$ | $0.1$ | $0$ | $0$ |

| $\textrm{Change}$ | $-10^{-5}$ | $+10^{-5}$ | $+10^{-5}$ |

| $\textrm{Equilibrium}$ | $\approx 0.1$ | $10^{-5}$ | $10^{-5}$ |

Since only a tiny fraction of phenol dissociates ($10^{-5} \ll 0.1$), the equilibrium concentration of phenol is approximately equal to its initial concentration.

Substituting into the $K_a$ expression:

$K_a = \dfrac{[\ce{H^{+}}][\ce{C6H5O^{-}}]}{[\ce{C6H5OH}]} = \dfrac{(10^{-5})^2}{10^{-1}} = \dfrac{10^{-10}}{10^{-1}} = 10^{-9}$
