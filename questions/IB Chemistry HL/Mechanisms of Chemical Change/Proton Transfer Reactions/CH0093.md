# CH0093

What is correct about a reaction between nitric acid and potassium carbonate?

- I.$\hspace{1em}$ It is exothermic\

II.$\hspace{0.7em}$ It produces carbon dioxide gas\

III.$\hspace{0.4em}$ It produces KNO$_3$

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Nitric acid and potassium carbonate react to produce potassium nitrate KNO$_3$, water, and carbon dioxide. Neutralization reactions are exothermic.
