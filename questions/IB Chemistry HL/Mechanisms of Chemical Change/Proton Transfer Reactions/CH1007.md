# CH1007

$\ce{H2X}$ is amphiprotic. Which combination below is correct?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | H$\mathbf{_2}$X conjugate base | H$\mathbf{_2}$X conjugate acid |
    |:-:|:-:|:-:|
    | A. | $\ce{HX-}$ | $\ce{H3X+}$ |
    | B. | $\ce{HX-}$ | $\ce{X^2-}$ |
    | C. | $\ce{H3X+}$ | $\ce{HX-}$ |
    | D. | $\ce{H3X+}$ | $\ce{X^2-}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

An amphiprotic species can accept or donate a proton. The conjugate acid and conjugate base can be formed as a result.

When $\ce{H2X}$ acts as a base, its conjugate acid is formed after accepting a proton, as shown below.

$\ce{H2X + H+ -> H3X+}$

$\ce{H3X+}$ is the conjugate acid of $\ce{H2X}$. When $\ce{H2X}$ acts as an acid, its conjugate base is formed after donating a proton as shown below.

$\ce{H2X -> HX- + H+}$

$\ce{HX-}$ is the conjugate base of $\ce{H2X}$.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as the student must recall the concept of amphiprotic, as well as that of conjugate acid and conjugate base for this specific species.
