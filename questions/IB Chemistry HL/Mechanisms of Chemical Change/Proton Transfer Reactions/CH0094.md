# CH0094

Which of the following acid-base pairs are correctly matched with the salt formed from their reaction? 

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  | | Acid | Base |Salt|

  | --- | :-: | :-: |:-:|

  | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hydrochloric| Potassium oxide | KCl|

  | B. | Nitric | Ammonia | NH$_4$Cl|

  | C. | Sulphuric | Copper(II) hydroxide |CuS|

  | D. | Ethanoic | Magnesium |MgCO$_3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Hydrochloric acid produces chloride salts, nitric acid produces nitrate (NO$_3^-$) salts, sulphuric acid produces sulphate (SO$_4^{2-}$) salts, and ethanoic acid produces ethanoate (CH$_3$COO$^-$) salts.
