# CH1176

Which ion is the conjugate acid of $\ce{H2CO3}?$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{HCO3-}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CO3^2-}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CO3-}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H3CO3+}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

A conjugate acid-base pair are species that differ by a single proton. When $\ce{H2CO3}$ accepts an additional proton, the conjugate acid of it is formed.

$\ce{H2CO3 + H+ -> H3CO3+}$

$\ce{H3CO3+}$ is the conjugate acid.

This question is categorized as easy as students must deduce the correct conjugate acid for a species.
