# CH1245

Which of the following is a conjugate acid-base pair?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H3PO4}$ and $\ce{PO4^3-}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H3PO4}$ and $\ce{HPO4^2-}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H2PO4-}$ and $\ce{PO4^3-}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{HPO4^2-}$ and $\ce{PO4^3-}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

A conjugate acid-base pair is a pair of species differing by a single proton. In this case, most of them differ by more than a single proton except for option D.

$\begin{aligned}
\text{Option A:}&\quad &\ce{H3PO4 &-> 3 H+ + PO4^3-} \\
\text{Option B:}&\quad &\ce{H3PO4 &-> 2 H+ + HPO4^2-} \\
\text{Option C:}&\quad &\ce{H2PO4- &-> 2 H+ + PO4^3-} \\
\text{Option D:}&\quad &\ce{HPO4^2- &-> H+ + PO4^3-}
\end{aligned}$

This question is categorized as easy as students must apply their knowledge of conjugate acid-base pairs to the phosphoric acid dissociation.
