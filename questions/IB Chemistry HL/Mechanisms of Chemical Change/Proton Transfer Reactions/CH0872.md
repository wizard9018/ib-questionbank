# CH0872

Which change in pH results in the largest decrease in the concentration of hydroxide ions, $\ce{[OH-]}?$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$From pH = 1 to pH = 2

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$From pH = 2 to pH = 1

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$From pH = 12 to pH = 14

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$From pH = 14 to pH = 12

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\
A decrease of one pH unit corresponds to a ten$-$fold increase in the concentration of $\ce{H+}$ ions and therefore a ten$-$fold decrease in the concentration of
$\ce{OH-}$ ions, since $\textrm{pH}+\textrm{pOH}=14.$ The largest decrease in $\ce{[OH-]}$ occurs when the pH decreases the most, from 14 to 12, producing a hundred$-$fold decrease in hydroxide ion concentration.
