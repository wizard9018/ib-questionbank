# CH0237

**Maximum mark: 4**

A bottle contains dilute ethanoic acid $\ce{CH3COOH}$ of unknown concentration. To determine the concentration of the acid, a student titrates the acid against a standard solution of $\ce{KOH}$ according to the following equation:

$\ce{CH3COOH(aq) + KOH(aq) -> CH3COOK(aq) + H2O(l)}$

1.  Calculate the mass of $\ce{KOH}$ that must be used to prepare 400.0 cm$^3$  of a 0.100 mol dm$^{-3}\,\ce{KOH}$ solution. [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the pH of the 0.100 mol dm$^{-3}\,\ce{KOH}$ solution given that it ionizes completely in solution as shown in the following equation: $\ce{KOH(aq) -> K+(aq) + OH-(aq)}$ [1 mark]

3.  During the titration, the student finds that exactly 20.0 cm$^3$ of the $\ce{KOH}$ solution neutralizes exactly 25.0 cm$^3$ of the $\ce{CH3COOH}$ solution. Calculate the concentration of the $\ce{CH3COOH}$ solution. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $n(\ce{KOH})  = C\times V = (0.100\,\text{mol dm}^{-3})(400.0\times 10^{-3}\,\text{dm}^3) =  0.0400\,\text{mol}\,\checkmark$

    $m(\ce{KOH}) = n\times M = (0.0400\,\text{mol})(56.11\,\text{g mol}^{-1}) = 2.24\,\text{g}\,\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    $M(\ce{KOH}) = 39.10 + 16.00 + 1.01 = 56.11$ g mol$^{-1}$*

2. $\begin{aligned}
    \\
    \ce{[H+]} &= \dfrac{1.00\times 10^{-14}}{0.100} = 1.00\times 10^{-13}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\checkmark \\
    \ce{pH} &= -\log \ce{[H+]} = 13.0\,\checkmark
    \end{aligned}$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    $\ce{[OH-]}=  0.100\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

    $K_w = \ce{[H+][OH-]}$

3.  $n(\ce{KOH}) = C\times V = (0.1\,\text{mol dm}^{-3})(20.0\times 10^{-3}\,\text{dm}^3) = 2.00\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}$

    Mole ratio $1:1$ in the chemical equation. Therefore, $\ce{n(CH3COOH)}$ that reacted = $2.00\times 10^{-3}$ mol. $\checkmark$

    $C(\ce{CH3COOH}) = \dfrac{n}{V} = \dfrac{2.00\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}}{25\times 10^{-3}\,\text{dm}^3} = 0.0800\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\checkmark$
