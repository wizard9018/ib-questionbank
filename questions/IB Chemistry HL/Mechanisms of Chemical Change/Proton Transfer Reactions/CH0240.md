# CH0240

An example of a strong acid solution is nitric acid. Which statement is correct for this solution?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$It is completely ionized in water

- B.$\hspace{1em}$It exists mainly as undissociated molecules in solution

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The solution reacts only with weak bases

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The solution has a $\ce{pH}$ value greater than 7

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

By definition, strong acids completely dissociate in water.
