# CH0238

**Maximum mark: 4**

The production of yogurt from milk is by bacteria fermentation, during which lactose $\ce{(C6H12O11)}$ is converted to lactic acid $\ce{(C3H6O3)}$ in an aqueous solution. A student recorded the $\ce{pH}$ of the solution on daily basis for 72 hours. The results are shown in the table below:

| $\enskip$Time ( hours)$\enskip$ |$\hspace{1em}$ pH $\hspace{1em}$|
| :---: | :---: |
| $0$ | $5.7$ |
| $24$ | $5.1$ |
| $48$ | $4.7$ |
| $72$ | $4.1$ |

1. Suggest how the measurements of $\ce{pH}$ can be made more reliable. [1 mark]

2. Calculate the change in hydroxide ions concentration after 48 hours. [2 marks]

3. Explain why scientists prefer to describe the acidity of solutions in terms of $\ce{pH}$ rather than concentration of hydrogen ions. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  perform multiple experiments and average values $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
    Reliability is linked to repeatable results. By performing more trials and calculating an average, reliability would be improved.

2.  $\Delta\textrm{pH} = -1$ $\checkmark$\
    $\ce{[OH-]}$ decreases by factor of 10 $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
    After 48 hours the $\ce{pH}$ drops by exactly one unit $(5.7 \rightarrow 4.7)$.

    Since the solution has become more acidic; therefore, the concentration of $\ce{H+}$ ions has increased by a factor of 10 whilst the concentration of $\ce{OH-}$ ions has decreased by a factor of 10.

    This ensures that the product of $\ce{[OH-]}$ and $\ce{[H+]}$ remains constant.

3.  $\ce{pH}$ values  are  easier to understand than  actual  $\ce{[H+]}$, which are very small  numbers $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
    Logarithmic scales such as the $\ce{pH}$ scale turn values with very high positive of negative orders of magnitude to more manageable values.
