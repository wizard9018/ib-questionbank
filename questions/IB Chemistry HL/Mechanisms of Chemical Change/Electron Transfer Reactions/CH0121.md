# CH0121

The diagram below shows a Daniel cell. Zinc is more reactive than copper. When the cell is operating, which statement about the ions in the cell is correct?

![CH0121](images/CH0121.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Positive ions in the salt bridge diffuse into the copper half cell

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Positive ions in the salt bridge diffuse into the zinc half cell

- C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Zn^{2+}(aq)}$ ions are deposited at the zinc electrode as $\ce{Zn(s)}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The concentration of $\ce{Cu^{2+}(aq)}$  ions increases in the copper half cell

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
The positive ions (cations) move in the direction of the cathode as the positive $\ce{Cu^{2+}}$ ions are used up.\
Zn being more reactive than copper, Zn(s) is the anode where oxidation occurs, and Cu(s) is the cathode where reduction of $\ce{Cu^{2+}(aq)}$ occurs.
