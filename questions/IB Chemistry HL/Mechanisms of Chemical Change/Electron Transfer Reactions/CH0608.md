# CH0608

What is the balanced chemical equation and $E^\ominus_{cell}$ for the spontaneous reaction between the following half-cells?

$\ce{Pb^{2+}(aq) + 2e- -> Pb(s)}$ $\hspace{1em}$  $-0.13 \textrm{\ V}$\

$\ce{Cu^{2+}(aq) + 2e- -> Cu(s)}$       $\hspace{1em}$ $+0.34 \textrm{\ V}$   

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  ||Balanced Chemical Equation| ***E***$^{⦵}_\textrm{cell}$|

  |:-:|:-:|:-:|

  A. $\hspace{1em}$|$\ce{Pb^{2+}(aq) + Cu(s) \longrightarrow Pb(s) + Cu^{2+}(aq)}$| $+0.47 \textrm{\ V}$

  B. $\hspace{1em}$|$\ce{Pb^{2+}(aq) + Cu(s) \longrightarrow Pb(s) + Cu^{2+}(aq)}$| $+0.21 \textrm{\ V}$

  C. $\hspace{1em}$|$\ce{Cu^{2+}(aq) + Pb(s) → Cu(s) + Pb^{2+}(aq)}$|$+0.47 \textrm{\ V}$

  D. $\hspace{1em}$|$\ce{Cu^{2+}(aq) + Pb(s) → Cu(s) + Pb^{2+}(aq)}$|$+0.21 \textrm{\ V}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The $\ce{Cu^{2+}/Cu}$ half cell has a more positive electrode potential and so will proceed in the forwards direction (reduction).

The $\ce{Pb^{2+}/Pb}$ half equation will therefore proceed in the backward direction (oxidation).

$E^\ominus_{cell}= E^\ominus_{reduction} - E^\ominus_{oxidation} = +0.34 +0.13 = +0.47 \textrm{\ V}.$
