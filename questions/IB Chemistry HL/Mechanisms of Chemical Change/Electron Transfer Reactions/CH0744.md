# CH0744

What is the sum of the coefficients when the following redox equation is balanced?

$\ce{\text{\_\_} O2(g) + \text{\_\_} Br^-(aq) + \text{\_\_} H^+(aq) -> \text{\_\_} H2O(l) + \text{\_\_} Br2(l)}$

$\ \mbox{}\\[-20pts]$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$13

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$10

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$8

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$6

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Oxidation: $(\ce{2 Br-(aq) -> Br2(l) + 2 e-})\quad \times 2$

Reduction: $\ce{O2(g) + 4 e- + 4 H+ -> 2 H2O(l)}$
***
Redox reaction: $\ce{4 H+(aq) + 4 Br-(aq) + O2(g) -> 2 Br2(l) + 2 H2O(l)}$

$4 + 4 + 1 + 2 + 2 = 13$
