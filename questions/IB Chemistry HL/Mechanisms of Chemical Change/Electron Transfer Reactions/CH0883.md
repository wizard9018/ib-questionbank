# CH0883

What deduction can be made about a voltaic cell represented by the following cell notation?

$\ce{Zn(s) | Zn^2+(aq) || Cu^2+(aq) | Cu(s)}$

- A. $\hspace{1em}$Zinc is oxidized at the cathode

- B. $\hspace{1em}$Zinc is the negative electrode

- C. $\hspace{1em}$Copper is the reducing agent

- D. $\hspace{1em}$Copper is oxidized at the anode

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Reduction occurs at the cathode $\ce{(Cu)}$ and oxidation at the anode $\ce{(Zn)}$.

The cell convention notes the reaction at the anode on the left and that at the cathode on the right.

In a voltaic cell, the cathode is the positive electrode.
