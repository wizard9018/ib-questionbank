# CH0614

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the following redox reaction:

$\ce{Cr2O7^2-(aq) + 14 H+(aq) + 6 Ag(s) -> 2 Cr^3+(aq) + 7 H2O(l) + 6 Ag+(aq)}$

1.  1.  Using section 24 (*Section 19- 2025 Syllabus*) of the data booklet, calculate the standard cell potential. [1 mark]

    2.  Predict, with a reason, whether this reaction is spontaneous or not. [1 mark]

2.  Using sections 1 and 2 of the data booklet, calculate the standard Gibbs free energy change in $kJ\, mol^{-1}$. [2 marks]

The standard entropy change for this reaction is 508 J K$^{-1}$ mol$^{-1}$.

3.  Using section 1 of the data booklet, calculate the standard enthalpy change for this reaction in $kJ\, mol^{-1}$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1.  $E^\ominus_{cell} = E^\ominus{red} - E^\ominus{ox} = 1.36 - 0.80 = 0.56\,\text{V}\,\checkmark$

    2.  Spontaneous, as the standard cell potential is positive. $\checkmark$

2.  Number of moles of electrons transferred = 6 $\checkmark$

    $\Delta G^\ominus = -nFE^\ominus_{cell} = (-6)(96500)(0.56) = -324240\,\text{J mol}^{-1} = -324\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}\,\checkmark$

    *Allow error carried forward from incorrect (i)*.

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    Using the data booklet, six electrons are gained by $\ce{Cr2O7^2-}$.

    To balance the electrons, the $\ce{Ag/Ag+}$ half equation must be multiplied to six.

3.  $\Delta G^\ominus = \Delta H^\ominus - T\Delta S^\ominus = -324\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}\,\checkmark$

    $\Delta H^\ominus = \Delta G^\ominus + T\Delta S = -324 + (298)\left(\dfrac{508}{1000}\right) = -173\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}\,\checkmark$

    *Allow error carried forward from incorrect (i) and (ii).*
