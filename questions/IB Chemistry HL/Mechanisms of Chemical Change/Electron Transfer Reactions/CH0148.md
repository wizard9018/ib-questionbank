# CH0148

The oxidation of propan$\text{--}$1$\text{--}$ol is a two$\text{--}$stage process. Each stage forms a different product. Which of the following shows the proper product in each stage?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  ||Stage 1| Stage 2|

  |:-:|:-:|:-:|

  A.$\hspace{1em}$|Propane|Propyne

  B.$\hspace{1em}$|Propanoic acid|Methyl propanoate

  C.$\hspace{1em}$|Propanoic acid|Propanal

  D.$\hspace{1em}$|Propanal |Propanoic acid

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

The oxidation of a primary alcohol is a two$\text{--}$stage process that first produces an aldehyde followed by a carboxylic acid.

![CH0148](images/CH0148.png)

The addition of first oxygen leads to the formation of the aldehyde (propanal). The reaction does not stop there and further oxidation leads to the formation of a carboxylic acid (propanoic acid).
