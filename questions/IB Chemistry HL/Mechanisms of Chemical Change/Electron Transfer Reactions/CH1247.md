# CH1247

Consider the chemical reaction below.

![CH1247](images/CH1247.png)

Which statement about the reaction is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The chemical reaction is an oxidation, the reactant is butan$-$3$-$ol and the product is butan$-$3$-$one.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The chemical reaction is an oxidation, the reactant is butan$-$2$-$ol and the product is butanone.

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The chemical reaction is a reduction, the reactant is butan$-$3$-$ol and the product is butan$-$3$-$one.

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The chemical reaction is a reduction, the reactant is butan$-$2$-$ol and the product is butanone.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Students must interpret the conversion of a secondary alcohol to a ketone as an oxidation reaction.

![CH1247ms](images/CH1247ms.png)

Ketones with short carbon chains (3 or 4 carbons) do not need to state the number for the location of the $\ce{C=O}$ bond as it can only be in one position.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty, as students must identify the oxidation process and give the correct IUPAC name for the alcohol and the ketone.
