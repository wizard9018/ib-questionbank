# CH0745

The Winkler redox titration method can determine dissolved oxygen levels. Which statement best describes the redox chemistry which occurs in step 1 of the titration shown below:

$\ce{2 Mn^2+(aq) + O2(g) + 4 OH-(aq) -> 2 MnO2(s) + 2 H2O(l)}$

$\  \mbox{}\\[-25pts]$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Mn^2+}$ acts as the oxidizing agent, and $\ce{O2}$ is oxidized

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MnO2}$ acts as the reducing agent, and $\ce{O2}$ is reduced

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{O2}$ acts as the reducing agent, and $\ce{Mn^{2+}}$ is oxidized

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Mn^2+}$ acts as the reducing agent, and $\ce{O2}$ is reduced

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

$\ce{O2}$ is reduced to $\ce{O^{2-}}$ in $\ce{H2O}$ and is the oxidizing agent. The oxidation state of O decreases from 0 to -2.

$\ce{Mn^2+}$ is oxidized to $\ce{Mn^4+}$ in $\ce{MnO2}$ and is the reducing agent. The oxidation state of Mn increases from +2 to +4.
