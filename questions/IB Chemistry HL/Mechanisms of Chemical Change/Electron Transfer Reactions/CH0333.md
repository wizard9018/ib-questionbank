# CH0333

The diagram below represents a methanol fuel cell.

 ![CH0333](images/CH0333.png)

Which combination is correct?

| $\hspace{1.em}$||| $\qquad$X$\qquad$ | $\qquad$Y$\qquad$ | $\qquad$Z$\qquad$ |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
|| A. || $\ce{H2O}$ | $\ce{CO2}$ | Ions flow |
|| B. || $\ce{CO2}$ | $\ce{H2O}$ | Ions flow |
|| C. || $\ce{CO2}$ | $\ce{H2O}$ | Electrons flow |
|| D. || $\ce{H2O}$ | $\ce{CO2}$ | Electrons flow |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The student must understand how a fuel cell operates. Methanol is the fuel and enters the left compartment, where it is oxidised at the anode. Air enters the right compartment, where oxygen is reduced at the cathode.

At the anode, methanol is oxidised to carbon dioxide:

$\ce{CH3OH}\textrm{(l)} + \ce{H2O}\textrm{(l)} \longrightarrow \ce{CO2}\textrm{(g)} + 6\ce{\,H+}\textrm{(aq)} + 6\ce{\,e^-}$

At the cathode, oxygen is reduced to water:

$\ce{O2}\textrm{(g)} + 4\ce{\,H+}\textrm{(aq)} + 4\ce{\,e^-} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,H2O}\textrm{(l)}$

Therefore X $= \ce{CO2}$ and Y $= \ce{H2O}.$

The remaining decision is whether Z represents the flow of ions or electrons. The electrolyte (Z) separates the two electrodes and allows ions, in this case $\ce{H+},$ to migrate from the anode to the cathode. Electrons cannot flow through the electrolyte; they travel through the external circuit via the resistor R.

This question is categorised as medium, as the student must identify the products at each electrode in a methanol fuel cell and distinguish between ion flow through the electrolyte and electron flow through the external circuit.
