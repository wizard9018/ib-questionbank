# CH1049

What is the cell potential, in $V$, for the reaction that occurs when the following two half$-$cells are connected?
$\ \newline \ $

$\begin{aligned}
\ce{2 H+(aq) + 2 e- &-> H2 (g)}\qquad & E^{\minuso} &= 0\textrm{\ V} \\
\ce{Cu+(aq) + e- &-> Cu(s)}\qquad & E^{\minuso} &= + 0.52\textrm{\ V}
\end{aligned}$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}+ 0.52\textrm{\ V}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}- 0.52\textrm{\ V}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}-1.04\textrm{\ V}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}+1.04\textrm{\ V}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
When these two half$-$cells are connected copper is reduced and hydrogen is oxidized. The standard electrode potential ($E^{\minuso}$) is greater for species with a greater tendency to be reduced. In this case, copper is reduced.

The standard cell potential can be calculated as shown below.

$E^{\minuso}_{cell} =  E^{\minuso}_{reduction} - E^{\minuso}_{oxidation}$

In this case:

$E^{\minuso}_{cell} =  + 0.52\textrm{\ V} - 0\textrm{\ V} = + 0.52\textrm{\ V}$

The standard cell potential is positive for a spontaneous or voltaic cell.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty. Students must understand how to calculate the standard cell potential.
