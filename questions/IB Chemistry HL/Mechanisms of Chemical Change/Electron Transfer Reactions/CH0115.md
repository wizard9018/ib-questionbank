# CH0115

The diagram below shows a voltaic cell. The overall reaction taking place in the cell is:

$$\ce{Mg(s) + Pb^2+(aq) -> Pb(s) + Mg^2+(aq)}$$

![CH0115](images/CH0115.png)

Which statement is correct for the magnesium half cell?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Magnesium strip is the cathode and is reduced

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Magnesium strip is the anode and is oxidized

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Magnesium strip is the cathode and is oxidized

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Magnesium strip is the anode and is reduced

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
$\ce{Mg(s)}$ loses electrons to produce $\ce{Mg^{2+}(aq)}.$ Loss of electrons is oxidation which takes place at the anode.
