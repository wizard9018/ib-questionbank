# CH1179

Which statement is correct?

-   A.

    $\mbox{}\\[-48pts]$

    -   Primary cells convert chemical energy into electrical energy, whereas secondary cells involve *reversible* redox reactions $\mbox{}\\[8pts]$

-   B.

    $\mbox{}\\[-48pts]$

    -   Primary cells convert chemical energy into electrical energy, whereas secondary cells involve *irreversible* redox reactions $\mbox{}\\[8pts]$

-   C.

    $\mbox{}\\[-48pts]$

    -   Primary cells convert electrical energy into chemical energy, whereas secondary cells involve *reversible* redox reactions  $\mbox{}\\[8pts]$

-   D.

    $\mbox{}\\[-38pts]$

    -   Primary cells convert electrical energy into chemical energy, whereas secondary cells involve *irreversible* redox reactions

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Primary cells, also known as voltaic cells, use spontaneous chemical reactions to generate electrical energy from reactants (which contain chemical energy).

Secondary cells, or rechargeable cells, use chemical reactions to generate electricity. This process can be reversed with an external energy source, so we say it is reversible.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students distinguish primary and secondary cells.
