# CH2466

Consider the electrolysis of aqueous concentrated copper(II) chloride. Which statement is correct?

-   I.$\hspace{1em}$A colourless gas is formed at the cathode.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The amount of substance produced at the cathode and anode is in a 1:2 ratio.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$An external source of energy is needed to drive the redox reaction.

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$I and III only

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$I, II and III

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$II only

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Concentrated copper(II) chloride solution contains $\ce{Cu^{2+}}$ ions, $\ce{Cl-}$ ions, and water. At each electrode, the question is whether the solute ion or water is discharged.

At the cathode, $\ce{Cu^{2+}}$ is preferentially reduced over water because its $E^{\minuso}$ $(+0.34\textrm{ V})$ is higher than that of water $(-0.83\textrm{ V}):$

$\ce{Cu^{2+}}\textrm{(aq)} + 2\ce{\,e-} \longrightarrow \ce{Cu}\textrm{(s)}$

Copper metal, not a gas, forms at the cathode. Statement I is incorrect.

At the anode, the $E^{\minuso}$ values for oxidation of water $(+1.23\textrm{ V}$ for $\ce{O2/H2O})$ and chloride $(+1.36\textrm{ V}$ for $\ce{Cl2/Cl-})$ are close. In a concentrated solution the high $[\ce{Cl-}]$ is enough to tip the balance, and chloride ions are oxidised in preference to water:

$2\ce{\,Cl-}\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow \ce{Cl2}\textrm{(g)} + 2\ce{\,e-}$

The overall equation is:

$\ce{Cu^{2+}}\textrm{(aq)} + 2\ce{\,Cl-}\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow \ce{Cu}\textrm{(s)} + \ce{Cl2}\textrm{(g)}$

One mole of copper is produced for every one mole of chlorine, giving a 1:1 ratio, not 1:2. Statement II is incorrect.

Electrolysis is a nonspontaneous process. The reduction of $\ce{Cu^{2+}}$ coupled with the oxidation of $\ce{Cl-}$ does not proceed on its own; an external source of energy such as a battery is required. Statement III is correct.
