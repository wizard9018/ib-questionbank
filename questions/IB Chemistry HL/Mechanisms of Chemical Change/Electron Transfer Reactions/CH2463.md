# CH2463

Which statement is correct about the electrolysis of aqueous copper(II) sulfate with inert electrodes?

-   I.$\hspace{1em}$The mass of the anode increases.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The blue colour of the solution fades.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$The pH of the solution decreases.

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$I, II and III

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$II and III only

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$II only

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
At the cathode, $\ce{Cu^{2+}}$ ions are preferentially reduced over water because copper has a higher $E^{\minuso}$ $(+0.34\textrm{ V})$ than water $(-0.83\textrm{ V}):$

$\ce{Cu^{2+}}\textrm{(aq)} + 2\ce{\,e-} \longrightarrow \ce{Cu}\textrm{(s)}$

Solid copper deposits on the cathode.

At the anode, the competing species are sulfate ions and water. Sulfur in sulfate is already in its highest oxidation state $(+6),$ so sulfate ions cannot be oxidised further. Although they migrate toward the anode as anions, they act as spectators and are not discharged. Water is oxidised instead:

$2\ce{\,H2O}\textrm{(l)} \longrightarrow 4\ce{\,H+}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{O2}\textrm{(g)} + 4\ce{\,e-}$

Oxygen gas forms at the anode. The anode does not change in mass. Statement I is incorrect.

As $\ce{Cu^{2+}}$ ions are removed from solution and deposited as metal, the concentration of $\ce{Cu^{2+}}$ decreases. These ions are responsible for the blue colour, so the solution fades. Statement II is correct.

The oxidation of water at the anode produces $\ce{H+}$ ions. As these accumulate, the solution becomes more acidic and the pH decreases. Statement III is correct.
