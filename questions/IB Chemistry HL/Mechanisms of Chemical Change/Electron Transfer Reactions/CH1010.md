# CH1010

Consider the voltaic cell below:

![CH1010](images/CH1010.png)

Which statement is correct?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Sn(s)}$ is the cathode of the voltaic cell.

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$The concentrations of $\ce{Sn^2+}$ and $\ce{Ag+}$ ions decrease as the redox reaction occurs.

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$Electrons move through the salt bridge.

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$The mass of the silver electrode increases as the redox reaction proceeds.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
In voltaic cells, the anode is the negative terminal, and the cathode is the positive terminal.

At the anode, oxidation occurs, and electrons are lost. In this case, since the diagram shows that electrons move from tin to silver electrodes, therefore, tin is oxidized, and $\ce{Sn^2+}$ ions are formed (the concentration of $\ce{Sn^2+}$ ions increases as the redox reaction occurs).

At the cathode, reduction occurs, and electrons are gained. In this case, silver ions are reduced, and solid silver is formed (the silver electrode increases in mass).

The external circuit allows the flow of electrons from the anode to the cathode.

The functions of the salt bridge are to close the circuit and to allow the flow of anions and cations to ensure the electroneutrality of both solutions by avoiding the build-up of charges.

This question is categorized as medium as the student must understand how this voltaic cell works.
