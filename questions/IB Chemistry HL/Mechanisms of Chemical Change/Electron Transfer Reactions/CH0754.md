        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        Since a power source or battery is attached, this is an electrolytic cell and the power source drives a non-spontaneous reaction. Whereas $\ce{Ca^2+(aq)}$ is found near the top of the standard potential table (table 24) and would normally undergo oxidation, in an electrolytic cell $\ce{Ca^2+(aq)}$ will undergo a non-spontaneous process and is reduced. Therefore $\ce{Br-(aq)}$ is oxidized.
