# CH0881

What is the sum of the coefficients when the half-equation is balanced using the smallest whole-number coefficients?

$\ce{Cl2(g) + H2O(l) -> ClO3-(aq) + e- + H+(aq)}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$30

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$31

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$32

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$33

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The balanced equation is:

$\ce{Cl2(g) + 6 H2O(l) -> 2 ClO3-(aq) + 10 e- + 12 H+(aq)}$

The sum of its coefficients is 31.

The number of atoms of each element and the charges on the reactant and product sides are balanced. Do not forget to add 1 i.e., the coefficient of $\ce{Cl2}$, to the sum.
