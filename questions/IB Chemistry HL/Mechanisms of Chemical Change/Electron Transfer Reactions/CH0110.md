# CH0110

**Maximum mark: 5**

Oxygen and water are produced when nitric acid reacts with potassium manganate(VII), KMnO$_{4}$.

$\ce{4 KMnO4(aq) + 12 HNO3(aq) -> 6 H2O(l) + 5 O2(g) + 4 KNO3(aq) + 4 Mn(NO3)2(aq)}$

1.  Define reduction in terms of electron transfer. [1 mark]

2.  State the oxidation state of manganese in KMnO$_{4}$ and in Mn(NO$_{3}$)$_{2}$. [2 marks]

3.  Identify which species has been oxidized in this reaction and specify the change in oxidation state it has undergone. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Gain of one or more electrons. $\checkmark$

2. Assign $x$ for the oxidation state of Mn.\
In KMnO$_{4}$: $x$ = :answer[+7]; $\checkmark$\
In Mn(NO$_{3}$)$_{2}$: $x$ = :answer[+2]; $\checkmark$

   *Must have + sign for mark.*

   *Award 1 mark if roman numerals or 7+ or 2+ used or if + signs are missing.*

   $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

   Assign $x$ for the oxidation state of Mn.

   In KMnO$_{4}$: $+1 +x +4(-2) = 0$, therefore $x = +7$

   In Mn(NO$_{3}$)$_{2}$: $x + 2(-1) = 0$, therefore $x = +2$

3. Oxygen has been oxidized. $\checkmark$

   Its oxidation state increased from $-2$ to 0 (in O$_{2}$). $\checkmark$

   $\underline{\text{Further Explanation:}}$

   Since the reaction is occurring in an acidic aqueous environment, the oxidation reaction is best described as water being oxidized to form oxygen gas, hydrogen ions, and electrons.

   The (net$-$ionic) half$-$reactions would be:

   $\begin{aligned}
   \ce{MnO_4^- + 8 H+ + 5 e- &-> Mn^{2+} + 4 H2O} \\
   \ce{2 H2O &-> O2 + 4 H+ + 4 e-}
   \end{aligned}$

   The lowest common multiple of 4 and 5 is 20, so we can combine the half reactions to get:

   $\ce{4 MnO_4^- + 32 H+ + 10 H2O -> 4 Mn^{2+} + 16 H2O + 5 O2 + 20 H+}$

   Canceling species that appear on both sides gives:

   $\ce{4 MnO_4^- + 12 H+ -> 4 Mn^{2+} + 6 H2O + 5 O2}$

   Adding the potassium and nitrate ions gives the equation in the problem statement:

   $\ce{4 KMnO4(aq) + 12 HNO3(aq) -> 6 H2O(l) + 5 O2(g) + 4 KNO3(aq) + 4 Mn(NO3)2(aq)}$
