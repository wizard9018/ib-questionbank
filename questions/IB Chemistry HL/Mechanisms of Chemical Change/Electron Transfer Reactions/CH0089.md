# CH0089

Given that reactivity is Zn > H > Ag, which of the following reactions would occur as written? 

  - I.$\hspace{1em}$Zn + H$_2$SO$_4$ → ZnSO$_4$ + H$_2$\

  II.$\hspace{0.7em}$2Ag + ZnCl$_2$ → 2AgCl + Zn\

  III.$\hspace{0.5em}$2Ag + 2HNO$_3$ →  2AgNO$_3$ + H$_2$

  

  - A.$\hspace{1em}$I only

  - B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

-  C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

 More reactive elements will displace less reactive elements from their compounds.\

  Zinc is more reactive than hydrogen and so will displace it.\

   Silver is not more reactive than zinc or hydrogen and so reactions II and III would not take place.

 Reactive metals are easily oxidized (or are strong reducing agents).  Here zinc is the most reactive metal, or the easiest to oxidize, or the best at reducing other metals.  When zinc metal is mixed with hydrogen ions or silver ions, it will reduce them to their elemental form as zinc becomes zinc cation.  

 Inactive metals (like silver) are poor reducing agents, and are seldom found as cations. On the other hand, silver cations are easily reduced (are strong oxidizing agents) and will oxidize reactive metals. When silver cations are mixed with zinc, the silver cations will oxidize the zinc to form zinc ions; silver metal will be formed.
