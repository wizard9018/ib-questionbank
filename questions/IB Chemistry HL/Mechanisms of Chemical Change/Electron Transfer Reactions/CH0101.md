# CH0101

What happens to the $\ce{Mn^{3+}(aq)}$ ion when it is converted to $\ce{MnO4-(aq)}$? 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Its oxidation number decreases and it undergoes reduction

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Its oxidation number increases and it undergoes oxidation

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Its oxidation number increases and it undergoes reduction

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Its oxidation number decreases and it undergoes oxidation

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Oxidation state of Mn in $\ce{MnO4-}$ is +7. The change in oxidation state is from +3 to +7.\

 Oxidation refers to an increase in oxidation state.
