# CH2056

The following data was collected for the redox titration of iron(II) sulfate $(\ce{FeSO4})$ with acidified potassium permanganate $(\ce{KMnO4}):$

$\ce{MnO4- + 5 Fe^2+ + 8 H+ -> Mn^2+ + 5 Fe^3+ + 4 H2O}$

$\\$
| $[\ce{FeSO4}] = 0.0500\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$ |
| :-- |
| Volume of $\ce{FeSO4}$ to reach equivalence $ = 20.00\text{ cm}^3$ |
| Volume of $\ce{KMnO4} = 50.00\text{ cm}^3$ |

What is the concentration of $\ce{KMnO4}$ in the $50.0\text{ cm}^3$ sample?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}0.00400\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}0.0200\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}0.0500\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}0.00250\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The equivalence point of a redox reaction is when the moles of reactants are equal according to their stoichiometric ratio. To determine the concentration of the unknown, the moles of the known reactant are first determined using the concentration and titre volume:

$n = CV$

$n_\ce{FeSO4} = (0.0500\text{ mol dm}^{-3})(20.00\text{ cm}^3)\left(\dfrac{1\text{ dm}^3}{1000\text{ cm}^3}\right)$

$n_\ce{FeSO4} = 0.00100\text{ mol}$

Therefore, there were

$0.00100\text{ mol }\ce{Fe^2+}\times \dfrac{1\text{ mol }\ce{MnO4-}}{5\text{ mol }\ce{Fe^2+}} = 0.000200\text{ mol }\ce{MnO4-}$

Or, there were $0.000200\text{ mol}$ of $\ce{KMnO4}$ in the $50.0\text{ cm}^3$ sample. To determine the concentration, the following calculation is performed:

$C = \dfrac{n}{V}$

$C_\ce{KMnO4} = \left(\dfrac{0.000200\text{ mol}}{50.00\text{ cm}^3}\right)\left(\dfrac{1000\text{ cm}^3}{1\text{ dm}^3}\right)$

$C_\ce{KMnO4} =  0.00400\text{mol dm}^{-3}$
