# CH0606

Which row of the table is correct for a spontaneous reaction?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  | |***E***$\mathbf{^\ominus_{cell}}$| $\mathbf{\Delta}$***G***$\mathbf{^\ominus}$

  |:-:|:-:|:-:|

  A.$\hspace{1em}$|Positive|Negative

  B.$\hspace{1em}$|Negative|Negative

  C.$\hspace{1em}$|Positive|Positive

  D.$\hspace{1em}$|Negative|Positive

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

$\Delta G^\ominus$ = $-nFE^\ominus_{cell}$

For spontaneous reactions, $\Delta G^\ominus$ is negative, therefore $E^\ominus_{cell}$ must be positive.
