# CH0149

Which of the below reactions shows the oxidation of a secondary alcohol?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$Propan-2-ol $\ce{->}$ Propanone

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$Propan-1-ol $\ce{->}$ Propanone

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$Ethanol $\ce{->}$ Ethanal

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$Ethanol $\ce{->}$ Ethanoic acid

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

A secondary alcohol is a compound in which the carbon attached to the hydroxyl group is also attached to two other carbons.

Upon its oxidation, a removal of 2H atoms occurs and a ketone is formed.
