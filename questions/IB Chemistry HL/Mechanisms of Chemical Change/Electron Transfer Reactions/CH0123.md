# CH0123

The equation for the overall reaction in a voltaic cell is:

$\ce{Cu(s)} + 2\ce{Ag^+(aq)→Cu^{2+}(aq)} + \ce{2Ag(s)}$

Which statements are correct for this cell?

   - I.$\hspace{1em}$Electrons flow from the copper electrode to the silver electrode
   - II.$\hspace{0.7em}$Silver ions are reduced in the reaction
   - III.$\hspace{0.41em}$The copper anode will become smaller in size as the reaction proceeds

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
In the wires, electrons flow from the negative terminal (anode) to the positive terminal (cathode).\
 $\ce{Cu}$ is being oxidized. As the $\ce{Cu(s)}$ reacts to form $\ce{Cu^{2+}(aq)}$, the mass and size of the copper electrode decrease.\
  $\ce{Ag^+(aq)}$ ions gain electrons to form $\ce{Ag(s)}$.
