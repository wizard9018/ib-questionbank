# CH0113

**Maximum mark: 4**

Alkali metals react with water to form metal hydroxide and hydrogen gas.

1. Write a balanced equation between sodium and water. [1 mark]

2. Explain why this reaction is considered as a redox reaction. [1 mark]

3. 3 other alkali metals W, X and Y also react with water.

    Based on the following observations, arrange the metals in order, starting from the weakest oxidizing agent. Explain your answer. [2 marks]

| Metal | Observations                 |
| :-:   | :-:                          |
| W     | Slow reaction with water     |
| X     | Vigorous reaction with water |
| Y     | Fast reaction with water     |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\ce{2 Na(s) + 2 H2O(l) -> 2 NaOH(aq) + H2(g)}\,\checkmark$

    *State symbols not required;\
    Allow multiples*

2.  The reaction is a redox reaction since the oxidation states of the species have changed from reactant to product side. $\checkmark$

    **OR** $\hspace{1em}$

    addition of Oxygen to Na / Removal of Oxygen to $\ce{H2O}$.

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    The oxidation state of sodium increases from 0 in Na to +1 in NaOH. Therefore, sodium is oxidized.

    The oxidation state of hydrogen decreases from +1 in H$_{2}$O to 0 in H$_{2}$. Therefore, hydrogen is reduced.

3.  X < Y < W $\checkmark$

    Most reactive metal is the weakest oxidizing agent/strongest reducing agent. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    An oxidizing agent is a substance that oxidizes another substance and itself gets reduced.

    A more reactive metal loses electrons and gets oxidized more readily.

    X is the most reactive metal, followed by Y and, lastly, W.
