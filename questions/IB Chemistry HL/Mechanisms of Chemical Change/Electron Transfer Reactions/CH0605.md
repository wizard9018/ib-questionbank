# CH0605

Which solution does not produce oxygen at the anode when electrolyzed with inert electrodes?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Dilute $\ce{H2SO4(aq)}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Dilute $\ce{NaCl(aq)}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Concentrated $\ce{NaCl(aq)}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Concentrated $\ce{CuSO4(aq)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

$\ce{H2O}$ is more readily oxidized than $\ce{Cl-}$ and $\ce{SO4^{2-}}$, however, when concentrated aqueous NaCl is used, chlorine is produced at the anode.
