# CH0127

**Maximum mark: 6**

Sodium metal may be obtained commercially from the electrolysis of molten sodium chloride in a Downs cell. This cell operates as an electrolytic cell.

1. Describe, using a diagram, the essential components of this electrolytic cell. [3 marks]

2. Suggest why molten sodium metal may be collected from the top of this type of cell. [1 mark]

3. Describe two ways in which current is conducted in an electrolytic cell. [2 marks]

*Labelled positive electrode (anode) and negative electrode (cathode). Positive and negative may be seen as polarity on the power supply.*$\,\checkmark$

*Labelled electrolyte, $\ce{NaCl(l)}$, state symbol must be included.*$\,\checkmark$

2. Molten sodium metal is **less dense** than the molten electrolyte and floats.$\,\checkmark$

3. Positive ions (cations) move to the negative electrode (cathode) **and** negative ions (anions) move to the positive electrode (anode) in the electrolyte.$\,\checkmark$

    In the conductors or wires, electrons flow from the anode (positive, oxidation) to the cathode (negative, reduction).$\,\checkmark$

    *Electrons in wires and ions in solution scores one $\checkmark$ only.*
