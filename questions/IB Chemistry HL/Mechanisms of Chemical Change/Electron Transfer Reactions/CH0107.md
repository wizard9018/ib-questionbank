# CH0107

Consider the following reactions involving metals and their aqueous nitrate solutions:

|   | ${\ce {XNO3}}$ |  $\ce{Y(NO3)2}$ |  $\ce{Z(NO3)2}$ |

|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|

|$\textbf{Metal X}$|  |No reaction|No reaction|

|$\textbf{Metal Y}$|Grey solid of metal X observed|   |No reaction| 

|$\textbf{Metal Z}$|Grey solid of metal X observed|Pink solid of metal Y observed|   |

Choose the correct order of the metals from the strongest oxidizing agent to the weakest?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Z $>$ Y $>$ X

- B.$\hspace{1em}$X $>$ Y $>$ Z

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Y $>$ X $>$ Z

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Y $>$ Z $>$ X

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

 Least reactive metal is the strongest oxidizing agent/ weakest reducing agent.

Z is the most reactive metal (can displace both X and Y), followed by Y(can only displace X), and lastly, X (cannot displace any).
