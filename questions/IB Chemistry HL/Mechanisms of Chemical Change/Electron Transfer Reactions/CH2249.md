# CH2249

In a methanol fuel cell, which combination correctly identifies the energy conversion and the half$-$equation occurring at the anode?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

|$\hspace{0.95em}$||$\hspace{0.1em}$|Energy change|Reaction at anode
:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
||A.||electrical to chemical|$\ce{6\,e-}+ \ce{6\,H+} +\ce{CO2} \longrightarrow \ce{CH3OH} + \ce{H2O}$
||B.||chemical to electrical|$\ce{CH_3OH}+ \ce{H_2O} \longrightarrow \ce{6\,e^-} + \ce{6\,H^+} + \ce{CO_2}$
||C.||chemical to electrical|$\ce{6\,O_2}+\ce{12\,H^+} + \ce{12\,e^-} \longrightarrow \ce{6\,H_2O}$
||D.||electrical to chemical|$\ce{6\,H_2O} \longrightarrow \ce{6\,O_2}+\ce{12\,H^+} + \ce{12\,e^-}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Fuel cells are voltaic electrochemical devices that spontaneously convert chemical energy stored in fuel molecules into electrical energy. In a methanol fuel cell, the chemical potential energy within the $\ce{C-H}$ and $\ce{C-O}$ bonds of methanol is released through controlled oxidation to carbon dioxide. This energy is harnessed to drive electrons through an external circuit, producing usable electrical current. Unlike combustion, which releases energy as heat, the fuel cell process allows for direct conversion to electrical energy with higher theoretical efficiency.

At the anode of any electrochemical cell, oxidation occurs, meaning electrons are generated and released into the external circuit. In the methanol fuel cell, methanol undergoes oxidation according to the half-reaction:

$\ce{CH3OH} + \ce{H2O} → \ce{CO2} + 6\ce{\,H+} + 6\ce{\,e-}$

The six electrons produced per methanol molecule flow through the external circuit to the cathode, where oxygen reduction occurs to form water according to:

$\ce{O2} + 4\ce{\,H+} + 4\ce{\,e-} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,H2O}$

The overall chemical reaction represents the complete combustion of methanol:

$\ce{CH3OH} + \frac{3}{2}\ce{O2} \longrightarrow \ce{CO2} + 2\ce{\,H2O}$

but occurs in a controlled electrochemical environment rather than as uncontrolled burning.
