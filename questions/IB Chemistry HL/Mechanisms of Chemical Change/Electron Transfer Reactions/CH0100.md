# CH0100

Which statement is correct?        

    

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Oxidation involves loss of electrons and a decrease in oxidation state

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Oxidation involves gain of hydrogen and an increase in oxidation state

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Reduction involves loss of oxygen and an increase in oxidation state

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Reduction involves gain of electrons and a decrease in oxidation state

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Oxidation involves the loss of electrons/gain of oxygen/loss of hydrogen and an increase in oxidation state. 

Reduction involves the gain of electrons/loss of oxygen/gain of hydrogen and a decrease in oxidation state.
