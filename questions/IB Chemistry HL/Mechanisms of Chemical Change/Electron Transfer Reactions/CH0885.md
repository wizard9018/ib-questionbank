# CH0885

Iron and silver half-cells are combined to form a voltaic cell. Silver is less reactive than Iron.

When this cell produces electricity, which of the following statements is true?    

![9-885](images/9-885.png)

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The concentration of $\ce{Ag+(aq)}$ decreases

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Iron oxidizes silver

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Silver is the negative electrode

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Salt bridge releases cation into iron electrolyte

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

At the anode (negative electrode), the process of oxidation of $\ce{Fe(s)}$ results in the release of electrons which travel along the external circuit and reach the cathode. There, they undergo consumption as the aqueous $\ce{Ag+}$ ions are reduced.

As a result of oxidation, $\ce{Fe(s)}$ undergoes a loss of mass at the iron electrode. This leads to an increase in the concentration of the $\ce{Fe^{2+}}$ ions during the functioning of the cell.

Simultaneously, the silver electrode experiences an increase in mass while the concentration of $\ce{Ag+}$ ions in the solution decreases due to their conversion to solid $\ce{Ag}$ through reduction.

During the process of oxidation and reduction occurring at the electrodes, ions present in the salt bridge move toward the two half-cells. Cations move toward the cathode half-cell, while anions move toward the anode half-cell. This movement of ions helps balance the charge present in the half-cell solutions.
