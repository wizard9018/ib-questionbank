# CH0108

Consider the following equations:

|$\textbf{Equation 1:}$| $\ce{2ClO3- (aq) + I2 (aq)  → Cl2 (aq) + 2IO3- (aq)}$

:--|:--

$\textbf{Equation 2:}$|   $\ce{Cl2 (aq) + 2NaOH (aq) → NaCl (aq) + NaClO (aq) + H2O (l) }$

Which statements about these reactions is/are $\textbf{INCORRECT}$?

-   I.$\hspace{1.27em}$Iodine is reduced in Equation 1.

-   II.$\hspace{0.84em}$Chlorine is reduced only in Equation 1.

-   III. $\hspace{0.3em}$Chlorine is oxidized and reduced in Equation 2.

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only 

- D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

In Equation 1, the oxidation state of Cl decreases from +5 to 0 (reduced), and the oxidation state of iodine increases from 0 to +5 (oxidized). Statement I is incorrect. 

In Equation 2, the oxidation state of Cl increases from 0 to +1 in NaClO (oxidized), and also decreases from 0 to $-1$ in NaCl (reduced).  Chlorine is disproportionated. Statement III is correct.

Statement II is incorrect, chlorine is reduced in *both* equations.
