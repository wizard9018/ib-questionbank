# CH0796

An organic compound **X** belongs to a homologous series with the general molecular formula $\ce{C_nH_{2n+2}O}$ and is **not** oxidized by $\ce{K2Cr2O7}.$ A 3D model of compound **X** is constructed by representing H, C and O atoms as white, grey and black spheres, respectively. ## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
$\ce{C_nH_{2n+2}O}$ is the general molecular formula of a non-cyclic alcohol or ether.

In an alcohol, the C atom attached to the hydroxyl (OH) group forms single bonds with three other groups in a tetrahedral bonding geometry.

Options C and D are carbonyls (a ketone and aldehyde, respectively) with the general molecular formula is $\ce{C_nH_{2n}O}$. The carbonyl C atom has a trigonal planar bonding geometry. $\ce{K2Cr2O7}$ oxidizes primary alcohols (to aldehydes and carboxylic acids), secondary alcohols (to ketones) and aldehydes (to carboxylic acids).

Tertiary alcohols such as option B are not oxidized, as the C atom of the $\ce{C-OH}$ group is attached to three other C atoms so cannot form a $\ce{C=O}$ bond.
