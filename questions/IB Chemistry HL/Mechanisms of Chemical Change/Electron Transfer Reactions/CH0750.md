# CH0750

An electrolytic cell contains molten magnesium chloride. Which of the following statements is correct using the standard cell potentials given below?

$\ce{Mg^2+(aq) + 2 e- -> Mg(s)}$| $-2.37\,\text{V}$

|:-:| :-:|

$\ce{\frac{1}{2}Cl2(g) + e- -> Cl-(aq)}$| $+1.36\,\text{V}$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$Magnesium is reduced and magnesium metal will appear at the negative cathode

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$Magnesium is reduced and magnesium metal will appear at the negative anode

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$Chlorine is oxidized and chlorine gas will appear at the negative anode

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$Chlorine is reduced and chlorine gas will appear at the positive cathode

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

An electrolytic cell drives a nonspontaneous redox reaction.

Therefore, $\ce{Mg^{2+}}$ is reduced and $\ce{Cl-}$ is oxidized.

Reduction always occurs at the cathode, but when a power source is added to drive the reaction, the source of electrons (the negative terminal of the power source) causes the reduction.
