# CH1014

Ethanol is oxidized in the presence of acidified $\ce{K2Cr2O7(aq)}$ heating under reflux.

Which combination is correct for the chemical reaction occurring?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | Product formed | Colour change |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | A. | Ethanal | Orange to green |
    | B. | Ethanal | Green to orange |
    | C. | Ethanoic acid | Orange to green |
    | D. | Ethanoic acid | Green to orange |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Ethanol is a primary alcohol. Primary alcohols are oxidized in a two-step reaction. An aldehyde is formed, and if oxidation continues, the aldehyde is oxidized further into a carboxylic acid.

Aldehydes have a lower boiling point than the preceding alcohols. As a result, if the oxidation occurs using distillation, the it produces the aldehyde (ethanal). However, if the oxidation is made by heating under reflux, carboxylic acid (ethanoic acid) forms; in this case, the ethanoic acid is produced.

The oxidizing agent is $\ce{K2Cr2O7}$.

Cr (VI) is orange. After the redox reaction, $\ce{Cr^3+}$ is formed, and Cr (III) is green. The color change is from orange to green.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as the student must understand the oxidation of alcohols in detail.
