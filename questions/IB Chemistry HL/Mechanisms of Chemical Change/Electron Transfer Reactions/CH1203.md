# CH1203

Consider the electrolysis described below.

![Mock-set1-2025- hlp1a- 1203](<images/Mock-set1-2025- hlp1a- 1203.png>)

Which statement is correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$The mass of the silver bar decreases, and $\ce{[Ag+]}$ decreases as the electrolysis takes place

- B. $\hspace{1em}$The mass of the silver bar increases, and $\ce{[Ag+]}$ increases as the electrolysis takes place

- C. $\hspace{1em}$Electrons flow through the electrolyte to the cathode

- D. $\hspace{1em}$The mass of the fork increases, and $\ce{[Ag+]}$ remains constant as the electrolysis takes place

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The question assesses the understanding of how electroplating occurs.

As silver is oxidized at the anode (silver bar), the mass of the anode decreases. Silver ions are formed when the same amount of ions are reduced at the cathode—the mass of the cathode (in this case, the fork) increases. However, the silver ion concentration remains constant as ions are produced and consumed at the same rate.

Electrons flow through the wire from the anode to the cathode; they do not move throughout the solution/electrolyte. Option C is incorrect, as only ions can flow throughout the electrolyte.
