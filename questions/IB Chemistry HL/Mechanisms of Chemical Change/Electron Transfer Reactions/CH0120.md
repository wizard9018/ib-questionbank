# CH0120

The diagram below shows a Daniel cell. Zinc is more reactive than copper. When the spontaneous reaction occurs, which statement about the Daniel cell is correct?

![CH0120](images/CH0120.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The mass of the copper electrode will decrease

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Electrons flow from the copper electrode to the zinc electrode when the cell is operating

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The blue colour of the copper (II) sulfate solution will fade over time

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The zinc electrode is the cathode in this cell

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
More reactive metals are more likely to lose electrons (be oxidised) and so, $\ce{Zn(s)}$ will lose electrons to produce $\ce{Zn^{2+}(aq)}$ .\
 $\ce{Cu^{2+}(aq)}$ will gain electrons forming $\ce{Cu(s)}$ leading to a decrease in $\ce{Cu^{2+}(aq)}$ concentration and a paler blue colour of the solution.\
Electrons flow from the anode(Zn) to the cathode (Cu).
