# CH0748

Given the voltaic cell diagram and standard electrode potentials below, which statement is correct?

![9-748](images/9-748.png)

|$\ce{Sn^2+(aq) + 2 e- -> Sn(s)}$ | $- 0.14\,\text{V}$

|:-:|:-:|

|$\ce{Cu^2+(aq) + 2 e- -> Cu(s)}$ | $+ 0.34\,\text{V}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The reduction of copper occurs at the anode

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The oxidation of tin occurs at the anode

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The oxidation of copper occurs at the anode

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The reduction of tin occurs at the cathode

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Sn is oxidized, and oxidation occurs at the anode.

$\ce{Cu^{2+}}$ is reduced, and reduction occurs at the cathode.

The standard cell potential of Cu is greater than that of Sn.
