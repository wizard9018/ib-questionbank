# CH0889

**Maximum mark: 9**

The reaction between arsenite and iodate takes place in three stages. Iodine combines with arsenous acid in the last step of the process. The reaction is as follows:

$\ce{H3AsO3(aq) + I2(aq) + H2O -> 2 I-(aq) + H3AsO4(aq) + 2 H+(aq)}$

1.  Arsenous acid, $\ce{H3AsO3}$, is a weak acid. Define weak acid. [1 mark]

2.  State the formula of the conjugate base of arsenous acid. [1 mark]

3.  Calculate the oxidation number of $\ce{As}$ in $\ce{H3AsO3}$ and $\ce{H3AsO4}$. [2 marks]

4.  Explain whether arsenic acid is oxidized or reduced using oxidation numbers. [2 marks]

5.  Calculate the concentration of $\ce{H+}$ ions in an $\ce{H3AsO4}$ solution with a pH $= 2.21$. [2 marks]

6.  State how the rate of this reaction can be monitored in the lab. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Weak acids dissociate partially/do not dissociate completely in their aqueous solutions. $\checkmark$

2.  $\ce{H2AsO3^-(aq)}\,\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    $\ce{H3AsO3(aq) <=> H+(aq) + H2AsO3-(aq)}$

3.  $\ce{H3AsO3} = +3\,\checkmark$

    $\ce{H3AsO4} = +5\,\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    For $\ce{H3AsO3}$,

    $\begin{aligned}
    3(+1) + x + 3(-2) &= 0 \\
    3 + x - 6 &= 0 \\
    x - 3 &= 0 \\
    x &= +3
    \end{aligned}$

    For $\ce{H3AsO4}$,

    $\begin{aligned}
    3(+1) + x + 4(-2) &= 0 \\
    3 + x - 8 &= 0 \\
    x - 5 &= 0 \\
    x &= +5
    \end{aligned}$

4.  It is oxidized $\checkmark$

    Since the oxidation state of arsenic increases during the reaction $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    An increase in oxidation number indicates oxidation, and a decrease indicates reduction.

5.  $\ce{[H+] = 6.17\times 10^{-3}}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\checkmark$

    (three significant figures) $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    $\text{pH} = -\log[\ce{H+}]$

    $\ce{[H+]} = 10^{-\text{pH}}$

    $\ce{[H+]} = 10^{-2.21} = 6.17\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$ (three significant figures)

6.  Any one of the following: change in color, conductivity, or variation of pH over time. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    Iodine is dark brown, while the other substances are colorless. As the reaction uses iodine, the solution turns from brown to colorless.

    Since the process causes ions like $\ce{I-}$ and $\ce{H+}$ to form, the conductivity of the solution is expected to increase as the number of ions rises.

    Since the reaction produces $\ce{H+}$ ions, the pH can be tracked with a meter.
