## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In a primary alcohol, the OH group is bonded to a $\ce{CH2}$ group. The alcohol group initially reacts with $\ce{KMnO4}$ to form an aldehyde (CHO) group, as in option B.

However, the aldehyde is further oxidzed to a carboxylic acid (COOH) group unless it is removed by distillation. Here, the solution is heated under reflux, so the carboxylic acid is the major product.
