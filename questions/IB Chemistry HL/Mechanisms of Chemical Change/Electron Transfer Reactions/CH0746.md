# CH0746

In a given reactivity series, element Y is the most reactive, and element X is the least reactive, as shown below. Which statement below is correct?

X  <  Z  <  Y

$\ \mbox{}\\[-25pts]$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MgZ2 + Y2 -> no reaction}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MgZ2 + X2 -> MgX2 + Z2}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MgY2 + Z2 -> MgZ2 + Y2}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MgZ2 + X2 -> no reaction}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

$\ce{X2}$ will not replace Y or Z in a compound because it is less reactive than Y and Z.

$\ce{Z2}$ will not replace Y in a compound because Z is less reactive than Y.

$\ce{Y2}$ will replace Z in a compound because Y is more reactive than Z.
