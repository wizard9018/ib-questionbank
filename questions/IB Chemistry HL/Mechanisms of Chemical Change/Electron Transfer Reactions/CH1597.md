# CH1597

What is true for the redox reaction below?

$\ce{5 C2O4^2- + 2 MnO4- + 16 H+ -> 10 CO2 + 2 Mn^2+ + 8 H2O}$

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

    | | Change in oxidation state of manganese | Reducing agent |

    | :-- | :-: | :-: |

    | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | $-3$ | $\ce{C2O4^2-}$ |

    | B.$\hspace{1em}$ | $-5$ | $\ce{C2O4^2-}$ |

    | C.$\hspace{1em}$ | $-3$ | $\ce{H+}$ |

    | D.$\hspace{1em}$ | $-5$ | $\ce{H+}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

To answer this question, the oxidation state of manganese in both $\ce{MnO4-}$ and $\ce{Mn^2+}$ needs to be calculated. 

-   In $\ce{MnO4-},$ the oxidation state of oxygen is $-2$. Since the sum of the oxidation states is equal to the overall charge, we can write the following equation to determine the oxidation state of Mn. 

    $x + 4(-2) = -1$

    $x - 8 = -1$

    $x = + 7$

-   In $\ce{Mn^2+},$ the sum of the  oxidation states must be equal to the charge on the ion, so the oxidation state of Mn is  +2.

-   The oxidation state of manganese decreases by 5. Hence, the answer to the first column is $-5.$ This narrows down the answer to option B or D.

To identify the answer for the second column, the reducing agent needs to be determined. The reducing agent is the substance that undergoes oxidation. Oxidation is an increase in oxidation number (loss of electrons) whereas reduction is decrease in oxidation number (gain of electrons).  From the calculations above, note that manganese is being reduced and the manganate(VII) ion is the oxidizing agent.

To deduce the reducing agent, we need to determine the oxidation state of carbon in  $\ce{C2O4^2-}$ and $\ce{CO2}$ and of hydrogen in $\ce{H+}$ and $\ce{H2O}.$

$\ce{H+}$ **changing to** $\ce{H2O}:$

-   The oxidation state of hydrogen in $\ce{H+}$ is +1

-   The oxidation state of hydrogen in water is also +1 as shown below. 

    $2x +(-2) = 0$

    $x = +1$

-   This shows that in this reaction, hydrogen does not undergo oxidation or reduction. 

$\ce{C2O4^2-}$ **changing to** $\ce{CO2}:$

-   The oxidation state of carbon in $\ce{C2O4^2-}$ is +3 as shown below.

    $2x + 4 (-2) = -2$

    $2x - 8 = -2$

    $2x = +6$

    $x = +3$

-   The oxidation state of carbon in carbon dioxide is $+4$  as shown below. 

    $x + 2 (-2) = 0$

    $x = +4$

-   Carbon in $\ce{C2O4^2-}$ undergoes oxidation as its oxidation state increases from +3 to +4. Hence, this is the reducing agent.
