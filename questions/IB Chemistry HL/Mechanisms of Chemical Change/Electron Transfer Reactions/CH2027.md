# CH2027

Which statement about copper(II) sulfate electrolysis using graphite electrodes is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The blue intensity of the solution remains constant, and the mass of the cathode increases.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The blue intensity of the solution remains constant, and the mass of the cathode decreases.

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The blue colour of the solution fades away, and the mass of the cathode increases.

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The blue colour of the solution fades away, and the mass of the cathode decreases.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand that electrolysis of aqueous copper(II) sulfate with graphite electrodes involves the oxidation of water at the anode and the reduction of copper ions at the cathode.

$\begin{aligned}
\ce{H2O(l) &-> \frac{1}{2}O2(g) + 2 H+ + 2 e-} \\
\ce{Cu^2+(aq) + 2 e- &-> Cu(s)}
\end{aligned}$

Copper's characteristic blue colour disappears as copper(II) ions are consumed at the cathode. In addition, the mass of the cathode increases as solid copper is produced by reduction of the ions onto its surface.

This question is categorized as medium as the student must distinguish the effect of graphite electrodes on the electrolysis of copper(II) sulfate.
