# CH0880

In which of the following species would chlorine be reduced when converted from $\ce{Cl2}$?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{PCl3}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{NaClO3}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{ClO2}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{ClF7}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

When $\ce{Cl2}$ changes to $\ce{PCl3}$, the oxidation number of chlorine will change from zero to $-1$. The oxidation numbers of chlorine in $\ce{NaClO3}$, $\ce{ClO2}$, and $\ce{ClF7}$ are $+5$, $+4$, and $+7$, respectively.
