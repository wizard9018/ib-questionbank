# CH0114

Which row, indicated in the table below, contains the correct species that are produced at each electrode during the electrolysis of molten zinc chloride?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  | | $\quad$Negative electrode (cathode)$\quad$ | $\quad$Positive electrode (anode)$\quad$ |
  | :-: | :-: | :-: |
  | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\ce{Zn (s)}$ | $\ce{Cl2 (g)}$ |
  | B.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\ce{Zn^2+ (l)}$ | $\ce{Cl- (l)}$ |
  | C.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\ce{Cl- (l)}$ | $\ce{Zn^{2+} (l)}$ |
  | D.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\ce{Cl2(g)}$ | $\ce{Zn (s)}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
$\ce{Zn^{2+}(l)}$ gains electrons and is reduced to $\ce{Zn(s)}$ at the cathode. $\ce{Cl-(l)}$ loses electrons and is oxidised to $\ce{Cl2(g)}$ at the anode.
