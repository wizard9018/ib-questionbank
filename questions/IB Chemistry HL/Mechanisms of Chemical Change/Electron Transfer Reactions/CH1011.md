# CH1011

Which statement(s) about molten lead (II) bromide electrolysis is(are) correct?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :- | :- |
    | I. | Lead is formed at the anode. |
    | II. | An external source of energy is needed for the electrolysis to occur. |
    | III. | Bromine is formed at the positive electrode. |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The reaction for the electrolysis is
$\ce{PbBr2(l) -> Pb(l) + Br2(g)}$ Lead ions are reduced at the cathode (negative electrode) while bromide is oxidized at the anode (positive electrode). Electrolysis takes place as an external source of energy drives the non$-$spontaneous redox reaction.

This question is categorized as medium as students must recall how electrolysis cells work and deduce it for a specific molten salt.
