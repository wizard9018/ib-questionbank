# CH2472

Which statement about hydrogen fuel cells is correct?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$The fuel cell is more efficient than a combustion engine because it converts chemical energy directly to electrical energy without an intermediate thermal step.

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$The fuel cell is 100% efficient because no thermal energy is released during operation.

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$The fuel cell is a secondary cell because it can be recharged by passing a current through it.

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$The fuel cell operates at standard conditions, so $E^{\minuso}_{\textrm{cell}}$ can be calculated directly from the half$-$reactions without correction.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
A hydrogen fuel cell converts chemical energy directly into electrical energy. A combustion engine first converts chemical energy to thermal energy, then to mechanical energy, losing energy at each conversion. By bypassing the thermal step, the fuel cell achieves a higher proportion of useful energy output. No energy conversion is 100% efficient. Although a fuel cell is more efficient than a combustion engine, some energy is still released as heat.

A fuel cell is neither primary nor secondary. It operates as long as fuel and oxidant are supplied. It is not recharged by reversing the current. Fuel cells rarely operate at standard conditions ($298\;\textrm{K}$, $100\;\textrm{kPa}$, $1\;\textrm{mol}\;\textrm{dm}^{-3}$). Operating temperature, pressure, and water activity all differ from standard state, so the actual cell potential deviates from $E^{\minuso}_{\textrm{cell}}$.
