# CH0118

Which statement about electrochemical cells is **not** correct?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The reaction in a voltaic cell produces electrical energy

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The reaction in an electrolytic cell is nonspontaneous

- C.$\hspace{1em}$In an electrolytic cell, the cathode is the positive electrode

- D.$\hspace{1em}$In a voltaic cell, the cathode is the positive electrode

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
Use the mnemomic PANiC; positive anode, negative cathode for the polarity in $\textbf{electrolytic}$ cells.
