# CH0743

What are the respective oxidation states of sulfur in the following sulfur-containing species?

$\ce{SO_3^2-, H2SO4, S8, S2O_3^2-}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$6, 4, 0, 2

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$4, 6, 0, 2

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$2, 6, 0, 4

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$4, 6, 2, 0

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Calculate the oxidation state of sulfur in each species:

$\ce{SO3^2-}$:

-   $\begin{aligned}
    \ce{S} + 3(2-) &= 2- \\
    \ce{S} &= \mathbf{+4}
    \end{aligned}$
-   (oxidation state of oxygen is 2-)

$\ce{H2SO4}$:

-   $\begin{aligned}
    \ce{S} + 4(2-) + 2(1+) &= 0 \\
    \ce{S} &= \mathbf{+6}
    \end{aligned}$
-   (oxidation state of oxygen is $2-$, H is 1+)

$\ce{S8}$ is an elemental form of sulfur with an oxidation state of **0**.

$\ce{S2O3^2-}$:

-   $\begin{aligned}
    2\,\ce{S} + 3(2-) &= 2- \\
    \ce{S} &= \mathbf{2+}
    \end{aligned}$
-   (oxidation state of oxygen is $2-$)
