# CH0117

The below diagram shows an electrolytic cell. At which electrode will bromine form, and what is the process taking place there?

![CH0117](images/CH0117.png)

| | $\quad$Electrode$\quad$ | $\quad$Process$\quad$ |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$ | R | reduction |
| $\hspace{1em}$B. | S | oxidation |
| $\hspace{1em}$C. | S | reduction |
| $\hspace{1em}$D. | R | oxidation |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
In electrolysis the positive electrode is the anode. Oxidation takes place at the anode, so bromine will be formed at S.

$\ce{Br^-}\textrm{(l)}\longrightarrow \frac{1}{2} \ce{Br2}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{e-}
$
