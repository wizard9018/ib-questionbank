# CH0610

Electrolysis of $\ce {Cu^{2+}}$ using a current of $1 \textrm{\ A} = 1 \textrm{\ C s}^{-1}$ for 10 s produces $x$ mol of Cu metal.

If $Q= It$, how many moles of Cu metal are produced if $\ce{Cu^+}$ is electrolyzed under the same conditions? 

-   A.$\hspace{1em}x$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}2x$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{x}{2}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$0

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

$\ce{Cu^+}$ requires half as many electrons to form $\ce {Cu}$ as  $\ce {Cu^{2+}}$.

Therefore, twice as many moles of Cu can be formed by transferring the same current over the same period of time.
