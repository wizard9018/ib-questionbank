# CH0882

What deduction can be made based on the following spontaneous reaction?
$\newline \ $

$\ce{Zn(s) + Cu^2+(aq) -> Zn^2+(aq) + Cu(s)}$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$Zinc is the oxidizing agent

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$Copper is more readily oxidized than zinc

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$Zinc is more active than copper

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$Copper is more active than zinc

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$ \
The oxidation number of zinc increases from zero to $+2$, which means it is oxidized and is the reducing agent.

Copper is reduced since its oxidizing number decreases from $+2$ to zero.

More active metals  are stronger reducing agents, and more easily oxidized.  Zinc is more active than copper.
