# CH0423

Which of the following is/are the most suitable reagent(s) for the conversion of butanoic acid to butan$-$1$-$ol?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{LiAlH4}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{H2}$ with a $\ce{Ni}$ catalyst

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Sn}$ and $\ce{HCl}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{KMnO4}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Converting butanoic acid ($\ce{CH3CH2CH2COOH}$) to butan$-$1$-$ol ($\ce{CH3CH2CH2CH2OH}$) is a reduction of a carboxylic acid to a primary alcohol. $\ce{LiAlH4}$ is a strong reducing agent capable of reducing carboxylic acids, and is the reagent specified in the IB syllabus for this conversion.

$\ce{H2}$ with a $\ce{Ni}$ catalyst reduces $\ce{C=C}$ double bonds but does not reduce carboxylic acids under standard conditions. $\ce{Sn}$ and $\ce{HCl}$ is a reducing mixture used to reduce nitro groups ($\ce{-NO2}$) to amines ($\ce{-NH2}$), not for carboxylic acid reduction. $\ce{KMnO4}$ is an oxidising agent, so it would not carry out a reduction.
