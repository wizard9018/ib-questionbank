# CH2470

A hydrogen$-$oxygen fuel cell operates under alkaline conditions. Which row correctly identifies the product at each electrode and the overall energy conversion?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

| $\hspace{1.em}$||| $\mathbf{Cathode}$ | $\mathbf{Anode}$ | $\mathbf{Energy\;conversion}$ |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
|| A. || $\ce{H2O}$ | $\ce{O2}$ | electrical $\rightarrow$ chemical |
|| B. || $\ce{H2O}$ | none | chemical $\rightarrow$ electrical |
|| C. || $\ce{OH^{-}}$ | $\ce{H2O}$ | chemical $\rightarrow$ electrical |
|| D. || $\ce{OH^{-}}$ | $\ce{O2}$ | electrical $\rightarrow$ chemical |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
In an alkaline hydrogen fuel cell, oxygen is reduced at the cathode:

 $\ce{O2}\textrm{(g)} + 2\ce{\,H2O}\textrm{(l)} + 4\ce{\,e^{-}} \longrightarrow 4\ce{\,OH^{-}}\textrm{(aq)}$

  and hydrogen is oxidised at the anode:

$2\ce{\,H2}\textrm{(g)} + 4\ce{\,OH^{-}}\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow 4\ce{\,H2O}\textrm{(l)} + 4\ce{\,e^{-}}$

The cathode product is $\ce{OH^{-}}$ and the anode product is $\ce{H2O}$.

The overall reaction is $2\ce{\,H2}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{O2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,H2O}\textrm{(l)}$, the same as hydrogen combustion, but the fuel cell achieves this conversion of chemical energy directly to electrical energy  without a flame by separating the two half$-$reactions.
