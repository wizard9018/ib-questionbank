# CH0747

Using the voltaic cell notation given below and the standard electrode potentials, determine the standard cell potential of the cell, $E^\ominus$. 

$\ce{Pb(s) | Pb^2+(aq) || Ag+(aq) | Ag(s)}$

-   |$\ce{Ag+(aq) + e- -> Ag(s)}$| $0.80 \textrm{\ V}$ 

    |:-:|--:|

    |$\ce{Pb^2+(aq) + 2 e- -> Pb(s)}$| $-0.13$ V

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$0.93 $\textrm{\ V}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$0.67 $\textrm{\ V}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$1.47 $\textrm{\ V}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$1.73 $\textrm{\ V}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Oxidation: $\ce{Pb(s) -> Pb^2+(aq) + 2 e-}\qquad E^\ominus = 0.13\,\text{V}$

Reduction: $\ce{Ag+(aq) + e- -> Ag(s)}\qquad E^\ominus = 0.80\,\text{V}$

$E^\ominus_{cell} = 0.93\,\text{V}$
