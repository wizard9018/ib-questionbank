# CH0612

What are the products formed at the anode and cathode for the electrolysis of an aqueous solution of $\ce{AgNO3}$ with inert electrodes? 

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  ||Anode|Cathode

  |:-:|:-:|:-:|

  A.$\hspace{1em}$|$\ce{Ag(s)}$|$\ce{O2(g)}$

  B.$\hspace{1em}$|$\ce{O2(g)}$|$\ce{H2(g)}$

  C.$\hspace{1em}$|$\ce{O2(g)}$|$\ce{Ag(s)}$

  D.$\hspace{1em}$|$\ce{H2(g)}$| $\ce{O2(g)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Oxidation takes place at the anode and reduction at the cathode.

At the anode, $\ce{H2O}$ is oxidized preferentially to $\ce{NO3^-}$ and therefore, oxygen is produced.

At the cathode, $\ce{Ag^+}$ is reduced preferentially to $\ce{H2O}$ and therefore, silver metal is produced.
