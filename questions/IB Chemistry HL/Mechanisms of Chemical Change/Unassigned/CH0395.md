# CH0395

Which of the statements below gives a correct feature of a ligand?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Electron pair acceptor

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Electron pair donor

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Coordinate bond acceptor

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Metal ion donor

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Ligands are Lewis bases with a lone pair of electrons that they can donate to a central metal ion.
