# CH1538

Consider the Arrhenius equation given below.

$k = Ae^{-\frac{E_a}{RT}}$

 Which statement is incorrect? 

- A.$\hspace{1em}A$, the frequency factor, is related to the frequency of the collisions and their orientation.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The greater the temperature, the greater the rate constant of a given reaction.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The greater the temperature, the greater the activation energy of the chemical reaction.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$If a catalyst is used for a chemical reaction, the rate constant increases.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\

The student must understand the expression itself.

$k = Ae^{-\frac{E_a}{RT}}$

- $k$: rate constant

- $A$: frequency factor$\text{---}$accounts for the frequency and orientation of the collisions

- $E_a$: activation energy$\text{---}$minimum energy needed so that reactants can turn into products

- $R$: ideal gas constant

- $T$: temperature in Kelvin

An increase in the temperature does not affect the activation energy (it affects the number of particles in the systems with enough energy to overcome the activation barrier). The activation energy is only affected by the addition of a catalyst. A catalyst provides an alternative pathway for the chemical reaction to occur, lowering the activation energy required.

This question is categorized as a medium as a student is expected to understand the Arrhenius expression and its implications.
