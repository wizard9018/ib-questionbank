# CH0845

Which gives the correct expression for the activation energy of the backward uncatalyzed reaction?

![CH0845](images/CH0845.png)

-   A. $\hspace{1em}x - z$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}y - x$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}y - z$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}y$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The activation energy is represented on an energy profile diagram as the difference in energy between the reactants and the top of the peak.

This means that $y$ represents the activation energy for the forward uncatalyzed reaction, and $x$ is the activation energy for the forward catalyzed reaction.

The respective energies of reactants and products are shown as horizontal lines. The difference in energy between the reactants and products is the enthalpy change, in this case, ($\pm$) $z$ is the enthalpy change for the backward and forward reactions.

$y - z$ leaves the activation energy for the backward uncatalyzed reaction as it is the difference between the products (which for the backward reaction act as the reactants) and the top of the uncatalyzed peak.
