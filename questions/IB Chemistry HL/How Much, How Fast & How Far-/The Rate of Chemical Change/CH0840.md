# CH0840

What changes have been made to system 1 to obtain system 2?

![CH0840](images/CH0840.png)

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | Temperature in System 2| Reaction Conditions |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | A. | Temperature increased | Catalyst added |
    | B. | Temperature increased | No change in conditions |
    | C. | Temperature decreased | Catalyst added |
    | D. | Temperature decreased | No change in conditions |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

At a higher temperature (curve 1), the energy distribution is broader, and the mean kinetic energy is greater. The proportion of particles with sufficient thermal energy to overcome the activation energy barrier has increased.

Curve 2, with a higher peak and shifting to the left, shows a decreased temperature as the average kinetic energy of particles has decreased.

Only one activation energy is marked, meaning no catalyst was added.
