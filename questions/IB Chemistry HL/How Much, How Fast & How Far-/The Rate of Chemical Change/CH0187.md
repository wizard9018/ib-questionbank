# CH0187

For an iodine clock reaction, the following experimental data was collected with respect to one of the reactants, the iodide ion, $\ce{I-}$.

-   :::tableoptions{}

    | $\hspace{1em}$ Trial $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ $[\ce{I-}]$ $\hspace{1em}$ $\\$ $\hspace{1em}$ (mol dm$^\mathbf{-3}$) $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ Reaction rate $\hspace{1em}$ $\\$ $\hspace{1em}$ (s$\mathbf{^{-1}}$) $\hspace{1em}$ |

    | :-: | :-: | :-: |

    | 1 | 0.2 | 0.40 |

    | 2 | 0.1 | 0.19 |

    | 3 | 0.3 | 0.61 |

What is the most likely order of the reaction with respect to iodide $\ce{I-}$ ?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.5

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$ 1

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$ 1.5

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$ 2

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Using trials 1 and 2, as the concentration of $\ce{I-}$ doubles, the rate also doubles. Rate and $[\ce{I-}]$ are therefore, directly proportional with an order of 1.
