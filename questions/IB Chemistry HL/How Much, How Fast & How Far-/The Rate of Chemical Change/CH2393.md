# CH2393

Which of the following energies are affected by the addition of a catalyst for the reaction given below?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{H_2}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{I_2}\textrm{(g)} \rightleftharpoons 2\ce{\,HI}\textrm{(g)}$

![CH2393](images/CH2393.png)

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$X only

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$Y only

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$X and Y only

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$X, Y and Z

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
An energy profile shows how the potential energy of the system changes as reactants are converted to products along the reaction coordinate. In this diagram, X represents the activation energy of the forward reaction (the energy difference between the reactant level and the peak of the curve), Y represents the activation energy of the reverse reaction (the energy difference between the product level and the same peak), and Z represents the enthalpy change of the reaction (the energy difference between reactant and product levels).

![CH2393ms](images/CH2393ms.png)

A catalyst provides an alternative reaction pathway with a lower activation energy. This means the peak of the catalysed pathway is lower than the uncatalysed peak, reducing both X and Y by the same amount. The enthalpy change, Z, is a state function that depends only on the relative energies of reactants and products. Since a catalyst does not change the energy of either the reactants or the products, Z remains unchanged.
