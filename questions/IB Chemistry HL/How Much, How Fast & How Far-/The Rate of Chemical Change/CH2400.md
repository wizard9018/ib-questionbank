# CH2400

In which of the following reactions is colorimetry **not** a suitable method for investigating the rate of reaction?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}\ce{Fe}^{3+}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{SCN}^-\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow [\ce{FeSCN}]^{2+}\textrm{(aq)}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}\ce{CH_3COCH_3}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{I_2}\textrm{(aq)} \xrightarrow{\ce{H}^+} \ce{CH_3COCH_2I}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{HI}\textrm{(aq)}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}2\ce{\,MnO_4}^-\textrm{(aq)} + 5\ce{\,C_2O_4}^{2-}\textrm{(aq)} + 16\ce{\,H}^+\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,Mn}^{2+}\textrm{(aq)} + 10\ce{\,CO_2}\textrm{(g)} + 8\ce{\,H_2O}\textrm{(l)}$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}\ce{CH_3COOH}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{CH_3CH_2OH}\textrm{(aq)} \xrightarrow{\ce{H}^+} \ce{CH_3COOCH_2CH_3}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{H_2O}\textrm{(l)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Colorimetry measures changes in the absorbance of light by a solution over time. It is only useful when a coloured species is either being produced or consumed during the reaction, causing a measurable change in the colour intensity of the solution.

In reaction A, the product $[\ce{FeSCN}]^{2+}$ is a coordination complex with an intense deep red colour. As the reaction proceeds, the solution becomes increasingly red, and this colour change can be tracked with a colorimeter.

In reaction B, iodine ($\ce{I_2}$) is a reactant that gives the solution a brown/yellow colour. As iodine is consumed during the iodination of propanone, the colour fades. A colorimeter can monitor this decrease in absorbance over time.

In reaction C, permanganate ions ($\ce{MnO_4}^-$) give the solution an intense purple colour. As the permanganate is reduced to the nearly colourless $\ce{Mn}^{2+}$ ion, the purple colour fades. This gradual loss of colour is easily tracked by a colorimeter.

In reaction D, the esterification of ethanoic acid with ethanol produces ethyl ethanoate and water. All reactants and products in this reaction are colourless in aqueous solution. There is no change in the colour of the solution at any point during the reaction, so colorimetry cannot be used.
