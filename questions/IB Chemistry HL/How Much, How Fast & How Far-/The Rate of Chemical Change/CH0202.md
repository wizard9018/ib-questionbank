# CH0202

Nitrogen dioxide reacts with carbon monoxide. The reaction occurs in two steps with the energy level diagram shown.

![16.2-202](images/16.2-202.png)

Which of the following is correct?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  ||Rate-determining step (RDS)|Sign of enthalpy change|

  |:-:|:-:|:-:|

   A.$\hspace{1em}$| 1| -

   B.$\hspace{1em}$| 1| +

   C.$\hspace{1em}$| 2| -

   D.$\hspace{1em}$| 2| +

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The first step is the RDS because it has the largest activation energy.

The reaction is exothermic because the final products have a lower enthalpy than the initial reactants.

The question is at the lower end of the “hard” category as students must know how to interpret a multi-step energy level diagram, identify both the RDS and the reaction enthalpy change and avoid confusing the enthalpy change for the RDS with the enthalpy change for the whole reaction.
