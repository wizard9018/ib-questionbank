# CH0200

Sodium hydroxide and the ester ethylethanoate react with the rate equation: 

$\underset{\textrm{ester}}{\ce{CH3COOCH2CH3}

}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{H2O}\textrm{(l)} \xrightarrow{\ce{NaOH}} \ce{CH3COOH}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{CH3CH2OH}\textrm{(aq)}$\

$\textrm{rate} = k[\text{ester}][\ce{NaOH}]$

What are the units of the frequency factor, $A$, for this reaction? 

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$mol dm$^{-3}$ s$^{-1}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$mol$^{-2}$ dm$^6$ s$^{-1}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$s$^{-1}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$mol$^{-1}$ dm$^3$ s$^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Using the rate expression 

$\textrm{rate} = k[\textrm{ester}][\ce{NaOH}]$

and inserting the units for the the rate, $\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \textrm{s}^{-1},$ and the concentrations, $\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3},$ the equation can be rearranged to determine the units of the rate constant.

$k = \dfrac{\textrm{rate}}{[\textrm{ester}][\ce{NaOH}]}$

$[k] = \dfrac{\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\text{s}^{-1}}{\text{mol}\,\text{dm}^{-3} \times \text{mol}\,\text{dm}^{-3}}$

$[k] = \textrm{mol}^{-1} \,\textrm{dm}^3 \textrm{s}^{-1}$

The Arrhenius equation

$k = Ae^{-\dfrac{E_a}{RT}}$

shows that the units of the rate constant and the frequency factor must be the same.

The question is at the higher end of the medium$-$difficulty category as students must be able to work out the units of a rate constant from a rate equation and also recognize that the units of the frequency factor are the same (or correctly interpret the given equation).
