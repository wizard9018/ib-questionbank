# CH1537

The chemical reaction below is investigated.

A$_2$ + BC$_2$ $\longrightarrow$ ABC + AC

Different experiments were conducted to determine the rate law, and the following information was obtained.

![CH1537](images/CH1537.png)

What is the correct rate law for this chemical reaction that is consistent with the processed data?

- A.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{rate} = k \big[ \textrm{A}_2 \big ] \big [ \textrm{BC}_2 \big ]^2 $

- B.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{rate} = k \big[ \textrm{BC}_2 \big ] \big [ \textrm{A}_2 \big ]^2 $

- C.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{rate} = k \big[ \textrm{BC}_2 \big ] $

- D.$\hspace{1em}\textrm{rate} = k \big[ \textrm{A}_2 \big ] $

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The student must interpret the result to deduce the order for these two reactants. Variations in the concentration of A:sub[2] do not affect the rate of reaction, as the rate is constant for all concentrations. This indicates the reaction is zeroth order with respect to A:sub[2]. Variations in the concentration of BC:sub[2] do affect the rate. There is a direct relationship, and the greater the concentration, the greater the reaction rate. This is a directly proportional relationship, as the graph shows a linear trend. This behaviour corresponds to a first order reaction.

As a result, the order with respect to A$_2$ is 0 and with respect to BC$_2$ is 1. The rate law can be stated as 

$\textrm{rate} = k \big[ \textrm{A}_2 \big ]^0 \big [ \textrm{BC}_2 \big ]^1 = k  \big [ \textrm{BC}_2 \big ]$

This question is categorized as a medium as the student must interpret the graph, deduce the orders and identify the correct rate expression.
