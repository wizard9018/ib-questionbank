# CH0210

**Maximum mark: 8**

Gaseous alkenes $X, Y$ and $Z$ react with hydrogen gas over a finely ground metal catalyst.

Second$\text{--}$order rate constants for the reaction of alkene $X$ were measured over a range of temperatures and used to draw an Arrhenius plot.

![16.2-CH0210](images/CH0210.png)

1. Explain why grinding the metal catalyst causes the reaction rate to increase. [1 mark]

2. Using the information in the Arrhenius plot and Section 1 of the IB Chemistry data booklet, estimate the value of $\ln A$ for the reaction of alkene $X$, to 1 decimal place. [1 mark]

3. Alkene $Y$ is more complex than alkene $X$ but reacts with the same activation energy, $E_a$. State how the use of alkene $Y$ instead of alkene $X$ would affect the slope (gradient) and the $y-$intercept of the Arrhenius plot. [2 marks]

4. The Arrhenius plot for the reaction of alkene $Z$ with hydrogen gas has a slope (gradient) of $-4.89 \times 10^3\, \textrm{K}$ and a $y-$intercept of 12.2.

    Calculate the rate constant for this reaction at $90^\circ \mathrm{C}$, in units of $\textrm{mol}^{-1}\textrm{dm}^3\textrm{s}^{-1}$.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Larger surface area / more active sites / more exposed metal / more exposed catalyst available (for reaction / binding / catalysis / adsorption). $\checkmark$

2.  $14.5 \: \checkmark$

    *Accept answers in range $14.3 -14.8$;*\
    *Do not accept answers not given to 1 d.p.*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    $\ln k = \dfrac{-E_a}{RT} + \ln A$ is of the form $y = mx+c$ (straight line equation). $\ln A$ is the $y-$intercept $c.$

3.  The slope / gradient would stay the same / remain fixed; $\checkmark$

    The $y-$intercept would decrease / get smaller. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    More complex reactants produce lower values of $A$ as it is more difficult for the molecules to collide in the correct orientation; $\ln A$ (the $y-$intercept) would therefore decrease.

    The slope is $\dfrac{-E_a}{R}$ and so if $E_a$ is the same, the slope will stay the same.

4.  $T = 273 + 90 = 363\,\text{K}\,\checkmark$

    $\begin{aligned}
    \ln k &= \dfrac{-E_a}{RT} + \ln A = (\text{gradient})\left(\dfrac{1}{T}\right) + \text{intercept} \textbf{OR} \ln k = \left(-4.89 \times 10^3\right) \left(\dfrac{1}{363}\right) + 12.2\,\checkmark \\
    \ln k &= -1.271\,\checkmark \\
    k &= e^{-1.271} = 0.281\,\textrm{mol}^{-1}\,\textrm{dm}^3\,\textrm{s}^{-1}\,\checkmark
    \end{aligned}$

    *Accept 0.28 $\text{mol}^{-1}\,\text{dm}^3\,\text{s}^{-1}$;*\
    *Accept 0.27 ***OR*** 0.273 $\text{mol}^{-1}\,\text{dm}^3\,\text{s}^{-1}$ if value of $\ln k = -1.3$*\
    *Award [3] for correct value of ln k without workings;*\
    *Award [4] for correct value of k without workings.*
