    **AND**

    $0.838\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol min}^{-1}$ $\ce{O2}$ $\checkmark$

    $rate = 0.838\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol min}^{-1}\times 22.7\,\text{dm}^3\,\text{mol}^{-1} = 0.0190\,\text{dm}^3\,\text{min}^{-1}\,\checkmark$

    *ALLOW ecf from answer to (a).*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    Dividing by the $M_r$ of $\ce{H2O2}$ gives the rate in mol min$^{-1}$.

    The ratio of $\ce{H2O2}:\ce{O2}$ is $2:1$.

    Multiplying the number of mol min$^{-1}$ of $\ce{O2}$ produced by the molar gas volume at STP (22.7 dm$^3$ mol$^{-1}$) gives the rate in dm$^3$ min$^{-1}$.
