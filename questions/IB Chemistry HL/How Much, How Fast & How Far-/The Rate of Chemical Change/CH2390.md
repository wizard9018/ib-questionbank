# CH2390

The Maxwell$-$Boltzmann distribution of kinetic energies for a gas at $50\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}$ and $80\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}$ is shown below.
Which letter represents the most probable kinetic energy of the gas particles at $80\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}$?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

![CH2390](images/CH2390.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The Maxwell$-$Boltzmann distribution plots the number of particles against their kinetic energy. The peak of each curve corresponds to the most probable kinetic energy, the energy possessed by the greatest number of particles at that temperature.

At a higher temperature, the distribution broadens and shifts to the right: the peak occurs at a higher energy but is lower in height, reflecting the wider spread of molecular speeds. The total area under the curve remains constant because the number of particles does not change.

The $80\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}$ curve is the broader, shorter distribution. On the energy axis, $\textbf{A}$ marks the most probable kinetic energy at $50\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}$ and $\textbf{B}$ marks the most probable kinetic energy at $80\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}.$ On the vertical axis, $\textbf{C}$ and $\textbf{D}$ label the number of particles at each peak, not energies.
