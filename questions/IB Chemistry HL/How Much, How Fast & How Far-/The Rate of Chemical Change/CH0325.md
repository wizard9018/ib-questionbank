# CH0325

Which of the following factors increases the rate of the reaction below?

$\ce{Mg(s) + 2 HNO3(aq) -> Mg(NO3)2(aq) + H2(g)}$        

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$Lowering the temperature

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$Decreasing the concentration of the acid

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$Increasing the volume of the acid

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$Using powdered magnesium instead of strips

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Four factors that can increase the rate of a chemical reaction are: increasing the temperature of the reaction, adding a catalyst, increasing the concentration of the reactants and decreasing the solid particle size of the reactants.
