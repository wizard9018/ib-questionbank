# CH0738

Cesium-137 has a half-life of 30 years, and its decay is first order.

How long will it take for a 400 g sample to be reduced to 25 g?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$90 years

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$30 years

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$60 years

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$120 years

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Reducing the sample from 400 g to 25 g would take 4 half-lives, $4\times 30 = 120$ years.

400 g $\xrightarrow{1}$ 200 g $\xrightarrow{2}$ 100 g $\xrightarrow{3}$ 50 g $\xrightarrow{4}$ 25 g
