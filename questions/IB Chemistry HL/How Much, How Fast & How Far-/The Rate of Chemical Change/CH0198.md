# CH0198

Which of the following reactions has the largest rate constant, $k,$ under the same reaction conditions? Recall, $k = Ae^{-Ea/RT}.$

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  |   | ***E***$_\mathbf{a}\,(\textbf{kJ mol}\mathbf{^{-1}})$ | ***A*** s$\mathbf{^{-1}}$ |

  |:-:|:-:|:-:|

  | A. | 30.0| 2 $\times 10^{12}$

  | B. |15.0| 2 $\times 10^{12}$

  | C. |30.0| 1 $\times 10^{12}$

  | D. | 15.0| 1 $\times 10^{12}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The rate constant increases with increasing $A$ and decreases with increasing $E_a$.

This question is on the lower end of the medium-difficulty category as students should be familiar with the effect of these variables on $k$, and are given the equation for assistance if needed.
