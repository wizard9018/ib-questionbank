# CH2398

A student investigates the decomposition of hydrogen peroxide using powdered manganese(IV) oxide as a catalyst.

$2\ce{\,H_2O_2}\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow \ce{O_2}\textrm{(g)} + 2\ce{\,H_2O}\textrm{(l)}$

The following four sets of conditions were investigated.

| $\mathbf{Experiment}$ | Volume of $\mathbf{H}_\mathbf{2}\mathbf{O}_\mathbf{2}\textbf{(aq)}\ /\ \textbf{cm}^\mathbf{3}$ | Concentration of $\mathbf{H}_\mathbf{2}\mathbf{O}\mathbf{_2}\mathbf{(aq)}\ /\ \mathbf{mol\ dm}^\mathbf{-3}$ | $\mathbf{Temperature}$ $\mathbf{/\,{}^\circ C}$ | $\mathbf{Catalyst}$ |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 1 | 40 | 0.5 | 40 | yes |
| 2 | 40 | 0.5 | 40 | no |
| 3 | 20 | 0.5 | 20 | yes |
| 4 | 40 | 1.0 | 40 | yes |

Which of the options below correctly identifies the experiment with the curve shown in the diagram?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

| $\hspace{1.em}$||| $\mathbf{Experiment\ 1}$ | $\mathbf{Experiment\ 2}$ | $\mathbf{Experiment\ 3}$ | $\mathbf{Experiment\ 4}$ |
|:-: |:-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
|| A. | | X | Y | W | Z |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The volume of oxygen gas produced at the plateau depends on the number of moles of hydrogen peroxide, while the steepness of the curve depends on the rate of reaction (determined by temperature, concentration, and presence of a catalyst).

Experiment 4 has the highest concentration $(1.0\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3})$, the highest temperature $(40\,{}^\circ\textrm{C})$, and a catalyst. It therefore has both the fastest rate and the most moles of $\ce{H_2O_2}$:

$n = 1.0\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \times 0.040\textrm{\ dm}^3 = 0.04\textrm{\ mol}$

This gives the steepest curve with the highest plateau, which is curve Z.

Experiments 1 and 2 are identical in volume $(40\textrm{\ cm}^3)$, concentration $(0.5\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3})$, and temperature $(40\,{}^\circ\textrm{C})$. They differ only in the presence of a catalyst. Both contain the same number of moles:

$n = 0.5\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \times 0.040\textrm{\ dm}^3 = 0.02\textrm{\ mol}$

They therefore produce the same total volume of oxygen (same plateau) but at different rates. Experiment 1 (with catalyst) reacts faster, and experiment 2 (without catalyst) reacts more slowly. These correspond to curves X and Y, which share the same plateau but have different initial steepness.

Experiment 3 has the lowest temperature $(20\,{}^\circ\textrm{C})$ and the smallest volume $(20\textrm{\ cm}^3)$, giving the fewest moles $(0.01\textrm{\ mol})$, the slowest rate, and the lowest plateau. This is curve W.
