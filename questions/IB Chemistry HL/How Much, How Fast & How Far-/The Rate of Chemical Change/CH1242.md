## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Tungsten acts as a catalyst in the synthesis of ammonia by providing an alternative reaction pathway with a lower activation energy, $E_a.$ The overall energy distribution of the particles, represented by the Maxwell$-$Boltzmann curve, remains unchanged because the temperature of the system is constant. However, the energy threshold that particles must exceed to react becomes smaller when a catalyst is introduced. As a result, a greater proportion of particles now have energies equal to or greater than this reduced activation energy, which is illustrated by the larger shaded region under the curve. These particles are more likely to undergo successful collisions and therefore increase the rate of product formation. The catalyst does not alter the distribution itself, it simply changes where the activation energy line intersects the curve.
