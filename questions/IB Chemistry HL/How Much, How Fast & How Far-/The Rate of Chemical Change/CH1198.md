# CH1198

The plot of $\ln k$ against $1/T$ is shown for a chemical reaction.

*The linearized expression of the Arrhenius equation: $\ln k = \dfrac{-E_a}{RT} + \ln A.$

![CH1198](images/CH1198.png)

What is the $y$-intercept magnitude?

- A. $\hspace{1em}-10489$

- B. $\hspace{1em}25.877$

- C. $\hspace{1em}1.73\times 10^{11}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}8.72\times 10^4$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The linearized expression of the Arrhenius equation:

$\ln k = \dfrac{-E_a}{RT} + \ln A$

$y = \ln k$ and $x = 1/T$

The *y-intercept* = *ln A*

![CH1198b](images/CH1198b.png)

$\ln A = 25.877$

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students should get information from the graph and apply the linearized expression for the Arrhenius equation.
