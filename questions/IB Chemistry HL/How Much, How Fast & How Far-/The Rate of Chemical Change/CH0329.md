# CH0329

In the reaction below, iron speeds up the reaction and remains chemically unchanged at the end of the reaction:

$\ce{N2 + 3 H2 -> 2 NH3}$

All statements below are true for the role of iron in the above reaction **EXCEPT**:

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$Iron does not affect the yield of products in the reaction

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$Iron provides an alternative pathway for the reaction

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$Iron increases the average kinetic energy of the reactants

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$Iron lowers the activation energy of the reaction

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Iron is a catalyst in this reaction. Catalysts speed up a chemical reaction by providing an alternative pathway with a lower activation energy.  As catalysts speed up both the forward and reverse reactions equally, they do not shift the equilibrium position or change the reaction yield.

Only increasing temperature will increase the average kinetic energy of the molecules, an iron cannot do that.
