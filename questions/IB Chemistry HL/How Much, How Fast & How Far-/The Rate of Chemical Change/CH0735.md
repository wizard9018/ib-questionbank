# CH0735

In the following reaction, the initial rate of appearance of $\ce{CO2}$ is measured to be $3.0\times 10^{-2}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\text{s}^{-1}$, what is the rate of disappearance of $\ce{O2}$?

$\ce{C2H6O(l) + 3 O2(g) -> 2 CO2(g) + 3 H2O(g)}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em} 4.5\times 10^{-2}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\text{s}^{-1}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em} 3.0\times 10^{-2}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\text{s}^{-1}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em} 6.0\times 10^{-2}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\text{s}^{-1}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em} 1.5\times 10^{-2}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\text{s}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The overall rate of the reaction is $\dfrac{3.0\times 10^{-2}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\text{s}^{-1}}{2} = 1.5 \times 10^{-2}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\text{s}^{-1}$.

O$_2$ disappears 3 times the overall, or $1.5\times 10^{-2}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\text{s}^{-1}\times 3 = 4.5\times 10^{-2}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\text{s}^{-1}$
