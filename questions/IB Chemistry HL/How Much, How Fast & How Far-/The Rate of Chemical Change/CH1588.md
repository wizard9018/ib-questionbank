# CH1588

Which option represents the increase in the number of particles with sufficient energy to react in the presence of a catalyst in the Maxwell$\textrm{--}$Boltzmann distribution diagram shown below?

![ch1588v2](images/ch1588v2.JPEG)

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$X

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Y

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\textrm{X} - \textrm{Y}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Y + X

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

To answer this question, the diagram needs to be analysed.

-   In a Maxwell$\textrm{--}$Boltzmann distribution curve, the horizontal axis represents the energy of particles and the vertical axis represents the number of particles that possess that energy.

-   Activation energy is the minimum energy required for the particles to successfully react. This is labelled on the $x\text{--}$axis; $E_a$  for the uncatalyzed reaction and $E_a^{cat}$ for the catalyzed reaction.

-   Area Y represents the fraction of particles that have enough energy to react without a catalyst.

-   The molecules in both areas X and Y represent the fraction of particles that have enough energy to react with a catalyst.

-    The increase in the number of particles with sufficient energy to react due to the presence of a catalyst is represented by the area between $E_a^{cat}$ and $E_a$ which is area X.
