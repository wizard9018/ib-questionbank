# CH0336

**Maximum mark: 7**

The equation below shows the reaction between sulfur dioxide and oxygen at 25 $^{\circ}$C.

$2 \ce{SO2}\textrm{(g)}  +  \ce{O2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2 \ce{SO3}\textrm{(g)}$

1.  List 2 characteristics that reactant molecules should have for a reaction to occur. [2 marks]

2.  The experiment is repeated at 35 $^{\circ}$C. Sketch the Maxwell$-$Boltzmann curve for the reactants at 25 $^{\circ}\textrm{C}$ and 35 $^{\circ}\textrm{C}.$ [3 marks]

3.  Using kinetic particle theory, explain how increasing the temperature will increase the rate of reaction. [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    Increasing the temperature increases the average kinetic energy of particles. This shifts the curve to the right as more particles have a higher energy. The curve's peak must be lower than the original to ensure that the total number of particles (represented by the area under the curve) is the same at both temperatures.
