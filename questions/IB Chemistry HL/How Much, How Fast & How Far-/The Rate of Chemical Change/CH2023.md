# CH2023

A student plotted the rate constants of a reaction at different temperatures. The graph obtained is shown below.

![CH2023](images/CH2023.png)

What does the $y\text{--}$intercept represent?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}E_a$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}-\dfrac{E_a}{RT}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}A$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ln A$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The student must be able to interpret the linearization of the Arrhenius equation

$k = Ae^{-\dfrac{E_a}{RT}}$

Taking the natural log of both sides, this expression is rewritten as

$\ln k = -\dfrac{E_a}{RT}+\ln A$

which is in the form $y=mx+b$ as evidencend by the linear graph. The dependent variable plotted is $\ln k,$ and the independent variable is $\dfrac{1}{T}.$  The expression can be written as

$\textcolor{blue}{\ln k} = -\dfrac{E_a}{R} \textcolor{blue}{\dfrac{1}{T}}+\ln A$

to emphasize that the slope is $-\dfrac{E_a}{R}$ and the intercept is $\ln A.$

This question is categorized as easy as the student must interpret the information that can be deduced from the linearized expression of the Arrhenius equation.
