# CH1241

$2.00\,\text{g}$ of powdered solid $\ce{CaCO3}$ are mixed with $200\,\text{cm}^3$ of HCl(aq) $2.00\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$ at $20\,{}^\circ\text{C}.$ The volume of carbon dioxide produced in this reaction is plotted against time with a dashed line.

The experiment is repeated, keeping all conditions constant except for the temperature. A second trial is performed at $50\,{}^\circ\text{C}.$ Which of these curves would correspond to the new trial?

![CH1241](images/CH1241.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
An increase in the temperature will increase the rate of the chemical reaction, so the curve will be steeper initially.

However, the amount of product produced should be exactly the same as the reactants have not been modified at all. The same final volume of carbon dioxide should be formed at the end of the chemical reaction. *Note: in a typical water displacement reaction, the carbon dioxide is collected at room temperature for every trial.*

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must predict the shape of the curve after the temperature is modified.
