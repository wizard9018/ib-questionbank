# CH0732

A rate law for a reaction is determined to be $\textrm{rate} = k[\ce{A}][\ce{B}]^2$, what are the units for the rate constant, $k$?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$mol$^{-1}$ dm$^{-3}$ s$^{-1}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$mol$^{-2}$ dm$^6$ s$^{-1}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$mol$^{-2}$ dm$^{-3}$ s$^{-1}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$mol$^{-3}$ dm$^{-9}$ s$^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

$\textrm{mol} \ \textrm{dm}^{-3} \textrm{\ s}^{-1} = k(\textrm{mol} \textrm{\ dm}^{-3})^{3}$\

$\textrm{s}^{-1} = k(\textrm{mol}\ \textrm{dm}^{-3})^{2}$\

$k = \textrm{mol}^{-2}\  \textrm{dm}^6\  \textrm{s}^{-1}$
