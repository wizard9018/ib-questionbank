# CH1196

Choose the correct statement based on the energy profile below:

![CH1196](images/CH1196.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$The chemical reaction occurs in two elementary steps

- B. $\hspace{1em}$M, O, and Q represent the intermediates in the reaction mechanism

- C. $\hspace{1em}$Step 1 is the rate-determining step

- D. $\hspace{1em}$N and P represent the transition states

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Many reactions occur in a series of elementary steps.

This diagram shows N and P as intermediates and three unstable transition states (M, O, and Q) corresponding to three elementary steps here (in green).

![CH1196B](images/CH1196B.png)

The greatest activation energy is that of the first elementary step, meaning that this will be the slow step (also known as the rate-determining step. RDS).

This question is categorized as hard as students should interpret the energy profile of a multistep reaction, identifying transition states and intermediates and recognizing the RDS from here.
