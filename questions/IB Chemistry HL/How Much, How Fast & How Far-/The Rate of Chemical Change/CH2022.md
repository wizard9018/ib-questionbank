# CH2022

Which statement about the chemical reaction that has the energy profile below is correct?

![CH2022a](images/CH2022a.png)

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | X and Z | Y |Rate$\text{--}$determining step |
    | :-- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Intermediates | Transition state | First step |
    | B. | Intermediates | Transition state | Second step |
    | C. | Transition states | Intermediate | First step |
    | D. | Transition states | Intermediate | Second step |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must be able to interpret the graph. The mechanism involves a sequence of two elementary steps. The activation energies for these two steps are labelled on the graph below.

![CH2022b](images/CH2022b.png)

The mechanistic steps of the chemical reaction can be written as

$\begin{aligned}
\text{Step 1:} &\quad & \ce{Reactants &-> Y} \\
\text{Step 2:} &\quad & \ce{Y &-> Products}
\end{aligned}$

Y represents the intermediate. X and Z are transition states for the two elementary steps.  There are two activation energies: $E_{a,1}$is the activation energy of step 1 while $E_{a,2}$ is the activation energy of step 2.

The rate$\text{--}$determining step is the slowest step. The slowest step corresponds to the one with the highest activation energy, in this case, step 2.

This question is categorized as medium as the student must be able to interpret the graph and deduce the RDS identifying intermediates and transition states.
