# CH0193

Hydrogen peroxide decomposes to form water and oxygen gas, according to the following reaction:

$\ce{2 H2O2(l) -> 2 H2O(l) + O2(g)}$

Potassium iodide increases the reaction rate without being itself consumed. Based on this information, which of the following statements is correct?     

- A.$\hspace{1em}$A temperature change will not affect the rate of the reaction.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The reaction must be second order with respect to $\ce{H2O2}.$

- C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{KI}$ addition causes a decrease of the activation energy of the reaction. 

- D.$\hspace{1em}$There will be no change in the rate constant value due to the addition of $\ce{KI}.$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\

The decomposition of hydrogen peroxide is a reaction that occurs slowly at room temperature because the activation energy is relatively high. When potassium iodide is added, it acts as a catalyst by providing an alternative reaction pathway with a lower activation energy. The catalyst itself is not consumed in the overall process; instead, it speeds up both the forward and reverse reactions equally. As a result, the rate constant $k$ increases, because the Arrhenius equation shows that a lower activation energy makes $k$ larger at a given temperature. This does not change the order of the reaction, which must be determined experimentally, nor does it make the system immune to changes in temperature. Therefore, the correct reasoning is that potassium iodide decreases the activation energy, allowing more molecules to have sufficient energy to react at the same temperature, and thus increasing the observed rate of decomposition.
