# CH0736

The reaction, $\ce{B -> products}$, is determined to be first order.

What should be plotted on the $x$ and $y$ axes, respectively, to determine the rate constant (k) for this reaction?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$time, [B]

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$time, $\dfrac{1}{[\ce{B}]}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$time, ln[B]

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$ln[B], time

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

For a first$-$order reaction, a plot of ln[B] vs. time will produce a linear relationship with a slope of $-k$.
