# CH1041

Consider the mechanism below.

$\begin{aligned}
\ce{A + B &<=> C}\qquad & fast \\
\ce{C &<=> F + M}\qquad & slow \\
\ce{M + F + B &<=> 2A + G}\qquad & fast
\end{aligned}$

Which is the correct rate expression for the overall chemical reaction?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\textrm{rate} = k\ce{[A][B]}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\textrm{rate}  = k\ce{[C]^2}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\textrm{rate}  = k\ce{[M][F][B]}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\textrm{rate}  = k\ce{[A][B]^2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The second step of the mechanism is the slow step. This step is known as the rate-determining step; the rate expression for the overall reaction is determined by step 2 in this case.

$\textrm{rate} = k^{\prime\prime}[\ce{C}]$

However, C is an intermediate. So, we can substitute [$\ce{C}$]. As it depends on the collision of A and B, we can state $k^{\prime}[\ce{A][B}]$ instead.

The final expression for the rate, using one overall rate constant, is

:answer[$\textrm{rate} = k\ce{[A][B]}$]

This question is categorized as hard. The student must understand how the rate$-$determining step leads to the rate expression.
