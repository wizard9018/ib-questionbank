# CH1173

Consider the chemical reaction given below.

$\ce{P4O6(s) + 12 HCl(aq) -> 4 PH3(g) + 6 Cl2O(g)}$

Which statements are correct for the rate of this chemical reaction?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | The rate of consumption of $\ce{P4O6}$ is greater than that of HCl |
  | II. | The production rate of $\ce{Cl2O}$ is greater than the consumption rate of $\ce{P4O6}$ |
  | III. | The rate of this chemical reaction can be monitored by controlling the pH as time goes by |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The reaction rate is expressed as the change in concentration per unit of time. In addition, there is a relationship between the reactants and products according to the stoichiometric coefficients.

$\begin{aligned}
\text{Rate of consumption of } \ce{P4O6} &= \dfrac{-\Delta[\ce{P4O6}]}{\Delta{t}} \\
\text{Rate of consumption of } \ce{HCl} &= \dfrac{-\Delta[\ce{HCl}]}{\Delta{t}}
\end{aligned}$

Rate of reaction = $\dfrac {-\Delta[\ce{P4O6}]}{\Delta{t}} = \dfrac{-\Delta[\ce{HCl}]}{12\Delta{t}}$

Then:

$\text{Rate of consumption of } \ce{P4O6} = \dfrac{1}{12}\times \text{Rate of consumption of HCl}$

Statement I is incorrect.

With the same reasoning, the production rate of Cl$_{2}$O is 6 times greater than that of the consumption rate of $\ce{P4O6}.$ Statement II is correct.

Finally, as an acid reacts, the pH should increase as the acid is consumed. Monitoring the pH change could be an effective method to monitor the rate of the chemical reaction. Statement III is correct.

This question is categorized as hard as comparisons must be made between reactants and products. In addition, a method to monitor the chemical reaction should be analyzed.
