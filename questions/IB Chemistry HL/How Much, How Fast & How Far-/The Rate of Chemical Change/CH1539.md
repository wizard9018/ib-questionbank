# CH1539

Which statement is correct about a catalyst?

- I.$\hspace{1em}$It increases the rate of a chemical reaction by increasing the activation energy.

- II.$\hspace{0.7em}$It is not consumed as the chemical reaction takes place.

- III.$\hspace{0.4em}$It increases the yield of the product obtained.

  

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

- C.$\hspace{1em}$II only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The student must understand that a catalyst provides an alternative mechanism for a chemical reaction to occur. This alternative mechanism has a lower activation energy, so more particles have enough energy (energy above the activation energy) to form products. Statement I is incorrect as it does not increase the activation energy.

The catalyst is not used up as the reaction proceeds; it remains chemically unchanged. Statement II is correct.

Finally, the use of a catalyst affects the rate but does not affect the yield of the product. Statement III is incorrect.

This question is categorized as easy as the student must understand the implications of the use of a catalyst.
