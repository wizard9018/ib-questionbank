# CH0201

The rate equation for the thermal decomposition of hydrogen peroxide is $\textrm{rate} = k[\ce{H2O2}]$.  Which of the following graphs shows the relationship between the rate and the temperature of this reaction?

![16.2-201](images/16.2-201.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

The rate constant, $k$, is the feature of the rate equation that is affected by temperature according to the Arrhenius equation $k = Ae^{-Ea/RT}.$

The question is of moderate difficulty as the use of a specific example and reference to rate rather than $k$ may derail some students. Confusion with the Arrhenius plot $(\ln k$ *vs.* $1/T)$ could also lead students to select option choose a linear graph.
