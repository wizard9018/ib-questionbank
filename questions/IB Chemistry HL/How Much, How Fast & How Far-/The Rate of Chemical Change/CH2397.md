# CH2397

As a reaction proceeds at constant temperature, the rate decreases. Which statement correctly explains why?

-   I.$\hspace{1em}$The activation energy increases as reactants are consumed.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The frequency of collisions between reactant particles decreases.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$The average kinetic energy of the particles decreases.

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$I only

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$II only

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$I and II only

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
As reactants are consumed, their concentration falls. Fewer particles per unit volume means fewer collisions per unit time, so the rate decreases. Statement II is the correct explanation.

The activation energy is the minimum energy required to break or weaken bonds in the reactants so that the transition state can be reached. It is a fixed property of the reaction and does not depend on how much reactant remains.  Statement I is incorrect.

At constant temperature, the Maxwell$-$Boltzmann distribution does not change. The average kinetic energy of the particles remains the same throughout the reaction. The rate decreases because there are fewer reactant particles available to collide, not because the particles move more slowly.  Statement III is incorrect.
