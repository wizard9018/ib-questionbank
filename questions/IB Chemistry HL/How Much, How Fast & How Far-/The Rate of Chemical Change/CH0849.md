# CH0849

**Maximum mark: 10**

The thermal stability of metal carbonates can be determined by measuring the rate at which they decompose.

$\ce{MCO3(s) -> MO(s) + CO2(g)}$

1.  Suggest a suitable experimental procedure to monitor the rate of this reaction over time and sketch an appropriate graph. [2 marks]

2.
    1.  Use Section 12 (*Section 13- 2025 Syllabus*) of the data booklet and the data in the table below to calculate $\Delta H$ for the thermal decomposition of $\ce{CaCO3}$ and sketch an energy profile diagram for this reaction. [4 marks]

    | Compound | $\mathbf{\Delta H_f}$ (kJ mol$\mathbf{^{-1}}$) |
    | :-: | :-: |
    | $\ce{CaCO3(s)}$ | $-1207.0$ |
    | $\ce{CaO(s)}$ | $-635.0$ |

    2.  Suggest why this reaction does not take place at room temperature. [1 mark]

3.
    1.  Other than increasing the temperature, suggest two ways to increase the rate of this reaction. [2 marks]

    2.  Sketch the curve for this change on your graph from part (a). [1 mark]
