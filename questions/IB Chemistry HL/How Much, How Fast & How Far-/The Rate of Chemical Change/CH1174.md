# CH1174

Which statement about the role of nickel in the hydrogenation of ethene is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$It acts as an oxidizing agent

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$It increases the activation energy

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$It increases the time taken for the chemical reaction to occur

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$It provides an alternative pathway for the chemical reaction to occur

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The hydrogenation of ethene is shown below.

$\ce{CH2CH2 + H2 ->[Ni and heat] CH3CH3}$

The addition of hydrogen is classified as a reduction; H$_{2}$ is the reducing agent whereas nickel acts as a catalyst.

Catalysts increase the reaction rate by providing an alternative pathway for the reaction with a lower activation energy.

This question is categorized as hard as students must combine their knowledge about hydrogenation with the interpretation of nickel as a catalyst.
