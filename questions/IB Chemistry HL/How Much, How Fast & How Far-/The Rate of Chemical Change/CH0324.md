# CH0324

In an experiment, nitric acid is reacted with sodium hydroxide to form sodium nitrate and water as shown in the equation below:

$\ce{NaOH(aq) + HNO3(aq) -> NaNO3(aq) + H2O(l)}$

The table below shows the concentrations of nitric acid measured at different time intervals. 

|Time (s)|Concentration of HNO$_3$ $\\$ (mol dm$^\textrm{-3}$)|

|:-:|:-:|

|0|0.450

|0.500|0.200

The rate of the reaction is equal to:

-   A.$\hspace{1em}0.125 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}\textrm{\ s}^{-1}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}0.500 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}\textrm{\ s}^{-1}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}0.250 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}\textrm{\ s}^{-1}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}1.25 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}\textrm{\ s}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

The average rate is equal to the change in concentration divided by the change in time. Hence, 

average rate = $-\dfrac{0.200\,\text{mol dm}^{-3} - 0.450\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}}{0.500\,\text{s} - 0\,\text{s}} = 0.500\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\text{s}^{-1}$

$\ $

*Note: This data is not accurate for this very fast neutralization reaction.*
