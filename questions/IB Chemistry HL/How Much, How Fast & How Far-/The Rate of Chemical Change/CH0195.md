# CH0195

**Maximum mark: 11**

The reactions of pollutants with ozone in the atmosphere is studied to investigate the effects on the hole in the ozone layer. One substance that reacts with ozone is nitrogen monoxide. 

The reaction is: $\ce{O3(g) + NO(g) -> O2(g) + NO2(g)}$

In an experiment, the following data were collected at 25 $^\circ$C:

| Trial | Concentration of $\mathbf{O}_\mathbf{3} \newline$ (mol dm$\mathbf{^{-3}}$) | Concentration of NO $\newline$ (mol dm$\mathbf{^{-3}}$) | Rate of reaction $\newline$ (mol dm$\mathbf{^{-3}}\,\textbf{s}\,\mathbf{^{-1}}$) |

| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |

| 1 | $3.00 \times 10^{-3}$ | $1.00 \times 10^{-3}$ | $6.60 \times 10^1$ |

| 2 | $6.00 \times 10^{-3}$ | $1.00 \times 10^{-3}$ | $1.32 \times 10^2$ |

| 3 | $9.00 \times 10^{-3}$ | $1.00 \times 10^{-3}$ | $1.98 \times 10^2$ |

| 4 | $9.00 \times 10^{-3}$ | $2.00 \times 10^{-3}$ | $3.96 \times 10^2$ |

| 5 | $9.00 \times 10^{-3}$ | $3.00 \times 10^{-3}$ | $5.94 \times 10^2$ |

1.  Use the data to determine the order of the reaction for each reactant, and the overall order of the reaction. [3 marks]

2.  Give the rate expression for this reaction. [1 mark]

3.  Calculate $k$ for this reaction using a set of data from the table and determine its correct units. [3 marks]

4.  What does the value of $k$ suggest about this reaction in the atmosphere? [2 marks]

5.  Suggest with a reason, a possible mechanism consistent with your rate expression. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  As ozone concentration doubles (and NO stays same), rate doubles/or calc to show same (eg. trial 1 to 2) $-$ therefore, first order in ozone; $\checkmark$

    As NO concentration doubles (eg. trial 3-4) while ozone conc is same, rate doubles/calc to show same $-$ therefore, first order with respect to NO; $\checkmark$

    (First order with respect to both NO and $\ce{O3}$ is) second order overall. $\checkmark$

    **OR**

    

    Do the calculations as per below:

    $rate = k[\ce{O3}]^m[\ce{NO}]^n$

    Trials 1 and 2:

    $\begin{aligned}

    rate_1 &= k(3.00\times 10^{-3})^m(1.00\times 10^{-3})^n \\

    rate_2 &= k(6.00\times 10^{-3})^m(1.00\times 10^{-3})^n \\

    \dfrac{rate_1}{rate_2} &= \dfrac{66}{132} = \dfrac{(3.00\times 10^{-3})^m}{(6.00\times 10^{-3})^m} \\

    0.5 &= (0.5)^m\quad \Longrightarrow \text{ therefore, } m = 1\,\checkmark

    \end{aligned}$

    

    Trials 3 and 4:

    $\begin{aligned}

    rate_3 &= k(9.00\times 10^{-3})^m(1.00\times 10^{-3})^n \\

    rate_4 &= k(9.00\times 10^{-3})^m(2.00\times 10^{-3})^n \\

    \dfrac{rate_3}{rate_4} &= \dfrac{198}{396} = \dfrac{(1.00\times 10^{-3})^n}{(2.00\times 10^{-3})^n} \\

    0.5 &= (0.5)^n\quad \Longrightarrow \text{ therefore, } n = 1\,\checkmark

    \end{aligned}$

    Overall order of the reaction $m + n = 2\,\checkmark$

2.  $rate = k[\ce{NO}][\ce{O3}]\,\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    Using the conclusions from part (a). The reaction is first order with respect to $\ce{O3}$ and first order with respect to NO.

3.  $k = \dfrac{rate}{\ce{[NO][O3]}}$

    Values from any row of data table acceptable; $\checkmark$

    so, $k = \dfrac{1.32\times 10^2}{(1.00\times 10^{-3})(6.00\times 10^{-3})} = 2.20\times 10^7\,\checkmark$

       

    Units for $k = \dfrac{\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\text{s}^{-1}}{(\text{mol}\,\text{dm}^{-3})(\text{mol}\,\text{dm}^{-3})} = \text{mol}^{-1}\,\text{dm}^3\,\text{s}^{-1}\,\checkmark$

4.  *Any two from the following:*$\checkmark$ $\checkmark$

    As $k$ is very large, this suggests rate is fast at 25 $^\circ$C and NO reacts with ozone very quickly;

    Causing reduction of ozone layer/hole in ozone layer;

    Might be different rate/not as reactive at lower temperatures than 25 $^\circ$C / higher at increased $T$;

    Species NO needs to be sufficiently present / high conc to react with ozone.

5.  The mechanism depends on both species equally/OWTTE;

    The slow or rate determining step includes one of each species; $\checkmark$

    Eg. Slow step: $\ce{NO + O3 ->}$ intermediate (eg $\ce{NO4}$)

    Fast step: Intermediate $\ce{-> NO2 + O2}$ $\checkmark$

    **OR**

    Other proposal consistent with rate expression.
