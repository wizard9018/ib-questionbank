# CH0337

**Maximum mark: 6**

Ammonia gas is produced when a fixed volume of 0.1 M NaOH was added to $\ce{(NH4)2SO4}$. The following table tracks the concentration of NaOH against time as the reaction progresses.

| Time (s) | [NaOH] (M) |

|:-:|:-:|

0|0.100

50|0.082

100|0.067

200|0.045

400|0.020

1.  Use the graph below to plot the concentration of NaOH against time. [2 marks]

    ![6.1-337](images/6.1-337.png)

2.  Calculate the initial rate of reaction using the graph.[2 marks]

3.  Calculate the rate of reaction at the 300$^{\th}$ second. Compare this value with the value calculated in part (b). Comment on the rate of reaction.[2 marks]

    Rate of reaction = $\dfrac{y_1-y_2}{x_1-x_2}$

    

    Initial rate of reaction = $\dfrac{0.060 - 0.100}{100 - 0}$ = :answer[0.00040] (orange line) $\checkmark$

    *Accepted range: 0.00030 to 0.00050.*\

    *Note: Any other points on the tangent are accepted to calculate the rate of reaction. Students are expected to draw the tangent line.* 

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    Drawing a tangent to the line at a given time allows calculating the gradient, and, therefore, the rate of reaction at that time. A tangent drawn at $t=0$ can be used to calculate the initial rate of reaction.
