# CH0204

The rate constant of a reaction at $298 \textrm{\ K}$ is $3\times 10^{-3}\,\textrm{s}^{-1},$ while the rate constant at $308 \textrm{\ K}$ is $6\times 10^{-3}\,\textrm{s}^{-1}.$

What is the activation energy of this reaction, in kJ mol$^{-1}$?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{8.31 \times \ln(2)}{1000 \times \left(\dfrac{1}{298} - \dfrac{1}{308}\right)}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{8.31 \times \ln(0.5)}{1000 \times \left(\dfrac{1}{298} - \dfrac{1}{308}\right)}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{1000 \times \ln(2)}{8.31 \times \left(\dfrac{1}{298} - \dfrac{1}{308}\right)}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{1000 \times \ln(0.5)}{8.31 \times \left(\dfrac{1}{298} - \dfrac{1}{308}\right)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

The linear Arrhenius equation can be expressed for two points as 

$\ln\left(\dfrac{k_1}{k_2}\right) = -\dfrac{E_a}{R} \left( \dfrac{1}{T_1} - \dfrac{1}{T_2} \right)$

Substituting the values from the problem: 

$\ln\left(\dfrac{3\times 10^{-3}\,\textrm{s}^{-1}}{6\times 10^{-3}\,\textrm{s}^{-1}}\right) = -\dfrac{E_a}{8.31 \textrm{\ J mol}^{-1}\textrm{K}^{-1}} \left( \dfrac{1}{298 \textrm{\ K}} - \dfrac{1}{308 \textrm{\ K}} \right)$

Rearranging to isolate the activation energy:

$E_a = -\dfrac{8.31 \times \ln\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)}{\left( \dfrac{1}{298} - \dfrac{1}{308} \right)} \textrm{\ J mol}^{-1} \times \dfrac{1 \textrm{\ kJ}}{1000 \textrm{\ J}} $

$E_a = - \dfrac{8.31 \times \ln\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)}{1000 \times \left( \dfrac{1}{298} - \dfrac{1}{308} \right)} \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1} = \dfrac{8.31 \times \ln\left(2\right)}{1000 \times \left( \dfrac{1}{298} - \dfrac{1}{308} \right)} \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1} $

The question is in the hard category since students must be able to identify the correct terms in the question for use in the equation, rearrange the equation correctly, and also perform a unit conversion.
