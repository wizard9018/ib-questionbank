# CH1004

Which change produces the greatest increase in the initial rate of the reaction between hydrochloric acid and magnesium?

$\ce{Mg(s) + 2 HCl(aq) -> MgCl2(aq) + H2(g)}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The concentration of HCl(aq) is doubled, and the temperature is increased.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The concentration of HCl(aq) is doubled, and the temperature is decreased.

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The concentration of HCl(aq) is halved, and the temperature is increased.

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The concentration of HCl(aq) is halved, and the temperature is decreased.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

An increase in temperature affects the rate of a chemical reaction. As temperature increases, there is a higher average kinetic energy, and the frequency of successful collisions increases.

In addition, if the concentration of aqueous reactants increases, there will be more particles per unit volume. As a result, the frequency of successful collisions increases as well. If temperature and concentration are increased, the rate of reaction increases.

This question is categorized as easy as students must apply their knowledge of the effect of temperature and concentration as factors affecting chemical reaction rates.
