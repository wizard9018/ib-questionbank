# CH0203

The rate constants for a reaction are plotted in a graph of $\ln k$ *vs.*  $1/T$.

![CH0203](images/CH0203.png)

What is the activation energy of this reaction?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{R \times a}{c}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{c}{R \times b}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{c}{R \times (a - b)}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{R \times (b - a)}{c}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Consider the relationship

$\ln k = -\dfrac{E_a}{R}\left( \dfrac{1}{T}\right) + \ln A$

When $\ln k$ is plotted againt $\dfrac{1}{T}$, the gradient of the Arrhenius plot is $\dfrac{-E_a}{R}.$ 

Students must recognize how to calculate the gradient from the values labelled in the graph. Using the two points $(0, b)$ and $(c, a)$, the gradient is calculated as 

$\textrm{gradient} = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \dfrac{a - b}{c-0}$

Finally, the gradient must be multiplied by $-R$ to obtain the required value

$E_a = \dfrac{R \times (b-a)}{c}$

The question qualifies as hard due to the number of steps in this process.
