Reaction 1 has a larger frequency factor, $A$, than Reaction 2 but a smaller activation energy, $E_a$.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

This question can be answered by interpreting the linear form of the Arrhenius equation because the graphs show $\ln k$ plotted agains $\frac{1}{T}$.

$\underset{y}{\textcolor{red}{\ln k}} = \underset{m}{\textcolor{green}{-\dfrac{E_a}{R}}} \underset{x}{\textcolor{blue}{\left(\dfrac{1}{T}\right)}} + \underset{b}{\textcolor{orange}{\ln A}}$

The $y-$intercept = $\ln A$ so must be larger for Reaction 1. The gradient (slope) is equal to $\frac{-E_a}{R}$ so, must be shallower (less negative) for Reaction 1.

The question is in the hard category as students must know how to interpret an Arrhenius plot and compare two reactions graphically.
