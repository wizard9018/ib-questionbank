# CH0191

For a reaction, a catalyst can:

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$Alter the reaction mechanism.

-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$Introduce a step with lower activation energy.

-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$Be involved in the rate determining step.

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only 

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only 

- C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only 

- D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II,  and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\

Catalysts provide an alternative pathway with a lower activation energy. They can therefore, change the mechanism and be involved in the rate determining step (slow step).
