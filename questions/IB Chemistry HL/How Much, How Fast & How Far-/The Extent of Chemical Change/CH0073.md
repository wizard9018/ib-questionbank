# CH0073

The equilibrium constant for the reaction $\ce{2NO{(g)} + Cl2{(g)} <=> 2NOCl{(g)}}$ is $K_c$.

What is the equilibrium constant for the reaction
$\mbox{}\\[10pt]$

$\ce{NO{(g)} + 1/2 Cl2{(g)} <=> NOCl{(g)}}$?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\sqrt{K_c}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\dfrac{1}{2}K_c$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $K_c$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $2K_c$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
In the original equation, the equilibrium constant expression is

$K_c = \dfrac{[\ce{NOCl}]^2}{[\ce{NO}]^2 [\ce{Cl2}]}$

The coefficients of all species in the equation are halved which results in the second exression, so the new equilibrium constant is

$K = \dfrac{[\ce{NOCl}]}{[\ce{NO}] [\ce{Cl2}]^{1/2}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{[\ce{NOCl}]^2}{[\ce{NO}]^2 [\ce{Cl2}]}} = \sqrt{K_c}$
