# CH0085

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the following reaction:

  $\ce{PCl5 (g) <=>PCl3 (g) + Cl2 (g)} \qquad K_c = 0.497$ at $500\,\text{K}$

1.
    1. Outline two characteristics of a closed system at dynamic equilibrium. [2 marks]

    2. State the equilibrium constant expression for this reaction. [1 mark]

A $100\, \textrm{cm}^{3}$ container was found to contain $0.723\,\text{mol}$ of $\ce{PCl5}$, $0.273\,\text{mol}$ of $\ce{PCl3}$ and $0.261\,\text{mol}$ of $\ce{Cl2}$.

2.
    1. State the difference between the reaction quotient and the equilibrium constant. [1 mark]

    2. Calculate the reaction quotient and deduce in which direction the reaction will proceed. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1. rates of the forward and reverse reaction are equal $\checkmark$\
        no change in concentrations of reactants and products.$\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
   By definition, at dynamic equilibrium, the rates of the forward and reverse reactions are the same. This does not mean that the concentrations of reactants and products will be equal, but rather the concentrations of each will not change as they are being consumed and produced at the same rate.

    2. $K = \dfrac{\ce{[PCl3}][\ce{Cl2}]}{\ce{[PCl5]}}$ $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
    The equilibrium constant expression for the reaction

    $a \ce{\,A} \rightleftharpoons b \ce{\,B} + c \ce{\,C}$

    is expressed as the ratio of the concentrations of the products raised to their stoichiometric coefficients over the concentration of the reactant raised to its:

    $K = \dfrac{[\ce{C}]^c}{[\ce{A}]^a[\ce{B}]^b}$

    For the decomposition of $\ce{PCl5},$ the equilibrium constant expression is

    $K = \dfrac{\ce{[PCl3}][\ce{Cl2}]}{\ce{[PCl5]}}$

2.  1.  $K$ is the ratio of products to reactants using equilibrium concentrations;\
    $Q$ is the same ratio using non$-$equilibrium concentrations. $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
        When a system has not reached equilibrium, the ratio of the concentration of products to reactants will not equal K$_c$. This ratio is called the reaction quotient $Q$. This helps to determine the progress of the reaction as it moves toward equilibrium and the direction of the reaction that is favored to establish equilibrium.

    2.  $Q = 0.986 \: \checkmark$\
        $Q > K$, reverse reaction favored $\checkmark$

        $\textrm{\underline{Further Explanation:}}$\
        Given the amounts and volume, calculate $Q$:

        $\dfrac{[\ce{PCl3}][\ce{Cl2}]}{[\ce{PCl5}]}$ = $\dfrac{\left(\dfrac{0.273 \textrm{\ mol}}{0.1 \textrm{dm}^3}\right)  \left(\dfrac{0.261 \textrm{\ mol}}{0.1 \textrm{\ dm}^3}\right)}{\dfrac{0.723 \textrm{\ mol}}{0.1 \textrm{\ dm}^3}} = 0.986$

        The concentration of products is greater than the equilibrium value so the reverse reaction is favored (the reaction shifts towards the reactants).
