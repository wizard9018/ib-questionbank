# CH2374

The equilibrium constants at different temperatures are given below for the reaction $\ce{A}\textrm{(g)} \rightleftharpoons \ce{B}\textrm{(g)}$.

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

| Temperature / K | $\mathbf{K}$ |
|:-:|:-:|
| 300 | $1.20 \times 10^{-2}$ |
| 350 | $2.35 \times 10^{-3}$ |
| 400 | $5.57 \times 10^{-4}$ |

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

Which statement is correct about this equilibrium?

- A.$\hspace{1.em}$The position of equilibrium is not affected by changes in pressure, and the forward reaction is exothermic.

- B.$\hspace{1.em}$The position of equilibrium is not affected by changes in pressure, and the forward reaction is endothermic.

- C.$\hspace{1.em}$The position of equilibrium is affected by changes in pressure, and the forward reaction is exothermic.

- D.$\hspace{1.em}$The position of equilibrium is affected by changes in pressure, and the forward reaction is endothermic.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
There are two deductions to make from the information given.

**Pressure:** The reaction has one mole of gas on each side $(\Delta n_{\textrm{gas}} = 0).$ A change in pressure has no effect on the position of equilibrium when the total moles of gas are equal on both sides.

**Enthalpy:** As the temperature increases from 300 K to 400 K, the equilibrium constant decreases. Increasing temperature always favours the endothermic direction. Since the forward reaction is disfavoured $(K$ decreases), the forward reaction must be exothermic $(\Delta H < 0).$
