# CH0084

**Maximum mark: 6**

Ammonia is produced through the Haber process according to the following reaction:

$\ce{N2}\textrm{(g)} + 3 \ce{\,H2}\textrm{(g)}  \rightleftharpoons 2 \ce{\,NH3}\textrm{(g)}  \quad  K_c = 360$

$\ce{NH3}$ is added to a $100\,\text{cm}^3$ container and allowed to equilibrate. At $t = T_1$, $\ce{N2}$ is added.

1. On the following graph, sketch the change in concentration of $\ce{N2}$ after $t = T_1$. [2 marks]

    ![CH0084](images/CH0084.png)

2. At equilibrium there are $8.97 \times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}$ of $\ce{N2}$ and $3.85\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}$ of $\ce{H2}$.

    $\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

    1. State the equilibrium constant expression for the formation of $\ce{NH3}$. [1 mark]

    2. Calculate the concentration of $\ce{NH3}$ at equilibrium. [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
    Adding $\ce{N2}$ causes a sudden vertical increase in $[\ce{N2}]$ at $T_1.$ The system is no longer at equilibrium. According to Le $\textrm{Ch\^{a}telier's}$ principle, the forward reaction is favoured to partially consume the added $\ce{N2}.$ The concentration of $\ce{N2}$ decreases exponentially and settles at a new equilibrium value that is higher than the original, since $K_c$ does not change when concentration changes.

2.
    1. $K_{c} =\dfrac{\ce{[NH_{3}]^{2}}}{\ce{[N_{2}][H_{2}]^{3}}} \: \checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
        The equilibrium constant expression places products over reactants, each raised to the power of its stoichiometric coefficient. For heterogeneous equilibria only gaseous and aqueous species are included.

    2. $[\ce{N_2}] = 8.97 \times 10^{-2}\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$, $[\ce{H_2}] = 3.85 \times 10^{-2}\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \: \checkmark$\
    $[\ce{NH_3}] = \sqrt{K_c \times [\ce{N_2}] \times [\ce{H_2}]^3} \: \checkmark$\
    ${[\ce{NH_3}] = 0.0429\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}}$ $\: \checkmark$

        *Award [3] marks for correct answer.*

        $\textrm{\underline{Further Explanation:}}$\
         Since the vessel volume is $100\textrm{\ cm}^3 = 0.100\textrm{\ dm}^3,$ the concentration of the reactants is

        $[\ce{N_2}] = \dfrac{8.97 \times 10^{-3}}{0.100} = 8.97 \times 10^{-2}\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$

        $[\ce{H_2}] = \dfrac{3.85 \times 10^{-3}}{0.100} = 3.85 \times 10^{-2}\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$

        Rearrange the $K_c$ expression for $[\ce{NH_3}].$

        $K_c = \dfrac{[\ce{NH_3}]^2}{[\ce{N_2}] \times [\ce{H_2}]^3} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad [\ce{NH_3}] = \sqrt{K_c \times [\ce{N_2}] \times [\ce{H_2}]^3}$

        Substitute in the concentrations and $K_c$:

        $[\ce{NH_3}] = \sqrt{360 \times 8.97 \times 10^{-2} \times (3.85 \times 10^{-2})^3}$

        $[\ce{NH_3}] = \sqrt{1.843 \times 10^{-3}} = 0.0429\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$
