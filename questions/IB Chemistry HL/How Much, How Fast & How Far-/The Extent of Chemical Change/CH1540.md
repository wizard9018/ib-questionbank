# CH1540

The system described below corresponds to the state of equilibrium for the reaction X $\rightleftharpoons$ Y at 298 K.

![CH1540A](images/CH1540A.png)

The temperature is increased to 398 K, and the system is allowed to reachieve equilibrium. This new equilibrium at 398 K is shown below.

![CH1540B](images/CH1540B.png)

Which statement is correct? 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The reaction is endothermic, and the increase in temperature favoured the forward reaction.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The reaction is endothermic, and the increase in temperature favoured the reverse reaction.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The reaction is exothermic, and the increase in temperature favoured the forward reaction.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The reaction is exothermic, and the increase in temperature favoured the reverse reaction.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The student must interpret the diagrams provided. The increase in the temperature shifted the equilibrium so that the reactant formation was favoured (two Y particles turned into two X particles). 

An increase in temperature favours the endothermic reaction; while both forward and reverse reactions are sped up with the increase in temperature, the endothermic reaction is sped up more. In this case, increasing the temperature favoured the reverse reaction.  Since the reverse reaction is endothermic,  we can conclude that the forward reaction is exothermic.

This question is categorized as a medium, as the student must interpret the effect of temperature on this equilibrium and work out the sign of the enthalpy change.
