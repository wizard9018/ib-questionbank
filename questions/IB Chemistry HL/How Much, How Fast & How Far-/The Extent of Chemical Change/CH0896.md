# CH0896

Which of the below statements are true of a system at equilibrium?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Gibbs Free Energy is zero |
  | II. | $Q = K_c$ |
  | III. | There is maximum disorder in the system |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

At equilibrium, $\Delta G$ (Change in Gibbs Free Energy) $= 0$ but Gibbs free energy itself is at a minimum.

$Q \neq K_c$ at non-equilibrium concentrations, but, at equilibrium, $Q = K_c$.

Entropy is at its maximum at equilibrium.

$\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

$\Delta G = \Delta G^\ominus + RT \ln Q$

$\Delta G = -RT \ln K_c + RT \ln Q = -RT \ln \frac{K_c}{Q}$

When $\Delta G = 0$, $K_c = Q$.  When $\Delta G < 0$, the reaction is spontaneous and $K_c > Q$.  When $\Delta G > 0$, the reaction is non-spontaneous, the reverse reaction is spontaneous, and $K_c < Q$.
