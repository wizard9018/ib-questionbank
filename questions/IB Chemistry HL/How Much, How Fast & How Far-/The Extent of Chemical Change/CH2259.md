# CH2259

Cyclopropane, $\ce{C3H6},$ undergoes hydrogenation according to the equation:

$\ce{C3H6}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{H2}\textrm{(g)}
\longrightarrow \ce{C3H8}\textrm{(g)}$

Use the the average bond enthalpies:

$\qquad \ce{C-C} \qquad$ | $\qquad x \qquad $
:-:|:-:
$\ce{C-H}$ | $y$
$\ce{H-H}$ | $z$

to estimate the ring strain in cyclopropane. The experimental enthalpy change for this reaction is $-120 \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}.$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}(x+z)-2y-120$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}\left[3x+6y+z\right]-\left[2x+8y\right]+120$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}\left[2x+8y\right]-\left[3x+6y+z\right]-120$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}2y-(x+z)+120$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
To calculate the ring strain energy, compare the theoretical enthalpy change for hydrogenation with the actual measured enthalpy. The extra energy released corresponds to the ring strain being relieved.

|$\textrm{bonds broken (reactants):}$ | $3x+6y+z$
|:--|:--|
bonds formed (products): | $2x+8y$

$\newline \ \newline$
$\Delta H^{\minuso} = \sum \textrm{bonds broken} - \sum \textrm{bonds formed}$

$\Delta H^{\minuso} = \left[3x+6y+z\right]-\left[2x+8y\right] = (x + z) - 2y$

Ring strain is the difference between the theoretical and actual enthalpy values:

$\textrm{Strain energy}  = \Delta H(\textrm{theoretical}) - \Delta H(\textrm{experimental})$

$\textrm{Strain energy} = \ $ :answer[$\left[3x+6y+z\right]-\left[2x+8y\right] + 120$]
