# CH0756

What will happen to the value of $K_c$ and equilibrium yield of $\ce{HCO3^-}$ if the volume of the reaction vessel is decreased?  

$\ce{CO2(g) + H2O(l) <=> H+(aq) + HCO3-(aq)}$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$Yield increases, and $K_c$ increases

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$Yield decreases, and $K_c$ decreases

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$Yield increases, and $K_c$ remains the same

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$Yield decreases, and $K_c$ remains the same

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Decrease in volume leads to an increase in pressure. Equilibrium shifts to the right as there are fewer gaseous moles.

$K_c$ only changes with temperature.
