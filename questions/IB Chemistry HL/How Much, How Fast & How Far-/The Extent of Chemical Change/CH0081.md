# CH0081

The following graph represents the change of concentration of a gaseous reactant and gaseous product during a reaction. At $t = 10 \textrm{\ min}$, the temperature is increased and at $t = 15 \textrm{\ min}$, the volume of the reaction vessel is increased.

![CH0081](images/CH0081.png)

Which row of the table below correctly describes the enthalpy change of the forward reaction and the moles of gas of the reactants and products?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  | | $\quad$Enthalpy change of forward reaction $\quad$ | $\quad$Moles of gas$\quad$ |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\quad$Exothermic | $\quad$Reactants more than the products $\quad$ |
  | B.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\quad$Exothermic | $\quad$Reactants less than the products $\quad$|
  | C.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\quad$Endothermic |$\quad$ Reactants more than the products $\quad$|
  | D.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\quad$Endothermic | $\quad$Reactants less than the products $\quad$|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
The endothermic reaction is favoured when the temperature increases. The concentration of products increases at $t=10 \textrm{\ min}$; therefore, the forward reaction must be endothermic.

 Increasing the volume decreases the pressure and causes the side with more moles of gas to be favoured. The concentration of reactants increases at $t=15 \textrm{\ min}$; therefore, the reactants must have more moles of gas than the products.
