# CH1006

What is the equilibrium constant expression for the following equation?

$\ce{X(s) + 2 Y2(g) <=> XY4(g)}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{\ce{[XY4]}}{[\ce{X}] + \ce{[Y2]^2}}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{\ce{[XY4]}}{\ce{[X][Y2]^2}}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{\ce{[Y2]^2}}{\ce{[XY4]}}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{\ce{[XY4]}}{\ce{[Y2]^2}}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

X should not be considered in the expression as it is a solid. The equilibrium expression includes the equilibrium concentrations of products in the numerator, in this case [$\ce{XY4}$], and the concentrations of reactants in the denominator, in this case only [$\ce{Y2}$]. Each concentration should be raised to the power of its coefficient in the balanced chemical equation, so $\ce{[Y2]^2}$ should appear in the denominator.

$K = \dfrac{[\ce{XY4}]}{\ce{[Y2]^2}}$

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as the student must deduce the $K$ expression correctly.
