1. State Le Chatelier's principle. [1 mark]

2. Write a balanced chemical equation for this reaction. [1 mark]

3.
    1. Predict, giving a reason, the effect of increasing the temperature on the yield of carbonic acid. [2 marks]

    2. Predict, giving a reason, the effect of decreasing the pressure on the yield of carbonic acid. [2 marks]
