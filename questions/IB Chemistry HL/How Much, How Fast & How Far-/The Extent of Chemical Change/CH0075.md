# CH0075

Which change does not affect the position of equilibrium?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Increasing the concentration of a reactant

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Increasing the concentration of a product

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Adding a catalyst
- D.$\hspace{1em}$Increasing the temperature

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
A catalyst provides an alternative pathway for the reaction lowering the activation energy. It affects both the forward and reverse reaction rates equally. The position of equilibrium will not change.
