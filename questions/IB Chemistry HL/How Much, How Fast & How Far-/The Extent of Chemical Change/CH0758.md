# CH0758

Consider the following reaction:

$\ce{CH4(g) + H2O(g) <=> CO(g) + 3 H2(g)}\qquad \Delta H = +210\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}$

At time $t$, the reaction quotient $Q = 4.2 \times 10^{-3}$. As the reaction proceeds to reach equilibrium, the pressure inside the reaction vessel increases.  What can be deduced about the value of $K$?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}K < 4.2 \times 10^{-3}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}K > 4.2 \times 10^{-3}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}K = 4.2 \times 10^{-3}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}K = 1$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

The increasing pressure inside the reaction vessel indicates that the forward reaction is being favored as the system moves toward dynamic equilibrium.  More products will be formed so $Q$ will continue to increase as the reaction approaches equilibrium.

$K$ is greater than $Q$.
