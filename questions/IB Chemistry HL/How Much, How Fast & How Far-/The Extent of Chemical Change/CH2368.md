# CH2368

Consider the following exothermic equilibrium.

$\ce{H_2}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{Br_2}\textrm{(g)} \rightleftharpoons 2\ce{\,HBr}\textrm{(g)}$

At time $t_0$, some $\ce{HBr}$ is removed from the mixture. Which graph correctly shows the concentration of $\ce{HBr}$ as a function of time?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Before $t_0$ the system is at equilibrium. Removing $\ce{HBr}$ causes an instantaneous drop in its concentration.

With less product present $(Q < K),$ the system shifts to the right, producing more $\ce{HBr}$ until equilibrium is re$-$established.

The equilibrium constant is unchanged (temperature is constant). However, because matter was permanently removed from the system, the new equilibrium concentration of $\ce{HBr}$ is lower than the original.
