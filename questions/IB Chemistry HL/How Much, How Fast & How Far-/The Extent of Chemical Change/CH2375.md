# CH2375

Which row correctly describes the effect of decreasing the temperature on this reaction at equilibrium?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{A}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{B}\textrm{(aq)} \rightleftharpoons \ce{C}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{D}\textrm{(aq)}$ $\hspace{3em}\Delta H \approx 0$

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

| $\hspace{1.em}$||| $\mathbf{K}$ | $[\ce{C}]$ |
|:-: |:-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
|| A. | | Decreases | Decreases |
|| B. | | Increases | Increases |
|| C. | | Unchanged | Unchanged |
|| D. | | Decreases | Unchanged |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The enthalpy change for this reaction is approximately zero $(\Delta H \approx 0).$ The equilibrium constant depends on temperature through the enthalpy change: when $\Delta H \approx 0,$ a change in temperature has essentially no effect on the equilibrium constant.

Since $K$ does not change, the position of equilibrium does not shift, and the concentrations of all species remain unchanged.
