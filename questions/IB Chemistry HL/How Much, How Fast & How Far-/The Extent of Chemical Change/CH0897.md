# CH0897

Which of the rows below is incorrect?

$\begin{aligned}
\Delta G &= -RT \ln K \\
\Delta G &= \Delta H - T \Delta S
\end{aligned}$

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | T | Sign of $\mathbf{\Delta H}$ | Sign of $\mathbf{\Delta S}$ | $\mathbf{K_c}$ |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | A. | high | + | + | > 1 |
    | B. | low | + | - | < 1 |
    | C. | high | - | + | > 1 |
    | D. | low | - | - | < 1 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Reactions are spontaneous when $\Delta G$ is negative. Using logarithmic rules, when $\Delta G$ is negative, $K_c > 1$.

For **A**, $\Delta G$ is negative ($- T \Delta S$ is large) so $K_c > 1 \longrightarrow$ correct.

For **B**, $\Delta G$ is positive at all temperatures so $K_c < 1 \longrightarrow$ correct.

For **C**, $\Delta G$ is negative at all temperatures so $K_c > 1 \longrightarrow$ correct.

For **D**, $\Delta G$ is negative at low temperatures $(- (- T \Delta S)$ is small so $K_c > 1 \longrightarrow$ incorrect.
