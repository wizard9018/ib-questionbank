## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The student must show that the equilibrium mixture corresponds to the minimum of the curve.

![CH2280ans](images/CH2280ans.png)

The equilibrium constant magnitude will be greater with a greater percentage of products as

$K \propto \dfrac{\textrm{[products]}}{\textrm{[reactants]}}$

or as the largest negative change in Gibbs free energy because

$\Delta G = -RT \ln K$

This question is categorized as medium as the student must interpret the graph and relate it to the equilibrium constant magnitude.
