# CH1541

The equilibrium constant is given for the reaction below.

 $\ \textrm{A}_2 + \ \textrm{B}_2 \rightleftharpoons$  2 AB    $\hspace{3em} K $

Which is the correct equilibrium constant expression for this reaction?

$\ce{AB} \rightleftharpoons \frac{1}{2}\ce{A_2} + \frac{1}{2}\ce{B_2}\hspace{3em}$  ? 

$\ $

 $\mbox{}\\[-20pts]$

- A.$\hspace{1em}\sqrt{K}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{K}}$ 

- C.$\hspace{1em}\frac{1}{2}K$

- D.$\hspace{1em}2K$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The student must see the relationship between these two equilibrium constant expressions.

$\ \textrm{A}_2 + \textrm{B}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2 \textrm{AB}$    $\hspace{2em}$    $\ K_c = \dfrac{[\textrm{AB}]^2}{[\textrm{A}_2][\textrm{B}_2]}$

$\ \textrm{AB} \rightleftharpoons \frac{1}{2}\textrm{A}_2 + \frac{1}{2}\textrm{B}_2$    $\hspace{2em}$    $K'_c = \dfrac{[\textrm{AB}]}{[\textrm{A}_2]^{\frac{1}{2}}[\textrm{B}_2]^{\frac{1}{2}}}$

Rearranging the equation for $K_c'$ we cand find the relationship to $K_c$:

 $K_c' =  \dfrac{[\textrm{AB}]}{\sqrt{[\textrm{A}_2]}\sqrt{[\textrm{B}_2]}} = \sqrt{\dfrac{[\textrm{AB}]^2}{[\textrm{A}_2][\textrm{B}_2]}} = \sqrt{K_c}$

This question is categorized as a medium as the student must find the relationship between the equilibrium constant expressions.
