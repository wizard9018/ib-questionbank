# CH0445

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the following equilibrium:

$\ce{H2(g) + I2(g) <=> 2 HI(g)} \qquad  K_c = 50.7$

1.  State the equilibrium constant expression for this reaction.  [1 mark]

2.  0.264 mol of $\ce{H2}$ and $\ce{I2}$ each are added to a $300 \textrm{\ cm}^3$ container and allowed to equilibrate.
    1. Calculate the initial concentrations of $\ce{H2}$ and $\ce{I2}$. [2 marks]

    2. Calculate the concentration of $\ce{HI}$ at equilibrium. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $K_c = \dfrac{[\ce{HI}]^2}{[\ce{H2}][\ce{I2}]} \, \checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    For the reaction $\ce{a A + b B <=> c C + dD}$

    $K_c = \dfrac{[\ce{C}]^c[\ce{D}]^d}{[\ce{A}]^a[\ce{B}]^b}$

2.  1.  $C_\ce{H2}=\dfrac{n_\ce{H2}}{V}=\dfrac{0.264}{0.3}=0.880\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}\ \checkmark $

        $C_\ce{I2}=\dfrac{n_\ce{I2}}{V}=\dfrac{0.264}{0.3}=0.880\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}\  \checkmark$

    2. $\mbox{}\\[-25pts]$

        |  | $\ce{H2}$ |$\ce{I2}$ |$\ce{HI}$ ||
        | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-:|
        | $\textbf{Initial}$ | 0.880 | 0.880 | 0 |
        | $\textbf{Change}$ | $-x$ | $-x$ | $\enskip +2x \enskip$ | $\ \ \ \ \checkmark$
        | $\enskip \textbf{Equilibrium} \enskip$ |$\enskip 0.880-x\enskip$ | $\enskip 0.880-x \enskip$ | $2x$ |

        $x=0.687\, \checkmark$

        $[\ce{HI}]=2x=1.37\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}\, \checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

        Use an ICE table to determine the amount of each species at equilibrium given the initial concentration and the equilibrium constant:

        $\begin{aligned}
        K_c = \dfrac{(2x)^2}{(0.880-x)^2} &= 50.7 \\
        \dfrac{2x}{0.880-x} &= \sqrt{50.7} \\
        2x &= 6.27-7.12x \\
        9.12x &= 6.27 \\
        x &= 0.687
        \end{aligned}$

        $[\ce{HI}] = 2x = 1.37\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

        The ratio of $\ce{H2:I2:HI}$ is $1:1:2$. Therefore, as the reaction proceeds, the concentrations of $\ce{H2}$ and $\ce{I2}$ will decrease by $x$, and the concentration of $\ce{HI}$ will increase by $2x$.
