# CH0438

A 1 dm$^3$ container contains 2 mol $\ce{NH3}$, 1 mol $\ce{N2}$ and 1 mol $\ce{H2}$ at equilibrium. What is the value of the equilibrium constant?

$\ce{N2 + 3 H2 <=> 2 NH3}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{3}{4}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{4}{3}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}4$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{1}{4}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$K_c =\dfrac{\ce{[NH3]^2}}{\ce{[N2][H2]^3}} = \dfrac{[2]^2}{[1][1]^3} = 4$
