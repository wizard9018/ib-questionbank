# CH0440

$2.0 \textrm{\ mol}$ of $\ce{SO3}$ is placed into a reaction vessel and allowed to reach equilibrium. Which row correctly represents the relative concentrations of $\ce{SO2}$ and $\ce{O2}$ at equilibrium?

$\ce{2SO3}(\textrm{g}) \ce{\ <=> 2SO2}(\textrm{g}) + \ce{O2}(\textrm{g}) $

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  | | $\enskip\ce{[SO2]}\enskip$ | $\enskip \ce{[O2]}\enskip$ |
  | :-: | :-: | :-: |
  | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | $x$ | $2x$ |
  | B.$\hspace{1em}$ | $2x$ | $x$ |
  | C.$\hspace{1em}$ | $x$ | $x$ |
  | D.$\hspace{1em}$ | $4x$ | $x$ |

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The ratio of $\ce{SO3:SO2:O2}$  is $2:2:1$. Therefore, half the number of moles of oxygen would be produced compared to $\ce{SO2}$.
