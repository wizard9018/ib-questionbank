# CH1044

Which is correct for a chemical reaction with $\Delta G^{\minuso} = -2900\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}?$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$spontaneous, $K > 1.$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$nonspontaneous, $K > 1.$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$spontaneous, $K < 1.$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$nonspontaneous, $K < 1.$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The standard Gibbs free energy changeis negative for spontaneous reactions. In addition, this expression relates Gibbs free energy change with the equilibrium constant:\

$\Delta G^{\minuso} = -RT \ln K$

If we consider a spontaneous reaction:

$\begin{aligned}
\Delta G^{\minuso} &< 0 \\
-RT\ln K &< 0 \\
RT\ln K &> 0
\end{aligned}$

$R$ and $T$ are always positive ($R = 8.31\,\text{J K}^{-1}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$ and $T$ is Kelvin/absolute scale).

$\begin{aligned}
\ln K &> 0 \\
K &> 1
\end{aligned}$

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must understand what is meant by spontaneous and should relate it with equilibrium.
