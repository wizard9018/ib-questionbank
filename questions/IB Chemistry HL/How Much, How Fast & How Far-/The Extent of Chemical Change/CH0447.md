# CH0447

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the following equilibrium:

$\ce{PCl5(g) <=> PCl3(g) + Cl2(g)}$

| $\enskip \ce{[PCl3]}_{initial}\enskip$ | $\enskip 0.100\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\enskip$ |
| :---: | :---: |
| $\ce{[Cl2]}_{initial}$ | $0.100\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$ |
| $\ce{[PCl5]}_{equilibrium}$ | $0.720\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$ |
| $K_c$ | $5.00 \times 10^{-2}$ |

1.  Using the data above, calculate the initial concentration of $\ce{PCl5}$. [3 marks]

    The graph below shows the relationship between $\ln(K)$  and $\dfrac{1}{T}$ for this reaction.

    ![17.1-CH0447](images/CH0447.png)

2.  Using the graph above, deduce whether this reaction is exothermic or endothermic. Explain your reasoning. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  | $\text{}$ | $\ce{PCl5}$ |$\ce{PCl3}$ |$\ce{Cl2}$ |
    | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
    | $\textbf{Initial}$ | $\enskip 0.720+x \enskip$ | 0.100 | 0.100 |
    | $\textbf{Change}$ | $-x$ | $+x$ | $\enskip +x \enskip$ |
    | $\enskip \textbf{Equilibrium} \enskip$ | $\enskip 0.720\enskip$ | $\enskip 0.100+x \enskip$ | $\enskip 0.100+x\enskip$ |

    $\mbox{\checkmark}$

    $\begin{aligned}
    K_c &= \dfrac{[\ce{PCl3}][\ce{Cl2}]}{[\ce{PCl5}]} \\
    0.05 &= \dfrac{(0.100+x)^2}{0.720} \\
    (0.100+x)^2 &= 0.036 \\
    0.100 + x &= \sqrt{0.036} \\
    x &= 0.0897\, \checkmark
    \end{aligned}$

    $\ce{PCl5}_{initial} = 0.720 + x = 0.810\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    For the reaction $\ce{a A + b B <=> c C + d D}$

    $K_c =\dfrac{[C]^c[D]^d}{[A]^a[B]^b}$, where all concentrations are at equilibrium.

    As the reaction proceeds, the concentration of $\ce{PCl5}$ decreases by $x$, with $\ce{PCl3}$ and $\ce{Cl2}$ both increasing by $x$ ($1:1:1$ ratio).

2.  There is a negative relationship between $\ln(K)$ and $\dfrac{1}{T}$, so the equilibrium constant increases as temperature increases. $\checkmark$

    From Le Chatelier's principle, the forward reaction must be endothermic (or vice versa)$\checkmark$

    to oppose the change/ absorb the additional heat when the temperature increases OWTTE. $\checkmark$
