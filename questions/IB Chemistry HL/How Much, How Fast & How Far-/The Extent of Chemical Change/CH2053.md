# CH2053

Which of the following shows the equilibrium constant expression for the decomposition of calcium carbonate?
$\\[10pts]$

$\ce{CaCO3(s) <=> CaO(s) + CO2(g)}$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}K_c = \dfrac{\ce{[CaO][CO2]}}{\ce{[CaCO3]}}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}K_c = \dfrac{1}{\ce{[CO2]}}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}K_c = \dfrac{\ce{[CaCO3]}}{\ce{[CaO][CO2]}}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}K_c = \ce{[CO2]}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The equilibrium constant expression is the concentration of the products divided by the concentration of the reactants where the concentrations are raised to the power of their stoichiometric constant and multiplied together. Only gases, aqueous ions, and liquids (in rare cases, where they are dissolved in another solvent) are included. Solids are never included. Therefore, the equilibrium constant expression for this reaction is $K_c = \ce{[CO2]}.$
