# CH0895

$Z$ moles of $\ce{N2}$ and $Z$ moles of $\ce{H2}$ are placed in a sealed flask and allowed to reach equilibrium, where $X$ moles of $\ce{N2}$ are used up.

Which row gives the correct expression for the total number of moles of all species at equilibrium?

$\ce{N2(g) + 3 H2(g) <=> 2 NH3(g)}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}2 (Z - X)$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}Z - X$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}Z + X$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}2 X - 2 Z$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

| | N$\mathbf{_2}$ | 3 H$\mathbf{_2}$ | 2 NH$\mathbf{_3}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Initial moles | $Z$ | $Z$ | $0$ |
| Change in moles | $-X$ | $-3X$ | $+2X$ |
| Equilibrium moles | $Z - X$ | $Z - 3X$ | $2X$ |

Total moles $= Z - X + Z - 3X + 2X = 2Z - 2X$
