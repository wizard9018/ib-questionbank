# CH0767

**Maximum mark: 7**

A titration between dichloroethanoic acid and barium hydroxide was carried out in the presence of the indicator thymol blue.

$\ce{2CHCl2COOH(aq) + Ba(OH)2(aq) -> (CHCl2COO)2Ba(aq) + 2H2O(l)}$

The indicator thymol blue, HThy$^-$, is in dynamic equilibrium with its deprotonated form Thy$^{2-}$.

$\ce{\underset{\large yellow}{HThy-(aq)} <=> \underset{blue}{Thy^2-(aq)} + H+(aq)}\qquad \Delta H = +ve$

1.  23.40 cm$^3$ of 0.100 mol dm$^{-3}$ dichloroethanoic acid was needed to exactly neutralize 25.00 cm$^3$ barium hydroxide. Calculate the concentration of the barium hydroxide solution. [2 marks]

2.  State the $K_c$ expression for the equilibrium of the indicator. [1 mark]

3.  In a barium hydroxide solution, thymol blue appears blue. Suggest and explain using Le Chatelier\'s principle how the color would change:

    1.  On heating of the barium hydroxide solution. [2 marks]

    2.  On addition of dichloroethanoic acid to the barium hydroxide solution. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $n(\ce{CHCl2COOH}) = \dfrac{23.40}{1000}\times 0.100 = 2.34\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}$

    **AND**

    $n(\ce{Ba(OH)2} = 1.17\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}\,\checkmark$

    Concentration = $\dfrac{1.17 \times 10^{-3}}{25.00 \times 10^{-3}} = 0.0468\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    $n = C \times V$

    Volume must be in dm$^3$.

    Ratio 2 : 1, therefore, $n(\ce{Ba(OH2)}) = \dfrac{n(\ce{CHCl2COOH})}{2}$

2.  $K_c = \dfrac{[\ce{Thy^{2-}}][\ce{H^+}]}{[\ce{HThy-}]}$ $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    For a reaction $\ce{a A + b B -> c C + d D}$,

    $K_c = \dfrac{[\ce{C}]^c[\ce{D}]^d}{[\ce{A}]^a[\ce{B}]^b}$

3.
    1.  Remains blue; $\checkmark$

        Equilibrium shifts to the right/towards the products/towards the endothermic side. $\checkmark$

        *ALLOW deeper blue/more blue*

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        Le Chatelier's principle dictates that the position of the equilibrium will shift to oppose any change to the system. As temperature is increased, the equilibrium shifts to reduce the temperature and so favors the endothermic reaction (forwards) which would increase the concentration of the blue Thy$^{2-}$.

    2.  Turns green/yellow; $\checkmark$

        Equilibrium shifts to the left/towards reactants to reduce the concentration of H$^+$. $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        Adding dichloroethanoic acid increases the H$^+$ concentration. Equilibrium shifts to the left to oppose that change and produces more yellow HThy$^-$ making the solution green as it mixes with the blue Thy$^{2-}$.
