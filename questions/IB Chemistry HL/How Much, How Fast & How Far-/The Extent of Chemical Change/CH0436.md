# CH0436

Which statement about the relationship between the standard Gibbs free energy change and the equilibrium constant is correct?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$If $\Delta G^{\minuso}$ is positive, $K_c<1$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$If $\Delta G^{\minuso}$ is positive, $K_c>1$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$If $\Delta G^{\minuso}$ is positive, $K_c=1$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$If $\Delta G^{\minuso}$ is positive, there is not enough information to determine the value of $K_c$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
$\Delta G^{\minuso} = -RT\,\ln K$

If $\Delta G^{\minuso}$ is positive, $\ln K$ must be negative. $\ln K$ is negative when $K < 1.$

A positive $\Delta G^{\minuso}$ value indicates that the reaction is not spontaneous under standard conditions. Therefore, the equilibrium concentrations of the reactants are larger than the ones of the products; the equilibrium constant is less than 1.
