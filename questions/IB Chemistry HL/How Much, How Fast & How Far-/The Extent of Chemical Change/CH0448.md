# CH0448

**Maximum mark: 6**

Hydrogen chloride is a colourless gas that can be produced by reacting colourless hydrogen gas with greenish-yellow chlorine gas.

1.  State a balanced chemical equation with state symbols for this reaction. [1 mark]

    The standard Gibbs free energy change for this reaction is $-191$ kJ mol$^{-1}$ at 25 $^\circ \mathrm{C}$.

2.  1.  Using sections 1 and 2 of the data booklet, calculate $K_c$ at 25 $^\circ \mathrm{C}.$ [3 marks]

    2.  Comment on this value of $K_c$ and its significance on the production of hydrogen chloride gas. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\ce{H2(g) + Cl2(g) <=> 2 HCl(g)}\,\checkmark$

    *State symbols required*

2.  1.  $T = 25 + 273 = 298\,\text{K}\,\checkmark$

        $\begin{aligned}
        \Delta G^\ominus &= -RT\,\ln(K) \\
        \ln(K) &= \dfrac{\Delta G^\ominus}{-RT} \\
        K &= e^{\frac{\Delta G^\ominus}{-RT}} = e^{-\frac{191000}{(-8.31)(298)}} \,\checkmark \\
        &= 3.14\times 10^{33}\,\checkmark
        \end{aligned}$

    2.  The value of $K_c$ is much greater than one $\checkmark$

        which means that equilibrium lies very far to the right and the production of $\ce{HCl}$ gas is highly favoured at this temperature. $\checkmark$
