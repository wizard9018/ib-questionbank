# CH1244

Consider the equilibrium below for a system at $273\,\text{K}$ in a $2.00\,\text{dm}^3$ vessel.

$\ce{XY + Y2 <=> XY3}$

The evolution of the system is plotted against time.

![CH1244](images/CH1244.png)

What is the value of the equilibrium constant at $273\,\text{K}?$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$0.150

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$6.67

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$3.33

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$0.300

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The value of the equilibrium constant is determined at a given temperature using the concentrations of the reactants and products at equilibrium. These concentrations can be deduced from the graph as the amounts for each reactant and product can be obtained after equilibrium is reached, where the amounts of products and reactants remain constant.

To deduce the concentrations, divide the amount of each species by the volume of the vessel.

$\ce{[XY3]} = \dfrac{3.00\,\text{mol}}{2.00\,\text{dm}^3} = 1.50\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

$\ce{[XY]} = \dfrac{5.00\,\text{mol}}{2.00\,\text{dm}^3} = 2.50\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

$\ce{[Y2]} = \dfrac{4.00\,\text{mol}}{2.00\,\text{dm}^3}=2.00\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

Then, we can determine the value of the equilibrium constant:

$K_c = \dfrac{[\ce{XY3}]}{[\ce{XY}][\ce{Y2}]} = \dfrac{1.50}{2.50 \times 2.00} = 0.300$

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must interpret the graph and determine the equilibrium concentrations to determine the $K_c$ magnitude.
