# CH1243

A chemical reaction between $\ce{A2}$ and $\ce{B2}$ is investigated.

$\ce{A2 + B2 <=> 2 AB}$

Consider the following evolution of the system.

![CH1243](images/CH1243.png)

Which statement about chemical equilibrium is correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$After 600 seconds, $K$ decreases, and the reaction shifts to the product formation

- B. $\hspace{1em}$After 600 seconds, $K$ decreases, and the reaction shifts to the reactant formation

- C. $\hspace{1em}$After 600 seconds, $K$ remains constant, and the reaction shifts to the product formation

- D. $\hspace{1em}$After 600 seconds, $K$ remains constant, and the reaction shifts to the reactant formation

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The initial system is kept at a constant temperature. That means that the equilibrium constant, $K,$ does not vary.

The initial and final states are shown below:

$\begin{aligned}
\text{Initial state:}&\qquad \ce{3 A2, 3 B2, and 14AB} \\
\text{Final state:}&\qquad \ce{6 A2, 6 B2, and 8AB}
\end{aligned}$

The reaction shifts by consuming $\ce{6 AB}$ molecules to produce $\ce{3 A2}$ and $\ce{3 B2}$ molecules. The reaction shifts to the reactant formation.

This question is categorized as hard as the interpretation of the system is expected to outline the changes in the system to achieve equilibrium.
