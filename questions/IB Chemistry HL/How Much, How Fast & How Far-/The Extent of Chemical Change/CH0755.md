# CH0755

At a certain temperature, the equilibrium constant for the following reaction is:

$\ce{H2 (g) + I2 (g) <=> 2 HI (g)} \qquad K = 54$

What is the value of $K$ for the following equilibrium at the same temperature?

$\ce{HI (g) <=> \frac{1}{2} H2 (g) + \frac{1}{2} I2 (g)} \qquad K =\ ?$

$\mbox{}\\[-20pts]$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{54}{2}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\sqrt{54}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{54}}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\dfrac{1}{27}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

To determine the equilibrium constant for the new reaction, we apply two rules:

-   * Reversing an equation inverts the equilibrium constant:

    $K' = \dfrac{1}{K}$

-   * Multiplying all coefficients in a balanced equation by a factor of $n$ raises the equilibrium constant to that power:

    $K' = K^n$

In this case, the original reaction was both reversed and halved, so:

$K' = \left(\dfrac{1}{K}\right)^{1/2} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{K}}$

Given that $K = 54,$ the new equilibrium constant is:

$K' = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{54}}$
