# CH0765

**Maximum mark: 8**

Nitrogen reacts with oxygen to produce nitrogen oxides including $\ce{NO2}$ as represented by the equation below.

$\ce{N2(g) + 2 O2(g) <=> 2 NO2(g)}$

1.  At 273 K and 100 kPa, 80 dm$^3$ of N$_2$ reacted with 120 dm$^3$ O$_2$.

    Calculate the number of moles of each gas and state which was present in excess. [2 marks]

2.  Write the $K_c$ expression for this reaction. [1 mark]

3.  $K_c$ for this reaction is $8.3 \times10^{-10}$ at 298 K and $9.1 \times 10^{-11}$ at 320 K.

    1.  Explain whether the forward reaction is exothermic or endothermic. [2 marks]

    2.  Deduce the value of $K_c$ for the reverse reaction at 298 K. [1 mark]

4.  Predict and explain the effect on the equilibrium yield of NO$_2$ if the volume of the reaction vessel was decreased. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $n_\ce{N2} = \dfrac{80}{22.7} = 3.5\,\text{mol}$

    **AND**

    $n_\ce{O2} = \dfrac{120}{22.7} = 5.3\,\text{mol}\,\checkmark$

    Ratio 1 : 2, therefore N$_2$ in excess. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    At STP, one mole of gas occupies 22.7 dm$^3$, therefore, $n = \dfrac{volume}{molar \ volume}$

    A 1 : 2 ratio means that for the 5.3 mol of $\ce{O2}$, only 2.65 mol of $\ce{N2}$ are required.

2.  $K_c= \dfrac{[\ce{NO2}]^2}{[\ce{N2}][\ce{O2}]^2}$ $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    For the equation $\ce{a A + b B <=> c C + d D}\qquad K_c = \dfrac{[\ce{C}]^c[\ce{D}]^d}{[\ce{A}]^a[\ce{B}]^b}$

3.
    1.  Exothermic;  $\checkmark$

        $K_c$ decreases **AND** Equilibrium shifts to the left/towards reactants/to endothermic side (when temperature is increased). $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        Le Chatelier's principle dictates that the position of the equilibrium will shift to oppose any change to the system. As temperature is increased, the equilibrium shifts to reduce the temperature and so favors the endothermic reaction (backwards) meaning the forward reaction must be exothermic.

    2.  $K_c= \dfrac{1}{8.3 \times 10^{-10}} = 1.2\times 10^9\,\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        $K_c(reverse)$ = $\dfrac{1}{K_c(forward)}$

4.  Yield increases; $\checkmark$

    Increased pressure **AND** equilibrium shift to the side with fewer moles of gas. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    Pressure and volume are inversely proportional. Le Chatelier's principle dictates that the position of the equilibrium will shift to oppose any change to the system. The equilibrium will shift to decrease the pressure by favoring the side with fewer moles of gas.
