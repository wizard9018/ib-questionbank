# CH1266

Consider the equilibrium below.

$\ce{\underset{\large colourless}{BiCl3(aq)} + H2O(l) <=> \underset{\large white}{BiOCl(s)} + 2 HCl(aq)}\qquad K_c = z \text{ at } 298\,\text{K}$

At a particular time, at $298\,\text{K},$ a reaction mixture has non-equilibrium concentrations, and $Q_c = 0.01\, z.$

Which statement is correct for the system at $298\,\text{K}?$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}K_c$ must increase, and the mass of the white solid will increase

-   B. $\hspace{1em}Q_c$ must decrease, and the mass of the white solid will decrease

-   C. $\hspace{1em}Q_c$ must increase, and the mass of the white solid will decrease

-   D. $\hspace{1em}Q_c$ must increase, and the mass of the white solid will increase

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The system presents a reaction quotient smaller than the equilibrium constant at this particular time.

$Q_c < K_c$

The system is not at equilibrium.

As the temperature is kept constant, $K_c$ will remain constant. Option A is incorrect.

To reach equilibrium, $Q_c$ must increase to reach $Q_c = K_c;$ Option B is incorrect.

$Q_c$ increases by shifting the reaction to the product side as $Q_c = \dfrac{\left[\ce{HCl}\right]^2}{\left[{\ce{BiCl3}}\right]\left[\ce{H2O}\right]}.$

As more product is formed, the mass of the white BiOCl solid increases.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must interpret the $Q_c / K_c$ relationship and determine the changes for this specific reaction.
