# CH0079

What effect would increasing the pressure have on the amount, in moles, of $\ce{H2}$ and $\ce{CO2}$ for the following reaction?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

 $\ce{H2O (g) + CO (g) <=> H2 (g) + CO2 (g)}$

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The amount of $\ce{H2}$ and $\ce{CO2}$ would increase.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The amount of  $\ce{H2}$ and $\ce{CO2}$ would decrease.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The amount of  $\ce{H2}$ and $\ce{CO2}$ would remain the same.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$There is not enough information.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
Both sides of the equation have the same number of gaseous moles, so the equilibrium position is unaffected by changes in pressure. With no shift, the amount of the reactants and products will stay the same.
