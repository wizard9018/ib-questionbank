# CH0435

Which of the following statements about a system at equilibrium are correct?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | The entropy is a maximum at equilibrium|
  | II. | The Gibbs free energy is a minimum at equilibrium |
  | III. | The Gibbs free energy change is zero at equilibrium |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

At equilibrium, neither reactants nor products are favored, resulting in maximum entropy and minimum Gibbs free energy, and therefore, no further change in Gibbs free energy.
