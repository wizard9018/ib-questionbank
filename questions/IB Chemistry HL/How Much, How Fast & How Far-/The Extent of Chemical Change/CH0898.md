# CH0898

What is the correct relationship between $Q$ and $K_c$ at the position marked on the curve with an X?

![CH0898_Image1](images/CH0898_Image1.png)

-   A. $\hspace{1em}Q > K_c$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}Q < K_c$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}Q = K_c$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}Q = 1$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Equilibrium can be found at the lowest point of the curve where Gibbs Free Energy is at a minimum. The reaction favors the products at X, so the products' concentrations are higher than the equilibrium concentrations. $Q\ >\ K_c$
