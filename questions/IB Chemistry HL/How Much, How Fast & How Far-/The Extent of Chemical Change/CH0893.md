# CH0893

107 g of $\ce{NH4Cl}$ decomposes in 2 dm$^{3}$ of water according to the equation below.

$\ce{NH4Cl(aq) <=> NH3(aq) + HCl(aq)}$

The equilibrium concentration of $\ce{HCl}$ is 0.25 mol dm$^{-3}$.

Which row gives the correct equilibrium concentration of $\ce{NH4Cl}$, and the total number of moles of all species at equilibrium?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | $\mathbf{[NH_4Cl]}$(eq) / mol dm$\mathbf{^{-3}}$| Total number of moles at equilibrium |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | A. | 1.5 | 3.0 |
    | B. | 1.5 | 2.5 |
    | C. | 0.75 | 2.5 |
    | D. | 0.75 | 3.0 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Initial moles,

$n(\ce{NH4Cl}) =\dfrac{mass}{M_r} = \dfrac{107}{(14 + 4 + 35.5)} = 2\,\text{mol}$

$n(\ce{HCl}_{eq}) = C\times V = (0.25)(2) = 0.5\,\text{mol}$

Ratio $\ce{HCl} : \ce{NH3} = 1 : 1$, therefore, $n(\ce{NH3}_{eq}) = 0.5\,\text{mol}$

$n(\ce{NH4Cl}_{eq}) = 2 - 0.5 = 1.5\,\text{mol}$

$\ce{[NH4Cl]}_{eq} =\dfrac{n}{V} = \dfrac{1.5}{2} = 0.75\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

Total moles $ = 0.5 + 0.5 + 1.5 = 2.5\,\text{mol}$
