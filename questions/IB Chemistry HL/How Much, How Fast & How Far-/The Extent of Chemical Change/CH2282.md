# CH2282

A mixture of $0.120 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ of A and $0.240 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ of B was placed in a vessel till equilibrium was reached according to the reaction

$\ce{A}\textrm{(g)} + 2 \ce{\,B}\textrm{(g)} \rightleftharpoons \ce{C}\textrm{(g)}$

At equilibrium, $[\ce{B}] =  0.100 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}.$ What is value of the equilibrium constant, $K_c?$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}14$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}140$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}0.071$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}0.0071$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The student must be able to work out the RICE table to evaluate the changes produced to reach equilibrium.

Reaction: |$\ce{A}\textrm{(g)}$ | + |  $2 \ce{\,B}\textrm{(g)}$ | $\rightleftharpoons$  | $\quad \ce{C}\textrm{(g)} \quad $
:--|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
$\textbf{Initial:}$|     0.120 ||        0.240    ||    0
$\textbf{Change:}$    | $-x$ ||    $-2x$ ||    $+x$
$\textbf{Equilibrium:}$ | $0.120 - x$ || $0.240 - 2x$ || $x$

At equilibrium, $[\ce{B}]_{eq}  = 0.100 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ of B.  This can be used to find the value of $x:$

$[\ce{B}]_{eq} = 0.100 = 0.240 - 2x ; \qquad x= 0.070 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$

All of the equilibrium concentrations can now be deduced.

$[\ce{A}]_{eq}$ | $=0.120-x=0.120-0.070  =0.050 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$
:-:|:--
$[\ce{B}]_{eq}$ | $= 0.100 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$
$[\ce{C}]_{eq}$ | $=x= 0.070 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$

The equilibrium constant can be deduced from here.

$K=\dfrac{[\ce{C}]}{[\ce{A}][\ce{B}]^2} = \dfrac{0.070}{0.050 \times 0.100^2} = $ :answer[140]

This question is categorized as medium as the student must work out the equilibrium concentrations to deduce the equilibrium constant magnitude.
