# CH2283

A chemical reaction has a Gibbs free energy change of $\Delta G = -5.53 \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}.$  Which is the correct expression for the equilibrium constant at $300\,{}^\circ \textrm{C}?$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}e^{\left(\dfrac{5.53}{8.31 \times 300}\right)}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}e^{\left(\dfrac{5530}{8.31 \times 300}\right)}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}e^{\left(\dfrac{5.53}{8.31 \times 573}\right)}$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}e^{\left(\dfrac{5530}{8.31 \times 573}\right)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The student must remember that $\Delta G=-RT \ln K,$ so the expression for the equilbrium constant is

$K = e^{-\left(\dfrac{\Delta G}{RT}\right)}$

However, it is essential to remember that the temperature should be expressed on the absolute, or Kelvin, scale.

$T = 300\,\degree\textrm{C} \longrightarrow 573 \textrm{\ K}$

The change in the Gibbs free energy must be expressed in $\textrm{J mol}^{-1}$ because $R$ has the units $\textrm{J mol}^{-1} \textrm{\,K}^{-1}.$ Substituting in the values:

$K = e^{-\left(\dfrac{-5.53 \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1} \times 1000 \textrm{\ J kJ}^{-1}}{8.31 \textrm{\ J mol}^{-1}\textrm{K}^{-1} \times (300+273) \textrm{\ K}}\right)} = \ $:answer[$e^{\left(\dfrac{5530}{8.31 \times 573}\right)}$]

This question is categorized as medium because the student must rearrange the free energy expression to isolate the equilibrium constant and achieve consistency in units.
