# CH0439

1.0 mol of $\ce{NO}$ and 1.0 mol of $\ce{Cl2}$ are put in a 1.0 dm$^3$ sealed flask and allowed to equilibrate. Which of the following statements is true for this reaction?

$\ce{2 NO(g) + Cl2(g) <=> 2 NOCl2(g)}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The equilibrium concentration of $\ce{Cl2}$ is greater than $\ce{NO}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The equilibrium concentration of $\ce{NOCl2}$ is the same as $\ce{NO}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The equilibrium constant is greater than one

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The reaction quotient is one at the beginning of the reaction

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The ratio of $\ce{NO}$ to $\ce{Cl2}$ is $2:1$, therefore as the moles of $\ce{NO}$ decrease by $2\alpha$, the number of moles of $\ce{Cl2}$ will decrease $\alpha$.
