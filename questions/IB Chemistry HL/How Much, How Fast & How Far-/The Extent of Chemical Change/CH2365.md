## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
For the equilibrium $\ce{M} \rightleftharpoons \ce{N},$ the equilibrium constant is

$K = \dfrac{[\ce{N}]}{[\ce{M}]}$

A larger value of $K$ means a higher ratio of $[\ce{N}]$ to $[\ce{M}]$ at equilibrium. On the graphs, this corresponds to the system where the $[\ce{N}]$ curve levels off at a concentration well above the $[\ce{M}]$ curve.
