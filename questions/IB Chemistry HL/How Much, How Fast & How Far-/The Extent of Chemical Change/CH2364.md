# CH2364

Consider the following equilibrium.

$\ce{X}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{Y}\textrm{(g)} \rightleftharpoons \ce{XY}\textrm{(g)} \qquad \Delta H > 0$

The external pressure on the piston is increased at constant temperature. Which diagram represents the correct final system?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The equilibrium has 2 mol of gas on the left and 1 mol of gas on the right. When the external pressure is increased at constant temperature, the system responds according to Le $\textrm{Ch\^{a}telier's}$ principle by shifting toward the side with fewer moles of gas, reducing the total number of gas particles to partially offset the pressure increase. This favours the forward reaction, producing more XY and consuming X and Y.

Option D is correct because it shows a decreased volume (piston lower), more XY molecules, fewer free X and Y molecules, and, crucially, the total number of atoms is conserved (the same number of X$-$atoms and Y$-$atoms as in the initial system, just rearranged into more XY pairs).

Option A is incorrect because the composition is unchanged (no shift has occurred, the gas is simply compressed). Option B is incorrect because it shows more free X and Y (a shift to the left, the wrong direction). Option C is incorrect because, although the shift direction is correct, the total number of atoms is not conserved.
