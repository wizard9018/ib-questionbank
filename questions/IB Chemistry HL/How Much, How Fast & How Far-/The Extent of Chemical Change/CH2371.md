# CH2371

Consider the equations below.

$\ce{W} \rightleftharpoons \ce{X}$ $\hspace{3em}K_1$

$\ce{X} \rightleftharpoons \ce{Y}$ $\hspace{3em}K_2$

Which is the correct expression for the equilibrium constant for the reaction $\ce{Y} \rightleftharpoons \ce{W}$?

- A.$\hspace{1.em}K_1K_2$

- B.$\hspace{1.em}K_1 + K_2$

- C.$\hspace{1.em}\dfrac{1}{K_1 K_2}$

- D.$\hspace{1.em}\dfrac{1}{K_1 + K_2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
**Step 1: Reverse both equations.**

The target reaction has $\ce{Y}$ as a reactant and $\ce{W}$ as a product, so both given equilibria must be reversed.

$\ce{X} \rightleftharpoons \ce{W}$ $\hspace{3em}\dfrac{1}{K_1}$

$\ce{Y} \rightleftharpoons \ce{X}$ $\hspace{3em}\dfrac{1}{K_2}$

Reversing an equilibrium inverts its equilibrium constant.

**Step 2: Add the reversed equations.**

$\ce{Y} \rightleftharpoons \ce{X} \rightleftharpoons \ce{W}$

$\ce{X}$ cancels as an intermediate, giving the target reaction $\ce{Y} \rightleftharpoons \ce{W}$.

When equilibria are added, their equilibrium constants are multiplied:

$K = \dfrac{1}{K_1} \times \dfrac{1}{K_2} = \dfrac{1}{K_1 K_2}$
