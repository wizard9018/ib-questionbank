# CH0072

Which of the following statements about a system in equilibrium is **incorrect**?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$It is a closed system

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The forward and reverse reactions occur at the same rate

- C.$\hspace{1em}$There is an equal amount of reactants and products

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The concentrations of reactants and products do not change

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
A dynamic equilibrium is reached in a closed system when the rates of the forward and reverse reactions are equal. The concentrations of reactants and products do not change but are non-stop being interconverted. The equilibrium could favor one side over the other.
