## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The student must understand that the quotient $Q$ provides a ratio of concentrations at a given time with nonequilibrium concentrations.

$Q \propto \dfrac{\textrm{[products]}}{\textrm{[reactants]}}$

A large $Q$ at time $0 \textrm{\ s}$ corresponds to a system with a lot of product, and little reactant, initially.  A large equilibrium constant $(K\gg1)$ indicates that the concentration of product must be greater than that of reactant at equilibrium.

This question is categorized as medium as the student must deduce information about the initial $Q$ and deduce the magnitude of the equilibrium constant based on a concentration *vs.* time graph.
