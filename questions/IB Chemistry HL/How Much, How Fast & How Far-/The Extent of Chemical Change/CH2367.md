# CH2367

Consider the equilibrium given below.

$\ce{A} + 2\ce{\,B} \rightleftharpoons \ce{AB_2} \qquad K = 54$

What is the correct equilibrium constant for $2\ce{\,AB_2} \rightleftharpoons 2\ce{\,A} + 4\ce{\,B}?$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}\dfrac{1}{54}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{54}}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}54^2$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}\dfrac{1}{54^2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The new equation is the reverse of the original, multiplied by 2. Two rules apply:

Reversing an equation inverts the equilibrium constant:

$K_{\textrm{rev}} = \dfrac{1}{K}$

Multiplying an equation by a factor $n$ raises the equilibrium constant to the power $n$:

$K_{\textrm{new}} = K^n$

Applying both: reverse first gives

$\dfrac{1}{K} = \dfrac{1}{54},$

then doubling gives

$K' = \left(\dfrac{1}{K}\right)^2 = \dfrac{1}{K^2} = \dfrac{1}{54^2}$
