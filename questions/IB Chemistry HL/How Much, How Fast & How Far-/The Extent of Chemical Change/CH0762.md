# CH0762

Consider the following exothermic reaction:

$\ce{2 \underset{\normalsize red-brown}{NO2(g)} <=> \underset{\normalsize colourless}{N2O4(g)}}\qquad K_c = 5.9\times 10^{-3}$

At time $t$, the reaction quotient, $Q$, is equal to $7.2 \times 10^{-2}$.

Which row shows the correct pressure and color change as the reaction approaches dynamic equilibrium?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  |  | $\quad$ Pressure $\quad$ | $\quad$ Color $\quad$ |
  | :-: | :-: | :-: |
  | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Increases | Darker |
  |B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Decreases | Paler |
  | C.$\hspace{1em}$  | Increases |Paler|
  | D.$\hspace{1em}$  | Decreases |Darker|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Since $Q > K_c$, the system is not at equilibrium and the backward reaction is favored. As the reaction shifts in the reverse direction, more moles of gas are formed on the reactant side (a $2:1$ ratio of reactants to products). This increases the total pressure of the system. Because $\ce{NO2}$ is red$-$brown, while $\ce{N2O4}$ is colorless, the mixture becomes darker as equilibrium is re$-$established.
