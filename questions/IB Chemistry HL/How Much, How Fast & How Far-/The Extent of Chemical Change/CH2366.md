# CH2366

Consider the equilibrium given below.

$\ce{A}\textrm{(aq)} + 2\ce{\,B}\textrm{(aq)} \rightleftharpoons 4\ce{\,C}\textrm{(aq)} \qquad \Delta H < 0$

Which statement is correct?

-   I.$\hspace{1em}K = \dfrac{[\ce{A}][\ce{B}]^2}{[\ce{C}]^4}$
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$An increase in pressure shifts the equilibrium to the right
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$An increase in temperature shifts the equilibrium to the left, and the equilibrium constant decreases.

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$I, II and III

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$I and III only

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$II only

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The equilibrium constant expression places products in the numerator and reactants in the denominator, each raised to the power of its stoichiometric coefficient. For this reaction:

$K = \dfrac{[\ce{C}]^4}{[\ce{A}][\ce{B}]^2}$

Statement I has the expression inverted, and is incorrect.

All species are aqueous. Changes in pressure have a significant effect only on equilibria involving gases (because gases are compressible). Since no gases are present, a pressure change does not shift this equilibrium. Statement II is incorrect.

The forward reaction is exothermic $(\Delta H < 0).$ An increase in temperature favours the endothermic (reverse) direction, shifting the equilibrium to the left. Because the position of equilibrium shifts away from products, the equilibrium constant decreases. Statement III is correct.
