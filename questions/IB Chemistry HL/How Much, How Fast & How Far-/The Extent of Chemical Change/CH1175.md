# CH1175

Consider the chemical reaction below.

$\ce{PCl3 + Cl2 <=> PCl5}$

Which statement about the equilibrium is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The removal of chlorine increases $K_c$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The removal of chlorine decreases $K_c$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The removal of chlorine shifts the chemical reaction to the product side

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The removal of chlorine shifts the chemical reaction to the reactant side

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Removing or adding reactants or products do not affect the value of the equilibrium constant, $K_c.$ The equilibrium constant is only affected by changes in temperature.

Le $\textrm{Ch\^{a}telier's}$ principle states that when equilibrium is disrupted, the chemical reaction shifts to counteract the change and reestablish equilibrium. Removing a reactant will be counteracted by shifting the reaction to the reactant side to reestablish the equilibrium.
