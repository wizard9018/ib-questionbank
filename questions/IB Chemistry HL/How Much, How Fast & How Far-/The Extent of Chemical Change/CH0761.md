# CH0761

Equal amounts of $\ce{PCl3}$ and $\ce{Cl2}$ gases are added to a reaction vessel and allowed to reach equilibrium. What is the relationship between the concentrations of $\ce{PCl3}$ and $\ce{PCl5}$ at equilibrium?

$\ce{PCl3} + \ce{Cl2} \rightleftharpoons \ce{PCl5} \hspace{1em}  K = 1$  at  350 K

$\ \mbox{}\\[-25pts]$ 

- A.$\hspace{1em}\sqrt{\ce{[PCl3]}}$ = [$\ce{PCl5}$]

- B.$\hspace{1em}[\ce{PCl3}$] = $\frac{1}{2}$[$\ce{PCl5}$]

- C.$\hspace{1em}[\ce{PCl3}$] = 2[$\ce{PCl5}$]

- D.$\hspace{1em}[\ce{PCl3}$] = $\sqrt{\ce{[PCl5]}}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

The equilibrium constant expression is:

$K$ = $\dfrac{[\ce{PCl5}]}{[\ce{PCl3}][\ce{Cl2}]}$. 

At equilibrium, [$\ce{PCl3}$] = [$\ce{Cl2}$], so

$K = \dfrac{[\ce{PCl5}]}{[\ce{PCl3}]^2} = 1$

$[\ce{PCl3}]^2$ = [$\ce{PCl5}]$\

$[\ce{PCl3}] = \sqrt{\ce{[PCl5]}}$
