# CH2372

Which row correctly shows the effect of increasing temperature on the following equilibrium?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{H_2O}\textrm{(l)} \rightleftharpoons \ce{H^{+}}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{OH^{-}}\textrm{(aq)}$ $\hspace{3em}\Delta H > 0$

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

| $\hspace{1.em}$||| $\mathbf{K}$ | pH |
|:-: |:-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
|| A. | | Decrease | Increase |
|| B. | | Decrease | Decrease |
|| C. | | Increase | Increase |
|| D. | | Increase | Decrease |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The forward reaction is endothermic ($\Delta H > 0$). Increasing the temperature favours the endothermic direction, shifting the equilibrium to the right. This increases the equilibrium constant.

A shift to the right produces more $\ce{H^{+}}$ and $\ce{OH^{-}}$. Since $[\ce{H^{+}}]$ increases, $\textrm{pH} = -\log[\ce{H^{+}}]$ decreases.

Note that although the pH is lower than 7, the solution remains neutral because $[\ce{H^{+}}] = [\ce{OH^{-}}]$ at all times.
