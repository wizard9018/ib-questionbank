# CH2370

Which statement is correct about the effect of adding a catalyst to a system at equilibrium at constant temperature?

- A.$\hspace{1.em}$It changes the equilibrium constant.

- B.$\hspace{1.em}$It shifts the position of equilibrium.

- C.$\hspace{1.em}$It lowers the activation energy of the forward reaction only.

- D.$\hspace{1.em}$It reduces the time taken to reach equilibrium.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
A catalyst provides an alternative reaction pathway with a lower activation energy. It lowers the activation energy of both the forward and reverse reactions by the same amount, so both rates increase equally. The system reaches equilibrium more quickly, but the position of equilibrium is unchanged.
