# CH0884

Iron (II) and permanganate ions react as follows in a redox titration:

$\ce{MnO4^-(aq) + 5 Fe^2+(aq) + 8 H+(aq) -> 5 Fe^3+(aq) + Mn^2+(aq) + 4 H2O(l)}$

What is the concentration of the potassium permanganate solution if 25.00 cm$^3$ of 0.200 mol dm$^{-3}$ iron (II) nitrate solution was titrated against $\ce{KMnO4}$ solution and 20.00 cm$^3$ of $\ce{KMnO4}$ solution was needed to reach the equivalence point?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$0.005 mol dm$^{-3}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$0.05 mol dm$^{-3}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$0.00500 mol dm$^{-3}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$0.0500 mol dm$^{-3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\
First, calculate the moles of iron (II) ions using the relationship $n=CV.$ The volume of the solution must be expressed in $\textrm{dm}^3.$ The moles of iron(II) ions will be equal to the moles of iron (II) nitrate.

$n_{\ce{Fe^{2+}}} = 0.02500 \textrm{\ dm}^3\times 0.200\,\textrm{mol dm}^{-3} = 5.00\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}$

Using the balanced chemical equation, there are 5 moles of iron (II) ions for every 1 mole of permanganate:

$n_{\ce{MnO_4^-}} = \dfrac{1 \textrm{\ mol\ }\ce{MnO_4^-}}{5 \textrm{\ mol\ }\ce{Fe^{2+}}} \times n_{\ce{Fe^{2+}}} = 1.00\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}$

The concentration of $\ce{KMnO4}$ is the moles of permanganate ion divided by the volume of the solution in $\textrm{\ dm}^3.$

$C{_\ce{KMnO4}}= \dfrac{1.00\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}}{0.02000\,\text{dm}^{-3}} = 0.0500\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}$

The answer should be reported to three significant figures as the data is all available to that precision.
