# CH0990

When balanced, what is the sum of the coefficients for the equation below with the lowest whole-number ratio?

$\ce{\_\_\_ NO2(g) + \_\_\_ H2O(l) -> \_\_\_ HNO3(aq) + \_\_\_ NO(g)}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$4

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$6

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$7

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$5

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Students must balance the chemical equation and add the coefficients.

$3 \ce{\,NO2}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{H2O}\textrm{(l)} \longrightarrow 2 \ce{\,HNO3}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{NO}\textrm{(g)}$

Sum of coefficients: $3+1+2+1 = 7$
