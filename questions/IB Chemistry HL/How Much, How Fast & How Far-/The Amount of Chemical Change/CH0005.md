# CH0005

What is the sum of the coefficients when the equation is balanced with whole numbers?

$\ce{\underline{\hspace{1em}}\,C_3H_7OH(l) + \underline{\hspace{1em}}\,O_2(g) -> \underline{\hspace{1em}}\,CO_2(g) + \underline{\hspace{1em}}\,H_2O(l)}$

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $12$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $13$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $24$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $25$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
The balanced chemical equation is $\ce{2C_3H_7OH(l) + 9O_2(g) -> 6CO_2(g) + 8H_2O(l)}$. The sum of the coefficients is $25$.
