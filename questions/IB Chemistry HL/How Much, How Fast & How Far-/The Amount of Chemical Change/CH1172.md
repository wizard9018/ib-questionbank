# CH1172

Consider the reaction below occurring between copper and nitric acid:

$\ce{Cu(s) + 4 HNO3(aq) -> Cu(NO3)2(aq) + 2 NO2(g) + 2 H2O(l)}$

-   | | Value |
    | :-: | :-: |
    | Amount $\ce{Cu}$ | 1.50 mol |
    | Amount $\ce{HNO3}$ | 7.00 mol |
    | Gas constant | 8.31 $\text{J K}^{-1}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$ |
    | Temperature| 298 K |
    | Pressure | 100 kPa |

What is the volume of NO$_2$(g) produced, in $dm{^3}?$

*(refer to the data given in the table above)*

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{3.50\times 8.31\times 298}{100\times 1.5}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{100\times 2}{3.00\times 8.31\times 298}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{3.50\times 8.31\times 298}{100000}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\dfrac{3.00\times 8.31\times 298}{100}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

We must compare the amounts to identify the limiting reagent.

$\begin{aligned}
\text{Amount of Cu} &= 1.50\,\text{mol} \\
\text{Amount of } \ce{HNO3} &= 7.00\,\text{mol}
\end{aligned}$

With a ratio of 1:4, Cu is the limiting reagent

The amount of $\ce{NO2}$ formed with a ratio of 1 : 2 is:

Amount of $\e{NO2} = 2\times 1.50\,\text{mol} = 3.00\,\text{mol}$

We use the ideal gas equation to determine the volume.

$\begin{aligned}
PV &= nRT \\
V &= \dfrac{nRT}{P} \\
&= \dfrac{3.00\,\text{mol}\times 8.31\,\text{J K}^{-1}\,\text{mol}^{-1}\times 298\,\text{K}}{100\,\text{kPa}}
\end{aligned}$
