# CH0665

36.0 cm$^3$ of a 1.00 mol dm$^{-3}$ solution of copper (II) nitrate reacts with 0.540 g of aluminum metal.

How many moles of copper metal would be produced in the following reaction?

$\ \newline \ $

$\ce{3 Cu(NO3)2 + 2 Al -> 2 Al(NO3)3 + 3 Cu}$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$0.0360 moles

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$0.0300 moles

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$0.0200 moles

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$0.0133 moles

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Find the moles of each reactant:

$\ce{Cu(NO3)2}$:

$n = C \times V = 1.00\,\textrm{mol dm}^{-3}\times 0.036\,\text{dm}^3 = 0.036\,\text{mol }\ce{Cu(NO3)2}$

$\ce{Al}$:

$n = \dfrac{m}{M} = \dfrac{0.540\,\text{g}}{27\,\text{g mol}^{-1}} = 0.020\,\text{mol Al}$

Determine the limiting reactant by finding the moles of copper(II) nitrate required to react with the aluminium:

$0.020\,\text{mol Al}\times \dfrac{3\,\text{mol }\ce{Cu(NO3)2}}{2\,\text{mol  Al}} = 0.030\,\text{mol }\ce{Cu(NO3)2}$

Because $0.036\,\text{mol }\ce{Cu(NO3)2} > 0.030\,\text{mol }\ce{Cu(NO3)2}$, Al is the limiting reactant.

Therefore, the yield of copper can be deduced:

$0.020\,\text{mol Al}\times \dfrac{3\,\text{mol Cu}}{2\,\text{mol Al}} = 0.030\,\text{mol Cu}$ 

The theoretical yield of copper is 0.030 mol Cu.
