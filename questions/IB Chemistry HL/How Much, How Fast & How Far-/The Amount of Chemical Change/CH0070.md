# CH0070

**Maximum mark: 5**

Sodium azide is an ionic compound found within the airbags of some older cars. The airbag is deployed as a safety feature in the event of an accident. In this application, the sodium azide decomposes rapidly, and the gas formed inflates the airbag.

The decomposition of sodium azide under these conditions is given below.

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{2NaN3(s)-> 2Na(s) + 3N2(g)}$

1. Suggest the formula and charge for the azide ion. [1 mark]

2. A typical airbag requires $65.0\,\text{dm}^{3}$ of nitrogen gas for complete inflation at STP. Using section $2$ of the data booklet, calculate the mass of sodium azide that the airbag must contain for this purpose. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The ion azide is $\ce{N3^-}$ $\checkmark$\
$\,{Award \ 0 \ if \ charge \ is\ (3-)}$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    $\ce{Na}$ is in group 1; therefore, the ion formed is $\ce{Na^1+}.$ The charges of the ions must cancel out to give an overall neutral compound.

2. $n(\ce{N2}) = \dfrac{V}{\textit{molar volume}}=\dfrac{65.0\,\text{dm}^{3}}{22.7\,\text{dm}^{3}\,\text{mol}^{-1}} = 2.86\,\text{mol}\,\checkmark$

    Mole ratio 2:3 applied for number of moles of sodium azide $n(\ce{NaN3}) = (2.86) \left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)= 1.91\,\text{mol}\,\checkmark$

    Molar mass for sodium azide calculated = $65.02\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}\,\checkmark$

    $m(\ce{NaN3}) = n\times M = (1.91\,\text{mol})(65.02\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}) = 124\text{g}$ (3 sf)$\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    The number of moles of gas at STP is calculated as volume divided by molar gas volume $(22.7\,\text{dm}^3\text{mol}^{-1})$

    $M(\ce{NaN3}) = 22.99 + (14.01)(3) = 65.02\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$
