# CH0661

If 1.20 g of magnesium metal reacts with excess hydrobromic acid, what volume of hydrogen gas is evolved at STP, given the molar volume at STP is 22.4 dm$^3$?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$0.224 dm$^3$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$2.24 dm$^3$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$1.12 dm$^3$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$4.48 dm$^3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$V = n \times V_m= \dfrac{1.20\,\text{g}}{24\,\textrm{g mol}^{-1}}\times 22.4\,\text{dm}^3\,\text{mol}^{-1} = 1.12\,\text{dm}^3$
