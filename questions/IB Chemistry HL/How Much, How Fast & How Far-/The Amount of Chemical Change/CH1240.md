# CH1240

Propan$-$2$-$ol, also known as rubbing alcohol, is used as a cleaner. Its synthesis is described below.

$\ce{C3H6 + H2O ->[H2SO4] C3H8O}$

What is the atom economy for the synthesis of propan$-$2$-$ol?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$100%

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$70%

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$38%

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$61%

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The atom economy measures the proportion of the reactant atoms that end up in the desired product:

$\textrm{atom economy} = \dfrac{\textrm{molar mass of desired product}}{\textrm{total molar mass of reactants}} \times 100\%$

This is an addition reaction: the two reactants combine to form a single product, so every atom of $\ce{C3H6}$ and $\ce{H2O}$ is incorporated into the propan$-$2$-$ol.

$\textrm{atom economy} = \dfrac{60.11}{42.09 + 18.02} \times 100\% = 100\%$

$\ce{H2SO4}$ is a catalyst. It is regenerated during the reaction and is excluded from the calculation; including it gives the incorrect value of 38%. Addition reactions always have an atom economy of 100%, which is why they are favoured in green chemistry.
