# CH0236

**Maximum mark: 6**

20.0 cm$^3$ of 0.100 mol dm$^{-3}\, \ce{NaOH}$ were slowly added to a 10.0 cm$^3$ sample of 0.100 mol dm$^{-3}\, \ce{HCl}$, and the $\ce{pH}$ was measured throughout the addition.

The equation for the reaction is as follows:

$\ce{NaOH(aq)  + HCl(aq) -> NaCl(aq) + H2O(l)}$

The ionic product constant of water at $25.0\,{}^\circ \mathrm{C}$ is $1.00 \times 10^{-14}.$

1.  Calculate the number of moles of $\ce{NaOH}$ remaining at the end of the addition. [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the concentration of OH$^-$ at the end of the addition. [2 marks]

3.  Using your value for $\ce{[OH-]}$ in (b), calculate the $\ce{pH}$ of the solution at the end of the addition. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $n(\ce{NaOH})  = C\times V = (0.100\,\text{mol dm}^{-3})(20.0\times 10^{-3}\,\text{dm}^3) = 2.00\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}$

    $n(\ce{HCl})  = C\times V =  (0.100\,\text{mol dm}^{-3})(10.0\times 10^{-3}\,\text{dm}^3) = 1.00\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}\,\checkmark$

    Mole ratio is $1:1$ and $\ce{NaOH}$ is in excess by $1.00\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}\, \checkmark$

2.  $n(\ce{NaOH}) = n(\ce{OH-}) = 1.00 \times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}$

    Volume of solution   $V_{total} = 20.0 + 10.0 = 30.0\,\text{cm}^3\,\checkmark$

    $\ce{[OH-]} = \dfrac{n}{V} = \dfrac{1.00\times 10^{-3}}{30.0\times 10^{-3}} = 0.0333\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    The total volume of $\ce{NaOH + HCl}$ must be considered when calculating the new concentration.

    NaOH is a strong base that dissociates completely in water:  $\ce{NaOH(aq) -> Na+(aq) + OH-(aq)}$

3.  $\begin{aligned}
    \\
    \ce{[H+]} &= \dfrac{1\times 10^{-14}}{0.0333} =  3.00\times 10^{-13}\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\,\checkmark \\
    \textrm{pH} &= -\log\ce{[H+]} = 12.5\,\checkmark  \\
    \end{aligned}$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    $K_w = \ce{[H+][OH-]}$
