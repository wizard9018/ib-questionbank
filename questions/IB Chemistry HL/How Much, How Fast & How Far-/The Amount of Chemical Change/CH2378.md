# CH2378

What volume of oxygen $(\ce{O_2})$ at STP is needed to completely react with $34.1\textrm{\ dm}^3$ of ethene $(\ce{C_2H_4})$? \
*Assume all gases are ideal gases.*

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{C_2H_4}\textrm{(g)} + 3\ce{\,O_2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,CO_2}\textrm{(g)} + 2\ce{\,H_2O}\textrm{(l)}$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}68.2\textrm{\ dm}^3$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}102.3\textrm{\ dm}^3$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}34.1\textrm{\ dm}^3$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}11.4\textrm{\ dm}^3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
For ideal gases at the same temperature and pressure, equal amounts (in moles) occupy equal volumes. This means the mole ratio in a balanced equation is also the volume ratio for any gaseous species.

From the balanced equation, $1\textrm{\ mol } \ce{C_2H_4}$ reacts with $3\textrm{\ mol } \ce{O_2}.$ Reading this as volumes:

$1\textrm{\ volume } \ce{C_2H_4}$ reacts with $3\textrm{\ volumes } \ce{O_2}$

So:

$V(\ce{O_2}) = 34.1 \textrm{\ dm}^3 \times \dfrac{3}{1} = 102.3\textrm{\ dm}^3$
