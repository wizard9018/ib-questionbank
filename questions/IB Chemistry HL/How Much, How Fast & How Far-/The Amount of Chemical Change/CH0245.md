# CH0245

A solution of $\ce{Ba(OH)2}$ of unknown concentration was titrated against $25.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3$ of $0.2 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ solution of ethanoic acid, $\ce{CH3COOH}$ using phenolphthalein indicator. The mean volume of titre was 12.5 cm$^3$. The same experiment was conducted with $25.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3$ of $0.2 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ nitric acid, $\ce{HNO3}.$ In this case, which of the following is the most likely value for the mean volume of titre?

- A.$\hspace{1em}12.5  \textrm{\ cm}^3$

- B.$\hspace{1em}10.0  \textrm{\ cm}^3$

- C.$\hspace{1em}25.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3$

- D.$\hspace{1em}15.0  \textrm{\ cm}^3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Both ethanoic acid and nitric acid are monoprotic, so each reacts with $\ce{Ba(OH)2}$ in a $2 : 1$ mole ratio:

$\ce{2HA}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{Ba(OH)2}\textrm{(aq)} \rightarrow \ce{Ba(A)2}\textrm{(aq)} + 2\ce{H2O}\textrm{(l)}$

Both acid solutions have the same concentration ($0.2 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$) and volume ($25.0 \textrm{\ cm}^3$), so the moles of acid are identical. Since the stoichiometry is also identical, the same volume of $\ce{Ba(OH)2}$ is required to reach the equivalence point: $12.5 \textrm{\ cm}^3.$

Whether the acid is strong or weak affects the pH at the equivalence point and the choice of indicator, but it does not affect the volume of base needed to neutralise a given amount of acid.
