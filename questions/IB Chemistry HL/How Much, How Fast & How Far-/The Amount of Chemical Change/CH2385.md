# CH2385

What is the atom economy for producing ethanol by fermentation of glucose?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{C_6H_{12}O_6}\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,C_2H_5OH}\textrm{(aq)} + 2\ce{\,CO_2}\textrm{(g)}$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}100\%$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}25\%$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}51\%$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}75\%$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Atom economy measures what fraction of the total reactant mass ends up in the desired product:

$\textrm{atom economy} = \dfrac{M \textrm{\ of desired product(s)}}{M \textrm{\ of all reactants}} \times 100$

The desired product is ethanol, $\ce{C_2H_5OH}.$ Using $A_r$: C $= 12,$ H $= 1,$ O $= 16$:

$M(\ce{C_2H_5OH}) = (2 \times 12) + (6 \times 1) + 16 = 46\textrm{\ g mol}^{-1}$

Two moles of ethanol are produced, so the mass of desired product is:

$2 \times 46 = 92\textrm{\ g}$

The molar mass of the reactant glucose is:

$M(\ce{C_6H_{12}O_6}) = (6 \times 12) + (12 \times 1) + (6 \times 16) = 180\textrm{\ g mol}^{-1}$

$\textrm{atom economy} = \dfrac{92}{180} \times 100 = 51\%$
