# CH1970

Hydrogen can be synthesised from methane as shown below.

$\ce{CH4}\textrm{(g)} + 2\ce{\,H2O}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 4\ce{\,H2}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{CO2}\textrm{(g)}$

What is the approximate atom economy for the production of hydrogen?

-    A.$\hspace{1.em}4\%$

-    B.$\hspace{1.em}8\%$

-    C.$\hspace{1.em}15\%$

-    D.$\hspace{1.em}50\%$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The student must calculate the atom economy for the production of hydrogen using approximate molar masses.

$\textrm{atom economy} = \dfrac{\textrm{molar mass of desired product}}{\textrm{total molar mass of products}} \times 100$

Using approximate molar masses from the data booklet:

$\textrm{atom economy} = \dfrac{4 \times 2}{(4 \times 2) + 44} \times 100 = \dfrac{8}{52} \times 100 \approx 15\%$

This question is categorised as medium, as the student must recall the atom economy formula and apply it without a calculator.
