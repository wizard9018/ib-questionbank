# CH1238

$18.8\,\text{g}$ of solid copper(II) nitrate $(M_r = 188)$ decomposes as shown below.

$\ce{2 Cu(NO3)2(s) -> 2 CuO(s) + 4 NO2(g) + O2(g)}$

Which statement is correct at STP?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}3.99\,\text{g}$ of CuO and $4.55\,\text{dm}^3$ of gas should be produced

-   B. $\hspace{1em}3.99\,\text{g}$ of CuO and $5.68\,\text{dm}^3$ of gas should be produced

-   C. $\hspace{1em}7.96\,\text{g}$ of CuO and $4.55\,\text{dm}^3$ of gas should be produced

-   D. $\hspace{1em}7.96\,\text{g}$ of CuO and $5.68\,\text{dm}^3$ of gas should be produced

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The amount of $\ce{Cu(NO3)2}$ is calculated as shown below.

$Amount = \dfrac{\text{mass}}{\text{molar mass}} = \dfrac{18.8\,\text{g}}{187.57\,\text{g mol}^{-1}} = 0.100\,\text{mol}$

For the balanced chemical equation, the amount of CuO formed is the same as the amount of $\ce{Cu(NO3)2},$ $0.100\,\text{mol}$ of CuO are formed. We can deduce the mass of CuO as shown below.

$\text{mass} = \text{molar mass}\times \text{moles} = (79.55\,\text{g mol}^{-1})(0.100\,\text{mol}) = 7.96\,\text{g}$

:u[Method 1]:

Calculate the total amount of moles of gas produced and deduce its total volume

From the balanced equation, the amount of gas produced should be $5\,\text{mol }(4\,\text{mol}$ of $\ce{NO2}$ and $1\,\text{mol}$ of $\ce{O2})$ for every $2\,\text{mol}$ of solid decomposed. The amount can be calculated as shown below.

$\text{Amount of gas} = (0.100\,\text{mol})(\dfrac{5}{2}) = 0.250\,\text{mol}$

The volume of a gas at $STP\, (273.15\,\text{K}$ and $100\,\text{kPa})$ can be deduced from the ideal gas law to be $22.7\,\text{dm}^3.$ Then the volume of gas can be calculated as shown below.

$V = (0.250\,\text{mol})(22.7\,\text{dm}^3{mol}^{-1}) = 5.68\,\text{dm}^3$

:u[Method 2]:
- Calculate the amount of moles of each of the gases produced;
- Calculate the volume of each of the gases produced
- Deduce total volume

From the balanced equation, we can deduce the amount of gas produced.

$\text{Amount of nitrogen dioxide} = (0.100\,\text{mol})(\dfrac{4}{2}) = 0.200\,\text{mol}$

$\text{Amount of oxygen} = (0.100\,\text{mol})(\dfrac{1}{2}) = 0.0500\,\text{mol}$

The volume of a gas at $STP\, (273.15\,\text{K}$ and $100\,\text{kPa})$ can be calculated as per below:

$\text{Volume of nitrogen dioxide} = (0.200\,\text{mol})(22.7\,\text{dm}^3\,\text{mol}^{-1}) = 4.54\,\text{dm}^3$

$\text{Volume of oxygen} = (0.0500\,\text{mol})(22.7\,\text{dm}^3\,\text{mol}^{-1}) = 1.135\,\text{dm}^3$

$\text{Total volume} = \text{Volume of nitrogen dioxide} + \text{volume of oxygen} = (4.54 + 1.135)\,\text{dm}^3 = 5.675\,\text{dm}^3 = 5.68\,\text{dm}^3$
