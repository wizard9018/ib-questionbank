# CH0673

**Maximum mark: 4**

Liquid butanol, $\ce{C4H9OH(l)}$, combusts in oxygen to produce $\ce{CO2(g)}$ and $\ce{H2O(g)}$.

1.  Write a balanced equation, including state symbols, for this reaction. [1 mark]

2.  When 1.250 cm$^3$ of butanol is combusted in excess oxygen, 1.921 dm$^3$ of water vapor is collected at a pressure of 125.0 kPa and 150.0 $^\circ$C. Calculate the moles of butanol that were combusted. [2 marks]

3.  Determine the density of liquid butanol in g cm$^{-3}$. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\ce{C4H9OH(l) + 6 O2(g) -> 4 CO2(g) + 5 H2O(g)}\,\checkmark$

    *States of matter required.*

2.  $n_\ce{H2O} = \dfrac{PV}{RT} = \dfrac{(125\,\text{kPa})(1.921\,\text{dm}^3)}{(8.31\,\text{J mol}^{-1}\,\text{K}^{-1})(423\,\text{K})} = 0.06831\,\text{mol }\ce{H2O}\,\checkmark$

    $n_{butanol} = 0.06831\,\text{mol }\ce{H2O}\times \dfrac{1\,\text{mol }\ce{C4H9OH}}{5\,\text{mol }\ce{H2O}} = 0.01366\,\text{mol }\ce{C4H9OH}\,\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    Use the ideal gas law to determine moles of water:

    $n_{\ce{H2O}}$ = $\dfrac{PV}{RT}$

    Use the moles of water found and the ratio from the balanced equation to determine the moles of butanol.

    $n_{butanol} = n_\ce{H2O}\times \dfrac{1\,\text{mol }\ce{C4H9OH}}{5\text{mol }\ce{H2O}}$

    Recall that 1 J = 1 kPa dm$^3$ = 1 Pa m$^3$

    Convert temperature to K by adding 273.

3.  $m_{butanol} = 0.01366\,\text{mol }\ce{C4H9OH}\times \dfrac{74.14\,\text{g }\ce{C4H9OH}}{1\,\text{mol }\ce{C4H9OH}} = 1.013\,\text{g }\ce{C4H9OH}$

    $d = \dfrac{1.013\,\text{g}}{1.250\,\text{cm}^3} = 0.8103\,\text{g cm}^{-3}\,\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    $m = n \times M$;

    Use the moles of butanol and the molar mass to find the mass of butanol.

    Then determine the density using $d = \dfrac{m}{V}$;

    $d(\ce{C4H9OH}) = \dfrac{m\ce{(C_4H_9OH)}}{V\ce{(C_4H_9OH)}}$
