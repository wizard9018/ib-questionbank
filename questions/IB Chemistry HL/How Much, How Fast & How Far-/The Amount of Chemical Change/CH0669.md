# CH0669

Propane gas is combusted in oxygen gas according to the following equation:

$\ce{C3H8(g) + 5 O2(g) -> 3 CO2(g) + 4 H2O(g)}$ 

When 11.0 g of propane gas $(M_r \approx 44)$ is combusted in 8.0 g of oxygen gas, 0.20 moles of water vapor is produced.

What is the excess reactant and how many moles remain after the reaction is complete?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{O2}$, 0.20 moles

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{C3H8}$, 0.20 moles

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{O2}$, 0.25 moles

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{C3H8}$, 0.25 moles

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Determine the Limiting Reagent: 

$\ce{C3H8}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dfrac{11.0\,\text{g}}{44\,\text{g mol}^{-1}} = 0.25\,\text{mol}$

                                    

$\ce{O2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dfrac{8.0\,\text{g}}{32\,\text{g mol}^{-1}} = 0.25\,\text{mol}$

$\dfrac{0.25}{5} < \dfrac{0.25}{1}$ 

$\ce{O2}$ is the limiting reagent, $\ce{C3H8}$ is in excess.

$0.25\,\text{mol }\ce{O2} \times \dfrac{1 \textrm{\ mol } \ce{C3H8}}{5 \textrm{\ mol } \ce{O2}} = 0.05\,\text{mol }\ce{C3H8}$

$0.25 - 0.05 = 0.20\ \text{mol remain}$
