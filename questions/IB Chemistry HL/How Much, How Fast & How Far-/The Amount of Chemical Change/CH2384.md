# CH2384

What total volume of gas is present after $1.12\textrm{\ dm}^3$ of oxygen gas reacts with $3.00\textrm{\ dm}^3$ of nitrogen monoxide at STP?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$2\ce{\,NO}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{O_2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,NO_2}\textrm{(g)}$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}0.76\textrm{\ dm}^3$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}2.24\textrm{\ dm}^3$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}3.00\textrm{\ dm}^3$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}4.12\textrm{\ dm}^3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
For gases at the same temperature and pressure, the mole ratio equals the volume ratio. This question also requires identifying the limiting reactant and accounting for any excess.

The equation shows $2\textrm{\ vol } \ce{NO}$ reacts with $1\textrm{\ vol } \ce{O_2}.$ For $1.12\textrm{\ dm}^3$ of $\ce{O_2}$ the volume of $\ce{NO}$ required is:

$1.12\textrm{\ dm}^3 \ce{\ O_2} \times \dfrac{2\textrm{\ dm}^3 \ce{\ NO}}{1\textrm{\ dm}^3 \ce{\ O_2}} = 2.24\textrm{\ dm}^3 \ce{\ NO reacted}$

Since $3.00\textrm{\ dm}^3$ of $\ce{NO}$ is available, $\ce{O_2}$ is the limiting reactant. The excess $\ce{NO}$ is:

$3.00 \textrm{\ dm}^3 - 2.24 \textrm{\ dm}^3  = 0.76\textrm{\ dm}^3 \ce{\ NO unreacted}$

The volume of $\ce{NO_2}$ produced equals the volume of $\ce{NO}$ that reacted (2:2 ratio):

$2.24\textrm{\ dm}^3 \ce{\ NO} \times \dfrac{2\textrm{\ dm}^3 \ce{\ NO2}}{2\textrm{\ dm}^3 \ce{\ NO}} = 2.24\textrm{\ dm}^3 \ce{\ NO_2 \textrm{\ product}}$

The total volume of gas after the reaction is the sum of the product and unreacted excess:

$0.76 \textrm{\ dm}^3 \ce{\ NO} + 2.24 \textrm{\ dm}^3 \ce{\ NO2} = 3.00\textrm{\ dm}^3 \textrm{\ total}$
