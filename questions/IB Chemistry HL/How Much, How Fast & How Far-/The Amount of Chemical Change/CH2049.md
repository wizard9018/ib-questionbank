# CH2049

A $25.00\text{ cm}^3$ sample of monoprotic strong acid is titrated with $10.00\text{ cm}^3$ of $0.75\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$ NaOH to reach the equivalence point. What is the concentration of the unknown acid?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}0.75\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}0.30\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}0.0075\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}1.88\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The equivalence point of a strong acid/strong base titration occurs when the moles of the titrant (in this case, NaOH) equals the moles of the unknown acid. It is at this point that the reactants are neutralized and the pH is equal to 7. To determine the concentration of the unknown acid, the moles of the base are first determined using the concentration and titre volume using the formula $n=CV:$

$n_\ce{OH-} = (0.75\text{ mol dm}^{-3})(10.00\text{ cm}^3)\left(\dfrac{1\text{ dm}^3}{1000\text{ cm}^3}\right)$

$n_\ce{OH-} = 0.0075\text{ mol}$

Therefore, there were $0.0075\text{ mol}$ of unknown acid in the flask. To determine the concentration, the following calculation is performed using the relationship $C = \dfrac{n}{V}:$

$C = \left(\dfrac{0.0075\text{ mol}}{25.00\text{ cm}^3}\right)\left(\dfrac{1000\text{ cm}^3}{1\text{ dm}^3}\right) = 0.30\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$
