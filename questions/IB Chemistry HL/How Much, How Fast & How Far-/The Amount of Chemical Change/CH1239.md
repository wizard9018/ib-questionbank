# CH1239

$100\,\text{cm}^3$ of $0.100\,\text{mol dm}^{-3}\ \ce{H2SO4(aq)}$ were mixed with $0.150\,\text{g}$ of solid NaOH.

The chemical reaction is shown below.

$\ce{H2SO4(aq) + 2 NaOH(s) -> Na2SO4(aq) + 2 H2O(l)}$

The salt was separated from the mixture and weighed. A mass of $0.258\,\text{g}$ of sodium sulfate was registered.
Which statement below is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The limiting reagent is sulfuric acid, and the percentage yield is 96%

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The limiting reagent is sodium hydroxide, and the percentage yield is 96%

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The limiting reagent is sulfuric acid, and the percentage yield is 18%

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The limiting reagent is sodium hydroxide, and the percentage yield is 18%

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The amount of salt formed is:

$ n = \dfrac{\text{mass}}{\text{molar mass}} = \dfrac{0.258\,\text{g}}{142.05\,\text{g mol}^{-1}} = 1.81\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}$

The amount of each reactant.

$n_{sulfuric\ acid} = C\times V = (0.100\,\text{mol dm}^{-3})(0.100\,\text{dm}^3) = 0.0100\,\text{mol}$

$n_{sodium\ hydroxide} = \dfrac{\text{mass}}{\text{molar mass}} = \dfrac{0.150\,\text{g}}{40.00\,\text{g mol}^{-1}} = 3.75\times {10}^{-3}\,\text{mol}$

The limiting reagent:

$n_{sulfuric\ acid} = 0.0100\,\text{mol} \longrightarrow n_{sodium\ sulfate} = 0.0100\,\text{mol}$

$n_{sodium\ hydroxide} = 3.75\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol} \longrightarrow n_{sodium\ sulfate} = \dfrac{3.75\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}}{2} = 1.88\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}$

Sodium hydroxide is the limiting reagent.

The student must compare the theoretical amount of sodium sulfate obtained with the experimental amount.

$\text{Percentage yield} = \left(\dfrac{\text{experimental amount}}{\text{theoretical amount}}\right)(100) =  \left(\dfrac{1.81\times {10}^{-3}\,\text{mol}}{1.88\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}}\right)(100) = 96\%$

This question is categorized as hard as students must determine the limiting reagent and the percentage yield while applying a series of calculations.
