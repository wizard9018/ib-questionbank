# CH2377

What mass of aluminium oxide $(\ce{Al_2O_3})$ is produced when $10.8\textrm{\ g}$ of aluminium $(\ce{Al})$ reacts with excess oxygen? $(M_r \textrm{ of } \ce{Al} = 27)$

$4\ce{\,Al}\textrm{(s)} + 3\ce{\,O_2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,Al_2O_3}\textrm{(s)}$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}40.8\textrm{\ g}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}20.4\textrm{\ g}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}10.2\textrm{\ g}$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}5.40\textrm{\ g}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The approach for any mass$-$to$-$mass stoichiometry problem is

mass $\longrightarrow$ moles $\longrightarrow$ mole ratio $\longrightarrow$ moles $\longrightarrow$ mass.

Convert the given mass to moles using the molar mass of $\ce{Al}$:

$n(\ce{Al}) = \dfrac{10.8}{27} = 0.400\textrm{ mol Al}$

Apply the mole ratio from the balanced equation. The coefficients show $4\textrm{ mol } \ce{Al} : 2\textrm{ mol } \ce{Al_2O_3},$ which simplifies to $2 : 1.$ So:

$n(\ce{Al_2O_3}) = 0.400 \textrm{\ mol Al} \times \dfrac{2 \textrm{\ mol\ } \ce{Al2O3}}{4 \textrm{\ mol Al}} = 0.200\textrm{ mol\ }\ce{Al2O3}$

Convert moles of product to mass. The molar mass of $\ce{Al_2O_3}$ is $(2 \times 27) + (3 \times 16) = 102\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}.$

$m(\ce{Al_2O_3}) = 0.200 \textrm{\ mol\ }\ce{Al2O3} \times 102 \textrm{\ g mol}^{-1} = 20.4\textrm{\ g}$
