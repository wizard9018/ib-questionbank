# CH0670

Aluminum hydrogen carbonate will decompose to produce aluminum oxide, carbon dioxide gas, and water according to the following equation:

$\ce{2 Al(HCO3)3(s) -> Al2O3(s) + 6 CO2(g) + 3 H_2O(l)}$

If 0.300 moles of aluminum hydrogen carbonate $(M_r \approx 210)$ decomposes and 10.2 g of aluminum oxide $(M_r \approx 102)$ is collected, what is the percent yield?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}67.0\%$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}33.0\%$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}75.0\%$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}133\%$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Determine the theoretical yield of $\ce{Al2O3}$ using the mole ratio from the balanced chemical equation:

$0.300\,\text{mol }\ce{Al(HCO3)3}\times \dfrac{1\,\text{mol }\ce{Al2O3}}{2\,\text{mol }\ce{Al(HCO3)3}} = 0.150\,\text{mol }\ce{Al2O3}$

Determine experimental yield of $\ce{Al2O3}$ in moles using the relationship $n = \dfrac{m}{M}$.

$\dfrac{10.2\,\text{g }\ce{Al2O3}}{102\,\text{g mol}^{-1}} = 0.100\,\text{mol }\ce{Al2O3}$

Take the ratio to find the percent yield:

$\dfrac{0.100\,\text{mol}}{0.150\,\text{mol}}\times 100\% = 67\%$
