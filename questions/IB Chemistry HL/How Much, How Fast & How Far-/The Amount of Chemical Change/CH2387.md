# CH2387

Hydrochloric acid reacts with calcium carbonate to produce carbon dioxide gas. What volume of $\ce{CO_2}$ at STP is produced when $50.0\textrm{\ cm}^3$ of $0.800\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ $\ce{HCl}$ is used?

The molar volume of a gas at STP is $22.7 \textrm{\ dm}^{3}\textrm{\,mol}^{-1}.$

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{CaCO_3}\textrm{(s)} + 2\ce{\,HCl}\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow \ce{CaCl_2}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{CO_2}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{H_2O}\textrm{(l)}$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}0.227\textrm{\ dm}^3$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}0.454\textrm{\ dm}^3$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}0.908\textrm{\ dm}^3$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}1.82\textrm{\ dm}^3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The solution follows the path:

concentration $\times$ volume $\longrightarrow$ moles $\longrightarrow$ mole ratio $\longrightarrow$ moles $\longrightarrow$ gas volume.

Find the moles of $\ce{HCl}$:

$n(\ce{HCl}) = 0.800\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \times 0.0500\textrm{\ dm}^3 = 0.0400\textrm{\ mol HCl}$

Apply the mole ratio. The equation shows $2\textrm{\ mol } \ce{HCl}$ produces $1\textrm{\ mol } \ce{CO_2}$:

$n(\ce{CO_2}) = 0.0400\textrm{\ mol HCl} \times \dfrac{1\textrm{\ mol } \ce{CO_2}}{2\textrm{\ mol HCl}} = 0.0200\textrm{\ mol } \ce{CO_2}$

Convert moles to volume using the molar volume at STP:

$V(\ce{CO_2}) = 0.0200\textrm{\ mol} \times 22.7\textrm{\ dm}^3\textrm{\ mol}^{-1} = 0.454\textrm{\ dm}^3$
