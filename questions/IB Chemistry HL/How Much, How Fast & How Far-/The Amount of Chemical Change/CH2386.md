# CH2386

$25.0\textrm{\ cm}^3$ of $0.0800\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ lead(II) nitrate solution is mixed with $25.0\textrm{\ cm}^3$ of $0.200\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ sodium chloride solution.

$\ce{Pb(NO_3)_2}\textrm{(aq)} + 2\ce{\,NaCl}\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow \ce{PbCl_2}\textrm{(s)} + 2\ce{\,NaNO_3}\textrm{(aq)}$

What is the concentration of chloride ions remaining in solution?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}0.0200\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}0.0400\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}0.0800\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}0.100\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
This question requires finding the excess chloride ions after a precipitation reaction, then dividing by the total combined volume.

Find the moles of each reactant:

$n(\ce{Pb(NO_3)_2}) = 0.0800\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \times 0.0250\textrm{\ dm}^3 = 2.00 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{\ mol Pb(NO}_3)_2$

$n(\ce{NaCl}) = 0.200\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \times 0.0250\textrm{\ dm}^3 = 5.00 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{\ mol NaCl}$

The equation shows $1\textrm{\ mol } \ce{Pb(NO_3)_2}$ reacts with $2\textrm{\ mol } \ce{NaCl}.$ The $\ce{NaCl}$ required is:

$2.00 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{\ mol Pb(NO}_3)_2 \times \dfrac{2\textrm{\ mol NaCl}}{1\textrm{\ mol Pb(NO}_3)_2} = 4.00 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{\ mol NaCl}$

Since $5.00 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{\ mol}$ is available, $\ce{NaCl}$ is in excess. Each $\ce{NaCl}$ provides one $\ce{Cl^-},$ so:

$n(\ce{Cl^-})_{\textrm{excess}} = 5.00 \times 10^{-3} - 4.00 \times 10^{-3} = 1.00 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{\ mol } \ce{Cl^-}$

The total volume of the combined solution is:

$V_{\textrm{total}} = 25.0\textrm{\ cm}^3 + 25.0\textrm{\ cm}^3 = 50.0\textrm{\ cm}^3 = 0.0500\textrm{\ dm}^3$

$[\ce{Cl^-}] = \dfrac{1.00 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{\ mol}}{0.0500\textrm{\ dm}^3} = 0.0200\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$
