# CH2383

Sodium hydroxide and sulfuric acid react according to the equation below.

$2\ce{\,NaOH}\textrm{(aq)} + \ce{H_2SO_4}\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow \ce{Na_2SO_4}\textrm{(aq)} + 2\ce{\,H_2O}\textrm{(l)}$

What is the volume of $0.500\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ $\ce{H_2SO_4}\textrm{(aq)}$ needed to neutralize $25.0\textrm{\ cm}^3$ of $0.120\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$ $\ce{NaOH}\textrm{(aq)}?$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}1.50\textrm{\ cm}^3$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}3.00\textrm{\ cm}^3$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}6.00\textrm{\ cm}^3$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}25.0\textrm{\ cm}^3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Neutralisation problems linking two solutions follow the path:

concentration $\times$ volume $\longrightarrow$ moles $\longrightarrow$ mole ratio $\longrightarrow$ moles $\longrightarrow$ volume.

Find the moles of $\ce{NaOH}$ using $n = CV.$ Remember to convert $\textrm{cm}^3$ to $\textrm{dm}^3$ by dividing by $1000.$

$n(\ce{NaOH}) = 0.120 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \times 0.0250 \textrm{\ dm}^3 = 3.00 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{\ mol NaOH}$

Apply the mole ratio. The balanced equation shows $2\textrm{\ mol } \ce{NaOH}$ reacts with $1\textrm{\ mol } \ce{H_2SO_4},$ so:

$n(\ce{H_2SO_4}) = 3.00 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{\ mol NaOH} \times \dfrac{1\textrm{\ mol\ } \ce{H_2SO_4}}{2\textrm{\ mol NaOH}} = 1.50 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{\ mol\ } \ce{H_2SO_4}$

Convert moles to volume using $V = \dfrac{n}{c}$:

$V(\ce{H_2SO_4}) = \dfrac{1.50 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{\ mol}}{0.500\textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}} = 3.00 \times 10^{-3}\textrm{\ dm}^3 = 3.00\textrm{\ cm}^3$
