# CH0043

**Maximum mark: 3**

Automobile airbags are inflated with $\ce{N2}$, a byproduct of sodium azide decomposition, according to the following reaction.

$\ce{2 NaN3(s) -> 2 Na(s) + 3 N2(g)}$

Calculate the mass (in grams) of sodium azide needed to generate $0.07 \,$m$^{3}$ of $\ce{N2}$ at $1.01 \times 10^{5} \,$Pa and $26.0 ^\circ$C.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$n_\ce{N_2} =
 \dfrac{1.01 \times 10^{5} \times 0.07}{8.31 \times 299} = 2.84\,\textrm{mol} \:  \checkmark $\
 $\mbox{}\\[-10pts]$
 $n_\ce{NaN_3} =  2.84 \times \dfrac{2}{3} = 1.89\, \textrm{mol} \: \checkmark$\
 $\mbox{}\\[-10pts]$
 $m_\ce{NaN_3} = 1.89  \times 65.02 = 123\,\textrm{g}  \: \checkmark$

$\textrm{\underline{Further Explanation:}}$\
Step 1:  Calculate the number of moles of $\ce{N2}$ produced in the reaction.

   $$
   n_\ce{N_2} = \dfrac{PV}{RT} =
 \dfrac{1.01 \times 10^{5}\,\textrm{Pa} \times 0.07\,\textrm{m}^{3}}{8.31\,\textrm{J}\,\textrm{K}^{-1}\,\textrm{mol}^{-1} \times 299\,\textrm{K}} = 2.84\,\textrm{mol N}_2
$$

*Notice Pa m$^3$ = kPa dm$^3$ = J.*

Step 2: Use the mole ratio from the balanced chemical equation to calculate the number of moles of sodium azide reacted.
$$
   n_\ce{NaN_3} =  2.84\, \textrm{mol}\, \ce{N2} \times \frac{2\,\textrm{mol}\,\ce{NaN_{3}}}{3\,\textrm{mol}\,\ce{N2}} = 1.89\, \textrm{mol} \, \ce{NaN3}
$$

Step 3:  Calculate the mass of $\ce{NaN3}$ using the molar mass.

$$
    m_\ce{NaN_3} = n M = 1.89 \, \textrm{mol} \times 65.02\ \textrm{g} \ \textrm{mol}^{-1} = 123\,\textrm{g} \, \ce{NaN3}
$$
