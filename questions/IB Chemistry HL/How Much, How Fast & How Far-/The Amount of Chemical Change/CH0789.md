# CH0789

When pentan$-$2$-$one undergoes complete combustion, carbon dioxide and water are the only products. The equation for this reaction is shown.

$\textrm{Pentan--2--one} \ce{+ a O2 -> b CO2 + b H2O}$

What are the values of *a* and *b* in this equation?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  | | $\hspace{3em}$ ***a*** $\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$ ***b***  $\hspace{3em}$ |
  | :-: | :-: | :-: |
  |A.$\hspace{1em}$| 7.0 | 5.0 |
  |B.$\hspace{1em}$ | 7.5 | 5.0 |
  |C.$\hspace{1em}$ | 8.0 | 6.0 |
  |D.$\hspace{1em}$ | 8.5 | 6.0 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The molecular formula of pentan$-$2$-$one is $\ce{C5H10O}$.

There are 5 C atoms on the reactant side so there must be 5 $\ce{CO2}$ molecules on the product side.  There are 10 H atoms on the reactant side so there must be 5 water molecules of the product side.  The value of ***b*** is therefore 5.

There are $5\times 2 + 5\times 1 = 15$ O atoms on the product side, one O atom in petan$-$2$-$one, so the compound must react with $\frac{1}{2}(15-1) = 7$ moles of $\ce{O2}$. The value of ***a*** is therefore 7.0.
