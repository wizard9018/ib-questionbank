# CH0689

What is the sum of all coefficients when the equation below is balanced:

$\ce{\text{\_\_}\ Al2O3 + \text{\_\_}\ NaOH + \text{\_\_}\ H2O -> \text{\_\_}\ NaAl(OH)4}$

$\ \mbox{}\\[-20pts]$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$5

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$6

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$7

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$8

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Balanced equation is:

$\ce{Al2O3 +  2 NaOH + 3 H2O -> 2 NaAl(OH)4}$.  

The sum of the coefficients is $1 + 2 + 3 + 2 = 8.$
