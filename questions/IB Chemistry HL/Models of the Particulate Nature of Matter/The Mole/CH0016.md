# CH0016

Calculate the number of moles in $20.5$ grams of sodium phosphate ($\ce{Na3PO4}$).

- A.$\hspace{1em}$0.125 mol

- B.$\hspace{1em}$1.25 mol

- C.$\hspace{1em}$12.5 mol

- D.$\hspace{1em}$125 mol

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:
The molar mass of sodium phosphate is $163.94 \textrm{\ g mol}^{-1}.$ Divide the mass (20.5 g) of sodium phosphate by the molar mass to calculate the number of moles of the compound.

$n = \dfrac{m}{M} = \dfrac{20.5 \textrm{\ g}}{163.94 \textrm{\ g mol}^{-1}} \approx \dfrac{1}{8} = 0.125 \textrm{ mol}$
