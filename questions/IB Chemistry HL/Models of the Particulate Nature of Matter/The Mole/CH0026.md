# CH0026

**Maximum mark: 6**

Ascorbic acid ($\ce{C6H8O6}$), or vitamin C, is a water-soluble vitamin that is essential for our immune system.

1. Calculate the number of moles in $2.50\,\textrm{g}$ of ascorbic acid. [1 mark]

2. Calculate the mass of $1.28\,\textrm{moles}$ of ascorbic acid. [1 mark]

3. Calculate the number of molecules in $50.0\,\textrm{g}$ of ascorbic acid. [2 marks]

4. Determine the number of moles of carbon in $225\,\textrm{mg}$ of ascorbic acid [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $n = \dfrac{m}{M} = \dfrac{2.50\,\textrm{g}}{176.14\,\textrm{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}}$ = :answer[$0.0142\,\text{mol}$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    Molar mass of ascorbic acid. $M(\ce{C6H8O6}) = (12.01)(6) + (1.01) (8) + (16.00)(6) = 72.06 + 8.08 + 96.00 = 176.14\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$

2.  $m = n\times M = (1.28\,\text{mol}) (176.14\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1})$ = :answer[$225\,\text{g}$] $\checkmark$

    $\textit {ALLOW error carried forward from incorrect }$M(C$_{6}$H$_{8}$O$_{6}$) $\textit {calculation in (a)}$

3.  Number of moles of ascorbic acid in 50.0 grams:

    n = $\dfrac{m}{M} = \dfrac{50.0\,\text{g}}{176.14\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}}$ = :answer[$0.284\,\text{mol}$] $\checkmark$

    Number of molecules:

    $N = n\times N_A = (0.284\,\text{mol})(6.02\times 10^{23})$ = :answer[$1.71\times 10^{23}$ molecules] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    $N_A$ is Avogadro's number given in the data booklet.

4.  Number of moles in 225 $\textit{mg}$ of ascorbic acid:

    $n = \dfrac{m}{M} = \dfrac{0.225\,\text{g}}{176.14\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}}$ = :answer[$1.277\times 10^{-3}\,\text{mol}$ ascorbic acid] $\checkmark$

    Number of moles of carbon:

    $(0.001277\,\text{mol}\ce{C6H8O6})\left(\dfrac{6\,\text{mol}\,\text{C}}{1\,\text{mol}\,\ce{C6H8O6}}\right)$ = :answer[$0.00766\,\text{mol}$ C] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    There are 6 moles of carbon in every mole of ascorbic acid. Therefore, multiplying the moles of ascorbic acid by 6 results in obtaining the moles of carbon.
