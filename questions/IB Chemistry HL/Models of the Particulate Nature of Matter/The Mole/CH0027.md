# CH0027

**Maximum mark: 7**

Lipoic acid is an antioxidant that can be taken as a supplement for the body.

1. Determine the empirical formula of lipoic acid. [4 marks]

| **Element**               | **Percentage Composition by mass**  |
| :-:                       | :-:                                 |
| C                         | 46.56%                              |
| H                         | 6.85%                               |
| O                         | 15.5%                               |
| S                         | 31.08%                              |

2. Calculate the molar mass of the empirical formula of lipoic acid. [1 mark]

3. If the actual molar mass of lipoic acid is $206.44\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$, determine its molecular formula. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Convert the percentages of elements into grams. Assume the sample is $100\,\text{g}$.

    C = $46.56\,\text{g}$ | H = $6.85\,\text{g}$ | O = $15.5\,\text{g}$ | S = $31.08\,\text{g}$ $\checkmark$

    Convert the mass of each element into its number of moles. Use the molar mass of each element. $\checkmark$

    $n =\dfrac{m}{M} = \dfrac{46.56\,\text{g}}{12.01\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}}$ = :answer[$3.88\,\text{mol}$ C]

    $n =\dfrac{m}{M} = \dfrac{6.85\,\text{g}}{1.01\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}}$ = :answer[$6.78\,\text{mol}$ H]

    $n =\dfrac{m}{M} = \dfrac{15.5\,\text{g}}{16.00\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}}$ = :answer[$0.97\,\text{mol}$ O]

    $n =\dfrac{m}{M} = \dfrac{31.08\,\text{g}}{32.07\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}}$ = :answer[$0.97\,\text{mol}$ S]

    Divide all the number of moles by the smallest mole value calculated in the previous step.

    The number of moles of C, H, O, and S will be divided by 0.97. $\checkmark$

    For C: $\dfrac{3.88}{0.97}$ = :answer[4]

    For H:  $\dfrac{6.78}{0.97}$ = :answer[6.99]

    For O:  $\dfrac{0.97}{0.97}$ = :answer[1]

    For S:  $\dfrac{0.97}{0.97}$ = :answer[1]

    Round your answers to the nearest whole number. The numbers rounded off will be the subscripts of each element in the empirical formula. The empirical formula of lipoic acid is :answer[C$_{4}$H$_{7}$OS]. $\checkmark$

2.  $M(\ce{C4H7OS}) = (4)(12.01) + (7)(1.01) + 16.00 + 32.07 =$  :answer[$103.18\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$] $\checkmark$

3.  $\dfrac{206.44}{103.18}$ = 2 $\checkmark$

    The molecular formula of lipoic acid is :answer[$\ce{C8H14O2S2}$] $\checkmark$

    $\textit{ALLOW error carried forward from incorrect empirical formula calculation in }\text{(a)}$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    To determine the molecular formula, first divide the molar mass of the compound by the molar mass of the empirical formula. Second, multiply the empirical formula by the scaling factor 2 which doubles the number of atoms of each element for the molecular formula.
