# CH2567

What is the mass, in g, of one molecule of $\ce{S8}$?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}5.327 \times 10^{-23}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}4.262 \times 10^{-22}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}32.07$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}256.56$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The molar mass of $\ce{S8}$ is found by multiplying the relative atomic mass of sulfur by eight:

$M(\ce{S8}) = 8 \times 32.07\textrm{ g mol}^{-1} = 256.56\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}$

This is the mass of one mole, which contains $6.02 \times 10^{23}$ molecules. The mass of a single molecule is:

$m = \dfrac{256.56\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}}{6.02 \times 10^{23}\textrm{ mol}^{-1}} = 4.262 \times 10^{-22}\textrm{ g}$

The molar mass gives the mass of one mole, not one molecule.
