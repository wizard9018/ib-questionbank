# CH0025

**Maximum mark: 5**

Eugenol is an oily, aromatic liquid extracted from essential oils such as clove and cinnamon. The molecular formula of eugenol is $\ce{C_{10}H_{12}O_{2}}.$

1. Calculate the molar mass of eugenol. [1 mark]

2. Determine the percentage by mass of carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen in eugenol. [3 marks]

3. Predict the simplest, whole$-$number ratio of the atoms in eugenol. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Molar mass: M(C$_{10}$H$_{12}$O$_{2}$) $= 12.01 \times 10 + 1.01 \times 12 +16.00 \times 2 = 120.1 + 12.12 + 32.00 =\  $:answer[$164.22 \textrm{\ g mol}^{-1}$] $\checkmark$

2. Percentage by mass

    % C = $\left(\dfrac{120.1}{164.22}\right)$ (100)  = :answer[73.13 %] $\checkmark$

    % H = $\left(\dfrac{12.12}{164.22}\right)$ (100) = :answer[7.38 %] $\checkmark$

    % O = $\left(\dfrac{32.00}{164.22}\right)$ (100) = :answer[19.49 %] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
    Divide the total mass of atoms of each element by the molar mass of eugenol and multiply it by 100

3. The molecular formula of eugenol is C$_{10}$H$_{12}$O$_{2}$. Therefore, its empirical formula is C$_{5}$H$_{6}$O $\checkmark$  (ratio C : H : O = 5 : 6 : 1)

   $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$ \
   Divide by the highest common factor (2) to determine the simplest whole number ratio
