# CH2571

A sample contains approximately $24.02\textrm{ g}$ of C, $2.42\textrm{ g}$ of H, $5.60\textrm{ g}$ of N and $3.20\textrm{ g}$ of O. What is the empirical formula of the compound?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}\ce{C10H12N2O}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}\ce{C10H10N2O}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}\ce{C10H12NO2}$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}\ce{C12H12NO2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Convert each mass to amount using the relative atomic mass:

$n(\ce{C}) = \dfrac{24.02\textrm{ g}}{12.01\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}} = 2.000\textrm{ mol}$

$n(\ce{H}) = \dfrac{2.42\textrm{ g}}{1.01\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}} = 2.40\textrm{ mol}$

$n(\ce{N}) = \dfrac{5.60\textrm{ g}}{14.01\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}} = 0.400\textrm{ mol}$

$n(\ce{O}) = \dfrac{3.20\textrm{ g}}{16.00\textrm{ g mol}^{-1}} = 0.200\textrm{ mol}$

Dividing each by the smallest value $(0.200\textrm{ mol})$ gives the whole$-$number ratio:

$\ce{C} : \ce{H} : \ce{N} : \ce{O} = 10 : 12 : 2 : 1$

The empirical formula is $\ce{C10H12N2O}.$
