# CH0040

**Maximum mark: 6**

Avogadro's law describes the relationship between the volume of gases and the number of moles measured at the same pressure and temperature.

1.  Draw a graph that shows the variation of $V$ as $n$ changes. [1 mark]

2.  A sample of nitrogen gas contains $1.78\,\text{mol}$ and occupies a volume of $2.60\,\text{dm}^{3}$ at room temperature. Determine the number of moles of the $\ce{N2}$ gas if it occupies a volume of $3.50\,\text{dm}^{3}$. [1 mark]

3.  $15.0\,\text{g}$ of $\ce{O2}$ gas occupies a volume of $6.20\,\text{dm}^{3}$. Calculate the volume occupied by $30.0\,\text{g}$ of $\ce{O2}$ at the same temperature and pressure. [2 marks]

4.  Calculate the volume occupied by $50.0\,\text{g}$ of $\ce{CO2}$ at STP. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The graph shows a straight line passing through the origin. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    $V$ and $n$ are directly proportional.

![1.3-40a](images/1.3-40a.png)

2.  $n_2 = \dfrac{(3.50\,\text{dm}^3)(1.78\,\text{mol})}{(2.60\,\text{dm}^3)} =$ :answer[$2.40\,\text{mol}$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    Use the Avogadro's law equation $\dfrac{V_1}{n_1} = \dfrac{V_2}{n_2}$

    Derive the formula for $n_2$:

    $n_2 = \dfrac{(V_2)(n_1)}{V_1}$

3.  $n_1 = \dfrac{15.0\,\text{g}}{32.0\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}} =$ :answer[$0.469\,\text{mol}$]

    $n_2 = \dfrac{30.0\,\text{g}}{32.0\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}} =$ :answer[$0.938\,\text{mol}$] $\checkmark$

    $V_2 = \dfrac{(V_1)(n_2)}{n_1}$

    $V_2 = \dfrac{(6.20\,\text{dm}^{3})(0.938\,\text{mol})}{(0.469\,\text{mol})} =$ :answer[$12.4\text{dm}^3$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    $M(\ce{O2}) = (16.0)(2) = 32.0\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$

    Step 1: Calculate the number of moles; $n = \dfrac{m}{M}$

    Step 2: Derive the formula for $V_2$ using Avogadro's law equation.

4.  n$_{\ce{CO2}} = \dfrac{50.0\,\text{g}}{44.01\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}} =$ :answer[$1.14\,\text{mol}$] $\checkmark$

    At STP, $V = n\times V_m = (1.14\,\text{mol})(22.7\,\text{dm}^{3}\,\text{mol}^{-1}) =$ :answer[$25.9\,\text{dm}^{3}$] $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    $M(\ce{CO2}) = 12.01 + (16.00)(2) = 44.01\,\text{g}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$

    Step 1: Calculate the number of moles of $\ce{CO2}$; $n = \dfrac{m}{M}$

    Step 2: At STP, $V = n\times \textrm{molar volume}$
