# CH2583

A sample of an ideal gas is trapped in a cylinder with a piston at constant pressure.

![CH2583](images/CH2583.png)

Which statement is correct?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$The piston moves up if the temperature of the system is increased.

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$The piston moves down if the temperature of the system is increased.

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$The piston remains in its position if the temperature of the system is increased.

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$The information is not enough to predict the behaviour of the piston if the temperature of the system is increased.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The amount of gas is fixed (closed system) and the pressure is constant (the piston adjusts its position so the pressure inside is equal to the external pressure). From the ideal gas law:

$pV = nRT$

Since $p$ and $n$ are constant, volume is directly proportional to absolute temperature:

$\dfrac{p}{nR} = \dfrac{T}{V} = \textrm{constant}$

An increase in the absolute temperature produces a proportional increase in volume. The gas expands and pushes the piston upward.
