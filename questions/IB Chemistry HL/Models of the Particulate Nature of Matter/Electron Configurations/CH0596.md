# CH0596

Which of the following ionization energies is the largest in magnitude?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The first ionization energy of sodium

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The second ionization energy of magnesium

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The third ionization energy of aluminium

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The fourth ionization energy of silicon

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Successive ionization energies are larger, as electrons are removed, there is less electron-electron repulsion, so the remaining electrons are pulled closer to the nucleus, and more energy is required to remove them.
