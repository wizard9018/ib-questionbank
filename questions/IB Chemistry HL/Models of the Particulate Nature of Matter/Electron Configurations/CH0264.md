# CH0264

Mass spectrometry uses fast-moving electrons to ionize particles and determine the mass-to-charge ratio of an unknown compound.

1.  Using the data booklet section 1 and 2, calculate the frequency of an electron with a wavelength of 1.68 m. [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the wavelength of an electron with a frequency of $2.00\times 10^8\,\text{s}^{-1}$. [1 mark]

3. State the effect of increasing the wavelength on frequency. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $f = \dfrac{c}{\lambda} = \dfrac{3.00\times 10^8 \,\text{m s}^{-1}}{1.68\,\text{m}} = 1.79\times 10^8\,\text{s}^{-1}\,\checkmark$

2.  $\lambda = \dfrac{c}{f} = \dfrac{3.00\times 10^8\,\text{m s}^{-1}}{2.00\times 10^8\,\text{s}^{-1}} = 1.50\,\text{m}\,\checkmark$

3.  As you increase the wavelength, you decrease the value of the frequency. Therefore, the frequency is inversely proportional to the wavelength. $\checkmark$
