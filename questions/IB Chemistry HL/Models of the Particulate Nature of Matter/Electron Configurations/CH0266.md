# CH0266

**Maximum mark: 4**

John is sitting on a beach on a warm summer evening watching the reddish sky ($\lambda=650$ nm) while his brother, Eric, is playing with his laser beam with a frequency of $4.60\times 10^{14}\,\text{s}^{-1}$.

1.  Calculate the frequency of the radiation from the red sky. [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the wavelength of the laser beam of Eric.   [1 mark]

3.  Compare the wavelength of the laser beam with the wavelength of the radiation of the red sky.   [1 mark]

4.  Deduce the colour of the laser beam of Eric.   [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\begin{aligned}
    \\
    f &= \dfrac{3.00\times 10^8\,\text{m s}^{-1}}{650\times 10^{-9}\,\text{m}} \\
    f &= 4.62\times 10^{14}\,\text{s}^{-1}\,\checkmark
    \end{aligned}$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    $\begin{aligned}
    \text{Rearranging the formula: } c &= \lambda f, \\
    \text{We obtain: } f &= \dfrac{c}{\lambda} \\
    \end{aligned}$

2.  $\begin{aligned}
    \\
    \lambda &= \dfrac{3.00\times 10^8\,\text{m s}^{-1}}{4.60\times 10^{14}\,\text{s}^{-1}} \\
    \lambda &= 652\,\text{nm}\,\checkmark
    \end{aligned}$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    $\begin{aligned}
    \text{Rearranging the formula: } c &= \lambda f, \\
    \text{We obtain: } f &= \dfrac{c}{\lambda} \\
    \end{aligned}$

3.  The wavelength of the laser beam and the red sky is almost the same, which is 650 nm vs 652 nm. $\checkmark$

4.  Since the laser beam and red sky have a very similar wavelength, the colour of the laser beam is also red. $\checkmark$
