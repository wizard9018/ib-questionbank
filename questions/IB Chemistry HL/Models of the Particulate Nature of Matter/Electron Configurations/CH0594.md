# CH0594

Which of the following statements about nitrogen is correct?

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$Oxygen has a higher first ionization energy than nitrogen.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$There is a large increase between the $5^{\text{th}}$ and $6^{\text{th}}$ ionization energies of nitrogen.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$Phosphorus has a lower first ionization energy than nitrogen.

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The electron configuration for oxygen is $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^4$ whereas nitrogen is $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^3$. Due to the increased stability of nitrogen's half-filled $p$-shell, more energy is required to remove an electron from a nitrogen atom than an oxygen atom. Statement I is incorrect.

Statement II is correct as nitrogen has 5 valence electrons and the 6th electron is removed from a shell closer to the nucleus.

Statement III is correct as first ionization energy decreases down a group.
