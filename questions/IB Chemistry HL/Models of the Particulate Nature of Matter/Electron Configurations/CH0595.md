# CH0595

The successive ionization energies in kJ mol$^{-1}$ for an element in the second period of the periodic table are 800, 2430, 3660, and 25000. Which element is this most likely to be?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Li}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Be}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{B}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{C}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

There is a large increase between the 3rd and 4th ionization energies which shows that the element is in group 13.

Large increases in ionization energy indicate electrons being removed from a shell closer to the nucleus.
