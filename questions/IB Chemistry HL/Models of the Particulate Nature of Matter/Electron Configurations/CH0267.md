# CH0267

**Maximum mark: 2**

Cobalt is an important cofactor that often inhibits enzyme activity involved in cellular respiration, such as catalase.

1.  Using the complete electron configuration, determine the electron configuration of $\ce{Co^2+}.$ [1 mark]

2.  Draw the orbital diagram of the $3d$ electrons of $\ce{Co}$. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. $\ce{Co^2+: 1s^2 2s^2 2p^6 3s^2 3p^6 3d^7}$ $\checkmark$

   $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

   Electron configuration of $\ce{Co}$: $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6\,4s^2\,3d^7$ ; the $4s$ electrons are lost.
