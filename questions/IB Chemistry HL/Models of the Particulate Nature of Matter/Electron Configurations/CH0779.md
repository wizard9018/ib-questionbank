# CH0779

What is the electron configuration for the $\ce{Cu+}$ ion?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6\,4s^2\,3d^9$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6\,3d^{10}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6\,4s^2\,3d^8$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6\,3d^9$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\ce{Cu}$ and $\ce{Cu+}$ have the following electron configurations:

$\ce{Cu}:\quad 1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6\,4s^1\,3d^{10}$

$\ce{Cu+}:\quad 1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6\,3d^{10}$

The valence electron lost in the $\ce{Cu+}$ ion is the electron in the $4s$ sublevel.
