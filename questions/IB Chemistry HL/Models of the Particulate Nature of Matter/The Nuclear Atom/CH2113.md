# CH2113

**Maximum mark: 7**

Nickel has five stable isotopes. The masses and percent abundances are tabulated below:
$\ \newline \ $

| Isotope | Percent abundance |
| :-: | :-: |
| $^{58}$Ni | 68.08\% |
| $^{60}$Ni | 26.22\% |
| $^{61}$Ni | 1.14\% |
| $^{62}$Ni | A |
| $^{64}$Ni | B |

1.  The relative atomic mass of nickel is 58.745, calculate the percent abundance of nickel$\text{--}$62 and nickel$\text{--}$64. [3 marks]

2.  State the number of protons and neutrons in nickel$\text{--}$62. [1 mark]

3.  Using your results from part (a), sketch the mass spectrum of nickel. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $58(68.08)+ 60(26.22) + 61(1.14)+ 62A+ 64B = 58.745(100)\ \checkmark$

    $A + B = 100 - (68.08 + 26.22 + 1.14) = 4.56\ \checkmark$

    $A = 4.36$ **AND** $B = 0.19\ \checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    The weighted average of the isotopes must be equal to the relative atomic mass of nickel, 58.74.

    $58.745 = \dfrac{(58)(68.08) + (60)(26.22) + (61)(1.14) + 62A + 64B}{100}$

    and the sum of the percent abundances must be 100.

    $68.08 + 26.22 + 1.14 + A + B = 100$

    Solving these two equations and two unknowns will give $A$ and $B.$ From equation 2,

    $A + B = 4.56\quad$ or $\quad B = 4.56 - A$

    Plugging that in to equation 1,

    $5874.5 = 5591.38 + 62A + 64(4.56 - A)$

    Solving for $A$ and $B$ give:

2.  protons: 28 **AND** neutrons:   $62 - 28 = 34 \: \checkmark$
