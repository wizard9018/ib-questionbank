# CH1127

The mass spectrum of a sample of an unknown element is shown. What is the relative atomic mass of this element?

$\mbox{}\\[-5pts]$

![CH1127](images/CH1127.png)

-   A.$\hspace{1em}206.20$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}206.25$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}207.86$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}207.24$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\
To estimate the relative atomic mass of the sample, consider only the three tallest peaks.

Approximate Abundance | m / z
:-:| :-:
24 | 206
22 | 207
52 | 208

To further simplify the problem, shift the masses down by 206 (the smallest $m/z$ value)

Approximate Abundance | Shifted m / z
:-:| :-:
24 | 0
22 | 1
52 | 2

Now, calculate the (shifted) mass using a weighted average.

$A_r = \sum \dfrac{m_1 a_1 + m_2 a_2 + \ldots}{a_1 + a_2 + \ldots }$

Substituting in the values

$A_r = \dfrac{0 \times 24 + 1 \times 22 + 2 \times 52}{24 + 22 + 52} = \dfrac{126}{98} \approx 1.25$

The approximate mass is $207.25 \textrm{\ amu}.$

The more traditional approach is to take the weighted average of the given values:

$A_r = \dfrac{1.4 \times 204 + 24.1 \times 206 + 22.1 \times 207 + 52.4 \times 208}{100}$

$A_r = \dfrac{285.6 + 4964.6 + 4574.7 + 10899.2}{100} = 207.24$
