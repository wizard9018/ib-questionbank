# CH0049

Which of the following statements is **false**?

-   I.$\hspace{1em}$Electrons have greater mass than protons and neutrons
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$Protons and neutrons are in the nucleus
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$Protons and electrons are charged subatomic particles
-   IV.$\hspace{0.4em}$Electrons are located outside the nucleus|

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$II and IV only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$II only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$I only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Statement I is false: electrons have a mass approximately 2000 times smaller than that of a proton or neutron.

Statements II  and IV  are true: protons and neutrons are located in the nucleus, while electrons occupy regions outside the nucleus called the electron cloud.

Statement III is true: protons have a $+1$ charge and electrons have a $-1$ charge.  Neutrons are uncharged.
