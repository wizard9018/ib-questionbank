# CH1095

A mixture of propanone, $\ce{CH3COCH3},$ and butan$-$1$-$ol, $\ce{CH3CH2CH2CH2OH},$ must be separated.

Which statements are correct?

- I.$\hspace{1em}$Distillation allows the separation of the components of this mixture.

- II.$\hspace{0.7em}$A condenser, thermometer, and evaporating basin are needed for distillation.

- III.$\hspace{0.4em}$The distillate would be propanone.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$II only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\

Distillation separates miscible liquids with different boiling points. Butan$-$1$-$ol molecules hydrogen bond through the $\ce{-OH}$ group, whereas propanone molecules, of similar molar mass, attract one another only through dipole$-$dipole and London forces. Butan$-$1$-$ol therefore boils at $118\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}$ and propanone at $56\,{}^\circ\textrm{C},$ a large enough difference for distillation to separate them. Statement I is correct.

An evaporating basin is not part of the apparatus; distillation requires a distillation flask, a thermometer and a condenser. Statement II is incorrect.

Propanone has the lower boiling point, so it vaporises first, passes into the condenser and is collected as the distillate. Butan-1-ol remains in the flask. Statement III is correct.
