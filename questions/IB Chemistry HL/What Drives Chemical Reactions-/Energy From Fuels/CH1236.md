# CH1236

In the United States, bioethanol is mainly made from corn grain; in Brazil, it is primarily made from sugarcane. Which statement(s) is (are) correct about these two?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | These are considered renewable energy sources  |
  | II. | Their use does not significantly contribute to global warming|
  | III. | Photosynthesis allows plants to produce chemical energy later converted into biofuels |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

- D. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The substances mentioned in the question are described as biofuels, which are produced from the biological fixation of carbon over a short period through photosynthesis. These are renewable sources of energy.

Biofuels have a reduced impact in terms of global warming as they can cause carbon dioxide while being burnt. Still, at the same time, carbon was fixed from the atmosphere throughout its production through carbon fixation.

This question is categorized as hard. Students must understand what biofuels mean and apply their knowledge to a specific case.
