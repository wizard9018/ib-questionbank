# CH0861

Which of the following correctly depicts the product(s) at the cathode and the direction of electron flow in an electrochemical fuel cell?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | Product(s) at the cathode | Direction of electron flow |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | A. | $\ce{H2O and CO2}$| anode to cathode |
    | B. | $\ce{H2O}$ | anode to cathode |
    | C. | $\ce{H2O and CO2}$| cathode to anode |
    | D. | $\ce{H2O}$ | cathode to anode |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

In an electrochemical fuel cell, the fuel (methanol, for example) is oxidized at the anode, while oxygen gas is reduced at the cathode to form water. As is the case for any electrochemical cell, the electrons flow through an external wire from the anode to the cathode.

This question is categorized as medium because it requires the knowledge that oxygen gas is reduced at the cathode and that the product is water. The $\ce{CO2}$ would be a product for oxidizing a fuel such as methanol.
