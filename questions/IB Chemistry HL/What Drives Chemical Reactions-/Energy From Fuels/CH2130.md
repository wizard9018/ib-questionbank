# CH2130

Which of these is a biofuel?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :- | :- |
    | I. | Liquified natural gas |
    | II. | Ethanol produced from fermentation of fruits |
    | III. | Methane produced by farming |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$II only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The student must identify biofuels as energy sources produced by organic compounds. Products of fruit fermentation are considered biofuels. Methane produced by farming through anaerobic digestion is also considered a biofuel as it breaks down organic waste into a gas that can undergo combustion.

Liquified natural gas is a fossil fuel and is not considered a biofuel.

This question is categorized as medium as the student must identify different types of fuels.
