# CH0860

Ethanol in biofuel is produced through which of the following methods?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Carbon fixation

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Photosynthesis

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Fermentation of plant material

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Combustion of hydrocarbons

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The formation of ethanol in biofuel occurs during the fermentation of plant material in anaerobic conditions and in the presence of yeast as a biological catalyst:

$\ce{C6H12O6 + yeast -> 2 CH3CH2OH + 2 CO2}$

To eliminate the incorrect answers, students must recognize that carbon fixation is the process where inorganic carbon (such as $\ce{CO2}$) is converted to organic compounds during photosynthesis.

Combustion of hydrocarbons would be the process that would take place when the biofuel is combusted.

This question is categorized as easy since the student needs only to recall that ethanol for biofuel is produced via the fermentation of plant material.
