# CH2051

For the combustion of strontium, which of the following are correct for the products and enthalpy of reaction?

$\ce{Sr(s) + O2(g) -> \ \ \ ?}\qquad \Delta H  = \ \ ?$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{SrO}; \Delta H > 0$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{SrO}; \Delta H < 0$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{SrO2}; \Delta H > 0$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{SrO2}; \Delta H < 0$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The combustion of a metal is an exothermic reaction where energy is released from the system. This is indicated by a negative enthalpy, so $\Delta H < 0.$ The product for the combustion of a metal is a metal oxide. Since strontium ion has a $2+$ charge and oxygen ion (oxide) has a $2-$ charge, the resulting compound is SrO.
