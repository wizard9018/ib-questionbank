# CH2128

Which statement is correct?

-   A.

    $\mbox{}\\[-48pts]$

    -   Methanol fuel cells do not contribute to an increase in the levels of $\ce{CO2}$ in the atmosphere, and produce electrical energy from chemical energy.  $\newline \ $

-   B.

    $\mbox{}\\[-48pts]$

    -   Biofuels do not contribute to an increase in the levels of $\ce{CO2}$ in the atmosphere, and produce electrical energy from chemical energy. $\newline \ $

-   C.

    $\mbox{}\\[-48pts]$

    -   Methanol fuel cells do not contribute to an increase in the levels of $\ce{CO2}$ in the atmosphere, and produce chemical energy from electrical energy. $\newline \ $

-   D.

    $\mbox{}\\[-38pts]$

    -   Biofuels do not contribute to an increase in the levels of $\ce{CO2}$ in the atmosphere, and produce chemical energy from electrical energy.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand that methanol oxidation produces carbon dioxide in methanol fuel cells, increasing the levels of methanol in the atmosphere.

Fuel cells allow the conversion of chemical energy stored in molecules and their bonds into electrical energy as electrons are gained and lost in the redox reaction.

Biofuels are organic compounds produced by carbon dioxide fixation through photosynthesis. Burning these biofuels produces carbon dioxide, but there is no net effect as the carbon dioxide fixation counteracts this production. Biofuels are usually considered carbon neutral as the carbon atoms in the biofuel originally come from the carbon dioxide fixed from the atmosphere. Once the combustion takes place, these carbon atoms return to the atmosphere.

This question is categorized as easy as the student must understand the impact of using methanol fuel cells and biofuels on carbon dioxide production.
