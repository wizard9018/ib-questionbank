# CH2125

In the diagram below, which is the anode?

![CH2125](images/CH2125.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand the functioning of the hydrogen fuel cell. In this cell, hydrogen is used as the source of electrons. Hydrogen undergoes an oxidation at the anode (A) so that hydrogen ions are produced. These electrons flow to produce an electric current to the cathode (C), where reduction occurs, and the oxidizing agent, oxygen from the air, gets reduced to water which exits at (D).  Hydrogen ions flow from the anode to the cathode through the polymer electrolyte membrane (B).

This question is categorized as medium as the student must understand how hydrogen fuel cells work to recognize the electrodes on a given diagram.
