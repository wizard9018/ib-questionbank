# CH0800

**Maximum mark: 5**

Propan$-$1$-$ol may be used as a fuel.

1.  State the **molecular** formula of propan$-$1$-$ol. [1 mark]

2.  Based on your answer to (i), write a balanced equation for the **complete** combustion of propan$-$1$-$ol. [2 marks]

3.  State **two** ways in which the equation for the **incomplete** combustion of propan$-$1$-$ol would differ from your answer to (ii). [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1. $\ce{C3H8O}$ $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        The molecular formula states the number of atoms of each element present in one molecule of a compound. A molecular formula begins with C, if present, followed by other elements in order of increasing atomic number. The number of atoms of each element is included as a subscript after the corresponding symbol. Propan-1-ol contains 3 C atoms, 8 H atoms and 1 O atom, so has the formula $\ce{C3H8O}$.

    2.  $\ce{C3H8O + \frac{9}{2} O2 -> 3 CO2 + 4 H2O} \: \checkmark \checkmark $\
        **OR**\
        $\ce{2 C3H8O + 9 O2 -> 6 CO2 + 8 H2O} \: \checkmark \checkmark$\
        *Award M1 for correct molecular formulas correct for all reactants and products\
        Award M2 for correct coefficients.*

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        When a compound of C, H and O undergoes complete combustion, $\ce{CO2}$ and $\ce{H2O}$ are the only products. The propan$-$1$-$ol molecule contains 3 C atoms, so combustion of one propan$-$1$-$ol molecule produces 3 $\ce{CO2}$ molecules.

        There are 8 H atoms in propan$-$1$-$ol, but $\ce{H2O}$ contains 2 H atoms per molecule, so combustion of one propan$-$1$-$ol molecule produces $\frac{8}{2} = 4\ \ce{H2O}$ molecules.

        There are $3 \times 2 + 4 \times 1 = 10$ O atoms in the product molecules, and 1 O atom in propan$-$1$-$ol. Therefore, 9 additional O atoms are needed. The O atoms are supplied in the form of $\ce{O2}$ molecules, so each propan$-$1$-$ol molecule must react with $\frac{9}{2} = 4.5\ \ce{O2}$ molecules.

    3.  Any two of the following:

        -   propan$-$1$-$ol would react with fewer $\ce{O2}$ molecules $\checkmark$
        -   less / no $\ce{CO2}$ would be produced $\checkmark$
        -   some CO  would be produced $\checkmark$
        -   some C / carbon / graphite / soot would be produced $\checkmark$

        *Award [1] if a correct equation for incomplete combustion is given but differences are not stated.*

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
        Complete combustion occurs when there is sufficient $\ce{O2}$ molecules are available for the fuel to be converted to $\ce{CO2}$ and $\ce{H2O}$ only. If less $\ce{O2}$ is present, $\ce{H2O}$ is still formed but the C atoms are incorporated into carbon monoxide gas, CO, or solid carbon, C. This process, known as incomplete combustion, is problematic, as it reduces the energy released by the fuel. Furthermore, CO is a colourless, odourless and highly toxic gas and carbon particles, or soot, can cause respiratory damage if inhaled.
