2.  $\text{bonds broken} = 614 + 346 + 6(414) + \frac{9}{2}(498) = 5685\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}\:\checkmark$\

    $\text{bonds formed} = 6(804) + 6(463) = 7602\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}\:\checkmark$\

    $\Delta H = 5685 - 7602 = -1917\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}\:\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\

    The enthalpy of the combustion reaction can be estimated using the bond enthalpy values.  To determine which bonds are broken and formed, start by drawing out the structures of the molecules in this reaction:

    ![CH0320](images/CH0320.png)

    The enthalpy of the bonds broken:

    $\begin{aligned}

    \text{bonds broken} &= (\ce{C=C}) + (\ce{C-C}) + 6(\ce{C-H}) + \frac{9}{2}(\ce{O=O})\\

     &= 614 + 346 + 6(414) + \frac{9}{2}(498) =  5685\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}

    \end{aligned}$

    The enthalpy of the bonds formed:

    $\begin{aligned}

    \text{bonds formed} &= 6(\ce{C=O}) + 6(\ce{O-H})\\

     &= 6(804) + 6(463) = 7602\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}

    \end{aligned}$

    The overall enthalpy change of the reaction is 

    $\Delta H^{\minuso}_{reaction} =\sum \Delta H^{\minuso}(\textrm{bonds broken}) - \sum \Delta H^{\minuso}(\textrm{bonds formed})$\

    $\Delta H = 5685 - 7602 = -1917\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}$
