# CH0856

What is the correct order of specific energy from highest to lowest for methane, hydrogen gas, ethanol, and butane?\
*Use the enthalpies of combustion in Table 14 of the data booklet.*

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Hydrogen gas, butane, ethanol, methane

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Hydrogen gas, butane, methane, ethanol

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Methane, butane, ethanol, hydrogen gas

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Hydrogen gas, methane, butane, ethanol

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Specific energy is calculated as follows:

Specific energy = $\dfrac{\ce{energy released from fuel}}{\ce{mass of fuel consumed}}$ = $\dfrac{\ce{molar enthalpy of combustion}}{\ce{molar mass}}$

Therefore, the following values are determined and calculated for this question:

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | $\mathbf{\Delta H_c}$ (kJ mol$\mathbf{^{-1}}$) | Molar mass (g mol$^{-1}$) | Specific energy (kJ g$^{-1}$) |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | methane | $-891$| 16.04| 55.5 |
    | hydrogen | $-286$ | 2.02| 141.6 |
    | ethanol | $-1367$ | 46.08|29.7 |
    | butane | $-2878$ | 58.12|49.5|

From this, the fuels' specific energy from the highest: hydrogen gas, methane, butane, and ethanol.

This question is categorized as hard because the student must recall the formula for specific energy and apply it correctly for each calculation.
