# CH2122

Which statement about the incomplete combustion of ethanol is correct?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :- | :- |
    | I. | It occurs if there is a limited supply of oxidizing agent. |
    | II. | It produces CO only. |
    | III. | It is less exothermic than complete combustion. |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$III only

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must distinguish between complete and incomplete combustion. Complete combustion forms carbon dioxide and water, whereas incomplete combustion can produce carbon monoxide or carbon soot along with carbon dioxide.

If the supply of oxidizing agents, such as oxygen from the air, is limited, incomplete combustion occurs. Statement I is correct.

Incomplete combustion does not produce carbon monoxide only. It can also produce carbon soot. Statement II is incorrect.

Finally, complete combustion releases more energy than incomplete combustion. Incomplete combustion is less exothermic than complete combustion because the carbon is not being oxidized as much: carbon soot has carbon with an oxidation state of 0 and carbon monoxide has carbon with an oxidation state of +2 while carbon dioxide has carbon with an oxidation state of +4.

This question is categorized as medium as the student must understand the nature of incomplete combustion and identify differences with complete combustion.
