# CH2047

What is the correct order of specific energy from highest to lowest for four unknown fuels (1, 2, 3, 4) using the enthalpies of combustion, molar masses, and densities shown below?

| Fuel | $\mathbf{\Delta H^{\minuso}_c}$ $\\$ (kJ mol$^\mathbf{-1}$) | M $\\$ (g mol$^{\mathbf{-1}}$)| density $\\$ (g dm$^{\mathbf{-3}}$) |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1 | $-800$ | 50 | 4 |
| 2 | $-1000$ | 125 | 1 |
| 3 | $-500$ | 25 | 10 |
| 4 | $-700$ | 100 | 70 |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$1, 2, 3, 4

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$3, 1, 2, 4

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$2, 1, 4, 3

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$4, 3, 1, 2

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

Specific energy, often called $e,$ is calculated as follows:

$\text{specific energy} = \dfrac{\text{energy released from fuel}}{\text{mass of fuel  consumed}}$

$e = -\dfrac{\Delta H^{\minuso}_c}{M}$

*(The density in the table is a distractor, it can be used to determine the energy density, or the amount of energy released per unit volume.)*

Therefore, the following values are determined and calculated for this question.

| Fuel | $\mathbf{\Delta H^{\minuso}_c}$ $\\$ (kJ mol$^\mathbf{-1}$) | M $\\$ (g mol$^{\mathbf{-1}}$)| e $\\$ (kJ g$^{\mathbf{-1}}$) |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1 | $-800$ | 50 | 16 |
| 2 | $-1000$ | 125 | 8 |
| 3 | $-500$ | 25 |  20 |
| 4 | $-700$ | 100 | 7 |

From this, the fuels' specific energy from the highest to lowest is: 3, 1, 2, 4
