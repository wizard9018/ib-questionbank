# CH2124

Which combination is correct for biofuels?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | Source | Cost of production | Chemical reaction for production of biofuel |
    | :- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Renewable |High | $\ce{6 CO2(g) + 6 H2O(l) -> C6H12O6(aq) + 6 O2(g)}$ |
    | B. | Renewable | Low | $\ce{6 CO2(g) + 6 H2O(l) -> C6H12O6(aq) + 6 O2(g)}$ |
    | C. | Nonrenewable | High | $\ce{C6H12O6(aq) + 6 O2(g) -> 6 CO2(g) + 6 H2O(l)}$ |
    | D. | Nonrenewable | Low | $\ce{C6H12O6(aq) + 6 O2(g) -> 6 CO2(g) + 6 H2O(l)}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand that biofuels are renewable energy sources that can be replenished. They depend on the sun's radiation to produce organic compounds through photosynthesis. Plants convert sunlight into chemical energy using this equation:

$\ce{6 CO2(g) + 6 H2O(l) -> C6H12O6(aq) + 6 O2(g)}$

One of the main concerns related to the production of biofuels is their high cost of production compared to fossil fuels. However, large$-$scale production can significantly reduce the cost.

This question is categorized as easy as the student must understand the implications of using biofuels.
