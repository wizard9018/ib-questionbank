# CH2126

Which of the following is the reducing agent in the hydrogen fuel cell?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H2}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{O2}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H2O}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H+}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand the functioning of the hydrogen fuel cell. In this cell, hydrogen is used as the source of electrons. Hydrogen undergoes an oxidation at the anode so that hydrogen ions are produced.

$\ce{H2 -> 2 H+ + 2 e-}$

These electrons flow to produce an electric current to the cathode, where reduction occurs, and oxygen from the air gets reduced. Hydrogen is the reducing agent and oxygen is the oxidizing agent.

$\ce{\frac{1}{2} O2 + 2 H+ + 2 e- -> H2O}$

This question is categorized as medium as the student must understand how the hydrogen fuel cell works.
