# CH1171

Which combination is correct about biofuels?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | Produced from | Type | Disadvantage |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | A. | Burning fuels | Renewable | Reduces the availability of land for growing food |
    | B. | Burning fuels |Non-renewable | Reduces the availability of land for growing food |
    | C. | Biological fixation of carbon | Renewable | Less pollution |
    | D. | Biological fixation of carbon | Renewable | Reduces the availability of land for growing food |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Biofuels are renewable resources of energy. These come from the biological fixation of carbon dioxide, known as carbon fixation.

The use of biofuels is cheap and allows for less polluting and renewable sources to be used. However, there is a high cost of harvesting and transportation; nutrients might be taken from the soil, and land use for this purpose reduces the availability of land for growing food. These are some disadvantages we should also consider for this source of energy.

This question is categorized as hard as students should comprehend biofuels' nature and limitations.
