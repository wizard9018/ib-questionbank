# CH1170

Which statement(s) is (are) correct about combustion reactions?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | They require oxygen to occur |
  | II. | They are always exothermic |
  | III. | They always produce carbon dioxide |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

- D. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Combustion reactions apply to the reactions of metals or non-metals with oxygen. Statement I is correct.

Most combustions are exothermic, although nitrogen reacts with oxygen by breaking a triple nitrogen-to-nitrogen bond, resulting in an endothermic reaction. Statement II is incorrect.

Finally, the combustion of organic compounds can produce carbon dioxide in the case of complete combustion, but carbon and carbon monoxide can also be formed if incomplete combustion occurs. Moreover, metals undergo combustion to form metal oxides, and no carbon dioxide is formed. Statement III is incorrect.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must recall the combustion concept as a broad idea that includes reactions of metals with oxygen as well as endothermic reactions (such as that on nitrogen).
