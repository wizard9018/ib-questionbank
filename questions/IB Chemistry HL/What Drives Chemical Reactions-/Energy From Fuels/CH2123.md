# CH2123

Given the information in the graph below determine which fuel has the largest specific energy?

![CH2123](images/CH2123.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand the concept of specific energy as the amount of heat energy released per mass of fuel burnt. We can deduce the specific energy from the gradient of this graph as

$\text{specific energy} = \dfrac{\Delta \text{ energy released}}{\Delta \text{ mass of fuel}}$

The greater the gradient the greater the specific energy of the fuel. Fuel A has the steepest curve resulting in the greatest specific energy.

This question is categorized as medium as the student must relate the  gradient with the fuel's specific energy.
