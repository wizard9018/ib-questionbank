# CH0859

Which of the following correctly states the products for the combustion of butane under conditions where there is very little oxygen?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CO2(g) + CO(g) + H2O(g)}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CO2(g) + H2O(g)}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CO(g) + H2O(g)}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{C(s) + H2O(g)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

When very little oxygen is present in the combustion of a hydrocarbon, the result is carbon soot (C solid) as opposed to carbon monoxide or carbon dioxide.

Sufficient oxygen $\rightarrow$ complete combustion resulting in $\ce{CO2}$ and water;

Limited oxygen $\rightarrow$ incomplete combustion resulting in CO and water;

Very limited oxygen $\rightarrow$ incomplete combustion resulting in C soot and water.

For this question, the student must recognize that when there is limited oxygen, there are fewer oxygens in the products. This question is categorized as easy as the student needs only to recognize that the amount of oxygen in the combustion directly relates to the number of oxygens in the products.
