# CH0865

**Maximum mark: 10**

Energy sources can be designated as either renewable or non-renewable, with efficiency calculated based on the amount of energy that can be produced when combusting a certain amount of fuel.

1.  State the balanced chemical equation, with state symbols, for the complete combustion of ethanol $\ce{C2H5OH}$ as a component of biofuel. [1 mark]

2.  Calculate the amount of heat energy in kJ released for the combustion of ethanol if 10.0 dm$^3$ of $\ce{CO2}$ is produced at STP using Sections 2 and 14 of the data booklet. [2 marks]

3.  Natural gas is a common fuel with a $70-90\%$ methane composition. Assuming that a sample of natural gas is $70\%$ methane, and using Sections 2 and 14 of the data booklet, calculate the volume of natural gas at STP that would be required to produce the same amount of heat energy as calculated for ethanol in (b).

    *If no answer was determined for (b), use 405 $kJ$ for this question.*     [3 marks]

4.  Using Sections 7 and 14 of the data booklet and a calculation to support your answer, state which fuel, ethanol or methane, has a higher specific energy. [2 marks]

5.  State whether natural gas is renewable or non-renewable and describe one advantage and one disadvantage to its use. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. $\ce{CH3CH2OH(l) + 3 O2(g) -> 2 CO2(g) + 3 H2O(g)}\,\checkmark$

    *Do **not** award M1 if the state symbols are missing or incorrect;\
    Do **not** award M1 if the incomplete reaction products (CO and/or C (s) are included);\
    Accept $\ce{C2H5OH}$ or $\ce{C2H6O}$ for ethanol.*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    A complete combustion results in $\ce{CO2}$ as a product, as opposed to CO or C soot as incomplete combustion products.

2.  $10\,\text{dm}^3\,\ce{CO2}\times \dfrac{1\,\text{mol}}{22.7\,\text{dm}^3}\times \dfrac{1\,\text{mol}\,\ce{CH3CH2OH}}{2\,\text{mol}\,\ce{CO2}} = 0.22\,\text{mol}\,\ce{CH3CH2OH}$

    $0.22\,\text{mol}\,\ce{CH3CH2OH}\times 1367\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1} = 301\,\text{kJ}\,\checkmark$

    *Award M2 if the energy is written as a negative or a positive value in the final answer.*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    Since the question states that the reaction is at STP, 22.7 dm$^3$ mol$^{-1}$ can be used to calculate the moles of $\ce{CO2}$ (g).

    Using the mole ratio of 1 mol of $\ce{CH3CH2OH}$ per 2 mol of $\ce{CO2}$, the moles of $\ce{CH3CH2OH}$ are then calculated. Multiplying the moles by the enthalpy of combustion results in the final answer.

    $\underline{\textrm{Formulas}}$:

    $moles = \dfrac{\text{Volume}}{\text{Molar volume}} = \dfrac{V}{22.7\,\text{dm}^3\,\text{mol}^{-1}}$

    Energy $q = moles\times molar\ enthalpy = n \times \Delta H$

3.  $301\,\text{kJ}\times \dfrac{1\,\text{mol}}{891\,\text{kJ}} = 0.34\,\text{mol}\,\ce{CH4}\,\checkmark$

    $0.34\,\text{mol}\,\ce{CH4}\times 22.7\,\text{dm}^3\,\text{mol}^{-1} = 7.67\,\text{dm}^3\,\ce{CH4}\,\checkmark$

    $\dfrac{7.67\,\text{dm}^3}{70\%} = 11.0\,\text{dm}^3$ natural gas $\checkmark$

    *Award M3 for a range of 10.8 to 11.1 $dm^3$;\
    Allow ecf;\
    If 405 $kJ$ was used for the energy, then award M1, M2, and M3 for: $0.45 \ mol$ of $\ce{CH4}$, $10.3 \ dm^3 \ce{CH4}$ and $14.7 \ dm^3$ natural gas.*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    Since the question states that the reaction is at STP, $22.7\,\text{dm}^3\,\text{mol}^{-1}$ can be used to calculate the moles of $\ce{CH4(g)}$.

    $\underline{\textrm{Formulas}}$:

    $moles = \dfrac{energy}{molar\ enthalpy} = \dfrac{q}{\Delta H}$

    $volume =  moles\times molar\ volume = n\times 22.7\,\text{dm}^3\,\text{mol}^{-1}$

4.  methane  $\checkmark$

    Specific energy of ethanol = $\dfrac{1367\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}}{46.08\,\text{g mol}^{-1}} = 29.7\,\text{kJ g}^{-1}$

    Specific energy of methane = $\dfrac{891\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}}{16.04\,\text{g mol}^{-1} } = 55.5\,\text{kJ g}^{-1}$ $\checkmark$

    *Award M2 if both calculations are correct.*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    Specific energy is calculated by dividing the enthalpy of combustion by the molar mass of the fuel.

5.  non-renewable $\checkmark$

    Advantage:

    *Any one of*
    - Relatively clean (compared to other fuels);
    - High specific energy;
    - Can be compressed or liquified $\checkmark$

    Disadvantage:

    *Any one of*
    - Produces $\ce{CO2}$ when combusted;
    - Uneven global distribution;
    - Contributes to sulfur acid precipitation when combusted;
    - Contributes to nitrogen acid precipitation when combusted $\checkmark$

    *Award M2 for a correct advantage **and** disadvantage;\
    Do not accept non-renewable energy sources as a disadvantage.*
