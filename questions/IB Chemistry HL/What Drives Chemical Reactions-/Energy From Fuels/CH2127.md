# CH2127

What is the correct oxidation half equation in the methanol fuel cell?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3OH(aq) + H2O(l) -> CO2(g) + 6 H+(aq) + 6 e-}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CO2(g) + 6 H+(aq) + 6 e- -> CH3OH(aq) + H2O(l)}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{O2(g) + 4 H+(aq) + 4 e- -> 2 H2O(l)}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{2 H2O(l) -> O2(g) + 4 H+(aq) + 4 e-}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand that methanol produces electrical energy in methanol fuel cells. Methanol is oxidized at the anode and burnt to carbon dioxide, as shown below.

$\ce{CH3OH(aq) + H2O(l) -> CO2(g) + 6 H+(aq) + 6 e-}$

At the cathode, oxygen from the air acts as an oxidizing agent and is reduced to water.

This question is categorized as easy as the student must understand the role of methanol as a fuel in the methanol fuel cell.
