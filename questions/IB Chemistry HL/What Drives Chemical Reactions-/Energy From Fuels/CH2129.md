# CH2129

Which statement about hydrogen fuel cells is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The electric current is obtained from hydrogen oxidation, and electrons flow from anode to cathode.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The electric current is obtained from oxygen oxidation in the air, and electrons flow from anode to cathode.

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The electric current is obtained from hydrogen oxidation, and electrons flow from cathode to anode.

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The electric current is obtained from oxygen oxidation in the air, and electrons flow from cathode to anode.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand the functioning of the hydrogen fuel cell. In this cell, hydrogen is used as the source of electrons. Hydrogen undergoes an oxidation at the anode so that hydrogen ions are produced. These electrons produce an electric current to the cathode, where reduction occurs, and the oxidizing agent, oxygen from the air, gets reduced.

This question is categorized as easy as the student must understand the electron flow in the hydrogen fuel cell.
