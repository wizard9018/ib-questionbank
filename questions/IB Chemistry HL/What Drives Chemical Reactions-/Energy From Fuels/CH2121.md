# CH2121

Which chemical reaction absorbs energy from the surroundings?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{N2(g) + 2 O2(g) -> 2 NO2(g)}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{2 Mg(s) + O2(g) -> 2 MgO(s)}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{S(s) + O2(g) -> SO2(g)}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{C2H5OH(l) + 3 O2(g) -> 2 CO2(g) + 3 H2O(l)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must remember that most combustions (including magnesium, sulfur and ethanol) are exothermic but the reaction of nitrogen gas with oxygen gas is endothermic. This is why $\ce{NO_x}$ are formed at the high temperatures inside internal combustion engines.

This question is categorized as hard as this exception is often overlooked.
