# CH2132

Which chemical reaction has the greatest increase in entropy?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}\ce{N2}\textrm{(g)}+3 \ce{\,H2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2 \ce{\,NH3}\textrm{(g)}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}2 \ce{\,SO2}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{O2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2 \ce{\,SO3}\textrm{(g)}$
-   C.$\hspace{1.em}\ce{CuSO4}\textrm{(aq)}  + \ce{Zn}\textrm{(s)} \longrightarrow \ce{ZnSO4}\textrm{(aq)}+\ce{Cu}\textrm{(s)}$
-   D.$\hspace{1.em}\ce{N2O4}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow \ce{N2}\textrm{(g)} + 2 \ce{\,O2}\textrm{(g)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation: }}$\
Entropy reflects the degree of disorder or dispersal of energy in a system. For substances at the same temperature, gases are far more disordered than liquids or solids, so $S_{gas} \gg S_{liquid} > S_{solid}.$ When the total number of gaseous moles decreases during a reaction, entropy decreases; when it increases, entropy increases.

In the formation of ammonia, $\ce{N2}\textrm{(g)}+3 \ce{\,H2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2 \ce{\,NH3}\textrm{(g)}$, four moles of gas become  two moles of gas, and the entropy is reduced.

In the synthesis of sulfur trioxide, $2 \ce{\,SO2}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{O2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2 \ce{\,SO3}\textrm{(g)}$, three moles of gas are converted to two moles of gas, and the entropy is reduced.

There is no significant change in entropy in the single replacement reaction, $\ce{CuSO4}\textrm{(aq)}  + \ce{Zn}\textrm{(s)} \longrightarrow \ce{ZnSO4}\textrm{(aq)}+\ce{Cu}\textrm{(s)}$, so the entropy change will be small.

The decomposition of dinitrogen tetroxide, $\ce{N2O4}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow \ce{N2}\textrm{(g)} + 2 \ce{\,O2}\textrm{(g)}$, converts one mole of gas into three moles of gas. As the amount of gas increases, so does the entropy.

This question is categorized as easy: The student must identify the reaction presenting the greatest increase in its entropy.
