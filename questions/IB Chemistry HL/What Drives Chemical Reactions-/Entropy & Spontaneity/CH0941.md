# CH0941

Which option correctly states the sign of $\Delta G^\ominus$ and the spontaneity for the reduction of a mole of iron oxide at a high temperature?

$\mbox{}\\[-30pts]$

$\Delta H^\ominus = + 98.8 \textrm{\ kJ}, \ \ \ \ \  \Delta S^\ominus = 1141.5 \textrm{\ J K}^{-1}$

$\mbox{}\\[-20pts]$

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  | | $\mathbf{\Delta G^{\ominus}}$ | Reaction |
  | :-- | :-: |:-: |
  | A. | $\qquad$ negative $\qquad$| spontaneous |
  | B. | positive | $\quad$nonspontaneous $\quad$|
  | C. | positive | spontaneous |
  | D. | negative | nonspontaneous |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

$\Delta G^\ominus = \Delta H^\ominus - T \Delta S^\ominus$

At a high temperature, with  $\Delta H^\ominus > 0$ and  $\Delta S^\ominus > 0$, $\Delta G^\ominus$ will be $< 0$.

When $\Delta G^\ominus$ is negative, the reaction will be spontaneous.
