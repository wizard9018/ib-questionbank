# CH0178

Which of the following statements about entropy is **INCORRECT**?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Entropy measures how disordered a system is

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Solids have higher entropy than liquids

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Gases have higher entropy than solids

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The entropy of a system increases with an increase in the total number of particles

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Liquids are more disordered than solids and so have a higher entropy.
