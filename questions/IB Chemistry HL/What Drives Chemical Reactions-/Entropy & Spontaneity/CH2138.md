# CH2138

Consider the information below for a reversible reaction.

|Temperature / K|$\mathbf{\Delta G}$/ kJ mol$^\mathbf{-1}$
:-:|:-:|
300|6.78
400|4.79

What can be concluded about the reaction?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta S>0$ and $\Delta H>0$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta S<0$ and $\Delta H>0$
-   C.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta S<0$ and $\Delta H<0$
-   D.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta S>0$ and $\Delta H<0$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The student must recall the equation $\Delta G^{\minuso}=\Delta H^{\minuso}-T\Delta S^{\minuso}.$

When $\Delta G^{\minuso}$ is plotted against $T,$ the y$-$intercept is $\Delta H^{\minuso}$ and the gradient is $-\Delta S^{\minuso}.$ With the data in the table,

![CH2138](images/CH2138.png)

The gradient is negative and the y$-$intercept is positive. We can conclude that $\Delta H^{\minuso}>0$ and $\Delta S^{\minuso}>0.$

This question is categorized as hard as the student must understand the trend and identify the sign for the standard entropy and enthalpy changes.
