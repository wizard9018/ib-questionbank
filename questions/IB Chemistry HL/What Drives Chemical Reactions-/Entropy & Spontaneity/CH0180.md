# CH0180

How does Gibbs free energy relate to chemical equilibrium?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$At equilibrium, ${G}$ is a minimum

- B.$\hspace{1em}$At equilibrium, ${G} = 0$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$At equilibrium, ${G} > 0$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$At equilibrium, ${G} < 0$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$G$ becomes more negative as a spontaneous reaction proceeds until it reaches its minimum. At this point, there will be no net change, and the system has reached equilibrium. $\Delta G$ will equal zero but $G$ will not.
