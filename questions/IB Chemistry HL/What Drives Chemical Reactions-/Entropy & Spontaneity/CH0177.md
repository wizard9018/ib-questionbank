# CH0177

What is the value of $\Delta G$ in $\textrm{kJ mol}^{-1}$ for a reaction with an enthalpy change of $-100 \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}$ and an entropy change of $100\textrm{\ J K}^{-1}\textrm{mol}^{-1}$ at 10 $^\circ \mathrm{C}$?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}-128.3$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}-1100$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}-12830$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}-101$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\Delta G = \Delta H - T \Delta S$

with $\Delta H = -100\textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}$, $\Delta S = 0.1  \textrm{\ kJ K}^{-1}\textrm{mol}^{-1},$ and $T = 283 \textrm{\ K.}$
