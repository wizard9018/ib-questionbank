# CH0183

**Maximum mark: 6**

The Gibbs free energy change can be used to determine the spontaneity of a reaction.

1.
    1. Define Gibbs free energy change of formation. [1 mark]

    2. Define spontaneous reaction. [1 mark]

2.
    1. State a correctly balanced equation that represents the complete combustion of butane. [2 marks]

    2. Using section 12 (*Section 13- 2025 Syllabus*) of the data booklet, calculate the Gibbs free energy change of this reaction. [1 mark]

    3. State, with a reason, whether this reaction is spontaneous or not. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  It is the free energy change when one mole of a compound is formed from its constituent elements in their standard states, under standard conditions at $298\,\text{K}$ and $100\,\text{kPa}\,\checkmark$

    2.  A reaction that proceeds **without any further energy input** so long as the activation energy is met.

        **OR**

        a reaction is said to be spontaneous when it moves towards either completion or equilibrium under a given set of conditions **without external intervention**. $\checkmark$

2.  1.  $\ce{C4H10(g) + \dfrac{13}{2} (g) -> 4 CO2(g) + 5 H2O(l)}\,\checkmark$

        *Correct equation with states;* $\checkmark$ \
        *Fully balanced* (*accept* $\ce{2C4H10(g) + 13 O2(g) -> 8 CO2(g) + 10 H2O(l)}$)

        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

        Complete combustion is the reaction of a substance with excess oxygen to form liquid water and carbon dioxide.  The enthalpy of combustion, entropy change of combustion, of Gibbs free energy change of combustion are defined as combusting 1 mole of the compound.

    2.  $\mbox{}\\[-10pts]
        \begin{aligned}
        \Delta G_{reaction} &= \sum \Delta G_{products} - \sum \Delta G_{reactants} \\
        \Delta G^\ominus_{reaction} &= 4\Delta G^\ominus_f(\ce{CO2}) + 5\Delta G^\ominus_f(\ce{H2O(l)}) - \Delta G^\ominus_f(\ce{C4H10}) \\
    &= 4 (- 394.4) + 5(- 237.1) + 17.0\\
    &= -2746.1\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}\,\checkmark
    \end{aligned}$

    *Allow error carried forward from b(i). If the coefficients were doubled, $\Delta G = -5492.2\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}$*.

    3.  The Gibbs free energy change is negative which means this reaction is spontaneous. $\checkmark$
