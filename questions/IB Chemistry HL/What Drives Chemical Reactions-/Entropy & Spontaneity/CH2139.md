# CH2139

Which statement is correct for a system at equilibrium?

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta G = 0$

-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}K=Q$
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$The concentrations of reactants and products are equal.

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$I only

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$I and II only
-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$II and III only
-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The student must understand the equilibrium conditions and their relationship with the change in the Gibbs free energy.

At equilibrium, the rates of the forward and reverse reaction are equal, so the concentrations of reactants and products are constant. However, the concentrations of the reactants and products do not have to be equal, statement III is incorrect.

At equilibrium, the reaction quotient $Q$ equals the equilibrium constant $K$, statement II is correct.

Finally, at equilibrium, the reaction is not spontaneous in the forward or reverse direction so $\Delta G = 0,$ statement I is correct.

This question is categorized as medium as the student must relate Gibbs free energy changes with equilibrium conditions.
