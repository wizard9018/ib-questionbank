# CH2137

Which conditions allow us to determine the spontaneity of a chemical reaction at constant pressure at any given temperature?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta G>0$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta H>0$   and $\Delta S>0$
-   C.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta H<0$   and $\Delta S>0$
-   D.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta H>0$  and $\Delta S<0$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\
The student must understand that, at constant pressure, a reaction is spontaneous when its change in Gibbs free energy change is negative.

Because $\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S,$ any reaction with a negative $\Delta H$ (exothermic) and a positive $\Delta S$ (greater disorder) will have a negative $\Delta G$ at all temperatures, guaranteeing spontaneity.

This question is categorized as medium as the student must understand the spontaneity criteria and apply the equation ∆G=∆H-T∆S.
