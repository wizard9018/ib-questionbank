# CH0901

$K > 1$ at low temperatures and $K < 1$ at high temperatures for a reversible, spontaneous reaction.

What are the correct signs of $\Delta H$ and $\Delta S$ for this reaction?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  | | $\mathbf{\Delta H}$ | $\mathbf{\Delta S}$ |
  | :-: | :-: | :-: |
  | A. | Positive | Positive |
  | B. | Negative | Negative |
  | C. | Positive | Negative |
  | D. | Negative | Positive |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Reactions are spontaneous when $\Delta G$ is negative.

When $\Delta G$ is negative, $K > 1$.

Using $\Delta G = \Delta H - T \Delta S$, for a negative $\Delta H$ and a negative $\Delta S$, $\Delta G$ is negative at low temperatures ($- (-T \Delta S)$ value is small).
