# CH0175

Which of the following statements about spontaneous reactions is correct?

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$The $\Delta G$ for the reaction is negative.
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The reaction proceeds without any further energy input if the activation energy is met.
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$The reaction results with an increase in the total entropy of the system and surroundings.

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
All three statements are correct descriptions of a spontaneous reaction.

Statement I is correct. A negative $\Delta G$ is the thermodynamic criterion for spontaneity, defined by the relationship $\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S.$ When $\Delta G < 0,$ the forward reaction is thermodynamically favoured.

Statement II is correct. A spontaneous reaction is one that can proceed on its own once the activation energy barrier has been overcome. Spontaneity refers to the thermodynamic tendency of a reaction to occur, not whether it happens instantly or without any initial energy input.

Statement III is correct. For any spontaneous process, the total entropy of the universe (system + surroundings) must increase. This is a statement of the second law of thermodynamics and is equivalent to the condition $\Delta G < 0,$ since $\Delta G = -T\Delta S_{\textrm{total}}.$
