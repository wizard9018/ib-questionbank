# CH0179

Which statement is true for an endothermic reaction that results in an increase in entropy?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The reaction is always spontaneous

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The reaction is never spontaneous

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The reaction is spontaneous at high temperatures

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The reaction is spontaneous at low temperatures

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\Delta G = \Delta H - T \Delta S$, and reactions are spontaneous if $\Delta G$ is negative.

In endothermic reactions, $\Delta H$ is positive, and if entropy increases, $\Delta S$ is also positive.

At high temperatures, $T\Delta S > \Delta H$ resulting in a negative $\Delta G$ value.
