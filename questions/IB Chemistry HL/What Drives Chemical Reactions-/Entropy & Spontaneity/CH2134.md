# CH2134

Airbags explode when the reaction below is triggered.

$2 \ce{\,NaN3}\textrm{(s)} \longrightarrow 2 \ce{\,Na}\textrm{(s)} + 3 \ce{\,N2}\textrm{(g)}$

Which statement is correct when the reaction occurs?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

|$\hspace{0.95em}$||$\hspace{0.1em}$|Entropy of the system|Entropy of the universe
:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
||A.||increases|increases|
||B.||decreases|decreases|
||C.||decreases|increases|
||D.||increases|decreases|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\
The student must identify that the entropy of the system increases as two moles of a solid are converted into two moles of solid and three moles of gas. The disorder in the gas is greater, so the system's entropy increases.

The Second Law of Thermodynamics states that for any spontaneous process $\Delta S_{univ} = \Delta S_{sys} + \Delta S_{surr} > 0.$  Here $\Delta S_{sys}$ is already positive, and the huge exothermic enthalpy release warms the surroundings, adding further disorder, $\Delta S_{surr} > 0.$  Consequently, $\Delta S_{univ}$ is unambiguously positive.

This question is categorized as hard as the student must recall the second law of thermodynamics and deduce the entropy change for this chemical reaction.
