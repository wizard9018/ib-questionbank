# CH0182

**Maximum mark: 6**

Dry ice, solid $\ce{CO2}$, is commonly used as a cooling or freezing agent in the medical and food industries.

1.  Write an equation representing the sublimation of $\ce{CO2}$. [1 mark]

2.  The enthalpy of sublimation for $\ce{CO2}$ is +26.1 kJ mol$^{-1}$ and the absolute entropy of $\ce{CO2}$ in the solid state is 51.1 J K$^{-1}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$.

    1.  Using section 12 (*Section 13- 2025 Syllabus*) of the data booklet, calculate the entropy change associated with the sublimation of $\ce{CO2}$. [1 mark]

    2.  Using your answer to (bi), calculate the Gibbs free energy change for the sublimation of $\ce{CO2}$ at $298\,\text{K}$. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the minimum temperature at which $\ce{CO2}$ sublimates. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\ce{CO2(s) -> CO2(g)}\,\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    Sublimation is the change of state from solid to gas.

2.
    1.  $\mbox{}\\[-10pts]\begin{aligned}
    \Delta S_{reaction} &= \sum \Delta S_{products} - \sum \Delta S_{reactants} \\
    \Delta S &= S^\ominus(\ce{CO2(g)}) - S^\ominus((\ce{CO2(s)}) \\
    &= 213.8 - 51.1 \\
    &= +162.7\,\text{J K}^{-1}\,\text{mol}^{-1}\,\checkmark
    \end{aligned}$

    2.  $\mbox{}\\[-10pts]\begin{aligned}
    \Delta G &= \Delta H - T\Delta S \\
    &= +26.1\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1} - (298\,\text{K})(0.1627\,\text{kJ K}^{-1}\,\text{mol}^{-1}) \\
    &=- 22.4\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}\,\checkmark\,\checkmark
    \end{aligned}$

    *Two marks for correct answer and working;*\
    *One mark if one error is made.*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    $\Delta S$ must be divided by 1000 to convert it to kJ K$^{-1}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$ in line with the kJ mol$^{-1}$ units for $\Delta H$.

3.  To find the minimum temperature at which $\ce{CO2}$ sublimates, we need to find the temperature at which the reaction is no longer spontaneous, i.e. when $\Delta G = 0\, \checkmark$

    $\begin{aligned}
    \Delta G &= \Delta H - T\Delta S\\
    T &= \dfrac{\Delta H - \Delta G}{\Delta S} \\
    &= \dfrac{26.1-0}{\left(\dfrac{162.7}{1000}\right)} = 160\,\text{K} \\
    T &>  160\,\text{K}\, \checkmark
    \end{aligned}$
