## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The combustion of a hydrocarbon is exothermic, so $\Delta H < 0$. Three moles of gas are present on the reactant side, and only two moles appear on the product side so $\Delta S < 0$.   Considering the relationship $\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S,$ as the equation for a line $y = mx+b$, the gradient is  $-\Delta S$ and the $y-$intercept is $\Delta H$.  The correct graph must have a negative $y-$intercept and a positive gradient.
