# CH0441

The equilibrium constant for a reversible reaction is 100 at 700 K. Which of the following statements is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The Gibbs free energy is zero

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Increasing the temperature will increase the equilibrium constant

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Increasing the temperature will decrease the equilibrium constant

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The standard Gibbs free energy change is negative

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\Delta G = -RT \ln K$

$\ln K$ is positive for values of $K$ greater than 1, and therefore, $\Delta G$ will be negative when $K = 100$.

Choice A is incorrect as the Gibbs free energy is at a minimum not zero.

The question does not state whether the reaction is exothermic or endothermic, and therefore, conclusions cannot be made about how temperature would affect the equilibrium constant.
