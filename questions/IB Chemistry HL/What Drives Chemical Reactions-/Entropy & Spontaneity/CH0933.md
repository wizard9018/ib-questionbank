# CH0933

Given the below chemical equation, what can be deduced about the production of ammonia?

$\ce{N2(g) + 3 H2(g) <=> 2 NH3(g)}\qquad \Delta G =  -33\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}$

$\ $
$\mbox{}\\[-20pts]$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$The forward reaction is spontaneous.

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$The backward reaction is spontaneous.

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$When the system reaches equilibrium, $\Delta G$ is at its maximum.

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$The equilibrium lies closer to the reactants.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\
Since $\Delta G$ is a negative value, we can deduce that the forward reaction is spontaneous. It also indicates that the position of equilibrium lies closer to the products than the reactants. When a system is at equilibrium, $\Delta G$ is at a minimum and entropy is at its maximum.
