# CH2140

Which combination is correct for a reaction at 298 K?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

|$\hspace{0.95em}$||$\hspace{0.1em}$|$\quad $Equilibrium Constant$\quad $|$\mathbf{\Delta G^{\mathbf{\minuso}}}$|
:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
||A.||$K>1$|$\qquad \Delta G^{\minuso} = 0\qquad $|
||B.||$K>1$|$\Delta G^{\minuso} < 0$|
||C.||$K<1$|$\Delta G^{\minuso} < 0$|
||D.||$K<1$|$\Delta G^{\minuso} = 0$|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\
The relationship $\Delta G^{\minuso} = - RT \ln K$ shows that when $K > 1$, $\ln K > 0$ so $\Delta G^{\minuso}<0$.
