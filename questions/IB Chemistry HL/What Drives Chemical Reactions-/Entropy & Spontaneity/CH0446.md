# CH0446

**Maximum mark: 6**

Methanol is produced by reacting carbon monoxide and hydrogen according to the following reaction:
$\ \newline \ $

$\ce{CO(g) + 2 H2(g) <=> CH3OH(g)}$

1.  Deduce the equilibrium constant expression for the formation of methanol. [1 mark]

2.  At 500$\,{}^\circ \mathrm{C}$, a 750 cm$^3$ sealed flask is found to contain 0.923 mol of $\ce{CH3OH}, 0.352\, \text{mol}$ of $\ce{CO}$ and 0.124 mol of $\ce{H2}$.

    1.  Calculate the value of $K$ at $500\,{}^\circ \mathrm{C}$. [2 marks]

    2.  Using sections 1 and 2 of the data booklet, calculate $\Delta G^\ominus$ at $500\,{}^\circ \mathrm{C}$. [3 marks]

    $\ $

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $K = \dfrac{[\ce{CH3OH}]}{[\ce{CO}][\ce{H2}]^2} \: \checkmark $

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
    For the reaction $\ce{a A +b B <=> c C + d D}$,

    $K_c =\dfrac{[C]^c[D]^d}{[A]^a[B]^b}$

2.   1.  $[\ce{CH3OH}] = \dfrac{0.923\textrm{\ mol}}{0.750\textrm{\ dm}^3} = 1.23 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$\
        $\mbox{}\\[-10pts]$
        $[\textrm{CO}] = \dfrac{0.352\textrm{\ mol}}{0.750\textrm{\ dm}^3} = 0.469 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3}$\
        **AND**\
        $[\ce{H2}] = \dfrac{0.124\textrm{\ mol}}{0.750\textrm{\ dm}^3} = 0.165 \textrm{\ mol dm}^{-3} \: \checkmark$\
        $\mbox{}\\[-5pts]$
        $K = \dfrac{1.23}{0.469 \times 0.165^2} = 95.9 \:\checkmark$\
        $\mbox{}\\[-10pts]$
        *Award [2] for final answer.*

            $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
            The concentrations of each gas must be calculated in $\textrm{ mol dm}^{-3}$ as

            $C = \dfrac{n}{V}$

            and then substituted into the expression in (a).

        2.  $T = 500 + 273 = 773\, \text{K} \checkmark$\
            $\Delta G^\ominus =-RT \ln K=-8.31 \times 773 \ln 95.9 \: \checkmark$\
            $\Delta G^{\ominus} = -29313\,\text{J mol}^{-1} = -29.3\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}\, \checkmark$

            $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
            The units for $R$ are $\textrm{\ J K}^{-1} \textrm{mol}^{-1}.$
