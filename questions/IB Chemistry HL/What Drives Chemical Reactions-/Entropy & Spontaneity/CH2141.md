# CH2141

Which of these have a $\Delta S>0?$

-   I.$\hspace{1.em}$The deposition of water
-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$The vaporization of ethanol
-   III.$\hspace{0.4em}$The combustion of magnesium

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$I, II and III

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$I and II only
-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$II only
-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
 The student must identify that the deposition of water involves $\ce{H2O}\textrm{(g)}\longrightarrow\ce{H2O}\textrm{(s)}.$ This change involves a decrease in the entropy as the system is getting more orders as the gas is converted into a solid.

The vaporization of ethanol, $\ce{C2H5OH}\textrm{(l)} \longrightarrow \ce{C2H5OH}\textrm{(g)}$, changes a mole of liquid into a mole of gas. The disorder increases and $\Delta S>0.$

Finally, the combustion of magnesium, $2 \ce{\,Mg}\textrm{(s)} + \ce{O2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2 \ce{\,MgO}\textrm{(s)}.$ There is an increase in order in this system as the number of moles of gas is one on the reactant side and zero on the product side. The system is getting ordered and the entropy change would be negative.

This question is categorized as medium as the student must interpret the different processes and associate the entropy changes with these.
