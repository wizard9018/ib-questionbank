# CH0174

Which of the following compounds are arranged in order of decreasing entropy?

-   A.$\hspace{1em} \ce{C(s) > H2O(l) > O2(g) > CO2(g)}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em} \ce{CO2(g) > O2(g) > H2O(l) > C(s)}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em} \ce{H2O(l) > C(s) > CO2(g) > O2(g)}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em} \ce{C(s) > O2(g) > CO2(l) > H2O(l)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Gases have the highest entropy followed by liquids and then solids.

$\ce{CO2}$ has a higher entropy than $\ce{O2}$ because it has more bonds, and therefore, more vibration modes.
