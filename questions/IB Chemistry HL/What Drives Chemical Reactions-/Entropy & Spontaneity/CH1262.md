# CH1262

Consider the chemical reaction below.

$\ce{2 Mg(s) + O2(g) -> 2 MgO(s) + heat}$

Which statements are correct?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | It is a combustion reaction   |
  | II. | The entropy of the system decreases |
  | III. | The entropy of the surroundings increases|

- A. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Magnesium burns in oxygen, and this chemical reaction is classified as a combustion. Reactive metals such as magnesium undergo combustion reactions when heated with oxygen. Statement I is correct.

Entropy refers to the distribution of available energy among the particles. The greater the number of possibilities in the energy distribution, the higher the entropy.

The entropy order of gas > liquid > solid at the same conditions.

If we consider the system, the gas consumption should lead us to expect a decrease in the system's entropy. Statement II is correct.

Moreover, this reaction releases a lot of heat, increasing the entropy of the surroundings. Statement III is correct.

This question is categorized as easy as students must interpret the entropy changes in the system and the surroundings and recognize this chemical reaction as combustion.
