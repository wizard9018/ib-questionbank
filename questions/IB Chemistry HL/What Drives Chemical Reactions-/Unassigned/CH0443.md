# CH0443

The equilibrium constant for the following reaction is 0.21 at 100 $^\circ \mathrm{C}$. Which expression represents the Gibbs free energy change at this temperature in $\textrm{\ kJ\, mol}^{-1}?$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}-8.31 \times (100+273) \times \ln 0.21$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}-8.31 \times 100 \times \ln 0.21$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}-\dfrac{8.31\times (100+273) \times\ln 0.21}{1000}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}-\dfrac{8.31 \times 100 \times \ln 0.21}{1000}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$T$ must be in Kelvin: $T =  100 + 273.$

As the units for $R$ are  $\textrm{J\,K}^{-1} \textrm{\,mol}^{-1}$, the answer must be divided by 1000 to give a $\Delta G$ value with the units $\textrm{kJ\, mol}^{-1}$.
