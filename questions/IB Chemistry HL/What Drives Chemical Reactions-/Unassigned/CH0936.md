# CH0936

Which of these is an exothermic process?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Na2O(s) -> 2 Na+(g) + O^2-(g)}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Na(g) -> Na+(g) + e-}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{O(g) + e- -> O-(g)}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{O-(g) + e- -> O^2-(g)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\
$\ce{Na2O(s) -> 2 Na+(g) + O^2-(g)}$:

-   Breaking apart the lattice into gas phase ions requires energy, the process is endothermic.

$\ce{Na(g) -> Na+(g) + e-}$:

-   The ionization energy is endothermic.  Energy is required to remove an electron from all atoms because the electrons are attracted to the positive nucleus.

$\ce{O}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{e-} \longrightarrow  \ce{O-}\textrm{(g)}$:

-   The first electron affinity for oxygen is  exothermic as it places a free electron into the $2p$ orbital where it experiences the attractive force of the nucleus.

$\ce{O-}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{e-} \longrightarrow \ce{O^2-}\textrm{(g)}$:

-   The second electron affinity, on the other hand, requires adding an electron to an already negatively charged ion where it feels repulsion. This is endothermic.
