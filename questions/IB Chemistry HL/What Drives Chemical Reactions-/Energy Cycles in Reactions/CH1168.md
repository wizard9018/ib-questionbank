# CH1168

What is the enthalpy change in $\text{kJ mol}^{-1}$ for the chemical reaction below?

$\ce{N2(g) + 3 H2(g) -> 2 NH3(g)}$

| Bond | Bond Enthalpy $\mathbf{(kJ\ mol^{-1})}$
| :-: | :-: |
| $\ce{N#N}$ | 945 |
| $\ce{H-H}$ | 436 |
| $\ce{N-H}$ | 391 |

- A. $\hspace{1em}- 945 - 3 \times 436 + 6 \times 391$

- B. $\hspace{1em}945 + 436 - 391$

- C. $\hspace{1em}945 + 3 \times 436 - 2 \times 391$

- D. $\hspace{1em}945 + 3 \times 436 - 6 \times 391$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The bonds in the reactants and products for this chemical reaction are shown below:

$\ce{N2(g) + 3 H2(g) -> 2 NH3(g)}$

![CH1168](images/CH1168.png)

$\Delta H = \sum BE(broken) - \sum BE(formed)$

$\begin{aligned}
\Delta H &= \ce{N#N} + 3 \ce{H-H} - 6 \ce{N-H} \\
&= 945 + 3 \times 436 - 6 \times 391
\end{aligned}$

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as Lewis structures must be deduced and average bond enthalpies should be correctly applied.
