# CH0316

What is the enthalpy change for the reaction below in kJ mol$^{-1}$?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{CH4(g) + 2 O2(g) -> CO2(g) + 2 H2O(g)}$ 

|$\hspace{1em}$ Bond $\hspace{1em}$|Bond enthalpy (kJ mol$^\mathbf{-1})$ |

|:-:|:-:|

|$\ce{C-H}$|414 |

|$\ce{O=O}$|498 |

|$\ce{C=O}$|804 |

|$\ce{O-H}$|463 |

-   A.$\hspace{1em}4(414) + 2(498) - 2(804) - 4(463)$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}4(414) + 2(498) - 804 - 2(463)$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}4(414) + 4(498) - 804 - 2(463)$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}4(414) + 4(498) - 2(804) - 4(463)$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

In this reaction, four $\ce{C-H}$ bonds and two $\ce{O=O}$ bonds are broken. Two $\ce{C=O}$ and four $\ce{O-H}$ bonds are formed.

$\Delta H_{rxn} =\sum \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}(\textrm{broken}) - \sum \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}(\textrm{formed})$

$\Delta H_{rxn} = 4 D(\ce{C-H})+ 2 D(\ce{O=O}) - \left[ 2 D(\ce{C=O}) + 4D(\ce{O-H)}\right] $

$\Delta H_{rxn} = 4(414) + 2(498) - 2(804) - 4(463)$
