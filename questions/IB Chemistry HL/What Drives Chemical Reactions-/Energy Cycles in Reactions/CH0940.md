# CH0940

Which is the best way to arrange these metal halides in increasing order of lattice enthalpy?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{LiI < KI < SrO < MgO}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{KI < LiI < SrO < MgO}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{SrO < MgO < LiI < KI}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MgO < SrO < LiI < KI}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

This can be deduced based on charge and size of ions.

Magnesium and strontium ions both have a $2+$ charge, but the smaller radius of the magnesium ion gives it a higher lattice enthalpy.

Between $\ce{LiI}$ and $\ce{KI}$, the lithium ion is smaller therefore, its lattice enthalpy is higher. Smaller ions form stronger lattices. Higher charges form strong lattices.
