# CH0305

Using the table below, choose the correct expression that represents the enthalpy change for the following hydrogenation reaction in $\textrm{kJ mol}^{-1}:$

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{C2H4 (g) + H2 (g) -> C2H6(g)}$

- :::tableoptions
  |$\quad$Substance $\quad$ |$\mathbf{\Delta H_f^{\minuso}}$ $\textbf{(kJ mol}^\mathbf{-1}\textbf{})$ |
  | :-: | :-: |
  |$\ce{C2H4 (g)}$| $x$ |
  |$\ce{C2H6(g)}$| $y$ |

- A.$\hspace{1em}-x + y $

- B.$\hspace{1em}x + y$

- C.$\hspace{1em}-2x + y $
- D.$\hspace{1em}-2x - 2y $

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

By applying Hess's Law and the enthalpy changes of ethene and ethane.

$\begin{aligned}
\ce{2C + 2H2 &-> C2H4} \\
\ce{2C + 3H2 &-> C2H6} \\
\end{aligned}$

Combining the reverse of the first equation (formation of ethene) and the forwards second equation (formation of ethane) gives the enthalpy change for the reaction in question.

$\textbf{OR}$

$\Delta H^{\minuso}_r  = \sum \left[\Delta H^{\minuso}_f (\textrm{products})\right] - \sum \left[\Delta H^{\minuso}_f (\textrm{reactants})\right] = y - x$
