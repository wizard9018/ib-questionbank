# CH0321

**Maximum mark: 6**

Carbon monoxide is produced when there is insufficient oxygen in combustion reactions. It reacts with oxygen to produce carbon dioxide according to the following equation:

$\ce{CO(g) + 1/2 O2(g) -> CO2(g)}$

1.  Define enthalpy change of combustion. [1 mark]

2.  Using sections 11 and 13 (*Sections 12 and 14- 2025 Syllabus*) of the data booklet, calculate the average bond enthalpy for $\ce{C#O}$. [5 marks]

*Important note: \

The section numbers mentioned in the video solution are specific to the 2024 syllabus.\

You can refer to the question stem to find the section numbers for the 2025 syllabus.*

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The energy released when one mole of a substance is completely burned in excess oxygen under standard conditions. $\checkmark$

2.  $\Delta H = \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}(\ce{C#O}) + \dfrac{1}{2} \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}(\ce{O=O}) - 2 \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}(\ce{C=O})\,\checkmark$

    $\Delta H = \Delta H_c(\ce{CO})$ (*from section 13 of the data booklet $\Delta H_c(\ce{CO}) = -283\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}$*) $\checkmark$

    $\Delta H_c(\ce{CO}) = \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}(\ce{C#O}) + \dfrac{1}{2} \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}(\ce{O=O}) - 2 \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}(\ce{C=O})\,\checkmark$

    $\Delta H^\_{BE}(\ce{C#O}) = \Delta H_c(\ce{CO}) - \dfrac{1}{2} \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}(\ce{O=O}) + 2 \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}(\ce{C=O})\,\checkmark$

    $\Delta H^\ominus_{BE}(\ce{C#O}) = - 283 - \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)(498) + (2)(804) = 1076\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}\,\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

    $\Delta H = \sum \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}(\text{bonds broken}) - \sum \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}(\text{bonds formed})$
