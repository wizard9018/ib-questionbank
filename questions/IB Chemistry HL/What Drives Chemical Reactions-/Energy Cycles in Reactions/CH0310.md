# CH0310

Which equation represents the average bond enthalpy for the C$-$H bond in $\ce{CH4}$?

-   A.$\hspace{1em} \ce{CH3(g) -> C(g) + 4 H(g)}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em} \ce{CH4(g) -> CH3(g) + H(g)}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em} \ce{\frac{1}{4} CH4(g) -> \frac{1}{4} C(g) + H(g)}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em} \ce{CH4(g) -> CH3(g) + \frac{1}{2} H2(g)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\

The average bond enthalpy of a $\ce{C-H}$ bond is the enthalpy change per mole of $\ce{C-H}$ bonds broken when all bonds in the molecule are broken homolytically, producing gaseous atoms. For $\ce{CH4},$ this requires breaking all four $\ce{C-H}$ bonds:

$\ce{CH4(g) -> C(g) + 4H(g)}$

Dividing through by $4$ gives the enthalpy change for breaking one mole of $\ce{C-H}$ bonds on average:

$\ce{\frac{1}{4}\,CH4(g) -> \frac{1}{4}\,C(g) + H(g)}$
