# CH0318

Which enthalpy change cannot be calculated directly from average bond enthalpy data?

-   A.$\hspace{1em} \ce{N2(g) + 3 H2(g) -> 2 NH3(g)}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em} \ce{SO2(g) + 1/2 O2(g) -> SO3(g)}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em} \ce{CH2O(g) + O2(g) -> CO2(g) + H2O(l)}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em} \ce{CH4(g) + Cl2(g) -> CH3Cl(g) + HCl(g)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Average bond enthalpies give data for species in the gaseous phase. In choice C, water is a liquid and so the enthalpy change for the condensation of water vapour would also be needed.
