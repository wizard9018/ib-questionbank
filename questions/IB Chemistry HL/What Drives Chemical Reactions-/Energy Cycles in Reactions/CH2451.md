# CH2451

Consider the following reaction.

![CH2451](images/CH2451.png)

The average bond enthalpies are given below.

| Bond | Average bond enthalpy $\newline$ / kJ mol$^\mathbf{-1}$ |
|:-:|:-:|
| $\ce{X-Y}$ | $153$ |
| $\ce{X-X}$ | $200$ |
| $\ce{X#X}$ | $434$ |

What is the bond enthalpy for the $\ce{Y-Y}$ bond in $\textrm{kJ mol}^{-1}?$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}-324$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}324$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}-162$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}162$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The relationship

$\Delta H = \Sigma(\textrm{bonds broken}) - \Sigma(\textrm{bonds formed})$

can be used to find the bond enthalpy of the $\ce{Y-Y}$ bond.

The reactant contains one $\ce{X-X}$ bond and four $\ce{X-Y}$ bonds. The products contain one $\ce{X#X}$ triple bond and two $\ce{Y-Y}$ single bonds.

$\Delta H^{\minuso} = [\ce{X-X} + 4(\ce{X-Y})] - [\ce{X#X} + 2(\ce{Y-Y})]$

$2(\ce{Y-Y}) = [\ce{X-X} + 4(\ce{X-Y})] - \ce{X#X} - \Delta H^{\minuso}$

$2(\ce{Y-Y}) = [200 + 4(153)] - 434 - 54 $

$2(\ce{Y-Y}) = 812 - 434 - 54 = 324\textrm{ kJ mol}^{-1}$

$\ce{Y-Y} = 162\textrm{ kJ mol}^{-1}$

Bond enthalpies are always positive (energy is required to break bonds), so the two negative options can be eliminated immediately.
