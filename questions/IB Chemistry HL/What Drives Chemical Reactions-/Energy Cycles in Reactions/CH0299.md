# CH0299

What is the enthalpy $\Delta H_1$ in kJ mol$^{-1}$?

![5.2-299](images/5.2-299.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ +57.2 kJ mol$^{-1}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $-57.2$ kJ mol$^{-1}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $-28.6$ kJ mol$^{-1}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $-123.6$ kJ mol$^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

$\begin{aligned}
\Delta H^\ominus &= -\Delta H^\ominus_f(\ce{NO(g)}) + \Delta H^\ominus_f(\ce{NO2(g)}) \\
&= -(90.4) + (33.2) = -57.2\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1} \\
\end{aligned}$
