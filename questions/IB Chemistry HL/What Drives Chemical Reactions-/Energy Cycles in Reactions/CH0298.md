# CH0298

Which expression gives the enthalpy change, $\Delta H$, for the production of hydrated copper (II) sulfate from its anhydrous salt:

![5.2-298](images/5.2-298.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta H_1 = \Delta H_2 -\Delta H$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta H = \Delta H_1 -\Delta H_2$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta H = \Delta H_1 +\Delta H_2$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta H_2 = \Delta H_1 +\Delta H$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

By applying Hess's law,  regardless of the route by which a chemical reaction proceeds, the enthalpy change will always have the same, provided that the initial and final states of the system are identical.
