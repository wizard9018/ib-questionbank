# CH0213

Which of the following is correct in an endothermic reaction?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  | | Temperature of the surroundings | Energy stored in bonds |
  | --- | :---: | :---: |
  | $\mbox{A.}\hspace{1em}$ | increases | increases |
  | B. | increases | decreases |
  | C. | decreases | increases |
  | D. | decreases | decreases |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Endothermic reactions absorb energy from the surroundings, causing the temperature of the surroundings to decrease while the chemical potential energy of the system increases. This absorbed energy is stored within the bonds of the products.

The enthalpy change of a reaction is determined by comparing the total energy required to break bonds in the reactants with the total energy released when new bonds form:

$\Delta  H= \sum \textrm{bonds broken} - \sum \textrm{bonds formed}$

ΔH is positive because more energy is needed to break the bonds in the reactants than is released when the new bonds form. This means the bonds in the products are weaker, or they release less energy when formed and therefore hold more potential energy. In other words, energy that once existed as heat in the surroundings is now stored as chemical energy within the products.
