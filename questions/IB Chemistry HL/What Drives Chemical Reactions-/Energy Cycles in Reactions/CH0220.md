# CH0220

When a gas$-$phase reaction releases heat, this shows that:

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Bonds are broken only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$New bonds are formed and are stronger than bonds broken

- C.$\hspace{1em}$New bonds are formed and are weaker than bonds broken

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Bonds are formed only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Breaking bonds absorbs energy, forming bonds releases energy. If more energy is released in making new bonds than was taken in breaking the original ones, the reaction releases heat overall.

$\Delta H = \sum \textrm{bonds broken} - \sum \textrm{bonds formed}$

Since bond breaking is endothermic, for the enthalpy of the reaction to be negative, the strength of the bonds formed must be greater than the strength of the bonds broken.
