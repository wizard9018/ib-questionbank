# CH1532

Consider the chemical reaction described below.

2 Li(g) + O(g) $\longrightarrow$ 2 Li$^+$(g) + O$^{2-}$(g)

Which energy changes are associated with the chemical changes described in the chemical reaction above?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Atomization of two lithium atoms and first and second ionization energy of oxygen

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Ionization of two lithium atoms and first and second electron affinity of oxygen

- C.$\hspace{1em}$First electron affinity of two lithium atoms and first and second ionization energy of oxygen

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Ionization energy of two lithium ions and first and second ionization energy of oxygen

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

 

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\

 The student must understand that the chemical reaction described involved removing one electron from each lithium and adding two electrons to the oxygen.

The removal of the electrons in lithium is associated with the first ionization energy of lithium.

||||

:-: | :-: | :-: |

Li(g) $\longrightarrow$ Li$^+$(g) + e$^-$ |  $\hspace{2em}$| $IE_1(\textrm{Li})$

The addition of two electrons to the oxygen atom is associated with the first and second electron affinities of oxygen.

||||

:-: | :-: | :-: |

O(g) + e$^-$ $\longrightarrow$ O$^-$(g) |  $\hspace{2em}$| $EA_1(\textrm{O})$

O$^-$(g) + e$^-$ $\longrightarrow$ O$^{2-}$(g)  |$\hspace{2em}$ | $EA_2(\textrm{O})$

This question is categorized as a medium as the student must distinguish ionization energy from electron affinity and figure out the energies associated with the process described.
