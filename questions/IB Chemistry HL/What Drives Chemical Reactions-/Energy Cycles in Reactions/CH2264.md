# CH2264

Given the following thermochemical data

|Equation|$\mathbf{\Delta}$H$^{\circ}$ / kJ mol$^\mathbf{-1}$
:-:|:-:
$\ce{Si}\textrm{(s)} \longrightarrow \ce{Si}\textrm{(g)}$|$+454$
$\ce{Si}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow \ce{Si^{4+}}\textrm{(g)} + 4\ce{\,e^-}$|$+9949$
$\ce{O2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2 \ce{\,O}\textrm{(g)}$|$+498$
$\ce{O}\textrm{(g)} + 2\ce{\,e-} \longrightarrow \ce{O^{2-}}\textrm{(g)}$ |$+737$
Si(s)+O2​(g)⟶SiO2​(s) | $-911$

which expression correctly represents the standard lattice enthalpy $(\Delta H_{lattice}^{\minuso})$ of $\ce{SiO2}?$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta H_{lattice}^{\minuso} = 911+454+9949+\frac{1}{2}(498)+737 $

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta H_{lattice}^{\minuso} =911+454+9949+498+2(737) $

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta H_{lattice}^{\minuso} =-911+454+9949+\frac{1}{2}(498)+737$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta H_{lattice}^{\minuso} =-911+454+9949+498+2(737) $

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The lattice enthalpy, $\Delta H^{\minuso}_{lattice},$ is defined as the enthalpy change when one mole of solid ionic compound is broken apart into its gaseous ions. In this question, the reference process is:

$\ce{SiO2}\textrm{(s)} \longrightarrow \ce{Si^{4+}}\textrm{(g)} + 2 \ce{\,O^{2-}}\textrm{(g)}$

To build this cycle we must separate the compound into elements in their standard states, atomize and ionize silicon, and then atomize and reduce oxygen.
