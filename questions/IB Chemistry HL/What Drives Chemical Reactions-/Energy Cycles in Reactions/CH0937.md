# CH0937

What is the enthalpy of formation of potassium fluoride, $\Delta H_f (\ce{KF(s)})$, based on the diagram below?

![CH0937v2](images/CH0937v2.png)

-   A. $\hspace{1em}- 242\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}- 297\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}- 570\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}- 625\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\
According to Hess's law:

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\begin{aligned}
\Delta H_f  &= \Delta H_{at} (\ce{K(s)}) + \Delta H_{at} (\ce{F2(g)}) + IE_1 (\ce{K}) + EA_1 (\ce{F}) - \Delta H_{latt} (\ce{KF(s)}) \\
&= 89\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1} +\ 79\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1} + 419\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1} + (- 328\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}) - (+ 829\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}) \\
&= 89\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1} + 79\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1} + 419\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1} - 328\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1} - 829\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1} \\
&= - 570\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1}
\end{aligned}$
