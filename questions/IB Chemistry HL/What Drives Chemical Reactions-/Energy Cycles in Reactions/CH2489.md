# CH2489

Consider the information given below.

| $\mathbf{Substance}$ | $\mathbf{\Delta H_\textrm{c}^{\minuso}\textbf{ / kJ mol}^\mathbf{-1}}$ |
|:-:|:-:|
| $\ce{H2}\textrm{(g)}$ | $-286$ |
| $\ce{C4H10}\textrm{(g)}$ | $-2878$ |
| $\ce{C4H8}\textrm{(l)}$ | $-2720$ |

What is the enthalpy change, in $\textrm{kJ mol}^{-1},$ for the reaction below?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{C4H8}\textrm{(l)} + \ce{H2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow \ce{C4H10}\textrm{(g)}$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}-128$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}-5884$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}-2320$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}2320$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
When enthalpy of combustion data are available for all reactants and products (or all non$-$elemental species), the enthalpy change of a reaction is calculated using:

$\Delta H = \Sigma(\Delta H_\textrm{c}^{\minuso}\textrm{ of reactants}) - \Sigma(\Delta H_\textrm{c}^{\minuso}\textrm{ of products})$

The direction of subtraction is the reverse of the formation data formula. Combustion data for the reactants are summed and the combustion data for the products are subtracted because Hess's law requires constructing a cycle through the combustion products $(\ce{CO2}$ and $\ce{H2O})$ as intermediates.

$\Delta H = [\Delta H_\textrm{c}^{\minuso}(\ce{C4H8}) + \Delta H_\textrm{c}^{\minuso}(\ce{H2})] - [\Delta H_\textrm{c}^{\minuso}(\ce{C4H10})]$

$\Delta H = [(-2720\textrm{ kJ mol}^{-1}) + (-286\textrm{ kJ mol}^{-1})] - [(-2878\textrm{ kJ mol}^{-1})]$

$\Delta H = -128\textrm{ kJ mol}^{-1}$
