# CH0163

Refer to the Born-Haber cycle for potassium chloride below:

![CH0163](images/CH0163.png)

What is the correct name for the enthalpy changes labeled **x**, **y**, and **z**?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
  | | x | y | z |
  | :-: | :-: | :-: |:-: |
  | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Atomisation of $\ce{Cl}$ | Ionisation of $\ce{K}$  | $\enskip$Electron affinity of $\ce{Cl} \enskip$  |
  | B.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\enskip$Atomisation of $\ce{Cl} \enskip$ | $\enskip$Electron affinity of $\ce{K} \enskip$ | Ionisation of $\ce{Cl}$  |
  | C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Atomisation of $\ce{Cl2}$ | Ionisation of $\ce{K}$  | Electron affinity of $\ce{Cl}$  |
  | D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Atomisation of $\ce{Cl2}$ | Electron affinity of $\ce{K}$ | Ionisation of $\ce{Cl}$  |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Atomisation is defined as the formation of one mole of gaseous atoms (often from an element in its standard state like Na(l) or $\ce{Cl2(g)}$. This could also be one half of the bond dissociation enthalpy as chlorine molecules are in the gas phase.).

Ionization is the process in which one mole of electrons is removed from one mole of gaseous atoms/ions.

Electron affinity is the addition of one mole of electrons to gaseous atoms/ions.
