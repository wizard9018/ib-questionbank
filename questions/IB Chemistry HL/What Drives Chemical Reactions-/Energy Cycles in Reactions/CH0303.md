# CH0303

Given the following information:

$\begin{aligned}
\ce{C(s) + O2(g) -> CO2(g)}; \quad & \Delta H^{\minuso}  &= -393.5\textrm{\ kJ\,mol}^{-1}\\
\ce{CO(g) + 1/2O2(g) -> CO2(g)}; \quad & \Delta H^{\minuso}  &= -283.0\textrm{\ kJ\,mol}^{-1}
\end{aligned}$

The standard enthalpy of formation of carbon monoxide $\ce{(CO)}$ is:

- A.$\hspace{1em}+110.5 \textrm{\ kJ\,mol}^{-1}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}-676.5 \textrm{\ kJ\,mol}^{-1}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}+676.5 \textrm{\ kJ\,mol}^{-1}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}-110.5 \textrm{\ kJ\,mol}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Combining the first equation and the reverse of the second equation gives the equation for the enthalpy change in question.

$\ce{C(s) + O2(g) -> CO2(g)}$  |$\qquad$|$\Delta H^{\minuso}  = -393.5\textrm{\ kJ\,mol}^{-1}$
--:|--:|--:
$\ce{CO2(g) -> CO(g) + 1/2O2(g)}$ ||  $\Delta H^{\minuso}  = +283.0\textrm{\ kJ\,mol}^{-1}$
$\ce{C(s) + 1/2O2(g) -> CO(g)}$ ||  $\Delta H^{\minuso}  = -110.5\textrm{\ kJ\,mol}^{-1}$
