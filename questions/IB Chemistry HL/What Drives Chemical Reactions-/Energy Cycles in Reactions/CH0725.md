# CH0725

Cracking is a process by which gaseous alkanes are converted to smaller-chain alkanes and alkenes. One possible reaction in the cracking of butane is shown.

![CH0725](images/CH0725.png)

-   | Bond | $\mathbf{\Delta H_{r}}$ $\newline$ (kJ mol$^\mathbf{-1}$) |
    | :-: | :-: |
    | $\ce{C=C}$ | 620 |
    | $\ce{C-C}$ | 350 |
    | $\ce{C-H}$ | 420 |

What is the enthalpy change of the cracking reaction?

-   A.$\hspace{1em} +80 \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em} +150 \textrm{\ kJ  mol}^{-1}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em} -80 \textrm{ kJ mol}^{-1}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em} -150 \textrm{\ kJ  mol}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
In this reaction, one $\ce{C-C}$ bond is broken and another is replaced by a $\ce{C=C}$ bond. There is no net change in the number of $\ce{C-H}$ bonds. Thus, the enthalpy change of the reaction is given by

$\Delta H_r = 2\Delta H_{BE}(\ce{C-C}) - \Delta H_{BE}(\ce{C=C}) = +80 \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}$

This question is categorised as medium difficulty, as the student must interpret structural formulas, construct an equation for the enthalpy change and carry out the calculation (or identify the most likely answer).
