# CH2455

Consider the chemical equations below.

$\ce{NH4NO2}\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow \ce{N2}\textrm{(g)} + 2\ce{\,H2O}\textrm{(l)}$ |  $\Delta H^{\minuso} = W$
:-:|:-:
$\ce{NH3}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{HNO2}\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow \ce{NH4NO2}\textrm{(aq)}$ | $\Delta H^{\minuso} = X$
$2\ce{\,NH3}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow \ce{N2}\textrm{(g)} + 3\ce{\,H2}\textrm{(g)}$ | $\Delta H^{\minuso} = Y$
$2\ce{\,H2}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{O2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,H2O}\textrm{(l)}$  | $\Delta H^{\minuso} = Z$

What is the correct expression for the enthalpy change of the reaction below?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{H2}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{N2}\textrm{(g)} + 2\ce{\,O2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,HNO2}\textrm{(aq)}$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}-2W - 2X + Y + 2Z$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}+2W + 2X - Y - 2Z$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}-W - X + Y + Z$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}-2X - 2Y + Z + 2W$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The target reaction produces $2\ce{\,HNO2}\textrm{(aq)}.$ $\ce{HNO2}$ appears only in the second equation, as a reactant. To place it on the product side, reverse and double the second equation:

$2\ce{\,NH4NO2}\textrm{(aq)} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,NH3}\textrm{(g)} + 2\ce{\,HNO2}\textrm{(aq)} \hspace{2em} \Delta H = -2X$

The $2\ce{\,NH4NO2}$ now required as a reactant can be produced by reversing and doubling the first equation:

$2\ce{\,N2}\textrm{(g)} + 4\ce{\,H2O}\textrm{(l)} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,NH4NO2}\textrm{(aq)} \hspace{2em} \Delta H = -2W$

The $2\ce{\,NH3}$ produced as an intermediate is consumed by the third equation as written:

$2\ce{\,NH3}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow \ce{N2}\textrm{(g)} + 3\ce{\,H2}\textrm{(g)} \hspace{2em} \Delta H = Y$

The $4\ce{\,H2O}$ required and the excess $\ce{H2}$ are accounted for by doubling the fourth equation:

$4\ce{\,H2}\textrm{(g)} + 2\ce{\,O2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 4\ce{\,H2O}\textrm{(l)} \hspace{2em} \Delta H = 2Z$

After cancelling intermediates $(\ce{NH4NO2},$ $\ce{NH3},$ $\ce{H2O}),$ the net equation simplifies to

$\ce{N2}\textrm{(g)} + \ce{H2}\textrm{(g)} + 2\ce{\,O2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,HNO2}\textrm{(aq)}$

The total enthalpy changes is $\Delta H = -2W - 2X + Y + 2Z.$
