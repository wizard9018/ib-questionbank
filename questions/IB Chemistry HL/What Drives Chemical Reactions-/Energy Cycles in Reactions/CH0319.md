# CH0319

The following diagram shows the enthalpy of the reactants and products of a reaction involving gaseous covalent compounds. Which statement is correct?

![5.3-319](images/5.3-319.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$The reactants have a higher total average bond enthalpy than the products

- B.$\hspace{1em}$The products have a higher total average bond enthalpy than the reactants

- C.$\hspace{1em}$The activation energy is the sum of the total bond enthalpies for the products and reactants

- D.$\hspace{1em}$The activation energy is the difference between the total bond enthalpies for the products and reactants

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The products are at a lower energy level than the reactants meaning that the total average bond enthalpy of formation of bonds in the products is greater than the total average bond enthalpy of breaking of bonds in the reactants.
