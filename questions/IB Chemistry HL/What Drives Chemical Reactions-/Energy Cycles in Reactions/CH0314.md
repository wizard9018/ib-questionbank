# CH0314

Which expression represents the enthalpy change for 

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{CO(g) + H2O(g) -> CO2(g) + H2(g)}$?

-   A.$\hspace{1em} -\Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(C#O)} - 2 \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(O-H)}+ 2 \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(C=O)} + \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(H-H)}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em} \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(C#O)} + 2 \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(O-H)} - 2 \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(C=O)} - \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(H-H)}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em} - \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(C#O)} - \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(O-H)} + \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(C=O)}+ \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(H-H)}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em} \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(C#O)} + \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(O-H)} - \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(C=O)} - \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\ce{(H-H)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Breaking bonds is an endothermic process, and so bond enthalpy values are positive. Making bonds is an exothermic process, and so bond enthalpy values are negative.

$\Delta H =\sum \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\textrm{(bonds broken)} - \sum \Delta H^\ominus_{BE}\textrm{(bonds formed)}$

One $\ce{C#O}$ and two $\ce{O-H}$ bonds are broken. Two $\ce{C=O}$ and one $\ce{H-H}$ bond are formed.
