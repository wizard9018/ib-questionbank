# CH0304

Which of the following statements is false?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Hess's law states that the total enthalpy change for a chemical reaction is independent of the route by which the reaction takes place

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta H^\ominus_r  = \sum \left[\Delta H^\ominus_f (products)\right] - \sum \left[\Delta H^\ominus_f (reactants)\right]$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The standard enthalpy of formation of $\ce{O2(g)}$ is zero

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The standard enthalpy of formation for a pure compound in its standard state is zero

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The standard enthalpy of formation of a pure element in its standard state is zero; not for a compound.
