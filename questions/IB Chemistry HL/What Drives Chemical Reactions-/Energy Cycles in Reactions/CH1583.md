# CH1583

Consider the combustion reaction below.

$\ce{C2H5OH(g) + 3 O2(g) -> 2 CO2(g) + 3 H2O(g)}$

Which statement is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The energy needed to break bonds is greater than the energy released in the bond formation, and products are more stable than reactants.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The energy needed to break bonds is smaller than the energy released in the bond formation, and products are more stable than reactants.

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The energy needed to break bonds is smaller than the energy released in the bond formation, and reactants are more stable than products.

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The energy needed to break bonds is greater than the energy released in the bond formation, and reactants are more stable than products.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The student must understand that complete combustion reactions of organic substances are always exothermic.

This occurs because the energy required to break the bonds in the reactants is less than the energy released when new bonds form in the products.

$\Delta H^{\minuso}_R = \sum BE(\text{bonds broken}) - \sum BE(\text{bonds formed}) < 0$

$\sum BE(\text{bonds broken}) < \sum BE(\text{bonds formed})$

Exothermic reactions have the following energy level diagram.

![CH1583v2](images/CH1583v2.JPEG)

The products have less energy than the reactants. As a result, the products are more chemically stable.

This question is categorized as easy. The student must understand that combustion is exothermic. When energy is released, we can deduce that the bonds broken are weaker than those formed and that the products are more stable than the reactants.
