# CH0160

Which statement about Born$-$Haber cycles is **NOT** correct?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$They utilize Hess's Law.

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$They are used for covalent compounds.

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$They are used for ionic compounds.

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$They calculate the enthalpy change using an indirect method.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Born$-$Haber cycles are used for ionic compounds only and not for covalent ones.
