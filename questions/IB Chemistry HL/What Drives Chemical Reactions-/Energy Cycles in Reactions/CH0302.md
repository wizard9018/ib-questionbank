# CH0302

Given the enthalpy changes for the reactions below.

$\begin{aligned}
\ce{1/2N2(g) + O2(g)->NO2(g)} ; \quad & \Delta \ce{H} = +33.9 \textrm{\ kJ}  \\
\ce{NO(g) + 1/2O2 -> NO2(g)};  \quad & \Delta \ce{H}= -56.5 \textrm{\ kJ} \\
\end{aligned}$

What will be the enthalpy change for:

 $\ce{1/2N2(g) + 1/2 O2(g)->NO(g)}$?

- A.$\hspace{1em}-22.6 \textrm{\ kJ}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}-90.4 \textrm{\ kJ}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}+90.4 \textrm{\ kJ}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}+22.6 \textrm{\ kJ}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Combining the first equation and the reverse of the second equation gives the equation for the enthalpy change in question.

$ (+33.9 \textrm{\ kJ})-(-56.5\textrm{\ kJ}) = +90.4\textrm{\ kJ}$
