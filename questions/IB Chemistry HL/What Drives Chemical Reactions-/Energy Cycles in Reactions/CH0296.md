# CH0296

Choose the correct equation for the diagram below:

![5.2-296](images/5.2-296.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta H_2 = \Delta H_1 + \Delta H_3$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta H_3 = \Delta H_1 + \Delta H_2$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta H_3 = -\Delta H_1 - \Delta H_2$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta H_1 = \Delta H_2 + \Delta H_3$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Many products can be made from the same reactants by more than one route. Regardless of the route by which a chemical reaction proceeds, the enthalpy change will always be the same provided that the initial and final states of the system are identical.
