# CH2450

The average bond enthalpies are given below.

| Bond | Average bond enthalpy $\newline$ / kJ mol$^\mathbf{-1}$ |
|:-:|:-:|
| $\ce{I-I}$ | $151$ |
| $\ce{Cl-Cl}$ | $242$ |
| $\ce{I-Cl}$ | $211$ |

What is the enthalpy change, in $\textrm{kJ},$ for the following reaction?

$\mbox{}\\[-15pts]$

$\ce{I2}\textrm{(g)} + 5\ce{\,Cl2}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2\ce{\,ICl5}\textrm{(g)}$

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}10 \times 211 - 151 - 5 \times 242$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}151 + 5 \times 242 - 2 \times 211$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}2 \times 211 - 151 - 5 \times 242$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}151 + 5 \times 242 - 10 \times 211$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The enthalpy change from bond enthalpies is

$\Delta H = \Sigma(\textrm{bonds broken}) - \Sigma(\textrm{bonds formed})$

![Screenshot 2026-04-23 at 1.52.02 PM](<images/Screenshot 2026-04-23 at 1.52.02 PM.png>)

***Bonds broken:*** one $\ce{I-I}$ bond and five $\ce{Cl-Cl}$ bonds.\
***Bonds formed:*** two $\ce{ICl5}$ molecules contains ten $\ce{I-Cl}$ bonds.

$\Delta H = 151 + 5 \times 242 - 10 \times 211$
