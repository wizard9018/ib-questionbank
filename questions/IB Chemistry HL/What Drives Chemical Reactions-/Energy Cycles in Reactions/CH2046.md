# CH2046

Which of the following correctly shows the equation necessary to calculate the enthalpy of reaction for the hydrogenation of propene when considering bond enthalpies using Section 12 of the IB Chemistry data booklet?

$\\[10pts]$

$\ce{CH3CH=CH2 + H2 ->[Pd] CH3CH2CH3}$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}[614 + 436] - [414 + 346]$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}[(2\times 614) + 436] - [(2 \times 414) + 346]$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}[(2\times 414) + 346] - [614 + 436]$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}[614 + 436] - [(2\times 414) + 346]$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

To calculate the enthalpy of a reaction using bond enthalpies, the following equation is used:

$\Delta H = \sum(\text{bonds broken}) - \sum(\text{bonds formed})$

In the hydrogenation of propene, 1 $\ce{C=C}$ bond and 1 $\ce{H-H}$ breaks with the formation of 2 $\ce{C-H}$ bonds and 1 $\ce{C-C}$ bond:

![CH2046](images/CH2046.png)

Therefore, the enthalpy of calculation determined with bond enthalpies is:

$\Delta H = [(\ce{C=C}) + (\ce{H-H})] - [2(\ce{C-H}) + \ce{(C-C)}]$

$\Delta H = [614 + 436] - [(2\times 414) + 346]$
