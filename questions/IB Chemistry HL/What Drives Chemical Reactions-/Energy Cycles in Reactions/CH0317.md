# CH0317

Which statement about ozone is correct?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$The average bond enthalpy for the oxygen$-$to$-$oxygen bonds in ozone is the same as the average bond enthalpy for $\ce{O-O}.$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$The average bond enthalpy for the oxygen$-$to$-$oxygen bonds in ozone is the same as the average bond enthalpy for $\ce{O=O}.$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$The average bond enthalpy for the oxygen$-$to$-$oxygen bonds in ozone depends on which bond is being broken.

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$The average bond enthalpy for the oxygen$-$to$-$oxygen bonds in ozone is intermediate between the average bond enthalpy for $\ce{O-O}$ and $\ce{O=O}.$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Ozone has a resonance structure. The oxygen$-$oxygen bond order is 1.5 (between orders 1 and 2).
