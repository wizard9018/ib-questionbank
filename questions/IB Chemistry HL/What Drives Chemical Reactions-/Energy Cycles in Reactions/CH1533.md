# CH1533

What is the correct expression for enthalpy of hydration of chloride, $\Delta H^{⦵}_{hyd}$ of $\ce{Cl^-}$?

$\ $

![hesslawCaCl2.001](images/hesslawCaCl2.001.JPEG)

$\ $

- A.$\hspace{1em}\Delta H^{⦵}_{sol}(\ce{CaCl_2})  - \Delta H^{⦵}_{hyd}(\ce{Ca^{2+}}) - \Delta H^{⦵}_{latt}(\ce{CaCl_2})$

- B.$\hspace{1em}\Delta H^{⦵}_{sol}(\ce{CaCl_2})  + \Delta H^{⦵}_{hyd}(\ce{Ca^{2+}}) + \Delta H^{⦵}_{latt}(\ce{CaCl_2})$

- C.$\hspace{1em}\frac{1}{2} \big[

 \Delta H^{⦵}_{sol}(\ce{CaCl2})   - \Delta H^{⦵}_{hyd}(\ce{Ca^{2+}}) - \Delta H^{⦵}_{latt}(\ce{CaCl2})\big]$

 - D.$\hspace{1em}\frac{1}{2} \big [-

 \Delta H^{⦵}_{sol}(\ce{CaCl2})   + \Delta H^{⦵}_{hyd}(\ce{Ca^{2+}}) + \Delta H^{⦵}_{latt}(\ce{CaCl2})\big]$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

Using Hess's Law,

$\Delta H^{⦵}_{sol}(\ce{CaCl2}) = \Delta H^{⦵}_{latt}(\ce{CaCl2}) +  \Delta H^{⦵}_{hyd}(\ce{Ca^{2+}}) + 2 \Delta H^{⦵}_{hyd}(\ce{Cl^-})$

As a result, we can isolate the enthalpy of hydration of the chloride ion:

$\Delta H^{⦵}_{hyd}(\ce{Cl-}) = \frac{1}{2} \big[\Delta H^{⦵}_{sol}(\ce{CaCl2})   - \Delta H^{⦵}_{hyd}(\ce{Ca^{2+}}) - \Delta H^{⦵}_{latt}(\ce{CaCl2}) \big]$

$\ $
