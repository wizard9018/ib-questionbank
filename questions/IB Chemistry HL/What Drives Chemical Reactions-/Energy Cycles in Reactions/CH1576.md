# CH1576

Consider the cycle below.

![CH1576_Image1](images/CH1576_Image1.JPEG)

Which of the following can be used to calculate the desired enthalpy?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :- | :- |
    | I. | $\Delta H_1 = \Delta H_4 + \Delta H_5$ |
    | II. | $\Delta H_2 = -\Delta H_4 + \Delta H_5 + \Delta H_3$ |
    | III. | $\Delta H_3 = \Delta H_1 - \Delta H_2$ |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$I only

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$ II only

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The student must understand that enthalpy changes for a chemical reaction do not depend on the pathway. We can analyze each statement as shown below.

Reaction 1 results from reversing reaction 4 and using reaction 5 in the forward direction. So, $\Delta H_1 = -\Delta H_4 + \Delta H_5$ and statement I is incorrect.

![CH1576_Image2](images/CH1576_Image2.JPEG)

Reaction 2 results from reversing reactions 4 and allowing 5 and 3 to go in the forward direction. So,  statement II is correct.

![Screenshot 2024-08-17 at 6.35.12 PM](<images/Screenshot 2024-08-17 at 6.35.12 PM.JPEG>)

Reaction 3 results from reversing reaction 1 and using reaction 2 in the forward direction. So, $\Delta H_3 = - \Delta H_1 + \Delta H_2$ and statement III is incorrect.

![Screenshot 2024-08-17 at 6.35.04 PM](<images/Screenshot 2024-08-17 at 6.35.04 PM.JPEG>)
