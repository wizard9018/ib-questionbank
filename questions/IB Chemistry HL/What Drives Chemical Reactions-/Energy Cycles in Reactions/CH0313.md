# CH0313

The average bond enthalpy for a $\ce{N#N}$ bond is 945 kJ mol$^{-1}$. What is the enthalpy change for $\ce{N2(g) -> 2 N(l)}$?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$945 kJ mol$^{-1}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$> 945 kJ mol$^{-1}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$< 945 kJ mol$^{-1}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}-$ 945 kJ mol$^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

945 kJ mol$^{-1}$ is required to form $\ce{2N}$ in the gaseous state. Energy is then released when the gaseous atoms condense to a liquid.

|$\ce{N#N}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2 \ce{\,N}\textrm{(g)}$| $\qquad$ |$\Delta H = 945 \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}$

:-:|:-:|:-:

$\underline{\qquad 2 \ce{\,N}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2 \ce{\,N}\textrm{(l)} \qquad}$| $\qquad$| $\underline{\qquad \Delta H < 0 \textcolor{white}{j}\qquad}$

|$\ce{N#N}\textrm{(g)} \longrightarrow 2 \ce{\,N}\textrm{(l)}$| $\qquad$ |$\Delta H < 945 \textrm{\ kJ mol}^{-1}$
