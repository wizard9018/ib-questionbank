## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Hess's law states that, regardless of the route by which a chemical reaction proceeds, the enthalpy change will always be the same, provided the initial and final states of the system are identical. $\checkmark$

2. In some cases, the heat of formation is impossible to measure directly. $\checkmark$

   Hess's law helps us break a reaction or process into a series of small/ more easily measured steps. $\checkmark$

3. $\ce{C + 2H2  + 1/2 O2 -> CH3OH}\, \checkmark$

   $\begin{aligned}
   \Delta H^\ominus_r &= \Delta H_c\ce{(C)} + 2 \Delta H_c\ce{(H2)} - \Delta H_c\ce{(CH3OH)}\checkmark \\
   &=  -394 + (2)(-286) - (-726) \\
   &=-240\,\text{kJ mol}^{-1} \checkmark
   \end{aligned}$

   *Note: the value given on table 12 is -239,  so award 0 for giving that answer (.eg. not working it out).*

   *($\Delta H_{c} \ce{(O2)}$  not needed = 0)*

   $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

   When provided with combustion data, $\Delta H_r = \Delta H_\text{combustion}(reactants) - \Delta H_{combustion}(products)$.
