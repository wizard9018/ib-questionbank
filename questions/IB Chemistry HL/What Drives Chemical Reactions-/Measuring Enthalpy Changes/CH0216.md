# CH0216

The enthalpy diagram below shows that:

![5.1-216](images/5.1-216.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The reactants are more stable than the products and $\Delta H$ is positive

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The  products are more stable than the reactants and $\Delta H$ is positive

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The  reactants are more stable than the products and $\Delta H$ is negative

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The products are more stable than the reactants and $\Delta H$ is negative

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The reactants are at a lower energy level to the products and are therefore more stable. The products are at a higher energy level to the products meaning the enthalpy change is positive (endothermic reaction).
