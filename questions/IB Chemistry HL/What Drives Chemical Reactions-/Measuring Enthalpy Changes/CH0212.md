# CH0212

Which of the following is true in exothermic reactions?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Heat transfer from surrounding to system

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Heat transfer from system to surrounding

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ No change

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Heat transfer from surrounding to surrounding

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

An exothermic reaction is one in which heat energy is transferred from system to surroundings. Endothermic reaction is one in which heat energy is transferred from surroundings to system.
