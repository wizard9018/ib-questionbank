# CH2558

Which statement is correct for an exothermic reaction?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$The temperature of the surroundings decreases.

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$The reactants have higher energy than the products.

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}$The products are less stable than the reactants.

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$Energy is transferred from the surroundings to the system.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
In an exothermic reaction the enthalpy of the reactants is greater than the enthalpy of the products, so $\Delta H = H_\textrm{products} - H_\textrm{reactants} < 0.$ The energy difference is released to the surroundings, which means the temperature of the surroundings increases, not decreases.

![CH2558 exo](<images/CH2558 exo.png>)

Stability and energy are inversely related: a species at lower energy is more stable. Because the products sit at a lower energy level on the energy profile, they are more stable than the reactants. The statement that the products are less stable reverses this relationship.
