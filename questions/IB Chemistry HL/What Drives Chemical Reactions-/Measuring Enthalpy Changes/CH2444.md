# CH2444

Which of the following is true in the energy profile diagram for an endothermic reaction?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}$The reactants have a lower energy than the products.

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}$The activation energy is higher than that for an exothermic reaction.

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}\Delta H < 0$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}$The activation energy is equal to zero.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
In an endothermic reaction the products sit at a higher energy level than the reactants on the energy profile. The enthalpy change is defined as $\Delta H = H_\textrm{products} - H_\textrm{reactants},$ and since $H_\textrm{products} > H_\textrm{reactants},$ $\Delta H$ is positive $(\Delta H > 0).$ The vertical gap between the product and reactant energy levels represents $|\Delta H|.$

![CH2444 endo](<images/CH2444 endo.png>)

The activation energy $E_a$ is the energy barrier between the reactants and the transition state. Its magnitude is determined by the reaction mechanism and has no fixed relationship to whether a reaction is endothermic or exothermic. An endothermic reaction can have a high or low activation energy, as can an exothermic reaction. Comparing activation energies between the two classes without specifying the reactions is not valid.

Every reaction has a non$-$zero activation energy. Even very fast reactions, such as ionic precipitation, require some minimum energy for the reactant particles to rearrange. The activation energy determines the rate of a reaction (a kinetic property) and is independent of $\Delta H$ (a thermodynamic property).
