## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The enthalpy change for this reaction is positive ($\Delta H = 100\,\text{kJ}$), so $H_{products} - H_{reactants} = 100\,\text{kJ}.$ This means that the enthalpy of the products is $100\,\text{kJ}$ greater than that of reactants.

Differentiating between enthalpy change and activation energy is important to solve this question.  Enthalpy change in the graph can be deduced as $\Delta H = H_{products} - H_{reactants}$, whereas the activation energy is the difference between the enthalpy of reactants and the peak in the curve, as shown below.

![CH1002ans](images/CH1002ans.png)
