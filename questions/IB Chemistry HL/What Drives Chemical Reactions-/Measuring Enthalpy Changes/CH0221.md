# CH0221

**Maximum mark: 5**

The temperature of dilute sulphuric acid rises when it reacts with zinc according to the following chemical equation:

$$
\ce{Zn(s) + H2SO4(aq) → ZnSO4(aq) + H2(g)}
$$

1.  State whether the reaction is exothermic or endothermic. [1 mark]

2.  Explain what this implies in terms of the chemical potential energy contained in the reactants and the products. [2 marks]

3.  Draw an energy level diagram for this reaction. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. exothermic $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
    The temperature of the surroundings increased. This means that a release of energy took place where energy was transferred from the system to the surroundings.

2.  products are  more stable than reactants $\checkmark$\
    $H_\ce{prod} < H_\ce{react}  \: \checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
    In an exothermic reaction, the bonds made are stronger.
