# CH0721

Sulfur dioxide ($\ce{SO2}$) reacts with oxygen and sulfuric acid ($\ce{H2SO4}$) to produce oleum ($\ce{H2S2O7}$) in two reaction steps. The enthalpy cycle for this process is shown.

![CH0721](images/CH0721.png)

This process is used to produce a dilute solution of oleum in 5000 g sulfuric acid from 0.80 $mol$ sulfur dioxide. During the reaction, the temperature of the sulfuric acid increased by 22.8 $^\circ$C.

What are the enthalpy changes of the second step, $\Delta H_2$, and the overall process, $\Delta H_{total}$, assuming no heat is lost during the reaction?

*Specific heat capacity of H$_2$SO$_4$, c = 1.60 $J \ g^{-1} \ K^{-1}$*

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | $\mathbf{\Delta H_2}$ (kJ mol$\mathbf{^{-1}}$) | $\mathbf{\Delta H_{total}}$ (kJ mol$\mathbf{^{-1}}$)|
    | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| $\Delta H_{total} + 99.0$ | $\dfrac{-(0.8 \times 1000 \times 1.60 \times 22.8)}{5000}$ |
    |$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\Delta H_{total} + 99.0$| $\dfrac{-(5000 \times 1.60 \times 22.8)}{(1000 \times 0.8)}$ |
    | $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\Delta H_{total} - 99.0$ | $\dfrac{-(0.8 \times 1000 \times 1.60 \times 22.8)}{5000}$ |
    | $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\Delta H_{total} - 99.0$ | $\dfrac{-(5000 \times 1.60 \times 22.8)}{(1000 \times 0.8)}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The enthalpy change of the overall reaction is given by $\Delta H_{total} = \Delta H_1 + \Delta H_2$, so

$\Delta H_2 = \Delta H_{total} - \Delta H_1 = \Delta H_{total} -(-99.0) = \Delta H_{total} + 99.0$.

The enthalpy change is related to the temperature change by $\Delta H_{total} = \dfrac{-q}{n} = \dfrac{-mc \Delta T}{n}$, where $n$ is the number of moles of sulfur dioxide reacted. The units of $\Delta H_{total}$ are kJ mol$^{-1}$, so the value of $q$ in Joules must be divided by 1000.

This question is categorised as very hard as it requires knowledge of both calorimetry and enthalpy cycles, and involves the manipulation of two enthalpy expressions.
