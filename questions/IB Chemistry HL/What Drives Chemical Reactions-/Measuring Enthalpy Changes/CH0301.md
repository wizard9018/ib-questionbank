# CH0301

Which of the four reactions below is an endothermic reaction?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{CH4(g) + 2O2(g) ->CO2(g) + 2H2O(l)}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{C(s) + O2(g) -> CO2(g)}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{NO(g) + 1/2O2 -> NO2(g)}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{CO2(g) ->CO(g) + 1/2O2(g)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Most combustion reactions are exothermic. D is the opposite of a combustion reaction.
