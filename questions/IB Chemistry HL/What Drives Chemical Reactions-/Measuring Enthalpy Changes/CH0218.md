# CH0218

Which reaction below is endothermic?

- A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{2NO(g) + O2(g) -> 2NO2(g)}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH4(g) + 2O2(g) -> CO2(g) + 2H2O (l)}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{4Al(s) + 3O2(g) -> 2Al2O3(s)}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CaCO3(s) -> CaO(s) + CO2(g)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Combustion and oxidation reactions, in which substances react with $\ce{O2},$ are exothermic $(\Delta H < 0).$ The formation of new bonds to oxygen releases more energy than is required to break the bonds in the reactants. The reactions of $\ce{NO},$ $\ce{CH4},$ and $\ce{Al}$ with $\ce{O2}$ all fall into this category.

The decomposition of $\ce{CaCO3}$ is endothermic. Energy must be continuously supplied to break the strong ionic bonds in the reactant, and the energy input exceeds the energy released when the products form $(\Delta H > 0).$ This is consistent with the fact that limestone must be heated to very high temperatures (above $800\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}$) to decompose. In general, thermal decomposition reactions are endothermic because they involve breaking a compound into simpler substances.
