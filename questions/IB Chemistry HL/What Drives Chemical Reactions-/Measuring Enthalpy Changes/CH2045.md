# CH2045

Which of the following is correct for an exothermic reaction?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :- | :- |
    | I. | The stability of the reactants is greater than the stability of the products. |
    | II. | The activation energy of the forward reaction is less than the activation energy of the reverse reaction. |
    | III. | Energy is released to the surroundings. |

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$II and III

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
The energy profile diagram for an exothermic reaction is as follows:

![CH2045](images/CH2045.png)

The products have a lower energy than the reactants, so the products are more stable. Statement I is incorrect.

Both directions pass over the same energy barrier, and the activation energy is measured from each starting level to the top. The reactants start at the higher level, so the forward reaction has the smaller activation energy: $E_a(\textrm{reverse}) = E_a(\textrm{forward}) + |\Delta H|.$ Statement II is correct.

The energy difference between reactants and products is released to the surroundings as heat. Statement III is correct.
