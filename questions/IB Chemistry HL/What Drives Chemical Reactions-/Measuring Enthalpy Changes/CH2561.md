# CH2561

A $5.0\textrm{ g}$ sample of an unknown metal is heated with $50.0\textrm{ J}$ of energy. The temperature of the metal increases by $15.0\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}.$

What is the specific heat capacity of the metal?

-   A.$\hspace{1.em}0.67\textrm{ J g}^{-1}\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}^{-1}$

-   B.$\hspace{1.em}1.5\textrm{ J g}^{-1}\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}^{-1}$

-   C.$\hspace{1.em}3.3\textrm{ J g}^{-1}\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}^{-1}$

-   D.$\hspace{1.em}10.0\textrm{ J g}^{-1}\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Rearranging $q = mc\Delta T$ for the specific heat capacity gives

$c = \dfrac{q}{m \times \Delta T} = \dfrac{50.0 \textrm{\ J}}{5.0 \textrm{\ g} \times 15.0\ {}^\circ\textrm{C}} =  0.67\textrm{ J g}^{-1}\,{}^\circ\textrm{C}^{-1}$
