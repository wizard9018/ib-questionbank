# CH1167

Which statement(s) is (are) correct about the energy profile given below?

![CH1167](images/CH1167.png)

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
 | | |
 | :-- | :-- |
 | I. | **z** is the enthalpy change for the catalyzed reaction |
 | II. | **x** corresponds to the activation energy of the catalyzed reaction |
 | III. | **y-x** is proportional to the energy released to the environment |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

![CH1167Ans](images/CH1167Ans.png)

Statement I is incorrect. The activation energy is lower for a catalyzed reaction than for an uncatalyzed reaction. Therefore, $\textbf{z}$ represents the activation energy of the catalyzed reaction.

Statement II is incorrect, as the activation energy is the minimum energy needed for reactants to produce products, and $\textbf{x}$ stands for the activation energy of the uncatalyzed reaction.

Finally, as the reaction is exothermic, a temperature change accompanies the reaction, releasing energy into the surroundings. The energy released is proportional to the enthalpy change $(\Delta H = \textbf{y} - \textbf{x}),$ calculated from the difference between the enthalpy of reactants compared to that of products. Then, statement III is correct.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must interpret the graph, deduce the enthalpy, and apply the activation energy interpretation for catalyzed and uncatalyzed reactions.
