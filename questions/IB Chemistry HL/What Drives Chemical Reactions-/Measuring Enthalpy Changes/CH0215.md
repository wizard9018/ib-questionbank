# CH0215

If a reaction is endothermic:

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta H$ is negative and heat is absorbed

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta H$ is positive and heat is absorbed

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta H$ is positive and heat is emitted

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\Delta H$ is negative and heat is emitted

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

If a chemical reaction leads to an increase in enthalpy, the heat energy is absorbed from the surroundings.
