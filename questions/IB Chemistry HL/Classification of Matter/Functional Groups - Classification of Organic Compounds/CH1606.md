# CH1606

The mass spectrum of a compound is given below.

![CH1606](images/CH1606.JPEG)

The compound shows a single peak in the $^1\text{H NMR}$ spectrum. Which of the following could be the structural formula of this compound?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH2CHO}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3COCH3}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{C4H10}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH2CH2OH}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The number of electrons in each species given:
This question requires recall of the fact that the peak with the largest $m/z$ value corresponds to the mass of the molecular ion, 58 amu. Since the NMR shows only one signal, there must be one chemical environment for the H atoms, or one type of hydrogen atom in the molecule.

$\ce{CH3CH2CHO}:$ The molecular mass is 58 amu and the fragments at 15 and 43 could be $\ce{CH3+}$ and $\ce{CH2CHO+}.$ There are three chemical environments, the methyl group, the methylene group, and the aldehyde hydrogen atom.  We would expect three peaks in the NMR with ratio $3:2:1.$

$\ce{CH3COCH3}:$ The molecular mass is 58 amu and the fragments at 15 and 43 could be $\ce{CH3+}$ and $\ce{COCH3+}$.  There is only one chemical environment as the two methyl groups are attached to the same carbonyl. This is consistent with all of the data presented.

$\ce{C4H10}:$ The molecular mass is 58 amu and the fragments at 15 and 43 could be $\ce{CH3+}$ and $\ce{CH3CH2CH2+}.$ There are two chemical environments, the 6 hydrogens on (exterior) carbons 1 and 4 are equivalent while the 4 hydrogens on (interior) carbons 2 and 3 are equivalent. Two peaks are expected in the NMR with ratio $3:2.$

$\ce{CH3CH2CH2OH}:$ The molecular mass is 60 amu, inconsistent with the mass spectrum. There are four unique chemical environments with relative intensity $3:2:2:1.$
