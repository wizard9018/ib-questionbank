## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The number of peaks on a $\ce{^1H}$ NMR spectrum gives the number of environments, and the integral ratio gives the number of H atoms in each of these environments. These molecules are all symmetrical resulting in H atoms on more than one carbon being in the same environment.
