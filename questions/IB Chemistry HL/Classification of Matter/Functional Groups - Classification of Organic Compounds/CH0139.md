    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    2,3$-$dimethylbutane has a four carbon chain with methyl groups on C2 and C3.

    The second compound has a four carbon chain with a hydroxyl group on C2 and, therefore, belongs to the alcohol class.

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    Primary haloalkanes have one carbon group attached to the $\ce{C-X}$ carbon, X being the halogen.

    Secondary haloalkanes have two carbon groups attached to the $\ce{C-X}$ carbon.

    Tertiary haloalkanes have three carbon groups attached to the $\ce{C-X}$ carbon.
