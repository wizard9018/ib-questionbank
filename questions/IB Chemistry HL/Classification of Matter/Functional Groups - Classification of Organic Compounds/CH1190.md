# CH1190

Which statement about the ${^1}\text{H NMR}$ spectrum of 3,3-dimethyl butanoic acid is correct?

|Type of proton| Chemical shift (ppm)|
|:-:|:-:|
|![pe-1190](images/pe-1190.png)| 9.0 - 13.0|
|![pe-1190-](images/pe-1190-.png)| 0.9 - 1.0|

- A. $\hspace{1em}$It has five signals

- B. $\hspace{1em}$The integration trace is 9 : 2 : 1

- C. $\hspace{1em}$There is a doublet near chemical shift $\delta = 1\,\text{ppm}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$There is a triplet near chemical shift $\delta = 8\,\text{ppm}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The structure of 3,3-dimethyl butanoic acid is given below.

![CH1190](images/CH1190.png)

As shown below, three different chemical environments for the hydrogens and three signals will be observed in the ${^1}\text{H NMR}$ spectrum. Option A is incorrect.

![CH1190B](images/CH1190B.png)

Signal 1: It represents 9 hydrogens, and its splitting pattern is a singlet as there are no neighbouring hydrogens.

Signal 2: It represents 2 hydrogens, and its splitting pattern is a singlet as there are no neighbouring hydrogens.

Signal 3: It represents 1 hydrogen, and its splitting pattern is a singlet as there are no neighbouring hydrogens.

Considering the three signals, the integration trace must be proportional to the hydrogens, so 9 : 2 : 1. Option B is correct.

Signal 3 should be between $9.0 - 13.0\,\text{ppm}$, and is a singlet.

Signal 1 should be near chemical shift $0.9 - 1.0\,\text{ppm},$ and is a singlet.

This question is categorized as hard as a deep understanding of ${^1}\text{H NMR}$ should be applied to deduce the spectrum and its splitting pattern.
