# CH2043

Which molecule and IUPAC name correctly matches the structure below?

![CH2043](images/CH2043.png)

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$propanoic acid

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$propanone

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$ethanone

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$propanamide

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The molecule in the image has three carbons with a carboxyl group. Therefore, this molecule is propanoic acid, indicating three carbons in the chain and a $\ce{-COOH}$ group.
