# CH2019

How many chiral carbon atoms are present in cholesterol?

![CH2019](images/CH2019.png)

-   A. $\hspace{1em}5$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}6$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}8$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}10$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must be able to identify chiral carbons as carbons with four different groups attached to them.

The cholesterol structural formula is below, and the chiral carbons are shown in green.

![CH2019b](images/CH2019b.png)

In complex molecules like cholesterol, it is helpful to systematically analyze each carbon atom to determine if it is chiral. Chiral carbons can be identified by finding carbons that are bonded to four different groups.  Alternatively, carbons bonded to two or more identical groups (*e.g.*, $\ce{-CH2-}$ or $\ce{-CH3}$ groups) are not chiral.

This question is categorized as a medium, as the student must interpret the given structure to determine the number of chiral carbons.
