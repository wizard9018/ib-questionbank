# CH0801

The mass spectrum of an alcohol was found to contain major peaks at $m/z$ 102, 57, and 45.

Which of the following compounds could this spectrum belong to?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$2,3$-$dimethylbutan$-$2$-$ol

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$3,3$-$dimethylbutan$-$2$-$ol

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$2$-$methylpentan$-$1$-$ol

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$hexan$-$3$-$ol

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
All four compounds have the molecular formula $\ce{C6H13OH}$ and so will produce a molecular ion peak at $m/$z 102.  Recall that fragments are usually formed from a single bond breaking in the molecular structure.

A fragment at 57 corresponds to the chemical formula $\ce{C4H9+}$ or $\ce{C3H5O+}$.  For saturated alcohols, a fragment of $\ce{C3H5O+}$ is impossible, so the molecule must include $\ce{C4H9+}$.

A fragment of 45 corresponds to the chemical formula $\ce{C3H9+}$ or $\ce{C2H5O+}$.  It is not possible to make the $\ce{C3H9+}$ fragment because propane ($\ce{C3H8}$) is fully saturated.

The molecules are shown below and we are looking for molecules with the fragments $\textcolor{red}{\ce{C4H9+}}$ and $\textcolor{blue}{\ce{C2H5O+}}$.
