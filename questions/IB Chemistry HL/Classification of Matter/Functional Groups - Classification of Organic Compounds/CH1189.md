# CH1189

Given the structures below:

![CH1189](images/CH1189.png)

Which pair presents $cis-trans$ isomerism?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$W and Y

- B. $\hspace{1em}$W and X

- C. $\hspace{1em}$X and Z

- D. $\hspace{1em}$Y and Z

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Stereoisomers have the same molecular formulae and connectivity but different spatial arrangements.

$cis-trans$ isomerism in cycloalkanes occurs due to the restriction in the plane of the cycle.

1,2-dimethyl cyclo butane presents two possible configurations as the methyl groups can be on the same side or not.

Z is different from the options given as it is 1,3-dimethyl cyclobutane.

In addition, W and Y are exactly the same molecule as both methyl groups are on the same side, resulting in the $cis$ isomer. We can deduce that X is the $trans$ isomer of W or Y (as both are the same). B is the correct option.

This question is categorized as hard, as students should analyze four molecules to identify $cis-trans$ isomers.
