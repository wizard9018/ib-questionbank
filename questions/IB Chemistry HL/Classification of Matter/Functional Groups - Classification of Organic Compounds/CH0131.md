# CH0131

What functional groups are both present in the following organic molecule?   The gray balls represent carbon, the white ball represent hydrogen, the red balls represent oxygen and the blue balls represent nitrogen.

![CH0131](images/CH0131.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Amido, carbonyl

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Hydroxy, amino

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Carboxyl, amido

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Amino, carboxyl

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The molecule shown is glycine which contains two functional groups attached to the same central carbon atom:

-   An amino group ($\ce{-NH2}$) represented by the blue nitrogen atom bonded to two white hydrogens, and

-   A carboxyl group ($\ce{-COOH}$) represented by the carbon atom double bonded to one red oxygen $\ce{(C=O)}$ and single bonded to another red oxygen in an $\ce{O-H}$ group.

These two functional groups define amino acids The molecule is not an amide, since the nitrogen is not directly bonded to a carbonyl carbon.
