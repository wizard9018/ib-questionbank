# CH0129

What is the correct IUPAC name of this molecule?

![CH0129v2](images/CH0129v2.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ ethylbutene

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 1$-$methylpentene

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 2$-$methylpent$-$1$-$ene

- D.$\hspace{1em}$2,4$-$dimethylbut$-$1$-$ene

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The longest carbon chain has 5 carbons and will include the C=C.

The alkene is given priority and is on carbon-1.

The methyl group is bonded to carbon-2.
