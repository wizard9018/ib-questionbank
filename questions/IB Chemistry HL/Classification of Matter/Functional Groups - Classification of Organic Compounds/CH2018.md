# CH2018

Which of the following correctly describes the $^1\text{H}$ NMR spectrum of $4\text{--}$nitrobenzoic acid?

![CH2018a](images/CH2018a.png)

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | Number of signals | Area ratio | Splitting pattern of hydrogen A |
    | :-- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | 5 | $1:1:1:1$ | doublet |
    | B. | 2 | $4:1$ | singlet |
    | C. | 2 | $4:1$ | doublet |
    | D. | 3 | $2:2:1$ | singlet |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The student must analyze the $^1\textrm{H}$ NMR spectrum of $4\text{--}$nitrobenzoic acid by considering the number of unique proton environments, their relative integration trace, and their expected splitting patterns.
There are three distinct chemical environments as shown here:

![CH2018b](images/CH2018b.png)

The first environment corresponds to the hydrogen on the  carboxyl group. The second corresponds to the two protons on the benzene ring adjacent to the carboxyl group. Finally, the two protons adjacent to the nitro group make up the third chemical environment. The integration ratio should be $2:2:1$, reflecting the number of hydrogen atoms in each environment.

Since there are no hydrogen atoms three bonds away from hydrogen A (the carboxyl hydrogen), its peak will not be split and it will appear as a singlet.
