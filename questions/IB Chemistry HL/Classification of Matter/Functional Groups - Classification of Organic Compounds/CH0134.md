# CH0134

Which of the following molecules contains a tertiary amine?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Dimethylamine

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Methylethylamine

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Dimethylethylamine

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Propylamine

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

A tertiary amine is when three carbon groups are attached to the amine.
