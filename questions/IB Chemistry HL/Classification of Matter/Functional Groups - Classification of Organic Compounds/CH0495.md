# CH0495

A chloroalkane has the following structural formula.

![21.1-CH0495](images/CH0495.png)

The $\ce{^1H NMR}$ spectrum of the chloroalkane is measured. Which of the following correctly describes the pattern of signals in the NMR spectrum?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$One doublet and two triplets

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Two doublets and three triplets

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$One singlet, two triplets and two quartets

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$One singlet, one triplet and one quartet

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The methyl group appears as a singlet, as there are no protons on the adjacent carbon atom. The ethyl groups are equivalent so produce only two signals: a quartet for the $\ce{CH2}$ group (coupled to the $\ce{CH3}$) and a triplet for the $\ce{CH3}$ (coupled to the $\ce{CH2}$).

The question is at the lower end of the “hard” category as the student must correctly identify equivalent protons and deduce the coupling pattern for multiple groups.
