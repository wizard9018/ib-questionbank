# CH1472

Which statement about propane, propene and propyne is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$They are all members of the same homologous series

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$All of them contain five carbons in their structure

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$All of them are unsaturated

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$All of them can undergo complete combustion reactions

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The student must distinguish alkanes, alkenes and alkynes. These three have something in common, all present the stem name PROP- meaning they have three carbons. Option B is incorrect. However, the suffixes are different as they belong to different homologous series. Option A is incorrect.

Only propene and propyne are unsaturated (as they have multiple bonds), while propane is a saturated molecule. Therefore, Statement C is incorrect.

All of them can undergo complete combustion reactions. Organic molecules undergoing complete combustion reactions react to produce carbon dioxide and water.
