# CH1018

What is the ratio of the areas of the signals in the $^1$H-NMR spectrum of heptan-4-ol?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}1:1:4:4:6$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}1:1:2:2:2:2:3:3$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}1:1:7:7$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}1:1:8:6$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The student must understand the splitting pattern of heptan-4-ol.

Hydrogen nuclei in different chemical environments have different chemical shifts and will be observed in different signals. However, some hydrogens here are equivalent (identified with colors in the diagram below).

![CH1018](images/CH1018.png)

$\ce{H_A}$ signal represents 1 hydrogen\
$\ce{H_B}$ signal represents 1 hydrogen\
$\ce{H_C}$ signal represents 4 hydrogens (equivalent hydrogens circled in red)\
$\ce{H_D}$ signal represents 4 hydrogens (equivalent hydrogens circled in green)\
$\ce{H_E}$ signal represents 6 hydrogens (equivalent hydrogens circled in violet)

Five signals will be displayed in the $^1$H NMR spectrum. The expected ratio of areas is $1:1:4:4:6$ (proportional to the number of hydrogens represented in each signal)

This question is categorized as hard as the student must understand the integration trace/area under the curve meaning in $^1$H-NMR.
