Compound $\mathbf{C}$ contains one $\ce{CH3}$ group and one functional group. The $\ce{^1H}$ NMR signals of the compound do not change if a base is added to the NMR sample.

1.  Using section 27 (*section 21- 2025 Syllabus*) of the data booklet, determine the number of $\ce{CH2}$ groups in compound $\mathbf{C}$.  [1 mark]

2.  Using sections 26 and 27 (*sections 20 and 21- 2025 Syllabus*) of the data booklet, identify the functional group in compound $\mathbf{C}$. Give **two** reasons for your answer. [3 marks]

3.  Draw the full structural formula of compound $\mathbf{C}$. [1 mark]

4.  The empirical formula of compound $\mathbf{C}$ was confirmed by measuring its elemental composition. Using your answer to (c) and section 6 (*section 7- 2025 Syllabus*) of the data booklet, calculate the expected abundance of carbon as a percentage of the molar mass. [3 marks]

1.  (From NMR chemical shifts and integrals) the signal at 0.9 ppm is a $\ce{CH3}$ group and the signals between 1 and 3 ppm are $\ce{CH2}$ groups.

    Therefore, number of $\ce{CH2}$ groups $\,= 3\,\checkmark$

    *Accept 6 protons / H atoms*
