# CH0133

Which pairs of molecules are structural isomers?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$1$-$Bromopropane, 1$-$bromobutane

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Methoxyethane $\ce{(CH3OCH2CH3)}$, propan$-$1$-$ol

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Ethanal, propanone

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Pentanoic acid, methylpropanoate $(\ce{CH3CH2COOCH3})$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Structural isomers share the same molecular formula but differ in how their atoms are connected.  There are three types of structural isomers: chain isomers, position isomers, or functional$-$group isomers. The quickest reliable test is to translate the names into molecular formulas and confirm the count of each type of atom matches.
