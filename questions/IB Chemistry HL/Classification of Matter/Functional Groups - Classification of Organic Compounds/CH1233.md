# CH1233

Fentanyl is a synthetic opioid drug used as an analgesic. It has been proven to cause strong dependence. Its chemical structure is given below.

![CH1233](images/CH1233.png)

Which statement is correct?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Its formula is $\ce{C22H30N2O},$ and its structure includes carbonyl and amino functional groups

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Its formula is $\ce{C22H30N2O},$ and its structure includes carboxyl and amino functional groups

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Its formula is $\ce{C22H28N2O},$ and its structure includes carbonyl and amino functional groups

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Its formula is $\ce{C22H28N2O},$ and its structure includes carboxyl and amino functional groups

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The structural formula as shown below helps deduce the correct molecular formula as $\ce{C22H28N2O}.$. The carbonyl and amino groups are highlighted.  (**Note:** The carbonyl is part of an amido group  $\ce{(-CONH-})$ so that nitrogen is not highlighted, it is not an amino group.)

![CH1233msv2](images/CH1233msv2.png)

There is no carboxyl group $(\ce{-COOH}).$

This question is categorized as hard as students must interpret the structure to deduce the molecular formula and its functional groups.
