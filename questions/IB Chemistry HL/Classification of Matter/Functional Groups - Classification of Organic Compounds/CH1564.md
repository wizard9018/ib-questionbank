# CH1564

Which of the following statements is correct for 3,3$\text{--}$dimethylbutan$\text{--}$2$\text{--}$ol?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}

    | | |

    | :- | :- |

    | I. | It has a chiral carbon. |

    | II. | Its high$\text{--}$resolution $^1$H NMR spectrum has a triplet. |

    | III. | The high$\text{--}$resolution $^1$H NMR spectrum has a $9:3:1:1$ ratio of peak areas. |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only 

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\

The student must first figure out the structural formula of 3,3$\text{--}$dimethylbutan$\text{--}$2$\text{--}$ol.

![CH1564_Image1](images/CH1564_Image1.JPEG)

It has a chiral carbon (marked with $\star),$ as chiral carbons have four different groups joined to them. Statement I is correct.

The high$\text{--}$resolution $^1\text{H NMR}$ spectrum requires us to analyze the different chemical environments for the hydrogens. As shown below, there are four different groups of hydrogens.

![CH1563_Image2](images/CH1563_Image2.JPEG)

Every signal represents hydrogens in the same chemical environment. This spectrum has 4 signals. The integration trace/area under the peak of every signal is proportional to the intensity of the abruption and to the number of hydrogens represented in that signal. Finally, the splitting pattern is determined by the number of neighboring hydrogens as $n+1$ $(n:$ neighboring hydrogens). The complete detail of the spectrum is shown below.

|Hydrogen|Intensity|Neighbors|Splitting|

|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|

$\textcolor{blue}{\textbf{H}}$ | 9 | 0| singlet

$\textcolor{red}{\textbf{H}}$ | 1 | $\dagger$| singlet

$\textcolor{violet}{\textbf{H}}$ | 1 | 3| quartet

$\textcolor{lightgreen}{\textbf{H}}$ | 3 | 1|doublet

$\small{\dagger\ \textit{The hydroxy hydrogen will not appear split and will not split its neighbors due to quick exchange with the solvent.}}$

Statement II is incorrect, and statement III is correct.

This question is categorized as a medium as the student must figure out the structural formula, deduce the presence of a chiral carbon and figure out the splitting pattern as well as the ratio of areas under the peak for the signals.
