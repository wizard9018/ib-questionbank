# CH1259

Which statements are correct about hexan-2-one?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
 | | |
 | :-- | :-- |
 | I. | Its IR spectrum presents a strong absorption near $1720\,\text{cm}^{-1}$ |
 | II. | The molecular ion in the mass spectrum is at $m/z = 54$ |
 | III. | It presents one unsaturation |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

- C. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The structural formula of hexan-2-one is shown below.

![CH1259](images/CH1259.png)

The IR spectrum shows a characteristic strong absorption of the $\ce{C=O}$ between $1700 - 1750\,\text{cm}^{-1}$ (this can be checked with the IB Data Booklet – Section 20). Statement I is correct.

The molecular ion peak in the mass spectrum is formed when the molecule loses one electron and remains with a positive charge. The mass should be the same as the molar mass.

In this case hexan-2-one is $\ce{C6H12O}$ and its molar mass is $100\,\text{g mol}^{-1}$ (Formula mass = (6)(12.01) + (12)(1.01) + (1)(16.00) = 100.18). Statement II is incorrect.

There is one unsaturation due to the $\ce{C=O}$ double bond. Statement III is correct.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must figure out information about the IR and mass spectra of hexan-2-one and the presence of unsaturation.
