# CH0377

**Maximum mark: 8**

An organic compound $\mathbf{B}$ consists of carbon, hydrogen and oxygen only.

1.  The elemental composition of compound $\mathbf{B}$ is given below.

    | Element | Percentage abundance by mass / % |
    | :-: | :-: |
    | C | 70.58 |
    | H | 5.92 |
    | O | 23.50 |

    Calculate the empirical formula of compound $\mathbf{B}.$ [3 marks]

2.  The mass spectrum of compound B is shown.

    ![CH0377-1](images/CH0377-1.png)
    [Source: webbook.nist.gov]

    1.  State the molecular mass, in g mol$^{-1}$, of compound $\mathbf{B}$. [1 mark]

    2.  Using your answer to (a), calculate the molecular formula of compound $\mathbf{B}$. [1 mark]

3.  The $\ce{^1H}$ NMR spectrum of compound $\mathbf{B}$ is shown.

    ![CH0377-2](images/CH0377-2.png)
    [Source: own work; data simulated using nmrdb.org]

    The signal at 3.8 ppm is produced by a $\ce{CH2}$ group.

    Name the **two** functional groups present in compound $\mathbf{B},$ using Section 21 of the data booklet. [2 marks]

4.  Based on your answers to (a), (b) and (c), draw the full structural formula of compound $\mathbf{B}$. [1 mark]
