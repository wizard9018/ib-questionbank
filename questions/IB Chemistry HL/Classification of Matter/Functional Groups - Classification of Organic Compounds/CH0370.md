# CH0370

A carboxylic acid has the following structural formula.

![CH0370](images/CH0370.png)

The $\ce{^1H NMR}$ spectrum of the carboxylic acid is measured. Which of the following correctly describes the pattern of signals in the $\ce{NMR}$ spectrum?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Four signals with integrals in the ratio $3:1:1:1$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Three signals with integrals in the ratio $6:1:1$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Three signals with integrals in the ratio $3:3:1$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Two signals with integrals in the ratio $6:1$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The methyl groups are equivalent so produce one signal with integral 6, while the other protons both produce signals with integrals 1.

The student must recognise that all protons are detectable by $\ce{NMR}$, including those attached to $\ce{O}$ atoms, and know how to count proton environments ($\ce{COOH}$ protons are sometimes too broad to detect due to proton exchange and/or hydrogen bonding, but this point is not covered at IB level).

The question is categorised as hard as several features of the $\ce{NMR}$ spectrum must be predicted.
