# CH1475

A molecule has the molecular formula $\ce{C2H5NO}.$ Which statement is always correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$It has an amido group

- B. $\hspace{1em}$It is mono$\text{--}$unsaturated

- C. $\hspace{1em}$It has one isomer only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$Every carbon in the molecule presents a tetrahedral arrangement

---

Not all of them exhibit an amido group, as shown below.

![Img6](images/Img6.png)

Option A is incorrect.

All of the isomers are monounsaturated: they each have one double bond. The degree of unsaturation can also be checked by calculating the degree of unsaturation or index of hydrogen deficiency (IHD) as shown below.

IHD = $\dfrac{1}{2}[\ce{2C + 2 - H - X + N}]$

IHD= $\dfrac{1}{2}[2 \times 2 + 2 - 5 - 0 + 1] = 1$

Option B is correct.

As shown above, many molecules exist with this formula, so there is not just one possible isomer. Option C is incorrect.

Some of the isomers proposed do not contain carbons with four single bonds, so carbons do not always have a tetrahedral arrangement. In the amido group, carbon is in a trigonal planar geometry. Option D is incorrect.

This question is categorized as a medium as the student must think about possible structures with this molecular formula and test the functional groups, the number of unsaturations, etc.
