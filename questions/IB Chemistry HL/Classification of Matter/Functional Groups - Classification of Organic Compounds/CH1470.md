# CH1470

Which statements are correct for this molecule?

The gray balls represent carbon, the white balls represent hydrogen, and the red balls represent oxygen.

![CH1470v2](images/CH1470v2.png)

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :- | :- |
    | I. | It is saturated. |
    | II. | Its molecular formula is $\ce{C4H4O}.$ |
    | III. | It contains a phenyl group. |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$I only

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The student must interpret the structure given. Its molecular formula is $\ce{C8H8O2},$ and its empirical formula is $\ce{C4H4O}.$ Statement II is incorrect.

This molecule presents a certain degree of unsaturation (four double bonds and a cyclic structure in the phenyl group).

The degree of unsaturation can also be calculated from a molecular formula. It is also known as the index of hydrogen deficiency (IHD) and is calculated as shown below.

$\ce{IHD = \dfrac{1}{2}[2C + 2 - H - X + N]}$

$\ce{IHD = \dfrac{1}{2}[2 \times 8 + 2 - 8 - 0 + 0] = 5}$

In addition, a phenyl group is present, so statement III is correct.
