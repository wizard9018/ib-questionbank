# CH1013

Which compounds are members of the same homologous series?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH2CHO}$ and $\ce{CH3COCH3}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH2CHO}$ and $\ce{CH3CH2CH2CHO}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH(OH)CH3}$ and $\ce{CH3OCH2CH3}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH3}$ and $\ce{CH2CH2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Successive members of a homologous series differ by a $\ce{-CH2-}$ group and can be represented with the same general formula.

$\ce{CH3CH2CHO}$ and $\ce{CH3CH2CH2CHO}:$\
Propanal and butanal can be represented with the general formula $\ce{C_nH_{2n}O}$, and differ by a $\ce{CH2}$ group. Both have the same functional group ($\ce{-CHO}$) and belong to the aldehyde class. They are members of the same homologous series.

$\ce{CH3CH2CHO}$ and $\ce{CH3COCH3}:$\
Propanal and propanone are structural isomers that belong to the aldehyde and ketone class, respectively. They do not belong to the same homologous series.

$\ce{CH3CH(OH)CH3}$ and $\ce{CH3OCH2CH3}:$\
Propan$-$2$-$ol and methoxyethane are structural isomers that belong to the alcohol and ether class, respectively. They do not belong to the same homologous series.

$\ce{CH3CH3}$ and $\ce{CH2CH2}:$\
Ethane and ethene belong to the alkane and alkene classes. They do not belong to the same homologous series.

This question is categorized as medium, as the student must understand what homologous series means and compare the pairs given.
