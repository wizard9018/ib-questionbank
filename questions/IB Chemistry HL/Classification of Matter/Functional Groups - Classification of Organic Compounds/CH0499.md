## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Option A also produces a singlet $\ce{(OCH3)}$, a triplet (ethyl $\ce{CH3}$) and a quartet $\ce{(CH2)}$ in a $3:3:2$ integral ratio, as observed.  However, it would **not** produce a $\ce{[M - 31]+}$ fragment ion in its mass spectrum: this corresponds to the loss of the $\ce{-OCH3}$ fragment (the loss of a $\ce{CH2CH3}$ fragment would produce a $\ce{[M - 29]+}$ fragment).

Option B produces a singlet $\ce{(OCH3)}$, a triplet (acetyl $\ce{CH3}$) and a quartet $\ce{(CH2)}$ in a $3:3:2$ integral ratio, as observed. It also contains a ethoxy fragment that is consistent with the mass spectrum. Option B is therefore consistent with all of the data.

Option C could produce the correct fragment ion peak, but its $\ce{NMR}$ spectrum would feature two singlets in a $6:2$ ratio.

Option D would not produce the correct fragment ion peak and displays only a triplet and a quartet in its $\ce{NMR}$ spectrum, in a $3:2$ integral ratio.

This question is categorized as very hard since the student must interpret data from two techniques, calculate the mass of a fragment ion and predict patterns of $\ce{NMR}$ signals from structural formulas.
