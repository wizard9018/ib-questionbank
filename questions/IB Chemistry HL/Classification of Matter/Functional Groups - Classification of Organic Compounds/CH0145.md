# CH0145

Which of the following is an example of unsaturated hydrocarbons?

-   I.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{CH4}$

-   II.$\hspace{0.7em}$ $\ce{CH3Cl}$

-   III.$\hspace{0.5em}$ $\ce{C2H4}$

-   IV.$\hspace{0.5em}$ $\ce{C2H2}$

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$IV only

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$II and III only

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$III and IV only

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Choices III and IV are ethene and ethyne with a double bond and a triple bond, respectively. They belong to the classes of alkenes and alkynes, which can be deduced by the general formulae, $\ce{C_nH_{2n}}$ and $\ce{C_nH_{2n-2}}$.

Choice I is the alkane methane, and choice II is a halogenoalkane (chloromethane).
