# CH1166

What is the IUPAC name of the structure below?

![CH1166](images/CH1166.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$pent-1-ene

- B. $\hspace{1em}$2-methyl-but-1-ene

- C. $\hspace{1em}$3-methylbutene

- D. $\hspace{1em}$3-methyl- but-1-ene

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The molecule has a straight chain of 4 carbons with a double bond on the first bond. It is a but-1-ene but also presents a methyl branch on the third carbon (carbon 1 is assigned as the first carbon in the double bond here). The complete name for it is 3-methyl but-1-ene

This question is categorized as medium difficulty, as students must apply the IUPAC rules for a specific alkene.
