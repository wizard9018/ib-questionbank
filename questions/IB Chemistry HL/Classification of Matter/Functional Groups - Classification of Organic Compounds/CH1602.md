# CH1602

Which molecules are from the same homologous series?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH2CH2OH}$ and $\ce{CH3CH2CHO}$

-   B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH2COOH}$ and $\ce{CH3CH2COCH3}$

-   C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{C2H6}$ and $\ce{C9H18}$

-   D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3OCH3}$ and $\ce{CH3CH2CH2OCH3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\

A homologous series is a group of organic compounds with the same functional group and a similar general formula, differing by a $\ce{CH2}$ group. Members exhibit a gradation in physical properties and have similar chemical behaviors.

To answer this question, identify the class of each molecule from its condensed structural formula since all molecules belonging to the same homologous series belong to the same class of organic compounds.     

-   $\ce{CH3CH2CH2OH}$ and $\ce{CH3CH2CHO}$: alcohol and aldehyde

-   $\ce{CH3CH2COOH}$ and $\ce{CH3CH2COCH3}$: carboxylic acid and ketone

-   $\ce{C2H6}$ and $\ce{C9H_{18}}$: alkane and alkene

-   $\ce{CH3OCH3}$ and $\ce{CH3CH2CH2OCH3}$: ether and ether
