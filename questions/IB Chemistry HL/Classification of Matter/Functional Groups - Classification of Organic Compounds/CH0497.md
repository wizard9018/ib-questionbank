## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The 120 $^\circ$ and 109 $^\circ$ bond angles are indicative of $sp^2$ (three electron domains) and $sp^3$ (four electron domains) carbon centres respectively, so the compound must be butanone.

This question is categorized as moderately difficult as the student must recall key bond angles from earlier in the course and also correctly interpret skeletal formulas.
