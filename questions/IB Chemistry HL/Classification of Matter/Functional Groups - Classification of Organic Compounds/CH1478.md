        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        The functional group gives a molecule its characteristic properties. It refers to a group of atoms in a molecule and is the reactive part of the molecule.

        Be careful to distinguish between class and functional group: a class is a broader category that defines a group of compounds sharing similar structural characteristics and reactivity, such as alcohols, ketones, or aldehydes, while a functional group is a specific group of atoms within a molecule that determines the molecule's characteristic chemical reactions, such as the hydroxy group in alcohols or the carbonyl group in ketones and aldehydes.  Also note that the name for the $\ce{-OH}$ group has been updated since the original publication of the IB DP Chemistry Subject Guide from hydroxyl to the correct hydroxy.

    2.  Presence of a multiple/double bond in the structure $\checkmark$

        $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

        A compound containing multiple bonds or cyclic structures is described as unsaturated. A cyclic structure or double bond introduces one degree of unsaturation to a molecule, while triple bonds introduce two degrees of unsaturation.
