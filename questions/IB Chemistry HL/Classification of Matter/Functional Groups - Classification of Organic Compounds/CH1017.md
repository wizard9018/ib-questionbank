# CH1017

Which statement(s) about ethanoic acid is(are) correct?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :- | :- |
    | I. | Its empirical formula is $\ce{CH2O}.$ |
    | II. | Its molecular ion in the mass spectrum has $m/z = 60.$ |
    | III. | It can be obtained when ethanol reacts with acidified potassium dichromate by distillation. |

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$I only

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$II only

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Ethanoic acid, $\ce{CH3COOH},$ has the molecular formula $\ce{C2H4O2}.$ Dividing the subscripts by their common factor of 2 gives the empirical formula $\ce{CH2O},$ the simplest whole$-$number ratio of atoms.

The molecular ion in a mass spectrum is formed when a molecule loses an electron. This is the largest mass peak in the mass spectrum. The $m/z$ is the same as the formula mass, $m/z = 60$.

Ethanol oxidizes in the presence of acidified potassium dichromate. However, ethanal forms if distillation takes place. Ethanoic acid is produced if the reaction is carried out by heating under reflux only.
