# CH0367

Compound $\mathbf{X}$ has a molecular mass of 148.

The mass spectrum of compound $\mathbf{X}$ contains a peak at *m/z* = 117.

Which of the following spectral fragments is lost from compound $\mathbf{X}$ to produce this peak in the mass spectrum?

Atomic masses (amu) $\ce{C} = 12$, $\ce{H} = 1$, $\ce{O} = 16$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH2CH3}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{COOH}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3O}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CO}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The masses of options A, B, C and D are 29, 45, 31 and 28, respectively.

The mass of the fragment ion is $148 - 117 = 31$, making option C correct.

This question is of moderate difficulty as two simple calculations are required.
