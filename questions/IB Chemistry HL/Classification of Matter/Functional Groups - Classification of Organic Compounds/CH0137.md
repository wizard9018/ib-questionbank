# CH0137

Which set of molecules are from the same homologous series $\textbf{and}$ correctly arranged in order of increasing boiling point?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$Ethyne, ethene, ethane

- B.$\hspace{1em}$Butanol, propanol, pentanol

- C.$\hspace{1em}$Dimethylpropane, methylbutane, pentane

- D.$\hspace{1em}$Butanal, ethanal, hexanal

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Branched alkanes have lower boiling points than their straight chain counterparts, as branched compounds pack together less efficiently and so form weaker London (dispersion) forces.
