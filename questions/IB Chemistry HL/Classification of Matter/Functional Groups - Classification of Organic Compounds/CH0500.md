# CH0500

The structural formula of ethylbenzene is shown.

![CH0500](images/CH0500.png)

One of the signals in the $\ce{^1H NMR}$ spectrum of ethylbenzene is a quartet.

What are the integrals of the four peaks in the quartet signal, expressed as a ratio?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}1:2:2:1$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}1:3:3:1$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}2:3:3:2$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}2:5:5:2$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The quartet signal is produced by the $\ce{CH2}$ group, as it is adjacent to three equivalent protons in the $\ce{CH3}$ group.

The student must recognize that a quartet signal displays the integral ratio $1:3:3:1$, in accordance with Pascal's triangle.

The question is of moderate difficulty due to the amount of additional information presented.
