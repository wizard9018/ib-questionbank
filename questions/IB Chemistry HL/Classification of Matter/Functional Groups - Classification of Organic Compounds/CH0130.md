  | II. | Propanol |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$I and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Saturated compounds contain only single bonds.

Butyne is an alkyne, and so, has a triple bond.
