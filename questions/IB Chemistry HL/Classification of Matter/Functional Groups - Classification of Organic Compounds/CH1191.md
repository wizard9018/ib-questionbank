# CH1191

An unknown molecule, **X**, is analyzed through mass spectrometry and IR spectroscopy.

The IR spectrum showed a broad absorption at $3600\,\text{cm}^{-1};$ the mass spectrum is shown below.

*IR Absorption 3200 - 3600 $cm^{-1}$: alcohols and phenols (with hydrogen bonding).*

![CH1191](images/CH1191.png)

What is the chemical formula of molecule **X**?

- A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{C3H8}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{C2H6}$

- C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{C3H7OH}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{C2H5OH}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The IR spectrum evidences a broad absorption near $3600\,\text{cm}^{-1}.$ This suggests the presence of hydroxyl group in an alcohol.

Options A and B can be disregarded as they are alkanes: $\ce{C3H8}$ is propane, and $\ce{C2H6}$ is ethane.

Mass spectrometry is a technique that breaks up compounds and detects their fragments as ions. The fragments correspond to parts of the molecule. The peak with the highest $m/z$ (mass-to-charge ratio) is a mass spectrum of the molecular ion peak and corresponds to the ion of the parent compound. We can use it to deduce the molar mass of the compound.

In this case, the molecular ion peak occurs at $m/z = 46.$

![CH1191B](images/CH1191B.png)

The molar mass of the compound is, therefore, $46\,\text{g mol}^{-1}.$ Option C is disregarded as the molar mass of $\ce{C3H7OH}$ is $60.11\,\text{g mol}^{-1}.$

The compound is ethanol $\ce{C2H5OH}.$

This question is categorized as hard as it requires a deep understanding of the interpretation of the MS spectra in combination with the IR spectrum to identify possible functional groups and deduce the molecule identity.
