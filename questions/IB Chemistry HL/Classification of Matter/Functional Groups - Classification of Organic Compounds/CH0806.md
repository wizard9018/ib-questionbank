# CH0806

Compound **Y** has the molecular formula $\ce{C4H8O2}$ and produces the following IR spectrum.

Which gives a possible structure for **Y**?

IR absorptions:

-   $\ce{C=O}: 1700 - 1750 \textrm{\ cm}^{-1}$;
-   $\ce{O-H}\,\textit{(alcohol)}: 3200 - 3600\,\text{cm}^{-1}\,\textit{(broad)}$;
-   $\ce{O-H}\,\textit{(carboxylic acid)}: 2500 - 3000\,\text{cm}^{-1}\,\textit{(very broad)}$

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The spectrum shows peaks for $\ce{O-H}$ (alcohol) and C=O. The $\ce{O-H}$ peak is a characteristic broad $\ce{O-H}$ peak associated with an alcohol and not one characteristic of the very broad signal at lower wavenumber in a carboxylic acid.

B is incorrect as it is a carboxylic acid and would have a very broad $\ce{O-H}$ (carboxylic acid): $2500 - 3000$  cm$^{-1}$.

C is an ester and so would not have the broad $\ce{O-H}$ (alcohol): $3200 - 3600$ cm$^{-1}$ peak.

D does not have a C=O bond and so would not have the strong peak at $1700 - 1750$ cm$^{-1}$.
