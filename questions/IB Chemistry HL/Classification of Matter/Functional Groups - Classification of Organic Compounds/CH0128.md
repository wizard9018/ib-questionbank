# CH0128

Which of the following groups of molecules all belong to the same homologous series?

- A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CHO}, \ce{CHOCH2CH2CH3}, \ce{CH3CH2CHO}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH2CH2}, \ce{CH3CHCHCH2CH3}, \ce{CH3CH2CCH}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3COCH3}, \ce{CH3CHO}, \ce{CH3COCH2CH3}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{CH3CH2CH2OH}, \ce{CH3CH2CH2CHO}, \ce{CH3CHO}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Molecules in a homologous series have the same functional group and differ by $\ce{CH2}$.

The molecules in A are all aldehydes. B includes alkenes and alkynes, C is a mixture of aldehydes and ketones, D is a mixture of aldehydes and alcohols.
