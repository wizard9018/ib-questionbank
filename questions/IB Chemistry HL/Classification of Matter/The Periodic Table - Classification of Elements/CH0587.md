# CH0587

**Maximum mark: 3**

The reaction shown below will not occur under standard conditions:       

$\ce{Br2(l) + 2 NaF(aq) -> F2(aq) + 2 NaBr(aq)}$

1.  State a balanced chemical equation for a reaction that will occur, producing sodium bromide. [2 marks]

2.  The reverse reaction

    $\ce{F2(g) + 2 NaBr(aq) -> Br2(aq) + 2 NaF(aq)}$

    will occur. Describe an observation that could be made to show that the reaction is occurring.  [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\ce{Br2(l) + 2 NaI(aq) -> I2(aq) + 2 NaBr(aq)}$ $\checkmark \checkmark$

    *Award M1 for selecting NaI\

    Award M2 for correct state symbols*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\

    A more reactive halogen will displace a less reactive halogen from its compound. Reactivity decreases down group 17 and so bromine will displace iodine but not fluorine (or chlorine).

2.  colorless to orange$-$brown

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\

    Aqueous bromine is orangish brown and fluorine gas is nearly colorless.  NaBr and NaF are colorless and would not have an observable effect.
