# CH0649

Which of the following is NOT a potential component of acid rain?                       

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Phosphorous acid, $\ce{H3PO3}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Nitrous acid, $\ce{HNO2}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Sulfurous acid, $\ce{H2SO3}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Sulfuric acid, $\ce{H2SO4}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Acid rain is formed from sulfur and nitrogen oxides dissolving in the clouds.
