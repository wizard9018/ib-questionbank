# CH2183

What is the ligand in the complex $\ce{K4Fe(CN)6}?$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{K+}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Fe^2+}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Fe^3+}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CN-}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand that a ligand is a molecule or ion with a lone pair of electrons that can be donated to the transition metal cation in a coordinate bond formation. In this case, the cyanide acts as a ligand as shown below.

![CH2183](images/CH2183.png)

This question is categorized as medium as the student must understand the term ligand and identify the corresponding ligand in the formula.
