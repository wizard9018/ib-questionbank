# CH2107

Consider the elements in the periodic table below:

![CH2107](images/CH2107.png)

Which statement is correct?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :- | :- |
    | I. | Atomic radius increases as $\ce{Z} < \ce{Y} < \ce{X}.$ |
    | II. | X has four valence electrons. |
    | III. | Electronegativity increases as $\ce{Z} < \ce{Y} < \ce{X}.$ |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$II and III

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$III only

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The atomic radius decreases to the right in a period (as the effective nuclear charge is greater) and increases down a group (as electrons are in higher energy level shells farther from the nucleus). Z has the largest radius as it appears in the third period so the outermost electron is in the $n = 3$ level. X and Y are both in the second period. Since X has more protons for the same number of shielding electrons, it is smaller than Y. Statement I is incorrect.

X belongs to group 16, so it has six valence electrons. Its outer electronic configuration is $ns^2\ np^4.$ Statement II is incorrect.

Electronegativity is defined as the tendency of an atom to attract a bonding pair of electrons. It increases across a period due to increasing nuclear charge and decreases down a group as the atomic radius increases, reducing the attraction between the nucleus and bonding electrons. Z has the lowest electronegativity and X has the highest. Statment III is correct.

This question is categorized as medium as the student must understand trends in the atomic radii, successive ionization energies and electronegativity.
