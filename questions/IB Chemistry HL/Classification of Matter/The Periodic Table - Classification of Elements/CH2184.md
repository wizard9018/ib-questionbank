# CH2184

Which variable does not affect the wavelength of maximum absorption of the complex ion formed by a particular transition element cation?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$concentration of the solution

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$oxidation state of the cation

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$identity of the ligand

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$identity of the transition metal

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand that light absorption in a transition metal complex ion depends on the split of the $d$ orbitals in the metal cation as the ligands form coordination bonds.

The magnitude of the split depends on the oxidation state of the transition element cation, the ligand, the number of ligands, the shape of of the complex ion, and the atomic number of transition element.

The split does not depend on the concentration of the complex ion. The concentration does affect the absorbance at the wavelength of maximum absorption, which allows for the use calibration curves. However, the wavelength of maximum absorption does not change with the concentration.

This question is categorized as hard as the student must identify variables affecting the magnitude of the energy gap produced by the split of $d$ orbitals in transition elements.
