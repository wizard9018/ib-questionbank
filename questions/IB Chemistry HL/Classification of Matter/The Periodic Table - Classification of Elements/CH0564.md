# CH0564

When $\ce{H2O}$ ligands in a transition metal complex are replaced by $\ce{Cl}^-$ ligands, the $d$ orbital splitting energy decreases.

Which of the following complexes would absorb light with the longest wavelength?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The student must recognize that a smaller $d$ orbital splitting energy corresponds to a longer wavelength of absorbed light and that lowering the oxidation state causes the separation to decrease also. The complex with the longest absorbed wavelength is, therefore, the hexachloro complex in which the metal has the smallest metal oxidation state.

This question is very hard as the student must recall multiple pieces of information, consider the effects of two different factors on $d$ orbital separation and deduce the oxidation state of a metal from a structural formula with only the overall charge given.
