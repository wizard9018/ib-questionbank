# CH0887

Which of the following is sulfuric(IV) acid?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H2S}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H2S2}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H2SO3}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{H2SO4}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Sulfur has an oxidation state of $+4$ in $\ce{H2SO3}$.

$\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$

In modern nomenclature, $\ce{H2SO3}$ is referred to as sulfuric(IV) acid while $\ce{H2SO4}$ is referred to as sulfuric(VI) acid. The number in Roman numerals refers to the oxidation state of the sulfur.

Traditionally, $\ce{H2SO3}$ is called sulfurous acid while $\ce{H2SO4}$ is called sulfuric acid.
