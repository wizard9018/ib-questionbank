# CH0109

Which compound contains two different elements with identical oxidation states?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{MgSO4}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{HBrO}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{NaNO3}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{CaCO3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

In HBrO, the oxidation states of both H and Br are +1.
