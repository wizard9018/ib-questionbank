# CH0586

**Maximum mark: 6**

The oxides of two period 3 elements, X and Y, react in water according to the equations below.

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide" col3="hide"}

    | | | |

    | :- | -: | :- |

    | I. | $\ce{XO2(g) + H2O(l) ->}$ | $\ce{H2XO3(aq)}$ |

    | II. | $\ce{YO(s) + H2O(l) ->}$ | $\ce{Y(OH)2(aq)}$ |

1.  Identify elements X and Y. Justify your response. [4 marks]

2.  Predict whether element X or Y would have a higher melting point. Justify your prediction. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Element X is sulfur. $\checkmark$

    Element Y is magnesium. $\checkmark$

    Element X is a nonmetal that forms a nonmetal oxide XO$_2$. Nonmetal oxides produce acidic solutions in water.$\checkmark$

    Element Y must be a metal that forms an ion with 2+ charge. Metal oxides produce a basic solution in water. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

    

    Consider Y: The oxide forms a hydroxide when combined with water, so it must be a basic oxide.  Metal oxides are basic.  Metal oxides and metal hydroxides are ionic compounds, form the stoichiometry of the compound, we can deduce the charge of the metal: Y ion should be 2+ to cancel out the charge of $1-$ of each hydroxide ion of the $2-$ of the oxide. Magnesium is the only metal in period 3 that forms an ion with a charge of 2+.

    Consider X: The oxide forms an acid when combined with water, so it must be a nonmetal oxide.  In Period 3, the common acidic oxides are $\ce{SiO2}$, $\ce{P4O6}$, $\ce{P4O_{10}}$, $\ce{SO2}$, $\ce{SO3}$, $\ce{Cl2O}$ and $\ce{Cl2O7}$. As phosporus and chlorine do not make an oxide with the correct formula, we can eliminate them.  $\ce{SiO2}$ is a solid with a very high melting point that is insoluble in water.  We can eliminate silicon.  For sulfur, we can consider the reaction of the oxide with water:

    $\ce{SO2} + \ce{H2O} \longrightarrow \ce{H2SO3}$

    Sulfur's oxide and corresponding acid support the conclusion that element X is S. 

2.  Element Y (Mg) will have a higher melting point than element X (S). $\checkmark$

    Mg demonstrates metallic bonding while sulfur is a nonmetal with weak intermolecular forces resulting in relatively low melting point compared to Mg.  $\checkmark$   

    $\textit{For the 2nd mark, both Mg and S must be mentioned.}$
