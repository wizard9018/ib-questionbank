# CH1510

Which of the following trends is observed in the periodic table across a period from left to right?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Atomic radius increases

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Electronegativity decreases

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$First ionization energy increases

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Metallic character increases

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

As we move from left to right, we add electrons to the same principal energy shell. At the same time, the nuclear charge increases as the number of protons in the nucleus increases. This increased nuclear charge and constant shielding cause

-  *    a decrease in the atomic radius
-  *   an increase in the electronegativity as the attraction for incoming electrons increases
-  *   an increase in the first ionization energy as attraction of the nucleus gets stronger on the valence shell electrons
-  *   a decrease in the metallic character as we move from metals to metalloids to nonmetals due to increasing ionization energies across the period.
