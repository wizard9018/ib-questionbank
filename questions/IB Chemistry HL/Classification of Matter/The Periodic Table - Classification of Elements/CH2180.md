# CH2180

What is the oxidation state of chromium in $\ce{[Cr(NH3)2(OH)4]-}?$

-   A. $\hspace{1em}+3$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}+2$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}+4$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}+6$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must be able to deduce the oxidation state of the transition metal in the complex ion.

The state of Cr is unknown, $x.$ The charge of ammonia is 0 as it is a neutral molecule, and the charge of the hydroxide ion is ${1-}.$ The addition of all of the components must be equal to the charge of the complex ion, ${1-}.$

$x + 2\times 0 + 4\times (-1) = -1$ \
$x = +3$

The oxidation state of chromium is +3 and the ion is $\ce{Cr^3+}.$

This question is categorized as easy as the student must work out the oxidation state of the transition metal as a cation in the complex ion.
