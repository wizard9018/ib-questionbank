# CH0571

**Maximum mark: 9**

The complexes $\ce{[Co(NH3)6]^{3+}}$ and $\ce{[Co(NH3)5Cl]^{2+}}$ both absorb visible light. The optical absorption spectra of the two complexes are shown.

![13.2-CH0571](images/CH0571.png)
[Source: own work, based on data from https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/ic020253o and https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/jp301459s]

1.  State the type of bond formed between the metal ion and ligand in the $\ce{[Co(NH3)6]^3+}$ ion. [1 mark]

2.  Draw the structural formula of the $\ce{[Co(NH3)6]^3+}$ ion, using dash$-$and$-$wedge notation to represent the geometry of the complex. [3 marks]

3.  Explain why a solution of the $\ce{[Co(NH3)6]^3+}$ ion appears orange, using Section 15 of the IB Chemistry data booklet. [2 marks]

4.  Based on the absorption spectra, state and explain which ligand $(\ce{NH3}$ or $\ce{Cl})$ is stronger. [2 marks]

5.  Suggest the colour of the $\ce{[Co(NH3)5Cl]^2+}$ ion in solution. [1 mark]
