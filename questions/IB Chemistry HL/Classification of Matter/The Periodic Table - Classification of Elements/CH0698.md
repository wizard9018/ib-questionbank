# CH0698

**Maximum mark: 8**

1.  Bromine has a relative atomic mass of 79.90 and two common isotopes $^{79}$Br and $^{81}$Br. Calculate the percentage abundance of the $^{81}$Br isotope. [2 marks]

2.  State the equation for the first electron affinity of iodine. [1 mark]

3.  Describe and explain the trend in the magnitude of electron affinity from fluorine to iodine. [3 marks]

4.  Fluorine reacts with sodium iodide. State the net ionic equation for this reaction and name the type of reaction. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $79.90 = \dfrac{79(100 - x) + 81x}{100} \: \checkmark$\
    $x = 45\%$ $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
    The relative atomic mass is the weighted average of the mass of each isotope.

    $A_r = \dfrac{(\textrm{mass isotope  1} \times \%\textrm{abundance}) + (\textrm{mass isotope  2} \times \% \textrm{abundance}) + \ldots}{100}$

    With only two isotopes, the abundances are unknown but must add up to $100\%$. The two abundances can therefore be substituted as $x$ and $100-x$.

2.  $\ce{I(g) + e- -> I-(g)} \: \checkmark$\
    *State symbols required.*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
    Electron affinity is the energy change when one mole of electrons is added to one mole of gaseous atoms.

3.  magnitude of electron affinity decreases / becomes less exothermic $\checkmark$\
    number of shells/shielding effect increases $\checkmark$\
    electrostatic force of attraction between the nucleus and electron decreases $\checkmark$

4.  $\ce{F2 + 2 I- -> 2 F- + I2}\,\checkmark$\
    (single) displacement **OR** redox $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:\
    The overall equation is $\ce{F2 + 2 NaI -> 2 NaF + I2}$. The $\ce{Na+}$ is a spectator ion and is not included in the net ionic equation. Fluorine is more reactive than iodine, displacing it from its compound. The fluorine is reduced to fluoride ions, and the iodide ions are oxidized to elemental iodine.
