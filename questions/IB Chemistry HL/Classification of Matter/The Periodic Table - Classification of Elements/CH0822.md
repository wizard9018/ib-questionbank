# CH0822

What is the oxidation state on the chromium ion in the complex ion with a formula $\ce{[Cr(H2O)2Cl4]-}$?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$+4

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$+3

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$+2

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$+1

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The overall charge on the complex ion is $1-$, water is neutral and the chloride ion has a preferred oxidation state of $-1.$

$-1 = 2(0) + 4(-1) + \ce{Cr}$

Cr has an oxidation state of $+3$.
