# CH2109

The formula of an unknown metal nitrate is $\ce{MNO3}.$ What is the formula of this metal sulfide?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{M2S}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{MS}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{M2S3}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{MS2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must be able to deduce the charge on M in the metal nitrate. The nitrate ion is $\ce{NO3-}.$ One nitrate ion is combined with one metallic cation if the formula is $\ce{MNO3}.$ As a result, the cation must be $\ce{M+}.$

The metal sulfide results from the combination of $\ce{M+}$ and $\ce{S^2-}.$ These two ions are combined in a $2:1$ ratio and the resulting formula is $\ce{M2S}.$
