# CH1511

Which of the following statements is true about the alkali metals as one moves down the group?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The electrical conductivity increases.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The reactivity decreases.

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The atomic radius decreases.

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The melting point increases.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Down group 1, the atomic radii increase due to the additional energy levels. As a result, the first ionization energy decreases as it becomes easier to remove electrons from the valence shell of the metal atom. All these factors cause

-   an increase in electrical conductivity due to decreasing ionization energy making loss of electrons to the sea of electrons easier

-   an increase in reactivity as the valence electron is easily removed to create a M$^+$ ion.
-   an increase in atomic radius because there are more electronic shells
-   a decrease in the strength of metallic bonding $\textit{i.e.},$ a weaker attraction between the metal cations and the delocalized electrons, causing a decrease in the melting point
