# CH0699

[Maximum marks: 10\]

Magnesium is an element in group 2 of the periodic table.

1.  State the full electron configuration for magnesium and the block it can be found in the periodic table. [2 marks]

2.  Explain how successive ionization energies can prove that magnesium is in group 2 and period 3 of the periodic table. [3 marks]

3.  State the equation for the second ionization energy of magnesium. [1 mark]

4.  State an equation for the reaction between magnesium oxide and water, and comment on the pH of the resultant solution. [2 marks]

5.  By referring to their relative ionization energies, compare the metallic behavior of magnesium to calcium and beryllium (values from the data booklet are not required). [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2$; $\checkmark$

    $s$ (block). $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    The $3s$ subshell is the highest energy subshell placing magnesium in the $s$-block.

2.  Group 2: Large increase in energy between 2$^{nd}$ and 3$^{rd}$ ionization energies shows that there are two electrons in the outermost shell. $\checkmark$

    Period 3: Two significant increases in energy (between 2$^{nd}$ and 3$^{rd}$, and 10$^{th}$ and 11$^{th}$ ionization energies) shows that there are three distinct energy levels. $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    Large increases in ionization energy suggest an electron is being removed from an energy level closer to the nucleus.

3.  $\ce{Mg+(g) -> Mg^2+(g) + e-}\,\checkmark$

    *State symbols required*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    The second ionization energy is the energy required to remove one mole of electrons from one mole of gaseous singly positive ions. This equation follows from the first ionization energy definition, which must be known.

4.  $\ce{MgO(s) + H2O(l) -> Mg(OH)2(aq)}\,\checkmark$

    *State symbols NOT required*

    pH greater than 7 $\checkmark$

    *ALLOW any value greater than 7*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation}}$:

    Metallic hydroxides are basic.

5.  Calcium is more metallic than magnesium, **AND** beryllium is less metallic than magnesium $\checkmark$

    Ionization energies decrease down group 2 $\checkmark$

    Metals have a greater tendency to lose electrons **OR** are more easily oxidized OWTTE $\checkmark$
