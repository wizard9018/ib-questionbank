# CH2181

Why is $\ce{Co(H2O)6^2+}$ pink?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Pink light is absorbed when electrons transition to a higher energy $d$ orbital.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Light complementary to pink is absorbed when electrons transition to higher energy $d$ orbital.

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Pink light is emitted when electrons transition from a higher energy $d$ orbital.

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Light complementary to pink is emitted when electrons transition from a higher energy $d$ orbital.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand that transition metal complexes are coloured due to light absorption when electrons are promoted to higher energy orbitals resulting from degenerate d orbitals splitting. The colour observed corresponds to the complementary colour of the colour absorbed.

This question is categorized as a medium as the student must understand the origin of the colour in transition element complexes and the relationship between the colours absorbed and observed.
