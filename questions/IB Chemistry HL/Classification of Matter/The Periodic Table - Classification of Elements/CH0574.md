# CH0574

**Maximum mark: 7**

An aqueous solution of the $\ce{[V(H2O)6]^3+}$ complex ion strongly absorbs light with a wavelength of 610 nm.

1. Explain why an aqueous solution of the $\ce{[V(H2O)6]^3+}$ ion appears $\textbf{blue}$, using the data booklet. [2 marks]

2. A $\textbf{neutral}$ vanadium atom contains five $d$ orbitals of equal energy, as shown in the diagram.

   ![13.2-CH0574](images/CH0574.png)

   Draw a similar diagram to show the most stable configuration of $d$ electrons in the $\ce{[V(H2O)6]^3+}$ ion. [2 marks]

3. In concentrated sulfuric acid, one water ligand in $\ce{[V(H2O)6]^3+}$ is replaced by a sulfate  ($\ce{SO4^2-}$) ion. The resulting solution is $\textbf{green}$ in colour.

   Explain how the relative strenght of the ligands can be deduced from the colour change in this reaction. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  610 nm corresponds to orange light $\checkmark$\
    orange is complementary to blue $\checkmark$

2.  lower energy level with three orbitals, higher  level with two orbitals $\checkmark$\
    two electrons with the same spin in separate orbitals of the lower level $\checkmark$

    ![13.2-CH0574-1](images/CH0574-1.png)

    *Award [1] if arrangement of orbitals is incorrect but two unpaired electrons with the same spin are drawn in the orbitals with the lowest available energy;\
    Do $\textbf{not}$ award M1 if the orbitals within each energy level are not clearly drawn with the same y ordinate;\
    Do $\textbf{not}$ award M1 if two distinct energy levels are not clearly visible;\
    Do $\textbf{not}$ award M2 if the spins of the electrons are unclear.*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
    The $d$ orbitals are split into two groups of different energy. The lower (energy) group contains 3 orbitals *AND* the higher (energy) group contains 2 orbitals. Electrons will singly occupy the lowest energy orbitals first with parallel spins.

3.  green is complementary to red $\checkmark$\
    red light has a lower energy than orange light absorbed by $\ce{[V(H2O)6]^3+} \checkmark$\
    sulfate is a weaker ligand  **OR** sulfate produces a smaller $d$ orbital  splitting than water $\checkmark$

    *Award M2 if the wavelength range of red light is stated;\
    Award M2 for a diagram or spectrum illustrating the relative energies of red and orange light;\
    Award M3 for a diagram illustrating that the $d$ orbital separation in the product complex is smaller.*

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\
    The stronger the ligand, the greater the splitting energy, and, therefore, the shorter the wavelength of light absorbed
