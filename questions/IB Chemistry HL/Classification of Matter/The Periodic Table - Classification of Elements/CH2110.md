# CH2110

What is the similarity between He, Ne, Ar and Kr?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :- | :- |
    | I. | They have 8 electrons in their outer shell. |
    | II. | They are diatomic gases at STP. |
    | III. | They have a low chemical reactivity. |

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$I, II and III

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must identify He, Ne, Ar and Kr as noble gases. These elements belong to Group 18 of the periodic table. All of them have a full outer shell. However, helium only has two electrons; its electron configuration is $1s^2.$ Statement I is incorrect.

In addition, all of them are monatomic gases as they are unreactive and do not tend to combine. They exist as X(g) rather than $\ce{X2(g)}.$ Statement II is incorrect and Statement III is correct.

This question is categorized as a medium as the student must recall the characteristics of noble gases.
