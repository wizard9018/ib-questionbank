# CH0106

The oxidation state of Cr does not change in all of the following $\textbf{except}$: 

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{CrO4^{2-} -> CrO3}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{CrO4^{2-} -> Cr2O7^{2-}}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{K2Cr2O7 -> CrO4^{2-}}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{K2CrO4 -> Cr2O3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The sum of the oxidation state of all atoms in a compound is 0 and in an ion is equal to the charge of the ion.

O in the above molecules have an oxidation state of -2.

Group I elements have an oxidation state of +1.

In choices A, B, and C, the oxidation states of Cr on both sides are +6.

In choice D, the oxidation state of Cr changes from +6 to +3.
