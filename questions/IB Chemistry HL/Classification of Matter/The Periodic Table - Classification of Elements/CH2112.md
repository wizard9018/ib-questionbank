# CH2112

Consider the species below with their corresponding radii.

![CH2112](images/CH2112.png)

Which combination is correct?

-   :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}
    | | $\qquad$X$\qquad$ | $\qquad$Y$\qquad$ | $\qquad$Z$\qquad$ |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: |:-: |
    | A.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\ce{F-}$ | $\ce{Na+}$ | $\ce{Mg^2+}$ |
    | B. | $\ce{Mg^2+}$ | $\ce{Na+}$ | $\ce{F-}$ |
    | C. | $\ce{N}$ | $\ce{O}$ | $\ce{F}$ |
    | D. | $\ce{F}$ | $\ce{N}$ | $\ce{O}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
From the image, $r_\ce{Y} > r_\ce{Z} > r_\ce{X}$.  The student must compare three isoelectronic species ($\ce{F-}$, $\ce{Na+}$, and $\ce{Mg^{2+}}$) and three neutral atoms (N, O, and F) to find the best answer.

***Isoelectronic species:***
The ions $\ce{F-}$, $\ce{Na+}$, and $\ce{Mg^{2+}}$ have the same electron configuration, $1s^2 2s^2 2p^6$, but different numbers of protons. The magnesium ion has 12 protons, the sodium ion has 11 protons, and the fluoride ion has 9 protons.  The increased attraction between the outermost electrons and the nucleus indicates that the ionic radii would be ordered $r_\ce{F-} > r_\ce{Na+} > r_\ce{Mg^{2+}}.$ X should be the magnesium cation, Z should be the sodium cation, and Y should be the fluoride ion.

***Atomic species:***
Across a period, the outermost electrons are in the same shell so the number of shielding electrons is constant but the number of protons is increasing.  Consequently, atomic radius decreases from left to right on the periodic table: $r_\ce{N} > r_\ce{O} > r_\ce{F}.$ X should be fluorine, Z should be the oxygen, and Y should be nitrogen.
