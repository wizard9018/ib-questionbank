**Maximum mark: 8**

The positions of selected elements in the $s$ and $d$ blocks are shown in the periodic table below:

![3.1-560](images/3.1-560.png)

1. State the full electron configuration of a neutral atom of Fe. [1 mark]

2. Atoms of Cu and K both contain one electron in the $4s$ subshell.

   Explain why copper is described as a $d$ block element, whereas potassium is found in the $s$ block. [2 marks]

3. The elements Ac and Rf are separated by 14 elements in the $f$ block of the periodic table.

   1. State the name given to the elements between Ac and Rf. [1 mark]

   2. Explain why there are 14 elements between Ac and Rf. [2 marks]

4. Fe and Cu both form atoms or ions with partially filled $d$ subshells. $\mbox{}\\[10pts]$

   1. State the name given to elements of this type. [1 mark]

   2. Suggest why the name stated in d(i) can also be applied to some of the 14 elements between Ac and Rf. [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\

    The atomic number of Fe is 26. In a neutral atom, the number of protons is equal to the number of electrons. Therefore, iron has 26 electrons in its atom.  Following the aufbau principle, the electrons are filled as:
