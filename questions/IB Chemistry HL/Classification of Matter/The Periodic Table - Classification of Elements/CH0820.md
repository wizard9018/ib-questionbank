# CH0820

Which complex ion below would be colorless?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{[Fe(H2O)6]^3+}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{[Sc(H2O)6]^3+}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{[CuCl4]^2-}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{[Co(NH3)6]^2+}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The $\ce{Sc^3+}$ ion  has an electron configuration $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6$, as the 3 electrons lost in the ion are the two electrons in the $4s$ sublevel and the one electron in the $3d$ sublevel. There are no d electrons present in the ion, therefore no field splitting as a result of a ligand will occur.
