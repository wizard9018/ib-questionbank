# CH0821

The diagram of the orbitals of the $d$ sublevel are shown in the diagram below.

![CH0821](images/CH0821.png)

Which statement best describes the orbital splitting that occurs if a $\ce{Co^3+}$ ion forms the complex ion $\ce{[Co(NH3)6]^3+}$?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}{d_{xy}, d_{zx}, d_{yz}}$ would have higher energy than ${d_{x^2 - y^2}}$ and ${d_{z^2}}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}{d_{xy}, d_{zx}}$ would have higher energy than ${d_{yz}, d_{x^2-y^2},}$ and $d_{z^2}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}d_{xy}, d_{zx}, d_{yz}$ and ${d_{z^2}}$ would have higher energy than $d_{x^2 -y^2}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}d_{x^2 - y^2}$ and $d_{z^2}$  would have higher energy than $d_{xy}, d_{zx}, d_{yz}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
$\ce{[Co(NH3)6]^3+}$ forms an octahedral complex, the six ligands are arranged around the central metal ion in a symmetrical manner. The electrostatic repulsion between the ligands results in the splitting of the $d$-orbitals of the Co ion into two groups.

The three $d-$orbitals $(d_{xy}, d_{xz},\,\text{and } d_{yz})$ that point in between the ligands experience less repulsion than the two $d$-orbitals $(d_{x^2-y^2}$ and $d_{z^2})$ that point directly at the ligands. As a result, the $d_{xy}, d_{xz},$ and $d_{yz}$ orbitals form a lower energy set of orbitals and the $d_{x^2-y^2}$ and $d_{z^2}$ orbitals form a higher energy set of orbitals.
