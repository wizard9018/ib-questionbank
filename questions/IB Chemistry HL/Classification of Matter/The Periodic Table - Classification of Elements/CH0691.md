# CH0691

Which gives the correct reason and trend in metallic character down group 17?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide"}

  |    |Trend|Reason|

  |:-:|:-:|:-:|

  |A.$\hspace{1em}$|Increases|Increasing ionization energy

  |B.$\hspace{1em}$|Decreases|Increasing number of electrons

  |C.$\hspace{1em}$|Increases|Decreasing ionization energy

  |D.$\hspace{1em}$|Decreases|Increasing electron affinity

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Metals are more readily oxidized (lose electrons).

Down group 17, ionization energy decreases; therefore, less energy is required to remove electrons.
