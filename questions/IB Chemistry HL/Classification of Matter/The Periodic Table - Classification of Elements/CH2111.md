# CH2111

Which element is more likely to belong to the $s$ block?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$An element that forms an acidic oxide.

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$An element that reacts violently with water.

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$An element with more than three possible oxidation states when combined.

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$An element with an electronegativity above 2.3.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must be able to identify the $s\text{--}$block elements as shown in the periodic table here.

![CH2111](images/CH2111.png)

***Formation of oxides:*** Helium does not combine with oxygen to form an oxide. The oxide of hydrogen is water. Metals from groups 1 and 2 form basic oxides (either $\ce{M2O}$ or $\ce{MO}).$

***Reaction with water:*** Alkali metals, and to a lesser extent, alkaline earth metals react vigorously with water to form hydrogen gas, metal hydroxides, and heat.

$\begin{aligned}
\ce{M + H2O &-> MOH + \frac{1}{2} H2 + heat} \\
\ce{M + 2 H2O &-> M(OH)2 + H2 + heat}
\end{aligned}$

***Oxidation state:***
Elements in the $s\text{--}$block do not have multiple oxidation states when combined. Hydrogen is typically +1, but can be $-1$ in some metal hydrides. Otherwise, alkali metals are +1 when combined and alkaline earth metals from +2 when combined.

***Electronegativity:***
Elements in the $s\text{--}$block have a low electronegativity, they are said to be electropositive.  Metals are strong reducing agents, often losing their electrons to form metal cations.

This question is categorized as hard as the student contrasts the features described with those of members of the $s\text{--}$block and recalls their characteristics.
