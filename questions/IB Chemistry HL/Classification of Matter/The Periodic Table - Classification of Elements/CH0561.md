# CH0561

The $d$ orbitals of the $\ce{[Zn(H2O)6]^2+}$ ion are split into two energy levels.
Why does the $\ce{[Zn(H2O)6]^2+}$ ion appear colourless?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$The separation of the energy levels is larger than the energy of visible light.

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$The separation of the energy levels is smaller than the energy of visible light.

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$Every $d$ orbital contains a pair of electrons.

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$Every $d$ orbital is empty.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The electron configuration of $\ce{Zn^2+}$ is $\ce{[Ar]} \,3d^{10}$. There are no vacancies in the $d$ subshell so a transition between the split energy levels of the octahedral complex is not possible.
