        $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\

        The atomic number of I is 53. In a neutral atom, the number of protons is equal to the number of electrons. Therefore, iodine has 53 electrons in its atom. Following the aufbau principle, the 53 electrons are arranged as:

        $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6\,4s^2\,3d^{10}\,4p^6\,5s^2\,4d^{10}\,5p^5$

        The noble gas Kr has the electron configuration $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^2\,3p^6\,4s^2\,3d^{10}\,4p^6,$ so the condensed electron configuration of iodine is:

        $[\ce{Kr}] 5s^2\,4d^{10}\,5p^5$

4.  iodine is a nonmetal **AND** astatine is a metalloid / semimetal $\checkmark$\

    nonmetals are insulators, metalloids are thermal conductors $\checkmark$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$\

    Nonmetals are located on the right side of the staircase line, in zone $z.$ Metalloids are located on the staircase line, in zone $y.$

    With greater metallic/semimetallic character, astatine is expected to have more mobile electrons, so heat is carried efficiently by electrons; iodine is a molecular solid with only London dispersion forces and few charge carriers, so it conducts heat poorly.
