# CH0576

Which chemical equation represents the definition of first ionization energy?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{X(s) + e^- -> X^-(s)}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{X(s) -> X^+(s) + e^+}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{X(g) -> X^+(g) + e^-}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{X(g) + e^- -> X^-(g)}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

First ionization energy is defined as the energy required to remove one mole of electrons from one mole gaseous atoms.
