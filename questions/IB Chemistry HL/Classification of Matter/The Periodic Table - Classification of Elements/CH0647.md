# CH0647

All rain is naturally acidic due to dissolved __________.    

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Various salts

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Nitrogen oxides

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Carbon dioxide

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Sulfur dioxide

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The air is naturally composed of 0.04% carbon dioxide, which dissolves in water to form the weak carbonic acid.
