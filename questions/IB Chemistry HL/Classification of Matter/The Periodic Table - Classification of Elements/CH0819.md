# CH0819

The splitting of $d$ orbitals in a transition metal ion when it forms a coordinate covalent bonds with ligand is known as crystal field splitting.

The cyanide ion, $\ce{CN-}$, is a strong$-$field splitting ligand. Which statement is true regarding the color of a metal ion complexed with $\ce{CN-}$?

-   A.$\hspace{1em}$The light absorbed by the cyanide complex is likely to have a long wavelength

-   B.$\hspace{1em}$The light transmitted by the cyanide complex is likely to have a short wavelength

-   C.$\hspace{1em}$The light absorbed by the cyanide complex is likely to have a short wavelength

-   D.$\hspace{1em}$The light absorbed and transmitted depends upon the metal ion and not the ligand

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The energy difference between the two sets of orbitals depends on the nature of the ligand and the geometry of the complex.

Ligands that are strong$-$field, such as cyanide, create a larger energy difference between the two sets of orbitals than weak$-$field ligands, such as water and chloride.  The greater the split, or the energy difference, the shorter the wavelength.

Higher energy corresponds to shorter wavelength light, $E = hf$ and $c = \lambda f$, so $E = \dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$.
