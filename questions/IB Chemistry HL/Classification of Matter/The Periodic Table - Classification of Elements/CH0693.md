# CH0693

When added to water, which of the following compounds will result in a solution with a pH below 7? 

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{MgO}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Al2O3}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{SiO2}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{P4O10}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

$\ce{MgO}$ is a metal basic oxide, and $\ce{Al2O3}$ is an amphoteric oxide (it can act as an acid and as a base).

$\ce{SiO2}$ and $\ce{P4O10}$ are nonmetal acidic oxides; however, $\ce{SiO2}$ is insoluble in water.
