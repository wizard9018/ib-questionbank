    $\textit{Accept either argument one mark:}$

    

    The atomic radii for Elements 2 and 3 are greater than the atomic radii of Elements 1 and 4. Therefore, Elements 2 and 3 have an additional energy level making their respective radii larger. Elements 2 and 3 are S and Cl in period 3 rather than N and F in period 2. $\checkmark$

    The 1st IE for Elements 2 and 3 is less than the 1st IE for Elements 1 and 4. Therefore Elements 2 and 3 have an additional energy level making the strength of attraction for the valence electrons less than for Elements 1 and 4. $\checkmark$

    $\textit{Accept either argument one mark:}$

    The atomic radius for Element 2 is greater than the atomic radius for Element 3, so Element 2 must have fewer protons and lie to the left of Element 3 due to less attraction of valence electrons to the nucleus of the atoms. $\checkmark$

    The 1st IE for Element 2 is less than the 1st IE for Element 3, therefore Element 2 has fewer protons than Element 3, resulting in less attraction for the valence electrons of Element 2. $\checkmark$
