# CH0403

**Maximum mark: 6**

Scandium is a transition metal that can form both $2+$ and $3+$ complexes.

1. Draw the electron in boxes notation for the $4s$ and $3d$ electrons in an $\ce{Sc^2+}$ ion. [1 mark]

2. State the equation for the third ionization energy of scandium, and indicate which electron is lost. [2 marks]

3. Explain why $\ce{[Sc(H2O)6]^2+}$ is coloured whereas $\ce{[Sc(H2O)6]^3+}$ forms a colourless aqueous solution. [3 marks]

    $\textit{State symbols required for mark}$

    $\underline{\textrm{Further explanation:}}$

    Third ionization energy is the energy required to remove one mole of electrons from one mole of gaseous $2+$ ions.
