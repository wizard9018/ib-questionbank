# CH1009

Which of the following species has the largest oxidation state for sulfur?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}\ce{S8}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}\ce{Na2S}$

-   C. $\hspace{1em}\ce{CaSO4}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}\ce{SO3^2-}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Students must  assign oxidation states for sulfur in each case.

In $\ce{S8}$, the oxidation state of sulfur is zero as atoms are in the uncombined elemental state.

In $\ce{Na2S}$, the oxidation state of sodium is $+1$, and that of sulfur is $-2$.

In $\ce{CaSO4}$, the oxidation state of Ca is $+2$, the oxidation state of oxygen is $-2$, and that of sulfur can be deduced as shown below.

$\begin{aligned}
(+2) + (\ce{S}) + (4)(-2) &= 0 \\
2 + \ce{S} - 8 &= 0 \\
\ce{S} - 6 &= 0 \\
\ce{S} &= + 6
\end{aligned}$

The oxidation state of sulfur in $\ce{CaSO4}$ is $+6$.

In $\ce{SO3^2-}$, oxygen's oxidation state is $-2$, and that of sulfur can be deduced, as shown below.

$\begin{aligned}
(\ce{S}) + (3)(-2) &= -2 \\
\ce{S} - 6 &= -2 \\
\ce{S} &= +4
\end{aligned}$

The oxidation state of sulfur in $\ce{SO3^2-}$ is $+ 4$.

$\ce{CaSO4}$ has the largest oxidation state for sulfur.

This question is categorized as medium difficulty as students must deduce the oxidation state according to the rules.
