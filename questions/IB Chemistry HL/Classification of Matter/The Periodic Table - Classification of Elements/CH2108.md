# CH2108

Which factor generally increases when the electronegativity increases?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Nuclear charge

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Shielding effect

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Metallic character

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Atomic radius

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：A**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

Electronegativity is the ability of an atom to attract a bonding pair of electrons. This property increases across a period due to the increasing nuclear charge, which enhances the nucleus's ability to attract electrons. As the number of protons increases and the atomic radius decreases, the pull on bonding electrons becomes stronger.

Shielding reduces electronegativity because additional inner electron shells reduce the attraction between the nucleus and the bonding pair of electrons.

Larger atoms have lower electronegativity: the attraction between the nucleus and the bonding electrons decreases as the atomic radius increases according to Coulomb's Law.

Metallic character decreases with increasing electronegativity; elements that easily lose electrons (typical of metals) tend to have low electronegativity.

This question is considered easy, as it requires students to connect the concept of electronegativity with underlying atomic structure and periodic trends.
