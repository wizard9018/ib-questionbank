# CH1188

$\ce{Cr(H2O)6^3+}$ is violet.

Which statement about this transition metal complex ion is correct?

![PE2024- 1188](<images/PE2024- 1188.png>)

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$The colour of the complex ion is due to the absorption of energy corresponding to a wavelength near $410\,\text{nm}$

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$The colour of the complex ion is due to light emission in its structure

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$The colour of the complex ion is due to the absorption of energy corresponding to a wavelength near $580\,\text{nm}$

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$The colour observed occurs as $p$ orbitals in the ligand undergo a split

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Transition metals form complex ions, and $d$ orbitals are split. The difference in energy allows electrons in a lower energy level to be promoted to higher energy levels if energy is absorbed. The colour observed is complementary to the colour absorbed.

In this case, violet is observed because the absorption of yellow occurs (its complementary colour). As a result, the energy absorbed has a wavelength between 575 and $585\,\text{nm}$ (*section 15 of the Data Booklet*). Option C is correct.

This question is categorized as hard as students must interpret the colour of a specific transition metal complex ion in terms of the split of $d$-sublevels.
