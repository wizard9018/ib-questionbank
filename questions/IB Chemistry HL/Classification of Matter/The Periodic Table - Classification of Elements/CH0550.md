# CH0550

An atomic nucleus is surrounded by two occupied electron shells, as shown below:

![3.1-550](images/3.1-550.png)

To which group of the periodic table does this atom or ion belong?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$9

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$15

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$16

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$17

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

The diagram illustrates a $(2-)$ ion, as there are two more electrons than protons. The nucleus contains 7 protons so the element is nitrogen. The ion therefore belongs to Group 15. This question is moderately difficult as the student must derive the necessary information from a diagram and avoid being misled by extraneous information, such as the presence of 7 electrons in the outer shell.
