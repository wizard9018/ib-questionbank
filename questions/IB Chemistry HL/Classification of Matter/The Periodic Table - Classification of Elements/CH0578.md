# CH0578

A group 2 metal oxide dissolved in water would produce a solution that is:

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$Acidic 

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$Basic

-   C. $\hspace{1em}$Neutral

-   D. $\hspace{1em}$Possibly either acidic or basic

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Group 2 metal oxides produce alkaline metallic hydroxides when dissolved in water.
