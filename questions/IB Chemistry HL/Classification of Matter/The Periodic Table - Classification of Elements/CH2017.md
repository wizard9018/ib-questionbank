# CH2017

A complex ion is observed as blue. What is the wavelength of the light absorbed by the complex ion?

-   A. $\hspace{1em}$689 nm

-   B. $\hspace{1em}$590 nm
-   C. $\hspace{1em}$503 nm
-   D. $\hspace{1em}$413 nm

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：B**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$

The student must understand that the colour observed for a complex ion is complementary to the colour absorbed. The absorbed wavelength should correspond to orange if the complex ion appears blue. The corresponding wavelength can be deduced using the colour wheel. It must be within orange.

![CH2017](images/CH2017.png)

The wavelength should be between 585 nm and 647 nm.

This question is categorized as medium as the student must understand the origin of colours in complex ions and should be able to use the colour wheel.
