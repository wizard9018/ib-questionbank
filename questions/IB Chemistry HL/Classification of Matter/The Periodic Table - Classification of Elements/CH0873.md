# CH0873

Which of the following statements are *NOT* true?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Rain is naturally acidic due to sulphur oxides and nitrogen oxides |
  | II. | The reaction between sulphur dioxide and water produces sulfuric acid |
  | III. | The reaction between nitrogen dioxide and water produces nitric and nitrous acid |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$I and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$

Rain is naturally acidic due to the carbon dioxide in the air (forming carbonic acid) and not due to sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides, therefore statement I is false.

The reaction between sulphur dioxide and water produces sulphurous acid $\ce{(H2SO3)}$ and not sulfuric acid $\ce{(H2SO4)}$:

$\ce{SO2(g) + H2O(l) <=> H2SO3(aq)}$

Therefore, statement II is also false.

Unlike sulphur dioxide, the reaction between nitrogen dioxide and water produces both nitric acid and nitrous acid:

$\ce{2 NO2(g) + H2O(l) -> HNO3(aq) + HNO2(aq)}$

Therefore, statement III is true.

This question asks for the statements that are ***NOT*** true, so the answer is Option A: I and II only.
