# CH0064

Which of the following is the correct formula for strontium sulfide?

- A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Sr2S2}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{SrS2}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{SrS}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Sr2S}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：C**

$\textrm{\underline{Explanation:}}$\
Strontium is in group 2 and forms $\ce{Sr^{2+}}$ ions. Sulfur is in group 16 and forms $\ce{S^{2-}}$ ions. The charges are equal and opposite, so one of each ion is required to balance the charges, giving the formula $\ce{SrS}.$
