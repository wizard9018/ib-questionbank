# CH0062

Metal M is in group 2 of the periodic table. Which is the correct formula?

- A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{M2(NH4)2}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{MCl3}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{M2SO4}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{M3(PO4)2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
The charges of the positive and negative ions must cancel out to give an overall neutral compound. Group 2 metals form ions with a 2+ charge. The formulae of the counterions above are:
$\ce{NH4+, Cl-, SO4^2-, and PO4^3-}.$
