# CH0066

What is the formula of the compound formed between lithium and nitrogen?

- A.$\hspace{1em}\ce{LiN3}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}\ce{LiN2}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Li2N3}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}\ce{Li3N}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**答案：D**

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Lithium is in group 1 and so forms Li$^+$ ions. The formula of the nitride ion is N$^{3-}$as nitrogen is in group 15. For the positive and negative charges to cancel out, three Li$^+$ ions are needed for each N$^{3-}$ ion.
