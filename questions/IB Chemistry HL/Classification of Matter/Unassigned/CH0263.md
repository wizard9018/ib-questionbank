## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. potassium / K $\checkmark$

   $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
   There are 19 electrons in the electron configuration. In a neutral atom, the number of electrons is equal to the number of protons. Therefore, $Z = 19 \rightarrow \text{potassium}.$ Alternatively, potassium is the first electron in the $s-$block of the fourth row of the periodic table.

2. argon / Ar  $\checkmark$

   $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
   There are 18 electrons in the electron configuration. In a neutral atom, the number of electrons is equal to the number of protons. Therefore, $Z= 18 \rightarrow \text{argon}.$ Alternatively, argon is the sixth electron in the $p-$block of the third row of the periodic table.

3. magnesium / Mg $\checkmark$

   $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
   The element in period 3 and group 2 (or the second element in the $s-$block) is magnesium.

4. fluorine / F   $\checkmark$

   $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
   There are 9 electrons in the electron configuration.  In a neutral atom, the number of electrons is equal to the number of progons.  Therefore, $Z=9 \rightarrow \textrm{fluorine}.$  Alternatively, fluorine is in the second row of the periodic table and is the fifth electron in the $p-$block.

5. sodium / Na   $\checkmark$

   $\underline{\textrm{Further Explanation:}}$\
   The electron configuration of the ion: $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6$ so the electron configuration of the atom: $1s^2\,2s^2\,2p^6\,3s^1.$ The atom is the first element in $s-$block of the third row of the periodic table, sodium.
