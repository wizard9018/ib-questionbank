# EC0455

In a recent study, it was found that impulse buying represents, on average, over 10\% of annual household spending in the United States.

1. State two of the assumptions supporting the theory of rational consumer choice. [2 marks]

2. List two limitations to rational consumer behaviour theory. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\
    Utility maximization\
    Consumer rationality

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:\
    Any **two** of the following ($\checkmark \checkmark$):
    - Consumer rationality
    - Utility maximization
    - Perfect information

2. $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\
    Bounded rationality\
    Imperfect information

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:\
    Any **two** of the following ($\checkmark \checkmark$):
    - Biases
    - Bounded rationality
    - Bounded self-control
    - Bounded selfishness
    - Imperfect information
