# EC0425

**Selected market data for automobiles and petrol**

| Price $\\$ (automobiles) | Quantity $\\$ demanded $\\$ (automobiles) | Quantity $\\$ demanded $\\$ (petrol) |

| :-: | :-: | :-: |

| €30 000 | 800 | 10 000 litres |

| €28 000 | 1 000 | 14 000 litres |

| €26 000 | 1 200 | 19 000 litres |

| €24 000 | 1 400 | 22 000 litres |

| €22 000 | 1 600 | 24 000 litres |

1.  Using information from the table, outline whether petrol is a substitute or a complement for automobiles. [1 mark]

2.  Using information from the table, sketch the change in the demand for petrol that occurs when the price of automobiles increases. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \

    Petrol is a complement for automobiles because the quantity demanded for petrol increases as the quantity demanded for automobiles increases.

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:

    | Price $\\$ (automobiles) | Quantity demanded $\\$ (automobiles) | Quantity demanded $\\$ (petrol) |

    | :-: | :-: | :-: |

    | €30 000 | 800 | 10 000 litres |

    | €28 000 | 1000 | 14 000 litres |

    For briefly describing: ($\checkmark$)

    -   the quantity demanded ($\mathrm{Q_D}$) for petrol increases when $\mathrm{Q_D}$ for automobiles increases

    -   therefore petrol is a complement to automobiles

2.  $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

    ![EC0425 fig1](<images/EC0425 fig1.png>)

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:\

When the price of automobiles increases, the quantity of automobiles demanded will decrease. Because petrol is a complement for automobiles, when fewer automobiles are demanded, demand for petrol will decrease.

    For drawing a **correctly labelled** ($\checkmark$) demand and supply diagram showing:

    -   The demand curve for petrol shifts ($\checkmark$)

    -   To the left ($\checkmark$)
