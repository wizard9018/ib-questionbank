# EC0677

Intercity buses in Canada connect small and medium-sized cities that are not served by trains or flights. Most users are elderly people who cannot drive. Adults who can drive do not use intercity buses as often; they prefer to use their own cars because they are faster and more convenient. 

The Canadian government has recently decided to reduce the basic fare of intercity buses, as shown in the diagram below. 

![677a](images/677a.png)

1.  Calculate the price elasticity of demand (PED) for non-drivers when the price is reduced. [2 marks]

2.  Explain why non-drivers are less sensitive to a reduction in price than adults who drive. [4 marks]

3.  Identify the change in consumer surplus due to the reduced fare price for drivers and non-drivers. [2 marks]

4.  Calculate the change in total revenue after the price is lowered. [3 marks]
