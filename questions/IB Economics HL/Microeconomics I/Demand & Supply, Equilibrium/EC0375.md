# EC0375

Cow milk production can yield many byproducts, such as cheese, yogurt, butter, and ice cream.

1. Sketch a diagram showing the impact on the market supply for cow's milk if the government were to subsidize the industry. [2 marks]

2. Sketch a diagram showing the impact on the market supply for butter if there were a rise in the market price of yogurt. [1 mark]

3. With reference to your answer from part (b), explain why the price of yogurt impacts the supply of butter. [3 marks]

3. $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\
    Because yogurt and butter are both produced with the same resources, when the price of one changes it would impact the supply of the other. This is the concept of competitive supply. In the case of butter and yogurt because the price of yogurt has increased, manufacturers will shift production towards yogurt and thus decrease supply of butter.

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:\
    For identifying that yogurt and butter are in competitive supply ($\checkmark$)

    For an explanation that:
    - Yogurt and butter are produced using similar resources ($\checkmark$)
    - When the price of yogurt rises, suppliers will switch production away from butter, so the supply of butter will decrease ($\checkmark$)
