# EC0664

The figures below represent the relationship between three different services in a city: taxi rides, electric bike rentals, and travel insurance.

**Figure 1**: the relationship between electric bike rentals and taxi rides.

![EC0664](images/EC0664.jpg)

**Figure 2**: the relationship between electric bike rental and travel insurance. 

![EC0664 (1)](<images/EC0664 (1).jpg>)

1. Using the information from **Figure 1**, explain the likely relationship between taxi rides and electric bike rentals.  [2 marks]

 

2. Using the information from **Figure 2**, explain why electric bike rentals and travel insurance should **not** be regarded as substitutes or complements.  [2 marks]

 

3. Using a demand and supply diagram, explain how an increase in car insurance costs paid by taxi companies would likely impact the market for electric bike rentals. [4 marks]

 

4. Explain why the imposition of an effective maximum price on taxi fares is likely to result in a higher demand for electric bike rentals.  [4 marks]

    Figure 1 shows that when the quantity of electric bike rentals increases, the quantity of taxi rides decreases, and vice versa. This means that one service can be used instead of the other, so the two services are substitutes. 

    $\underline{\textrm{Explanation: }}$\

    For identifying that the two services are substitutes ($\textcolor{black}{\checkmark}$)

    For explaining that they are substitutes since the figure shows that an increase in the quantity of one service determines a reduction in the quantity of the other one ($\textcolor{black}{\checkmark}$)

2.  $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer: }}$\

        The figure shows that no relationship exists between electric bike rentals and travel insurance: when the quantity of electric bike rentals changes, there is no change in the quantity of travel insurance. This justifies why the two services are neither complements nor substitutes.

    $\underline{\textrm{Explanation: }}$\

    For identifying that no relationship exists between the quantity of bike rentals and the quantity of travel insurance / the two services are independent of each other ($\textcolor{black}{\checkmark}$)

    For explaining that the two services are independent of each other because a change in the quantity of bike rentals does not affect the quantity of travel insurance ($\textcolor{black}{\checkmark}$)

3.  $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer: }}$

    ![0664](images/0664.JPEG)

    The increase in car insurance cost would cause a leftward shift of the supply curve in the market for taxi rides, which would increase their price. As taxi rides and electric bike rentals are substitutes, more people would choose bike rentals over the now more expensive taxi rides. This will cause the demand curve for electric bike rentals to shift to the right as shown above, resulting in a greater equilibrium quantity and price. 

    $\underline{\textrm{Explanation: }}$\

    For drawing a **fully labelled** diagram showing a rightward shift of the demand curve for electric bike rentals ($\textcolor{black}{\checkmark}$)

    For explaining that the surge in car insurance costs would determine a leftward shift of the supply curve in the market for taxi rides ($\textcolor{black}{\checkmark}$)

    For explaining that the leftward shift of the supply curve in the market for taxi rides would determine a higher equilibrium price, causing the price of taxi rides to increase ($\textcolor{black}{\checkmark}$)

    For explaining that, as taxi rides and electric bike rentals are substitute services, more people would switch to electric bike rentals. This would determine a rightward shift in the demand curve for electric bike rentals ($\textcolor{black}{\checkmark}$)

4.  $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer: }}$

    ![0664.2](images/0664.2.png)

    An effective maximum price must be imposed below the market equilibrium: it would determine a reduction in the quantity supplied and an increase in the quantity demanded, resulting in a shortage of taxi rides. Due to the reduced quantity supplied, fewer people could use this service and more people would have to choose the substitute electric bike rentals. This would determine a higher demand for electric bike rentals. 

    $\underline{\textrm{Explanation: }}$\

    For identifying that an effective maximum price would result in a shortage of taxi rides / greater quantity demanded than quantity supplied of taxi rides ($\textcolor{black}{\checkmark}$)

    For drawing a fully labelled diagram representing an effective maximum price in the market for taxi rides ($\textcolor{black}{\checkmark}$)

    For explaining that an effective maximum price would cause a fall in the quantity supplied of taxi rides which would result in fewer users for this service ($\textcolor{black}{\checkmark}$)

    For explaining that more people would switch to using electric bike rentals as it is a substitute service for taxi rides, causing the demand curve for electric bike rentals to shift to the right ($\textcolor{black}{\checkmark}$)
