1.  Describe the opportunity costs faced by the economy shown in **Figure 1**. [2 marks]

2.  Using **Figure 1**, describe how a production possibilities curve illustrates the concepts of scarcity and opportunity cost.  [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

    This economy is facing increasing opportunity costs. This can be seen in Figure 1 as the proportion of butter that is given up for every increase in output of guns rises as the output of guns increases. The opportunity cost is greater when moving from B to C than from A to B. 

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:

    ![EC0366 fig2](<images/EC0366 fig2.png>)

    For stating:

    -   The model shows increasing opportunity cost ($\checkmark$)

    -   Seen as the proportional increase in loss of one good when increasing output of the other ($\checkmark$)

2.  $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

    The PPC shows all of the possible combinations of output an economy can produce of two products given their resource allocation. Because resources are scarce/finite, producing beyond the PPC is impossible due to a lack of resources. Therefore, if an economy wishes to increase the production of one good, it can only be done by reallocating resources away from the other good. This situation illustrates the opportunity cost of production.

    

    $\underline{\textrm{Solution}}$:\

    Answers should include:

    -   PPC illustrates output combinations given a set of resources ($\checkmark$)

    -   Resources are assumed to be scarce/finite and therefore producing beyond the PPC is not possible ($\checkmark$)

    -   Increasing output of any good cannot be done without reallocating resources from the other good illustrating the existence of opportunity cost ($\checkmark$)
