# AA104

**Maximum mark: 6**

The system of equations given below represents three planes in space.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x + 5z &= 2 \\[6pt]
-2x + y - 6z &= -1 \\[6pt]
2y + 8z &= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Show that this system of equations has an infinite number of
    solutions. [3 marks]

2.  Find the parametric equations of the line of intersection of the
    three planes. [3 marks]

1.  If we convert the given system of equations into an augmented
    matrix, we have

    Hence, using the method of Gaussian Elimination, we get

    ![e2d7661b43a236c006ef48f0c2b01b8969087034.svg](images/e2d7661b43a236c006ef48f0c2b01b8969087034.svg)

    The last row of zeros tells us that the system is consistent. As there are only two pivot positions corresponding to $x$ and $y$, the variable $z$ is free. Hence the system has an infinite number of solutions.

2.  We have

    ![9e7ed3301e98f940e211c9d9825320385d1b6446.svg](images/9e7ed3301e98f940e211c9d9825320385d1b6446.svg)

    If we express $x$ and $y$ in term of $z = t$, we obtain $x = 2 - 5t$
    and $y = 3 - 4t$.

    Hence the parametric equations of the line of intersection of the
    $3$ planes are
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x = 2-5t,\hspace{0.5em}y = 3-4t,\hspace{0.5em}z = t
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    where $t \in \mathbb{R}$.
