# AA840

**Maximum mark: 13**

Grant wants to save $$\$40\hspace{0.15em}000$$ over 5 years to help his
son pay for his college tuition. He deposits $$\$20\hspace{0.15em}000$$
into a savings account that has an interest rate of $6\%$ per annum
compounded **monthly** for $5$ years.

1.  Show that Grant will not be able to reach his target. [2 marks]

2.  Find the minimum amount, to the nearest dollar, that Grant would
    need to deposit initially for him to reach his target. [3 marks]

Grant only has $$\$20\hspace{0.15em}000$$ to invest, so he asks his
sister, Caroline, to help him accelerate the saving process. Caroline is
happy to help and offers to contribute part of her income each year. Her
annual income is $$\$37 \hspace{0.15em}500$$ per year. She starts by
contributing one fifth of her annual income, and then decreases her
contributions by half each year until the target is reached. Caroline's
contributions do not yield any interest.

3.  Show that Grant and Caroline together can reach the target
    in 5 years. [4 marks]

Grant and Caroline agree that Caroline should stop contributing once she
contributes enough to complement the deficit of Grant's investment.

4.  Find the **whole** number of years after which Caroline
    will stop contributing. [4 marks]

3.  The amount that Caroline contributes in the first year is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P} &= \dfrac{1}{5}(37\hspace{0.15em}500) \\[6pt]
    &= \$7\hspace{0.15em}500
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The total
    amount that Caroline contributes after $5$ years is expressed by

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A &= 7500 + 7500\left( \dfrac 1 2 \right) + 7500\left( \dfrac 1 2 \right)^2 +  + \dots + 7500\left( \dfrac 1 2 \right)^{4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We have a geometric series with the first term
    $u_1 = 7500$ and $r = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

    Hence, using the formula $S_n = \dfrac{u_1(r^n - 1)}{r - 1}$ with
    $\text{\(n = 5\)}$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{A} &= S_{5} \\[8pt]
    \hspace{2.75em} &= 7\hspace{0.15em}500\bigg[\dfrac{\left( \frac 1 2 \right)^5 - 1}{\frac{1}{2} - 1}\bigg] \\[12pt]
    &\approx \$14\hspace{0.15em}531
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Therefore, the total amount that Grant and Caroline can save in $5$
    years is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.5em} \text{Total amount} &\approx 26\hspace{0.15em}977 + 14\hspace{0.15em}531 \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.35em} \approx \$41\hspace{0.15em}508
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, Grant and Caroline together can reach the target as the total
    amount saved exceeds the target of $$\$40\hspace{0.15em}000$$.

4.  The amount Caroline must contribute to help Grant reach the target
    is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.75em} C &= 40\hspace{0.15em}000 - 26\hspace{0.15em}977 \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[6pt]
    &= \$13\hspace{0.15em}023
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore, we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    7500 + 7500\left( \dfrac 1 2 \right) + 7500\left( \dfrac 1 2 \right)^2 +  \dots \hspace{1em} &> 13\hspace{0.15em}023
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We have a geometric series with the first term $u_1 = 7500$ and
    $r = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

    Hence, using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{u_1(r^n - 1)}{r - 1}$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    7500\bigg[\dfrac{\left( \frac 1 2 \right)^n - 1}{\frac{1}{2} - 1}\bigg]\hspace{0.2em} &> 13\hspace{0.15em}023 \\[12pt]
    n &> 2.92358 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, Caroline will stop contributing after
     $3\hspace{0.3em} \text{years}$
