# AA706

**Maximum mark: 6**

Emily deposits $2000$ Australian dollars (AUD) into a bank account. The
bank pays a nominal annual interest rate of
$4$ %, **compounded monthly**.

1.  Find the amount of money that Emily will have in her bank account
    after $5$ years. Give your answer correct to **two decimal places**.
    [3 marks]

Emily will withdraw the money back from her bank account when the amount
reaches $3000$ AUD.

2.  Find the time, **in years and months**, until Emily withdraws the
    money from her bank account. [3 marks]
