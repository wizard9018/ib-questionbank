# AA1053

**Maximum mark: 6**

On the $1$st of January  $2024$ Fabian bought a new car for \$23,000. He knows from research that the car will depreciate at a rate of $8\%$ for every complete year.

1.  Find the value ($V_f$) of Fabian's car on the $1$st of January $2030$.[3 marks]

Fabian's colleague Sven also bought a new car on $1$st of January $2024$. Sven knows from research what the value (in \$) of his new car will be on the $1$st of January for the following years.

$$\\[1em]$$

![q1053](images/q1053.png)

2.  Assuming the value of Sven's car can be approximately modelled by the equation $V_s=ax+b$, find out the difference in value of Fabian and Sven's cars on $1$st of January $2030$, according to the model. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We can use a geometric sequence to model the value of Fabian's car ($V_f$).
    $$
     \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    $$
    $\textcolor{white}{gap}$

    The common ratio, $r$, will be $1-0.08=0.92$, and the first term, $u_1$, will be $23\,000$. There are $6$ complete years between $1$st of January $2024$ and $1$st of January $2030$. We are looking for $u_7$.
    $$
     \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    $$
    $\textcolor{white}{gap}$

    Hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        V_f &= u_1 \times r^{7-1}\\[6pt]
        &=23\,000\times 0.92^6\\[6pt]
        &=13\,946.16...\\[6pt]
        &=\$13\,900 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We can find a linear model using the G.D.C. Such that $a=-3\,228.84...$ and $b=6\,574\,789.23... $, hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        V_s&=-3228.84... x+6574789.23... \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Now we can predict the value ($V_s$) with $x=2030$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        V_s&=-3228.84...\times 2030+6574789.23...\\
        &=20\,231.53...\tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore the difference in value of the two cars is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        V_s-V_f&=20\,231.53...- 13\,946.16...\\[6pt]
        &=\$6\,290 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
