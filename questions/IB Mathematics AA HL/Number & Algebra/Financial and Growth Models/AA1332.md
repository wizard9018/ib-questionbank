# AA1332

**Maximum mark: 6**

On $1$st January $2021$, Nyla buys a yacht.

The price of the yacht depreciates at $7\%$ per annum.

On $1$st January $2025$, she sells the yacht for \$23\,564 .

1.    Find the price of the yacht when Nyla bought it. [3 marks]

Nyla wants to buy a speedboat.

The speedboat is priced at \$45\,000 on $1$st January $2021$, and it depreciates at $0.2\%$ per month.

On $1$st June $2025$, Nyla buys the speedboat.

In addition to the money she received for selling the yacht she was required to add \$$K$ to buy the speedboat.

2.    Find the value of $K$ to the nearest dollar. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. We can determine the price of the boat by using the compound depreciation formula
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{FV}&=\mathrm{PV}\left(1-\frac{r}{100}\right)^{n}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    where
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{FV}&=\text{ Future Value}\\[6pt]
    \mathrm{PV}&=\text{ Present Value}\\[6pt]
    r&=\text{ annual rate of depreciation, as a percentage}\\[6pt]
    n&=\text{ number of compounding periods}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    In this case we are told the future value and are expected to find the present value.

    This means we have $\mathrm{FV}=$\$23\,564, $k=1$, $r=7$, and $n=4$.

    Hence we can substitute these values into the formula to solve for the initial value (PV).
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    23\,564&=\mathrm{PV}\times \left(1-\frac{7}{100}\right)^4\tag*{\textbf{{(M1A1)}}}\\[12pt]
    23\,564&=\mathrm{PV}(0.93)^4\\[6pt]
    31\,500.4...&=\mathrm{PV}\\[6pt]
    \mathrm{PV}&=\$31\,500\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. We are told that Nyla will use the money from the sale of the boat and an additional amount $K$ to buy the speedboat.

    Hence, letting $\mathrm{FV}_S$ represent the future value of the speedboat we can write the formula
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    23\,564+K&=\mathrm{FV}_S\\[6pt]
    K&=\mathrm{FV}_S-23\,564
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    To determine $\mathrm{FV}_S$ at the time when Nyla buys the speedboat we can use the formula in part (a).

    We know that $\mathrm{PV}=$ \$45\,000 and $r=0.2$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{FV}&=45\,000\left(1-\frac{0.2}{100}\right)^{n}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The value of $n$ is the number of compounding periods so we need to determine how many months are between $1$ January $2021$ and $1$ June $2025$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    n&=4\times12+5\\[6pt]
    &=53\tag*{\textbf{{(M1)}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Substituting $n=53$ we can solve for the value of the speedboat.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{FV}_S&=45\,000\left(1-\frac{0.2}{100}\right)^{53}\tag*{\textbf{{(M1)}}}\\[12pt]
    &=\$40\,469.813...
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Now we can use this value to solve for $K$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    K&=\mathrm{FV}_S-23\,564\\[6pt]
    &=40\,469.813-23\,564\\[6pt]
    &=16\,905.813...\\[6pt]
    &=\$16\,906\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
