# AA703

**Maximum mark: 5**

Maria invests $$\$25\hspace{0.15em}000$$ into a savings account that pays
a nominal annual interest rate of $4.25$%,
**compounded monthly**.

1.  Calculate the amount of money in the savings account after $3$
    years. [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the number of years it takes for the account to reach
    $$\$40\hspace{0.15em}000$$. [3 marks]
