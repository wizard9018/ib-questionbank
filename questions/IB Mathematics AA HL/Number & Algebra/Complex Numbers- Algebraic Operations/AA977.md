# AA977

**Maximum mark: 7**

Consider the equation $z^4-6z^3+cz^2-30z+13=0$ where $z \in \mathbb{C}$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}$.

Three of the roots of the equation are $2-3i$, $\alpha$ and $\alpha^4$, where $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$.

1. Find the value of $\alpha$.[4 marks]

2. Hence, or otherwise, find the value of $c$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Since $2-3i$ is a root its conjugate $2+3i$ is a root too.

    Now, using the formula for the product of the roots, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \text{product of roots}&=(-1)^4\dfrac{a_0}{a_4}\\[6pt]

    (2-3i)\cdot(2+3i)\cdot \alpha \cdot \alpha^4 &=(1)\dfrac{13}{1}\\[8pt]

    13\cdot \alpha^5&=13\\[8pt]  

    &\hspace{-0.65em}\boxed{\alpha =1} \hspace{4em}[\text{since $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$}]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The complex polynomial can be factorised as

    $$

    \begin{aligned} 

     p(x)&=(x-(2+3i))(x-(2-3i))(x-1)(x-1)\\[8pt]

     &=(x^2-4x+13)(x-1)^2\\[8pt]

     &=(x^2-4x+13)(x^2-2x+1)\\[8pt]

     &=x^4-6x^3+22x^2-30x+13

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore, we conclude that $\hspace{0.29em}\boxed{c=22}$
