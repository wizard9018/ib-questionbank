# AA115

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the equation $2z^4 + az^3 + bz^2 +cz + d = 0$, where $a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{R}$ and $z \in \mathbb{C}$.\
Two of the roots of the equation are $\log_2 10$ and $i\sqrt{5}$ and the sum of all the roots is $4 + \log_2 5$.

Show that $15a = -(d + 90)$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Since $\im\sqrt{5}$ is a root of the equation with real coefficients, its conjugate $-\im\sqrt{5}$ is also a root.

$$\\[-2.5em]$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{M1}}
\end{aligned}
$$
Let $z_4$ denote the fourth root. Using the sum of all four roots.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cancel{\im\sqrt{5}} + (-\cancel{\im\sqrt{5}}) + \log_2 10 + z_4 &= 4 + \log_2 5 \\[6pt]
z_4 &= 4 + \log_2 5 - \log_2 10
\end{aligned}
$$
Applying the **laws of logarithms (1.7)**, to simplify $\log_2 10$, $\log_a xy = \log_a x + \log_a y$.
$$
\begin{aligned}
z_4 &= 4 + \log_2 5 - (\log_2 2 + \log_2 5) \\[6pt]
    &= 4 - 1 \\[6pt]
z_4 &= 3 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
\end{aligned}
$$
Using the **sum and product of roots of polynomial equations (2.12)**, to express $a$ and $d$ in terms of the four roots, $\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n} a_r x^r = 0 \Rightarrow \text{sum is } \dfrac{-a_{n-1}}{a_n}\,;\ \text{product is } \dfrac{(-1)^n a_0}{a_n}$, with $n = 4$, $a_n = 2$, $a_{n-1} = a$ and $a_0 = d$.

From the sum of the roots.
$$
\begin{aligned}
-\dfrac{a}{2} &= 4 + \log_2 5 \tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[6pt]
a &= -8 - 2\log_2 5 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
\end{aligned}
$$
From the product of the roots.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{d}{2} &= (\im\sqrt{5})(-\im\sqrt{5})(\log_2 10)(3) \tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[6pt]
             &= 5(3)\log_2 10 \\[6pt]
d &= 30\log_2 10 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
\end{aligned}
$$
Computing $15a + d$ and applying the laws of logarithms to $\log_2 10 = \log_2 2 + \log_2 5$.
$$
\begin{aligned}
15a + d &= 15(-8 - 2\log_2 5) + 30\log_2 10 \\[6pt]
        &= -120 - \cancel{30\log_2 5} + 30\log_2 2 + \cancel{30\log_2 5} \\[6pt]
        &= -120 + 30 \\[6pt]
15a + d &= -90
\end{aligned}
$$
Rearranging the terms.
$$
\begin{aligned}
15a &= -d - 90 \\[6pt]
&\boxed{15a = -(d + 90)}\hspace{1em}\text{... as required.} \tag*{\textbf{AG}}
\end{aligned}
$$
