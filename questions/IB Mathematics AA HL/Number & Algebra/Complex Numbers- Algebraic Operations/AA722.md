# AA722

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $z = re^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\frac{\pi}{6}}$ where
$r \in \mathbb{R}^+$.

1.  For $r = \sqrt{3}$, express $z^2$ and $z^3$ in the form
        $a+b{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ where
        $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$. [3 marks]

2.  Given that the integer powers of
    $w = \dfrac{z}{6+2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}}$ lie on a
    unit circle centred at the origin, find the value of $r$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  To express $z^2$ and $z^3$ in Cartesian form, we need to use **the Polar form of a complex number (1.13)**, $z=r(\cos\theta+\mathrm{i}\sin\theta)=re^{\mathrm{i}\theta}=r\,\text{cis}\,\theta$

    and **De Moivre's Theorem (1.14)**, $[r(\cos\theta+\mathrm{i}\sin\theta)]^n=r^ne^{\mathrm{i} n\theta}$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    z^2 &= (\sqrt{3})^2e^{\mathrm{i}(2\times\frac{\pi}{6})} \\[5pt]
    &= 3e^{\mathrm{i}\frac{\pi}{3}} \\[5pt]
    &= 3\Big[\hspace{-0.1em}\cos\Big(\frac{\pi}{3}\Big) + \mathrm{i}\sin\Big(\frac{\pi}{3}\Big)\Big] \\[5pt]
    &= 3\Big[\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\frac{1}{2}\Big) + \mathrm{i}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\Big)\Big] \\[9pt]
    z^2 &=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2} + \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\mathrm{i}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    z^3 &= (\sqrt{3})^3e^{\mathrm{i}(3\times\frac{\pi}{6})} \\[5pt]
    &= 3\sqrt{3}e^{\mathrm{i}\frac{\pi}{2}} \\[5pt]
    &= 3\sqrt{3}\Big[\hspace{-0.1em}\cos\Big(\frac{\pi}{2}\Big) + \mathrm{i}\sin\Big(\frac{\pi}{2}\Big)\Big]\\[5pt]
    &= 3\sqrt{3}\Big[\hspace{-0.1em}(0) + \mathrm{i}(1)\Big]\\[9pt]
    z^3&=\boxed{0+3\sqrt{3}\mathrm{i}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Finding the modulus of $6+2\mathrm{i}$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |6+2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}| &= \sqrt{6^2 + 2^2} \hspace{2.2em} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{40} \\[6pt]
    &= 2\sqrt{10}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the properties of modulus,
    we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |w^k| &= 1 \hspace{3em} [\text{for all $k \in \mathbb{Z}$}]  \\[16pt]
    |w|^k &= 1 \\[17pt]
    |w| &= 1 \\[15pt]
    \hspace{3.9em} \Big|\dfrac{z}{6+2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}}\Big| &= 1 \hspace{3em} [w = z/(6+2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu})] \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{|z|}{|6+2{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}|} &= 1 \\[9pt]
    \dfrac{r}{2\sqrt{10}} &= 1 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    r = 2\sqrt{10} \hspace{1.3em}
    $$
