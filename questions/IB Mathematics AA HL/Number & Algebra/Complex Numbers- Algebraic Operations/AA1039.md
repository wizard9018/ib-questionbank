# AA1039

**Maximum mark: 7**

Consider the complex numbers $z_1=-1+\sqrt{3}ai$ and $z_2=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}(a-1)}{3a+1}+i$.  Where $a \in \mathbb{R}, a\neq-\tfrac{1}{3}$.

Another complex number is defined such that $z_3=\dfrac{z_2}{z_1}$.

Let $\theta=\arg({z_3})$, where $-\pi \leq \theta \leq \pi$.

1. Show that $\tan{\theta}$ is constant for $a \in \mathbb{R}, a\neq-\tfrac{1}{3}$. [4 marks]

2. Hence, find the two possible values of $\theta$ and the intervals of $a$ for which they will occur.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Begin by dividing the two complex numbers
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    z_3&=\frac{\tfrac{\sqrt{3}(a-1)}{3a+1}+i}{-1+\sqrt{3}ai}\\[3pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Multiply the top and bottom by the complex conjugate of $z_1$, then simplify
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        &=\frac{\tfrac{\sqrt{3}(a-1)}{3a+1}+i}{-1+\sqrt{3}ai}\times\frac{{-1-\sqrt{3}ai}}{{-1-\sqrt{3}ai}} \\[6pt]
        &=\frac{\tfrac{\sqrt{3}(a-1)(-1-\sqrt{3}ai)}{3a+1}+(-1-\sqrt{3}ai)i}{3a^2+1} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\[3pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Write the numerator as a single fraction and simplify
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        &=\tfrac{1}{3a^2+1}\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}(a-1)(-1-\sqrt{3}ai)+(-i+\sqrt{3}a)(1+3a)}{3a+1}\right)\\[3pt]
        &=\tfrac{1}{3a^2+1}\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}(-a-\sqrt{3}a^2i+1+\sqrt{3}ai)-i-3ai+\sqrt{3}a+3\sqrt{3}a^2}{3a+1}\right) \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Cancel out terms.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &=\tfrac{1}{3a^2+1}\left(\frac{\cancel{-\sqrt{3}a}-3a^2i +\sqrt{3}+\cancel{3ai}-i-\cancel{3ai}+\cancel{\sqrt{3}a}+3\sqrt{3}a^2}{3a+1}\right)\\[3pt]
    &=\tfrac{1}{3a^2+1}\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}+3\sqrt{3}a^2-(3a^2+1)i}{3a+1}\right)\\[3pt]
    &=\tfrac{1}{\cancel{3a^2+1}}\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}\cancel{(3a^2+1)}-\cancel{(3a^2+1)i}}{3a+1}\right)\\
    &=\tfrac{1}{3a+1}\left(\sqrt{3}-i\right) \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we can find $\tan\theta$ independent of $a$
    $$
    \tag*{\textbf{R1}}
    $$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        &=-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\\[3pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore $ \tan{\theta}\text{ is constant for a }\in \mathbb{R}, a\neq-\tfrac{1}{3}$

2. First we can find the two values of $\theta$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \theta&=\arctan{\left(-\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)}\\[3pt]
        &=-\frac{\pi}{6},\,\frac{5\pi}{6} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We can now sketch two representations of $z_3=\frac{1}{3a+1}(\sqrt{3}-i)$ on Argand diagrams which both have the same value for $\tan \theta$.
    $$
    \tag*{\textbf{M1}}
    $$

    When $\frac{1}{3a+1}>0$ then $\theta = -\dfrac{\pi}{6}$ and when $\frac{1}{3a+1}<0$ then $\theta = \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$

    ![ans1](images/ans1.png)

    We can use a sign diagram (or a sketch) to determine the conditions for the sign of $\tfrac{1}{3a+1}$.

    ![ans2](images/ans2.png)

    Therefore $\tfrac{1}{3a+1}>0$ when $a>-\tfrac{1}{3}$ and $\tfrac{1}{3a+1}<0$ when $a<-\tfrac{1}{3}$

    Hence
    $$
    \theta = -\tfrac{\pi}{6} \text{ for } a>-\tfrac{1}{3} \text{ and } \theta = \tfrac{5\pi}{6} \text{
    for } a<-\tfrac{1}{3}\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    $$
