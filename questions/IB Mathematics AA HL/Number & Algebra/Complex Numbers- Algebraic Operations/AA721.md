# AA721

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $z = re^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\frac{\pi}{3}}$ where
$r \in \mathbb{R}^+$.

1.  For $r = \sqrt{2}$, express $z^2$ and $z^3$ in the form
        $a+b{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ where
        $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$. [3 marks]

2.  Given that the integer powers of
    $w = (3-3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu})\hspace{0.05em}z$ lie
    on a unit circle centred at the origin, find the value of $r$.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  To express $z^2$ and $z^3$ in Cartesian form, we need to use **the Polar form of a complex number (1.13)**, $z=r(\cos\theta+\mathrm{i} \sin\theta)=re^{\mathrm{i}\theta}=r\,\text{cis}\,\theta$

    and **De Moivre's Theorem (1.14)**, $[r(\cos\theta+\mathrm{i}\sin\theta)]^n=r^ne^{\mathrm{i} n\theta}$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    z^2 &= (\sqrt{2})^2e^{\mathrm{i}(2\times\frac{\pi}{3})} \\[5pt]
    &= 2e^{\mathrm{i}\frac{2\pi}{3}} \\[5pt]
    &= 2\Big[\hspace{-0.1em}\cos\Big(\frac{2\pi}{3}\Big) + \mathrm{i}\sin\Big(\frac{2\pi}{3}\Big)\Big] \\[5pt]
    &= 2\Big[\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(-\frac{1}{2}\Big) + \mathrm{i}\Big(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\Big)\Big] \\[9pt]
    z^2&=\boxed{-1 + \sqrt{3}\mathrm{i}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    z^3 &= (\sqrt{2})^3e^{\mathrm{i}(3\times\frac{\pi}{3})} \\[5pt]
    &= 2\sqrt{2}e^{\mathrm{i} \pi} \\[5pt]
    &= 2\sqrt{2}\Big[\hspace{-0.1em}\cos \pi + \mathrm{i}\sin \pi \Big] \\[5pt]
    &= 2\sqrt{2}\Big[\hspace{-0.1em}(-1) + \mathrm{i}(0)\Big] \\[9pt]
    z^3&=\boxed{-2\sqrt{2} + 0\mathrm{i}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  First, we find the modulus of $3-3\mathrm{i}$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |3-3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}| &= \sqrt{3^2 + (-3)^2} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{18} \\[6pt]
    &= 3\sqrt{2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the properties of modulus,
    we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |w^k| &= 1 \hspace{3em} [\text{for all $k \in \mathbb{Z}$}]  \\[13pt]
    |w|^k &= 1 \\[14pt]
    |w| &= 1 \\[14pt]
    \hspace{3.1em} |(3-3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu})z| &= 1 \hspace{3em} [w = (3-3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu})z] \\[14pt]
    |3-3{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}||z| &= 1 \\[12pt]
    3\sqrt{2}\hspace{0.05em}r &= 1 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    r = \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{6} \hspace{1.4em}
    $$
