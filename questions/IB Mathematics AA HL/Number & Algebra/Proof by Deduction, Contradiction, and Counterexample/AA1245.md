# AA1245

**Maximum mark: 5**

Consider the 3 digit number $m=134$.

The **digits** of $m$ are 1, 3 and 4.

The sum of the digits of $m$ is $1+3+4=8$, and we could write the number $m$ as

$$
\begin{aligned}
    m &= 100 \times 1 + 10 \times 3 + 1 \times 4
\end{aligned}
$$

Now consider a 4 digit number $n$.

Prove that if the sum of the digits of $n$ is divisible by 3, then $n$ is also divisible by 3.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let the digits of $n$ be $p, q, r$ and $s$, where $p,q,r,s\in\mathbb{Z^+}$, $1 \leq p \leq 9$, and $0 \leq q,r,s\leq9$.

We can therefore rewrite $n$ as:

$$
\begin{aligned}
n=1000p+100q+10r+s \tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}
\end{aligned}
$$

Because the sum of the digits is divisible by three, we know that

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{p+q+r+s}{3} &= k \hspace{1em}(k\in\mathbb{Z^+})
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence

$$
\begin{aligned}
    p+q+r+s=3k
    \tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}\\
\end{aligned}
$$

Rearranging to make $s$ the subject, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
    s=3k-p-q-r \tag*{\textbf{{M1}}}
\end{aligned}
$$

Substituting this into the equation for $n$, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
    n&=1000p+100q+10r+3k-p-q-r \tag*{\textbf{{M1}}}\\[6pt]
    &=999p+99q+9r+3k\\[6pt]
    &=3(333p+33q+3r+k)\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}\\[6pt]
    &=3v \hspace{1em}[\text{where } v=333p+33q+3r+k \text{ and }v \in \mathbb{Z^+}]\\[6pt]
\end{aligned}
$$

Therefore, $$\text{if the digits of $n$ are divisible by 3, then $n$ is also divisible by $3$}$$ ... as required.
