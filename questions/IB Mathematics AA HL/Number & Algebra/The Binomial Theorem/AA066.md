# AA066

**Maximum mark: 6**

1.  Write down and simplify the expansion of $(3-x)^5$ in descending
    order of powers of $x$. [3 marks]

2.  Hence find the exact value of $(2.9)^5$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the binomial theorem, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (3-x)^5 &= ((-x) + 3)^5 \\[6pt]
    &= \sum_{k = 0}^5 \binom{5}{k}\big[(-x)^{5-k}\big]\big[3^k\big] \\[6pt]
    &= -x^5 + 15x^4 - 90x^3 + 270x^2 - 405x + 243
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Hence, substituting $x = 0.1$ in the expression $(1)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (2.9)^5 &=  -(0.1)^5 + 15(0.1)^4 - 90(0.1)^3 + 270(0.1)^2 - 405(0.1) + 243 \\[6pt]
    &= -0.00001 + 0.0015 - 0.09 + 2.7 - 40.5 + 243 \\[6pt]
    &= -40.59001 + 245.7015 \\[6pt]
    &= 205.11149
    \end{aligned}
    $$
