# AA058

**Maximum mark: 4**

Expand $(2x + 1)^4$ in descending powers of $x$ and simplify your
answer.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using Pascal triangle

![d6143401ad9783a3554c63b7ab804d89d1d7b5f1.svg](images/d6143401ad9783a3554c63b7ab804d89d1d7b5f1.svg)

we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2em} (2x + 1)^4 &= (2x)^4 + 4(2x)^3(1) + 6(2x)^2(1)^2 + 4(2x)(1)^3 + (1)^4 \\[9pt]
&= 16x^4 + 4(8x^3) + 6(4x^2) + 4(2x) + 1 \\[9pt]
&= 16x^4 + 32x^3 + 24x^2 + 8x + 1
\end{aligned}
$$
