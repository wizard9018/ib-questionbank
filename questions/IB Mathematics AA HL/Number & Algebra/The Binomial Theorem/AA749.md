# AA749

**Maximum mark: 5**

Consider the expansion of $(x-3)^8$.

1.  Write down the number of terms in this expansion. [1 mark]

2.  Find the coefficient of the term in $x^6$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The number of terms in this expansion is
     $9$

2.  The term in $x^6$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \binom{8}{2} \big[(x)^{8-2}\big]\big[(-3)^2\big] &= 28\big[x^6\big]\big[9\big] \\[6pt]
    &= 252x^6
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the coefficient of the term in $x^6$
    is  $252$
