# AA062

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the expansion of $(2x-1)^9$.

1.  Write down the number of terms in this expansion. [1 mark]

2.  Find the coefficient of the term in $x^2$. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The number of terms in this expansion is
     $10$

2.  The term in $x^2$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \binom{9}{7} \big[(2x)^{9-7}\big]\big[(-1)^7\big] &= 36\big[4x^2\big]\big[-1\big] \\[6pt]
    &= -144x^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the coefficient of the term in $x^2$
    is  $-144$
