# AA988

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the expansion of $\dfrac{(x+a)^7}{bx}$, where $a > 0$. The coefficient of the term in $x^5$ is $2$, and the coefficient of the term in $x^3$ is $1690$.

Find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Because each term in the expansion of $(x+a)^7$ will be divided by $bx$, we require the terms in $x^6$ and $x^4$. Using the Binomial Theorem, we have

$$

\begin{aligned}

    \text{Term in $x^6$} &= \binom{7}{1}x^6a \\[8pt]

    &= 7x^6a \\[8pt]

    \text{Term in $x^4$} &= \binom{7}{3}x^4a^3 \\[8pt]

    &= 35x^4a^3

\end{aligned}

$$

Dividing both these terms by $bx$ we get

$$

\begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{7x^6a}{bx} &= \dfrac{7x^5a}{b} \\[8pt]

    \dfrac{35x^4a^3}{bx} &= \dfrac{35x^3a^3}{b}

\end{aligned}

$$

Given that the coefficient of $x^5$ is $2$, and the coefficient of $x^3$ is $1690$, we have

$$

\begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{7a}{b} &= 2 \\[8pt]

    \dfrac{35a^3}{b} &= 1690

\end{aligned}

$$

And therefore 

$$

\begin{aligned}

    7a &= 2b \tag*{[1]}\\[8pt] 

    35a^3 &= 1690b \tag*{[2]}

\end{aligned}

$$

Dividing equation [2] by equation [1] we have

$$

\begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{35a^3}{7a} &= \dfrac{1690b}{2b} \\[8pt]

    5a^2 &= 845 \\[8pt]

    a^2 &= 169 \\[8pt]

    a &= \pm 13

\end{aligned}

$$

Given that $a>0$, $\boxed{a = 13}$. Substituting into equation [1],

$$

\begin{aligned}

    7a &= 2b \\[8pt]

    7(13) &= 2b \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{b = 45.5}

\end{aligned}

$$
