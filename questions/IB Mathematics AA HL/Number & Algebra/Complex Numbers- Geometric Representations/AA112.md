# AA112

**Maximum mark: 18**

1.  Express $-4 + 4\sqrt{3}{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ in the
    form $re^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\theta}$, where
    $r > 0$ and $- \pi < \theta \leq \pi$. [5 marks]

Let the roots of the equation
$z^3 = -4 + 4\sqrt{3}{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ be $z_1$,
$z_2$ and $z_3$.

2.  Find $z_1$, $z_2$ and $z_3$ expressing your answers in the
    form $re^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\theta}$, where
    $r > 0$ and $-\pi < \theta \leq \pi$. [5 marks]

On an Argand diagram, $z_1$, $z_2$ and $z_3$ are represented by the
points A, B and C, respectively.

3.  Find the area of the triangle ABC. [4 marks]

4.  By considering the sum of the roots $z_1$, $z_2$ and $z_3$,
    show that

    $$
    \cos\Big(\dfrac{2\pi}{9}\Big) + \cos\Big(\dfrac{4\pi}{9}\Big) + \cos\Big(\dfrac{8\pi}{9}\Big) = 0

    $$
    [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we express $-4 + 4\sqrt{3}{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$
    in the form $re^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\theta}$, we
    have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} -4 + 4\sqrt{3}{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}&= 8\bigg[\hspace{-0.3em}-\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\bigg] \\[5pt]
    &= 8\Big[\cos\Big(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\Big) + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\sin\Big(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\Big)\Big] \\[12pt]
    &= 8e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\frac{2\pi}{3}} \hspace{3em} [\text{by Euler's formula}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using de Moivre's theorem, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.2em} z &= \sqrt[3]{8}\Big[\cos\Big(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\Big) + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\sin\Big(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\Big)\Big]^{\frac{1}{3}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.6em} &= \sqrt[3]{8}\Big[\cos\Big(\dfrac{2\pi/3 + 2k\pi}{3}\Big) + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\sin\Big(\dfrac{2\pi/3 + 2k\pi}{3}\Big)\Big] \\[6pt]
    &= 2\Big[\cos\Big(\dfrac{2\pi}{9} + \dfrac{2k\pi}{3}\Big) + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\sin\Big(\dfrac{2\pi}{9} + \dfrac{2k\pi}{3}\Big)\Big]  \\[11pt]
    &= 2e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}(\frac{2\pi}{9} + \frac{2k\pi}{3})} \hspace{3em} [\text{by Euler's formula}] \hspace{0.2em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    for $k = -1,0,1$.

    Hence the roots of the equation
    $z^3 = -4 + 4\sqrt{3}{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ are
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    z_1 = 2e^{-{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\frac{4\pi}{9}},\hspace{0.5em}z_2 = 2e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\frac{2\pi}{9}},\hspace{0.5em}z_3 = 2e^{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}\frac{8\pi}{9}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  If we sketch the triangle ABC on an Argand diagram, we have

    ![be310f824ce127a4f24f53c5e5e7cb15aa1f5774.svg](images/be310f824ce127a4f24f53c5e5e7cb15aa1f5774.svg)

    Hence the area of the triangle ABC is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Area} &= 3\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}(2)(2)\sin \bigg(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\bigg)\bigg] \\[4pt]
    &= 3\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}(2)(2)\bigg(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\bigg)\bigg]  \\[12pt]
    &= 3\sqrt{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The sum of the roots of the equation
    $z^3 = -4 + 4\sqrt{3}{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ is zero.

    Hence we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{Re}\left(z_1 + z_2 + z_3\right) &= 0 \hspace{3em} \\[10pt]
    \cos\Big(\hspace{-0.3em}-\dfrac{4\pi}{9}\Big) + \cos\Big(\dfrac{2\pi}{9}\Big) + \cos\Big(\dfrac{8\pi}{9}\Big) &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \cos\Big(\dfrac{2\pi}{9}\Big) + \cos\Big(\dfrac{4\pi}{9}\Big) + \cos\Big(\dfrac{8\pi}{9}\Big) = 0 \hspace{2.05em}
    $$
