# AA716

**Maximum mark: 6**

The complex numbers $z$ and $w$ correspond to the points A and B as
shown on the diagram below.

![0c46fa70e78de8ef6fa11a8d28566cc1742d4a83.svg](images/0c46fa70e78de8ef6fa11a8d28566cc1742d4a83.svg)

1.  Find the exact value of $|z - w|$. [2 marks]

2.
    1.  Find the exact perimeter of triangle AOB.

    2.  Find the exact area of triangle AOB. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} |z - w|^2 &= |z|^2 + |w|^2 - 2|z||w|\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right) \\[6pt]
    |z - w|^2 &= 5^2 + 5^2 - 2(5)(5)\hspace{-0.1em} \bigg[\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\bigg] \\[10pt]
    |z - w|^2 &= 25(2-\sqrt{3}) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    |z - w| = \textstyle 5\sqrt{2-\sqrt{3}} \hspace{4.3em}
    $$

2.
    1.  The exact perimeter of triangle AOB is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{perimeter} &= |z| + |w| + |z-w| \\[6pt]
        &= 5 + 5 + \textstyle 5\sqrt{2-\sqrt{3}} \\[6pt]
        &= \textstyle 10 + 5\sqrt{2-\sqrt{3}}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The exact area of triangle AOB is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.1em} \text{area} &= \dfrac{1}{2}|z||w|\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right) \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{1}{2}(5)(5)\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}\bigg] \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{25}{4}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
