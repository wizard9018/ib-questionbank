# AA717

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $z = 2\mathop{\mathrm{cis}}2\theta$ where $0 < \theta < \ang{45}$.
Find the modulus and argument of $z + 2$, expressing your answers in
terms of $\theta$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Illustrating $z$ and $z+2$ on an Argand diagram, we have

![1d7aa7a6ad70d5559224e1ad164664c7c8b18e90.svg](images/1d7aa7a6ad70d5559224e1ad164664c7c8b18e90.svg)

Since $\mathrm{OA} = \mathrm{AB} = \mathrm{BC} = \mathrm{OC} = 2$, the
shape OABC is a rhombus.

Letting M be the midpoint of OB, we further have

![ab69dba55452ae052577c8e1dbf5234f80b40234.svg](images/ab69dba55452ae052577c8e1dbf5234f80b40234.svg)

Since OB bisects the angle AOC, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\arg(z+2) &= \dfrac{1}{2}\arg z \\[6pt]
\arg(z+2) &= \dfrac{1}{2}(2\theta) \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\arg(z+2) = \theta \hspace{2.45em} \\
\end{aligned}
$$
Using
the right triangle AOM, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{15.2em} \cos \theta &= \dfrac{\mathrm{OM}}{|z|} \\[8pt]
\mathrm{OM} &= 2\cos \theta \hspace{3em} [\text{since $|z|=2$}] \\[14pt]
\mathrm{OB} &= 4\cos \theta \hspace{3em} [\text{since M is a midpoint}] \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hspace{1.3em} |z+2| = 4\cos \theta
$$
