# AA628

**Maximum mark: 6**

The fourth term, $u_4$, of a geometric sequence is $135$. The fifth
term, $u_5$, is $81$.

1.  Find the common ratio of the sequence. [2 marks]

2.  Find $u_1$, the first term of the sequence. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the sum of the first $20$ terms of the sequence.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The common ratio is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{u_5}{u_4} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{81}{135} \\[11pt]
    &= 0.6 \hspace{3.2em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_4r^{n-4}$ with $n = 1$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_1 &= u_4r^{1-4} \\[6pt]
    &= 135(0.6)^{1-4} \\[6pt]
    &= 625
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{u_1(1-r^n)}{1-r}$
    with $n = 20$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.7em} S_{20} &= \dfrac{u_1(1-r^{20})}{1-r} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{625(1-(0.6)^{20})}{1-0.6} \\[12pt]
    &\approx 1560
    \end{aligned}
    $$
