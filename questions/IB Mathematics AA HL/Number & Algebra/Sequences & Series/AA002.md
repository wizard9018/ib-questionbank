# AA002

**Maximum mark: 6**

An arithmetic sequence has $u_1= 40$, $u_2 = 32$, $u_3 = 24$.

1.  Find the common difference, $d$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $u_8$. [2 marks]

3.  Find $S_8$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The common difference of an arithmetic sequence is found by subtracting any term in the sequence from the next term in the sequence. We could subtract $u_1$ from $u_2$, or subtract $u_2$ from $u_3$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= u_2 - u_1 \hspace{1.5em} \\[6pt]
    &= 32 - 40 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    d&=-8\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with $n = 8$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} u_8 &= u_1 + (8-1)d   \\[6pt]
    &= 40 + (8-1)(-8) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    u_8&=-16\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(u_1 + u_n)$
    with $n=8$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} S_8 &= \dfrac{8}{2}(u_1 + u_8) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{8}{2}(40 + (-16)) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[11pt]
    S_8 &=96 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
