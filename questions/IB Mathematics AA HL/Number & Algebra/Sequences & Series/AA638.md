# AA638

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the infinite geometric sequence $9000$, $-7200$, $5760$,
$-4608$, ...

1.  Find the common ratio. [2 marks]

2.  Find the $25$th term. [2 marks]

3.  Find the **exact** sum of the infinite sequence. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have $u_1 = 9000$ and $u_2 = -7200$.
    Hence the common ratio is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{u_2}{u_1} \\[8pt]
    &= -\dfrac{7200}{9000} \hspace{2.9em} \\[8pt]
    &= -\dfrac{4}{5}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1r^{n-1}$ with $n = 25$, we
    find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_{25} &= u_1r^{25-1} \\[6pt]
    &= 9000\left(\hspace{-0.2em}-\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^{25-1} \\[8pt]
    &\approx 42.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of an infinite geometric sequence formula
    $S_{\infty} = \dfrac{u_1}{1-r}$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_{\infty} &= \dfrac{9000}{1-(-4/5)} \hspace{1.9em} \\[9pt]
    &= 5000
    \end{aligned}
    $$
