# AA018

**Maximum mark: 6**

The sum of the first three terms of a geometric sequence is $81.3$, and
the sum of the infinite sequence is $300$. Find the common ratio.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_3 &= 81.3 \hspace{4.8em} \\[6pt]
\dfrac{u_1(1-r^3)}{1-r} &= 81.3
\end{aligned}
$$
and

$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{\infty} &= 300 \\[6pt]
\dfrac{u_1}{1-r} &= 300 \\[5pt]
u_1 &= 300(1-r)
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{u_1(1-r^3)}{1-r} &= 81.3 \hspace{8.5em} \\[6pt]
\dfrac{300(1-r)(1-r^3)}{1-r} &= 81.3 \hspace{8.5em} \\[12pt]
300(1-r^3) &= 81.3 \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hspace{9.3em} r = 0.9 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
$$
