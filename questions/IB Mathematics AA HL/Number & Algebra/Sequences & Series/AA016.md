# AA016

**Maximum mark: 6**

Sarah walks to school each morning. During the first minute, she travels
$130$ $\text{metres}$. In each subsequent minute, she travels
$5$ metres less than the distance she travelled during the previous
minute. The distance from her home to school is $950$ metres. Sarah
leaves her house at $8$:$00$ am and must be at
school by $8$:$10$ am.

Will Sarah arrive to school on time? Justify your answer.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have an arithmetic sequence with the first term $u_1 = 130$ and the
common difference $d = -5$.

If we attempt to solve $S_n = 950$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_n &= 950 \\[9pt]
\dfrac{n}{2}(2(130)+(n-1)(-5)) &= 950 \\[6pt]
\dfrac{n}{2}(265-5n) &= 950 \\[8pt]
n &\approx 8.55 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
\end{aligned}
$$

$\text{Hence it takes approximately \(8.55\) minutes for Sarah to travel \(950\) metres.}$

$\text{Therefore, Sarah \textbf{will} arrive at school on time.}$
