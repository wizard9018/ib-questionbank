# AA001

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider an arithmetic sequence $2,6,10,14,\dots$

1.  Find the common difference, $d$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the $10$th term in the sequence. [2 marks]

3.  Find the sum of the first $10$ terms in the sequence. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have $u_1 = 2$ and $u_2 = 6$.
    Hence the common difference is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= u_2 - u_1 \hspace{1.5em} \\[6pt]
    &= 6 - 2 \\[6pt]
    &= 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with $n = 10$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.8em} u_{10} &= u_1 + (10-1)d \\[6pt]
    &= 2 + (10-1)(4) \\[6pt]
    &= 38
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(u_1 + u_n)$
    with $n=10$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_{10} &= \dfrac{10}{2}(u_1 + u_{10}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{10}{2}(2 + 38) \\[11pt]
    &= 200
    \end{aligned}
    $$
