# AA019

**Maximum mark: 14**

The first two terms of an infinite geometric sequence, in order, are

$$
3\log_3x,\,\, 2\log_3x,\,\, \text{where $x > 0$.}
$$

1.  Find the common ratio, $r$. [2 marks]

2.  Show that the sum of the infinite sequence is $9\log_3 x$.
    [3 marks]

The first three terms of an arithmetic sequence, in order, are

$$
\log_3x,\,\, \log_3 \dfrac{x}{3},\,\, \log_3\dfrac{x}{9},\,\, \text{where $x > 0$.}
$$

3.  Find the common difference $d$, giving your answer as an
    integer. [3 marks]

Let $S_6$ be the sum of the first $6$ terms of the arithmetic sequence.

4.  Show that $S_6 = 6\log_3 x - 15$. [3 marks]

5.  Given that $S_6$ is equal to one third of the sum of the
    infinite geometric\
    sequence, find $x$, giving your answer in the form
     $a^p$ where $a,p \in \mathbb{Z}$.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The common ratio is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{u_2}{u_1} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{2\log_3 x}{3\log_3 x} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{2}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the sum of an infinite geometric sequence formula
    $S_{\infty} = \dfrac{u_1}{1-r}$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_{\infty} &= \dfrac{3\log_3x}{1 - 2/3} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{3\log_3x}{1/3} \\[12pt]
    &= 9\log_3x \hspace{5em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the properties of logarithms, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.9em} d &= u_2 - u_1 \\[6pt]
    &= \log_3 \dfrac{x}{3} - \log_3x \\[6pt]
    &= (\cancel{\log_3x} - \log_33) - \cancel{\log_3x} \hspace{0.5em} \\[10pt]
    &= -1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1 + (n-1)d)$ with
    $n=6$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.8em} S_6 &= \dfrac{6}{2}(2u_1 + (6-1)d) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{6}{2}(2(\log_3 x) + (6-1)(-1)) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{6}{2}(2\log_3 x - 5) \\[11pt]
    &= 6\log_3 x - 15
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  If we solve the equation $S_6 = \dfrac{1}{3}S_{\infty}$ for $x$, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    6\log_3 x - 15 &= \dfrac{1}{3}(9\log_3x) \hspace{3.6em} \\[8pt]
    6\log_3 x - 15 &= 3\log_3x \\[12pt]
    3\log_3x &= 15 \\[12pt]
    \log_3x &= 5 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    x = 3^5 \hspace{2.5em}
    $$
