# AA668

**Maximum mark: 5**

In an arithmetic sequence, the sum of the 2nd and 6th term is $32$.

Given that the sum of the first six terms is $120$, determine the first
term and common difference of the sequence.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let $u_1$ be the first term of the arithmetic sequence and $d$ be the
common difference. The first six terms are
$$
\begin{aligned}
u_1 \hspace{0.15em},\hspace{0.15em} u_1+d \hspace{0.15em},\hspace{0.15em} u_1+2d \hspace{0.15em},\hspace{0.15em} u_1+3d \hspace{0.15em},\hspace{0.15em} u_1+4d \hspace{0.15em},\hspace{0.15em} u_1+5d
\end{aligned}
$$

We know that
$$
\begin{aligned}
u_2 + u_6= 32\qquad s_6=120
\end{aligned}
$$
thus, we can form two
equations
$$
\begin{aligned}
2u_1+6d &= 32 \\[5pt]
6u_1+15d &= 120
\end{aligned}
$$
We can solve this system of
linear equations by using the elimination method or using our G.D.C. We
get
$$
\begin{aligned}
u_1=40 \qquad d = -8
\end{aligned}
$$
