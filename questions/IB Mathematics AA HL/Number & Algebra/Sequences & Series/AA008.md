# AA008

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the following sequence of figures.

![AA008](images/AA008.PNG)

Figure 1 contains $6$ line segments.

1.  Given that Figure $n$ contains $101$ line segments, show that
    $n = 20$.[3 marks]

2.  Find the total number of line segments in the first $20$ figures.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have an arithmetic sequence with $u_1 = 6$, $u_2 = 11$ and
    $u_3 = 16$.

    The common difference is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d &= u_2 - u_1 \\[6pt]
    &= 11 - 6 \\[6pt]
    &= 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, if we substitute $u_n = 101$, $u_1 = 6$
    and $d = 5$ in $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ and solve the resulting equation
    for $n$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.9em} 101 &= 6 + (n-1)(5) \\[6pt]
    101 &= 5n+1 \\[6pt]
    100 &= 5n
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    n = 20\hspace{2.3em}
    $$

2.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(u_1 + u_n)$
    with $n=20$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em} S_{20} &= \dfrac{20}{2}(u_1 + u_{20}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{20}{2}(6 + 101) \\[12pt]
    &= 10(107) \\[16pt]
    &= 1070
    \end{aligned}
    $$
