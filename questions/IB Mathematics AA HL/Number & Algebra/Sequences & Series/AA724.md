# AA724

**Maximum mark: 6**

A $3$D printer builds a set of $49$ Ei$\text{f}$fel Tower
Replicas in different sizes. The height of the largest tower in this set
is $64$ cm. The heights of successive smaller
towers are $95$ % of the preceding larger tower,
as shown in the diagram below.

![AA724a](images/AA724a.PNG)

1.  Find the height of the smallest tower in this set. [3 marks]

2.  Find the **total** height if all $49$ towers were placed one on top
    of another. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have a geometric sequence with the first term $u_1 = 64$ and the
    common ratio $r = 0.95$.

    Hence, using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1r^{n-1}$ with $n=49$,
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_{49} &= u_1r^{49-1} \\[6pt]
    &= 64(0.95)^{49-1} \\[7pt]
    &\approx 5.46 \text{ cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{u_1(1-r^n)}{1-r}$
    with $n=49$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.9em} S_{49} &= \dfrac{u_1(1-r^{49})}{1-r} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{64\hspace{0.05em}(1-0.95^{49})}{1-0.95} \\[12pt]
    &\approx 1176.33 \\[16pt]
    &\approx 1180 \text{ cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
