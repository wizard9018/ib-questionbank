# AA632

**Maximum mark: 6**

The first term of an arithmetic sequence is $24$ and the common
difference is $16$.

1.  Find the value of the $62$nd term of the
    sequence. [2 marks]

The first term of a geometric sequence is $8$. The $4$th term of the
geometric sequence is equal to the $13$th term of the arithmetic
sequence given above.

2.  Write down an equation using this information. [2 marks]

3.  Calculate the common ratio of the geometric sequence.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have $u_1 = 24$ and $d = 16$.
    Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with $n = 62$, we
    find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} u_{62} &= u_1 + (62-1)d \\[6pt]
    &= 24 + (62-1)(16) \\[6pt]
    &= 1000
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have $v_1 = 8$ and $v_4 = u_{13}$.
    Using the $n$th term formula $v_n = v_1r^{n-1}$ with $n = 4$, we
    have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    v_4 &= v_1r^{4-1} \hspace{1.15em} \\[6pt]
    &= 8r^{4-1} \\[6pt]
    &= 8r^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 + (n-1)d$ with $n = 13$, we
    have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} u_{13} &= u_1 + (13-1)d \\[6pt]
    &= 24 + (13-1)(16) \\[6pt]
    &= 216
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    v_4 = u_{13} \hspace{2.6em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    8r^3 = 216 \hspace{3.05em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  If we solve the equation $8r^3 = 216$ for $r$ using G.D.C., we
    obtain  $r = 3$
