# AA011

**Maximum mark: 7**

The first three terms of a geometric sequence are $u_1 = 0.4$,
$u_2 = 0.6$, $u_3 = 0.9$.

1.  Find the value of the common ratio, $r$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the sum of the first ten terms in the sequence. [2 marks]

3.  Find the greatest value of $n$ such that $S_n < 650$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The value of $r$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{u_2}{u_1} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.6}{0.4} \\[13pt]
    &= 1.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{u_1(r^n-1)}{r-1}$
    with $n=10$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.6em} S_{10} &= \dfrac{u_1(r^{10} - 1)}{r-1} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.4(1.5^{10} - 1)}{1.5-1} \\[12pt]
    &\approx 45.3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_n &< 650 \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{u_1(r^n-1)}{r-1} &< 650 \\[4pt]
    \hspace{7.7em} \dfrac{0.4(1.5^n-1)}{1.5-1} &< 650 \\[8pt]
    0.8(1.5^n-1) &< 650 \\[13pt]
    n &< 16.528 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the greatest value of $n$ such that $S_n < 650$ is
     $n=16$
