# AA625

**Maximum mark: 9**

A tennis ball is dropped from a height of $h_0\,\text{cm}$ and bounces on the ground $n$ times. The height the ball reaches after each
bounce, $h_1, h_2, h_3, \dots,h_n,$ form a geometric sequence. The
height that the ball reaches after the first bounce, $h_1$, is
$80$ cm, and after the second bounce, $h_2$, is
$60$ cm.

1.  Find the value of the common ratio for the sequence. [2 marks]

2. Hence, calculate the value of $h_0$. [2 marks]

3.  Determine the height that the ball reaches after the tenth bounce, $h_{10}$.
    [2 marks]

4.  Find the **total distance** travelled by the ball at the moment it bounces on the ground for the sixth time. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The common ratio can be found by taking any term in a geometric sequence, and dividing it by the previous term. We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{h_2}{h_1} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{60}{80} \\[12pt]
    r &= 0.75 \hspace{2.75em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. In any geometric sequence, the second term, $u_2$, is equal to the first term multiplied by the common ratio. Therefore, we can write

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_1r &= u_2 \\[6pt]
    u_1 &= \frac{u_2}{r} \\[6pt]
    h_0 &= \frac{h_1}{r} \\[6pt]
    &= \frac{80}{0.75} \\[6pt]
    &= 106.6...\\[6pt]
    h_0 &= 107\,\text{cm}\,\text{(3 s.f.)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the $n$th term formula $h_n = h_1r^{n-1}$ with $n = 10$ and $h_1=80$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h_{10} &= h_1r^{10-1} \\[6pt]
    &= 80(0.75)^{10-1} \\[6pt]
    &=6.006...\\[7pt]
    h_{10}&= 6.01\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}\,\text{(3 s.f.)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  When the ball bounces on the ground for the 6th time, it has travelled **downwards** 6 times, with a total distance of

    $h_0+h_1+h_2+h_3+h_4+h_5$

    and **upwards** 5 times, with a total distance of

    $h_1+h_2+h_3+h_4+h_5$

    this is shown in the following diagram.

    ![bounce](images/bounce.png)

    We therefore have $h_0$ plus 2 times the sum of the first 5 terms of a geometric sequence with first term $h_1$ and common ratio $r=0.75$. We have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Total distance} &= h_0 + 2\left(\dfrac{h_1\left(1-0.75^5\right)}{1-0.75}\right) \\[10pt]
    &= 106.6...+2\left(\dfrac{80\left(1-0.75^5\right)}{0.25}\right) \\[10pt]
    &= 594.7...\\[10pt]
    \text{Total distance} &= 595\,\text{cm}\,\text{(3 s.f.)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
