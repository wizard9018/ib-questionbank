# AA005

**Maximum mark: 6**

In an arithmetic sequence, $u_5 = 24$, $u_{13} = 80$.

1.  Find the common difference. [2 marks]

2.  Find the first term. [2 marks]

3.  Find the sum of the first $20$ terms in the sequence. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_5 + (n-5)d$ with $n = 13$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_{13} &= u_5 + (13-5)d \\[6pt]
    80 &= 24 + (13-5)d \hspace{1.5em} \\[6pt]
    56 &= 8d
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    d = 7 \hspace{6.35em}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_5 + (n-5)d$ with $n = 1$, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_1 &= u_5 + (1-5)d \\[6pt]
    &= 24 + (1-5)(7) \hspace{0.9em} \\[6pt]
    &= -4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2u_1 + (n-1)d)$ with
    $n=20$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.7em} S_{20} &= \dfrac{20}{2}(2u_1 + (20-1)d) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{20}{2}(2(-4) + (20-1)(7)) \\[11pt]
    &= 1250
    \end{aligned}
    $$
