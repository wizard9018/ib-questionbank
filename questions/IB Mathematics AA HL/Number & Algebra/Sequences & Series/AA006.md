# AA006

**Maximum mark: 6**

The first three terms of a geometric sequence are $u_1 = 32$,
$u_2 = -16$, $u_3 = 8$.

1.  Find the value of the common ratio, $r$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $u_6$. [2 marks]

3.  Find $S_{\infty}$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The value of $r$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{u_2}{u_1} \\[6pt]
    &= -\dfrac{16}{32} \\[6pt]
    &= -\dfrac{1}{2} \hspace{3.6em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_1 r^{n-1}$ with $n = 6$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_6 &= u_1 r^{6-1} \\[8pt]
    &= 32\bigg(\hspace{-0.3em} -\dfrac{1}{2}\bigg)^{6-1} \\[4pt]
    &= 32\bigg(\hspace{-0.3em}-\dfrac{1}{32}\bigg) \\[9pt]
    &= -1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the sum of an infinite geometric sequence formula
    $S_{\infty} = \dfrac{u_1}{1-r}$, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.405em} S_{\infty} &= \dfrac{32}{1-(-1/2)} \hspace{5.45em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{32}{3/2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{64}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
