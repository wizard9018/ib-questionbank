# AA012

**Maximum mark: 5**

The third term of an arithmetic sequence is equal to $7$ and the sum of
the first $8$ terms is $20$.

Find the common difference and the first term.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the $n$th term formula $u_n = u_3 + (n-3)d$ with $n = 1$ and
$n = 8$, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
u_1 &= u_3 + (1-3)d \\[6pt]
&= 7-2d
\end{aligned}
$$
and

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_8 &= u_3 + (8-3)d \\[6pt]
&= 7+5d
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the sum of $n$ terms formula
$S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(u_1 + u_n)$ with $n=8$, we get

 $$
 \begin{aligned}
 \hspace{4em}  S_8 &= \dfrac{8}{2}(u_1 + u_8) \\[6pt]
 20 &= \dfrac{8}{2}((7-2d) + (7+5d)) \\[6pt]
 20 &= \dfrac{8}{2}(14 + 3d) \\[10pt]
 20 &= 56 + 12d \\[14pt]
 -36 &= 12d \\
 \end{aligned}
 $$

$$
d = -3 \hspace{4em}
$$
Hence
we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
u_1 &= u_3-2d \\[6pt]
&= 7 - 2(-3) \hspace{1.5em}  \\[6pt]
&= 13
\end{aligned}
$$
