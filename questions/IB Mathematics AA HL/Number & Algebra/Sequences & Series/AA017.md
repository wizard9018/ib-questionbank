# AA017

**Maximum mark: 6**

Jack rides his bike to work each morning. During the first minute, he
travels $160$ metres. In each subsequent minute, he travels
$80$ % of the distance travelled during the
previous minute.

The distance from his home to work is $750$ metres. Jack leaves his
house at $8$:$30$ am and must be at work at
$8$:$40$ am.

Will Jack arrive to work on time? Justify your answer.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have a geometric sequence with the first term $u_1 = 160$ and the
common ratio $r = 0.8$.

If we use the sum of $n$ terms formula $S_n = \dfrac{u_1(1-r^n)}{1-r}$
with $n=10$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{10} &= \dfrac{u_1(1-r^{10})}{1-r} \\[6pt]
&= \dfrac{160(1-0.8^{10})}{1-0.8} \\[12pt]
&\approx 714
\end{aligned}
$$

$\text{Jack only travels \(714\) metres by \(8\):\(40\)\hspace{0.2em}am. Therefore, Jack \textbf{will not} arrive to work on time.}$
