# AA080

**Maximum mark: 6**

A police department has $4$ male and $7$ female officers. A special
group of $5$ officers is to be assembled for an undercover operation.

1.  Determine how many possible groups can be chosen. [2 marks]

2.  Determine how many groups can be formed
    consisting of $2$ males and $3$ $\text{females.}$[2 marks]

3.  Determine how many groups can be formed consisting of at least one
    male. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We choose $5$ people from $4+7 = 11$ males and females.
    Hence the number of possible groups is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \binom{11}{5} &= \dfrac{11!}{5!\cdot6!} \\[6pt]
    &= 462
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We choose $2$ officers from $4$ males and then we choose $3$ more
    officers from $7$ females. Hence the number of groups consisting of
    $3$ males and $2$ females is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.8em} \binom{4}{2}\binom{7}{3} &= \dfrac{4!}{2!\cdot2!}\cdot\dfrac{7!}{3!\cdot4!} \\[6pt]
    &= 210
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The number of groups consisting of $0$ males and $5$ females is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.8em} \binom{4}{0}\binom{7}{5} &= \dfrac{4!}{0!\cdot4!}\cdot\dfrac{7!}{5!\cdot2!} \\[6pt]
    &= 21
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the number of groups consisting of at
    least $1$ male is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    462 - 21 &= \hspace{0.525em}441 \hspace{1.65em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
