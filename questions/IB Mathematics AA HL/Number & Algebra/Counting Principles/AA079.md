# AA079

**Maximum mark: 6**

A school basketball team of $5$ students is selected from $8$ boys and
$4$ girls.

1.  Determine how many possible teams can be chosen. [2 marks]

2.  Determine how many teams can be formed consisting of $3$ boys and
    $2$ girls? [2 marks]

3.  Determine how many teams can be formed consisting of at most $3$
    girls? [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We choose $5$ students from $8+4 = 12$ boys and girls.
    Hence the number of possible teams is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \binom{12}{5} &= \dfrac{12!}{5!\cdot7!} \\[6pt]
    &= 792
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We choose $3$ students from $8$ boys and then we choose $2$ more
    students from $4$ girls. Hence the number of teams consisting of $3$
    boys and $2$ girls is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.8em} \binom{8}{3}\binom{4}{2} &= \dfrac{8!}{3!\cdot5!}\cdot\dfrac{4!}{2!\cdot2!} \\[6pt]
    &= 336
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  A team of $5$ students can either have $0,1,2,3$ or $4$ girls. The
    number of teams consisting of $1$ boy and $4$ girls is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \binom{8}{1}\binom{4}{4} = 8 \hspace{3.5em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the
    number of teams consisting of at most $3$ girls is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    792 - 8 &= \hspace{0.525em}784 \hspace{1.25em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
