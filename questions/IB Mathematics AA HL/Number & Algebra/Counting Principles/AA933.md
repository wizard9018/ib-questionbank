# AA933

**Maximum mark: 7**

A professor and five of his students attend a talk given in a lecture series. They have a row of 8 seats to themselves.

Find the number of ways the professor and his students can sit if

1. the professor and his students sit together. [3 marks]

2. the students decide to sit at least one seat apart from their professor. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. We can consider the 8 seats as three "blocks", where one block consists of the 6 adjacent seats that the professor and his students occupy, and the other two blocks are the remaining two single seats. There are three possible arrangements of these blocks:

    ![AA933a](images/AA933a.PNG)

    Within the block of six seats occupied by the professor and his students, they can arrange themselves in $6!$ different ways. Let $k$ be the number of possible ways the six people can sit. The total number of arrangements is

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    k &= 3 \times 6! \\[11pt]

    &\hspace{-0.8em}\boxed{k = 2\hspace{0.15em}160}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Let $k$ be the number of possible ways the students can sit at least one seat apart from their professor. We have two cases:

    

    $\bullet$ If the professor sits at one of the ends, then the students have $6$ seats available to them. There are two possibilities: 

    ![AA933b](images/AA933b.PNG)

    The students can arrange themselves in any order among the six seats. So we have 

    ![AA933d](images/AA933d.PNG)

    $\bullet$ If the professor sits in one of the six seats in the middle, the students have only $5$ seats available to them, noting that the five students do not all need to sit together. There are six possible seats where the professor can sit, one example of which is shown here:

    ![AA933c](images/AA933c.PNG)

    The students can arrange themselves in any order among the five seats. So we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}  

    k_2 &= 6 \times 5! \\[10pt]

    &= 720

    \end{aligned}

    $$  

    The total will be $k = k_1 + k_2$. 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    k &= 1\hspace{0.15em}440 + 720 \\[10pt]

    &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{k = 2\hspace{0.15em}160}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
