# AA880

**Maximum mark: 7**

There are six office cubicles arranged in a grid with two rows and three
columns as shown in the following diagram. Aria, Bella, Charlotte,
Danna, and Emma are to be stationed inside the cubicles to work on
various company projects.

![AA880_q](images/AA880_q.png)

Find the number of ways of placing the team members in the cubicles in
each of the following cases.

1.  Each cubicle is large enough to contain the five team members, but
    Danna and Emma must not be placed in the same cubicle.[2 marks]

2.  Each cubicle may only contain one team member. But Aria and Bella
    must not be placed in cubicles which share a boundary, as they tend
    to get distracted by each other.[5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We start counting the number of ways we can place each of the team
    members. Aria, Bella, Charlotte and Danna can be placed in any of
    the six cubicles, but once Danna is placed, Emma can be placed in
    the remaining five cubicles. Hence, the total number of ways of
    placing the team members in this case is

    $$
    6 \times 6 \times 6 \times 6 \times 5  = \enskip  6\hspace{0.15em}480
    $$

2.  Let's label the cubicles as follows

    ![46e01a12bceb8a96908234fb3cd05cae223de337.svg](images/46e01a12bceb8a96908234fb3cd05cae223de337.svg)

    We have several ways of placing Aria and
    Bella:

    | Aria's cubicle | Bella's cubicle |
    |:--------------:|:---------------:|
    |       1        |     3, 5, 6     |
    |       2        |      4, 6       |
    |       3        |     1, 4, 5     |
    |       4        |     2, 3, 6     |
    |       5        |      1, 3       |
    |       6        |     1, 2, 4     |

    Thus, there are $16$ ways of placing Aria and Bella in cubicles that
    do not share a boundary.

    After that, Charlotte can be placed in any of the 4 remaining
    cubicles, Danna in any of the 3 remaining cubicles, and Emma in any
    of the 2 remaining cubicles.

    Therefore, the total number of arrangements in this case is

    $$
    16 \times 4 \times 3 \times 2 = \enskip384
    $$
