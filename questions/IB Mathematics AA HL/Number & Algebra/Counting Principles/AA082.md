# AA082

**Maximum mark: 6**

There are $11$ players on a football team who are asked to line up in one straight line for a team photo. Three of the team members named Adam, Brad and Chris refuse to stand next to each other. There is no restriction on the order in which the other team members position themselves.

Find the number of different orders in which the $11$ team members can be positioned for the photo.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

To ensure Adam, Brad and Chris are not next to each other, arrange the other $8$ players first and then slot Adam, Brad and Chris into the gaps between them.

The $8$ unrestricted players can be arranged in $8!$ ways.

$$\\[-2.5em]$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
\end{aligned}
$$
This creates $9$ gaps in which Adam, Brad and Chris can stand.
$$\_\,P\,\_\,P\,\_\,P\,\_\,P\,\_\,P\,\_\,P\,\_\,P\,\_\,P\,\_$$
Adam, Brad and Chris must occupy three different gaps, and since they are distinct people the order in which they fill the gaps matters, so we use permutations.

Using the **permutations formula (1.10)**, to count the number of ordered arrangements of $3$ people chosen from $9$ gaps, $^nP_r = \dfrac{n!}{(n-r)!}$.

Choosing and arranging Adam, Brad and Chris in the $9$ gaps gives $^9P_3$.

$$\\[-2.5em]$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
\end{aligned}
$$

Evaluating the permutation, $^9P_3 = \dfrac{9!}{6!} = 504$.

$$\\[-2.5em]$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
    \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
\end{aligned}
$$

Multiplying this with the number of arrangements of the $8$ unrestricted players to count the total number of orderings.
$$
\begin{aligned}
n &= 8! \times {}^9P_3 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]
  &= 8! \times \dfrac{9!}{6!} \\[6pt]
  &= 8! \,(504) \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}} \\[6pt]
n &= \boxed{20\hspace{0.15em}321\hspace{0.15em}280} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
\end{aligned}
$$
**Alternative method**

To find $n$, subtract from $11!$ the number of orderings in which exactly two, and exactly three, of Adam, Brad and Chris stand next to each other.
$$
\begin{aligned}
n &= 11! - 8!\times\binom{3}{2}\times2!\times\binom{9}{2}\times2! - 8!\times\binom{3}{3}\times3!\times\binom{9}{1}\times1! \\[6pt]
  &= 11! - 8!\times3\times2!\times9\times8 - 8!\times3!\times9 \\[6pt]
n &= \boxed{20\hspace{0.15em}321\hspace{0.15em}280}
\end{aligned}
$$
