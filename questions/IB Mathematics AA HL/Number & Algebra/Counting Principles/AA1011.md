# AA1011

**Maximum mark: 6**

An arts and crafts store is offering a special package on personalized keychains. 

The store has a selection of $6$ distinct types of charms.

Customers can personalize their keychains with between 1 to $3$ distinct charms from the selection mentioned above.

Determine how many ways a customer can personalize a keychain if

1. The order of the selections is important. [3 marks]

2. The order of the selections is **not** important. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. A customer can select either 1, 2 or 3 unique charms, all of which are different. Therefore we have 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        k &= {}^6P_1 + {}^6P_2 + {}^6P_3

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Using the formula for permutations, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        k &= \dfrac{6!}{5!} + \dfrac{6!}{4!} + \dfrac{6!}{3!} \\[10pt]

        &= \dfrac{6\times\cancel{5!}}{\cancel{5!}} + \dfrac{6\times5\times\cancel{4!}}{\cancel{4!}} + \dfrac{6\times5\times4\times\cancel{3!}}{\cancel{3!}} \\[10pt]

        &= 6 + 30 + 120

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    In total there are $\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{156}\hspace{0.525em}$ ways in which a customer can personalize a keychain if the order is important.

2. A customer can select either 1, 2 or 3 unique charms, all of which are different. Therefore we have 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        k &= {}^6C_1 + {}^6C_2 + {}^6C_3

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Using the formula for combinations, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        k &= \dfrac{6!}{1!5!} + \dfrac{6!}{2!4!} + \dfrac{6!}{3!3!} \\[10pt]

        &= \dfrac{6\times\cancel{5!}}{1\times\cancel{5!}} + \dfrac{6\times5\times\cancel{4!}}{2\times\cancel{4!}} + \dfrac{\cancel{6}\times5\times4\times\cancel{3!}}{\cancel{3!}\times\cancel{3!}} \\[10pt]

        &= 6 + 15 + 20

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    In total there are $\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{41}\hspace{0.525em}$ ways in which a customer can personalize a keychain if the order is not important.
