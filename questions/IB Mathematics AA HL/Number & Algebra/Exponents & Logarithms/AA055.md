# AA055

**Maximum mark: 6**

Find the value of

1.  $\log_7 98 - \log_7 2$; [2 marks]

2.  $49^{\log_7 6}$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the properties of logarithms, we get

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \log_7 98 - \log_7 2 &= \log_7\Big(\dfrac{98}{2}\Big) \hspace{3.1em} \\[6pt]
     &= \log_7 49 \\[10pt]
     &= \log_7\big(7^2\big) \\[12pt]
     &= 2 \log_7 7 \\[12pt]
     &= 2
     \end{aligned}
     $$

2.  Using the properties of exponents and logarithms, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    49^{\log_7 6} &= \big(7^2\big)^{\log_7 6} \\[8pt]
    &= 7^{2\log_7 6} \\[6pt]
    &= 7^{\log_7 (6^2)} \\[7pt]
    &= 7^{\log_7 36} \\[9pt]
    &= 36
    \end{aligned}
    $$
