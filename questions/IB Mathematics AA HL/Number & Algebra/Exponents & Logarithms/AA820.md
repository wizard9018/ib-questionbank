# AA820

**Maximum mark: 7**

Solve the simultaneous equations:
$$
\begin{aligned}
1 + 2\log_5 x &= \log_5 7y \\[6pt]
\log_7 (6x-1) &= 1 + \log_7 y
\end{aligned}
$$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the properties of logarithms, we rewrite the given equations as
follows.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\log_5 5 + \log_5 x^2 &= \log_5 7y \\[6pt]
\log_5 5x^2 &= \log_5 7y \\[6pt]
5x^2 &= 7y \hspace{5em}{(1)} \\[6pt]
\log_7 (6x-1) &= 1 + \log_7 y \\[6pt]
\hspace{2.75em} \log_7 (6x-1) &= \log_7 7 + \log_7 y \\[6pt]
\log_7 (6x-1) &= \log_7 7y \\[6pt]
6x - 1 &= 7y \hspace{5em}{(2)}
\end{aligned}
$$
From the equations (1) and (2),
we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
5x^2 &= 6x - 1 \\[6pt]
5x^2 - 6x + 1 &= 0 \\[6pt]
(5x - 1)(x - 1) &= 0 \\[6pt]
x &= \dfrac{1}{5} \text{ or } x = 1
\end{aligned}
$$

Substituting $x = \dfrac{1}{5}$ into the equation (1), we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
5 \left(\dfrac{1}{5} \right)^2 &= 7y \hspace{2.5em}\\[12pt]
\dfrac{1}{5} &= 7y \\[12pt]
y &= \dfrac{1}{35}\\
\end{aligned}
$$
Substituting $x = 1$ into the
equation (1), we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
5(1)^2  &= 7y \\[12pt]
5 &= 7y \\[12pt]
y &= \dfrac{5}{7}
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus, the solutions are

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\hspace{-2.15em}x = \dfrac{1}{5}, \, y = \dfrac{1}{35} \\[12pt]
& \hspace{-1.9em}x = 1, \,\, y = \dfrac{5}{7}
\end{aligned}
$$
