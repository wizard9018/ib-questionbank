# AA048

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $p=\ln 2$ and $q = \ln 6$. Write down the following expressions in
terms of $p$ and $q$.

1.  $\ln 12$ [2 marks]

2.  $\ln 3$ [2 marks]

3.  $\ln 48$ [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the properties of natural logarithms, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \ln 12 &= \ln (2\cdot6) \\[6pt]
    &= \ln 2 + \ln 6 \\[6pt]
    &= p + q
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the properties of natural logarithms, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} \ln 3 &= \ln \bigg(\dfrac{6}{2}\bigg) \\[3pt]
    &= \ln 6 - \ln 2 \\[10pt]
    &= q - p
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the properties of natural logarithms, we obtain

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{0.6em} \ln 48 &= \ln \big(2^3\hspace{-0.2em}\cdot6\big) \\[6pt]
     &= 3\ln2 +  \ln 6 \\[6pt]
     &= 3p + q
     \end{aligned}
     $$
