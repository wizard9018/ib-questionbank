# AA047

**Maximum mark: 7**

Find the value of each of the following, giving your answer as an
integer.

1.  $\log_{10} 100$. [2 marks]

2.  $\log_{10} 50 + \log_{10} 2$. [2 marks]

3.  $\log_{10} 4 - \log_{10} 40$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the properties of logarithms, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.65em} \log_{10} 100 &= \log_{10} \big(10^2\big) \\[6pt]
    &= 2\log_{10} 10 \\[6pt]
    &= 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the properties of logarithms, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \log_{10} 50 + \log_{10} 2 &= \log_{10} (50\cdot2) \hspace{2.4em} \\[6pt]
    &= \log_{10} 100 \\[6pt]
    &= 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the properties of logarithms, we obtain

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \log_{10} 4 - \log_{10} 40 &= \log_{10} \bigg(\dfrac{4}{40}\bigg) \hspace{2.5em} \\[3pt]
     &= \log_{10} \bigg(\dfrac{1}{10}\bigg) \\[9pt]
     &= \log_{10} \big(10^{-1}\big) \\[16pt]
     &= (-1) \log_{10} 10 \\[9pt]
     &= -1
     \end{aligned}
     $$
