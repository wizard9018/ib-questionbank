# AA052

**Maximum mark: 6**

1.  Write down the value of
    1.  $\log_2 8$;

    2.  $\log_5\Big(\dfrac{1}{25}\Big)$;
    3.  $\log_9 3$. [3 marks]

2.  Hence solve
    $\log_2 8 + \log_5\Big(\dfrac{1}{25}\Big) + \log_9 3 = \log_{16} x$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1.  Using the properties of logarithms, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \log_2 8 &= \log_2 \big(2^3\big) \\[6pt]
        &= 3\log_2 2 \\[6pt]
        &= 3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the properties of logarithms, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \log_5\Big(\dfrac{1}{25}\Big) &= \log_5\big(5^{-2}\big) \hspace{1.4em} \\[6pt]
        &= (-2)\log_5 5 \\[10pt]
        &= -2
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3.  Using the properties of logarithms, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \log_9 3 &= \dfrac{\ln 3}{\ln 9} \\[10pt]
        &= \dfrac{\ln 3}{\ln \big(3^2\big)} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{\ln 3}{2\ln 3} \\[10pt]
        &= \dfrac{1}{2}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \log_2 8 + \log_5\Big(\dfrac{1}{25}\Big) + \log_9 3 &= \log_{16} x \hspace{6.5em} \\[6pt]
    3 + (-2) + \dfrac{1}{2} &= \log_{16} x \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{3}{2} &= \log_{16} x \\[10pt]
    16^{3/2} &= x \\[14pt]
    4^3 & = x \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $$
     \hspace{2.7em} x = 64
     $$
