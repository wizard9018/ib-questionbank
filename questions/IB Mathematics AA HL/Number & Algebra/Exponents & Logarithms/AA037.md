# AA037

**Maximum mark: 6**

Given that $\log_a 2 = 5$.

1.  Find the exact value of $\log_a 32$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the exact value of $\log_{\sqrt{a}} 2$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the value of $a$, giving your answer correct to $3$ significant
    figures. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the properties of logarithms, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \log_a 32 &= \log_a 2^5 \hspace{2.1em} \\[6pt]
    &= 5 \log_a 2 \\[6pt]
    &= 5(5) \\[6pt]
    &= 25
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the properties of logarithms, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \log_{\sqrt{a}} 2 &= \dfrac{\log 2}{\log \sqrt{a}} \hspace{2.1em} \\[2pt]
    &= \dfrac{\log 2}{\frac{1}{2}\log a} \\[6pt]
    &= 2\log_a 2 \\[12pt]
    &= 2(5) \\[12pt]
    &= 10
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the definition of logarithm, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \log_a 2 &= 5 \hspace{4em} \\[6pt]
    a^5 &= 2 \\[4pt]
    a &= 2^\frac{1}{5} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    a \approx 1.15 \hspace{0.8em}
    $$
