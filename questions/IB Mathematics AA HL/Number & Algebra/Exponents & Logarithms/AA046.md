# AA046

**Maximum mark: 6**

Find the value of each of the following, giving your answer as an
integer.

1.  $\log_6 6$. [2 marks]

2.  $\log_6 9 + \log_6 4$. [2 marks]

3.  $\log_6 72 - \log_6 2$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\log_6 6 = 1$

2.  Using the properties of logarithms, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \log_6 9 + \log_6 4 &= \log_6 (9\cdot4) \\[6pt]
    &= \log_6 36 \\[5pt]
    &= \log_6 \big(6^2\big) \\[6pt]
    &= 2\log_6 6 \\[6pt]
    &= 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the properties of logarithms, we obtain

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \log_6 72 - \log_6 2 &= \log_6 \bigg(\dfrac{72}{2}\bigg) \hspace{0.15em} \\[5pt]
     &= \log_6 36 \\[12pt]
     &= 2
     \end{aligned}
     $$
