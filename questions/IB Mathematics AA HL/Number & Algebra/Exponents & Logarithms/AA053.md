# AA053

**Maximum mark: 6**

1.  Write down the value of
    1.  $\log_3 81$;

    2.  $\log_2\Big(\dfrac{1}{8}\Big)$;
    3.  $\log_{25} 5$. [3 marks]

2.  Hence solve
    $\log_3 81 + \log_2\Big(\dfrac{1}{8}\Big) + \log_{25} 5 = \log_{9} x$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  1.  Using the properties of logarithms, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \log_3 81 &= \log_3\big(3^4\big) \\[6pt]
        &= 4\log_3 3 \\[6pt]
        &= 4
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the properties of logarithms, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \log_2\Big(\dfrac{1}{8}\Big) &= \log_2\big(2^{-3}\big) \hspace{0.3em} \\[6pt]
        &= (-3)\log_2 2 \\[10pt]
        &= -3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3.  Using the properties of logarithms, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \log_{25} 5 &= \dfrac{\ln 5}{\ln 25} \\[10pt]
        &= \dfrac{\ln 5}{\ln \big(5^2\big)} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{\ln 5}{2\ln 5} \\[10pt]
        &= \dfrac{1}{2}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \log_3 81 + \log_2\Big(\dfrac{1}{8}\Big) + \log_{25} 5 &= \log_{9} x \hspace{7.6em} \\[6pt]
    4 + (-3) + \dfrac{1}{2} &= \log_{9} x \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{3}{2} &= \log_{9} x \\[10pt]
    9^{3/2} &= x \\[14pt]
    3^3 & = x \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $$
     \hspace{2.4em} x = 27
     $$
