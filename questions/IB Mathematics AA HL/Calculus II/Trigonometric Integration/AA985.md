# AA985

**Maximum mark: 6**

The function $f$ is defined as $f(x)=\sin{(x^2\ln (1.5-x) + 2)}+x^3$, for $-1.3\leq x \leq 1.3$. The graph of $f$ is shown in the following diagram.

![aa985a](images/aa985a.PNG)

The graph of $f$ intersects the $x$-axis at point A, and has a local maximum at point B.

1. 1.  Find the coordinates of B.

    2. Find the $x$-coordinate of A.[3 marks]

2. Find the total area enclosed by the graph of $f$, the $x$-axis and the lines $x=-1.3$ and $x=1.3$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using the G.D.C. we find that

    1.  $$\boxed{\text{The coordinates of B are $(1.12,2.11)$.}}$$

    2.  $$\boxed{\text{The $x$-coordinate of A is $-0.818$.}}$$

2. The area enclosed by $f$, the $x$-axis, $x=-1.3$ and $x=1.3$ is shown in the following diagram.

    ![aa985b](images/aa985b.PNG)

    Using the G.D.C. we can evaluate the definite integrals. Hence,

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \text{Total Area} &=  R_1 + R_2 \\[8pt]

    &=  \int_{-1.3}^{1.3} f(x) \,dx  \\[8pt]

    &=  -\int_{-1.3}^{-0.818} f(x) \,dx  + \int_{-0.818}^{1.3} f(x) \,dx\\[8pt]

    &\approx 0.5763 + 2.330 \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-4.75em}\boxed{\text{Total Area} \approx 2.91}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
