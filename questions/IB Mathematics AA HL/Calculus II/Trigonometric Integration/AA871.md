# AA871

**Maximum mark: 14**

Consider the function $f(x)$ defined by $f(x) = 12 - 12\sin x$, for
$0 \leq x \leq 3\pi$.

The following diagram shows the graph of $y = f(x)$.

![bc2e839433d2b4b92bb9023f3a84ace75deaa6a1.svg](images/bc2e839433d2b4b92bb9023f3a84ace75deaa6a1.svg)

The graph of $f$ touches the $x$-axis at points A and B, as shown. The
shaded region is enclosed by the graph of $y = f(x)$ and the $x$-axis,
between the points A and B.

1.  Find the $x$-coordinate of A and B. [3 marks]

2.  Show that the area of the shaded region is $24\pi$. [4 marks]

The following right cone has a total surface area of $24\pi$, equal to
the shaded area in the previous diagram.

![b92d7c53920c6d4b2c99b29a92442bcb40021d46.svg](images/b92d7c53920c6d4b2c99b29a92442bcb40021d46.svg)

The cone has a base radius of $3$, height $h$, and slant height $l$.

3.  Find the value of $l$. [3 marks]

4.  Hence, find the volume of the cone. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we solve the equation $f(x) = 0$ for $x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    12 - 12\sin x &= 0 \\[10pt]
    \sin x &= 1 \\[6pt]
    x &= \dfrac{\pi}{2}, \, \dfrac{5\pi}{2} \hspace{3em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, the $x$-coordinate of A is
     $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$
      and the $x$-coordinate of B is
     $\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$

2.  The area of the shaded region is defined by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5em} A &= \displaystyle \int_{\pi/2}^{5\pi/2} (12-12\sin x)\, \mathrm{d}{x} \\[6pt]
    &= \left[12x + 12\cos x\right]_{\pi/2}^{5\pi/2} \\[6pt]
    &= \left[12\left( \dfrac{5\pi}{2}\right) + 12\cos \left( \dfrac{5\pi}{2}\right)\right] - \left[12\left( \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right) + 12\cos \left( \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)\right] \\[8pt]
    &= (30\pi + 0) - (6\pi + 0) \\[10pt]
    &= 24\pi \hspace{3em} \text{[Shown]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the total surface area of a right cone formula
    $A = \pi rl + \pi r^2$ with $r = 3$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \pi (3)l + \pi (3)^2 &= 24\pi \\[6pt]
    3l + 9 &= 24 \\[6pt]
    3l &= 15 \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-0.5em}l = 5 \hspace{2.5em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using the Pythagorean theorem, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h^2 + r^2 &= l^2 \\[6pt]
    h^2 + 3^2 &= 5^2 \\[6pt]
    h^2 + 9 &= 25 \\[6pt]
    h^2 &= 16 \\[6pt]
    h &= 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the volume of a right cone
    formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.5em} V &= \dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2h \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{3}\pi (3)^2(4) \\[10pt]
    &= 12\pi \text{ unit}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
