# AA527

**Maximum mark: 7**

1.  Find $\displaystyle \int \dfrac{e^{3x}}{1+e^{3x}}\, \mathrm{d}x$.
    [3 marks]

2.  Find $\displaystyle \int \sin 5x \cos 5x\, \mathrm{d}x$ [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting $u =1 + e^{3x}$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int \dfrac{e^{3x}}{1+e^{3x}}\, \mathrm{d}x
    & = \dfrac 1 3\int \dfrac 1 {u}\,\textrm du \\[6pt]
    & = \dfrac 1 3 \ln( u) + C\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.25em} = \dfrac 1 3 \ln(1 + e^{3x}) + C
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Since $\sin 5x \cos 5 x = \dfrac 1 2\sin 10x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int \sin 5x \cos 5x\, \mathrm{d}x
    & = \dfrac 1 2\int \sin 10 x \, \textrm d x\hspace{1.1cm}\\[10pt]
    &\hspace{.25em} = -\dfrac 1 {20} \cos 10 x + C
    \end{aligned}
    $$
