# AA557

**Maximum mark: 6**

The graph of $f$ passes through the point
$\left(\dfrac {\pi} {12},2\right)$.

Given that $f'(x) = 2 \cos (2x)$, find $f(x)$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We integrate
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x)        & = \int 2 \cos(2x)\,\textrm dx\\[6pt]
& = \sin (2x) + C
\end{aligned}
$$

Let's determine $C$. Substituting $\left(\dfrac {\pi} {12},2\right)$
into the expression for $f$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{5mm}
2       & = \sin \left(\dfrac \pi {6}\right) + C\\[6pt]
2       & = \dfrac 1 2 + C\\[6pt]
C       & = \dfrac 3 2
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence
  $f(x) = \sin (2x) + \dfrac 3 2$
