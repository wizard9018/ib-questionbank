# AA602

**Maximum mark: 15**

Consider the differential equation
$\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} - \dfrac{y}{x} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, where
$x > 0$.

1.  Given that $y(1) = 2$, use Euler's method with step length $h = 0.5$
    to find an approximation for $y(3)$. Give your answer correct to two
    significant figures. [6 marks]

2.  Solve the equation
    $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} - \dfrac{y}{x} = \dfrac{1}{2}$ for
    $y(1) = 2$.  [6 marks]

3.  Find the percentage error when $y(3)$ is approximated by the final
    rounded value found in part (a). Give your answer correct to two
    significant figures. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} &= \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{y}{x} \hspace{3em} [x > 0] \hspace{1.6em} \\[6pt]
    \hspace{6.55em} y(1) &= 2 \\[10pt]
    h &= 0.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7em} x\text{-iteration:}\hspace{0.75em} x_{n+1} &= x_n + 0.5 \\[4pt]
    y\text{-iteration:}\hspace{0.75em} y_{n+1} &= y_n +0.5\times\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{y_n}{x_n}\bigg] \hspace{3em} [\text{for $n \in \mathbb{N}$}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using Euler's method, we obtain

    ![74696bc6706165e57bfc152ad13c76ba39254d2a.svg](images/74696bc6706165e57bfc152ad13c76ba39254d2a.svg)

    Hence we find
     $y(3) \approx 7.4$

2.  An integrating factor is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    I(x) &= e^{\footnotesize\raisebox{0.2em}{\mbox{$\int-\frac{1}{x}\,\mathrm{d}x$}}} \\[7pt]
    &= e^{-\ln x} \\[5pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{x}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, multiplying both sides of the
    differential equation by $1/x$, we deduce
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{1}{x}\cdot\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} - \dfrac{y}{x^2} &= \dfrac{1}{2x} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\bigg[\dfrac{y}{x}\bigg] &= \dfrac{1}{2x}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Integrating both sides of the equation $(1)$ with respect to $x$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.7em} \int \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\bigg[\dfrac{y}{x}\bigg]\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{d}x &= \int\dfrac{1}{2x}\,\mathrm{d}x \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{y}{x} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\ln x + C \hspace{3em} [\text{since $x > 0$}] \\[8pt]
    y &= \dfrac{1}{2}x\ln x + x\hspace{0.05em}C
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting $x = 1$ and $y = 2$ in the equation $(2)$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.4em} 2 &= \dfrac{1}{2}(1)(0) + (1)C \\[6pt]
    C &= 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.15em} y = \dfrac{1}{2}x\ln x + 2x
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Evaluating $y(x)$ for $x = 3$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.9em} y(3) &= \dfrac{1}{2}(3)\ln 3 + 2(3) \\[9pt]
    &= 7.6479\dots \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the percentage error
    formula
    $\epsilon = \bigg|\dfrac{v_\mathrm{A} - v_\mathrm{E}}{v_\mathrm{E}}\bigg|\times 100\hspace{0.05em}\%$
    with $v_\mathrm{E} = (7.6479\dots)$ and $v_\mathrm{A} = 7.4$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \epsilon &= \bigg|\dfrac{7.4 - (7.6479\dots)}{(7.6479\dots)}\bigg|\times 100\hspace{0.05em}\% \\[9pt]
    &\approx 3.2\hspace{0.05em}\%
    \end{aligned}
    $$
