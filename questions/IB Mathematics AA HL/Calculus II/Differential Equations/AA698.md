# AA698

**Maximum mark: 5**

Consider the differential equation
$\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} - e^x = 1$.

Given that $y\left(0\right) = 1$, use Euler's method with step length
$h = 0.25$ to find an approximation for $y\left(1\right)$. Give your
answer correct to two decimal places.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} &= e^x +1  \hspace{1.6em} \\[6pt]
\hspace{6.25em} y(0) &= 1 \\[6pt]
h &= 0.25
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
x\text{-iteration:}\hspace{0.75em} x_{n+1} &= x_n + 0.25 \\[2pt]
y\text{-iteration:}\hspace{0.75em} y_{n+1} &= y_n +0.25\times\bigg[e^{x_n} + 1 \bigg] \hspace{2em} [\text{for $n \in \mathbb{N}$}] \\
\end{aligned}
$$

Using Euler's method, we obtain

![5012cde401b92b0132542335f99910ec80cc28af.svg](images/5012cde401b92b0132542335f99910ec80cc28af.svg)

Hence we find
 $y(1) \approx 3.51$
