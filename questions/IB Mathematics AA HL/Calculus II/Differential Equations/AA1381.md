# AA1381

**Maximum mark: 6**

Solve the differential equation

$$

\begin{aligned}

    (1+x^2)\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}=4xy^2

\end{aligned}

$$

for $y$, which satisfies the initial condition $y(0)=-1$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Rewriting the differential equation and integrating with respect to $x$ and $y$, we have 

$$

    \begin{aligned}

    \textcolor{white}{placeholder}\tag*{{\textbf{M1}}}

    \end{aligned}

$$

$$

    \begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{y^2} &=\dfrac{4x}{1+x^2}\,\mathrm{d}x\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\[8pt]

    \int\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{y^2} &=\int \dfrac{4x}{1+x^2}\,\mathrm{d}x\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\[4pt]

    -\dfrac{1}{y} &= 2\ln(1+x^2)+C \rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}\quad [\hspace{0.01em}\text{since }1+x^2>0]\hspace{2em}[1] \tag*{\textbf{{A1A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

$$

Substituting $x=0$ and $y=-1$ in [1], we get

$$

\begin{aligned}

\textcolor{white}{placeholder}\tag*{{\textbf{M1}}}

\end{aligned}

$$

$$

\begin{aligned}

    -\dfrac{1}{(-1)} &= 2\ln(1+(0)^2)+C \rule[-5mm]{0pt}{7mm}\\

    1 &= \cancel{2\ln(1)}+C\rule[-5mm]{0pt}{10mm}\\

    C &= 1\rule[-2mm]{0pt}{7mm}\tag*{\textbf{{(A1)}}}

\end{aligned}

$$

Substituting the value of $C$ in [1], we get

$$

    \begin{aligned}

    -\dfrac{1}{y} &= 2\ln(1+x^2)+1 \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\[2pt]

    &\hspace{-3mm}y = -\dfrac{1}{2\ln(1+x^2)+1}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9mm}\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

$$
