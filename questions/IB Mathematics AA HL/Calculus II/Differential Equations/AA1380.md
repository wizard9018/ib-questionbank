# AA1380

Consider the differential equation $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} + \dfrac{y}{x + 2} = x$, $x > -2$, which passes through the point $(0, 2)$.

1. Using Euler's method with a step length of $h = 0.5$, find an approximation for the value of $y$ when $x = 1$. Give your answer to two decimal places.[3 marks]

The exact solution of the differential equation is $y = f(x)$, where

$$

\begin{aligned}

    f(x) = \dfrac{1}{x + 2}\left(\dfrac{x^3}{3} + x^2 + 4\right).

\end{aligned}

$$

2.  1. Sketch the graph of $y = f(x)$ for $0 \le x \le 1$.[2 marks]

    2. Hence determine whether your answer in part (a) is an overestimate or an underestimate of the true value of $y(1)$. Justify your answer.[2 marks]
