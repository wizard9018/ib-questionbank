# AA1385

**Maximum mark: 18**

The mass $M$ (in kilograms) of a growing crystal after $t$ hours can be modelled by the differential equation  

$$

\begin{aligned}

\hspace{5mm}\dfrac{\mathrm{d}M}{\mathrm{d}t} = kM^{1/3}\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{9mm}, \qquad k,\,t>0

\end{aligned}

$$

1. Show that the mass of the crystal is given by  

$$

\begin{aligned}

    M(t)=\left(\dfrac{2kt}{3}+M_0^{2/3}\right)^{3/2}\rule[-4.5mm]{0pt}{10.5mm}, \quad t>0

\end{aligned}

$$

- where $M_0$ is the initial mass of the crystal.  [4 marks]

It is known that the initial mass of the crystal was $64$ kg, and that after $9$ hours the mass had doubled.

2. Find the value of $k$ to three significant figures.  [2 marks]

3. Estimate the mass of the crystal after $15$ hours to the nearest kilogram.  [2 marks]

After further analysis, a refined model for the mass of the crystal is given by  

$$

\begin{aligned}

\dfrac{\mathrm{d}M_2}{\mathrm{d}\hspace{0.05em}t}

=

\big(1.6+2\sin(0.25\pi t)\big)M_2^{1/3}

\end{aligned}

$$

where $t$ is measured in hours, $t \geq 0$.

4. Verify that  

$$

\begin{aligned}

M_2=\left(\dfrac{2(1.6)t}{3}-\dfrac{16\cos(0.25\pi t)}{3\pi}+64^{2/3}\right)^{3/2}

\end{aligned}

$$

- is a solution of this new di\-ffe\-ren\-tial equation. [5 marks] 

5. Sketch the graph of $M_2(t)$ and the graph of $M(t)$ found in parts (a) and (b) on the same axes, for $0 \leq t \leq 40$.  [3 marks]

6. Use $M_2(t)$ to estimate the number of **whole** hours it takes for the mass of the crystal to reach $150$ kg. [2 marks]

1. Solving the separable differential equation, we obtain 

$$

\begin{aligned}

    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}M}{\mathrm{d}\hspace{0.05em}t} &= kM^{1/3} \tag*{\textbf{{M1}}}\\[12pt] 

    \int M^{-1/3}\,\mathrm{d}M &= \int k \, \mathrm{d}\hspace{0.05em}t \\[12pt]

    \dfrac{3}{2}M^{2/3} &= k\hspace{0.05em}t+C \\[12pt]

    M^{2/3} &= \dfrac{2k\hspace{0.05em}t}{3} + A \hspace{2em}[1]\tag*{\textbf{{A1A1}}}\\[12pt]

    M &= \left(\dfrac{2k\hspace{0.05em}t}{3}+A\right)^{3/2} \hspace{2em}[2]\\[-10pt]

\end{aligned}

$$

- Solving [1] for the initial value $M(0)=M_0$, we have

$$

\begin{aligned}

    A &= M_0^{2/3} \hspace{2em}[3]\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}

\end{aligned}

$$

- Substituting [3] in [2], we obtain

$$

\begin{aligned}

    &\hspace{-6.65mm}M(t) = \left(\dfrac{2k\hspace{0.05em}t}{3}+M_0^{2/3}\right)^{3/2} \hspace{2em} \text{... as required}
