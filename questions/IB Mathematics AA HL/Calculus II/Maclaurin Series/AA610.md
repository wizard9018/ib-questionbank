# AA610

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let the Maclaurin series for $\cot x$ be

$$
\cot x = \dfrac{a_1}{x}+a_2x+a_3x^3+\cdots
$$
where $a_1$, $a_2$ and
$a_3$ are non zero constants.

1.  Find the series for $\csc^2 x$, in terms of $a_1$, $a_2$ and $a_3$,
    up to and including the $x^2$ term
    1.  by differentiating the above series for $\cot x$;

    2.  by using the relationship $\csc^2 x= 1+\cot^2 x$.[4 marks]

2.  Hence, by comparing your two series, determine the values of $a_1$,
    $a_2$ and $a_3$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Differentiating the Maclaurin series for $\cot x$, we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x} \cot x  &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\left(\dfrac{a_1}{x}+a_2x+a_3x^3+\cdots\right)\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\
        -\csc^2 x &= -\dfrac{a_1}{x^2}+a_2+3\hspace{0.15em}a_3x^2+\cdots\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{8mm}\\[3pt]
        &\hspace{-6mm}\csc^2 x = \dfrac{a_1}{x^2}-a_2-3\hspace{0.15em}a_3x^2+\cdots\rule[0mm]{0pt}{5mm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Considering only products of terms that will result in powers
        less than or equal to $2$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \csc^2 x &= 1+\left(\dfrac{a_1}{x}+a_2x+a_3x^3+\cdots\right)^2\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{3mm}\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{0.3em}= 1+\dfrac{a_1^2}{x^2}+2a_1a_2+(a_2^2+2a_1a_3)x^2+\cdots
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Comparing the two series we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a_1^2 &= a_1 \\
    1+2a_1a_2 &= -a_2 \\
    a_2^2+2a_1a_3 &= -3a_3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    From (1) it is clear that
    $a_1=1$, substituting $a_1=1$ into (2) gives us $a_2=-1/3$ and
    substituting $a_1$ and $a_2$ into (3) gives us $a_3=-1/45$.

    Therefore the values of the constants are
    $$

    a_1 = 1, \, a_2=-\dfrac{1}{3}, \, \text{and} \, a_3 = -\dfrac{1}{45}

    $$
