# AA577

**Maximum mark: 15**

A particle $P$ moves along a straight line so that its velocity, $v$
ms$^{-1}$, after $t$ seconds, is given by $v = \sin 3 t - 2 \cos t -2$,
for $0 \leq t \leq 6$. The initial displacement of $P$ from a fixed
point $O$ is $5$ metres.

1.  Find the displacement of $P$ from $O$ after $6$ seconds.[5 marks]

The following sketch shows the graph of $v$.

![ad441389a1006d49da4e5d171d3673ad636a23b9.svg](images/ad441389a1006d49da4e5d171d3673ad636a23b9.svg)

2.  Find when the particle is first at rest.[2 marks]

3.  Write down the number of times the particle changes
    direction.[2 marks]

4.  Find the acceleration of $P$ after $2$ seconds.[2 marks]

5.  Find the maximum speed of $P$.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  the displacement form $O$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4mm}
    d       & = 5 + \int_0^6 (\sin 3 t - 2 \cos t -2)\,\textrm d t\\[6pt]
    & = 5 - 11.3\qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = -6.30\,\mbox m
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The particle is at rest when $v(t) = 0$ hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{3cm} t = 2.32\,\mbox s
    \qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}\hspace{2.4cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The particle changes direction when $v(t) = 0$, hence
      $\mbox{two times}$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a(t)    & = v'(t)\\[6pt]
    & = 3\cos 3 t + 2 \sin t \\[6pt]
    a(2)    & = 3 \cos 3(2) + 2 \sin 2\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = 4.70\,\mbox{ms}^{-2}\qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  The maximum speed occurs when $v'(t) = 0$, hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    3\cos 3 t + 2 \sin t    & = 0\\[6pt]
    t               & = 5.85\qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-1.25cm} |v(5.85)|     = 4.78\,\mbox{ms}^{-1}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
