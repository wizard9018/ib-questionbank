# AA942

**Maximum mark: 12**

A particle moves along the $x$-axis with a velocity, $v\,\text{ms}^{-1}\,$, at time $t$ seconds given by the function

$v(t) = 2 + 7t -4t^2$

For $0 \leq t \leq 3$. The particle is initially at the origin.

1. Find the value of $t$ when the particle reaches its maximum velocity. [3 marks]

2. Sketch a graph of $v$ against $t$ showing any points of intersection with the axes. [5 marks]

3. Find the displacement of the particle from the origin after 2 seconds. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The particle reaches its maximum velocity when $v'(t)=0$. So, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    v'(t) &= 0 \\[12pt]

    7 - 8t &= 0 \\[12pt]

    t &= \dfrac{7}{8}\\[-9pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Since $v''(t) = -8$ we conclude that $v_{max}$ is at $\hspace{0.25em}t = \dfrac{7}{8}$

3. We get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \text{displacement} &= \int_0^{2}v(t) \, \mathrm{d}t \\[8pt]

     &=\int_0^{2}  (2+7t-4t^2) \, \mathrm{d}t\\[8pt]

     &= 2t + \dfrac{7t^2}{2} - \dfrac{4t^3}{3} \Bigg|_{0}^{2}\\[8pt]

     &=2\left(2\right)+\dfrac{7\left(2\right)^2}{2}-\dfrac{4\left(2\right)^3}{3}\\[10pt]

     &=4+14-\dfrac{32}{3}\\[8pt]

    &\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{=\dfrac{22}{3} \hspace{0.2em}\text{m}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
