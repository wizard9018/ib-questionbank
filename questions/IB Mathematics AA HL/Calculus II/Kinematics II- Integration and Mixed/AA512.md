# AA512

**Maximum mark: 15**

A particle moves back and forth in a straight line. Its velocity
$v$ m s$^{-1}$ at time
$t$ seconds is given by

$$
v = 3\hspace{0.025em}t - \dfrac{3}{4}\hspace{0.05em}t^2, \quad 0 \leq t \leq 7.
$$

At time $t = 0$, the displacement $s$ of the particle from the starting
point is $1$ m.

1.  Find the displacement of the particle when $t = 5$. [3 marks]

2.  Sketch a displacement/time graph for the particle,
    $0 \leq t \leq 7$, showing clearly where the curve meets the axes
    and the coordinates of the points where the displacement takes the
    greatest and least values. [5 marks]

For $t > 7$, the displacement of the particle is given by

$$
s = \alpha + \beta \cos\bigg(\dfrac{2\pi t}{7}\bigg)
$$
such that $s$
is continuous for all $t \geq 0$.

3.  Given that $s = 9$ when $t = 10.5$, find the values of
    $\alpha$ and $\beta$. [3 marks]

4.  Find the times $t_1$ and $t_2$ $(0 < t_1 < t_2 < 12)$ when
    the particle returns to its starting point. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The displacement of the particle is given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    s & =  \int 3\hspace{0.05em}t - \dfrac{3}{4}\hspace{0.05em}t^2\, \mathrm{d}t \\[3pt]
    & =  \dfrac{3}{2}\hspace{0.05em}t^2 - \dfrac{1}{4}\hspace{0.05em}t^3 + c.
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Substituting $t = 0$ and $s = 1$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    s & =  \dfrac{3}{2}\hspace{0.05em}t^2 - \dfrac{1}{4}\hspace{0.05em}t^3 + c \\[3pt]
    1 & =  \dfrac{3}{2}(0)^2 - \dfrac{1}{4}(0)^3 + c \\[10pt]
    c & =  1.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, substituting $t = 5$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    s & =  \dfrac{3}{2}\cdot5^2 - \dfrac{1}{4}\cdot5^3 + 1 \\[10pt]
    & =  7.25 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m} \quad \quad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Here is a sketch of displacement/time graph for the particle:

    ![c48bb76b8c3fc2aaf78a8fef30dce4bdfe8db748.svg](images/c48bb76b8c3fc2aaf78a8fef30dce4bdfe8db748.svg)

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -11.25  & = \alpha + \beta \cos 2\pi  \quad \quad \quad [\text{for $t = 7$}] \\[6pt]
    9       & = \alpha + \beta \cos 3\pi \quad \quad \quad [\text{for $t = 10.5$}] \\[6pt]
    &\text{or} \\[6pt]
    -11.25  & = \alpha + \beta \\[6pt]
    9       & = \alpha - \beta
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Solving the system of equations given above for $\alpha$ and
    $\beta$, we get

    $$
    \alpha = -1.125 \ \  \mbox{and} \ \ \beta = -10.125
    $$

4.  Solving the equation $s(t) = 1$ for $0 < t < 7$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    1 + \dfrac{3}{2}\hspace{0.05em}t^2 - \dfrac{1}{4}\hspace{0.05em}t^3 & = 1 \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{3}{2}\hspace{0.05em}t^2 - \dfrac{1}{4}\hspace{0.05em}t^3 & = 0 \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{3}{2} - \dfrac{1}{4}\hspace{0.05em}t & = 0 \quad \quad \quad [\text{since $t > 0$}] \\[8pt]
    t & = 6
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Solving the equation $s(t) = 1$ for
    $7 < t < 12$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -1.125 - 10.125 \cos\bigg(\dfrac{2\pi t}{7}\bigg)  & =  1 \\[5pt]
    t & \approx  8.99 \quad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence  $t_1 = 6\hspace{0.25em}\mbox s$
      and
     $t_2 \approx 8.99\hspace{0.25em}\mbox s$
