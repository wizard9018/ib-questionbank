# AA534

**Maximum mark: 5**

The acceleration,
$a$ m s$^{-2}$, of a
particle at time $t$ seconds is given by

$$
a = \dfrac{3}{t} + 5\cos 2t, \hspace{0.4em}\text{for}\hspace{0.5em}t\geq1.
$$

The particle is at rest when $t=1$.

Find the velocity of the particle when $t=4$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

The velocity is given by the integral
$$
\begin{aligned}
v(t)    & = \int \left(\dfrac{3}{t} + 5\cos 2t\right)\textrm d t\\[10pt]
& = 3 \ln t + \dfrac 5 2 \sin 2 t + C\\[10pt]
0    & = 3 \ln 1 + \dfrac 5 2 \sin 2 (1) + C \qquad [\mbox{as $v(1)=0$ was given}]\\[10pt]
0    & = 3(0) + \dfrac 5 2 \sin (2) + C\\[10pt]
C    & = -\dfrac 5 2 \sin (2)\\[10pt]
v(t)    & = 3 \ln t + \dfrac 5 2 \sin 2 t - \dfrac 5 2 \sin (2)\\[10pt]
v(4)    & = 3 \ln 4 + \dfrac 5 2 \sin 2 (4) - \dfrac 5 2 \sin (2)\\[14pt]
& \hspace{.275em}  = 4.36\,\mbox{ms}^{-1}\qquad\quad[\mbox{by using G.D.C.}]
\end{aligned}
$$
