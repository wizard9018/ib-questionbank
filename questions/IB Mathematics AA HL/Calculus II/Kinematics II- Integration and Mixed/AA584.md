# AA584

**Maximum mark: 13**

A particle moves in a straight line and its velocity, $v$ ms$^{-1}$, at
time $t$ seconds, is given by $v(t) = (t^2 - 2)^2$, for
$0 \leq t \leq 2$.

1.  Find the initial velocity of the particle.[2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $t$ for which the particle is at rest. [2 marks]

3.  Find the total distance travelled by the particle in the first $2$
    seconds.[3 marks]

4.  Show that the acceleration of the particle is given by
    $a(t) = 4 t^3 - 8t$. [3 marks]

5.  Find the values of $t$ for which the velocity is positive and the
    acceleration is negative. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    v(0)        & = (0^2 - 2)^2\hspace{2.4cm}\\[5pt]
    & \hspace{.275em} = 4 \,\mbox{ms}^{-1}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The particle is at rest when $v(t) = 0$, hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (t^2 - 2)^2 & = 0\\[6pt]
    &  \hspace{-1mm} t = \sqrt 2 \,\mbox s \hspace{0.2cm}$or$\hspace{0.2cm} 1.41 \,\mbox s
    \qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The distance is given by the integral
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1cm}
    d   & = \int_0^2|(t^2 - 2)^2|\,\textrm d t\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em} = 3.73   \,\mbox m\qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a(t)        & = v'(t)\\[5pt]
    & = 2(t^2 - 2)(2t)\hspace{1.9cm}\\[5pt]
    &\hspace{.275em}= 4t^3 - 8t
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Using G.D.C. we find   $0 < t < 1.41$
