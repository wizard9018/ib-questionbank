# AA823

**Maximum mark: 12**

A particle moves along the $x$-axis with a velocity, $v$ ms$^{-1}$, at
time $t$ seconds given by the function

$v(t)=3+8t-3t^2 \hspace{2em}$

For $0\leq t \leq 4$. The particle is initially at the origin.

1.  Find the value of $t$ when the particle reaches its maximum
    velocity.[3 marks]

2.  Sketch a graph of $v$ against $t$ showing any points of intersection
    with the axes.[5 marks]

3.  Find the distance of the particle from the origin after 3 seconds.
    [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The particle reaches its maximum velocity when $v'(t) = 0$. So, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    v'(t) &= 0 \\[10pt]
    8-6t &= 0 \\[10pt]
    t &= \dfrac{4}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Since $v''(t)=-6$ we conclude
    that $v_{max}$ is at
    $t=\dfrac{4}{3}$

3.  We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{dist} &= \int_0^{3}v(t) \, \mathrm{d}t \\[10pt]
    &=\int_0^{3}  (3+8t-3t^2) \, \mathrm{d}t\\[10pt]
    &=3 t + 4 t^2 - t^3 \Big|_{0}^{3}\\[10pt]
    &=3\left(3\right)+4\left(3\right)^2-\left(3\right)^3\\[10pt]
    &=9+36-27\\[10pt]
    &=18 \hspace{0.2em}\text{m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
