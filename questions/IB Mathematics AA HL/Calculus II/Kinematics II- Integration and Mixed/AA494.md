# AA494

**Maximum mark: 7**

A particle moves in a straight line such that its velocity,
$v$ m s$^{-1}$, at time
$t$ seconds, is given by
$$
v(t) = \left\{\begin{array}{clc}
6t - t^2, &\text{for}& 0 \leq t \leq 5, \\[6pt]
\dfrac{1}{2}(15 - t), &\text{for}& t > 5.
\end{array}\right.
$$

1.  Find the value of $t$, for $t>0$, when the particle is
    instantaneously at rest. [2 marks]

The particle returns to its initial position at $t = T$.

2.  Find the value of $T$. Give your answer correct to three
    significant figures. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The particle is instantaneously at rest when\
    $v(t) = 0$ $\Longrightarrow$ $\dfrac{1}{2}(15 - t) = 0$
    $\Longrightarrow$
    $\hspace{.275em}t = 15\hspace{0.25em}\mbox s$

2.  Let $d$ be the distance travelled before coming to rest. We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d   & =  \int_0^5 (6t - t^2)\,\textrm dt + \dfrac{1}{2}\int_5^{15}(15-t)\,\textrm dt \\[8pt]
    & \approx  58.333\hspace{0.25em}\text{m} \quad \quad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Solving the following equation for $T$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{1}{2}\int_{15}^{T}|(15 - t)|\,\textrm dt     & =  58.333\hspace{6cm} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{2.2cm}T     \approx  30.3\hspace{0.25em}\text{s}  \qquad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
