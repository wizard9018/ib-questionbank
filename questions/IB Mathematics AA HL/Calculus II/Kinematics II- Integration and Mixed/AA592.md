# AA592

**Maximum mark: 16**

A particle P moves along a straight line so that its velocity, $v$,
ms$^{-1}$, after $t$ seconds, is given by
$v = 2 \sin t - \cos 5 t + 0.1$, for $0 \leq t \leq 4$. The initial
displacement of P from a fixed point O is $2$ metres.

![9ef1e30cd19bb1c81d67684bb5cdd6acbc3c0de8.svg](images/9ef1e30cd19bb1c81d67684bb5cdd6acbc3c0de8.svg)

1.  Find the displacement of P from O after $4$ seconds. [5 marks]

2.  Find the second time for $t$, when the particle is at rest.
    [2 marks]

3.  Write down the number of times P changes direction. [2 marks]

4.  Write down the number of times P is neither accelerating or
    decelerating. [2 marks]

5.  Find the maximum distance of P from O during the time
    $0\leq t \leq 4$ and justify your answer. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The displacement of P from O is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5cm}
    s   & = 2 + \int_0^4 v(t)\,\textrm d t\\[6pt]
    & = 2 + \int_0^4(2 \sin t - \cos 5 t + 0.1)\,\textrm dt\\[6pt]
    & =     2 + 3.52\qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em}= 5.52\,\mbox{m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The particle at rest when $v(t) = 0$, hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2 \sin t - \cos 5 t + 0.1   & = 0\\[5pt]
    &\hspace{-1mm}t = 3.38\,\mbox{s}
    \qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \hspace{1.8cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The particle changes direction, when $v(t)$ changes its sign
    (crosses the $t$-axis).

    From the diagram we see that the particle changes direction
      $\mbox{two times}$

4.  The particle is neither accelerating / decelerating when
    $a(t) = v'(t)= 0$.

    From the diagram we see that it happens
      $6\mbox{ times}$

5.  The displacement is given by the integral
    $\displaystyle \int_0^t v(t)\,\textrm dt$.\
    From the diagram, we see that this integral is at a maxiumum when
    $v(t)$\
    crosses the $t$-axis for the second time, at $t = 3.38$ s (part
    (b)). We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d_{\max}        & = 2 + \int_0^{3.38} v(t)\,\textrm d t\\[6pt]
    & = 2 + \int_0^{3.38}(2 \sin t - \cos 5 t + 0.1)\,\textrm dt\\[6pt]
    & =  2 + 4.47\qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{.275em}= 6.47\,\mbox{m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
