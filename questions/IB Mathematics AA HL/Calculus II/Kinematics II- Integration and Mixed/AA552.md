# AA552

**Maximum mark: 6**

A particle travels with velocity $v$ ms$^{-1}$ for $9$ seconds. This is
shown in the graph below.

![cf002adef4ecbdd36c9bfaf67f9b2bb8d9bd2905.svg](images/cf002adef4ecbdd36c9bfaf67f9b2bb8d9bd2905.svg)

1.  Write down the car's velocity at $t = 4$.

2.  Find the car's acceleration at $t = 2$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the total distance travelled. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $v(4) = 6 \mbox{ m/s}$

2.  The car's acceleration at $t=2$ is equal to the slope, $m$, of
    $v(t)$ at $t=2$, hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m   & = \dfrac 6 3\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = 2\,\mbox{m/s}^2\hspace{1cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The total distance, $d$, is equal to the area under the curve
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2cm}
    d   & = \dfrac 1 2 (6)(3) + 2(6) + \dfrac 1 2 (4)(6)\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = 33\,\mbox{m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
