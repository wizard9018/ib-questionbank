# AA660

**Maximum mark: 8**

**In this question distance is in centimetres and time is in seconds.**

Particle X is moving along a straight line such that its displacement
from a point A, after $t$ seconds, is given by

$s_{\text{X}} = 24-t-5t^3e^{-0.6t}$  , $\hspace{3em} 0 \leq t \leq 30$.

This is shown in the following diagram.

![57dc605df0f876326f48c9f31184664f7f576253.svg](images/57dc605df0f876326f48c9f31184664f7f576253.svg)

1.  Find the value of $t$ when particle X first changes direction.
    [2 marks]

2.  Find the total distance travelled by particle X in the first $3.5$
    seconds. [2 marks]

Another particle, Y, moves along the same line, starting at the same
time as particle X. The velocity of
particle Y is given by $v_{\text{Y}} = 5 - t$,
 $0 \leq t \leq 30$.

3.
    1.  Given that particle X and Y start at the same
        point, find the displacement
        function $s_{\text{Y}}$ for particle Y.

    2.  Find the other value of $t$ when particles X and Y
        meet.[4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Solving the equation $s'_{\text{X}} = 0$ for $t$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\big[24-t-5t^3e^{-0.6t}\big] &= 0 \\[10pt]
    \hspace{4.6em} -1 - 15t^2e^{-0.6t} + 3t^3e^{-0.6t} &= 0 \hspace{3em} [\text{by derivative rules}] \\[10pt]
    t &\approx 5.28,\hspace{0.25em} 14.5 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we find
     $t\approx5.28 \text{ seconds}$

2.  Evaluating $s_{\text{X}}$ for $t=0$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.4em} s_{\text{X}}(0) &= 24-0-5(0)^3e^{-0.6(0)} \\[8pt]
    &= 24
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Evaluating $s_{\text{X}}$ for $t=3.5$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.8em} s_{\text{X}}(3.5) &= 24-3.5-5(3.5)^3e^{-0.6(3.5)} \\[8pt]
    &\approx -5.75
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{total distance} &= 24+5.75 \hspace{9.8em} \\[8pt]
    &\approx 29.8 \text{ cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.  Integrating $v_{\text{Y}}$ with respect to $t$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{8.6em} s_{\text{Y}} &= \int v_{\text{Y}}\,\mathrm{d}t \\[5pt]
        &= \int (5-t)\,\mathrm{d}t \\[5pt]
        &= 5t - \dfrac{1}{2}t^2 + C \hspace{3em} [\text{for some $C \in \mathbb{R}$}] \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Solving the equation $s_{\text{X}}(0) = s_{\text{Y}}(0)$ for
        $C$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.2em} 24 &= 5(0) - \dfrac{1}{2}(0)^2 + C \\[8pt]
        24 &= C
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence we obtain

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        s_{\text{Y}} = 5t - \dfrac{1}{2}t^2 + 24 \hspace{1.5em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Solving the equation $s_{\text{X}} = s_{\text{Y}}$ for $t > 0$,
        we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \cancel{24} -t-5t^3e^{-0.6t} &= 5t - \dfrac{1}{2}t^2 + \cancel{24} \hspace{9.1em} \\[6pt]
        \dfrac{1}{2}t^2 - 6t - 5t^3e^{-0.6t} &= 0 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \hspace{9.9em} t \approx 12.8 \text{ seconds} \hspace{3.5em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        $$
