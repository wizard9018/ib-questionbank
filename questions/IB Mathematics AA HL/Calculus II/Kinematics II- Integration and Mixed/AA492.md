# AA492

**Maximum mark: 5**

A particle moves in a straight line such that at time $t$ seconds
($t \geq 0$), its velocity is given by $v = 18t^3e^{-3t^2}$. Find the
exact distance travelled by the particle in the first two seconds.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

The distance $d$ travelled by the particle in the first two seconds is
given by
$$
\begin{aligned}
d & =  \int_0^2 v(t)\,\textrm dt \\[8pt]
& =  18\int_0^2 \left[t^3e^{-3t^2}\right]\textrm dt \quad \quad \quad \Big[\text{let $u = t^2$ and $\textrm dv/\textrm dt = te^{-3t^2}$}\Big] \\[8pt]
& =  18 \left[-\dfrac{1}{6}\left(t^2e^{-3t^2}\right)\Big|_0^2  + \dfrac{1}{3}\int_0^2 te^{-3t^2}\textrm dt \right] \\[8pt]
& =  18\left[-\dfrac{2}{3}e^{-12}  - \dfrac{1}{18}\left(e^{-3t^2}\right)\Big|_0^2 \right] \\[8pt]
& =  18\left[-\dfrac{2}{3}e^{-12}  - \dfrac{1}{18}\left(e^{-12} - 1\right) \right] \\[10pt]
& =  -12e^{-12}  - \left(e^{-12} - 1\right) \\[12pt]
&\hspace{.275em}  =  1 -13e^{-12}
\end{aligned}
$$
