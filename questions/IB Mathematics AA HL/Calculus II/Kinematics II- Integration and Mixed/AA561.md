# AA561

**Maximum mark: 7**

A particle moves along a straight line such that its velocity, $v$,
ms$^{-1}$, is given by $v(t) = 5 t e^{-1.2t}$, for $t \geq 0$.

1.  On the grid below, sketch the graph of $v$, for $0 \leq t \leq 3$.
    [3 marks]

    ![9e220f7900c7ae0744aee17bcbc0834aa0ea52fb.svg](images/9e220f7900c7ae0744aee17bcbc0834aa0ea52fb.svg)

2.  Find the distance travelled by the particle in the first $3$
    seconds.[2 marks]

3.  Find the maximum velocity of the particle in the first $3$ seconds.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have the following sketch.

    ![7bc45070b48f8dedd825164c932e2bb4061749da.svg](images/7bc45070b48f8dedd825164c932e2bb4061749da.svg)

2.  The distance travelled by the particle is given by the integral
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d       & = \int_0^3 |5 t e^{-1.2t}|\,\textrm d t\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = 3.04\,\mbox{m}\qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The maximum velocity occurs when $t = 0.833$, hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    & \hspace{-1.2cm}v(0.833) = 1.53\,\mbox{ms}^{-1}\qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
