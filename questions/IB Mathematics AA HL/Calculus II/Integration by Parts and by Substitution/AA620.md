# AA620

**Maximum mark: 13**

1.  Use L'Hôpital's rule to find
    $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to \infty} x^3 e^{-x}$.[4 marks]

2.  Show that the proper integral
    $\displaystyle \int_0^\infty x^3e^{-x}\, \mathrm{d}x$ converges, and
    state its value.[9 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using L'Hôpital's rule repeatedly, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \lim_{x\to \infty} x^3 e^{-x}&= \lim_{x\to \infty} \dfrac{x^3}{e^x}\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{5mm}\\
    &= \lim_{x\to \infty} \dfrac{3x^2}{e^x} \rule[-3mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &= \lim_{x\to \infty} \dfrac{6x}{e^x}\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &= \lim_{x\to \infty}\dfrac{6}{e^x}\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{9mm}\\
    &=0\rule[0mm]{0pt}{6mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Integrating from $0$ to $R$, by parts, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_0^R x^3 e^{-x} \, \mathrm{d}x &= -e^{-x}x^3\bigg\lvert_0^R +3\int_0^R e^{-x}x^2 \, \mathrm{d}x\rule[-6mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &=-e^{-R}R^3+3\left[-e^{-x}x^2 \bigg\lvert_0^R + 2\int_0^R xe^{-x}\, \mathrm{d}x\right]\rule[-6mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &= -e^{-R}R^3-3e^{-R}R^2+6\left[-xe^{-x}\bigg\lvert_0^R+\int_0^R e^{-x}\,\mathrm{d}x\right]\rule[-6mm]{0pt}{12mm}\\
    &= -e^{-R}R^3-3e^{-R}R^2-6e^{-R}R-6e^{-R}+6\rule[-3mm]{0pt}{9mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Therefore, by the definition of a proper integral
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_0^\infty x^3e^{-x}\,\mathrm{d}x &= \lim_{R\to\infty} \int_0^R x^3e^{-x}\,\mathrm{d}x \rule[-4mm]{0pt}{7mm}\\
    &= \lim_{R\to \infty} \left[ -e^{-R}R^3-3e^{-R}R^2-6e^{-R}R-6e^{-R}+6 \right]\quad\quad(1)\\
    &=6\rule[0mm]{0pt}{6mm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Note each term multiplied by $e^{-R}$ in (1) vanishes by the
    intermediate steps in the solution of (a).
