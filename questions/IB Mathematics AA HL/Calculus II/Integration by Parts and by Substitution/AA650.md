# AA650

**Maximum mark: 14**

Let $y = (x^2+x)^{\frac{3}{2}}$, for $x \geq 0$.

1.  Find $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}$. [3 marks]

2.  Hence find
    $\displaystyle \int \textstyle (2x+1)\sqrt{x^2+x}\,\mathrm{d}x$.
    [3 marks]

Consider the functions $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{4}\sqrt{x^2+x}$ and
$g(x) = 5 - \dfrac{x}{2}\sqrt{x^2+x}$, for $x \geq 0$.

The graphs of $f$ and $g$ are shown in the following diagram.

![b13d6c069881bca9dffc49b90d0991e7e07b6530.svg](images/b13d6c069881bca9dffc49b90d0991e7e07b6530.svg)

The shaded region $S$ is enclosed by the graph of
$f$ , the graph of $g$, the
$y$-axis and the line $x = 2$.

3.  Write down an expression for the area of $S$. [1 mark]

4.  Hence find the exact area of $S$. [7 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the chain rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.1em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\Big[(x^2+x)^{\frac{3}{2}}\hspace{-0.05em}\Big] \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{3}{2}(x^2+x)^{\frac{1}{2}}(2x+1) \\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{3}{2}(2x+1)\textstyle \sqrt{x^2+x}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.8em} \int \textstyle (2x+1)\sqrt{x^2+x}\,\mathrm{d}x &= \int \dfrac{2}{3}\bigg[\dfrac{3}{2}\textstyle (2x+1)\sqrt{x^2+x}\bigg]\mathrm{d}x \\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{2}{3} \int \bigg[\dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\Big[(x^2+x)^{\frac{3}{2}}\hspace{-0.05em}\Big]\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg]\mathrm{d}x \hspace{2.2em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[12pt]
    &= \dfrac{2}{3}(x^2+x)^{\frac{3}{2}} + C
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    $\int_0^2 \big[g(x) - f(x)\big]\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{d}x$

4.  Hence, evaluating the definite integral, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.9em} \text{Area of $S$} &= \int_0^2 \bigg[\hspace{-0.1em}\Big[5 - \textstyle\dfrac{x}{2}\sqrt{x^2+x}\Big]\hspace{-0.15em} - \hspace{-0.1em}\Big[\textstyle\dfrac{1}{4}\sqrt{x^2+x}\Big]\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg] \mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]
    &= \int_0^2 \bigg[5 - \textstyle\dfrac{x}{2}\sqrt{x^2+x} - \textstyle\dfrac{1}{4}\sqrt{x^2+x}\bigg] \mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]
    &= \int_0^2 \bigg[5 - \dfrac{1}{4}(2x+1)\textstyle\sqrt{x^2+x}\bigg]\mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]
    &= 5\int_0^2 1 \,\mathrm{d}x  - \dfrac{1}{4}\int_0^2(2x+1)\textstyle\sqrt{x^2+x} \,\mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]
    &= 5\Big[x\Big]\Big|_0^2 - \dfrac{1}{4}\bigg[\dfrac{2}{3}(x^2+x)^{\frac{3}{2}}\bigg]\bigg|_0^2 \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (b)}] \\[8pt]
    &= 5\Big[2-0\Big]\hspace{-0.15em} - \dfrac{1}{4}\bigg[\dfrac{2}{3}(2^2+2)^{\frac{3}{2}}-0\bigg] \\[8pt]
    &= 10 - \dfrac{1}{4}\bigg[\dfrac{2}{3}(6\textstyle\sqrt{6})\bigg] \\[14pt]
    &= 10 - \textstyle\sqrt{6}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
