# AA564

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $f(x) = \dfrac {\ln x} x$

1.  Find $\displaystyle \int f(x)\,\textrm d x$. [4 marks]

2.  Find $\displaystyle\int_1^e f(x) \,\textrm d x$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $u = \ln x$ then $x\hspace{0.1em}\textrm d u = \textrm d x$ and
    we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int f(x)\,\textrm d x  & = \int \dfrac {\ln x} x \,\textrm d x\\[6pt]
    & = \int u\,\textrm d u\\[6pt]
    & = \dfrac {u^2}2 + C\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}  = \dfrac {(\ln x)^2} 2 + C
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.4cm}
    \int_1^e \dfrac {\ln x} x\,\textrm d x  & = \left[\dfrac {(\ln x)^2} 2\right]_1^e\\[6pt]
    & =  \left[\dfrac {(\ln e)^2} 2\right]
    -  \left[\dfrac {(\ln 1)^2} 2\right]\\[6pt]
    & = \dfrac  1 2  - 0\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em} = \dfrac 1 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
