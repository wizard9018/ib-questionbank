# AA919

**Maximum mark: 5**

Using the substitution $u=\sqrt{x}-1$, find the value of $\displaystyle\int_1^4 \dfrac{2\sqrt{\sqrt{x}-1}}{\sqrt{x}}\,\,\mathrm{d}x$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using integration by substitution, let $u = \sqrt{x}-1$. Therefore

$$

\begin{aligned}

u &= x^{1/2} - 1 \\[10pt]

\dfrac{\mathrm{d}u}{\mathrm{d}x} &= \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}} \\[10pt]

2\hspace{0.15em}\mathrm{d}u &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{x}}

\end{aligned}

$$

We also note that when $x=1$, $u=0$ and when $x=4$, $u=1$.

Substituting $u = \sqrt{x}-1$ and $2\,\mathrm{d}u = \dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{x}}$ , and changing the limits to $u=0$ and $u=1$ we get

$$

\begin{aligned}

\displaystyle\int_{x=1}^{x=4} \dfrac{2\sqrt{\sqrt{x}-1}}{\sqrt{x}}\,\,\mathrm{d}x & = \displaystyle\int_{u=0}^{u=1} 4\sqrt{u} \,\textrm{d}u \\[10pt] %\hspace{3em} [\hspace{0.05em}\text{using substitution}\hspace{0.05em}] \\[6pt]

& = 4 \displaystyle\int_{u=0}^{u=1} u^{1/2} \,\textrm{d}u \\[10pt]

&= 4\Big[\dfrac{2}{3}\,u^{3/2}\Big]_{u=0}^{u=1} \\[10pt]

&= \dfrac{8}{3}\Big(1^{3/2} - 0^{3/2}\Big) \\[10pt]

&\hspace{-8em}\boxed{\displaystyle\int_1^4 \dfrac{2\sqrt{\sqrt{x}-1}}{\sqrt{x}}\,\,\mathrm{d}x = \dfrac{8}{3}}

\end{aligned}

$$
