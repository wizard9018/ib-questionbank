# AA498

**Maximum mark: 6**

Using integration by parts, find
$\displaystyle \int x^2 \sin x\, \textrm dx$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Integrating by parts twice, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int x^2 \sin x\, \textrm dx    & =  x^2(-\cos x) + 2 \int x\cos x\,\textrm dx \qquad  \quad
[\text{$u = x^2$, $\textrm dv = \sin x\,\textrm dx$}]\\[3pt]
& =  -x^2\cos x + 2 \left[x\sin x - \int \sin x\,\textrm dx\right] \qquad \quad
[\text{$u = x$, $\textrm dv = \cos x\,\textrm dx$}]\\[8pt]
&\hspace{.275em} =  -x^2\cos x + 2x\sin x + 2\cos x + C
\end{aligned}
$$
