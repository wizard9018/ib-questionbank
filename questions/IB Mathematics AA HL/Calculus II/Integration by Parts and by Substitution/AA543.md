# AA543

**Maximum mark: 7**

1.  Find $\displaystyle \int 4xe^{x^2-9}\,\mathrm{d}x$. [4 marks]

2.  Find $f(x)$, given that $f'(x) = 4xe^{x^2-9}$ and $f(3) = 7$.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using integration by substitution with $u = x^2 - 9$ and
    $\dfrac {\textrm d u} {2x} = \textrm d x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int 4x e^{x^2 - 9}\,\mathrm{d}x
    & = \int 4 \cancel{x} e^u \dfrac {\textrm d u} {2 \cancel{x}}\hspace{1cm}\\[6pt]
    & = \int 2 e^u \textrm d u\\[6pt]
    & = 2 e^u + C\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = 2 e^{x^2 - 9} + C
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We know that $\displaystyle f(x) = \int f'(x) \textrm \,d x$ and
    from part (a) $f(x)  = 2 e^{x^2 - 9} + C$. Let's determine $C$. We
    have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.3cm}
    7   & = 2 e^{(3)^2 - 9} + C\\[6pt]
    7   & = 2 + C\\[6pt]
    C   & = 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We found
      $f(x) = 2 e^{x^2 - 9} +5$
