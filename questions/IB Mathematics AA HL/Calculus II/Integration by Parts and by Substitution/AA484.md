# AA484

**Maximum mark: 5**

Find $\displaystyle \int \arccos x\, \textrm dx$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Integrating by parts with $u = \arccos x$ and $\textrm dv =\textrm  dx$,
we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int \arccos x\,\textrm  dx     & =  x\arccos x + \int \dfrac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\,\textrm dx \\[6pt]
& =  x\arccos x - \int \dfrac{1}{2}u^{-\frac{1}{2}}\,\textrm du  \qquad                              [\text{with $u = 1 - x^2$}]  \\[6pt]
& =  x\arccos x - u^{\frac{1}{2}} + C \\[8pt]
&\hspace{.275em} =  x\arccos x - \sqrt{1-x^2} + C
\end{aligned}
$$
