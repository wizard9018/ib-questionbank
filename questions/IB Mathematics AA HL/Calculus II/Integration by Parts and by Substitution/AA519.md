# AA519

**Maximum mark: 7**

Consider the functions $f, g$, defined for $x \in \mathbb{R}$, given by
$f(x) = e^{2x}\sin x$ and $g(x) = e^{2x}\cos x$.

1.  Find
    1.  $f'(x)$;

    2.  $g'(x)$.[3 marks]

2.  Hence, or otherwise, find
    $\displaystyle \int_0^\pi e^{2x}\cos x\,\mathrm{d}x$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using the product rule, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        f'(x) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\big[e^{2x}\sin x\big] \\[8pt]
        &= 2e^{2x}\sin x + e^{2x}\cos x
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  and
         $$
         \begin{aligned}
         g'(x) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\big[e^{2x}\cos x\big] \\[8pt]
         &= 2e^{2x}\cos x - e^{2x}\sin x
         \end{aligned}
         $$

2.  From part (a), we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f'(x) + 2g'(x) &= 5e^{2x}\cos x \\[6pt]
    e^{2x}\cos x &= \dfrac{1}{5}\big[f'(x) + 2g'(x)\big] \hspace{3em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, integrating both sides of the equation ($\ast$) with respect
    to $x$ from $0$ to $\pi$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_0^\pi e^{2x}\cos x\,\mathrm{d}x &= \int_0^\pi \dfrac{1}{5}\big[f'(x) + 2g'(x)\big]\,\mathrm{d}x \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{5}\big[f(\pi) + 2g(\pi)\big] - \dfrac{1}{5}\big[f(0) + 2g(0)\big] \hspace{3em} [\text{by FTC}]\\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{5}\big[0 + 2(-e^{2\pi})\big] - \dfrac{1}{5}\big[0 + 2(1)\big] \\[9pt]
    &= -\dfrac{2}{5}\big[e^{2\pi}+1\big]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
