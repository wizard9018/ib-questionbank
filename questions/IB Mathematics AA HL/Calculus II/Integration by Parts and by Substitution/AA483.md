# AA483

**Maximum mark: 5**

Using integration by parts find
$\displaystyle \int x^2 \cos x\, \textrm dx$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Integrating by parts twice, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int x^2 \cos x\,\textrm  dx    & =  x^2\sin x - 2 \int x\sin x\,\textrm dx \quad \quad \quad
[\text{with $u = x^2$, $\textrm dv = \cos x\,\textrm dx$}]\\[6pt]
& =  x^2\sin x - 2 \left[x(-\cos x) + \int \cos x\,\textrm dx\right]
\quad   [\text{with $u = x$, $\textrm dv = \sin x\,\textrm dx$}]\\[8pt]
& \hspace{.275em}= x^2\sin x + 2x\cos x - 2\sin x + C
\end{aligned}
$$
