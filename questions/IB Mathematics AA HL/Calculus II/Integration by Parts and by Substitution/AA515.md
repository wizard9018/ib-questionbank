# AA515

**Maximum mark: 18**

The following diagram shows the graph of
$y = x\hspace{0.05em}(\ln x)^2$,
 $x > 0$.

![878824d0096f82bb6a1a431149415223a532a0b9.svg](images/878824d0096f82bb6a1a431149415223a532a0b9.svg)

1.  Given that the curve passes through the point $(p,0)$, state the
    value of $p$. [1 mark]

The region $R$ is enclosed by the curve ($p \leq x \leq e$), the
$x$-axis and the line $x = e$.

2.  Integrate by parts twice to find the area of the region
    $R$. [5 marks]

Let $I_n = \displaystyle \int_1^e x^2(\ln x)^n\,\mathrm{d}x$,
 $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

3.
    1.  Find the value of $I_0$.

    2.  Show that $I_n = \dfrac{1}{3}\big(e^3 - nI_{n-1}\big)$.
    3.  Hence find the values of $I_1, I_2$ and $I_3$. [7 marks]

The region $R$ is rotated through $2\pi$ radians about the $x$-axis.

4.  Find the volume of the solid formed. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $\hspace{.275em}p = 1$

2.  Integrating by parts twice, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Area}     & =  \int_1^e x\hspace{0.05em}(\ln x)^2\,\mathrm{d}x \quad \quad \quad \big[\text{let $u = (\ln x)^2,$ $\mathrm{d}v = x\,\mathrm{d}x$}\big] \\[6pt]
    & = \big[uv\big]\Big|_1^e - \int_1^e v\,\mathrm{d}u \quad \quad \quad \big[\text{$\Rightarrow v = \dfrac{x^2}{2},$ $\mathrm{d}u = \dfrac{2\ln x}{x}\,\mathrm{d}x$}\big] \\[6pt]
    & = \dfrac{1}{2}\big[x^2(\ln x)^2\big]\Big|_1^e - \int_1^e x \ln x \,\mathrm{d}x \quad \quad \quad \big[\text{let $u = \ln x,$ $\mathrm{d}v = x\,\mathrm{d}x$}\big] \\[6pt]
    & = \dfrac{e^2}{2} - \bigg(\dfrac{1}{2}\big[x^2 \ln x\big]\Big|_1^e - \dfrac{1}{2}\int_1^e x\,\mathrm{d}x\bigg) \quad \quad \quad \big[\text{$\Rightarrow v = \dfrac{x^2}{2},$ $\mathrm{d}u = \dfrac{1}{x}\,\mathrm{d}x$}\big] \\[6pt]
    & = \cancel{\dfrac{e^2}{2}} - \bigg(\cancel{\dfrac{e^2}{2}} - \dfrac{1}{4}\big[x^2\big]\Big|_1^e\bigg) \\[8pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}=  \dfrac{1}{4}\big[e^2 - 1\big]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        I_0         & =  \int_1^e x^2\,\mathrm{d}x \\[6pt]
        & =  \dfrac{1}{3}\big[x^3\big]\Big|_1^e \\[8pt]
        & =  \dfrac{1}{3}\big[e^3 - 1\big]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Integrating by parts, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        I_n         & =  \int_1^e x^2(\ln x)^n\,\mathrm{d}x \quad \quad \big[\text{let $u = (\ln x)^n,$ $\mathrm{d}v = x^2\,\mathrm{d}x$}\big] \\[3pt]
        & = \dfrac{1}{3}\big[x^3(\ln x)^n\big]\Big|_1^e - \dfrac{n}{3}\int_1^e x^2(\ln x)^{n-1} \,\mathrm{d}x \quad \quad \big[\text{$ v = \dfrac{x^3}{3},$ $\mathrm{d}u = \dfrac{1}{x}\hspace{0.05em}n\hspace{0.05em}(\ln x)^{n-1}\,\mathrm{d}x$}\big] \\[5pt]
        & = \dfrac{e^3}{3} - \dfrac{n}{3}\hspace{0.05em}I_{n-1} \\[8pt]
        & =  \dfrac{1}{3}\big[e^3 - n\hspace{0.05em}I_{n-1}\big]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        as required.

    3.  Using parts (c)
        (i) - (ii), we
        get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        I_1     & =  \dfrac{1}{3}\big[e^3 - I_0\big] \\[6pt]
        & =  \dfrac{e^3}{3} - \dfrac{1}{9}\big[e^3 - 1\big] \\[10pt]
        & =  \dfrac{1}{9}\big[2\hspace{0.05em}e^3 + 1\big]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        I_2     & =  \dfrac{1}{3}\big[e^3 - 2I_1\big] \\[6pt]
        & =  \dfrac{e^3}{3} - \dfrac{2}{27}\big[2e^3 + 1\big] \\[10pt]
        &  =  \dfrac{1}{27}\big[5\hspace{0.05em}e^3 - 2\big]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        I_3     & =  \dfrac{1}{3}\big[e^3 - 3I_2\big] \\[6pt]
        & =  \dfrac{e^3}{3} - \dfrac{1}{27}\big[5e^3 - 2\big] \\[10pt]
        &  =  \dfrac{1}{27}\big[4\hspace{0.05em}e^3 + 2\big]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

4.  The volume of the solid formed is given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Volume}   & = \pi  \int_1^e \big[x\hspace{0.05em}(\ln x)^2\big]^2\,\mathrm{d}x \\[6pt]
    & = \pi  \int_1^e x^2(\ln x)^4\,\mathrm{d}x \\[10pt]
    & = \pi I_4 \\[10pt]
    & =  \dfrac{\pi}{3}\big[e^3 - 4I_3\big] \quad \quad \quad [\text{by part (c) (ii)}] \\[8pt]
    & = \pi\bigg(\dfrac{e^3}{3} - \dfrac{4}{81}\big[4\hspace{0.05em}e^3 + 2\big]\bigg) \quad \quad \quad [\text{by part (c) (iii)}] \\[10pt]
    &  = \dfrac{\pi}{81}\big[11\hspace{0.05em}e^3 - 8\big]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
