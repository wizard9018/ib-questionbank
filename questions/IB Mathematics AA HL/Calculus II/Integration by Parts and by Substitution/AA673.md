# AA673

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $f(x) = \dfrac{20x}{\sqrt{(5x^2+4)^3}}$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  Find $\displaystyle \int f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x$. [4 marks]

2.  Find $\displaystyle \int_0^3 f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the integration by substitution method with $u=5x^2+4$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int \dfrac{20x}{\sqrt{(5x^2+4)^3}} \,\textrm{d}x \hspace{0.5em} & = \int \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{u^3}} \,\textrm{d}u \hspace{3em} [\text{using substitution}] \\[6pt]
    & = 2\int u^{-\frac{3}{2}} \,\textrm{d}u \\[4pt]
    &= 2\left[\dfrac{u^{-\frac{1}{2}}}{-\frac{1}{2}}\right] + C \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{-4}{\sqrt{u}} + C \\[10pt]
    & = \textstyle \dfrac{-4}{\sqrt{5x^2+4}} + C
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Evaluating the definite integral, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_0^3 \dfrac{20x}{\sqrt{(5x^2+4)^3}} \,\textrm{d}x \hspace{0.5em} & = \left[\textstyle \dfrac{-4}{\sqrt{5x^2+4}}\right]_0^3 \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[8pt]
    &= \left[\textstyle \dfrac{-4}{\sqrt{5(3)^2+4}}\right] \hspace{0.2em} - \hspace{0.2em} \left[\textstyle \dfrac{-4}{\sqrt{5(0)^2+4}}\right] \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{-4}{7} \hspace{0.2em} - \hspace{0.2em} \dfrac{-4}{2} \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{-4}{7} \hspace{0.2em} + \hspace{0.2em} \dfrac{14}{7} \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{10}{7}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
