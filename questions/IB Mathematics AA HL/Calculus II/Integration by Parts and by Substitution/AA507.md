# AA507

**Maximum mark: 6**

Show that
$\displaystyle \int_1^3 x^2\ln x\,\mathrm{d} x = 9\ln 3 - \dfrac{26}{9}$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Integrating by parts, with $u = \ln x$ and
$\mathrm{d}v = x^2\,\mathrm{d}x$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_1^3 x^2\ln x\,\mathrm{d} x
& = \dfrac{x^3\ln x}{3}\bigg|_1^3 - \dfrac{1}{3}\int_1^3 x^2 \,\mathrm{d}x \hspace{4cm}\\[10pt]
& =  \left[\dfrac{27\ln 3}{3} - 0\right] - \dfrac{x^3}{9}\bigg|_1^3 \\[10pt]
& =  9\ln 3 - \left[\dfrac{27}{9} - \dfrac{1}{9}\right] \\[12pt]
&\hspace{.275em}=  9\ln 3 - \dfrac{26}{9} \quad \quad \text{as required.}
\end{aligned}
$$
