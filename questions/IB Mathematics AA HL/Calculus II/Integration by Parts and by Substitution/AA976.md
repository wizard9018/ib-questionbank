# AA976

**Maximum mark: 5**

Consider the function $f$ such that $f'(x)=\dfrac{8x}{x^2+5}$ and the graph of $y=f(x)$ passes through the point $(2,9\ln 3)$. 

Find an expression for $f(x).$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have that $f(x) = \int f'(x)\,\mathrm{d}x$. Hence

$$

\begin{aligned}

    f(x)&=\int \dfrac{8x}{x^2+5}\,\mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]

    &=4 \int \dfrac{2x}{x^2+5} \,\mathrm{d}x\\[8pt]

    &= 4\int \dfrac{1}{u}\,\mathrm{d}u \hspace{3em} [\text{using }u=x^2+5, \,\mathrm{d}u=2x\,\mathrm{d}x]\\[8pt]

    &= 4\ln\,u + C\\[8pt]

    f(x) &= 4 \ln(x^2+5)+C\hspace{3em}[\text{substituting back}]

\end{aligned}

$$

Since the graph of the function passes through the point $(2,9\ln 3)$, we get

$$

\begin{aligned}

    9\ln 3 &= 4\ln(2^2+5)+C\\[9pt]

    9\ln 3 &= 4\ln 9 + C \\[9pt]

    C &= 9\ln 3 -4\ln(3^2)\\[9pt]

    C &= 9\ln 3 -8\ln 3\\[9pt]

    C &= \ln 3

\end{aligned}

$$

 Hence $\boxed{f(x)=4\ln(x^2+5)+\ln 3}$  or  $\boxed{f(x)=\ln\left(3(x^2+5)^4\right)}$
