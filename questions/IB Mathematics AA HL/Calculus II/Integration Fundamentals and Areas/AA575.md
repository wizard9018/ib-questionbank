# AA575

**Maximum mark: 13**

Let $f(x) = \ln x$ and $g(x) = 2 + 3 \ln (x - 1)$, for $x > 1$.

The graph of $g$ can be obtained from the graph of $f$ by two
transforms:

a vertical stretch of scale factor $q$

a translation of $\binom h k$

1.  Write down the value of
    1.  $q$;

    2.  $h$;
    3.  $k$.[3 marks]

Let $h(x) = g(x) \cdot \cos(0.1x)$, for $1 < x < 8$. The following
diagram shows the graph of $h$ and the line $y = x$.

![cb7483cb56f1d952788497253145250d6c19a449.svg](images/cb7483cb56f1d952788497253145250d6c19a449.svg)

2.
    1.  Find $\displaystyle \int_{2.02}^{5.57} (h(x) - x)\,\textrm d x$.

    2.  Hence, find the area of the region enclosed by the graphs of $h$
        and $h^{-1}$.[5 marks]

3.  Let $d$ be the vertical distance from a point on the graph
    of $h$ to the line $y = x$. There is a point $Q(x,y)$ on the graph
    of $h$ where $d$ is a maximum. Find the coordinates of $Q$, where
    $2.02 < x < 5.57$. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  $ q = 3$

    2.  $h = 1$

    3.  $k = 2$

2.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1cm}\int_{2.02}^{5.57} (h(x) - x)\,\textrm dx
        & = \int_{2.02}^{5.57} [(2 + 3 \ln(x - 1)) \cos(0.1 x) - x]\,\textrm d x\\[6pt]
        & \hspace{.275em}  = 2.28\qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  By part (b) (i), we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        2 \int_{2.02}^{5.57} (h(x) - x)\,\textrm dx & = 2(2.28)\hspace{4.2cm}\\[6pt]
        & \hspace{.275em} = 4.56
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  The distance is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d   & = h(x) - x\\[6pt]
    d   & = (2 + 3 \ln(x - 1))\cos(0.1 x) - x\\[6pt]
    x   & = 3.44\qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}\\[6pt]
    h(3.44)     & = 4.40
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence
      $\mbox Q (3.44,4.40)$
