# AA489

**Maximum mark: 7**

A function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = \dfrac{3x + 2}{4x + 1}$,
$x\in \mathbb{R}$, $x \neq -\dfrac{1}{4}$.

1.  Find an expression for $f^{-1}(x)$. [4 marks]

2.  Given that $f(x)$ can be written in the form
    $f(x) = A + \dfrac{B}{4x + 1}$, find the values of the constants $A$
    and $B$. [2 marks]

3.  Hence write down
    $\displaystyle \int \dfrac{3x + 2}{4x + 1}\,\textrm dx$. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Setting $y = f^{-1}(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \\
    f(f^{-1}(x))    & =  x \\[6pt]
    f(y)            & =  x \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{3y + 2}{4y + 1}  & =  x \qquad \quad [\text{solve for $y$}] \\[6pt]
    3y + 2      & =  4xy + x \\[6pt]
    y(3 - 4x)       & =  x - 2 \\[6pt]
    y           & =  \dfrac{x - 2}{3 - 4x}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we have
      $\hspace{.275em} f^{-1}(x) = \dfrac{x - 2}{3 - 4x}$

2.  Solving the following equation for $A$ and $B$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{3x + 2}{4x + 1} & =  A + \dfrac{B}{4x + 1} \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{3x + 2}{4x + 1} & =  \dfrac{4Ax + A + B}{4x + 1} \\[10pt]
    & \Longrightarrow  \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
     A  =  \dfrac{3}{4},\ \  B  =  \dfrac{5}{4}
    $$

3.  Using part (b), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int \dfrac{3x + 2}{4x + 1}\, \textrm dx & =  \int \left[\dfrac{3}{4} + \dfrac{5}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4x + 1}\right]\textrm dx \\[14pt]
    &  \hspace{.275em}=  \dfrac{3}{4}x + \dfrac{5}{16}\ln|4x+1| + C
    \end{aligned}
    $$
