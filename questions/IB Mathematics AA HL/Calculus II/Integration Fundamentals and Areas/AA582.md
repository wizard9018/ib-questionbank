# AA582

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider a function $g(x)$ such that
$\displaystyle \int_3^7 g(x)\,\textrm d x = 8$.

1.  Find $\displaystyle \int_3^7 5 g (x)\,\textrm dx$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $\displaystyle \int_4^8(g(x-1) + 2)\,\textrm dx$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_3^7 5g(x)\,\textrm d x         & =  5\int_3^7 g(x)\,\textrm d x\hspace{3mm}\\[6pt]
    & = 5(8)\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em}  = 40
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4mm}
    \int_4^8 (g(x-1) + 2)\,\textrm d x  & = \int_4^8 g(x-1)\,\textrm d x + \int_4^8 2 \,\textrm d x\\[6pt]
    & = \int_3^7 g(x)\,\textrm d x + \int_4^8 2 \,\textrm d x\\[6pt]
    & = 8 + \left[2x\right]\Big|_4^8\\[6pt]
    & = 8 + [16 - 8]\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}  = 16
    \end{aligned}
    $$
