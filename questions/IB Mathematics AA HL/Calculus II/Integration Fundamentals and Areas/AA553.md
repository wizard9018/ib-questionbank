# AA553

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider a function $f(x)$ such that
$\displaystyle \int_1^5 f(x)\,\textrm d x = 6$.

1.  Find $\displaystyle \int_1^5 2 f (x)\,\textrm dx$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $\displaystyle \int_1^5(f(x) + 3)\,\textrm dx$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Removing the constant from the integral, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_1^5 2f(x)\,\textrm d x         & =  2\int_1^5 f(x)\,\textrm d x\hspace{3mm}\\[6pt]
    & = 2(6)\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em}  = 12
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Writing as the sum of two integrals, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4mm}
    \int_1^5 (f(x) + 3)\,\textrm d x    & = \int_1^5 f(x)\,\textrm d x + \int_1^5 3 \,\textrm d x\\[6pt]
    & = 6 + \left[3x\right]\Big|_1^5\\[6pt]
    & = 6 + [15 - 3]\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}  = 18
    \end{aligned}
    $$
