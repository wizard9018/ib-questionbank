# AA549

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f'(x) = 6x^2 + 2x - 1$. Given that $f(2) = 5$, find $f(x)$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x)        & = \int f'(x)\,\textrm d x\\[6pt]
& = \int(6x^2 + 2x - 1)\,\textrm d x\\[6pt]
& = 2x^3 + x^2 - x + C
\end{aligned}
$$

Using the fact that $f(2) = 5$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
5   & = 2(2)^3 + 2^2 - 2 + C\\[6pt]
5   & = 16  + 4 - 2 + C\\[6pt]
C   & = -13
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore
  $f(x) = 2x^3 + x^2 - x - 13$
