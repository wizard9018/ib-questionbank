# AA499

**Maximum mark: 6**

Given that $\displaystyle \int_0^6 \dfrac{3}{3x+2}\,\textrm dx = \ln k$,
find the value of $k$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle \int_0^6 \dfrac{3}{3x+2}\,\textrm dx & =  \ln k \hspace{5.2cm}\\[8pt]
\ln(3x+2)\big|_0^6 & =  \ln k \\[11pt]
\ln 20 - \ln 2 & =  \ln k \\[9pt]
\ln \Big(\frac{20}{2}\Big) & =  \ln k \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hspace{2cm}k  =  10 \qquad\quad[\text{since $x \mapsto \ln x$ is one-to-one}]
$$
