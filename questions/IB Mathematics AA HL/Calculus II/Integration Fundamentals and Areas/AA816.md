# AA816

**Maximum mark: 12**

1.  Show that $3\log_{a^3}x = \log_a x$ where $a$, $x\in \mathbb{R}^+$.
    [2 marks]

It is given that
$\log_2 y + \log_8 4x^2 + \log_8 2x = 0$.

2.  Express $y$ in terms of $x$. Give your answer in the form
    $y=bx^c$ where $b$, $c$ are constants.[6 marks]

The region $R$, is bounded by the graph of the function found in part
(b), the $x$-axis, and the lines $x=1$ and $x=k$ where $k>1$. The area
of $R$ is $\dfrac{3}{2}$.

3.  Find the value of $k$.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the properties of logarithms, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    3\log_{a^3}x &= 3 \, \dfrac{\log_a x}{\log_a a^3} \\[10pt]
    &= \cancel{3} \,\dfrac{\log_a x}{\cancel{3}} \\[10pt]
    &=\log_a x
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the properties of logarithms and part (a), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \log_2 y + \log_8 4x^2 + \log_8 2x  &= 0  \\[8pt]
    \log_2 y + \log_8 8x^3 &= 0 \\[8pt]
    \log_2 y + 3 \log_8 2x &= 0 \\[8pt]
    \log_2 y +  \log_2 2x &= 0  \\[8pt]
    \log_2 y &= -\log_2 2x   \\[8pt]
    \log_2 y &= \log_2 (2x)^{-1}   \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-0.5em}y = \dfrac{1}{2}x^{-1}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  First we find the area of $R$ using integration, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Area of }R &= \int_1^k \dfrac{1}{2} \hspace{0.05em}x^{-1} \, \mathrm{d} x \\[8pt]
    &= \left[ \dfrac{1}{2}\ln x \right]_1^k \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\ln k \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{3}{2}&= \dfrac{1}{2}\ln k \\[8pt]
    \ln k &= 3 \\[8pt]
    & \hspace{-0.5em} k = e^3  \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
