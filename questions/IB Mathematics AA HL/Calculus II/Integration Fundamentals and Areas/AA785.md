# AA785

**Maximum mark: 8**

The values of the functions $f$ and $g$ and their derivatives for
$x = 2$, $x=3$ and $x = 6$ are shown in the following table.

![d0a6640466a6441c708713c23e21d5cb05c7fd52.svg](images/d0a6640466a6441c708713c23e21d5cb05c7fd52.svg)

1.  Evaluate $\displaystyle \int_{2}^{3} g''(x)  \,\mathrm{d}x$.
    [2 marks]

2.  Let $k(x)=\dfrac{f(x)}{g(x)}$. Find $k'(2)$. [2 marks]

3.  Let $h(x) = f(g(x))$. Find the equation of the tangent to $h$ at
    $x=6$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \displaystyle \int_{2}^{3} g''(x)  \,\mathrm{d}x &= g'(x) \Big|_2^3\hspace{3.5cm}\\[12pt]
    &= g'(3)-g'(2)\\[12pt]
    &=2-0\\[12pt]
    &\hspace{0.5em}=2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the quotient rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    k'(2) &=    \dfrac{g(2)f'(2)-f(2)g'(2)}{[g(2)]^2} \hspace{2em}\\[12pt]
    &=\dfrac{(-3)(-2)-(2)(0)}{(-3)^2} \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{0.3em}=\dfrac{2}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  First, we find the gradient of the tangent to $h$ at $x = 6$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m   & = h'(6) \hspace{8em}\\[12pt]
    &= f'(g(6))\cdot g'(6)\\[12pt]
    &= f'(3)\cdot(-1)\\[12pt]
    &= -7
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Finding the $y$-coordinate on $h$ at
    $x=6$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= h(6) \\[12pt]
    &= f(g(6)) \\[12pt]
    &= f(3)\\[12pt]
    & = 8
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the point-gradient formula with
    $\text{P}(6,8)$ and $m=-7$, we get Hence, finding the equation of
    the tangent to $h$ at $x=6$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    %y-y_1 &= m(x-x_1) \hspace{7em}\\[8pt]
    y-8 &= -7(x-6)\\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-0.5em}y = -7x+50
    \end{aligned}
    $$
