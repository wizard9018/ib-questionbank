# AA825

**Maximum mark: 15**

Consider a function with domain $a< x < b$. The following diagram shows
the graph of $f'$, the derivative of $f$.

![ffaa11a4b19671c0de5905aef42c6954ba3def71.svg](images/ffaa11a4b19671c0de5905aef42c6954ba3def71.svg)

From the graph above $p$, $0$ and $s$ are $x$-intercepts of $f'$, and
there is a local minimum at $x=q$ and a local maximum at
$x=r$.

1.  Find all the values of $x$ where the graph of $f$ is increasing.
    Justify your answer. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $x$ where the graph of $f$ has a local minimum.
    Justify your answer. [2 marks]

3.  Find the value of $x$ where the graph of $f$ has a local maximum.
    Justify your answer. [2 marks]

4.  Find the values of $x$ where the graph of $f$ has points of
    inflexion. Justify your answer.[4 marks]

The total area of the region enclosed by the graph of $f'$ and the
$x$-axis between $x=p$ and $x=s$ is $25$.

5.  Given that $f(p)+f(s)=13$, find the value of
    $f(0)$.[5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  $f(x)$ is increasing when $f'(x)>0$. Therefore
    $f \text{ is increasing on interval } (0,s) $

2.  $f(x)$ has a local minimum when the function changes from decreasing
    to increasing.

    Therefore,
    $f \text{ has a local minimum at } x=0 $

3.  $f(x)$ has a local maximum when the function changes from increasing
    to decreasing.

    Therefore,
    $f \text{ has a local maximum at } x=s $

4.  The graph of $f$ has an inflection point at $x$ if $f''(x)=0$ and
    $f'$ changes from increasing to decreasing or vice-versa.

    Therefore,
    $f \text{ has inflection points at } x=p, x=q \text{ and } x=r $

5.  Using the formula for the total area enclosed by a function and the
    $x$-axis, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Total area} &= \int_p^s |f'(x)| \,\mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]
    &= \int_p^0 -f'(x) \,\mathrm{d}x + \int_0^s f'(x) \,\mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]
    &= -f(x)\Big|_{p}^{0} + f(x)\Big|_{0}^{s}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    So, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Total area} &= -f(0) + f(p) + f(s) - f(0) \\[12pt]
    &= -2f(0) + f(p) + f(s)\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using that the total
    area is $25$ and $f(p)+f(s)=13$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -2f(0) + f(p) + f(s) &= 25 \\[12pt]
    -2f(0) + 13  &= 25 \\[12pt]
    -2f(0) &= 12 \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-1.88em}f(0) = -6
    \end{aligned}
    $$
