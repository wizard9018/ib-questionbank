# AA548

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f'(x) = 3x^2 - 3$. Given that $f(2) = 1$, find $f(x)$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x)        & = \int f'(x)\,\textrm d x\\[6pt]
& = \int(3x^2 - 3)\,\textrm d x\\[6pt]
& = x^3 - 3x + C
\end{aligned}
$$

Using the fact that $f(2) = 1$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
1   & = 2^3 - 3(2) + C\\[6pt]
1   & = 8 - 6 +C\\[6pt]
C   & = -1
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore
  $f(x) = x^3 - 3 x - 1$
