# AA525

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = 3x^2 - 4x$. The graph of $f$ is shown in the following
diagram.

![e2c3c697e83174aad434bec213190a266a8c7cf8.svg](images/e2c3c697e83174aad434bec213190a266a8c7cf8.svg)

1.  Find $\displaystyle \int (3x^2 - 4x)\,\mathrm{d}x$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the area of the region enclosed by the graph of $f$, the
    $x$-axis and the lines $x = 2$ and $x = 4$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Integrating $f(x)$ term by term, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int (3x^2 - 4x) \,\textrm d x  & = \int 3x^2 \,\textrm d x + \int (-4 x)\,\textrm d x\\[4pt]
    & = \dfrac {3x^3} 3 - \dfrac {4x^2} 2 + C\\[7pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = x^3 - 2x^2 + C
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The area enclosed by the graph of $f$, the $x$-axis and the lines
    $x = 2$ and $x = 4$ is given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.6cm}
    A       & = \int_2^4 (3x^2 - 4x) \,\textrm d x \\[4pt]
    & = (x^3 - 2x^2)\Big|_2^4\\[6pt]
    & = [4^3 - 2(4^2)] - [2^3 - 2(2^2)]\\[7pt]
    & = [64 - 32] - [8 - 8]\\[7pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = 32
    \end{aligned}
    $$
