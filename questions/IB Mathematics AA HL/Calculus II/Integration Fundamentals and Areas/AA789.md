# AA789

**Maximum mark: 6**

The graph of a function $f$ passes through the point $(\ln 2,15)$.\
Given that $f'(x)=9e^{3x}$, find $f(x)$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

An expression for $f(x)$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x)& = \int f'(x)  \,\mathrm{d}x \\[10pt]
& = \int9e^{3x}  \,\mathrm{d}x\\[10pt]
& =  \dfrac{9}{3}e^{3x} +C \\[12pt]
& =  3\hspace{0.05em}e^{3x} +C \\
\end{aligned}
$$
as the graph of $f$
passes through the point $(\ln 2,15)$, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
15 &= 3e^{3(\ln2)}+C \\[12pt]
15 &= 3e^{\ln \left(2^3\right)}+C \\[12pt]
15 &= 3e^{\ln 8}+C \\[12pt]
15&=3(8)+C\\[12pt]
C&=-9
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\hspace{-1.95em}f(x)=3e^{3x}-9
\end{aligned}
$$
