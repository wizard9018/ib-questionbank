# AA873

**Maximum mark: 15**

Consider the function $f$ defined by $f(x) = 2\ln (24-1.5x)$ for
$x < 16$.

The line $L_1: y = x$ intersects the graph of $f$ at point P.

The line $L_2$ is perpendicular to $L_1$ and tangent to the graph of $f$
at point Q.

![44437a6d91aede9e10da2b2d5464a439b2f48901.svg](images/44437a6d91aede9e10da2b2d5464a439b2f48901.svg)

1.  Find the $x$-coordinate of point P, to three significant figures.
    [2 marks]

2.
    1.  Find the exact coordinates of point Q.

    2.  Show that the equation of $L_2$ is $y = -x + 2\ln 3 + 14$.
        [6 marks]

The shaded region $A$, as shown in the previous diagram, is enclosed by
the graph of $f$, the line $L_1$ and the line $L_2$

3.
    1.  Find the exact $x$-coordinate of the point where $L_2$
        intersects $L_1$.

    2.  Hence, find the area of $A$, to two decimal places. [5 marks]

The line $L_2$ is also tangent to the graph of the inverse function
$f^{-1}$.

![7e7a6456e0689f23b9b11eaa2d64983fc0f6ebb9.svg](images/7e7a6456e0689f23b9b11eaa2d64983fc0f6ebb9.svg)

4.  Find the shaded area enclosed by the graphs of $f$,
    $f^{-1}$ and the line $L_2$. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we solve the equation $y = f(x)$ with $y = x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(x) &= x \\[8pt]
    \hspace{5em} 2\ln (24-1.5x) &= x \\[8pt]
    x &\approx 5.51 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the $x$-coordinate of point P is
     $5.51$

2.
    1.  The gradient of the line $L_1$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        m_{L_1} &= 1 \hspace{4.25em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Since the line
        $L_2$ is perpendicular to the line $L_1$, we find

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        m_{L_2} &= -\dfrac{1}{m_{L_1}} \\[6pt]
        &= -\dfrac{1}{1} \\[6pt]
        &= -1 \hspace{3.25em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Since the line
        $L_2$ is tangent to the graph of $f$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{6.75em} f'(x) &= -1 \\[6pt]
        \dfrac{2(-1.5)}{24-1.5x} &= -1 \\[6pt]
        \dfrac{-3}{24-1.5x} &= -1 \\[6pt]
        x &= 14 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Substituting $x = 14$ in the equation $y = f(x)$, we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{4.25em} y &= 2\ln (24 - 1.5(14)) \\[6pt]
        &= 2\ln 3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Thus, the coordinates of point Q are
         $(14, 2\ln 3)$

    2.  Using the equation of a line in the form $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$
        with $(x_1,y_1) = (14, 2\ln 3)$ and $m = -1$, we have

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{7em} y - 2\ln 3 &= -1(x-14) \\[6pt]
        y - 2\ln 3 &= -x+14 \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{-0.5em}y = -x + 2\ln 3 + 14\hspace{3em} [\text{Shown}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.
    1.  Substituting $y = x$ in the equation of the line $L_2$, we
        obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3.5em} x &= -x + 2\ln 3 + 14 \\[8pt]
        2x &= 2\ln 3 + 14 \\[8pt]
        x &= \ln 3 + 7
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence, $L_1$ and $L_2$ intersect at
         $x=\ln 3 + 7$

    2.  Hence, the area of $A$ is defined by
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        A &= \displaystyle \int_{5.51}^{\ln 3 + 7} \left[x - f(x) \right]\,\mathrm{d}x+ \displaystyle \int_{\ln 3 + 7}^{14} \left[(-x +2\ln 3 + 14) - f(x) \right] \,\mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]
        %     &= \displaystyle \int_{5.51}^{8.10} \left[x - 2\ln (24-1.5x)\right] \,\mathrm{d}x \\[1pt]
        %     & \hspace{1em}+ \displaystyle \int_{8.10}^{14} \left[(-x +2\ln 3 + 14) - 2\ln (24-1.5x)\right] \,\mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]
        &\approx 4.05 + 7.50  \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\[10pt]
        &\approx 11.55 \text{ unit}^2
        \end{aligned}
        $$

4.  Since the graphs of $f$ and $f^{-1}$ are symmetric about the line
    $y = x$, the line splits the shaded region into two equal areas, one
    of which is $A$.

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\text{shaded region}} &= 2A \\[8pt]
    &= 2(11.55) \\[10pt]
    &= 23.1 \text{ unit}^2 \hspace{7.5em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
