# AA522

**Maximum mark: 7**

A function $f$ satisfies the conditions $f(0) = \ln 2$, $f(1) = 0$ and
its second derivative is
$f''(x) = 40x^\frac{2}{3} + (x+1)^{-2}$,
$x \geq 0$.

Find $f(x)$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

If we integrate $f''$ with respect to $x$, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1.75em} \int f''(x)\,\mathrm{d}x
&= \int \Big[40x^\frac{2}{3} + (x+1)^{-2}\Big] \,\mathrm{d}x \\[6pt]
f'(x)       &= 40\bigg[\dfrac{x^\frac{5}{3}}{\frac{5}{3}}\bigg] + \left[-\dfrac{1}{x+1}\right] + A \hspace{3em} [\text{by FTC}] \\[8pt]
f'(x)       &= 24x^\frac{5}{3} - \dfrac{1}{x+1} + A
\end{aligned}
$$
If we
integrate $f'$ with respect to $x$, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{4.15em} \int f'(x)\,\mathrm{d}x
&= \int \Big[24x^\frac{5}{3} - \dfrac{1}{x+1} + A\Big] \,\mathrm{d}x \\[6pt]
f(x)        &= 24\bigg[\dfrac{x^\frac{8}{3}}{\frac{8}{3}}\bigg] - \ln(x+1) + Ax + B \hspace{3em} [\text{by FTC}] \\[11pt]
f(x)        &= 9x^\frac{8}{3} - \ln(x+1) + Ax + B \tag*{$(\ast)$}
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, substituting $x = 0$ in $(\ast)$ and using the condition
$f(0) = \ln 2$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(0)        &= 9(0)^\frac{8}{3} - \ln(0+1) + A(0) + B \hspace{0.6em} \\[6pt]
\ln 2       &= 0 - \ln 1 + 0 + B \\[6pt]
B       &= \ln 2.
\end{aligned}
$$
Similarly, substituting $x = 1$ in
$(\ast)$ and using the condition $f(1) = 0$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{0.1em} f(1)         &= 9(1)^\frac{8}{3} - \ln(1+1) + A(1) + \ln 2 \\[6pt]
0                   &= 9 - \cancel{\ln 2} + A + \cancel{\ln 2} \\[6pt]
A                   &= -9
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{.275em}f(x) = 9x^\frac{8}{3} - \ln(x+1) - 9x + \ln 2 \hspace{1.5em}
\end{aligned}
$$
