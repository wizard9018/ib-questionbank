# AA537

**Maximum mark: 8**

A function $f(x)$ has derivative $f'(x) = 6x^2-24x$. The graph of $f$
has an $x$-intercept at $x = 1$.

1.  Find $f(x)$. [4 marks]

2.  The graph of $f$ has a point of inflexion at $x = k$. Find $k$.
    [2 marks]

3.  Find the values of $x$ for which the graph of $f$ is concave-up.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(x)        & = \int (6x^2 - 24x) \textrm d x\\[4pt]
    & = \dfrac {6x^3} 3 - \dfrac {24 x^2} 2 + C\\[6pt]
    & = 2 x^3 - 12 x^2 + C
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Since the
    $x$-intercept is at $x = 1$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{.9cm}
    0       & = 2(1^3) - 12 (1^2) + C\\[6pt]
    C       & = 10
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence
      $f(x) = 2x^3 - 12 x^2 + 10$

2.  The second derivative is zero at the point of inflection, hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f''(x)  & = 12 x - 24\hspace{1.4cm}\\[4pt]
    0       & = 12 x - 24\\[4pt]
    x       & = 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence   $k = 2$

3.  The function $f$ is concave-up when $f''(x) > 0$, then
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    12 x - 24       & > 0 \hspace{2.8cm}\\[4pt]
    12x             & > 24\\[4pt]
    &\hspace{-2mm}x > 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
