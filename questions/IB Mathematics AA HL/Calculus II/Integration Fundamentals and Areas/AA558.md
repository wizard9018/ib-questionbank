# AA558

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $\displaystyle f(x) = \int \dfrac 8 {2x - 1}\,\textrm d x$, for
$x > \dfrac 1 2$. The graph of $f$ passes through $(1,5)$.

Find $f(x)$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We integrate
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(x)        & = \int \dfrac 8 {2x - 1}\,\textrm d x\\[6pt]
& = \dfrac {8 \ln (2x - 1)} 2 + C\\[6pt]
& = 4 \ln(2x - 1) + C
\end{aligned}
$$

Let's determine $C$. Substituting $(1,5)$ into the expression for $f$,
we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{5mm}
5       & = 4 \ln (2(1) - 1) + C\\[6pt]
5       & = 4\ln (1) + C\\[6pt]
C       & = 5
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence
  $f(x) = 4 \ln(2x - 1) + 5$
