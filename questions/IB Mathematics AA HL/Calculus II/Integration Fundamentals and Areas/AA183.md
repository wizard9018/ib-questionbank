# AA183

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = \dfrac{\ln(x+2)}{2}$, for $x > -2$. The diagram below shows
part of the graph of $f$.

![0b26b0e195f4a087c10216491d624f903712efff.svg](images/0b26b0e195f4a087c10216491d624f903712efff.svg)

1.  Find the coordinates of:
    1.  the $x$-intercept;

    2.  the $y$-intercept. [2 marks]

2.  Find the equation of the vertical asymptote to the graph of $f$.
    [2 marks]

3.  Find the area of the region enclosed by the graph of $f$, the
    $x$-axis and\
    the $y$-axis. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Solving the equation $f(x) = 0$ for $x$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3.8em} \dfrac{\ln(x+2)}{2} &= 0 \\[4pt]
        x &= -1 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence the coordinates of the $x$-intercept are
         $(-1,0)$

    2.  Evaluating $f(x)=\dfrac{\ln(x+2)}{2}$ for $x = 0$, we have

         $$
         \begin{aligned}
         \hspace{0.4em} f(0) &= \dfrac{\ln 2}{2} \\[8pt]
         &\approx 0.347
         \end{aligned}
         $$
         Hence the coordinates of the
        $y$-intercept are
         $(0,0.347)$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \lim_{x \rightarrow 0^+} \ln x &= -\infty \hspace{3em} \\[7pt]
    \lim_{x \rightarrow -2^+} \ln (x+2) &= -\infty \\[6pt]
    \lim_{x \rightarrow -2^+} f(x) &= -\infty \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence
    the equation of the vertical asymptote is
     $x = -2$

3.  The area of the region enclosed by the graph of $f$, the $x$-axis
    and the $y$-axis is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10em} A &= \int_{-1}^0 \dfrac{\ln(x+2)}{2}\,\mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]
    &\approx 0.193 \text{ units}^2 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
