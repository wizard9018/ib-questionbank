# AA817

**Maximum mark: 5**

Given that
$\displaystyle \int_0^{e^k} \dfrac{2}{1+2x}\, \mathrm{d}x = \ln 3$, find
the value of $k$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using integration by inspection or substitution, we have

 $$
 \begin{aligned}
 \int_0^{e^k} \dfrac{2}{1+2x}\, \mathrm{d}x &= \big[ \ln ( 1+ 2x)\big]_0^{e^k} \\[12pt]
 &= \ln \left(1+2e^k\right) - \ln (1) \\[12pt]
 &= \ln \left(1+2e^k\right)
 \end{aligned}
 $$
 Hence we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\ln \left(1+2e^k\right) &= \ln 3 \hspace{5.6em}\\[12pt]
1+2e^k &= 3 \\[12pt]
2e^k &= 2 \\[12pt]
e^k &= 1 \\[12pt]
&\hspace{-0.525em}k = 0
\end{aligned}
$$
