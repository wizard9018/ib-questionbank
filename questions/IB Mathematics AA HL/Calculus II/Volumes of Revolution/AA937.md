# AA937

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows the curve $\dfrac{x^2}{400}+\dfrac{(y+5)^2}{225}=1$, where $0 \leq y \leq h$.

![AA937a](images/AA937a.PNG)

The curve from point C to point P is rotated $360^\circ$ about the $y$-axis to form a lamp shade. The rectangle ABCD, of height $(10-h)$ cm, is rotated $360^\circ$ about the $y$-axis to form a solid ceiling fixture.

The lamp shade is assumed to have a negligible thickness.

Given that the interior volume of the lamp shade is to be $6\hspace{0.15em}000\hspace{0.15em}\text{cm}^3$, determine the height of the ceiling fixture, length AD in the diagram.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

The volume of the solid formed by rotating $360^\circ$ about the $y$-axis is given by

$$

\begin{aligned}

V &= \int_a^b\pi\hspace{0.15em}x^2\,\mathrm{d}y

\end{aligned}

$$

Rearranging the given equation to make $x^2$ the subject, we get

$$

\begin{aligned}

\dfrac{x^2}{400}+\dfrac{(y+5)^2}{225} &= 1 \\[10pt]

\dfrac{x^2}{400} &= 1 - \dfrac{(y+5)^2}{225} \\[10pt]

x^2 &= 400\bigg(1 - \dfrac{(y+5)^2}{225}\bigg)

\end{aligned}

$$

Substituting this expression for $x^2$ into the volume formula, with $a=0$ and $b=h$, we have

$$

\begin{aligned}

V &= \int_0^h 400\pi\hspace{0.15em}\bigg(1 - \dfrac{(y+5)^2}{225}\bigg)\,\mathrm{d}y

\end{aligned}

$$

The interior volume of the lampshade is required to be $6\hspace{0.15em}000\hspace{0.15em}\text{cm}^3$, hence

$$

\begin{aligned}

400\pi\,\int_0^h \hspace{0.15em}1 - \dfrac{(y+5)^2}{225}\,\mathrm{d}y &= 6\hspace{0.15em}000 \\[10pt]

\int_0^h \hspace{0.15em}1 - \dfrac{(y+5)^2}{225}\,\mathrm{d}y &= \dfrac{6\hspace{0.15em}000}{400\pi} \\[10pt]

\bigg[\hspace{0.15em} y - \dfrac{(y+5)^3}{675} \hspace{0.15em}\bigg]_{y=0}^{y=h} & = \dfrac{15}{\pi} \\[10pt]

h - \dfrac{(h+5)^3}{675} - \bigg(0 - \dfrac{(0+5)^3}{675}\bigg) &= \dfrac{15}{\pi} \\[10pt]

h - \dfrac{(h+5)^3}{675} + \dfrac{5}{27} &= \dfrac{15}{\pi}

\end{aligned}

$$

Using the G.D.C. to sketch the graphs of $y=h - \dfrac{(h+5)^3}{675} + \dfrac{5}{27}$ and $y=\dfrac{15}{\pi}$, we get

![AA937b](images/AA937b.PNG)

The height of the lampshade is approximately $7.445$ cm and therefore the height of the ceiling fixture is

$$

\begin{aligned}

10 - 7.445 &= 2.555

\end{aligned}

$$

The height of the ceiling fixture is approximately $2.56\,\mathrm{cm}\,$.
