# AA293

**Maximum mark: 11**

The following table shows the probability distribution of a discrete
random $\text{variable}$ $Z$,
in terms of an angle $\theta$.

![00e66067cc36a0da32be98bbfb87aea3442f8386.svg](images/00e66067cc36a0da32be98bbfb87aea3442f8386.svg)

1.  Show that $\cos \theta = \dfrac{3}{4}$. [4 marks]

2.  Find $\tan \theta$, given that $\tan \theta > 0$. [3 marks]

Let $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{\cos x}$, for $0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

The graph of $y = f(x)$ between $x = \theta$ and $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$ is
rotated $\ang{360}$ about the $x$-axis.

3.  Find the volume of the solid formed. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the double angle identity for cosine, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8em} \mathrm{P}(Z = 0) + \mathrm{P}(Z = 1) &= 1 \hspace{2em} [\text{the axiom of probability}] \\[10pt]
    2\cos 2\theta + \cos \theta &= 1 \\[8pt]
    2(2\cos^2 \theta - 1) + \cos \theta &= 1 \\[8pt]
    4\cos^2 \theta + \cos \theta - 3 &= 0 \\[9pt]
    (\cos \theta + 1)(4\cos \theta - 3) &= 0 \\[9pt]
    4\cos \theta - 3 &= 0 \hspace{2em} [\text{since $\mathrm{P}(Z = 1) = \cos \theta \geq 0$}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{1.4em} \cos \theta = \dfrac{3}{4}
    $$

2.  Since $\tan \theta > 0$ and $\cos \theta = 3/4 > 0$, we have
    $\sin \theta > 0$.

    Hence, using the Pythagorean identity, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.6em} \cos^2 \theta + \sin^2 \theta &= 1 \\[11pt]
    \dfrac{9}{16} + \sin^2 \theta &= 1 \\[5pt]
    \sin \theta &= \textstyle \sqrt{1-\dfrac{9}{16}} \\[6pt]
    \sin \theta &= \dfrac{\sqrt{7}}{4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \tan \theta &= \dfrac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\sqrt{7}/4}{3/4} \hspace{1.7em}  \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\sqrt{7}}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the volume of revolution formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{9em} V_{\text{solid}} &= \int_{\theta}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \pi y^2 \,\mathrm{d}x \\[6pt]
    &= \pi \int_{\theta}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \dfrac{1}{\cos^2 x} \,\mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]
    &= \pi\Big[\tan x\Big]\Big|_{\theta}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \\[12pt]
    &= \pi\Big[\tan \dfrac{\pi}{4} - \tan \theta\Big] \\[8pt]
    &= \pi\bigg[1 - \dfrac{\raisebox{-0.1em}{$\sqrt{7}$}}{3}\hspace{0.05em}\bigg] \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (b)}] \\[6pt]
    &= \pi - \dfrac{\pi\sqrt{7}}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
