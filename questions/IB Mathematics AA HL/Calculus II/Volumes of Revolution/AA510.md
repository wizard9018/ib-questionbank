# AA510

**Maximum mark: 6**

The graph of $y = f(x)$ is obtained from the graph of $y = \ln x$ by a
horizontal translation $3$ units in the negative $x$ direction and a
vertical translation $\ln 6$ units in the positive $y$ direction.

1.  Find $f(x)$.[2 marks]

2.  The region bounded by the graph of $y = f(x)$ and the two axes is
    rotated through $2\pi$ radians about the $y$-axis. Find the volume
    generated. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(x)        & = \ln\big(x - (-3)\big) + \hspace{0.05em}\ln 6 \\[8pt]
    & =  \ln\big(6(x + 3)\big) \\[10pt]
    &  = \ln\big(6x + 18\big)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y       & =  \ln\big(6x + 18\big) \\[8pt]
    e^y         & = 6x + 18 \\[8pt]
    x       & = \dfrac{1}{6}(e^y - 18).
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    When $x = 0$,
    $y = \ln 18$. Hence the volume generated is given by

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V       & = \pi\int_0^{\ln 18} x^2\,\mathrm{d}y \\[8pt]
    & = \dfrac{\pi}{36}\int_0^{\ln 18} \big(e^y - 18\big)^2\,\mathrm{d}y \\[8pt]
    &  \approx  42.4 \qquad \quad  [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
