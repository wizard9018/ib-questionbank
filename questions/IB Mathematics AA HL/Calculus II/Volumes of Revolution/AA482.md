# AA482

**Maximum mark: 19**

The following graph shows the relation
$x = 5\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi y}{30}\right) + 10$,  $0 \leq y \leq 60$.

![a07598c5c0c0ff67d90ad4f1942f075b64d82b6c.svg](images/a07598c5c0c0ff67d90ad4f1942f075b64d82b6c.svg)

The curve is rotated $360^\circ$ about the $y$-axis to form a volume of
revolution.

1.  Calculate the value of the volume generated. [8 marks]

A vase with this shape is made with a solid base of diameter $20$ cm.
The vase is filled with water from a faucet at a constant rate of
$150$ cm$^3$ sec$^{-1}$.
At time $t$ sec, the water depth is $h$ cm, $0 \leq h \leq 60$ and the
volume of water in the vase is $V$ cm$^3$.

2.
    1.  Given that
        $\dfrac{\textrm dV}{\textrm dh} = \pi \Big[5\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi h}{30}\Big) + 10\right]^2$,
        find an expression for $\dfrac{\textrm dh}{\textrm dt}$.

    2.  Find the value of $\dfrac{\textrm dh}{\textrm dt}$ when
        $h = 45$ cm. [4 marks]

3.
    1.  Find $\dfrac{\textrm d^2h}{\textrm dt^2}$.

    2.  Find the values of $h$ for which
        $\dfrac{\textrm d^2h}{\textrm dt^2} = 0$.

    3.  By making specific reference to the shape of the vase, interpret
        $\dfrac{\textrm dh}{\textrm dt}$ at the values of $h$ found in
        part (c) (ii). [7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Calculating the value of the volume of revolution, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V & =  \pi\int_0^{60} x^2\, \textrm dy \\[3pt]
    & =  \pi\int_0^{60} \Big[5\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi y}{30}\right) + 10\Big]^2 \textrm dy \quad \quad \quad \Big[\text{let $u = \dfrac{\pi y}{30}$}\Big] \\[3pt]
    & =  (30\cdot25)\int_0^{2\pi} \left[\sin u + 2\right]^2 \textrm du  \\[3pt]
    & =  750 \int_0^{2\pi} \left[\sin^2 u + 4\sin u + 4\right]\,\textrm du  \quad \quad \quad \Big[\text{replace $\sin^2 u = \dfrac{1}{2}\big(1 - \cos(2u)\big)$}\Big] \\[3pt]
    & =  750 \int_0^{2\pi} \Big[\dfrac{1}{2}\big(1-\cos(2u)\big) + 4\sin u + 4\Big]\,\textrm du \\[3pt]
    & =  375 \int_0^{2\pi} \left[9-\cos(2u) + 8\sin u\right]\,\textrm du \\[6pt]
    & =  375\Big[9u - \dfrac{1}{2}\sin(2u) - 8\cos u\Big]\Big|_0^{2\pi} \\[8pt]
    & =  375\Big[(18\pi-8) - (-8)\Big] \\[12pt]
    & =  6750 \pi \hspace{0.25em}\text{cm$^3$}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{\textrm dh}{\textrm dt} & =  \dfrac{\textrm dV}{\textrm dt}\cdot\dfrac{\textrm dh}{\textrm dV} \\[6pt]
        & =  \dfrac{150}{\pi \Big[5\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi h}{30}\right) + 10\Big]^2} \\[8pt]
        &  =  \dfrac{6}{\pi \Big[\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi h}{30}\right) + 2\Big]^2}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Evaluating $\dfrac{\textrm dh}{\textrm dt}$ at height $h = 45$
        cm, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{\textrm dh}{\textrm dt} & =  \dfrac{6}{\pi \Big[\sin\left(\dfrac{45\pi}{30}\right) + 2\Big]^2} \\[6pt]
        & =  \dfrac{6}{\pi \big[(-1)+ 2\big]^2} \\[8pt]
        &  =  \dfrac{6}{\pi}\mbox{\hspace{0.25em}cm \hspace{0.1em}sec}^{-1}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.
    1.  Calculating the $2$nd derivative of $h$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{\textrm dh}{\textrm dt} & = \dfrac{6}{\pi}\Big[\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi h}{30}\right) + 2\Big]^{-2} \\[10pt]
        \dfrac{\textrm d^2h}{\textrm dt^2} & = -\dfrac{12}{\pi}\Big[\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi h}{30}\right) + 2\Big]^{-3}\Big[\dfrac{\pi}{30}\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi h}{30}\right)\left(\dfrac{\textrm dh}{\textrm dt}\right)\Big] \\[10pt]
        &= -\dfrac{12}{\pi}\Big[\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi h}{30}\right) + 2\Big]^{-3}\Bigg[\dfrac{\pi}{30}\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi h}{30}\right)\dfrac{6}{\pi}\Big[\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi h}{30}\right) + 2\Big]^{-2}\Bigg]\\[10pt]
        &  =  -\dfrac{12\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi h}{30}\right)}{5\pi\Big[\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi h}{30}\right) + 2\Big]^5}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Solving $\dfrac{\textrm d^2h}{\textrm dt^2} = 0$ for $h$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \cos\left(\dfrac{\pi h}{30}\right) & =  0 \\[6pt]
        \dfrac{\pi h}{30} & =  \dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{3\pi}{2} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \hspace{26mm} h  =  15\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}, \ 45\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}
        $$

    3.  Considering the shape of the vase and part (c) (ii), we can make
        the following conclusions:

        -   $\dfrac{\textrm dh}{\textrm dt}$ is a minimum at $h = 15$ cm
            and the vase is widest at this height.

        -   $\dfrac{\textrm dh}{\textrm dt}$ is a maximum at $h = 45$ cm
            and the vase is narrowest at this height.
