# AA540

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let
$f(x) = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3x-6}}$,
for  $x > 2$.

1.  Find $\displaystyle \int \big(f(x)\big)^2\,\mathrm{d}x$.[2 marks]

Part of the graph of $f$ is shown in the following diagram.

![f2933739f5f260e67ff6bfcc6c9e334c39baed29.svg](images/f2933739f5f260e67ff6bfcc6c9e334c39baed29.svg)

The shaded region $R$ is enclosed by the graph of $f$, the $x$-axis, and
the lines $x = 3$ and $x = 11$.

2.  Find the exact volume of the solid formed when $R$ is
    rotated $360\text{\textdegree}$ about the $x$-axis.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int \left( \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3x-6}}\right)^2\textrm d x
    & = \int \dfrac 1 {3x - 6}\,\textrm d x\hspace{1.5cm}\\[9pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}= \dfrac 1 3 \ln |3x - 6| + C
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The volume is given by the integral
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.7cm}
    V   & = \pi \int_3^{11} \left( \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3x-6}}\right)^2\textrm d x\\[6pt]
    & = \pi \left[\dfrac 1 3 \ln(3x - 6)\right]_3^{11}\\[6pt]
    & = \dfrac \pi 3 \left[\ln(27) - \ln (3)\right]\\[9pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = \dfrac \pi 3 \ln 9
    \end{aligned}
    $$
