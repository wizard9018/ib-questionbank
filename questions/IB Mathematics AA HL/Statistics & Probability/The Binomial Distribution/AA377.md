# AA377

**Maximum mark: 6**

The probability of obtaining tails when a biased coin is tossed is
$0.6$. The coin is tossed $400$ times.

1.
    1.  Write down the mean number of tails.

    2.  Find the variance of the number of tails. [3 marks]

2.  Find the probability that the number of tails obtained is less than
    one standard deviation away from the mean.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $T$ be the number of tails obtained when the coin is tossed
    $400$ times.

    We have $T \sim \mathrm{B}(400,0.6)$.
    1.  Hence, using the formula for the expected value of $T$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{E}[T] &= np \\[6pt]
        &= (400)(0.6) \hspace{2.2em} \\[8pt]
        &= 240
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Hence, using the formula for the variance of $T$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.5em} \mathrm{Var}(T) &= np(1-p) \\[6pt]
        &= (400)(0.6)(1-0.6) \\[8pt]
        &= 96
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Using the formula for the standard deviation of $T$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} \sigma &= \sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(T)} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{96} \\[7pt]
    &\approx 9.80
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.4em} \mathrm{P}(|T - 240| < 9.80) &= \mathrm{P}(240-9.80 < T < 240+9.80) \\[8pt]
    &= \mathrm{P}(230.2 < T < 249.8) \\[8pt]
    &= \mathrm{P}(231 \leq T \leq 249) \\[8pt]
    &= \text{binomCdf}(400,0.6,231,249) \\[8pt]
    &\approx 0.668
    \end{aligned}
    $$
