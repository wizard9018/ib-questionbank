# AA437

**Maximum mark: 7**

John likes to play two games, $A$ and $B$.

For game $A$, the probability John wins is $0.8$. He plays game $A$ five
times.

1.  Find the probability that he wins exactly two games. [2 marks]

For game $B$, the probability John wins is $p$. He plays game $B$ six
times.

2.  Write down an expression, in terms of $p$, for the
    probability that he wins exactly four games. [2 marks]

3.  Hence find the values of $p$ such that the probability that
    he wins exactly four games is $0.2$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X$ be the number of games John wins when he plays game $A$ five
    times.

    We have $X \sim \mathrm{B}(5,0.8)$.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X = 2) &= \text{binomPdf}(5,0.8,2) \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.0512
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $Y$ be the number of games John wins when he plays game $B$ six
    times.

    We have $Y \sim \mathrm{B}(6,p)$.

    Hence, using the probability mass function of $Y$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(Y = 4) &= \hspace{-0.075em}\binom{6}{4}\hspace{-0.05em}\big[p^4\big]\big[(1-p)^{6-4}\big] \hspace{0.3em}  \\[6pt]
    &= 15\big[p^4\big]\big[(1-p)^2\big]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $\mathrm{P}(Y = 4) = 0.2$ for $0 < p < 1$, we
    find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    15\big[p^4\big]\big[(1-p)^2\big] &= 0.2 \hspace{10.9em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{10.3em} p \approx 0.464,\hspace{0.25em} 0.834 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
