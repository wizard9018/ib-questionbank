# AA378

**Maximum mark: 6**

The probability of obtaining heads when a biased coin is tossed is
$0.65$. The coin is tossed seven times. Find the probability of
obtaining:

1.  at least four heads; [2 marks]

2.  the fourth tail is on the seventh toss. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $H$ be the number of heads obtained when the coin is tossed
    seven times.

    We have $H \sim \mathrm{B}(7,0.65)$.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.2em} \mathrm{P}(H \geq 4) &= \text{binomCdf}(7,0.65,4,7) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.800
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $T$ be the number of tails obtained among the first six tosses
    of the coin.

    We have $T \sim \mathrm{B}(6,0.35)$.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.4em} \mathrm{P}(T = 3) &= \text{binomPdf}(6,0.35,3) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.23549
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using independence of coin
    tosses, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(\text{$4$th ``tails'' on $7$th toss}) &=  \mathrm{P}(T = 3)\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(\text{``tails'' on $7$th toss}) \\[6pt]
    &= (0.23549)(0.35) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.0824
    \end{aligned}
    $$
