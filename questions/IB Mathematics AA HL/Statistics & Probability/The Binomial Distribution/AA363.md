# AA363

**Maximum mark: 8**

A biased coin is tossed $10$ times. Let $X$ be the number of tails
obtained.

The probability of obtaining a tail in any one throw is $p > 0$.

1.  Find, in terms of $p$, an expression for $\mathrm{P}(X = 8)$.
    [2 marks]

2.
    1.  Determine the value of $p$ for which $\mathrm{P}(X = 8)$ is a
        maximum.

    2.  For this value of $p$, determine the expected number of tails.
        [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since $X \sim \mathrm{B}(10,p)$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.7em} \mathrm{P}(X = 8) &= \hspace{-0.075em}\binom{10}{8}\hspace{-0.05em}\big[p^8\big]\big[(1-p)^{10-8}\big] \\[6pt]
    &= 45\big[p^8\big]\big[1 - 2p + p^2\big] \\[12pt]
    &= 45\big(p^8 - 2p^9 + p^{10}\big)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  Differentiating $\mathrm{P}(X = 8)$ with respect to $p$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{4.925em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}p}\big[\mathrm{P}(X = 8)\big] &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}p}\big[45\big(p^8 - 2p^9 + p^{10}\big)\big] \\[6pt]
        &= 45\big(8p^7 - 18p^8 + 10p^9\big) \hspace{3em} [\text{by power rule}] \\[11pt]
        &=\hspace{-0.075em}\big(90p^7\big)\big(4 - 9p + 5p^2\big)
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence, solving the equation
        $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}p}\big[\mathrm{P}(X = 8)\big] = 0$
        for $p$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \big(90p^7\big)\big(4 - 9p + 5p^2\big) &= 0 \\[12pt]
        5p^2 - 9p + 4 &= 0 \hspace{3em} [\text{since $p > 0$}] \hspace{1.1em} \\[12pt]
        (5p - 4)(p - 1) &= 0 \\[6pt]
        p &= \dfrac{4}{5},\hspace{0.25em} 1
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence $4/5$
        and $1$ are the only critical numbers of $\mathrm{P}(X = 8)$.\
        Therefore, $\mathrm{P}(X = 8)$ is a maximum for
         $p = \dfrac{4}{5}$

    2.  Using the formula for the expected value of
        $X \sim \mathrm{B}(10,4/5)$, we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        4

        \mathrm{E}[X] &= 10\times\dfrac{4}{5} \\[8pt]
        &= 8
        \end{aligned}
        $$
