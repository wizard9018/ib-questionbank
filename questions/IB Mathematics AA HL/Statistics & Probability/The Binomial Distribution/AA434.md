# AA434

**Maximum mark: 6**

A multiple choice exam consists of $20$ questions. Each question has
four answers. Only one of the answers is correct. For each question,
Jack randomly chooses one of the four answers.

1.  Find the expected number of questions Jack answers correctly.
    [2 marks]

2.  Find the probability that Jack:

    1.  answers exactly four questions correctly;

    2.  answers more than four questions correctly. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X$ be the number of questions Jack answers correctly.
    We have $X \sim \mathrm{B}(20,1/4)$.

    Hence, using the formula for the expected value of $X$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{E}[X] &= np \\[6pt]
    &= 20\times\dfrac{1}{4} \hspace{3em} \\[7pt]
    &= 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have $X \sim \mathrm{B}(20,1/4)$.
    1.  Hence we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(X = 4) &= \text{binomPdf}(20,1/4,4) \hspace{1.35em} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.190
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Hence we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.15em} \mathrm{P}(X > 4) &= \text{binomCdf}(20,1/4,5,20) \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.585
        \end{aligned}
        $$
