# AA773

**Maximum mark: 8**

AB Technologies produces DC motors of which $5\%$ are defective. The
motors are packaged into boxes, each box containing $60$ motors. A box
is selected at random.

1.  Find the probability that the box contains:
    1.  exactly one defective motor;

    2.  at least two defective motors. [4 marks]

2.  Given that there are at least two defective motors in the box, find
    the probability that there are at most four defective motors.
    [2 marks]

James orders 5 boxes of motors for his company.

3.  Find the probability that James will receive at least one
    but no more than fifteen defective motors in his order. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Let $X$ be the number of defective motors in the box.\
        We have $X \sim \mathrm{B}(60,0.05)$.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm P(X=1) &= \text{binomPdf}(60,0.05,1) \\[12pt]
        &\approx 0.145
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(X \geq 2) &= \text{binomCdf}(60,0.05,2,60) \\[12pt]
        &\approx 0.808
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Using the conditional probability formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X \leq 4\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em} X \geq 2) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(2 \leq X \leq 4)}{\mathrm{P}(X \geq 2)} \\[12pt]
    &= \dfrac{\text{binomCdf}(60,0.05,2,4)}{\mathrm{P}(X \geq 2)} \\[12pt]
    &\approx \dfrac{0.628}{0.808} \\[12pt]
    &\approx 0.777
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Let $Y$ be the number of defective motors in 5 boxes.\
    We have $Y \sim \mathrm{B}(300,0.05)$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(1 \leq Y \leq 15) &= \text{binomCdf}(300,0.05,1,15)\hspace{1.8em} \\[12pt]
    &\approx 0.568
    \end{aligned}
    $$
