# AA989

**Maximum mark: 8**

Consider the function $f$ such that

$$ 

\begin{aligned}

     f(x) = \left\{

           \begin{array}{ll}

         \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x\, ,      & a \leq x \leq 3 \\[8pt]

         \hspace{0.8em}0\hspace{1.8em}, & \mathrm{otherwise} 

           \end{array}

         \right.

\end{aligned}

$$

   where $a \in \mathbb{R}^+$.

1. Given that $f$ defines a probability density function for a continuous random variable $X$, find the value of $a$. Give your answer to four significant figures.[5 marks]

2. Hence find $\mathrm{Var}(X)$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Given that $f$ defines a probability density function, we must have 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \int_{a}^{3} f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x &= 1 \\[8pt]

    \int_{a}^{3} \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x\,\mathrm{d}x &= 1

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Using the formula for the integral of $a^x$, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \int a^x\,\mathrm{d}x &= \dfrac{1}{\ln a}a^x + C \\[8pt]

    \int_{a}^{3} \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x\,\mathrm{d}x &= \dfrac{1}{\ln \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)}\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x\,\Bigg|_{a}^{3} \\[8pt]

    1 &= \dfrac{1}{\ln \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)}\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x\,\Bigg|_{a}^{3} \\[8pt]

    \ln \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right) &= \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^3 - \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^a \\[8pt]

    \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^a &= \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^3 - \ln \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right) \\[24pt]

    &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{a \approx 0.2896} \hspace{3em}\text{[Using G.D.C.]}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. First we must find $\mathrm{E}(X)$. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{E}(X) &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} xf(x)\,\mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]

    &= \int_{0.2896}^{3} x\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x \mathrm{d}x \\[8pt]

    \mathrm{E}(X) &\approx 1.243

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Hence we can find the variance:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{Var}(X) &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x^2f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x - \left(\mathrm{E}(X)\right)^2 \\[8pt]

    &= \int_{0.2896}^{3} x^2\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x \mathrm{d}x - (1.243)^2 \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-3.7em}\boxed{\mathrm{Var}(X) \approx 0.519}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
