# AA375

**Maximum mark: 15**

A continuous random variable $X$ has a probability density function $f$
given by
$$
\hspace{8.1em} f(x) = \left\{
\begin{array}{cl}
\dfrac{x^3}{a} + \dfrac{1}{b}, & 0 \leq x \leq 3, \hspace{1em} \text{where $a,b\in\mathbb{Z}^+$}, \\[9pt]
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{array} \right.
$$
It is given that $\mathrm{P}(X \leq 2) = 0.31481$,
correct to five decimal places.

1.  Show that $a = 27$ and $b = 12$, correct to the nearest integer.
    [5 marks]

2.  Find $\mathrm{E}[X]$. [2 marks]

3.  Find $\mathrm{Var}(X)$. [2 marks]

4.  Find the median of $X$. [3 marks]

Twelve independent observations of $X$ are now taken.

5.  A random variable $Y$ is the number of observations of $X$
    such that $X \geq 2$.

    1.  Find $\mathrm{E}[Y]$.
    2.  Find $\mathrm{P}(Y\geq4)$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_0^3 \bigg[\dfrac{x^3}{a} + \dfrac{1}{b}\bigg]\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{d}x &= 1 \\[5pt]
    \bigg[\dfrac{x^4}{4a} + \dfrac{x}{b}\bigg]\bigg|_0^3 &= 1 \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{81}{4a} + \dfrac{3}{b} &= 1
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X \leq 2) &= 0.31481 \hspace{2.5em} \\[12pt]
    \int_0^2 \bigg[\dfrac{x^3}{a} + \dfrac{1}{b}\bigg]\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{d}x &= 0.31481 \\[5pt]
    \bigg[\dfrac{x^4}{4a} + \dfrac{x}{b}\bigg]\bigg|_0^2 &= 0.31481 \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{4}{a} + \dfrac{2}{b} &= 0.31481
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, solving the system of equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $a$ and $b$,
    we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8.4em} a \approx 27\hspace{0.525em} \text{ and } \hspace{0.525em}b \approx 12 \hspace{2em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the formula for the expected value of $X$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{9.1em} \mathrm{E}[X] &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} xf(x)\,\mathrm{d}x \\[6pt]
    &= \int_0^3 x\bigg[\dfrac{x^3}{27} + \dfrac{1}{12}\bigg]\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{d}x \\[6pt]
    &= \int_0^3 \bigg[\dfrac{x^4}{27} + \dfrac{x}{12}\bigg]\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{d}x \\[14pt]
    &= 2.175 \quad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the formula for the expected value of $X^2$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.2em} \mathrm{E}[X^2] &= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x^2f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x \\[6pt]
    &= \int_0^3 x^2\bigg[\dfrac{x^3}{27} + \dfrac{1}{12}\bigg]\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{d}x \\[6pt]
    &= \int_0^3 \bigg[\dfrac{x^5}{27} + \dfrac{x^2}{12}\bigg]\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{d}x \\[14pt]
    &= 5.25 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence,
    using the formula for the variance of $X$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{Var}(X) &= \mathrm{E}[X^2] - (\mathrm{E}[X])^2 \\[6pt]
    &= 5.25 - (2.175)^2 \\[7pt]
    &\approx 0.519 \hspace{3em} [= 0.519375] \hspace{0.2em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  If we solve the equation $\mathrm{P}(X \leq m) = 0.5$ for $m$, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.5em} \int_0^m \bigg[\dfrac{x^3}{27} + \dfrac{1}{12}\bigg]\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{d}x &= 0.5  \hspace{3em} [\text{since $0 < m < 3$}] \\[4pt]
    \bigg[\dfrac{x^4}{108} + \dfrac{x}{12}\bigg]\bigg|_0^m &= 0.5  \\[4pt]
    \dfrac{m^4}{108} + \dfrac{m}{12} &= 0.5 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{12.9em} m \approx 2.39 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

5.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X \geq 2) &= 1- \mathrm{P}(X \leq 2) \hspace{0.9em} \\[6pt]
    &= 1 - 0.31481 \\[6pt]
    &= 0.68519
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence
    $Y\hspace{-0.1em}\sim \mathrm{B}(12,0.68519)$.
    1.  Using the formula for the expected value of $Y$, we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{E}[Y] &= np \\[6pt]
        &= (12)(0.68519) \hspace{1.1em} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 8.22
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3.2em} \mathrm{P}(Y \geq 4) &= \text{binomCdf}(12,0.68519,4,12) \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.998
        \end{aligned}
        $$
