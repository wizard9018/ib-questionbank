# AA677

**Maximum mark: 7**

A continuous random variable $X$ has a probability density function $f$
given by
$$
f(x) = \left\{
\begin{array}{cl}
\dfrac{\ln x}{x^2}, & \hspace{0.5em}1 \leq x < \infty, \\[6pt]
0, & \hspace{0.5em}\text{otherwise}.
\end{array} \right.
$$

1.  Find $\mathrm{P}(X < e^3)$. [5 marks]

2.  Hence, or otherwise, find $\mathrm{P}(X \geq e^3)$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the integration by parts formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X < e^3) &= \int_{-\infty}^{e^3} f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x \\[6pt]
    &= \int_1^{e^3} \dfrac{\ln x}{x^2}\,\mathrm{d}x \\[6pt]
    &= \int_1^{e^3} u\,\mathrm{d}v \hspace{2em} \big[u = \ln x,\hspace{0.5em} \mathrm{d}v = \dfrac{1}{x^2}\,\mathrm{d}x\big] \\[6pt]
    &= \Big[uv\Big]\bigg|_1^{e^3} - \int_1^{e^3} v\,\mathrm{d}u \hspace{2em} \big[\textstyle v = -\dfrac{1}{x},\hspace{0.5em} \mathrm{d}u = \dfrac{1}{x}\,\mathrm{d}x\big] \\[6pt]
    &= \Big[\hspace{-0.2em}-\dfrac{\ln x}{x}\Big]\bigg|_1^{e^3} - \int_1^{e^3} \left[\hspace{-0.2em}-\dfrac{1}{x^2}\right]\hspace{0.025em}\mathrm{d}x \\[9pt]
    &= \Big[\hspace{-0.2em}-\dfrac{\ln x}{x}\Big]\bigg|_1^{e^3} - \left[\dfrac{1}{x}\right]\bigg|_1^{e^3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &= \left[-\dfrac{\ln x}{x}-\dfrac{1}{x}\right]\bigg|_1^{e^3} \\[14pt]
    &= \left[-\dfrac{3}{e^3}-\dfrac{1}{e^3}\right] - \Big[-1\Big] \\[13pt]
    &= 1-\dfrac{4}{e^3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \mathrm{P}(X \geq e^3) &= 1 - \mathrm{P}(X < e^3) \\[9pt]
    &= 1 - \left(1-\dfrac{4}{e^3}\right) \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{4}{e^3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
