# AA382

**Maximum mark: 16**

The continuous random variable $X$ has a probability density function
given by
$$
f(x) = \left\{\begin{array}{ccl}
h\cos\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi x}{12}\Big)\hspace{-0.1em}    &\text{if}& |x| \leq 6, \\[9pt]
0    &\text{if}& |x| > 6.
\end{array}\right.
$$

1.  Find the value of $h$. [4 marks]

2.  By considering the graph of $f$, write down the mean, median and
    mode of $X$.[3 marks]

3.
    1.  Show that $\mathrm{P}(-2 \leq X \leq 0) = 0.25$.

    2.  Hence state the interquartile range of $X$. [6 marks]

4.  Calculate
    $\mathrm{P}(X \geq -2 \hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em} X \leq 2)$.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x &= 1 \hspace{6em} \\[3pt]
    \int_{-6}^6 h\cos\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi x}{12}\Big)\hspace{0.1em}\mathrm{d}x &= 1 \\[4pt]
    \dfrac{12h}{\pi} \Big[\sin\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi x}{12}\Big)\Big] \Big|_{-6}^6 &= 1 \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{12h}{\pi}\Big[\sin\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big)\hspace{-0.15em} - \sin\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\hspace{-0.2em}-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big)\Big] \hspace{-0.15em} &= 1 \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{12h}{\pi}\left[1 - (-1)\right]&= 1 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{5.6em} h = \dfrac{\pi}{24}
    $$

2.  If we sketch the graph of $y = f(x)$, we have

    ![4d0ce004711da07012e065ef6acf79ba30c52d7b.svg](images/4d0ce004711da07012e065ef6acf79ba30c52d7b.svg)

    Hence we get
     $\text{mean} = 0,\hspace{0.25em} \text{median} = 0,\hspace{0.25em} \text{mode} = 0$

3.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(-2 \leq X \leq 0) &= \int_{-2}^0 \dfrac{\pi}{24}\cos\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi x}{12}\Big)\hspace{0.1em}\mathrm{d}x \\[3pt]
        &= \dfrac{1}{2}\Big[\sin\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi x}{12}\Big)\Big] \Big|_{-2}^0 \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{1}{2}\Big[\sin\hspace{0.05em}(0) - \sin\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\hspace{-0.2em}-\dfrac{\pi}{6}\Big)\Big] \hspace{2.4em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{1}{2}[0 - (-1/2)] \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{1}{4} \\[11pt]
        &= 0.25
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using part (c) (i) and the symmetry of the graph of $y = f(x)$,
        we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{8.7em} q_1 &= -2 \hspace{3em} [\text{lower quartile of $X$}] \\[6pt]
        q_3 &= 2\phantom{-} \hspace{3em} [\text{upper quartile of $X$}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{IQR} &= q_3 - q_1 \\[6pt]
        &= 2 - (-2) \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
        &= 4
        \end{aligned}
        $$

4.  Using the conditional probability formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X \geq -2 \hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em} X \leq 2) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(X \geq -2 \hspace{0.15em}\cap\hspace{0.15em} X \leq 2)}{\mathrm{P}(X \leq 2)} \hspace{0.6em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(-2 \leq X \leq 2)}{\mathrm{P}(X \leq 2)} \\[7pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.5}{0.75} \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{2}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
