# AA794

**Maximum mark: 13**

Ali works at an amusement park on a small island in Indonesia. Ali
drives from his home to the ferry depature terminal and then catches the
ferry to the island. The time that it takes him, $T_1$ minutes, to drive
from his home to the ferry is normally distributed with
$T_1 \sim \mathrm{N}(16, 4^2)$.

Ali always leaves his house at $07$:$40$. The first ferry that he can
catch departs at $08$:$00$.

1.  Find the probability that it will take Ali between $20$ to $30$
    minutes to drive to the ferry. Round your answer to $4$ significant
    figures. [2 marks]

The length of time, $T_2$ minutes, of the ferry journey to the island is
normally distributed with $T_2 \sim \mathrm{N}(25, \sigma^2)$. The
probability that the ferry journey takes less than $30$ minutes is
$0.9520$.

2.  Find $\sigma$. Round your answer to $1$ significant figure.
    [3 marks]

3.  Find the probability that the ferry journey takes less than
    $20$ minutes. [2 marks]

If Ali misses the first ferry, he can take another one which departs at
$08$:$10$. He must arrive at work by $08$:$30$ to be on time. He will
not make it to the island if he misses both ferries. The variables $T_1$
and $T_2$ are independent.

4.  Find the probability that Ali will arrive to work on time.
    Round your answer to $4$ significant figures. [4 marks]

This year, Ali will go to work on $214$ days.

5.  Calculate the number of days Ali is expected to arrive at
    work on time. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(20 < T_1 < 30) &= \text{normCdf}(20, 30, 16, 4) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 0.1584 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(T_2 < 30) &= 0.9520 \\[10pt]
    \mathrm{P}\left( Z < \dfrac{30-25}{\sigma}\right) &= 0.9520 \\[10pt]
    \dfrac{30-25}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.9520) \\[10pt]
    \dfrac{5}{\sigma} &\approx 1.66456 \hspace{3.25em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{0.5em}\\[10pt]
    &\hspace{-0.525em}\sigma \approx 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The probability that the ferry journey takes less than $20$ minutes
    is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(T_2 < 20) &= \text{normCdf}(0, 20, 25, 3) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 0.0478 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Let $F_1$ represent the event that Ali gets on the first ferry,\
     $F_2$ represent the event that
    Ali gets on the second ferry,\
    $O$
    represent the event that Ali
    arrives on time.

    We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(F_1) &= \mathrm{P}(T_1 < 20) \hspace{3em} \\[10pt]
    &= \text{normCdf}(0, 20, 16, 4) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 0.841313... \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{1em}\\[10pt]
    \mathrm{P}(F_2) &= \mathrm{P}(20\leq T_1 <30) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 0.1584 \hspace{3em} [\text{found in part (a)}] \\[10pt]
    \mathrm{P}(O|F_1) &= \mathrm{P}(T_2 < 30) \\[10pt]
    &= 0.9520 \hspace{3em} [\text{given for part (b)}] \\[10pt]
    \mathrm{P}(O|F_2) &= \mathrm{P}(T_2 < 20) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 0.0478 \hspace{3em} [\text{found in part (c)}]\\
    \intertext{Therefore, we get}
    \mathrm{P}(O) &=  \mathrm{P}(F_1) \times \mathrm{P}(O|F_1) + \mathrm{P}(F_2) \times \mathrm{P}(O|F_2) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 0.841313 \times 0.9520 + 0.1584 \times 0.0478 \\[10pt]
    &\approx 0.8085 \\[-2cm]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Let $X$ be the number of days Ali arrives on time.\
    We have $X \sim \mathrm{B}(214,0.8085)$.\
    Hence, using the formula for the expected value of $X$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{E}(X) &= np \\[10pt]
    &\approx (214)(0.8085) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 173
    \end{aligned}
    $$
