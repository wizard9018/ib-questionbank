# AA370

**Maximum mark: 6**

It is given that three in ten men take less than $220$ minutes to run a
marathon. It is also known that one in five men
take more than $274$ minutes to run the same
marathon.

Assume that the time taken, in minutes, for men to run a marathon is
modelled by a normal distribution.

Find the mean and standard deviation.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let $X$ be the time taken, in minutes, for a man to run the marathon.

We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}(\mu,\sigma^2)$ and $Z \sim \mathrm{N}(0,1)$.

Hence we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{P}(X < 220) &= 0.3 \\[11pt]
\mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z < \dfrac{220-\mu}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.3 \\[5pt]
\dfrac{220-\mu}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.3) \hspace{0.25em} \\[7pt]
\dfrac{220-\mu}{\sigma} &\approx -0.524 \\[11pt]
\mu - 0.524\sigma &\approx 220
\end{aligned}
$$
and

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{P}(X > 274) &= 0.2 \\[11pt]
\mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z > \dfrac{274-\mu}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.2 \\[5pt]
\dfrac{274-\mu}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.8) \hspace{0.25em} \\[7pt]
\dfrac{274-\mu}{\sigma} &\approx 0.842 \\[11pt]
\mu + 0.842\sigma &\approx 274  \\
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence,
solving the system of linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $\mu$ and
$\sigma$, we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{10.4em} \mu \approx 241 \text{ min}\hspace{0.525em} \text{ and } \hspace{0.525em}\sigma \approx 39.5 \text{ min} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
\end{aligned}
$$
