# AA432

**Maximum mark: 6**

A random variable $X$ is normally distributed with $\mu = 120$ and
$\sigma = 15$.

Find the interquartile range of $X$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

If we draw the normal curve for $X$, we have

![8a52d7a99181e065b38d9ab0218832e1b3b3402d.svg](images/8a52d7a99181e065b38d9ab0218832e1b3b3402d.svg)

Hence, using $X \sim \mathrm{N}(120,15^2)$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
q_1 &= \text{invNorm}(0.25,120,15) \approx 109.9 \\[6pt]
q_3 &= \text{invNorm}(0.75,120,15) \approx 130.1
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence we
obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2em} \text{IQR} &= q_3 - q_1 \\[6pt]
&= 130.1 - 109.9 \\[6pt]
&= 20.2
\end{aligned}
$$
