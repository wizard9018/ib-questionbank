# AA397

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let the random variable $X$ be normally distributed with mean $16$, as
shown in the following diagram.

![ffdc26c8f7b3d1f4ea14a7214377676d556d4b31.svg](images/ffdc26c8f7b3d1f4ea14a7214377676d556d4b31.svg)

The shaded region between $16$ and $20$ represents $35$% of the
distribution.

1.  Find $\mathrm{P}(X>20)$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the standard deviation of $X$. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.4em} \mathrm{P}(X > 20) &= \mathrm{P}(X > 16) - \mathrm{P}(16 < X \leq 20) \\[6pt]
    &= 0.5 - 0.35 \\[6pt]
    &=0.15
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}(16,\sigma^2)$ and
    $Z  \sim \mathrm{N}(0,1)$.

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X \leq 20) &= 1 - \mathrm{P}(X > 20) \hspace{1.25em} \\[18pt]
    \mathrm{P}(X \leq 20) &= 1 - 0.15 \\[11pt]
    \mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z \leq \dfrac{20-16}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.85 \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{4}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.85) \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{4}{\sigma} &\approx 1.036 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{1.05em} \sigma \approx 3.86
    $$
