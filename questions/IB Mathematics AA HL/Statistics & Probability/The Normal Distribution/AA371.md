# AA371

**Maximum mark: 8**

Domino Sugar Bags are packaged by machines. The weights $X$, in grams,
of bags of sugar can be modelled by a
normal distribution where $X \sim \mathrm{N}(\mu,\sigma^2)$. A
bag of sugar is considered to be
underweight if it weights less than $4480$ grams.

1.  Given that $\mu = 4536$ and $\sigma = 25$, find the probability that
    a randomly selected bag of sugar
    is underweight. [2 marks]

The manufacturer makes the decision that the probability that a bag of
sugar is underweight should be
$0.001$. To do this $\mu$ is increased and $\sigma$ is kept unchanged.

2.  Calculate the new value of $\mu$, rounding your answer to
    the nearest gram. [3 marks]

The manufacturer is happy with the decision that the probability that a
bag of sugar is underweight should be
$0.001,$ but is unhappy with the way in which
this was achieved. The machines are
now adjusted to reduce $\sigma$ and return $\mu$ back
to $4536$ grams.

3.  Calculate the new value of $\sigma$, rounding your answer
    to the nearest gram. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}(4536,25^2)$.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \mathrm{P}(X < 4480) &= \text{normCdf}(-\infty, 4480, 4536, 25) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.0125
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}(\mu,25^2)$ and $Z \sim \mathrm{N}(0,1)$.
    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X < 4480) &= 0.001 \hspace{6.6em} \\[12pt]
    \mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z < \dfrac{4480-\mu}{25}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.001 \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{4480 - \mu}{25} &= \text{invNorm}(0.001) \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{4480 - \mu}{25} &\approx -3.09 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{14.3em} \mu \approx 4557 \text{ grams} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

3.  We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}(4536,\sigma^2)$ and
    $Z \sim \mathrm{N}(0,1)$.

    Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X < 4480) &= 0.001 \hspace{8em} \\[12pt]
    \mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z < \dfrac{4480 - 4536}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.001 \\[6pt]
    -\dfrac{56}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.001) \\[8pt]
    -\dfrac{56}{\sigma} & \approx -3.09 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{13.3em} \sigma \approx 18 \text{ grams} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
