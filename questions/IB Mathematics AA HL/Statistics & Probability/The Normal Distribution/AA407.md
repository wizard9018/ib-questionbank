# AA407

**Maximum mark: 5**

The random variable $X$ is normally distributed with a mean of $90$. The
following diagram shows the normal curve for $X$.

![970ac43c386b584507ca57023f99c8602810fc8b.svg](images/970ac43c386b584507ca57023f99c8602810fc8b.svg)

Let $R$ be the shaded region under the curve, to the left of $84$. The
area of $R$ is $0.22$.

1.  Write down $\mathrm{P}(X < 84)$. [1 mark]

2.  Find $\mathrm{P}(84 < X < 90)$. [2 marks]

3.  Find $\mathrm{P}(84 < X < 96)$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\mathrm{P}(X < 84) = 0.22$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(84 < X < 90) &= \mathrm{P}(X < 90) - \mathrm{P}(X \leq 84) \hspace{0.9em} \\[6pt]
    &= 0.5 - 0.22 \\[6pt]
    &= 0.28
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the symmetry of the normal curve for $X$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(84 < X < 96) &= 2\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(84 < X < 90) \hspace{1.1em} \\[6pt]
    &= 2\times0.28 \\[6pt]
    &= 0.56
    \end{aligned}
    $$
