# AA373

**Maximum mark: 13**

A survey is conducted in a large technology company. It is found that
$25\%$ of the employees drink more than $30$ cups
of coffee and $4\%$ of the employees drink less
than $12$ cups of coffee during a five-day week. The number of cups
of $\text{coffee}$ consumed by the
employees of the company can be modelled by a
normal $\text{distribution}$ with mean
$\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$.

1.
    1.  Determine two linear equations satisfied by $\mu$ and $\sigma$.

    2.  Find the values of $\mu$ and $\sigma$, giving your answers
        correct to one decimal place. [6 marks]

2.  Find the probability that an employee drinks more that $40$ cups of
    coffee during a five-day week. [2 marks]

On Monday mornings, there are team meetings held by project managers.

3.  There are $12$ employees attending a particular meeting.
    1.  Find the probability that at least three of the employees drink
        more than $40$ cups of coffee during a
        five-day week.

    2.  Given that at least one of the employees drinks more than $40$
        cups of coffee, find the probability that
        there are fewer than
        three $\text{employees}$ drinking
        more than $40$ cups of coffee during a five-day week.
        [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X$ be the number of coffee cups consumed by an employee of the
    company.

    1.  We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}(\mu,\sigma^2)$ and
        $Z \sim \mathrm{N}(0,1)$.

        Hence we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(X < 12) &= 0.04 \\[10pt]
        \mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z < \dfrac{12-\mu}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.04 \\[4pt]
        \dfrac{12-\mu}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.04) \hspace{1.7em} \\[6pt]
        \dfrac{12-\mu}{\sigma} &\approx -1.751 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \mu - 1.751\sigma \approx 12 \hspace{4.6em}
        $$
        and
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(X > 30) &= 0.25 \\[10pt]
        \mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z > \dfrac{30-\mu}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.25 \\[4pt]
        \dfrac{30-\mu}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.75) \hspace{1.7em} \\[6pt]
        \dfrac{30-\mu}{\sigma} &\approx 0.674 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \mu + 0.674\sigma \approx 30  \hspace{4.6em}
        $$

    2.  Solving the system of linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $\mu$ and
        $\sigma$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{5.6em} \mu \approx 25.0 \text{ cups}\hspace{0.525em} \text{ and } \hspace{0.525em}\sigma \approx 7.4 \text{ cups} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}(25.0,7.4^2)$
    Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.3em} \mathrm{P}(X > 40) &= \text{normCdf}(40, \infty, 25.0, 7.4) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.0213
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Let $Y$ be the number of employees attending the meeting who drink
    more than $40$ cups of coffee. We have
    $Y \hspace{-0.1em}\sim \mathrm{B}(12,0.0213)$.$\\[12pt]$

    1.  Hence we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.25em} \mathrm{P}(Y\geq3) &= \text{binomCdf}(12,0.0213,3,12) \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.00184
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Hence, using the conditional probability formula, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(Y < 3 \hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em} Y \geq 1) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(Y < 3  \hspace{0.1em}\cap\hspace{0.1em} Y \geq 1)}{\mathrm{P}(Y \geq 1)} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(Y = 1 \hspace{0.1em}\cup\hspace{0.1em} Y = 2)}{\mathrm{P}(Y \geq 1)} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(Y = 1) + \mathrm{P}(Y = 2)}{1 - \mathrm{P}(Y = 0)} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{\text{binomPdf}(12,0.0213,1) + \text{binomPdf}(12,0.0213,2)}{1 - \text{binomPdf}(12,0.0213,0)} \hspace{0.5em} \\[11pt]
        &\approx 0.992
        \end{aligned}
        $$
