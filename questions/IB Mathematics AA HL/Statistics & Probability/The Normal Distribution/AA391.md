# AA391

**Maximum mark: 5**

The heights of giraffes in a savanna can be modelled by a normal
distribution with mean $5.2$ metres and standard
deviation $\sqrt{0.1}$ metres.

1.  Find the probability that a giraffe selected at random is at least
    $5$ metres tall.[2 marks]

Twelve giraffes are independently selected at random and their heights
recorded.

2.  Find the probability that more than nine of these giraffes
    are at least $5$ metres tall.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $H$ be the height, in metres, of a giraffe in the savanna.
    We have $H \sim \mathrm{N}(5.2, 0.1)$.

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em} \mathrm{P}(H \geq 5) &= \text{normCdf}(5, \infty, 5.2, \sqrt{0.1}) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.736
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $X$ be the number of selected giraffes whose height is at least
    $5$ metres.

    We have $X \sim \mathrm{B}(12, 0.736)$.
    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.4em} \mathrm{P}(X > 9) &= \text{binomCdf}(12, 0.736, 10, 12) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.349
    \end{aligned}
    $$
