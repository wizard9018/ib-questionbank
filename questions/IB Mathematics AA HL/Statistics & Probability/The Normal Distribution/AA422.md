# AA422

**Maximum mark: 6**

A random variable $X$ is normally distributed with a mean of $50$ and a
standard deviation of $10$.

1.  On the following diagram, shade in the region representing
    $\mathrm{P}(X \geq 45)$. [2 marks]

    ![8f5acb14ecf70c9d048e4418c3f5098c2ce5e6cd.svg](images/8f5acb14ecf70c9d048e4418c3f5098c2ce5e6cd.svg)

2.  Find $\mathrm{P}(X \geq 45)$. [2 marks]

3.  Given that $\mathrm{P}(X \leq k) = 0.8$, find the value of $k$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we shade in the region representing $\mathrm{P}(X \geq 45)$, we
    get

    ![809f632e67f6c7263358cdcb66497105f451b0d4.svg](images/809f632e67f6c7263358cdcb66497105f451b0d4.svg)

2.  We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}(50,10^2)$.
    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X \geq 45) &= \text{normCdf}(45,\infty,50,10) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.691
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}(50,10^2)$.
    Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X \leq k) &= 0.8 \\[6pt]
    k &= \text{invNorm}(0.8, 50,10) \hspace{0.8em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    k \approx 58.4 \hspace{4.1em}
    $$
