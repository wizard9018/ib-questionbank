# AA403

**Maximum mark: 15**

A health study on body fat percentage is conducted on adult male
participants in two dietary groups. The
participants in Group A consume less than $52$
grams of sugar daily. Their body fat percentages
are normally distributed with mean $15$% and
standard deviation $3$%.

1.  A person is selected at random from Group A. Find the probability
    that their body fat percentage is greater
    than $20$%. [2 marks]

The participants in Group B consume more than $52$ grams of sugar daily.
Their body fat percentages are normally
distributed with mean $24$% and standard
deviation $\sigma\hspace{0.05em}\%$.

2.  The probability that the body fat percentage of a person in
    Group B is less than $20$% is $0.0548$, find
    the value of $\sigma$. [4 marks]

In the study, there are $160$ participants in Group A and $80$
participants in Group B.

3.  A randomly selected participant has a body fat percentage
    greater than $20$%. Find the probability that
    the participant is in Group A. [6 marks]

4.  Twelve of the participants with body fat percentages
    greater than $20$% are selected at random.
    Find the probability that at least two of them
    are in Group A. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X$ be the body fat percentage of a participant in Group A.
    We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}(15,3^2)$.

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.1em} \mathrm{P}(X > 20) &= \text{normCdf}(20,\infty,15,3) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.0478
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Let $Y$ be the body fat percentage of a participant in Group B.
    We have $Y\hspace{-0.1em} \sim \mathrm{N}(24,\sigma^2)$ and
    $Z \sim \mathrm{N}(0,1)$. Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(Y < 20) &= 0.0548 \\[12pt]
    \mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z < \dfrac{20-24}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.0548 \\[6pt]
    -\dfrac{4}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.0548) \hspace{2em} \\[8pt]
    -\dfrac{4}{\sigma} &= -1.6 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \sigma = 2.5\% \hspace{0.3em}
    $$

3.  Using the conditional probability formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(\text{A} \hspace{0.15em} | \hspace{0.15em} \text{BFP} > 20) &=  \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(\text{A})\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(X > 20)}{\mathrm{P}(\text{A})\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(X > 20) + \mathrm{P}(\text{B})\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(Y > 20)} \\[16pt]
    &= \dfrac{\raisebox{0.65em}{$\dfrac{160}{240}\times0.0478$}}{\raisebox{-0.75em}{$\dfrac{160}{240}\times0.0478 + \dfrac{80}{240}\times(1 - 0.0548)$}} \\[16pt]
    &\, \\
    &\approx 0.0919
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Let $S$ be the number of selected participants that are in Group A.
    We have $S \sim \mathrm{B}(12,0.0919)$. Hence we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \mathrm{P}(S \geq 2) &= \text{binomCdf}(12,0.0919,2,12) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.304
    \end{aligned}
    $$
