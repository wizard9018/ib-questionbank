# AA443

**Maximum mark: 15**

A garden bed has a large number of flowers. The heights of the flowers
are $\text{normally}$ distributed with a mean of
$43$ cm and a standard deviation of
$6$ cm. A flower is classified as tall if it is
taller than $48$ cm.

1.  A flower is selected at random.
    1.  Find the probability that this flower is tall.

    2.  Given that the flower is tall, find the probability it is taller
        than $55$ cm.[6 marks]

2.  Two flowers are selected at random. Find the probability they are
    both tall.[3 marks]

3.  Five hundred flowers are selected at random.
    1.  Find the probability that at least $100$ of these $500$ flowers
        are tall.

    2.  Find the expected number of tall flowers among these $500$
        flowers. [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $H$ be the height, in centimetres, of a flower in the garden
    bed.

    We have $H \sim \mathrm{N}(43,6^2)$.

    1.  Hence we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(\text{tall flower}) &= \mathrm{P}(H  > 48) \\[6pt]
        &= \text{normCdf}(48,\infty,43,6) \hspace{1.65em} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.202
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the conditional probability formula, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(H > 55\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}\text{tall flower}) &= \mathrm{P}(H > 55\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em} H > 48) \\[8pt]
        &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(H > 55)}{\mathrm{P}(H > 48)} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{\text{normCdf}(55,\infty,43,6)}{\text{normCdf}(48,\infty,43,6)} \hspace{5.25em} \\[6pt]
        &\approx \dfrac{0.0228}{0.2023} \\[10pt]
        &\approx 0.112
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Using the independence of flowers, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(\text{both tall flowers}) &= \mathrm{P}(\text{tall flower})\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(\text{tall flower}) \\[6pt]
    &= (0.202)^2 \\[7pt]
    &\approx 0.0408
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Let $X$ be the number of tall flowers among the selected $500$
    flowers.

    We have $X \sim \mathrm{B}(500,0.202)$.
    1.  Hence we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.55em} \mathrm{P}(X \geq 100) &= \text{binomCdf}(500,0.202,100,500) \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.562
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the formula for the expected value of $X$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{E}[X] &= np \\[6pt]
        &= (500)(0.202) \\[6pt]
        &= 101 \text{ tall flowers} \hspace{2em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
