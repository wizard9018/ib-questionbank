# AA440

**Maximum mark: 6**

The weights $W$, in grams, of tea bags are normally distributed with a
mean of $3.5$ grams and a standard
deviation of $0.53$ grams. A tea bag is
considered small if it weights less
than $w$ grams.

1.  Given that $5.2$ % of tea bags are small,
    find $w$. [3 marks]

2.  A selected tea bag is small. Find the probability that it weighs at
    least $2.25$ grams. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have $W \sim \mathrm{N}(3.5, 0.53^2)$.
    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(W < w) &= 0.052 \\[6pt]
    w &= \text{invNorm}(0.052,3.5,0.53) \hspace{0.2em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    w \approx 2.64 \text{ grams} \hspace{2.4em}
    $$

2.  Using the conditional probability formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(W \geq 2.25 \hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em} W < 2.6383...) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(2.25 \leq W < 2.6383...)}{\mathrm{P}(W < 2.6383...)} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\text{normCdf}(2.25,2.6383...,3.5,0.53)}{0.052} \hspace{2em} \\[8pt]
    &\approx \dfrac{0.0428252...}{0.052} \\[13pt]
    &\approx 0.824
    \end{aligned}
    $$
