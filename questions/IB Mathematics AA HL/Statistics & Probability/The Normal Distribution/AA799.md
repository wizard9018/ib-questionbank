# AA799

**Maximum mark: 15**

The volumes, in mL, of olive oil bottles can be modelled by a normal
distribution, with mean $508$ mL and standard
deviation $12$ mL.

1.  Find the probability that a randomly selected bottle has a volume
    less than $500$ mL. Round your answer to $3$
    significant figures. [2 marks]

2.  The probability that a randomly selected bottle has a volume greater
    than $v$  mL is $0.366$. Find the value of
    $v$. [2 marks]

3.  A bottle is randomly selected. Given that the bottle has a volume
    greater than $500$ mL, find the probability
    that it has a volume greater than $520$ mL.
    Round your answer to $3$ significant figures. [3 marks]

4.  From a random sample of $200$ bottles, determine to the nearest
    integer the number of bottles that would be expected to have a
    volume within $2$ standard deviations of the mean. [4 marks]

5.  The bottles of olive oil are delivered to supermarkets in packs of
    $30$. Determine the probability that at least $5$ bottles from a
    randomly selected pack have a volume less than
    $495$ mL. Round your answer to $3$
    significant figures. [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X$ represent the volume of an olive oil bottle.
    We are given $X \sim \mathrm{N}(508, 12^2)$. Hence the probability
    that a randomly selected bottle has a volume less than
    $500$ mL is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X < 500) &= \text{normCdf}(0, 500, 508, 12) \\[12pt]
    &\approx 0.252 \hspace{3em} [\hspace{0.1em}\text{by using G.D.C.}\hspace{0.1em}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X > v) &= 0.366 \\[12pt]
    \mathrm{P}(X \leq v) &= 1 - 0.366 \\[12pt]
    \mathrm{P}(X \leq v) &= 0.634 \\[12pt]
    v &= \text{invNorm}(0.634, 508, 12) \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-0.525em}v \approx 512 \hspace{5.52em} [\hspace{0.1em}\text{by using G.D.C.}\hspace{0.1em}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the conditional probability formula
    $\mathrm{P}(A|B) = \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(A \cap B)}{\mathrm{P}(B)}$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X > 520 \hspace{0.1em}|\hspace{0.1em} X > 500) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}\left(\{X > 520\} \cap \{X > 500\}\right)}{\mathrm{P}(X > 500)} \hspace{1em}\\[12pt]
    &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(X > 520)}{\mathrm{P}(X > 500)} \\[12pt]
    &= \dfrac{\text{normCdf}(520, \infty, 508, 12)}{\text{normCdf}(500, \infty, 508, 12)} \\[12pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.158655}{0.747508} \hspace{3em} [\hspace{0.1em}\text{by using G.D.C.}\hspace{0.1em}]\\[12pt]
    &\approx 0.212
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The probability that the volume of a randomly selected bottle lies
    within $2$ standard deviations of the mean is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(\mu - 2\sigma < X < \mu + 2\sigma) &= \mathrm{P}(508 - 24 < X < 508 + 24) \\[12pt]
    &= \mathrm{P}(484 < X < 532) \\[12pt]
    &=\text{normCdf}(484, 532, 508, 12) \\[12pt]
    &\approx 0.9545 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the expected number of bottles that have a volume lying within
    $2$ standard deviations of the mean is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{E(number of bottles)} &\approx 200 \times 0.9545\\[6pt]
    &\approx 191
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  The probability that the volume of a randomly selected bottle have a
    volume less than $495$ mL is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X < 495) &= \text{normCdf}(0,495,508, 12) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.13933 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Let $Y$ be the number of bottles that have a volume less than
    $495$ mL in the pack.

    We have $Y \sim \mathrm{B}(30, 0.13933)$. Hence the probability that
    at least $5$ bottles from the pack have a volume less than
    $495$ mL is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(Y \geq 5) &= \text{binomCdf}(30, 0.13933, 5, 30) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 0.409 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
