# AA388

**Maximum mark: 6**

The heights $X$, in metres, of mature dwarf fruit trees in a tree garden
can be modelled by a normal distribution.

It is given that $1$% of the dwarf trees are shorter than
$2.46$ m and $10$% are
taller than $2.91$ m.

Find the mean and standard deviation of the heights of the dwarf trees.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}(\mu,\sigma^2)$ and $Z \sim \mathrm{N}(0,1)$.

Hence we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{P}(X < 2.46) &= 0.01 \\[12pt]
\mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z < \dfrac{2.46-\mu}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.01 \\[6pt]
\dfrac{2.46-\mu}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.01) \\[8pt]
\dfrac{2.46-\mu}{\sigma} &\approx -2.326 \\[12pt]
\mu - 2.326\sigma &\approx 2.46
\end{aligned}
$$
and

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{P}(X > 2.91) &= 0.1 \\[12pt]
\mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.10em}\bigg(Z > \dfrac{2.91-\mu}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.1 \\[6pt]
\dfrac{2.91-\mu}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.9) \\[8pt]
\dfrac{2.91-\mu}{\sigma} &\approx 1.282 \\[12pt]
\mu + 1.282\sigma &\approx 2.91
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, solving
the system of linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $\mu$ and $\sigma$, we
obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{11em} \mu \approx 2.75 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m}\hspace{0.525em} \text{ and } \hspace{0.525em}\sigma \approx 0.125 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
\end{aligned}
$$
