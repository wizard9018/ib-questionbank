# AA695

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $X$ and $Y$ be normally distributed with
$X \sim \mathrm{N}(135,\sigma^2)$ and
$Y \sim \mathrm{N}(105,\sigma^2)$.

1.  Find $k$ such that $\mathrm{P}(Y>k)=\mathrm{P}(X<k)$.[2 marks]

2.  Given that $\mathrm{P}(X>130)=0.8944$, find
    $\mathrm{P}(90<Y<110)$.[5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Recognizing that $X$ and $Y$ have the same standard deviation, $k$
    is midway between the means of $X$ and $Y$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    k = \dfrac{135 + 105}{2} \hspace{1em}= 120
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}(135,\sigma^2)$ and
    $Z \sim \mathrm{N}(0,1)$. Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X > 130) &= 0.8944 \\[12pt]
    \mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z > \dfrac{130-135}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.8944 \\[6pt]
    -\dfrac{5}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.1056) \hspace{4em} \\[8pt]
    -\dfrac{5}{\sigma} &\approx -1.25027 \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.65em}\sigma \approx 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    We have $Y \sim \mathrm{N}(105,4^2)$. Hence we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(90<Y<110) &= \text{normCdf}(90,110,105,4) \hspace{1em}\approx 0.894
    \end{aligned}
    $$
