# AA447

**Maximum mark: 6**

The lengths $L$, in millimetres, of caterpillars in an enclosure are
normally distributed with a mean of
$12.5$ mm and a standard deviation of
$\sigma$ mm.

1.  Write down the probability that a randomly selected
    caterpillar has a length greater
    than $12.5$ mm. [1 mark]

2.  Given that $6.5$% of caterpillars have a length greater than
    $14$ mm, find:

    1.  the value of $\sigma$;
    2.  the probability that a randomly selected caterpillar has a
        length less than
        $11.5$ mm. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\mathrm{P}(L > 12.5) = 0.5$

2.
    1.  We have $L \sim \mathrm{N}(12.5,\sigma^2)$ and
        $Z \sim \mathrm{N}(0,1)$.

        Hence we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(L > 14) &= 0.065 \\[12pt]
        \mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z > \dfrac{14-12.5}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.065 \\[6pt]
        \dfrac{1.5}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.935) \hspace{4em} \\[8pt]
        \dfrac{1.5}{\sigma} &= 1.514 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \hspace{1.1em} \sigma \approx 0.991 \text{ mm}
        $$

    2.  We have $L \sim \mathrm{N}(12.5,0.991^2)$.
        Hence we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2em} \mathrm{P}(L < 11.5) &= \text{normCdf}(-\infty,11.5,12.5,0.991) \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.156
        \end{aligned}
        $$
