# AA452

**Maximum mark: 15**

A pencil manufacturer measures the length, $L$ mm, of their new batch of
pencils. The lengths of the pencils are normally distributed, where
$L \sim \mathrm{N}(110,\sigma^2)$.

1.  Find $\mathrm{P}(110 - 2\sigma < L < 110 + \sigma)$. [3 marks]

The probability that the length of a pencil is less than
$116$ mm is $0.95$.

2.  Find the value of $\sigma$. [3 marks]

All pencils with length at least $k$ mm are
classified as large pencils.

3.  A pencil is chosen at random. The probability it is a large
    pencil is $0.6$. Find the value of $k$. [3 marks]

4.
    1.  A pencil is chosen at random from the large pencils. Find the
        probability that the length of this pencil is less than
        $111$ mm.

    2.  Twenty pencils are chosen at random from the large pencils.
        Find the probability that at least three
        pencils have a length less than
        $111$ mm. [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have $L \sim \mathrm{N}(110,\sigma^2)$ and
    $Z \sim \mathrm{N}(0,1)$.

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(110 - 2\sigma < L < 110 + \sigma) &= \mathrm{P}(- 2\sigma < L-110 < \sigma) \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
    &= \mathrm{P}(- 2 < Z < 1) \\[6pt]
    &= \text{normCdf}(-2,1,0,1) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.819
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(L < 116) &= 0.95 \\[12pt]
    \mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z < \dfrac{116-110}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.95 \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{6}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.95) \hspace{4.5em} \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{6}{\sigma} &= 1.645 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.85em} \sigma \approx 3.65 \text{ mm}
    $$

3.  We have $L \sim \mathrm{N}(110,3.65^2)$.
    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.7em} \mathrm{P}(L > k) &= 0.6 \\[6pt]
    k &= \text{invNorm}(0.4,110,3.65) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.65em} k \approx 109 \text{ mm}
    $$

4.
    1.  Using the conditional probability formula, we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(L < 111\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em} L > 109) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(109 < L < 111)}{\mathrm{P}(L > 109)} \\[5pt]
        &= \dfrac{\text{normCdf}(109,111,110,3.65)}{0.6} \hspace{1.1em} \\[9pt]
        &\approx \dfrac{0.2159}{0.6} \\[12pt]
        &\approx 0.360
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Let $X$ be the number of pencils with length less than
        $111$ mm among the twenty large pencils.
        We have $X \sim \mathrm{B}(20,0.360)$.
        Hence we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(X \geq 3) &= \text{binomCdf}(20,0.360,3,20) \\[6pt]
        &\approx 0.990
        \end{aligned}
        $$
