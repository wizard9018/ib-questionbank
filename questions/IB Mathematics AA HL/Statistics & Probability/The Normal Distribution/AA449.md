# AA449

**Maximum mark: 7**

The time taken for a student to finish a task is normally distributed
with a mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$. It is found that $6\%$
of students take less than $7$ minutes to complete the task and $75$%
take less than $22$ minutes.

Find the value of $\mu$ and the value of $\sigma$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let $T$ be the time, in minutes, it takes for a student to complete the
task.

We have $T \sim \mathrm{N}(\mu,\sigma^2)$ and $Z \sim \mathrm{N}(0,1)$.

Hence we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{P}(T < 7) &= 0.06 \\[12pt]
\hspace{0.5em} \mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z < \dfrac{7-\mu}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.06 \\[6pt]
\dfrac{7-\mu}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.06) \\[8pt]
\dfrac{7-\mu}{\sigma} &\approx -1.555 \\[12pt]
\mu - 1.555\sigma &\approx 7
\end{aligned}
$$
Similarly, we
obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{P}(T < 22) &= 0.75 \\[12pt]
\mathrm{P}\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(Z < \dfrac{22-\mu}{\sigma}\bigg)\hspace{-0.125em} &= 0.75 \\[6pt]
\dfrac{22-\mu}{\sigma} &= \text{invNorm}(0.75) \\[8pt]
\dfrac{22-\mu}{\sigma} &\approx 0.674 \\[12pt]
\mu + 0.674\sigma &\approx 22
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, solving
the system of linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $\mu$ and $\sigma$, we
find
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{11.8em} \mu \approx 17.5 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m}\hspace{0.525em} \text{ and } \hspace{0.525em}\sigma \approx 6.73 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
\end{aligned}
$$
