# AA438

**Maximum mark: 7**

The time taken for a student to complete an exam is normally
distributed with a mean of $40$ minutes and a
standard deviation of $5.5$ minutes.

1.  A student is selected at random. Find the probability that the
    student competes the task in less than $48$
    minutes. [3 marks]

2.  The probability a student takes between $k$ and $48$ minutes is
    $0.4$. Find the value of $k$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $X$ be the time taken for a student to complete the exam.
    We have $X \sim \mathrm{N}(40,5.5^2)$.

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(X < 48) &= \mathrm{normCdf}(-\infty,48,40,5.5) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 0.927
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  If we draw the normal curve for $X$, we have

    ![0e9a12c9a3ca8ecdd2cee616c2d8ec39077026f9.svg](images/0e9a12c9a3ca8ecdd2cee616c2d8ec39077026f9.svg)

    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.85em} \mathrm{P}(X \leq k) &= \mathrm{P}(X < 48) - \mathrm{P}(k < X < 48) \\[6pt]
    \mathrm{P}(X \leq k) &= 0.927 - 0.4 \\[6pt]
    \mathrm{P}(X \leq k) &= 0.527 \\[6pt]
    k &= \text{invNorm}(0.527,40,5.5) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    k \approx 40.4 \hspace{5.2em}
    $$
