# AA943

**Maximum mark: 11**

Carmen plays a game at a stall at a local fair. The stall owner lays $n$ cards face down on a table. Three of the cards say "Win" on the hidden side, the others say "Try Again". Carmen draws cards from the table, one after the other, without replacement. The game ends when Carmen draws a card that says "Win".

1. Find the probability, in terms of $n$, that the game will end on her $2$nd draw. [2 marks]

2. Given that $n = 8$, find the probability that the game will end on her $6$th draw. [2 marks]

Carmen plays the game when $n = 8$. She pays $$\$\hspace{0.025em}6$$ to play and can earn money back depending on the number of draws it takes to obtain a card that says "Win". She earns no money back if she obtains a card that says "Win" on her $6$th draw. Let $M$ be the amount of money that she earns back playing the game. This information is shown in the following table. 

![AA943a](images/AA943a.PNG)

3. Find the value of $k$ so that this is a fair game. [7 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(\text{game ends on $2$nd draw}) &= \dfrac{n-3}{n}\times\dfrac{3}{n-1} \hspace{4em} \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{= \dfrac{3(n-3)}{n(n-1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \hspace{0.8em} \mathrm{P}(\text{game ends on $6$th draw}) &= \dfrac{8-3}{8}\times\dfrac{7-3}{7}\times\dfrac{6-3}{6}\times\dfrac{5-3}{5}\times\dfrac{4-3}{4}\times\dfrac{3}{3} \\[8pt]

    &= \dfrac{5}{8}\times\dfrac{4}{7}\times\dfrac{3}{6}\times\dfrac{2}{5}\times\dfrac{1}{4}\times\dfrac{3}{3} \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{=\dfrac{1}{56}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \hspace{3em} \mathrm{P}(\text{game ends on $1$st draw}) &= \dfrac{3}{8} \\[8pt]

    &= \dfrac{21}{56} \\[-13pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    And using the expression from part (a)

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \hspace{3em} \mathrm{P}(\text{game ends on $2$nd draw}) &= \dfrac{3(8-3)}{8(8-1)} \\[8pt]

    &=\dfrac{15}{56} \\[-13pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    The probabilities of the game ending on the 3rd, 4th, or 5th draw are as follows:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \hspace{3em} \mathrm{P}(\text{game ends on $3$rd draw}) &= \dfrac{8-3}{8}\times\dfrac{7-3}{7}\times\dfrac{3}{6} \\[8pt]

    &= \dfrac{5}{8}\times\dfrac{4}{7}\times\dfrac{3}{6} \\[8pt]

    &=\dfrac{10}{56} \\[8pt]

    \hspace{3em} \mathrm{P}(\text{game ends on $4$th draw}) &= \dfrac{8-3}{8}\times\dfrac{7-3}{7}\times\dfrac{6-3}{6}\times\dfrac{3}{5} \\[8pt]

    &= \dfrac{5}{8}\times\dfrac{4}{7}\times\dfrac{3}{6}\times\dfrac{3}{5} \\[8pt]

    &=\dfrac{6}{56} \\[8pt]

    \hspace{3em} \mathrm{P}(\text{game ends on $5$th draw}) &= \dfrac{8-3}{8}\times\dfrac{7-3}{7}\times\dfrac{6-3}{6}\times\dfrac{5-3}{5}\times\dfrac{3}{4} \\[8pt]

    &= \dfrac{5}{8}\times\dfrac{4}{7}\times\dfrac{3}{6}\times\dfrac{2}{5}\times\dfrac{3}{4} \\[8pt]

    &=\dfrac{3}{56} \\[-13pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    From part (b) we have P(game ends on 6th draw) $=\dfrac{1}{56}$.

    Hence the probability distribution of $M$ is

    ![AA943b](images/AA943b.PNG)

    The game is fair if $\mathrm{E}[M] = 6$.

    Hence, using the formula for the expected value of $M$, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \hspace{3em} \mathrm{E}[M] &= \sum x\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(X = x) \\[7pt]

    6 &= 10\hspace{-0.05em}\cdot\hspace{-0.1em}\dfrac{21}{56}+5\hspace{-0.05em}\cdot\hspace{-0.1em}\dfrac{15}{56}+4\hspace{-0.05em}\cdot\hspace{-0.1em}\dfrac{10}{56}+\dfrac{3k}{4}\hspace{-0.05em}\cdot\hspace{-0.1em}\dfrac{6}{56}+\dfrac{k}{3}\hspace{-0.05em}\cdot\hspace{-0.1em}\dfrac{3}{56}+0\hspace{-0.05em}\cdot\hspace{-0.1em}\dfrac{1}{56} \\[6pt]

    6 &= \dfrac{210 + 75 + 40 + \dfrac{9k}{2} + k + 0}{56} \\[6pt]

    6 \times 56 &= 210 + 75 + 40 + \dfrac{9k}{2} + k + 0 \\[6pt]

    336 &= 325 + \dfrac{11k}{2} \\[6pt]

    11 &= \dfrac{11k}{2} \\[6pt]

    &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{k = 2}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
