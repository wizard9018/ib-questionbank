# AA691

**Maximum mark: 8**

A new video streaming service has a monthly subscription cost of $$\$15$$,
which allows access to the content on only one device. Customers can add
up to 5 additional devices on their account, each additional device
having an extra fee of $$\$4.10$$ per month. Let $X$ be the number of
**additional** devices purchased by a randomly selected customer. The
variable $X$ has the following probability distribution.

![77dd5cd32e19a31cc59e2186bf0a672ae5a6702e.svg](images/77dd5cd32e19a31cc59e2186bf0a672ae5a6702e.svg)

1.  Given that $\mathrm{P}(X < 4) = 0.85$, find
    1.  the value of $p$.

    2.  the value of $q$.[4 marks]

It is known that $\mathrm{E}[X]=2.13$, and $\mathrm{Var}(X) = 1.3131$.
Let the variable $Y$ represent the total amount, in dollars, charged to
a customer per month. Find

2.
    1.  $\mathrm{E}[Y]$ to two decimal places.

    2.  $\mathrm{Var}(Y)$ to two decimal places. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using the probability distribution table for $X$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(X < 4) &= 0.85  \\[8pt]
        \mathrm{P}(X = 1) + \mathrm{P}(X = 2) + \mathrm{P}(X=3) &= 0.85 \\[8pt]
        p+0.27+0.19 &= 0.85 \\[9pt]
        &\hspace{-0.45em}p = 0.39 \hspace{3.5em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Given that the sum of probabilities of all possible values is
        $1$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        1 &= 0.39 + 0.27 + 0.19 + q + 0.03 \\[9pt]
        1 &= 0.88 + q \\[9pt]
        & \hspace{-0.5em}q = 0.12 \\[9pt]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{E}[Y] &= \mathrm{E}[4.10\hspace{0.05em}X + 15]  \\[9pt]
        &= 4.10\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{E}[X] + 15 \\[9pt]
        &= 4.10(2.13) + 15 \hspace{0.4em} \\[9pt]
        &\approx \$23.73 \hspace{3.5em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{Var}(Y) &= \mathrm{Var}(4.10\hspace{0.05em}X + 15) \\[9pt]
        &= (4.10)^2\mathrm{Var}(X) \\[9pt]
        &= (4.10)^2(1.3131) \\[9pt]
        &\approx \$22.07 \hspace{3.5em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$
