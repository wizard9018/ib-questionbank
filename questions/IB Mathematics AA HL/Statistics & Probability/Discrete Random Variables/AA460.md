# AA460

**Maximum mark: 6**

In an airport terminal, the number of passengers, $X$, who ride in an
airport cab has the following probability distribution.

![b198afeefb785c9a3864342d8822a0937eb44207.svg](images/b198afeefb785c9a3864342d8822a0937eb44207.svg)

After the opening of a new ring road around the urban centre that
charges a toll, an airport transfer company introduces a charge for
passengers who use the new ring road. The charge is $$\$4.80$$ per airport
cab plus $$\$1.25$$ per passenger. Let $Y$ represent the amount, in
dollars, that is charged by the airport transfer company per ride.

1.  Find $\mathrm{E}[Y]$. [4 marks]

2.  Given that $\mathrm{Var}(X) = 0.9144$, find $\mathrm{Var}(Y)$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the formula for the expected value of $X$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6em} \mathrm{E}[X] &= \sum x\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(X = x) \\[6pt]
    &= (1)(0.25) + (2)(0.46) + (3)(0.19) + \cdots \\[6pt]
    &\phantom{= (1)(0.25)\hspace{0.28em} } + (4)(0.08) + (5)(0.02) \\[6pt]
    &= 2.16
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em} \mathrm{E}[Y] &= \mathrm{E}[1.25X + 4.80] \\[6pt]
    &= 1.25\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{E}[X] + 4.80 \\[6pt]
    &= 1.25(2.16) + 4.80 \\[6pt]
    &= \$7.50
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.1em} \mathrm{Var}(Y) &= \mathrm{Var}(1.25X + 4.80) \\[6pt]
    &= (1.25)^2\mathrm{Var}(X) \\[6pt]
    &= (1.25)^2(0.9144) \\[7pt]
    &\approx \$1.43
    \end{aligned}
    $$
