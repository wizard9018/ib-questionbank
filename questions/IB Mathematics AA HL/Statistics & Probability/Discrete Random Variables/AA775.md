# AA775

**Maximum mark: 5**

A random variable $X$ has a probability distribution given in the
following table.

![573473f006b35710dd8b353664972d8f3d4c90c7.svg](images/573473f006b35710dd8b353664972d8f3d4c90c7.svg)

Given the expected value of $X$ is $2.65$, find the value of $a$ and of
$b$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the formula for the expected value of $X$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{E}[X] &= \sum x\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(X = x) \\[12pt]
2.65&= (1)(5a) + (2)(4b) + (3)(0.3) \\[3pt]
& \qquad + (4)(0.15) + (5)(0.05) \\[12pt]
2.65&=5a+8b+1.75 \\[12pt]
5a+8b &= 0.9 \hspace{10.5em}{(1)}
\end{aligned}
$$
We also know that

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum \hspace{0.055em} \mathrm{P}(X = x) &= 1 \\[12pt]
5a+4b+0.3+0.15+0.05 &= 1
\\[12pt]
5a+4b &= 0.5 \hspace{2em}{(2)} \\
\end{aligned}
$$
Solving equations $(1)$ and
$(2)$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
&a= 0.02 \quad \text{ and} \quad b= 0.1
\end{aligned}
$$
