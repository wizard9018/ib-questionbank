# AA461

**Maximum mark: 6**

In a river city, the number of passengers, $X$, who ride in a taxi cab
has the following probability distribution.

![464f93413f3f930783f890ac749cb5aaf4a150ee.svg](images/464f93413f3f930783f890ac749cb5aaf4a150ee.svg)

After the closing of the central river bridge for reconstruction, a taxi
cab $\text{company}$ introduces a charge for passengers who use
car ferries to cross the river. The charge is $$\$8.00$$ per taxi cab plus
$$\$1.60$$ per passenger. Let $Y$ represent the amount, in dollars, that
is charged by the taxi cab company per ride.

1.  Find $\mathrm{E}[Y]$. [4 marks]

2.  Given that $\mathrm{Var}(X) = 1.0275$, find $\mathrm{Var}(Y)$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the formula for the expected value of $X$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6em} \mathrm{E}[X] &= \sum x\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(X = x) \\[6pt]
    &= (1)(0.55) + (2)(0.25) + (3)(0.12) + \cdots \\[6pt]
    &\phantom{= (1)(0.55)\hspace{0.28em} } + (4)(0.06) + (5)(0.02) \\[6pt]
    &= 1.75
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em} \mathrm{E}[Y] &= \mathrm{E}[1.60X + 8.00] \\[6pt]
    &= 1.60\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{E}[X] + 8.00 \\[6pt]
    &= 1.60(1.75) + 8.00 \\[6pt]
    &= \$10.8
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.1em} \mathrm{Var}(Y) &= \mathrm{Var}(1.60X + 8.00) \\[6pt]
    &= (1.60)^2\mathrm{Var}(X) \\[6pt]
    &= (1.60)^2(1.0275) \\[7pt]
    &\approx \$2.63
    \end{aligned}
    $$
