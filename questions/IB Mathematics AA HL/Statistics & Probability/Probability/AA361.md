# AA361

**Maximum mark: 11**

Jack and John decided to run a total of $250$ km
in $31$ days. To help them $\text{decide}$ whether it is fine to
go running, they classify each day in August as rainy or dry. Given that
a day in August is rainy, the probability that the next day is dry is
$0.25$. Given that a day in August is dry, the probability that the next
day is dry is $0.8$. The weather forecast for the $1$st of August
predicts that the probability that it will be dry is $0.9$.

1.  Draw a tree diagram to display all the possible outcomes for the
    first three days of August. [4 marks]

2.  Find the probability that the $3$rd of August is dry. [3 marks]

3.  Find the probability that the $1$st of August was dry given that the
    $3$rd of August is rainy. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Drawing a tree diagram to display all the possible outcomes for the
    first three days of August, we have

    ![0381b67154e9baaebf51e12b3773010451b2c167.svg](images/0381b67154e9baaebf51e12b3773010451b2c167.svg)
    D : Dry;
    R : Rainy.

2.  Using the tree diagram from part (a), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(3\text{rd Aug Dry}) &= \mathrm{P}(\text{DDD}) + \mathrm{P}(\text{DRD}) + \mathrm{P}(\text{RDD}) + \mathrm{P}(\text{RRD}) \\[8pt]
    &= (0.9)(0.8)(0.8)+(0.9)(0.2)(0.25)+ \cdots \\[8pt]
    &\phantom{+(0.1)(0.25)(0.8)}+(0.1)(0.25)(0.8)+(0.1)(0.75)(0.25) \\[8pt]
    & = 0.65975
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the conditional probability formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.45em}\mathrm{P}(\text{$1$st Aug Dry}\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}\text{$3$rd Aug Rainy}) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(\text{$1$st Aug Dry and $3$rd Aug Rainy})}{\mathrm{P}(\text{$3$rd Aug Rainy})} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(\text{DDR}) + \mathrm{P}(\text{DRR})}{1 - \mathrm{P}(\text{$3$rd Aug Dry})} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{(0.9)(0.8)(0.2) + (0.9)(0.2)(0.75)}{1 - 0.65975} \\[6pt]
    &= 0.819985 \\[8pt]
    &\approx 0.820
    \end{aligned}
    $$
