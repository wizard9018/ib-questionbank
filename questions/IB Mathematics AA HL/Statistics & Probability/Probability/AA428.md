# AA428

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following Venn diagram shows two events $A$ and $B$, where
$\mathrm{P}(A) = 0.6$, $\mathrm{P}(A\cap B) = 0.1$ and
$\mathrm{P}(A\cup B) = 0.9$. The values of $p$ and $q$ are
$\text{probabilities}$.

![26f9ec4aca9d307c31eec8ca219e6fb844c8d795.svg](images/26f9ec4aca9d307c31eec8ca219e6fb844c8d795.svg)

1.  Find the value of $q$ and of $p$. [3 marks]

2.  Find $\mathrm{P}(B^{\prime})$. [2 marks]

3.  Find
    $\mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}B^{\prime})$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    q = 0.1 \hspace{5.2em}
    $$
    \
    Using the Venn diagram, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A) &= p + \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) \hspace{2.3em} \\[6pt]
    0.6 &= p + 0.1 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    p = 0.5 \hspace{5.2em}
    $$

2.  Using the Venn diagram, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.8em} \mathrm{P}(B) &= \mathrm{P}(A\cup B) - p \\[6pt]
    &= 0.9 - 0.5 \\[6pt]
    &= 0.4
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the complementary events formula, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(B^{\prime}) &= 1 - \mathrm{P}(B) \hspace{1.3em} \\[6pt]
    &= 0.6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  If we shade in the region that represents
    $A \cap B^{\prime}$, we have

    ![4f45e013a0498a8d457ff1414a5e7d931bd2a791.svg](images/4f45e013a0498a8d457ff1414a5e7d931bd2a791.svg)

    Hence, using the conditional probability formula, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}B^{\prime}) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(A \cap B^{\prime})}{\mathrm{P}(B^{\prime})} \hspace{1.9em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.5}{0.6} \\[12pt]
    &= \dfrac{5}{6}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
