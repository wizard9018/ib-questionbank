# AA448

**Maximum mark: 14**

In a group of $25$ students, $18$ students take chemistry and $12$
students take physics. Three students take neither chemistry nor
physics. The Venn diagram below shows the events $C$ (studying
chemistry) and $P$ (studying physics). The values of $p, q, r$ and $s$
represent numbers of students.

![b392cf8c9ae567be0ff74befbb22c9570a52c927.svg](images/b392cf8c9ae567be0ff74befbb22c9570a52c927.svg)

1.
    1.  Write down the value of $s$.

    2.  Find the value of $r$.
    3.  Write down the value of $p$ and the value of $q$. [5 marks]

2.
    1.  A student is selected at random from the group. Given that the
        student studies chemistry, write down the probability they study
        physics.

    2.  Hence show that studying chemistry and physics are not
        independent events. [5 marks]

3.  Two students are selected at random one after the other. Find the
    probability the first student studies only chemistry and the second
    student studies only physics. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $s = 3$

    2.  Using the inclusion-exclusion principle, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3em} n(C \cup P) &= n(C) + n(P) - n(C \cap P) \\[6pt]
        22 &= 18 + 12 - r \\[6pt]
        r &= 30 - 22 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        r = 8 \hspace{3.7em}
        $$

    3.
        $p = 10$  and
         $q = 4$

2.
    1.  Using the Venn diagram, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(P\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}C) &= \dfrac{n(C \cap P)}{n(C)} \hspace{3.05em} \\[9pt]
        &= \dfrac{8}{18}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(P) &= \dfrac{12}{25} \neq \mathrm{P}(P\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}C) = \dfrac{8}{18} \hspace{2.4em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence $C$ and $P$ are not independent.

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(\text{$1$st only chem \& $2$nd only phys}) &= \dfrac{10}{25}\times\dfrac{4}{24} \hspace{3.7em} \\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{15}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
