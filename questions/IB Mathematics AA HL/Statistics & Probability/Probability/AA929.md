# AA929

**Maximum mark: 6**

$A$ and $B$ are independent events such that $\mathrm{P}(A \cap {B\,} ^\prime) = 0.09$ and $\mathrm{P}({A\,}^\prime \cap B) = 0.49$. 

Let $x = \mathrm{P}(A\cap B)$.

1. 1. Express $\mathrm{P}(A)$ in terms of $x$.

    2. Express $\mathrm{P}(B)$ in terms of $x$. [2 marks]

2. Find the value of $x$. [2 marks]

3. Find $\mathrm{P}(B\,|\,A)$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 1. We have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \mathrm{P}(A) &= \mathrm{P}(A\cap B) + \mathrm{P}(A \cap {B\,}^\prime) \\[11pt]

        &\hspace{-8.1mm}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(A) = x + 0.09}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

    2. We have

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \mathrm{P}(B) &= \mathrm{P}(A\cap B) + \mathrm{P}({A\,}^\prime \cap B) \\[11pt]

        &\hspace{-8.35mm}\boxed{ \mathrm{P}(B) = x + 0.49}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

2. Using the independent events formula, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(A)\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(B) &= \mathrm{P}(A\cap B) \\[10pt]

    (x+0.09)(x+0.49) &= x \\[10pt]

    x^2 + 0.58 x + 0.0441 &= x \\[10pt]

    x^2 - 0.42 x + 0.0441 &= 0 \\[10pt]

    &\hspace{-0.55em}\boxed{x = 0.21} \hspace{3em} [\hspace{0.1em}\text{using G.D.C.}\hspace{0.1em}]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. Using the result of previous part and part (a) (i), we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(A) &= 0.21 + 0.09 \\[10pt]

    \mathrm{P}(A) &= 0.3 \\[-12pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Using the conditional probability formula, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(B\,|\,A) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(A\cap B)}{\mathrm{P}(A)}\\[10pt]

    &= \dfrac{0.21}{0.3} \\[11pt]

    &\hspace{-3.5em}\boxed{\mathrm{P}(B\,|\,A) = 0.7}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
