# AI332

**Maximum mark: 6**

At the local supermarket, out of the $100$ cartons of eggs on sale, $8$
have at least one egg inside that has cracked.

If two cartons are chosen at random from the $100$ **without
replacement**, find the probabilities of:

1.  **Neither** cartons having a cracked egg inside. [2 marks]

2.  **Both** cartons having at least one cracked egg inside. [2 marks]

3.  One carton **not** having a cracked egg inside, while the other
    having at least one cracked egg inside. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $C_1$ = {$1$st carton has a cracked egg inside},
    $C_2$ = {$2$nd carton has a cracked egg inside}.

    The event that neither cartons have a cracked egg inside can be
    illustrated by the paths highlighted in red on the tree diagram
    below.

    ![ebf59df4398674c730598565ce738e626c3b701b.svg](images/ebf59df4398674c730598565ce738e626c3b701b.svg)

    Hence the probability of neither cartons having a cracked egg inside
    is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(neither cartons, crack)} &= \text{Pr}(C_1') \cdot \text{Pr}(C_2'|C_1') \\
    &= \dfrac{92}{100} \cdot \dfrac{91}{99}\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.846
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The paths highlighted in red on the following tree diagram represent
    the event that both cartons have at least one cracked egg inside.

    ![5ff48c85fdd43a88d2fd5358b5e4769beec88ad5.svg](images/5ff48c85fdd43a88d2fd5358b5e4769beec88ad5.svg)

    Based off this tree diagram, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(both cartons, crack)} &= \text{Pr}(C_1) \cdot \text{Pr}(C_2|C_1) \\
    &= \dfrac{8}{100} \cdot \dfrac{7}{99}\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.00566
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The paths highlighted in red on the following tree diagram represent
    the event that one carton has a cracked egg inside while the other
    has at least one cracked egg inside.

    ![e312e81017a764be8813d6e9025ef817dd19e707.svg](images/e312e81017a764be8813d6e9025ef817dd19e707.svg)

    Hence,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Pr(one crack, one no crack)} &= \text{Pr}(C_1) \cdot \text{Pr}(C_2'|C_1) + \text{Pr}(C_1') \cdot \text{Pr}(C_2|C_1')\\
    &=  (\dfrac{8}{100} \cdot \dfrac{92}{99}) + (\dfrac{92}{100} \cdot \dfrac{8}{99})\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{0.525em}\approx 0.149
    \end{aligned}
    $$
