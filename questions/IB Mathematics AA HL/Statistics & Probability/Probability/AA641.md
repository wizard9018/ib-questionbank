# AA641

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two events $A$ and $B$ are such that $\mathrm{P}(A) = 0.5$ and
$\mathrm{P}(A\cap B) = 0.1$.

1.  Find $\mathrm{P}(A\cap B^{\prime})$. [2 marks]

2.  Given that
    $\mathrm{P}(A'\hspace{-0.075em}\cap B^{\prime}) = 0.2$,
    find :

    1.  $\mathrm{P}(A\cup B)$;
    2.  $\mathrm{P}(B)$;

    3.  $\mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}B^{\prime})$.
        [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we shade in the region $A\cap B^{\prime}$ on the
    Venn diagram below, we have

    ![e6eef933fb5ecc12a077b0f32950628643b6a12a.svg](images/e6eef933fb5ecc12a077b0f32950628643b6a12a.svg)

    Hence, using the Venn diagram above, we deduce
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.95em} \mathrm{P}(A\cap B^{\prime}) &= \mathrm{P}(A) - \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) \\[6pt]
    &= 0.5 - 0.1 \\[6pt]
    &= 0.4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        (A\cup B)^{\prime} &= A'\hspace{-0.075em}\cap B^{\prime} \hspace{5em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence, using the complementary events formula, we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(A'\hspace{-0.075em}\cap B^{\prime}) &= 1 - \mathrm{P}(A\cup B) \hspace{3.2em} \\[6pt]
        0.2 &= 1 - \mathrm{P}(A\cup B) \\[6pt]
        \mathrm{P}(A\cup B) &= 1 - 0.2 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \mathrm{P}(A\cup B) = 0.8 \hspace{7.25em}
        $$

    2.  Using the combined events formula, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2.5em} \mathrm{P}(A\cup B) &= \mathrm{P}(A) + \mathrm{P}(B) - \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) \\[6pt]
        0.8 &= 0.5 + \mathrm{P}(B) - 0.1 \\[6pt]
        0.8 - 0.4 &= \mathrm{P}(B) \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \mathrm{P}(B) = 0.4 \hspace{5.4em}
        $$

    3.  Using the complementary events formula, we know
        $\mathrm{P}(B^{\prime}) = 1 - \mathrm{P}(B)$

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(B^{\prime}) &= 0.6 \hspace{6em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence, using the conditional probability formula, we find

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}B^{\prime}) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(A \cap B^{\prime})}{\mathrm{P}(B^{\prime})} \hspace{3.65em} \\[7pt]
        &= \dfrac{0.4}{0.6} \\[8pt]
        &= \dfrac{2}{3}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
