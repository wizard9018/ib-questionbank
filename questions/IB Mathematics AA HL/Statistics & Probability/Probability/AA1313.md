# AA1313

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two events $A$ and $B$ are such that $\mathrm{P}(A\cap B)=0.1$, $\mathrm{P}(A\cup B)'=0.3$ and $\mathrm{P}(A\,|\,B) =0.2$.

1.    Find $\mathrm{P}(B)$. [2 marks]

2.    Hence find $\mathrm{P}(A)$. [2 marks]

3.    Determine whether events $A$ and $B$ are independent. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. As we are given conditional probability,  let's use the formula

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(A\,|\,B)&=\frac{\mathrm{P}(A\cap B)}{\mathrm{P}(B)}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Substituting the given values

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    0.2&=\frac{0.1}{\mathrm{P}(B)}\tag*{\textbf{{(M1)}}}\\[9pt]

    \mathrm{P}(B)&=\frac{0.1}{0.2}\\[9pt]

    &=0.5\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. To find $\mathrm{P}(A)$ lets use the formula

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(A\cup B)&=\mathrm{P}(A)+\mathrm{P}(B)-\mathrm{P}(A\cap B)

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    We were given $\mathrm{P}(A\cap B)$ and we found $\mathrm{P}(B)$ in part (a).

    We also know that complementary events add up to one, so we can find $\mathrm{P}(A\cup B)$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(A\cup B)+\mathrm{P}(A\cup B)'&=1\\[6pt]

    \mathrm{P}(A\cup B)+0.3&=1\\[6pt]

    \mathrm{P}(A\cup B)&=0.7

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Substituting the information into the formula, now we can solve for $\mathrm{P}(A)$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    0.7&=\mathrm{P}(A)+0.5-0.1\tag*{\textbf{{(M1)}}}\\[6pt]

    0.7&=\mathrm{P}(A)+0.4\\[6pt]

    \mathrm{P}(A)&=0.3\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Note: You could also try to solve part (a) and (b) using a Venn diagram.

    We can also add the values for $m$ and $n$ after we have done the calculations.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    P(B)&=0.1+n&&P(A)=m+0.1\\

    0.5&=0.1+n&&\hspace{1em}0.3=m+0.1\\

    n&=0.4&&\hspace{1.4em}m=0.2

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. If events $A$ and $B$ are independent then

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(A)\times \mathrm{P}(B)&=\mathrm{P}(A\cap B)

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Substituting information into this formula

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(A)\times \mathrm{P}(B)&=\mathrm{P}(A\cap B)\\[6pt]

    0.3\times0.5&\,\,\fbox{?}\,\,0.1\\[6pt]

    0.15&\neq0.1\tag*{\textbf{{R1}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore $$\text{the events $A$ and $B$ are not independent}$$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
