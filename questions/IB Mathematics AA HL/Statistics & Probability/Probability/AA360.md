# AA360

**Maximum mark: 5**

A box contains $8$ green and $4$ blue marbles. Two marbles are selected
at random (without replacement). Find the probability that selected
marbles are:

1.  of the same colour; [3 marks]

2.  of different colours. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(\text{of the same colour}) &= \mathrm{P}(\text{both green})+\mathrm{P}(\text{both blue})\\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{8}{12}\times\dfrac{7}{11} + \dfrac{4}{12}\times\dfrac{3}{11}\\[12pt]
    &= \dfrac{17}{33}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the complementary events formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(\text{of different colours}) &= 1 - \mathrm{P}(\text{of the same colour}) \hspace{0.5em} \\[12pt]
    &= 1 -  \dfrac{17}{33} \hspace{2.9em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{16}{33}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
