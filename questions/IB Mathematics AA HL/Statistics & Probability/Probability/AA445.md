# AA445

**Maximum mark: 15**

Jane commutes to work five days a week and travels by either bike or
train. When it rains, the probability Jane rides her bike is $0.2$. When
it does not rain, the probability she catches the train is $0.3$. The
probability it rains on any given day is $0.4$.

1.  On a randomly selected day, find the probability Jane catches the
    train to work. [4 marks]

2.  Given that Jane catches the train, find the probability it was
    raining that day. [3 marks]

3.  In a randomly chosen work week, find the probability Jane catches
    the train on exactly two out of the five days. [3 marks]

4.  If Jane travels to work $48$ weeks a year, riding her bike is free
    and the train costs $$\$1.70$$ per day, calculate how much Jane would
    expect to pay per year on her commute to work.[5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we draw a tree diagram that shows the probabilities for the
    events, we have

    ![07648a103ad92692987791c0e75265c8b8c5c499.svg](images/07648a103ad92692987791c0e75265c8b8c5c499.svg)
    $R$ : rains;
    $R^\prime$ : does not
    rain;  $B$ : rides
    bike;  $T$ : catches
    train.

    Hence, using the tree diagram, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(T) &= \mathrm{P}(R)\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(T\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}R) + \mathrm{P}(R^\prime)\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(T\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}R^\prime) \\[6pt]
    &= (0.4)(0.8) + (0.6)(0.3) \\[6pt]
    &= 0.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the conditional probability formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(R\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}T) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(R)\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(T\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}R)}{\mathrm{P}(T)} \\[5.5pt]
    &= \dfrac{(0.4)(0.8)}{0.5} \\[12pt]
    &= 0.64
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Let $X$ be the number of days Jane catches the train in the chosen
    work week.

    We have $X \sim \mathrm{B}(5,0.5)$.
    Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em} \mathrm{P}(X = 2) &= \text{binomPdf}(5,0.5,2) \\[6pt]
    &= 0.3125
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using the expected value formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em} \mathrm{E}[\text{pay per day}] &= 0\times\mathrm{P}(B) + 1.70\times\mathrm{P}(T) \\[6pt]
    &= (0)(0.5) + (1.70)(0.5) \\[6pt]
    &= \$0.85
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em} \mathrm{E}[\text{pay per year}] &= (\#\hspace{0.15em}\text{weeks})(\#\hspace{0.15em}\text{days})\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{E}[\text{pay per day}]  \\[6pt]
    &= (48)(5)(0.85) \\[6pt]
    &= \$204
    \end{aligned}
    $$
