# AA384

**Maximum mark: 7**

Isabella studies at the University of Chicago and has relatives in
Detroit and New York City. Each time she visits
her relatives, she either drives or flies.

When travelling to Detroit, the probability she drives is
$\dfrac{3}{4}$, and when
travelling

to New York City, the probability she flies is
$\dfrac{7}{8}$.

Given that the probability she drives when visiting her relatives is
$\dfrac{5}{12}$, find the probability that for a
particular trip, she:

1.  travels to Detroit, giving your answer as a fraction; [4 marks]

2.  is on her way to Detroit, given that she is flying. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Let $p$ be the probability that Isabella travels to Detroit for a
    particular trip.

    Drawing a tree diagram to display all the possible outcomes of the
    trip, we get

    ![90b21769236578a3a31ddf8fe047240e677f1ee0.svg](images/90b21769236578a3a31ddf8fe047240e677f1ee0.svg)

    Hence, using the tree diagram, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{P}(\text{Drives}) &= \dfrac{5}{12} \hspace{3em} \\[9pt]
    \text{P}(\text{DET})\hspace{0.05em}\text{P}(\text{Drives\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}DET}) + \text{P}(\text{NYC})\hspace{0.05em}\text{P}(\text{Drives\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}NYC}) &= \dfrac{5}{12} \\[9pt]
    p\times\dfrac{3}{4} + (1-p)\times\dfrac{1}{8} &= \dfrac{5}{12} \\[9pt]
    \dfrac{5p}{8} + \dfrac{1}{8} &= \dfrac{5}{12} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{19.1em} p = \dfrac{7}{15}
    $$

2.  Using the conditional probability formula, we have

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \mathrm{P}(\text{DET}\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}\text{Flies}) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(\text{DET} \cap \text{Flies})}{\mathrm{P}(\text{Flies})}\\[5pt]
     &= \dfrac{\raisebox{0.6em}{$\dfrac{7}{15}\times\dfrac{1}{4}$}}{\raisebox{-0.7em}{$\dfrac{7}{15}\times\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{8}{15}\times\dfrac{7}{8}$}} \hspace{3.4em} [\text{using tree diagram}] \\[12pt]
     &= \dfrac{\raisebox{0.6em}{$\dfrac{7}{60}$}}{\raisebox{-0.7em}{$\dfrac{7}{12}$}} \\[12pt]
     &= \dfrac{1}{5}
     \end{aligned}
     $$
