# AA025

**Maximum mark: 11**

Mia and Oliver play a game with a box containing two green marbles and
three blue marbles. Each player in turn randomly selects a marble from
the box, notes its color and replaces it. Mia wins the game if she
selects a green marble and Oliver wins the game if he selects a blue
marble. Mia starts the game.

Find the probability that

1.  Mia wins on her first turn; [1 mark]

2.  Mia wins on her second turn; [2 marks]

3.  Oliver wins on one of his first three turns; [4 marks]

4.  Oliver eventually wins. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  To win on her $1$st turn, Mia has to select a green marble on her
    $1$st turn.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(\small{\text{Mia wins on her $1$st turn}}) &= \dfrac{2}{5} \hspace{3.8em} \\[12pt]
    &= 0.4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  To win on her $2$nd turn, Mia has to select a blue marble on her
    $1$st turn, then Oliver has to select a green marble on his $1$st
    turn, and then Mia has to select a green marble on her $2$nd turn.

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(\small{\text{Mia wins on her $2$nd turn}}) &= \dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{2}{5}\cdot\dfrac{2}{5} \\[5pt]
    &= \bigg(\dfrac{2}{5}\bigg)^2\bigg(\dfrac{3}{5}\bigg) \\[10pt]
    &= 0.096
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  To win on one of his $3$ turns, Oliver has to select a blue marble
    on his $1$st turn, or on his $2$nd turn, or on his $3$rd turn.

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(\small{\text{Oliver wins on one of his 3 turns}}) &= \dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5} + \dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{2}{5}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5} + \dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{2}{5}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot  \dfrac{2}{5}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5} \\[6pt]
    &= \bigg(\dfrac{3}{5}\bigg)^2 + \bigg(\dfrac{2}{5}\bigg)\bigg(\dfrac{3}{5}\bigg)^3 + \bigg(\dfrac{2}{5}\bigg)^2\bigg(\dfrac{3}{5}\bigg)^4 \\[12pt]
    &\approx 0.467
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(\small{\text{Oliver eventually wins}}) &= \bigg(\dfrac{3}{5}\bigg)^2 + \bigg(\dfrac{2}{5}\bigg)\bigg(\dfrac{3}{5}\bigg)^3 + \bigg(\dfrac{2}{5}\bigg)^2\bigg(\dfrac{3}{5}\bigg)^4 + \cdots \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{9}{25} + \bigg(\dfrac{9}{25}\bigg)\bigg(\dfrac{6}{25}\bigg) + \bigg(\dfrac{9}{25}\bigg)\bigg(\dfrac{6}{25}\bigg)^2 + \cdots
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the sum of an infinite geometric sequence formula
    $S_{\infty} = \dfrac{u_1}{1-r}$ with $u_1 = \dfrac{9}{25}$ and
    $r = \dfrac{6}{25}$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(\small{\text{Oliver eventually wins}}) &= S_{\infty} \\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{9/25}{1 - 6/25} \\[9pt]
    &\approx 0.474
    \end{aligned}
    $$
