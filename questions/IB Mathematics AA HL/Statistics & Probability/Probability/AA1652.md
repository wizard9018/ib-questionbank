# AA1652

**Maximum mark: 8**

A manufacturer sources components from three suppliers $X$, $Y$, and $Z$. The table below shows the percentage of components provided by each supplier and the probability that a component from that supplier passes a quality inspection.

|$\hspace{1.5em}\textbf{Supplier}\hspace{1.5em}$|$\hspace{1.5em}\textbf{Percentage of components}\hspace{1.5em}$|$\hspace{1.5em}\textbf{P(passes)}\hspace{1.5em}$|

|:---:|:---:|:---:|

| $X$ | $45\%$ | 0.96 |

| $Y$ | $35\%$ | 0.88 |

| $Z$ | $20\%$ | 0.80 |

A component is selected at random.

1.  Find the probability that the component passes inspection.[3 marks]

2.  Given that the component passes inspection, find the probability that it came from Supplier $Z$.[2 marks]

3.  Given that the component fails inspection, find the probability that it came from Supplier $X$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  A component may come from any of the three suppliers $X$, $Y$, or $Z$, and from each it either passes or fails inspection. These possibilities are shown on the tree diagram below.

    ![AA1652](images/AA1652.JPEG)

    Using the law of total probability, with $P$ representing the event that a component passes inspection, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

      \mathrm{P}(P) &= \mathrm{P}(X) \cdot \mathrm{P}(P \mid X) + \mathrm{P}(Y) \cdot \mathrm{P}(P \mid Y) + \mathrm{P}(Z) \cdot \mathrm{P}(P \mid Z) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]

           &= 0.45 \times 0.96 + 0.35 \times 0.88 + 0.20 \times 0.80 \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}} \\[6pt]

      \mathrm{P}(P) &= \boxed{0.900} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2.  We are told that the component passes inspection and asked for the probability that it came from Supplier $Z$. This reverses the natural order of events, in which a component is first supplied and then inspected, so we can apply Bayes' theorem. 

    Applying **Bayes' theorem (4.13)** with the result from part (a), we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

      \mathrm{P}(Z \mid P) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(P \mid Z) \cdot \mathrm{P}(Z)}{\mathrm{P}(P)} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]

                  &= \dfrac{0.80 \times 0.20}{0.900} \\[6pt]

                  &= 0.1778\ldots \\[6pt]

      \mathrm{P}(Z \mid P) &= \boxed{0.178}\hspace{1em}\text{(3 s.f.)} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3.  Here the component fails inspection, so we work with the event $F$ that a component fails. 

    Since $\mathrm{P}(P) = 0.900$, the probability of failure is $\mathrm{P}(F) = 1 - 0.900 = 0.100$. 

    

    The failure probability for each supplier is the complement of passing and we need to find $\mathrm{P}(F \mid X)$. Hence

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(F \mid X) &= 1-\mathrm{P}(P \mid X) \\[6pt]

    &= 1-0.96\\[6pt]

     \mathrm{P}(F \mid X) &= 0.04

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Substituting values into our formula,

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

      \mathrm{P}(X \mid F) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(F \mid X) \cdot \mathrm{P}(X)}{\mathrm{P}(F)} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]

                  &= \dfrac{0.04 \times 0.45}{0.100} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}} \\[6pt]

      \mathrm{P}(X \mid F) &= \boxed{0.18} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
