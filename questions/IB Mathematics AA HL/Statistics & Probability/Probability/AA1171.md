# AA1171

**Maximum mark: 6**

A botanist grows a type of flower that has three known variations, $A$, $B$ and $C$.

She grows the flowers in a large field. The proportion of each type of flower,$A:B:C$, in the field, is in the ratio $2:1:\tfrac{1}{2}$.

The stem length of each variation is known to be normally distributed.

Variation $A$ has a stem length with a mean of $8.3\,$cm and a standard deviation of $0.5\,$cm.

Variation $B$ has a stem length with a mean of $7.6\,$cm and a standard deviation of $0.3\,$cm.

Variation $C$ has a stem length with a mean of $9.5\,$cm and a standard deviation of $0.7\,$cm.

The botanist randomly chooses a flower from the field and measures the stem length.

The length is between $8.1$ and $8.2\,$cm.

What is the probability the flower is of variation $A$?

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

This question involves Baye's theorem for $3$ events.

We can either use the formula from the formula booklet or use a tree diagram method. This solution will use the formula directly.

$$
\begin{aligned}
    P(B_1|A)&=\frac{P(B_1)P(A|B_1)}{P(B_1)P(A|B_1) + P(B_2)P(A|B_2) +P(B_3)P(A|B_3)}
\end{aligned}
$$

First we will define the events used in the formula
$$
\begin{aligned}
    A&=\text{The length of the stem is between }8.1 \text{ and } 8.2\,\text{cm}\\[6pt]
    B_1&=\text{ choosing variation A} \\[6pt]
    B_2&=\text{ choosing variation B} \\[6pt]
    B_3&=\text{ choosing variation C}
\end{aligned}
$$

The variations are in the ratio $2:1:\tfrac{1}{2}$.

If we mutliply this ratio by $2$ we get $4:2:1$.

This tells us that $\tfrac{4}{7}$ of the flowers are of type $A$, $\tfrac{2}{7}$ of the flowers are of type $B$ and $\tfrac{1}{7}$ of the flowers are of type $C$.

Therefore $P(B_1)=\frac{4}{7}$, $P(B_2)=\frac{2}{7}$ and $P(B_3)=\frac{1}{7}$.

Using the information given in the question we can use a G.D.C. to evaluate the following
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{Variation $A$ having a length from $8.1$ to $8.2$\,cm}\\[6pt]
&P(A|B_1)=\text{normalCDF(8.1, 8.2, 8.3, 0.5)}\\[6pt]
&\hspace{3.8em}=0.07616...\\[6pt]
&\text{Variation $B$ having a length from $8.1$ to $8.2$\,cm}\\[6pt]
&P(A|B_2)=\text{normalCDF(8.1, 8.2, 7.6, 0.3)}\\[6pt]
&\hspace{3.8em}=0.02504...\\[6pt]
&\text{Variation $C$ having a length from $8.1$ to $8.2$\,cm}\\[6pt]
&P(A|B_3)=\text{normalCDF(8.1, 8.2, 9.5, 0.7}\\[6pt]
&\hspace{3.8em}=0.008895..
\end{aligned}
$$

Substituting all of these values into the formula we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
    P(B_1|A)&=\frac{\tfrac{4}{7}\times 0.07616...}{\tfrac{4}{7}\times 0.07616... + \tfrac{2}{7}\times 0.02504... +\tfrac{1}{7}\times 0.008895...}\\[18pt]
    &=0.838
\end{aligned}
$$
