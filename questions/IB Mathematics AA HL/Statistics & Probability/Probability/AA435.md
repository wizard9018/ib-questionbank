# AA435

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two events $A$ and $B$ are independent with
$\mathrm{P}(A\cap B) = \mathrm{P}(A \cap B^\prime) = 0.3$.

1.  Find $\mathrm{P}(A)$. [2 marks]

2.
    1.  Find $\mathrm{P}(B)$.

    2.  Find $\mathrm{P}(A \cup B)$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.8em} \mathrm{P}(A) &= \mathrm{P}(A\cap B) + \mathrm{P}(A \cap B^\prime) \\[6pt]
    &= 0.3 + 0.3 \\[6pt]
    &= 0.6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  Using the independent events formula, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(A\cap B) &= \mathrm{P}(A)\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(B) \hspace{5em} \\[12pt]
        0.3 &= 0.6\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(B) \\[6pt]
        \mathrm{P}(B) &= \dfrac{1}{2} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \mathrm{P}(B) = 0.5 \hspace{6.4em}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(A\cup B) &= \mathrm{P}(A \cap B^\prime) + \mathrm{P}(B) \hspace{4.25em} \\[6pt]
        &= 0.3 + 0.5 \\[6pt]
        &= 0.8
        \end{aligned}
        $$
