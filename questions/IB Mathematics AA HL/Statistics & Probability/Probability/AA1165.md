# AA1165

**Maximum mark: 6**

A class of university students can decide to take language electives as part of their degree. They can either choose to take _Russian_,
_Spanish_, both of those languages or neither of them.

If a randomly selected student is chosen the probability they take both languages is $0.39$ and the probability they take __only__ _Russian_ is $0.05$ __more__ than if they take __only__ _Spanish_.

Let event $R$ be the chosen student takes _Russian_ and event $S$ be they take _Spanish_.

1.  Represent the information above on the following Venn diagram in terms of $x$.

    You do __not__ need to complete the region which represents the students who take _neither_ subject. [2 marks]

    ![v_q](images/v_q.png)

2. Find the probability the chosen  student takes _Spanish_ in the case that $R$ and $S$ are independent events. [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  From the information given we can write down $P(R \cap S)=0.39$.

    We can also let $P(S \cap R')=x$ which means that $P(R \cap S')=x+0.05$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \textcolor{white}{space} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Adding these to the Venn diagram we get

    ![venn_a](images/venn_a.JPEG)

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \textcolor{white}{space} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. We need to find $P(S)$ when $R$ and $S$ are independent.

    If $R$ and $S$ are independent then, using the formula, we know that the following relationship must be true
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        P(R \cap S)&=P(R)\times P(S) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    From the Venn diagram we can write $P(R)=x+0.44$ and $P(S)=x+0.39$ in terms of $x$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \textcolor{white}{space} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Next substitute these expressions back into the formula for independent events and solve for $x$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        0.39&=(x+0.44)(x+0.39)\tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\[6pt]
        x&=0.21 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]\\[6pt]
        P(S)&=0.39+0.21\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{-2.2em}P(S)=0.6 \hspace{3em}  \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
