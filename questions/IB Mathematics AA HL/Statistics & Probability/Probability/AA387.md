# AA387

**Maximum mark: 4**

1.  On the Venn diagram below, shade in the region
    $A' \cup B^{\prime}$. [1 mark]

    ![1fd0957270ac7c4fcc059cb50a5d91e2c9d3ed49.svg](images/1fd0957270ac7c4fcc059cb50a5d91e2c9d3ed49.svg)

Two events $A$ and $B$ are such that
$\mathrm{P}(A' \cup B^{\prime}) = 0.85$ and
$\mathrm{P}(B^{\prime}) = 0.6$.

2.  Find $\mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}B)$
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we shade in the region $A'\cup B^{\prime}$ on the
    Venn diagram below, we get

    ![2636dd88f0e51ebfc727ddd0a040c82b706b1a28.svg](images/2636dd88f0e51ebfc727ddd0a040c82b706b1a28.svg)

2.  Using the complementary events formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) + \mathrm{P}(A'\cup B^{\prime}) &= 1 \\[6pt]
    \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) + 0.85 &= 1 \\[6pt]
    \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) &= 0.15 \hspace{1em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(B) + \mathrm{P}(B^{\prime}) &= 1 \\[6pt]
    \mathrm{P}(B) + 0.6 &= 1 \\[6pt]
    \mathrm{P}(B) &= 0.4 \hspace{1em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the
    conditional probability formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}B) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(A \cap B)}{\mathrm{P}(B)} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.15}{0.4} \\[12pt]
    &= 0.375
    \end{aligned}
    $$
