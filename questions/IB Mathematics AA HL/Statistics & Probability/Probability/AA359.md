# AA359

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two events $A$ and $B$ are such that
$\mathrm{P}(A' \cap B) = \mathrm{P}(A \cap B') = 0.3$ and
$\mathrm{P}(A \cap B) = 0.2$.

1.  Calculate $\mathrm{P}(A' \cap B')$. [2 marks]

2.  Calculate $\mathrm{P}(B')$. [2 marks]

3.  Hence find $\mathrm{P}(A'\hspace{0.1em}|\hspace{0.1em}B')$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the complementary events formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \mathrm{P}(A' \cap B') &= 1 - \mathrm{P}(A \cup B) \hspace{3em} [\text{since $A' \cap B' = (A \cup B)'$}] \\[6pt]
    &= 1 - [\mathrm{P}(A' \cap B) + \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) + \mathrm{P}(A \cap B')] \\[6pt]
    &= 1 - [0.3 + 0.2 + 0.3] \\[6pt]
    &= 0.2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the complementary events formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \mathrm{P}(B') &= 1 - \mathrm{P}(B) \\[6pt]
    &= 1 - [\mathrm{P}(A' \cap B) + \mathrm{P}(A \cap B)] \\[6pt]
    &= 1 - [0.3+0.2] \\[6pt]
    &= 0.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Hence, using the conditional probability formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A'\hspace{0.1em}|\hspace{0.1em}B') &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(A' \cap B')}{\mathrm{P}(B')} \hspace{1.2em} \\[5pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.2}{0.5} \\[12pt]
    &= 0.4
    \end{aligned}
    $$
