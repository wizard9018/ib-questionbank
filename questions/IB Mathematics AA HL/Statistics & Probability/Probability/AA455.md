# AA455

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $A$ and $B$ be events such that $\mathrm{P}(A) = 0.35$,
$\mathrm{P}(B) = 0.5$ and $\mathrm{P}(A\cup B) = 0.65$.

Find $\mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}B)$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the combined events formula, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2.9em} \mathrm{P}(A\cup B) &= \mathrm{P}(A) + \mathrm{P}(B) - \mathrm{P}(A\cap B) \\[6pt]
0.65 &= 0.35 + 0.5 - \mathrm{P}(A\cap B) \\[6pt]
\mathrm{P}(A\cap B) &= 0.85 - 0.65 \\[6pt]
\mathrm{P}(A\cap B) &= 0.2
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the conditional
probability formula, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{0.8em} \mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}B) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(A\cap B)}{\mathrm{P}(B)} \\[6pt]
&= \dfrac{0.2}{0.5} \\[9pt]
&= \dfrac{2}{5}
\end{aligned}
$$
