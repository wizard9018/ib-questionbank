# AA396

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two events $A$ and $B$ are such that $\mathrm{P}(A) = 0.58$ and
$\mathrm{P}(A\cap B) = 0.16$.

1.  Find $\mathrm{P}(A\cap B^{\prime})$. [2 marks]

2.  Given that $\mathrm{P}(A'\cap B^{\prime}) = 0.28$,
    find
    $\mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}B^{\prime})$.
    [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we shade in the region $A\cap B^{\prime}$ on the
    Venn diagram below, we have

    ![e6eef933fb5ecc12a077b0f32950628643b6a12a.svg](images/e6eef933fb5ecc12a077b0f32950628643b6a12a.svg)

    Hence, using the Venn diagram above, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A\cap B^{\prime}) &= \mathrm{P}(A) - \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) \\[6pt]
    &= 0.58 - 0.16 \\[6pt]
    & = 0.42
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the complementary events formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8em} \mathrm{P}(A'\cup B^{\prime}) &= 1 - \mathrm{P}(A\cap B) \hspace{2.4em} [\text{since $A' \hspace{-0.1em}\cup B^{\prime} = (A\cap B)^{\prime}$}] \\[6pt]
    \mathrm{P}(A') &= 1 - \mathrm{P}(A)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the
    combined events formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A'\cup B^{\prime}) &= \mathrm{P}(A') + \mathrm{P}(B^{\prime}) - \mathrm{P}(A'\cap B^{\prime}) \\[6pt]
    \hspace{2.4em} 1 - \mathrm{P}(A\cap B) &= (1-\mathrm{P}(A)) + \mathrm{P}(B^{\prime}) - \mathrm{P}(A'\cap B^{\prime}) \\[6pt]
    1 - 0.16 &= (1 - 0.58) + \mathrm{P}(B^{\prime}) - 0.28 \\[6pt]
    \mathrm{P}(B^{\prime}) &= 0.7 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the conditional probability formula, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}B^{\prime}) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(A \cap B^{\prime})}{\mathrm{P}(B^{\prime})} \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.42}{0.7} \\[12pt]
    &= 0.6
    \end{aligned}
    $$
