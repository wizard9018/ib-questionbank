# AA379

**Maximum mark: 5**

$A$ and $B$ are independent events with $\mathrm{P}(A) = 0.28$ and
$\text{\(\mathrm{P}(A \cup B) = 0.75\)}$. Find $\mathrm{P}(B)$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the combined events formula, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{9.5em} \mathrm{P}(A\cup B) &= \mathrm{P}(A) + \mathrm{P}(B) - \mathrm{P}(A\cap B) \\[8pt]
\mathrm{P}(A\cup B) &= \mathrm{P}(A) + \mathrm{P}(B) - \mathrm{P}(A)\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(B) \quad \quad [\text{by independence}] \\[8pt]
0.75 &= 0.28 + \mathrm{P}(B) - 0.28\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(B) \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hspace{3.2em} \mathrm{P}(B) \approx 0.653 \hspace{2.5em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
$$
