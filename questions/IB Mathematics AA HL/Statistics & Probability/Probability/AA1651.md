# AA1651

**Maximum mark: 6**

A factory uses three machines, $A$, $B$, and $C$, to manufacture items. The table below shows the percentage of the total output produced by each machine and the probability that an item produced by that machine is defective.

|$\hspace{1.5em}\textbf{Machine}\hspace{1.5em}$|$\hspace{1.5em}\textbf{Percentage of output}\hspace{1.5em}$|$\hspace{1.5em}\textbf{P(defective)}\hspace{1.5em}$|

|:---:|:---:|:---:|

| $A$ | $50\%$ | 0.02 |

| $B$ | $30\%$ | 0.05 |

| $C$ | $20\%$ | 0.08 |

An item is chosen at random from the factory's total output.

1.  Find the probability that the item is defective.[3 marks]

2.  Given that the item is defective, find the probability that it was produced by Machine $C$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  There are three ways an item can be defective, depending on whether it was produced by Machine $A$, Machine $B$, or Machine $C$. These possibilities are shown on the tree diagram below.

    ![AA1651](images/AA1651.JPEG)

    Hence using the law of total probability we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

      \mathrm{P}(D) &= \mathrm{P}(A) \cdot \mathrm{P}(D \mid A) + \mathrm{P}(B) \cdot \mathrm{P}(D \mid B) + \mathrm{P}(C) \cdot \mathrm{P}(D \mid C) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]

           &= 0.50 \times 0.02 + 0.30 \times 0.05 + 0.20 \times 0.08 \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}} \\[6pt]

      \mathrm{P}(D) &= \boxed{0.041} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2.  We are told that the item is defective and asked for the probability that it came from Machine $C$. This reverses the natural order of events, in which an item is first produced and then found to be defective. This tells us we can apply Bayes' theorem. Using the formula for **Bayes' theorem (4.13)** with the result from part (a), we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

      \mathrm{P}(C \mid D) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(D \mid C) \cdot \mathrm{P}(C)}{\mathrm{P}(D)} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]

                  &= \dfrac{0.08 \times 0.20}{0.041} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}} \\[6pt]

                  &= 0.3902\ldots \\[6pt]

      \mathrm{P}(C \mid D) &= \boxed{0.390}\hspace{1em}\text{(3 s.f.)} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
