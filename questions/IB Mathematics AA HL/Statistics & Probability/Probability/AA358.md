# AA358

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two events $A$ and $B$ are such that $\mathrm{P}(A \cup B) = 0.78$,
$\mathrm{P}(A \cap B) = 0.27$ and
$\mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.1em}|\hspace{0.1em}B) = 0.45$.

1.  Find $\mathrm{P}(B)$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $\mathrm{P}(A)$. [2 marks]

3.  Hence show that events $A'$ and $B$ are independent. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the conditional probability formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.1em}|\hspace{0.1em}B) &=  \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(A \cap B)}{\mathrm{P}(B)} \hspace{1.1em} \\[6pt]
    0.45 &= \dfrac{0.27}{\mathrm{P}(B)} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{7.7em} \mathrm{P}(B) = 0.6 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

2.  Using the combined events formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.9em} \mathrm{P}(A \cup B) &= \mathrm{P}(A) + \mathrm{P}(B) - \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) \\[6pt]
    0.78 &= \mathrm{P}(A) + 0.6 - 0.27 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{8.2em} \mathrm{P}(A) = 0.45 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

3.  Using the equality
    $A' \cap B = B\hspace{-0.05em}\setminus\hspace{-0.1em}(A \cap B)$,
    we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.2em} \mathrm{P}(A' \cap B) &= \mathrm{P}(B) - \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) \\[6pt]
    &= 0.6 - 0.27 \\[6pt]
    &= 0.33
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the complementary events formula, we
    have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A')\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(B) &= [1 - \mathrm{P}(A)]\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(B) \\[6pt]
    &= [1 - 0.45](0.6) \\[6pt]
    &= 0.33 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $\text{Since \(\mathrm{P}(A' \cap B) = 0.33 = \mathrm{P}(A') \mathrm{P}(B)\), events \(A'\) and \(B\) are independent.}$
