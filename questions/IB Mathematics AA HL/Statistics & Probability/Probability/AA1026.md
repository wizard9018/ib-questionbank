# AA1026

**Maximum mark: 5**

Consider two events $A$ and $B$ such that $\mathrm{P}(A)=k$, $\mathrm{P}(B)=2k$, $\mathrm{P}(A \cap B) = 4k^2-0.11$ and $\mathrm{P}(A \cup B) = 0.65$.

Find the value of $k$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the formula for combined events, we have

$$

\begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(A \cup B) &= \mathrm{P}(A) + \mathrm{P}(B) - \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) \\[8pt]

    0.65 &= k + 2k - (4k^2 - 0.11) \\[8pt]

    0.65 &= 3k - 4k^2 + 0.11 \\[8pt]

    4k^2 - 3k + 0.54 &= 0 \\[8pt]

    k &= 0.3 \text{ or } 0.45 \hspace{3em} \text{[using G.D.C.]}

\end{aligned}

$$

If $k=0.45$, we have $\mathrm{P}(A)=0.45$ and

$$

\begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) &= 4(0.45)^2-0.11 \\[8pt]

    &= 0.7

\end{aligned}

$$

$\mathrm{P}(A \cap B)$ cannot be greater than $\mathrm{P}(A)$ and therefore $k\neq0.45$. $\boxed{k=0.3}$
