# AA362

**Maximum mark: 11**

Elizabeth and Georgia decided to cycle a total of
$750$ km in $30$ days. To help them decide
whether it is safe to go cycling, they classify each day in June as
rainy or dry. Given that a day in June is dry, the probability that the
next day is rainy is $0.25$. Given that a day in June is rainy, the
probability that the next day is rainy is $0.85$. The weather forecast
for the $1$st of June predicts that the probability that it will be dry
is $0.8$.

1.  Draw a tree diagram to display all the possible outcomes for the
    first three days of June. [4 marks]

2.  Find the probability that the $3$rd of June is rainy. [3 marks]

3.  $\text{Find the probability that the \(1\)st of June}$ was dry
    given that the $3$rd of June is dry.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Drawing a tree diagram to display all the possible outcomes for the
    first three days of June, we have

    ![b1b8b3e1cbf17007422f3deb5a51c6ddd3d63a0f.svg](images/b1b8b3e1cbf17007422f3deb5a51c6ddd3d63a0f.svg)

    D : Dry;
    R : Rainy.

2.  Using the tree diagram from part (a), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(3\text{rd Jun Rainy}) &= \mathrm{P}(\text{DDR}) + \mathrm{P}(\text{DRR}) + \mathrm{P}(\text{RDR}) + \mathrm{P}(\text{RRR}) \\[8pt]
    &= (0.8)(0.75)(0.25)+(0.8)(0.25)(0.85)+ \cdots \\[8pt]
    &\phantom{= (0.8)(0.75)(0.25)\hspace{0.28em}}+(0.2)(0.15)(0.25)+(0.2)(0.85)(0.85) \\[8pt]
    &= 0.472
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the conditional probability formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.6em}\mathrm{P}(\text{$1$st Jun Dry}\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}\text{$3$rd Jun Dry}) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(\text{$1$st Jun Dry and $3$rd Jun Dry})}{\mathrm{P}(\text{$3$rd Jun Dry})} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(\text{DDD}) + \mathrm{P}(\text{DRD})}{1 - \mathrm{P}(\text{$3$rd Jun Rainy})} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{(0.8)(0.75)(0.75) + (0.8)(0.25)(0.15)}{1 - 0.472} \\[12pt]
    &\approx 0.909
    \end{aligned}
    $$
