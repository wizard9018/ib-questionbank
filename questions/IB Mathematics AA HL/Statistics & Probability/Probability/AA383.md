# AA383

**Maximum mark: 4**

$A$ and $B$ are two events such that
$\mathrm{P}(A) = 0.5, \mathrm{P}(B) = 0.7$ and
$\mathrm{P}(A \cup B) = 0.85$.

1.  Find $\mathrm{P}(A \cap B)$. [2 marks]

2.  Determine whether events $A$ and $B$ are independent. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the combined events formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \mathrm{P}(A \cup B) &= \mathrm{P}(A) + \mathrm{P}(B) - \mathrm{P}(A\cap B) \\[6pt]
    0.85 &= 0.5 + 0.7 - \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) \\[6pt]
    \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) &= 0.5 + 0.7 - 0.85 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) = 0.35 \hspace{7.1em}
    $$

2.  Using the conditional probability formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}B) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(A \cap B)}{\mathrm{P}(B)}\hspace{3em} \\[5pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.35}{0.7} \\[6pt]
    &= 0.5 \\[6pt]
    &= \mathrm{P}(A) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence
     $\text{\(A\) and \(B\) are independent}$
