# AA429

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following Venn diagram shows two events $A$ and $B$, where
$\mathrm{P}(A) = 0.3$, $\mathrm{P}(B) = 0.8$ and $\mathrm{P}(A\,\cap\,B) = 0.2$. The values of $p$, $q$, $r$ and $s$ are probabilities.

![6cea7543b52909d937298497fa90fc1b3f7389b8.svg](images/6cea7543b52909d937298497fa90fc1b3f7389b8.svg)

1.  Find the value of $r$, $p$, $q$, and $s$. [4 marks]

2.  Find
    $\mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}B^{\prime})$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    r = 0.2 \hspace{5.2em}
    $$
    \
    Using the Venn diagram, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A) &= p + \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) \hspace{2.3em} \\[6pt]
    0.3 &= p + 0.2 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    p = 0.1 \hspace{5.2em}
    $$

    Using the Venn diagram, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(B) &= \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) + q \hspace{2.4em} \\[6pt]
    0.8 &= 0.2 + q \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    q = 0.6 \hspace{5.15em}
    $$

   $$
   \begin{aligned}
   \hspace{0.55em} \mathrm{P}(U) &= \mathrm{P}(A \cup B) + s \\[6pt]
   1 &= (p + r + q) + s \\[6pt]
   1 &= (0.1 + 0.2 + 0.6) + s \\[10pt]
   &\hspace{-0.75em}s = 0.1
   \end{aligned}
   $$

2.  If we shade in the region that represents
    $A \cap B^{\prime}$, we have

    ![1e26e9ce5436705e1f13a9b121cf8501a3704284.svg](images/1e26e9ce5436705e1f13a9b121cf8501a3704284.svg)

    Hence, using the conditional probability formula, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A\hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}B^{\prime}) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(A \cap B^{\prime})}{\mathrm{P}(B^{\prime})} \hspace{0.5em}= \dfrac{0.1}{0.2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
