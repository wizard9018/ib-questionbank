# AA416

**Maximum mark: 6**

Sophie can randomly select one chocolate from a large box full of
chocolates of different types and sizes.

The probability that a chocolate is:

- sugar free is $0.6$;

- king size is $0.25$;

- sugar free or king size
    is $0.8$.

Find the probability that the chocolate chosen by Sophie is **not** a
sugar free king size.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{P}(\text{SF}) &= 0.6\phantom{5} \hspace{3em} [\text{SF: sugar free}] \\[6pt]
\mathrm{P}(\text{KS}) &= 0.25 \hspace{3em} [\text{KS: king size}] \\[6pt]
\hspace{7.6em} \mathrm{P}(\text{SF} \cup \text{KS}) &= 0.8
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, using the combined events formula, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{P}(\text{SF} \cup \text{KS}) &= \mathrm{P}(\text{SF}) + \mathrm{P}(\text{KS}) - \mathrm{P}(\text{SF} \cap \text{KS}) \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
0.8 &= 0.6 + 0.25 - \mathrm{P}(\text{SF} \cap \text{KS}) \\[6pt]
\mathrm{P}(\text{SF} \cap \text{KS}) &= 0.05
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence we
obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{P}(\text{not ``sugar free king size''}) &= 1 - \mathrm{P}(\text{SF} \cap \text{KS}) \\[6pt]
&= 1 - 0.05 \\[6pt]
&= 0.95
\end{aligned}
$$
