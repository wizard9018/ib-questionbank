# AA414

**Maximum mark: 6**

Two events $A$ and $B$ are such that $\mathrm{P}(A) = 0.25$ and
$\mathrm{P}(A\cup B) = 0.7$.

Find $\mathrm{P}(B)$, given that $A$ and $B$ are:

1.  mutually exclusive; [2 marks]

2.  independent. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the mutually exclusive events formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(A \cup B) &= \mathrm{P}(A) + \mathrm{P}(B) \hspace{2.3em} \\[8pt]
    0.7 &= 0.25 + \mathrm{P}(B) \\[8pt]
    0.7 - 0.25 &= \mathrm{P}(B) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \mathrm{P}(B) = 0.45 \hspace{4.2em}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10em} \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) &= \mathrm{P}(A)\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(B) \hspace{2.7em} [\text{A \& B are independent}] \\[6pt]
    &= 0.25\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(B)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the
    combined events formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.2em} \mathrm{P}(A \cup B) &= \mathrm{P}(A) + \mathrm{P}(B) - \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) \\[12pt]
    0.7 &= 0.25 + \mathrm{P}(B) - 0.25\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(B) \\[6pt]
    0.45 &= 0.75\hspace{0.05em}\mathrm{P}(B) \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{0.45}{0.75} &= \mathrm{P}(B) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \mathrm{P}(B) = 0.6 \hspace{4.7em}
    $$
