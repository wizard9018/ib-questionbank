# AA1162

**Maximum mark: 6**

In Andy's athletics club, $8$ runners are training for the $100\,$ metre sprint.

Andy decided to analyse the training data of the runners to see if he could improve his own performance.

He decided to record the number of hours per day each athlete trained and their personal best time for the $100\,$metres.

The following data shows a summary of his findings.

|$\hspace{0.5em}\text{Average hours per day training }(h)\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}1.51\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}0.915\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}1.30\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}0.511\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}1.09\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}0.711\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}1.96\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}1.78\hspace{0.5em}$|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|Personal best time in seconds $(t)$|11.2|12.0|11.6|12.9|11.6|11.8|10.8|11.0|

1.  1.  Find Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient $r$, for this data.

    2.  Comment on the correlation of the two variables.  [2 marks]

Andy wants to use the data to make a prediction about his own training.

He finds the regression equation for $t$ on $h$.

The gradient of this line is $m$.

2.  Find $m$. [1 mark]

3.  Andy uses this value of $m$ to predict how much he can reduce his personal best by.
    1.  He trains an extra $30$ minutes per day. Using the value of $m$, predict how much time he can reduce his personal best by.

    2.  Briefly describe one factor that could cause an error with his prediction?  [3 marks]
