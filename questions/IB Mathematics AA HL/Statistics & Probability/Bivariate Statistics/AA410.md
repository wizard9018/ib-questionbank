# AA410

**Maximum mark: 12**

The following table shows values of $\ln x$ and $\ln y$.

![8c64f77af5ec93279ceb96452d0a87f353ec5871.svg](images/8c64f77af5ec93279ceb96452d0a87f353ec5871.svg)

The relationship between $\ln x$ and $\ln y$ can be modelled by the
regression equation $\ln y = a \ln x + b$.

1.  Find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$. [3 marks]

2.  Use the regression equation to estimate the value of
     $y$  when
     $x = 4.12$. [3 marks]

The relationship between $x$ and $y$ can be modelled using the formula
$y = px^q$, where $p\neq 0$ and $q \neq 0$, $q\neq 1$.

3.  Expressing $\ln y$ in terms of $\ln x$, find the value of
    $p$ and the value of $q$. [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $a \approx -0.564$
    and  $b \approx 4.74$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

2.  Substituting $x = 4.12$ in the equation
    $\ln y = -0.564\ln x + 4.74$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.8em} \ln y &= -0.564\ln (4.12) + 4.74 \\[6pt]
    y &= e^{-0.564\ln (4.12) + 4.74} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    y \approx 51.5 \hspace{6em}
    $$

3.  Using the properties of logarithms, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \ln y &= -0.564\ln x + 4.74 \\[6pt]
    \ln y &= \ln x^{-0.564} + \ln e^{4.74} \hspace{0.5em} \\[6pt]
    \ln y &= \ln\hspace{-0.15em}\big([x^{-0.564}][e^{4.74}]\big) \\[6pt]
    y &= [e^{4.74}][x^{-0.564}] \\[6pt]
    y &= 114\hspace{0.05em}x^{-0.564} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we obtain
     $p \approx 114$
    and
     $q \approx -0.564$
