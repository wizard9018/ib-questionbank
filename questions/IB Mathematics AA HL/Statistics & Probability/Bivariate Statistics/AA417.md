# AA417

**Maximum mark: 6**

A coffee shop owner believes that the number of coffees they sell on a
given day is related to the average temperature of
that day. The average temperature $T$, in degrees
Celsius, on seven randomly selected days is shown in the
following table. The table also shows the number
of coffees, $N$, the coffee shop sells on those
seven days.

![e182b3471e48ce381f17d81a5fd34ed73e64dd78.svg](images/e182b3471e48ce381f17d81a5fd34ed73e64dd78.svg)

1.  The relationship between the variables can be modelled by the
    regression equation $N = aT+b$.

    1.  Find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$.
    2.  To the nearest integer, estimate the number of coffees sold on a day when    the average temperature is
        $16$ $\text{\textdegree{C}}$. [4 marks]

2.  Find the value of $r$, the correlation coefficient. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $a \approx -9.93$
        and
         $b \approx 222$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    2.  Evaluating $N = -9.93\hspace{0.05em}T + 222$ for $T = 16$, we
        get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        N &= -9.93(16) + 222 \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
        &= 63.12 \\[6pt]
        &\approx 63 \text{ coffees}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    $r \approx -0.955 \hspace{5em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]$
