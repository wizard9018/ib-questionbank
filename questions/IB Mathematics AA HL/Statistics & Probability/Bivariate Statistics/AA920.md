# AA920

**Maximum mark: 7**

A real estate company keeps a register of the monthly cost of rent, $R$, of their apartments and their corresponding area, $A$, in m$^2$. 

The areas of the apartments registered are summarised in the following box and whisker diagram.

![AA920a](images/AA920a.PNG)

1. Find the smallest area $A$ that would not be considered an outlier. [3 marks]

The regression line $A$ on $R$ is $A=\dfrac{5}{12}R-50$.

Meanwhile, the regression line $R$ on $A$ is $R=\dfrac{5}{2}A+100$.

2. One of the apartments has a monthly rent of $$\$480$$. Estimate the area of the rental. [2 marks]

3. Find the mean rental cost of all the real estate company's apartments. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. We have

    ![AA920b](images/AA920b.PNG)

    Hence we obtain

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \text{IQR} &= q_3 - q_1 \\[10pt]

    &= 276 - 176 \\[10pt]

    &= 100

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    In general, the smallest value in a data set that is not considered an outlier is $q_1 - 1.5 \times \text{IQR}$. Therefore, the smallest area $A$ that would not be considered an outlier is

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A &= 176 - 1.5\times 100  \\[10pt]

    &\hspace{-0.75em}\boxed{A = 26\hspace{0.15em}\text{m}^2}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Substituting $R = 480$ into the regression equation $A$ on $R$, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A &= \dfrac{5}{12}(480)-50  \\[10pt]

    &= 150

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    An apartment with a rent of \$480 has an area of approximately $150\hspace{0.15em}\text{m}^2$

3. We need to find the value of $\bar{R}$. By definition, the mean point $(\bar{R},\bar{A})$, or $(\bar{A},\bar{R})$, lies on both regression lines. Hence the mean point will be found at the intersection of the regression lines. 

    By substituting $A=\dfrac{5}{12}R-50$ into the regression equation $R$ on $A$, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    R &= \dfrac{5}{2}\bigg(\dfrac{5}{12}R-50\bigg)+100  \\[10pt]

    R &= \dfrac{25R}{24} -\dfrac{250}{2} + 100 \\[10pt]

    R &= \dfrac{25R}{24} -25 \\[10pt]

    25 &= \dfrac{25R}{24} - R \\[10pt]

    25 &= \dfrac{R}{24} \\[10pt]

    R &= 25(24)

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    The mean rental cost is $$\$600$$
