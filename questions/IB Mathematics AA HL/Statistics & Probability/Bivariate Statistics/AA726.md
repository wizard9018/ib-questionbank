# AA726

**Maximum mark: 12**

The following table shows the total revenue, $y$, in Australian dollars
(AUD), $\text{obtained}$ daily during the first week of January
$2020$, by Peppy's Pizza $\text{restaurant}$ and the number of
guests, $x$, served.

![9736e8eb9c9cfe8a065bfa8f5344721e7887b704.svg](images/9736e8eb9c9cfe8a065bfa8f5344721e7887b704.svg)

1.
    1.  Calculate the Pearson's product-moment correlation coefficient,
        $r$, for this data.

    2.  Comment on the result. [3 marks]

2.  Write down the equation of the regression line $y$ on $x$.
    [1 mark]

3.  Use the line of the regression to estimate the revenue of serving
    $70$ guests. Give your answer correct to **the nearest AUD**.
    [2 marks]

The daily maintenance cost for the restaurant is $300$ AUD. Additionally,
the cost of serving one guest is $5$ AUD.

4.  Determine if the restaurant expects to make a profit when serving
    $50$ guests on a $\text{particular}$ day. [2 marks]

5.
    1.  Find an expression for the profit of the restaurant when serving
        $x$ guests on a particular day.

    2.  Find the least number of guests required to be served to result
        in a profit for the day. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $r = 0.991$ $\hspace{3em}$\[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]
    2.
        $\text{Strong, positive correlation between the number of guests and revenue}$

2.
    By using G.D.C.,
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 10.41...(x) - 14.255... \\[6pt]
    &y = 10.4x - 14.3\hspace{0.5em} \text{(3 s.f.)}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Evaluating $y = 10.4x - 14.3$ for $x = 70$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 10.4(70) - 14.3 \\[6pt]
    &= 713.7 \\[6pt]
    &=\boxed{714 \text{ AUD}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The expected profit of the restaurant when serving $50$ guests on a
    particular day is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Profit} &= \text{Revenue} - \text{Costs} \\[6pt]
    &= 10.4(50) - 14.3 - (300 + 5(50)) \\[6pt]
    &= -44.3 \text{ AUD} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $\text{Hence the restaurant expects to make a loss when serving only \(50\) guests.}$

5.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3.4em} \mathrm{Profit} &= \text{Revenue} - \text{Costs} \\[6pt]
        &= (10.4x - 14.3) - (300 + 5x) \\[6pt]
        &= 10.4x - 14.3 - 300 - 5x \\[6pt]
        &= 5.4x - 314.3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Solving the inequality $\text{Profit} > 0$ for $x$, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.1em} 5.4x - 314.3 &> 0 \\[6pt]
        x &> 58.2 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence
         $\text{at least \(59\) guests}$
        are required to be served to result in a profit.
