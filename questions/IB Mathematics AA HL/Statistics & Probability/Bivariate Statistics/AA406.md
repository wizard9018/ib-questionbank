# AA406

**Maximum mark: 6**

Extended power failure occurred in John's neighbourhood last
winter. He $\text{measured}$ the temperature of his house as air
inside cooled.

The following table shows air temperature $T$, in degrees Celsius, $t$
hours after power failed.

|$\hspace{0.5em}t\text{ (hours)}\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}0\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}4\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}8\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}12\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}16\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}20\hspace{0.5em}$|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|$T\,\,(^\circ C)$|$\hspace{0.5em}22.0\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}18.2\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}12.5\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}9.84\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}4.48\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}1.15\hspace{0.5em}$|

1.
    1.  Write down the independent variable.

    2.  Write down the warmest air temperature of the house. [2 marks]

John believes that the relationship between $T$ and $t$ can be modelled
by a linear regression equation.

2.  John describes the correlation as **very strong**. Circle
    the value below which best represents the correlation coefficient.
    [2 marks]

    |$\hspace{2.5em}0.997\hspace{3em}0.272\hspace{3em}0\hspace{2.5em}-0.272\hspace{2.5em}-0.997\hspace{2.5em}$|
    |:---:|

3.  John's model is $T = -1.06\hspace{0.05em}t + 22.0$, for
    $0 \leq t \leq 20$. Use this model to predict the decrease in
    temperature for any $2$ hour interval. [2 marks]
