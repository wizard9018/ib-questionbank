# AA453

**Maximum mark: 15**

A study records the number of chickens and ducks on a farm after $t$
years, starting on $1$ January, $1997$.

Let $c$ be the number of chickens on the farm after $t$ years. The
following table shows the number of chickens after $t$ years.

![445a139e443a870563b0c4df38d9119c2a8c93c1.svg](images/445a139e443a870563b0c4df38d9119c2a8c93c1.svg)

The relationship between the variables can be modelled by the regression
equation $c = at + b$, where $a$ and $b$ are constants.

1.  Find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$. [3 marks]

2.  Use the regression equation to estimate the number of chickens on
    the farm when $t = 6$. [2 marks]

Let $d$ be the number of ducks on the farm after $t$ years. The number
of ducks can be modelled by the equation $d = 400e^{-kt}$, where $k$ is
a constant.

3.  Find the number of ducks on the farm on $1$ January,
    $1997$. [3 marks]

4.  After six years, there are $377$ ducks on the farm. Find
    the value of $k$. [3 marks]

5.  Find the year during which the number of chickens and ducks
    were the same.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $a \approx 14.9$  and
     $b \approx 119$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

2.  Evaluating $c(t) = 14.9t + 119$ for $t = 6$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.8em} c(6) &= 14.9(6) + 119 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 208 \text{ chickens}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Evaluating $d(t) = 400e^{-kt}$ for $t = 0$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.2em} d(0) &= 400e^{-k(0)} \\[6pt]
    &= 400 \text{ ducks}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Solving the equation $d(6) = 377$ for $k$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    400e^{-k(6)} &= 377 \hspace{4.9em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{11.1em} k \approx 0.00987 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

5.  Solving the equation $c(t) = d(t)$ for $t$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7.9em} 14.9t + 119 &= 400e^{-0.00987t} \\[6pt]
    t &\approx 15.1 \text{ years} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the number of chickens and ducks were the same during
     $2012$
