# AA436

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following table shows the number of skateboards $y$ that are sold
$x$ days after the shop opened.

![8ca41bbf1b72b153da5e126adf69caff34abe2e2.svg](images/8ca41bbf1b72b153da5e126adf69caff34abe2e2.svg)

This data can be modelled by the regression line with equation
$y = ax + b$.

1.
    1.  Write down the value of $a$ and the value of $b$.

    2.  Explain what the gradient $a$ represents. [4 marks]

2.  Use the model to estimate the number of skateboards sold in $5$
    days after the shop opened. Round
    your answer to the nearest integer. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $a \approx 4.44$
        and
         $b \approx 1.07$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    2.  :::answer
        $4.44\hspace{0.025em};\hspace{0.25em}$ each day after the shop opened the number of skateboards sold increases by $4.44$

2.  Evaluating $y = 4.44x + 1.07$ for $x = 5$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y &= 4.44(5) + 1.07 \hspace{1.5em} \\[6pt]
    &= 23.27 \\[6pt]
    &\approx 23 \text{ skateboards}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
