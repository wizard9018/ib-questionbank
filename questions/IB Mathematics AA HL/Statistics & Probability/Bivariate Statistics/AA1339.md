# AA1339

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following table shows the results of a study measuring the amount of sleep, $S$ hours, and blood pressure, $B$ mmHg.

| $\textrm{Hours of sleep (S)}$ &nbsp; |&nbsp; $4.2$ &nbsp;|&nbsp; $a$ &nbsp;| &nbsp;$6.3$&nbsp; | &nbsp;$7.8$&nbsp; | &nbsp;$7.4$&nbsp; | &nbsp;$8.2$&nbsp; | &nbsp;$8.7$&nbsp; | &nbsp;$9.0$&nbsp; |

| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |

| Blood Pressure (B)|&nbsp; $142$ &nbsp;|&nbsp; $138$ &nbsp;|&nbsp; $134$ &nbsp;|&nbsp; $\,\,\,b\,\,\,$ &nbsp;| &nbsp; $128$ &nbsp; | &nbsp; $118$ &nbsp; | &nbsp; $116$ &nbsp; | &nbsp; $\,111\,$ &nbsp; |

The mean point is found to be $(\,7.125,\,126\,)$.

1.    Find the value of

    1.    $a$. 

    2.    $b$. [3 marks]

2.    Hence find the equation of the regression line $S$ on $B$. [2 marks]

A person is considered to have high blood pressure if their blood pressure measures $130$ mmHg or higher.

3.    Find an estimate for the maximum number of hours of sleep for an adult with a high blood pressure. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 1.   The mean point tells us that $\large\bar{x}=7.125$ and $\large\bar{y}=126$.

        Hence we can add all the $x$ values and divide by the number of terms to find the mean.

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

            \tag*{\textbf{{(M1)}}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

            7.125&=\frac{4.2+a+6.3+7.8+7.4+8.2+8.7+9.0}{8}\\[9pt]

            57&=51.6+a\\[6pt]

            a&=5.4\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

    

    2.  In a similar fashion we can find $b$.

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

            126&=\frac{142+138+134+b+128+118+116+111}{8}\\[9pt]

            1008&=887+b\\[6pt]

            b&=121\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

2. Now that we have all the data points we can use the G.D.C. to find the regression line.

    We are asked for the regression line of $S$ on $B$ (sleep _on_ blood pressure).

    This means we want to have a line that estimates sleep $(S)$ given a known value for blood pressure.

    So $B$ is the independent variable and $S$ is the dependent variable. When entering our lists we want to make $B$ the $x$ list and $S$ the $y$ list.

    Hence we find $S=-0.147B+25.6$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{{A2}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. We can use the equation found in (b) to estimate the maximum number of hours of sleep for a person with high blood pressure. 

    Let's substitute $130$ into the equation to find $S$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    S&=-0.1467...(130)+25.61...\\[6pt]

    &=6.538...\\[6pt]

    &=6.54\text{ hours}\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
