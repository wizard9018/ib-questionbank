# AA458

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following table below shows the points scored by eight basketball
players in two games.

|$\hspace{0.5em}\text{Game 1 }(x)\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}5\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}6\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}9\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}12\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}12\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}16\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}20\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}25\hspace{0.5em}$|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|Game 2 $(y)$|6|8|12|13|15|17|24|22|

Let $L_1$ be the regression line of $x$ on $y$. The equation of the line
$L_1$ can be written in the form $x = ay + b$.

1.  Find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$. [2 marks]

Let $L_2$ be the regression line of $y$ on $x$. The lines $L_1$ and
$L_2$ both pass through the same point with coordinates $(p,q)$.

2.  Find the value of $p$ and the value of $q$. [2 marks]

3.  Andrew's injury didn't allow him to play in the first game
    but he played and scored $10$ points in the second game. Use an
    appropriate regression equation to estimate the number of points
    that Andrew would have scored in the first game. Round your answer
    to the nearest point. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Carefully solving for $x$ on $y$ we find
    $a \approx 1.05$  and
     $b \approx -2.16$
      \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

2.  Both lines pass through the mean point of the data given in the
    table.

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8em} p &= \bar{x} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 13.1 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\[12pt]
    q &= \bar{y} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 14.6 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Evaluating $x = 1.05y - 2.16$ for $y = 10$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x &= 1.05(10) - 2.16 \hspace{0.4em} \\[6pt]
    &= 8.34 \\[6pt]
    &= 8 \text{ points}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
