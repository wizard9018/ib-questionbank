# AA405

**Maximum mark: 5**

The maximum temperature $T$, in degrees Celsius, on six randomly
selected days is shown in the following table. The table also shows the
number of soda cans purchased, $N$ , from a
vending machine on those six days.

![b5d0bcdbf9120dbd7d7dee1dd6d718bafc18d83b.svg](images/b5d0bcdbf9120dbd7d7dee1dd6d718bafc18d83b.svg)

1.  The relationship between the variables can be modelled by the
    regression equation $N = aT+b$.

    1.  Find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$.
    2.  Estimate the number of soda cans purchased on a day when\
        the maximum temperature is $23$ $\text{\textdegree{C}}$.
        [4 marks]

2.  Find the value of $r$, the correlation coefficient. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $a \approx 0.702$
        and
         $b \approx 22.9$
          \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\]

    2.  Evaluating $N = 0.702\hspace{0.05em}T + 22.9$ for $T = 23$, we
        get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        N &= 0.702(23) + 22.9 \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
        &= 39.046 \\[6pt]
        &\approx 39 \text{ cans}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    $r \approx 0.931 \hspace{3em}\text{[by using G.D.C.]}$
