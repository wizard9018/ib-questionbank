# AA418

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following table shows the average weights
($y$ kg) for given heights
($x$ cm) in a population
of high school males.

|$\hspace{0.5em}\text{Heights }(x\text{ cm})\hspace{1em}$|$\hspace{1em}160\hspace{1em}$|$\hspace{1em}165\hspace{1em}$|$\hspace{1em}170\hspace{1em}$|$\hspace{1em}175\hspace{1em}$|$\hspace{1em}180\hspace{1em}$|$\hspace{1em}185\hspace{1em}$|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|Weights ($y$ kg)|62.0|65.9|68.4|71.8|74.6|76.3|

1.  The relationship between the variables can be modelled by the
    regression $\text{equation}$
    $y = ax+b$.

    1.  Write down the value of $a$ and the value of $b$.
    2.  Hence estimate the weight of a male whose height is
        $167$ cm. [4 marks]

2.
    1.  Write down the correlation coefficient.

    2.  State which **two** of the following describe the correlation
        between the variables.
        $$
        \begin{array}{lll}
        \text{negative} & \text{zero} & \text{no correlation} \\[6pt]
        \text{strong} & \text{weak} & \text{positive}
        \end{array} \hspace{1em}
        $$
        [3 marks]
