# AA426

**Maximum mark: 5**

There are $8$ numbers in a data set. The sum of the numbers is $48$.

1.  Find the mean. [2 marks]

The variance of this data set is $2$. Each number in the set is
multiplied by $3$.

2.  Find the value of:
    1.  the new mean;

    2.  the new variance. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{mean} &= \dfrac{48}{8} \hspace{3.85em} \\[6pt]
    &= 6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{new mean} &= 3\times(\text{old mean}) \hspace{2.9em} \\[6pt]
        &= 3\times6 \\[6pt]
        &= 18
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{new variance} &= 3^2\hspace{-0.1em}\times(\text{old variance}) \hspace{2.55em} \\[6pt]
        &= 3^2\hspace{-0.1em}\times2 \\[7pt]
        &= 18
        \end{aligned}
        $$
