    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{range} &= \text{max} - \text{min} \hspace{3em} \\[6pt]
        &= 11 - 1 \\[6pt]
        &= 10
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    1.  Using the mean formula, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.45em} \bar{x} &= \bigg[\sum_{i=1}^5 f_ix_i\bigg]\bigg/ \bigg[\sum_{i=1}^5 f_i\bigg] \\[5pt]
        &= \dfrac{(12)(1)+(20)(3)+(26)(6)+(32)(9)+(5)(11)}{12+20+26+32+5} \\[15pt]
        &\approx 6.01
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using our G.D.C. to calculate the variance, $\sigma^2$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \sigma^2 &= (3.06592...)^2 \hspace{5.275em} \\[8pt]
        &\approx 9.40
        \end{aligned}
        $$
