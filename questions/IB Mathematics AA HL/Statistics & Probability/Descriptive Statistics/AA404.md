# AA404

**Maximum mark: 5**

The following box-and-whisker plot represents the commute time, in
minutes, for a group of workers.

![9e28a9e7a6be31076ba32a58199c00ff7ac4e0ab.svg](images/9e28a9e7a6be31076ba32a58199c00ff7ac4e0ab.svg)

1.  Write down the median commute time. [1 mark]

The range is $23$ minutes, and the interquartile range is $10$ minutes.

2.  Find the value of:
    1.  $p$;

    2.  $q$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\text{median} = 22 \text{ min}$

2.  We have

    ![5afececa95307fe0573e845ed475d6688879b302.svg](images/5afececa95307fe0573e845ed475d6688879b302.svg)

    1.  Hence we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{range} &= \text{max} - \text{min} \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
        23 &= 37 - p \\[6pt]
        p &= 37 - 23 \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{-0.75em}p = 14
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Hence we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{IQR} &= q_3 - q_1 \hspace{4.6em} \\[6pt]
        10 &= q - 18 \\[6pt]
        10+18 &= q \\[6pt]
        &\hspace{-0.75em}q = 28
        \end{aligned}
        $$
