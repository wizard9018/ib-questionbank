# AA1304

**Maximum mark: 6**

The histogram below shows the number of working hours recorded by $145$

employees in a company during a particular week, each measured correct

to the nearest hour and then rounded to the nearest units of $10$ hours.

![AA1304q](images/AA1304q.JPEG)

1.  Write down the modal working time of the employees. [1 mark]

2.  Find the median working time. [3 marks]

 

The upper quartile is $50$ working hours.

3.  Calculate 

    1.  the lower quartile;

    2.  the interquartile range. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The modal value is the one that occurs at the highest frequency.

    From the histogram, we can see that the highest column is $40$,

    which is the frequency of $40$ working hours. Hence,

    

    $$

    \hspace{0.525em}\text{Modal working time} = 40 \text{ hours}

    $$

2.  Using the histogram, we can create a cumulative frequency table for

    the data, as follows

    |$\hspace{0.5em}$Working time, hours$\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}$Frequency$\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}$Cumulative frequency$\hspace{0.5em}$|$\hspace{0.5em}$Grouped $n$-th value$\hspace{0.5em}$|

    | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |

    |10|10|10|1-10|

    |20|15|25|11-25|

    |30|25|50|26-50|

    |40|40|90|51-90|

    |50|30|120|91-120|

    |60|20|140|121-140|

    |70|5|145|141-145|

    Using the formula for

    $\text{Median} = \dfrac 1 2 (n+1) \text{ th value}$ with $n=145$, we

    get 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \text{[Median]} &= \dfrac 1 2 (145+1) \text{ th value} \\

    &= 73 \text{ rd value} \\

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    The

    Median is the $73$rd value. The cumulative frequency table shows

    that this is in the row of $40$ hours. Hence,

    

    $$

    \hspace{0.525em}\text{Median working time} = 40 \text{ hours}.

    $$

3.  

    1.  Using the formula for $Q_1 = \dfrac 14(n+1) \text{ th value}$,

        we get 

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        Q_1 &= \dfrac 1 4 (145+1) \text{ th value} \\

        &= 36.5 \,\text{th value} 

        \end{aligned}

        $$

        So this is the average of the $36$th

        and $37$th values. The cumulative frequency table shows that

        both these values are $30$ hours. Hence, 

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        Q_1 &= \dfrac{30+30}2 \\[6pt]

        &\hspace{0.525em}= 30 \text{ hours}

        \end{aligned}

        $$

        

    2.  Using the interquartile range formula with $Q_3=50$ and

        $Q_1=30$, we get 

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        \text{IQR} &= Q_3 - Q_1 \\

        &= 50 - 30\\[6pt]

        & \hspace{0.525em}=20

        \end{aligned}

        $$
