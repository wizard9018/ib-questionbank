# AA431

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following cumulative frequency diagram shows the distance $d$, in
kilometres, students need to travel to get to school.

![3a843b422c1f5b2908abeaa8d4be3c920695dff9.svg](images/3a843b422c1f5b2908abeaa8d4be3c920695dff9.svg)

1.  Write down the median distance a student travels to school.
    [2 marks]

2.  Find the number of students who travel between
    $2$ km and
    $4$ km to get
    to school. [3 marks]

3.  Find the percentage of students who travel more than
    $4.5$ km to get
    to school. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\text{median} = 3 \text{ km}$

2.  Using the cumulative frequency diagram, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.1em} f(2 \leq d \leq 4) &= 130 - 30 \hspace{2.75em} [\text{$f$: frequency}] \\[6pt]
    &= 100 \text{ students}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the cumulative frequency diagram, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(d > 4.5) &= \dfrac{20}{160} \hspace{5.8em} \\[6pt]
    &= 0.125
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the percentage of students who travel
    more than $4.5$ km is
     $12.5\%$
