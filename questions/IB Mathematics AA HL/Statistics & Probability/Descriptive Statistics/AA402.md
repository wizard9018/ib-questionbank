# AA402

**Maximum mark: 6**

The cumulative frequency curve below represents the time spent by $120$
students to solve a challenging mathematics problem in class.

![a2c4f9406907d2f9018f779304bfc2c8dd701ec1.svg](images/a2c4f9406907d2f9018f779304bfc2c8dd701ec1.svg)

Use the graph to answer the following.

1.  Write down the median time. [1 mark]

2.  A student is chosen at random. Find the probability that this
    student spent less than $30$ minutes.
    [2 marks]

3.  Given that $90$ % of the students spent
    greater than $k$ minutes, find the value
    of $k$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $\text{median} = 34 \text{ min}$

2.  Using the cumulative frequency diagram, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(t < 30) &= \dfrac{F(30)}{F(60)} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{32}{120} \\[12pt]
    &\approx 0.267 \hspace{3em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We know $10$% of the students spent less than $k$ minutes.
    Hence we deduce
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.3em} F(k) &= 10\text{th percentile} \\[6pt]
    &= 0.1(120) \\[6pt]
    &= 12
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the cumulative frequency diagram,
    we find  $k = 20$
