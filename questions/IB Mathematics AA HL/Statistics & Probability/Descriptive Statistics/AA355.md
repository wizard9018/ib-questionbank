# AA355

**Maximum mark: 6**

The data of the goals scored by players in a futsal club during the
winter games are given in the following table.

![aa570444b72f1299be96ea27144d1264429491cc.svg](images/aa570444b72f1299be96ea27144d1264429491cc.svg)

1.  Given that the mean number of goals scored per player is $4.8$,
    find the value of $k$. [3 marks]

It is discovered that there is a mistake in the data and that two
players, who scored $2$ and $13$ goals, have not
been included in the table.

2.
    1.  Find the correct mean number of goals scored per player.

    2.  Find the correct standard deviation of the numbers of goals
        scored per player. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the mean formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \bar{x} &= \bigg[\sum_{i=1}^7 f_ix_i\bigg] \bigg/ \bigg[\sum_{i=1}^7 f_i\bigg] \\[5pt]
    4.8 &= \dfrac{(2)(0)+(3)(2)+(6)(3)+(k)(5)+(3)(7)+(2)(11)+(1)(13)}{2+3+6+k+3+2+1} \\[10pt]
    \hspace{4.0em} 4.8 &= \dfrac{5k+80}{k+17} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    k = 8 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{10.3em}
    $$

2.
    1.  Using the mean formula, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \bar{x}_{\text{correct}} &= \dfrac{(5(8) + 80) + (1)(2) + (1)(13)}{(8+17) + 1 + 1} \hspace{11.5em} \\[6pt]
        &= 5
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using G.D.C. we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        &\hspace{-12.275em}\sigma_{\text{correct}} \approx 3.45
        \end{aligned}
        $$
