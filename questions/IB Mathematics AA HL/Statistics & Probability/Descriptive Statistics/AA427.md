# AA427

**Maximum mark: 6**

A data set has a mean of $15$ and a standard deviation of $4$.

Each element in the data set has $5$ added to it.

1.  Find the value of:
    1.  the new mean;

    2.  the new standard deviation. [3 marks]

Each element in the original data set is multiplied by $5$.

2.  Find the value of:
    1.  the new mean;

    2.  the new variance. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{new mean} &= \text{old mean} + 5 \hspace{3.7em} \\[6pt]
        &= 15 + 5 \\[6pt]
        &= 20
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{new SD} &= \text{old SD} \hspace{5.35em} \\[6pt]
        &= 4
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{new mean} &= 5\times(\text{old mean}) \hspace{2.9em} \\[6pt]
        &= 5\times15 \\[6pt]
        &= 75
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{new variance} &= 5^2\hspace{-0.1em}\times(\text{old variance}) \hspace{2.5em} \\[6pt]
        &= 5^2\hspace{-0.1em}\times(\text{old SD})^2 \\[6pt]
        &= 5^2\hspace{-0.1em}\times4^2 \\[7pt]
        &= 400
        \end{aligned}
        $$
