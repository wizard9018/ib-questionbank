# AA408

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following box-and-whisker plot shows the number of tweets sent by
people in a coffee shop on a particular day.

![ebae3d0b08e3f773d62c15865c46eb9c6cd7747a.svg](images/ebae3d0b08e3f773d62c15865c46eb9c6cd7747a.svg)

1.  Find the value of the interquartile range. [2 marks]

2.  One person sent $k$ tweets, where $k > 7$. Given that $k$ is an
    outlier, find the least value of $k$.
    [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have

    ![e7dbe926c0ed4173f21a424ed08eaffe27133857.svg](images/e7dbe926c0ed4173f21a424ed08eaffe27133857.svg)

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{IQR} &= q_3 - q_1 \hspace{0.5em} \\[6pt]
    &= 7 - 2 \\[6pt]
    &= 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{upper inner fence} &= q_3 + 1.5(\text{IQR}) \hspace{3.2em} \\[6pt]
    &= 7 + 1.5(5) \\[6pt]
    &= 14.5 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the least value of $k$ is
     $15$
