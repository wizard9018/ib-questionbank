# AA1068

**Maximum mark: 6**

A study of the retirement age of a number of professional soccer players was conducted.

The following boxplot was produced from the results.

![AA1068Q](images/AA1068Q.JPEG)

1. Describe the distribution of the plot. [1 mark]

2. What is the interquartile range? [2 marks]

3. Due to an injury, a player is forced to retire early at age $18$. Justify whether or not this player would be classified as an outlier. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The plot has a long tail to the left of the $x-$axis, this is known as

    $\text{negative skew}$.

    $$                    

    \begin{aligned}            

    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}    

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The interquartile range is

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        IQR &=Q_3-Q_1\\

        &=34-27 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\

        &=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{7} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. Using the formula we can find the lower fence for outliers

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        &=Q_1-1.5\times IQR\\

        &=27-1.5\times 7 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\

        &=16.5 \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    The player's age of $18$ is larger than the lower fence for outliers. Therefore the player would $\boxed{ \text{not be an outlier}}$.

    $$                    

    \begin{aligned}            

    \tag*{\textbf{A1}}    

    \end{aligned}

    $$
