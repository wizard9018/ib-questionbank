# AA684

**Maximum mark: 7**

Paul records the duration, in minutes, of 10 customer service calls he
receives between 1 and 6 pm on Wednesday. The results are as follows

$$
\begin{aligned}
3.4\hspace{1.6em} 8.5\hspace{1.6em} 10\hspace{1.6em} 11.6\hspace{1.6em} 14\hspace{1.6em} 16.2\hspace{1.6em} 19\hspace{1.6em} 21.4\hspace{1.6em} 25.8\hspace{1.6em} 37.1
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Find the mean duration, in minutes, of the calls he received.
    [2 marks]

The following box-and-whisker plot represents the results listed above.

![0cc2d89607279da637de2689f3d3b23d6df2a35b.svg](images/0cc2d89607279da637de2689f3d3b23d6df2a35b.svg)

2.
    1.  Find the value of $p$.

    2.  Find the interquartile range. [3 marks]

Calls greater than $r$ minutes are considered
outliers.

3.  Find the value of $r$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the mean formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.1em} \bar{x} &= \dfrac{1}{10}\sum_{i = 1}^{10} x_i \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{10}(3.4+8.5+10+11.6+14+16.2+19+21.4+25.8+37.1) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{10}(167) \hspace{1em}= 16.7 \text{ min}\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  $p$ is the median, hence
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        p &= \dfrac{14+16.2}{2} \hspace{1.1em} = 15.1 \hspace{10em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have

        ![403a79bf5b933151bd212cbd918dba6588db15cb.svg](images/403a79bf5b933151bd212cbd918dba6588db15cb.svg)

        Hence we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{IQR} &= q_3 - q_1 \\[6pt]
        &= 21.4 - 10 \\[6pt]
        &= 11.4 \text{ min} \hspace{2.8em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= q_3 + 1.5(\text{IQR})  \\[7pt]
    &= 21.4 + 1.5(11.4) \\[7pt]
    &= 38.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
