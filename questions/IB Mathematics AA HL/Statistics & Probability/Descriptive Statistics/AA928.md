# AA928

**Maximum mark: 4**

The following table shows the number of overtime hours worked by employees in a company.

![AA928a](images/AA928a.PNG)

It is known that the mean number of overtime hours is $1$.

1. Find the value of $x$. [2 marks]

2. Find the standard deviation of the data. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using the mean formula, we get 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \bar{x} &= \bigg[\sum_{i=1}^5 f_ix_i\bigg]\bigg/ \bigg[\sum_{i=1}^5 f_i\bigg] \\[7pt]

    1 &= \dfrac{(17)(0)+(4)(1)+(3)(2)+(x)(3)+(2)(4)}{17+4+3+x+2} \\[7pt]

    1 &= \dfrac{18+3x}{26+x} \\[10pt]

    26+x &= 18+3x \\[12pt]

    2x &= 8 \\[12pt]

    &\hspace{-0.57em}\boxed{x = 4} 

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Using our G.D.C. to calculate the standard deviation, $\sigma$, we get 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &\hspace{-0.58em}\boxed{\sigma \approx 1.34} 

    \end{aligned}

    $$
