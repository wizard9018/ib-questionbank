# AA1022

**Maximum mark: 5**

There are $20$ players in local basketball club who have a competition to see how many free throws they can score from five attempts. The scores obtained by the 20 players are shown in the following table.

![Screenshot 2023-08-30 at 11.55.39 AM](<images/Screenshot 2023-08-30 at 11.55.39 AM.png>)

1. State the value of $x$. [1 mark]

2. One of the players is chosen at random. Find the probability that this player's score was $3$ or more. [2 marks]

3. Calculate the mean score. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Given that $\Sigma f = 20$, $x=4$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \mathrm{P}(\text{$3$ or more}) &= \dfrac{4+2+1}{20} \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{= \dfrac{7} {20}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3. Using the mean formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \bar{x} &= \dfrac{\sum_{i=1}^6 f_ix_i}{n} \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{(2)(0)+(7)(1)+(4)(2)+(4)(3)+(2)(4)+(1)(5)}{20} \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{40}{20} \\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-0.525em}\boxed{\bar{x} = 2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
