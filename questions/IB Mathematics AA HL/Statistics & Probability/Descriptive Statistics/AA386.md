# AA386

**Maximum mark: 4**

At a sprinting competition the mean time of the first three sprinters is
$10.5$ $\text{seconds}$. The time for the fourth sprinter is
then recorded and the mean time of the first four
sprinters is $11.0$ seconds.

Find the time achieved by the fourth sprinter.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the mean formula, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar{x}_{\text{three}} &= \dfrac{x_1 + x_2 + x_3}{3} \hspace{3em} \\[6pt]
10.5 &= \dfrac{x_1 + x_2 + x_3}{3} \\[10pt]
x_1 + x_2 + x_3 &= (10.5)(3) \\[15pt]
x_1 + x_2 + x_3 &= 31.5 \text{ seconds}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bar{x}_{\text{four}} &= \dfrac{x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + x_4}{4} \\[6pt]
11.0 &= \dfrac{31.5 + x_4}{4} \\[10pt]
\hspace{0.75em} 31.5 + x_4 &= (11.0)(4) \\[15pt]
x_4 &= 44.0 - 31.5 \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hspace{1.15em} x_4 = 12.5 \text{ seconds}
$$
