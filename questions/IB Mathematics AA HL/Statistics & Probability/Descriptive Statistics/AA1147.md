# AA1147

**Maximum mark: 6**

A dataset has a minimum value of $12$, a lower quartile of $18$ and a median of $22$.

A partially drawn boxplot of the dataset is shown below.

![AA1147_q](images/AA1147_q.JPEG)

It is known that the lower boundary for outliers is $9$.

1. Find the interquartile range. [2 marks]

The maximum value is known to be $26$.

2.  Hence, complete the sketch of
    the boxplot on the grid above. [3 marks]

3.  Describe the distribution of the dataset.[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  To find the interquartile range ($IQR$) we can use the following formula for the lower boundary ($LB$) of outliers
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        LB&=Q_1-1.5\times IQR\\[6pt]
        9&=18-1.5\times IQR\tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[6pt]
        1.5\times IQR&=9\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{-2.2em}IQR=6\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
2.  Using the formula for the $IQR$ we can find $Q_3$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        IQR&=Q_3-Q_1\tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
        6&=Q_3-18\\[6pt]
        Q_3&=24 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
