# AA444

**Maximum mark: 15**

The following cumulative frequency diagram shows the lengths of $80$
flower stems in a garden, in centimetres.

![921b64fb9e6a89f74d566714ee1961c1829f99b3.svg](images/921b64fb9e6a89f74d566714ee1961c1829f99b3.svg)

1.
    1.  Write down the median length.

    2.  Find the percentage of flower stems that are
        $60$ cm or greater. [4 marks]

The same data is presented in the following frequency table.

![f143e487c5fd5118594467694ae06b43fae4188f.svg](images/f143e487c5fd5118594467694ae06b43fae4188f.svg)

2.  Find the value of:
    1.  $p$;

    2.  $q$. [4 marks]

3.  Use the values from the frequency table above to estimate
    the mean and standard deviation of
    the lengths. [3 marks]

A flower with stem length of $60$ cm or greater
is considered as mature flower.

4.  Given that a randomly selected flower is mature, find the
    probability that its stem length
    is $90$ cm or greater. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $\text{median} = 50\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}$
    2.  Using the cumulative frequency diagram, we have

         $$
         \begin{aligned}
         \hspace{0.5em} \mathrm{P}(x \geq 60) &= \dfrac{F(120) - F(60)}{F(120)} \\[5pt]
         &= \dfrac{80-55}{80} \\[12pt]
         &= 0.3125
         \end{aligned}
         $$

    Hence the percentage of flower stems that are
    $60$ cm or greater is
     $31.25\%$

2.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        10 + p &= F(60) \hspace{0.7em} \\[6pt]
        10 + p &= 55 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        p = 45
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        10 + 45 + 20 + q &= F(120) \hspace{4.6em} \\[6pt]
        75 + q &= 80 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        q = 5 \hspace{0.5em}
        $$

3.  Evaluating the midpoints of the length intervals, we have

    ![0838315d2ce8123458abe04d067cfba49f85e835.svg](images/0838315d2ce8123458abe04d067cfba49f85e835.svg)

    Hence, using G.D.C., we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} \bar{x} = 52.5 \text{ cm}&  \\[9pt]
    \sigma = 22.5 \text{ cm}&
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using the frequency table, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} \mathrm{P}(x \geq 90) &= \dfrac{q}{80} \\[5pt]
    &= \dfrac{5}{80} \\[12pt]
    &= 0.0625 \hspace{2em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the conditional
    probability formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(x \geq 90 \hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}\text{mature flower}) &= \mathrm{P}(x \geq 90 \hspace{0.15em}|\hspace{0.15em}x \geq 60) \hspace{3.05em} \\[12pt]
    &= \dfrac{\mathrm{P}(x \geq 90)}{\mathrm{P}(x \geq 60)} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{0.0625}{0.3125} \\[12pt]
    &= 0.2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
