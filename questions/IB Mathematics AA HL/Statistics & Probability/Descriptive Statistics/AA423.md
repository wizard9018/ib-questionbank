# AA423

**Maximum mark: 6**

A school conducts a research into how many minutes each day their
students spend browsing the internet. Jake's class is studied first. The
number of minutes spent browsing the internet by each student in Jake's
class is shown in the following stem and leaf plot.

![3d3da4e0e583b1c9fd9eef0d86d8f4e2d11cbc6e.svg](images/3d3da4e0e583b1c9fd9eef0d86d8f4e2d11cbc6e.svg)

**Key:
$\bm{2}$ \| $\bm{3}$
represents $\bm{23}$ minutes**

1.
    1.  Write down the number of students in Jake's class.

    2.  Find the median number of minutes spent browsing the internet.
        [3 marks]

The following box-and-whisker plot also displays the number of
minutes spent browsing the internet by
students in Jake's class.

![cf76cc1c5a44d16ab1d972adbcd564f5afe0b2ba.svg](images/cf76cc1c5a44d16ab1d972adbcd564f5afe0b2ba.svg)

2.
    1.  Write down the value of $q$.

    2.  The interquartile range is $13$. Find the value of $p$.
        [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $\text{\# of students} = \text{\# of leafs} = 16$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{med} &= \text{middle leaf} \\[10pt]
        &= \dfrac{8\text{th leaf} + 9\text{th leaf}}{2} \hspace{1.3em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{31 + 33}{2} \\[11pt]
        &= 32 \text{ min}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  We have

    ![1446e39b0f014ccb70f21ba0d91c1a4eb128361e.svg](images/1446e39b0f014ccb70f21ba0d91c1a4eb128361e.svg)

    1.
        $q = \text{max} = 16\text{th leaf} = 45$

    2.  Hence we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{IQR} &= q_3 - q_1 \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
        13 &= 37 - p \\[6pt]
        p &= 37 - 13 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        p = 24 \hspace{2.65em}
        $$
