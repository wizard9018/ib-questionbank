# AA463

**Maximum mark: 16**

A primary school surveyed $120$ of its students to find out how much
time they spend using digital devices on a given day. The results of the
survey are shown in the following cumulative frequency diagram.

![e07ae0d3554141d985a49db0473a55cc80fdc5e7.svg](images/e07ae0d3554141d985a49db0473a55cc80fdc5e7.svg)

1.  Find the median number of minutes spent using digital devices.
    [2 marks]

2.  Find the number of students whose screen time is within $30$
    minutes of the median. [3 marks]

Only $5$% of the students spent more than $k$ minutes using digital
devices.

3.  Find the value of $k$. [3 marks]

The results of the survey can also be displayed on the following
box-and-whisker diagram.

![08577a2480ca259edd11aced36c54e86044abea1.svg](images/08577a2480ca259edd11aced36c54e86044abea1.svg)

4.  Write down the value of $q$. [1 mark]

5.
    1.  Find the value of $p$.

    2.  Hence find the interquartile range. [4 marks]

Screen times of less than $r$ minutes are considered outliers.

6.  Find the value of $r$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    F(\text{median}) &= 50\text{th percentile} \hspace{0.2em} \\[6pt]
    &= 0.5(120) \\[6pt]
    &= 60
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the cumulative frequency diagram,
    we find
     $\text{median} = 180 \text{ min}$

2.  Using the cumulative frequency diagram, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    F(180-30) &= F(150) = 26 \\[6pt]
    F(180+30) &= F(210) = 98 \hspace{1.75em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    F(210) - F(150) &= 98 - 26 \\[6pt]
    &= 72 \text{ students} \hspace{4.7em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We know $95$% of the students spent less than $k$ minutes using
    digital devices.

    Hence we deduce
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} F(k) &= 95\text{th percentile} \\[6pt]
    &= 0.95(120) \\[6pt]
    &= 114
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the cumulative frequency diagram,
    we find  $k = 250$

4.
    $q = 300$

5.  We have

    ![36458c76fb6f9a2eadb240150c50ee9502065b89.svg](images/36458c76fb6f9a2eadb240150c50ee9502065b89.svg)

    1.  Hence we deduce
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.3em} F(p) &= 75\text{th percentile} \\[6pt]
        &= 0.75(120) \\[6pt]
        &= 90
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence, using the cumulative frequency
        diagram, we find
         $p = 200$

    2.  Hence we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{IQR} &= q_3 - q_1 \\[6pt]
        &= 200 - 155 \hspace{1.8em} \\[6pt]
        &= 45
        \end{aligned}
        $$

7.  Using the lower inner fence formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.7em} r &= q_1 - 1.5(\text{IQR}) \\[6pt]
    &= 155 - 1.5(45) \\[6pt]
    &= 155 - 67.5 \\[6pt]
    &= 87.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
