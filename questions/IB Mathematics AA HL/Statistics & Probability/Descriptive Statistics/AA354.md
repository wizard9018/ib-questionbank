# AA354

**Maximum mark: 4**

There are $40$ players in a clay pigeon shooting club who take part in a
local tournament. The scores obtained after the
first round of shootings are shown in the
following table.

![4b291e250d6c5c0e77239c566e5e7ce2032b760e.svg](images/4b291e250d6c5c0e77239c566e5e7ce2032b760e.svg)

1.  One of the players is chosen at random. Find the probability that
    this player's score was $3$ or more.
    [2 marks]

2.  Calculate the mean score. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{P}(\text{$3$ or more}) &= \dfrac{10+6+3}{40} = \dfrac{19} {40}\\[12pt]
    &= 0.475
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the mean formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \bar{x} &= \bigg[\sum_{i=1}^6 f_ix_i\bigg]\bigg/ \bigg[\sum_{i=1}^6 f_i\bigg] \\[5pt]
    &= \dfrac{(2)(0)+(7)(1)+(12)(2)+(10)(3)+(6)(4)+(3)(5)}{40} \\[15pt]
    &= 2.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
