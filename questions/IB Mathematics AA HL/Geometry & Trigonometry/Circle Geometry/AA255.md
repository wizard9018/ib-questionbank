# AA255

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following diagram shows a circle with centre O and radius
$10$ cm.

![d680d624c5579097af69d19f4c53f5a964e7e9c9.svg](images/d680d624c5579097af69d19f4c53f5a964e7e9c9.svg)

Points A, B lie on the circle and $\text{A\^{O}B} = 2.2$ radians.

1.  Find:
    1.  the length of the minor arc AB;

    2.  the perimeter of the shaded region. [4 marks]

2.  Find the area of the shaded region. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using the length of an arc formula, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        l_{\text{arc}} &= (\text{A\^{O}B})(\text{OA}) \hspace{1.2em} \\[6pt]
        &= (2.2)(10) \\[6pt]
        &= 22 \text{ cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Hence the perimeter of the shaded region is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        P_{\text{shaded}} &= 2(\text{OA}) + l_{\text{arc}} \hspace{2.7em} \\[6pt]
        &= 2(10) + 22 \\[6pt]
        &= 42 \text{ cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Using the area of a sector formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} A_{\text{shaded}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OA}^2\big](\text{A\^{O}B}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[10^2\big](2.2) \\[10pt]
    &= 110 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
