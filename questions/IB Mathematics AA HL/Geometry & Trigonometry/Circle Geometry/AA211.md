# AA211

**Maximum mark: 5**

The following diagram shows a radioactivity warning symbol made out of a
circle in the centre and three equal blades.

![cbb464d0bf450f25830c94376d8c375dc107d08d.svg](images/cbb464d0bf450f25830c94376d8c375dc107d08d.svg)

Given that $\text{OA} = 2$ cm,
$\text{AB} = 1$ cm,
$\text{BC} = 4$ cm, and
$\text{C\^OD} = \ang{30}$, find the area of the symbol.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have $\text{C\^OD} = \ang{30} = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$.

Using the area of a sector formula, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{5.8em} A_{\text{blade}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OC}^2 - \text{OB}^2\big](\text{C\^OD}) \\[6pt]
&= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[7^2 - 3^2\big]\Big[\dfrac{\pi}{6}\Big] \\[6pt]
&= \dfrac{10\pi}{3} \text{ cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence the area of the
symbol is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{3.2em} A_{\text{symbol}} &= \pi \big[\text{OA}^2\big] + 3A_{\text{blade}} \\[6pt]
&= \pi\big[2^2\big] + 3\bigg[\dfrac{10\pi}{3}\bigg] \\[6pt]
&= 4\pi + 10\pi \\[12pt]
&= 14\pi \text{ cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
