# AA738

**Maximum mark: 13**

A loaf pan is made in the shape of a cylinder. The pan has a diameter of
$24\,\mathrm{cm}$ and a height of
$5\,\mathrm{cm}$.

![a347288bee589088e6dce052611dad40b73c72e7.svg](images/a347288bee589088e6dce052611dad40b73c72e7.svg)

1.  Calculate the volume of this pan. [2 marks]

Gloria prepares enough bread dough to exactly fill the pan. The dough
was in the shape of a sphere.

2.  Find the radius of the sphere in cm, correct to one decimal place. [3 marks]

The bread was cooked in a hot oven. Once taken out of the oven, the
bread was left in the kitchen.

The temperature, $T$, of the bread, in degrees
Celsius, $\text{\textdegree{C}}$, can be
modelled by the function

$$
T(t) = a\times(1.51)^{-\frac{t}{3}} + 21, \hspace{0.5em} t \geq 0,
$$

where $a$ is a constant and $t$ is the time, in minutes, since the bread
was taken out of the oven.

When the bread was taken out of the oven its temperature was
$205$ $\text{\textdegree{C}}$.

3.  Find the value of $a$. [2 marks]

4.  Find the temperature that the bread will be $10$ minutes
    after it is taken out of the oven. [2 marks]

The bread can be eaten once its temperature drops to
$35$ $\text{\textdegree{C}}$.

5.  Calculate, to the nearest minute, the time since the bread
    was taken out\
    of the oven until it can be eaten. [3 marks]

6.  In the context of this model, state what the value of $21$
    represents. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the volume of a cylinder formula with $r = 12$ and $h = 5$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.05em} V_{\text{pan}} &= \pi r^2h \\[6pt]
    &= \pi(12)^2(5) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{= 720\pi \approx 2260 \text{ cm}^3} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the volume of a sphere formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.4em} V_{\text{dough}} &= V_{\text{pan}} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3 &= 720\pi \hspace{3em} [\text{from  part (a)}] \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, solving this equation for $r$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{equation*}
    \hspace{12.9em} \boxed{r \approx 8.1 \text{ cm}} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{equation*}
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $T(0) = 205$ for $a$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a\times(1.51)^0 + 21 &= 205 \hspace{5.65em} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]
    a + 21 &= 205 \\[-12pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    $$
    \begin{equation*}
    \hspace{0.8em} \boxed{a = 184} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{equation*}
    $$

4.  Evaluating $T(t)$ for $t=10$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4em} T(10) &= 184\times(1.51)^{-\frac{10}{3}} + 21 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[9pt]
    &\hspace{0.275em}\boxed{\approx 67.6 \text{\hspace{0.15em}\textdegree{C}}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Solving the equation $T(t) = 35$ for $t$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.6em} 184\times(1.51)^{-\frac{t}{3}} + 21 &= 35 \tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[6pt]
    t &\approx 18.8 \hspace{3.5em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[-16pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    $$
    \begin{equation*}
    \hspace{1.8em} \boxed{t \approx 19 \text{ minutes}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{equation*}
    $$
