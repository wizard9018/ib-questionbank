# AA302

**Maximum mark: 6**

A sphere made out of gold has a radius of
$13.2$ cm.

1.  Find the volume of the sphere, expressing your answer in the form
    $a\times10^k$, $1 \leq a < 10$ and $k \in \mathbb{Z}^+$. [3 marks]

The sphere is to be melted down and remoulded into the shape of a
**cylinder**\
with a height of $18.4$ cm.

2.  Find the radius of the base of the cylinder, giving your
    answer correct to $2$ significant figures.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the volume of a sphere formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{\text{sphere}} &= \dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3 \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{4}{3}\pi (13.2)^3 \\[10pt]
    &\approx 9634.08 \text{ cm}^3 \\[12pt]
    &\approx 9.63\times10^3 \text{ cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the volume of a cylinder formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{\text{cylinder}} &= \pi r^2h \\[6pt]
    \hspace{10.5em} 9634.08 &= \pi r^2(18.4) \hspace{2.8em} [\text{since $V_{\text{cylinder}} = V_{\text{sphere}}$}] \\[7pt]
    r &\approx 12.91 \text{ cm} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    r \approx 13 \text{ cm} \hspace{1.6em}
    $$
