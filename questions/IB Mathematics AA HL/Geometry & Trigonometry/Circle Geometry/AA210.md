# AA210

**Maximum mark: 5**

The app logo, for a mobile arcade game, is a sector of a circle of
radius $3$ cm, shown as shaded in the diagram
below. The area of the logo is $6 \pi$ cm$^2$.

![78e72d0c99835aa59c5c4bbe3bca760a063b6070.svg](images/78e72d0c99835aa59c5c4bbe3bca760a063b6070.svg)

1.  Find, in radians, the measure of the angle AOB. [3 marks]

2.  Find the total length of the perimeter of the logo. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the area of a circle and the area of a sector formulas, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7.05em} \text{area of the logo} &= \pi r^2 - \dfrac{1}{2}r^2 \theta \hspace{3em} [\text{where $\theta = \text{A\^OB}$}] \\[6pt]
    6\pi &= \pi(3)^2 - \dfrac{1}{2}(3)^2 \theta \\[6pt]
    2\pi &= 3\pi - \dfrac{3\theta}{2} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{3\theta}{2} &= \pi \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \theta = \dfrac{2\pi}{3} \hspace{0.7em}
    $$

2.  Using the length of an arc formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{length of the (major) arc AB} &= r\hspace{0.05em}(2\pi - \text{A\^OB}) \\[6pt]
    &= 3\Big(2\pi - \dfrac{2\pi}{3}\Big) \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \hspace{0.5em} \\[8pt]
    &= 4\pi \text{ cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{perimeter of the logo} &= 4\pi + 2r \\[6pt]
    &= 4\pi + 2(3) \\[6pt]
    &= 4\pi + 6 \text{ cm} \hspace{6.3em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
