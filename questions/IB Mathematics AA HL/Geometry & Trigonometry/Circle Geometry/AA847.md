# AA847

**Maximum mark: 4**

The diameter of a spherical object is $3 \times 10^{8}\hspace{0.2em}$cm.

1.  Write down the radius of the object. [1 mark]

The volume of the object can be expressed in the form
$\pi (a \times 10^k)$ cm$^3$ where
$1 \leq a < 10$ and $k \in \mathbb{Z}$.

2.  Find the value of $a$ and the value of $k$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $1.5 \times 10^8 \hspace{0.2em}\text{cm}$

2.  Using the volume of a sphere formula with $r = 1.5 \times 10^8$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V &= \dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3 \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{4}{3}\pi \left( 1.5 \times 10^8 \right)^3 \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{4}{3}\pi \left( \dfrac{27}{8} \times 10^{24} \right) \\[8pt]
    &= \pi \left(\dfrac{9}{2} \times 10^{24} \right)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence,
     $a = 4.5$
    and   $k = 24$
