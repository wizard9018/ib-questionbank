# AA289

**Maximum mark: 7**

The diagram below shows the cross section of a cylindrical pipe,
$80$ cm in length, carrying water. The pipe has
a radius of $15$ cm.

![4719558002970c7289015f8defc8959d46793d7a.svg](images/4719558002970c7289015f8defc8959d46793d7a.svg)

The pipe is not at full capacity, such that the chord length of the
water level \[AB\] is $20$ cm. Find the volume
of water in the pipe.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have

![15b78589197cdc906193828635817a7d6cacb10e.svg](images/15b78589197cdc906193828635817a7d6cacb10e.svg)

Using the cosine rule, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{AB}^2 &= \text{AO}^2 + \text{BO}^2 - 2(\text{AO})(\text{BO})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{O}A}) \\[13pt]
20^2 &= 15^2 + 15^2 - 2(15)(15)\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{O}A}) \\[6pt]
\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{O}A}) &= \dfrac{15^2 + 15^2 - 20^2}{2(15)(15)} \\[7pt]
\text{B\^{O}A} &\approx 1.46 \text{ radians} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{3.65em}
\end{aligned}
$$

Using the area of a sector and the area of a triangle formulas, we
obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{3.65em} A_{\text{shaded}} &= A_{\text{circle}} - (A_{\text{sector}} - A_{\triangle\text{AOB}}) \\[10pt]
&= \pi\big[\text{AO}^2\big] - \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{AO}^2\big](\text{B\^{O}A}) + \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AO})(\text{BO})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{O}A}) \\[6pt]
&= \pi\big[15^2\big] - \dfrac{1}{2}\big[15^2\big](1.46) + \dfrac{1}{2}(15)(15)\sin\hspace{0.05em}(1.46) \\[10pt]
&\approx 654.42 \text{ cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence the volume of water in
the pipe is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{0.8em} V_{\text{pipe}} &= A_{\text{shaded}}\times l_{\text{pipe}} \\[8pt]
&= (654.42)(80) \\[6pt]
&\approx 52\hspace{0.15em}400 \text{ cm}^3
\end{aligned}
$$
