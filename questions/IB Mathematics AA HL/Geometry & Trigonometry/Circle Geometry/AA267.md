# AA267

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following diagram shows a circle with centre O and radius
$5$ cm.

![7502343217aad3149465f358ed929a0bf497a477.svg](images/7502343217aad3149465f358ed929a0bf497a477.svg)

Points A, B lie on the circle and $\text{A\^{O}B} = 2.1$ radians.

1.  Find the length of:
    1.  minor arc AB;

    2.  chord AB. [4 marks]

2.  Find the area of the shaded region. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using the length of an arc formula, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        l_{\text{arc}} &= (\text{A\^{O}B})(\text{OA}) \hspace{0.8em} \\[6pt]
        &= (2.1)(5) \\[6pt]
        &= 10.5 \text{ cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the cosine rule, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.2em} \text{AB}^2 &= \text{OA}^2 + \text{OB}^2 - 2(\text{OA})(\text{OB})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{O}B}) \\[6pt]
        \text{AB} &= \sqrt{5^2 + 5^2 - 2(5)(5) \cos\hspace{0.05em}(2.1)} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \text{AB} \approx 8.67 \text{ cm} \hspace{11.9em}
        $$

2.  Using the area of a sector and the area of a triangle formulas, we
    find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.7em} A_{\text{shaded}} &= A_{\text{sector}} - A_{\triangle\text{OAB}} \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OA}^2\big](\text{A\^{O}B}) - \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{OA})(\text{OB})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{O}B}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[5^2\big](2.1) - \dfrac{1}{2}(5)(5)\sin\hspace{0.05em}(2.1) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 15.5 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
