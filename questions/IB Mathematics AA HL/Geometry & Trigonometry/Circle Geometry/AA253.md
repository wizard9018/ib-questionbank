# AA253

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows a circle with the centre O and radius
$8$ cm.

![4701a00e1152364c7844fcad6bd746938b6e55aa.svg](images/4701a00e1152364c7844fcad6bd746938b6e55aa.svg)

Points A, B lie on the circle and $\text{A\^{O}B} = 1.4$ radians.

1.  Find the length of the minor arc AB. [3 marks]

2.  Find the area of the shaded region. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the length of an arc formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.1em} l_{\text{arc}} &= (\text{A\^{O}B})(\text{OB}) \\[6pt]
    &= (1.4)(8) \\[6pt]
    &= 11.2 \text{ cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the area of a sector formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.6em} A_{\text{shaded}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OB}^2\big](\text{A\^{O}B}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[8^2\big](1.4) \\[10pt]
    &= 44.8 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
