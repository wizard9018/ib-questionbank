# AA283

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following diagram shows a circle with centre O and radius $r$ cm.

![4a2de1c9425e4d830bbf4132ba8ee6e58293cd69.svg](images/4a2de1c9425e4d830bbf4132ba8ee6e58293cd69.svg)

Points A, B lie on the circle and $\text{A\^{O}B} = 1.2$ radians.

1.  Show that $\text{OC} = r\cos 1.2$. [1 mark]

2.  The area of the shaded region is
    $35$ cm$^2$. Find the value of $r$.
    [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the right triangle OAC, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos 1.2 &= \dfrac{\text{OC}}{r} \hspace{1.5em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{1.5em} \text{OC} = r\cos 1.2
    $$

2.  Using the area of a sector and the area of a triangle formulas, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\text{shaded}} &= A_{\text{sector}} - A_{\triangle\text{OAC}} \\[10pt]
    A_{\text{shaded}}  &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OA}^2\big](\text{A\^{O}B}) - \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{OA})(\text{OC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{O}C}) \\[6pt]
    35 &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[r^2\big](1.2) - \dfrac{1}{2}(r)(r\cos 1.2)(\sin 1.2) \\[6pt]
    35 &= \dfrac{1}{2}r^2(1.2- \cos 1.2\times\sin 1.2) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    r \approx 9.01 \text{ cm} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{0.7em}
    $$
