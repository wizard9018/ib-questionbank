# AA268

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following diagram shows a circle with centre O and radius
$7.2$ cm.

![d4977f00fb021efbd366ac34dea773c85923bdcd.svg](images/d4977f00fb021efbd366ac34dea773c85923bdcd.svg)

Points A, B lie on the circle and $\text{A\^{O}B} = 1.3$ radians.

1.  Find the length of:
    1.  minor arc AB;

    2.  chord AB. [4 marks]

2.  Find the area of the shaded region. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using the length of an arc formula, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        l_{\text{arc}} &= (\text{A\^{O}B})(\text{OA}) \hspace{0.8em} \\[6pt]
        &= (1.3)(7.2) \\[6pt]
        &= 9.36 \text{ cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the cosine rule, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.5em} \text{AB}^2 &= \text{OA}^2 + \text{OB}^2 - 2(\text{OA})(\text{OB})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{O}B}) \\[6pt]
        \text{AB} &= \sqrt{7.2^2 + 7.2^2 - 2(7.2)(7.2) \cos\hspace{0.05em}(1.3)} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \text{AB} \approx 8.71 \text{ cm} \hspace{11.6em}
        $$

2.  Using the area of a sector and the area of a triangle formulas, we
    find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.95em} A_{\text{shaded}} &= A_{\text{sector}} - A_{\triangle\text{OAB}} \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OA}^2\big](\text{A\^{O}B}) - \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{OA})(\text{OB})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{O}B}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[7.2^2\big](1.3) - \dfrac{1}{2}(7.2)(7.2)\sin\hspace{0.05em}(1.3) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 8.72 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
