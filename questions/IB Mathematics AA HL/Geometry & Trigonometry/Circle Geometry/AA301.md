# AA301

**Maximum mark: 6**

A bronze sphere has a radius of $10.5$ cm.

1.  Find the volume of the sphere, expressing your answer in the form
    $a\times10^k$, $1 \leq a < 10$ and $k \in \mathbb{Z}^+$. [3 marks]

The sphere is to be melted down and remoulded into the shape of a
**cone**\
with a height of $11.9$ cm.

2.  Find the radius of the base of the cone, giving your answer
    correct to $3$ significant
    figures. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the volume of a sphere formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{\text{sphere}} &= \dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3 \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{4}{3}\pi (10.5)^3 \\[10pt]
    &\approx 4849.05 \text{ cm}^3 \\[12pt]
    &\approx 4.85\times10^3 \text{ cm}^3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the volume of a cone formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{\text{cone}} &= \dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2h \\[6pt]
    \hspace{9.7em} 4849.05 &= \dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2(11.9) \hspace{2.4em} [\text{since $V_{\text{cone}} = V_{\text{sphere}}$}] \\[10pt]
    r &\approx 19.73 \text{ cm} \hspace{3.35em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    r \approx 19.7 \text{ cm} \hspace{1.5em}
    $$
