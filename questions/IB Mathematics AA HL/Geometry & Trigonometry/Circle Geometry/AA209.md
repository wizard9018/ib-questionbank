# AA209

**Maximum mark: 5**

The diagram below shows a sector of a circle with radius $r$ where
$\text{A\^OB} = x$ radians

and the length of the arc AB is $\dfrac{4}{x}$ cm.

![396fb6507d7c9bef4b6d659a48ea8869f9e85fd4.svg](images/396fb6507d7c9bef4b6d659a48ea8869f9e85fd4.svg)

Given that the area of the sector is
$27$ cm$^2$, find the length of the arc AB.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the length of an arc formula, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{3.5em} l_{\text{arc}} &= (\text{A\^OB})(r) \\[8pt]
\dfrac{4}{x} &= xr \\[4pt]
r &= \dfrac{4}{x^2} \text{ cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the area of a
sector formula, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{4.6em} A &= \dfrac{1}{2}r^2(\text{A\^OB}) \\[6pt]
27 &= \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{16}{x^4} \cdot x \\[6pt]
27 &= \dfrac{8}{x^3} \\[6pt]
x^3 &= \dfrac{8}{27} \\[4pt]
x &=  \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{8}{27}} \\[6pt]
x &= \dfrac{2}{3} \text{ radians}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence the length of the
arc AB is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2.9em} l &= \dfrac{4}{x}  \\[5pt]
&= \dfrac{4}{(2/3)} \\[8pt]
&= 6 \text{ cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
