# AA1054

**Maximum mark: 7**

A gardener is designing a flower decoration around a fountain shown by the shaded region of the figure below.

![AA1054](images/AA1054.JPEG)

The inner circle, of radius $r$ m represents the fountain. The outer circle has a radius of $8$ m and $\theta =2.4$ radians.

1. Show that the area to be decorated is $(\pi-1.2)\left(64-r^2\right)$.[2 marks]

The area to be decorated is $32$ metres squared.

2. Find the value of $r$.[2 marks]

3. Find the perimeter of the shaded area.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using **the formula for the area of a sector (3.4)**, $A=\frac{1}{2}r^2 \theta$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \text{Area of shaded region} &= \text{Outer region} - \text{Inner region} \tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[8pt]
        &= \left(\dfrac{
    2\pi-2.4}{2}\right)(8^2)- \left(\dfrac{
    2\pi-2.4}{2}\right)(r^2) \tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\[8pt]
        &= \left(\pi-1.2\right)\left(8^2\right)- \left(\pi-1.2\right)\left(r^2\right)\\[8pt]
        &=
    \left(\pi-1.2\right)\left(64-r^2\right) \hspace{0.5em}\text{... as required} \tag*{\textbf{AG}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. By solving the corresponding equation, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \left(\pi-1.2\right)\left(64-r^2\right) &= 32  \tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[8pt]
        r&=6.8933...\\[6pt]
    r&=\boxed{6.89\,\text{m}}\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3. Using **the formula for the length of an arc (3.4)**, $l = r \theta$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \text{Perimeter} &= \text{Outer arc} + \text{Inner arc} + 2(8-6.893...)  \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}\\[8pt]
    &= \left(2\pi-2.4\right)(8) + \left(2\pi-2.4\right)(6.893...) + 2(8-6.893...) \tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\[8pt]
    &=60.04...\\[6pt]
    &=\boxed{60.0\, \text{m}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
