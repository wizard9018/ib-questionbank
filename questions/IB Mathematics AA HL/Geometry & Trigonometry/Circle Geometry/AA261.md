# AA261

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following diagram shows a circle with centre O and radius
$8$ cm.

![d599dde5f666e8b2b70a0d2ef18e3de9f1744b7f.svg](images/d599dde5f666e8b2b70a0d2ef18e3de9f1744b7f.svg)

Points A, B lie on the circle and $\text{A\^{O}B} = 2.7$ radians.

1.  Find:
    1.  the length of the major arc AB;

    2.  the perimeter of the shaded region. [4 marks]

2.  Find the area of the shaded region. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using the length of an arc formula, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.3em} l_{\text{arc}} &= (2\pi - \text{A\^{O}B})(\text{OA}) \\[6pt]
        &= (2\pi - 2.7)(8) \\[6pt]
        &\approx 28.7 \text{ cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Hence the perimeter of the shaded region is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        P_{\text{shaded}} &= 2(\text{OA}) + l_{\text{arc}} \hspace{2.5em} \\[6pt]
        &\approx 2(8) + 28.7 \\[6pt]
        &\approx 44.7 \text{ cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Using the area of a sector formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.05em} A_{\text{shaded}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OA}^2\big](2\pi-\text{A\^{O}B}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[8^2\big](2\pi-2.7) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 115 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
