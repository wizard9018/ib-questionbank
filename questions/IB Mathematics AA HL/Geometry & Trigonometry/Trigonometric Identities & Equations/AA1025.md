# AA1025

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the equation $\sin x - \cos x = \pm\dfrac{1}{3}$.

1. Show that the equation can be expressed as $\sin 2x = \dfrac{8}{9}$[4 marks]

2. Hence or otherwise, solve the equation $\sin x - \cos x = \pm\dfrac{1}{3}$ for $0 \leq x \leq 2\pi$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. By squaring both sides of the equation, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \left(\sin x - \cos x\right)^2 &= \left(\pm\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^2 \\[8pt]

    \sin^2x - 2\sin x \cos x + \cos^2x &= \dfrac{1}{9} \\[8pt]

    1 - 2\sin x \cos x &= \dfrac{1}{9} \hspace{3em}\text{[by the Pythagorean identity]} \\[8pt]

    2\sin x \cos x &= 1 - \dfrac{1}{9} \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-2.5em}\boxed{\sin 2x = \dfrac{8}{9}} \hspace{3em}\text{[by the double angle identity]}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Sketching the graphs of $y= \sin 2x$ and $y = \dfrac{8}{9}$ for $0 \leq x \leq 2\pi$, we find that there are four points of intersection:

    ![aa1025](images/aa1025.PNG)

    Hence, for $0 \leq x \leq 2\pi$, the solutions to $\sin x - \cos x = \pm\dfrac{1}{3}$ are 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \boxed{x = 0.547, 1.02, 3.69, 4.16}  

    \end{aligned}

    $$
