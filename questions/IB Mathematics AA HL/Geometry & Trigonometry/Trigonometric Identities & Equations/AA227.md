# AA227

**Maximum mark: 6**

Find all solutions to the equation $\tan 2x - 3\tan x  = 0$ where
$\ang{0} \leq x < \ang{360}$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the double angle identity, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\tan 2x - 3\tan x  &= 0 \\[5pt]
\dfrac{2\tan x}{1 - \tan^2 x} - 3\tan x &=  0 \\[6pt]
\hspace{2.1em} 2\tan x - 3\tan x\hspace{0.05em}(1 - \tan^2 x) &= 0 \\[10pt]
\tan x\hspace{0.05em}(2 - 3 + 3\tan^2 x) &= 0 \\[10pt]
\tan x\,(3\tan^2 x - 1) &= 0 \\[5pt]
\tan x &=  0,\hspace{0.25em} \pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence
we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{0.5em} x &= \arctan(0),\hspace{0.25em} \ang{180}+\arctan(0) \\[6pt]
&= \ang{0},\hspace{0.25em} \ang{180}
\end{aligned}
$$

and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{4.2em} x &= \arctan(1/\sqrt{3}),\hspace{0.25em} \ang{180}+\arctan(1/\sqrt{3}) \\[6pt]
&= \ang{30},\hspace{0.25em} \ang{210}
\end{aligned}
$$

and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{5.7em} x &= \arctan(-1/\sqrt{3}),\hspace{0.25em} \ang{180}+\arctan(-1/\sqrt{3}) \\[6pt]
&= \ang{150},\hspace{0.25em} \ang{330}
\end{aligned}
$$
