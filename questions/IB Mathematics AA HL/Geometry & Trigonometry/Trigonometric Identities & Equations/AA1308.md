# AA1308

**Maximum mark: 12**

The first three terms of a geometric sequence are $\sin{x},\tan{x},\tan{x} \sec{x}\dots$

1.  Find an expression for the common ratio, $r$, as a single trigonometric ratio.[2 marks]

2.  Find an expression for $u_4$, expressing your answer in terms of only $\tan x$.[3 marks]
3.  Find an expression for $u_9$, expressing your answer in terms of $\tan{x}$ and $\sec{x}$.[2 marks]
4.  Show that the sum of the first four terms is $\displaystyle S_4=-\tan^3{x}\left(\dfrac{\cos^2{x}+1}{\cos{x}-1}\right)$.[5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The common ratio, $r$, is equal to any term divided by the previous term.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \dfrac{u_2}{u_1} \\[6pt]
      &= \dfrac{\tan{x}}{\sin{x}} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the **identity for $\tan\theta$ (3.5)**, to rewrite $\tan x$, $\tan\theta = \dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$, together with the **reciprocal trig identity (3.9)**, to express the result as a single ratio, $\sec\theta = \dfrac{1}{\cos\theta}$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    r &= \left(\dfrac{\sin{x}}{\cos{x}}\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{\sin{x}}\right) \\[6pt]
      &= \dfrac{1}{\cos{x}} \\[6pt]
    r &= \boxed{\sec{x}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
2.  Using the **geometric sequence formula (1.3)**, to find $u_4$ with $u_1 = \sin x$ and $r = \sec x$, $u_n = u_1 r^{n-1}$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_4 &= \sin{x}(\sec{x})^{4-1} \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]
        &= \sin{x}\sec^{3}{x}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    To write the answer in terms of $\tan x$, factorise $\sec^3 x$ and apply the **Pythagorean identity (3.9)**, to replace $\sec^2 x$, $1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta$, together with the identity $\tan x = \dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_4 &= \sin{x}\sec{x}(\sec^{2}{x}) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]
        &= \sin{x}\left(\dfrac{1}{\cos{x}}\right)(\sec^{2}{x}) \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{\sin{x}}{\cos{x}}(\sec^{2}{x}) \\[6pt]
        &= \tan{x}(1 + \tan^2{x}) \\[6pt]
    u_4 &= \boxed{\tan{x} + \tan^3{x}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
3.  Using the **geometric sequence formula (1.3)** again with $n = 9$, $u_n = u_1 r^{n-1}$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_9 &= \sin{x}(\sec{x})^{9-1} \\[6pt]
        &= \sin{x}(\sec{x})^{8}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    To write the expression in terms of $\tan x$ and $\sec x$, factorise a single $\sec x$ and apply the **identity for $\tan\theta$ (3.5)**, $\tan\theta = \dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    u_9 &= \sin{x}(\sec x)(\sec x)^7 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[10pt]
        &= \sin{x}\left(\dfrac{1}{\cos{x}}\right)\sec^7{x} \\[10pt]
    u_9 &= \boxed{\tan{x}\sec^7{x}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
4.  Using the **geometric sum formula (1.3)**, to find the sum of the first four terms with $u_1 = \sin x$, $r = \sec x$ and $n = 4$, $S_n = \dfrac{u_1(1-r^n)}{1-r}$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_4 &= \dfrac{\sin{x}(1-\sec^4{x})}{1-\sec{x}} \tag*{\textbf{M1}} \\[10pt]
        &= \dfrac{\sin{x}\left(1 - \tfrac{1}{\cos^4{x}}\right)}{1 - \tfrac{1}{\cos{x}}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Writing $1 - \tfrac{1}{\cos^4{x}}$ and $1 - \tfrac{1}{\cos x}$ with common denominators.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_4 &= \dfrac{\sin{x}\left(\tfrac{\cos^4{x}-1}{\cos^4{x}}\right)}{\tfrac{\cos{x}-1}{\cos{x}}} \\[8pt]
        &= \sin{x}\left(\dfrac{\cos^4{x}-1}{\cos^4{x}}\right)\left(\dfrac{\cos{x}}{\cos{x}-1}\right) \\[8pt]
    S_4 &= \sin{x}\left(\dfrac{\cos^4{x}-1}{\cos^3{x}(\cos{x}-1)}\right) \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the difference of two squares, $\cos^4{x}-1 = (\cos^2{x}-1)(\cos^2{x}+1)$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_4 &= \sin{x}\left(\dfrac{(\cos^2{x}-1)(\cos^2{x}+1)}{\cos^3{x}(\cos{x}-1)}\right) \tag*{\textbf{M1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Applying the **Pythagorean identity (3.6)**, to replace $\cos^2 x - 1$ with $-\sin^2 x$, $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    S_4 &= \sin{x}\left(\dfrac{(-\sin^2{x})(\cos^2{x}+1)}{\cos^3{x}(\cos{x}-1)}\right) \tag*{\textbf{M1}} \\[8pt]
        &= \dfrac{-\sin^3{x}(\cos^2{x}+1)}{\cos^3{x}(\cos{x}-1)} \tag*{\textbf{A1}} \\[8pt]
    &\boxed{S_4 = -\tan^3{x}\left(\dfrac{\cos^2{x}+1}{\cos{x}-1}\right)}\hspace{1em}\text{... as required.} \tag*{\textbf{AG}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
