# AA229

**Maximum mark: 8**

Consider the equation
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\sqrt{\sqrt{2}-1}}{\sin x} + \dfrac{\sqrt{\sqrt{2}+1}}{\cos x}  = 2\sqrt[4]{8}, \hspace{0.5em} 0 < x < \dfrac{\pi}{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Given that
    $\sin\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{8}\Big) = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2 - \sqrt{2}}$
    and
    $\cos\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{8}\Big) = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}$,
    verify that $x = \dfrac{\pi}{8}$ is a solution to the equation.
    [3 marks]

2.  Hence find the other solution to the equation. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Substituting $x = \dfrac{\pi}{8}$ in the left hand side of the
    equation $(1)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\sqrt{\sqrt{2}-1}}{\raisebox{-0.5em}{$\sin\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{8}\Big)$}} + \dfrac{\sqrt{\sqrt{2}+1}}{\raisebox{-0.5em}{$\cos\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{8}\Big)$}} &= \dfrac{\sqrt{\sqrt{2}-1}}{\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2 - \sqrt{2}}} + \dfrac{\sqrt{\sqrt{2}+1}}{\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}} \\[3pt]
    &= \dfrac{\sqrt{\sqrt{2}-1}}{\dfrac{\sqrt[4]{2}}{2}\sqrt{\sqrt{2} - 1}} + \dfrac{\sqrt{\sqrt{2}+1}}{\dfrac{\sqrt[4]{2}}{2}\sqrt{\sqrt{2} + 1}} \\[5pt]
    &= \dfrac{2}{\sqrt[4]{2}} + \dfrac{2}{\sqrt[4]{2}} \\[13pt]
    &= \dfrac{4}{\sqrt[4]{2}}\times\dfrac{\sqrt[4]{8}}{\sqrt[4]{8}} \\[21pt]
    &= 2\sqrt[4]{8} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence $x = \dfrac{\pi}{8}$ is a
    solution to the equation $(1)$.

2.  Solving the equation $(1)$ for $x \neq \dfrac{\pi}{8}$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\sqrt{\sqrt{2}-1}}{\sin x} + \dfrac{\sqrt{\sqrt{2}+1}}{\cos x} &= 2\sqrt[4]{8} \\[3pt]
    \dfrac{\raisebox{0.5em}{$\dfrac{\sqrt[4]{2}}{2}\sqrt{\sqrt{2}-1}$}}{\sin x} + \dfrac{\raisebox{0.5em}{$\dfrac{\sqrt[4]{2}}{2}\sqrt{\sqrt{2}+1}$}}{\cos x} &= \dfrac{\sqrt[4]{2}}{2}(2\sqrt[4]{8}) \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{\raisebox{0.5em}{$\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2 - \sqrt{2}}$}}{\sin x} + \dfrac{\raisebox{0.5em}{$\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2}}$}}{\cos x} &= 2 \\[9pt]
    \dfrac{\raisebox{0.5em}{$\sin\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{8}\Big)$}}{\sin x} + \dfrac{\raisebox{0.5em}{$\cos\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{8}\Big)$}}{\cos x} &= 2 \\[19pt]
    \cos x \sin\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{8}\Big) + \sin x \cos\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{8}\Big) &= 2\sin x \cos x \\[19pt]
    \sin\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(x + \dfrac{\pi}{8}\Big) &= \sin 2x \hspace{3em} [\text{by trig identities}] \\[19pt]
    x + \dfrac{\pi}{8} &= \pi - 2x \hspace{3em} [\text{since $x\neq\pi\hspace{-0.1em}/8$}] \\[19pt]
    3x &= \dfrac{7\pi}{8} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.65em} x = \dfrac{7\pi}{24}
    $$
