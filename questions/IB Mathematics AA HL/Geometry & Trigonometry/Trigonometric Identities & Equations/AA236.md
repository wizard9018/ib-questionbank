# AA236

**Maximum mark: 6**

Find all solutions to the equation
$\dfrac{1}{\tan x} + \dfrac{1}{\tan 2x}  = 0$ where $0 < x < 2\pi$ and
$x \neq \pi$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the double angle formula for tangent, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{1}{\tan x} + \dfrac{1}{\tan 2x} &=  0 \\[6pt]
\hspace{2em} \dfrac{1}{\tan x}  + \dfrac{1 - \tan^2 x}{2\tan x} &=  0 \\[6pt]
\dfrac{3 - \tan^2 x}{2\tan x} &= 0 \\[12pt]
3 - \tan^2 x &= 0 \\[14pt]
\tan x &= \pm \sqrt{3}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{4em} x &= \arctan(\sqrt{3}), \hspace{0.25em} \pi + \arctan(\sqrt{3}) \\[6pt]
&= \dfrac{\pi}{3}, \hspace{0.25em} \dfrac{4\pi}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{5.55em} x &= \arctan(-\sqrt{3}), \hspace{0.25em} \pi + \arctan(-\sqrt{3}) \\[6pt]
&= \dfrac{2\pi}{3}, \hspace{0.25em} \dfrac{5\pi}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$
