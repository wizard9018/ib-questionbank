# AA291

**Maximum mark: 6**

Given that $\cos x = \frac{3}{4}$, where $0 < x < \frac{\pi}{2}$, find
the value of $\sin 4x$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have

![681b1162a7884afd1f69dcb04042193ca0edb27d.svg](images/681b1162a7884afd1f69dcb04042193ca0edb27d.svg)

Using the right triangle above & the double angle identities  $(\sin 2\theta=2\sin\theta\cos\theta$ and $\cos 2\theta=2\cos^2\theta-1)$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{4em} \sin 4x &= \sin(2(2x))\\[12pt]
&=2\sin 2x\cos 2x \\[16pt]
&= 2(2\sin x\cos x)(2\cos^2\hspace{-0.05em}x - 1) \\[8pt]
&= 4\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(\dfrac{\sqrt{7}}{4}\bigg)\hspace{-0.2em}\bigg(\dfrac{3}{4}\bigg)\hspace{-0.2em}\bigg[2\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg(\dfrac{3}{4}\bigg)^2\hspace{-0.2em}-1\bigg] \\[16pt]
&= \dfrac{3\sqrt{7}}{4}\bigg[\dfrac{9}{8} - 1\bigg] \\[12pt]
\sin 4x \hspace{0.525em}&= \dfrac{3\sqrt{7}}{32}
\end{aligned}
$$
