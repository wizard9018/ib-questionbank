# AA226

**Maximum mark: 6**

Given that $\sin x - \cos x = \dfrac{1}{4}$, find $\cos 8x$, rounding
your answer to $3$ significant

figures.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the double angle identity, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
(\sin x - \cos x)^2 &= \dfrac{1}{16} \hspace{1.1em} \\[6pt]
(\sin^2 x+\cos^2 x) - 2\sin x \cos x &= \dfrac{1}{16} \\[6pt]
1 - \sin 2x &= \dfrac{1}{16} \\[6pt]
\sin 2x &= \dfrac{15}{16}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the double angle
identities, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{0.8em} \cos 8x &= 1 - 2\sin^2 4x \\[12pt]
&= 1 - 2(2\sin 2x \cos 2x)^2 \\[12pt]
&= 1 - 8(\sin 2x)^2(\cos 2x)^2 \\[12pt]
&= 1 - 8(\sin 2x)^2\big[1 - (\sin 2x)^2\big] \\[12pt]
&= 1 - 8(\sin 2x)^2 + 8(\sin 2x)^4 \\[4pt]
&= 1 - 8\bigg(\dfrac{15}{16}\bigg)^2 + 8\bigg(\dfrac{15}{16}\bigg)^4 \\[6pt]
&= 0.149
\end{aligned}
$$
