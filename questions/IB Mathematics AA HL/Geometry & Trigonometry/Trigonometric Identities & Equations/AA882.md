# AA882

**Maximum mark: 4**

It is given that $\operatorname{cosec}\theta=-\dfrac{5}{3}$, where
$\dfrac{\pi}{2}<\theta<\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$. Find the exact value of
$\cot \theta$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the definition of $\operatorname{cosec}\theta$, we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
%\operatorname{cosec}\theta &= -\dfrac{5}{3} \\[10pt]
\dfrac{1}{\sin\theta} &= -\dfrac{5}{3} \\[11pt]
\sin\theta &= -\dfrac{3}{5}
\end{aligned}
$$
Now, by the Pythagorean
identity, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos \theta &= \pm \sqrt{1-\left(-\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^2} \\[11pt]
&= \pm \sqrt{1-\dfrac{9}{25}}\\[11pt]
&= \pm \sqrt{\dfrac{16}{25}} \\[11pt]
&= -\dfrac{4}{5} \hspace{3em}  \left[\text{ since }\dfrac{\pi}{2}<\theta<\dfrac{3\pi}{2} \right]
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we substitute in the definition of $\cot \theta$, as follows

$$
\begin{aligned}
\cot \theta &= \dfrac{\cos \theta}{\sin\theta} \\[11pt]
&= \dfrac{-4/5}{-3/5} \\[11pt]
&\hspace{0.35em}= \dfrac{4}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$
