# AA883

**Maximum mark: 4**

It is given that $\sec\theta=-\frac{6}{5}$, where $\pi<\theta<2\pi$.
Find the exact value of $\tan \theta$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the definition of $\sec\theta$, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
%\operatorname{cosec}\theta &= -\dfrac{5}{3} \\[10pt]
\dfrac{1}{\cos\theta} &= -\dfrac{6}{5} \\[11pt]
\cos\theta &= -\dfrac{5}{6}
\end{aligned}
$$
Now, by the Pythagorean
identity, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin \theta &= \pm \sqrt{1-\left(-\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^2} \\[11pt]
&= \pm \sqrt{1-\dfrac{25}{36}}\\[11pt]
&= \pm \sqrt{\dfrac{11}{36}} \\[11pt]
&= -\dfrac{\sqrt{11}}{6} \hspace{3em}  \left[\text{ since }\pi<\theta<2\pi \right]
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, we substitute in the definition of $\tan \theta$, as follows

$$
\begin{aligned}
\tan \theta &= \dfrac{\sin \theta}{\cos\theta} \\[11pt]
&= \dfrac{-\sqrt{11}/6}{-5/6} \\[11pt]
&\hspace{0.35em}= \dfrac{\sqrt{11}}{5}
\end{aligned}
$$
