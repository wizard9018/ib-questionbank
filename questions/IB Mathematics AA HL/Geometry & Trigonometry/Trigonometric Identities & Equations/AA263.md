# AA263

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $\sin x = \dfrac{2}{3}$, where $x$ is obtuse.

1.  Find $\cos x$. [3 marks]

2.  Find $\cos 2x$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the Pythagorean identity, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos^2 x + \sin^2 x &= 1 \\[8pt]
    \cos^2 x + \bigg(\dfrac{2}{3}\bigg)^2 &= 1 \\[5pt]
    \cos^2 x &= 1 - \dfrac{4}{9} \hspace{6.4em} \\[8pt]
    \cos^2 x &= \dfrac{5}{9} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{9.2em} \cos x = -\dfrac{\sqrt{5}}{3} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\pi\hspace{-0.1em}/2 < x < \pi$}]
    $$

2.  Using the double angle identity for cosine, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos 2x &= 1 - 2\sin^2 x \hspace{0.3em} \\[8pt]
    &= 1 - 2\bigg(\dfrac{2}{3}\bigg)^2 \\[6pt]
    &= 1 - \dfrac{8}{9} \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{9}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
