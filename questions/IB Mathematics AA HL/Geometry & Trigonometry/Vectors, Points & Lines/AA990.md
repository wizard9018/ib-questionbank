# AA990

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the vectors $\bm{u}=\sqrt{3}\bm{i}+\bm{j}$ and $\bm{v}=\tan(k)\bm{i}+\bm{j}$, where $k \in \mathbb{R}^+, k \neq\frac{\pi}{2}.$

Let $\theta$ be the angle between $u$ and $v$.

1. Find an expression for $\cos \theta$ in terms of $k$. [3 marks]

2. Find the exact value of $\theta$ as $k \rightarrow \frac{\pi}{2}$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Use the dot product formula and the Pythagorean identity formula to write an expression for $\cos{\theta}$ in terms of $k$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \cos{\theta} &= \dfrac{\bm{u}\cdot\bm{v}}{|\bm{u}||\bm{v}|}\\[6pt]
        &=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}\tan{k}+1}{\sqrt{4}\times\sqrt{\tan^2{k}+1}}\\[6pt]
        &=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}\tan{k}+1}{2\times\sec k}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
2. Multiply the top and bottom of the fraction by $\cos{k}$ and then find the limit as $k \rightarrow \frac{\pi}{2}$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        &= \dfrac{1}{2}\left(\sqrt{3}\sin{k}+\cos{k}\right)\\[6pt]
        &= \lim_{k \to \frac{\pi}{2}} \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\sqrt{3}\sin{k}+\cos{k}\right)\right)\\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{1}{2}\lim_{k \to \frac{\pi}{2}} \left(\sqrt{3}\sin{k}+\cos{k}\right)\\[6pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Apply the limit of $k$ to each term in the expression
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        &= \dfrac{1}{2}\left(\lim_{k \to \frac{\pi}{2}} \left(\sqrt{3}\sin{k}\right)+\lim_{k \to \frac{\pi}{2}} \left(\cos{k}\right)\right)\\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\sqrt{3}+0)\\[6pt]
        \cos{\theta}&=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\\[6pt]
        \theta&=\dfrac{\pi}{6}\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
