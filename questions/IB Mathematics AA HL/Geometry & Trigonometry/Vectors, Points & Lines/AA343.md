# AA343

**Maximum mark: 13**

The diagram shows a parallelogram ABCD.

![ed67c1c8e1a3dbf0935691e5a18e4790b5f71757.svg](images/ed67c1c8e1a3dbf0935691e5a18e4790b5f71757.svg)

The coordinates of A, B and D are A$(2,3,4)$, B$(8,5,7)$ and D$(4,7,8)$.

1.
    1.  Show that
        $\vec{\text{AB}} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 6 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix*}$
         .

    2.  Find $\vec{\text{AD}}$.
    3.  Hence show that
        $\vec{\text{AC}} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 8 \\ 6 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix*}$ .
        [3 marks]

2.  Find the coordinates of point C. [2 marks]

3.
    1.  Find $\vec{\text{AB}}\cdot\vec{\text{AD}}$.

    2.  Hence find BÂD. [5 marks]

4.  Hence, or otherwise, find the area of the parallelogram. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \vec{\text{AB}} &= \vec{\text{OB}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[6pt]
        &=\hspace{-0.15em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 8 \\ 5 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} - \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} =  \hspace{0.525em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 6 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{1em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \vec{\text{AD}} &= \vec{\text{OD}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[6pt]
        &=\hspace{-0.15em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ 7 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} - \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} =  \hspace{0.525em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 4 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{1.1em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \vec{\text{AC}} &= \vec{\text{AB}} + \vec{\text{AD}} \\[6pt]
        &=\hspace{-0.15em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 6 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 4 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} =  \hspace{0.525em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 8 \\ 6 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{1em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} \vec{\text{OC}} &= \vec{\text{OA}} + \vec{\text{AC}} \\[6pt]
    &=\hspace{-0.15em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 8 \\ 6 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 10 \\ 9 \\ 11 \end{pmatrix*} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of C are
     $(10,9,11)$

3.
    1.  Using the scalar product formula, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \vec{\text{AB}}\cdot\vec{\text{AD}} &=\hspace{-0.15em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 6 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}\cdot\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 4 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
        &= (6)(2) + (2)(4) + (3)(4) \\[12pt]
        &= 32
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        |\vec{\text{AB}}| &= \sqrt{6^2 + 2^2 + 3^2} \hspace{2.2em} \\[6pt]
        &= 7
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        and

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        |\vec{\text{AD}}| &= \sqrt{2^2 + 4^2 + 4^2} \hspace{2.3em} \\[6pt]
        &= 6
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence, using the angle between two vectors
        formula, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}D}) &= \dfrac{\vec{\text{AB}} \cdot \vec{\text{AD}}}{\raisebox{-0.35em}{$\big|\vec{\text{AB}}\big|\big|\vec{\text{AD}}\big|$}} \hspace{1.8em} \\[6pt]
        \cos \hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}D}) &= \dfrac{32}{(7)(6)} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \text{B\^{A}D} \approx \ang{40.4} \hspace{2.05em}
        $$

4.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.75em} A_{\square\text{ABCD}} &= 2A_{\triangle\text{ABD}} \\[12pt]
    &= 2\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}\big|\vec{\text{AB}}\big|\big|\vec{\text{AD}}\big|\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}D})\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= 2\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}(7)(6)\sin \ang{40.4}\bigg] \\[12pt]
    &\approx 27.2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
