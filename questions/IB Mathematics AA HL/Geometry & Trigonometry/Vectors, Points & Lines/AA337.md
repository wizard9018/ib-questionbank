# AA337

**Maximum mark: 5**

Five equilateral triangles, each with side length
$4$ cm, are arranged to form a truss bridge
model.

This is shown in the following diagram.

![ed76e097fdbcfa4a334e429debc3794ef7294dc7.svg](images/ed76e097fdbcfa4a334e429debc3794ef7294dc7.svg)

The vectors $\mathbf{a}$, $\mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{c}$ are shown on the
diagram.

Find $\mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + 2\mathbf{c})$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have

![72b03b21af49b6b96e653ed87e3fec1251aa4940.svg](images/72b03b21af49b6b96e653ed87e3fec1251aa4940.svg)

Hence, using the scalar product formula, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b} + 2\mathbf{c}) &= \mathbf{a}\cdot(3\mathbf{c}) \\[10pt]
&= |\mathbf{a}||3\mathbf{c}|\cos \ang{60} \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
&= \dfrac{1}{2}(4)(12) \\[6pt]
&= 24
\end{aligned}
$$
