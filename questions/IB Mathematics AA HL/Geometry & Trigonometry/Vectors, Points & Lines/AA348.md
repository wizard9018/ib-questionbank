# AA348

**Maximum mark: 12**

Consider the points A$(3,2,-5)$ and B$(-3,6,-5)$.

1.  Find $\vec{\text{AB}}$. [2 marks]

Let C be a point such that
$\vec{\text{AC}} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 0 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}$ .

2.  Find the coordinates of C. [2 marks]

The line $L$ passes through B and is parallel to $\vec{\text{AC}}$.

3.  Write down a vector equation of $L$ in the form
    $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{p} + t\hspace{0.05em}\mathbf{d}$. [1 mark]

4.  Given that $|\vec{\text{AB}}| = k|\vec{\text{AC}}|$, find
    $k$. [3 marks]

The point D lies on $L$ such that $|\vec{\text{AB}}| = |\vec{\text{BD}}|$.

5.  Find the possible coordinates of D. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} \vec{\text{AB}} &= \vec{\text{OB}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[6pt]
    &=\hspace{-0.15em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] -3 \\ 6 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} - \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 2 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
    &=  \hspace{0.525em} \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -6 \\ 4 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{OC}} &= \vec{\text{OA}} + \vec{\text{AC}} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 2 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 0 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 6 \\ 2 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix*} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of C are
     $(6,2,-3)$

3.
    $\mathbf{r} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -3 \\ 6 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 0 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em},\hspace{0.5em} t \in \mathbb{R}$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |\vec{\text{AB}}| &= \textstyle \sqrt{(-6)^2 + 4^2 + 0^2} = \sqrt{52} \\[6pt]
    |\vec{\text{AC}}| &= \textstyle \sqrt{3^2 + \hspace{-0.35em}\phantom{(}0^2 + 2^2} = \sqrt{13}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} |\vec{\text{AB}}| &= k|\vec{\text{AC}}| \\[6pt]
    \sqrt{52} &= k\sqrt{13} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{10.6em} k = 2 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

5.  We have

    ![efbc3d2593cbb7ee3d35f1d9edfc31a76ed4fc37.svg](images/efbc3d2593cbb7ee3d35f1d9edfc31a76ed4fc37.svg)

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.4em} \vec{\text{BD}} &= \lambda\hspace{0.05em}\vec{\text{AC}} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\vec{\text{BD}} \parallel \vec{\text{AC}}$}\hspace{0.025em}] \\[6pt]
    &= \lambda\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 0 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}  \hspace{2.05em} [\text{for some $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}$}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |\vec{\text{AB}}| &= |\vec{\text{BD}}| \\[6pt]
    \hspace{4.25em} 2|\vec{\text{AC}}| &= |\lambda\hspace{0.05em}\vec{\text{AC}}| \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (d)}] \\[6pt]
    2|\vec{\text{AC}}| &= |\lambda||\vec{\text{AC}}| \\[8pt]
    2 &= |\lambda| \\[8pt]
    \lambda &= \pm 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.4em} \vec{\text{OD}} &= \vec{\text{OB}} + \vec{\text{BD}} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] -3 \\ 6 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} \pm \hspace{0.05em}2\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 0 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 6 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{0.15em} \text{or} \hspace{0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -9 \\ 6 \\ -9 \end{pmatrix*} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the possible coordinates of D are
     $(3,6,-1)$
    and  $(-9,6,-9)$
