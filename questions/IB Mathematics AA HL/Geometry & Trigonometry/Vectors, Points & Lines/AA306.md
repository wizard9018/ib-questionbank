# AA306

**Maximum mark: 7**

ABCD is a parallelogram where
$\vec{\text{AB}} = -2\textbf{i} - \textbf{j} + 6\textbf{k}$ and
$\vec{\text{AD}} = -4\textbf{i} + \textbf{j} + 3\textbf{k}$.

1.  Find the area of the parallelogram ABCD. [3 marks]

2.  By using a suitable scalar product of two vectors, determine whether
    AB̂C is acute or obtuse. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the vector product formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7.8em} \vec{\text{AB}} \times \vec{\text{AD}} &= (-2\textbf{i} - \textbf{j} + 6\textbf{k})\times(-4\textbf{i} + \textbf{j} + 3\textbf{k}) \\[8pt]
    &= \left|\begin{matrix*}[r] \textbf{i} & \textbf{j} & \hspace{0.65em}\textbf{k} \\ \hspace{0.25em}-2 & -1 & 6 \\ \hspace{0.25em}-4 & 1 & 3  \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.25em}\right| \\[8pt]
    &= [-3-6]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{i} - [-6-(-24)]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{j} + [-2-4]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{k} \\[8pt]
    &= -9\textbf{i} - 18\textbf{j} - 6\textbf{k} \hspace{2.3em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the area of a parallelogram formula, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.1em} A_{\square \text{ABCD}} &= \big|\vec{\text{AB}} \times \vec{\text{AD}}\big| \\[8pt]
    &= |\hspace{-0.2em}-9\textbf{i} - 18\textbf{j} - 6\textbf{k}| \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{(-9)^2 + (-18)^2 + (-6)^2} \\[8pt]
    &= 21
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the scalar product formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5em} \vec{\text{AB}} \cdot \vec{\text{AD}} &= (-2\textbf{i} - \textbf{j} + 6\textbf{k}) \cdot (-4\textbf{i} + \textbf{j} + 3\textbf{k}) \\[6pt]
    &= (-2)(-4) + (-1)(1) + (6)(3) \\[6pt]
    &= 25
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the angle between two vectors
    formula, we deduce
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{D\^{A}B}) &= \dfrac{\vec{\text{AB}} \cdot \vec{\text{AD}}}{\raisebox{-0.35em}{$\big|\vec{\text{AB}}\big|\big|\vec{\text{AD}}\big|$}} \hspace{4.4em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{25}{\raisebox{-0.35em}{$\big|\vec{\text{AB}}\big|\big|\vec{\text{AD}}\big|$}} \\[10pt]
    &> 0
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence $\text{D\^{A}B}$ is acute and
    $\text{A\^{B}C} = \ang{180} - \text{D\^{A}B}$ is
     $\text{obtuse}$
