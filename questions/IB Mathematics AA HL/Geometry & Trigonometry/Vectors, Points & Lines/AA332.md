# AA332

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows the parallelogram ABCD.

![c83a023b0ba0a590fcaaa9dff5b2c31f1012ca2a.svg](images/c83a023b0ba0a590fcaaa9dff5b2c31f1012ca2a.svg)

Let E denote the midpoint of \[BD\], $\textbf{p} = \vec{\text{BA}}$ and
$\textbf{q} = \vec{\text{BC}}$.

Find each of the following vectors in terms of $\textbf{p}$ and
$\textbf{q}$.

1.  $\vec{\text{AC}}$ ; [2 marks]

2.  $\vec{\text{BE}}$ ; [2 marks]

3.  $\vec{\text{AE}}$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{AC}} &= \vec{\text{AB}} + \vec{\text{BC}} \\[6pt]
    &= (-\textbf{p}) + \textbf{q} \\[8pt]
    &= \textbf{q} - \textbf{p}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.8em} \vec{\text{BE}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\hspace{0.05em}\vec{\text{BD}} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\hspace{-0.05em}\big(\vec{\text{BA}} + \vec{\text{AD}}\big) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\hspace{-0.05em}\big(\vec{\text{BA}} + \vec{\text{BC}}\big) \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\textbf{p} + \textbf{q})
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.6em} \vec{\text{AE}} &= \vec{\text{AB}} + \vec{\text{BE}} \\[9pt]
    &= (-\textbf{p}) + \dfrac{1}{2}(\textbf{p} + \textbf{q}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(-2\textbf{p}) + \dfrac{1}{2}(\textbf{p} + \textbf{q}) \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\textbf{q} - \textbf{p})
    \end{aligned}
    $$
