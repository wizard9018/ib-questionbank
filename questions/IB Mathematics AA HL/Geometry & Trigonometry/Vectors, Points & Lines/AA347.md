# AA347

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let
$\mathbf{u} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ -1 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix*}$
and
$\mathbf{v} = \hspace{-0.15em} \begin{pmatrix*}[c] m \\ n \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*}$ .
Given that $\mathbf{v}$ is a unit vector perpendicular to $\mathbf{u}$,

find the possible values of $m$ and the possible values of $n$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
|\mathbf{v}| &= 1 \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\mathbf{v}$ is a unit vector}] \\[6pt]
\textstyle \hspace{9.8em} \sqrt{m^2 + n^2 + 0^2} &= 1 \\[6pt]
m^2 + n^2 & =1
\end{aligned}
$$
and

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v} &= 0 \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\mathbf{u} \perp \mathbf{v}$}] \\[6pt]
(2)(m) + (-1)(n) + (5)(0) &= 0 \\[6pt]
2m - n &= 0 \\[6pt]
2m &= n
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the equations $(1)$ and
$(2)$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
m^2 + (2m)^2 & =1 \\[10pt]
5m^2 & =1 \\[6pt]
m^2 &= \dfrac{1}{5} \\[3pt]
m &= \pm \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{5}} \hspace{0.55em} \\
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence the
possible values of $m$ and $n$ are

$$
\begin{aligned}
&m = \dfrac{-1}{\sqrt{5}},\hspace{0.25em} n = \dfrac{-2}{\sqrt{5}} \\[9pt]
&m = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{5}},\hspace{0.25em} n = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{5}}
\end{aligned}
$$
