# AA891

**Maximum mark: 5**

Given any two non-zero vectors, $u$ and $v$, show that
$|u\times v|^2 = |u|^2|v|^2 - (u\cdot v)^2$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the vector product formula, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
|u\times v| &= |u||v|\sin \theta \\[12pt]
|u\times v|^2 &= |u|^2|v|^2\sin^2\theta \\[12pt]
&= |u|^2|v|^2\left(1-\cos^2\theta\right) \\[12pt]
&= |u|^2|v|^2-|u|^2|v|^2\cos^2\theta \\[12pt]
&= |u|^2|v|^2-\left(|u||v|\cos\theta\right)^2 \\[12pt]
&= |u|^2|v|^2-\left(u\cdot v\right)^2
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, we have
