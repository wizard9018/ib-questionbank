# AA331

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $\textbf{u} = 5\textbf{i} + 2\textbf{j} - \textbf{k}$ and
$\textbf{v} = 2\textbf{i} - 3\textbf{j} + n\textbf{k}$ where
$n \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  Given that $\textbf{u}$ is perpendicular to $\textbf{v}$, find the
    value of $n$. [3 marks]

Let $\textbf{w} = 7\textbf{i} + p\textbf{j} - 3\textbf{k}$ where
$p \in \mathbb{R}$.

2.  Given that $|\textbf{w}| = \sqrt{74}$, find the possible
    values for $p$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the scalar product formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textbf{u} \cdot \textbf{v} &= 0 \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\textbf{u} \perp \textbf{v}$}] \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
    (5\textbf{i} + 2\textbf{j} - \textbf{k}) \cdot (2\textbf{i} - 3\textbf{j} + n\textbf{k}) &= 0 \\[6pt]
    (5)(2) + (2)(-3) + (-1)(n) &= 0 \\[6pt]
    4 - n &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{2.15em} n = 4
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    |\textbf{w}| &= \sqrt{74} \hspace{3.75em} \\[6pt]
    |7\textbf{i} + p\textbf{j} - 3\textbf{k}| &= \sqrt{74} \\[6pt]
    \textstyle \sqrt{7^2 + p^2 + (-3)^2} &= \sqrt{74} \\[6pt]
    \textstyle 49 + p^2 + 9 &= 74 \\[6pt]
    \textstyle p^2 &= 16 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{3em} p = \pm 4
    $$
