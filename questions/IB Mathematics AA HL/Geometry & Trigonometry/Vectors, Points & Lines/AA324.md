# AA324

**Maximum mark: 6**

The position vectors of the points A, B and C are $\mathbf{a}$,
$\mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{c}$, respectively,
relative to the origin O.

![266be74a9d66d8ad28b4a59082e625f3c9478eac.svg](images/266be74a9d66d8ad28b4a59082e625f3c9478eac.svg)

Given that $\mathbf{a}$ is perpendicular to $\vec{\text{BC}}$ and
$\mathbf{b}$ is perpendicular to
 $\vec{\text{CA}}$, prove
that $\mathbf{c}$ is perpendicular to
$\vec{\text{AB}}$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$

\begin{array}{rcccl}
\vec{\text{AB}} &=& \vec{\text{OB}} - \vec{\text{OA}} &=& \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a} \\[6pt]
\vec{\text{BC}} &=& \vec{\text{OC}} - \vec{\text{OB}} &=& \mathbf{c} - \mathbf{b} \\[6pt]
\vec{\text{CA}} &=& \vec{\text{OA}} - \vec{\text{OC}} &=& \mathbf{a} - \mathbf{c}
\end{array}
$$
Hence, using the properties of scalar product, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{a} \cdot \vec{\text{BC}} &= 0 \\[5pt]
\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{c} - \mathbf{b}) &= 0 \\[5pt]
\hspace{0.15em} \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c} - \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} &= 0 \\[5pt]
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c} &= \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} \hspace{0.9em}
\end{aligned}
$$

and
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{b} \cdot \vec{\text{CA}} &= 0 \\[5pt]
\mathbf{b} \cdot (\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{c}) &= 0 \\[5pt]
\mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{c} &= 0 \\[5pt]
\mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{a} &= \mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{c} \hspace{0.9em}
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, using the properties of scalar product, we obtain

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{13em} \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} &= \mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{a} \\[5pt]
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c} &=  \mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{c} \hspace{4em} [\text{by $(1)$ and $(2)$}] \\[5pt]
0 &= \mathbf{b} \cdot \mathbf{c} - \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{c} \\[5pt]
(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}) \cdot \mathbf{c} &= 0 \\[4pt]
\vec{\text{AB}} \cdot \mathbf{c} &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence $\mathbf{c}$
is perpendicular to $\vec{\text{AB}}$.
