# AA335

**Maximum mark: 5**

In the following diagram, $\vec{\text{CA}} = \mathbf{p}$,
$\vec{\text{CB}} = \mathbf{q}$ and
$\vec{\text{AD}} = \dfrac{1}{2}\vec{\text{AB}}$.

![c071bff1f572ad731d80b239235e4ab30d7ec1aa.svg](images/c071bff1f572ad731d80b239235e4ab30d7ec1aa.svg)

Express each of the following vectors in terms of $\mathbf{p}$ and
$\mathbf{q}$.

1.  $\vec{\text{AB}}$ ; [2 marks]

2.  $\vec{\text{CD}}$ . [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{AB}} &= \vec{\text{AC}} + \vec{\text{CB}} \hspace{1.9em} \\[6pt]
    &= (-\mathbf{p}) + \mathbf{q} \\[6pt]
    &= \mathbf{q} - \mathbf{p}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8.2em} \vec{\text{CD}} &= \vec{\text{CA}} + \vec{\text{AD}} \\[10pt]
    &= \vec{\text{CA}} + \dfrac{1}{2}\vec{\text{AB}} \\[6pt]
    &= \mathbf{p} + \dfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{q} - \mathbf{p}) \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(2\mathbf{p}) + \dfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{q} - \mathbf{p}) \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{p} + \mathbf{q})
    \end{aligned}
    $$
