# AA931

**Maximum mark: 9**

Consider two vectors $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ such that $\mathbf{u} = \begin{pmatrix*}[c] -6 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix*}$ and $|\mathbf{v}|=20$.

1. Find the possible range of values for $|\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}|$. [2 marks]

2. Given that $\mathbf{v}=k \mathbf{u}$ for some $k\in\mathbb{R}$, find $\mathbf{v}$ when $|\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}|$ is a minimum. [2 marks]

3. Find the vector $\mathbf{w}=\begin{pmatrix*}[c] a \\ b \end{pmatrix*}$ such that $a,b \in \mathbb{R}^+$, $|\mathbf{w}|=|\mathbf{v}|$ and $\mathbf{w}$ is perpendicular to $\mathbf{u}$. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. First we find the magnitude of $\mathbf{u}$:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    |\mathbf{u}| &= \sqrt{(-6)^2+8^2} \\[10pt]

    &= \sqrt{100} \\[10pt]

    &= 10\\[-12pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Using the triangle inequality, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \big||\mathbf{u}| - |\mathbf{v}|\big| &\leq |\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}| \leq |\mathbf{u}| + |\mathbf{v}| \\[12pt]

    \big|10 - 20\big| &\leq |\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}| \leq 10 + 20 \\[12pt]

    \big|-10\big| &\leq |\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}| \leq 30\\[12pt]

    & \hspace{-0.99em}\boxed{10\leq |\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}| \leq 30}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Let $\mathbf{v} = k\mathbf{u} = \begin{pmatrix*}[c] -6k \\ 8k \end{pmatrix*}$, then $\mathbf{v}$ satisfies:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \big|\mathbf{v}\big| &= 20 \\[10pt]

    \sqrt{(-6k)^2 + (8k)^2} &= 20\\[10pt]

    100k^2 &= 400 \\[10pt]

    k^2 &= 4 \\[10pt]

    k &= \pm 2 \\[-12pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Now, since $|\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}| = 10$ we must have $k = -2$. Therefore

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &\hspace{-2.16mm}\boxed{\mathbf{v} = \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 12 \\ -16 \end{pmatrix*}} 

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. Using that the scalar product of two perpendicular vectors is zero, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{w} &= 0 \\[10pt]

    -6a+8b &= 0 \\[10pt]

    8b &= 6a \\[8pt]

    b &= \dfrac{3}{4}a

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Hence,

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathbf{w} &= \begin{pmatrix*}[c] a \\[4pt] \dfrac{3}{4}a \end{pmatrix*}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Now, using that $|\mathbf{w}| = 20$, we obtain

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \sqrt{a^2+\left(\dfrac{3}{4}a\right)^2} &= 20 \\[8pt]

    a^2 + \dfrac{9}{16}a^2 &= 400 \\[10pt]

    \dfrac{25}{16}a^2 &= 400 \\[10pt]

    a^2 &= 256 \\[12pt]

    a &= 16 \hspace{3em} [\text{since }a>0]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore,

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &\hspace{-3mm}\boxed{\mathbf{w} = \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 16 \\[4pt] 12 \end{pmatrix*}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
