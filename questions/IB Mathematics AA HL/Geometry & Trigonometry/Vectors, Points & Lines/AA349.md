# AA349

**Maximum mark: 8**

The line $L$ has equation
$\mathbf{r} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}, \hspace{0.25em} t \in \mathbb{R}$.

The point A has coordinates $(4,8,-3)$. The point B lies on $L$ such
that $\vec{\text{AB}}$\
is perpendicular to $L$.

1.  Find $\vec{\text{OB}}$. [4 marks]

2.  Find the shortest distance from A to $L$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the coordinates of the reflection of the point A in the line
    $L$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.5em} \vec{\text{OB}} &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{2em} [\text{for some $t \in \mathbb{R}$}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    and
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{AB}} &= \vec{\text{OB}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] 4 + t\phantom{\hspace{0.1em}} \\ \hspace{-0.25em} -1 + t\phantom{\hspace{0.1em}} \\ 0 - t\phantom{\hspace{0.1em}} \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} - \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ 8 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{1.75em} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] t \\ t-9 \\ 3-t \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \begin{pmatrix*}[c] t \\ t-9 \\ 3-t \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}\cdot\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*} &= 0 \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\vec{\text{AB}} \perp L$}] \\[6pt]
    \hspace{4.8em} (t)(1) + (t-9)(1) + (3-t)(-1) &= 0 \\[9pt]
    3t - 12 &= 0 \\[9pt]
    t &= 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, substituting $t = 4$ in the equation
    $(1)$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.7em} \vec{\text{OB}} &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}4\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{0.525em}\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 8 \\ 3 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have

    ![9202e38aec1b6f80eafe90905f697bf0d80eab98.svg](images/9202e38aec1b6f80eafe90905f697bf0d80eab98.svg)

    Substituting $t = 4$ in the equation $(2)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{AB}} &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ 4-9 \\ 3-4 \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{2.7em} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ -5 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.4em} d_{\text{min}} &= |\vec{\text{AB}}| \\[6pt]
    &= \textstyle \sqrt{4^2 + (-5)^2 + (-1)^2} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{42}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textstyle  \vec{\text{OA}'} &= \vec{\text{OB}} + \vec{\text{AB}} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 8 \\ 3 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ -5 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 12 \\ -2 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix*} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of $\text{A}'$ are
     $(12,-2,-5)$
