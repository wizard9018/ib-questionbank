# AA329

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $\vec{\text{AB}} = \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ 2 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*}$
and $\vec{\text{AC}} = \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ -2 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix*}$.

1.  Find $\vec{\text{BC}}$. [1 mark]

2.  Find the unit vector in the direction of $\vec{\text{AB}}$.
    [3 marks]

3.  Show that $\vec{\text{AB}}$ is perpendicular to $\vec{\text{AC}}$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{11.8em} \vec{\text{BC}} &= \vec{\text{BA}} + \vec{\text{AC}} \\[6pt]
    &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -4 \\ -2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em} + \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ -2 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\vec{\text{BA}} = - \vec{\text{AB}}$}] \\[9pt]
    &= \hspace{0.525em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -1 \\ -4 \\ 9 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} \big|\vec{\text{AB}}\big| &= |4\textbf{i} + 2\textbf{j} - \textbf{k}| \\[6pt]
    &= \textstyle \sqrt{4^2 + 2^2 + (-1)^2} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{21}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the unit vector in the direction of $\vec{\text{AB}}$ is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\vec{\text{AB}}}{\raisebox{-0.3em}{$\big|\vec{\text{AB}}\big|$}} &= \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{21}}\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ 2 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*} \\[9pt]
    &= \hspace{0.525em}\begin{pmatrix*}[r] \frac{4}{\sqrt{21}} \\[3pt] \frac{2}{\sqrt{21}} \\[3pt] \frac{-1}{\sqrt{21}} \end{pmatrix*} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the scalar product formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.8em} \vec{\text{AB}} \cdot \vec{\text{AC}} &= (4\textbf{i} + 2\textbf{j} - \textbf{k})\cdot(3\textbf{i} - 2\textbf{j} + 8\textbf{k}) \\[6pt]
    &= (4)(3) + (2)(-2) + (-1)(8) \\[6pt]
    &= 12-4-8 \\[6pt]
    &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence $\vec{\text{AB}}$ is perpendicular to
    $\vec{\text{AC}}$.
