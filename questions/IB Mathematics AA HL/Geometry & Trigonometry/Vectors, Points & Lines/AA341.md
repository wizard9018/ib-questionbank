# AA341

**Maximum mark: 13**

Two points A and B have coordinates $(2,3,1)$ and $(5,9,3)$
respectively.

1.
    1.  Find $\vec{\text{AB}}$.

    2.  Find $\big|\vec{\text{AB}}\big|$. [4 marks]

Let $\vec{\text{AC}} = 4\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - 4\mathbf{k}$.

2.  Find the angle between $\vec{\text{AB}}$ and
    $\vec{\text{AC}}$. [4 marks]

3.  Find the area of triangle ABC. [2 marks]

4.  Hence, or otherwise, find the shortest distance from C to
    the line passing through A and B.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \vec{\text{OA}} &= 2\textbf{i} + 3\textbf{j} + \textbf{k} \\[6pt]
        \vec{\text{OB}} &= 5\textbf{i} + 9\textbf{j} + 3\textbf{k} \hspace{2em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{5.55em} \vec{\text{AB}} &= \vec{\text{OB}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[6pt]
        &= (5\textbf{i} + 9\textbf{j} + 3\textbf{k}) - (2\textbf{i} + 3\textbf{j} + \textbf{k}) \\[6pt]
        &= 3\textbf{i} + 6\textbf{j} + 2\textbf{k}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \big|\vec{\text{AB}}\big| &= \big|3\textbf{i} + 6\textbf{j} + 2\textbf{k}\big| \\[6pt]
        &= \textstyle \sqrt{3^2 + 6^2 + 2^2} \hspace{1.7em} \\[7pt]
        &= 7
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{AB}} &= 3\textbf{i} + 6\textbf{j} + 2\textbf{k} \\[6pt]
    \vec{\text{AC}} &= 4\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - 4\mathbf{k}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the angle between two vectors formula, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \cos \theta &= \dfrac{\vec{\text{AB}} \cdot \vec{\text{AC}}}{\raisebox{-0.35em}{$\big|\vec{\text{AB}}\big|\big|\vec{\text{AC}}\big|$}} \\[5pt]
    \cos \theta &= \dfrac{(3)(4) + (6)(2) + (2)(-4)}{\big[\sqrt{3^2+6^2+2^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]\big[\sqrt{4^2+2^2+(-4)^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.2em} \theta \approx \ang{67.6} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.9em} \big|\vec{\text{AC}}\big| &= \big|4\mathbf{i} + 2\mathbf{j} - 4\mathbf{k}\big| \\[6pt]
    &= \textstyle \sqrt{4^2 + 2^2 + (-4)^2}  \\[7pt]
    &= 6
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the area of a triangle formula, we
    find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.4em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big|\vec{\text{AB}}\big|\big|\vec{\text{AC}}\big|\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(7)(6)\sin \ang{67.6} \\[11pt]
    &\approx 19.4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  If we sketch triangle ABC, we have

    ![eafe1ba171cbc18d6717423f09cd26875612d70c.svg](images/eafe1ba171cbc18d6717423f09cd26875612d70c.svg)

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    d_{\text{min}} &= 6\sin \ang{67.6} \hspace{1.15em} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 5.55
    \end{aligned}
    $$
