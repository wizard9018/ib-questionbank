# AA317

**Maximum mark: 4**

O, A, B and C are distinct points such that
$\vec{\text{OA}} = \mathbf{a}$, $\vec{\text{OB}} = \mathbf{b}$ and
$\vec{\text{OC}} = \mathbf{c}$.

It is given that $\mathbf{a}$ is perpendicular to $\mathbf{b}$ and
$\mathbf{c}$.

Prove that $\mathbf{a}$ is perpendicular to $\vec{\text{BC}}$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1em} \vec{\text{BC}} &= \vec{\text{OC}} -\vec{\text{OB}} \\[6pt]
&= \mathbf{c} - \mathbf{b}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the properties of
scalar product, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{10.95em} \mathbf{a} \cdot \vec{\text{BC}} &= \mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{c} - \mathbf{b})  \\[6pt]
&= \mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{c} - \mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} \\[6pt]
&= 0 - 0 \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\mathbf{a}\perp\mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{a}\perp\mathbf{c}$}] \\[6pt]
&= 0
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence $\mathbf{a}$ is perpendicular to
$\vec{\text{BC}}$.
