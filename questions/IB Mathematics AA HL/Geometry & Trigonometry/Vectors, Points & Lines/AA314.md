# AA314

**Maximum mark: 14**

The position vectors of the points A, B and C are
$\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b}$ and $\mathbf{c}$,
respectively, relative to an origin O.

The following diagram shows the triangle ABC and points N, R, S and T.

![cb7aed8c5ba2f3296562ebe80fd7f4a0d37ebcea.svg](images/cb7aed8c5ba2f3296562ebe80fd7f4a0d37ebcea.svg)

$$
\begin{array}{cccccl}
\text{N} & \text{is a point on} &\text{[AB]}& \text{such that } \vec{\text{AN}} &=& \dfrac{3}{7}\hspace{0.2em}\vec{\text{AB}}. \\[9pt]
\text{R} & \text{is a point on} &\text{[BC]}& \text{such that } \vec{\text{BR}} &=& \dfrac{2}{5}\hspace{0.2em}\vec{\text{BC}}. \\[9pt]
\text{S} & \text{is a point on} &\text{[CA]}& \text{such that } \vec{\text{CS}} &=& \dfrac{2}{5}\hspace{0.2em}\vec{\text{CA}}. \\[9pt]
\text{T} & \text{is a point on} &\text{[RS]}& \text{such that } \vec{\text{RT}} &=& \dfrac{2}{3}\hspace{0.2em}\vec{\text{RS}}.
\end{array} \hspace{20em}
$$

1.
    1.  Express $\vec{\text{AN}}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and
        $\mathbf{b}$.

    2.  Hence show that
        $\vec{\text{CN}} = \dfrac{4}{7}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{a} + \dfrac{3}{7}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{c}$.
        [4 marks]

2.
    1.  Express $\vec{\text{RC}}$ in terms of $\mathbf{b}$ and
        $\mathbf{c}$ and $\vec{\text{CS}}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and
        $\mathbf{c}$.

    2.  Hence show that
        $\vec{\text{RT}} = \dfrac{4}{15}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{a} - \dfrac{6}{15}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{b} + \dfrac{2}{15}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{c}$.
        [5 marks]

3.  Prove that T lies on \[CN\]. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \vec{\text{AN}} &= \dfrac{3}{7}\hspace{0.2em}\vec{\text{AB}} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{3}{7}(\vec{\text{OB}} - \vec{\text{OA}}) \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{3}{7}(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a})
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using part (a) (i), we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2.5em} \vec{\text{CN}} &= \vec{\text{CA}} + \vec{\text{AN}} \\[9pt]
        &= (\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{c}) + \dfrac{3}{7}(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}) \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{4}{7}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{a} + \dfrac{3}{7}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{c}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \vec{\text{RC}} &= \vec{\text{BC}} - \vec{\text{BR}} \\[9pt]
        &= \vec{\text{BC}} - \dfrac{2}{5}\hspace{0.2em}\vec{\text{BC}} \hspace{0.1em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{3}{5}\hspace{0.2em}\vec{\text{BC}} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{3}{5}(\vec{\text{OC}} - \vec{\text{OB}}) \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{3}{5}(\mathbf{c} - \mathbf{b})
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Similarly, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.2em} \vec{\text{CS}} &= \dfrac{2}{5}\hspace{0.2em}\vec{\text{CA}} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{2}{5}(\vec{\text{OA}} - \vec{\text{OC}}) \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{2}{5}(\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{c})
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using part (b) (i), we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{5.1em} \vec{\text{RT}} &= \dfrac{2}{3}\hspace{0.2em}\vec{\text{RS}} \\[8pt]
        &= \dfrac{2}{3}(\vec{\text{RC}} + \vec{\text{CS}}) \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{2}{3}\bigg[\dfrac{3}{5}(\mathbf{c} - \mathbf{b}) + \dfrac{2}{5}(\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{c})\bigg] \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{4}{15}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{a} - \dfrac{6}{15}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{b} + \dfrac{2}{15}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{c}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{CT}} &= \vec{\text{CR}} + \vec{\text{RT}} \\[10pt]
    \hspace{2em} [\text{by part (b)}] \hspace{3em} &= \bigg[\hspace{-0.2em}-\dfrac{3}{5}(\mathbf{c} - \mathbf{b})\bigg] + \bigg[\dfrac{4}{15}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{a} - \dfrac{6}{15}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{b} + \dfrac{2}{15}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{c}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{4}{15}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{a} + \dfrac{3}{15}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{b} - \dfrac{7}{15}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{c} \\[7pt]
    &= \dfrac{7}{15}\bigg[\dfrac{4}{7}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{a} + \dfrac{3}{7}\hspace{0.1em}\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{c}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    [\text{by part (a)}] \hspace{3em} &= \dfrac{7}{15}\hspace{0.2em}\vec{\text{CN}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence T is common to $\vec{\text{CT}}$ and $\vec{\text{CN}}$.

    Therefore T lies on \[CN\].
