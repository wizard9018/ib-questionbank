# AA339

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows the triangle ABC.

![5b86518022b4b342c8c9fbb7f26177d16d5c984d.svg](images/5b86518022b4b342c8c9fbb7f26177d16d5c984d.svg)

Given that $\vec{\text{BA}} \cdot \vec{\text{BC}} = -6$ and
$\big|\vec{\text{BA}}\big|\big|\vec{\text{BC}}\big| = 12$, find the area
of triangle ABC.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the angle between two vectors formula, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2em} \cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) &= \dfrac{\vec{\text{BA}} \cdot \vec{\text{BC}}}{\raisebox{-0.35em}{$\big|\vec{\text{BA}}\big|\big|\vec{\text{BC}}\big|$}} \\[6pt]
\cos \hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) &= -\dfrac{6}{12} \\[11pt]
\cos \hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) &= -\dfrac{1}{2} \\[11pt]
\text{C\^{B}A} &= \dfrac{2\pi}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the area
of a triangle formula, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big|\vec{\text{BA}}\big|\big|\vec{\text{BC}}\big|\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[9pt]
&= \dfrac{1}{2}(12)\sin\hspace{-0.15em}\Big(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\Big) \\[6pt]
&= \dfrac{1}{2}(12)\hspace{-0.05em}\bigg[\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\bigg] \\[11pt]
&= 3\sqrt{3}
\end{aligned}
$$
