# AA352

**Maximum mark: 5**

**In this question, distances are measured in kilometres.**

Two boats, A and B, are observed from an origin O. Relative to O, their
position vectors at time $t$ hours after midday
are given by
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{r}_\text{A} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -4 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.05em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}, \\[16pt]
\mathbf{r}_\text{B} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -2 \\ 9 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.05em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 5 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em},
\end{aligned}
$$

Find the minimum distance between the two boats.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{3em} \mathbf{r}_\text{B} - \mathbf{r}_\text{A} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\left[\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -2 \\ 9 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.05em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 5 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}\right]\hspace{-0.1em}- \left[\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -4 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.05em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}\right] \\[6pt]
&= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.05em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
&= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2+t \\ 6-3t \end{pmatrix*}
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence the distance between the boats at time $t$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2em} d &= |\mathbf{r}_\text{B} - \mathbf{r}_\text{A}| \\[6pt]
&= \textstyle \sqrt{(2+t)^2 + (6-3t)^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

graph of $d$ vs $t$ \[by using G.D.C.\], we get

Hence the minimum distance between the boats is
 $3.79\hspace{0.25em}\text{km}$
