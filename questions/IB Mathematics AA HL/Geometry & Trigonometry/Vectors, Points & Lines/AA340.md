# AA340

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let
$\mathbf{u} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 3 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix*}$
and
$\mathbf{v} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] k \\ 2 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix*}$
where $k > 0$. The angle between $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ is
$\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

Find the value of $k$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the angle between two vectors formula, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{10.65em} \cos \theta &= \dfrac{\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v}}{|\mathbf{u}||\mathbf{v}|} \\[9pt]
\cos\hspace{-0.15em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{4}\Big)\hspace{-0.15em} &= \dfrac{(1)(k) + (3)(2) + (-2)(-4)}{\big[\sqrt{1^2+3^2+(-2)^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]\big[\sqrt{k^2+2^2+(-4)^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]} \\[6pt]
\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} &= \dfrac{k + 14}{\big[\sqrt{14}\hspace{0.1em}\big]\big[\sqrt{k^2+20}\hspace{0.1em}\big]} \\[8pt]
\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \textstyle \big[\sqrt{14}\hspace{0.1em}\big]\big[\sqrt{k^2+20}\hspace{0.1em}\big]\hspace{-0.15em} &= k + 14 \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hspace{6.7em} k \approx 6.18 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
$$
