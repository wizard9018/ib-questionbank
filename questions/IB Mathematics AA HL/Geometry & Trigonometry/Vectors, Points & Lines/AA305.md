# AA305

**Maximum mark: 4**

The points A and B have position vectors
$\vec{\text{OA}} = \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 4 \\ 12 \end{pmatrix*}$
and
$\vec{\text{OB}} = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 \\ 1 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}$.

1.  Find $\vec{\text{OA}} \times \vec{\text{OB}}$. [2 marks]

2.  Hence find the area of the triangle OAB. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the vector product formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.9em} \vec{\text{OA}} \times \vec{\text{OB}} &= (2\textbf{i} + 4\textbf{j} + 12\textbf{k})\times(\textbf{j} + 2\textbf{k}) \\[8pt]
    &= \left|\begin{matrix*}[c] \hspace{0.25em}\textbf{i} & \hspace{0.25em}\textbf{j} & \textbf{k} \\ \hspace{0.25em}2 & \hspace{0.25em}4 & 12 \\ \hspace{0.25em}0 & \hspace{0.25em}1 & 2  \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.25em}\right| \\[8pt]
    &= [8-12]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{i} - [4-0]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{j} + [2-0]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{k}  \\[12pt]
    &= -4\textbf{i} - 4\textbf{j} + 2\textbf{k} \hspace{2.8em} \\[8pt]
    &= \begin{pmatrix*}[r] -4 \\ -4 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.3em} A_{\triangle\text{OAB}} &= \dfrac{1}{2} \big|\vec{\text{OA}} \times \vec{\text{OB}}\big| \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2} \big|\hspace{-0.2em}-4\textbf{i} - 4\textbf{j} + 2\textbf{k}\big| \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{(-4)^2 + (-4)^2 + 2^2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{36} \\[12pt]
    &= 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
