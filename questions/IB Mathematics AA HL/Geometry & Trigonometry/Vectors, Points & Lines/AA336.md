# AA336

**Maximum mark: 8**

The line $L_1$ passes through the points A$(4,7,-4)$ and B$(4,6,-3)$.

1.
    1.  Show that
        $\vec{\text{AB}} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}$ .
    2.  Write down the vector equation of $L_1$ in the form
        $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{p} + t\hspace{0.05em}\mathbf{d}$.
        [2 marks]

Another line $L_2$ has equation
$\mathbf{r} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}s\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em},\hspace{0.25em} s \in \mathbb{R}$.
The lines $L_1$ and $L_2$

intersect at point C.

2.  Find $\vec{\text{OC}}$. [3 marks]

3.  Find the acute angle between $\vec{\text{OC}}$ and $L_1$.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \vec{\text{AB}} &= \vec{\text{OB}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[6pt]
        &=\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ 6 \\ -3  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} - \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ 7 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix*} =\hspace{0.525em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{0.7em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.
        $\mathbf{r} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ 7 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em},\hspace{0.5em} t \in \mathbb{R}$

2.  Using the vector equations of $L_1$ and $L_2$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.6em} 2 - s &= 4   \\[6pt]
    1 &= 7 - t
    %4 + s &= -4 + t
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Solving the system of
    linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $s$ and $t$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.75em} s = -2\hspace{0.75em}\&\hspace{0.75em} t = 6 \hspace{9.6em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, substituting $s = -2$ in the equation of $L_2$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.65em} \vec{\text{OC}} &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} - \hspace{0.1em}2\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} \\[6pt]
    &=\hspace{0.525em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ 1 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{9.15em} \mathbf{c} &= \hspace{0.31em}4\textbf{i} + \textbf{j} + 2\textbf{k} \hspace{3em} [= \vec{\text{OC}}] \\[6pt]
    \mathbf{d} &= - \textbf{j} + \textbf{k} \hspace{5.075em} [\text{direction vector of $L_1$}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the angle between two vectors formula, we find

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{5.7em} \cos \theta &= \dfrac{\mathbf{c}\cdot\mathbf{d}}{|\mathbf{c}||\mathbf{d}|} \\[5pt]
     \cos \theta &= \dfrac{(4)(0) + (1)(-1) + (2)(1)}{\big[\sqrt{4^2+1^2+2^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]\big[\sqrt{0^2+(-1)^2+1^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]} \\[9pt]
     \theta &\approx \ang{81.1} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
     \end{aligned}
     $$

    Hence the acute angle between $\vec{\text{OC}}$ and $L_1$ is
     $\ang{81.1}$
