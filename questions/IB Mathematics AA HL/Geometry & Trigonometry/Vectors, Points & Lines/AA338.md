# AA338

**Maximum mark: 11**

Let A and B be points such that
$\vec{\text{OA}} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix*}$
and
$\vec{\text{OB}} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ -2 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*}$ .

1.  Show that
    $\vec{\text{AB}} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -2 \\ -3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}$ .
    [2 marks]

Let C and D be points such that ABCD is a rectangle.

2.  Given that
    $\vec{\text{AD}} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] q \\ -4 \\ -6 \end{pmatrix*}$ ,
    show that $q = 3$. [3 marks]

3.  Find the coordinates of point C. [3 marks]

4.  Find the area of the rectangle ABCD. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \vec{\text{AB}} &= \vec{\text{OB}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[6pt]
    &=\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ -2 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} - \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix*} \\[9pt]
    &=\hspace{0.525em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -2 \\ -3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have

    ![f442ac6be90e1a4f1865bfcdc42b6af4c70e81f6.svg](images/f442ac6be90e1a4f1865bfcdc42b6af4c70e81f6.svg)

    Hence, using the scalar product formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \vec{\text{AB}} \cdot \vec{\text{AD}} &= 0 \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\vec{\text{AB}} \perp \vec{\text{AD}}$}] \\[6pt]
    \begin{pmatrix*}[c] -2 \\ -3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} \cdot \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] q \\ -4 \\ -6 \end{pmatrix*} &= 0 \\[6pt]
    \hspace{3.8em} (-2)(q) + (-3)(-4) + (1)(-6) &= 0 \\[8pt]
    -2q + 6 &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{6.2em} q = 3
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{9.7em} \vec{\text{OC}} &=  \vec{\text{OB}} + \vec{\text{BC}} \\[8pt]
    &= \vec{\text{OB}} + \vec{\text{AD}} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\vec{\text{AB}} \perp \vec{\text{AD}}$}] \\[6pt]
    &=\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ -2 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ -4 \\ -6 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
    &=\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 4 \\ -6 \\ -7 \end{pmatrix*} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the coordinates of C are
     $(4,-6,-7)$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.9em} \big|\vec{\text{AB}}\big| &= \textstyle \sqrt{(-2)^2 + (-3)^2 + 1^2} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{4 + 9 + 1} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{14}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.9em} \big|\vec{\text{AD}}\big| &= \textstyle \sqrt{3^2 + (-4)^2 + (-6)^2} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{9 + 16 + 36} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{61}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the area of a rectangle
    formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\square\text{ABCD}} &= \big|\vec{\text{AB}}\big|\big|\vec{\text{AD}}\big| \\[6pt]
    &= \big(\sqrt{14}\big)\hspace{-0.1em}\big(\sqrt{61}\big) \hspace{1.7em} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{854}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
