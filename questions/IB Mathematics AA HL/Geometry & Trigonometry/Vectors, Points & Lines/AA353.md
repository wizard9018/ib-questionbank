# AA353

**Maximum mark: 5**

**In this question, distances are measured in kilometres.**

Two tankers, A and B, are observed from an origin O. Relative to O,
their position vectors at time $t$ hours after 6:00pm are given by

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{r}_\text{A} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.05em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 7 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}, \\[16pt]
\mathbf{r}_\text{B} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 6 \\ 9 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.05em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 5 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em},
\end{aligned}
$$

Find the time when the distance between the two tankers is at a minimum.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1.5em} \mathbf{r}_\text{B} - \mathbf{r}_\text{A} &= \hspace{-0.1em}\left[\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 6 \\ 9 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.05em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 5 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}\right]\hspace{-0.1em}- \left[\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.05em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 7 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}\right] \\[6pt]
&= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 5 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.05em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -2 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
&= \hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 5-2t \\ 8-t \end{pmatrix*}
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence the distance b$/$n the tankers at time $t$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2em} d &= |\mathbf{r}_\text{B} - \mathbf{r}_\text{A}| \\[6pt]
&= \textstyle \sqrt{(5-2t)^2 + (8-t)^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
If we draw the
graph of $d$ vs $t$ \[by using G.D.C.\], we get

![0a9045a2c8ff75e4cf565b8206c94e84658fadde.svg](images/0a9045a2c8ff75e4cf565b8206c94e84658fadde.svg)

Hence the time when the distance b$/$n the two tankers is at a minimum
is  $\text{9:36pm}$
