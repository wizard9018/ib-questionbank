# AA330

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $\textbf{u} = 6\textbf{i} - 2\textbf{j} - \textbf{k}$ and
$\textbf{v} = 3\textbf{i} + 5\textbf{j} + 3\textbf{k}$.

1.  Find:
    1.  $\textbf{u} + \textbf{v}$;

    2.  $|\textbf{u}|$;
    3.  $|\textbf{v}|$. [3 marks]

2.  Find $\textbf{u}\cdot\textbf{v}$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the angle between $\textbf{u}$ and $\textbf{v}$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3.2em} \textbf{u} + \textbf{v} &= (6\textbf{i} - 2\textbf{j} - \textbf{k}) + (3\textbf{i} + 5\textbf{j} + 3\textbf{k}) \\[8pt]
        &= 9\textbf{i} + 3\textbf{j} + 2\textbf{k}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The magnitude of $\textbf{u}$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.2em} |\textbf{u}| &= |6\textbf{i} - 2\textbf{j} - \textbf{k}| \\[6pt]
        &= \textstyle \sqrt{6^2 + (-2)^2 + (-1)^2} \\[8pt]
        &= \sqrt{41}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3.  The magnitude of $\textbf{v}$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        |\textbf{v}| &= |3\textbf{i} + 5\textbf{j} + 3\textbf{k}| \\[6pt]
        &= \textstyle \sqrt{3^2 + 5^2 + 3^2} \hspace{2.15em} \\[8pt]
        &= \sqrt{43}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Using the scalar product formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \textbf{u}\cdot\textbf{v} &= (6\textbf{i} - 2\textbf{j} - \textbf{k})\cdot(3\textbf{i} + 5\textbf{j} + 3\textbf{k}) \\[6pt]
    &= (6)(3) + (-2)(5) + (-1)(3) \\[6pt]
    &= 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the angle between two vectors formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7.15em} \cos \theta &= \dfrac{\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v}}{|\mathbf{u}||\mathbf{v}|} \\[5pt]
    \cos \theta &= \dfrac{5}{(\sqrt{41})(\sqrt{43})} \hspace{2.5em} [\text{by parts (a) \& (b)}]\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{5.1em} \theta \approx \ang{83.2} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
