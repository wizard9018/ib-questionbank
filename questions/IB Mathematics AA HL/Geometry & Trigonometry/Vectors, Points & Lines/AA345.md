# AA345

**Maximum mark: 9**

Two lines $L_1$ and $L_2$ are represented by the vector equations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
L_1:&\hspace{0.75em} \mathbf{r}_1 = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.05em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ 1 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em},\hspace{0.25em} t \in \mathbb{R}, \\[6pt]
L_2:&\hspace{0.75em} \mathbf{r}_2 = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -1 \\ -3 \\ 11 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.05em}s\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 2 \\ k \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.1em},\hspace{0.25em} s \in \mathbb{R}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The lines $L_1$ and $L_2$ are perpendicular to each other.

1.  Show that $k = -1$. [2 marks]

The lines $L_1$ and $L_2$ intersect at the point A.

2.  Find $\vec{\text{OA}}$. [3 marks]

Let
$\vec{\text{OB}} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 2 \\ 7 \end{pmatrix*}$
and
$\vec{\text{BC}} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}$ .

3.
    1.  Find $\vec{\text{BA}}$.

    2.  Hence find the angle CB̂A. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the scalar product formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ 1 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} \cdot \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 2 \\ k \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.25em} &= 0 \hspace{3em} [\text{since $L_1 \perp L_2$}] \\[6pt]
    \hspace{1.5em} (0)(1) + (1)(2) + (2)(k) &= 0 \\[10pt]
    2 + 2k &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{2.3em} k = -1
    $$

2.  Using the equations of $L_1$ and $L_2$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2 &= -1 + s  \\[6pt]
    1 + t &= -3 + 2s \hspace{1.1em}
    %4 + 2t &= 11 - s
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    If we solve the system
    of linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$, we get $s = 3$ and $t = 2$.

    Hence, substituting $s = 3$ in the equation of $L_2$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.5em} \vec{\text{OA}} &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -1 \\ -3 \\ 11 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.05em}3\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 2 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*} \\[10pt]
    &= \hspace{0.525em}\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 3 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \vec{\text{BA}} &= \vec{\text{OA}} - \vec{\text{OB}} \\[6pt]
        &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 3 \\ 8 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} - \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 2 \\ 7\end{pmatrix*} \hspace{2.7em} \\[10pt]
        &= \hspace{0.525em}\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 0 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the angle between two vectors formula, we find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3.5em} \cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) &= \dfrac{\vec{\text{BA}} \cdot \vec{\text{BC}}}{\raisebox{-0.35em}{$\big|\vec{\text{BA}}\big|\big|\vec{\text{BC}}\big|$}} \\[6pt]
        \cos \hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) &= \dfrac{(0)(1) + (1)(0) + (1)(1)}{\big[\sqrt{0^2 + 1^2 + 1^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]\big[\sqrt{1^2 + 0^2 + 1^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]} \\[9pt]
        \cos \hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) &= \dfrac{1}{2} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \text{C\^{B}A} = \dfrac{\pi}{3} \hspace{7.8em}
        $$
