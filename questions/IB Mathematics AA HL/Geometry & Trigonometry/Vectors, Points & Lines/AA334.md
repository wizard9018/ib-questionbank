# AA334

**Maximum mark: 8**

A line $L_1$ passes through the points A$(2,1,-1)$ and B$(5,-5,-4)$.

1.
    1.  Show that
        $\vec{\text{AB}} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ -6 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix*}$ .
    2.  Write down the vector equation of $L_1$ in the form
        $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{p} + t\hspace{0.05em}\mathbf{d}$.
        [2 marks]

A line $L_2$ has equation
$\mathbf{r} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 5 \\ 5 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}s\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -3 \\ 4 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em},\hspace{0.25em} s \in \mathbb{R}$.

2.  Find the acute angle between $L_1$ and $L_2$. [3 marks]

3.  The lines $L_1$ and $L_2$ intersect at point C. Find
    $\vec{\text{OC}}$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.7em} \vec{\text{AB}} &= \vec{\text{OB}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[4pt]
        &=\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 5 \\ -5 \\ -4  \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} - \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*} \\[10pt]
        &=\hspace{0.525em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ -6 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix*}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.
        $\mathbf{r} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ -6 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em},\hspace{0.5em} t \in \mathbb{R}$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.25em} \mathbf{d}_1 &= \phantom{-}3\textbf{i} - 6\textbf{j} - 3\textbf{k} \hspace{3em} [\text{direction vector of $L_1$}] \\[6pt]
    \mathbf{d}_2 &= -3\textbf{i} + 4\textbf{j} + 2\textbf{k} \hspace{3em} [\text{direction vector of $L_2$}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the angle between two vectors formula, we get

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{5.7em} \cos \theta &= \dfrac{\mathbf{d}_1\hspace{-0.1em}\cdot\mathbf{d}_2}{|\mathbf{d}_1||\mathbf{d}_2|} \\[12pt]
     \cos \theta &= \dfrac{(3)(-3) + (-6)(4) + (-3)(2)}{\big[\sqrt{3^2+(-6)^2+(-3)^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]\big[\sqrt{(-3)^2+4^2+2^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]} \\[16pt]
     \theta &= \ang{170.2...} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\
     \end{aligned}
     $$

    Hence the acute angle between $L_1$ and $L_2$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \ang{180} - \ang{170.2...} &=9.759...\\[6pt]
    &=\ang{9.76}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the vector equations of $L_1$ and $L_2$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    5 - 3s &= 2 + 3t \hspace{0.2em}  \\[6pt]
    5 + 4s &= 1 - 6t
    %1 + 2s &= -1 - 3t
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Solving the system of
    linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $s$ and $t$, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.75em} s = 5\hspace{0.75em}\&\hspace{0.75em} t = -4 \hspace{9.7em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, substituting $s = 5$ in the equation of $L_2$, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.65em} \vec{\text{OC}} &= \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 5 \\ 5 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}5\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -3 \\ 4 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} \\[8pt]
    &=\hspace{0.525em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -10 \\ 25 \\ 11 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
