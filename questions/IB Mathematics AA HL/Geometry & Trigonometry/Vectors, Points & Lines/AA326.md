# AA326

**Maximum mark: 5**

The points P and R have position vectors
$\vec{\text{OP}} = \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 0 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*}$ and
 $\vec{\text{OR}} = \begin{pmatrix*}[c] -2 \\ 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}$.

1.  Find $\vec{\text{OP}}\times\vec{\text{OR}}$. [3 marks]

2.  Hence find the area of the triangle OPR. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the vector product formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.75em} \vec{\text{OP}} \times \vec{\text{OR}} &= (2\textbf{i} - \textbf{k})\times(-2\textbf{i} + 2\textbf{j} + \textbf{k}) \\[8pt]
    &= \left|\begin{matrix*}[r] \hspace{0.25em}\textbf{i} & \hspace{0.75em}\textbf{j} & \textbf{k} \\  2 & 0 & -1 \\ -2 & 2 & 1  \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.25em}\right| \\[8pt]
    &= [0-(-2)]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{i} - [2-2]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{j} + [4-0]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{k}  \\[12pt]
    &= 2\textbf{i} + 4\textbf{k} \hspace{2.8em} \\[8pt]
    &= \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 2 \\ 0 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle\text{OPR}} &= \dfrac{1}{2} \big|\vec{\text{OP}} \times \vec{\text{OR}}\big| \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2} \big|2\textbf{i} + 4\textbf{k}\big| \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2^2 + 0^2 + 4^2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{20} \\[12pt]
    &= \sqrt{5}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
