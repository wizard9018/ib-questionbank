# AA264

**Maximum mark: 8**

The following diagram shows triangle ABC.

![4ccf804b6fad9991f97515916077867f3bac6aa8.svg](images/4ccf804b6fad9991f97515916077867f3bac6aa8.svg)

$\text{AB} = 12$ cm,
$\text{AC} = 16$ cm,
$\text{B\^{A}C} = \ang{34}$.

1.  Find BC. [3 marks]

2.  Find CB̂A, given that it is obtuse. [3 marks]

3.  Find the area of the triangle ABC. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \text{BC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{AC}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{AC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6pt]
    \text{BC} &= \sqrt{12^2 + 16^2 - 2(12)(16)\cos \ang{34}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.15em} \text{BC} \approx 9.04 \text{ cm} \hspace{3em} [\approx 9.0360 \text{ cm}]
    $$

2.  Using the sine rule, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\text{AC}}{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A})} &= \dfrac{\text{BC}}{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C})} \hspace{2.5em} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{16}{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A})} &= \dfrac{9.0360}{\sin \ang{34}} \\[6pt]
    \sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) &= \dfrac{16\sin \ang{34}}{9.0360} \\[6pt]
    \text{C\^{B}A} &\approx \ang{82.0}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $\textbf{Note:}$ This calculation produces the solution $\text{C\^{B}A} \approx \ang{82.0}$, however we have been told that $\text{C\^{B}A}$ is obtuse. This is an ambiguous case of the sine rule. Given that $\sin{\theta} = \sin{180^{\circ}-\theta}$, we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{C\^{B}A} &\approx 180^{\circ}-82^{\circ}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{8.1em} \text{C\^{B}A} \approx \ang{98.0} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

3.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.9em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(\text{AC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(12)(16)\sin \ang{34} \\[11pt]
    &\approx 53.7 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
