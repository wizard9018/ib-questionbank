# AA303

**Maximum mark: 16**

Jack sets out for a horse ride from his family ranch at point A. He
rides at an average speed of
$6.3$ km$/$h for $28$ minutes, on a bearing of
$\ang{40}$ from the ranch, until he
stops for a break near a spring at point B.

1.  Find the distance from point A to point B. [2 marks]

![895ff3d50c5e85f43580cba54d0b885fd3fc2664.svg](images/895ff3d50c5e85f43580cba54d0b885fd3fc2664.svg)

Jack leaves point B on a bearing of $\ang{112}$ and continues to ride
for a distance of
$5.8$ km until he reaches a river at point C.

![9719e0168f5f8056b390bc6270e68e32d772fab3.svg](images/9719e0168f5f8056b390bc6270e68e32d772fab3.svg)

2.
    1.  Show that AB̂C is $\ang{108}$.

    2.  Find the distance from point A to point C. [5 marks]

3.  Find BĈA. [3 marks]

Jack's brother John wants to ride a horse directly from the ranch to
meet Jack at point C.

4.  Find the bearing that John must take to point C.
    [3 marks]

John rides at an average speed of $12.9$ km$/$h.

5.  Find, to the nearest minute, the time it takes for John to
    reach point C. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the formula
    \"$\text{distance} = \text{speed} \times \text{time}$\", we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{AB} &= 6.3\times\dfrac{28}{60} \hspace{2.9em} \\[6pt]
    &= 2.94 \text{ km}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{A\^{B}C} &= \ang{40} + (\ang{180} - \ang{112}) \hspace{1.4em} \\[8pt]
        &= \ang{108}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the cosine rule, we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{AC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{BC}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{BC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{B}C}) \\[7pt]
        \text{AC} &= \sqrt{(2.94)^2 + (5.8)^2 - 2(2.94)(5.8)\cos \ang{108}} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \text{AC} \approx 7.27 \text{ km} \hspace{13.65em}
        $$

3.  Using the sine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\text{AB}}{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{C}A})} &= \dfrac{\text{AC}}{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{B}C})} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{2.94}{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{C}A})} &= \dfrac{7.27}{\sin \ang{108}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{10.6em} \text{B\^{C}A} \approx \ang{22.6} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.85em} \text{C\^{A}B} &= \ang{180} - (\text{A\^{B}C} + \text{B\^{C}A}) \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{180} - (\ang{108} + \ang{22.6}) \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{49.4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{bearing} &= \ang{40} + \text{C\^{A}B} \hspace{2.9em} \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{40} + \ang{49.4} \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{89.4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  Using the formula \"$\text{time} = \text{distance}/\text{speed}$\",
    we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10.7em} \text{time} &= \dfrac{\text{AC}}{\text{speed}} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{7.27}{12.9} \\[12pt]
    &\approx 0.564 \text{ hours} \hspace{3em} [\approx 0.564\times 60 \text{ minutes}] \\[15pt]
    &\approx 34 \text{ minutes}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
