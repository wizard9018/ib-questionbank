# AA275

**Maximum mark: 12**

The following diagram shows three cities A, B and C.

City B is $70$ km from A, on a bearing of
$\ang{130}$. City C is $40$ km from City
B, on bearing of $\ang{75}$.

![5713f313b248309218955735d1649078b8a3cc98.svg](images/5713f313b248309218955735d1649078b8a3cc98.svg)

1.  Find CB̂A. [2 marks]

2.  Find the distance from City A to C. [3 marks]

3.  If you wanted to travel from city A directly to City C, find the
    bearing you would need to travel.
    [4 marks]

4.  Find the area enclosed by connecting the three cities in a triangle
    ABC. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.1em} \text{C\^{B}A} &= (\ang{180}-\ang{130}) + \ang{75} \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{125}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{AC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{BC}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{BC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[6pt]
    \text{AC} &= \sqrt{70^2 + 40^2 - 2(70)(40)\cos \ang{125}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{AC} \approx 98.5 \text{ km} \hspace{11.4em}
    $$

3.  Using the sine rule, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.9em} \dfrac{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C})}{\text{BC}} &= \dfrac{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A})}{\text{AC}} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C})}{40} &= \dfrac{\sin \ang{125}}{98.5} \\[12pt]
    %\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) &= \dfrac{40\sin \ang{125}}{98.5} \\[12pt]
    \text{B\^{A}C} &\approx \ang{19.4196} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.45em} \text{bearing} &= \ang{130} - \text{B\^{A}C} \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{130} - \ang{19.4196} \\[6pt]
    &\approx \ang{111}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.4em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(\text{BC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[7pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(70)(40)\sin \ang{125} \\[12pt]
    &\approx 1150 \text{ km}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
