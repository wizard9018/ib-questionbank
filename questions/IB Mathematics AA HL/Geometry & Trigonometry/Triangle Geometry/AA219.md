# AA219

**Maximum mark: 6**

The triangle ABC is equilateral of side $5$ cm.
The point D lies on \[BC\] such that
$\text{DC} = 2$ cm. Find
$\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{D}A})$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

If we sketch the equilateral triangle ABC, we have

![34344a6ec2ffed1263f1efd0cd9e6fde9a8a3c10.svg](images/34344a6ec2ffed1263f1efd0cd9e6fde9a8a3c10.svg)

Using the cosine rule, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{3em} \text{AD}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{BD}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{BD})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[7pt]
\text{AD}^2 &= 5^2 + 3^2 - 2(5)(3)\cos \ang{60} \\[6pt]
\text{AD} &= \sqrt{25 + 9 - 30(1/2)} \\[6pt]
\text{AD} &= \sqrt{19} \text{ cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, using the cosine rule, we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{6em} \text{AC}^2 &= \text{AD}^2 + \text{DC}^2 - 2(\text{AD})(\text{DC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{D}A}) \\[13pt]
5^2 &= (\sqrt{19})^2 + 2^2 - 2(\sqrt{19})(2)\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{D}A}) \\[13pt]
25 &= 19 + 4 - 4\sqrt{19}\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{D}A}) \\[9pt]
\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{D}A}) &= \dfrac{19 + 4 - 25}{4\sqrt{19}} \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{D}A}) = -\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{19}} \hspace{9.15em}
$$
