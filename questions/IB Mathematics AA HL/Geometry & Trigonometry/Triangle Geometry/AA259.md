# AA259

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following diagram shows triangle ABC.

![95a5a986a0e57f71e696c7f03164eaaf921eab87.svg](images/95a5a986a0e57f71e696c7f03164eaaf921eab87.svg)

$\text{AC} = 14$ cm,
$\text{C\^{B}A} = \ang{115}$, $\text{B\^{A}C} = \ang{38}$.

1.  Find BC. [3 marks]

2.  Find the area of the triangle ABC. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the sine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\text{AC}}{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A})} &= \dfrac{\text{BC}}{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C})} \hspace{2.65em} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{14}{\sin\ang{115}} &= \dfrac{\text{BC}}{\sin \ang{38}} \\[9pt]
    \text{BC} &= \dfrac{14\sin \ang{38}}{\sin\ang{115}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{BC} \approx 9.51 \text{ cm} \hspace{0.7em}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.6em} \text{A\^{C}B} &= \ang{180} - (\text{C\^{B}A} + \text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{180} - (\ang{115} + \ang{38}) \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{27}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the area of a triangle
    formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.8em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AC})(\text{BC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{C}B}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(14)(9.51)\sin \ang{27} \\[12pt]
    &= 30.2 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
