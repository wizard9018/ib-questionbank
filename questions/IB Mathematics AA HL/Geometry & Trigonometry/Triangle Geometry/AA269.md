# AA269

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows triangle ABC.

![75c0aac3e03cc79c60f3a1a4e03229068b9a63fc.svg](images/75c0aac3e03cc79c60f3a1a4e03229068b9a63fc.svg)

$\text{AB} = 10$ cm,
$\text{AC} = 8$ cm, $\text{C\^{B}A} = \ang{52}$.

1.  Find AĈB, given that it is acute. [3 marks]

2.  Find BC. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the sine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\text{AB}}{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{C}B})}  &= \dfrac{\text{AC}}{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A})} \hspace{2.4em} \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{10}{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{C}B})} &= \dfrac{8}{\sin \ang{52}} \\[8pt]
    \sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{C}B}) &= \dfrac{10\sin \ang{52}}{8} \\[4pt]
    \text{A\^{C}B} &= 80.0681...
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{10.2em} \text{A\^{C}B}  = \ang{80.1}\hspace{0.25em} \text{(3 s.f.)} \hspace{2em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.9em} \text{B\^{A}C} &= \ang{180} - (\text{C\^{B}A} + \text{A\^{C}B}) \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{180} - (\ang{52} + \ang{80.1}) \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{47.9}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the cosine rule, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.3em} \text{BC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{AC}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{AC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6pt]
    \text{BC} &= \sqrt{10^2 + 8^2 - 2(10)(8)\cos \ang{47.9}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{BC} \approx 7.53 \text{ cm} \hspace{.5em} [\text{accept BC} =7.54 \text{ cm} ]
    $$
