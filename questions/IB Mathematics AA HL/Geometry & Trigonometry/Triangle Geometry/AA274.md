# AA274

**Maximum mark: 8**

The following diagram shows triangle ABC.

![93335bfd8c3cbe4d6c4bbc0674016f78c5337789.svg](images/93335bfd8c3cbe4d6c4bbc0674016f78c5337789.svg)

$\text{AC} = 10$ cm,
$\text{B\^{A}C} = \ang{35}$.

The area of the triangle ABC is $22$ cm$^2$.

1.  Find AB. [3 marks]

2.  Find BC. [3 marks]

3.  Find CB̂A. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.4em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(\text{AC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[7pt]
    22 &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(10)\sin \ang{35} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{22}{5\sin \ang{35}} &= \text{AB} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{7.9em} \text{AB} \approx 7.67 \text{ cm} \hspace{3em} [\approx 7.6712 \text{ cm}]
    $$

2.  Using the cosine rule, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \text{BC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{AC}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{AC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6pt]
    \text{BC} &= \sqrt{(7.6712)^2 + 10^2 - 2(7.6712)(10)\cos \ang{35}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{BC} \approx 5.76 \text{ cm} \hspace{3em} [\approx 5.7593 \text{ cm}] \hspace{2.95em}
    $$

3.  Again using the cosine rule, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) &= \dfrac{\text{AB}^2+\text{BC}^2-\text{AC}^2}{2(\text{AB})(\text{BC})}\\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{7.67^2 + 5.76^2 - 10^2}{2(7.67)(5.76)} \hspace{3em} \\[10pt]
    &\approx -0.09047 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{3.6em} \text{C\^{B}A} \approx \ang{95.2} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
