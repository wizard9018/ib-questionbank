# AA1333

**Maximum mark: 5**

The diagram shows a right-pyramid with vertex $A$ and triangular base $BCD$. 

![AA1333q](images/AA1333q.JPEG)

Point $B$ has coordinates $(2,-1,4)$ and point $D$ has coordinates $(6,0,1)$.

1.    Find $BD$. [2 marks]

The volume of pyramid $ABCD$ is $39.2$ units$^3$.

2.    Given that triangle $BCD$ is an equilateral triangle, find the height of the pyramid $AX$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. We are given coordinates of $B$ and $D$. Let's use the distance between two points formula to find the length of edge [BD]. 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    d&=\sqrt{(x_1-x_2)^2+(y_1-y_2)^2+(z_1-z_2)^2}\\[6pt]

    BD &=\sqrt{(2-6)^2+(-1-0)^2+(4-1)^2}\tag*{\textbf{{(M1)}}}\\[6pt]

    &=5.099...\\[6pt]

    &=5.10\text{ units}\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The formula for the volume of a right pyramid is

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    V&=\frac{1}{3}Ah

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    where $A=$ area of the base and $h=$ height of the pyramid.

    We are given volume and we need to find height. This tells us that we first need to find a value for $A$, the area of the base.

    We know the base is equilateral so all sides are the same length and we found the length of $BD$ in part (a).

    We also know that in an equilateral triangle, all angle measures are also the same $(\frac{180^\circ}{3}=60^\circ)$.

    ![AA1333a](images/AA1333a.JPEG)

    Hence we can use the area of a triangle formula to calculate the area.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    A&=\frac{1}{2}ab\sin C\tag*{\textbf{{(M1)}}}\\[6pt]

    &=\frac{1}{2}(5.099...)^2\sin 60^\circ\\[6pt]

    &=11.25...\tag*{\textbf{{(A1)}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Substituting $V=39.2$ and $A=11.25...$ into the volume formula we can solve for $h$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    39.2&=\frac{1}{3}(11.25...)h\\[6pt]

    h&=10.44...\\[6pt]

    &=10.4\text{ units}\tag*{\textbf{{A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
