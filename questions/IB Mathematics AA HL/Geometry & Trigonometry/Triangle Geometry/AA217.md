# AA217

**Maximum mark: 5**

ABCD is a quadrilateral where
$\text{AB} = 9$ cm,
$\text{BC} = 12$ cm,
$\text{CD} = 11$ cm, $\text{\(\text{DA} = 8.5\)\hspace{0.25em}cm}$
and $\text{A\^{B}C} = \ang{90}$. Find $\text{A\^{D}C}$, giving your
answer correct to **the nearest degree**.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

If we sketch quadrilateral ABCD, we have

![7201405f218b4c00163aa096cd77a2c58d3f16b6.svg](images/7201405f218b4c00163aa096cd77a2c58d3f16b6.svg)

Using the Pythagorean theorem, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1em} \text{AC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{BC}^2 \\[6pt]
\text{AC} &= \sqrt{9^2 + 12^2} \\[8pt]
\text{AC} &= 15 \text{ cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the cosine rule,
we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2.8em} \text{AC}^2 &= \text{AD}^2 + \text{DC}^2 - 2(\text{AD})(\text{DC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{D}C})  \\[6pt]
15^2 &= 8.5^2 + 11^2 - 2(8.5)(11)\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{D}C}) \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\text{A\^DC} \approx \ang{100} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{1em}
$$
