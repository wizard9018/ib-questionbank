# AA245

**Maximum mark: 4**

In a triangle ABC, $\text{AB} = 4$ cm,
$\text{BC} = 10$ cm and
$\text{C\^{B}A} = \ang{105}$.

1.  Find the area of the triangle ABC. [2 marks]

2.  Find AC. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(\text{BC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(4)(10) \sin \ang{105} \\[11pt]
    &\approx 19.3 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the cosine rule, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \text{AC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{BC}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{BC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[6pt]
    \text{AC} &= \sqrt{4^2 + 10^2 - 2(4)(10)\cos \ang{105}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{AC} \approx 11.7 \text{ cm} \hspace{9.8em}
    $$
