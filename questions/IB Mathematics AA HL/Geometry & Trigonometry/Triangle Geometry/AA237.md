# AA237

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider a triangle with sides
$\text{AB} = 10$ mm and
$\text{AC} = 8$ mm. Given that the area of the
triangle is $24$ mm$^2$, find the possible
values for the length of \[BC\].

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the cosine rule, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{6em} \text{BC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{AC}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{AC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6pt]
\text{BC} &= \sqrt{10^2 + 8^2 - 2(10)(8)\cos\hspace{0.05em}\smash{\raisebox{-0.05em}{$(\text{B\^{A}C})$}}} \hspace{8.5em}{(1)}
\end{aligned}
$$

Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{6.55em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(\text{AC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6pt]
24 &= \dfrac{1}{2}(10)(8) \sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[9pt]
\text{B\^{A}C} &\approx \ang{36.870},\hspace{0.25em} \ang{143.13} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{3em}{(2)}
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, substituting $(2)$ in the equation $(1)$, we obtain

$$
\text{BC} \approx 6.00 \text{ mm}, \hspace{0.25em} 17.1 \text{ mm} \hspace{1em}
$$
