# AA221

**Maximum mark: 8**

In a triangle ABC, $\text{AB} = 3$ cm,
$\text{BC} = 5$ cm and
$\text{A\^{C}B} = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$.

1.  Find, to three significant figures, the two possible lengths of
    \[AC\]. [5 marks]

2.  Find the difference between the areas of the two possible triangles
    ABC.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.7em} \text{AB}^2 &= \text{AC}^2 + \text{BC}^2 - 2(\text{AC})(\text{BC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{C}B}) \\[7pt]
    3^2 &= \text{AC}^2 + 5^2 - 2(\text{AC})(5)\cos\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\Big) \\[6pt]
    9 &= \text{AC}^2 + 25 - 5\sqrt{3}\hspace{0.05em}(\text{AC}) \\[10pt]
    0 &=  \text{AC}^2 - 5\sqrt{3}\hspace{0.05em}(\text{AC}) + 16 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{5.65em} \text{AC} \approx 2.67\text{ cm}, \hspace{0.25em} 5.99\text{ cm} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

2.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10.4em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AC})(\text{BC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{C}B}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AC})(5)\sin\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\Big) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{5}{4}(\text{AC}) \hspace{3em} [\text{where $\text{AC} \approx 2.67,\hspace{0.25em} 5.99$}] \\[10pt]
    &\approx 3.34 \text{ cm}^2, \hspace{0.5em} 7.49 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the difference b/n the areas of the two possible triangles ABC
    is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \triangle A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= 7.49 - 3.34 \hspace{2.1em} \\[6pt]
    &= 4.15 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
