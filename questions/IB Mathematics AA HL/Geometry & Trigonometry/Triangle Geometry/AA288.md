# AA288

**Maximum mark: 15**

The following diagram shows quadrilateral ABCD.

![36057acad3a985fe731526cd66cbe2dc1378d991.svg](images/36057acad3a985fe731526cd66cbe2dc1378d991.svg)

$\text{AB} = 12\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm},\hspace{0.25em} \text{AD} = 4\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm},\hspace{0.25em} \text{A\^{C}B} = \ang{85},\hspace{0.25em} \text{C\^{B}A} = \ang{38}$.

1.  Find AC. [3 marks]

2.  Find the area of triangle ABC. [4 marks]

The area of triangle ABC is three times bigger than the area of triangle
ACD.

3.  Find the acute angle CÂD. [5 marks]

4.  Find CD. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the sine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.2em} \dfrac{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A})}{\text{AC}} &= \dfrac{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{C}B})}{\text{AB}} \hspace{5.8em} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{\sin \ang{38}}{\text{AC}} &= \dfrac{\sin\ang{85}}{12} \\[8pt]
    \text{AC} &= \dfrac{12\sin\ang{38}}{\sin \ang{85}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.4em} \text{AC} \approx 7.42 \text{ cm}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.7em} \text{B\^{A}C} &= \ang{180} - (\text{A\^{C}B} + \text{C\^{B}A}) \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{180} - (\ang{85} + \ang{38}) \\[6pt]
    &= \ang{57}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the area of a triangle
    formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.9em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(\text{AC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(12)(7.42)\sin \ang{57} \\[10pt]
    &\approx 37.3 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= 3A_{\triangle\text{ACD}} \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(\text{AC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) &= 3\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AC})(\text{AD})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{A}D})\bigg] \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{1}{2}(12)(7.42)\sin \ang{57} &= 3\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}(7.42)(4)\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{A}D})\bigg] \\[12pt]
    \sin \ang{57} &= \sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{A}D}) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{C\^{A}D} = \ang{57} \hspace{2.6em}
    $$

4.  Using the cosine rule, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{CD}^2 &= \text{AC}^2 + \text{AD}^2 - 2(\text{AC})(\text{AD})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{A}D}) \\[6pt]
    \text{CD} &= \sqrt{7.42^2 + 4^2 - 2(7.42)(4)\cos \ang{57}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{CD} \approx 6.22 \text{ cm} \hspace{12em}
    $$
