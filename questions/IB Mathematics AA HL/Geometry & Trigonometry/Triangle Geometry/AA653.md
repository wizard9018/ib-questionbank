# AA653

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows triangle ABC, with
$\text{AB}=7\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}$,
$\text{AC}=5\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}$,

$\ang{0} < \text{B\^{A}C} < \ang{90}$ and
$\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C})\hspace{-0.05em} = \dfrac{4}{5}$.

![04f131b9a7ba33f696cab0509a4218f2b57360a1.svg](images/04f131b9a7ba33f696cab0509a4218f2b57360a1.svg)

1.  Find the area of triangle ABC. [2 marks]

2.  Find $\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C})$. [2 marks]

3.  Find BC. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(\text{AC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(7)(5)\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg[\dfrac{4}{5}\bigg] \\[10pt]
    &= 14 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the Pythagorean identity, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos^2(\text{B\^{A}C}) + \sin^2(\text{B\^{A}C}) &= 1 \\[10pt]
    \cos^2(\text{B\^{A}C}) + \bigg[\dfrac{4}{5}\bigg]^2 &= 1 \\[6pt]
    \cos^2(\text{B\^{A}C}) &= 1 - \dfrac{16}{25} \\[6pt]
    \cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) &= \pm \textstyle\sqrt{\dfrac{9}{25}} \hspace{6em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{9.8em} \cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) = \dfrac{3}{5} \hspace{3em} [\text{since B\^{A}C is acute}]
    $$

3.  Using the cosine rule, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5em} \text{BC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{AC}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{AC}) \cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6.5pt]
    \text{BC}^2 &= 7^2 + 5^2 - 2(7)(5)\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg[\dfrac{3}{5}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    \text{BC}^2 &= 49 + 25 - 42 \\[12pt]
    \text{BC} &= \sqrt{32} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{BC} = 4\sqrt{2} \text{ cm} \hspace{6.8em}
    $$
