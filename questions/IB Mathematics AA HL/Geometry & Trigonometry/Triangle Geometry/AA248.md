# AA248

**Maximum mark: 8**

1.  Given that $\theta = \ang{18}$ satisfies the equation
    $4\sin^2\theta + 2\sin \theta - 1 = 0$, find the value of
    $\sin \ang{18}$. [2 marks]

2.  Hence find the value of $\cos \ang{36}$. [3 marks]

The following diagram shows the triangle ABC where
$\text{AB} = 4$ cm,
$\text{BC} = 5$ cm and
$\text{C\^{B}A} = \ang{36}$.

![4bb160f97a0ceec9cc59915b7c9a8a2c358dd84c.svg](images/4bb160f97a0ceec9cc59915b7c9a8a2c358dd84c.svg)

3.  Find AC in the form $\sqrt{a + b\sqrt{5}}$ cm where
    $a,b \in \mathbb{Z}$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the quadratic formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10.6em} \sin \ang{18} &= \dfrac{-2+\sqrt{2^2-4(4)(-1)}}{2(4)} \hspace{2em} [\text{since $\sin \ang{18} > 0$}] \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{-2 + \sqrt{20}}{8} \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{-1+\sqrt{5}}{4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Hence, using the double angle identity for cosine, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8em} \cos \ang{36} &= \cos\hspace{0.05em}(2\hspace{0.05em}(\ang{18})) \\[19pt]
    &= 1 - 2\sin^2 \ang{18} \\[12pt]
    &= 1 - 2\bigg[\dfrac{-1+\sqrt{5}}{4}\bigg]^2 \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[6pt]
    &= 1 - 2\bigg[\dfrac{6-2\sqrt{5}}{16}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= 1 - \dfrac{3-\sqrt{5}}{4} \\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{1+\sqrt{5}}{4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the cosine rule, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \text{AC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{BC}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{BC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[8pt]
    \text{AC} &= \sqrt{4^2 + 5^2 - 2(4)(5)\cos \ang{36}} \\[8pt]
    \text{AC} &= \textstyle \sqrt{16 + 25 - 40[(1+\sqrt{5})/4]} \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (b)}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{AC} = \textstyle \sqrt{31 - 10\sqrt{5}} \hspace{11.25em}
    $$
