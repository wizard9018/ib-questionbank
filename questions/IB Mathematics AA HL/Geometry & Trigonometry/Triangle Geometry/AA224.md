# AA224

**Maximum mark: 8**

1.  Expand and simplify $(1 + \sqrt{3})^2$. [1 mark]

2.  By writing $\ang{75}$ as $\ang{30} + \ang{45}$, find the value of
    $\cos \ang{75}$. [3 marks]

The following diagram shows the triangle ABC where
$\text{BC} = \sqrt{6}$, $\text{CA} = 2$ and\
$\text{A\^{C}B} = \ang{75}$.

![eb9eb28a6c1d96f4f66bea5d31bd0d663c466f82.svg](images/eb9eb28a6c1d96f4f66bea5d31bd0d663c466f82.svg)

3.  Find AB in the form $a + \sqrt{b}$ where
    $a,b \in \mathbb{Z}$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \textstyle (1 + \sqrt{3})^2 &= \textstyle 1 + 2\sqrt{3} + (\sqrt{3})^2 \\[6pt]
    &= \textstyle 4 + 2\sqrt{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the compound angle identity, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.2em} \cos \ang{75} &= \cos (\ang{30} + \ang{45}) \\[17pt]
    &=  \cos \ang{30} \cos \ang{45} - \sin \ang{30}\sin \ang{45} \\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\times\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} - \dfrac{1}{2}\times\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the cosine rule, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6em} \text{AB}^2 &= \text{AC}^2 + \text{BC}^2 - 2(\text{AC})(\text{BC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{C}B}) \\[12pt]
    \text{AB}^2 &= \textstyle 2^2 + (\sqrt{6})^2 - 2(2)(\sqrt{6})\cos \ang{75} \\[6pt]
    \text{AB}^2 &= 4 + 6 - 4\sqrt{6}\bigg[\dfrac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4}\bigg] \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (b)}] \\[6pt]
    \text{AB}^2 &= \textstyle 10 - (6 - 2\sqrt{3}) \\[10pt]
    \text{AB} &= \textstyle \sqrt{4 + 2\sqrt{3}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{AB} = \textstyle 1 + \sqrt{3} \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \hspace{1em}
    $$
