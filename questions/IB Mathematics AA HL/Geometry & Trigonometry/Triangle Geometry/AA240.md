# AA240

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows triangle ABC. Point D lies on \[AC\] so that
\[DB\]\
bisects CB̂A. The area of the triangle ABC is
$3$ cm$^2$.

![9bed4f14a5fbef1e1ee63e96d70de815c633ae41.svg](images/9bed4f14a5fbef1e1ee63e96d70de815c633ae41.svg)

$\text{AB} = 2\sqrt{7}$ cm,
$\text{BC} = x$ cm, and
$\text{C\^{B}D} = \theta$, where $\sin \theta = \dfrac{3}{4}$

Find the value of $x$ in the form of $\dfrac{a}{b}$ where $a$ and $b$
are positive integers.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the Pythagorean identity, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta &= 1 \\[14pt]
(3/4)^2 + \cos^2 \theta &= 1 \\[10pt]
\cos^2 \theta &= 1 - \dfrac{9}{16} \hspace{2em} \\[7pt]
\cos^2 \theta &= \dfrac{7}{16} \\[4pt]
\cos \theta &= \dfrac{\sqrt{7}}{4}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the area
of a triangle formula, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1.85em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(\text{BC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[7pt]
3 &= \dfrac{1}{2}(2\sqrt{7})(x)\sin\hspace{0.05em}(2\theta) \\[10pt]
3 &= (\sqrt{7}x) (2\sin \theta \cos \theta) \\[8pt]
3 &= (2\sqrt{7}x) \bigg[\dfrac{3}{4}\bigg]\bigg[\dfrac{\sqrt{7}}{4}\bigg] \\[5pt]
3 &= \dfrac{21x}{8} \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
x = \dfrac{8}{7} \hspace{4.35em}
$$
