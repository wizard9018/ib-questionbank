# AA294

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following diagram shows a cell tower AB
$6.4$ m tall on the roof of a commercial
building. The angle of depression from A to a point C on the horizontal
ground is $\ang{49}$. The angle of elevation of the top of the building
from C is $\ang{45}$.

![AA294](images/AA294.PNG)

Find the height of the building.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have

![7a8a205b1328ef343ffbd24e4ecabed6176ebc59.svg](images/7a8a205b1328ef343ffbd24e4ecabed6176ebc59.svg)

where:

1\.
    $\text{AB} = 6.4$ m,
    $\text{BD} = \text{CD} = h$ m;

2\. $\text{C\^{A}D} = \ang{41}$,
    $\text{C\^{B}D} = \ang{45}$,
    $\text{D\^{C}A} = \ang{49}$.

Hence, using the sine rule, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{D\^{C}A})}{\text{AB} + \text{BD}} &= \dfrac{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{A}D})}{\text{CD}} \\[6pt]
\dfrac{\sin \ang{49}}{6.4+h} &= \dfrac{\sin \ang{41}}{h} \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hspace{13.05em} h \approx 42.6 \text{ m} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
$$
