# AA216

**Maximum mark: 5**

In a triangle ABC, $\text{AB} = 6$ cm,
$\text{BC} = 10$ cm and
$\text{C\^{B}A} = \ang{70}$.

1.  Find the area of the triangle. [2 marks]

2.  Find $\text{AC}$. [3 marks]

Give your answers correct to $3$ significant figures.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(\text{BC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(6)(10)\sin \ang{70} \\[10pt]
    &\approx 28.2 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the cosine rule, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \text{AC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{BC}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{BC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[6pt]
    \text{AC} &= \sqrt{6^2 + 10^2 - 2(6)(10)\cos \ang{70}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{AC} \approx 9.74 \text{ cm} \hspace{9.8em}
    $$
