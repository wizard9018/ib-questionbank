# AA215

**Maximum mark: 6**

Cabin boy Jim is at the top of a cliff on Tortuga Island, standing
$100$ m above the sea level, and observes two
ships in the Caribbean sea.

The Black Pearl (P) is at an angle of depression of $\ang{25}$ and the
Hispaniola (H) is at an angle of depression of $\ang{50}$.

The following three dimensional diagram shows the foot of the cliff at
O, Jim at J, two ships at P and H, and the angle
$\text{POH} = \ang{75}$.

![e810f0e7222fd0cb0521ed78d1f929940c404119.svg](images/e810f0e7222fd0cb0521ed78d1f929940c404119.svg)

Find the distance between the two ships, giving your answer correct to
$3$ significant figures.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the right triangle JOP, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1.6em} \tan\hspace{0.05em}(\text{O\^{P}J}) &= \dfrac{\text{JO}}{\text{OP}} \\[6pt]
\tan \ang{25} &= \dfrac{100}{\text{OP}} \\[6pt]
\text{OP} &= \dfrac{100}{\tan \ang{25}} \\[10pt]
\text{OP} &\approx 214.45 \text{ m}
\end{aligned}
$$
Using the right
triangle JOH, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1.6em} \tan\hspace{0.05em}(\text{O\^{H}J}) &= \dfrac{\text{JO}}{\text{OH}} \\[6pt]
\tan \ang{50} &= \dfrac{100}{\text{OH}} \\[6pt]
\text{OH} &= \dfrac{100}{\tan \ang{50}} \\[10pt]
\text{OH} &\approx 83.910 \text{ m}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the
cosine rule, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{4em} \text{PH}^2 &= \text{OP}^2 + \text{OH}^2 - 2(\text{OP})(\text{OH}) \cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{P\^{O}H}) \\[6pt]
\text{PH} &= \sqrt{(214.45)^2 + (83.910)^2 - 2(214.45)(83.910)\cos\ang{75}} \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\text{PH} \approx 209 \text{ m} \hspace{15em}
$$
