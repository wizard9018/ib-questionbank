# AA683

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows triangle PQR, with
$\text{PQ}=10\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}$,
$\text{PR}=6\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}$,

$\ang{0} < \text{Q\^{P}R} < \ang{90}$ and
$\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{Q\^{P}R})\hspace{-0.05em} = \dfrac{4}{5}$.

![785f0c71e31141f8770621b1b3ca397532c130be.svg](images/785f0c71e31141f8770621b1b3ca397532c130be.svg)

1.  Find $\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{Q\^{P}R})$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the area of triangle PQR. [2 marks]

3.  Find QR. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the Pythagorean identity, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos^2(\text{Q\^{P}R}) + \sin^2(\text{Q\^{P}R}) &= 1 \\[8pt]
    \bigg[\dfrac{4}{5}\bigg]^2 + \sin^2(\text{Q\^{P}R}) &= 1 \\[6pt]
    \sin^2(\text{Q\^{P}R}) &= 1 - \dfrac{16}{25} \\[6pt]
    \sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{Q\^{P}R}) &= \pm \textstyle\sqrt{\dfrac{9}{25}}\\[6pt]
    \therefore \hspace{1em} &\hspace{-4.7em}\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{Q\^{P}R}) = \dfrac{3}{5} \hspace{1.5em} [\text{since Q\^{P}R is acute}]\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle\text{PQR}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{PQ})(\text{PR})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{Q\^{P}R}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(10)(6)\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg[\dfrac{3}{5}\bigg] \\[10pt]
    &= 18 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Using the cosine rule, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5em} \text{QR}^2 &= \text{PQ}^2 + \text{PR}^2 - 2(\text{PQ})(\text{PR}) \cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{Q\^{P}R}) \\[6.5pt]
    \text{QR}^2 &= 10^2 + 6^2 - 2(10)(6)\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg[\dfrac{4}{5}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    \text{QR}^2 &= 100 + 36 - 96 \\[12pt]
    \text{QR} &= \sqrt{40} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{QR} = 2\sqrt{10} \text{ cm} \hspace{6.8em}
    $$
