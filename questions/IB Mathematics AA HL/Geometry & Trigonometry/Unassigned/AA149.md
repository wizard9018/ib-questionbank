# AA149

**Maximum mark: 8**

Consider the function $f(x) = x^2\arcsin(x)$, for $-1 \leq x \leq 1$.

1.  Sketch the graph of $y = f(x)$. [3 marks]

2.  Write down the range of $f$. [2 marks]

3.  Solve the inequality $\left|x^2\arcsin(x)\right| > 0.5$. [3 marks]

    $\left[-\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$

3.  If we solve the equation $|x^2\arcsin(x)| = 0.5$ for $x$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{9.4em} x^2\arcsin(x) &= \pm 0.5 \\[6pt]
    x &= \pm 0.761 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}

    \begin{array}{rclcrcl}
    -\dfrac{\pi}{2} \leq x^2\arcsin(x) &<& -0.5 \hspace{0.5em}\,&\text{ or }\hspace{0.5em}\,& 0.5 &<& x^2\arcsin(x) \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2} \\[9pt]
    -1 \leq x &<& -0.761 \hspace{0.5em}\,&\text{ or }\hspace{0.5em}\,& 0.761 &<& x \leq 1 \\
    \end{array} \hspace{0.9em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the set of solutions
    to the inequality $|x^2\arcsin(x)| > 0.5$ is

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     [-1,-0.761)\cup(0.761,1]
     \end{aligned}
     $$
