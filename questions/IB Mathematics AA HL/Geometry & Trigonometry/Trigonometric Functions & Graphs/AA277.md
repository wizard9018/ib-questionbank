# AA277

**Maximum mark: 7**

The Singapore Flyer is a giant observation wheel in Singapore with
diameter of $150$ metres. The wheel rotates at a
constant speed and completes one rotation in $32$
minutes. The bottom of the wheel is $d$ metres above the ground.

![cd3a2b0940f065b11cf681392f0c1e722b522460.svg](images/cd3a2b0940f065b11cf681392f0c1e722b522460.svg)

A seat starts at the bottom of the wheel.

1.  After $16$ minutes, the seat is $165$ metres above the ground. Find
    $d$. [2 marks]

After $t$ minutes, the height of the seat above the ground is given by

$$
h(t) = 90 + a\cos\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{16}\hspace{0.05em}t\Big), \hspace{0.5em}\text{for}\hspace{0.65em} 0\leq t \leq 64.
$$

2.  Find the value of $a$. [2 marks]

3.  Find when the seat is $60$ metres above the ground for the
    third time. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The wheel completes $1/2$ a rotation in $16$ minutes.
    Hence we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    150+d &= 165 \hspace{2em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{1.35em} d = 15 \text{ m}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h(0) &= 15 \hspace{2.1em} \\[6pt]
    90 + a &= 15 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.9em} a = -75
    $$

3.  If we draw the graph of $y = h(t)$ for $0 \leq t \leq 64$
    \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\], we have

    ![fb7adc5561dbacfc841595d13e8910816224a82b.svg](images/fb7adc5561dbacfc841595d13e8910816224a82b.svg)

    Hence the seat is $60$ m above the ground
    for the $3$rd time when
     $t = 37.9 \text{ min}$
