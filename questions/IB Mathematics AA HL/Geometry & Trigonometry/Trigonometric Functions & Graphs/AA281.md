# AA281

**Maximum mark: 9**

Let $f(x) = a\sin\hspace{0.05em}(k(x-d)) + c$, for $1 \leq x \leq 7$.

The graph of $f$ is shown on the diagram below.

![281a6e2c15162a8657e750c0b5180c5816112269.svg](images/281a6e2c15162a8657e750c0b5180c5816112269.svg)

The graph of $f$ has a maximum at A$(2,5)$ and a minimum at B$(4,1)$.

1.  Find the value of:
    1.  $a$;

    2.  $c$. [3 marks]

2.
    1.  Find the value of $k$.

    2.  Find the smallest possible value of $d$, given $d > 0$.
        [4 marks]

The graph of $f$ is translated vertically by $b$ units to give the graph of a function $g$ such that $g(x) = 3$ has only one solution in the given domain.

3.  Find the value of $b$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        a &= \dfrac{f_{\text{max}} - f_{\text{min}}}{2} \hspace{0.1em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{5-1}{2} \\[12pt]
        &= 2
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        c &= \dfrac{f_{\text{max}} + f_{\text{min}}}{2} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{5+1}{2} \\[12pt]
        &= 3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    1.  The period of $f$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{period} &= 2(4-2) \hspace{4.2em} \\[6pt]
        &= 4
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        k &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\text{period}} \hspace{2.4em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{2\pi}{4} \\[7pt]
        &= \dfrac{\pi}{2}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the coordinates of A, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        f(2) &= 5 \\[10pt]
        \hspace{1em} 2\sin\hspace{0.05em}((\pi\hspace{-0.05em}/2)(2-d)) + 3 &= 5 \\[10pt]
        \sin\hspace{0.05em}((\pi\hspace{-0.05em}/2)(2-d)) &= 1 \\[6pt]
        \dfrac{\pi}{2}(2-d) &= \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi \hspace{2.05em} [\sin^{-1} (1) = \pi\hspace{-0.05em}/2] \\[6pt]
        2 - d &= 1 + 4n \\[10pt]
        d &= 1 - 4n \hspace{3em} [\text{for $n \in \mathbb{Z}$}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence the smallest possible (positive) value of $d$ is

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1em} d &= 1-4(0) \\[8pt]
        &= 1
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  If we sketch the graphs of $f$ and $g$ on the same set of axes, we
    have

    ![731a1c7f3f185db0dcd4bfe3c99dc9bd68b0d00d.svg](images/731a1c7f3f185db0dcd4bfe3c99dc9bd68b0d00d.svg)

    Hence we find
     $b = 2$
