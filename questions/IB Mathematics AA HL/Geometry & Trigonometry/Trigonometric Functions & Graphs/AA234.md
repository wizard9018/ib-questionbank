# AA234

**Maximum mark: 10**

Let $g(x) = -4x - \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ and $h(x) = 5\cos x - 1$, for
$x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  Show that
    $(h\circ g)(x) = 5\cos\hspace{-0.1em}\left(-4x - \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) - 1$.
     [1 mark]

2.  Find the range of $h\circ g$.  [2 marks]

3.  Given that $(h\circ g)\Big(\dfrac{5\pi}{12}\Big) = 4$, find the next
    value of $x$, greater than $\dfrac{5\pi}{12}$,

    for which $(h\circ g)(x)=4$. [2 marks]

4.  The graph of $y = (h\hspace{0.075em}\circ\hspace{0.075em}g)(x)$ can
    be obtained by applying five $\text{transformations}$ to the
    graph of $y = \cos x$. State what the five transformations represent
    $\text{geometrically}$ and give the order in which they are
    applied. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (h\circ g)(x) &= h(g(x)) \\[8pt]
    &= h\hspace{-0.1em}\left(-4x - \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) \\[5pt]
    &= 5\cos\hspace{-0.1em}\left(-4x - \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) - 1
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.25em} -1 &\leq \cos\hspace{-0.1em}\left(-4x - \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) \leq 1 \\[3pt]
    -5 &\leq 5\cos\hspace{-0.1em}\left(-4x - \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) \leq 5 \\[3pt]
    -6 &\leq 5\cos\hspace{-0.1em}\left(-4x - \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)-1 \leq 4 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the range of $h\circ g$ is
     $[-6,4]$

3.  The period of the function $h\circ g$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathrm{T} &= \dfrac{2\pi}{|-4|} \hspace{1.05em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\pi}{2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the next value of $x$ is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x &= \dfrac{5\pi}{12} + \dfrac{\pi}{2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{11\pi}{12}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{transformation $1$}:& \hspace{0.75em}\hspace{0.525em} \text{compression parallel to the $x$-axis by a factor of $1/4$} \hspace{1.25em} \\[6pt]
    \text{transformation $2$}:& \hspace{0.75em}\hspace{0.525em} \text{reflection in the $y$-axis} \\[6pt]
    \text{transformation $3$}:& \hspace{0.75em}\hspace{0.525em} \text{stretch parallel to the $y$-axis by a factor of $5$} \\[6pt]
    \text{transformation $4$}:& \hspace{0.75em}\hspace{0.525em} \text{horizontal translation $\pi/12$ units to the left} \\[6pt]
    \text{transformation $5$}:& \hspace{0.75em}\hspace{0.525em} \text{vertical translation $1$ unit down}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
