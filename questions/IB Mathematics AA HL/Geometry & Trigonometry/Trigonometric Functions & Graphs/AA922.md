# AA922

**Maximum mark: 7**

Consider the functions $f(x)=3\cos(x)+\dfrac{9}{2}$ and $g(x)=3\cos\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)+A$, where $x\in \mathbb{R}$ and $A< \dfrac{9}{2}$.

1. Describe a sequence of two transformations that transforms the graph of $f$ to the graph of $g$. [3 marks]

The $y$-intercept of the graph $g$ is at the point $\left(0\,,\dfrac{9}{2}\right)$

2. Find the range of $g$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Applying a translation of $a$ units in the direction of the negative $x$-axis to a function $f$ is shown as $f(x+a)$, and a translation of $b$ units in the direction of the negative $y$-axis is shown as $f(x)-b$. 

    Therefore a translation of $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ units in the direction of the negative $x$-axis would be $f\bigg(x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\bigg)$. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    f\bigg(x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\bigg) &= 3\cos\bigg(x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\bigg)+\dfrac{9}{2}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Given that $A < \dfrac{9}{2}$, if we then apply a translation of $\dfrac{9}{2} - A$ units in the direction of the negative $y$-axis, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    f\bigg(x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\bigg) - \bigg(\dfrac{9}{2} - A\bigg) &= 3\cos\bigg(x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\bigg)+\dfrac{9}{2} - \bigg(\dfrac{9}{2} - A\bigg) \\[10pt]

    &= 3\cos\bigg(x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\bigg) + A \\[10pt]

    &= g(x)

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore we have the following transformations in any order

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    T_1 &: \hspace{0.5em}\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{\text{translation of $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ units in the direction of the negative $x$-axis}} \\[10pt]

    T_2 &: \hspace{0.5em}\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{\text{translation of $\dfrac{9}{2} - A$ units in the direction of the negative $y$-axis}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. We have $g(0)=\dfrac{9}{2}$. Solving for $A$, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    3\cos\left(0+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)+A &= \dfrac{9}{2} \\[10pt]

    3\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right) + A & = \dfrac{9}{2} \\[10pt]

    A & = 3 \\[-12pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore $g(x)=3\cos\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)+3$ , and the following inequalities hold:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    -1\leq \cos\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) &\leq 1 \\[10pt]

    -3\leq 3\cos\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right) &\leq 3 \\[10pt]

    0\leq 3\cos\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)+3 &\leq 6 \\[10pt]

    0\leq g(x) &\leq 6 \\[-12pt]

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore the range of $g$ is $0\leq g(x) \leq 6$
