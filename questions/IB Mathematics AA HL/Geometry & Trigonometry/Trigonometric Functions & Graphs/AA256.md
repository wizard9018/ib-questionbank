# AA256

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = a\cos bx$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$, where $b > 0$.

Part of the graph of $f$ is shown on the diagram below.

![efa784ef04c29864a818e047d0aa0869e63c18b0.svg](images/efa784ef04c29864a818e047d0aa0869e63c18b0.svg)

1.  Find the value of $a$. [2 marks]

2.  Find:
    1.  the period of $f$;

    2.  the value of $b$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since the graph of $f$ has a minimum point at $(0,-2)$, we know
    $a < 0$.

    Hence we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4em} a &= -\bigg[\dfrac{f_{\text{max}} - f_{\text{min}}}{2}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= -\bigg[\dfrac{2-(-2)}{2}\bigg] \\[12pt]
    &= -2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  The period of $f$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{period} &= \dfrac{3\pi}{2} - \dfrac{\pi}{2} \hspace{4.2em} \\[6pt]
        &= \pi
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Hence we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        b &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\text{period}} \hspace{2.3em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\pi} \\[10pt]
        &= 2
        \end{aligned}
        $$
