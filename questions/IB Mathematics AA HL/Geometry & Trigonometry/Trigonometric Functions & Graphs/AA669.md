# AA669

**Maximum mark: 14**

Let $f(x) = 3\cos\hspace{0.05em}(2x) + 5$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

The range of $f$ is $p \leq f(x) \leq q$.

1.  Find the value of $p$ and the value of $q$. [3 marks]

Let $g(x) = 4\hspace{0.05em}f(3x)$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

2.  Find the range of $g$. [3 marks]

The function $g$ can be written in the form
$g(x) = 12\cos\hspace{0.05em}(kx) + c$.

3.
    1.  Find the value of $k$ and the value of $c$.

    2.  Find the period of $g$. [5 marks]

The equation $g(x) = 10$ has two solutions for
$\dfrac{2\pi}{3} \leq x \leq \pi$.

4.  Find both solutions. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The min and max values of cos($2x$) are $-1$ and $+1$. Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.7em} p &= f_\text{min}\\[6pt]
    &= 3(-1)+5 \\[6pt]
    &= 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.5em} q &= f_\text{max} \\[6pt]
    &= 3(+1)+5 \\[6pt]
    &= 8
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    g_\text{min} &= 4\hspace{0.05em}f_\text{min} = 4\hspace{0.05em}(2) = 8\\[10pt]
    g_\text{max} &= 4\hspace{0.05em}f_\text{max} = 4\hspace{0.05em}(8) = 32\\[10pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the range of $g$ is
     $8 \leq g(x) \leq 32$

3.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2.5em} g(x) &= 4\hspace{0.05em}f(3x) \\[8pt]
        &= 4\hspace{0.05em}[3\cos\hspace{0.05em}(2(3x)) + 5] \\[8pt]
        &= 12\cos\hspace{0.05em}(6x) + 20
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence we obtain
         $k = 6$
        and  $c = 20$

    2.  The period of $g$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{period} &= \dfrac{2\pi}{k} = \dfrac{2\pi}{6} \hspace{0.975em}= \dfrac{\pi}{3} \hspace{5em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

4.  Solving the equation $g(x) = 10$ for
    $\dfrac{2\pi}{3} \leq x \leq \pi$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    12\cos\hspace{0.05em}(6x) + 20 &= 10 \hspace{8.9em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{13em} x \approx 2.52,\hspace{0.25em}2.72 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
