# AA809

**Maximum mark: 14**

The following diagram shows a ball attached to the end of a spring,
which is suspended from a ceiling.

![e7dfdc0e552808c20b66447ca8a440bb4c1183bf.svg](images/e7dfdc0e552808c20b66447ca8a440bb4c1183bf.svg)

The height, $h$ metres of the ball above the ground at time $t$ seconds
after being released can be modelled by the function
$h(t)=0.5\cos(\pi t)+2.2$, where $t\geq0$.

1.  Find the height of the ball above the ground when it is released.
    [2 marks]

2.  Find the minimum height of the ball above the ground.[2 marks]

3.  Show that the ball takes $2$ seconds to return to its initial height
    above the ground for the first time. [2 marks]

4.  For the first $2$ seconds of its motion, determine the amount of the
    time that the ball is less than $2.2+0.25\sqrt{2}$ meters above the
    ground. [5 marks]

5.  Find the rate of change of the ball's height above the ground when
    $t=\dfrac{1}{3}$.\
    Give your answer in the form $p\pi\sqrt{q} \text{ ms}^{-1}$, where
    $p\in \mathbb{Q}$ and $q \in \mathbb{Z}^{+}$[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the fact that the ball is released at $t=0$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h(0)&=0.5\cos(\pi\cdot 0)+2.2 \\[12pt]
    &=0.5(1)+2.2\\[12pt]
    &=2.7\hspace{0.25em}\text{m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The minimum height occurs when $\cos(\pi t)=-1$. We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h_{\text{min}}&=0.5(-1)+2.2 \hspace{1cm}\\[12pt]
    &=1.7\text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The ball returns to its initial height after a time period $T$. We
    have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{2\pi}{T}&=\pi \hspace{2cm}\\[12pt]
    T&=\dfrac{2\pi}{\pi}\\[12pt]
    &=2\hspace{0.25em}\text{s}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Calculating the time stamps at which the ball gains the height of
    $2.2+0.25\sqrt{2}$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0.5\cos(\pi t)+2.2&=2.2+0.25\sqrt{2}\hspace{2cm}\\[12pt]
    0.5\cos(\pi t)& = 0.25\sqrt{2} \\[11pt]
    \cos(\pi t)&=\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\\[11pt]
    \pi t &= \dfrac{\pi}{4},\dfrac{7\pi}{4}\\[12pt]
    t &= \dfrac{1}{4},\, \dfrac{7}{4} \hspace{4em} [\hspace{0.1em}0\leq t\leq 2\hspace{0.1em}]\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    The amount of the time that the ball is less than $2.2+0.25\sqrt{2}$
    meters above the ground is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    t&=\dfrac{7}{4}-\dfrac{1}{4}\hspace{0.5cm}\\[12pt]
    &=1.5\text{ s}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    %h'(t) &= -0.5\pi\sin(\pi t)\\[6pt]
    h'\left(\dfrac{1}{3} \right)&= -0.5\hspace{0.05em}\pi \sin\left( \dfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\\[12pt]
    &=-0.5\hspace{0.05em}\pi\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\\[12pt]
    &=-0.25\hspace{0.05em}\pi\sqrt{3} \text{ ms}^{-1}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
