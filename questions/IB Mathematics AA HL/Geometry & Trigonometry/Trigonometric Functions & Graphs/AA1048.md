# AA1048

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows part of the graph of $f(x)=\cos(bx)$ for $-p\leq x \leq 5p$ expressed in radians.

![question](images/question.png)

1. Find an expression for $p$ in terms of $b$. [2 marks]

2. On the same axes, sketch the graph of $-3\cos\left(\dfrac{bx}{2}\right)$ for $-2p\leq x \leq 10p$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using the period formula for a cosine function, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}\\[-12pt]
        b &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\text{period}} \tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[7pt]
        %b &= \dfrac{2\pi}{6p} \\[7pt]
        b &= \dfrac{2\pi}{4p} \\[7pt]
        %3pb &= \pi \\[7pt]
        & \hspace{-0.49em}p = \dfrac{\pi}{2b} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. The graph of $-3\cos\left(\dfrac{bx}{2}\right)$ is obtained from the graph of $f$ after the following transformations:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}\\[-14pt]
        \text{transformation 1\,:} & \text{ horizontal stretch by a factor of 2}\tag*{\textbf{A1}} \\[3pt]
        \text{transformation 2\,:} & \text{ reflection in the $x$-axis} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\[3pt]
        \text{transformation 3\,:} & \text{ vertical stretch by a factor of 3}\tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\[-12pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
