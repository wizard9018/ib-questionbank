# AA230

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows the curve
$y = a\cos\hspace{0.05em}(k(x - d)) + c$ where
$a, k, d$ and $c$ are all positive constants. The
curve has a minimum point at $(1.5,2)$ and
a $\text{maximum}$ point at $(3.5,7)$.

![3729611ba3a439e48776fd9a8da5e25aa14a08a8.svg](images/3729611ba3a439e48776fd9a8da5e25aa14a08a8.svg)

1.  Write down the value of $a$ and the value of $c$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $k$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the smallest possible value of $d$, given $d > 0$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4em} a &= \dfrac{y_{\text{max}} - y_{\text{min}}}{2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{7-2}{2} \\[10pt]
    &= 2.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.1em} c &= \dfrac{y_{\text{max}} + y_{\text{min}}}{2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{7+2}{2} \\[10pt]
    &= 4.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The period of the curve is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em} \text{period} &= 2(3.5 - 1.5) \\[6pt]
    &= 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.6em} k &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\text{period}} \\[5pt]
    &= \dfrac{2\pi}{4} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\pi}{2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Substituting $x = 3.5$ & $y = 7$ in the equation
    $y = a\cos\hspace{0.05em}(k(x-d))+c$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} y &= 2.5\cos\hspace{0.05em}((\pi/2)(x - d)) + 4.5 \\[12pt]
    7 &= 2.5\cos\hspace{0.05em}((\pi/2)(3.5 - d)) + 4.5 \hspace{0.5em} \\[12pt]
    \cos\hspace{0.05em}((\pi/2)(3.5 - d)) &= 1 \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{\pi}{2}(3.5 - d) &= 2n\pi \\[7pt]
    d &= 3.5 - 4n \hspace{3em} [\text{for $n \in \mathbb{Z}$}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the smallest possible (positive) value of $d$ is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} d &= 3.5 - 4(0) \\[6pt]
    &= 3.5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
