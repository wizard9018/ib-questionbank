# AA258

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $f(x) = a\cos bx + d$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$, where $b > 0$.

Part of the graph of $f$ is shown on the diagram below.

![bbd6f0daac341d4d5a3032afd8b59e2c8111e4ad.svg](images/bbd6f0daac341d4d5a3032afd8b59e2c8111e4ad.svg)

1.  Find the value of $a$. [2 marks]

2.
    1.  Write down the period of $f$.

    2.  Find the value of $b$. [3 marks]

3.  Find the value of $d$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.2em} a &= \dfrac{f_{\text{max}} - f_{\text{min}}}{2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{4-(-2)}{2} \\[12pt]
    &= 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.
        $\text{period} = \pi$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        b &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\text{period}} \hspace{2.3em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\pi} \\[10pt]
        &= 2
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.2em} d &= \dfrac{f_{\text{max}} + f_{\text{min}}}{2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{4+(-2)}{2} \\[12pt]
    &= 1
    \end{aligned}
    $$
