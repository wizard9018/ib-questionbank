# AA265

**Maximum mark: 5**

The height, $h$ metres, of a seat on a Ferris wheel after $t$ minutes is
given by
$$
\begin{aligned}
h(t) &= -23.5\cos\hspace{0.05em}(0.4t) + 25, \hspace{0.25em} \text{for} \hspace{0.4em} t \geq 0.
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Find the initial height of the seat. [2 marks]

Once a passenger's seat is more than $30$ m
above the ground, there are no trees in view and
they can take unobstructed photographs of a nearby city.

2.  Given that passengers only complete one rotation on the
    Ferris wheel, $\text{calculate}$ how
    long they can take unobstructed photographs of the
    nearby city. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $h(t)$ for $t = 0$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.2em} h(0) &= -23.5\cos\hspace{0.05em}(0) + 25 \\[6pt]
    &= -23.5(1) + 25 \\[6pt]
    &= 1.5 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  If we draw the graph of $y = h(t)$ for $0 \leq t \leq 5\pi$ \[by
    using G.D.C.\], we have

    ![7704e8c4b43167e1de2ba1b2edf0c396041c3afd.svg](images/7704e8c4b43167e1de2ba1b2edf0c396041c3afd.svg)

    Hence, using the $t$-coordinates of points A and B, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    t_{\text{unobstructed}} &= 11.2450-4.4630 \hspace{0.1em} \\[6pt]
    &= 6.7820 \\[6pt]
    &= 6.78 \text{ min}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
