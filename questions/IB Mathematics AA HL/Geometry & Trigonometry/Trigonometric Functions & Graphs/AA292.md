# AA292

**Maximum mark: 15**

Consider the function $f(x) = \sin x$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$, where $x$
is in radians.

1.  Write down:
    1.  the maximum value of $f$;

    2.  the smallest positive value of $x$ for which the maximum of $f$
        occurs. [3 marks]

Let $g(x) = 2\sin\hspace{-0.15em}\Big(x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\Big)$, for
$x \in \mathbb{R}$, where $x$ is in radians.

2.
    1.  Determine the two transformations the graph of $f$ undergoes to
        form the graph of $g$.

    2.  Hence find the maximum value of $g$ and the smallest positive
        value of $x$ for which this maximum occurs. [4 marks]

Let
$h(x) = \dfrac{4}{2\sin\hspace{-0.15em}\big(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\big)-3}$,
for $x \in \mathbb{R}$, where $x$ is in radians.

3.  Determine if the graph of $h$ has a vertical asymptote.
    Justify your answer. [4 marks]

4.  Find the range of $h$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $f_{\text{max}} = 1$

    2.
        $x = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$

2.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1em} g(x) &= 2\sin\hspace{-0.15em}\Big(x+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\Big) \\[6pt]
        &= 2f(x + \pi/4) \\[10pt]
        &= 2f(x - (-\pi/4))
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the two transformations
        are
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.75em} \text{transformation $1$}: &\hspace{0.75em}\hspace{0.525em}\text{vertical stretch by a factor of $2$} \\[8pt]
        \text{transformation $2$}: &\hspace{0.75em}\hspace{0.525em}\text{translation by the vector $\begin{pmatrix} -\pi\hspace{-0.05em}/4 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Hence the maximum value of $g$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        g_{\text{max}} &= 2f_{\text{max}} \hspace{1.85em} \\[6pt]
        &= 2(1) \\[6pt]
        &= 2
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        and this
        maximum occurs when
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        x &= \dfrac{\pi}{2} - \dfrac{\pi}{4} \\[8pt]
        &= \dfrac{\pi}{4}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \text{denominator of $h(x)$} &=  2\sin\hspace{-0.15em}\Big(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\Big)-3 \\[6pt]
    &\leq 2(1) - 3 \\[10pt]
    &\leq -1 \hspace{3.05em} [\text{for $x \in \mathbb{R}$}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{2em} \text{denominator of $h(x)$} \neq 0 \hspace{3.8em} [\text{for $x \in \mathbb{R}$}] \\[2pt]
    $$

    $\text{Hence the graph of \(h\) has no vertical asymptotes.}$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.8em} h_{\text{min}} &\leq h(x) \leq h_{\text{max}} \\[12pt]
    \dfrac{4}{2(1)-3} &\leq h(x) \leq \dfrac{4}{2(-1)-3} \\[6pt]
    -4 &\leq h(x) \leq -\dfrac{4}{5} \hspace{3em} [\text{for $x \in \mathbb{R}$}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the range of $h$ is
     $[-4,-4/5]$
