# AA270

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = 3\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\pi x)+1$.

1.  Write down the amplitude of $f$. [1 mark]

2.  Find the period of $f$. [2 marks]

3.  On the following grid, sketch the graph of $f$, for
    $0 \leq x \leq 4$. [3 marks]

    \begin{aligned}
    \text{period} &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\pi} \hspace{1.7em} \\[6pt]
    &= 2
