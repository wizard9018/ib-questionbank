# AA231

**Maximum mark: 6**

A function is defined by $f(x) = a\cos(kx) + c$, for
$-\pi \leq x \leq \pi$, where $k > 0$.

Part of the graph of $y = f(x)$ is shown on the diagram below.

![332ebe7e7e1639f2dc5953bac5a0772eedc61176.svg](images/332ebe7e7e1639f2dc5953bac5a0772eedc61176.svg)

1.  Find the value of:
    1.  $a$;

    2.  $k$;
    3.  $c$. [4 marks]

2.  Solve $f(x) = 2$, for $0 \leq x \leq \pi$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Since the curve has a minimum point at $(0,-1)$, we know
        $a < 0$.

        Hence we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3.6em} a &= -\bigg[\dfrac{f_{\text{max}} - f_{\text{min}}}{2}\bigg] \\[6pt]
        &= -\bigg[\dfrac{3-(-1)}{2}\bigg] \\[12pt]
        &= -2
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  The period of the curve is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{period} &= 2\Big[\dfrac{\pi}{3} - 0\Big] \hspace{1.8em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{2\pi}{3}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        k &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\text{period}} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{2\pi}{2\pi\hspace{-0.15em}/\hspace{-0.05em}3} \hspace{1.45em} \\[8pt]
        &= 3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    3.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.8em} c &= \dfrac{f_{\text{max}} + f_{\text{min}}}{2} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{3+(-1)}{2} \\[12pt]
        &= 1
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Solving the equation $f(x) = 2$ for $x \in [0,\pi]$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5em} -2\cos 3x + 1 &= 2 \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[9pt]
    \cos 3x &= -\dfrac{1}{2} \\[6pt]
    3x &= \dfrac{2\pi}{3} + 2n\pi,\hspace{0.4em}  \dfrac{4\pi}{3} + 2n\pi \\[6pt]
    x &= \dfrac{2\pi + 6n\pi}{9},\hspace{0.4em}  \dfrac{4\pi+6n\pi}{9} \hspace{2em} [\text{for $n \in \mathbb{Z}$}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{10.4em} x = \dfrac{2\pi}{9}, \hspace{0.25em} \dfrac{4\pi}{9},\hspace{0.25em} \dfrac{8\pi}{9} \hspace{3.4em} [\text{since $0 \leq x \leq \pi$}]
    $$
