# AA271

**Maximum mark: 8**

Let $f(x) = p\sin(qx) + r$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

Part of the graph of $f$ is shown on the diagram below.

![e58978d4cb9da418d75b3e727bb726aa22d90f25.svg](images/e58978d4cb9da418d75b3e727bb726aa22d90f25.svg)

The graph of $f$ has a minimum at A$(3,-1)$ and a maximum at B$(9,5)$.

1.
    1.  Find the period of $f$.

    2.  Hence find the value of $q$. [3 marks]

2.  Find the values of:
    1.  $p$;

    2.  $r$. [3 marks]

3.  Solve $f(x) = 4$, for $0 \leq x \leq 15$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  The period of $f$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{period} &= 2(9-3) \hspace{1.8em} \\[6pt]
        &= 12
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Hence we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        q &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\text{period}} \hspace{0.1em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{2\pi}{12} \\[7pt]
        &= \dfrac{\pi}{6}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    1.  Since the graph of $f$ has a minimum point at $(3,-1)$, we know
        $p < 0$.

        Hence we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{4.1em} p &= -\bigg[\dfrac{f_{\text{max}} - f_{\text{min}}}{2}\bigg] \\[6pt]
        &= -\bigg[\dfrac{5-(-1)}{2}\bigg] \\[12pt]
        &= -3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2.3em}  r &= \dfrac{f_{\text{max}} + f_{\text{min}}}{2} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{5+(-1)}{2} \\[10pt]
        &= 2
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  Solving the equation $f(x) = 4$ for $0 \leq x \leq 15$, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -3\sin\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{\pi x}{6}\Big) + 2 &= 4 \hspace{6.9em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{14em} x \approx 7.39, \hspace{0.25em}10.6 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
