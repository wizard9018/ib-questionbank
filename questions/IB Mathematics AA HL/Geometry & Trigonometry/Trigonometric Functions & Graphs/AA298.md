# AA298

**Maximum mark: 16**

The London Eye is an observation wheel in England with diameter of $120$
metres. The wheel rotates at a constant speed and completes $2.5$
rotations every hour. The bottom of the wheel is $15$ metres above the
ground.

![c6172973a9b2e90bb590b73a948d81d6dd74a43f.svg](images/c6172973a9b2e90bb590b73a948d81d6dd74a43f.svg)

A seat starts at the bottom of the wheel.

1.  Find the maximum height above the ground of the seat.[2 marks]

After $t$ minutes, the height $h$ metres above the ground of the seat is
given by

$$
h(t) = 75 + a\cos (b\hspace{0.01em}t), \hspace{0.25em} b > 0.
$$

2.
    1.  Show that the period of $h$ is $24$ minutes.

    2.  Write down the **exact** value of $b$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the value of $a$. [3 marks]

4.  Sketch the graph of $h$, for $0 \leq t \leq 48$.
    [4 marks]

5.  In one rotation of the wheel, find the probability that a
    randomly selected seat is at least $110$ metres above the ground.
    [5 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.2em} h_{\text{max}} &= h_{\text{min}} + 120 \\[6pt]
    &= 15 + 120 \\[6pt]
    &= 135 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.
    1.  The period of $h$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{period} &= \dfrac{60}{2.5} \hspace{4.8em} \\[6pt]
        &= 24 \text{ minutes}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        b &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\text{period}} \hspace{1em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{2\pi}{24} \\[9pt]
        &= \dfrac{\pi}{12}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h(0) &= 15 \hspace{6.6em} \\[6pt]
    75 + a\cos (0) &= 15 \\[6pt]
    75 + a(1) &= 15 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

     $$
     a = - 60 \hspace{0.8em}
     $$
