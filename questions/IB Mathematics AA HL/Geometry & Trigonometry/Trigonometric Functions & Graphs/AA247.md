# AA247

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows the curve
$y = a\sin\hspace{0.05em}(k(x - d)) + c$ where
$a, k, d$ and $c$ are all positive constants. The
curve has a minimum point at $(1.5,1)$ and
a $\text{maximum}$ point at $(3,7)$.

![7052ffd5de84afb1800aac020deb2608b5dacd1d.svg](images/7052ffd5de84afb1800aac020deb2608b5dacd1d.svg)

1.  Write down the value of $a$ and the value of $c$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $k$. [2 marks]

3.  Find the smallest possible value of $d$, given $d > 0$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4em} a &= \dfrac{y_{\text{max}} - y_{\text{min}}}{2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{7-1}{2} \\[10pt]
    &= 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.1em} c &= \dfrac{y_{\text{max}} + y_{\text{min}}}{2} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{7+1}{2} \\[10pt]
    &= 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The period of the curve is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.7em} \text{period} &= 2(3 - 1.5) \\[6pt]
    &= 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.6em} k &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\text{period}} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{2\pi}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Substituting $x = 3$ & $y = 7$ in the equation
    $y = a\sin\hspace{0.05em}(k(x-d))+c$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} y &= 3\sin\hspace{0.05em}((2\pi/3)(x - d)) + 4 \\[13pt]
    7 &= 3\sin\hspace{0.05em}((2\pi/3)(3 - d)) + 4 \hspace{0.5em} \\[13pt]
    \sin\hspace{0.05em}((2\pi/3)(3 - d)) &= 1 \\[9pt]
    \dfrac{2\pi}{3}(3 - d) &= \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi \\[5pt]
    3 - d &= \dfrac{3}{4} + 3n \\[10pt]
    d &= 2.25 - 3n \hspace{3em} [\text{for $n \in \mathbb{Z}$}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the smallest possible (positive) value of $d$ is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.6em} d &= 2.25 - 3(0) \\[6pt]
    &= 2.25
    \end{aligned}
    $$
