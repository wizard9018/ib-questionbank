# AA254

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = a\sin bx$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$. The following diagram
shows part of the graph of $f$.

![906d6d1d20c7e7bd0c948dab43e1239ca11f38db.svg](images/906d6d1d20c7e7bd0c948dab43e1239ca11f38db.svg)

1.
    1.  Write down the amplitude of $f$.

    2.  Find the value of $a$. [3 marks]

2.
    1.  Write down the period of $f$.

    2.  Find the value of $b$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $\text{amplitude} = 3$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        a &= \dfrac{f_{\text{max}} - f_{\text{min}}}{2} \hspace{0.2em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{3-(-3)}{2} \\[12pt]
        &= 3
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    1.
        $\text{period} = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        b &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\text{period}} \hspace{2.5em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\pi\hspace{-0.075em}/2} \\[9pt]
        &= 4
        \end{aligned}
        $$
