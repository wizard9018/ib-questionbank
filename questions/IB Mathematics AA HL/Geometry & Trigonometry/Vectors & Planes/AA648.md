# AA648

**Maximum mark: 5**

The plane $\mathit{\Pi}$ has the Cartesian equation $x - y + pz = 10$,
where $p \in \mathbb{R}$, $p<0$.

The line $L$ has the vector equation
$\mathbf{r} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 5 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.15em}t\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em}$,
$t \in \mathbb{R}$.

The acute angle between $\mathit{\Pi}$ and $L$ is $\ang{30}$. Find the
value of $p$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{12em} \mathbf{n} &= \textbf{i} - \textbf{j} + p\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{k} \hspace{2.8em} [\text{normal vector to $\mathit{\Pi}$}] \\[6pt]
\mathbf{d} &= \textbf{i} + \textbf{j} + \textbf{k} \hspace{2.9em} [\text{direction vector of $L$}]
\end{aligned}
$$

The acute angle between the normal vector $\mathbf{n}$ and the direction
vector $\mathbf{d}$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\theta &= \ang{90} - \ang{30} \hspace{0.1em} \\[6pt]
&= \ang{60}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the (acute) angle between two
vectors formula, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{10.6em} \cos \theta &= \dfrac{\mathbf{n}\hspace{-0.05em}\cdot\mathbf{d}}{|\mathbf{n}||\mathbf{d}|} \\[8pt]
\cos \ang{60} &= \dfrac{(\textbf{i} - \textbf{j} + p\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{k})\cdot(\textbf{i} + \textbf{j} + \textbf{k})}{|\textbf{i} - \textbf{j} + p\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{k}||\textbf{i} + \textbf{j} + \textbf{k}|} \\[8pt]
\dfrac{1}{2} &= \dfrac{(1)(1) + (-1)(1) + (p)(1)}{\big[\sqrt{1^2+(-1)^2+p^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]\big[\sqrt{1^2+1^2+1^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]} \\[8pt]
\dfrac{1}{2} &= \dfrac{p}{\big[\sqrt{2+p^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]\big[\sqrt{3}\hspace{0.1em}\big]} \\[8pt]
\textstyle \sqrt{6 + 3p^2} &= 2p \\[10pt]
6 + 3p^2 &= 4p^2 \\[6pt]
6 &= p^2 \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hspace{6.4em} p = - \sqrt{6} \hspace{2.8em} [\text{since $p<0$}]
$$
