# AA309

**Maximum mark: 6**

Find the Cartesian equation of the plane $\mathit{\Pi}$ containing the
points A$(3,-1,3)$ and B$(4,1,-1)$, and
perpendicular to the plane $2x - 5y + z = 10$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2em} \vec{\text{AB}} &= \vec{\text{OB}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[6pt]
&= (4\textbf{i} + \textbf{j} - \textbf{k}) - (3\textbf{i} - \textbf{j} + 3\textbf{k}) \\[6pt]
&= \textbf{i} + 2\textbf{j} - 4\textbf{k}
\end{aligned}
$$
A normal vector
to the plane $2x - 5y + z = 10$ is
$\textbf{n}_1 = 2\textbf{i} - 5\textbf{j} + \textbf{k}$.

Hence, using the vector product formula, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{6.5em} \mathbf{n}_2 &= \vec{\text{AB}} \times \mathbf{n}_1 \hspace{3em} [\text{normal vector to $\mathit{\Pi}$}] \\[8pt]
&= \left|\begin{matrix*}[r] \textbf{i} & \textbf{j} & \textbf{k} \\ \hspace{0.25em}1 & 2 & -4 \\ \hspace{0.25em}2 & -5 & 1 \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.25em}\right| \\[8pt]
&= [2-20]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{i} - [1-(-8)]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{j} + [-5-4]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{k}  \\[8pt]
&= -18\textbf{i} - 9\textbf{j} - 9\textbf{k}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using
the scalar product formula, we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2.2em} \mathbf{n}_2\cdot \vec{\text{OA}} &= (-18)(3) + (-9)(-1) + (-9)(3) \\[6pt]
&= -72
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the equation of a plane formula, we
find
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{n}_2\cdot\mathbf{r} &= \mathbf{n}_2\cdot \vec{\text{OA}} \\[6pt]
\hspace{0.5em} -18x- 9y - 9z &= -72 \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
2x + y + z = 8
$$
