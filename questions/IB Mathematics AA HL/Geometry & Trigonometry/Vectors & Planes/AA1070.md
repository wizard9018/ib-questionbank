# AA1070

**Maximum mark: 18**

Two remote controlled toy planes, taking part in an organised display, took off from a field some time ago.

The planes, $A$ and $B$, have position vectors with respect to an origin $O$ given respectively as

$$

\begin{aligned}

   \mathbf{r_A}&=\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 1 \\ 4 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix*} + t\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -3 \\ 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}\\[15pt]

   \mathbf{r_B}&=\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -3 \\ 2 \\ 0.5 \end{pmatrix*} + t\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ -1 \\ 1.5 \end{pmatrix*}

\end{aligned}

$$

Where $t$ is measured in seconds and $0\leq t\leq 2$.

Entries in each column vector give the displacement east of $O$, the displacement north of $O$ and the distance above the ground, all measured in metres.

1. At time $t=k$ seconds the toy planes are closest together.

    1. Find $k$.

    

    2. Hence, write down the distance between the planes at this time.[5 marks]

The directions of the two planes are known to be non-parallel.

2. 1. Show that the lines describing the flight paths of the planes are skew.

    

    2. Find the minimum distance between the flight paths of the planes. [12 marks]

    

Due to safety regulations, the distance between the toy planes at any time $t$ must always exceed $4$ metres.

3. Comment on your answers with regards to the safety regulations. [1 mark]
