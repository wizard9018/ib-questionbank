# AA351

**Maximum mark: 7**

The plane $\mathit{\Pi}$ has the Cartesian equation $x + y + 3z = 5$.
The line $L$ has the vector

equation
$\mathbf{r} = \hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 3 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.15em} + \hspace{0.1em}\lambda\hspace{-0.15em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -1 \\ 1 \\ p \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.25em},\hspace{0.25em} \lambda, p \in \mathbb{R}$.
The acute angle between the line $L$ and

the plane $\mathit{\Pi}$ is $\ang{60}$. Find the possible values of $p$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{11.1em} \mathbf{n} &= \phantom{-}\textbf{i} + \textbf{j} + 3\textbf{k} \hspace{3em} [\text{normal vector to $\mathit{\Pi}$}] \\[6pt]
\mathbf{d} &= -\textbf{i} + \textbf{j} + p\textbf{k} \hspace{3em} [\text{direction vector of $L$}]
\end{aligned}
$$

The acute angle between the normal vector $\mathbf{n}$ and the line $L$
is $\ang{90} - \ang{60} = \ang{30}$.

Hence, using the (acute) angle between two vectors formula, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{7.45em} \cos \ang{30} &= \dfrac{\mathbf{n}\hspace{-0.1em}\cdot\mathbf{d}}{|\mathbf{n}||\mathbf{d}|} \\[6pt]
\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} &= \dfrac{(1)(-1) + (1)(1) + (3)(p)}{\big[\sqrt{1^2+1^2+3^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]\big[\sqrt{(-1)^2+1^2+p^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]} \\[3pt]
\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} &= \dfrac{3p}{\big[\sqrt{11}\hspace{0.1em}\big]\big[\sqrt{p^2 + 2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]} \\[9pt]
\sqrt{33p^2+66} &= 6p \\[12pt]
33p^2+66 &= 36p^2 \\[12pt]
66 &= 3p^2 \\[12pt]
p^2 &= 22 \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
p = \pm \sqrt{22} \hspace{3.7em}
$$
