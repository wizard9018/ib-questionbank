# AA307

**Maximum mark: 6**

The points A and B are given by A$(-1,2,0)$ and B$(2,4,1)$.

1.  Find a vector equation of the line $\mathit{L}$ passing through the
    points A and B. [3 marks]

The plane $\mathit{\Pi}$ is defined by the equation $x + 2y - 2z = 18$.

2.  Find the coordinates of the point of intersection of the
    line $\mathit{L}$ with the plane $\mathit{\Pi}$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.4em} \vec{\text{AB}} &= \vec{\text{OB}} - \vec{\text{OA}} \\[9pt]
    &= \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 2 \\ 4 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*} - \begin{pmatrix*}[c] -1 \\ 2 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*} \\[6pt]
    &= \begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the vector equation of a line formula, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{r} &= \vec{\text{OA}} + t\hspace{0.05em}\vec{\text{AB}} \hspace{0.5em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{7.4em} \mathbf{r} = \hspace{-0.2em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] -1 \\ 2 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*}\hspace{-0.2em} + \hspace{0.1em} t\hspace{-0.1em}\begin{pmatrix*}[c] 3 \\ 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*} \hspace{3em} [t \in \mathbb{R}]
    $$

2.  Substituting $x = -1 + 3t$, $y = 2 + 2t$, $z = t$ in the equation of
    $\mathit{\Pi}$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x + 2y - 2z &= 18 \hspace{4.4em} \\[6pt]
    (-1+3t) + 2(2+2t) - 2t &= 18 \\[6pt]
    3 + 5t &= 18 \\[6pt]
    t &= 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, evaluating $x = -1 + 3t$, $y = 2 + 2t$,
    $z = t$ for $t = 3$, we obtain
    $$

    \begin{array}{rcrcl}
    x &=& -1+3(3) &=& 8 \\[7pt]
    y &=& 2 + 2(3) &=& 8 \\[7pt]
    && z &=& 3
    \end{array}
    $$
    Hence the coordinates of the point of intersection of
    $\mathit{L}$ and $\mathit{\Pi}$ are
     $(8, 8, 3)$
