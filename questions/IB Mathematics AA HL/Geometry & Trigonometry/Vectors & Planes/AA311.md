# AA311

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the following two planes:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathit{\Pi}_1 &:\hspace{0.5em} x - 5y + 3z = 10, \\[6pt]
\mathit{\Pi}_2 &:\hspace{0.5em} 2x + 6y - z = 12.
\end{aligned}
$$
Find
the acute angle between $\mathit{\Pi}_1$ and $\mathit{\Pi}_2$, giving
your answer correct to the nearest degree.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{4em} \mathbf{n}_1 &=  \textbf{i} - 5\textbf{j} + 3\textbf{k} \hspace{3em} [\text{normal vector to $\mathit{\Pi}_1$}] \\[6pt]
\mathbf{n}_2 &=  2\textbf{i} + 6\textbf{j} - \textbf{k} \hspace{3em} [\text{normal vector to $\mathit{\Pi}_2$}]
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, using the angle between two vectors formula, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{3.8em} \cos \theta &= \dfrac{\mathbf{n}_1\hspace{-0.1em}\cdot\mathbf{n}_2}{|\mathbf{n}_1||\mathbf{n}_2|} \\[6pt]
\cos \theta &= \dfrac{(1)(2) + (-5)(6) + (3)(-1)}{\big[\sqrt{1^2+(-5)^2+3^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]\big[\sqrt{2^2+6^2+(-1)^2}\hspace{0.1em}\big]} \\[10pt]
\theta &\approx \ang{145}  \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence the acute angle between $\mathit{\Pi}_1$ and $\mathit{\Pi}_2$ is
$\ang{180} - \ang{145} = \hspace{0.525em}\ang{35}$
