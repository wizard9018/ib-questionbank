# AA322

**Maximum mark: 7**

Consider the vectors given by
$\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{i} + 3\mathbf{j} - 4\mathbf{k}$ and
$\mathbf{v} = a\mathbf{i} + c\hspace{0.025em}\mathbf{k}$, where $a$ and
$c$ are constants.

It is given that
$\mathbf{u}\times\mathbf{v} = 3\mathbf{i} + b\mathbf{j} - 6\mathbf{k}$,
where $b$ is a constant.

1.  Find the value of each of the constants $a, b$ and $c$. [5 marks]

2.  Hence find the Cartesian equation of the plane containing the
    vectors $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ and passing through the point
    P$(3,1,0)$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the vector product formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} &= \left|\begin{matrix*}[c] \hspace{0.25em}\textbf{i} & \hspace{0.75em}\textbf{j} & \hspace{0.7em}\textbf{k} \\ \hspace{0.25em}1 & \hspace{0.75em}3 & -4 \\ \hspace{0.25em}a & \hspace{0.75em}0 & \hspace{0.7em}c \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.25em}\right| \\[6pt]
    \hspace{6em} 3\mathbf{i} + b\mathbf{j} - 6\mathbf{k} &= [3c-0]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{i} - [c-(-4a)]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{j} + [0-3a]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{k} \\[12pt]
    3\mathbf{i} + b\mathbf{j} - 6\mathbf{k} &= 3c\hspace{0.025em}\textbf{i} - (c+4a)\textbf{j} - 3a\textbf{k}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we deduce
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    3 &= 3c \\[6pt]
    b &= -(c+4a) \hspace{1.1em}  \\[6pt]
    -6 &= -3a  \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Solving the equations $(1)$
    and $(3)$ for $a$ and $c$, we get
     $a = 2$
    and  $c = 1$

    Hence, substituting $a = 2$ and $c = 1$ in the equation $(2)$, we
    find  $b = -9$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{11.2em} \mathbf{n} &= 3\textbf{i} - 9\textbf{j} - 6\textbf{k} \hspace{2.5em} [\text{normal vector to the plane}] \\[6pt]
    \mathbf{p} &= 3\textbf{i} + \textbf{j} \hspace{5.325em} [= \vec{\text{OP}}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the scalar product formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.55em} \mathbf{n}\cdot\mathbf{p} &= (3)(3) + (-9)(1) + (-6)(0) \\[6pt]
    &= 0
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the equation of a plane formula, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mathbf{n}\cdot\mathbf{r} &= \mathbf{n}\cdot\mathbf{p} \hspace{1.5em} \\[6pt]
    3x- 9y - 6z &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    x- 3y - 2z = 0 \hspace{2.5em}
    $$
