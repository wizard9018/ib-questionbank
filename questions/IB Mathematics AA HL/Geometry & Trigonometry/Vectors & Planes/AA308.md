# AA308

**Maximum mark: 5**

Consider the vectors
$\mathbf{u} = 3\textbf{i} - \textbf{j} - 4\textbf{k}$ and
$\mathbf{v} = \textbf{i} + 2\textbf{j}$.

1.  Find $\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}$. [2 marks]

2.  Hence find the Cartesian equation of the plane containing the
    vectors $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$, and passing through the point
    P$(1,-2,2)$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the vector product formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{6.3em} \mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} &= (3\textbf{i} - \textbf{j} - 4\textbf{k})\times(\textbf{i} + 2\textbf{j}) \\[6pt]
    &= \left|\begin{matrix*}[r] \textbf{i} & \textbf{j} & \textbf{k} \\ \hspace{0.25em}3 & -1 & -4 \\ \hspace{0.25em}1 & 2 & 0  \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.25em}\right| \\[8pt]
    &= [0-(-8)]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{i} - [0-(-4)]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{j} + [6-(-1)]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{k} \\[12pt]
    &= 8\textbf{i} - 4\textbf{j} + 7\textbf{k}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{11.2em} \mathbf{n} &= 8\textbf{i} - 4\textbf{j} + 7\textbf{k} \hspace{2.5em} [\text{normal vector to the plane}] \\[6pt]
    \mathbf{p} &= \phantom{8}\textbf{i} - 2\textbf{j} + 2\textbf{k} \hspace{2.5em} [= \vec{\text{OP}}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the scalar product formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.55em} \mathbf{n}\cdot\mathbf{p} &= (8)(1) + (-4)(-2) + (7)(2) \\[6pt]
    &= 30
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the equation of a plane formula,
    we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \mathbf{n}\cdot\mathbf{r} &= \mathbf{n}\cdot\mathbf{p} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    8x- 4y + 7z = 30 \hspace{2.7em}
    $$
