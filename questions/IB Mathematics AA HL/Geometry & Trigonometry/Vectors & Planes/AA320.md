# AA320

**Maximum mark: 5**

Consider the vectors
$\mathbf{a} = 2\textbf{i} + \textbf{j} - 4\textbf{k}$ and
$\mathbf{b} = \textbf{i} - 2\textbf{j} + 5\textbf{k}$.

1.  Find $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}$. [2 marks]

2.  Hence find the Cartesian equation of the plane containing the
    vectors $\textbf{a}$ and $\textbf{b}$ and passing through the point
    P$(2,-1,0)$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the vector product formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.4em} \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} &= (2\textbf{i} + \textbf{j} - 4\textbf{k})\times(\textbf{i} - 2\textbf{j} + 5\textbf{k}) \\[8pt]
    &= \left|\begin{matrix*}[r] \textbf{i} & \textbf{j} & \textbf{k} \\ \hspace{0.25em}2 & 1 & -4 \\ \hspace{0.25em}1 & -2 & 5 \end{matrix*}\hspace{0.25em}\right| \\[8pt]
    &= [5-8]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{i} - [10-(-4)]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{j} + [-4-1]\hspace{0.05em}\textbf{k}  \\[12pt]
    &= -3\textbf{i} - 14\textbf{j} -5\textbf{k}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10.25em} \mathbf{n} &= -3\textbf{i} - 14\textbf{j} - 5\textbf{k} \hspace{2.5em} [\text{normal vector to the plane}] \\[6pt]
    \mathbf{p} &= \phantom{-}2\textbf{i} - \textbf{j}\phantom{14 - 5\textbf{k}} \hspace{2.5em} [= \vec{\text{OP}}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the scalar product formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em} \mathbf{n}\cdot\mathbf{p} &= (-3)(2) + (-14)(-1) + (-5)(0) \\[6pt]
    &= 8
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the equation of a plane formula, we
    find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \mathbf{n}\cdot\mathbf{r} &= \mathbf{n}\cdot\mathbf{p} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    -3x- 14y - 5z = 8 \hspace{2.7em}
    $$
