# AA279

**Maximum mark: 8**

The following diagram shows a circle with centre O and radius
$r$ cm.

The circle is divided into three equal sectors.

![7f5a64927561552456b4327a3478b469df993141.svg](images/7f5a64927561552456b4327a3478b469df993141.svg)

Points A, B lie on the circle and $\text{A\^{O}B} = \theta$ radians.

1.  Find the exact value of $\theta$, giving your answer in terms of
    $\pi$. [2 marks]

The area of the shaded sector AOB is
$3\pi$ cm$^2$.

2.  Find the radius of the circle, $r$. [3 marks]

3.  Find the length of the chord AB. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \theta &= \dfrac{1}{3}(2\pi) \hspace{0.8em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{2\pi}{3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the area of a sector formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.7em} A_{\text{sector}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OB}^2\big](\text{A\^{O}B}) \\[6pt]
    3\pi &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[r^2\big]\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\frac{2\pi}{3}\Big) \\[10pt]
    9 &= r^2 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    r = 3 \text{ cm} \hspace{1.3em}
    $$

3.  Using the cosine rule, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} \text{AB}^2 &= \text{OA}^2 + \text{OB}^2 - 2(\text{OA})(\text{OB})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{O}B}) \\[6pt]
    \text{AB}^2 &= 3^2 + 3^2 - 2(3)(3) \cos\hspace{0.05em}(2\pi/3) \\[6pt]
    \text{AB}^2 &= 9 + 9 - 18(-1/2) \\[6pt]
    \text{AB}^2 &= 27 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{AB} = 3\sqrt{3} \text{ cm} \hspace{11em}
    $$
