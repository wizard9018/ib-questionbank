# AA296

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following diagram shows triangle ABC, with
$\text{AB} = 9\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}$,
$\text{BC} = 6\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}$, and
$\text{A\^{B}C} = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$.

![2bbd2aea52569ece11b343d5214200f61ca7f323.svg](images/2bbd2aea52569ece11b343d5214200f61ca7f323.svg)

1.  Show that $\text{AC} = 3\sqrt{7}\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}$.
    [4 marks]

2.  The shape in the following diagram is formed by adding a semicircle
    with diameter \[AC\] to the triangle.

    ![36f59d82426967dc80b1fcd4a0a947b096d711ec.svg](images/36f59d82426967dc80b1fcd4a0a947b096d711ec.svg)

    Find the exact perimeter of this shape. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.5em} \text{AC}^2 &= \text{AB}^2 + \text{BC}^2 - 2(\text{AB})(\text{BC})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A}) \\[6pt]
    \text{AC} &= \sqrt{9^2 + 6^2 - 2(9)(6)\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\pi\hspace{-0.05em}/3)} \\[6pt]
    \text{AC} &= \sqrt{81 + 36 - 108\hspace{0.05em}(1/2)} \\[6pt]
    \text{AC} &= \sqrt{63} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{AC} = 3\sqrt{7} \text{ cm} \hspace{10.3em}
    $$

2.  Using the circumference of a (semi) circle formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    P_{\text{shape}} &=  \text{AB} + \text{BC} + \pi\bigg[\dfrac{\text{AC}}{2}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= 9 + 6 + \pi\bigg[\dfrac{3\sqrt{7}}{2}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= 15 + \pi\bigg[\dfrac{3\sqrt{7}}{2}\bigg] \text{ cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
