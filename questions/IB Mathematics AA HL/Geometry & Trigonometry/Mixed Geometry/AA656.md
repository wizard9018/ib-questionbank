# AA656

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows a right-angled triangle ABC and a sector ABD
of a circle with centre A. The point E lies on [AC] such that [BE] is perpendicular to [AC]. The region $R$ is bounded by [DE], [BE] and arc BFD.

![587218ebb535066e62e4c39fa3e3aee886495568.svg](images/587218ebb535066e62e4c39fa3e3aee886495568.svg)

$\text{AB} = 5$ cm,
$\text{AC} = 13$ cm,
$\text{BC} = 12$ cm.

1.  Find BÂC, giving your answer in radians. [2 marks]

2.  Find the area of $R$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos \hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) &= \dfrac{5}{13} \hspace{4.1em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{13.25em} \text{B\^{A}C} \approx 1.18 \text{ radians} \hspace{3.5em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \cos \hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) &= \dfrac{\text{AE}}{5}\\[6pt]
    \text{AE} &= 5\cos \hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6pt]
    &= 5\hspace{-0.15em}\left[\dfrac{5}{13}\right] \\[6pt]
    &\approx 1.92 \text{ cm}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the area of a
    sector and the area of a triangle formulas, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.7em} A_{\text{shaded}} &= A_{\text{sector}} - A_{\triangle\text{ABE}} \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{AB}^2\big](\text{B\^{A}C}) - \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AB})(\text{AE})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{A}C}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[5^2\big](1.18) - \dfrac{1}{2}(5)(1.92)\sin\hspace{0.05em}(1.18) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 10.3 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
