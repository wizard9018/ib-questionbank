# AA252

**Maximum mark: 8**

Consider the following diagram.

![48262080f0126eb0c2b6a75a97f728bd1bfa9403.svg](images/48262080f0126eb0c2b6a75a97f728bd1bfa9403.svg)

The sides \[AB\] and \[BC\] of the isosceles triangle ABC have lengths
$5$ cm and the third
side \[AC\] has length $6$ cm. The midpoint of
\[AC\] is denoted by M. The circular arc AC has
centre, G, the midpoint of \[BM\].

1.
    1.  Find AG.

    2.  Find MĜA in radians. [5 marks]

2.  Find the area of the shaded region. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Using the right triangle ABM, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{8em} \mathrm{BM} &= \sqrt{\mathrm{AB}^2 - \mathrm{AM}^2} \\[6pt]
        &= \sqrt{5^2 - 3^2} \hspace{3em} [\text{since M is a midpoint}] \\[9pt]
        &= 4 \text{ cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence, using the right triangle
        AGM, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{8em} \mathrm{AG} &= \sqrt{\mathrm{GM}^2 + \mathrm{AM}^2} \\[6pt]
        &= \sqrt{2^2 + 3^2} \hspace{2em} [\text{since G is a midpoint}] \\[8pt]
        &= \sqrt{13} \text{ cm} \hspace{2.65em} [\approx 3.61 \text{ cm}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \tan\hspace{0.05em}(\mathrm{M\hat{G}A}) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{AM}}{\mathrm{GM}} \\[8pt]
        \tan\hspace{0.05em}(\mathrm{M\hat{G}A}) &= \dfrac{3}{2} \\[11pt]
        \mathrm{M\hat{G}A} &= \tan^{-1}(1.5) \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \hspace{3.5em} \mathrm{M\hat{G}A} \approx 0.983 \text{ radians}
        $$

2.  Since M is a midpoint of \[AC\] and $\mathrm{AB} = \mathrm{BC}$, we
    have $\mathrm{M\hat{G}A} = \mathrm{C\hspace{0.025em}\hat{G}M}$.

    Using the area of a sector and the area of a triangle formulas,
    we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4em} A_{\text{shaded}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\mathrm{AG}^2\big](\mathrm{C\hat{G}A}) - \dfrac{1}{2}(\mathrm{AC})(\mathrm{GM}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(13)(2\tan^{-1}(1.5)) - \dfrac{1}{2}(6)(2) \hspace{2em} [\text{since }\mathrm{C\hat{G}A} = 2\mathrm{M\hat{G}A}] \\[11pt]
    &\approx 6.78 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
