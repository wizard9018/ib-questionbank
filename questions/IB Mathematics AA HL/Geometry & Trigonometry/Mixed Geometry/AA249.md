# AA249

**Maximum mark: 6**

The diagram below shows a fenced triangular enclosure in the middle of a
grassy field where $\mathrm{AC} = 3$ m,
$\mathrm{DC} = 2$ m, $\text{C\^{B}A} = \alpha$
radians and $\text{A\^{C}B} = \frac{\pi}{3}$ radians.

One end of a rope is attached at point D on the outside edge of the
enclosure, and the other end is attached to a goat G.

Given that the rope is $6$ m long and the area of field outside the
enclosure that the goat is able to graze is
$74$ m$^2$, find the value of $\alpha$.

![8da8003bc3b284d5b66ba703992b3a876529d9a6.svg](images/8da8003bc3b284d5b66ba703992b3a876529d9a6.svg)

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

If we sketch the region outside the fenced triangular enclosure that the
goat is able to graze (three sectors, shaded in light blue), we have

![e56faebedfc9bba1814ecb333696d1a613652900.svg](images/e56faebedfc9bba1814ecb333696d1a613652900.svg)

where:

1\. $\mathrm{CE} = 4$ m and
    $\mathrm{AH} = 1$ m;

2\. $\text{E\^{C}F} = \dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ and
    $\text{F\^{A}H} = \dfrac{\pi}{3} + \alpha$.

Hence we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{\text{sector\hspace{0.2em}1}}+A_{\text{sector\hspace{0.2em}2}}+A_{\text{sector\hspace{0.2em}3}} &= 74 \hspace{3em} \\[6pt]
\dfrac{1}{2}\big[6^2\big]\big[\pi\big] + \dfrac{1}{2}\big[4^2\big]\bigg[\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\bigg] + \dfrac{1}{2}\big[1^2\big]\bigg[\dfrac{\pi}{3} + \alpha\bigg] &= 74 \\
%18\pi + \dfrac{16\pi}{3} + \dfrac{\pi}{6} + \dfrac{\alpha}{2} &= 74 \\[10pt]
%\dfrac{47\pi}{2} + \dfrac{\alpha}{2} &= 74 \\[9pt]
%\alpha &= 2\bigg[74 - \dfrac{47\pi}{2}\bigg] \\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hspace{4.6em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \hspace{3em} \alpha \approx 0.345
$$
