# AA1167

**Maximum mark: 7**

Two circles, with centres $A$ and $B$, intersect at points $S$ and $T$.

The radii of the circles are $5\,$cm and $2\,$cm and angle $\angle SAT = 30^\circ$. This is shown below.

![AA1167_q](images/AA1167_q.JPEG)

The shaded region $A$ is enclosed by the arcs of the two circles from their intersection points.

1.  Find $\overline{ST}$. [2 marks]

2.  Hence find the angle $\angle SBT$. [2 marks]

Morris is making a sign for his ice cream shop based on the circles shown above and he wants to paint region $A$.

3. What is the area of region $A$? [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the cosine rule we can write a formula $\overline{ST}$ and then use our G.D.C.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \overline{ST}^2&=5^2+5^2-2\times 5 \times 5 \times \cos{30^\circ} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}\\[6pt]
        &=2.5881...\\[6pt]
        \overline{ST}&=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{2.59\,\text{cm}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
2.
    Consider the triangle $SBT$. We can use the cosine rule and the answer from part(b) to find $\angle SBT$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \cos{\angle SBT}&=\frac{2^2+2^2-(2.5881...)^2}{2\times 2^2} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\[6pt]
        \angle SBT&=80.63...^\circ\\[6pt]
        &=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{80.6^\circ} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  For this part of the question we need to break region $A$ into two parts. This is shown below as the dark blue and dark red regions.

    ![AA1167_a](images/AA1167_a.JPEG)

    We will first find the dark blue region ($A_B$) by subtracting the area of the blue triangle ($A_t$) from the area of the blue sector ($A_s$). Using the formula booklet
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        A_B&=A_s - A_t\\[6pt]
        &=\tfrac{30}{360}\times \pi \times 5^2 - \frac{1}{2}\times 5^2 \times \sin{30^\circ}\\[6pt]
        &=0.29498...\text{cm}^2 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using a similar method we can find the area of the red region.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        A_R&=A_s - A_t\\[6pt]
        &=\tfrac{80.63...}{360}\times \pi \times 2^2 - \frac{1}{2}\times 2^2 \times \sin{80.63...^\circ}\\[6pt]
        &=0.8414...\text{cm}^2 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Therefore we can find the total area of $A$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        A&=A_b+A_r\\[6pt]
        &=0.29498...+0.8414...\\[6pt]
        &=\hspace{0.525em}1.14\,\text{cm}^2  \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
