# AA213

**Maximum mark: 12**

Farmer Edward owns a triangular field with ratio of the sides
$15:15:15\sqrt{2}$. Edward attaches a rope to a wooden post at the right
angle corner of his field, and attaches the other end to his cow Gertie.

1.  Given that the rope is $10$ m long,
    calculate the percentage of Edward's field that Gertie is able to
    graze. Give your answer correct to **the nearest percent**.
    [3 marks]

Edward replaces the rope with another one, this time of length $b$
metres, $10 < b < 15$, so that Gertie can graze exactly $90$% of his
field.

2.  Show that $b$ satisfies the equation
    $$
    \hspace{-2em} b^2\bigg[\dfrac{\pi}{4} - \arccos\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\dfrac{15\sqrt{2}}{2b}\bigg)\bigg] + \bigg(\dfrac{15\sqrt{2}}{2}\bigg)\sqrt{b^2 - \dfrac{225}{2}} = 101.25. \\
    $$
    [7 marks]

3.  Find the value of $b$. Give your answer correct to **two
    decimal places**. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we sketch Edward's field and the region that his cow Gertie is
    able to graze (single sector, unshaded), we have

    ![142acc37a1d81643a131037b3368808fab24ad5d.svg](images/142acc37a1d81643a131037b3368808fab24ad5d.svg)

    Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.4em} A_{\text{field}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{OB})(\text{OC}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(15)(15) \\[11pt]
    &= 112.5 \text{ m$^2$}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the area of a sector
    formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.6em} A_{\text{sector}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OA}^2\big](\text{B\^{O}C}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[10^2\big]\Big[\dfrac{\pi}{2}\Big] \hspace{0.1em} \\[11pt]
    &= 25\pi \text{ m$^2$}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the percentage of
    Edward's field that Gertie is able to graze is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{A_{\text{sector}}}{A_{\text{field}}}\times100\% &= \dfrac{25\pi}{112.5}\times100\% \hspace{2.65em} \\[6pt]
    &\approx 70\%
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  If we sketch Edward's field and the region that his cow Gertie is
    able to graze ($2 \text{ sectors} + 2 \text{ triangles}$, unshaded),
    we have

    ![c656b73af5e41c0e3158f9fbdcc110f74db38ea1.svg](images/c656b73af5e41c0e3158f9fbdcc110f74db38ea1.svg)

    where:

    1\. $\text{OA} = 15$ m and
        $\text{AE} = 15\sqrt{2}$ m;

    2\.
        $\text{OC} = \text{AC} = \text{CE} = \dfrac{15\sqrt{2}}{2}$ m;

    3\. $\text{OB} = b$ m;

    4\.
        $\text{BC} = \sqrt{b^2 - \dfrac{225}{2}}$ m;

    5\. $\theta = \text{B\^{O}C}$;

    6\. $\text{A\^{O}B} = \dfrac{\pi}{4} - \theta$.

    Using the right triangle BOC, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.7em} \cos \theta &= \dfrac{\text{OC}}{\text{OB}} \\[6pt]
    \cos \theta &= \dfrac{15\sqrt{2}}{2b} \\[5pt]
    \theta &= \arccos\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\dfrac{15\sqrt{2}}{2b}\bigg)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, using the area of a sector and the area of a triangle
    formulas, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.7em} A_{\text{shaded}} &=  2\bigg[ \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OB}^2\big](\text{A\^OB}) + \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{OC})(\text{BC})\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= \big[\text{OB}^2\big]\bigg[\dfrac{\pi}{4} - \theta\bigg] + (\text{OC})(\text{BC}) \\[3pt]
    &= b^2\bigg[\dfrac{\pi}{4} - \arccos\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\dfrac{15\sqrt{2}}{2b}\bigg)\bigg] + \bigg(\dfrac{15\sqrt{2}}{2}\bigg)\sqrt{b^2 - \dfrac{225}{2}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    On the other hand, $90\%$ of the area of Edward's field is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\text{shaded}} &= (112.5)(0.9) \hspace{0.2em} \\[6pt]
    &= 101.25 \text{ m$^2$}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we obtain

    $$
    b^2\bigg[\dfrac{\pi}{4} - \arccos\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\dfrac{15\sqrt{2}}{2b}\bigg)\bigg] + \bigg(\dfrac{15\sqrt{2}}{2}\bigg)\sqrt{b^2 - \dfrac{225}{2}} = 101.25
    $$

3.  If we solve the equation $(1)$ for $b$
    \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\], we find
     $b \approx 11.83 \text{ m}$
