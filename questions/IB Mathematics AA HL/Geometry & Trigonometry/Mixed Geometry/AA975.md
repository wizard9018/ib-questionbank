# AA975

**Maximum mark: 7**

Consider the following cone inscribed in a cylinder.

![aa975a](images/aa975a.PNG)

The coordinates of points P and Q are P$(-5,7,-2)$ and Q$(3,-1,2)$, and are the endpoints of the diameter of the circular base.

1. Find the coordinates of the centre of the circular base of the cone.[2 marks]

2. Find the radius, $r$, of the circular base of the cone.

[2 marks]

The cylinder has a volume of $96\pi\,\text{cm}^3$.

3. Find the volume of the inscribed cone.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Using the formula for the coordinates of the midpoint of a segment, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{Centre} &= \text{Midpoint} (\mathrm{P},\mathrm{Q}) \\[10pt]

    &= \left(\dfrac{-5+3}{2},\dfrac{7+(-1)}{2},\dfrac{-2+2}{2}\right) \\[10pt]

    &\boxed{= (-1,3,0)}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The radius is the length of the segment joining point P or Q with the centre. 

    Using the distance formula 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        d=\sqrt{(x_1-x_2)^2+(y_1-y_2)^2+(z_1-z_2)^2}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    We get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        r &= \sqrt{(-5-(-1))^2+(7-3)^2+(-2-0)^2} \\[10pt]

        &= \sqrt{(-4)^2+(4)^2+(-2)^2} \\[10pt]

        &= \sqrt{16+16+4} \\[10pt]

        &= \sqrt{36} \\[10pt]

        &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{r = 6}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. Using the volume formula for a cone and a cylinder, we obtain

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \text{Volume of the cylinder} &= \pi r^2 h \\[10pt]

    \text{Volume of the cone} &= \dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h \\[10pt] 

    &= \dfrac{1}{3}\times\text{Volume of the cylinder} \\[10pt]

    &= \dfrac{1}{3}\left(96\pi\right) \\[10pt]

    & \hspace{-0.75em}\boxed{V=32\pi\,\text{cm}^3}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
