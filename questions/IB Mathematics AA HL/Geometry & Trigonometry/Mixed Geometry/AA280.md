# AA280

**Maximum mark: 7**

The following diagram shows triangle ABC and sector ACD of a circle with
centre C.

![a8aa4bb770e086d30cb351efe48b6aa3488058dd.svg](images/a8aa4bb770e086d30cb351efe48b6aa3488058dd.svg)

$\text{AC} = 5$ cm,
$\text{BC} = 8$ cm,
$\text{the area of triangle ABC} = 10\sqrt{3}\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}^2$.

1.  Find AĈB. [4 marks]

2.  Find the exact area of sector ACD. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.6em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AC})(\text{BC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{C}B}) \\[8pt]
    10\sqrt{3} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(5)(8)\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{C}B}) \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} &= \sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{C}B}) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{7.8em} \text{A\^{C}B} = \dfrac{\pi}{3} \hspace{3em} [\text{since \text{A\^{C}B} is acute}]
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{D\^{C}A} &= \dfrac{\pi}{2} - \text{A\^{C}B} \hspace{0.75em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\pi}{2} - \dfrac{\pi}{3} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{\pi}{6}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the area of a sector
    formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.9em} A_{\text{sector}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{AC}^2\big](\text{D\^{C}A}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[5^2\big]\hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\frac{\pi}{6}\Big) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{25\pi}{12} \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
