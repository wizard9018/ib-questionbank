# AA290

**Maximum mark: 15**

The following diagram shows a circular crop field.

![d32147c4e7101351ca3a3ac5d91bed2a551229ea.svg](images/d32147c4e7101351ca3a3ac5d91bed2a551229ea.svg)

The circle has centre O and a radius of $400$ m,
and the points A, B, and C lie on the circle. The angle AOB is $1.6$
radians.

1.  Find the length of chord AB.[3 marks]

2.  Find the area of triangle AOB. [2 marks]

The angle BOC is $2.5$ radians.

3.  Find the length of the minor arc AC. [3 marks]

4.  Find the area of the shaded region. [3 marks]

The shaded region is to be planted with corn. Corn seeds are sold in
bags which cost $$\$140$$ each. One bag is enough for seeding
$8960$ m$^2$.

5.  Find the cost of the corn seeds. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{AB}^2 &= \text{AO}^2 + \text{BO}^2 - 2(\text{AO})(\text{BO})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{O}B}) \\[6pt]
    \text{AB} &= \sqrt{400^2 + 400^2 - 2(400)(400)\cos\hspace{0.05em}(1.6)} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{AB} \approx 574 \text{ m} \hspace{12.9em}
    $$

2.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.1em} A_{\triangle\text{AOB}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AO})(\text{BO})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{O}B}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(400)(400)\sin\hspace{0.05em}(1.6) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 80\hspace{0.15em}000 \text{ m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.1em} \text{C\^{O}A} &= 2\pi - (\text{A\^{O}B} + \text{B\^{O}C}) \\[6pt]
    &= 2\pi - (1.6 + 2.5) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 2.1832 \text{ radians}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, using the
    length of an arc formula, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    l_{\text{arc}} &= (\text{C\^{O}A})(\text{CO}) \\[6pt]
    &= (2.1832)(400) \\[6pt]
    &\approx 873 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using the area of a sector formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} A_{\text{shaded}} &= A_{\text{sector}} + A_{\triangle\text{AOB}} \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OA}^2\big](\text{C\^{O}A}) + 80\hspace{0.15em}000 \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[400^2\big](2.1832) + 80\hspace{0.15em}000 \\[10pt]
    &\approx 255\hspace{0.15em}000 \text{ m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

5.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{\# bags needed} &= \left\lceil \dfrac{A_{\text{shaded}}}{8960}\right\rceil \hspace{2.1em} \\[6pt]
    &= \left\lceil\dfrac{255\hspace{0.15em}000}{8960}\right\rceil \\[12pt]
    &= 29
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the cost of the corn seeds is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} \text{cost} &= (29)(140) \\[6pt]
    &= \$4060
    \end{aligned}
    $$
