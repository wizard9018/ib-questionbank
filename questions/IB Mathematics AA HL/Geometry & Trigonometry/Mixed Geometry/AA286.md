# AA286

**Maximum mark: 15**

The diagram below shows a circle of radius
$9$ cm. The points A, B, C and D lie on the
circle.

![e3afdde75d5d2e0f844a6a5d7274cc20537c886c.svg](images/e3afdde75d5d2e0f844a6a5d7274cc20537c886c.svg)

$\text{AD} = 9\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm},\hspace{0.25em} \text{DC} = 12\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm},\hspace{0.25em} \text{BC} = 16\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm},\hspace{0.25em} \text{A\^{D}C} = \ang{108},\hspace{0.25em} \text{D\^{C}B} = \ang{75}$.

1.  Find AC. [3 marks]

2.
    1.  Find DĈA.

    2.  Hence find AĈB. [5 marks]

3.  Find the area of triangle ACD. [3 marks]

4.  Hence, or otherwise, find the total area of the shaded region.
    [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{AC}^2 &= \text{AD}^2 + \text{CD}^2 - 2(\text{AD})(\text{CD})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{D}C}) \\[6pt]
    \text{AC} &= \sqrt{9^2 + 12^2 - 2(9)(12)\cos \ang{108}} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{AC} \approx 17.1 \text{ cm} \hspace{12em}
    $$

2.
    1.  Using the sine rule, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{D\^{C}A})}{\text{AD}} &= \dfrac{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{D}C})}{\text{AC}} \hspace{3.8em} \\[6pt]
        \dfrac{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{D\^{C}A})}{9} &= \dfrac{\sin\ang{108}}{17.1} \\[8pt]
        \sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{D\^{C}A}) &= \dfrac{9\sin\ang{108}}{17.1} \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        $$
        \hspace{6.7em }\text{D\^{C}A} \approx \ang{30.0} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        $$

    2.  Hence we obtain
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{A\^{C}B} &= \ang{75} - \text{D\^{C}A} \hspace{3.2em} \\[6pt]
        &= \ang{75} - \ang{30.0} \\[6pt]
        &= \ang{45.0}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.25em} A_{\triangle\text{ACD}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AD})(\text{CD})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{D}C}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(9)(12)\sin \ang{108} \\[10pt]
    &\approx 51.4 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Using the area of a triangle formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.1em} A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{AC})(\text{BC})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{C}B}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}(17.1)(16)\sin \ang{45.0} \\[10pt]
    &\approx 96.7 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} A_{\text{shaded}} &= A_{\text{circle}} - A_{\triangle\text{ACD}} - A_{\triangle\text{ABC}} \\[6pt]
    &= 81\pi - 51.4 - 96.7 \\[6pt]
    &= 106 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
