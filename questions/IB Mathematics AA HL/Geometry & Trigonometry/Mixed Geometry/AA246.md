# AA246

**Maximum mark: 12**

Peggy owns a rectangular field, $12$ m by
$5$ m. Peggy attaches a rope to a metal post at
the upper left corner of her field, and attaches the other end to her
cow Daisy.

1.  Given that the rope is $6$ m long, calculate
    the percentage of Peggy's field that Daisy is able to graze. Give
    your answer correct to **the nearest percent**. [5 marks]

2.  Peggy replaces Daisy's rope with another one of length $\alpha$ m,
    $5 < \alpha < 12$, so that Daisy can now graze **exactly three
    quarters** of Peggy's field.

    Show that $\alpha$ satisfies the equation

    $$
    \textstyle 5\sqrt{\alpha^2 - 25} + \alpha^2\arcsin\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\dfrac{5}{\alpha}\bigg) =  90.
    $$
    [5 marks]

3.  Find the value of $\alpha$. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we sketch Peggy's field and the region that her cow Daisy is able
    to graze ($\text{right triangle} +\text{sector}$, unshaded), we have

    ![874caa3225f5635c09acdbdf186647f4983800de.svg](images/874caa3225f5635c09acdbdf186647f4983800de.svg)

    Using the area of a rectangle formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\text{field}} &= (\text{OB})(\text{OE}) \\[7pt]
    &= (12)(5) \\[6pt]
    &= 60 \text{ m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the area of a triangle
    formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.2em} A_{\triangle\text{DOE}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{DE})(\text{OE}) \\[7pt]
    &= \textstyle \dfrac{1}{2}(5)(\hspace{-0.05em}\sqrt{6^2-5^2}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{5\sqrt{11}}{2} \text{ m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the area
    of a sector formula, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{12em} A_{\text{sector}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OD}^2\big](\text{D\^{O}A}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[6^2\big](\theta) \\[5pt]
    &= 18\arcsin\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\dfrac{5}{6}\bigg) \text{ m}^2 \hspace{2em} [\text{since $\sin \theta = 5/6$}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the percentage of Peggy's field that Daisy is able to graze is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \bigg[\dfrac{A_{\triangle\text{DOE}} + A_{\text{sector}}}{A_{\text{field}}}\bigg]\hspace{-0.05em}\times100\% &= \bigg[\dfrac{\frac{5\sqrt{11}}{2} + 18\arcsin\hspace{-0.1em}\big(\frac{5}{6}\big)}{60}\bigg]\hspace{-0.05em}\times100\% \hspace{0.8em} \\[8pt]
    &\approx 43\%
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  If we sketch Peggy's field and the region that her cow Daisy is able
    to graze ($\text{right triangle} +\text{sector}$, unshaded), we have

    ![5d7c138857cb64746d28b7646c6a98bd09037be8.svg](images/5d7c138857cb64746d28b7646c6a98bd09037be8.svg)

    where:

    1\. $\text{DE} = \sqrt{\alpha^2 - 25}$ m and
        $\theta = \arcsin\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\dfrac{5}{\alpha}\bigg)$
        radians;

    2\.
        $A_{\triangle\text{DOE}} = \dfrac{1}{2}(5)(\hspace{-0.05em}\sqrt{\alpha^2 - 25})$
        m$^2$;

    3\.
        $A_{\text{sector}} = \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\alpha^2\big](\theta)$
        m$^2$;

    4\. $A_\text{field} = 60$ m$^2$.

    Hence we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\triangle\text{DOE}} + A_{\text{sector}} &= \dfrac{3}{4}\hspace{0.05em}A_\text{field} \\[10pt]
    \textstyle \dfrac{1}{2}(5)(\hspace{-0.05em}\sqrt{\alpha^2 - 25}) + \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\alpha^2\big](\theta) &= \dfrac{3}{4}(60) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \textstyle 5\sqrt{\alpha^2 - 25} + \alpha^2\arcsin\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\dfrac{5}{\alpha}\bigg) =  90 \hspace{2.15em}
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $(1)$ for $\alpha$
    \[$\text{by using G.D.C.}$\], we get
     $\alpha \approx 9.46 \text{ m}$
