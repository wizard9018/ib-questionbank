# AA686

**Maximum mark: 5**

The following diagram shows a right-angled triangle ABC and a sector CBE
of a circle with centre C. The point D lies on [AC] such that [BD] is perpendicular to [AC]. The region $R$ is bounded by [BD], [DE] and arc BFE.

![bb277c6f5a06c81f51a26fa62ffabd73438e8bbe.svg](images/bb277c6f5a06c81f51a26fa62ffabd73438e8bbe.svg)

$\text{AB} = 8$ cm,
$\text{AC} = 10$ cm,
$\text{BC} = 6$ cm.

Find the area of the shaded region $R$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos \hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{C}E}) &= \dfrac{6}{10} \hspace{4.1em} \\[8pt]
\text{B\^{C}E} &\approx 0.927295 \text{ radians} \hspace{1.5em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
\end{aligned}
$$

We also have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos \hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{C}E}) &= \dfrac{\text{CD}}{6}\\[6pt]
\text{CD} &= 6\cos \hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{C}E}) \\[6pt]
&= 6\hspace{-0.15em}\left[\dfrac{6}{10}\right] \\[6pt]
&= 3.6 \text{ cm}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, using the area of a sector and
the area of a triangle formulas, we find
$$
\begin{aligned}
A_{\text{shaded}} &= A_{\text{sector}} - A_{\triangle\text{BCD}} \\[9pt]
&= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{BC}^2\big](\text{B\^{C}E}) - \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{BC})(\text{CD})\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{B\^{C}D}) \\[8pt]
&= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[6^2\big](0.927295) - \dfrac{1}{2}(6)(3.6)\sin\hspace{0.05em}(0.927295) \\[10pt]
&\approx 8.05 \text{ cm}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
