# AA300

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows part of a circle with centre O and radius
$5$ cm.

![d3a02f29895c6ce0b991606f98b7b99a2c21d836.svg](images/d3a02f29895c6ce0b991606f98b7b99a2c21d836.svg)

Points A, B lie on the circle, chord AB has a length of $8$ cm and
$\text{A\^{O}B} = \theta$.

1.  Find the value of $\theta$, giving your answer in radians.
    [3 marks]

2.  Find the area of the shaded region. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the cosine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} \text{AB}^2 &= \text{OA}^2 + \text{OB}^2 - 2(\text{OA})(\text{OB})\cos\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{O}B}) \\[6pt]
    8^2 &= 5^2 + 5^2 - 2(5)(5)\cos \theta \\[7pt]
    64 &= 50 - 50\cos \theta \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{2.5em} \theta \approx 1.85 \text{ radians} \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$

2.  Using the area of a sector formula, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A_{\text{shaded}} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OA}^2\big](\text{B\^{O}A}) \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{1}{2}\big[5^2\big](2\pi - 1.85) \\[10pt]
    &\approx 55.4 \text{ cm}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
