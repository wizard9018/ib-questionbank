# AA551

**Maximum mark: 7**

The diagram shows part of the graph of $y = f'(x)$. The $x$-intercepts
are at points $A$ and $C$. There is a minimum at point $B$ and a maximum
at point $D$.

![80f456da3204172f8b19b401e13f9469b7dc48f1.svg](images/80f456da3204172f8b19b401e13f9469b7dc48f1.svg)

1.
    1.  Write down the value of $f'(x)$ at $A$.

    2.  Hence, show that $A$ corresponds to a maximum on the graph of
        $f$. [3 marks]

2.  Which of the points $A,B,C,D$ corresponds to a minimum on the graph
    of $f$.[1 mark]

3.  Which of the points $A,B,C,D$ corresponds to a point of inflection
    of $f$. Justify your answer. [3 marks]
