# AA784

**Maximum mark: 4**

The following diagram shows the graph of $f'$, the derivative of $f$.

![43a326bcac6e745530fb72b906e2afd3683a2a60.svg](images/43a326bcac6e745530fb72b906e2afd3683a2a60.svg)

The points M$(-\sqrt{2},-60)$ and N$(\sqrt{2},-60)$ lie on the graph of
$f'$.

The point P$(\sqrt{2},40)$ lies on the graph of the function $f$.

1.  Write down the gradient of the curve of $f$ at P. [1 mark]

2.  Find the equation of the tangent to the curve of $f$ at P.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The gradient of the curve of $f$ at P is
    $f'(\sqrt{2}) = -60$

2.  Using the point-gradient formula with $\text{P}(\sqrt{2},40)$ and
    $m = -60$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    %y - y_1    &=  m(x - x_1) \hspace{8em}\\[12pt]
    y - 40 &= -60(x - \sqrt{2}) \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-0.5em}y=-60x+60\sqrt{2}+40
    \end{aligned}
    $$
