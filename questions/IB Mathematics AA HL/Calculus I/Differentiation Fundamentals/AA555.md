# AA555

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram show part of the graph of $y = f(x)$.

![dbdc7b0d9715bcdcbe90ca12f9685be23c9b6d26.svg](images/dbdc7b0d9715bcdcbe90ca12f9685be23c9b6d26.svg)

The graph has a local maximum at A, where $x = -2$, and a local minimum
at B, where $x = 8$.

1.  On the graph above, sketch the graph of $y = f'(x)$. [4 marks]

2.  Write down in order from least to greatest: $f(-2)$, $f'(8)$,
    $f''(-2)$. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Since $f$ has extrema at $x = -2$ and $x = 8$, that means $f'$ has
    $x$-intercepts at those points, and the vertex in between, which is
    at $x = 3$.

    ![9941fb47747408bde670b0c57078a69678df2459.svg](images/9941fb47747408bde670b0c57078a69678df2459.svg)
