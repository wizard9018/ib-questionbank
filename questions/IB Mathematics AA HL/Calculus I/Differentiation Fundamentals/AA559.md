# AA559

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = 0.5 x^3 - 3.2 x$. There is a local maximum at point A and a
local minimum at point B.

1.
    1.  Find the coordinates of point A.

    2.  Find the coordinates of point B.[2 marks]

2.  Find the coordinates of the point of inflection of $f$. [2 marks]

3.  Write down the values of $x$ for which the graph of $f$ has a
    negative rate of change. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Graphing $f$, the coordinates of the local maximum are

        $\text{A}(-1.46,3.12)$
    2.  Graphing $f$, the coordinates of the local minimum are

        $\text{B}(1.46,-3.12)$

2.  A point of inflection occurs when $f''(x) = 0$, hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f'(x)   & = 1.5 x^2 -3.2\\[5pt]
    f''(x)  & = 3 x\\[5pt]
    0       & = 3x\\[5pt]
    x       & = 0\\[5pt]
    f(0)        & = 0.5(0^3) - 3.2(0)\\[5pt]
    & = 0
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the coordinates of the point of
    inflection are   $(0,0)$

3.  $f$ has a negative rate of change, when $f'(x) < 0$. It happens
    between the

    maximum and minimum   $-1.46 < x < 1.46$
