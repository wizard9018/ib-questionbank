# AA796

**Maximum mark: 5**

Find the equation of the tangent to the curve $y=e^{2x-4} - x$ at the
point where $x=2$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Differentiating $y=e^{2x-4} - x$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} &=  2 e^{2x-4} - 1 \hspace{0.5em}
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence the gradient of the tangent to the curve at the point where
$x = 2$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= 2e^{2(2)-4} - 1 \\[12pt]
%      &= 2e^0 - 1 \\[6pt]
&= 2(1) - 1 \\[12pt]
&= 1 \\
\end{aligned}
$$
The $y$-coordinate of the point at $x=2$ is

$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= e^{2(2)-4} - 2 \\[12pt]
&= e^0 - 2 \\[12pt]
&= -1 \\
\end{aligned}
$$
Using the point-gradient formula with
$\text{P}(2,-1)$ and $m = 1$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
y - (-1) &= 1(x - 2) \\[12pt]
y + 1 &= x -2 \\[12pt]
&\hspace{-0.525em}y = x - 3
\end{aligned}
$$
