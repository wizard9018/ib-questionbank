# AA774

**Maximum mark: 5**

Note: In this question, distance is in metres and time is in seconds.

A tennis ball is thrown in the air. Its height $h$ above the ground
after time $t$ is given by
$$
\begin{aligned}
\\
h(t)  = -5t^2+20t+4, \textrm { for  } 0 \leq t \leq 4.
\end{aligned}
$$

1.  Find $h'(t)$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the maximum height attained by the ball. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{-2em}h'(t)     = -10t+20
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The maximum height occurs when $h'(t) = 0$. Therefore, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    -10t + 20 & = 0\\[12pt]
    t &= 2\\
    % t & \hspace{-1.5mm}p = -3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The maximum
    height occurs at $t=2$. Therefore, evaluating $h(2)$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h(2)    & = -5(2)^2 + 20(2)+4\\[12pt]
    &\hspace{1mm}=24 \text{ m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
