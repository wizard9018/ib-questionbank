# AA536

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = 6x - \ln x$, for $x > 0$.

1.  Find $f'(x)$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $f''(x)$. [1 mark]

3.  Solve $f'(x) = f''(x)$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Differentiating $f(x)$ with respect to $x$, we get
    $$
    f'(x)  = 6 - \dfrac 1 x
    $$

2.  Differentiating $f'(x)$ with respect to $x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{.5cm}
    f''(x)      & = - (-1) \dfrac 1 {x^2}\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = \dfrac 1 {x^2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Equating the answers to part (a) and part (b), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.1cm}
    6 - \dfrac 1 x  & = \dfrac 1 {x^2} \\[8pt]
    6 x^2 - x       & = 1\\[8pt]
    6x^2 - x - 1    & = 0\\[8pt]
    x           & = \dfrac {1 \pm \sqrt{1 - 4(6)(-1)}} {2(6)}\\[2pt]
    & = \dfrac 1 2,\,-\dfrac 1 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Since
    $x > 0$ the only solution is   $ x = \dfrac 1 2$
