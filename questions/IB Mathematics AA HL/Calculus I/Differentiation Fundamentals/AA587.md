# AA587

**Maximum mark: 15**

Let $f(x) = \sqrt{2x + 1}$ for $x \geq -0.5$.

1.  Find
    1.  $f(12)$;

    2.  $f'(12)$ [4 marks]

Consider another function $g(x)$. Let P be a point on the graph $g$. The
$x$-coordinate of P is $12$. The equation of the tangent to the graph at
P is $y = x + 3$.

2.  Write down $g'(12)$.[2 marks]

3.  Find $g(12)$. [2 marks]

4.  Let $h(x) = f(x) \times g(x)$. Find the equation of the
    tangent of $h$ at the\
    point where $x = 12$. [7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Substituting $x=12$ into $f(x)$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        f(12)   & = \sqrt{2(12) + 1}\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{.275em} = 5
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Evaluating $f'(x)$ at the point $x=12$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{.7cm}
        f'(x)       & = \dfrac 1 {\sqrt{2x + 1}}\\[6pt]
        f'(12)      & = \dfrac 1 {\sqrt{2(12) + 1}}\\[12pt]
        &\hspace{.275em} = \dfrac 1 5
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    $g'(12) = 1$

3.  Substituting $x=12$ into $g(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    g(12)   & = 12 + 3\\[5pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = 15
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.8cm}
    h(12)   & = f(12) \times g(12)\\[6pt]
    & = 5 ( 15) = 75\\[6pt]
    h'(12)  & = f(12) \times g'(12) + g(12)\times f'(12)\\[6pt]
    & = 5 (1) + 15\left( \dfrac 1 5\right) = 8\\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the equation of the tangent $y - y_1 = m (x - x_1)$ with
    $m= 8$, $x_1 = 12$, and $y_1 = 75$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5mm}
    y - 75  & = 8 (x - 12)\\[5pt]
    &\hspace{-1.7mm}y = 8x - 21
    \end{aligned}
    $$
