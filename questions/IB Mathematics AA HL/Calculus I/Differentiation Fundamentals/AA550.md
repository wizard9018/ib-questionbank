# AA550

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $f(x) = e^{3x}$. The line $L$ is the tangent to the curve of $f$ at
$(0,1)$.

Find the equation of $L$ in the form $y = mx + c$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

First we find $m$. We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
f'(x)   & = 3 e^{3x}\hspace{.7cm}\\[6pt]
m   & = f'(0)\\[6pt]
& = 3e^{3(0)}\\[6pt]
& = 3
\end{aligned}
$$
The equation of $L$ is
$$
\begin{aligned}
y - y_1     & = m(x - x_1)\\[6pt]
y - 1       & = 3(x - 0)\\[6pt]
& \hspace{-1.8mm}y = 3x + 1
\end{aligned}
$$
