# AA821

**Maximum mark: 7**

The function $f$ is defined for all $x\in \mathbb{R}$. The tangent to
the graph of $f$ at $x=3$ has equation $y=4x-2$.

1.  Write down the value of $f'(3)$.[1 mark]

2.  Find $f(3)$.[1 mark]

The function $g$ is defined for all $x\in \mathbb{R}$ where
$g(x)=11-2x^2$ and $h(x)=f(g(x))$.

3.  Find $h(2)$.[2 marks]

4.  Hence, find the equation of the tangent to the graph of $h$
    at $x=2$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  As the gradient of the tangent line is $4$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{-2.15em}f'(3) = 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  As the line passes through $(3,f(3))$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(3) &= 4(3)-2 \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-1.82em}f(3) = 10
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  We get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h(2) &= f(g(2)) \\[12pt]
    &= f(11-2(2)^2) \\[12pt]
    &= f(3) \\[12pt]
    &= 10
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The slope of the tangent line to $h$ at $x=2$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h'(2) &= f'(g(2))\times g'(2) \\[12pt]
    &= f'(3)\times-4(2)\\[12pt]
    &=(4)(-8)\\[12pt]
    &=-32
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using the point-gradient formula with $\text{P}\left(2, 10 \right)$
    and $m = -32$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y-10 &= -32(x-2) \\[12pt]
    y-10 &= -32x+64 \\[12pt]
    &\hspace{-0.5em}y=-32x+74
    \end{aligned}
    $$
