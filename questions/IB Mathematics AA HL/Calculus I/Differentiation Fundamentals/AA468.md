# AA468

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the curve $y = \dfrac{2x}{1+x}$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$,
$x \neq -1$.

1.  Find $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}$. [2 marks]

2.  Determine the equation of the normal to the curve at the point
    P$(-2,4)$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Differentiating $y$ with respect to $x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10.5em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\bigg[\dfrac{2x}{1+x}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{(2)(1+x) - (2x)(1)}{(1+x)^2} \hspace{3em} [\text{by quotient rule}] \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{(2 + \cancel{2x}) - \cancel{2x}}{(1+x)^2} \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{2}{(1+x)^2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Evaluating the gradient of the tangent to the curve at P, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.8em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}\bigg|_{x=-2} &= \dfrac{2}{(1+(-2))^2} \\[5pt]
    &= \dfrac{2}{(-1)^2} \\[11pt]
    &= 2 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the gradient of the normal to the curve
    at P is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m &= -\dfrac{1}{2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Using the point-gradient formula
    with P$(-2,4)$ and $m = -1/2$, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.8em} y - y_1 &= m(x - x_1) \\[10pt]
    y - 4 &= -\dfrac{1}{2}(x - (-2)) \\[6pt]
    y - 4 &= -\dfrac{1}{2}x - 1 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{2.6em} y =  -\dfrac{1}{2}x + 3
    $$
