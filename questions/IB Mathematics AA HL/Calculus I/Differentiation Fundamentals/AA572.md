# AA572

**Maximum mark: 7**

Consider the function $f(x) = \ln(2x - 1)$. Let point A be the point on
the curve where $x = 3$.

1.  Write down the gradient of the curve at A. [2 marks]

2.  The normal to the curve at A cuts the $x$-axis at P. find the
    coordinates of P. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $$
    f'(3) = 0.4 \qquad\quad \mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    $$

2.  First, we find the equation of the normal. Using the equation of the
    normal $y - y_1 = m_n(x - x_1)$ with $m_n = -\dfrac 1 {0.4}$,
    $x_1 = 3$, and $y_1 = \ln(5)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y - \ln(5)  & = - \dfrac 1 {0.4} (x - 3)\\[6pt]
    y       & = - 2.5(x - 3) + \ln(5)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Substituting
    $y = 0$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    0   & = - 2.5(x - 3) + \ln(5)\\[5pt]
    x   & = 3.64\qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The
    coordinates of P are   $(3.64,0)$
