# AA547

**Maximum mark: 17**

Consider a function $f$. The line $L_1$ with equation $y = 2x - 1$ is a
tangent to the graph of $f$ when $x = 3$.

1.
    1.  Write down $f'(3)$.

    2.  Find $f(3)$. [4 marks]

Let $g(x) = f(x^2 - 1)$ and P be the point on the graph of $g$ where
$x = 2$.

2.  Show that the graph of $g$ has a gradient of $8$ at P.
    [5 marks]

Let $L_2$ be the tangent to the graph of $g$ at P. The line $L_1$
intersects $L_2$ at the point Q.

3.  Find the $y$-coordinate of Q.[8 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  The slope of the tangent line $L_1$ at $x = 3$ is $m = 2$, hence

          $$
          \hspace{-1.1cm}f'(3) = 2
          $$

    2.  Since $L_1$ and $f$ have a common point at $x = 3$, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        f(3)       & = 2(3) - 1\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{.275em} = 5
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.9cm}
    g'(x)       & = f'(x^2 - 1) \cdot 2 x\\[6pt]
    g'(2)       & = f'(2^2 - 1)\cdot 2 (2)\\[6pt]
    & = f'(3)\cdot 4\\[6pt]
    & = 2(4)\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}= 8
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  First we find the $y$-coordinate of P. We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3mm}
    g(2)    & = f(2^2 - 1)\\[5pt]
    & = 5.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence P has coordinates $(2,5)$.

    Next, we find the equation of $L_2$. Using the equation of the
    tangent $y - y_1 = m (x - x_1)$, with $m = 8$, $x_1 = 2$, and
    $y_1 = 5$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y - 5   & = 8(x - 2)\\[6pt]
    y       & = 8x - 11
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Let's find the $x$-coordinate of the intersection point of $L_1$ and
    $L_2$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2x - 1     & = 8x - 11\hspace{2mm}\\[6pt]
    10         & = 6x\\[6pt]
    x      & = \dfrac 5 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Substituting $x = \dfrac 5 3$ into the equation of
    $L_1$, we find the $y$-coordinate of Q
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1cm}
    y       & = 2\left(\dfrac 5 3\right) - 1\\[6pt]
    & = \dfrac {10} 3 - \dfrac 3 3\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}= \dfrac 7 3
    \end{aligned}
    $$
