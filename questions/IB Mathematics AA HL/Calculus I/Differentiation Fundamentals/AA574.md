# AA574

**Maximum mark: 7**

The values of the functions $f$ and $g$ and their derivatives for
$x = 3$ and $x = 7$ are shown in the following table.

![c8404d702928a0dd31b44066b9bd662f1194c9ac.svg](images/c8404d702928a0dd31b44066b9bd662f1194c9ac.svg)

Let $h(x) = f(x) g(x)$.

1.  Find $h(3)$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the equation of the normal to $h$ when $x = 7$. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h(3)        & = f(3) g(3)\\[5pt]
    & = 3 (-2)\\[5pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = -6
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  First, we find the gradient of the tangent to $h$ at $x = 7$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2cm}
    m_T         & = h'(7)\\[5pt]
    & = f(7)g'(7) + f'(7) g(7)\\[6pt]
    & = 5(3) + 1(6)\\[6pt]
    & = 21
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the gradient of the normal to
    $h$ at $x = 7$ is $m_n = -\dfrac 1 {21}$.

    The equation of the normal is given by $y - y_1 = m_n(x - x_1)$.
    Here $x_1 = 7$, hence we find $y_1$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y_1 & = h(7)\hspace{2mm}\\[5pt]
    & = 5(6)\\[5pt]
    & = 30
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1cm}
    y - 30  & = -\dfrac 1 {21} (x - 7)\\[6pt]
    y       & = -\dfrac 1 {21} x + \dfrac 1 {3} + 30\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-1.5mm}  y  = -\dfrac 1 {21} x + \dfrac {91} {3}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
