# AA563

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $f'(x) = x^3 - 9 x^2 + 24 x + 3$.

1.  There are two points of inflection on the graph of $f$. Write down
    the $x$-coordinates of these points. [3 marks]

2.  Let $h(x) = f''(x)$. Explain why the graph of $h$ has no points of
    inflection. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  A point of inflection occurs when $f''(x) = 0$, hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f''(x)  & = 3x^2 - 18 x + 24\\[6pt]
    0       & = 3x^2 - 18 x + 24\\[6pt]
    0       & = x^2 - 6 x + 8\\[6pt]
    0       & = (x-2)(x-4)\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{-2mm}  x = 2,\, 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  From (a) we get $h(x) = 3x^2 - 18 x + 24$. Let's find $h''(x)$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    h'(x)   & = 6 x - 18\hspace{1.1cm}\\[6pt]
    h''(x)  & = 6
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Since $6 \neq 0$ we conclude that
    $h(x)$ has no points of inflection.
