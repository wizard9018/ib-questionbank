# AA1156

**Maximum mark: 8**

Consider the function $f(x)=5xe^x+1$.

The function has a single point of inflexion at $A$.

1.  Show that the point of inflexion  of $f(x)$ occurs when $x=-2$. [3 marks]

2.  Sketch $f(x)$ on the grid below, label point $A$ and any asymptotes.[1 mark]

    ![AA1156_q](images/AA1156_q.JPEG)

3.  The line $g(x)=mx+c$ passes through $A$ for all values of $m$ and $c$.

    1.  Evaluate $m$ when $g(x)$ is tangent to $f(x)$ at point $A$.

    2.  Hence find the range of values of $m$ for which $f(x)=g(x)$ has exactly $3$ solutions. [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Recognise that inflexion points occur when $f''(x)=0$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \textcolor{white}{hack}\tag*{\textbf{M1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Use the product rule to differentiate $f(x)$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        f'(x)&=(5x)'e^x+5x(e^x)'\\[6pt]
        &=5e^x+5xe^x
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Now we use a similar method to find $f''(x)$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        f''(x)&=5e^x+5e^x+5xe^x\\[6pt]
        &=10e^x+5xe^x\tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Now we solve the equation $f''(x)=0$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        0&=10e^x+5xe^x\\[6pt]
        &=5e^x(2+x) \tag*{\textbf{A1}}\\[6pt]
        2+x&=0\\[6pt]
        &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{x=-2} \hspace{2em} \text{...as required.}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

        $$
