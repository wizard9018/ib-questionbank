# AA772

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $f(x)=2x^2-5x+7$. The line $L$ intersects $f$ at P$(3,10)$ and is
perpendicular to the tangent to the curve of $f$ at P. Find the equation
of $L$ in the form $y = mx + c$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

First we find the slope ($m$) of the tangent to $f$ at P. We have

$$
\begin{aligned}
f'(x)   & = 4x-5\\[12pt]
f'(3)   & = 4(3)-5\\[12pt]
m   & = 7\\
\end{aligned}
$$
The slope ($m_{\perp}$) of $L$, perpendicular
to the tangent is
$$
\begin{aligned}
m_\perp     & = -\dfrac{1}{m}\hspace{.7cm}\\[12pt]
& = -\dfrac{1}{7}
\end{aligned}
$$
We find the equation of $L$ that
passes through P$(3,10)$ with slope $m_{\perp}=-\dfrac{1}{7}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
y - y_1     & = m_{\perp}(x - x_1)\\[12pt]
y - 10      & = -\dfrac{1}{7}(x - 3)\\[12pt]
& \hspace{-1.8mm}y = -\dfrac{1}{7}x +\dfrac{73}{7}
\end{aligned}
$$
