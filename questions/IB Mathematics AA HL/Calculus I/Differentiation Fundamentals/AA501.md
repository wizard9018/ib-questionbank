# AA501

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = e^{-3x}$.

1.  Write down $f'(x), f''(x)$ and $f'''(x)$. [3 marks]

2.  Find an expression for $f^{(n)}(x)$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $$
    \hspace{4mm}f'(x) = -3 e^{-3x} \\[8pt]
    $$

    $$
    f''(x) =  9 e^{-3x} \\[8pt]
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{4mm}f'''(x) = -27 e^{-3x}
    $$

2.
    $$
    \hspace{6mm}f^{(n)}(x) =  (-3)^n e^{-3x}
    $$
