# AA532

**Maximum mark: 7**

Consider $f(x),\, g(x)$ and $h(x) = (f\circ g)(x)$, for
$x \in \mathbb{R}$. Given that $g(5) = 8,\, g'(5) = 2$ and $f'(8) = -4$,
find the gradient of the normal to the curve $y = h(x)$ at $x = 5$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
h'(x)       & = \dfrac {\textrm d } {\textrm d x}f(g(x))\\[3pt]
& = f'(g(x))\cdot g'(x)\\[8pt]
h'(5)       & = f'(g(5)) \cdot g'(5)\\[3pt]
& = f'(8)\cdot 2\\[3pt]
& = -4\cdot 2\\[3pt]
& = -8.
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence the gradient of the normal to the
curve $y = h(x)$ at $x = 5$ is
 $m = \dfrac 1 8$
