# AA812

**Maximum mark: 6**

Use L'Hôpital's rule to determine the value of
$\displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{x^2e^{2x}}{1 - \cos x}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Since $\displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} x^2e^{2x} = 0$ and
$\displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} (1 - \cos x) = 0$, we use L'Hôpital's rule
and get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{x^2e^{2x}}{1 - \cos x} &= \displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{\left[x^2e^{2x}\right]'}{[1 - \cos x]'} \\[12pt]
&= \displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{2xe^{2x} + x^2 \cdot 2e^{2x}}{-(-\sin x)} \\[12pt]
&= \displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{e^{2x} (2x + 2x^2)}{\sin x}\\[5pt]\\
\intertext{Since $\displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0}  e^{2x} (2x + 2x^2) = 0$ and $\displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} \sin x = 0$, we use L'H\^opital's rule again:}
\displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{e^{2x} (2x + 2x^2)}{\sin x} &= \displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{\left[e^{2x} (2x + 2x^2)\right]'}{[\sin x]'} \\[12pt]
&= \displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{2e^{2x}(2x+2x^2) + e^{2x} (2 + 4x) }{\cos x} \\[12pt]
&= \displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{e^{2x} (4x + 4x^2 + 2 + 4x)}{\cos x} \\[12pt]
&= \displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{e^{2x} (4x^2 + 8x + 2)}{\cos x} \\[12pt]
&= \displaystyle \lim_{x\to 0} \dfrac{e^{2(0)} \left[4(0)^2 + 8(0) + 2 \right]}{\cos (0)} \\[12pt]
&\hspace{0.525em}= 2
\end{aligned}
$$
