# AA511

**Maximum mark: 15**

A curve is defined by $x^2 + y^2 - 3xy + 45 = 0$.

1.  Show that
    $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = \dfrac{3y-2x}{2y-3x}$.[3 marks]

2.  Find the equation of the normal to the curve at the point $(21,9)$.
    [4 marks]

3.  Find the distance between the two points on the curve where each
    tangent is perpendicular to the $y$-axis. [8 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Applying implicit differentiation, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x^2 + y^2 - 3xy + 45    & =  0 \\[12pt]
    2x + 2y\cdot\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} - 3y - 3x\cdot\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}             & =  0 \qquad \quad [\text{by chain and product rules}]\\[6pt]
    (2y - 3x)\cdot\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}  & = 3y - 2x \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}        & =  \dfrac{3y-2x}{2y-3x}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    as required.

2.  The slope of the tangent to the curve at $(x,y) = (21,9)$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}
    & =  \dfrac{3\cdot9-2\cdot21}{2\cdot9-3\cdot21} \\[6pt]
    & =  \dfrac{1}{3}.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the slope of the
    normal to the curve at $(x_0,y_0) = (21,9)$ is $m = -3$ and the
    equation of the normal is given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y-y_0       & =  m(x - x_0) \\[6pt]
    y-9             & =  -3(x-21) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{5mm}y =  -3x + 72
    $$

3.  The slope of the tangents must be zero. Hence setting the equation
    $$
    \dfrac{3y-2x}{2y-3x} = 0,

    $$
    we obtain $3y = 2x$. Now substituting
    $3y = 2x$ into original equation and solving for $x$, we get

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x^2 + y^2 - 3xy + 45
    & =  0 \qquad \quad [\text{multiply by $9$}]\\[3pt]
    9x^2 + (3y)^2 - (9x)\cdot(3y) + 405
    & =  0 \\[3pt]
    9x^2 + (2x)^2 - (9x)\cdot(2x) + 405
    & =  0 \\[3pt]
    9x^2 + 4x^2 - 18x^2 + 405
    & =  0   \\[3pt]
    - 5x^2 + 405
    & =  0 \quad \quad \quad [\text{divide by $-5$}] \\[3pt]
    x^2 - 81    & =  0 \\[3pt]
    x       & = \pm 9.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the two points are $(-9,-6)$
    and $(9,6)$. The distance between them is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \sqrt{18^2+12^2}    & =  \sqrt{36\cdot(9+4)} \\[10pt]
    &   =  6\sqrt{13}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
