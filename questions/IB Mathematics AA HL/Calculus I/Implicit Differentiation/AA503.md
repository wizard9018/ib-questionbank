# AA503

**Maximum mark: 7**

A curve has equation $2y^2e^{x+1} - 5x^2 = 3$.

1.  Find an expression for $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}$ in terms
    of $x$ and $y$. [3 marks]

2.  Find the equations of the tangents to this curve when $x = -1$.
    [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using implicit differentiation, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \left(4ye^{x+1}\right)\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} + 2y^2e^{x+1} - 10x & =  0 \\[3pt]
    \left(4ye^{x+1}\right)\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} & =  10x - 2y^2e^{x+1} \\[3pt]
    \left(2ye^{x+1}\right)\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} & =  5x - y^2e^{x+1} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{3.8cm}\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}  =  \dfrac{5x - y^2e^{x+1}}{2ye^{x+1}}
    $$

2.  Substituting $x = -1$ into the following equation and solving it for
    $y$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2y^2e^{x+1} - 5x^2 &= 3 \\[3pt]
    2y^2e^{-1+1} - 5\cdot(-1)^2 &= 3 \\[3pt]
    2y^2e^0 - 5\cdot1 &= 3 \\[3pt]
    2y^2 - 5 & =  3 \\[3pt]
    2y^2 & =  8 \\[3pt]
    y^2 & =  4 \\[3pt]
    y & =  \pm 2.
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Substituting $(x,y) = (-1,\pm2)$ into
    $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}$, we get the slopes of the
    tangents as follows:
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    m & =  \dfrac{5\cdot(-1) - (\pm2)^2e^{-1+1}}{2\cdot(\pm2)e^{-1+1}} \\[3pt]
    & =  \dfrac{-5 - 4}{(\pm4)} \\[3pt]
    & =  \dfrac{9}{4} \quad \quad \quad [\text{for $y = -2$}] \\[5pt]
    & =  -\dfrac{9}{4} \quad \quad \quad [\text{for $y = 2$}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the equations of the tangents to the curve at
    $(x_0,y_0) = (-1,\pm2)$ are given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y - y_0  & =  m(x - x_0) \\[10pt]
    y + 2 = \dfrac{9}{4}(x + 1) &\textrm{ and } y - 2 = -\dfrac{9}{4}(x + 1)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    y = \dfrac{9}{4}x + \dfrac{1}{4} \textrm{ and } y = -\dfrac{9}{4}x - \dfrac{1}{4}
    $$
