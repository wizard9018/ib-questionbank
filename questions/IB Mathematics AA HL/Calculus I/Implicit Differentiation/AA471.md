# AA471

**Maximum mark: 14**

A curve is defined by $x^2 + 10xy + y^2 = 48$.

1.  Show that
    $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = -\dfrac{2x + 10y}{10x + 2y}$.
    [3 marks]

2.  Find the equation of the normal to the curve at the point
    P$(-2,22)$. [4 marks]

3.  Find the distance between the two points on the curve where each
    tangent is parallel to the line $y = -x$.
    [7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using implicit differentiation, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.6em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\big[x^2 + 10xy + y^2\big] &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\big[48\big] \\[12pt]
    2x + 10y + 10xy' + 2yy' &= 0 \hspace{3em} [\text{by differentiation rules}] \\[15pt]
    (10x + 2y)y' &= -(2x + 10y) \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{3.4em} y' = -\dfrac{2x + 10y}{10x + 2y}
    $$

2.  Evaluating the gradient of the tangent to the curve at P, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3.1em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}\bigg|_{\substack{x=-2\\[1pt]y=22\hspace{0.2em}}} &= -\dfrac{2(-2) + 10(22)}{10(-2) + 2(22)} \\[6pt]
    &= - 9 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the gradient of the normal to the curve at P is

     $$
     \begin{aligned}
     \hspace{1.5em} m &= -\dfrac{1}{(-9)} \\[6pt]
     &= \dfrac{1}{9}
     \end{aligned}
     $$
     Hence, using the point-gradient
    formula with P$(-2,22)$ and $m = 1/9$, we
    get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} y - y_1 &= m(x - x_1) \\[11pt]
    y - 22 &= \dfrac{1}{9}(x - (-2)) \\[6pt]
    y - 22 &= \dfrac{1}{9}x + \dfrac{2}{9} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{3.1em} y = \dfrac{1}{9}x + \dfrac{200}{9}
    $$

3.
    Hence we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} &= -1 \\[7pt]
    -\dfrac{2x + 10y}{10x + 2y} &= -1 \\[10pt]
    2x + 10y &= 10x + 2y \hspace{1.7em} \\[15pt]
    8y &= 8x \\[15pt]
    y &= x
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, substituting $y = x$ in the equation of the curve, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    x^2 + 10x^2 + x^2 &= 48 \\[6pt]
    12x^2 &= 48 \\[6pt]
    x^2 &= 4 \\[7pt]
    x &= \pm 2 \hspace{5.85em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the two points are
    A$(-2,-2)$ and B$(2,2)$.

    Finally, using the distance between two points formula, we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{7em}\mathrm{AB} &= \sqrt{(2-(-2))^2 + (2-(-2))^2} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{4^2 + 4^2} \\[8pt]
    &= 4\sqrt{2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
