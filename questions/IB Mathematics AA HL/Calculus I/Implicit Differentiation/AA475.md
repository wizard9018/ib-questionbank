# AA475

**Maximum mark: 6**

An oval-shaped superspeedway can be described by the curve
$x^2 + 12y^2 = 1 + 10x^2y^2$,

shown in the diagram below. A F$1$ racing car is moving along the track
with
$\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} = 240\hspace{0.25em}\text{km}\hspace{0.1em}\text{h}^{-1}$
when $x = 0.67\hspace{0.25em}\text{km}$.

![1ca7c44c955ccd2b9c01d16c7fb83b17ecb1d9a3.svg](images/1ca7c44c955ccd2b9c01d16c7fb83b17ecb1d9a3.svg)

Find the value of $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}$, giving your answer
to the nearest km h$^{-1}$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using implicit differentiation, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{10.6em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\big[x^2 + 12y^2\big] &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\big[1 + 10x^2y^2\big] \\[6pt]
2x\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}  + 24y\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= 20xy^2\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} + 20x^2y\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} \hspace{2.5em} [\text{by chain rule}] \\[6pt]
x\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}  + 12y\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= 10xy^2\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} + 10x^2y\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} \\[6pt]
(12y - 10x^2y)\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= (10xy^2 - x)\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} \\[6pt]
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \bigg[\dfrac{10xy^2 - x}{12y - 10x^2y}\bigg]\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}
\end{aligned}
$$

Substituting $x = 0.67$ in the equation of the curve, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^2 + 12y^2 &= 1 + 10x^2y^2 \\[6pt]
\hspace{6.1em} (0.67)^2 + 12y^2 &= 1 + 10(0.67)^2y^2 \\[7pt]
y &\approx 0.271 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, evaluating $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}$ when $x = 0.67$,
$y = 0.271$ and $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} = 240$, we find

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{7.5em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \bigg[\dfrac{10(0.67)(0.271)^2 - 0.67}{12(0.271) - 10(0.67)^2(0.271)}\bigg]\hspace{-0.15em}\times240 \\[12pt]
&\approx -21 \text{ km\hspace{0.1em}h$^{-1}$}
\end{aligned}
$$
