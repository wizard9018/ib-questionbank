# AA473

**Maximum mark: 8**

The double folium is a curve defined by the equation
$(x^2 + y^2)^2 = 8xy^2$, shown in the diagram below.

![552dc05568884371101edad81b0dc8a6f51cb176.svg](images/552dc05568884371101edad81b0dc8a6f51cb176.svg)

Determine the exact coordinates of the point P on the curve where the
tangent line is parallel to the $x$-axis.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using implicit differentiation, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{6.6em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\big[(x^2 + y^2)^2\big] &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\big[8xy^2\big] \\[9pt]
2(x^2 + y^2)(2x + 2yy') &= 8(y^2 + 2xyy') \hspace{2em} [\text{by chain rule}]
\end{aligned}
$$

The gradient of the tangent to the curve at P is $y' = 0$.

Hence, substituting $y' = 0$ in the equation $(1)$, we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{7em} 4x(x^2 + y^2) &= 8y^2 \\[12pt]
x(x^2 + y^2) &= 2y^2 \\[12pt]
x^3 + xy^2 &= 2y^2 \\[12pt]
x^3 &= (2-x)y^2 \\[6pt]
y^2 &= \dfrac{x^3}{2-x} \hspace{3em} [\text{$0 < x < 2$}]
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, substituting the expression $(2)$ in the equation of the curve,
we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
(x^2 + y^2)^2 &= 8xy^2 \\[9pt]
\bigg(x^2 + \dfrac{x^3}{2-x}\bigg)^2 &= 8x\bigg(\dfrac{x^3}{2-x}\bigg) \hspace{0.4em}\\[3pt]
x^4\bigg(1+ \dfrac{x}{2-x}\bigg)^2 &= \dfrac{8x^4}{2-x} \\[3pt]
\bigg(\dfrac{2}{2-x}\bigg)^2 &= \dfrac{8}{2-x} \\[7pt]
\dfrac{4}{(2-x)^2} &= \dfrac{8}{2-x} \\[7pt]
\dfrac{1}{2} &= 2-x \\[10pt]
x &= \dfrac{3}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
Finally, solving the equation $(2)$
\[where $x = 3/2$\] for $y$, we find
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{0.7em} y^2 &= \dfrac{\raisebox{0.25em}{$\big(\frac{3}{2}\big)^3$}}{\raisebox{-0.2em}{$2-\frac{3}{2}$}} \\[5pt]
y^2 &= \dfrac{27}{4} \\[5pt]
y &= \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} \\
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence the exact coordinates
of P are
 $\bigg(\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\bigg)$
