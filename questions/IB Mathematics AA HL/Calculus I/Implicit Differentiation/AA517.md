# AA517

**Maximum mark: 16**

The curve $C$ is defined by the equation $y^2 + 4xy + e^x = 10$. The
point P$(0,b)$ lies on $C$ where $b < 0$.

1.  Find the value of $b$. [2 marks]

2.  Show that
    $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = - \dfrac{4y+e^x}{2y+4x}$.
    [4 marks]

3.  Find the equation of the normal to $C$ at the point P.[3 marks]

4.  Find the coordinates of the second point at which the normal found
    in part (c) intersects $C$.[4 marks]

5.  Given that $u = y^4$,  $y < 0$,  find
     $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}u}{\mathrm{d}x}$  at  $x = 0$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  When $x = 0$, we have $y = b$. Hence, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y^2 + 4xy + e^x     & =  10\hspace{3.5cm} \\[3pt]
    b^2 + 4(0)(b) + e^0     & =  10 \\[3pt]
    b^2 + 1         & = 10\\[3pt]
    b^2                 & =  9 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{1.5cm}b  =  -3 \qquad  \quad [\text{since $b < 0$}]
    $$

2.  Using implicit differentiation, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y^2 + 4xy + e^x     & =  10 \\[12pt]
    2y\cdot\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} + 4\bigg(y + x\cdot\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}\bigg) + e^x
    & =  0  \qquad \quad [\text{by chain and product rules}] \\[4pt]
    (2y + 4x)\cdot\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}
    & = -4y - e^x \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}
    & = -\dfrac{4y + e^x}{2y + 4x}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    as
    required.

3.  The slope of the tangent to $C$ at $(0,-3)$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}
    & = -\dfrac{4\cdot(-3) + 1}{2\cdot(-3) + 0} \\[6pt]
    & = -\dfrac{11}{6}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the slope of the
    normal to $C$ at $(x_0,y_0) = (0,-3)$ is $m = \dfrac{6}{11}$ and the
    equation of the normal line is given by
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y-y_0   & = m(x - x_0) \hspace{.5cm}\\[8pt]
    y-(-3)  & = \dfrac{6}{11}x \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{1cm}y  = \dfrac{3}{11}(2x-11)
    $$

4.  Substituting $y = \dfrac{3}{11}(2x-11)$ into the equation of the
    curve $C$ and solving for $x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y^2 + 4xy + e^x     & = 10 \\[3pt]
    \dfrac{9}{121}(2x-11)^2 + \dfrac{12x}{11}(2x-11) + e^x & =  10 \\[4pt]
    x               & = 0, \,3.19808\dots  \quad \quad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}] \\[5pt]
    x               & \approx  3.20
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Substituting
    $x \approx 3.1981$ into the equation of the normal line, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    y       & = \dfrac{3}{11}(2x-11) \\[3pt]
    y       & \approx  \dfrac{3}{11}(3\cdot3.1981-11) \\[10pt]
    y       & \approx  -1.26  \quad \quad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the other point on $C$ has coordinates
     $(3.20, -1.26)$

5.  Evaluating derivative of $u = y^4$ at $x = 0$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}u}{\mathrm{d}x} & =  4y^3\cdot\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} \\[3pt]
    & =  4\cdot(-3)^3\cdot\bigg(-\dfrac{11}{6}\bigg) \\[9pt]
    & =  198  \qquad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
