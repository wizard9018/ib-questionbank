# AA566

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $f(x) = 4 x \sin (2x)$.

1.  Find $f'(x)$. [3 marks]

2.  Find the gradient of the curve when $x = \dfrac \pi 2$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the product rule with $u = 4 x$ and $v = \sin (2x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f'(x)   & = u' v + u v'\\[5pt]
    & = (4x)' \sin (2x) + 4x(\sin(2x))'\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} = 4 \sin (2x) + 8 x \cos(2x)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We must find the value of $f'(x)$ when $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f'\left(\dfrac \pi 2 \right)    & = 4 \sin \left(2 (\dfrac \pi 2)\right)
    + 8 \left(\dfrac \pi 2\right)\cos\left(2 (\dfrac \pi 2)\right)
    \\[6pt]
    & = 4(0) + 4 \pi (-1)\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{-3mm}m = -4\pi
    \end{aligned}
    $$
