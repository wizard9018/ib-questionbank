# AA287

**Maximum mark: 16**

The diagram below shows the graph of
$f(x) = a\sin\hspace{0.05em}(k(x-d)) + c$, for $2 \leq x \leq 14$.

![4b63d8795f4a8298ac4b50b87dc5e3453b8a7026.svg](images/4b63d8795f4a8298ac4b50b87dc5e3453b8a7026.svg)

The graph of $f$ has a maximum at P$(5,15)$ and a minimum at Q$(11,-5)$.

1.  Write down the value of:
    1.  $a$;

    2.  $c$. [3 marks]

2.
    1.  Show that $k = \dfrac{\pi}{6}$.

    2.  Find the smallest possible value of $d$, given $d > 0$.
        [4 marks]

3.  Find $f'(x)$.  [3 marks]

4.  At a point R, the gradient is $-\dfrac{5\pi}{3}$. Find the
    $x$-coordinate of R. [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        a &= \dfrac{f_{\text{max}} - f_{\text{min}}}{2} \hspace{0.75em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{15 - (-5)}{2} \\[10pt]
        &= 10
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  We have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        c &= \dfrac{f_{\text{max}} + f_{\text{min}}}{2} \hspace{0.65em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{15 + (-5)}{2} \\[10pt]
        &= 5
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.
    1.  The period of $f$ is
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \text{period} &= 2(11-5) \hspace{4.3em} \\[6pt]
        &= 12
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence we get

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        k &= \dfrac{2\pi}{\text{period}} \hspace{3.1em} \\[6pt]
        &= \dfrac{2\pi}{12} \\[7pt]
        &= \dfrac{\pi}{6}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Using the coordinates of P, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        f(5) &= 15 \\[12pt]
        \hspace{0.3em} 10\sin\hspace{0.05em}((\pi\hspace{-0.05em}/6)(5-d)) + 5 &= 15 \\[12pt]
        \sin\hspace{0.05em}((\pi\hspace{-0.05em}/6)(5-d)) &= 1 \\[8pt]
        \dfrac{\pi}{6}(5-d) &= \dfrac{\pi}{2} + 2n\pi \hspace{2.55em} [\sin^{-1} (1) = \pi\hspace{-0.05em}/2] \\[8pt]
        5 - d &= 3 + 12n \\[12pt]
        d &= 2 - 12n \hspace{3em} [\text{for $n \in \mathbb{Z}$}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence the smallest possible (positive) value of $d$ is

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.8em} d &= 2-12(0) \\[8pt]
        &= 2
        \end{aligned}
        $$

3.  Using the chain rule, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f'(x) &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\Big[10\sin\hspace{0.05em}((\pi\hspace{-0.05em}/6)(x-2)) + 5\Big] \\[10pt]
    &= 10\Big(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\Big)\hspace{-0.15em}\cos\hspace{0.05em}((\pi\hspace{-0.05em}/6)(x-2)) \\[8pt]
    &= \dfrac{5\pi}{3}\cos\hspace{0.05em}((\pi\hspace{-0.05em}/6)(x-2))
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  Solving the equation $f'(x) = -5\pi/3$ for $x$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.85em} \dfrac{5\pi}{3}\cos\hspace{0.05em}((\pi\hspace{-0.05em}/6)(x-2)) &= - \dfrac{5\pi}{3} \\[8pt]
    \cos\hspace{0.05em}((\pi\hspace{-0.05em}/6)(x-2)) &= -1 \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{\pi}{6}(x-2) &= \pi + 2n\pi \hspace{2.8em} [\cos^{-1} (-1) = \pi] \\[8pt]
    x-2 &= 6 + 12n \\[12pt]
    x &= 8 + 12n \hspace{3em} [\text{for $n \in \mathbb{Z}$}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the $x$-coordinate of R is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.1em} x &=  8 + 12(0) \\[8pt]
    &= 8
    \end{aligned}
    $$
