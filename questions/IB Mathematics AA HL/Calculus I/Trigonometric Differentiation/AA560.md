# AA560

**Maximum mark: 6**

Consider the function $f(x) =\dfrac {2x} {\cos x}$. Find $f'(\pi)$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the quotient rule, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
f'(x)       & = \dfrac {2 \cos x + 2 x \sin x} {\cos^2 x}\\[6pt]
f'(\pi) & = \dfrac {2 \cos \pi + 2 \pi \sin \pi} {\cos^2 \pi}\\[6pt]
& = \dfrac {2(-1) + 2 \pi (0)} {(-1)^2}\\[6pt]
& \hspace{.275em}= -2
\end{aligned}
$$
