# AA244

**Maximum mark: 8**

In a triangle ABC, $\text{AB} = 2$ cm,
$\text{C\^{B}A} = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$ and $\text{B\^{A}C} = \theta$.

1.  Show that $\text{AC} = \dfrac{2}{\cos \theta + \sin \theta}$.
    [4 marks]

2.  Given that AC has a minimum value, find the value of $\theta$ for
    which this occurs.[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we sketch triangle ABC, we have

    ![9801060c55056d1d9b63618f5ec449a71b496347.svg](images/9801060c55056d1d9b63618f5ec449a71b496347.svg)

    Using the sine rule, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4em} \dfrac{\text{AC}}{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{C\^{B}A})} &= \dfrac{\text{AB}}{\sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{A\^{C}B})} \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{\text{AC}}{\sin \frac{\pi}{4}} &= \dfrac{2}{\sin\hspace{-0.1em}\big(\frac{3\pi}{4} - \theta\big)} \\[8pt]
    \dfrac{\text{AC}}{\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}} &= \dfrac{2}{\sin \frac{3\pi}{4}\cos \theta - \cos \frac{3\pi}{4}\sin \theta}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\text{AC}}{\cancel{\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}}} &= \frac{2}{\cancel{\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}} \cos \theta + \cancel{\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}}\sin \theta} \hspace{0.2em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \text{AC} = \dfrac{2}{\cos \theta + \sin \theta} \hspace{2.9em}
    $$

2.  Using the quotient rule, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (\text{AC})' &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}\theta}\bigg[\dfrac{2}{\cos \theta + \sin \theta}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{0 - 2\hspace{0.05em}(-\sin \theta + \cos \theta)}{(\cos \theta + \sin \theta)^2} \\[7pt]
    &= \dfrac{2\sin \theta - 2\cos \theta}{(\cos \theta + \sin \theta)^2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, solving the equation $(\text{AC})' = 0$ for $\theta$, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{2\sin \theta - 2\cos \theta}{(\cos \theta + \sin \theta)^2} &= 0 \hspace{4em} \\[6pt]
    2\sin \theta - 2\cos \theta &= 0 \\[12pt]
    \tan \theta &= 1 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{12.8em} \theta = \dfrac{\pi}{4} \hspace{3em} [\text{since $0 < \theta < \pi$}]
    $$
