# AA585

**Maximum mark: 8**

Let $f(x) = \sin (3x)$ and $g(x) = \ln (2x + 1)$.

1.
    1.  Find $f'(x)$;

    2.  Find $g'(x)$.[4 marks]

2.  Let $h(x) = f(x) \times g(x)$. Find $h'\left(\dfrac \pi 2\right)$.
    [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $$
        f'(x) =3 \cos (3x)\hspace{2cm}
        $$

    2.
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        g'(x) = \dfrac 2 {2x + 1}\hspace{2cm}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Using the product rule with $u = f(x)$ and $v = g(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3cm}
    h'(x)   & = 3 \cos (3x)\times \ln(2 x + 1) + \sin (3x) \times \dfrac 2 {2x + 1}\\[6pt]
    & = 3 \cos (3x) \times \ln ( 2x + 1) + \dfrac {2 \sin(3x)}{2x + 1}\\[6pt]
    h'\left(\dfrac \pi 2\right) & = 3 (0) \times \ln (\pi +1) + \dfrac {2(-1)}{\pi +1}\\[8pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}= -\dfrac 2 {\pi +1}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
