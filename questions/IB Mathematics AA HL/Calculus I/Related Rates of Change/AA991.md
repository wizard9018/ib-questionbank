# AA991

**Maximum mark: 6**

An inverted cone is being filled with water.

![cone](images/cone.png)

Initially, the cone is empty. The empty cone has a radius of $4\,$cm and a height of $20\,$cm.

Liquid is poured into the cone at a rate of $28.5\, \mathrm{cm}^3\, \mathrm{s}^{-1}$.

The volume of water in the cone is $V$ at any particular time $t$, and $h$ is the height of the water above the vertex of the cone as shown in the diagram.

You may assume the liquid is always horizontal as it fills.

1. Show that $V=\dfrac{5}{3}\pi r^3$. [2 marks]
2. Find the rate at which the radius is increasing when $h=12\,$cm. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the concept of similar triangles we can see that $h=5r$.

    Using the formula for the volume of a cone

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        V_{cone}&=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2h\\[6pt]
        &=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2(5r)\\[6pt]
        &=\dfrac{5}{3}\pi r^3\\[6pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    As required.

2. We want to find out the value of $\dfrac{dr}{dt}$.
Hence, using the chain rule we can write a relation linking the three variables $V$, $r$ and $t$.
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{dr}{dt}&= \dfrac{dr}{dV}\times \dfrac{dV}{dt}\\[6pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    The value of $\dfrac{dV}{dt}$ is given in the question as  $28.5\, \mathrm{cm}^3$.

    We can find $\dfrac{dr}{dV}$ by differentiating the formula from part (a). Hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{dV}{dr}&= \dfrac{d\left(\dfrac{5}{3}\pi r^3\right)}{dr}\\[6pt]
        &=5\pi r^2\\[6pt]
        \dfrac{dr}{dV}&=\dfrac{1}{5\pi r^2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We know when $h=12$ that $r=2.4\,$cm. Putting all of this together
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
        \dfrac{dr}{dt}&=\dfrac{1}{5\pi (2.4)^2}\times 28.5\\[6pt]
        &=0.315 \mathrm{cm}\mathrm{s^{-1}}\\[6pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
