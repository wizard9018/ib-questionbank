# AA505

**Maximum mark: 7**

A bird is $4$ km East and $6$ km North of its nest. It is flying East at a rate of $14$ km h$^{-1}$ and North at a rate of $18 \text{ km\,h}^{-1}.$

Calculate the rate that its distance from the nest is changing.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

The distance from the nest is
$$
\begin{aligned}
r   & = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \\[3pt]
& = \big(x^2 + y^2\big)^\frac{1}{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence the rate of change of above distance function is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}t}
& = \dfrac{1}{2}\big(x^2 + y^2\big)^{-\frac{1}{2}}\Big(2x\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} + 2y\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}\Big) \\[8pt]
& = \dfrac{x\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} + y\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}t}}{\big(x^2 + y^2\big)^\frac{1}{2}} \\[8pt]
& = \dfrac{\big(4\hspace{0.25em}\text{km}\big)\big(14\hspace{0.25em}\text{km\hspace{0.1em}h}^{-1}\big) + \big(6\hspace{0.25em}\text{km}\big)\big(18\hspace{0.25em}\text{km\hspace{0.1em}h}^{-1}\big)}{\Big((4\hspace{0.25em}\text{km})^2 + (6\hspace{0.25em}\text{km})^2\Big)^\frac{1}{2}} \\[16pt]
&\hspace{.275em} \approx 22.7\hspace{0.25em}\text{km\hspace{0.1em}h}^{-1}  \qquad \quad [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
\end{aligned}
$$
