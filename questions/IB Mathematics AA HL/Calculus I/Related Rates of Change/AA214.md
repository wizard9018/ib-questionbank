# AA214

**Maximum mark: 14**

A water truck tank which is $3$ metres long has a uniform cross-section
in the shape of a major segment. The tank is divided into two equal
parts and is partially filled with water as shown in the following
diagram of the cross-section. The centre of the circle is O, the angle
AOB is $\alpha$ radians, and the angle AOF is $\beta$ radians.

![5ea5dc5dff760c4e76844ca31202e2b591b61f26.svg](images/5ea5dc5dff760c4e76844ca31202e2b591b61f26.svg)

1.  Given that $\alpha = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$, calculate the amount of water,
    in litres, in the right part

    of the water tank. Give your answer correct to **the nearest
    integer**. [3 marks]

2.  Find an expression for the volume of water $V$, in m$^3$, in the
    left part of the water tank in
    terms of $\beta$. [3 marks]

The left part of the tank is now being filled with water at a constant
rate of
$\text{\(0.001\)\hspace{0.25em}m\(^3\)}$ per second.

3.  Calculate $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\beta}{\mathrm{d}t}$ when
    $\beta = \dfrac{3\pi}{5}$. Round your answer to $3$ significant
    figures.[4 marks]

4.  Calculate the amount of time it will take for the left part
    of the tank to be fully filled with water. Give your answer in
    minutes and correct to **the nearest integer**. [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have

    ![0d7f0df5c42b7d349ae8aa91d1b35d3e63931aef.svg](images/0d7f0df5c42b7d349ae8aa91d1b35d3e63931aef.svg)

    Hence, using the area of a sector and the area of a triangle
    formulas, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} V_{\text{water\hspace{0.2em}(right)}} &= 3\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}\big[\text{OB}^2\big](\text{A\^{O}B}) - \dfrac{1}{2}(\text{BG})(\text{OG})\bigg] \\[5pt]
    &= 3\bigg[\dfrac{\alpha}{2} - \dfrac{1}{2}(\cos \alpha)(\sin \alpha)\bigg] \\[4pt]
    &= 3\bigg[\dfrac{\pi}{8} - \dfrac{1}{2}\bigg(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\bigg)\hspace{-0.1em}\bigg(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\bigg)\bigg] \\[7pt]
    &= \dfrac{3\pi}{8} - \dfrac{3}{4} \\[10pt]
    &\approx 0.428 \text{ m}^3 \\[10pt]
    V_{\text{water\hspace{0.2em}(right)}}&\approx428 \text{ litres}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Using the right triangle EOD, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \sin\hspace{0.05em}(\text{D\^OE}) &= \dfrac{1}{2} \hspace{3.9em} \\[6pt]
    \text{D\^OE} &= \dfrac{\pi}{6}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the left part of
    the tank is fully filled with water when
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \beta &= \pi - \text{D\^OE} \\[11pt]
    &= \pi - \dfrac{\pi}{6} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{5\pi}{6}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    We must consider 2 cases:
    1. $0 \leq \beta \leq \dfrac{\pi}{2}$

    2. $\dfrac{\pi}{2} < \beta \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$

    In the first case, similar to part (a) using **the area of a sector formula (3.4)**, $A=\frac{1}{2}r^2\theta$ and **the area of a triangle formula (3.PL)**, $A=\frac{1}{2}bh$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{\text{water\hspace{0.2em}(left)}} &= 3\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}\beta - \dfrac{1}{2}(\cos \beta \times \sin \beta)\bigg] \\[7pt]
    &= 3\bigg[\bigg(\dfrac{1}{4}\times2\bigg)\beta - \bigg(\dfrac{1}{4}\times2\bigg)(\cos  \beta \times \sin \beta)\bigg] \\[7pt]
    &= \dfrac{3}{4}\bigg[2\beta - 2\cos  \beta \sin \beta\bigg]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using **the double angle identity (3.6)**, $\sin2\theta=2\sin\theta\cos\theta$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{\text{water\hspace{0.2em}(left)}} &= \dfrac{3}{4}(2\beta - \sin 2\beta)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    In the second case, we have

    ![aa214c](images/aa214c.PNG)

    Therefore

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{\text{water\hspace{0.2em}(left)}} &= 3\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}\beta + \dfrac{1}{2}(\cos (\pi - \beta) \times \sin (\pi - \beta))\bigg]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Noting that $\cos (\pi - \beta) = -\cos \beta$ and $\sin (\pi - \beta) = \sin \beta$, we have

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V_{\text{water\hspace{0.2em}(left)}} &= 3\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}\beta - \dfrac{1}{2}(\cos  \beta \times \sin \beta)\bigg] \\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{3}{4}\bigg[2\beta - 2\cos  \beta \sin \beta\bigg]\\[10pt]
    &= \dfrac{3}{4}(2\beta - \sin 2\beta)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence for all valid values of $\beta$, $0 \leq \beta \leq \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$,
    $$
    \begin{equation*}
    \boxed{V = \dfrac{3}{4}(2\beta - \sin 2\beta)}
    \end{equation*}
    $$

3.  From part (b)

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V = \dfrac{3}{4}(2\beta - \sin 2\beta)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Using implicit differentiation and adapting **the chain rule (5.6)**, we can differentiate the volume of water function with respect to $t$.

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{Chain Rule}\hspace{2em}\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}=\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}u}\times\dfrac{\mathrm{d}u}{\mathrm{d}x}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    To differentiate the RHS of the volume formula with respect to $t$, this becomes

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}(f(\beta))=f'(\beta)\times\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\beta}{\mathrm{d}t}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \dfrac{3}{4}\bigg[2\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\beta}{\mathrm{d}t} -  2(\cos 2\beta)\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\beta}{\mathrm{d}t}\bigg]\\[6pt]
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \dfrac{3}{2}(1 - \cos 2\beta)\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\beta}{\mathrm{d}t}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Rearranging to make $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\beta}{\mathrm{d}t}$ the subject

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{\mathrm{d}\beta}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \bigg[\dfrac{2}{3(1 - \cos 2\beta)}\bigg]\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence, substituting $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t} = 0.001$ and $\beta = \dfrac{3\pi}{5}$ in the expression, we obtain

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.4em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}\beta}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \bigg[\dfrac{2}{3(1 - \cos\hspace{-0.1em}\big(\frac{6\pi}{5}\big))}\bigg]\hspace{-0.2em}\times0.001 \\[9pt]
    &\approx\boxed{0.000369 \text{ rad s}^{-1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  From part (b)

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    V = \dfrac{3}{4}(2\beta - \sin 2\beta)
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Evaluating the volume of water function for $\beta = \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2em} V_{\text{full\hspace{0.2em}water\hspace{0.2em}(left)}} &= \dfrac{3}{4}\bigg[\dfrac{5\pi}{3} - \sin \hspace{-0.1em}\Big(\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\Big)\bigg] \\[6pt]
    &\approx 4.577 \text{ m}^3 \\[12pt]
    &\approx 4577 \text{ litres}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence initially there was approximately $4577 - 1351 = 3226$ litres of empty space in the left part of the water tank. With the rate of $0.001$m$^3$ per second or $1$ litre per second, this empty space will be filled with water in
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    3226\text{ seconds} &= \dfrac{3226}{60} \text{ minutes} \\[6pt]
    &\approx\boxed{54 \text{ minutes}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
