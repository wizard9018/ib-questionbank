# AA476

**Maximum mark: 5**

Air is being pumped into a spherical balloon so that its volume is
increasing at a constant rate of
$15\hspace{0.3em}\text{cm}^3\hspace{0.1em}\text{min}^{-1}$.

The surface area $S$ and the volume $V$ of a sphere of radius $r$ are given by $S = 4\pi r^2$ and $V = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$.

Find the
rate at which the surface area of the balloon is increasing when its
radius hits $10$ cm.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Differentiating $S$ and $V$ with respect to $t$, we have

 $$
 \begin{aligned}
 \hspace{10.6em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}S}{\mathrm{d}t} &= 8\pi r\dfrac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}t} \\[9pt]
 \dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t} &= 4\pi r^2\dfrac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}t} \hspace{3em} [\text{by chain rule}]
 \end{aligned}
 $$

Hence we deduce
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{3.2em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}S}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \dfrac{2}{r}\bigg[4\pi r^2\dfrac{\mathrm{d}r}{\mathrm{d}t}\bigg] \\[9pt]
&= \dfrac{2}{r}\cdot\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t}
\end{aligned}
$$

Evaluating $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}S}{\mathrm{d}t}$ when $r = 10$ and
$\dfrac{\mathrm{d}V}{\mathrm{d}t} = 15$, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{1.1em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}S}{\mathrm{d}t} &= \dfrac{2}{10}\cdot15 \\[12pt]
&= 3 \\
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence the surface area is increasing at the rate
of
 $3 \text{ cm\(^2\)\hspace{0.1em}min\(^{-1}\)}$
when $r = 10\hspace{0.25em}\text{cm}$.
