# AA545

**Maximum mark: 15**

Jack makes an open container in the shape of a cuboid with square base,
as shown in the following diagram.

![7c5e6537084929c30c7c2d4d621b774227d60004.svg](images/7c5e6537084929c30c7c2d4d621b774227d60004.svg)

The container has base length $x$ m and height $y$ m. The volume is $32$
m$^3$.

Let $A(x)$ be the outside surface area of the container.

1.  Show that $A(x) = \dfrac{128}{x} + x^2$. [4 marks]

2.  Find $A'(x)$.  [2 marks]

3.  Given that the outside surface area is a minimum, find the base
    length of the container.  [4 marks]

4.  Jack coats the outside of the container with waterproof resin. A can
    of resin covers a surface area of $5$ m$^2$ and costs
    $$\$\hspace{0.05em}15$$. Find the total cost of the cans needed to
    coat the container. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The volume of the cuboid is $x^2 y = 32$ m$^3$, hence
    $y = \dfrac {32} {x^2}$.

    The outside area of the container consists of a square base and four
    equal sides (rectangles), we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A(x)        & = x^2 + 4 xy\\[6pt]
    & = x^2 + 4x \dfrac {32} {x^2}\\[8pt]
    & \hspace{.275em} = \dfrac {128} x + x^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
     A'(x)  = 2 x - \dfrac {128} {x^2}\hspace{2mm}
    $$

3.  The minimum occurs when $A'(x) = 0$, hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2 x - \dfrac {128} {x^2}    & = 0\\[6pt]
    2 x                     & = \dfrac {128} {x^2}\hspace{1.6cm}\\[6pt]
    x ^3                    & = 64\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-2mm}x = 4\,\mbox{m}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

4.  The area that Jack needs to cover is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    A(4)        & = 4^2 + \dfrac {128} 4\\[6pt]
    & = 48\,\mbox {m}^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    This area requires
    $\dfrac {48} 5 \approx 10$ cans of resin, which cost is

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \mbox{Cost}     & = 10 (15)\hspace{3mm}\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em} = \$150
    \end{aligned}
    $$
