# AA538

**Maximum mark: 13**

A closed cylindrical can with radius $r$ cm and height $h$ cm has a
volume of $24\pi$ cm$^3$.

![b2d9004fe101620336600f8d88cc9fbb24300180.svg](images/b2d9004fe101620336600f8d88cc9fbb24300180.svg)

1.  Express $h$ in terms of $r$. [2 marks]

The material for the base and top of the can costs $15$ cents per cm$^2$
and the material for the curved side costs $10$ cents per cm$^2$. The
total cost of the material, in cents, is $C$.

2.  Show that $C = 30\pi r^2 + \dfrac{480\pi}{r}$.
    [4 marks]

3.  Given that there is a minimum value for $C$, find this
    minimum value in terms of $\pi$. [7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the volume formula for the cylinder $V = \pi r^2 h$ with
    $V = 24 \pi$ cm$^3$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    24 \pi  & = \pi r^2 h\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-.2cm} h = \dfrac {24} {r^2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The area of the base and top is $2 \pi r^2$ cm$^2$ and the area of
    the curved side is $2\pi r h$, hence
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.8cm}
    C   & = 15 (2\pi r^2) + 10(2 \pi r h)\\[6pt]
    & = 30 \pi r^2 + 20 \pi r h\\[6pt]
    & = 30 \pi r^2 + 20 \pi r \left (\dfrac {24} {r^2}\right)\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em} = 30 \pi r^2 + \dfrac {480 \pi} r
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Let's differentiate $C$ as a function of $r$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    C' (r)      & = 60 \pi r - \dfrac {480 \pi}{r^2} \\[6pt]
    0       & = 60 \pi r - \dfrac {480 \pi}{r^2} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac {480 \pi} {r^2}  & = 60 \pi r\\[6pt]
    r^3                     & = 8\\[6pt]
    r                   & = 2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, the minimum value of
    $C(r)$ occurs when $r = 2$
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5mm}
    C(2)        & = 30 \pi (2^2) + \dfrac {480 \pi} 2\\[6pt]
    & = 120\pi + 240 \pi\\[6pt]
    & \hspace{.275em}  = 360\pi
    \end{aligned}
    $$
