# AA477

**Maximum mark: 11**

Farmer Thomas wants to build a sheep farming field in the shape of a
rectangle with semicircles of radius $r$ on two sides, as shown on the
diagram. He has decided to use in total $350$ metres of wooden fencing.

![3892b0192f3c881f07dfd8fd9a4e829c08a254c5.svg](images/3892b0192f3c881f07dfd8fd9a4e829c08a254c5.svg)

1.
    1.  Find an expression for the area of the farming field in terms of
        $r$.

    2.  Find the width of the farming field when the area is a maximum.
        [9 marks]

2.  Show that in this case the length of the rectangle is equal to zero
    and the farming field is the
    circle of radius $175\hspace{0.05em}\pi^{-1}$ metres. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Let $l$ and $w$ be the length and width of the rectangular part
        of the field.

        The perimeter of the field is fixed at $350$ metres.

        Hence we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        P &= 2\pi r + 2l \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
        350 &= 2\pi r + 2l \\[6pt]
        175 &= \pi r + l \\[6pt]
        l &= 175 - \pi r
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the area of the field is
        given by
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3.5em} A &= \pi r^2 + lw \\[6pt]
        &= \pi r^2 + (175 - \pi r)(2r) \\[6pt]
        &= \pi r^2 + 350r - 2\pi r^2 \\[6pt]
        &= 350r - \pi r^2
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.  Differentiating $A$ with respect to $r$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{0.4em} \dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}r} &= \dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}r}\big[350r - \pi r^2\big] \\[9pt]
        &= 350 - 2\pi r
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Solving the equation
        $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}r} = 0$ for $r$, we obtain

        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        350 - 2\pi r &= 0 \\[12pt]
        175 - \pi r &= 0 \\[6pt]
        r &= \dfrac{175}{\pi}
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence
        $r = 175\hspace{0.05em}\pi^{-1}$ is the only critical number of
        $A$.

        Therefore, $A$ is a maximum when
        $r = 175\hspace{0.05em}\pi^{-1}$.
        Evaluating $w = 2r$ for $r = 175\hspace{0.05em}\pi^{-1}$, we
        find
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        w &= 2\times \dfrac{175}{\pi} \\[9pt]
        &= \dfrac{350}{\pi} \text{ metres} \hspace{0.65em}
        \end{aligned}
        $$

2.  Evaluating $l = 175 - \pi r$ for $r = 175\hspace{0.05em}\pi^{-1}$,
    we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} l &= 175 - \pi \times \dfrac{175}{\pi} \\[6pt]
    &= 175 - 175 \\[10pt]
    &= 0 \text{ metres} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the length of the rectangular part of the field is zero.

    Therefore the field is a circle of radius
    $175\hspace{0.05em}\pi^{-1}$ metres.
