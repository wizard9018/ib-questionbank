# AA590

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $f(x) = 24 - 2 x^2$, for $x \in \mathbb R$. The following diagram
shows part of the graph of $f$ and the rectangle OPQR, where R is on the
positive $x$-axis, P is on the positive $y$-axis and Q is on the graph of $f$.

![fc8d9b54a495190d0f0425a2c9aaaafa45786244.svg](images/fc8d9b54a495190d0f0425a2c9aaaafa45786244.svg)

Find the coordinates of point R$(x,y)$ that gives the maximum area of
OPQR.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let's denote $x =$ OR and $y =$ OP. Hence the area is
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{5mm}
A(x)        & = xy\\[6pt]
& = x(24 - 2x^2)
\end{aligned}
$$
The maximum of $A$ occurs when
$A'(x) = 0$, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
A'(x)   & = 24 - 6 x^2\\[6pt]
0       & = 24 - 6x^2\\[6pt]
6x^2    & = 24\\[6pt]
x^2         & = 4\\[6pt]
x       & = 2
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore we find
  $\mbox R(2,0)$
