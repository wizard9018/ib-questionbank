# AA578

**Maximum mark: 7**

A farmer wants to build a rectangular enclosure for his chickens. The
area of the enclosure must be $350$ m$^2$. The fencing used for the side
$AB$ costs \$13 per metre. The fencing for the other three sides costs
\$4 per metre. The farmer wants the cost of the enclosure to be a
minimum. Find the minimum cost.

![85385e28e826f6afebd300b35460cb2bf58db5e7.svg](images/85385e28e826f6afebd300b35460cb2bf58db5e7.svg)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Let's denote $x = AB = DC$ and $y = AD = BC$. The area is $xy = 350$
and\
$y = \dfrac {350} x$. Then the cost, $C$, is
$$
\begin{aligned}
C   & = 13x + 4x + 4y + 4y\\[6pt]
C   & = 17x + 8y\\[6pt]
C   & = 17x + 8 \left(\dfrac{350}x\right)\\[6pt]
&\hspace{-7mm}C_{\min}      = \$436
\qquad\quad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
\end{aligned}
$$
