# AA885

**Maximum mark: 5**

Use L'Hôpital's rule to find
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\to 0}\hspace{0.25em} \dfrac{\arctan(3x)}{\tan(4x)}
\end{aligned}
$$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

We have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\rightarrow 0} \hspace{0.25em}\arctan(3x)&= 0 \\[12pt]
\lim_{x\rightarrow 0} \hspace{0.25em}\tan(4x) &= 0\\
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, using L'Hôpital's rule, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\rightarrow 0}\hspace{0.25em} \dfrac{\arctan(3x)}{\tan(4x)} &= \lim_{x\rightarrow 0}\hspace{0.25em} \dfrac{\left[\arctan(3x)\right]'}{\left[\tan(4x)\right]'}\\[12pt]
&= \lim_{x\rightarrow 0}\hspace{0.25em} \dfrac{\dfrac{3}{1+9x^2}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9.5mm}}{4\sec^2(4x)} \\[12pt]
&= \dfrac{\hspace{-0.4em}\displaystyle\lim_{x\rightarrow 0}\hspace{0.25em} \dfrac{3}{1+9x^2}\rule[-3.5mm]{0pt}{9.5mm}}{\displaystyle\lim_{x\rightarrow 0}\hspace{0.25em} \dfrac{4}{\cos^2(4x)}\rule[-4mm]{0pt}{9.5mm}} \\[13pt]
&\hspace{0.3em} =\dfrac{3}{4}
\end{aligned}
$$
