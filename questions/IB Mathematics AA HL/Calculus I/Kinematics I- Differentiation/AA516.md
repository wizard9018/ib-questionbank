# AA516

**Maximum mark: 8**

The displacement, $s$, in metres, of a particle $t$ seconds after it
passes through the origin is given by the expression
$s = \ln\big(1+ t\hspace{0.025em}e^{-t}\big)$,  $t \geq 0$.

1.  Find an expression for the velocity, $v$, of the particle at time
    $t$. [3 marks]

2.  Find an expression for the acceleration, $a$, of the particle at
    time $t$. [3 marks]

3.  Find the acceleration of the particle at time $t=0$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The velocity of the particle at time $t$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    v(t) & =  s'(t) \\[4pt]
    & =  \dfrac{0 + \big(e^{-t} - t\hspace{0.025em}e^{-t}\big)}{1 +  t\hspace{0.025em}e^{-t}}  \\[6pt]
    & =  \dfrac{(1- t)\hspace{0.025em}e^{-t}}{1 +  t\hspace{0.025em}e^{-t}}\cdot\dfrac{e^t}{e^t}\\[10pt]
    & =  \dfrac{1 - t}{e^t + t}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The acceleration of the particle at time $t$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a(t) & =  v'(t) \\[6pt]
    & =  \dfrac{(-1)(e^t + t) - (1-t)(e^t + 1)}{\big(e^t+t\big)^2} \quad \quad \quad [\text{by quotient rule}] \\[6pt]
    & =  \dfrac{-e^t - \cancel{t} - e^t - 1 + t\hspace{0.05em}e^t + \cancel{t}}{\big(e^t+t\big)^2} \\[10pt]
    & =  \dfrac{(t-2)\hspace{0.025em}e^t - 1}{\big(e^t+t\big)^2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Substituting $t = 0$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a(0) & =  \dfrac{(0-2)\cdot1 - 1}{\big(1+0\big)^2} \\[8pt]
    & =  -3 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m}\hspace{0.1em}s^{-2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
