# AA597

**Maximum mark: 7**

The displacement, in centimeters, of a particle from an origin, O, at
time $t$ seconds, is given by $s(t) = t \sin2t - 7 \sin t \cos t$,
$0 \leq t \leq 3$.

1.  Find the maximum distance of the particle from O. [3 marks]

2.  Find the acceleration of the particle at the instant it changes
    direction for the the second time. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The maximum distance occurs when \|$s(t)$\| is at its maximum.\
    The maximum distance from O is
      $2.76 \ \mbox{cm}$

2.  The particle changes direction for the second time at $t = 2.18$
    seconds.\
    The acceleration is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5em}
    a(t)        & = s''(t)\\[6pt]
    & = 4 \cos 2t - 2(2t - 7)\sin 2t\\[8pt]
    &\hspace{-1.1cm} a(2.18)= -6.37\ \mbox{cm\,s}^{-2}     \qquad\mbox{[by using G.D.C.]}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
