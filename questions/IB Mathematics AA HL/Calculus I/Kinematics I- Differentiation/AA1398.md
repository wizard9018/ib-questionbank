# AA1398

**Maximum mark: 7**

A weight on the end of a spring moves along a straight vertical rod. The diagram below shows the velocity, $v$ $\text{ms}^{-1}$, of the weight at time $t$ seconds, for $0 \leq t \leq 5$.

![AA1398A](images/AA1398A.png)

The graph intersects the $x$-axis at $C$, $E$ and $G$, where $t = 2$, $t = 3.5$ and $t = 5$ respectively. There are local maxima at $A$ and $D$ and local minima at $B$ and $F$.

1. Write down the velocity at point $C$.[1 mark]

2. Write down the values of $t$, for $0 < t < 5$, at which the weight changes direction.[2 marks]

3. State the value of the acceleration of the weight at point $D$, justifying your answer.[2 marks]

4. Explain why the weight is slowing down for $D < t < E$.[2 marks]

---

1. Reading the $y$-value at point $C$ from the graph.

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Identifying where $v$ changes sign by reading the $t$-intercepts ($x$-axis) within the open interval $0 < t < 5$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        t = \hspace{0.2em}2 \quad \text{and} \quad t = \hspace{0.2em}3.5 \tag*{\textbf{A1A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Note: $t = 5$ is not included as the domain given in this question part was $0<t<5$.

3. Point $D$ is a local maximum of $v$, so the graph of $v$ has a horizontal tangent there and $v'(t) = 0$. $$\\[-2.3em]$$

    $$

    $$

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        a = \hspace{0.2em}0 \text{ m\,s}^{-2} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
