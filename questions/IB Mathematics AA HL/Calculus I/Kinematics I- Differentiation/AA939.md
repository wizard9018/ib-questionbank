# AA939

**Maximum mark: 15**

A physicist is studying the motion of two separate particles moving in a straight line. She measures the displacement of each particle from a fixed origin over the course of 10 seconds.

The physicist found that the displacement of particle $A$, $s_A$ cm, at time $t$ seconds can be modelled by the function $s_A(t)=7t+9$, where $0 \leq t \leq 10$.

The physicist found that the displacement of particle $B$, $s_B$ cm, at time $t$ seconds can be modelled by the function $s_B(t)=\mathrm{cos}(3t+5)+8t+4$.

1. Use the physicist's models to find the initial displacement of

    1. Particle $A$;

    2. Particle $B$ correct to three significant figures. [3 marks]

2. Find the values of $t$ when $s_A(t)=s_B(t)$. [3 marks]

3. For $t>6$, prove that particle $B$ was always further away from the fixed origin than particle $A$. [3 marks]

4. For $0 \leq t \leq 10$, find the total amount of time that the velocity of particle $A$ was greater than the velocity of particle $B$. [6 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 1. The initial displacement of particle $A$ is found by substituting the value $t=0$ into $s_A(t)$.

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        s_A(t) &= 7t+9 \\[10pt]

        s_A(0) &= 7(0)+9

        \end{aligned}

        $$

        The initial displacement of particle $A$ is $9\,\mathrm{cm}\,$.

    

    2. The initial displacement of particle $B$ is found by substituting the value $t=0$ into $s_B(t)$.

        $$

        \begin{aligned}

        s_B(t) &= \mathrm{cos}(3t+5)+8t+4 \\[10pt]

        s_B(0) &= \mathrm{cos}(3(0)+5)+8(0)+4 \\[10pt]

        &\approx 4.284

        \end{aligned}

        $$

        The initial displacement of particle $B$ is approximately $4.28\,\mathrm{cm}\,$.

2. Using the G.D.C. to sketch the graphs of $s_A(t)$ and $s_B(t)$ for $0 \leq t \leq 10$, we get

    The graphs of $s_A(t)$ and $s_B(t)$ intersect at points A, B and C. 

    Therefore, the particles have the same displacement at approximately $4.34\text{ seconds, }5.21\text{ seconds and }5.85\text{ seconds}$

3. We must prove that for $t > 6$, $s_B(t) > s_A(t)$. Therefore we consider

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{cos}(3t+5)+8t+4 &> 7t + 9

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Subtracting $(7t + 4)$ from both sides of the inequality, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{cos}(3t+5)+t &> 5

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    The cosine function has a minimum value of $-1$ and a maximum value of $1$. 

    Therefore, for any value of $t$, $\mathrm{cos}(3t+5) \geq -1$.

    Given that $t>6$, the sum $\mathrm{cos}(3t+5) + t$ must be greater than 5.

    Hence we have proven that for $t>6$,

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{cos}(3t+5)+t &> 5

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Adding $7t + 4$ to both sides of the inequality, we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \mathrm{cos}(3t+5)+8t+4 &> 7t + 9 \text{ , for } t>6

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    as required.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    & \hspace{-0.575em}\boxed{\text{For }t>6\text{ , }s_B(t) > s_A(t)}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

4. Velocity is the derivative of displacement, therefore we must find expressions for $s_A'(t)$ and $s_B'(t)$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    s_A(t) &= 7t+9 \\[10pt]

    s_A'(t) &= 7 \\[10pt]

    s_B(t) &= \mathrm{cos}(3t+5)+8t+4 \\[10pt]

    s_B'(t) &= -3\hspace{0.15em}\mathrm{sin}(3t+5)+8

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Hence $v_A=7$ and $v_B=-3\hspace{0.15em}\mathrm{sin}(3t+5)+8$. We must determine the values of $t$ for which $v_A > v_B$. Using the G.D.C. to sketch the graphs of $v_A=7$ and $v_B=-3\hspace{0.15em}\mathrm{sin}(3t+5)+8$ for $0 \leq t \leq 10$, we get

    ![AA939b](images/AA939b.PNG)

    Between points A and B, $v_A > v_B$. There are in total 5 periods during which $v_A > v_B$, and due to the periodic nature of $v_B$, each has the same duration.

    The total time for which $v_A > v_B$ is therefore found by:

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    5 \times (1.3616 - 0.54101) &= 4.103

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    For $0 \leq t \leq 10$, the total time that the velocity of particle $A$ is greater than the velocity of particle $B$ is approximately $4.10\text{ seconds}$
