# AA491

**Maximum mark: 7**

The displacement, $s$, in metres, of a particle $t$ seconds after it
passes through the origin is given by the expression
$s = \ln\left(3+t-2e^{-t}\right)$,  $t \geq 0$.

1.  Find an expression for the velocity, $v$, of the particle at time
    $t$. [2 marks]

2.  Find an expression for the acceleration, $a$, of the particle at
    time $t$. [3 marks]

3.  Find the acceleration of the particle at time $t=0$.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The velocity of the particle at time $t$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    v(t) & =  s'(t) \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} =  \dfrac{1+2e^{-t}}{3+t-2e^{-t}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  The acceleration of the particle at time $t$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a(t) & =  v'(t) \\[4pt]
    & =  \dfrac{\left(-2e^{-t}\right)\left(3+t-2e^{-t}\right) - \left(1+2e^{-t}\right)^2}{\left(3+t-2e^{-t}\right)^2} \\[6pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} =  -\dfrac{1 + (10+2t)e^{-t}}{\left(3+t-2e^{-t}\right)^2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  Substituting $t = 0$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a(0) & =  -\dfrac{1 + (10+0)\cdot1}{\left(3+0-2\cdot1\right)^2} \\[7pt]
    &\hspace{.275em} =  -11 \hspace{0.25em}\text{m}\hspace{0.1em}\text{s}^{-2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
