# AA1397

**Maximum mark: 7**

The displacement of the particle, $s$ metres, from a fixed origin O after $t$ seconds is given by

$$

\begin{aligned}

s(t) = t\,e^{2-t}, \quad t \geq 0.

\end{aligned}

$$

1. Show that $v(t) = e^{2-t}(1 - t)$.[3 marks]

2. Write down the initial velocity of the particle.[1 mark]

3. Find the displacement of the particle when it is at rest.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. To differentiate $s(t)$ we need to use the $\textbf{product rule (5.6)}$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} &= u\dfrac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}x}+v\frac{\mathrm{d}u}{\mathrm{d}x}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    If we let $u=t$ and $v=e^{2-t}$ we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \frac{\mathrm{d}u}{\mathrm{d}t}=1\hspace{1em}\text{and}\hspace{1em}\frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}t}=-e^{2-t}  \tag*{\textbf{M1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Substituting these results into the formula

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        v(t)&= t \cdot \left(-e^{2-t}\right)+e^{2-t}\cdot 1 \tag*{\textbf{M1A1}} \\[6pt]

        &= \hspace{0.2em}e^{2-t}(1 - t)\hspace{0.5em}\text{... as required} \tag*{\textbf{AG}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. Using the result from part (a) we can substitute $t = 0$ to find the initial velocity. 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        v(0) &= e^{2}(1 - 0)\\[6pt]

        &= e^2\text{ m\,s}^{-1} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. First we need to find the time when the particle is at rest. Setting $v(t) = 0$ we can solve for $t$. Recall that $e^{2-t} > 0$ for all $t$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        e^{2-t}(1 - t) &= 0 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Recall that $e^{2-t} > 0$ for all $t$, hence

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        1 - t &= 0 \\[6pt]

        t &= 1 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Now we can substitute $t = 1$ into $s(t)$ to find the displacement when the particle is at rest.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        s(1) &= 1 \cdot e^{2-1} \\[6pt]

        &= e\text{ metres} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
