# AA493

**Maximum mark: 6**

A particle moves such that its velocity
$v$ m s$^{-1}$ is related
to its displacement $s$ m by the equation
$v(s) = 2\arctan\left(\cos s\right)$,  $0 \leq s \leq \pi$.

1.  Find the particle's acceleration
    $a$ m s$^{-2}$ in
    terms of $s$. [4 marks]

2.  Using an appropriate graph, find the particle's displacement when
    its acceleration is
    $0.5$ m s$^{-2}$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  The particle's acceleration
    $a$ m s$^{-2}$ in
    terms of $s$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a (s)       & =  v\dfrac{\textrm dv}{\textrm ds} \\[3pt]
    & =  2\arctan\left(\cos s \right) \left[-\dfrac{2\sin s}{\cos^2 s + 1}\right] \\[8pt]
    &   \hspace{.275em}= - \dfrac{4\sin s \arctan\left(\cos s \right)}{\cos^2 s + 1}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
