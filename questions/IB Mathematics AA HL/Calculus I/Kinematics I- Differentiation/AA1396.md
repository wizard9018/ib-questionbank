# AA1396

**Maximum mark: 7**

The displacement of a car, $s$ metres, from a fixed point O after $t$ seconds is modelled by

$$

\begin{aligned}

    s(t) = t^3 - 6t^2 + 9t, \quad \text{ where }0 \leq t \leq 5.

\end{aligned}  

$$ 

1. Find an expression for the velocity of the car, $v(t)$.[2 marks]

2. Find the values of $t$ when the car is at rest.[2 marks]

3. Find the displacement of the car when the acceleration is equal to $12\,\text{m\,s}^{-2}$.[3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. To find the velocity function we need to differentiate $s(t)$ with respect to $t$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        v(t) &= s'(t) \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]

        v(t) &= \hspace{0.2em}3t^2 - 12t + 9 \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2. The car is at rest when $v(t) = 0$. Hence we can set the equation from part (a) equal to zero and solve for $t$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        3t^2 - 12t + 9 &= 0 \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}} \\[6pt]

        3(t - 1)(t - 3) &= 0 \\[6pt]

        t = \hspace{0.2em}1\text{ s} \hspace{1em} &\text{or} \hspace{1em} t = \hspace{0.2em}3\text{ s} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. We are given information about the acceleration. Hence we need a equation for acceleration. We obtain this by differentiating $v(t)$. 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        a(t)&=v'(t)\\[6pt]

        a(t) &= 6t - 12 \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Next we can set the equation equal to 12 to find the time when $a(t)=12\,\text{m\,s}^{-2}$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        6t - 12 &= 12 \\[6pt]

        t &= 4 \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Finally, substituting $t = 4$ into $s(t)$ we find the displacement when $a(t)=12$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        s(4) &= (4)^3 - 6(4)^2 + 9(4) \\[6pt]

        &= 64 - 96 + 36 \\[6pt]

        &= \hspace{0.2em}4\text{ m} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
