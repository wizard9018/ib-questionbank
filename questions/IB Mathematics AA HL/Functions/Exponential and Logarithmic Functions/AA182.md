# AA182

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $f(x) = 0.2e^{x+2} - 4$, for $-3 \leq x \leq 2$.

1.  On the following grid, sketch the graph of $y = f(x)$. [3 marks]

    ![219ede634589aee775d4d590dd79b30ff5c63914.svg](images/219ede634589aee775d4d590dd79b30ff5c63914.svg)

2.  Find the coordinates of:
    1.  the $x$-intercept;

    2.  the $y$-intercept. [2 marks]

The graph of $f$ is reflected in the $x$-axis, then translated 1 unit in the direction of the positive $x$-axis and 2 units in the direction of the positive $y$-axis to obtain the graph of a function $g$.

3.  Find $g(x)$. [2 marks]

2.
    1.  Solving the equation $f(x) = 0$ for $x$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{3.2em} 0.2e^{x+2} - 4 &= 0 \\[6pt]
        x &\approx 0.996 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
        \end{aligned}
        $$

        Hence the coordinates of the $x$-intercept are
         $(0.996,0)$

    2.  Evaluating $y = f(x)$ for $x = 0$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        y &= f(0) \\[6pt]
        &= 0.2e^{0+2} - 4 \hspace{0.4em} \\[7pt]
        &\approx -2.52
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the coordinates of the
        $y$-intercept are
         $(0,-2.52)$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.5em} g(x) &= -f(x-1) + 2 \\[6pt]
    &= -\big[0.2e^{(x-1)+2} - 4\big] + 2 \\[6pt]
    &= -0.2e^{x+1} + 6
    \end{aligned}
    $$
