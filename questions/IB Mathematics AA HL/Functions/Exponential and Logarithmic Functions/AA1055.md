# AA1055

**Maximum mark: 6**

The temperature of a chocolate is given by the equation
$$
%60 \hspace{0.1em} e^{-0.1t} + 20
T(t) = A \left( 2^{-kt} \right) + B
$$
where $A$ and $B$ and $k$ are constants, $k>0$, $T$ is measured in degrees Celsius and $t$ is the number of minutes since the chocolate was served.

It is known that the initial temperature is $84^\circ$ and the temperature at time $t=\dfrac{1}{k}$ is $52^\circ$.

1. Show that $A=64$ and $B=20$. [2 marks]

It took $40$ minutes to get a temperature of $23^\circ$.

2. Find the value of $k$.[2 marks]

3. Find the number of minutes it takes to get a temperature of $21^\circ$.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Since the initial temperature is $84^\circ$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    T(0) &= A \left(2^{-k(0)}\right) +  B \\[7pt]
    84 &= A + B \hspace{2em}\textcolor{red}{[1]} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Evaluating $T$ at $t=\dfrac{1}{k}$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    T\left(\dfrac{1}{k}\right) &= A \left(2^{-k/k}\right) +  B \tag*{\textbf{A1}} \\[7pt]
    52 &= \dfrac{A}{2} + B \hspace{2em}\textcolor{red}{[2]} \\[-10pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    To find $A$ take the difference $\textcolor{red}{[2]}-\textcolor{red}{[1]}$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{-0.75em} A = 64  \\[-10pt]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Finally, using $\textcolor{red}{[1]}$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    &\hspace{-0.8em} B = 20
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2. Solving for $k$ in the equation $T(40)=23$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    64 \left(2^{-k(40)}\right) + 20 &= 23 \tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[7pt]
    &=0.11037...\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{ k = 0.110} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3. Solving for $t$, $T(t)=21$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    64 \left( 2^{-0.110t} \right) + 20 &= 21 \tag*{\textbf{M1}}\\[9pt]
    &=54.545...\\[6pt]
    &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{ t = 54.5\,\text{minutes}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
