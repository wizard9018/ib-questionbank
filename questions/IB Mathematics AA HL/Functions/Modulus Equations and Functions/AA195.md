# AA195

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $f(x) = \dfrac{9-12x}{cx - 20}$, for $x \neq \dfrac{20}{c}$, where
$c \neq 0$.

1.  The line $x = 5$ is a vertical asymptote to the graph of $y = f(x)$.
    1.  Find the value of $c$.

    2.  Write down the equation of the horizontal asymptote to the graph
        of $y = f(x)$.[4 marks]

2.  The line $y = h$, where $h \in \mathbb{R}$, intersects the
    graph of $y = \big|f(x)\big|$ at exactly one point. Find the
    possible values of $h$. [3 marks]

---

1.
    1.  Using the equation of the vertical asymptote, we get
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        &= 0 \hspace{8.6em} \\[6pt]
        5c - 20 &= 0 \\[6pt]
        5c &= 20 \\[4pt]
        &\hspace{-0.67em} c = 4
        \end{aligned}
        $$

    2.
        $y = -3$
