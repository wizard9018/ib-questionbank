# AA124

**Maximum mark: 5**

Consider the graphs of $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}|x|$ and $y = 2|x| - a$, where
$a \in \mathbb{Z}^+$.

1.  Sketch the graphs on the same set of axes. [2 marks]

2.  Given that the graphs enclose a region of area 40 square units,\
    find the value of $a$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we sketch the graphs of $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}|x|$ and
    $y = 2|x| - a$, we have

    ![68ec0f82b42dd94d75c48aa4929409d0a553c4d8.svg](images/68ec0f82b42dd94d75c48aa4929409d0a553c4d8.svg)

2.  The two lines in the $4$th quadrant intersect when
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{9.4em} -\dfrac{1}{2}x &= 2x -a \hspace{3em} [\text{since $x > 0$}] \\[6pt]
    -x &= 4x - 2a \\[10pt]
    2a &= 5x \\[6pt]
    x &= \dfrac{2a}{5}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence the area enclosed between
    the two graphs is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}(\text{base}\times\text)\bigg] &= 2\bigg[\dfrac{1}{2}\Big(a\times\dfrac{2a}{5}\Big)\bigg] \hspace{2.2em} \\[6pt]
    &= \dfrac{2a^2}{5}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence, given that the area is $40$
    square units, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \dfrac{2a^2}{5} &= 40 \\[6pt]
    a^2 &= 100 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.7em} a = 10
    $$
