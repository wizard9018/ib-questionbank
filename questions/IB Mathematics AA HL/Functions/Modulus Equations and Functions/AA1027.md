# AA1027

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $f(x) = \dfrac{4-ax}{5x - 2}$.

1. Write down the equation of the vertical asymptote of the graph of $y = f(x)$.[1 mark]

The line $y = -1.4$ is a horizontal asymptote to the graph of $y = f(x)$.

2. Find the value of $a$.[2 marks]

3. The line $y = x - c$, where $c \in \mathbb{R}^+$, intersects the graph of $y = \big|f(x)\big|$ at exactly one point. Find the possible values of $c$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Solving the equation $5x-2=0$, we have $\boxed{x = \dfrac{2}{5}}$

2. The horizontal asymptote will be equal to $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to \pm \infty}f(x)$. We have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \displaystyle\lim_{x \to \pm \infty}f(x) &= \displaystyle\lim_{x \to \pm \infty}\dfrac{4-ax}{5x-2} \\[8pt]

    -1.4 &= -\dfrac{a}{5} \\[8pt]

    &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{a=7}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3. If we draw the graph of $y = |f(x)|$ [by using G.D.C.], we have

    ![aa1027a](images/aa1027a.PNG)

    A line of the form $y = x - c$ where $c \in \mathbb{R}^+$ has a gradient of $m=1$ and a $y$-intercept that is less than zero. The following diagram shows the graph of $y=x$, which represents an absolute upper limit for the $y$-intercept.

    ![aa1027b](images/aa1027b.PNG)

    The line $y=x$ intersects the graph of $y = \big|f(x)\big|$ at three points.

    The following diagram shows the graph of $y=x-0.571$, which passes through the point A$(0.571,0)$ and has a $y$-intercept of $-0.571$.

    ![aa1027c](images/aa1027c.PNG)

    The line $y=x-0.571$ intersects the graph of $y = \big|f(x)\big|$ at two points. Any line of gradient $m=1$ and $y$-intercept of less than $-0.571$ will intersect the graph of $y = \big|f(x)\big|$ exactly once.

    Therefore, $\boxed{c > 0.571}$
