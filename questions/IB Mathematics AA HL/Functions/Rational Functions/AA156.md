# AA156

**Maximum mark: 7**

The function $f$ is given by $f(x) = \dfrac{4x^2 - 128}{x^2 - 16}$, for
$x \neq \pm 4$.

1.  Prove that $f$ is an even function. [2 marks]

2.
    1.  Sketch the graph of $y = f(x)$.

    2.  Write down the range of $f$. [5 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  For any $x \in \mathbb{R}$, $x \neq \pm 4$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(-x) &= \dfrac{4(-x)^2 - 128}{(-x)^2 - 16} \\[5pt]
    &= \dfrac{4x^2 - 128}{x^2 - 16} \\[12pt]
    &= f(x)
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence $f$ is an even function.
