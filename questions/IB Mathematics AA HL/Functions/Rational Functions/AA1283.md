# AA1283

**Maximum mark: 4**

Consider the function $f(x)=\dfrac{3x+4}{2x+k}$.

1.  Write down the domain of $f$ in terms of $k$. [1 mark]

2.  Find $f^{-1}(x)$ in terms of $k$. [2 marks]

3.  Hence write down the value of $k$ such that $f(x)$ is a self-inverse function.[1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  A rational function is undefined when the denominator is equal to zero.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    2x+k&=0\\[6pt]

    x&=-\frac{k}{2}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Any other value for $x$ is valid.

    Hence we get the domain

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{x \in \mathbb{R}, x\neq-\frac{k}{2}}\tag*{\textbf{\textcolor{black}{A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

2.  We are trying to find the function $y=f^{-1}(x)$.

    The definition of an inverse states that

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        (f \circ f^{-1})(x)&=x

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Hence we can rewrite this to get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        f(f^{-1}(x))&=x\\[6pt]

        f(y)&=x

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Hence we get

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    f(y)&=x\\[6pt]

    \frac{3y+4}{2y+k}&=x

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Recall, we want $y=f^{-1}(x)$ therefore we should rearrange this to make $y$ the subject.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    x(2y+k)&=3y+4\\[6pt]

    2xy+kx&=3y+4

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    To isolate $y$, we move all of the $y$ terms to one side and then factorise out $y$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    2xy-3y&=-kx+4\\[6pt]

    y(2x-3)&=-kx+4\tag*{\textbf{\textcolor{black}{(M1)}}}\\[6pt]

    y&=\frac{-kx+4}{2x-3}\\[10pt]

    &\hspace{-3.2em}f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{-kx+4}{2x-3}\tag*{\textbf{\textcolor{black}{A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

3.  A self-inverse function is such that $f(x)\equiv f^{-1}(x)$.

    Therefore

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \frac{3x+4}{2x+k}&\equiv \frac{-kx+4}{2x-3}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Comparing either the numerators or denominators,

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    3x+4&=-kx+4

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    &\hspace{-0.5em}\boxed{k=-3}\tag*{\textbf{\textcolor{black}{A1}}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
