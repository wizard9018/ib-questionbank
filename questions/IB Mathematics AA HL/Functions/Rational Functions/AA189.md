# AA189

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = \dfrac{2}{x-a} + b$, for $x \neq a$.

The line $x = 1$ is a vertical asymptote of the graph of $y = f(x)$.

1.  Write down the value of $a$. [1 mark]

The graph of $y = f(x)$ passes through the point P$(0,3)$.

2.  Find the value of $b$. [3 marks]

3.  Find $\displaystyle \lim_{x \rightarrow \infty} f(x)$.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $a=1$

2.  Using the coordinates of P, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(0) &= 3 \hspace{4em} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{2}{0-1} + b &= 3 \\[6pt]
    -2+b &= 3 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    b = 5 \hspace{0.3em}
    $$

3.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{3em} \lim_{x \rightarrow \infty} f(x) &= \lim_{x \rightarrow \infty}\left[\dfrac{2}{x-1} + 5\right] \\[6pt]
    &= 0 + 5 \\[12pt]
    &= 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
