# AA168

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $f(x) = a(x-h)^2 + k$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

The vertex of the graph of $f$ is at P$(3,4)$ and the graph passes
through Q$(1,-4)$.

1.  Write down the values of $h$ and $k$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $a$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $h = 3$  and
     $k = 4$

2.  Using the coordinates of Q, we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(1) &= -4 \\[6pt]
    \hspace{3em} a(1-h)^2 + k &= -4 \\[6pt]
    a(1-3)^2 + 4 &= -4 \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[7pt]
    4a &= -8 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    a = -2
    $$
