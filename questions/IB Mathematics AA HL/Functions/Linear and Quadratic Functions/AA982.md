# AA982

**Maximum mark: 5**

Let $f(x)=4x-2$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1. The graph of a linear function $g$ is perpendicular to the graph of $f$ and passes through the origin. Find an expression for $g(x)$.[2 marks]

2. The graph of a linear function $h$ is parallel to the graph of $f$ and passes through the point $(3,4)$. Find an expression for $h(x)$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The slope of $f$ is $m_f=4$, then $m_g=-\dfrac{1}{4}$ is the slope of the function $g$ that is perpendicular to $f$, we have

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    y-y_1&=m(x-x_1)\\[6pt]

     y-0&=-\dfrac{1}{4}(x-0)\\[6pt]

     y&=-\dfrac{1}{4}x

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore, $\boxed{g(x)=-\dfrac{1}{4}x}.$

2. The slope of the function $h$ is $m_h=4$ because $h$ is parallel to the graph $f$. We have, 

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    y-y_1=m(x-x_1)\\[6pt]

    y-4&=4(x-3)\\[6pt]

    y-4&=4x-12\\[6pt]

    y=4x-8

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore, $\boxed{h(x)=4x-8}.$
