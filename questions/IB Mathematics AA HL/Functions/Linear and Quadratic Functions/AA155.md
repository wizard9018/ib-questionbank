# AA155

**Maximum mark: 5**

1.  Express $3x^2 + 18x + 20$ in the form $a(x+b)^2 + c$ where
    $a,b,c \in\mathbb{Z}$. [2 marks]

2.  Given that $f(x) = x + 3$ and $(g\circ f)(x) = 3x^2 + 18x + 20$,
    find $g(x)$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using the method of completing the square, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    3x^2 + 18x + 20 &= 3\big[x^2 + 6x\big] + 20 \\[6pt]
    &= 3\big[(x+3)^2 - 9\big] + 20 \\[6pt]
    &= 3(x+3)^2 - 27 + 20 \\[6pt]
    &= 3(x+3)^2 - 7
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{10.8em} (g\circ f)(x) &= 3x^2 + 18x + 20 \\[6pt]
    g(f(x)) &= 3(x+3)^2 - 7 \hspace{3em} [\text{by part (a)}] \\[6pt]
    g(x+3) &= 3(x+3)^2 - 7 \hspace{3em} [\text{since $f(x) = x + 3$}] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    g(x) = 3x^2 - 7 \hspace{0.6em}
    $$
