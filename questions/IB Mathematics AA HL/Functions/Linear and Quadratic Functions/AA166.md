# AA166

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $f(x) = x^2 - 4x + 3$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  For the graph of $y = f(x)$, find:
    1.  the $y$-intercept;

    2.  the $x$-intercepts. [3 marks]

2.  The function $f$ can be expressed in the form $f(x) = (x-h)^2 + k$.
    Find\
    the value of $h$ and the value of $k$. [2 marks]

3.  Sketch the graph of $y = f(x)$ on the grid below. Clearly label\
    the intercepts with the axes, and the vertex. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Evaluating $y = f(x)$ for $x = 0$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{2.8em} y &= f(0) \\[6pt]
        &= 0^2 - 4(0) + 3 \\[7pt]
        &= 3
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the $y$-intercept is
         $\mathrm{A}(0,3)$

    2.  Solving the equation $f(x) = 0$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        x^2 - 4x + 3 &= 0 \hspace{8em} \\[6pt]
        (x-1)(x-3) &= 0 \\[6pt]
        x &= 1,\hspace{0.25em} 3
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the $x$-intercepts
        are
         $\mathrm{B}(1,0)$
        and
         $\mathrm{C}(3,0)$

2.  Using the method of completing the square, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{5.8em} f(x) &= x^2 - 4x + 3 \\[6pt]
    &= \big[(x-2)^2 - 4\big] + 3 \\[6pt]
    &= (x-2)^2 - 1
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find
     $h = 2$
    and  $k = -1$
