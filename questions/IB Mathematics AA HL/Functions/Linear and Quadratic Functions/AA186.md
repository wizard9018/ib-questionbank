# AA186

**Maximum mark: 15**

Let $f(x) = 3x^2 + 12x + 9$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  For the graph of $f$, find:
    1.  the $y$-intercept;

    2.  the $x$-intercepts. [4 marks]

The function $f$ can be written in the form $f(x) = a(x-h)^2+k$.

2.  Find the values of $a$, $h$ and $k$. [5 marks]

3.  For the graph of $f$, write down:
    1.  the coordinates of the vertex;

    2.  the equation of the axis of symmetry. [2 marks]

The graph of a function $g$ is obtained from the graph of $f$ by a reflection in the $x$-axis, followed by a translation of 4 units in the direction of the positive $y$ axis.

4.  Find $g(x)$, giving your answer in the form
    $g(x) = px^2 + qx + r$.[4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  Evaluating $f(x) = 3x^2 + 12x + 9$ for $x = 0$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{5em} f(0) &= 3(0)^2 + 12(0) + 9 \\[6pt]
        &= 9
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the $y$-intercept is
         $\mathrm{A}(0,9)$

    2.  Solving the equation $f(x) = 0$ for $x$, we have
        $$
        \begin{aligned}
        \hspace{1.5em} 3x^2 + 12x + 9 &= 0 \hspace{2em} \\[6pt]
        x^2 + 4x + 3 &= 0 \\[7pt]
        (x+3)(x+1) &= 0 \\[7pt]
        x &= -3,\hspace{0.25em}-1 \\
        \end{aligned}
        $$
        Hence the
        $x$-intercepts are
         $\mathrm{B}(-3,0)$
        and
         $\mathrm{C}(-1,0)$

2.  Using the method of completing the square, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.9em} f(x) &= 3x^2 + 12x + 9 \\[6pt]
    &= 3\big[x^2 + 4x\big] + 9 \\[6pt]
    &= 3\big[(x + 2)^2 - 4\big] + 9 \\[6pt]
    &= 3(x + 2)^2 - 3 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find
     $a = 3,\hspace{0.25em} h = -2$
    and  $k = -3$

3.
    1.
        $(-2,-3)$

    2.
        $x = -2$

4.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4em} g(x) &= -f(x)+4 \\[6pt]
    &= -(3x^2 + 12x + 9)+4 \\[6pt]
    &= -3x^2 - 12x - 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
