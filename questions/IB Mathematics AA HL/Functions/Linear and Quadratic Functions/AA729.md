# AA729

**Maximum mark: 6**

A farmer is going to fence two equal adjacent parcels of land. These
parcels share one side (which also requires fencing) as shown in the
following diagram. The farmer has only $80$ metres of fence.

![580f23d360211ad7dd2c45e79d30c7d9c7d24f56.svg](images/580f23d360211ad7dd2c45e79d30c7d9c7d24f56.svg)

1.  Write down the equation for the total length of the fence,
    $80$ m, in terms of $x$ and $y$.
    [1 mark]

2.  Write down the total area of both parcels of land in terms of $x$.
    [2 marks]

3.  Find the maximum area, in m$^2$, of one parcel of land. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $80 = 3x + 4y$

2.  The total area of both parcels of land in terms of $x$ is
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{8em} A(x) &= x(2y) \\[18pt]
    &= 2xy \\[11pt]
    &= 2x\left(\dfrac{80 - 3x}{4}\right) \hspace{3em} [\text{from part (a)}] \\[7pt]
    &= 40x - \dfrac{3}{2}x^2
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  If we sketch the graph of $y = A(x)$ \[by using G.D.C.\], we have

    ![d34855549a850484b1041b3153951956e33882fa.svg](images/d34855549a850484b1041b3153951956e33882fa.svg)

    Hence the maximum area of one parcel of land is
    $\dfrac{266.67}{2} \approx$
     $133 \text{ m}^2$
