# AA188

**Maximum mark: 15**

Let $f(x) = 2x^2-8x+6$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  Write down the value of $f(0)$. [1 mark]

2.  Solve the equation $f(x) = 0$. [3 marks]

The function $f$ can be written in the form $f(x) = a(x-h)^2+k$.

3.  Find the values of $a$, $h$ and $k$. [5 marks]

4.  For the graph of $f$, write down:
    1.  the coordinates of the vertex;

    2.  the equation of the axis of symmetry. [2 marks]

The graph of a function $g$ is obtained from the graph of $f$ by a reflection in the $x$-axis, followed by a translation of 1 unit to the right and 3 units up.

5.  Find $g(x)$, giving your answer in the form
    $g(x) = px^2+qx+r$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $f(0) = 6$

2.  Solving the equation $f(x) = 0$ for $x$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    2x^2-8x+6 &= 0 \\[6pt]
    x^2-4x+3 &= 0 \\[7pt]
    (x-1)(x-3) &= 0 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{6.8em} x = 1,\hspace{0.25em}3
    $$

3.  Using the method of completing of square, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} f(x) &= 2x^2-8x+6 \\[6pt]
    &= 2\big[x^2-4x\big]+6 \\[6pt]
    &= 2\big[(x-2)^2-4\big]+6 \\[6pt]
    &= 2(x-2)^2-2
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find
     $a = 2,\hspace{0.25em}h=2$
    and  $k=-2$

4.
    1.
        $(2,-2)$

    2.
        $x = 2$

5.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{4.4em} g(x) &= -f(x-1)+3 \\[6pt]
    &= -\big[2((x-1)-2)^2-2\big]+3 \\[6pt]
    &= -2(x-3)^2+5 \\[6pt]
    &= -2(x^2-6x+9)+5 \\[6pt]
    &= -2x^2+12x-13
    \end{aligned}
    $$
