# AA1050

**Maximum mark: 6**

Part of the graph of a function, $f$, is shown in the following diagram. The graph of $y=f(x)$ has two horizontal asymptotes, $y=4$ and $y=-2$.

The $x-$intercept of $f(x)$ is $a$ and the $y-$intercept is $-b$.

![question](images/question.png)

Consider the function $g(x)=|f(|x|)|$.

1. Sketch the graph of $y=g(x)$ on top of the image above, labelling any new axis intercepts, in terms of $a$ and $b$, and giving the equation of any new asymptotes. [3 marks]

2. Find the possible values of $r$ such that $(g(x))^2=r$ has exactly two solutions.[3 marks]
