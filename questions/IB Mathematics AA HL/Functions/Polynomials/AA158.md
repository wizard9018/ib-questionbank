# AA158

**Maximum mark: 8**

Two distinct roots for the polynomial equation
$z^4 - 10z^3 + cz^2 + dz + 170$ are
$a + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ and
$1 + b{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}$ where
$a,b,c,d \in \mathbb{Z}$, $b > 0$.

1.  Write down the other two roots in terms of $a$ and $b$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$. [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    $a - {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu},\hspace{0.25em}1 - {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}b$

2.  Using Vieta's formulas, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{sum of roots} &= -\dfrac{a_3}{a_4} \\[6pt]
    (a + \cancel{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}}) + (a - \cancel{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}}) + (1 + \cancel{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}b}) + (1 - \cancel{{\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}b}) &= -\dfrac{(-10)}{1} \hspace{7.5em} \\[12pt]
    2a + 2 &= 10 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{5em} a = 4
    $$
    and
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{product of roots} &= (-1)^4\bigg[\dfrac{a_0}{a_4}\bigg] \\[6pt]
    (4 + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu})(4 - {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu})(1 + {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}b)(1 - {\mathrm{\hspace{0.05em}i}\mkern 1mu}b) &= (-1)^4\bigg[\dfrac{170}{1}\bigg] \hspace{2em} \\[12pt]
    17(1 + b^2) &= 170 \\[16pt]
    1 + b^2 &= 10 \\[16pt]
    b^2 &= 9 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{13.4em} b = 3  \hspace{3em} [\text{since $b > 0$}]
    $$
