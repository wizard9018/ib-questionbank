# AA139

**Maximum mark: 7**

Consider the equation $32x^3 - 144x^2 + 214x - 105 = 0$.

1.  Find the sum and product of the roots of this equation. [1 mark]

2.  The roots of this equation are three consecutive terms of an
    arithmetic\
    sequence. Taking the roots to be $a - d, a, a + d$, solve the
    equation. [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Using Vieta's formulas, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{sum of roots} &= -\dfrac{a_2}{a_3} \\[6pt]
    &= -\dfrac{(-144)}{32} \\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{9}{2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{product of roots} &= (-1)^3\bigg[\dfrac{a_0}{a_3}\bigg] \hspace{0.9em} \\[6pt]
    &= -\dfrac{(-105)}{32} \\[9pt]
    &= \dfrac{105}{32}
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (a-d)+a+(a+d) &= \dfrac{9}{2} \hspace{9.3em} \\[6pt]
    3a &= \dfrac{9}{2} \\[6pt]
    a &= \dfrac{3}{2}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    and

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    a(a-d)(a+d) &= \dfrac{105}{32} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{3}{2}\bigg[\dfrac{9}{4}-d^2\bigg] &= \dfrac{105}{32} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{9}{4}-d^2 &= \dfrac{35}{16} \\[6pt]
    \dfrac{9}{4}-\dfrac{35}{16} &= d^2\\[6pt]
    d^2 &= \dfrac{1}{16} \\[6pt]
    d &= \dfrac{1}{4} \hspace{3em} \bigg[\text{or $-\dfrac{1}{4}$}\bigg]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence the roots of the equation $32x^3 - 144x^2 + 214x - 105 = 0$
    are
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.1em} a - d &= \dfrac{3}{2} - \dfrac{1}{4} = \hspace{0.525em}\dfrac{5}{4} \\[8pt]
    a &=\hspace{0.525em}\dfrac{3}{2} \\[8pt]
    a + d &= \dfrac{3}{2} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \hspace{0.525em}\dfrac{7}{4}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
