# AA144

**Maximum mark: 5**

The quadratic equation $x^2 - kx + (k-1) = 0$ has roots $\alpha$ and
$\beta$. Without solving the equation, find the possible values of the
real number $k$ given that $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = 17$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using Vieta's formulas, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha+\beta &=-\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} \\[4pt]
&= -\dfrac{(-k)}{1} \\[12pt]
&= k
\end{aligned}
$$
and

$$
\begin{aligned}
\hspace{2.9em} \alpha\beta &= (-1)^2\bigg[\dfrac{a_0}{a_2}\bigg] \\[4pt]
&= \dfrac{k-1}{1} \\[12pt]
&= k-1
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence we get

$$
\begin{aligned}
(\alpha + \beta)^2  &= k^2 \\[6pt]
\hspace{5.8em} \alpha^2 + \beta^2 + 2\alpha\beta &= k^2 \\[6pt]
17 + 2(k - 1)  &=  k^2  \hspace{3em} [\text{since $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = 17$}] \\[6pt]
2k + 15  &=  k^2 \\[6pt]
0 &= k^2 - 2k - 15 \\[7pt]
0 &= (k+3)(k-5)\\
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\hspace{1.4em} k = -3,\hspace{0.25em}5
$$
