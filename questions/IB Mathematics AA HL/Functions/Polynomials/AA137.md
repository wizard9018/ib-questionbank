# AA137

**Maximum mark: 6**

Given that $(x-4)$ is a factor of $f(x) = x^3 - 2x^2 + ax + b$ and that
division of $f(x)$ by $(x+2)$ leaves a remainder of $18$, find the value
of $a$ and the value of $b$.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Using the factor theorem, we get
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(4) &= 0 \\[5pt]
4^3 - 2(4)^2 + a(4) + b &= 0 \\[6pt]
32 + 4a + b &= 0 \\[6pt]
4a + b &= -32 \hspace{2em}
\end{aligned}
$$
Using the remainder
theorem, we obtain
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(-2) &= 18 \\[5pt]
(-2)^3 - 2(-2)^2 + a(-2) + b &= 18 \\[6pt]
-16 - 2a + b &= 18 \\[6pt]
-2a + b &= 34  \hspace{6em}
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence, solving the
system of two linear equations $(1)$-$(2)$ for $a$ and $b$, we find

$$
\begin{aligned}
a = -11\hspace{0.525em}\text{ and }\hspace{0.525em}b = 12
\end{aligned}
$$
