# AA169

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = \sqrt{x-3}$, for $x \geq 3$.

1.  Find $f^{-1}(x)$. [2 marks]

Let $g(x) = x^2 +1$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

2.  Find $(g\circ f)(28)$. [2 marks]

3.  Write down the range of:
    1.  $f^{-1}$;

    2.  $g$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we solve the equation $(f\circ f^{-1})(x) = x$ for
    $y = f^{-1}(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(f^{-1}(x)) &= x \\[7pt]
    f(y) &= x \\[5pt]
    \sqrt{y - 3} &= x \\[6pt]
    y - 3 &= x^2 \\[6pt]
    y &= x^2 + 3 \hspace{1.7em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    f^{-1}(x) = x^2 + 3 \hspace{0.4em}
    $$

2.  Evaluating $f(x) = \sqrt{x - 3}$ for $x = 28$, we have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1em} f(28) &= \sqrt{28-3} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{25} \\[8pt]
    &= 5
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence we find

    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (g\circ f)(28) &= g(f(28)) \\[7pt]
    &= g(5) \\[6pt]
    &= 5^2 +1 \hspace{2.2em} \\[7pt]
    &= 26
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.
    1.
        $[3,\infty)$ or $y \geq 3$

    2.
        $[1,\infty)$ or $y \geq 1$
