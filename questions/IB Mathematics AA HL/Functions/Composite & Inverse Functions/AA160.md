# AA160

**Maximum mark: 7**

Let $f(x) = x^3 + 1$ and $g(x) = x-2$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  Find $f(2)$. [2 marks]

2.  Find $f^{-1}(x)$. [2 marks]

3.  Solve $(f\circ g)(x) = 0$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Evaluating $f(x) = x^3 + 1$ for $x = 2$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{2.6em} f(2) &= 2^3 + 1 \\[6pt]
    &= 8 + 1 \\[6pt]
    &= 9
    \end{aligned}
    $$

2.  Solving the equation $(f\circ f^{-1})(x) = x$ for $y = f^{-1}(x)$,
    we find
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{0.6em} f(f^{-1}(x)) &= x \\[6pt]
    f(y) &= x \\[6pt]
    y^3 + 1 &= x \\[6pt]
    y^3 &= x - 1 \\[5pt]
    y &= \sqrt[3]{x-1} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{2em} f^{-1}(x) = \sqrt[3]{x-1}
    $$

3.  Solving the equation $(f\circ g)(x) = 0$ for $x$, we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(g(x)) &= 0 \\[7pt]
    f(x-2) &= 0 \\[6pt]
    \hspace{4.6em} (x-2)^3 + 1 &= 0 \\[6pt]
    x-2 &= \sqrt[3]{-1} \hspace{3em} [=-1] \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{1.7em} x = 1
    $$
