# AA175

**Maximum mark: 6**

The following diagram shows the graph of $y = f(x)$, for
$-4 \leq x \leq 5$.

![8dc73726e7704b5f5e31e8ab0e8d2ebbdc109321.svg](images/8dc73726e7704b5f5e31e8ab0e8d2ebbdc109321.svg)

1.  Write down the value of:
    1.  $f(1)$;

    2.  $f^{-1}(2)$. [2 marks]

2.  Find the domain of $f^{-1}$. [2 marks]

3.  Sketch the graph of $y = f^{-1}(x)$ on the same grid above.
    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
        $f(1) = 3$

    2.
        $f^{-1}(2) = 0$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{domain of $f^{-1}$} &= \text{range of $f$} \hspace{1.7em} \\[6pt]
    &= [-2,4]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

3.  The graph of $y = f^{-1}(x)$ is a reflection of the graph of
    $y = f(x)$ across the line $y = x$.
