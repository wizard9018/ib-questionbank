# AA174

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = (x+2)^3$, for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

1.  Find $f^{-1}(x)$. [3 marks]

Let $g$ be a function so that $(f\circ g)(x) = 27x^6$.

2.  Find $g(x)$. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we solve the equation $(f\circ f^{-1})(x) = x$ for
    $y = f^{-1}(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(f^{-1}(x)) &= x \\[7pt]
    f(y) &= x \\[6pt]
    (y+2)^3 &= x \\[6pt]
    y+2 &= \sqrt[3]{x} \\[6pt]
    y &= \sqrt[3]{x} - 2 \hspace{1.1em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{0.3em} f^{-1}(x) = \sqrt[3]{x} - 2
    $$

2.  If we solve the equation $(f\circ g)(x) = 27x^6$ for $y = g(x)$, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(g(x)) &= 27x^6 \\[6pt]
    f(y) &= 27x^6 \\[6pt]
    (y+2)^3 &= 27x^6 \\[6pt]
    y+2 &= 3x^2 \\[6pt]
    y &= 3x^2 - 2 \hspace{0.1em} \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{1.5em} g(x) = 3x^2 - 2
    $$
