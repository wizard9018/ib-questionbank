# AA125

**Maximum mark: 6**

The function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = \sqrt[3]{2x+1}$, for
$-14 \leq x \leq 13$.

1.  Write down the range of $f$. [2 marks]

2.  Find an expression for $f^{-1}$. [2 marks]

3.  Write down the domain and range of $f^{-1}$. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  We have
    1 $f$ is continuous and increasing;

    2 $f(-14) = -3$ and $f(13) = 3$.

    Hence the range of $f$ is
     $[-3,3]$

2.  If we solve the equation $(f\circ f^{-1})(x) = x$ for
    $y = f^{-1}(x)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(f^{-1}(x)) &= x \\[13pt]
    f\left(y\right) &= x \\[12pt]
    \sqrt[3]{2y+1} &= x \\[12pt]
    2y+1 &= x^3 \\[6pt]
    y &= \dfrac{x^3 - 1}{2} \hspace{1.3em}
    \end{aligned}
    $$
    Hence an
    expression for $f^{-1}$ is
     $f^{-1}(x) = \dfrac{x^3 - 1}{2}$

3.  The domain and range of $f^{-1}$ are
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \text{domain}: & \hspace{0.75em} \hspace{0.525em}[-3,3] \\[8pt]
    \text{range}: & \hspace{0.75em} \hspace{0.525em}[-14,13]
    \end{aligned}
    $$
