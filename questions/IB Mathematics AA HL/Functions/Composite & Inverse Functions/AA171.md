# AA171

**Maximum mark: 6**

Let $f(x) = \sqrt{x+7}$, for $x \geq -7$.

1.  Find $f^{-1}(3)$. [3 marks]

Let $g$ be a function such that $g^{-1}$ exists for all real numbers.

2.  Given that $g(9) = 4$, find $(f\circ g^{-1})(4)$.
    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we solve the equation $(f\circ f^{-1})(3) = 3$ for
    $y = f^{-1}(3)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    f(f^{-1}(3)) &= 3 \hspace{3.8em} \\[6pt]
    f(y) &= 3 \\[4pt]
    \sqrt{y + 7} &= 3 \\[6pt]
    y + 7 &= 9 \\[6pt]
    y &= 2 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    f^{-1}(3) = 2 \hspace{2.4em}
    $$

2.  We have
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    4 &= g(9) \\[6pt]
    g^{-1}(4) &= g^{-1}(g(9)) \\[6pt]
    \hspace{11em} g^{-1}(4) &= 9 \hspace{3.5em} [\text{since $(g^{-1}\hspace{-0.1em}\circ g)(9) = 9$}]
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    Hence we obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    (f\circ g^{-1})(4) &= f(g^{-1}(4)) \hspace{0.9em} \\[8pt]
    &= f(9) \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{9+7} \\[6pt]
    &= \sqrt{16} \\[8pt]
    &= 4
    \end{aligned}
    $$
