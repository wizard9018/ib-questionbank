# AA1063

**Maximum mark: 5**

Consider the real polynomial $f(x)=x^5-5.5x^4-...-604.662$.

The roots of the polynomial form a geometric sequence with first term $u_1$ and common ratio $r$.

Where $u_1>0$ and $r<-1$.

1. Find two distinct functions for $u_1$ in terms of $r$. [3 marks]

2. Hence find $u_1$ and $r$.[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The roots of $f(x)$ form the following geometric sequence $u_1, u_1r, u_1r^2, u_1r^3, u_1r^4$.

    Hence we can write the sum of the roots as $u_1\tfrac{r^5-1}{r-1}$ and the product of the roots as $u_1^5r^{10}$.

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    \tag*{\textbf{(M1)}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    We can find the sum and product of the roots of $f$ using the formula, hence

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

        -\dfrac{(-5.5)}{1}&=u_1\left(\frac{r^5-1}{r-1}\right) &(-1)^5\dfrac{(-604.662)}{1}=u_1^5r^{10} \tag*{\textbf{(A1)}}\\

    \end{aligned}

    $$

    Therefore

    $$

    \begin{aligned}

    u_1&=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{\frac{5.5(r-1)}{r^5-1}} &u_1=\hspace{0.525em}\boxed{\sqrt[5]{\frac{604.662}{r^{10}}}} \tag*{\textbf{A1}}

    \end{aligned}

    $$
