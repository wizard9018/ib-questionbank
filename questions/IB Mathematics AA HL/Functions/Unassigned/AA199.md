# AA199

**Maximum mark: 8**

Let $f(x) = e^{3\sin \left(\frac{\pi x}{4}\right)}$, for $x > 0$.

The $k$th maximum point on the graph of $f$ has $x$-coordinate $x_k$,
where $k \in \mathbb{Z}^+$.

1.  Given that $x_{k+1} = x_k + d$, find $d$. [4 marks]

2.  Hence find the value of $n$ such that
    $\displaystyle \sum_{k=1}^n x_k = 992$. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  If we draw the graph of $y= f(x)$ \[by using G.D.C.\], we have

    ![97f38ce996ffbe7f9b884196663bd4ac3db522d5.svg](images/97f38ce996ffbe7f9b884196663bd4ac3db522d5.svg)

    Hence, using the $x$-coordinates of the points A$(2,20.1)$ and
    B$(10,20.1)$, we get
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \hspace{1.7em} x_2 &= x_1 + d \\[6pt]
    10 &= 2 + d \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    d = 8
    $$

2.  We have an arithmetic sequence with the first term $x_1 = 2$ and the
    common difference $d = 8$.

    Hence, using the sum of $n$ terms formula
    $S_n = \dfrac{n}{2}(2x_1+(n-1)d)$, we
    obtain
    $$
    \begin{aligned}
    \sum_{k=1}^n x_k &= 992 \\[6pt]
    S_n &= 992 \hspace{3em} [\text{with $x_1 = 2$ \& $d = 8$}] \\[13pt]
    \hspace{4em} \dfrac{n}{2}(2(2)+(n-1)(8)) &= 992 \\
    \end{aligned}
    $$

    $$
    \hspace{10.1em} n = 16 \hspace{3em} [\text{by using G.D.C.}]
    $$
