# BI2836

**Maximum mark: 9**

A student investigates the effect of exercise on cardiovascular function by measuring a volunteer’s blood pressure and heart rate before, during, and after cycling on a stationary bike.

![BI2836a](images/BI2836a.png)

1. Deduce the time at which post-workout recovery appears to begin. Use evidence from the graph to support your answer. [2 marks]

2. Compare and contrast the changes in systolic and diastolic pressure shown in this graph. [2 marks]

3. Identify one reason for this change in systolic pressure during exercise. [1 mark]

4. Heart rate can be measured by manually taking a pulse. State the name of one of the arteries which is usually used for measuring the pulse rate [1 mark]

5. Explain how the valves in the heart are opened and closed during the cardiac cycle. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. **both** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ 10-15 minutes [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ heart rate **OR** diastolic pressure is elevated until that time and then begins to fall, indicating the exercise has stopped [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The volunteer exercised starting at 0 minutes and continued until **10 minutes** ($\checkmark$) because the heart rate and pressure **increased** until 10 minutes and **then began to decrease**, which means they stopped exercising ($\checkmark$).

2. any **two** of the following (*must include **one** similarity and **one** difference to gain full marks*):

 ***Similarities***:

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** pressures increase during exercise [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** pressures start **AND** finish at baseline levels before exercise [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** pressures peak at the end of exercise [1 mark]

 ***Differences***:

 $\ce{ -> }$ systolic pressure shows a large increase **OR** increases significantly **OR** increases by 60 mmHg **OR** by 50% **OR** by half **WHEREAS** diastolic pressure remains relatively constant **OR** increases slightly **OR** increases by 10 mmHg **OR** by 1/8 [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ systolic pressure starts at a higher pressure THAN diastolic pressure **OR** systolic pressure baseline is ~120 mmHg W**WHEREAS** diastolic pressure baseline is ~80 mmHg [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 **Both** systolic and diastolic pressure **increase during exercise**, which shows that the cardiovascular system responds to the increased demand for oxygen ($\checkmark$). However, **systolic** pressure shows a **large increase** of about **60 mmHg**, whereas **diastolic** pressure **increases** only **slightly** by about **10 mmHg** ($\checkmark$).

3. any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ increase demand for oxygen [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ increase cardiac output [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ increased stroke volume **OR** heart rate [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ more blood is ejected into arteries during systole **OR** systolic pressure is caused by ventricular contractions [1 mark]

 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The systolic pressure increases during exercise because the **heart pumps faster** and with a **higher volume** ($\checkmark$) of blood with each pump to deliver more oxygen to muscles.

4. any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ carotid [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ radial [1 mark]

 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 One of the arteries used for measuring pulse rate is the **carotid** artery ($\checkmark$).

5. any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ during atrial systole **OR** when atria contract pressure increase causing atrioventricular **OR** AV **OR** tricuspid **OR** bicuspid valves to open [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ during ventricular systole OR when ventricles contract, pressure increases, forcing atrioventricular **OR** AV **OR** tricuspid **OR** bicuspid valves shut [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ during ventricular systole **OR** when ventricles contract **OR** as ventricular pressure increases further, semilunar valves open [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ during diastole, **OR** as ventricles relax, pressure decreases, closing semilunar valves [1 mark]

 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 During **atrial** systole, the **atria contract**, **increasing** the **pressure** in them, which **forces** the **atrioventricular valves open** ($\checkmark$). As the blood passes into the ventricles, the atria relax and the **ventricles contract, increasing** the **pressure** inside them. This increase in pressure inside the ventricles forces the **atrioventricular valves shut** ($\checkmark$) and the **semilunar valves** in the pulmonary artery and aorta **open** ($\checkmark$).
