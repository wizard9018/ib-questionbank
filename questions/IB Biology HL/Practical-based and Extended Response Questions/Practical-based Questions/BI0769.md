# BI0769

**Maximum mark: 10**

An investigation was carried out into the effects of household detergents on aquatic habitats. Scientists tested the effect of different household detergents on the surface tension of water in order to better understand the impact they may have on organisms living on the surface.

A paper clip was folded slightly on one side and rested on the surface of the water, maintained at a constant temperature and contained within a water tank. Drops of detergent solution were slowly added to the water tank until the paperclip sank to the bottom. The volume of detergent solution taken to sink the paperclip was recorded.

Detergent solutions were made by adding 5 ml of detergent to 15 ml of water.

![BI0769A](images/BI0769A.png)

The results are shown in the table.

![BI0769B](images/BI0769B.png)

1. State the relationship between the volume of detergent solution added and the surface tension of water. [1 mark]

2.
 1. For the **Easy Clean** detergent, calculate the mean volume of detergent needed to sink the paper clip. [1 mark]

 2. Estimate the volume of **Easy Clean** detergent solution it would take to sink 5 paper clips. [1 mark]

3.
 1. State **one** variable, other than those given, which would need to be controlled in this experiment in order to achieve a valid result. [1 mark]

 2. Suggest **one** method that could be used to keep the water at a constant temperature. [1 mark]

 3. Explain the importance of maintaining water at a constant temperature. [2 marks]

 4. Identify **one** potential source of error in the methodology. [1 mark]

4. Suggest why different numbers of drops of detergent solution were needed to sink the paperclip between each type of detergent. [2 marks]
