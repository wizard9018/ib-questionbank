# BI0777

**Maximum mark: 3**

The following image shows the productivity of a small terrestrial ecosystem with the numbers showing the flow of energy (kJ m:sup[-2] yr:sup[-1]).

![BI0777CORA](images/BI0777CORA.png)

Net production = Gross production – energy lost to respiration

1. Outline how light energy is sequentially transformed into other forms of energy in the terrestrial ecosystem shown in the image. [2 marks]

2. Calculate the percentage of energy captured by the producers that is available to the secondary consumers in the ecosystem shown above. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ light energy $\ce{ -> }$ chemical energy [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ chemical energy $\ce{ -> }$ heat energy [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ light energy from the sun is converted to chemical energy in organic molecules during photosynthesis [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ in respiration chemical energy in organic molecules is converted to chemical energy in ATP [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ during respiration some chemical energy is converted to heat energy [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 During photosynthesis **light energy** from the Sun **is converted into chemical energy** ($\checkmark$) and then during **respiration** some of the **chemical energy is converted into heat energy** ($\checkmark$).
