# BI2066

**Maximum mark: 14**

Germinating seeds can respire aerobically or anaerobically. A student used the apparatus shown below to measure the rate of respiration in germinating seeds, by measuring the volume of $\ce{CO_2}$ produced.

They:

- positioned the apparatus in a water bath at 25 $\degree$C
- left the apparatus for one hour before starting the investigation
- placed the apparatus in complete darkness.

![BI2066](images/BI2066.png)

1. State **two** reasons why the apparatus was maintained at 25$\degree$C. [2 marks]

2. Give **one** reason why it was important that the student left the apparatus for one hour, excluding any temperature-related factors. [1 mark]

3. Suggest why it was important that the apparatus was placed in complete darkness. [1 mark]

Every 10 minutes a reading was taken from the apparatus shown above, and the coloured liquid was reset.

Below is a graph showing the results.

![BI2066b](images/BI2066b.png)

4. Suggest why the rate of $\ce{CO_2}$ produced decreased after 2.5 hours. [1 mark]

The rate of aerobic respiration in germinating seeds can be calculated by repeating the experiment above but using potassium hydroxide (KOH) instead of alkaline pyrogallol. This time the coloured liquid moved to the left.

5. Explain why the coloured liquid moved to the left. [3 marks]

The student wanted to calculate the rate of oxygen uptake in this experiment. They reset the coloured liquid and timed its movement for 5 minutes. The distance moved along the capillary tubing was measured as 12cm, and the diameter as 2mm

6. Using 3.14 for the value of $\pi$, and the equation provided below,calculate the rate of oxygen uptake in cm$^3$ min$^{-1}$, giving your answer to three significant figures. Show your working. [2 marks]

volume of cylinder = $\pi$ r$^{2}$ x h

7. Explain why, in plants, the rate of $\ce{CO_2}$ production during aerobic respiration is different to the rate during anaerobic respiration. [2 marks]

8. Suggest, with a reason, what difference would be expected if small invertebrates were used in this second experiment instead of germinating seeds. [2 marks]
