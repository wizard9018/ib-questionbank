# BI2886

**Maximum mark: 7**

A student extracted photosynthetic pigments from hibiscus leaves and separated them using thin layer chromatography. Their results are shown in the diagram, and the table gives the Rf values, along with the colours, of some of the pigments.

![BI2886ai](images/BI2886ai.png)

| | Pigment | Rf value | Colour
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$ | β-carotene | 0.94 | yellow-orange |
| $\hspace{1em}$ | Chlorophyll a | 0.68 | blue-green |
| $\hspace{1em}$ | Chlorophyll b | 0.47 | green |
| $\hspace{1em}$ | Xanthophylls | 0.32 - 0.38 | yellow|

1. 1. Calculate the Rf value of spot Y, giving your answer to significant figures. Show your working. [2 marks]

 2. Identify pigment Y. [1 mark]

2. The solvent used in the experiment was non-polar. Identify the spot that is most polar, and give a reason for your choice. [1 mark]

The graph shows an action spectrum for photosynthetic pigments.

![BI2886bi](images/BI2886bi.png)

3. From the graph, identify the wavelength of light at which the rate of photosynthesis is lowest. [1 mark]

4. Outline the reason for this low photosynthesis rate. [2 marks]
