# BI0782

**Maximum mark: 10**

The graph shows the mean blood glucose levels in a study of 300 people, 10 minutes after eating a meal and 3 hours after eating a meal. Of the 300 people studied, 100 people were non-diabetic, 100 people had untreated Type 1 diabetes and 100 people had untreated Type 2 diabetes.

![BI0782A](images/BI0782A.png)

1. Compare and contrast the blood glucose levels of people without diabetes and those with diabetes. [2 marks]

2. Suggest one variable, other than those already mentioned in the question, that needs to be controlled to make this study valid. [1 mark]

3. Predict how the mean blood glucose concentration after 3 hours would change if the Type 1 diabetics were injected with insulin 15 minutes after eating. [1 mark]

4.
 1. Explain the effect of insulin on blood glucose levels. [2 marks]

 2. State the name of the cells that make and secrete insulin. [1 mark]

5. Calculate the percentage change between the blood glucose concentration of people with Type 2 diabetes 10 minutes after eating a meal and 3 hours after eating a meal. [1 mark]

6. Explain why Type 2 diabetics have a lower blood glucose concentration 3 hours after eating a meal, compared to Type 1 diabetics. [2 marks]

6. Any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ Type 1 diabetes is caused by an inability to produce insulin **OR** in Type 1 diabetes there is destruction of ꞵ cells that produce insulin **OR** with Type 2 diabetes there is reduced ability of the body to respond or process insulin **OR** with Type 2 diabetes there is a deficiency of insulin receptors **OR** glucose transporters [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
