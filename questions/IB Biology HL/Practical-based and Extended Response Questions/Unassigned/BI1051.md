# BI1051

**Maximum mark: 16**

1. Outline the binomial naming system and its importance.     [4 marks]

2. Explain how a sample of cells taken from a foetus can be used to determine whether the foetus will be biologically male or biologically female at birth.        [5 marks]

3. Outline the outcomes of the human genome project and their impacts on different fields of science. [7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **four** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ binomial naming system as the system used to name species     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ genus name and species name     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ species with the same genus share many characteristics **OR** are closely related    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ internationally agreed        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ facilitates understanding and communication between scientists     [1 mark]
    [4 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The binomial naming system is the system which scientists use to **name species** ($\checkmark$) and this is **internationally agreed** ($\checkmark$), which means it's used all over the world, which aids in the **communication** between scientists ($\checkmark$) in different countries. The name of each species has two parts, the first highlights the **genus** to which it belongs and the second part refers to the **species** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **five** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ cells grown in a culture medium [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ cells stained [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ observed under a light microscope [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ photomicrograph taken during mitosis **OR** mitotic metaphase [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ chromosomes arranged according to size **OR** position of centromeres [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ look at chromosome pair 23 [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ two large chromosomes mean foetus is XX [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ one large chromosome and one small chromosome means foetus is XY [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ XX is biologically female **AND** XY is biologically male [1 mark]
    [5 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Once the sample of cells has been taken from the foetus they are grown in a **culture medium** ($\checkmark$). They are then **stained** ($\checkmark$) and observed under a **light microscope** ($\checkmark$). A **photomicrograph** is taken ($\checkmark$), and the image is cut up so that the **chromosomes** can be **arranged by size** and position of the **centromere** ($\checkmark$). A foetus with **two large X** chromosomes will be **biologically female** ($\checkmark$), and a foetus with **one large X** and **one small Y** chromosome will be **biologically male** ($\checkmark$).

3. any **seven** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ the Human Genome Project (HGP) sequenced the entire human genome    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ it determined the total number of base pairs in the human genome    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ it estimated the number of genes in the human genome             [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ identified the loci of genes    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ identified repeat sequences     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ identified locations of single nucleotide polymorphisms            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ identified gene sequences associated with certain diseases **OR** biological processes **OR** traits    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ HGP stimulated further development **OR** reduced the price of sequencing technology    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ technology developed for the HGP is now used to diagnose diseases **OR** identify micro-organisms OR DNA matching in forensics and medicine **OR** genetic modification  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ contributed to the field by identifying genetic markers that affect how individuals metabolise and respond to medications **OR** contribute information for personalised medicine        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ insights into evolutionary relationships **OR** genetic similarities **OR** genetic differences [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ identification of regulatory elements **OR** non-coding RNAs **OR** components that play essential roles in gene expression **OR** cellular function  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ emphasised importance of consideration of ethical **OR** legal **OR** social implications of genomic research  [1 mark]
    [7 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The Human Genome Project (HGP) **sequenced** the entire human genome ($\checkmark$), **identified the loci** of genes ($\checkmark$) and identified gene sequences that are connected to some **diseases** ($\checkmark$) such as breast cancer. Locations of **single nucleotide polymorphisms** ($\checkmark$) were discovered which are variations in individuals DNA base pairs. The results have contributed to the development of **personalised medicine** ($\checkmark$) as genetic markers have been identified that affect how individuals respond to medications. The technology developed for the HGP has led to further advancements in technology, **reducing the cost** of genetic sequencing ($\checkmark$).  The HGP also emphasised how important it is to consider the **ethical implications** ($\checkmark$) of genomic research and the importance of informed consent.
