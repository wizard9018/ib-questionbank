# ​​BI1356

\[Maximum marks: 16\]

1. Distinguish between obligate aerobes, obligate anaerobes, and facultative anaerobes. [3 marks]

2. Describe holozoic nutrition in animals. [5 marks]

3. Using named examples, outline the relationship between dentition, musculature, and diet in herbivorous and omnivorous members of the family *Hominidae*. [8 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. \

    $\ce{ -> }$ obligate aerobes require oxygen for respiration **OR** respire aerobically **OR** carry out aerobic respiration, **OR** require oxygen to grow **OR** survive, **OR** rely on aerobic respiration        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ obligate anaerobes do not require oxygen for respiration **OR** respire anaerobically **OR** respire in the absence of oxygen, **OR** are poisoned by oxygen-rich environments, **OR** cannot survive **OR** grow in the presence of oxygen [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ facultative aerobes can respire aerobically or anaerobically **OR** can respire in the presence or absence of oxygen, **OR** can survive **OR** grow in the presence or absence of oxygen [1 mark] 

[3 marks]

:u[Sample answer]:

Obligate aerobes **require oxygen** to respire and grow ($\checkmark$), whereas obligate anaerobes do **not require oxygen** to respire ($\checkmark$) and are, in fact, poisoned by oxygen due to a lack of defence mechanisms against oxidation. Facultative aerobes can respire **with or without oxygen** ($\checkmark$), they have the ability to respire aerobically and anaerobically.

2. any **five** of the following:

    

    $\ce{ -> }$ food is ingested            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ food is digested internally [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ food is absorbed [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ food is assimilated [1 mark] 

    $\ce{ -> }$ undigested **OR** indigestible food is egested [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ herbivores **OR** primary consumers feed on plant material     [1 mark] 

    $\ce{ -> }$ carnivores **OR** omnivores **OR** secondary consumers **OR** any other trophic level above secondary consumers feed on animal material     [1 mark] 

[5 marks]

:u[Sample answer]:

Holozoic nutrition refers to a mode of nutrition in which an organism **ingests** ($\checkmark$) the food and then **digests it internally** ($\checkmark$). After digestion, the food is **absorbed** ($\checkmark$) and then **assimilated** ($\checkmark$). **Herbivores** consume plant **material**($\checkmark$), and **carnivores** consume **animal material**($\checkmark$). 

3. any **eight** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ herbivorous *Hominids* consume plants **AND** omnivorous hominids eat plants and animals        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ example of herbivorous *Hominids* e.g. gorilla (*Gorrila gorrila*) 

    [1 mark] 

    $\ce{ -> }$ herbivorous *Hominids* have large masseter muscles to grind food     

[1 mark] 

    $\ce{ -> }$ herbivorous *Hominids* have large temporal muscles that allow for forcible bites     

[1 mark]             

    $\ce{ -> }$ herbivorous *Hominids* have larger **OR** broader premolars **OR** molars for grinding plant matter 

[1 mark]      

    $\ce{ -> }$ herbivorous Hominids can use their incisors to bite chunks of vegetation 

[1 mark] 

    $\ce{ -> }$ herbivorous and omnivorous Hominids can have large canines for social interactions (e.g. fighting or ritual displays)

[1 mark] 

    $\ce{ -> }$ example of omnivorous *Hominidae* e.g. chimpanzee

[1 mark] 

    $\ce{ -> }$ omnivorous *Hominids* have relatively smaller masseter muscles

[1 mark] 

    $\ce{ -> }$ omnivorous *Hominids* have relatively smaller temporal muscles 

[1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ omnivorous *Hominids* can use canines to grab and tear prey     

[1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ omnivorous *Hominids* can use incisors to bite chunks from prey     

[1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ omnivorous *Hominids* have relatively smaller premolars **OR** molars 

[1 mark]

[8 marks]

 :u[Sample answer]:

**Herbivorous Hominids** such as the **gorilla** ($\checkmark$) have **large masseter** muscles ($\checkmark$) and **molars** to help them **grind vegetation** ($\checkmark$). They also have **large temporal** muscles, which allow them to **bite down** on chunks of vegetation with force ($\checkmark$). **Omnivorous** *Hominids*, such as **chimpanzees** ($\checkmark$), have relatively **smaller** **masseter** muscles ($\checkmark$) and **molars** ($\checkmark$) compared to gorillas. Unlike gorillas, chimpanzees can use their **canines** to **hold prey** or **tear flesh** ($\checkmark$). They can also use their **incisors** to **bite chunks** out of their prey ($\checkmark$).
