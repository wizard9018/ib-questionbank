# BI0783

**Maximum mark: 10**

The graphs show the results from a long-term study on 86 pairs of monozygotic twins, over 80 years.

![BI0783A](images/BI0783A.png)

![BI0783B](images/BI0783B.png)

1. Describe the relationship between the percentage similarity in DNA methylation pattern and age. [2 marks]

2. Explain the relationship that you referred to in part (a). [1 mark]

3. Explain the effect that DNA methylation most likely has on gene expression. [1 mark]

4. Explain why monozygotic twins were used in this study. [2 marks]

5. Suggest one limitation of conducting a study in humans over 80 years. [1 mark]

6. Evaluate whether this study provides evidence that decreases in the percentage of DNA methylation is a predictor of an early death. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ as age increases, the percentage similarity in the DNA methylation pattern decreases **OR** there is a negative correlation between age and percentage similarity in the DNA methylation pattern [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ between the ages of 20 - 40, the percentage similarity in DNA methylation pattern remains relatively stable **OR** the percentage similarity changed the most between ages 0 - 20 **OR** the greatest change was between 10 - 20 years [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 As **age increases**, the percentage similarity in the DNA methylation pattern **decreases** ($\checkmark$), however between the ages of **20 - 40**, the percentage similarity remains **relatively stable** ($\checkmark$), at approximately 82%.

2. Any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ differences in environment **OR** changes in lifestyle **OR** diet **OR** exposure to toxins cause differential expression of genes **OR** in the epigenome [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ errors in DNA replication **OR** damage to DNA over time [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Monozygotic twins have more differences in their patterns of methylation as they age, as the **differences**in their **environments** cause **changes** in their **gene expression** patterns ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ downregulates gene expression by inhibiting transcription **OR** preventing transcription factors from binding **OR** silences gene expression [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Direct DNA methylation will most likely **downregulate** gene expression by **inhibiting** gene **transcription** ($\checkmark$).

4. Any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ allows for the control of genetic variation [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ identical twins **OR** monozygotic twins are often raised in the same environment so helps isolate the effects of age [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ born with the same sequence of genes **OR** share the same genetic material **OR** develop from a single fertilised ovum **OR** develop from the same zygote that splits [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ can be used to study how the environment and genes interact **OR** the epigenome [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Monozygotic twins were used in this study, as they are born with the **same alleles** ($\checkmark$), allowing for the **control of genetic variation**, so studies conducted on them can be used to study how **genes and the environment interact** ($\checkmark$).

5. Any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ researchers cannot control the subject’s lifestyle **OR** difficulty in controlling environmental factors [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ subjects may die before the age of 80 [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ small sample size [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 One limitation of conducting a human study over 80 years is that the subjects may **die before the age of 80** ($\checkmark$).
