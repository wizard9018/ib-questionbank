# BI0812

**Maximum mark: 10**

The table shows the results from a study on the percentage of cholesterol found in the cell membrane of epithelial skin cells of different animals.

![BI0812A](images/BI0812A.png)

1. Calculate the mean percentage of cholesterol found in the cell membrane across all the animals listed in the table. [1 mark]

2. State one role of cholesterol in the cell membrane. [1 mark]

3. Suggest why arctic foxes have a greater mean percentage of cholesterol in their cell membranes than kangaroos. [2 marks]

4. Estimate, with a reason, the percentage of cholesterol that would be found in the cell membranes of polar bears. [1 mark]

5. Explain why the mass of cholesterol in the cell membranes is calculated as a percentage rather than a whole number. [2 marks]

6. Suggest why the cells studied may contain a higher percentage of cholesterol than shown in the table. [1 mark]

7.
 1. Based on the data provided, suggest which animal in the study exhibits the highest fluidity in its epithelial skin cell membrane. [1 mark]

 2. Explain your answer to part (i). [1 mark]
