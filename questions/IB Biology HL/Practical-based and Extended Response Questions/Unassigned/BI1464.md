1.  Compare and contrast the **structure** of cardiac muscle cells and skeletal muscle cells.    [4 marks]

2.  Within the right atrium wall is a group of cardiac cells with the ability to initiate an action potential. State the name of those groups of cells.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Any **four** of the following\

    *Answer must include at least **one** point from similarities and **one** from differences to gain full marks*

    **Similarities**\

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** are striated    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have a similar arrangement of myosin **AND** actin    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** contain many mitochondria    [1 mark]

    **Differences**\

    $\ce{ -> }$ cardiac muscles are shorter and wider **THAN** skeletal muscle cells **OR** skeletal muscle cells are longer and narrower **THAN** cardiac muscle cells    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ cardiac muscles can have one nucleus **OR** two nuclei per cell **WHEREAS** skeletal muscle cells are multinucleated    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ cardiac muscles are branched **WHEREAS** skeletal muscles are unbranched    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ s there are gap junctions **OR** intercalated discs between cardiac muscle cells **WHEREAS** there are no gap junctions **OR** intercalated discs between skeletal muscle cells [1 mark]

    [4 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

    Both skeletal and cardiac muscle cells are **striated** ($\checkmark$)  and contain **many mitochondria** ($\checkmark$). However, cardiac muscle cells are **branched**, while skeletal muscle cells are **linear** ($\checkmark$), and cardiac muscle cells only have **one or two nuclei per cell**, whereas skeletal muscle cells are **multinucleated** ($\checkmark$).

2.  Any **one** of the following

    $\ce{ -> }$ sinoatrial **OR** SA node [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ pacemaker [1 mark]

    [1 mark]

    

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

    The **sinoatrial node**($\checkmark$).
