# BI1123

**Maximum mark: 10**

Below is a graph showing the gestation periods and body masses of four different animals.

![image](images/image.png)

1. Describe the relationship between body mass and the gestation period. [1 mark]

2. Using the data, deduce, with a reason, whether a rabbit would have a more similar gestational period to a hamster or an elephant. [1 mark]

3. State one method, other than body mass, to measure body size of animals. [1 mark]

4. The foetus of a mammal that develops a placenta can remain in the uterus to a later stage of development than in mammals that do not. State one substance that passes from the biological mother to the foetus that helps their development. [1 mark]

5. Humans have a longer gestation period than organisms with similar body mass. State the term used to indicate extreme data points that stand out and do not fit the pattern of a graph. [1 mark]

6. Determine the gestation period for giraffes from the graph. [1 mark]

7. Explain the roles of the main hormones that, when secreted, help bring about childbirth in humans. [4 marks]

7. Any **four** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ oxytocin stimulates **OR** induces **OR** regulates **OR** causes more frequent **OR** more vigorous uterine contractions [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ prostaglandins soften cervix **OR** prepare cervix for labour **OR** help cause dilation of cervix [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ prostaglandins help initiate uterine contractions [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ relaxin relaxes the ligaments in the pelvis **OR** helps soften **OR** widen cervix [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ oestrogen enhances sensitivity of uterine muscle cells to oxytocin **OR** increases number of oxytocin receptors in uterus wall [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ positive feedback loop continues until baby born [1 mark]
 [4 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 **Oxytocin** ($\checkmark$) and **prostaglandins** stimulate **contractions** of the **uterine wall** ($\checkmark$). **Prostaglandins** also help **soften** the **cervix** allowing it to **dilate** ($\checkmark$). **Oestrogen** enhances the **sensitivity** of the **uterine muscle** cells to **oxytocin** ($\checkmark$). As **contractions** become more **intense**, they help **dilate** the **cervix** and aid the **descent** of the **baby** through the birth canal ($\checkmark$).
