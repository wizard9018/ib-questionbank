# BI1355

**Maximum mark: 16**

1. Compare and contrast type I and type II pneumocytes.        [4 marks]

2. Explain how human gametes are adapted for their reproductive roles.        [5 marks]

3. Explain the properties and location of stem cells and distinguish between totipotent, pluripotent and multipotent stem cells.    [7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **four** of the following: \
Answer must include at least one similarity **and** one difference to receive full marks.

    ***Similarities:*** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ type I **AND** type II pneumocytes are **BOTH** found in the alveoli **OR** the lungs **OR** form part of the alveolar epithelium    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ type I **AND** type II pneumocytes are **BOTH** adapted for their functions        [1 mark]

    ***Differences:*** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ type I pneumocytes have a flattened shape **WHEREAS** type II pneumocytes have a cuboidal **OR** rounded shape            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ type I pneumocytes have minimal cytoplasm **OR** organelles **WHEREAS** type II pneumocytes have more cytoplasm **OR** organelles **OR** contain lamellar bodies
type I pneumocytes are adapted for diffusion of gases **WHEREAS** type II pneumocytes are not adapted for diffusion    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ type II pneumocytes produce surfactants **WHEREAS** type I pneumocytes do not produce surfactants        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ type I pneumocytes are approximately 95% of the surface of alveoli **WHEREAS** type II pneumocytes are approximately 5% of the surface of alveoli **OR** type I pneumocytes are the majority of the alveolar lining **WHEREAS** type II pneumocytes are only a small percentage of the alveolar lining            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ type I pneumocytes have limited regenerative capacity **OR** are more vulnerable to damage **WHEREAS** type II pneumocytes can regenerate **OR** can differentiate into type I pneumocytes [1 mark]
    [4 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Type I and type II pneumocytes are **both found in the alveoli** ($\checkmark$). Type **I pneumocytes are thin**, whereas **type II pneumocytes are cuboidal** ($\checkmark$). This is because **type I** pneumocytes are adapted for **gaseous exchange** ($\checkmark$), whereas **type II** pneumocytes are adapted to **produce surfactants** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **four** of the following (*answer must include at least **one** point for each cell type to gain full marks*):

    ***Sperm cells:*** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ flagella **OR** tail for movement **OR** sperm cells can swim        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ acrosome containing enzymes for digesting the jelly coat of the egg **OR** for penetrating the egg                    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ large numbers of mitochondria for release of energy             [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ haploid nucleus **OR** half the normal amount of genetic information for fertilisation    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ small cells so can be produced in large quantities               [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ streamlined shape to reduce drag **OR** friction **OR** to make it easier to swim to egg    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ little cytoplasm **OR** few organelles OR no Golgi apparatus **OR** no ribosomes to reduce size **OR** weight to make it easier  for them to swim **OR** move        [1 mark]

    ***Egg cells*** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ large as contain organelles **OR** resources **OR** nutrients  for early development of the zygote **OR** egg cells contain resources for early development of the embryo    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ contain enzymes **OR** cortical granules which release enzymes to change the outer layers after fertilisation to prevent polyspermy                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ zona pellucida for sperm-egg recognition **OR** to prevent polyspermy    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ usually only one develops at a time to reduce risk of multiple fertilisations **OR** for more efficient use of resources                     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ haploid nucleus **OR** half the normal amount of genetic information for fertilisation    [1 mark]
    [5 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Sperm cells have a **flagellum for movement** ($\checkmark$), **enzymes in the acrosome** to digest the outer layers of the egg ($\checkmark$) and **large numbers of mitochondria** in the midpiece for energy release for swimming ($\checkmark$). Egg cells have **a large cytoplasm with the organelles** and **resources** for the early development of the zygote ($\checkmark$).  **Both** sperm and egg cells have a **haploid nucleus** so that the normal **diploid number** of chromosomes is **restored at fertilisation** ($\checkmark$).

3. any **seven** of the following (*answer must include at least **two** points about properties and location of stem cells and at least **two** differences between totipotent, pluripotent and multipotent stem cells to gain full marks*):

    ***Properties and locations of stem cells:*** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ stem cells have the capacity to keep dividing            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ stem cells have the ability to differentiate **OR** can differentiate into different types of cells [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ stem cells are found in specific locations such as embryos **OR** umbilical cord **OR** stem cell niches [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ adult stem cells are found in hair follicles **OR** bone marrow **OR** brain **OR** any relevant example of a location of a stem cell niche             [1 mark]

    ***Differences between totipotent, pluripotent and multipotent stem cells:*** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ totipotent and pluripotent stem cells are found in embryos **BUT** multipotent stem cells are found in adult tissues                    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ totipotent stem cells are found in early-stage embryos **OR** totipotent stem cells can develop into both embryonic and extraembryonic cells  **WHEREAS** pluripotent cells are found in embryos at the blastocyst stage **OR** pluripotent cells can only differentiate into embryonic cells                     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ totipotent stem cells are stem cells with the greatest potential for differentiation **WHEREAS** pluripotent stem cells can differentiate into most types of cells OR totipotent stem cells can differentiate into any type of cell **WHEREAS** multipotent stem cells have the least capacity for differentiation              [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ multipotent stem cells can only develop into cells for the tissue where they are found **WHEREAS** totipotent and pluripotent stem cells can develop into cells for different tissues                            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ totipotent stem cells develop into pluripotent stem cells **BUT** multipotent stem cells do not develop into other types of stem cells    [1 mark]
    [7 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Stem cells have the capacity to **differentiate** ($\checkmark$) and the **ability to continue dividing** ($\checkmark$). Stem cells are found in **embryos and stem cell niches in adult tissue** ($\checkmark$). Adult stem cells are found in stem cell niches such as **hair follicles and bone marrow** ($\checkmark$). Totipotent and pluripotent stem cells are **found in embryos** but **multipotent** stem cells are found in **adult tissues** ($\checkmark$). **Totipotent** cells are found in early-stage embryos and can develop into both **embryonic** and **extraembryonic cells**, but **pluripotent** cells are found in embryos at the **blastocyst stage** and can only differentiate into **embryonic** cells ($\checkmark$).  **Pluripotent** and **totipotent** can develop into cells for **different tissues** but **multipotent** cells can only develop into **cells for the tissues where they are found** ($\checkmark$).
