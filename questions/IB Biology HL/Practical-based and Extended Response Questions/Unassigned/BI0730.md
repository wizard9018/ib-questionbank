# BI0730

**Maximum mark: 16**

1. Outline the adaptations of sperm cells.      [4 marks]

2. Describe how positive feedback results in contractions during pregnancy.         [4 marks]

3. Discuss the importance of cell division in pregnancy. [8 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **four** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ a flagellum **OR** tail enables the sperm cell to swim towards the egg [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ numerous mitochondria in the midpiece produce ATP to provide energy for movement [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ an acrosome contains enzymes that help the sperm penetrate the outer layers surrounding the egg [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ a haploid nucleus contains one set of chromosomes, allowing the diploid chromosome number to be restored at fertilisation [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ a streamlined shape **OR** reduced cytoplasm reduces resistance during movement through fluid [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ receptors on the sperm cell membrane enable recognition of **OR** binding to the egg [1 mark]
[4 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Sperm cells have **a flagellum that enables them to swim towards the egg** ($\checkmark$). They contain **numerous mitochondria in the midpiece that produce ATP to provide energy for movement** ($\checkmark$). **The acrosome contains enzymes that help the sperm penetrate the outer layers surrounding the egg** ($\checkmark$). Sperm cells also have **a haploid nucleus containing one set of chromosomes, allowing the diploid chromosome number to be restored at fertilisation** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **four** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ positive feedback acts to increase an effect    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ oxytocin stimulates contractions in the uterus **OR** myometrium     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the contractions are detected by stretch receptors  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ signal to the pituitary gland to release more oxytocin    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ more oxytocin makes the contractions more vigorous and frequent     [1 mark]
[4 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    **Positive feedback acts to increase an effect** ($\checkmark$). Towards the end of pregnancy, levels of progesterone fall and levels of oxytocin increase. **Oxytocin stimulates contractions in the myometrium** ($\checkmark$), which is detected by **stretch receptors** ($\checkmark$), this sends a signal to the **pituitary gland to release more oxytocin** ($\checkmark$), which makes the **contractions even more vigorous** and frequent ($\checkmark$), causing even more oxytocin secretion, allowing the baby to be born more rapidly.

3. any **eight** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ occurs during oogenesis in the ovaries **OR** spermatogenesis in the testes       [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ mitosis occurs first, allowing spermatocytes **OR** oocytes to be made       [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ precursor to two rounds of meiosis [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ allowing mature gametes to be made  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ without mature gametes, there would be no fertilisation **OR** continuation of life [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ post fertilisation, the zygote divides many times via mitosis   [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ forming pluripotent cells with high differentiation potential  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ if extracted at this point, they can be used for stem cell treatments or research     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ if they continue to divide via mitosis, a blastocyst will eventually form [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ outer layer of blastocyst implants into the endometrium [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ outer layer of blastocyst will eventually differentiate into the placenta, which allows for exchange of materials between mother and foetus [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ allowing the foetus to grow and develop     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ inner part of blastocyst will eventually differentiate into the foetus     [1 mark]
[8 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Cell division occurs during both **oogenesis and spermatogenesis** ($\checkmark$), in the ovaries and testes, respectively. **Mitosis occurs first** ($\checkmark$), allowing spermatocytes or oocytes to be made, next the cells undergo **two rounds of meiosis** ($\checkmark$), which allows **mature egg and sperm cells to be made** ($\checkmark$). Without the mature gametes, **there would be no fertilisation** ($\checkmark$). **Post fertilisation**, the zygote (first cell formed after fertilisation) **divides many times via mitosis** ($\checkmark$) forming identical **pluripotent cells** with the ability to differentiate into most cell types ($\checkmark$), if these cells are extracted at this point, they can be used for **stem cell treatments** ($\checkmark$) such as treating Stargardt disease. If the cells are not extracted, at this point they will continue to divide via **mitosis**, until a **blastocyst** ($\checkmark$) (a hollow ball of cells)  is formed. The **outer layer** of the blastocyst will **implant into the endometrium** ($\checkmark$) and will eventually **differentiate into the placenta** which allows for **exchange of materials** ($\checkmark$) such as nutrients, waste and oxygen between the mother and child, **sustaining the foetus’s life** ($\checkmark$). The **inner part of the blastocyst will differentiate into the foetus** ($\checkmark$) which will eventually develop into a child.
