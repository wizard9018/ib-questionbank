# BI0809

**Maximum mark: 10**

Neonicotinoids are synthetic compounds that are widely used as pesticides.  However, there are concerns about their effect on honey bees and other pollinating insects.  The table below shows the results from a study where honey bees (*Apis mellifera carnica*) were treated with non-lethal doses of two neonicotinoids, Imidacloprid and Clothianidin, and the effect it had on their homing instinct (ability to fly back to their hive).

![BI0809A](images/BI0809A.png)
[Source: Adapted from Fischer, J., Müller, T., Spatz, A.-K., Greggers, U., Grünewald, B., & Menzel, R. (2014). *Neonicotinoids interfere with specific components of navigation in honeybees*. PLOS ONE. Retrieved June 7, 2023, https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0091364 . Copyright under CC BY 4.0]

1. Describe the mode of action of pesticides containing neonicotinoids on cholinergic synapses in insects.    [2 marks]

2. Using the data, outline the effect of the neonicotinoids on honey bees.    [2 marks]

3. Calculate the percentage increase between the control and those treated with 0.6 μM of Imidacloprid which did **not** arrive back at their hive.     [1 mark]

4. State what the dependent variable is in this study.    [1 mark]

5.
    1. Based on the data, predict the effect that exposing honey bees to neonicotinoids will have on the fitness of the honey bee colony.        [1 mark]

    2. Explain your answer to part (i).                    [1 mark]

6. The researchers concluded that neonicotinoids should not be used as pesticides due to their unintended effects on honey bees. Evaluate whether this data supports their conclusion.    [2 marks]

1. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ neonicotinoids bind to acetylcholine receptors irreversibly **OR** cannot be broken down by acetylcholinesterase [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ acetylcholine is unable to bind to acetylcholine receptors [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ synaptic transmission is prevented [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Neonicotinoids work by **binding** to **acetylcholine receptors** that are present on cholinergic postsynaptic membranes **irreversibly** ($\checkmark$) which **prevents acetylcholine from binding** to its own receptors ($\checkmark$), **preventing synaptic transmission** ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ with higher doses of imidacloprid **OR** 0.9 µM of imidacloprid the percentage of bees that arrived back at the hive decreased dramatically, **OR** the percentage of bees that did not arrive back at the hive increased dramatically [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Treating honeybees with neonicotinoids **decreases the percentage of honey bees that arrive back** ($\checkmark$) at the hive compared to the controls, and with the **highest dose of imidacloprid** the percentage of bees that arrived back at the hive **decreased by almost two thirds** ($\checkmark$) compared to the controls.
