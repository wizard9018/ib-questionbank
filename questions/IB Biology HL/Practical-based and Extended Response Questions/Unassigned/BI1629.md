 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:

 Rf value = distance moved by pigment from origin ÷ distance moved by solvent front from the origin\

 Rf value = (29 - 5) ÷ (40 - 5)\

 Rf value = 24 ÷ 35 = 0.69 ($\checkmark$)
