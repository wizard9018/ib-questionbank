# BI0788

**Maximum mark: 10**

The graph shows the effect of differing concentrations of the growth hormone Indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) on the shoot growth rate of the sesame seed plant (*Sesamum indicum*). IAA is an auxin.

![BI0788](images/BI0788.png)

1.
 1. Describe the trend shown in the graph. [1 mark]

 2. Outline why percentage change was used rather than change in length. [2 marks]

2.
 1. State two variables that would need to be controlled in this experiment. [2 marks]

 2. Explain why it is important that one of the factors you chose in (i) needs to be controlled in this experiment. [2 marks]

3.
 1. Explain how auxins such as IAA promote shoot growth. [2 marks]

 2. Deduce, with a reason, which concentration of IAA should be used to grow sesame seed plants. [1 mark]

 The graph shows that between **0 µM and 75 µM**, the percentage change in shoot length **increased** and between **75 µM and 100 µM**, the percentage change in shoot length **decreased** ($\checkmark$).

 $\ce{ -> }$ there should only be one independent variable in an experiment [1 mark]

 It is important that **light intensity** is controlled as it affects the rate of photosynthesis and so affects the **growth rate** ($\checkmark$) of the shoot, and there should only be **one independent variable** ($\checkmark$) in an experiment.

3.
 1. Any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ IAA **OR** auxin enters the cells of shoot and upregulates gene expression for production of expansins [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ expansins increase elasticity of cell wall [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ IAA **OR** auxin activates proton pumps in the plasma membrane so hydrogen ions are pumped into the cell wall [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ hydrogen ions cause bonds holding cellulose fibres together to break [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ breaking bonds in cell wall makes it more elastic so, as water enters the vacuole, the cell elongates [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ IAA **OR** auxin stimulates cell division [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Auxin is a positive phototropic growth hormone in plants. It enters the cells in the shoot and **upregulates gene expression of expansins** ($\checkmark$), which **increase the elasticity** of the cell wall ($\checkmark$). Auxin activates **proton pumps** in the plasma membrane of cells in the shoot so **hydrogen ions are pumped** into the cell wall ($\checkmark$). The hydrogen ions cause bonds holding **cellulose fibres together in the cell wall to break** ($\checkmark$), making the **cell walls more elastic**, which allows **water to enter the vacuole** , and causes the cell to **elongate** ($\checkmark$).

3.
 2. Any **one** of the following:

 *Both a value and a reason are needed for one mark*

 $\ce{ -> }$ 75 µM because it is the concentration with the highest percentage change [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The concentration of IAA that should be used to grow sesame seed plants is **75 µM** because it is the concentration that results in the **highest percentage increase** ($\checkmark$) in shoot length.
