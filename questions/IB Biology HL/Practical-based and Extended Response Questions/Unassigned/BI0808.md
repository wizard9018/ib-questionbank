1. Any **two** of the following (one point for GPP and one point for NPP):

 $\ce{ -> }$ gross primary productivity is the energy produced by the process of photosynthesis by green plants **OR** primary producers [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ GPP represents the total rate at which primary producers convert light energy into chemical energy [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ net primary productivity is the actual growth of the producer [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ net primary productivity is the energy available after the producer has carried out respiration [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ net primary productivity is the Gross Primary Productivity minus energy lost in respiration **OR** NPP = GPP -R **OR** Net primary productivity = Gross Primary Productivity - Respiration [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Gross primary productivity is the total **rate** at which primary producers **convert** light energy into **chemical energy** ($\checkmark$) in the form of organic compounds.

 Net primary productivity is the **energy** available **after** the producer has carried out **respiration** ($\checkmark$).

5. Any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ food chains include only one type of food the organism eats, whereas a food web is a network of interconnecting food chains **OR** more realistic way of showing interconnections between organisms [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ food webs reflect how interactions affect the energy transfer between trophic levels, whereas food chains show a simplified flow of energy [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ food webs indicate how stable, **OR** resilient, the ecosystem is [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
