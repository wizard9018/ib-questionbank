# BI0794

**Maximum mark: 10**

The diagram shows how the effect of growing bacteria with different antibiotics can be investigated. A study was conducted to test the degree of antibiotic resistance that bacteria from a hospital door handle had developed. Measurements of the zone of inhibition were taken every 3 days, for 15 days.

![BI0793A](images/BI0793A.png)

![BI0794B](images/BI0794B.png)

1. State which antibiotic is the least effective at preventing bacterial growth. [1 mark]

2. State two variables that must be controlled in this experiment, other than those already given. [2 marks]

3.
 1. Identify the anomalous result from the study. [1 mark]

 2. Suggest how the anomalous result may have arisen. [1 mark]

4. Explain how natural selection can lead to antibiotic resistance in bacteria. [2 marks]

5. The researchers suggested that vancomycin could be used more extensively to treat bacterial infections. Discuss to what extent you agree with this. [3 marks]

4. Any **two** of the following

 $\ce{ -> }$ bacterium within a population develops a random mutation that confers resistance [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ mutation spreads through population through transfer of plasmid **OR** horizontal transfer **OR** reproduction **OR** conjugation [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ bacteria without the mutation **OR** that are susceptible to antibiotics are killed by antibiotics, reducing competition for those with the resistance gene [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ bacteria with the mutation survive and breed [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 A bacterium within a population develops a **random mutation** which confers resistance ($\checkmark$) to antibiotics, and this mutation can **spread** throughout the population through **horizontal transfer** ($\checkmark$), so that more bacteria are resistant to antibiotics. The bacteria with resistance are more likely to **survive** ($\checkmark$) when treated with antibiotics, and so can go on to **reproduce** so that the population of antibiotic-resistant bacteria increases.

5. Any **three** of the following:

 **Agree because…**

 $\ce{ -> }$ it had the largest zone of inhibition over the whole study [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the zone of inhibition didn't change drastically in size over the 15 days [1 mark]

 **Disagree because…**

 $\ce{ -> }$ if it is overused, it could lead to more resistance to vancomycin in a range of bacteria [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ only bacteria from door handles were tested [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the study does not include repeats [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the study only used a limited number of antibiotics [1 mark]

 *Answer must include at least one point from agree and one point from disagree to achieve full marks*
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The evidence from the study supports the suggestion of the researchers that vancomycin could be used more extensively to treat bacterial infections as it had a **larger zone of inhibition** compared to the rest of the antibiotics ($\checkmark$) across the whole study. However, it shouldn't be used to treat bacterial infections as **only bacteria from door handles** were tested ($\checkmark$) and other locations in the hospital may have bacteria that are resistant to vancomycin. Also, its overuse could result in bacteria eventually **developing resistance** to it ($\checkmark$).
