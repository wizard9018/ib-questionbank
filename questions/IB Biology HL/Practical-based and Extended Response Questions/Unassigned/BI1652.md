# BI1652

**Maximum mark: 6**

The karyogram is from a rat (*Rattus rattus*) cell without any chromosomal abnormalities. Biological sex is determined in the same way in rats and humans.

1. State, with a reason, the stage of mitosis at which chromosomes are photographed to make a karyogram.                     [1 mark]

2. Deduce, with a reason, the biological sex of the rat.    [1 mark]

3. State how the chromosomes have been arranged to create the karyogram.    [2 marks]

4. Using the karyogram, compare and contrast between the chromosomes of rats and humans.        [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. \
    $\ce{ -> }$ metaphase **BECAUSE** the chromosomes are visible **OR** condensed **OR** aligned on the equator of the cell so that it is easier to sort them    [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The chromosomes are in **metaphase** because the chromosomes are **visible** and aligned on the **equator** of the cell, which makes it **easier to sort** them ($\checkmark$).

2. \
    $\ce{ -> }$ male **BECAUSE** it has a Y chromosome **OR** has both X and Y chromosomes        [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The rat is **male** because it has a  **Y** chromosome ($\checkmark$).

3. any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ by decreasing length    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ using banding patterns **OR** centromere positions    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ in pairs **OR** homologous pairs **OR** one chromosome from each parent forms a pair    [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The chromosomes have been arranged by **decreasing length** ($\checkmark$) and using their **banding patterns** ($\checkmark$).

4. any **two**  of the following: \
    *Answer must include at least **one** similarity and at least **one** difference to gain full marks*

    ***Similarities***\
   $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have pairs of chromosomes **OR** homologous chromosomes        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have specific banding patterns **OR** genes on the chromosomes        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have a pair of sex chromosomes **OR** X and Y chromosomes    [1 mark]

    ***Differences*** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ rats have fewer chromosomes **OR** autosomes than humans **OR** humans have more chromosomes **OR** autosomes than rats **OR** rats have 21 pairs of chromosomes **AND** humans have 23 pairs of chromosomes **OR** rats have 42 chromosomes **AND** humans have 46  chromosomes **OR**  rats have 40 autosomes and 2 sex chromosomes **AND** humans have 44 autosomes and 2 sex chromosomes **OR**  rats have 20 pairs of autosomes and 1 pair of sex chromosomes **AND** humans have 22 pairs of autosomes and 1 pair of sex chromosomes    [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Both rats and humans have **homologous chromosomes** ($\checkmark$), however, rats have **fewer chromosomes** (42) than humans (46) ($\checkmark$).
