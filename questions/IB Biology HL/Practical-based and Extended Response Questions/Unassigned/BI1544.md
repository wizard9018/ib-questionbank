# BI1544

**Maximum mark: 5**

ECGs are graphs that plot the electrical activity from the SA and AV nodes. The ECG below is from a person with atrial fibrillation, characterised by rapid, irregular beating of the atrial chambers of the heart.

![BI1544eDIT](images/BI1544eDIT.png)

1. Calculate the mean heart rate of this person. [1 mark]

2. Suggest, with a reason, how atrial fibrillation will affect the pumping of the blood from the ventricles. [1 mark]

3. Describe the passage of the electrical impulse and resulting mechanical actions in the heart during the QRS complex of a normal ECG. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. $\ce{ -> }$ 23 bpm **OR** 23 beats min$^{-1}$ (allow 23 - 30) [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:

 ![BI1544AnsA](images/BI1544AnsA.png)

 Time from end of the first QRS stage to end of last = 13s

 Number of beats in 13 seconds = 5

 Beats in one second = $(5 \div 13) \times 60$ = **23 beats min**$^{-1}$ ($\checkmark$)

2. Any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ less blood is pumped from ventricles per beat **OR** decreased cardiac output **BECAUSE** incomplete filling of ventricles [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ inefficient and erratic ventricular contractions **BECAUSE** of irregular atrial signals [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ ventricles have to contract more times per minute **OR** rate of ventricular systole increases **BECAUSE** lower volume of blood pumped out per beat [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Atrial fibrillation will **reduce** the **efficiency** of blood **pumping from** the **ventricles** because the **irregular** and **rapid beating** of the **atria** leads to **incomplete filling** of the ventricles ($\checkmark$), resulting in **decreased cardiac output** ($\checkmark$) and potentially insufficient blood supply to the body.

3. Any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ electrical impulse passes through AV **OR** atrioventricular node [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ impulse passes down bundle of His **OR** down Purkinje fibres [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ ventricles contract from apex upwards [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ ventricular depolarisation [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ ventricular systole [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ blood pressure rises rapidly [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ atrioventricular valves shut **OR** semi-lunar valves open [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ atrial diastole **OR** atria begin to refill **OR** atrial repolarisation [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The QRS complex represents the **depolarisation** of the **ventricles** ($\checkmark$), triggered as the **electrical impulse passes through** the **AV node** ($\checkmark$). It travels down to the **apex** of the heart so the **ventricles** can **contract upwards** to push the blood out of the heart ($\checkmark$). The phase corresponds to **ventricular systole** ($\checkmark$).
