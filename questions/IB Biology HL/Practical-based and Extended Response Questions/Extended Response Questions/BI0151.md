# BI0151

**Maximum mark: 16**

1. Compare the concentration and potency of carbon dioxide and methane as greenhouse gases in the atmosphere.  [3 marks]

2. Explain the reasoning of climate scientists for identifying human activity as the main cause of climate change. [5 marks]

3. Discuss the consequences of global warming with reference to the climate, marine organisms, and changes to coastal regions and Arctic ecosystems. [8 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ carbon dioxide concentrations are higher **THAN** those of methane   [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ methane is a more potent greenhouse gas **THAN** carbon dioxide (20:1)  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ carbon dioxide stays in the atmosphere for a longer time **THAN** methane [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ methane is converted to carbon dioxide and water in the atmosphere   [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ carbon dioxide is the main greenhouse gas  [1 mark]
[3 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    **The levels of carbon dioxide are more than methane** in the atmosphere ($\checkmark$). **Carbon dioxide persists for a longer time than methane** ($\checkmark$). **Methane is converted to carbon dioxide and water in the atmosphere** after a while ($\checkmark$). The potency or **warming potential of methane is more than that of carbon dioxide**, almost 20 times more ($\checkmark$). It can trap more heat energy.

2. any **five** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ scientists have calculated that Earth would be much cooler without the added greenhouse gases (GHG) [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ global temperatures are influenced by GHG concentrations  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the burning of fossil fuels has added carbon dioxide to the atmosphere [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ global fluctuations **OR** increase and decrease of carbon dioxide and temperature are strongly correlated [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ for hundreds of years, CO$_2$ concentrations were between 200 and 300 parts per million [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ currently CO$_2$ concentrations have risen to almost 400 parts per million [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ human activity is causing the increase **OR** release of GHG in the atmosphere [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the GHGs carbon dioxide, methane, halocarbons, and nitrogen oxides are associated with global warming [1 mark]
[5 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Scientists have calculated **that the Earth would be much cooler without the effects of warming** caused by greenhouse gases ($\checkmark$). **Global temperatures are influenced by the concentration of greenhouse gases** ($\checkmark$).

    Starting about 200 years ago which was the start of the industrial age, carbon dioxide levels on Earth have been steadily increasing. This shows that **burning fossil fuels has added extra carbon dioxide to the atmosphere** and this will continue unless alternative technologies are established ($\checkmark$).

    Ice core data has shown that **carbon dioxide levels and temperature are strongly correlated** in the past ($\checkmark$). Therefore, **human activities which release carbon dioxide and other greenhouse gases** are clearly responsible for the rising temperatures associated with climate change ($\checkmark$).

3. Maximum of **two** marks from each category

    Climate (any **two** of the following):

    $\ce{ -> }$ consequences will vary from region to region   [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ some colder countries will have warmer temperatures and may benefit through increased agricultural production   [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increased global temperatures cause heat waves **OR** typhoons **OR** hurricanes **OR** change of ocean currents     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increased ice melting may disrupt ocean currents [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increase in frequency or severity of droughts **OR** desertification in some areas  [1 mark]

    Marine organisms (any **two** of the following):

    $\ce{ -> }$ coral reefs will undergo bleaching  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ loss of biodiversity in the oceans  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ higher temperatures will lead to ocean acidification [1 mark]

    Change to coastal regions (any **two** of the following):

    $\ce{ -> }$ coastal flooding **OR** coastal cities could be submerged [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ some islands may disappear completely     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ existing coastal ecosystems may be lost    [1 mark]

    Arctic ecosystems (any **two** of the following):

    $\ce{ -> }$ Arctic **OR** Antarctic ice could melt **OR** melting of glaciers   [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ loss of habitat for animals (like polar bears)  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ permafrost melting **OR** permafrost melting will result in the release of methane  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the latitude and altitude of biomes may shift [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increased spread of tropical diseases  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ other correct consequences can be accepted   [1 mark]
[8 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    The **consequences of global warming will vary from region to region** ($\checkmark$). Though mostly detrimental, some countries will have **warmer temperatures and may benefit from increased agricultural production** ($\checkmark$). Increased temperatures are occurring in most places and in some places, **increased rainfall is causing flooding**.

    **Coral reefs will undergo bleaching** ($\checkmark$) due to **ocean acidification** ($\checkmark$). There will be a **loss of biodiversity in oceans** as well ($\checkmark$).

    **Coastal cities will be submerged** ($\checkmark$) when sea levels rise due to the melting of ice sheets and increased rainfall.  Some **islands may be completely submerged** ($\checkmark$).

    Global warming will lead to the **loss of habitat for animals like polar bears** ($\checkmark$) in the Arctic and penguins in the **Antarctic due to large areas of ice melting** ($\checkmark$).

    Overall, scientists foresee an increase in catastrophic weather events for humans everywhere as temperatures continue to rise globally.
