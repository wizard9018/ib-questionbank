# BI0111

**Maximum mark: 16**

1. Compare and contrast how detritivores and saprotrophs obtain nutrition. [3 marks]

2. Outline how a quadrat can be used to estimate the population of poppies in a field. [5 marks]

3. Energy and nutrients move through ecosystems differently.  Describe how energy and nutrients move through terrestrial ecosystems. [8 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **three** of the following (must include at least one similarity and one difference to gain full marks):

    ***Similarities***

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** obtain energy/nutrients exclusively from dead organic matter/detritus/dead leaves/feathers/animal carcasses [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** are heterotrophic [1 mark]

    ***Differences***

    $\ce{ -> }$ detritivores ingest dead organic matter **WHEREAS** saprotrophs secrete enzymes that digest dead organic matter outside their body [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ saprotrophs absorb soluble/digested nutrients externally across their cell membranes/walls, **WHEREAS** detritivores absorb digested nutrients internally across a gut/digestive system wall [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ example of detritivore e.g. earthworms **AND** example of saprotrophs e.g. fungi/bacteria [1 mark]
[3 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Both detritivores and saprotrophs feed on **dead organic matter** ($\checkmark$) such as leaves. **Detritivores** such as **earthworms**, which are found in soil, **ingest dead organic matter**, and digestion occurs in their digestive system. **Saprotrophs such as fungi and bacteria** ($\checkmark$) **secrete enzymes** into the environment, **digest** the organic matter **outside of themselves** and then **absorb** some of the **resulting products** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **five** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ drawing shown of a field roughly divided to show where quadrats can be placed to sample the number of poppies [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ description of quadrat as a square with known dimensions [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ random **OR** systematic placing of quadrats in the field to measure poppy abundance [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ abundance of poppies in each quadrat recorded [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ repeated sampling [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ average (mean) number of poppies in quadrats is calculated [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ extrapolate to estimate the size of the population in the field [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ quadrats are easy to carry to the study site [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ population of poppies in the field using quadrat sampling is only an estimate [1 mark]
[5 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    ![Picture 1](<images/Picture 1.png>)

    **(Quadrats placed randomly used to estimate numbers of poppies in the area shown)**

    Quadrats, which **have a known dimension** ($\checkmark$), can be used in fields  to estimate the abundance of sessile plants and animals. They are placed **randomly or in a systematic pattern** ($\checkmark$), and the **number of poppies** in **each quadrat** is recorded ($\checkmark$). This process is **repeated to obtain a representative sample** ($\checkmark$) and the **mean abundance of poppies per quadrat is calculated** ($\checkmark$).  The mean abundance of poppies per quadrat is used to **extrapolate an estimate of the population** ($\checkmark$) of poppies in the field.

3. any **eight** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ energy is not recycled in ecosystems [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ light energy is converted to chemical energy by autotrophs through photosynthesis [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ chemical energy moves from autotrophs to heterotrophs through feeding [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ when energy is transferred between trophic levels most of the energy is lost [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ energy is lost between trophic levels as heat **OR** excreted waste products **OR** indigestible parts of a plants or animals such as hair [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ plants absorb nutrients and water from the soil [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ nutrients move from one trophic level to the next through feeding [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ saprotrophs are decomposers, break down dead organic matter and release nutrients into the environment **OR** detritivores consume dead organic matter and release nutrients into the environment [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ saprotrophs secrete enzymes into the environment and digest their food externally **OR** detritivores ingest their food and break it down internally [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ plants absorb nutrients released through decomposition [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ nutrients are recycled in ecosystems [1 mark]
[8 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Plants **are autotrophs** and **make organic compounds via photosynthesis using light energy** ($\checkmark$).  **Primary consumers feed on autotrophs** and **energy flows** ($\checkmark$) from primary to secondary to tertiary consumers. All organisms that feed on others are known as **heterotrophs**. **As energy is transferred** from one trophic level to the next, energy is **lost as heat** ($\checkmark$) and **cannot be reused** ($\checkmark$).

    **Saprotrophs are decomposers** and break **down dead organic matter** in the soil by **secreting enzymes** ($\checkmark$).  This process **releases nutrients into the environment** ($\checkmark$) and **plants absorb and use them** ($\checkmark$). In this manner **nutrients are recycled** ($\checkmark$).
