2. any **nine** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ the carbon cycle keeps the long-term concentration of carbon in the atmosphere **AND** Earth in balance which helps to keep the Earth's temperature relatively stable [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ climate change can be damaging to organisms on Earth by making some places uninhabitable **OR** resulting in flooding **OR** causing droughts **OR** increasing the risk of extreme weather events **OR** increasing the spread of pathogens **OR** contributing to the death of organisms that are unable to adapt [1 mark]

 ***Terrestrial heterotrophs***

 $\ce{ -> }$ organisms consume carbon-containing compounds to use for energy **OR** respiration [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ organisms release carbon dioxide during respiration, which contributes to carbon dioxide in the atmosphere which helps to regulate the Earth's temperature [1 mark]

 ***Photosynthetic autotrophs***

 $\ce{ -> }$ carbon dioxide from the atmosphere is used by terrestrial autotrophs **OR** plants for photosynthesis **OR** to make carbohydrates **OR** necessary carbon compounds [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ autotrophs fix carbon **OR** store carbon which prevents too much carbon dioxide from being released into the atmosphere, mitigating the greenhouse effect **OR** reducing the impacts of climate change [1 mark]

 ***Archaeans***

 $\ce{ -> }$ archaeans use carbon dioxide **OR** acetate **OR** carbon-containing compounds for methanogenesis **AND** the presence of methane in the atmosphere can help to regulate the Earth's temperature [1 mark]

 ***Oceanic processes***

 $\ce{ -> }$ soluble carbon dioxide **OR** hydrogen carbonate ions can be absorbed by aquatic plants **OR** aquatic autotrophs so they can carry out photosynthesis **OR** make carbohydrates [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ corals **OR** molluscs have hard outer layers made of calcium carbonate that act as protection [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ gas exchange of carbon dioxide gas between the ocean and atmosphere helps to maintain the Earth's temperature **AND** maintain the pH of the oceans for aquatic life [1 mark]

 ***Decomposition***

 $\ce{ -> }$ saprotrophs digest non-living organic matter (containing carbon) for nutrition **OR** energy [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ without saprotrophs, organic waste would accumulate on Earth [1 mark]

 ***Geological***

 $\ce{ -> }$ fossilised organic matter **OR** coal **OR** oil **OR** natural gas are formed from large deposits of carbon from past geological eras and can provide fuel for cooking **OR** warmth [1 mark]
 [9 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The carbon cycle keeps the **long-term concentration of carbon** in the atmosphere and Earth in **balance**, which helps to keep the Earth's **temperature** relatively **stable** ($\checkmark$). **Carbon dioxide** in the **atmosphere** is used by plants for **photosynthesis** ($\checkmark$). Plants can also **store** or fix carbon which prevents too much carbon dioxide from being **released into the atmosphere**, contributing to the **greenhouse effect** ($\checkmark$). This is useful, as drastic and uncontrolled **increases** to global **temperatures** can be **damaging to organisms** on Earth by making some places uninhabitable for organisms to live ($\checkmark$). **Heterotrophs** **consume** carbon-containing compounds to use for **energy** ($\checkmark$) and also release carbon dioxide during **respiration**, which contributes to carbon dioxide in the atmosphere, which helps to **regulate** the **Earth's temperature** ($\checkmark$). **Soluble** carbon dioxide can be absorbed by **aquatic plants** for **photosynthesis** ($\checkmark$), which uses light energy to convert it into chemical energy. **Molluscs** have **hard outer shells** made of **calcium carbonate** that act as **protection** ($\checkmark$). **Saprotrophs** **digest** dead **organic matter** containing carbon for **nutrition** ($\checkmark$), releasing carbon dioxide back into the atmosphere through respiration.

3. any **four** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ it can form up to four covalent bonds with a range of different atoms so can form complex structures **OR** it can form bonds with other carbon molecules to create large macromolecules [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ covalent bonds are strong, so it can form stable molecules [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ it can form single **AND** double bonds so can form a diverse range of molecules [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ carbon atoms can link together in long chains **OR** branched structures **OR** rings [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ it is an abundant element on Earth **OR** is the 15th most abundant element on Earth, so is available to bond with many elements [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ it can bond with ions as well as elements to allow for increased diversity of molecular compounds **OR** can add functional groups to it to make a diverse range of compounds [1 mark]

 [4 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Carbon is present in many biological molecules, because it can form up to **four, strong covalent** bonds ($\checkmark$), with a range of **different atoms** so it can form **stable complex** structures ($\checkmark$). Carbon can form **single** and **double bonds** to form a **diverse range** of molecules ($\checkmark$) and can bond with **ions** as well as **elements** to allow for **increased diversity** of molecular compounds ($\checkmark$).
