# BI0249

**Maximum mark: 16**
1. Describe how mutations can result in natural selection. [4 marks]

2. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of a population producing more offspring than the environment can support. [4 marks]

3. Explain how the misuse of antibiotics can lead to the development of bacterial resistance. [8 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.    any **four** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ mutations are a random change in the DNA sequence [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ natural selection as the mechanism by which organisms better adapted survive and pass on their traits **OR** characteristics **OR** genes to their offspring     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increase variation **OR** diversity within a population **OR** species  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ mutations can be neutral, advantageous or detrimental    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ individuals with advantageous mutations are better adapted for the environment  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ individuals with advantageous mutations, survive and reproduce **OR** they pass these mutations onto their offspring [1 mark]

    [4 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    Mutations are **random changes in the DNA sequence** ($\checkmark$)
 that can lead to an increase in the **variation** within a species ($\checkmark$). Individuals with advantageous mutations that make them **better adapted** to their environment ($\checkmark$) **survive and reproduce**, so they are able to pass these advantageous mutations onto their **offspring** ($\checkmark$).

2.    any **four** of the following (advantages and disadvantages must be present for full marks):

    **Advantages:**

    $\ce{ -> }$ increases genetic diversity  **OR** variation [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ individuals with favourable characteristics **OR** advantageous traits **OR** better adapted, survive and reproduce    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ species becomes better adapted (to the environment) over time [1 mark]

    **Disadvantages:**

    $\ce{ -> }$ increases competition for resources    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ higher population densities increase disease transmission **OR** environmental degradation [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ struggle for survival **OR** suffering of individuals less adapted **OR** with unfavourable characteristics    [1 mark]

    [4 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    Producing more offspring than the environment can support can be advantageous because it can **increase the genetic diversity** ($\checkmark$) within a population
and increasing diversity allows individuals with more **favourable characteristics** to survive ($\checkmark$)
, which leads to the population becoming **better adapted** to the environment over time ($\checkmark$)
. However, this can also be a disadvantage because if there are a large number of individuals within a population it could increase the **spread of disease** as the population is more dense ($\checkmark$).

3.    any **eight** of the following (maximum of 7 marks if no example of misuse is stated):

    $\ce{ -> }$ antibiotics as a selective pressure [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ some bacteria are resistant to some antibiotics **OR** possess a mutation **OR** gene for antibiotic resistance    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ bacteria (with antibiotic resistance) are not killed by antibiotics **OR** antibiotics are ineffective [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ transfers of the gene for antibiotic resistance to another bacterium through conjugation **OR** using plasmids     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ resistance gene can pass to next generation **OR** offspring, through cell division **OR** binary fission [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ resistant bacteria have no competition and proliferate in the absence of other bacteria **OR** proportion of resistance bacteria in a population increase [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ MRSA (methicillin resistant *Staphylococcus aureus*) is an example of antibiotic resistant bacteria **OR** any other valid example [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ example of misuses such as over prescription of antibiotics **OR** failure to complete the full course    [1 mark]

    [8 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    **Overprescribing antibiotics** to patients who don’t need them can lead to the development of bacterial resistance ($\checkmark$). This is an issue which is becoming a global health concern.

    Some bacteria may **possess a mutation** which confers resistance to an antibiotic ($\checkmark$). If this antibiotic is used to treat a bacterial infection, then it acts as a **selective pressure** ($\checkmark$), which results in the death of bacteria that do not possess resistance, but the **survival** of those that do ($\checkmark$). Surviving bacteria are able to **pass on the gene for resistance** to the next generation through **binary fission** ($\checkmark$) or to other bacteria through **plasmids** ($\checkmark$). This would result in the **increase in the proportion** of resistant bacteria in a population ($\checkmark$), which means that another antibiotic would have to be used to help treat the infection. Some bacteria, such as **MRSA**, can have multiple resistances, ($\checkmark$) which means that a combination of antibiotics must be used.
