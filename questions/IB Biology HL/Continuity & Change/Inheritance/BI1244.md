# BI1244

A set of identical (monozygotic) twins separated at birth are raised by different adoptive parents. As adults, their heights differ significantly. Which of the following could explain this observation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Mutation

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Natural selection

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Phenotypic plasticity

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Polygenic inheritance

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Phenotypic plasticity is the capacity for traits to vary as a result of environmental differences and gene expression. Environmental factors such as diet could explain the variation in the adult height of the twins.

**Choice A** is incorrect because identical twins only differ by a very small number of mutations which are unlikely to affect their height. **Choice B** is incorrect because natural selection acts over generations, not within an organism's lifetime. **Choice D** refers to traits that are influenced by multiple genes, of which height is an example, however this cannot explain the differences observed here as identical twins would have the same genotype.
