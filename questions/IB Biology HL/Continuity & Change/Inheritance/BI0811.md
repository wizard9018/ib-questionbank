# BI0811

**Maximum mark: 10**

In guinea pigs (*Cavia porcellus*), long hair (L) is dominant to short hair (l), and curly hair (C) is dominant to straight hair (c). A test cross was carried out between male heterozygous long, curly-haired guinea pigs and female short, straight-haired guinea pigs and a chi-squared test (χ2) was carried out on the offspring.

![BI0811A](images/BI0811A.png)

![BI0811B](images/BI0811B.png)

![BI0811C](images/BI0811C.png)

1.
 1. Using a genetic diagram, state the expected ratio of the cross between the two parents. [3 marks]

 2. Identify the recombinants. [1 mark]

2.
 1. Calculate the ratio of the observed phenotypes. [1 mark]

 2. Suggest a reason for the difference between the expected and observed ratios. [1 mark]

3.
 1. State the null hypothesis in this study. [1 mark]

 2. Deduce with reasons whether the null hypothesis should be accepted. [3 marks]

This indicates a **significant difference** between the observed and expected ratios ($\checkmark$) and so the **null hypothesis should be rejected** ($\checkmark$).
