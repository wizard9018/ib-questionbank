# BI0694

Which is the correct definition of a gene locus?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ The DNA sequence of a gene

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ A variant of the same gene
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ A gene with the same sequence as another gene
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ The position of a gene on a chromosome

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
A gene locus (plural: loci) refers to the specific, fixed position of a gene on a chromosome. It indicates where a gene is located within the genome. **Choice D** is correct

**Choice A** is incorrect because this describes the genetic code or nucleotide sequence of a gene, not its physical position. The sequence and the locus are related but not the same. The locus tells you where the gene is found, not what it codes for.

**Choice B** is incorrect because this defines an allele, not a locus. Alleles are different versions of the same gene that occupy the same locus on homologous chromosomes.

**Choice C** is incorrect because the locus refers to the location, not sequence similarity, of a gene.
