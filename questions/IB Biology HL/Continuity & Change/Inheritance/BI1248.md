# BI1248

The following graphs show the weights of piglets born from 27 litters in Utrecht, the Netherlands. Weights were compared based on birth order at various life stages.

![BI1248](images/BI1248.png)
[Source: Slegers, Y., Oolbekkink, Y., Roelofs, S., Van der Staay, F. J., & Nordquist, R. (2021). *Effects of Birth Order on Performance and Affective State of Pigs*. Frontiers in Animal Science, 2(July), 1-13. https://doi.org/10.3389/fanim.2021.669692. Copyright under CC BY 4.0]

Which of the following is **NOT** true regarding the data?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ No pig weighed less than 80kg at the end of the study.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The median weight of last-born pigs was higher than first-born pigs at all life stages.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Outliers were recorded for each group of pigs in the study.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ At the time of weaning, last-born pigs had weights between 6 and 10kg.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
At the time of weaning, last-born pigs had weights of between 6 and 10kg in the 2nd and 3rd quartiles only. If you include all pigs, their weights fall between 4 and 12kg instead, making **Choice D** a false statement.

**Choices A, B**, and **C** are all true based on the data and are therefore incorrect responses.
