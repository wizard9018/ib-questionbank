# BI0219

Which of these statements about genetic disorders is/are correct?

- I.    $\hspace{1em}$ The majority of genetic disorders are caused by recessive alleles.
- II. $\hspace{.7em}$    Genetic disorders can be sex-linked.
- III. $\hspace{.4em}$    Offspring of two parents with the same genetic disease will always be
affected.

- A.    $\hspace{1em}$All three

- B.    $\hspace{1em}$II only
- C.    $\hspace{1em}$I and III
- D.     $\hspace{1em}$I and II

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because **Statements I** and **II** are both true for genetic disorders. Dominant alleles cause some genetic disorders, but most are caused by recessive ones (**Statement I**) and some genetic disorders, such as haemophilia, are sex-linked (**Statement II**).

If a parent has a genetic disorder that is caused by a dominant allele, they may be heterozygous, so two heterozygous individuals can have a child that is homozygous dominant for the characteristic and they will not have the disease, therefore **Statement III** is not true.

**Choices A** and **C** include **Statement III** and so are incorrect. **Choice B** is incorrect as it is missing **Statement I**
