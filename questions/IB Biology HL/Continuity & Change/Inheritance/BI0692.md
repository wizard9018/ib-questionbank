# BI0692

The graph shows the frequency of birth weight of children born in London in 1991.

![BI0692QB](images/BI0692QB.png)

What type of inheritance is indicated by this distribution?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Sex-linked

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Polygenic
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Monogenic
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Autosomal recessive

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Polygenic inheritance (inheritance caused by more than one gene) can show the pattern of normal distribution in the graph. **Choice B** is correct.

**Choices A**, **C** and **D** are incorrect as they do not show a pattern of normal distribution.
