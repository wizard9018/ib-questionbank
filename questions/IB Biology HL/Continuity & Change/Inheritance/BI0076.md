# BI0076

The diagram shows a pair of homologous chromosomes.

How many loci are shown?

![BI0076](images/BI0076.png)
[ 2022. Created with BioRender.com]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 1

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 2

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 4

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 8

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
A locus is the location of a gene, regardless of the allele. Homologous chromosomes have genes for the same characteristics, therefore, also the same loci. The diagram shows alleles for four genes because they are in the same location. The location of four genes is shown (**Choice C**).

There is one pair of homologous chromosomes (**Choice A**). There are two chromosomes (**Choice B**). There are eight alleles shown (**Choice D**) but only 4 loci.
