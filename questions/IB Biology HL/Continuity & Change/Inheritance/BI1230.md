# BI1230

Cystic fibrosis is caused by a recessive allele. Which individuals in generation II must be heterozygous for the trait?

![BI1230](images/BI1230.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 5 and 3

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 3 and 4

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 9 and 5

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 6 and 3

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
To be heterozygous for a trait, a person must have one dominant and one recessive allele (Cc). Both persons 1 and 2 must be heterozygous in order for them to not have cystic fibrosis and have offspring (individual 6) with the disease (cc). In generation 2, all of the offspring of persons 1 and 2 could be heterozygous except person 6, who has cystic fibrosis (cc). However, only persons 3 and 4 must be heterozygous for the trait, as they do not have the disease and one of their offspring, person 10 has cystic fibrosis. **Choice B** is correct.

Without more information, it is difficult to say whether person 5 is heterozygous (Cc) or homozygous unaffected (CC), they have a 50% chance of being either; **Choice A** is incorrect. Person 9 is in generation 3; **Choice C** is incorrect. Person 6 is homozygous for the cystic fibrosis trait (cc); **Choice D** is incorrect.
