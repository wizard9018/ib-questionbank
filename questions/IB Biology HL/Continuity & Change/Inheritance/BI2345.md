# BI2345

Box-and-whisker plots can be used to represent continuous data. The box-and-whisker plots below show data for heights of two groups of people.

![box and whisker plots heights BI2345RV](<images/box and whisker plots heights BI2345.png>)

Which statement is correct for one of these box-and-whisker plots?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Mean has the same value as the median and the lower quartile is 124 cm.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Mean has a lower value than the median and the lower quartile is 128 cm.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Mean has a higher value than the median and the upper quartile is 133 cm.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Mean has a lower value than the median and the interquartile range is the larger of the two plots.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because ‘X’ represents the mean, while the line inside the box represents the median. In Plot I, the mean (X) is higher than the median (line inside the box). Additionally, the right side of the box corresponds to the upper quartile, which is 133 cm.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the mean and median are not equal in either Plot I or Plot II.

**Choice B** is incorrect because Plot II has a mean which is lower than the median but the lower quartile is not 128 cm.

**Choice D** is incorrect because the mean has a lower value than the median in Plot II but the interquartile range is smaller than in Plot I.
