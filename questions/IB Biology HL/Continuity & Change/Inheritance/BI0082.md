# BI0082

Mendel studied eight characteristics of pea plants, including flower colour. If the pea plants Mendel was studying can only be white or purple and follow the rules of Mendelian genetics, which of these statements was correct?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Pea plants show co-dominance for flower colour

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Flower colour is an example of polygenic inheritance

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Flower colour is controlled by genes at two loci

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Pea plants have two alleles for flower colour

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Two different colours show that there are two alleles for this characteristic (**Choice D**). Polygenic inheritance results in continuous variation (**Choice B**).  Two distinct colours are an example of discontinuous variation. If there was co-dominance, there would be three possible flower colour variations (**Choice A**). If there were genes at two loci that controlled flower colour, then the trait would be a polygenic trait and show continuous variation (**Choice C**).
