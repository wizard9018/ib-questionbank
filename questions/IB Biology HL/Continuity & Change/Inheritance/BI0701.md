# BI0701

In dandelions (*Taraxacum officinale*), the allele for diamond-shaped seeds (D) is dominant over oval-shaped seeds (d) and the allele for long seeds (L) is dominant over short seeds (l).

In a genetic cross between a dandelion that is heterozygous for both traits and a dandelion that is homozygous recessive for both traits, what would be the expected proportion of long seeds?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 2 out of 16
- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 4 out of 16
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 5 out of 16

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 8 out of 16

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choices A, B**  and **C**  are incorrect as when the cross is carried out there is a 50% chance of producing long seeds. In a punnett square, there are 4 possible genotypes; DdLl (diamond oval seeds), Ddll (diamond short seeds), ddLl (oval long seeds) and ddll (oval short seeds), each with a 4 out of 16 probability of being produced.  Therefore, there is an 8 out of 16 chance of producing long seeds. **Choice D is correct.**
