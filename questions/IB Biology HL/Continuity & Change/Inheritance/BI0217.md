# BI0217

Mendel observed that there are two possible variations of flower colour in pea plants, purple and white. After a genetic cross, the ratio of plants with purple flowers to white flowers is 1:1. What were the genotypes of the parents?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Both parents are heterozygous.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ One parent is heterozygous and the other is homozygous recessive.
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Both parents are homozygous recessive.
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ One parent is heterozygous and the other is homozygous dominant.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct, because for a 1:1 ratio in the offspring, one parent must be heterozygous (Rr) so that half of the offspring receive the dominant allele and half receive the recessive allele from that parent, and one parent must be homozygous recessive (rr).

| | R | r |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$r$\hspace{1em}$| $\hspace{1em}$ Rr $\hspace{1em}$  |  $\hspace{1em}$ rr $\hspace{1em}$  |
|$\hspace{1em}$r$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ Rr $\hspace{1em}$  | $\hspace{1em}$  rr $\hspace{1em}$  |

**Choice A** is incorrect, because the ratio of offspring in two heterozygous parents would be 3:1. **Choice C** is incorrect, because all of the offspring from two homozygous recessive parents will have the same (recessive) characteristic. **Choice D** is incorrect, because with one heterozygous parent and one homozygous dominant parent, the offspring would all have the same (dominant) characteristic.
