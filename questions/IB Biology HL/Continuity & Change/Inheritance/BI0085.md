# BI0085

**Maximum mark: 5**

The majority of the genes in the human genome have already been mapped, meaning the exact locations of the genes on chromosomes are known. The diagram below shows one representation of a homologous pair of chromosomes.

![Picture 1](<images/Picture 1.png>)

1. Define locus. [1 mark]

2. Identify the number of loci shown in the diagram. [1 mark]

3. Distinguish between gene, allele, and locus. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ locus is the specific location of a gene. [1 mark]
[1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ :u[Sample answer]:

$\hspace{1em}$ The locus of a gene is its **specific location** ($\checkmark$).

2.
$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ there are 3 loci shown in the diagram. [1 mark]
[1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ :u[Sample answer]:

$\hspace{1em}$ This diagram has **three loci** ($\checkmark$) because it shows the location of alleles for three different genes.

3. Any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ a gene is a heritable factor consisting of DNA that controls a specific characteristic **WHEREAS** an allele is a version of a gene [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the gene for blood type codes for a protein on the surface of red blood cells **WHEREAS** alleles of this gene code for different types of proteins associated with blood groups (or any other valid examples) [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ a locus is the specific location for a gene **WHILE** genes are sequences of base pairs coding for proteins [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ a locus is the specific location of a gene **WHILE** alleles are different versions of a gene **OR** genes code for proteins [1 mark]

 **Accept any other valid examples instead of definitions**
[3 marks]

 :u[Sample answer]:

 A gene is a **heritable factor** made of DNA that controls a specific characteristic while alleles are **different versions** of a gene ($\checkmark$). As an example of differences between the gene and allele, we can discuss red blood cells. The **gene for blood type codes for proteins** on red blood cells while **alleles** of this gene code for **different versions of this protein** on red blood cells ($\checkmark$) . A **locus is the specific location** of a gene on a chromosome while **genes code for proteins** ($\checkmark$).
