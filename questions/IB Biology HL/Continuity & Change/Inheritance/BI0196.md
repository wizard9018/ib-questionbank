# BI0196

Neurons of the fruit fly (*Drosophila melanogaster)* have 8 chromosomes. How many chromosomes are in a fertilised oocyte of a fruit fly?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$8

- B.    $\hspace{1em}$4
- C.    $\hspace{1em}$2
- D.    $\hspace{1em}$16

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
At fertilisation, two gametes, which are haploid, fuse together to produce a diploid cell. Gametes of a fruit fly have a haploid number of 4, so fertilisation produces a cell with 8 chromosomes (**Choice A**).

**Choice B** is incorrect because 4 is the haploid number. **Choice C** is incorrect because fruit fly somatic cells have 8 chromosomes and fertilised egg cells have 8 chromosomes. **Choice D** is incorrect because 16 is twice the diploid number of fruit flies.
