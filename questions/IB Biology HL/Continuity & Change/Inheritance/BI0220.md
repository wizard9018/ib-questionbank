# BI0220

A biological man and a biological woman have a biological daughter and son. Their biological daughter has normal vision, but their biological son is colourblind. Which one of these genotypes could be correct for the biological mother?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ X $^{N}$ Y

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ X $^{n}$ Y

-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ X $^{N}$ X $^{n}$

-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ X $^{N}$ X $^{N}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because the biological mother passes on the X chromosome to a biological son and the biological father contributes only with Y. For the biological son to be colourblind, he must have an allele for it on the X chromosome. Therefore the biological mother must have at least one recessive allele (n).

**Choices A** and **B** are incorrect because individuals with X and Y are biologically male.
**Choice D** is incorrect because, in this case, no biological sons would have the condition.
