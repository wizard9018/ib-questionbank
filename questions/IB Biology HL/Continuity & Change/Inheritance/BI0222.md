# BI0222

In some animals, the agouti allele causes individual hairs to have a banded pattern. The non-agouti allele causes hairs to be a single colour. Individuals who are heterozygous have hairs with a banded pattern.

![BIO222](images/BIO222.png)

Which combination of parent genotypes would allow for production of offspring that do **not** have the agouti characteristic?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ One heterozygous parent and one parent who is homozygous for the agouti allele.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Two parents who are both heterozygous.
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ One parent who is homozygous for the agouti allele, and one parent who is homozygous for the non-agouti allele.
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Two parents who are homozygous for the agouti allele.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The question states that heterozygous individuals have the banded pattern on their hairs, which means that the agouti trait is dominant. Two heterozygous parents will produce offspring in the likely phenotypic ratio of 3 agouti:1:non-agouti (**Choice B**).

![B](images/B.jpg)

The offspring of a heterozygous individual and a homozygous agouti individual will all receive at least one dominant allele, so they would all be agouti (**Choice A**).

![Choice A](<images/Choice A.jpg>)

The offspring of one parent who is homozygous for the agouti allele, and one parent who is homozygous for the non-agouti allele (**Choice C**) would all be heterozygous and so banded.

![C](images/C.jpg)

The offspring of two parents who are homozygous for the agouti allele will all be the same as their parents. (**Choice D**).

![D](images/D.jpg)
