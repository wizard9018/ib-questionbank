## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because human biological males have one X chromosome and one Y chromosome.

 **Choice A** shows the sex chromosomes of a biological female. **Choice C** only shows one X chromosome, and **Choice D** only shows the Y chromosome, both of which are incorrect as biological males have an X and a Y chromosome.
