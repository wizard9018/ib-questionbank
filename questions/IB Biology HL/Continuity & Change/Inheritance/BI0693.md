# BI0693

Which is the correct definition of linked genes?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Genes that are found on the same chromosome

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Genes that are found on different chromosomes
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Genes that are recombined during crossing over
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Alternative forms of the same gene found on homologous chromosomes

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is incorrect as it describes unlinked genes. **Choice C** is incorrect as all genes can be recombined during crossing over.  However, the frequency of recombination due to crossing over is much lower in linked genes, depending on how close they are. **Choice D** is incorrect as it describes alleles.
