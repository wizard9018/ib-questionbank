# BI0218

Sickle cell anaemia is caused by a recessive allele (Hb$^{S}$). The dominant allele is Hb$^{A}$. This image shows the inheritance of sickle cell anaemia in a family.

![BI0218i](images/BI0218i.png)

What are the genotypes for individuals 1 and 7?

| | Individual 1 | Individual 7 |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| Hb$^{A}$ Hb$^{S}$| Hb$^{S}$ Hb$^{S}$ |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hb$^{A}$ Hb$^{S}$| Hb$^{A}$ Hb$^{S}$ |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hb$^{A}$ Hb$^{A}$| Hb$^{A}$ Hb$^{S}$|
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hb$^{A}$ Hb$^{A}$ | Hb$^{S}$ Hb$^{S}$|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct, because individual 1 (and 2) must be heterozygous because they are both unaffected but have affected biological children. Individual 7 must be homozygous recessive (Hb$^{S}$ Hb$^{S}$) because they are affected.
**Choices B** and **C** are incorrect, because individual 7 is affected and thus must be homozygous recessive.
**Choice D** is incorrect, because if individual 1 were homozygous dominant (Hb$^{A}$ Hb$^{A}$), none of their offspring would be affected.
