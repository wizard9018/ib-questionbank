# BI0075

Which of these statements is a correct definition of a gene?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ A gene is the length of a chromosome

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ A gene is a length of DNA that is wrapped around histones

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ A gene is a heritable factor made of DNA that influences a specific characteristic

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Genes control all characteristics of an organism

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is a correct definition of a gene. Chromosomes are made of DNA and protein (**Choice A**), while genes are only made of DNA. When DNA is at various levels of supercoiling, it is wrapped around histone proteins.  However, the presence of histones is not part of the definition of a gene (**Choice B**). Genes control some characteristics, but some characteristics are also affected by the environment (**Choice D**).
