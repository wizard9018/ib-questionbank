# BI0793

**Maximum mark: 10**

1. Compare and contrast between the distribution of sickle cell trait and malaria in Africa.    [2 marks]

2.
    1. State the mode of inheritance of sickle cell anaemia.    [1 mark]

    2. Using a genetic diagram, determine the probability of a child having sickle cell anaemia, if both their parents are carriers of the disease.        [2 marks]

3. Blood samples were taken from children to create the graph. Suggest one difference between the appearance of blood cells in Hb AA and Hb AS samples.    [1 mark]

4. State at which age there is the largest difference between the number of parasites found in the blood of Hb AA and Hb AS samples.    [1 mark]

5. Suggest one difficulty of collecting sufficient data for this study. [1 mark]

6. Using your own knowledge and the data provided, discuss whether carrying the sickle cell allele offers protection from malaria.    [2 marks]
