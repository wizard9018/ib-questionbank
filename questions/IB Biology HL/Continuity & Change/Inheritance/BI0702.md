# BI0702

**Maximum mark: 7**

In Mongolian horses (*Equus przewalskii*), fur colour is determined by a number of genes. Brown colour (B) is dominant to sandy colour (b) and an ombre (two tone) colour (P) is dominant to one tone (p).

3.    Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ genes are linked [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the calculated chi square value is higher than the critical value    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the degrees of freedom is calculated by subtracting one from the number of categories [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ three degrees of freedom [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the null hypothesis should be rejected **OR** the alternative hypothesis should be accepted [1 mark]

    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
There are four possible offspring phenotypes in the double heterozygous genetic cross. To calculate the degrees of freedom, we need **to subtract 1 from 4** ($\checkmark$); therefore, there are 3 degrees of freedom in this cross. The genes are **linked** ($\checkmark$), as the calculated chi-square value is **higher than the critical value** ($\checkmark$) of 7.815 (p<0.05) in the p=0.05 column, at **three degrees of freedom** ($\checkmark$). The **null hypothesis** assuming that there is no link between the genes,  is therefore **rejected** ($\checkmark$).
