# BI1149

What type of inheritance is shown in this pedigree chart?

![BI1149FCM](images/BI1149FCM.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ X-linked dominant

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ X-linked recessive

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Autosomal dominant

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Autosomal recessive

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Autosomal recessive conditions are the only ones that allow for male carriers.

**Choices A** and **C** are incorrect because dominant genetic disorders cannot result in male or female carriers. **Choice B** is incorrect because males cannot be carriers for X-linked conditions, which is another reason why **Choice A** is incorrect.
