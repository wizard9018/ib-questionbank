# BI1180

What is the correct reason for variation in skin colour between humans?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Monogenic inheritance and exposure to UV rays

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Nutrition and monogenic inheritance

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Sex-linked inheritance

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Polygenic inheritance and exposure to UV rays

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Traits that are inherited polygenically are caused by more than one gene and are usually influenced by the environment. Skin colour is influenced both by a mixture of genes and UV rays; **Choice D**  is correct.

Skin colour is a result of polygenic, not monogenic, inheritance; **Choices A** and **B** are incorrect. The gene for skin colour is not found on the sex chromosomes, skin colour is not sex-linked; **Choice C**  is incorrect.
