# BI1246

A man is homozygous for blood type B. His wife has blood type AB. What would be the possible blood types for their children?

| | Type A | Type B | Type AB |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | 25% | 75% | 0% |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | 50% | 50% | 0% |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | 25% | 50% | 25% |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | 0% | 50% | 50% |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The man has the genotype I:sup[B]I:sup[B] while his wife has the genotype I:sup[A]I:sup[B]. Their children have to inherit the IB allele from the father, but the mother has a 50% chance of giving either one of her alleles to the children. This would give them a 50% chance of being type B and a 50% chance of being type AB.

**Choices A, B**, and **C** represent incorrect or impossible phenotype frequencies.
