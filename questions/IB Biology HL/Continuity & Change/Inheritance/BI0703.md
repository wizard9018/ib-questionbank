# BI0703

**Maximum mark: 3**

1.     Distinguish between linked and unlinked genes. [2 marks]

2.    Coat colour is a polygenic trait. State the meaning of *polygenic*. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.    Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$  linked genes are always found on the same chromosome **WHEREAS** unlinked genes are usually found on different chromosomes [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  linked genes are close together on the chromosome **WHEREAS** if unlinked genes are on the same chromosome they are far apart [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  linked genes do not segregate independently during meiosis **BUT** unlinked genes do [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  crossing over can cause linked genes to segregate independently, **BUT** unlinked genes always independently segregate. [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  linked genes are usaully inherited together **WHEREAS** unlinked genes are rarely inherited together [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
**Linked genes** are found on the **same chromosome** whereas **unlinked genes** are usually found on **different chromosomes** ($\checkmark$).  This means that during meiosis **linked genes do not segregate independently but unlinked genes do** ($\checkmark$).

2.    $\ce{ -> }$   traits that are genetically determined by two or more genes [1 mark]

    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
A **polygenic** trait is one that is genetically determined by **two or more genes** ($\checkmark$)
