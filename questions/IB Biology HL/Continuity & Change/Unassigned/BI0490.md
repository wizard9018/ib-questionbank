# BI0490

**Maximum mark: 4**

Hormones can be used as medical treatments in some situations.

1. Name the hormone that can be used to treat jet lag and state its effect on the body. [2 marks]

2. Outline two uses of hormones in IVF. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **two** of the following (*the name must be given*):

    $\ce{ -> }$ melatonin is the hormone that can be used to treat jet lag [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ melatonin regulates the sleep-wake cycle **OR** melatonin regulates circadian rhythms     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ melatonin concentration increases in the evening [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ melatonin promotes sleepiness [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ changing time zones affects the release of melatonin so the sleep-wake cycle is affected     [1 mark]

    [2 marks]
    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
**Melatonin** is the hormone used to treat jet lag ($\checkmark$). It is produced by the pineal gland and **affects the circadian rhythm** of the body ($\checkmark$). The **concentration of melatonin increases** in the **evening** ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ daily FSH injections are given to increase the development of follicles **OR** daily LH injections are given daily to increase the development of follicles **OR** daily FSH and LH injections are given to increase the development of follicles [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ injection of hCG **OR** LH is given which stimulates the follicles to mature before ova are collected [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ oestrogen used to prepare endometrium for implantation **OR** to prepare for embryo transfer [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ after embryos are placed in the uterus a progesterone pessary **OR** tablet is placed in the vagina to maintain the endometrium to allow implantation [1 mark]

    [2 marks]
    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    IVF uses hormones in a number of ways. After a nasal spray is used to stop the release of FSH and LH from the pituitary gland a woman is given **daily FSH and LH injections** for approximately ten days to stimulate the **development of follicles** ($\checkmark$). An **injection of hCG** is then given to **stimulate the follicles to mature** ($\checkmark$). After embryos are placed in the uterus **a progesterone pessary** is placed in the vagina to **maintain the endometrium to allow implantation** ($\checkmark$).
