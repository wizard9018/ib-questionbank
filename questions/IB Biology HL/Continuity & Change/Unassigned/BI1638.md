# BI1638

**Maximum mark: 16**

1. Outline how some flowers are adapted to increase their chance of pollination by insects. [4 marks]

2. Describe how the structure of the placenta is adapted for its role in the development of the foetus. [5 marks]

3. Explain the process of IVF (in vitro fertilisation) in humans.     [7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **four** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ coloured petals to attract insects     towards them **OR** large petals for insects to land on     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ insect-pollinated flowers have nectar that provides nutrients **OR** carbohydrates **OR** sugars for insects    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ anthers produce pollen that is consumed by **OR** provides proteins for insects        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ nectary is inside the flower so that insects touch the anthers and stigma when feeding    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ pollen grains have spines which stick to insects    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ smell **OR** scent to attract insects        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ filament holds anther in best position to touch insect body    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ top of stigma is sticky so holds pollen in place when insect brushes it        [1 mark]
    [4 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    **Coloured petals** ($\checkmark$) and the **scent** attract insects to the flower ($\checkmark$). When insects **feed** on nectar and **pollen** on one plant, some of the **pollen** from the anther **sticks** to the insect due to **spikes** on the pollen ($\checkmark$).  Then, when the same insect **feeds** on another plant's nectar or pollen, some of the pollen lands on the sticky **stigma** ($\checkmark$) because the nectary is inside the flower. **Nectar provides carbohydrates** ($\checkmark$) to the insect, and **pollen provides protein** ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **five** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ chorionic villi **OR** large surface area for exchange of materials        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ thin exchange membrane facilitates efficient diffusion of gases **OR** nutrients    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ rich blood supply **OR** maternal blood pools around villi for efficient nutrient **OR** gas exchange [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ intervillous spaces allow maternal blood to flow freely for exchange of substances    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ foetal capillaries close to maternal blood for effective exchange of substances        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ selective permeability protects foetus by allowing essential nutrients **OR** gases **OR** substances through **WHILE** blocking harmful substances    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ hormone production **OR** progesterone **OR** hCG secretion to maintain uterine lining **OR** support pregnancy                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ active transport of nutrients **OR** glucose **OR** amino acids    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ immune protection due to transfer of maternal antibodies to foetus        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ removal of foetal waste products **OR** carbon dioxide **OR** urea    [1 mark]
    [5 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The placenta is adapted for foetal development with **chorionic villi**, providing a **large surface area** for **material exchange** ($\checkmark$). It has a **thin exchange membrane** that facilitates **efficient diffusion** of **gases** and **nutrients** ($\checkmark$). A **rich blood supply** surrounds the **villi**, and **intervillous spaces** allow **maternal blood** to **flow freely** ($\checkmark$) for effective nutrient and gas exchange. **Foetal capillaries** are **close to maternal blood** ($\checkmark$), enhancing **substance exchange**. The placenta's **selective permeability** permits essential nutrients while **blocking harmful substances** ($\checkmark$). It produces **hormones** like **progesterone** and **hCG** to **maintain** the **uterine lining** ($\checkmark$) and support pregnancy.

3. Any **seven** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ fertilisation outside the body **OR** in the lab        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ drug used to arrest normal menstrual cycle        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ high levels of FSH injected to stimulate maturation of multiple ovules **OR** follicles **OR** superovulation    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) agonists are injected to prevent premature ovulation **OR** control the timing of ovulation        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ hCG injected to complete maturation of follicles    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ ova **OR** eggs harvested **OR** removed from ovaries    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ sperm sample collected **OR** concentrated for fertilisation            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ ova **OR** eggs mixed with sperm  **OR** ova **OR** eggs fertilised with selected sperm        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ fertilised ova **OR** eggs **OR** zygotes **OR** embryos cultured in lab for several days        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ progesterone injected before the fertilised egg is implanted to improve the chances of successful implantation                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ several embryos selected for implantation into uterus        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ pregnancy test used to check if pregnancy has occurred **OR** to check if embryo implanted                        [1 mark]
    [7 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    In vitro fertilisation (IVF) involves **fertilisation outside the body** ($\checkmark$) in a laboratory. First, **drugs** are used to **suppress** the **menstrual cycle**  ($\checkmark$), followed by injection of high levels of **FSH** injections to **stimulate** multiple **ova to mature** ($\checkmark$). **Gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) agonists** are injected to **prevent premature ovulation** ($\checkmark$), and **hCG** is injected to complete the **maturation of follicles** ($\checkmark$). The **mature eggs** are **retrieved from** the **ovaries**  ($\checkmark$) and **mixed** with **sperm** in the **lab** ($\checkmark$). Selected **embryos** are then **implanted** into the **uterus**  ($\checkmark$), and a **pregnancy test** is performed to confirm **implantation** ($\checkmark$).
