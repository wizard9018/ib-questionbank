## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.  any **two** of the following:

        $\ce{ -> }$ more recent forefoot is longer **THAN** the older forefoot (or vice versa) [1 mark]

        $\ce{ -> }$ more recent forefoot is more narrow **THAN** the older forefoot (or vice versa) [1 mark]

        $\ce{ -> }$ more recent forefoot has **FEWER** toes **OR** only one toe **COMPARED** to the older forefoot **OR** more recent foreleg has **FEWER** individual **OR** fused bones **COMPARED** to the older forelegs [1 mark]

        [2 marks]

        $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    The more recent forefoot is **longer than the earlier forefoot** ($\checkmark$) and has fewer **toes** ($\checkmark$).

    2. any **four** of the following:

        $\ce{ -> }$ heritable variation in toe number OR forefoot length existed in the population    [1 mark]
        $\ce{ -> }$ random mutation produced individuals with fewer toes OR more weight on the central toe OR longer forefoot                                    [1 mark]
        $\ce{ -> }$ environmental selection pressure meant this phenotype gave a fitness advantage OR better survival OR greater reproductive success                        [1 mark]
        $\ce{ -> }$ individuals with the advantageous trait survived and reproduced more than others    [1 mark]
        $\ce{ -> }$ trait was inherited by offspring                            [1 mark]
        $\ce{ -> }$ over many generations, the frequency of the advantageous allele increased in the population                                        [1 mark]

        [4 marks]

        $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    **Random mutations** and recombination produced individuals with **variation** in forefoot structure, including **reduced toe number**  and a **longer central toe** ($\checkmark$). In **changing environments**, individuals with these traits had a **fitness advantage** ($\checkmark$), and their **offspring inherited the alleles** ($\checkmark$). Over **many generations, allele frequencies shifted** ($\checkmark$), and the reduced-toe forefoot became common.

    3. **one** of the following:

        $\ce{ -> }$ wouldn\'t survive **OR** would die **BECAUSE** they are less adapted to the environment **OR** would be outcompeted **OR** would be selected against **OR** other reasonable justification [1 mark]
        [1 mark]

        $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    Ancestral horses **would not survive**  ($\checkmark$) in the wild because they are not as well **adapted to the environment** ($\checkmark$) as modern horses.

2. $\ce{ -> }$ heritable characteristics of a species change over time [1 mark]

    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    Evolution is when the **heritable characteristics** ($\checkmark$) of a species change over time.

3. any **two** of the following (*must be from the same pair of statements to gain full marks*):
