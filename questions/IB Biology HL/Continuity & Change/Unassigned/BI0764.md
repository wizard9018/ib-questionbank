# BI0764

**Maximum mark: 15**

The mitotic index is an indirect way of measuring plant growth. A study was conducted on white onions (*Allium cepa*), under controlled conditions in a lab, to investigate the effect of soil pH on the mitotic index of the roots.

![BI0764A](images/BI0764A.png)

1. Describe the trends shown in the graph. [2 marks]

2. Outline how the mitotic index is calculated. [1 mark]

3. Discuss whether the data support the hypothesis that onion plants should be grown in soil with a pH of 5.5. [2 marks]

4. Suggest how a pH of 9 affects the process of mitosis. [2 marks]

5.
 1. Explain why measuring the mitotic index in the root is an indirect way of measuring the overall growth rate of the onion plant. [1 mark]

 2. Suggest another method, apart from measuring mitotic index, that could be used to measure plant growth. [1 mark]

6. State one of the variables that must be controlled to make this experiment valid. [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ pH 5.5 shows the highest mitotic index [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The graph shows that from **pH 3 to 5.5, the mitotic index increases** and when the **pH is higher than 5.5, the mitotic index decreases** ($\checkmark$). At pH **5.5**, the onion root has the highest **mitotic index** ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ number of cells in mitosis **OR** prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase divided by the total number of cells [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ Mitotic index = $\left(\dfrac{\text{Number of cells in mitosis}}{\text{Total number of cells}}\right) $ **OR**
 Mitotic index = $\left(\dfrac{\text{Number of cells in mitosis}}{\text{Total number of cells}}\right) $X100%
[1 mark]
[1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The mitotic index can be calculated by counting the **number of cells** undergoing **mitosis** and **dividing** that by the **total number of cells** ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **two** of the following:

 *Answer must include at least one positive and one negative statement to achieve full marks*

 *Reasons it supports hypothesis:*

 $\ce{ -> }$ 5.5 is the pH with the highest mean mitotic index **OR** results indicate that root growth rate is highest at pH 5.5 [1 mark]

 *Reasons it does not support hypothesis:*

 $\ce{ -> }$ the error bars for pH 5.5 overlap with the error bars for pH 5 and 6 [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ mitotic index is an indirect method of measuring growth **OR** in this study only the rate of growth of the root was investigated [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ in the study, the pH increases in increments of 0.5, however the optimum pH for growth in roots could be either side of 5.5 [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ there is no data showing an increase in dry mass [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The data supports the hypothesis that onion plants should be grown at a **pH of 5.5**, as at pH 5.5 the onion root has the **highest mitotic index** ($\checkmark$), however, the **error bars for pH overlap** with the error bars for **pH 5 and 6** ($\checkmark$) suggesting that there is no significant difference between the mitotic index at those pHs.

4. Any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ enzymes **OR** cyclin-dependent kinases control the progression of cells through mitosis [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ at pH 9, enzymes may begin to denature **OR** the active site changes shape irreversibly [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ at pH 9, the formation of the mitotic spindle may be adversely affected **OR** any other named process involved in mitosis [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ so less cells will be actively undergoing mitosis [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The mitotic index at pH 9 is relatively lower, as progression through the cell cycle is controlled by the activity of **cyclin dependent kinases** ($\checkmark$). Cyclin dependent kinases are enzymes so may begin to **denature** at pH 9 ($\checkmark$), so **less cells** will undergo **mitosis** ($\checkmark$), reducing the mitotic index.
