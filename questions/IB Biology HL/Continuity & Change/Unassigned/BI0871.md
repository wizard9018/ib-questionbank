# BI0871

The diagram shows prophase, an early stage of mitosis.

![BIO871ii](images/BIO871ii.png)

What is the fate of the nuclear membrane immediately after prophase is complete?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Forms pores to allow movement of molecules in and out of the nucleus

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Reassembles around the sister chromatids

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Breaks down into small vesicles

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Reforms at the poles of the cell

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because immediately after prophase is prometaphase, during which the nuclear membrane breaks down into small vesicles.

**Choice A** is incorrect because, during prophase, the nuclear membrane already has existing pores. **Choice B** is incorrect because the nuclear membrane only reassembles during telophase, which is the last stage of mitosis, and not immediately after prophase. **Choice D** is incorrect because the mitotic spindle forms between the poles of the cell immediately after prophase.
