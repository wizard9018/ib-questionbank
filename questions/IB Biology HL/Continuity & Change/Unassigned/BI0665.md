# BI0665

**Maximum mark: 16**

1. Outline the importance of international cooperation in fighting infectious diseases. [3 marks]

2. Outline the role of monoclonal antibodies in home pregnancy tests. [5 marks]

3. Explain the specific immune response to a pathogen in terms of antibodies and lymphocytes.    [8 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ infectious diseases can cross borders [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ if a disease is found in one population it can spread to other populations [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ herd immunity reduces rate of transmission [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ knowledge needs to be shared internationally so that all countries can take measures to prevent disease transmission **OR** so that countries know the best ways to prevent disease transmission [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ resources such as vaccinations and other medications need to be shared between richer and poorer countries  [1 mark]

    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
International cooperation is needed to fight infectious diseases because diseases **cross borders** ($\checkmark$). **Knowledge** ($\checkmark$) and **vaccines** **need to be shared**  ($\checkmark$) between richer and poorer countries so that all countries can **effectively reduce rates of transmission**.

2. any **five** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ home pregnancy tests contain monoclonal antibodies to hCG [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ home pregnancy tests have a test site and control site    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the urine of a pregnant woman contains hCG [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ if hCG is present, it binds to monoclonal antibodies [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the monoclonal antibodies to hCG have a dye attached    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ if there are antibodies with hCG attached, they will bind at the test site producing a line     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ monoclonal antibodies with dye attached bind to the control site regardless of the presence of hCG [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ a line at the control site shows that the test is valid [1 mark]

    [5 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
Home pregnancy tests contain monoclonal antibodies for the hormone **hCG** ($\checkmark$), which is found in the **urine of pregnant women** ($\checkmark$). If hCG is present it **binds to monoclonal antibodies** ($\checkmark$) near the start of the strip. The antibodies move along the strip with the urine. If there are antibodies with hCG attached, they bind to the **test site producing a line** ($\checkmark$), which indicates a positive result. The test is considered to be valid if there is a line appearing at the **control site** ($\checkmark$), regardless of the pregnancy status.

3. any **eight** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ B lymphocytes and T lymphocytes are **both** part of the specific immune response [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ B lymphocytes and T lymphocytes **both** react to antigens [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ B lymphocytes and T lymphocytes **both** produce memory cells [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ B lymphocytes produce antibodies that are specific to one antigen [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ helper T cells activate B lymphocytes **OR** activated helper T cells activate B lymphocytes     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ B lymphocytes divide, producing plasma B cells     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ antibodies are produced by plasma B cells  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ antibodies bind to antigens on pathogens [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ antibodies cause agglutination **OR** antibodies cause pathogens to stick together [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ antibodies help phagocytes bind to and engulf pathogens [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ antibodies prevent pathogens from attaching to host cells [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ antibodies cause the destruction of pathogens [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ memory cells give long-term immunity [1 mark]

    [8 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
**B lymphocytes and T lymphocytes** are both part of the specific immune response ($\checkmark$) and **both react to antigens** ($\checkmark$). B lymphocytes and T lymphocytes **both produce memory cells** ($\checkmark$). **Helper T cells** activate **B lymphocytes** ($\checkmark$) which divide, producing memory B cells and **plasma B cells** ($\checkmark$). Plasma B cells produce **antibodies** ($\checkmark$). **Antibodies bind to antigens** ($\checkmark$). Antibodies help **phagocytes** bind to and **engulf pathogens** ($\checkmark$), **prevent pathogens from attaching to host cells** ($\checkmark$) and cause **agglutination of pathogens** ($\checkmark$).
