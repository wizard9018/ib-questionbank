# BI1425

Which of the following proteins is a hormone?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$  Haemoglobin

- B. $\hspace{1em}$  Collagen
- C. $\hspace{1em}$  Glucagon
- D. $\hspace{1em}$  Amylase

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Glucagon is a hormone involved in maintaining blood glucose levels; **Choice C** is, therefore, the correct answer.

Haemoglobin is a protein responsible for the transport of oxygen and carbon dioxide in red blood cells; **Choice A** is therefore not correct. Collagen is a structural protein, and amylase is an enzyme that catalyses the hydrolysis of starch; **Choices B** and **D** are, therefore, also not correct.
