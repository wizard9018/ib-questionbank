# BI2003

**Maximum mark: 10**

An investigation was carried out to estimate the osmolarity of potato tissue. Potato tissue samples were cut into cylinders of equal dimensions, and the mass of each was recorded. The cylinders were then submerged in sucrose solutions of varying concentrations for one hour, and their final mass was recorded.

Five cylinders were used at each concentration. The results are shown in the table below.

| Molarity / M | Mean change in mass / g | Mean percentage change in mass / % | Standard deviation of percentage change in mass / % |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 0.0 | 0.54 | 4.50 | 0.8 |
| 0.2 | 0.38 | 3.20 | 0.4 |
| 0.4 | 0.05 | 0.40 | 1.8 |
| 0.6 | -0.96 | -8.00 | 1.4 |
| 0.8 | -1.14 | -9.50 | 2.3 |

1. Explain why the results are shown as percentage change in mass rather than change in mass. [1 mark]

2. State, **two** variables, not described in the method above, that must be controlled in this investigation. Provide a reason for why each variable needs to be controlled. [4 marks]

3. Use the standard deviation values to comment on the consistency of the results from this investigation. [1 mark]

In a similar investigation, a graph was plotted using data from five trials at each concentration. The error bars shown on the graph represent ±1 standard deviation.

4. Using the line of best fit, estimate, with a reason, the osmolarity of the potato tissue. [2 marks]

5. Discuss the variability of the data shown in the graph. [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 **Percentage change in mass** is used because it **standardises** the results, **allowing a direct comparison** between the samples even if their **starting masses** are **different** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **four** of the following *(maximum. **two** marks for variables to be controlled and maximum **two** marks for appropriate reason matched to variable)*:

 $\ce{ -> }$ temperature [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BECAUSE** it affects kinetic energy of molecules , influencing the rate of osmosis [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ volume of solution [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BECAUSE** a small volume of solution would change concentration significantly as water moves into or out of the tissue **OR** it ensures the tissue is fully submerged [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ age **OR** type of potato [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BECAUSE** potato of different ages **OR** type may have different solute concentrations [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ presence of skin on potato [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BECAUSE** potato skin may have a different permeability to water compared to the inner tissue [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ same method of drying potato tissue before finding mass [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BECAUSE** drops of water on the potato tissue increase the mass [1 mark]
 [4 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 **Temperature** ($\checkmark$) needs to be kept constant because **it affects** the **kinetic energy** of water **molecules** which influences the **rate of osmosis** ($\checkmark$). Each cylinder of potato tissue also needs to be **dried** in the **same way** before recording the final mass, to ensure that **surface water** does not **artificially increase** the **final mass** ($\checkmark$).
