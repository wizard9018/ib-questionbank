# BI0975

Which is the most likely solute potential  (ψ${_s}$) of  50 cm:sup[3] distilled water if 10g of salt is added to it?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ -0.2 kPa

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.8 kPa

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 0 kPa

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.1 kPa

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Distilled water has a solute potential of 0 kPa, and with increasing solute concentration the solute potential decreases. -0.2 kPa is below 0 kPa, **Choice A** is correct.

**Choice C**  is incorrect as 0 kPa is the solute potential of distilled water. **Choices B** and **D**  are incorrect, as solute potentials cannot be above zero.
