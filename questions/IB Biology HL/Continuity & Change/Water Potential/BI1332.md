# BI1332

At which location in a plant would water potential be highest?

- A. Photosynthesising leaf

- B. Growing root

- C. Root hair cells

- D. Phloem

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

**Choice C** is correct because root hair cells absorb mineral ions mainly by active transport. The mineral ions decrease the water potential inside the cells, therefore water enters the root hair cells, increasing water potential.  

**Choice A** is incorrect because a photosynthesising leaf has a high concentration of sugars, so the water potential is low. Also, the leaves are losing water through their stomata, leading to a lower water potential. **Choice B** is incorrect because sugars are transported to meristems (locations of plant growth), so the water potential would be low. **Choice D** is incorrect because phloem transports sugars, so water potential is low.
