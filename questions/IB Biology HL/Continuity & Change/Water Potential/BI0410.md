# BI0410

Which of the following surface area to volume ratios would result in the highest rate of diffusion for a cell?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ The higher the surface area to volume ratio, the higher the rate of diffusion

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ The lower the surface area to volume ratio, the higher the rate of diffusion

-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ The higher the volume to surface area ratio, the higher the rate of diffusion

-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ The lower the volume to surface area ratio, the lower the rate of diffusion

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Having a high surface area to volume ratio results in increased rates of diffusion across the cell membrane as a greater surface area of the plasma membrane is available for the exchange of materials compared to the cell’s volume.

Lower surface area to volume ratios (**Choice B**) and higher volume to surface area ratios (**Choice C**) result in lower rates of diffusion. A lower volume to surface area ratio would result in an increased rate of diffusion, not a decreased rate (**Choice D**).
