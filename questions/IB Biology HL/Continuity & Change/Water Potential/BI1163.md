# BI1163

The image below shows red blood cells placed in an isotonic solution and a solution of unknown solute concentration. Which type of solution is that of unknown concentration, and what effect does this have on the volume of the cells?

![BI1163](images/BI1163.png)
[Source: Adapted from Zephyris. (2012). *Human Erythrocytes Osmotic Pressure Phase Contrast Plain [Micrograph]*. Retrieved April 21, 2022, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?curid=18401754. Copyright under CC BY-SA 3.0]

| | Type of solution | Effect on the volume of red blood cell |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hypotonic | Increase |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hypotonic | Decrease |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hypertonic | Increase |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hypertonic | Decrease |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
A hypotonic solution has a low concentration of solute and a high concentration of water compared to the inside of the cell. A red blood cell placed in a hypotonic solution will increase in volume, as water will move into the cell via osmosis, from an area of higher water concentration/lower solute concentration to an area of lower water potential concentration/higher solute concentration across the cell surface membrane. **Choice A**  is correct, and **Choice B** is incorrect.

A hypertonic solution has a higher concentration of solute and a lower concentration of water compared to the cell. A red blood cell placed in a hypertonic solution would shrivel or become crenated. **Choice C** is incorrect. Although the cell is in a hypotonic solution, this caused it to increase in volume, not decrease; **Choice D** is incorrect.
