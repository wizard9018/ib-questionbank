1. State what is meant by the term isotonic solution.    [1 mark]

2. Identify which concentrations were hypotonic and which were hypertonic to the red blood cells.    [2 marks]

3. If red blood cells were placed in a hypotonic solution, explain the likely changes in ion concentration in the cytoplasm of the cells.    [2 marks]

4. Describe the process of how a solid granule of NaCl becomes a solute when placed in water.    [2 marks]

5. Compare and contrast how animal and plant cells are affected by being placed in hypotonic solutions and hypertonic solutions.     [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ solute concentration **OR** water potential in the cytoplasm **OR** cytosol **OR** cell **OR** across the cell membrane is the same as its environment **OR** solution    [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    An isotonic solution is a solution that has the **same** tonicity as the **cytoplasm** of a cell ($\checkmark$).

2. **BOTH** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ concentration A was hypertonic to the red blood cells [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ concentration C was hypotonic to the red blood cells [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Concentration **A** was **hypertonic** to the red blood cells ($\checkmark$) and concentration **C** was **hypotonic** to the red blood cells ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ ion concentrations would decrease        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water would diffuse into the cells down its concentration gradients **OR** from higher water potential to lower water potential **OR** by osmosis **OR** water molecules diffuse into cell from a lower to higher solute concentration    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increased water concentration in red blood cells OR decreased ion concentration        [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The ion concentration would **decrease** ($\checkmark$) as a result of water **diffusing** into the cell by **osmosis** ($\checkmark$), which would **increase** the **percent** composition of **water** in the cytosol ($\checkmark$).

4. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ hydrogen bonds form between water molecules and Na+ and Cl- OR NaCl [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$slightly positive hydrogen atoms are attracted to the negatively charged chloride ions OR slightly negative oxygen atoms are attracted to the positively charged sodium ions [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water surrounding Na+ and Cl- ions weakens ionic bonds holding the NaCl crystal together [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ Na+ and Cl- ions separate and a shell of water molecules surround each ion [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ hydrated sodium and chloride ions are now dispersed throughout the water OR move independently of each other [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water surrounds the Na+ and Cl- ions [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water prevents ionic bonds from reforming [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    When NaCl is placed in water, polar **water molecules** form **hydrogen bonds** with the **Na⁺** and **Cl⁻** ions ($\checkmark$), surrounding each ion to form **hydration shells** (:span), which **prevent** the **ionic bonds between Na⁺ and Cl⁻** from **reforming** ($\checkmark$).

5. Any **three** of the following (students must include **one** similarity and **one** difference for full marks):

    **In hypotonic solutions…**

    **Similarity** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ in **BOTH** plant and animal cells, water diffuses into the cells **OR** moves in by osmosis    [1 mark]

    **Difference** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ plant cells become turgid **WHEREAS** animal cells may lyse     [1 mark]

    **In hypertonic solutions…**

    **Similarity** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ in **BOTH** plant and animal cells, water diffuses out of the cells **OR** moves out by osmosis    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** plant and animal cells may die in hypertonic solutions [1 mark]

    **Differences** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ in animal cells, the cell will shrivel **OR** become crenated **WHEREAS** in plant cells the cell will become plasmolysed **OR** flaccid    [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    In hypotonic solutions, water **diffuses into** plant and animal cells ($\checkmark$) and in hypertonic solutions water **diffuses out** of plant and animal cells ($\checkmark$).  However, **animal cells** may **lyse in hypotonic solutions**, whereas **plant cells** become **turgid** ($\checkmark$).  Finally, **plant cells** may become **plasmolysed in hypertonic solutions**, whereas **animal cells** may become **crenated** ($\checkmark$).
