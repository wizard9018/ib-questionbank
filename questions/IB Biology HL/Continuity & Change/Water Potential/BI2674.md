# BI2674

Which row best describes the movement of water across cell membranes?

| | Water concentration gradient | Solute concentration gradient |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | High $\rarr$ Low | High $\rarr$ Low|
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | High $\rarr$ Low | Low $\rarr$ High |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Low $\rarr$ High | High $\rarr$ Low|
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Low $\rarr$ High | Low $\rarr$ High |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because osmosis is a form of diffusion, and diffusion always involves movement from a region of higher concentration to a region of lower concentration. In the case of water, this means movement from high water concentration to low water concentration, which corresponds to low solute concentration to high solute concentration, since solutes reduce the free energy of water (i.e., lower its water potential, $\psi$).

**Choice A, Choice C**, and **Choice D** are incorrect because they show combinations that contradict the natural direction of osmosis—either by suggesting water moves from low to high concentration, or by reversing the solute gradient.
