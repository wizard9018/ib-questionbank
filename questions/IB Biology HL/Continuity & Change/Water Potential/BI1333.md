# BI1333

Which factor plays a crucial role in determining the pressure potential component of water potential in plants?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Solute concentration in the soil

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Turgor pressure within the cells

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Atmospheric pressure

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Surface area of the leaves

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Turgor pressure, the pressure exerted by the cell contents against the cell wall, is a key component of pressure potential within plant cells. **Choice B** is, therefore, correct.

While solute concentration in the soil (**Choice A**) influences water uptake by plant roots, it primarily affects solute potential rather than pressure potential. The solute concentration gradient between the soil and root cells affects the movement of water into the roots but does not directly contribute to pressure potential within the cells.
Pressure potential relates to the pressure exerted by the cell contents against the cell wall due to water uptake, not to atmospheric pressure; **Choice C** is incorrect.
The surface area of leaves affects factors like gas exchange and transpiration but does not directly contribute to pressure potential within cells. **Choice D** is, therefore, incorrect.
