# BI1334

Which environmental condition generally increases water potential in the cells of plant leaves?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ High humidity

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ High temperature

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ High atmospheric pressure

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ High light intensity

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
High humidity reduces the water potential gradient between the inside of the leaf and its surrounding environment, thereby decreasing the rate of water loss via evaporation. This allows water to accumulate in the plant's cells, thus increasing their water potential. **Choice A** is correct.

High temperatures are likely to lead to greater loss of water from the leaves, and so reduce water potential; **Choice B** is therefore incorrect. High atmospheric pressure does not significantly influence water potential within plant cells; **Choice C** is therefore incorrect. High light intensity is likely to increase water loss from the leaves, and so decrease the water potential inside the cells of the leaf; **Choice D** is therefore incorrect.
