# BI2677

The pressure potential inside the tissue of a terrestrial plant was calculated to be -2877 kPa. Which type of tissue is this likely to have been?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Xylem vessel

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Phloem sieve tube

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Phloem companion cell

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Root hair cell

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because a strongly negative pressure potential (–2877 kPa) indicates tension, not actual physical compression. Such extreme negative pressure is typical of xylem vessels, especially in transpiring plants, where water is being pulled up from the roots through the stem. This tension is due to cohesion and adhesion of water molecules, as well as evaporation from leaf surfaces during transpiration.

**Choice B, C** and **D** are incorrect because phloem and root hair cells operate under positive pressure. The phloem is responsible for the translocation of sugars and other nutrients around the plant; root hair cells absorb water from the soil by osmosis. Therefore, both cell types are typically under positive pressure, to ‘push’ materials around the plant.
