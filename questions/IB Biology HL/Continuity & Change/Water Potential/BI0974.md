# BI0974

The diagram shows a piece of potato tissue that has been placed in distilled water. Which is the correct reason for the movement of water from the distilled water into the potato cells?

![beaker and potato tissue](<images/beaker and potato tissue.png>)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The distilled water has a lower water potential than the cytoplasm of the potato cells

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The cytoplasm of the potato cells has a lower solute potential than the distilled water

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The cytoplasm of the potato cells has a higher water potential than the distilled water

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The distilled water has a lower solute potential than the cytoplasm of the potato cells

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Distilled water has a solute potential of zero, and the cytoplasm of the potato cells has a negative solute potential due to the presence of solute. Water potential is dependent on solute potential and pressure potential, and the lower solute potential of the cytoplasm will make the water potential inside the potato cells lower, or more negative. This means water will move by osmosis from an area of higher, or less negative, water potential (distilled water) to an area of lower, or more negative, water potential (cytoplasm). **Choice B** is therefore correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect as the distilled water has a higher water potential than the cytoplasm of the potato cells, due to the absence of solutes. **Choice C** is incorrect as the cytoplasm of the potato cells has a lower water potential than the distilled water due to the presence of solutes. **Choice D** is incorrect as the distilled water has a higher solute potential than the cytoplasm of the potato cells.
