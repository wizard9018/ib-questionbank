# BI0408

The graph below shows the change in mass of a potato slice (%) at different concentrations of sucrose solution ($mol$ $dm^{-3}$).

![BI0408QB](images/BI0408QB.png)

Which of the concentrations is the closest estimate of the molarity of the potato cells?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.01 $mol$ $dm^{-3}$

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.22 $mol$ $dm^{-3}$
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.38 $mol$ $dm^{-3}$
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.57 $mol$ $dm^{-3}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The trendline crossing the horizontal axis at a 0.0% change in mass indicates that the potato cells are in an isotonic solution with no net movement of water by osmosis. Here, the concentration of the sucrose solution would be equal to the molarity of the potato cells.

The changes in mass at sucrose concentrations of 0.01 $mol$ $dm^{-3}$ (**Choice A**) and 0.22 $mol$ $dm^{-3}$ (**Choice B**) is positive, and the change is negative at 0.57 $mol$ $dm^{-3}$ (**Choice D**) thus indicating that the concentrations of the sucrose solutions were not equal to the molarity of the potato cells.
