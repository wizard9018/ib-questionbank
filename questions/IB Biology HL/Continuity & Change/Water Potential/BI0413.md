# BI0413

**Maximum mark: 16**

Membrane transport is important for allowing particles to move across the cell membrane and respond to changes in the chemical composition of the extracellular fluid.

1. Outline the effects of placing a liver cell in a hypotonic solution.    [3 marks]

2. Outline how the fluidity of membranes allows materials to be taken into cells or to be released from cells.  [6 marks]

3. Compare and contrast four types of membrane transport. Include an example of each. [7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ the liver cell swells **OR** enlarges **OR** expands **OR** increase in volume [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the liver cell ruptures **OR** burst **OR** lyses    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water diffuses into the liver cell **OR** water moves from the medium of lower solute concentration  to a medium where there is higher solute concentration  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ osmotic pressure favors the movement of water into the cell **OR** water will diffuse from a medium of lower osmotic pressure to higher osmotic pressure [1 mark]

    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    Once a liver cell is placed in a hypotonic solution, **water** will diffuse from a **medium of lower solute concentration to a region of higher solute concentration** ($\checkmark$) by osmosis. This results in **an influx of water into the liver cell** that **causes the cell to swell**($\checkmark$) and eventually **rupture** ($\checkmark$) depending on how steep the concentration gradient is.

2. any **six** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ the phospholipid composition of the cell membrane allows it to be in a fluid state [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the fluidity of the membrane allows membranes to change shape **OR** move [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the fluidity of the membrane allows the membrane to interact **OR** fuse with vesicles **OR** vesicles pinch off from  the membrane  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ vesicles are composed of phospholipids [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ during exocytosis, material to be exported by the cell is packaged in membrane-bound vesicles that fuse with the plasma membrane [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ during exocytosis, vesicles release their contents to the cell exterior and the membrane flattens out again [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ during endocytosis, materials to be imported into the cell is packaged in membrane-bound vesicles that pinch off  the plasma membrane and are transported to the cell interior     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ proteins in the membrane form vesicles using ATP [1 mark]

    [6 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\
    The cell membrane is a **phospholipid bilayer that is fluid** ($\checkmark$). This allows **membranes to change shape** ($\checkmark$) and to **form vesicles that pinch off of it or receive vesicles that fuse with it.** ($\checkmark$)  These vesicles are involved in membrane transport known as endocytosis and exocytosis. **Exocytosis** is an active form of transport in which cells export material to the cell membrane or to the extracellular fluid via vesicles. These vesicles are transported to the cell surface where they **fuse with the plasma membrane and release their contents** to the extracellular fluid ($\checkmark$). Vesicles fusing with the cell membrane help rebuild the cell membrane as they too are composed of **phospholipids**. ($\checkmark$) In **endocytosis**, material entering the cell is engulfed by the cell membrane which then pinches inwards to form a vesicle surrounding the molecules. These vesicles are then **transported to the interior of the cell** ($\checkmark$).
