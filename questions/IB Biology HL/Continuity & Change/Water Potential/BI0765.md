# BI0765

**Maximum mark: 10**

In an investigation into the effect of solute concentration on diffusion rate, starch solutions, of varying concentrations, were placed inside starch permeable membrane balloons. Each balloon was put into a beaker of distilled water and at 3 minute intervals, samples were taken from the solution surrounding the membrane balloons in each beaker, placed on spotting tiles and tested for the presence of starch. The time taken for a positive test to show was recorded for each trial.

![BI0765A](images/BI0765A.png)

The results are shown in the graph.

![BI0765COR](images/BI0765COR.png)

1.
 1. Identify the dependent and independent variable in this investigation. [2 marks]

 2. State one variable, other than time of testing, that should be kept constant in this investigation to ensure the results are valid. [1 mark]

2. Describe a positive test for starch. [1 mark]

3. Explain why the method used in this study would not be appropriate for an investigation into the effects of solute concentration on active transport. [2 marks]

4.
 1. Samples taken from the 5% and 10% starch solution both tested positive for starch after 3 minutes. Suggest a way in which the experiment could be improved to provide a more accurate result. [1 mark]

 2. Predict the time taken for a positive starch test for a starch concentration of 3%. [1 mark]

5. Suggest modifications to the methodology that would make it suitable to investigate the effect of glucose concentration on the rate of diffusion. [2 marks]
