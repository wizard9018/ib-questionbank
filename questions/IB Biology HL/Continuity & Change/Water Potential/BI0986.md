# BI0986

Two patients were given solutions intravenously as part of a medical treatment.  One patient had a generally negative health outcome while the other had a positive health outcome.  Blood samples were taken from both patients and then viewed through a light microscope.

![BI0986](images/BI0986.png)
[Source: Adapted from Zephyris (2012, February 16). Tonicity-Tonicity.  Wiki.  Retrieved July 24th, 2023, from https:// en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File: Human_Erythrocytes_OsmoticPressure_PhaseContrast_Plain.svg. CC 3.0]

Which correctly identifies the health outcomes for the two patients and the likely reasons for these outcomes?

| | Patient A | Patient A | Patient B | Patient B
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-:
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Negative health outcome | Solution was hypotonic | Survived | Solution was isotonic |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Negative health outcome | Solution was hypertonic | Survived | Solution was isotonic|
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Survived | Solution was hypertonic| Negative health outcome | Solution was hypotonic |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Survived | Solution was isotonic | Negative health outcome| Solution was hypertonic |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because when red blood cells are exposed to hypertonic solutions, water diffuses out of the cells causing the cells to become crenated (shrivelled) which can lead to negative effects on a person's health.  Patient B survived because red blood cells typically function normally in isotonic solutions.

**Choices A** and **C** are incorrect because in hypotonic solutions, red blood cells may lyse whereas the red blood cells of patient A are crenated and the those of patient B have retained their original shape.  **Choice D** is incorrect because the red blood cells of patient A are crenated and in an isotonic solution they would not be crenated.  In patient B the red blood cells are not crenated which would be expected if they were placed in an isotonic solution.
