# BI1328

Which of the following factors is essential for calculating water potential in a solution?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Surface area of the container

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Rate of transpiration in the plant

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Solute concentration in the solution

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ pH level of the surrounding environment

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

Solute concentration is a crucial factor in determining water potential. A higher solute concentration results in a more negative solute potential, contributing to a lower water potential. **Choice C** is therefore correct.

While surface area can affect the rate of evaporation or condensation it does not directly affect water potential. **Choice A** is therefore incorrect.  Transpiration affects water potential gradients, but it is not used in the calculation of water potential. **Choice B** is therefore not correct.  pH level can influence various biochemical reactions and cellular processes, but it does not directly impact water potential. **Choice D** is therefore incorrect.
