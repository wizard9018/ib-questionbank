## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because the cell becomes turgid. Hypotonic solution means there is a higher water concentration outside the cell and, therefore a lower solute concentration. This causes water to diffuse in by osmosis. The influx of water causes the solute concentration inside the cell to reduce, and the extra water causes an increase in pressure. Overall, the water potential increases inside the cell. \
**Choice B** is incorrect because the cell is unchanged in shape, i.e. still flaccid. This would be expected from placing the cell in an isotonic solution, not a hypotonic one. \
**Choice C** is incorrect because, while it correctly shows the cell becoming turgid, the solute concentration does not increase; the extra water moving into the cell causes the solute concentration to fall within the cell. \
**Choice D** is incorrect because the cell is shown as plasmolysed; this would be the result of placing the tissue in a hypertonic solution, not a hypotonic one.
