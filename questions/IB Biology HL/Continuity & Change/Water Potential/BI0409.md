# BI0409

Which statement best describes the osmotic concentration of a solution used to bathe organs for use in organ transplant procedures?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ The solution must have an osmotic concentration higher than that of the organ to be transplanted.

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ The solution must have an osmotic concentration lower than that of the organ to be transplanted.

-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ The solution must be isotonic compared to the cells of the organ to be transplanted.

-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ The solution must be mixed with cells from the organ to be transplanted.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The solution used to bathe organs to be used in a transplant should have an osmotic concentration equal to that of the organ to be transplanted and so the solution is isotonic compared to the organ’s tissues.

A solution with an osmotic concentration higher than that of the organ to be transplanted (**Choice A**) would cause crenation of tissue cells, while a solution with an osmotic concentration lower than that of the organ to be transplanted (**Choice B**) would cause lysis of organ cells. There are no described protocols that indicate that the solution should be mixed with cells from the organ to be transplanted (**Choice D**).
