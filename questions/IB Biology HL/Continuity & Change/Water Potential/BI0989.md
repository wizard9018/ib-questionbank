# BI0989

What is the likely effect on oxygen transport in a human if a hypotonic solution is given intravenously?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Oxygen transport by red blood cells will increase.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Oxygen transport in blood plasma will increase.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Oxygen transport in blood plasma will decrease.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Oxygen transport by red blood cells will decrease.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because a hypotonic solution can cause red blood cells to swell and lyse, reducing the number of cells available to transport oxygen.

**Choice A** is incorrect because red blood cell lysis decreases, not increases, oxygen transport.

**Choices B** and **C** are incorrect  because adding a hypotonic solution does not significantly change the amount of oxygen dissolved in blood plasma.
