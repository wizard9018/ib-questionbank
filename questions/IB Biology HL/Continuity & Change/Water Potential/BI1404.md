# BI1404

Four shell-less eggs were placed into 4 different solutions for 24 hours, after the mass of each was recorded. After 24 hours the eggs were removed from the solution, dried, and their final mass was recorded. The ratio of the final mass to the initial mass was then calculated. The results are shown below.

| Egg | Ratio of final mass to initial mass |
| :-: | :-: |
| A | 1.31:1 |
| B | 0.85:1 |
| C | 1.14:1 |
| D | 0.92:1 |

Which egg was placed in the solution nearest to being isotonic?

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The solution nearest to being isotonic is the one which is closest to the solute concentration inside the egg. This solution would result in the least net osmotic movement of water across the egg membrane and, therefore, the least change in mass. The egg with the ratio of final mass to initial mass which is closest to 1:1 is **Choice D**, with a ratio of 0.92:1.

**Choice A** is the egg that gained the most mass, indicating it was placed in the most hypotonic solution. **Choice C** was placed into a weaker hypotonic solution, as it gained less mass. Egg **Choice B**, lost the most mass and so was placed in the most hypertonic solution.
