# BI1091

Below is a diagram showing three red blood cells that have been placed in solutions of different solute concentrations.

![red blood cells hypotonic hypertonic isotonic](<images/red blood cells hypotonic hypertonic isotonic.png>)

Which row correctly matches each cell with the type of solution that it has been immersed in?

| | I | II | III
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Isotonic | Hypertonic | Hypotonic |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hypertonic | Hypotonic | Isotonic |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hypotonic | Isotonic | Hypertonic |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Isotonic | Hypotonic | Hypertonic|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Cell I** is the normal shape for a red blood cell, so there has been no net movement of water into or out of the cell. **Cell II** is swollen due to a net movement of water into the cell. **Cell II** has therefore been placed in a hypotonic solution, which is a solution with a lower solute concentration than inside the cell, and so water has moved into the cell by osmosis. **Cell III** is crenated due to a net movement of water out of the cell. **Cell III** has therefore been placed in a hypertonic solution, which is a solution with a higher solute concentration than inside the cell, and so water has moved out of the cell by osmosis. **Choice D** correctly matches the cells with the solution they have been put into.

**Cell II** has been put into a hypotonic solution, and **Cell III** has been put into a hypertonic solution, therefore, **Choice A** is incorrect. All cells are incorrectly matched with solutions in **Choice B**. **Cell I** has been put into an isotonic solution, and **Cell II** has been put into a hypertonic solution, therefore, **Choice C** is incorrect.
