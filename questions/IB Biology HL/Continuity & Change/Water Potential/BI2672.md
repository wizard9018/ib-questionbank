# BI2672

Which of the following combinations of water potential values will result in the net movement of water into the cell?

| | Outside the cell | Inside the cell |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | 400 | 100 |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | -250 | -25 |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | 0 | 52 |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | -20 | -263 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because water potential values are always negative numbers when any amount of solute is present; the highest possible value (pure water) is zero. The value outside the cell (−20) is a higher number than the value inside the cell (−263), so water will diffuse down its concentration gradient, −20 $\rarr$ −263 kPa.

**Choice A** and **Choice C** are incorrect because water potential values are always negative numbers; the highest possible value, for pure water, is zero.

**Choice B** is incorrect because −250 outside the cell is a lower number than −25 inside the cell. In such a case, water would diffuse out of the cell, not into it.
