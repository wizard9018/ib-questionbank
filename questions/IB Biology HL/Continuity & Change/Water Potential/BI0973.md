# BI0973

Which is **not** a correct definition or equation for water potential?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The sum of the solute and pressure potential of a solvent

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ ψ${_w}$ = ψ${_s}$ + ψ${_p}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The direction that water will move through a semipermeable membrane

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The potential energy of water per unit volume

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Water potential refers to the energy stored in water, not the direction that water will move. **Choice C** is not true of water potential, and so is the correct choice.

**Choice B**  represents the correct equation for water potential, where ψ${_w}$ represents water potential, ψ${_s}$ represents solute potential, and ψ${_p}$  represents pressure potential. **Choice A** is true of water potential and is the same as the equation in **Choice B** but in words rather than symbols. **Choice D**  is a true definition of water potential. **Choices A**, **B** and **D** are, therefore all incorrect answers to the question.
