# BI0976

The diagram shows the setup for an osmosis experiment. The potato tissue was placed in a solution of unknown concentration for 3 days. After 3 days, the potato had lost mass. Which type of solution was the potato placed in, and what was the effect on the pressure potential?

![beaker and potato tissue](<images/beaker and potato tissue.png>)

| | Concentration of solution | Effect on pressure potential of tissue |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hypertonic | Decrease |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hypotonic | Increase |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hypertonic| Increase |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hypotonic | Decrease |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The term hypertonic means that the unknown solution has a higher concentration of solutes than the potato tissue, indicating that the solution has a lower, or more negative, water potential than inside the potato cells. This will cause water to move out of the potato cells via osmosis, decreasing the pressure inside the cells and thereby decreasing the pressure potential. **Choice A** is correct.

**Choices B** and **D** are incorrect as the solution must be hypertonic for water to leave the potato cells and cause the loss in mass. **Choice C** is incorrect, as when the potato tissue loses mass, the pressure inside the potato cells will decrease, decreasing the pressure potential inside the cells.
