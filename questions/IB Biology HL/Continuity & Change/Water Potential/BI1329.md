# BI1329

The diagram represents two solutions with different water potentials separated by a selectively permeable membrane.

![Water and Sugar Molecule Distribution](<images/Water and Sugar Molecule Distribution.png>)

Select the row with the correct direction for the net movement of water molecules and the correct reason for the movement.

| | Direction of Movement | Reason of Movement |

| :-: | :-: | :-: |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$A.$\hspace{1.5em}$| Left to Right | Lower water potential on left of membrane |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$B.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Left to Right | Higher water potential on left of membrane |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$C.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Right to Left | Lower water potential on left of membrane |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$D.$\hspace{1.5em}$ | Right to Left | Higher water potential on left of membrane |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The solution on the left has a higher water potential, or lower solute potential, than the right-hand side. This is because even though both sides have the same number of water molecules, there are fewer glucose molecules on the left-hand side. The net movement of water molecules will be from the higher water potential (or lower solute potential), on the left to the lower water potential (or higher solute potential) on the right; **Choice B** is, therefore, correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the solution on the left does have a higher water potential, but water will move from left to right. **Choice C** is incorrect because water molecules will move from left to right. **Choice D** is incorrect because water molecules will move from left to right because there is a higher water potential on the left.
