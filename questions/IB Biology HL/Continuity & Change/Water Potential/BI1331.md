# BI1331

Which equation is the correct equation for the water potential of cells with cell walls?

- A.$\Psi_w$ = $\Psi_w$ + $\Psi_p$

- B.$\Psi_w$ = $\Psi_s$ $-$ $\Psi_p$

- C.$\Psi_w$ = $\Psi_s$ + $\Psi_p$

- D.$\Psi_w$ = $\Psi_p$ $-$ $\Psi_s$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

**Choice C** is correct because water potential is equal to solute potential plus pressure potential. **Choice A** is incorrect because water potential is not equal to water potential plus pressure potential. **Choice B** and **Choice D** are incorrect because water potential is calculated by adding solute and pressure potential, not subtracting.
