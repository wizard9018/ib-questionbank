# BI1473

Different plant tissues were placed in solutions of different sucrose concentrations, and percentage change in mass was recorded. What is the concentration of sucrose in the potato tissue?

![BI1473](images/BI1473.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.33 $\mu$M

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 5.8 $\mu$M

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.53 $\mu$M

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 0.00 $\mu$M

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

Isotonic solutions have the same concentration of sucrose as the plant tissue. When tissue is placed in an isotonic solution, the net movement of solvent is zero, therefore the mass of the potato would not change. The sucrose concentration at which the percentage change in mass is 0% is approximately 0.53 $\mu$M. **Choice C** is correct.

**Choices A, B** and **D** are incorrect.
