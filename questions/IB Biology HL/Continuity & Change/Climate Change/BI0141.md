# BI0141

Which of the following greenhouse gases is correctly paired with its source?

| | Greenhouse gas | Source |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| A. $\hspace{1em}$ | Methane | Melting of permafrost in frozen tundra |
| B. $\hspace{1em}$ | Nitrous oxide | Landfills |
| C. $\hspace{1em}$ | Carbon dioxide | Production of aluminium and magnesium |
| D. $\hspace{1em}$ | Fluorinated gases | Melting of permafrost in frozen tundra |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The melting of permafrost releases methane gas into the atmosphere. Other sources of methane include landfills, oil and natural gas systems, and agriculture.

Nitrous oxide (**Choice B**) is emitted from the combustion of fossil fuels, from solid waste, and also from agricultural sources. Carbon dioxide (**Choice C**) is released by the combustion of fossil fuels, solid waste, trees and other biological materials. Fluorinated gases like hydrofluorocarbons
(**Choice D**) are emitted from household and industrial processes; even if present in small amounts, they trap much more heat than carbon dioxide.
