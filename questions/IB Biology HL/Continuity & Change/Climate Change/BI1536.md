# BI1536

Which of the following are **NOT** associated with the tipping point from net carbon storage to net release in boreal forests?

- I. $\hspace{1.6em}$ The influence of higher temperatures and reduced winter snow cover on the increased occurrence and severity of wildfires.

- II. $\hspace{1.3em}$ Increased incidence of drought and decrease in the growth rate of forest vegetation in taiga, leading to forest browning.

- III. $\hspace{1em}$ The accelerated growth of tropical forests as a result of higher levels of atmospheric carbon dioxide.

- IV. $\hspace{1em}$ The combustion of stored organic carbon due to more frequent and severe wildfires in boreal forests.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and IV only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II, III and IV only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

Higher temperatures and decreased winter snowfall lead to drier conditions, increasing the frequency and severity of wildfires. These fires contribute to a net carbon loss as trees, which normally act as carbon sinks, are destroyed.  **Statement I** is, therefore, a true statement.

Warmer temperatures and reduced precipitation, leading to drought conditions. Drought conditions reduce forest vegetation growth, causing forest browning, which weakens the ability of the forest to absorb and store carbon. This contributes to the shift from net carbon storage to net carbon release. **Statement II** is, therefore, a true statement.

Increased frequency and intensity of wildfires result in the burning of stored carbon in the form of dead wood, leaf litter, and soil organic matter. This combustion releases significant amounts of carbon dioxide back into the atmosphere, increasing the shift from carbon sink to carbon source in these forests. **Statement IV** is, therefore, a true statement.

**Statement III** describes the accelerated growth of tropical forests due to higher CO₂ levels, which has **NO** direct impact on the tipping point of boreal forests.

**Choice B** is therefore correct as it includes the only statement of conditions **NOT** associated with the tipping point of net carbon storage to net carbon release in boreal forests.

**Choices A, C** and **D** are incorrect as they all include statements about conditions that **ARE** associated with the tipping point.
