# BI0146

Scientists predict that global warming will have devastating effects in the future. Which of the following are the consequences of global warming?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Coastal flooding and loss of coastal cities  |
  | II. | Bleaching of coral reefs |
  | III. | Increased spread of tropical diseases  |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Scientists predict that global warming will have devastating effects which include coastal flooding, loss of coastal cities, and loss of atolls in places like the Maldives.  Scientists predict a majority of corals will experience bleaching as a result of climate change.   As biomes shift in latitude more tropical diseases will spread towards the north and south poles because pathogens thrive in warmer environments.

All other choices (**Choice A, Choice B and Choice C**) do not include all the predicted effects of global warming.
