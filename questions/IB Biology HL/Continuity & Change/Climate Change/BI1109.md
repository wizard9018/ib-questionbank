# BI1109

Which of the following is an example of evolution due to climate change?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Increase in brown tawny owl morphs in Finland in response to change in snow levels

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Increase in the number of life cycles of spruce bark beetle on north-facing slopes of a boreal forest in one year

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Increase in numbers of bees in spring in temperate regions in response to increased temperatures breaking hibernation early

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Increase of reindeer calves during one breeding season due to warmer temperatures

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Microevolution involves changes in the genetic makeup of a population over generations in response to selective pressures. **Choice A** is an example of such a change, as it shows an increase in the brown morph of owls due to better camouflage in areas with reduced snow cover caused by increasing temperatures.
**Choice C** is incorrect as it represents a phenological response to climate change, meaning a change in the timing of life cycle events, but it does not involve genetic changes in the bee population. The reduction in the number of bees is due to a lack of food caused by flowers not being open. Flowers in temperate zones open in response to the length of the day, not temperature.  **Choice D** is incorrect; is not an example of microevolution, but rather a disruption of a phenological event caused by climate change. Climate change is likely to reduce the number of calves due to the disruption to the synchrony of the growth of plants that the reindeer eat and the return of the reindeer to their breeding sites. **Choice B** is also incorrect as it is a response to temperature caused by the position of the tree where the spruce bark beetle is living. The spruce bark beetle tends to go through one life cycle a year on the north-facing slopes, and two on south-facing slopes which get more sun.
