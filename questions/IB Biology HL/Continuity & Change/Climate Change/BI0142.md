# BI0142

![BI0142](images/BI0142.png)
[Source: Adapted from  Inaglory, B. (2005, September). *File: permafrost pattern.jpg - Wikimedia Commons*. Retrieved May 2, 2022, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Permafrost_pattern.jpg Copyright under CC BY-SA 3.0]

Scientists have been studying the effects of global warming for some time now, and are worried about the thawing of frozen ice and permafrost in the Arctic region. The image above was taken from a helicopter and shows the crack pattern in permafrost.

Which of the following statements best describes the concerns of scientists?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | There is frozen organic matter in the Arctic which contains ancient bacteria and viruses  |
  | II. | With the thawing of ice and organic matter, more carbon dioxide will be released into the air |
  | III. | The melting of permafrost in the Arctic will cause the release of methane |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Dead plants and animals have been frozen in permafrost for millions of years. When permafrost thaws, so will the ancient bacteria and viruses which could infect other organisms (**Statement I**).  The thawing of ice and permafrost will lead to the release of both carbon dioxide  (**Statement II**) and methane  (**Statement III**)  from the permafrost.

All statements are true. The other choices (**Choices A, C and D**) do not include all statements.
