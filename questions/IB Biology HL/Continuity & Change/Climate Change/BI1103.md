# BI1103

Which term refers to research into the timing of biological events?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Phenology

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Synchrony

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Photoperiod

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Periodontology

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because phenology is the study of cyclic and seasonal changes, especially in relation to climate and living organisms. It involves observing and recording the timing of events such as flowering, migration, and hibernation. **Choice B** is incorrect because synchrony, in biology, refers to events occurring simultaneously or in coordination. **Choice C** is incorrect because photoperiod refers to the duration of light exposure in a 24-hour period and how it influences the behaviour and physiology of organisms. **Choice D** is incorrect because periodontology refers to a speciality in dentistry.
