# BI1107

Which of the following is/are primarily influenced by photoperiod?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Bird migration |
    | II. | Bird nesting |
    | III. | Hatching of eggs |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because only **Statement I** is correct, as many bird species rely on the changing day length as a signal for the timing of their migratory journeys.
Bird nesting (**Statement II**) is primarily influenced by factors such as food availability and temperature, and the hatching of eggs (**Statement III**)  is influenced mainly by the warmth of the nest and embryonic development. Therefore, **Choices B, C** and **D** are incorrect.
