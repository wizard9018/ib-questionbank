1. Describe the method used by scientists to deduce carbon dioxide concentrations from thousands of years ago.         [2 marks]

2. Explain why rising levels of atmospheric carbon dioxide are the main cause of climate change. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ columns of ice **OR** ice cores have been drilled in the Antarctic   [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ ice from deeper layers is older than ice closer to the surface [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ as new ice forms on the surface it traps air bubbles in the newly formed ice [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ bubbles of air trapped in the ice can be used to deduce atmospheric gas concentrations at the time the ice formed [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Ice that has been frozen over the years in the Antarctic **has been drilled and extracted** for analysis ($\checkmark$). The ice from **deeper down is older than the ice that is towards the surface** ($\checkmark$). The **bubbles of air trapped in the ice can be used to determine past concentrations of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere** ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ global temperature and CO$_2$ fluctuations are strongly correlated over long periods of time **OR** show a similar pattern of increase and decrease [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ since the start of the industrial revolution the use of fossil fuels has increased  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ since the start of the industrial revolution, more carbon dioxide has been released into the air  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ CO$_2$ is abundant in the atmosphere **OR** CO$_2$ is a stable atmospheric gas     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ CO$_2$ has been shown to absorb (and re-emit) long-wave radiation **OR** CO$_2$ is a greenhouse gas [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    CO$_2$ is a known **greenhouse gas** ($\checkmark$). Over the last 800,000 years, the patterns of global temperature and carbon dioxide have been cyclical, **showing similar patterns of increase and decrease** ($\checkmark$). In the last 200 years, since the start of industrialization, **the use of fossil fuels has increased** ($\checkmark$). Consequently, **more carbon dioxide is being released into the air** causing temperatures to rise ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ CO$_2$ from the air dissolves in water [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ some CO$_2$ combines with water to form the carbonic acid  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ lowers the pH **OR** makes it acidic   [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ seawater has a buffer capacity that combines the dissolved CO$_2$ with H$_2$O to make HCO$_3$:sup[-] **OR** increased CO$_2$ leads to the decrease of carbonate ion (**OR** CO$_{3}$ $^{2-}$) concentration [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ carbonate ions in the water are needed for the building of the coral  CaCO$_3$ skeletons [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increased acidity will cause dissolving of the calcium carbonate from the coral skeleton [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ threat to the existence of corals **OR** slow growth of corals  [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Increased carbon dioxide in the air leads to more **dissolution of carbon dioxide in oceans causing the formation of carbonic acid** ($\checkmark$). This leads to increased acidity **and a decrease in ocean water pH** ($\checkmark$). This **lowers the availability of carbonate ions in the water** ($\checkmark$), so corals **cannot make the calcium carbonate** needed to make their skeletons ($\checkmark$). Also, **the lower pH can dissolve the calcium carbonate in corals** which is threatening their existence ($\checkmark$).
