# BI1427

Which of the following is **NOT** a source of methane?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Decomposition of organic matter in landfill sites

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Digestive systems of ruminants

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Combustion of fossil fuels

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Melting permafrost

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because the combustion of fossil fuels produces carbon dioxide and water, not methane. Methane is consumed during combustion rather than released. Although methane can be emitted during the extraction, transportation, and storage of fossil fuels (particularly natural gas), it is not a product of the combustion process itself.

**Choice A** is incorrect because organic matter decomposes anaerobically in landfill sites, producing methane as a by-product of microbial activity.

**Choice B** is incorrect because ruminants such as cattle produce methane during digestion through enteric fermentation carried out by microorganisms in the rumen.

**Choice D** is incorrect because thawing permafrost can release methane that has been trapped in frozen soils and can also allow anaerobic decomposition of organic matter, both of which contribute methane to the atmosphere.
