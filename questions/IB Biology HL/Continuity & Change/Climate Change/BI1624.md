# BI1624

The emperor penguin (*Aptenodytes forsteri*) lives and breeds on land-fast ice in Antarctica. Their primary diet is fish and crustaceans caught in the nearby ocean. Satellite imagery has been used to count their populations accurately. The Antarctic population declined from 252,000 in 2009 to 226,000 in 2018.

The following factors are thought to have contributed to this population decline.

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}

    | | |

    | :-- | :-- |

    | I. | Increased competition from other penguin species |

    | II. | More difficulty accessing their food sources |

    | III. | Melting of land-fast ice and consequent loss of habitat |

Which of the following gives a rank order of the likely level of impact (highest to lowest) of each of these factors on the decline of the population?

$\hspace{3em}$ Highest impact $\to$ lowest impact

| | |

| :-- | :-- |

| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | III $\to$ II $\to$ I |

| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | I $\to$ III $\to$ II |

| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | II $\to$ I $\to$ III  |

| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | III $\to$ I $\to$ II |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

**Statement III** is the most impactful factor because emperor penguins use land-fast ice (ice fastened to the coast) for breeding and raising their chicks. Loss of the ice due to melting deprives them of their important breeding grounds, forcing them to seek land-based areas that are inherently more hazardous due to predation.

**Statement II** has a moderate impact as changes in sea ice can also affect the availability and accessibility of fish and crustaceans, their primary diet. Difficulties accessing food due to ice melt or changes in ocean currents can lead to decreased survival and breeding success.

**Statement I** has the lowest impact. While competition from other penguin species could have some impact, emperor penguins have unique breeding and feeding habits compared to other species. Therefore, this factor will likely have a lower impact than habitat loss and food accessibility issues.

Therefore, the order is **Statement III → II → I**, making **Choice A** the correct answer to the question.
