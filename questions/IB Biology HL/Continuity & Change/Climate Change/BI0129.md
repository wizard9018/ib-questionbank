# BI0129

Which of the following statements about methane in the atmosphere are true?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Methane is emitted during the extraction of natural gas, coal and oil |
  | II. | Methane is released into the atmosphere from cattle farms and other agricultural practices |
  | III. | Methane is short-lived in the atmosphere and is converted to carbon dioxide and water |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
All the statements are true, **Choice D** includes all the statements. Methane is emitted during the production and transport of fuels and is emitted from cattle farms. Atmospheric concentration of methane is limited because it is converted to carbon dioxide and water.

The other choices (**Choice A, Choice B, and Choice C**) do not include all the statements.
