# BI1623

Which of the following are the **three** major carbon sinks in a boreal forest?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}

    | | |

    | :-- | :-- |

    | I. | Peat deposits |

    | II. | Forest biomass |

    | III. | Lake water |

    | IV. | Soils |

| $\text{A.}$ $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ $\text{II, III and IV}$ $\hspace{1em}$ |

| --- | :-- |

| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ I, III and IV $\hspace{1em}$ |

| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and IV $\hspace{1em}$ |

| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III $\hspace{1em}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

**Statements I, II** and **IV** are all important carbon sinks. 

Peat deposits (**Statement I**) accumulate partially decayed organic matter in waterlogged conditions. They are significant carbon sinks because the anaerobic conditions slow down the decomposition process, allowing carbon to be stored for long periods.

Forest biomass (**Statement II**) refers to the total mass of all living plants and trees in the forest. Plants absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere (for photosynthesis) and form organic compounds, which are stored in their biomass. Thus, forests are important carbon sinks.

Soils (**Statement IV**), rather like peat deposits, contain dead and decaying organic matter (leaf litter and humus), microorganisms such as fungi and bacteria and small animals such as worms and soil insects, all of which add up to a large carbon sink held in the soil. The low temperatures in boreal regions slow down the rate of decomposition, allowing carbon to be stored for long periods. 

**Choice C** is, therefore, the correct answer to this question.

While boreal forests are characterised by numerous lakes (**Statement III**), they are not considered major carbon sinks. Lakes can store carbon in the form of dissolved carbon dioxide, organic carbon (like plant debris), and sediments that accumulate at the bottom. However, the amount of carbon stored is relatively small compared to other components of the boreal forest ecosystem.\

**Choices A, B,** and **D** are incorrect as they all include **Statement III**.
