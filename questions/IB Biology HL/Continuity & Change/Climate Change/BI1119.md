# BI1119

**Maximum mark: 8**

The tawny owl (*Strix aluco*) has two colour variants, a brown form and a grey/white form. Studies have shown that the grey/white form survives better during winter periods, with heavy snow cover.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ global warming is the increase in the average global temperature    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the increase in global average temperatures leads to changing weather patterns **OR** climate change         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ changes in climate lead to changes in snow cover **OR** reduced snow cover (in the tawny owls' environment)             [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ tawny owl brown variant has a selective advantage **OR** is fitter **OR** is better adapted to reduction in snow **OR** is better camouflaged in habitats with less snow **OR** vice versa             [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Global warming refers to the increase in the Earth's global **average temperature** ($\checkmark$). This increase in temperature changes the long-term **weather patterns** of different climates and environments ($\checkmark$). This could lead to a **reduction** in the snow cover of the tawny owl's habitat ($\checkmark$). This means that the grey/white colour variant isn't as well camouflaged, and therefore, the brown variant has a **selective advantage** ($\checkmark$) and is more fit to survive and reproduce.

1.
    2. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ as ocean temperatures increase, they release stored carbon dioxide into the atmosphere **WHICH** amplifies greenhouse effect **OR** environmental temperature [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ loss of snow **OR** ice cover **OR** arctic ice **OR** glaciers **OR** arctic ice-albedo feedback leading to an increase in the absorption of solar radiation **WHICH** contributes to global warming **OR** sea levels rise **WHICH** causes further melting  of ice sheets [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increased rate of peat **OR** undecomposed organic matter decomposition in permafrost releases more CO:sub[2] into the atmosphere **WHICH** increases greenhouse effect **OR** environmental temperature                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ methane is released into the atmosphere from melting permafrost **WHICH** increases greenhouse effect **OR** environmental temperature     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increased incidence of forest fires releasing carbon dioxide into the atmosphere **WHICH** increases greenhouse effect **OR** increases temperatures further **OR**  increased incidence of droughts increase the chance of forest fires which release carbon dioxide into the atmosphere when they burn **WHICH** increases greenhouse effect **OR** environmental temperature    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ as the air becomes warmer, it can hold more water vapour **WHICH** is a greenhouse gas **OR** increases greenhouse effect **OR** contributes  to further warming [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Because of global warming, the temperature of the oceans has increased. **Warmer oceans** have a lower capacity for dissolved gases, so they **release** stored **carbon dioxide** into the atmosphere, which further enhances the greenhouse effect ($\checkmark$). **Warmer oceans** due to global warming also **melt arctic ice**, which reduces the Earth's **albedo**. This leads to an increase in the **absorption of solar radiation** and enhances global warming ($\checkmark$).
