# BI1626

Which of the following statements is/are example(s) of poleward range shifts of species associated with climate change?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}

    | | |

    | :-- | :-- |

    | I. | A species of butterfly native to North Africa appearing in Southern Europe and becoming less abundant in Africa. |

    | II. | Expansion of a species’s active latitude range from 45 $-$ 60 $\text{\textdegree}$N to 40 $-$ 65 $\text{\textdegree}$N. |

    | III. | A mammal normally found at an altitude range of 500 $-$ 600 m above sea level, occupying ground at 800 m above sea level. |

| $\text{A.}$ $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ $\text{I only}$ $\hspace{1em}$ |

| --- | :-: |

| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ I and II $\hspace{1em}$ |

| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ II and III $\hspace{1em}$ |

| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III $\hspace{1em}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

**Statement I** is true because the active range of this butterfly species has moved northward towards the North Pole, which is a clear example of a poleward range shift. The information about the species becoming less abundant in Africa suggests that climate change has made conditions in Africa less habitable for this species. As a result, the butterfly has migrated to find its niche further north in Europe. **Choice A** is correct because **Statement I** is the only true statement.

**Statement II** is not true because this species' active latitude range has widened, but not only in a poleward direction; it has also moved equatorward (towards latitude 40$\text{\textdegree}$N), so this is not an example of a poleward range shift. There are many reasons why a population may be able to spread in this way, such as an improved availability of food and habitat. 

**Statement III** is not true because it is an example of an upslope range shift, not a poleward one. For this question, it is important to understand the difference between the two similar words, “altitude” (a place’s vertical height above sea level) and “latitude” (a place’s angular distance from the equator). 

**Choices B, C** and **D** are incorrect because they all include one or more untrue statements.
