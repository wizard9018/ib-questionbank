# BI1389

The following graph shows how the pH of seawater, obtained from Aloha station in Hawaii, has changed between 1988 and 2021. What could account for the decrease in pH?

![BI1389](images/BI1389.png)
[Source: University of Hawaii (2021, April 16). *Conserve and sustainably use the oceans, seas, and marine resources*. Our World in Data. Retrieved December 30, 2023, from https://ourworldindata.org/sdgs/life-below-water#targets. Copyright BY SA]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Increased ocean salinity

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Increased ocean temperatures

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Increased atmospheric carbon dioxide

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Increased sea level

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because increased atmospheric carbon dioxide leads to increased diffusion of $\ce{CO_2}$ into seawater. When $\ce{CO_2}$ dissolves in seawater, it reacts with water molecules to form carbonic acid.

**Choice A** is incorrect since increased salinity will generally lead to an increase in pH rather than a reduction. **Choice B** is incorrect since pH is largely unaffected by ocean temperature. **Choice D** is incorrect since sea level changes are unlikely to have an effect on pH.
