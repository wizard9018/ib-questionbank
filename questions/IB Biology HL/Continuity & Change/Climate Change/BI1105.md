# BI1105

Climate change can impact which of the following events?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Growth of *Cerastium arcticum* (Arctic mouse-ear chickweed) |
    | II. | The arrival of migrating *Rangifer tarandus* (reindeer) |
    | III. | The mating of *Parus major* (great tit) |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

*Note: Starting May 2028, this question may be assessed differently due to changes to the Biology guide.*

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statements I, II**, and **III** are all correct as they can all be influenced by changes in temperature, which are influenced by climate change. Rising temperatures, changing precipitation patterns, and altered growing seasons can affect the availability of suitable plant habitats and resources (**Statement I**). As temperatures and ecosystems change, the availability of food sources and suitable habitats for migratory species can be affected, leading to changes in the timing of migrations as animals respond to shifts in resource availability (**Statement II**). Mating behaviours, including courtship and breeding, are often influenced by environmental cues such as temperature, food availability, and day length (**Statement III**). Therefore, **Choice D** is correct because it contains all three true statements.

**Choices A**, **B** and **C** are incorrect as they are missing one or two of the true statements.
