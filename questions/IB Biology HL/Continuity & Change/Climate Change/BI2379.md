# BI2379

Which of the following is/are possible consequences of a climate change-induced temporal shift in the reproduction cycle of the great tit (*Parus major*)?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Earlier hatching and fledging of great tit chicks. |
    | II. | Change of food sources sought by great tit parents to feed their young.|
    | III. | Extinction. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

*Note: Starting May 2028, this question may be assessed differently due to changes to the Biology guide.*

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is true because one of the environmental cues for egg-laying and hatching is temperature. The current climate change trend is for warmer, wetter winters that are likely to give great tit parents earlier cues for spring laying, and hence the eggs may hatch earlier in the season, with earlier fledging times for the juvenile birds.

**Statement II** is true because many species, including great tits, are highly adaptable and can find alternative food sources if their preferred sources are unavailable. For example, when their preferred food sources of caterpillars, ants and grubs might not be present in larger numbers, great tits often switch to beech mast (nut-like fruits of the beech tree) as an alternative food source.

**Statement III** is true because, whilst great tits are an extant species, they might not be able to withstand continued selective pressure from climate change, which could lead to their disappearance from habitats and eventual extinction.

**Choice D** is correct because it contains all three true statements (**I, II** and **III**).

**Choices A, B** and **C** are incorrect because they omit one or more of the true statements.
