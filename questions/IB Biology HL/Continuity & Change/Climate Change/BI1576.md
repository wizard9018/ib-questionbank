What conditions cause stress to the coral that could lead to reef bleaching?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Overfishing and ocean acidification

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Overfishing and invasive species

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Climate change and ocean acidification

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Climate change and invasive species

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because both rising temperatures due to climate change and ocean acidification from increased atmospheric carbon dioxide stress corals. When temperatures become too high, corals expel the zooxanthellae living within their tissues, which provide them with colour and essential nutrients through photosynthesis. This expulsion causes coral bleaching, turning corals white and potentially leading to their death if the stress continues. At the same time, increased carbon dioxide diffuses into the ocean, forming carbonic acid and lowering ocean pH. This process weakens coral skeletons by reducing the availability of carbonate ions needed for calcium carbonate formation. The additional stress from acidification further compromises coral health and structural integrity, making them more susceptible to damage and bleaching.

Although overfishing and invasive species are significant problems, they do not contribute to coral bleaching. **Choices A, B**, and **D** are incorrect because they contain one or more of these processes.
