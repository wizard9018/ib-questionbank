# BI0149

**Maximum mark: 4**

1. Outline what is meant by the greenhouse effect. [2 marks]

2. List two of the main greenhouse gases present in the atmosphere. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ greenhouse gases **OR** CO$_2$, methane, water vapour and nitrous oxides are responsible for the greenhouse effect[1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ warming of Earth by gases is called the greenhouse effect [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ greenhouse gases trap the Sun’s heat **OR** longwave radiation [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ short wave radiation from the Sun passes through the atmosphere and reaches the surface of the earth [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the greenhouse effect results in a warmer planet **OR** more suitable environment for life  [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Greenhouse gases in the atmosphere **trap the Sun’s heat** ($\checkmark$) and **make the Earth warmer and more suitable for living things** ($\checkmark$). This is called the greenhouse effect.
