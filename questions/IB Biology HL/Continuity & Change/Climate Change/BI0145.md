# BI0145

Scientists analyzed the main contributors to greenhouse gas emissions from human-related activities. The resulting data is presented in the charts below.

![bi0145](images/bi0145.png)

Which contributor is most responsible for the production and release of methane and nitrogen oxides into the atmosphere?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Land use and biomass burning

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Industrial use

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Residential sources

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Agricultural production

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The largest area in the pie charts showing sources of methane and nitrogen oxides is agricultural production.

Land use and biomass burning (**Choice A**) are a source of both methane and nitrous oxide but the area in the pie charts is less than agricultural production.

Industrial use (**Choice B**) releases mainly nitrous oxide.

Residential sources (**Choice C**) also release both methane and nitrous oxide but in lesser amounts than agricultural production.
