# BI1627

The image shows an example of silvopasture, which integrates trees and plants with the grazing and foraging of domesticated animals.

![BI01627 fig1](<images/BI01627 fig1.png>)

[Source:  United States Department of Agriculture, USDA NRCS Texas, ”Cattle grazing in a silvopasture”.  Retrieved May 2024, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Cattle_grazing_in_a_silvopasture._(25112780735).jpg, Copyright-free]

It is claimed that pastures with trees can sequester 5 to 10 times as much carbon as an equivalent-sized piece of treeless pastureland. 

Which of the following statements could be claimed as additional benefits of silvopasture?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}

    | | |

    | :-- | :-- |

    | I. | Greater biodiversity in the silvopasture than in traditional treeless pastureland |

    | II. | Ability to plant any kind of tree in a silvopasture |

    | III. | Greater combined productivity from silvopasture |

    | IV. | Protection of livestock animals from wind and intense sunlight |

| $\text{A.}$ $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ $\text{I, II and IV}$ $\hspace{1em}$ |

| --- | :-: |

| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ I and II $\hspace{1em}$ |

| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ I and IV $\hspace{1em}$ |

| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ I, III and IV $\hspace{1em}$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

**Choice D** is correct because **Statement II** is the only untrue statement.

**Statement I** is true because trees in silvopasture systems significantly increase biodiversity. Trees provide habitats for various species, such as insects, birds, and small mammals. Additionally, tree shade creates microclimates that support a wider range of ground-level plants and grasses. This diversity in flora and fauna enhances the overall ecosystem biodiversity. 

**Statement II** is untrue because not all tree species will be suitable. Livestock are likely to feed on certain species of trees, especially young saplings. Care should be taken to select trees of the right species and manage those trees through their early growth with techniques such as ring-fencing and pollarding.  

**Statement III** is true because the greater productivity will be proportional to the increased carbon sequestration by photosynthesis. Silvopasture systems combine the productivity of trees, forage, and livestock. Trees contribute to carbon sequestration through photosynthesis and can produce timber, fruits, or nuts. The forage provides food for livestock, and integrating these components can lead to higher overall productivity compared to a single-use system.

**Statement IV** is true because trees can shade livestock and protect them from the worst excesses of wind and rain. This can reduce instances of wind chill and heatstroke, thereby improving the animals' mortality.
