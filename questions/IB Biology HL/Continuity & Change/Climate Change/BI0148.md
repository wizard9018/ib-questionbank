# BI0148

Which of the following would not delay or decrease the severity of the effects of global warming?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Increased use of renewable energy.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Decreased deforestation and increased forestation.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Increased use of natural fertilizers and decreased use of nitrogen fertilizers.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Forests converted to farms.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Cutting down forests and clearing land for agricultural purposes causes a decrease in the intake of carbon dioxide from the atmosphere which is a leading cause of global warming.

By using more renewable energy (**Choice A**), there will be a decrease in the burning of coal and fossil fuels for generating electricity in power stations. Reducing deforestation (**Choice B**) will enhance the retention of carbon dioxide by trees through photosynthesis. The use of natural fertilizers like compost (**Choice C**) instead of fertilizers enriched in nitrogen will reduce the release of nitrous oxide which is a greenhouse gas.
