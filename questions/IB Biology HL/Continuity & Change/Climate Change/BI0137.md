# BI0137

**Maximum mark: 5**

Forest fires occur naturally in various parts of the world and contribute to the health of ecosystems. With an increase in global warming, forest fires have become more frequent.

![BI0137](images/BI0137.png)
[Source: Strandberg, C. (2009, June 2). *Forest fire*. Commons Wikimedia. Retrieved May 7, 2022, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Fire-Forest.jpg.  Copyright under Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 Generic]

1. State the name of the process that describes how forest fires are involved in the carbon cycle.      [1 mark]

2. Outline how forests can benefit from forest fires. [2 marks]

3. Describe the effects of increased forest fires on the carbon cycle. [2 marks]

$\hspace{1em}$ :br
$\hspace{1em}$ Forest fires are part of the carbon cycle and fall under the category of **combustion** ($\checkmark$). The burning of fossil  fuels by humans is also part of the combustion process.

2. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ forest fires can clear out underbrush **OR** destroy climax communities [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ open areas make light available to smaller plants and trees for photosynthesis [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ new growth takes place in burned areas      [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ may increase biodiversity [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ some seeds need exposure to fire to break out of dormancy   [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Forest fires are useful in **opening up** dense areas of forest  **to make light available** for smaller plants and trees ($\checkmark$), the undergrowth in the floor of the forests. Some seeds on the ground **need exposure to fire to break the dormancy** ($\checkmark$) and start germinating.

3. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ increase in combustion **OR** burning of biomass       [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increase in carbon dioxide in the atmosphere   [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ can lead to **OR** enhance global warming  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ immediately after forest fires, there is a decrease in the amount of photosynthesis because there are fewer trees     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ as the forest regrows there is an increase in the total amount of photosynthesis  [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Increased forest fires are detrimental, they cause an **increase in carbon dioxide in the atmosphere** ($\checkmark$). This can subsequently **lead to global warming** ($\checkmark$).
