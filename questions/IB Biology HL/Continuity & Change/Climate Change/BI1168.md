# BI1168

The graph shows trends in atmospheric CO:sub[2] concentrations in Mauna Loa. What conclusions can be made from this graph?

![BI1168](images/BI1168.png)
[Source: Adapted from Semhur S (n.d).*File: Mauna Loa carbon dioxide-en svg*. Wikimedia Commons. Retrieved April 27.2022. from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Mauna_Loa_Carbon_Dioxide-en.svg. Copyright-free]

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | The concentration of carbon dioxide is always highest in March |
    | II. | Increasing carbon dioxide concentration is directly correlated with climate change |
    | III. | Since 1960, the atmospheric carbon dioxide concentration has increased in Mauna Loa |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The data shows that since 1960, the atmospheric carbon dioxide concentration has increased in Mauna Loa (**Statement III**); **Choice C**  is correct.

The graph for the annual fluctuations of carbon dioxide concentration shows the carbon dioxide concentrations are highest in May (**Statement I**); **Choices A** and **D**  are incorrect. The graph does not refer to climate change, therefore, it cannot be concluded that increasing carbon dioxide concentration is correlated with climate change (**Statement II**); **Choice B**  is incorrect.
