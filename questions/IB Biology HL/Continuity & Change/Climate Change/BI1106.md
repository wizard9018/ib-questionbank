# BI1106

Spruce bark beetles burrow under the bark of spruce trees and other conifers. The tawny owl has two colourations, brown and white. Climate change can have which of the following impacts?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Decrease in the number of life cycles per year of the spruce bark beetle

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Increase in the number of life cycles per year of the spruce bark beetle

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Decrease in the fitness of the brown colour variant of the tawny owl

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Increase in the fitness of the white colour variant of the tawny owl

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because warmer temperatures can accelerate the life cycle of many insect species, including the spruce bark beetle, as they can reproduce and develop more rapidly, making **Choice A**  incorrect. **Choices C** and **D** are incorrect because the fitness of the brown variant of the tawny owl is increasing, and the fitness of the white variant is decreasing because, as snow cover decreases, the brown variant is better camouflaged.
