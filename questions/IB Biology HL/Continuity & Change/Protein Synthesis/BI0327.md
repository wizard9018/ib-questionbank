# BI0327

Ribosomes have three binding sites. Which statements are correct for the functions of these sites?

| | A site | P site | E site |

| :- | :- | :- | :- |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$ A. $\hspace{1.5em}$ | Amino acid carried by the tRNA is bound to the growing polypeptide chain | Empty tRNA is released | tRNA carrying an amino acid binds to the corresponding codon |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$ B. $\hspace{1.5em}$ | Empty tRNA is released  | Amino acid carried by the tRNA is bound to the growing polypeptide chain | tRNA carrying an amino acid binds to the corresponding codon |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$ C. $\hspace{1.5em}$ | tRNA carrying an amino acid binds to the corresponding codon | Amino acid carried by the tRNA is bound to the growing polypeptide chain | Empty tRNA is released  |

| $\hspace{1.5em}$ D. $\hspace{1.5em}$ | Amino acid carried by the tRNA is bound to the growing polypeptide chain | tRNA carrying an amino acid binds to the corresponding codon | Empty tRNA is released  |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The A site (aminoacyl or acceptor site) is where the incoming tRNA, carrying an amino acid, binds to the codon on the mRNA through complementary base pairing. The P site (peptidyl site) holds the tRNA with the growing polypeptide chain and is where the new amino acid is added through the formation of a peptide bond. The E site (exit site) is where the uncharged ("empty") tRNA is released from the ribosome after its amino acid has been transferred. **Choice C** is therefore the correct answer.

**Choice A**  is incorrect because peptide bond formation and chain elongation occur at the P site, not the A site.

**Choice B**  is incorrect because the E site, not the A site, is responsible for releasing the empty tRNA.

**Choice D**  is incorrect for the same reason as A — peptide bond formation does not occur at the A site.
