# BI0604

The diagram below shows pre-mRNA and three mature mRNA transcripts where the introns have been removed.

![604](images/604.png)

What is an advantage of the above process?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ The rate of transcription increases.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Different polypeptides can be produced from one pre-mRNA transcript.
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Translation occurs immediately after transcription.
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Prokaryotes can produce a variety of proteins from one section of DNA.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The above process shows the splicing of mRNA in which introns are removed and exons are joined together in different arrangements. The advantage of mRNA splicing is that the number of different polypeptides that can be produced by one segment of DNA is increased. mRNA splicing only occurs in eukaryotes.

Splicing does not increase the rate of transcription as it occurs after transcription is complete (**Choice A**).

In eukaryotes, translation takes place after post-transcriptional modifications are complete which requires several steps, so translation does not occur immediately after transcription (**Choice C**).

Splicing of mRNA does not take place in prokaryotes because there are no introns in prokaryotic DNA (**Choice D**).
