# BI0542

The table gives the genetic code.

![BI0542](images/BI0542.png)
[Source: CNX OpenStax. (2016, November 11). *File: OSC Microbio 11 04 GenCode.jpg*. Wikimedia Commons. Retrieved January 4, 2023, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: OSC_Microbio_11_04_GenCode.jpg.  Copyright under CC-BY 4.0]

Which polypeptide is produced by the mRNA sequence AUG GCU AAA UAG CGA?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Met-Ala-Lys

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Met-Ala-Lys-Tyr-Arg

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Met-Arg-Asn

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Met-Arg-Asn-Cys-Arg

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
According to the table:
- AUG codes for Met
- GCU codes for Ala
- AAA codes for Lys
- UAG is a stop codon
- CGA codes for Arg

When translational machinery encounters a stop codon, the ribosome dissociates from the mRNA and translation ends. So, the translational machinery will produce a polypeptide containing Met, Ala, and Lys, but will stop translation when it reaches the UAG codon. Therefore, the polypeptide produced by translation of this mRNA sequence will only contain Met, Ala, and Lys, in that order. **Choice A** is correct.

**Choice B** is incorrect, as translation cannot continue beyond Lys, due to the stop codon. **Choice C** and **Choice D** are incorrect as GCU codes for Ala, not Arg and AAA codes for Lys, not Asn.
