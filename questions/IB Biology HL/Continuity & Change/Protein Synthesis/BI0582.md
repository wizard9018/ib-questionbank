# BI0582

Which of the following statements is **false** regarding translation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Translation needs mRNA, which carries genetic information in the form of codons.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The process of translation starts with the start codon AUG.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Translation takes place in the nucleus.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ tRNAs contain anticodons which are complementary to the mRNA's codons.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A, B**, and **D** – These statements are true.

**Choice C** -This statement is false. In eukaryotic cells, translation takes place in the cytoplasm. Prokaryotic cells do not have a nucleus.
