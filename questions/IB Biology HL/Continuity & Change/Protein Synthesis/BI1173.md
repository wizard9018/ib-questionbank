# BI1173

What is the role of the A-site in the ribosome?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ It is where the initiator tRNA binds to the start codon

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ It holds the tRNA with the growing polypeptide chain whilst a peptide bond forms between adjacent amino acids

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ To allow complementary base pairing between codons and anticodons, so that the correct amino acid is incorporated into the polypeptide

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ It is where the discharged tRNA exits from the ribosome

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
There are three sites on the ribosome which are important in translation. The role of the A-site is to allow complementary base pairing of incoming tRNA molecules (anticodons) and mRNA (codons) so that the correct amino acid is incorporated into the growing polypeptide. **Choice C**  is correct.

The site where the initiator tRNA binds to the start codon on the mRNA is the P site; **Choice A**  is incorrect. Whilst the A site does hold the incoming tRNA while a peptide bond is formed between the amino acid on this tRNA and the growing polypeptide chain, the growing polypeptide chain is on the P-site tRNA; **Choice B**  is incorrect. The site where the discharged tRNA exits from the ribosome is the E site; **Choice D**  is incorrect.
