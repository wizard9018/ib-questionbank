# BI2340

Which statement best describes the assembly of amino acid chains by ribosomes?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Ribosomes translate DNA codons to an amino acid chain by condensation.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Ribosomes move along tRNA joining amino acids, forming peptide bonds.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Ribosomes move along mRNA joining amino acids, forming peptide bonds.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Ribosomes move along tRNA joining amino acids, using condensation reactions.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because ribosomes move along the sequence of codons on mRNA, reading the genetic information to guide the assembly of an amino acid chain. Transfer RNA (tRNA) molecules bring specific amino acids to the ribosome, and these amino acids are joined to the growing chain by peptide bonds.

**Choice A** is incorrect because ribosomes translate RNA codons (on mRNA), not DNA codons. DNA is transcribed into mRNA before translation.

**Choices B** and **D** are incorrect because ribosomes move along mRNA, not tRNA. tRNA delivers amino acids to the ribosome but is not the template for translation.
