# BI2642

**Maximum mark: 3**

The image shows two mRNA transcripts of the cell death regulator, Bcl-extra (Bcl-x). When the longer mRNA transcript (Bcl-xL) is expressed, it promotes cell survival. However, when the shorter transcript (Bcl-xS) is expressed, it promotes cell death.

![BI2642](images/BI2642.png)

Discuss the disadvantages and advantages of the alternative splicing of Bcl-x.    [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

any **three** of the following:\
*Answer must include at least **one** advantage and **one** disadvantage to gain full marks*

**Advantages of alternative splicing of Bcl-x**\
$\ce{ -> }$ allows cells to quickly adapt, maintaining tissue homeostasis **OR** the right balance of cell death and cell division **OR** proliferation e.g. cell death can be triggered in damaged cells or functional cells can be kept alive        [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ Bcl-xS expression can prevent uncontrolled cell division **OR** remove damaged **OR** mutated cells, lowering the risk of cancer        [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ a single gene can produce both forms of Bcl-x, saving genetic space     [1 mark]

**Disadvantages of alternative splicing of Bcl-x**\
$\ce{ -> }$ dysregulation **OR** loss of regulation of splicing can lead to errors in splicing    [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ which may hinder cell death and contribute to cancer development    [1 mark]

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
The advantages of the alternative splicing of Bcl-x are that it allows cells to **quickly adapt** maintaining **tissue homeostasis** e.g. cell death can be triggered in damaged cells ($\checkmark$). And a **single gene** can produce **both forms of Bcl-x**, which saves genetic **space** ($\checkmark$). A disadvantage of the alternative splicing of Bcl-x is that if it is **dysregulated**, this can lead to errors in splicing ($\checkmark$) which may **prevent cell death** and contribute to **cancer** development ($\checkmark$).
