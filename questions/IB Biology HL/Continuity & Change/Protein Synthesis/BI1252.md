# BI1252

The image below shows part of the transcription process. What can be concluded from the information in the diagram?

![BI1252](images/BI1252.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Different proteins can be produced from the same gene.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Exons are not involved in gene expression.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The DNA sequence is unrelated to the final mRNA sequence.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The removal of introns causes exons to arrange themselves in a random order.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The diagram shows alternative splicing, where exons can be combined in different orders to create different proteins from the same gene. This makes **Choice A** correct.

**Choice B** is incorrect, as exons do have a role in gene expression. **Choice C** is incorrect as the DNA sequence still determines the mRNA sequence, even though the order of nucleotides may be different depending on the exons included. **Choice D** is incorrect since the process of alternative splicing is not random and has little to do with the introns themselves. Furthermore, this cannot be concluded from the diagram.
