# BI0518

The diagram shows how RNA is transcribed from DNA. What structures are indicated by the labels X, Y, and Z?

![BI0518](images/BI0518.png)

[Source: Adapted from National Human Genome Research Institute. (n.d.). *Transcription*. Retrieved March 18, 2022, from https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Transcription.  Copyright under Public Domain]

| | X | Y | Z |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | 5' end of DNA | 3' end of RNA | RNA polymerase |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | 5' end of DNA | 3' end of RNA | DNA ligase |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | 3' end of DNA | 5' end of RNA | RNA polymerase |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | 3' end of RNA | 5' end of DNA | DNA polymerase |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Transcription proceeds in the 5' to 3' direction. During transcription, RNA polymerase adds the 5' end of an RNA nucleotide to the 3' end of the growing RNA transcript.

In the image, Y marks the start of the RNA transcript. Since RNA is transcribed in the 5' to 3' direction, the start of the transcript is the 5' end. Therefore, Y represents the 5' end of RNA, not the 3' end (**Choices A** and **B**).

X marks the start of the DNA template strand. The RNA transcript is synthesised antiparallel to the DNA. So, the transcription starts at the 3' end of DNA. Therefore, X represents the 3' end of DNA.

Z marks the enzyme synthesising the new RNA transcript. RNA is produced by RNA polymerase. Therefore, Y represents RNA polymerase, not DNA polymerase (**Choice D**).
