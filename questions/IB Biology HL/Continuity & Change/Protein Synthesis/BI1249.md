# BI1249

Which is **not** a reason why some proteins are broken down by proteolysis?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ They have become damaged

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ They are misfolded

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ They have become too large

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ They are no longer needed by the cell

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Proteins must be continuously broken down in the cell to provide a constant supply of amino acids for protein synthesis. This is accomplished through proteolysis. The main reasons proteins must be broken down are when they become damaged, misfolded, or are no longer needed by the cell. Proteins growing too large is not why a protein needs to be degraded, so **Choice C** is the correct option.

**Choice A** is incorrect, as proteins can sometimes become damaged due to various factors and are then targeted for proteolysis to prevent them from interfering with regular cell functions. **Choice B** is incorrect as misfolded proteins have a modified 3-D shape, which may disrupt normal function, potentially leading to cellular dysfunction and disease. **Choice D** is incorrect since proteins that are no longer required by the cell are often targeted for proteolysis, allowing their amino acids to be recycled.
