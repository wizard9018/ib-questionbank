# BI1566

What would the translated amino acid sequence be for a DNA sense strand with base sequence TTGTTAGAAAACCCG?

![BI1566](images/BI1566.png)
[Source: Mouagip, (2009, February 18). *File: Aminoacids table.svg*. Wikimedia Commons. Retrieved April 24, 2024, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Aminoacids_table.svg. Public domain]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Asn-Asn-Leu-Leu-Gly

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Leu-Ile-Glu-Asn-Pro

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Asn-Asp-Leu-Leu-Gly

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Leu-Leu-Glu-Asn-Pro

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because the mRNA strand is transcribed from the DNA antisense strand. This means that the mRNA sequence matches the DNA sense strand given, except that thymine (T) is replaced by uracil (U). The mRNA sequence would therefore be UUG-UUA-GAA-AAC-CCG. When translated to amino acids, this yields Leu-Leu-Glu-Asn-Pro.

**Choices A, B,** and **C** are incorrect as they contain one or more incorrect amino acids.
