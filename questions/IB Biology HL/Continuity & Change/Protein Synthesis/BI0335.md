# BI0335

The diagram shows a tRNA molecule. 

![335](images/335.png)

[Adapted from Vossman. (2005, November 7). *Category: TRNA*. Wikimedia Commons. Retrieved June 1, 2022, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category: TRNA. Copyright free]

Which of the following would bind to the position labelled R?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Anticodon

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Codon

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ rRNA

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ An amino acid

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The position labelled R on the tRNA molecule represents the anticodon. The anticodon is a sequence of three nucleotide bases on the tRNA that binds to the complementary codon on the mRNA strand during translation. This specific base pairing ensures that the correct amino acid, carried by the tRNA, is added to the growing polypeptide chain. **Choice B** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the anticodon is the region labelled R on the diagram of the tRNAs, and this binds to the codon on the mRNA. 

 **Choice C** is incorrect because rRNA is part of the ribosomal structure and does not bind to the anticodon.

 **Choice D** is incorrect because the amino acid binds to the 3′ end of the tRNA, not to the anticodon region.
