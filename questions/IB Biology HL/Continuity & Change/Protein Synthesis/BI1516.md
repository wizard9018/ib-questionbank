# BI1516

Which diagram shows a mature mRNA immediately prior to attachment to a ribosome?

- A. ![BI1516 fig1](<images/BI1516 fig1.png>)

- B. ![BI1516 fig2](<images/BI1516 fig2.png>)

- C. ![BI1516 fig3](<images/BI1516 fig3.png>)

- D. ![BI1516 fig4](<images/BI1516 fig4.png>)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

Immediately before a mature mRNA is attached to a ribosome, the spliceosome complex will have spliced out the introns, leaving only exons. A cap will be added to the 5' end and a poly-A tail to the 3' end. **Choice D** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect as it contains both exons and introns. **Choice B** is incorrect as it is missing a 5' cap. **Choice C** is incorrect as the poly-A tail is on the 5' end, and the cap is on the 3' end.
