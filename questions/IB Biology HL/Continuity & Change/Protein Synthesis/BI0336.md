## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. 

    1. $\hspace{1em}$ Any **two** of the following:

        $\ce{->}$ Q is the polypeptide chain **OR** chain of amino acids [1 mark]

        $\ce{->}$ R is a ribosome [1 mark]

        [2 marks]

    2. Any **three** of the following (an annotated diagram is also acceptable):

        $\ce{->}$ three binding sites, A, P and E (in the large subunit of the ribosome) [1 mark]

        $\ce{->}$ one binding site for mRNA in the small subunit [1 mark]

        $\ce{->}$ A site is the binding site for the tRNA carrying the the next amino acid [1 mark]

        $\ce{->}$ P site is where initiator tRNA binds [1 mark]

        $\ce{->}$ P site is where the amino acid from tRNA is bound to the growing polypeptide [1 mark]

        $\ce{->}$ E site is for the tRNA that is exiting the ribosome [1 mark]

        [3 marks]

        $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

        Ribosomes have three binding sites — **A, P and E** ($\checkmark$), on the large subunit of the ribosome and **mRNA binding site**  on the small subunit ($\checkmark$). The **A site** is where the **tRNA** ($\checkmark$) with the amino acid attached enters the ribosome. The **P site** is where the amino acid from the tRNA is bound by the peptide bond to the growing **polypeptide chain** ($\checkmark$) ,  and the **E site** is where the empty tRNA **exits** ($\checkmark$) the ribosome.

    3. $\hspace{1em}$

        $\ce{->}$ polysome **OR** polyribosome  [1 mark]

        [1 mark]

2. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$ ribosomes are the site of protein synthesis [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ a polysome is multiple ribosomes bound to the same mRNA molecule **OR** Multiple ribosomes bind to the same mRNA molecule [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ many copies of the same polypeptide can be made at the same time [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ ribosomes move from the 5' end of mRNA to the 3'-end [1 mark]

    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

    Ribosomes are the site of **protein synthesis** ($\checkmark$). A polysome is **multiple ribosomes** bound to the same mRNA molecule ($\checkmark$); therefore, many **copies of the same polypeptide** ($\checkmark$) chain can be made at the same time. Ribosomes assemble around the start codon at the **5' end** ($\checkmark$) of the mRNA and then synthesise polypeptide while moving towards the 3' end. 

3. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$ due to to hydrogen bonding [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ hydrogen bonds form between carboxyl and amino groups, **OR** C=O and N-H groups, of different amino acids **OR** between amino acids within the same polypeptide chain [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ results in the formation of alpha helices **OR** beta pleated sheets [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ bonding happens only between groups involved in the peptide bonds [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

    Secondary structures are formed by folding within the same polypeptide chain. This is caused by the **hydrogen bond** ($\checkmark$) formation between **carboxyl**  (C=O) and **amino** ($\checkmark$) (N-H) groups of peptide bonds on different amino acids. This can form secondary structures called **alpha helices** ($\checkmark$) and beta-pleated sheets.
