# BI1520

What is needed to sustain a functional proteome?

- I. $\hspace{1.6em}$ Breakdown of proteins

- II. $\hspace{1.3em}$ Synthesis of proteins

- III. $\hspace{1em}$ Recycling of amino acids

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

A proteome is the complete set of proteins that can be expressed by a cell, tissue or organism. To maintain a functional proteome, there must be constant, regulated, breakdown of proteins (**Statement I**). This is crucial for removing damaged, misfolded, or unnecessary proteins from the cell to help maintain cellular health. Constant synthesis of proteins (**Statement II**) is driven by the genetic information encoded in DNA and mediated by RNA. Protein synthesis is fundamental for cell growth, repair, and the maintenance of cellular functions. The constant recycling of amino acids (**Statement III**) is important for efficient cellular operation, allowing cells to reuse them to synthesise new proteins or for other metabolic functions. **Choice D**  is correct.

**Choices A, B** and **C**  are incorrect as they do not include all three statements.
