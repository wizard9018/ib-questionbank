# BI1519

The diagram illustrates a process involving ubiquitin and the proteasome system.

![BI1519](images/BI1519.png)

From the diagram and your own knowledge, deduce which of the following is the role of ubiquitin.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ To hydrolyse proteins into short peptides

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ To mark proteins for degradation

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ To create a pool of amino acids that have been recycled from degraded proteins

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ To act as a signal to the proteasome, preventing protein degradation.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

Ubiquitin is a short-chained regulatory protein that attaches to proteins so that the proteasome recognises them. The proteasome breaks down proteins into peptides so that they can be further degraded into amino acids; **Choice B** is correct.

Ubiquitin does not hydrolyse proteins, the proteasome does; **Choice A** is incorrect. Ubiquitin does not create a pool of amino acids that have been recycled from degraded proteins; **Choice C** is incorrect. Ubiquitin acts as a signal to the proteosome to promote degradation of the tagged protein, not prevent it; **Choice D** is incorrect.
