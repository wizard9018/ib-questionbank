# BI0599

Which of the following shows the correct sequence of mRNA synthesis?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ RNA polymerase matches free nucleotides to the DNA strand.
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Transcription continues until it reaches a terminator sequence.
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$  RNA polymerase binds to the promoter region.

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$  III, I, II

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$  I, II, III
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$  II, I, III
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$  II, III, I

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
RNA polymerase binds to the promoter region of the DNA strand (**Statement III**). RNA polymerase then matches free nucleotides to the exposed template strand of DNA and continues adding complementary nucleotides to the growing mRNA strand (**Statement I**). Transcription continues until RNA polymerase reaches a terminator sequence, which signals the end of transcription, and the new mRNA strand is released (**Statement II**).

**Choices B**, **C** and **D** are incorrect as they are not showing the correct sequence.
