# BI0470

The diagram shows a molecule that plays an important role in protein synthesis.

![BI0470](images/BI0470.png)
[Source: Adapted from CNX OpenStax (2016, November). *File: OSC Microbio 11 04 tRNA.jpg.* Wikimedia commons. Retrieved May 13, 2022, from https:// commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:OSC_Microbio_11_04_tRNA.jpg. Copyright under CC-BY-4.0]

Which is found at X?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ Uncoiled DNA
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Amino acid binding site
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ RNA nucleotides

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ II only

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The diagram shows a tRNA molecule. It is made of RNA nucleotides (**Statement III**). The region labelled X represents the binding site for an amino acid (**Statement II**). This means that both **Statements II and III** are true of X, so **Choice C** is correct.

**Choice A** only gives **Statement II** so is not correct, as **Statement III** also applies to X.  DNA contains genetic information that is transferred to mRNA for protein synthesis. However, this is not a DNA molecule, therefore **Statement I** does not apply to X and makes **Choices B and D** incorrect.
