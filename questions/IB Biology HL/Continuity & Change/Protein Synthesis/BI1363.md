# BI1363

**Maximum mark: 10**

A study was conducted on germinating soybeans (*Glycine max*)  to observe the effects of cold stress conditions (10°C) and osmotic stress conditions on the germination rate. Researchers were also interested in the effects of osmotic stress and cold stress conditions on the concentration of polysomes in the germinating soybeans. The control soybeans were grown at 25 °C, in sterilised water.

1. Identify the independent variable in this study.    [1 mark]

2. Describe the effect of cold and osmotic stress on germination.        [2 marks]

The mean concentrations of polysomes in the soybean seeds were measured at various times during their germination, in the different stress conditions. The results are shown in the graph below. Polysomes are clusters of ribosomes which simultaneously translate a single mRNA molecule to synthesise multiple polypeptide chains.

3. State the stress condition that has the greatest effect on polysome formation compared to the controls.    [1 mark]

4. Calculate the difference in polysome concentration between the control and the soybean seeds under osmotic stress at 24 hours.    [1 mark]

5. Explain how an increase in polysome formation could affect germination.    [2 marks]

6. Suggest a method that could be used to put the soybean seeds under osmotic stress.    [1 mark]

7. Explain why it is important that the soybeans under osmotic stress are grown at 25°C.        [1 mark]

8. Predict with a reason what would happen to the average concentration of polysomes in soybeans if they were put under heat stress conditions of 50°C.        [1 mark]
