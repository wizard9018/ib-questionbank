# BI0333

What is the correct function of the tRNA-activating enzymes?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Matches mRNA codons to tRNA anticodons 

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Adds a specific amino acid to the 5' end of the correct tRNA

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Transfers tRNAs from the A site to the P site within the ribosome

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Adds a specific amino acid to the 3' end of the correct tRNA

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

tRNA-activating enzymes add specific amino acids to the 3’ end of their corresponding tRNAs using energy from ATP. **Choice D** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because matching codons to anticodons occurs through complementary base pairing during translation, not via tRNA-activating enzymes.

 **Choice B**  Is incorrect because the amino acid is added to the 3' end of the tRNA, not the 5' end.

 

  **Choice C**  is incorrect because tRNA movement within the ribosome (from A to P site) is facilitated by the ribosome itself, not by tRNA-activating enzymes.
