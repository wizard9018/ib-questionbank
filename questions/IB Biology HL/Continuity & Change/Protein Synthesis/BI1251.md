# BI1251

What mRNA modifications must take place immediately following transcription in eukaryotes?

| | Modification I | Modification II | Modification III |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Removal of introns | Splicing of introns | Addition of 3' caps and 5' poly-A tails |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Removal of exons | Splicing of introns | Addition of 5' caps and 3' poly-A tails |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Removal of introns | Splicing of exons | Addition of 5' caps and 3' poly-A tails |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Removal of exons | Splicing of exons | Addition of 3' caps and 5' poly-A tails  |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Introns are non-coding segments between exons within a gene, that are transcribed but then removed during RNA processing, to make the mature mRNA. Exons are coding regions of a gene that contain the genetic information necessary to be transcribed into mRNA and translated into functional proteins. Once introns have been removed from the immature mRNA,  the exons are spliced back together to form mature mRNA.  As introns are removed, and exons are spliced back together during the modification of immature mRNA, **Choices B** and **D** are incorrect.

5' caps (not 3' caps) are modified nucleotides added to the 5' end of eukaryotic mRNA, and polyA-tails are added to the 3' end (not the 5' end) during transcription. Their purpose is to stabilise the mRNA, aid its transport out of the nucleus and help facilitate the initiation of translation. Therefore **Choice A** is incorrect, and **Choice C** is correct.
