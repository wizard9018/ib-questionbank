 2. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ if introns are not spliced out **OR** are retained, this may lead to the incorrect amino acid sequence being translated **OR** truncated proteins due to the introduction of premature stop codons **OR** the translation of nonfunctional proteins [1 mark]

If **exons are removed** from the mRNA, this could result in the translation of a **truncated or non-functional protein** ($\checkmark$).
