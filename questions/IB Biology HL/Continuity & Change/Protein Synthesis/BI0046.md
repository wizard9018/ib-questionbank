# BI0046

Which nucleic acid carries genetic material coding for proteins?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Ribosomal RNA

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ mRNA

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ ATP

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ tRNA

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
mRNA, also known as messenger RNA, carries the genetic information needed for protein synthesis (**Choice B**).

Ribosomal RNA is found in ribosomes. This RNA contributes to the structure of the ribosome and does not code for proteins (**Choice A**).

ATP (**Choice C**) is a molecule made out of a nucleotide (adenosine tri-phosphate), while nucleic acid is made out of many nucleotides.

tRNA (**Choice D**), also known as transfer RNA, brings amino acids to the ribosomes and does not code for proteins.
