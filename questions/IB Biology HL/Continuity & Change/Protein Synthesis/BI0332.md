# BI0332

Which statements about translation are **not** true?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}

  | | |

  | :-- | :-- |

  | I. | Mature mRNA binds to the large subunit of the ribosome before the small subunit. |

  | II. | The A site is the first site that the initiation tRNA binds to. |

  | III. | The polypeptide chain is released when release factors bind to the E site. |

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

**Choices A, B** and **C**  are incorrect, as all of the statements about translation are false. **Statement I** is incorrect as mature mRNA binds to the small subunit first, specifically at the mRNA's ribosome-binding site. Then, the large subunit joins, forming the complete ribosome-mRNA complex. **Statement II** is incorrect as the initiator tRNA binds to the P site first. **Statement III** is incorrect as the polypeptide chain is released when the release factors bind to the A site. These release factors promote the hydrolysis of the bond between the polypeptide chain and the tRNA at the P site, resulting in the release of the completed polypeptide chain from the ribosome. The E site (exit site) is where the deacylated tRNA exits the ribosome after releasing its amino acid.
