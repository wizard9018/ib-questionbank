# BI1518

Insulin is a protein that undergoes a two-step post-translational modification before it can function. The micrograph shows a pancreatic cell.  In which part of the cell would the post-translational modification of insulin take place?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

Insulin is synthesised as pre-proinsulin by the ribosomes. When it enters the lumen of the rough endoplasmic reticulum, it undergoes initial processing to remove the signal peptide, forming proinsulin. Proinsulin then moves to the Golgi apparatus, where it is further processed into mature insulin by cleaving the proinsulin to remove the C$-$peptide, resulting in the formation of the active insulin molecule, which consists of an A and B chain linked by disulfide bonds. A indicates the Golgi apparatus; **Choice A** is correct.

**Choice B** is incorrect as it indicates a mitochondrion. **Choice C**  is incorrect as it indicates the nucleus. **Choice D**  is incorrect as it indicates a vesicle.
