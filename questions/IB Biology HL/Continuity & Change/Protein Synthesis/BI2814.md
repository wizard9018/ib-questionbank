# BI2814

A mRNA molecule has the following codon.

![BI2814, BI2815 D1.2 MCQ Julia.jpeg](<images/BI2814, BI2815 D1.2 MCQ Julia.jpeg.JPEG>)

What is the corresponding DNA code on the sense strand?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ TGA

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ ACU

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ ACT

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ AGT

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The mRNA codon UGA is transcribed from the DNA template strand (also called the antisense strand). Complementary base pairing means that mRNA to DNA:

U (in mRNA) pairs with A (in DNA template)\
G (in mRNA) pairs with C (in DNA template)\
A (in mRNA) pairs with T (in DNA template)

So, mRNA UGA corresponds to the DNA template strand bases ACT. Therefore, the sense strand (same as mRNA but with T instead of U) is TGA. **Choice A** is the correct answer.
