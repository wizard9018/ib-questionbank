# BI0607

What happens when RNA polymerase binds to DNA at the promoter region?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Introns are removed and exons are joined together.

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Synthesis of a new strand of DNA begins.
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ RNA polymerase detaches from the promoter region.
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ RNA nucleotides are added to the 3' end of the elongating RNA strand.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
After RNA polymerase binds to DNA at the promoter region (a process known as initiation), elongation takes place. During elongation, the 5\' end of the free RNA nucleotide binds to the 3\' OH end of the elongating RNA strand  (**Choice D**). \
After the elongation, termination takes place, during which RNA polymerase detaches from the promoter region (**Choice C**). \
Removal of introns and the joining of exons (**Choice A**) is a step in the post-transcriptional modifications of mRNA, which takes place after termination is over. \
Synthesis of a new DNA strand is part of DNA replication and not transcription (**Choice B**).
