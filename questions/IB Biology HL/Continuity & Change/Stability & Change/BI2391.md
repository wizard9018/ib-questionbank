# BI2391

Which of the following events could lead to the process of ecological succession?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | A nuclear accident that destroys plant and animal life within a 20 km radius. |
    | II. | The dumping of spoil from shale oil mining in large heaps that resemble hills. |
    | III. | Any human intervention on an ecosystem, followed by human abandonment.  |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is true because a nuclear accident, such as the Chornobyl (Ukraine) disaster of 1986, caused the abandonment of many towns, including nearby Pripyat. There are many examples where Pripyat’s once urban landscape has become rewilded as succession has taken place. For example, mature trees now stand around where the town’s sports centre is located.

**Statement II** is true because this has occurred in several locations. For example, near Edinburgh (Scotland), where the remnants of shale oil mining were dumped into large hills or ‘bings’ in the 19th and 20th centuries. The tallest of these is 95 metres. Initially a barren landscape, succession on these bings has been so successful that they are now a protected nature reserve, with their own extensive and well-established food webs including some otherwise rare plant and animal species.

**Statement III** is true because any human activity that disrupts a climax community will create the conditions for succession to take place. Even the simple act of felling a tree in mature woodland will allow sunlight to reach the forest floor, favouring species of ground-living plants, and in turn, the consumers that feed on them directly and indirectly. Humans must leave the area undisturbed for succession to continue and for a climax community to become re-established.

**Choice D** is correct because it contains the three true statements (**I, II** and **III**).

**Choices A, B** and **C** are incorrect because they all omit one or more of the true statements.
