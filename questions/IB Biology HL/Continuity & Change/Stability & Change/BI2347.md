# BI2347

Which statement is correct for how alien plants have been managed in the rewilding of Hinewai Reserve in New Zealand?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Some are tolerated to provide shelter for young trees, which then outcompete the alien plants for light

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Tolerated to provide food for native herbivorous species

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ All are systematically removed to prevent competition with native plants

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ All are systematically removed and replaced with seedlings for native plants

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because, in the rewilding of Hinewai Reserve, some alien plant species are tolerated as they provide shelter for young seedlings of native species. Over time, once the native trees establish themselves, they outcompete the alien plants for light, leading to the eventual decline of these alien species. However, highly invasive species that threaten native ecosystems are not tolerated and are managed accordingly.

**Choice B** is incorrect because alien plant species are not tolerated to provide food for native herbivorous species. Instead, their role is primarily to protect native saplings during early growth stages.

**Choices C** and **D** are incorrect because alien plants are not systematically removed. Only highly invasive species are actively managed, while others are allowed to coexist temporarily to support the regeneration of native plants.
