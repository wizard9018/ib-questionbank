# BI1496

Which statement concerning ecological succession is correct?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ A climax community is always reached

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Human activity does not affect ecological succession

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Communities become less complex as succession progresses

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Succession can occur in areas which are devoid of vegetation and soil

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

Primary succession occurs in lifeless areas where there is no soil present, such as on bare rock exposed after a landslide, lava flows, areas left after a glacier retreats, or on artificial structures like buildings. Pioneer species such as lichens and certain types of algae are the first to colonise these areas, eventually breaking down rock into soil and allowing plants that require soil to take root, leading to a more complex ecosystem over time. **Choice D**  is correct.

**Choice A**  is incorrect as a climax community may not be reached if cyclical succession is taking place or succession is arrested. For example, if land that is going through succession is changed into farmland, the climax community would not be reached. **Choice B**  is incorrect because humans can affect ecological succession such as through deforestation. **Choice C**  is incorrect because complexity generally increases as succession progresses.
