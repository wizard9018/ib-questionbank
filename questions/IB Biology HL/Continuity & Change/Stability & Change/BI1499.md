# BI1499

Which set of statements is correct about gross primary productivity (GPP) and net primary productivity (NPP) in the early, middle and late ages of ecological succession?

| | Early stages | Middle stages | Late stages |

| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |

| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | GPP is low, and NPP makes up a high percentage of GPP | GPP and NPP decrease | GPP begins to increase, and NPP remains low |

| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | GPP is low, and NPP makes up a high percentage of GPP | GPP increases, and NPP reaches a peak and falls | GPP remains high, but NPP decreases |

| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | GPP is high, and NPP makes up a high proportion of GPP | GPP and NPP decrease | Both GPP and NPP continue to decrease |

| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | GPP is high, but NPP makes up a low proportion of GPP | GPP decreases, but NPP makes up a higher proportion of GPP | Both GPP and NPP decrease to zero |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

Gross primary productivity (GPP) is the total amount of energy that producers convert to organic matter (biomass) per unit area per unit time. Net Primary Productivity (NPP) is the amount of energy that remains available for the growth of the producer and for consumption by herbivores and decomposers after subtracting the energy lost from the producer during respiration. NPP = GPP - respiration by producer. Early succession ecosystems tend to have low GPP because plant biomass is initially low. However, because the biomass is low, the respiration rates are also low, meaning a relatively high proportion of the GPP is available as NPP. During the middle stages of succession, producer biomass increases, leading to an increase in both GPP and NPP. The ecosystem becomes more productive as producers synthesise more organic matter through the processes of photosynthesis or chemosynthesis. NPP reaches its peak during these stages because while GPP increases due to higher producer biomass, the energy used in respiration by producers also increases but not to the extent that it diminishes the gains in GPP. In the late stages of succession, the ecosystem stabilises, and while GPP remains high due to the substantial biomass of mature producers, NPP may decrease from its peak. This decrease is because a significant portion of GPP is used for respiration by these mature and often larger producers present in a climax community. **Choice B**  is correct.

**Choice A**  is incorrect as GPP does not decrease in the middle stage of succession as the biomass in the community increases. **Choice C**  is incorrect as GPP is not high during the early stage of succession, as the biomass in the community is low due to a low number of producers. Also, GPP does not decrease in the middle and late stages of succession, as the biomass in the community increases, which increases the GPP. **Choice D**  is incorrect because the GPP is not high in the early stages of succession and GPP and NPP do not fall to zero in the late stages of succession if a climax community is formed.
