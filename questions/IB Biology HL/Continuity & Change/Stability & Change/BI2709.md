# BI2709

Why are wolves considered a keystone species in Yellowstone National Park?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ They are the most abundant animal in their region, and their population density determines ecosystem health.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ They compete with other species for food, creating a more balanced community structure.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Their presence is key to maintaining biodiversity within their ecosystem.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ They physically modify the park’s landscape to produce and maintain habitats for other species.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because keystone species help maintain biodiversity by regulating populations and interactions within the ecosystem, which prevents imbalances that could lead to species loss. \
**Choice A** is incorrect. Their abundance or population density does not define keystone species.\
**Choice B** is incorrect. While keystone species can affect competition, their role is not defined solely by competition. \
**Choice D** is incorrect. Some keystone species may modify habitats (e.g., beavers building dams), but physical landscape modification is not a required characteristic of a keystone species.
