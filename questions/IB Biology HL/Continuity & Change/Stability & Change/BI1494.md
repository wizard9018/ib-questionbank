# BI1494

The coast of California is composed of a climax community of shrub-sized vegetation. Every 10-15 years the area is destroyed by naturally occurring forest fires, and it takes several years for the climax community to be restored. What is this an example of?

![BI1493 fig1](<images/BI1493 fig1.png>)

[Source: Adapted from Region, U. P. S. (2011, August 11). Coastal sage scrub and riparian habitat at San Diego National Wildlife Refuge. Flickr.  Retrieved November 28, 2023, from https://www.flickr.com/photos/usfws_pacificsw/6033289259. Copyright under CC BY 2.0]

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Cyclical succession

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Reforestation

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Arrested succession

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Ecosystem restoration

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

Cyclical succession refers to a repeated ecological sequence of events, where a community undergoes a series of changes following a disturbance (like forest fires), eventually returning to a similar state as before the disturbance. The Californian coastline community goes through a similar cycle of succession every 10-15 years. **Choice A**  is correct.

**Choice B** is incorrect as reforestation is the replanting of trees in an area that was formerly forested. 

Arrested succession is when an ecological succession is halted at an early or mid-successional stage due to persistent environmental conditions or disturbances that prevent the ecosystem from developing into a climax community. The California coast scenario describes a system that does reach a climax community before being reset by fire, so it does not fit the definition of arrested succession; **Choice C**  is therefore incorrect.

Ecosystem restoration involves active human intervention to restore degraded, damaged, or destroyed ecosystems and habitats to their original state. While the cyclical process occurring on the California coast describes a natural process of ecosystem recovery following fires, it does not indicate that human intervention is involved in the restoration process; **Choice D**  is therefore incorrect.
