# BI2762

Which of the following is most likely to be a pioneer species after a glacier retreats, exposing bare rock?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Pincushion moss

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Buffalo grass

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Oak tree

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Prickly rose bush

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because moss is a common pioneer species that can grow on bare rock, sand, or other hard surfaces where soil has not yet developed. This makes them ideal for primary succession, such as after a glacier retreat.

**Choices B, C** and **D** are incorrect because unlike mosses and lichens, grasses, trees and bushes require soil to anchor their roots and absorb nutrients. In primary succession (e.g., after a volcanic eruption or glacier retreat), there is no soil initially, making it impossible for them to establish themselves.
