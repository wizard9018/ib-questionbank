# BI0934

What are the effects of plastic pollution in marine waters?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Temporary formation of microplastics due to sunlight, wind and water action. |
    | II. | Sea turtles consume plastic because of its transparency and resemblance to jellyfish. |
    | III. | Plastic nets entrap fish, sea turtles and mammals. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Sea turtles often mistake plastic bags and other transparent plastics for jellyfish, a primary food source. Consuming plastic can cause blockages in their digestive system, leading to malnutrition, injury, or death; **Statement II** is true.

Discarded plastic fishing nets, known as "ghost nets," pose a serious threat to marine life. Fish, sea turtles, and marine mammals can become entangled in these nets, leading to injury, suffocation, or drowning; **Statement III** is true.

**Statement I**: the temporary formation of microplastics due to sunlight, wind, and water action is false because the formation of microplastics is not temporary. Microplastics are formed when larger plastic debris is broken down by sunlight (UV radiation), wind, and water action. However, once microplastics are formed, they persist in the environment for long periods of time and are not easily degraded. Their presence is a long-term environmental concern, not a temporary one.

**Statements II** and **III** are the only true statements, making **Choice B** the correct answer.

**Choice D** is not the correct answer as it is missing the true **Statement II**. **Choices A** and **C** are not correct as they include the false **Statement I**.
