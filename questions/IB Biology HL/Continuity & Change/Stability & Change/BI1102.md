# BI1102

Which of the following is a characteristic of a climax community?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ High species turnover

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Constant change in species composition

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Stable and persistent community

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Low biodiversity

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
A climax community is characterised by its stability and persistence over time, making **Choice C** correct.

A climax community has low species turnover, therefore, **Choice A** is incorrect. In a climax community, the species composition remains relatively stable over time, with only minor changes in response to long-term environmental conditions. High species turnover is more typical of disturbed or transitional communities.

While there may be some minor fluctuations in the presence of species, a climax community generally maintains a relatively consistent species composition, therefor, **Choice B** is incorrect.

A low biodiversity, **Choice D**, is more characteristic of disturbed or simplified ecosystems. Climax communities represent a mature and diverse ecosystem with a wide variety of species that have adapted to the prevailing environmental conditions.
