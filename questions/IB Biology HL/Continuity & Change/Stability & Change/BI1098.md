# BI1098

Traditional farming methods often come at a high environmental cost, and are not sustainable in the long term. Which of the following factors is correctly paired with the direct effect on the environment?

| | Factors | Effect
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Soil erosion | Leaching of nutrients |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Leaching of nutrients | Increases availability of nutrients for crops |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Application of agrochemicals | Pollution of water |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Use of fungicide | Decreases availability of nutrients for crops |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Application of agrochemicals (**Choice C**) is the addition of a chemical used in agriculture, such as fertilisers, herbicides and other pesticides, growth regulators, and soil enhancers. While they increase crop yields and productivity, they can dissolve in water and leach into nearby water bodies, leading to **water pollution**. This pollution can harm aquatic ecosystems, degrade water quality, and contribute to issues like eutrophication, harming biodiversity and long-term agricultural sustainability.

Soil erosion (**Choice A**) is the removal of soil due to the forces of wind or water, and it can affect the sustainability of agriculture. Once the topsoil layers are removed, **soil fertility is reduced**, and it can take a long time to be restored. Soil erosion may indirectly affect the leaching of nutrients, but it is not a direct effect.

Leaching of nutrients (**Choice B**) is the process by which water-soluble nutrients are washed out of the soil, typically by rainfall or irrigation. This results in a loss of essential nutrients, **decreasing** the availability of nutrients (not increasing) for crops, and can negatively affect plant growth.

**Choice D**, the use of fungicides, is the use of an agrochemical to prevent fungal diseases from crops. Fungicides are water-soluble and lead to pollution. They are not directly related to nutrient availability to crops.
