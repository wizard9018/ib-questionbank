# BI2710

Data about the biochemical oxygen demand (BOD) and fish biomass (kg) were collected from a lake over a period of 12 months. The graph below shows the results:

![BI2710](images/BI2710.png)

Which of the following processes is most likely responsible for this change in the lake's ecosystem?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Acidification due to increased industrial activity

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Eutrophication caused by nutrient overload

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Water temperature rise due to climate change

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Overfishing and depletion of fish resources

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct. Eutrophication occurs when excess nutrients lead to algal blooms. When these algae die and decompose, oxygen is depleted, increasing BOD and causing fish populations to decline due to hypoxia. \
**Choice A** is incorrect. Acidification from industrial activity could lower pH and impact aquatic life, but it does not directly explain the observed increase in biochemical oxygen demand (BOD) and decline in fish biomass. \
**Choice C** is incorrect. Rising water temperatures can stress aquatic life, but they do not directly cause a significant increase in BOD or nutrient-driven oxygen depletion. \
**Choice D** is incorrect. Overfishing reduces fish populations, but it does not cause an increase in BOD.
