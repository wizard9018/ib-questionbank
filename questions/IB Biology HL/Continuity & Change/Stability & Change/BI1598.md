# BI1598

**Maximum mark: 6**

The table below shows the biomagnification of DDT in an aquatic food chain.

|  | DDT concentration (ppm) |
| :-: | :-: |
| Water | 0.05 |
| Zooplankton | 0.10 |
| Small fish | 0.50 |
| Large fish | 2.00 |
| Birds eating fish | 25.00 |

1. Describe the change in the concentration of DDT as the trophic level increases.    [2 marks]

2. Distinguish between biomagnification and bioaccumulation. [1 mark]

3.
    1. Outline the use of DDT, with a reason why it was so popular.    [2 marks]

    2. State a property of DDT that makes it accumulate in biological tissues.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ the concentration of a DDT increases with each ascending trophic level in the food chain  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the increase in concentration between water and zooplankton **OR** producers is two-fold   [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ between zooplankton and small fish increase is five-fold                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increase is four-fold between small fish and larger fish            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increases exponentially at higher trophic levels **OR** largest increase **OR** twelve-fold increase from the large fish to sea birds    [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    **DTT concentration increases** fivefold as we move **up the trophic levels** from **zooplankton to small fish** ($\checkmark$). The greatest **increase is twelvefold from large fish to sea birds** ($\checkmark$), showing it is the most concentrated in the top predators.

2. the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ biomagnification is the process by which chemicals become more concentrated in the tissues of living organisms as they move up the trophic levels **WHEREAS** bioaccumulation is the build-up of non-degradable substances in the body of individual living organisms over time **OR** accumulation of a substance in an organism's tissues because the organism absorbs the substance faster than it can eliminate it     [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    **Biomagnification** is the process by which **chemicals become more concentrated** in the tissues of **living organisms** as they **move up the trophic levels**, whereas **bioaccumulation** is the **build-up** of **non-degradable substances** in the bodies of individual living organisms **over time** ($\checkmark$).

3.
    1. any **two** of the following (must include **one** use and **one** reason to gain full marks):

    **Use:** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ DDT was used to control pest outbreaks in cropland **OR** to control mosquitoes **OR** to help control incidence of malaria **OR** other suitable insect          [1 mark]

    **Reason:** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ DDT is effective at low concentrations in preventing outbreaks of pests        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ initially thought to be of low toxicity to mammals             [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ DDT has a broad-spectrum effect as an insecticide        [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    DDT is a synthetic pesticide that was widely used to **control insect populations**, particularly **mosquitoes** that **transmit malaria**, as well as **agricultural pests** ($\checkmark$). Its use became popular because it is **effective** at **low concentrations** ($\checkmark$) and against a **wide variety of insects** ($\checkmark$).

3.
    2. any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ DDT is persistent **OR** chemically stable **OR** resistant to degradation so has long-lasting effects on the environment.    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ DDT is fat-soluble **OR** is retained in fatty tissues without being readily excreted    [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    DDT accumulates in biological tissues because it is a **fat-soluble chemical** ($\checkmark$) and tends to be retained in fatty tissues.
