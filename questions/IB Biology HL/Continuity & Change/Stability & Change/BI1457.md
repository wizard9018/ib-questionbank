2. Any **two** of the following

 $\ce{ -> }$ cattle have a higher respiration **OR** metabolic rate than fish [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ cattle are endothermic, so need to expend a significant portion of their ingested energy to maintain a constant body temperature, whereas fish are ectothermic temperature of their bodies adjusts to match the water in which they live [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ digestion in cattle is less energy-efficient compared to the digestion processes in fish [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ cattle spend energy on locomotion and supporting their body weight against gravity, whereas fish benefit from buoyancy, which reduces the energy needed for supporting their body weight and movement [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ growth rate of cattle is slower than that of fish [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

 **Cattle**, being **endothermic**, expend **significant energy** to maintain a **constant body temperature**, contrasting with **ectothermic fish** whose body temperature adapts to their aquatic environment, leading to **lower metabolic demands** ($\checkmark$). Additionally, **cattle** need to support their **body weight** against **gravity**, and their locomotion on land consumes **more energy** than **fish**, which are supported by **buoyancy** in water, **reducing** the **energy required** for **movement** ($\checkmark$) and contributing to their lower food conversion ratio. This results in **cattle** having a notably **higher respiration** rate than fish ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **four** of the following

 $\ce{ -> }$ nitrates **OR** phosphates enter water course [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ excess nutrients to the water bodies will cause algae to grow rapidly **OR** algal blooms [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ growth of algae will block sunlight to underwater plants, causing them to die [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ increase in nutrients **OR** dead organic matter leads to more algal growth [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ increase in decomposing dead organic matter [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ aerobic bacteria are decomposers [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ greater oxygen demand **OR** biochemical oxygen demand **OR** BOD [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ oxygen-depleted water can lead to fish **OR** other aquatic organisms dying [1 mark]

 [4 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

 The runoff from using **fertilisers** in agricultural fields can contaminate local water bodies, leading to an **increase** in the **growth of algae** ($\checkmark$) due to added nutrients. The algal growth will **block light** from penetrating the water bodies, so the underwater plants die and decompose ($\checkmark$). The death of **underwater plants** will cause an **increase** in dead organic matter($\checkmark$), promoting the **growth** of **bacteria** ($\checkmark$). These aerobic bacteria will cause a **decrease** in **dissolved oxygen** ($\checkmark$) in the water as they respire and may lead to the **death** of the **fish** and other aquatic **organisms** ($\checkmark$).
