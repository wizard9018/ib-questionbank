# BI1340

What is the role of DNA ligase in DNA replication?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Creates hydrogen bonds between the original and newly synthesised DNA strand

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Synthesises the new DNA strand

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Removes RNA primers

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Sticks DNA fragments together

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

DNA polymerase III synthesises Okazaki fragments on the lagging strand during DNA replication. The role of DNA ligase in DNA replication is to connect these Okazaki fragments together to form a seamless strand, **Choice D** is correct.

No enzyme is involved in forming hydrogen bonds between the complementary bases on the original and newly synthesised DNA strands. **Choice A**  is incorrect. DNA ligase does not synthesise the new DNA strand; instead, this is the role of DNA polymerase I and III. **Choice B**  is incorrect. DNA ligase does not remove RNA primers from the newly synthesised strand. The removal of RNA primers is carried out by DNA polymerase I. **Choice C** is incorrect.
