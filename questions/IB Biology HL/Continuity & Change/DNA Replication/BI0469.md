## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Structure P is DNA polymerase which adds free nucleotides to the 3' end of the DNA strand being produced. Structure Q is the newly synthesized strand which runs (is produced) in the 5' to 3' direction whereas structure S is helicase which unwinds and separates the DNA strand (**Choice A**).

Free nucleotides are not added in the 5' end of the growing strand of DNA as replication runs in the 5' to 3' direction and the strands are antiparallel. Transfer RNA (tRNA) molecules carry amino acids to the ribosome during translation and are not involved in DNA replication (**Choice B**).

The sense strand of DNA has the same sequence of bases as the RNA strand that has been produced using the template strand (antisense strand) of DNA during transcription (**Choice C**).
