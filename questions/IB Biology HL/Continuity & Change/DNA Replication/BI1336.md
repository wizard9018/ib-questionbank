# BI1336

The image shows a DNA strand during replication. What is the position of the mismatched nucleotide, and which enzyme removes the mismatched base?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

In a DNA strand, Cytosine (C) matches up with Guanine (G), and Adenine (A) matches up with Thymine (T). DNA is synthesised in the 5' to 3' direction, meaning that nucleotides are only added to the 3' end, so the top strand is the newly synthesised strand. C should not be matched with A (position P). DNA polymerase III is involved in proofreading by removing the mismatched base and replacing it with the correct one. **Choice C** is, therefore, correct.

**Choices A** and **B** are incorrect as DNA polymerase I replaces the RNA primer at the start of the Okazaki fragment with a DNA strand during DNA replication. **Choice D** is incorrect as Q points to a base on the template strand.
