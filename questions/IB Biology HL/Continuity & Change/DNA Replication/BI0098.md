1. List three purposes for the DNA copies that are produced using PCR. [3 marks]

2. Outline the function of DNA primers in the polymerase chain reaction. [2 marks]

3. Explain the reason for using Taq polymerase in the polymerase chain reaction. [2 marks]

4. Describe one application of the DNA profiles that are produced by PCR and visualized by gel electrophoresis. [3 marks]

1. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ forensics [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ paternity tests [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ testing for genetic diseases [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ human genome project [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ detecting mutations in viral diseases e.g. Covid and HIV [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ any other valid use [1 mark]
[3 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    The copies of DNA that are produced by PCR are used in **paternity testing** ($\checkmark$), **forensic analysis** ($\checkmark$), **analysis of mutations in viruses and bacteria** ($\checkmark$), and were used in the **Human Genome Project** ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ primers attach to the separated strands of DNA [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ primers prevent hydrogen bonds between the separated bases from reforming **OR** prevent original helix from recombining [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ provide a starting point for replication as Taq polymerase attaches the first nucleotide to the 3'-end of the primer [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ primers can be designed to target specific sequences in the genome [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Primers are short sections of DNA that **attach to specific separated strands of DNA** ($\checkmark$). Primers **stop the original strands from combining** to re-form ($\checkmark$) the double helix. Primers then serve as the **starting point for replication as DNA polymerase attaches the first nucleotide** ($\checkmark$) to the 3'-end of the primer.

3. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ it can withstand higher temperatures than human DNA polymerase [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ a temperature of 95 $^o$C is necessary in PCR to separate the DNA strands [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ in PCR a DNA polymerase is required to synthesize the complementary strands [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ Taq polymerase is isolated from thermophilic bacteria [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Taq polymerase is isolated from **thermophilic bacteria** ($\checkmark$). It is used instead of human DNA polymerase as it is **heat resistant** ($\checkmark$). This prevents the polymerase from denaturing when the **solution is heated to 95 $^o$C** **to separate strands of DNA** ($\checkmark$).

4. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ gel electrophoresis separates DNA fragments based on size to produce a unique pattern of bands [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ PCR is used to amplify alleles with STR sequences [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ each individual has a unique combination of allele lengths with STR sequences [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ unique patterns of bands can be used in paternity testing **OR** forensics [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ in **paternity** testing, the pattern of an individual is compared with the patterns of possible  parents [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ in **forensics**, the pattern from a crime scene is compared with the patterns of victims and suspects [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ in a **paternity** test, half of the child's bands match half of the mother’s bands and half of the child’s bands match half of the father’s bands [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ in a forensic investigation,  investigators look for an exact match between all of the bands from the crime scene and the suspect [1 mark]
[3 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    DNA profiles are used in **forensics** to identify suspects. DNA profiles are made from samples from the **victim, crime scene and suspects**. To create forensic profiles **alleles containing STR sequences are amplified using PCR** ($\checkmark$). Each individual has a **unique combination** of allele lengths with **STR sequences** ($\checkmark$) creating a **unique**  “DNA fingerprint”($\checkmark$). **These profiles are compared** ($\checkmark$) to identify the profile matches.
