# BI1150

What is the role of Taq polymerase in the PCR process?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Copies DNA segments at a high temperature

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Used to separate the DNA double helix

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Used to cut DNA into fragments for use in electrophoresis

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Attaches DNA primers to target sequences

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The role of Taq polymerase is to produce copies of target DNA sequences. Taq polymerase is an enzyme that functions at 72⁰C during the annealing phase of PCR.

**Choice B** is incorrect, as no enzymes are required for this step. Instead, the DNA is heated to 95⁰C to split it into individual strands. **Choice C** is incorrect, as the role of restriction enzymes is to splice DNA, and is not a part of the PCR process. **Choice D** is incorrect, as DNA primers attach to the target region without the need for enzymes.
