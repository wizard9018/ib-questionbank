1. Explain how the characteristics of Taq polymerase make it suitable for use in the polymerase chain reaction (PCR).  [2 marks]

2. Describe the possible consequences for a cell if DNA replication stopped. [2 marks]

3. Outline the roles of helicase and DNA polymerase in DNA replication.      [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.    any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ extremely heat resistant **OR** heat-stable so does not denature at  high temperatures used to separate DNA strands in PCR     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ optimal activity around  70 - 75$\degree$C allowing it to efficiently add nucleotides during the extension step               [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ does not need to be replenished after each denaturation cycle, making PCR
continuous and efficient
 [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\
    Taq polymerase is **thermostable**, so it **does not denature** at the **high temperatures** used to **separate DNA** strands during PCR.  ($\checkmark$). It also has an **optimal** working **temperature** of around **70–75$\degree$C**, allowing **efficient** DNA strand extension during the replication phase. ($\checkmark$).

2.    any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ cell division **OR** mitosis **OR** meiosis would not occur [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ cell growth would stop because division is needed for growth  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ damaged DNA could not be repaired [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ eventual  loss of tissue maintenance **OR** tissue **OR** organ malfunction **OR** death [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\
    If DNA replication stopped, the cell would be **unable** to **divide** because there would be no duplicated genetic material to pass on to daughter cell ($\checkmark$). .As a result, **tissues** dependent on **cell renewal**, such as skin or blood, would eventually fails ($\checkmark$).

3. any **two** of the following:\

    $\ce{ -> }$  helicase unwinds **OR** separates DNA **OR** double-stranded DNA **OR** breaks hydrogen bonds between DNA bases    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ DNA polymerase adds free nucleotides  to form new strand **OR** ensures complementary base pairing using original DNA strand as template [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\
    **Helicase unwinds** the double-stranded **DNA**  by **breaking** the **hydrogen bonds** between complementary bases ($\checkmark$). **DNA polymerase** then **adds free nucleotides** to form a new strand, ensuring c**omplementary base pairing** using the original DNA strand as a template ($\checkmark$).
