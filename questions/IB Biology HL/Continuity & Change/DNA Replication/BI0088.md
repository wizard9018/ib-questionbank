# BI0088

What is the function of the Polymerase Chain Reaction?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ To show that DNA replication is semi-conservative

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ To determine the sequence of bases in DNA

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ To make many copies of a sample of DNA

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ To incorporate Taq DNA into the sample

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
PCR is used to amplify samples of DNA so they can be used for analysis.

PCR replicates DNA but does not show that replication is semi-conservative (**Choice A**).

PCR does not determine the base sequence of DNA.  It produces copies of short sections of DNA that can then be further analyzed (**Choice B**).

Taq polymerase is an enzyme used in PCR but no Taq DNA is incorporated.  (**Choice D**).
