# BI0466

What distinguishes DNA polymerase from RNA polymerase?

| | DNA polymerase | RNA polymerase |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| Synthesises a new DNA strand in the 5' to 3' direction | Synthesises an RNA strand in the 3' to 5' direction |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Binds thymine-containing nucleotides to adenine-containing nucleotides | Binds uracil-containing nucleotides to adenine-containing nucleotides |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Binds uracil-containing nucleotides to adenine-containing nucleotides | Synthesises an RNA strand in the 3' to 5' direction |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Used in transcription | Used in RNA replication |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
DNA contains the bases thymine, guanine, adenine, and cytosine.  RNA contains all of these bases except for thymine, which is replaced by uracil.  Therefore, DNA polymerase adds thymine-containing bases to the growing DNA strands, whereas RNA polymerase adds uracil to growing RNA strands, so **Choice B** is correct.

DNA polymerase is an enzyme that synthesises new DNA strands during DNA replication, whereas RNA polymerase is an enzyme that synthesises RNA strands during transcription; therefore, **Choice D** is incorrect. Both enzymes synthesise in the 5' to 3' direction therefore **Choice A** is incorrect.

DNA polymerase binds thymine to adenine and RNA polymerase synthesises in 5' to 3'
direction therefore **Choice C** is incorrect.
