# BI0504

The diagram shows a DNA profile of a child and the profiles of three potential pairs of parents. Which pair(s) could be the actual parents of the child?

![BI0504](images/BI0504.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Parents X only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Parents Y only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Parents Y and Z only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Parents X and Z only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
A child inherits some of their short tandem repeats (STRs) from their father and some from their mother. So, the DNA profile of a child will contain some bands that align to bands on their father’s DNA profile and some bands that align to bands on their mother’s DNA profile. All bands in the child's profile must come either from the mother or from the father.

The image below shows that the bands from the DNA profile of the child align with the bands from the DNA profiles of Parents Y.
![BI0504A](images/BI0504A.png)

Only Parents Y could be the parents of the child.
The top band from the child’s profile is absent in both parents of pair X (**Choices A** and **D**) and Z (**Choices C** and **D**).
