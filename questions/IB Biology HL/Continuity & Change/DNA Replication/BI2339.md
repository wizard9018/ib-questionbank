# BI2339

Which of these is responsible for the semi-conservative replication of DNA?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Action of DNA polymerase

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Complementary base pairing

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Action of DNA helicase

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Hydrogen bonding

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because complementary base pairing means that adenine always pairs with thymine, and guanine always pairs with cytosine. This ensures that during DNA replication, each strand acts as a template for a new DNA strand, so the new helix has one strand of parent DNA and one strand of newly synthesised DNA, with the DNA code conserved.

**Choice A** is incorrect because DNA polymerase is essential for forming the sugar-phosphate backbone of the new strand by linking nucleotides. However, it does not determine the sequence of bases; this is dictated by complementary base pairing.

**Choice C** is incorrect because DNA helicase unwinds and separates the two strands of DNA, it does not have a role in accurate replication of the DNA base sequence on the parent strand.

**Choice D** is incorrect because hydrogen bonds form between complementary base pairs and hold the double helix together, but hydrogen bonding does not have a direct role in determining the sequence of bases on the DNA strands.
