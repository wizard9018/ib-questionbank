# BI1165

Which enzyme involved in DNA replication is matched to its correct function?

| | Enzyme | Function |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Helicase | Unwinds the DNA strand by breaking covalent bonds |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | DNA polymerase | Links the phosphate and sugars of adjacent nucleotides on the new strand |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Ligase | Prevents the DNA strand from becoming tangled  |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | DNA Primase | Sticks newly synthesised DNA fragments together |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
During DNA replication, one role of DNA polymerase is to link phosphate and deoxyribose sugars of adjacent nucleotides on the newly synthesised DNA  strand; **Choice B**  is correct.

Helicase unwinds the DNA strands by breaking the hydrogen bonds between paired nucleotides; **Choice A**  is incorrect. Ligase sticks newly synthesised DNA fragments together to form one continuous strand; **Choice C**  is incorrect. DNA primase adds a short length of RNA to the DNA template strand; **Choice D**  is incorrect.
