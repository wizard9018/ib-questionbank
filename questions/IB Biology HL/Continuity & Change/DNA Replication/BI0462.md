# BI0462

Unlike the majority of other DNA polymerases, Taq polymerase, which is used in PCR reactions can withstand high temperatures without denaturing. Which of the following is a likely source for Taq polymerase?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Bacteria living in the Arctic

- B. $\hspace{1em}$    Bacteria from the hot springs in Yellowstone National Park
- C. $\hspace{1em}$    Bactweria in Siberia's deep permafrost
- D. $\hspace{1em}$    Bacteria found in the undergrowth of rainforests

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Taq polymerase comes from Thermus aquaticus, a thermophilic bacterium that thrives in hot springs like those in Yellowstone National Park. Unlike most other DNA polymerases, it can withstand the high temperatures used in PCR (typically around 95°C during denaturation) without denaturing. Therefore, **Choice B** is the correct answer.

Bacteria living in the Arctic and bacteria in permafrost are adapted to cold temperatures, not heat; **Choices A and C** are not correct.
Bacteria in rainforest undergrowth live in warm but not extreme high-temperature environments. **Choice D** is  not correct.
