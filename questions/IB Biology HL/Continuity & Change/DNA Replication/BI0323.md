2. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$ **BOTH** work on **OR** are involved in building the lagging strand [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ **BOTH** work in a 5' to 3' direction [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ DNA polymerase I removes **OR** replaces RNA primer, **WHEREAS** DNA primase adds **OR** synthsizes, RNA primers [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ DNA polymerase I primarily works on the lagging strand **WHEREAS** DNA primase works on both the lagging and the leading strands [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

    DNA polymerase I and DNA primase are enzymes which are both involved in building the **lagging strand** ($\checkmark$) during DNA replication and they both work in **5' to 3' direction** ($\checkmark$). However while DNA primase adds **RNA primers**, DNA polymerase I **replaces** ($\checkmark$) them with DNA.
