# BI2721

Which letter indicates the type of bonds broken by the enzyme helicase?

![BI2721](images/BI2721.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because helicase breaks the hydrogen bonds between nitrogenous bases to separate the two strands of DNA.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the sugar-phosphate bond between a deoxyribose sugar and a phosphate group is not broken by helicase. **Choice B** is incorrect because the glycosidic bond between a deoxyribose sugar and a nitrogenous base is not broken by helicase. **Choice D** is incorrect for the same reason as **Choice B**.
