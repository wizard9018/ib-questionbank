# BI1387

The following diagram shows the result of gel electrophoresis using samples taken from six individuals. What can be concluded based on these results?

![BI1387](images/BI1387.png)

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
 | | |
 | :-- | :-- |
 | I. | Subject 1 is more closely related to subject 3 than any of the other subjects.|
 | II. | Bands on the right indicate the largest molecules sampled. |
 | III. | Subject 2 and subject 6 may be related.|

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Subject 1 and subject 3 share two bands in common, more than any other pairing; **Statement I** is, therefore, true. Bands on the right have travelled the farthest from the origin and are, therefore, the smallest molecules, not the largest; **Statement II** is, therefore, false. Subject 2 and subject 6 have three bands in common and are, therefore, very likely to be related; **Statement III** is, therefore, true. **Choice C** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because it is missing **Statement III**. **Choices B** and **D** are incorrect because they contain **Statement II**.
