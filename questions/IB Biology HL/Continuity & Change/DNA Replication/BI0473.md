# BI0473

Which of the following is/are characteristic(s) of Taq polymerase?

- I. $\hspace{1em}$ Requires a DNA primer
- II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Works well at temperatures above 98$\degree$C
- III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Used in gel electrophoresis to fluorescent tag DNA molecules

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III only
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Taq polymerase is used to produce multiple copies of DNA using the polymerase chain reaction (PCR). Taq polymerase requires primers, which are short sequences of single-stranded DNA nucleotides which are added at the start of PCR (**Statement I**).

Taq polymerase is very heat-stable and works well at temperatures as high as 72$\degree$C but activity significantly declines as temperatures approach 98$\degree$C (**Statement II**).

Taq polymerase is used in PCR but not in gel electrophoresis. Other molecules are used to add fluorescent tags to DNA in gel electrophoresis (**Statement III**).\
**Choice B** includes **Statement III**, so is not correct. **Choices C** and **D** include **Statements II** and **III**, so are not correct.
