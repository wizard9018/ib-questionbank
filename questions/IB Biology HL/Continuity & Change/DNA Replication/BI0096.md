# BI0096

Which row represents the steps of the polymerase chain reaction (PCR) in the correct order?

| | I | II | III | IV |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Sample is heated to 95 ⁰C | Temperature is reduced to 53⁰C | At 72⁰C Taq DNA polymerase produces two double-stranded copies | Primers attach to both strands |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Sample is heated to 95 ⁰C | Temperature is reduced to 53⁰C | Primers attach to both strands | At 72⁰C Taq DNA polymerase produces two double-stranded copies |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Primers attach to both strands | Sample is heated to 95 ⁰C | At 72⁰C Taq DNA polymerase produces two double-stranded copies | Temperature is reduced to 53⁰C |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Primers attach to both strands | At 72⁰C Taq DNA polymerase produces two double-stranded copies | Sample is heated to 95 ⁰C | Temperature is reduced to 53⁰C |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B**  is correct. In PCR, the sample is first heated to approximately 95 °C to separate the two strands of the DNA double helix (denaturation). The temperature is then lowered to around 53 °C so that primers can anneal to complementary sequences on each DNA strand. Finally, the temperature is raised to 72 °C, the optimum temperature for Taq DNA polymerase, which extends the primers by adding nucleotides to synthesise two double-stranded DNA molecules.

**Choices A, C**, and **D** are incorrect because although they contain the correct PCR stages and temperatures, the steps are arranged in the wrong order.
