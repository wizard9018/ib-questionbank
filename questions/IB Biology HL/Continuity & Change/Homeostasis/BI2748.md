# BI2748

Which statement is correct for excretion or osmoregulation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Osmoregulation is the control of the level of metabolic wastes in the blood.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Excretion removes metabolic wastes from the blood.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Excretion controls the water and salt concentration of the blood.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Osmoregulation removes metabolic wastes from the blood.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because excretion is the process that removes metabolic wastes such as carbon dioxide, urea, and excess salts from the blood. These waste products are produced by metabolic processes and must be eliminated to maintain homeostasis.\
**Choice A** is incorrect because osmoregulation refers to the regulation of water and salt concentration in the blood; it is not the removal of metabolic wastes from the blood.\
**Choices C** and **D** are incorrect because the definitions are with the wrong terms. Excretion is the removal of metabolic wastes from blood, and osmoregulation is the regulation of the water and salt concentration of the blood.
