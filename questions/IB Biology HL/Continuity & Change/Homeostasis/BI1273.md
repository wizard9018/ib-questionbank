# BI1273

Which graph resembles the pattern observed in thermoregulators such as birds and mammals?

![BI1273](images/BI1273.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Thermoregulators, or endotherms, maintain a relatively constant internal body temperature despite changes in environmental temperature. This includes birds and mammals. **Choice A** is correct because it shows a constant body temperature across a range of environmental conditions.

**Choice B** is incorrect because it shows body temperature changing with environmental temperature, whereas endotherms maintain a relatively constant internal temperature across a range of conditions.

**Choice C** is also incorrect because it shows body temperature decreasing as environmental temperature increases, which is not characteristic of either endotherms or ectotherms.

**Choice D** is incorrect because it shows body temperature increasing linearly with environmental temperature, which is characteristic of ectotherms that rely on external heat sources.
