# BI0733

Which row shows a correct difference in the blood concentration of various substances between the renal artery and renal vein?

| | Substance | Renal artery | Renal vein |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Oxygen | Low | High |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Carbon dioxide | Low | High |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Glucose | High | High |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Urea | Low | High |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because carbon dioxide concentration is higher in the renal vein due to aerobic respiration in the kidney.

**Choice A** is incorrect because oxygen concentration is higher in the renal artery; it is used by kidney cells for aerobic cellular respiration.

**Choice C** is incorrect because glucose concentration is lower in the renal vein as some of it is used for metabolic processes in kidney cells.

**Choice D** is incorrect because urea concentration is higher in the renal artery; urea is filtered out in the nephrons.
