# BI2754

Which is the best description of the effect of antidiuretic hormone?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Increased volume of urine production.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Movement of aquaporins from vesicles to membranes in the collecting duct.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Increased active transport of sodium ions in the loop of Henle.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Increased reabsorption of water in the descending limb of the loop of Henle.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because antidiuretic hormone increases the permeability to water of the distal convoluted tubule and collecting ducts by causing aquaporins to move from vesicles to cell membranes. Aquaporins are membrane channel proteins that facilitate the movement of water by osmosis.

**Choice A** is incorrect because antidiuretic hormone increases water reabsorption in the distal convoluted tubule and collecting duct so the volume of urine is reduced.

**Choice C** is incorrect because antidiuretic hormone causes increased reabsorption of water, not increased active transport of sodium ions.

**Choice D** is incorrect because antidiuretic hormone does not affect the permeability of the loop of Henle.
