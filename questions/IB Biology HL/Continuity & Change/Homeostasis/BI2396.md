# BI2396

A typical adult male has 6 $\ce{dm^3}$ of blood. The blood passes through the kidneys 300 times per day. The volume of urine produced is 3 $\ce{dm^3}$.
What proportion of the kidneys’ input is excreted as urine?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 50%

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 1.7%

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 1%

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.17%

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because 6 $\ce{dm^3}$ of blood passes through the kidneys 300 times so the kidneys’ total input is 6 $\times$ 300 = 1800 $\ce{dm^3}$. \
The proportion of the kidneys’ input that is excreted as urine, as a percentage, is:
3dm3 of urine output as a proportion of 1800 $\ce{dm^3}$ total input = $\dfrac{3}{1800} \times 100$% = $1.7 \times 10^{-3} \times 100$% = 0.17%

**Choice A** is incorrect because it calculates urine output as a proportion of the volume of blood in a person’s body, which is not what the question requires.

**Choice B** is incorrect because it is too high by a factor of 10.

**Choice C** is incorrect because it is a miscalculation.
