# BI1274

When body temperature begins to fall, the change is detected by thermoreceptors in the skin. This causes the __I__ to stimulate thyroxin production in the __II__, resulting in a rise in basal metabolic rate. Which row in the table is correct for glands I and II?

| | I | II |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hypothalamus | Pituitary Gland |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Hypothalamus | Thyroid Gland |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Pancreas | Thyroid Gland |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Pancreas | Pituitary Gland |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
When body temperature drops, the hypothalamus responds by releasing thyrotropin-releasing hormone (TRH). This stimulates the anterior pituitary to release thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH), which in turn acts on the thyroid gland, causing it to secrete thyroxin. Thyroxin increases the basal metabolic rate, generating heat and helping restore body temperature. **Choice B** is, therefore, the correct answer.

**Choices A** and **D** are incorrect because although the pituitary gland releases TSH, it does not produce thyroxin - this is the role of the thyroid gland.
**Choice C** is incorrect because the pancreas regulates blood glucose levels and plays no role in thermoregulation.
