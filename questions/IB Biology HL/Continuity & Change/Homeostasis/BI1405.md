    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    It would need to be an **isotonic** solution ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **four** of the following (must include at least **one** adaptation for the glomerulus and **one** for the Bowman's capsule)

    $\ce{ -> }$ afferent arteriole **OR** arteriole leading to glomerulus wider diameter than efferent arteriole **OR** arteriole leading away from it, increasing hydrostatic pressure **OR** blood pressure    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ capillaries of the glomerulus are under high blood pressure **OR** high hydrostatic pressure to allow ultrafiltration                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ fenestrations **OR** gaps **OR** pores between cells of capillary walls, allow fluid **OR** plasma to escape [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ basement membrane prevents most plasma proteins **OR** larger molecules from being filtered out            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ podocytes **OR** filtration slits **OR** inner wall of Bowman's capsule prevent larger molecules from being filtered out of the blood        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ cup shape of Bowman's capsule collects filtrate                [1 mark]
    [4 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The **afferent arteriole** entering the glomerulus is wider than the **efferent arteriole** leaving it, thereby causing **high blood pressure** ($\checkmark$)  in the **glomerulus**, which brings about **ultrafiltration** ($\checkmark$). The high pressure forces **plasma out** through the **fenestrations** of the **capillary walls** ($\checkmark$). Two layers of filters **prevent larger molecules** from entering the **filtrate**. These are the **basement membrane** ($\checkmark$), which is found around the outside of the capillary walls, and the **filtration slits** between the **podocytes** of the Bowman's capsule's wall ($\checkmark$).
