# BI1490

The graph shows blood hormone levels before and after a person consumes a meal. Which line represents insulin?

![BI1490i](images/BI1490i.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

After a meal, beta cells in the pancreas synthesise and secrete insulin so insulin levels quickly rise. Insulin triggers the uptake of glucose by the liver and muscles and then insulin levels return to a baseline after blood glucose levels drop. **Choice D** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect as although insulin levels rise immediately after a meal, they do not remain high for many hours after. **Choice B** is incorrect as insulin levels do not fall after a meal. **Choice C** is incorrect as it rises and falls irrespective of meal consumption.
