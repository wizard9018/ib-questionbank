# BI1276

**Maximum mark: 10**

The graph below shows how the human body typically responds to changing blood glucose concentrations throughout the day.

![BI1276](images/BI1276.png)
[Source: Adapted from J. Suckale and M. Solimena. (2011). *Glucose-day-english.svg*. Retrieved October 10, 2023, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Glucose-day-english.svg. Copyright under CC BY-SA 3.0]

1. State the hormone that is responsible for reducing blood glucose concentration.     [1 mark]

2.
    1. Suggest a reason why the blood glucose concentration in the graph rises sharply several times throughout the day.    [1 mark]

    2. Explain why blood glucose concentration remains stable at night.     [3 marks]

3.
    1. List two examples of foods that could be considered sucrose-rich.    [2 marks]

    2. Suggest, with a reason, one possible outcome for an adult with a diet that is consistently high in sucrose-rich foods.    [2 marks]

4. State one other homeostatic variable that is controlled by negative feedback loops.
[1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ insulin        [1 mark]
[1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
$\hspace{1em}$ The hormone responsible for reducing blood glucose concentration is **insulin** ($\checkmark$).

2.
    1.
    $\ce{ -> }$ blood glucose concentration rises several times due to the digestion of carbohydrates **OR** consumption of food **OR** meals  [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Rises in blood glucose concentration coincide with **meals consumed** ($\checkmark$) during the day.

2.
    2. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ blood glucose concentration is maintained through homeostasis [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ as blood glucose concentrations begin to drop, the pancreas, **OR** alpha cells, secrete glucagon         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ glucagon promotes gluconeogenesis **OR** the breakdown of glycogen **OR** the hydrolysis of glycogen in the liver **OR** glycogenolysis        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ glucose is released into the blood to maintain the blood glucose concentration                                            [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Blood glucose concentrations remain stable at night because **homeostasis** keeps glucose levels **balanced** ($\checkmark$). As glucose concentrations begin to **decrease, pancreatic alpha cells release glucagon** ($\checkmark$), which acts on the **liver** to promote the **breakdown** of stored **glycogen into glucose** ($\checkmark$).

    1. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ table sugar         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ syrups **OR** maple syrup **OR** suitable example of syrup        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ fruit **OR** any named fruit        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ desserts **OR** cake **OR** cookies **OR** any other named sweet dessert [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ processed food **OR** microwave meals **OR** snacks **OR** crackers **OR** any other suitable example    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ sweetened beverages **OR** juice **OR** soda **OR** alcohol        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ sauces **OR** salad dressing **OR** ketchup **OR** BBQ sauce **OR** any other named sauce    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ any other correctly named food high in sucrose        [1 mark]

    **Apples** and **soda** ($\checkmark$).

3.
    2. Any **one pair** of the following (must include both a suggested outcome and a reason to achieve full marks):

    *Diabetes* \
    $\ce{ -> }$ diabetes **OR** type II diabetes     [1 mark]
    $\ce{ -> }$ **BECAUSE** elevated glucose levels could lead to insulin resistance        [1 mark]

    *Obesity* \
    $\ce{ -> }$ obesity **OR** weight gain             [1 mark]
    $\ce{ -> }$ **BECAUSE** more food is ingested than needed **OR** imbalance between food intake and energy expenditure **OR** excess glucose is converted to fat **OR** stored as fat    [1 mark]

    *Dental problems* \
    $\ce{ -> }$ dental problems **OR** tooth decay             [1 mark]
    $\ce{ -> }$ **BECAUSE** the sugar is broken down by bacteria that harm the teeth **OR** acid produced by bacteria erodes tooth enamel **OR** demineralises the teeth     [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    An adult who consumes too much sucrose-rich food on a regular basis could develop **diabetes** ($\checkmark$) because **elevated blood glucose levels** may lead to **insulin resistance** ($\checkmark$).
