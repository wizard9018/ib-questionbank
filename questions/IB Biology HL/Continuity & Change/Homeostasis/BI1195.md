# BI1195

**Maximum mark: 9**

Below is an image of a kangaroo rat.  Kangaroo rats are a group of species belonging to the genus *Dipodomys* and are well-adapted to dry, arid habitats.  They are generally nocturnal and are capable of producing urine approximately 14 times more concentrated than their blood.  These, and other adaptations, enable kangaroo rats to survive indefinitely without drinking water.

![BI1195](images/BI1195.png)
[Source: Adapted from Rex (2005, September 25). *Kangaroo rats (Dipodomys)*. Retrieved September 5, 2023, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kangaroo_rat#. Copyright under public domain.]

1. State which part of the nephron is responsible for ultrafiltration.     [1 mark]

2. In proportion to their size, kangaroo rats have a loop of Henle that is much longer than other animals.  Explain how a longer loop of Henle adapts the kangaroo rat to desert environments.  [3 marks]

3. Outline why, when organisms get too hot, the enzyme-controlled reactions in their cells start to fail.        [2 marks]

4. Explain how natural selection may have led to kangaroo rats adapting to dry, arid conditions.      [3 marks]

2. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ descending limb of loop of Henle is permeable to water but impermeable to sodium ions              [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ ascending limb of loop of Henle is permeable to sodium ions but impermeable to water             [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ ascending limb pumps sodium ions into the medulla, by active transport, maintaining a high solute concentration in the medulla            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ as filtrate flows down the descending loop of Henle, water is moves out by osmosis     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ longer loop of Henle will therefore increase the surface area of reabsorption, conserving water             [1 mark]

    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The **descending** loop of Henle is relatively **permeable to water** but **impermeable to sodium ions** ($\checkmark$) whereas the **ascending** loop of Henle is **permeable to sodium ions** but **not** to **water** ($\checkmark$). The **ascending loop** of Henle **pumps sodium ions** into the medulla by **active transport**, leading to a **high solute concentration** in the **medulla** ($\checkmark$), so **water** moves out of the loop of Henle by osmosis ($\checkmark$). The longer loops of Henle in desert rats will **enhance water reabsorption**  from the filtrate, concentrating urine further to **help survive** ($\checkmark$) droughts.

3. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ enzymes are globular proteins            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ 3D structure held together by weak **OR** hydrogen bonds    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increase in temperature increases kinetic energy of molecules    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increased kinetic energy **OR** vibration of the molecules can disrupt the weak bonds **OR** break the bonds, holding the 3D structure together        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ shape of the active site changes **OR** destroyed        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ substrate no longer fits **OR** binds to the active site so the reaction cannot take place    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ enzyme is denatured **OR** denaturation of enzyme takes place    [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Enzymes are **globular proteins** held together by **hydrogen bonds** ($\checkmark$). An **increase** in **temperature increases** the **kinetic energy** ($\checkmark$) of molecules. This **increased kinetic energy** can **disrupt** these **weak bonds** ($\checkmark$), so the shape of the enzyme starts to break down. This may **change** the **shape** of the **active site** ($\checkmark$), sometimes irreversibly, so the enzyme can no longer function and is **denatured** ($\checkmark$).

4. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ variety of traits **OR** genetic diversity found in the population of kangaroo rats    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ some traits **OR** suitable example of trait may be more adapted to fit a drier climate    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ these individuals with the adaptive traits will be able to survive **OR** natural selection will allow the individual with the adaptive traits to survive    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ these individuals will pass their traits to their offspring     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ over time, more of the individuals in the population will be able to live in the dry, arid climate            [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    There is usually a variety of traits or **genetic diversity** in a **population** ($\checkmark$), such as kangaroo rats. Some of these **traits** make the individuals **more adapted** to living in a **drier climate** ($\checkmark$). **Natural selection** will allow the individuals with these adaptive traits to **survive** (survival of the fittest) ($\checkmark$). These individuals will pass their traits to their offspring, and so **eventually, more of the population** will be able to **live** in the **dry, arid conditions** of the desert ($\checkmark$).
