# BI2665

**Maximum mark: 6**

The diagram below is a simple outline of the nephron.

![BI2665](images/BI2665.png)

1. Name the structures labelled I, II, III and IV in the diagram. [4 marks]

2. State the functions of structures I and II from part (a). [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ I – Bowman’s capsule [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ II – Descending limb of the loop of Henle [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ III – Ascending limb of the loop of Henle [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ IV – Distal convoluted tubule [1 mark]
[4 marks]

2. Any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ I (Bowman’s capsule) is the location of ultrafiltration **OR** small substances are filtered from the blood into the Bowman’s capsule [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ in II (the descending limb of the loop of Henle), water is reabsorbed, **OR** the walls of the descending limb are permeable to water but not to sodium ions [1 mark]
[2 marks]

 :u[Sample answer]:

 The Bowman’s capsule (I) is the location of **ultrafiltration** ($\checkmark$), small substances are filtered here from the blood into the nephron. **Water is reabsorbed** ($\checkmark$) in the descending limb of the loop of Henle (II).
