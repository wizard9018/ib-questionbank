# BI0185

Which fluid acts as a coolant in the human body?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Tears
- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Sweat

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Urine
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Saliva

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Sweat (**Choice B**) is a coolant and helps to cool the body.\
Tears (**Choice A**), urine (**Choice C**) and saliva (**Choice D**) do not lower or raise the body temperature.
