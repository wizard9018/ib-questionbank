# BI0739

Which row correctly describes the effect of a decrease in the water concentration of the blood?

| | ADH production | Permeability of collecting duct | Urine concentration |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Decrease | Increase | Increase|
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Decrease | Decrease | Increase |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Increase | Increase | Increase |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Increase | Decrease | Decrease |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because when the water concentration of the blood decreases, ADH production increases. ADH increases the permeability of the collecting duct so that more water is reabsorbed, and this increases the concentration of urine.

**Choices A** and **B** are incorrect because when the water concentration of the blood decreases, ADH production increases. The increase in ADH production increases the permeability of the collecting duct.

**Choice D** is incorrect because increased ADH production increases the permeability of the collecting duct and increases the concentration of urine.
