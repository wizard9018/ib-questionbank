# BI0737

Which statement(s) is/are true for antidiuretic hormone (ADH)?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | ADH affects the permeability of the loop of Henle |
    | II. | ADH production increases with low water intake |
    | III. | ADH increases the permeability of the collecting duct |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is not true, ADH does not affect the loop of Henle.

**Statements II** and **III** are both true. Low water intake stimulates ADH production. ADH increases the water reabsorption at the collecting duct, resulting in smaller volumes of more concentrated urine and so reducing water loss.
