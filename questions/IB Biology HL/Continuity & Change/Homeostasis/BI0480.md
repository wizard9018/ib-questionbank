# BI0480

Which row is correct for glucagon?

| | Stimulated by: | Secreted by: |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| High blood glucose concentration | Beta cells |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | High blood glucose concentration | Alpha cells |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Low blood glucose concentration | Beta cells |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Low blood glucose concentration | Alpha cells |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because low blood glucose concentration is detected by the alpha cells in the pancreas and they respond by releasing glucagon. **Choice A** is incorrect because high blood concentration does not cause the secretion of glucagon and it is not secreted by beta cells. **Choice B** is incorrect because high blood concentration does not cause the secretion of glucagon, it causes the release of insulin. **Choice C** is incorrect because glucagon is not secreted by beta cells.
