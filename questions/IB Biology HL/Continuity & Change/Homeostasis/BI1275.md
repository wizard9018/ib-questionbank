# BI1275

Which is **not** a physiological human response to a decrease in temperature?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Vasodilation of blood vessels

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Hairs on the skin become erect

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Sweat glands reduce sweat production

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Metabolic activity increases in the liver

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
When exposed to low temperatures, the body reduces the volume of blood that reaches the extremities by vasoconstriction (not vasodilation), thereby reducing heat loss; **Choice A** is the correct answer.

**Choices B, C**, and **D** are incorrect since these are all accurate physiological responses to a drop in temperature.
