# BI0740

Which of these substances should not pass through the glomerulus and into Bowman’s capsule?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Amino acids

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ White blood cells

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Water

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Glucose

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because, in a healthy kidney, white blood cells are too large to pass from the glomerulus into the Bowman’s capsule. **Choices A, C** and **D** are incorrect because amino acids, water and glucose are small enough to be filtered from the capillaries in the glomerulus into the Bowman’s capsule.
