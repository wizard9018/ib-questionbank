# BI0738

Which substance is normally almost completely reabsorbed from the filtrate in the proximal tubule?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Urea

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Glucose

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Water

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Sodium ions

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because, under normal conditions, almost all filtered glucose is reabsorbed in the proximal tubule and returned to the blood. Glucose enters the tubule cells by sodium–glucose cotransport and leaves them towards the blood by facilitated diffusion.

**Choice A** is incorrect because only part of the filtered urea is reabsorbed in the proximal tubule; urea is not almost completely reabsorbed there.

**Choice C** is incorrect because, although most filtered water is reabsorbed in the proximal tubule by osmosis, a substantial proportion remains in the filtrate and passes into the loop of Henle.

**Choice D** is incorrect because only part of the filtered sodium ions is reabsorbed in the proximal tubule. Further sodium reabsorption occurs in later regions of the nephron.
