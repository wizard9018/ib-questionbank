# BI1237

Which is **not** a role of the kidneys?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ To regulate the concentration of ions and water in the blood

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ To make urine

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ To remove waste products from the blood

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ To make bile

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The kidney does not make bile, the liver does, **Choice D**  is correct.

**Choices A, B** and **C**  are incorrect as they are all functions of the kidneys.
