# BI2752

Which row could be correct for the labelled parts of the nephron?

![BI2752](images/BI2752.png)

| $\hspace{2em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ Osmolarity / mOsmol $\mathbf{L^{-1}}$ $\hspace{1em}$ |
| --- | :-: |

| | $\hspace{1em}$ W $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ X $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ Y $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ Z $\hspace{1em}$ |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | 1200 | 300 | 400 | 1000 |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | 300 | 400 | 1000 | 1200 |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | 400 | 1000 | 1200 | 300 |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | 1000 | 1200 | 300 | 400 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct. **W** is the medulla. Active transport in the ascending limb of the loop of Henle moves sodium ions into the medulla and water does not move by osmosis from the ascending limb because the walls of this part of the nephron are impermeable to water. This increases the solute concentration of the medulla up to 1200 mOsmol $\ce{L^{-1}}$.

**X** is the proximal convoluted tubule. Reabsorption of water and solutes produces an isotonic solution of approximately 300 mOsmol $\ce{L^{-1}}$.

**Y** is the ascending limb of the loop of Henle. Active transport of sodium ions after reabsorption of water in the descending limb reduces the solute concentration in this part of the nephron.

**Z** is the collecting duct. Antidiuretic hormone (ADH) increases water reabsorption from the collecting duct which increases the osmolarity in this part of the nephron.

**Choices B, C** and **D** are incorrect because the osmolarity for at least one of the labelled parts is outside the normal range.
