# BI1184

Which row correctly describes the results due to a decrease in the blood solute concentration?

| | ADH production | Permeability of collecting duct | Urine concentration |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Decreases | Decreases| Decreases |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Decreases | Increases | Decreases |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Increases | Increases | Increases |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Increases | Decreases | Decreases |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because when the blood solute concentration decreases, ADH production decreases. This means there are fewer aquaporins in the cell membranes of the cells lining the collecting duct, so less water is reabsorbed into the blood, and urine concentration decreases.

**Choices C** and **D** are incorrect because when the solute concentration of the blood decreases, ADH production decreases.

**Choice B** is incorrect because decreased ADH production decreases the permeability of the collecting duct.
