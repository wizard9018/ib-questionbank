# BI0529

Which letter indicates the part of the nephron that is impermeable to salts and plays a role in increasing filtrate solute concentration to 200 - 300% relative to normal blood solute concentration?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The descending limb of the loop of Henle is permeable to water but not salts. As the filtrate flows down the descending limb, it becomes progressively more concentrated as osmosis draws water out of the filtrate into the medulla. The result near the bottom of the descending limb is a filtrate whose solute concentration is between 200% and 300% that of the normal blood solute concentration. This position is labelled **B** in the diagram.

In the proximal convoluted tubule (**Choice A**), substances such as sodium are selectively reabsorbed from the filtrate into the blood. In the ascending limb of the loop of Henle (**Choice C**), sodium, but not water, is actively transported into the medulla. In the distal convoluted tubule (**Choice D**), the concentrations of individual solutes, such as sodium, potassium, and calcium, are adjusted by active transport.
