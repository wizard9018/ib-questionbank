# BI0736

This diagram shows the basic structure of a nephron.

![BI0736](images/BI0736.png)

How does the nephron differ between animals that have evolved in areas with low availability of water, for example, in the desert?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Larger surface area in glomerulus

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Higher density of capillaries in glomerulus

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Shorter proximal convoluted tubule

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Longer loop of Henle

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because animals that have evolved in environments where they need to conserve water have longer loops of Henle for increased reabsorption of water.

**Choice A** is incorrect because a larger surface area in the glomerulus would lead to increased water concentration in the filtrate.

**Choice B** is incorrect because a greater number of capillaries in the glomerulus would lead to more water being filtered out into the urine.

**Choice C** is incorrect because a shorter proximal convoluted tubule would reduce the reabsorption of water and other substances.
