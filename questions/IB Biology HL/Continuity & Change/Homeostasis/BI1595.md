# BI1595

Antidiuretic hormone (ADH) helps regulate the amount of water in the body. In normal adults, the brain produces a steady supply of ADH at night, however, many children are deep sleepers and do not produce as much ADH as a result.

What is a possible consequence of reduced ADH production in children?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Increased urine production

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Decreased thirst

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ High blood pressure

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Low blood sodium levels

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because ADH increases water reabsorption in the collecting ducts, reducing the quantity of urine produced. Children who secrete less ADH would produce more urine as a result.

**Choices B, C,** and **D** are incorrect because excessive thirst (not decreased thirst), low blood pressure (not high blood pressure), and high blood sodium (not low blood sodium) are all symptoms of low ADH production.
