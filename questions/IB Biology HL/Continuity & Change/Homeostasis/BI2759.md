# BI2759

Which of the following are true of homeostatic mechanisms in humans?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | They maintain body temperature at a constant 37.0$\degree$C |
    | II. | They maintain blood glucose concentration within a preset range |
    | III. | They maintain osmotic concentration of human blood plasma at a constant 296 mOsmol $\ce{L^{-1}}$ |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Homeostasis is the maintenance of a stable internal environment within narrow limits, despite changes in the external environment.

Human body temperature fluctuates within a narrow range (typically ~36.1$\degree$C to 37.2$\degree$C) due to circadian rhythm, activity, and hormonal cycles. **Statement I** is false.

Insulin and glucagon regulate blood glucose within a homeostatic preset range of approximately 4–8 mmol  $\ce{L^{-1}}$. **Statement II** is true.

Plasma osmolarity is not maintained at exactly 296 mOsmol $\ce{L^{-1}}$; homeostatic mechanisms regulate it within a typical range of 280–296 mOsmol $\ce{L^{-1}}$. Therefore, **Statement III** is false.

**Choice A** is therefore the correct answer, as it only includes the one true **Statement II**.

**Choices B, C** and **D** are incorrect as they include one or more false statement.
