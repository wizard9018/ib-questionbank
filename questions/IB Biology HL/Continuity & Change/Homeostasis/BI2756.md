# BI2756

Which row is correct for the effect of the three conditions on blood flow to the kidney?

| | Vigorous exercise | Wakeful rest | Sleep |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-:
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Increased | High | Increased |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Increased | Low | Reduced |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Reduced | High | Reduced |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Reduced | Low | Reduced |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because blood supply to the kidneys is reduced during both vigorous physical exercise and sleep. During exercise, blood flow to the muscles increases to bring oxygen and glucose for aerobic respiration to facilitate muscle contraction, which decreases blood flow to the kidneys. During sleep, arterioles contract and reduce blood flow to the glomeruli.

**Choice A** is incorrect because during vigorous exercise and sleep, blood flow to the kidney is reduced, not increased.

**Choice B** is incorrect because during vigorous exercise, blood flow to the kidneys is reduced, and blood flow to the kidneys during wakeful rest is high.

**Choice D** is incorrect because during wakeful rest, blood flow to the kidneys is high, not low.
