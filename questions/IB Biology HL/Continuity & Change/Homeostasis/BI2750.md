# BI2750

The concentration of proteins in blood plasma is approximately 750 g $\ce{dm^{-3}}$. Which row is correct for protein concentrations under normal body conditions?

| | Concentration in glomerular filtrate / $\mathbf{g\ dm^{-3}}$ |
| --- | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | 40 |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | 700 |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | 350 |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | 0 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because, under normal physiological conditions, proteins are not found in the glomerular filtrate because, due to their large size and negative charge, they cannot pass through the basement membrane. The filtration barrier is selectively permeable, allowing water and small solutes through but retaining plasma proteins in the blood.

**Choices A, B** and **C** are incorrect because they suggest the presence of proteins in the filtrate. However, under normal conditions, proteins do not pass into Bowman’s capsule, so their concentration in the filtrate is effectively 0 g $\ce{dm^{-3}}$.
