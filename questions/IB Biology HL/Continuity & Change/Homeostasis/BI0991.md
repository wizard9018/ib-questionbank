# BI0991

Identify the line in the table below which gives the correct responses of the skin to a drop in environmental temperature.

| | Sweat glands | Arterioles | Erector pili muscles
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ |Increase sweat production | Vasodilation | Relax |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ |Decrease sweat production | Vasoconstriction | Contract |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Increase sweat production | Vasoconstriction | Relax |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Decrease sweat production | Vasodilation | Contract|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
When the environmental temperature drops, your body responds by trying to conserve heat. Reducing sweat production means there is less sweat to evaporate from the skin's surface, so less heat is lost. Vasoconstriction of the blood vessels reduces the volume of blood reaching the surface of the heat, so less heat is lost by radiation. Contraction of the erector pili muscles makes the hairs of the skin stand up, trapping an insulating layer of air next to the skin, again reducing heat loss by radiation and also by convection. **Choice B** is, therefore, the correct answer.

**Choices A**, **C** and **D** are incorrect as they have one or more factors wrong.
