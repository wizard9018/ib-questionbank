# BI1373

**Maximum mark: 5**

Congenital leptin deficiency is a genetic disorder caused by mutations in the leptin gene. A symptom of congenital leptin deficiency is extreme early-onset obesity. The diagram shows a pedigree chart for a family affected by the disease.

![BI1373](images/BI1373.png)

1.
 1. State the mode of inheritance of congenital leptin deficiency. [2 marks]

 2. Explain your reasoning for your answer to part (i). [1 mark]

 3. Deduce the genotype of individual X. [1 mark]

2. Leptin regulates obesity via a negative feedback mechanism. Define the term ‘negative feedback'. [1 mark]
