# BI0479

Which row is correct for insulin?

| | Stimulated by: | Secreted by: |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| High blood glucose concentration | Beta cells |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | High blood glucose concentration | Alpha cells |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Low blood glucose concentration | Beta cells |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Low blood glucose concentration | Alpha cells |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because high  blood glucose concentration is detected by the beta cells in the pancreas and they respond by releasing insulin. **Choice B** is incorrect because alpha cells produce glucagon and not insulin. **Choice C** is incorrect because the release of insulin is triggered by high blood glucose concentration and not low blood glucose concentration. **Choice D** is incorrect because low blood glucose concentration does not cause the release of insulin and insulin is not secreted by alpha cells.
