# BI2346

Which row is correct for uncoupled respiration in brown adipose tissue?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ ATP is produced, used to generate heat, and is more common in adulthood.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ ATP is produced, used to generate heat, and is more common in childhood.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ No ATP is produced, and is more common in adulthood.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ No ATP is produced, and is more common in childhood.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because in uncoupled respiration, ATP is not produced by the electron transport chain. Instead, the energy released from the electron transport chain is entirely converted into heat. This process is vital for thermoregulation, especially in children who are more prone to heat loss due to their higher surface-area-to-volume ratio. Brown adipose tissue is more abundant in childhood to generate heat and prevent hypothermia.

**Choice A** is incorrect because ATP is not produced by uncoupled respiration in brown adipose tissue, and there is a higher percentage of brown adipose tissue in childhood.

**Choice B** is incorrect because ATP is not produced by uncoupled respiration in brown adipose tissue.

**Choice C** is incorrect because there is a higher percentage of brown adipose tissue in childhood.
