# BI1272

Which is **NOT** a correct example of a homeostatic response?

| | Stimuli | Homeostatic Response |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Rise in body temperature | Sweating |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Rise in blood glucose levels | Release of insulin |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Fall in blood glucose levels | Release of glycogen |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Fall in body temperature | Shivering |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
When blood glucose levels fall, the cells of the pancreas release the hormone glucagon (not glycogen).

Glucagon promotes the breakdown of glycogen stored in the liver into glucose (glycogenolysis). This process, along with gluconeogenesis (the synthesis of glucose from non-carbohydrate sources), raises blood glucose levels. **Choice C** is, therefore, the correct answer.
