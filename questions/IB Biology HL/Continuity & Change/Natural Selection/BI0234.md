# BI0234

Which of the following rows correctly identifies a similarity and a difference between natural selection and selective breeding in the context of genetics and evolution?

| | Similarity | Difference |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| Both processes rely on specific trait selection | Natural selection is driven by environmental pressures |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Both processes enhance genetic diversity | Selective breeding occurs primarily in wild populations |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Both processes rely on specific trait selection | Selective breeding increases genetic variation |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Both processes enhance genetic diversity | Natural selection results in rapid change in phenotypes |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because in both selective breeding and natural selection, specific traits are selected for by either the environmental pressures (natural selection) or humans (selective breeding). **Choice B** is incorrect because selective breeding typically reduces rather than increases genetic diversity, and it is controlled by man, so occurs primarily in domestic situations. **Choice C** is incorrect as although both do rely on specific trait selection, selective breeding reduces genetic variation.  **Choice D** is incorrect as selective breeding decreases genetic diversity, and the pace of natural selection varies depending on environmental factors.
