# BI1127

Prader-Willi syndrome is a rare autosomal dominant disease which causes excessive, uncontrolled eating in adulthood. Assuming that the Prader-Willi trait is in Hardy Weinberg equilibrium, if the frequency of the dominant allele in the general population is 1 in 10000, what is the frequency of the recessive allele?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.9999

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.0009

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.99999999

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.9

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The formula to represent the frequency of dominant and recessive alleles in the population is p + q =1, where p represents the frequency of dominant alleles, and q represents the frequency of recessive alleles. Therefore, p = 1 in 10000, which is equivalent to 0.0001, so 0.0001 + q = 1, q = 1 - 0.0001, q = 0.9999, **Choice A**  is correct.

**Choices B, C** and **D** are incorrect.
