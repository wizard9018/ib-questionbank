# BI2599

Which of the statements explains why artificially selected characteristics can sometimes cause problems for an organism?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Artificial selection increases mutation rates, resulting in a higher number of genetic disorders.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Desirable traits selected by humans do not always improve the survival of organisms in a natural environment.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Artificial selection makes organisms more resistant to environmental changes.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Artificial selection results in higher genetic diversity so populations are more vulnerable to disease.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Artificial selection involves humans selectively breeding animal or plant organisms over many generations so that their offspring have desired traits.  However, desirable traits selected by humans do not always improve the survival of organisms in a natural environment. For example, the selective breeding of dogs for aesthetic reasons, such as short snouts, may have the unintended consequences of dogs having breathing difficulties; **Choice B**  is correct.

Mutations occur randomly in DNA, they are not due to artificial selection. **Choice A**  is incorrect. \
Artificial selection results in certain alleles becoming more prominent in populations, which reduces genetic diversity. A reduction in genetic diversity makes organisms less able to adapt to environmental changes; **Choice C**  is incorrect. \
Artificial selection results in lower genetic diversity; **Choice D**  is incorrect.
