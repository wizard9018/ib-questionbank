# BI1129

In the Hardy Weinberg formula, p:sup[2] + 2pq + q:sup[2] =1 , what do p:sup[2], 2pq and q:sup[2] represent?

| | p:sup[2] | 2pq |  q:sup[2] |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Frequency of dominant alleles | Frequency of heterozygous individuals | Frequency of recessive alleles |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Frequency of homozygous dominant individuals | Frequency of heterozygous individuals | Frequency of homozygous recessive individuals |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Frequency of recessive alleles | Frequency of homozygous dominant individuals | Frequency of heterozygous individuals |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Frequency of homozygous recessive individuals | Frequency of heterozygous individuals | Frequency of homozygous dominant individuals |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
In the Hardy-Weinberg equation, p:sup[2] represents the frequency of homozygous dominant individuals, 2pq represents the frequency of heterozygous individuals, and q:sup[2] represents the frequency of homozygous recessive individuals. **Choice B**  is correct.

**Choices A, C** and **D**  are incorrect.
