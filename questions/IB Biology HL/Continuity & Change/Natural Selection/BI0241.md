# BI0241

Which statement(s) about natural selection is/are true?

- I. $\hspace{1em}$ Learned behaviour changes over time.
- II. $\hspace{.7em}$    Individuals that are better adapted are more likely to survive.
- III. $\hspace{.4em}$    It decreases the frequency of characteristics that make individuals better adapted.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$    I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$    II only
- C. $\hspace{1em}$    II and III only
- D. $\hspace{1em}$    I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is false because learned behaviours are acquired during an individual’s lifetime and are not encoded in the base sequence of genes, so they are not heritable and do not cause evolutionary change by natural selection.

**Statement II** is true because individuals with traits that increase fitness are more likely to survive and reproduce (differences in adaptation, survival and reproduction are the basis of natural selection).

**Statement III** is false because natural selection tends to increase, not decrease, the frequency of advantageous heritable characteristics (those linked to higher survival/reproductive success).

**Choice B** is, therefore, the correct answer, as it provides only the one true **Statement II**

**Choices A, C** and **D** are incorrect as they include one or more of the false **Statements I** and **Statement III**
