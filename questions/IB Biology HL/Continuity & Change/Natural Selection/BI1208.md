# BI1208

Which statement(s) indicate how genetic variation contributes to natural selection?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Offspring with unfavourable characteristics are more likely to survive  |
    | II. | Offspring that look different from others in their species are more likely to survive and breed |
    | III. | Offspring with favourable alleles are able to survive and reproduce |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Genetic variation (differences within species caused by differences in gene expression or mutations) can influence natural selection, as offspring with favourable alleles or characteristics for survival, such as increased speed, camouflage or disease resistance, are more likely to survive, reproduce and pass on their alleles to the next generation (**Statement III**), **Choice C**  is correct.

**Statement I**  is incorrect, as offspring with unfavourable characteristics are less likely to survive. **Statement II**  is incorrect, as offspring that look different from other species are only more likely to survive if those differences are favourable for survival, such as better camouflage from predators. **Choice A** is incorrect, as it includes **Statement I**. **Choices B** and **D**  are incorrect, as they include **Statements I** and **II**.
