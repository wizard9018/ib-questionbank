# BI0711

Which would **not** cause changes to the allele frequency within a population?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Mutations

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Reproductive isolation

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Variable protein expression

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Natural selection

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choices A, B** and **D**  are all incorrect as they all cause changes to allele frequency.
