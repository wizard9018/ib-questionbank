# BI1206

Which factor is **not** a density-independent factor that affects the survival of organisms within a population?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Temperature

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Presence of predators

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Extreme weather conditions

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Rainfall levels

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Density-independent factors can affect population growth but are not influenced by the population size. The presence of predators is a density-dependent factor as its impact on a population’s survival and growth becomes more significant as the population’s density increases. **Choice B**  is correct.

Temperature (**Choice A**), extreme weather conditions (**Choice C**) and rainfall levels (**Choice D**) are all density independent factors, as they affect the survival of organisms within a population regardless of the population’s size or density. **Choices A, C** and **D** are incorrect.
