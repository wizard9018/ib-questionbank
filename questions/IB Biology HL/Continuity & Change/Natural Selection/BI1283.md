# BI1283

Which of these is not an abiotic selection pressure?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Competing for mates

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ High temperatures

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Oxygen availability

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Pollution

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Abiotic factors are non-living factor, and competing for mates (**Choice A**) is a living, or biotic factor. **Choice B, choice C** and **choice D** are incorrect because they are all non-living (abiotic) factors.
