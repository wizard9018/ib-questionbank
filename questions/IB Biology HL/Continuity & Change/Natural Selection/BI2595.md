# BI2595

Sickle cell anaemia is a recessively inherited disease. People who are affected by sickle cell anaemia (ss) have sickle-shaped red blood cells that can occlude blood vessels. The table shows the frequency of the sickle cell anaemia genotype in a local population in Nigeria. What is the frequency of homozygous dominant individuals?

| Genotypes $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{SS and Ss}$ $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ss}$ $\hspace{1em}$ |
| :-- | :-: | :-: |
| **Frequency** $\hspace{1em}$ | 0.75 | 0.25 |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.5

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.75

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.25

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.05

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The Hardy-Weinberg formula is used to calculate genotype frequencies in a population and is summarised by: $\ce{p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1}$. Where $\ce{p^2}$ equals the frequency of dominant homozygous individuals, 2pq equals the frequency of heterozygous individuals, and $\ce{q^2}$ equals the frequency of homozygous recessive individuals. The general formula to represent the frequency of dominant and recessive alleles is p + q = 1, where p equals the frequency of dominant alleles and q equals the frequency of recessive alleles.

Therefore $\ce{q^2 = 0.25, so q = \sqrt{0.25} = 0.5}$\
p = 1 - 0.5 = 0.5\
The frequency of dominant homozygous individuals = $\ce{p^2 = 0.5^2 = 0.25}$

**Choice C** is correct.

**Choices A, B** and **D** are incorrect.
