# BI0238

Which is **not** an example of natural selection?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Elephants and Hippopotami have similar body types.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$    Peacocks with bright feathers are more likely to breed.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$    Giraffes with longer necks are less likely to starve as they can reach leaves higher up in trees.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$    Zebras that are born with white fur are more likely to be killed by predators.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Elephants and hippopotami having similar body types is not an example of adapting and surviving better in their environments. **Choice A** is correct.

**Choices B** and **C** are all examples of organisms that survive better in their environments, they represent a selective advantage, as they have favourable adaptations. **Choice D** is an example of how mutation (albinism) can be selected against, through predation.
