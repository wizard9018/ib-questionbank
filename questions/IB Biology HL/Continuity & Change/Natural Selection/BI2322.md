# BI2322

John Endler studied sexual selection and natural selection pressures using guppies (*Poecilia reticulata*). Which row contains his observations for colourful males?

| | Mating success | Survival in shallow water |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Low | Low |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Low | High |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | High | High |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | High | Low |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because colourful male guppies have a reproductive advantage due to sexual selection. Females preferentially select colourful males for reproduction, giving them high mating success. However, in shallow water, colourful males are more visible to predators, resulting in low survival due to increased predation.

**Choice A** is incorrect because colourful males have high, not low, mating success.

**Choice B** is incorrect because colourful males have low survival in shallow water, not high.

**Choice C** is incorrect because colourful males do not have high survival in shallow water.
