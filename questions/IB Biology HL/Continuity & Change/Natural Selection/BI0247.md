$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct, as the beak is curved and thin, adapted to suck nectar from a plant. **Choice A**  is incorrect as the beak is wide and the lower part protrudes; it is adapted for water surface skimming. **Choice B** is incorrect as the beak is thick and adapted to eating fruits and cracking the nuts. **Choice D** is incorrect, as its beak is too short and stunted and adapted to tear flesh.
