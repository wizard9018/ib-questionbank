# BI0713

Evolutionary fitness is a term used to describe adaptations.  Which of the following best describes the term evolutionary fitness?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ How effectively a phenotype attracts mates

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The probability that a phenotype will be found in the next generation

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ How well a phenotype is suited to an environment

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The underlying genotype is susceptible to mutations

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The evolutionary fitness of a phenotype is a measure for how many offspring will inherit the phenotype from their parents. (**Choice B**)

**Choice A** is incorrect because although the attractiveness of a phenotype improves the chances of mating, this by itself does not guarantee that offspring will inherit the trait.

**Choice C** is incorrect because although the suitability of the phenotype to the environment plays an important role in the survival of the individual, this by itself does not guarantee that the offspring will inherit the trait.

**Choice D** is incorrect because mutations in the genotype would change the phenotype and it would be a new trait. Fitness of a phenotype is the frequency of how much we see this trait in the offspring unchanged.
