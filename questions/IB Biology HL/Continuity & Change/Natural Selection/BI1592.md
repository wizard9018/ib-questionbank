# BI1592

HLA (human leukocyte antigen) genes help the immune system differentiate between pathogens and the body's own cells. The HLA A*02:01 allele has been recorded in over 300 populations and may be associated with an improved ability to clear the body of hepatitis C infections. The map shows the incidence of this allele across South and Central America.

![BI1592i](images/BI1592i.png)

Which of the following statements are supported by the data?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
 | | |
 | :-- | :-- |
 | I. | This allele may have originated in Peru. |
 | II. | A population from South America may have settled in Mexico. |
 | III. | Some Argentinians may be more resistant than Brazilians to hepatitis C infection. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because Peru has the largest frequency (0.5) for this allele, suggesting that it may have originated in this population (**Statement I**), although it doesn't exclude the possibility that it may have originated elsewhere. Mexico has a higher allele frequency than nearby countries to the south. This suggests that a population from further south may have migrated to and settled in Mexico at some point (**Statement II**), although other explanations are possible. Lastly, Brazil has a lower allele frequency than Argentina, which suggests that they may be more susceptible to hepatitis C infection (**Statement III**).

**Choices A, B,** and **C** are incorrect as they are missing one or more statements.
