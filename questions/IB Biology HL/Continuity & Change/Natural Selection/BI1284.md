# BI1284

Which of these is not a consequence of natural selection?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Evolution

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Changes in biodiversity

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Changes in allele frequencies

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Environmental change

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because environmental change contributes to evolution by natural selection, it is not a consequence.

**Choice A** is incorrect because natural selection leads to evolution. **Choice B** is incorrect because natural selection can increase biodiversity by the evolution of new species (e.g. adaptive radiation) or decrease biodiversity by the reduction of species (e.g. extinction). **Choice C** is incorrect because natural selection favours reproduction of individuals with suitable adaptations, which leads to changes in allele frequencies in populations.
