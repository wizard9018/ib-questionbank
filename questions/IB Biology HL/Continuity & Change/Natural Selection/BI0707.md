# BI0707

What is the correct definition of a gene pool?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ All the genes and alleles, in an interbreeding population.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ All the genes and proteins, in an interbreeding population.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ All the phenotypes and genotypes, in an interbreeding population.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ All the species present, in an interbreeding population.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choices B, C** and **D**  are incorrect, as the gene pool consists of all the genes and alleles in an interbreeding population.
