# BI0705

The graph shows the relationship between the neck length of adult Masai giraffes (*G. tippelskirchi*) and their mean lifespan. What type of selection is shown by the graph?

![BI0705QB](images/BI0705QB.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Disruptive selection

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Directional selection

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Stabilising selection

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ No selection

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
In directional selection, the population favours one extreme, as they are better adapted to the environment. Giraffes with longer necks have longer lifespans. **Choice B** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect, as disruptive selection would result in the extremes being favoured, i.e. giraffes with shorter and longer necks would have longer lifespans. **Choice C** is incorrect as stabilising selection would result in the intermediate neck lengths having the longest lifespans. **Choice D** is incorrect as long-necked giraffes are being ‘selected' for.
