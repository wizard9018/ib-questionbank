# BI0710

Which statement is true of punctuated equilibrium?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ It doesn’t account for intermediate evolutionary forms.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ It involves changes happening gradually over time.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ It is not supported by the fossil record.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ It has periods of rapid evolutionary changes.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The punctuated equilibrium is a theory that evolutionary development has long periods of stasis punctuated by short periods of rapid change (**Choice D**). **Choice A** is incorrect as punctuated equilibrium explains that long periods of stability are interrupted by periods of rapid evolution and so accounts for intermediate forms. **Choice B** is incorrect, as punctuated equilibrium causes evolution to happen rapidly. **Choice C** is incorrect, as punctuated equilibrium is supported by the fossil record.
