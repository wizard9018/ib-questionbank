# BI0244

Broad-leaved plants are often found near the ground in tropical rainforests. Which characteristic of the leaf is an example of a useful adaptation?

![BI0244](images/BI0244.png)
[Source: ysen. (2020, March 5). *Broad-leaved flat pea - free photo on Pixabay*. Pixabay. Retrieved May 26, 2022, from https://pixabay.com/photos/broad-leaved-green-leaf-4900105/.  Copyright free]

- A.    $\hspace{1em}$ Leaves are covered in a thin layer of wax, which allows for the absorption of more water.

- B.    $\hspace{1em}$ Leaves have a large surface area, allowing them to float on water.

- C.    $\hspace{1em}$ Leaves have curled edges to capture more rainwater.
- D.    $\hspace{1em}$ Leaves have a large surface area to absorb more sunlight.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because broad leaves provide a large surface area, increasing light capture in the shaded rainforest understorey.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the waxy cuticle mainly reduces water loss and protects the leaf; water uptake occurs primarily via roots, not leaf absorption.

 **Choice B** is incorrect because floating is irrelevant for ground plants in rainforests.

  **Choice C** is incorrect because curled edges are not a typical adaptation for understorey plants and would not primarily increase light absorption.
