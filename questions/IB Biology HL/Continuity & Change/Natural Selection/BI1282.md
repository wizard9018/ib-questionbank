# BI1282

Which of these are factors affecting evolution by natural selection?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Competition |
    | II. | Mutation |
    | III. | Overproduction of offspring |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is true as individuals within a population often compete with each other for resources, and those with advantageous traits are more likely to survive and reproduce. **Statement II** is true, as mutations that provide a selective advantage to a particular environment can become more common in future generations through natural selection.

**Statement III** is true as it leads to competition among offspring for limited resources, and the ones with traits that make them better suited to the environment are more likely to survive and pass on their genes to the next generation **Choice D** is therefore correct.

**Choice A, Choice B** and **Choice C** are incorrect because they do not include all three statements.
