# BI2395

Which of the following characteristics would not be subject to selective breeding by farmers?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Size of wheat kernels

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Muscle bulk in beef cattle

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Longevity of sheep

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Wool yield from alpacas

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because longevity, whilst a desirable characteristic (because a longer-living animal will, in theory, produce more wool in its lifetime) cannot be selected for. Selective breeding involves a farmer deciding which animals can be allowed to mate and which cannot, thereby concentrating the gene pool in favour of desirable characteristics. The only way a farmer will know of the longevity of an animal is to let it live its full life, at which point it will likely be too old to reproduce.

**Choice A** is incorrect because a large wheat kernel means more endosperm, which is the ‘cash crop' as it contains the starch-containing flour that will be milled from the crop.

**Choice B** is incorrect because muscle mass is important in beef herds; more muscle means more saleable meat per head of cattle. For this reason, a farmer may choose only the largest and most muscle-bound bulls and cows to reproduce.

**Choice D** is incorrect because alpaca wool is a valuable crop, and the animals that produce the greatest yield (mass of saleable wool per animal) will be selected for by a farmer.
