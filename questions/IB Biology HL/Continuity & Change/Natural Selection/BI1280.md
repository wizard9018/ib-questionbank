# BI1280

The photograph shows one species of bird of paradise, a family of birds renowned for their bright colours.

![bird of paradise](<images/bird of paradise.png>)
[Source: Ltshears. *Bird of Paradise 001.jpg*. Wikimedia. Retrieved, September 28th 2023, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Bird_Of_Paradise_001.jpg. Copyright under Public Domain]

What type of selection pressure likely acts on the colours of the feathers of a bird of paradise?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Abiotic factor

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Overproduction of offspring

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Sexual selection

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Interspecific competition

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because the feather colour of birds of paradise is a trait that is sexually selected because it affects mating success. **Choice A** is incorrect because abiotic factors are unlikely to affect feather colour. **Choice B** is incorrect because overproduction of offspring does not affect the feathers of birds of paradise. **Choice D** is incorrect because the feathers of birds of paradise have a role in mating rituals and will not be affected by interspecific competition, which is competition between different species.
