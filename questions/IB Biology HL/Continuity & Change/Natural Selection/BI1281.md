# BI1281

Which row is correct for inherited and acquired traits?

| | Inherited traits | Acquired traits |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Not encoded in genes | Encoded in genes |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Encoded in genes | Encoded in genes |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Encoded in genes | Not encoded in genes |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Not encoded in genes | Not encoded in genes |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because inherited characteristics are encoded in genes, and acquired traits are not encoded in genes; they are affected by environmental factors. **Choice A** and **Choice D** are incorrect because inherited traits are encoded in genes. **Choice B** is incorrect because acquired traits are not encoded in genes.
