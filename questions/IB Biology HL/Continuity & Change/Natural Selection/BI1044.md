# BI1044

**Maximum mark: 10**

Sickle cell anaemia is a trait that is inherited in a codominant manner. The graph shows the proportions of people who are homozygous affected (Hb$^S$Hb$^S$), homozygous unaffected (Hb$^A$Hb$^A$) and heterozygous (Hb$^A$Hb$^S$) for the trait over 50 years in Nigeria, West Africa. Malaria, which is a parasitic disease that infects red blood cells, is prevalent in Nigeria.

![BI1044](images/BI1044.png)

1. Using the graph, explain whether the sickle cell trait is likely to be in Hardy Weinberg equilibrium. [1 mark]

2.
 1. Describe the trends observed in the graph. [3 marks]

 2. Suggest a reason for the trend in heterozygotes found in this malaria infected region of Africa. [1 mark]

3. The frequency of the recessive allele in the population was found to be 0.62, calculate the frequency of the dominant allele in the population. [1 mark]

4.
 1. Assuming that this population is in Hardy Weinberg equilibrium, predict what would happen to the frequency of recessive alleles in the population over 50 more years. [1 mark]

 2. Outline a reason for your answer. [1 mark]

5. Explain why, under ‘real world’ conditions, it is difficult for organisms to be in Hardy Weinberg equilibrium. [2 marks]
