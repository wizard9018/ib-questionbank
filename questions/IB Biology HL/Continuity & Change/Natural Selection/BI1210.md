# BI1210

Which term related to natural selection is matched correctly to its definition?

| | Term | Definition |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Fitness | An organism's ability to pass its genetic material to its offspring |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Niche | The interactions between different species in an area |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Species | A group of organisms in an area that can interbreed |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Differential mortality | Differences in lifespan due to differences in gene expression |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
An organism well adapted for survival in its environment is said to be fit for its environment. If an organism has high fitness, it is more likely to survive, reproduce and pass on its genes to its offspring, passing on the beneficial characteristics; **Choice A**  is correct.

A niche is an area where individuals of the same species live; **Choice B** is incorrect. A species is a group of organisms that can interbreed to produce fertile offspring. **Choice C**  is incorrect as it is missing the last part of the definition.  Differential mortality refers to organisms that are less well adapted to their environment not surviving, whereas organisms that are better adapted are more likely to survive, breed and pass on their alleles to the next generation; **Choice D**  is incorrect.
