# BI0716

**Maximum mark: 6**

1. Outline the possible causes of changes to a gene pool.         [3 marks]

2. Suggest **one** possible cause of the gene pool getting smaller.  [1 mark]

3. Explain how a small gene pool could have a detrimental effect on a population.  [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$ a gene pool is all the genes **OR** alleles in an interbreeding population     [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ changes in allele frequency      [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ reproductive isolation **OR** temporal isolation **OR** behavioural isolation **OR** geographical isolation  [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ natural selection **OR** evolution     [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ accumulation of mutations      [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ crossing over during meiosis     [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ mixing with individuals from another population     [1 mark]
[3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    A gene pool is all the **genes in an interbreeding population** ($\checkmark$). Changes occur in a gene pool when the **allele frequency changes** ($\checkmark$), and this can be caused by **reproductive isolation** ($\checkmark$), **natural selection** ($\checkmark$) or **crossing over during meiosis** ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$ **MAY** be due to organisms with a specific allele which died before having offspring     [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ **MAY** be due to inbreeding         [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ **MIGHT** be due to a small population size         [1 mark]
[1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    **Inbreeding** ($\checkmark$) can lead to the gene pool getting smaller as no new alleles are coming in, and many homozygous recessive genotypes can **reduce survival rate** ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$ small gene pool has a limited variety of traits in the population     [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ reduces the ability of a species to respond to environmental changes **OR** reduces disease resistance [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ increased risk of extinction **OR** reduced population growth rate         [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    A small gene pool has a **limited variety of gene alleles** in the population ($\checkmark$). This can **reduce the ability** of a species to **adapt to environmental changes** ($\checkmark$) and so **increases** the **risk** of the species **becoming extinct** ($\checkmark$).
