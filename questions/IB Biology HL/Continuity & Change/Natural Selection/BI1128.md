# BI1128

In a population of 100 daisies, where white petals (W) are dominant to purple petals (w), 75 daisies are white, and 25 daisies are purple. What is the frequency of recessive alleles in the population?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.75

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.25

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.50

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.06

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Daisies with white petals have the genotype of either WW or Ww, and have a frequency of 75 out of 100, which is the equivalent of 0.75. Daisies with purple petals have the genotype of ww, and have a frequency of 25 out of 100, which is the equivalent of 0.25. Using the Hardy-Weinberg formula, the frequency of homozygous recessive flowers is represented by q:sup[2]. q:sup[2] = 0.25, therefore q = 0.5 (the square root of 0.25). **Choice C**  is correct.

**Choices A, B** and **D**  are incorrect.
