# BI1125

In a population of beetles that meets the conditions for Hardy Weinberg equilibrium, brown shells are recessive to black shells. If the frequency of homozygous brown beetles is 0.16, what is the frequency of dominant alleles in the population?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.4

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.84

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.6

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.16

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The Hardy-Weinberg equation is p:sup[2] + 2pq + q:sup[2] = 1, where q2 equals the frequency of homozygous recessive individuals in the population. Therefore, q:sup[2] = 0.16, the square root of  q:sup[2]  is 0.4. The general formula to represent the frequency of dominant and recessive alleles in a population is p + q = 1, where p represents the frequency of dominant alleles, and q represents the frequency of recessive alleles.  Therefore, p + 0.4 = 1, and p = 1 - 0.4 = 0.6. **Choice C**  is correct.

**Choices A, B** and **D**  are incorrect.
