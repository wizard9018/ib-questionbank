# BI1213

What is the role of microtubules during mitosis?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Align homologous chromosomes on the equator

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Supercoil DNA

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Move sister chromatids to opposite poles

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Separate daughter chromatids

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Microtubules are the proteins that form spindle fibres and move sister chromatids to opposite poles during mitosis; **Choice C**  is correct. **Choice A**  is incorrect because although microtubules are involved in aligning the pairs of sister chromatids at the cell's equator, they do not align as homologous pairs in mitosis - that occurs in meiosis 1. In mitosis a single line of pairs of sister chromatids, joined together at the centromere, are arranged along the equator of the cell by microtubules. **Choice B**  is incorrect because microtubules are not involved in this process. **Choice D**  is incorrect because microtubules separate sister chromatids, not daughter chromatids.
