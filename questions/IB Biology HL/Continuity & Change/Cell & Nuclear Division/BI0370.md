## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Crossing-over typically occurs at points called chiasmata, where two non-sister chromatids overlap and exchange genetic material. The diagram above shows three distinct crossing-over points where the chromatids intersect. These points represent potential sites of genetic recombination during meiosis;**Choice B** is the correct answer.

**Choices A, C** and **D**  are incorrect.
