# BI0240

How does meiosis result in variation?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ New combinations of gametes fuse together.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ New combinations of alleles are produced in body cells.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ New combinations of alleles are produced in gametes.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ New combinations of alleles are produced by base substitution.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because meiosis produces new combinations of alleles in gametes via random orientation of bivalents (independent assortment) and crossing over.

**Choice A** is incorrect because it describes fertilisation (mixing of gametes), not how meiosis creates new allele combinations.

**Choice B** is incorrect because meiosis produces gametes, not new allele combinations in body (somatic) cells.

**Choice D** is incorrect because base substitution is a mutation, not a feature of meiosis (mutation is a separate source of genetic variation).
