# BI0033

Which word describes the formation of secondary tumours from primary tumours as a result of the movement of cells?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Malignant

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Carcinoma

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Benign

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Metastasis

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Term “malignant” (**Choice A**) describes cancerous tumours which can metastasize. “Carcinoma” (**Choice B**)  is another word for a malignant tumour. The term “benign” (**Choice C**) describes a tumour that is unlikely to cause significant harm to the body.
