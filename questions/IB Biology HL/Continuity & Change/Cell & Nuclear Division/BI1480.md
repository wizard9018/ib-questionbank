## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

A haploid nucleus contains a single set of chromosomes and has half the number of chromosomes of a diploid nucleus. The diploid number for horse threadworms is four, therefore the haploid number is two. **Choice A** is correct.

**Choices B, C** and **D** are incorrect as they do not contain exactly two different chromosomes.
