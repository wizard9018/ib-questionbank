# BI0205

Which stage of meiosis involves the separation of homologous chromosomes?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Metaphase I

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$  Anaphase I
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$  Metaphase II
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$  Anaphase II

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Homologous chromosomes are separated during meiosis 1, specifically during anaphase I (**Choice B**).  In metaphase I, homologous chromosomes line up on the equator (**Choice A**). In metaphase II, individual chromosomes line up on the equator (**Choice C**). In anaphase II, sister chromatids (copies of the chromosome) are separated (**Choice D**). **Choice B** is therefore correct.
