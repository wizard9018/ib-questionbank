# BI1560

Cyclins are a class of proteins that control the cell cycle by binding with and activating cyclin-dependent kinases (CDKs). Their concentrations vary throughout the cell cycle, as seen in the graph.

![BI1560ii](images/BI1560ii.png)

Deduce which event is initiated by the increased concentration of cyclin E?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Condensation of homologous chromosomes

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Duplication of organelles

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ DNA replication

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Appearance of spindle fibres

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because the concentration of cyclin E peaks during the transition between the G1 and S phases of the cell cycle. Cyclin E helps initiate DNA replication by phosphorylating target proteins involved in DNA synthesis.

**Choices A** and **D** are incorrect because the chromosomes do not condense and the spindle fibres do not appear until prophase of mitosis. **Choice B** is incorrect because the organelles are copied during the G1 and G2 phases of the cell cycle which are controlled by cyclins A and D, not cyclin E.
