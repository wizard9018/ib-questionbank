# BI1400

Which of the following correctly describes events occurring in meiosis I and meiosis II?

| | Meiosis I | Meiosis II |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | DNA replication | Crossing over |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Independent assortment of chromosomes | Separation of recombinant sister chromatids |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Crossing over | Independent assortment of chromosomes |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Random orientation of sister chromatids | Cytokinesis |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because the independent assortment of homologous chromosomes during meiosis I refers to the process by which homologous chromosome pairs align independently at the metaphase plate randomly and independently of each other. This random assortment results in the mixing of maternal and paternal chromosomes, leading to genetically unique daughter cells. Separation of recombinant sister chromatids occurs along the metaphase plate during metaphase II.  The sister chromatids, now possibly carrying different genetic information due to crossing over in meiosis I (recombinants), are pulled apart towards opposite poles of the cell.

DNA replication occurs during the S phase of interphase, not during meiosis. Crossing over occurs during meiosis I, not meiosis II. **Choice A** is, therefore, incorrect. **Choice C** is incorrect because the independent assortment of chromosomes only takes place during meiosis I. **Choice D** is incorrect because it is the random orientation of homologous pairs that takes place during meiosis I, and cytokinesis is not part of meiosis, it occurs straight after meiosis I and II.
