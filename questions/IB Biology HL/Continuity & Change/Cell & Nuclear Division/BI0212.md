4. Any **four** of the following (maximum of **three** marks awarded for **only** similarities or differences provided in the answer):

 $\ce{ -> }$ both meiosis **AND** mitosis involve prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ both meiosis **AND** mitosis are followed by cytokinesis [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ both meiosis **AND** mitosis are preceded by DNA replication [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ mitosis involves one cycle of prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase, **BUT** meiosis involves two cycles of prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ mitosis produces two cells, **BUT** meiosis produces four cells [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ mitosis produces diploid cells, **BUT** meiosis produces haploid cells [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ mitosis produces genetically identical cells **BUT** meiosis produces genetically distinct cells [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ mitosis is involved in growth and repair **OR** asexual reproduction **WHEREAS** meiosis is involved in sexual reproduction **OR** gamete formation. [1 mark]

 [4 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
 **Both** meiosis and mitosis involve **prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase** ($\checkmark$). In addition, **before both** meiosis and mitosis **DNA is replicated** ($\checkmark$), and **after both cytokinesis** occurs ($\checkmark$).

 Meiosis and mitosis are different in the following ways:

 - - meiosis produces **genetically different cells**, whereas mitosis produces **genetically identical cells**. ($\checkmark$)

 - - meiosis produces **haploid cells**, while mitosis produces **diploid cells**. ($\checkmark$)
 - - meiosis produces gametes (sperm and eggs) for **sexual reproduction**, while mitosis is used to produce new cells for **growth and repair**.($\checkmark$)
 - - meiosis produces **four cells**, while mitosis produces **two cells**.($\checkmark$)
 - - **meiosis has two cycles of prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase**. In mitosis, these stages only happen **once**. ($\checkmark$)
