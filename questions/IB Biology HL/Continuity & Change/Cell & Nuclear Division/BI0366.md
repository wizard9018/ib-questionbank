# BI0366

How does meiosis promote variation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Crossing over of sister chromatids

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Independent assortment during telophase I

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Crossing over during prophase I

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Fusion of gametes during sexual reproduction

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Crossing over occurs during prophase I. (**Choice C**)

**Choice A**  is incorrect as non-sister chromatids on homologous chromosomes cross over. **Choice B**  is incorrect as independent assortment occurs as a result of random orientation during metaphases I and II. **Choice D** is incorrect as the fusion of gametes does not occur during meiosis but during the fertilization.
