# BI2342

Which of these is an example of unequal cytokinesis?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Mitosis in root tips

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Spermatogenesis in testes

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Binary fission in bacteria

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Budding in yeast

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because during budding in yeast, the cytoplasm is not divided equally between the parent cell and the bud. The bud receives less cytoplasm than the parent cell, making it an example of unequal cytokinesis.

**Choice A** is incorrect because mitosis in root tips produces many small cells that are approximately equal in size, with equal cytoplasmic division.

**Choice B** is incorrect because spermatogenesis in the testes produces sperm cells that are equal in size and have nearly identical cytoplasmic volumes.

**Choice C** is incorrect because binary fission in bacteria divides the cytoplasm equally to produce two daughter cells with approximately the same volume of cytoplasm.
