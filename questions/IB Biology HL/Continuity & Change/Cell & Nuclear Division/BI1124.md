# BI1124

**Maximum mark: 7**

The graph shows the relationship between parental age and incidence of Down syndrome in offspring. The study was conducted using chorionic villus sampling (CVS).

1. Describe the pattern between paternal age and incidence of Down syndrome.  [2 marks]

2. A researcher concluded that increasing maternal age has a greater effect on the incidence of Down syndrome in offspring than increasing paternal age. Comment, giving a reason, whether you agree with this conclusion. [1 mark]

3. Using karyotyping, it is possible to test whether an unborn child has Down syndrome. State how a karyogram would identify a child with Down syndrome.   [1 mark]

4. Explain how the production of ova by meiosis can lead to Down syndrome. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ between 16 - 35 years, the chances of offspring having Down syndrome increases    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ after 35 years, the chances of offspring having Down syndrome decrease [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ between 35 - 50 years, the chances of offspring having Down syndrome stabilises    [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Between the ages of **16 -35**, the chances of offspring having Down syndrome **increases** ($\checkmark$) from 1 to 31%, however, **after 35 years**, the chances of having offspring with Down syndrome **decrease** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **one** of the following (answer must include a reason to gain the mark):

    $\ce{ -> }$ yes **BECAUSE** there is positive relationship between increasing maternal age and risk of Down syndrome            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ no **BECAUSE** up until the age of 35, increasing paternal age is correlated with a much higher risk of Down syndrome **OR** data might suggest trend but without statistical testing cannot come to this conclusion                [1 mark]

    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Yes, because as maternal age increases, there is also a consistent increase in the incidence of Down syndrome in offspring, whereas there is only an increase in incidence with paternal age up to the age of 35 ($\checkmark$).

3. any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ 3 chromosome 21s **OR** trisomy 21    [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    If the unborn child had Down syndrome, **three chromosome 21s** ($\checkmark$) would be visible in the karyogram.

4. any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ spindle apparatus errors    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ non-disjunction        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ homologous chromosomes do not separate during anaphase I                             [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ sister chromatids do not separate during anaphase II            [1 mark]

     $\ce{ -> }$ results in gametes OR sex cells OR ova with an extra chromosome **OR** abnormal number of chromosomes     [1 mark]

    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    **Spindle apparatus errors** ($\checkmark$) can lead to **non-disjunction** ($\checkmark$) of **homologous chromosomes** during **anaphase I** ($\checkmark$), or of **sister chromatids** during **anaphase II** ($\checkmark$).
