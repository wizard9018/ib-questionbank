# BI1326

Which is the correct definition of metastasis?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ A tumour that is formed when cancerous cells detach from a primary tumour

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ An aggressive tumour that grows rapidly, invades and destroys nearby tissues

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ A cell that undergoes uncontrolled cell division due to cell cycle errors

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The process by which cancer cells spread to other parts of the body

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

Metastasis is the process by which cells break off from a primary tumour and move to other parts of the body where they can set up secondary tumours; **Choice D**  is correct.

Metastasis is a process, all the other choices are descriptions of different tumours. A tumour that is formed when cancerous cells detach from a primary tumour is called a secondary tumour; **Choice A**  is incorrect. An aggressive tumour that grows rapidly, invades and destroys nearby tissues is called a malignant tumour; **Choice B** is incorrect. A cell that undergoes uncontrolled cell division due to cell cycle errors is the general description of a cancerous cell; **Choice C**  is incorrect.
