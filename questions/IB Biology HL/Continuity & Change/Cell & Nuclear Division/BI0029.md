# BI0029

The table shows the number of cells in different stages of the cell cycle in four samples of lung tissue from four patients. Which tissue has the lowest mitotic index?

|| **Interphase** | **Prophase** | **Metaphase** | **Anaphase** | **Telophase** | **Total** |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.  | 80 | 30| 4 | 5 | 1 | 120 |
| B.  | 100 | 9 | 1 | 7 | 8 | 125 |
| C.  | 30 | 15 | 5 | 10 | 0 | 60 |
| D.  | 70 | 20 | 4 | 0 | 6 | 100 |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The mitotic index is calculated by adding together all the cells undergoing mitosis (the cells in prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase) and dividing that value by the total number of cells.

For **Choice A**, the mitotic index is 0.3, ((30 + 4+ 5+ 1)/120).

For **Choice B**, the mitotic index is 0.2, ((9 + 1+ 7 + 8)/125).

For **Choice C**, the mitotic index is 0.5, ((15 + 5 + 10 + 0)/60).

For **Choice D**, the mitotic index is 0.3, ((20 + 4+ 0 + 6)/100).
