# BI2614

Plant meristem cells are typically smaller than mature plant cells. A ball of meristem cells on a plant shoot was measured to be 1.50 mm in radius. The meristem cells within the ball were determined microscopically to have a mean diameter of 5.0 $\mu$m. If all of the available space in the meristematic ball is occupied by cells, how many cells were present?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ $3 \times 10^2$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ $6 \times 10^2$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ $2.7 \times 10^7$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ $2.16 \times 10^8$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because:

**Calculation Method 1**

*Convert both to the same linear measurement of length:*
Ball radius $\times$ 2 = ball diameter
1.5 mm $\times$ 2 = 3 mm diameter

*Convert mm to $\mu$m so both structures are expressed in the same units:*\
Ball diameter = 3 mm $\times$ 1000 = 3000 $\mu$m\
Cell diameter = 5 $\mu$m

*Calculate the linear scale factor between the ball and an individual cell:*\
Linear scale factor =  $\dfrac{\ce{3000 \mu m}}{\ce{5 \mu m}}$ = 600 x

*Area scale factor = (linear scale factor)$^2$*\
*Volume scale factor = (linear scale factor)$^3$*

*Because there is no unoccupied space in the ball, we can calculate the number of cells in the whole ball by converting this linear scale factor to a volume scale factor by raising it to the power of 3*\
$600^3$ = 216 000 000 cells

*Convert to standard form:*

216 000 000 = $2.16 \times 10^8$

**Calculation Method 2**

Calculate the volumes of the ball of cells and of a single cell using the formula for the volume of a sphere, $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$\
*Convert both radii to the same linear measurement of length ($\mu$m):*\
Ball radius = 1.5 mm $\times$ 1000 = 1500 $\mu$m\
Cell radius = $\dfrac{\ce{Cell diameter}}{2}$ = $\dfrac{\ce{5 \mu m}}{2}$ = 2.5 $\mu$m

*Because we are dividing the volume of the ball by the volume of one cell to calculate the number of cells present, we can cancel the $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi$ term from top and bottom and be left with the division:*

Number of cells in the ball = $\dfrac{\ce{volume of ball}}{\ce{volume of one cell}} = \dfrac{\ce{\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3 (ball)}}{\ce{\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3 (cell)}}$

Cancel the $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi$ term from the numerator and denominator:

$\dfrac{\ce{\cancel{\dfrac{4}{3}\pi} r^3 (ball)}}{\ce{\cancel{\dfrac{4}{3}\pi} r^3 (cell)}} = \dfrac{1500^3}{2.5^3} = \dfrac{3.375 \times 10^9}{15.625}$ = 216 000 000

*In standard form:*

216 000 000 = 2.16 $\times 10^8$

**Choices A, B** and **C** are incorrect because they all contain calculation errors.
