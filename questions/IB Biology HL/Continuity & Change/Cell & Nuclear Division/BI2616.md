# BI2616

Human skin cells are replaced frequently; therefore, they undergo regular cell cycles. \
Which row correctly identifies a phase of a skin cell's cycle and the events that take place within that phase?

| | Phase | Event |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\ce{G_1}$ | The cell remains the same size |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | S | DNA replication and transcription |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\ce{G_2}$ | Synthesis of proteins necessary for meiosis |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Cytokinesis | Chromosomes move to the poles of the cell |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because the S phase stands for ‘synthesis' (of DNA, by replication). Transcription also occurs during the S phase, although is carefully regulated in order to prevent DNA that is being replicated from being transcribed at the same time, which may cause errors in either process. \
**Choice A** is incorrect because, during $\ce{G_1}$, the cell increases in size as do some of the organelles within it.\
**Choice C** is incorrect because, during $\ce{G_2}$, the proteins necessary for mitosis (not meiosis) are synthesised. This is a skin cell, which cannot produce gametes, so does not need to perform meiotic division. \
**Choice D** is incorrect because cytokinesis occurs when a membrane forms between two halves of a cell, causing two daughter cells to be produced. ‘Chromosomes move to the poles of the cell' describes an event of anaphase (part of mitosis).
