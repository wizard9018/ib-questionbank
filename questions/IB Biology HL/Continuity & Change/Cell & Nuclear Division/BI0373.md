# BI0373

Which process does not occur during meiosis?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Crossing over

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Formation of two identical daughter cells

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Random orientation

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Separation of homologous chromosomes

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because meiosis results in four genetically unique haploid cells, not two identical ones. The formation of two genetically identical daughter cells is the defining characteristic of mitosis, which is used for growth and tissue repair rather than gamete production.

**Choice A** is incorrect because crossing over occurs during Prophase I of meiosis, where non-sister chromatids exchange genetic material to increase variation.

**Choice C** is incorrect because random orientation occurs during Metaphase I and II, ensuring that maternal and paternal chromosomes are distributed independently to the daughter cells.

**Choice D** is incorrect because the separation of homologous chromosomes is the primary event of Anaphase I, which reduces the chromosome number from diploid to haploid.
