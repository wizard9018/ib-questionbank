# BI1540

The diagram below shows the concentrations of different cyclins during the cell cycle.  Deduce which cyclin is involved in preparing for DNA replication?

![cyclins graph i](<images/cyclins graph i.png>)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Cyclin D

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Cyclin E

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Cyclin A

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Cyclin B

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
From the graph it is possible to work out that **Choice B** is correct. This is because levels of Cyclin E peak at the beginning of the S phase, which is when the DNA is replicated.

**Choice A** is incorrect because Cyclin D is high throughout mitosis, triggering the movement of cells from G${_0}$ to G${_1}$ and to the S phase.

**Choice C** is incorrect because cyclin A peaks in the G${_2}$ phase after the DNA has been replicated.

**Choice D**  is incorrect as Cyclin B peaks at the beginning of mitosis, and DNA replication has already happened by then.
