# BI0207

Which of the following statements is/are correct for meiosis?

- I. $\hspace{1em}$ It is a reduction division
- II.  $\hspace{.7em}$ It involves random orientation
- III. $\hspace{.4em}$ It produces genetically identical cells

- A.    $\hspace{1em}$  I only

- B.    $\hspace{1em}$  II only
- C.     $\hspace{1em}$  I and II
- D.    $\hspace{1em}$  I and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Meiosis reduces the number of chromosomes by half (**Statement I**), and random orientation of chromosomes along the equator occurs during metaphase I and II (**Statement II**), so **choice C** is correct because both statements are correct.

Meiosis produces genetically different cells, so **Statement III** is not true, making **Choice D** incorrect. **Choices A** and **B** are both missing one of the true statements.
