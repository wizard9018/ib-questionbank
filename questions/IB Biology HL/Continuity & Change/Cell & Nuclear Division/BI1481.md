# BI1481

The diagram shows a process that occurs during prophase I of meiosis. What does this process directly result in?

![BI1481 fig1](<images/BI1481 fig1.png>)

[Adapted from: Source Images, I. A. B. (2015, August 23). Image from page 172 of "cytology" (1961).  Flickr. Retrieved June 5, 2022, from https://www.flickr.com/photos/internetarchivebookimages/20640324138. Copyright free]

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Exchange of genes between sister chromatids

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Exchange of telomeres between non-homologous chromosomes

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Exchange of centromeres between non-sister chromatids

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Exchange of alleles between homologous chromosomes

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

The diagram shows the process of crossing over. Homologous chromosomes are chromosomes with the same sequence of genes but different alleles. Crossing over results in the exchange of alleles between homologous chromosomes. **Choice D**  is correct.

Sister chromatids are found on the same chromosome and are connected by a centromere. Crossing over does not occur between sister chromatids; **Choice A**  is incorrect. Crossing over does not occur between non homologous chromosomes; **Choice B**  is incorrect. Centromeres are what hold sister chromatids together, but they are not involved in crossing over; **Choice C**  is incorrect.
