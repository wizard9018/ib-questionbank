# BI1327

The micrograph shows onion cells undergoing mitosis. What is the mitotic index of the cells?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: 

![BI1327 answer](<images/BI1327 answer.jpg>)

[Source: Adapted from Laurent, E. (2022, July 15). File:onion diagram.svg. Wikimedia Commons. Retrieved November 11, 2023 from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Onion_diagram.svg. Copyright under CC BY-SA 4.0]

The mitotic index is calculated by adding together all the cells undergoing mitosis (the cells in prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase) and dividing that value by the total number of cells. There are three cells undergoing mitosis and twelve cells in total, therefore, the mitotic index is 3 $\div$ 12 $=$ 0.25. **Choice A  is correct**.

**Choices B**, **C** and **D**  are incorrect.
