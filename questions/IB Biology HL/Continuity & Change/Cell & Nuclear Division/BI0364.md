# BI0364

Which statement(s) are true of meiosis I but **not** meiosis II?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    |  I. | Crossing over occurs during prophase |
    | II. | Sister chromatids are pulled apart during anaphase |
    | III. | Chromosomes supercoil and become visible during prophase |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Crossing over occurs between homologous pairs during prophase I, but not during prophase II, as homologous chromosomes have already been separated during meiosis 1. **Choice A** is correct.

**Statement II** is incorrect as sister chromatids are pulled apart during anaphase II (**Choices B, C** and **D**). **Statement III** is incorrect, as chromosomes supercoil and become visible during prophase I and II (**Choice D**).
