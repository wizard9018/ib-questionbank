# BI0354

Which process does **not** happen in the shoot apex?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Meiosis

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Mitosis

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Cell elongation

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Primary growth

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because meiosis happens in the anther and ovule of flowers, it does not happen in the cells of the shoot apex. **Choice B**, **Choice C** and **Choice D** are incorrect because mitosis and cell elongation happen in the shoot apex to produce new cells and allow for primary growth (plant lengthening).
