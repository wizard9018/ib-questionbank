# BI2619

The table shows the concentrations of three types of cyclin (X, Y and Z) at three different stages of cell proliferation.

![BI2619](images/BI2619.png)

Which hypothesis is supported by the data in the table?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Cyclin X maintains the undifferentiated state of stem cells.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Cyclin Y inhibits the process of cell differentiation.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Cyclin Z is required for the maintenance of fully differentiated cells.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Cyclin concentrations remain constant regardless of the stage of differentiation.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct  because Cyclin X is at its highest concentration (18 units) in totipotent stem cells and decreases as cells become more specialised, reaching 0 in fully differentiated cells. This strongly suggests that Cyclin X plays a role in maintaining an undifferentiated state.

**Choice B** is incorrect because Cyclin Y concentration is at its highest (11 units) in fully differentiated cells. This suggests that Cyclin Y promotes or maintains the differentiated state, rather than inhibiting it.

**Choice C** is incorrect because Cyclin Z levels drop significantly (from 15 to 1 unit) once the cell becomes fully differentiated. This suggests Cyclin Z is more likely involved in the transition between stem cell stages (proliferation) rather than the maintenance of differentiated cells.

**Choice D** is incorrect because the table shows significant fluctuations in the levels of all three cyclins (X, Y, and Z) as the cell changes state.
