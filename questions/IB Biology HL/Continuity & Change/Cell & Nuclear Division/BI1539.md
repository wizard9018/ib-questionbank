# BI1539

Below is a diagram representing the cell cycle. At which point A-D does CDK combine with cyclins to form MPF?

![Cell cycle labelled A-Di](<images/Cell cycle labelled A-Di.png>)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
As shown in the diagram, the cell cycle includes two primary stages, interphase and mitosis. Interphase is divided into three phases: the G${_1}$, S, and G${_2}$ phases. There are three main checkpoints: G${_1}$, G${_2}$ and M. At these checkpoints specific signals are required for the cell to proceed to the next stage. For the G${_2}$ checkpoint to be passed, cyclins must combine with CDK (cyclin-dependent kinase) molecules to form the mitosis-promoting factor (MPF). Once MPF is active, the cell can proceed from G${_2}$ to mitosis. **Choice A** is therefore the correct answer.

![cell cyle with G1 G2 and S phase marked on i](<images/cell cyle with G1 G2 and S phase marked on i.png>)

**Choice B** indicates the end of mitosis when all the MPF will have been broken down. **Choice C** indicates the G${_1}$ checkpoint before the cell goes into the S phase. **Choice D** indicates the end of the S phase and the beginning of the G${_2}$ phase.
