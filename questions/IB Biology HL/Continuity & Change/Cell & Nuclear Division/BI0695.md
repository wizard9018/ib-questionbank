# BI0695

Which statement(s) about homologous chromosomes is/are **not** true?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ Homologous chromosomes have the same centromere position.
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Homologous chromosomes have genes with the same loci.
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Homologous chromosomes are identical to each other.

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ II only
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ III only
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement III**  is not true, as homologous chromosomes often have different alleles (forms of genes), so are generally not identical to each other.

**Statements I** and **II** are true, as homologous chromosomes are pairs of chromosomes that have their centromeres and genes at the same positions. Therefore, **Choice C** is correct.
