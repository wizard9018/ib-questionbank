# BI0351

The photomicrograph below shows a root tip meristem. Which of the following sentences correctly defines a meristem?

![BI0351](images/BI0351.png)
[Source: Berkshire Community College Bioscience Image Library (2014 February 10th) *Apical meristem in onion root tip (34886438370 .jpeg*. Wikimedia commons. Retrieved August 15th 2022 from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apical_Meristem_in_Onion_Root_Tip_(34886438470).jpg. Copyright under CC0]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ A region of a plant with differentiated cells that undergo cell division

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ A region of a plant with undifferentiated cells that undergo cell division

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ A region of a flower that undergoes cell division

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ A region of a plant where cell differentiation occurs in response to a change in day length

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because a meristem is a part of a plant with undifferentiated cells that undergo cell division. **Choice A** is incorrect because meristems do not have differentiated cells that undergo cell division. **Choice C** is incorrect because a meristem is not defined as a region of a flower. **Choice D** is incorrect because cell division in a meristem is not influenced by day length.
