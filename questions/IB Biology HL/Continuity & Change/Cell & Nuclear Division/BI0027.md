# BI0027

Which statement(s) about mitosis is/are true?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Sister chromatids are pulled apart during metaphase. |
  | II. | The nuclear envelope reforms during telophase. |
  | III. | Chromosomes supercoil and become visible during prophase. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The four stages of mitosis are prophase, where chromosomes supercoil and become visible (**Statement III**); Metaphase, where chromosomes line up at the equator of the cell; anaphase, where sister chromatids are pulled apart and telophase, where the nuclear envelope reforms (**Statement II**) and two new daughter nuclei are formed. **Choice C** is, therefore, the correct answer.

**Statement I** is not true as sister chromatids are not pulled apart during metaphase.  They are pulled apart during anaphase.
**Choices A** and **D** are incorrect as they include the untrue **Statement I**. **Choice B** is incorrect as it is missing **Statement III**.
