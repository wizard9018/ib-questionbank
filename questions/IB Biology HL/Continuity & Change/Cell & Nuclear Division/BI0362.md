# BI0362

**Maximum mark: 5**

The diagram represents a bud with a shoot apical meristem.

![BIO362](images/BIO362.png)

1. State the type of cell division that occurs in the shoot apical meristem. [1 mark]

2. Outline what happens to the cells produced in the shoot apical meristem. [2 marks]

3. State two types of tissue that are produced by the differentiation of cells found at the shoot apical meristem [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{->}$ mitosis [1 mark]
[1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\
$\hspace{1em}$ **Mitosis** ($\checkmark$) is the type of cell division which occurs in the stem tip.

2. Any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{->}$ new cells can differentiate [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ new cells can elongate [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ some cells remain undifferentiated **OR** remain as apical meristem cells [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ new cells become new tissues **OR** new cells differentiate to produce leaves **OR** dermal tissue **OR** vascular tissue [1 mark]
[2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\
 The cells produced by cell division in the apical meristem can **differentiate** ($\checkmark$) and become **dermal tissue** ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{->}$ dermal tissue [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ vascular tissue **OR** phloem **OR** xylem [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ pith [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ cortex [1 mark]
[2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\
 The new tissues produced by differentiation include **dermal tissue** ($\checkmark$) and **vascular tissue** ($\checkmark$).
