# BI0084

This table is a short section of the output from a DNA sequence database comparing the cytochrome C gene sequence in two species. What does it show?

![Picture 1](<images/Picture 1.png>)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ These sequences are for two different genes

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ There are two differences in the base sequence between the two species

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ There are three differences in the base sequence between the two species

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ There are four differences in the base sequence between the two species

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because the base sequences differ at two positions:

- Position 3: A in the top sequence vs T in the bottom
- Position 6: T in the top sequence vs A in the bottom

This indicates two nucleotide differences between the two sequences.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the question specifies these are from the same region of the cytochrome C gene. These are homologous sequences; the high degree of similarity (8 out of 10 bases are identical) confirms they are variations of the same gene in different species, likely resulting from a relatively recent common ancestor.

.**Choice C** is incorrect because the sequences differ at two positions, not three.

**Choice D** is incorrect because the sequences differ at two positions, not four.
