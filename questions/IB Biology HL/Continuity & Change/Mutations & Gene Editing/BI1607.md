# BI1607

At which stage of the cell cycle are mutations most likely to happen?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ S phase

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Anaphase I

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Prophase

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Metaphase II

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

Mutations are more likely to occur during DNA replication in both somatic cells and gametes. However, only mutations in gametes can be inherited and potentially have consequences for future generations. DNA replication occurs during the S phase of interphase, making this the stage where mutations are most likely to occur; **Choice A**  is correct.

**Choice B** is incorrect as homologous chromosomes are pulled apart during anaphase I, but individual DNA bases are unaffected. Meiosis is also not usually considered part of the cell cycle as it is a one-way reduction division that produces gametes. **Choice C**  is incorrect as during prophase of mitosis, the chromosomes condense, but individual DNA bases are unaffected.  **Choice D**  is incorrect as, firstly, metaphase II is a stage of meiosis. During metaphase II, chromosomes align along the equator of the cell, but again, the individual DNA bases are unaffected.
