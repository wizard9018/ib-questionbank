# BI1218

What feature do knockout organisms have in common?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ They are all mammals

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ They have one non-functional gene

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ They express the same genes as humans

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ They have the same phenotype as non-knockout organisms

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Gene knockout is a technique that produces a genetically engineered organism with one non-functional gene; **Choice B**  is correct.

Knockout organisms are not all mammals; **Choice A** is incorrect. Not all knockout organisms express the same genes as humans; **Choice C** is incorrect. Knockout organisms sometimes have the same phenotype as non-knockout organisms, but not always; **Choice D** is incorrect.
