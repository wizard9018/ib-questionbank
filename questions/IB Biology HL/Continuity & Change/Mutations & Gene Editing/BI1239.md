# BI1239

What is the purpose of gene knockout techniques?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Overexpress a gene to study its function

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Make a gene non-functional to study its function

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Lower the expression of a gene to study its function

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Sequence genes to understand how they work

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct, as the purpose of gene knockout techniques is to make genes inoperative so that their function can be studied. **Choice A**  is incorrect, as gene knockout techniques do not increase the expression of their target genes. **Choice C** is incorrect as gene knockout techniques aim to completely stop the expression of a gene, not just lower it. **Choice D** is incorrect, as gene knockout techniques are not used to sequence genes.
