# BI0881

The DNA sequence shown has been taken from exon 1 of DNA within the human insulin gene. Which sequence corresponds to a deletion mutation in the sequence?

![BI0881A](images/BI0881A.png)
[Source: Adapted from NCBI]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ TACAAGGACAAGCTGCAT

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ TACAAGAGACAAGCTGCA

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ TACAAGGAAAGCTGCATC

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ TACAAGGACAAGCGGCAT

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C**  is correct, as it shows a deletion mutation where the 9th nucleotide (C) from the original sequence has been deleted. This causes a frameshift, altering all downstream codons.

**Choice A** is incorrect because it is identical to the original sequence, so no mutation has occurred.

**Choice B** is incorrect because it represents an insertion mutation where an additional adenine has been inserted at the 7th position, shifting the reading frame.

**Choice D** is incorrect because it shows a base substitution where a G has replaced the T at the 14th position, altering one codon but not shifting the frame.
