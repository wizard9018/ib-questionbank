# BI0883

What is a possible cause of a genetic mutation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ An organism’s need to adapt to the environment

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Random errors during DNA replication

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Physical damage to body tissue

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Random errors during transcription

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B**  is correct, as mutations are random changes to the DNA sequence and many of them occur during DNA replication.

**Choice A**  is incorrect as mutations are random and not controlled by organisms. **Choice C**  is incorrect as physical damage does not affect the genome. **Choice D**  is incorrect as errors during transcription will affect mRNA not DNA (the genome).
