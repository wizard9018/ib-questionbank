# BI1217

Which is a possible feature of a conserved sequence?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Does not have a similar sequence in multiple organisms

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ High mutation rate

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Any changes to the sequence are likely to have no effect on the organism

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Non-coding sequence that regulates the expression of other genes

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
A conserved sequence is a DNA sequence that has remained relatively unchanged over long periods of evolution, often because the sequence has an important function for life. Conserved sequences can be coding or non-coding, and when they are non-coding often have a regulatory role in gene expression; **Choice D** is correct.

Conserved sequences have a similar sequence in multiple organisms; **Choice A** is incorrect. Conserved sequences have slower rates of mutation compared to the background mutation rate; **Choice B** is incorrect. Conserved sequences are maintained by natural selection, due to their functional role, therefore, any changes to their sequence are not conducive to life; **Choice C** is incorrect.
