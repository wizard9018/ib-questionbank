# BI0245

In species that do **not** carry out sexual reproduction, what is a possible cause of variation?

- A.    $\hspace{1em}$ Meiosis

- B.    $\hspace{1em}$ Mitosis
- C.    $\hspace{1em}$ Mutation
- D.    $\hspace{1em}$ Growth

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Mutation (**Choice C**)  is correct as random changes to the DNA sequence can result in variation. **Choice A** is incorrect as meiosis does not occur in organisms that do not carry out sexual reproduction. **Choice B** is incorrect as mitosis produces genetically identical cells. **Choice D** is incorrect as growth does not result in genetic variation.
