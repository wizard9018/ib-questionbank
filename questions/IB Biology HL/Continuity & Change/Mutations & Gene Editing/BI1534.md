# BI1534

**Maximum mark: 1**

Which is **not** a risk of using CRISPR technology in humans?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ CRISPR-Cas9 may be recognised as foreign causing an adverse immune reaction

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ The incorrect DNA sequence may be removed by the Cas-9 enzyme

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Whole cells may be removed from the body, resulting in tissue breakdown

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Off-target effects may affect other functional genes

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

CRISPR technology targets DNA sequences, not whole cells; **Choice C** is correct.

CRISPR-Cas9 may be recognised as foreign causing an adverse immune reaction; this is a risk of using CRISPR technology in humans, and therefore **Choice A**  is not the correct answer to this question. If the incorrect DNA sequence is removed by the Cas-9 enzyme, this may cause unanticipated effects; this is a risk of using CRISPR technology in humans, and therefore **Choice B**  is not the correct answer to this question. Off-target effects may affect the expression of other essential, functioning genes; this is a risk of using CRISPR technology in humans, and therefore **Choice D**  is not the correct answer to this question.
