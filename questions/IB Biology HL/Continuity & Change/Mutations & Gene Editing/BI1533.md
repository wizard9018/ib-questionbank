# BI1533

Which statement(s) explain how CRISPR technology can potentially be used in cancer treatments?

- I. $\hspace{1.6em}$ Can be used to remove tumour-suppressor genes

- II. $\hspace{1.3em}$ Can be used to target mutated genes that cause uncontrolled cell proliferation

- III. $\hspace{1em}$ Can be used to prevent the expression of oncogenes

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation:}}$\

CRISPR technology can be used to edit genes. It can be used to target and repress, or cut out mutated genes that cause uncontrolled cell proliferation resulting in tumour growth (**Statement II**). CRISPR can also be used to stop the expression of oncogenes (**Statement III**). Oncogenes are mutated genes that have the potential to cause cancer. By preventing their expression, the risk of developing cancer can be reduced; **Choice B** is correct.

**Statement I**  is incorrect as the role of tumour suppressor genes is to inhibit cell proliferation and the development of tumours. Therefore, by removing tumour suppressor genes, cancerous tumours are more likely to develop. **Choices A, C** and **D** are incorrect as they all include **Statement I**.
