# BI0688

In which location does fertilisation take place?

![Untitled 4](<images/Untitled 4.png>)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because fertilisation takes place in the ovule. **Choice A** is incorrect because although pollen lands on the stigma, fertilisation occurs in the ovule. **Choice C** is incorrect because the anther produces pollen. **Choice D** is incorrect because the nucleus of the pollen just travels through the style in order to arrive at the ovule, where fertilisation takes place.
