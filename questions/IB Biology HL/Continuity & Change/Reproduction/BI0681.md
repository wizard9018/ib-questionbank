# BI0681

Which statement is the correct definition of pollination?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ The fusion of a male and female gamete

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ The transfer of pollen from an anther to a stigma
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ The transfer of seeds away from the parent plant
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ The transfer of pollen by a pollinator

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because pollination is the transfer of pollen from an anther to a stigma. **Choice A** is incorrect because the fusion of a male and female gamete is fertilization. **Choice C** is incorrect because the transfer of seeds away from the parent plant is dispersal. **Choice D** is incorrect because pollination can occur without a pollinator.  Some plants are wind-pollinated.
