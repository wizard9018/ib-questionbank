# BI0687

Which of these is **not** part of plant reproduction?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Pollination

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Fertilisation

-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Seed dispersal

-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Transpiration

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because transpiration is the loss of water from the stomata of a plant, it is not a stage of plant reproduction. **Choices A**, **B** and **C** are incorrect because pollination, fertilisation and seed dispersal are all stages of plant reproduction.
