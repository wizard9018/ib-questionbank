# BI1254

Why is hormone replacement therapy (HRT) used as a treatment in biological females?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ It regulates the menstrual cycle

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ It stimulates ovulation

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ It increases fertility

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ It reduces the symptoms of menopause

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
During menopause, the production of progesterone and oestradiol is reduced by the ovaries. This is thought to cause many of the symptoms associated with menopause, including temperature changes and difficulty focusing. HRT is used to replace biologically female hormones in order to reduce these symptoms.

**Choice A** is incorrect, as menstruation stops during menopause. **Choice B** is incorrect since the hormone responsible for ovulation is luteinising hormone (LH), which is no longer effective during menopause. **Choice C** is incorrect since fertility treatment typically relies on FSH and LH, while HRT uses progesterone and oestradiol.
