# BI1178

What feature is **not** likely to  be found in an insect-pollinated flower?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Large, colourful petals

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Feathery stigma dangling outside the flower

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Strong scent

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Nectary present inside flower

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Flowers can be insect or wind-pollinated. Flowers that are insect-pollinated have a sticky stigma inside the flower. As the insect enters the flower, some of the pollen carried on the insect sticks to this stigma. In wind-pollinated flowers, a feathery stigma hangs out of the flower to catch pollen carried in the air. **Choice B**  is correct.

Insect-pollinated flowers often have large, colourful petals, a strong scent and nectar produced in nectaries, to attract insects; **Choices A, C** and **D** are true of insect-pollinated flowers, and so are not  correct answers.
