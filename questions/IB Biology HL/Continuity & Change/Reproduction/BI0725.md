# BI0725

Which of the following is an advantage of external fertilization?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Predators will consume gametes.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Extreme changes in temperature will affect gametes.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Greater genetic variation in the offspring.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Lower chance of gametes coming in contact for fertilisation.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
External fertilisation often bypasses the selection of the mates. This, and a greater variety of the gametes (because of the increased number), leads to the greater genetic variation of the offspring as compared to offspring of organisms following the internal fertilisation strategy. **Choice C**  is correct.

**Choices A, B** and  **D** are all disadvantages of external fertilisation. **Choices A, B** and  **D** are incorrect.
