# BI0724

What is the purpose of the cortical reaction?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ To allow the fertilised egg to implant properly.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ To prevent polyspermy.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ To allow fertilisation to take place.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ To trigger oogenesis.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
During the cortical reaction, vesicles (cortical granules) near the membrane of the egg release enzymes that make the zona pellucida hard and prevent more than one sperm (polyspermy) from fertilising the egg. **Choice B**  is correct.

**Choices A, C** and **D** do not describe the purpose of the cortical reactions. **Choices A, C** and **D** are incorrect.
