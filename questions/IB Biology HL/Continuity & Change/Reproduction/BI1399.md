# BI1399

Carrot (*Daucus carota*) seeds were collected over a number of seasons and stored in a cool, dark place. Seeds of each age were then grown and from the results, the probability of germination was calculated based on seed age, as seen in the graph below. What can be concluded based on the results of this experiment?

![1399i](images/1399i.png)

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
 | | |
 | :-- | :-- |
 | I. | Seeds from the most recent season were the most likely to germinate. |
 | II. | The germination rate for all seeds was highest in week 1. |
 | III. | It can be estimated that 60% of seeds from a 2020 pack would germinate by week 5. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The graph indicates that by week 10, more than 90% of the 2022 season carrot seeds will likely have germinated. This is considerably higher than either the 2021 or 2020 season seeds, so **Statement I** is true. During week 1, the graph indicates that it is likely that none of the seeds will have germinated, so **Statement II** would be false. By week 5, it can be estimated that 40% of the 2020 season seeds had germinated, not 60% as **Statement III** indicates. Therefore Choice A is correct.

**Choices B, C**, and **D** are incorrect as they all include one or more untrue statements.
