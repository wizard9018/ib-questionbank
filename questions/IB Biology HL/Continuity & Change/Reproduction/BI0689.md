# BI0689

**Maximum mark: 6**

The diagram below shows an animal-pollinated flower.

![BI0689](images/BI0689.png)

1. Identify the processes that occur in locations I and II. [2 marks]

2. Sexual reproduction in flowers produces seeds. Outline the process of germination in seeds. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ location I: meiosis or pollen formation [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ location II: meiosis or fertilisation [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
**Meiosis** occurs at location I ($\checkmark$). **Fertilisation** happens at location II ($\checkmark$).

2. any **four** of the following features clearly drawn with a label line in contact with each part:

 $\ce{ -> }$ imbibition occurs **OR** water is absorbed by the seed [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ seed coat **OR** testa ruptures due to swelling of seed [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ metabolic activity is reactivated [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ water uptake brings about gibberellin production [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ gibberellins stimulate production of enzymes **OR** amylase [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ amylase hydrolyses **OR** digests stored starch **OR** enzymes digest food stores [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ soluble products **OR** glucose **OR** maltose **OR** sugars **OR** amino acids transported to embryo [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ radicle **OR** embryonic root emerges before plumule **OR** plumule **OR** embryonic shoot grows after the radicle [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ mitosis occurs in meristematic regions of the embryo for growth [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ energy for growth comes from aerobic respiration of the mobilised food reserves [1 mark]

 [4 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

 **Water uptake** by the seed (**imbibition**) initiates germination and **reactivates metabolic processes** ($\checkmark$). **Gibberellins** produced in the embryo **stimulate** the **enzyme synthesis**, including **amylase** ($\checkmark$). Amylase **breaks down starch** into **sugars** ($\checkmark$), which are used in **aerobic respiration** to release **energy** for **growth** ($\checkmark$). The **radicle grows first**, forming the root, followed by the **plumule**, which develops into the shoot ($\checkmark$).
