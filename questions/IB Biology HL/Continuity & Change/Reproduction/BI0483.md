# BI0483

Which of these is **not** an effect of testosterone?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Development of testes

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Development of embryonic gonads into testes
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Sperm production
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Development of secondary male characteristics

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because a protein called testis determining factor (TDF) stimulates the development of embryonic gonads as testes. Testosterone causes the development of the testes (**Choice A**), stimulates sperm production (**Choice C**) and stimulates the development of secondary male characteristics (**Choice D**).
