# BI1293

The image shows a butterfly bush being pollinated by a bee and butterfly. What is not a factor that attracts pollinators to flowers?

![BI1293x](images/BI1293x.png)

[@ 2025]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Colour of petals

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Scent of flower

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Using fertilisers

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Presence of nectar

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Fertiliser around a plant can mask the scent of a flower so that pollinators are less attracted or may be harmful to pollinators; **Choice C** is correct.

Pollinators such as bees are attracted to flowers because of their brightly coloured petals, attractive scents emanating from flowers or the presence of nectar which they feed on. **Choices A, B** and **D** are incorrect.
