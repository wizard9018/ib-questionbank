# BI1602

Which of the following statements is/are true?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Gonadotrophin-releasing hormone (GnRH) triggers the release of follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) and luteinising hormone (LH) in biological females but not biological males.  |
    | II. | Development of both ova and sperm involves the secretion of follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH). |
    | III. | Primary oocytes and spermatocytes follow a similar pathway through meiosis. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
FSH stimulates the growth and maturation of follicles in the ovaries, which contain developing ova, producing mature eggs capable of being fertilised. FSH also stimulates the development of Sertoli cells. These cells play a vital role in supporting the developing sperm cells, contributing to the production of functional sperm cells. **Statement II** is the only true statement therefore **Choice B** is correct.

GnRH triggers the release of FSH and LH in both biological females and males. In biological females, FSH and LH play essential roles in regulating the menstrual cycle, follicle development, ovulation, and hormone production. In males, FSH and LH regulate spermatogenesis and testosterone production. **Statement I** is, therefore, not true.

While both primary oocytes and spermatocytes undergo meiosis, their pathways through meiosis differ significantly. In females, primary oocytes undergo meiosis I during foetal development but arrest in prophase I until puberty. A primary oocyte completes meiosis I upon ovulation to form a secondary oocyte and a polar body. Meiosis II only occurs after fertilisation. In males, spermatocytes undergo meiosis I and meiosis II after puberty to produce four haploid sperm cells that may then fertilise an ovum. **Statement III** is, therefore, not true.

**Choices A, C** and **D** are incorrect answers to the question as they include **Statement I** and/or **Statement III**.
