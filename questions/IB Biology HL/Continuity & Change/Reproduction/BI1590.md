# BI1590

The diagram shows a half-view of an almond flower (*Prunus dulcis*).

![BI1590](images/BI1590.png)

What feature indicates that this species is adapted for animal pollination?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Large petals

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Many stamens

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Presence of a central stigma

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Stigma longer than stamen

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because large petals are a characteristic feature of animal-pollinated flowers. They attract pollinators with their size and colour and provide a surface for smaller pollinators to land on.

**Choice B** is incorrect because both wind and animal-pollinated flowers typically have many stamens to increase pollen production. **Choice C** is incorrect because a central stigma is common for most types of flowers, regardless of whether they are wind or animal-pollinated. **Choice D** is incorrect because having a longer stigma than the stamen does not indicate what type of pollination a flower requires. Instead, it promotes cross-pollination by reducing self-pollination.
