# BI0558

The diagram shows a frontal view of the female reproductive organs.

![BI0558i](images/BI0558i.png)

What structures are labeled by the numbers I, II, and III?

| | I | II | III|
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | ovary | uterus | cervix |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | fallopian tube | uterus | vagina|
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | ovary | cervix | vagina |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | fallopian tube | cervix | cervix |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The structure labelled I is the fallopian tube. It transports the egg from the ovary to the uterus and is the site of fertilisation.

The structure labelled II is the uterus. It is where the fertilised egg implants and the embryo develops during pregnancy.

The structure labelled III is the vagina. It serves as the passage through which sperm enters the female reproductive system and also serves as the birth canal during delivery.

**Choice B** is therefore the correct answer.

The cervix, which is located between the vagina and the uterus, is not labelled in this diagram. Therefore, answer **Choices A, C**, and **D** are incorrect.
