# BI0530

The diagram shows the transfer of a substance from maternal blood to foetal blood through the placenta. Which substance is being transferred?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Water

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Antibodies

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Glucose

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Electrolytes

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The diagram shows the transfer of a substance across the placenta by endocytosis. The substance from the maternal blood is transported into cells of the placenta through the invagination of the cell membrane and subsequent formation of a vesicle (endosome), which then empties its contents into the foetal blood. Of the answer choices, only antibodies require transport via endocytosis to reach foetal blood.

Water (**Choice A**) crosses the placenta through osmosis. Glucose (**Choice C**) crosses the placenta by facilitated diffusion through glucose transporters. Electrolytes (**Choice D**) are transported across the placenta by facilitated diffusion or active transport via carrier proteins.
