# BI0726

Which two hormones are involved in a positive feedback loop to promote muscle contractions during childbirth?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Oxytocin and estrogen

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Oestradiol and progesterone

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Human chorionic gonadotropin and oxytocin

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Progesterone and oxytocin

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
During the last third of pregnancy, progesterone levels fall, resulting in the rise of oxytocin levels. There is also a rise in oestradiol levels which increases the number of oxytocin receptors on the muscle cells in the uterine wall. When oxytocin binds to these receptors, the muscle contracts, and the contraction results in the secretion of more oxytocin. This is positive feedback. **Choice A** is correct.

**Choices B** and **D** are incorrect, as high progesterone levels prevent uterine contractions. **Choice C**  is incorrect because human chorionic gonadotropin stimulates the ovary to produce progesterone during early pregnancy and is at lower levels during later pregnancy.
