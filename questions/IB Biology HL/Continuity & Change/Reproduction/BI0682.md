# BI0682

Which statement is the correct definition for seed dispersal?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$  The fusion of a male and female gamete

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$  The transfer of pollen from an anther to a stigma
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$  The transfer of seeds away from the parent plant
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$  The transfer of pollen by a pollinator

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because the transfer of seeds away from the parent plant is dispersal. **Choice A** is incorrect because the fusion of a male and female gamete is fertilization. **Choice B** is incorrect because the transfer of pollen from an anther to a stigma is pollination. **Choice D** is incorrect because pollen transfer involves the movement of pollen, not seeds.
