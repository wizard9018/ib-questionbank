# BI1061

Which statement(s) is/are correct for the hormones of the menstrual cycle?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Progesterone inhibits FSH. |
    | II. | There is positive feedback between oestradiol and FSH. |
    | III. | There is positive feedback between progesterone and LH. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Progesterone inhibits the secretion of FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone) by negative feedback. This mechanism prevents the development of additional follicles during the luteal phase when progesterone levels are high; **Statement I** is true. During the follicular phase, moderate levels of oestradiol inhibit FSH secretion via negative feedback. However, when oestradiol levels peak (just before ovulation), they stimulate a surge in FSH secretion via positive feedback, which helps trigger ovulation; **Statement II** is true.

**Choice C** is therefore correct because **Statements I** and **II** are true.

Progesterone does not have positive feedback on LH (luteinizing hormone). Instead, high levels of progesterone inhibit LH secretion through negative feedback. This inhibition occurs during the luteal phase to prevent a second ovulation and maintain the uterine lining; **Statement III** is not true.

**Choices A** and **B** are not correct as they are missing one true statement.
**Choice D** is not correct as it includes the untrue **Statement III**.
