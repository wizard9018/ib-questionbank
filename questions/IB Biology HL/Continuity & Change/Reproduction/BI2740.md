# BI2740

Which of the following statements about the relationship between hormone replacement therapy (HRT) and coronary heart disease (CHD) are correct?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Early observational studies indicated that HRT resulted in a lower CHD risk |
    | II. | Randomised controlled trials later found a slight increase in CHD risk with HRT use |
    | III. | Women who take HRT under the age of 50 have a higher risk of CHD. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is true because early observational studies did show an apparent association between HRT use and reduced CHD risk. However, this association was later challenged, as the observed benefit was likely due to confounding factors like socio-economic status and health behaviours.

**Statement II** is true because randomised controlled trials later found that HRT was associated with a slight increase in CHD risk, especially when initiated after age 60.

**Statement III** is false because this contradicts current evidence. In fact, younger postmenopausal women (< 60 years or within 10 years of menopause) typically show no increased CHD risk and may even see some cardiovascular benefit when HRT is initiated early.

**Choice A** is correct because it includes the two true **Statements I** and **II** and not the false one.

**Choices B, C** and **D** are incorrect because they all include the false **Statement III**.
