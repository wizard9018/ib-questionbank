# BI1295

The image shows the reproductive system of a flower. What method is this plant using to prevent self-pollination?

![BI1295](images/BI1295.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The anther and stigma mature at different times

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ It only contains female reproductive organs

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The insects have to brush past the stigma to reach the anthers

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ It only contains male reproductive organs

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Self-pollination is when pollen is transferred from the anther to the stigma of the same flower. The diagram shows anthers that have already matured and released their pollen. This temporal separation ensures that the flower is unlikely to self-pollinate because its own pollen is no longer available when the stigma is ready to receive pollen from a flower of the same species. **Choice A** is correct.

The stigma, style and ovary that make up the female reproductive organs and the anther and filament that make up the male reproductive organs are clearly visible. **Choices B** and **D** are incorrect. If the insects have to brush past the stigma to reach the anthers, then the insects may deposit the pollen from the anthers on the stigma as they leave the flower, which could allow self-pollination, not prevent it; **Choice C** is incorrect.
