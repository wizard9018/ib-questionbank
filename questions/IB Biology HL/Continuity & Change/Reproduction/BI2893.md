# BI2893

Which of the following is typically suspended during IVF treatment to ensure successful egg retrieval?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Luteal phase hormone production

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Production of oestradiol and progesterone

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Ovulation of eggs without stimulation

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Hormone production from the hypothalamus and pituitary gland

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because during IVF treatment, the natural hormone production by the hypothalamus and pituitary gland is suppressed using drugs like GnRH agonists or GnRH antagonists. These drugs inhibit the secretion of gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH), which in turn reduces the production of luteinising hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) from the pituitary gland. This suppression prevents premature ovulation and allows for precise control over the timing of egg.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the luteal phase is not suspended during IVF. In fact, it is artificially induced after egg retrieval and embryo transfer by administering progesterone to support the uterine lining and prepare it for potential implantation.

**Choice B** is incorrect because oestradiol and progesterone levels are regulated via external hormone supplementation during IVF. These hormones are used to stimulate the ovaries for egg maturation and to maintain the uterine lining, but they are not suppressed.

**Choice C** is incorrect because ovulation is not left to occur naturally. In IVF, ovulation is carefully triggered using hormones like hCG after the eggs are matured through the administration of FSH and LH. This ensures the eggs are retrieved at the correct time.
