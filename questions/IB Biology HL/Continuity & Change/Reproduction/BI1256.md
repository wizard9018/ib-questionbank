# BI1256

The graph shows the relative risks of various health outcomes as a result of hormone replacement therapy (HRT) collected from a meta-analysis of 38908 postmenopausal biological females. The researchers compared the risks of combined or oestrogen only HRT to a control group that received a placebo. Oestradiol is a type of oestrogen.

![BI1256QBi](images/BI1256QBi.png)

What conclusion could be drawn from the results?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Patients receiving oestrogen-only HRT have a reduced risk of death from all causes.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Patients receiving oestrogen-only HRT have a reduced risk for all health outcomes.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The risk of stroke is significantly lower for patients not receiving HRT.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The risk of coronary events is higher for patients receiving HRT.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The risk of stroke is elevated for both the combined therapy and oestrogen-only HRT groups. This means that the placebo group had a lower risk of stroke than either group receiving HRT.

Although the risk of cardiac death appears to be reduced for oestrogen-only HRT patients, the risk of total deaths has not been reduced, so **Choice A** cannot be correct. **Choice B** is incorrect as oestrogen-only HRT led to a slightly increased risk for total deaths, and stroke in particular. **Choice D** is incorrect since the data shows that oestrogen-only HRT may reduce the risk of coronary events while combined therapy HRT may increase it.
