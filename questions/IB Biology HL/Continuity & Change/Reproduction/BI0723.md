# BI0723

Which is the correct order for the stages in spermatogenesis?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Meiosis → Mitosis →  Cell growth → Differentiation

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Mitosis → Differentiation → Cell growth → Meiosis

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Mitosis → Cell growth → Meiosis→ Differentiation

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Cell growth → Meiosis → Mitosis →  Differentiation

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
During spermatogenesis, firstly the germinal epithelium cells divide via mitosis, then these cells grow larger to produce primary spermatocytes, then they carry out two rounds of meiosis to produce secondary spermatocytes and spermatids, respectively. Finally, the spermatids differentiate into mature sperm cells. **Choice C** is correct.

**Choices A, B** and **D** are incorrect.
