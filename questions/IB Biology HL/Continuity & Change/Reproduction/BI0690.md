# BI0690

**Maximum mark: 4**

1.    List the two types of gametes in flowering plants.    [2 marks]

2.    Outline the role of pollinators in plant reproduction.    [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.    Both of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ pollen (male gamete) [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ ovum **OR** ova (female gamete) [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
**Pollen** ($\checkmark$) and **ova** ($\checkmark$).

2.    Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$  to transfer the male gamete to the female part of a flower [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  collect pollen from the anther of insect-pollinated flowers [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  bring pollen to the stigma of the flower [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  for fertilisation [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Pollinators are needed for **fertilisation** ($\checkmark$) in animal-pollinated flowers, as pollinators transfer the **male gamete** to the **female part** of the flower ($\checkmark$). Pollinators collect pollen from the **anther** ($\checkmark$) and transfer it to the **stigma** ($\checkmark$) of insect-pollinated flowers.
