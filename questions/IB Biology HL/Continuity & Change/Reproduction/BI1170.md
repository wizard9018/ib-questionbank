# BI1170

Which row correctly shows a hormone involved in the menstrual cycle matched to where it is secreted from and its function?

| | Hormone | Secreted from… | Function |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Oestradiol (oestrogen) | Ovaries | Stimulates the build-up of the endometrium |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Progesterone | Pituitary gland | Maintains the endometrial lining |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | FSH | Hypothalamus | Stimulates the maturation of egg cells in the follicles |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | LH | Pituitary gland | Stimulates an increase in the number of FSH receptors |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Oestradiol, or oestrogen, is secreted from the ovaries and stimulates the build-up of the endometrial lining; **Choice A**  is correct.

Although progesterone maintains the endometrium lining, it is secreted from the ovaries and corpus luteum; **Choice B**  is incorrect. Although FSH does stimulate the maturation of egg cells in the follicles, it is secreted from the pituitary gland; **Choice C**  is incorrect. Although LH is secreted from the pituitary gland, it does not stimulate an increase in the number of FSH receptors, instead, it stimulates ovulation and promotes development of the corpus luteum; **Choice D**  is incorrect.
