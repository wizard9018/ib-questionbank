# BI0803

**Maximum mark: 3**

To understand the effects of abiotic stress, investigations were carried out using barley seeds. The researchers wanted to select strains tolerant to drought. Petri dishes were set up with some cotton wool spread out evenly at the bottom and the barley seeds placed on top, as shown below.

![BI0803](images/BI0803.png)

To simulate the effects of drought, polyethylene glycol (PEG) was added at a concentration of 20% to experimental petri dishes while distilled water (without PEG) was added to control petri dishes.

Germination percentage was calculated as follows:

where ‘n' is number of seeds germinated on the final day and ‘N' is the total number of seeds.

Germination pace is expressed as GP

Where **N** is the total number of germinated seeds at the end of the experiment and n is the number of germinated seeds on day **g** (1, 2, 3..).

1. Based on the results shown, explain how a drought is likely to affect a crop such as barley. [2 marks]

2. State another abiotic factor, apart from water, that is required for germination of seeds. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **two** of the following

 $\ce{ -> }$ the germination rate of the barley crop will decrease overall [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the percentage of seeds that germinate is reduced to approximately 80% [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the pace at which the seeds germinate will decrease [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ delay in the time taken by the seeds to germinate will delay growth of the crop [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The **germination rate will decrease** ($\checkmark$), meaning fewer seeds will germinate than when there is no drought and there will be a **delay in the time taken** ($\checkmark$) for the seeds to germinate during a drought period.

2. Any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ oxygen [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ suitable temperature **OR** warmth [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ suitable pH [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Another abiotic factor required for seed germination is **oxygen** ($\checkmark$).
