# BI1582

The diagram shows the male reproductive system. Which structure is responsible for secreting alkaline fluid and nutrients to support the sperm?

![BI1582](images/BI1582.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because it points to the seminal vesicles, which produce alkaline fluid to offset the acidic environment of the male and female reproductive systems. They also secrete nutrients such as fructose, which provides energy to the sperm.

**Choice B** is the urethra, a duct not involved in secretion. **Choice C** is the epididymis, which is involved in sperm storage and maturation. **Choice D** is the testes, which produces sperm and reproductive hormones.
