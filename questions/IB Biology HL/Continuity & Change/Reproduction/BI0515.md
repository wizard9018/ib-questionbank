# BI0515

Which hormone is released by the ovary and stimulates the release of the hormone that triggers ovulation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ FSH

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Oestradiol

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ LH

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Progesterone

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
During the menstrual cycle, one follicle in the ovaries becomes the dominant follicle. This follicle releases the hormone oestradiol. As the follicle continues to develop, it secretes more oestradiol, which leads to an increase in its concentration in the bloodstream. The high levels of oestradiol stimulate the pituitary gland to release luteinising hormone (LH). The surge in LH is responsible for triggering ovulation, the release of the egg from the ovary. **Choice B** is the correct answer.

**Choice A** s incorrect because follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) is released by the pituitary gland and stimulates the development of ovarian follicles, but it does not trigger ovulation.

**Choice C** is incorrect because luteinising hormone (LH) is the hormone released by the pituitary that triggers ovulation, but it is stimulated by the increased levels of oestradiol.

**Choice D** is incorrect because progesterone is produced after ovulation by the corpus luteum and plays a role in preparing the uterus for pregnancy. It inhibits the release of LH and FSH to prevent further ovulation after the egg has been released.
