# BI1063

Which pair of hormones are produced by the gland labelled X?

![BI1063QB](images/BI1063QB.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ FSH and oestradiol

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ FSH and progesterone

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Oestradiol and progesterone

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ FSH and LH

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because gland X is the pituitary gland and FSH and LH are produced by the anterior pituitary gland whereas progesterone and oestradiol are produced by the ovary (**Choice A, B,** and **C**).
