# BI1094

Which is **not** a role of progesterone during pregnancy?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Stimulates the corpus luteum to produce hCG and oestradiol  |
    | II. | Inhibits the secretion of oxytocin  |
    | III. | Prepares the uterus lining for implantation |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is **not** a role of progesterone. Progesterone is produced by the corpus luteum; hCG is produced by the blastocyst after implantation and this hCG  maintains the corpus luteum at the beginning of pregnancy. **Choice B** is correct.

**Statements II** and **III** are roles of progesterone. **Choices A, C** and **D** are therefore incorrect.
