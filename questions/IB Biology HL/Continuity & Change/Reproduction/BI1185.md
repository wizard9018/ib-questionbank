# BI1185

Which line represents the levels of progesterone during pregnancy and childbirth?

![BI1185QB](images/BI1185QB.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The role of high progesterone levels is to maintain the uterus lining in preparation and during pregnancy. It is produced by the corpus luteum until approximately the 9th week of pregnancy when the placenta takes over and the corpus luteum degrades. Immediately before birth, progesterone levels decline sharply allowing contractions to start. **Choice D** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect as progesterone levels do not remain constant during pregnancy and childbirth. **Choice B** is incorrect as progesterone levels do not start at a high level at the beginning of pregnancy and then decrease throughout pregnancy as this would lead to the breakdown of the endometrium. **Choice C** is incorrect, as progesterone levels do not drop halfway through pregnancy.
