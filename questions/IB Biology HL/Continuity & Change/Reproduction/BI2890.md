# BI2890

Which of the following statements is true about meiosis and genetic diversity?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Meiosis produces genetically identical haploid gametes. |
    | II. | Crossing over during meiosis creates new combinations of alleles. |
    | III. | Independent assortment leads to genetic variation in offspring.|

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is false because meiosis produces genetically diverse gametes, not identical ones. This is due to the processes of crossing over and independent assortment during meiosis, both of which contribute to genetic variation.

**Statement II** is true because during prophase I of meiosis, crossing over occurs between homologous chromosomes. This process results in the exchange of genetic material, creating new combinations of alleles and increasing genetic diversity.

**Statement III** is true because independent assortment of chromosomes during metaphase I of meiosis leads to the random distribution of maternal and paternal chromosomes into gametes. This process results in different combinations of chromosomes in the offspring, further contributing to genetic variation.

**Choice B** is correct because it only includes the true **Statements II** and **III**.\
**Choices A, C** and **D** are all incorrect as they include the false **Statement I**.
