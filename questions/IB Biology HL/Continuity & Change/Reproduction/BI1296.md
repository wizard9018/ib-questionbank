# BI1296

To prevent self-pollination, plants have self-incompatibility mechanisms. Which statement about self-incompatibility mechanisms is **incorrect**?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ They are only activated in response to changes in the environment

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ They involve the carpel recognising and rejecting pollen produced by the anthers of the same flower

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ They are genetically controlled

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ They inhibit the growth of the pollen tube if the pollen has been produced in the same flower as the style

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Self-incompatibility mechanisms are not only activated in response to environmental changes, they are genetically controlled and activated throughout the plant's lifetime, **Choice A**  is correct.

Self-incompatibility involves the carpel recognising and rejecting pollen produced by the same flower (**Choice B**), and this involves genetic control (**Choice C**). One mechanism used to reject the pollen produced by the same flower is to inhibit the growth of the pollen tube (**Choice D**).
