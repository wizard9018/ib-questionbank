# BI0719

The micrograph shows spermatogenesis inside a mammalian seminiferous tubule. Which arrow indicates a spermatocyte?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Arrow C is pointing to a round cell with a large nucleus, that is closer to the middle of the tubule than the basement membrane. **Choice C** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect as it is pointing to the space between the cells (interstitial cells). **Choice B** is incorrect as it is pointing to spermatids, as indicated by the presence of tails and smaller nuclei. **Choice D** is incorrect as it is pointing to the basement membrane, the membrane surrounding the seminiferous tubules.
