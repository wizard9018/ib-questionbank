# BI2733

What is the correct chronological order of the events in spermatogenesis?

1\. Growth into primary spermatocytes\
2\. Meiosis I produces secondary spermatocytes\
3\. Meiosis II produces spermatids\
4\. Mitosis of spermatogonia\
5\. Differentiation into spermatozoa

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 1 $\rarr$ 4 $\rarr$ 2 $\rarr$ 3 $\rarr$ 5

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 4 $\rarr$ 2 $\rarr$ 1 $\rarr$ 3 $\rarr$ 5

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 4 $\rarr$ 1 $\rarr$ 2 $\rarr$ 3 $\rarr$ 5

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 4 $\rarr$ 1 $\rarr$ 2 $\rarr$ 5 $\rarr$ 3

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because spermatogenesis occurs in the following sequence: 1. Spermatogonia undergo mitotic divisions. 2. Some spermatogonia grow and differentiate into primary spermatocytes. 3. Primary spermatocytes undergo meiosis I, producing secondary spermatocytes. 4. Secondary spermatocytes undergo meiosis II, forming spermatids. 5. Spermatids mature through a process called spermiogenesis, where they develop into spermatozoa.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the growth into primary spermatocytes happens after mitosis of spermatogonia, so placing 1 before 4 is incorrect.

**Choice B** is incorrect because meiosis I cannot happen before primary spermatocytes are formed. The primary spermatocytes must first grow before they can enter meiosis.

**Choice D** is incorrect because differentiation into spermatozoa must happen after meiosis II, not before. This choice incorrectly places differentiation before the final step of meiosis.
