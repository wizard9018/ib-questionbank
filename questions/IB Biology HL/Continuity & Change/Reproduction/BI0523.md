# BI0523

Which seed structures are represented by the labels X, Y, and Z?

![BI0523ii](images/BI0523ii.png)

| | X | Y | Z |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Plumule (embryonic shoot) | Radicle (embryonic root) | Testa (seed coat) |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Radicle (embryonic root) | Plumule (embryonic shoot) | Cotyledon (first leaves)|
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Testa (seed coat) | Plumule (embryonic shoot) | Radicle (embryonic root) |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Testa (seed coat) | Radicle (embryonic root) | Cotyledon (first leaves)|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The radicle is the first part of a seedling to emerge from the seed during germination. The radicle will become the root of the plant. The radicle is labelled by X in the diagram. The plumule is the seedling stem and will give rise to the leaves. The plumule is labelled by Y in the diagram. The cotyledon is the seed leaf within the embryo of a seed. Cotyledons supply the nutrition a plant embryo needs to germinate. They make up a significant portion of the seed. The cotyledon is labelled by Z in the diagram. **Choice B** is, therefore, the correct answer.
