# BI0684

Which row correctly describes the structure of insect-pollinated flowers?

| | Petal | Stigma and anther | Nectary |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| Green | Outside flower | Absent |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Green | Inside flower | Absent |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Colourful | Inside flower | Present |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Colourful | Outside flower | Present |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because insect pollinated flowers have colourful petals with the stigma and anther inside the flower and have a nectary. **Choice A** is incorrect because insect-pollinated flowers have colourful petals with the stigma and anther inside the flower and have a nectary. **Choice B** is incorrect because insect-pollinated flowers have colourful flowers and have a nectary. **Choice D** is incorrect because insect-pollinated flowers have the stigma and anther inside the flower.
