# BI1292

Which statement(s) is a method of promoting cross-pollination?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | A bat brushing against the anther of a plant as it consumes nectar |
    | II. | The wind carrying pollen across a field of flowers |
    | III. | Floating pollen between aquatic plants |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Cross-pollination is when pollen is transferred from the anther of one flower to the stigma of another flower of the same species. Cross-pollination can occur when animals such as bats rub against the anther of one plant and fly to another plant of the same species (**Statement I**). Cross-pollination can occur when wind carries pollen between one plant and lands on the stigma of another plant of the same species (**Statement II**).  Cross-pollination can occur when pollen floats between aquatic plants and lands on the stigma of aquatic plants of the same species (**Statement III**). **Choice D**  is correct.

**Choices A, B** and **C** are incorrect as they do not include all three statements.
