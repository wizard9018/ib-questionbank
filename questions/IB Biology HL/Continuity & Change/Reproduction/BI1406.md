# BI1406

**Maximum mark: 6**

The diagram shows how a pregnancy test works to detect hCG in urine.

![1406 new D3.1](<images/1406 new D3.1.png>)

1. Monoclonal antibodies are used in pregnancy tests. Describe what a monoclonal antibody is. [2 marks]

2. Outline the purpose of the second coloured line in the pregnancy test. [2 marks]

3. Explain the role of hCG in pregnancy. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ antibodies produced by a single clone **OR** one particular type of B-lymphocytes **OR** plasma B cells [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ specific to one antigen **OR** binds to only one particular antigen [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ produced under laboratory conditions [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ produced using hybridoma cells **OR** by recombinant DNA technology [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Monoclonal antibodies are antibodies that bind to only **one specific antigen** ($\checkmark$). They are produced by a single **clone** of **hybrididoma** ($\checkmark$) **plasma B cells** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ acts as control line [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ to confirm that the test is working correctly **OR** to confirm urine has been absorbed properly **OR** to show test is valid [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ contains immobilised antibodies specific to the mobile antibodies **OR** produce colour regardless of hCG presence [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The second coloured line acts as a **control** ($\checkmark$), ensuring the **test is working correctly** by confirming that **urine** has been **absorbed** and the test is **valid** ($\checkmark$).

3. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ maintains the corpus luteum **OR** stimulates corpus luteum to continue producing progesterone **OR** oestradiol **OR** oestrogen [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ thereby supporting early pregnancy **OR** maintenance of uterine lining **OR** endometrium [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ involved in foetal development **OR** plays a role in placental development [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 hCG maintains the **corpus luteum** as an **endocrine gland** ($\checkmark$)that secretes **progesterone** to **maintain** the **uterine lining** during pregnancy($\checkmark$).
