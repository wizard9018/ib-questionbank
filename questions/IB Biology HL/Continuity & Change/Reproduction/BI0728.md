    $\ce{->}$ large surface area for exchange of named substances between mother and foetus (direction must be stated) [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ the placenta barrier, **OR** membrane, between mother's and foetus' blood is thin to allow for rapid diffusion [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The placenta is the primary physical connection between mother and child; it has **villi that provide a large surface area**  for the **exchange of material** ($\checkmark$) such as **oxygen and nutrients** from the mother to the fetus ($\checkmark$). It also releases **oestrogen and progesterone** to **maintain the pregnancy** and prevent premature contractions ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$ maintains the endometrium     [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ to allow for successful implantation of the embryo [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ inhibits the secretion of the hormone oxytocin [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ which prevents early contractions [1 mark]
[3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Progesterone **maintains the endometrial lining** ($\checkmark$) in the uterus to allow for the successful **implantation of the embryo** ($\checkmark$).  It also **inhibits the secretion of the hormone oxytocin** ($\checkmark$), thereby **preventing early contractions** ($\checkmark$).
