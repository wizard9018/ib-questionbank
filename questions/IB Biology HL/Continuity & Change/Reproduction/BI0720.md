# BI0720

The micrograph shows a sperm cell. Which part contains enzymes to break down the zona pellucida of the egg cell?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Arrow A is pointing to the acrosome, which is found at the front of the sperm head. The acrosome contains enzymes that break down the zona pellucida around the egg. **Choice A** is correct.

**Choice B** is pointing to the mid-piece, the site of the mitochondria which produce ATP to power the movement of the cell. **Choice C** is pointing to the nucleus, which contains the genetic information. **Choice D** is pointing to the tail of the sperm cell which propells the cell forward.
