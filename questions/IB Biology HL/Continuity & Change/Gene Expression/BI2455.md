# BI2455

The diagram shows methyl tags attached to histone tails.

![BI2455](images/BI2455.png)

Which of the following best describes how air pollution affects histone methylation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ It always increases methylation

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ It always decreases methylation

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ It can either increase or decrease methylation

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ It only causes acetylation, not methylation

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because air pollution can lead to either increased or decreased methylation depending on the type of pollutant, the specific histone modification and the tissue or cell type.

**Choice A** is incorrect because while air pollution can increase methylation at certain histone sites, it does not do so universally. Some genes show decreased methylation in response to pollutants.

**Choice B** is incorrect because numerous studies show increased methylation at certain histone marks or gene promoters due to air pollution.

**Choice D** is incorrect because air pollution can influence both acetylation and methylation of histone, depending on the molecular pathways involved.
