# BI2287

Which of the following statements about gene expression in eukaryotic organisms is correct?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ A single gene always produces one protein.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Gene expression can be regulated at multiple stages.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ All genes are expressed in all cells.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Gene expression is not affected by environmental factors.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Gene expression is a complex process that can be regulated at various stages, including transcription, RNA processing, mRNA stability, translation, and post-translational modification of proteins. This flexibility allows cells to control protein production based on internal and external factors. **Choice B** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because a single gene can produce multiple proteins through mechanisms such as alternative splicing, post-translational modifications, or the use of alternative promoters. These processes enable a single gene to produce a variety of protein isoforms depending on the cellular context.

**Choice C** is incorrect because not all genes are expressed in all cells. The expression of genes is highly cell-specific and depends on the cell type, function, and environment. For example, liver cells express genes for detoxification enzymes, while neurons express genes for neurotransmitter receptors.

**Choice D** is incorrect because environmental factors such as temperature, light, stress, diet, and exposure to pollutants can significantly impact gene expression. For example, environmental stress can activate stress response genes, and dietary components like lactose can regulate operons in bacteria.
