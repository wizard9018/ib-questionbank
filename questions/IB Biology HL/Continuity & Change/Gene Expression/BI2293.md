Which statement about DNA methylation is correct?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Methylation enhances gene expression.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Methylation prevents transcription.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Methylation alters the DNA sequence.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Methylation increases protein synthesis.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
DNA methylation typically occurs at cytosine residues near promoter regions. This modification represses transcription by blocking the binding of transcription factors or recruiting proteins that condense chromatin (e.g., methyl-CpG binding proteins). As a result, transcription is inhibited, and the gene is effectively silenced. **Choice B** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because DNA methylation generally represses, rather than enhances, gene expression by preventing transcription factor binding and recruiting chromatin-condensing proteins.

**Choice C** is incorrect because methylation does not change the nucleotide sequence of DNA. It is an epigenetic modification that adds a methyl group to cytosine bases without altering the underlying genetic code.

**Choice D** is incorrect because methylation reduces or silences gene expression by repressing transcription, ultimately decreasing, not increasing, protein synthesis.
