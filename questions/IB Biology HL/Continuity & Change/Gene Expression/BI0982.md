# BI0982

Which of the following correctly pairs changes in the rate mRNA is degraded in the cytoplasm with the effect on the rate polypeptides for the corresponding gene are synthesised?

| | Rate mRNA is degraded | Rate polypeptides for this gene are synthesised
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | increases | decreases |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | increases | increases |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | decreases | decreases |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | decreases | unchanged |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because when there are fewer copies of a given strand of mRNA in the cytoplasm, ribosomes have fewer copies to bind to and thus the rate polypeptides are synthesised based on this strand will decrease, not increase (**Choice B**).

**Choices C** and **D** are incorrect because if the rate mRNA is degraded decreases, there will be more copies of this strand of mRNA resulting in an increase in the rate ribosomes bind to these strands and synthesise polypeptides.
