# BI0606

Gene regulation is important for cell differentiation and the development of an organism. For example, the developmental pattern of a common fruit fly (*Drosophila melanogaster*).

Which of the following play a direct role in regulating gene expression?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ Enhancer
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Repressor
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Epigenetic modifications

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$  II only
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Enhancers and repressors bind to specific base sequences of DNA to regulate transcription and, therefore, gene expression. Epigenetic modifications are, notably DNA methylation and histone modification act as a layer of control, turning genes on or off in response to various internal and external signals, thereby determining which genes are expressed and to what extent.

**Choices A**, **B** and **C** are incorrect as they do not include all three.
