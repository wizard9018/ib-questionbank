# BI0985

The rate mRNA is degraded in the cytoplasm is sometimes reduced in response to environmental stimuli.  How might this mechanism of producing more polypeptides for a given gene increase an individual's fitness?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Greater number of polypeptides increases fitness

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Polypeptide degradation decreases

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Rate of mRNA synthesis increases

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Quantity of ATP used per polypeptide synthesised decreases

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because if mRNA persists in the cytoplasm for a longer period of time, less mRNA needs to be synthesised per polypeptide synthesised.  mRNA synthesis requires ATP so a reduced need to synthesise mRNA conserves energy which may enhance the organism's fitness.

**Choice A** is incorrect because a greater number of polypeptides does not necessarily increase fitness.  **Choice B** is incorrect because a reduced rate of mRNA degradation does not affect the rate polypeptides are degraded.  **Choice C** is incorrect because if mRNA persists in the cytoplasm for longer periods of time, less mRNA needs to be synthesised to replace degraded mRNA.
