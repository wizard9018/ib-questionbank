# BI2456

Which statement explains the low numbers of epigenetic tags on the DNA in ova and sperm?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Epigenetic reprogramming during gametogenesis and early stages of zygote.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Epigenetic reprogramming of germ cells in testes and ova.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Epigenetic tags are removed from parent DNA before meiosis.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Epigenetic tags are not found on DNA in germ cells in the testes and ova.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because epigenetic reprogramming occurs during gametogenesis (spermatogenesis and oogenesis) and again in the early zygote, leading to the removal of most epigenetic tags from DNA.

**Choice B** is incorrect because epigenetic epigenetic reprogramming happens during meiosis of the cells that are produced by mitosis of the germ cells, it does not happen in the germ cells. **Choice C** is incorrect because some of the epigenetic tags are passed to haploid cells by meiosis. **Choice D** is incorrect because epigenetic tags are found on the DNA of germ cells in the testes and ova.
