# BI2450

Which of these enzymes has a role in regulating translation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Nucleases

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Proteases

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Polymerases

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Ligases

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because nucleases play a role in regulating translation by degrading mRNA. Once mRNA is degraded, the ribosome can no longer translate it into a protein, effectively stopping translation.

**Choice B** is incorrect because proteases break down proteins into amino acids, they do not degrade mRNA. **Choice C** is incorrect because DNA and RNA polymerases assemble these molecules, they do not break down DNA or RNA. **Choice D** is incorrect because ligases join molecules together, for example, DNA ligase.
