# BI2448

Which row is correct for the mechanism by which genes affect the phenotype?

| | Name of mechanism | Product of mechanism |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Gene expression | mRNA |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Translation | mRNA |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Transcription | Protein |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Gene expression | Protein |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is the correct answer because gene expression is the mechanism by which genes affect the phenotype. When a gene is expressed the sequence is transcribed onto mRNA and this mRNA is used by the ribosome to synthesise amino acids in the correct sequence to produce the protein which is the functional final product.

**Choice A** is incorrect because mRNA is not the end product of gene expression. mRNA would be used by the ribosome to create a protein, which is the functional gene product that ultimately affects the genotype.

**Choices B** and **C** are incorrect, as transcription (DNA to RNA) and translation (RNA to protein) are steps in the mechanism of gene expression.
