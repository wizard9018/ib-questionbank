![BI2451](images/BI2451.png)

Which statement is true for the effect(s) of epigenetic changes?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Gene expression is always decreased

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Genotype is altered but phenotype is not

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Phenotype is altered but genotype is not

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Gene expression is always increased

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because epigenetic tags regulate gene expression, altering the phenotype without changing the DNA sequence.

**Choice A** and **Choice D** are incorrect because epigenetic tags can either increase or decrease gene expression. **Choice B** is incorrect because epigenetic tags do not alter the genotype; they affect gene expression, which affects the phenotype.
