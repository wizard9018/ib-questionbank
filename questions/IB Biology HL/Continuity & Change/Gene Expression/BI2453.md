# BI2453

Which row is correct for the effect of methylation on transcription?

| | Methylation of promoters | Methylation of amino acids in histones|
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Represses transcription | Represses or activates transcription |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Activates transcription | Represses transcription |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Represses or activates transcription | Represses or activates transcription |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Activates transcriptions | Activates transcription |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because when a methyl group attaches to promoters it reduces transcription. When a methyl group attaches to amino acids in histones it can repress or activate transcription.

**Choices B, C**, and **D** are incorrect because at least one of the statements is incorrect.
