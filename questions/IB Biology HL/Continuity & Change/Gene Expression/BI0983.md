# BI0983

Which are correct explanations of why the genome, transcriptome and proteome of monozygotic twins may differ?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | The genome of two monozygotic twins will differ only due to random mutations as each individual grows and develops from a zygote into an adult.|
    | II. | The genome, transcriptome and proteome of two monozygotic twins are the same.|
    | III. | The transcriptome and proteome of monozygotic twins differ mostly due to environmental factors which affect gene regulation. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is correct because monozygotic twins are genetically identical immediately after an embryo divides into two embryos.  As repeated rounds of cell division and DNA replication occur different mutations accumulate in each twin and so differences in the genome, transcriptome and proteome develop (**Statement II**).  **Statement III** is correct because environmental factors affect which genes are turned on or off and to what extent each gene is transcribed and translated into polypeptides (**Statement II**). Therefore Choice C is correct.

**Choices A** and **D** are incorrect as they include **Statement II**. **Choice B** is incorrect as it does not include **Statement III**.
