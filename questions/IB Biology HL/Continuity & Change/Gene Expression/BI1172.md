# BI1172

The image shows cytosine and methylated cytosine. What effect does the direct methylation of cytosine in the promoter region of a gene have on gene expression?

![BI1172](images/BI1172.png)
[Source: Adapted from Walter, M. (2016, December 20). *File: DNA methylation.svg*. Wikimedia Commons. Retrieved September 13, 2023, from Wikimedia commons. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: DNA_methylation.svg. Copyright under CC BY-SA 4.0]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Promotes gene expression

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Inhibits gene expression

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Has no effect on gene expression

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Increases the expression of the gene twofold

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Direct methylation of cytosines in DNA represses transcription and inhibits gene expression; **Choice B** is correct.

**Choices A, C** and **D**  are incorrect.
