## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because acetylation involves the addition of acetyl groups to the histone proteins around which DNA is wound. This modification generally reduces the positive charge on histones, weakening their interaction with the negatively charged DNA. As a result, the chromatin becomes more open and accessible to transcription factors and RNA polymerase, enhancing transcription. Conversely, methylation of DNA reduces the binding of transcription factors, effectively silencing genes. Since this segment of chromatin shows only acetylation, transcription would be increased.

**Choice A** is incorrect because DNA methylation and acetylation of histones have antagonistic effects, possibly causing no change in the rate of transcription. **Choice C** is incorrect because only DNA methylation is occurring, resulting in gene silencing. **Choice D** is incorrect because there is more DNA methylation than acetylation of histones, most likely leading to reduced transcription.
