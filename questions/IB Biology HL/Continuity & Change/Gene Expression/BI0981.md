# BI0981

Which of the following statements is / are correct regarding the epigenetic regulation of gene expression?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | The base that undergoes most frequent methylation is adenine. |
    | II. | Gene transcription is inhibited by methylation of DNA |
    | III. | Methylation of histones only causes activation of transcription |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
When DNA is methylated at promoter regions, it leads to a closed chromatin structure and prevents transcription factors from binding to the promoter (**Statement II**).  This inhibits the initiation of gene transcription and results in reduced gene expression.

The most frequently methylated base in epigenetic regulation is cytosine, not adenine (**Statement I**).

Methylation of histones does not just activate transcription (**Statement III**), it can both activate and repress gene transcription, depending on the specific histone residue being methylated and the context of other epigenetic modifications.

**Choice A** is therefore the correct answer.
**Choices B**, **C** and **D** are incorrect as they include **Statements I** or **III** or both.
