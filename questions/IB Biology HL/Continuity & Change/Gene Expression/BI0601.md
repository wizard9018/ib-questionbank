# BI0601

Nucleosomes play an important role in regulating transcription, which influences gene expression. The diagram below shows tightly packed nucleosomes and loosely packed nucleosomes.

![BI0601](images/BI0601.png)
[Source: Adapted from CNX OpenStax, (2016, May). *File:Figure 16 03 02.png*. Wikimedia commons. Retrieved May 28, 2022, from https:// commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Figure_16_03_02.png. Copyright under CC-BY-4.0]

Which of the following statements is true about the nucleosome?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Transcription enzymes are excluded from loosely packed nucleosomes.
-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Removal of an acetyl group from the histone tail causes the nucleosome to be less tightly packed.

-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Gene expression is turned on when nucleosomes are tightly packed.
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Addition of a methyl group to the cytosine of the DNA inactivates the gene.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Chemical modifications to histones and DNA regulate transcription and hence gene expression. The addition of methyl groups to cytosine bases in DNA, that is tightly coiled around histone proteins, maintains the tight packing of the nucleosome (**Choice D**). This inactivates genes (**Choice C**). Genes are turned back on again when the methyl groups are removed, and the nucleosomes become more loosely packed.

Histone acetylation (**not** acetyl group removal) results in less tightly packed DNA, which upregulates transcription (**Choice B**).

When nucleosomes are tightly packed, there is less access for transcription enzymes to reach the DNA molecule (**Choice A**).
