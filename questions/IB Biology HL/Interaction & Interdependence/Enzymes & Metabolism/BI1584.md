# BI1584

The diagram shows a metabolic pathway catalysed by enzymes.

![BI1584](images/BI1584.png)

Which of the following would be a correct example of end-product inhibition?

| | Inhibitor | Enzyme |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | W | U|
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Z | S |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | T | V |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | U | V |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because the end product, Z, binds to and inhibits the enzyme S early in the metabolic pathway, slowing the overall reaction.

**Choice A** is incorrect because W is not the end product in the metabolic pathway and cannot inhibit enzymes that come after it. **Choices C** and **D** are incorrect because products inhibit enzymes in end-product inhibition, not the other way around.
