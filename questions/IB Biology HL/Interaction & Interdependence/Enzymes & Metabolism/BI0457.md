# BI0457

Enzyme A (Enz A) was immobilised on a matrix, and its activity was compared with that of a free enzyme in solution. The graph shows the relative enzyme activity (y-axis) with varying pH (x-axis).

![BI0457QB](images/BI0457QB.png)

Which of the following statements is true?

- I. $\hspace{1em}$ Both soluble Enz A and immobilised Enz A are inactive at pH values higher than 8.
- II. $\hspace{.7em}$ The immobilised Enz A demonstrates greater activity than soluble Enz A at acidic pH values.
- III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Immobilising Enz A did not decrease its catalytic activity.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I only
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
At pH 8 the relative activity of both soluble Enz A and immobilised Enz A is 0% so **Statement I** is true.

The immobilised Enz A shows greater relative activity than the soluble Enz A at pHs up to 7, so **Statement II** is true.

The immobilised Enz A showed greater or approximately the same activity as soluble Enz A at the full range of pHs, apart from pH 7.5, so **Statement III** is true.

As all three statements are true, so **Choice C** is the correct answer to this question.

**Choices A**, **B** and **D** do not include the three statements, so they are not correct.
