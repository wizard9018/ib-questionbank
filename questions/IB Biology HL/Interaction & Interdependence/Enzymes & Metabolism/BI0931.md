# BI0931

What is a characteristic that separates mammals or endotherms from ectotherms that have fluctuating body temperatures?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ They are able to absorb heat from external sources to maintain their body temperatures.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ They use the heat generated from metabolic processes in their bodies to maintain their body temperature above a set lower limit.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ They have a very efficient metabolism that does not release much waste as heat energy.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ They can survive without eating for longer periods because they do not depend on metabolising what they eat.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Endotherms tend to have a more consistent and higher baseline metabolic rate than ectotherms.   Metabolic reactions are not very efficient and so energy is dissipated as heat, therefore, **Choice C**  is incorrect. Endotherms use the heat released to maintain their body temperature above a lower set limit, therefore **Choice B** is correct.

Both ectotherms and endotherms absorb heat from external sources.  However, ectotherms rely on this heat to maintain their body temperature as they cannot maintain their own body temperature (**Choice A**).

It's the ectotherms that can survive for long periods without eating (**Choice D**) as they are not dependent on metabolising what they eat to generate heat. They also tolerate variations in internal temperatures better than endotherms.
