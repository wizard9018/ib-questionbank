# BI0650

**Maximum mark: 2**

Outline two ways enzyme catalysed reactions can be measured. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Any **two** of the following:

$\ce{ -> }$ the quantity **OR** mass **OR** volume of substrate used over time **OR** per minute **OR** per second     [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ the quantity **OR** mass **OR** volume of product formed over time **OR** per minute **OR** per second  [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ volume of gas (oxygen) released over time **OR** per minute **OR** per second, **OR** any other valid example of a specific experiment [1 mark]

[2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
The rate of reaction can be obtained by measuring the **quantity of substrate used per minute** ($\checkmark$)and the **quantity of product formed per minute**. ($\checkmark$).
