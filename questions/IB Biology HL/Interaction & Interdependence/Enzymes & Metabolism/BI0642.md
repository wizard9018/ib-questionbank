# BI0642

Which of the following describes the role of inhibitors in enzyme-catalysed reactions?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Activates the enzyme involved in the reaction.

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Facilitates binding of the substrate to the active site.
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Speeds up the rate of reaction.
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Reduces the rate of reaction.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Inhibitors  prevent enzymes from catalysing reactions and therefore decrease the rate of enzyme catalysed reactions.\
Inhibitors do not activate enzymes, thereby increasing the rate of reaction (**Choice A**).\
Inhibitors do not help the substrate bind to the active site (**Choice B**).  In fact they usually prevent the substrate from binding to the active site.\
Inhibitors decrease the rate of reaction, they do not speed it up (**Choice C**).
