# BI1397

Metabolic reactions in mammals are not completely efficient. What happens to the energy wasted during these reactions?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Used to maintain body temperature.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Used for active transport.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Used to generate action potentials.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Used for muscle contractions.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because much of the energy from metabolic processes is wasted in the form of heat, which is used to maintain a consistent body temperature in mammals (and birds).

**Choices B, C**, and **D** are incorrect because active transport, action potentials, and muscle contractions all rely on energy from ATP, produced through cellular respiration.
