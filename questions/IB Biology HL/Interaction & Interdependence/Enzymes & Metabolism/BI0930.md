# BI0930

Which of the following describes some types of extracellular enzymes?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Enzymes that are located in the mitochondria

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Enzymes that are involved in various metabolic pathways in the Krebs cycle

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Enzymes that are used for the chemical breakdown of nutrients in the digestive system

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Enzymes that are found in lysosomes

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Extracellular enzymes are secreted by cells and function outside of cells, like digestive enzymes, which break down complex molecules into simpler products that can be absorbed.

Intracellular enzymes are located inside the cell and can be cytosolic or within organelles like the mitochondria (**Choice A**)

Intracellular enzymes are involved in metabolic pathways, such as the Krebs cycle,  and catalyse reactions that contribute to the growth, maintenance and survival of organisms (**Choice B**).

Lysosomes are membrane-bound vesicles containing digestive enzymes such as glycosidases and peptidases. The enzymes are in membrane-bound sacs, mainly inside animal cells, so therefore are intracellular enzymes. (**Choice D**).

Examples of extracellular enzymes are digestive enzymes like amylases and lipases, and examples of intracellular enzymes are Krebs cycle enzymes, DNA or RNA polymerases, and hexokinases.
