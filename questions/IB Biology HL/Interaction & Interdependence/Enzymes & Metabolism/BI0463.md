# BI0463

**Maximum mark: 4**

Many enzymatic reactions happen as pathways involving multiple steps and multiple enzymes.

![BI0463](images/BI0463.png)

1. Describe how enzymes catalyze reactions. [2 marks]

2. Outline the advantages of enzyme-substrate specificity. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **two** of the following (accept specific examples if the mechanism is described correctly):

 $\ce{ -> }$ substrate binds to the active site on the enzyme [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ space of the active site is specific **OR** complementary to the shape of the substrate [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ enzymes decrease activation energy required to change a substrate into product [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the shape of the substrate changes in a way that stresses the bonds **OR** orients substrates thus facilitating bond formation [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the products are released from the active site freeing the enzyme to bind to another substrate molecule [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
 The **substrate** binds to the **active site** of the enzyme ($\checkmark$). The enzyme **changes the shape** of the substrate in a way that **stresses bonds** in the substrate ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ enzymes are specific to one substrate **OR** only one specific substrate can bind to the active site of an enzyme [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the specificity helps to control where a reaction takes place [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the specificity helps to control when reactions takes place [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the specificity helps to control which reactions should take place **OR** prevents unnecessary reactions from taking place [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
**Only one specific substrate** can bind to the **active site of an enzyme** ($\checkmark$), and this specificity helps to **control where** the reactions take place ($\checkmark$).
