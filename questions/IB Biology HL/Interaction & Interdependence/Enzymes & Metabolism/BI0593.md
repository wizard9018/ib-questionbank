# BI0593

Which of the following are involved in denaturation?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$   A change to the three dimensional structure of the protein
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$   A change in the amino acid sequence of the protein
- III. $\hspace{.4em}$   Changes in the properties of the protein

- A. $\hspace{1em}$   I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$   I and III only
- C. $\hspace{1em}$   II and III only
- D. $\hspace{1em}$   I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Denaturation impacts the three dimensional structure of a protein (**Statement I**), which in turn can impact its properties (**Statement III**). The amino acid sequence is not changed by denaturation (**Statement II**) as the peptide bonds between amino acids remain intact. **Choice B** is correct as it contains both correct statements.

**Choices A, C** and **D** are incorrect as they all include **Statement II**.
