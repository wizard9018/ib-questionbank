# BI1381

The following graph shows the relative reaction rates of several common enzymes tested at different pH values. Which of the following statements is/are correct?

![image](images/image.png)

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
 | | |
 | :-- | :-- |
 | I. | Comparing the relative activity of each enzyme at their optimum pH, amylase has the highest percentage activity. |
 | II. | Catalase functions over the smallest pH range at relative activity levels over 80%. |
 | III. | Amylase has a lower optimal pH than pepsin. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
All the enzymes have a maximum relative activity of 100% so the maximum relative activity of amylase is not higher than the other enzymes; **Statement I** is, therefore, false. Catalase does have the narrowest pH range at relative activity levels over 80% (approximately pH 8.5 -9.5) when compared to the other enzymes (pepsin 0.8 - 3.0 and amylase 4.7 - 7.2 approximately) **Statement II** is, therefore, true. Amylase has a higher, not lower, optimal pH than pepsin (pH 6 compared to pH 2), so **Statement III** is false. **Choice B** is, therefore, correct.

**Choices A, C**, and **D** are incorrect as they include one or more incorrect statements.
