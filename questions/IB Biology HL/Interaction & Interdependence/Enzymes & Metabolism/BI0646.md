# BI0646

Which of the following statements are true regarding regulation by end-product inhibition?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ The end product binds to the allosteric site of the enzyme.
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ The binding of the end product is reversible.
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ End product inhibition is an example of negative feedback regulation.

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II only
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ I, III only
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
In many metabolic reactions, the product of the last reaction acts as an inhibitor and binds to the allosteric site of the enzyme that catalyses the first reaction in the pathway (**Statement I**). The binding is reversible, and when the end product is removed, the enzyme goes back to its original conformation where the active site can bind to the substrate (**Statement II**). It is an example of negative feedback. When there is an excess of the product, the pathway is switched off. When there is less of the end product, the pathway is switched on by the detachment of the end product from the enzyme that catalyses the first reaction (**Statement III**).

Only **Choice D** has all the correct statements mentioned (I, II and III).
