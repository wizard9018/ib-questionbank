Which of the following characterises the active site of an enzyme?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ It is rigid and cannot change its shape.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$    It has a binding site for a substrate(s) and catalyses reactions.
- C. $\hspace{1em}$    It is always found in the middle of globular enzymes.
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ It is changed irreversibly in shape after binding with the substrate.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The active site of an enzyme has a binding site and a catalytic site. Once the substrate moves into the active site, the enzyme changes shape to accommodate the substrate better, making the substrate change shape so it is more likely to react and form a product.
The active site is not rigid (**Choice A**) as it can change shape slightly to fit a substrate. The active site can be found anywhere in the protein (**Choice C**) and there can be more than one active site on an enzyme. An enzyme is not changed irreversibly by the reaction (**Choice D**). Once the reaction is completed, the enzyme reverts back to its original conformation and is ready to catalyse another reaction.
