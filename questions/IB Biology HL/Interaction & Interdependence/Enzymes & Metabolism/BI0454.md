# BI0454

Which statement about enzymes is **not** true?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Enzymes are made of one or more polypeptides curled into very specific shapes.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ The active site of an enzyme can accommodate many different substrates.
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ When the substrate fits into the active site, the shape of the whole enzyme changes slightly.
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Enzymes are responsible for catalysing most metabolic reactions that occur in living cells.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The active site of an enzyme is specific to a particular substrate. All other statements are true (**Choices A**, **C** and **D**).
