 The above graph shows the effect of increasing substrate concentration on the activity of an enzyme (**no inhibitor**) and on the activity of the same **enzyme in the presence of a competitive inhibitor**.

 The **shape** of competitive inhibitors is **similar to the substrate** ($\checkmark$), so the inhibitor can also bind to the active site of the enzyme. By occupying the active site, competitive inhibitors **prevent the substrate from binding** ($\checkmark$). The rate of reaction of **enzymes with an inhibitor present will still increase with increasing substrate concentration**($\checkmark$). If substrate concentrations increase enough, the rate of reaction with an inhibitor present will reach the **same maximum rate** ($\checkmark$) as when no inhibitor is present.
