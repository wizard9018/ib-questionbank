# BI0459

Which of the following statements describes the advantages of using immobilized enzymes instead of free enzymes in the food, pharmaceutical and detergent industries?

- I. $\hspace{1em}$ Immobilization increases the stability of enzymes at a wider range of pH and temperatures.
- II. $\hspace{.7em}$    Immobilization makes reusing and recovering enzymes a simpler process.
- III. $\hspace{.4em}$    Immobilization makes the process more expensive.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$    I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$    I only
- C. $\hspace{1em}$    II only
- D. $\hspace{1em}$    I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
When an enzyme is immobilized, it is attached to material that is insoluble and inert.  In addition to increasing the stability of enzymes at a wider range of pH and temperatures, immobilizing also makes reusing and recovering enzymes easier and makes the process less expensive.

**Choices B** and **C** do not include statements I **and** II. Immobilization makes the process more expensive (**statement III**) is not an advantage.
