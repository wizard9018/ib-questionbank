# BI0641

Enzymes play a role in catalysing reactions. Which of the following statements are true?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ The substrate is temporarily provided with energy before being converted to a product.
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Enzymes reduce the activation energy needed for the reaction.
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ The substrate binds to the active site making it easier for bonds to be broken or formed.

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$  I only

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$  I, II only
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$  I, III only
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$  I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
In an enzyme-catalysed reaction, the substrate first binds to the enzyme's active site, as described in **Statement III**. This binding positions the substrate in the correct orientation and stresses the bonds in the substrate, making it easier for the bonds to be broken or formed. Once the substrate is bound to the active site, the enzyme lowers the activation energy required for the reaction to proceed. **Statement II** is true because enzymes reduce the activation energy by providing an alternative reaction pathway with a lower energy requirement, allowing the reaction to proceed more efficiently at lower temperatures. **Statement I** is true because, in reaching the transition state, the substrate is temporarily provided with energy, which helps break or form bonds. This is the activation energy needed to drive the reaction forward, and the enzyme helps reduce the amount of energy needed to reach this state. Once the reaction occurs, the product is formed and released from the active site.

**Choice D** is therefore correct because it includes all three true statements.

**Choices A, B** and **C** are incorrect because they do not include all three statements.
