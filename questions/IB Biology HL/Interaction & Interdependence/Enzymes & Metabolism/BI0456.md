# BI0456

Which of the following statements describes how enzymes interact with substrates to catalyse reactions?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ Enzymes and substrates in solution collide, and the substrate binds to the active site.

-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ There is greater substrate movement in a reaction because the substrate is smaller than the enzyme.

-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ When the substrate binds to the active site, the enzyme's shape changes slightly to accommodate it.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$  I, II and III

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only
- C. $\hspace{1em}$  I and III only
- D. $\hspace{1em}$  II only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Enzymes are biological catalysts made up of one or more polypeptide chains, which fold into a specific 3D shape. This shape is very important for the enzyme's function: to speed up the reaction, the enzyme must be able to bind the substrate. When the enzyme and substrate are in solution, they collide with each other, and successful collisions result in the substrate binding to the enzyme’s active site (**Statment I**). As the substrate binds, the active site changes shape slightly to better fit the substrate and stabilise the transition state  (**Statement III**). As **Statements I** and **III** are true, **Choice C** is the correct answer.

**Statement II** is incorrect because, in many catabolic reactions, the substrate is larger than the enzyme, for example, starch and amylase. Also, the movement of both substrates and enzymes in a solution is primarily determined by temperature and other physical factors, rather than their relative sizes. **Choices A, B** and **D** are incorrect as they include **Statement II**.
