## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because as temperature increases, the activity of enzymes, like lipase, also increases. However, when the temperature surpasses the optimum temperature of the enzyme, the enzyme rapidly denatures and the enzyme\'s activity rapidly decreases. \
**Choice A** is incorrect because above the optimum temperature the enzyme becomes denatured, thus the rate of reaction wouldn\'t continue to increase. \
**Choice B** is incorrect because the rate of reaction wouldn\'t plateau, it would start to decrease when the enzyme becomes denatured. \
**Choice D** is incorrect because the rate of reaction wouldn't be highest at the lowest temperature.
