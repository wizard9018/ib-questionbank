# BI0427

Which of the following statements is best depicted by the figure below?

![BI0427](images/image.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Reaction X is a representation of anabolic reactions.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Reaction X is a representation of catabolic reactions.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Reaction X is a representation of all metabolic reactions.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Reaction Y is a representation of anabolic reactions.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

Reaction X represents the synthesis of a more complex compound from simpler molecules and therefore is a depiction of an anabolic reaction. **Choice A** is threfore correct.

Reaction X cannot be catabolic (**Choice B**) since a more complex molecule is produced. Catabolism results in simpler products compared to the reactants. Reaction X cannot represent all metabolic reactions (**Choice C**) since metabolic reactions include both anabolic and catabolic reactions while reaction X represents only anabolic reactions. Reaction Y represents a catabolic reaction and not an anabolic reaction (**Choice D**).
