# BI0932

The mevalonate pathway is shown below. Inhibitors to the enzyme HMG- CoA reductase can lower the production of cholesterol.

![BI0932](images/BI0932.png)

Statins lower cholesterol, reduce the risk of heart attacks, and prevent strokes. They are known to inhibit HMG-CoA reductase, which is the rate-limiting enzyme of cholesterol biosynthesis in the mevalonate pathway (image above).

Which of the following describes the mechanism of action of statins in lowering cholesterol?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Statins are competitive inhibitors of HMG-CoA reductase, the rate-limiting enzyme of cholesterol biosynthesis.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Statins bind to an allosteric site on HMG – CoA reductase, an enzyme in the pathway to cholesterol biosynthesis.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Statins bind to the enzyme-substrate complex of HMG- CoA reductase and thereby inhibit enzyme activity.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Statins are irreversible inhibitors of the enzyme HMG- CoA reductase.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Statins are competitive inhibitors of the substrate for HMG-CoA reductase and bind at the active site of the enzyme (**Choice A**).

Statins do not bind at an allosteric site of the enzyme HMG-CoA reductase (**Choice B**), so they are not non-competitive inhibitors.

Statins do not inhibit by binding to the enzyme-substrate complex to inhibit activity (**Choice C**).

Statins are competitive inhibitors, so inhibition is reversible in the presence of increasing substrate concentrations (**Choice D**).
