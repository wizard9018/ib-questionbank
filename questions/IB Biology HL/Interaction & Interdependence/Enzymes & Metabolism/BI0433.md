# BI0433

Metabolic reactions can break down or synthesise biomolecules. Which type(s) of metabolic reactions are condensation reactions?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ Hydrolysis

-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Catabolic

-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Anabolic 

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$  II only

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$  III only

-    C. $\hspace{1em}$  I and II only

-    D. $\hspace{1em}$  II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

Condensation reactions are anabolic synthesis reactions in which a water molecule is produced while joining two other molecules to form one larger molecule.

Hydrolysis (**Statement I**) is an example of a catabolic reaction. Catabolic reactions (**Statement II**) are reactions in which molecules are broken down, and this does not happen via dehydration synthesis. Anabolic reactions (**Statement III**) are reactions in which larger molecules are synthesised from smaller molecules.

**Statement III** is the only true statement, making **Choice B** the correct answer to this question.

**Choices A, C** and **D** are incorrect as they all include one or more untrue statement.
