# BI0639

Which are correct descriptions of the enzyme catalysed metabolic pathways shown below? Letters represent various products of individual reactions.

![BI0639](images/BI0639.png)

| | Pathway 1 | Pathway 2 |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| Chain: Intermediate A is constantly regenerated | Chain: End product is D |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Chain: End product is D | Cyclical: Intermediate A is constantly regenerated |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Chain: Intermediate A is constantly regenerated | Cyclical: End product is D |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Cyclical: End product is D | Cyclical: Intermediate A is constantly regenerated |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
Both metabolic pathways are catalysed by a series of different enzymes. Pathway 1 proceeds in a linear manner, and the end product is D. Pathway 2 is a cycle where the various products, such as A, are constantly regenerated. \
Pathway 1 is a chain reaction and Pathway 2 is a cyclical reaction, so **Choices A** and **D** are not correct. \
The end product for Pathway 1 is D, and the products keep cycling in Pathway 2, so **Choice C** is not correct.
