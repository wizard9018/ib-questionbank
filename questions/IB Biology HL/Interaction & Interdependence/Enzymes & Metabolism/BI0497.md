# BI0497

In an experiment testing how increasing temperature affects the activity of the digestive enzyme lactase, which of the following is the most appropriate dependent variable?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Change in temperature

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Variation in pH

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Change in lactase concentration

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Rate of glucose production

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The lactase enzyme hydrolyzes the disaccharide lactose into the monosaccharides glucose and galactose. So, an appropriate dependent variable for the experiment is the rate at which one of these products is formed. Therefore, the rate of formation of glucose is an appropriate dependent variable for this experiment.

The change in temperature (**Choice A**) is the independent variable (the variable being tested) and so is not an appropriate dependent variable. pH (**Choice B**) does not change when lactose is broken down into glucose and galactose, so it would not indicate the activity of the enzyme. An enzyme is not consumed in a reaction, so there will be no change in lactase concentration (**Choice C**).
