# BI0647

Which of the following statements correctly describe(s) how penicillin helps fight infections?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ It involves the lysing of viral particles.

-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ It involves the inhibition of transpeptidase.

-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ It prevents peptide links between polysaccharide chains.

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ II only
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Penicillin is an antibiotic, not an antiviral, so it targets bacteria, not viruses; **Statement I** is false.

Bacterial cell walls are made of peptidoglycan, composed of polysaccharide chains cross-linked by peptides. Penicillin prevents the formation of peptide cross-links between these polysaccharide chains by inhibiting transpeptidase. This weakens the cell wall, leading to osmotic lysis of the bacterium; **Statements II** and **III**  are true.

Therefore, **Choice B** is the correct answer because it only includes the two true **Statements II** and **III**.

**Choices A** and **C** are incorrect because they are missing one of the true statements. **Choice D** is incorrect because it includes the false **Statement I**.
