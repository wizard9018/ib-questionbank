# BI0643

There are several ways in which inhibitors work.

Which of the following best describes the inhibitor in the image below?

![BI0643](images/BI0643.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Competitive inhibitor

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Non-competitive inhibitor
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ End-product inhibitor
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Cofactor

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
It is a competitive inhibitor because it binds to the active site which prevents the substrate from binding.\
Non-competitive inhibitors (**Choice B**) do not bind to the active site. They bind at the allosteric site which alters the structure of the active site. This prevents the substrate from binding to the active site.\
End product inhibitors (**Choice C**) also regulate enzyme activity by binding at an allosteric site.\
A cofactor (**Choice D**) is usually a non-protein molecule reversibly bound to the enzyme. Cofactors are not inhibitors and can be inorganic (e.g. zinc or iron).
