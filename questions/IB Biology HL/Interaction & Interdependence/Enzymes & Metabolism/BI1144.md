# BI1144

What would happen to the rate of an enzyme catalysed reaction at high substrate concentrations, as seen in the diagram below?

![BI1144](images/BI1144.png)
[Source: Adapted from YassineMrabet (2007, August 13). *File: KinEnzymo(en).svg* - wikimedia commons. Wikimedia commons. Retrieved September 20, 2023, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: KinEnzymo(en).svg Copyright under CC BY 3.0]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The reaction rate slows because the enzymes begin to denature.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The reaction rate speeds up because there is more substrate available.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The reaction rate reaches a plateau because the enzymes become saturated.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The reaction stops because the enzymes are used up.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
At high substrate concentrations, all enzymes are occupied (saturated) and are working at maximum capacity. Thus, the reaction rate plateaus regardless of how much excess substrate is present.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the enzymes do not denature in response to increasing substrate concentrations. **Choice B** is true up to the point where the enzymes become saturated, as depicted in the diagram. Thus, the reaction rate would not increase further and **Choice B** cannot be correct. **Choice D** is incorrect since enzymes are not used up during the reactions they catalyse.
