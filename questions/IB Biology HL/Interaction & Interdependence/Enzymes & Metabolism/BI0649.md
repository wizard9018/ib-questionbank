1. State how the activation energy is changed by adding an enzyme to a reaction. [1 mark]

2. Distinguish between anabolic and catabolic pathways. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ enzymes reduce **OR** lower the activation energy of a reaction [1 mark]

    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Enzymes bind to their substrate which **lowers** the activation energy ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ anabolic pathways take in energy **WHILE** catabolic pathways release energy [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ anabolic pathways build complex compounds from simpler compounds **WHILE** catabolic pathways break down complex compounds to form simpler compounds [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the synthesis of glycogen from glucose is an anabolic reaction, **WHILE** the breakdown of food during digestion is a catabolic reaction **OR** any other valid reaction comparisons    [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
While anabolic pathways **take in energy**, catabolic pathways **release energy** during reactions ($\checkmark$). Anabolic pathways **build complex compounds** from simple molecules, but catabolic pathways **break down** complex molecules to form simpler ones ($\checkmark$).
