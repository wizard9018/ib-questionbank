# BI0648

Which of the following would **not** be useful in determining the rate of an enzyme-catalyzed reaction?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Mass of the substrate used per unit of time.

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Volume of product formed per unit of time.
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Mass of product formed per unit of time.
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Mass of enzyme used per unit of time.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The mass of enzyme added remains the same throughout because the enzyme acts as a catalyst and does not get used up during the reaction.
All other choices (**Choices A**, **B** and **C**) are used in obtaining the rate of an enzyme-catalysed reaction since they involve measuring the substrate used or product formed over a specified amount of time.
