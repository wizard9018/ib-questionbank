## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because the activation energy with an enzyme is lower than without an enzyme. Activation energy is defined as the difference between the energy of the reactants and the highest energy point (maximum energy level) reached during the reaction.

**Choice A** is incorrect because this line represents the activation energy without an enzyme.

**Choice C** is incorrect because this line represents the activation energy with an enzyme plus the energy difference between reactants and products.

**Choice D** is incorrect because this line represents the difference between activation energy without an enzyme and activation energy with an enzyme.
