# BI0428

Which of the following processes may be described as catabolism?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Reactions between many amino acids leading to the formation of a polypeptide.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Synthesis of a starch molecule from many glucose molecules.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Digestion of a single starch molecule resulting in many molecules of maltose.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Formation of DNA molecules from nucleotides.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The digestion of starch is a catabolic reaction where starch molecules are broken down into maltose by hydrolysis.

Peptide formation from amino acids (**Choice A**), starch synthesis from glucose (**Choice B**), and DNA synthesis from nucleotides (**Choice D**) are all examples of anabolic reactions.
