# BI2441

**Maximum mark: 3**

The image below shows an example of a transamination and an oxidative deamination reaction.

![BI2441](images/BI2441.png)

1.
 Deduce whether oxidative deamination is a catabolic or anabolic reaction. [1 mark]

2. Justify your response to (a). [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ catabolic [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Oxidative deamination is a **catabolic** reaction ($\checkmark$).

2.

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ catabolic reactions involve the breakdown of macromolecules **OR** larger molecules **OR** complex molecules into monomers **OR** smaller products **OR** simpler products [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ during oxidative deamination glutamic acid is broken down into $\ce{NH_3}$ **OR** ammonium and keto acid **OR** is broken down into smaller **OR** simpler products **OR** ammonium is often released as a product of amino acid catabolism [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Catabolic reactions are when larger molecules are **broken down** into monomers or smaller molecules ($\checkmark$). Based on the diagram, glutamic acid is broken down into **ammonium and keto acid**, so this would be catabolic ($\checkmark$).
