# BI0970

**Maximum mark: 4**

The image below shows a hydrothermal vent deep in the oceans, where a diverse ecosystem is supported. A number of organisms thrive in these areas of high pressure and darkness.

![BI0970A](images/BI0970A.png)
[Source: Adapted from US Department of Commerce, N. O. and A. A. (2008, February 1). *What is an extremophile?. NOAA’s National Ocean Service*. https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/extremophile.html Copyright free CC BY 4.0]

1. State an example of a chemoautotroph that is likely to be found in the hydrothermal vent shown above.     [1 mark]

2. Compare and contrast photoautotrophs and chemoautotrophs.[3 marks]

2. Any **three** of the following (at least one from each section):

    **Similarities** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ they are both producers **OR** form the base of food chains **OR** food webs     [1 mark]
    $\ce{ -> }$ both contribute to the flow of energy **OR** nutrients in ecosystems             [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ both use carbon dioxide from their environments to synthesise organic compounds      [1 mark]

    **Differences** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ photoautotrophs convert light energy to chemical energy in the process of photosynthesis **WHEREAS** chemoautotrophs convert energy obtained from chemical reactions involving inorganic compounds into chemical energy [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ photoautotrophs are found in ecosystems like grasslands, forests, oceans, freshwater bodies **WHEREAS** chemoautotrophs are found in extreme environments living in places with no light, and having high temperatures or at high pressure [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ photoautotrophs use pigments such as chlorophyll **WHEREAS** pigments are not required for the reactions carried out by chemoautotrophs [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Both photoautotrophs and chemoautotrophs are **producers**  ($\checkmark$) and are at the base of the food chain. Both types of organisms use **carbon dioxide from their respective environments to make organic compounds** ($\checkmark$). **Photoautotrophs** convert **light energy to chemical energy** whereas **chemoautotrophs** convert the energy from **chemical reactions involving inorganic compounds to chemical energy** ($\checkmark$).
