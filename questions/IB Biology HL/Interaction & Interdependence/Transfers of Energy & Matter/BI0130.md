What kind of fuels can be formed in conditions such as those shown in the picture above?  The soil is anaerobic, acidic and waterlogged.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$  Peat

- B. $\hspace{1em}$  Crude Oil

- C. $\hspace{1em}$  Natural gas

- D. $\hspace{1em}$  Tar sands

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
When soil is waterlogged, anaerobic conditions can develop and the soil sometimes becomes acidic.  In these conditions, partially decomposed organic matter will form peat.

Crude oil is formed when organic matter buried deep in the earth is compressed by pressure and heat over millions of years. (**Choice B**)

Natural gas (**Choice C**) is found in underground shale formations and sandstone beds.
Tar sand is a mix of sand, clay, water and bitumen (used for road surfacing) (**Choice D**).
