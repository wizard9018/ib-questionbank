# BI0125

What is the name of the process that converts carbon dioxide in the air to carbohydrates?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Respiration

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Photosynthesis

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Decomposition

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Transpiration

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Photosynthesis (**Choice B**) is the process by which carbon dioxide in the air is converted to carbon compounds in green plants.

Respiration releases carbon dioxide (**Choice A**), and decomposition by decomposers  (**Choice C**) also releases carbon dioxide.
Transpiration is the process by which water is drawn up the stem of a plant to the leaves where it evaporates (**Choice D**) and is not involved directly in the carbon cycle.
