# BI1482

Which is not an energy conversion that can occur in living organisms?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Light energy into chemical energy

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Heat energy into kinetic energy

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Chemical energy to electrical energy

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Chemical energy to chemical energy

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

Living organisms cannot convert heat energy into other forms, heat energy is lost to the environment; **Choice B**  is correct.

Light energy is converted into chemical energy during photosynthesis; **Choice A**  is incorrect. Chemical energy is converted into electrical energy in nerve cells; **Choice C**  is incorrect.  Chemical energy is converted into chemical energy in chemical reactions such as respiration or photosynthesis; **Choice D**  is incorrect.
