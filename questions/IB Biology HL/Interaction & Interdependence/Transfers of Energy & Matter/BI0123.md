3. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ energy loss limits population size **OR** only small percentage **OR** approximately 10% of energy is transferred between trophic levels [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ dependence on prey species **OR** top predators rely on populations of lower trophic levels, making them vulnerable to fluctuations in prey availability **OR** animals in Level IV require large habitats to support sufficient prey populations [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ greater impact of human and environmental threats **OR** habitat destruction **OR** hunting **OR** bioaccumulation of toxins **OR** other suitable human impact disproportionately affect top predatorss [1 mark]
[2 marks]

 :u[Sample answer]:

 Animals at Level IV are most vulnerable to extinction because only a **small fraction** (about **10%**) of **energy** is **transferred between trophic levels** ($\checkmark$), leading to lower population sizes. They require **large habitats** to provide **enough prey**, making them more **susceptible to habitat loss** and fragmentation ($\checkmark$).
