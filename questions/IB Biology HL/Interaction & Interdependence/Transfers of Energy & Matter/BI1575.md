# BI1575

The diagram shows the carbon cycle.

![BI1575](images/BI1575.png)
[Adapted from *“The Carbon Cycle”*, by BioRender.com (2024). Retrieved from https://app.biorender.com/biorender-template]

Identify the processes X, Y, and Z in the diagram.

| | X | Y | Z |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Ingestion | Fossilisation | Combustion |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Egestion | Fossilisation | Evaporation |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Ingestion | Compaction | Combustion |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Egestion | Compaction | Evaporation |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because processes X, Y, and Z represent ingestion, fossilisation, and combustion, respectively. Carbon compounds are ingested by animals and converted into complex organic molecules. Following decomposition, organic matter becomes fossilised over millions of years underground. After it is extracted and refined, it is burned through combustion, releasing carbon dioxide into the atmosphere.

**Choice B** is incorrect because process X is ingestion and process Z is combustion. **Choice C** is incorrect because process Y is fossilisation, not compaction. **Choice D** is incorrect because all three processes are mislabelled.
