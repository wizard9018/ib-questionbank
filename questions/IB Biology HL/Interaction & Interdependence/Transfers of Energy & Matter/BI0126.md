# BI0126

There is continuous cycling of carbon from the atmosphere to other carbon sinks. Which is the main source of energy for the carbon cycle?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Heat

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Sunlight

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Chemical energy

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Energy from respiration

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Energy from the Sun (**Choice B**) is used to form carbon compounds through the process of photosynthesis.
Heat (**Choice A**) is a form of energy that cannot be converted to chemical energy by living organisms.

Chemical energy (**Choice C**) is held in the bonds of organic compounds which are produced by photosynthesis, or comes from hydrothermal vents deep in the oceans. Even though it is an energy source, it is not the main source of energy for the carbon cycle.

Energy stored in chemical bonds is released (**Choice D**) during respiration forming ATP which is used to power the cells metabolic processes.
