# BI1441

A researcher observed an apple with apple rot fungus growing on it for 48 hours. There was no visible water loss from the apple, and there was free movement of air. Which of the following is the most likely outcome based on the information provided?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The mass of the apple with apple rot will remain unchanged as the fungal growth balances the breakdown of apple tissue.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The mass of the apple with apple rot will increase significantly due to rapid fungal growth.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The mass of the apple with apple rot will decrease slightly due to the fungus converting apple biomass into fungal biomass and carbon dioxide.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The mass of the apple with apple rot will decrease significantly due to the fungus converting apple biomass into ethanol, carbon dioxide and ATP.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
As the fungus breaks down the apple biomass to form its own, it carries our respiration to release the energy required for the metabolic processes. We are told that conditions are aerobic, therefore the waste product is carbon dioxide, which is being released into the atmosphere. The growth of fungal biomass does not entirely offset this loss of carbon dioxide. **Choice C** is the correct answer to this question.

Because carbon dioxide and heat energy are lost to the atmosphere during respiration, as the fungus converts apple biomass into fungal biomass, the overall mass cannot increase or remain the same. **Choices A** and **B** are not the correct answers.

Ethanol is produced during anaerobic respiration in fungi, but we are told conditions are aerobic; therefore, **Choice D** is not the correct answer.
