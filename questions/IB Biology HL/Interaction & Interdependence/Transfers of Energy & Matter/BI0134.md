# BI0134

Which of the following processes in the Carbon cycle requires the longest time?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Compounds present in plankton and plants turn into fossil fuels.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Respiration breaks down organic compounds into carbon dioxide.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Photosynthesis captures solar energy and fixes CO$_2$.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Forest fires, which occur naturally and burn the underbrush clean the forest floor of debris.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Heat and pressure from the Earth's crust decompose the remains of dead plants and animals over millions of years to form oil, natural gas or coal (fossil fuels).

Respiration (**Choice B**) takes place in a relatively short time as does photosynthesis which requires light to synthesize organic compounds (**Choice  C**). Forest fires (**Choice D**) often occur naturally and release carbon dioxide into the atmosphere.  Global warming has increased the number of wildfires.
