# BI0103

Woodlice, earthworms, sea cucumbers and springtails feed on the remains of dead plants and animals.

What term describes the mode of nutrition of these organisms?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Autotrophs

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Mixotrophs

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Detritivores

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Saprotrophs

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C**  is correct because these organisms feed on detritus (dead plant and animal remains) by ingesting it (taking it into the body) and digesting it internally. Earthworms, woodlice, sea cucumbers and springtails are all examples of detritivores.

**Choice A** is incorrect because autotrophs make their own organic molecules from inorganic substances (e.g., photosynthesis in plants and chemosynthesis in some bacteria). The organisms listed are feeding on existing organic matter, so they are not autotrophs.

**Choice B** is incorrect because mixotrophs are organisms that obtain energy and nutrients through both autotrophic **and** heterotrophic modes of nutrition. The named organisms in the question can only obtain nutrients through consuming their food.

**Choice D** is incorrect because saprotrophs (e.g., many fungi and bacteria) feed on dead organic matter by external digestion: they secrete enzymes onto the material, break it down outside the body, and then absorb the soluble products. The named organisms here feed by ingestion, not external digestion and absorption.
