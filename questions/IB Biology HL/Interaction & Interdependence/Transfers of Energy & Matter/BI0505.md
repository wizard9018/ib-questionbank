# BI0505

Zooxanthellae are algae that live inside coral. Corals feed on the carbon compounds produced by zooxanthellae from carbon dioxide, water, and sunlight. Which describes the nutrition of these organisms?

| | Zooxanthellae | Coral |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Autotroph | Heterotroph |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Autotroph | Detritivore |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Heterotroph | Autotroph |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Detritivore | Heterotroph |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Zooxanthellae make carbon compounds from carbon dioxide, water and energy from sunlight. So, zooxanthellae are autotrophs (organisms that make their own food).
The coral feed on the carbon compounds produced by zooxanthellae. So, the corals are heterotrophs (organisms that must obtain carbon compounds from other organisms).

Detritivores feed on dead organic matter (**Choices B** and **D**).\
**Choice C** has the nutrition of these organisms backwards.
