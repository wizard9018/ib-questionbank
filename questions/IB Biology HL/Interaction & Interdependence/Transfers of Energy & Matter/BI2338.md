# BI2338

Which term describes biomass, peat, coal, oil and natural gas before combustion?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Trophic level

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Carbon flux

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Carbon source

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Carbon sink

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is the correct answer because before combustion, biomass, peat, coal, oil and natural gas all contain large quantities of carbon in stable forms, they are carbon sinks. Combustion changes these carbon sinks to carbon sources. Combustion moves carbon from one part of the ecosystem to another, it is a carbon flux.

**Choice A** is incorrect because a trophic level is a feeding level in a food chain.

**Choice B** is incorrect because a carbon flux is a process that moves carbon from one organism, or part of an ecosystem, to another.

**Choice C** is incorrect because a carbon source releases carbon dioxide into the atmosphere. Biomass, peat, oil and natural gas are only carbon sources when combusted.
