# BI1483

Which process releases carbon dioxide into the atmosphere?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Photosynthesis

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Peat formation

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Respiration in saprotrophs

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Ingestion

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

During respiration carbon dioxide is released into the atmosphere; **Choice C**  is correct.

Photosynthesis takes in carbon dioxide from the atmosphere; **Choice A** is incorrect. Peat formation traps carbon dioxide in waterlogged soils; **Choice B**  is incorrect. Ingestion is the consumption of carbon containing compounds; **Choice D**  is incorrect.
