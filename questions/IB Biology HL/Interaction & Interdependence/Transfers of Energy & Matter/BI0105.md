# BI0105

The illustration shows a rice cultivation system that eliminates the need for fertilizers, herbicides and pesticides by using ducks in the organic cultivation of rice. In addition, *Azolla*, an aquatic floating fern, is grown on these fields because it harbours blue-green algae which can fix atmospheric nitrogen. Using this system, rice, ducks, and fish can all be produced for human consumption in one location.

![Picture 1](<images/Picture 1.png>)

Which option includes two of the autotrophs in the system?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ *Azolla* and rice

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ *Azolla* and ducks

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Rice and fish

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Fish and ducks

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Ducks and fish are consumers (**Choice B, C and D**), which is a type of heterotroph, whereas *Azolla* and rice can carry out photosynthesis and are, therefore, autotrophs (self-feeding).
