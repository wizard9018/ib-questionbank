# BI1231

Phytoplankton, shown below, are organisms that obtain their energy from the sun and live in the ocean and freshwater systems. What type of nutrition do they use?

![BI1231](images/BI1231.png)
[Source: Adapted from Anderson, S. (2018, August 15). *Phytoplankton*. Wikipedia.  Retrieved September 18, 2023, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phytoplankton. Copyright free]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Heterotrophic

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Autotrophic

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Saprotrophic

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Detritivorous

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Organisms that use autotrophic nutrition make their own food, often using photosynthetic mechanisms; **Choice B**  is correct.

Organisms that use heterotrophic nutrition feed on other organisms for nutrition; **Choice A** is incorrect. Organisms that use saprotrophic nutrition feed on dead organic matter and digest it externally; **Choice C**  is incorrect. Organisms that are detritivorous also feed on dead organic matter but digest it internally; **Choice D**  is incorrect.
