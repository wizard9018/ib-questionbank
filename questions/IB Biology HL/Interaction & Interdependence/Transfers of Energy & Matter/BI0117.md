# BI0117

The figure below shows the flow of energy per unit area per year in arbitrary units. Gross production is the total kJ obtained through photosynthesis, and net production is the kJ remaining after respiration.

![bi0117](images/bi0117.png)

What percentage of net production (kJ after respiration) is lost to decomposers by primary consumers?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 100%

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 75%

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 50%

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 25%

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The primary consumers lose 1200 kJ out of 1600 kJ to decomposers, which translates to 75%.
