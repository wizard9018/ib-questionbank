# BI0113

The illustration shows a rice cultivation system that eliminates the need for fertilizers, herbicides and pesticides by raising ducks in rice paddies. Additionally, *Azolla*, an aquatic floating fern, is grown in these fields because they harbour blue-green algae that fixes N$_2$. This system produces duck, fish and rice for human consumption.

![Picture 1](<images/Picture 1.png>)

What would be a possible food chain for the rice cultivation system shown in the image?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ algae $\rightarrow$ fish $\rightarrow$ duck $\rightarrow$ human

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ algae $\leftarrow$ fish $\leftarrow$ duck $\leftarrow$ human

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ insects $\rightarrow$ algae $\rightarrow$ duck $\rightarrow$ human

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ insects $\leftarrow$ algae $\leftarrow$ duck $\leftarrow$ human

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Algae is a producer, (**Choice B**), but the arrows show the flow of energy in the wrong direction.
The food chain cannot start with insects (**Choice C**) as it is not an autotroph. Algae do not eat ducks and ducks do not eat humans (**Choice D**).
