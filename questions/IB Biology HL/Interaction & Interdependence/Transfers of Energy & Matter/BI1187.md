# BI1187

The energy pyramid represents organisms in a tropical grassland. What is a reason that the lions have less energy available to them than the giraffes?

![BI1187](images/BI1187.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Energy is lost as heat, a waste product of respiration, at each level

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ There are a higher number of giraffes in tropical grasslands than lions

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Giraffes have a higher rate of respiration than lions

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Giraffes can derive more energy from acacia trees than lions are able to from hyenas

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
There is a loss of energy between trophic levels for a number of reasons including heat from respiration; **Choice A** is correct.

The size of the trophic levels in pyramids of energy do not represent the number of organisms; **Choice B** is incorrect. Pyramids of energy offer no information on respiration rates; **Choice C** is incorrect. Approximately 90% of energy is lost between trophic levels, regardless of the organisms involved; **Choice D** is incorrect.
