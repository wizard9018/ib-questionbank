# BI1151

The image below shows a pyramid of energy for a grassland ecosystem.

![BI1151](images/BI1151.png)

Which organism(s) in the diagram synthesise carbon compounds from simple inorganic substances?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ IV only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II, III, and IV

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II, III, and IV

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Only autotrophs can synthesize their own carbon compounds from inorganic substances. Autotrophs are always found at the base of a pyramid of energy, so **Choice B** is the correct response.

**Choices A, C**, and **D** cannot be correct as they all involve heterotrophs.
