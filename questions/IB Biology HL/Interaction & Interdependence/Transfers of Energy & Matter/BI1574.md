# BI1574

The diagram shows a biomass pyramid for an arctic ecosystem.

![BI1574ii](images/BI1574ii.png)

Which of the following causes the biomass of organisms to decrease at the higher trophic levels?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Decreased primary productivity due to competition for resources.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Energy loss through respiration and heat production.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Enhanced efficiency of energy transfer through the food chain.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Elevated rates of predation and mortality among higher trophic levels.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because biomass is lost at each trophic level due to the inefficiency of energy transfer resulting from heat generation and respiration. Only a fraction of the energy contained within each trophic level is transferred to the level above it.

**Choice A** is incorrect because decreased primary productivity would lead to a reduced number of autotrophs, which would affect all trophic levels equally, not just the highest ones. **Choice C** is incorrect because energy efficiency does not increase at higher trophic levels. **Choice D** is incorrect as rates of predation and mortality should be relatively consistent at each trophic level in a balanced ecosystem.
