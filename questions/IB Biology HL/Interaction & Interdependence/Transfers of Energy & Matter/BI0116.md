## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
In an ecosystem, energy pyramids illustrate how energy decreases as it moves through trophic levels. Producers have the most energy, converting sunlight into chemical energy through photosynthesis. Primary consumers gain only about 10% of the energy from producers, and this pattern continues up the trophic levels, with only 10% of energy passing to the next level.
**Choice C** correctly represents this, with the base (producers) widest and each successive level narrower.

**Choice A** is upside down.
**Choice B** has the primary consumer level too narrow.
**Choice D** has incorrect secondary and tertiary consumer levels.
