# BI1484

The graph shows the annual carbon emissions by different global regions. Which conclusion is correct?

![BI1484i](images/BI1484i.png)

[Source: Adapted from DB, P. (2009, January 29). File: Carbon Emissions by Country.jpg. Wikimedia Commons. Retrieved February 11 , 2024,  from https://en.wikiversity.org/wiki/File:Carbon_Emissions_by_Country.jpg. Copyright by CC2.0]

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Asia and Oceania contribute the most towards climate change

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ There is a steady increase in carbon emissions in North America between 1980 and 2005

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Europe has the lowest carbon emissions between 2000 and 2005

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Eurasia burns the least fossil fuels compared to other regions

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

The graph shows that there is a steady increase in carbon emissions in North America between 1980 and 2005. **Choice B** is correct.

Although there is a correlation between carbon emissions and climate change, a direct causation cannot be concluded based on this data alone. Also, although Asia and Oceania have higher emissions than the other global regions in later years, they were below the other regions in previous time periods; **Choice A**  is incorrect. **Choice C**  is incorrect as between 2000 and 2005, Eurasia has the lowest carbon emissions, not Europe. **Choice D**  is incorrect as carbon emissions do not solely come from the burning of fossil fuels.
