# BI1152

Which of the organisms in the food web below occupies more than one trophic level?

![BI1152correcti](images/BI1152correcti.png)
[Source: Adapted from Cmglee. (2003, October). *File: Arctic Food Web.svg* - Wikimedia Commons. Wikimedia Commons. Retrieved April 14, 2022, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Arctic_food_web.svg. Copyright under CC BY-SA 3.0]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Arctic char

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Carnivorous zooplankton

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Arctic cod

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Capelin

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Arctic cod feed on both herbivorous zooplankton (primary consumers) and carnivorous zooplankton (secondary consumers). This means that Arctic cod function as both secondary and tertiary consumers, occupying both the third and fourth trophic levels. Therefore, **Choice C** is correct.

Arctic char feed only on carnivorous zooplankton, which are secondary consumers. This means Arctic char are tertiary consumers in this food web, occupying only the fourth trophic level. **Choice A** is incorrect because Arctic char do not feed at multiple trophic levels.
In this food web, carnivorous zooplankton feed exclusively on herbivorous zooplankton, which are primary consumers. As a result, they are secondary consumers and occupy the third trophic level. **Choice B** is incorrect because carnivorous zooplankton only occupy one trophic level.
Capelin feed only on carnivorous zooplankton, which are secondary consumers. This means Capelin function as tertiary consumers, occupying only the fourth trophic level. **Choice D** is incorrect because Capelin also do not occupy more than one trophic level.
