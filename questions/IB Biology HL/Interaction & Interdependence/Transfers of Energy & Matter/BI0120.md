# BI0120

What units are typically used for pyramids of energy?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Calories

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Kilocalories

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ kJm$^{-2}$

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ kJm$^{-2}$y$^{-1}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Calories (**Choice A**) and Kilocalories (**Choice B**) are measures for energy but do not include area and time which is how productivity is measured in an ecosystem.  kJm$^{-2}$ (**Choice C**) does include the area but not time. To see energy values over a specified time, usually a year, the units kJm$^{-2}$y$^{-1}$ are used.
