# BI0971

**Maximum mark: 6**

The image shows a food web in a salt marsh ecosystem.

1. Identify all the detritivores in the food web shown above.[1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ transfer of energy from one trophic level to another is not 100% efficient [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ energy is lost as heat **OR** during respiration **OR** during metabolic reactions [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ not all parts of organism digestible **OR** herbivores do not assimilate all the energy from plant materials that they consume OR lack of break down of cellulose **OR** lignin by consumers **OR** parts of animal are not digested OR suitable example [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ not all parts of organism are eaten [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ waste production **OR** loss of energy in faeces **OR** urine **OR** egestion **OR** excretion [1 mark]

4. Any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ from a one metre squared area **OR** suitable sized area harvest all above plant material from that area [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ dry the plant material in an oven until it reaches constant mass and record mass [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ one month later repeat the process removing the crop from same sized in same field [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ calculate gross primary production by calculating increase in biomass per m:sup[2] per month **OR** GPP = (total dry biomass of second sample - total dry biomass of first sample) / area for the month [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
