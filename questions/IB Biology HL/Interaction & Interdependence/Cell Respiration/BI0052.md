# BI0052

Which of the following requires ATP as an immediate source of energy?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Transmission of a nerve impulse |
  | II. | Osmosis |
  | III. | Condensation of a polypeptide |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$  I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$  I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$  II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$  I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
ATP from cell respiration is an immediate source of energy for metabolic processes.

Neurons transmit information in the form of electrical impulses (**Statement I**). This transmission can only happen when there is a difference in concentrations of Na:sup[+] and K:sup[+] ions across the axonal membrane of a neuron. Energy in the form of ATP is required for the Na:sup[+]/K:sup[+] pump to restore the ionic balance after the voltage-gated response.

Osmosis (**Statement II**) does not require ATP. It is a passive transport process where water can move through a partially permeable membrane from an area of lower solute concentration to an area of higher solute concentration.

The condensation reaction of a polypeptide (**Statement III**) is the joining of amino acids together to form a polypeptide. It is an anabolic reaction where ATP is required to join amino acids together to form a peptide bond, and water is produced.
