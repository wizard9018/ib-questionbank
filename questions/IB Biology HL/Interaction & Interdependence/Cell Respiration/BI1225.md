# BI1225

The graph shows the results from a study on the effect of temperature on the respiration rate of germinating seeds, using a respirometer, which measures the volume of carbon dioxide produced over time. What can be concluded from the results?

![BI1225i](images/BI1225i.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The rate of respiration decreases as the concentration of glucose decreases

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The rate of respiration is fastest between 0 and 10 °C

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ As temperature increases, the rate of respiration also increases

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Once the temperature is above 40 °C, the rate of respiration decreases

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Once the temperature is above 40°C the graph begins to fall, indicating that the rate of respiration is beginning to decrease. **Choice D** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect, as the graph does not refer to glucose concentration. **Choice B** is incorrect as the highest rate of respiration is at 40°C, where the graph reaches its peak. **Choice C** is incorrect, as this statement is only true between 0 and 40°C, after 40°C the rate of respiration falls.
