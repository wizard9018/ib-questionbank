# BI0156

Which of the following processes involve decarboxylation?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | Phosphorylation of glucose |
  | II. | Oxidation of pyruvate to form acetyl CoA |
  | III. | Oxidation of an acetate group to reform the four-carbon oxaloacetate |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Decarboxylation is a reaction where carbon dioxide is lost. There are a total of three decarboxylation reactions in the stages of aerobic cellular respiration. One of the decarboxylations happens in the link reaction where pyruvate gets oxidised to acetyl CoA (**Statement II**).

The other decarboxylation reactions happen in the Krebs cycle. The acetyl CoA which is produced in the link reaction then gets oxidised and the 4 carbon oxaloacetate is reformed (**Statement III**). Here, carbon dioxide is removed again.

Phosphorylation of glucose (**Statement I**) takes place before the link reaction, where glucose is converted to pyruvate. Here, no carbon dioxide is removed so it is incorrect.
