A mitochondrion is shown in the diagram. Which letter indicates the site where protons accumulate prior to the formation of ATP?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The concentration of H:sup[+] ions is the highest in the intermembrane space of the mitochondria (the site where protons build-up to establish the concentration gradient). The intermembrane space is pointed to by **Choice A**.

![BI0520A](images/BI0520A.png)

The outer membrane (**Choice B**) separates the interior of the mitochondrion from the rest of the cell. The mitochondrial matrix (**Choice C**) contains enzymes for the Krebs cycle. The inner membrane (**Choice D**) contains the electron transport chain and ATP synthases.
