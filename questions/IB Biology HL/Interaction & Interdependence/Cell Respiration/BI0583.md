# BI0583

The graph below shows the changes in the levels of lactate on the skin of athletes during pre-training (rest), training, and recovery.

![BI0583QBi](images/BI0583QBi.png)

Based on the graph, which is true?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ There was no lactate during pre-training because there was no cellular respiration.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ As exercise intensity increased, anaerobic respiration increased.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ During periods of exercise, lactate levels remained constant.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ During the recovery periods, aerobic and anaerobic respiration continuously increased.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** – A is wrong because there was approximately 0.39 mmol L$^{-1}$ during rest.

**Choice B** - During the training period, there was an increase in the intensity of exercise which required an increase in ATP synthesis. Since the ATP output of aerobic respiration was inadequate, the body also used anaerobic respiration to produce ATP. The increased rate of anaerobic respiration was evidenced by the increasing levels of lactate on the skin.

**Choice C** – During the training period, lactate levels increased because the intensity of exercise increased.

**Choice D** – During the recovery period, lactate levels decreased because the body was transitioning back to utilising aerobic respiration only.
