# BI0153

In which step of respiration is pyruvate decarboxylated?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Glycolysis

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Link reaction

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Krebs cycle

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Oxidative phosphorylation

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
In glycolysis (**Choice A**), glucose is broken down into pyruvate and this happens in the cytoplasm. Glycolysis is incorrect because pyruvate is only produced at the end of glycolysis and is not converted to acetyl CoA.

Pyruvate produced during glycolysis enters the mitochondria where it is decarboxylated and converted to acetyl CoA during the link reaction. (**Choice B**)

The Link reaction (**Choice B**) is the reaction that bridges glycolysis with the next stage which is the Krebs cycle (**Choice C**). Hence, the Krebs cycle is incorrect because acetyl CoA is the reactant and needs to be produced prior to entering the Krebs cycle.

The stage after the Krebs cycle is known as oxidative phosphorylation (**Choice D**) which involves the synthesis of ATP.
