# BI2034

**Maximum mark: 10**

The image shows a respirometer that could be used to measure the rate of respiration in seeds. The experiment was used to compare the respiration rates at different temperatures in ml min$^{-1}$.

![BI2034](images/BI2034.png)

1. Explain the purpose of the soda lime in this experiment. [2 marks]

2. Outline how the rate of oxygen uptake of seeds can be calculated using the experimental set up seen in the diagram if the experiment were run for 5 minutes. [3 marks]

3. Suggest one variable that should be controlled in this study. [1 mark]

The graph shows how much oxygen is consumed by germinating and non-germinating seeds at two different temperatures, over 5 minutes.

![BI2034b](images/BI2034b.png)

4. Suggest reasons for the trends seen in the graph. [2 marks]

5. Calculate the percentage increase in oxygen consumption by germinating seeds, over five minutes, due to the 10 $\degree$C increase in temperature. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ to absorb the carbon dioxide **OR** $\ce{CO_2}$ produced by the seeds [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ allows volume of oxygen used in respiration can be indirectly measured **OR** any volume change in the respirometer reflects oxygen consumption [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The purpose of the soda lime is to **absorb the carbon dioxide** produced by the respiring seeds ($\checkmark$), so that the **oxygen uptake** is the only variable causing the **syringe to move** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ record the initial volume of oxygen in the syringe [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ after five minutes, record the new volume of oxygen in the syringe [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ subtract the initial volume of oxygen from the final volume of oxygen to determine volume of oxygen uptake **OR** oxygen consumption [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ divide volume of oxygen consumed in five minutes by five to calculate the rate [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Rate of oxygen uptake = $\dfrac{\ce{Initial vol (ml) - final volume (ml)}}{\ce{5\ min}}$

 The **initial volume of the oxygen** in the syringe should be recorded ($\checkmark$). After the experiment has run for **five minutes** the **new volume of oxygen** in the syringe should be recorded ($\checkmark$). The **initial volume** of oxygen should be **subtracted** from the **final volume** of oxygen to calculate the volume of oxygen taken up in five minutes ($\checkmark$). This **volume** is then **divided by five** to give the oxygen consumption rate in **volume per minute** ($\checkmark$).

4. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ germinating seeds consume more oxygen than non-germinating seeds indicating they are more metabolically active **OR** are growing **OR** undergoing cell division **OR** non-germinating seeds consume less oxygen than germinating seeds indicating minimal metabolic activity **OR** they are dormant and require less oxygen for respiration [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ both germinating and non-germinating seeds consume more oxygen at 30℃ compared to 20$\degree$C indicating respiration is occurring at a higher rate due to increased enzyme activity **OR** indicating that 30℃ is closer to optimum temperature of respiratory enzymes [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the rate of oxygen consumption for each condition is consistent **OR** constant over time indicating the energy needs **OR** respiration rate of seeds is stable throughout the duration of the study [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 **Germinating seeds** consume **more oxygen** than non-germinating seeds, **indicating** they are **more metabolically active**, growing, or undergoing cell division, while **non-germinating** seeds consume less oxygen, indicating **minimal metabolic activity** or dormancy ($\checkmark$). **Both** germinating and non-germinating seeds consume **more oxygen** at **30**$\degree$**C** compared to 20$\degree$C, suggesting **respiration** occurs at a **higher rate** due to **increased enzyme activity** or that **30**$\degree$**C** is **closer** to the **optimum temperature** for respiratory enzymes ($\checkmark$).

5. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ $\dfrac{\ce{Final - Initial}}{\ce{Initial}} \times 100\%$ **OR** $\dfrac{\ce{12\ ml - 10\ ml}}{\ce{10\ ml}} \times 100\%$ [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ 20 (allow in range 18 - 22, % not required for mark) [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:

 ![AnsExp](images/AnsExp.png)

 Percentage increase = $\dfrac{\ce{Final - Initial}}{\ce{Initial}} \times 100\%$

 percentage increase in oxygen consumption from 20$\degree$C to 30$\degree$C = $\dfrac{\textbf{12\ ml - 10\ ml}}{\textbf{10\ ml}} \times$ **100**% ($\checkmark$)

 $\hspace{27em}$ = $\dfrac{\ce{2\ ml}}{\ce{10\ ml}} \times 100\%$\
 $\hspace{27em}$ = 0.2 $\times$ 100 %\
 $\hspace{27em}$ = **20** % ($\checkmark$)
