# BI1228

During which step of respiration is acetyl-CoA formed?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Krebs cycle

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Glycolysis

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Link reaction

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Oxidative phosphorylation

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
During the link reaction pyruvate is decarboxylated and oxidised to form an acetyl group which is then combined with acetyl coenzyme A to form acetyl-CoA. **Choice C**  is correct.

**Choices A, B** and **D**  are incorrect because they do not involve acetyl-CoA.
