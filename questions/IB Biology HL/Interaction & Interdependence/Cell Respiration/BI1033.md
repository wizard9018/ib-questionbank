# BI1033

The graph shows the total volume of oxygen consumed by a cricket over 1 minute, measured using a respirometer. What is the mean rate of respiration of the cricket?

![BI1033](images/BI1033.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.01 mm:sup[3] s:sup[-1]

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.1 mm:sup[3] s:sup[-1]

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 1 mm:sup[3] s:sup[-1]

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 10 mm:sup[3] s:sup[-1]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The graph is linear, so the respiration rate can be calculated by choosing a point on the y-axis and dividing it by the corresponding point on the x-axis.

![BI1033ANSA](images/BI1033ANSA.png)

Rate of respiration = Total volume of oxygen consumed ÷ time

$\hspace{8em}$ = 4 mm:sup[3] ÷ 40 s

$\hspace{8em}$ = 0.1 mm:sup[3] s:sup[-1]

**Choice B** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect as to get 0.01 mm:sup[3] s:sup[-1], 4 mm:sup[3] would have to be collected over 400 seconds. **Choice C** is incorrect as to get 1 mm:sup[3] s:sup[-1], 4 mm:sup[3] would have to be collected over 4 seconds, and **Choice D** is incorrect as to get 10 mm:sup[3] s:sup[-1], 40 mm:sup[3] would have to be collected over 4 seconds.
