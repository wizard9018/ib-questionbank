# BI1426

What results from the hydrolysis of ATP?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$  The products ADP and water
- B. $\hspace{1em}$  The products oxygen, hydrogen, phosphorus, carbon and nitrogen
- C. $\hspace{1em}$  The product pyruvate with the release of energy
- D. $\hspace{1em}$  The products ADP and inorganic phosphate with the release of energy

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Cellular respiration is the process by which chemical energy in organic compounds is transferred to chemical energy in adenosine triphosphate (ATP). ATP consists of the nitrogenous base adenine, a ribose sugar and three phosphate groups. These three components contain the elements oxygen, hydrogen, phosphorus, carbon and nitrogen. Even though ATP is made up of these five elements, they are not products of hydrolysis; therefore, **Choice B** is not correct.

ATP allows energy to be readily available for cellular processes. When ATP undergoes hydrolysis the energy which is stored in the third phosphate bond of ATP is released. This hydrolysis reaction leads to the formation of adenosine diphosphate (ADP) and inorganic phosphate. **Choice D** is, therefore, the correct answer.

Hydrolysis reactions require water molecules, they do not result in water molecules being formed, therefore **Choice A** is incorrect. Pyruvate is the product of glycolysis, not the product of the hydrolysis of ATP, therefore **Choice C** is incorrect.
