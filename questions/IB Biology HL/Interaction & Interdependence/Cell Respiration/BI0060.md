# BI0060

The graph shows the concentration of oxygen inside a chamber containing yeast cells and substrates. The experimental investigation compared the rate of oxygen consumption by the yeast cells during aerobic respiration using three different substrates: sucrose, fructose, and glucose.

![BI0060QBi](images/BI0060QBi.png)

What can be concluded from the graph?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The hydrolysis of sucrose forms glucose and fructose

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The breakdown of glucose and fructose is much slower than sucrose

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The rate of cellular respiration decreases the more complex the substrate

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The cumulative number of oxygen molecules consumed by the yeast cells increases over time

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The graph shows that oxygen concentration decreases over time, indicating that oxygen is being consumed as the yeast cells perform aerobic respiration with each substrate. Since oxygen is required for aerobic respiration, this depletion aligns with a continuous and cumulative oxygen usage over time. **Choice D** is, therefore, correct.

Although it's a biochemical fact that sucrose hydrolyses into glucose and fructose, this specific information cannot be inferred directly from the oxygen consumption data presented in the graph. The graph only shows how much oxygen is used, not the biochemical breakdown of sucrose. **Choice A** is, therefore, incorrect as it cannot be deduced from the graph alone.

The graph shows that all three substrates lead to similar oxygen consumption rates, with lines of best fit closely following each other. This indicates that metabolic breakdown does not vary significantly among the substrates. **Choice B** is incorrect.

The statement suggests that the rate of respiration decreases with substrate complexity, which would mean that sucrose, the more complex molecule, should be metabolised more slowly. However, the graph shows that oxygen is consumed slightly faster with sucrose, contradicting the idea that the respiration rate decreases with substrate complexity. Therefore, **Choice C** is also incorrect.
