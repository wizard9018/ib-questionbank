## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The direction of the movement of the coloured liquid in the manometer would be up the right side where the germinating seed is and down on the left side where the control tube is (**Choice A**). In the tube containing the germinating seed, the living organism undergoes respiration where oxygen is consumed and carbon dioxide and water are produced. The alkali at the bottom of the test tube functions to absorb carbon dioxide.

The test tube on the left side without the germinating seed acts as a control. There is no oxygen uptake and no carbon dioxide produced because there is no living organism present.

When oxygen uptake in the tube containing the germinating seed occurs, the amount of oxygen gas in the tube decreases. This causes the air pressure inside the tube containing the germinating seed to decrease. Inside the manometer, the higher air pressure in the tube without the germinating seed will then push the coloured liquid in the direction of the tube containing the germinating seed. Hence, the coloured liquid on the right side will move up towards the tube containing the germinating seed and the coloured liquid on the left side will move down.
