# BI0997

Which of the following processes does not require energy from ATP?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Sodium-potassium pumps

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Facilitated diffusion

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Endocytosis

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Anabolic reactions

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Facilitated diffusion is a passive process that allows the movement of molecules across a cell membrane with the help of transport proteins. It does not require energy input (ATP) because molecules move down their concentration gradient. Therefore **Choice B** is correct.

Sodium-potassium pumps (**Choice A**) actively transport sodium ions out of cells and potassium ions into cells against their concentration gradients. This process requires energy in the form of ATP.
Endocytosis (**Choice C**) is an active process by which cells engulf substances by invagination of the cell membrane to form a vesicle around them. This process requires energy in the form of ATP.
Anabolic reactions (**Choice D**) involve the synthesis of complex molecules from more simple ones. These reactions typically require energy input in the form of ATP.
