3. any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ carbon dioxide causes the bread to rise **OR** prevents the formation of a flat bread, **OR** causes the dough to increase in volume  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ carbon dioxide forms pockets of gas, **OR** holes, in the dough **OR** carbon dioxide causes bread to be spongy, **OR** dough is leavened, **OR** dough is less dense [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Yeast undergoes anaerobic respiration to produce carbon dioxide and ethanol, which are important products needed to make food and beverages. In the making of bread, the carbon dioxide produced by the yeast is trapped inside the dough of the bread as **pockets of gas** ($\checkmark$). The carbon dioxide gas expands and causes the **bread to rise** ($\checkmark$).

4. any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ anaerobic respiration is better suited **BECAUSE** it can proceed in the absence of oxygen [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ during high-intensity exercise, demand for ATP exceeds the amount that can be produced by aerobic respiration per unit time [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ aerobic respiration is better suited **BECAUSE** it produces a high yield of ATP per glucose molecule, which powers muscle contractions     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ anaerobic respiration is less suited to sustained activity **BECAUSE** it produces lactate which can lower blood pH [1 mark]
[3 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    During the initiation of intense exercise, oxygen is supplied to muscle cells so they can respire aerobically.  **Aerobic respiration produces a high yield of ATP** ($\checkmark$) which is used to initially power muscle contractions.  However, high-intensity exercise **demands more ATP per unit time**  than aerobic respiration can produce alone ($\checkmark$).  Therefore **anaerobic respiration is better suited**  to meeting the additional demand during short bursts of  high-intensity exercise because it **does not require oxygen** ($\checkmark$) and produces ATP.
