# BI0054

Which substance is present in the brewing of beer but not found in baked bread?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Carbon dioxide

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Lactic acid

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Ethanol

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Water

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because while both processes involve ethanol fermentation by yeast, the baking process involves high temperatures that cause the ethanol to evaporate. Therefore, ethanol remains as a primary constituent of beer but is not found in the final baked bread.

**Choice A** is incorrect because carbon dioxide is present in both. In beer, it provides carbonation; in bread, it creates the air pockets that remain even after baking.

**Choice B** is incorrect because lactic acid is a product of a different type of anaerobic respiration (lactic acid fermentation) found in humans and certain bacteria, not the yeast fermentation used in standard brewing or bread-making.

**Choice D** is incorrect because water is a main ingredient and component of both the final liquid beer and the moisture content within baked bread.
