# BI1032

The diagram shows a respirometer that is being used to measure the oxygen consumption rate in germinating seeds. In 2 minutes, the ink in the syringe moved by 3 ml.

What is the rate of respiration of the germinating seeds in ml s:sup[-1]?

![BI1032-SLHL](images/BI1032-SLHL.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 1.5 ml s:sup[-1]

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 0.025 ml s:sup[-1]

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 6 ml s:sup[-1]

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 360 ml s:sup[-1]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The respiration rate is calculated by dividing the change in ink volume (3 ml) by the time (2 minutes = 120 seconds); 3ml ÷ 120s = 0.025 ml s:sup[-1], **Choice B** is correct.

The answer can be worked out by eliminating incorrect answers. **Choice A** is incorrect because 1.5 mL s⁻¹ is obtained by dividing 3 mL by 2 minutes without converting minutes into seconds. **Choice C** is incorrect, because 6 mL s⁻¹ is far greater than the measured oxygen consumption rate and does not result from the correct calculation. **Choice D** is incorrect because 360 mL s⁻¹ is an unrealistically large value and is not obtained using the correct method.
