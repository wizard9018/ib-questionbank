## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

The stage that takes place in the cytoplasm is glycolysis where glucose is converted to pyruvate (**Choice D**).

**Choice A** is the Krebs cycle where acetyl CoA is oxidized in the mitochondrial matrix. **Choice B** is the link reaction where pyruvate gets converted to acetyl CoA. The link reaction occurs before the Krebs cycle. **Choice C** is the electron transport chain where most of the ATP is produced and takes place on the cristae of the mitochondria.
