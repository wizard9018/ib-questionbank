# BI1567

Why is yeast often included in bread recipes?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The production of ethanol keeps the bread from drying out.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The production of carbon dioxide makes the bread rise.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The production of glucose makes the bread sweeter.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The production of lactic acid makes the bread soft by denaturing proteins.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because the products of anaerobic respiration in yeast are ethanol and carbon dioxide. The ethanol evaporates during baking while the carbon dioxide creates bubbles, causing the bread to rise.

**Choice A** is incorrect because ethanol evaporates during the baking process and does not prevent drying. **Choice C** is incorrect because glucose is a substrate of respiration, not a product. **Choice D** is incorrect because lactic acid is a product of anaerobic respiration in humans, not yeast.
