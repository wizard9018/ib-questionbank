# BI1036

Which statement(s) are correct in explaining why lipids are used as a long-term energy store instead of carbohydrates?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Lipids contain more energy per gramme than carbohydrates |
    | II. | Lipids are insoluble, whereas carbohydrates are not |
    | III. | Lipids contain less oxygen than carbohydrates |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I**  is correct, as lipids transfer around twice as much energy as carbohydrates during respiration. **Statement II**  is correct as lipids are insoluble, so they can be stored without affecting the osmotic balance of tissues, whereas carbohydrates are soluble. **Statement III**  is correct, as lipids contain less oxygen than carbohydrates, allowing them to store more energy in more carbon-hydrogen bonds. **Choice D**  is correct.

**Choices A, B** and **C**  are incorrect as they do not include all three statements.
