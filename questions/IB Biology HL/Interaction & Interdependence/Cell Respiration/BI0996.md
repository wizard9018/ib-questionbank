# BI0996

Which of the following diagrams shows the correct structure of ATP?

![BI0996a](images/BI0996a.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct, as ATP is short for adenosine triphosphate. As the name tells us, it has three phosphate groups, represented by the three circles attached to the central ribose sugar. The rectangle represents the adenine.

**Choice A** is a cholesterol molecule, **Choice B** is a glucose molecule, and **Choice D** is a molecule of ADP (adenosine diphosphate), which is the molecule that results when ATP loses its third phosphate molecule, releasing energy.
