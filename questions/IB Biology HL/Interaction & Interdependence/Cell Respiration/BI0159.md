# BI0159

The Krebs cycle is one of the stages in aerobic cellular respiration. Which of the following correctly describes the electron carriers?

| | Electron carriers | Oxidation or Reduction |Number of times produced in one cycle |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | NAD:sup[+] | Reduced to NADH | 1 |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | FAD:sup[+]| Oxidized to FADH:sub[2] | 3 |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | NAD:sup[+] | Oxidized to NADH | 3 |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | FAD:sup[+] | Reduced to FADH:sub[2] | 1 |

*Note: Starting May 2028, this question may be assessed differently due to changes to the Biology guide.*

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
In one turn of the Krebs cycle, the production of NADH occurs three times and the production of FADH:sub[2] happens once. Both NAD:sup[+] and FAD:sup[+] have to undergo reduction where they gain electrons and hydrogen ions to form NADH and FADH:sub[2]. Oxidation is the process where a substance loses electrons and hydrogen. NAD:sup[+] and FAD:sup[+] are the oxidised forms whereas NADH and FADH:sub[2] are the reduced forms.
