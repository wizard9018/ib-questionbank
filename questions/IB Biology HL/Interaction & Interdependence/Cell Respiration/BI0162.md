    :u[Sample answer]:

        Oxidative phosphorylation consists of the electron transport chain and chemiosmosis. In the electron transport chain, **NADH is oxidised** ($\checkmark$) as it **loses electrons to electron carriers** ($\checkmark$).  The movement of electrons from one electron carrier to the next releases energy which is used to pump hydrogen ions into the intermembrane space, creating a **proton gradient** ($\checkmark$).

4. any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ organism could not use all the energy at once [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ large amounts of energy would be released all at once **OR** not released in small, controlled amounts [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ glycolysis **OR** Krebs cycle **OR** anaerobic respiration would not happen [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ heat energy would be released all at once **OR** cell temperature cannot be maintained OR cell temperature would increase [1 mark]
[3 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

        Cellular respiration is the controlled release of energy from organic compounds to produce ATP.  Without a series of controlled steps, large amounts of energy would be released **all at once** ($\checkmark$), and this huge amount of energy would be mostly **lost as heat** and not trapped in ATP or electron carriers step-by-step ($\checkmark$). Thus limiting the **amount of energy that could be used** from a single glucose molecule ($\checkmark$).
