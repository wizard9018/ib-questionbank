# BI2664

The graph below shows oxygen consumption, in arbitrary units, over time in two tissue types (X and Y):

![2664QB](images/2664QB.png)

Which conclusion is best supported by the data?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Tissue Y performs anaerobic respiration only.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Tissue X is not undergoing respiration.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Tissue X likely has more mitochondria than Tissue Y.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Tissue Y is producing more ATP overall.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Tissue X** shows a higher rate of oxygen consumption over time, which indicates a greater reliance on aerobic respiration. Since aerobic respiration occurs in the mitochondria, it is likely that **Tissue X** contains more mitochondria than **Tissue Y**.\
**Choice C** is therefore correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because anaerobic respiration occurs in the absence of oxygen, yet **Tissue Y** is still consuming oxygen—just at a lower rate—so it is not relying solely on anaerobic respiration.\
**Choice B** is incorrect because **Tissue X** is clearly undergoing respiration, as shown by its high oxygen consumption.\
**Choice D** is incorrect because aerobic respiration (**Tissue X**) yields significantly more ATP per glucose molecule than anaerobic respiration (**Tissue Y**), so **Tissue X** is likely producing more ATP overall.
