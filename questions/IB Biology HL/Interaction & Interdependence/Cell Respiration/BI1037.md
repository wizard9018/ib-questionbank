# BI1037

Which biological molecule is the substrate for glycolysis, the common initial stage of both aerobic and anaerobic cellular respiration?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Protein

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Cholesterol

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Carbohydrate

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Water

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Carbohydrates are the only substrate that can be used in glycolysis, **Choice C**  is correct.

Protein can be broken down into amino acids, some of which can enter the Krebs cycle, but they cannot enter glycolysis. **Choice A** is incorrect.  Cholesterol cannot be used directly in glycolysis. **Choice B**  is incorrect. Water is a waste product of aerobic respiration and does not feed into glycolysis. **Choice D** is incorrect.
