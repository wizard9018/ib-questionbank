# BI0664

**Maximum mark: 3**

Antibodies are proteins with a specific shape that are produced by B lymphocytes.

Describe how antibodies defend against pathogens. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

any **three** of the following:

$\ce{ -> }$ prevent pathogens attaching to body cells [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ opsonisation **OR** help phagocytes to recognise pathogens      [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ complement activation **OR** cause pathogens to lyse    [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ cause agglutination **OR** cause pathogens to stick together so they cannot enter cells    [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ some antibodies neutralise ** bind to toxins that are produced by pathogens    [1 mark]

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Antibodies bind to pathogens and **prevent pathogens from attaching to body cells** ($\checkmark$). Some antibodies **neutralise toxins produced by pathogens** ($\checkmark$). **Opsonisation** is when antibodies **help phagocytes to recognise pathogens** which allows more of them to be engulfed ($\checkmark$).
