# BI1362

At the height of the COVID-19 pandemic, the mean daily incidence of COVID-19 in Spain was 70 cases per 100 people, and the mean daily incidence of COVID-19 in England was 30 cases per 100 people. What was the percentage difference in the mean daily incidence between the two countries?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 65$\%$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 42$\%$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 80$\%$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 57$\%$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

Percentage difference and percentage change are often confused. The percentage difference is looking at the percentage of the difference when compared to the mean between two numbers. The equation for percentage difference is (Difference in values $\div$ Mean) $\times$ 100$\%$  \

The difference in the values is: 70 $-$ 30 $=$ 40\

Mean $=$ (70$+$30) $\div$ 2 $=$ 50\

Percentage difference $=$ (40 $\div$ 50) $\times$ 100$\%$ $=$ 80$\%$.  **Choice C**  is correct.

**Choice D** is the percentage change which is calculated by finding the difference between the two numbers and dividing by the original number, so here $\{(30-70) / 70\}$ $\times$ 100$\%$ $=$ $-$57$\%$. Percentage change compares two values, an original value and a new or updated value and looks at the direction and relationship. A positive change represents an increase, and a negative change represents a decrease. Percentage change would be more useful if looking at changes in the number of incidences of COVID-19 in one country over a period of time.  **Choices A and B** are incorrect as they do not refer to percentage difference.
