# BI0309

Which row contains a cause and consequence of antibiotic resistance?

| | $\hspace{1em}$ Cause  $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ Consequence $\hspace{1em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| Patients not completing courses of antibiotics | Affects the ability of antibiotics to treat viral infections |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Overuse of antibiotics | Affects the ability of antibiotics to treat HIV |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Regular use of antibiotics in agriculture | Affects the ability of antibiotics to fight bacterial infections |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Mutation(s) in viral DNA | Affects the ability of antibiotics to fight bacterial infections |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because the use of antibiotics in agriculture exposes bacteria to antibiotics frequently, increasing selection for resistant strains. As a result, antibiotics become less effective at treating bacterial infections because resistant bacteria survive and spread. \
**Choice A** is incorrect because antibiotics are not used to treat viral infections. \
**Choice B** is incorrect because HIV is a virus, and antibiotics are ineffective against it. \
**Choice D** is incorrect because antibiotic resistance arises in bacteria, not from mutations in viruses.
