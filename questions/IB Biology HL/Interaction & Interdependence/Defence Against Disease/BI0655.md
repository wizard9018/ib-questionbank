# BI0655

Which of the following statement(s) is/are true for plasma cells?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ Clones of B lymphocytes
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Produce large numbers of antibodies
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Have extensive rough endoplasmic reticulum

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$  I, II and III

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ I only
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because all statements are true. Plasma cells are clones of B lymphocytes, produce large numbers of antibodies and have extensive rough endoplasmic reticulum.
