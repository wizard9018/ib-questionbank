## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because, following infection, helper T lymphocytes activate B lymphocytes, which differentiate into plasma cells, which produce antibodies.

**Choices B** and **C** are incorrect because plasma cells do not activate B or T lymphocytes. **Choice D** is incorrect because B lymphocytes do not activate T lymphocytes.
