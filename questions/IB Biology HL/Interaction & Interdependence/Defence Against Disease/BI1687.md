# BI1687

The diagram below shows part of the immune response.

![BI1687 fig1](<images/BI1687 fig1.png>)

Which row is correct in the table below?

| | Name of leucocyte Z | Label of organelle that synthesises antibody proteins |

| --- | :-: | :-: |

| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | B cell | I |

| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | B cell | II |

| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | T cell | I |

| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | T cell | II |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

**Choice B** is correct because B cells differentiate into the antibody$-$producing plasma B cells when they are activated, and antibodies are synthesised by the ribosomes on the rough endoplasmic reticulum.

**Choice A** is incorrect because although leucocyte Z is a B cell, the Golgi apparatus only modifies the proteins that form antibodies, it does not synthesise the antibody proteins.

**Choice C** is incorrect because although antibodies are synthesised by the ribosomes on the rough endoplasmic reticulum (**II**) leucocyte Z is not a T cell.

**Choice D** is incorrect because leucocyte Z is not a T cell and the Golgi apparatus does not synthesise the antibody proteins, it only modifies and packages them.
