# BI0310

**Maximum mark: 7**

Below is a diagram of Vibrio cholerae, the pathogen that causes the disease cholera.

![BI0310i](images/BI0310i.png)

1. Identify which group of pathogens Vibrio cholerae belongs to. [1 mark]

Stomach acid and the mucous membranes of the digestive system are important primary defences against cholera infection.

2. Outline how mucous membranes defend the body against pathogens. [2 marks]
3. Explain why blood clotting is described as a cascade reaction. [4 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **one** of the following: \
$\ce{ -> }$ bacteria [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotes [1 mark]

 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
 **Bacteria** $\checkmark$.

2. any **two** of the following: \
$\ce{ -> }$ produce a sticky mucus to trap pathogens [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ act as a physical barrier preventing pathogens from reaching **OR** invading deeper tissues [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ contain enzymes such as lysozymes that chemically digest bacterial cell walls [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ cilia in respiratory tract move trapped pathogens up trachea to back of throat **OR** to be swallowed [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
 Mucous membranes secrete **mucus** $\checkmark$, which **traps** the **pathogens**, helping to **prevent** their **entry into tissues** $\checkmark$.

3. any **four** of the following: \
$\ce{ -> }$ blood clotting is a cascade reaction in which a series of reactions occur where the product of one step activates the next [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ clotting factors are released from platelets [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ sequential activation of clotting factors leads to conversion of prothrombin to thrombin **OR** thrombin converts fibrinogen to fibrin [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ fibrin forms a mesh that traps erythrocytes to form a clot [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ the cascade results in amplification where a small initial signal produces a large response [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ the cascade allows rapid and efficient clot formation [1 mark]

 [4 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
 Blood clotting is a **cascade reaction** in which a series of reactions occur where the **product of one step activates the next** $\checkmark$. **Platelets** release **clotting factors** that initiate this sequence $\checkmark$. These factors lead to the conversion of **prothrombin to thrombin**, which converts **fibrinogen into fibrin** $\checkmark$. Fibrin forms a **mesh that traps erythrocytes**, resulting in **clot formation** $\checkmark$.
