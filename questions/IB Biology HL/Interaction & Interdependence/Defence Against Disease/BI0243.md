# BI0243

Which of the following could lead to the development of antibiotic resistance?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Transfer of a plasmid from one bacterium to another

- B. $\hspace{1em}$    The development of new antibiotics
- C. $\hspace{1em}$    Human populations became resistant to the antibiotics
- D. $\hspace{1em}$    Antibiotics switch on resistance transcription factors

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because bacteria can gain antibiotic-resistance genes via horizontal gene transfer (often on plasmids), spreading resistance through a population.

**Choice B** is incorrect because developing new antibiotics does not, in itself, cause resistance; rather, resistance increases when antibiotics act as a selection pressure that favours resistant bacteria.

 **Choice C** iis incorrect because antibiotics target bacteria, not human cells; humans do not “become resistant” to antibiotics in the evolutionary sense.

 **Choice D** is incorrect because antibiotic resistance develops mainly through genetic changes (such as mutation or the acquisition of genes), not because antibiotics generally “switch on transcription factors” to create resistance.
