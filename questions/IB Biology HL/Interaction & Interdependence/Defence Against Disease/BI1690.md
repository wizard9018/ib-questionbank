# BI1690

Below is a diagram of a blood smear, as observed under a microscope.

![BI1690 fig1](<images/BI1690 fig1.png>)

Which cell labelled A to D is a neutrophil, a type of phagocytic cell?

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

Neutrophils have a lobed nucleus to make them more flexible and manoeuvre through the narrow spaces and tight junctions in tissues and blood vessels and to facilitate the engulfment and digestion of pathogens. **Choice A** therefore indicates a neutrophil cell.

**Choice B** indicates a red blood cell, or erythrocyte, which does not have a nucleus.

**Choice C** indicates a cell with a very large round nucleus and so is likely to be a lymphocyte.

**Choice D** indicates a platelet which is a small, disc-shaped cell fragment that plays a crucial role in blood clotting and wound repair.
