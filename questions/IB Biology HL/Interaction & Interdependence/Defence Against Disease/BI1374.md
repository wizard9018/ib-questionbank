# BI1374

**Maximum mark: 4**

The diagram shows the results from PCR tests conducted on patients to find out who was infected with the COVID-19 virus.

![BI1374](images/BI1374.png)

1. Identify which patients have been infected with COVID-19.[1 mark]

2. Determine, with a reason, whether COVID-19 should be treated with antibiotics. [1 mark]

3. Another disease that affects humans is HIV.

 1. Name one immune system cell that is targeted by HIV. [1 mark]

 2. HIV is an enveloped virus. State the origin of the viral envelope. [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ patients 1 **AND** 2 **AND** 4 [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Patients **1, 2** and **4** ($\checkmark$) have been infected with COVID-19.

2. any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ no **BECAUSE** COVID-19 is a virus **AND** antibiotics are ineffective against viruses [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ no **BECAUSE** antibiotics are only effective against bacteria **OR** function by disrupting **OR** inhibiting the metabolism of bacteria but viruses do not have their own metabolism [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The COVID 19 virus should not be treated with antibiotics because antibiotics work by **inhibiting metabolic processes** specific to **bacteria**, so are **ineffective against viruses** ($\checkmark$).
