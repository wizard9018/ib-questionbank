# BI0311

**Maximum mark: 10**

The diagram below represents the interaction of antigens and antibodies in the human immune response.

![BI0311i](images/BI0311i.png)

1. Outline what an antigen is. [3 marks]
2. Explain the role of memory B-cells in the human adaptive immune response. [3 marks]
3. Describe four differences, other than those involving memory cells, between the innate and adaptive immune systems. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **three** of the following:\
$\ce{ -> }$ molecule **OR** substance that triggers an immune response **OR** antibody production [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ molecule recognised as foreign **OR** non-self [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ usually a protein **OR** glycoprotein **OR** polysaccharide [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ usually located on the surface of a pathogen **OR** bacteria **OR** virus **OR** a cancer cell **OR** pollen [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ has specific epitopes **OR** binding sites recognised by specific antibodies **OR** lymphocyte receptors [1 mark]

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
An antigen is a **foreign molecule** $\checkmark$ that is usually a **protein or glycoprotein** $\checkmark$ found on the **surface of pathogens** $\checkmark$, which triggers an **immune response** such as **antibody production** $\checkmark$.

2. any **three** of the following:\
$\ce{ -> }$ produced during the primary immune response **OR** after clonal selection [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ remain in the blood **OR** lymph nodes for long periods [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ retain the ability to produce specific antibodies for the same antigen [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ upon re-infection **OR** secondary exposure, they provide a faster **OR** stronger response [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ rapidly proliferate **OR** undergo clonal expansion into plasma cells [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ ensures antibody concentration rises quickly to prevent disease symptoms [1 mark]

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Memory B-cells are **long-lived lymphocytes** that **remain** in the body **after** the **primary immune response** $\checkmark$, which on **secondary exposure** to the **same antigen** rapidly **divide** and **differentiate into plasma cells** $\checkmark$, producing **large amounts** of **specific antibodies more quickly** than in primary response, leading to **immunity** $\checkmark$.

3. any **four** of the following:\
$\ce{ -> }$ innate immunity is non-specific **WHEREAS** adaptive immunity is specific to particular antigens [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ innate immunity provides a rapid **OR** immediate response **WHEREAS** adaptive immunity has a delay **OR** lag phase on first exposure [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ innate immunity uses phagocytic white blood cells **WHEREAS** adaptive immunity involves lymphocytes **OR** antibody production [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ innate immunity recognises common pathogen-associated patterns **WHEREAS** adaptive immunity recognises specific antigens **OR** epitopes [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ innate immunity uses fixed receptors **WHEREAS** adaptive immunity has a diverse range of receptors [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ innate immunity includes physical **OR** chemical barriers **WHEREAS** adaptive immunity involves internal cellular **OR** molecular responses [1 mark]
$\ce{ -> }$ innate immunity does not involve clonal selection **WHEREAS** adaptive immunity involves clonal selection and expansion of specific B cells **OR** T cells [1 mark]

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
**Innate** immunity is **non-specific**, whereas **adaptive** immunity is **specific** to particular antigens $\checkmark$. **Innate** immunity provides a **rapid response**, whereas **adaptive** immunity has a **delay on first exposure** $\checkmark$. **Innate** immunity involves **phagocytic cells**, whereas **adaptive** immunity involves **lymphocytes and antibody production** $\checkmark$. **Innate** immunity does not involve clonal selection, whereas **adaptive** immunity involves **clonal selection and expansion** of specific lymphocytes $\checkmark$.
