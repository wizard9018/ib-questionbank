# BI1159

The following graph shows the incidences (number of cases/10,000 admissions) of multidrug-resistant *Streptococcus dysgalactiae* obtained from six hospitals in the Kyoto-Shiga region of Japan between 2011 and 2020.

![BI1159](images/BI1159.png)
[Source: Adapted from Shinohara, K., Murase, K., Tsuchido, Y., Noguchi, T., Yukawa, S., Yamamoto, M.Nagao, M. (2023). *Clonal Expansion of Multidrug-Resistant Streptococcus dysgalactiae Subspecies equisimilis Causing Bacteremia, Japan, 2005–2021*. Emerging Infectious Diseases, 29(3), 528-539. Retrieved September 20 2023, from https://doi.org/10.3201/eid2903.221060. CC0]

Which of the following statements is **NOT** true regarding the above data?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The incidence of multi-resistant infections roughly doubled between 2014 and 2015.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 2012 represents the lowest incidence of multi-resistant infections throughout the study.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The incidences of multi-resistant infections decreased in 2012, 2014, 2016, and 2018 compared to the previous year.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ There were approximately 8 cases/10,000 admissions in 2018.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
There was a decrease in the incidence of cases compared to the previous year in 2012, 2014, 2016, and 2019. There was an increase in 2018, not a decrease as stated in **Choice C**. This makes **Choice C** false and therefore the correct answer.

**Choices A, B**, and **D** are all true according to the graph.
