# BI0306

In the diagram below, what type of molecule is X pointing to?

![BI0306 i](<images/BI0306 i.png>)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Antibiotics

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Antigens
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Antibodies
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Receptors

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Explanation:
X is pointing to antigens, molecules that are recognised as "foreign" by the immune system and trigger an immune response. When a phagocytic cell engulfs a pathogen, it often displays the antigen of the pathogen on its cell surface membrane to help trigger an adaptive immune response; **Choice B** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because antibiotics are substances used to treat bacterial infections.

**Choice C** is incorrect because antibodies are proteins produced by plasma B cells in response to an antigen.

**Choice D** is incorrect because receptors are specialised proteins or glycoproteins found on the surface of or within cells that bind to specific molecules (ligands), such as hormones, neurotransmitters, or antigens, and initiate a cellular response.
