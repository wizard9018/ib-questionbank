# BI1234

Which is **not** a method to prevent antibiotic resistance?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Development of new antibiotics

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Patients taking the full course of antibiotics

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Farmers not using antibiotics in animal feeds

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Doctors prescribing antibiotics for viral infections

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Antibiotics are only effective against bacteria, not viruses. If antibiotics are taken for viral infections, then they are being overused, and bacteria are more likely to develop antibiotic resistance; **Choice D**  is correct.

The development of new antibiotics can prevent antibiotic resistance as new antibiotics may be more effective against bacteria that have developed resistance to older antibiotics; **Choice A**  is incorrect. Patients taking the full course of antibiotics means bacteria are less likely to develop antibiotic resistance; **Choice B**  is incorrect. Farmers not using antibiotics in animal feeds, means that antibiotics are not being overused, which lowers the chances of bacteria in livestock developing antibiotic resistance; **Choice C**  is incorrect.
