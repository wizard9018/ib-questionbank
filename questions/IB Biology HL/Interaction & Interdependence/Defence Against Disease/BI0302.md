# BI0302

Which of these statements about antibiotics are true?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ Antibiotics are ineffective against viruses.
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Antibiotics block metabolic reactions in prokaryotes.
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Antibiotic resistance is increasing in bacteria.

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ II only

-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
All three statements are true. Antibiotics are chemicals that affect metabolic processes in prokaryotes (**Statement II**) but are not effective against viruses (**Statement I**). Antibiotic resistance is an increasing global health problem (**Statement III**). **Choice A** is correct.

**Choices B, C** and **D** are all incorrect as they are missing one or more of the true statements.
