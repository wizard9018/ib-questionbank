# BI0305

What is the name of the process by which white blood cells engulf and destroy pathogens?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Lysis

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Phagocytosis
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Plasmolysis
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Crenation

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Phagocytosis is the term for the process by which phagocytes engulf and destroy pathogens; **Choice B** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because lysis is the breaking down or rupture of a cell, not the engulfing process.

**Choice C** is incorrect because plasmolysis occurs in plant cells when water leaves the cell, causing the cell membrane to shrink away from the cell wall.

**Choice D** is incorrect because crenation happens in animal cells when they shrink due to water loss in a hypertonic solution.
