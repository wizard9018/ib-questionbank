4.  3.  Any **three** of the following

$\ce{ -> }$

 might not be enough vaccines for the population [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$

$\ce{ -> }$

:u[Sample answer]:\

It can be difficult to achieve herd immunity to COVID-19 because there might **not be enough vaccines** for the population, which means that **not enough people** in the population may develop immunity ($\checkmark$) so COVID-19 is more likely to spread. Vaccinations can be **expensive** to develop. Therefore, COVID-19 may spread through populations **before vaccines are developed** ($\checkmark$). The virus may also **mutate**, so **current vaccines** may **not** be **effective in the long term** ($\checkmark$), resulting in a reduced chance of reaching herd immunity.
