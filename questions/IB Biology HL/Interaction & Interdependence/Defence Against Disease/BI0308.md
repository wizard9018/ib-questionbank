# BI0308

Which of these statements are true for white blood cells?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ White blood cells are part of the non-specific and specific defence systems.
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Antibodies are only produced by lymphocytes.
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Lymphocytes provide long-term immunity.

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only
-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because all three statements are true. Phagocytes and lymphocytes are both types of white blood cell. Phagocytes are part of the innate defence system, and lymphocytes are part of the adaptive defence system (**Statement I**). Antibodies are only produced by plasma B cells, a specific type of lymphocyte (**Statement II**). Lymphocytes provide long-term memory by forming memory B and T cells, which allow the immune system to respond more quickly and effectively to future infections by the same pathogen (**Statement III**).

**Choices A, C** and **D** are incorrect as they are missing one or more of the true statements.
