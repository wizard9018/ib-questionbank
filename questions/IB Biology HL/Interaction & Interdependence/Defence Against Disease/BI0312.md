# BI0312

**Maximum mark: 16**

1. Outline the methods of HIV transmission and its effects on the immune system.     [3 marks]

2.    Discuss the need for international cooperation to fight contagious diseases such as Ebola. [5 marks]

3.    Antibiotics are an essential tool of modern medicine. Outline the strengths and limitations of antibiotics. [8 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **three** of the following (both the method of transmission and effects on the immune system are required for full marks):

    *Method of transmission:*

    $\ce{ -> }$ unprotected sexual intercourse **OR** sharing needles **OR** mother to child **OR** breast feeding    [1 mark]

    *Effects of HIV on the immune system:*

    $\ce{ -> }$ HIV targets helper T cells **OR** CD4 cells      [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ reduced number of helper T cells **OR** CD4 cells     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ reduced ability to fight infection **OR** disease     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  (without treatment) can progress to acquired immunodeficiency syndrome [1 mark]

    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    HIV can be transmitted through **unprotected sex** with infected person ($\checkmark$). HIV targets cells of the immune system, specifically **helper T cells** ($\checkmark$), which possess CD4 receptors. The virus **reduces** the **number of helper T cells** ($\checkmark$) which help fight infection and thus a decrease in their number makes a person more **prone to infection** ($\checkmark$). Without the correct treatment, HIV infection can progress to **AIDS** ($\checkmark$), which is a result of the immune system becoming severely compromised.

2.    any **five** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ contagious diseases can cross borders     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ international cooperation is needed to prevent the spread **OR** trace transmission    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ to share knowledge and expertise [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ medicines need to be shared [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ mass vaccination programs are needed to prevent spread [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ mass vaccination programs are needed to protect the populations of different countries [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ monitoring and surveillance is needed to identify sources of infection    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ developed countries may need to give financial and medical support to developing countries [1 mark]

    [5 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Contagious diseases can move from one population to another and **across borders** ($\checkmark$). Countries need to **share knowledge and expertise** to work together to prevent the spread of disease ($\checkmark$). **Medicines and resources** need to be shared ($\checkmark$), particularly from **developed to developing countries** ($\checkmark$), to help to reduce and prevent transmission . Countries can also work together to **monitor the spread of disease and identify sources** ($\checkmark$) of infection. Mass **vaccination** programs are needed **to protect populations** ($\checkmark$) and need to happen in more than one country.

3.    any **eight** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ antibiotics are chemicals that disrupt the metabolism of bacteria     [1 mark]

    *Strengths*

    $\ce{ -> }$ can be used to treat bacterial infections     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ can be applied using a variety of methods **OR** orally, topically and intravenously [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ a number of antibiotics exist **OR** different antibiotics can be used to treat different infections [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ antibiotics do not harm eukaryotic cells **OR** body cells [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ antibiotics are fast acting [1 mark]

    *Limitations*

    $\ce{ -> }$ antibiotics are not effective against viruses [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ antibiotics can have side effects **OR** cause allergic reactions [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ misuse of antibiotics has led to increased antibiotic resistance **OR** antibiotic resistance is increasing     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ over prescription of antibiotics **OR** use of antibiotics in animal feed to increase muscle mass **OR** individuals not completing the course of antibiotics increases the prevalence of antibiotic resistance [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ antibiotic resistant bacterial strains cause illnesses that cannot be treated with current antibiotics [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ examples of antibiotic-resistant bacterial diseases are MRSA (methicillin-resistant *Staphylococcus aureus*) **OR** multi-drug resistant tuberculosis **OR** any other verifiable example [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ limited number of antibiotics to fight bacterial infections [1 mark]

    [8 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Antibiotics are chemicals, which are produced by some species of fungi, that have the ability to disrupt the **metabolism** of bacteria ($\checkmark$) and because of this they are used to treat **bacterial infections** ($\checkmark$). The use of antibiotics has many benefits, but also some limitations.

    *Benefits of Antibiotics*

    Antibiotics are available in a **variety of forms**, such as oral pills, topical creams and intravenous injections ($\checkmark$). They **work quickly** ($\checkmark$) and despite being harmful to bacteria, they do not affect **eukaryotic cells** ($\checkmark$).

    *Limitations of Antibiotics*

    The use of antibiotics is restricted to treating bacterial infections as they have no effect on **viral** infections ($\checkmark$). Furthermore, the **overuse** of antibiotics in contexts such as agriculture, has led to development of antibiotic resistance ($\checkmark$). This is when strains of bacteria become resistant to antibiotics, which renders them ineffective at treating the infection. Due to a **limited number** of antibiotics being currently available antibiotic resistant strains ($\checkmark$), which are resistant to multiple antibiotics, such as **MRSA** are becoming a global health concern ($\checkmark$).
