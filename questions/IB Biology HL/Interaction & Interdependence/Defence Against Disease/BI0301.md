# BI0301

Which of the following is associated with the primary defence of the body?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Lymphocytes

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Antibodies
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Memory cells
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Mucus

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Mucus is part of the primary (first-line) defence of the body. It acts as a physical barrier to pathogens, trapping microbes in the respiratory and digestive tracts. Along with skin, cilia, stomach acid, and enzymes in tears and saliva, mucus helps prevent pathogens from entering the body in the first place. **Choice D** is the correct answer.

**Choice A** is incorrect because lymphocytes are a type of white blood cell involved in the secondary, not primary, immune response. They recognise and target specific pathogens.

**Choice B** is incorrect because antibodies are produced by B lymphocytes in the adaptive immune response, which is part of the secondary, not primary, immune response.

**Choice C** is incorrect because memory cells are specialised lymphocytes that "remember" a specific pathogen after a primary infection. They are crucial for a faster and stronger response during subsequent exposures to the same pathogen and are therefore part of the secondary immune response.
