# BI0663

**Maximum mark: 7**

The graph shows the typical immune response to two doses of a vaccine.

![BI0663](images/BI0663.png)

1. State which type of molecule stimulates antibody production. [1 mark]
2. Distinguish between the primary and secondary responses. [3 marks]

3. Explain how vaccination leads to long-term immunity. [3 marks]

2. Any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ a smaller number of antibodies are produced by the primary response **WHEREAS** a larger number of antibodies are produced by the secondary response [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the primary response is slower **BUT** the secondary response is faster [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ primary response occurs following first contact with the pathogen **OR** antigen **WHEREAS** secondary response occurs following second contact with the pathogen **OR** antigen [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
The primary response is **slower** and produces a **smaller number** of antibodies, whereas a **larger number** ($\checkmark$) of antibodies are produced more **rapidly** ($\checkmark$) by the secondary response. The primary response occurs after an organism's **first exposure to the antigen**, whereas the secondary response occurs during **the second exposure** ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **three** of the following (**AT LEAST** one point must link vaccination to long-term immunity):

 *Vaccination*\
 $\ce{ -> }$ vaccines contain an attenuated version of the pathogen **OR** antigens of the pathogen [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the vaccine triggers an immune response that triggers antibody production [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ B and T lymphocytes are activated [1 mark]

 *Long-term immunity*\
 $\ce{ -> }$ memory cells are produced, which give long-term immunity [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ long-lived memory B cells, **OR** other correct memory cells, circulate in the blood [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ memory cells result in the production of antibodies at a greater rate if there is a second exposure to the same pathogen [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ antibodies specific to antigen continue to circulate in the blood [1 mark]

 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
**Vaccines contain antigens** for a specific pathogen ($\checkmark$). Vaccines give long-term immunity because they trigger an **immune response which produces antibodies** ($\checkmark$) and because **memory B cells** are also produced which give long-term immunity ($\checkmark$).
