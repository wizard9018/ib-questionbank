# BI2337

The World Health Organisation has identified 95% vaccination coverage as the threshold for herd immunity for measles. Which statement best describes the consequence of 95% measles vaccination coverage?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Reduces the need for measles vaccination programs in the future

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Eradicates measles in the population

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Protects individuals who cannot be vaccinated and significantly reduces transmission of measles

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Protects all vaccinated individuals from measles

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because herd immunity at 95% measles vaccination coverage reduces the number of people who can be infected and prevents transmission of measles. Lower infection rates reduce the chance of infection for people who cannot be vaccinated, such as very young children or individuals with a compromised immune system.

**Choice A** is incorrect because while herd immunity can reduce transmission, continued vaccination programs are necessary to maintain herd immunity. Without ongoing vaccination, immunity levels in the population will drop over time, leading to outbreaks.

**Choice B** is incorrect because vaccination can eradicate diseases, but measles is very difficult to eradicate due to the high 95% level needed for herd immunity, and some characteristics of the disease.

**Choice D** is incorrect because while vaccination significantly reduces the chance of infection, it is not 100% effective, and therefore does not protect all vaccinated individuals from contracting measles.
