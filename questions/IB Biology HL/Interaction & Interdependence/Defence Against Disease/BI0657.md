# BI0657

This diagram shows the interaction between a helper T-cell and a B-lymphocyte.

![activated B cell by helper T cell](<images/activated B cell by helper T cell.png>)

What is X?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Fibrin

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Antibody
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Antigen
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Killer T-cell

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because antigens activate Helper T-cells when they bind to them and these then activate B lymphocytes. **Choice A** is incorrect because fibrin is an insoluble, fibrous protein that forms a mesh-like network during blood clotting; it is not involved in B-lymphocyte activation. **Choice D** is incorrect because killer T-cells are white blood cells that destroy infected body cells.
