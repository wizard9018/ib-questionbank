# BI0412

**Maximum mark: 10**

Neuronal cells have special membrane properties that allow their axons to propagate action potential from one cell to another. The diagram below shows a portion of an axonal plasma membrane.

![sodium potassium pump](<images/sodium potassium pump.png>)

1.
 1. State the role of a sodium-potassium pump in the axon. [1 mark]

 2. Describe the action of the sodium-potassium pump. [3 marks]

2. Distinguish between simple diffusion, facilitated diffusion and active transport. [2 marks]

3. Compare and contrast sodium-potassium pumps and voltage-gated potassium channels. [4 marks]

 [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ helps establish **OR** restore **OR** maintain the resting membrane potential [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
 The sodium-potassium pump **uses energy** to move Na$^{+}$ and K$^{+}$ ions against their concentration gradients **to maintain a higher concentration** of sodium **outside** the axon and a higher concentration of potassium **inside** the axon. ($\checkmark$)

 2. Any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ three sodium ions attach to their binding sites on the pump which is open to the inside of the axon [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ ATP transfers a phosphate group from itself to the pump causing the pump to change shape and the interior is then closed [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the interior of the pump opens to the outside of the axon **OR** the three sodium ions are released [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ two potassium ions from the outside can then attach to their binding sites [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the binding of potassium causes the release of the phosphate group **OR** this causes the pump to change shape again so that it is only open to the inside of the axon [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the interior of the pump opens to the inside of the axon thus releasing the two potassium ions [1 mark]

 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
The sodium-potassium pump is responsible for active transport which maintains a high concentration of sodium and a low concentration of potassium inside the axon. When the pump is open to the inside of the axon, **three sodium ions attach to their binding sites** ($\checkmark$). The **pump changes shape once ATP transfers a phosphate group** from itself to the pump ($\checkmark$). **Three sodium ions are released** to the outside and **two potassium ions attach** to their binding sites ($\checkmark$). The **pump changes shape again so that the two potassium ions are released towards the inside of the axon**. ($\checkmark$)

2. Any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ simple diffusion and facilitated do not require energy **WHILE** active transport requires energy [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ simple diffusion and facilitated diffusion transport substances down their concentration gradient **WHILE** active transport transports substances up their concentration gradient [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ simple diffusion does not involve membrane proteins **WHILE** facilitated diffusion and active transport require membrane bound proteins [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
**Simple diffusion transports substances** down their concentration gradient requiring **neither energy nor membrane proteins**. Facilitated diffusion requires **membrane proteins** but does not use energy while active transport requires **both membrane proteins** ($\checkmark$) and **energy** ($\checkmark$).
