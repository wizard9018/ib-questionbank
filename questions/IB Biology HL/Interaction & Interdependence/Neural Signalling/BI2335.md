# BI2335

What is the correct order of events for nerve transmission at a synapse?

|  $\ce{A}.$ $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{Neurotransmitter released}$ $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ $\rarr$ $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{Neurotransmitter diffuses}$ $\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$ $\rarr$ $\hspace{1em}$ | $\ce{Neurotransmitter binds to}$ $\\$ $\ce{transmembrane receptors}$  |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Neurotransmitter diffuses |$\rarr$ | Neurotransmitter binds to $\\$ transmembrane receptors | $\rarr$ | Neurotransmitter released |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Neurotransmitter binds to $\\$ transmembrane receptors | $\rarr$ | Neurotransmitter released | $\rarr$ |Neurotransmitter diffuses |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Neurotransmitter diffuses | $\rarr$ | Neurotransmitter released | $\rarr$ | Neurotransmitter binds to $\\$ transmembrane receptors |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because when a nerve impulse reaches the synaptic terminal, calcium ions enter the presynaptic knob, triggering the release of neurotransmitters via exocytosis. The released neurotransmitter diffuses across the synaptic cleft and binds to specific receptors on the postsynaptic membrane, leading to the initiation of a response in the postsynaptic neuron.

**Choices B** and **D** are incorrect because neurotransmitter diffusion cannot occur before it is released.

**Choice C** is incorrect because neurotransmitter binding cannot happen before it is released or diffused.
