# BI0511

The graph below shows membrane voltage over time during an action potential. Which letter represents the segment of the action potential when voltage-gated sodium channels are open and voltage-gated potassium channels are closed?

![511_6.5](images/511_6.5.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Once a neuron reaches threshold potential, the voltage-gated sodium channels in the membrane open. Once these channels are open, sodium diffuses down its concentration gradient into the cell. The rapid influx of positively charged sodium ions depolarises the cell to a positive voltage. **Choice B** on the graph indicates the depolarisation phase, so corresponds to the time during the action potential in which voltage-gated potassium channels are closed and voltage-gated sodium channels are open.

At resting potential (**Choice A**), both voltage-gated sodium and potassium channels are closed. During the repolarisation phase (**Choice C**), voltage-gated potassium channels are open and voltage-gated sodium channels are closed. During the hyperpolarisation phase (**Choice D**), voltage-gated sodium channels are closed and voltage-gated potassium channels are in the process of closing.
