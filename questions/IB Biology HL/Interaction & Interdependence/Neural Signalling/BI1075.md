# BI1075

What triggers the release of neurotransmitters from the presynaptic membrane?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Depolarisation of the presynaptic membrane

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ A refractory period after depolarisation of the presynaptic membrane

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Repolarisation of the presynaptic membrane

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Activation of receptors on the postsynaptic neuron

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Depolarisation  triggers the influx of calcium ions into the presynaptic knob, which brings about the release of neurotransmitters from the presynaptic membrane, **Choice A**  is correct.

**Choices B** and **C**  are incorrect, as neither depolarisation nor repolarisation can occur during the refractory period. **Choice D**  is incorrect as activation of receptors on the postsynaptic membrane is caused by neurotransmitter binding; it does not trigger their release.
