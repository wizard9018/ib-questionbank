The diagram shows a neuron.

Which arrow indicates a dendrite?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

**Choice A** is correct because it points to a dendrite, a branching extension of the cell body that receives signals from other neurons and carries electrical changes towards the cell body.

**Choice B** is incorrect because it points to a node of Ranvier, a gap between adjacent segments of the myelin sheath where the axon membrane is not covered by myelin.

**Choice C** is incorrect because it points to a Schwann cell, which wraps around a section of the axon and forms a segment of the myelin sheath.

**Choice D** is incorrect because it points to a terminal branch of the axon, not a dendrite. These branches occur at the far end of the axon and form connections with target cells.
