# BI0627

What are the main factors that maintain a neuron's resting potential?

| | Sodium ions | Potassium ions |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| Diffuse out | Diffuse in |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Pumped in | Pumped out |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Pumped out | Pumped in |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Diffuse in | Diffuse out |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choices A** and **D** are incorrect as the main movement of ions that contributes to the resting potential is done by the Na$^+\text{\textendash}$ K$^+$ pump which is an example of active transport (as opposed to diffusion, which is passive transport).  **Choice B** is incorrect as sodium ions are pumped out of the neuron, and potassium ions are pumped in, which creates the resting potential.
