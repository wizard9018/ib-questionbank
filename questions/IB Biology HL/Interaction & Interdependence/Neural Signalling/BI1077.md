# BI1077

Which statement does **not** apply to an axon undergoing depolarisation with a membrane potential of 10mV?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Simultaneously, other parts of the axon will be at resting potential

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The neuron will be more negative on the inside than on the outside

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Sodium channels will be open, while potassium channels will remain closed

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The threshold potential would have been surpassed

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
During depolarisation, the inside of the neuron is more positive than the outside due to the influx of sodium ions via sodium channels. **Choice B**  is the statement that does not apply to this axon and is, therefore, the correct answer to this question.

**Choice A**  is incorrect as nerve impulses travel through a neuron via local currents, so the depolarisation of one part of an axon causes the next part of the axon to rise from its resting potential. **Choice C**   is incorrect as the opening of sodium channels causes sodium to diffuse into the neuron, causing the membrane potential to rise, **Choice D**  is incorrect as in order for depolarisation to occur, the threshold potential must have been surpassed.
