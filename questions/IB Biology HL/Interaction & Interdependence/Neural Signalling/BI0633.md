# BI0633

How do neonicotinoid pesticides work?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Binds to receptors in synapses, preventing neurotransmitters from binding

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Inhibits neurotransmitter release at the presynaptic cleft
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Reverses the direction of action potentials
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Damages the receptors on synapses

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Neonicotinoids bind irreversibly to acetylcholine receptors at cholinergic synapses so that acetylcholine (a neurotransmitter) is unable to bind and an action potential cannot be generated.\
**Choices B**, **C** and **D** are incorrect as they do not describe how neonicotinoids work.
