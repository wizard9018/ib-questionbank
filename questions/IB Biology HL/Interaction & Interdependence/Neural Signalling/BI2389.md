# BI2389

A teacher drew the following unlabelled diagram of a synapse to illustrate the mode of action of cocaine.

!['teachers' drawing of synapse labelled A-D i](<images/_teachers_ drawing of synapse labelled A-D i.png>)

Which label (**A - D**) shows the site of action of cocaine in the teacher's drawing?

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because this is the transporter protein that normally allows the reuptake of the neurotransmitters (principally dopamine, serotonin and norepinephrine) from the synaptic gap. Cocaine's mode of action is to block this receptor. The resulting high concentration of neurotransmitters in the synaptic gap amplifies the response in the postsynaptic cell, giving short-term feelings of euphoria and high energy. This is only short term, in that a dangerous dependency can ensue quickly with repeated cocaine misuse.

**Choice A** is incorrect because this shows the presynaptic vesicles that carry the neurotransmitter; these are unaffected by cocaine.

**Choice C** is incorrect because this shows the neurotransmitter molecules crossing the synaptic gap. Cocaine has no direct molecular effect on neurotransmitters.

**Choice D** is incorrect because these are the receptors that bind to the neurotransmitter on the postsynaptic membrane in order to relay the impulse.
