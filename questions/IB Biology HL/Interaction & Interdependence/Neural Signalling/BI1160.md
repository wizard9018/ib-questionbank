# BI1160

The graph below shows membrane voltage over time during an action potential. Which letter represents the segment of the action potential when voltage-gated sodium channels are closed and voltage-gated potassium channels are open?

![BI1160](images/BI1160.png)
[Source: Adapted from Losh, M., & Llamocca, D. (2019) *A Low-Power Spike-Like Neural Network Design*. Electronics 8, 1479. Retrieved March 16, 2022, from https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics8121479. Copyright under CC BY 4.0]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
During the repolarisation phase of an action potential, voltage-gated potassium channels are open and the voltage-gated sodium channels are closed. This allows K:sup[+] ions to leave the cell, returning the cell to a negative state.

**Choice B** on the graph indicates the depolarisation phase, so corresponds to the time during the action potential in which voltage-gated potassium channels are closed and voltage-gated sodium channels are open. Between the resting potential and threshold potential (**Choice A**), both voltage-gated sodium and potassium channels are closed. During the hyperpolarisation phase (**Choice D**), voltage-gated sodium channels are closed and voltage-gated potassium channels are either closed or in the process of closing.
