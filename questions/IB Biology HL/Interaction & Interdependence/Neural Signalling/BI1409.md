# BI1409

Which row is correct regarding the repolarisation of a neuron during an action potential?

| | Voltage-gated sodium ion channels | Voltage-gated potassium ion channels | Membrane potential |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| open | closed | becomes more negative |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | closed | open | becomes more negative |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | open | closed | becomes more positive |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | closed | open | becomes more positive |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Repolarisation occurs after depolarisation during an action potential, and it is when the inside of the neuron returns to a negative charge compared to the outside. When the membrane potential reaches the peak of its positive charge, the voltage-gated sodium ion channels shut, and the voltage-gated potassium ion channels open. The potassium ions flood out of the neuron, making the inside more negative on the inside compared to the outside. **Choice B** is the correct answer.

**Choice A** is incorrect as the voltage-gated sodium ion channels are closed during repolarisation, and the voltage-gated potassium ion channels are open.\
**Choice C** is incorrect because during repolarisation, the voltage-gated sodium ion channels are closed and the voltage-gated potassium ion channels are open, and the membrane potential becomes more negative, not more positive.\
**Choice D** is incorrect because the membrane potential becomes more negative, not positive during repolarisation.
