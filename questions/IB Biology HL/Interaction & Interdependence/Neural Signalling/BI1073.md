# BI1073

The diagram shows two neurons of equal length and diameter. Which neuron will transfer an electrical impulse faster?

| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Y | because it has more axon terminals |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Electrical impulses can jump between nodes of Ranvier (the gaps in the myelin sheath) in a process called saltatory conduction; this process is faster than continuous transmission along an unmyelinated fibre, **Choice C**  is correct.

**Choice A**  is incorrect as neurons X and Y have the same number of dendrites, and neuron Y is unmyelinated. **Choice B**  is incorrect, as unmyelinated neurons have a slower rate of electrical transmission. **Choice D**  is incorrect as it is myelination, not the number of axon terminals, that speeds up electrical transmission.
