# BI0630

What is **not** a function of the myelin sheath?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ To increase impulse transmission rate

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ To insulate the axon
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ To prevent depolarisation
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ To release neurotransmitters

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Neurotransmitters are not transferred by the myelin sheath.\
**Choices A**, **B** and **C** are incorrect, as they are all functions of the myelin sheath.
