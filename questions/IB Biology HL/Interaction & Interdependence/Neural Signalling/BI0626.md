# BI0626

Below is an action potential graph.

![BI0626](images/BI0626.png)

What is happening at point X?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Repolarisation due to potassium ions diffusing out

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Repolarisation due to sodium ions diffusing in
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Depolarisation due to sodium ions diffusing out
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Depolarisation due to potassium ions diffusing in

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
When voltage-gated potassium channels open, K$^+$ ions diffuse out down their concentration gradient, causing the inside of the neuron to become more negative. **Choice A** is correct.\
**Choice B** is incorrect as when sodium ions diffuse into the neuron the membrane potential rises due to the increase in positive charge inside the neuron. \
**Choices C** and **D** are incorrect as the term depolarisation means an increase in the membrane potential.
