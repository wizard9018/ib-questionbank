# BI0636

**Maximum mark: 3**

Explain how the resting potential is maintained in a neuron.     [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Any **three** of the following:

$\ce{ -> }$ a neuron that is not transmitting an impulse has a resting potential of -70mV [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ it is due to the difference in positive and negative charges across the membrane [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ sodium-potassium pump pumps three Na$^+$ ions out and two K$^+$ ions into the cell [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ the membrane is more permeable to K$^+$ ions than Na$^+$ ions [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ K$^+$ ions diffuse down their concentration gradient out of the neuron [1 mark]

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
A neuron that is not transmitting an impulse has a resting potential of **-70mV** ($\checkmark$) due to the **imbalance** of positive and negative charges across the membrane ($\checkmark$) which is maintained by the action of sodium-potassium pumps. The **sodium-potassium pump** pumps three Na$^+$ ions out and two K$^+$  ions into the cell ($\checkmark$).
