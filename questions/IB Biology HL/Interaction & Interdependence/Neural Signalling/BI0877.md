# BI0877

Which two features are more abundant per unit length in a non-myelinated neuron than in a myelinated neuron?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Sodium-potassium pumps and synaptic vesicles

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Potassium ion channels and sodium-potassium pumps

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Rough endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi apparatus

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Nodes of Ranvier and Schwann cells

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because cell membranes of myelinated neurons largely lack ion channels and pumps in regions of the axon that are myelinated. **Choice A** is incorrect because synaptic vesicles are not present in axons. **Choice C** is incorrect because the rough endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi apparatus are present in the cell body, not the axon.  **Choice D** is incorrect because Nodes of Ranvier and Schwann cells are present on myelinated neurons, not non-myelinated neurons.
