# BI1072

Which statement explains why a nerve impulse is electrical?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ It releases hormones from glands into the bloodstream

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Neurotransmitters carry electrical charge between neurons

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Electrons jump from node to node as electrical signals are transferred across neurons

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ It involves the movement of positively charged ions

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
A nerve impulse is described as electrical because it involves the movement of positively charged ions, which causes a charge imbalance, **Choice D** is correct.

**Choice A**  is incorrect as it describes how chemicals are released via the endocrine system. **Choice B**  is incorrect as neurotransmitters do not carry electrical charges themselves, they just trigger an action potential when they bind to receptors on the postsynaptic membrane. **Choice C**  is incorrect, as nerve impulses are primarily carried by the movement of positive ions across the cell membrane, not electrons.
