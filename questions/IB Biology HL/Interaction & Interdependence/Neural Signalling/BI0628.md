# BI0628

Which membrane potential triggers the opening of the voltage-gated sodium channels?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ -70 mV

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ -50mV
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ +30 mV
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ 0 mV

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
-50mV is the threshold potential that must be reached for voltage-gated sodium channels to open.\
**Choice A** is incorrect, as -70 mV is the resting potential of a neuron. **Choice C** is incorrect as at +30 mV, voltage-gated potassium channels are triggered to open. **Choice D** is incorrect as at 0mV the voltage-gated sodium channels are already open.
