# BI2700

Which row correctly describes the effect and consequence of inhibitory neurotransmitter binding to the postsynaptic neuron?

| | Effect | Consequence |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Increases permeability to negative ions | Hypo-polarisation |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Increases permeability to negative ions | Hyperpolarisation |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Decreases permeability to positive ions | Hypo-polarisation |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Increases permeability to positive ions | Hyperpolarisation |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because inhibitory neurotransmitters increase membrane permeability to negative ions, allowing them to enter the neuron. This makes the inside of the neuron more negative than the resting potential, resulting in hyperpolarisation, which reduces the likelihood of an action potential.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the movement of more negative ions into the postsynaptic neuron causes hyperpolarisation, not hypo-polarisation.

**Choices C** and **D** are incorrect because inhibitory neurotransmitters increase permeability to negative ions, not positive ions.
