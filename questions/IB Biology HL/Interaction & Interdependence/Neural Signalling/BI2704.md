# BI2704

How do stimuli such as skin damage, heat, and certain chemicals affect pain-sensitive neurons with free nerve endings in the skin?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Trigger an influx of positively charged ions into the neuron

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Trigger an efflux of positively charged ions out of the neuron

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Trigger an influx of negatively charged ions into the neuron

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Trigger an efflux of negatively charged ions out of the neuron

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because stimuli such as skin damage, heat, or certain chemicals activate pain-sensitive neurons by opening ion channels in free nerve endings. This allows an influx of positively charged ions into the neuron. The entry of these ions makes the inside of the neuron less negative, bringing it to threshold potential and triggering an action potential that carries the pain signal to the brain.

**Choice B** is incorrect because stimuli at the skin cause positive ions to move into the neuron, not out of the neuron.

**Choices C** and **D** are incorrect because stimuli at the skin increase the inward movement of positive ions to bring the neuron to threshold potential; they do not affect the movement of negative ions.
