# BI2923

A student grows a yeast culture to study a sigmoid growth curve.

![2923](images/2923.png)

Which label identifies the stage during which the availability of glucose in the culture becomes a primary limiting factor for the yeast population's growth?

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because it refers to the decelerating phase of the sigmoid growth curve, where the growth rate starts to slow as resources like glucose become limited. In this phase, density-dependent factors, such as competition for nutrients, begin to have a noticeable impact on the population's growth, causing the curve to flatten as the population approaches its carrying capacity.

**Choice A** is incorrect because it represents the lag phase, during which the population is adjusting to the new environment and growth is slow due to factors like adaptation to the environment. There is not yet a significant resource limitation.

**Choice B** is incorrect because it refers to the log phase (also known as the exponential phase), where the population is growing at its fastest rate. In this phase, resources are still relatively abundant, and the graph shows the steepest slope.

**Choice D** is incorrect because it corresponds to the stationary phase, where the population has levelled off after reaching carrying capacity. At this point, the birth rate and death rate are approximately equal, and resources cannot support further growth.
