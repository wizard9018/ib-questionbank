# BI1459

**Maximum mark: 4**

Below is a graph representing the population size of a bacteria colony.

![growth curve copy ii](<images/growth curve copy ii.png>)

Describe what is happening at stages X and Y on the graph. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Any **four** of the following (there must be at least **one** statement for X and **one** statement for Y to achieve full marks)

$\ce{ -> }$ X shows exponential growth **OR** the log phase [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ at X bacteria are dividing at the maximum rate **OR** natality exceeds mortality [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ at X there are plenty of nutrients **OR** plenty of resources **OR** little competition [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ at X temperature **OR** pH **OR** oxygen levels are optimal [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ Y is the transition phase of the growth curve [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ at Y mortality rate is almost as high as natality rate [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ at Y essential nutrients have been consumed **OR** reduction in nutrients available **OR** less resources **OR** competition has increased [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ at Y metabolic wastes **OR** toxins have accumulated [1 mark]

[4 marks]
