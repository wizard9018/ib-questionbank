# BI0959

The cyclical variations in the population densities of a predator and its prey are shown in the image below.

![BI0959](images/BI0959.png)

Which of the following factors may have the least direct effect on the populations of the predator and prey species depicted here?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Pressure due to predation

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Reproductive rate of predators

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Food availability

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Weather conditions

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The weather (**Choice D**)does not play a significant role in the growth of the predator-prey population but could influence it. It may impact the behaviour or visibility of the predator or prey indirectly. The other factors play a more prominent role.

Predators sometimes exert a significant and direct influence on prey populations by consuming them, decreasing the prey population.
As the predator population increases, the predation pressure (**Choice A**) on the prey increases, leading to a decline in the prey population. The reproductive rates of predators and prey play a role in the growth of the populations. The prey usually has a higher reproductive rate (**Choice B**). The availability of food impacts both predator and prey. A decrease in the prey population will cause a decline in the predator population, leading to cyclical variations in predator and prey population densities (**Choice C**).
