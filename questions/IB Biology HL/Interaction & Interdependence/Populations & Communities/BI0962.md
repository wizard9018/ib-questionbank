# BI0962

What is the correct description of 'interspecific competition'?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Interactions between predator and prey

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Individuals of the same species competing for limited resources

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Individuals of different species competing for limited resources

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Individuals of different species cooperating for limited resources

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Competition between different species in the same ecosystem (**Choice C**) is referred to as interspecific competition. Different species may compete for resources such as food, water and shelter.

Individuals of the same species competing for resources (**Choice B**) is ‘intraspecific
Competition.'  **Choice A** refers to predator-prey interactions and is not any type of competition. Individuals of different species cooperating for limited resources (**Choice D**) describes a mutualistic relationship, not a competitive one.
