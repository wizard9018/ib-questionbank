# BI2930

Farmers often use rye, planting it before their main cash crop, as it suppresses weed growth in their fields by releasing chemicals.  Which term best describes this biological interaction?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Parasitism

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Commensalism

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Antibody secretion

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Allelopathy

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because allelopathy refers to the biological interaction where a plant releases chemical compounds that inhibit the growth of other plants. In this case, the rye suppresses weed growth by releasing chemicals, making allelopathy the best description of this interaction.

**Choice A** is incorrect because parasitism involves one organism benefiting at the expense of another, often by living on or inside the host to derive nutrients. The rye does not derive nutrients from the weeds, so this interaction does not fit the definition of parasitism.

**Choice B** is incorrect because commensalism refers to a relationship where one organism benefits, and the other is neither harmed nor helped. In the case of the rye and the weeds, the weeds are being harmed by the chemicals released by the rye, which makes commensalism an inappropriate choice.

**Choice C** is incorrect because antibody secretion refers to the production of antibodies by an organism to defend against pathogens. While allelopathy is a form of antibiosis (chemical defence), it specifically applies to plants and the release of chemicals to inhibit the growth of other plants, which makes allelopathy the more accurate term in this context.
