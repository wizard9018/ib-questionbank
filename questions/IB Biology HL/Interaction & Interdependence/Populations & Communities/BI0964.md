# BI0964

**Maximum mark: 3**

Two different mesocosms were used for a study to understand the population dynamics of two ecosystems, one without fish (C ) and one with fish (F). Each mesocosm had the following added to it: two different species of snails and the same (one) species of phytoplankton. The snails feed on the phytoplankton and are, in turn, preyed upon by the fish.

The following diagrams show the mesocosms used for the study. C is the control without fish, and F is the experimental mesocosm with one fish added. Both mesocosms were maintained at the same temperature and light conditions.

![mesocosms phytoplankton, snails, fish black and white created in BR](<images/mesocosms phytoplankton, snails, fish black and white created in BR.png>)

After a period of time, as shown in the graph below, the density of phytoplankton was seen to vary in the two mesocosms used in the study.

![BI0964B](images/BI0964B.png)

1. Outline a reason for the variation seen in the density of the phytoplankton in the two mesocosms, as shown above. [1 mark]

2. Explain whether this is indicative of a top-down or bottom–up control of the simulated ecosystem. [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ the snails are eaten by the fish in mesocosm F, and therefore there is an increase in phytoplankton density [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ in the absence of fish, as in mesocosm C, the snails eat the phytoplankton, and the density of phytoplankton is controlled and remains lower than in mesocosm F. [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 In **mesocosm F**, the **snails are eaten by the fish** ($\checkmark$); therefore, the phytoplankton density is higher than in mesocosm C.

2. Any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ the fish control the abundance **OR** density of the snail, which controls the density of the producer [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ this is indicative of a top-down control [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ in the case of bottom-up control, with increasing light **OR** temperature **OR** nutrients, increasing algae will cause growth of more snails, thus more fish can be supported [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ no data on nutrient content in either mesocosm, so cannot determine if there are bottom-up limiting factors **OR** if there is bottom-up control [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 In mesocosm F, the **fish reduce** the abundance of **snails** ($\checkmark$), and therefore phytoplankton density increases in the presence of the fish. This is indicative of **top-down control of the ecosystem** ($\checkmark$).
