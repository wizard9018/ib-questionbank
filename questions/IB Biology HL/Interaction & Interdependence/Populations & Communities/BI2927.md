# BI2927

A group of students surveyed several forest plots, recording the presence or absence of two plant species.  They then used a chi-squared test to analyse their data.  What is the purpose of performing a chi-squared test in this investigation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ To determine if there's a significant difference in the allele frequencies of the two species across the plots.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ To determine the statistical relationship between the distribution of the two species.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ To establish a causal link between the distribution of the two species.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ To predict future population trends and estimate the carrying capacity for each species.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because the chi-squared test for association is used to determine if the observed distribution of the two species is statistically different from what would be expected by chance, indicating a statistically significant association.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the chi-squared test for association is not used to analyse allele frequencies.  This distractor was designed to be confused with Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium.\
**Choice C** is incorrect because the chi-squared test for association does not indicate a direct cause-and-effect relationship.  This would require more experimental data and analysis.\
**Choice D** is incorrect because the data collection is a snapshot of current distribution patterns and cannot provide any longitudinal data.  Population growth models would be needed to predict future population trends.
