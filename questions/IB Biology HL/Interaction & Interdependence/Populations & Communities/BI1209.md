# BI1209

Which statement is an example of intraspecific competition that could lead to evolution by natural selection?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Sharks and dolphins eat the same types of fish

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Parasitic vines growing on tall kapok trees in the rainforest

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Roses and weeds grow in the same areas and compete for nutrients

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Male zebras fight over territory, and female partners

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Intraspecific competition is competition between organisms of the same species. Male zebras are from the same species and compete with each other over territory and mates; **Choice D** is correct.

**Choices A** and **C** are incorrect as they describe interspecific competition, which is competition between organisms from different species. The growth of vines on tall kapok trees represents a parasitic relationship in which the vines derive nutrients and water from the kapok trees. This interaction does not involve intraspecific competition; **Choice B**  is incorrect.
