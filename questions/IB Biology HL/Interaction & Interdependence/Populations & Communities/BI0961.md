# BI0961

Which statement(s) describes allelopathy in plants?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ Allelopathy is the process by which a plant releases secondary metabolites that inhibit the growth of other plants.
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Allelopathy is the presence of mechanical defences such as thorns and spines in plants.
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Allelopathy may involve a decreased cell elongation rate in one plant species in response to a chemical secreted by another plant species.

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ II only
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statements I** and **III** describe the process of allelopathy, which is
the release of secondary metabolites by plants to prevent the growth and development of other plants. The decreased rate of cell elongation is one of the physiological effects that can occur as a result of allelopathic interactions. Therefore **Choice A** is correct.

**Choice D** is incorrect because it only includes one true statement.
**Statement II** is incorrect because, in addition to chemical defences, some plants may have mechanical defences, like thorns which provide physical protection against herbivores. **Choices B** and **C** are incorrect as they include this statement.
