4. Any **three** of the following ( at least **one** from each section):

 ***top down control*** \
 $\ce{ -> }$ top-down control is when higher trophic level **OR** predator **OR** fish abundance **OR** behaviour influences **OR** controls the lower trophic levels abundance **OR** community structure [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ fish feed on snails, which, in turn, feed on algae **OR** phytoplankton [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ high fish population means more snails consumed so less snails to feed on algae **OR** phytoplankton **AND** algae **OR** phytoplankton abundance increases **OR** decreased grazing pressure on algae **OR** phytoplankton [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ presence of top level predators **OR** fish helps maintain balance in ecosystem by preventing unchecked growth of herbivores **OR** snails, and so controlling algal populations [1 mark]

 ***bottom up control*** \
 $\ce{ -> }$ bottom-up control refers to regulation of abundance **OR** community structure of higher trophic levels **OR** fish by nutrient availability **OR** primary production **OR** resource limitation [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ algal abundance is influenced **OR** limited by nutrient availability **OR** light **OR** other suitable factors [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ if nutrient availability increases it can promote growth of algal **OR** phytoplankton **OR** if nutrient availability decreases leads to reduced population size of algae **OR** phytoplankton [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ if nutrients are restricted it can lead to reduced populations of algae **OR** phytoplankton, which leads to reduction in population size of snails **OR** fish [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ increased algal population, provides food source for snails so snail population increases providing more food for fish and fish population increase [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The difference between top-down control and bottom-up control is the direction of influence within a lake ecosystem. **Top-down** control occurs when **predators**, such as **fish**, exert **control** over **herbivores**, like **snails**, which, in turn, **indirectly** influence the abundance of **lower** trophic level **organisms**, including **algae** ($\checkmark$).

 Conversely, **bottom up** control is characterised by the **dependence** of **fish** populations **on** environmental factors, such as **nutrient availability** ($\checkmark$) which directly impacts the **growth** of primary producers such as **algae** ($\checkmark$). The increased abundance of **algae** then **promotes** the growth of herbivores, like **snails** and **subsequently** affects other consumers, including **fish** ($\checkmark$).
