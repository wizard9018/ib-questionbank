# BI1177

The photomicrograph shows a section through a shoot apex, with the sunlight coming from the left side. Which sequence shows the route taken by auxin from its production to its role in phototropism?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
1 is the meristem, 4 is the base of the shoot and 2 and 3 are the sunny and shaded side, respectively. Auxin is produced in the meristem of the stem tip and is moved to the shady side by efflux pumps. The resulting high concentrations on the shady sides cause cell elongation; **Choice A**  is correct.

Auxin is not produced in the base of the shoot, nor does it move to the sunny side or inhibit mitosis; **Choice B**  is incorrect. Auxin does not move to the sunny side or inhibit cell elongation; **Choice C**  is incorrect.  Auxin does not directly promote mitosis; **Choice D**  is incorrect.
