# BI0484

Which hormone is secreted by the gland labelled X?

![BI0484](images/BI0484.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Adrenaline

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Thyroxin
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Progesterone
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Melatonin

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because the gland X is the pineal gland, and it produces melatonin, a hormone that regulates circadian rhythms (sleep-wake cycles).

 **Choice A** is incorrect because adrenaline (epinephrine) is produced by the adrenal glands.

 **Choice B** is incorrect because thyroxin is produced by the thyroid gland.

 **Choice C** is incorrect because progesterone is produced by the corpus luteum in the ovary and by the placenta during pregnancy.
