# BI2585

The table shows the data collected from an experiment which investigated the effect of cytokinin concentration on root and shoot growth.

| Cytokinin concentration / mg dm$^{-3}$ | Root growth / mm | Shoot growth / mm |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 0.0 | 11 | 78 |
| 0.2 | 32 | 57 |
| 0.5 | 49 | 54 |
| 1.0 | 70 | 38 |
| 2.0 | 79 | 33 |
| 4.0 | 62 | 19 |

If a plant is treated with 5 mg dm$^{-3}$ cytokinin, what would be expected?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Shoot growth will be at its lowest and root growth will increase.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Shoot growth will be at its highest and root growth will decrease.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Root growth will increase and shoot growth will be its lowest.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Root growth and shoot growth will both decrease.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because based on the data in the table, root growth and shoot growth decrease from $2.0-4.0$ mg dm$^{-3}$, so this trend will likely continue if increased to 5 mg dm$^{-3}$.

**Choice A** is incorrect because we are unable to determine if shoot growth will reach its lowest point, and root growth is unlikely to increase. **Choice B** is incorrect because we are unable to determine if shoot growth will be at its highest point; this is also very unlikely based on the data. **Choice C** is incorrect because root growth is unlikely to increase.
