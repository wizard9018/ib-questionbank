# BI1298

Which phytohormone’s production brings about the ripening of fruit?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Auxin

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Cytokinin

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Gibberellin

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Ethylene

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Ethylene is a phytohormone whose production starts to increase as the fruit approaches maturity, and it plays a vital role in initiating and regulating the ripening process; **Choice D**  is correct.

**Choices A, B** and **C**  are incorrect as they are all involved in plant growth and development.
