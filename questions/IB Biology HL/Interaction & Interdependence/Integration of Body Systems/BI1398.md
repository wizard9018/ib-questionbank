# BI1398

Which plant tropism is correctly matched with its response?

| | Tropism | Response |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Positive phototropism | Shoot growth towards light |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Positive phototropism | Root growth towards light |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Negative phototropism | Root growth towards light |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Negative phototropism | Shoot growth towards light |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because phototropism is the ability of plants to respond to light. Differential auxin concentrations in the stem allow plants to grow towards the light. This is positive phototropism.

**Choices B, C** and **D** are incorrect, as the tropisms are mismatched with the responses.
