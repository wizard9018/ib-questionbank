# BI1060

**Maximum mark: 5**

The autonomic nervous system helps regulate the control of heart rate. Below is a diagram of the human brain.

![BI1060a](images/BI1060a.png)

1. State the name of the part of the brain that is involved in the control of heart rate. [1 mark]

2. Baroreceptors and chemoreceptors play an important role in this control. Compare and contrast how these two types of receptors help with the regulation of heart rate. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ medulla **OR** medulla oblongata [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The part of the brain responsible for the control of heart rate is the **medulla** ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **four** of the following (must include at least one similarity and one difference):

 **Similarities:** \
 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** located at aorta and carotid arteries [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** send information to medulla [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ stimulation of **BOTH** baroreceptors and chemoreceptors can cause decrease in heart rate **OR** stroke volume [1 mark]

 **Differences:** \
 $\ce{ -> }$ baroreceptors located inside aorta and carotid arteries **WHEREAS** chemoreceptors are located in tissue on outside of aorta and carotid arteries [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ baroreceptors detect changes in blood pressure **WHEREAS** chemoreceptors detect changes in pH of blood **OR** oxygen concentration **OR** carbon dioxide concentration [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ baroreceptors only send action potentials when there is an increase in blood pressure **OR** stretching of artery walls **WHEREAS** chemoreceptors send action potentials when there is an increase or decrease away from the ‘norm' [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ three different types of chemoreceptors **OR** chemoreceptors to detect oxygen levels, carbon dioxide levels and pH **WHEREAS** baroreceptors only detect stretching of walls of arteries [1 mark]
[4 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Both baroreceptors and chemoreceptors send impulses to the **medulla oblongata** ($\checkmark$) of the brain when stimulated to control heart rate. Also both types of receptors are located at the **aorta and carotid arteries** ($\checkmark$), but exact location is different, while baroreceptors are **inside the arterial tissue**, chemoreceptors are located in the tissues **adjacent to the arteries** ($\checkmark$). Additionally, the signal those receptors detect are different: baroreceptors detect the changes in **pressure** by measuring the stretch of the walls of the arteries, while chemoreceptors can detect the change in the **pH levels** ($\checkmark$).
