 2. any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{->}$ phototropins in the stem tip detect higher light intensity on one side [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ auxin efflux carriers **OR** pumps become repositioned on shaded side **OR** to transport auxin laterally toward the shaded side [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ auxin diffuses freely **OR** passively moves into the cells of the stem tip but efflux **OR** movement out is active **OR** ATP-driven **OR** pumped out [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ a auxin accumulates on the shaded side of the stem tip, creating a concentration gradien [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ auxin binds to receptors in target cells initiating changes in gene expression **OR** tiggereing transcription of genes related to elongation [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ auxin stimulates proton pumps to secrete H⁺ ions into the cell wall **OR** to acidify cell wall [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ acidification loosens cellulose cross-links [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ allows cells on the shaded side to elongate more than those on the lit side the stem bends toward the light **OR** causes greater elongation on shaded side [1 mark]

 $\ce{->}$ this growth response increases exposure of leaves to sunlight **OR** causes stem tip to grow towards sun [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 **Phototropins** in the stem tip **detect higher light intensity** on **one side** of the shoot ($\checkmark$). **Auxin efflux carriers** are repositioned to **transport auxin** toward the **shaded side** ($\checkmark$). This causes **auxin** to **accumulate** on the **shaded side**, creating a **concentration gradient** ($\checkmark$). **Auxin stimulates proton pumps** to secrete **hydrogen ions** into the **cell wall**, **acidifying it** and allowing **cell elongation** ($\checkmark$).
 If higher light intensities are detected, by **phototropins**, on **one side** of the plant ($\checkmark$), then **efflux pumps** transport auxin to the more **shaded** side ($\checkmark$). This leads to an **increase in auxin concentration** on the more shaded side of the plant ($\checkmark$), which stimulates **cell elongation** ($\checkmark$) and results in the stem tip growing towards the **sun** or light source ($\checkmark$).
