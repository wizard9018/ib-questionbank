# BI0353

Which of the following is a function of auxin efflux pumps?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ To move auxin upwards in a stem

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ To move auxin to the shaded side of a stem

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ To move auxin to the sunny side of a stem

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ To move auxin into flowers

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because in phototropism auxin efflux pumps move auxin to the shady side of the stem, which stimulates growth and results in the bending of the stem towards the light source. **Choice A** is incorrect because auxin moves downwards in stems. **Choice C** is incorrect because auxin efflux pumps move auxin to the shady side of a stem. **Choice D** is incorrect because auxin does not move into flowers.
