1.
    1. State the name of the hormone released from the adrenal glands that is responsible for the ‘fight or flight' response.    [1 mark]

    2. Explain how secretion of the hormone you named in part (i) affects the heart to prepare the body for vigorous exercise.    [3 marks]

2. Suggest an effect on the body if the adrenal glands do not function properly.    [1 mark]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1. any **one** of the following:

        $\ce{ -> }$ epinephrine **OR** adrenaline    [1 mark]
        [1 mark]

        $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
        Epinephrine ($\checkmark$).

1.
    2. any **three** of the following:

        $\ce{ -> }$ binds to receptors at the sinoatrial node, increasing heart rate    [1 mark]

        $\ce{ -> }$ an increased heart rate pumps blood more rapidly to working muscles        [1 mark]

        $\ce{ -> }$ delivers more oxygen and glucose for increased aerobic respiration        [1 mark]

        $\ce{ -> }$ greater ATP supply prepares the muscles for vigorous activity [1 mark]
        [3 marks]

        $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
        Epinephrine binds to the **sinoatrial node** of the heart and **increases heart rate**  ($\checkmark$). This allows **blood** to reach the **muscles more rapidly**, delivering more **oxygen** and **glucose** ($\checkmark$). The increase in **aerobic respiration**  produces **more ATP**, preparing the body for vigorous exercise  ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ altered **OR** slow response to stress **OR** stress response impairment [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ low energy levels **OR** tiredness                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ low blood pressure                            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ stomach pain when stressed                    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ metabolic disruption **OR** change in body weight **OR** blood sugar irregularities     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ immune system suppression                    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ mood swings **OR** changes in emotions            [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    If the adrenal glands did not work properly, then a person might have an **altered response to stress** ($\checkmark$).
