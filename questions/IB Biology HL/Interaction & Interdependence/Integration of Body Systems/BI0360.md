# BI0360

Which sequence shows the route taken by auxin from its production to its role in phototropism?

| | Step 1 | Step 2| Step 3 |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Produced in meristem | Moves to shaded side| Inhibits cell elongation
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Produced on shady side | Changes gene expression | Promotes cell elongation |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Produced on sunny side | Changes gene expression | Inhibits cell elongation |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Produced in meristem |Moves to shaded side | Promotes cell elongation|

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because auxin is produced at the meristem in the stem tip and is moved to the shady side by efflux pumps, and the high concentrations on the shady side cause cell elongation. **Choice A** is incorrect because auxin does not inhibit cell elongation on the shady side. **Choice B** is incorrect because auxin is not produced on the shady side. **Choice C** is incorrect because auxin is produced at the meristem in the stem tip, not only on the shady side, and it does not inhibit cell elongation in stems.
