# BI1299

**Maximum mark: 16**

1. Compare and contrast the principal sources of auxin and cytokinins and their roles in plant growth.        [4 marks]

2. Describe how positive feedback is involved in synchronised and rapid fruit ripening.        [5 marks]

3. Explain how the phototropic response, mediated by auxin in the shoot, triggers changes in the cell wall structure.    [7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **four** of the following (*answer must include at least **one** similarity and **one** difference to gain full marks*):

***Differences***

$\ce{ -> }$ auxin is produced mainly in shoot apical meristems **OR** young leaves, **WHEREAS** cytokinins are produced mainly in root apical meristems      [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ auxin primarily promotes cell elongation, **WHEREAS** cytokinins primarily promote cell division     [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ auxin from the shoot apex promotes apical dominance **OR** inhibits lateral bud growth, **WHEREAS** cytokinins promote lateral bud growth         [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ auxin promotes the initiation of lateral **OR** adventitious roots, **WHEREAS** a relatively high cytokinin concentration promotes shoot formation     [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ auxin delays leaf **OR** fruit abscission, **WHEREAS** cytokinins delay leaf senescence     [1 mark]

***Similarities***

$\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** are chemical messengers that regulate plant growth and development at low concentrations      [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** are produced in actively growing plant tissues      [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** regulate the growth and development of roots and shoots      [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ the relative concentrations of **BOTH** hormones help coordinate root and shoot development      [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** can be transported from their principal sites of production to other parts of the plant      [1 mark]

[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:

**Auxin** is produced mainly in **shoot apical meristems and young leaves**, whereas **cytokinins** are produced mainly in **root apical meristems** ($\checkmark$). **Both hormones** regulate the growth and development of **roots and shoots** ($\checkmark$). Auxin primarily promotes **cell elongation**, whereas cytokinins primarily promote **cell division** ($\checkmark$). **Auxin** from the **shoot apex** inhibits **lateral bud growth**, whereas **cytokinins** promote lateral bud growth ($\checkmark$).

2. any **five** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ positive feedback is when a change stimulates further change in the same direction **OR** the output of a process increases the rate of that process        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ ethylene **OR** ethene is produced in ripening fruit **OR** maturing fruit        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ ethylene **OR** ethene speeds up the ripening process of the fruit that produced it     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ as the fruit ripens further, it produces more ethylene    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ nearby fruit detect ethylene **OR** ethene        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ nearby fruit starts to ripen                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ overall ethylene **OR** ethene production increases        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ more fruit ripen **OR** a large number of fruit ripen **OR** fruit ripening is synchronised due to the amplifying nature of the positive feedback loop        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ synchronised ripening means many fruits are ripe at the same time, attracting seed-dispersing animals **OR** making seed dispersal more efficient    [1 mark]
    [5 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    **Positive feedback** is when a **change** stimulates **further change** in the **same direction** ($\checkmark$). This can occur in synchronised and rapid fruit ripening, where **ethylene** is produced in **ripening fruits**  ($\checkmark$), and other nearby fruits **detect** the ethylene ($\checkmark$), so they **also** begin to **ripen** ($\checkmark$). The ripe fruit then releases its **own ethylene**, so that even **more** nearby fruit **ripens**, so ripening is **rapid** and **synchronised** ($\checkmark$).

3. any **seven** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ auxin is positively phototropic **OR** is produced in the shoot tip **OR** coleoptile in response to light            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ light causes auxin to diffuse into cells on the shaded side of the shoot, **OR** light causes auxin to be actively transported out of cells on the illuminated side of the shoot     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ auxin accumulates on the dark side of the shoot    **OR** auxin redistributes to the shaded side [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ auxin stimulates the activity of proton pumps in the cell membrane    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ proton pumps actively transport protons (H:sup[+]) from the cytoplasm into the cell wall    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ auxin promotes hydrogen ion secretion into  non-living part of the cells **OR** cell walls/cell wall junctions    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ acidifying the cell wall    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ low pH activates expansins    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ altering the pattern of hydrogen bonding in polysaccharides in the cell wall **OR** expansins trigger the breakage of crosslinks within cellulose **OR** expansins break hydrogen bonds between cellulose microfibrils and other cell wall components        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the resistance of the cell wall to stretching **OR** cell elongation is decreased, **OR** cell wall becomes more flexible, **OR** cell can take up water more readily, leading to turgor pressure-driven cell expansion or elongation                    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ cell turgor causes stretching **OR** elongation of the cell wall            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ auxin regulates gene expression to promote cell elongation on the shaded side    [1 mark]
    [7 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Auxin is a phytohormone, produced in **shoot tips** in response to **light**  ($\checkmark$). Light causes **auxin** to **diffuse** into cells on the **shaded** side of the shoot ($\checkmark$) so that it **accumulates** in the **shaded** side ($\checkmark$). Auxin promotes **hydrogen ion secretion** into the **cell walls and cell wall junctions**  ($\checkmark$), which **acidifies** the **cell wall** ($\checkmark$). Low pH activates proteins called **expansins** ($\checkmark$), and expansins alter the pattern of **hydrogen bonding in polysaccharides** in the cell wall ($\checkmark$), which **lowers** the resistance of cell walls to **stretching**  ($\checkmark$). As **water enters** the cell the **turgor pressure** causes the **stretching** and **elongation** of the cell ($\checkmark$).
