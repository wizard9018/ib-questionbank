# BI0359

Which statement correctly describes the role of auxin in phototropism?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ High concentrations stimulate cell elongation in the stem.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Low concentrations stimulate cell elongation in the stem.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ High concentrations inhibit cell division in the stem.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Low concentrations inhibit cell division in the stem.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because high auxin concentrations stimulate cell elongation in the stem tip. **Choice B** is incorrect because low auxin concentrations do not stimulate cell elongation in the stem tip. **Choice C** and **Choice D** are incorrect because in phototropism auxin does not inhibit cell division, it affects cell elongation.
