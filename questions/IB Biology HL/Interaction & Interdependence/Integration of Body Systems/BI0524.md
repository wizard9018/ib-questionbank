# BI0524

Plant shoots detect and respond to light. Which phytohormone is responsible for positive phototropism in shoots, and what mechanism is responsible for its directional movement?

| | Protein | Mechanism |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Auxin | Passive efflux channels |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Cytokinin  | Passive efflux channels |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Auxin | Active efflux pumps |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ |  Cytokinin | Active efflux pumps |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Auxin is the phytohormone responsible for positive phototropism in shoots. Active efflux pumps transport auxin laterally from the illuminated side to the shaded side of the shoot. This causes differential growth, bending the shoot toward the light. **Choice C** is the correct answer.

Passive auxin-influx channels allow facilitated diffusion of auxin into the cells, but it is efflux pumps that actively transport auxin out of the cell and direct its redistribution. Cytokinins are not involved in phototropism; they primarily regulate cell division and shoot/root development. **Choices A, B** and **D** are all incorrect because they include either passive movement, cytokinins or both.
