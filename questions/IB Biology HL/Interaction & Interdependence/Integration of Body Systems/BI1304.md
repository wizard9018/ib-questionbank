# BI1304

Which of the following statements describes the effect of epinephrine on the body?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Causes the sinoatrial node to increase the heart rate |
    | II. | Stimulates the liver cells to convert glucagon into glucose |
    | III. | Causes the pupils to dilate |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Epinephrine is a peptide hormone secreted by the adrenal glands to prepare for vigorous activity. It causes the sinoatrial node (pacemaker) to increase the heart rate (**Statement I**) and the pupils to dilate (**Statement III**). **Choice C**  is correct.

Glucagon is a hormone released by the pancreas in response to low blood glucose levels; it converts stored glycogen into glucose. Epinephrine initiates the conversion of glycogen into glucose; **Statement II**  is incorrect. **Choices A, B** and **D**  are incorrect as they all include **Statement II**.
