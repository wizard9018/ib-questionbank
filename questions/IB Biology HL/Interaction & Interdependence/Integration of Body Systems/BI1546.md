# BI1546

**Maximum mark: 6**

Explain how the ventilation rate in humans changes with strenuous exercise.         [6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

**Maximum mark: 6**

Any **six** of the following:

$\ce{ -> }$ rate of cellular respiration increases with exercise    [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ increased concentration of carbon dioxide in blood        [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ blood becomes more acidic **OR** blood pH decreases        [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ decrease in pH **OR** increase in hydrogen ion concentration detected by chemoreceptors  [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ impulse **OR** signal sent to respiratory control centre **OR** inspiratory centre     [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ located in the **OR** medulla oblongata **OR** brainstem        [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ impulse **OR** signal sent to intercostal muscles **OR** diaphragm to increase rate **OR** strength of contraction [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ tidal volume **OR** depth of breathing increases        [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ training reduces resting breathing **OR** ventilation rate        [1 mark]
[6 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
During strenuous exercise, the **rate** of **cellular respiration increases** ($\checkmark$), leading to a **higher concentration** of **carbon dioxide** in the blood ($\checkmark$), which **lowers blood pH** ($\checkmark$) and makes it more acidic. **Chemoreceptors** such as those in the **aorta**, **detect** this **decrease in pH** ($\checkmark$) and send **nervous impulses** to the **respiratory control centre** in the **medulla oblongata** ($\checkmark$). The **medulla** then sends nervous **impulses** to the **intercostal muscles** and **diaphragm** to **increase** their **contraction rate** ($\checkmark$), resulting in an **increased breathing rate** ($\checkmark$) to excrete more carbon dioxide and take up more oxygen.
