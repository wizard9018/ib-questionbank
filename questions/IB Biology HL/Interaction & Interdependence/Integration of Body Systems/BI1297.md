# BI1297

Which row correctly identifies how auxin moves into and out of shoot cells during phototropism?

| | Movement into cell | Movement out of cell |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Facilitated diffusion | Simple diffusion |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Simple diffusion | Active transport |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Endocytosis | Active transport |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Active transport | Simple Diffusion |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Auxin can diffuse freely into plant cells but cannot diffuse out through the plasma membrane. Auxin efflux carriers actively transport auxin out of cells. When these carriers are concentrated on the same side of successive cells, they produce directional transport of auxin through the shoot and allow auxin to accumulate on the shaded side. Therefore, **Choice B**  is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because auxin enters cells by simple diffusion rather than facilitated diffusion and does not leave by simple diffusion.

**Choice C** is incorrect because auxin does not enter cells by endocytosis, although its movement out of cells involves active transport.

**Choice D** is incorrect because auxin enters cells by simple diffusion and leaves through efflux carriers by active transport, not the reverse.
