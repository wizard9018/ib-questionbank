# BI1688

Antibodies pass across the placenta from mother to baby. What type of immunity is this an example of?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Natural active

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Natural passive

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Artificial active

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Artificial passive

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

Passive immunity occurs when antibodies are transferred from one individual to another, rather than being produced by the recipient’s immune system. Natural immunity refers to immunity acquired without medical intervention. Since the antibodies are naturally transferred from mother to baby via the placenta, this classifies as passive natural immunity.   **Choice B** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because active immunity requires the body to produce its own antibodies after exposure to a pathogen. In this case, the baby receives pre-made antibodies from the mother, making it passive, not active, immunity.

**Choice C** is incorrect because artificial immunity involves medical interventions, such as vaccines, which stimulate the immune system to produce its own antibodies. Since no vaccine or medical treatment is involved, this is not artificial active immunity.

**Choice D** is incorrect because, although passive immunity is correct, artificial passive immunity involves medical treatment (e.g., antibody injections). Since the antibodies were transferred naturally across the placenta, this is natural, not artificial, passive immunity.
