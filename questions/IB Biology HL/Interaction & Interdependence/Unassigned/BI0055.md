# BI0055

The graph shows the changes in blood pH levels of an individual before exercise, during exercise and after exercise.

![BI0055](images/BI0055.png)

Which statement may explain the changes in blood pH shown in the graph?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The blood pH decreases due to the build-up of lactate.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The blood pH decreases due to muscle cells undergoing anaerobic respiration in the presence of oxygen.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The blood pH decreases due to the continued anaerobic respiration caused by oxygen debt.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The blood pH decreases due to the large yield of ATP produced during strenuous exercise.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because during strenuous exercise, muscle cells may not receive enough oxygen to meet energy demands through aerobic respiration alone. As a result, some muscle cells begin anaerobic respiration, producing lactate. Lactate accumulation lowers blood pH, explaining the decrease shown in the graph.

**Choice B** is incorrect because anaerobic respiration occurs in the absence of oxygen, not in the presence of oxygen.

**Choice C** is incorrect because oxygen debt develops after strenuous exercise due to insufficient oxygen availability, but it is not the direct cause of the decrease in blood pH. The decrease in pH is caused by lactate accumulation.

**Choice D** is incorrect because anaerobic respiration produces only a small yield of ATP per glucose molecule. ATP production itself does not directly decrease blood pH.
