# BI0136

**Maximum mark: 4**

1. Compare and contrast forests and marine phytoplankton in their role in the carbon cycle. [2 marks]

2. Describe how aquatic autotrophs absorb carbon dioxide from the surroundings.  [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ **both** are autotrophs **OR  both** carry out photosynthesis     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ trees in forests absorb carbon dioxide from the air **WHILE**  marine phytoplankton absorbs carbon dioxide dissolved in water [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ ancient trees were compressed beneath the soil and formed coal **WHILE** phytoplankton were compressed beneath sediment and formed oil and natural gas [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Both marine phytoplankton and trees carry out **photosynthesis** ($\checkmark$).  However, the trees in the forests take in carbon dioxide from the **air** while marine phytoplankton take in **dissolved carbon dioxide** from the water ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ carbon dioxide in water is absorbed by aquatic autotrophs by diffusion  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ carbon dioxide dissolves in water to form carbonic acid     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ carbonic acid can dissociate to form hydrogen and hydrogen carbonate ions  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ aquatic autotrophs use both dissolved carbon dioxide and hydrogen carbonate ions for photosynthesis [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    In water; **carbon dioxide forms carbonic acid** ($\checkmark$) which can **dissociate** to form hydrogen and **hydrogen carbonate ions** ($\checkmark$). The aquatic **autotrophs** can use both dissolved **carbon dioxide** and  **hydrogen carbonate ions** for **photosynthesis** ($\checkmark$).
