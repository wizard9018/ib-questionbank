# BI0772

**Maximum mark: 10**

In an investigation into the impacts of climate change on the rate of photosynthesis, pondweed was placed in a number of sealed conical flasks, each containing a solution of water and carbonic acid.

![BI0772A](images/BI0772A.png)
[ 2022. Created with Chemix (https://chemix.org)]

Each conical flask was placed in a water bath maintained at a constant temperature. At regular time intervals, samples of the solution were taken from each conical flask and measured using a pH probe.

![BI0772B](images/BI0772B.png)
[ 2022. Created with Chemix (https://chemix.org)]

The results are shown in the table.

![BI0772C](images/BI0772C.png)

1.
 1. Identify the dependent and independent variables in this investigation. [2 marks]

 2. State one variable that must be kept constant in this investigation to ensure the results are valid. [1 mark]

2. Describe the relationship between the temperature, time and pH of the solution. [2 marks]

3. Predict the pH of the dilute carbonic acid solution at 40 :sup[o]C after 60 minutes. [1 mark]

4.
 1. Calculate the rate of change in pH between 30 - 40 minutes and 40 - 50 minutes for the trial kept at 40 :sup[o]C. [2 marks]

 2. Suggest a reason for the difference in the rate of change you calculated in (i). [1 mark]

5. Suggest one improvement to the design of this experiment and state whether it would increase the **accuracy** or **reliability** of the results. [1 mark]
