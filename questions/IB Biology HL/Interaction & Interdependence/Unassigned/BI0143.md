# BI0143

![BI0143](images/BI0143.png)
[Source: Adapted from  Semhur, S.(n.d.). *File: Mauna Loa carbon dioxide-en svg.* Wikimedia Commons. Retrieved April 27, 2022, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Mauna_Loa_Carbon_Dioxide-en.svg. Copyright free]

The above data obtained from Mauna Loa show trends in atmospheric CO$_{2}$ concentrations. What conclusions can be made from this graph?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
  | | |
  | :-- | :-- |
  | I. | There is an annual fluctuation of carbon dioxide concentration in the atmosphere. |
  | II. | There is a continuous increase in the average concentration of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere over the years. |
  | III. | Increased use of fossil fuels has caused an increase in carbon dioxide in the atmosphere as recorded in Mauna Loa. |

- A. I only

- B. I and II only

- C. I and III only

- D. I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Explanation:

The data show an annual fluctuation in the concentration of carbon dioxide **(I)** and a constant increase in average concentration over the many years measured **(II)**.

**Choice A** includes annual fluctuations but not a constant increase in carbon dioxide.

**Choice C** includes a conclusion about annual fluctuations but also the conclusion that says increased use of fossil fuels has caused the increase in carbon dioxide in the atmosphere **(III)** which cannot be determined based on this graph.

**Choice D** includes all three conclusions and since conclusion III is incorrect, it is not the right choice.
