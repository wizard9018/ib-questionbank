# BI0775

**Maximum mark: 7**

1\.
A student had a small pond in the backyard and wanted to add some fish. The fish would be predators of the pond and feed on the zooplankton (prey) growing in the pond. Zooplankton are small floating or weakly swimming organisms that are dependent on the phytoplankton or algae for their supply of nutrition.

She wanted to first determine whether Fish A or Fish B would be more suitable, or if both fish could be added to the pond. As a trial, she set up four mesocosms using water from the pond. She monitored the mesocosms and then added the fish to three of the mesocosms as shown. She took samples of water from the different mesocosms and tested them for chlorophyll content at a research lab nearby.

![BI0775](images/BI0775.png)

1. Outline why the mesocosms used would be considered a ‘closed system’. [1 mark]

2. Describe why adding mud or soil to the mesocosms would be advantageous. [1 mark]

3. Outline the reason for including ‘Mesocosm A’ with the experiment. [1 mark]

4. Explain the reason for monitoring chlorophyll content to analyse the effects of the fish as a predator. [1 mark]

5. State another variable that could be monitored to see the effect of adding fish to the mesocosms. [1 mark]

The following table shows the results obtained for chlorophyll (μg dm:sup[-3]) with the different additions of fish.

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ energy can enter and leave the mesocosm system,(even if mesocosms are covered) [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ matter **OR** organisms, cannot enter or leave the mesocosm system, mesocosms are covered [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The mesocosms are a ‘closed system’ because they are covered, and though energy can enter and leave the system, the **matter is not added or removed from the system** ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **one** of the following

 $\ce{ -> }$ mud will contain saprotrophs **OR** detritus [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ mud will aid decomposition and cycling of nutrients in the mesocosm [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ mud provides nutrients for algae [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ mud increases habitat complexity so more surfaces for microorganisms to attach [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ more realistic ecosystem- more closely mimics the pond ecosystem [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 If mud is added to the mesocosms, this would also add decomposed material and aid in the **decomposition and cycling of nutrients** ($\checkmark$) by bacteria and other organisms.

3. Any **one** of the following

 $\ce{ -> }$ control for the experiment [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ obtain results without fish to compare results when fish are added [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Mesocosm A serves as a **control for the experiment** ($\checkmark$) and shows the values for chlorophyll when no fish are added.

4. Any **one** of the following

 $\ce{ -> }$ chlorophyll concentrations will indicate how much (green) algae is present in the system [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the zooplankton eat the algae, so it is an indirect measure of zooplankton levels [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the more the algae, the lesser the levels of zooplankton [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ It is possible **OR** easier to obtain algae levels by measuring chlorophyll concentrations [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 It is possible to **estimate algae levels** ($\checkmark$) in the system by measuring chlorophyll concentrations. As the fish eat the zooplankton, algal levels might increase, so it is an indirect measure of the zooplankton present.

5. Any **one** of the following

 $\ce{ -> }$ the zooplankton levels could be monitored [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the zooplankton could be counted directly (using microscopy and counting of the organisms) [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ diversity of zooplankton could be measured [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ water quality **OR** dissolved oxygen levels **OR** pH **OR** nutrient levels **OR** turbidity **OR** nitrate levels **OR** phosphate levels [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The **zooplankton populations** ($\checkmark$) could be monitored directly by counting them.

6. Any **two** of the following

 $\ce{ -> }$ the prey **OR** zooplankton populations were maintained in the presence of the predator **OR** fish [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ both fish A and fish B did not decrease the zooplankton population significantly when both were added together [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the fish A and fish B added together did not interfere with the stability of the system [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ adding fish A and B together did not significantly affect primary productivity **OR** chlorophyll levels [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The **zooplankton populations were maintained** ($\checkmark$) when fish A and fish B were added together. The addition of **both fish did not decrease** ($\checkmark$) the zooplankton population. Both fish could survive together in the mesocosms.
