# BI0776

**Maximum mark: 7**

A student wanted to study how food chains in leaf litter can be affected by the mass of initial leaf litter added. The student placed an increasing mass of leaf litter in three different tree holes and waited a few weeks. To collect data, the student collected all the leaf litter and placed it in an apparatus, as shown below. The heat from the light bulb dried out the litter from above and the heat and light drove the invertebrates through the wire mesh to the beaker below. The invertebrates were collected, the number of species counted, the species were identified and then they were put into their respective trophic groups.

3. Any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ fresh leaf litter may have different proportions of moisture [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ dry biomass gives a better measure of the mass of organic matter in the leaf litter available to the invertebrates [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ different proportions of water in fresh leaf litter can skew the results obtained **OR** make it difficult to compare results between samples [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 To compare the results obtained, it is better to use the biomass of dry leaf litter. Based on the position of the tree hole, such as the sunny or shady side, **the different samples of leaf litter could have different proportions of water** ($\checkmark$) and will affect the results obtained.
