1. Explain the changes in membrane potential between II and IV. [2 marks]

2. Use the graph to estimate the number of impulses per minute that this neuron would likely transmit if there were 2 ms between the end of one action potential and the start of the next. [2 marks]

3. Researchers used oscilloscope traces to study how the membrane potential changed in the fingertips of professional basketball players when different levels of pressure were applied. Five players participated in the study, with three trials conducted for each of the three pressure levels.

 1. State **two** factors that need to be controlled in this investigation. [2 marks]

 2. Suggest a control experiment that could be used for this investigation. [1 mark]

1. any **two** of the following *(must include **one** statement for depoarisation, and **one** statement for between repolarisation to gain full marks)*:

 **Depolarisation:**\
 $\ce{ -> }$ at II, the membrane potential is -55 mV **OR** reaches the threshold potential, **WHICH** causes the voltage-gated sodium channels to open [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ at III, the diffusion of sodium ions into the neuron causes depolarisation **OR** the membrane to change from negative to positive **OR** to between +30 mV and +40 mV [1 mark]

 **Repolarisation**\
 $\ce{ -> }$ at approximately +30 mV voltage-gated sodium channels shut and voltage-gated potassium channels open, **WHICH** causes potassium ions to diffuse out of the neuron [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ diffusion of potassium ions out of the neuron through potassium channels causes repolarisation **OR** makes membrane potential negative again **OR** makes the inside negative compared to the outside **OR** changes the membrane potential towards -70mV **OR** resting potential **OR** -80mV [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 At point II, the membrane potential reaches the **threshold** (-55 mV), **causing voltage-gated sodium channels to open** ($\checkmark$) and **sodium ions** to **enter** the neuron, leading to **depolarisation** ($\checkmark$). Around **+40mV voltage-gated potassium channels close** and **voltage-gated potassium channels open** ($\checkmark$) allowing **potassium ions to move out** of the neuron, which causes **repolarisation** ($\checkmark$).

2. **both** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ 2.6 ms, *(accept answers in range 2.3 ms - 2.8 ms)* **OR** 4.6 ms, *(accept answers in teh range 4.3 ms - 4.8 ms)* [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ 13,043 min$^{-1}$ *(accept answers in range 12,500 - 14,000)* [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:

 ![Ans](images/Ans.png)

 Start of depolarisation =1.2 ms,\
 Restoration of resting potential = 3.8 ms

 Time for one action potential = 3.8 ms - 1.2 ms = **2.6 ms** ($\checkmark$)\
 Interval between each action potential = 2ms \
 Total time between start of one action potential and start of next = 2.6 ms + 2.0 ms = **4.6 ms** ($\checkmark$)\
 1 min = 60,000 ms\
 Number of impulses in one minute = $\dfrac{\mathbf{60,000}}{\mathbf{4.6}}$ = **13,043 min**$^{-1}$ ($\checkmark$).
