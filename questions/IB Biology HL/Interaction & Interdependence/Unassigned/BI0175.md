# BI0175

**Maximum mark: 7**

Compare and contrast the light-dependent and light-independent reactions. [7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **seven** of the following (*must include at least **two** similarities and **two** differences to gain full marks*):

    ***Similarities***

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** reactions take place in the chloroplast   [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** reactions involve enzymes   [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** reactions can take place during day time     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** reactions are part of photosynthesis [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** reactions are linked OR dependent on the activity of the other [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** rely on coenzymes [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** involve energy transfer [1 mark]

    ***Differences***

    $\ce{ -> }$ light-dependent reactions take place in the intermembrane space of the thylakoid and thylakoid membrane **WHEREAS** light-independent reactions take place in the stroma  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ light-dependent reactions require light, **WHEREAS**  the light-independent reactions do not require light       [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ oxygen is formed in the light-dependent reactions **WHEREAS**  sugar **OR** carbohydrates are formed in the light-independent reactions  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ electrons and protons for the light-dependent reactions are supplied from the photolysis of water **WHEREAS**   electrons and protons in the light-independent reaction come from reduced NADPH     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ ATP is formed in the light-dependent reactions **WHEREAS**  ATP is broken down to supply energy in the light-independent reactions [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ NADP is reduced **OR** NADP accepts electrons and protons in the light-dependent reactions **WHEREAS**  NADPH is oxidised **OR** reduced NADP donates electrons and protons in light-independent reaction        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ light-dependent reactions involve an electron transport chain and chemiosmosis WHEREAS light-independent reactions involve carbon fixation and the Calvin cycle
    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ light-dependent reactions produce short-term energy molecules **OR** ATP and NADP **WHEREAS**   light-independent reactions produce long-term energy storage molecules
 [1 mark]
[7 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    The similarities between the light-dependent reactions and light-independent reactions are that both of these reactions are **part of photosynthesis** ($\checkmark$) which is conducted by photosynthesizing organisms. Both reactions **involve enzymes** ($\checkmark$) such as the cytochrome b6f complex in the light-dependent reactions and rubisco in the light-independent reactions.

    The differences include that the light-dependent reactions take place in the **intermembrane space as well as the thylakoid membranes** whereas the light-independent reactions take place in the **stroma** of the chloroplast ($\checkmark$). The light-dependent reactions **need light** for photophosphorylation, but the light-independent reactions **do not need light** ($\checkmark$). In the light-dependent reactions, **oxygen** and **ATP are formed** ($\checkmark$) whereas in the light-independent reactions, **ATP is broken down** to supply energy to form **sugar** ($\checkmark$). **NADP is reduced** in light-dependent reactions, whereas in the light-independent reactions, the reduced NADP is **oxidised to form NADP** ($\checkmark$)  .
