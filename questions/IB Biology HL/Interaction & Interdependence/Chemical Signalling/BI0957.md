# BI0957

The diagram shows a flow chart.

![BI0957a](images/BI0957a.png)

Which is the correct sequence of reactions shown in the flow chart?

| | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-:
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Insulin binds to the intracellular domain of the tyrosine kinase receptor | Phosphorylation of tyrosine kinase receptor | Glucose transporter vesicles move to the plasma membrane |  Glucose enters the cell |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Phosphorylation of transmembrane domain of tyrosine kinase receptor | Insulin binds to the intracellular domain of the tyrosine kinase receptor | Glucose enters the cell | Glucose transporter vesicles move to the plasma membrane|
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Insulin binds to the extracellular domain of the tyrosine kinase receptor | Phosphorylation of tyrosine kinase receptor | Glucose transporter vesicles move to the plasma membrane | Glucose enters the cell |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Glucose enters the cell | Glucose transporter vesicles move to the plasma membrane | Phosphorylation of transmembrane domain of tyrosine kinase receptor| Insulin binds to the intracellular domain of the tyrosine kinase receptor |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because the sequence is in the right order. Insulin first needs to bind to the extracellular domain of the tyrosine kinase receptor to activate the kinase.

**Choices A**, **B** and **D** are incorrect because the sequence is in the wrong order. When specific ligands, such as insulin, activate the tyrosine kinase receptor, the phosphorylation of the intracellular domain occurs, which brings about further cascade events.
