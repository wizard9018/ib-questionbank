# BI2953

Which of the following rows gives features that correctly compare hormones and neurotransmitters?

| | Feature | Hormones | Neurotransmitters |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Distance of action | Short-distance | Long-distance |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Mode of transport | Carried by red blood cells | Carried in the plasma |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Duration of effect | Long lasting | Short-lived |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Site of release | Synaptic vesicles | Endocrine glands |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Hormones are carried in the blood to often distant target tissues and usually produce long-lasting effects. In contrast, neurotransmitters act rapidly and briefly across a synapse to enable fast neural communication. Therefore, **Choice C** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because hormones typically act over long distances via the bloodstream, while neurotransmitters act over very short distances across the synaptic cleft.

**Choice B** is incorrect because although hormones are transported in the blood, they are transported in the plasma, not by red blood cells. Also, neurotransmitters are not carried in the plasma; they diffuse freely across the synaptic cleft.

**Choice D** is incorrect because hormones are not released by synaptic vesicles; they are released by specific glands or organs. Additionally, neurotransmitters are not released by endocrine glands; they are released by synaptic vesicles into the synaptic cleft.
