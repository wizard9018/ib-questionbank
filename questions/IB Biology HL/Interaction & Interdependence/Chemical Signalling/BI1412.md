# BI1412

Which of the following shows the correct order of events for cellular responses due to the release of epinephrine (adrenaline)?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Adrenaline binds to surface receptor $\longrightarrow$ ATP converted to cAMP $\longrightarrow$ activation of phosphorylase kinase $\longrightarrow$ glycogen converted to glucose

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Adrenaline binds to surface receptor $\longrightarrow$ activation of phosphorylase kinase $\longrightarrow$ ATP converted to cAMP $\longrightarrow$ glycogen converted to glucose

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Adrenaline binds to surface receptor $\longrightarrow$ cAMP converted to ATP $\longrightarrow$ activation of glycogen phosphorylase $\longrightarrow$ glycogen converted to glucose

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Adrenaline binds to surface receptor $\longrightarrow$ cAMP converted to ATP $\longrightarrow$ activation of phosphorylase kinase $\longrightarrow$ glycogen converted to glucose

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
When epinephrine (adrenaline) is released into the blood, it binds to G protein-coupled receptors, activating G proteins, which in turn activate adenylyl (adenylate) cyclase. ATP molecules are then converted to cAMP, which activates phosphorylase kinase. The phosphorylase kinase activates glycogen phosphorylase, an enzyme that catalyses the breakdown of glycogen into glucose. **Choice A** is, therefore, correct.

**Choices B, C** and **D** are incorrect, as the sequences of events are in the wrong order.
