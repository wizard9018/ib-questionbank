# BI0952

Which of the following is true for hormones but not neurotransmitters?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ They are signalling chemicals found in animals

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ They act on distant target cells

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ They are transmitted across a synaptic gap

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ They result in a short-term effect

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because hormones are secreted into the blood and are transported by the plasma to act on distant target cells.

**Choice A** is incorrect because both hormones and neurotransmitters are signalling chemicals found in animals. **Choices C** and **D** are incorrect because these are characteristics of neurotransmitters and not hormones. Hormones are transported by the blood, and they result in long-term effects.
