# BI2457

How does the steroid hormone cortisol influence gene expression?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ By binding to cytoplasmic receptors and activating transcription factors in the nucleus.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ By directly binding to ribosomes to promote protein synthesis.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ By methylating DNA to silence genes.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ By increasing RNA polymerase activity across all genes.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because hormones affect gene expression by binding to cytoplasmic receptors, which activate transcription factors in the nucleus. These transcription factors cause the target genes to be expressed and specific proteins to be produced.

**Choice B** is incorrect because hormones do not bind to ribosomes, they bind to cytoplasmic receptors.

**Choice C** is incorrect because hormones do not cause methylation of DNA, they increase gene expression by activating transcription factors which are mainly in the cytoplasm.

**Choice D** is incorrect because hormones do not increase RNA polymerase activity or have an effect on all genes.
