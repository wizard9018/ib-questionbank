# BI2952

Which is a primary reason for the existence of a wide range of chemically diverse signalling molecules in animals?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ To allow signalling molecules to pass easily through membranes

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ To enable cells to respond differently to various stimuli

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ To reduce the need for receptors on target cells

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ To ensure all signals result in increased gene expression

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
A wide variety of chemically diverse signalling molecules allows cells to detect and respond to many different types of stimuli, ensuring specificity and appropriate physiological responses.
Therefore, **Choice B** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because signalling molecules do not need to cross membranes to function. Many, such as peptide hormones and cytokines, bind to extracellular receptors and trigger intracellular signalling pathways.\
**Choice C** is incorrect because greater chemical diversity in signalling molecules actually requires more receptor types, not fewer, to ensure accurate signal recognition.\
**Choice D** is incorrect because signalling does not always increase gene expression. Some pathways decrease transcription, and others trigger rapid cellular changes without affecting gene expression at all.
