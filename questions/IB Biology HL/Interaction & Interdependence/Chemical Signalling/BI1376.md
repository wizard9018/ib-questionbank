# BI1376

Dopamine acts as both neurotransmitter between synapses and a hormone. Parkinson’s disease is caused by the death of nerve cells that produce dopamine. To treat Parkinson’s, patients are given the drug Levodopa.

![BI1376](images/BI1376.png)
[Source: Adapted from 721tx, M. (2013, October 24). *File: L-DOPA-to-dopamine.svg*. Wikimedia Commons.  Retrieved December 04, 2023 from URL https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: L-DOPA-to-dopamine.svg. Copyright free]

1. Suggest why Levodopa can be used to treat Parkinson’s disease.    [1 mark]

2. Outline the processes that cause the stimulation and release of dopamine into the synaptic cleft.    [2 marks]

3. Distinguish between hormones and neurotransmitters.        [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ it has a similar shape **OR** chemical structure to dopamine    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ it can bind to the dopaminergic **OR** dopamine receptors    [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Levodopa has a **similar shape** to dopamine, so it can bind to dopamine **receptors** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ depolarisation **OR** arrival of action potential at presynaptic knob **OR** bulb    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ stimulates the opening of calcium channels **OR** calcium ions diffuse into the presynaptic knob **OR** bulb    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ stimulates vesicles containing dopamine to move towards the presynaptic membrane     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ vesicles fuse with presynaptic membrane releasing dopamine **OR**  dopamine is released by exocytosis    [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    **Depolarisation** of the **presynaptic** knob ($\checkmark$) stimulates the opening of **calcium channels**, so calcium ions **diffuse** into the presynaptic knob ($\checkmark$). The diffusion of calcium ions stimulates dopamine to be released into the **synaptic cleft** via **exocytosis** ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ hormones bring about a slower response **THAN** neurotransmitters **OR** neurotransmitters bring about a more rapid response **THAN** hormones        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ hormones are can bring about a response **OR** bind to, distant target cells **WHEREAS** neurotransmitters only affect neighbouring neurons        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ hormones affect a wider range of target cells **OR** tissues **OR** organs **WHEREAS** neurotransmitters only affect specific target cells **OR** tissues        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ hormones have a longer-term effect on the body **COMPARED** neurotransmitters have a shorter-term effect on the body            [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
   **Hormones** bring about a slower ($\checkmark$) **but longer-lasting response** than neurotransmitters ($\checkmark$).  ($\checkmark$). **Hormones** can bring about a **response** in **cells** or tissues **all over the body**, whereas **neurotransmitters** only bring about a **response** in **neighbouring neurons** ($\checkmark$).
