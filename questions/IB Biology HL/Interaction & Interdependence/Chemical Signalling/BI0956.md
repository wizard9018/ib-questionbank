# BI0956

The diagram represents the regulation of glycogen metabolism by the epinephrine receptors. What occurs after cyclic AMP (cAMP) is formed?

![cAMP cyclic AMP](<images/cAMP cyclic AMP.png>)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Acts on ATP

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Binds to G protein-coupled receptor

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Activates G protein

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Binds to an inactive protein kinase (PKA)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because cAMP binds to inactive PKA after being formed to activate PKA.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the enzyme adenyl cyclase (AC) acts on ATP to form cAMP. This has to take place first to form cAMP. **Choices B** and **C** are incorrect because these two reactions take place before cAMP is formed. A ligand such as epinephrine binds to the G protein-coupled receptor, resulting in guanosine triphosphate (GTP) binding to the G protein to activate it.
