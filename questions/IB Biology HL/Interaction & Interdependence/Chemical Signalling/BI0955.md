# BI0955

A signalling chemical is needed to activate the G protein by binding to the G protein-coupled receptor. What other substance is needed to activate the G protein?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ GTP

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ cAMP

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ GDP

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ ATP

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because GTP, guanosine triphosphate, needs to bind with the G protein and replace the GDP to activate the G protein.

**Choice C** is incorrect because GDP is attached to an inactive G protein, which is then replaced by GTP to activate the G protein. **Choices B** and **D** are incorrect because cAMP is the secondary messenger that gets formed from ATP in reactions that take place after the G protein is activated.
