# BI1411

A signalling molecule binds to a G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) on the plasma membrane of a target cell, leading to activation of an intracellular signalling pathway.

Which sequence most accurately describes the events following binding of the signalling molecule?

A. $\hspace{1em}$ Ligand binds GPCR $\rarr$ receptor changes conformation $\rarr$ GTP is hydrolysed to GDP on the G protein $\rarr$ G protein is activated $\rarr$ effector protein is activated

B. $\hspace{1em}$ Ligand binds GPCR $\rarr$ receptor changes conformation $\rarr$ GDP is replaced by GTP on the receptor $\rarr$ receptor is activated $\rarr$ effector protein is activated

C. $\hspace{1em}$ Ligand binds GPCR $\rarr$ receptor changes conformation $\rarr$ G protein phosphorylates the receptor $\rarr$ G protein is activated $\rarr$ effector protein is activated

D. $\hspace{1em}$ Ligand binds GPCR $\rarr$ receptor changes conformation $\rarr$ GDP is replaced by GTP on the G protein $\rarr$ G protein is activated $\rarr$ effector protein is activated

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

**Choice D** is correct because binding of the signalling molecule causes a conformational change in the GPCR. This promotes the replacement of GDP with GTP on the G protein, activating it. The activated G protein then interacts with an effector protein, activating an intracellular signalling pathway.

**Choice A** is incorrect because hydrolysis of GTP to GDP inactivates the G protein rather than activating it.

**Choice B** is incorrect because GDP/GTP exchange occurs on the G protein, not the receptor. The GPCR changes conformation and activates the G protein.

**Choice C** is incorrect because GPCR signalling does not involve the G protein phosphorylating the receptor. The receptor activates the G protein through a conformational change that promotes GDP-to-GTP exchange.
