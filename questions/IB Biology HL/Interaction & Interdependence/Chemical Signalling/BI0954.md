# BI0954

Which feature correctly describes the binding of a ligand to a receptor?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Receptors can bind to any ligand.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Both the ligand and receptor must have a complementary shape.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ All ligands can penetrate the cell to bind with intracellular receptors.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ All ligands bind irreversibly to receptors.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because both the ligand and receptor must have a complementary shape to bind together, just like enzyme substrate specificity.

**Choice A** is incorrect because receptors bind to specific ligands and not any ligands. **Choice C** is incorrect because not all ligands can penetrate the cell to bind with intracellular receptors. Ligands have to be hydrophobic to enter the cell, and some ligands are hydrophilic, which means they will remain outside the cell. **Choice D** is incorrect because not all ligands bind irreversibly to receptors, some bind reversibly.
