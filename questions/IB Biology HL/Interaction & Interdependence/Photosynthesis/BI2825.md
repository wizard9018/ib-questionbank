## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because the rate of photosynthesis is highest at wavelengths that pigments such as chlorophyll have the highest rate of absorption, between approximately 400 and 500 nm and between approximately 650 nm and 700 nm. The axes are correctly labelled, with wavelength on the x-axis and rate of photosynthesis on the y-axis.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the axes are incorrectly labelled, the x-axis should be wavelength and the y-axis should be photosynthesis rate. **Choice B** is incorrect because the rate of photosynthesis is low between 500 nm and 600 nm, but this graph shows the opposite. **Choice C** is incorrect because the values on the x-axis are incorrect, and the highest rates of photosynthesis are not between 200 nm and 300 nm and 400 nm and 450 nm.
