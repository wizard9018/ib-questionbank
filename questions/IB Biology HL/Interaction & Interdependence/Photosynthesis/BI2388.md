# BI2388

Which of the following are the advantages of photosynthetic pigments being held in photosystems within the thylakoid membrane?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | More electrons are available for excitation.|
    | II. | Light energy can be captured from a wide variety of wavelengths of the visible spectrum. |
    | III. | Spatial channelling of excited electrons towards the reaction centre can be achieved.  |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is false because having the photosynthetic pigment molecules aggregated in a photosystem does not in itself affect the molecular structure of those pigments, and in turn, does not affect the number of electrons within each pigment molecule that would be available for excitation by light energy.

**Statement II** is true because different photosynthetic pigments have different absorption and action spectra and so can work together to absorb a wider range of wavelengths of light from the spectrum found in sunlight.

**Statement III** is true because the spatial arrangement of pigments within the photosystem ensures that excited electrons are transferred efficiently to the reaction centre, where they can be passed on for further steps in the photosynthetic process. This spatial organisation is essential for the function of photosystems and allows photosynthesis to proceed efficiently.

**Choice C** is correct because it contains the two true statements (**II** and **III**).

**Choices A** and **D** are incorrect because they contain the false statement (**I**).

**Choice B** is incorrect because it omits one of the true statements (**III**).
