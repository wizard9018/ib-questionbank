# BI0521

Which structure of the chloroplast is most involved in the production of reduced NADP **and** ATP?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Thylakoid space

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Stroma

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Thylakoid membrane

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Chloroplast membrane

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The light-dependent reactions of photosynthesis use energy from sunlight to produce reduced NADP (NADPH) and ATP. These reactions take place on the thylakoid membranes within chloroplasts. **Choice C** is therefore correct.

During the light-dependent reactions, protons accumulate within the thylakoid space (**Choice A**) to create the proton gradient that will power the production of ATP. However, the thylakoid space is not involved in the production of reduced NADP, and the actual production of ATP occurs in the thylakoid membrane where the ATP synthase enzyme is located. The stroma (**Choice B**) contains the enzymes of the Calvin cycle for the light-independent reactions. The chloroplast membrane (**Choice D**) separates the chloroplast from the rest of the cell.
