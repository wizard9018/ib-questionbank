# BI0168

The diagram below shows Calvin’s lollipop apparatus used to investigate the light-independent reactions of photosynthesis. The experiment used radioactive ¹⁴CO₂ to trace carbon in photosynthesis. Algae were killed at timed intervals with hot alcohol, and compounds were separated by chromatography and detected by X-ray film.

![calvins lollipop with correct carbon 14 label i](<images/calvins lollipop with correct carbon 14 label i.png>)
![Calvin's lollipop results i](<images/Calvin's lollipop results i.png>)

Deduce which of the following were results that enabled the determination of the sequence of reactions in the Calvin cycle.

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Algae *Chlorella* were killed by being added to hot alcohol.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ A high percentage of radioactivity was found in glycerate-3-phosphate in the first sample.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ A large amount of triose phosphate and other sugar phosphates were found in the first sample.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ :sup[12]CO$_2$ was replaced by :sup[14]CO$_2$, a radioactive isotope of carbon which was supplied to the algae *Chlorella*.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Because glycerate-3-phosphate is the first stable product of carbon fixation in the Calvin cycle, it makes sense that a high percentage of radioactivity was found in glycerate-3-phosphate in the first sample. **Choice B** is the correct answer.

Hot alcohol was used to kill the algae and stop metabolism instantly, allowing researchers to analyse the intermediates at specific time points. This step was part of the method, not the results. **Choice A** is incorrect.

Triose phosphate is not the first product of the Calvin cycle, it is produced in later steps. Therefore, radioactive triose phosphate and other sugar phosphates would not be found in the first sample, they would be found in later samples. **Choice C** is incorrect.

¹²CO₂ was replaced by ¹⁴CO₂, a radioactive isotope of carbon which was supplied to the algae. This allowed the tracking of carbon atoms through the cycle. However, this was part of the method, not a result. **Choice D** is incorrect.
