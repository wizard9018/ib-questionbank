# BI0171

What is the main source of hydrogen ions in the light-dependent reactions for photosynthesis?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ H$_2$O

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ ATP

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ C$_6$H:sub[12]O$_6$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ NADPH

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
In the light-dependent reactions for photosynthesis, hydrogen ions, also known as the protons, are important in creating a proton gradient.

The main source of the hydrogen ions in the light-dependent reactions is water, H$_2$O. Photolysis of water results in the release of electrons, hydrogen ions and oxygen.

ATP (**Choice B**) is not a source of hydrogen ions.  ATP is generated through the use of a proton gradient and chemiosmosis.

C$_6$H:sub[12]O$_6$, (**Choice C**) or glucose is a product formed during the light-independent reactions. Glucose is not a source of hydrogen ions.  However, it does require the hydrogen ions from NADPH in order to be synthesized in the Calvin cycle.

NADPH (**Choice D**) is a source of hydrogen ions in the Calvin cycle, where glucose is produced. This takes place in the light-independent reactions, however, and not in the light-dependent reactions of photosynthesis.
