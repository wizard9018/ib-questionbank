# BI0167

Which of the following **correctly** shows where chemiosmosis takes place in photosynthesis and aerobic cell respiration?

| | Photosynthesis | Aerobic cell respiration |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Thylakoid membrane | Inner mitochondrion membrane |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Stroma | Mitochondrial matrix |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Starch grain | Cytoplasm  |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Chloroplast envelope | Cell membrane |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Chemiosmosis is a process that is common to both photosynthesis and aerobic cell respiration. In photosynthesis, chemiosmosis takes place along thylakoid membranes. In aerobic cell respiration, chemiosmosis takes place along the inner mitochondrial membrane.

Chemiosmosis does not take place inside the stroma but instead, the Calvin cycle occurs here. Chemiosmosis does not take place inside the mitochondrial matrix but instead, the Krebs cycle and the link reaction of aerobic cell respiration take place here (**Choice B**).

Chemiosmosis also does not take place on or in starch grains.  Glucose, produced by photosynthesis, is stored as starch. Similarly, chemiosmosis does not take place in the cytoplasm during aerobic cell respiration; glycolysis takes place here (**Choice C**).

The chloroplast envelope is the boundary of the chloroplast and the cell membrane similarly is the boundary of the cell.  Chemiosmosis does not take place in either of these structures (**Choice D**).
