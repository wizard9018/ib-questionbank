# BI0073

**Maximum mark: 8**

*Coleus blumei* is an attractive houseplant with red leaves.

![BI0073](images/BI0073.png)
[Source: Juni (2004, October 13). *Coleus blumei*. [Image]. Flickr. Retrieved April 11, 2022, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Coleus_blumei.jpg. Copyright under CC BY]

1. Determine with a reason whether **or** not this plant is still able to conduct photosynthesis with red leaves. [1 mark]

2. Red leaves absorb and reflect different coloured light than green leaves. Distinguish between red light and green light in the visible spectrum. [1 mark]

3. Outline how limiting factors affect the rate of photosynthesis. [3 marks]

4. Explain how oxygen produced during photosynthesis can be used during respiration. [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.  Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ can conduct photosynthesis **BECAUSE** plants have multiple photosynthetic pigments **OR** plants do not only have chlorophyll **OR** green pigment [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ can conduct photosynthesis **BECAUSE** there is anthocyanin [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ can conduct photosynthesis **BECAUSE** chlorophyll is present in the leaves [1 mark]
[1 mark]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    The plant can still photosynthesize because chlorophyll is not the only pigment capable of trapping photons. When chlorophyll is not available, **other pigments** ($\checkmark$) take over that role.

2. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ red light has a longer wavelength **THAN** green light [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ red light has less energy **THAN** green light [1 mark]
[1 mark]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Wavelength of red light is typically around 650nm, while green light is around **550 nm** ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$  limiting factors include carbon dioxide concentration, temperature **AND** light intensity [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increases in carbon dioxide concentration **OR** light intensity increase the rate of photosynthesis [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ light energy is needed for photolysis of water **OR** to excite electrons in chlorophyll **OR** to excite electrons in the light dependent stage **OR** to excite electrons to enter the electron transport chain [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ carbon dioxide is needed to form glucose **OR** carbohydrates **OR** organic molecules [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  increases in temperature up to the optimum temperature increase the frequency of successful collisions between substrates and active sites increasing rate of photosynthesis [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ high temperatures **OR** temperatures beyond the optimum temperature, alter the tertiary structure **OR** start to denature enzymes **OR** denature enzymes **OR** decrease the enzymes activity OR decrease rate of photosynthesis  [1 mark]
[3 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    Limiting factors affect the rate of photosynthesis and are the **concentration of carbon dioxide**, **temperature and light intensity** ($\checkmark$). **Carbon dioxide** is needed as a substrate for photosynthesis to form **glucose** ($\checkmark$), so an increase in carbon dioxide concentration would increase the rate of photosynthesis. There is an **optimum temperature** for photosynthesis when there are **maximum successful collisions** between substrates and enzymes ($\checkmark$). **Temperatures that are too high denature** ($\checkmark$) the enzymes, reducing the rate of photosynthesis. Light intensity is important for the **excitation of electrons in chlorophyll a** ($\checkmark$). An **increase in light intensity increases the rate** of photosynthesis ($\checkmark$) until another limiting factor comes into play.

4. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ photolysis of water produces oxygen during photosynthesis [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ oxygen is used in aerobic cellular respiration, **OR** in mitochondria [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ oxygen is the terminal electron acceptor (in aerobic respiration) [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ oxygen is transformed into water (as a result of aerobic cellular respiration) [1 mark]
[3 marks]

    :u[Sample answer]:

    In photosynthesis, carbon dioxide and water are used, and glucose and oxygen are produced. **Photolysis of water produces oxygen**  during photosynthesis ($\checkmark$), and this oxygen is then used in **aerobic cellular respiration** ($\checkmark$) to produce water in **mitochondria** ($\checkmark$).
