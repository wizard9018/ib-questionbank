# BI1229

ATP and reduced NADP from the light-dependent reactions of photosynthesis are used in the light-independent reactions in photosynthesis. What is used to reduce NADP and provide the energy for ATP synthesis in light-dependent reactions?

| | Source of NADP reduction | Source of energy for ATP synthesis |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Protons from the thylakoid space | Energy released from the movement of electrons from carrier to carrier |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Electrons from photosystem II | Active transport  of protons through ATP synthase |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Electrons from the photolysis of water | Simple diffusion of protons across the thylakoid membrane |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Electrons from photosystem I | Facilitated diffusion of protons through ATP synthase |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The excited electrons from photosystem I are passed two at a time to NADP, an electron acceptor, which with the addition of a hydrogen ion from the stroma, is reduced. The energy for ATP production comes from protons moving down ATP synthase, which is used to phosphorylate ADP to form ATP. **Choice D**  is correct.

Protons from the thylakoid space create the electrochemical gradient, which provides the energy for ATP to be made; **Choice A**  is incorrect. The movement of electrons from photosystem II provides the energy to pump electrons into the thylakoid space; **Choice B**  is incorrect. Electrons from the photolysis of water are passed from electron carrier to carrier, and protons move by facilitated diffusion through ATP synthase; **Choice C**  is incorrect.
