## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because the action spectrum of a photosynthetic pigment is determined by the rate of photosynthesis at each wavelength of light. By contrast, the absorption spectrum is based on the light absorption by a pigment at each wavelength. For chlorophyll A, there is increased photosynthetic activity when exposed to red and blue light, corresponding to the upper and lower end of the visible spectrum from 400-700nm.

**Choice A** is incorrect because this graph represents the absorption spectrum, not the action spectrum. The shape of the curve is also slightly different. **Choice B** is incorrect because the wavelengths of light are incorrect and the shape of the curve is different. **Choice C** is incorrect because the y-axis shows absorption rather than the rate of photosynthesis.
