# BI2887

Which of the following statements best describe(s) the energy transformation during photosynthesis?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Light energy is absorbed by chlorophyll and converted into chemical energy.  |
    | II. | Chemical energy stored in glucose is released as heat energy during photosynthesis. |
    | III. | The energy from sunlight is stored in the bonds of glucose molecules. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
During photosynthesis, light energy is absorbed by chlorophyll and converted into chemical energy, which is stored in molecules like ATP and NADPH. This energy is then used to convert carbon dioxide into glucose. This transformation of light energy into chemical energy is a key part of photosynthesis. **Statement I** is true.

Photosynthesis is the process of storing energy in glucose molecules, not releasing it as heat. The chemical energy in glucose is stored and can later be used by the plant for growth, development, or metabolism, but it is not released as heat during the process of photosynthesis. **Statement II** is false.

During photosynthesis, light energy is used to form chemical bonds in glucose molecules. These bonds store energy in the form of glucose, which can later be broken down to release energy when needed by the plant. **Statement III** is true.

**Choice C** is the correct answer because it includes only the true **Statements I** and **III**.

**Choice A** is incorrect as it misses the true **Statement III**.\
**Choices B** and **D** are incorrect because they include the false **Statement II**.
