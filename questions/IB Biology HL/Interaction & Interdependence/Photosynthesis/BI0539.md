# BI0539

The most abundant catalytic protein in a vascular plant is found in the leaves. What is the name of this protein?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Insulin

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Rubisco

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Rhodopsin

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Collagen

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
A catalytic protein is an enzyme. The most abundant enzyme in plants is rubisco, the enzyme that catalyzes the first step of carbon fixation during the light-independent reactions of photosynthesis.

**Choice A**: Insulin is a hormone found in humans. Insulin binds to receptors on cells to allow them to take up glucose.

**Choice C**: Rhodopsin is a pigment found in the photoreceptors of the retina. Rhodopsin is involved in initiating the response of the cell to light.

**Choice D**: Collagen is a structural protein that gives strength to skin, bones, tendons, and ligaments.
