# BI1040

Which of the following is **not** a way light is used in the light-dependent reactions in photosynthesis?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ In the breakdown of water

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ To produce ATP and reduced NADP

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ To release carbon dioxide from photosystem I

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ To excite electrons in photosystem II

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The carbon dioxide used during photosynthesis comes from the atmosphere, not from photosystem I, **Choice C**  is, therefore, correct.

**Choice A**  is incorrect as light is used in the photolysis reaction (the breakdown of water using light), which is a light-dependent reaction. **Choice B**  is incorrect as the products of the light-dependent reaction that are used in the light-independent reaction are ATP and reduced NADP. **Choice D**  is incorrect, as light is used to excite electrons in photosystem II.
