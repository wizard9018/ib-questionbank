2. any **four** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ carbon dioxide is a substrate in the light-independent reactions **OR** Calvin cycle    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ Calvin cycle takes place in stroma                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ requires the enzyme rubisco        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ carbon fixation **OR** carboxylation of ribulose bisphosphate **OR** carbon from carbon dioxide is added to ribulose bisphosphate **OR** a five-carbon compound        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ to form an unstable six-carbon compound which splits into two G3P **OR** two, 3 carbon compounds         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ carbon dioxide binds more strongly with rubisco **OR** ribulose bisphosphate carboxylase than oxygen does with rubisco     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ one sixth **OR** some molecules of triose phosphate leaves the cycle to form glucose while five sixths **OR** rest of molecules are used to regenerate ribulose bisphosphate molecules            [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Carbon dioxide is needed as a substrate in the **light-independent reactions** ($\checkmark$) to form carbohydrates. **Carboxylation of ribulose bisphosphate** ($\checkmark$) takes place in the **stroma** ($\checkmark$) where carbon dioxide is added to ribulose bisphosphate to form an unstable **six- carbon compound** which splits into **two molecules** of **glycerate-3-phosphate** ($\checkmark$). These are then further reduced to form **triose phosphate**, some of which are used to **form glucose**, and the **rest** are used to **regenerate RuBP** ($\checkmark$).
