# BI0561

In which structure of the chloroplast does ribulose bisphosphate undergo carboxylation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Thylakoid space

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Stroma

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Thylakoid membrane

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Chloroplast membrane

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The carboxylation of ribulose bisphosphate is the first step in the light-independent reactions (the Calvin cycle) of photosynthesis.

During the light-dependent reactions, protons accumulate within the thylakoid space (**Choice A**) to create the proton gradient that powers the production of NADPH and ATP. The light-dependent reactions of photosynthesis take place on the thylakoid membrane (**Choice C**). The chloroplast membrane (**Choice D**) separates the chloroplast from the rest of the cell.
