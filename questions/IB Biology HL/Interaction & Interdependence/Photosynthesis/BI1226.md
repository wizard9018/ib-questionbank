# BI1226

The graph shows the rate of photosynthesis for a plant placed in a greenhouse where temperature and carbon dioxide concentration are controlled. At what time would light intensity be **least likely** to limit the rate of photosynthesis?

![BI1226](images/BI1226.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 12:00

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 20:00

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 10:00

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 00:00

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
At 12:00, the plant is receiving maximum sunlight and has the highest rate of photosynthesis, therefore light is unlikely to be limiting the rate of photosynthesis; **Choice A** is correct.

At 20:00, 10:00 and 00:00, the rates of photosynthesis are lower than at 12:00, suggesting that light intensity is the limiting factor since carbon dioxide concentration and temperature are controlled. **Choices B, C** and **D** are incorrect.
