# BI1586

Where is chlorophyll located and what is its role in photosynthesis?

| | Location | Function |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Thylakoid membrane | Facilitates photolysis of water |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Stroma | Facilitates photolysis of water |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Thylakoid membrane | Uses proton gradient to generate ATP |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Stroma | Uses proton gradient to generate ATP |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because chlorophyll is a photosynthetic pigment found in the photosystems of the thylakoid membrane. It absorbs sunlight to facilitate the photolysis, or splitting, of water molecules.

**Choices B** and **D** are incorrect because chlorophyll is not present in the stroma. **Choice C** is incorrect because chemiosmosis, or the production of ATP using proton gradients, is accomplished with ATP synthase, not chlorophyll.
