# BI2334

Which of these is the purpose of FACE experiments?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Investigate rates of photosynthesis and growth of aquatic plants at higher concentrations of dissolved carbon dioxide.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Investigate rates of photosynthesis and plant growth in natural ecosystems at higher temperatures.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Investigate rates of photosynthesis and plant growth in natural ecosystems at higher concentrations of atmospheric carbon dioxide.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Investigate energy loss from natural ecosystems at higher concentrations of atmospheric carbon dioxide.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because FACE (Free-Air Carbon Dioxide Enrichment) experiments are designed to study how increased atmospheric carbon dioxide affects photosynthesis, plant growth, and overall ecosystem dynamics in natural and agricultural environments. This matches the purpose of these experiments.

**Choice A** is incorrect because FACE experiments investigate the effect of increased carbon dioxide concentration on terrestrial ecosystems.

**Choice B** is incorrect because FACE experiments do not investigate the effect of temperature on natural ecosystems.

**Choice D** is incorrect because FACE experiments do investigate the effect of higher atmospheric concentrations of carbon dioxide but do not investigate the effect of this on energy loss.
