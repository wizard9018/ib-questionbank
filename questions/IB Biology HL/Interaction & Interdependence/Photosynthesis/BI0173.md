# BI0173

Which products are formed at the end of the light-dependent and light-independent reactions?

| | Light-dependent | Light-independent |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Water, NADP | ATP, reduced NADP  |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | ATP, reduced NADP, oxygen | carbohydrates |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ |  ATP, reduced NADP | Water, NADP |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ |  Carbohydrates | ATP, reduced NADP, oxygen |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
In light-dependent reactions, the products from the reactions are ATP, reduced NADP and oxygen. For the light-independent reactions, the products are carbohydrates. Carbohydrates are not the products of the light-dependent reactions (**Choice D**).

Water and NADP are reactants that are needed to form the products of the light-dependent reactions. The products of the light-dependent reactions, ATP and reduced NADP are the reactants for the light-independent reactions (**Choice A** and **Choice C**).
