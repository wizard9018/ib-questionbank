# BI2826

Which description is correct for FACE experiments?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Carbon dioxide concentration is increased, and all other variables are controlled.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Use enclosed greenhouses to predict rates of photosynthesis at increased concentrations of carbon dioxide.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Require control plots under carefully controlled conditions.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Use agricultural situations and natural ecosystems to predict rates of photosynthesis at increased concentrations of carbon dioxide.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because FACE experiments are free-air carbon dioxide enrichment experiments, which involve increasing carbon dioxide concentration to plants in an open scenario where other environmental factors cannot be controlled. For FACE, carbon dioxide is artificially increased for the plants in the experiment plot and a separate plot is used as a control group under normal atmospheric conditions.

**Choice A** is incorrect because in FACE experiments, carbon dioxide concentration is increased, but apart from carbon dioxide concentration, the environmental conditions are natural. This means that factors such as temperature, rainfall, humidity and wind are all variables. **Choice B** is incorrect because FACE experiments are conducted outside under environmental conditions where many factors vary. **Choice C** is incorrect because FACE experiments use control plots, but the control plots are open to environmental conditions and are not controlled.
