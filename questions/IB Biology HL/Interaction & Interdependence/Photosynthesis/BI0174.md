5. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ photosystem II absorbs the highest percentage of light at wavelengths of 680nm **WHILE** photosystem I absorbs the highest percentage of light at wavelengths of 700nm [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ photosystem II is a structure before the electron transport chain **WHEREAS** photosystem I is after the electron transport chain [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ photosystem II passes the electrons to the electron transport chain **WHILE** photosystem I pass the electrons to NADP [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ lost electrons in the reaction centres of photosystem II are filled by electrons from the photolysis of water **WHEREAS** lost electrons in the reaction centres of photosystem I are replaced by electrons from photosystem II [1 mark]
[2 marks]

 :u[Sample answer]:

 **Photosystem II** is activated by light that has a **wavelength of 680nm** whereas **photosystem I** is activated by light that has a **wavelength of 700nm** ($\checkmark$). The excited electrons in photosystem II are transferred to the **electron transport chain** whereas the excited electrons in photosystem I are transferred to **NADP** ($\checkmark$).

6. Any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ cyclic photophosphorylation **OR** non-cyclic photophosphorylation [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ takes place in thylakoid membrane [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ involves light energy **OR** energy from photons of light **OR** light-dependent reactions [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ photoactivation of photosystem II **OR** light absorption of light by photosystem II [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ transfer of excited electrons from photosystem II to photosystem I [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ excited electrons provide energy to make ATP **OR** excited electrons generate proton gradient which leads to ATP synthesis [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ phosphorylation of ADP to ATP **OR** make ATP from ADP and Pi [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ ATP is synthesized by ATP synthase [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ excited electrons from photosystem I reduces NADP **OR** NADP to NADPH [1 mark]
[3 marks]

 :u[Sample answer]:

 Photophosphorylation is a **light-dependent reaction** ($\checkmark$) that takes place in the **thylakoid membrane** ($\checkmark$). The transport of **excited electrons from photosystem II to photosystem I** ($\checkmark$) in a linear path produces a **proton gradient** ($\checkmark$) in thylakoids that leads to the phosphorylation of **ADP to form ATP by ATP synthase** ($\checkmark$).
