# BI0170

The Hill reaction shows electron transfer in isolated chloroplasts for the light-dependent reactions of photosynthesis. A blue dye, DCPIP (2,6-dichlorophenolindophenol) is used instead of the natural final electron acceptor. What is the final electron acceptor in photosynthesis?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ CO$_2$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Oxygen

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ ATP

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ NADP

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
In the light-dependent reaction, NADP is the final electron acceptor in Photosystem I and this is reduced to form NADPH.

CO$_2$  is fixed by the enzyme Rubisco and is not an electron acceptor  (**Choice A**).

ATP is synthesized by ATP synthase using the proton gradient in the thylakoids and is not an electron acceptor (**Choice C**).

Oxygen (**Choice B**) is the terminal electron acceptor in aerobic cell respiration, but not in photosynthesis.
