# BI0591

Which of the following catalyses carbon fixation during photosynthesis?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$   RuBP

- B. $\hspace{1em}$   Rubisco
- C. $\hspace{1em}$   NADP
- D. $\hspace{1em}$   Decarboxylase

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
**Choice B** is correct  because Rubisco is the name of the enzyme which catalyses the fixation of carbon during photosynthesis.

RuBP (ribulose bisphosphate) is a 5-carbon molecule involved in the Calvin cycle. It reacts with carbon dioxide but does not catalyse the reaction; **Choice A** is incorrect.

NADP is a coenzyme, not an enzyme. It plays a role in the light-dependent reactions, accepting electrons and protons to form NADPH. NADPH is then used in the Calvin cycle, but NADP does not catalyse carbon fixation; **Choice C** is incorrect.

Decarboxylase enzymes remove carbon dioxide from molecules, so they perform decarboxylation which is the opposite of carbon fixation; **Choice D** is incorrect.
