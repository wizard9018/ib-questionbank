# BI0854

**Maximum mark: 16**

1. Outline the varied forms of genetic material found in viruses.    [4 marks]

2. Discuss the evidence supporting that the diversity of viruses exists as a result of convergent evolution.    [5 marks]

3. Compare the structure of the coronavirus with the human immunodeficiency virus, HIV. [7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **four** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ single-stranded RNA     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ single-stranded DNA        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ double-stranded RNA        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ double-stranded DNA        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ nucleic acid **OR** DNA **OR** RNA     [1 mark]
    [4 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
There are many different types of viruses. The types of genetic information found in different types of viruses include **single-stranded RNA** ($\checkmark$), **double-stranded RNA** ($\checkmark$), **single-stranded DNA** ($\checkmark$), and **double-stranded DNA** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **five** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ convergent evolution refers to the appearance of similar traits in unrelated organisms as a response to similar selection pressures     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ viruses exhibit a wide range of forms **OR** structures **OR** mechanisms to adapt to different hosts **OR** environments            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ presence of a capsid surrounding genetic material **OR** nucleocapsid is a shared feature that evolved independently in different viral lineages                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ presence of specific protein receptors or spikes have evolved independently to facilitate host cell attachment **OR** suitable example such as similar spike proteins in coronaviruses and influenza viruses    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ independent evolution of similar replication enzymes **OR** reverse transcriptase        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ viruses utilise either DNA **OR** RNA as their genetic material, demonstrating molecular convergence in adapting to different replication strategies         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ convergent traits enhance viral fitness by aiding in host cell attachment **OR** immune evasion **OR** efficient replication        [1 mark]
    [5 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Convergent evolution occurs when **unrelated organisms** evolve **similar traits** due to **similar selection pressures** ($\checkmark$). In viruses, a wide **range** of **structures** and **shapes** are observed, which enable them to multiply in **different hosts**($\checkmark$). One example is the **independent evolution** of **protein spikes** in **coronaviruses** and **influenza viruses** to aid in **host cell attachment** ($\checkmark$). Similarly, unrelated viruses have independently evolved **reverse transcriptase** for **efficient replication**  ($\checkmark$). Additionally, viruses use either **DNA or RNA** as genetic material, showing **molecular convergence** in adapting to various **replication needs** ($\checkmark$).

3. any **seven** of the following (*answer must include at least **two** similarities and **two** differences to gain full marks*):

    ***Similarities***\
    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** are spherical in shape [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** contain (viral) envelope, **OR** both are enveloped with a lipid bilayer    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** contain RNA as the genetic material    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** contain a capsid    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** contain spikes [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** spikes are made of glycoprotein [1 mark]

    ***Differences***\
    $\ce{ -> }$ HIV contains reverse transcriptase, **WHILE** coronaviruses do not        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ HIV contains two (single) strands of RNA, **WHILE** the coronavirus contains one single strand of RNA **OR** HIV genome is shorter or about 10,000 nucleotides, **WHILE** coronavirus is longer (about 30,000 nucleotides)    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ HIV has an enveloped capsid, **WHILE** coronavirus contains a helical (nucleo)capsid  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ HIV genome is within enveloped capsid, **WHILE**  coronavirus genome is a nucleocapsid/not in envelope         [1 mark]
    [7 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    In comparing the coronavirus and the human immunodeficiency virus (HIV), there are several similarities and differences.  Both the coronavirus and HIV are **spherical in shape** ($\checkmark$), the genetic information is enclosed by a **capsid** ($\checkmark$), and both types of viruses contain **RNA as the genetic material** ($\checkmark$). However, **HIV** is a retrovirus and **contains the enzyme reverse transcriptase**, while the **coronavirus does not contain reverse transcriptase** ($\checkmark$). Both viruses **contain spikes** ($\checkmark$) for attachment to cells, **and both HIV spikes and coronavirus spikes are made of glycoprotein** ($\checkmark$). **HIV contains two** single **strands of RNA; however, the coronavirus contains one single strand of RNA** ($\checkmark$).
