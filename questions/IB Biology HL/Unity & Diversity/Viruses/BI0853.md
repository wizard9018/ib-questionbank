4. Any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ reverse transcriptase is an enzyme used in HIV replication [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ uses viral RNA as template [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ synthesises viral DNA from RNA **OR** reverses transcription to make viral DNA from RNA [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ viral DNA is integrated into host chromosome with the help of reverse transcriptase [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ integrated viral DNA is transcribed by host cell to synthesise viral proteins [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 HIV is categorised as a retrovirus. The enzyme reverse transcriptase is contained in the virus's capsid and **is needed for HIV replication** ($\checkmark$). The enzyme synthesises **a complementary DNA strand** ($\checkmark$) **from the HIV genetic material, RNA** ($\checkmark$). Reverse transcriptase **facilitates the integration of viral DNA into the host chromosome** ($\checkmark$).
