# BI2941

The diagrams show the external structures of a coronavirus and a bacteriophage.

![2941a](images/2941a.png)

What is the difference in their structure?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The coronavirus has a tail to attach to host cells, whereas the bacteriophage lambda does not.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The bacteriophage lambda has an RNA genome, whereas the coronavirus has a DNA genome.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The coronavirus has an envelope surrounding its capsid, whereas the bacteriophage lambda does not.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Bacteriophage lambda does not have a capsid, whereas the coronavirus does.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Bacteriophage lambda infects prokaryotes, while coronaviruses infect eukaryotic cells. Coronaviruses use the host cell’s membrane to form an envelope, protecting the capsid and aiding infection through glycoproteins that bind to host receptors. In contrast, bacteriophage lambda lacks an envelope and uses its tail to attach to host cells. **Choice C** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the coronavirus does not have a tail for attachment, whereas the bacteriophage lambda does. **Choice B** is incorrect because the coronavirus has an RNA genome, and the bacteriophage lambda has a DNA genome. **Choice D** is incorrect because all viruses possess a capsid to protect their genome.
