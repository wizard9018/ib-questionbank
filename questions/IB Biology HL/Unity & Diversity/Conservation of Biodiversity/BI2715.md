# BI2715

The following image shows the restoration of an old quarry site into a rehabilitated park.

![BI2715](images/BI2715.png)

]

What term describes this process?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Rewilding

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Reclamation

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Sustainable development

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ In-situ conservation

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because reclamation is the process of restoring disturbed land, such as an old quarry, to a functional ecosystem or other land use (e.g., a park). This often involves soil restoration, planting vegetation, and reconstructing habitats.\
**Choice A** is incorrect because rewilding refers to the restoration of ecosystems by reintroducing native species and minimising human intervention. While rewilding can occur in some restoration projects, quarry rehabilitation typically involves more active human efforts.  \
**Choice C** is incorrect because sustainable development refers to using resources in a way that meets present needs without compromising the needs of future generations. \
**Choice D** is incorrect because in-situ conservation focuses on protecting species in their natural habitats. This term does not describe the process of rehabilitating a disturbed land area, such as a quarry.
