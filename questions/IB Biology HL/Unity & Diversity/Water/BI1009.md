# BI1009

Which of the following is **not** a reason that scientists look for the presence of water on planets as a sign of extraterrestrial life?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Water is a necessary resource for most living organisms

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Most enzymes catalyse reactions in solutions containing water

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Water molecules can stick to each other

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Water is a universal solvent

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C**  is correct because the ability of water molecules to stick to each other, known as cohesion, is important for some biological processes, but is not a primary reason scientists search for water as evidence of extraterrestrial life.

**Choice A** is incorrect because water is required by most known living organisms for metabolic processes and survival.

**Choice B** is incorrect because enzymes generally function in aqueous environments, where water acts as the medium for biochemical reactions.

**Choice D** is incorrect because water is a universal solvent due to its polarity, which allows many substances to dissolve and participate in chemical reactions essential to life.
