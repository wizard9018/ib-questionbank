# BI0178

What intermolecular bond is responsible for water's cohesive, adhesive and thermal properties?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Covalent bond

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Hydrogen bond
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Ionic bond
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Metallic bond

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because water is a polar molecule (dipolar). Hydrogen bonds form due to the attraction between the partially positive (δ:sup[+]) hydrogen atom of one molecule and the partially negative (δ:sup[-]) oxygen atom of another. These intermolecular forces are responsible for:

- Cohesion: Water molecules sticking to each other.
- Adhesion: Water molecules sticking to other polar/charged surfaces.
- Thermal Properties: High specific heat capacity and latent heat of vaporisation due to the energy required to break these bonds.

**Choice A** is incorrect because covalent bonds are intramolecular; they hold the hydrogen and oxygen atoms together within a single water molecule, but do not hold different water molecules to one another.

**Choices C** and **D** are incorrect because water is a molecular compound; it does not contain metals (metallic bonding) nor does it involve the complete transfer of electrons between ions (ionic bonding).
