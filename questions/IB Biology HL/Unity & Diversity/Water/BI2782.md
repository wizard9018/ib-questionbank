# BI2782

Which statement(s) explain why evidence of liquid water is essential for identifying the possible existence of life on other planets, as found on Earth?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Water is the only molecule that can exist as a solid, liquid and gas in the ‘Goldilocks' zone (approximately -50$\degree$C to 80$\degree$C). |
    | II. | Liquid water acts as a universal solvent, so it facilitates the biochemical reactions necessary for life. |
    | III. | Water is necessary for the polymerisation reaction in polynucleotides and polypeptides. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The ‘Goldilocks zone' is the area around a star where the temperature is neither too hot nor too cold for liquid water to exist on the surface of planets.

Many molecules exist as liquids in the temperature range of Earth's Goldilocks zone. For example, ethanol, benzene, sulfur trioxide and bromine can exist in solid, liquid and gaseous states within the temperature range of approximately -50$\degree$C to 80$\degree$C found on Earth (the Goldilocks zone). Therefore, water is not the only molecule that can exist in all three states in the Goldilocks zone. **Statement I** is false. \
The polar nature of water and its ability to form hydrogen bonds mean that it can dissolve a range of molecules and enable enzymatic reactions. As such, water forms the basis of all life as found on Earth. **Statement II** is true. \
Nucleotides form polymers such as DNA or RNA, and amino acids form polypeptides using condensation reactions. However, water is not required for condensation reactions, but rather, is a product of such reactions. By contrast, water is needed for hydrolysis reactions, which break down polymers into monomers. **Statement III** is false.

**Choice B** is correct because it only includes the true **Statement II**.\
**Choices A, C** and **D** are incorrect as they all include the false **Statements I** and **III**.
