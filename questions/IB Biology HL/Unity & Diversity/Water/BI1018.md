**Maximum mark: 9**

The diagram shows a solar system very similar to ours, where five planets orbit a central star.

![BI1018](images/BI1018.png)

1. Define the term “Goldilocks zone”. [1 mark]

2.
 1. Label with the letter R the planet that is least likely to have extraterrestrial life present. [1 mark]

 2. Explain reasons for your answer to part (i). [3 marks]

3.
 1. Some of the planets in this solar system have water present on them. Suggest where this water might have come from. [1 mark]

 2. Explain why the planets referred to in part (i) have retained water, whereas others in this solar system have not.[2 marks]

4. Suggest one other biological molecule, other than water, that may be a sign of life on a planet. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ the zone around a star where temperatures allow for water to exist in a liquid state on the surface of planets [1 mark]
[1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
$\hspace{1em}$ The Goldilocks zone is the zone **around a star** where **temperatures** allow **water** to exist in a **liquid** state on the surface of planets ($\checkmark$).

2.
 2. Any **three** of the following:

 **If R is the planet furthest from the star:** \
 $\ce{ -> }$ temperature is too cold [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ chemical reactions may be slower **OR** not happen at all due to the lack of water as a solvent **OR** enzymes are unable to catalyse chemical reactions [1 mark]

 **If R is the planet closest to the star:** \
 $\ce{ -> }$ temperature is too hot [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ chemical reactions may not happen at all due to the lack of water as a solvent, **OR** enzymes are denatured **OR** unable to catalyse chemical reactions [1 mark]

 **Applies to either answer:** \
 $\ce{ -> }$ unlikely to have liquid water on the surface of the planet **OR** is outside the Goldilocks zone **AND** water is a necessary resource for the survival of all known organisms [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The planet furthest from the star is the least likely to have extraterrestrial life, as it is furthest from the heat and light source, so it may be **too cold** for organisms to survive ($\checkmark$). It is outside the Goldilocks zone, which means that it is **unlikely to have liquid water** and water is a **necessary resource** for the survival of all known organisms ($\checkmark$). The lack of liquid water also means that any **chemical reactions** necessary for life may be slower or not happen at all due to the **lack of a solvent** ($\checkmark$).

 1.

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Water on some of the planets may have come from **asteroids** ($\checkmark$) which contained ice and collided with the planet.

3.
 2. **BOTH** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ gravity stops water from escaping [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ temperatures low enough to condense water [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Some planets in the solar system retain water because the force of **gravity** on those planets prevents water from escaping those planets ($\checkmark$), and the distance from the star keeps the **temperatures** on those planets **low** enough to allow water to **condense** ($\checkmark$).
