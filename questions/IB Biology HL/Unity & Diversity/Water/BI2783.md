# BI2783

**Maximum mark: 10**

The pie chart shows the chemical composition of the Murchison meteorite, which fell in Australia in 1969. It belongs to a class of meteorites called carbonaceous chondrites, some of the solar system's oldest meteorites.

1. Explain why analysis of carbonaceous chondrites has led to the hypothesis that meteorites are most likely to be the source of water on Earth.            [2 marks]

2. Explain **two** properties of water that make it an essential molecule for life.        [2 marks]

3. Other than water, deduce with a reason **one other substance** found on the Murchison meteorite that is essential for life.                        [1 mark]

The ‘Goldilocks' zone is the area around a star, where the conditions are optimal for the presence of liquid water.

4. Other than liquid water, state **two** properties that a planet in the Goldilocks zone may need for life to evolve.                                    [2 marks]

5. Carbonaceous chondrites have also collided with planets in the solar system that are outside the ‘Goldilocks zone'. Suggest why life may not have appeared on those planets.                                        [2 marks]

6. If a planet within the ‘Goldilocks zone' **does not** have an atmosphere, suggest how this would affect the retention of liquid water.                    [1 mark]

    Carbonaceous chondrites are thought to be the most likely source of water on Earth because they contain water with a **similar ratio of hydrogen isotopes** to **ocean** water ($\checkmark$) and they have been found to contain **liquid water** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ water is a universal solvent **OR**  dissolves a wide range of substances **WHICH** facilitates cellular metabolic reactions **OR** transport of dissolved substances        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water is cohesive **OR** water molecules can stick to each other due to hydrogen bonding **WHICH** allows for droplet formation **OR** capillary action **OR** surface tension in plants    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water is adhesive **OR** water molecules stick to other surfaces due to hydrogen bonding **WHICH** allows for processes such as the transpiration stream in plants            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water has a high specific heat capacity **OR** it resists sudden changes in temperature **WHICH** helps organisms maintain homeostasis **OR** stabilises temperatures in large bodies of water [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water has a high heat of vaporisation **OR** it requires a large amount of energy to convert liquid water into gas **WHICH**  allows organisms to regulate body temperature through sweating or transpiration        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ solid water (ice) is less dense than liquid water therefore, ice can float **WHICH** means that aquatic ecosystems are insulated from large fluctuations in temperature            [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Water is a **universal solvent** which facilitates a range of **cellular metabolic reactions** ($\checkmark$), and it is **cohesive**, which allows for processes such as **water droplet formation** or the formation of **large bodies** of water ($\checkmark$).

5. any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ the planet may be too small so may not have sufficient gravity to sustain an atmosphere **OR** retain liquid water                     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the planet may be too large resulting in excessive gravity **OR** a dense atmosphere which results in surface temperatures which are too high for chemical **OR** metabolic reactions         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the planet may be too cold **OR** too far from the star **OR** the Sun for photosynthesis **OR** liquid water to exist        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the planet may be too warm **OR** too close to the star **OR** the Sun for  liquid water to exist **OR** chemical **OR** metabolic reactions to take place **OR** for complex molecules to be stable        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ The planet's atmosphere may contain toxic gases that prevent the development of stable life forms             [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the planet may not have a strong enough magnetic field to protect the planet **OR** organic molecules **OR** surface conditions from harmful radiation                [1 mark]

Also, the planet may be **too warm** or **close to the Sun** for **liquid water to be present** ($\checkmark$).
