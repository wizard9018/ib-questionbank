# BI2376

Which evidence led biologists to classify Archaea in a separate domain from Bacteria?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The presence of a cell wall

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The presence of 70s ribosomes

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The presence of circular chromosomes

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Differences in ribosomal RNA base sequences

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The ribosomal RNA (rRNA) base sequencing carried out by Carl Woese and George Fox (1977) revealed that Archaea and Bacteria form distinct evolutionary groups. The molecular differences provided evidence that organisms previously grouped together as prokaryotes should be classified in separate domains. This evidence supported the development of the three-domain classification system. **Choice D** is, therefore, correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because cell walls are also found in Bacteria. The presence of a cell wall, rather than its composition, does not distinguish Archaea from Bacteria.

**Choice B**  is incorrect because both Archaea and Bacteria have 70S ribosomes. Differences in their rRNA base sequences, rather than the presence of this type of ribosome, provide evidence for their evolutionary separation.

**Choice C** is incorrect because circular chromosomes occur in both Archaea and Bacteria. Their presence, therefore, does not provide a distinguishing feature that supports classification into separate domains.
