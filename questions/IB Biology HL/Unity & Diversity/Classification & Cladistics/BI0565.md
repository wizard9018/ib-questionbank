## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
    $\ce{->}$ placental mammals [1 mark]
[1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    **Placental mammals** ($\checkmark$) are most closely related to marsupials. They are both members of Theria.

    2. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$ most species are extinct [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ must rely on fossil evidence [1 mark]
[1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    **Most of the species** in the cladogram **are extinct** ($\checkmark$), so scientists **must rely on fossils** ($\checkmark$) of these organisms to identify relatedness and determine the divergence of the platypus.

2.
    1. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$ DNA sequence **OR** gene sequences **OR** amino acid sequences **OR** protein sequences [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ compare sequences for shared gene **OR** protein in different species [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ number of differences in sequences indicates degree of relatedness **OR** the more differences there are between species the more distantly related they are [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Scientists can use **DNA sequences** ($\checkmark$) to determine evolutionary relationships. For example, scientists might **compare** the nucleotide **sequence of a shared gene** across species ($\checkmark$). By comparing the sequences of nucleotides in the same gene, scientists can infer **the order in which the changes most likely occurred**. This allows them to determine the most likely **divergence order** ($\checkmark$) among the organisms.

    2. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$ identification **OR** comparison of homologous structures [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ identification of vestigial structures [1 mark]
[1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Scientists could **identify and compare homologous structures** ($\checkmark$) in the platypus to those of other mammals. For example, if the webbed feet of the platypus are more like the feet of marsupials than the feet of ancestral species like *Docodonta*, the platypus may be more closely related to Theria than it is to *Docodonta*.

    **OR Alternative answer**

    Scientists could **identify vestigial structures** ($\checkmark$) in the platypus and other mammals. For example, if the platypus and marsupials have similar vestigial structures that are also similar to functional structures that were present in earlier groups of mammals, the platypus likely emerged after *Docodonta* in the evolution of mammals.

3. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$ domain is the largest rank [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ the three domains are Archaea, Eubacteria, and Eukarya [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ domain Eukarya is split into kingdoms  [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ from largest to smallest, kingdoms are divided into phyla **OR** phylum, classes, orders, families, genera **OR** genuses, and species     [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ species are sometimes divided into subspecies  [1 mark]
[2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The **largest rank** in the classification system is the **domain** ($\checkmark$). There are three domains – **Archaea, Eubacteria, and Eukarya** ($\checkmark$). The **domain Eukarya is divided into 4 kingdoms** ($\checkmark$) – Protista, Fungi, Plantae, and Animalia. Each **kingdom is further divided, from largest to smallest into phyla, class, order, family, genus, and species** ($\checkmark$).
