# BI0254

Natural classification groups organisms according to which characteristic?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Morphology

- B. $\hspace{1em}$    Anatomical characteristic
- C. $\hspace{1em}$    Modes of nutrition
- D. $\hspace{1em}$    Common ancestry

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because natural classification groups organisms according to their evolutionary relationships and common ancestry.

**Choice A** and **Choice B** are incorrect because morphology and anatomical characteristics may be used as evidence when classifying organisms, but natural classification is ultimately based on evolutionary relationships rather than just structural similarities.

**Choice C** is incorrect because mode of nutrition may describe how organisms obtain energy, but it is not the primary basis of natural classification.
