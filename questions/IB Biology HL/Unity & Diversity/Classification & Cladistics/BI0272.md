1. Identify the organism(s) most closely related to humans in the cladogram based on…

    1. **DNA** sequences    [1 mark]

    2. **amino acid** sequences    [1 mark]

2. Describe the advantage of using DNA sequences to construct cladograms compared to amino acid sequences. [2 marks]

3. Suggest why cladograms made using physical features are not reliable predictors of phylogenetic relationships.     [2 marks]

2. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ the nucleotide sequence of a gene contains more information than the amino acid sequence of the corresponding polypeptides [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ silent mutations alter the DNA sequence but do not affect the amino acid sequence [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ degeneracy in the genetic code means a mutation does not necessarily change the amino acid sequence    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ greater information can be obtained by DNA sequence analysis because variability in both the coding and non-coding regions of the genome can be examined [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ DNA samples can be easily sequenced **OR** DNA is cheaper to sequence **OR** DNA sequencing yields more data whereas the approach to sequencing of proteins varies with type of protein [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ DNA sequence can be used to establish amino acid sequence, but not the other way around [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    Using DNA sequences to construct cladograms is advantageous because due to the existence of **coding and non-coding regions** ($\checkmark$) of DNA, **more information** ($\checkmark$) is held in the nucleotide sequence than the amino acid sequence . Also mutations may occur in the DNA which do not show up in the amino acid sequence due to the **degenerative code** ($\checkmark$) leading to **silent mutations** ($\checkmark$).

3. Any two  of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ adaptive radiation can result in closely related species showing very different structural features [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ convergent evolution means that distantly related organisms may show very similar structural features [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ analogous structures are derived through separate evolutionary pathways and are not indicators of evolutionary relationships  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ DNA and amino acid analysis have shown that some classification of organisms based on shared structural features was incorrect    [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Cladograms are no longer based on structural features due to the presence of **analogous structures**, which are the result of **convergent evolution** ($\checkmark$) and so are **not indicators of evolutionary relationships** ($\checkmark$). DNA and amino acid analysis have shown that many cladograms based solely on **shared structural features** are incorrect ($\checkmark$).
