# BI1444

Below is a diagram of an experimental setup in which boiled nutrient broth was placed into three different swan-necked flasks. Flask X was open to the air, but the swan neck trapped dust and microorganisms. Flask Y was sealed. Flask Z had its neck broken off, exposing it directly to the air.

![BI1444 fig1](<images/BI1444 fig1.png>)

How do the results of this experiment support the idea that cells arise from pre-existing cells?

- I. $\hspace{1em}$ Broth in flask X remained clear because dust and microbes were trapped in the neck, preventing contamination.

- II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Broth in flask Y remained clear, indicating no microbial growth, as the sealed environment prevented any external contamination.

- III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Broth in flask Z remained clear, proving that exposure to air alone does not lead to microbial growth.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ II only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

**Statement I** is true because in flask X the swan neck traps dust and microorganisms, preventing them from reaching the broth. As a result, the broth remains clear, showing that microorganisms in the air must physically enter the broth for growth to occur. This supports the idea that new cells arise from existing cells rather than forming spontaneously.

**Statement II** is true because flask Y is sealed after sterilisation, so no microorganisms can enter. The broth remains clear, indicating that no microbial growth occurs in the absence of contamination. This provides strong evidence that cells do not arise spontaneously from non-living matter.

**Statement III** is false because in flask Z, where the neck is broken, airborne microorganisms can directly enter the broth. This would cause the broth to become cloudy due to microbial growth. Therefore, the statement is incorrect in claiming the broth remained clear.

As only **Statements I** and **II** are true, answer **Choice C** is the correct answer to this question.
