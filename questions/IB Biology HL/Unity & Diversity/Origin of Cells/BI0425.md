Describe how this experiment showed that the origin of life was possible on early Earth. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Any **three**  of the following:

$\ce{ -> }$ ammonia (NH$_3$), methane (CH$_4$) and Hydrogen (H$_2$) were the major gases present in the early atmosphere  [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ heat was used to simulate higher temperatures [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ steam was passed through ammonia (NH$_3$), methane (CH$_4$) and Hydrogen (H$_2$) **OR**  through a mixture of compounds     [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ electricity was used to simulate lightning [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ resulting mixture was cooled down [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ the amino acids **OR** organic carbon compounds needed for life were produced [1 mark]

[3 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Miller and Urey conducted experiments which showed that the compounds present in the **early atmosphere**  (**methane, ammonia and hydrogen** ($\checkmark$)) could be used to make the organic carbon compounds necessary for life.  They did this by heating up water and **passing the steam** ($\checkmark$) through ammonia, methane and hydrogen and by using **electricity to simulate the lightning** ($\checkmark$).  They found that these conditions **created amino acids** ($\checkmark$) and other organic carbon compounds.
