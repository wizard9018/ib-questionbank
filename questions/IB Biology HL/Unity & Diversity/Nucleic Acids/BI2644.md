# BI2644

The following dichotomous key can be used to identify unknown molecules:

1\. Does the molecule have a sugar linked to a nitrogenous base?

-   -Yes: Go to Step 2\
    -No: It is molecule V

2\. Does the sugar have a hydroxyl group (-OH) at the 2' carbon?

-   -Yes: Go to Step 3\
    -No: It is a type of molecule W

3\. Is the number of phosphate groups present three?

-   -Yes: It is molecule Y\
    -No: It is molecule Z

Which row shows the correct identification of the molecules?

| | Molecule V | Molecule W | Molecule Y | Molecule Z |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Ribonucleotide | Deoxyribonucleotide | ATP | ADP |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Deoxyribonucleotide | Ribonucleotide | ATP | ADP |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Ribose sugar |Ribonucleotide | ATP | Deoxyribonucleotide |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ |  Ribose sugar | Deoxyribonucleotide | ATP | ADP |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because all four identifications follow the dichotomous key.

Molecule V is free ribose. The “No” branch at Step 1 identifies a molecule that does not have a sugar linked to a nitrogenous base. All nucleotides contain a sugar linked to a nitrogenous base, so free ribose is the only possible identification within the stated set.

Molecule W is a deoxyribonucleotide. It contains a sugar linked to a nitrogenous base, but its sugar lacks a hydroxyl group (–OH) at the 2′ carbon. Deoxyribose has a hydrogen atom at this position instead of the hydroxyl group found in ribose. Therefore, W is a nucleotide containing deoxyribose.

Molecule Y is ATP. Its sugar has a hydroxyl group at the 2′ carbon, identifying it as ribose. The stem states that all ribose-containing nucleotides in this set contain adenine. With three phosphate groups, Y is therefore adenosine triphosphate (ATP).

Molecule Z is ADP. Like Y, it contains ribose and adenine, but it does not contain three phosphate groups. Since the stem restricts these nucleotides to either two or three phosphate groups, Z must contain two and is therefore adenosine diphosphate (ADP).

**Choice A** is incorrect because molecule V should be ribose sugar (not a ribonucleotide), and molecule W should be a deoxyribonucleotide (not a ribonucleotide).

**Choice B** is incorrect because molecule V should be ribose sugar (not a deoxyribonucleotide), and molecule W should be a deoxyribonucleotide (not a ribonucleotide).

**Choice C** is incorrect because molecule W should be a deoxyribonucleotide (not a ribonucleotide), and molecule Z should be ADP (not a deoxyribonucleotide).
