# BI3213

The diagram shows three domains of living organisms - bacteria, archaea and eukaryotes, together with a separate group of RNA viruses.

![BI3213](images/BI3213.png)

What conclusion is supported by the diagram?

A. $\hspace{1em}$ RNA viruses should be classified as living organisms because they contain heritable genetic information.

B. $\hspace{1em}$ DNA evolved from RNA, so all early living organisms used RNA as their genetic material.

C. $\hspace{1em}$ All living organisms use DNA as their genetic material, whereas some viruses use RNA and are not considered living.

D. $\hspace{1em}$ The presence of genetic material is sufficient for an entity to be considered living.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

Bacteria, archaea and eukaryotes represent the three domains of living organisms, and all use DNA as their genetic material. Some viruses, including influenza viruses and HIV, use RNA as their genetic material. However, viruses are not considered living organisms. Therefore, the occurrence of RNA genomes in some viruses does not contradict the statement that DNA is the genetic material of all living organisms. **Choice C** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because containing heritable genetic information is not, by itself, sufficient for an entity to be considered living.

**Choice B** is incorrect because the diagram does not show that DNA evolved from RNA or that the earliest living organisms used RNA as their genetic material.

**Choice D** is incorrect because viruses contain genetic material but are not considered living organisms.
