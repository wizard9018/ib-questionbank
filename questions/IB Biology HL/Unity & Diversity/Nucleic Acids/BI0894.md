# BI0894

Hershey and Chase conducted an experiment to investigate whether DNA or protein serves as the genetic material. They cultured 2 batches of viruses, one in a medium containing radioactive :sup[35]S and one in radioactive :sup[32]P. They then introduced each batch of viruses to separate cultures of bacteria. After allowing time for the viruses to infect the bacteria, the culture solutions were centrifuged to separate the viral remains from the bacterial cells, forming two layers, the pellet and the supernatant.

![BI0894A](images/BI0894A.png)

Which component of the tube would provide evidence that DNA is the genetic material?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Supernatant with radioactive :sup[35]S

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Pellet with radioactive :sup[35]S

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Supernatant with radioactive :sup[32]P

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Pellet with radioactive :sup[32]P

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because the bacterial cells form the pellet, and they would contain :sup[32]P as DNA contains phosphorus but not sulphur. The viruses cultured in :sup[32]P incorporated it into their DNA, and they infected the bacteria with this radioactive DNA.

**Choices A** and **C** are incorrect as the bacteria with the radioactive DNA should be found in the pellet, whereas the supernatant contains the viral remains. **Choice B** is incorrect as if the bacteria in the pellet contained :sup[35]S then this would indicate that protein, which contains sulphur, and not phosphorus, serves as the genetic material.
