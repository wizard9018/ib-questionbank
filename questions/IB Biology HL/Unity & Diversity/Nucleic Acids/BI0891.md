# BI0891

Which of the labelled structures shows the correct directionality of the DNA molecule?

![BI0891B](images/BI0891B.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because the left strand is running in the 5’ - 3’ direction with the phosphate group attached to the 5th carbon at the 5’ end, and the 3rd carbon of the terminating deoxyribose sugar is free. The right strand is running antiparallel in the 3’-5’ direction.

**Choice A** is incorrect because both strands are labelled as running in the 5’ - 3’ direction, and the carbons of the deoxyribose sugars are incorrectly labelled. **Choice B** is incorrect because the strands are incorrectly labelled with the 5’ and 3’ ends, and the carbons of the deoxyribose sugars are incorrectly labelled on both strands. **Choice D** is incorrect because both strands are labelled as running in the 3’ - 5’ direction, and the carbons of the deoxyribose sugars on both strands are incorrectly labelled.
