# BI3210

Which of the following statements about nucleic acids are correct?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | The coding of genetic information depends on the sequence of bases.|
    | II. | Adenine, cytosine and guanine occur in both DNA and RNA. |
    | III. | Covalent bonds form between the phosphate group of one nucleotide and the pentose sugar of a neighbouring nucleotide.|

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

**Statement I** is true because genetic information is encoded by the sequence of nitrogenous bases.

**Statement II** is true because adenine, cytosine and guanine occur in both DNA and RNA. DNA contains thymine as its fourth base, whereas RNA contains uracil.

**Statement III** is true because covalent bonds between phosphate groups and pentose sugars in neighbouring nucleotides produce the continuous sugar–phosphate backbone of a nucleic acid strand.

Therefore, **Choice D** is correct.

**Choices A, B,** and **C** are all incorrect because they omit one or more of the true statements.
