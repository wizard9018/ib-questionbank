# BI0042

Which of the following are correct percentages of bases in a sample of DNA?

| | Adenine | Cytosine | Guanine | Thymine |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | 10% | 25% | 30% | 35% |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | 10% | 15% | 15% | 10% |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | 20% | 30% | 30% | 20% |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | 20% | 20% | 30% | 30% |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
DNA molecules consist of 4 nitrogenous bases: adenine (A), cytosine (C), guanine (G) and thymine (T). Chargaff's rule states that A will pair with T and C will pair with G. The percentages of all 4 bases should add up to 100% because there are no other bases present.

**Choices A and D** are incorrect because the amount of A is not equal to the amount of T, and the amount of C is not equal to the amount of G.

**Choice B** is incorrect because the sum of all percentages does not add up to 100%.
