# BI1542

**Maximum mark: 5**

The micrograph shows a cell of an exocrine gland from the stomach of the European beaver (*Castor fiber*).
Exocrine glands are glands that release their secretions through a duct.

1. Suggest one role of exocrine glands.        [1 mark]

2. Using the letter X, label the organelle with the function of producing proteins that are exported outside the cell.        [1 mark]

3. Explain how **two** organelles are related to the function of an exocrine cell.    [2 marks]

4. Pancreatic cells are both exocrine and endocrine glands. Name one hormone produced by the pancreas and state what the stimulus is for its secretion.    [1 mark]
