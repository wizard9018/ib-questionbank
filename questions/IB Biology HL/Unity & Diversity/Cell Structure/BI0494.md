# BI0494

The electron micrograph below shows a cell of the bacterium *Vibrio cholerae*. Which of the labelled structures maintains the cell's shape and protects the cell from lysing during osmosis?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The cell membrane of prokaryotes is surrounded by a rigid cell wall. The cell wall provides shape and structure to the organism. It also prevents the cell from bursting when the cell takes up water through osmosis.  The cell wall is located between the cell membrane and the capsule, a sticky outer layer of polysaccharide.
