## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ fungal cell walls are primarily composed of chitin **WHEREAS** plant cell walls are composed of cellulose [1 mark]
[1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
$\hspace{1em}$ The major component of fungal cell walls is **chitin**, but in plants, it’s **cellulose** ($\checkmark$).

2.
 1. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ plants use chloroplasts for photosynthesis **AND** are autotrophs **OR** allows plants to make their own food **OR** convert light energy to chemical energy [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ animals and fungi do not need chloroplast to photosynthesise because they are heterotrophs **OR** feed on other organisms **OR** obtain energy by consuming organic matter rather than producing it [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Plants are **autotrophs** and make their own food by photosynthesis. **Chloroplasts** contain chlorophyll which absorbs the light energy needed for **photosynthesis** ($\checkmark$). Animals and fungi are **heterotrophs**, they consume their food and **do not need chloroplast** for photosynthesis ($\checkmark$).

 2. the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ chromoplasts synthesise **OR** store pigments (other than chlorophyll) [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ leucoplasts are involved in storage **OR** metabolism [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Plant cells have **chromoplasts** which synthesise and **store pigments**, such as carotenoids ($\checkmark$).
