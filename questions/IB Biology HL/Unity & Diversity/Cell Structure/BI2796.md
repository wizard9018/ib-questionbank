## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. to award a mark, the response must include the number of nuclei **AND** a reason for this

    $\ce{ -> }$ skeletal muscle cells have multiple nuclei, providing multiple copies of genes to produce the large numbers of proteins needed for muscle contraction    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ phloem sieve tube elements lack a nucleus (at maturity), which creates space for the efficient transport of sugars and other organic molecules    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ aseptate fungal hyphae have multiple nuclei to allow for rapid growth **OR** distribution of nutrients **OR** cytoplasmic streaming throughout the extensive network **OR** multinucleation occurs due to nuclear divisions without cytoplasmic division    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ red blood cells lack a nucleus, which increases space for haemoglobin, maximising oxygen transport     [1 mark]

    [4 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    **Skeletal muscle cells** have **many nuclei** because they must make many **copies of proteins** for contractions along the entire length of the cell, which may be **very long** ($\checkmark$). **Phloem** sieve tube elements have **no nucleus** to allow for more space for transporting **sugars** more efficiently ($\checkmark$). **Aseptate hyphae** have **many nuclei** throughout the cell to help it grow quickly and **distribute nutrients** ($\checkmark$). **Red blood cells** have **no nuclei** at maturity for greater volume to **carry haemoglobin** ($\checkmark$).

    ***Note a table showing the comparisons would also be acceptable:***

    | Cell | No. of nuclei | Relation to cell function | |
    | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
    | Skeletal muscle | **many** | **for many copies of protein synthesis** | ($\checkmark$) |
    | Phloem sieve tube elements | **none** | **to allow room for translocation** | ($\checkmark$) |
    | Aseptate fungal hyphae | **many** | **to allow rapid transfer of nutrients** | ($\checkmark$) |
    | Red blood cell | **none** | **to create more room for haemoglobin** | ($\checkmark$) |

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ fungal cell walls are composed of chitin, **WHEREAS** plant cell walls are composed of cellulose [1 mark]

[1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
$\hspace{1em}$ **Fungal cell walls** are composed of **chitin**, while **plant cell walls** are composed of **cellulose** ($\checkmark$).
