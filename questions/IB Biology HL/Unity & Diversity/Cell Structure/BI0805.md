## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **two** of the following:

    **Similarities**

    $\ce{ -> }$ **both** have plasma membranes        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **both** contain circular **OR** naked DNA        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ mitochondrial ribosomes are more similar in size **OR** structure to prokaryotes than eukaryotes **OR both** contain 70S ribosomes        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **both** are similar in size        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **both** are similar shapes **OR** oval **OR** elongated        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **both** have ATPase proteins embedded in surrounding membrane        [1 mark]

    **Differences**

    $\ce{ -> }$ mitochondria do not have a cell wall **OR** a capsule whereas, prokaryotes do        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotes may have flagella **OR** pili but mitochondria do not        [1 mark]

    *Answer must include at least one difference and one similarity to gain full marks*

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The cell in the diagram is a prokaryotic cell and is similar in structure to a mitochondrion, as mitochondrial **ribosomes** are more **similar in size** and **structure** to prokaryotes than eukaryotes, ($\checkmark$), and they both have **naked circular DNA** ($\checkmark$). However mitochondria **do not have a cell wall** ($\checkmark$), whereas **prokaryotic cells do**.

2. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ endosymbiotic theory    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ mitochondria evolved from free-living prokaryotes    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ that were engulfed **BUT** not digested by larger prokaryotes    [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The prokaryotic cell in the diagram has structural similarities to a mitochondrion, due to the **endosymbiotic theory** ($\checkmark$) which explains that mitochondria evolved from **free-living prokaryotes** ($\checkmark$) that were **engulfed though not digested** ($\checkmark$) by larger cells.
