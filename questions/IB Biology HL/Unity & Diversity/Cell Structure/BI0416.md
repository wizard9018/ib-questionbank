Which organelle\'s origin is due to the endosymbiosis of ancient cells?

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**D** has visible cristae and is oval-shaped; it is a mitochondrion. Mitochondria originated from engulfed prokaryotes via endosymbiosis but have since evolved into specialised organelles that are genetically integrated with and fully dependent on the host eukaryotic cell. **Choice D** is therefore the correct answer.

**Choice A**, the cytoplasm, is not correct as it is simply the internal fluid matrix of the cell. It is part of the original cell structure and not evolved from engulfed bacteria.

**Choice B**, the nucleus, is not correct as it is thought to have formed by invagination (folding inward) of the plasma membrane around the genetic material of an early cell. It does not exhibit the features of an engulfed prokaryote, such as independent circular DNA and 70S ribosomes, and therefore is not considered an endosymbiont.

**Choice C**, a vacuole, is not correct as it is formed through the infolding and fusion of internal membranes within the cell.
