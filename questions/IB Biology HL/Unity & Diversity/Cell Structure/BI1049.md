# BI1049

**Maximum mark: 16**

1. Outline one way unicellular organisms can carry out each of nutrition, movement and excretion. [3 marks]

2. Draw a single mitochondrion, and annotate it to outline the role of four labelled structures. [5 marks]

3. Compare and contrast the structure of E. coli and a palisade mesophyll cell. [8 marks]

3. any **eight** of the following (*must include at least **two** similarity and **two** differences to gain full marks*):

 ***Similarities***

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** *E.coli* and palisade mesophyll cells have cell **OR** plasma membranes [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** *E.coli* and palisade mesophyll cells have cytoplasm [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** *E.coli* and palisade mesophyll cells have a cell wall [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** *E.coli* and palisade mesophyll cells contain ribosomes [1 mark]

 ***Differences***

 $\ce{ -> }$ *E.coli* cell wall is made up of peptidoglycan **WHEREAS** palisade mesophyll cell walls are made of cellulose [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ palisade mesophyll cells have a compartmentalised structure **WHEREAS** *E.coli* do not [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ palisade mesophyll cells have 80S ribosomes **WHEREAS** *E.coli* have 70S [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ palisade mesophyll cells have nuclei **WHEREAS** *E.coli* have a nucleoid [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ *E.coli* can have plasmids **OR** circular DNA **WHEREAS** palisade mesophyll cells have linear DNA [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ *E.coli* have external structures such as flagella **OR** pili **BUT** palisade mesophyll cells do not [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ palisade mesophyll cells have membrane bound organelles such as golgi apparatus, rough endoplasmic reticulum, chloroplasts, mitochondria **WHEREAS** *E.coli* do not [1 mark]
 [8 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Palisade mesophyll cells and *E.coli* both have **cell membranes** ($\checkmark$) and **cytoplasm** ($\checkmark$). They also both can have **cell walls** ($\checkmark$), however the cell wall in *E.coli* is composed of **peptidoglycan**, whereas the cell wall in palisade mesophyll cells is composed of **cellulose** ($\checkmark$). Palisade mesophyll cells are full of **membrane bound** organelles such as the golgi apparatus, which gives them a **compartmentalised** structure, whereas *E.coli* **do not** have a compartmentalised structure or membrane bound organelles ($\checkmark$). Both palisade mesophyll cells and *E.coli* have **ribosomes** ($\checkmark$), but *E.coli* have **70S** ribosomes and palisade mesophyll cells have **80S** ribosomes ($\checkmark$). DNA is also stored differently in palisade mesophyll cells and *E.coli*. Palisade mesophyll cells store their DNA inside the **nucleus** and it has a **linear** structure, whereas *E.coli* have DNA floating freely in the cytoplasm as a **nucleoid** ($\checkmark$) and can sometimes contain extra DNA in the form of a **plasmid** ($\checkmark$). E.coli can also have external structures such as **pili** or flagella ($\checkmark$).
