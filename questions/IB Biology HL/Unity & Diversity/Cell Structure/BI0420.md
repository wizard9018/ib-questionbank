# BI0420

Which statement applies to both mitochondria and prokaryotes?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ They both contain DNA.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$    They both have flagella.
- C. $\hspace{1em}$    They both contain lysosomes.
- D. $\hspace{1em}$    They both have a cell wall.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Mitochondria and prokaryotes both contain their own DNA (**Choice A**).

**Choice B** is incorrect because not all prokaryotes have flagella (many are non-motile), and mitochondria never have flagella.

**Choice C** is incorrect because lysosomes are membrane-bound organelles found only in eukaryotic cells.

**Choice D** is incorrect because prokaryotes typically have a cell wall made of peptidoglycan, but mitochondria do not have cell walls.
