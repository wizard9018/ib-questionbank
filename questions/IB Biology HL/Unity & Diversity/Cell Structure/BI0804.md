## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1. Prokaryote *(needed for mark)*

    then any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ no nucleus    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ has a nucleoid    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ no membrane bound organelles **OR** a named organelle such as a Golgi apparatus, rough endoplasmic reticulum or mitochondria    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ size is typical of prokaryote (0.1μm - 5μm)    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ rod shaped    [1 mark]

    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The cell is a **prokaryotic**  cell as it contains **no nucleus** ($\checkmark$) or **membrane bound organelles** ($\checkmark$).

    2.

    $\ce{ -> }$ 110,000 x    [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    magnification= image size ÷  actual size

    image size = 11 mm = 11,000,000 nm

    actual size = 100 nm

    11,000,000nm ÷ 100 nm = **110,000** x ($\checkmark$)

    3. any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ nutrition: consuming food for energy **OR** metabolic processes **OR** growth **OR** reproduction    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ reproduction: producing offspring    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ metabolism: chemical reactions that occur within the cell    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ homeostasis: maintaining a stable internal environment    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ growth: a permanent  increase in number **OR** size of cells    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ excretion: the removal of metabolic waste    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ response: the ability to react to changes in the environment [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ movement: change in position OR location in relation to the surroundings [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Two functions of life carried out by the cell are **excretion**, which is the **removal of metabolic waste** ($\checkmark$) and **reproduction** which is **producing offspring** ($\checkmark$).
