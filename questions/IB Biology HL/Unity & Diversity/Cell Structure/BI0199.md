# BI0199

**Maximum mark: 3**

All organisms contain genetic material. The type, quantity and location of genetic material differ between kingdoms and species.

1. State if the cell shown in the image is a prokaryotic or eukaryotic cell. Give a reason for your answer. [1 mark]

![BI0199](images/BI0199.png)

2. Distinguish between prokaryotic and eukaryotic DNA. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. The following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic cell **BECAUSE** chromosome is looped **OR** no nucleus is present     [1 mark]

    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
    This is a **prokaryotic**($\checkmark$) cell because the chromosome is **not within a nucleus**($\checkmark$).

2. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic DNA is looped, **BUT** the majority of eukaryotic DNA is linear  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic DNA is not contained in a nucleus, **BUT** the majority of eukaryotic DNA is found within a nucleus     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ prokaryotic DNA is found in a chromosome and plasmids **WHEREAS** the majority of eukaryotic DNA is found in chromosomes and not in plasmids [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the majority of eukaryotic DNA is always associated with proteins (histones) **WHILE**  prokaryotic DNA is not bound to proteins (with the exception of *Archae* whose DNA is associated with proteins)  [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Prokaryotic DNA is looped while the majority of eukaryotic **DNA is linear**($\checkmark$). DNA can be found in **plasmids in some prokaryotes**($\checkmark$), while the only plasmids found in eukaryotes are present in mitochondria and chloroplasts.
