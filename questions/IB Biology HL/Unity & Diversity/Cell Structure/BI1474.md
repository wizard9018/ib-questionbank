# BI1474

Which statement(s) provide evidence for the endosymbiotic theory?

- I. $\hspace{1.6em}$ Mitochondria and chloroplasts have adaptations that increase their surface area

- II. $\hspace{1.3em}$ Mitochondria and chloroplasts can make ATP

- III. $\hspace{1em}$ Mitochondria and chloroplasts have their own ribosomes

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ III only

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

The endosymbiotic theory explains that modern eukaryotic cells evolved from prokaryotic cells, when larger prokaryotes engulfed, but did not digest, other prokaryotes that are the precursors of modern chloroplasts and mitochondria. Evidence for this includes mitochondria and chloroplasts being the only organelles in eukaryotes that have their own ribosomes (**Statement III**). **Statement II**  is also correct, as this is a key similarity to prokaryotes, which also produce ATP through processes like cellular respiration (mitochondria) and photosynthesis (chloroplasts). This supports the idea that these organelles were once free-living bacteria capable of producing ATP. **Choice C**  is therefore correct.

**Statement I**  is incorrect as the adaptations that increase the surface area of mitochondria and chloroplasts are not evidence for endosymbiosis. 

**Choice B** is incorrect as it is missing one of the true statements.

**Choices A** and **D** are incorrect as they contain **Statement I**
