# BI1375

The image shows a cladogram for several species of hamster.

![BI1375](images/BI1375.png)

1. State what is represented by the letter **X**. [1 mark]

2. Other than their positions on the cladogram, explain what information indicates that *Phodopus campbelli* and *Phodopus sungorus* are closely related.    [1 mark]

3. Modern hamsters have small, furry ears. The common ancestor of the hamsters in the cladogram has been hypothesised to have large, furry ears. Suggest how natural selection explains these changes.        [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$  node    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$  the last common ancestor of *Phodopus campbelli* **AND** *Phodopus sungorus*        [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Letter X represents a **node** ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ they are from the same genus **OR** in the *Phodopus* genus        [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    They are in the same **genus** ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ natural selection is the process by which species change over time in response to changes in the environment **OR** competition between organisms in order for species to survive [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ random mutation resulted in new allele for smaller ears        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increases genetic variation **OR** diversity within the  population        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ mutation **OR** new allele **OR** allele for smaller ears proved beneficial **OR** led to greater chance of survival        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ increased reproductive success    **OR** more chance of having offspring **OR** having more offspring        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ the offspring inherited the allele for smaller ears **OR** beneficial alleles passed onto next generation            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ over many generations the allele for shorter ears increased in frequency in the population **OR** small-eared hamsters increased in number in the population [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
   Many years ago, **random mutations** occurred in some hamsters that gave them **new alleles** for smaller ears ($\checkmark$), the allele for smaller ears gave those hamsters a **survival advantage** ($\checkmark$). These hamsters were **more likely** to have **offspring** ($\checkmark$), and the **beneficial allele** for small ears was **passed onto** the **offspring** ($\checkmark$). Over **many generations**, there was an **increase** in the number of hamsters with **smaller ears** and a **decrease** in the number of hamsters with **larger ears** ($\checkmark$).
