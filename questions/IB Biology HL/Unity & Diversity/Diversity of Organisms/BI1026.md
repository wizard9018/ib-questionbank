# BI1026

Which row in the table is correct for either the biological or morphological species concept?

| | Concept | Species Definition | Proposed by… |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Biological | A group of organisms that can interbreed to produce fertile offspring | Linnaeus |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Biological | A group of organisms with shared traits | Mayr |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Morphological | A group of organisms that can interbreed to produce fertile offspring | Mayr |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Morphological | A group of organisms with shared traits | Linnaeus |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The morphological concept refers to a species as a group of organisms with shared traits. It assumes that individuals that look alike are part of the same species. This was proposed by Linnaeus. **Choice D** is correct. **Choice A** is incorrect because although the definition of the biological species concept is correct, it was not proposed by Linnaeus, it was proposed by Mayr. **Choices B** and **C** are incorrect because neither the definitions nor the proposers are correct.
