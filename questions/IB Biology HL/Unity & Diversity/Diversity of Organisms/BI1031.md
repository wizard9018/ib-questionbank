# BI1031

Environmental DNA can be used to form which of the following?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Barcodes

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Karyotypes

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Dichotomous keys

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Binomial naming system

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because environmental DNA is genetic material shed by organisms into their environment and can be used to produce barcodes for each species.  **Choice C** is incorrect because environmental DNA does not directly lend itself to the construction of dichotomous keys, as these keys are usually based on observable physical characteristics rather than genetic material. **Choice D** is incorrect as while eDNA analysis can help identify species, it doesn't play a direct role in assigning scientific names, so is not used as the basis for the binomial naming system. **Choice B** is incorrect, as environmental DNA does not provide enough detailed information about individual chromosomes to construct karyotypes.
