- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Sperm

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Egg
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Brain cell
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Skin cell

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because there are 23 chromosomes, so it is a haploid cell, and one of the sex chromosomes is the ‘Y’ chromosome.  Egg cells do not have the Y chromosome. **Choice B** is incorrect because all egg cells have one ‘X’ sex chromosome. **Choice C** and **choice D** are incorrect because skin and brain cells are diploid, so they have two of each chromosome: 46 in total.
