# BI2663

The diagram below shows the results obtained from the genetic fingerprinting of environmental DNA in a soil sample.

![BI2663](images/BI2663.png)

How many different species are present in this sample?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 9

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 8

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 4

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 6

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because there are four different types of DNA profiles with the same banding patterns visible. The well on the left contains the DNA ladder and so should not be counted as a species. Samples 1, 7, and 8 are all the same species, as are samples 3 and 6 and samples 4 and 5. Sample 2 is a unique species.

**Choice A** is not correct because many of the DNA profiles are identical so they must be from the same species, not different ones. It also includes the DNA ladder in the count

**Choice B** is not correct because it counts repeating DNA profiles as different species.

**Choice D** is not correct because it also counts organisms with the same profiles as different species.
