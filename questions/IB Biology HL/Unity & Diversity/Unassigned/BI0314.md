# BI0314

Which statement highlights the importance of nucleosomes in DNA supercoiling ?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ To fit the length of DNA into the nucleus

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ To access specific DNA sequences

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ To form a circular chromosome

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ To hold the information needed for coding amino acids

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

A nucleosome helps to supercoil the DNA in order to package the DNA into a more condensed and compact structure. With this packaging, a length of DNA, totalling 2m, would be able to fit into the nucleus. **Choice A** is correct.

Even though the DNA is packed into the nucleus, nucleosomes can still allow access to specific DNA sequences for the transcription process (**Choice B**). DNA can be accessed with or without the nucleosomes present.

The circular chromosome found in bacterial cells is shorter than the chromosomes of  eukaryotic cells so they do not require any DNA packaging, hence the absence of nucleosomes (**Choice C**). 

DNA acts as a template for RNA transcription, which then is used to code for amino acids. A nucleosome is used to supercoil the DNA and does not hold the information to code for amino acids (**Choice D**).
