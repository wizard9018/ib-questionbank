# BI0572

**Maximum mark: 8**

1. Outline the steps in calculating the magnification of a micrograph.  [4 marks]

2. Describe the steps taken to focus on a specimen using the 40x objective lens of a light microscope.      [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **four** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$ measure (using a ruler) the scale bar on the drawing **OR** micrograph   [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ note the stated length which the scale bar represents [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ convert the represented and real length or scale bar into the same units [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ M= I/A **OR** any correct example of the calculation     [1 mark]
[4 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    To calculate the magnification of a microphotograph, the first step is to measure **the scale bar using a ruler** ($\checkmark$). The second step is **to note the length the scale bar represents** ($\checkmark$). This gives you the actual size of the image. These two measurements should be converted to **the same units** ($\checkmark$). The last step is to **divide the length measured by the ruler by the length represented by the scale bar** ($\checkmark$). During this calculation, units cancel out.  Therefore, the **magnification does not have units**.

2. Any **four** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$  use the lowest power **OR** 4X objective lens first        [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ place the specimen **OR** slide directly below the objective lens **OR** on the centre of the microscope stage   [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$  turn the coarse focus knob to focus on the specimen at 4X  [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ adjust the turret **OR** change the objective lens so the medium power **OR** 10X objective lens is used     [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ refocus on the specimen using a fine focus knob at 10X and 40X [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$  adjust the turret **OR** change the objective lens, so the high power **OR** 40X objective lens is used [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ while fine focusing always move the lens away from the slide [1 mark]
[4 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
Place the specimen **in the centre** of the microscope stage  ($\checkmark$).  Use the **4x objective lens first**  ($\checkmark$) and use the **coarse focus knob**  ($\checkmark$) to focus on the specimen.  Adjust the position of the specimen so it is in the middle of the field of view and then **switch to the 10x**  ($\checkmark$) objective lens and **refocus using the fine focus knob**, ($\checkmark$) by moving lens **away from the specimen**  ($\checkmark$) to prevent lens breakage.  Finally, switch to the **40x objective lens** ($\checkmark$) and use the **fine focus knob** to refocus on the specimen.
