## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ clearly observable [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ binary **OR** providing a clear either/or choice **OR** two mutually exclusive options [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the characteristic should be consistently present in the organism it aims to group and consistently absent in the alternative group [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the two states of the characteristic should not be overlapping **OR** ambiguous [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the characteristic used should be present **OR** interpretable across all items being identified by the key [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 A good characteristic for a dichotomous key should be **clearly visible** ($\checkmark$) so that it can be easily observed in all organisms being identified. It should also offer a clear **either/or option**, such as presence or absence, so the user can make a **definitive choice** ($\checkmark$).

2. **three** of the following (*must include at least **one** advantage and **one** disadvantage to gain full marks*):

 ***advantages***\
 $\ce{ -> }$ ease of observation of colour [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ there are a wide variety of flower colours, increasing the possibility of differentiating between species [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ can be a genetically fixed trait in some species [1 mark]

 ***disadvantages***\
 $\ce{ -> }$ subjective perception of colour [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ variability within a species [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ environmental influence [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ image limitations (black and white) [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ colour change with flower developmental stage [1 mark]

 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Using colour for a dichotomous key has advantages and disadvantages. On the positive side, colour is **easy to observe** ($\checkmark$) and there is a **wide variety of colours** so that it is easy to distinguish between species ($\checkmark$). Colour can also be a disadvantage because different people may describe or **interpret the same colour differently** ($\checkmark$), leading to inconsistent results. Also, **flower colour can vary** within a single plant ($\checkmark$), and the colour of a single flower can change as it grows.

3. any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ use of appropriate observable characteristics **OR** each step uses clear binary, and non-overlapping characteristics [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ structure of the key is logical and dichotomous **OR** the key follows a binary format at each step [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ accurate identification and separation of all four flowers [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ clear formatting and correct labelling of the key **OR** steps are numbered or lettered correctly and lead logically to species names [1 mark]

 *students may use any observable traits visible in the diagram*

 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

 **1a**. Flower head with a prominent central disk surrounded by many petals— **Sunflower**\
 **1b**. ($\checkmark$) Flower without a prominent central disk… **go to 2** ($\checkmark$)\
 2a. Flower with petals fused into a distinct central trumpet or funnel shape… **Daffodil**\
 2b Flower without petals fused into a distinct central trumpet or funnel shape… go to 3\
 3a Numerous and overlapping petals… **Garden Peony**\
 3b Flowers clustered together on the stem… **Purple Loosestrife** ($\checkmark$)
