# BI0856

Which of the following provides evidence for evolution through selective breeding?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Phenotypic variation in the domesticated dog (*Canis familiaris*) has increased significantly as a result of humans selecting desirable traits. |
    | II. | Crop yield in domesticated crop plants is significantly greater than their wild relatives. |
    | III. | *Homo sapiens* in many parts of the world have alleles that provide resistance to diseases endemic to their region. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because **Statements I** and **II** are correct.  **Statement I** supports the theory of evolution because an increase in phenotypes is a component of the process of evolution.  **Statement II** supports the theory of evolution because it exemplifies that humans, selecting for desirable traits, can cause directional selection in crop plants.

Although **Statement III** does support the theory of evolution, it does not do so as an example of selective breeding.  Humans have not been selectively bred.  **Choices B, C** and **D** do not exclusively include **Statements I and II**, so they are incorrect.
