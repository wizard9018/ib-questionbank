# BI0232

Which of the following is **not** an example of speciation?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Evolution of different beak shapes in finches (*C. olivacea*)

- B. $\hspace{1em}$    Breeding varieties of green apples (*M. domestica*) with desirable characteristics

- C. $\hspace{1em}$    Squirrels on the North and South rims of the grand canyon, who cannot interbreed

- D. $\hspace{1em}$    Buffalo grass (*B. dactyloides*) is able to grow in polluted soil but other species of grass cannot

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because it refers to selective breeding, not speciation. **Choices A**, **C** and **D** are all examples of speciation.
