# BI0858

What sequence of events usually occur during the process of speciation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Reproductive isolation → one species becomes two species → random mutations

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Random mutations → reproductive isolation → one species becomes another species

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Geographic isolation → reproductive isolation → one species becomes another species

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Geographic isolation → reproductive isolation → one species becomes two species

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because geographic isolation leads to a buildup of different mutations between two populations, leading to reproductive isolation and the production of two species from one.

**Choice A** is incorrect, as reproductive isolation cannot occur prior to a buildup of random mutations.  **Choices B** and **C** are incorrect because speciation involves one species becoming two, not one.
