# BI0228

Which of the following is/are example(s) of selective breeding?

- I. $\hspace{1em}$ A dog is trained to sit on command
- II. $\hspace{0.7em}$ Farmers repeatedly breed cattle with the highest milk yield over many generations

- III. $\hspace{0.5em}$A bacterial population becomes antibiotic-resistant after exposure to antibiotics

- A. $\hspace{1em}$    I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$    II only
- C. $\hspace{1em}$    II and III only
- D. $\hspace{1em}$    I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is not true. Training a dog changes behaviour through learning. It does not involve choosing parents to reproduce, and the learned behaviour is not a genetic trait passed on through reproduction.
**Statement II** is true. This is classic artificial selection, where humans choose which cows reproduce based on a desired inherited trait (high milk yield). Over many generations, the alleles linked to that trait become more common.
**Statement III** is not true. Antibiotics act as an environmental selection pressure. Resistant bacteria survive and reproduce more successfully, so resistance increases in the population. This is natural selection, not humans selecting parents to breed for a desired trait.

**Choice B** is correct because it includes the only true **Statement II**.

**Choices A, C** and **D** are all incorrect as they include one or more of the untrue **Statements I** and **III**.
