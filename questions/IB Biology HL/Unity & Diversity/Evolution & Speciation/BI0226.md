# BI0226

Which statement best defines evolution?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Humans breed organisms to produce desired traits

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Two populations become reproductively isolated

-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ A change in the heritable characteristics of a population over generations

-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Individuals change their characteristics during their lifetime in response to the environment

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because evolution is defined as a change in heritable characteristics (genetic traits) within a population over generations. The key ideas are population-level change and inheritance across generations.

**Choice A** is incorrect because this describes selective breeding (artificial selection), where humans choose which organisms reproduce to increase desired traits. It can cause evolutionary change, but it is not the definition of evolution.

**Choice B** is incorrect because reproductive isolation (no interbreeding or gene flow) can be a mechanism leading to speciation, but isolation itself is not the definition of evolution. Evolution is about changes in heritable traits in a population.

**Choice D** is incorrect because this describes acquired characteristics or acclimatisation (changes during an individual's lifetime). These changes are generally not inherited genetically, so they do not define evolution.
