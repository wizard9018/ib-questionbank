# BI0855

Which statement best illustrates the process of evolution?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ A horse that increases its maximum running speed has faster offspring.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ A random mutation gives resistance to a disease and spreads in the population.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Mutations occur as needed to increase fitness in individuals and populations.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The most complex organisms are the most evolved.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because evolution is the change in heritable characteristics of a population, and random mutations alter heritable traits.  In this case the mutation confers resistance to a disease, making these individuals more likely to survive and pass on the mutation to their offspring.  Over time resistance to the disease becomes more prevalent in the population.

**Choice A** is incorrect because traits acquired through an organism's lifetime cannot be passed on to the next generation.  **Choice C** is incorrect because mutations are random and do not occur because an individual or population requires a different trait.  **Choice D** is incorrect because complexity is not a measure of evolutionary progress, as evolution refers to change over time.
