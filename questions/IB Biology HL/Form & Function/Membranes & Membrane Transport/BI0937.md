# BI0937

Which statement describes endocytosis?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Protein-containing vesicles from the Golgi apparatus fusing with the cell membrane

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The contractile vacuole in *Paramecium* expelling water

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Oxygen diffusing from a red blood cell to a respiring tissue

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ A bacterium being taken up by a macrophage.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The macrophage, a type of white blood cell, engulfs the bacterium through the process of phagocytosis, a specific form of endocytosis.  The cell membrane surrounds the bacterium and forms a vesicle, called a phagosome, which then fuses with a lysosome. The resulting phagolysosome contains enzymes that break down and destroy the bacterium.  **Choice D** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because it is describing exocytosis, the process by which substances are expelled from cells via vesicles merging with the plasma membrane and releasing their contents to the external environment. **Choice B** is incorrect as it is a process which involves the expelling of water from the cell rather than taking a substance into the cell. **Choice C** is incorrect as it is an example of simple diffusion, the random movement of particles from an area of high concentration to an area of low concentration.
