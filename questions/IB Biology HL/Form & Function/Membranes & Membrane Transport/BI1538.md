# BI1538

What type of transport is phagocytosis?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Endocytosis, which is a form of transport requiring energy

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Endocytosis, which is a form of passive transport
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Exocytosis, which is a form of transport requiring energy
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Exocytosis, which is a form of passive transport

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Phagocytosis, carried out by phagocytic cells, is a type of bulk transport that involves moving large particles into the cell using energy. This process is a form of endocytosis and is classified as a transport mechanism that requires energy, similar to active transport. Choice A is therefore the correct answer to this question.

Endocytosis requires energy expenditure and is therefore not passive (Choice B). Pinocytosis moves material into the cell, so is considered a type of endocytosis, not exocytosis (Choices C and D).
