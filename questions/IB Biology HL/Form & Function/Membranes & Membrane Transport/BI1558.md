# BI1558

Which of the following structures plays an important role in modulating membrane fluidity at different temperatures?

![BI1558ii](images/BI1558ii.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because the structure indicated is cholesterol. Cholesterol is embedded within the phospholipid bilayer and plays a critical role in modulating membrane fluidity by stabilising the membrane at higher temperatures and preventing it from becoming too rigid at lower temperatures.

**Choice A** is incorrect because it represents a glycoprotein, which is involved in cell recognition and immune responses. **Choice B** is incorrect because it represents a channel protein which is involved in transporting substances. **Choice C** is incorrect because it represents the phosphate head of a phospholipid, which does not directly influence membrane fluidity, but rather contributes to the overall structure and organisation of the membrane.
