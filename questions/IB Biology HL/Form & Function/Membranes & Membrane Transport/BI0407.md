# BI0407

Which of the following types of membrane transport do **NOT** require energy expenditure?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ Facilitated diffusion
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Active transport
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Osmosis

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ II only

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Facilitated diffusion and osmosis are both passive means of transport and therefore do not require energy.

Active transport (**Choice A**) requires energy expenditure.  Facilitated diffusion is a passive form of transport but active transport requires energy (**Choice B**).  Facilitated diffusion (**Choice D**) does not require energy expenditure but this answer choice does not include osmosis.
