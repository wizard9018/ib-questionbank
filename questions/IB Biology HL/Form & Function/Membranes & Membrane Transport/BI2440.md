# BI2440

**Maximum mark: 4**

Below is a diagram representing the interaction between two cells.

![BI2440](images/BI2440.png)

1.
 Identify the type of molecule X represents in the diagram. [1 mark]

2. Explain the importance of these molecules in facilitating cell adhesion. [3 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
 the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ glycoprotein [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The molecule is likely to be a **glycoprotein** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ carbohydrate chains on glycoproteins interact with receptors **OR** complementary molecules on the surface of adjacent cells **WHICH** allows cells to recognise and adhere to each other [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ glycoproteins help form tight junctions **WHICH** creates a barrier that prevents the passage of molecules between cells [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ glycoproteins form desmosomes **WHICH** are specialised structures that maintain physical connections between cells **OR** anchor cells together **OR** provide mechanical strength [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ glycoproteins enable cell migration during development **OR** wound healing, where cells must adhere to one another to build **OR** regenerate tissues [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ disruptions in glycoprotein function can lead to impaired adhesion **WHICH** can lead to cells failing to adhere properly **OR** promoting metastasis in cancer **OR** other diseases [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Cells adhere to each other because the **carbohydrate chains** on the glycoproteins of one cell, recognise and then bind to **complementary** molecules on the other cell ($\checkmark$). These glycoproteins help to form **tight connections** between cells, preventing molecules from passing between them ($\checkmark$). **Desmosomes**, formed by glycoproteins, are specialised structures that provide additional strength, holding cells securely together ($\checkmark$).
