# BI0403

Which one of the following transport proteins only carries out active transport?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Protein channels

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Protein pumps
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Protein carriers
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Aquaporins

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Protein pumps are membrane proteins that carry out active transport.  They require the energy to pump molecules up their concentration gradient.

Protein channels (**Choice A**), protein carriers (**Choice C**), and aquaporins (**Choice D**) all conduct passive transport.
