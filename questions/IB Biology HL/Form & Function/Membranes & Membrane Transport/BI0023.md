# BI0023

Which of the following is **not** a function of membrane proteins?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Cell adhesion

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Cell-to-cell communication

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Transport of oxygen gas

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Enzymatic action to catalyse chemical reactions

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Oxygen gas is exchanged by simple diffusion,  it diffuses freely across the lipid bilayer of the cell membrane due to its small size and nonpolar nature and so does not need to be transported by a membrane protein. **Choice C** is therefore the correct answer to this question.

Cell adhesion is a function of membrane proteins; this is fundamental for the formation of tissues and organs and is mediated by specific adhesion molecules. **Choice A** is, therefore, an incorrect answer to this question.

Membrane proteins are essential for cell communication. They can act as receptors that bind signalling molecules (ligands) such as hormones and neurotransmitters, which trigger internal cellular responses. **Choice B** is, therefore, an incorrect answer to this question.

Some membrane proteins are enzymes that catalyse biochemical reactions on the inner or outer surface of the cell membrane, e.g. ATP synthase. **Choice D** is, therefore, an incorrect answer to this question.
