# BI1378

The following diagram shows the structure of a plasma membrane. What is the function of the structure labelled X?

![BI1378b](images/BI1378b.png)

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Modulates membrane fluidity

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Aids in the facilitated diffusion of glucose

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Increases membrane permeability to ions and polar molecules

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Acts as a surface receptor to communicate with other cells

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The molecule shown in the diagram is cholesterol. **Choice A** is correct because cholesterol plays an important role in maintaining the structural integrity and fluidity of the plasma membrane.

**Choice B** is incorrect since glucose transport is controlled by carrier proteins, not cholesterol. **Choice C** is incorrect since cholesterol reduces the membrane's permeability to substances such as ions and polar molecules rather than increasing it. **Choice D** is incorrect as surface receptors are generally glycoproteins or glycolipids, not cholesterol.
