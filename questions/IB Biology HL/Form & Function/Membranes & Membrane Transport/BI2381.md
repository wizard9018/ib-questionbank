# BI2381

In neonatal mammals, intestinal epithelial cells acquire antibodies from maternal milk by taking in fluid via vesicle formation.\
Which term best describes this process?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Phagocytosis

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Endocytosis

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Pinocytosis

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Exocytosis

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because pinocytosis is the uptake of liquid in bulk using vesicles - which carry material in quantities more than one molecule at a time. The latter would happen in active transport or diffusion across the bilayer.

**Choice A** is incorrect because phagocytosis is the transport of solid particles into a cell such as the process that precedes the destruction of live pathogens in the human immune system.

**Choice B** is incorrect because endocytosis is a broad term that refers to uptake into a cell, whether in liquid form (pinocytosis) or solid form (phagocytosis). As such, the more precise answer, **Choice C**, is a subset of **Choice B**.

**Choice D** is incorrect because exocytosis occurs when vesicles inside the cell fuse with the cell membrane and eject material out of the cell.
