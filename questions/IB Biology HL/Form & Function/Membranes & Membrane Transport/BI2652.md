# BI2652

Which of the following membrane proteins always uses ATP to transport particles across membranes?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Channel proteins

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Aquaporins

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Pump proteins

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Carrier proteins

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Pump proteins actively transport substances across membranes against their concentration gradient using energy from ATP; **Choice C** is correct.

Channel proteins allow passive transport, by facilitated diffusion, of ions or molecules down their concentration gradient, so do not use ATP. **Choice A** is incorrect.\
Aquaporins are specialised channel proteins which allow water to diffuse passively across the membrane, so again do not use ATP. **Choice B** is incorrect.\
Carrier proteins can be involved in passive or active transport., so not all carrier proteins use ATP, which means the term alone is too broad to be correct here. **Choice D** is incorrect.
