# BI2326

Membrane permeability is determined by the mosaic of membrane components. Which structure in the membrane facilitates the transport of small, hydrophobic molecules?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because the phospholipid bilayer facilitates the transport of small, hydrophobic molecules via simple diffusion. Small and hydrophobic molecules can pass directly through the phospholipid bilayer's hydrophobic core without requiring a protein channel or carrier. The size and properties of the molecules determine the bilayer's selective permeability.

**Choice  A** is incorrect because transmembrane proteins facilitate the transport of molecules via facilitated diffusion or active transport, which are required for larger, hydrophilic, or charged particles. These proteins are not involved in the simple diffusion of hydrophobic molecules.

**Choice B** is incorrect because cholesterol primarily regulates the fluidity and stability of the membrane at different temperatures. It does not directly influence the movement of small, hydrophobic molecules through simple diffusion.

**Choice C** is incorrect because glycoproteins are involved in cell recognition and adhesion. They play no role in the direct transport of molecules across the membrane.
