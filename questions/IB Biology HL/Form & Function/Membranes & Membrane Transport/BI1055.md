3. any **eight** of the following (must include at least **two** similarities and **two** differences to gain full marks):

 ***Similarities:*** \
 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have carbohydrate chains as part of their structure [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** glycoproteins and glycolipids are involved in cell recognition [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** contribute to cell adhesion [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ carbohydrate chains on **BOTH** molecules act as unique markers that allow cells to interact with their environment [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** types of molecules contribute to the glycocalyx, providing protection and lubrication for the cell surface [1 mark]

 ***Differences:*** \
 $\ce{ -> }$ glycoproteins consist of a protein backbone with attached carbohydrate chains, **WHEREAS** glycolipids consist of phospholipid with attached carbohydrate chains [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ glycoproteins have a variety of roles **WHEREAS** glycolipids primarily contribute to membrane stability and lubrication [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ glycoproteins act as hormone receptors, **WHEREAS** glycolipids do not [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ glycoproteins can have enzymatic roles **WHEREAS** glycolipids do not [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ glycolipids play a more direct role **THAN** glycoproteins in affecting membrane fluidity due to their lipid component [1 mark]
 [7 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 **Both** glycoproteins and glycolipids have **carbohydrate chains** ($\checkmark$) as part of their structure, which act as **unique markers** that allow **interaction** with the environment ($\checkmark$). However, in **glycoproteins** the carbohydrate is attached to a protein whereas, in **glycolipids** it is attached to a **phospholipid** ($\checkmark$). **Both** glycolipids and glycoproteins are involved in **cell recognition** ($\checkmark$) and contribute to **cell adhesion** ($\checkmark$). However, **glycoproteins** have a **greater variety** of roles than glycolipids ($\checkmark$), which are **primarily** involved in **membrane stability** and lubrication ($\checkmark$). **Glycoproteins** can also act as **hormone receptors** ($\checkmark$) and **enzymes** ($\checkmark$).
