# BI0576

The important properties of animal cell membranes are fluidity and permeability. Which molecule is responsible for ensuring the membrane’s fluidity?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Cholesterol

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Integral membrane proteins

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Glycolipids

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Peripheral membrane proteins

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** - Cholesterol is positioned between the phospholipids and maintains the fluidity of the cell membrane.

**Choice B** - Integral Membrane Proteins are responsible for the transport of materials in and out of the cell as well as other functions such as cell-cell communication.

**Choice C** - Glycolipids allow cells to recognize each other.

**Choice D** - Peripheral membrane proteins are involved in maintaining the extracellular matrix and cytoskeleton, in cell-cell communication, and sometimes function as enzymes.
