# BI1510

Which of the following are examples of molecules that are cotransported with sodium through indirect active transport?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}

    | | |

    | :-- | :-- |

    | I. | Glucose absorption into small intestine epithelial cells |

    | II. | Potassium through sodium-potassium pumps |

    | III. | Glucose reabsorption in nephrons |

| | |

| --- | :-- |

| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | I only |

| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | I and II only |

| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | I and III only |

| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | I, II and III |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

Indirect active transport, also known as secondary active transport, involves the use of energy stored from an electrochemical gradient of one molecule to transport another molecule against the concentration gradient.

Glucose is cotransported with sodium ions from the small intestine lumen into epithelial cells via sodium-glucose cotransporters (SGLTs). The sodium-potassium pump maintains a low intracellular sodium concentration by expelling sodium, indirectly providing the energy for glucose absorption against its concentration gradient; **Statement I** is true.

The sodium-potassium pump uses ATP for direct active transport, moving sodium out and potassium into the cell against their concentration gradients. It maintains the sodium gradient crucial for indirect active transport but does not involve the cotransport of sodium with another molecule. This gradient enables indirect active transport.; **Statement II** is therefore not true. 

Glucose reabsorption in nephrons is an example of indirect active transport. In the kidneys, particularly in the proximal tubule, glucose is reabsorbed from the filtrate alongside sodium ions via sodium-glucose cotransporters. The sodium-potassium pump maintains the sodium concentration gradient necessary for this cotransport, enabling glucose to be reabsorbed against its concentration gradient; **Statement III** is, therefore, true. 

**Statements I** and **III** are true, so **Choice C** is the correct answer to this question. 

**Choice A** is incorrect as it does not contain **Statement III**. **Choices B** and **D** include **Statement II** so are not correct.
