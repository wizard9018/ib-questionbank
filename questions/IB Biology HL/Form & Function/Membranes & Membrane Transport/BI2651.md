# BI2651

The diagram shows a membrane with four structures labelled I - IV.

![BI2651](images/BI2651.png)

Which row correctly names all four of the structures in the diagram?

| | I. | II. | III. | IV. |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Glycolipid | Cholesterol | Channel protein | Glycoprotein |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Glycoprotein | Glycolipid | Channel protein | Cholesterol |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Glycoprotein | Glycolipid | Carrier protein | Cholesterol |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Glycoprotein | Cholesterol | Carrier protein | Glyoclipid |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**I** s glycoprotein, as it shows a carbohydrate chain attached to a protein. \
**II** is a glycolipid, as it shows a carbohydrate chain attached to a lipid. \
**III** is a channel protein, as it spans the membrane and has a pore through the middle. \
**IV** a cholesterol molecule, as it is a small molecule, made up of carbon rings, embedded in the bilayer.\
**Choice B** is therefore the correct row.

**Choice C** is incorrect because **III**. is not a carrier protein. Carrier proteins have specific binding sites, change shape during transport and they open to one side at a time. This protein shows a straight pore, or channel, running through the middle of it so it is a channel protein.

**Choice A** is incorrect as it only labels the channel protein correctly, and **Choice D** is incorrect as it only labels the glycoprotein correctly.
