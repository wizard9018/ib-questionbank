# BI0015

Which of these membrane proteins carry out active transport?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Channel proteins

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Pump proteins

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Peripheral proteins

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Receptors

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Protein pumps are membrane proteins that pump molecules up their concentration gradient using energy in the form of ATP.

Channel proteins (**Choice A**) carry out passive transport while receptors (**Choice D**) and peripheral proteins  (**Choice C**) do not transport proteins.
