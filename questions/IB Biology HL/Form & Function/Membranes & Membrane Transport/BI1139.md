---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The image shows endocytosis.

**Choices B** and **C** are incorrect since they are types of passive transport. **Choice D** is incorrect since this is endocytosis, not exocytosis.
