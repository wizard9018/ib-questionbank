# BI0406

Which type of transport is represented by this diagram?

![BI0406ii](images/BI0406ii.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Active transport

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Simple diffusion
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Facilitated diffusion
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Protein channel

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Facilitated diffusion occurs with the help of protein channels or carrier proteins.

Active transport (**Choice A**) requires energy expenditure in the form of ATP and moves substances up their concentration gradient. Simple diffusion (**Choice B**) occurs through the cell membrane without the use of membrane proteins. Protein channels (**Choice D**) often carry out facilitated diffusion, but they are not themselves a type of transport.
