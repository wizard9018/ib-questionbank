# BI1511

The diagram below represents a voltage-gated sodium channel in a neuron. Which is likely the **function** of structure I, after it has gone through this conformational change, and the **effect** of structure II in a neuron?

![sodium v-g channel ii](<images/sodium v-g channel ii.png>)

| | I | II |

| --- | :-: | :-: |

| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$Inactivation of channel$\hspace{3em}$ | $\hspace{3em}$Depolarisation$\hspace{3em}$ |

| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Inactivation of channel | Repolarisation |

| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Activation of channel | Depolarisation |

| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Activation of channel | Repolarisation |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

**Label I** indicates the activation of the voltage-gated channel. **Label II** indicates Na:sup[+] diffusion, which leads to depolarisation in a neuron. **Choice C** is, therefore, correct.

**Choices A, B** and **D** are all incorrect as they do not have both the correct function of structure I and the effect of structure II.
