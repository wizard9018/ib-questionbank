# BI0016

Which of the following is **not** a function of cholesterol in cell membranes?

- A. It reduces membrane fluidity by restricting the movement of phospholipids.

- B. It increases membrane flexibility.

- C. It serves as a transport channel.

- D. It reduces the membrane's  permeability to hydrophilic substances.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Cholesterol is a steroid, not a transport channel protein.

Reducing membrane fluidity (**Choice A**), increasing membrane flexibility (**Choice B**), and reducing permeability to hydrophilic substances (**Choice D**) are all roles of cholesterol in cell membranes.
