# BI0940

Which allows cells to stick together to form tissues?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Cell cohesion molecules

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Channel proteins

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Cell adhesion molecules

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Cell-cell junction molecules

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Cell adhesion molecules (CAMs) are cell surface proteins that help cells bind together and maintain the structural integrity of tissues, **Choice C**  is therefore correct.

**Choice A**  is incorrect as it is a made up term. **Choice B**  is incorrect, as channel proteins allow molecules to move across the plasma membrane using facilitated diffusion. **Choice D**  is incorrect, as cell-cell junctions are specialised structures that physically connect adjacent cells, facilitating communication and coordinating cellular activities within tissues. Cell-cell junctions can only be formed after the process of cell adhesion has occurred.
