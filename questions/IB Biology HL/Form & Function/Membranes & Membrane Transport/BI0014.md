# BI0014

Which of the structures below can transport water molecules across the cell membrane?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Cholesterol

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Glycoproteins

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Protein pumps

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Channel proteins

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Water is transported passively via channel proteins known as aquaporins.

Cholesterol (**Choice A**) and glycoproteins (**Choice B**) are not components responsible for transport while protein pumps (**Choice C**) carry out active transport which is not utilised in the transport of water.
