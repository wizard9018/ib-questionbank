# BI0017

![plasma membrane with peripheral protein labelled X](<images/plasma membrane with peripheral protein labelled X.png>)

Which component of the cell membrane is represented by structure X?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Transmembrane protein

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Protein channel

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Peripheral protein

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Cholesterol molecule

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Structure X is a peripheral protein that is attached to one surface of the cell membrane.

Protein channels span the width of membranes (**Choice B**) as do transmembrane proteins (**Choice A**). Cholesterol molecules do not have a globular structure (**Choice D**).
