# BI0926

Which of the following is/are true for holozoic nutrition?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Demonstrated by saprotrophs |
    | II. | Involves complete or partial ingestion |
    | III. | Food is digested internally |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Holozoic nutrition refers to the type of nutrition where organisms ingest whole or partially whole food items (**Statement II**). The ingested food is then broken down by the organism's internal digestive system (**Statement III**). Therefore **Choice B**  is correct.
Saprotrophs (**Statement I**) are organisms that obtain their nutrients by decomposing dead and decaying organic matter externally and then absorbing the nutrients. Therefore **Choices A**, **C** and **D** are not correct as they all include **Statement I**.
