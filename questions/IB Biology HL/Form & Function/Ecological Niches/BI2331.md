# BI2331

Which statement is correct for adaptations of herbivores for feeding on plants?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Chewing mouthparts help insects to prevent damage from spines and thorns.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Chewing mouthparts allow insects to directly access vascular tissues.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Piercing-sucking mouthparts allow insects to feed directly on cell contents.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Piercing-sucking mouthparts allow direct feeding from the phloem sap.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because piercing-sucking mouthparts, such as stylets of aphids, are adapted to penetrate plant tissues and access phloem vessels directly. The phloem sap flows into the insect's digestive system without the need for further processing.

**Choice A** is incorrect because chewing mouthparts, such as mandibles, are designed to physically cut and chew leaves but do not prevent damage from plant defences like spines or thorns.

**Choice B** is incorrect because chewing mouthparts do not access vascular tissues such as the phloem; they are for consuming plant material at the surface.

**Choice C** is incorrect because piercing-sucking mouthparts are adapted for feeding on phloem sap, not individual cell contents. Feeding on cell contents would require different adaptations, such as enzymes, to break cells open.
