## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ incisors are used for biting chunks of food                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ canines are used for tearing food                         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ premolars **OR** molars are used for slicing and crushing food [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ molars are used for grinding food                         [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Incisors are found at the front of the mouth and are used for **biting chunks** of food  ($\checkmark$). Canines are located at the side of the mouth and are used for **tearing** food  ($\checkmark$). Molars are found at the back of the mouth and are used for **grinding** food into a paste ($\checkmark$).

1.
    2.
    $\ce{ -> }$ for display **OR** to appear intimidating to rivals **OR** to scare intruders **OR** to fight    [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Gorillas have pronounced canines, possibly to scare away or **intimidate rivals** ($\checkmark$).

1.
    3. Any **two** of the following: (must include a similarity and a difference):

    **Similarities**\
    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have the same types of teeth **OR** have incisors, canines, premolars and molars     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** use incisors for tearing chunks    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** use premolars for slicing or crushing food     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** use molars for grinding food                     [1 mark]

    **Differences**\
    $\ce{ -> }$ canines are more pronounced in carnivores **WHEREAS**, in most herbivores, canines are either small or absent         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ carnivores have long, pointed canines designed for stabbing and piercing their prey, **WHEREAS** herbivores do not primarily rely on killing or tearing apart prey.     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ premolars **OR** molars are flatter/greater surface area in herbivores **WHEREAS** carnivores have sharper premolars and molars / less surface area.            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ carnivores have premolars and molars that help slice through flesh and break bone, **WHEREAS** herbivores have premolars and molars with rough surfaces to break down tough plant cell walls and extract nutrients from vegetation [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ herbivores have a greater number of premolars and molars  [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    **Both** carnivores and herbivores have the **same types of teeth** ($\checkmark$), but the canines are often **larger** and more pronounced in carnivores, whereas in **herbivores**, they are usually **small or absent** ($\checkmark$).

[1 mark]
