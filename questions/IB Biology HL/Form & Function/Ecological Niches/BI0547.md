# BI0547

Chytrids are unicellular organisms that feed on pollen through extracellular secretion of digestive enzymes and absorption of the breakdown products. Which statement describes the nutritional mode of this organism?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Autotrophic producer

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Autotrophic consumer

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Heterotrophic detritivore

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Heterotrophic saprotroph

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The process of secreting digestive enzymes outside of an organism and absorbing the breakdown products is called external digestion. An organism that breaks down organic materials, such as pollen, by external digestion, is a saprotroph. All saprotrophs are heterotrophs (organisms that cannot fix carbon to produce their own food, but instead must consume sources of fixed organic carbon). So, chytrids are described as heterotrophic saprotrophs.

**Choice A** is incorrect because an autotrophic producer synthesises its own food by fixing inorganic carbon (such as from carbon dioxide), which chytrids cannot do.

**Choice B** is incorrect because an autotroph synthesises its own food, which chytrids do not do.  Furthermore autotrophs, by definition, are producers not consumers.

**Choice C** is incorrect because detritivores use internal digestion, not external, to break down food.
