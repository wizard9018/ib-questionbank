# BI0127

Which of the following is NOT a habitat for methanogenic archaeans?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Deep sea waters

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Stomachs of cows

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Well aerated loamy soils

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Swamps

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Methanogenic archaea require anaerobic (oxygen-free) environments to survive, so well-aerated loamy soils are unsuitable habitats because they contain oxygen. This makes **Choice C** the correct answer.

Habitats for methanogenic archaea include deep-sea waters  (**Choice A**), the stomachs of cows and other ruminants  (**Choice B**), and swampy areas that are deficient in oxygen (**Choice D**).
