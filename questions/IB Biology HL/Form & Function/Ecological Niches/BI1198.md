# BI1198

**Maximum mark: 7**

The African bush elephant, *Loxodonta africana*, pushes over trees, maintaining grasslands that provide essential resources such as food for many other animals. Its dung also fertilises the soil and provides a habitat for many species of beetle and fungi. The following diagram shows the flow of inorganic nutrients from elephants to herbivores within this ecosystem.

![BI1198-SL](images/BI1198-SL.png)

1. Define the term ecosystem. [1 mark]

2. Identify one abiotic factor and one biotic factor, other than food availability, affected by the presence of African bush elephants. [2 marks]

3. The faeces buried by the dung beetle is broken down by decomposers. Outline how the action of decomposers increases soil nutrient content. [3 marks]

4. Suggest one soil element, other than carbon, that would probably experience a reduction in concentration if dung beetles were absent from the ecosystem. [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ all living organisms **OR** different living species, along with their physical environment **OR** abiotic factors [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 An ecosystem is **all** the **living organisms**, along with their **physical environment**, that **interact** and function together in a specific habitat ($\checkmark$).

2. any **one** abiotic factor and any **one** biotic factor:

 ***abiotic factors:*** \
 $\ce{ -> }$ increased soil nutrient content [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ increased light intensity at ground level (due to reduced tree canopy) [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ water availability (as a result of the digging of water holes) [1 mark]

 ***biotic factors:*** \
 $\ce{ -> }$ increased abundance of beetles **OR** fungi **OR** decomposers **OR** soil microbes [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ decreased abundance of trees **OR** increased area of grassland [1 mark]

 *Note: if a direction of change is given, it must be correct. Do not accept “increased abundance of trees”. Do not accept “vegetation” unless it is clear whether trees or grass is meant.*

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 African bush elephants push over trees, which increases light intensity at ground level, an **abiotic factor** ($\checkmark$), and decreases the abundance of **trees**, a **biotic factor** ($\checkmark$).

3. any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ decomposers break down organic compounds present in faeces using enzymes [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ releases inorganic nutrients into the soil [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ saprotrophs break down organic compounds outside their bodies using enzymes, and some of the inorganic nutrients released remain in the soil [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ detritivores consume organic compounds present in faeces, digest them internally and release **OR** excrete **OR** egest inorganic nutrients into the soil [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Decomposers increase soil nutrient content by **breaking down organic compounds** present in faeces using **enzymes** ($\checkmark$). The breakdown of these organic compounds **releases inorganic nutrients** into the soil ($\checkmark$). For example, **saprotrophs** break down organic compounds **externally** using enzymes, and much of the resulting nutrients released **remain in the soil** ($\checkmark$).

4. any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ nitrogen **OR** phosphorus **OR** sulphur **OR** potassium **OR** calcium **OR** magnesium **OR** any other correct, named element [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 **Nitrogen** content in the soil would decrease if dung beetles were removed from the ecosystem ($\checkmark$).
