# BI0950

Identify the row in the table which gives the correct information about the different types of organisms.

| | Obligate anaerobes | Facultative anaerobes | Obligate aerobes |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | No tolerance to oxygen | Can carry out anaerobic and aerobic respiration | Require oxygen for respiration |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Can carry out anaerobic and aerobic respiration | Cannot survive in conditions low in oxygen | Require oxygen for respiration |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Cannot survive in conditions low in oxygen | Can respire when oxygen is present | Can carry out anaerobic and aerobic respiration |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Can respire when oxygen is present | Can carry out anaerobic and aerobic respiration | Cannot survive in conditions low in oxygen |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Obligate anaerobes are single-celled organisms that will not survive if oxygen is present. Facultative anaerobes are organisms that can either respire aerobically or anaerobically. Obligate aerobes can only respire if oxygen is present and cannot survive in conditions low in oxygen. Therefore **Choice A** is correct.

**Choices B** and **D** are incorrect because obligate anaerobes cannot respire when oxygen is present. **Choice C** is incorrect as obligate anaerobes require anoxic conditions, and obligate aerobes cannot carry out anaerobic respiration.
