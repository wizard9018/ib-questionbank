# BI0928

What is the purpose of incisors?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Ripping and tearing food

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Biting pieces of food

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Crushing or slicing food

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Grinding food

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because incisors are used for biting pieces of food. **Choice A** is incorrect because tearing is the role of canines. **Choices C** and **D** are incorrect because crushing or slicing is the role of the premolars, and grinding is the role of molars.
