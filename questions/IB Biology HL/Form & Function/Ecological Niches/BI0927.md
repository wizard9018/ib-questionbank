# BI0927

Which term refers to organisms that are both autotrophic and heterotrophic?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Mixotrophic

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Multitrophic

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Saprotrophic

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Facultrophic

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because mixotrophic refers to both autotrophic and heterotrophic organisms.

**Choices B** and **D** are incorrect because multitrophic refers to food webs or ecosystems involving multiple trophic levels, where organisms interact at different levels of the food chain, and facultrophic is not a standard scientific term. **Choice C** is incorrect because saprotrophic organisms are solely heterotrophic and obtain their nutrition by decomposing dead organic matter externally and absorbing the nutrients.
