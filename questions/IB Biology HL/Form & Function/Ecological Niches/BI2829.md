# BI2829

Which condition would most strongly inhibit saprotrophic nutrition?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Low temperature and low oxygen availability

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Presence of antibacterial compounds secreted by the saprobiont

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Anaerobic conditions and high nitrogen availability

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Moderate warmth and access to cellulose-rich material

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct. Saprotrophic organisms like fungi and bacteria rely on enzymes to break down organic matter. Low temperatures reduce enzyme activity, and low oxygen limits aerobic respiration, which many saprotrophs depend on. Together, these conditions strongly inhibit decomposition.

**Choice B** is incorrect. Some saprotrophs produce antibacterial compounds to inhibit other microbes. These substances reduce competition but do not harm the saprotrophs that secrete them.

**Choice C** is incorrect because although anaerobic conditions inhibit aerobic saprotrophs, some anaerobic bacteria can still function, and the presence of nitrogen may actually promote microbial growth.

**Choice D** is incorrect because moderate temperatures support optimal enzyme activity, and cellulose provides a plentiful substrate for fungi and bacteria to decompose.
