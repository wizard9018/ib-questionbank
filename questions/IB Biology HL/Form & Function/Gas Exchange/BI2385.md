# BI2385

Which of the following is an **inaccurate** statement about leaves?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Spongy mesophyll cells increase the surface area available for evaporation of water.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Stomata change their cell contents to allow opening and closing.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The waxy cuticle prevents excessive water loss from the leaf by evaporation.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Carbon dioxide absorption is maximised when the rate of photosynthesis is at its peak.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because it is not the stomata that change their cell contents, it is the guard cells. Stomata are just holes between guard cells, so as such are not tangible structures. Guard cells alter their water content so they are curved in shape when turgid; this causes a stoma to open. Guard cells become flaccid at night, and this causes them to straighten, so they lie flat against each other, closing the stoma between them.

**Choices A, C** and **D** are incorrect because they are all accurate statements.
