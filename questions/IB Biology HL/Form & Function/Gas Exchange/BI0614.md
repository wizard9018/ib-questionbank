# BI0614

Air is carried to the lungs by entering through the nostrils into the nasal cavity, and then as shown in the pathway below.

![BI0614](images/BI0614.png)

Which of the following statements are correct descriptions of structures **P** and **Q**?

| | Characteristic of P | Characteristic of Q |
| :-- | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| Large number of air sacs and a large total surface area | Wall containing C-shaped rings of cartilage |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Cells that secrete a surfactant | Muscles that relax to form a domed shape |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Muscles that relax to form a domed shape | Cells that secrete a surfactant |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Wall containing C-shaped rings of cartilage | Large number of air sacs and a large total surface area |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Air enters the nasal cavity and then passes down the trachea (**P**). The trachea's wall consists of C shaped rings of cartilage. Air that passes down the trachea first enters the bronchi, then bronchioles and finally reaches the alveoli (**Q**). A characteristic of alveoli is the presence of a large number of air sacs and the presence of cells that secrete a surfactant.

**Choice A** is incorrect answer options are switched; characteristic P is referring to alveoli, not the trachea and characteristic of Q is referring to the trachea, not alveoli.

**Choice B** is incorrect because cells that secrete a surfactant is referring to alveoli rather than trachea; and muscles that relax to form a dome is referring to the diaphragm, not alveoli.

**Choice C** is incorrect because muscles that relax to form a dome is referring to the diaphragm, not the trachea.
