# BI1288

Which leaf structure adaptation is correctly matched to its function?

| | Adaptations | Function |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Waxy cuticle | Contains many pores to facilitate gas exchange |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Spongy mesophyll | Creates a large impermeable barrier to prevent loss of gases and water vapour from the surface |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Stomatal guard cells | Controls gas exchange and water loss by opening and closing |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Lower epidermis | Gives a large surface area for gas exchange |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because stomatal guard cells are found mostly at the lower epidermis, which controls gas exchange and water loss by opening and closing the stomata.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the waxy cuticle creates a large impermeable barrier to prevent gases and water vapour loss from the surface. **Choice B** is incorrect because spongy mesophyll provides a large surface area for gas exchange. **Choice D** is incorrect because the lower epidermis contains many stomata to facilitate gas exchange.
