# BI2691

Which of the following statements explains why the oxygen dissociation curve has this characteristic S-shape?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Haemoglobin's first oxygen molecule binds with difficulty, but subsequent oxygen molecules bind more easily. |
    | II. | The steep middle section of the curve results from cooperative binding, where haemoglobin's affinity for oxygen increases. |
    | III. | The plateau at high oxygen partial pressures occurs because haemoglobin is nearly fully saturated, limiting further oxygen binding.|

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is true because at low oxygen partial pressures, haemoglobin is in the Tense (T) state, meaning it has a low affinity for oxygen. This is because the first oxygen molecule binds with difficulty, as the haem groups are not yet exposed in an ideal conformation for oxygen attachment.\
This slow initial binding is why the left end of the oxygen dissociation curve begins with a shallow slope.

**Statement II** is true because the middle section of the curve (around 3–6 kPa) shows a steep increase in saturation. This happens because after the first oxygen binds, haemoglobin undergoes a conformational change that increases its affinity for oxygen. This positive feedback mechanism, known as cooperative binding, results in the rapid uptake of oxygen. As a result, small increases in oxygen partial pressure cause large increases in haemoglobin saturation in this region of the curve.

**Statement III** is true because at high oxygen partial pressures (e.g., 8–10 kPa, found in the lungs), haemoglobin is already nearly fully saturated with oxygen. The oxygen dissociation curve plateaus because there are fewer unoccupied haem groups available for binding. Even though oxygen partial pressure continues to increase, oxygen binding slows down since nearly all haemoglobin molecules are already carrying their maximum of four oxygen molecules.

**Choice D** is correct as it contains all three true statements.

**Choices A, B** and **C** are incorrect as they are missing one or more true statement.
