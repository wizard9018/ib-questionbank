# BI0617

Which of the following takes place during forced exhalation?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ The diaphragm forms a dome shape
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Internal intercostal muscles contract
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Pressure increases inside the lungs

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ II only
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ I and III only
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I**  is true because during exhalation, the diaphragm relaxes and lifts upwards to form a dome shape.
**Statement II** is true because, although passive exhalation does not usually use internal intercostal muscles, during forced exhalation, internal intercostal muscles contract and pull the ribcage inwards and downwards.
**Statement III** is true because as thoracic volume decreases, the pressure inside the lungs increases, pushing air out.

This makes **Choice D** the correct answer as it includes all three true statements.
**Choices A, B** and **C** are all incorrect because they omit one or more true statements.
