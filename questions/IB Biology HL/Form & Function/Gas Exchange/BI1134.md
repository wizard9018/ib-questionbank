# BI1134

Which statement(s) correctly describes a situation where a haemoglobin protein will have a high affinity for oxygen?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | In the capillaries surrounding the alveoli |
    | II. | Near heavily respiring tissues |
    | III. | When two oxygen molecules have bound to haemoglobin |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is correct, as haemoglobin will have a high affinity for oxygen because the partial pressure of oxygen is high due to the oxygen diffusing from the lungs into the blood. **Statement III** is correct, as when oxygen binds to haemoglobin, it leads to a conformational change in the haemoglobin molecule, making it easier for other oxygen molecules to bind. **Choice B**  is correct.

**Statement II** is incorrect because, in heavily respiring tissues, oxygen is able to be loaded easily from haemoglobin to the respiring tissues due to haemoglobin having a low affinity for oxygen. **Choices A, C**  and **D**  are incorrect as they all include **statement II**.
