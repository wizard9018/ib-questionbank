# BI0622

**Maximum mark: 4**

Premature babies sometimes suffer from infant respiratory distress syndrome, which could be caused by a lack of sufficient surfactant in the pulmonary alveoli. The diagram below shows two different alveoli structures.

![BI0622iii](images/BI0622iii.png)

1. Describe **one** feature of the surfactant. [1 mark]

2. Suggest a treatment for infant respiratory distress syndrome. [1 mark]

3. Outline how concentration gradients are maintained at the lungs to enable gaseous exchange. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ reduces surface tension in alveoli **OR** disrupts cohesive forces between water molecules lining alveoli [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ forms a monolayer on the alveolar surface [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ hydrophilic heads facing water **OR** hydrophobic tails facing air [1 mark]

 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
The pulmonary surfactant is a lipoprotein complex that is secreted by type II pneumocytes. It reduces the **surface tension** in alveoli ($\checkmark$) by orienting its **hydrophobic tails** towards the **air** and its **hydrophilic heads** towards the **water** lining the alveoli ($\checkmark$).

2. any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ provide pure oxygen **OR** breathing support [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ administer surfactant from other sources, **OR** from animal lungs [1 mark]

 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Too little surfactant in infants can lead to shortness of breath, so an **oxygen** mask and breathing assistance is necessary ($\checkmark$). Doctors could also **extract the surfactant** from the lungs of animals or produce them in a lab and then administer the surfactant to the baby ($\checkmark$).

3. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ continuous movement of blood [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ inhalation **OR** exhalation **OR** ventilation **OR** movement of air into and out of the lungs [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ binding of oxygen to haemoglobin [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ dense network of capillaries [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

 The **continuous movement of blood** ($\checkmark$)ensures that oxygenated blood is transported away from the lungs and deoxygenated blood is delivered to them. **Ventilation of the lungs** ($\checkmark$) brings air rich in oxygen into the lungs and expels air high in carbon dioxide.
