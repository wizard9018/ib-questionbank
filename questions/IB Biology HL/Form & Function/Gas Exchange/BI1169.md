# BI1169

Which factor does **not** help maintain the oxygen concentration gradient in the alveoli?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ A dense network of capillaries surrounding the alveoli

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Thin-walled alveoli

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Continuous blood flow

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Ventilation from the air

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because, while thin walls are essential for efficient respiration, they are a structural feature that facilitates the movement across the barrier, rather than a functional mechanism that prevents the oxygen concentrations on either side from reaching equilibrium.

**Choice A** is incorrect because a dense network of capillaries ensures that oxygenated blood is rapidly carried away from the alveoli, maintaining a lower oxygen concentration in the blood and preserving the gradient.

**Choice C** is incorrect because continuous blood flow replaces oxygenated blood with deoxygenated blood, preventing equilibrium from being reached and maintaining the gradient.

**Choice D** is incorrect because ventilation continually supplies fresh air to the alveoli, maintaining a high oxygen concentration compared to the surrounding capillaries.
