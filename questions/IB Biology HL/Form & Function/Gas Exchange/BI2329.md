# BI2329

This micrograph shows a print from the underside of a leaf at 40x magnification. Each square of the grid represents 0.5 $\ce{mm^2}$. \
Which value is the most accurate for the stomatal density of the leaf tissue in this photomicrograph, based on squares I and II?

![BI2329 nail polish stomata imprint with 2 boxes labelled](<images/BI2329 nail polish stomata imprint with 2 boxes labelled.png>)
[Adapted from Espt123 (2009 October 7). *Nail polish impression of stomata.jpg*. Retrieved November 25th, 2024 from: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Nail_polish_impression_of_stomata.jpg. Public domain.]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 23 stomata per $\ce{mm^2}$

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 64 stomata per $\ce{mm^2}$

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 28 stomata per $\ce{mm^2}$

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 44 stomata per $\ce{mm^2}$

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:

![stomatal count](<images/stomatal count.png>)

$\hspace{1em}$Square I = 23 stomata

$\hspace{1em}$Square II = 21 stomata

$\hspace{1em}$Total stomata in both squares: 23 + 21 = 44 stomata

$\hspace{1em}$Total area of both squares: 0.5 $\ce{mm^2}$ + 0.5 $\ce{mm^2}$ = 1.0 $\ce{mm^2}$

$\hspace{1em}$Stomatal density = 44 stomata  1 $\ce{mm^2}$

Therefore, 44 stomata per 1$\ce{mm^2}$ is the most accurate value, making **Choice D** correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because this is the number of stomata in square I, which has an area of 0.5 $\ce{mm^2}$, not 1 $\ce{mm^2}$.

**Choice B** is incorrect because 64 stomata per $\ce{mm^2}$ is too high for the stomatal density in boxes I and II.

**Choice C** is incorrect because 28 stomata per $\ce{mm^2}$ is too low for the stomatal density in boxes I and II.
