    1. Describe, using the data provided, the relationship between temperature and the rate of water uptake.    [1 mark]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
    $\ce{ -> }$ to the left **OR** towards the plant **OR** away from the beaker        [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    As transpiration occurs, the bubble will move **towards the plant** ($\checkmark$).

1.
    2. any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ humidity  [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ air movement **OR** wind speed [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ light intensity [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water availability [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ atmospheric pressure [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    One abiotic factor that needs to be kept constant in this experiment is **wind speed** ($\checkmark$).

2.
    1. any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ rate of water uptake increases up to 30 ⁰C **AND** then decreases above 30 ⁰C **OR** rate of water uptake is highest at 30 ⁰C with 2.2 mm min:sup[-1] **AND** rate of water uptake decreases at 35 ⁰C and 40 ⁰C with 2.0 and 1.2 mm min:sup[-1] respectively         [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Rate of water uptake **increases from** 1.6 mm min:sup[-1] at **20 ⁰C** to 2.2 mm min:sup[-1] at **30 ⁰C** and **then decreases** to 1.2 mm min:sup[-1] at **40 ⁰C** ($\checkmark$).

2.
    2. any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ water uptake is lower at low temperatures because water evaporates more slowly at low temperatures **OR** because molecules have less energy at low temperatures    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water uptake increases at higher temperatures because transpiration is faster at higher temperatures **OR** water evaporates more quickly at higher temperatures    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ stomata close at higher temperatures to reduce water loss **OR** reduced transpiration at 35 ⁰C to conserve water in the plant **OR** reduced transpiration at 35 ⁰C because stomata are closed    [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Water uptake increases at higher temperatures because **evaporation is faster at higher temperatures** ($\checkmark$). There is reduced transpiration at 35 ⁰C because **stomata close to reduce water loss** ($\checkmark$) to conserve water.

2.
    3.  any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ Greater stomatal closure at 40 ⁰C to conserve water     [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    At 40 ⁰C **stomata are closed** ($\checkmark$) to conserve water.
