## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. ***both name and adaptation need to be correct to gain each mark***:

    $\ce{ -> }$ P: waxy cuticle forms a water-resistant **OR** a protective layer to reduce water loss **OR** protect the leaf from pathogens **OR** physical damage **OR** UV radiation **OR** any other suitable example    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ Q: guard cells which regulate opening and closing of stomata **OR** control gas exchange **OR** water loss     [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Structure P is the **waxy cuticle** which forms a **water-resistant** barrier to **reduce water loss** from the leaf ($\checkmark$). Q points to **guard cells**, which **regulate** the **opening and closing** of the **stomata** and thus help **control gas exchange** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **three** of the following (must include at least **one** statement for water entering and at least **one** for water leaving to receive full marks):

    **water movement into Q:** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ becomes turgid    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ bow outwards **OR** curve outwards due to increased internal pressure [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ stomata open         [1 mark]

    **water movement out of Q:** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ becomes flaccid         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ collapses inwards **OR** shrinks inwards due to decreased internal pressure    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ stomata close    [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    When water enters the guard cells by osmosis, they become **turgid** ($\checkmark$). This results in them **bending outwards** due to **increased internal pressure** ($\checkmark$), **opening** the **stomata** (√). When the guard cells lose water, they become **flaccid** ($\checkmark$), resulting in the **stomata closing** ($\checkmark$).

3. 1. any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ xylem vessels carry water from the roots to the mesophyll cells **OR** leaves     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ to replace water loss from evaporation **OR** transpiration        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ negative pressure gradient **OR** transpirational pull draws water up the xylem vessels     [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    **Xylem vessels** carry **water** from the **roots** to the **mesophyll cells** ($\checkmark$). As **water** molecules **evaporate** from the leaves, they create a **negative pressure gradient** within the xylem, which **pulls up more water** from the roots ($\checkmark$) to **replace that lost** through the stomata ($\checkmark$).

    2. the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ reduces **OR** decreases **OR** slows down            [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ because the water potential **OR** concentration gradient between leaf and air is reduced **OR** less steep     [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    High environmental humidity **reduces** the **rate** of transpiration ($\checkmark$). This is because the **water potential gradient** between the **inside** of the **leaf** and the **surrounding air** is **decreased**  ($\checkmark$).

4. any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ provides a large surface area for gas exchange        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ air spaces for circulation of gases **OR** maintaining concentration gradient of gases **OR** efficient gas exchange            [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    A benefit to the plant of having spongy mesophyll cells, the loosely packed cells, is a **larger surface area** for **gas exchange** ($\checkmark$) and **more air spaces** for the **circulation of gases** ($\checkmark$).
