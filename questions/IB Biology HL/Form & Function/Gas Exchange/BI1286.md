# BI1286

The graph shows a spirometer trace of a student who took part in a breathing experiment.

The vital capacity of the student is 4100 cm:sup[3].

![BI1286](images/BI1286.png)

What is the tidal volume of the student?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 500 cm:sup[3]

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 800 cm:sup[3]

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 2800 cm:sup[3]

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 3600 cm:sup[3]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because the tidal volume (TV) can be seen as the central wave that is constant throughout the graph.

TV = VC $-$ IRV $-$ ERV \
$\hspace{1.5em}$ = 4100 cm:sup[3] $-$ 2800 cm:sup[3] $-$ 800 cm:sup[3] \
$\hspace{1.5em}$ = 500 cm:sup[3]

**Choice B** 800 cm:sup[3] is the expiratory reserve volume (ERV) which can be obtained by finding the difference between the level after normal exhalation at rest and the maximum expiratory level.

**Choice C** 2800 cm:sup[3] is the inspiratory reserve volume (IRV) which can be obtained by finding the difference between the level after normal inhalation at rest and the maximum inspiratory level.

**Choice D** 3600cm:sup[3] is the sum of the expiratory reserve volume and the inspiratory reserve volume.

**Choices B, C** and **D** are all incorrect.
