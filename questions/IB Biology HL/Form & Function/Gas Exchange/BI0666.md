# BI0666

Which of the following substances typically moves either in or out of a leaf\'s inner air space via stomata?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ Water
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Oxygen
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Mineral ions

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ III only
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Water diffuses out of stomata from the inner air space of leaves during transpiration (**Statement I**). Because oxygen is produced during photosynthesis, its concentration is generally higher in the inner air space than the atmosphere which means it diffuses out through the stomata (**Statement II**). Mineral ions (**Statement III**) enter plants through root cells, not through the stomata.
