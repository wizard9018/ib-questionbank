# BI1587

The micrograph shows the stomata on the underside of a leaf.

![BI1587](images/BI1587.png)
[Source: Adapted from Thongdumhyu, P. (n.d.). *Leaf Epidermis Stomata under microscope*. Dreamstime.com. Retrieved April 26, 2024, from https://www.dreamstime.com/close-up-plant-epidermis-stomata-leaf-under-microscope-image169274602. Royalty free image]

Which of the following describes the most typical state of the leaf in the micrograph?

| | Guard Cells | Time | Transpiration Rate |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Turgid | Day | High |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Turgid | Night | Low |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Flaccid | Day | Low |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Flaccid | Night | High |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because the micrograph shows that the stomata are open. This is accomplished when the guard cells are turgid. The opening of the stomata allows gas exchange, which indicates that it is daytime and photosynthesis is taking place. A consequence of both photosynthesis and the opening of the stomata is the loss of water, which results in a higher rate of transpiration.

**Choice B** is incorrect because the open stomata indicate that this micrograph was taken during the day and the rate of transpiration would be high, rather than low. **Choice C** is incorrect because the guard cells are turgid to allow the stomata to open, not flaccid, and the transpiration rate would be high instead of low. **Choice D** is incorrect because the guard cells would not be flaccid and it would not be night.
