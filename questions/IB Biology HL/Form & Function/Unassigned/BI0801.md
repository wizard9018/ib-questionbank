2. Any **one** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ large diameter vessels    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ tissue in the vascular bundles closer to the centre of the stem    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ thick lignified walls **OR** thick wall    [1 mark]
    [1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Xylem vessels are located in the vascular bundles and have a **large diameter** ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ walls are thickened with lignin to withstand pressure **OR** to waterproof them    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ vessels are hollow **OR** vessels have no cytoplasm to give space for the water column OR no obstruction to flow of water and minerals [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ large diameter **OR** wide lumen, for movement of water and minerals **OR** to reduce resistance to flow of water and minerals        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ pits in the walls of the vessels allow water to move between the vessels [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ end walls broken down **OR** largely broken down [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Xylem vessels are non-living and have **no cytoplasm** ($\checkmark$) which allows space for movement of water and minerals. The walls of xylem vessels are **thickened with lignin** ($\checkmark$) which allows them to withstand pressure.
