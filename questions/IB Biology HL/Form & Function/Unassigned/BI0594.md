3. any **two** of the following (answers must contain named example and function for full marks):

 ***Collagen***

 $\ce{ -> }$ fibrous protein that provides structural support in connective tissues **OR** resists stretching **OR** supports tissue integrity [1 mark]
 $\ce{ -> }$ three polypeptide chains wound into a triple helix, giving it high tensile strength [1 mark]
 $\ce{ -> }$ glycine every third residue allows chains to pack tightly [1 mark]
 $\ce{ -> }$ hydrogen bonds between chains **OR** covalent cross-links between molecules increase stability and strength [1 mark]

 ***Insulin***

 $\ce{ -> }$ globular protein hormone involved in the regulation of blood glucose [1 mark]
 $\ce{ -> }$ two polypeptide chains **OR** α and β chains giving quaternary structure [1 mark]
 $\ce{ -> }$ specific shape allows insulin to bind precisely to insulin receptors on target cell membranes [1 mark]

 [2 marks]
 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Collagen is a **fibrous protein** that provides **structural support** in **connective tissue** ($\checkmark$). Its **three polypeptide chains** are wound into a **triple helix**, which is reinforced by **hydrogen bonds**, giving it **high tensile strength** ($\checkmark$) and making it **resistant to stretching** ($\checkmark$).

4.
 1. any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ extreme temperatures can lead to denaturation [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ denaturation is the change in the 3-dimensional shape **OR** tertiary structure **OR** secondary **OR** quaternary structure of a protein [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ high temperatures break hydrogen bonds [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ protein can no longer function [1 mark]

 [3 marks]
 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Temperatures which are too high can lead to a protein becoming **denatured** ($\checkmark$), which causes a change in the **3-dimensional shape** of the protein ($\checkmark$). If the shape of a protein is changed, it may no longer be able to carry out its **function** ($\checkmark$).

 2. $\ce{ -> }$ pH [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ other factor that disrupts hydrogen bonding such as salts or ethanol [1 mark]

 [1 mark]
 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
**pH** ($\checkmark$) levels outside the optimum range can lead to a protein becoming denatured.
