# BI0787

**Maximum mark: 10**

A group of students were asked to design an experiment to test the effect of the number of leaves on transpiration rates in plant shoots.

Students took cuttings of shoots with different numbers of leaves from the same plant and placed the stems in a simplified potometer. A potometer is a piece of apparatus used to measure the rate of water uptake by a plant.

Each of the stems was placed by a window, and the water level was recorded every day for 3 days.

![BI0787A](images/BI0787A.png)

The results are shown in the table.

![BI0787B](images/BI0787B.png)

1. State clearly which is the dependent variable and independent variable in this investigation. [2 marks]

2. Identify the two days between which the plant shoots lost the greatest volume of water. Suggest a reason for this. [2 marks]

3. Calculate, as a percentage, the total water lost from the plant shoot with two leaves by day 3. [1 mark]

4.
 1. Suggest a reason for the similarity in results between the plant shoot with two leaves and three leaves. [1 mark]

 2. Based on the results of the plant shoot with one leaf and assuming all other factors were controlled, predict the volume of water left in the graduated cylinder on day 2 for a plant shoot with 5 leaves. [1 mark]

5.
 1. Suggest a way in which the students could improve the reliability of their experiment. [1 mark]

 2. Evaluate the use of the students' methodology in testing the effect of the number of leaves on the transpiration rate of plant shoots. [2 marks]

1.

$\hspace{1em}$ $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
$\hspace{1em}$ The independent variable is **the number of leaves** ($\checkmark$) on the plant shoot and the dependent variable is the **volume of water in the graduated cylinder** which is measured in **cm:sup[3]** ($\checkmark$).

2.

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ day 2 - 3 [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ temperature of the room **OR** environment, was higher **OR** more sunlight **OR** humidity was lower **OR** lights were left on for longer in the lab **OR** window was opened and the wind increased [1 mark]
[2 marks]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
$\hspace{1em}$ The greatest loss of water was seen between **day 2 and 3** ($\checkmark$). This may have been caused by an **increase** in the surrounding **environmental temperature** ($\checkmark$) or more sunlight from the window.
