    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The structure of this cell is adapted to its function because it contains a large amount of **rough endoplasmic reticulum**. The **ribosomes** on the rough endoplasmic reticulum are the site of the **synthesis** of the **polypeptides** ($\checkmark$) and these **polypeptides** are then **folded and modified into functional proteins** in the **lumen** of the **rough endoplasmic reticulum** ($\checkmark$).
