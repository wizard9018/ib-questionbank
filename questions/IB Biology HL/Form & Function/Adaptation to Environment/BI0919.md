# BI0919

What best defines a habitat?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The role an organism has within its ecosystem

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The community of organisms and their environment

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The place an organism lives

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ A group of organisms living in the same place

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because the habitat is a place a community, population, species, or organism lives. **Choice A** refers to an organism's niche, **Choice B** refers to the ecosystem, and **Choice D** refers to a community.
