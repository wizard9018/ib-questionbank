# BI0920

Which of the following is not a factor required for coral reef formation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Low pH

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Shallow enough water

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Temperatures between 20 :sup[o]C and 30 :sup[o]C

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ High enough salinity

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because low pH is not a factor corals need for reef formation, in fact, low pH is detrimental to coral growth. Shallow enough water (**Choice B**) is important to allow enough light to reach the photosynthetic Zooxanthellae found in the coral polyps. Temperatures between 20 :sup[o]C and 30 :sup[o]C (**Choice C**) are important as corals can only survive within this narrow range. High enough salinity (**Choice D**) is important for various reasons, including the maintenance of an osmotic balance for proper cellular function.
