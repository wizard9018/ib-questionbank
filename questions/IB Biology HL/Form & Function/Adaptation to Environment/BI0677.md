2. Outline the adaptations of the cactus which enables it to survive in extremely dry environmental conditions.          [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
To adapt to life in extremely dry environments, xerophytes have **small leaves** ($\checkmark$), which **reduces the surface area** for water loss by evaporation ($\checkmark$). Stomata are **few** ($\checkmark$), located in **recessed pits** ($\checkmark$) and **open at night** when temperatures are cooler ($\checkmark$).  These adaptations result in a decrease in water loss through stomata. Xerophytes also have **long roots** ($\checkmark$) enabling them to absorb water located at a greater distance.
