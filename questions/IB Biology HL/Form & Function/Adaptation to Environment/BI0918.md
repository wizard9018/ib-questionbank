# BI0918

Which of the following would not be considered an abiotic factor?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Sunlight

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Disease

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Precipitation

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ pH

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because disease is a living factor and is, therefore, a biotic factor, not an abiotic one. Sunlight (**Choice A**), precipitation (**Choice C**), and pH (**Choice D**) are all non-living factors and are therefore abiotic.
