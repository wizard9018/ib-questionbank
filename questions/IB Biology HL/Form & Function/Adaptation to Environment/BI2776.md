# BI2776

The image shows a line transect being used on a rocky seashore.

![BI2776](images/BI2776.png)
[Source: Adapted from ThalassLib (2019, June 6). *Intertidal transect sampling 02.jpg*. Wikimedia. Retrieved April 29, 2025, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Intertidal_transect_sampling_02.jpg. Copyright under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain]

Which abiotic factor could this transect be used to investigate?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The effect of herbivory on the distribution of seaweed species.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ The effect of predation on the distribution of crab species.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ The effect of wave action on the distribution of crab species.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ The effect of wave action on the distribution of seaweed species.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because wave action is an abiotic factor that varies along a rocky seashore and can influence the distribution of seaweed species. Seaweeds are stationary organisms, making them suitable for surveying using quadrats placed at intervals along a line transect.

**Choice A** is incorrect because although herbivory does affect the distribution of seaweed species, it is a biotic (living) factor, not an abiotic factor.

**Choice B** is incorrect because predation is a biotic factor, and crabs are mobile, so they cannot be surveyed with a line transect.

**Choice C** is incorrect because while wave action is an abiotic factor, crabs are mobile, so it is not possible to collect data about their distribution with a quadrat.
