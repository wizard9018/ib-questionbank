# BI0922

Which of the following factors create biomes?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Annual Precipitation |
    | II. | Air Temperature |
    | III. | Keystone species |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Biomes are primarily determined by climatic and geographic conditions. **Choice C** is correct because air temperature (**Statement II**) and annual precipitation (**Statement I**) are both factors involved in biome formation.

Keystone species (**Statement III**) are species that have a disproportionately large impact on their ecosystem relative to their abundance. While they play a crucial role in shaping and maintaining the structure of specific ecosystems, they are not a direct factor in creating biomes. **Choices A**, **B** and **D** are therefore incorrect.
