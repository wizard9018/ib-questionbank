# BI0949

What is a common limiting factor for plant growth in arid (dry) environments?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ High humidity

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Excessive rainfall

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Soil compaction

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Low light intensity

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
In arid environments, water availability is often a primary limiting factor. However, soil compaction (**Choice C**) can exacerbate this limitation by reducing water infiltration and root penetration. High humidity (**Choice A**), excessive rainfall (**Choice B**), and low light intensity (**Choice D**) are less likely to be limiting factors in arid environments.
