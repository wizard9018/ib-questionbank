# BI0924

**Maximum mark: 7**

The kapok tree, pictured below, is found in the tropical rainforests of Central and South America. It has buttress roots that extend above the soil in order for it to grow tall.

1.
    1. Outline the climatic conditions which are typical of tropical rainforests.               [2 marks]

    2. For a named organism other than the kapok tree, describe the adaptations it has to live in tropical rainforests.         [2 marks]

    3. Tropical rainforests are an example of a biome. Define the term biome.     [1 mark]

2. Explain the need for the kapok tree to grow tall and fast.     [2 marks]

1.  Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ high temperature **OR** high insolation        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ high precipitation **OR** rainfall             [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ high humidity                             [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Tropical rainforests are characterised by **high temperatures** ($\checkmark$) and **high** levels of **precipitation** ($\checkmark$), which makes them very **humid** ($\checkmark$).

2.

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ high levels of competition (in tropical rainforest biome) **OR** high plant density         [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ **AND SO** the kapok tree must grow quickly to reach sunlight (in order to photosynthesise)    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ growing tall allows it to reach the upper canopy     [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ **WHICH** provides a greater area for pollination and seed dispersal, enhancing reproductive success                                              [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ reach a more physically stable and resilient state faster [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ **IN ORDER TO**  withstand natural disturbances such as tree falls     [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ can elevate its foliage beyond the reach of some herbivores     [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ **WHICH** reduces the risk of herbivory                               [1 mark]
[2 marks]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
$\hspace{1em}$ In tropical rainforests, there is a **high density of plants** ($\checkmark$), so to **reach sunlight** ($\checkmark$), the kapok tree must grow tall, and quickly, to photosynthesise.
