## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ primary production is dependent on light **AND** water **OR** moisture available [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ precipitation is a direct source of water for plants, and so determines type **OR** amount of vegetation in a biome        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ soil moisture content influences soil chemistry **OR** microbial activity **OR** soil fertility     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ amount and timing of precipitation influence local climate conditions including temperature     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ areas with high precipitation tend to have more stable and moderate temperatures    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ areas with low precipitation often experience more extreme temperatures and aridity         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ availability of water from precipitation supports diverse ecosystems **OR** high levels of biodiversity  [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Precipitation is a **direct source** of **water** for plants, determining the **type** and **amount** of **vegetation** in a biome ($\checkmark$). It influences **soil moisture content**, which affects **soil fertility** ($\checkmark$) and, therefore, the types of plants that can grow there. Areas with **high precipitation**, such as **rainforests**, tend to have **more stable** and **moderate temperatures**  ($\checkmark$). Areas with **low precipitation**, such as **deserts**, often experience more extreme temperatures and aridity ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ during summer base of pyramid **OR** primary producer level would be wider **OR** represent more energy **THAN** winter pyramid    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ during summer the higher trophic levels **OR** consumer trophic levels would be broader **OR** represent more energy **THAN** those in winter        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ summer pyramid is likely to include more trophic levels **THAN** winter pyramid        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ winter pyramid will have much steeper **OR** narrower shape **THAN** summer pyramid    [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    In Aklavik, Canada, the **summer** pyramid of energy would have a **wider base** ($\checkmark$) than the winter pyramid. The **winter** pyramid would be much **steeper and narrower**  ($\checkmark$), with **fewer trophic levels** supported than in the summer($\checkmark$).
