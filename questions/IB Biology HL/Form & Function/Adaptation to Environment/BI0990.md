# BI0990

Which of the following biomes is best characterised by high temperatures, high rainfall, and high humidity?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Tropical rainforests

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Tundra

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Grasslands

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Temperate forests

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because tropical rainforests are characterised by high temperatures, high rainfall and high humidity. **Choice B** is incorrect because tundra would have low temperatures and low humidity. **Choice C** is incorrect because grasslands would have low humidity, and **Choice D** is incorrect because temperate forests would have lower temperatures and low humidity.
