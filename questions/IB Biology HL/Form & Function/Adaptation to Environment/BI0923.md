# BI0923

**Maximum mark: 8**

Marram grass, pictured below, is a plant species often found growing on sand dunes.

![BI0923](images/BI0923.png)

1.
    1. Distinguish between abiotic and biotic factors.         [1 mark]

    2. Suggest adaptations Marram grass could have in order to grow on sand dunes.     [2 marks]

    3. For a named abiotic variable, outline how the range of tolerance for Marram grass could be measured in its natural habitat.             [3 marks]

2. Describe an adaptation that allows a named organism to survive in desert habitats.  [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1.
    1.
    $\ce{ -> }$ abiotic factors are non-living components of an ecosystem **OR** non-living factors which affect organisms **WHEREAS** biotic factors are living components of an ecosystem **OR** living factors which affect organisms                    [1 mark]
[1 mark]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Abiotic factors are the **non-living components** of an ecosystem, whereas **biotic** factors are the **living components** of an ecosystem ($\checkmark$).

1.
    2. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ dense root system **OR** long **OR** creeping rhizomes to maximise water uptake **OR** anchor plant in the sand      [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ deep roots to tap into deeper water sources [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ narrow leaves **OR** thick cuticles to reduce transpiration        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ stomata close if roots are dry **OR** during periods of drought        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ small hairs **OR** trichomes around stomata to reduce transpiration rate **OR** to build up humidity around stomata OR to reduce water concentration difference inside and outside the leaf     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ can tolerate high salinity **OR** salt spray **OR** immersion in salt water     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ stiff stems help it withstand strong winds     [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Marram grass could have a **dense root system** which helps maximise water absorption from the surface of the sand after it rains ($\checkmark$). Their leaves are also typically **narrow** to help reduce the rate of transpiration ($\checkmark$).

1.
    3. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ temperature range **OR** pH range **OR** salinity **OR** water availability **OR** light intensity **OR** other named abiotic variable    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ using a transect **OR** line transect **OR** belt transect     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ measurements of the abiotic variable taken at regular intervals using an appropriate sensor   [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ quadrat **OR** measuring stick **OR** measuring tape placed at regular intervals along the transect to count the number of individuals      [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ measure characteristics of Marram grass, e.g. height, density or stem thickness     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ analyse data to identify trends between changes in the grass and the abiotic factor     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ repeat measurements     [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    In order to measure the effect of **pH** ($\checkmark$) on the distribution of Marram grass, you could use a **line transect** ($\checkmark$). A **pH probe** ($\checkmark$) would be used to measure the pH at regular intervals along the line transect. A metre stick could then be used at regular intervals to determine whether Marram grass is present or not.

2.

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ named organisms, e.g. Fennec fox    [1 mark]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\ce{ -> }$ description of suitable adaptation, e.g. large vascular ears help to dissipate heat    [1 mark]
[2 marks]

$\hspace{1em}$ $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
$\hspace{1em}$ The **Fennec fox** ($\checkmark$) is adapted to live in desert habitats because it has **large vascular ears**, which help to dissipate heat and keep it cool ($\checkmark$).
