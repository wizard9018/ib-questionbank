# BI0925

**Maximum mark: 16**

1. Define the terms species, population, and community.    [3 marks]

2. Explain the conditions required for coral reef formation. [6 marks]

3. Using three named organisms, describe the adaptations they have to different
environments.     [7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ a species is a group of organisms capable of interbreeding to produce fertile offspring         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ a population is a group of individuals of the same species occupying the same area        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ a community is all the populations of different species living in the same geographical area       [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    A species is often defined as a **group of organisms** that are capable of **interbreeding to produce fertile offspring** ($\checkmark$). A population is a group of individuals of the **same species** which occupy the **same geographical area** ($\checkmark$). A community is all the **populations of different species** living in the **same geographical area** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **six** of the following (maximum three marks for named conditions):

    $\ce{ -> }$ suitable water temperature **ALLOWS** symbiotic algae to grow **OR** increased temperatures cause the algae to produce substances that are toxic to the coral so corals become stressed and expel zooxanthellae **OR** symbiotic algae, causing bleaching of the coral and making them more vulnerable to starvation and disease    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ clearwater **OR** water clarity **ALLOWS** sunlight through for photosynthesis     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ shallow water **OR** suitable water depth **ALLOWS** sunlight to reach symbiotic algae         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ suitable pH **ALLOWS** algae to survive **OR** skeleton to form **OR** as the pH drops, the availability of carbonate ions in seawater decreases, making it more challenging for corals to build their calcium carbonate skeletons **OR** calcium carbonate dissolves in acidic solutions, so the lowered pH of the water erodes the skeleton, removing its support and protection, leading to death **OR** increased acidity can cause coral bleaching, where corals expel the zooxanthellae    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ suitable salinity **OR** saltwater **ALLOWS** coral to survive and grow OR if the salinity is too high or too low, it can disrupt the osmotic balance, leading to dehydration or water overload within the coral cells **OR** the concentration of calcium and carbonate ions in the water is influenced by salinity **OR** the correct salinity plays a role in the chemical cues that attract larvae to appropriate settlement areas on the reef        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ coral larvae need a hard substrate on which they can attach and begin their growth        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ gentle currents help to deliver nutrients and oxygen to the corals while flushing away sediment and waste        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ adequate nutrients **BECAUSE** although corals have a symbiotic relationship with zooxanthellae, they also depend on capturing plankton and organic matter from the water for additional nutrition  [1 mark]
    [6 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Corals require **suitable temperatures**, which allow the symbiotic algae to **survive** and grow ($\checkmark$). They need to be in relatively **shallow water**, which allows the **sunlight** to reach the symbiotic algae ($\checkmark$). Corals also need **clear water**, which also allows sunlight to reach them so that the symbiotic algae can **photosynthesise** ($\checkmark$). Coral larvae also need a **hard substrate**, such as rock, on which they can **attach** and begin to grow ($\checkmark$).Finally, Corals also need a suitable **salinity and pH**. Salinity directly influences **calcium carbonate** ion concentration ($\checkmark$), and pH can influence the development of the **skeleton** ($\checkmark$).

3. any **seven** of the following (maximum **three marks** for named organisms and where it's found):

    $\ce{ -> }$ 3 named organisms and their correct habitat **OR** environment, e.g. Fennec fox found in the desert                 [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ adaptation and advantage of adaptation for each organism, e.g. large vascular ears to dissipate heat             [1 mark]
    [7 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The **Fennec fox** is adapted to live in **desert** ($\checkmark$) environments because it has **large vascular ears** which allow it to **dissipate heat** ($\checkmark$). It can also use its large ears to **detect small insects**, which are its prey ($\checkmark$). **Marram grass** is adapted to live in **sand dunes** ($\checkmark$) because it has **dense shallow roots** which allow it to **collect water** when it rains ($\checkmark$). It can also **close the stomata** in its leaves to reduce water loss when water is in short supply ($\checkmark$). The **kapok tree** is found in **tropical rainforests** ($\checkmark$) it has **buttress roots** that extend above ground. This can help it to **grow tall** in shallow soil ($\checkmark$).
