# BI1424

**Maximum mark: 6**

The photograph below is of the sea oat (*Unola paniculata*). This plant is found on sand dunes.

2. Describe the importance of the solvent properties of water for transport in plants and humans.            [3 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ wide shallow root system that helps anchor them securely in shifting sands **OR**  to allow rapid absorption of water from sand **OR** to stabilise sand reducing rate of erosion        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ salt tolerance to survive exposure to salt spray from the sea    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ narrow leaves that reduce water loss through transpiration    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ flexible **OR** bendy stems allow sea oats to sway with the wind without breaking    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ long bristle-like structures on seeds that catch the wind to aid seed dispersal    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ some sea oat populations have a symbiotic relationship with nitrogen-fixing bacteria in their root nodules enabling them to survive in nutrient-poor beach soils    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ nodes **OR** rhizomes allow regeneration after a fire **OR** to reproduce asexually to survive being covered by sand    [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The **wide, shallow root** system of sea oats allows them to **anchor themselves** in the shifting sand ($\checkmark$) of the dunes and to **absorb water rapidly** ($\checkmark$). Their **narrow leaves reduce water loss** through **transpiration** ($\checkmark$) and their **flexible stems** allow them to survive the **sea winds without breaking** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **three** of the following (*need **one** example for plants and **one** example for humans to gain maximum marks*):

    $\ce{ -> }$ water molecules are polar **OR** have an uneven distribution of charge so attract other polar molecules **OR** ions through hydrogen bonding **OR** water dissolves hydrophilic substances                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ in cellular transport water allows movement of ions **OR** hydrophilic molecules **OR** polar molecules around the cell            [1 mark]

    ***Plants*** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ xylem **OR** transpiration stream carries minerals **OR** minerals **OR** potassium **OR** calcium **OR** magnesium from roots [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ phloem carries nutrients **OR** sucrose **OR** carbohydrates **OR** glucose **OR** amino acids    [1 mark]

    ***Humans*** \
    $\ce{ -> }$ blood plasma dissolves and transports sodium chloride **OR** glucose **OR** amino acids **OR** wastes **OR** hormones **OR** oxygen **OR** carbon dioxide                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ oxygen dissolution in aqueous lining of lungs allows uptake of oxygen into blood [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ waste products are filtered out at the kidney **OR** wastes are transported in glomerular filtrate **OR** water allows the transport of wastes through the nephron to the bladder for excretion    [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Because **water molecules** are **polar, hydrophilic substances dissolve** in **aqueous solutions** ($\checkmark$). In **plants**, water transports **minerals** up **from** the **roots**, where they have been absorbed from the soil, to where they are needed in the plant ($\checkmark$).  In humans, water acts as the medium for **blood plasma, transporting** essential **nutrients** and **oxygen** around the body ($\checkmark$).
