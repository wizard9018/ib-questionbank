# BI2773

Which description best fits the definition of a habitat?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ The Arctic Circle.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ An ocean.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ A peatland at low altitude.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ A coastline.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because a habitat is the specific place where an organism lives, characterized by particular physical conditions (such as moisture, temperature, and light) and the biological community it belongs to. A peatland at low altitude describes a defined environment with unique ecological conditions, making it a suitable example of a habitat.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the Arctic Circle is a geographical region, not a specific habitat. It encompasses a wide range of ecosystems and conditions, so it does not meet the criteria of a clearly defined habitat.\
**Choices B** and **D** are incorrect because oceans and coastlines are broad and general terms. They refer to large physical environments that contain many different habitats rather than describing one specific set of environmental conditions.
