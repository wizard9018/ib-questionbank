# BI1367

Which of the following is capable of moving through nuclear pores?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | transcription proteins|
    | II. | tRNA |
    | III. | ribosomal subunits |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Nuclear pores control the movement of substances between the nucleus and cytoplasm. Substances required by the nucleus, such as the proteins involved in transcription (**Statement I**), can be transported into the nucleus through nuclear pores. Conversely, substances produced in the nucleus, such as various forms of RNA (**Statement II**) and ribosomal subunits composed of rRNA and ribosomal proteins (**Statement III**), are transported through nuclear pores to the cytoplasm, where they can perform their functions. **Choice D** is correct since all three statements are true.

**Choices A, B**, and **C** are incorrect as they are missing one or more statements.
