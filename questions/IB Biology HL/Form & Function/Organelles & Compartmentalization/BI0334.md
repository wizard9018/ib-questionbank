# BI0334

Which statement about translation only applies to prokaryotes?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Translation occurs at the ribosomes.

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Translation happens immediately after transcription.

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Translation creates an amino acid sequence from the mRNA sequence.

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Translation happens after mRNA processing.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

**Choice B** is correct because in prokaryotes there are no post-transcriptional modifications made to the mRNA and thus mRNA is translated immediately after transcription. **Choices A** and **C** are incorrect as they apply to translation in both eukaryotes and prokaryotes. **Choice D**  is incorrect as it applies to eukaryotes, not prokaryotes. In eukaryotes, mRNA needs to be processed but not in prokaryotes.
