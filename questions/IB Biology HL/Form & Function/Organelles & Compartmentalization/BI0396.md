# BI0396

Which of the following is **not** a function of the Golgi apparatus?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Protein synthesis

- B. $\hspace{1em}$    Protein packaging into vesicles
- C. $\hspace{1em}$    Protein modification
- D. $\hspace{1em}$    Lipid packaging into vesicles

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Ribosomes carry out protein synthesis, not the Golgi apparatus. **Choice A** is correct. **Choices B**, **C** and **D** are incorrect as they are all functions of the Golgi apparatus.
