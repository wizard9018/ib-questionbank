2. any **five** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ enzymes and substrates for a specific process can be kept in close proximity [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ optimal pH **OR** chemical environment can be maintained within organelles [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ reactions can proceed more quickly **OR** efficiently [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ damaging substances can be safely contained [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ organelles can be moved around the cell to meet localised energy **OR** functional demands [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ post-transcriptional modifications **OR** splicing **OR** other suitable example can occur in the nucleus [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ lower chance of non-specific interactions between molecules [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ allows simultaneous and incompatible reactions to occur in the same cell [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ concentration gradients can be maintained across membranes [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ specific targeting of proteins to organelles helps regulate functions [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ increased surface area for membrane-bound reactions [1 mark]

 [5 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Eukaryotes have a compartmentalised cell structure, meaning that **enzymes** and **substrates** for specific processes can be kept in **close proximity** ($\checkmark$), and **optimal pH can be maintained** within certain **organelles** ($\checkmark$), allowing **reactions** to proceed **more efficiently** ($\checkmark$). **Harmful substances** such as hydrolytic enzymes can be **safely contained** ($\checkmark$), and **organelles** such as ribosomes **can be repositioned** within the cytoplasm **as needed** ($\checkmark$). Compartmentalisation also enables processes like pre-mRNA **splicing**, increasing the diversity of polypeptides produced from a single gene ($\checkmark$).

3. any **four** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ ultracentrifuge allows separation of cell components based on size **OR** density [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ heavier **OR** more dense organelles pellet first **OR** lighter **OR** less dense organelles pellet later [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ enabled isolation of individual organelles [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ function of organelles could be studied in isolation [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ helped confirm compartmentalisation of cellular functions [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ made biochemical analysis of specific organelles possible [1 mark]

 [4 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The ultracentrifuge allowed scientists to **separate cell components** based on their **size** and **density** ($\checkmark$). **Heavier organelles** such as **nuclei pellet first**, while **lighter ones** like **ribosomes pellet later** ($\checkmark$). This made it possible to **isolate** individual **organelles for study** ($\checkmark$). As a result, the **functions** of **specific organelles** could be **investigated** in **isolation**, confirming that different organelles carry out **different roles** ($\checkmark$).
