# BI1370

Which of the following is **not** an advantage of cell compartmentalisation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Ability to control the distribution of metabolites and enzymes

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Improved nutrient storage

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Reduced energy costs

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Improved metabolic efficiency

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Compartmentalisation involves organising cell components into distinct and specialised organelles, typically using membranes. Although compartmentalisation has many advantages, maintaining organelles and their membranes to achieve this separation involves considerable energy costs. **Choice C** is correct.

Compartmentalisation allows cells to separate different metabolites and enzymes, ensuring they are in the correct place and concentration, thus ensuring the efficiency of biochemical reactions. **Choice A** is, therefore, an incorrect answer, as the ability to control the distribution of metabolites and enzymes is an advantage. **Choice B** is an incorrect answer since nutrient storage is possible in vacuoles and other organelles. **Choice D** is an incorrect answer since cell compartmentalisation allows cells to prevent incompatible metabolic processes from taking place simultaneously in the same region of the cell.
