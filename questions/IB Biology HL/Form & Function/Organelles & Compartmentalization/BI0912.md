# BI0912

The micrograph below shows part of an animal cell.  Which statement is true about the parts labelled X and Y

![BI0912](images/BI0912.png)
[©Source: Adapted from T.Voekler (August 1986). File: Nucleus of a chloride cell.jpg. Wikimedia Commons. Retrieved July 22nd, 2023, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Nucleus_of_a_chloride_cell.jpg. CC-BY-SA-3.0]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Both X and Y are primarily composed of water and solutes.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Only X has a specialised function, and Y does not.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Only Y is an organelle, and X is not.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Both X and Y are found in prokaryotes.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because Y is the nucleus and is an organelle, whereas X is the cytosol and not an organelle.

**Choice A** is incorrect because only X, the cytosol, is primarily composed of water and solutes, Y, the nucleus, primarily contains the DNA. **Choice B** is incorrect because Y, the nucleus, has a specialised function. **Choice D** is incorrect because Y, the nucleus, is not found in prokaryotes, but both X, the cytosol, and Y, the nucleus, are found in eukaryotes.
