# BI0154

Below is a micrograph of mitochondria with four structures or locations labelled. Which is a correct description of how one of these structures is adapted to its function?

![BI0154](images/BI0154.png)

| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | P       | Projections to increase surface area for the electron transport chain |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Q       | Contains enzymes required by the link reaction and Krebs cycle |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | R       | Large surface area for oxidative phosphorylation  |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | S | Barrier between the inner parts of the mitochondrion and the cytoplasm which creates an ideal space with optimum conditions for cellular respiration |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
P is the outer mitochondrial membrane (**Choice A**). It acts as a barrier between the inner part of the mitochondrion and the cytoplasm which creates an ideal space with optimum conditions for cellular respiration.

Q is the inner mitochondrial membrane (**Choice B**) where the electron transport chain and ATP synthase are located.

R is the cristae, which are projections of the inner mitochondrial membrane which has a large surface area for oxidative phosphorylation to take place.

S is the matrix (**Choice D**) where enzymes required for the Krebs cycle and link reaction are found.
