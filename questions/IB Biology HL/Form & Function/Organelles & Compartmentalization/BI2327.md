# BI2327

Which organelle requires compartmentalisation to concentrate enzymes and prevent damage to the contents of cells?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Lysosome

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Chloroplast

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Mitochondrion

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Contractile vacuole

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because lysosomes contain digestive enzymes that would digest the cell if they were not membrane-bound.

**Choice B** is incorrect because the membrane of a chloroplast maintains a high concentration of enzymes for the reactions of photosynthesis inside the chloroplast but these enzymes would not digest the cell.

**Choice C** is incorrect because the membrane of a mitochondrion maintains a high concentration of enzymes for the reactions of respiration but these enzymes would not digest the cell.

**Choice D** is incorrect because a contractile vacuole collects and removes excess water to maintain osmolarity, it does not contain digestive enzymes.
