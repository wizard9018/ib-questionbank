# BI0913

Which of the following processes occurs in prokaryotic cells but not eukaryotic cells?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Transcription takes place in the cytoplasm

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Post-transcriptional modification of mRNA

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Regulation of transcription

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Translation of mRNA into a polypeptide chain

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because there is no compartmentalisation in prokaryotes, hence transcription and translation both take place in the cytoplasm.

**Choice B** is incorrect because post-transcriptional modification of mRNA does not occur in prokaryotes, but does occur in the nucleus of eukaryotic cells. **Choice C** and **D** are incorrect because the regulation of transcription and translation of mRNA into a polypeptide chain occurs in both prokaryotes and eukaryotes.
