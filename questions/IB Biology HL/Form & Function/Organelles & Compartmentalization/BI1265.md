# BI1265

Which of these is not an adaptation of chloroplasts for photosynthesis?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Small volumes of fluid inside thylakoids

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Large surface area of thylakoids

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Contain own DNA for making enzymes for photosynthesis

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Infoldings of inner membranes

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because chloroplasts do not have infoldings, it is the mitochondria that have these.

**Choice A** is incorrect because chloroplasts do have small volumes of liquids inside thylakoids. **Choice B** is incorrect because thylakoids in chloroplasts do have a large surface area. **Choice C** is incorrect because chloroplasts do contain their own DNA for making enzymes needed for photosynthesis.
