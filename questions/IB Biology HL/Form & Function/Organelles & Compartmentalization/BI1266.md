# BI1266

Which of these is not a benefit of the nuclear membrane?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Pores allow mRNA to move through between transcription and translation

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Regularly allow transcription factors to move out of the nucleus

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Able to dissociate into vesicles during mitosis

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Provides compartmentalisation to separate transcription from translation

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Transcription factors regularly move into the nucleus, not out of it; **Choice B** is therefore **NOT** a benefit of the nuclear membrane and is the correct answer.

**Choice A** is a benefit and so an incorrect answer. Nuclear pores allow mRNA to exit the nucleus after transcription so it can be translated in the cytoplasm.

**Choice C** is a benefit and so an incorrect answer. During mitosis, the nuclear membrane breaks down into vesicles, facilitating chromosome segregation and later reassembly.

**Choice D** is also a benefit, and so is an incorrect answer. The nuclear membrane allows transcription to occur in a controlled compartment, separated from translation in the cytoplasm, which is essential for gene regulation in eukaryotes.
