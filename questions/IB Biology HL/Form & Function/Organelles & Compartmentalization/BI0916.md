# BI0916

What is the name of the organelle on the surface of the rough endoplasmic reticulum that is needed to synthesise polypeptide chains?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Nucleus

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Vesicle

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Ribosome

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Mitochondrion

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because ribosomes are organelles found on the surface of the rough endoplasmic reticulum and are needed to synthesise polypeptide chains.

The nucleus (**Choice A**) contains DNA, some of which codes for the polypeptides made by the ribosomes. The role of vesicles (**Choice B**) is to store substances and to transport substances, including proteins, between cellular compartments. The mitochondria (**Choice D**) are the organelles responsible for aerobic respiration.
