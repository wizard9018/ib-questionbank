# BI0914

Which statement applies to a lysosome?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ It usually fuses with a vacuole in the cytoplasm.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ It participates in anabolic reactions of cellular components.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ It forms from vesicles budding from the nucleus.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ It is an organelle which lacks a membrane and contains enzymes.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because a lysosome usually fuses with phagocytic vacuoles containing pathogens or cellular debris, or other vacuoles in the cytoplasm. The enzymes are then released from the lysosome into the vacuole to digest the contents.

**Choice B** is incorrect because lysosomes contain digestive enzymes that break down cellular components, which involve catabolic reactions, not anabolic reactions. **Choice C** is incorrect because lysosomes form from vesicles budding off of the Golgi apparatus. **Choice D** is incorrect because lysosomes are membrane-bound organelles.
