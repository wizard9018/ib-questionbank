# BI1279

**Maximum mark: 7**

The diagram shows the basic structure of a chloroplast.

![BI1279](images/BI1279.png)

1. Identify the structures labelled W and Y. [1 mark]

2. Identify the locations of (i) the increased proton concentration for chemiosmosis, (ii) the photosystems and (iii) the reactions of the light-independent stage. [1 mark]

3. Explain why chloroplasts are green. [1 mark]

4. Outline the adaptations of chloroplasts for their function. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. **BOTH** are needed:

 $\ce{ -> }$ W is the thylakoid membrane **OR** lamella **AND** Y is the stroma [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 W is the **thylakoid membrane, and Y is the stroma** ($\checkmark$).

2. All **three** answers required:

 $\ce{ -> }$ (i) the build-up of the proton concentration occurs in Z **AND** (ii) photosystems are located on the membrane of W **AND** (iii) reactions of the light-independent stage happen in Y [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 (i) The increased proton concentration for chemiosmosis occurs inside the **grana or in the intermembrane space (Z)**; (ii) the location of the photosystems is the **thylakoid membranes (W)**; (iii) the location of the independent stage of photosynthesis is the **stroma (Y)** ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ chloroplasts contain chlorophyll **WHICH** is green [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ chloroplasts contain chlorophyll which reflects green light **OR** absorbs colours other than green [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Chloroplasts contain pigments for absorbing light and one of the pigments is **chlorophyll which reflects green light** ($\checkmark$).

4. Any **four** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ outer membrane provides protection [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ inner membrane for regulation of the transport of molecules in and out [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ chloroplasts contain chlorophyll **OR** chloroplasts contain pigments that can absorb light and convert it to chemical energy [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ many grana provide large surface area for light absorption **OR** thylakoid membranes provide large surface area for light absorption **OR** large surface area for electron transport chain **OR** reactions of the light-dependent stage [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ own DNA and ribosomes allow chloroplasts to synthesise their proteins **OR** enzymes [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ compact arrangement of grana allows optimal light absorption in space provided [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ narrow intermembrane space of grana **OR** thylakoids for rapid formation of concentration gradient of protons [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ stroma contains enzymes that catalyse the reactions of the light-independent stage [1 mark]
 [4 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Chloroplasts **contain pigments that can absorb light** ($\checkmark$) and convert it to chemical energy. **Thylakoid** membranes provide a **large surface area** for **light absorption** ($\checkmark$). Chloroplasts have their **own DNA** and **ribosomes** to synthesise their **proteins**, such as **enzymes** needed for photosynthesis ($\checkmark$). A **narrow intermembrane space** between the thylakoid membranes means a **high concentration of protons** can rapidly build up for chemiosmosis ($\checkmark$).
