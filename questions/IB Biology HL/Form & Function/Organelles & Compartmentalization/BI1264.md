# BI1264

Which of these is **not** an adaptation of mitochondria for the production of ATP?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Large surface area of cristae

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Small volumes of liquid inside thylakoids

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Small volume of intermembrane space

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Enzymes compartmentalised in matrix

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because thylakoids are found in chloroplasts, not mitochondria. **Choice A** is incorrect because the large surface area of cristae gives a large surface area for the electron transport chain. **Choice C** is incorrect because mitochondria do have a small volume of intermembrane space, which allows a concentration gradient to be established. **Choice D** is incorrect because enzymes are compartmentalised in the matrix for the reactions of the Krebs cycle.
