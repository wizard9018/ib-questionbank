# BI1366

Which of the following would be considered an organelle?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ cell wall

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ vesicles

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ cytoskeleton

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ cytoplasm

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Organelles are discrete parts of a cell specialised to carry out particular functions. **Choice B** is correct because vesicles are membrane-bound structures that have various roles within the cell, such as transporting proteins.

**Choices A, C,** and **D** are incorrect as they are not considered organelles due to their non-discrete nature and non-specific roles within cells. With the exception of the cytoplasm (**Choice D**), they are also not enclosed within membranes, which is a typical feature of most organelles.
