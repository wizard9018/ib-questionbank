# BI0999

Which of the following is true of centrifugation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ It separates substances by density

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ It separates substances by charge

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ It separates substances by chemical composition

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ It separates substances by colour

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Centrifugation is a technique that primarily separates substances based on their density. When a mixture is centrifuged, particles or components with higher density sediment to the bottom of the tube. **Choice A** is, therefore, correct.

Centrifugation does not separate substances based on their charge. Electrophoresis is a technique that separates particles or molecules based on their charge. **Choice B** is, therefore, incorrect.
Centrifugation does not directly separate substances based on their chemical composition. Other techniques like chromatography may be more suitable for separating substances by chemical composition. **Choice C** is, therefore, incorrect.
Centrifugation does not separate substances based on their colour. Colour differences might be observed during centrifugation, but they are not the basis for separation. **Choice D** is, therefore, incorrect.
