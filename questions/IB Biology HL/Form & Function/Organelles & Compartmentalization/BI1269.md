2. Any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have a double membrane **OR** inner and outer membrane [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have a large surface area [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have electron transport chains **OR** proteins that facilitate electron transport [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have their own DNA **OR** circular DNA molecules [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have 70S ribosomes **OR** can synthesise their own proteins [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have small intermembrane spaces where a concentration gradient can be established [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** have enzymes **OR** ATP synthases [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Chloroplasts and mitochondria both have a **double membrane** ($\checkmark$). Chloroplasts and mitochondria both have **70s ribosomes** ($\checkmark$) and their **own, circular, DNA** ($\checkmark$).

3. **Three** from (at least one function for each organelle):

 $\ce{ -> }$ rough endoplasmic reticulum is the location of protein synthesis of proteins that will be released from the cell **OR** to be secreted [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ rough endoplasmic reticulum is the location of proteins for transport within the cell [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ rough endoplasmic is where fixed ribosomes attach to [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ ribosomes on rough endoplasmic reticulum synthesise polypeptide chains [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ polypeptide chains enter the lumen of the rough endoplasmic reticulum **OR** interior of the rough endoplasmic reticulum where they are folded to form proteins [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ rough endoplasmic reticulum is involved with modification of proteins OR identification of misfolded **OR** improperly assembled proteins [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ rough endoplasmic reticulum packages proteins into transport vesicles to be sent to the Golgi apparatus [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ Golgi apparatus processes **OR** modifies proteins from the rough endoplasmic reticulum [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ Golgi apparatus processes **OR** modifies proteins for secretion [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ Golgi apparatus produces vesicles for secretion of proteins [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ Golgi apparatus has a role in the formation of lysosomes [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The rough endoplasmic reticulum produces **proteins to be secreted** ($\checkmark$) and **proteins for transport in the cells** ($\checkmark$). The Golgi apparatus **processes proteins for secretion** ($\checkmark$).

4. Answer must include **one** point about the formation and **one** point about the function:

 *Formation:* \
 $\ce{ -> }$ vesicles are surrounded by a lipid bilayer [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ clathrin is a protein that has a role in vesicle formation [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ some vesicles are formed when the cell membrane invaginates **OR** some vesicles are formed by the Golgi apparatus **OR** some vesicles are formed by the rER [1 mark]

 *Function:* \
 $\ce{ -> }$ vesicles can act as storage organelles [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ vesicles are used to transport substances inside cells [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ vesicles are used for endocytosis **OR** phagocytosis **OR** pinocytosis [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ vesicles are used for exocytosis **OR** release of neurotransmitters **OR** any other suitable example of exocytosis [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ vesicles can form lysosomes **OR** peroxisomes **OR** any other suitable example [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Vesicles are cell structures **surrounded by a lipid bilayer** ($\checkmark$) that contain substances. Vesicles can be used to **transport substances** ($\checkmark$) around cells, to release substances from cells and to take substances into cells.
