# BI0383

Which of the following brings about movement of the skeleton?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Cartilage

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Ligaments

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Muscles

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Tendons

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The contraction and relaxation of muscles (**Choice C**) cause bones to move. Cartilage (**Choice A**) is the smooth tissue covering the end of bones in joints to reduce friction. Ligaments (**Choice B**) join bone to bone and help keep joints together. Tendons (**Choice D**) connect muscle to bone, but aren't responsible for movement.
