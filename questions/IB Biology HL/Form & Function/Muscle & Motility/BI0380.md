# BI0380

The diagram below represents a sarcomere. Which structures are labeled I and II ?

![BI0380](images/BI0380.png)

| | I | II |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Tropomyosin | Actin |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Myosin | Troponin |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Actin | Myosin |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Myosin | Actin |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The protein filament that is thicker and has heads is made of myosin (arrow I). The thinner protein filaments are made of actin, troponin and tropomyosin. Arrow II is pointing to the actin component of the thin filament (**Choice D**). Tropomyosin is a protein in the thin filament that masks myosin-binding sites (shown as thin black lines). Troponin is also a protein found in thin filaments. It has binding sites for calcium ions (shown as small double circles attached to tropomyosin lines).
