1. State one way that a daisy moves.    [1 mark]

2. State two life processes other than movement that the two organisms have in common.    [1 mark]

3. Explain three adaptations that the penguin has for efficient movement in water and on land.    [3 marks]

4. In order to reproduce, the penguin may have to travel to find a mate.

    1. Outline one advantage of travelling to find a mate.            [1 mark]

    2. Outline one disadvantage of travelling to find a mate.        [1 mark]

6. State two reasons other than reproduction that the penguin may need to travel.    [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ stem grows upwards **OR** phototropism        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ root grows downwards **OR** geotropism **OR** hydrotropism **OR** chemotropism    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ closing of petals at night **OR** nyctinasty **OR** nastic response    [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    The daisy can move by the elongation of cells allowing **phototropism**, a growth response to light ($\checkmark$) to occur.

2. Any **two** of the following:\
*Answer must have at least two points to gain one mark*

    $\ce{ -> }$ nutrition [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ metabolism **OR** respiration [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ growth [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ response [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ excretion [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ homeostasis [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ reproduction [1 mark]
    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    **Nutrition** ($\checkmark$) and **metabolism** ($\checkmark$).

3. Any **three** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ streamlined body, which reduces resistance **OR** friction in wind **OR** water, allowing for faster movement    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ flat flippers, which allow the penguin to turn easily in water **OR** push water behind its body to propel itself through water **OR** can be placed against its body to make it more streamlined    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ webbed feet to move efficiently on the ice **OR** snow without sinking        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ has additional air sacs inside lungs that allow air to circulate so all oxygen can be extracted so that they can swim for great lengths of time        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ overlapping feathers that provide waterproofing so penguin remains light when moving [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ strong wing and chest muscles, enabling them to generate powerful flapping motions to propel themselves through water    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ countercurrent heat exchange allows warm arterial blood flowing to the extremities to transfer heat to the cooler venous blood returning to the body, minimising heat loss to the cold water, conserving heat during prolonged swimming        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ upright posture helps them maintain balance and conserve energy while walking    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ strong feet and claws that provide grip and stability on ice and rocky surfaces    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ special oil gland that produced waterproofing oil, which they spread over their feathers and beaks     [1 mark]
    [3 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Three adaptations that the penguin has for efficient movement are a **streamlined** body, which **reduces air or water resistance** ($\checkmark$), so that it can move faster, **webbed feet** to move efficiently on ice and snow **without sinking** ($\checkmark$) and **additional air sacs** in its lungs so that it can **extract all of the oxygen** from the air it breathes and **swim for longer periods of time** ($\checkmark$).
