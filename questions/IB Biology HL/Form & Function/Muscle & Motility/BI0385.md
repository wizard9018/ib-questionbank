# BI0385

Which row shows the correct sequence of events in muscle contraction?

| | I | II | III | IV |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Ca:sup[2+] ions released from sarcoplasmic reticulum | ATP breaks cross-bridge | Myosin heads attach to binding sites | Hydrolysis of ATP causes myosin heads to pull actin inwards |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Ca:sup[2+] ions released from sarcoplasmic reticulum | Troponin moves tropomyosin | Myosin heads attach to binding sites | ADP and energy is released, and myosin heads pull actin inwards |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Troponin moves tropomyosin | Ca:sup[2+] ions released from sarcoplasmic reticulum | Myosin heads attach to binding sites | ATP breaks cross-bridge |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Troponin moves tropomyosin | Myosin heads attach to binding sites | Hydrolysis of ATP causes myosin heads to pull actin inwards | Release of ADP breaks cross-bridge |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because Ca:sup[2+] ions are released from the sarcoplasmic reticulum. Calcium diffuses into the sarcomere, binds to troponin, and causes troponin to move the tropomyosin away from the binding sites on actin. Myosin heads then attach to actin, and when ADP and energy are released, myosin swivels and pulls the actin inwards. **Choice A** is incorrect because cross-bridges are broken after actin has been pulled inwards. **Choice C** is incorrect because Ca:sup[2+] ions are released before troponin moves tropomyosin. **Choice D** is incorrect because hydrolysis of ATP does not cause myosin heads to swivel and pull actin inwards.
