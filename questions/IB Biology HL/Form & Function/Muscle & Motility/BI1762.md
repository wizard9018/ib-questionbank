3. any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ calcium ions bind to troponin bringing about conformational change     [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ this change in troponin moves tropomyosin away from myosin-binding sites on actin **OR** exposes myosin-binding sites on actin        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ allowing myosin to bind to actin **OR** form cross-bridges to enabling power stroke to occur [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

    Once calcium ions are released from the sarcoplasmic reticulum, they **bind to troponin**, causing a **conformational change** in the protein ($\checkmark$). This change **shifts tropomyosin away from** the **myosin-binding sites** on the **actin** filaments ($\checkmark$). With these binding sites now exposed, **myosin heads** can **attach to actin**, forming **cross-bridges**, allowing the power stroke to occur ($\checkmark$).
