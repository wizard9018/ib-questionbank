# BI0873

The photo shows an Australian shelduck (*Tadorna tadornoides*).
Which is **not** a feature that is adapted to aid in locomotion?

![BI0873](images/BI0873.png)
[Source: Adapted from Harrison, J. (2023, July 25). *Australian shelduck*. Wikipedia. Retrieved July 26, 202, from URL https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_shelduck. Copyright under CC BY 4.0]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Dense bones

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Wings

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Webbed feet

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Oily coating on feathers

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A**  is correct because dense bones would make the duck heavier, therefore making it harder for it to move.

**Choice B**  is incorrect, as wings enable the duck to fly. **Choice C**  is incorrect, as webbed feet aid the duck in swimming more efficiently. **Choice D** is incorrect as the oily coating on feathers helps to waterproof the duck and keep it light so it does not become waterlogged.  This helps the duck to swim and fly more easily.
