# BI0820

**Maximum mark: 10**

Chitin is an abundant polysaccharide in nature and is a major component of arthropod exoskeletons. In an experiment, scientists tested the effect of various chitin synthesis inhibitors - diflubenzuron (DFB), hexaflumuron (HX) and teflubenzuron (TFB) - on the moulting process of the salmon louse (*Lepeophtheirus salmonis*) which is a known parasite of salmon.

1.
    1. State, which is the independent variable and dependent variable. [2 marks]

    2. Suggest which chitin synthesis inhibitor is most effective at reducing the moulting success of salmon louse. Justify your answer. [1 mark]

    3. Suggest why the chitin synthesis inhibitors were administered at different concentrations.    [1 mark]

2.
    1. Exoskeletons provide an anchorage for muscles to attach to. In mammals, which type of tissue attaches muscle to the bone?     [1 mark]

    2. Describe another function of the exoskeleton.     [1 mark]

3. Outline the relationship between the type of chitin inhibitor, concentration and the percentage of incomplete moultings in salmon lice (*Lepeophtheirus salmonis*).     [3 marks]

4. As shown, chitin is visible as dark areas present on the surface of arthropods. Suggest one apparatus that could have been used to view the exoskeletons of the moulting salmon lice accurately.     [1 mark]
