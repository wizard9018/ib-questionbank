# BI1183

The diagram below shows part of a sarcomere. Which structure represents actin?

![BI1183](images/BI1183.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
During muscle contraction, myosin filaments pull the actin filaments inwards towards the centre of the sarcomere. Line B is pointing to an actin filament. **Choice B** is correct.

**Choices A** and **C** are incorrect, as they are pointing to myosin filaments. **Choice D** is incorrect as it indicates a titin filament.
