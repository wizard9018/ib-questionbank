# BI0381

The diagram below represents two sarcomeres. Which structure does **NOT** shorten during muscle contraction?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
During muscle contraction the thick filaments and thin filaments slide inwards across each other bringing the Z lines closer together, shortening the sarcomere (**Choice A**). As a result the I band (**Choice B**) and H zone (**Choice D**)  get narrower. However, seeming as the A band (**Choice C**) is where the thick filaments are found, whether on their own or with actin,  and the thick bands do not physically change length it means that the A band stays the same width. This means that **Choice C** is correct. **Choices A, B** and **D** all change length and so are incorrect.
