# BI0382

Which type of ion binds to filaments in the sarcomere to initiate the process of muscle contraction?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Calcium

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Potassium

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Sodium

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Hydrogen

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The release of Calcium ions from the sarcoplasmic reticulum initiates the process of muscle contraction (**Choice A**). Potassium ions (**Choice B**) and sodium ions (**Choice C**) are involved in nerve transmission and raising the membrane potential of muscle fibres to bring about contraction, but they do not bind to the sarcomere. Hydrogen ions (**Choice D**) do not diffuse from the sarcoplasmic reticulum.
