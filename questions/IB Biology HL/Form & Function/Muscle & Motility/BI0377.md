Which letter points to the cartilage?

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is the correct answer because cartilage is a tissue that covers the ends of bones and reduces friction.

 The joint capsule keeps the joint together and keeps the synovial fluid in the joint (**Choice A**). The joint is filled with the synovial fluid that lubricates the joint (**Choice C**). Ligaments join bone to bone (**Choice D**).
