# BI0384

Which of the following statements relates to myofibrils?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ A structure made of two proteins

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ A bundle of muscle fibres

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ A cylinder surrounded by the sarcolemma

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ A cylinder made of repeating sarcomeres

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Myofibrils are cylinders made of repeating sarcomeres (**Choice D**). Myofibrils contain more than two types of proteins (**Choice A**). **Choice B** is incorrect because muscle fibres are made of myofibrils. **Choice C** is incorrect because the sarcolemma is the membrane that surrounds a muscle fibre.
