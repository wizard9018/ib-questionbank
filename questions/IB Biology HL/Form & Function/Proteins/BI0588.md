# BI0588

Which of the following is a hormone?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$  Haemoglobin

- B. $\hspace{1em}$  Insulin
- C. $\hspace{1em}$  Collagen
- D. $\hspace{1em}$  Glycogen

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Insulin is a hormone produced by the pancreas (β-cells) that helps control blood glucose concentration; **Choice B** is the correct answer.

Haemoglobin is a protein but not a hormone. It is found in red blood cells and transports oxygen from the lungs to body tissues; therefore, **Choice A** is not correct.

Collagen is a structural protein, found in connective tissues like skin, tendons, and bones. It provides strength and elasticity but has no role in cell signalling or hormonal regulation; **Choice C** is, therefore, not correct.

Glycogen is a storage polysaccharide found in animals and fungi. In humans, it stores glucose in liver and muscle cells. The hormone insulin promotes the formation of glycogen, while the hormone glucagon triggers its breakdown. Glycogen is not a hormone so **Choice D** is not correct.
