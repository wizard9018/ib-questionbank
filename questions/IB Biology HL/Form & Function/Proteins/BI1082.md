# BI1082

Which of the following refers to the primary structure of a protein?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Sequence and conformation of amino acids

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Sequence and position of amino acids

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Hydrogen bonds stabilise beta-pleated sheets

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Hydrogen bonds stabilise alpha helices

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because the primary structure of a protein refers to the sequence and position of amino acids along the polypeptide chain, which determines the 3D shape. **Choice A** is incorrect as the primary structure does not refer to the conformation of amino acids. **Choices C** and **D** are incorrect as they refer to the secondary structure of a protein.
