# BI0585

Which of the following reactions results in the formation of a dipeptide from two amino acids?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Condensation

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Denaturation
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Hydrolysis
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Catabolism

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Two amino acids form a dipeptide through a condensation reaction (**Choice A**), which involves the loss of hydrogen from the amine group of one amino acid and a hydroxyl group from the carboxyl group of another amino acid. This results in the formation of a peptide bond and the formation of one water molecule. \
**Choice B** is incorrect, as denaturation refers to a change in the 3D shape of a protein. \
**Choice C** is incorrect, as hydrolysis reactions produce smaller molecules, such as amino acids, from larger molecules, such as dipeptides. \
**Choice D** is incorrect, as catabolism is a breaking-down reaction, while dipeptide formation is an anabolic reaction.
