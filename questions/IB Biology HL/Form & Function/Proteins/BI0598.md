# BI0598

Which level(s) of protein structure would be affected by denaturation due to high temperatures?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ Primary
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Secondary
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Tertiary

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ II only
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because denaturation changes the secondary and tertiary structure of a protein (**Statement II** and **III**); however, the peptide bonds which hold the amino acid chain together are not affected, and thus neither is the primary structure (**Statement I**).
