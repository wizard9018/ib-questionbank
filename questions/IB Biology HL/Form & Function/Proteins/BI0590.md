# BI0590

The diagram below shows a protein composed of three polypeptide chains, each chain contains 20 amino acids.

![BI0590](images/BI0590.png)

How many water molecules would be released in the synthesis of one of these chains?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ 17

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ 18
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ 19
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ 20

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$:\
**Choice C** is correct because amino acids are joined by peptide bonds as a result of condensation reactions which release water. In order to join 20 amino acids together, 19 peptide bonds would have to form which would result in the release of 19 water molecules.
