# BI0596

Which of the following is a distinguishing characteristic of the quaternary structure of a protein?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ The amino acid chain is arranged into an alpha helix

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ The amino acid chain is arranged into beta-pleated sheets
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Polypeptide chains are held together by R-group interactions
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ The amino acid sequence of the protein is held together by peptide bonds

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The R-groups, or side chains, of the amino acids in different polypeptide chains can interact with each other through various types of bonds and interactions, such as hydrogen bonds, ionic bonds, hydrophobic interactions, and disulfide bridges. These interactions hold the multiple subunits of a quaternary protein together; **Choice C** is therefore the correct answer.

The alpha-helix, where the polypeptide chain coils into a helix, and the beta-pleated sheet, where the polypeptide chain folds into sheet-like arrangements, are secondary structures of proteins, which are stabilised by hydrogen bonds. **Choices A** and **B** are therefore not correct. **Choice D** refers to the primary structure of a protein.
