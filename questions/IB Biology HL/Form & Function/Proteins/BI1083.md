# BI1083

The tertiary structure of a protein can be influenced by which of the following?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | Disulfide covalent bonds |
    | II. | Ionic bonds |
    | III. | Hydrophobic interactions |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because **Statements I, II** and **III** are all correct. Disulfide covalent bonds, ionic bonds and hydrophobic interactions can influence the tertiary structure of a protein. Disulfide bonds form between non-adjacent cysteine amino acids when they get in close proximity to each other. Ionic bonds form between two amino acids with oppositely charged R groups. Non–polar amino acids are repelled by water and so end up in the centre of the folds of the polypeptide chain, away from the aqueous solution, and this is known as hydrophobic interactions. **Choices A, B** and **C** are incorrect because they do not contain all three statements.
