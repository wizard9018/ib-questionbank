# BI0584

Which of the following contain peptide bonds?

- I. $\hspace{1em}$ Amino acids
- II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Dipeptides
- III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Proteins

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is false because amino acids do not contain peptide bonds by themselves. They are the basic building blocks of proteins, but a peptide bond forms only when two or more amino acids are linked together. Therefore, an amino acid on its own does not contain a peptide bond.

**Statement II** is true because a dipeptide is formed when two amino acids join through a peptide bond. The bond occurs between the carboxyl group of one amino acid and the amino group of another. Thus, dipeptides do contain peptide bonds.

**Statement III** is true because proteins are large molecules made up of many amino acids joined together by peptide bonds. These long chains of amino acids fold into specific structures, and the peptide bonds are essential for maintaining the protein's primary structure.

**Choice C** is the correct answer because it only includes the two correct Statements II and III.

**Choices A, B** and **D** are all incorrect as they include the false Statement I.
