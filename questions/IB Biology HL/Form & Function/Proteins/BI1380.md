# BI1380

Which of the following would not lead to the denaturation of proteins?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Reduction in pH

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Increase in temperature

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Mechanical stress

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Dilution in water

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Denaturation is a change in the shape of a protein caused by a breakdown of the bonds that usually hold it together. Although some proteins are soluble in water, their shape remains intact and denaturation will not occur. Therefore, **Choice D** is correct.

**Choice A**  is incorrect because a reduction in pH is likely to cause denaturation for most proteins. This is because the attractive forces between amino acids, such as hydrogen bonds, are disrupted, leading to a change in the 3-dimensional shape of the protein. **Choice B** is incorrect because an increase in temperature tends to cause denaturation as the increased kinetic energy is likely to disrupt bonds. **Choice C** is incorrect because, while less common than pH or temperature changes, physical stress is another potential cause of denaturation.
