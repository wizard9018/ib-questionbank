## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The -R group is connected to the carbon atom which the amino group is attached.

The amino group (**Choice A**) and the carboxyl group (**Choice B**) are functional groups but are not the -R group that distinguishes one amino acid from another. The part of the molecule shown in **Choice D** comprises part of the -R group but is missing a -CH$_2$.
