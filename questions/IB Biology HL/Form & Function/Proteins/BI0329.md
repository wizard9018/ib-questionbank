# BI0329

Which of the following accounts for the secondary structure of a protein?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Hydrogen bonding between two different amino acids in different polypeptide chains

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Hydrogen bonding between two different amino acids in the same polypeptide chain

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Covalent bonding between R groups of amino acids on the same polypeptide chain

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Peptide bonds between two different amino acids in the same polypeptide chain

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The secondary structure of a protein is formed by hydrogen bonding between two different amino acids within the same chain, which can result in the formation of alpha-helices and/or beta pleated sheets (**Choice B**). 

**Choice A** is incorrect, as any bonding between different polypeptide chains would be part of the quaternary structure. **Choice C** is incorrect as it describes the tertiary structure. **Choice D** is incorrect as it describes the primary structure.
