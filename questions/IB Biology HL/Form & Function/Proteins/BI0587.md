# BI0587

Which of the following are monomers that combine to form polypeptides?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$  Fatty acids

- B. $\hspace{1em}$  Amino acids
- C. $\hspace{1em}$  Proteins
- D. $\hspace{1em}$  Nucleotides

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Amino acids (**Choice B**) can form polypeptides through the formation of peptide bonds as a result of condensation reactions. Fatty acids (**Choice A**) are involved in the formation of lipids and are not monomers. Proteins (**Choice C**) are composed of one or more polypeptides and are polymers. Nucleotides (**Choice D**) are the building blocks of DNA and RNA.
