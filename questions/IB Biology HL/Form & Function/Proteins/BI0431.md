# BI0431

What type of molecule is represented in the diagram below?

![BI0431](images/image.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Fatty acid

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Phospholipid

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Nucleotide

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Amino acid

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The amino group and the carboxyl group indicate that this molecule is an amino acid.

Fatty acids and phospholipids (**Choices A** and **B**) do not have an amino group, while a nucleotide (**Choice C**) should have a phosphate group, a ribose or deoxyribose sugar and a nitrogenous base.
