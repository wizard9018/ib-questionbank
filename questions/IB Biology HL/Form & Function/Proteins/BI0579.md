# BI0579

Biomolecules have diverse forms and structures. Which of the following statements is **false**?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Deoxyribose can be differentiated from ribose because of the absence of the hydroxyl (-OH) group on the 2’ carbon.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Amino acids consist of an amino group, an acidic carboxyl group and an organic R group.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Phospholipids are lipid molecules with a hydrophobic “head” and two hydrophilic “tails.”

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Glucose is an example of a monosaccharide.

 *Note: Starting May 2028, this question may be assessed differently due to changes to the Biology guide, including the use of a data booklet.*

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** – The statement is false. Phospholipids are a class of lipids which have a hydrophilic “head” and two hydrophobic “tails.” The heads contain a phosphate group and the two tails each contain one fatty acid.

**Choice A, B**, and **D** – These statements are all true.
