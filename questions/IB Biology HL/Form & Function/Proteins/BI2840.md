# BI2840

Which letter choice correctly matches all three polypeptides with their correct functions?

| Polypeptide | Function|
| :-: | :-: |
| I. Insulin | a. Enzyme |
| II. Collagen | b. Structural support |
| III. Helicase | c. Hormone |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I $\rarr$ a, II $\rarr$ b, III $\rarr$ c

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I $\rarr$ c, II $\rarr$ a, III $\rarr$ b

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I $\rarr$ b, II $\rarr$ a, III $\rarr$ c

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I $\rarr$ c, II $\rarr$ b, III $\rarr$ a

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because insulin is a hormone involved in blood glucose regulation, collagen is a structural protein that provides tensile strength in connective tissues, and helicase is an enzyme that unwinds DNA during replication.

**Choices A, B**, and **C** are incorrect because they each contain at least one incorrect match between a polypeptide and its function.
