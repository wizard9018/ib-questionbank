# BI0328

The image shows part of a protein.

![BI0328 tertiary structure with disulfide bond BR](<images/BI0328 tertiary structure with disulfide bond BR.png>)

Which is the highest level of protein structure represented in this image?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Primary

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Secondary

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Tertiary

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Quaternary

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The image shows a single polypeptide chain folded into a complex 3D shape. It includes α-helices (secondary structures) and a disulfide bridge (a covalent bond between two cysteines), both of which contribute to the 3D conformation of the polypeptide chain. This overall folding, including interactions like hydrogen bonds, ionic bonds, hydrophobic interactions, and disulfide bonds, defines the tertiary structure. **Choice C** is, therefore, correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the primary structure refers only to the linear sequence of amino acids.

**Choice B** is incorrect because the secondary structure refers to local structures like α-helices and β-pleated sheets, but not the overall folding, and these structures do not contain disulfide bridges.

**Choice D** is incorrect because the quaternary structure involves two or more polypeptide chains, and the image shows only one.
