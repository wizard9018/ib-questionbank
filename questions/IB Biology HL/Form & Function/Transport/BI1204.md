# BI1204

Which bodily substance is matched **incorrectly** to its description?

| | Substance | Description |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Plasma | Part of blood that transports nutrients, proteins and hormones |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Tissue fluid | Intracellular fluid where most metabolic reactions take place |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Lymph | Fluid found in lymph ducts |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Surfactant | Lipids and proteins found in the fluid lining the surface of alveoli |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B**  is correct, as tissue fluid is the extracellular fluid that surrounds cells, not the intracellular, and it is not the site of most metabolic reactions. **Choices A, C** and **D**  are incorrect, as the substances are correctly described.
