# BI0340

The image below shows a cross-section of a stem of the dicotyledonous plant *Helianthus*.

Which letter identifies phloem?

![dicot stem with labels A-D i](<images/dicot stem with labels A-D i.png>)

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The vascular tissue in the stem is arranged into bundles of phloem and xylem (vascular bundles). Within the vascular bundle, the phloem is arranged closer to the outside of the stem, and the xylem vessels are located closer to the inside of the stem. **Choice D** represents phloem as it is inside the vascular bundles and is arranged closer to the outside of the stem.

**Choice A**is not correct because this is the epidermis, a single layer of cells on the outside of the stem, not located in a vascular bundle.

**Choice B** is not correct because this is the cortex, made up of medium-sized cells with thin walls, which help support the stem.

**Choice C** is incorrect because although this tissue is inside a vascular bundle, it indicates the xylem vessels, which are orientated towards the inside of the stem.
