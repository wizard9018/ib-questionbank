        $\ce{ -> }$ valves to prevent the backflow of blood        [1 mark]

        $\ce{ -> }$ thin **OR** less elastic walls to transport high volumes of blood at low pressure **OR** more flexible to accommodate changes in blood volume    [1 mark]

    2.  any **one** of the following\

        *Answer must include* ***BOTH*** *the adaptation and function to receive one mark*

        $\ce{ -> }$ they have thick, muscular, elastic walls to withstand high pressure        [1 mark]

        $\ce{ -> }$ they have a narrow lumen to maintain high pressure    [1 mark]

        $\ce{ -> }$ smooth endothelium and minimises friction between the blood and the vessel walls    [1 mark]

        [1 mark]

        $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\

        Arteries have **thick**, **elastic** walls to **withstand high pressure** ($\checkmark$).

3.  any **two** of the following\

    $\ce{ -> }$ baroreceptors **OR** stretch receptors in the walls of the aorta **OR** carotid artery detect high blood pressure    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ sensory neurons send impulses to the brain        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ processing by the cardiovascular centre **OR** medulla in the brain        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ impulses sent via the vagus nerve **OR** parasympathetic nervous system to lower the heart rate        [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\

    **Baroreceptors** in the walls of the aorta **detect high blood pressure** ($\checkmark$), which signals **sensory neurons** to **send impulses** to the brain($\checkmark$). The impulses are processed by the **cardiovascular centre** in the brain ($\checkmark$), which sends impulses via the **vagus nerve** to **lower the heart rate** ($\checkmark$).
