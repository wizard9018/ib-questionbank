# BI0674

Which of the following is likely to have the highest lignin content?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Xylem tissue

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Spongy mesophyll of leaves
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Phloem tissue
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Parenchyma tissue

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The cell walls of xylem are highly lignified (**Choice A**). Phloem tissue (**Choice C**), parenchyma tissue (**Choice D**) and spongy mesophyll cells (**Choice B**) have far less lignin compared to the cell walls of xylem.
