3. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ at sources sucrose **OR** organic compounds loaded into phloem AND at sinks sucrose **OR** organic compounds removed [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ high sucrose **OR** organic compound concentration at source lowers water potential **OR** increases solute potential so water enters by osmosis **OR** low sucrose **OR** organic compound concentration at sink increases water potential **OR** decreases solute potential so water leaves by osmosis [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ water entering phloem at source causes high hydrostatic pressure which increases rate of flow **OR** water leaving phloem at sinks causes low hydrostatic pressure reducing flow [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Sucrose is **actively transported** into the **phloem** at the **source**, which **increases** the **solute potential** (decreases water potential) ($\checkmark$) of the sap, so **water follows** by **osmosis** ($\checkmark$). This increase in water content of the sap causes a **high hydrostatic pressure**, **increasing** rate of **flow** ($\checkmark$). At the **sink**, **sucrose leaves** the phloem ($\checkmark$), so **solute potential decreases** (water potential increases), and **water leaves** the phloem by **osmosis**. This causes a **lower hydrostatic pressure** and so a **lower** rate of **flow** at the sinks.
