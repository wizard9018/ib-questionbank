# BI0184

Which component of blood is mainly composed of water and transports water-soluble substances to different parts of the body?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Red blood cells
- B. $\hspace{1em}$ White blood cells
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Platelets

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Blood plasma

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Blood plasma (**Choice D**) is the liquid component of blood and is composed of water which contains proteins, ions, and dissolved nutrients.
\
Red blood cells (**Choice A**) transport oxygen. \
Platelets (**Choice C**) are responsible for the process of blood clotting. \
White blood cells (**Choice B**) are part of the immune system.
