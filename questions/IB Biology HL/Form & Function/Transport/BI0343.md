# BI0343

The graph shows the results of an experiment which used radioactively labelled carbon dioxide (:sup[14]CO$_2$) to investigate the time taken for sap to travel to different colonies of aphids present on a plant.

![BI0343](images/BI0343.png)

Which conclusion could be supported by these results?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ All colonies received an equal amount of sap.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ All colonies were equally spaced along the stem of the plant.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Colony 1 fed on the sap more quickly than other colonies.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Colony 1 was located closest to the leaves.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Carbon dioxide is absorbed through the stomata in leaves, where the carbon is fixed during the light-independent reactions of photosynthesis to form organic compounds. These organic compounds can then be loaded into phloem to be transported to areas of the plant which can’t produce their own (sinks).

The time taken for radioactive carbon to be found in samples taken from the stylets of aphids was lowest in colony 1. This is because Colony 1 was the closest colony to the leaves, which is where the organic compounds which contained the radioactive carbon were synthesised.

**Choice A** is incorrect as the graph doesn’t provide any information to support the amount of sap each colony received. **Choice B** is incorrect because if the colonies were equally spaced along the stem, then we might expect to see a consistent time period between when radioactive carbon was found in each of their samples. **Choice C** is incorrect as we don’t have information to support how quickly the aphids were feeding, only the time taken for radioactive carbon to be found in their samples, which is not indicative of the feeding rate.
