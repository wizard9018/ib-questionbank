# BI0293

Which of the following carries oxygenated blood from the lungs to the heart?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Aorta

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Vena cava
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Pulmonary veins
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Pulmonary arteries

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Many blood vessels enter and leave the heart. Two pulmonary veins, one from each lung lead into the left atrium of the heart to deliver oxygenated blood (**Choice C**). \
Oxygenated blood is pumped out of the heart from the left ventricle to the aorta, which is the first route to all other parts of the body (**Choice A**). \
The blood passes through body tissues and becomes deoxygenated, it is then brought to the heart by the two vena cavae (singular: vena cava) (**Choice B**). \
Deoxygenated blood from the vena cava enters the right atrium and then to the right ventricle. It is pushed out through the pulmonary arteries to the lungs (**Choice D**).
