# BI1580

Amphibians have a double circulatory system that carries blood between the heart, the lungs, and the rest of the body. Their heart consists of two atria and one ventricle, as seen in the diagram.

![BI1580](images/BI1580.png)
[Source: Nefronus, (2020, March 28). *File: Amphibian circulatory system (numberated).svg*. Wikimedia Commons. Retrieved April 25, 2024, from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Amphibian_circulatory_system_(numberated).svg. Public domain]

What is a consequence of this circulatory structure?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Oxygenated and deoxygenated blood mixes before it is received by the body.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Increased blood pressure compared to mammals.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Amphibians have a less efficient circulatory system than fish.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Amphibians have a more efficient circulatory system than birds.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because the amphibian three-chambered heart causes oxygenated and deoxygenated blood to become mixed before it is pumped to the body.

**Choice B** is incorrect because amphibians have much lower blood pressure than mammals. **Choice C** is incorrect because fish have a single circulatory system that reduces the supply of oxygen received by some tissues, making it less efficient than the amphibian circulatory system. **Choice D** is incorrect because birds, like humans, have a double circulatory system with a four-chambered heart. This makes their circulation much more efficient than an amphibian's.
