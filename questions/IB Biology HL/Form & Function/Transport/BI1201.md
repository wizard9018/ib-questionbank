# BI1201

During the formation of tissue fluid, which is **not** a transport method between the blood plasma and cells?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Diffusion

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Osmosis

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Active transport

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Filtration

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C**  is correct, as energy is not involved in the formation of tissue fluid.

**Choice A**  is incorrect because gasses such as carbon dioxide and oxygen are exchanged between tissues and the capillaries via diffusion. **Choice B**  is incorrect, as the movement of water from the plasma between capillaries and tissues is via osmosis. **Choice D**  is incorrect, as tissue fluid is formed by pressure filtration of plasma from the capillaries.
