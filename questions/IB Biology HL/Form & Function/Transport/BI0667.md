# BI0667

What effect does the evaporation of water in leaves have on other parts of a plant?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ It causes the active uptake of mineral ions.

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ It causes water and mineral ions to move upwards in xylem vessels.
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ It helps to conserve water in dry environmental conditions.
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ It facilitates the formation of hydrogen bonds between water molecules.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Water loss by evaporation in the leaves is known as transpiration which helps move water and mineral ions upwards in xylem vessels (**Choice B**).\
Water loss by evaporation does not cause the active uptake of mineral ions (**Choice A**); instead, active uptake of mineral ions in roots causes absorption of water by osmosis. \
Water loss by evaporation is increased in dry environmental conditions (**Choice C**). Plants living in dry conditions have adaptations to reduce water loss by evaporation.\
The formation of hydrogen bonds between water molecules (**Choice D**) is due to the cohesive property of water and is not related to whether or not water is evaporating in the leaves.
