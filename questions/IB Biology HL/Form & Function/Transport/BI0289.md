Which of the following statement(s) is/are true?

- I. $\hspace{1em}$ When the walls of the atria contract, blood flows from the atria to the ventricles because the atrioventricular valves are open
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ When the walls of the ventricles contract, the increase in pressure causes the atrioventricular valves to close which prevents the backflow of blood from the ventricles to the atria.
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ When the ventricles stop contracting, the pressure falls and the semilunar valves close preventing backflow from the arteries to the ventricles.

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ I only
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
All three statements are correct and describe the flow of blood in the cardiac cycle. **Choice B** includes all three statements.
All the other choices (**Choices A**, **C** and **D**) do not include all three statements.
