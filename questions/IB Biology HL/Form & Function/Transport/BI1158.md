Which pathway correctly describes the flow of blood after it leaves the lungs?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Pulmonary Vein → Right Atrium → Right Ventricle → Vena Cava

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Pulmonary Vein → Left Atrium → Left Ventricle → Aorta

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Pulmonary Artery → Right Atrium →  Right Ventricle → Vena Cava

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Pulmonary Artery → Left Atrium → Left Ventricle → Aorta

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Oxygenated blood from the lungs travels to the heart via the pulmonary vein. It then enters the left atrium before passing into the left ventricle before leaving the heart via the aorta on its way to the body. **Choice B** is, therefore, correct.

**Choices A, C**, and **D** are incorrect as they all contain structures that are found on the right side of the heart (vena cava, right atrium, right ventricle, and pulmonary artery). The right-hand side of the heart is the side that pumps deoxygenated blood back to the heart.
