# BI0341

The image below shows a cross-section of a stem of the dicotyledonous plant *Helianthus*.

What function is performed by the structure labelled **X**?

![BI0341 i](<images/BI0341 i.png>)
[Source: adapted from Berkshire Community College Bioscience Image Library (2014, February 10). *Herbaceous Dicot Stem: Young Helianthus*. Retrieved May 20, 2025, from  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File: Herbaceous_Dicot_Stem_Young_Helianthus_(34731656652).jpg. CC0]

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Transport of water from the roots to the leaves

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Transport of organic compounds from sources to sinks

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Transport of organic compounds from sinks to sources

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Transport of water from sources to sinks

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
X in the diagram represents phloem, which is the part of the vascular bundle in the stem that is positioned towards the outside of the stem. Phloem's role is to transport organic compounds such as sugars and amino acids from their area of production/storage (source) to the areas of the plant which require them but cannot produce them themselves e.g. roots (sinks).

**Choices A** and **D** refer to the transport of water, which is the role of the xylem vessels. **Choice C** states that phloem transports organic compounds from the sink to the source which is incorrect.
