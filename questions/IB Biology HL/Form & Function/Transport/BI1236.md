# BI1236

The image below is an electron micrograph of a cross-section of a stem of the monocot *Zea mays*. Which letter identifies the vessel that transports sucrose around the plant?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
In the stem, the vascular tissue is arranged into bundles of phloem and xylem (vascular bundles). Within the vascular bundle, the phloem is arranged closer to the outside of the stem and the xylem vessels are located closer to the inside of the stem. The phloem are also typically smaller than the xylem. The phloem transports sucrose around the plant and the xylem transports water from the roots to the rest of the leaves. **Choice D** is correct.

**A** and **B** are not located within a vascular bundle, **Choices A**  and **B**  are incorrect. **C** indicates the xylem vessels, which are orientated towards the inside of the stem, **Choice C**  is incorrect.
