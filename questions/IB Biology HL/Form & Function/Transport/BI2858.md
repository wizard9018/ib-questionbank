# BI2858

The diagram shows a transverse section of an artery:

![BI2858 B3.2 core MCQ.jpeg](<images/BI2858 B3.2 core MCQ.jpeg.JPEG>)

Which row correctly matches the layer with its function?

| | X | Y | Z |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Reduces resistance to flow | Provides strength and helps prevent bursting | Recoils to maintain flow |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Reduces resistance to flow | Recoils to maintain flow | Provides strength and helps prevent bursting |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Provides strength and helps prevent bursting | Recoils to maintain flow | Reduces resistance to flow |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Recoils to maintain flow | Reduces resistance to flow | Provides strength and helps prevent bursting |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Layer X** is the tunica intima. This innermost layer is made of smooth endothelium, providing a low-friction surface for efficient blood flow.

**Layer Y** is the tunica media. This middle layer contains smooth muscle and elastic fibres, allowing the artery to stretch and recoil with each heartbeat, helping to maintain blood pressure and flow.

**Layer Z** is the tunica externa. This outer layer contains collagen fibres that provide mechanical strength, and helps prevent rupturing under the high pressure.

**Choice B** is therefore the correct answer to the question.
