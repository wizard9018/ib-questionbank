# BI2819

Which of the following statements about venous blood flow is/are true?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | It depends on the contraction of skeletal muscles |
    | II. | It occurs under low pressure compared to arterial flow|
    | III. | It is aided by elastic recoil of vein walls |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ I and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Statement I** is true because veins, especially in the limbs, are surrounded by skeletal muscle. Contraction of these muscles compresses the veins, pushing blood toward the heart. One-way valves in veins prevent backflow, making this mechanism essential for venous return.\
**Statement II** is true because after passing through capillaries, blood pressure in veins is much lower than in arteries. Veins do not have enough internal pressure to move blood effectively without help from muscle contractions, valves, and respiratory movements.\
**Statement III** is false because, unlike arteries, veins contain very few elastic fibres and do not recoil significantly. Elastic recoil is a feature of arterial walls, especially the aorta and large arteries, to maintain blood flow during diastole.

**Choice A** is the correct answer because it only includes the two true **Statements I** and **II**.\
**Choices B, C** and **D** are all incorrect as they include the false **Statement III**.
