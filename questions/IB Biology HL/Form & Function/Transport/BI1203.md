# BI1203

What is the role of the valves in the lymph vessels?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ To absorb tissue fluid from the surrounding cells

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ To prevent backflow of lymph

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ To allow lymph to flow in two directions

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ To aid the contraction of the lymph ducts

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B**  is correct, as valves prevent the backflow of lymph. **Choice A** is incorrect as the valves themselves do not absorb fluid, only control its flow. **Choice C**  is incorrect, as valves allow the lymph to only flow in one direction. **Choice  D** is incorrect, as valves do not control contraction, muscle tissue does.
