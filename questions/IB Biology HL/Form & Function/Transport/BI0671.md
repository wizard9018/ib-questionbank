# BI0671

Which of the following adaptations are seen in xerophytes for water conservation?

| | Broad leaves | Thick cuticle | Sunken stomata |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| | | $\checkmark$ |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\checkmark$ | $\checkmark$ | |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | $\checkmark$ | | |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | | $\checkmark$ | $\checkmark$ |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Xerophyte adaptations for water conservation include thick cuticles and sunken stomata, which help reduce water loss through evaporation and transpiration. **Choice D** is correct, as it includes both of these key adaptations.

Broad leaves are not typical of xerophytes, as they increase surface area and water loss.
Xerophyte leaves are narrow, small or rolled to reduce water loss by evaporation. **Choices B** and **C**  are incorrect as they include broad leaves.

**Choice A** is incorrect because, although it correctly includes sunken stomata, it omits the thick cuticle, another major adaptation of xerophytes.
