# BI0297

**Maximum mark: 3**

The diagram below represents the circulatory system of a fish.

![BI0297](images/BI0297.png)

1. Distinguish between a double circulatory system and a single circulatory system. [1 mark]

2. Describe the advantages of a double circulatory system in humans. [2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. Any **one** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ the blood passes through the heart twice for every complete circuit of the body in a double circulatory system, **AND** it passes only once through the heart in a single circulatory system [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ the pulmonary circulation is separate from the systemic circulation in a double circulatory system; the pulmonary circulation is not separate from the systemic circulation in a single circulatory system [1 mark]

 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
While the blood passes **twice** through the heart for every complete circuit of the body in a double circulatory system, as found in humans, it passes only **once** in a single circulatory system ($\checkmark$), as seen in a fish.

2. Any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ double circulation separates deoxygenated blood from oxygenated blood in the heart [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ in single circulation, blood flows at lower pressure from the lungs to the tissues of the body [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ in double circulation oxygenated blood from the lungs returns to the heart where it is pumped to tissues of the body under high pressure **OR** double circulation ensures that more highly oxygenated blood is transported under higher pressure [1 mark]

 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
In double circulation, **deoxygenated** blood is **separated from oxygenated** ($\checkmark$) blood in the heart. After blood is **oxygenated** in the lungs, it comes **back to the heart** and is **pumped** out directly **to tissues** under **greater pressure** ($\checkmark$). This ensures that more highly oxygenated blood reaches every part of the body.
