# BI0288

Which row in the table below is **not** correct for the structure and function of different blood vessels?

| | $\hspace{1em}$Blood Vessel$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$Structure$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$Function$\hspace{1em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| arteries | thick, non-elastic walls | receive blood at high pressure from the ventricles |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | veins | thin wall, wide lumen | receive blood at low pressure from the tissues |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | arteries | thick, elastic walls | receive blood at high pressure from the ventricles |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | capillaries | thin, permeable walls | allow exchange of materials between the blood and tissues |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is the correct response because arteries are not non-elastic, instead they do have a degree of elasticity. **Choices B**, **C** and **D** all correctly match the structure and function of each blood vessel.
