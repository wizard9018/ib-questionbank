Which structure is labelled X?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Plasmodesmata

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Proton pump

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Co-transporter protein

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Stomata

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Sucrose can be loaded into the phloem passively through channels called plasmodesmata, which provide cytoplasmic continuity between the phloem and adjacent cells. Sucrose moves due to differences in its concentration gradient.

**Choice B** refers to proton pumps which actively transport H:sup[+] ions. **Choice C** refers to co-transporter proteins which utilise the proton gradient created by the active transport of H:sup[+] to load sucrose into the phloem, so this loading is not unaided as referred to in the question. **Choice D** refers to the stomata which absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere and allow the evaporation of water from the leaf.
