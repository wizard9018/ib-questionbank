    $\ce{ -> }$ phloem    sieve tubes have a small amount of cytoplasm **OR** lack most cellular components, which gives space for transport of phloem sap **OR** to facilitate the flow of sap         [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ sieve plates between sieve tubes allow easy movement of phloem sap **OR** allow sap to flow freely between adjacent cells        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ companion cells carry out cellular functions **OR** provide metabolic support for the sieve tubes        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ cell walls of cellulose and lignin **OR** sclerenchyma cells **OR** arrangement of cells, provide mechanical support and prevent collapse under high hydrostatic pressure [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ active transport mechanisms **OR** indirect active transport **OR**  sucrose-proton symport allow for the movement of nutrients **OR** sucrose against concentration gradients for loading at source    [1 mark]

    Phloem sieve tubes have a **small amount of cytoplasm**, which gives **space for the transport of phloem sap** ($\checkmark$) and **sieve plates** ($\checkmark$) between the sieve tubes **allow the phloem sap to move easily** ($\checkmark$).

2.
    2. any **four** of the following (answer **must** include at least **one** difference **and one** similarity):

    *Similarities*

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** are composed of tubes **OR** vascular tissues        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** are both adapted for transport                [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** contain lignin in cell walls [1 mark]

    *Differences*

    $\ce{ -> }$ xylem is a non-living **OR** dead at maturity tissue, but phloem is a living tissue    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ xylem cells lack cell contents whereas in phloem sieve tube elements contain some cellular components **OR** companion cells contain normal cellular components    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ xylem contains more lignin **THAN** phloem        [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ xylem vessels have a wider diameter than phloem tubes    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ primary function of xylem is to transport water and minerals, whereas primary function of phloem is to transport organic compounds **OR** sucrose **OR** sugars    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ unidirectional transport in xylem, whereas bidirectional transport in phloem    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ main cell types in xylem are tracheids and vessel elements, whereas main cell types in phloem are sieve tube elements and companion cells    [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ transport through the xylem mainly relies on cohesion, adhesion and tension **OR** driven by transpiration, whereas transport through  phloem relies on active transport **OR** indirect active transport **OR**  sucrose-proton symport **OR** hydrostatic pressure [1 mark]

    [4 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
    Xylem and phloem are **both tubular structures**, or **vascular tissue**, ($\checkmark$) and **adapted** to **transport substances** around the plant ($\checkmark$). However, **xylem** is a **non-living tissue** that transports **water** around the plant in **one direction**, whereas **phloem** is a **living tissue** ($\checkmark$) that transports **organic compounds** ($\checkmark$)  both **up and down** the plant ($\checkmark$).
