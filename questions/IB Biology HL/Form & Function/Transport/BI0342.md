# BI0342

Which row best represents the solute concentration and hydrostatic pressure within the sieve tubes near the leaves and roots, during the summer months?

| | Solute concentration in leaves | Solute concentration in roots | Hydrostatic pressure in leaves | Hydrostatic pressure in roots|
| --- | :-: | :-: |:-: |:-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | High | Low | High | Low |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | High | Low | Low | High |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Low| High | High | Low |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Low | High |Low | High |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
During the summer months, green plants are usually actively growing and photosynthesising. This means that during this season, the leaves are sources, where organic compounds such as sucrose are loaded into the sieve tubes. This creates a high solute concentration in the sieve tubes and a concentration gradient between the sieve tubes and surrounding cells. The concentration gradient results in the movement of water by osmosis into the sieve tubes, which increases the hydrostatic pressure.

Roots are sinks, where organic compounds such as sucrose are unloaded from the sieve tubes into the surrounding cells. This results in a decrease in the sucrose concentration in the sieve tubes and as a result, water moves out of the sieve tube by osmosis and the hydrostatic pressure decreases.

This makes **Choice A** the correct answer.

**Choice B** is incorrect as the hydrostatic pressure would be high in the leaves and low in the roots. **Choices C** and **D** are both incorrect as the solute concentration would be high in the sieve tubes near the leaves.
