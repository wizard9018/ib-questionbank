# BI2330

Which row contains an adaptation with a matching advantage for xylem vessels?

| | Adaptation | Advantage |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Incomplete end walls | Allow entry and exit of water to other tissues |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Lignified walls | Allows unimpeded flow of water |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Pits | Withstands tension due to upward movement of water |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Lack of cell contents | Allows unimpeded flow of water |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because xylem vessels contain significantly reduced cell contents which prevents obstruction to water flow in the xylem.

**Choice A** is incorrect because while incomplete end walls allow water to flow easily from one xylem vessel to the next, water does not flow between xylem vessels and plant tissues through the incomplete end walls.

**Choice B** is incorrect because xylem walls do have lignified walls, but the function of the lignified walls is to prevent the collapse of xylem vessels under tension caused by the transpiration stream, not to allow unimpeded water flow.

**Choice C** is incorrect because pits in the walls of xylem vessels allow water to move between xylem vessels, pits do not withstand the tension created by water's upward movement.
