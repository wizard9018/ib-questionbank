# BI0675

How are most mineral ions in the soil absorbed by roots?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ Absorbed by osmosis

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ Absorbed with water
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ Active transport by root hair cells
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ Diffuse into root hair cells from wet soil

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Most mineral ions in the soil are absorbed by active transport in root hair cells (**Choice C**).

Active uptake of mineral ions causes the absorption of water by osmosis.  By definition, osmosis is the movement of water, not ions (**Choice A**).

Water gets absorbed passively, but most ions require active transport (**Choice B**).

Diffusion is a type of passive transport and happens to some extent with mineral ion uptake. However the concentration of most minerals in the soil is low so active transport is generally required (**Choice D**).
