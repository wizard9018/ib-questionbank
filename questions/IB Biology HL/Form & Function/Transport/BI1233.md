# BI1233

The diagram shows the frontal plane of the heart. Which letter indicates where the pacemaker would be found?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The heart diagram is always inverted so that the left is on the right and vice versa. The sinoatrial node or pacemaker is located in the right atrium of the heart. The atria are the two top chambers in the heart, and the right side of the heart can be identified as it has a thinner outer wall than the left side. **Choice A**  is correct.

Line B indicates the right ventricle; **Choice B**  is incorrect. Line C indicates the pulmonary artery; **Choice C**  is incorrect. Line D indicates the left ventricle; **Choice D** is incorrect.
