# BI0347

Which row correctly identifies features of **mature** sieve tube elements?

| | Present | Absent|
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Plasmodesmata **and** sieve plate |  Nucleus|
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Plasmodesmata **and** complete end wall |   Lignin|
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Lignin **and** sieve plate |   Plasma membrane|
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Plasma membrane **and** nucleus |   Complete end wall |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Mature sieve tube elements contain sieve plates and are connected to adjacent companion cells by numerous plasmodesmata. Mature sieve tube elements lose their nucleus during development. Therefore, **Choice A** is correct.

**Choice B** is incorrect because mature sieve tube elements have sieve plates containing pores rather than complete, unperforated end walls. Although plasmodesmata are present and lignin is absent, the row is therefore incorrect.

**Choice C** is incorrect because mature sieve tube elements retain a plasma membrane and their cell walls are not lignified. Although a sieve plate is present, the other two entries are incorrect.

**Choice D** is incorrect because mature sieve tube elements lose their nucleus during development. Although they retain a plasma membrane and lack complete, unperforated end walls, the row is therefore incorrect.
