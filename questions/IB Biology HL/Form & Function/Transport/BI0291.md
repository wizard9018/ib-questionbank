# BI0291

Which of the following shows the direction of blood flow into and out of the heart?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$  Vena cava $\rightarrow$ right ventricle $\rightarrow$ right atrium $\rightarrow$ lungs $\rightarrow$ left ventricle $\rightarrow$ left atrium $\rightarrow$ aorta

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$  Right ventricle $\rightarrow$ right atrium $\rightarrow$ lungs $\rightarrow$ aorta $\rightarrow$ left atrium $\rightarrow$ left ventricle $\rightarrow$ vena cava

-    C. $\hspace{1em}$  Vena cava $\rightarrow$ right atrium $\rightarrow$ right ventricle $\rightarrow$ lungs $\rightarrow$ left atrium $\rightarrow$ left ventricle $\rightarrow$ aorta

-    D. $\hspace{1em}$  Lungs $\rightarrow$ right atrium $\rightarrow$ left atrium $\rightarrow$ left ventricle $\rightarrow$ right ventricle $\rightarrow$ aorta

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Blood flows from the vena cava into the right atrium of the heart, and then to the right ventricle.  Blood is then pumped to the lungs after which it flows back into the heart via the left atrium. It then flows into the left ventricle and from there it is pumped into the aorta before being carried away to the tissues (**Choice C**).

Blood does not flow from the lungs into the left ventricle (**Choice A**). Blood does not flow from the lungs to the aorta (**Choice B**). Blood does not flow from the lungs to the right atrium (**Choice D**).
