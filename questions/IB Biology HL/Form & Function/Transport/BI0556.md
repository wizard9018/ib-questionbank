# BI0556

From which chamber of the heart is blood pumped into the pulmonary artery?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Right atrium

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Left atrium

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Right ventricle

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Left ventricle

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The right ventricle receives oxygen-poor blood from the right atrium and pumps this blood to the lungs via the pulmonary artery. So, blood is pumped into the pulmonary artery by the right ventricle.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the right atrium pumps blood to the right ventricle, not the pulmonary artery

**Choice B** is incorrect because the left atrium pumps blood into the left ventricle.

**Choice D** is incorrect because the left ventricle pumps blood to the body via the aorta.
