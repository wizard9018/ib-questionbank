# BI1488

The image shows a transverse section of an artery and vein. Which blood vessel carries oxygenated blood to body tissues?

| | Letter | Explanation |

| --- | :-: | :-: |

| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | X | It has a wider lumen to carry a higher volume of blood |

| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | X | It has thin walls to allow diffusion of oxygen to body tissues |

| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Y | It has thick walls to carry blood at high pressure |

| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Y | It has valves to stop the backflow of blood |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

Arteries have a narrow lumen and thick wall to carry oxygenated blood at high pressure to the body tissues; vessel Y is an artery. Veins have a wide lumen and thin walls, as they carry deoxygenated blood at lower pressure than the arteries; vessel X is a vein. **Choice C**  is correct.

**Choice A**  is incorrect as although it has a wide lumen, it is a vein and does not carry oxygenated blood. **Choice B** is incorrect as capillaries allow diffusion of gases due to their thin walls, not arteries or veins, and most veins do not carry oxygenated blood. **Choice D** is incorrect as veins contain valves but arteries carry oxygenated blood.
