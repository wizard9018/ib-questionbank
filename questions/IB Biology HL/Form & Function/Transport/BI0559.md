# BI0559

What helps prevent the backflow of blood in a vein?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Relaxation of skeletal muscles

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Venous valves

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Relaxation of the heart

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Thin vessel walls

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Veins carry deoxygenated blood from the body back to the heart. As muscles surrounding the veins contract, they push the blood towards the heart and venous valves open to allow the flow of blood. When the muscles relax, the valves close to prevent the blood from flowing backwards in response to gravity. So, venous valves help prevent the backflow of blood away from the heart (**Choice B**).

The relaxation of skeletal muscles decreases blood pressure in veins and does not play a role in preventing the backflow of blood in veins (**Choice A**).  When the heart relaxes, this decreases blood pressure in arteries, it does not affect blood pressure in veins and plays no role in preventing the backflow of blood in veins (**Choice C**).  Thin vessel walls are most exemplified in capillaries whose walls are one cell thick and allow exchange of substances.  Thin walls play no role in preventing the backflow of blood (**Choice D**).
