# BI0513

What is the position of the atrioventricular and semilunar valves at the point when the pressure in the left ventricle is the greatest?

| | Atrioventricular Valves | Semilunar Valves |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | open | open |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | open | closed |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | closed | open |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | closed | closed |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The pressure in the ventricles is highest when the ventricles contract. The ventricular contraction causes the atrioventricular valves to close (to prevent backflow of blood to the atria) and the semilunar valves to open so blood may be sent to the arteries.

Atrioventricular valves are open when blood is passing from the atria into the ventricles, not when the ventricles are contracting to push blood out of the heart (**Choice A** and **Choice B**). Semilunar valves have to open, not closed, to allow the blood out of the ventricles when they contract (**Choice D**).
