# BI0292

Which region of the heart is involved in controlling the frequency at which it beats?

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$   Sinoatrial node

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$   The opening of the atrioventricular valves
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$   Pulmonary circulation
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$   The opening of the semilunar valves

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The sinoatrial node is a region of specialized cardiac muscle cells found in the wall of the right atrium. The sinoatrial node acts as a pacemaker and is involved in controlling heart rate (**Choice A**).  \
The opening of the atrioventricular valves occurs when there is an increase in pressure inside the atria. Blood flows to the ventricles when these valves are open. This action does not control the heart rate (**Choice B**). \
Pulmonary circulation is when blood flows into the lungs from the right ventricle and is returned to the left atrium after it is oxygenated, and does not control the heart rate (**Choice C**). \
The opening of the semilunar valves occurs when the pressure in the ventricles increases (**Choice D**). This does not control heart rate.
