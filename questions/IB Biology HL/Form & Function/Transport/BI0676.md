Xylem vessels have walls that are **thick** ($\checkmark$) and impregnated with **lignin** in addition to cellulose ($\checkmark$). This helps the xylem to withstand low pressures without collapsing.

3.    Any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{ -> }$ protein pumps, **OR** active transport, in root cells move the mineral ions into the root [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ higher solute, **OR** mineral ion concentration in the roots than in soil [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ hypotonic conditions **OR** lower water potential in the roots compared to the soil [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water enters the roots from the soil by osmosis [1 mark]

    $\ce{ -> }$ water diffuses down its concentration gradient into the roots [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Root hair plasma membranes contain **protein pumps** specializing in the uptake of specific mineral ions needed by the plant ($\checkmark$). The movement of minerals to the inside of the roots creates a **lower water potential** in the root cells compared to the surrounding environment ($\checkmark$). This allows water to move into the roots by **osmosis**($\checkmark$).
