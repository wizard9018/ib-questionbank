Based on the diagram, which labels identify the aorta and the left ventricle?

| | $\hspace{1em}$Aorta$\hspace{1em}$ | $\hspace{1em}$Left ventricle$\hspace{1em}$ |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| IV | II |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | I | V |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | III | II |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | V | III |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
In the diagram, **V** is the aorta and **III** is the left ventricle, therefore **Choice D** is correct.\
**IV** identifies the pulmonary veins, and **II** the right ventricle (**Choice A**). \
**I** identifies valves in the heart and **V** is the aorta (**Choice B**). \
**III** is the left ventricle and **II** is the right ventricle (**Choice C**).
