   3. Any **two** of the following

        $\ce{ -> }$ impulses are brought from the medulla of the brain to the SA node by two nerves [1 mark]

        $\ce{ -> }$  impulses from one of the nerves to the SA node causes the pacemaker to slow down while impulses from the other causes it to increase [1 mark]

        $\ce{ -> }$ the sympathetic nerve (releases a neurotransmitter norepinephrine) that increases the heart rate [1 mark]

        $\ce{ -> }$ the parasympathetic nerve **OR** vagus nerve (releases the neurotransmitter acetylcholine) that decreases the heart rate [1 mark]

        $\ce{ -> }$ in response to signals from the brain, the hormone epinephrine is released that causes an increase in the heart rate [1 mark]

        [2 marks]

        $\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \
Messages are brought from the **medulla** of the brain to the SA node by **two nerves** ($\checkmark$). Impulses from the **sympathetic nerve** causes the release of a neurotransmitter **norepinephrine** and increases the heart rate ($\checkmark$). Impulses from the **parasympathetic** nerve causes the release of neurotransmitter **acetylcholine** and decreases the heart rate. To prepare for physical activity, the brain also stimulates the release of the hormone **epinephrine** that causes an increase in the heart rate.
