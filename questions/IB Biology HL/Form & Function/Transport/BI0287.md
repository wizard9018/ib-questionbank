# BI0287

Which row correctly identifies a tissue where capillaries with fenestrations are present, along with an appropriate reason for their presence?

| | Tissue | Reason |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Skeletal muscle | To allow contraction |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Brain | To maintain the blood-brain barrier |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Kidney | To enhance the filtration of plasma |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Skin | To increase thermoregulation |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Capillaries in the kidney glomerulus are fenestrated, allowing rapid filtration of plasma, including water, glucose, ions, and urea, while retaining plasma proteins and blood cells. The fenestrations in the endothelial layer increase permeability, but the basement membrane acts as a molecular filter, preventing larger molecules like proteins from being lost in urine. **Choice C** is correct.

Skeletal muscle contains continuous capillaries, which lack fenestrations. These capillaries provide regulated exchange of gases and nutrients, such as oxygen and glucose, but do not require rapid permeability. Also, contraction is a function of muscle fibres, not capillaries; capillaries do not “allow” contraction. **Choice A** is incorrect.

The brain contains continuous, non-fenestrated capillaries with tight junctions. These create the blood–brain barrier, which restricts substance exchange to protect neural tissue. Fenestrations would compromise the barrier's selectivity. **Choice B** is incorrect.

Skin capillaries are typically continuous, not fenestrated, and they help regulate temperature through vasodilation and vasoconstriction, not by increased permeability. **Choice D** is incorrect.
