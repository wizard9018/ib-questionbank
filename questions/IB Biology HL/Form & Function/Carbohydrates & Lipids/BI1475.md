# BI1475

Which biomolecule does **NOT** contain a carboxyl group?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Dipeptide

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Saturated fatty acid

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Amino acid

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Triglyceride

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

A carboxyl group (COOH) consists of a carbon molecule joined to an oxygen atom by a double bond and an OH/hydroxyl group by a single bond. Although carboxyl groups are found in one of the monomers in triglyceride; in fatty acids, the carboxyl group reacts with glycerol to form an ester bond in a condensation reaction. Therefore carboxyl groups are not found in triglycerides. **Choice D**  is correct.

Dipeptides, saturated fatty acids and amino acids all contain carboxyl groups. **Choices A, B** and **C**  are incorrect.
