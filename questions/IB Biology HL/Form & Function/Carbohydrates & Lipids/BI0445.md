---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

Cellulose is an unbranched chain of  $\beta-$ glucose molecules with 1,4 linkages and adjacent glucose molecules in alternating orientations. **Choice A**  is correct.

**Choices C** and  **D** are incorrect as both starch and glycogen are branched molecules. **Choice B**  is incorrect as amylose is made of $\alpha-$ glucose molecules.
