# BI0438

**Maximum mark: 6**

1. Distinguish between the formation and breaking down of a polymer. [2 marks]

2. Compare and contrast the structures of carbohydrates, lipids and proteins. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **two** of the following:

    $\ce{->}$ in polymer formation, a dehydration **OR** condensation reaction occurs **WHILE** in the breaking down of a polymer a hydrolysis reaction occurs [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ in polymer formation, one molecule of water **OR** $\ce{H2O}$ is produced **WHILE** in the breaking down of a polymer water **OR** $\ce{H2O}$ molecules are split/added [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ polymer formation is an anabolic reaction **WHILE** breaking down a polymer is a catabolic reaction [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ polymer formation is an endergonic **OR** endothermic reaction or stores energy **WHILE** breaking down a polymer is an exergonic **OR** exothermic reaction or releases energy. [1 mark]

    [2 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \

    **To form a polymer, a dehydration reaction occurs** ($\checkmark$) between monomers resulting in the formation of bonds between two molecules **while to break down a polymer, a hydrolysis reaction occurs** ($\checkmark$) which breaks bonds between two monomers. Forming a polymer is an **anabolic reaction** while breaking down a polymer is a **catabolic reaction**. ($\checkmark$)

2. any **two** of the following (at least **one** from compare and **one** from contrast):

    *Compare:* \

    $\ce{->}$ carbohydrates, lipids and proteins are **ALL** organic compounds **OR** contain C,H, and O [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ carbohydrates, lipids and proteins are **ALL** macromolecules [1 mark]

   

    *Contrast:* \

    $\ce{->}$ carbohydrates **AND** proteins are polymers **WHILST** lipids are not [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ carbohydrates and lipids are composed of carbon, hydrogen and oxygen **WHILE** proteins are composed of carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen and sometimes sulphur [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ the monomers for carbohydrates are monosaccharides **WHILE** the monomer for proteins are amino acids, and for lipids they are fatty acids and glycerol [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ carbohydrates contain glycosidic linkages between monomers **WHILE** proteins contain peptide linkages **WHILE** in lipids there are ester bonds [1 mark]

    $\ce{->}$ carbohydrates have aldehyde or ketone groups **WHILE** proteins have amino groups and carboxyl groups, and lipids only have carboxyl groups [1 mark]

    [4 marks]

    $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:

| Carbohydrates | Proteins | Lipids | | 

| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | 

| **Organic macromolecules** | **Organic macromolecules** | **Organic macromolecules** | ($\checkmark$) |

| Contain **glycosidic linkages** | Contain **peptide linkages** | Contain **ester bonds** | ($\checkmark$) |

| Composed of **C, H, and O** | Composed of **C, H, O, N and sometimes S** | Composed of **C, H, and O** | ($\checkmark$) | 

| Monomers are **monosaccharides** | Monomers are **amino acids** | Made out of  **fatty acids** and **glycerol** | ($\checkmark$) |
