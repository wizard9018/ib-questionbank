# BI0282

Which row in the table below correctly identifies the constituents of the named macromolecules?

| | Starch | Protein | Triglycerides |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $\hspace{1em}$A.$\hspace{1em}$| Beta glucose | Peptides | Fatty acids and glycerol |
|$\hspace{1em}$B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Beta glucose | Amino acids | Fatty acids and glycogen |
| $\hspace{1em}$C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Alpha glucose | Peptides | Fatty acids and glycogen |
| $\hspace{1em}$D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Alpha glucose | Amino acids | Fatty acids and glycerol |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because starch is made of chains of alpha glucose, proteins are made of chains of amino acids and triglycerides are made of fatty acids and glycerol. **Choice A** is incorrect because starch is not composed of beta glucose and peptides are not the simplest units of proteins. **Choice B** is incorrect because starch is not composed of beta glucose and triglycerides do not contain glycogen. **Choice C** is incorrect because peptides are not the smallest unit of proteins and triglycerides do not contain glycogen.
