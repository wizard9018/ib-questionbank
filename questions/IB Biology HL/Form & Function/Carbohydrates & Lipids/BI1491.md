# BI1491

Which of the following statements about lipids is true?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Triglycerides are made up of a four-carbon glycerol molecule linked to three fatty acids.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ There is a carbon-carbon triple bond in monounsaturated fats.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Polyunsaturated fats are made up of carbon-carbon single bonds to keep them in a liquid state.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Saturated fats are made up of carbon-carbon single bonds.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Saturated fats are "saturated" with hydrogen atoms, meaning that the carbon atoms are connected by single bonds, and there are no double bonds. This structure allows the fat molecules to pack closely together, making them solid at room temperature. **Choice D** is correct.

While triglycerides are made up of three fatty acids linked to a glycerol molecule, the glycerol molecule contains three carbon atoms, not four. **Choice A** is incorrect.

Monounsaturated fats have one double bond (hence "mono") in the fatty acid chain. A triple bond is not characteristic of fats but is more often associated with certain specialised organic compounds. **Choice B** is incorrect.

Polyunsaturated fats have more than one (hence "poly") carbon-carbon double bond in their structure. These multiple double bonds create kinks in the fatty acid chains, preventing the molecules from packing tightly together, which contributes to their liquid state at room temperature. **Choice C** is incorrect.
