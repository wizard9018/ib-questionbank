# BI2324

Which characteristics of triglycerides make them suitable for functions in organisms?

| | Energy per gram | Solubility in water | Heat conduction |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Half compared to carbohydrates | Yes | High|
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Twice compared to carbohydrates | Yes | Low |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Half compared to carbohydrates | No | High |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Twice compared to carbohydrates | No  | Low |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because triglycerides contain twice the energy per gram compared to carbohydrates, making them an efficient long-term energy storage molecule. They are insoluble in water, so they can be stored without affecting the water potential of cells. Additionally, triglycerides are poor conductors of heat, which makes them effective thermal insulators, helping to reduce heat loss as a secondary function.

**Choice A** is incorrect because triglycerides contain twice the energy per gram compared to carbohydrates, not half. Furthermore, they are insoluble in water and are poor conductors of heat, contrary to what is stated in this option.

**Choice B** is incorrect because triglycerides are not soluble in water; their hydrophobic nature prevents them from dissolving.

**Choice C** is incorrect because triglycerides provide twice the energy per gram compared to carbohydrates, not half, and they are poor conductors of heat, not good conductors..
