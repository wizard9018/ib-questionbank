# BI0496

A disaccharide is formed by a condensation reaction between a glucose molecule and a fructose molecule. Which molecule fits this description?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Maltose

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Lactose

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Sucrose

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Galactose

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The disaccharide sucrose is made up of the monosaccharides glucose and fructose. Sucrose forms when glucose and fructose combine in a condensation reaction (a reaction that releases water). **Choice C** is the correct answer.

Maltose (**Choice A**) is a disaccharide made up of two glucose molecules. Lactose (**Choice B**) is a disaccharide made up of glucose and galactose. Galactose (**Choice D**) is a monosaccharide, not a disaccharide.
