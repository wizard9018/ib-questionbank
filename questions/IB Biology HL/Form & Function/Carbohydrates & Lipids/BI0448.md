# BI0448

What bond is formed between two monosaccharides to form a disaccharide?

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Ester

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Peptide

- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Glycosidic

- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Sugar-phosphate

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The bond formed between two monosaccharides is a glycosidic bond. **Choice C** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect because hydrogen bonds are weak interactions and do not form covalent links between monomers to create polymers. **Choice B** is incorrect because peptide bonds  link amino acids in polypeptides, not carbohydrates. **Choice D** is incorrect because sugar-phosphate (phosphodiester) bonds  link nucleotides in nucleic acids, connecting the ribose sugars and phosphate groups.
