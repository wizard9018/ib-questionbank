# BI0570

**Maximum mark: 4**

Distinguish between the structure of cellulose, starch and glycogen. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

Any **four** of the following:

$\ce{->}$ cellulose is composed of beta-D-glucose **OR** glucose monomers **WHEREAS** starch and glycogen are composed of alpha-D-glucose monomers  [1 mark]

$\ce{->}$ amylose and cellulose are linear chains **WHEREAS** amylopectin and glycogen are branched [1 mark]

$\ce{->}$ amylose has alpha 1,4 glycosidic bonds **WHEREAS** amylopectin and glycogen have both alpha 1,4 and 1,6 glycosidic bonds [1 mark]

$\ce{->}$ polymers of cellulose form many hydrogen bonds with each other **WHEREAS** starch and glycogen do not [1 mark]

$\ce{->}$ **COMPARED TO**  starch **OR** amylopectin, glycogen is a more highly branched polymer   [1 mark]

$\ce{->}$ every other monomer in cellulose is rotated 180° **WHEREAS** in starch and glycogen the monomers are all oriented the same  [1 mark]
[4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:

|Cellulose | Starch | Glycogen| |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Composed of **beta** glucose | Composed of **alpha** glucose | Composed of **alpha** glucose | ($\checkmark$) $\hspace{3em}$ |
| **Linear** chains of glucose | **Branched** and linear chains of glucose | **Branched** chains of glucose | ($\checkmark$) $\hspace{3em}$ |
| **Many hydrogen bonds** formed between polymers | **Does not form** large numbers of hydrogen bonds between polymers | **Does not form** large numbers of hydrogen bonds between polymers | ($\checkmark$) $\hspace{3em}$ |
| Every other monomer **rotated** 180 degrees | All monomers have the **same** orientation | All monomers have the **same** orientation | ($\checkmark$) $\hspace{3em}$ |
