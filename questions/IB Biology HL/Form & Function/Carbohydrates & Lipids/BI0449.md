# BI0449

What is the product of this reaction?

![BI0449](images/BI0449.png)

- A. $\hspace{1em}$ Fatty acid

- B. $\hspace{1em}$ Starch
- C. $\hspace{1em}$ Glycogen
- D. $\hspace{1em}$ Triglyceride

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \

The diagram shows glycerol and 3 fatty acids. When these are joined in a condensation reaction, a triglyceride is formed. **Choice D** is correct.

**Choices A, B** and **C** are incorrect.
