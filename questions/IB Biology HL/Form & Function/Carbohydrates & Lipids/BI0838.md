# BI0838

Which of the following is a steroid?

![BI0838](images/BI0838.png)

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice D** is correct because all steroids have four linked carbon rings.

**Choice A** is an α-glucose molecule as shown by its hexagonal structure and molecular formula, C${_6}$H:sub[12]O${_6}$. **Choice B** is incorrect as it is an amino acid, as shown by the amine (NH${_2}$) and carboxyl (COOH) group on either end of the R group on the central carbon. **Choice C** is incorrect as it is a triglyceride, as shown by the three long fatty acids attached to the glycerol molecule.
