Describe how the properties of phospholipids allow for the formation of selectively permeable membranes. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

any **four** of the following *(allow any of the following in an annotated diagram)*:

$\ce{ -> }$ phospholipids are amphipathic **OR** have hydrophilic heads and hydrophobic tails [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ hydrophilic heads are orientated towards water **OR** face outwards **OR** towards the aqueous environment [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ hydrophobic tails are orientated away from water **OR** face inwards **OR** away from the aqueous environment [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ the hydrophobic core **OR** hydrophobic tails prevent the passage of polar **OR** charged molecules (through the membrane) [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ small non-polar molecules can pass through the membrane [1 mark]

$\ce{ -> }$ larger **OR** polar **OR** charged molecules require protein channels to pass through the membrane [1 mark]
 [4 marks]

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Phospholipids have **hydrophilic heads** and **hydrophobic tails** ($\checkmark$), they are amphipathic. The hydrophilic heads attract water, so they are orientated **toward** the aqueous environment ($\checkmark$), whilst the hydrophobic tails repel water and are orientated **away** from the aqueous environment ($\checkmark$). The **hydrophobic core** of the bilayer allows only small, **non-polar molecules** such as oxygen to pass ($\checkmark$), however larger or **charged molecules** require protein channels ($\checkmark$).
