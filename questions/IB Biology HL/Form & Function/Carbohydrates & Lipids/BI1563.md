# BI1563

Which row in the table correctly compares and contrasts the features of starch and glycogen?

| | Similarity | Difference |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Both are highly branched polymers | Glycogen has a helical structure |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Both contain 1-4 and 1-6 glycosidic linkages | Glycogen is stored in animal and fungal, but not plant, cells |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Both are stored in plant cells | Starch is composed of both amylose and amylopectin |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Both are composed of alpha glucose monomers | Starch is more water soluble than glycogen |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because glycogen and amylopectin, one of the molecules found in starch, are both composed of 1-4 and 1-6 glycosidic linkages. Glycogen is used for energy storage in animal and fungal cells, as well as some bacteria, while amylopectin is used for energy storage in plants.

**Choice A** is incorrect because amylose, a component of starch, has a helical structure; glycogen does not. **Choice C** is incorrect because glycogen is not found in plant cells. **Choice D** is incorrect because glycogen is more water soluble than starch.
