# BI1550

**Maximum mark: 1**

The following diagram shows the sizes of human gametes (not drawn to scale).

![BI1550A](images/BI1550A.png)

Which of the following correctly describes how these cell sizes accommodate their functions?

| | Oocyte | Spermatozoa |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Large volume to accommodate genetic material | Small volume to increase concentration of nutrients |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Large volume to provide nutrient reserves for developing embryo | Small volume to improve mobility |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Large surface area to improve chances of fertilisation | Small surface area to reduce heat loss |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Large surface area to improve chances of implantation | Small surface area to make fusion with oocyte more efficient |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct oocytes have a large volume to provide room for the nutrients and mitochondria required for embryonic development. This is particularly true for non-mammalian species that rely on external development following fertilisation. Spermatozoa are specialised for motility by having a streamlined, compact design with a small volume of cytoplasm, allowing faster and more efficient movement in order to reach the oocyte.

**Choice A** is incorrect because the oocyte does not need a large volume to contain the genetic material. The sperm cell contains the same amount of genetic material despite being far smaller. **Choice C** is incorrect because heat loss is not a significant concern for sperm cells. **Choice D** is incorrect since the size of the oocyte does not significantly impact the chances of implantation in the uterine wall.
