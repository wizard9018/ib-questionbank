# BI1353

The diagram below shows human gametes.

![BI1353](images/BI1353.png)

Which organelle is particularly abundant in the part labelled X?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Ribosomes

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Mitochondria

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Rough endoplasmic reticulum

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Nucleoids

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because the mid-piece of sperm cells contains a large number of mitochondria for the release of energy to enable the sperm cell to swim towards the egg. While ribosomes are present in sperm cells, they are not particularly abundant in the midpiece, which is primarily dedicated to providing energy for motility; **Choice A** is incorrect. The rough endoplasmic reticulum is involved in protein synthesis and is studded with ribosomes. It plays a role in the production of proteins within the cell but is not specifically abundant in the midpiece of sperm cells; **Choice C** is incorrect. **Choice D** is incorrect because sperm cells are not prokaryotic cells and so do not contain nucleoids.
