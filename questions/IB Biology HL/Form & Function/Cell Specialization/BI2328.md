# BI2328

Which process is controlled by gradients of signalling chemicals in an embryo?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Growth of unspecialised cells in early-stage embryos.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Growth of unspecialised cells in late-stage embryos.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Differentiation of unspecialised cells in early-stage embryos.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Differentiation of unspecialised cells in late-stage foetuses.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because gradients of signalling chemicals in the early-stage embryo determine the differentiation of cells into different cell and tissue types. Cell positions in the embryo determine the gradient and, therefore, determine the pathways in which cells will differentiate.

**Choice A** is incorrect because signalling chemicals primarily determine cell differentiation, not growth.

**Choice B** is incorrect because signalling chemicals determine cell differentiation in early-stage embryos, not cell growth in late-stage embryos.

**Choice D** is incorrect because chemical signalling controls differentiation in early-stage embryos, not late-stage foetuses.
