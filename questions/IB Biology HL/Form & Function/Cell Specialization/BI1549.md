# BI1549

Which of the following are features of stem cells?

- :::tableoptions{col1="hide" col2="hide"}
    | | |
    | :-- | :-- |
    | I. | They have the capacity to divide continuously while remaining undifferentiated. |
    | II. | They are present, in different states of potency, in all stages of an organism's development. |
    | III. | They are found in specialised cell niches such as the skin and bone marrow. |

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ I only

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ I and II only

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ II and III only

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ I, II, and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Stem cells have the ability to divide endlessly, some of which will remain undifferentiated in order to sustain the population of cells (**Statement I**). Although the degree of potency changes throughout life, with less flexibility as the organism ages, stem cells remain an integral part of tissue growth, maintenance, and repair in all stages of development (**Statement II**). Finally, in adult organisms, stem cells are located in stem cell niches, which contain different types of stem cells that can differentiate into appropriate specialised cells depending on the body region where they are found (**Statement III**). This includes stem cell populations located in the skin, hair follicles, and bone marrow, among others. Since all three statements are true, **Choice D** is correct.

**Choices A, B**, and **C** are incorrect because they are missing one or more true statements.
