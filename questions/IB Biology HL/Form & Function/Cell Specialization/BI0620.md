# BI0620

Which of the following characteristics of type I pneumocytes contribute to increasing the rate of gas exchange?

-    I. $\hspace{1em}$ Cells are flattened
-    II. $\hspace{.7em}$ Secretion of a surfactant
-    III. $\hspace{.4em}$ Cover less than 10\% of the alveolar surface

-    A. $\hspace{1em}$ I only

-    B. $\hspace{1em}$ II only
-    C. $\hspace{1em}$ II and III only
-    D. $\hspace{1em}$ I, II and III

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Type I pneumocytes are flattened cells found in the alveoli (**Statement I**). The cells are very thin which means gases have a shorter distance to diffuse.  This results in an increased rate of gas exchange.

The wall of an alveolus is covered mostly (>90\%) by type I pneumocytes (**Statement III**). Type II pneumocytes cover less than 10\% of an alveolus. Type II pneumocytes secrete a surfactant which aids gas exchange because oxygen first dissolves in the surfactant before diffusing across type I pneumocytes (**Statement II**).
