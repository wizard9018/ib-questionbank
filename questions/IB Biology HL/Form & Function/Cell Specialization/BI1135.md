# BI1135

The dimensions of a theoretical cube are doubled. By what factor would its volume increase?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ 2

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ 4

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ 6

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ 8

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
If x represents the unknown dimensions of the cube, then:

Volume of cube = x:sup[3]

Doubling the dimensions, the new volume would be:

Volume of cube = (2x):sup[3 ]

= 8x:sup[3]

Comparing the two volumes:

8x:sup[3] / x:sup[3] = 8

Therefore, the volume of the cube has increased by a factor of 8.

**Choice A** would only be correct if one dimension of the cube were doubled.

**Choice B** would only be correct if two dimensions of the cube were doubled.

**Choice C** could be miscalculated using 2 $\times$ 3 instead of 2:sup[3].
