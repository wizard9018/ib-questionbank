# BI0759

Which row correctly describes the differentiation potential of a zygote, embryonic stem cells and adult stem cells?

| | Zygote | Embryonic stem cells | Adult stem cells |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Can give rise to any cell type of the body and extra-embryonic tissue | Can give rise to a limited range of cell types | Can give rise to any cell type of the body |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Can give rise to any cell type of the body and extra-embryonic tissue | Can give rise to any cell type of the body | Can give rise to a limited range of cell types |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Can give rise to any cell type of the body | Can give rise to any cell type of the body and extra-embryonic tissue | Can give rise to a limited range of cell types |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Can give rise to any cell type of the body and extra-embryonic tissue | Can give rise to any cell type of the body and extra-embryonic tissue | Can give rise to a limited range of cell types |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The zygote is a totipotent cell that can give rise to all cell types of the body and to extra-embryonic tissues, such as the fetal part of the placenta. Embryonic stem cells are pluripotent cells that are derived from the inner cell mass of the blastocyst. These are capable of differentiating into any cell type of the body but not extra-embryonic tissues. Adult stem cells are multipotent cells found in specific tissues and are generally limited to differentiating into cell types of their tissue of origin. **Choice B** is therefore correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect, as embryonic stem cells are pluripotent and can give rise to any cell type of the body, whereas adult stem cells are multipotent and can only give rise to a limited range of cell types.

**Choice C** is incorrect, as the zygote is totipotent and can give rise to extra-embryonic tissues, but embryonic stem cells are pluripotent and cannot. \
**Choice D** is incorrect as embryonic stem cells are pluripotent, not totipotent. They can give rise to any cell type of the body but cannot give rise to extra-embryonic tissues.
