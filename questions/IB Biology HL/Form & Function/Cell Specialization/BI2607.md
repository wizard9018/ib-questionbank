# BI2607

Which feature is **not** found in both skeletal and cardiac muscle cells?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Many mitochondria

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Striated appearance

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Branched cell structure

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Sarcoplasmic reticulum

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Cardiac muscle cells have a branched cell structure, but skeletal muscle cells do not; **Choice C** is correct.

Both skeletal and cardiac muscle cells contain many mitochondria to generate ATP for muscle contraction; **Choice A** is incorrect. Both skeletal and cardiac muscle cells have a striated appearance due to the arrangement of actin and myosin filaments; **Choice B** is incorrect. Both skeletal and cardiac muscle cells contain specialised endoplasmic reticulum called sarcoplasmic reticulum which stores and releases calcium ions for contraction; **Choice D** is incorrect.
