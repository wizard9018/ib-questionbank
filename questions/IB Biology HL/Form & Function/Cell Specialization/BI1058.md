**Maximum mark: 5**

1. Outline **one** functional reason for the difference in **size** between the human sperm and egg cell. [1 mark]

2. Compare and contrast totipotent, pluripotent and multipotent stem cells. [4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. the following (must all be correct to award mark):

 $\ce{ -> }$ the egg cell is larger because it contains nutrient reserves **OR** cytoplasm **OR** organelles to support early embryonic development after fertilisation [1 mark]
 $\ce{ -> }$ the sperm cell is smaller to allow efficient swimming **OR** to reduce resistance **OR** reduce mass for movement towards the egg [1 mark]
 $\ce{ -> }$ sperm cell is smaller because it contributes only DNA **OR** a haploid nucleus and does not need a large cytoplasm [1 mark]
 [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The **egg** cell is much **larger** because it contains **nutrient reserves** needed to **support** the **early stages** of **embryonic development** after fertilisation ($\checkmark$).

2. Any **four** of the following (must include at least one similarity and one difference):

 **Similarities** \
 $\ce{ -> }$ **ALL** three are undifferentiated **OR** are unspecialised cells [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ totipotent, pluripotent and multipotent stem cells are found in embryonic stages [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** pluripotent and multipotent stem cells have potential applications in regenerative medicine and research [1 mark]

 **Differences** \
 $\ce{ -> }$ totipotent stem cells can give rise to both embryonic and extraembryonic tissues, **WHILE** pluripotent stem cells can only differentiate into embryonic tissues [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ **ONLY** multipotent stem cells are typically found in adult tissues [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ totipotent cells have the highest differentiation potential, followed by pluripotent cells, and then multipotent cells [1 mark]

 [4 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Totipotent, pluripotent and multipotent stem cells are all **undifferentiated** cells ($\checkmark$). **All three** are found in **embryos** ($\checkmark$), however, **only multipotent** stem cells are typically found in **adults** ($\checkmark$). **Totipotent** stem cells can give rise to both embryonic and **extraembryonic** (e.g. placental) tissues, while **pluripotent** stem cells can **only** differentiate into **embryonic** tissues ($\checkmark$). **Pluripotent** and **multipotent** stem cells have potential use in **regenerative medicine** and **research** ($\checkmark$), but totipotent stem cells are not commonly used due to ethical considerations.
