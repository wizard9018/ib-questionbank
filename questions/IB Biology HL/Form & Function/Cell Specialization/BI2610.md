# BI2610

Which row of the table shows the correct functions of structures found in and around a mature secondary oocyte?

| | Structure | Function |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| A.$\hspace{1em}$ | Zona pellucida | Prevents polyspermy |
| B.$\hspace{1em}$ | Zona pellucida | Nourishes and protects the oocyte |
| C.$\hspace{1em}$ | Cortical granules | Contains microvilli to absorb nutrients |
| D.$\hspace{1em}$ | Cortical granules | Contains enzymes which break the head of the sperm during fertilisation |

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
The zona pellucida is a jelly coat found around the oocyte. When a sperm reaches the egg, it binds to the zona pellucida, which triggers the acrosome reaction in the sperm cell. This allows the sperm membrane to fuse with the oocyte membrane and the nucleus of the sperm cell to enter for fertilisation to take place. Cortical granules release enzymes after fertilisation that modify the zona pellucida to prevent the entry of additional sperm and, so, prevent polyspermy. **Choice A** is correct.

**Choice B** is incorrect as it is the role of follicle cells to nourish and protect the oocyte. **Choices C** and **D** are incorrect as the role of the cortical granules is to prevent polyspermy during fertilisation.
