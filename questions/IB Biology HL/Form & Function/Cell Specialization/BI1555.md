2. Any **two** of the following (answer must contain at least one similarity and one difference in order to receive full marks):

 *Similarities:* \
 $\ce{ -> }$ **BOTH** adult and embryonic stem cells can differentiate into multiple types of specialised cells [1 mark]

 *Differences:* \
 $\ce{ -> }$ embryonic stem cells can divide indefinitely under correct conditions **WHEREAS** adult stem cells usually have a limited capacity for self-renewal [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ embryonic stem cells are pluripotent (stem cells of inner mass) **OR** totipotent (in zygote / early embryo up to 8-cell stage) **OR** capable of becoming any adult cell type **WHEREAS** adult stem cells are multipotent **OR** capable of becoming a limited number of cell types within a specific lineage [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ embryonic stem cells are found throughout the developing embryo **WHEREAS** adult stem cells are found in various stem cell niches throughout the body [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Both adult and embryonic stem cells have the ability to **differentiate into multiple cell types** ($\checkmark$) as needed. However, embryonic stem cells are more versatile since they are usually **pluripotent** or able to **differentiate into nearly any cell type** ($\checkmark$), whereas adult stem cells are generally **multipotent** or limited to a **smaller number of possible cell types** ($\checkmark$) within a particular **cell lineage**.

 $\ce{ -> }$ studying how organisms develop may provide evidence for evolutionary relationships **OR** help us understand developmental abnormalities [1 mark]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Stem cells are of great potential value to scientists for a number of reasons. Firstly, they have the potential to **grow and divide endlessly**, providing a **reliable supply** of cells for **research** ($\checkmark$). They can also be used to **grow tissues** and perhaps **organs in vitro**, which could one day **replace tissue and organ donation** ($\checkmark$). Finally, stem cells could be used to **treat a variety of diseases** due to their **ability to differentiate** ($\checkmark$).
