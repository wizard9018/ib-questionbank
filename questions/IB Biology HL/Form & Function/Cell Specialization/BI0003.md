# BI0003

Which of the following statements about the effect of surface area and volume on cell processes is **false**?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$  The rate at which materials enter or leave a cell depends on it's surface area.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$  Small cells exchange materials more efficiently by diffusion than large cells.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$  The rate at which materials are used and produced depends on the surface area.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$  A large spherical cell may not be able to excrete waste products fast enough by diffusion.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is a false statement because the rate at which materials are used and produced depends mainly on the volume of the cell, not its surface area. As this is a false statement, it makes **Choice C** the correct answer to the question.

**Choice A** is a true statement because diffusion occurs across the cell membrane, so the exchange rate depends on the surface area.

**Choice B** is a true statement because smaller cells have a larger surface area-to-volume ratio, allowing more efficient diffusion.

**Choice D** is a true statement because large cells have a smaller surface area-to-volume ratio, which can limit the diffusion of waste products out of the cell.
