# BI1548

**Maximum mark: 1**

What is the role of cell signalling gradients in early-stage embryonic development?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ They facilitate neuronal communication within the embryo.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ They control the rate of meiosis.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ They regulate gene expression.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ They ensure proper distribution of nutrients until organ systems begin to develop.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** is correct because cell signalling gradients modify gene expression in cells depending on where they are located in the developing embryo. Although all of the cells in an embryo have the same genome, the use of gradients and variable gene expression leads to differentiation and cell specialisation.

**Choice A** is incorrect because early-stage embryos do not yet have neurons. **Choice B** is incorrect as early cell divisions are mitotic, not meiotic. **Choice D** is incorrect since at the embryonic stage, nutrient distribution is achieved by the concentration gradients of the nutrients themselves, not by cell signaling chemicals.
