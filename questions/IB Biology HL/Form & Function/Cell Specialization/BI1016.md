# BI1016

An experiment investigating tidal volume in the lungs was conducted using a spirometer. In the experiment, the volume of air inhaled and exhaled with each breath was measured for one minute while the student was at rest. The results obtained from the spirometer are displayed in the graph below. The measurement was repeated for the student during exercise.

![BI1016-SLHL](images/BI1016-SLHL.png)

1. Identify the independent and dependent variables in this experiment. [2 marks]

2. State what is measured to give the tidal volume of the student. [1 mark]

3. Calculate the mean tidal volume for one minute, giving the units. [2 marks]

4. Suggest how the tidal volume would differ during exercise. [1 mark]

5. State which cells of the alveoli are specialised to allow gaseous exchange to occur. [1 mark]

6. Describe an experimental method, not involving a spirometer, that could be used by students to measure ventilation rate. [3 marks]

6. Any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ ventilation rate is the number of breaths per given time **OR** per minute **OR** second [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ using a stopwatch [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ count the number of times air is breathed in **OR** breathed out through observation **OR** using differential pressure sensor **OR** inflatable chest belt around the thorax [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ measure **OR** repeat several times **OR** collect at least three sets of results **OR** calculate mean from several sets of results [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ any relevant controlled variable, such as using the same person to conduct the activity over a period of time **OR** sitting position **OR** breathing techniques are controlled that can affect the ventilation [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ baseline measurement for comparison [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The **ventilation rate** is the **number of breaths** over a **set period of time**, usually the number of **breaths per minute** ($\checkmark$). This can be measured by **counting** the number of times air is **inhaled** and then **exhaled** in a **minute** at rest, through **observation** of **chest movements** ($\checkmark$). A **stopwatch** is used to measure the time ($\checkmark$). This is then **repeated** so that at least **three results** are recorded to **calculate the mean**. ($\checkmark$).
