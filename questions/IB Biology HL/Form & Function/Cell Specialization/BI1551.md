## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice B** is correct because for cuboidal cells, as side length increases, the volume increases at a greater rate than the surface area. This leads to a progressively lower surface area-to-volume ratio.

**Choice A** is incorrect as the relationship between cell size and surface area to volume ratio is not linear. **Choice C** is incorrect as the relationship is neither linear nor positive. **Choice D** is incorrect since the surface area to volume ratio decreases rapidly rather than gradually as the dimensions of the cell increase.
