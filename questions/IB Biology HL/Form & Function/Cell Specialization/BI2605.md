# BI2605

Which of the following best describes the role of surfactant in the alveoli?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Breaks down excess mucus in the alveoli, helping the airways to remain clear

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Increases surface tension, preventing overexpansion during inhalation

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Maintains osmotic pressure in the alveoli, ensuring that pneumocytes maintain their shape

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Decreases surface tension, preventing alveolar collapse during exhalation

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
Surfactant is a detergent secreted by Type 2 pneumocytes in the alveoli. The function of surfactant is to reduce surface tension in the alveoli so that the alveoli do not stick together or collapse during exhalation; **Choice D** is correct.

Surfactant does not break up excess mucus in the alveoli. Mucus is swept away from the airways by ciliated cells in the airways; **Choice A** is incorrect. Surfactant does not increase surface tension; **Choice B** is incorrect. Surfactant’s function is not related to osmotic pressure regulation; **Choice C**  is incorrect.
