# BI1553

**Maximum mark: 7**

A student wants to investigate how cell size affects the rate of diffusion. They carried out an experiment using alkaline agar cubes of different dimensions infused with pink phenolphthalein indicator, as shown in the diagram below. The phenolphthalein indicator becomes clear at pH values below 9.

![BI1553](images/BI1553.png)

Three cubes of each size are submerged in an acetic acid solution and removed every two minutes so that the volume diffused can be calculated. The results of the experiment are summarised in the table below.

![Untitled 5](<images/Untitled 5.png>)

1. Explain how the student was able to determine the percentage volume diffused in this experiment. [3 marks]

2. Evaluate, using the data, the student’s hypothesis that the rate of diffusion decreases linearly as cube side length increases. [2 marks]

3. How do the results of this experiment relate to the functional limitations of cells based on their sizes? [2 marks]

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

1. any **three** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ phenolphthalein indicator turns clear **OR** changes colour **OR** goes from pink to clear when exposed to acetic acid **OR** ethanoic acid [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ cubes become progressively clear as acetic acid diffuses in [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ dimensions of pink **OR** clear region can be measured with a ruler [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ volume is calculated by multiplying length, width, and height **OR** length cubed [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ divide the volume of the diffusion zone by the total cube volume and multiply by 100% to express it as a percentage [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ repeat readings made **OR** outliers eliminated **AND** mean values calculated
 [1 mark]
 [3 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 The student needs to **compare the dimensions of the diffused and undiffused portions** ($\checkmark$) of each cube. This can be observed since **phenolphthalein changes** from **pink to clear** when **exposed to acetic acid** ($\checkmark$). The pink undiffused region of each cube can be **measured with a ruler** ($\checkmark$) and its **length cubed** to determine **volume** ($\checkmark$). This value is then **subtracted from the total volume** ($\checkmark$) in order to **calculate percentage diffusion**. Repeat readings allow outliers to be identified and eliminated so a **reliable mean can be calculated** ($\checkmark$).

2. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ hypothesis rejected OR not supported [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ because The relationship is non-linear **OR** geometric **OR** exponential [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ doubling the dimensions of a cube results in a diffusion percentage **OR** rate of diffusion that is much less than expected for a linear decrease **OR** less than half [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ volume increases faster than surface area **OR** surface area to volume ratio decreases as cube size increases **OR** surface area is squared WHEREAS volume is cubed [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ resulting in much slower rates of diffusion for larger cubes [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 As cube size increases, the **rate of diffusion decreases more than expected** ($\checkmark$) based on the dimensional differences. For example, the 4 cm cube, despite being only twice as wide as the 2 cm cube, has a rate of diffusion and percentage volume diffusion **much less than half**. This would indicate that the student’s **hypothesis would not be supported** ($\checkmark$).

3. any **two** of the following:

 $\ce{ -> }$ there is a size limitation on cells [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ because diffusion is less effective **OR** slower as cell size **OR** volume increases [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ because they require nutrients **OR** gas exchange **OR** removal of waste products **OR** the efficient movement of substances into and out of the cytoplasm to survive [1 mark]

 $\ce{ -> }$ because surface area increases more slowly than volume **OR** volume increases more quickly than surface area **OR** surface area to volume ratio decreases as cells grow larger [1 mark]
 [2 marks]

 $\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$: \
 Cells are **unable to grow beyond a certain size** ($\checkmark$) because **diffusion would be insufficient** ($\checkmark$) to allow them to **receive** the **nutrients** they **require** to **survive** ($\checkmark$).
