# BI1552

Which of the following correctly describes stem cell differentiation?

- A.$\hspace{1em}$ Totipotent stem cells can differentiate into any embryonic or adult cell type plus extraembryonic tissues such as the placenta, while multipotent stem cells can only differentiate into cell types within a specific lineage.

- B.$\hspace{1em}$ Multipotent stem cells can differentiate into any embryonic or adult cell type plus extraembryonic tissues such as the placenta, while pluripotent stem cells can only differentiate into cell types within a specific lineage.

- C.$\hspace{1em}$ Pluripotent stem cells can differentiate into any embryonic or adult cell type excluding extraembryonic tissues, while totipotent stem cells can only differentiate into cell types within a specific lineage.

- D.$\hspace{1em}$ Totipotent stem cells can differentiate into any embryonic or adult cell type plus extraembryonic tissues such as the placenta, while pluripotent stem cells can only differentiate into cell types within a specific lineage.

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice A** is correct because totipotent stem cells, found in embryos, have the ability to differentiate into any cell type needed to form an entire organism, plus extraembryonic tissues such as the placenta. Pluripotent stem cells can become nearly any cell type in the organism except for extraembryonic tissues, whereas multipotent stem cells are limited to a particular cell lineage, such as blood cells produced by bone marrow stem cells.

**Choice B** is incorrect since multipotent stem cells are not able to become any cell type. **Choice C** is incorrect as pluripotent stem cells are not able to produce embryonic cells. Finally, **choice D** is incorrect since pluripotent cells are not limited to particular cell lineages.
