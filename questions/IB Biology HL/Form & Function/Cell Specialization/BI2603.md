The micrograph shows the proximal convoluted tubule in the kidney. Which letter labels a structure that is an adaptation for exchange?

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Explanation}}$: \
**Choice C** points to the brush border of microvilli, which greatly increases the surface area of the proximal convoluted tubule (PCT) cells, facilitating efficient reabsorption of substances from the filtrate in the nephron; **Choice C** is correct.

**Choice A** is incorrect, as it points to a mitochondrion which produces ATP during aerobic respiration, providing the energy needed for many processes in the nephron. **Choice B** is incorrect, as it points to the nucleus, which contains the genetic information and controls the activities of the cell. **Choice D** is incorrect, as it points to the lumen of the proximal convoluted tubule (PCT) which is not an adaptation for exchange itself. The lumen is simply the central space through which filtrate flows.
