# PS0754

Other than heuristics, explain two additional reasons why the dual process model suggests that System 1 is considered to be more likely than System 2 to make mistakes.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Any **two** of the following would be acceptable.

System 1 thinking is quicker than system 2 thinking. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) This speed may result in more errors. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

In contrast to System 2, System 1 thinking is not likely to consider alternative explanations or options when making decisions. This means that superior or more accurate choices or explanations are overlooked. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

System 1 thinking requires less mental/cognitive effort than system 2 thinking. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) This lack of cognitive effort may result in more errors. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

System 1 thinking is more prone to being influenced by biases than System 2,  (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) increasing the likelihood of error.  (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
