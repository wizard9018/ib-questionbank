# PS0723

Outline the main function of short-term memory (STM) according to the multi-store model.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\

To temporarily hold a limited amount of information in our conscious awareness long enough (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) for us to be able to manipulate it to perform everyday functions such as problem-solving and decision-making. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
