# PS0755

Briefly discuss the accuracy of the following statement in relation to the dual process model, $\text{\textquotedblleft}$When making a decision, we only use system 1 or system 2.”
[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

- The use of the word only in this statement is inaccurate (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) as it is overly simplistic (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

- It would be accurate to state that in some situations (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) we only use System 1 thinking,(:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

- In many situations, we make use of System 1 to make a quick or intuitive judgement or decision, (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) but we may then pass the information to our System 2 for further consideration. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) 

- Activation of System 1 is activated before System 2 (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) and in some instances, we are satisfied with our System 1 conclusion and do not pass the information onto System 2. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

$\underline{\textrm{Marking Guidance}}$:\

Marks should be awarded for any similarly worded or additional accurate points to a maximum of 6. Both inaccuracies and accuracies need to be stated. If only accuracies or inaccuracies are stated then marks should be awarded to a maximum of **3 marks**.
