# PS0721

Describe the processes that take place for a memory to make its way to our short-term memory store. Begin your description at the entry point of memory. Your description should make use of the correct terminology from the multi-store model. Finally, explain what happens to information that does not make it to short-term memory.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\

The entry point of memory is sensory memory. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

All sensory information that reaches our senses is temporarily stored in relevant sensory registers in sensory memory. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

Information that is attended to is transferred to short-term memory. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

After temporary storage, any information that is not attended to will be permanently lost or forgotten. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
