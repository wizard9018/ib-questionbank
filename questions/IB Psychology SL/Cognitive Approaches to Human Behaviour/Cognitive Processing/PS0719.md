# PS0719

Outline two (2) functions of sensory memory according to the multi-store model.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\

To temporarily store all sensory information that is received by our senses in its raw original form. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

To hold sensory information long enough for it to be transferred to short-term-memory if needed. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

To prevent overload of information by ensuring that not all information from our senses makes its way to short-term memory. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
