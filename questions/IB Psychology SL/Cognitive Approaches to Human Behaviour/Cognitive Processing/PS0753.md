# PS0753

According to the dual process model, explain two reasons why System 1's reliance on heuristics may result in more errors in decision-making.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Any **two** of the following would be acceptable:

Using heuristics allows us to focus on key elements of a problem or situation while ignoring less pertinent information. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)  In doing so, important information may be overlooked. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) 

Heuristics may be easily influenced by cognitive biases, (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)  increasing the likelihood of error. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) 

Heuristics allow for the simplification of complex problems  (:span$\color{red}

\checkmark$) , potentially leading to oversimplification and, therefore, inaccurate decisions.  (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
