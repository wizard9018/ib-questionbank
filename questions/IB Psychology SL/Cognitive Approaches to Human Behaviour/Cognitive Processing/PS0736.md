# PS0736

Apply your knowledge:\
Caitlin was busy studying for her IB Psychology exams. She usually finds it relaxing to listen to music but was frustrated because it was difficult to study and listen to her favourite Taylor Swift songs at the same time. Her dad suggested that she should try listening to his classical music (instrumental) CDs while she studies, instead of her regular music. To Caitlin’s surprise, this was a successful approach. Use your knowledge of the working memory model to explain why this might be the case.
[7 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample answer}}$:\

The phonological loop is one of the slave systems of the working memory model. It deals with auditory information and is made up of the phonological store and the articulatory process. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) The phonological store temporarily stores external sounds that we hear (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$), while the articulatory process allows us to mentally/internally rehearse words. Each of these components has limited capacity. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

When studying, Caitlin would make use of her articulatory process to mentally rehearse the information she was trying to learn.  (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) When she tried to listen to music with lyrics (eg., Taylor Swift), these lyrics would be competing for the limited capacity available in her phonological loop. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) Therefore, there would likely be interference and studying would be difficult.  (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

However, when listening to music without lyrics, Caitlin’s phonological store would be required to temporarily listen to and store the sounds of the music.  (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) At the same time, her phonological loop would be required to mentally rehearse the information she is studying. As these two tasks use separate subsystems, they are not competing for the limited available capacity. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) Therefore, there is less interference and it would be easier to do both things simultaneously.  (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

$\underline{\textrm{Marking Guidance}}$:\

Any accurate statements that are similarly worded should also be awarded marks up to a maximum of **7 marks**. However, the response must contain the following information:

- Identification of the relevant components of working memory: Phonological loop, phonological store, articulatory process

- Acknowledgement of the limited capacity of the phonological loop.

- Identification of the articulatory process being used when listening to music with lyrics and when studying.  

- Identification of the phonological store being used when listening to music without lyrics. 

- The presence of lyrics in the music would compete with the limited capacity of the phonological store, thereby creating interference and making studying difficult.

- The absence of lyrics in the music would not compete with the limited capacity of the phonological store. This would result in no interference and studying would be easier.
