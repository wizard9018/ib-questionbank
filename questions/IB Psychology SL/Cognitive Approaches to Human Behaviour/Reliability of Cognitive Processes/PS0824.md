# PS0824

Explain one $`$real-life example' of how the availability heuristic could result in faulty decision-making.
[4 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Bernice is planning a family holiday to Italy. She joins an Italy travel planning group on social media. The day before she leaves for her trip, a person in the group creates a post recounting their recent experience of being pickpocketed while walking around a busy piazza in Rome. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) As a result of the availability heuristic, Bernice becomes excessively anxious while on her trip and is always worried when in busy places (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$). As this recounted story was fresh in her mind (easily available), (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) Bernice may significantly overestimate the actual risk of being pickpocketed in Italy. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

(Overestimating the likelihood of a terrorist attack or a plane crash would also be suitable examples, particularly if these have recently been reported on the news.)
