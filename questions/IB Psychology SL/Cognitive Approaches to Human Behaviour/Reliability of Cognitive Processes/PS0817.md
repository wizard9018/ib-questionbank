# PS0817

Explain one way in which you might be able to avoid making faulty judgements based on confirmation bias. 

[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

By being aware that confirmation bias exists (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) we might be able to avoid it by actively seeking out contradictory evidence before making a judgement. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
