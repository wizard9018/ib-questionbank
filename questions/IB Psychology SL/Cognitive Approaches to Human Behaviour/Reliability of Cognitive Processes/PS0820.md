# PS0820

Explain one $`$real-life example' of how anchoring bias could result in faulty decision-making.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Retailers will often set the price of a product at \$19.99 instead of \$20.  The “19” acts as an anchor, so the product seems cheaper than it really is. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) This can result in faulty decision-making because customers may believe they are getting a bargain and decide to buy the item, even though the difference in cost is only one cent. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
