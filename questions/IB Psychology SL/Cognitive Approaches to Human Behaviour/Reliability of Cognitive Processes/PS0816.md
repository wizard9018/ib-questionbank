# PS0816

Explain one $`$real-life example' of how confirmation bias could result in faulty decision-making. 

[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

Minnie's favourite YouTuber always uses a particular brand of headphones, and as a result, Minnie thinks that they are high-quality headphones. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) Before buying the headphones, Minnie reads and watches a load of online reviews for the headphones. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) When doing so, Minnie searches for positive reviews (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) but does not read or watch any negative reviews (even though there are plenty of them available). (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) In this situation, confirmation bias has resulted in Minnie actively seeking out evidence that confirms their pre-existing beliefs about the quality of the headphones. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) It also meant that Minnie failed to search for contradictory evidence. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) As a result, Minnie's currently held belief that they are high-quality headphones was strengthened, (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) even though this belief may be false. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
