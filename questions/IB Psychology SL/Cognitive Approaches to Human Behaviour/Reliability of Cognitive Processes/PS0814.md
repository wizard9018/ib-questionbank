# PS0814

Outline the concept of cognitive bias.
[2 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:\

A cognitive bias is a predictable error in decision-making or thinking (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) that can systematically occur when an individual is processing and interpreting information. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
