# PS0766

Define the following key terms: twin studies, adoption studies, and pedigree studies. Then, explain how each helps in understanding the role of genetics in psychological disorders.
[6 marks]

---

## 评分标准 / Mark scheme

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$:

**Twin Studies**\

Twin studies compare the similarity of psychological traits or disorders between identical twins (MZ), who share 100% of their genes, and fraternal twins (DZ), who share 50% of their genes. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$) Twin studies help us understand the role of genetics in psychological disorders by checking if identical twins are more likely to both have the same disorder than fraternal twins, which suggests that genes play a part. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)  

**Adoption Studies**\

Adoption studies investigate the psychological traits or disorders of adopted individuals, comparing them to their biological parents (genetic influence) and adoptive parents (environmental influence). (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

Adoption studies help separate the effects of genetics and environment by showing whether adopted people are more like their biological parents (genetics) or their adoptive parents (environment). (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)

**Pedigree Studies**\

Pedigree studies track the inheritance of traits or disorders in families across generations to identify genetic patterns. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)  Pedigree studies help identify whether certain psychological disorders run in families, indicating a potential genetic predisposition to those disorders. (:span$\color{red}\checkmark$)
