# PS0536

Outline the role of one agonist to human behaviour, using one study.
[9 marks]

-    Synapse - the junction between two neurons where information passess

$\underline{\textrm{Sample Answer}}$: \

One agonist that influences human behaviour is serotonin. $\textcolor{#000000}{[\textbf{DA}]}$  Neurotransmitters such as serotonin are released into the gap between neurons after which they bind with the receptors on the dendrites of the post-synaptic neuron. In doing so, these neurotransmitters impact the chances of an action occurring in the next neuron. Neurotransmitters can be affected by agonists which are substances that can increase the chances of an action occurring, or they can amplify the effects of a neurotransmitter. Agonists act by binding to the receptors present at the end of the dendrites  $\textcolor{#000000}{[\textbf{DA}]}$ Agonists may be produced in the body in the form of hormones or other neurotransmitters known as endogenous agonists.  They may also be introduced into the body in the form of drugs, known as exogenous agonists.  $\textcolor{#F04438}{[\textbf{KT}]}$. One study that investigated the effect of the endogenous agonist and neurotransmitter serotonin on brain activity and reactions to emotional stimuli is the investigation done by Passamonti et. al (2012). $\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$

Passamonti et. al (2012) aimed to investigate the effect of reduced serotonin in our interpretation of threats from the environment leading to impulsive behaviour.The study had 19 healthy participants both men and women with a mean age of 25. The participants were then divided into 2 conditions, condition 1 consumed a tryptophan-depleting drink which reduced serotonin levels, and condition 2 drank a placebo. The participants were then placed in an fMRI machine and were shown images of happy, neutral, or sad faces. The brain activity of the participants in condition 1 showed a reduced activation of the prefrontal cortex (PFC) compared to condition 2 when they were viewing the angry faces. The fMRI also showed that expected communication between the PFC and the amygdala when viewing the angry faces was disrupted in the participants with less serotonin. The amygdala is a brain structure involved in processing emotions such as fear and aggression. As serotonin can inhibit activation of the amygdala, lack of communication means that any communication to inhibit amygdala activation may not take place. This could potentially lead to increased aggression. $\textcolor{#008c3a}{[\textbf{S}]}$

This gives evidence to the role of serotonin as an agonist. When serotonin levels are reduced, this is associated with reduced activation of the prefrontal cortex and reduced communication between the PFC and the amygdala. Which may, in turn, influence levels of aggression. $\textcolor{#4472C4}{[\textbf{L}]}$
